# Platform Narrative Pulse — 2026-09-01

## Movement Summary

- **LinkedIn**: polish dial holds, but the crowd now punishes AI-patterned polish directly (1M+ "seems like AI slop" flags, ~40% view suppression). Floor rose; direction didn't move.
- **Instagram Reels**: polish dial holds (PRODUCED), but production got commoditized (Instagram First Draft, auto-cut Reels in <10s). Differentiator shifts from craft to POV specificity.
- **X/Twitter**: temperature holds COLD, but the *mechanism* was wrong. Published algorithm weights show dwell is nearly worthless (0.05) and forwardability (copy-link share = 20, follow = 4) is what X actually pays for.
- **TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Substack, Threads**: no dial movement found in-window. Threads' B2B-fit flag escalates from "thin" to "needs an owned A/B — desk research is exhausted."
- **YouTube long-form**: no dial movement; a metrics change (views count from first frame, Aug 24) makes any before/after comparison across that date invalid.

## Per-Platform Delta

**LinkedIn.** [VERIFIED] LinkedIn's "Seems like AI slop" flag passed 1M+ uses in ~3 weeks (Slashdot, The Register, Fortune, Social Media Today, Entrepreneur — all Aug 21–25). [LIKELY] Flagged posts see ~40% fewer views, with a public "some members told us this seems like AI" notice (Moneywise). The often-repeated "41% of longform posts are AI-generated" trigger stat is [UNCONFIRMED] — no primary found. Card call unchanged (I-story, full arc, polished-written, HOT); what changed is the cost of getting "polished" wrong. Specificity is now armor, not decoration.

**Instagram (Reels + feed).** [VERIFIED] Instagram shipped First Draft Aug 25 — auto-trims and assembles a rough Reel cut in under 10 seconds (TechCrunch, corroborated by PetaPixel, Dataconomy, Social Media Today). [LIKELY] Edits app added Loudness Match, 200+ SFX, custom audio import in the same window (multi-source, no single fetchable primary). Baseline metrics (carousels edging Reels in Q2 engagement, single-image posts down ~46% YoY) are stable, not new. Card's PRODUCED call holds; the note to add is that "produced" no longer differentiates — everyone can hit that bar now.

**TikTok.** [LIKELY] No algorithm change shipped in the window (SocialPilot, unconfirmed publish date). Creator-tooling batch (Creator Chat Rooms, Content Check Lite) is real but not dial-moving. [UNCONFIRMED] A tested "Don't Show on Profile" post type would decouple raw feed experiments from a curated grid — interesting, too thin to act on. The load-bearing find is niche-specific: [VERIFIED] TikTok's own advertiser guidance requires credential disclosure for health creators, against [LIKELY] research showing 69% of health creators carry no relevant qualification and only 2% disclaim (The Protein Works, synthesizing academic sources). Card call (face-to-camera, raw, single-beat, WARM) unchanged; the competitive-lane read strengthens.

**YouTube long-form.** [VERIFIED] Effective Aug 24, a public view counts from the first frame of playback, across long-form, Shorts, and Live — YouTube's own language, corroborated by vidIQ, Android Police, RouteNote, LiveReacting (identical quoted framing across all four is consistent with one official source I could not fetch directly). This doesn't touch retention/watch-time, which is the metric the card's HOTTEST call actually rests on — but it does mean **no view-count comparison should span that date.** No dial change.

**YouTube Shorts.** [LIKELY, announced Aug 10, effective Feb 1 2027] YPP now requires a rolling 10M qualified Shorts views per 90 days to keep earning on Shorts, regardless of original qualification path (AIR Media-Tech, Kotaku, vidpros — none fetchable as primary). Card's COOL call is confirmed and sharpened: at practitioner scale, Shorts is a funnel into long-form, not a revenue surface. Optimize for click-through, not Shorts-native metrics.

**X/Twitter — the substantive find.** [VERIFIED, fetched as primary] xAI open-sourced the actual ranking weights (github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm, Aug 13–14). Score = Σ(weight × P(action)). Positive weights: copy-link share 20.0, reply 5.0, quote 5.0, DM share 5.0, follow author 4.0, favorite 0.5, dwell **0.05**. Negative: report −234, mute −58.8, not-interested −43.2. [VERIFIED] This falsifies the dominant SEO-blog claim (repeated across SocialPilot, posteverywhere, Socialync, MomentumHive) that dwell carries a +10 weight and rewards long-form reading. It doesn't. X pays for being *sent*, not *read*. Card call (COLD, info-first, no arc) holds — but the stated reason should change: length isn't the liability, unforwardability is. The 4.0 follow-weight is also the strongest documented case for identity-led POV content X has offered, which mildly complicates a pure info-first framing.

**Substack (newsletter + Notes).** No primary published in-window. Most recent relevant item is Feb 2026 (Notes feed deprioritizing followed accounts in favor of restacks) — already reflected in the card. [UNCONFIRMED] One practitioner claim that ~1-in-3 Notes now carries photo/video, which would be a real polish-dial move (crafted-casual → crafted-visual) if verified. Flagged as a next-pulse watch item, not actioned.

**Threads.** No dial movement. [LIKELY] Composer now auto-splits a pasted long paragraph into linked updates — a mild affordance toward longer text, working against the strict single-open-loop-beat framing, not yet strong enough to move the card. Every "Threads works for B2B" source found (Postory, SociaVault, Junction Creative) is vendor content with no outcome data for expert-practitioner accounts specifically, and several actively argue for depth/authority over the open-loop cut. The B2B-fit flag is not resolvable by more search.
