## 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)

The evidence says this business works, but not the way the "sell Claude skills" content says it works. The raw skill layer is commoditized to zero: 30,000+ free skills exist, marketplaces price vertical skills at $3-25 and pay creators almost nothing, and VC-backed vertical agents own the top (Harvey: $300M ARR). **The structurally empty tier is $50-500 products that pair vertical SOP depth + a human-review gate + a checkable output, sold direct off your own site to an owned audience.** The single primary-source-verified seller at your target income is Ryan Doser (~$3-5K in 50 days, Stripe-screenshotted). He sold a $99 bundled 20-skill stack with a $1,000 setup upsell off his own landing page to a pre-built audience. Zero marketplaces, zero ads. Your fastest credible path: launch a **Real Estate Compliance Skill Pack** (Fair Housing listing auditor as the lead skill) where Jen gives you same-week receipts on real listings, price it $99 with a $500-750 install upsell, and build the authority site as an email-capture engine. Product #2 is the Insurance E&O audit pack, the thinnest-supply and highest-premium vertical found. $200-300/mo is 3 pack sales. $3-5K/mo is packs plus 3-5 setup upsells per month, which the evidence says requires the owned audience you'd be building from day one.

## 2. The Top 10 Markets (ranked by WTP evidence × underservedness · Swarm A)

1. **Legal (solo/small firm): document workflows with citation verification.** Highest WTP ($100-700/mo already spent), lowest adoption (12%). 1,148+ hallucinated-citation cases and $145K+ in 2026 sanctions make *verification itself* the product. ⚠ Professional-liability tail; no domain access for you.
2. **Insurance agencies: E&O compliance documentation.** ~2,900 hrs/yr compliance burden per 10-person agency; 1-in-7 policy files has a gap; **thinnest bundled supply of any vertical checked**. Carriers literally pay for the deliverable (5-20% E&O premium discounts for performed audits).
3. **Accounting/bookkeeping: reconciliation + month-end close.** $10.87B/44.6% CAGR, but Anthropic/Intuit's free QuickBooks MCP + free GAAP packs commoditize the base layer; only audit trails and review gates are sellable.
4. **Healthcare admin: prior auth/RCM denials (never clinical).** $21.49B market; emotionally loaded bottleneck. ⚠ Compliance kill-switch sits above the buyer.
5. **Real estate: sub-verticalized, voice-preserving.** Live testimonial: 5 Claude skills "replaced $2,000/mo of work." Shallow prompt-pack layer already saturated at $17-197; the compliance/authority layer is not.
6. **GovCon/RFP shredding.** 15+ hrs → under 5 per RFP; buyers speak in revenue ("win rate 15%→28%", "$18M in new contracts").
7. **Construction estimating/takeoff.** Validated $20-299/mo ladder; monetizes best as recurring community ($149/mo Skool precedent).
8. **Freight/logistics dispatch paperwork.** Proven $49-1,999/mo spend band.
9. **Mortgage/lending document processing.** $3,500 setup deals documented; bank-statement/paystub extraction is the named bottleneck.
10. **Property management + dental front office.** Module-stacking pricing template: $99-299/mo per narrow add-on task.

**Excluded by evidence**: developer tooling (identity resistance + $5-50 ceiling + platform bundling) and banking/finance (Anthropic + OpenAI are both first-party competitors there).

## 3. The Business Model Truth (Swarm B)

- **Only owned distribution clears $500+/mo.** Doser ($99 stack, $1K upsell, 33K-subscriber audience) is the verified ceiling case, while cold-audience sellers document $57-493 in *total* sales. On Gumroad the median seller makes $72/mo, 44% of products earn $0, and the top 1% take 99.5% of revenue.
- **Marketplaces are a mirage**: Smithery pays creators nothing, ClaudeLog has no seller mechanism, and the GPT Store's payout pilot medians under $100/quarter across ~3M submissions. "Codex skills" is not a separate market; cross-platform compatibility is a checkbox on one pack.
- **Download vs hosted-access is the real packaging split.** Static file packs cap around $200-800/mo, and practitioners call the model "objectively terrible." The same IP earns 10-50x as setup plus retainer ($1,500-20K setup, $400-15K/mo). The durable shape is a productized service, with file packs as the entry rung.
- **Buyer psychology**: the purchase is identity-protective ("outsourcing tedium, not competence") and trust-scarce, since grifter fatigue is the buyer's default prior. Sellers sell **proof-of-not-being-slop** before the product: real file trees, a free sample skill, named-dollar testimonials, 14-day refunds, live revenue badges.
- **Channel truth**: no channel out-converts another. OWNED vs BORROWED traffic decides. The repeating funnel: build reach anywhere, capture to email, and **the email send closes**. SEO and curated lists are the one standalone-ROI channel because a ranking page is an owned asset.
- **Timing window**: Claude Code sits at ~$1B ARR with no native skill-monetization rail yet. Whoever owns audience and vertical authority now is positioned for when Anthropic or Vercel turns on native billing. Windows are compressing from ~18 toward ~6 months per wrapper generation, so treat any pack as a wasting asset with a services cash-out.

## 4. The Products (Swarm C · SOP-verified skill-pack candidates)

Cross-vertical pattern: **every lead candidate is a bounded classification, date-math, or diff task traced to a regulator, GSE, or association primary source, priced against a per-file human fee or a hard-dollar loss event.** Everything else failed on one of five counts: no authority spine, low frequency, judgment-heavy, needs live integrations, or already free.

### Pack 1 · Real Estate Compliance Pack (LAUNCH WEDGE)
| Skill | Encodes | Authority | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RE-1 Fair Housing Listing-Copy Auditor** (lead) | Pre-publish MLS remarks scan vs banned/cautionary word tiers + protected classes; compliant rewrites | HUD Advertising Word/Phrase List + NAR Article 12 (VERIFIED) | ~$19,787 first HUD violation; $249-499/listing coordination spend |
| **RE-2 Follow-Up Cadence Executor** | KW 8x8/33-touch ledger + speed-to-lead (MIT Oldroyd study, VERIFIED: 100x contact-odds drop at 30 min) | KW MREA + MIT/InsideSales | $300-800/file TCs; $99-1,299/mo coaching |
| **RE-3 CMA Disclosure Formatter** (bonus layer) | NAR SoP 11-1's five mandatory opinion-of-value disclosures · binary checkable | NAR Article 11 (VERIFIED) | $25-200/mo CMA software |

### Pack 2 · Insurance E&O Defensibility Pack (PREMIUM EXPANSION)
- **INS-1 E&O Self-Audit Runner** (lead): structured gap report vs ~9 fixed categories per policy file: IIAT 10-stage Loss Control Guide + KAIA per-file checklist + Oak & Associates. **Carriers pay the buyer to run it** (5-20% premium discounts, up to $12,500 deductible reduction).
- **INS-2 Coverage-Declination Documentation Artifact**: converts call/email records into the signed, dated artifact that defends an E&O claim ("documentation equals defensibility": Big I, VERIFIED). Anchor: $8/hr VAs below, $100K+ claims above. No automation vendor builds for defensibility.

### Pack 3 · Mortgage Ops Pack (ADJACENT · Jen's lender network)
- **MTG-1 TRID Timeline Compliance Tracker** (lead): 12 CFR §1026.19(e)-(g) exact business-day math: LE/CD dates + violation flags. The rarest asset found: a fully specified federal SOP. Regulation-correct or wrong, zero taste.
- **MTG-2 Pre-Submission Condition-Completeness Checker**: DU conditions vs document-set diff; "kicked back for partial conditions" is the vertical's most-repeated complaint. Nobody packages this.
- **MTG-3 Document Age/Format Validator**: Fannie B3-3.2-01 exact rules; feeds MTG-1.

### Pack 4 · GovCon Proposal Pack (LATER · highest per-unit labor value)
- **GC-1 RFP Shred/Compliance-Matrix Builder**: shall/must extraction → OST five-column matrix → L→M→C outline diff. Anchor: 40-80 unbilled founder-hours or $60-250/hr freelance per RFP.
- **GC-2 Pink/Red-Team Self-Check**: Shipley color reviews (VERIFIED primary) for shops that skip them; $5K-15K per consultant cycle avoided.

### Pack anatomy (all packs · the 6 load-bearing parts, per teardowns)
1. Auditable inventory ("14 skills across 4 compliance domains"; never "a bundle")
2. Visible layered file tree (README + Start Here + SKILL-INDEX + HOW-TO-USE); buyers inspect before paying
3. Standalone-unit framing (cherry-pickable, no mega-orchestrator lock-in)
4. Named real license (MIT/Apache-with-attribution or honest "final sale")
5. Update-cadence promise + risk reversal (lifetime updates + 14-30 day guarantee)
6. Explicit limits disclaimer ("compliance aid, not legal advice"); the liability hedge and the trust signal are the same sentence

**Kill list (do not build)**: TC checklists (free everywhere), listing marketing packages (physical), CMA comp-selection (judgment + live MLS), contingency day-count calculators (state divergence = liability), mortgage condition *decision* engines (underwriter judgment), COI automation (commoditized), capability statements (low frequency), capture strategy/win themes (stays human), and **any pitch built on unverifiable percentage claims** (the "91% fewer E&O claims" and "73% win-rate" numbers trace to vendor marketing; pitch on regulation text and carrier-endorsed standards only).

## 10. Counter-Narrative (steelmanned)

1. **Anthropic is your biggest competitor and has pulled the rug before** (24-hour third-party cutoff in April 2026, free vertical suites in legal/finance/accounting). Hedge: verticals Anthropic ignores (insurance agencies, mortgage ops, GovCon SMB), the services layer, and the owned email list. A platform release deletes none of those.
2. **The category's trust substrate is compromised** (26% of skills carry vulnerabilities; top-seller supply-chain poisoning documented). This is your headline *feature*: authority-sourced, human-review-gated, inspectable packs. Security posture is a selling point in a poisoned pond.
3. **Demand may be shallower than CAGRs imply.** MIT found 95% of GenAI pilots show no ROI, Gartner predicts 40%+ of agentic projects canceled by 2027, and GPT Store creators ceiling at $100-500/mo. The hedge is already built in: individual economic buyers, operational (not professional) liability verticals, sub-procurement pricing, services cash-out.
4. **Honest base rate**: most sellers in this category never clear four figures. The verified path that did required an audience. Treat months 1-2 revenue as validation, not the business; the compounding asset is the list + the receipts.
