## The bottom line

Two numbers decide it: **(1) what fees eat, and (2) when she actually gets paid.** Warm audience → discovery is worth $0 → don't pay a marketplace 10%+ to find a crowd she already has. Optimize for low fee + easy link + cash before she travels.

**Pick: Ticket Tailor.** Flat **75¢/ticket** (no %), pays **as tickets sell** (no event hold), branded page + one link.

## Top 3
1. **Ticket Tailor** (https://www.tickettailor.com/pricing/) — lowest fees anywhere, paid as tickets sell, branded page + link, ~15-min Stripe connect. Best on her stated priorities.
2. **Luma** (https://luma.com/pricing) — easiest/prettiest, publish in ~5–10 min, 5% (~$210 on this event). Trade ~$200 for zero friction.
3. **Posh** (https://posh.vip) — DJ/nightlife-native page, 10%+99¢ passed to buyer (she nets full), daily payouts past $1k.

## Skip (and why)
- **Eventbrite** — highest fees + holds payout until after event. Wrong on both for the timeline.
- **Zeffy** — truly $0 but nonprofit-only; for-profit party ineligible / flag risk.
- **DICE / Shotgun / Tixr** — gated signup + funds held until after event. Revisit for a bigger event #2.
- **PayPal / Venmo** — worst fees + freeze risk on volume spike while traveling.

## Two profit moves
1. **Round-number pricing ($40 or $45).** Card cost disappears inside the number; don't itemize a fee line to a warm crowd. Ticket Tailor/Posh auto-charge the buyer.
2. **Set up the payment account today.** Clear ID/payout verification on day one so nothing stalls while she's in Costa Rica.
