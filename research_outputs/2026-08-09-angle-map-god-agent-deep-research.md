## Executive verdict

### The Angle Map: TEST

The market buys positioning, message testing, claim review, qualitative research, and pre-production strategy. Public scopes range from low-cost workshops and claims reviews to [$20,000+ positioning engagements](https://www.inthekitchen.is/pricing) and much larger integrated CPG programs. The exact $750 Angle Map is therefore economically plausible but remains `UNTESTED / NO EVENT`.

The customer job is now more precise:

> Make one defensible, production-usable commercial decision before a launch, retail, packaging, creator, or media window closes—and ensure the product truth, permitted proof, retailer logic, and creative brief tell the same story.

The offer should not sell “three ideas.” It should sell the recommended argument and proof boundary an existing production team can use. Three distinct routes are useful only because they make the tradeoff legible.

### Company-Intelligence Install: NARROW TO A PAID WARM PROOF

Public evidence supports narrow work units: calls, intake, booking, routine support, document processing, and recurring reports, as illustrated by public cases from [ServiceTitan](https://www.servicetitan.com/case-studies/riley-virtual-agents), [Gorgias](https://www.gorgias.com/customers/pajar), and [Mindbody](https://www.mindbodyonline.com/sites/default/files/public/education/learning-assets/2025%20State%20of%20the%20Industry%20Report.pdf). It does not show comparable demand among 5–50 person owner-operated firms for a broad premium “AI employee that knows the business.”

Generic agents are compressed by platforms costing tens to hundreds of dollars per month, including public plans from [Zapier](https://zapier.com/pricing) and [Relevance AI](https://relevanceai.com/docs/get-started/pricing). A premium install must win on one costly, recurring responsibility that requires business-specific judgment, sources, exceptions, evaluation, human review, and stewardship. The current $12,000 founding install is within the asking range of a [public custom-implementation offer](https://plainpath.ai/services/ai-agent-implementation-small-business), but willingness to pay for this exact judgment layer is `UNTESTED`.

### Recurring trigger pattern

The three-source floor is met for these observable triggers:

1. Reformulation or a new benefit makes the old promise incomplete.
2. Retail expansion introduces a new buyer and shelf test.
3. A printer, retailer, launch, creator, or media deadline makes indecision expensive.
4. Portfolio growth or partner proliferation fragments the story.
5. A previously effective campaign or channel plateaus.
6. Lookalike competition erodes the usefulness of generic category language.
7. Claim, platform, or retailer-science scrutiny makes the boldest argument unusable.

The source floor is not met for a universal claim that these triggers cause internal conflict or measurable waste. That consequence remains `PROVISIONAL` until a recipient confirms it.

### Problem-Qualified Segment

**Verdict: PROVISIONAL**

An established supplement, functional beverage, recovery, or performance brand with a real product and a live launch, reformulation, retail expansion, creator campaign, packaging decision, or paid-media reset inside 90 days, where a founder or brand/marketing owner must choose one commercially useful argument that the product evidence, claim boundary, retailer/channel context, and existing production team can all support.

**Required company evidence**

- Live commercial occasion and deadline.
- Multiple plausible benefit, occasion, identity, or proof directions.
- Existing product truth and approved evidence can be inspected.
- A production owner is ready to use the output.
- The buyer can make a $750 decision without undefined procurement.

**Required person evidence**

- Current founder, CMO, brand lead, or marketing owner.
- Owns or materially influences the campaign argument.
- Can identify final claim/legal/science approval where relevant.

**Disqualifiers**

- Pre-product or still deciding what to sell.
- No live occasion or output user.
- The message hierarchy is already explicit in current first-party language.
- Primary bottleneck is product experience, price, distribution, attribution, supply, or media mechanics.
- Wants legal certification, revenue guarantees, rankings, or full campaign execution.
- Cannot provide approved evidence or a decision owner.

**Promotion rule:** The PQS becomes `QUALIFIED` only after direct recipient evidence—past action, interview, purchase, or verbatim confirmation—shows the decision is live and consequential. Public launch traces alone cannot promote it.

## Customer Truth Dossier: Company-Intelligence Install

### What is verified

- Narrow AI work units have visible SMB deployment proof in home services, legal intake, ecommerce support, and fitness front-desk workflows.
- The strongest outcomes are operational: response, booking, routine-case resolution, and staff time. Broad revenue and headcount-replacement effects are much weaker.
- Buyers need setup, source cleanup, rules, testing, staff training, monitoring, and ongoing support.
- Accuracy, privacy, permissions, outdated source data, integration change, and confident wrong answers are material adoption risks.
- Platform-native agents and business AI products increasingly provide company knowledge, connectors, workflows, and administration for tens to hundreds of dollars per month.

### Unknowns not resolved by this research event

- That 5–50 person owner-operated firms broadly want or understand a “company-intelligence employee.”
- That they pay a premium specifically for encoded judgment rather than connected automation.
- That a $12,000 founding install clears a native-platform comparator.
- That the system can lower review time without creating more staff questions, maintenance, or risk.
- That one buyer’s encoded judgment transfers economically to a second customer.

### Problem-Qualified Segment

**Broad segment verdict: REJECTED.** “Owner-operated businesses with 5–50 employees” is a demographic proxy, not a problem-qualified segment.

**Warm discovery segment: PROVISIONAL.** A multi-location fitness/wellness operator with a recurring staff-facing member-policy responsibility that:

- produces enough ambiguous cases to measure;
- currently requires owner or senior-manager judgment;
- has canonical policies and historical cases;
- creates rework, delay, refunds, complaints, or staff interruption;
- is not already solved at least four-fifths by Mindbody or another native platform;
- can remain human-reviewed and fail closed during the proof.

This is a research segment, not validated market demand.

### Paid proof design

1. **One buyer, one responsibility, one primary platform.**
2. Require a paid Foundation checkpoint before custom implementation; proposed proof counter: $3,000 cleared.
3. Freeze 30–50 historical cases into a blinded evaluation set.
4. Compare current human baseline, native-platform/default automation, and the custom judgment overlay.
5. Run in shadow mode: internal drafts, exact policy citations, explicit unknowns, zero consequential writes.
6. Track reviewer time, agreement, missing-evidence detection, rework, escalation accuracy, critical errors, staff question burden, and support hours.
7. Run at least 20 prospective cases before claiming live value.
8. Promote only if the custom layer reduces reviewer time, preserves critical accuracy/permission, and beats the native comparator materially.

**Illustrative test rules—not market findings and still to be buyer-approved:** reviewer-time reduction of at least three-tenths; reviewer agreement in at least nine of ten low-risk classifications; policy citations on every policy-dependent answer; zero unauthorized actions; zero critical privacy/factual failures; support burden under two hours per week.

## Contradictions resolved

1. **High AI adoption vs low agent readiness.** Surveys use different definitions and produce widely different rates. General AI use is not agent-install demand.
2. **Platform affordability vs custom-service prices.** Both are true. The custom service must prove a workflow-specific judgment advantage after platform comparison.
3. **Angle Map price is low vs real category spending.** The founding price reduces friction but can weaken seniority signaling. Do not raise it before exact-offer evidence; do not infer validation from higher adjacent prices.
4. **Claims expertise is valuable vs legal-risk boundary.** Editorial proof mapping is valuable, but legal/scientific approval remains with qualified reviewers.
5. **Public triggers are findable vs internal pain unknown.** A public event qualifies the research target, not the problem. The recipient must confirm or correct the gap.

## One message-market-fit test

**Test:** Five verified Angle Map discovery exposures using a one-page Decision Note.

**Frozen hypothesis:** A source-backed note that identifies one unresolved, time-bound commercial argument and the proof/production tradeoff will earn direct problem confirmations or corrections from qualified founders/brand owners before the offer is introduced.

**Proof state:** PQS is provisional; this is a discovery exposure, not validation.

**Batch:** five candidates that pass same-day role, trigger, message-hierarchy, source, and direct-route checks. One value-bearing follow-up after three business days. No external send without explicit approval.

**Counters:** qualified exposures; meaningful decision-specific responses; problem confirmations; requested notes/next steps; held conversations; exact $750 quoted; sold; collected.

**Promote message/PVP:** at least 2 of 5 meaningful responses and at least 1 problem confirmation.  
**Iterate qualification:** responses reject the diagnosis or show the decision is already made.  
**Kill segment/problem hypothesis:** 10 qualified exposures plus allowed follow-up yield zero problem confirmations.
