## Sniper List Criteria

### Green Flags (Ideal Client Signals)
| Signal | Where to Find |
|--------|---------------|
| Posted inconsistently (3+ month gaps) | LinkedIn activity |
| Bio mentions "purpose" or "impact" | Headline/About |
| Bootstrapped company | About or website |
| Mentioned on Conscious Capitalism | Profile or search |
| Complained about content/ghostwriters | Posts or comments |
| 100K+ revenue indicators | Company size, team |

### Red Flags (Avoid)
| Signal | Why |
|--------|-----|
| Marketing/Content in title | They do this themselves |
| Agency employee | Not decision maker |
| Large corporate | Wrong buyer type |
| VC-funded "growth at all costs" | Values mismatch |
| Recent ghostwriter posts | Already solved |

## Discovery Call Framework

### Pre-Call: Qualification
- Confirmed founder/executive role
- Expressed content frustration
- Revenue indicators ($200K+)

### Call Structure (15 min)
1. **Context** (2 min): "What made you interested in this?"
2. **Pain** (5 min): "What's been frustrating about content so far?"
3. **Solution** (3 min): Brief MES 3.0 overview
4. **Fit** (3 min): "Here's what working together looks like"
5. **Next Step** (2 min): Send proposal OR schedule deeper call

## First Client Target Profile

**Ideal First Client**:
- Founder/CEO of $200K-$2M service business
- Purpose-driven or "conscious" in orientation
- Has tried ghostwriters or DIY and failed
- Values authenticity over vanity metrics
- Has budget ($2K+/mo is trivial for them)
- BONUS: Already in your network or one degree away
