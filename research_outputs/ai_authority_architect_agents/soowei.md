## Leverage Architecture

### What Creates Leverage

| Lever | How It Works |
|-------|--------------|
| **MES 3.0 System** | AI does 60-70% of content generation |
| **Brain Dump Format** | Client time = 30 min/month, not hours of calls |
| **Template Library** | Reusable formats accelerate production |
| **Async-First** | No back-to-back client calls |
| **Capacity Scarcity** | Waiting list creates premium positioning |

### What Kills Leverage

| Anti-Lever | Mitigation |
|------------|------------|
| Unlimited revisions | Cap at 2 rounds per post |
| Scope creep | Clear deliverables (X posts only) |
| Real-time availability | Office hours, not on-demand |
| Under-pricing | $2K minimum maintains premium |

## Recommended Tier Structure

| Tier | Price | Deliverables | Ideal For |
|------|-------|--------------|-----------|
| **Authority Launch** | $2,000/mo | 8 posts, 1 brain dump, 1 call | Testing fit |
| **Authority Accelerator** | $3,500/mo | 12 posts, 2 brain dumps, 2 calls, content calendar | Most clients |
| **Authority Partner** | $5,000/mo | 16 posts, unlimited brain dumps, weekly call, repurposing | Enterprise founders |

---

## Recommendation

**BUILD FOR 6 CLIENTS AT $3K.** This hits $18K/month (close to $20K target) while staying within 2-hour/day work constraint. Use waiting list to create premium scarcity once capacity is reached.
