## Post Templates

### Educational (Carousel)
```
Slide 1: Hook (contrarian statement)
Slide 2-6: The framework/steps
Slide 7: Summary
Slide 8: CTA (follow, DM, comment)
```

### Story (Text)
```
Line 1: Hook line (7 words max)
P1: Setup (the situation)
P2: Conflict (what went wrong)
P3: Insight (what I learned)
P4: Resolution (what changed)
Final: Universal takeaway
```

### Offer (Text)
```
Line 1: Direct statement
P1: Who this is for
P2: What you get
P3: How it works
P4: Next step (DM or link)
```
