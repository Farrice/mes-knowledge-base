It's a sharp three-pillar data point though: naming the *specific* tests a brand runs (PFAS, microplastics) is the opposite move from the vague "third-party tested" claim the rest of the category leans on.

## Category Tensions Identified

- **Capital is starting to follow the same proof discipline the claim-safe thesis has always demanded of copy.** General Catalyst didn't fund IM8's message — it funded 19 months of already-working unit economics, priced as revenue-share instead of equity precisely because the risk was already retired. It's the same proof-before-spend logic as Pillar 1, just showing up on the cap table instead of the launch budget.
