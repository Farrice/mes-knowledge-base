**1. Create Wellness — class-action alleges the label math is wrong (regulatory/legal). VERIFIED.**
Filed 2026-04-22 in NY federal court (plaintiff John Connolly), still active. Suit alleges Create's creatine gummies are labeled at 4.5g creatine/serving but independent testing found ~4.1g (~10% short); a separate 2024 third-party test on the 1.5g line found only ~80% of claimed content. Theory: creatine degrades by reacting with water inside the gummy matrix — a formulation flaw, not a one-off QC miss. $5M+ sought under NY GBL, breach of warranty, unjust enrichment.
*Why it's a purchase occasion:* Create Wellness is already Dream 100 #1 with a "teardown DM on the rebrand copy" plan on file. This changes the plan — the vulnerability isn't their positioning language, it's whether the number behind the positioning holds up under a lab test. That's a sharper, more urgent claim-safe conversation than a rebrand critique.
Sources: [Top Class Actions](https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/false-advertising/create-wellness-class-action-alleges-gummies-contain-less-creatine-than-advertised/) · [NutraIngredients, 2026-04-30](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/04/30/class-action-filed-against-create-wellness-for-creatine-gummies-claims/) · [Rob Freund (plaintiff's counsel) on X](https://x.com/RobertFreundLaw/status/2047077654898389298)
