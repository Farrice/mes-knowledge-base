## What Moved (This Run)

**1. TruHeight breaks silence on the FTC order — frames its claims-compliance rebuild as already done, not newly promised. VERIFIED.**
Following the FTC's finalized order (covered run #2, 2026-07-22), a TruHeight spokesperson told press: *"The FTC's final order reflects the settlement announced earlier this year, and our position remains unchanged. We cooperated fully with the FTC throughout the process, entered into a voluntary settlement, and are moving forward under the terms of the final order."* The spokesperson said the company began implementing a formal claims-review process with outside regulatory counsel, plus new testimonial/review procedures, roughly 18 months before the settlement was made public.
*Why it's a purchase occasion:* This directly resolves run #2's watchpoint ("do they address it or go silent"). They didn't go silent — they responded by dating their compliance fix to before the public order, which is itself a claim-safe positioning move worth scrutinizing on its own terms. Sharpens the existing Dream 100 #4/#7 (A-tier) angle: the DM hook is no longer just "the order landed," it's "you're already telling the market you fixed it — does the market believe you, and does the language hold up."
Sources: [Nutraceuticals World, 2026-07](https://www.nutraceuticalsworld.com/breaking-news/ftc-finalizes-order-against-truheight-for-deceptive-and-unsubstantiated-advertising/) · [FTC press release, 2026-07-22](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/07/ftc-approves-final-order-against-truheight-deceptive-unsubstantiated-advertising-supplements-kids) (prior run's primary source, for the order itself)

**2. SuanNutra to acquire IFF's specialty natural-ingredients businesses — a ~700-person consolidated supplier platform. VERIFIED. Not a Dream 100 fit — content fodder only.**
SuanNutra (Carbyne Equity Partners-backed, parent of Monteloeder) signed an agreement 2026-07-20 to acquire a portfolio of specialty natural-ingredients businesses from IFF (branded ingredients, botanical extracts, fermented vitamins/minerals, natural colors, antioxidants, flavors). Combined group: ~700 employees across Spain, Slovenia, Peru, and the US. Deal expected to close by end of 2026, subject to regulatory clearance.
*Why it's content, not a prospect:* SuanNutra is an ingredient supplier, not a consumer-facing brand — outside the offer's founder-led-brand target. But it's a sharp Pillar 4 (Sea of Same) data point at the supply layer: when a single consolidated supplier is behind the "science-backed natural" ingredient story for a widening roster of client brands, differentiation increasingly can't live in the ingredient claim — because more brands are now sourcing the identical substantiation. Worth naming as the upstream cause of a downstream problem (identical claims) rather than treating sameness as purely a copywriting failure.
Sources: [NutraIngredients, 2026-07-21](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/07/21/suannutra-to-acquire-iff-specialty-natural-ingredients/) · [PRNewswire, 2026-07-20](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/suannutra-a-carbyne-equity-partners-portfolio-company-expands-product-portfolio-with-specialty-natural-ingredients-businesses-from-iff-302829558.html)
