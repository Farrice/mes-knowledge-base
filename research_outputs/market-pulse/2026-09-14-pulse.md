The money moved in twelve months from Series A to a 3x-up Series B; nothing in the public record suggests the positioning work moved at the same speed.

The other (older, but the live counter-evidence) shows the brand consumers buy most isn't the brand the machines recommend most, and that gap tracks clinical/editorial credibility, not organic rank. Both can't be the operative truth for the same buyer at once — see AI-Search Signals below.

That's a sharper, more defensible version of the pillar's thesis than "AI doesn't know you exist" — most brands DO show up; the real gap is between showing up and being the answer.
