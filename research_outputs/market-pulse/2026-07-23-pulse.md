## What Moved (This Run)

**1. TruHeight — FTC finalizes $4M judgment (suspended to $750K) the same week it expands into Nordstrom + JCPenney. VERIFIED.**
FTC approved its final order 2026-07-22 against Vanilla Chip LLC (dba TruHeight) and principals Eden Stelmach and Justin Rapoport: $4M judgment, partially suspended to $750K on inability-to-pay grounds, barring false/unsubstantiated health claims and fake or incentivized reviews. The underlying complaint (filed April 2026) alleged TruHeight's height-growth claims for kids/teens lacked competent and reliable scientific evidence, and that "5-star" reviews came from employees, vendors, and consumers paid in free product — plus bot-run fake social profiles posting automated comments on Facebook/Instagram.
One day earlier (2026-07-21), TruHeight announced it's joining the Nordstrom Marketplace and JCPenney Marketplace, layering onto its June debut in 5,000 CVS stores and existing Target/Amazon/iHerb presence.
*Why it's a purchase occasion:* TruHeight is Dream 100 #4/#7 (A-tier, "claim-safe angle for skeptical parent on shelf"). The order just turned that angle from a hypothetical parent-trust pitch into a live, dated, resolved enforcement action — landing the exact week they're adding two more retail surfaces. This is sharper and more current than the CVS-debut framing already on file.
Sources: [FTC press release, 2026-07-22](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/07/ftc-approves-final-order-against-truheight-deceptive-unsubstantiated-advertising-supplements-kids) · [NutraIngredients, 2026-07-22](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/07/22/ftc-finalizes-truheight-order-over-supplement-growth-claims/) · [FTC original complaint, 2026-04](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/04/ftc-takes-action-against-truheight-deceptive-unsubstantiated-advertising-supposed-height-enhancing) · [PRNewswire, 2026-07-21 — Nordstrom/JCPenney](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/truheight-joins-nordstrom-and-jcpenney-marketplaces-as-wellness-brands-reshape-the-department-store-302830436.html)

**3. IM8 (Prenetics) — $1B growth financing tied to marketing performance, not equity. VERIFIED. Not a Dream 100 fit — content fodder only.**
Prenetics' IM8 (David Beckham's supplement brand) closed $1B in growth financing from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund (announced 2026-07-14). Structure: GC finances up to 70% of IM8's marketing spend and takes a capped share of income from the customer cohorts it funds — no equity, warrants, or convertible instruments — and once GC recovers its investment plus capped return per monthly cohort, all further value reverts to IM8. Prenetics lifted 2026 revenue guidance for IM8 to $210–220M. Separately, IM8 runs "an AI-native engine" continuously testing thousands of automated ads across 43 countries.
*Why it's content, not a prospect:* IM8 is celebrity-funded and post-PMF at nine figures — outside the founder-personal-check-writer band the offer targets. But the financing structure is a sharp counter-example for Pillar 1: capital here follows *proven* message-to-customer economics, inverting the usual "spend on the launch before the message is settled" failure mode. The AI-native ad-testing detail is a Pillar 5 data point — the machine isn't just reading brand copy anymore, it's authoring and iterating it at scale.

**4. AI-search commerce — ChatGPT Merchant Program formalizes the machine-legibility ask. VERIFIED (program) / LIKELY (conversion stat). Standing trend, not a single dated event.**
OpenAI's ChatGPT Merchant Program is live, letting brands submit product feeds directly so ChatGPT can access structured product data; ecommerce guidance converging on three requirements: AI crawler access (allow OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt), complete JSON-LD Product schema server-side rendered, and 95%+ attribute completion in the underlying product feed.
The widely-repeated "AI-referred shoppers convert up to 23x higher than organic" figure traces to an Ahrefs analysis (0.5% of traffic driving 12.1% of signups on the site Ahrefs studied) — it is being cited everywhere without a linkable original report, so it's carried here as **LIKELY**, not VERIFIED. More conservatively sourced figures in the same window: Shopify's 2026 merchant data shows AI-referred conversion beating organic SEO in 23 of 25 categories (avg. +56%, 14% higher AOV); Adobe's analysis of 1T+ US retail visits found AI-driven traffic converting 42% better in March 2026.
*Why it matters to Pillar 5:* the "Machine Reading Label" pillar now has a concrete, testable checklist (crawler access, JSON-LD schema, feed completeness) instead of just a vibe — useful for AI-Search Shadow module framing.
