## Category Tensions Identified

- **"Healthspan" is becoming the new "clinically dosed" — a word the whole category is racing to say at once.** The NutraIngredients trend piece documents a wholesale vocabulary shift, not one brand's positioning choice. When an entire category adopts the same replacement word inside the same quarter, the word itself stops being differentiation almost as soon as it lands — the same sea-of-same risk "science-backed" already ran into.
- **Ownership structure is being tested as a standalone differentiator.** NORTH Performance is betting that "co-owners, not ambassadors" carries positioning weight independent of formula specifics. That's a genuinely new mechanic worth watching — if it converts skeptical buyers, it's a reusable proof pattern; if buyers shrug, it confirms that structure alone doesn't survive contact with "does it work."
- **Comparative claims ("superior absorption," "better than X") are drawing a different kind of legal scrutiny than dosage claims** — they invite a literal head-to-head test rather than a label-vs-assay comparison. Brands using comparative language should treat it as a materially higher-proof-burden claim type, not a marketing flourish.
