## Category Tensions Identified

- **"Healthspan" just became a shared vocabulary problem, not a differentiator.** When trade press names the eight-ingredient stack as the category default, every brand entering that language starts from the same sentence. The brand that earns each ingredient's presence on the label — rather than borrowing credibility from ingredient count — is the one a skeptical buyer (or a compliance reviewer) can actually trust.
- **Flavor-line expansion is the easiest growth lever to fund and the easiest one to mistake for positioning progress.** Create Wellness adding three flavors answers "how do we keep buyers subscribed," not "does 'creatine is for everyone' land with someone who's never lifted." Distribution and SKU count move faster than message-testing, same tension flagged for Cure Hydration last run.
