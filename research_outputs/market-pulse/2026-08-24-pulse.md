## Category Tensions Identified

- **The GLP-1-companion category is filling shelf space faster than it's filling vocabulary.** A pharmacy counter display next to GLP-1 prescriptions, aimed at muscle preservation, is a genuinely new occasion — there's no settled script yet for what a pharmacist or a pharmacy customer expects that message to say. Whoever writes the first sentence that lands owns the frame other entrants will react to.
- **Distribution scale is outrunning message-testing cadence.** Cure went from 1,227 Target doors to 4,500 Walmart stores in roughly ten weeks. That's proof of demand, not proof the positioning at 1,227 doors survives 27,000 — the same message now reaches an order of magnitude more first-time buyers before anyone's re-tested whether it converts a colder audience.
- **A four-product-line class action reframes "clinically dosed" as a company-wide posture question, not a single-SKU risk.** Qunol's suit doesn't allege one formulation error — it alleges the same gap across omega-3, CoQ10, magnesium, and turmeric. That pattern is the sharper story: a labeling instinct across a whole portfolio, not a manufacturing accident on one line.
