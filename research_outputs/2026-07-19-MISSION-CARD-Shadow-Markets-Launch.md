## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

✓ **Speed Over Polish**: First version (landing page + ads) built in <8 hours per opportunity. V1 ugly, V2 refined based on sales signals.

✓ **Validation Before Scaling**: $50-100 ad spend per opportunity first, before committing $300+/day.

✓ **Hook Variation Buffer**: All 5 variations posted in parallel (Days 3 & 13). At least 1 will outperform by 3-10x (statistical inevitability).

✓ **CAC-Driven Pricing**: Every pricing decision tied to customer acquisition cost, not "feeling." If CAC > price, pivot immediately.

✓ **Email as Primary Lever**: All conversions driven through lead magnet → email capture → sales email. Not cold traffic directly to checkout.

✓ **Day 5-7 First Sale Target**: If no sale by Day 7 in Wave 1, market signal is weak—pivot to Wave 1 support opportunity immediately.

## RISK MITIGATION

| Risk | Signal | Response |
|------|--------|----------|
| **CAC > Price** | Conversion rate drops below 1% after Day 3 | Stop campaign, pivot to alternative market |
| **Market Saturation** | 500+ ad accounts found in market | Deprioritize; move to Wave 3 test (2-3 day test max) |
| **Weak Lead Magnet** | Email capture rate <5% | Redesign within 24 hours; relaunch with new angle |
| **Wrong Audience Targeting** | High ad spend, zero sales by Day 5 | Shift audience segment (age, income, interests); retest |
| **Weak Hook** | Completion rate <30% at 3-second mark | Use backup hook immediately; don't wait for stats |
