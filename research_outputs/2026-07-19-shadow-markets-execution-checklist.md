- [ ] Upsell page live ($197-$297 backend offer)

- [ ] Create Ad Account + Payment Method ($289 budget)

- [ ] 2-3 ad creatives designed (lead magnet emphasis)
- [ ] Campaign structure: Lead Magnet → Core Product → Upsell

- [ ] $289 split: $150 lead magnet ads (drive email list), $100 core product ads, $39 reserve
- [ ] Target: Break even on lead magnet (email cost offset by sales from list)

## RISK MITIGATION

### If CAC Exceeds Estimate
- [ ] Pivot ad creative (different angle, audience)
- [ ] Optimize landing page (lower friction, stronger headline)
- [ ] Shift to organic acquisition (Reddit, YouTube, email outreach)
- [ ] Accept higher CAC if LTV can scale via upsells

### If Conversion Rate Misses
- [ ] Product feedback: is pain statement true? Does product solve it?
- [ ] Pricing test: lower to $67 or $49 (test elasticity)
- [ ] Lead quality: refine audience targeting (tighter ICP)
- [ ] Copy test: reframe value proposition

### If First Sale Delays Beyond Day 10
- [ ] Email sequence refresh (stronger urgency, proof, bonuses)
- [ ] Ads pause: budget analysis, creative refresh
- [ ] Pivot to adjacent market (Wave 2) to validate niche
- [ ] Phone outreach: 5-10 cold calls to ideal customers for feedback
