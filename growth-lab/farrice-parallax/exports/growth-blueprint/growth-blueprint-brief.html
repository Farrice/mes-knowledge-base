chapter 1 · who we're for

The Formulator-Founder: 33-50, credentialed face of an already-selling supplement or performance brand under $15M, near-zero time, spending his own runway against a dated occasion inside 60-90 days. His midnight fear - the dossier holds it verbatim - is being the most knowledgeable person in the category while a louder half-expert becomes the name. Content that flatters visibility loses him. Content that arms credibility recruits him.

The discovery that shapes everything: across seven forum threads where this buyer talks, every reply sold a different mechanism - quiz funnels, CAPI hygiene, agency accounts, AI media buyers. Not one told the founder to decide what the brand proves and to whom. The lane has no competition inside the buyer's own feed.

chapters 4-5 · what we make, and how

Three buckets with named mechanisms; three vehicles - two proven pairs and one labeled bet.

01

The Claim Ladder: compression promise x talking-head + receipts

Proven pair (14.1x specimen); his payloads are enumerable; 60-90 minutes per rep, voice-memo-first because speech is the ore

02

The Regulatory News-Jack: shift analysis on the FTC/FDA beat

Proven skeleton (64.2x ceiling, person-effect flagged), unclaimed beat; Monday 20-minute sweep, 24-48 hour turnaround

03

The Counter Consult: self-witness with the claim ladder as paper prop - THE bet

Nobody in-pool runs self-witness monologue; one bet allowed, fallback written: guest the stories to the operator roundtables

04

Batch plan: 7/week in 3-2-1 shape, first kill/keep at batch 3, hero crowning at batch 3, scan refresh at day 45

No verdict inside 24 hours; no demotion before batches 2-3; the what-counts-as-working thresholds decide in dollars and counts
