define, hit, expand

THE AIM

The center is one person: the founder-formulator of an already-selling supplement or performance brand under $15M with a dated launch occasion inside 60-90 days, who keeps buying mechanisms to avoid an unmade message decision. Each ring outward drops exactly one named filter - the launch clock, then the category, then the regulated-claims constraint, then everything. Rings are auditable decisions, not vibes: you can argue about which filter comes off first. The batch shape is 3-2-1: two narrow buckets at Ring 2 (trust and conversion), one broad at Ring 4 (reach, fenced), one chaos dart. Ring 5 gets a warning band, not a bucket.

05

the two-zone rule

SOURCING DOCTRINE

Topics stay on the ring the bucket sits on: The Defensible Claim and The Counter Files source Rings 1-3 - the buyer's own words are the deepest well - and The Fix Aisle sources Ring 4, then re-aims every piece at the operating supplement founder by name in line one. Craft - formats, hooks, storytelling, editing, pacing - comes from anywhere on the board, Ring 5 and other niches included: borrow the compression promise (14.1x), the news-jack timing (64.2x), the interview rhythm (15.9x). Ring 5 is craft-only; sourcing topics there is how a claim-safe strategist's channel drifts into being another marketing channel. And an empty ring is not a problem - it is your opening: pull from the adjacent ring and re-aim.
