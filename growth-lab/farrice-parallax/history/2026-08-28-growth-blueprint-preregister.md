## 1 · Who we're for

The Formulator-Founder: 33–50, credentialed face of an already-selling supplement or performance brand under $15M, total decision authority, near-zero time ("he'd rather be in the lab or the gym than on LinkedIn"), spending his own runway against a dated occasion inside 60–90 days. His midnight fear: "I'm the most knowledgeable person in this category and I'm invisible — and some kid with half my expertise and twice the posting cadence is becoming the name people associate with this." His identity: "I'm the real one. I earned this the hard way." Visibility feels like joining the influencers he built the brand against; content that flatters visibility loses him, content that arms credibility recruits him. Secondary buyer: the Head of Brand who "carries the number… and is the one who will get blamed."

Identity layer in one pass: he believes the copy problem is a writing/volume problem (mis-diagnosed as AEO); not-buying protects "I'm the real one"; admitting the problem costs him the confession that a lesser expert is winning the name. The villain is the sameness machine and the lie-friendly platform, never the buyer.
