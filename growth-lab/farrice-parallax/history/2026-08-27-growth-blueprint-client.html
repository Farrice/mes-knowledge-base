chapter 1 · who we're for

The Formulator-Founder: 33-50, credentialed face of an already-selling supplement or performance brand under $15M, near-zero time, spending his own runway against a dated occasion inside 60-90 days. His midnight fear, from the research: I'm the most knowledgeable person in this category and I'm invisible - and some kid with half my expertise and twice the posting cadence is becoming the name people associate with this. Content that flatters visibility loses him. Content that arms credibility recruits him.

The mining discovery that shapes everything: across seven forum threads where this buyer talks, every reply sold a different mechanism - quiz funnels, CAPI hygiene, agency accounts, AI media buyers. Not one told the founder to decide what the brand proves and to whom. The lane has no competition inside the buyer's own feed.
