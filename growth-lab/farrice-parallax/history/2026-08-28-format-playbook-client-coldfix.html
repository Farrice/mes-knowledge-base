THE ANTI-CONFOUNDING LAYER

A format is two things layered: the storytelling structure (how the spoken words are organized) and the visual layout (how it is shot). Every in-pool multiple bundles channel effect, topic effect, and guest effect - Sabri's 14.1x rides a 504K channel, foundr's 15.9x rides a half-billion-dollar guest, and the 64.2x ceiling is one channel's nineteen-year franchise. So every structure carries a mechanism card: the named psychological reason it holds attention, how it dies in unskilled hands, and what must be true for it to survive the jump to this operator's topics and his 1-3 hour naptime constraint. Format hunting is finite: test three, crown one or two heroes by batch three, then reps.
