## Storytelling structures (four-part named: plain name · one-liner · 12-second example · linked specimen)

### S1 · The Compression Promise
A time-boxed teaching promise with audacious specific numbers. *12-second example:* "Give me four minutes and you'll know the six claims your supplement brand can legally make. Number one costs most brands $53,088 to learn." Beats: (1) numbered promise → (2) credential flash → (3) numbered steps, each opening the next loop → (4) recap + CTA. Carries: education topics (buckets D/F; his Claim School). In-pool: 5 rows, median 10.8×, max 14.1×. Specimens: [27 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HsjuAvqs3zU) 201K · 14.1× · 2026-07-31 · [97% of Sales](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rsgvkkpNg7Y) 43K · 10.8× · 2026-08-22. VERIFIED.

### S2 · The Shift Analysis (news-jack)
An event decoded into "what this means for you." *12-second example:* "Last week the FTC sent 14 warning letters to supplement brands. One sentence in letter #6 should change your next launch." Beats: (1) event with a hard number → (2) why the power is scared / what actually changed → (3) the translation to YOU → (4) the move. Carries: buckets A/B; the Fix Aisle IS this skeleton pointed at mechanisms. In-pool: 6 rows, median about 13.7×, ceiling 64.2×. Specimens: [Amazon control-loss](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CpIZPIbU0cU) 1.4M · 64.2× · 2026-04-28 (transcript-verified beat order) · [Claude × Meta ads](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ReYEYoLsppk) 41K · 21.8× · 2026-08-09. VERIFIED; person-effect on the ceiling flagged in the scan.

### S3 · The Witness Interview
An outcome cold-open, then the scar that built it. *12-second example:* "You exited for half a billion dollars… When that happened, all this hustle, blood, sweat, tears." Beats: (1) outcome cold open → (2) origin scar → (3) contrarian operating beliefs → (4) the lesson. Carries: bucket C; proof stories. In-pool: 7 rows, median 5.7×, max 15.9×. Specimens: [Quad Lock exit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CERZXt6lpxk) 39K · 15.9× · 2026-07-30 (transcript) · [Viral Cookie](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nowcsh1wDzE) 398K · 9.7× · 2026-07-28. VERIFIED; foundr guest-effect confound printed below.

### S4 · The Template Reveal
The exact artifact behind a result, walked through. *12-second example:* "This is the claim-safety sheet we run before any supplement ad ships. Three columns. The middle one saves accounts." Beats: (1) "the exact template we use" → (2) annotated walkthrough → (3) the mistakes most make → (4) where to get it. Carries: bucket F; feeds the lead magnet. In-pool: 4 rows, median ~6.8×, max 19.9×. Specimens: [Product-page blueprint](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8eHZqKtnymY) 12K · 19.9× · 2025-01-10 (transcript) · [Ecom offer survey](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RHZ39WfEuM0) 5.4K · 6.8× · 2026-08-22. VERIFIED.

### S5 · The Roundtable Segment
Named operators disagreeing usefully. *12-second example:* "Your CMO says ROAS is up. Haus's data says half of it never happened. Which do you believe on budget day?" Beats: (1) guest + sharp question → (2) war story with numbers → (3) cross-talk disagreement → (4) operational takeaway. Carries: bucket D. In-pool: 7 rows, median 6.4×, max 14.2×. Specimens: [Scale CPG](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6psqtXu4u88) 9.9K · 14.2× · 2026-08-13 · [Haus incrementality](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jl5jCVogRcY) 4.8K · 3.6× · 2026-08-15. VERIFIED. ### Pick 3 · The Counter Consult (S3/S4 hybrid × L1 paper-prop) : THE whitespace bet, labeled

**Hero-format rule, stated plainly:** after 2–3 batches the calibration tracker crowns 1–2 heroes on "working =" numbers; testing ends, reps begin. **Honest ceiling:** recording and delivery cannot be automated by any of this.
