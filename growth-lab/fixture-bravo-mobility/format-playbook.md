# Format Playbook — Fixture Bravo Mobility

## Starting formats

| Format | Source receipt | Use | Human-owned input |
|---|---|---|---|
| Replacement claim | 6.4× direct mobility signal | Demonstrate a bounded four-minute alternative to the abandoned long routine | Exact movements, safety limits, and who should not use it |
| Receipt confession | 9.0× format-only signal | Turn 11 years of practice into one observed pattern | The real pattern, case boundary, and permission-safe story |
| Meta-test | 3.7× format-only signal | Compare conflicting mobility rules without false certainty | The standard selected, evidence basis, and qualified verdict |

## Production constraint

Three pieces per week: two direct-to-buyer shorts and one authority explanation. Keep production founder-shot. Use simple demonstrations or a single paper prop only when they improve comprehension.

## Failure rules

- Never convert “11 years” into a clinical outcome claim.
- Never prescribe an exercise before the founder supplies contraindications.
- Never turn a format transfer into proof that the topic will work.
- Never publish an AI-written opinion as the founder's own.
