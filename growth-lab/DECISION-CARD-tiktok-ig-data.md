# Decision card — TikTok/IG data lane (5-minute decision, pre-researched 2026-08-27)

**Trigger:** a client engagement (or your own run) where short-form IG/TikTok performance data is load-bearing for the deliverable — i.e., the buyer's content ecosystem lives there and YouTube evidence alone reads thin.

**Option A — Owned metrics (DEFAULT, $0, best evidence).** Ask the client for their own analytics exports: IG Professional Dashboard → export, TikTok Creator/Business analytics → CSV. Feed via the radar's `manual_csv`/`owned_metrics` lane → same signal pack, `coverage: measured (owned)`. Their real numbers beat any scraper's estimates, and competitors' public winners can still be referenced by URL as specimens without metrics claims.

**Option B — Sandcastles Pro ($39/mo annual · $49 monthly, 7-day free trial first).** Their MCP becomes a drop-in data lane behind our existing pack contract (`source_lane: sandcastles_mcp` — socket already built, no skill changes). Buys: cross-platform outlier database + competitor coverage on IG/TikTok. Cancel anytime. Known caveats from reviews: 100 credits/mo runs out fast; $39 requires annual billing.

**Option C — decline the scope honestly.** The artifact states YouTube-measured + interview-validated, IG/TikTok directional only. Acceptable for positioning/strategy work; not for a performance audit.

**Rule of thumb:** A for client work (always ask first — it's also a trust move), B only if a specific engagement needs *competitor* IG/TikTok metrics A can't provide and the fee covers 3+ months of it, C when the deliverable is strategy, not measurement.
