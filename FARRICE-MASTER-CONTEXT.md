## §2 — THE STORY (public-safe arc)

Chicago → southern Illinois in the formative years: displacement, zero social network, a biracial kid rebuilding identity from scratch between two cultures that didn't fully accept him. In high school — alternative punk and emo taste in a predominantly Black school — severely bullied for being different, and thrown into lockers for being faster than the fastest kid on the track team. Severe depression and isolation; sought relief in religion and didn't find it; survived suicidal ideation. **That crisis became the catalyst for the entire journey into transformation** (his framing, Depth 10/10 in his interest excavation).

The body was the first thing he learned to build; everything else followed. Depression pushed him to the weight room; getting stronger became the first act of self-authorship, then a career. *"Best trainer at every gym for getting actual results — not just sales. Great at results, struggled with sales and putting myself out there due to fear of rejection."* That sentence is the whole tension of his professional life.

Then: COVID-era self-taught design → MyBPM streetwear with Jennifer → prompt engineering → AI systems architecture at a level he correctly identifies as rare. Then fatherhood, which turned presence into a discipline. Then writing — Parallax, his Substack, the first place he felt *"truly expressive of the deep, nuanced insights and things going on in my head"*; reading his own editions back *"energized me and got me moving and feeling alive again, which I have not felt for a long time."*

The arc in one line: **a man who spent his life at 90% learning, in public, to ship the last 10%.**

## §6 — VOICE LAW

**Depth lives in `_active/farrice-brand/voice/VOICE-CARD.md` (v1.2, canonical) — load it for stylometrics, registers, and the MIRROR/BLEND/STRETCH/OFF dial.** What follows is the law and the calibration bank, which live here because nothing else holds them.

**The one test:** *would Farrice say this to a smart friend over coffee?*

**Registers** — warm, direct, coaching-across-the-table; quiet defiance; emotionally precise without sentimentality; confident not prescriptive; imperfect on purpose.

**Binding rules (from his own briefs):**
- **Spoken, not writerly.** *"If I wouldn't say it to a friend over coffee, it's wrong."* Writing "canister" instead of "supplement" is **the class of error that killed four drafts.**
- **Not every sentence is a gem.** Flat sentences doing ordinary work are required. **Uniform polish reads as AI.**
- **Pick a spine.** *Confession-I* when his story is the proof. *Recognition-you* (Bayer Mirror) when a real thesis sits under the piece — one seven-word first-person receipt after the cold open, nothing else. ***Dictation-you is banned.***
- **Latin runway, Saxon landing.** Build the line backwards from a short hard last word. One device per line, never stacked.
- **Banned as physical law:** "Here's what/why/how" openers · "It's not X. It's Y." · twin-sentence endings · triple anaphora · more than 2 em dashes per piece · cheap question closes · status-words ("thought leader," "passionate about") · forced jargon · performance of any kind. **The wince test is final.**

**Content architecture — Wide → Deep → Bridge → Home.** Open on a truth any operator recognizes. Prove it with supplement specifics nobody else can claim. One paragraph bridging past supplements (software, services, devices — anywhere the honest claim needs a paragraph and the lie fits on a banner). Land on the offer. **Opening on supplements loses every non-supplement buyer in line one.**

**Taste signature:** bimodal grader — clear PASS or clear FAIL, narrow marginal band. 7.0–8.0 "almost there" work is actively suspicious to him. Structure without heartbeat caps at 5/10.

**The rule:** if a draft line reads like the *failed* column — abstract, polished, impressive-sounding, or credentialing — it is wrong, no matter how true the claim.
