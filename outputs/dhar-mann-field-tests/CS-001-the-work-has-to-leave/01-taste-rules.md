# Audience Contact Series Taste OS

## Purpose

Use these rules to keep a Farrice transformation series human, source-grounded, cumulative, and useful. They apply at four points: plan, draft, review, and one-variable repair.

FT-001 remains a provisional seed. Its local checks passed; Farrice’s felt verdict and audience evidence remain pending.

## Layer 1: Hard Vetoes

Any one failure stops publication review.

1. **Provenance and privacy**
   - No invented lived scene, private feeling, response, or result.
   - Family autobiography stays private unless Farrice explicitly clears it.
   - Missing source permits strategy or outline only, not a testimony body.

2. **Claim safety**
   - Do not compare content to clinical care, promise outcomes, or ask for unsafe or coercive action.

3. **Mission repayment**
   - Any tension or emotional stakes borrowed by the package creates a debt. The body owes the reader recognition, a clearer frame, repair, or agency in return.
   - Analytics cannot override the human mission.

4. **One composition spine**
   - Choose recognition-you or testimony-I.
   - Choose narrative or educational.
   - Name one body author.
   - Do not blend modes because several experts are available.

5. **Metric separation**
   - Discovery, retention, connection, and transformation remain distinct.
   - Views never prove meaning, trust, or behavioral change.

6. **Backstage stays backstage**
   - HEART, expert names, prompt mechanics, and internal stitching do not become public jargon.
   - Use the expert’s thinking, not an imitation of the expert.

7. **Farrice hard floor**
   - Cut the canned “Here’s…” setup.
   - Cut the X/Y negation-reveal template.
   - Cut cheap question closes.
   - Refuse dictation-you.
   - Remove forced jargon, scripted charisma, symmetrical triple beats, and performance-polish.
   - Open LinkedIn pieces with a statement or scene, never a question.

8. **Proof state stays honest**
   - Before USE, publication, and audience receipts, call the work prospective.
   - A clean local score is not a winner verdict.

## Layer 2: Taste Multipliers

These are not quotas. Use judgment and keep only the moves that make the specific piece stronger.

1. **Open from the selected spine**
   - Confession or testimony starts inside a real, concrete moment.
   - Recognition-you starts inside the reader’s precise private situation and uses one seven-word first-person receipt directly after the cold open.

2. **Recognition before instruction**
   - Name the private sentence before the lesson.
   - Mirror the pattern; do not lecture the reader about it.

3. **Embody before explaining**
   - Show a consequential choice, behavior, object, or proof gap that allows the shift to be inferred.

4. **Lived specificity over elevated language**
   - Prefer real counts, places, objects, and body verbs.
   - Let one clear image carry the idea instead of stacking metaphors.

5. **Reader contract**
   - Every personal scene turns toward a useful reader move.
   - The story earns attention by helping the reader locate themselves.

6. **Uneven human rhythm**
   - Vary paragraph and sentence length.
   - Allow plain connective sentences.
   - Flat lines can carry texture; they cannot carry the emotional payoff.

7. **Hard landing**
   - Let the runway breathe, then land in short, plain words.
   - Close on an image, decision, declaration, or earned bookend.

8. **After-state before format**
   - Decide what the reader should feel, understand, or do before choosing text, video, carousel, or another surface.

## Layer 3: Series Continuity Rules

1. **One dramatic question**
   - A series needs a changing question, not a repeated topic.

2. **Design the climax first**
   - Name the final viewer transformation and integrated asset before planning the opening installment.

3. **Every installment has two jobs**
   - Deliver one named, saveable asset noun.
   - Advance the dramatic question.

4. **Standalone and cumulative**
   - A first-time reader gets a complete payoff.
   - A returning reader sees prior assets become more useful.

5. **Keep one substantive mystery active**
   - The next question must be real and later repaid.
   - “More soon” is not an open loop.

6. **Act II must destabilize**
   - The middle must challenge the founding assumption, not decorate it with more tips.

7. **Preserve the transformation invariant**
   - Preserve this order: honest promise, recognition, consequential choice or proof gap, interpretation shift, safe action, mission-safe feedback.

8. **Audience gets a vote, not the pen**
   - Ask only about a decision the response can change.
   - Change one primary variable per iteration.
   - Preserve creator judgment and accepted voice decisions.

9. **Resolve and compound**
   - The final asset integrates prior assets and resolves the main question.
   - The next-series question opens only after the current promise is repaid.

## The Field Taste Gate

### Before drafting

- Is the source real and usable?
- Can the private sentence be stated without guessing?
- Is exactly one body mode and one change route selected?
- Name the different decision this installment helps the reader make.
- Name the asset.
- Which mystery does it open, deepen, or resolve?
- State the evidence that would hold the body or change one variable.

### After drafting

- Did the package borrow more tension than the body repaid?
- Does the reader feel recognized before being taught?
- Can the exact source detail be removed without weakening the piece? If yes, the piece may be generic.
- Is the Field Card usable without the rest of the series?
- Does the callback add meaning rather than require homework?
- Is any sentence performing intelligence instead of doing work?
- Does the last line land, or merely ask for engagement?

### Verdict

- **KEEP:** No hard vetoes; the piece makes one distinct decision useful; source, recognition, shift, and action remain intact.
- **Change one variable:** The premise holds, but one scoreboard or taste dimension is weak.
- **HOLD:** Evidence is too small or conflicting.
- **REJECT:** Voice, source, mission, or premise fails.

## Revision Protocol

1. Freeze the accepted primary before editing.
2. Name one observed failure class.
3. Change one primary variable.
4. Rerun the same checks.
5. Compare the new version against the frozen primary.
6. Promote or reject the branch explicitly.

Do not blend two variants into a compromise. A revision must earn its promotion.

## Design Hypotheses, Not Source Laws

Keep these visibly provisional:

- The exact seven-part count.
- Exact installment titles.
- Any fixed cadence.
- The mapping of text to interiority, video to emotion, or carousel to tools.
- Mandatory format rotation.
- Exact Field Card names.
- A reader-turn percentage.
- A quota for new verbs, metaphors, or flat sentences.
- FT-001’s local threshold of three recognition signals plus one reported action in seven days.

The underlying principles are source-earned. These implementations must survive Farrice’s felt verdict and live audience evidence.

## FT-001 Preservation Lock

If FT-001 receives a USE verdict, preserve:

- “Nine workflows. Ten prompts. Nothing from the factory had met an audience.”
- “I built all nine before this story.”
- “One more pass. Then I’ll publish.”
- The distinction between local operation and audience meaning.
- “The 11th prompt can wait.”

Do not turn those five moves into a formula for the remaining six parts. The series should keep the factory-door motif while changing the object, decision, and rhythm.
