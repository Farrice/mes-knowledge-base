# Reality Before Rhetoric

## A Co-Creative Depth Protocol for Farrice + Codex

## The operating insight

The missing ingredient sits upstream of writing technique: enough consequential reality for the writing to answer to.

AI slop often begins before the prose. A model finds a missing bridge between what happened and what it supposedly meant, then completes that bridge with the most plausible explanation. The result is coherent, polished, and emotionally counterfeit.

The correction is simple but demanding:

> A missing bridge becomes a question, a research task, or a smaller claim. Never a prettier sentence.

We cannot manufacture lived truth. We can manufacture the conditions that recover it, distinguish it from interpretation, and use only what it earns.

## What becomes the universal floor

Personal story is only one route. The common floor is:

`particular -> pressure or contradiction -> decision or update -> observable consequence -> bounded meaning`

The nouns change by artifact.

| Artifact | Load-bearing particular | Hinge | Honest consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal content | A real behavior, phrase, artifact, setting, or choice | Exact trigger, accumulated change, or first observable counterchoice | What changed in behavior, interpretation, or willingness |
| Offer or copy | A buyer sentence, hesitation, failed alternative, objection, or proof object | What evidence changes the buyer's choice | Action, inaction, doubt resolved, or an explicitly untested hypothesis |
| Strategy | A real constraint, dependency, resource limit, or baseline | The tradeoff: what is chosen and rejected under current conditions | Milestone, falsifier, or accepted cost |
| Research | A traceable passage, observation, dataset, method detail, or contradiction | The evidence update that changes confidence or rules out an explanation | Narrower claim, changed confidence, or unresolved uncertainty |
| Operating or system work | A reproducible trace, routing receipt, state transition, or observed failure | The scoped intervention or control decision | Verified behavior delta or an honest structural-only diagnosis |

Story is therefore one expression of depth, not the universal requirement. A strategy's story is a tradeoff. Research's transformation is a confidence update. A system's hinge is an observed state change.

## The three source modes

Depth does not require confession.

- `LIVED`: Farrice's real experience.
- `OBSERVED`: a witnessed audience, customer, team, category, or market pattern.
- `ARTIFACT`: an inspectable draft, message, decision, object, result, transcript, trace, or proof trail.

Choose the least invasive mode that can honestly earn the intended claim or after-state.

## The Co-Creative Depth Loop

### 1. Retrieve before asking

Codex searches the current workspace, approved memory, prior artifacts, sources, receipts, and voice material first.

It asks Farrice only for what lives in his head or requires his ownership:

- lived truth;
- private context;
- felt meaning;
- taste;
- permission to use sensitive material.

### 2. Name what the material must earn

Before gathering details, state the exact belief, decision, claim, or state change the artifact is trying to create.

This prevents interesting autobiography or research from becoming decorative.

Do not invent that objective from a convenient thesis. If the intended change is absent from the brief and source, mark it missing and ask or hold.

### 3. Diagnose the missing link

Identify the earliest missing element:

- load-bearing particular;
- pressure, contradiction, or constraint;
- protective behavior or current decision;
- observable cost;
- causal hinge or evidence update;
- first counterchoice or changed behavior;
- aftermath or verifier;
- reader/user transfer.

### 4. Interview adaptively

Ask one non-leading retrieval question at a time. Use no more than five unless Farrice explicitly continues.

Follow the strongest concrete detail, contradiction, energy change, or causal gap. Do not run a questionnaire and do not steer Farrice toward the thesis Codex already wants.

Useful question families:

1. “Take me to the last ordinary moment when this was still true. Where were you, and what were you actually doing?”
2. “In that moment, what were you trying to get, avoid, or protect?”
3. “Walk me through what you did next, before you knew what it meant.”
4. “Was there one trigger, or did the change accumulate?”
5. “Name the first choice you made differently.”
6. “Afterward, was anything different that could be seen, saved, measured, or checked?”
7. “Which part of this is memory, which part is your current interpretation, and which part are you unsure about?”

Only ask the question that closes the highest-value gap next.

### 5. Mirror and certify the source

Return a compact Source Depth Packet for correction before drafting:

```text
Source mode:
Claim or after-state this must earn:
Claim class, when relevant:
Provenance and use permission:
Load-bearing particular:
Before-state and pressure:
Want or protective choice:
Observable cost:
Hinge: exact / accumulated / missing
First counterchoice or try:
Aftermath and proof limit:
Verbatim language:
Interpretations:
Inferences:
Privacy vetoes:
Draft ceiling:
Source status: source-ready / source-limited / source-missing / permission-blocked
Authorized artifact tier: draft / acquire-lived-source / acquire-external-evidence / diagnostic-only / hold
Next acquisition action:
```

An `accumulated` hinge is valid. Real life does not owe us a cinematic epiphany. Use the first observable counterchoice instead of inventing a revelation.

### 6. Choose the honest artifact tier

Source status and artifact authority are separate decisions. A rich founder history, for example, does not become buyer-outcome proof. Complete system files do not prove the behavior fired.

Use one source status:

- `source-ready`: the required source is present and attributable.
- `source-limited`: the source supports a narrower claim or form.
- `source-missing`: a load-bearing source element is absent.
- `permission-blocked`: the material exists but its use is not authorized.

Then choose one authorized artifact tier:

- `draft`: enough reality exists for the intended claim and artifact.
- `acquire-lived-source`: ask Farrice for the private or experiential input that only he can supply.
- `acquire-external-evidence`: retrieve or research the missing public fact, buyer evidence, source, or runtime trace.
- `diagnostic-only`: the material supports an observation or prospective recommendation but not the intended transformation, outcome, or readiness claim.
- `hold`: privacy, provenance, proof, or decision authority prevents responsible continuation.

When the source is thin, lower the claim or change the form. Do not fabricate the source.

After five questions, a refusal, an unavailable source, or a privacy veto, stop acquisition. Lower the claim, select a smaller form, or hold. Never keep interviewing until Farrice gives the story the model wants.

### Claim classes that prevent proof laundering

For offer and copy work, label the intended claim before drafting:

- `process-or-mechanism`;
- `testable-promise-or-hypothesis`;
- `buyer-language-observation`;
- `demonstrated-outcome`;
- `case-study-transformation`.

Untested offers may use the first three when the language stays honest. The last two require real outcome evidence.

For system work, label the proof state:

- `structural-only`;
- `runtime-observed`;
- `behavior-delta-verified`.

Structural-only evidence may authorize diagnosis or a prospective patch. It may not authorize “working,” “fixed,” “ready,” or equivalent behavior claims.

### 7. Compose once, then test reality again

One owner writes the artifact. Downstream craft improves architecture, language, rhythm, platform fit, and reader pull. It may not create facts, scenes, stakes, or causal bridges.

Before delivery, identify:

- the strongest line;
- the least-earned line;
- the load-bearing particular;
- the one missing source that would most raise the ceiling;
- the artifact's honest proof state.

## Source-threshold gate

For a transformation-bearing artifact, the following are required:

| Element | Pass question |
|---|---|
| Particular | Is there something real and inspectable whose removal would change the interpretation? |
| Pressure | Are two real wants, values, constraints, or realities in conflict? |
| Behavior | Can we see what the person or system did under that pressure? |
| Cost | Is there an observable consequence rather than merely a declared feeling? |
| Hinge | Is the change earned by an event, accumulated evidence, or counterchoice? |
| Aftermath | Did something become observably different, or is the result honestly still unproven? |
| Transfer | Can the reader or operator use the meaning without being promised an outcome? |

If the hinge is missing, do not write a full transformation. Use an unresolved reflection, story fragment, bounded diagnosis, or evidence-led analysis.

## The prompt to use now

```text
Partner mode. Run Reality Before Rhetoric before drafting.

Use everything already available in the workspace, memory, source files, prior
artifacts, voice evidence, and receipts before asking me anything. Infer the
artifact type. State the real belief, decision, claim, or state this artifact
must change only when the brief or source supports it; otherwise mark the
objective missing.

Identify the one load-bearing particular, the real pressure or contradiction,
the current protective behavior or decision, the observable cost, the hinge,
the first counterchoice or evidence update, the aftermath, and the strongest
meaning the evidence honestly earns.

Separate what is observed, remembered, approximate, interpreted, inferred,
unavailable, private, and unproven. Do not add drama, sensory detail, dialogue,
causal connective tissue, buyer proof, audience response, or resolution that
the source does not contain.

If a material gap remains, do not draft around it. Tell me the exact ceiling
the gap creates, then interview me one non-leading question at a time, maximum
five questions, following the most concrete detail or contradiction. Ask only
for lived truth, taste, private context, or permission that you cannot retrieve
or research yourself.

Mirror the recovered material back as a Source Depth Packet. Give two separate
decisions: source status (`source-ready`, `source-limited`, `source-missing`, or
`permission-blocked`) and authorized artifact tier (`draft`,
`acquire-lived-source`, `acquire-external-evidence`, `diagnostic-only`, or
`hold`). Name the next acquisition action
when source is missing. Treat the hinge as exact, accumulated, or missing;
never manufacture an epiphany.

For offer/copy work, label the claim as process/mechanism, testable
promise/hypothesis, buyer-language observation, demonstrated outcome, or
case-study transformation. For system work, label evidence `structural-only`,
`runtime-observed`, or `behavior-delta-verified`. Never promote one class into
the next without the required evidence.

When the source is ready, choose one functional owner, create the architecture,
and write one integrated draft in my voice. Repay every abstraction with a
particular, tradeoff, proof point, consequence, or observable action. Let the
reader feel recognized before being taught. Preserve unresolved truth rather
than forcing closure.

If a draft is authorized, end with a Reality Receipt:
- strongest line;
- least-earned line;
- load-bearing particular;
- perspective or decision shift;
- proof boundary;
- remaining source gap;
- USE / REVISE / REWORK recommendation.

If drafting is not authorized, replace the line review with:
- strongest supported finding;
- claim that remains unauthorized;
- exact evidence needed next;
- safest smaller artifact, if one exists.

After five questions, a refusal, unavailable evidence, or a privacy veto,
lower the claim, change the form, or hold. Do not keep interviewing and do not
draft through the gap.

I own lived truth, felt meaning, privacy, and taste. You own retrieval, research,
pattern recognition, pressure-testing, architecture, and language. At genuine
taste forks, show me two unblended choices, recommend one, and explain what each
protects. Do not publish or reuse private material without my approval.
```

## Short invocation after the protocol is wired

```text
Partner mode. Run Reality Before Rhetoric. Do not draft until the source earns the intended shift.
```

Until the protocol is wired into the live front doors, use the full prompt above.

## Composition and integration

The protocol fills a missing acquisition layer; it does not replace the existing system.

```text
raw intent
  -> Co-Creative Launchpad: center, edges, success standard
  -> Reality Before Rhetoric: retrieve or recover consequential source
  -> artifact owner: strategy, content, copy, research, or system route
  -> Dhar Seen-Heard Brief only when transformation is the job
  -> High-Taste Writing OS: compose, challenge, and veto
  -> Writing Depth Layer only when an existing draft has a craft deficit
  -> grounding, anti-slop, claim, and human taste gates
  -> field receipt
```

Function ownership stays clean:

- Launchpad decides whether a missing input changes execution.
- Reality Before Rhetoric retrieves and certifies the material.
- Dhar designs a transformation when transformation is appropriate.
- The domain owner writes the artifact.
- High Taste governs composition and evidence.
- Writing Depth repairs an existing draft; it does not invent missing source.
- Farrice owns felt truth and final use.

## Behavior proof from FP-001

- **Input tested:** Parallax transformation post.
- **Weakness diagnosed:** the story jumped from two years of narrowing to a polished explanation and publication without a source-earned causal hinge.
- **Mechanic applied:** remove the synthetic meaning map; use the exact published paragraph and the observable choice, “I left it in. Then I published it.”
- **Behavior delta:** the system stopped explaining what Farrice had not said and showed what the source could prove.
- **Validation:** deterministic prose, grounding, voice, claim-risk, and factory checks passed.
- **Remaining risk:** the broader finalizer remained 7/10 because the private transition event is still unavailable.

This is correct behavior. The protocol improved truth and force without pretending it had recovered the missing life.

## Failure cases

1. **Story laundering:** adding a vivid founder anecdote to hide absent buyer evidence, strategic tradeoffs, research provenance, or runtime behavior.
2. **Detail theater:** adding a room, cup, time, or line of dialogue that does not change the interpretation.
3. **Gate-induced paralysis:** demanding a cinematic hinge for every artifact instead of lowering the claim or selecting a non-story form.
4. **Leading interview:** asking questions designed to confirm the model's preferred thesis.
5. **Confession bias:** treating trauma or private autobiography as the only source of depth.
6. **Polish concealment:** letting downstream craft write smooth connective tissue over a source gap.
7. **Global leakage:** storing or reusing recovered private material without explicit permission.
