# FP-001: Parallax Transformation + Proof-to-Market Branch

## Executive verdict

This is the first run in the Dhar field-test set built from a real character cost and an observable public counterchoice rather than a repository state.

It produces two deliberately separate artifacts:

1. **Transformation artifact:** a Parallax LinkedIn post that moves the reader from “focus requires cutting parts of me away” to “a focused promise can be carried by a whole person.”
2. **Commercial field-test branch:** a current-offer LinkedIn post that moves a supplement founder from “we need more hooks” to “we need the decision those hooks are supposed to carry.”

They are not blended. The Parallax piece protects the emotional repayment. The Proof-to-Market piece protects the buying context and exact offer boundary.

## Reader Contracts

### Parallax

- **Reader enters with:** “My range makes me difficult to understand, so serious work requires hiding part of it.”
- **The post will do:** let the reader inhabit the cost of applying a business-positioning rule to identity, then give one exercise that converts a hidden interest into useful perception.
- **Reader leaves with:** a narrower, more defensible belief: focus belongs in the promise; coherence, not amputation, belongs in the person.
- **Honest limit:** the post cannot prove that integration improves reach, revenue, or well-being.

### Proof-to-Market

- **Reader enters with:** “The copy is tired; we need another batch of hooks.”
- **The post will do:** mirror how a real claim gets thinned, expose the missing decision, and let the team test its alignment before hiring another writer.
- **Reader leaves with:** three question blocks and a way to detect whether copy or upstream decision-making is the real bottleneck.
- **Honest limit:** the post cannot prove offer demand or a client result before publication and buyer response.

## Human Wound and Stakes

1. **Public-safe wound:** Farrice learned to make himself easier to understand by making less of himself visible.
2. **Surface problem:** his interests looked difficult to position together.
3. **Private pressure:** being professionally legible seemed to require hiding the parts that made the work feel alive.
4. **Protective behavior:** “just fitness” stayed public; psychology, spirituality, and anime were held back.
5. **Observable cost:** the content earned engagement and likes, but he reported no desire to make the next piece.
6. **Human stake:** whether success becomes work he wants to continue making, and whether the reader can choose clarity without repeating the same self-reduction.

## Dhar Transformation Run: Parallax

### HEART governance

- **Honor the story:** use only the existing coach scene, two-year “just fitness” period, suppressed interests, reported creative cost, and recorded Parallax publication.
- **Earn trust:** concede the legitimate business need for a clear promise. Do not turn the coach into a villain.
- **Architect the system:** preserve source, claim, beat, handoff, and field-test records so the next piece can improve from evidence.
- **Reach hearts:** name the quiet wound as successful self-erasure, not generic “fear of authenticity.”
- **Turn into impact:** give one small reflection that creates a usable sentence; do not promise therapy or transformation.

### Transformation contract

- **Visible desire:** become clear, credible, and marketable.
- **Hidden need:** make focused work without hiding the interests already present in his authored voice.
- **Counterforce:** a valid market demand for clarity applied too far, until it becomes an identity rule.
- **Protective behavior:** bury psychology and spirituality; keep anime out; publish only “just fitness.”
- **Observable cost:** engagement remains, but the desire to create the next piece disappears.
- **Recognition line:** “The content worked, at least in the ways I knew how to count.”
- **Five-second change:** from treating range as material to cut to leaving the full intersection in the work.
- **Observable new choice:** keep Neville Goddard, a Romanian deadlift, and Goku's training arc in one opening paragraph, then publish Parallax.
- **Viewer shift:** from “I need a smaller identity” to “I need a clearer use for my range.”
- **Safe action:** complete one intersection sentence using an interest the reader has been deleting.
- **Residue:** “A focused promise does not require a smaller person.”

### Packaging covenant

- **Opening promise:** a costly “niche down” decision changed what Farrice allowed himself to make.
- **Literal payoff:** the reader sees exactly what was buried, what the narrow content cost, and the paragraph Farrice later chose to leave intact.
- **Perspective payoff:** the business advice was valid at one level and harmful at another.
- **Action payoff:** a one-sentence intersection test.
- **Trust payoff:** no broad creator-performance claim, fake hinge scene, or unearned reader result.

## Proof-to-Market Branch Run

### Preservation lock

The canonical “Cards Face Up” post remains unchanged. This branch tests one variable only:

> Does adding Farrice's buyer → vitamin-shop counter → trainer provenance plus a team alignment exercise create deeper recognition and more qualified response than the thesis-only primary?

No line is promoted into the primary until Farrice chooses `PROMOTE`, `REJECT`, or `HOLD` after reading both cold.

### Transformation contract

- **Starting state:** “Our writers and agency need more angles.”
- **Private sentence:** “The copy got prettier and kept saying nothing.”
- **Protective behavior:** order more variants instead of forcing the team to resolve the message disagreement.
- **Cost:** a folder full of finished language that gives the buyer no reason to choose.
- **Recognition hinge:** the competitor's unsupported certainty is working while the careful claim disappears.
- **Perspective shift:** volume multiplies the quality of the upstream decision; it cannot replace that decision.
- **Safe action:** legal, creative, and media answer the three question blocks separately and compare the answers.
- **Commercial bridge:** free public-surface teardown, delivered privately before public use; DM PROOF.
- **Proof state:** offer `UNTESTED`; no send, buyer response, sale, or result is represented.

### Buyer and amplifier design

- **Buyer:** supplement or performance founder who recognizes legal/creative/agency dilution and can authorize a teardown or Angle Map.
- **Amplifier:** marketer, copywriter, operator, or agency lead who has watched “creative fatigue” hide an undecided message.
- **Reason to share:** the team alignment check gives people a non-blaming way to identify the upstream disagreement.
- **Reason to buy:** Farrice demonstrates the exact decision he owns and grounds it in buyer-side history.

## Why the Dhar production level works

The source points to a production culture, not a secret sentence formula.

| Layer | Source-earned mechanism | Portable implementation here |
|---|---|---|
| Story culture | Ordinary lived incidents become competing loglines before prose. | Three loglines were generated from one true source; the most transformative, not the loudest, won. |
| Embodiment | An everyday choice under pressure carries the meaning. | The choice is not “be authentic.” Farrice paid, narrowed, withheld, and later published the intersection. |
| Packaging debt | Conflict may earn attention only if the content repays it with recognition, reinterpretation, and action. | $14,000 earns the opening; nuance, publication, and the exercise repay it. |
| Immediate fulfillment | The opening quickly delivers the clicked experience. | Dollar cost, coach, and obedience arrive before explanation. |
| Human useful act | The strongest brand examples make the useful act itself part of the story. | The post demonstrates one micro-capability: separating promise from identity and offering an executable sentence. |
| Handoff continuity | Approved story is broken into units whose purpose and invariants survive production. | The beat map and grouped production units protect each major transition and handoff. |
| Freshness check | A human table-read can reject recently seen, false, or flat material. | Cold read and Farrice wince test remain mandatory; no verifier may self-award exceptional quality. |
| Learning velocity | Small complete releases create evidence faster than one oversized bet. | One hero post and one isolated commercial branch; no carousel or video derivative before a human verdict. |
| Community co-greenlight | Audience input changes real future production decisions. | If published, reader responses will be clustered by the hidden interest or message disagreement they name; that evidence can greenlight the next installment. |
| Separate scoreboards | Discovery, retention, connection, and transformation answer different questions. | The measurement plan below refuses to call views impact. |

The transcript does **not** earn screenplay dialogue, actor direction, shot design, edit rhythm, music, sound, casting, budgeting, or a clone of the studio's software. Those remain separate craft lanes.

## Adversarial false-note pass

### Parallax

- **Performative risk removed:** “magnetic force,” “fully realized humans,” and “goes to the bone.”
- **Generic risk removed:** anti-niche rant and broad “be all of yourself” advice.
- **Overproof removed:** spiritual-tradition, creator-obsession, and thousands-of-people claims.
- **Emotionally unearned risk held:** no invented breakdown, epiphany, reader reply, or physical reaction.
- **Remaining risk:** the precise private event that led from two-year narrowing to the Parallax decision is still absent; the post uses the published paragraph as the smallest observable counterchoice and does not invent the missing epiphany.

### Proof-to-Market

- **Performative risk removed:** no claims of client transformation or market demand.
- **Generic risk removed:** founder receives a three-part team alignment check and concrete retail provenance.
- **Overproof removed:** no statistics, logos, adjacent revenue receipts, or borrowed authority.
- **Emotionally unearned risk held:** no named shopper or dramatic counter event.
- **Remaining risk:** it shares thesis territory with the locked About and “Cards Face Up”; only a cold comparison can decide whether the source layer adds or dilutes.

## Audience Co-Greenlight Loop

### Before publication

Farrice gives each artifact:

- `USE`, `REVISE`, or `REJECT`;
- the line that feels most like him;
- the line that feels least like him;
- one mark where he leaned forward and one where he felt the post writing at him.

This feedback may change the body. Automated scores may not overrule it.

### After publication, if authorized

For Parallax, collect responses that name:

- a hidden interest the reader removed to look serious;
- the sentence they completed;
- a concrete piece of work they now see differently.

For Proof-to-Market, collect:

- founder/marketing profiles that restate the message disagreement;
- team answers that split across the three question blocks;
- qualified DMs asking for the teardown or Angle Map.

Use the response cluster to choose the next story. Do not ask a decorative engagement question whose answer cannot change production.
