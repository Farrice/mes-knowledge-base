## The line that carries the change

>
> Then I published it.

The line is stronger than an explanation because it turns belief into behavior. Dhar's portable mechanism is not “always add drama.” It is “make the internal shift visible through a small choice under pressure.”

## Why the offer branch did not win

The source-enriched offer branch is useful and commercially direct, but it demonstrates a separate taste truth: source depth is not automatically narrative force.

- The primary starts with a sharper buyer wound.
- The branch explains Farrice's authority after the main argument is already complete.
- The retail history is credible, but in this position it feels attached rather than inevitable.
- The branch's team exercise improves utility and commercial pull, but lowers memorability and voice concentration.

The correct system behavior is therefore `HOLD`, not “blend the best parts.” A later branch can test one narrower hypothesis: begin inside a single real counter moment, if Farrice has a public-safe moment with enough sensory and decision detail.

## Honest ceiling

This is a strong publishable draft, not a claimed masterpiece. One missing source event still caps it: the private hinge between two years of narrowing and the decision to publish Parallax. Without that event, the post can show the old strategy and the public counterchoice, but it cannot truthfully dramatize the exact instant the belief changed.

The system behaved correctly by leaving that space open instead of manufacturing an epiphany.
