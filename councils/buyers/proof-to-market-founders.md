## 1. The Personal-Yes Founder (PRIMARY buyer, economic gatekeeper)
`social-grounded (corpus: councils/buyers/corpora/proof-to-market-founders/, harvested 2026-07-19)`
Founder of a sub-$15M supplement/performance brand, profitable or imminently fundable, writes the $2,500 check herself — no committee. Has a dated occasion (raise, launch, retail push) in 60-90 days and feels the clock personally. Defends: her own runway and the story she's about to tell investors. Triggers yes: proof the message-fix is bounded (10 days, not an open retainer) and tied to the dated occasion. Objection voice: *"I already know my product works — why do I need someone to tell me how to say it?"* Corroborating corpus voice, verbatim: *"I realized I wasn't paying for elite marketing performance. I was just funding their sales machine"* (agency-trap fear) and *"Guys the supplement space feels INSANELY crowded... what helped you get attention without selling out or faking it?"* (the invisibility-not-clarity frame — see `zeitgeist-digest.md`).

## 2. The Skeptical VP Brand (secondary, committee buyer)
Head of Brand/Product Marketing at a larger funded brand (Create Wellness/GNC-scale). Reads content, carries compliance and career risk, buys on committee cycle over weeks — never cold-DM'd, always nurtured. Defends: her own judgment call if she champions this internally. Triggers yes: a stress-test case study she can forward without her name on the risk. Objection voice: *"Show me this worked on a brand our size before I put my name on the recommendation."*

## 3. Economic Gatekeeper — Co-Founder/CFO type
`social-grounded (corpus: councils/buyers/corpora/proof-to-market-founders/, harvested 2026-07-19)`
Not marketing-literate, controls runway, evaluates every non-payroll spend against burn rate. Defends: cash runway to the next raise or profitability milestone. Triggers yes: a number — "this sentence costing us the raise" framed as bigger risk than $2,500. Objection voice, VERBATIM: *"Walk me through why this costs more than hiring someone part-time for a month."* Corroborating corpus voice, verbatim: *"I just wanted to share this for any founder currently staring at a $5k to $10k proposal on their desk right now. Take a fraction of that money to hire someone directly, and put the rest into your actual ad spend"* — the same runway-defense logic, independently sourced.

## 4. NO-FIT — Founder with a Formalized In-House Brand Team
Runs a $20M+ brand with a full-time Head of Brand, in-house copywriter, and an agency of record already on positioning. Correctly has no problem this offer solves at the $2,500 sprint tier. Tests over-claim: does the offer's language ("message before the money") sound like it applies to anyone selling anything, or does it correctly signal "sub-$15M, founder-authorized, no committee" and let this seat self-disqualify? Objection voice: *"We already have three people whose job this is — what would you even be doing that they aren't?"*

## 5. Bonus — Investor/Board Advisor (raise-stakes lens)
Sits across the table when Pillar 4 ("The Sea of Same") gets tested — evaluates whether the founder's positioning differentiates in a room full of similar decks. Defends: portfolio-fund reputation for picking winners, not the founder's feelings. Triggers a founder's yes indirectly: if this persona would raise an eyebrow at flat positioning, the founder feels the stakes. Objection voice: *"Your deck reads like the last three I saw this quarter — what's actually different here?"*
