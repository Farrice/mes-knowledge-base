## 1. The Launch-Mode Educator (Robertson shape — primary JUMP candidate)
20-yr S&C coach, gym co-owner, courses/mentorship LIKELY $2K+, LinkedIn ~3K, long-running blog/email. **Live trigger: launching new course bundles "in the next 1-2 weeks" [VERIFIED].** Incentive: make this launch outsell the last one without adding work during his busiest fortnight. Defends: his educator reputation — 20 years of published thinking; a stranger's words on his name is the risk. Triggers yes [LIKELY]: a spec sample that proves S&C fluency (periodization, assessment-beats-template) he can verify in 60 seconds, delivered during the launch window with zero ask. Objection voice: *"I've been writing this stuff since before content was a job. What do you see that I don't?"*

## 2. The Off-Platform King with the Dead LinkedIn (Rusin shape — hardest legitimate target)
DPT/strength coach, 16-20K certified coaches, VERIFIED $2K-4K programs, Men's Health Top-50, big IG + email funnel — and ~418 LinkedIn followers. Incentive: protect the brand machine that already works; new channels are a distraction until proven. Defends: his time and his filter — he gets pitched daily and deletes fast [LIKELY]. Has staff/funnel copywriters already, so "I'll write your emails" is a solved problem to him. Triggers yes [LIKELY]: proof someone at his scale used this and got a number; or a spec so precise about HIS positioning it embarrasses his current copy. Objection voice: *"You have 69 followers and you're telling me my audience isn't buying? Who have you done this for?"*

## 3. The Evidence Scientist with the 20K List (Rosner shape — the proof-demander)
Ex-NFL sport scientist, beehiiv 20K+ [VERIFIED], sells 16-week cohorts, posts daily, wedge = "evidence beats hype / 25 PhDs not influencers." Incentive: convert free readers to cohort seats without contaminating the evidence brand with marketing stink. Defends: epistemic credibility — one hype-shaped claim from you and you're filed under influencer [LIKELY]. Already writes well and mails constantly, so the pain is conversion, not silence. Triggers yes [LIKELY]: dated, verifiable specifics; a free→paid conversion mechanism he can A/B; zero round numbers he can't check. Objection voice: *"Show me the data. '1,000+ transformations' — measured how?"*

## 4. ECONOMIC GATEKEEPER — The Cohort-Runner Who Already Sells Out (Broxterman shape)
RD since 2010, ~$45K launch off her own emails zero ad spend (Get Enlifted podcast, title-level — the launch was on her Prosper coach-cert side; her VIP is a $2,500 rolling 1-on-1 email-application mentorship, corrected 2026-07-21), writes every sequence herself. She is her own CFO: every $2K is weighed against "I already did this myself and it worked." Incentive: buy back launch-week labor, not capability. Defends: unit economics and her own proven process [LIKELY]. Triggers yes [LIKELY]: the offer framed as removing her manual launch labor at equal-or-better conversion — time-back, not skill-gap; a make-right clause she can hold. Objection voice: *"I did $42K off my own emails. Walk me through what your $2,000 gets me that my Sunday afternoon doesn't."* Price-signal antenna: reads pricing as a competence claim — too cheap = doubts the operator, too vague = smells improvisation.

## 5. NO-FIT — The Coach-for-Coaches Marketer (Goodman/Rigsby shape, excluded class)
His business IS marketing to fitness pros; audience large, list monetized, would build any of this himself in a weekend. Correctly has no problem the offer solves. Tests over-claim: headline says "fitness and wellness founders" — does the copy make HIM think it's for him (failure: over-broad), or do "practitioner-founders," the Practitioner Test, and the extraction frame make him self-disqualify (pass)? Secondary tell: he's fluent in feed cadence — any line that scans as LinkedIn-guru template ("X is the moat") gets clocked instantly. Objection voice: *"Cute frame. I teach this. Who's it actually for?"*
