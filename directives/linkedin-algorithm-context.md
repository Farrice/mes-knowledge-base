## Key Rules from the Algorithm Knowledge Base

1. **Profile = AI Prompt.** LinkedIn converts your entire profile to a text prompt fed into a 150B parameter LLM. Optimize accordingly.
2. **Semantic Matching, Not Keywords.** The system matches content by meaning, not hashtags or keywords. Write for meaning.
3. **Dwell Time is the New Primary Metric.** Optimize for time-on-content, not likes or comment velocity.
4. **Voice is the Moat.** In an AI-content world, your unique voice is the only non-replicable differentiator.
5. **Dead Tactics:** Engagement pods, timing hacks, hashtag stuffing, generic advice content, mass connection requests.
6. **Evergreen Compounding:** Content can resurface weeks later when new matching audiences are found.
7. **LinkedIn SEO:** LinkedIn is the 2nd most cited source in AI Overviews. Optimize for Google discoverability.
8. **Pre-Publish Checklist:** Run the 10-point decision framework (Section 6 of the KB) before publishing any post.
