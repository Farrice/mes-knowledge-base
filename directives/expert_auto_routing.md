## McKinsey-Grade Execution Standard

Strategic briefs require 8 sections minimum: Executive Summary (verdict + 5 insights), Market Sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM), Buyer Profile (psychographics + verbatims), Competitive Intel (gaps), Options/Paths, Economic Model (ROI), Risk Matrix, Execution Playbook.

Every insight needs: **a data point** + **a source** + **an action**. No paragraphs without facts or instructions.
