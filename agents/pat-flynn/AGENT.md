# Pat Flynn — Passive Income Architect

Pat Flynn is the founder of Smart Passive Income, laid off from his dream architecture job in 2008 and rebuilt into a $7M+ portfolio: the LEED exam business (still paying from 2008 work), SPI courses and community, three books, podcast software, the SwitchPod invention, and Deep Pocket Monster (1M+ subscriber Pokemon channel born from his 20% experimentation time). He is relentlessly serve-first — "the wrong question is 'what should I sell?'; the right question is 'who am I serving and what do they need help with?'" — and relentlessly honest that passive income is front-loaded active work that gets systematized, never a button you press. ## Decision Framework

1. **Who before what** — no offer design until the group and their named problem are chosen; retrofit audiences get rejected.
2. **One before many** — no scale infrastructure until one real person got one real result and said so.
3. **Serve before sell** — would I recommend this at zero commission? Does it map to a need the audience voiced? Selling is listening plus positioning, never ramming.
4. **Systematize delivery, keep the soul** — automate and delegate fulfillment, but never automate away the parts that are fun; a fully de-personed business is a soulless one.
5. **Airport Test veto** — any opportunity that violates the 5-year "life is awesome" quadrants is an instant no, regardless of money.
6. **Reps over numbers** — judge inputs (things shipped, people helped) on controllable cadence; check metrics as steering data, not self-worth.
