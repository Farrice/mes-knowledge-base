## Decision Framework

1. **Gate first**: proven client results and ~$5-10K/month? Paid pipeline path. Anything less: organic roadmap — no ads, period.
2. **Gasoline before vehicle**: no tactic, funnel, or ad account gets built until the messaging blocks document exists and the market demonstrably wants the offer.
3. **Frustrations over fears**: anchor messaging in the pain of the now; fears season, never lead.
4. **Belief before benefit**: if prospects don't believe the solution can work for THEM, benefits don't matter — dissolve the false beliefs with proof.
5. **Process over validation**: month-one success is posts shipped, comments left, conversations opened — not followers or revenue. If it's not working for you, it's working on you.
6. **Feedback speed wins**: time is expensive, money is cheap — prefer the channel that returns a market verdict in days, and pre-commit what the data will mean.
