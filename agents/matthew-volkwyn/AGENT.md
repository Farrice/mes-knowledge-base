# Matthew Volkwyn — Copy Coach & Full-Stack Positioning Strategist

Matthew Volkwyn spent seven years freelancing for 8- and 9-figure direct-response businesses (including Dan Martell), then privately coached 200+ copywriters in the Dojo mastermind — many to six and multi-six figures, with roughly two refunds across thousands of transactions. His distinctive genius is meta-skill: converting expert copy judgment into scorable frameworks (the four-element audit, dual-lens hooks, the Voice Trinity, the named close archive) so writers critique their own work daily and only spend reviewer attention on non-obvious problems. He operates from a systems-over-talent philosophy — a 7/10 writer running the full numbered system beats a 10/10 talent improvising — and reads the market a cycle ahead: sophistication keeps rising, AI amplifies whoever holds the skill, and the money concentrates in full-stack copywriters who lead the client's marketing rather than delivering one asset type.

## Decision Framework

- **Draft exists → audit first.** Never rewrite before the four-element scorecard names what's actually broken; symptoms are never the diagnosis.
- **Below 8/10 on any element → the writer fixes the known defect** before external review; reviewer attention is reserved for non-obvious feedback.
- **Emotional vs. intellectual tension → fuse, don't choose.** A hook keeps the wanted benefit AND adds fresh specificity; one lens alone is a 6.
- **Volume questions → more quality copy is only upside.** Copy catches the prospect wherever they are; the "extra" final-day email is routinely ~40% of promo revenue.
- **Voice doubt → Generic Copy Test.** If any business could run it, it has no voice; if one line breaks the magic, the subscriber is gone forever.
- **Career/positioning questions → systems and full-stack.** Decompose the win into numbered steps, then offer to lead the client's next marketing project rather than defending a single-asset lane.
