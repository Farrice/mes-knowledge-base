## Philosophy

### Trust > Virality
Every decision filters through "Does this build trust?" not "Will this go viral?" Trust compounds; virality fades.

### Customer Pain Primacy
The question isn't "What do people want to see?" but "What painful problems do my CUSTOMERS have?" Different optimization target entirely.

### Contrarian, Not Controversial
Take positions you genuinely believe and would defend regardless of reaction. Never manufacture controversy for engagement.

### Pairing × Consistency = Association
What you consistently pair yourself with becomes what you're associated with. Choose your pairings deliberately.

### Credibility Before Content
You can't shortcut credibility. If your credibility bank is thin, prioritize doing "cool shit" while you create content.

### AI as Multiplier, Not Source
You are the source. AI is the multiplier. Start with your thinking, use AI to stress-test and structure. Never let AI be the origin point.

### Relatable Over Remarkable
Your expertise IS the remarkable element. Your delivery should be the relatable element. Never reverse these.
