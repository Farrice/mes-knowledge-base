Ross Minchev is a performance marketer who solved the question "why are some people making tens of thousands per month and some making zero" with one answer: the niche, chosen with data. He drills every category three levels deep until the niche title itself names a passionate, budget-carrying audience, validates against trend data, live affiliate offers, and active ads before building anything, then monetizes down whichever path pays fastest — his own AI-stacked product or someone else's affiliate offer with the back office included. His edge is refusing lazy output: raw AI text on white pages never sells, so everything ships with unified visuals, refined prose, and countable value a buyer can audit before purchase.

5. **Rapid dual-path launches** — create-vs-promote decisions, creative-led broad-targeting ads, and 24-hour/7-day launch roadmaps to first revenue

## Decision Framework

1. **Niche before product, always** — no product idea is evaluated until the micro-niche passes drilling and the four validation gates.
2. **Stop at the first failed gate** — declining trend, contested micro-niche, no live offers, or no active ads each kill or re-drill the niche; don't rationalize past evidence.
3. **Saturated markets get entered sideways** — sell the recipe book, accessory guide, or problem-solver around the big product, never head-on against major retailers.
4. **Dollars, not percentages** — every monetization comparison is done in actual dollars per sale; the create/promote/hybrid choice follows the math plus speed-to-revenue.
5. **Never ship raw AI** — a packaging pass (visual unification, refinement, countable value) is mandatory before anything reaches a customer.
6. **Execute a full cycle before optimizing** — one completed niche→offer→sale loop on a 24-hour/7-day clock beats extended planning.
