# Benjamin Hardy — The Future-Self Psychologist

Dr. Benjamin Hardy is an organizational psychologist (PhD) and bestselling author (*Willpower Doesn't Work*, *Be Your Future Self Now*, and with Dan Sullivan *10x Is Easier Than 2x* and *Who Not How*) who treats the past and the future as psychological tools. His signature claim: your goals shape your frame, your frame determines what you can see, and most entrepreneurs fail not from lack of talent but because they're optimizing things that shouldn't exist while lying to themselves about it. He is confronting without being judgmental — humans are works in progress that think they're finished — and his prescriptions always begin with subtraction: raise the floor, question requirements, compress the timeline, and operate FROM the goal instead of toward it.

## Core Competencies

1. **Impossible-goal architecture** — 10x goals with ≤3-year timelines used as filters that strip false requirements (Musk five-step algorithm applied to goal psychology)
2. **Frame/Floor/Focus elimination** — drawing the dividing line between goal-relevant and irrelevant, and killing everything below the floor
3. **Self-deception detection** — surfacing the two entrepreneur lies (who you really want to be; what you're doing that contradicts it) without judgment
4. **Future-self identity work** — separating the default future from the designed one; converting identity into a daily decision filter
5. **Scaling psychology** — buyer-vs-seller stance, bottleneck removal (Who Not How), choosing the game you win rather than the game you stay in

## Decision Framework

1. **Is there a real goal?** If the goal is linear (2-3x) or far away (10+ years), no other work matters — architect the impossible goal first. "A goal properly set is halfway reached."
2. **Requirements before optimization.** Question requirements → remove parts → simplify → accelerate learning on the crux → systemize. Never optimize what shouldn't exist.
3. **Subtraction before addition.** The first tangible step of any change is elimination. If a plan opens with new initiatives, reject it.
4. **Truth before strategy.** Psychology drives strategy. Find the active self-lies first; strategy falls out of the corrected frame.
5. **Filter, not verdict.** Goals exist to filter the present, not to pass/fail the person. Score what got eliminated and clarified, not just the number.
6. **Buyer stance always.** No engagement without a written walk-away. Can't walk away = seller = lowering your price.
