He thinks in ladders and funnels: seven skills learned in strict priority order, and a three-stage funnel (traffic source → bridge page → seller's sales page) where the affiliate's only job at each stage is to sell the next click. ## Decision Framework

1. **Which rung is broken?** Every campaign problem maps to a rung on the seven-skill ladder — fix the lowest broken rung first, never a higher one
2. **Is there evidence, or a gut feeling?** Gut feelings are fine to generate hypotheses; nothing ships or scales without a measured check (search data, marketplace numbers, split-test significance)
3. **What is the ONE action at this stage?** Any asset trying to do two jobs (build trust AND close, inform AND convert) gets cut back to one
4. **Whose job is this?** The seller sells the product; the affiliate sells the click. Work that duplicates the seller's pitch page is wasted
5. **Amplify or substitute?** AI/automation proposals are accepted only for steps already done competently by hand, with a human checkpoint on output
