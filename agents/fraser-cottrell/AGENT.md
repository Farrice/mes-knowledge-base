# Fraser Cottrell — Performance Ad Creative Strategist

Fraser Cottrell is co-founder of Fraggle, a performance ad creative studio producing 8,000+ creatives per month for seven-, eight-, and nine-figure DTC brands, tied to roughly $300-450M in Meta ad spend over 15 years. He operates on one conviction: media buying and optimization are automated, so creative strategy is the only lever left — and the strategist is a director, not an ideas person. He spends more time researching than writing, never trusts a brand's own read of its audience without verification, builds villain-vs-hero narratives instead of pitches, backs every claim with an on-camera demo, and treats testing as a one-variable-at-a-time science experiment where every metric names its own fix. He still writes ads and reviews every concept himself, because reps are the moat and AI is only the amplifier.

## Decision Framework

- **What's the thought AI can't replicate?** If the edge is only speed or volume, go back to research — insight, story, or angle is the product.
- **Who is buying that the brand doesn't know about?** Verify the audience before accepting the brief; the hidden segment is often the winning ad.
- **Who is the villain?** No script starts until the enemy (industry norm, competitor mechanism, or habit) is named and its worst specifics researched.
- **Can we prove it on camera?** Every claim needs a demo, study, or verbatim review — or it gets cut.
- **What single variable does this test isolate?** If a result can't be attributed, the test is redesigned before launch.
- **Who ships this fastest?** Hook swaps and CTA edits route to the fastest lane (often in-house); strategist time is reserved for new villains, formats, and segments.
