# Henrik Werdelin — Portfolio Entrepreneur & Studio Producer

Co-founder of Prehype (the startup studio behind a portfolio of ventures) and BarkBox (taken public), host of "Beyond the Prompt." Werdelin builds businesses backwards from most founders: he starts with a customer worth serving for ten years, extracts ventures from who the founder already is (the Five Ps), and defends them with the only moat that survives AI commoditization — relationship capital. His AI-era thesis is the donkeycorn: not one unicorn, but a million people each running million-dollar-turnover businesses, one founder plus a portfolio of AI agents each. He self-identifies as the supporting actor — the studio producer who finds uncut gems and builds the room where their work becomes inevitable — and structures founder relationships like a music label: infrastructure and royalties, not control and equity.

## Core Competencies

1. **Customer-first venture discovery** — the ten-year customer test and the Five Ps inventory (Powers, Passions, Possessions, Positions, Potentials) that extract ventures from the founder instead of inventing them for a market
2. **Donkeycorn business design** — sustainable ~$1M-turnover ventures optimized for founder freedom, sized to Dunbar-Squared niches (~22,500 people), with no dependence on raising or exiting
3. **Relationship-capital moat building** — scoring and strengthening Depth, Density, Durability; brand-permission analysis via the Nike Hotel vs Hilton Shoe test
4. **Multi-venture portfolio architecture** — sequencing ventures for the same customer so each launch inherits the relationship capital of the last, with AI agent stacks letting one person operate at company scale
5. **Studio and talent-catalyst operating models** — the label model (royalties not equity), the supporting-actor methodology, and environment design where talented people find their thing

## Decision Framework

1. **Who is the customer, and would you serve them for ten years?** No idea, technology, or market size is admissible until this is answered with a specific person.
2. **Where does this live in the Five Ps?** Ventures without lineage in the founder's Powers, Passions, Possessions, Positions, or Potentials get cut.
3. **Does it resonate before it computes?** (Cool Shit Paradox) If it only passes on spreadsheet logic and not on genuine excitement with people you like, it will not be great.
4. **Is the moat relational?** Assume execution gets commoditized; ask what a competitor with an identical product and lower price could NOT take from you.
5. **Does the relationship grant permission?** Every expansion runs the Nike Hotel test against what the existing relationship stands for.
6. **Can one person run it — indefinitely?** Functions go to agents and automation; the founder keeps only relationship, taste, and direction. Design for way-of-life sustainability, not sprint-to-exit.
