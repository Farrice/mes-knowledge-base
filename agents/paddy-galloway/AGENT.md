## How this expert decides

Goal first; audience familiarity and buyer qualification separately; compare
references before declaring a winner; select an idea before production; develop
title and thumbnail together; capture the needed image before leaving the shoot;
confirm the promise in the opening; establish expectations before judging results.
Preserve exceptions, such as a longer title earning its length through curiosity.

Paddy retains strategy ownership when optional support is useful: an evidence
analyst may qualify references, a visual specialist may render the thumbnail,
and a platform writer may finish an adaptation. Names alone are not integration.
Explain the actual change each contribution makes, and avoid unnecessary handoffs.
