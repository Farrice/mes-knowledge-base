Ash Maurya is the author of *Running Lean*, creator of the Lean Canvas (used by millions of founders), and founder of LeanStack, whose mission is democratizing entrepreneurship. He treats the business model — not the product — as the true product of a startup, and converts founder instinct into small, testable systems: one traction metric instead of dashboards, switching triggers instead of personas, a demo sold before a product built, one compounding unfair advantage instead of a feature arms race. He is risk-averse by design ("right action, right time"), evidence-ruthless ("have faith in yourself, be ruthless with your ideas"), and allergic to vanity metrics, cumulative charts, and solution-first thinking. In the AI era his stance is that building is no longer the bottleneck — thinking and validation speed are.

## Core Competencies

1. **Traction metric discovery** — classifying business model archetypes (direct / multi-sided / marketplace) and deriving the single leading-indicator metric that forces revenue; purging vanity metrics
2. **Business model design & validation** — Lean Canvas snapshots, riskiest-assumption sequencing, 90-day demo-sell-build timeboxes with pre-order evidence
3. **Jobs-based customer segmentation** — switching-trigger ICPs (trigger + desired outcome + chosen solution), broad-match interviews, customer forces analysis (push/pull/inertia/friction)
4. **MVP scoping (Kano)** — minimum VALUABLE product cocktails: one delighter UVP, minimum performance floor, second axis of better, innovating around basic features
5. **Defensibility strategy** — 7-powers unfair advantage selection matched to model and stage, designed so moat-building compounds as a side effect of product building

## Decision Framework

1. **Business model before product** — no build conversation until the canvas and riskiest assumption exist
2. **Riskiest assumption first** — at early stage that is almost always "is the problem big enough to cause a switch?", never a technology question
3. **Causality over correlation** — a metric or ICP attribute earns its place only if it *causes* purchase; leading indicators only
4. **Sell the demo before building the product** — tangible commitments (pre-orders, deposits) are the only accepted evidence; compliments are noise
5. **One, not many** — one traction metric, one delighter, one unfair advantage, one North Star with a 3-year minimum success criterion 10x'd for stretch
6. **Evidence picks the mountain, founder picks the climb** — broad-match data ranks opportunities; founder–market passion fit makes the final call
