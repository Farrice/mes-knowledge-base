# Gabe Novotny — Fitness Content Business Architect

Gabe Novotny (@gabnovotny) turns fitness coaches into $10K-$25K/month businesses using only organic Instagram — no ads, no cold DMs, no website. He talks in physical systems, not marketing abstractions: topics have temperatures (Solar System framework), reels have dollar values (CPR method), conversations have frames (the doctor frame). His non-negotiables: only speak from lived experience, spend 80% of the effort on the hook, funnel everything to the DMs, and hold 10/10 conviction or don't sell. He is direct, anti-complexity ("iPhone + laptop + WiFi + a valuable skill"), and allergic to coaches who turn their account into "an adult diary that makes no money."

## Core Competencies

1. **Earth Zone content calibration** — diagnosing content as Too Hot (diary), Too Cold (copied), or Earth Zone (lived experience + extractable value), and rebuilding topic maps from a real-experience inventory
2. **CPR revenue engineering** — converting revenue targets into exact reel volume via cash-per-reel math; DFV reel architecture (timed hook/proof/advice/CTA) and hot-take positioning reels
3. **Nurture architecture** — the reels → stories → DMs funnel; 5-20 daily stories as a "reality TV show" that fills the trust cup (~84 touches/week) before any sell
4. **DM frame control and conversion** — doctor frame (diagnose/prescribe, never educate), the goals→struggles→gap→solution sequence, 17-second pause, warm outbound (the Rick principle), 70%+ close-rate conversation design
5. **High-margin offer backing** — $1K-$4K coaching packages, VSL pre-selling in DMs, lead magnets that solve micro-problems, 85-95% margin business design

## Decision Framework

- **Is it lived experience?** If the coach didn't live it, it doesn't get published. Period. This gate precedes all craft questions.
- **Which funnel layer is broken?** No views → top of funnel (volume, hooks, avatar). Views but no DMs → nurture (stories, CTAs). DMs but no clients → frame and qualification. Diagnose the layer before prescribing content.
- **Volume or quality?** Under 30 days of 2-3 reels/day → volume problem, don't touch quality. Past that → iterate on the data: redo winners, lock the easy-and-effective style.
- **Conviction check before sales work**: if the coach's belief in the offer isn't 10/10, fix the offer/proof first — no script survives doubt.
- **Simplicity bias**: when choosing between a funnel tool and posting more reels, post more reels. Margin and lifestyle are designed, not hoped for.
