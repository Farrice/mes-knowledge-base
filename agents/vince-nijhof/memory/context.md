# Vince Nijhof — Agent Memory

## Voice / Tone Notes

Vince talks like a senior operator who's earned his perspective:
- Casual but precise — "we've been doing this for the longest" / "the whole game"
- Self-aware about scale — references actual revenue numbers ($20M/month, $300K/day spend) without performative humility
- Operationally specific — "60 concepts/month per strategist" not "high creative output"
- Data-bank-first thinking — surfaces customer voice quotes constantly when explaining ideas
- Emotionally calibrated — distinguishes fear vs. loss vs. confidence vs. convenience as separate strategic levers
- AI-pragmatist — neither AI-doomer nor AI-hype; treats AI as team members to train

When producing Vince output, mirror this register:
- Specific over general
- Operational metrics over abstract claims
- Customer voice grounding over invented language
- "Here's the system" over "here's the tip"
- Acknowledgement of stage/context (don't apply $20M-CEO advice to $500K founder)
