## Core Frameworks (one-line each)

1. **Creative Pyramid**: Avatar / Idea-Style-Hook / Volume-Diversity-Relevance
2. **Content Grid**: Ideas (X) × Styles (Y) = concept matrix; multiply with hook variants
3. **Vacation Test**: viewer comes back from vacation, sees next ad — "I've seen this" or "this is new"?
4. **5-Step Wealth Loop**: Identify → Solve → Systematize → Delegate → Sell
5. **Care Square**: Results / Perception / Relationship / Efficiency
6. **Three S's**: System / Selling / Strategy (founder focus; Execution gets delegated)
7. **2 Atoms of Attention**: Relevance + Difference (everything else is derivative)
8. **Land & Expand**: front-end with current buzzword, expand inside the account
9. **Title-as-Mechanism**: titles are positioning tools, not identity statements
10. **IVOC**: Reddit/YouTube comments as primary research source

## Operational Standards (the bar)

- Diversity at FUNDAMENTAL layer (Idea / Style / Hook), not surface (color / copy-tweak)
- Andromeda Vacation Test pass rate: 80%+ on top 10 ads in any batch
- IVOC research: 50+ verbatim quotes from 3+ unmoderated venues
- Hook coverage: 5 hook types layered per concept
- Care Square: all 4 dimensions audited monthly per client
- Three S's audit: quarterly; if Execution >40% past Year 2, hire decision is overdue
- AI deployment: proprietary inputs ALWAYS load first; AI amplifies, never generates research

## Anti-Patterns (Alex would reject)

- Surface-only diversification (color/copy variants masquerading as different ads)
- AI-generated research ("show me your research" → opens Claude chat = automatic rejection)
- Skill-selling not problem-selling ("I write emails" caps you at email pricing)
- Conceptual frameworks without operational SOPs
- Niching down as ideology (before you've done enough breadth to know what compounds)
- Saying no to in-domain client requests in years 1-5 (early stagnation)
- Optimizing Results without Perception (clients leave operators who deliver numbers but don't show their work)
- Pre-Andromeda creative advice repeated as current

## Decision Framework (when in doubt)

1. Ad-creative decision? → Creative Pyramid
2. Business-bottleneck decision? → 5-Step Wealth Loop (which step are you stuck at?)
3. Client-relationship decision? → Care Square (which dimension is weak?)
4. "Should I take this work?" decision? → Years 1-5 yes-first; Years 5+ Three S's filter
5. Attention/hook decision? → Force-classify as Relevance or Difference. Both = best.
6. "How should I position myself?" → Adopt the current market buzzword. Don't argue.
