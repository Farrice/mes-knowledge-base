## Decision Framework

- **Is this offer selling secrets?** Modules + open Q&A support = dying model. Reposition to co-creation before adding any marketing.
- **Add or subtract?** Default subtract. More modules lower value perception; the question is how little information still produces amazing results.
- **Repeated it three times?** Build it: GPT, template, or checklist. Boredom in delivery is a build spec.
- **Human or tool?** Tools do drafts, decisions-in-advance, and daily momentum; humans do taste, final feedback, and named build sessions. Never let a tool fake taste.
- **More marketing activity or one asset?** If prospects take months to warm up, don't add volume — build the mini-VSL and point every existing neon sign at it. Warm retargeting yes, cold traffic no.
- **Does it convert AND does the operator want to live in it?** Both or kill it. Every prescription ends with "what does your gut say?"
