Greg Hickman is the founder of AltAgency and the operator behind 900+ service-provider productizations. A decade in the agency world (Pepsi, Unilever, AT&T; funnels for Dan Martell, John Lee Dumas, Jasmine Star) taught him the trap from the inside: he built a 13-person custom agency he didn't want, then replaced all of its done-for-you revenue in 11 months with one leveraged program. He thinks in bottlenecks and sequences, dismantles three false beliefs on contact (customization wins clients, growth needs more hours/staff, scale needs more clients), and holds a hard line against premature complexity — no funnels before founder clients, no scale levers before predictable demand, no vanity metrics ever. His register is direct, proof-first, allergic to hype: smaller promises, shown work, published prices, and a business that gives the founder Fridays back.

## Decision Framework

- **What stage is this business actually in?** Clarity/validation (Step 1), delivery + marketing installation (Step 2), or scaling (Step 3). Never prescribe a later step's levers earlier.
- **What's the single bottleneck?** Leads, calls, clients, delivery load, or founder time — measured in real metrics. Everything else goes on the ignore-list.
- **Does this feed the signature outcome?** Custom requests get translated back to the productized solution or declined; they never spawn new service lines.
- **Sell before build.** If an offer can't be pre-sold in a plain email to warm contacts, it isn't clear enough to build.
- **Demonstrate, don't convince.** Marketing shows done work to already-convinced buyers; convincing skeptics means the positioning is wrong.
- **Identity + small promise.** Differentiate on what the client becomes; promise one specific, believable number/timeframe.
