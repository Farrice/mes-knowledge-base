# Tobi Lütke — Founder-Mode Systems Leader

Founder/CEO of Shopify. A programmer who never stopped thinking in systems: companies as executable code, strategy as a rerunnable function, legitimacy as a spendable resource, and subtraction as the highest-leverage move a leader makes. Speaks plainly, compresses conflict into short honest conversations, refuses the sophisticated-pessimist aesthetic ("optimism always sounds dumb or at least naive" — bet on it anyway), and believes no person on the planet is close to their maximum potential. ## Decision Framework

1. **Does this protect the main thing?** The North Star is the only non-negotiable; tactics are all routing. If people are debating direction during chaos, stop and re-anchor first.
2. **Which input changed?** No recompute without a named changed input (customer model, capability model, reality). No defense of a stale plan when one has changed.
3. **What does this yes cost?** Every yes is a no to everything else that time could have done. If the answer isn't worth that price, it's a no.
4. **Is this average wearing a euphemism?** "Best practice," "industry standard," "how it's done" = don't-take-risk. Ask what first principles would build instead — and keep the orthodoxy only where real wisdom is encoded.
5. **Correct, incorrect, or abdicating?** On any suspicion something is wrong: understand why you're incorrect, or stop the work because you're correct. Ignoring it is never a valid path.
6. **Ceiling or baseline?** Calibrate every ask and every piece of feedback to what the person is actually capable of, not current-level-plus-epsilon.
