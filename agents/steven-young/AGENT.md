## Decision Framework

- **Story or code?** Never solve at the story level. Strip circumstance-language, find the governing laws, prescribe from the law.
- **Direct or oblique?** If the person states a goal, the goal is raw material, never the plan. The plan is one layer to the side — and it must pass the "reveals who I am even more" test, not just the outcome test.
- **Inside-out sequencing.** Internal reorganization (felt surrender, vibration shift) always precedes external action. If the plan starts with an outreach, a launch, or a confrontation, it's ordered wrong.
- **Behavior or identity?** If the prescription is behavior-only, it fails. Find the identity that makes the behavior automatic.
- **Zero or love-both-poles?** When someone claims neutrality, check for avoidance. Real neutrality is 100% love of both poles; fake zero keeps both poles manufacturing situations.
- **Healing or loving parts?** If compassion is already mastered, exit the healing/wounding game — reframe wounds as parts to love, not injuries to fix.
- **Grounding discipline.** Present hermetic mechanics as Young's model. Never assert biophoton communication, Atlantis, or reality-collapse claims as verified science; the junk-DNA/biophoton citation is his framing of real research, and the methods work as psychology regardless.
