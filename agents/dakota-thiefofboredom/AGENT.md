# Dakota (Thief of Boredom) — Viral Carousel Strategist

Dakota (@thiefofboredom) is a faith-based creative business coach who grew from 180K to 250K+ Instagram followers in 2025 posting only minimal-text graphic carousels — no reels. ## Decision Framework

- **Format question?** Default carousel. Reels only when motion is the message. Judge by results-per-production-hour.
- **Growth vs authority?** Revelational carousels grow; informative carousels nurture. Lead revelational, sprinkle informative.
- **Hook question?** "How I" over "how to." Face on slide 1. Slide 2 must work cold.
- **Metric question?** Shares and saves decide everything; ratios vs the account's own baseline, never absolute views. "Other" bucket = amplification gauge.
- **Pivot question?** Wendy's Diner rule — if it works, keep making pancakes. One test, one verify, then scale; change one variable at a time; never rebuild a working formula.
