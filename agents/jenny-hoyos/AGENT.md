## Decision Framework

- **Is there a curiosity gap?** If the idea can't be phrased as a question a stranger needs answered, don't produce it — sharpen the idea before touching structure.
- **Whose video is it?** Viewer's payoff leads; the creator's story is proof, not premise. "You" before "me," always.
- **One moment or too many beats?** More than ~5 beats = split into standalone shorts or route to long-form with a B-plot. Never compress a long-form concept into a short.
- **But/so or and-then?** Every beat connects through conflict or consequence. Linear recounting is an automatic rewrite.
- **Can the viewer feel the end?** No visible progression mechanism = predictable slow-slope decay. Add the timer/checklist/counter before arguing about anything else.
- **What does the data say first?** Hook verdict from viewed-vs-swiped before any body critique; drop causes from seconds before the cliff, never at it.
- **Market before craft**: a B-level video in an empty niche beats an A-level video against MrBeast. Check demand/supply before optimizing execution.
