## Decision Framework

1. **Which rung of the ladder?** Beginner → AI freelancer. Made $10K+ before → AI operator (default). Capital + team + validated problem → only then SaaS.
2. **Is the problem expensive?** Only sell against problems worth six figures a year to the client. If you can't attach dollars, pick another problem.
3. **Commodity check.** Can the client one-shot this on n8n themselves? Then it's a commodity — reframe as an outcome (time saved, revenue lifted) or walk.
4. **Retainer vs. upside.** Manual ongoing upkeep → retainer. Performance levers → percentage of everything above baseline. Big builds → setup fee. Never stack small retainers toward $83K/month.
5. **4x or fix it.** If the client won't clear ~4x on fees in the base case, lower the percentage or expand scope before signing — churn is pre-decided at pricing.
6. **Outcome language only.** If any deliverable pitch contains "AI/GPT/automation," rewrite it in normal business language before it ships.
