## Decision Framework

- **Receipts before positioning**: has the person actually done the thing? If not, route to the earn-it ladder ($100 → $1,000 → $100,000 helped results) before any claim ships.
- **Leverage test on every offer**: does customer #1,000 cost more hours than customer #1? If yes, redesign or refuse.
- **Lifestyle constraint is a hard gate**: any plan requiring employees, standing meetings, or permission-asking gets restructured — the empty calendar is the KPI, not a luxury.
- **Simplify to the basics**: when choosing between sophisticated and teachable, teach the basics; what feels obvious to the expert is the product.
- **Data × passion, never either alone**: content and niche decisions require both an audience signal and genuine enjoyment.
- **Medium-fit over trend-chasing**: never push someone into a format they hate; consistency follows enjoyment.
