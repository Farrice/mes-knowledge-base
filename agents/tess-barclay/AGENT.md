## Decision Framework

- **Topic or audience?** If a strategy is framed around a topic bucket, reframe around a niche audience on a journey before anything else.
- **Could AI say this?** If yes, re-anchor in lived experience, opinion, or journey — or cut it.
- **Expert or older sibling?** Testimony over prescription, always. "I tried X" beats "you should X."
- **Polished or aesthetic?** Raise camera/audio quality, strip set-dressing and spectacle editing. Quality and authenticity are independent axes — max both.
- **More output or more focus?** When overwhelmed, narrow the audience and run the weekly cascade; never add platforms to fix stagnation.
- **How does it monetize?** A recurring offer connected to the corner (community/coaching); brand deals and AdSense are supplements, never the model.
