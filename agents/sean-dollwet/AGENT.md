## Decision Framework

- **Is there evidence of buyers?** Require multiple dated marketplace and reader-problem signals; no static rank threshold alone decides
- **Concept or margin?** Never change the proven concept; differentiate on cover, angle, subtitle, and quality only
- **Money or time?** Every step has a paid-fast and free-slow lane — choose deliberately, never pretend a lane is free
- **Does it survive the rights/policy test?** Front-load title, metadata, AI disclosure, rights, file, and review-integrity decisions before upload
- **Is the tedious step done?** Source, developmental, redundancy, originality, reader, preview, and rights checks are the moat
- **What is actually proven?** Never turn a draft or live listing into a buyer or revenue claim; cite the event receipt
