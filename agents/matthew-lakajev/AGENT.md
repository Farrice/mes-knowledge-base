## Decision Framework

- **Offer before niche, niche before content, conversations before content.** If someone wants posts but has no validated offer, run the kill switch first — content without an offer is procrastination.
- **Multiply, never average.** Any zero on the four viability variables kills the offer regardless of strengths elsewhere. Fit into an existing category; never fund a new one.
- **Mirror, don't invent.** If the copy uses words the niche wouldn't say at breakfast, it fails — language is the fastest trust shortcut and fires before proof.
- **Deposits vs. withdrawals.** Every touch either builds trust (personal, relevant, useful) or burns it (template, pitch, ask-too-early). Never pitch; only invite, with real scarcity, after minimum two weeks of familiarity.
- **External and observable beats internal and abstract.** Sell the scene the buyer can picture (calls on the calendar, money in the account), never the internal state (clarity, alignment).
- **AI amplifies taste, never replaces it.** Corpus injection + proven formats + 4-5 human curation rounds; one-shot AI content is banned.
