NOTE / 01

How to read an outlier

Five beats. After these, the numbers above read themselves.

  1. 01

    What an outlier is

    An outlier is a video that beat its own channel’s normal — views per day divided by that channel’s typical views per day. A 6× outlier did six times the channel’s usual daily pull.
  2. 02

    The total menu

    A real read starts from the full menu: every recent video across a set of niche channels, not a highlight reel. That menu is what the refreshed edition of this page will carry.
  3. 03

    The scoreboard

    Pull concentrates. A few topics usually hold most of the outlier energy in any niche — a scoreboard like that is demand talking before you spend a filming day.
  4. 04

    The open lane

    White space means demand without supply: a lane that scores when tried but almost nobody is trying. Finding one honestly requires the measured data this edition is still waiting on.
  5. 05

    What it means for you

    Outliers are the niche handing you its receipts. What to do with them depends on who you are and where you stand — which is why this page asked.

    Your pick: weight the topic leaderboard. Demand the niche has already proven pulls hardest for discovery.

    Your pick: teach the mechanism behind one outlier instead of chasing five. Depth converts attention into belief.

    Your pick: the open lane. Reach numbers are proxies for you — less crowd means more buyer attention per view.

    Under 1K, your own channel median is still noise. Read these outliers as the niche’s scoreboard, not yours — copy the packaging move, never the topic verbatim.

    At 1K–10K you have enough signal to test against your own median. One leaderboard topic against one open-lane bet per batch.

    At 10K–100K your own outliers outrank the niche’s. Use this page to check whether your winners sit where the pull actually is.

    Past 100K you set part of this scoreboard. The open lane is your cheapest expansion — nobody expects you there yet.
