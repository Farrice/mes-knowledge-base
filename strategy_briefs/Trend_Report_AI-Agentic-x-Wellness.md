## Phase 2 — Trend Research (LIVE)

### The Single Converging Thesis (all 3 tracks landed here independently)

> **AI is bifurcating the funnel.** The top (discovery/content) is being automated to near-zero cost — and AI engines now *gate* who gets seen. The bottom (trust/conversion) is becoming the most expensive, most defensible, and most *human* it has ever been. The founders losing money saw content costs drop and bought **more volume**. The winners saw the same drop and bought **more veracity**.

The strategic question is no longer "should I use AI?" It is: **"Which half of my funnel am I accidentally letting AI cannibalize?"**

### Track A — Macro Rising Trends (founder-relevant, datable)

1. **The Great Bifurcation: vertical agents win, horizontal "autonomous employee" agents get rolled back** `[VERIFIED]` — Industry-specific agents growing ~62.7% CAGR (≈2× horizontal), holding 40–50× ARR multiples; 74% of horizontal comms-agent deployments were rolled back. *(agentmarketcap.ai 04-05; aigovernance.com 06-05)*
2. **"Agent washing" is the term of the year** `[VERIFIED]` — Gartner: only ~130 of thousands of "agentic" vendors are real; an analysis of 847 deployments found 76% never reached production. **Most ownable contrarian wedge available.** *(particula.tech 03-11; Gravity AI 06-01)*
3. **Outcome-based / "fractional-FTE" pricing** `[VERIFIED]` — Buyers now demand ROI framing ("2 hrs → 5 min"), not intelligence claims. *(Insight Partners 04-27)*
4. **"Applications of AI / AI-as-infrastructure" is underhyped; "autonomy" is overhyped** `[VERIFIED/LIKELY]` — Karpathy: functional agents "still ~a decade away." Smart-money reframe: the unit of automation is the **workflow**, not the autonomous worker. *(Alex Lieberman poll; theagentarchitect)*
5. **The buyer shifted: line-of-business leaders now out-buy IT** `[VERIFIED]` — LOB (46%) > CIO (38%); 70% want to self-serve trial first. GTM rewrite: sell to the operator with the pain. *(Mayfield CXO Survey 01-28)*
6. **Platform land-grab** `[VERIFIED]` — Microsoft Build (Windows as agent OS), Google I/O ("agentic Gemini," always-on Spark), OpenAI redesigning ChatGPT into an agent/services + Ads platform. Build on a platform; don't try to be one. *(aitoolsrecap 05-31; mean.ceo 06-04)*
7. **"Token maxing" — the silent budget killer** `[LIKELY]` — Sophisticated enterprises running 1,000+ agents "consume annual AI budgets in 90 days." The quiet counter-narrative to efficiency headlines. *(Insight Partners 04-27)*

### Track B — Wellness/Performance Intersection (vertical-specific)

1. **AI is the new shelf in supplements — and revenue doesn't buy the shelf** `[VERIFIED]` — Across 3,800 supplement prompts (Jan–Apr 2026), Thorne/Seed/AG1/Momentous captured >47% of AI citation share; AG1 (category's largest by revenue) ranked *third*. Win condition: machine-readable structure-function/dosing data, not marketing copy. *(5W AI Visibility Index 05-14)*
2. **Incumbents normalized $10/mo AI coaching** `[VERIFIED]` — MyFitnessPal "AI Coach" (06-10); Google rebranded Fitbit → "Google Health" + Gemini "Health Coach" at $9.99/mo (05-19). A founder's human-coaching premium must now justify itself against a $10 baseline.
3. **Purpose-built AI agents now target wellness brands by name** `[VERIFIED]` — Olivia AI (DTC design agent "trained on supplement/wellness brands," lists Ritual/Seed/Thorne) compresses a SKU launch from 4–8 weeks → one session. The agency layer is being disintermediated. *(tryolivia.ai; WellnessLiving 04-29)*
4. **Capital concentrating in "famous-human + AI engine" coach startups** `[VERIFIED]` — Kotcha (Kipchoge), Zing Coach (Palta), Fountain Life (Diamandis/Robbins, $18M Series B). The pattern: AI doesn't kill the trust premium — it gets *wrapped around* a human authority. *(Athletech 12-01)*
5. **The "Authenticity Recession" is now a named market force** `[VERIFIED]` — Ogilvy 2026 predictions: as AI floods marketing with "scalable perfection," human imperfection becomes premium; KPI shifts from content *volume* → content *veracity.* Bites hardest in trust-dependent wellness. *(Ogilvy 01-25; AdPulse 02-23)*
6. **Regulatory friction hardening fast** `[VERIFIED]` — FDA updated General Wellness + Clinical Decision Support guidance (01-06); EU AI Act Art. 50 AI-disclosure enforceable **Aug 2, 2026** (penalties to €15M / 3% turnover); AI-persona supplement promotion needs *double* disclosure. AI scale multiplies legal exposure in a health-claim category. *(Dr. Wallace 01-21; OmniGems 05-07)*

## Phase 3 — Shadow Market Assessment (Samuel Thompson logic)

**Definition:** Shadow Market = High Desperation + Low Competition Quality.

**VERDICT: SHADOW MARKET CONFIRMED — "AI-assisted but trust-defensible."**

- **Desperation: HIGH.** Every wedge of the beachhead (wellness brands, fitness coaches, agencies, consultants) is hitting the *same* wall simultaneously — they can produce AI content fast but have no defensible way to *prove the human is still in it* to clients, to skeptical buyers, and to the AI engines that now decide who gets cited.
- **Competition quality: LOW.** The market is **flooded** with "use AI to go faster" advice and **starved** of "use AI without it costing you trust, voice, or AI-citation visibility." The top answers to the load-bearing questions are folk remedies and influencer think-pieces — no canonical method, no signature framework.
- **The unclaimed center:** Nobody is connecting the *disclosure/voice-trust* problem (creator-coach pain) to the *AEO/AI-citation visibility* problem (wellness-brand pain). They are the **same problem** — being legibly human in an AI-mediated funnel — and whoever names it owns it.

**Confidence note on the wedge:** The voice/authenticity pain is *loud but partly performative* (free Reddit venting, a saturated "lose your voice" creator genre — verify willingness-to-pay). The **harder-evidenced, higher-budget** pains are: agent-reliability backlash, wellness **AI-citation visibility**, BCG tool-overload, and the disclosure expectation gap. Anchor paid offers on those; use the voice-drift language as the emotional hook.

## Phase 4 — Synthesis

### Top 3 Rising Trends to Ride
1. **Agent washing → the vertical, scoped, outcome-proven reality** (most ownable contrarian wedge; datable Gartner + 76%-fail stats).
2. **The Authenticity Recession / Funnel Bifurcation** (the strategic spine — automate discovery, humanize conversion).
3. **AI Visibility / "the new shelf"** (get cited by the engines or vanish — concrete, testable, founder-actionable).

### Suggested Attack Angle (the content wedge)
**"AI-assisted, trust-defensible."** Fuse the two strongest verticals: *voice-drift / client-trust* (coach-creator) + *AI-citation visibility* (wellness brand). Everyone teaches "go faster." Nobody teaches **"go faster without going invisible or going fake."** That's the entire wedge.

- **Lead hook candidate:** the literal Reddit line — **"Who actually wrote this?"** (their language, load-bearing, indexes the exact fear).
- **Disarm → deliver structure:** open with Karpathy "a decade away" / agent-washing to disarm the hype-fatigued reader, then deliver the constructive bifurcation playbook so the B2B buyer leaves with a checklist.

### Signature Offer the Content Should Point To
A **"Funnel Bifurcation Audit"** for any wellness/performance brand:
1. **Top of funnel — *Are you AI-visible?*** Test the brand's top ~20 category prompts across ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini; score citation presence (5W methodology, replicable free).
2. **Bottom of funnel — *Is your trust layer visibly human?*** Inventory every place a buyer can see a named human, real practitioner, documented claim.
The thesis writes the diagnosis: **most founders are over-invested in exactly the wrong half.**

### Counter-Reads (intellectual honesty for the content)
- **The bifurcation could collapse** if personalized AI agents (with bloodwork/sleep access) become the trusted buying intermediary — then being the brand the *agent* recommends beats human-conversion proof. Watch agent-mediated commerce as the leading indicator.
- **ROI data is contradictory** (80% positive vs. 95%-zero-return vs. 23% agent-specific). Optimistic figures trace to vendor-adjacent reports. The *disillusionment* thesis is better-sourced than the *boom* thesis.
- **"Authenticity Recession" is partly agency self-interest** (Ogilvy sells human craft). Directionally true; pair with first-party data before betting a client's strategy.
