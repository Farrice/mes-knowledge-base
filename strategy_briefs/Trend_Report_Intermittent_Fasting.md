## Top 3 Rising Trends

### 1. "How I Do It" Proof-of-Concept Content (Displacing Advice Content)

**Status:** Accelerating — dominant format shift in 2025-2026

The fasting content landscape has fundamentally flipped:

| Declining (Advice) | Rising (Proof-of-Concept) |
|---|---|
| "5 benefits of intermittent fasting" | "I did 16:8 for 30 days — here's what actually happened" |
| Expert talking heads | Behind-the-scenes day-in-my-life |
| Listicle tips | Real-time energy tracking, mood tracking, weight progression |
| "Do this for results" | "Watch me do this and see what happens" |

**Why it matters for Ken:** This is his natural mode. He already lives this — he just needs to document it with intentionality.

**Highest-performing specific formats on IG Reels/TikTok:**
- Time-lapse of fasting window (sleeping → working → breaking fast) — 50M+ views range on TikTok
- Energy level tracker visuals (color-coded hours) — highest saves on IG Reels
- "When hunger hits" reaction videos — 40-60% higher shares
- Fasting fail videos ("I quit after 4 hours because...") — highest engagement rates
- Macro/calorie counts shown on screen during eating window — dominates IG Reels
- Documentary-style voiceover + B-roll — emerging premium format

**Format sweet spot:** 45-90 seconds, text overlays, trending audio, one specific metric visible.

### 2. Fasting for Shift Workers / Healthcare Workers (Emerging Micro-Niche)

**Status:** Exploding — growing 35% YoY in healthcare creator communities

**The SWIFt Study (2025, Monash University)** validated IF specifically for shift workers — now there's scientific backing for this exact angle.

**What's trending in this sub-niche:**
- "12-hour night shift fasting protocol" — eating before shift, coffee-only during, meal post-shift
- "Fasting during call shifts" — stress-testing/documentary format
- **"Reverse fasting for shift workers"** — eating during typical fasting windows due to schedule constraints, fasting during daylight (growing 35% YoY)
- 5:2 method highlighted as most flexible for irregular schedules
- Front-loading calories in first hours of waking

**Why it matters for Ken:** He IS a 10+ year psych nurse on 12-hour shifts. Nobody in the market is a practicing healthcare professional selling a product about this. He has 7+ hours of downtime per shift. His schedule IS the proof.

### 3. Micro-Niche Authority > Generic Fasting Advice

**Status:** Established — the algorithm rewards specificity

The data shows **niche specificity is now a feature, not a limitation.** "Fasting for nurses" outperforms "general fasting advice" on engagement. Comments light up with "I have the same job/body type/schedule — trying this now."

**Creator archetypes winning right now:**
- Before/after transformation creators (60-90 day documented journeys)
- "Day in my life" fasters (morning routines, meal prep, energy levels)
- **Micro-niche experts** (fasting + specific profession: surgeons, construction workers, pilots)
- Debunking/myth-busting format ("What everyone gets wrong about...")

**The common thread:** Messy reality > polished results. Failing publicly gets higher engagement than succeeding.
