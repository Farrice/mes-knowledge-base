# /founder-voice — Founder Go-Direct Positioning Engine

Transform a founder/CEO from invisible and PR-managed into magnetically interesting. Mines their original ideas, strips old-media conditioning via unscripting protocol, calibrates controversy levels, and produces a 30-day go-direct launch plan with first content pieces outlined.

**When to use**: A founder is letting a PR firm speak for them. Their competitor's CEO has a podcast and is winning mindshare. They have original ideas but have been trained to suppress them. Any founder positioning or thought leadership project.

## Usage

```
/founder-voice [founder name] [company]
/founder-voice "Jane Smith" "Acme Corp" --industry "cybersecurity" --competitors "CrowdStrike CEO, Palo Alto CEO"
```

## Steps

### 1. Load Skills
Read these files:
1. `skills/andreessen-horowitz-new-media/SKILL.md`
2. `skills/andreessen-horowitz-new-media/genius.md`
3. `skills/andreessen-horowitz-new-media/workflows/04-founder-go-direct.md`

### 2. Gather Founder Intelligence
Ask if not provided:
- What's the founding story? Why did they start this company?
- What do they say privately about their industry that they'd never say publicly?
- What are their 3 strongest opinions?
- What's their current media footprint? (If any)
- Who are the competitor CEOs with public presence?

### 3. Execute Workflow
Follow every step in `04-founder-go-direct.md`:
- Step 1: Joe Rogan Test (current score + diagnosis)
- Step 2: Original Idea Inventory (mine 3-5 ideas from founding insight and industry beliefs)
- Step 3: Unscripting Protocol (old-media kill list + new-media replacements)
- Step 4: Controversy Calibration (consensus → mild → strong → third rail, with context packages)
- Step 5: 30-Day Go-Direct Launch Plan (week by week)

### 4. Draft First Content Pieces
Outline the first 3 pieces of content:
1. **Substack/Essay**: Full-length version of the #1 original idea
2. **Podcast Episode**: Guest appearance plan OR first episode of founder's own show
3. **Tweet Thread**: Oral-culture extraction of the most provocative insight

### 5. Optional: Cross-Reference with Grace Andrews
If building for Farrice or a brand with existing content:
- Load `skills/grace-andrews-media-company/SKILL.md` (Tier 0 card only)
- Map founder positioning into city metaphor: where does the founder's voice fit in the content city?

### 6. Save Output
Save to `research_outputs/[date]-founder-voice-[name-slug].md`
