# /oren-performer-architect

Build the **Performer Content Architecture** — entertainment-driven brand content.

## Instructions
1. Load `skills/oren-brand-archetypes/genius.md` — Patterns 3 (Performer section), 6, 9 (Show Question)
2. Prerequisite: Archetype diagnostic must have selected PERFORMER

## Context
Performer brands drive affinity through entertainment. The product is OMNIPRESENT but never pitched. Funnel mechanic: Entertainment → brand affinity → omnipresent recognition → purchase.

**Canonical exemplars**: Duer Shoots (fashion skits — clothes always on), Mohawk Chevrolet (The Office spinoff at dealership), LC Sign (skit-to-promotion pipeline).

## Process

### Step 1: The Show Concept (Pattern 9)
Answer the qualifying question with high specificity:
> "If our brand had a show, what would it be? What would it be about? Would people watch it without knowing it was ours?"

Develop the show concept:
- **Format**: Skit series? Character-driven? Documentary-style? Compilation?
- **Premise**: What's the hook that makes non-customers watch?
- **Setting**: Where does the show live? (Must embed product naturally)
- **Characters**: Who are the recurring characters?
- **Tone**: Comedy? Drama? Absurdist? Slice-of-life?

**Kill switch**: If the brand can't answer this compellingly → redirect to Oracle or Catalyst. Don't force entertainment without a narrative engine.

### Step 2: Omnipresence Audit (Pattern 6)
Design the product embedding strategy:
- Product appears in EVERY frame without being discussed
- The product is architecture, not advertisement
- **The test**: A viewer watches 10 posts and knows what the brand sells without ever being explicitly told

Map how the product naturally appears:
| Content Type | How Product Appears | Visibility Level |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Main series | [Specify] | Always visible |
| One-off content | [Specify] | Present but secondary |
| Community content | [Specify] | Implied |

### Step 3: Episode Bible
Build the production framework:
- **Episode 1-5 concepts** with one-line descriptions
- **Recurring elements** (characters, catchphrases, settings)
- **Production specs** (iPhone or camera? How many people? Editing style?)
- **Posting cadence** (how often? What platform first?)

### Step 4: Promotion-to-Entertainment Ratio
Design the skit-to-promotion pipeline (LC Sign model):
- **80%+ entertainment** — pure show content
- **≤20% promotion** — direct product content (launches, sales)
- The promotion content performs BECAUSE the entertainment content built the affinity

## Produces
Show Concept + Omnipresence Strategy + Episode Bible (5+ episodes) + Production Specs
