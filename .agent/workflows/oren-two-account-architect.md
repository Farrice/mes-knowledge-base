# /oren-two-account-architect

Architects the two-account method where a founder's personal account drives top-of-funnel attention and the brand account captures conversion intent. "In this two-account method, you're able to post your typical content on main... but then his social media is the real driver."

## Usage
```
/oren-two-account-architect [brand name] [founder name]
```

## Prerequisites
- Load `genius.md` Pattern 4
- Archetype should be Oracle (primary use case) — works for Catalyst too
- Founder must be willing to create personal content

## Steps

### 1. Role Definition

Define what each account DOES and DOESN'T do:

**Founder Account**:
- ✅ Expert opinions, hot takes, trend analysis
- ✅ Behind-the-scenes of thinking process
- ✅ Industry history and education (wide reach)
- ✅ Personal POV on industry developments
- ✅ Engagement with other creators/experts
- ❌ Direct product promotion
- ❌ Corporate messaging
- ❌ Polished brand content

**Brand Account**:
- ✅ Product showcases, launches, carousels
- ✅ Curated/polished brand content
- ✅ Customer testimonials and case studies
- ✅ Company news and updates
- ✅ Reposting/amplifying founder content
- ❌ Hot takes or controversial opinions
- ❌ Unpolished personal content
- ❌ Competing with founder for top-of-funnel

### 2. Content Split Matrix

Design the weekly content rhythm for BOTH accounts:

| Day | Founder Account | Brand Account |
|-----|----------------|---------------|
| Mon | Expert opinion / hot take | Product feature spotlight |
| Tue | Industry history / education | Customer story / testimonial |
| Wed | Trend analysis / prediction | Behind-the-brand process |
| Thu | Personal POV / reaction | Product comparison / education |
| Fri | Engagement / collaboration | Community highlight / UGC |
| Sat | — | Curated inspiration |
| Sun | Reflection / personal story | — |

Adapt rhythm to actual posting capacity. Minimum viable: founder 3x/week, brand 3x/week.

### 3. Cross-Pollination System

Design how the two accounts feed each other:

**Founder → Brand flows**:
- Founder creates educational content → Brand reposts with product tie-in
- Founder builds audience → CTA to brand account for "the products behind the expertise"
- Founder engages in industry conversations → Brand credibility increases by association

**Brand → Founder flows**:
- Brand launches product → Founder creates "why I made this" content
- Brand receives customer feedback → Founder responds with expert context
- Brand hits milestone → Founder shares personal meaning

**Shared content moments**:
- Product launches (founder does the story, brand does the specs)
- Industry events (founder does live commentary, brand does curated highlights)
- Milestones (coordinated celebration from both perspectives)

### 4. Platform-Specific Implementation

| Platform | Founder Strategy | Brand Strategy |
|----------|-----------------|----------------|
| **LinkedIn** | Thought leadership posts, article repurposing, comment engagement | Company page, employee advocacy, product updates |
| **Instagram** | Stories (raw), Reels (expert takes), carousel (education) | Feed (polished), Reels (product), Stories (BTS) |
| **TikTok** | Talking-head expert content, trend reactions | Product-focused, process videos, UGC amplification |
| **YouTube** | Long-form educational, podcast clips | Product reviews, tutorials, brand films |
| **X/Twitter** | Hot takes, threads, engagement | Company news, product updates, support |

### 5. Metrics Split

**Founder account success metrics**:
- Reach / impressions (top-of-funnel driver)
- Follower growth rate
- Engagement rate on expert content
- Click-throughs to brand account/website
- Target: 3-5x the reach of brand account

**Brand account success metrics**:
- Conversion rate (clicks → purchases/inquiries)
- Product launch engagement
- Customer acquisition from social
- Target: Higher conversion metrics, lower reach (this is expected)

**Cross-pollination metric**:
- % of brand account followers who also follow founder
- % of purchases attributable to founder content journey
