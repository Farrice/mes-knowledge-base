# /gw-20-percent

The 20% targeting ritual — dump the full plate, run the brutal star test ("does this drive 80% of the results?"), name the ONE priority, and sort starred items into AI-use-case vs human-only.

## Trigger
`/gw-20-percent`

## Workflow
`skills/geoff-woods-ai-thought-partner/workflows/03-twenty-percent.md`

## Quick Use
Provide:
1. Your full plate this week, dumped unfiltered (pen and paper is the intended front half)
2. What "results" means here — the outcome the 20% is measured against
3. Your 2-3 genuine strengths (optional, sharpens the human-vs-AI split)

## Output
An analog-first ritual: full plate inventory, a star test held to the brutal 80% bar, the single number-one priority named with everything sequenced behind it, and each starred item sorted AI vs human-only via the 50%-curveball rule ("if AI is 50% as good as you now, start handing it over").

## Stacks With
→ `/gw-crit` (build a CRIT for a 20% item you're handing to a thought partner)
→ `/gw-thought-partner` (run a full session on the ONE)
→ `/gw-feedback-loop` (elevate whatever the AI produces on a handed-over item)
