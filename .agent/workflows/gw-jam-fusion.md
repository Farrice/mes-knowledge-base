# /gw-jam-fusion

A co-created taste-bearing deliverable — Woods' interview inversion raises the floor of BOTH takes before divergence, then the /jam gut-verdict loop replaces "accepting the first answer."

## Trigger
`/gw-jam-fusion`

## Workflow
`skills/geoff-woods-ai-thought-partner/workflows/10-jam-fusion.md`

## Quick Use
Provide:
1. The deliverable in play (post / concept / naming / strategy frame / design direction) + any draft or spark
2. Why it matters — is it a 20% priority or an 80% "better email"?
3. Who it's for / what it must do
4. Whether it's in Farrice's own voice (routes a voice layer before drafting)

## Output
Interview transcript (3-5 questions, one at a time) → raised brief → Take A / Take B on that brief → gut-verdict cuts → feedback triad on the winner → optional Challenger pass → ship-ready deliverable.
