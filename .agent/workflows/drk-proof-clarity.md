# Dr. K × Luke Iha — Proof-from-Clarity Engine

Process emotional resistance in prospects/clients before deploying persuasion. Proof only works on clear minds — samskara-clouded prospects reject even perfect evidence.

## Why This Stack Works

Luke Iha's proof ladder is the best persuasion architecture in the system. But proof bounces off people operating from reactive emotion. Dr. K processes the emotional charge first. Then the proof lands on clarity instead of resistance.

## Steps

1. **Load Dr. K**: `skills/dr-k-consciousness/genius.md`
2. **Load Luke Iha**: `skills/luke-iha-copywriting/genius.md`
3. **Phase 1 — EMOTIONAL OBJECTION SCAN (Dr. K)**:
   - What emotional state is the prospect in? (guna check)
   - What samskara is driving their objection? (past bad experience? identity threat?)
   - Are they fused with the objection (can't step back from it)?
   - What would processing look like for this specific resistance?
4. **Phase 2 — EMOTIONAL PROCESSING DESIGN (Dr. K)**:
   - Design copy/content that creates observing-mind distance
   - Use validation-first framework: name their emotional experience accurately
   - Build "sitting with" moments into the sales architecture
   - Get them to the clarity state where evidence can be received
5. **Phase 3 — PROOF DEPLOYMENT (Luke Iha)**:
   - Deploy proof ladder AFTER emotional processing section
   - Match proof type to the original emotional objection
   - Stack proof: results → method → credibility → social → personal
   - Braid proof through narrative (not dump at the end)
6. **Phase 4 — INTEGRATION**:
   - Check: Does the copy flow from processing → clarity → proof → action?
   - Validate: Would Dr. K approve the emotional handling?
   - Validate: Would Luke approve the proof architecture?
7. Output: Proof-from-Clarity Copy Architecture (can be used for VSLs, emails, landing pages)
8. Run quality gate against both rubrics
