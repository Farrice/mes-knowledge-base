1. **Belief Excavation** — Map belief types, processing chains, emotional sediment, metacognitive profile
2. **ELM Route Diagnosis** — Central vs peripheral processing, rebuttal type prediction
3. **Threshold Calculation** — Social death scores, 30% calibration, binding constraint identification
4. **Consumer Posture Translation** — Belief-enriched Dai Media 3D posture with individual finder
5. **Messaging Architecture** — Route-matched, accommodation-designed, rebuttal-defended message strategy

## Integration Points

## When to Use

- Entering a new market with unexplained resistance
- Content gets agreement but no conversion (assimilation problem)
- ICP research feels shallow — you know WHAT they want but not WHY they won't act
- Launching paradigm-shifting offers requiring genuine belief change
