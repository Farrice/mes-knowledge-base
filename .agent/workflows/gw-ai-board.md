# /gw-ai-board

A deployable AI board of advisors, built Geoff Woods' way: CRIT on yourself, interview to surface unconscious gaps, world-class personas with permission rules, and your future self as the most valuable seat.

## Trigger
`/gw-ai-board`

## Workflow path
`skills/geoff-woods-ai-thought-partner/workflows/05-ai-board.md`

## Quick Use
Provide:
1. The operator + the seat where the buck stops
2. 10-year vision + business plan (+ culture docs if you have them)
3. Self-assessed strengths and weaknesses, flagged "just the ones I'm aware of"

## Output
A board spec in markdown: gap map, derived-and-culled skills, casting table (skill → world-class exemplar), advisor personas with can/cannot permission rules, a future-self seat, and a deploy note — loadable as a project or split into agents.

## Stacks With
→ `/gw-persona-flip` (run any board member as a challenger against a live deliverable)
→ `/gw-simulate-room` (build stakeholder personas the same interview way)
→ `/gw-crit` (the CRIT engine underneath the board build)
