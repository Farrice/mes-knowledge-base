# /oren-helper-architect

Designs the complete Helper archetype content ecosystem. Helper brands are PRACTICAL — they offer useful, relatable content that solves specific problems. Content is shared because it WORKS, not because it inspires. The key distinction from Catalyst: Helper is realistic ("here's how to fix this"), Catalyst is aspirational ("here's who you could become").

"What are little helpful things we can do that our customer will appreciate?"

## Usage
```
/oren-helper-architect [brand name]
```

## Prerequisites
- Load `genius.md` Pattern 3 (Helper section)
- Archetype must be confirmed as Helper via `/oren-archetype-diagnostic`

## Steps

### 1. Map the Utility Landscape

Identify every practical problem the brand can solve for its audience:

| Category | Problems They Have | Content Solutions |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Selection | "Which one should I pick?" | Comparison content, side-by-side reviews |
| Process | "How do I do this?" | Visual tutorials, step-by-step walkthroughs |
| Decision | "Is this worth it?" | Honest assessments, price breakdowns |
| Maintenance | "How do I keep this working?" | Care guides, troubleshooting content |
| Discovery | "What don't I know I don't know?" | Curated lists, insider knowledge |

The goal: map 15-20 specific problems the brand's audience faces that the brand is uniquely positioned to address.

### 2. Design the Utility Content Matrix

| Content Type | Problem Category | Format | Frequency | Example |
|-------------|-----------------|--------|-----------|---------| 
| Comparison content | Selection | Carousel, short video, split-screen | 2x/week | "Product A vs Product B for [specific use case]" |
| Visual tutorials | Process | Reels, Stories, step-by-step carousel | 2x/week | "How to [specific task] in under 60 seconds" |
| Curated lists | Discovery | Carousel, thread, long-form | 1x/week | "7 things I wish I knew about [topic]" |
| Story vlogs | Process + Discovery | Stories, raw video | 3x/week | "Testing [product] for a week — honest take" |
| Interview clips | Discovery | Reels, video clips | 2x/month | "I asked [expert] about [common question]" |
| User-generated tips | Process | Reposts, compilation | 1x/week | Community-sourced solutions |
| Product integration | All | Subtle placement within utility content | As needed | Product visible, never pitched |

### 3. The Relatable Host Framework

Helper content doesn't require expertise — it requires RELATABILITY. The host is "on the journey with you," not lecturing from above.

**Host characteristics to select for**:
- Genuinely curious (asks questions the audience would ask)
- Not afraid to say "I didn't know this either"
- Comfortable showing process, including mistakes
- Personality people want to spend time with (the Kiyoko Beauty, HashiHome model)

**Host screening questions**:
1. Can this person explain something they just learned without sounding like a textbook?
2. Would the audience want to watch them even if they weren't teaching anything?
3. Can they be wrong on camera without it damaging the brand?

**Multi-creator option** (the Kiyoko Beauty model): Use MULTIPLE relatable creators, each covering a different problem domain. This scales content production and allows format variety without diluting the Helper archetype.

### 4. The Organic-to-Paid Bridge

The Helper archetype's hidden superpower: informational content makes PAID ADS perform better through ambient brand recognition.

**Bridge mechanics**:
1. **Organic helper content** establishes the brand as trustworthy, knowledgeable, and useful
2. **When someone sees a paid ad** from the same brand, existing trust reduces friction
3. **CPA drops** because the paid ad isn't fighting for credibility — the organic content already built it

"Just being that entry point, helping reduce the money or the barrier to entry it requires when you're advertising is a massive value add."

**Measurement framework**:
| Metric | What It Measures | Target |
|--------|-----------------|--------|
| Branded search volume | Ambient awareness from organic | +20% over 90 days |
| Paid ad CPA trend | Trust reduction from organic helper content | -15% over 90 days |
| Save rate (organic) | Content utility signal | Top 10% vs. category average |
| Time-to-first-conversion (new audiences) | Trust velocity | Decreasing quarter over quarter |

### 5. The Niche Audience Math

Helper content often produces SMALL but highly VALUABLE audiences. Don't judge by vanity metrics:

**Audience Value Calculation**:
```
Audience Value = (Audience Size) × (Purchase Probability) × (Average Order Value) × (Lifetime Multiplier)

Example: 2,000 engaged followers × 5% conversion × $500 AOV × 3x lifetime = $150,000 pipeline
vs. 100,000 passive followers × 0.1% conversion × $50 AOV × 1x = $5,000 pipeline
```

Use this math to justify Helper strategy to leadership that only understands follower counts.

### 6. Helper vs. Catalyst Calibration

Run this diagnostic to confirm you're in Helper territory, not Catalyst:

| Question | Helper Answer | Catalyst Answer |
|----------|--------------|-----------------|
| Why does someone share this? | "This solves my problem" | "I'm becoming better" |
| What emotion does it trigger? | Gratitude, relief | Aspiration, belonging |
| Does it require expertise from host? | No — relatable "on the journey" voice | Not necessarily — multiple voices work |
| What's the brand's role? | Practical guide | Bridge/enabler |
| How does it monetize? | Tools, templates, products, paid ad bridge | Programs, community, challenges |

If answers lean Catalyst → redirect to `/oren-catalyst-architect`.
