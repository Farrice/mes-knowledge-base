# /ctm-to-content

Put the map to work: content. Turn a finished map's pains, jobs, and gaps into 15 content ideas that each carry the specific quote they're built on — no idea invented "on a slow Tuesday" — then outline one long-form piece from the single widest gap and hand off. The discipline is one quote per idea, no exceptions.

## Trigger
`/ctm-to-content`

## Workflow
`skills/customer-truth-map/workflows/ctm-to-content.md`

## Quick Use
Provide a finished map + a gap-width-ranked `/ctm-gaps` table + the channel(s). Runs prompt P8. Lean on flagged vivid/repeated quotes and ⚠ WORKAROUND lines.

## Pipeline
1 Generate 15 grounded ideas, each with its source quote + format → 2 Outline the widest-gap long-form (name it in their words → validate the current-fix failure → point to a better way) → 3 Hand off (do not finish here)

## Output
The 15-idea bank (each paired with its harvested source quote + format) + the three-beat widest-gap outline + the explicit handoff line. Does NOT produce the published piece.

## Stacks With
→ finishing engines: `/novelty-forge` (held belief + real language), `/parallax` (Substack), `/diandra-*` (hooks only)
→ upstream `/ctm-map`, `/ctm-jobs`, `/ctm-gaps` · fact-verifier for claims
