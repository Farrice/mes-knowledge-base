# /oren-boring-industry-excavation

"I don't think anything is too boring. There's a story behind every screw, every bolt, every welding piece." — Oren

## Usage
```
/oren-boring-industry-excavation [industry or brand]
```

## Prerequisites
- Load `genius.md` Pattern 5

## Steps

### 1. Diagnose the Real Problem

| Signal | Real Problem | Fix |
|--------|-------------|-----|
| "Nobody cares about what we do" | Haven't found niche audience | Niche Audience Math |
| "Our product isn't visual" | Thinking product-shots, not process-stories | Process Excavation |
| "Can't compete with lifestyle brands" | Comparing wrong — B2B is different | Value Math Reframe |
| "No one good on camera" | Resource gap, not boring-ness | Resource Audit |
| "Tried and nothing works" | Wrong content TYPE | Archetype Mismatch |

### 2. Interest Excavation Protocol (8 Questions)

#### Insider Fascination
1. What does an insider find fascinating that outsiders don't know?
2. What arguments happen between experts in this space?
3. What's the history behind something everyone takes for granted?
4. What process would blow someone's mind if they saw it?

#### Drama & Conflict
5. What goes wrong that people don't talk about?
6. What's the most expensive mistake your customers make?
7. What does your competitor do that drives you crazy?
8. What trend is coming that nobody is prepared for?

### 3. Process Excavation

Map the production/service process. Identify 6 moments:

1. **Precision Moment**: Where extreme accuracy is required
2. **Transformation Moment**: Raw → finished product
3. **Danger/Risk Moment**: What goes wrong if you're careless
4. **Scale Moment**: Where volume is impressive
5. **Human Moment**: Where human judgment still matters
6. **History Moment**: Origin story of a technique or tool

### 4. Watch Test

Score each angle:
- **10**: Would go viral beyond industry
- **7-9**: Attracts adjacent-industry interest
- **4-6**: Industry insiders love it
- **1-3**: Only your team watches

**Target**: 5+ angles at 7+, 15+ angles at 4+.

### 5. Value Math Reframe

For B2B: `2,000 engaged viewers × $[contract value] = pipeline worth more than 100K unqualified views × $0`.

### 6. Content Angle Assembly

Organize into archetype content types (Oracle top/mid/bottom or Helper tips/tutorials/recommendations).

### 7. Deliverable: Industry Content Goldmine

Compile: diagnosis, 15+ angles with scores, top 5 viral angles, process map, value math, recommended archetype, 30-day calendar.
