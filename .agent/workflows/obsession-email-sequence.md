# Obsession Email Sequence

Architect obsession-level email sequences using Kallaway's progressive framework.

## Execution

1. Load and execute `skills/kallaway-audience-obsession/workflows/obsession-email-sequence.md`
2. Map emails to obsession levels, script each with appropriate mechanics
3. Deliver complete sequence with architecture map and bankshot CTAs

## Required Inputs
- **Sequence purpose**: Welcome, launch, nurture, re-engagement, or post-purchase?
- **Target audience**: Who receives these emails?
- **Desired action**: What should they do after the sequence?
- **Sequence length** (optional): Defaults to 5 emails
- **Available proof**: Results, data, testimonials
