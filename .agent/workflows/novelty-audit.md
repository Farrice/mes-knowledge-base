# /novelty-audit

Diagnose why an existing piece isn't landing — scored against the 3 questions and 5 components.

## Trigger
`/novelty-audit`

## Workflow
`skills/kallaway-illusion-of-novelty/workflows/novelty-audit.md`

## Quick Use
Provide:
1. The existing piece of content
2. The intended audience + outcome (optional)

## Output
A compact scorecard (3 questions + 9-criterion rubric), the primary failure zone (hook/body/delivery), and a prioritized fix list routed to the repair workflows.

## Stacks With
→ `/novelty-reveal` `/novelty-contrast` `/novelty-urgency` `/novelty-proof` `/novelty-protect` (the routed fixes)
