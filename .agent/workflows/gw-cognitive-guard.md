# /gw-cognitive-guard

A cognitive-sovereignty audit plus a personalized thinking-time protocol — Geoff Woods' guard against letting AI replace your thinking instead of enhancing it.

## Trigger
`/gw-cognitive-guard`

## Workflow path
`skills/geoff-woods-ai-thought-partner/workflows/09-cognitive-guard.md`

## Quick Use
Provide:
1. A candid sample of how you actually use AI (answers-accepted, or interviews-run?)
2. A recent hard problem — and whether AI did the thinking or you did
3. The kind of thinking your role actually requires

## Output
Two self-test findings (who asks the questions / can you still fly without it), a usage-pattern map placing you on the dance task by task, the thinking-chair ritual (think analog first → sovereignty questions → then dump-and-interview), personalized guard rules with tripwires, and one decide-line for think-first vs. AI-first.
