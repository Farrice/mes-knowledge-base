# /oren-catalyst-architect

Designs the complete Catalyst archetype content ecosystem. Catalyst brands are BRIDGES — they help their audience become better versions of themselves. Content is aspirational, shared because it makes people FEEL like they're leveling up. This is fundamentally different from Helper (which is practical).

"How can our content be the bridge between who our customer is and who they want to be?"

## Usage
```
/oren-catalyst-architect [brand name]
```

## Prerequisites
- Load `genius.md` Pattern 3 (Catalyst section)
- Archetype must be confirmed as Catalyst via `/oren-archetype-diagnostic`

## Steps

### 1. Define the Aspiration Gap

Map the transformation the audience wants:

| Dimension | Current State ("I am...") | Desired State ("I want to be...") |
|-----------|--------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Identity | How they see themselves now | How they want to see themselves |
| Skill | What they can do now | What they want to be able to do |
| Status | How others perceive them now | How they want to be perceived |
| Lifestyle | How they live now | How they want to live |

The Catalyst brand positions itself as the BRIDGE between columns 2 and 3. Not the destination — the vehicle.

### 2. Design the Aspiration Ladder

Build a content progression that moves the audience UP the aspiration ladder:

**Rung 1: Recognition** ("I see myself in this")
- Content that names the audience's current state with empathy, not judgment
- "You've been doing [thing] and wondering why it's not working"
- Sharing mechanic: "This is SO me" → identity recognition → follow

**Rung 2: Possibility** ("I could actually do this")
- Content showing the transformation IS achievable
- Process documentation, training content, behind-the-scenes of growth
- Sharing mechanic: "I'm saving this" → aspiration bookmark → engage

**Rung 3: Community** ("I'm not alone in wanting this")
- Community highlights, challenges, shared milestones
- Curated content from the community (the Artifaxing model at scale)
- Sharing mechanic: "I belong here" → community identity → advocate

**Rung 4: Enablement** ("This brand helps me get there")
- The brand's product/service as the bridge tool
- Not "buy our thing" but "this is what helps people make the leap"
- Sharing mechanic: "This is how I'm doing it" → proof of transformation → convert

### 3. Content Type Matrix

| Content Type | Aspiration Rung | Format | Frequency |
|-------------|----------------|--------|-----------|
| Transformation stories | Recognition → Possibility | Video testimonial, carousel narrative | 2x/month |
| Training/process documentation | Possibility | Tutorial, screen-share, step-by-step | 2x/week |
| Inspirational challenges | Possibility → Community | Stories, Reels, challenge posts | 1x/week |
| Community highlights/curation | Community | Reposts, compilation, feature posts | 2x/week |
| Behind-the-scenes of growth | Possibility | Stories, vlog-style, raw footage | 3x/week |
| Expert guest content | Recognition | Interview clips, collaboration posts | 2x/month |
| Product-as-bridge content | Enablement | Subtle integration, not hard sell | 1x/week |

### 4. Community Engine Design

The Catalyst archetype's superpower is community. Design the flywheel:

1. **Seed**: Brand creates aspirational content
2. **Attract**: Audience that identifies with the aspiration follows
3. **Activate**: Challenge or prompt that gets audience creating/sharing
4. **Curate**: Brand highlights best community content
5. **Scale**: Community content becomes the brand's content → the Artifaxing model

**Community content flywheel target**: Within 6 months, 30%+ of brand content should be community-sourced or community-inspired.

### 5. Catalyst vs. Helper Calibration

Run this diagnostic to ensure you're in Catalyst territory, not Helper:

| Question | Catalyst Answer | Helper Answer |
|----------|----------------|---------------|
| Why does someone share this? | "I'm becoming better" | "This solves my problem" |
| What emotion does it trigger? | Aspiration, belonging | Gratitude, relief |
| Does it require expertise? | Not necessarily — multiple voices work | Relatable "on the journey" voice |
| What's the brand's role? | Bridge/enabler | Practical guide |
| How does it monetize? | Programs, community, challenges | Tools, templates, products |

If answers lean Helper → redirect to `/oren-helper-architect`.
