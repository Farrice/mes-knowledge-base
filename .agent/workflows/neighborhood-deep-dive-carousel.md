## When to Use

- Want to OWN a specific SFV neighborhood in search and saves
- Buyer asks "Should I look in X or Y?" — answer becomes a carousel
- Need authority content NOT tied to a specific listing's expiration

## Output

7-slide carousel:
1. Cover (contrarian or comparison hook)
2. Pain/Setup (buyer's stuck position)
3. Numbers (median price, YoY, DOM, rent, source)
4. Lifestyle Reality (4-6 named places, no generic adjectives)
5. FTHB Angle (program eligibility, inventory pattern, home types)
6. Honest Trade-Off (one real downside, named clearly)
7. Action (Save / Send / Schedule — pick one)
