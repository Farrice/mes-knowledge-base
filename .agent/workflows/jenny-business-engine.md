# /jenny-business-engine

A monetization roadmap for a channel or brand, staged by channel size, built on Jenny Hoyos' creator-economics playbook.

## Trigger
`/jenny-business-engine`

## Workflow
`skills/jenny-hoyos-shorts/workflows/10-creator-business-engine.md`

## Quick Use
Provide:
1. Channel/brand context (subscriber count, monthly views, niche, platform mix)
2. Audience profile (age band, buying power, what they already trust the creator for)
3. Current revenue (what's monetizing now, or none)
4. Goal + constraints (team size, time, legal/contract appetite)

## Output
Stage verdict (sub-scale/mid/scale), a derived revenue-mix target, sponsor architecture, stage-gated growth bets, a sequenced roadmap with gating metrics, and a claims ledger attributing every Hoyos figure.

→ `/jenny-lens-niche` (niche legibility feeds the sponsor-fit pitch)
→ `/jenny-longform-umbrella` (the shorts→long funnel feeds the roadmap)
