# Obsession Ladder Sprint

Architect a 3-5 piece content series using Kallaway's obsession level progression.

## Execution

1. Load and execute `skills/kallaway-audience-obsession/workflows/obsession-ladder-sprint.md`
2. Reverse-engineer from Level 3 action, map beliefs, design series
3. Produce all content pieces with architecture map

## Required Inputs
- **Core topic/theme**: What is the series about?
- **Target audience**: Who specifically?
- **Ultimate action**: What should they do after the full series?
- **Content format**: Video scripts, blog posts, emails, social posts, etc.
