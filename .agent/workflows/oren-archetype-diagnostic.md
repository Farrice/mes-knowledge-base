# /oren-archetype-diagnostic

Run the **Archetype Diagnostic** — the front door to all Oren brand strategy workflows.

## Instructions
1. Load `skills/oren-brand-archetypes/genius.md` — ALL patterns (this is the operating system)
2. Execute this workflow inline (no separate workflow file needed — the genius.md IS the methodology)

## Process

### Phase 1: Resource-Reality Audit (Pattern 1)
Ask the client/brand these 4 questions. Do NOT skip or abbreviate:
1. **Camera talent**: Who can be on camera? (Expert/founder vs. hired creator vs. nobody)
2. **Design capability**: Who can produce? (Internal designer, agency, budget for freelancer?)
3. **Showcasable assets**: What can you actually showcase? (Physical product, process, space, knowledge?)
4. **Acquirable resources**: What can you GET that you don't have?

Document every answer in a **Resource Inventory Table**.

### Phase 2: Archetype Elimination (Pattern 3 + Pattern 11)
Cross-reference resources against all 5 archetypes using the Decision Architecture (genius.md Pattern 3):

| Archetype | Requires | Risk Level | Eligible? |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Oracle** | Core expert on camera who can storytell | Safest | ✅/❌ |
| **Helper** | Relatable creator (not deep expert) | Low | ✅/❌ |
| **Catalyst** | Educational capacity + aspirational framing | Medium | ✅/❌ |
| **Performer** | Creative talent, visual product, risk tolerance | Higher | ✅/❌ |
| **World Builder** | Budget, creative director/team, high risk tolerance | Highest | ✅/❌ |

**Eliminate** any archetype the brand cannot execute. Do not allow aspirational selection (Pattern 1 anti-pattern).

### Phase 3: Qualifying Questions (Pattern 3)
For each remaining eligible archetype, run its qualifying question:
- **Oracle**: "What does our customer need to understand about our world that nobody else is explaining properly?"
- **Performer**: "If our brand had a show, what would it be? Would people watch it without knowing it was ours?" (Pattern 9 — The Show Question)
- **World Builder**: "Can we make something creative that people will love so much they love the brand right next to it?"
- **Catalyst**: "How can our content be the bridge between who our customer is and who they want to be?"
- **Helper**: "What are little helpful things we can do that our customer will appreciate?"

### Phase 4: Selection + Content Roadmap (Pattern 2 + Pattern 12)
1. Select ONE archetype — enforce Single-Archetype Discipline (Pattern 2)
2. Generate 15-20 content ideas from the selected archetype's content types
3. If Oracle with charismatic founder — recommend Two-Account Method (Pattern 4)
4. If client says "our industry is boring" — deploy Nothing Is Too Boring Protocol (Pattern 5)

### Phase 5: Exit Condition Verification (Pattern 12)
The deliverable is INCOMPLETE unless all four are present:
- ✅ Selected archetype (one)
- ✅ Resource inventory (what you have, don't have, can get)
- ✅ 15-20 content ideas from the archetype's content types
- ✅ Buy-in confirmation (ideas they're excited about, not just agreeing to)

## Produces
Archetype Match Report + Resource Inventory + Content Roadmap (15-20 ideas) + Funnel Mechanic Map
