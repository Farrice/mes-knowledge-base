# /novelty-contrast

Step 2 — position a new reveal against the audience's held belief to open the intrigue gap.

## Trigger
`/novelty-contrast`

## Workflow
`skills/kallaway-illusion-of-novelty/workflows/novelty-contrast.md`

## Quick Use
Provide:
1. The new reveal / angle
2. What the audience currently believes about this topic (the "old" belief)

## Output
The held-belief articulation + true-opposite contrast lines (5+ variants) + the explicit gap, with a confusion-vs-contrast check.

## Stacks With
→ `/novelty-reveal` (supplies the reveal)
→ `/mcraney-deep-canvass` + icp skills (supply the held belief)
→ `/novelty-hook` (fold the contrast into the opener)
