# `/diandra-rehook-teardown` — Re-Hook Teardown (productized service)

Takes posts that underperformed *despite good content* and proves, post by post, that the hook (not the insight) cost the reach — then rebuilds it. Output is a client-facing diagnostic, not a chat log. This is the engine behind the deployable Re-Hook Teardown offer.

## When to Use
- A lead magnet: tear down one of a prospect's flopped posts for free to open a conversation
- A paid audit: 10 posts + cross-post pattern diagnosis ($150–$400 tier)
- Your own back catalog: reclaim reach from posts you already paid to produce
- Client onboarding: the "before/after" artifact that proves you operate above "I'll write your posts"

## Usage
```
/diandra-rehook-teardown "[paste 1-10 full post texts]" --register [formal|informal] --client "[name]"
```
Ask for text, not screenshots. Include impressions/follower count per post if available (sharper diagnosis); never fabricate metrics.

## What It Does
1. **Loads**: `skills/diandra-escobar-linkedin-growth/genius.md` (Patterns 6, 19, 20, 21) + the format library, 40 rules, and 131-hook corpus
2. **Reads**: `workflows/21-rehook-teardown.md` + the offer/template in `references/rehook-teardown-kit.md`
3. **Per post**: hook autopsy (named failure mode) → finds the buried gap → 3 validated rebuilds across ≥2 formats → the pick
4. **Across posts**: the recurring failure + the blind-spot format + the ONE habit to change
5. **Delivers**: the client-facing report from the kit template + a soft next step
