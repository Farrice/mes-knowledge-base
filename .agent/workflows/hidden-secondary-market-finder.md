# /hidden-secondary-market-finder

> A thin discovery alias for Rory Sutherland's canonical differentiation strategy prompt.

## Quick Deploy

```text
Load: skills/rory-sutherland-marketing/genius.md
Prompt: skills/rory-sutherland-marketing/references/prompts-v2/the-differentiation-strategy-generator.md
```

## When to Use

- A sharply focused product attracts users outside its named primary audience.
- Accessibility, simplicity, restraint, or a specialist feature creates an adjacent benefit.
- You want to expose secondary demand without weakening the primary position.

## Input

- Primary audience and position to protect
- Focused feature, rule, or benefit
- Observed adjacent users, language, workarounds, or demand signals
- Markets that should not be pursued
- Brand, legal, safety, and capability constraints

## Alias Boundary

This command creates no new methodology. It routes to the Differentiation Strategy Generator. Adjacent demand must be marked `OBSERVED`, `REPORTED`, or `HYPOTHESIZED`; the primary position stays intact, and the secondary market is made discoverable through a proxy-safe route rather than a wholesale repositioning.

## Required Canonical Outputs

Return the primary-position preservation lock, adjacent-user evidence map, ranked secondary-market hypotheses, proxy-safe discovery language, and smallest demand test.
