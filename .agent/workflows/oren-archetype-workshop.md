# /oren-archetype-workshop

Run the **Archetype Workshop Facilitator** — the Exercise-as-Deliverable model for client sessions.

## Instructions
1. Load `skills/oren-brand-archetypes/genius.md` — focus on Patterns 3, 9, 1, 2, 12
2. Execute this facilitation protocol

## Context
This is NOT a presentation. It's a facilitated exercise (Pattern 9). The client generates the answers. You guide the discovery. "This format makes you look more professional and helps establish buy-in."

Critical for: younger employees convincing leadership. The archetype language maps to concepts "CMOs and executives are familiar with."

## Facilitation Flow

### Opening (5 min)
Frame the problem: "Your brand needs ONE clear role on social media. Not 5 content pillars. One strategic commitment that every piece of content serves."

Present the 5 archetypes with one-sentence descriptions:
1. **Oracle** — "We're the experts. We teach what no one else is explaining properly."
2. **Performer** — "We entertain. People watch us because we're fun, and our product is always there."
3. **World Builder** — "We create culture. People love what we make so much they love us."
4. **Catalyst** — "We inspire. We're the bridge between who you are and who you want to be."
5. **Helper** — "We help. We solve small problems that add up to real loyalty."

### Resource Audit (15 min) — Pattern 1
Run the 4-question resource audit WITH the client (not for them):
1. Who can be on camera?
2. Who can produce content?
3. What's showcasable?
4. What can you acquire?

Build the Resource Inventory together. Then cross-reference against archetype requirements.

**Elimination round**: Remove any archetype the resources can't support. Be direct. "You don't have an expert who can be on camera, so Oracle is off the table."

### Qualifying Questions (15 min) — Pattern 3
For remaining archetypes, ask each qualifying question. Let the CLIENT answer. Their energy tells you which one fits:
- High energy + specific answers = strong fit
- Vague, forced answers = wrong archetype

### Selection + Ideation (20 min) — Pattern 2
1. Lock ONE archetype
2. Set a timer: "In 10 minutes, generate as many content ideas as you can from this archetype"
3. Use the archetype's content types as prompts
4. Target: 15-20 ideas minimum
5. If "boring industry" objection surfaces → deploy Pattern 5 (Interest Excavation)

### Close (5 min) — Pattern 12
Verify all exit conditions:
- ✅ One archetype selected
- ✅ Resource inventory documented
- ✅ 15-20 content ideas generated
- ✅ Team is genuinely excited (not just compliant)

Complete Facilitated Workshop Deliverable + Content Idea Brainstorm + Client Buy-In
