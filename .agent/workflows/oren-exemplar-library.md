# /oren-exemplar-library

Build the **Archetype Exemplar Library** — real brand proof for every archetype.

## Instructions
1. Load `skills/oren-brand-archetypes/genius.md` — Pattern 3 (all exemplars)
2. Reference extraction artifact for Hall of Fame Exemplars section

## Context
Oren provides 3-5 real brand examples for EVERY archetype, across different verticals. He doesn't describe theory — he shows proof. This workflow builds the presentation-ready exemplar deck.

## Process

### Step 1: Core Exemplar Catalog
Start with the canonical exemplars from the extraction:

**Oracle**:
- Ken Sakata / Front Office (workwear — full 3-layer funnel)
- Blam Motorworks (Porsche restoration)
- Kreiss (interiors — two-account method with founder)
- Rarify (collectible furniture)

**Performer**:
- Duer Shoots (fashion skits — clothes always on, never pitched)
- Mohawk Chevrolet (The Office spinoff at a car dealership)
- LC Sign (skit-to-promotion pipeline)

**World Builder**:
- Death to Stock (visual culture commentary for stock photo platform)
- Cluey (narrative-driven office series — bad product, great content)
- Fern (fragrance short films)

**Catalyst**:
- Crease Group (manufacturing — boring industry proof)
- Artifaxing (community curation at scale)
- Base Living (lifestyle curation)

**Helper**:
- Kiyoko Beauty (Korean skincare comparisons, multiple creators)
- HashiHome / Certigrad (relatable host content)
- Marpipe (informational marketing for catalog ad SaaS)

### Step 2: Industry-Match Extension
For a specific client engagement, research 2-3 additional exemplars matched to the client's industry vertical. For each:
- Brand name + platform
- Which archetype they execute
- What makes their execution excellent
- Specific content examples to reference

### Step 3: Tactical Breakdown Per Exemplar
For each exemplar used in a presentation, document:
- **Content format** (what they post)
- **Posting cadence** (how often)
- **Production level** (iPhone? Studio? Mixed?)
- **Funnel mechanic** (how content converts)
- **Why it works** (the specific insight)

### Step 4: Anti-Exemplar
Always include the anti-exemplar: "The Everything-to-Everyone Brand" — educational Monday, entertainment Wednesday, motivational Friday, product Saturday. No archetype, no identity, no conversion.

## Produces
Industry-Matched Brand Exemplar Library + Tactical Breakdowns + Anti-Exemplar
