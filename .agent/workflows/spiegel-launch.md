# /spiegel-launch — Distribution Launch

> Launch with distribution pre-solved. Sequence the rollout so distribution advantage compounds from day one.

## When to Use
- Product/offer is built and ready to ship
- Distribution audit is complete (run `/spiegel-distribution` first)
- Planning a launch sequence
- Transitioning from launch to moat-building

## Inputs Required
1. Product/offer ready to ship
2. Distribution audit results (from `/spiegel-distribution`)
3. Seed audience (who are your first 50-100 users?)
4. Launch timeline and resources

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: Temporal Window Check
Re-validate the distribution window:
- Is the window still open? (platform shifts, market timing)
- How long until incumbents arrive?
- What's the urgency multiplier?

### Step 2: Close-Friends Seed Strategy
Apply GP-3 — Right people > most people:
1. Identify 10-50 "best friend equivalent" users — high-density connections
2. Onboard them personally (not at scale)
3. Design their first experience to be remarkable and shareable
4. Let THEM bring the next wave (organic pull > paid push)

### Step 3: Distribution-First Sequencing
Order launch activities by distribution impact:
1. **Week -2**: Seed the close-friends network, get testimonials/proof
2. **Week -1**: Create shareable moments from seed users (social proof)
3. **Week 0**: Launch to broader audience via primary distribution channel
4. **Week 1**: Double down on working channels, kill non-performing
5. **Week 2+**: Transition from launch mode to moat-building mode

### Step 4: Viral Coefficient Design
Engineer shareability into the product:
- What makes a user invite another user? (utility, status, necessity)
- What's the natural share moment? (output, achievement, collaboration)
- Can you make the product better when used with others? (network effect)

### Step 5: Post-Launch Moat Transition
Shift from "get users" to "keep users permanently":
- When does launch energy become moat energy?
- What ecosystem elements launch in Month 2?
- What switching costs accumulate from day one?

## Output Format
```
## DISTRIBUTION LAUNCH — [Product]
### Window Status: [Open/Closing]
### Seed Network: [10-50 names/profiles]
### Launch Sequence (Week-by-Week)
### Viral Coefficient Design
### Post-Launch Moat Timeline
### Recommended: /spiegel-moat at Week 4
```

## Quality Gate
- Seed network must have specific people identified, not "find influencers"
- Launch sequence must be distribution-first, not feature-first
- Must include post-launch moat transition plan
- Viral coefficient must be designed, not hoped for
