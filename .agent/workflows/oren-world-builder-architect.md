# /oren-world-builder-architect

Build the **World Builder Content Architecture** — the creative moonshot strategy.

## Instructions
1. Load `skills/oren-brand-archetypes/genius.md` — Patterns 3 (World Builder section), 6, 11
2. Prerequisite: Archetype diagnostic must have selected WORLD BUILDER

## Context
World Builder is the HIGHEST RISK archetype. "Is this an easy strategy? No. Is it one that makes them by far the best content of anyone they're competing with? Yes." Requires budget, creative team, and genuine risk tolerance.

Funnel mechanic: Cultural relevance → "they get us" affinity → organic brand love → purchase.

**Canonical exemplars**: Death to Stock (visual culture commentary for stock photo platform), Cluey (narrative-driven office series), Fern (fragrance short films).

## Process

### Step 1: Creative Vision Definition
Answer the qualifying question:
> "Can we make something creative that people will love so much they love the brand right next to it?"

Define the creative territory:
- **Cultural lane**: What aspect of culture does this brand inhabit? (Art, design, tech, craft, food, music?)
- **Content format**: Short films? Fictional series? Immersive intellectual content? Cultural curation?
- **Production ambition**: What production value is achievable AND sustainable?
- **The "they get us" test**: When someone encounters this content, what should they feel about the brand?

### Step 2: Risk Assessment (Pattern 11)
Be explicit about what World Builder requires:

| Risk Factor | Assessment |
|:---|:---|
| Budget commitment | Monthly content budget required: $_____ |
| Creative talent | Who's directing? In-house or agency? |
| Timeline to ROI | Months before measurable brand affinity: _____ |
| Failure tolerance | Can the brand survive 6 months of content without direct conversion? |
| Leadership buy-in | Is leadership prepared for content that doesn't mention the product? |

**If any risk factor is unacceptable** → redirect to a safer archetype. Do not let aspiration override capacity.

### Step 3: Immersive Content Series Plan
Design the first content series:
- **Series concept** (1-2 sentences)
- **Episode/piece count** (minimum 6 for series identity)
- **Production requirements** per piece
- **Distribution strategy** (platform priority, posting cadence)
- **Omnipresence embedding** (Pattern 6 — how does the brand appear without being featured?)

### Step 4: The "Bad Product, Great Content" Warning
Address the Cluey signal: "Their product objectively is bad. No one knows what Cluey does. Two people are buying from Cluey, but their social media is amazing."

World Builder content can create brand love WITHOUT product conversion if the product doesn't have legs. Audit:
- Does the product have genuine market fit?
- Will brand affinity from great content translate to purchase intent?
- Or is this content strategy a substitute for product-market fit?

### Step 5: Creative Brief
Produce a creative brief that the production team can execute from:
- Visual references (mood, tone, lighting, editing style)
- Narrative voice (who's "speaking"?)
- Brand integration rules (what's allowed vs. forbidden)
- Success metrics (NOT vanity — cultural penetration, brand recall, save rate)

## Produces
Creative Brief + Immersive Content Series Plan + Risk Assessment + Omnipresence Rules
