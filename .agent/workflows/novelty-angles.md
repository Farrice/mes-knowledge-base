# /novelty-angles

Generate 20+ distinct new angles on a saturated/boring topic — the "I'm out of ideas" engine.

## Trigger
`/novelty-angles`

## Workflow
`skills/kallaway-illusion-of-novelty/workflows/novelty-angles.md`

## Quick Use
Provide:
1. The saturated / boring topic
2. The audience + what they want (optional, sharpens scoring)

## Output
20+ angles across levers (new frame, name, update, finding, mechanism, contrast, outcome, audience, comparison, enemy, timeframe), each scored freshness × outcome-pull, ranked, top 5 flagged.

## Stacks With
→ `/novelty-reveal` (convert a chosen angle into a hook)
→ `/novelty-campaign` (deploy multiple angles as a campaign)

**Execution prompts**: before producing the deliverable, check `skills/kallaway-illusion-of-novelty/references/prompts-v2/` for the matching structure-pure prompt and honor its Output Contract (prompt-load sweep, 2026-07-21).
