# /gw-simulate-room

A pre-test of a real deliverable against simulated stakeholders — per-person predicted reactions, derail risks, and the exact three things to say — closed by a mandatory reality-calibration loop that diffs the simulation against what actually happened.

## Trigger
`/gw-simulate-room`

## Workflow path
`skills/geoff-woods-ai-thought-partner/workflows/07-simulate-room.md`

## Quick Use
Provide:
1. The actual artifact that faces the room (deck / proposal / pitch / post), in full
2. Who's in the room, by name and role
3. What you know about each person + the meeting's stakes

## Output
Triad-verified personality profiles per stakeholder, a room-dynamics map, a per-person prediction table (reaction → where it fires → derail risk), ranked speakable adjustments, and a mandatory calibration protocol that diffs simulation vs. reality after the meeting and edits the profiles so the next simulation is truer.

## Stacks With
→ `/gw-persona-flip` (harden the artifact before you simulate the room reading it)
→ `/gw-ai-board` (the profiles built here can seed a standing AI board)
→ `/gw-crit` (the interview + triad engine underneath the profiling)
