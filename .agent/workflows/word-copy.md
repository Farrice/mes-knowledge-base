# /word-copy

Build articulation muscles by studying master writers — analyze rhythm patterns, extract templates, and mirror-write originals using their structural DNA.

## Execution

1. **Read skill files:**
   - `skills/kallaway-word-mastery/genius.md` — Focus on The Copy-Speech Methodology
   - `skills/kallaway-word-mastery/workflows/copy-speech-exercise.md`

2. **Gather inputs:**
   - Model piece (URL, text, or title of a piece you admire)
   - Your topic (must be DIFFERENT from the model's domain)
   - What specifically draws you to this piece (rhythm? grip? tone? all?)

3. **Execute the Copy-Speech Exercise** end-to-end (all 6 steps)

4. **Deliver:**
   - Full model analysis with 5-dimension rhythm map
   - Extracted rhythm template (reusable)
   - Mirror-written original piece on your topic
   - Detachment audit

## Stacks With

- Stand-alone exercise for skill development
- Chain with `/word-sprint` to apply the learned rhythm to production content
