# /smith-natural-strategist-audit

Run the Natural Strategist Audit — Smith's meta-diagnostic that scores a business against all 4 daily questions and surfaces the highest-leverage strategic gap.

## Instructions

1. **Load** `skills/alex-m-smith-natural-strategy/genius.md` (full genius context)
2. **Execute** `skills/alex-m-smith-natural-strategy/workflows/01-natural-strategist-audit.md` (full workflow)

The skill carries:
- The 4 principles (different actions / value vs. problems / only > best / sacrifice)
- Voice & style rules (no consultant-speak, keep the discomfort, refuse to soften)
- Quality rubric (7 dimensions, savant standard at 10)
- Anti-patterns to refuse (generic differentiation, both/and answers, JTBD retrofits)

## When to Deploy

- Strategy review or annual planning
- Positioning health check
- Pre-pivot diagnostic
- Founder self-audit (recurring cadence)
- "Something is wrong but I can't name it" moments

## What This Workflow Refuses to Produce

- Soft scores or hedged diagnoses
- Recommendations that don't include 30-day commitment tests
- "Gravedigger Detail" findings that are generic ("you should think about positioning more")
- Any output that doesn't survive Smith's own quality rubric

## Stacks With

- After audit, route to the lowest-scoring dimension's deep workflow (`/smith-supply-side-audit`, `/smith-sacred-truth-inventory`, `/smith-big-fat-thing`, or `/smith-value-creation-audit`)
- After dimension fix, return here for re-audit
- For execution after audit: `/brief` to convert findings into strategic brief

**Execution prompts**: before producing the deliverable, check `skills/alex-m-smith-natural-strategy/references/prompts-v2/` for the matching structure-pure prompt and honor its Output Contract (prompt-load sweep, 2026-07-21).
