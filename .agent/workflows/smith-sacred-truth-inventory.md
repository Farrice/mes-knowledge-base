# /smith-sacred-truth-inventory

Run the Sacred Truth Inventory — Smith's IKEA-frame deployed at category scale. Forces the explicit list of category non-negotiables, then crosses each off and traces what value gets unlocked.

## Instructions

1. **Load** `skills/alex-m-smith-natural-strategy/genius.md` (full genius context)
2. **Execute** `skills/alex-m-smith-natural-strategy/workflows/02-sacred-truth-inventory.md` (full workflow)

The skill carries:
- The IKEA Sacrifice Cascade (canonical Hall of Fame Exemplar)
- The "What If We Just Didn't?" Frame
- Innovation is Subtractive, Not Additive (hidden knowledge)
- The Sacrifice Asymmetry (visibility is cheap, willingness is the moat)

## When to Deploy

- Saturated market entry (new D2C brand, new SaaS in mature category)
- Looking for whitespace in established category
- Innovation offsite preparation
- Brand pivot planning
- "Every competitor looks the same" diagnosis

## What This Workflow Refuses to Produce

- Vague sacred truths ("good UX") instead of specific norms
- Cascades that stop at first-order ("we save money") instead of tracing to category-level outcomes
- Sacrifice rankings without 30-day commitment tests
- "Hardest Question" sections that don't name a specific sacrifice the founder will resist
- Additive innovation suggestions (this workflow is purely subtractive)
