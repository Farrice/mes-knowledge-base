# Trailer Treatment

Apply movie trailer storytelling frameworks to any content — product launches, brand campaigns, social content, events, personal brand. Uses the 4-act structure, 7 archetypes, emotional frequency mapping, and sound design to create narrative-driven content plans with AI production prompts.

## Expert Loading

Load `skills/creative-direction/SKILL.md` at Tier 1. Load `genius.md` Section 5 (Trailer Storytelling) at Tier 2 for archetypes, emotional mapping, and sound elements. For video execution, also reference Section 3 (AI Prompting).

## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the Content

- **What is being "trailered"?** (product, brand, event, campaign, personal brand, service, collection drop)
- **Format?** (video 15s/30s/60s, social post series, email sequence, landing page, presentation, multi-platform)
- **Length/scope?** (TikTok 15s, Reel 30s, hero video 60s, multi-post campaign)

### Step 2: Select the Trailer Archetype

| Archetype | Strategy | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Cold Open | Most arresting moment first, no context | Mystery, thriller, high-concept |
| Slow Burn | Atmosphere building, tension through pacing | Drama, luxury, art house |
| Spectacle | Biggest visual moments first, overwhelm | Action, product launches |
| Character Study | One person's journey, transformation | Personal brand, biopic |
| Mystery Box | Fragments that don't connect, puzzle | Teasers, curiosity campaigns |
| Emotional Gut Punch | Feel before understand | Cause marketing, emotional brands |
| Hype Machine | Pure energy, rapid cuts, music-driven | Sports, streetwear drops, events |

### Step 3: Map the 4-Act Structure

Adapt to format:

**Act 1: The World** — Establish context
- Visual: Wide shots, slow pacing, ambient
- Emotion: Curiosity, intrigue
- Sound: Minimal, atmospheric
- Pacing: 4-6 second cuts

**Act 2: The Disruption** — What changes
- Visual: Medium shots, reactions, first action
- Emotion: Tension, anticipation
- Sound: Building, rhythmic, main theme
- Pacing: 2-4 second cuts, accelerating

**Act 3: The Escalation** — Peak intensity
- Visual: Close-ups, rapid montage, action
- Emotion: Awe, excitement, overwhelm
- Sound: Full peak, driving rhythm
- Pacing: 0.5-2 second cuts, rapid-fire

**Act 4: The Resolve** — Open loop
- Visual: One powerful image, title card
- Emotion: Anticipation, "I NEED this"
- Sound: Sudden silence or sustained note > title hit
- Pacing: Sudden deceleration, one long hold

### Step 4: Define Emotional Frequency

Map per act using Emotional Frequency table:
- Primary emotion
- Visual treatment (from mapping)
- Sound design (reference 10 signature elements: Braaam, Riser, Hit, Whoosh, Silence, Heartbeat, Vocal Chop, Reverse, Stinger, Needle Drop)
- Pacing/edit rhythm

### Step 5: Generate Execution Plan

For each act:
- **Shot list** (specific shot types, camera movements, lighting setups)
- **AI prompts** (platform-specific — Higgsfield for video, Kittl/Midjourney for stills)
- **Sound design direction** (specific elements from the 10 signatures)
- **Edit pacing guide** (cut lengths per act)
- **Title card / text overlay moments**
- **Speed ramp recommendations**
