# `/suzuki-product-source` - Product & Offer Sourcing

## When To Use
- "I have no product — find me something to sell."
- "I have no proof or experience — how do I start?"
- Decide between building, partnering, or affiliating an offer.

## Load
1. `skills/alex-suzuki-digital-product-revenue-os/SKILL.md`

## Execute
Run Product & Offer Sourcing. Apply the decision rule (no product + no proof → Partner; want a ready offer → Affiliate; have expertise → Build). Proof must be real (own/partner) or product-validated (affiliate); disclose partner/affiliate; no income guarantees.

## Usage
```text
/suzuki-product-source [your expertise?] [your proof?] [niche] [budget/time]
```

## Required Output
- Starting position + path chosen (why)
- Offer spec / partner brief / affiliate brief
- Offer ladder (tripwire → mid → high-ticket application)
- Proof plan
- Disclosure block
- Next action today
