# /gw-thinking-leverage

The AI-Driven Leadership operating protocol — train a person (report, client, or yourself) to own 100% of their job by bringing their own ranked 20% plan, not a task list. The leader's job shrinks to helping them see what they don't see.

## Trigger
`/gw-thinking-leverage`

## Workflow path
`skills/geoff-woods-ai-thought-partner/workflows/13-thinking-leverage.md`

## Quick Use
Name the mode first — coaching a **client**, training a **report**, or running your own **operator** relationship with this workspace. Then bring: what they're on the hook to deliver, the raw plate of everything they think they own, and where they currently wait to be told. The session runs Woods' "show me your plan" one-on-one: they rank their own 20% ("are those in order of priority?"), you hand back the wheel ("this is your meeting, you're driving"), and you add value only by closing the gaps they can't see.

## Output
Mode + own-100% frame, a daycare-vs-department-chair dependency read, their self-produced ranked plan with the ONE priority, the gap read that is the leader's real contribution, a superpowers → role's-20% match, the networks-not-hierarchy agency move, and a concrete "if your boss has to follow up, you're not owning 100%" self-test with a standing cadence. Reference arc: the Keller day-one lesson and the EA coaching story.
