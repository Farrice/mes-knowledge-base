# Dr. K × Donald Miller — Purpose-to-Position Pipeline

Connect internal purpose discovery (dharma) with external narrative positioning (StoryBrand) for complete alignment between who you ARE and how you PRESENT.

## Why This Stack Works

Most positioning work starts externally (what does the market want?). Dr. K starts internally (what is your dharma?). Miller then takes that authentic purpose and structures it into a clear market narrative. The result: positioning that's both authentic AND commercially effective.

## Steps

1. **Load Dr. K**: `skills/dr-k-consciousness/genius.md`
2. **Load Miller**: `skills/donald-miller-storybrand/genius.md` (if available)
3. **Phase 1 — INTERNAL DISCOVERY (Dr. K)**:
   - Run the Dharma Compass: what activities produce flow? What problems call you?
   - Map current guna state for professional energy
   - Diagnose false-dharma: is your positioning driven by ego or by purpose?
   - Identify the authentic purpose signal
4. **Phase 2 — NARRATIVE BRIDGE**:
   - Translate dharma signals into StoryBrand language
   - What is the external problem your dharma solves?
   - What is the internal problem (identity/emotion) your audience feels?
   - What is the philosophical stake (why this matters)?
5. **Phase 3 — STORYBRAND ARCHITECTURE (Miller)**:
   - Build the SB7 BrandScript FROM dharma-grounded truth
   - Character (ideal customer) → Problem (internal/external/philosophical)
   - Guide (you, positioned through authentic purpose) → Plan → CTA
   - Ensure the one-liner comes from dharma, not market mimicry
6. **Phase 4 — ALIGNMENT CHECK**:
   - Does the positioning feel true? (Dr. K sattva check)
   - Does the positioning sell? (Miller clarity check)
   - Would you still do this if nobody paid? (Dharma authenticity gate)
7. Output: Purpose-Position Alignment Report with dharma map + complete BrandScript
8. Run quality gate against both rubrics
