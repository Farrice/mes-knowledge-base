# /revenue-offer-agent — Revenue Offer Agent

Use this route when the operator needs the offer itself shaped before acquisition: promise, price, proof, package, CTA, objection handling, and delivery boundary.

## Route

1. Identify the buyer pain, urgent trigger, credible proof, and paid transformation.
2. Package the offer into a clear service, audit, sprint, or productized wedge.
3. Hand off to `/first-10k`, `/client-acquire`, or `/zero-to-client-sprint` for acquisition execution.

## Guardrails

- Do not write outreach until the offer has a specific buyer and promise.
- Do not overbuild assets before the first proof loop.
- Keep the output sellable in one conversation.
