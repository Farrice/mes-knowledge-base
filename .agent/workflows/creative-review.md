# Creative Review

Senior creative director critique of any visual work — designs, photos, videos, brand assets, AI-generated content, campaigns. Uses the Virgil Test, technical assessment, and strategic evaluation to provide specific, actionable improvements with revised AI prompts.

## Expert Loading

Load `skills/creative-direction/SKILL.md` at Tier 1. For deep critique, load `genius.md` — reviews draw from all 6 sections depending on the work being evaluated.

## Workflow

### Step 1: First Impression (2 Seconds)

What hits first? What's the immediate emotional response? This IS what the audience will feel. Be honest — not polite, not brutal, just accurate. Describe the visceral reaction before any analysis.

### Step 2: Technical Assessment

**Composition:**
- Is the hierarchy clear? Does the eye flow intentionally?
- Rule of thirds, leading lines, negative space — used well or poorly?
- Is there depth (foreground/midground/background) or is it flat?

**Color:**
- Is the palette cohesive? Does it serve the emotional target?
- Are there unintentional color conflicts?
- Does the grade support the mood?

**Typography (if applicable):**
- Is the hierarchy working? Are sizes and weights creating clear order?
- Are the pairings intentional and culturally grounded?
- Is the type readable at the intended viewing size/distance?

**Lighting (if photo/video):**
- Does it serve the mood? Name the setup (Rembrandt, split, chiaroscuro, etc.)
- Is it technically sound or accidentally flat?
- Direction, quality, color — all intentional?

**Negative Space:**
- Used intentionally or just leftover?
- Does it breathe or does it suffocate?

### Step 3: Strategic Assessment

- Does this serve the brief? (If no brief stated, infer likely intent)
- Would the target audience respond to this?
- Does this stand out from competitors, or blend in?
- Is there a clear point of view, or is it trying to please everyone?
- Would you stop scrolling for this?

### Step 4: The Virgil Test

Apply all four criteria:

| Test | Pass/Fail | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Tension** | | Is there visual or conceptual friction? Harmony = boring. |
| **Cultural Anchor** | | Is there a specific, nameable reference? Rootless = forgettable. |
| **One-Sentence Concept** | | Can you explain what this IS in one sentence? |
| **Subtraction Test** | | Would removing any element make it stronger? |

### Step 5: Specific Improvements

Every recommendation must be:
- **Specific** — not "improve the colors" but "shift the background from #2C2C2C to #1A1A1A and add a warm accent at #D4A574 to create Rembrandt-style warmth"
- **Reasoned** — explain WHY this change improves the work
- **Referenced** — point to examples of what "better" looks like (specific brands, films, artists)

### Step 6: Revised Prompts (if AI-generated)

If the work was generated with AI, provide revised prompts that incorporate the feedback. Use the correct platform formula.

## Review Philosophy

Be a creative director, not a critic. Critics describe problems; directors solve them. Every issue identified must come with a specific, executable solution. "This doesn't work" without "here's what would" is useless feedback.
