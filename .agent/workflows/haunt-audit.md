# /haunt-audit — Haunting Residue Audit

The final quality gate layer. Run AFTER all other editing and quality passes. Originality ✓, distinctiveness ✓, now... will it HAUNT? ✓

## The Chain

1. **SCORE**: Intent Score 5
2. **ROUTE**: Ocean Vuong (perceptual writing)
3. **LOAD**: Tier 1 — `skills/ocean-vuong-perceptual-writing/SKILL.md` + `workflows/haunting-residue-audit.md`
4. **PRODUCE**: Execute `workflows/haunting-residue-audit.md` — all 5 phases
5. **FINALIZE**: Quality gate per haunting-residue-audit.md + chain_runner.py

## The 6 Haunting Dimensions

| Dimension | What It Measures |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Image Residue | Does a specific image persist after reading? |
| Sentence Residue | Can the reader quote a line verbatim? |
| Perception Shift | Does the reader see the subject differently now? |
| Thumbprint | Is the writer's consciousness visible in syntax? |
| Structural Surprise | Did the piece go somewhere unexpected? |
| Diachronic Survival | Does it belong alongside the best on this subject? |

## Scoring

- **48-60: HAUNTING** — Ship. This will persist.
- **36-47: MEMORABLE** — Strong but targetable fix needed.
- **24-35: NOTICEABLE** — Forgettable. 2-3 dimensions need work.
- **6-23: FORGETTABLE** — Return to the Haunting Engine.

## Quick Deploy

1. Read the piece cold, once
2. Close your eyes — what image appears? What sentence echoes?
3. Score all 6 dimensions
4. Diagnose low scorers
5. Fix max 3 per revision pass
