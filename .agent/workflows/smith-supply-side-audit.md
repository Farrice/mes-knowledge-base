# /smith-supply-side-audit

Run the Supply-Side Audit — Smith's Only > Best framework deployed as a diagnostic. Forces the supply-side analysis before any growth investment is approved.

## Instructions

1. **Load** `skills/alex-m-smith-natural-strategy/genius.md` (full genius context)
2. **Execute** `skills/alex-m-smith-natural-strategy/workflows/03-supply-side-audit.md` (full workflow)

The skill carries:
- Principle 3 (Only > Best)
- The Supply-Side Audit pattern
- The Temporal Decay Map (every "best practice" advantage decays as supply expands)
- Best Practice as Trap (post-AI, widespread competence = baseline, not edge)
- Three distinct paths from "best" to "only": Subtractive / Customer-Cohort / Mechanism

## When to Deploy

- Pricing power eroding (sales cycles extending, win rate dropping)
- Pre-investment in growth tactics (audit before spend)
- Post-AI strategy review (tools have democratized your skill)
- Commodity-territory diagnostic (suspect you're stuck but can't prove it)
- Annual strategic planning ("are we still scarce?")

## What This Workflow Refuses to Produce

- Commodity Risk Scores < 6 with no honest reasoning (most modern offers ARE in commodity territory)
- "Best Trap" diagnoses that are generic ("you should differentiate more")
- 3 paths that overlap (Subtractive / Cohort / Mechanism must be genuinely distinct)
- 30-day commitment tests that are missing or vague
- Decay forecasts using hedged language ("might face pressure")
