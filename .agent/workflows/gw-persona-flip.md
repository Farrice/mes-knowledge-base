# /gw-persona-flip

An adversarially hardened deliverable, stress-tested by serial persona rotation — Challenger, then ideal customer, then aggressive board member — plus a reusable anti-sycophancy custom-instructions block.

## Trigger
`/gw-persona-flip`

## Workflow path
`skills/geoff-woods-ai-thought-partner/workflows/06-persona-flip.md`

## Quick Use
Provide:
1. The plateaued deliverable (already iterated to your ceiling), in full
2. What it's trying to achieve — the 20% it serves
3. The real audience / decision-makers who judge it (so the customer persona is grounded, not generic)

## Output
Three adversarial reports (cracks/biases/assumptions, customer objections, where-it-plays-small), the deliverable rebuilt through all three passes with a change log, surfaced persona conflicts for your decision, and a reusable Level-2 global custom-instructions block that red-teams every future answer automatically.

## Stacks With
→ `/gw-ai-board` (run a named board member as the challenger persona)
→ `/gw-simulate-room` (when the audience is a specific room you'll present to live)
→ `/gw-crit` (iterate the deliverable to its ceiling before you flip)
