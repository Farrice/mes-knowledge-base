# /oren-archetype-revenue-bridge — Archetype-to-Revenue Bridge

Translates archetype selection into revenue architecture. This is where "content strategy" becomes "business strategy." Each archetype has a distinct conversion pathway, and this workflow maps the selected archetype to specific funnel mechanics, offer structures, and revenue metrics.

## Usage

```
/oren-archetype-revenue-bridge [archetype] [brand/client context]
```

## Prerequisites
- Archetype must be selected (run `/oren-archetype-diagnostic` first)
- Load `genius.md` before executing

## Steps

### 1. Map the Archetype Funnel Mechanic

Each archetype converts differently. Identify which pathway applies:

| Archetype | Funnel Mechanic | Conversion Psychology |
|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| **Oracle** | Education → Trust → Product Credibility → Purchase | "This person knows what they're talking about — I trust their product" |
| **Performer** | Entertainment → Brand Affinity → Omnipresent Recognition → Purchase | "I love this brand's world — I want to be part of it" |
| **World Builder** | Cultural Relevance → "They Get Us" Affinity → Organic Brand Love → Purchase | "This brand understands my aesthetic/values — they must make great things" |
| **Catalyst** | Aspiration → Community Belonging → Brand as Enabler → Purchase | "This brand is helping me become who I want to be — I need their tools" |
| **Helper** | Practical Value → Ambient Recognition → Paid Ad Performance Lift → Purchase | "I already know and trust this brand — their ad just reminded me to buy" |

### 2. Design the Conversion Architecture

For the selected archetype, build the 4-layer revenue system:

#### Layer 1: Content-to-Attention (Top of Funnel)
- **What content type drives discovery?** (Maps to archetype content types)
- **What platform(s)?** (Where does this archetype's content naturally live?)
- **What's the volume target?** (Posts per week that sustains growth without burnout)
- **Key metric**: Reach / impressions / new followers per week

#### Layer 2: Attention-to-Trust (Middle of Funnel)
- **What content deepens the relationship?** (Mid-funnel content from archetype)
- **What's the lead capture mechanism?** (Newsletter, DM automation, quiz, free resource?)
- **What's the email/owned-audience strategy?** (How do you move people off-platform?)
- **Key metric**: Email subscribers / DM conversations / lead magnet downloads

#### Layer 3: Trust-to-Intent (Bottom of Funnel)
- **What content creates buying intent?** (Product reveals, case studies, testimonials, process content)
- **How is the offer presented?** (Embedded in content vs. dedicated launch vs. always-available?)
- **What's the objection-handling content?** (FAQ posts, comparison content, behind-the-scenes)
- **Key metric**: Website clicks / product page views / consultation requests

#### Layer 4: Intent-to-Revenue (Conversion)
- **What's the offer?** (Product, service, subscription, course, community?)
- **What's the price point?** (Matches audience value and archetype positioning)
- **What's the sales process?** (Self-serve checkout vs. discovery call vs. application?)
- **What's the LTV play?** (Repeat purchase, upsell, referral program?)
- **Key metric**: Revenue / AOV / conversion rate / CAC

### 3. Archetype-Specific Revenue Models

#### Oracle Revenue Model
```
Best offers: Courses, consulting, premium products, membership communities
Price psychology: HIGH — expertise justifies premium pricing
Sales process: Education-first → "if you want me to do it for you" → premium service
LTV play: Students → clients → advocates (Ken Sakata model: content → product drops)
Stack with: /offer-stack, Kallaway Ramp Architecture
```

#### Performer Revenue Model
```
Best offers: Physical products (omnipresent), brand partnerships, merchandise, experiences
Price psychology: VARIES — affinity drives impulse + aspiration
Sales process: Product is scenery → recognition builds → purchase feels natural
LTV play: Brand community → repeat purchase → lifestyle identity
Stack with: /georgi-copy (dopamine-driven purchase triggers)
```

#### World Builder Revenue Model
```
Best offers: Premium/luxury products, creative services, membership/community, media licensing
Price psychology: HIGH — cultural capital justifies premium
Sales process: Longest cycle — build world → attract aligned audience → reveal product
LTV play: Cultural community → multiple product categories → brand ecosystem
Stack with: /grace-new-media (media company revenue model)
```

#### Catalyst Revenue Model
```
Best offers: Programs, challenges, toolkits, community subscriptions, coaching
Price psychology: MID-HIGH — transformation justifies investment
Sales process: Inspire → prove transformation is possible → offer the vehicle
LTV play: Challenge → program → community → premium tier
Stack with: /sinem-revenue-architect (4-layer Substack model), /offer-stack
```

#### Helper Revenue Model
```
Best offers: SaaS, tools, affordable products, affiliate recommendations, templates
Price psychology: LOW-MID — practical value = accessible pricing
Sales process: Be useful → ambient recognition → paid ads perform better → purchase
LTV play: First purchase → related tools → bundled subscriptions
SPECIAL: Measure organic ROI by paid ad performance lift, NOT organic metrics
Stack with: Kallaway 4 Blockers, /oren-paid-organic-bridge
```

### 4. Revenue Timeline Reality Check

Set realistic expectations based on archetype:

| Archetype | Time to First Revenue | Time to Sustainable Revenue | Why |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------------|-----|
| Oracle | 1-3 months | 6-12 months | Trust-building requires consistent expertise demonstration |
| Performer | 3-6 months | 12-18 months | Entertainment → affinity → purchase is the longest emotional chain |
| World Builder | 6-12 months | 18-24 months | Highest investment, longest payoff, biggest moat |
| Catalyst | 1-3 months | 3-6 months | Aspiration-driven audiences are fast to join communities/programs |
| Helper | 2-4 months | 6-9 months | Utility builds trust quickly; paid ad synergy accelerates |

### 5. Metrics Dashboard Design

Produce a 5-metric dashboard specific to the archetype:

**Universal metrics** (all archetypes):
1. Content velocity (posts/week)
2. Owned audience size (email list / community members)

**Archetype-specific metrics** (pick 3):

| Archetype | Metric 3 | Metric 4 | Metric 5 |
|-----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Oracle | Expertise perception score (survey) | Product launch engagement lift | Content-attributed revenue |
| Performer | Brand recognition rate | Omnipresence score (product visibility %) | Affinity-driven purchase rate |
| World Builder | Cultural relevance mentions | Brand sentiment score | Media licensing revenue |
| Catalyst | Community participation rate | Challenge completion rate | Program enrollment rate |
| Helper | Paid ad CPA trend | Organic-to-paid lift ratio | Returning visitor rate |
