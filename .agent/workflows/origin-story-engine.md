# /origin-story-engine — Origin Story Engine

Build a backlog of Inspiration-layer content that manufactures the creator's origin story — the specific pieces that make a stranger able to recount who they are and why they do this. Built from Tom Noske's Gun-to-Head Origin Filter. Origin story is manufactured as content, not inherited.

## Usage

```
/origin-story-engine [the creator's raw story — where they came from, turning points, failures, beliefs] [+ offer/niche] [+ comfort line: what they WON'T share]
```
