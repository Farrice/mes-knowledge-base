## Hypothesis

Adding a "Keystone Belief Triangulation" phase (Phase 1.5) between Belief Identification and Origin Trace will produce more precise belief change sequences. Instead of targeting the most obvious/loudest blocking belief, the new phase maps the belief dependency graph to find the single keystone belief whose reframing cascades into dissolving the entire resistance cluster. This produces 3-5x more belief shift per intervention and makes the change feel like self-discovery because the reader dissolves downstream beliefs THEMSELVES after the keystone is reframed.

The insight comes from inverting McRaney's own Hidden Knowledge #7 (beliefs are load-bearing structures). The existing skill uses this defensively ("don't remove load-bearing beliefs without replacing them"). The variant uses it offensively: find the load-bearing belief in the RESISTANCE structure and reframe it so the entire resistance architecture restructures.

## Benchmark

**Prompt**: "Design a belief change sequence for S&C coaches who believe 'posting content is beneath me' — move them to willingly adopting the Authority Flywheel."

### Control (Current workflow — single-belief dissolution)

- **Target**: "Posting content is beneath me" (the stated belief)
- **Approach**: Standard 6-phase dissolution — identify belief, trace origin, metacognitive activation, dissonance, face-saving, copy
- **Result**: Solid dissolution of the stated belief. Coach understands content isn't beneath them. But residual beliefs remain: "I don't have time," "my clients would judge me," "I'd have to become someone I'm not." These require separate dissolution sequences.
- **Structural weakness**: Treats resistance as monolithic (one belief) when it's a cluster (5+ interdependent beliefs)

### Variant (+ Keystone Belief Triangulation)

- **Belief Cluster Excavated**: 5 beliefs (B1: "real expertise sells itself via referrals," B2: "content creators compensate for lack of skill," B3: "clients would lose respect," B4: "I'd have to become someone I'm not," B5: "I don't have time")
- **Keystone Identified**: B1 ("real expertise sells itself") — all other beliefs use it as a premise
- **Cascade Designed**: Reframe B1 ("you're already doing content through every client conversation — you're just not capturing it") → B2 auto-dissolves ("content isn't compensation, it's amplification") → B3 evaporates ("clients see you doing what you already do, just publicly") → B4 self-resolves ("no persona change needed — capture, not create") → B5 flips ("time cost is capture, not creation")
- **Result**: Single reframe cascades into dissolving all 5 beliefs. Face-saving narrative writes itself: "I was right that my conversations are where the value lives. I just wasn't capturing them."

## Why It Works

1. **Precision over volume**: Instead of needing 5 separate dissolution sequences for 5 beliefs, one keystone reframe cascades through the cluster
2. **Self-discovery effect**: The reader dissolves downstream beliefs themselves — you only dissolve the keystone, they arrive at the rest
3. **Reframe > Destroy**: The keystone is reframed (honors original experience, changes implication), not destroyed. This preserves dignity and makes the cascade feel like growth
4. **Adversarial resilience**: A coach can't easily object because each downstream dissolution follows logically from accepting the keystone reframe. You don't argue 5 beliefs — you shift 1 and the rest become self-evident

## Key Insight for Cross-Pollination

The belief dependency graph concept could transfer to any skill that deals with audience resistance: consumer posture profiles, objection prevention, sales copy. Any time resistance appears multi-headed, there's usually a single structural keystone.
