## Hypothesis

Adding a "Status Topology Mapping" phase (Phase 0) — where you map the target audience's specific status hierarchy, status currencies, anti-status signals, and insider codes BEFORE deploying any luxury psychology patterns — will prevent luxury positioning from feeling pretentious to non-consumer-luxury audiences by calibrating every pattern to the audience's actual definition of "premium."

The core insight: different audiences have different status currencies. S&C coaches define premium through competence and results. Architects through design awards and peer recognition. Consultants through client logos and revenue impact. Deploying taste-based luxury codes (the current default) to a competence-based audience produces positioning that feels like "marketing person who doesn't get my world."
