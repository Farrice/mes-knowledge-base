## Hypothesis

The existing comedy skill has comprehensive mechanics (12 genius patterns, 8 hidden knowledge items) but lacks a calibration layer for WHEN comedy amplifies vs. undermines authority in professional content. Adding an Authority Resonance Index — a decision framework that gates comedy output through expertise-signal, competence-direction, and self-deprecation-audit filters — will produce comedy that builds credibility rather than just generating laughs.

## What Was Added

New workflow: `workflows/authority-comedy-calibration.md`
- Three Laws of Authority Comedy (FROM > ABOUT, Specificity = Membership, Competence Before Comedy)
- Authority Resonance Score (4 dimensions: Expertise Signal, Competence Direction, Warmth Generation, Memorability)
- Comedy Style Selection matrix matched to authority goals
- Three-gate filter (Expertise Test, Credibility Direction Test, Self-Deprecation Audit)

## Key Insight

The largest gain (+4) was on Authority Signal. The baseline produced jokes that ANYONE could write about coaching and content ("treating content like cardio"). The variant produced jokes that REQUIRE domain knowledge to construct ("periodization expertise" + "perma-deload," "CSCS" + "prayer with a spotter," "rotator cuff progressions" from a voice memo). The joke itself becomes the credential. This is the core insight: in authority contexts, domain-specific comedy is a proof mechanism, not just an entertainment mechanism.
