### Failure 1 — a correction list was treated as a positioning brief
Farrice's opening message carried a **BIOGRAPHY** block framed as *"earlier drafts got this wrong."* That was a fence (where not to go), not source material. It was mined as if it were the brief. The loudest correction in it — *"the ONLY setting where 'put it back on the shelf' is literally true"* — became the most salient fact, and every subsequent headline leaned on the vitamin-shop counter harder. Most-recently-corrected detail became the whole thesis.
