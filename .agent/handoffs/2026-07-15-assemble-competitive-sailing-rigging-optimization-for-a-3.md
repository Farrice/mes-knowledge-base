## Net-New Principle
Log the prediction before the reading, and the tuning card becomes a drift detector for everything else. When the trimmer's felt hypothesis is recorded BEFORE the tape comes out (Oz's third column, born from Farrice's loop), the residual between guess and gauge — trended over a season — becomes the only instrument that can see slow structural change: a widening feel-vs-number gap is the earliest signal that Mara's compliant axis has crept, that Ingrid's chainplate bed has moved, or that the crew's ear is drifting — and it distinguishes between them, because crew drift shows up across all wind bands while structural drift clusters in the loaded ones. No single member's instrument can do this; it exists only in the stack. Corollary the whole panel converged on from three directions: every calibration artifact (log, feel, compliance budget) decays silently, so each one must be assigned another member's instrument as its freshness check — confirmed-once is the failure mode all four were independently attacking.
