# AAR — Casual Gacha Copilot Market Validation

## Mission

Transform a raw idea for a multi-game gacha companion into a grounded market,
commercial, legal, and build verdict.

- **Date:** 2026-08-04
- **Scale:** Campaign-shaped research with three bounded support workstreams and
  main-thread synthesis
- **User redirections:** 0 corrective redirections
- **User verdict:** Near-ideal; requested as a common adaptive Codex behavior

## Outcome

The mission recommended a paid, one-game roster-rescue validation test for
BLEACH: Soul Resonance before software, held the broad multi-game app, and
treated Grand Summoners as a later or differentiated lane because a close free
competitor already exists.

## What Worked

1. The synthesis found the truer problem: decision compression, not another
   information library.
2. It pushed back on the original scope with direct competitor and player
   evidence.
3. It separated category activity from exact-offer demand, payments, and
   commercial permission.
4. Each support workstream changed the decision: market evidence changed the
   game choice, commercial evidence changed the offer sequence, and build/legal
   evidence changed the architecture and public-launch boundary.
5. The main thread resolved disagreements instead of averaging them into expert
   soup.
6. The final surface stayed plain-spoken and closed with `LOCKED`, `PARKED`, and
   `NEXT ACTION`.

## What Did Not Work

- Raw-intent routing initially seated extraction governance rather than the
  product-validation owner; main-thread synthesis corrected the ownership.
- The automated finalizer scored intent alignment at 4 despite the later
  explicit near-ideal user verdict. The machine score is provisional and must
  not outrank direct human calibration.
- The routing-intelligence scoreboard crashed on an unrecognized `auto_miss`
  feedback value during the follow-up audit.

## Effectiveness

- **Main-thread product-validation synthesis:** 5/5 — converted evidence into
  one commercial decision.
- **Market scan:** 5/5 — direct competitors and player workarounds materially
  changed the positioning and first-game choice.
- **Commercial path:** 5/5 — produced the concierge wedge, pricing hypotheses,
  and paid-demand gates.
- **Build and legal feasibility:** 5/5 — replaced the generic chatbot with a
  deterministic, source-grounded product core and exposed the permission gate.
- **Raw-intent routing layer:** 4/5 — preserved the vision but required owner
  re-seating.

## Repeatable Operating Pattern

Default the reasoning principles; adapt the workflow depth:

`intent mirror -> senior pushback -> truer job -> one owner -> bounded evidence
workstreams -> evidence of change -> proof states -> reconciled verdict ->
LOCKED / PARKED / NEXT ACTION`

Use the full pattern for raw, strategic, ambiguous, research-heavy, or
high-consequence work. Keep tiny and mechanical turns light. Explicit
`/raw-intent-bridge` invocation is optional and primarily useful when the
compiled packet itself needs to be inspected.

## Prevention Rules

- Research earns inclusion only when it changes a decision or constraint.
- Explicit human verdicts close calibration; pre-feedback automated scores stay
  provisional.
- Do not add another router or super-agent for this behavior. Keep the global
  layer thin and point to the canonical operating-alignment contract.
