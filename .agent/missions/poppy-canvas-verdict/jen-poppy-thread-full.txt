The Formula (why this wins, not just what)

Every winning piece follows the same shape: Title Hook (curiosity) → Visual Hook (something you can't scroll past) → Verbal/Text Hook (the myth) → Flip (the reassurance) → Soft CTA (DM a word). Myth-busting content works because it hits both your ICP (a confused first-time buyer) and your "dream follower" (anyone who's ever complained about rent/rates) — that overlap is what gets it pushed to more people. Rap and B-roll pieces exist purely to get top-of-funnel reach (likability + shareability); carousels and yap-to-cam exist to convert that reach into DMs.
