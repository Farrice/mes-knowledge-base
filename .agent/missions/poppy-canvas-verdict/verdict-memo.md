# Verdict — is the Canvas worth finishing? (2026-09-10)

## The plain read
The thing you felt in Poppy is real and it is here. Four Hormozi videos went onto a
board, one chat read all four, quoted the actual first lines, named the hook pattern,
wrote three in that shape, and flagged a caption garble instead of repeating it.
Sixteen seconds to ingest, fifteen to answer, on the Claude plan you already pay for.
That is the Poppy job. What you saw earlier ("can't reach the server") was not the
tool failing; it was the tool not being on the server you opened. That is fixed on
port 8766 now and lands on 8765 when main is absorbed.

## Do
- Use the Canvas for one real job this week on the page you can open now. Judge it on
  that job, not on the demo board.
- Let Poppy's $1 trial run out. Do not buy a tier until the week is over.
- If IG or TikTok transcripts turn out to be weekly work, wire vidIQ watch (credits
  you already pay for) rather than buying Poppy for that one input.

## Don't
- Don't chase feature parity. Streaming replies, multiplayer, and image nodes are
  polish that would cost sessions and, for streaming, break the $0 rule by moving
  Claude to the metered API.
- Don't upgrade the Codex CLI just for the GPT seat unless you want it for other work.
  Sonnet and Gemini cover the job.
- Don't build a second board surface anywhere else. Extend `/canvas`.

## Wrong if
- You mostly analyze Instagram and TikTok videos rather than YouTube. Then the free
  path covers listing but not transcripts, and the honest comparison is Poppy's fee
  against vidIQ credit burn.
- Reply latency is the deal-breaker. Poppy streams; the Canvas lands whole after
  6–15 s. If that ruins the feel, the fix is the Anthropic API seat at ~$0.30 a turn.
- You will not open a local page. Poppy is a tab you already live in; the Canvas is
  a tab on your own machine. If the habit does not form in a week, park it and keep
  Poppy at the cheapest tier.

## What would reverse this
A parity run on YOUR fixture (packet 1) where the Canvas misses something Poppy
catches. Not polish, a miss. That has not happened on the stand-in.
