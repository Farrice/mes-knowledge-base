## Verdict wall (verbatim-sourced, `calibration-log.md` — the pen that ignores these repeats them)

- **FAIL (v3, all 8 blocks)**: briefed from a BAN LIST, not an architecture — *"a banned-tells list can only make copy less wrong; it cannot make copy land."* Every brief carries the reader-architecture ruling.
- **FAIL (v4/v5 process)**: expert-less and route-less briefs — *"We have so much arsenal. Why are you not routing through any of it? That is probably the number one failure and it drives me nuts."*
- **DOCTRINE (2026-07-30, ICP verbatim)**: *"those are great and that should have been in there exactly instead of doing it with pageantry."* When research carries the ICP's own words, ship those words. Elevating them fails SILENTLY — *"no one talks like that in this industry."*
- **PASS mechanism (2026-07-30, the facts-panel block)**: *"Your buyer skips the hero. They go straight to the supplement facts panel… And if the panel gives them a blend where a number should be, they're gone. Not angry. Just gone."* Buyer-side lived observation beats research-inferred founder mind-reads. Write from where he stood.
- **FAIL (colon-labels)**: *"The [noun]:"* openers — *"he does not speak in section labels."* Max one earned instance.
- **FAIL (named-place principle)**: *"the only room where you get to tell someone to put it back on the shelf"* → he says the plain named place — "a counter at a vitamin shop." *"Specificity is lived-in experience; generalized cleverness is the tell."*
- **FAIL (theatrical repetition)**: *"I waited for the work to speak. It doesn't speak. It never once spoke."* — *"No one talks like that."* Credibility beats shrink to one plain line.
- **RULE (imperfection principle)**: *"every sentence being a gem IS the tell."* Flat sentences as connective texture — never as payoff beats (amended 07-28).
- **RULE (Latin×Saxon)**: conceptual runway, land on a short hard word. One device per line. PASS exemplar: *"Careful sounds like maybe. And maybe always loses to a lie."*
- **RULE (heat)**: contempt in the nouns, not the volume — *"fillers, fairy-dust doses, the label does all the work."* Short cuts, no grandizing, and no flattening either.
- **RULE (reader contract)**: the back third belongs to the reader's usable move, not the writer's feelings. Vulnerability without payoff = journal entry.
- **RULE (register bar, his verbatim)**: *"silent confidence and swag with wit and humor."* Clear beats clever. Humor = reported speech + self-deprecation, never written quips.
