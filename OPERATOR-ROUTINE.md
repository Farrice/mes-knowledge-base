## The 3-command day

**1. First session of the day → type `/cos`** (~2 min)
You get: the campaign's next action, any questions waiting on you, open-mission count, money check-ins due. Answer what's there or don't — it never blocks. (A macOS notification also brings you the day's one-liner without opening anything.)

**2. To work → just talk. Raw is fine. One refinement (learned 2026-07-29):**
- **Thinking, questions, edits, vibes → just talk.** No command needed, ever.
- **A deliverable you'll JUDGE (your name or money on it) → `/go` + the rant.** Not because raw talk is wrong — because /go runs the routing as machinery and shows you a card naming which experts are loading BEFORE anything gets written. Five seconds to glance, redirect, or approve. Bare talk relies on session discipline; /go makes the arsenal structurally present.
- **"route?" — one word, any time.** The session must name exactly what's loaded or stop producing. This is your kill switch for "AI winging it with my arsenal sitting right there."

**3. Done for the day → `/end-session`**
Commits, closes, writes the handoff. Next session picks up with `/resume`.

That's the whole ritual. Everything else below is situational.

## The moves that matter

| You want to… | Say / type |

| Weekly reset | `/weekly-closeout` Sundays (~10 min now — drain the money questions, one queue decision, done) |

## What the system now does FOR you (don't carry these in your head)

- **Remembers open work** — every session opens with the open-mission count; at 3+ it asks you to finish or park one. Never blocks.
- **Guards the money lane** — if system work tries to jump ahead of the campaign, you get one line asking you to confirm. Your call, always.
- **Learns your taste automatically** — every verdict you give gets read by a real tool (`voice_evaluator`) that checks future copy against your actual FAIL history. You never re-explain a wince.
- **Recovers stranded work daily** — cloud jobs, branches, handoffs: harvested automatically. Nothing you finish disappears anymore.
- **Tracks outcomes without nagging** — only real revenue work gets a money check-in (4 right now, not 190). The morning brief carries them.

## How we work together (the standing rhythm)

- **You bring:** raw thoughts, felt verdicts, private facts, real decisions. **I bring:** everything else — research, drafts, harvests from your own corpus, the mechanics.
- **I ask you shaping questions at the forks** — short ones, at the right moment, options ready to tap. You said you prefer the back-and-forth over long silence: that's now the standing mode. Long autonomous runs happen only when you say "run with it."
- **I never ask you for what's already on disk.** Your history, your voice, your stories — I harvest first. You fill blanks, not pages.
- **Two rejections = I stop making variants** and go back to your source input. Automatically.

## The one warning

The failure mode of this whole system was never your prompting — it was **building more system instead of shipping the work.** When in doubt, the priority is whatever puts work in front of a stranger. The machine is healthy. Drive it.
