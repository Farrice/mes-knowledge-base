## The mental model

The system is built around one test: could a capable model create the same post without access to the operator's life, company, customers, private numbers, or physical world? If yes, the post is still commodity content. The Three-Moat filter repairs that weakness with the cheapest truthful proof available.

The second distinction is between **method fidelity** and **production readiness**. EVAL-038 says the workflow can produce something recognizably aligned with Tommy Clark's method. It does not replace Farrice's felt judgment, voice alignment, source checking, or a live market result. A-tier promotion therefore stays open until the human comparison is complete.
