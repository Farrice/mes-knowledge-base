## The mental model

**Sameness has causes, not vibes.** Oren refuses "everything feels the same" as a mood and names four machines producing it. Every tactic in this skill must name which machine it defeats — a plan whose moves don't map to causes is decoration.

**Copying is now faster than originating.** Ad Library, Alibaba, Import Yeti, and X strategy-sharing mean anything good "becomes an echo… with a decay in its efficacy." So value concentrates in two places only: what can't be copied (owned references, real people, a held standard) and speed of origination.

**People lie in surveys and tell the truth in carts.** The insight engine works because it targets the subconscious layer — claims people would deny. "Marketers are great at ignoring the conscious." Validation reads *who* responded, not how many.

**Excitement is an input.** "If I was unexcited by any of those, the program wouldn't work." No niche is boring; the marketer's enthusiasm is the variable, and it's generated deliberately through stories, pop-culture bridges, and pain agitation.

## The capabilities

### Slop-Era Audit (flagship)
**What it is:** a causal diagnosis — four machine sections, each requiring ≥2 citations from real observed assets, ending in 4 ranked escape moves that each name their machine and pillar, plus one committed founder-facing verdict.
**When to reach for it:** a brand looks competent and lands nowhere; you need a teardown that earns a paid engagement.
**When NOT to:** object-level critique of a single asset — that's `oren-taste-development`'s CEV matrix, cheaper and sharper for one image.
**How to invoke:** `/oren-slop-era-creative-strategy`, then accept the flagship offer. Contract: `skills/oren-slop-era-creative-strategy/references/prompts-v2/slop-era-standout-map.md`.
**Worked example:** `extractions/oren-slop-era-creative-strategy/blind-pass-sample.md` — Grüns, chosen because Oren names their ad account in-source ("Grüns is popular. What's in Grüns ad account?"). Verdict shipped: *"You won the format war so hard the format stopped being yours."*
**Honest edges:** never run on a live client subject yet. The blind pass is model-judged only.

### Insight Engine
**What it is:** observation → falsifiable thesis (claim + conscious cover story + behavioral tell) → two-channel validation with pre-committed thresholds.
**When to reach for it:** before writing angles for a new segment, or when creative keeps testing flat.
**When NOT to:** when you already have validated angles banked — go straight to production.
**How to invoke:** `/insight-engine`. Contract: `references/prompts-v2/consumer-insight-battery.md`.
**Honest edges:** the ads-validation half assumes an account with spend; without one you get content-test validation only, which is slower and noisier.

### Reference Edge
**What it is:** the scarcity system behind his rate — IRL capture with sourcing metadata, flight/Sunday batch organizing, boards titled by the decision they win.
**When to reach for it:** references are aggregator-sourced (therefore shared), or you're moving toward creative-direction work where owned references are the billable edge.
**When NOT to:** board *sign-off* mechanics with stakeholders — that's `oren-taste-development`'s moodboard-conversation-system; this workflow ends where acquisition ends.
**How to invoke:** `/reference-edge`. Contract: `references/prompts-v2/reference-library-os.md`.
**Honest edges:** the edge compounds over quarters, not sessions. Oren's took two decades and heavy travel; the constraint-removal path (synthesizing equivalent scarcity without airfare) is named in the dossier's transcendence section but unbuilt.

### Excellence Latch
**What it is:** stranger-testable reject lines, a workshop-up ritual, a per-asset human-idea rule, and one sustainable IRL element.
**When to reach for it:** mid work is shipping under volume pressure, especially with AI in the stack.
**When NOT to:** as a mid-stream blocker — house doctrine holds that a quality latch stopping work is a bug. This one nudges at ship time and carries an explicit waive hatch.
**How to invoke:** `/excellence-latch`. Contract: `references/prompts-v2/standard-of-excellence-doc.md`.
