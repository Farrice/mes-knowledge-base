## The mental model

**Three ideas make the rest obvious.**

**1. Mixing ≠ blending.** The orchestration layer (router, `/convene`, `/assemble`, swarm, expert-composition-governor) picks who's in the room and who runs when. It is *forbidden* to fuse voices — one author per body, dissent preserved, consensus-blending is a documented failure mode. So two experts can only truly merge at design time, in a compiled crossing. Orchestration deploys blends; it cannot create them.

**2. A crossing is a slot pairing, not an expert pile.** Each parent occupies one contribution slot — spine, differentiator, mechanism, craft, risk-gate. Same-slot pairs are redundant by construction (that rule killed five candidate pairings this session). The five shapes that keep recurring: mechanism→vessel, WHY→HOW, upstream→downstream, data→craft, layered passes.

**3. A prediction machine earns trust only by being falsifiable.** The buyer council writes a specific prediction to the ledger *before* reality lands. The panel that flipped two verdicts this session also refused to flip a third cleanly and refused to overclaim its grounding — that refusal is the instrument working.

## Crossings: Waves 2 and 3

**What it is.** A crossing is a workflow living in the *upstream* parent's skill dir, plus a thin router in `.agent/workflows/` and a `.claude/commands/` mirror, that loads both parents' `SKILL.md` + `genius.md` and runs a fused sequence. Each carries a Stacking Partners block (who supplies what) and a "Not This" line routing the adjacent job elsewhere. The host skill's `### Tier 3 — Stacking` table gets a row.

**When to reach for it.** You keep manually pairing two experts, and the handoff between them re-derives the same synergy every time.

**When NOT to.** One-off pairing → just load both skills for that task; the crossing is only worth compiling when the pairing recurs *and* the parents sit in different slots. Expert-side councils → `/convene`. Full positioning build from scratch → the Dunford front doors directly.

**How to invoke.** `/`+ the crossing name (all 9 verified present in `.agent/workflows/`). Nine older crossings predate this session (`ash-priestley-handshake`, `vince-x-luke-data-driven-hooks`, `omar-kallaway-pattern-interrupt`, etc.) — `ls .agent/workflows/ | grep -E "handshake|-x-"` is the honest inventory.

**Worked example.** `/dunford-handshake-haynes` ran on the live offer doc hours after being forged and produced the finding that reshaped the sprint: the $250-500 stress-test walkthrough was filed as a parked emergency SKU while cold DMs pitched $2,500 to strangers. Restaging the walkthrough as the official cold tier fixed audience-state mismatch and cash timing in one move.

**Honest edges.** Prose classifier scores structured workflow specs FLAGGED 5-8/10 by convention — sanding them further corrupts tables and quotes; don't chase it. Two bench candidates remain unforged: `geoff-woods-handshake-bos`, `baldacci-x-sean-dollwet-kdp`. The recurrence leg of the compilation rule was substituted with forward-demand for the whole Wave 2 pool (skills too young to have accumulated recurrence) — that substitution is disclosed, not hidden.

## Buyer Council + the grounding ladder

**What it is.** `/buyer-council` convenes simulated buyers against any artifact. COUNCIL mode uses standing roster seats (`councils/buyers/`), requires a NO-FIT seat and an economic gatekeeper, and returns per-seat first-15-seconds reaction, verbatim strongest objection, flip condition, and KILL/REVISE/SHIP verdicts — **never averaged**. TRIAGE mode is Mike Taylor's 5-minute process: 10 cold personas answer individually, then combine "as if these people had collaborated in writing a joint anonymous answer."

**When to reach for it.** Before spending money or launch capital on an artifact whose buyer response you're guessing at. TRIAGE for directional copy calls; COUNCIL for offers, launches, positioning.

**When NOT to.** It pre-filters the "99 questions you can't afford to ask" — it does not replace real research on the questions that survive. Cold-offer-stack-specific stress tests still route to `/haynes-handshake-geoff-stakeholder`. Expert opinion → `/convene`.

**How to invoke.** `/buyer-council` (pick mode), grounded first via `/mt-persona-grounding`. Panels on disk: `proof-to-market-founders.md`, `mybpm-streetwear.md`, `practitioner-founders.md`. Corpus: `councils/buyers/corpora/proof-to-market-founders/{raw-quotes.md,zeitgeist-digest.md}`.

**Worked example.** Run v1 on the offer flagged a missing guarantee. After both rungs got make-right clauses, the v2 retest (cold verdicts first, flips required to quote the causing clause) moved S1 REVISE→SHIP citing the DM's *"Screenshot's yours, no pitch riding on it"* plus the you-don't-pay line, and S3 REVISE→SHIP-**with-watch** — deliberately not clean, because the CFO's frame is opportunity-cost and no delivery guarantee closes that. Calibration updated: reply band unchanged (a guarantee is read after engaging, so it can't move first-touch trust), booked band 0-1 → 1-2.

**Honest edges.** Seats 2/4/5 remain cold-generated — the corpus is sub-$15M founder-forum voice and cannot speak for VP-Brand-scale buyers. No prediction has met reality yet: **all 3 ledger lines are pending.** Until Monday's replies land, the panel is a well-built instrument with zero track record. The corpus expires ~45 days from harvest.
