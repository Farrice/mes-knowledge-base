## The mental model

### One expert, two skill systems, five doors

Joanna already had two canonical skill owners: `joanna-wiebe-persuasion-mastery` for persuasive craft and `joanna-wiebe-writing-careers` for the commercial architecture of writing. The new source belongs downstream of persuasion mastery. It adds belief-community mechanics; it does not justify a third Joanna skill or a standalone cult-marketing expert.

`/joanna-wiebe` is the wide door. It reads both skill maps, diagnoses the job, and loads the needed genius context and workflow. The four belief commands are narrow doors for cases where the job is already clear. All global skills remain thin wrappers pointing back to these project-owned files.

### Capability availability is not mandatory application

The front door has two modes. Adaptive Joanna is the default: use the smallest relevant capability set. Full Joanna Deployment loads both genius contexts and considers six families—conversion diagnosis, persuasive production, authority craft, belief community, messaging and funnels, and writing business. It must then state what was used and what was parked.

This distinction protects quality. A landing-page rewrite may need money-word mining, skepticism calibration, story, and authority craft without needing writing-career pricing. A writing-service offer may need positioning, outcome-based pricing, and messaging without needing a community ritual. Full expertise means better judgment about relevance.

### The belief system is a dependency chain

The extracted mechanism is sequenced:

`worldview lens → ethical contrast → symbol → voluntary ritual → shared language → earned conviction`

Starting with conviction creates certainty theater. Starting with merchandise creates branded decoration. Adding friction to manufacture devotion creates coercion. Each layer must earn the next, and observable adoption—not asset completion—is the eventual proof.

## Capability: belief-community architecture

### What it is

The conductor turns an approved worldview into six connected layers and then into finished channel expressions. The central craft constraint is ethical contrast: belief gains definition by opposing a broken mechanism without turning people into targets.

### When to reach for it

Use it when a creator or brand has good individual assets but no worldview that compounds across them; when community language feels generic; or when the offer, content, and community seem to belong to different belief systems.

### When not to

Do not use it to rescue weak product proof, invent demand, or manufacture tribal hostility. If the need is only a campaign hook, use the normal copy or content route. If the audience has never demonstrated the proposed belief, mark it `UNTESTED` and design an adoption event.

### How to invoke

```text
/belief-community-architecture: Build the belief system for [brand]. Here is the evidence, audience language, offer, and current category frame: [...]
```

### Worked example

The verifier's positive control uses a claim-safe content worldview: the lens is that safer content makes evidence visible; the enemy is confidence theater as a behavior; the symbol is a VERIFIED/LIKELY/UNCONFIRMED strip; the ritual is a voluntary Proof Pass; the shared language is “proof-bounded”; and conviction remains conditional. Four negative controls prove the system rejects a human enemy, coercive ritual, unearned certainty, and missing language.

### Honest edges

The source video is primary evidence for Joanna's stated sequence, not universal scientific proof for every community claim. The research audit separates source-observed rhetoric, corroborated mechanisms, and unverified generalization. Keep certainty attached to the ledger.

## Capability: focused belief routes

### What they are

The three focused routes allow partial construction without pretending the whole system exists. `/worldview-contrast-system` owns the lens and enemy mechanism. `/participation-language-system` owns signal, ritual, language, and adoption instrumentation. `/earned-conviction-deployment` owns the final future statement and its channel expressions.

### When to reach for them

Use the route matching the missing layer. This is especially useful when category strategy has already produced the worldview, or when a client has existing language and rituals that need audit rather than wholesale replacement.

### When not to

Do not run earned conviction on an empty foundation. Do not invent a phrase before defining what it means and what wrong use looks like. Do not treat merchandise as a symbol merely because it contains a logo.

### How to invoke

```text
/worldview-contrast-system: Turn this evidence and category frame into a truthful lens and ethical contrast: [...]

/participation-language-system: Give this worldview a useful voluntary ritual, portable language, and observable adoption events: [...]

/earned-conviction-deployment: Calibrate the strongest honest future claim and deploy it across these channels: [...]
```

### Honest edges

Adoption metrics remain `NO EVENT` until real people quote the language accurately, complete the ritual voluntarily, recognize the signal, or change comparison behavior. A completed phrase bank is readiness, not proof.
