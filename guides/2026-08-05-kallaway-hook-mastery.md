## The mental model

**Four ideas make everything else obvious.**

**One — a hook is a reflex, not an argument.** The feed is a highway: thumb moving, eyes glazed. You are not convincing anyone; you are triggering an involuntary orienting response (Kallaway's image: looking up to see a wolf in your backyard). That reframes the whole craft — you design stimulus, not rhetoric. It also dissolves the clickbait anxiety: accuracy is enforced downstream, in the lock-in zone, not by softening the hook.

**Two — two questions, four ingredients.** Every hook must prompt and answer *what is this about* and *what do I get if I stay*. The 4 S's are how those answers land: Subject (named unmistakably), Stakes (the outcome they actually want, or a curiosity scenario for entertainment), Speed (compression — 8 words maps in the brain, 28 does not; but rushing delivery so it cannot be absorbed fails equally), Super Clear (one interpretation; half a second of confusion loses the thumb). Miss one ingredient and it does not hook.

**Three — three channels that must predict each other.** Text hook, spoken hook, visual hook. Watch the visual muted and you should be able to guess the other two. This is the failure Kallaway names in every business owner he coaches: they polish the spoken hook and orphan the rest. Priority order matters too — text first (eyes hunt words faster than they comprehend images), spoken second, visual last (yappers win with no visual hook at all).

**Four — the hook is the appetizer.** The lock-in zone (the next 5–10 seconds) is where the viewer decides to stay, and its psychology is arguably more important than the hook's. Two things happen there every time: the claim gets confirmed (no bait-and-switch) and the messenger gets a rapid trust evaluation. In an internet full of fake gurus, a strong claim is not enough — proof is the trust anchor, and it is the most important of the four lock-in levers (looks, packaging, relatability, proof).

## The skill

**What it is.** `skills/kallaway-hook-mastery/` — SKILL.md (workflow table + stacking guide), `genius.md` (12 patterns, hidden knowledge, 7 real exemplars with performance context, 6 signature moves, a 10-dimension rubric, sourced anti-patterns, recognition test), three references (`text-hook-rules-card.md`, `visual-hook-pattern-library.md`, `source-quotes.md`), 10 workflows across three tiers, and 7 execution prompts in `references/prompts-v2/`.

**When to reach for it.** Any time the first line of anything is being written — video hooks, post openers, subject lines, ad headlines, DM first sentences, slide-1 titles.

**When NOT to.** Body copy and full scripts belong downstream: `kallaway-content-psychology` for the Dopamine Ladder and full-script architecture, `writers-room` for refining an existing draft, `/ghostwrite` for LinkedIn from scratch. This skill owns the opening and the lock-in, not the whole piece.

**How to invoke.** `/kallaway-hook` loads skill + genius; any of the 11 commands above fires a specific workflow. Execution prompts are auto-surfaced when SKILL.md is read (the prompt-menu hook), or read directly from `skills/kallaway-hook-mastery/references/prompts-v2/`.

**Worked example.** The blind-pass sample at `extractions/kallaway-hook-mastery/blind-pass-generated-sample.md` ran `/interrupt-hook-engine` on AI search rerouting supplement buyers (the Proof-to-Market lane): three hook sets at 8–10 words each, with text-hook line breaks, visual directions timed to specific words, caption lines, an alignment audit, and a lock-in zone anchored on a 12-brand listening receipt.

**Honest edges.** The blind-pass PASS is model-judged against two real-corpus pieces — A-tier promotion requires Farrice's own side-by-side verdict. Power phrases in the sample are flagged untested, as doctrine requires, and stay hypotheses until real performance data validates them. `hook-landscape-scan` has never been run against a live niche; it needs real research tools (Playwright, exports, analytics) and must never generate a landscape from training memory. The source's mining tactics lean on Kallaway's own tool (Sandcastles); the protocol was extracted tool-agnostically, so the workflow accepts any winner corpus.

## The universal adapter

**What it is.** `/hook-anything` maps the triple-hook channels onto non-video assets. A LinkedIn post has all three channels and does not know it: line 1 is the spoken hook, formatting and line breaks are the text hook, the image is the visual hook, lines 2–4 plus a receipt are the lock-in zone. The workflow carries that mapping table for posts, emails, static ads, VSLs, long-form YouTube, sales pages, decks, and cold DMs.

**When to reach for it.** Campaign work — every Cash Launch post and every outreach message is a hook problem wearing different clothes.

**When NOT to.** If the asset is a short-form video, use `/interrupt-hook-engine` directly; the adapter exists for surfaces where the channels are disguised.

**How to invoke.** `/hook-anything`, then name the asset type and topic. In Farrice's voice, the workflow loads `_active/farrice-brand/voice/VOICE-CARD.md` as a layer first and runs `prose_classifier.py` on written output.

**Honest edges.** The adapter architects; it does not own platform idiom. It names the specialist to finish (Diandra for LinkedIn, Luke Iha for ads, Jenny Hoyos for shorts, Georgi for curiosity copy) and hands off — one author per body, never stitched.
