## The mental model

1. **Content is trust advertising for conversations — nothing more.** 97% of the market isn't ready, 3% is, and only a sliver ever inbounds; posting-and-praying markets to that sliver. The system starts conversations with everyone who engages, so a year later there's "an inbox full of 1,000 people who all really like and trust you."
2. **Trust is an account walked through six gates.** Every touch deposits (personal, useful, verifiable) or withdraws (template, pitch, too-early ask). The AI era starts everyone at *negative* trust, which makes verifiability (LinkedIn's unfakeable graph) and in-person the two biggest arbitrages. Trust is lane-scoped — it doesn't transfer across offers.
3. **The founder's nervous system is part of the funnel.** Prospecting avoidance is a self-trust deficit, not a scripts problem; demand surplus ("so many leads you're saying no") is what actually buys calm. State work and lead flow run in parallel, never instead of each other.
4. **Pieces come from motion.** Every venture is a 250-piece puzzle with 100 pieces given; planning can't produce the rest — action can. 70%-ready is ready.

## Capability: Closed-Lost Campaign (workflow 04)

**What it is** — a monthly offer campaign to everyone the business ever talked to, on the mechanism that people only decide on compelling events and an ask *manufactures* one. Two scripts: the Jono reopener ("where did we drop the ball?") and the scarcity ask ("working with a couple more clients next month — still interested?").
**When to reach for it** — established business, dormant list, needs revenue before new-audience builds. **When NOT to** — true cold-starts (no list exists); route to `/ml-network-ladder` tier-1 instead.
**How to invoke** — `/ml-closed-lost`; the v2 prompt `references/prompts-v2/closed-lost-campaign-build.md` carries the output contract.
**Honest edges** — proof numbers ($45K week-one) are Lakajev's own claims from the podcast; not yet run on Farrice's or a client's real list.

## Capability: Opinion Ladder (workflow 05)

**What it is** — the exact never-pitch sequence: three opinion asks (about the thing *they* engaged with, then about their answers), a free small gift, seven days of deliberate silence, then a scarcity-framed invite. Five exchanges with no ask makes the sender unclassifiable as a salesperson; the silence does the selling.
**When to reach for it** — any engagement signal (like, profile view, comment). **When NOT to** — a live hand-raise: speed-to-lead overrides the ladder, book immediately.
**How to invoke** — `/ml-opinion-ladder`; worked exemplar in genius.md is the verbatim Candace exchange (she asked to buy at rung two).
**Honest edges** — scripts must be re-voiced per sender (voice-card layer is binding for Farrice-named sends).

## Capability: Six Gates of Trust Audit (workflow 06)

**What it is** — Lakajev's proprietary model (he mined it from his own subconscious; "doesn't exist on the internet") run as a LENS over real assets: each gate scored PASS/LEAK/FAIL with quoted evidence, one dominant stall gate named, prescriptions in gate order (never fix gate-5 copy while gate 1 fails), plus a gate-6 plan that engineers buyer *action* and referral moments.
**When to reach for it** — funnels with attention but no clients; before rebuilds; productizable client teardowns. **When NOT to** — no assets exist yet (nothing to audit — build with 01/02 first).
**How to invoke** — `/ml-six-gates` with the actual asset text; the audit quotes, never vibes.
**Honest edges** — the model is single-sourced from this podcast; gate wording is fixed verbatim to avoid drift.

## Capability: Twin Targeting + Daily Rhythm (workflows 07-08)

**What it is** — list-building from the graph ("your next best client is the twin of your current best client"; Sales Navigator connections-of/niche/radius filters; outreach carries mutual + named twin + free proof doc) feeding a rhythm with quotas in personalization-*time*, not touch counts — the anti-Hormozi doctrine ("100 reach-outs for 100 days… destroys people's souls").

**How to invoke** — `/ml-twin-targeting` then `/ml-daily-rhythm`; prompt `twin-client-prospect-map.md` carries the contract.

## Capability: Tier-3 meta layer (workflows 09, 11-13)

**What it is** — the warm-network ladder (tier-matched acquisition, including the heresy prescription "stop posting on LinkedIn entirely" when 20 years of referral trust points elsewhere), founder state reset (no-input mornings, loop-closing walks, promise repair — with an explicit therapy boundary), subconscious model mining (the IP factory that produced the Six Gates; heaviness warning ships with it), and high-agency moves (≥3 moves today, silver-platter asks).
