# The Propaganda Engine — /jw-engine: What We Built 2026-06-14 and How to Use It

> The capstone of the John Whiting extraction: a general-purpose content/offer/pipeline OS you can **point at ANY objective** — content, offer, service, launch, raw idea — and it deploys Whiting's attention→attraction→pipeline genius end to end, ethics-gated, expert-stacked, shipping finished work. The only net-new pieces are the objective router and the OS spine; everything else it WIRES (`/autopilot`, `/copy-engine`, `/supercomputer`, the expert roster). Sources: `skills/john-whiting-propaganda-machine/SKILL.md` (18 workflows) · `workflows/jw-engine.md` · `references/objective-router.md` · `.agent/workflows/jw-engine.md`. Standing rule: **never rebuild a content engine from scratch — extend this.**

## ⚡ If you only read 10 lines

- Fire it at anything: `/jw-engine <any objective>` — `sell the Authority Flywheel without sales calls`, `get eyeballs on [topic] this week`, `make this offer irresistible`, or a raw voice memo.
- Not input-gated: it grounds from your files + the `avatar_manifold` cache + Recall, infers hard, asks only for a missing load-bearing unrecoverable fact, and labels assumptions `[ASSUMED:…]` — never hard-blocks.
- Not-input-gated ≠ invented: real grounding or a labeled assumption, never a fake fact. Any unlabeled/unsourced claim fails Gate 1 at the fabrication audit.
- Engine vs machine: `/jw-engine` classifies ANY objective and picks the sequence; `/jw-propaganda-machine` is specifically the sell-without-calls build — which the engine itself calls for PIPELINE/OFFER objectives.
- Classification runs through the objective router: 9 archetypes × jobs EYEBALLS / ATTRACTION / PIPELINE / OFFER / QUALITY.
- Ethics Gate (`/jw-ethics-gate`) runs on EVERY asset before ship: true claims, real proof, buyer's interest, reversible respect — or kill it.
- Voice rule: keep the confrontational spine; Mode B drops the profanity, never the edge. Sanded-down = failed asset, don't ship.
- The one belief that kills all objections: `/jw-big-domino` — one sentence, no escape hatch; re-anchored in every asset ("number of times over = certainty").
- Retargeting spec: ~5×/day to the warmest ~600, ~2×/week to the mid ~1,000, testimonial-led TOF.
- Honest edge: built + adversarially reviewed (7.5→8.5 post-fix, SHIP) but **never run live**. Start with EYEBALLS/ATTRACTION objectives (Parallax/LinkedIn); PIPELINE/OFFER waits on the still-open Authority-Flywheel-vs-coaching vehicle decision.

## The mental model

1. **Content that closes.** Whiting's bridge — record every objection, belief-shift, and close ONCE, clip it, and force-feed it to the right people at controlled frequency until they close themselves. "I'm basically creating a whole bunch of setters and closers and I'm deploying them as an army." No setters, no closers, no calls.
2. **The engine wires, never rebuilds.** Five phases — Intake (zero schema) → Classify (objective router) → Ground Once → run the jw-sequence (big-domino → objection-arsenal → self-selection → tone-ladder → produce → distribution) → Gate → Output+Learn — and the heavy machinery is all borrowed: `/autopilot` for gates/fan-out/finalize, `/copy-engine` for the $0-reuse grounding cache, `/supercomputer` for multi-deliverable.
3. **Conversion by repulsion.** Polarization is the qualification filter — content that tells wrong-fits to leave is what makes the right-fits self-close. A deliverable that must please everyone is the wrong system.
4. **The surpass mechanism.** Whiting solo-cuts every clip (his ceiling); the OS out-produces him with parallel fan-out × stacked craft experts × quality gates × the grounding cache × a production-learning loop.

## 1. `/jw-engine` — the front door

**What it is.** The OS orchestrator. Load order (from `.agent/workflows/jw-engine.md`): `genius.md` (14 patterns, dual-voice system, Ethics Gate, rubric) → `references/objective-router.md` → `workflows/jw-engine.md`. `/create` also composes this engine for campaign/persuasion work.

**When to reach for it.** Any objective where the job is eyeballs, attraction, pipeline, or offer — especially when you'd otherwise start building a bespoke content engine. That impulse is the tell; this exists so you never do that again.

**When NOT to.** A single tool is enough (one objection asset, one diagnostic) — invoke the component workflow directly. Pure brand-awareness content with no conversion goal — use the content/platform experts directly. A deliverable that needs to please everyone.

**How to invoke.** `/jw-engine <any objective>` — no schema, no prerequisites beyond the objective itself.

**Honest edges.** Zero live runs. The fabrication audit (Phase-4 step 0, the checked assumption-label pass) closed the adversarial review's #1 finding on paper — its first real test comes with the first real objective.

## 2. `/jw-propaganda-machine` — the sell-without-calls hero build

**What it is.** Five stages: mine the sales psychology from real recorded conversations → build the asset army (each asset headlines the objection it kills, carries the spine) → engineer self-selection → distribute at 3-tier frequency → close without closing (the first real conversation happens AFTER payment). Ships as a Propaganda Machine Build Sheet with metrics: frequency hit rate, completed-play rate, time-to-close, self-close rate.

**When to reach for it.** Selling coaching/high-ticket services with no sales calls — the hero use case is the Authority Flywheel.

**When NOT to (this gate is physical in the workflow).** Zero real sales reps — the machine is built FROM live objection-handling; no recordings = no source material. Route: do 20–50 real conversations first, record them. Vague offer with no clear A→B — `/jw-one-best-client` + `/jw-the-map` first.

**Honest edges.** PIPELINE/OFFER deployment for Farrice is gated by the unresolved vehicle decision (Authority Flywheel vs coaching/consulting) — the machine is ready; the offer it would sell is not yet locked.

## 3. `/jw-big-domino` — the one-belief install

**What it is.** Find the single load-bearing belief upstream of every objection (Whiting's: "you need all 7 components or the trip crashes"), write it as one declarative with no escape hatch, install it by dissolving the competing belief (× McRaney — surface → contrast → let them arrive), re-anchor it in every asset (vary the surface, never the spine), and keep reselling it post-purchase (retention = continuously reselling the big idea).

**When to reach for it.** The offer isn't converting and you're tempted to add more selling points. A list of benefits is the failure signature — "if you can't pick ONE, you haven't found it."

**When NOT to.** The domino can't survive the Ethics Gate under oath — it's the most-repeated claim in the entire machine; even slightly inflated means force-feeding a lie at frequency. Kill it.

**Honest edges.** The worked domino in the file ("a car driven at 54%") is a constructed illustration, not a Whiting quote — treat it as format, not evidence.
