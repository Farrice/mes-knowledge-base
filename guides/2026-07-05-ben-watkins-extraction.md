## The mental model

1. **People buy on emotion, not logic — and the room is against you.** Watkins walks in assuming the exec is bored, tired, and on their sixth pitch. Flipping that state *is* the memorable event: "if I flip the script, I just became the best part of your day." Facts sit in a room; they never open one.
2. **Mastery is subtraction.** Every detail is momentum, a withheld seed, or dead weight. Total clarity kills the lean-in. But CS-02 proved the cut has a failure mode: severing connective tissue makes correct beats read jolty. Flow is graded as hard as the pillars now.
3. **Lane discipline.** Miller frames the message → Stanton architects the piece → **Watkins sells it in the room and hooks it on the feed.** Loading Ben for template work or premise-digging wastes the wedge.

## Capability 1 — The Pitch Room

**What it is.** `/bw-pitch-room` builds a live pitch as modular, truncatable sections: lock the north star into one infectious sentence they can repeat to their boss verbatim, design a specific flip per assumed-hostile person in the room, plant (never open on) one genuine overlap, open on surprise or proof-of-belief, and tag every section with the buy-in signal that triggers its cut — "as soon as you know you have somebody emotionally, that's when you move on."

**When to reach for it.** Any real-time sell: client pitch, offer call, proposal walkthrough, investor conversation. Also pre-call prep — the read-the-room table is a rehearsal instrument.

**When NOT to.** Written persuasion → `copy-engine` / `luke-iha-vsl-leads` (Watkins sets the emotional arc; Luke writes converting copy inside it). A pitch whose *idea* is still mush → `/bw-north-star` first; the workflow's own gate stops you anyway.

**Worked example.** The workflow ships Watkins's own general-meeting story worked as a truncation table — he flew 3,000 miles in winter for a "general" and the casting director invented a scene on the spot. Proof-of-belief as the opening beat, not exposition.

## Capability 2 — Subtraction + the Flow Gate

**What it is.** `/bw-subtraction` sorts every detail into momentum / withheld-seed / dead-weight, then tightens so what you leave out does the work. Since 2026-07-10 it runs under the flow-and-cadence layer: 17 flow laws (Provost contour, information-gap loops, known-new contract, greased slide, read-aloud ship-gate) with a 5-item Flow Gate binding on all 18 workflows.

**When to reach for it.** Anything over-explained, rambling, or so clear there's nothing left to lean toward. Pairs with `/bw-anatomy-audit` → `/bw-elevate` as the rewrites-always-open loop: audit first (9+ scorers log to `references/case-studies.md`), then two takes beside the untouched original.

**When NOT to.** Line-level slop scrubbing → `prose_classifier.py`; structural spinelessness → Hawley first. Subtraction presumes the beats are right.

**Honest edges.** Born from a real failure: the first DWA Threads set passed every deterministic gate and still read jolty (CS-02). The Flow Gate is the patch — but it's judgment-assisted, not deterministic; the read-aloud test is still your ears.

## Capability 3 — The audit-and-elevation loop

**What it is.** `/bw-anatomy-audit` X-rays an existing piece: north star reverse-engineered, per-criterion scores each with a verbatim proof line, what's FIRING named before anything weak. `/bw-elevate` then produces Take A (scalpel: flow pass, subtraction pass, one pillar raised) and Take B (blank-page reimagining with a named theory of surpassing) — the original never silently replaced, the felt verdict deciding.

**When to reach for it.** Any existing post, edition, pitch script, sales page, or VSL you're tempted to rewrite. The loop enforces the 2026-07-10 binding: no "never rewrite elevated" rule, but no blind rewrites either — audit before elevate.

**When NOT to.** Fresh material → `/bw-story-anatomy` builds instead of audits.
