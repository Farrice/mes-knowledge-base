The system is workspace-local, source-grounded, and deliberately held at **54/100, keep as a workflow** until independent field runs earn plugin promotion.

## Mental model: transformation spine first, production interface second

Think of the system as one invariant traveling through several containers:

`real source → private recognition → embodied conflict → earned reinterpretation → safe action → community learning`

The early workflows decide what the content is for and what it must repay. Packaging then makes an honest promise. The craft layer chooses how the shift is earned. Only after those decisions exist does the production layer translate them into units, owners, dependencies, and unresolved specialist questions.

This order matters. A production team can simplify an environment, combine a segment, or adapt a delivery format. It cannot silently remove the recognition moment, consequence, perspective shift, safe action, or mission boundary. Those are transformation invariants. Workflow 08 makes them visible at each handoff; workflow 09 prevents efficiency decisions from erasing them later.

The operating rule is: **capacity may reshape delivery, but it does not get to redefine the human outcome.**

## Capability 1: the Transformational Content Factory

### What it is

The factory is a conductor, not a mega-skill. It selects and sequences the smallest useful set of existing components, preserves one function owner, and returns a Composition Ledger showing what each component actually changed.

### When to reach for it

Use it when a raw idea must become a coherent creator asset, especially when high engagement is desired but the deeper bar is that viewers leave with recognition, relief, agency, or a useful perspective shift. It is also the right route when packaging, body craft, community learning, and production handoffs must stay aligned.

### When not to

Skip the whole factory for a title-only adjustment, a generic retention audit, or a finished draft that only needs one bounded repair. If no real source exists for a personal scene, the system may build strategy but must stop before authorship.

### How to invoke it

Give it a raw thought or source, a defined audience in a specific situation, the starting belief or feeling, the intended after-state, a real scene or evidence source, the format/runtime, the mission boundary, and one safe positive action. If the output will ship in Farrice's voice, load the voice card before visible prose.

### Worked example

Input: a creator wants to tell a story about mistaking exhaustion for lack of discipline. The factory identifies the private sentence (“I keep treating depletion like a character flaw”), chooses a recognition-you Bayer mode, routes the belief turn through an educational bridge, packages the tension without promising a cure, and produces a beat map that ends with one safe action: change the question asked before adding more pressure. If production mode is active, workflow 08 then maps the proof scene, explanation, contrast, and action into owned units.

### Honest edges

The factory cannot guarantee reach, retention, emotional transformation, or therapeutic benefit. It cannot manufacture a lived experience. It also does not make every dependency hot: Kallaway stays cold until a draft exists, and Kieran Content Ops remains downstream rather than authoring the content.

## Capability 2: Story-to-Production Continuity (workflow 08)

### What it is

Workflow 08 is the interface between an approved creative structure and the people or systems that must produce it. It creates a development intake, a concept-to-capacity checkpoint, a map of producible units, a department handoff matrix, a freshness review, and a visible register of specialist gaps.

### When to reach for it

Use it once the team approves three things: the Viewer Transformation Contract, the Package Covenant, and the story structure. Activate it when meaning is likely to be lost between writer, producer, editor, designer, researcher, or on-camera owner. It also helps when the idea may be too expensive, complex, or permission-sensitive in its present form.

### When not to

It cannot write dialogue, direct performance, choose shots, solve legal or safety questions, or design an edit. A content idea alone is not enough. Without an approved transformation and structure, this workflow would organize ambiguity instead of preserving intent.

### How to invoke it

Provide the source, transformation contract, package covenant, beat map or script, format/runtime, known assets, constraints, owners, permissions, and desired production-lock date. Every unit must name its purpose, owner, dependencies, and at least one transformation invariant.

### Worked example

For the exhaustion story, units might be: cold-open recognition, lived incident, evidence-backed distinction, old-belief consequence, new question, and safe action. The editor receives the promise and shift each unit must protect, while unsupported sound, clinical, or legal judgments remain named gaps. If the necessary proof source is unavailable, the work pauses for that source or permission rather than accepting a plausible substitute.

### Honest edges

This packet can clarify production work; it does not prove a production owner will accept it. PR-001 showed that intake quality can remain marginal when the premise or decision owner is underspecified. That is the first issue to watch in a real run.

## Capability 3: Production Capacity Learning Loop (workflow 09)

### What it is

Workflow 09 converts observed local stoppages into the smallest evidence-supported capacity decision. It maps reusable assets, concurrency assumptions, repeated waits, freshness limits, and the next observation that could confirm or reverse the decision.

### When to reach for it

Use it when production repeatedly waits on the same person, environment, template, proof source, or edit capacity; when several pieces are expected to run concurrently; or when reuse creates visible sameness.

### When not to

A single inconvenience does not justify new capacity. Dhar Mann Studios' reported scale, review cadence, and infrastructure are examples, not recommendations. Available assets also cannot determine the mission or story.

### How to invoke it

Bring the next slate, local assets and owners, concurrency assumptions, recent wait/rework evidence, freshness notes, affected transformation contracts, and a decision horizon. Label every number or assertion `OBSERVED`, `PLANNED`, or `UNKNOWN`.

### Worked example

If three creator videos stall because one proof-review step is shared, the first response may be resequencing or a reusable evidence checklist instead of adding staff. The loop records the constraint, rejects expansion-first reasoning, and specifies the next evidence: whether the new sequence removes the wait without reducing trust or freshness.

### Honest edges

Capacity decisions remain hypotheses until local use generates evidence. Throughput and transformation quality must stay separate; a faster system that makes the content feel generic is a failed optimization.

## Evidence and plugin boundary

The rollup contains 9 workflows and 10 deterministic prompts grounded in two video sources and two cited published stories. The second source contributed only the production-continuity and capacity-learning components. Its operational details are leadership self-reports, not audited performance data or portable staffing prescriptions.

Three receipts prove that the new components can produce their contracted artifacts against distinct provenance-bearing inputs. They do not prove cold-start behavior, production-owner acceptance, published performance, audience transformation, portability, or Farrice's felt verdict. The current plugin score is therefore **54/100, keep as a workflow**.

Promotion requires three independent prospective runs, at least one production-owner receipt, a felt verdict, one published-result review across the four separate scoreboards, a cold-start test, and a fresh readiness audit. Until then, packaging would freeze an interface before real use has earned it.
