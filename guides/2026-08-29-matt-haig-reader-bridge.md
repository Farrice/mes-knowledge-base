## The mental model

### 1. The signal and the bridge are different objects

The private signal is the feeling, pressure, odd premise, or necessary truth that made the work alive before approval entered. The bridge is the story, image, title, action, sequence, and language that lets another person carry it. When readers cannot enter, do not automatically replace the signal with something more familiar. Inspect the bridge first.

This is why the system can simplify a title, sentence, or story engine while protecting a strange premise. It is also why trend tables cannot choose the originating idea: they describe what has already arrived, not what this writer needs to make.

### 2. A marked sentence may be a symptom

The core revision move is causal, not cosmetic. If a line repeatedly feels vague, search upward. The sentence may be compensating for a scene with no consequence, a motive the reader cannot infer, a sequence that hides the event, or a premise carrying too many concepts. Line-editing the symptom produces smoother confusion.

The system therefore asks for the highest supported diagnosis and the reader consequence behind each material change. It keeps a protected original so late polish can be compared against access, surprise, rhythm, exposure, and residue.

### 3. The stop decision is part of the craft

Every route ends with continue, hold, or revert. Finish is not the same as life. When a revision improves legibility but lowers charge, the right move may be to restore the earlier image or rhythm. Farrice's blind verdict supplied the concrete calibration: the repaired passage was easier to read and had the better ending, while the earlier version sometimes painted the stronger picture. The operating lesson is precise: preserve strong images when they do not force the reader to decode what literally happened.

## 1. Diagnose and repair reader access

### What it is

`/haig-reader-bridge` is the default front door. It separates the target feeling, the current carrier, the visible friction, and the highest supported cause. `/haig-reader-access-rewrite` takes that diagnosis through a complete revision with a causal change ledger and stop decision.

### When to reach for it

Use it when a meaningful passage feels guarded, jumpy, overexplained, overpolished, or emotionally sealed off. It is especially useful when feedback says “confusing” or “not landing” without naming the causal layer.

### When not to

Do not use it on a passage with no demonstrated reader friction merely because another style is available. Preserve is a valid output. Do not use it to make prose “more Haig”; route voice to the writer's own Voice Card.

### How to invoke

Paste the original material and say:

```text
/haig-reader-bridge diagnose the highest supported cause of reader friction in this passage. Preserve the original, produce the smallest finished intervention that changes the reader experience, and stop before access gains cost surprise, rhythm, exposure, or residue.
```

### Worked example

The first blind repair compared an initial model passage with a bounded revision. Farrice preferred the revision because it was easier to read and its ending made the event clearer. He also identified the cost: some images in the initial version were stronger. That judgment now governs the repair route; plain causal legibility comes before conspicuous metaphor, but image quality remains protected when it does not obscure the event.

### Honest edges

One successful blind passage does not prove consistency across novels, memoir, brand writing, or every reader. The system has human craft calibration, not public readership data.

## 2. Move feeling into story without forcing literal confession

### What it is

Three routes handle creation before conventional polish. `/haig-percolation-draft` captures the warm scene while discovery is active. `/haig-feeling-carrier` replaces stated theme with action, choice, consequence, and image. `/haig-oblique-truth` changes the mask (identity, world, species, age, place, or time) while preserving emotional pressure.

### When to reach for it

Use these when the writer knows what the work is about but the draft explains rather than embodies it, or when literal autobiography produces self-censorship. The point of distance is not fantasy decoration. It is permission to become more exact.

### When not to

Do not transform factual claims, consent boundaries, or safety constraints into fiction to evade them. Do not add a strange form after the emotional pressure has disappeared; novelty without necessity is costume.

### Worked example

The source-grounded model is *The Humans*: an alien mask carries personal alienation and rediscovery. The system transfers the decision to change the mask while keeping the pressure, without borrowing Haig's plot, sentences, titles, or voice.

### Honest edges

Visual evidence from the source video was unavailable. Gesture, facial-expression, and staging claims are therefore excluded. The primary mechanics come from a 56:55 transcript with 9,738 clean words and 1,393 timestamped caption segments.

## 3. Repair specific failure modes

The practitioner routes are deliberately narrow:

- `/haig-authentic-optimism` keeps damage, uncertainty, and cost present so hope changes what action feels possible instead of reassuring the reader away from reality.
- `/haig-structural-pull-edit` is for repeated local failure. It repairs the root layer and then the visible line.
- `/haig-simple-word-residue` removes abstraction and prestige performance, then tests the ending for image, echo, and forward pull.
- `/haig-child-story-reset` recovers person, want, obstacle, stakes, and one concrete generative object when concept density has stopped story motion.

Use one route because its failure signature is present. Do not stack all four as a quality ritual. The smallest intervention that changes the reader experience is the standard.

## 4. Protect taste and prove the result

### Taste boundary

`/haig-taste-boundary` records the writer's pre-consensus response, translates feedback into predicted reader consequences, and separates useful evidence from taste, prestige, and market pressure. External judgment may sharpen the instrument; it cannot replace the original reading automatically.

### Proof route

`/haig-reader-bridge-proof` produces the rubric, source trace, negative-control result, and blind-pass packet. The deterministic verifier then checks five independent surfaces:

1. skill, genius patterns, workflows, and prompt contracts;
2. source package, command wrappers, and live prompt discovery;
3. eight negative controls, including imitation and reach promises;
4. proof-output structure and prohibited claims;
5. the human promotion receipt and latest calibrated PASS.

The final integrated result is:

```text
RESULT PASS — STRUCTURAL_VERIFIED + MODEL_RUNTIME_OBSERVED + HUMAN_APPROVED; MARKET_OBSERVED remains false
```

### Honest edges

A-tier was earned through preference, not indistinguishability. In the published-corpus spot-check, Farrice wanted to continue reading the generated passage and one real passage; another real passage felt disjointed and repetitive. Provenance is calibration, not automatic superiority. The result does not establish Matt Haig endorsement, voice equivalence, publication quality across every format, reader response, sales, or market performance.
