## ⚡ If you only read 10 lines

- Every LinkedIn post must carry a moat AI can't copy: **narrative, data, or physical**. No moat = don't ship. Run `/tc-uncopyable-filter`.
- The filter question, verbatim: "Is there a narrative, data, or physical moat I can add that elevates this above the AI noise?"
- Cheapest moat first: inject 1–2 company-specific anecdotes into existing advice content (Tommy's 10-minute hiring post → ~60k impressions off two anecdote lines).
- Story relevance rule: anecdotes come from sales calls, conferences, customer work — never "I just got engaged, here's what it taught me about B2B sales."
- Reach decline is platform economics, not your fault: lean into Thought Leader Ads at **$30/day (~$900/mo)**, don't post harder.
- TLA sequence is strict: publish link-free → ~1 week organic → edit in PS-line URL → boost. The PS line IS the CTA (boost objectives have no CTA button).
- Target TLAs with an **Apollo company-list upload**, not LinkedIn native filters.
- Daily bend-the-algorithm: 20 connects + 5–10 comments in one 10–15-min window; comments often out-impress posts now.
- Data moat = one proprietary chart, one claim, source line ON the graphic (Peter Walker/Carta pattern; designed for Slack forwarding).

## The mental model

1. **AI commodified content; moats are what's left.** "Valuable content" is now the baseline anyone gets from a prompt. The durable differentiators are things an LLM cannot fabricate: verifiable history (narrative), private numbers (data), photographic proof you exist (physical).
2. **LinkedIn matured; stop grieving organic.** Platforms suppress organic reach as they monetize. The play is arbitrage on the ad unit that still looks organic (TLAs) while manually bending distribution (connects, comments, Apollo lists).
3. **IRL exists to feed the feed.** The Billboard Phenomenon: the LinkedIn photo of the billboard outperforms the billboard. Plan the photo before the event.

## Per-capability sections

### Uncopyable Post Filter (Tier 1)
**What it is:** Three-moat audit + injection pass — scores a post for narrative/data/physical moats, then injects the cheapest available one from real raw material (never invented).
**When to reach for it:** Final gate on every LinkedIn deliverable; teardown diagnostic on a prospect's last 10 posts (sellable as an "Uncopyable Audit").
**When NOT to:** Prose-quality problems — that's `prose_classifier.py` (phrasing) or `/writers-room` (craft). The filter judges substance, not style.
**How to invoke:** `/tc-uncopyable-filter`, or load `skills/tommy-clark-linkedin-growth/workflows/uncopyable-post-filter.md` + `references/prompts-v2/uncopyable-post-audit.md`.
**Worked example:** `extractions/tommy-clark-linkedin-growth/blind-pass-sample-uncopyable-filter.md` — generic hiring listicle → anecdote-moated rewrite.
**Honest edges:** The Claude Test is a judgment call; a lazy pass rubber-stamps weak anecdotes. Fabricated "specifics" are worse than generic advice — the quality gate bans them explicitly.

### Thought Leader Ad Engine (Tier 1)
**What it is:** Organic-to-paid amplification: ICP-weighted winner selection, Apollo company-list audience, the edit-post PS-line CTA sequence, $30/day ramp with weekly scale/kill review.
**When to reach for it:** A post attracted visibly right-fit commenters; monthly paid-organic review on any founder account.
**When NOT to:** No post has proven ICP pull yet (you'd pay to scale the wrong audience), or the post is moat-less (never pay to distribute slop).
**How to invoke:** `/tc-tla-engine`; contract in `references/prompts-v2/thought-leader-ad-launch.md`.
**Honest edges:** PS-line-survives-boost and no-CTA-button-on-boost-objectives are practitioner-reported behaviors (ledger: LIKELY) — **re-verify in Campaign Manager before spending client money**. "Links don't hurt organic anymore" is UNCONFIRMED.

### Data Moat Visualization (Tier 2)
**What it is:** Proprietary data → one-claim branded chart, insight-first title, source line on the graphic, designed for dark-social (Slack/group-chat) forwarding. The Peter Walker/Carta pattern.
**When to reach for it:** Building an authority lane; a take needs proof that can't be dismissed as opinion.
**When NOT to:** Only public data available — re-charting public data is not a moat. Generate proprietary data first (survey/poll), then chart it.
**How to invoke:** `/tc-data-moat`; hand the chart spec to the `dataviz` skill or `fantastic-posters`.
**Honest edges:** Client data needs an aggregation/anonymization check before publishing — the pre-flight gate names it, but the call is the operator's.

### Physical Moat Library (Tier 2)
**What it is:** A capture *system*, not a photo tip: named shooter, office-as-set rule, event-capture rule, rolling 30-day shot list mapped to the content calendar.
**When to reach for it:** Standing up founder-led content anywhere with physical surface area; before any conference, launch, or stunt.
**When NOT to:** As a one-off "add a photo" note — a shot list without an assigned shooter produces zero photos; install the system or skip it.
**How to invoke:** `/tc-physical-moat`; contract in `references/prompts-v2/physical-moat-shot-list.md`.
**Honest edges:** AI-generated "IRL-style" imagery inverts the entire moat — hard ban, no exceptions.
