## Mental model: capability is broad, authority is narrow

The extraction was meant to make the system more capable. Dhar contributes a
way to help an audience feel recognized, experience consequence, reinterpret a
situation, and take one safe positive action. The production expansion carries
that human outcome through packaging, scripting structure, handoffs, editing
intent, feedback, and local capacity decisions.

Reality Before Rhetoric solves a different problem. It helps recover or
calibrate one load-bearing piece of reality when the available source cannot
support the intended claim. That is valuable, but it does not make RBR the
owner of creation. It governs assertion authority at the smallest unsupported
unit.

The repair separates those jobs:

`open creation -> expert craft -> claim-local evidence check -> safe artifact`

The creative system can explore hypotheses, metaphors, structures, packages,
and alternative angles. If a lived scene or outcome is missing, the system can
retrieve it, ask one non-leading question, narrow the claim, mark a placeholder,
or omit that beat. It may not invent the missing connective tissue. One weak
unit does not contaminate every supported unit around it.

## Capability 1: the restored Dhar factory

### What it is

The factory is the harvested expertise layer. It coordinates the Viewer
Transformation Contract, Seen-Heard brief, Packaging Promise Covenant,
accelerated beat map, community co-greenlight loop, recognition mode,
non-preachy transformation route, production-continuity packet, and capacity
learning loop. It keeps one composer responsible for the body.

### When to reach for it

Use it when content needs more than a clever hook. The tell is that Farrice
wants the audience to feel understood, leave with a more useful interpretation,
and want to return because the content consistently improves their inner or
practical state. It fits LinkedIn proof-to-market posts, Parallax editions,
creator video, offer narratives, creative briefs, and production packets.

### When not to

Skip the full factory for a title repair, a mechanical edit, or a finished piece
that needs one bounded diagnosis. Do not force a personal story into research,
status, procedure, or system documentation when evidence should remain primary.
Use a story fragment or no story when that better protects the job.

### How to invoke it

Bring a real source or idea, one audience in a specific situation, their private
sentence, the current belief or feeling, the desired after-state, the format,
the mission boundary, and one safe action. Load Farrice's voice evidence before
visible prose. Let the conductor select the smallest useful workflow stack.

### Worked example

For a LinkedIn proof-to-market post, the factory might begin with the prospect
who keeps producing content but cannot tell whether any of it changes buyer
perception. The source-backed recognition is not “content is hard.” It is the
more private experience of doing visible work while remaining commercially
unrecognized. The package promises a useful reframe, the body shows the cost of
mistaking activity for proof, and the ending gives one testable action. If a
specific Farrice transition is essential but absent, the system marks that
exact hinge instead of fabricating a polished origin story.

### Honest edges

The factory cannot guarantee reach, retention, community, sales, emotional
transformation, or therapeutic benefit. Its three existing receipts validate
components against fixtures. They are not three independent creator runs. The
current readiness remains `54/100 - KEEP AS WORKFLOW`.

## Capability 2: optional Reality Before Rhetoric

### What it is

RBR is a co-creative interview and evidence-calibration practice. It is useful
when one consequential claim, lived scene, causal bridge, outcome, or permission
boundary cannot be resolved from available sources. It searches first, then may
ask Farrice one non-leading question at a time while each answer materially
raises the source ceiling.

### When to reach for it

Use it when the piece depends on a real transition that only Farrice can name,
when an offer claim risks outrunning buyer proof, when research language sounds
more certain than its source, or when a system repair is being called “fixed”
without runtime evidence. The recommendation should name the exact affected
unit and why recovery would improve the intended artifact.

### When not to

Do not invoke it because the task says story, copy, strategy, research, repair,
or content. Do not invoke it when the source is already sufficient, when the
claim can simply be omitted, or when the work is exploratory and clearly
labeled. Do not convert ordinary context gathering into an intimate interview.

### The four hard vetoes

1. A known false or fabricated fact, scene, quotation, or causal hinge.
2. Proof-state promotion, such as structural evidence becoming “working” or an
   untested mechanism becoming a demonstrated outcome.
3. Use of private material outside its authorized boundary.
4. Use, reuse, publication, or action without permission.

Each veto applies to the smallest violating unit. The whole artifact holds only
when that unit is its essential premise and no honest smaller form survives.

### Honest edges

The original V1 helper remains frozen for historical replay. It still contains
the rejected keyword and route logic, but no active workflow imports or calls
it. The restoration verifier fails if Launchpad reconnects it. Archiving or
renaming that helper is a separate repair and is not needed before the field
test.

## Capability 3: the Anti-Shackle regression boundary

### What it is

The Anti-Shackle Constitution protects creative range from accumulated quality
rules. It gives the system three modes:

- `OPEN`: generate, connect, challenge, and explore.
- `SHADOW`: observe possible evidence debt without blocking or adding ceremony.
- `ENFORCE`: block one exact truth, proof, privacy, permission, cost, destructive,
  or external-action violation.

New quality rules do not self-arm. Three receipts alone are not enough. Default
advisory promotion also requires a blind comparison and no material loss of
creative range. Enforcement requires a recognized veto family, zero safe-task
false blocks, and explicit approval.

### Worked regression

The old integration activated RBR for ordinary supplied-fact content, strategy,
research, and system tasks. It also required artifact tiers and content-card
fields. The new frozen set runs six safe controls that must continue without
ceremony, four epistemic violations that must still be caught, and three Dhar
surfaces that must remain intact. This dual test prevents a future repair from
“solving” overconstraint by deleting the useful expert capability.
