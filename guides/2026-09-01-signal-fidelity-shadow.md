# Signal Fidelity SHADOW — What We Built 2026-09-01 and How to Use It

> Signal Fidelity preserves the operator-specific meaning that is most likely to be lost when work passes through sources, decisions, human collaboration, and AI execution. It is not a new workflow or writing format. The active contract is `semantic_libraries/antigravity/primitives/signal-fidelity-minimal-contract.md`; the executable proof is `execution/verify_signal_fidelity_minimal.py`.

## ⚡ If you only read 10 lines

- Signal Fidelity is a silent SHADOW companion, not a command or new owner.
- Public readers see the native content with no receipt, provenance block, or system language.
- Human collaborators receive natural language unless execution risk genuinely requires more.
- AI agents may receive a six-field execution capsule because machines benefit from explicit boundaries.
- Strategy owners may receive an optional three-line decision spine: premise, choice, boundary.
- The layer asks no mandatory questions and creates no safe-work block.
- It may not promote itself, enforce a schema, or add activation surfaces.
- Run `python3 execution/verify_signal_fidelity_minimal.py` after changing its contract or recipient modes.
- Current proof: deterministic recipient behavior and negative controls PASS; long-horizon production impact is `NO EVENT`.
- The doctrine: preserve the signal where translation risk exists and disappear everywhere else.

## The mental model

The original insight was valid: what makes co-creation valuable is not just taste or judgment in the abstract. It is the specific delta an operator adds: the premise they refuse to lose, the distinction that changes the work, the permission boundary, and the felt reason one option is right. That signal often degrades each time the work is summarized, delegated, transformed, or executed by a different kind of recipient.

The first prototype overexpressed that insight. It made the preservation machinery visible, which increased burden and made good work feel more artificial. The repaired version treats fidelity as a translation problem, not a universal document format. Different recipients need different amounts of explicit structure. A public reader usually needs none. A human collaborator can infer more from natural language. An AI agent needs explicit constraints because ambiguity turns into execution drift. A strategy owner benefits from a tiny decision spine only when consequences are real.

The highest-level behavior is therefore selective absence. Signal Fidelity succeeds when it protects the meaning without making the recipient experience feel like a system receipt.

## Capability 1: Recipient-specific preservation

### What it is

One underlying intent is translated into the smallest form each recipient can use. The active fixture defines four experiences: public reader, human collaborator, AI agent, and strategy owner. The system does not expose one universal representation to all four.

### When to reach for it

Use it when a consequential premise, scope boundary, approval state, or “do not rebuild” instruction could be lost between decision and execution. The tell is not complexity alone; it is translation risk with a meaningful downside.

### When NOT to

Do not use it for ordinary prose polishing, conversational collaboration, low-consequence drafts, or public-facing content. Native craft is the cheaper and better owner there.

### How to invoke

There is no public invocation. Existing owners may apply the contract silently. Verification is explicit: `python3 execution/verify_signal_fidelity_minimal.py`.

### Worked example

The blind pilot showed that an AI handoff benefited from a direct execution capsule while the human version became more readable when the same information stayed conversational. Public content improved only when the internal receipt disappeared entirely.

### Honest edges

The verifier proves the designed modes and rejects visible or expanded variants. It does not prove months-long reduction in revisions, better commercial outcomes, or universal recipient preference. Those remain `NO EVENT`.

## Capability 2: The AI execution capsule

### What it is

A six-field capsule that preserves the execution-critical information an AI agent is most likely to blur: intended change, locked premise, allowed transformation, forbidden transformation, evidence or approval boundary, and stopping condition.

### When to reach for it

Use it when an AI agent must execute rather than merely discuss, especially when a wrong assumption would cause scope expansion, rebuild approved material, or fabricate permission.

### When NOT to

Do not paste it under every prompt. If the request is sharp, local, reversible, and already carries its constraints, the capsule adds bureaucracy without fidelity gain.

### Worked example

In the production receipt, both human and AI handoffs recovered all six tested facts. The AI version used more words but made execution boundaries machine-readable. That justified AI-specific structure, not a universal handoff template.

### Honest edges

More fields are not automatically safer. The verifier rejects incomplete capsules, but the system still depends on the owner choosing only consequential information. An exhaustive capsule can become another form of drift.

## Capability 3: The strategy decision spine

### What it is

An optional three-line structure that carries the inherited premise, the actual choice, and the boundary around that choice. It exists for consequential strategy, not as a mandatory planning card.

### When to reach for it

Use it when downstream work could preserve the action while losing why the action was chosen. It is especially useful when several plausible alternatives remain and one premise makes the difference.

### When NOT to

Do not use it for every recommendation or to prove that analysis happened. If the decision is obvious in the native artifact, adding the spine is repetition.

### Worked example

The repaired strategy receipt improved tested recoverability from five of six decision facts to six of six with nine normalized added words. That is the right shape of gain: bounded comprehension improvement at very low burden.

### Honest edges

This was artifact-level proof, not a large independent-human study. The design is justified as optional SHADOW behavior; it is not justified as enforced process.

## Composition and ownership

| Existing owner | Signal Fidelity contribution | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy or domain owner | Optional premise-choice-boundary spine | Cannot replace the owner's judgment |
| Human handoff owner | Preserve critical meaning in natural language | No visible internal receipt by default |
| Agentic engineering owner | Six-field execution capsule when risk earns it | No recursive orchestration or new agent |
| Content craft owner | Owner-only audit before delivery | Public artifact remains native |
| System-audit | Detect burden, drift, or accidental promotion | Does not activate Signal Fidelity globally |

## What was deliberately rejected

The system does not include a Signal Fidelity command, router, agent, mandatory schema, score, hook, task creator, universal provenance block, or public receipt. Those were not missing features; they were the bureaucracy risk the production proof exposed.

Promotion is also rejected for now. Passing structural tests means the bounded architecture is safe to keep. It does not mean the layer should become visible or compulsory. Any future promotion requires new production evidence, preserved creative range, zero false blocks on safe controls, and Farrice's explicit approval.
