## The mental model

**Three ideas make the rest obvious.**

**1. Optimize for trust, not virality — and the extreme version is deliberately losing reach.** Everyone quotes the first half. The v4.0 material supplies the second: sandbagging. If a piece exists to convert, narrow it until only the buyer can stand it — hyper-niche topic, insider terminology left unexplained. Success is named-target inbound; views are noise you declare irrelevant *before* publishing so nobody panics at the number later.

**2. Every decision traces to a selfish outcome.** Caleb refuses "what brand do you want to build" and asks what you want to *have happen as a consequence*. Missions are real but untestable; "speak on more stages" is testable. The four questions convert an outcome into an association, then actions, then a skills roadmap — and multiple goals get multiple maps, never a blend.

**3. Data discipline is a ratchet, not a dashboard.** Medians (averages lie when one video pops), outliers quarantined before you read anything, hypotheses on *structural* variables only (topic, format, hook structure, guest type — never "she wore a red shirt"), then ≤3 validated learnings promoted monthly to a permanent pre-publish checklist. After a year you predict performance instead of hoping for it — the same shape as this repo's own ground-truth promotion loop.

## The capabilities

### Sandbagged Reach (Pattern 33) — the campaign-relevant one

**What it is.** A conversion lane where reach is a *cost*, not a goal. Topic narrowed past mass appeal, insider lingo deployed unexplained so outsiders self-select out, one inbound-shaped metric declared before publishing. Caleb's receipt: a wildly niche video with almost no views got a creative director to reach out because "the struggles that I called out were the struggles they were facing."

**When to reach for it.** You have named targets and need revenue, not audience. Directly applicable to the five-send LinkedIn cash launch.

**When NOT to.** As your only lane — sandbagging everything starves discovery; keep a growth lane running (`/caleb-stage-one-os`, or the existing `/caleb-format-strategy`). If you don't yet know *who* the buyer is, run `/caleb-brand-journey` first; a sandbag plan without named targets is just a boring post.

**How to invoke.** `/caleb-sandbag-strategy`, or read `skills/caleb-ralston-personal-brand/workflows/caleb-sandbag-strategy.md`. Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/sandbag-content-plan.md`.

**Honest edges.** Untested in production. The lingo filter assumes real domain fluency — deployed thinly it reads as jargon, not signal.

### Brand Journey Framework (Pattern 26)

**What it is.** Four questions run backwards from a selfish, dated outcome: what do I want to have happen → what must I be known for → what must I DO ("you don't get known for [it] by just talking. It's actions") → what must I LEARN. Refreshed every 6–12 months because outcomes drift once opportunities arrive.

**When to reach for it.** Discovery calls, quarterly reviews, or any moment where content strategy has no outcome to test against. Strong $2,500-Sprint onboarding artifact — high perceived value, zero production cost.

**When NOT to.** Mid-execution when the outcome is already clear and dated; you'd be re-litigating a settled decision.

**How to invoke.** `/caleb-brand-journey`. Prompt: `references/prompts-v2/brand-journey-map.md`.

**Worked example.** Already run on Farrice's live cash outcome — `extractions/caleb-ralston-personal-brand/blind-pass-sample-brand-journey-farrice.md`. Two maps (cash lane anchored, teaching lane parked at rung 4), with osmosis-decline logic applied to the sequencing.

### Median Multiplier Review (Pattern 32)

**What it is.** 90-day medians per metric, every piece expressed as a multiplier, outliers quarantined first, top-10% studied for structural commonalities, hypotheses forced into the next batch with minimal lag, ≤3 validated learnings ratcheted into a permanent checklist monthly.

**When to reach for it.** Monthly reviews; client-facing data audits — this is a sellable deliverable, not just an internal chore.

**When NOT to.** Under ~30 pieces of history, medians are meaningless. Use `/caleb-stage-one-os` first-signal thresholds instead (baseline ~100 views → one piece at ~415 is your first signal).

**How to invoke.** `/caleb-multiplier-review`. Prompt: `references/prompts-v2/multiplier-review-report.md`.

**Honest edges.** Requires the raw per-piece export; platform analytics UIs won't hand you medians.
