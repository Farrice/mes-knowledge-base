# Voice OS — What We Built 2026-07-07 and How to Use It

> The always-on voice alignment layer: a compiled voice card measured from real corpus, a 4-mode fidelity dial, and a felt-verdict loop that makes the card sharper every time you react to a line. Canonical card: `_active/farrice-brand/voice/VOICE-CARD.md` · spine: `skills/voice-os/SKILL.md` · portable export: `_active/farrice-brand/voice/PORTABLE-VOICE-CARD.md`.

## ⚡ If you only read 10 lines

- Anything under your own name (posts, editions, Notes, emails, DMs, bios) loads Voice OS as a LAYER before the content workflow runs — no exceptions.
- Load order: `skills/voice-os/SKILL.md` → `VOICE-CARD.md` (always) → only the §4 channel register you're writing for.
- The Dial: **MIRROR** 95% (personal comms) · **BLEND** ~70% **default** ("better version of me") · **STRETCH** 40% (explicit exploration) · **OFF** (client work — their voice docs govern).
- Explicit mode naming always wins. Say a mode, get that mode.
- Hard floor never lifts in ANY mode: privacy law, no fabrication about real people, slop ban bank, wince test, no cheap question closes.
- Reaction to a line (praise or wince) → `/voice-ratchet` logs it silently, in-session, verbatim. Never deferred.
- `python3 execution/voice_ratchet.py status` — pending count; 5+ pending = run `/voice-compile`.
- Before a MIRROR/BLEND draft ships: `python3 execution/prose_classifier.py check <file>` + pattern-match against VOICE-CARD §6 PASS/FAIL banks.
- BLEND is not homogenization: identity anchors + voice law + banned moves are HARD; craft and fresh perspectives from expert lenses are WELCOME.
- If VOICE-CARD.md is missing, stop — never improvise your voice from memory.

## The mental model

1. **The card is a floor and a compass, not a ceiling.** Voice OS owns identity — who you are, how you actually talk, what you never say. It owns zero craft. Writers-room, `/parallax`, `/ghostwrite`, and the How-I-Write OS keep full craft authority; Voice OS just keeps the piece inside your fingerprint while they write.
2. **The dial measures fidelity, not quality.** The question each mode answers is "how much of this piece is my literal fingerprint vs. an elevated or explored version of it." Everything sounding exactly like you all the time is explicitly NOT the goal — that's why BLEND, not MIRROR, is the default.
3. **The loop feeds on felt verdicts, not periodic rewrites.** The card improves because reactions get logged the moment they happen, then get compiled. Skip the capture and the loop starves.

## Capability 1: VOICE-CARD.md — the compiled fingerprint

**What it is.** A single canonical card (v1.0, compiled 2026-07-07) with eight sections: §1 Identity Spine, §2 Voice Law, §3 Stylometrics, §4 Channel Registers, §5 Banned Moves, §6 Calibration Bank, §7 The Dial, §8 Loop Protocol. §3 is *measured* from shipped corpus, not vibes — the load-bearing find: sentence-length stdev of 8-10 vs. AI's typical 3-5 (the variance IS the fingerprint), ellipses belong to the LinkedIn/spoken register only, and punch density (~30% ≤5-word sentences) gets dialed in during the tightening pass, not the raw draft.

**When to reach for it.** Any writing task carrying your name. Load the SKILL.md first, then the card, then only the relevant channel register.

**When NOT to.** Client deliverables (Jen, Andrea — OFF; their voice docs govern) and craft work with no identity stakes (research synthesis, deterministic scripts). For those the card doesn't even open.

**How to invoke.** It's grounding to absorb, not a command — content workflows load it per the CLAUDE.md Routing Summary. Never quote the card at a reader or narrate "per my voice guidelines."

**Honest edges.** No email/DM corpus exists — that register is extrapolated and flagged inside the card. The thought-bank inbox was nearly empty at compile time, so raw-speech fidelity is thin; most corpus is approved AI-assisted drafts. More raw verbatim capture directly improves MIRROR mode.

## Capability 2: The Dial — four modes, one floor

**What it is.** MIRROR (95%, full fingerprint including imperfect texture) · BLEND (~70%, identity + voice law + bans HARD, expert craft WELCOME) · STRETCH (40%, only the hard floor holds; a foreign voice may lead) · OFF (client work). Selection cues: "sound like me" → MIRROR; default content → BLEND; "surprise me" / "through X's lens" / 3-variant divergence → STRETCH; client deliverable → OFF.

**When to reach for it.** Every mode decision is implicit in the artifact type — DMs and personal email default MIRROR; LinkedIn posts, editions, and your own offer copy default BLEND; internal memos are MIRROR-adjacent (no polished exemplar exists yet).

**When NOT to.** Don't use STRETCH as a slop loophole — the hard floor (privacy law, no fabrication, ban bank, wince test, no question closes) holds at 40% exactly as it does at 95%.

**Worked example.** The design intent on record: BLEND exists because "everything sounding like him all the time is NOT the goal — don't over-supersede creative influence from other voices." The dial is balance engineering, not mimicry enforcement.

## Capability 3: The ratchet loop — `/voice-ratchet` → `/voice-compile`

**What it is.** The physical write path for calibration. Capture is silent and in-session, same law as thought-bank capture: the verbatim line (never a paraphrase), the verdict (`pass`/`fail` — his reaction, not your judgment), one specific sentence of why, and optionally the source.

**How to invoke.**

```bash
python3 execution/voice_ratchet.py add --verdict <pass|fail> --line "<verbatim line>" --why "<why>" --source "<source>"
```

The script dedupes on exact line text — "Already logged" means stop, don't re-run with variations. `status` reports the pending count and flags RECOMPILE RECOMMENDED at 5+. Compile folds verdicts into §6, checks stylometric deltas, bumps the version, and regenerates the portable card.

**Honest edges.** Re-exporting the portable card to external consumers (Drive, other agents) after a compile is flagged as a recommendation, not automated — it's on you to sync.

## Verification before any MIRROR/BLEND draft ships

Two checks, always: (1) `python3 execution/prose_classifier.py check <file>` — 5+ signals caps Expert Standard at 6 per the root Quality Gate; (2) read the draft's strongest lines against the §6 PASS/FAIL banks — the bank catches voice-specific tells (forced jargon, tells-instead-of-shows, generic question closes) the generic classifier doesn't know. Fail either → rewrite, re-check, then deliver.
