## The mental model

1. **The product is a sentence the buyer says about themselves.** Not "is my design good?" but "can this stop a person who is not trying to buy anything and make them feel something strong enough to act?" The winning question is identity, not craft — her hand-drawn competition-winning woodblock sold zero in four months; a hiker with bold text sold 3,714 (UNCONFIRMED).
2. **Mirror beats poster.** A poster describes a category ("loves the outdoors"); a mirror reflects lived behavior ("the one who needs a water break every 10 minutes"). Buyers are named as behavioral moments, never demographics.
3. **50 milliseconds decides.** The buyer's brain has ruled before the logic loads — so emotion buys and logic justifies afterward, and clarity at scroll speed outranks everything ("one thing loudly rather than three things quietly").
4. **Taste cannot override data.** Kill/scale rules are pre-committed numbers. "The market does not care what you love. The market cares about what it loves."

## 1. `/meg-trigger-audit` — the sell-or-die judgment

**What it is.** Mirror-or-poster gate → 50ms pass → 10-criterion scoring (Identity Signal, Recognition Speed, Specificity, Social Currency, Familiar/Twist, Emotion-First, Wearability, IP Safety, 50ms Clarity, Evergreen Index) → verdicts with ONE copy/design-ready revision directive per REVISE and a recognition lead line per LEAD. "That's nice" at any point = automatic FAIL regardless of arithmetic — "Nice doesn't open the wallet."

**When to reach for it.** 1–12 concepts, any state (files, mockups, text descriptions), before any spend. Also logos, thumbnails, landing pages via the adaptation table.

**When NOT to.** Target buyer unknown — run `/meg-sub-identity-map` first; auditing against "everyone" is auditing nothing. Need concepts, not judgments — `/meg-concept-sprint`.

**Worked example.** MyBPM run: 12 mirror-only concepts scored → 8 LEAD, 4 REVISE, 0 KILL; top leads "The Kandi Keeper" ("I still have your kandi") and "Stranger-to-Friend," both 5.0.

## 2. `/meg-50ms-gate` — the five-minute visual pre-flight

**What it is.** Four fast checks: focal-point count (1–2 pass, 3+ chaos), the 5-word test, the conceptual-interrupt check (surprise must live in the IDEA and lead somewhere — "It's the door. It's not the room."; an AI with 27 fingers is attention WITH confusion), and the squint/shrink test at thumbnail size. One failure = FAIL; the directive names the specific element to remove and the anchor that remains.

**When to reach for it.** Any visual before ad spend — apparel, ad creative, thumbnails, landing heroes, pitch slides. Run it COLD, before reading the brief; a warm eye is the one variable that breaks the test.

**When NOT to.** As a substitute for the full audit — this gate checks survival at scroll speed, not whether the identity triggers fire. It deliberately ignores the brief and the story.

**Honest edges.** Her "1–2 elements = 56% of all sales" stat is UNCONFIRMED — deploy the principle, label the number.

## 3. `/meg-factory-loop` — the operating system

**What it is.** The five-step cadence installed as a written one-pager: GENERATE (90-day volume target from stated capacity: solo part-time 3–5/wk, full-time 8–15/wk, team 20+/wk; ONE product type locked) → TEST (paid ads as measurement instrument: $10–20/day per design, 48h minimum, CPC is the signal) → SCALE OR CUT (the data bands; no second-chance tests; scale in controlled increments — double, not 10×) → EMAIL (post-purchase capture + 2–3 campaigns/week; click-to-conversion, not opens) → REPEAT (no process redesign before week 12).

**When to reach for it.** A brand has validated concepts and needs rhythm, not more tactics. Pre-flight gate: a sub-identity and one validated design-format anchor must exist first — "a cadence installed on top of a market mismatch accelerates the wrong thing."

**When NOT to.** Zero concepts or unvalidated niche → `/meg-sub-identity-map` + `/meg-trigger-audit` first. Diagnosing a leak in an already-running funnel → `/meg-funnel-doctor` is the cheaper tool.

**Worked example.** MyBPM launch plan: 3-design slate (Kandi Keeper, Stranger, Sunrise), 14 days, $200 ad floor; success triggers ROAS ≥2.5 / ATC ≥7% / ≥3 sales; kill triggers ROAS <2.0, zero multi-buys, ATC <5%.

**Honest edges.** All thresholds are POD/Meta 2026 calibration defaults, to be recalibrated on live MyBPM data — and that live launch (designs commissioned, Shopify + Meta + email setup) is still pending Farrice's execution. The method is proven in production vocabulary (Josh swing-nerd shirts); the OS's own numbers are not yet field-validated. Both finalize scores sat at 7.25/10 (marginal band, stable).
