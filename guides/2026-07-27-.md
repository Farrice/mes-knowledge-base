## The mental model

**A spec sample is proof-of-homework wearing the shape of a deliverable.** Its claims about the prospect are the product; one unverifiable claim converts the whole artifact from "operator" to "spammer." So the build order is inverted from normal copywriting: evidence collection first (verbatim corpus with URLs), then copy that only says what the corpus supports, then adversarial review whose findings are resolved *against the corpus* — receipt beats rewrite.

**QA gates are calibrated for OUR voice, not the prospect's.** A prospect's own signature constructions look like AI tells to the classifier and like fabrications to a cold reviewer (Rosner's "90% of your runs" was his own article title; Broxterman's program name drew an "invented fact" flag while sitting verbatim on her live page). The failure mode is obedience: rewriting to satisfy the gate destroys the voice-match that is the artifact's entire value. The discipline is a receipts ledger in the artifact's internal notes.

**Mimicry has an intimacy boundary.** Accuracy doesn't neutralize creepiness on the forward test — a stranger reproducing your emoji sign-off reads as skin-wearing to any third party who lacks the receipts. De-mimic the intimate markers, keep the structural voice, and *name* the one bold mimicry choice in the read ("I matched your opener on purpose; if that's a step too far, it's the first thing I'd cut"). Naming converts presumption into craft-confidence.

## Capability: the receipted-override protocol (prospect-voice spec builds)

**What it is.** A 4-move build discipline for copy in a real person's voice: (1) corpus-first research with mandatory URL receipts, (2) flag resolution by receipt not rewrite, (3) intimate-marker de-mimicry + naming the mimicry aloud, (4) selectivity-objection preemption for capacity-capped offers. Full card: `docs/solutions/2026-07-21-spec-sample-receipted-override-protocol.md`.

**When to reach for it.** Any spec-first cold outreach where the artifact is written as/for a specific real person — the remaining Tier-1/2 prospects (Robertson next), and any future client sample. Also whenever a QA gate flags copy you *know* is grounded: the re-solve guard on the card is the checklist.

**When NOT to.** Content in Farrice's own voice (VOICE-CARD + normal classifier obedience is cheaper and correct — flags there are real). Warm conversations where a sample would over-engineer what a plain message does.

**How to invoke.** No wrapper — it's a protocol: dispatch `deep-research` with the corpus brief (voice samples verbatim + labeled economics + live triggers + confidence labels), build to the artifact shape (Parts 1-4: read / email / post / DM + internal notes with receipts), split-check each piece with `prose_classifier.py check`, dispatch `adversarial-reviewer` with the 3-pass gauntlet brief, resolve findings against the corpus.

**Worked example.** This session, twice. Rosner: research found his newsletter never pitches his own cohort (closes on Stryd/Science in Sport links) and his cohort has no public price — those two facts became the read's thesis and the placeholder discipline. Gauntlet returned 8 findings; 2 "critical" ones (invented stat, invented program name pattern) dissolved against the corpus, 6 were real and fixed. Broxterman: research *corrected the prospect sheet* before copy existed (VIP = $2,500 rolling 1-on-1, not a $42K cohort), which is the protocol's quiet second payoff — the research pass audits your own intel.

**Honest edges.** The true blind test — the prospects reading their own voice cold — hasn't happened; Expert Standard was scored 8, not 9, for exactly this reason. The gauntlet seats are cold-generated personas (Tier 3, prospect-anchored), not harvested corpora. Both artifacts carry placeholders only the prospect can fill; if a reply comes back "send it," nothing new may be improvised mid-thread. And the protocol has run on exactly two artifacts — treat the Robertson build as its third trial, not routine.
