## ⚡ If you only read 10 lines

- **Novelty is a feeling you engineer, not a fact you possess.** No space runs out of new angles on old things — this skill is the water-into-wine conversion for boring/old/saturated topics.
- Finished content end-to-end → `/novelty-engine` (the conductor, with live research + closed-loop Gut-Check repair). Single asset built by hand → `/novelty-forge`.
- Stuck for ideas → `/novelty-angles` (20+ angles). Flat content diagnosis → `/novelty-audit`. Mine your own winners → `/novelty-pattern`.
- The 5 components: New Reveal + Outcome Mapping → Contrast Framing → Urgency (optional, only if REAL) → Bullseye Proof (Trust Ladder) → Protect the Illusion (no hedging, whisper not billboard).
- **Honesty spine, non-negotiable:** only the *novelty* is manufactured — facts, urgency windows, and proof must be real. Fake urgency = auto-fail.
- Reveal + contrast must land in the hook zone (first ~3-5 lines); burying them is the most common losing pattern.
- Honest urgency skip = 1 of 2 points, capping at 9/10 — that's correct, not a defect.
- The doc wins: where any workflow or genius.md diverges from `references/illusion-of-novelty-doc.md`, the doc is truth.
- **Known blind spot:** the Gut-Check scorecard is lane-blind to body coherence — for original-vs-rewrite ranking use `/novelty-to-addictive` or a prose lens, never the novelty scorecard alone.
- Retention after the hook is a different skill: hand off via `/novelty-to-addictive`.

## The mental model

1. **Attention runs on three viewer questions** — Relevant ("do I care?"), Novel ("is this new?"), Interesting ("am I intrigued?"). Relevance is free (call them out). Novelty and intrigue are the work, and for boring categories they must be manufactured. Each component defeats a specific brain default.
2. **New only registers against old.** A reveal means nothing in isolation; the brain reads novelty through contrast with the avatar's *actual* held belief. The gap ("I might be wrong") is the intrigue. Mis-paired contrast — an adjacent or strawman belief — produces confusion, worse than no contrast.
3. **Protect the illusion or lose it.** One hedge ("this is really just X that's existed for decades") shows the mascot and the whole effect collapses. Commit to the storyline; deliver like a gossip whisperer, never a town crier.

## Capability 1 — The build engines (`/novelty-engine`, `/novelty-forge`)

**What it is.** `/novelty-forge` is Kallaway's Co-Writer mode operationalized: a 6-question pre-flight (Path A genuinely-new vs. Path B mine-a-new-angle — ~90% of boring verticals are Path B; held belief; wanted outcome; honest urgency check; highest honest Trust-Ladder rung; pre-spotted mascot temptations), then the 5-component pipeline with a brainstorm-3-to-5-angles-pick-by-outcome-tie selection rule. `/novelty-engine` wraps it with live research (Phase 0.5), the Gut-Check gate with closed-loop repair, and SINGLE/CAMPAIGN/BATCH modes.

**When to reach for it.** A topic that's "too boring/saturated to make interesting," a hook that won't stop the scroll, an old idea that needs to feel new, entering a stale niche.

**When NOT to.** You already have a genuinely sharp angle → the regular content chain is cheaper. Retention/watch-time problems → this is the front end only; that's `kallaway-addictive-storytelling`.

**Worked example.** The forge workflow threads a full build through commercial gutter cleaning — maximally dull vertical — landing on "the month you clean your gutters matters more than how often" tied to a $9,000 water-damage bill. The angle-selection rule (strongest outcome tie, not abstract novelty) is demonstrated live.

## Capability 2 — Diagnosis and calibration (`/novelty-audit`, `/novelty-pattern`)

**What it is.** `/novelty-audit` runs the canonical Gut-Check scorecard (0-2 per component, /10, plus the Illusion Integrity pass/fail override and 3-question sanity test). `/novelty-pattern` is the apex loop: compare your winners vs. losers through the 5 components and extract niche-specific execution rules — "one-size framework, bespoke execution; read your own data."

**When to reach for it.** Content that's accurate but ignored; a batch of posts where some hit and some died and you don't know why.

**When NOT to — the benchmark finding (2026-06-20, 26 runs).** The scorecard is honest and restrained (it did not over-flag human-refined prose, and it refused to fabricate fixes), but it is **lane-blind to body coherence and narrative-loop integrity** — it scored two versions 10/10 apiece when Farrice ranked one clearly above the other. For original-vs-rewrite quality ranking, route to `/novelty-to-addictive` (the only workflow that caught a dropped narrative loop) or a writers-room/prose lens. And watch `/novelty-hook` on long-form: its raw density score misreads it (scored a great curiosity opener 5/10) — trust the prose, not the number.

## Capability 3 — Scale and handoff (`/novelty-campaign`, `/novelty-to-addictive`)

**What it is.** `/novelty-campaign` atomizes one proven angle across assets and platforms; `/novelty-to-addictive` hands the front-end hook to the Addiction Loop for retention. The workflows carry cross-domain adaptation tables (`references/cross-domain-adaptations.md` — 5 components × 10 asset types), so one angle deploys as video, LinkedIn, thread, email, ad, sales page, or ghostwriting.

**When to reach for it.** After `/novelty-pattern` or real performance data says an angle works — scale the winner, not the guess.

**Common sequences:** `/novelty-angles` → `/novelty-reveal` → `/novelty-hook` (idea → angle → hook) · `/novelty-audit` → the routed fix workflow · `/novelty-forge` → `/novelty-campaign` → `/novelty-pattern` (build → scale → calibrate).

## Honest edges (system-wide)

- The 9/10 cap without a real urgency window is by design — a real dated window is the only legitimate 10th point (the AI-boom × wellness pieces earned it with a real Jan-Apr 2026 study).
- Contrast needs the avatar's *actual* held belief — no avatar profile means the mini-canvass (two questions) or `kallaway-audience-obsession` first; guessing the belief breaks Step 2 silently.
