## The mental model

### 1. Diagnose upstream before editing downstream

Most writing systems start at the sentence. This one starts at the bottleneck. A polished draft can still have no worthwhile idea; a defensible idea can still be softened because the writer is afraid to say it. The primary workflow separates those conditions before spending craft effort.

The cheapest valid move follows the verdict. `IDEA` goes to observation mining and bit refinement. `COURAGE` surfaces the feared supported sentence, the evidence for it, and the human decision; the system cannot choose bravery for Farrice. `CRAFT` unlocks the smallest relevant POP repair. Missing discriminating evidence returns `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`.

### 2. Timeliness is a carrier, not permission to distort

A current earns use only when it has a real event, source date, audience fit, and expiry. If those are absent, the system holds. If the window expired, it can preserve the evergreen thesis without pretending the current is live.

When the current passes, `/david-perell-timely-shell-timeless-core` freezes the core claim first. Every shell then carries a claim-delta ledger so urgency cannot quietly inflate certainty, import a new causal claim, or turn cultural proximity into evidence.

### 3. Proof states do not collapse upward

The system distinguishes supplied observation from verified fact, public reps from audience response, audience response from conversion, and conversion from revenue. These labels are operating controls:

- `HOLD`: required evidence or permission is absent.
- `EXPIRED CURRENT`: a real event existed, but the supplied window passed.
- `NO EVENT`: no public rep or market event occurred.
- `NO PERMISSION`: the action would require Farrice's authorization.
- `UNTESTED EFFECT`: the artifact exists, but no outcome has been observed.

That discipline prevents a structurally sound content system from laundering readiness into performance claims.

## Capability 1: Bottleneck triage and idea development

**What it is.** A diagnostic front end plus two idea-building routes. Triage names one primary constraint. Observation Mind-Mine converts supplied details into candidate leads with provenance labels. The 60-20-10 Bit Refinery expands an idea orally, compresses it through repeated passes, and records what meaning was lost.

**When to reach for it.** Use it when edits keep multiplying without improving the center of gravity, or when the draft is technically competent but nobody can state the idea in one sentence.

**When not to.** Do not use it to manufacture personal experience, a controversy, or a hot take. If the request is live signal discovery, route to `attention-hijack-hooks` first and return with a sourced signal packet.

**Worked example.** Runtime fixture F02 supplied a supportable claim the writer was afraid to state. Triage returned the courage route rather than laundering the discomfort into softer copy. F01, an empty viral ambition, stopped instead of inventing a topic.

**Honest edge.** The system can show the decision and its evidence. It cannot make the human courage decision.

## Capability 2: Evidence-safe cultural timing

**What it is.** A three-stage timing layer: diagnose whether a current is real and usable, build a timely shell around a locked timeless core, then schedule multiple supported currents without converting the calendar into fake urgency.

**When to reach for it.** Use it when an evergreen idea may gain relevance from a dated event, platform shift, cultural conversation, or recurring calendar moment.

**When not to.** Do not use it for trend discovery, lifecycle forecasting, publishing, or analytics. `lulu-cheng-meservey-communications` owns dated trajectory work; platform systems own publication and measurement.

**Worked example.** Runtime fixture F05 initially lacked the audience, brand territory, developed claim set, and identifiable event required by the architecture. The workflow correctly returned `HOLD`. Once the fixture supplied those facts, it returned `ROUTE CURRENT`, preserved the thesis, and produced a clean claim-delta ledger. The guardrail was right; the test was repaired.

**Honest edge.** A timely shell can improve fit and comprehension. It does not prove reach, conversion, or revenue.

## Capability 3: POP craft, memory, history, and place

**What it is.** The three preserved POP workflows remain available for diagnosis, compression, and fresh drafting. Past-Present Braid adds a supported historical-to-present structure. Placeful Voice Audit identifies generic location language and separates usable lived detail from missing evidence.

**When to reach for it.** Use POP only after triage returns `CRAFT`. Use the braid when historical evidence should illuminate a present tension. Use placefulness when prose is competent but geographically or materially anonymous.

**When not to.** Do not treat the 2026 interview as POP provenance; those source lanes remain separate. Do not use a place audit as license to invent streets, weather, dialogue, or local identity. Deep line-level literary repair may earn Dan Wang, Susan Orlean, Ocean Vuong, or `high-taste-writing-os` instead.

**Worked example.** Runtime fixture F10 withheld place evidence. The audit returned a bounded heatmap and evidence request, not decorative local color. Fixture F11 asked only about POP provenance and received a `Provenance Boundary` rather than an unsolicited rewrite.

**Honest edge.** This layer protects evidence and structure. Farrice's taste verdict still decides whether the prose feels alive.

## Capability 4: Reps and creative-portfolio protection

**What it is.** Public Reps Learning Loop distinguishes authorized practice from claimed effect. Current-or-Soul Portfolio gives dated attention opportunities a bounded allocation while protecting slow, soul-stirring work with stop rules and a review date.

**When to reach for it.** Use it when public practice is becoming a vague virtue claim, or when timely content opportunities are consuming the work that matters most.

**When not to.** Do not use it to publish, schedule externally, or infer performance. Those require separate permission and actual events.

**Worked example.** Runtime fixture F14 prevented viral reach from becoming a quality or revenue claim. F17 kept a private observation distinct from a public rep and correctly returned `NO EVENT` for outcome evidence.
