## Core Operating System

Content for tech founders must balance brand-building with lead generation through a rigid weekly structure (4-3-2-1 matrix) while making technical expertise feel personal and battle-tested. Every post follows "How I" not "How to," wraps education inside personal credibility arcs, and targets both buyers (ICP) and algorithmic cheerleaders (IFP) simultaneously.

---

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The 4-3-2-1 Content System
Map exactly 4 posts per week (Edu, Story, Edu-Tell, Edu) against 3 pillars (TAM, Growth, Sales) targeting 2 personas (ICP buyer, IFP cheerleader) offering 1 Lead Magnet. Eliminates decision fatigue and ensures strategic balance across the week.

### GP2: The Unselfish "Financial Fast Principle"
Transition instantly from a "me" story to a "you" payoff — audiences only care about stories that benefit them. Inject a financial or time-saving hook directly into the first two lines of any personal anecdote. Success: engagement driven by comments discussing lessons, not congratulations.

### GP3: "Edu-Telling" Technical Translation
Prevent dry technical education by wrapping how-to payloads inside personal credibility arcs. Open with a 1-sentence authority marker ("I built two B2B SaaS..."), then transition instantly into the technical tutorial. Generates broad non-technical engagement while establishing builder authority.

### GP4: Format Reverse-Engineering
Strip formatting from viral outliers in UNRELATED niches and inject your technical knowledge into that formatting. Clone structural elements (line breaks, lists, bolding) of a viral hook/post and rewrite words completely for the tech niche. Achieves predictable virality through proven visual formatting.

## Signature Moves

1. **The Double-Tap Hook** — Crafts two distinct lines, each 8 words or less, where the second creates an open loop compelling "see more" click. Deploy when initiating any social media content.
2. **The SLAY-First Blueprint** — Outlines content using Story, Lesson, Actionable, You framework before writing the body. Deploy for value-dense posts needing reader retention.
3. **The "How I" Reframe** — Shifts any generic "how-to" advice into proprietary "how-I" narrative embedding battle-tested methodology. Deploy when sharing insights or best practices.
4. **The 30-Minute Engagement Sprint** — Immediately upon publishing, dedicates 30 uninterrupted minutes responding to every comment to trigger algorithm's initial boost. Deploy when any high-priority content goes live.
5. **The Un-Tag Authority** — References high-profile individuals without tagging their @ handle unless back-channel confirmation of immediate engagement exists. Deploy when leveraging halo effect without risking algorithmic suppression.
