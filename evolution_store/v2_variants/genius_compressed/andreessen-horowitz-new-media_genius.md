## Genius Patterns

### P1: The Binary Commitment
All communication is old-media OR new-media. No hybrid. Old-media instincts (approval gates, PR review, corporate voice) actively sabotage new-media dynamics. Classify every decision.

### P2: The 80-Year Aberration
Corporate branding (1940s-2020s) = artifact of narrow channels, not natural state. Internet is ending this. Direction of history = MORE personality-first, founder-direct. The old model was always fake.

### P3: The Interestingness Test
Primary quality gate: "Is this interesting?" — never "Is this safe?" Boring is the one unforgivable sin. CEO who "deliberately said nothing and was thrilled" = anti-exemplar.

### P4: Gaming Lobby Genealogy
Internet toxicity traced: CoD lobbies → Something Awful → YouTube → social comments. Understanding this origin DEFUSES fear. Comments come from 4-follower accounts or bots. Ignore defensive instincts.

### P5: Structural Founder Advantage
Professional CEOs selected through political processes rewarding inoffensiveness — structurally incapable of new media. Founders have original ideas = inherently interesting. Selection mechanism, not personality.

### P6: Talent Inversion
Traditional media experience = LIABILITY. Hired 18-year-old for Instagram because native platform taste > experience. Optimal hire: grew up on the platform, understands VIBE not just mechanics.

### P7: Long-Form First
Every complex/controversial position established in long-form BEFORE short-form extraction. Context IS the defense mechanism. No hot take without long-form anchor.

### P8: Flood the Zone
When attacked, never defend. Produce 3-5 genuinely interesting pieces in 36 hours. Viral memory window: 12h up → 24h down → 36h forgotten. Attack erased by volume.

### P9: Platform-Native Staffing
One obsessed expert per platform. No cross-posting. Content born on each platform. Each platform = distinct culture requiring native-born content.

### P10: The Joe Rogan CEO Standard
Measure: could CEO hold 3-hour unscripted conversation and be most interesting person in room? If no → not ready for new media. This is the bar.

### P11: OODA Loop Speed
Measure decision-to-publish cycle. Compress it. Entity cycling fastest causes permanent competitor paralysis. Speed > polish.
