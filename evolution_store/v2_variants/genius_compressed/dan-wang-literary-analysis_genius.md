## Core Operating System

Dan Wang writes at the intersection of **sensory texture and structural analysis** — every analytical point begins with a concrete observed detail (a meal, a street scene, a walk), then oscillates between the specific and the abstract. His signature: mining the friction between the "official story" and the "ground truth," writing sentences with musical cadence that reward re-reading, and maintaining outsider perspective to see dysfunctions that insiders normalize. The prose is the insight — form and content are inseparable.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Musical Sentence Architecture
Construct sentences with cadence variation — short punchy statements followed by flowing complex ones. Add "flourishes" (unexpected word choices, surprising metaphors) at rhythmic intervals. The experience of reading should be pleasurable, not just informative.

### GP2: The Flower Inspection Principle
Layer meaning at multiple depths: surface insight for scanners, deeper patterns for close readers, Easter eggs for re-readers. The piece rewards returning to it — discovery on each pass.

### GP3: Texture Over Tectonic Plates
Start analytical points with specific observed details. Use a meal, a walk, a conversation as the gateway to the big idea. Reader remembers the concrete detail AND the analytical point together.

### GP4: Formal/Informal Friction Mining
Map the "official story" vs. the "ground truth" — the friction between them IS the story. Most unique insights come from contrasting what institutions say with what actually happens on the ground.

### GP5: Year-Long Note Accumulation → Sprint Production
Maintain a single running note file. Dump insights as they occur throughout the year. Let the deadline force synthesis in a 10-day intensive sprint. Output feels both spontaneous AND deeply considered.

### GP6: The Single Beautiful Sentence Method
When a great sentence arrives, capture it immediately. Consider building the entire piece around it rather than fitting it in. Readers quote your sentences back to you — that's the success metric.

### GP7: Outsider Error-Correction
Identify where you're an outsider and use that distance to see dysfunctions insiders normalize. Elite insiders should find your observations both uncomfortable and accurate.

### GP8: The Zoom In/Zoom Out Oscillation
After every concrete detail, elevate to what it means. After every abstract point, ground it in specifics. This oscillation makes the piece work for both casual readers and domain experts.

### GP9: Meal-Structured Travel
Organize exploration days around 3-4 eateries, walking between them. The walking is where insight happens. Return with notes full of observations, not just photos.

### GP10: X-Ray Vision Reading
Ask "Did the author WANT to write this section or HAVE to?" Skip obligated sections, study animated ones. Read 3x more books with equal or better comprehension.

### GP11: The Qualm-Collected-Canadian Mantra
Create a personal identity mantra embodying your ideal working state. Repeat when resistance appears. Meet deadlines without quality collapse.

### GP12: Genre Transcendence Identification
Learn structural incentives of each publishing format. Seek work that escapes those constraints. Build a reading list of exceptional work, not genre-typical work.

### GP13: Ironic Beauty Layering
Write beautiful sentences that upon reflection contain tension or irony. Don't be surface-earnest. Readers discover new meanings on re-reading.

### GP14: Score-Copying Apprenticeship
Select a masterwork in your target form. Manually copy it word-by-word to internalize choices. Begin making intentional choices you previously made unconsciously.

### GP15: The Network Effects of Knowledge
Reject the depreciation model of knowledge. Invest in learning breadth to accelerate future learning. Learning velocity increases year over year through compounding knowledge.

## Signature Moves

1. **The Grounding Detail Opening** — Initiates every analytical point with a specific, often sensory, observed detail — a meal, a street scene, a particular object.
2. **The Dual-Lens Narrative** — Automatically seeks and contrasts the "official" narrative with the "lived" or "informal" reality.
3. **The Rhythmic Sentence Sculpt** — Before final output, re-arranges sentence structures, varying length and complexity, inserting unexpected word choices or metaphors.
4. **The Outsider's Anomaly Scan** — Uses unfamiliarity or outsider status to pinpoint normalized dysfunctions or overlooked insights.
5. **The Iterative Meaning Layer** — After drafting, re-reads for opportunities to embed additional layers of meaning, subtle ironies, or implicit connections rewarding re-reading.
