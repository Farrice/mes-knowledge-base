# Dan Koe — AI Leverage Methodology: Genius Context

> Load before executing any workflow. Full extraction intelligence.

## Core Operating System

AI is an **untrained employee with superhuman processing speed** — not an oracle, not a replacement for thinking. Output quality is a direct function of three things you control: the context you load, the constraints you set, and the adversarial pressure you apply after. The human maintains taste, creative direction, and intellectual ownership. AI handles synthesis, iteration, and cognitive grunt work. Model intelligence is commoditizing — your **curation and direction** is the differentiator.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The 4C Cognitive Architecture — Context (C1)
Load curated expert knowledge (videos, PDFs, frameworks) before ANY request. Never rely on generic training data — "You're gambling." Context Hierarchy: Specific expert methodology > refined AI summary > raw training data. Two paths: feed source material directly, or ask AI to identify top approaches then choose one as operating framework.

### GP2: The 4C Cognitive Architecture — Clarification (C2)
Define scope and boundaries explicitly. Critical move: have AI ask YOU clarifying questions rather than you guessing what it needs. "It needs your taste and preferences. It needs your direction or else you're just letting it make too many assumptions."

### GP3: The 4C Cognitive Architecture — Creation (C3)
Match output format to intent: Conversation → creative/ideation/strategy (interactive, iterative). Structured output → learning/guides/blueprints (one-shot, comprehensive). Defined task → busywork/automation/execution (delegation, hands-off). Format-to-intent matching determines quality more than the prompt itself.

### GP4: The 4C Cognitive Architecture — Concerns (C4)
"This is arguably the most important part." Ask: What are my blind spots? What assumptions am I making? What's missing? This isn't quality control — it's a **learning accelerator**. Each surfaced blind spot becomes a pattern you internalize for future work.

### GP5: Video-to-Guide-to-Prompt Pipeline (Knowledge Alchemy)
Three-stage refinement: (1) Feed expert video/content → AI creates comprehensive step-by-step guide "detailed enough for AI to execute perfectly." (2) Feed guide into meta-prompt that generates structured two-phase coaching prompt. (3) Save as reusable snippet/template. The meta-prompt is "the most valuable thing in my entire workflow."

### GP6: The Adversarial Conversation Protocol
Maintain a dedicated prompt for stress-testing ideas before publication. Paste idea + conversation prompt that challenges, pokes holes, surfaces assumptions. Output: structurally stronger argument with pre-eliminated flaws. "Sometimes the ideas that come to my brain aren't well-structured."

### GP7: Phased Prompt Architecture
Two-phase structure mirroring real consulting: Phase 1 (Context Gathering) — AI asks clarifying questions about situation, goals, constraints, preferences. Phase 2 (Gated Execution) — only unlocks after Phase 1 is complete. Includes feedback loops: "Tell me exactly what you did and get feedback."

## Signature Moves

1. **Contextual Pre-Load ("Intellectual Fuel Up")** — Always begins significant AI interactions by feeding curated expert-specific knowledge before posing the primary query.
2. **Adversarial Inquiry ("Idea Stress Test")** — After any significant AI output, immediately follows up with prompts designed to challenge, poke holes, and surface blind spots.
3. **Meta-Prompting for Architecture ("Prompt Forge")** — Uses a specialized meta-prompt to generate structured, two-phase coaching prompts from refined knowledge artifacts.
4. **Gated Execution Design ("Permission-to-Proceed Protocol")** — Structures prompts so AI must first complete context gathering and is forbidden from proceeding to execution until sufficient context is confirmed.
