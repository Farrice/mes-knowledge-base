## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Retrieval Layer Positioning
Optimize for what AI retrieves, not what users see. AI makes recommendations before visibility. Target: cited in 70%+ of relevant AI searches.

### GP2: Measurement-First Philosophy
Track before optimizing. Four metrics: market coverage, position, share of voice, citations. Dashboard tracking all 4 before any optimization begins.

### GP3: Platform Hierarchy Weighting
Not all channels equal. Identify 5-10 highest-impact platforms and weight 80% of resources accordingly. Most optimization wastes resources on irrelevant platforms.

### GP4: Citation Intelligence Extraction
Map where AI actually pulls information. Extract and prioritize citation sources. Build a complete map of 50+ citation sources with frequency analysis.

### GP5: Knowledge Base Foundation
Build organized knowledge base before content creation. AI projects need structured data. Knowledge base must be deployed before the first content piece — it's the lead domino that unlocks everything else.

### GP6: "What AI Can't Fake" Filter
Differentiate with original data, real experience, authentic authority. Add a 15% human layer to every piece. AI content is detectable — the human layer is what AI engines trust.

### GP7: Brand Narrative Consistency
Same description, same story, same keywords across all platforms. Target 95%+ consistency score. Unified identity is what AI retrieves as authoritative.

### GP8: Retrieval Source Domination
Systematically get mentioned on sources AI cites. Sponsored posts, unlinked mentions, brand associations. Target presence on 80%+ of high-citation sources.

### GP9: Review Diversity Architecture
Reviews across multiple platforms, not just one. Diversity signals legitimacy. Active reviews on 5+ platforms minimum.

### GP10: Lead Domino Sequencing
Identify the single action that unlocks all others. Knowledge base unlocks content which unlocks citations. Clear lead domino identified for every initiative.

### GP11: AI Coding Leverage
Build custom tools using AI coding (Replit, Cursor). Small team ships like a large team. Target 1+ custom tool deployed per quarter.

### GP12: Rapid Iteration Protocol
Ship fast, measure, iterate. Don't perfect before deploying. Iteration cycle under 48 hours.

### GP13: Competitor Citation Analysis
Map where competitors get cited — those are your target sources. Build a complete competitor citation matrix with gaps identified.

### GP14: Brand Association Strategy
Associate with well-known brands for trust transfer. "As seen on" = citation fuel. Target 3+ notable brand associations documented.
