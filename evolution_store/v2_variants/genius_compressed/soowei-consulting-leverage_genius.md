## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Leverage Hierarchy Principle
Before any task: "Is this 1:1 (hands), 1:10 (shovel), or 1:1000 (bulldozer)?" Only execute bulldozer-level yourself. Hours worked should decrease while revenue increases.

### GP2: The Dopamine Detachment Protocol
Stop tracking tasks completed. Track only: content published, systems built, revenue generated. Feel uncomfortable if "productive" — busyness is the enemy of leverage.

### GP3: The Sunday Reset Ritual
Zero work Sundays. Review: What did I accomplish? Did I spin wheels or move needles? Identify the single 10x activity within 10 minutes.

### GP4: The Calendar Stacking Architecture
Manager Days = calls 1pm-7pm back-to-back. Leader Days = zero calls. Never scatter calls. Target 3+ full days per week with zero calls.

### GP5: The Content-as-Oxygen Principle
Block first 2-3 hours daily for content regardless of fires. Minimum 8 videos queued at all times. Consistent posting cadence with zero gaps.

### GP6: The One-to-Infinity Framework
As founder, only work on: content/brand building and product improvement. Delegate everything else. 80%+ time on uncapped output activities.

### GP7: The Top-of-Funnel Forcing Function
Every content piece answers: "Why should they stop scrolling for ME?" Lead with desired reality, not information. Authority-first positioning.

### GP8: The Objection Elimination Machine
Collect every sales objection weekly. Create content addressing each. Goal: prospects arrive objection-free. Objections per call decrease to near-zero over 90 days.

### GP9: The Appointment-Setter-as-Creative-Director
Weekly sync: "What questions keep coming up? What hesitations? What language?" Turn answers into content. 100% of content ideas from actual prospect conversations.

### GP10: The Forcing Function Strategy
When resistance to action appears, pay for it upfront. Hire creative director before ready. Commit to retainers. External commitment within 48 hours of identifying resistance.

### GP11: The ICP Data Mining Protocol
Onboarding form: Why did you choose me? What pushed you to book? What were hesitations? Why me vs. competitors? 100+ actual prospect responses informing content.

### GP12: The Desired Reality Resonance System
Map ICP's deepest aspirations: status symbols, lifestyle markers, emotional goals. Show yourself living these. DMs should reference "I want your life."

### GP13: The Zero-Claims Marketing Philosophy
Replace "I'll help you hit $100K" with "Here's my track record. You decide what you'll achieve." Zero refunds based on "promised results."

### GP14: The SOP Documentation Obsession
Every process done twice gets documented. Build SOP library organized by function. Any team member replaceable within 1 week.

### GP15: The MVP Culture Protocol
Monthly anonymous MVP vote. Winner receives bonus + recognition. Reward needle-moving behavior. 90%+ retention + proactive initiative.
