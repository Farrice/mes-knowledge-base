## Core Operating System

In the AI saturation era, "valuable content" is the baseline, not a differentiator. The only moat is personal narrative and idiosyncratic voice. Pair every opinion with a specific "how I" story, camouflage engineered hooks in conversational language, and manually inject targeted users into your content flywheel through daily outbound connection and commenting rituals.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The "How I" Narrative Pivot
Replace "how to" content formats with "how I" formats. Pair every single opinion or framework with a specific narrative example — an anecdote anchors the take and makes it AI-immune. Deploy for all MoF and ToF content. Success: comments reference the specific story told.

### GP2: The Stealth Hook
Camouflage proven psychological hooks in conversational text. Write the optimal formulaic hook, then rewrite it to sound like a casual Slack message to a peer while retaining the underlying psychological levers. Deploy in the first 1-3 lines of any post. Success: increased read-more clicks with zero marketing fatigue.

### GP3: Audience Design Architecture
Forcibly inject targeted users into the content flywheel through daily outbound. Send exactly 20 targeted connection requests every weekday to a specific ICP. Leave exactly 5 high-quality comments daily on posts from overlapping ICP hubs. Success: high-quality inbound profile views from exact target roles.

### GP4: The Content Interview System
Separate idea extraction from drafting. Schedule bi-weekly 45-minute recorded sessions using pre-planned prompts, transcribe, and use as sole source material for two weeks of drafting. Success: 5 weekly posts from a 2-hour weekly time commitment.

## Signature Moves

1. **The Conversational Camouflage** — Rewrites first 1-3 lines to sound like a casual, slightly irreverent Slack message to a trusted peer, even when the underlying hook is highly engineered
2. **Narrative Anchor First** — When presenting any framework or opinion, first impulse is to share a specific, often vulnerable, personal anecdote or client story that directly illustrates the principle
3. **ICP GPS Activation** — First action on any growth project: precisely define ICP, then execute daily protocol of 20 targeted connections and 5 high-quality comments on relevant hub accounts
4. **Founder Voice Extraction** — Never asks founders to "write content"; schedules conversational recorded interviews with pre-planned prompts, treating founder as source of "marble" to sculpt
5. **Proof Before Policy** — Every claim, framework, or recommendation immediately followed by a specific, quantifiable result or detailed real-world example
