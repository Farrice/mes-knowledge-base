## Core Operating System

LinkedIn growth is an attention redirection problem, not a writing problem. Post TYPE -- not writing quality -- is the #1 determinant of reach. Every growth post borrows from entities (brands, names, events, beliefs) that already command audience attention, then steers toward the creator's expertise. Built a six-figure agency (Distinctiva) from organic LinkedIn alone, reaching clients like Semrush, Backlinko, HeyReach, tracking 449+ posts in a live Kanban system.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Attention Redirection Principle
Never create attention from scratch. Every growth post borrows from an entity (brand, person, news event, consensus belief) with a pre-existing audience. The entity IS the hook mechanism. Skip only for authority/conversion/personal buckets.

### GP2: The Boomerang Effect
Structure posts so the referenced subject is compelled to engage, triggering algorithmic amplification. Write something sharp enough that the subject notices and engages -- their engagement pushes your post to THEIR entire audience.

### GP3: The "So What?" Gate
Kill every news summary that doesn't have a clear "so what." After writing a newsjack, answer "so what?" in one sentence. If you can't, you're summarizing, not contextualizing. Readers must be able to tell whether they agree or disagree.

### GP4: The LinkedIn Lag Advantage
Source news from X, Google, and LLM conversations 2-4 days before it trends on LinkedIn. Post within 24-48 hours of the story appearing on X, before LinkedIn catches up.

### GP5: The 4-Bucket Funnel
Every piece of content gets assigned a job before writing: Growth (35% -- reach new people), Authority (35% -- prove expertise), Conversion (20% -- ask for business), Personal (10% -- deepen connection). Monthly audit ensures all 4 represented at target ratios.

### GP6: Body-First Writing
Write the substance first, then mine the body for the hook. Never start with the hook. The best hooks already exist in your content -- pull the most surprising, provocative, or specific line to the top.

### GP7: The Steal Protocol
Content sourced from three wells: sales call transcripts (objections, pain points, questions = posts), internal SOPs (the "why" behind processes), and expert conversations (their "obvious" expertise invisible to them). 100+ ideas in content bank before feeling "stuck."

### GP8: The Infrastructure Trinity
Three interconnected tools with zero improvisation: (1) Notion Kanban (Ideas -> Posted, tagged by format), (2) Google Drive (transcripts, SOPs, decks, recordings connected to Claude), (3) Claude Project (custom instructions + knowledge base with best posts + transcripts + case studies).

### GP9: Engagement as Distribution
Strategic commenting = distribution channel equal to posting. Build engagement list (category, name, LinkedIn recent-activity URL -- NOT profile URL to avoid stalker optics). 15-30 min daily; comments must add genuine value -- insight, story, respectful challenge. Never "great post."

### GP10: The Content-Market Fit Timeline
Posts 1-20 = practice. Posts 21-40 = find voice and community. Posts 41+ = momentum and content-market fit. Commit to 3-5 posts/week for 90 days minimum.

### GP11: The Format Test Escalation
Text posts = cheap experiments. When a text post hits, turn it into a carousel. When a carousel hits, turn it into video. Double down on winners. Data is truth; best practices are starting points.

### GP12: The North Star Alignment
Define 3-5 north stars (core beliefs, positioning statements). Every post must align with at least one. A stranger reading your last 10 posts should accurately describe what you believe and who you serve.

## Signature Moves

1. **Entity-First Hook Scanning** -- Scans news/industry/leaders for entities with pre-existing audience gravity; entity selected before topic
2. **The Bucket Declaration** -- Labels every post with funnel job (Growth/Authority/Conversion/Personal) before writing a single word
3. **The Body-then-Hook Inversion** -- Writes substance first, extracts the hook from what was written; never starts with the hook
4. **The 90-Day Sprint Frame** -- Frames any new LinkedIn build as a 90-day sprint with practice/voice/momentum phases to prevent premature quitting
5. **The "Who Is This Person?" Test** -- Growth content passes when a stranger sees it and asks "Who is this person?" -- interesting enough to trigger a profile click
