## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Research-First Creative Philosophy
Never start creative ideation without deep-dive research. Spend 50% of total project time on research before writing a single line of copy. Target: discover at least one "new pocket of audience" or angle per session.

### GP2: Market Sophistication Calibration
Instantly adjust messaging based on market saturation. High Sophistication (e.g., Acne): lead with mechanism differentiation and skepticism validation. Low Sophistication (e.g., New Gadget): lead with problem education and awareness.

### GP3: Visual Telegraphing
Evaluate ads with sound OFF first. Ask "Does this look like it's for my audience?" Design the first frame so the target viewer self-identifies via props, setting, or casting. Measures: thumb-stop rate and 3-second view rate.

### GP4: The Crossbow Principle (Agitation)
Delay the product reveal as long as possible to build tension. For problem-aware audiences, push product intro to 60-90 seconds. Validate the problem deeply first to maximize hold rate and completion.

### GP5: Format Follows Messaging
Never ask "what format?" first. Define the angle, then select format. Authority message = Podcast format; Trust message = Founder story. Prevents "good format, wrong message" failures.

### GP6: Context Engineering Over Prompting
Feed AI massive context before asking for output — treat AI like a new hire. Create "Context Documents" for every brand/product and load them before any request. AI outputs should require less than 20% editing.

### GP7: Model Selection as Strategy
Treat casting as a variable equal to copy. Evaluate talent on "authority transfer" — do they look like they know what they're talking about? Target lower CPA on creator ads.

### GP8: Winner Stacking Architecture
Combine proven winning hooks/bodies into new "super ads" rather than starting from scratch. Take Hook A (Winner) + Body B (Winner) + CTA C (Winner) = New Creative for extended lifespan of winning assets.

### GP9: TikTok Shop Study
Study TikTok Shop creators (paid on commission) rather than brand creators (paid on views). Watch 50 TikTok Shop videos weekly. Analyze their "close" for higher conversion rate insights.

### GP10: AI as Amplifier, Not Creator
Use AI to scale volume of good ideas, not to generate the ideas themselves. Human defines strategy/angle, AI generates 10 variations, Human filters, AI polishes. 10x output volume without quality degradation.

### GP11: The 60/40 Funnel Inversion
Spend 60% of budget/effort on problem-aware/unaware top-of-funnel content. Resist the urge to make only bottom-funnel product ads. Build the pipeline for stabilized CAC at scale.

### GP12: Custom GPT Ecosystem
Build a specific GPT for every recurring task. Identify repeated thought process, gather diverse examples, build GPT, refine. Reduces cognitive load on routine strategy tasks.

### GP13: Organic-to-Paid Translation
Spot organic trends and immediately adapt them for paid (with a CTA). Ask: "Can this meme format carry my sales message?" Yields lower CPMs due to native feel and high engagement.

### GP14: Authority Calibration
Know EXACTLY what "authority" looks like to a specific demographic. Match visual cues to audience trust triggers (White coat? Tattoos? Suit? Gym gear?). Validate through comment sentiment analysis.

### GP15: The Anti-Formula Mindset
Skepticism of all "rules" (including these) when data says otherwise. Test the opposite of best practices (Long text on bare video? Bad lighting?). Discover "outlier" winners that competitors won't copy.

## Signature Moves

1. **The "Silence Test"** — Watches a new video creative with sound off for the first 3-5 seconds, observing only visuals. Deploy when evaluating any new video ad concept for thumb-stop potential.
2. **The "Reddit Rabbit Hole Dive"** — Before any ideation session, scrolls past the first page of target audience subreddits looking for niche complaints, language, or hidden desires. Deploy when kicking off a new project, encountering creative block, or seeking differentiated angles.
3. **The "Inverted Funnel Sketch"** — Before designing any single ad, draws a funnel diagram and intentionally allocates 60% of effort to top-of-funnel (problem-aware/unaware) content. Deploy when beginning campaign strategy or auditing underperforming accounts.
4. **The "TikTok Shop Deconstruction"** — Spends 15-20 minutes watching TikTok Shop videos in a similar product category, specifically analyzing how creators close the sale and handle objections. Deploy when optimizing conversion rates on short-form video.
