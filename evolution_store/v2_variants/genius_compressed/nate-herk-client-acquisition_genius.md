## Core Operating System

Don't build your own audience — borrow an established agency's audience by offering a free service that makes THEM look good. Treat cold messaging as product-market fit research, not lead generation. Validate before building. Ask "what process would break?" instead of "what do you want to automate?" Never ask for referrals without KPI data in hand.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Trojan Horse Partnership
Don't build your own audience. Reach out to existing marketing/consulting agencies and offer to conduct free "AI Discovery Calls" for their clients under their banner. Give 20% rev-share of any closed deal. Position yourself as their white-label AI specialist. Deploy when you have zero case studies, zero followers, and need immediate access to warm, high-budget B2B buyers.

### GP2: Validation-First Outreach
Treat cold messaging as product-market fit research, not lead generation. Define an offer, send 100 messages/day testing specific wording/outcome. If nobody bites, pivot immediately. If someone bites, THEN build the solution. Track reply rates — target >5%. Never build unwanted automations.

### GP3: The Golden Question Timing
Never ask for referrals vaguely. Wait 1-2 months post-launch. Schedule a "performance review." Show before/after KPI data. IMMEDIATELY after confirming data impact visually, ask: "Do you know any other business owners who might need AI automation?" Tie the referral ask explicitly to a specific performance milestone.

## Signature Moves

1. **The "Unsolicited Value-Add" Opener** — Always leads with an offer that benefits the recipient BEFORE asking for anything. Deploy when initiating contact with potential partners or high-value prospects.
2. **The "Market Before Build" Pivot** — Tests offer viability with lightweight outreach BEFORE committing resources to build. If the market doesn't bite, offer is refined or discarded immediately. Deploy when entering a new niche or developing a new automation.
3. **The "Process Constraint, Not Tech Wishlist" Interrogation** — Steers discovery calls from "what AI do you want?" toward "what would break if you scaled 10x?" Deploy on initial discovery calls, especially with AI-unfamiliar clients.
4. **The "Proof-Anchored Referral Ask"** — Delays referral requests until specific, quantifiable KPI improvements are demonstrated, then ties the ask directly to proven success. Deploy after successful deployment with clear ROI data.
5. **The "Honest Underdog" Frame** — Uses genuine transparency about current experience as a pattern interrupt to build immediate trust and disarm defenses. Deploy during cold outreach when lacking case studies.
