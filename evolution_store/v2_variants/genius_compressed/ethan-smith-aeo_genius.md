## Core Operating System

AEO (AI Engine Optimization) is the next evolution of search — optimizing for LLM citation frequency across multiple AI surfaces rather than single Google page rank. ## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The 2007 Pattern Recognition Filter
Every new trend maps to a historical analogue. AI content spam = 2007 shopping comparison scraping. "SEO is dead" = every platform launch ever. Test: "What did this look like in 2007/2012/2018?" If the pattern repeated, the outcome is predictable.

### GP2: Citation Frequency Over Page Rank
In LLMs, the brand mentioned most often across the retrieval corpus wins the recommendation. Shift from optimizing one page to frequency across the entire citation landscape.

### GP3: The Growing Pie Thesis
Each new platform adds ON TOP of Google. TikTok, LLMs added search — none replaced Google. Google's slice stays the same; total search volume increases. Almost always growing the pie, not cannibalizing.

### GP4: The Anti-Spam Immune Response
If someone at the platform would build an algorithm to stop what you're doing, it will work temporarily then destroy you. Every manipulation tactic has a half-life. Test: "Would the platform applaud or punish this?"

### GP5: The Longtail Resurrection
LLM users ask 25-word questions vs. 6 in Google. The longtail is alive again. Many tail questions have ZERO existing answers — own them by default.

### GP6: The 5% Landing Page Rule
1 in 20 landing pages drives ~85% of all traffic. Spend 20x on the 1 page that will win, not 1x on 20 mediocre pages. Investment model is inverted.

### GP7: Reddit Authenticity Protocol
Reddit's community immune system is stronger than any automation. Winning strategy: real person, real name, genuinely useful answer. Even 5 high-quality comments can drive significant AEO impact.

### GP8: The Experimental Design Imperative
Control groups vs. test groups. Track over time. Reproduce at least once. Published "best practices" are unvalidated hypotheses until personally reproduced.

### GP9: Surface Divergence Awareness
ChatGPT citations overlap only 35% with Google. Perplexity overlaps 70%. Track share of voice across 3+ LLM surfaces, not just one.

### GP10: Information Gain Heuristic
The fundamental quality signal: did you say something nobody else said? If content is too similar to existing results, it's flagged as derivative. Content without information gain is invisible to both humans and algorithms.

### GP11: Help Center as AEO Weapon
Help centers are ideal AEO assets — LLM users ask detailed product questions mapping perfectly to help center content. Most companies have zero coverage of the tail.

### GP12: Hidden Attribution Problem
LLM traffic is massively underreported. Users copy brand names and type into Google or navigate directly. Measure AEO impact through post-conversion surveys AND branded search correlation, not just referral tracking.
