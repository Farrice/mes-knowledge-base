# Made to Stick (Dan & Chip Heath) — Genius Context

> Load before executing any workflow. Full extraction intelligence.

## Core Operating System

Sticky ideas share six principles (SUCCESs): Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories. The deepest insight is that the Curse of Knowledge is the single greatest barrier to effective communication — once you know something, you can't unknow it, making it nearly impossible to imagine not knowing it. Every principle in this system fights the Curse.

---

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Commander's Intent (Finding the Core)
Strip away everything until you find the ONE thing. If you can only communicate one thing, what would it be? That's your Commander's Intent. Decision-makers should be able to make aligned choices without additional clarification.

### GP2: Schema Violation for Attention
Identify the audience's existing mental model (schema), create a violation that breaks expectations, then resolve the violation into your message. The surprise grabs attention; the resolution delivers meaning.

### GP3: Knowledge Gap Creation
Open a gap between what they know and what they want to know. The gap creates curiosity that demands closure. Maintain gaps across content — audience continues consuming until gap is closed.

### GP4: Curse of Knowledge Reversal
Identify your expert assumptions (jargon, context, steps you skip). Translate each assumption into language for someone with zero background. Complete novices should understand AND take correct action.

### GP5: Concrete Over Abstract
Replace every abstract word with sensory-specific language. "Improve performance" → "Cut processing time from 2 hours to 12 minutes." Message should create mental image, not mental fog.

### GP6: Testable Credentials
Instead of claiming authority, give the audience a way to verify for themselves. "Try this free before you buy" beats "Trust our expertise." The audience convinces themselves rather than being told.

### GP7: Human-Scale Statistics
Make numbers relatable to human experience. "37 grams of fat" → "A full day's fat in every serving." Anchor to known quantities so the audience FEELS the number, not just reads it.

### GP8: The Individual Zoom
Move from statistics about masses to story about ONE person. "Millions affected" → "Meet Sarah, who..." The individual activates emotions; the mass numbs.

### GP9: Identity Over Self-Interest
Frame appeal as "this is who you ARE" rather than "this is what you GET." People act consistently with identity even when it costs them. Behavior change persists without constant reinforcement.

### GP10: Three Universal Story Plots
Match your message to the right plot: **Challenge** (protagonist overcomes obstacles — inspires perseverance), **Connection** (relationships across gaps — inspires collaboration), **Creativity** (mental breakthrough — inspires innovation).

## Hidden Knowledge

| HK7 | Sticky ideas feel discovered, not told. The most memorable messages feel like insights the audience generated themselves. Testable credentials work because the audience does the convincing. | Message delivery strategy |

## Signature Moves

1. **The "Novice Interrogation"** — Seeks out someone completely unfamiliar with the topic and asks them to explain the message back in their own words. Their confusion = your blindness.
2. **The "Sensory Snapshot"** — For every abstract concept, immediately replaces with vivid sensory-rich image or specific action. "Improve performance" → "Cut processing time from 2 hours to 12 minutes."
3. **The "Emotional Pre-Load"** — Before presenting data or logical arguments, introduces a relatable individual story that evokes emotional response, counteracting analysis mode.
4. **The "Curiosity Catalyst"** — Structures information flow to create a knowledge gap or unexpected element early, posing a question or counter-intuitive fact before providing resolution.
5. **The "Proverbial Polish"** — After crafting a message, distills core essence into a short, memorable, easily repeatable phrase using analogy or unexpected comparison.

## Expert-Specific Quality Rubric

| Criterion | 4 (Acceptable) | 7 (Good) | 10 (Savant) |
|-----------|----------------|----------|-------------|
| Core Message Singularity | Main point identifiable but requires effort | Clear and concise | Unmistakable, instantly graspable, actionable |
| Schema Violation & Resolution | Surprising element but feels random | Surprise grabs attention and hints at message | Surprise perfectly integrated, compelling gap resolves directly into core message |
| Concreteness & Sensory Detail | Some concrete elements but relies on jargon | Mostly concrete, few key ideas remain abstract | Every key idea in vivid sensory language creating clear mental images |
| Self-Convincing Credibility | Relies on assertion or generic data | Some data human-scaled or verification implied | Direct compelling way to experience/verify truth, or powerfully human-scaled statistics |
| Emotional & Identity Resonance | Appeals mostly to logic or self-interest | Touches emotions/values but not deeply | Deeply resonates with identity, values, core emotions — drives intrinsic motivation |
| Narrative Integration | Presents facts, no story arc | Attempts story but feels tacked on | Message intrinsically woven into clear inspiring narrative (Challenge/Connection/Creativity) driving action |
| Curse of Knowledge Avoidance | Some unavoidable jargon, assumes prior knowledge | Mostly clear, few points might confuse complete novice | Completely clear to someone with zero prior context — all expert assumptions translated |
