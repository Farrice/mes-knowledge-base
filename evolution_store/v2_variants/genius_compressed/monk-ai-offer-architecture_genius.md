## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Offer Pyramid Logic
Design 3+ tiers where each lower tier naturally leads to the next. Entry offers prove value AND surface bigger opportunities. The goal isn't to close everyone at the top — it's to never let a qualified buyer leave without some paid commitment.

### GP2: First Yes Psychology
The first paid transaction transforms the relationship entirely. A $500 client who's experienced your work is 10x more likely to buy $15K than a cold prospect. Optimize entry offers for CONVERSION, not revenue — remove every friction point, make saying yes obvious.

### GP3: The Scope Creation Mechanism
A well-designed audit doesn't just find problems — it creates DEMAND for implementation. Structure audit deliverables to surface 3-5 specific implementation opportunities with clear ROI projections that make the client unable to NOT take action.

### GP4: Trust Gradient Pricing
Price according to TRUST LEVEL, not value. At first meeting, trust is low — price entry offers at what that trust level can bear. After successful delivery, trust increases. Never ask for more trust than you've earned; each tier should feel like the natural next step.

### GP5: The Downsell as Value Creation
The downsell isn't a backup plan — it's PART of the sales architecture. Design the downsell BEFORE you need it. Having a graceful smaller option makes the whole conversation feel consultative rather than pushy. It should be a genuine value-creator, not a discount.

### GP6: Compound Pipeline Architecture
Most consultants "hunt" for new clients monthly. This system creates COMPOUND momentum — each closed deal feeds referrals, case studies, and upsells that reduce next month's hunting. Track: referral rate per client, upsell rate, reactivation rate.

### GP7: Stakeholder Pre-Selling
The formal proposal should be a CONFIRMATION ceremony, not a persuasion event. Map stakeholders before proposing. Have individual conversations. Arm your champion with materials to sell internally. If pre-selling is done correctly, the pitch meeting is just paperwork.

## Signature Moves

1. **The "Micro-Commitment Ladder"** — Identifies the lowest-friction, highest-value paid engagement (audit or workshop) to transform prospect status from "lead" to "client." Deploy when engaging new leads or when larger offers meet resistance.
2. **The "Demand-Sculpting Audit Blueprint"** — Structures every audit deliverable to quantify cost of inaction and frame the next-tier service as the inevitable, highest-ROI solution. Deploy when crafting scope for any entry-level assessment.
3. **The "Internal Champion Empowerment Pack"** — Privately equips the internal advocate with data-backed talking points, anticipated objection responses, and ROI scenarios before the formal proposal. Deploy when identifying a key internal champion.
4. **The "Value-First Downsell Pre-Design"** — Proactively develops a genuinely valuable, standalone lower-tier offer that solves a specific urgent pain point and builds trust. Deploy when building the full offer pyramid or facing budget limitations.
