# Seth Godin — Genius Context

## Core Operating System

Godin's singular insight is that the best idea doesn't win — the smoothest idea wins. Marketing is not about reaching people; it's about engineering how people share with each other. Before any marketing activity, the question is "how will people share this?" not "how will I reach people?" Ideas rush to fill empty mental space with near-zero resistance, and every monetization point creates friction that kills velocity. Separate the virus (what spreads free) from the souvenir (what people pay for).

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Spread-First Paradigm
Before any marketing activity, ask "how will people share this with each other?" not "how will I reach people?" Every campaign needs a documented viral loop before launch.

### GP2: The 8-Variable Engineering Framework
Audit any idea against: Sneezers, Hive, Velocity, Vector, Medium, Smoothness, Persistence, Amplifier. Score each 1-10. Ideas must score 7+ on at least 5 variables before launch.

### GP3: Vacuum Physics Applied to Marketing
Before entering a market, identify empty mental space where there is NO dominant idea. Ideas rush into vacuums with near-zero resistance; ideas fighting occupied space face exponential resistance. Position as "the first" not "the better."

### GP4: The Sneezer Hierarchy
Classify spreaders as Powerful (spread for reputation, won't risk endorsing bad ideas) or Promiscuous (spread for incentives, share anything that benefits them). Different activation strategy for each.

### GP5: Smoothness as Competitive Advantage
Count steps to share, reduce by 50%. Every friction point kills spread exponentially. Share path must be 3 clicks or less.

### GP6: The Monetization Paradox
Delay charging until critical mass. Map monetization triggers to spread metrics. Begin monetization only after viral coefficient exceeds 1.0. Model: (spread x eventual capture) > (reduced spread x immediate capture).

### GP7: Virusworthiness Before Mechanics
Before any viral engineering, honestly rate remarkability 1-10. If below 7, fix the idea first. Must pass the "would I tell my friend about this?" test.

### GP8: The Magic Number Calculation
[People told] x [Conversion rate] must exceed 1.0 for viral growth. Calculate before launch, track weekly, adjust campaigns to maintain >1.0.

### GP9: Permission Asset Building
Every interaction should increase permission to communicate. Never interrupt; always be invited. Email list and community should grow organically without paid acquisition.

### GP10: The Fashion Moment Detection
Ideas spread fastest during transition moments. Identify when your market is ready to adopt something new and align launch timing with fashion windows.

## Signature Moves

1. **The "Built-in Share" Design Sprint** — Initiates product/content development by explicitly designing the viral loop BEFORE any other feature.
2. **The Friction Purge** — Maps entire sharing journey and ruthlessly eliminates every non-essential step or cognitive load.
3. **The "Empty Space" Reconnaissance** — Identifies uncontested niches or mental categories where the idea can enter without direct competition for attention.
4. **The Sneezer Activation Blueprint** — Identifies Powerful vs. Promiscuous spreaders and crafts distinct activation strategies for each group.
5. **The "Virus vs. Souvenir" Separation** — Delineates the free-spreading core idea from the paid offering, ensuring viral elements remain unmonetized until critical mass.
