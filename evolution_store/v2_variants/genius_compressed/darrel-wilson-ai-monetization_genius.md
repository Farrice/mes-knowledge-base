# Darrel Wilson — Genius Context (AI Monetization)

## Core Operating System

Monetize AI by building tools people USE (not content they read), pre-building finished solutions before prospecting, and converting one-time sales into recurring revenue through low-friction entry pricing. Every manual task gets the "Can AI handle this?" interrogation. Proof-first, always.

---

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Warm Lead Philosophy
Verify prospect has demonstrated buying behavior (job posts, RFPs, budget announcements) before any outreach. 80%+ of outreach should go to verified warm prospects with stated budgets.

### GP2: Flip the Process Method
Pre-build 3-5 niche-specific solutions before prospecting. Use as visual pitches rather than proposals. 3-5x closing rate when showing finished products vs. pitching concepts.

### GP3: Tool Over Opinion Positioning
Build tools people USE, not content they READ. Identify the action users want to take and build the tool that facilitates it with affiliate links embedded. Tool must provide genuine utility independent of affiliate component.

### GP4: Recurring Revenue Lock-In
Price entry low ($200) but structure as monthly subscriptions. Transform one-time sales into predictable recurring revenue. Target 70%+ of clients on recurring plans within 90 days.

### GP5: Ugly Website Arbitrage
Scan Google Maps using evaluation criteria (mobile responsiveness, load speed, design age) to identify high-probability prospects. Target 50+ qualified prospects identified per hour of scanning.

### GP6: Proof-First Demonstration
Lead every interaction with live demonstration of finished product in action. "Let me show you" precedes "here's how." Prospects see working proof within first 90 seconds.

### GP7: Niche Template Arsenal
Pre-build templates for 5-10 high-volume niches. Customize only the branding layer for individual clients. Client delivery time under 48 hours using template + customization.

### GP8: AI Efficiency Multiplier
For every manual task, ask "Can AI handle this?" Focus human attention on strategy and client relationships. Target 80%+ of repetitive tasks automated within any workflow.

### GP9: RFP Goldmine Recognition
Build automated scraping for RFP boards, government contract sites, institutional procurement pages. Surface 10+ qualified RFP opportunities weekly through automation.

### GP10: Value Ladder Pricing
Structure 3-4 price points: free/low entry, mid-tier, high-ticket. Each tier naturally leads to the next. Revenue distributed across all tiers with 60%+ from mid-tier and above.

### GP11: Practical Proof Content Style
Every piece of content includes concrete demonstration of working system, real numbers, or live example. Tangible proof elements visible within first 60 seconds.

### GP12: Cross-Niche Workflow Transfer
Design systems with variable inputs so core logic deploys across multiple industries by changing keywords/parameters. Single workflow deployable to 5+ niches with minimal modification.

### GP13: Traffic Hierarchy Understanding
Prioritize by engagement quality: video content > social media > SEO > paid ads. 50%+ of traffic from high-intent sources.

### GP14: System Sale Over Time Sale
Package expertise as repeatable systems with ongoing value. Price based on business value, not time invested. 80%+ of revenue from systems/products vs. hourly billing.
