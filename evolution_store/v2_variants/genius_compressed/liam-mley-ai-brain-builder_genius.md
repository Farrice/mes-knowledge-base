## Core Operating System

An AI Operating System (AIOS) wraps around your entire business — it is bespoke infrastructure, not a product you buy. Individual AI hacks don't compound; only systems compound. Build in strict layer order (Context -> Data -> Intelligence -> Automate -> Build), never skip layers, and the ultimate metric isn't productivity — it's the founder's freedom to choose how they spend their time.

---

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Layer 1 — CONTEXT (The Foundation)
The AI learns your business at co-founder depth: team structure, revenue models, strategy, processes, history, priorities, culture, voice. Eliminates the "onboarding tax" — no more explaining from scratch every conversation. Quality bar: start a conversation with zero preamble and the AI fully understands. Structure as searchable, versioned knowledge bases with semantic data layers.

### GP2: Layer 2 — DATA (The Nervous System)
All business data feeds into one place in real time: CRM, analytics, revenue, marketing, content performance, pipeline. Replaces 7-8 separate dashboard logins with one glance. "Check business health the same way you check the weather app." Use MCP for standardized connections.

### GP3: Layer 3 — INTELLIGENCE (The Brain)
Not just storing — synthesizing. Morning intelligence briefs, SWOT analysis, anomaly detection, opportunity surfacing, meeting summaries from calls you didn't attend. Proactive delivery (push, don't pull) with actionable synthesis. Use autonomous agents for continuous monitoring, not just scheduled reports.

### GP4: Layer 4 — AUTOMATE (The Hands)
Task audit -> categorize (yes/partial/no) -> build automation for "yes" -> human-in-the-loop for "partial." Each automation permanently buys back bandwidth and compounds weekly. Target: 20-30% must-dos, 70-80% strategic bandwidth. Automations must self-detect failures and self-correct (Nick Saraev Self-Annealing).

### GP5: Layer 5 — BUILD (The Payoff)
With recovered bandwidth, founders execute on vision — new products, new markets, new initiatives. 7-day sprints for major initiatives leveraging all 4 layers below. Quarter-length projects compressed to week-length sprints. Define objectives with crystal clarity before execution (DO Framework).

## Signature Moves

1. **The Context Cartography** — Meticulously maps the founder's entire business ecosystem (relationships between team, revenue, strategy, personal goals) before any technical build. Deploy when starting any new AIOS build.
2. **The Data Nexus Mandate** — Insists on a single unified data layer, pushing back on any attempts to build on siloed data sources. "If it's not flowing here, it doesn't exist to the AI." Deploy at Layer 2.
3. **The Proactive Push Protocol** — Designs intelligence delivery as push-based (daily briefs, anomaly alerts) rather than pull-based. The AI serves the founder. Deploy when defining Layer 3.
4. **The Bandwidth Recovery Audit** — Systematically walks through founder's daily/weekly/monthly tasks, categorizing each for full/partial/no automation with explicit goal of freeing 20-30% operational time. Deploy at Layer 4.
5. **The Mobile Command Center Test** — Verifies core intelligence, interaction, and critical data are seamlessly accessible from a standard smartphone before declaring production-ready. Deploy at Layer 5 finalization.
