## Core Operating System

Oren's operational genius is systems that grow with you — minimal entry point, maximum ceiling. Every system follows the same architecture: capture in transit, separate ideation from scheduling, make invisible work visible, and document by recording (not writing). The compounding reference effect transforms how you think about creative work: after 200+ organized references, your brain shifts from "find inspiration" to "retrieve the answer." Install one system at a time; each builds organizational muscle memory for the next.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Reference Repository Architecture
Build a structured database (Name, Type, Link, Status: Reference/Used/Winner, Notes) for all content consumption. Add from browser/X/Instagram/TikTok saves every Sunday night. Pull for all creative work. After 200+ references, your brain shifts from "find inspiration" to "retrieve the answer" — the invisible marker separating junior from senior creative directors.

### GP2: Ideas-to-Calendar Pipeline
Separate ideation from scheduling. Dump ideas in bulk (no filter, no judgment), then promote best to calendar slots. Never ideate and schedule simultaneously. Track: name, publish date, format, status (idea/edit/record/done), editor. Eliminates "blank calendar" paralysis.

### GP3: Capture-Organize-Deploy Loop (Phone Folders)
Four folders on phone: Me, People, Places, Things. Sort footage during dead time (Uber rides, waiting rooms). Heart/favorite anything that could be a hook shot. 80/20 of content friction is FINDING footage, not shooting it. Goal: find any piece in under 30 seconds.

### GP4: Weekly Update Protocol
Every Friday: TLDR (2-3 sentence highlights) > Accomplished (checklist) > Next Week (plan) > Blockers (specific names and missing deliverables). Clients fire freelancers not for bad work but for feeling out of the loop. The blocker section creates documented accountability — it's insurance, not just communication.

### GP5: Team Tracker Database
Shared database: Task Name, Assignee, Status, Due Date, Final Link. Optional: intake form. The real win isn't task management — it's converting invisible creative labor into visible, quantifiable output. "21 assets done this week, 11 for your team" silences cross-department complaints. It's a political weapon disguised as a management tool.

### GP6: Process Doc Framework (Loom-First)
Never start docs by writing. Record a Loom doing the task while narrating > transcribe > feed to ChatGPT for step-by-step > add quality checklist > add FAQ section that grows with each new hire. Activation energy drops to near zero. Video is primary training asset; text is searchable backup.

### GP7: Signature Series Strategy
Identify your single most differentiated, most resonant content format and serialize it. Audit past content for: (a) only you do this way, (b) most engagement, (c) consistently producible. Name it, design repeatable template, commit to cadence. Recognition beats novelty in oversaturated feeds.

### GP8: Content Pillar Measurement System
Track 3-5 pillars monthly: views, saves, shares, reach, hook rate. Decision matrix: Improving = invest more. Steady = don't change. Declining = try one improvement. Dead = replace. "Change one thing per month" prevents overcorrection. Data creates defensible narrative for strategy decisions.
