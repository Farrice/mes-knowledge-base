## Core Operating System

The lead is not a miniature sales letter — it's a CURIOSITY ARCHITECTURE. The first 500-2,000 words of any sales asset operate on movie trailer logic: create desire to watch, not desire to buy. The lead's job is to get them to the mechanism reveal; the mechanism's job is to sell. Every element exists to create questions only the body can answer. The moment you resolve curiosity in the lead, you've killed the body's conversion power. Stack open loops so high the reader can't NOT continue.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The 7 Micro Lead Types
Seven distinct openers, each creating a specific psychological pull: (1) **Contrarian Claim** — cognitive dissonance; (2) **Insider Confession** — forbidden knowledge; (3) **Paradoxical Question** — intellectual itch; (4) **Look Closer** — "how did I miss that?"; (5) **Surprise Connection** — pattern recognition desire; (6) **Invisible Difference** — anxiety to distinguish; (7) **Quiz Opening** — self-knowledge desire.

### GP2: Lead Body Phases
Four sequential phases: (1) **Hook Phase** (micro lead) — stop them, create momentum; (2) **Build Belief Phase** — story + problem escalation + failed solutions + mechanism tease + credibility bridge; (3) **Handle Objection Phase** — pre-emptive skepticism + proof stack + contrarian positioning; (4) **Close Phase** — future pacing + fascinations + constraints + bridge to body.

### GP3: Curiosity Architecture Over Selling
The lead is a pure curiosity engine. Never resolve curiosity in the lead, never sell in the lead. If you attempt to close or pitch before the mechanism reveal, you kill the body's conversion power. Every sentence must create more questions than it answers.

### GP4: Open Loop Stacking
Each section opens a new question while partially answering the previous one. By the end of the lead, the viewer should have 3-5 unresolved questions that can ONLY be answered by continuing. Cascading loops where each feels more intriguing than the last create irresistible psychological pull.

## Signature Moves

1. **The "You're Not Broken" Problem Reframe** — Shifts blame for past failures from personal shortcomings to a flawed system, common misconception, or hidden truth. Validates struggles and positions offer as the true solution to an external problem.
2. **The Calculated Curiosity Spike** — Introduces a complex domain-specific term (jargon flurry) without explanation, implying deep expertise and creating an intellectual itch to understand the mechanism.
3. **Pre-emptive Skepticism Disarmament** — Addresses a potential flaw/limitation explicitly *before* the prospect thinks of it, then reframes as minor compared to the overwhelming benefit.
4. **The "Safety Net" Proof Layering (Tease)** — Hints at psychological proof elements (candid communication, explanatory reasoning) to create an emotional sensation of trust *before* full empirical data.
5. **The "Mechanism Cliffhanger"** — Concludes the lead by promising to reveal the unique mechanism, making the transition to the VSL body feel like an automatic, necessary next step.
