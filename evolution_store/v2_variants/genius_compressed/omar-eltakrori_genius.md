## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Becoming Before Getting
Identity transformation precedes outcome. Omar lost 35 lbs before his biggest challenge ($722K vs ~$400K average) — same offer, same ads, same script, different person. When a client is stuck on revenue, start with "Who are you becoming?" not "What tactic should you try?"

### GP2: The $100K Devaluation Principle
Omar lost $100K in a Ponzi scheme and nothing changed — mortgage paid, food on table. The unlock: "If $100K isn't a lot of money, maybe $1M isn't a lot of money." Money anxiety is a scale problem. If you can't think in $100K units, you'll never operate at $1M. Deploy when someone fears investing or charging premium.

### GP3: Price = Attention (Psychology of Investment)
"When you pay, you pay attention. When you pay more, you pay more attention." Founders at $2K/yr weren't implementing as aggressively as $25K members. Premium pricing creates commitment > results > testimonials > more premium clients. It's a flywheel. Corollary: "If you're cheap, don't be surprised when people want discounts from you."

### GP4: Sales as Energy Transfer
"Before you get better at sales, how you think about yourself starts with sales" (Brian Tracy). The physical health > confidence > sales energy pipeline is real and measurable in Omar's revenue data. Before any sales event: "Is my energy right?" comes before "Is my script ready?"

### GP5: Do It for the Data
"If you're afraid, you're overthinking perfectionism and underthinking benefits." Reframe: "I'm gonna do this for the data" removes emotional weight and enables action. Apple gave 17 iterations and still hasn't gotten it "right" — but they ship. Apply to first launches, first webinars, first offers.

### GP6: Faith & Stewardship Economics
Wealth philosophy rooted in Christian theology: God gives the power to get wealth; love of money (not money itself) is the problem; Parable of Talents = multiply or be called wicked/lazy; self-care precedes service; tithing first 10%. Resolves "Is it okay to pursue wealth?" for faith-adjacent clients. Secular translation: stewardship beats hoarding.

### GP7: I Am Boring (Discipline Over Novelty)
Omar runs the same challenge format, same offer structure, same content cadence. The play works. His offer went from $2K to $25K without changing the core structure — different version of himself delivering it. "If you're willing to be underpaid long enough, you will be overpaid soon enough" (Tom Brady). Redirect shiny-object clients: "Have you maxed out the play you're already running?"

### GP8: The Obsession Signal
"The person who appears most obsessed with a topic gets the business." Creating frameworks, coining terms, drawing visual models signal obsession — not credentials, not years. It communicates "I've thought of this more than you have." For personal branding, obsession is the differentiator.

### GP9: Manufactured Scarcity & Urgency
"Two things that increase sales: urgency and scarcity." Manufactured scarcity (25 spots per challenge), manufactured urgency ("if you get off this call, the price will be different"), founders pricing (early adopter identity + locked-in pricing). This isn't manipulation — Rolex, Apple, Supreme all do it. It's business architecture.
