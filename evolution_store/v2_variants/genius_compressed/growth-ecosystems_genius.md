## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Trust Recession Thesis
The info market's defining structural condition: skeptical buyers, rising ad costs, increased sophistication. Funnels treating leads as walking ATMs are broken for sub-$1M businesses. Being genuinely ethical is hard competitive advantage, not soft virtue.

### GP2: The Three-Yes Framework
No sale until prospect says YES to three things simultaneously: (1) YES to Outcome ("this person gets me from A to B"), (2) YES to Method ("their approach is credible"), (3) YES to You ("I trust this human"). Never close until all three are green. Engineer "Hm" (curiosity, not conviction) during first impressions.

### GP3: The 15% Trap
Only 15% of audience is purchase-ready at any moment (Schwartz). Traditional funnels optimize for 15% and ignore 85%. The inversion: build systems nurturing the 85% until they organically cross. Perpetual lead gen that compounds while competitors burn through buyers.

### GP4: The FOREST Content Thesis
Every long-form piece follows invisible sales architecture: F(oundation) — specific credibility/proof. O(utcome) — outcome framework. R(esonance) — shared frustration with broken status quo. E(xpertise) — demonstrate through frameworks not flexing. S(ocial Proof) — client examples mirroring audience. T(rust) — close with transparency and invitation, not pitch.

### GP5: Hybrid Offer Architecture (Music Metaphor)
Same transformation at three tiers: DIY (Sheet Music) $1.5K-$4K — frameworks, courses, templates. DWY (I teach you to play) $12.5K/4mo — mentorship, consulting, flagship. DFY (My band plays for you) $15K + 30% profit share — full implementation. Lower feeds higher; higher generates SOPs improving lower.

### GP6: Instagram-YouTube Flywheel
Instagram = first impression + nurture (NO->HM on 3 yeses). YouTube = conviction engine (HM->YES on method). "Binge-bank" of architecturally placed long-form content — 8 videos beat 100 random uploads. Architecture > frequency.

### GP7: Triage Setter Model
Not DM setter — TRIAGE setter. Three jobs: Qualify (fit, values, situation), Nurture (route to ecosystem assets based on 3-yes position), Triage (only purchase-ready leads reach calls). KPI is call quality not volume: 95% close on 27 calls vs. 20-30% on 80.

### GP8: The Conviction Video Pattern
One architecturally perfect long-form video as silent closer. Vincent's single video converted $178K+ — 26/27 closes came through watching before call. Triage setter routes leads to this as final "method YES" catalyst.

### GP9: Lifetime Value Flywheel
Test thesis -> SOPs -> DFY implementation -> feedback -> updated SOPs -> DWY assets -> feedback -> DIY content -> new leads -> repeat. 75% retention = half of revenue from re-closes. Backend > frontend.

### GP10: Reverse-Engineered Goal Architecture
Define Initial Conditions (audience, cases, revenue), Magic Number (target revenue with offer-mix math), Business Rules (non-negotiables: hours, margins, client type). Then — and ONLY then — design the ecosystem.

### GP11: The Cardinal Rules
Every decision passes through: (1) Authenticity over Vanity, (2) Simplicity over Complexity, (3) High Profit Margins 80%+, (4) Dream Clients Only — the filter IS the funnel, (5) Leverage at All Times.

### GP12: Customer Journey Awareness Stages
Unaware (~85%) -> Content Aware -> Brand Resonant -> Offer Aware -> Purchase Ready (~15%). Content moves people between stages. Never force stage-skipping.
