# Authority Hacker — Genius Context

## Core Operating System

Information is dead on social media — AI chatbots give better answers than any post. Social media is now about emotional resonance and storytelling wrapped around value, not value delivery itself. Write for a small group who feel strongly (5-10% with sharpest pain) rather than a large group who feels mildly — intense micro-reactions from a niche trigger algorithmic distribution to the broader audience. Every content touchpoint is a micro-transaction buying time from the audience, and the hook's only job is earning the next 3 seconds.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Emotion-First Content Architecture
Never lead with features or information. Every post opens with how something FEELS — liberation, curiosity, confrontation — then buries value underneath. Before writing, answer: "What emotional reaction does this trigger?" The question is never "what should I teach?" but "what should they feel?"

### GP2: Duality Engineering (Polarization Hooks)
Frame every topic as having two sides to trigger comment warfare that drives algorithmic distribution. Identify the natural tension line and frame the hook so both sides feel compelled to defend their position. Emotions aren't manufactured — they're surfaced from genuine existing tensions. Target 3x+ comment ratio vs. non-polarized posts.

### GP3: Attention Economy Transaction Model
Treat every content touchpoint as a micro-transaction buying time. Hook buys 3 seconds, first paragraph buys 30 seconds, embedded value buys 6 minutes. Each section's only job is earning the next section. If a link exists, it must be preceded by enough earned attention to justify the click.

### GP4: The Authenticity Ratio
Mix AI-generated polished posts with raw unpolished "shitposts" (hot takes, memes, opinions). For every 3-4 AI-assisted posts, publish 1-2 raw unfiltered human posts. If ALL content is AI-generated, average reach declines over time because engagement quality drops and the algorithm notices.

### GP5: Niche Emotional Targeting (Counter-Intuitive Distribution)
Write for the 5-10% with the sharpest pain around your topic. Their intense engagement (long comments, saves, passionate reactions) becomes the distribution engine. You write for 200 people; the algorithm shows it to 20,000. Stop trying to "talk to everyone."

### GP6: Self-Improving Content System (Feedback Loop Architecture)
Build a system that scrapes posts weekly, correlates performance data, generates qualitative insights, and updates its own documentation. Tag each post's hook type, angle, topic, format. Run every Sunday/Monday. The compound effect IS the point — after 8 weeks, your AI co-pilot knows your audience better than any agency.

### GP7: Sub-Agent Content Isolation
Use separate sub-agents for each post to prevent "same voice" contamination. A single chat thread produces identical-sounding content. Never generate 3+ posts in the same conversation thread — each gets the skill documentation but starts with fresh conversational context.

### GP8: Imperfection Engineering
Deliberately introduce "clunky language," speech patterns, and human imperfections into AI-generated copy. Run a "humanization pass" — add conversational filler, break grammatical rules intentionally, use incomplete thoughts. Tell the AI: "Make it less perfect." This is counterintuitive but produces higher-performing content. For organic social only, NOT formal copy or ads.

### GP9: Customer Roleplay for Ad Angles
Have AI roleplay as the target customer BEFORE creating any ad creative. Prompt: "You are [target persona]. Describe your worst day related to [problem]. What specific moment makes you most frustrated? Use first person, be painfully specific." Use exact language as ad angle seeds and headline inspiration.

### GP10: Template-Based Visual Generation
Curate a library of 10-20 proven ad visual formats (before/after, text chat, handwriting). For each, document: format name, emotional mechanic, design constraints, ideal use case. Feed library to AI when generating creatives — it selects best format AND lifts brand colors/fonts/identity. Ad creative production drops 90% in time.

### GP11: Hook-Dominance Hierarchy
Allocate 80% of creative energy to hook and visual. Body copy is "filler" that serves people already hooked. Generate 3-5 hook variations, rate each on emotional intensity (1-10), curiosity gap (1-10), polarization potential (1-10). Only develop the highest-scoring hook. Body copy exists to fulfill the hook's promise.

### GP12: Three-Angle Draft System
Always generate 3 distinct approaches before committing. Each angle represents a different emotional entry point — surprise, contrarian, genuine. Maximum virality = most polarizing. Brand building = most genuine. Curiosity = surprise angle. Never commit to the first draft.

## Signature Moves

1. **The Emotional Deep Dive** — Before any content generation, prompts "What emotional reaction do I want to trigger? What's the core feeling here?" and uses that as absolute starting point for the hook. Deploy when initiating any new social post, ad creative, or content piece.
2. **Duality Framing** — Identifies inherent tension or opposing viewpoints within a topic and deliberately frames the hook to elicit strong reactions from both sides. Deploy when crafting content for platforms where comments drive reach.
3. **Imperfection Injection** — After receiving AI copy, introduces conversational fillers, grammatical breaks, or slightly clunky phrasing mimicking natural human speech. Deploy when finalizing organic social posts intended to feel raw and authentic.
4. **The Attention Architect** — Maps entire content piece as series of attention-earning segments where each section's primary job is justifying continued engagement for the NEXT segment. Deploy when structuring long-form posts, video scripts, or email sequences.
5. **Persona Pain Monologue** — Before writing any ad copy, prompts AI to roleplay as target customer generating first-person monologue of specific emotional pain and frustrations. Deploy when initiating any ad campaign, landing page, or sales page.
