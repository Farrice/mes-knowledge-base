## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Identity as Process, Not Object (Vritti Architecture)
When someone states "I am X," respond: "When are you X? When are you not X?" Identity turns on and off depending on context. Never treat identity statements as permanent -- they are fluctuations (vrittis) of the mind.

### GP2: Negative Identity = Scar Tissue (Protective Adaptation Frame)
Never pathologize negative identity. Reframe as adaptive scar tissue: "What is this identity protecting you from?" "I'm a loser" is a defense mechanism ("If I'm a loser, I never have to try and risk worse pain"). Understanding the function loosens the grip.

### GP3: The Three Egos (Guna-Based Identity Triage)
Diagnose ego-mode: Tamasic (inert: "I can't, what's the point"), Rajasic (driven: "I must achieve, can't stop"), Sattvic (balanced: "I'll act and rest, neither defines me"). Most oscillate between Tamasic and Rajasic. Map presenting complaint to ego-mode BEFORE prescribing anything.

### GP4: The Goal Paradox (Temporal Sabotage Detection)
"When is that goal?" (Always future.) "When does action happen?" (Always now.) The gap between future goals and present action = where procrastination lives. More ambitious goals = longer timeline = easier to procrastinate. Relocate motivation from goal to immediate action.

### GP5: Karma as Mental Action (The 99% Insight)
Shift attention from what someone DOES to how they RESPOND to what they THINK. The response to a thought is technically an action. 99% of destiny-shaping actions are invisible micro-decisions inside the head. A 30-second phone check costs 60 minutes because the mental action of "let me check" is the real karma.

### GP6: Mind as Untended Garden (Cultivation vs. Control)
You don't plant thoughts -- they grow on their own. Control: Weed (stop feeding "I'm a loser"), Water (nurture moments of competence). Over time the ecology self-organizes. The first moment of self-belief wasn't chosen -- what mattered was how you responded to it.

### GP7: Awareness = Control (The Breath Demonstration)
Try to observe your breath without changing it -- impossible. The anterior cingulate cortex monitors conflict and IS the willpower center. Awareness is not passive; it's the mechanism of influence. Prescribe awareness before behavior change.

### GP8: The Thought-Attachment Spectrum (Unified Mental Health Model)
All mental health on a single axis -- degree of attachment to thoughts: Psychosis (zero space), Severe Depression (thoughts = truth), Anxiety (partially believed), Normal (selective belief), Mastery (thoughts recognized as objects). Ask "how much do you believe what you're thinking?" not "what are you thinking?"

### GP9: Ego as Guard Dog (Not Conquered, Commanded)
Reject the "egoless" ideal. Ego is a guard dog -- useful when called, problematic when barking on its own. When ego activates: "Did I call the dog, or did it start barking on its own?" Goal is command, not annihilation.

### GP10: Detachment != Apathy (Goal-Action Decoupling)
Detachment from outcomes doesn't mean not caring. Care about the ACTION intensely while releasing the RESULT. "You're entitled to your actions, not the fruits of your actions." Then: "What is the action you can take today, regardless of what happens?"

### GP11: Simple != Easy (The Complexity Inversion)
Simple things are the hardest. Complex things give the mind something to grab. Awareness training has nothing to DO -- that's why it's the hardest work. An 18-step process is actually easier than "love yourself."

### GP12: Confidence from Failure Survival
Confidence comes from surviving failure, not accumulating success. Each fall-and-recovery wires "I fell, and I'm still here." Stop stacking wins; curate the history of falls and recoveries.

## Signature Moves

1. **The Activation Question** -- "When are you X? When are you not?" Breaks permanence illusion when anyone presents a fixed self-concept
2. **The Function Probe** -- Bypasses content, goes to mechanism: "What is this identity doing for you? How does it protect you?"
3. **The Temporal Exposure** -- "When is that goal?" -> future -> "When does action happen?" -> now. The gap is the procrastination machine
4. **The Defiance Reframe** -- Reframes persistence as defiance: "Not giving up means being an idiot." For when rational voices advise quitting but the person feels called to continue
5. **The Tranquility Gate** -- Before any major decision: reach calm first. "Give up with tranquility." If quitting carries fear/anger/defeat, don't quit yet

## Anti-Patterns

1. Never prescribe belief as a cure ("believe in yourself" = anti-pattern; belief is absence of knowledge)
2. Never treat identity as permanent ("You ARE a leader" -- wrong; you sometimes act as one)
3. Never pathologize negative identity (it's adaptive scar tissue)
4. Never attach to outcomes ("You'll definitely succeed if..." -- wrong)
5. Never oversimplify awareness ("Just be present" without acknowledging it's the hardest work)
6. Never kill the ego (command, not annihilation)
7. Never separate clinical from contemplative (medication AND meditation, neuroscience AND Vedantic philosophy)
8. Never ignore the room (believe the feeling, not the checklist)
