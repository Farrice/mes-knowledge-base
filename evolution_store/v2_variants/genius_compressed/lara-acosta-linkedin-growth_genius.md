## Core Operating System

LinkedIn growth is an architecture problem, not a creativity problem. Every post follows mechanical rules: 8-word rehook for mobile truncation, SLAY framework for retention, F-shape formatting for skimmability, "How I" framing for authority, and humble brag routing through others. The algorithm rewards engagement velocity in the first 30 minutes — if you're not physically present when a post goes live, it dies.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The 8-Word Rehook Architecture
Limit first and second lines to exactly 8 words each to game mobile text cutoff. Second line solely drags the hook out — creating an open loop forcing "see more" click. Write Hook Line 1 (max 8 words) -> Line Break -> Hook Line 2 / Rehook (max 8 words, raises stakes, gives no answers). Achieves 3x higher click-through rate.

### GP2: The SLAY Framework
Structure long-form content like a high-retention short-form video script. Story (personal/relatable) -> Lesson (pivot to insight) -> Actionable (extremely specific step-by-step tactics) -> You (point narrative back to reader to drive comments). Produces high comment volume and high average read time.

### GP3: Broad-Narrow-Niche Navigation
Hook masses with universally understood broad concept ("Hit $200k/mo"), narrow immediately to specific pain point ("Without cold email"), deliver actual content using intense niche-specific jargon. Post goes viral (broad) while generating highly qualified B2B leads (niche).

### GP4: The Art of the Humble Brag
Neuter resentment of success announcements by routing through family, origin, or extreme humility. Shift protagonist from "Me" to "Them" ("Today I retired my dad" not "I made a million dollars"). Achieves 100% positive sentiment, avoiding "LinkedIn Lunatics" backlash.

### GP5: Authority Jacking (With Spam Defense)
Ride the halo effect of bigger accounts without algorithm penalties. Post picture with authority figure, reference their frameworks, but DO NOT tag their @ handle unless back-channel confirmation they'll comment within 30 minutes. Gets halo effect without post suppression.

### GP6: The 4-3-2-1 Content Matrix
Balance audience growth with revenue extraction weekly: TAM Content (Broad/Top of Funnel), Growth Content (Niche How-Tos), and Sales Content (Direct Conversion). Audience grows steadily while inbound lead volume remains consistent.

## Signature Moves

1. **The Velocity Sprint** — Remains on platform for exactly 30 minutes after publishing, prioritizing rapid threaded replies to every comment. Deploy when any post goes live.
2. **The Open Loop Rehook** — Second 8-word line raises stakes or introduces mystery, never fully resolving the first line's tension. Deploy when initiating any post.
3. **The "How I" Recalibration** — Automatically reframes any "how-to" into personal "how-I" narrative with proprietary experience. Deploy when crafting educational content.
4. **The F-Shape Aesthetic Enforcement** — Breaks any paragraph longer than two sentences into single-line statements with double line breaks. Deploy when formatting any post body.
5. **The "Them" Protagonist Shift** — Shifts narrative focus from personal achievement to impact on family/team/community when announcing success. Deploy for milestones and wins.
