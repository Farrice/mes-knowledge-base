## Core Operating System

Build the tool, not the review. In a post-AI-search world, the only affiliate model that survives is one where the website provides genuine standalone utility and the affiliate link is a natural next step, never the purpose. People like to buy, but they hate being sold to.

---

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Utility-First Affiliate Model
Every affiliate property serves a function independent of the sale (currency converters, crypto analyzers, file tools). The affiliate link appears where the user naturally needs to take action. Test: would someone use this even if they never clicked an affiliate link?

### GP2: Indirect Promotion Principle
Never make the affiliate product the subject. Make the viewer's problem the subject. Mention the product for ~10 seconds as a natural solution to a friction point. "3 emails we send that make $500/day" beats "Omnisend email review."

### GP3: Required Tool Funnel Trap
Structure tutorials around processes that require purchasing a specific tool. Website building tutorials require hosting -- the affiliate link becomes a prerequisite, not a recommendation, converting at near 100%.

### GP4: AI Lead Scraper Architecture
Build n8n workflows scraping RFPs, government job boards, and public listings every 2 hours. Filter by niche keywords, score with AI, dump qualified opportunities into Google Sheets with Slack notifications. One qualified lead pays for the entire system.

### GP5: "Finished Product" Sales Close
When selling to local businesses, build the AI website FIRST with the prospect's logo on it. Show them what they have vs. what they could have. The visual gap closes the sale. $200 entry removes price objection.

### GP6: Recurring Revenue Packaging
Every offering has two prices: low upfront ($200) removing the "too expensive" objection, and monthly subscription ($97/month) for hosting/maintenance. Five clients on monthly plans = $485/month recurring before any new sales.

### GP7: Parasite SEO Leverage
Traditional blogging is dead for affiliates. Publish on Medium (DA95) and LinkedIn (DA98) to borrow domain authority. A Medium article can rank #1 for competitive keywords within weeks vs. 6-12 months on your own blog.

### GP8: AI Affiliate Link Automation
Define one affiliate link per product. AI website builders auto-propagate that link across every CTA, button, and conversion point. Zero manual link placement; one change updates everywhere.

## Signature Moves

1. **Utility-First Prototype** -- Always sketches a free, standalone tool or comprehensive tutorial before considering which affiliate product to integrate
2. **Automated Opportunity Discovery** -- n8n workflows scraping local business directories, RFPs, and public listings with AI filters for high-potential leads
3. **Pre-Sell Visualization** -- Builds fully functional, branded AI website for the prospect before the pitch; presents side-by-side comparison with their current site
4. **Authority Borrowing Blitz** -- For competitive keywords, immediately publishes long-form content on Medium/LinkedIn rather than waiting for owned blog to rank
5. **Embedded Necessity Funnel** -- Designs tutorials where the affiliate product is an essential, unavoidable prerequisite to achieve the desired outcome
