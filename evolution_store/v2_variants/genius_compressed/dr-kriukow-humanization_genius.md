## Core Operating System

All humanization decisions flow from one insight: AI text is the MOST statistically predictable version of any given content. Structural changes (sentence order, paragraph flow, argument progression) defeat AI detection far more reliably than vocabulary changes. Don't memorize tricks -- apply the principle of statistical unpredictability. Priority: structure -> tense/voice -> vocabulary.

---

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Statistical Unpredictability Principle (SUP)
AI text is the most statistically predictable version. Any structural change reduces predictability. Test: "Did I change the structure, or just the words?" Structure change = pass. Words only = fail.

### GP2: Structure-Over-Words Hierarchy
Structural changes (sentence order, paragraph flow, argument progression) defeat detection far more reliably than vocabulary swaps. If showing AI draft and edit side-by-side, the SHAPE must look different, not just the words.

### GP3: Meaning Preservation Rewrite
Read 2-3 sentences as a meaning unit. Extract core intent. Re-express without looking at original sentence structure. "Close your eyes and say it your way." Meaning preserved but structurally unrecognizable.

### GP4: Deliberate Imperfection Injection
AI writes "perfectly" -- clean, efficient, active voice, balanced sentences. Humans don't. Inject: unexpected passive voice, unusual connector placement, sentence fragments, rhetorical asides, varied lengths. 2-3 structural surprises per paragraph.

### GP5: Order Reversal Technique
AI enumerations follow the most statistically common order. Reverse or reshuffle every list. Rephrase at least one item with different syntax ("social development" -> "development of social skills"). If any list matches AI's likely order, still predictable.

### GP6: Anti-Mold Principle
The deepest AI tell is the "mold" -- the paragraph's structural shape (sentence count, argument flow, tense distribution). Identify the mold, then deliberately violate 2+ features per paragraph. If you can describe the structural fingerprint as a template, it's still molded.

### GP7: Holistic Context Window
Never edit at the sentence level. AI detectors evaluate cross-sentence patterns. Editing sentences independently can leave inter-sentence relationships intact and predictable. Edit paragraphs as units, never sentences individually.

## Quality Tests

1. **Structural Divergence Test**: Compare AI original and humanized edit. Different sentence order? Different lengths? Argument flow rearranged? At least 2/3 must be yes.
2. **Prediction Test**: Read first sentence of humanized text. Try to predict the second. If you can = too predictable. If you can't quite = human-sounding.
3. **GPT-Zero Test**: Run through most aggressive detector. Passes there = passes everywhere.
4. **"Worse Is Better" Test**: At least one slight imperfection (passive construction, unexpected aside, slightly too-long sentence). "Too clean" = still AI-shaped.

## Signature Moves

1. **The "Structural X-Ray"** -- Mentally outlines the AI text's paragraph structure (sentence count, argument progression, tense consistency, list order) as if it were a template before any editing
2. **The "Core Intent Distillation"** -- Reads 2-3 sentences, physically turns away or closes eyes, articulates the single core message in own words without referencing original phrasing
3. **The "Deliberate Jolt"** -- After humanized draft feels "too smooth," consciously inserts a structural anomaly: sudden passive voice, unexpectedly long/short sentence, informal aside, unusual transition
4. **The "List Logic Flip"** -- Identifies any enumerated/sequential information and reverses order of at least two items or completely rephrases one item's syntax
5. **The "Paragraph as Canvas"** -- Never edits a single sentence in isolation; views entire paragraph as fluid canvas, making changes that ripple through multiple sentences
