## Core Operating System

Brock designs every piece of content around one question: "Why would someone send this to a friend?" — not "Why would someone like this?" His genius is engineering **shareability as the primary metric**, building content around human sharing psychology (identity expression, social currency) rather than platform-specific algorithm hacks. One shareworthy piece outperforms 30 mediocre pieces through exponential network effects.

---

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Share-Trigger Architecture
Before creating any content, identify the specific social currency it provides the sharer — making them look smart, helpful, funny, or in-the-know. Design for sharing, not liking. Target share-to-view ratio above 3%.

### GP2: The Relatability Reflex
Default to universal micro-experiences rather than aspirational content — the "I thought I was the only one" moments. Mine everyday frustrations, habits, and internal monologues that 80%+ of audience experiences but nobody talks about publicly.

### GP3: Pattern Interrupt Instinct
Within the first 0.5 seconds, disrupt the expected scroll pattern using visual, auditory, or conceptual disruption. Open with something that doesn't belong in the expected feed context — unexpected visuals, contradictory statements, mid-action entry points. Target 3-second retention above 70%.

### GP4: Save-Worthy Density
Structure educational content so dense with value that a single viewing isn't enough — creating the instinct to bookmark. Pack actionable frameworks, templates, or step-by-step processes into formats requiring re-reference. Target save rate above 5%.

### GP5: Controversy Calibration
Walk the exact line between "bold take" and "offensive." Challenge consensus beliefs with evidence-backed counterpoints. Target the belief, not the person. Give dissenters a graceful exit ("I used to think this too"). Goal: genuine debate, not hate.

### GP6: Aspiration Gap Engineering
Create just enough distance between current and aspirational state to motivate, not discourage. Show the "2 steps ahead" version, not "10 steps ahead." Audience should feel "I could actually do this." Target DM inquiries asking "how did you do this?"

### GP7: Algorithm Transcendence
Build content around timeless human psychology rather than current algorithm trends. Optimize for human sharing triggers (social currency, emotion, practical value, identity) rather than platform-specific hacks. Content survives algorithm updates and works cross-platform.

## Signature Moves

1. **The "Forward-to-a-Friend" Test** — Before concept finalization, identify a specific person who would *need* to see this and articulate the exact reason they'd hit share.
2. **The Micro-Observation Mine** — 15 minutes daily observing mundane universal human behaviors or unspoken internal thoughts, logged as potential relatability content anchors.
3. **The "Value Stack" Outline** — Structure educational content as nested, interlinked insights where each layer adds new dense value compounding on the last.
4. **The Disagreement Dial** — For controversial topics, identify the prevailing unchallenged assumption, craft evidence-based counter-narrative targeting the idea (not the person), providing a logical pathway for perspective shifts.
