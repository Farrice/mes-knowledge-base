## Core Operating System

Lead magnets must create the exact problem your paid offer solves — not just build goodwill. The person who diagnoses the problem is automatically assumed to have the cure. Quality standard: if you wouldn't charge $29-$99 for it, it's not good enough to give away free. Deploy lead magnets across the entire funnel as a compounding trust system, not just top-of-funnel email capture.

---

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Diagnostic Positioning Pattern
Frame lead magnets through the doctor-patient analogy — diagnosing the problem positions you as the solution provider without selling. Design lead magnets that reveal a specific gap, score, or diagnosis. Users complete it and immediately ask "how do I fix this?"

### GP2: The Obvious Next Step Bridge
Every lead magnet should create the exact problem your paid offer solves. Work backwards from your paid offer: "What problem, if I created or revealed it, would make my paid offer the only logical next step?" This is Rule 5 — the only profitability gate.

### GP3: The Multi-Touch Value Stack
Use lead magnets across the entire funnel: clarifiers early (reveal gap), samplers middle (prove you can help), starters for momentum, shortcuts for convenience. Design 3-5 that chain together, each increasing commitment. For high-consideration purchases (>$500) or long sales cycles.

### GP4: The "Charge-Worthy" Quality Standard
If your lead magnet isn't good enough to charge $29-$99 for, it's not good enough to give away. The internal logic: "If the free stuff is this good, the paid stuff must be insane." Unsolicited "I can't believe this is free" comments = success.

### GP5: The Generosity-to-Sale Conversion
Design with intention of genuinely helping. Measure quality by whether someone feels grateful, not manipulated. Genuine value converts at higher rates than aggressive pitching. Reverse the exploitative mindset.

## Expert Heuristics

- Count conversions to paid, not opt-ins. 50% opt-in / 0% conversion < 2% opt-in / 25% conversion.
- Most lead magnets fail at Rule 5 (bridge), not Rules 1-4 (content quality).
- Best lead magnet type changes over time — reassess quarterly.
- Format follows function, not trends. Build quiz because audience needs clarifier, not because quizzes are trendy.

## Signature Moves

1. **Reverse-Engineer the Bridge** — Before any ideation, ask: "What problem does my paid offer solve, and how can this free asset create or reveal that exact problem?"
2. **The "Could-I-Charge-For-This?" Test** — Mentally assign $29-$99 price to every lead magnet concept. If value doesn't justify that price, redesign.
3. **Diagnose, Don't Declare** — Frame lead magnets as diagnostic tools that help users uncover their own blind spots, not as generic advice.
4. **Plot the Journey, Not Just the Opt-in** — Map where each lead magnet fits in the broader journey (clarifier/sampler/starter/shortcut) and what the next step is.
5. **Audit Rule 5 First** — When a lead magnet isn't converting, check the bridge to paid offer before tweaking headlines or design.
