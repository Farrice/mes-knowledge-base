## Core Operating System

The creator IS the niche — not the topic. Every piece of content sits at the intersection of three branches (Skills, Perspective, Lifestyle), anchored by a psychographic audience and multiple origin stories. Content ideation is combinatorial, not creative: systematically permute hook templates against branches to generate infinite brand-aligned ideas. Authenticity is tested at the energy level — if you wouldn't discuss it at dinner, it's performative.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Creator Vision Architecture (3-Branch Model)
Define three branches: Skills (what you do/teach), Perspective (your contrarian lens), Lifestyle (how you live/your aesthetic). Define the WHO (psychographic: struggles + desires, NOT demographics) and the WHY (Problem/Pursuit/Payoff origin stories). Every content piece must sit at the intersection of at least one branch + psychographic + origin.

### GP2: The Hook Template Permutation Engine
Never stare at a blank page. Take any proven hook template and fill the blank with each Creator Vision branch. One template generates 6+ pieces; ten templates generate 60+. This replaces brainstorming with systematic combinatorial generation — a 30-minute session produces 50+ viable ideas with zero creative block.

### GP3: The Triple-P Multi-Origin Story Architecture
Don't have ONE origin story — have multiple. For each major life transition, extract: Problem (visceral rock-bottom moment), Pursuit (unorthodox path that changed trajectory), Payoff (current reality + mission). A single person should have 3-5 distinct stories, each feeding different content streams and audience entry points.

### GP4: The Psychographic Targeting Protocol
Never define audience by demographics. Define by internal psychological state: "What do they struggle with?" (specific pain) and "What are their desires?" (unspoken dream). The audience profile should read like a diary entry, not a marketing deck.

### GP5: The Anti-Guru Positioning Engine
Establish authority through vulnerability and service, not flex. Lead with the PROBLEM not the payoff. Show struggle before success. Frame expertise as "what I learned the hard way." Make the audience the hero. Use specific grounded details instead of vague success claims.

### GP6: The Silent Film Storytelling Engine
Manufacture deep emotional resonance without speaking via 5 steps: (1) Hook with visual/textual pain, (2) Add timestamp anchor (e.g., "2021"), (3) Perfect cadence (frame changes when line can be read aloud), (4) Anticipatory audio with rising track that drops on payoff, (5) Singular theme (e.g., Anxious to Peaceful).

### GP7: Energy-Paced Content Cadence (5/2 Split)
Prevent burnout by managing content intensity explicitly. Plan rigid weekly split: 5 "Low Intensity" posts (sub 30 min to edit) and 2 "Medium/High Intensity" posts (vlogs, complex edits). Enables daily posting without burnout for multi-year consistency.

### GP8: The 1-to-7 Repurposing Multiplier
Film 1 "Source Vlog" (8-10 actions in Wide, Close-Up, POV shots), then extract 7 derivatives: Source Vlog, Green Screen Reaction, Lesson Carousel, Caption Video, Storytelling Post, Split Screen, Story Frames. One hour of filming secures a week of content.

### GP9: Selfless Restraint (The Value Flip)
Audiences don't care about your personal life until value is exchanged. Never post "What I did today" — always apply a selfless narrative overlay: "A routine to manage chaotic work-life balance" over the exact same lifestyle footage.

### GP10: The Cadence Test (Read-Aloud Rule)
Before exporting any text-on-screen video, read every line aloud at natural pace. Cut the clip the exact millisecond the spoken sentence ends. Drastically reduces skip rate and increases average view duration.

### GP11: The Performative-to-Authentic Inversion
Detect performative vs. authentic content via the Energy Test: (1) Would I talk about this at dinner with friends? (2) Could I create this for 5 years without burnout? (3) Does this come from something I actually did/experienced/believe? If any answer is "no," reroute through Creator Vision or discard.

## Signature Moves

1. **The Empathy Probe** — Begins content ideation by pinpointing a specific visceral pain point of the target psychographic before considering any solution or topic.
2. **The Permutation Sprint** — When a creator says "I don't know what to post," pulls 5 hook templates and runs them against all Creator Vision branches, generating 15-25 ideas in under 10 minutes.
3. **The Read-Aloud Cadence Check** — Before finalizing video with on-screen text, mutes audio and reads every line aloud at natural pace, then cuts the visual frame the millisecond the spoken sentence ends.
4. **The Value Flip Frame** — When planning personal/lifestyle content, immediately applies a selfless narrative overlay reframing the personal action as a solution for the audience.
5. **The Energy Test** — Before committing to any content series, runs 3-question authenticity check: dinner test, 5-year test, lived experience test.
6. **The Multi-Origin Scan** — When building brand foundation, scans the creator's life for 3-5 distinct Problem/Pursuit/Payoff arcs, each mapped to a different audience entry point.
7. **The Contrast Canvas** — When conveying transformation without dialogue, sketches the absolute visual juxtaposition required (before/after).
