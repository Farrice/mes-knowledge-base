## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Arbitrage of Human Nature
Separate variables into rapidly changing surface dynamics and unchanging human nature constants. Build systems that exploit the constants. Identify 3+ "human nature constants" in any new domain within the first analysis session.

### GP2: The Encyclopedia Protocol
When entering new territory, consume comprehensively first, then identify which elements "speak to your soul" for deep dive. Complete broad survey before any specialization; identify 5+ unexpected connections.

### GP3: Pre-Fall/Post-Fall Assessment
Assess people and ideas: Has this been "punched in the face" by reality? Did they integrate the lesson or double down on prior beliefs? Predicts how person/idea will handle future adversity.

### GP4: The Book as Career Catalyst
Write the definitive resource in your domain before you need it. Send to gatekeepers before official release. Use controversy strategically. Book should generate 10x+ value in opportunities vs. direct sales.

### GP5: Practitioners Over Academics
When consuming theory, immediately ask: "What happens when I actually implement this? What are the real-world constraints?" Zero theories adopted without implementation constraint analysis.

### GP6: The Feedback Obsession
Choose domains with immediate, objective feedback. Use feedback to calibrate and improve, not to feel good. Daily capture, weekly pattern analysis, monthly strategy adjustment.

### GP7: Many Paths to Heaven
When encountering different approaches, ask: "Is this person's process right FOR THEM given how their mind works?" Successfully identify optimal methodology for others, not just self.

### GP8: The Reread Protocol
Identify 5-10 foundational texts. Reread annually. Document what's different — you are the variable, not the text. Generates novel insights from each annual revisit.

### GP9: The Four Horsemen Defense
Before significant decisions, audit: Am I being driven by fear? Greed? Hope? Am I simply ignorant of something crucial? Pre-decision audit documented for all major choices.

### GP10: Authenticity Over Polish
For communications meant to connect (not perform), resist editing. Let imperfections stand. Recipients should feel genuine connection vs. receiving a polished artifact.

### GP11: The Consensus Reality Check
When evaluating strong consensus, ask: "Is this true, or is this powerful because everyone believes it's true?" Accurately categorize phenomena as truth-based vs. belief-powered.

### GP12: The Synthesis Engine
After learning anything significant, immediately ask: "Where else does this apply that isn't obvious?" Identify 3+ non-obvious applications for every significant insight.

### GP13: Saturated Intuition Recognition
Track pattern exposure in any domain. Below threshold: use systematic approaches. Above threshold: artistic intuition becomes valid. Know which domains allow intuitive vs. systematic judgment.

### GP14: The Rebel Integration
Channel rebellious energy into strategic disruption rather than random opposition. Rebellious energy should produce structural change, not just noise.

### GP15: The Four Acts Architecture
Structure career as sequential acts: Act 1 (Learning), Act 2 (Empire Building), Act 3 (Ecosystem Creation), Act 4 (Legacy/Transmission). Clear transitions with deliberate capability transfer between acts.

### GP16: Money as Information System
Reframe money from objective to information. What is it telling you about value creation? Make money decisions based on information value, not accumulation instinct.

### GP17: The Mind-Body Reintegration
When facing persistent physical ailments without clear cause, investigate emotional suppression as potential source. Resolution comes through emotional awareness work.
