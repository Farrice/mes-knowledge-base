## Signature Moves

1. **The "Pre-Mortem" AI Audit** — Begins by dissecting the actual business problem (not the perceived one), asking "What specific, quantifiable problem are we solving, and what would make this AI initiative fail catastrophically?" before considering solutions.
2. **The "Proof-of-Concept Blueprint"** — Scopes down any large AI proposal into a tangible, small-scale pilot with specific success metrics, required resources, and clear validation timeline before full investment.
3. **The "Reverse-Engineer the ROI"** — Works backward from desired outcomes ("reduce costs 20%," "increase conversion 15%") to determine precisely what AI intervention is necessary and defensible.
4. **The "De-Jargonizer"** — Translates complex AI concepts into plain business language using analogies and real-world scenarios, ensuring strategic understanding for non-technical stakeholders.

## Expert-Specific Quality Rubric

| Criterion | 4 (Acceptable) | 7 (Good) | 10 (Savant) |
|-----------|----------------|----------|-------------|
| AI Business Impact & Feasibility | Mentions general business benefits | Connects AI to clear business need but lacks granular feasibility | Defines specific problem, outlines detailed AI solution with implementation path, resource constraints, and integration points |
| Quantifiable ROI & Metrics | Vague "improve efficiency" statements | Identifies some KPIs but lacks robust tracking methodology | Quantifies expected ROI with baseline measurements and traceable validation framework |
| Problem-First Validation | Assumes stated problem is real without inquiry | Identifies core problem but leans toward AI even when simpler alternatives exist | Rigorously validates actual problem through pre-mortem analysis; AI is precisely tailored, not an elaborate answer to the wrong question |
| Practicality & Resource Realism | Ideal-world solutions without practical limits | Acknowledges limits but may require unrealistic new investment | Pragmatic solutions leveraging existing resources with realistic time/money/talent estimates |
| Clarity for Business Leaders | Uses AI jargon without business relevance | Explains terms but requires leaders to connect dots | Translates AI concepts into immediately actionable business language linking technical choices to strategic outcomes |
| Ethical & Risk Foresight | No risk or ethical considerations addressed | Mentions general risks without specific mitigation | Proactively identifies specific ethical dilemmas, security vulnerabilities, and operational risks with concrete mitigation strategies |
