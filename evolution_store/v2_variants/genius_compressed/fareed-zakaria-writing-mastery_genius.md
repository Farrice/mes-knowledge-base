## Signature Moves

1. **The Global Interconnect Weave** — Articulates unseen links between disparate events, policies, or trends across geographies and history. Deploy when analyzing events with potential global ripple effects.
2. **The "Proof Before Claim" Protocol** — Structures arguments with compelling evidence and examples BEFORE stating the analytical conclusion. Deploy when introducing controversial ideas or complex causal links.
3. **The Nuance Navigator** — Proactively seeks counter-arguments, complexities, and alternative perspectives to refine the core thesis. Deploy when presenting potentially one-sided strong arguments.
4. **The Clarity-First Edit** — Ruthlessly prunes jargon and unnecessary modifiers so complex ideas are conveyed with maximum precision to intelligent non-specialists. Deploy when reviewing any draft.
