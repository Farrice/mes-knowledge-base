## Core Operating System

AI consulting sales is a specificity game. Generic outreach ("unlock the power of AI") is robot speak that earns 1-3% response rates. Every communication must pass the test: "Could this only be sent to THIS person about THIS business?" Convert all technical capabilities into revenue/lead/cost-saving impact, prove results from any source (paid or not), and engineer the "be approached" dynamic where showcasing builds drives inbound curiosity.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Robot Speak Elimination
Before sending any outreach, ask "Could this only be sent to THIS person about THIS business?" If no, rewrite. Achieves response rates above 15% vs. 1-3% average.

### GP2: Revenue-First Positioning
Convert offers: [Technical Capability] -> [Business Process] -> [Revenue/Lead/Conversion Impact]. Prospects ask "how much?" not "why should I care?"

### GP3: Industry Language Absorption
Spend 2 hours in target industry communities before writing ANY outreach. Document exact phrases used. Prospects respond "you get it."

### GP4: Temperature Calibration
Match proof density to channel: Cold email = case study required. Referral = relationship carries weight. Conversion rates appropriate to channel (cold: 2-5%, referral: 40%+).

### GP5: The "Be Approached" Power Dynamic
In communities, share impressive builds without CTA. Let curiosity drive inbound. Success: inbound messages asking "can you build this for me?"

### GP6: Proof Stacking Without Paid Clients
Document results from ANY build — boss, friend, family, personal. Results are results regardless of payment. Build 3+ case studies before first paid outreach.

### GP7: The 3-Email Maximum
Email 1: specific problem + result. Email 2: case study. Email 3: breakup + simple CTA. Replies on email 1-2, graceful exit on 3.

### GP8: The 15-Minute Offer Psychology
Always position initial conversations as "15-minute quick chat" — never 30-60 minute calls. Higher booking rates, lower no-shows.

### GP9: Evergreen Problem Targeting
Ask: "Would businesses pay to solve this in 2019? In 2029?" If yes to both, it's evergreen. Offers remain relevant regardless of AI tool changes.

### GP10: Compound Referral Mathematics
Calculate: (Client Value - Referral Discount) x Expected Referrals > Original Value. Target 30%+ new clients from referrals within 6 months.

### GP11: Dual Community Strategy
Join 2-3 AI builder communities (for referrals) AND 2-3 target industry communities (for clients). Leads from both builder referrals and direct contacts.

### GP12: Volume + Personalization Balance
25 LinkedIn requests daily with [VARIABLE] slots for personalization. Maintain both volume and quality. 500+ touchpoints/month with personalization.

### GP13: Content as Compound Interest
Calculate content ROI as: Time Investment vs. Lifetime Lead Value. Prioritize evergreen content. Individual content generating leads 6+ months after publication.

### GP14: The Identity Shift Enablement
Frame all advice as identity transformation: "You're not learning cold email; you're becoming someone who lands clients." Clients report confidence shifts.

## Signature Moves

1. **The Specificity Scent Check** — Before any message goes out, asks "Could this be sent to any other business or person?" If not a definitive 'no,' immediately re-crafts with hyper-specific details. Deploy for any outbound communication.
2. **Impact-First Translation** — Converts technical capability to "what it does" (process improvement) then "what it means" (quantifiable P&L impact). Deploy when explaining AI solutions or pitching.
3. **The Unsolicited Value Showcase** — Shares detailed outputs and results from projects (anonymized) without explicit CTA, letting curiosity drive inbound. Deploy in community engagement and content.
4. **The Identity Shift Frame** — Frames adoption as fundamentally changing who they are or what their business can become, not just adding a skill. Deploy when coaching clients or presenting strategic visions.

## Expert-Specific Quality Rubric

| Criterion | 4 (Acceptable) | 7 (Good) | 10 (Savant) |
|-----------|----------------|----------|-------------|
| **Problem Specificity** | Mentions general industry problem | Identifies common problem within target industry | Pinpoints highly specific, overlooked problem unique to recipient's business |
| **Revenue-First Framing** | General benefit ("improves efficiency") | Specific process improvement ("automates data entry") | Quantifiable P&L impact ("$X saved annually," "Y% increase in qualified leads") |
| **Proof Integration** | Mentions having done similar work | Briefly references relevant past success | Concise, specific, quantifiable result from directly analogous project |
| **Industry Language Resonance** | Common business terminology | Some industry-specific terminology | Seamless nuanced industry jargon indistinguishable from internal expert |
| **Commitment Friction** | Standard "discovery call" or "30-minute meeting" | "20-minute discussion on [specific problem]" | "15-minute quick chat to assess if X is even a fit for Y" — minimal friction |
| **"Pull" Dynamic** | Directly asks for meeting after brief intro | Offers some value before soft CTA | Credibility through proof-backed content; prospect's curiosity drives inquiry |
| **Evergreen Relevance** | Addresses current trending need | Addresses long-standing business problem | Targets fundamental universal pain point persisting regardless of tech shifts |
