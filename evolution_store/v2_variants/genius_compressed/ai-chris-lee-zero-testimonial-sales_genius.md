## Genius Patterns

### P1: The Proof Paradox Diagnosis
"Can't get X without Y, can't get Y without X" = false binary. Third option always exists: manufacture proof through free audit, self-test, or borrowed results. Identify and expose.

### P2: Feature-to-Outcome Flip
Chain: Feature → Benefit → Outcome → Specific Number. Ask "so what?" until reaching measurement. "I help businesses automate" → "I help roofing companies book 8 estimates instead of 3."

### P3: The 3-Question Proof Test
Before any proof asset: (1) Who was this for? (2) What problem did you fix? (3) What outcome happened? All 3 answered in under 60 seconds or rework.

### P4: Permission-Tax Elimination
"Mind if I share..." = tax on attention. Replace with "[Here's the proof]. Is this relevant to [their situation]?" Proof leads, permission follows.

### P5: The One Proof Asset Constraint
Build exactly ONE 2-5 minute Loom. No website. No portfolio. No deck. One asset answers "Why should I trust you?" with one link.

### P6: Proof Type Selection Logic
Any result (paid, free, self-test) → Outcome Proof. Zero results → Audit Proof. Select correct type in under 60 seconds.

### P7: The Warren Buffett Reframe
Resisting free work? "You need the outcome more than they need the service. This is investment, not charity." Eliminates resistance.

### P8: The 5-Section Loom Architecture
Every proof Loom: (1) Who + What → (2) Before State → (3) Solution Built → (4) Outcome → (5) CTA. Scripted and recorded in under 30 minutes.

### P9: Specificity Intensification
Generic → specific until reader sees themselves. "I help businesses" → "I help roofing companies automate lead follow-up so they book 8 estimates instead of 3."

### P10: Ship Over Perfect
One asset. Under 5 minutes. Created within 24 hours. No revisions after first take. Shipped same day conceived.

### P11: Cost-of-Inaction Projection
"3 months—still at zero. 6 months—still getting ready. 12 months—looking for a job." Urgency without manipulation.

### P12: The Competitor Contrast
"Someone with worse skills just closed their third client—not better testimonials, [specific action]." Shifts from "learn more" to "execute now."

### P13: Proof-First Outreach Structure
"[Specific outcome] + [Proof link] + Is this relevant to [their company]?" Replies about proof, not about who you are.

### P14: The GIF Embed Technique
Export first 3 seconds of Loom as GIF → embed in email body → link to full video. 2-3x click-through rate increase.
