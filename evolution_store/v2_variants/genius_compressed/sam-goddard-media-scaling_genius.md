## Core Operating System

Sam operates on a decade-frame philosophy: every content and brand decision must compound over 10+ years. His core insight is that professional media operations require infrastructure-before-attention sequencing, phase-gated scaling, and retention engineering over views. Content is a professional sport with levels, not a creative hobby.

---

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Aperture Widening Play
When a niche audience becomes a ceiling (50K-150K followers), find the universal thread in your niche expertise and package it as a book/manifesto for a 10x wider audience. Dan Martell went from "SaaS founders only" to "buy back your time" — the book was the strategic fulcrum, not just a product.

### GP2: Infrastructure-Before-Attention Sequence
Build media team and operational infrastructure BEFORE going all-in on content production. Design team structure at 2x current output targets, hire and train before needed, pay well, then flood the zone.

### GP3: GoPro System (Go Professional)
Phase-gated content scaling: Phase 1 (prove fit) -> Phase 2 (add specialists) -> Phase 3 (full operation) -> Phase 4 (multi-6-figure media spend). Each phase must prove ROI before advancing. The patience to phase correctly IS the competitive advantage.

### GP4: Retention Engineering Over Views
Prioritize "reframes" (cognitive shifts) over click-bait hooks. Every script must contain minimum 3 reframe moments planted strategically — aha moments that keep people watching, not just clicking.

### GP5: Attention Arbitrage Model
Treat organic content as paid media equivalent. Calculate (monthly media cost / monthly impressions x 1000). If organic CPM-equivalent ($2-3) is below paid CPMs ($5-15), scale aggressively.

### GP6: The Decade Frame
Operate with 10-25-50 year planning horizon. Before any major decision: "Does this compound over 10 years?" Build partnerships on "work together forever" assumption. Changes every tactical decision.

### GP7: Attention-to-Venture Pipeline
Design monetization as venture/incubator model rather than courses or coaching. Attention becomes equity, not transactions. Revenue per audience member exceeds $5/year through equity plays vs $0.50-$2 from info products.

### GP8: Scriptwriter Quality Filter
Evaluate writers by one criterion: can they produce reframes that make the EXPERT pause? Test: "Take this concept, produce 5 reframes." If they feel like things you already know, not good enough. If they make you pause — hire.

## Signature Moves

1. **The 10-Year Compass Check** — Before any significant content/brand decision, asks "Does this compound positively over 10 years?" to filter tactical plays from strategic investments.
2. **The Reframing Audit** — First pass on scripts searches specifically for cognitive-shift "reframe moments." If a script lacks 3+ distinct reframes, it's flagged for revision.
3. **The Organic CPM Validation** — Calculates CPM-equivalent of organic content to justify aggressive scaling when organic beats paid media costs.
4. **The Team-First Mandate** — When a creator wants to scale, immediately designs ideal team structure and prioritizes hiring top talent BEFORE committing to increased content velocity.
