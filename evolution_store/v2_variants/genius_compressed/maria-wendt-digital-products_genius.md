## Core Operating System

Maria Wendt built a $3M+ digital product business by replacing passion-first creation with a research-first, outcome-driven, trust-compounding system. She treats her own excitement as a warning signal, validates externally before building, launches rough to learn fast, and lets the market tell her what to build next. Every product is a test, every launch a data-collection event, every customer a trust investment.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Research-First Kill Switch
Mine Reddit, Facebook groups, Amazon reviews, and creator comments for exact buyer language — 20+ verbatim pain statements. These become your title, copy, and positioning. Formula: "Who struggles with what when trying to do something?" If you can't find 20+ quotes, the product doesn't exist yet.

### GP2: The Destination Painting Protocol
Rewrite every product description as an after-state narrative. Translate features into vivid outcomes: "Get your first 1,000 email subscribers" beats "email marketing guide." Lead with the result, never the vehicle. A stranger should picture their life after using it from the one-liner alone.

### GP3: The Value-Anchored Pricing Engine
Price against the outcome's economic value, not effort or competitors. Calculate the buyer's problem cost, price at 5-25% of that value. For first versions, price lower + label "beta." Never underprice to suspicion — a $7 fix for a $10K problem triggers distrust.

### GP4: The MVP Launch Velocity Principle
Launch the smallest version solving the core problem — in 60 minutes if possible. Ship at 80%, never 100%. Time from idea to live product measured in days, not months. First customer feedback within 7 days. "If you're not embarrassed by your first version, you waited too long."

### GP5: The Trust Depth Multiplier
Shrink audience to increase revenue. 1,000 engaged > 10,000 passive. Give away 90% of best material so paid products feel like the natural next step. Build real relationships in one place consistently. Sales become byproduct of demonstrated expertise.

### GP6: The Pre-Sell Validation Gate
Before building anything, find 10 people who'd buy it. Offer pre-sale at discount. If fewer than 3/10 say yes, rework the concept. Use pre-sale revenue to fund creation. This is demand validation, not desperation.

### GP7: The Headline Dominance Rule
Spend 80% of marketing effort on the headline. Write 20+ variations. Each must pass: "Does this tell a stranger what they GET and HOW FAST?" Clarity always beats creativity. A cold reader must explain what the product does from the headline alone.

### GP8: The Find-One-Help-One Method
For first sales, skip content marketing. Go directly to individuals who posted about the exact problem. Reach out 1:1 with authentic, helpful messaging. Goal: one paying customer from direct outreach.

### GP9: The Identity Shift Product
Design $5-$10 micro-products to shift buyer identity from "wants to make money online" to "HAS made money online." Revenue is secondary; psychological shift is primary. Create in ≤60 minutes using free tools. One sale compounds into everything that follows.

### GP10: The Product Ladder Architect
Every product is a stepping stone: $5-$10 micro → $27-$47 core → $97-$197 premium → $500+ program. Each level solves the "what now?" the previous creates. 20%+ of buyers at each tier naturally ask about the next without hard selling.
