## Core Operating System

Voice mastery is not research -> replicate, it's research -> predict -> calibrate. Mabry's core insight is that building a predictive mental model of a client — one that can anticipate their stance on topics they've never addressed — is the AI-proof moat in ghostwriting. AI handles templates; it cannot build a predictive model of a human, map their controversy boundaries, or collect their hidden gems. Voice writing is the surviving lane.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Prediction Discipline
By Week 2 of any engagement, generate 5-10 predictions per review cycle: "What would this client say about a topic they've never addressed?" Each wrong prediction, corrected in a review call, refines the internal model exponentially faster than passive consumption.

### GP2: The Controversy Line Map
Every lasting personal brand has a hidden three-zone map: Zone 1 (Flag-Plant) — core identity, go hard, use for bold hooks. Zone 2 (Nuanced) — engage carefully with caveats, use for depth pieces. Zone 3 (No-Go) — actively avoid regardless of "be polarizing" advice. Map it from existing content without needing to be taught.

### GP3: The Hidden Gem Collector
The most powerful stories are ones the client tells casually on podcasts but doesn't think to suggest for content. Maintain two story banks: "Their Picks" (stories they suggest) and "My Picks" (stories you found). My Picks should outnumber Their Picks 2:1.

### GP4: The Authorship Pyramid
A first book should capture the base of the industry pyramid (dreamers, aspirational readers), not the tactical middle. The base becomes evangelists; elite peers respond to influence over the base. Write a memoir showing the full hero's journey. Alex Hormozi and Dan Martell executed this exactly.

### GP5: The Mode-Split Protocol
Creative brain and workhorse brain use identical horsepower for opposite purposes — never switch between them. Workhorse mode: timed, clock-obsessed, Parkinson's Law, for maintenance content. Deep work mode: clock-forgotten, immersive, no Pomodoro, for transformative projects. Schedule in separate blocks.

### GP6: The Trust Ladder
Voice writing autonomy earned through specific progression: Stage 1 (write -> client reviews -> someone else publishes), Stage 2 (write -> client reviews -> you publish), Stage 3 (write -> publish without review). The "no edits" milestone at Stage 2 is the most important moment. Don't rush to Stage 3 — let the client offer it.

### Meta-Pattern: Voice as the AI-Proof Moat
Voice writing is the one copywriting lane AI cannot commoditize. If you can write in voice, everything else is just a different workflow. This is the surviving lane.

## Signature Moves

1. **The Predictive Probe** — Generates 5-10 specific predictions about client's stance on unaddressed topics, presents for validation, tracks accuracy over time.
2. **The Boundary Map Refinement** — Categorizes every client opinion into Flag-plant, Nuanced, or No-go zones; continuously updates based on review feedback.
3. **The Submerged Narrative Extraction** — Listens to every casual interaction (podcasts, interviews, off-hand comments) specifically for character-revealing stories with concrete details.
4. **The Kinesthetic Voice Imprint** — Handwrites 3-5 of the client's best-performing pieces to internalize cadence, rhythm, and specific word choice.
5. **The Autonomy Acceleration Protocol** — After achieving "no edits" milestone, proactively proposes taking over scheduling and publishing responsibility.
