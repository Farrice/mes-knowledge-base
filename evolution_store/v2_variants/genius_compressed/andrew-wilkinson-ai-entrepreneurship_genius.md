## Signature Moves

1. **Self-Problem Validation** — Before investing time, explicitly articulates a personal expensive problem the AI solution would solve, quantifying its current cost in time, money, or friction. Deploy when initiating a new project or evaluating a business idea.
2. **AI Staffing Audit** — Reviews bottlenecks and asks "If I had to hire an employee for this, how much would it cost?" then maps that task to an AI capability. Deploy when optimizing workflows or identifying automation opportunities.
3. **Vibe Code Sprint** — Opens a natural language interface and describes desired outcome in conversational language, then rapidly iterates until a functional prototype emerges. Deploy when rapid prototyping, exploring new ideas, or building custom tools.
4. **Moat-First Market Scan** — Before entering a market, conducts analysis for "pennies in front of steamroller" scenarios and prioritizes ventures with distribution advantages or defensible positions. Deploy when doing strategic planning or evaluating investments.
5. **Frictionless Workflow Integration** — When adopting any new tool, meticulously maps integration into existing workflows, eliminating friction points, context switching, and device silos. Deploy when designing productivity systems or evaluating software.
