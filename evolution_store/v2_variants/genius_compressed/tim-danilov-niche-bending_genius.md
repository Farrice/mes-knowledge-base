## Core Operating System

Every piece of content is two independent layers: Market (audience vertical) and Format (delivery container). Markets are fixed commodities; formats are infinite and transferable. Innovation lives in transplanting proven viral formats across markets, not in inventing new topics.

---

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Format-Market Decomposition
See every content piece as [Market] + [Format] — two independent variables. Markets are fixed; formats are infinite and transferable. Never think in "topics," only in these two layers. Deploy when starting any content strategy or competitive analysis.

### GP2: The Empty Square Method (Grid Mapping)
Map proven viral formats (columns) against major markets (rows) and hunt for absences. Every empty cell is a blue ocean opportunity with dual validation: format works elsewhere, audience exists. Target 3+ empty squares you can fill with genuine expertise.

### GP3: Viral Format Transplantation
Take formats proven viral in one market and surgically transplant them into different markets. Extract the format skeleton (title structure, pacing, narrative arc, hook mechanics) and rebuild with your market's content. Virality mechanics exploit universal psychology, not market-specific interest. Target 3x+ outperformance.

### GP4: Language Adoption (Borrowed Fluency)
When bending a format, study the vocabulary, metaphors, and cultural references of the source market. Content must be bilingual — fluent in both your expertise domain AND the borrowed format's native tongue. Example: Tazoo uses full gaming language ("current meta," "patch update," "nerfed," "OP African builds") for biology content.

### GP5: Audience Bridge Architecture
Identify your target audience's entertainment diet — formats they consume for fun. Use those as bridge structures to deliver expertise to people who wouldn't otherwise encounter it. The gateway changes, not the expertise. Success: viewers say "I don't usually watch this kind of thing."

### GP6: The Expertise Constraint
Non-negotiable quality gate: never bend a format you can't fill with genuine expertise. Ask "Can I deliver world-class content inside this container?" If no, find a different format. This prevents niche bending from becoming gimmickry.

### GP7: Outlier-Driven Format Scouting
The best format intelligence comes from small channels with outlier videos (3x-100x their normal views). Big channels succeed through distribution; small channels with outliers succeed through format innovation — the signal is undiluted. Prioritize the 1,000-subscriber channel with a million-view outlier.

### GP8: Saturation Arbitrage
Target saturated markets deliberately. Saturation means proven demand; audience boredom with existing formats means hunger for novelty. Use the grid to find where format innovation hasn't happened yet. Saturation is a prerequisite, not a problem.
