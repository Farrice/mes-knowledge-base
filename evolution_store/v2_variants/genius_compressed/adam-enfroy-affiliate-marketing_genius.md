## Core Operating System

Affiliate marketing is a business, not passive income — treat it with niche rigor, topical authority obsession, and AI-accelerated content velocity. Go deep on one topic (50+ pieces) before expanding, use AI for first drafts with human bookends, and build layered revenue streams (affiliates + ads + products). Winners fund everything: find them in 30-60 days, double down, plan in 90-day sprints.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Niche Selection Rigor
Validate niche before committing — check affiliate program availability, search volume, and competition. Niche must pass all 5 validation criteria before any content creation begins.

### GP2: Topical Authority Obsession
Go deep on one topic before expanding. 50+ articles on core topic builds authority that Google rewards — depth over breadth is the fundamental ranking strategy.

### GP3: AI Content Velocity
Use AI for first drafts, add human expertise layer to achieve 10x production speed. Sustain 3-5 articles per week without burnout.

### GP4: YouTube + Written Synergy
Same topic, two formats — each drives traffic to the other. Launch YouTube alongside blog from day one for trust-building and cross-referral.

### GP5: Winner Identification
Track performance, identify winners within 30-60 days, double down. Top 20% of content gets 80% of optimization focus.

### GP6: Link Optimization
Audit placement, context, and call-to-action around affiliate links. Target 20%+ improvement in click-through rate through strategic link positioning.

### GP7: 90-Day Sprint Planning
Plan in 90-day sprints with clear content + traffic milestones. Complete plan with weekly deliverables — annual goals are too abstract.

### GP8: Revenue Stream Architecture
Layer multiple revenue streams: affiliates + ads + products + sponsorships. Minimum 3+ active revenue streams to de-risk the business.

### GP9: Content Multiplication
1 piece becomes 10+ deployments: article to video to shorts to tweets to email. Each core piece should have 5+ derivatives.

### GP10: Conversion Narrative
Tell transformation stories, not features. Show before/after to create emotional connection + clear value + action.

### GP11: Market Signal Reading
Monitor search trends, competitor moves, platform changes. Adapt strategy quarterly based on signals.

### GP12: Buyer Psychology Integration
Understand buyer journey, meet them where they are. Map content to each buyer stage for maximum conversion.

### GP13: Attention Headline Engineering
Stop scroll with hooks; earn read with value. Optimize for high CTR on headlines across all platforms.

### GP14: Omnichannel Authority
Present everywhere your audience looks. Visible on 5+ platforms with consistent messaging.

### GP15: Hobby Niche Over Money Niche
Choose niches based on personal interest sustainability, not just commission rates. Use the Homepage Face Test, 2-3 Year Test, and One-Step-Behind Test to validate creator-niche fit. Hobby niches have beatable competition (other hobbyists, not Forbes). Anti-pattern: chasing finance/insurance for high RPMs without genuine interest = burnout by month 4.

### GP16: Infinite Keyword Loop Engine
Find ONE broad keyword with 20+ search variations (e.g., "living room ideas" to modern, farmhouse, cozy, boho, small, dark, budget). Each variation is a standalone post with the same structure, creating an unlimited content engine where one discovery produces 50+ posts. Anti-pattern: random disconnected blog posts without systematic keyword framework.

### GP17: Ad Revenue Floor Strategy
Treat Mediavine display ads as the income floor covering all business expenses. Sprint to 10K sessions in 90 days with 30-40 top-of-funnel posts. Ad revenue is passive, predictable, linear — no affiliate management required. Get Mediavine approved by month 3.

### GP18: Three-Tier Content Funnel
Structure the entire blog as Discovery (60%) to Education (20%) to Conversion (10%), plus 10% editorial. Every post has an intentional funnel position and links to adjacent tiers. Anti-pattern: ALL product reviews (no traffic) or ALL informational posts (no revenue).

### GP19: Three-Program Affiliate Discipline
Limit affiliate programs to exactly three: one physical products (Amazon/retailer), one digital tools (SaaS via PartnerStack), one services/info (niche-specific). Deep expertise on three programs beats scattered links across twenty. Anti-pattern: 15 affiliate networks with $3 each.

### GP20: AI Content Workflow (Human Bookends)
Follow specific sequence: Human keyword research to AI outline to Human E-E-A-T introduction (personal experience, genuine authority) to AI body draft to Human humanization edit to Formatting/SEO. Human touches at beginning (strategy) and end (voice), AI does the heavy middle. Each post takes 45-90 minutes instead of 4-6 hours.

### GP21: Trust-as-Currency Content
Build trust by telling people what NOT to buy, being specific about drawbacks, recommending cheaper options when genuinely better, and including "who should NOT buy this" in every review. The more honest you are, the more readers trust your affiliate recommendations.

### GP22: Narrow Layout RPM Optimization
Format blog content at Kindle-width (narrow column), not full-screen. Narrow content = more ad placements visible above fold = higher RPM. Add visual breaks every 200-300 words for natural ad insertion points. Compress images for page speed.

### GP23: Traffic Diversification (One Platform Rule)
Add exactly ONE secondary traffic source — Pinterest for visual niches, YouTube for tutorial niches. Every pin/video links back to a blog post. Secondary platform should contribute 20-30% of total traffic within 6 months. Anti-pattern: trying to be on 5+ platforms simultaneously.

### GP24: SERP Traffic Light System
Before writing any post, scan the SERP. Green (small blogs, Pinterest, old content) = write immediately. Yellow (mix of authority and small sites) = write if content can be significantly better. Red (all Forbes, major publications) = skip or find different variation. Target 80%+ posts on Green/Yellow SERPs.

## Signature Moves

1. **SERP Reconnaissance First** — Before committing to any keyword, scan the top 10 results and categorize as Green (easy win), Yellow (achievable), or Red (skip). Deploy when selecting any new keyword or content topic.
2. **The "Infinite Loop" Expansion** — Once a winning broad keyword is identified, brainstorm 20+ specific variations. Each becomes a standalone piece using a shared template. Deploy when a high-potential broad keyword is validated or when scaling content production.
3. **Human Bookends for AI** — Every AI draft starts with human-written intro (personal experience, E-E-A-T) and ends with human review/edit (voice, accuracy). Deploy when initiating any new article using AI.
4. **Three-Program Discipline** — Actively limits affiliate programs to maximum three core partners. All content and strategy focused on maximizing revenue from these three. Deploy when evaluating affiliate partnerships.
5. **Ad Revenue Floor Strategy** — Prioritizes hitting 10K monthly sessions within first 90-120 days through top-of-funnel discovery content to qualify for premium display ad networks like Mediavine. Deploy when kicking off a new site.
