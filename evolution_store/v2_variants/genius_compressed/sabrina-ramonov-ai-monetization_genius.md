## Core Operating System

Sabrina's singular insight is that sequence determines success: brand before product, audience before offer, commitment before execution. The same people who fail product-first would succeed brand-first. She treats AI as a once-in-a-generation market timing opportunity where the playbook is lock in, learn in public, build distribution, then monetize through recurring community.

---

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Lock-In Protocol
Choose one AI sub-domain, commit for 12 months minimum, spend first 3 months in pure learning mode with zero monetization attempts. Refuse all alternative paths. Identity-level commitment before execution eliminates the #1 failure mode: strategy-hopping.

### GP2: The Learning-in-Public Engine
Post daily about tools tested, discoveries, mistakes, and surprising results while learning — zero polish required. You don't learn THEN create content; content about learning IS the brand-building mechanism from day 1.

### GP3: The Order-of-Operations Insight
Invest first 3-6 months entirely in brand building via learning in public. Only begin product/offer design after you have distribution. Same product launched into the void fails; launched into an audience asking for solutions succeeds.

### GP4: The Recurring Revenue Selector
Default to community/subscription (Skool) over one-time products (courses). Price low to start ($50/mo), increase as value compounds. This is a pre-made decision, not a debate.

### GP5: The Community Moat Taxonomy
Build four defensible layers: (1) continuous education, (2) collected wins/case studies, (3) recurring live events, (4) active peer forum. Wins are the hardest to clone and become the strongest moat — competitors can copy curriculum but not member success stories.

### GP6: The Price-Ramp Churn Reducer
Launch at ~$50/month, add education modules regularly, raise price as content library grows. Price increases function as both revenue driver AND churn reduction mechanism because perceived value compounds.

### GP7: The Anti-Shiny-Object Protocol
When an alternative strategy looks appealing, acknowledge it, write it down, ignore it for the remainder of the 12-month commitment. The antidote to strategy-hopping is naming the temptation and choosing not to act.

### GP8: The Market Timing Leverage Pattern
Choose the domain where capital is flowing AND tooling is accessible AND demand exceeds supply. Ride tailwinds, don't fight headwinds. AI is the current asymmetric opportunity for newcomers.
