# Donald Miller — StoryBrand Mastery — Genius Context

> Load before executing any workflow. Full extraction intelligence.

## Core Operating System

Miller's genius: **story structure is a neurological exploit.** The brain daydreams 30% of waking hours — story is the only format that stops it because story = simulated survival experience. Communication that doesn't follow story structure is invisible.

**Three Laws:**
1. **Hero Law**: Customer is the hero. You are the guide. Make yourself hero = lose customer.
2. **Sequence Law**: Products cannot appear before you've earned the right. Problem → Agitation → Thesis → Product.
3. **Clarity Law**: Complexity = survival-instinct trigger → disengage. Zero cognitive load = maximum engagement.

## The SB7 BrandScript

| # | Element | Function | Psychology |
|---|---------|----------|------------|
| 1 | **Character** | Customer as hero — identify desire | Opens story, establishes who it's for |
| 2 | **Problem** | External + Internal + Philosophical | Creates conflict (survival threat) |
| 3 | **Guide** | Brand with Empathy + Authority | Trust: "I understand AND I can help" |
| 4 | **Plan** | Process steps + Agreement plan | Reduces fear: "here's the clear path" |
| 5 | **Call to Action** | Direct + Transitional CTA | Resolves decision anxiety |
| 6 | **Failure** | Negative stakes | Loss aversion — the cliff |
| 7 | **Success** | Positive transformation | The summit — life they could have |

### Three Problem Levels
- **External**: Tangible issue ("plunger doesn't work")
- **Internal**: Emotional experience ("feel disgusted and helpless")
- **Philosophical**: Why it's wrong ("shouldn't be this unpleasant")

**Critical**: Customers buy solutions to INTERNAL problems. External = rationalization. Internal = motivation.

### One-Liner Formula
Problem → Solution → Result. "Most [customers] struggle with [problem]. We provide [mechanism]. So they can [outcome]."

### CTA Resolution
Never imperative ("Buy now"). Use: "If you are struggling with [X], [purchasing Y] is the right decision." Resolves internal debate.

### Marketing Made Simple Funnel
1. **One-Liner** → Core message (everywhere)
2. **Website** → SB7-structured homepage
3. **Lead Generator** → Transitional CTA (free guide)
4. **Nurture Emails** → Weekly value (trust compounding)
5. **Sales Emails** → 6-email conversion (problem → proof → paradigm → close)

## Anti-Patterns (Structural Violations)

| ID | Violation | Detection |
|----|-----------|-----------|
| AN-1 | **Brand as Hero** | First 3 sentences: brand = subject of >1 → hero violation |
| AN-2 | **Credentials Before Value** | Credentials before problem statement → wrong sequence |
| AN-3 | **Jargon as Clarity** | 3+ industry terms in one sentence → rewrite in plain language |
| AN-4 | **Vague Pressure CTAs** | "Buy Now"/"Get Started" → replace with cognitive resolution |
| AN-5 | **Features Before Transformation** | Feature lists before Success element → restructure |
| AN-6 | **Skip-Presenting Plan** | No "Step 1-2-3" between Guide and CTA → missing Plan |
| AN-7 | **Missing Villain** | Can't identify what customer fights against → add villain |

## Signature Moves

1. **The "You-First" Scan** — Highlight every "we/us/our" in first 100 words → replace centering on "you/your." → Reviewing any marketing copy.
2. **The Three-Problem Drill** — "External? How that makes them feel (internal)? Why it's wrong (philosophical)?" All three addressed. → Starting messaging or diagnosing low performance.
3. **The Caveman Clarity Check** — Read aloud. Caveman grunts "What this mean?" → simplify. → Finalizing any message.
4. **The Earned Product Introduction** — No product mention until character desire + three-level problem established. → Structuring any content.
5. **The Stakes Polarity Engine** — After positive transformation → pivot to negative consequences of inaction. Cliff + summit equally clear. → Crafting resolution (Elements 6 & 7).

## Expert-Specific Quality Rubric

| Criterion | 4 (Acceptable) | 7 (Good) | 10 (Savant) |
|-----------|----------------|----------|-------------|
| **Hero-Centricity** | Brand mentioned occasionally | Customer consistently the subject; brand supportive | Customer undeniable hero; brand nearly invisible |
| **Problem Depth** | External clearly stated | External + internal identified and agitated | All three vividly articulated and deeply resonant |
| **Guide Positioning** | States authority or empathy, not both | Both empathy + authority established | Empathy through shared experience, authority through simple solutions |
| **CTA Resolution** | Direct ("Buy Now") | Hints at resolution, still feels like command | Explicitly resolves internal debate — the obvious "right decision" |
