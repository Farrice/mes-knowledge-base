# Luke Iha — Proof Ladder Mastery — Genius Context

> Load before any proof, copy, or persuasion workflow. The operating system behind copy that PROVES instead of CLAIMS.

## How Iha Thinks: Proof-First OS

Persuasion = proof architecture. Every word builds trust or burns it. The reader runs a continuous credibility audit; copy must pass at every checkpoint.

**Foundational Law**: Every claim needs immediate proof. A "naked claim" depletes trust — the reader feels "sounds like marketing" and disengages.
**Structural Law**: Proof braided into EVERY section (Siamese Twins). Claim and proof born attached. Separating kills both.
**Conversion Law**: CR = proof density × proof diversity × proof placement. Most proof, most varied forms, right positions — wins.

## The Proof Ladder (5-Tier Architecture)

| Tier | Type | Power | Difficulty |
|------|------|-------|-----------|
| **5 — Aspirational** | Media, awards, expert endorsements, published work, speaking | Highest (borrowed authority) | Hardest |
| **4 — Scientific** | Data, A/B tests, third-party studies, benchmarks | Very high (objectivity) | High |
| **3 — Social** | Testimonials, case studies, user count, name drops, community | High (peer validation) | Medium |
| **2 — Demonstrable** | Screenshots, before/after, live demos, process breakdowns | Medium (seeing is believing) | Low-Medium |

**Climbing**: Everyone starts T1. Bridge to next tier through deliberate experiments. T1→T3 should take 30-90 days of systematic execution. Don't stall waiting for higher-tier proof.

## The Proof Audit (How Iha Reads Copy)

1. **Mark every claim** — underline everything asking the reader to believe something
2. **Check adjacent proof** — proof within 2 sentences? If not → naked claim
3. **Assess proof variety** — using same type repeatedly? Diversify across tiers
4. **Check placement** — front-loaded? Back-loaded? Braided? Back-loaded = reader already disengaged
5. **"So what?" test** — would a skeptic say "so what?" → proof too generic, upgrade tier or add specificity

## Anti-Patterns

| # | Pattern | Problem | Detection |
|---|---------|---------|-----------|
| AN-1 | Naked Claims | "We deliver results" — no adjacent proof | Highlight every "take my word for it" sentence; each needs proof within 2 sentences |
| AN-2 | Proof at Bottom | Testimonials section below the fold | If proof concentrated in bottom 20% → redistribute via braiding |
| AN-3 | Stats Without Context | "97% satisfaction" — compared to what? | Any stat without "compared to [X]" or "which means [Y]" → add anchor |
| AN-4 | Generic Testimonials | "Great service!" — interchangeable with any business | Must have: (a) specific situation before, (b) specific action, (c) specific measurable result |
| AN-5 | Price Before Proof | Price shown before value established | Count proof elements before price. Fewer than 5 diverse elements → too early |
| AN-6 | "Trust Me" Phrasing | Explicitly asking for trust = proof insufficient | Search trust/believe/promise vocabulary; add proof that makes the word unnecessary |
| AN-7 | Proof/Claim Mismatch | T1 proof supporting T4 claim | If boldness > proof tier → upgrade proof or downgrade claim |

## Authority Manufacturing (Zero-Proof Scenarios)

1. Mine personal results → T1 (you already have it)
2. Run live experiment, document with screenshots → T2 in 48 hours
3. Borrow authority — cite industry data, studies, expert statements → T4 borrowed
4. Offer free/discounted work for detailed testimonials → T3 in 2-4 weeks
5. Build in public — document process transparently → T2 in real time

**Rule**: Never launch copy with zero proof. Even 24-hour sprint generates T1-2 proof.

## Signature Moves

1. **Pre-Emptive Doubt Map** — Map every objection, skepticism, "so what?" question before writing → Deploy at project start or audit
2. **Siamese Twin Weave** — For every claim, insert proof within same sentence or next → Deploy on any benefit/outcome/USP sentence
3. **Tiered Proof Ascension** — Design experiments to generate next-tier proof systematically → Deploy when launching new offer or stuck at T1
4. **Authority Jargon Burst** — After accessible language, deploy 1-2 sentences of dense unexplained technical jargon → Deploy when introducing mechanism/differentiator
5. **Disarming Admission** — Highlight genuine minor flaw early, follow with overwhelming proof → Deploy before price or major benefit to pre-empt objections

## Quality Rubric

| Criterion | 4 (Acceptable) | 7 (Good) | 10 (Savant) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Proof-Claim Adjacency | Most claims have proof, some naked claims | All significant claims supported within 2 sentences | Every claim immediately supported, zero naked claims |
| Tier-Claim Match | Low-tier proof on moderate claims | Generally matched, minor mismatches | Consistently meets/exceeds. Bold claims are "proof-wrapped" |
| Density & Variety | 1-2 proof types, inconsistent density | 3-4 types, adequate density | 5+ types across tiers, density scales with claim boldness |
| Contextualized Authority | Stats without context | Partially contextualized | Fully contextualized, zero "so what?" responses |
| Strategic Flaw Integration | No flaws mentioned | Minor flaw, weak disarming effect | Genuine flaw introduced early + overwhelming proof follows |
| Jargon Flurry | Uniform language, no strategic shifts | Attempted but poorly placed/timed | Precise burst after accessible language, establishes expertise |
| Proof Placement | Back-loaded or clustered | Generally braided, some stacking before CTAs | Masterfully braided throughout, strategic stacks at doubt nodes |
