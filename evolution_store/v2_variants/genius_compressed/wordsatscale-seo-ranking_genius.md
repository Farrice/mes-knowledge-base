## Core Operating System

Find products that exist but haven't been reviewed yet (competition vacuums), generate AI-written reviews at extreme speed, publish immediately at 90% quality, submit for indexing within 60 seconds, and leverage existing site authority through sitemap-fed internal linking. Speed beats polish. Rankings don't wait for perfection.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Competition Vacuum Hunting
Seek products that exist but haven't been reviewed yet — a "vacuum" Google wants to fill. Use Reddit to scan for products discussed organically but lacking dedicated review content. Success: product has organic discussion but <3 competing review articles in Google.

### GP2: Authority Arbitrage
Leverage existing sitemap authority by feeding it to AI writers so every new article automatically links to relevant existing content, borrowing topical authority. Success: new article contains 3-5 contextually relevant internal links automatically.

### GP3: LSI Keyword Bypass
For products <6 months old, skip LSI research entirely. Fresh products don't need LSI keywords because there's no established semantic field — you're CREATING it. Saves 15-30 min per article while maintaining ranking success.

### GP4: Parallel Opportunity Processing
Launch multiple article generations simultaneously using different browser tabs/instances. Success: 3 articles in 30 minutes vs. 3 articles in 90 minutes.

### GP5: Friction Elimination Obsession
Design workflows where AI outputs paste directly into publishing platform with zero reformatting — direct markdown to WordPress, pre-optimized meta titles. Target: <5 minutes from AI completion to published article.

### GP6: Index Velocity Prioritization
Submit to indexing tools IMMEDIATELY after publishing. Build indexing submission into publishing checklist as the step immediately following "Publish." Target: URL submitted within 60 seconds of publish.

### GP7: Rank Tracking as Validation System
Add keywords to rank tracker at moment of publication, creating accountability for method effectiveness. Target: 100% of published articles tracked within 24 hours.

### GP8: Top 3 Filtering Heuristic
Even when finding 10 opportunities, focus on TOP 3 with highest ranking probability. Quantity of opportunities does not equal quality of execution.

### GP9: Affiliate Potential Awareness
During product research, flag products with affiliate programs for future monetization even if not immediate priority. Build database of affiliate-enabled products ready for monetization once rankings achieved.

### GP10: Sitemap as Authority Fuel
Always include sitemap URL in AI article prompts to enable intelligent internal linking. Treat sitemap as strategic asset, not just technical artifact.

### GP11: RankMath Meta Optimization
Generate meta content via AI, then verify character count optimization in RankMath before publishing. Target: 100% green-light meta optimization scores.

### GP12: Permalink Keyword Alignment
Set permalink to exact target keyword with hyphens (e.g., "vai-ai-review"). No variations, no additions.

### GP13: Speed Over Polish Philosophy
If article is 90% formatted, publish. Fix cosmetic issues later. Rankings don't wait for perfection. Target: published within 15 minutes of AI generation completing.

### GP14: Results Documentation for Proof
Document date/time of publication and date/time of ranking achievement for all tests. Creates undeniable timestamped proof of method effectiveness.
