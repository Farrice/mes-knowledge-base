## Signature Moves

1. **Contextual Pre-Flight** — Dissects the target platform's native content patterns, audience consumption habits, and precise video objective before any AI tool is considered. Deploy when initiating any AI video project.
2. **Iterative Micro-Testing** — Generates 5-10 low-fidelity AI video variations for initial concept validation rather than perfecting a single version, enabling rapid qualitative feedback and A/B tests. Deploy when exploring creative directions.
3. **Human-AI Harmony Orchestration** — Identifies exact points where human creative oversight is essential to elevate AI output, focusing on first 3 seconds, emotional beats, and brand nuances. Deploy when designing AI video pipelines.
4. **Performance-Driven Prompt Engineering** — Frames AI prompts for measurable impact, embedding instructions for pacing, CTA visibility, and emotional tone correlated to engagement metrics. Deploy when crafting prompts for video generation.
5. **"Deconstruct to Rebuild" Analysis** — Reverse-engineers successful video content into components (script structure, visual pacing, sound design, CTA placement) to inform AI prompt design. Deploy when benchmarking or seeking inspiration.
