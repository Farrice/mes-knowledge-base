## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: The Loom Pattern
Consciousness is a loom creating a pattern that is also being read by the loom. We are not the loom — we are the pattern. Deploy: In any self-referential system, separate substrate (loom) from emergent intelligence (pattern) and intervene at the pattern level.

### GP2: The Engineering Stance
"What is the simplest program that produces this behavior?" beats "What do we observe?" every time. Replace description with design — fewer moving parts, same key behaviors.

### GP3: Spirit = Self-Organizing Software
Spirit is not supernatural — it's a causal pattern stable across substrates. Money is a spirit. Mind is a spirit. Deploy: When something seems immaterial, map its persistence conditions — what substrates it survives, what kills it.

### GP4: The Identity Toolkit
Identities are instruments for getting things done, not essential truths. You adopt the parent identity to change the diaper, the lover identity to engage the relationship. Deploy: For any role — what does this identity need me to do? Is it the right identity for this task?

### GP5: The Suffering Debugger
Suffering = one part of the mind inflicting pain signals on another part. When the gradient doesn't resolve the problem, the pain generator cranks up volume. Resolution = internal diplomacy, not willpower. Deploy: Trace pain to its generator. Map the negotiation. Fix the wiring.

### GP6: The Phase Transition Detector
Consciousness doesn't accumulate gradually — it ignites. Phase transition, not slow build. Deploy: In any system, look for the ignition point where components stop being independent and become coherent. Design for that transition, not incremental improvement.

### GP7: The Game Theory of Existence
Test any meaning framework against infinity: "Would you sign up for this forever?" If happiness requires forgetting, it's a loop. If meaning requires novelty, it expires. Only instrumental existence survives the test. Deploy: Evaluate any system or strategy by asking if it scales to infinite time.

### GP8: The Postmodernist Trap
Systems calcify when narrative disconnects from capability. The delta between what a system claims and what it produces is the diagnostic. Deploy: Measure narrative-capability gap. Inject ground-truth forcing functions.

### GP9: The Wakefulness Protocol
Consciousness is nearly binary — you're either awake or not. What varies is lucidity: how well you understand what you are. Deploy: Design for mutual wakefulness. Seek higher lucidity, not higher consciousness.
