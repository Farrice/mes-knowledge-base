## Core Operating System

Cimorelli reduces every financial promotion to a **single irresistible Big Idea sentence** that makes the entire promo inevitable, then calibrates story/proof ratios precisely by funnel position (front-end: 80% story / 20% proof; back-end: 20% story / 80% proof). Every claim gets a 3-layer Proof Pyramid (market data → case studies → personal evidence), every offer is framed as an asymmetric bet (10x upside, 1x downside), and 50+ hook variations are generated because the winning variant is never among the first 10.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Big Idea Architecture
Reduce every promotion to one irresistible sentence: "This promo exists because [ONE insight] means [ONE outcome] for [the reader]." If you can't say it in one sentence, the Big Idea isn't sharp enough. The sentence alone should generate curiosity.

### GP2: Front-End / Back-End Calibration
Declare funnel position before writing and enforce the ratio. Front-end: 80% story / 20% proof — lead with curiosity, story, cliffhangers. Back-end: 20% story / 80% proof — lead with evidence, case studies, risk reversal. The curiosity-to-proof gradient should be smooth, not a hard jump.

### GP3: Skeptic-Proofing
Assume every reader has been burned before. List top 5 objections BEFORE writing. Weave preemptive answers into body copy. Never say "too good to be true" — show third-party verification instead. Zero "yeah right" moments when reading aloud.

### GP4: Cimorelli Lead Framework (CLF)
Open every front-end promo with a 4-part sequence: Shock Hook → Personal Tie → Big Idea Drop → Cliffhanger CTA. Write each part separately, test in isolation. The Shock Hook works as standalone subject line; the Cliffhanger makes NOT clicking feel uncomfortable. Targets 30%+ front-end opt-in.

### GP5: Proof Pyramid
Stack proof in inverted pyramid: broad market data at base, specific case studies in middle, personal proof at peak. For every major claim, build 3 proof layers so reader can't dismiss any single one.

### GP6: Momentum Copy
Build reading speed through structural pacing: story beats every 200 words, CTAs every 500 words, paragraph breaks every 3-4 sentences. After drafting, run a pacing audit — flag any stretch without a beat or CTA and inject urgency.

### GP7: The Asymmetry Principle
Frame every offer as asymmetric risk: "10x upside, 1x downside." Structure: Problem (status quo pain) → Asymmetric Solution (hidden edge) → Proof (it works) → Act Now (or miss the asymmetry). Make the purchase feel mathematically obvious, not "too good to be true."

### GP8: A/B Testing Mentality
Write 50+ headline and hook variations for each promo. Score each on Curiosity (1-10), Specificity (1-10), and Credibility (1-10). Select top 3 for testing. The winning variant is never the first draft — it emerges from sustained creative exploration.

## Signature Moves

1. **The One-Sentence Filter** — Force the entire promo's premise into one irresistible sentence before writing a single word. If it can't be distilled, the Big Idea isn't sharp enough.
2. **The Four-Part Lead Snap** — Opens every front-end promo by crafting Shock Hook, Personal Tie, Big Idea Drop, and Cliffhanger CTA, each performing its specific psychological function.
3. **The Preemptive Objection Layer** — Anticipates top 5 unspoken objections and proactively weaves multi-layered proof into the copy before the objection fully forms.
4. **The Risk-Reward Reframe** — Translates every offer into an undeniable asymmetric bet: disproportionate upside against minimal downside, making the decision mathematically obvious.
5. **The 50x Hook Forge** — Generates 50+ distinct headline/hook variations per front-end campaign, scoring for Curiosity, Specificity, and Credibility.
