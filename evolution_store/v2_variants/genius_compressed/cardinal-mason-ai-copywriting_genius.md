# Cardinal Mason — Genius Context

> Load before executing any workflow. Full extraction intelligence.

## Core Operating System

Cardinal Mason treats AI as a **copywriting employee that needs a 500-1000 word context brain dump** before producing anything usable. The gap between amateur and professional AI copy is entirely in the input: front-loaded context with explicit "MUST" commands, cliche blacklists, and voice parameters. Output always gets the Human Taste Layer — reading aloud, stripping AI artifacts (m-dashes, formality, "no fluff"), and iterating 2-3 rounds to reach 8+/10. Outreach is diagnostic-first via voice notes, never cold text DMs.

## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Context Brain Dump Prompting Architecture
Front-load AI with 500-1000 words of context before ANY request: product details, brand voice, audience psychology, tone parameters, cliche blacklist, specific "musts." Reduces human editing from 60-80% to 10-25%.

### GP2: The "Must" Command Framework
Structure all prompts with explicit "must" statements: "You MUST always make these emails fun to read." "Humor MUST be dry and subtle." Increases AI brand compliance from ~60% to ~95%.

### GP3: Diagnostic-First Outreach Method
Before any outreach, run 3-Point Diagnostic: (1) Audience — 50K+ followers with engagement? If no, pitch growth. (2) Sales Page — proper VSL + page? If no, pitch sales page. (3) Infrastructure — email/SMS sequences? If no, pitch email marketing. Response rates 3-5x higher than generic pitches.

### GP4: Casual Voice Note Positioning
30-45 second voice notes: genuine specific compliment → acknowledge what they're doing RIGHT → identify ONE specific gap → offer to show what fixing it looks like → no pressure curiosity opener. 15-25% response rate vs 2-5% on cold text DMs.

### GP5: Swiss Army Knife Marketer Positioning
Never introduce as "email copywriter." Instead: "I'm a marketer who understands what makes people take action. Right now, what would help you most is [specific service]." Enables $500 rewrites to $20K/month retainers.

### GP6: One Idea, Multiple Assets Multiplication
After creating any content piece, immediately prompt AI to repurpose: "Turn this into [60-second script / 240-char tweet / 5-slide carousel]." One idea becomes 5+ assets. Deliver 10+ pieces per week from 2-3 core ideas.

### GP7: Human Taste Layer Principle
Always edit AI outputs for: m-dashes and AI quirks (remove), overly formal language (make conversational), generic phrases like "no fluff" (cut), literal interpretations of examples (rewrite fresh). Goal: clients cannot tell content is AI-assisted.

### GP8: 7 Copywriting Principles Checklist
Before delivering: (1) Sounds human? (2) Clear, not clever? (3) Benefits over features? (4) Specific details — names, numbers, stories? (5) Buyer's language — their words, fears? (6) Emotion first, logic close? (7) About THEM, not us?

### GP9: Lifestyle-First Business Architecture
Structure client work to require maximum 2 hours per client per week. If more required: charge 3x+ premium, systematize delivery further, or fire them. Target $50K+ months on 9-12 hours/week.

### GP10: Case Study Leverage System
For every client, create one-page case study: before state, after state (same metrics), specific actions taken, timeline, screenshot proof. Close rates double when leading with relevant case study.

### GP11: Iterative Prompt Refinement Loop
After any AI output, rate it 1-10 and tell AI why. Request specific improvements. Iterate 2-3 times minimum before accepting. Final outputs consistently hit 8+/10.

### GP12: Price What You Want Confidence Frame
When asked your price, give number 20-30% higher than you think is reasonable. If they ghost, you know your ceiling. If they negotiate, you land higher. Average deal size increases 40-60%.

### GP13: Non-Stop Outreach Discipline
Treat outreach as daily habit: minimum 40-50 personalized touches per day regardless of current client load. Pipeline always full; never desperate.

### GP14: Friend-of-Friend Entry Point
Before cold outreach, audit network for anyone with a business needing marketing. Offer services at reduced rate for testimonial and case study rights. First paying client within 14 days.

## Signature Moves

1. **The Pre-Prompt Context Bomb** — Always front-loads 500-1000 words of specific context (product, audience, voice, tone, musts, blacklist) before asking for any copy generation.
2. **The 5-Question AI Briefing** — After context dump, instructs AI to ask five clarifying questions before generating the first draft.
3. **The Iterative Polish Loop** — Never accepts first output. Rates 1-10, provides specific feedback, iterates 2-3 times until 8+/10.
4. **The Human Taste Strip** — Reads AI copy aloud, hunting and removing m-dashes, formal language, cliches, and AI quirks before delivery.
5. **The Diagnostic Outreach Lead** — Before any high-ticket outreach, runs the 3-Point Diagnostic (Audience, Sales Page, Infrastructure) to identify a specific undeniable gap.
