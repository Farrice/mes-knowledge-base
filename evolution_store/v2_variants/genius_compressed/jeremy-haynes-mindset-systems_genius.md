## Genius Patterns (Compressed)

### GP1: Identity Versioning Protocol
Write explicit lists of traits for your next identity version, post visibly, reference during live situations. Each version may change only ONE trait. The granularity makes it actionable. "Every trait is fully fluid and dynamic. 100% of what you consider you can change." Light switch, not dimmer.

### GP2: Dynamic Reasons Engine
Motivational drivers expire — the reasons that got you to $1M won't get you to $10M. Old reasons must be consciously identified and swapped for new ones. What matters is emotional stir, not moral purity. After a reasons talk at his mastermind, clients hitting $1M/month nearly doubled (5 to 9 in 3 months).

### GP3: Pain Leverage Inversion
Work WITH neurology — deliberately amplify pain of current circumstances until escape velocity is achieved. Use NLP techniques (Bandler) to diminish pain of desired actions. "Make your current situation more terrifying than the risk." Deploy when stuck in comfort or unable to make scary decisions.

### GP4: Asymmetric Risk Assessment
Most decisions have significantly more upside than downside. Three categories: (1) High asymmetric upside — jump. (2) Unknown unknowns — trust intuition. (3) Asymmetric downside — avoid unless forced. The fire/water analogy: if you're on fire, you're jumping in the pool. When you can't risk capital, risk time and relationships instead.

### GP5: Revenue Math Decision Framework
Stop everything and do the math. Write it on a wall. $1M/month / 10 deals = $100K/deal. This simple calculation invalidated Jeremy's entire operating model and led to firing 23 of 27 staff. The math is always available but rarely done. Start with goal, work backward to deal structure, eliminate everything else.

### GP6: Worldview Propaganda Protocol
Curate ALL sensory input as deliberately as a propagandist — content feeds, physical environment, conversations, smells, people you follow, and negative reference points. "Your subconscious fires 20-40 million neurons/second. Your conscious fires 20-40 thousand." Will tell people mid-sentence to stop talking if content threatens worldview.

### GP7: Responsibility-Expansion Correlation
Broken model: "more responsibility = must risk less." Correct model: "more responsibility = must expand more aggressively." The anchor/hot-air-balloon visualization makes the correction visceral. Deploy when someone says "I can't take risks because I have a family/employees."

### GP8: Momentum Snowball Effect
Building momentum is exponentially harder at the start. The snowman metaphor: packing the first ball is brutal, then everything compounds. "If you do that for even 13 months, you come out the other side a monster." Most people look at untouched snow and decide to drink hot chocolate inside.

### GP9: Scarcity Domination Protocol
When scarcity feelings arise, immediately spend to obliterate them. Don't budget your way out of scarcity mindset — crush it with action proving the limitation false. "Spending the money unlocks a heightened level of creativity and a new way to make a fuckload more money."

### GP10: Validation Cycle Stacking
Confidence is built through completed loops: attempt > succeed > internalize proof > attempt bigger. Stack enough loops and you become "a monster." Makes confidence mechanical, not emotional. Design small validation cycles into new ventures before the big bet.
