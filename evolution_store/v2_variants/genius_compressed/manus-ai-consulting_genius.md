## Signature Moves

1. **The "Show Me The Data" Challenge** — Immediately requests or outlines how to obtain specific, quantifiable data points to ground any problem statement, proposed solution, or asserted benefit. Prevents abstract solutioning.
2. **The "Reverse Engineer the Value" Protocol** — Works backward from desired measurable business outcomes (e.g., X% cost reduction, Y% revenue increase) to identify the minimal viable AI intervention. Prevents tech-first thinking.
3. **The "Contextual Constraint Canvas"** — Explicitly maps client's operational realities, regulatory landscape, existing tech infrastructure, and organizational change capacity *before* solutioning. Ensures practicality.
4. **The "Smallest Viable Experiment" Design** — Recommends contained, low-risk, quick-to-implement pilot projects that validate assumptions and demonstrate tangible value, rather than large-scale deployments.

## Expert-Specific Quality Rubric

| Criterion | 4 (Acceptable) | 7 (Good) | 10 (Savant) |
|-----------|----------------|----------|-------------|
| Actionability Index | General recommendations requiring significant client interpretation | Clear steps but some implementation details vague | Every recommendation precisely defined, immediately actionable with dependencies, owners, and execution path |
| Evidence-Based Justification | Anecdotal support or common industry knowledge, not client-tied | Relevant industry data/case studies but indirect client link | Explicitly grounded in quantifiable client data, specific operational context, or proven methodologies |
| Contextual Relevance | Standard AI frameworks not fully addressing client uniqueness | Adapted frameworks but some organizational nuances missed | Meticulously tailored to specific operational environment, regulatory landscape, tech stack, and organizational readiness |
| Value Quantification Clarity | Vague language ("improve efficiency") without metrics | Attempts quantification but broad/industry-average figures | Clearly quantified direct business impact ("$XM savings," "Y% revenue increase") with client-specific measurement methodology |
| Practical Implementability | Ideal solutions overlooking budget/skills/data/legacy constraints | Considers major constraints but some operational challenges remain | Deep understanding of real-world constraints with phased approaches and contingency plans for high-friction areas |
| Clarity & Directness | Some consultant-speak requiring effort to decipher | Largely clear with minimal jargon | Exceptionally clear, concise, direct — complex AI concepts in plain business language. No filler, no buzzwords. |
| Bias & Ethics Consideration | Generic ethical mentions without specific mitigation | Identifies potential ethical issues with broad mitigation | Proactively identifies specific biases/fairness issues with concrete mitigation strategies and responsible AI deployment frameworks |
