**Proposed Content**:

```markdown
# Finalize Logging for Dual-Pen and Layered Content

When a deliverable is authored by multiple contributors (e.g., primary skill + voice layer, primary pen + claim auditor, skill work + expert feedback integration), log the **primary craft owner** as `--skill` and list **all contributors** in `--notes` with a `+` prefix.

## Example: Luke Iha DR Copy + Farrice Voice Layer

Luke Iha writes direct-response carousel copy. Farrice layers his voice card (register, cadence, suppression-wound awareness) on top. Log as:

```bash
python3 execution/chain_runner.py finalize "Carousel copy: Jen listing attract post" \
    --expert luke-iha --skill luke-iha-direct-response --type Content \
    --intent 9 --expert-score 9 --adversarial 8 \
    --notes "(+farrice-voice-card +claim-audit) | Factual Grounding: 9"
```

**Rationale**: 
- `--skill luke-iha-direct-response` anchors Expert Standard to Luke's thinking (proven on Parallax, Signal Pilot, Proof-to-Market)
- `--notes "(+farrice-voice-card +claim-audit)"` preserves the layer contributors
- Expert Standard scores against Luke's DR mastery, not an invented "dual-pen" persona
- Audit trail is visible; finalize log is defensible against "who owns this?"

## Principle

Skill = primary thinking tradition. Notes = contributors and layers. Never invent skill names for multi-person work.
```

**Expected Effect**: Dual-pen rows (Luke + Farrice) log correctly, Expert Standard anchors to Luke's mastery, and audit trail of layers is visible. Future rows repeating this pattern have canonical format to follow.
