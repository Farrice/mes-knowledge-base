- [ ] {{FOUNDER_NAME}} moves to the booth at the announced time (or when the room feels ready — {{FOUNDER_NAME}}'s call)

## Critical Failure Modes (And Real-Time Triage)

### Failure 1: A Hunter or Drunk Group slipped through curation
**Real-time response**:
- Don't escalate publicly. Pull aside the curator with the cleanest read.
- Give them one warm, firm message: "This isn't quite the right room. We'd rather refund you than have you uncomfortable here."
- Walk them out. Refund processed within 24h.
- Per `03-marketing/05-crisis-comms.md` Section "Hunter Slipped Through" for full protocol.

### Failure 2: DJ cancels last-minute
**Triage**:
- {{FOUNDER_NAME}} takes the full set if needed (she's prepared a 3-hour solo set as backup — non-negotiable prep item).
- Or: Farrice taps the DJ Homies network for a same-day fill-in.
- Communication to attendees: only if the lineup was a draw (e.g., JR was named). If it wasn't named publicly, no announcement needed.

### Failure 3: Venue issue (lockout, AC failure, sound issue)
**Triage**:
- Backup venue (if any) was identified at T-14.
- Refund + reschedule communication template ready (drafted at T-7 as contingency).
- Keep attendees informed within 30 min of any issue — silence breeds rumors.

### Failure 4: Attendance falls below 30
**This is fine. Do not pad.**
- Quality at 25 beats fill at 50.
- The room's energy is a function of curation, not headcount.
- Use the night to over-document — 25 well-curated attendees produce more proof than 50 mixed ones.

### Failure 5: Energy lulls and the floor empties
**Read the room**:
- Drop the "if it's lulling" backup track ({{FOUNDER_NAME}} has this in queue every event)
- Curator team brings water, smiles, moves bodies on the floor (not commanding, just being present)
- Sometimes a 4-minute reset (slow track, room takes a breath) is the best move
- Don't panic-build to a peak. The room finds its rhythm if you trust the curation.
