# Brief — Flyer & Poster (Digital + Physical)

*Inherits from `00-master-creative-brief-template.md`. Open every flyer session by pasting Section 1 + Section 4. Fill all 9 sections before producing.*

---

## Section 1 — The Spine Reminder (paste-in)

> *{{BRAND_NAME}} is heart encounters, not head encounters — a daytime, sober dance party in {{CITY}} for people who want to meet a partner. The mechanic is body-first: the music does the emotional labor so the people don't have to. The metric is {{SUCCESS_METRIC}}.*

---

## Section 2 — What This Brief Is For (purpose)

This brief produces flyers and posters in two formats: digital (1080×1350 IG portrait crop) and physical (8.5×11 letter portrait). Its job is to communicate the existence and shape of an upcoming {{BRAND_NAME}} event with one image, one headline, and the smallest amount of supporting type a reader needs to either say *that's me, I'm applying* or *that's not me, I'll keep scrolling.* The flyer sits at the very top of the funnel — it's the first object someone sees, often without context, often glanced at for 0.8 seconds. The one thing it must do: read as a real photograph of a real moment with a single sentence over it, not as graphic-design event-promo. If a reader can't tell what kind of room this is from the image alone, the flyer failed.

## Section 3 — ICP Target

The flyer targets the umbrella ICP — the 30-40 year old {{CITY}} single who is out-loud asking why it's so hard to meet a good person — but the photograph and the headline together do the profile-specific work.

- **For {{ICP_PROFILE_1_NAME}} (Profile #1, arts worker)**: the photograph reads as *editorial* — daylight on a wood floor, body-mid-gesture, the kind of frame she'd save to her own moodboard. The headline reads as a sentence she'd say to a friend.
- **For {{ICP_PROFILE_2_NAME}} (Profile #2, helping professional)**: the photograph signals *not-wellness, not-recovery* — {{CITY}} architecture in frame, mixed-race adults, no candles, no linen. The headline signals *you're not here to do emotional labor.*
- **For {{ICP_PROFILE_3_NAME}} (Profile #3, the quiet man)**: the photograph signals *safe to be soft* — Black men visibly relaxed in the frame, no posed group shots, no "look at the camera and smile." The headline doesn't address him directly; it describes the room.

**Bridge Message (umbrella, used as headline territory)**: *A daytime {{CITY}} dance room — sober, curated, where the music does the emotional labor so you don't have to.*

The Audience State the flyer most needs to reach is **contemplation** — the people who've named the problem, haven't named the solution. The flyer is rarely the first touch (that's IG content + word-of-mouth), but it's the *crystallizing* touch — the object that turns *I keep hearing about this* into *I'm applying.*

## Section 4 — Voice Rules (compressed)

> Warm, direct, confident, never preachy, never corporate. Show > tell at sentence level. Heart-encounter language as headline, body-first only as explanation. Em-dashes ≤2 per piece (often zero on a flyer — every dash is a sentence the design isn't doing). No "Here's what/why/how" openers. Name the enemy by name only when the headline can carry it. Never preach sobriety. Voice test: would {{FOUNDER_NAME}} staple this to a corkboard at a Pilsen coffee shop and feel proud of it?

**Patterns this asset leans on most**:
1. **Mechanic-as-headline** — the headline says one mechanic in eight words or fewer.
2. **Crystallized-phrase** — the body type carries 2–4 short period-stacked phrases (Pattern 3 in voice doc).
3. **Specific-location-over-generic** — *"{{CITY}}. June 14, 2026."* not *"Coming soon to a city near you."*
4. **Application-not-ticket** — the CTA points to the why-gate, not to a checkout cart.

## Section 5 — Format Spec

### Digital Flyer (IG Portrait Crop)

- **Dimensions**: 1080×1350 px (4:5 portrait — IG feed-optimized).
- **File format**: JPG (sRGB) for IG; PNG only when a transparent corner is needed for share-card composition.
- **Photo coverage**: 65–75% of the design surface.
- **Headline**: ≤8 words, GT Sectra 64–72pt at 1080px wide canvas (scales down to legible at 540px IG preview).
- **Body type (supporting line)**: ≤30 words total. Inter 18–24pt. Single block.
- **Date / location stamp**: Inter label-caps 14pt — *JUNE 14, 2026 · CHICAGO* — in Slate-500 or Cream-100 depending on photo contrast.
- **CTA**: A single line at the bottom — *Application opens [date] · {{BRAND_NAME_LOWER}}.[domain]/apply*. No button. The text link IS the CTA. Inter 14pt, Terracotta-600.
- **Wordmark**: {{BRAND_NAME}} text wordmark, GT Sectra 24pt, Cream-100 or Midnight-900 against the photo. Top-left or bottom-left corner. Never centered.

### Physical Flyer (Letter Portrait, 8.5×11)

- **Dimensions**: 8.5×11 in, portrait, 0.25" bleed for print, 0.5" safe area.
- **Resolution**: 300 dpi.
- **Color profile**: CMYK for print. The cream-100 background converts to a warm uncoated-paper white; the terracotta-600 prints true on uncoated stock (it loses saturation on glossy — confirm print stock at proof stage).
- **Paper stock**: 100lb uncoated text or 80lb cover. Never glossy. Glossy reads as nightclub-flyer.
- **Photo coverage**: 60–70% of the design surface (slightly less than digital — physical needs more breathing room).
- **Headline**: same ≤8 words; GT Sectra 72–96pt at 8.5×11 canvas.
- **Body, date, CTA, wordmark**: same constraints as digital, scaled for letter size.

### Anti-patterns (kill on sight)

- **NO QR code as a focal element**. A QR may appear in the bottom corner at <0.5" — it is never the visual center.
- **NO hashtags** anywhere on the flyer. Hashtags are an IG-caption mechanic, not a flyer mechanic.
- **NO sponsor logos**. {{BRAND_NAME}} does not run sponsor logos on flyers, ever (per non-negotiable §11 — no sponsor stage).
- **NO drop shadows** behind type. Tonal layering only.
- **NO AI-generated portraits** in the photograph. Per `01-visual/photography-rules.md` §3.
- **NO stock dancers**. Per same.
- **NO club lighting in the photograph**. Per same.
- **NO neon colors, no gradient backgrounds, no "save the date" badge graphics**.
- **NO multiple typefaces beyond the system** (GT Sectra + Inter; Caveat allowed once if {{FOUNDER_NAME}}'s signature is included).

## Section 6 — Hook & Structure Patterns

### Pattern 1: Single-Image Dominance

The photograph carries 65–75% of the surface. The type is a caption to the image, never a headline competing with it. The frame the image holds is what the brand is selling.

> **GOOD:** A daytime photograph of two adults mid-laugh on a wood floor, {{CITY}} architecture in the background, sun on the floor, occupies 70% of the design. Below: GT Sectra headline + date stamp + small text link.

> **BAD:** A photograph used as a "background image" with text overlaid across the entire surface in a black-translucent box. (The photo is decoration, not the protagonist.)

### Pattern 2: Mechanic-as-Headline (eight words or fewer)

The headline is one mechanic, in plain English, that filters the unsure away.

> **GOOD:** *"A daytime dance room. Sober. {{CITY}}."* (Five words. Three filters. Filtered audience reads on.)

> **GOOD:** *"You don't have to drink to dance."* (Seven words. The filter is the headline.)

> **GOOD:** *"{{CITY}}. 2pm. The music does the work."* (Seven words. Mechanic + atmosphere.)

> **BAD:** *"Join {{CITY}}'s premier curated singles dance experience for an unforgettable afternoon of authentic connection!"* (Sixteen words. Three adjectives. Zero filtering.)

> **BAD:** *"A new way to meet someone."* (Six words but says nothing — could be any product.)

### Pattern 3: Specific-Location-Over-Generic

The flyer always names the city and the specific date. *{{CITY}}* is mandatory; the neighborhood may appear in the body line.

> **GOOD:** *"{{CITY}}. June 14, 2026. Doors at 2pm."*

> **BAD:** *"Coming summer 2026 — stay tuned!"* (Vague. Reads as not-yet-real.)

### Pattern 4: Application-Not-Ticket (CTA discipline)

The CTA points to the why-gate application, not to a Ticketmaster checkout. *"Application opens June 1"* — never *"Get tickets now."*

> **GOOD:** *"Application opens June 1. {{BRAND_NAME_LOWER}}.[domain]/apply"*

> **BAD:** *"Buy tickets — only 50 spots, going fast!"* (Manufactures urgency. Treats the room as a commodity.)

## Section 7 — Visual Spec

The flyer obeys `01-visual/DESIGN.md` and `01-visual/photography-rules.md` in full. Specifics for this asset:

- **Photograph**: a daytime room frame from a real event (post-Event-#1) or a daytime founder-portrait of {{FOUNDER_NAME}} at the decks (pre-Event-#1, single use). Full bleed of the dominant area. The 11pm test applies — kill any image that could read as taken at 11pm.
- **Color palette**: Cream-100 (#F5EFE3) for background where the photo doesn't cover. Midnight-900 (#0F1A2E) for type. Terracotta-600 (#B8492E) for the headline accent (single use) and the URL link. Slate-500 (#7A6F62) for the date stamp and metadata.
- **Typography**: GT Sectra 64–72pt (digital) / 72–96pt (print) for the headline. Inter 18–24pt for the body line. Inter label-caps 14pt for date stamp. Caveat 28pt for {{FOUNDER_NAME}}'s signature (single use, only if used).
- **Layout grid**: photograph occupies the upper 65–75%; type occupies the lower 25–35%. Wordmark in upper-left or lower-left corner. Date stamp in label-caps directly beneath the photograph. Headline below date stamp. URL link beneath headline. White space between elements: at least `lg` (24px) at digital scale; at least 0.5" at print scale.
- **Tonal layering**: depth via cream-50 and cream-100 steps, never via shadow. The photograph is the only "depth" in the design.
- **Anti-patterns**: see Section 5.

## Section 9 — Self-Check Questions

1. **Spine fidelity**: Does the photograph honor daytime? The 11pm test passes?
2. **Profile fit**: Does the photograph signal *not-wellness, not-recovery, not-club, not-wedding*? Is mixed-race / mixed-body / mixed-gender visible in the frame?
3. **Headline discipline**: ≤8 words? Names a mechanic, not a benefit?
4. **Specificity**: Is the city + date explicit and named?
5. **CTA discipline**: Points to application (why-gate), not to ticket-sale checkout?
6. **AI tells**: Em-dashes ≤2 (often zero on a flyer)? No marketing-cliché phrases ("limited spots, going fast")?
7. **Banned phrases**: No community/intentional/conscious/sacred/high-vibe/curated-three-times?
8. **Anti-patterns**: No QR-as-focal, no hashtags, no sponsor logos, no drop shadows, no stock dancers, no neon?
9. **Coffee-shop test**: Would {{FOUNDER_NAME}} pin this to a corkboard at a Pilsen coffee shop and feel proud of it?
