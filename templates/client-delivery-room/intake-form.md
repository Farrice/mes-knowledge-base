# The Angle Map + Message-Market-Fit Intelligence — Client Intake

**Time:** 10–15 minutes

**Next step:** a 45–60 minute kickoff conversation
**Scope:** one live occasion, one buyer, one message decision, one final
decision-maker, and one deadline

## Before you begin

Please do not send unreleased, regulated, customer-identifying, or otherwise
confidential material until you have received and approved the project’s exact
transfer, storage, access, retention, deletion, and AI-handling terms.

## 1. The decision

1. What is the live occasion: launch, retail expansion, reformulation,
   repositioning, or another specific moment?
2. What exact message decision must be made before more creative or campaign
   work is funded?
3. When must that decision be made?
4. Who owns the decision, and who gives final approval?

## 2. The offer and buyer

5. What product or offer are we working on? Include the current URL when public.
6. Who is the buyer for this occasion? Describe the situation they are in, not
   only their age or demographic.
7. What do you currently believe that buyer must understand or believe before
   choosing you?
8. What are they likely to do instead of choosing you?

## 3. The current message

9. Paste the current headline, campaign message, product-page language, or
   internal direction we should evaluate.
10. Where does the team currently disagree or feel uncertain?
11. What has already been tried? What happened? `UNKNOWN` is an acceptable
    answer when no usable result was recorded.

## 4. Evidence and boundaries

12. Which studies, substantiation, product facts, reviews, interviews, sales
    calls, support records, campaign results, or customer language may be used?
13. For each item, tell us whether it is public, client-approved for this
    project, internal-only, or not yet cleared.
14. What claims, topics, comparisons, competitors, or words require legal,
    compliance, founder, or brand review?
15. What must never appear in the client room or future examples?

## 5. Brand and delivery

16. Who will read the final room, and what do they need to decide after reading?
17. Upload the current logo, brand guide, approved photography, and one or two
    examples that feel visually right. Farrice Cain Premium Minimal remains the
    default delivery frame unless a different mode is agreed in scope.
18. What would make the final room feel immediately useful rather than merely
    impressive?

## 6. Handling permission

19. Is this a non-confidential demonstration, human-only project, or an
    AI-assisted project with separate written permission?
20. Confirm the approved transfer channel, authorized people/services, working
    location, NDA state, retention period, deletion request path, and reuse
    state supplied in the Ready-to-Start Receipt.

## Kickoff depth prompts

These are asked live after reviewing the form:

- Walk me through the moment this message decision became urgent.
- What is the polished explanation—and what is the version the team says when
  the meeting ends?
- What would make a plausible campaign direction impossible to approve?
- Which customer action or phrase carries more weight than the team’s current
  theory?
- What would the final decision-maker need to see to choose one direction and
  stop reopening the decision?
