promote, iterate, or kill

The test diagnoses qualification, value, and language separately.

01

Promote to Batch 2

At least 2 of 5 meaningful responses and at least 1 direct problem confirmation. This is message evidence, not demand proof.

02

Initial commercial proof

One qualified buyer accepts the unchanged $750 scope and cleared money is recorded.

03

Iterate one gate

Replies rejecting the problem repair qualification; problem recognition without note requests repairs value; misunderstanding repairs language.

04

Kill the segment/problem hypothesis

Ten qualified exposures plus permitted follow-up produce zero problem confirmations.

TRUTH BOUNDARY

what this does not prove

Public launch language does not reveal internal disagreement, urgency, budget, or willingness to pay. Prepared messages are not exposures. Local capability is not customer adoption. Compliments and AI curiosity are not problem confirmation. The current test remains NO PERMISSION until Farrice separately authorizes outreach.
