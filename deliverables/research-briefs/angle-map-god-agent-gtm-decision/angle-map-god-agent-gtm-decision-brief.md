_ONE OFFER · ONE TEST_
Test The Angle Map now. It has a fixed transaction, bounded delivery, an observable target universe, and explicit commercial counters. It is still market-untested: the canonical state is 0 sent, 0 held, 0 sold, and $0 collected.

Its honest next state is a narrow warm proof install after the active revenue test, not another public acquisition lane.

## the problem-qualified segment
The observable trigger is strong enough to test. The experienced consequence still belongs in UNKNOWN.
1. **Segment** — Established supplement, functional-beverage, recovery, or performance brands with a real product and a live market occasion inside 90 days.
2. **Company state** — Several plausible message directions, usable evidence and claim boundaries, one decision deadline, and authority to spend $750.
3. **Person** — Founder, CEO, brand lead, or marketing owner responsible for choosing the campaign argument and able to supply the final decision-maker.
4. **Worst version** — The team funds more creative before settling which buyer belief and approved proof should organize the campaign.

## the one-gap decision-note test
Verify → Diagnose → Offer value → Expose → Count → Learn
  - Verify: role, live occasion, public language, route, payment rail
  - Diagnose: one source-backed A-versus-B campaign decision
  - Offer value: ask permission to send the one-page Decision Note
  - Expose: five qualified messages; no autonomous send
  - Count: meaningful response, confirmation, held, sold, collected
  - Learn: repair only the failed gate before Batch 2

## promote, iterate, or kill
The test diagnoses qualification, value, and language separately.
1. **Promote to Batch 2** — At least 2 of 5 meaningful responses and at least 1 direct problem confirmation. This is message evidence, not demand proof.
2. **Initial commercial proof** — One qualified buyer accepts the unchanged $750 scope and cleared money is recorded.
3. **Iterate one gate** — Replies rejecting the problem repair qualification; problem recognition without note requests repairs value; misunderstanding repairs language.
4. **Kill the segment/problem hypothesis** — Ten qualified exposures plus permitted follow-up produce zero problem confirmations.

## what this does not prove
_TRUTH BOUNDARY_
Public launch language does not reveal internal disagreement, urgency, budget, or willingness to pay. Prepared messages are not exposures. Local capability is not customer adoption. Compliments and AI curiosity are not problem confirmation. The current test remains NO PERMISSION until Farrice separately authorizes outreach.
