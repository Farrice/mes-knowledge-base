# what creative buyers are actually buying now

> MARKET DEMAND BRIEF · CREATIVE STRATEGY · window: 2025-2026 demand signals · lens: health · performance · wellness brands · sources: 8 current market and platform sources · compiled: aug 11, 2026

A force-ranked offer and inbound-asset map: where current buyer spend is moving, what AI is commoditizing, and how to package creative judgment into a paid diagnostic, sprint, and recurring growth system.

## the market is buying judgment, not volume
_THE VERDICT_
The strongest demand is at the intersection of performance creative, creator-led distribution, platform-native video, and AI-assisted production. The premium is moving away from raw asset creation. Buyers increasingly need someone to turn audience and community signals into the right message, direct creators and AI tools, design a learnable test, and carry performance learning into the next batch.

My recommendation for Farrice is one vertical promise for established supplement, recovery, wellness, and performance brands facing a live launch, reformulation, claims change, creator campaign, or creative-fatigue decision. [VERIFIED] Keep The Angle Map as the paid entry in the current offer canon. Use the 10-day Proof-to-Market Sprint as the core implementation offer. Test a recurring Creative Intelligence Partner retainer after paid delivery creates proof.

## what current spending supports
- **Creative strategy and brand judgment have paid marketplace demand.** [VERIFIED] — Upwork's 2026 index, based on completed US jobs and freelancer earnings during 2025, lists social media strategy at +36%, display advertising at +26%, and brand strategy at +26%. AI video generation and editing grew +329%. (https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upworks-demand-skills-2026-demand-top-ai-skills-more-doubles-ai)
- **AI expands creative volume faster than it improves creative quality.** [VERIFIED] — A 2026 WARC-TikTok study of 400 global marketing leaders reported 90% using AI as a core creative tool and 88% increasing volume, but only 45% reporting significant quality improvement. This supports selling the intelligence and direction layer, not access to tools. (https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/warc-report-new-creative-advantage?redirected=1)
- **Creator creative has matured into a full-funnel media channel.** [VERIFIED] — IAB projected US creator ad spend at $37B in 2025, up 26% year over year; 48% of ad buyers considered creators a must-buy. Buyers named awareness, new audiences, trust, and online sales as goals, while 40% ranked overall ROI as their top creator KPI. (https://www.iab.com/news/creator-economy-ad-spend-to-reach-37-billion-in-2025-growing-4x-faster-than-total-media-industry-according-to-iab/)
- **Platform-native creator assets now have explicit performance infrastructure.** [LIKELY] — Meta reports that adding partnership ads to usual campaigns can reduce acquisition cost by 19%. Google is also expanding AI-assisted matching between advertisers and creator communities. The opportunity is creator strategy, casting, briefing, rights-ready asset planning, and paid adaptation—not influencer lists. (https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/creator-marketplace)
- **B2B founder and expert-led content can produce qualified demand, not only impressions.** [VERIFIED] — LinkedIn reports that 82% of surveyed buyers are influenced by B2B creator content; 47% visited a vendor website and 38% engaged with sales after consuming it. Thought Leader Ads were reported to improve lead form completion and cost per lead versus benchmark campaigns. (https://www.linkedin.com/business/marketing/blog/linkedin-ads/b2b-creator-thought-leadership)
- **Low-value production is becoming less defensible even as content demand rises.** [VERIFIED] — Fiverr reports strong demand for AI UGC, AI video, Instagram content, YouTube services, and human editing, but its Q2 2026 results also cite weakness in categories most exposed to AI automation and a strategic shift toward higher-value work. Sell decisions and systems; treat editing as fulfillment support. (https://investors.fiverr.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fiverr-announces-second-quarter-2026-results)

## the inbound asset system
Each asset should demonstrate the decision layer and end at the same paid entry. Do not turn the content engine into free custom consulting.
1. **Weekly Health-Performance Creative White-Space Teardown** — Publish one evidence-backed visual teardown: buyer tension, missing audience or proof cell, one unowned angle, one test, and what not to make. Repurpose it as a LinkedIn post, 5-8 slide carousel, and 60-90 second founder video. Measure qualified DMs naming a live campaign problem.
2. **Monthly Health Performance Creative Signals Brief** — Synthesize customer language, claims and proof shifts, creator formats, top-spend patterns, and three production implications. Publish as a visual brief, email edition, and LinkedIn document. Measure saves, internal forwards, and replies from founders, growth leads, creative directors, and agencies.
3. **Decision Artifact Before-and-After** — Make invisible strategy inspectable without disclosing confidential information: raw product truth and customer language -> three angles -> lead choice -> proof direction -> build-priority card. The success signal is prospects asking about scope and decisions instead of asking whether you edit videos.
4. **[LIKELY] What Not to Make Series** — Take one common category trope and explain the missing signal, performance risk, and sharper alternative. Use short video, single-image teardown, or a newsletter section. This filters for teams already spending on creative and feeling the cost of weak iteration.

## what this evidence doesn't prove
_PROOF BOUNDARY_
Marketplace reports show completed-job or search demand, not your close rate. Platform studies are commercially interested sources and may not generalize across every brand. The public numbers validate the service categories and market direction; they do not validate your exact promise, price, buyer, or delivery burden.
