01

the diagnosis

Decision, not decoration

Rello is qualified for a public-source specimen. The brand has a live DTC product, nationwide Sprouts availability, current NSF Certified for Sport listings for both bar flavors, and active Meta advertising.

The public opportunity is message expansion, not a claim that the current work is failing. Four active Rello ads found in the Meta Ad Library use the same primary text and near-identical lo-fi creator treatment. The current message compresses protein, creatine, clean ingredients, strength, recovery, energy, and everyday performance into one unit. This specimen isolates one sharper job: make creatine easier to remember by putting it inside a protein bar the buyer already carries.

One job per concept

two concepts worth testing

Concept A isolates the strongest owned mechanism. Concept B preserves the product's performance breadth but gives it a cleaner entry point.

01

A · One less supplement ritual

Target: creatine-aware active professionals who buy powders but struggle with consistency. Tension: the ingredient is not the hard part; remembering another scoop is. Promise: protein plus 1.2g creatine in the bar already going into the bag. Proof: current label facts, portable format, NSF listing. Best first vessel: lo-fi creator/product demonstration.

02

B · The protein bar with a performance layer

Target: solution-aware protein-bar buyers comparing labels. Tension: most bars make protein the entire story. Promise: 20g protein plus creatine and fiber in one bar. Proof: product panel and ingredient list. Best first vessel: headliner static, then matched creator video if the message earns a second test.

06

one creator script

CONCEPT A · 35-45 SECONDS
Lo-fi product demonstration
OPEN · Show a creatine tub, shaker, and Rello bar on a kitchen counter.

VOICE: If your creatine routine keeps dying at the scoop, this is the idea behind Rello.

PROBLEM · Pack the tub away; put the bar in a work or gym bag.

VOICE: Another powder only helps if it survives your actual day. Rello puts 20 grams of protein and 1.2 grams of creatine into a bar you can carry.

PROOF · Tight shots of the wrapper and nutrition panel.

VOICE: The current bar also lists 9 grams of fiber, and both flavors appear in NSF's Certified for Sport directory.

BOUNDARY · Creator holds the bar; no body-transformation imagery.

VOICE: This is not a promise that one bar replaces every supplement or that one amount fits everyone. It is a simpler way to put creatine into an existing snack routine.

CTA · If the scoop is the step you keep skipping, read the label and see whether Rello fits your routine.

PRODUCTION NOTE · A creator may not present this as personal experience unless it is true and documented. Final spoken claims require brand approval.
07

one static brief

HEADLINER · CONCEPT A
1:1 and 4:5 production specification
GOAL · Make the format and mechanism legible in one second.

HEADLINE · The protein bar with creatine built in.

SUPPORT · 20g protein · 1.2g creatine · 9g fiber

PROOF CHIP · NSF Certified for Sport, only after the exact mark usage is approved.

VISUAL HIERARCHY · Headline first. Centered wrapper plus broken-open bar second. A shaker and scoop sit small and out of focus in the background as the displaced routine, never crossed out.

CTA · See the ingredients

DESIGN · High contrast, restrained palette taken from current Rello packaging, generous empty space, no athlete collage, no four-benefit icon cloud.

ONE-SECOND TEST · A stranger must be able to answer: this is a protein bar with creatine.

Hold back · strength gains, cognition, longevity, skin, gut, energy, recovery, cost savings, or replacement claims. Those would turn one test back into the current message pile.

the test sequence

01

0 · Claim lock

→
02

1 · Hook test

→
03

2 · Read the signal

The media buyer defines spend sufficiency and evaluates hold, click, conversion, and downstream quality. Public spec work supplies no threshold.

→
04

3 · Format swap

Move the winning Concept A wording into the headliner static. This tests video versus static without changing the core message.

→
05

4 · Concept challenger

Only after a usable vessel signal, introduce Concept B in the same winning format. Do not change concept and format together.

→
06

5 · Learning record

Log what ran, spend, audience, dates, hook definition, conversion result, fatigue state, and the next decision.

Keep the message and vessel tests separate.
WHAT THIS SPECIMEN CANNOT KNOW

strict proof boundaries

VERIFIED: current product facts, public brand language, current NSF listings, Sprouts availability, and the four active Meta ads observed.

LIKELY: a habit-friction message is underrepresented in the visible active sample and deserves a controlled test.

UNCONFIRMED: spend, CTR, hold rate, CPA, ROAS, audience breakdown, conversion volume, creative winner status, customer objections, creator rights, inventory depth, contribution margin, landing-page performance, and which claims Rello's reviewer has approved.

PROHIBITED INFERENCE: active does not mean winning; repeated copy does not mean fatigue; public-facing language does not prove customer demand. No concept launches until the client supplies account context, approved claims, production constraints, and an approval owner.
