# Glow Up: make the all-in-one drink say one thing

> Founder proof object · public-source only · window: Public sources checked September 3, 2026 · lens: Sparkling collagen protein · functional beverage · paid-social message architecture · sources: Melting Forest product page + Sprouts launch release · compiled: September 3, 2026

A one-page creative strategy specimen for Sean McDonald. Two tests separate the format advantage from the beauty-and-wellness occasion.

## the message gap
_All-in-one can become all-at-once_
Glow Up has multiple credible product stories: protein without a shake, collagen, prebiotic fiber, mushrooms, magnesium, beauty botanicals, low sugar, culinary flavors, and sparkling refreshment. That breadth makes the formula interesting but weakens the first second of an ad.

The cleanest split is between a category-displacement test for protein-shake rejectors and a specific beauty-wellness occasion. In both, the unused ingredients become supporting proof rather than additional promises.

## two concepts to separate
1. **A · Protein without the shake** — Target buyers who want protein but reject heaviness, chalk, or another creamy bottle. Open on the clear pour and carbonation. Use 15g collagen protein as the proof after the sensory contrast lands.
2. **B · The afternoon beauty ritual** — Target an ingestible-beauty buyer at one specific occasion: the cold can between lunch and evening. Open on the cold can in a real afternoon moment. Collagen and vitamin C can support the story only within approved claim language.

## six example hooks
**Concept A · protein format**
```
A1  Fifteen grams of collagen protein without another thick shake.

A2  Watch what happens when a protein drink pours clear and sparkling.

A3  For people who want the protein and refuse the chalk.
```
**Concept B · beauty occasion**
```
B1  A cold-can beauty ritual built for the afternoon.

B2  Your afternoon ritual can taste like apple and ginger, not supplement powder.

B3  One sparkling can. One beauty-and-wellness occasion. No ingredient lecture.
```
