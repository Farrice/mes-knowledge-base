# From safe research to hireable strategy

> Buyer-confidence edit · internal decision brief · window: Revision completed September 3, 2026 · lens: Creative strategy · decision intelligence · copy elevation · sources: Six proof briefs + user 5/10 calibration · compiled: September 3, 2026

The original pack proved diligence. This revision makes the interpretation, emotional tension, creative judgment, and paid-work boundary visible without weakening the evidence standard.

## the governing verdict
_User calibration outranks a clean machine score_
The first pack was structurally strong and commercially incomplete. It often moved from fact to recommendation without exposing the interpretation in between, so accurate product language became safe hooks and competent scripts. Farrice's 5/10 verdict is the baseline.

The repair adds a disciplined middle: observation, pattern, labeled interpretation, plausible alternative, decision delta, and falsifier. Only then does copy craft enter. The creative now has a human pressure point to dramatize instead of a feature list to decorate.

## preservation lock
1. **Keep** — Public-source rigor, source links, VERIFIED / LIKELY / UNCONFIRMED states, visible unknowns, test logic, visual readability, and claim safety.
2. **Change** — Reporting-only diagnoses, generic feature hooks, emotionally flat scripts, and the missing boundary between the free proof note and paid account-informed work.
3. **Do not disturb** — No invented buyer emotions, performance results, customer language, medical outcomes, creator experience, or causal certainty.
4. **Gate** — A founder should see one non-obvious read, understand why it is defensible, feel the buyer tension in the sample copy, and know exactly what evidence could prove the strategist wrong.
