# GateDrop: choose the energy occasion

> Founder proof object · public-source only · window: Public sources checked September 3, 2026 · lens: Energy gummies · DTC paid social · challenger positioning · sources: GateDrop product page + launch release + founder post · compiled: September 3, 2026

A one-page creative strategy specimen for Tim Near. It turns GateDrop's public launch language into two distinct tests without claiming access to private performance data.

## the message gap
_One product, too many occasions_
GateDrop has a sharp category challenger: portable, portionable energy without a can. The current product page then stretches that idea across focus, productivity, workouts, long shifts, travel, and action sports. The first paid-social test should choose one moment of substitution instead of asking the gummy to mean everything at once.

A second issue needs claim governance before scale: the current site and April launch release state 25mg caffeine per gummy, while an earlier founder launch post stated 20mg. Current label truth should govern every ad.

## two concepts to separate
1. **A · Energy you control** — Target the caffeine-sensitive or dose-conscious buyer. The enemy is the all-or-nothing can. Show the buyer choosing one gummy, then deciding whether the moment needs another. Use only current, label-approved dose language.
2. **B · Pocket energy for the long shift** — Target one shift-based use case, not all busy people. Show the product surviving a bag, pocket, car, and break room while a can is impractical. The promise is convenience and format, not medical alertness or safety.

## six example hooks
**Concept A · control**
```
A1  Your energy should not come in an all-or-nothing can.

A2  One gummy. 25 milligrams of caffeine. You decide what the moment needs.

A3  The energy format for people who want the dial, not the blast.
```
**Concept B · long shift**
```
B1  A four-pack of energy that fits where a can does not.

B2  Long shift. Small pocket. No can required.

B3  When the next break is unpredictable, carry the format that is not.
```

## visible unknowns
UNCONFIRMED: active paid-social inventory, sales velocity, strongest buyer, onset experience, repeat rate, creator rights, approved comparison language, and production capacity.

CLAIM HOLD: reconcile the older 20mg founder post with the current 25mg product page and label before any asset uses a number. Avoid claims about being safer, healthier, crash-free, faster acting, or equivalent to a named energy drink unless the brand supplies substantiation and approval.
