> EXTRACTION · JORDAN CRAWFORD · window: public corpus through aug 8, 2026 · lens: customer dossiers · message-market fit · GTM engineering · sources: 8 transcripts · 25,529 words · public first-party pages · compiled: aug 8, 2026

A source-grounded system that turns customer truth into a problem-qualified universe, independently valuable message, and compounding learning loop without confusing personalization, activity, or simulation with proof.

## the real advantage is information asymmetry
_ARCHITECTURAL VERDICT_
Jordan Crawford's method is not a copy framework or a lead-scraping recipe. The durable asset is a customer truth base that preserves observed actions, verbatim language, operator claims, model inference, and unknowns as different evidence classes.

That truth base makes the worst version of a problem visible, makes the right market enumerable, and gives the operator enough information advantage to create something useful for the recipient before asking for a sale. The message becomes a compressed expression of the targeting evidence.

## the compounding loop
Customer evidence → Truth dossier → Problem-qualified segment → Target universe → Personally valuable proposition → Message-market-fit test → Learning return
  - Customer evidence: actions, quotes, claims, unknowns
  - Truth dossier: provenance, conflicts, problem clusters
  - Problem-qualified segment: worst version + segment/company/person fit
  - Target universe: observable indicators, enumeration, disqualification
  - Personally valuable proposition: useful even without purchase
  - Message-market-fit test: qualification, value, language, commercial counters
  - Learning return: events become evidence and belief deltas

## start at the earliest missing truth layer
- **LOW EVIDENCE · LOW READINESS**: Zero-data discovery (known-good cases, Five Cs, prediction saturation)
- **HIGH EVIDENCE · LOW READINESS**: Customer truth dossier (reconcile actions, voice, CRM, conflicts, unknowns)
- **LOW EVIDENCE · HIGH PRESSURE**: Hold messaging (protect UNKNOWN; refuse fabricated ICP and autonomous outreach)
- **HIGH EVIDENCE · HIGH READINESS**: PQS -> universe -> PVP -> test (freeze one hypothesis and return outcomes to the dossier)

## what is locked now
One upstream owner, bounded downstream craft, and proof states that cannot be inflated by language.
1. **Owner: Jordan governs customer truth through message-market-fit learning** — Adjacent positioning, offer, copy, and RevOps experts receive source-locked handoffs; they do not compete for upstream ownership.
2. **Evidence: facts and reasoning remain separate stores** — Facts and quotes survive reinterpretation; conflicts, failed searches, and unknowns remain visible.
3. **Authority: no autonomous external action** — Outreach, publishing, CRM writes, paid enrichment, and global promotion require explicit approval.
4. **Packaging: plugin work remains parked** — A local connected system exists; portability, privacy, cold-start, and real-world outcome proof must precede packaging.

## the 10-workflow operating spine
Workflows 1–3 establish truth. Choose the dossier path when evidence exists, the zero-data path when it does not, then qualify the worst observable problem across segment, company, and person.

Workflows 4–6 build the market and the value. Map observable indicators, enumerate and disqualify the universe, choose research methods by right-answer cost, then create a PVP that helps before a purchase.

Workflows 7–10 operate and compound. Freeze one message hypothesis, keep external execution approval-gated, unitize only proven steps, hand source-locked evidence to one craft owner, and return outcomes to the dossier.
