## the honest proof ladder
Public trigger → Problem confirmation → Exact offer → Collected payment → Downstream use → Repeatability

  - Problem confirmation: recipient confirms or corrects the decision
  - Exact offer: unchanged scope and price stated
  - Collected payment: cleared money, not interest
  - Downstream use: production owner applies the output
  - Repeatability: second buyer and measured outcome
