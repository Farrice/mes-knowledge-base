01

the correction

ONE PERSON, NOT FIVE NICHES

Jen should not perform a crime-scene character, a market-pundit character, or a collection of clever franchises. Her differentiation is the experience of being represented by her: she notices what matters, explains it plainly, protects the decision, coordinates the next step, and lowers the emotional load.

The creative formats can change. The promise cannot.

RECOMMENDATION

three angles — one brand

These should stack instead of competing. The promise is emotional, the proof is operational, and the CTA is concrete.

01

1. Brand promise — The whole move feels handled

Best match for Jen's actual service: warm, anticipatory, protective, and low-friction. It is broad enough for buyers and sellers, but must be proven through specific client moments so it does not become generic luxury language.

02

2. Content proof — You'll always know what happens next

Turns informing and empowering into a repeatable editorial standard. Every useful post should reduce uncertainty, clarify a choice, or show how Jen lowers the load.

03

3. Lead invitation — Send me the street or the number

Makes the next action feel like Jen: specific, calm, and useful. It can serve buyers now, while seller variants use 'send me what changed' or 'send me the move you're trying to make.'

KILL / PARK

what to stop doing

01

Kill the crime-scene angle

It is anxiety-heavy, costume-like, and asks Jen to play a personality she does not have. It also invites professional and brand risk.

02

Park public franchise names

The system may use repeatable structures internally, but the audience should experience one Jen—not a menu of branded concepts.

03

Stop treating reach as lead proof

Views and comments are attention signals. A qualified DM, property review, consultation, or client conversation is the lead-path event.

CAVEATS WORTH KEEPING

what this still cannot prove

The 30-day Insights window sits inside the 90-day window. It is a recent snapshot, not an independent before-and-after cohort.

The feed efficiency number is interaction share divided by view share, not a conventional per-post engagement rate. It is directional.

Post-level saves, shares, qualified DMs, appointments, signed clients, and closed revenue are incomplete. No content angle is yet proven to generate clients.

The promise should be 'the move feels handled,' not a literal guarantee that Jen personally performs every specialist's work.

Local content must remain compliant with fair-housing rules and avoid ranking neighborhoods for protected classes.
