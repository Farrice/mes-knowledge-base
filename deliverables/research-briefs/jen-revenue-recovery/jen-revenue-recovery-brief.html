THE COMMERCIAL PATH

run one two-part demonstration

Jen should see both the thing that serves her and the small offer that can serve agents she trusts. Do not ask the demo to sell four offers.

01

Show Jen First Home Valley as the service-to-her.

This keeps the emotional center correct: you are helping her build a buyer-education and conversation engine, not using her as a lead list. Capture her first-post pick, voice verdict, and channel greenlight.

02

Ask for permission to show one listing package to three agents.

Permission is the first missing proof event. Her yes unlocks the warm channel without implying endorsement, results, or an obligation to sell for you.

03

Offer three founding Listing Launch Kits at $200 each.

The deliverable exists, the scope is bounded, and three sales equal $600. Keep payment, delivery, use, conversations, repeat purchase, and referrals as separate receipts.

04

Earn the right to sell the $750 install later.

After one or more agents pay and use the flagship, the missing Lead Reactivation module can be built from real inputs. Until then, the Revenue Desk stays a parked expansion—not the front-door offer.
