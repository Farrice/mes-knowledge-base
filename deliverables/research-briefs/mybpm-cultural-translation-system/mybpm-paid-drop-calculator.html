1 · Product truth

Physical sample passes fit, hand, print, wash, color, placement, packaging, and photography. Mockups do not count.

2 · Checkout truth

Decoration, size surcharges, exact origin, shipping, taxes, and app fees are copied from a live test checkout.

3 · Demand truth

The minimum is paid units inside a disclosed window—not likes, follows, comments, or waitlist size.

4 · Reserve integrity

Production cash, refund/defect reserve, and operating money remain separate. Gross receipts are not available cash.

5 · Promise integrity

The product page gives a reasonable ship date. Delays trigger consent or prompt refunds in line with FTC requirements.

6 · Learning receipt

Close with paid units, gross, landed costs, refunds, defects, on-time rate, retained contribution, and next product request.
