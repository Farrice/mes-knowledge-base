THE OPENING

where the lane actually is

Five moves this week, ranked. Each one is downstream of a number in the evidence above.

01

Write to the repositioning incumbent, not the AI-native builder.

10 of 10 profiles returned for "AI consultant" are program managers, strategy consultants, architects and a recruiter. The people who rank for the term are the people who need help holding it.

02

Put a named offer and a price in the first two lines of every post and the profile itself.

0 of 10 profiles name a service or a number a buyer can act on; the most concrete line in the whole set is a VAT registration.

03

Run the receipt format: three numbers, one lesson, no adjectives.

The @kevbuildsai post carries three specific figures (24 orders, $433, 0 hours) and no claims, while the superlative post in the same pull carries zero figures.

04

Mirror their sentence back before you break it.

Two unrelated profiles reach for the same abstraction: "translating complex technical constraints... into scalable business outcomes" and "solutions that enhance customer satisfaction and drive innovation". Quote it, then show what it costs them.

05

Skip agent-architecture explainer content this week.

The only agent-criteria post the pull surfaced (@leadgenman on memory, loops and observability) is dated 2025-10-31. Nine months old, and nothing fresher came with it.

what this isn't

Most reliable: the ten LinkedIn profile summaries and the four Threads post texts. Those are verbatim from the pull, each with a resolvable URL, and the quotes above are unedited.

Less reliable: every pattern claim drawn across those profiles. Ten results from a single query is a sample, not a census, and LinkedIn people-search ranking is opaque. A different query string would likely return a different ten.

Not reliable at all: anything about X today. The twitter actor returned fifteen identical KaitoEasyAPI billing placeholders and no posts, so silence from that lane means the actor failed, not that the conversation stopped. The Reddit pull failed separately with a 400 Bad Request and contributed nothing.

Missing entirely: engagement. No item in this pull carries a like, reply, or view count, so nothing here is ranked by performance. It is ranked only by what was said and when. Recency is uneven too: of four Threads posts, the dates are today, a month ago, five weeks ago, and October 2025.
