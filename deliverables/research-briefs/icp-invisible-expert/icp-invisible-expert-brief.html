the identity-level read

MCRANEY DEPTH
WHO THIS BUYER IS

This buyer built real mastery and then discovered the market cannot see it. The distance between what they know and what they can say online has a name in the source research: the translation gap. It is an identity crisis disguised as a marketing problem. Telling a respected psychologist to build a personal brand reads to her as a demand to become someone she is not, so the advice itself triggers the threat it is trying to solve.

The resistance lives below argument. Identity threats activate the same neural circuits as physical threats, and the brain covers the flinch with rational-sounding excuses: no time, referrals work fine, not a writer. In the source's words, the identity of the expert who does not need marketing IS the resistance. The winning message never asks them to change who they are. It splits the roles. You talk. We translate. Your authority grows.

HOW TO WRITE TO HER

bridge moves, ranked

what the profile supports doing, in order.

01

Open with the feeling, never the solution

The audience is problem-aware, not solution-aware, and the register read is frustrated plus ashamed plus defensive. Validation has to land before any pitch can.

02

Reframe visibility as service, not self-promotion

Their primary value is impact. The line that flips it, from the source: your expertise is someone's solution, and if you don't share it, they can't find you.

03

Use their vocabulary and retire marketing's

The composite avatar paid $3,000 for a personal branding course and quit at module 2 because the language made her cringe. Word choice is the first credibility test.

04

Name the identity threat out loud

The resistance is pre-rational and the excuses are cover for a threatened self-concept. Saying you don't have to become an influencer disarms what argument cannot.

05

Honor referrals, then surface the ceiling

Referrals bring roughly 85% of the avatar's clients and the profile confirms they work. The opening is the unasked question: what happens when your top referrer leaves.

06

Keep the role split clean in every touchpoint

The bridge message wins because they stay the expert and the translation burden moves to you. Ten minutes of voice memo produces a week of content.

07

Sell the difficulty as proof of depth

The curse-of-expertise reframe turns their failure to write posts into evidence of mastery, which dissolves the shame that blocks the sale.

COPY-PASTE
bridge-message opener
You spent years becoming an expert. Why does it feel impossible to explain what you do?

That difficulty has a name: the Curse of Expertise. The deeper your knowledge, the harder plain language gets. The struggle is evidence of depth, not a flaw.

So don't write. Talk.

You talk. We translate. Your authority grows.

No blank page. No performing. You stay the expert; the translation is our job.

what this profile is not

This is a resistance and language map, not a demographic persona and not an offer document. It does not price, package, or sequence the Authority Sprint.

Dr. Maya Patel is a composite built from published research and community language, not a live interview. Quotes attributed to her are constructed; treat them as LIKELY, never as field data. The big percentages (70%, 90%, 60%) come from the research the source cites, not from Farrice's own funnel.

Staleness: the source document is dated March 2026 by its own header, so community language and platform mechanics carry several months of drift. Re-verify the LinkedIn algorithm claims and refresh the language map before any paid campaign leans on this profile.
