## Regression guard

Reject any future proof-note revision that:

- replaces a diagnostic statement with a coined slogan;
- names an emotion without a source or a visible behavior;
- removes the “what we observed / what it means / what to test” chain;
- scores differentiation by novelty of wording;
- requires the founder to decode the point.

The positive standard is: the diagnosis should be non-obvious before it is said and obvious after it is shown. Human psychology enters through recognition, tension, and consequence. Rhetoric earns one memorable line only after the thought is clear.
