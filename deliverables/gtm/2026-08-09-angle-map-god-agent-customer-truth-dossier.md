## 3. Problem clusters

### Cluster A: The pre-production message decision

An established brand reaches a live retail, launch, reformulation, or campaign occasion with several plausible messages: ingredient science, lifestyle, usage occasion, audience identity, or proof. The worst version is not “the copy could be better.” It is that the team funds creative and distribution before agreeing which buyer belief and approved proof should organize the campaign.

**Evidence strength:** moderate for the observable trigger; weak for the buyer’s experienced consequence. Public pages show live occasions and message breadth. No customer has yet confirmed rework, delay, internal disagreement, or wasted spend.

### Cluster B: Claim-sensitive proof selection

Supplement and functional-product brands must connect campaign language to usable evidence while preserving legal/compliance ownership. The Angle Map explicitly addresses that boundary.

**Evidence strength:** offer-side only. The corpus proves this is a designed capability, not that the first-wave buyers currently seek or will pay for it.

### Cluster C: The owner as operating system

The God Agent hypothesis says a 5–50-person owner-operated company has repeat work trapped in the owner’s head, inbox, and memory. The worst version is missed follow-up, slow decisions, and an owner who cannot step away.

**Evidence strength:** weak. It is a plausible operator narrative with one proposed restaurant proof case, but no customer action, quote, acceptance, or measured consequence exists in the available record.

## 4. Conflicts and edge cases

1. **An execution checklist contains “campaign complete” prose, while its own boxes remain unchecked and the canonical pipeline remains at zero.** The action ledger wins: no send event is recorded.
2. **The Angle Map looks finished locally but remains commercially untested.** Offer architecture, prospect research, copy, delivery templates, and visual assets establish readiness, not demand.
3. **The God Agent is both “uncaged” in a positioning document and sequenced behind first collected revenue in the adversarial verdict.** Preserve both decisions. The current evidence supports internal development and warm discovery, not a co-equal public campaign.
4. **Public company language can establish a trigger but not an internal problem.** A sharp one-gap interpretation is still an inference until the recipient confirms or corrects it.
5. **A positive response is not market fit.** Compliments, curiosity, and AI interest do not equal problem confirmation, a held sales event, an accepted price, or cleared money.

## 5. Provisional interpretation

The Angle Map is closer to truth because its list can be defined from observable events: a real product, a live occasion, multiple public message directions, proof constraints, and an identifiable owner of the campaign decision. Its missing layer is direct problem evidence. The right next move is exposure instead of more offer construction.

The God Agent has the inverse profile: unusually strong mechanism evidence and unusually weak buyer evidence. Turning it into a public offer now would ask the message to compensate for an unqualified problem, an undefined support burden, and zero external receipts. Its warm proof install should be used later to collect customer actions and language, not represented as validated demand.

## 6. Problem-Qualified Segment

### Angle Map verdict: PROVISIONAL

**PQS statement:** An established supplement, functional-beverage, recovery, or performance brand with a real product and a live launch, retail expansion, reformulation, or creator campaign inside 90 days, where a founder or marketing owner must choose one campaign argument from multiple plausible occasions, beliefs, or proof routes before more creative is funded, and can supply approved evidence and claim boundaries.

#### Worst-version problem

- **Consequence:** creative production and distribution proceed around an unresolved argument, creating rework or a campaign that asks the buyer to hold too many ideas at once.
- **Trigger/timing:** an externally visible launch, new distribution, reformulation, repositioning deadline, or campaign deadline inside 90 days.
- **Failed alternative:** generating more hooks or letting each channel lead with a different benefit before choosing the governing belief.
- **Observable trace:** several occasions or value claims share the same launch language; the responsible decision-maker and deadline are identifiable.

#### Five-Cs evidence

| Dimension | Current evidence | State |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | Public product and launch language; no direct problem confirmations | PROVISIONAL |
| Company | Established products and active distribution/launch events are findable | SUPPORTED |
| Category | Supplements and functional products carry proof and claim boundaries | SUPPORTED, buyer urgency unconfirmed |
| Competition | Status quo is more concepts, agency production, or internal debate | INFERENCE |
| Context | A live occasion and deadline make the decision time-bound | SUPPORTED |

#### Concentric qualification

- **Segment:** established supplement, functional beverage, recovery, or performance product business.
- **Company:** real product; active occasion inside 90 days; multiple plausible message directions; approved evidence available; can authorize $750 without an undefined committee.
- **Person:** founder, CEO, brand lead, or marketing owner directly responsible for the campaign argument and able to provide the final decision-maker and deadline.

#### Positive indicators

- New retail distribution or product-line expansion.
- Reformulation, new packaging, or a new ingredient/proof story.
- Several public consumption occasions competing for emphasis.
- Claim-sensitive language requiring an approved proof boundary.
- A named campaign decision-maker and deadline.

#### Hard disqualifiers

- Pre-revenue and still deciding what to sell.
- No live occasion or decision deadline.
- Wants legal certification, guaranteed sales, rankings, retailer acceptance, or AI citations.
- Cannot provide approved evidence or a final decision-maker.
- Wants campaign production rather than a bounded message decision.

#### Counterexample test

A pre-revenue wellness coach with no product, launch, proof set, or campaign owner may like “three angles,” but does not have the observable worst-version problem this offer is designed to solve. Reject rather than broaden the ICP.

#### Next evidence needed

At least five qualified exposures and direct recipient reactions to the one-gap diagnosis. The PQS advances from `PROVISIONAL` only when recipients confirm that the choice is real and consequential, not merely interesting.

### God Agent verdict: REJECTED as a public now-offer; PROVISIONAL as warm discovery

**Provisional research segment:** A trusted owner-operator of a multi-location service or hospitality business with repeat monitoring, reporting, review, or follow-up work that is demonstrably delayed or personally handled by the owner.

This is not yet a qualified segment. The available record contains no customer action or quote proving the owner bottleneck, no adoption event, and no outcome. The proposed Chris restaurant install is the smallest reasonable discovery unit because it narrows the mechanism to three observable jobs and can produce usage evidence. It should not compete with The Angle Map’s public acquisition path.

## 7. Message-Market-Fit Test: The One-Gap Decision Note

### Permission state: No external-send permission

This charter designs the test only. Farrice must separately authorize outreach, and each prospect’s current role, product page, message language, channel, and payment rail must be verified before any external action.

### Frozen hypothesis

For established supplement and functional-product founders with a live market occasion, a source-backed one-page note that identifies **one unresolved campaign argument and the tradeoff it creates** will produce problem confirmations and qualified next steps because it helps them make a decision before asking them to buy The Angle Map.

Only these variables are tested:

- **Segment/problem:** live occasion + multiple plausible message directions.
- **PVP:** One-Gap Decision Note.
- **Offer:** unchanged `$750 prepaid` Angle Map.
- **Channel:** one-to-one LinkedIn message to a verified decision-maker.

Do not change price, scope, channel, ICP, proof object, and message simultaneously.

### Personally valuable proof object

The **One-Gap Decision Note** contains:

1. the brand’s current public language with source;
2. the two competing message directions visible in that language;
3. the single campaign decision those directions create;
4. the approved-proof question the team should answer before production;
5. one “do not build both at once” boundary.

It must be useful even if the recipient never purchases. It must not pretend to know internal disagreement, performance, or compliance status.

### Message shell

> [Name], [verified live occasion] puts “[verbatim company phrase]” next to [second observed occasion, proof route, or promise].
>
> That creates one decision before more creative gets built: **[direction A] or [direction B] has to organize the campaign first.**
>
> I mapped the public evidence into a one-page Decision Note showing the tradeoff, the proof question, and what I would not force into the same campaign.
>
> Want me to send it?

The note carries the value instead of a clever opener. The gap sentence remains labeled `INFERENCE` until the recipient confirms or corrects it.

### First universe slice

Use the existing five source-grounded candidates only after same-day verification:

1. Tim Near / GateDrop: distribution expansion; action-sports identity versus broader convenience buyer.
2. Daniel Adix / LiQure: reformulation and retail return; recovery science versus premium ritual.

4. Thomas Eddleston / FABRIC: new Zero line; functional thesis versus several consumption occasions.
5. Ivan Tsvilik / True Sea Moss: Whole Foods expansion; distinctive ingredient versus first-use ritual.

These are candidate hypotheses, not proven problems. A failed verification removes the row rather than weakening the qualification standard.

### Three gates

| Gate | Pass condition | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| Right recipient | Current role, live occasion, decision ownership, direct route, and fit screen verified | Candidate list exists; same-day verification pending |
| Disproportionate information value | Recipient requests the note, corrects the diagnosis meaningfully, or uses it to discuss the decision | UNTESTED |
| Customer-native language | Verbatim company language anchors the note; recipient recognizes the decision without needing internal jargon explained | PREDICTED, not observed |

### Counters

| Counter | Definition |
|---|---|
| Qualified exposure | Verified decision-maker receives the message and the send is logged |
| Meaningful response | Recipient engages the specific decision: confirms, corrects, disputes, or asks to see the note |
| Problem confirmation | Recipient states the choice is live or names a concrete consequence, deadline, rework, or internal decision tied to it |
| Requested next step | Requests the note, scope, price, source review, or a scheduled conversation |
| Held | A two-way qualified sales conversation occurs |
| Price quoted | The exact `$750` is stated to a qualified buyer |
| Sold | Buyer explicitly accepts the scoped offer |
| Collected | Cleared payment is received |

Compliments, connection accepts, likes, profile views, generic AI curiosity, and “interesting” do not satisfy these counters.

### Batch and follow-up

- **Batch 1:** five qualified exposures, one message each.
- **Follow-up:** one value-bearing follow-up after three business days only if delivery is verified and no response exists. Do not send a generic “bumping this.”
- **Batch 2:** five additional qualified exposures only after reviewing which gate failed. Preserve the original hypothesis; make one line-level correction at most.
- **Commercial infrastructure:** a verified payment route must exist before quoting or sending a payment request.

### Promote / iterate / kill

**PROMOTE the message/PVP to Batch 2 when:** at least 2 of 5 produce meaningful responses and at least 1 confirms the problem. This is message evidence, not demand proof.

**Initial commercial proof:** one qualified buyer accepts the unchanged scope and `$750` clears. Record `sent`, `held`, `sold`, and `collected` separately.

**ITERATE qualification when:** recipients reply but reject the diagnosis or report no live choice. Tighten the segment; do not make the message cleverer.

**ITERATE value when:** the right recipients recognize the problem but do not request the note or another step. Improve the Decision Note, not the offer architecture.

**ITERATE language when:** recipients need the message translated or repeatedly misunderstand the decision. Replace operator terminology with their corrections.

**KILL this segment/problem hypothesis when:** 10 qualified exposures plus the permitted follow-up produce zero problem confirmations. Do not kill the entire Angle Map from an unqualified list.

**RETAIN the existing offer kill gate:** 20 qualified conversations with the exact price quoted and zero sales rejects the current offer/price combination.

### No-event rules

Return `NO EVENT` when fewer than five qualified messages were actually sent, delivery was not verified, the follow-up window is still open, or the record contains only drafts. Return `NO PERMISSION` while external outreach remains unauthorized. Neither state is a negative market verdict.

### Learning capture

For every exposure, preserve:

- source and verified role;
- exact message sent;
- whether the recipient confirmed, corrected, or rejected the inferred gap;
- exact customer language;
- requested next step;
- held, price-quoted, sold, and collected events;
- the single gate diagnosis;
- the smallest evidence-backed change before the next batch.

Feed these rows back into the dossier. Never overwrite the original public evidence with a model summary.
