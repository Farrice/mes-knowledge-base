## Paid-pilot hypothesis

### Buyer

Founder, Head of Growth, or lean marketing lead at a funded or revenue-backed U.S. supplement, performance-nutrition, or recovery brand, usually $1M–$15M and already selling, with active Meta creative, no senior creative-strategy seat, one priority SKU, and a launch/reformulation/creative-fatigue moment.

Disqualify pre-revenue brands, accounts with no usable control or outcome evidence, products Farrice would not defend, teams seeking legal certification or guaranteed media performance, and brands unable to provide a qualified reviewer and launch owner.

### Painful job

The team needs a fresh creative test against its current control, but more production alone risks repeating the same idea, triggering claim-review rework, or producing files the account cannot learn from.

### Promise

> In 72 hours, turn one approved product truth and one current control into five static ads and one video concept, each tied to a clear creative hypothesis and ready for the client’s reviewer and media owner to evaluate.

This is a delivery and learning promise, not a promise to beat the control.

### Deliverables

1. One fit/intake screen covering the priority SKU, control, buyer, substantiation, reviewer, and launch owner.
2. One frozen hypothesis map showing what each creative is meant to test and how the ideas materially differ.
3. Five static ad concepts plus one video concept, delivered in 72 hours after complete inputs.
4. One claim-boundary sheet: brand-approved, reviewer-needed, and prohibited/unsupported zones.
5. One simple launch/readout template so `LAUNCHED`, `RESULTS RETURNED`, and `NO EVENT` remain distinguishable.
6. One policy-rejection make-right: fix or replace a rejected supplied creative without restarting the original delivery clock, limited to rejection attributable to the delivered creative and not to landing page, account, targeting, product, or later client edits.

The Angle Map is not bundled automatically. If the message is too undecided to support six useful executions, stop and quote the separate diagnostic rather than making prettier confusion.

### Duration

- **Build:** 72 hours after all required inputs are accepted.
- **Observation:** up to 14 calendar days after client launch for one results return.
- **Closeout:** one correction memo after results or an explicit `NO EVENT` if the client does not launch or return data.

### Price hypothesis

**$500 prepaid**, preserving the current Control-Beater offer.

This is a founding evidence price, not proof of sustainable economics. It tests whether a qualified buyer will pay for the bounded job. Track actual labor, revision burden, asset-production cost, and results-return friction before changing price or introducing a retainer.

### Proof gate

| Proof state | Exact event |
|---|---|
| `WANTED` | Qualified buyer asks for fit, scope, price, or next step after seeing the exact job |
| `SOLD` | Buyer explicitly accepts $500 prepaid scope and input/learning obligations |
| `COLLECTED` | $500 clears; this is the primary success event |
| `DELIVERED` | Six agreed creative outputs and supporting maps are accepted |
| `LAUNCHED` | Client confirms at least one planned test went live |
| `OUTCOME` | Authorized account readout is returned against the stated baseline |
| `REPEATABLE` | A second qualified paid pilot completes without uncontrolled scope or proof drift |

One payment proves a first purchase event. It does not prove repeatability, performance lift, retention, or product-market fit.

### Claim boundary

**Permitted service claims:** exact deliverables, $500 prepaid price, 72-hour delivery after complete inputs, screening against current published FTC/FDA guidance and the platform rules available during the run, and the bounded policy-rejection fix/replace make-right.

**Not permitted without new evidence and authority:** “pre-cleared,” “compliant,” “legally approved,” “guaranteed to pass Meta,” “will beat your control,” or any promise of CTR, CPA, ROAS, revenue, conversion, or health outcome improvement.

The brand must provide product-specific substantiation and identify which claims it has approved. Farrice may classify, question, narrow, and rewrite claims; he does not certify their legality. The client’s qualified reviewer approves final copy and net impression, and the client’s media owner decides what launches. FTC guidance requires health advertising to be truthful, non-misleading, and adequately substantiated, including implied claims; FDA distinguishes structure/function claims from disease claims and requires the manufacturer to hold substantiation for applicable labeling claims.

Current primary references: [FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance) and [FDA Structure/Function Claims](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-food-labeling-and-critical-foods/structurefunction-claims). Meta policy specifics must be rechecked at the time of each client run because the public policy surface was not fully inspectable during this audit.

## Minimum demand test

No exposure is authorized by this artifact. When Farrice separately approves a live test:

1. Put the exact $500 prepaid pilot in front of **10 qualified buyers** who meet the fit conditions; count the exposure only when scope and price are visible.
2. Record `sent/exposed`, `substantive replies`, `fit conversations`, `exact proposals`, `sold`, and `collected` separately in the accompanying ledger.
3. **First evidence threshold:** one $500 cleared payment.
4. **Diagnosis after 10 qualified exposures:**
   - zero substantive replies → buyer/trigger/opening problem;
   - replies but no fit conversations → proof object or qualification problem;
   - fit conversations but no exact proposals → operator/sales-process problem;
   - exact proposals but no sales → offer, scope, trust, or price problem;
   - sold but not collected → payment/commitment problem.
5. **Kill or materially redesign gate:** 20 qualified fit conversations in which the exact $500 prepaid offer is presented, with zero purchases. Do not kill the offer merely because ten cold exposures are quiet.
