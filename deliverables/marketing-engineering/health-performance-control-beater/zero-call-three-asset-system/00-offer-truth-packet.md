## Buyer

Founder, Head of Growth or lean marketing lead at an already-selling U.S. supplement, performance-nutrition or recovery brand. The brand has active Meta creative, one priority SKU and no senior creative-strategy seat. The buying event is creative fatigue, launch, reformulation, retail expansion or a new evidence/claim opportunity.

## Painful job

The team needs a fresh creative round against a current control. More production alone risks six files built around one unexamined idea, claim-review rework and no clean account learning.

## Failed game

Treating creative volume as creative strategy: change the hook, crop, color or format, then call each file a test even though the underlying reason to believe never changed.

## One defensible belief

> More creative output does not produce better account learning when every asset is a variation of the same untested idea.

## Better model

**Decision before design:** define materially different hypotheses first; then produce assets that give each hypothesis a fair expression. The creative batch becomes a learning instrument instead of a folder-count target.

## Vehicle

1. Accept one current control and define what makes it the control.
2. Inspect recent winner/loser evidence and name the decision gap.
3. Freeze a hypothesis map with materially different reasons to care or believe.
4. Screen product truth and claim boundaries before copy hardens.
5. Produce five static concepts and one video concept against the map.
6. Hand the client reviewer and media owner a launch/readout plan.
7. Record `LAUNCHED`, `OUTCOME` or `NO EVENT` without inventing learning.
