# Control-Beater Zero-Call Close Path

## Stage map

| From | Required entry state | Message/action | Exit state | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Public content / private trigger | Problem-aware | Send Model | Solution-aware | Buyer must understand the failed-game shift |
| Model | Solution-aware | Offer private Machine inspection | Product-aware | No offer-first shortcut |
| Machine | Product-aware | Offer private Invite | Decision-ready | Vehicle, inputs and limits understood |
| Invite | Decision-ready | Five-question fit screen | `WANTED` or `DISQUALIFIED` | Exact price/scope visible |
| Fit screen | Qualified | Written scope and terms | `SOLD` if explicitly accepted | Payment rail must be verified before request |
| Written acceptance | `SOLD` | Authorized payment request | `COLLECTED` only after clearing | Verbal yes is not cash |
| Cleared payment | `COLLECTED` | Complete input/permission review | `BUILD` or `DISCOVER_FIRST/HOLD` | Clock starts only after complete accepted inputs |
| Delivery | Accepted outputs | Client review/launch | `DELIVERED` / `LAUNCHED` | Client owners retain approval/launch |
| Results return | Authorized account evidence | Correction memo | `OUTCOME` or `NO EVENT` | No invented learning |

## Transition copy

### Public content or private trigger -> Model

> Most creative rounds are six files testing one idea. I made a public walkthrough of why that produces output without clean account learning. Start there. If the shift is useful, the private process is available at the end.

### Model -> Machine

> If “decision before design” is the part your current process is missing, I recorded the full Control-Beater walkthrough: control intake, evidence read, hypothesis map, claim boundary, six concepts and the results loop. It is private and there is no opt-in or call behind it. Reply `MACHINE` and I will send it.

### Machine -> Invite

> You now know how the vehicle works and where it stops. The private document has the exact $500 scope, inputs, exclusions and five-question fit screen. Read it only if you have a current control and people who can review, launch and return results. Reply `INVITE` and I will send it.

### Invite -> fit screen

> Send the five answers in one message. If the inputs fit, I will return the exact written scope and terms. If they do not, I will tell you which state applies: `DISCOVER_FIRST`, `HOLD` or `REJECT`. No pitch call either way.

### Fit -> written scope

> This fits the Control-Beater as written: five static concepts plus one video concept, $500 prepaid, 72 hours after cleared payment and complete accepted inputs. Your team owns substantiation, final claim approval, launch and results return. I do not promise platform acceptance or performance. Reply `ACCEPTED` if those terms match your understanding.

### Written acceptance -> payment

> Scope accepted. The payment route is [VERIFIED LINK]. Work is reserved only when $500 clears; the 72-hour clock starts after payment and the input packet are both complete.

Do not use this message until the rail is verified and Farrice authorizes the exact payment request.

### Cleared payment -> delivery intake

> Payment cleared. I am checking the control, account evidence, product/claim sources, reviewer, launch owner and results owner now. I will confirm `BUILD`, `DISCOVER_FIRST` or `HOLD` in writing before the 72-hour clock starts.

## Five-question fit screen

1. Which SKU or launch needs the next creative round?
2. What is the current Meta control, and what account outcome makes it the control?
3. What recent winner and loser evidence can you share?
4. Which product sources and advertising claims are already approved?
5. Who approves claims, launches creative and returns results?

## Async objection responses

| Trigger | Truthful response | State |
|---|---|---|
| “Can you guarantee it beats our control?” | “No. The promise is a disciplined outside round with distinct hypotheses and a legible readout. If guaranteed performance is required, this is not the right job.” | `REJECT` if guarantee remains required |
| “Can you guarantee Meta approval?” | “No. I screen against published guidance and available platform rules. Your qualified reviewer owns final approval; Meta owns enforcement.” | `HOLD` if reviewer absent |
| “We cannot share account data.” | “Then I cannot responsibly call the work a control-beating test. We can discuss a bounded public-surface diagnostic separately, but this pilot stays `DISCOVER_FIRST`.” | `DISCOVER_FIRST` |
| “Why only six concepts?” | “The founding pilot is deliberately bounded so each hypothesis stays legible and the labor/revision burden can be measured. It is not a full campaign build.” | Remain decision-ready |
| “Can we start before payment?” | “No. Cleared payment and complete accepted inputs are both start conditions.” | `SOLD`, not `COLLECTED` |
| “Can we jump on a call?” | “I keep the pre-sale path asynchronous so the written scope, proof and boundaries stay inspectable. Send the five answers here. Any synchronous work starts after payment and is operational.” | Continue async or `DISQUALIFIED` |
| “Send examples/results.” | “I can send prepared capability work and the exact proof ledger. I do not have a supplement-brand performance case study for this offer yet, and I will not imply one.” | Remain decision-ready |
| Silence | No follow-up is authorized by this system. Record `NO EVENT`; do not rewrite the offer from one exposure. | `NO EVENT` |

## Payment handoff

- Current route state: `PENDING / UNVERIFIED`.
- Payment request requires: qualified fit, written scope acceptance, verified rail and Farrice's explicit authorization for that external action.
- `SOLD` = exact written acceptance.
- `COLLECTED` = $500 cleared.
- Only `COLLECTED` plus complete accepted inputs can start delivery.

## Event ledger

| Date | Buyer | Event | Evidence | Next allowed action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| — | — | `NO EVENT` | Nothing sent | None without approval |

Allowed states: `NO EVENT`, `REPLIED`, `WANTED`, `DISQUALIFIED`, `SOLD`, `COLLECTED`, `DELIVERED`, `LAUNCHED`, `OUTCOME`, `REPEATABLE`.

## Do not

- Add a calendar link or pre-sale call.
- Use a setter, closer or “quick chat” to save the sale.
- Chase silence, manufacture urgency or discount to create motion.
- Send a payment request before authority and rail verification.
- Begin work from verbal acceptance or pending payment.
- Promise performance, legal approval, compliance or platform acceptance.
- Treat delivery, launch, outcome and repeatability as the same event.
- Publish, send, automate or contact anyone without separate approval.
