# Private Expert-Business Adapter

**Status:** private portability layer
**Not:** a public service offer, generic funnel package or permission to market this system

## What stays invariant

| Stage | Invariant job |
|---|---|
| Model | Move problem-aware -> solution-aware by replacing one failed game with one defensible belief |
| Machine | Move solution-aware -> product-aware by exposing the vehicle, inputs, proof, ownership and limits |
| Invite | Move product-aware -> decision-ready by disqualifying wrong fits and showing exact commercial truth |
| Close Path | Qualify, confirm scope, collect payment and start delivery asynchronously |

## What must change by vertical

| Variable | Required adaptation |
|---|---|
| Buyer | Named role, buying event, decision authority and actual vocabulary |
| Failed game | Observable current behavior, not a generic enemy |
| Proof | Domain-native evidence and honest proof gaps |
| Vehicle | The real delivery method, not Control-Beater language with nouns swapped |
| Risk | Legal, claim, privacy, financial, reputational or implementation constraints |
| Fit | Behaviors and assets that predict delivery success |
| Price/terms | Exact offer truth for that deployment |
| Decision friction | The objections that exist in sourced conversations or records |
| Payment/start | Real rail, cleared event and complete-input rule |

## Readiness rules

- `BUILD`: considered purchase, complete offer truth, defensible belief, inspectable vehicle, real proof boundary and attention source.
- `DISCOVER_FIRST`: buyer/job fit is plausible, but conversations, evidence, baseline or traffic truth is missing.
- `HOLD`: a known permission, reviewer, owner, payment rail or source is pending.
- `REJECT`: impulse purchase, unsafe claim, fabricated proof, false scarcity, guaranteed outcome or mandatory pre-sale-call process.

## Vertical examples

### Expert consultant

- Model: replace “I need more tactics” with a defensible diagnosis of the missing operating decision.
- Machine: expose diagnostic method, working sequence, client inputs, proof and boundaries.
- Invite: exact project/retainer truth, right/wrong fit and async intake.

### Coach with a considered transformation

- Model: replace the buyer's failed explanation of stalled progress.
- Machine: show the coaching mechanism, accountability structure, client responsibility and evidence boundary.
- Invite: disqualify buyers seeking guarantees, passive rescue or a result the coach cannot responsibly promise.

### B2B implementation service

- Model: surface the operational cost of the current workaround.
- Machine: show system boundaries, data/access requirements, implementation owners and acceptance criteria.
- Invite: state scope, security/privacy needs, exclusions and async commercial next step.

## Portability gate

Do not generalize from Control-Beater copy. Generalize the awareness jobs, truth packet, stage handoffs, proof states and no-call rules. A new vertical must pass one applied cold-start fixture before it is called supported.

Public packaging stays parked until the Control-Beater produces field evidence and Farrice makes a separate positioning decision.
