## This is for you if

You run an already-selling supplement, performance-nutrition or recovery brand. One SKU needs a fresh creative round because of fatigue, launch, reformulation, retail expansion or new evidence. You can share the current control, approved product truth and enough account context to make the next six concepts materially different.

## The job

Turn one current control, approved product truth and account evidence into six claim-aware creative concepts that test distinct ideas and return a legible learning event.

The job is not “make more ads.”

It is to decide what deserves to be tested, give each hypothesis a fair expression and make the result usable.

## What you receive

1. A fit and input screen covering the SKU, control, evidence, claims, reviewers and owners.
2. A frozen hypothesis map showing what each concept tests and how the ideas materially differ.
3. Five static ad concepts.
4. One video concept.
5. A claim-boundary sheet separating approved, reviewer-needed and unsupported language.
6. A launch/readout template separating `DELIVERED`, `LAUNCHED`, `OUTCOME` and `NO EVENT`.
7. One correction memo after returned results or an explicit no-event closeout.

Concepts include copy and visual direction. Final production depth is limited to the scope confirmed in writing; no hidden campaign build, media buying or platform upload is included.
