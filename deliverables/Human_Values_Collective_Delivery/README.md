# Human Values Collective - Master Deliverables

Welcome to the Master Delivery Folder for the Human Values Collective. This directory contains all the strategic assets, research, and foundations created to help scale your Sunday Dinners initiative and build the broader brand.

## Folder Structure & Guide

### 📂 1_Research_and_Discovery/
This folder contains the deep-dive research into your audience, the landscape, and the core identity of the Human Values Collective.
- **human_values_collective_landscape_report.md**: A comprehensive view of the market, audience needs, and where the Collective fits in.
- **human_values_collective_profile.md**: The foundational profile mapping the identity and structure of the initiative.
- **brand-intelligence.md**: Core insights determining how the brand should behave and communicate.
- **grounding-document.md**: The "True North" reference sheet outlining the absolute essentials of your mission.

### 📂 2_Brand_Foundation/
This folder translates the research into actionable brand messaging and identity structure.
- **brand-foundation.md**: The definitive guide to your brand voice, tone, and positioning.
- **storybrand-brandscript.md**: A StoryBrand framework (SB7) mapping your audience’s journey and how you act as their guide.
- **storybrand-message-audit.md**: An evaluation of current/draft messaging against the StoryBrand framework to ensure high conversion and resonance.

### 📂 3_Strategy_and_Growth/
This folder contains the execution plans and growth frameworks for scaling your impact.
- **grace-city-blueprint.md**: The comprehensive architectural plan for how your community, content, and initiatives interconnect.
- **growth-strategy.md**: The specific, tactical roadmap for growing the Sunday Dinners footprint.
- **intent-validation-and-strategy.md**: Strategic alignment confirming the roadmap perfectly matches the goals of the Collective.
- **newsletter-flywheel.md**: A blueprint for building an engaging, high-retention newsletter to keep your community connected between events.

---

### How to Use These Assets
You can upload this entire folder directly to Google Drive. The documents are in Markdown (`.md`) format, which is plain text and easy to read. If you’d prefer, you can copy the contents of any `.md` file directly into a Google Doc for collaborative editing with your team.

You now have a world-class strategic foundation. Time to build!
