## The Core Insight (The "Broken Vase" Principle)

> If a child brings up a small concern ("I broke the vase") and the parent reacts with anger, the child learns it's **not safe** to bring up bigger things — like depression or suicidal thoughts.

**This is the psychological hook that drives everything.** It's concrete, instantly relatable, and emotionally devastating. Every parent recognizes this moment.

**The Sunday Dinner scripts solve this** by normalizing values conversations weekly, so when the big stuff comes, the channel is already open.

Sunday Night Dinner is the **brand**, but the practice is **adaptive**:
- Dinner table

- Diaper-changing table
- Car ride
- Trampoline or physical activity

**This is universal human values → universal application.** The dinner is the ritual anchor, not the prison.
