## Evidence vocabulary

- **Collected-payment proof:** Money paid for Farrice's exact offer. None yet.
- **Platform earnings:** Marketplace-reported supplier earnings.
- **Budgeted buyer demand:** A buyer posted a budget or paid engagement for related work.
- **Listed supplier price:** A seller's public asking price; not proof of purchase.
- **Problem evidence:** A buyer describes the pain or desired workflow.
- **Category/adoption evidence:** An authoritative report describes the market category.
- **Unverified seller claim:** A seller's performance statement without independent substantiation.
