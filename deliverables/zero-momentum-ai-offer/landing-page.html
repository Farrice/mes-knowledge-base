FAQ

Before you apply.

Is this an AI consulting engagement?

AI may support qualification and drafting, but the product is a repaired revenue handoff. The workflow still makes sense without AI in the headline.

Will it send proposals automatically?

No. Every proposal and external message stays on hold for a named human approver.

Do I need a new CRM?

No. The starting assumption is the tools you already use. The pilot is capped at two core integrations.

What counts as a successful demo?

The agreed test must capture an inquiry, summarize qualification, flag exceptions, draft from approved material, create follow-up and CRM output, and enforce human approval.

Is this right if we have no leads yet?

No. This repairs an existing handoff. It does not create demand or replace lead generation.

What happens after five days?

You receive the working flow, documentation, walkthrough, and 14 days of defect correction for the agreed workflow. Ongoing support is separate and is not assumed.
