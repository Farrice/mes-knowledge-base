The leak

The lead is warm. The handoff is not.

The loss rarely looks dramatic. It looks like copied details, one more clarification email, a proposal waiting on an owner, and follow-up happening whenever somebody remembers.

01 / Fragmented context

Nobody sees one accountable record.

Inquiry details move between email, forms, Slack, notes, and the CRM. The next person starts by reconstructing the story.

02 / Proposal drag

Missing information pauses the buyer.

Required details surface late, after drafting begins. A warm lead waits while the team asks questions it could have flagged earlier.

03 / Unowned follow-up

The next move becomes optional.

No named owner, visible due date, exception path, or approval state means the handoff depends on memory and mood.
