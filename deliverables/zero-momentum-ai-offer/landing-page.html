The problem

The lead is warm. The handoff is not.

When details live across email, forms, notes, and your CRM, the team loses time rebuilding the story. Proposals slow down, follow-up becomes inconsistent, and warm prospects wait.

01 / No clear owner

Everyone can see the lead. Nobody owns the next step.

Questions, notes, and decisions sit in different places while the inquiry waits for somebody to move it forward.

02 / Proposal delay

The proposal starts before the details are ready.

Missing information surfaces late, creating more email, more rework, and another delay for the buyer.

03 / Inconsistent follow-up

The next move depends on memory.

Without a clear owner and due date, follow-up happens late—or not at all.
