## Qualification rule

A name moves from **candidate** to **prospect** only when all are verified:

1. 5–25 employees or a similarly lean delivery team.
2. Existing service inquiries and project proposals.
3. A source-linked trigger more specific than “they are an agency.”
4. An owner or operations lead who can own implementation.
5. No assumption of pain based solely on size or a contact form.

The safest first pass is the 18 closest size/service matches. Rows 24–30 are deliberate negative controls: they may be too small or outside the primary ICP and should not be forced into the list.
