# Website Rebuild Brief

## Failure classification

- **Primary failure:** creative revision degradation.
- **Failed example:** `screenshots/after-desktop-1280.png` and the August 19 user verdict: “straight trash,” “spacing is terrible,” and “no way that this can be a functioning website.”
- **Mechanism:** an editorial asset system was enlarged into a 7,600px conversion page. Display-scale typography, 128px release zones, and poster-like sections replaced navigation, density, interaction, and a usable trust sequence.

## Preservation Lock

- **Keep:** Lead-to-Proposal Proof Sprint offer facts; $1,500 founding pilot; $750 to begin; five-business-day window; human approval before external sending; honest `UNTESTED / NO PAYMENT EVENT` proof state; verified local demo results; canonical Parallax wordmark, palette, and type roles.
- **Change:** every layout decision, the proof-ledger concept, section order, scale hierarchy, spacing rhythm, page density, and CTA experience.
- **Do not disturb:** offer scope, proof boundaries, acceptance-based second payment, negative controls, Briefing Room link repair, local-only/no-deployment boundary.
- **Risk:** mistaking “premium” for either giant empty poster composition or generic SaaS component polish.
- **Gate:** a cold visitor must understand buyer, problem, offer, timeframe, price, proof state, and next action within the first viewport; desktop, tablet, and mobile must be rendered and inspected; the page must have working navigation, FAQ, and demonstration interaction.

## Website job

This is a focused B2B service landing page for agency and consultancy owners who already receive inquiries but lose momentum before proposal and follow-up. It must feel calm, exact, and operator-built. The site earns trust by showing the workflow behaving, not by performing “premium design.”

## Conversion spine

1. **Identity:** This is for my team and names the leak I recognize.
2. **Competence:** I can see the workflow and the human hold working.
3. **Process:** I understand what happens in five days.
4. **Risk:** I know the price, limits, proof state, and acceptance terms.
5. **Action:** I know what to bring to a Workflow Loss Review.

## Acceptance criteria

- Header and primary navigation function at every breakpoint.
- Hero fits comfortably in a standard laptop viewport without swallowing the next section.
- No display headline exceeds 68px desktop or 44px mobile.
- Standard section padding stays between 64px and 96px desktop and 48px and 72px mobile.
- No arbitrary release zone, decorative offset, or empty viewport.
- At least one real interaction demonstrates value.
- One primary CTA treatment, one accent color, no gradients, glows, shadows, pills, or bento wall.
- Page remains useful with motion disabled.
- Booking destination remains explicitly unconnected until Farrice supplies it.

## Replay prompt

Repeat this class of work. Preserve the offer truth, proof boundaries, human approval, and canonical Parallax identity assets. Build from visitor intent and a website-specific density system, not the newsletter cover system. Do not ship unless the first viewport communicates buyer, leak, five-day outcome, price, and action; the workflow demonstration functions; and desktop, tablet, and mobile screenshots survive literal inspection.
