# Lead-to-Proposal Proof Sprint — Offer Canon

## The plain-language offer

I help small agencies and consultancies stop warm leads from stalling between inquiry and proposal. In five business days, I map the handoff, build the working follow-up and proposal workflow in the tools you already use, and prove it on real cases before the final payment is due.

## Buyer

The owner or operations lead of a 5–25-person agency or consultancy that already receives inquiries, produces proposals manually, follows up inconsistently, and does not require sensitive regulated data in this workflow.

## Price and terms

- $1,500 founding pilot.
- $750 paid to begin.
- $750 due only when the agreed demonstration passes.
- No revenue guarantee.
- If the workflow does not pass the agreed acceptance test by the delivery deadline, the client keeps the diagnosis and owes no final balance.
- Three founding pilots, based on delivery capacity rather than invented scarcity.

## Scope

- One team and one lead-to-proposal workflow.
- Up to two core system integrations.
- One existing proposal template.
- Five-business-day build.
- Human approval before any proposal or external message is sent.
- Fourteen-day defect correction for the agreed workflow.

Not included: custom applications, paid-media management, multi-department transformation, ongoing IT support, autonomous sending, legal review, or a revenue guarantee.

## What the client receives

1. Owner and operator workflow interview.
2. Current-state map and dollar/time leakage model using buyer-supplied inputs.
3. Working inquiry-to-qualification-to-proposal workflow.
4. Follow-up schedule and CRM-ready record.
5. Human review and exception path.
6. Ten normal-case tests and eight adversarial controls.
7. Before-and-after demonstration.
8. Owner SOP, walkthrough, and measurement sheet.

## Acceptance test

The workflow must take an approved or deidentified inquiry and:

1. Capture it once without creating a duplicate record.
2. Produce a qualification summary while flagging missing or conflicting information.
3. Draft a proposal only from the client's approved template and supplied facts.
4. Create the agreed follow-up task and CRM-ready record.
5. Hold every external message and proposal for a named human approver.
6. Produce an inspectable run receipt.

Passing the demo means the agreed workflow works. It does not mean the client will close revenue.

## CTA

**Apply for a 20-minute Workflow Loss Review.**

The review identifies one stalled handoff, estimates its cost using the buyer's own inputs, and decides whether a five-day build is appropriate. It is not a free transformation plan.

## Kill gates

- **Validated:** One $750 deposit collected.
- **Promising but unproven:** Qualified calls or proposals occur but no deposit; revise only the dominant objection.
- **Channel problem:** Little qualified response despite clear targeting; change the list or outreach before the offer.
- **Offer-market failure:** Twenty qualified conversations and zero deposits; park this adapter and activate the supplement test.

Testimonials, likes, replies, demonstrations, and completed builds remain separate from collected-payment proof.
