## Message structures

### Warm

Hey [Name], I’m testing a five-day service for small agencies and consultancies where warm leads get stuck between inquiry, qualification, proposal, and follow-up.

I thought of you because [real relationship or workflow context]. I’m not asking you to pretend you have the problem. If one recent inquiry took too long or needed manual rescue, I’d like to map the handoff with you for 20 minutes and tell you whether there is anything worth fixing.

The pilot is $1,500 if there is a fit: $750 to start and $750 after the agreed demo passes. Want me to send the one-page scope?

### Referral

Quick calibration request: do you know an owner or ops lead at a 5–25-person agency or consultancy who already gets warm inquiries but still assembles proposals and follow-up manually?

I built a five-day sprint around that one handoff. I’m looking for three founding pilots, not a broad introduction blast. If someone comes to mind, I can send a two-sentence forwardable note and let them decide.

### Cold, trigger-specific

[Name], I noticed [public fit trigger with source]. That does not prove your team has a follow-up problem, so I won’t pretend it does.

The reason I’m reaching out: firms with active service lines and multiple client handoffs often lose time between a warm inquiry and a reviewable proposal. I build that one workflow in five business days, inside the tools the team already uses, with human approval before anything is sent.

If that handoff is already clean, ignore me. If it still depends on somebody remembering, I can record a five-minute teardown of the public intake path. No access needed.

### Follow-up 1

Closing the loop on this. The useful question is not “do you want AI?” It is: has a qualified inquiry waited because the team had to reconstruct context, ownership, or the proposal from scratch?

If yes, I can show the five-day acceptance test. If no, there is nothing to sell here.

### Follow-up 2

Last note. I’m keeping this pilot bounded to one team, one lead-to-proposal handoff, two integrations, and one proposal template.

If the timing is wrong, no reply needed. If the handoff is a live irritation, send me the words “show the test” and I’ll send the two-minute demonstration.

## Five-minute Loom teardown

1. **Permission boundary:** “This uses only the public intake path. I do not know the internal workflow.”
2. **Observed fact:** show the public contact, booking, or request form.
3. **Possible handoff:** identify where owner, qualification facts, proposal inputs, or follow-up may need a system of record. Say “may,” not “does.”
4. **Test:** show how one synthetic version would be captured, flagged, drafted, and held for approval.
5. **Decision:** name one question the owner can answer: “Can you see who owns the next step and when the proposal is due without asking somebody?”
6. **CTA:** invite a 20-minute Workflow Loss Review. Do not give a free implementation plan.

## Discovery-call scorecard

Score 0, 1, or 2. A viable pilot normally needs 10+ of 14, no regulated-data requirement, and a named owner.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Existing inquiry volume | No active inquiries | Sporadic | Consistent |
| Visible stall | None | Suspected | Recent specific example |
| Proposal process | Already automated | Partly manual | Rebuilt manually each time |
| Follow-up ownership | Clear | Inconsistent | Founder-dependent |
| Tool readiness | No system | One usable tool | CRM + proposal/email tool |
| Decision access | No owner | Influencer | Owner/ops lead present |
| Five-day scope fit | Multi-department/custom app | Needs trimming | One workflow/two integrations |

**Disqualifiers:** no existing leads, wants autonomous sending, requires sensitive regulated data, demands a revenue guarantee, expects custom software, or cannot name an approver.

## Proposal handoff checklist

- Repeat the buyer's specific stalled handoff in their words.
- State one workflow, one team, two integrations, and one template.
- Attach the agreed acceptance test and exception cases.
- State $1,500, $750 start, $750 after demo passes.
- State no revenue guarantee and no autonomous sending.
- Name buyer and Farrice responsibilities.
- Name access prerequisites and deadline clock start.
- Include 14-day defect correction boundary.
- Record dominant objection verbatim if declined.
