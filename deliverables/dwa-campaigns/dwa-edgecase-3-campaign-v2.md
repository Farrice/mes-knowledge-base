```markdown
## Why This Exists

Most DWA posts answer the easy question:

"Can I sell this?"

The better question is:

"What exactly am I getting into after checkout?"

I went through public DWA/MRR review and comment threads. The same buyer questions keep showing up:

- Is this education, resale, affiliate marketing, PLR, or all of the above?
- What can I actually reuse?
- What costs extra?
- What if I do not have an audience?
- Is this just a digital MLM?
- What happens if I buy through the wrong person?
- Will anyone answer me after I buy?

This map is not a pitch. It is the filter I would want before touching the link.

## 1. What Are You Actually Buying?

DWA is best understood as digital marketing education with an affiliate path attached.

Public DWA pages describe training around digital products, affiliate marketing, content, funnels, and online selling. Current public pages also mention community, updates, live calls, content resources, PLR-style assets, and an affiliate program.

Source: https://digitalwealthacademy.com/ and https://rachell.digitalwealthacademy.com/

Do not treat the purchase as a business by itself.

The business is still:

- choosing a buyer;
- creating trust;
- making useful content;
- answering objections;
- routing people through a clear offer path.

## 2. Are You Buying Education Or A Shortcut?

If you are buying because you want structure, this might be worth studying.

If you are buying because you need fast cash, pause.

## 3. What Can You Reuse?

Before buying, ask the seller:

- What assets can I use publicly?
- What assets cannot be reposted?
- Can I edit content vault assets?
- Can I show course screens?
- Can I use sales page screenshots?
- What brand/IP rules apply?

Do not assume course videos can be reposted. Public affiliate discussions around DWA 3.0 specifically distinguish content-vault assets from course video content.

Source signal: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=03P2bpQgnOc

## 4. What Costs Extra?

Potential setup stack:

- domain;
- email address;
- payment processor;
- funnel page;
- email follow-up tool;
- content design tools;
- time to post and answer comments;
- possible bonus or support assets if you want to stand out.

Do not buy unless you know whether these are included, optional, or your responsibility.

## 5. What If You Have No Audience?

If you have no audience, your first job is not reselling.

Your first job is proving one of these:

- you can write useful posts for one specific buyer;
- people comment with real questions;
- people reply to your DMs;
- you can explain the offer without income claims;
- you can keep a 14-day content test alive.

If you cannot do that yet, study first.

## 6. Is This Just A Digital MLM?

The clean answer:

- If you sell income dreams, it will feel MLM-ish.
- If you hide the affiliate connection, it will feel MLM-ish.
- If your only product is "buy this so you can sell this," it will feel MLM-ish.
- If you explain the education, risks, fit, costs, and alternatives, you create a cleaner path.

I do not recommend selling DWA as "join me and make money."

## 7. Should You Sell DWA Or Build Your Own Product?

Use this fork:

- If you need education: study first.
- If you already have an audience: affiliate test may make sense.
- If you have a specific skill: build your own small product first.
- If you have no buyer, no content habit, and no budget cushion: skip for now.

## 8. What Should You Ask The Seller?

Before asking for a link, ask:

1. What changes in the current version?
2. What can I reuse publicly?
3. What cannot be reposted?
4. What costs extra after checkout?
5. Is there support if I get stuck?
6. What happens if I buy through the wrong affiliate?
7. Is there a refund?
8. What payment options exist in my country?
9. How would you use it if you were starting with no audience?
10. What would make you tell me not to buy?

## 9. My Fit Buckets

### Skip

You need fast money, cannot afford the purchase, or want an income promise.

### Study

You want structured digital marketing education but should not sell yet.

### Build

You should make a smaller product from your own skill before promoting DWA.

### Affiliate Test

You understand the risks, have an audience or a clear angle, and can run a no-income-claim content test.

## 10. My Recommendation

Do not ask me for the DWA link first.

Ask me which bucket you are in.

If you are not a fit, I will tell you.
```

## Launch Debugger V2

Primary risk is no longer only hype. It is trust contradiction.

Risk table:

| Risk | Why It Matters | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Scam-hook contradiction | Calling it scammy while selling it weakens trust. | Use buyer-question hook instead. |
| No-audience buyer | They want to buy the answer before building demand. | Send no-audience decision tree before link. |
| Content-reuse confusion | People may assume they can repost assets they cannot use. | Add reuse-rights checklist. |
| Payment/support anxiety | Questions often arise after purchase. | Make support boundaries explicit. |
| Income-claim contamination | Other promoters make claims around the product. | Do not repeat or imply them. |
| Faceless trust tax | No face means less social proof. | Use public clarity, diagrams, and transparent fit checks. |

Publishing approval:

- Approved for draft testing if using `QUESTIONS`, not `MAP`, as the keyword.
- Do not use "scam?" as the main hook.
- Do not send the affiliate link until the buyer chooses a question category.
- Do not promise support you do not want to provide.
- Do not mention DWA 3.0 as official unless you can show official/backend proof.
