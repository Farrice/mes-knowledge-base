## What V3 Got Wrong

V3 made Farrice look careful.

It did not make the right buyer feel enough forward motion.

The Buyer Question Map was useful, but it asked people to think harder before it helped them decide. That created trust, then parked the buyer in analysis.

V4 changes the main asset from:

```text
Here are the questions to ask before buying.
```

to:

```text
Here is how to decide your next move, and what your first 7 days would look like if DWA is actually the right move.
```

The questions stay, but they move into the appendix.

## Public Claim Rules

Allowed:

- DWA is positioned publicly as digital marketing education with digital product, affiliate marketing, content, funnel, community, PLR/content resource, and affiliate components.
- Farrice is an affiliate and may earn a commission.
- Results vary.
- Public DWA terms say sales are final/non-refundable.
- The asset can help a buyer decide whether to wait, study, build, or consider DWA.

Avoid:

- urgent-money language;
- payback language;
- one-sale payoff language;
- comeback/trend language;
- saturation arguments;
- fast-result faceless language;
- resale-first commands;
- income-path wording;
- screenshots or implied result claims you cannot verify and properly disclose.

## New Free Asset

```markdown
# The DWA Ready-or-Not Map

A 15-minute fit test for deciding whether DWA is a smart next step, a study-only purchase, or something to skip for now.

Disclosure: I am a DWA affiliate. If you buy through my link, I may earn a commission. This is not an income promise, and I do not recommend DWA for every buyer.

## Why I Made This

I have gone back and forth on DWA.

The course has useful material in it.

The way it often gets sold online is the part I do not want to copy.

Too much of the public pitch sounds like:

"buy this, resell this, trust me."

That is not a plan.

A better question is:

"If I bought this, what would I actually do with it in week one?"

That is what this map answers.

By the end, you should know which lane fits you:

- Wait
- Study
- Build your own small offer first
- Consider DWA with eyes open

## 1. The Real DWA Fork

Before you think about a link, name what you are actually trying to buy.

Choose the closest one:

### A. I want digital marketing structure

You want help understanding content, offers, funnels, affiliate marketing, and digital products.

This is the cleanest reason to study DWA.

### B. I want a product I can promote

You are interested in the affiliate/resale side.

This can be legitimate only if you are willing to build trust, explain fit, disclose clearly, and avoid income promises.

### C. I want assets I can use

You care about templates, PLR/content resources, or examples.

Your first question should be what you can and cannot reuse publicly.

### D. I want a shortcut

Pause.

If you need the purchase to solve urgent money pressure, I would not start here.

## 2. The Ready-or-Not Score

Score each line from 0 to 2.

0 = not true yet
1 = somewhat true
2 = true enough to act

| Readiness Signal | Score |
|---|---:|
| I know who I would talk to online | 0 / 1 / 2 |
| I can post for 14 days without disappearing | 0 / 1 / 2 |
| I can explain DWA without income claims | 0 / 1 / 2 |
| I can disclose that I am an affiliate without feeling weird | 0 / 1 / 2 |
| I can afford the purchase without panic | 0 / 1 / 2 |
| I am willing to ask support/reuse questions before promoting | 0 / 1 / 2 |
| I can handle hearing "this is not a fit yet" | 0 / 1 / 2 |

### Score 0-5: Wait

Do not buy yet.

Your first move is a no-cost proof test:

- pick one buyer;
- write 5 posts;
- answer real questions;
- see if you can stay consistent without income language.

### Score 6-9: Study

DWA may make sense as education, but selling should wait.

Your first move is to study the structure and build your own buyer clarity before promoting anything.

### Score 10-12: Build

You may be better off turning your existing skill, service, or lived experience into a small product first.

DWA might support your marketing thinking, but it should not replace your own offer.

### Score 13-14: Consider DWA

You are the group I would move into a purchase conversation.

Not because results are promised.

Because you already have enough readiness to use the material with judgment.

## 3. The First 7 Days If You Buy

If you buy DWA, do not start by posting "who wants the link?"

Start here.

### Day 1: Orientation

Go through the current course structure and write down what you are actually there to learn:

- digital product creation;
- affiliate marketing;
- content;
- funnels;
- PLR/content resources;
- audience building;
- support/community.

### Day 2: Reuse Rules

Before posting anything, clarify:

- what assets you can use;
- what assets you cannot repost;
- whether sales-page screenshots are allowed;
- how content-vault resources differ from course material;
- what brand/IP rules apply.

### Day 3: Buyer Choice

Pick one buyer.

Not "people who want money."

Pick a real person with a real situation:

- new digital marketer;
- burned-out beginner;
- faceless creator;
- service provider wanting a small digital offer;
- parent trying to learn online selling without becoming a hype account.

### Day 4: Three No-Claim Posts

Write three posts that sell clarity, not income:

1. what DWA is and is not;
2. what to ask before buying;
3. what your first 7 days would look like if you bought.

### Day 5: DM Answer Bank

Prepare clean answers for:

- Is this MLM-ish?
- What can I reuse?
- What costs extra?
- Can I do this without an audience?
- What happens after checkout?
- Why buy through you?

### Day 6: Support Check

Ask the affiliate or community what support exists for:

- setup;
- content;
- reuse rules;
- payment/country issues;
- community access;
- current-version changes.

### Day 7: Decide Your Lane

After one week, choose:

- Study only;
- Build your own offer first;
- Run a 14-day no-claim affiliate test;
- Stop and revisit later.

## 4. What I Would Help With If You Buy Through Me

I am not offering unlimited coaching.

If you buy through me, the useful support I can provide is directional:

- sort your fit bucket;
- help you avoid income-claim positioning;
- map your first 7 days;
- review your first no-claim post angle;
- help you decide whether to study, build, or affiliate-test.

That is the guide role I am comfortable owning.

## 5. The Questions Appendix

Ask these before checkout:

1. What changed in the current version?
2. What can I reuse publicly?
3. What cannot be reposted?
4. What costs extra after checkout?
5. What support exists if I get stuck?
6. What happens if I buy through the wrong affiliate?
7. Is there a refund?
8. What payment options exist in my country?
9. How would I use this if I am starting with no audience?
10. What would make this a bad fit?
11. What parts are education?
12. What parts are affiliate marketing?
13. What parts are PLR/content resources?
14. What do I still need to build myself?
15. What can I say publicly without misleading people?

## My Recommendation

Do not ask me for the link first if you are still in panic mode.

Ask for the map.

If your score puts you in Study or Consider DWA, I can help you map the first 7 days.

If that path still makes sense, I can send the link with the disclosure and final-sale reminder before you buy.
```
