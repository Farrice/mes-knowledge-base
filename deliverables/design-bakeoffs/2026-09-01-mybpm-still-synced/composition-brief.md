# Sealed Composition Brief

## Candidate A

- Leverage: the cropped `SYNCED` title owns the frame.
- Rhythm: punch → slow → pull → release.
- Grid: asymmetric structure with one intentional break.
- Movement: the EKG trace moves through the title rather than decorating beneath it.
- Friction: controlled crop and overlap; never at the cost of campaign recognition.
- Release: quiet brand lockup and data line after the visual peak.
- Transfer: recomposed for each aspect ratio; no simple scale-and-stack.

## Candidate B

- Leverage: centered campaign title owns the frame.
- Rhythm: stable, even, and immediately legible.
- Grid: symmetrical modular stack.
- Movement: EKG trace sits as a clear supporting divider.
- Friction: minimal.
- Release: brand and campaign data resolve in a conventional footer.
- Transfer: the same hierarchy is responsively restacked across formats.
