## About

```text
People do not talk about supplements the way supplement ads do.

I learned that behind a Vitamin Shoppe counter, then heard it for another 18 years as a personal trainer.

Is this going to make me jittery?
Why does this cost $20 more than the one beside it?
I stopped taking the last one after a week.

Those questions are where I start. The ad library comes later.

I work as the creative strategist between your product page and your ad account. I find the buyer problem worth building around, choose the claim the brand can honestly support, write the hooks, and turn the decision into testable creative.

The first way to hire me is the Control-Beater.

$500, prepaid. Delivered in 72 hours.

You send your current best-performing ad and the product assets. I deliver five static ads and one video concept to test against that control. Every claim is screened against published Meta health policy and FTC guidance.

If Meta rejects one of my ads on policy review, I fix or replace it free without restarting the clock.

Sometimes the message is not ready for six ads. If the product is still trying to sell every benefit at once, I will not spend 72 hours making prettier confusion.

We settle the campaign angle first. Three named directions, the hooks and proof each one needs, the claim boundary, and a straight answer on which one I would build. Then the winner becomes creative.

The market decides whether an ad wins. My job is to give you a clean test, grounded in how the buyer actually talks and what the product can honestly prove.

DM PROOF with the product and the ad you are tired of looking at.
```
