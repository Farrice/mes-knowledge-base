# Farrice Cain LinkedIn OS deployment readout

This draft deployment is complete. It contains a profile conversion architecture, a ranked bank of twenty sourced ideas, ten finished draft posts with three hook candidates each, a human taste-test packet, a source ledger, and an empty results ledger that refuses to turn preparation into performance evidence.

The public baseline came from [Farrice Cain's LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/farricecain/). The recent search snapshot showed 65 followers, eight visible posts, one article, broad services, thin Experience detail, and several competing AI, coaching, and content offers. Those counts may lag the live account. The strategic diagnosis does not depend on the exact count: the visible posts carry more authority than the profile converts.

The proposed profile gives every surface one job. The headline explains the 18-year buyer-side crossover, the deliverable, the speed, the Meta-review guarantee, and DM PROOF. The banner owns the risk reversal. The About explains the Control-Beater, Angle Map, and Proof-to-Market Sprint as one ladder. Featured holds the smallest offer, the strategy door, and public-surface proof. The coaching and Vitamin Shoppe entries supply different credibility instead of repeating the About story.

The content block stays inside one semantic lane: buyer truth, campaign decisions, and claim-safe creative for supplement and performance brands. Four Growth posts use Transparent Labs, Momentous, Create Wellness, and Puori. Four Authority posts cover counter-side judgment, belief diversity, claim-safe angle design, and the all-in-one message problem. One Conversion post explains the Control-Beater without invented results. One Personal post explains the confidence behind Farrice's early supplement buying without turning the account into a journal.

The current all-in-one post was checked against official pages for [DIZZY Daily Flow](https://www.dizzysupps.com/shop), [Isagenix WITHIN](https://www.isagenix.com/en-us/shop/within), and [Attaboy](https://attaboylabs.com/pages/hp5). The four teardown posts use dated source captures and explicitly avoid claims about internal strategy or business performance.

Diandra's operating logic is active throughout. The profile comes before the calendar. Twenty ideas carry a source, bucket, north star, attention score, time score, combined rank, and status. Every post declares one job and one primary metric. Bodies were written before hooks. The selected hooks satisfy the mobile line limits. The first ten results will change one variable for the next block rather than trigger a new positioning debate.

All ten post bodies and the profile About pass the deterministic anti-slop classifier. The voice evaluator found no deterministic tell-class hits. The claim-risk scan is clean. The runtime, ranking, schema, and empty-ledger rejection checks pass. The headline is 184 characters and the About is 1,905 characters.

This is ready for Farrice's human taste test. It is not approved for publication. The external state remains zero published posts, zero measured leads, zero collected revenue, and no permission to edit the profile, comment, DM, schedule, or publish.
