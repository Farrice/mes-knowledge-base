# About v2: copy-ready tension pass

I spent years behind a Vitamin Shoppe counter. People did not talk like supplement ads.

They asked if the pills were too big. Whether something mixed with their medication. Why this tub cost $20 more than the one beside it.

Then I spent 18 years training the same buyer.

That is where I learned the problem most supplement creative never solves. A formula can be excellent and the label can survive review. The buyer can still shrug.

That shrug gets expensive.

Brands answer it with more hooks and another batch of edits. But twenty versions of an undecided message are still one weak idea.

I find the belief standing between the formula and the sale. Then I build the campaign around what the brand can honestly prove.

For supplement, recovery, and performance brands, there are three ways to hire me:

Angle Map | $750 | 48 hours

Send the campaign brief, product page, and approved claims. You get three named campaign angles, hook directions, proof direction, a claim-safety line for each, and my verdict on which one to build first. If the first read does not produce three usable angles inside the agreed scope, I keep working until it does.

Control-Beater | $500 | 72 hours

Already know the message? Send your current best-performing ad and product assets. You get five statics and one video to test against the current control. I screen every claim against published Meta health policy and FTC guidance. If Meta rejects one on policy review, I fix or replace it free without restarting the clock.

Proof-to-Market Sprint | $2,500 | 10 business days

For a launch, reformulation, retail push, or new ingredient that needs the whole message settled. You get positioning, proof hierarchy, a campaign-angle bank, launch messaging, and one priority campaign asset.

Claims screening is editorial, not legal advice. Your qualified reviewer keeps final approval. I do not promise revenue, invent science, or say a spec ad beat a control before it did.

DM PROOF with the product and the ad you are tired of looking at. I will tell you which door fits.
