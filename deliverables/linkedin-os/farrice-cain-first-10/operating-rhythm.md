# Operating rhythm

## Every two weeks

- Add 20 ideas.
- Score attention potential and time to create from 1-5.
- Reject ideas below 6 unless strategic judgment overrides the score with a note.

## Every post

- Draft the body before the hook.
- Create three hook options.
- Use one dopamine-density device only when the source or proof supports it.
- Record the result in posts.csv after publishing.

## Every 10 posts

- Run review.
- Inspect the top posts by follower efficiency and depth rate.
- Change one variable for the next block; preserve the rest.

## Every 100 posts

- Keep the top 10 patterns.
- Retire weak topic-hook-format combinations.
- Start the next 100-post learning cycle.
