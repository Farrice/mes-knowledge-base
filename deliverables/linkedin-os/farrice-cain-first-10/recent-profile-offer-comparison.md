## Drift and contradictions to resolve—not silently blend

1. **Primary offer drift:** July made the $750 Angle Map the protected public entry. August introduced the $500 Control-Beater as a co-equal or cheaper first door. Current v2 orders the offers correctly but still presents three visible choices.
2. **CTA drift:** `DM ANGLE` fit the Angle Map-only position; `DM PROOF` fit the broader ladder. The final keyword should follow the offer hierarchy, not personal preference in isolation.
3. **Tenure conflict:** historical files say both three and five years at Vitamin Shoppe. “Spent years” is the only safe current wording until the number is verified.
4. **Role drift:** July positioned Farrice primarily as the person who chooses the claim and campaign angle. August added direct ad production. The profile needs one strategic owner; production should read as a route downstream, not a second identity.
5. **Demand boundary:** these are progressively refined draft systems, not market validation. The recovered sessions recorded no sent offers, held calls, sales, or collected revenue for the Angle Map at the time.
