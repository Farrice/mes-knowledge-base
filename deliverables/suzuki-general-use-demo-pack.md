## 1. Use Now: Non-DWA `$200` Ebook Test

### Intent Lock

Product: `The 5-Day Client Signal Book`
Price: `$200`
Buyer: solo consultants and fractional operators who need better client-acquisition content but do not want cold outreach scripts.
Platform: X first, with Threads and email reuse.
Proof available: table of contents, 3 sample signals, 1 before/after post rewrite.
Proof missing: buyer testimonials, sales history, niche-by-niche case studies.

### Buyer Readiness Diagnosis

| Lane | Signal | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Cold | Likes the idea but has no market picked | Send `SIGNAL`, ask for niche |
| Warm | Has a niche and wants better posts/emails | Send worked example and first 24-hour plan |
| Ready | Asks for price, table of contents, or checkout | Run fit check, then send checkout by permission |
| Wrong fit | Wants instant clients without research or posting | Send no-cost signal collection task |

### X Post

```text

a $200 ebook for consultants who want to turn market signals into:

- 7 posts from real buyer frustration
- 5 email openings tied to active pain
- 3 sales-call prompts
- 1 weekly signal review

The free version is a 20-signal checklist plus one worked example.

Comment SIGNAL and I will send the checklist.

Already considering the ebook? Comment MAP and I will send the fit check before the checkout link.
```

### Purchase-Adjacent Free Asset

`20-Signal Fit Map`

It includes:

- 20 buyer signals to scan this week;
- a 0-2 fit score;
- one worked example;
- first 24 hours after buying the ebook.

Why it works: it lets cold buyers test the method, lets warm buyers see the first implementation, and lets ready buyers ask for checkout without pressure.

### DM Routing

| Intent | DM |
|---|---|
| Cold | `Sending it. Start with the score, then reply with your niche. If you do not have a niche yet, I would collect signals before buying anything.` |
| Warm | `Your score is enough to test. Use the worked example first. If you want the full 5-day map, I can send the checkout page after one fit check.` |
| Ready | `I can send the checkout link. Quick fit check first: do you have a niche, can you post for 5 days, and can you use the ebook without needing a done-for-you service?` |
| Wrong fit | `I would not buy yet. Your first move is to collect 10 raw buyer complaints and turn one into a post.` |

### Paid Path

1. Post with `SIGNAL` and `MAP`.
2. DM the free map.
3. Ask for niche and score.
4. Send checkout only after fit check.
5. Day 1 email: first signal exercise.
6. Day 3 email: before/after post rewrite.
7. Day 7 email: sample page, FAQ, fit reminder.

### First 24 Hours After Purchase

1. Pick one market.
2. Collect 10 raw buyer signals.
3. Score three strongest signals.
4. Turn the strongest signal into one post.
5. Turn the same signal into one email opening.
6. Save one call prompt.

### 7-Day Replay Sprint

| Day | Asset | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Main ebook post | SIGNAL / MAP |
| 2 | Before/after post rewrite | SIGNAL |
| 3 | 3 signals consultants miss | SIGNAL |
| 4 | Sample page proof post | MAP |
| 5 | Objection: "Will this work in my niche?" | NICHE |
| 6 | First 24-hour path | PLAN |
| 7 | Recap and checkout reminder | MAP |

### Floor Verdict

Pass.

This is not DWA-shaped. It has a specific buyer, concrete ebook proof, value lane, purchase lane, DM routing, checkout path, first-use path, and replay plan.

## Universal Suzuki Floor Checklist

Before any output ships, check:

| Gate | Pass Question |
|---|---|
| Buyer | Can the right buyer recognize themselves? |
| Proof | Is the proof concrete or clearly missing? |
| Asset | Does the free asset move the buyer toward a decision? |
| CTA | Are value and purchase intent separated? |
| DM | Are cold, warm, ready, and wrong-fit buyers routed differently? |
| Price | Does the paid path match risk and trust? |
| First use | Does the buyer know what happens after purchase? |
| Replay | Can the launch continue after one post? |

## Final Verdict

The raised floor holds outside DWA.

The general mechanism is:

```text
specific buyer hook -> concrete proof -> purchase-adjacent asset -> value/purchase lanes -> routed DM -> checkout/application/conversation -> first-use path -> replay sprint
```

DWA remains one fixture. It is not the OS.
