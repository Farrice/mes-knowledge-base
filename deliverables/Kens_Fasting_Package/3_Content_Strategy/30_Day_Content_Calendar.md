## Posting Times (Optimize for Your Audience)

| Audience | Best Times | Why |
|----------|-----------|-----|
| **Night shift workers** | 6-8 AM (post-shift scrolling), 6-8 PM (pre-shift prep) | They're awake when most people aren't |
| **Day shift / professionals** | 7-8 AM (commute), 12-1 PM (lunch), 7-9 PM (evening wind-down) | Standard engagement windows |
| **General** | Start with 6 AM and 6 PM, then check your Instagram Insights after 2 weeks and adjust | Data beats assumptions |

---

## Monthly Review Checklist

At the end of each month:

- [ ] Which 3 Reels got the most views? **Make more like those.**
- [ ] Which content got the most saves? **That's your highest-value content — double down.**
- [ ] Which content got the most comments? **Those topics resonate — explore deeper.**
- [ ] How many DMs did you get about the product? **Track conversion.**
- [ ] What did people ask most in DMs/comments? **That's next month's content.**
- [ ] How many $7 sales this month? How many upsells? **The only numbers that matter.**
