## The One Decision You Need to Make Before Starting

Before you build anything, you need to choose your **brand positioning**. This isn't complicated — it's one choice with three options:

### Option A: Transparent Guide (Low Risk)
Share your personal journey. "How I learned to fast during night shifts." Humble, authentic, easy to defend. You never claim to be a guru — you're a nurse who figured something out and wants to share it.

### Option B: Mindset Guru (Higher Risk)
Frame fasting as identity transformation. "Fasting is a rite of passage." Aspirational and motivating, but harder to defend if someone challenges your claims.

### Option C: Hybrid (Recommended)
**This is what we recommend.** Build the product as a Responsible Guide — safety-first, transparent, no snake oil. But market it with aspirational language that makes people feel like they're joining something bigger. The foundation is responsible. The feeling is transformational.

**Our recommendation: Go with Option C.** You get the credibility of transparency with the energy of aspiration.
