# 48-Hour First Sale Sprint Plan

*Based on the Maria Wendt Digital Product Mastery system. Your exact playbook to go from zero to first $7 sale in one weekend.*

---

## The Target Customer

**Who they are:** People who want to fast for weight loss but constantly fail on Day 2 or 3 because of headaches, cravings, and nighttime binges.

**What they say (their exact words):** "I keep trying to do 16:8 or 24-hour fasts but I get horrible headaches and binge eat at night. How do you survive the hunger?"

**What they want to become:** Shift their identity from "I can't stick to a fast" to "I completed my first 72 hours smoothly without crashing."

---

## The 48-Hour Timeline

### Hours 1-2: Build the Product (60 Minutes)

- [ ] Open Google Docs
- [ ] Create a simple 3-page document. No videos. No fancy design. Just clear, actionable content.
- [ ] **Name it:** "The Night Shift Fast: A Nurse's Guide to Fasting When Your Schedule Says You Can't" (or whichever niche you chose from the Niche Variations doc)

**What goes on each page:**

**Page 1: The Hydration Code**
- The basic mineral balance (sodium/potassium/magnesium) to prevent fasting headaches
- How much water to drink and when
- What you CAN drink during a fast (black coffee, water, plain tea)
- What to avoid (anything with calories, artificial sweeteners during the fast)

**Page 2: The "Binge-Breaker" Meal Formula**
- What macro split to eat for your first meal to prevent the evening binge
- The 3 worst foods to eat when breaking a fast (and why they trigger cravings)
- Your actual meal examples — what YOU eat to break your fast on shift

**Page 3: The 3-Day Shift Checklist**
- A simple daily checklist: water intake, salt, mental reframing
- Day 1: What to expect (hunger peaks, energy dips, how to push through)
- Day 2: The hardest day — specific strategies for the 2 PM / 2 AM crash
- Day 3: The corner — what it feels like when your body adapts
- The Eject Button: when to stop (non-negotiable safety signs)

**Important:** Add a disclaimer at the top: "This is my personal experience as a nurse. It is not medical advice. Consult your physician before starting any fasting protocol."

- [ ] Export to PDF
- [ ] Done. Move on. Don't perfect it.

### Hour 3: Set Up Checkout (30 Minutes)

- [ ] Go to **Gumroad.com** (free to set up, they take a small percentage per sale)
- [ ] Create a product listing
- [ ] Upload your PDF
- [ ] Set price: **$7**
- [ ] Add this checkout page copy:

> "Stop giving up on your fast at 8 PM because of headaches, shakes, and cravings. The Night Shift Fast gives you the exact hydration protocol and meal structure to get through the first 72 hours without crashing. Built by a nurse who fasts through 12-hour night shifts. By day 4, you'll wake up with more energy, zero cravings, and the confidence that you can actually do this."

- [ ] **Optional checkout bump:** "Add the 30-Day Shift Fasting Protocol for $37 (50% off)" — you can set this up later if you don't have the upsell product ready yet
- [ ] Test the checkout — buy your own product to make sure it works
- [ ] Refund yourself

### Hours 4-5: Find Your First Prospects (1-2 Hours)

**Where to look:**

**Reddit:**
- Go to **r/intermittentfasting** (1.1M members)
- Search for: "headache," "failed," "binge," "night shift," "nurse," "too hungry to sleep"
- Filter by "New" or "This Week"
- Find 10-15 posts from people describing exactly this problem

**Facebook:**
- Search for groups: "intermittent fasting beginners," "fasting for weight loss," "nurse health and wellness"
- Join 3-5 groups
- Find frustrated posts from the last 7 days

- [ ] Make a simple list: Name, Platform, Link to their post, Their specific problem
- [ ] Aim for 10-15 people minimum

### Hours 6-48: Send DMs and Close Your First Sale

- [ ] Send personalized DMs to each person using the scripts below
- [ ] Respond to replies within 1-2 hours
- [ ] Answer questions genuinely — don't be salesy
- [ ] Goal: 1 sale out of 10-15 DMs = success

## After Your First Sale

### What to Do Immediately

1. **Screenshot the sale notification.** This is your first proof. You'll use this in content later.
2. **Follow up in 24 hours** with the message above.
3. **Ask for a testimonial on Day 4-5:** "Hey, how's it going? If the checklist helped, would you mind sharing a quick line about your experience? I'm building this out to help more people."

### What This $7 Sale Means

This $7 isn't about revenue. It's about **proof of concept.**

- Someone paid real money for what you know
- You validated that the problem is real and people will pay to solve it
- You now have a customer you can learn from (what worked? what didn't? what questions did they have?)
- You have the beginning of a testimonial pipeline
- You have the warmest possible lead for your $47-$97 upsell

### The Upsell Path

Once someone buys the $7 checklist and succeeds:
- Day 4 email: "Did you survive? Here's what to do next."
- Offer the 30-Day Protocol ($47-$97)
- This customer already trusts you. The conversion rate on warm upsells is 10-20x higher than cold traffic.
