# DM Outreach Scripts — Reddit + Facebook

---

## The Strategy

You're not "selling" in DMs. You're finding people who already have the problem, offering genuine help, and pointing them to a $7 solution. The product sells itself once you find the right person.

**Target:** People who want to fast for weight loss but keep failing on Day 2 or 3 because of headaches, cravings, and nighttime binges.

**Their exact words:** "I keep trying to do 16:8 or 24-hour fasts but I get horrible headaches and binge eat at night. How do you survive the hunger?"

## Outreach Rules

1. **Only DM people who posted about this problem in the last 7 days.** Old posts = cold leads.
2. **Personalize every message.** Reference their specific post, their specific words. Never copy-paste without editing.
3. **Don't sell in the first message.** Offer help. Let them ask for the link.
4. **One follow-up max.** If they don't respond to the first message, move on. Don't be that person.
5. **Keep a simple spreadsheet.** Track: Name, Platform, Date Sent, Response, Purchased (Y/N). This is your pipeline.
6. **Goal: 10-15 DMs per day for the first week.** Not forever — just enough to validate the product and get your first reviews.
