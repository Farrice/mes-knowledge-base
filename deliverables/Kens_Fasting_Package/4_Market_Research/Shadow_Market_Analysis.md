## What's a Shadow Market?

A shadow market exists when there's **high desperation** (people desperately want a solution) combined with **low competition quality** (the existing solutions are garbage). It's the most profitable place to launch a digital product because the demand already exists — you're not creating desire, you're fulfilling it.

### Desperation Level: Very High

Nurses and shift workers face a unique combination of health challenges that make traditional diet advice useless:

| Challenge | Why It Makes Fasting Hard |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| **Circadian disruption** | Your body doesn't know when "morning" is. Meal timing advice based on daylight is irrelevant. |
| **Vending machine traps** | The only food available at 2 AM is junk. You can't meal prep for every scenario. |
| **Irregular meal breaks** | Some shifts you get a real break. Some you eat standing up in 3 minutes. |
| **Stress eating patterns** | High-stress work (psych nursing, ER, ICU) drives cortisol-based eating. |
| **Metabolic syndrome risk** | Shift workers have significantly higher rates of obesity, diabetes, and heart disease. |
| **Social isolation** | You can't do "family dinner at 6 PM" when you work nights. Social eating norms don't apply. |

**These people are DESPERATE for someone who gets their reality.** Every generic fasting guide assumes you wake up at 7 AM, work 9-5, and eat dinner at 6 PM. That's not their life.
