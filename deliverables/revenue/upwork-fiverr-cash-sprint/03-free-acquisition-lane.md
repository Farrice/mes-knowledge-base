**Status:** Ready for proof-link insertion and manual approval before any send. No outreach has been sent.

## The lane

Sell one fixed starter offer through researched, one-to-one email:

**Source-backed health carousel: $200**

- One 6–8-slide educational carousel
- Topic and public-source research
- Slide copy, caption, CTA, and source notes
- Editable Canva link plus PDF or PNG exports
- One revision
- Four-day delivery after requirements are complete

This is not medical, legal, or regulatory review. The brand approves all final product and health claims.

## Why this lane comes first

It costs no proposal credits, reaches brands during a real launch or visibility event, and lets the finished health carousel function as proof instead of waiting for marketplace discovery. Run five researched messages before opening another platform.
