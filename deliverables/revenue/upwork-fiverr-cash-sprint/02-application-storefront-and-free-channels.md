### Project summary

Health content often fails in one of two places: the science gets flattened until the post says nothing, or the copy makes claims the source material cannot support.

I create premium educational carousels for supplement, wellness, fitness, and expert-led health brands. Each project combines topic research, slide-by-slide copy, visual structure, and Canva design.

You receive a clear hook, a coherent six-to-ten-slide narrative, a caption and CTA, an editable Canva link, and export files. Source-backed projects also include the public references used and a note identifying claims that need brand approval.

This service fits product education, founder authority, myth correction, mechanism explainers, and evidence-led brand content. It does not include medical, legal, or regulatory approval. The client remains responsible for approving final product and health claims.

The gallery examples are labeled as portfolio or speculative work when they were not commissioned by a client.

### Work steps

1. **Research and claim map:** Review the approved topic, supplied materials, audience, and permissible claims.
2. **Narrative script:** Build the hook, slide sequence, caption, CTA, and source notes.
3. **Design and delivery:** Apply the approved visual direction in Canva and provide the editable link plus PDF or PNG exports.

Do not add client requirements, review, or approval as work steps; Upwork already handles those stages.

### Client requirements

Mark requirements 1–5 as mandatory.

1. Who is the audience, and what should they understand or do after reading the carousel?
2. What topic, product, or offer should the carousel cover? Attach product pages, labels, studies, briefs, or internal source material.
3. Which health or product claims has your brand already approved? List anything that must be included or avoided.
4. Upload the brand guide, logo, fonts, colors, and any images you have permission to use.
5. Confirm that you have the right to use every file and brand asset supplied for this project.
6. Share two or three visual references and explain what you like about them. Optional.
7. State the destination platform, CTA, export preference, and requested Canva access. Optional.

### Frequently asked questions

**Do you research the topic?**  
Yes. I can work from your approved sources and supplement them with credible public references. Product-specific claims still require your approval.

**Is this medical or regulatory review?**  
No. I provide editorial research and claim discipline, not medical, legal, or regulatory approval.

**Can you match an existing visual identity?**  
Yes, when you supply a brand guide, fonts, colors, and examples. I will not copy another creator's design.

**Will I receive the Canva file?**  
Yes. Each package includes an editable Canva link plus the agreed export files.

**What counts as a revision?**  
A revision adjusts the agreed topic, copy, or design direction. A new topic, new audience, or complete concept reset requires an add-on or custom offer.

**Can I order copy without design?**  
Yes. Request a custom copy-only offer before purchasing.
