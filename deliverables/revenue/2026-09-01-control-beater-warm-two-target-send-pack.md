## Why these do not read like cold pitches

The opening earns attention with a true observation about the recipient’s current commercial problem. The second line gives a useful tension they can recognize even if they never buy. Farrice’s authority is stated narrowly and truthfully: he knows how supplement buyers hear category claims. The offer then stays small, concrete, price-visible, and easy to decline.

The humor is intentionally light. It comes from recognizing the absurdity inside the work, not from performing a joke at a stranger.
