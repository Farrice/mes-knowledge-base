## Decision rules

- A reply counts as `WANTED` only if the buyer has seen the $500 scope and asks about fit, scope, price, or the next step.
- Interest without a current control is useful discovery, not pilot qualification.
- Verbal acceptance is `SOLD`, not `COLLECTED`.
- Only cleared $500 payment changes the primary metric.
- Silence from one target does not authorize another offer rewrite.
- Diagnose the funnel after 10 qualified, price-visible exposures. Do not kill or materially redesign the offer until 20 qualified fit conversations have presented the exact offer with zero purchases.
