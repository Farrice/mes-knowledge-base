## V01: Problem-Solution VSL

### Primary script

Give me 75 seconds and I'll show you the four decisions I want made before another creative round.

First: what does the customer already care about? I look for repeated language in reviews, support tickets, surveys, and the ads that already ran.

Second: what should the next concept ask that person to believe? One round should contain genuinely different arguments, not a pile of new openings around the same promise.

Third: what proof can the brand responsibly use? The approved-claims file sets the ceiling. Missing evidence stays out of the script.

Fourth: what can the creator and editor actually make? The concept has to become a hook, spoken copy, shot direction, and a clean test cell.

In my five-day Paid Social Creative Test Sprint, those decisions become two campaign concepts, 12 hooks, four creator-ready scripts, one production brief, and one test matrix.

Your team keeps production and media buying. I give them a researched direction for the next round.

Send me SPRINT if you want the input checklist.

### Alternate openings

- A full content calendar can still leave the media team with nothing new to learn.
- Before you ask for 10 more ads, name the two beliefs you want to test.

## A01: Amateur Investigation

### Primary script

I followed one public Instagram Stories landing path to the Momentous Creatine page and mapped what the creative has to prove before the buy button.

Account data is private, so this is a message read. There is no performance verdict here.

Observation: the page repeatedly emphasizes purity, mixability, a six-stage testing protocol, and third-party certifications.

My inference: the page asks the buyer to see creatine quality as a manufacturing and verification decision, rather than treating every powder as interchangeable.

The open creative question is which reason should lead the ad: the drinking experience, purity controls, or trust in verification?

From there, I would separate the reasons into distinct concepts, hold every product statement to the brand's evidence, and give the production team a clean test for each.

That is the work inside my five-day Creative Test Sprint. Send me SPRINT if you want the one-product input checklist.

### Alternate openings

- I cannot see this brand's ad account. I can still show you the message decisions visible on the page.
- One creatine page is carrying three different reasons to believe. I would not test them as one idea.
