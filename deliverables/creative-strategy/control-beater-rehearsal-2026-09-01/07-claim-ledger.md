## Net impression

A skimming consumer is likely to conclude that Momentous Creatine differentiates on label simplicity, current sports certification, testing, and ease of fitting into a routine. The ads do **not** reasonably promise treatment, diagnosis, disease prevention, guaranteed physical/cognitive outcomes, or superiority of health results.

The inspection visual in the video could drift toward a “safer than competitors” impression if paired with contamination language or fear cues. The production brief therefore prohibits “safe/safer,” contaminant imagery, hazard symbols, and unapproved impurity comparisons.
