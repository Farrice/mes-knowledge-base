## Test sequence

1. **Round 1 — territory discovery:** Incumbent plus S1–S5. Budget evenly; do not crown a winner before the account's normal decision threshold.
2. **Round 2 — isolate the winner:** Keep the winning territory and visual system fixed. Test headline A/B, then proof order A/B. One variable per comparison.
3. **Round 3 — format transfer:** Translate only the validated message into the 45-second video concept. Do not infer that a static winner automatically transfers to video.
4. **Round 4 — scale or kill:** Scale only if the account's precommitted CPA/CVR rule holds across the agreed spend window. Otherwise archive the learning, not just the file.
