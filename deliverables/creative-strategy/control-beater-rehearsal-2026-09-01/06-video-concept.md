# Video Concept 01 — “The Powder Test”

**Status:** PRODUCTION-READY CONCEPT · SPEC / UNAFFILIATED · HOLD FOR BRAND REVIEW
**Length / format:** 45 seconds, 9:16, Meta Reels / Stories
**Audience:** Supplement-literate, training-oriented adult comparing creatine products
**Acquisition job:** Make third-party certification and label simplicity salient before the click
**Message:** Five grams is easy to print. The proof around it is harder to build.
**Product reveal:** 0:24
**CTA:** See the testing standard

## Sound-off first-frame contract

A stainless supplement scoop sits under a harsh inspection lamp. A gloved hand tips identical white powders from three unbranded jars into three identical trays. One tray receives a bold orange `VERIFIED` stamp; the others remain blank. Within two seconds, the viewer understands that identical-looking powder can carry different proof.

## Production direction

- **Camera:** 65–90mm macro for powder and label; 35–50mm for product hero. Locked-off frames plus one deliberate slow push. No gym montage.
- **Light:** One hard 5000K top source for inspection-table scenes; warm 3200K orange edge on the reveal. Preserve a real black point.
- **Palette:** Warm gray, black, white, Momentous orange. No blue clinical wash.
- **Talent:** Hands only. Clean neutral glove for inspection shots; bare hand for routine shot. No lab coat or medical authority cue.
- **Edit:** Uneven cadence: 3s / 5s / 6s / 5s / 5s / 6s / 6s / 5s / 4s. Let the product wait until 0:24.
- **Audio:** Sparse percussive clicks built from tray, scoop, and stamp sounds. Understated 90–100 BPM pulse enters at 0:08.
- **Text safety:** Keep all essential type inside 108 px left/right and 180 px top/bottom safe zones.
- **Plan B:** If the inspection-table metaphor reads too laboratory-like, replace gloves with a buyer's kitchen counter and three handwritten proof cards; preserve the same edit and message.

## Must avoid

- Do not say or imply “safer,” “cleanest,” “purest,” “better results,” “more effective,” or “banned-substance free” without the brand's approved claim language and records.
- Do not display internal test reports, COAs, lab names, athlete/team logos, or endorsements unless supplied and cleared.
- Do not use a testimonial, medical costume, body transformation, strength PR, cognition outcome, or guaranteed performance language.
- Do not turn the NSF mark into a product-performance endorsement. It supports certification status, not efficacy.
