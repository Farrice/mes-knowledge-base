## Claim-safe portfolio test plan

### Stage 0: Review before recording

1. Recheck offer scope, counts, timing, and CTA against the current offer canon.
2. Confirm every sample input is owned, anonymized, or visibly labeled `SAMPLE`.
3. Run a unit-level claim audit on spoken copy, captions, on-screen text, thumbnails, and CTA together.
4. For A01, capture the public sources and choose named-brand internal review or anonymized deployment candidate.
5. Record no client logo, outcome figure, product-health image, or compliance badge unless the specific use is approved.

### Stage 1: Produce lean cells

Produce one master and the named hook variants for each format. Keep message, audience, offer, and landing destination stable inside each format test. Do not spend until the media owner supplies:

- the actual platform and placement mix;
- the incumbent benchmark or an explicit no-control declaration;
- the minimum delivery floor appropriate to the account;
- the conversion event and attribution window;
- a receipt path for platform and first-party evidence;
- the brand's approved-claims file and prohibited-language list.

### Stage 2: Screen without declaring winners

| Format | Required attention evidence | Required downstream evidence | Failure interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| F01 | 3-second hook rate from raw counts | Qualified inquiry naming the idea-pipeline gap | Weak recognition, unclear founder credibility, or mismatched delivery |
| V01 | 3-second rate plus 15-second retention receipt | Correct offer restatement and qualified inquiry | Mechanism too dense or wrong awareness stage |
| I01 | CTR/save receipt; no video hook field | Qualified share, referral, or inquiry using the distinction | Graphic unclear or educational job disconnected from buyer intent |
| A01 | 3-second hook rate from raw counts | Qualified diagnosis request plus clean rights/claim review | Curiosity without commercial trust, or risk cost exceeds learning value |

### Stage 3: Promotion, demotion, and fatigue decisions

- **Promote:** Requires the format's job-specific attention signal, comparable delivery, and downstream conversion evidence. A human records the decision event and cites the receipts.
- **Hold:** Use when delivery is too small, the comparator is mismatched, attribution is incomplete, or a claim/rights dependency remains open.
- **Demote:** Requires evidence that the format repeatedly attracts the wrong audience, obscures the offer, or costs more to produce than the learning it returns. Hook rate alone is insufficient.
- **Retire:** Requires at least two materially different executions under adequate, buyer-approved delivery plus the absence of downstream evidence. Preserve the message and persona fields so the decision does not blame the vessel for an untested argument.
- **Fatigue:** Compare the asset with its own earlier matched window. Record frequency, date range, hook rate, conversion evidence, and any audience or placement change. Do not infer fatigue from a single down day.

### Stage 4: Commercial proof stays separate

Creative attention is supporting evidence. The offer remains `UNTESTED` until a qualified buyer is approached with approval. A collected deposit is commercial proof; views, saves, hook rate, and compliments are not.
