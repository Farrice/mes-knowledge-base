The Angle Map already has a clear, disciplined transaction: $750 prepaid, one 60-minute working session, three materially different campaign angles, one lead recommendation, and final delivery within 48 hours.

The first hard blocker is simpler than a persuasion problem: the real $750 payment rail does not exist, so the intended buyer cannot complete the paid action. Directly behind it is an order problem. One canonical activation message says payment happens before the buyer sends the complete source pack and before the session is booked. The operational contract and dry run say the source pack must be confirmed and the session slot reserved before payment. Those paths cannot both govern a live sale. If the wrong one runs, a buyer can become prepaid before Farrice knows whether the evidence is reviewable or the session can occur early enough to protect the 48-hour promise.

The correct move is a bounded operational patch, not an offer rebuild.

## Real-Burden Reconstruction

### Current path

1. The buyer sees or receives the offer and sends **DM ANGLE**.
2. The written sources diverge:
   - one path takes payment, then requests files, then sends the calendar link;
   - the operationally safe path checks the files, reserves the session, and only then sends the payment link.
3. The buyer must interpret what counts as approved evidence, claim language, substantiation, and claim boundaries.
4. Farrice reviews the message and evidence, develops five candidate directions, and brings the strongest three to the session.
5. The buyer attends one 60-minute decision session.
6. Farrice delivers the three-angle decision package inside 48 hours.
7. The buyer encounters two different make-right descriptions: one revision cycle versus revisions until the usability standard is met.

### Patched path

1. Buyer sends **DM ANGLE** and names the live occasion and deadline.
2. Buyer sends the existing page/deck and whatever approved evidence and claim boundaries already exist through an approved channel. The buyer is not asked to classify or perfect the pack.
3. Farrice performs a bounded readiness check and returns one **Pre-Read Receipt**: accepted occasion, files received, usable/needs-support/out-of-scope source status, confidentiality and AI-use path, named decision-maker, reserved session, and exact clock-start conditions.
4. Only after the receipt is green and the session is protected does Farrice send the real $750 payment link.
5. Payment clears; readiness remains confirmed; the 48-hour clock starts.
6. The live session ends with one named lead angle and one explicit **What not to build** decision.
7. The final package arrives with the Build First Card first, followed by the Decision Book and optional supporting views.
8. One usability question activates the make-right if needed. Any proof sharing is a separate, optional permission event and defaults to private.

### Burden removed, added, and shifted

- **Removed from the buyer:** guessing whether the source pack is complete; uncertainty about booking versus payment; interpreting when the clock starts; reconciling two make-right promises.
- **Shifted to Farrice:** readiness classification, slot protection, and explicit source/confidentiality confirmation before money changes hands.
- **Added:** one short Pre-Read Receipt and one optional usability response.
- **Protected:** evidence, compliance, final-decision authority, the 60-minute session, the 48-hour promise, and the existing three-angle scope.

- the named buyer and final decision-maker;

- the approved transfer and human-only/AI-assisted handling path;
- the reserved session;
- whether the 48-hour clock is stopped or ready; and
- the two T0 conditions: payment cleared and complete inputs confirmed.
