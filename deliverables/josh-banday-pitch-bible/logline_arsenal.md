# Logline Arsenal: 30 Loglines for Three Shows
### Hook Forge × Comedy Intelligence × Proof-of-Work Hooks

> **Purpose**: Arm Josh with multiple logline variants for every context — elevator pitch, general meeting, written submission, social media announcement, and the "what's your show about?" cocktail party answer.

## POTLUCK — 10 Loglines

### The Elevator (sub-10 seconds)
1. **"A music teacher accidentally becomes South Central's most trusted therapist. He has no license, no degree, and a growing waitlist."**

2. **"Abbott Elementary meets Shrinking — but the therapist is a fraud, and the community needs him anyway."**

### The General Meeting (opens a conversation)
3. **"Marcus is a queer, mixed-race music teacher in South Central LA who started listening to parents after school one day and accidentally built an unlicensed therapy practice. Now half the neighborhood depends on him, the evidence trail is growing, and he has to decide: earn the credential, or pray nobody asks to see it."**

4. **"A music teacher with four heritages and zero psychology degrees discovers he's better at fixing people than teaching scales. The show is about what happens when the most unqualified person in the room is also the best one for the job."**

### The Written Submission (logline for a one-sheet or fellowship application)
5. **"When Marcus Reyes-Goldstein — a queer, Black-Filipino-Mexican-Jewish music teacher in South Central LA — accidentally becomes his school community's unlicensed therapist, he must navigate the growing demand, the mounting evidence, and the terrifying possibility that helping people might require him to finally help himself."**

6. **"A failed stand-up comedian turned music teacher discovers his improv training makes him a better therapist than most licensed professionals — a revelation that builds an accidental practice, a ticking secret, and a community that would burn down to protect him."**

### The Social Media / Trades Announcement
7. **"POTLUCK: The only show where the therapist is more scared than the patient. Coming to [network]. 🎺🛋️"**

8. **"What happens when a music teacher starts asking 'how does that make you feel?' and people actually answer?"**

### The Cocktail Party (the "what's your show about?" answer)
9. **"You know how everyone says 'I should've been a therapist'? My guy actually did it. No degree. Just vibes and improv training. And it's *working*."**

10. **"It's about a music teacher in South Central who's accidentally running a therapy practice out of his classroom, and the secret that could end it all — which is that he's literally just a music teacher with really good instincts."**

## DAD'S PLUS-ONE — 10 Loglines

### The Elevator (sub-10 seconds)
11. **"The biggest movie star on Earth gets full custody of his daughter. He doesn't know what grade she's in."**

12. **"Schitt's Creek at school drop-off — but the rich person is the hottest man alive, and his nine-year-old runs his life."**

### The General Meeting (opens a conversation)
13. **"Daniel Banday is an A-list movie star who just got full custody of his nine-year-old daughter. He can negotiate a $20-million deal but can't work the carpool app. The show is about what happens when the most powerful man in any room becomes the least powerful person at his kid's school."**

14. **"A newly single A-list actor discovers that his entire life — his marriage, his household, his relationship with his daughter — was managed by his ex-wife. Now she's gone, and the man who can command any room in Hollywood can't command a school bake sale."**

### The Written Submission
15. **"When A-list movie star Daniel Banday gains full custody of his preternaturally competent nine-year-old daughter after a high-profile divorce, he must navigate the mortifying universe of school pickups, parent committees, and supervised playdates — discovering that the only role he's never prepared for is the one that matters most."**

16. **"Daniel Banday has 14 million Instagram followers and one nine-year-old daughter who manages his life because nobody else will anymore. A comedy about fame, fatherhood, and the devastating realization that being beloved by millions doesn't mean you can pack a lunch."**

### The Social Media / Trades Announcement
17. **"DAD'S PLUS-ONE: He saves the world on screen. He can't find the school supply list. A new comedy about the world's most helpless movie star. ⭐🎒"**

18. **"What if the sexiest man alive had to volunteer for the school book fair?"**

### The Cocktail Party
19. **"It's about a movie star — like, the biggest movie star — who gets full custody and realizes his ex-wife was running literally everything. He packed his kid sushi for lunch. She's nine."**

20. **"Imagine if Brad Pitt had to do carpool. Every day. And his daughter was more organized than him. That's the show."**

## GUEST STAR — 10 Loglines

### The Elevator (sub-10 seconds)
21. **"An actor who's been on 47 shows — but never as the lead — tries to sabotage his career. The harder he fails, the more famous he gets."**

22. **"Barry meets Episodes — but the actor isn't dangerous, he's invisible. And visibility is scarier than anything."**

### The General Meeting (opens a conversation)
23. **"Josh Marin is Hollywood's most versatile actor. He's been on every show you've ever watched. You just can't remember his name. When he decides to burn it all down and deliberately tank his career, his sabotage keeps backfiring into bigger opportunities — forcing him to confront why he's spent fifteen years being everyone except himself."**

24. **"A lifelong 'that guy' actor — forty-seven credits, zero leads — turns career self-sabotage into an accidental art form. Every attempt to fail spectacularly succeeds spectacularly, and the show asks: what terrifies you more — being invisible, or being seen?"**

### The Written Submission
25. **"Josh Marin has appeared on 47 television shows across 15 years. He has been a detective, a doctor, a murder victim (four times), and a best friend (seven times). He has never been the lead. When a midlife reckoning drives him to systematically sabotage his own career, his increasingly unhinged on-set behavior keeps being mistaken for genius — launching the fame he never wanted and forcing a confrontation with the man he forgot to become."**

26. **"In an industry that celebrates stars and forgets everyone else, a career guest star wages a one-man war against his own résumé — only to discover that the performance nobody claps for is the one that happens when the cameras stop."**

### The Social Media / Trades Announcement
27. **"GUEST STAR: He's been on every show. He's been killed four times. You've definitely seen his face. You have never, ever known his name. A new comedy. 🎬"**

28. **"47 shows. 0 leads. 1 man trying to get fired. What could go wrong?"**

### The Cocktail Party
29. **"You know that guy who's on, like, everything? He's a cop in one show, he dies in another, he's the best friend in three? That guy decides to burn his career down, and every time he tries to fail he becomes more famous. But always as the guest. Never the star."**

30. **"It's about the most invisible famous person in Hollywood. He tries to quit and the industry won't let him. Because they need guys like him — and he's starting to realize that's not a compliment."**
