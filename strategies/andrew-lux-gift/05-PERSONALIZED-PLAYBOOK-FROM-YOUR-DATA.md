Keep making comedy when it comes naturally. Stop forcing it when it doesn't.

**2. Major Milestone Posts**

"Freek-A-Leek" remix: 17,200 views. "Street Player" on YouTube: 143,000 views. The math is clear — when you flip a song people already love, it reaches further than anything else.

**1. Generic Outdoor Shots**
2,200 views. Nobody cares about a random palm tree. Stop.

2,500 views. If there's no strong opening line or visual, people scroll.

**4. Cross-Posting to Dead Platforms**
Every minute spent formatting for TikTok (20-70 likes) is a minute not spent on something that moves the needle.

### What To Start

**1. Production Content (Your Untapped Category)**

Your peer **Rose Motion** (9,500 followers, similar career stage) focuses on production breakdowns and behind-the-scenes content. It's building her technical credibility with other producers.

You have Ableton skills, keyboard chops, and ghost production experience that would make devastating production content. But your feed barely shows it.

**Start with:**

- "How I flipped [recognizable song]" breakdowns

**2. The Vulnerability Series**

Your burnout post got your second-highest engagement EVER. This isn't a one-time thing — it's a content category.

Not every day. Not performative sadness. But when something's real, share it. The audience showed you they want it.

**Keep it short. Keep it honest. Don't script it.**

**3. YouTube Remix Strategy**

Your 143K-view "Street Player" remix proves the model. But your recent uploads get 50-100 views because they're not remixes of recognizable songs.

**The formula:**
- Take a song everyone knows
- Give it the Andrew Lux bass house treatment

- Link to SoundCloud/YouTube for full version

This is the one strategy that requires the LEAST personality and the MOST production skill. It's literally just: make a great remix and upload it.

**4. SoundCloud-First Release Strategy**

You have 21,700 followers there. That's 3x your Instagram. Start treating SoundCloud as your PRIMARY release platform:
- Upload works-in-progress and bootlegs
- Engage with the community
- Let SoundCloud's algorithm push your music
