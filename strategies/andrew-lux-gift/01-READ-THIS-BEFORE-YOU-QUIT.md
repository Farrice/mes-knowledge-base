## What Content Could Look Like For You

### Monday: Production Breakdown
Screen recording of your DAW. Show how a bass sound was built. Layer by layer. No talking needed. Music speaks.

### Wednesday: Studio Session
Timelapse of a session. Sped up. Your track underneath. Hands on the gear. No face required.

### Friday: The Craft
Sample flip, sound design showcase, or a tease of something new. 30-60 seconds of pure skill.

### When You Feel Like It: WIP Tease
15 seconds of a hook or drop from something unreleased. Text overlay: "New one cooking." That's it.

**Total weekly time: About 90 minutes of content work.**

Compare that to the hours you've been spending on content you hate.
