# The Producer's Content Playbook

## For Artists Who Want to Be Known for Their Music, Not Their Personality

---

## The Core Principle

**Let the music do the work.**

Every piece of content should showcase SKILL and SOUND, not personality and performance.

You don't have to become a content creator to succeed. You have to show your work in a way that reaches people.

---

## What Works (No Dancing Required)

### 1. Production Breakdowns

Show how a sound, hook, or track was built.

**Format:**
```
[0-5 sec] The finished sound
[5-45 sec] Building it layer by layer
[45-60 sec] The finished sound again
```

Charlie Puth's "Light Switch" production breakdown got **119.5M views**. He didn't dance. He showed the process.

### 2. Studio Timelapses

Record your session, speed it up, overlay with the final track. Shows dedication. Zero talking required. Music is the star.

### 3. Sample Flips

Take something unexpected and turn it into your sound. Classical → bass house. 80s pop → modern production. The transformation IS the content.

### 4. Before/After Sessions

"What this track sounded like at midnight vs. what it sounds like now." Progress and craft — no face required.

### 5. Sound Design Spotlights

Focus on one element: "This bass took 2 hours to get right. Here's why." Show the processing chain. A/B comparison.

### 6. Visualizers

Audio + visual treatment. No face needed. AI-generated visualizers get **40% more engagement** than static images. Tools: Revid.ai, EchoWave, SongRender.

### 7. Gear/Studio Content

Your workspace IS content. Close-up shots of synths/controllers. "New arrival" posts. Studio aesthetic. @producer_setups has 116K followers doing only this.

### 8. Work in Progress Teases

15-30 second clip of a drop or hook. Text overlay: "New one cooking." That's it.

---

## The Weekly Rhythm

**Sustainable schedule (not a grind):**

| Day | Content Type | Time |
|-----|--------------|------|
| Mon | Production breakdown | 30 min |
| Wed | Studio timelapse or WIP tease | 15 min |
| Fri | Visualizer or sample flip | 30 min |
| Optional | Gear shot or moment | 5 min |

**Total: 4 posts per week, ~90 minutes of effort.**

---

## What to STOP Doing

- Dancing to your own music (unless you genuinely enjoy it)
- Trend-based content that requires performing
- Face-to-camera vlogs
- Forced humor or goofy content
- Anything that makes you think "I hate doing this"

If it drains you, it's not sustainable. And unsustainable = inconsistent = invisible.

---

## Platform Notes

**YouTube** — Long-form production content, tutorials, sessions. Evergreen (people search for production tutorials forever).

**Instagram** — Curated aesthetic, studio shots, visualizers. Feed for polished, Stories for raw.

**TikTok** — Production clips, sample flips. If they perform, great. If not, no forcing.

**SoundCloud** — Bootlegs, remixes, works in progress. The underground platform.

---

## The Visual System

Since you're not showing face, show AESTHETIC.

- Same color grading on all content
- Same fonts for text overlays
- Recognizable studio setup
- Simple logo/watermark

**Goal:** Someone scrolling should recognize your content before reading your name.

---

## Measuring Success Differently

### What matters more than follower count:
- Other producers watching and respecting your work
- Artists reaching out for production work
- Industry people in your comments/DMs
- Quality of attention, not quantity

### The long game:
Build reputation among the right 1,000 people. Those 1,000 are producers, artists, A&Rs, bookers. They create opportunities that 100K random followers don't.

---

## The Mindset Shift

**From:** "How do I get attention?"
**To:** "How do I show what I can do?"

The content creator mindset: entertain to get followers.
The producer mindset: demonstrate to earn respect.

Different goals, different approaches.

---

## Content Idea Bank

### Quick Wins (Under 30 min)
- Screen record 5 min of production, speed up to 30 sec
- Record just the drop with a visualizer
- Post close-up of favorite gear with caption
- Before/after of any track in progress
- 15-sec hook tease from something unreleased

### Medium Effort (30-60 min)
- Full production breakdown of one element
- Sample flip from unexpected source
- Studio timelapse of a full session
- "Making a beat in 60 seconds" challenge

### Higher Effort (Worth It)
- Full "making of" for a released track
- Collaboration content
- Ghost production reveal (when legally possible)
- Tutorial on your signature technique

---

## The Bottom Line

The DJs who dance on TikTok are playing one game.

The producers who let their craft speak are playing another.

Both games can win. But you have to pick the one that fits who you are.

**No dancing required.**
