## What The Data Says

### TikTok
- Videos tagged with electronic music: **13 billion views** in 2024 (45% increase)
- **84%** of songs entering Billboard Global 200 went viral on TikTok first
- But: Production breakdown content (no dancing) performs strongly
- Charlie Puth's process video: **119.5M views**

### Instagram
- Artists posting consistent aesthetic content build recognition faster than personality content
- Feed curation matters more than posting frequency
- Industry contacts (labels, bookers) take Instagram more seriously for partnerships

### YouTube
- Production tutorials are **evergreen** — they get views for years
- Andrew Huang: **2.35M subscribers, 300M+ views** — 100% educational music content
- Long-form sessions drive deeper engagement than short clips

### SoundCloud
- Still important for bass/electronic genres
- Bootlegs and remixes live safely here
- New Artist Plan (2024): Pro tools at $3.25/month

## The Fred Again Model (Music-First Social Media)

Fred Again is the closest example of someone doing social media RIGHT as a music-focused artist:

- **20+ IG stories a day** — but of MOMENTS, not performances
- **Phone selfies as album art** — authentic, not polished
- **Spontaneous releases** — drops throughout the week, not just Fridays
- **Social media as source material** — literally samples clips INTO his music
- **No trends, no dances** — just documenting his creative life

> "Authenticity has been at the heart of everything he does. No posts are polished or look like he's even thought about it."

**He's not a content creator. He's an artist who documents.**

## The Producer's Producer Path

If performing isn't the goal, there's a whole other path:

### How Industry Respect Gets Built
1. **Signature sound** that other producers recognize
2. **Collaborations** that demonstrate range and relationships
3. **Selectivity** — not working with everyone signals standards
4. **Mentorship** — sharing knowledge builds legacy
5. **Consistency** — a body of work that demonstrates depth

### Producers Who Built Empires Without Being Performers
- **Max Martin** — 28 #1 Billboard hits. First interview in 16 years.
- **Rick Rubin** — "Production by reduction." Listens lying down with eyes closed.
