# Alyssa Stalker — 2026 Agent Content Playbook

Stuck agents don't have a posting problem, they have an audience problem. "Most of the time it's not a consistency problem... they really aren't speaking to anyone specific." The 2026 fix is mechanical because Instagram now distributes by interest test: the more specific the post, the less you fight for views. This skill runs the path Alyssa Stalker and The Broke Agent laid out for the second half of 2026: read your own outliers, pick one person, frame every local post as topic + who + lens, make that person feel seen before you sell, mix local / listing / authority by goal, and run one thing long enough to learn.

## Expert Identity

Alyssa Stalker is Head of Education & Strategy at Coffee & Contracts (agent content templates), host of the Above Asking podcast, a former agent who audits agent profiles weekly, and a practitioner of her own advice: one repeatable carousel format aimed at one feeling took a stagnant ~10,000-follower account to ~15,000 in 2026. Eric Simon (The Broke Agent, BAM) co-sources the outlier worked example, the Ricky Carruth exemplars, and the authority reframes.

## When to Load This Skill

- "I post all the time and nothing moves" — any stuck-account complaint

## Quick Reference

- **Diagnose from data.** Six months of posts, find the outlier, name the attribute. "It's just data."
- **One person.** Not upsizer/downsizer. Pickleball Friday or couch-rot weekend.
- **Topic + Who + Lens.** "3 things to do in [city]" → "...you've probably never heard of, especially if you're [person]."
- **Specificity is reach.** Instagram tests by interest for a day after one search; narrow lands, broad drowns.
- **Feel seen before you sell.** Feeling in the hook, offer on the last slide as permission: "you're not behind — that's exactly who I help."
- **Share test.** Would they reshare this to look good to their own audience?
- **Lo-fi wins.** Camera-roll photo + text. Tweet it, screenshot it, post it.
- **Tag the goal.** Local = grow, Listing = convert, Authority = nurture (smallest, ~monthly, expect fewer likes).
- **Authority is a story.** Pull the review's hook line; green-screen the award; film at the keys.
- **One message, 20–30 seconds.**
- **AI reframes, never writes.** "Reframe this hook for my audience, from my point of view."
- **Get data, then pivot or double down.** Never five experiments at once.

## Fair Housing (system layer)

The source's who-clause examples use parental status. For any post that touches housing, frame the person by life situation or interest (renter, relocating, dog parent, midnight-Zillow scroller), never by protected class. Client CLAUDE.md fair-housing rules outrank any example in this skill.
