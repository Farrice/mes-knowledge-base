## Diagnose Before Treat

Find where the current audience definition stops being a person. "First-time buyers in the Valley" is a segment. "Dual-income couples 30–42" is a demographic. Neither tells you where they eat. Mark the line where the definition goes abstract; that is where this workflow starts.

## Execution

1. **Personal brand first** — "who are you? What do you represent? What do you want people to know about you? Who do you immediately connect with?" [05:00–05:07]. Capture in the agent's own words.
2. **Pick the person** — from the connection answer, choose one. Not the biggest segment; the one the agent lights up about.
3. **Write the scene** — life stage, a weekday evening, a Friday night, a Sunday morning; where they eat, what they scroll, what they've stopped saying out loud. Alyssa's prompts: early parenting, dog parents, relocating professionals for a new company hub [07:32–07:43]. Housing-safe framing when the content will touch housing.
4. **Name the private feeling** — the sentence they would never post but would recognize instantly (this feeds `04-comfort-content-engine`).
5. **Personal-lens inventory** — 5–8 signals the audience needs to know about the agent "in order to like me, trust me, and then work with me" [09:36–09:40]. Eric's set: Star Wars, USC, baseball cards, the book, Yankees, the new baby, the bets [08:47–09:18]. Real signals only.
6. **One-slide test** — write the whole message as one single-image post plus caption. "What can I fit in one slide... that's almost how you can reverse engineer who you're speaking to" [06:29–06:36]. If it won't fit, the person is still too broad.
7. **Five seed topics through the lens** — local, listing-adjacent, and comfort topics, each already carrying who + lens.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Output destination | Adaptation |

## Quality Gate

- Could you picture this person at one specific table on one specific night?
- Are the personal-lens signals real and agent-supplied, not generated?
- Does the one-slide test fit a single image plus caption?
- Is the housing-safe frame explicit?
- Did the agent confirm the card, or is it marked DRAFT?
- Anti-pattern check: no "upsizer/downsizer," no "buyers and sellers," no "everyone."
