# /alyssa-stalker-create-mode-text-post — Tweet It, Screenshot It, Post It

Ricky Carruth's all-black create-mode posts: "If your real estate agent is still answering the phone at 9:00 p.m. on Saturday, you don't have an agent. You have someone who cares about your family's future" (~11,000 likes) [22:04–22:13]. The production rule: "if you have an idea, just one thing, tweet it out, screenshot it, and then post it" [23:09–23:13]. The mechanism: written FOR agents THROUGH the agent's lens, so agents reshare it "so that their potential clients see it and it would make them look good" [23:36–23:46].

## Diagnose Before Treat

Decide who reshares. Agent-to-agent lines get reshared by agents to their clients (Ricky's play). Consumer lines get reshared by the buyer to their partner or group chat. The lens is the resharer's, not the writer's. Name the resharer before writing a word.

## Execution

1. **Name the resharer and what the reshare says about them** — "I work this hard"; "we're not crazy for waiting"; "this is what my agent is like."
2. **Find the true sentence** — from the agent's own words, a client's words, or a lived moment. Structures in source: the contrast ("you don't have an agent. You have someone who…"); the redefinition ("that's called courage"); the reframe of a trait ("confidence isn't arrogance. It's…").
3. **Compress** — ≤25 words, one message [25:40–25:49]. Cut every qualifier.
4. **Write 5–7** — vary the structure; at least two consumer-facing and two agent-facing unless the run specifies one audience.
5. **Run the share test on each** — who reshares, and what it makes them look like [23:36–23:53]. Kill any line that only informs.
6. **Production note** — create mode, all-black background, default type; or type it as a tweet, screenshot, post [23:02–23:13]. No design.
7. **Voice check** — the agent would say it out loud. Rough beats smooth; a fragment is fine.

## Quality Gate

- Is every line one message, ≤25 words?
- Does each line name a resharer and what resharing says about them?
- Is it an opinion or a recognition, not a tip or a stat?
- Is it producible in create mode with zero design?
- Would the agent say it out loud?
- Anti-pattern check: no AI-generated voice, no "look at me" line about awards or listings.
