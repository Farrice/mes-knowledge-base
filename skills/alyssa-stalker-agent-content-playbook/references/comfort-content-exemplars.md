## The consumer flip — source structure, [20:10–20:51]

Hook: "Self-care as someone in their mid-30s who's yet to buy a house."
Middle: things they can do to take care of their mindset, take care of themselves, start the process — "something funny at first, something cheeky, something supportive."
Payoff (last): "Don't worry, you're not behind. Lots of people are like that and guess what? That's exactly who I help."

Reader path: "oh, self-care as a person who hasn't done this yet. That's me. Okay, I'll keep swiping" [20:28–20:32].

## Anti-exemplars (from source)
