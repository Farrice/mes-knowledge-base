## Fair-housing filter (system addition)

If the post concerns housing, do not select the who by protected class (race, color, religion, national origin, sex, familial status, disability). Frame by life situation, habit, interest, or private feeling. "Mom of two" is fine on a things-to-do post; it is not the frame for a "best neighborhoods" post.
