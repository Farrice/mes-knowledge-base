# Dhar Mann: Transformational Community Storytelling

This skill turns a Marketing Against the Grain interview with Dhar Mann, a TheWrap production-operations panel with Dhar Mann Studios leadership, and two cited published stories into a focused transformation-and-production-continuity system.

It is not a Dhar Mann imitation engine. It does not provide therapy, guarantee views, reproduce his dialogue, reconstruct proprietary software, or claim to teach directing, editing, screenplay, or the complete studio production method.

## Distinct capability

Use this skill when the content must do more than hold attention:

`feel seen -> witness a meaningful choice -> reinterpret the situation -> take one safe positive action -> deepen community connection`

Dhar owns the viewer transformation and community spine. Existing skills remain responsible for prose voice, screenplay craft, belief-change craft, retention audits, and content operations.

## Default selection

- No clear mission or viewer after-state -> workflow 01.
- Raw thought, life pattern, or audience struggle -> workflow 02.
- Title, thumbnail, hook, or opening problem -> workflow 03.
- Slow setup or weak escalation -> workflow 04.
- Concept validation or community learning -> workflow 05.
- Deep-topic creator content -> workflow 06.
- Moral, lesson, or perspective shift feels preachy -> workflow 07.
- Approved structure needs a production-ready handoff -> workflow 08.
- Repeated production waits, shared assets, or freshness debt -> workflow 09.
- End-to-end creator run -> `/dhar-transformational-content-factory`.

## Required inputs

- raw thought, source, or topic
- target audience
- intended format
- starting feeling or belief
- intended after-state
- one real lived scene, observed example, or evidence source
- mission boundary
- one safe positive action
- for production modes: known assets, constraints, owners, and unresolved specialist needs

If the lived source is missing, mark `[PROVENANCE MISSING]`. Retrieve existing
evidence first. Reality Before Rhetoric is an optional co-creative recovery
practice when Farrice explicitly invokes it or accepts one source-recovery
recommendation. If it is declined or the gap remains, continue with a
source-labeled strategy, outline, placeholder, or smaller claim. Do not
fabricate a personal scene, causal hinge, outcome, permission, or full body.

## Always-on mission and claim gate

Fail the run when any are true:

- a lived scene or result is invented;
- the content implies therapy equivalence or replacement;
- engagement, retention, impact, or transformation is guaranteed;
- the package uses pain the body does not repay;
- the positive action is coercive, unsafe, or beyond the creator's authority;
- optimization changes what the brand is trying to be known for;
- views, retention, connection, and transformation are collapsed into one metric.

## Quality bar

Every practitioner output must contain:

- viewer starting state and after-state;
- exact private sentence or recognition moment;
- embodied conflict or a named proof gap;
- honest package promise;
- earned interpretation shift;
- one safe action;
- mission/claim verdict;
- evidence labels;
- next feedback decision.
- for production modes: capacity verdict, transformation-preserving handoffs, and unsupported craft gaps.
