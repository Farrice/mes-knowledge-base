# Workflow 01: HEART Transformation North Star

Use this before production when the idea has a topic but no trustworthy human outcome, or when algorithmic opportunity is pulling against the creator's mission.

## Inputs

- Raw thought, source, or concept.
- Target audience.
- Intended format and surface.
- Creator or brand mission.
- Real story, observation, or evidence source.
- Desired viewer after-state.
- Known audience-trust and claim boundaries.
- Existing system/cadence, if any.

## Diagnose before treatment

Name the primary failure:

- `H — SOURCE`: no provenance or lived connection.
- `E — TRUST`: audience is broad, unseen, or being talked at.
- `A — SYSTEM`: the idea is an isolated hit with no repeatable loop.
- `R — CONNECTION`: the piece has information but no emotional hinge.
- `T — IMPACT`: the desired action is missing, coercive, or unmeasurable.
- `MISSION CONFLICT`: a promising package or topic violates what the creator wants to be known for.

Do not solve every category equally. Fix the primary failure while preserving the others as constraints.

## Steps

1. **Honor the source.**
   - Name the real scene, observation, or evidence path.
   - If absent, mark `[PROVENANCE MISSING]`.
   - State what the source authorizes and what it does not.

2. **Earn audience trust.**
   - Define one audience in situational, not demographic, terms.
   - Write their starting feeling, belief, and private sentence.
   - Name the recognition moment the piece must earn before advising.

3. **Architect the loop.**
   - Define the smallest complete content unit.
   - State where audience greenlight enters before production.
   - State the post-publication decision the evidence will change.

4. **Reach the heart.**
   - Name the meaningful choice or contrast that embodies the struggle.
   - Define the exact perspective shift.
   - Name the emotional residue to avoid: shame, helplessness, manipulation, or false closure.

5. **Turn views into impact.**
   - Define one safe, non-coercive positive action.
   - Choose one transformation signal and its collection method.
   - Keep discovery, retention, connection, and transformation evidence separate.

6. **Apply the mission veto.**
   - List any title, topic, visual, or action direction that would break audience trust.
   - Reject it even if it could improve short-term performance.

7. **Issue the run verdict.**
   - `READY`: source, audience, shift, action, and mission are clear.
   - `READY FOR BRIEF ONLY`: strategy is clear but provenance is missing.
   - `REVISE`: one named failure can be repaired.
   - `STOP`: the idea requires unsafe, fabricated, clinical, or mission-breaking claims.

## Output contract

Produce:

- Primary diagnosis.
- HEART Transformation Card.
- Viewer starting state and after-state.
- Mission veto list.
- Smallest complete test.
- Four-scoreboard measurement plan.
- Run verdict and next workflow.

Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/heart-transformation-north-star.md — honor its Output Contract.

## Output schema

```text
PRIMARY DIAGNOSIS: [one failure]
SOURCE STATUS: [path / supplied scene / PROVENANCE MISSING]

HEART TRANSFORMATION CARD
H — [source and permission]
E — [audience, private sentence, recognition moment]
A — [smallest complete unit, greenlight point, learning loop]
R — [choice, shift, emotional residue]
T — [safe action, transformation signal]

MISSION VETOES:
- [choice the system will not make]

SCOREBOARDS:
- Discovery: [metric]
- Retention: [metric]
- Connection: [metric]
- Transformation: [metric + collection method]

VERDICT: [READY / READY FOR BRIEF ONLY / REVISE / STOP]
NEXT WORKFLOW: [workflow + reason]
```

## Adaptations

- **Creator-led insight:** require a real scene and choose a Bayer mode later.
- **Dramatized narrative:** stop at a strategy brief unless screenplay craft is separately loaded.
- **Brand activation:** use a human objective and tangible act; require independent measurement proof.
- **Short-form:** keep the full transformation covenant even when the unit is compressed.

## Quality gate

- Is one primary failure named?
- Is the source real or explicitly missing?
- Is the audience defined by a current struggle?
- Is the shift more specific than “feel inspired”?
- Is the action safe and non-coercive?
- Are the four scoreboards distinct?
- Does the mission veto reject at least one plausible but wrong move when tension exists?
