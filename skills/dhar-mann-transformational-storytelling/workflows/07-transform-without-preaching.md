# Workflow 07: Transform Without Preaching

Use this when the lesson, moral, or perspective shift is correct but currently arrives as explanation, authority, or command.

This workflow selects one transformation craft route:

- narrative -> Andrew Stanton;
- educational/cognitive -> Kobi Brown.

It does not copy either expert's methodology into this skill.

## Inputs

- Seen-Heard Story Brief.
- Packaging Promise Covenant.
- Selected Bayer Mode Packet, if creator-led.
- Intended format.
- Belief-before and belief-after.
- Available real scenes, proof, or examples.
- Desired positive action.

## Diagnose before treatment

Choose the primary preaching failure:

- `MORAL FIRST`: the conclusion appears before the viewer has evidence.
- `AUTHORITY BRIDGE`: “trust me” or credentials cause the shift.
- `NO CHOICE`: the character or viewer is never forced to reinterpret.
- `FALSE CERTAINTY`: the shift exceeds available proof.
- `SHAME CLOSE`: the lesson condemns the current self rather than restoring agency.
- `GENERIC CTA`: the ending requests engagement instead of a useful action.
- `ROUTE CONFLICT`: narrative and educational mechanisms are both active without a reason.

## Steps

1. **Lock the transformation claim.**
   - Belief-before.
   - Belief-after.
   - Evidence strength.
   - What remains uncertain.

2. **Select one route.**
   - `NARRATIVE`: the shift is earned through character intention, obstacle, recognition, and choice.
   - `EDUCATIONAL`: the shift is earned through contrast, explanation, proof, and a cognitive bridge.
   - Stop if both are necessary but no integration owner is defined.

3. **Build the route handoff.**
   - Narrative -> premise, protagonist spine, intention, obstacle, change moment, earned ending; load `skills/andrew-stanton-audience-engineering/workflows/stanton-change-engine.md`.
   - Educational -> belief-before, belief-after, bridge, proof, identity implication; load `skills/kobi-brown-educational-virality/workflows/10-universal-aha-engine.md`.

4. **Hide the moral until earned.**
   - Show the cost of the old interpretation.
   - Let a choice, contrast, or proof create the new interpretation.
   - State the moral only if clarity requires it after the shift.

5. **Design the ending.**
   - Literal resolution.
   - Viewer reinterpretation.
   - Positive action.
   - Residue: what should stay with them after the final line.

6. **Check agency and safety.**
   - Remove coercion, diagnosis, certainty theater, and substitute-care language.
   - The action should be possible without buying, confessing, or publicly disclosing.

7. **Create integration evidence.**
   - Name the exact beat, line, or decision the craft route changed.
   - Confirm Dhar still owns mission, after-state, and positive action.

## Output contract

Produce:

- preaching diagnosis;
- transformation claim with evidence status;
- one selected craft route;
- route handoff packet;
- moral-withholding plan;
- earned-ending design;
- safe action;
- integration evidence;
- remaining proof gap.

## Adaptations

- **Narrative video essay:** the creator can be the character only when the real source supports it.
- **Educational explainer:** use Kobi's cognitive route and keep emotional stakes connected to a real situation.
- **Dramatized story:** Stanton structures; a screenplay author writes scenes and dialogue.
- **Short-form:** one contrast or choice may carry the full shift, but it must still be earned.

## Quality gate

- Is one craft route selected?
- Does evidence justify the belief-after?
- Is the moral withheld until a choice, contrast, or proof earns it?
- Does the ending give agency instead of shame?
- Is the positive action safe and voluntary?
- Is the exact contribution from Stanton or Kobi visible?
- Does Dhar remain the transformation and mission owner?
