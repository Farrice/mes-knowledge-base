# Workflow 06: Recognized Before Transformed

Use this when the transformation is strategically clear but the audience must feel privately understood before the lesson or creator authority can land.

This workflow routes to exactly one David Bayer mode. It does not duplicate Bayer's methodology.

## Inputs

- Seen-Heard Story Brief.
- Real creator scene or proof source.
- Intended content class.
- Viewer private sentence.
- Perspective shift.
- Creator voice target.
- Surface and length.
- Farrice voice-card dial when shipping under Farrice's name.

## Diagnose before treatment

Choose the body-author mode:

- `RECOGNITION-YOU`: the reader/viewer's inner world is the spine; creator receipt is minimal. Use `/bayer-mirror`.
- `TESTIMONY-I`: the creator's lived experience is the spine and authority comes from reporting what happened. Use David Bayer's Magnetic Authority Content System.
- `NO BODY AUTHOR`: provenance is missing or the content class requires another author.

Hard fail if both Bayer modes are selected.

## Steps

1. **Confirm provenance.**
   - Identify the real scene or source.
   - Separate what Farrice lived, observed, inferred, and cannot claim.

2. **Classify the content.**
   - Deep-topic, spiritual, or life-design insight centered on the viewer -> recognition-you.
   - Creator journey, lesson, launch, receipt, or case grounded in Farrice's experience -> testimony-I.
   - Dramatized screenplay or fictional scene -> no Bayer body author by default.

3. **Build the recognition hinge.**
   - Private sentence.
   - Everyday situation.
   - One analogy candidate that can carry the shift.
   - Empathetic bridge that reduces shame without dissolving agency.

4. **Preserve the Dhar spine.**
   - Starting state.
   - Witnessed choice or source moment.
   - Perspective shift.
   - Positive action.
   - Mission boundary.

5. **Build the Bayer Mode Packet.**
   - Selected mode.
   - Body-author dependency path.
   - Allowed first-person material.
   - Forbidden point-of-view drift.
   - Voice-card requirement.
   - Preserve list.

6. **Invoke or hand off.**
   - Recognition-you -> load and execute `.agent/workflows/bayer-mirror.md`.
   - Testimony-I -> load `skills/david-bayer-elite-communication/genius.md` and `skills/david-bayer-elite-communication/workflows/magnetic-authority-content-system.md`.
   - Do not blend their body contracts.

7. **Return integration evidence.**
   - Show which line, section, or decision Bayer changed.
   - Confirm the transformation spine remains intact.

## Output contract

Produce:

- mode diagnosis;
- provenance status;
- recognition hinge;
- Bayer Mode Packet;
- dependency path;
- preserve and forbid lists;
- integration evidence requirement;
- next craft route.

If the dependency is executed in the same run, append the authored body and a short Composition Ledger.

Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/recognized-before-transformed.md — honor its Output Contract.

## Adaptations

- **Farrice deep-topic content:** recognition-you is the default candidate, not an automatic selection.
- **Farrice case/receipt content:** testimony-I only when the source scene is real and supplied.
- **Client content:** use the client's source and voice system; do not transplant Farrice receipts.
- **No provenance:** deliver the packet and one focused question; do not draft the body.

## Quality gate

- Is exactly one Bayer mode selected?
- Is the real source named?
- Does the private sentence belong to the viewer rather than the expert?
- Is first-person material bounded by the selected mode?
- Is the Dhar shift/action spine preserved?
- Is the correct Bayer dependency loaded rather than paraphrased from memory?
- Is voice-card alignment required for Farrice-authored output?
