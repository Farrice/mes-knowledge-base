## Claims policy

- Treat scale, action-rate, therapeutic-comparison, sentiment, brand-lift, partnership, production-volume, crew, studio, set, cycle-time, and virality claims as `SELF-REPORTED`.
- Never use the therapeutic comparison as a promise, credential, analogy, or substitute-care claim.
- Never guarantee engagement, views, retention, community, or transformation.
- Label the tension-to-repair covenant, three transformations, and four scoreboards as `INFERRED` Antigravity system translations.
