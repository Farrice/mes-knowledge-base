# Cross-Domain Patterns

## Behavior-change product design

Treat content as a before-state, mechanism, after-state, and evidence problem rather than a post-generation problem.

## Community-led demand validation

Let the audience shape decisions before production while the creator retains authorship and mission judgment.

## Promise covenant

Packaging earns entry, the opening proves the contract is active, and the ending settles the emotional debt.

## Mission-constrained optimization

Use data inside the set of choices the brand is willing to stand for.

## Creative research cadence

Optimize for complete learning loops, not raw volume. The smallest useful unit still needs promise, recognition, shift, action, and feedback.

## Latent brand activation

Human objective -> creator/brand bridge -> useful act -> captured human effect -> trusted community distribution -> measured response.

Keep this as a future extension until independent campaign and measurement sources are added.
