# Quality Rubric

## 4/10: Source-compliant

- Viewer starting state and after-state exist.
- Package promise is honest.
- No therapy, guarantee, or invented-provenance claim appears.
- The result may remain generic or over-explained.

## 7/10: Practitioner-grade

- A specific scene or proof source embodies the struggle.
- The private sentence is precise.
- The opening and ending repay the package.
- A choice earns the shift.
- The positive action is safe and non-coercive.
- Discovery, retention, connection, and transformation are separate.
- A feedback decision is defined.

## 10/10: Exceptional candidate

- Provenance is unmistakable without confessional excess.
- Character, viewer, and community transformations reinforce one another.
- Tension is high while mission trust remains intact.
- The ending is surprising in form and inevitable in meaning.
- The action gives agency.
- Community learning changes the next decision.
- No framework labels, expert stitching, or imitation are visible.
- Farrice's felt verdict and real audience evidence confirm the effect.

The system cannot self-award the final item.

## Production-continuity overlay

### 4/10: Handoff-compliant

- Source and production units are traceable.
- Owners and known constraints are visible.
- Unsupported directing, editing, screenplay, software, and production decisions are not invented.

### 7/10: Practitioner-grade handoff

- Capacity fit happens before avoidable development expansion.
- Every unit preserves an explicit transformation invariant.
- Department needs, timing, continuity, and unresolved questions are actionable.
- Reuse and concurrency assumptions are labeled.
- Repeated bottleneck evidence supports the response.
- A human freshness and continuity review is named.

### 10/10: Exceptional production candidate

- The handoff reduces ambiguity without flattening creative intent.
- A low-resource reshape preserves recognition, consequence, shift, action, and mission.
- The next evidence can reverse a capacity decision.
- Real production owners confirm that the packet prevented rework or caught a continuity loss.

The system cannot self-award owner confirmation or production impact.

## Hard failures

- fabricated lived scene;
- therapy equivalence or replacement;
- guaranteed outcome;
- click promise not fulfilled;
- unsafe or coercive action;
- mission betrayal;
- more than one body author;
- views treated as transformation evidence.
- copied studio counts or cycle times presented as recommendations;
- proprietary software behavior invented beyond the source;
- production efficiency that breaks freshness, story meaning, or the transformation contract;
- a missing craft owner concealed inside a generic checklist.
