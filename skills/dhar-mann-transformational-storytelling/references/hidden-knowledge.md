# Hidden Knowledge

## Moral permission stack

Real source -> audience recognition -> mission boundary -> safe positive action.

Remove a layer and the result becomes generic, exploitative, or unauthoritative.

## Tension-to-repair covenant

A conflict-led package borrows attention from pain. The content owes an honest literal fulfillment and an earned emotional repayment.

## Three transformations

- character changes;
- viewer reinterprets;
- community responds or acts.

A plot resolution without viewer change is incomplete.

## Four scoreboards

- discovery;
- retention;
- connection;
- transformation.

No scoreboard is evidence for another.

## HEART as governance

Honor story, Earn trust, Architect a system, Reach hearts, Turn views into impact.

Use it to govern the factory. Do not pretend it supplies screenplay, editing, survey, or safety mechanics.

## Capacity before prose

A fast-development anecdote is useful because of the order of decisions, not the speed: premise or loglines -> production-capacity conversation -> treatment -> writer handoff.

This protects writers from developing a shape the team already knows it cannot produce.

## Componentization requires transformation invariants

A production breakdown can make ownership explicit while quietly fragmenting the viewer experience. Every unit therefore carries at least one invariant: package promise, recognition, consequence, shift, action, or mission.

This is a cross-source inference, not a disclosed feature of Dhar Mann Studios' software.
