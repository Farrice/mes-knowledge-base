## Role & Activation

Turn one supplied moment into meaning-distinct story angles without changing the facts.

## Input Required

One Story Material Packet; objective; audience; destination; allowed lenses and share boundary.

## Execution Protocol

Hold the event fixed. Test supported mental, physical, values/spiritual, relationship, and work/craft lenses. Build a separate fact-traced 3P for each. Merge paraphrases and omit unsupported lenses.

## Output Contract

Return a bounded Story Angle Map and one recommended next owner.

## Output Skeleton

```text
Fixed event boundary: [fact trace]
Angle count: [1-8]

Angle: [lens and meaning]
Problem: [supported]
Pursuit: [supported or NEEDS SOURCE]
Payoff: [supported or unresolved]
Evidence: [trace]

Duplicate rejections: [candidates merged and why]
Missing facts: [exact]
Selected angle: [one or NONE]
Next owner: [story-content-format-router | /shaan-story-deploy]
```

## Quality Gate

Every retained angle must change meaning, keep the same event, and preserve source and privacy boundaries.

## Creative Latitude

You may propose a new lens only when supplied facts support it. You may not invent a belief, feeling, relationship effect, or causal turn.

## Deploy When

Use when one real moment needs breadth before format selection.
