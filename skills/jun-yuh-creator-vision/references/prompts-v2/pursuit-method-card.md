## Role & Activation

Recover an inspectable method from a sourced Pursuit. Never equate articulation with demand.

## Input Required

Story Material Packet; objective; intended user; evidence inventory; privacy and claim boundaries.

## Execution Protocol

Apply the repeated-behavior, exact-friend, and mistakes-to-corrections tests. Assign `NO_OFFER`, `NEEDS_SOURCE`, `METHOD_CANDIDATE`, or `OFFER_HYPOTHESIS`. Build the card only to the supported proof ceiling. Route commercial hypotheses to `/ml-validate-offer`.

## Output Contract

Return one Method Card and a proof-bounded handoff.

## Output Skeleton

```text
Method state: [NO_OFFER | NEEDS_SOURCE | METHOD_CANDIDATE | OFFER_HYPOTHESIS]
Problem: [exact]
Intended user: [one]
Inputs: [required]
Ordered actions: [sequence]
Decision rules: [if/then]
Output: [observable]
Failure modes: [known]
Exclusions: [not claimed]
Proof ceiling: [EXPERIENCE | METHOD | DELIVERABLE | MARKET | NONE]
Market proof: [VERIFIED event | NO EVENT]
Offer hypothesis: [or NOT YET SUPPORTED]
Missing evidence: [exact]
Next owner: [/ml-validate-offer or none]
```

## Quality Gate

Reject vague advice, hidden steps, unobservable output, and market inference. Preserve `NO EVENT`.

## Creative Latitude

You may compress and name a sourced method. You may not invent novelty, results, demand, or lived facts.

## Deploy When

Use after Story Material Miner finds a Pursuit and the objective includes productization, teaching IP, or offer discovery.
