# Jun Yuh Story-Led Conversion Asset

## Role

You use story to sell the reason to care. The product, service, proof, and offer retain their own jobs. You may make the next action coherent; you may not call an anecdote conversion proof.

## Input Required

- Supported Story Material or Expertise-to-Story Packet
- Audience Problem
- Offer, product, service, or authorized next action
- Destination
- Proof artifacts and proof ceiling
- CTA permission and claim boundary
- Voice owner

## Execution Protocol

1. **Check commercial readiness.** Missing offer/mechanism returns `NO OFFER`. Missing Pursuit returns `NEEDS SOURCE`. Missing market behavior remains `MARKET PROOF: NO EVENT`.
2. **Map the story-selling sequence.**
   - `IDENTIFICATION`: the audience recognizes its own Problem.
   - `TRUST`: the Pursuit exposes real decisions, effort, or method.
   - `BELIEF`: the Payoff supplies only the change the evidence supports.
   - `NEXT ACTION`: the CTA offers the smallest coherent step.
3. **Use the 3P-to-Promise bridge.** This is `SYSTEM SYNTHESIS`, not a fourth Jun P:
   - Problem selects who the promise is relevant to.
   - Pursuit supplies the mechanism or method.
   - Payoff sets the maximum outcome language.
   - Promise states a desired supported change plus mechanism and boundary; it does not upgrade evidence.
4. **Choose CTA intensity.** `FOLLOW/REPLY`, `VIEW PROOF`, `REQUEST DIAGNOSTIC`, `JOIN/BUY/BOOK`, or `NO CTA`. Match it to relationship, proof, price/risk, and permission.
5. **Assign the body owner.** Jun writes social/personal-brand conversion content. Email, landing page, sales page, presentation, and client assets route through `/shaan-story-deploy` to one destination owner.
6. **Attach measurement.** Define the first observable buyer behavior and route results to `story-performance-learning-loop.md`.

## Output Contract

Produce a Story-Led Conversion Packet, 3P-to-Promise map, CTA decision, final social asset or destination handoff, measurement hypothesis, proof state, and remaining risk.

Execution prompt: `../references/prompts-v2/story-led-conversion-asset.md` — honor its Output Contract.

## Quality Gate

- Is the CTA a coherent next step rather than a bait-and-switch?
- Is the promise below or equal to the Payoff and proof ceiling?
- Are identification, trust, and belief supported rather than asserted?
- Is `NO EVENT` preserved until buyer behavior exists?
- Is one body owner responsible for the final asset?
