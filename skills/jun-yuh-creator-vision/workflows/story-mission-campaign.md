# Jun Yuh Story Mission Campaign Builder

## Role

You turn one supported 3P into a coherent campaign. Each asset has one job. Reusing the same story is allowed; repeating the same angle with different hooks is not.

## Input Required

- Story Material Packet or Expertise-to-Story Packet
- Audience and message
- Destination/platform
- Offer or next action when relevant
- Proof ceiling and share boundary
- Available assets and production capacity

## Execution Protocol

1. Validate the source packet. Missing Pursuit returns `NEEDS SOURCE`; a direct-work task returns `NO STORY`.
2. Create four mission briefs:
   - `ATTRACT`: maximize recognition. Lead with the sharpest audience Problem and one open loop. CTA asks for low-friction attention or identification.
   - `NURTURE`: deepen understanding. Reveal the Pursuit, mistakes, decisions, and what changed. CTA invites reflection, save, reply, or continued learning.
   - `POSITION`: demonstrate judgment. Contrast the expert's method with the common approach and show the supported standard or mechanism. CTA points to a proof artifact or deeper explanation.
   - `CONVERT`: connect the audience Problem to the supported method and a fitting next action. Use the strongest admissible Payoff; never imply demand or client results that are not present.
3. Give each mission a meaning-distinct angle, evidence job, and CTA. Collapse duplicates.
4. Select one of Jun's six source-demonstrated formats for each social asset, or route a non-social destination through `/shaan-story-deploy`.
5. Preserve a serial bridge: when supported, one Payoff may seed the next asset's Problem.
6. Produce finished social drafts only when the facts, voice owner, and destination are sufficient. Otherwise produce executable briefs.

## Output Contract

Produce a four-row Story Mission Map, four asset briefs or finished social assets, a serial Payoff-to-Problem bridge, production order, proof/claim boundary, and campaign receipt.

Execution prompt: `../references/prompts-v2/story-mission-campaign.md` — honor its Output Contract.

## Quality Gate

- Does every asset have exactly one primary mission?
- Are the four angles meaning-distinct rather than hook variants?
- Does NURTURE expose real Pursuit detail and POSITION expose real judgment?
- Does CONVERT stop at the proof ceiling and use an authorized CTA?
- Is campaign continuity earned by the source rather than invented?
