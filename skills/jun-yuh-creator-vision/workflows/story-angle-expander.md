# Jun Yuh Story Angle Expander

## Role

You change the meaning lens, not the biography. You may find fewer angles than requested; you may not invent experience to fill a matrix.

## Skill Acquisition

Read `../references/selling-course-ledger.md` and the supplied Story Material Packet. Keep hook libraries and the Content Permutation Engine cold.

## Input Required

- One bounded lived moment and its fact trace
- Supported Problem, Pursuit, and Payoff material
- Objective, audience, destination, and share boundary

## Execution Protocol

1. Hold the event boundary fixed.
2. Test only supported lenses: mental, physical, values/spiritual, relationship, work/craft, or another operator-supplied lens.
3. For each lens, construct a separate fact-traced 3P. Mark missing beats `[NEEDS SOURCE]`.
4. Reject paraphrases: two candidates are duplicates when they share the same Problem, causal Pursuit, and Payoff meaning despite different wording.
5. Rank 1–3 strongest angles by objective fit and evidence completeness. A supported Payoff may seed the next Problem, but chronology and causality must remain sourced.
6. Send the selected angle back to `story-content-format-router` for Jun social work or `/shaan-story-deploy` for cross-domain dosage.

## Output Contract

Return a Story Angle Map containing the fixed event boundary, 1–8 meaning-distinct angles, evidence labels, separate 3Ps, duplicate rejections, missing facts, ranked recommendation, and next owner.

Execution prompt: `../references/prompts-v2/story-angle-map.md`.

## Quality Gate

- Is every angle traceable to the same supplied moment?
- Does each retained angle change meaning rather than wording?
- Does each have its own supported 3P or explicit gap?
- Were unsupported lenses omitted?
- Can one angle or no angle win?
