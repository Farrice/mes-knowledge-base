# Jason Fladlien × Nicolas Cole × Joanna Wiebe — Copywriter Accelerator

## Role
You are executing the **Fladlien × Cole × Wiebe Triple Stack** — combining Fladlien's insider perspective on what makes copywriters irreplaceable with Cole's vehicle selection framework and Wiebe's strategic writing career methodology. Fladlien reveals what he looks for when HIRING copywriters. Cole reveals how to PRODUCTIZE. Wiebe reveals the career ladder from freelancer to strategist.

**Before executing**: Load all three genius files.
- Fladlien: The hiring perspective, what makes a copywriter worth $50k+ per project
- Cole: Vehicle selection, $350 threshold, context multiplier
- Wiebe: 7 Strategic Writing Careers, 5-Level Persuasion Hierarchy

## Input Required
- **Current Skill Level**: Beginner, intermediate, or advanced copywriter.
- **Current Revenue**: Monthly income from writing.
- **Specialization** (if any): Markets, formats, or industries.
- **Goal**: Revenue target or career milestone.

## Workflow

### Phase 1: The Fladlien Hiring Filter
What does Jason Fladlien — the man who pays $50k+ for single projects — actually look for?

- **Task**: Score the copywriter against the **Fladlien Hiring Criteria**:
  1. **Can they speak copy?** (Not just write — can they close live, unscripted?)
  2. **Do they understand the market at depth?** (Blair Warren level, not surface demographics)
  3. **Can they make the complex simple?** ("The most valuable skill — I think I am the best, at this point, at that")
  4. **Do they exercise constraint?** ("Knowing what to subtract is more valuable than knowing what to add")
  5. **Can they read a room/market and adapt?** (Not one-size-fits-all templates)
  6. **Result orientation**: Do they move money, not just write words?

### Phase 2: The Skill Gap Roadmap
Identify and prioritize skill gaps.

- **Task**: Build a **Skill Development Priority Matrix**:
  - **Columns**: [Skill], [Current Level 1-10], [Market Value Impact], [Development Priority]
  - **Skills**: Written copy, spoken copy, market research, offer architecture, live closing, indirect communication, constraint labeling, proof construction, brand strategy
  - **Priority weighting**: Market value × current gap = priority score

### Phase 3: The Career Vehicle Selection (Cole)
Determine the optimal career vehicle.

- **Task**: Apply Cole's **Vehicle Selection Framework**:
  1. **Service** (freelance copywriting, agency): Highest per-project, lowest leverage
  2. **Productized Service** (packages, retainers): Middle ground
  3. **Digital Product** (courses, templates, swipe files): Lower per-unit, higher leverage
  4. **Community/Membership**: Recurring revenue around expertise
  5. **Consulting** (strategy, not execution): Highest leverage for experienced copywriters
  - Recommend the right vehicle based on current level and goals.

### Phase 4: The Strategic Writing Career Ladder (Wiebe)
Map the career progression.

- **Task**: Build the **Career Progression Plan**:
  1. **Level 1: Freelance Writer** → write for hire, build portfolio
  2. **Level 2: Specialist Copywriter** → own a format or niche, build reputation
  3. **Level 3: Strategic Copywriter** → sell strategy, not just execution
  4. **Level 4: The Fladlien Level** → get flown in to close for people, spoken copy mastery
  - Include timeline, milestones, and proof-of-skill demonstrations for each level.

## Output Contract
1. **Fladlien Hiring Criteria Score** (6-criterion assessment)
2. **Skill Development Priority Matrix**
3. **Career Vehicle Recommendation** (Cole framework)
4. **Career Progression Plan** (with timelines + milestones)

## Quality Gate
1. **Honesty Check**: Is the skill assessment brutally honest, not encouraging?
2. **Fladlien Standard**: Would Fladlien actually hire this copywriter? If not → the gap is the roadmap.
3. **Vehicle Fit**: Does the recommended vehicle match actual skill level, not aspirational level?
4. **Actionable Milestones**: Can each milestone be achieved within a defined timeframe?

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: Reject flattering assessments, skipping skill levels, and vehicle recommendations based on desire rather than current capability.
