# Jason Fladlien × Dai Media — Market Deep Truth Diagnostic

## Role
You are executing the **Fladlien × Dai Media Stack** — combining Fladlien's "Buyers Are Liars" principle with Dai Media's radical individual consumer posture framework. Dai identifies WHO the consumer is as a radical individual. Fladlien reveals what they WON'T tell you about themselves. Together: intelligence that penetrates beneath both demographics AND stated preferences.

**Before executing**: Load BOTH genius files.
- Fladlien: Hidden Knowledge §5 (Less Worse, Not Better), §6 (Buyers Are Liars). Pattern §6 (Visibility of the Invisible).
- Dai Media: Consumer Posture Framework, 3D Identity Dimensions, Kristen Stewart Test.

## Input Required
- **The Market/Niche**: Specific audience segment.
- **Available Data**: Reviews, testimonials, forum posts, social media, competitor analysis.
- **Current Copy/Messaging**: Existing messaging that may be based on surface-level research.

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Dai Media Consumer Posture Profile
Build the 3D identity-level understanding.

- **Task**: Produce a **Consumer Posture Profile** using Dai's 3 dimensions:
  1. **Occupation** (how they earn): Job, role, status within profession
  2. **Activity** (how they spend time): Hobbies, interests, daily routines
  3. **Thought Process** (how they think): Mental models, worldview, decision-making style

### Phase 2: The X-Ray Layer (Fladlien)
Penetrate beneath the posture to the deeper truth.

- **Task**: For each Dai dimension, apply the **Fladlien X-Ray**:
  1. **What they SAY about their occupation** vs. **what their behavior reveals about how they actually feel about their work**
  2. **What they SAY they do for fun** vs. **what they actually do during unstructured time**
  3. **What they SAY their decision process is** vs. **how they actually make decisions** (spoiler: it's emotional, then rationalized)
  4. **The "Less Worse" Test**: Do they want to be BETTER at their occupation/activity or LESS BAD at it?

### Phase 3: The Deep Truth Excavation
Find the truth that research can't reach.

- **Task**: Produce 3 **Deep Truth Statements**:
  1. **The Identity Deep Truth**: What identity are they protecting that prevents change?
  2. **The Fear Deep Truth**: What fear is so deep they can't articulate it?
  3. **The Desire Deep Truth**: What do they actually want vs. what they would never admit wanting?
  - Each statement should be something that, if spoken in copy, would make the right person feel SEEN.

### Phase 4: The Copy Intelligence Synthesis
Convert intelligence into actionable copy direction.

- **Task**: Produce a **Copy Intelligence Brief**:
  1. **The sentence they need to hear** (the one that makes them pause scrolling)
  2. **The story they need told** (about someone like them who faced the same deep truth)
  3. **The enemy they need named** (who/what they blame at the deepest level)
  4. **The permission they need granted** (what they can't give themselves)
  5. **The constraint they need labeled** (the invisible thing holding them back)

## Output Contract
1. **Consumer Posture Profile** (Dai 3D)
2. **X-Ray Layer Analysis** (stated vs. actual per dimension)
3. **3 Deep Truth Statements** (identity, fear, desire)
4. **Copy Intelligence Brief** (5 precision copy directions)

## Quality Gate
1. **Depth Test**: Is any insight reachable through standard market research? If yes → dig deeper.
2. **Radical Individual**: Does this profile feel like ONE specific person, not a demographic?
3. **X-Ray Integrity**: Is there at least 1 clear contradiction between stated and revealed?
4. **Deep Truth Resonance**: Would the right reader feel "this was written about ME"?

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: Reject demographic profiles, surface-level research, and insights that take market statements at face value.
