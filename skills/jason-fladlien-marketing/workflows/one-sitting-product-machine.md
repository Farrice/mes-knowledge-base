# Jason Fladlien — One-Sitting Product Machine (The Quantity Leap)

> "One problem. One solution. One sitting. Sold at an absurdly low price. That's the doctor's orders."
> — Jason Fladlien

> "You have an uncashed check inside of you in the form of a product — you just haven't figured out a way to liberate it."
> — Jason Fladlien (Product eClass)

## Role
You are executing Fladlien's info-product velocity engine — the system that took him from a $4 product to funding a $100M webinar business, and that took his student Maddie from a 3-month-stalled idea to 272 sales in 24 hours. The core bet: momentum beats perfection, quantity produces quality, and a few of many shipped products will change the operator's life. This workflow produces small products FAST and stacks them into ladders.

**Before executing**: Load `genius.md` — especially patterns #28-#29 (One Problem/One Solution/One Sitting, Inevitable Success Frame), #22 (E-Class Ladder), and hidden knowledge "Page Nine of the 384-Page Book."

## Input Required
- **Operator's expertise pool**: What they know, teach, or have results in (the "uncashed checks")
- **Audience access**: List size, platform, or marketplace where buyers exist
- **Pain inventory**: Who is HURTING right now in this market (not who wants improvement)
- **Time available per sitting**: Realistic single-session block (2-8 hours)
- **Existing flops/assets** (optional): Prior products that can be redeployed as bonuses

## Workflow

### Phase 1 — Extract the Singular Problem
1. Inventory half-baked ideas and stalled projects — anything "almost there for months" goes on the list first (these are the uncashed checks).
2. **Page-Nine mining**: scan the market's bestselling how-to books/courses; find the one buried element carrying most of the actionable value. Gut the other 95%. A 10-page outcome at $7 beats a 384-page history lesson.
3. Select by **Pain Before Gain**: "Before you sell the plan to heaven, provide the path out of hell." Pick Jane with the crippling toothache (existing pain, pays quicker, larger amounts, lower expectations, likelier to succeed) over Thomas who wants a whiter smile (aspiration).
4. Lock the spec: **one problem, one solution, one sitting**. If it needs two sittings, the problem is too big — split it into two products.

### Phase 2 — The Four Multipliers Pass
Run the product spec through each multiplier; strengthen until all four hold:
1. **Better Than Free**: Do the buyer's math out loud — time saved × their hourly value vs. price. The product must be MORE EXPENSIVE NOT TO BUY. (John the $15/hr barista: 10 hours of research = $150 opportunity cost vs. your $9 ebook.)
2. **Pain Before Gain**: The promise names the escape from an existing hurt, not a future upgrade.
3. **Instant Gratification**: Smaller result sooner beats bigger result later. The buyer gets a usable win the SAME DAY. (Then they'll happily pay you for the bigger result later.)
4. **Hyper-Specificity**: Narrow the title/promise until one person with one problem knows it's unmistakably for them. Absurdly low price is a feature: it buys momentum, buyers, and proof.

### Phase 3 — Ship, Ladder, Repeat (Inevitable Success)
1. **One-sitting build**: outline live (problem → solution steps → same-day win), record/write in a single session, brown-paper-bag packaging (an order form is enough — resonance first, polish later).
2. Pre-commit the **Inevitable Success branches**: sells well → scale it; flops → becomes a free lead magnet or bonus on the next product; does okay → ship the next one. There is no failure branch, so there is no procrastination.
3. **Ladder it (E-Class model)**: the product's 4-8 steps become a 4-6 session live class sold to buyers ("same product, more depth, more examples, more interaction — $197"); the final session pitches the next class. Demonstrate the value with the media you sell.
4. Set the cadence: one product per sitting, sittings on the calendar, post-mortem after each launch ("run the film back — what did I learn?"). Quality emerges from the volume.
