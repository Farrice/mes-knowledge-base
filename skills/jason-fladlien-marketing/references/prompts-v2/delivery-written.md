## Execution Protocol

1. Identify the reader, their task and the supported action. Mark claimed buyer beliefs as supplied evidence or hypothesis.
2. Select one weak link: delayed relevance (D01/D06), naked insight (D04), absent demonstration (D09), excessive promise (D12), or vague ending (D17).
3. Preserve the draft's argument and useful language. Give the insight a concrete setup with one relevant example. Demonstrate the work using supplied material; label any hypothetical outside the copy.
4. Compress spoken repetition into one precise line (D03/D05). Replace call-response with a usable self-check only when it earns its space. No stage directions or invented audience reaction.
5. If an offer is present, set up its component through the need it solves (D07), state real scope and retain alternatives. A free action must work without buying (D12).
6. Finish on the smallest useful next action. Produce the actual post, email or page section at requested length, then a compact trace. Apply the user's existing voice and anti-slop checks.

## Output Contract

Finished written artifact; short before/after diagnosis; source IDs; factual gaps; one outcome test.
Return finished work first. Put analytical labels outside audience-facing copy. Source-trace entries use D01-D17 from the source ledger. Do not expose technique terminology to the audience.

## Quality Gate

- Reader can identify the useful outcome and actual action.
- Every factual claim has supplied support or is marked outside the copy as unresolved.
- Medium-specific behavior is respected; no fabricated crowd or simulated consent.
- One concrete demonstration or an explicitly named missing proof object.
- Voice, original promise and existing safety boundaries survive the rewrite.
- Final action is feasible; free and paid paths are honestly distinguished when used.
- No claim of conversion improvement, scientific validation or human taste approval from a structural check.

## Deploy When

Use written mode in `/fladlien-delivery` when the content exists or the brief is clear and reception/delivery is the consequential gap. Do not replace offer strategy, source research or visual production with this adapter.
