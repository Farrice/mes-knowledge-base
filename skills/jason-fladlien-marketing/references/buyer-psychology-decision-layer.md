## Supporting Corpus Moves

### Four response modes

`8U0BDpRnPFU`, beginning `03:36`, describes four responses: agree, challenge, reflect, or ignore.

- Default to **reflect** when the premise or emotion is not yet understood.
- **Agree** with the valid part without automatically adopting the conclusion.
- **Challenge** softly and with evidence when preserving the current view creates a material error or harm.
- **Ignore** only for genuinely non-material noise; never ignore a disclosure, safety concern, consent boundary, material objection, or factual correction.

This is a response-selection aid, not a license to steer every conversation toward a sale.

### Loaded-language dual use

`ooGeFK70d5U`, beginning `01:04`, defines loaded language as emotionally charged wording and spends substantial time on defensive literacy. Apply it in two passes:

1. **De-load for truth:** replace the charged word with a neutral alternative and ask whether the claim remains accurate.
2. **Re-load for honest salience:** use vivid language only when the emotional charge matches the underlying evidence and stakes.

Do not use a charged word to smuggle in a fact, moral judgment, urgency, or enemy that the evidence does not support.

### Schema source: empathy, not targeting

`nGZbkwKboVU` summarizes five domains and eighteen early maladaptive schemas beginning `00:33`. Because this is clinical-adjacent material and Jason is not the scientific owner:

- do not label a buyer with a schema;

Clinical application belongs to qualified professionals and appropriate evidence, not this marketing layer.

### Applied webinar sequence

The explanatory lab `H_TvNSNbRiU` demonstrates a practitioner sequence:

1. open with interaction that also surfaces objections (`00:00–03:00`);
2. set a proofable promise and open loop (`03:00–05:00`);
3. define the model and teach in named chunks (`05:00–16:00`);
4. transition by acknowledging the limits of a single session (`16:00–19:00`);
5. present core, price, bonuses, risk reversal, and questions (`19:00–30:11`).
