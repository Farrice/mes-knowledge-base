# 8-Fold Insight Elaborator

Take a promising raw insight vector and develop it through Luke Iha's 8-beat structure — producing a complete copy ammunition package ready for deployment in VSLs, ads, content, emails, or landing pages.

---

## Inputs Required

1. **Raw Insight Vector** — The one-sentence vector (from `/insight-vectors` or provided directly)
2. **Vector Type** — Which systems-grammar type is it? (Reverse causation, hidden constraint, etc.)
3. **Target Format** — What will this elaborate into? (VSL lead, ad script, content piece, landing page, email)
4. **Proof Assets Available** — What research, data, testimonials, or case studies exist to support this?

---

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: The raw vector must pass SIN ≥21 before elaboration. If unscored, run the SIN filter from `genius.md` first.

## The 8-Fold Elaboration

### Beat 1: Paradoxical Question (The Hook)
Craft a question that violates the audience's expectations. This IS the hook.

**Formula**: "Why does [thing audience does/believes] sometimes produce [opposite of expected result]?"

- Must create a knowledge gap the audience physically cannot ignore
- Must reference something the audience has EXPERIENCED, not just heard about
- Test: Would someone stop scrolling to find out the answer?

**Output**: 3 variations of the paradoxical question, ranked by gut punch.

### Beat 2: UMP/UMS (The Mechanism)
Name and explain the mechanism that resolves the paradox.

**If UMP (Unique Mechanism of the Problem)**:
- Name the problem mechanism: the hidden process causing the suffering
- Characterize it (2-3 word name: "The Fat Brake," "Commitment Evaporation")
- Explain it in one paragraph using accessible language

**If UMS (Unique Mechanism of the Solution)**:
- Name the solution mechanism: the hidden process that fixes things
- Characterize it
- Explain why this mechanism works when others don't

**Output**: Named mechanism with one-paragraph explanation.

### Beat 3: Trigger (The Urgency Creator)
Identify the emotional moment that makes this vector urgent NOW, not later.

**Formula**: "By the time most people notice [symptom], [mechanism] has been [doing damage] for [timeframe]."

- Must be time-sensitive ("your window is closing")
- Must feel diagnostic ("you might already be...")
- Must NOT be fear-based manipulation — it should feel like a doctor's honest warning

**Output**: 2 trigger statements, one fear-forward, one empowerment-forward.

### Beat 4: Testable Proof (The Credibility Anchor)
Provide evidence that validates the vector. In order of persuasive power:

1. **Specific research** — "[University] study in [Journal], [year], found [specific finding with numbers]"
2. **Observable experience** — "Try this yourself: [simple test the audience can run]"
3. **Social proof** — "[Person/group] discovered that [result]"
4. **Logical proof** — "If [mechanism] is true, then you'd expect [prediction]. And that's exactly what [data] shows."

**Output**: 1-2 proof statements from the strongest available category.

### Beat 5: Intensifiers (The Compound)
Elements that make the vector feel more relevant, personal, and urgent:

- **Age intensifier**: "And it gets worse after [age]..."
- **Demographic intensifier**: "Women/men are [X]% more susceptible because..."
- **Compounding intensifier**: "Each time you [common behavior], the effect compounds..."
- **Proximity intensifier**: "Right now, [percentage] of people reading this are already..."

**Output**: 2-3 intensifiers that compound without feeling manipulative.

### Beat 6: Myths & Mistakes (The Demolition)
Wrong beliefs this insight vector corrects. Each myth demolished clears space for your solution.

**Formula**: "This is why [common advice] fails. You're [metaphor for inefficiency]."

- Name 2-3 myths the audience currently holds
- For each, show WHY it fails in light of the mechanism
- Use the mechanism as the demolition tool

**Output**: 2-3 myth demolitions with mechanism-based explanations.

### Beat 7: Cause (The Root Explanation)
The deepest layer — the ROOT cause that the mechanism traces back to.

**Formula**: "The root cause is [systemic/structural/environmental factor] creating [ongoing process]."

- Must feel like the "final answer" — no further "but why?" remains
- Should feel slightly surprising — deeper than the audience expected
- Can reference external/environmental/systemic factors (not blame the audience)

**Output**: One root-cause statement that closes the causal loop.

### Beat 8: Resolution (The Product Bridge)
What to do about all of this — naturally leading to your product/offer.

**Formula**: "Breaking this cycle requires addressing [root cause/mechanism] FIRST, before [common approach] can work."

- Position the product as the upstream fix
- Create logical necessity: "You MUST do X before Y can work"
- Never hard-sell — the elaboration above should make the resolution feel inevitable

**Output**: Resolution statement + natural product bridge.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Format | Beat Order | Beats to Emphasize | Beats to Compress |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **VSL Lead** | 1→2→3→5→4→6→7→8 | Beat 1 (hook), Beat 3 (urgency) | Beat 7 (cause — can be brief) |
| **Ad Script (30-60s)** | 1→2→6→8 | Beat 1 (hook), Beat 6 (myth bust) | Beats 3,4,5,7 (compressed or cut) |
| **LinkedIn Post** | 1→6→2→4→8 | Beat 1 (hook), Beat 4 (proof) | Beats 3,5 (usually cut) |
| **Email** | 1→3→2→4→8 | Beat 3 (trigger), Beat 2 (mechanism reveal) | Beat 5 (intensifiers — save for email 2) |
| **Landing Page** | 1→2→4→5→6→7→8 | All — full deployment | Beat 3 (placed in subheadline) |

---

## Quality Gate

- ☐ Paradoxical question creates genuine curiosity (not just shock)
- ☐ Mechanism has a characterization name (not unnamed)
- ☐ Proof is specific (not "studies show")
- ☐ Resolution feels inevitable, not forced
- ☐ No beat feels fabricated — all grounded in available reality
- ☐ Deployment notes include format-specific beat ordering

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: Review `genius.md` § Anti-Patterns. Watch for fabricated proof, claimed vectors, and complexity without clarity.
