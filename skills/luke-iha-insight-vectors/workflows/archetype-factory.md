# Archetype Factory

People are compulsively drawn to classification systems that explain their own behavior. This workflow builds a complete typing system for any domain — creating the "What's your ______ type?" framework that generates high-engagement content, lead magnets, quiz funnels, and audience segmentation.

---

## Inputs Required

1. **Domain** — What are we classifying? (Metabolism types, learning styles, leadership profiles, creative blocks, etc.)
2. **Audience** — Who will be typed? What do they already believe about their own category?
3. **Product/Offer** — What solution connects to each type? (Determines if each type gets a unique prescription)
4. **Content Goal** — Quiz funnel? Content series? Lead magnet? Email segmentation?

---

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: Load `genius.md` § Pattern 5 (System Archetypes). Typing systems work because they validate the audience's intuition that they're different from average.

## Phase 1: Observable Behavior Inventory

List 10-15 observable behaviors, symptoms, or experiences that DIFFER within the audience:

| # | Observable Behavior | Variation A | Variation B | Variation C |
|---|-------------------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | "[specific thing that varies across the audience]" | [one way it manifests] | [another way] | [third way if applicable] |

**Mining prompts**:
- "Not everyone in this audience has the same experience. What differs?"
- "When two people try the same approach, why does one succeed and one fail?"
- "What do people in this niche argue about?"

---

## Phase 2: Cluster Analysis

Group the behaviors into 3-5 natural clusters:

### Cluster Rules:
1. **3-5 types** is optimal. Fewer feels shallow; more creates decision fatigue.
2. **Types should feel mutually exclusive** (I'm clearly THIS one, not THAT one)
3. **No type should feel "bad"** — every type has strengths AND challenges
4. **Types should map to different solutions** (if your product has variation, each type gets a tailored prescription)

### Clustering Template:

| Type (Working Name) | Defining Behaviors | Core Struggle | Hidden Strength | Percentage (est.) |
|---------------------|-------------------|---------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Type A | [behaviors 2, 5, 7] | [their specific pain] | [what they do BETTER than other types] | [~X%] |
| Type B | [behaviors 1, 4, 9] | [their specific pain] | [hidden strength] | [~X%] |
| Type C | [behaviors 3, 6, 8, 10] | [their specific pain] | [hidden strength] | [~X%] |

## Phase 3: Characterization (Naming)

Each type needs a name that:
- ☐ Is instantly visual — creates an image
- ☐ Feels aspirational or at least neutral (never insulting)
- ☐ Is 2-3 words maximum
- ☐ Creates "I'm TOTALLY that one!" identification
- ☐ Works in conversation ("I'm a ______")

## Phase 4: Diagnostic System

Build the sorting mechanism (quiz questions, self-assessment markers):

### 5-7 Diagnostic Questions:

| # | Question | Type A Answer | Type B Answer | Type C Answer |
|---|---------|--------------|--------------|--------------|
| 1 | "[Scenario or behavior question]" | [their response] | [their response] | [their response] |

### Rules for diagnostic questions:
- Ask about BEHAVIORS, not beliefs (people lie about beliefs, not behaviors)
- Each question should clearly differentiate at least 2 types
- Questions should feel insightful ("Wow, I never thought about it that way")
- The diagnosis itself should feel like a mini insight vector

---

## Phase 5: Content & Deployment Architecture

### Content Series (one per type):
| Type | Content Topic | Hook | Insight Vector Embedded |
|------|-------------|------|----------------------|
| [Type A] | "[topic specific to their struggle]" | "[hook that makes them self-identify]" | [which vector type is embedded] |

### Lead Magnet / Quiz Structure:
- **Title**: "What's Your [Domain] Type?" or "The [Domain] Type Quiz"
- **Length**: 5-7 questions
- **Result page**: Type name + description + specific prescription + CTA

### Email Segmentation:
- Each type gets a separate email sequence
- Opening email acknowledges their type's specific experience
- Sequence addresses their specific struggle → product as tailored solution

---

## Quality Gate

- ☐ 3-5 types that feel mutually exclusive and complete
- ☐ All type names pass the characterization test (visual, memorable, non-insulting)
- ☐ Diagnostic questions differentiate types through behavior, not identity
- ☐ Each type has a unique prescription (not "same product for everyone")
- ☐ Content series has at least one piece per type with an embedded insight vector
- ☐ The typing system itself feels like a genuine insight (not arbitrary categories)

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: Types must emerge from real behavioral variation, not be imposed. If you can't find 3 distinct clusters from real observable differences, this domain doesn't support an archetype system — don't force it.
