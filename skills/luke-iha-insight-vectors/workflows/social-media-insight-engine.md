# Social Media Insight Engine

Transform insight vectors into platform-native social content where every post delivers a genuine micro-epiphany instead of recycled advice. Each piece is engineered to earn saves and shares (not just likes) by creating the "I never thought about it that way" reaction.

---

## Inputs Required

1. **Insight Vectors** — 3-5 shortlisted vectors from `/insight-vectors` (or will generate from topic)
2. **Platform(s)** — LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram (carousel/caption), TikTok/Reels script, or all
3. **Brand Voice** — Professional, provocative, warm-authority, analytical, or provide sample posts
4. **Audience** — Who follows you on this platform? What content performs best?
5. **Content Goal** — Authority building? Lead generation? Audience growth? Email opt-ins?

## Phase 2: Platform-Specific Content Generation

### LinkedIn Posts (2-3 per session)

**Structure for LinkedIn Insight Posts:**
```
[Hook — paradoxical question or counter-intuitive statement]

[Blank line — LinkedIn truncation gap, must earn the "see more"]

[Setup — what the audience currently believes (1-2 sentences)]

[Flip — the insight vector delivered as revelation (1-2 sentences)]

[Elaboration — mechanism + one proof point (2-3 sentences)]

[Implication — what this means for the reader's next action]

[CTA or open question that drives comments]
```

**Rules**:
- First line must stop the scroll — use the paradoxical question from the 8-Fold structure
- NEVER start with "I" (too common, trains the algorithm to compress)
- The flip must happen BEFORE "see more" if possible, or immediately after
- End with a question that invites personal experience sharing
- 800-1,200 characters optimal for LinkedIn

### Twitter/X Threads (1-2 per session)

**Structure for X Insight Threads:**
```
Tweet 1: [Hook — bold counter-intuitive claim] (this IS the insight vector)
Tweet 2: [The belief most people hold]
Tweet 3: [The flip — what's actually true + mechanism name]
Tweet 4: [Proof — specific data, study, or observable experience]
Tweet 5: [Implication — what to do differently]
Tweet 6: [CTA — retweet if, follow for more, link]
```

**Rules**:
- Tweet 1 must work as a standalone viral tweet
- Each tweet is one idea — no compound sentences
- Use numbers and specifics
- Thread length: 5-8 tweets (longer = more follower drift)

### Twitter/X Single Tweets (3-5 per session)

**Template options:**
- **Hot Take**: "[Counter-intuitive claim]. Here's why: [1-sentence mechanism]."
- **Loop Reveal**: "[Vicious cycle described in one tweet]. Most people don't see the loop."
- **Leading Indicator**: "Stop tracking [lagging indicator]. Start tracking [leading indicator]. You'll see the [outcome] shifts 3 weeks earlier."
- **Archetype Bait**: "There are 3 types of [domain]: [Type A], [Type B], and [Type C]. Most people think they're [most common] but they're actually [surprise]."

### Instagram Carousel (1-2 per session)

**Structure for Insight Carousels (7-10 slides):**
```
Slide 1: [Bold hook — designed for the Explore page]
Slide 2: [What "everyone" believes about this topic]
Slide 3: [But here's what's actually happening...]
Slide 4: [The mechanism — named + visualized]
Slide 5-6: [Proof + intensifiers]
Slide 7: [What this means for you]
Slide 8: [CTA — save this for later / share with someone who needs this]
```

**Rules**:
- Font must be large enough to read on mobile without zooming
- Slide 1 hook must work on Explore page (no context assumed)
- Use contrast colors for "what you think" vs "what's actually true"
- Final slide CTA should drive saves (algorithm booster)

### Reel/TikTok Script (1-2 per session)

**Structure for Insight Reels (30-60s):**
```
[0-3s] Hook: [Paradoxical question — must stop the scroll]
[3-10s] Setup: "Most people think [common belief]..."
[10-20s] Flip: "But [insight vector]. And here's why..."
[20-40s] Mechanism: [Named mechanism explained simply]
[40-50s] Proof: [One specific proof point]
[50-60s] CTA: "Follow for more [domain] breakdowns"
```

## Quality Gate

- ☐ Every piece contains at least one genuine insight vector (not just advice)
- ☐ Platform-native formatting (LinkedIn ≠ Twitter ≠ Instagram)
- ☐ Hooks pass the scroll-stop test (paradoxical, counter-intuitive, or type-bait)
- ☐ No AI slop words (leverage, utilize, robust, delve, etc.)
- ☐ CTA matches content goal (authority → no hard sell; lead gen → clear opt-in)
- ☐ Save/share triggers are embedded naturally, not pasted on

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: Social content that TEACHES without creating an AHA MOMENT is just advice. Every piece must deliver a micro-epiphany that the reader FEELS, not just understands. If a piece reads like "5 tips for..." — it's not using an insight vector.
