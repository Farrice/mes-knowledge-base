# Insight Content Series

Design a multi-part content series (5-12 episodes) where each piece delivers a different insight vector type. The series builds audience conviction through STACKED EPIPHANIES — each episode makes the next feel more inevitable, creating what Luke calls "the conversion cascade" where the audience doesn't just believe you, they feel like they've independently discovered the truth.

---

## Inputs Required

1. **Series Theme** — The overarching topic (e.g., "Why everything you know about weight loss is engineered backwards")
2. **Product/Offer** — What the series eventually sells (or positions you for)
3. **Platform** — Newsletter, YouTube, podcast, LinkedIn series, Instagram carousel series, blog
4. **Audience** — Who, what they believe, what they've tried
5. **Insight Vectors** — Pre-generated from `/insight-vectors` (or will generate during workflow)
6. **Series Length** — 5, 7, 10, or 12 episodes (recommended: 7 as default)

---

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: Load `genius.md` § HK8 (Vectors Stack — And the Stack Creates Religious Certainty). The key principle: each vector in the series must point to the SAME conclusion from a DIFFERENT angle.

## Phase 1: Series Architecture

### Step 1.1: Convergent Thesis
Define the ONE conclusion all episodes point toward:
- **Convergent Thesis**: "[The single statement the audience will believe by the end of the series]"
- This is NOT your product pitch — it's the UNDERSTANDING that makes your product the obvious choice

### Step 1.2: Arc Structure

| Arc Phase | Episodes | Purpose | Audience Emotional State |
|----------|---------|---------|------------------------|
| **Setup** (Ep 1-2) | Hook + Disrupt | Shatter the current mental model | Curiosity → "Wait, what?" |
| **Deepening** (Ep 3-5) | Reveal + Stack | Build the new model with evidence | Realization → "Oh, that explains it..." |
| **Turn** (Ep 6) | Consolidate | Name the complete system | Conviction → "Now I see the whole picture" |
| **Resolution** (Ep 7+) | Apply + Bridge | What to do about it (→ product) | Agency → "I need to act on this" |

### Step 1.3: Cliffhanger Architecture
Each episode must end with a FORWARD HOOK into the next:
- **"But there's a catch..."** — introduce a complication that the next episode resolves
- **"And it gets worse..."** — reveal an intensifier that escalates in the next episode
- **"The question is..."** — pose the paradox that the next episode answers
- **"Next, we'll show you..."** — direct tease (lowest engagement — use sparingly)

## Phase 2: Episode Vector Assignment

Assign a unique insight vector TYPE to each episode:

| Episode | Title (Working) | Vector Type | Specific Vector | Role in Arc |
|---------|----------------|------------|-----------------|-------------|
| 1 | "[Hook title]" | **Reverse Causation** or **False Assumption** | [specific vector] | DISRUPT — shatter the primary causal belief |
| 2 | "[Setup title]" | **Vicious Cycle** | [specific vector] | TRAP — show them the invisible loop they're in |
| 3 | "[Deepening title]" | **Hidden Constraint** | [specific vector] | EXPLAIN — reveal the REAL bottleneck |
| 4 | "[Evidence title]" | **Leading Indicator** | [specific vector] | DIAGNOSE — give them a new measurement tool |
| 5 | "[System title]" | **Multiple Causation** | [specific vector] | COMPLEXIFY — show the full system, not just one cause |
| 6 | "[Naming title]" | **Structural Revelation** | [specific vector] | NAME — give the complete system a name (mechanism characterization) |
| 7 | "[Resolution title]" | **Hidden Condition** | [specific vector] | RESOLVE — the condition for success (→ your product) |

### Assignment Rules:
1. **Episode 1 MUST be the highest-impact vector** (strongest scroll-stop)
2. **Episodes 2-5 should use DIFFERENT CATEGORIES** (don't repeat categories)
3. **Episode 6 should CONSOLIDATE** — name the master mechanism
4. **Final episode should feel INEVITABLE** — the product bridge as logical conclusion
5. **Each vector must advance the convergent thesis** — no tangential insights

## Phase 4: Series Continuity

### Progressive Revelation Map

Show how each episode builds on the previous:

```
Ep 1: "Everything you know about [X] is backwards" (Reverse Causation)
  ↓ "But WHY is it backwards? Because..."
Ep 2: "You're trapped in a loop you can't see" (Vicious Cycle)
  ↓ "But what's CAUSING the loop? The real bottleneck is..."
Ep 3: "The real constraint isn't what you think" (Hidden Constraint)
  ↓ "So how do you KNOW if this applies to you? Measure THIS..."
Ep 4: "The signal nobody tracks" (Leading Indicator)
  ↓ "But it's not just ONE thing — it's actually..."
Ep 5: "Four forces converging against you" (Multiple Causation)
  ↓ "There's a name for this entire system..."
Ep 6: "The [Named Mechanism]" (Structural Revelation — consolidation)
  ↓ "Now that you understand the mechanism, here's what actually fixes it..."
Ep 7: "Breaking the [Named Mechanism] requires this ONE precondition" (Hidden Condition → Product)
```

### Audience Conviction Progression

| Episode | Belief State | Conviction Level |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Before Ep 1 | "I know how [domain] works" | 0% — default model |
| After Ep 1 | "Wait, maybe I'm wrong about the basics" | 15% — doubt seeded |
| After Ep 2 | "I can see the trap now" | 30% — problem recognized |
| After Ep 3 | "The real issue isn't what I thought" | 45% — reframe accepted |
| After Ep 4 | "I can verify this myself" | 60% — personal confirmation |
| After Ep 5 | "The whole system is connected" | 75% — systems understanding |
| After Ep 6 | "There's a name for what's happening to me" | 85% — named mechanism |
| After Ep 7 | "I know exactly what I need to do" | 95% — product bridge (conversion) |

## Phase 5: Platform Adaptation

### Newsletter Series
- **Episode length**: 800-1,200 words
- **Cadence**: Weekly
- **Cliffhanger placement**: Final paragraph + P.S.
- **CTA**: "Forward this to someone who needs to see this" (growth) + "Reply and tell me: [diagnostic question]" (engagement)

### YouTube Series
- **Episode length**: 8-15 minutes
- **Thumbnail formula**: [Vector name in bold] + [counter-intuitive image]
- **Retention structure**: Insight vector revealed at 30% mark, proof at 60%, cliffhanger at 95%
- **End screen**: "Watch the next episode" with thumbnail preview

### LinkedIn Series
- **Episode length**: 1,000-1,500 characters
- **Cadence**: Monday/Wednesday/Friday or daily
- **Opening formula**: "Part [N] of [Total]: [Hook]"
- **Engagement**: Each post asks a diagnostic question related to its vector

### Podcast Series
- **Episode length**: 20-40 minutes
- **Structure**: Cold open (hook) → Context → Deep dive on vector → Proof → Listener exercise → Next episode tease
- **Companion asset**: One-page PDF per episode summarizing the insight vector

## Quality Gate

- ☐ Convergent thesis is clear and specific (not vague inspiration)
- ☐ Each episode uses a DIFFERENT vector type from a different category
- ☐ Progressive revelation map shows logical flow (each episode builds on the last)
- ☐ Cliffhangers create genuine "I need the next one" momentum
- ☐ Conviction progression reaches ≥85% by the naming episode
- ☐ Product bridge feels inevitable, not forced
- ☐ No episode is "filler" — every episode delivers a genuine micro-epiphany
- ☐ Platform adaptation is complete for the primary platform

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: A content series that's just "5 tips" spread across 5 episodes is NOT an insight series. Each episode must create a genuine AHA moment that reshapes the audience's mental model. If an episode could be summarized as "here's some good advice" — it doesn't have an insight vector.
