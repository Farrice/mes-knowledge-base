## Step 1: Attention District Diagnosis

Grace's critical distinction: **Attention ≠ Trust**. Attention is cheap, trust is expensive. Most creators over-invest in attention and under-invest in the trust stages.

Audit your current attention district:

| Metric | Current | Target | Gap |
|--------|---------|--------|-----|
| **Attention volume** (impressions/month) | [Number] | [Number] | [Over/Under/Right-sized] |
| **Attention-to-Discovery conversion** | [% who go from seeing you to finding more] | [10-25% bench] | [Gap] |
| **Forgettable seconds ratio** | [% of attention content that's forgettable] | [<50%] | [Gap] |
| **Virality dependency** | [Do you rely on viral spikes or steady flow?] | [Steady flow] | [Risk level] |

**Quality gate**: If attention volume is already high but trust conversion is low, STOP optimizing attention. The problem is downstream. Run Workflow 02 (Trust Pathway Planner) instead.

## Step 4: Attention → Trust Bridge Design

Critical: attention without a trust bridge is wasted energy. For each platform, design the explicit bridge:

```
PLATFORM: [name]
  └── Attention captured via: [hook type]
  └── Bridge to Discovery: [What do they do next? Click profile? Follow? Subscribe?]
  └── Bridge to Connection: [How do they go deeper? Newsletter? DM? Community?]
  └── Failure mode: [Where do you lose them?]
  └── Fix: [What changes to prevent the loss?]
```

**Grace's anti-pattern**: "Don't optimize for attention that has nowhere to go. A viral piece without a trust bridge is a sugar rush — it spikes your ego and warps your judgment."

---

## Step 5: Virality Risk Assessment

Apply Grace's Virality Skepticism lens:

- [ ] Are you creating ANY content purely for virality? (If yes — does it still connect to Grand Central?)
- [ ] If your most viral piece gets 10M views tomorrow, where do those people go next? (If "nowhere" → build the bridge FIRST)
- [ ] Are you measuring attention or trust? (If only attention → add trust metrics)
- [ ] Have you ever changed your content strategy because a viral piece "worked"? (If yes → that's the come-down warping your judgment)
