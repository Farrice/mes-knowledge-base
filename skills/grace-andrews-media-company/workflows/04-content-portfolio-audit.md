# Content Portfolio Audit

> **Produces**: Diagnostic report with actionable prescriptions  
> **Used When**: Content isn't driving business outcomes, feeling strategically lost, or post-viral confusion  
> **Time**: 60-90 minutes  
> **Genius Patterns**: Forgettable/Memorable Split, Trust Pathway Sequencing, Business Outcome Anchoring, Virality Skepticism, Anti-Romanticism

---

## Pre-Flight Check

- [ ] Access to content performance data (views, engagement, conversions — ideally 30+ days)
- [ ] Loaded `genius.md`
- [ ] Clear on current business outcomes (revenue events, subscriber milestones, etc.)

## Step 2: Forgettable / Memorable Split

Apply the 48-Hour Memory Test to each piece:

**Scoring**:
- **Forgettable Seconds** (F): Would not be remembered in 48 hours, would not be told to a friend
- **Memorable Minutes** (M): Would be remembered OR told to a friend
- **Memorable + Sharable** (M+): Both remembered AND shared/referenced

| Piece | F / M / M+ | Production Investment (Low/Med/High) | Investment Aligned? |
|-------|-----------|-------------------------------------|-------------------|
| [Title] | [F/M/M+] | [L/M/H] | ✅ / ❌ |

**Diagnosis**:
- If >60% of high-investment content is Forgettable → **Production misallocation.** Redirect to memorable minutes.
- If most Memorable content is low-investment → **Under-investment in winners.** Scale production on what works.
- If zero M+ content exists → **No emotional residue.** Content is informational but not impactful.

- Any stage at 0% → **Pipeline break.** Audiences can't progress from the previous stage.
- Attention >50% → **Top-heavy.** Getting eyes but not building trust.
- Trust <15% → **No depth.** Short-form dominated, no memorable minutes.

## Step 4: Business Outcome Linkage Test

For each content piece, attempt the 2-step trace:

```
Content Piece → [Step 1: What does this lead the audience to do next?] → [Step 2: How does that connect to revenue?]
```

**Scoring**:
- ✅ **Connected** — Clear 2-step trace to business outcome
- ⚠️ **Weak** — Vague connection, no clear next step
- ❌ **Orphaned** — No traceable connection to any business outcome

**Diagnosis**:
- >30% orphaned content → **Content without a job.** Either give it a job or stop making it.
- Most connected content is Conversion-stage → **Over-selling.** Not enough trust/connection content.

## Output Format

Deliver:
1. **Portfolio Health Score** — composite rating across all 5 audit dimensions
2. **Forgettable/Memorable Map** — visual split with investment alignment
3. **Trust Pathway Gap Analysis** — which stages are broken
4. **Business Linkage Report** — connected vs. orphaned content
5. **Prescriptions** — specific, prioritized actions for the next 30 days
