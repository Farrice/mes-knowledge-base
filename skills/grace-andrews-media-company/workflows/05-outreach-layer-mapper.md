# Outreach Layer Mapper

> **Produces**: Concentric connection map with warming strategy for high-value targets  
> **Used When**: Pitching dream guests, partners, media placements, or clients — need warm paths  
> **Time**: 30-45 minutes per target  
> **Genius Patterns**: Outreach Layer Mapping, Bar-Raising Reflex, Early Intensity

---

## Pre-Flight Check

- [ ] Target list defined (who do you want to reach?)
- [ ] Your own value proposition clear (what do you offer THEM?)
- [ ] Loaded `genius.md`

---

## Step 1: Target Definition

For each high-value target, define:

| Target | Who Are They | Why Them? | What You Offer Them | Timeline |
|--------|-------------|-----------|---------------------|----------|
| [Name] | [Title/platform/influence] | [Why this person specifically?] | [Genuine value for THEM, not you] | [Ideal timeframe] |

**Quality gate**: "What You Offer Them" must be genuinely valuable to the target, not just "exposure" or "collaboration." If you can't articulate real value, you're not ready.

---

## Step 2: Connection Layer Mapping

For each target, draw 3 concentric circles:

### Layer 0: Direct Contact
- [ ] You already know them personally
- [ ] You have their contact info AND they'd recognize your name
- If YES → proceed to Step 4 (warm outreach)
- If NO → move to Layer 1

### Layer 1: One Degree of Separation
List everyone you know who has a relationship with the target:

| Connection | Their Relationship to Target | Your Relationship to Connection | Warmth Level (Cold/Warm/Hot) |
|------------|-----------------------------|---------------------------------|-----|
| [Name] | [How they know the target] | [How you know them] | [Level] |

### Layer 2: Two Degrees of Separation
For each Layer 1 connection, list THEIR connections who know the target:

| Your Connection | Their Connection | Relationship to Target | Path |
|----------------|-----------------|----------------------|------|
| [Your contact] | [Their contact] | [How they know target] | You → [Contact] → [Contact] → Target |

### Layer 3: Public Access Points
If no Layer 1 or 2 connections exist:
- [ ] Events where target speaks/attends
- [ ] Communities where target participates
- [ ] Content the target publishes (engagement opportunities)
- [ ] Mutual interests or causes
- [ ] Former colleagues or collaborators discoverable online

---

## Step 3: Warming Strategy

For each layer, design a warming action:

### Warming from Layer 3 → Layer 2
| Action | Timeline | Success Signal |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| Engage meaningfully with their content (thoughtful comments, shares, replies) | 2-4 weeks | They notice and respond |
| Attend the same event/community | 1 event | Brief introduction made |
| Provide unsolicited value (share their work, write about their ideas, cite their research) | 2-4 weeks | Acknowledgment or DM |

### Warming from Layer 2 → Layer 1
| Action | Timeline | Success Signal |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| Ask mutual connection for warm introduction | 1 ask | Introduction made |
| Get mentioned by mutual connection in target's presence | Situational | Target hears your name positively |
| Collaborate with mutual connection on something visible to target | 2-4 weeks | Target sees your work |

### Warming from Layer 1 → Layer 0
| Action | Timeline | Success Signal |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| Request warm introduction from Layer 1 connection | 1 ask with proper framing | Direct conversation initiated |
| Send value-first outreach mentioning mutual connection | 1 message + follow-up | Response received |
| Offer something genuinely useful with no ask attached | First touchpoint | Relationship opened |

---

## Step 4: Outreach Execution

When you've reached Layer 0 (warm contact established):

**The Grace Andrews Outreach Template**:
1. **Lead with value** — what you're offering THEM, not what you want
2. **Reference the warm connection** — mention how you're connected
3. **Be specific about the ask** — don't be vague ("let's connect" = delete)
4. **Make it easy to say yes** — remove friction (scheduling link, clear next step)
5. **Follow up with grace** — one follow-up at 7 days, one at 14 days, then let it rest for 30 days minimum before trying again

**Anti-pattern**: Never cold-pitch Layer 0 when you haven't warmed through layers. It burns the connection permanently.

---

## Step 5: Persistence Protocol

Grace's DOAC team treated "no" as "not yet."

| Response | Interpretation | Action |
|----------|---------------|--------|
| No response | Busy, not hostile | Follow up once at 7 days, once at 14. Then wait 30+ days. |
| Polite decline | Not now | Thank them. Warm the layer again in 60 days. |
| Hard no | Not ever (rare) | Respect it. Move to next target. |
| "Maybe later" | Interested but not ready | Set calendar reminder for 45 days. Re-approach with new value. |
| Yes | 🎉 | Deliver extraordinary value. Turn them from target into advocate. |

Deliver:
1. **Target Map** — each target with concentric layers drawn
2. **Warming Action Plans** — specific actions per layer with timelines
3. **Outreach Scripts** — value-first messages tailored to each warm path
4. **Persistence Calendar** — follow-up schedule with response-contingent branching
