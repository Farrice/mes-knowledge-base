# Workflow 17: Audience Niche Laser

> **Genius Patterns**: GP-12 (Niche Precision Despite Breadth) + Hidden Knowledge HK-4 (Relief-Seeking Behavior) + HK-3 (Discoverability Mess)
> **Purpose**: DOAC content appears mass-market but was engineered for specific audience segments. This workflow forces "speak to ONE person" precision on every piece — while designing for the messy, non-linear, multi-touchpoint reality of how audiences actually discover content.

## Pre-Flight Check

Before starting:
- [ ] You have either a content concept to target, or a brand that needs audience definition
- [ ] The city map exists or will be created inline
- [ ] genius.md has been read this session

## Step 1: The Named Person Exercise (GP-12)

Grace's rule: define a specific PERSON — not a demographic — who is your target for each content piece.

### 1A: For Brand-Level Targeting

If defining the audience for the entire brand:

```
THE NAMED PERSON
Name: [Give them a real name]
Age: [Specific age, not range]
Current Life Situation: [What's happening in their life RIGHT NOW?]
The Problem They're Aware Of: [What do they know they're struggling with?]
The Problem They're NOT Aware Of: [What's actually causing the struggle?]
Where They Spend Time Online: [Specific platforms, subreddits, accounts they follow]
What They'd Search For: [Actual search queries they'd type]
What Makes Them Click: [What hooks work on THIS person?]
What Makes Them Stay: [What keeps THIS person consuming beyond the first 30 seconds?]
What Would Make Them Tell a Friend: [What's remarkable enough to share?]
```

### 1B: For Per-Piece Targeting

If targeting a specific content piece within an existing brand:

```
THIS PIECE IS FOR: [Name from brand-level targeting, OR a specific sub-persona]
Their Specific Entry Point: [How are they finding this piece? Platform + discovery mechanism]
Their Emotional State When Finding It: [Bored? Anxious? Procrastinating? Motivated? Curious?]
What They Want FROM This Piece: [Specific outcome — not what YOU want to give them]
```

**Quality gate**: If you write "entrepreneurs" or "content creators" or any group noun → you've failed. Try again with a real name and real specifics.

## Step 2: Relief-Seeking Behavior Design (HK-4)

> People don't go to social media to be SOLD to. They seek relief and entertainment.

For the named person, design the piece to satisfy their immediate need BEFORE serving your strategic goal:

```
RELIEF-FIRST AUDIT
What relief does this piece provide IMMEDIATELY?
  □ Entertainment (makes them laugh, feel something, enjoy)
  □ Validation ("finally someone said what I was thinking")
  □ Curiosity satisfaction ("I always wondered about this")
  □ Competence boost ("now I know how to do X")
  □ Escape ("this is more interesting than what I was doing")

Is the relief upfront? [Does the first 20% deliver relief, or does it lecture/sell first?]
Where does YOUR strategic goal appear? [What % through the piece?]
Is the strategic goal ORGANIC or BOLTED ON? [Does it flow naturally from the relief, or does it feel like a switch?]
```

**Rule**: If your strategic goal (CTA, plug, offer) appears before the relief is delivered → restructure. The audience owes you nothing. Earn the right to serve your goal by serving their need first.

## Step 3: Multi-Touchpoint Entry Design (HK-3)

> The marketing funnel isn't a funnel anymore. Discoverability is messy, non-linear, and multi-touchpoint.

Design this content as a valid entry point to your ecosystem — not as a step in a linear funnel:

```
ENTRY POINT DESIGN
If this is the FIRST thing someone encounters from your brand:
  - Does it make sense standalone? [Y/N]
  - Does it convey your core identity? [Y/N]
  - Does it contain a thread that leads deeper? [Y/N — what's the "want more?" signal?]
  - Is there a natural next step? [What would they click/search/follow after this?]

Multi-touchpoint compatibility:
  - Can this be clipped/screenshotted and shared out of context? [Y/N]
  - If shared, does the clip/screenshot still represent your brand accurately? [Y/N]
  - Are there 2+ valid "next content" pathways from this piece? [List them]
```

**Rule**: Every content piece should work as a valid entry point. If it only makes sense for existing followers → it's not doing discovery work.

## Step 4: The Breadth Paradox Resolution

Grace's paradox: DOAC content appears mass-market but targets specific people. Resolve this:

```
BREADTH vs. PRECISION CHECK
Written FOR: [The specific named person]
Accessible TO: [Who else might resonate with this? Why?]
Inaccessible TO: [Who will NOT resonate? Is this a feature, not a bug?]
```

The ideal content piece:
- Written with such specificity for one person that OTHERS who share that person's situation feel seen
- NOT written broadly enough that no one feels specifically addressed

**Anti-pattern**: "This is for anyone who wants to grow their business" → Too broad. Nobody feels seen.
**Pattern**: "This is for the freelancer at month 3 who has 2 clients but can't figure out how to get from $3K to $10K" → Specific enough to feel personal, broad enough to resonate with thousands of similar freelancers.

## Quality Gate

- ✅ Named person defined (specific individual, not demographic)
- ✅ Relief-seeking need identified and placed upfront
- ✅ Strategic goal positioned AFTER relief delivery
- ✅ Multi-touchpoint entry point design completed
- ✅ Breadth paradox resolved (specific enough to feel personal, accessible enough to resonate broadly)
