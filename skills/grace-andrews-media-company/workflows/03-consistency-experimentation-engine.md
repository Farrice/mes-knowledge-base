# Consistency × Experimentation Engine

> **Produces**: Growth sprint plan with cadence protocol + experimentation framework  
> **Used When**: Need to systematize content production with built-in innovation  
> **Time**: 30-60 minutes  
> **Genius Patterns**: Consistency × Experimentation, Anti-Romanticism, Testing Obsession, Bar-Raising Reflex

## Step 1: Consistency Baseline

Define your non-negotiable publishing frequency — the cadence you WILL maintain regardless of quality concerns, perfectionism, or inspiration levels.

**Key principle**: The cadence must be sustainable for 90+ days. Ambitious but not fragile.

| Channel | Frequency | Quality Floor | Non-Negotiable? |
|---------|-----------|--------------|----------------|
| [Channel 1] | [X per week] | [Minimum acceptable quality] | ✅ |
| [Channel 2] | [X per week] | [Minimum acceptable quality] | ✅ |

**Quality gate**: 
- ✅ Cadence is specific (not "regularly")
- ✅ Sustainable for 90 days without burnout
- ✅ Has a defined quality floor (not "perfect every time")
- ❌ NOT aspirational cadence you've never maintained

---

## Step 2: Experimentation Allocation

Designate **20-30%** of your content as explicit experiments.

For each experiment, define:

```
EXPERIMENT: [Name]
  └── Hypothesis: "I believe [format/hook/topic/angle] will [measurable outcome]"
  └── Variable Changed: [What's different from your baseline?]
  └── Constants: [What stays the same?]
  └── Sample Size: [How many pieces before evaluating?]
  └── Success Metric: [What number or signal means "winner"?]
  └── Kill Criteria: [What number or signal means "kill"?]
  └── Duration: [Maximum runtime before evaluation]
```

**Experiment types to rotate**:
1. **Format experiments** — Try a new content format (carousel, thread, live stream, essay)
2. **Hook experiments** — Same content, different opening strategy
3. **Topic experiments** — Adjacent topic your audience hasn't seen from you
4. **Angle experiments** — Same topic, different perspective (contrarian, personal story, tutorial, listicle)
5. **Distribution experiments** — Same content, different channel or posting time

---

## Step 3: Testing Protocol

Apply Grace's testing methodology:

### Weekly Check (Every 7 Days)
- Review experiment performance vs. baselines
- Flag any experiment showing >2x or <0.5x baseline performance
- Note qualitative signals (DMs, comments mentioning specific content)

### Monthly Data Kill Switch (Every 30 Days)
For each active strategy, format, and experiment, answer:
1. **Is the defense emotional or empirical?** ("I believe in this" = emotional. "The data shows X" = empirical.)
2. **If emotional → KILL or PIVOT.** No exceptions.
3. **If empirical → KEEP or SCALE.** Document why.

### Graduation Protocol
When an experiment wins:
1. Document what made it work (not just THAT it worked)
2. Integrate into your Consistency baseline
3. Replace the experiment slot with a new hypothesis

---

## Step 4: Anti-Perfectionism Guard

Install these circuit breakers:

| Trap | Signal | Override |
|------|--------|----------|
| **Perfectionism** | Haven't published on schedule because "it's not ready" | Ship at quality floor. Iterate next week. |
| **Romanticism** | Defending a failing experiment with emotion | Run Data Kill Switch immediately. |
| **Comparison** | Delaying because competitor did it better | Bar-Raising Reflex: articulate what makes yours different. If you can't, iterate. Ship anyway. |
| **Viral Chasing** | Abandoning experiments to replicate a random hit | Return to baseline. Viral is noise. Experiments are signal. |
