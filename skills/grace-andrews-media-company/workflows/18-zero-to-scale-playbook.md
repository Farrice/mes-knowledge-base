# Workflow 18: Zero-to-Scale Playbook

> **Source**: Extraction Report Methodology Levels 1-3 + Implementation Pathway (24-hour/7-day/30-day)
> **Purpose**: Grace's extraction defined three distinct levels — Foundation, Growth, Scale — with specific outputs and actions at each. This workflow unifies them into a single executable playbook that guides a creator from zero audience through first 1,000 to 10K+ with appropriate tooling at each stage.

## Pre-Flight Check

Before starting:
- [ ] Identify current stage: Zero (no audience) / Foundation (<100) / Growth (100-1,000) / Scale (1,000+)
- [ ] genius.md has been read this session
- [ ] Brand/niche is defined at minimum

---

## Stage 1: The 24-Hour Quickstart (Zero → Foundation Started)

**Objective**: In 24 hours, create the strategic anchor that prevents all future "what should I post?" paralysis.

### Hour 1-2: Grand Central Station

Run a compressed version of Workflow 01 (City Map Architect):

```
GRAND CENTRAL STATION
Your editorial mission in one sentence: [NOT "I help X do Y" — the WHY behind everything]
Test: Does this mission → make you want to create? / excite you at the thought of 500 episodes? / feel bigger than a sales pitch?
```

### Hour 2-3: Initial City Map

```
INITIAL CITY MAP (simplified)
Grand Central: [from above]
Destination 1: [First quarter goal — specific and measurable]
Destination 2: [Second quarter goal]
Line 1: [Primary content channel — the ONE you'll commit to]
Line 2: [Supporting content channel — the one that feeds Line 1]
Passenger: [Named person from Workflow 17 — who rides these lines?]
```

### Hour 3-4: First Trust Pathway

```
STARTER TRUST PATHWAY
Attention District: [How will people FIND you? What format/platform?]
Connection District: [How will people KNOW you? What makes you memorable?]
Trust District: [How will people BELIEVE you? What proof/depth do you offer?]
```

**Hour 4: Commit to one cadence.** Writing this down IS the quickstart:
- "I will publish [format] on [platform] every [frequency] starting [date]."

### Deliverable: The Anchor Document

Save as `deliverables/[brand]-anchor-document.md`:
- Grand Central Station
- Initial City Map
- Starter Trust Pathway
- Publishing commitment

## Stage 2: The 7-Day Sprint (Foundation → First Content In-Market)

**Objective**: 7 pieces of content, each tagged by trust stage. At least 2 experiments. 3 warm outreach connections.

### Day 1-2: Content Production (Attention + Discoverability)
- Produce 2 pieces designed for discovery — short-form, shareable, hooks optimized
- Tag: Attention stage
- Apply Workflow 17 (Audience Niche Laser) to both

### Day 3-4: Content Production (Connection + Trust)
- Produce 2 pieces designed for depth — longer-form, valuable, memorable
- Tag: Connection / Trust stage
- Apply Workflow 15 (Emotional Residue Engineer) to the Trust piece

### Day 5: Experiment Day
- Produce 2 pieces that deliberately break your emerging pattern
- Different format, angle, hook style, or platform
- Tag: Experiment (per Workflow 03)

### Day 6: Outreach Activation
- Run a compressed Workflow 05 (Outreach Layer Mapper):
  - Identify 3 dream connections
  - Map 1 connection layer per target
  - Begin warming outer layers (engage with their content, share their work, comment substantively)

### Day 7: Content #7 + Sprint Review
- Publish your 7th piece (any trust stage that's underrepresented)
- Run a mini-audit:
  - Trust stage coverage: How many stages represented?
  - Experiment results: Any early signals?
  - Engagement signals: What got the most response?

## Stage 3: The 30-Day Integration (First Content → Consistent System)

**Objective**: Install the systems — cadence lock, data kill switch, C×E ratio.

### Week 1: Cadence Lock
- Maintain your committed publishing frequency
- Track: Did you miss a single date? If yes, why? (Resource, motivation, perfectionism?)

### Week 2: Consistency × Experimentation
- Run Workflow 03 (C×E Engine) to formalize your experiment protocol
- Designate which 20-30% of next month's content is experiments

### Week 3: Data Kill Switch
- Run the 30-day kill-or-keep assessment (GP-5):
  - For every content concept/format you've used: data defense or emotional defense?
  - Kill or pivot anything defended only by emotion
  - Double down on anything defended by data

### Week 4: City Map Revision
- Re-run Workflow 01 (City Map Architect) with 30 days of data
- Adjust: destinations, lines, passenger profiles
- Plan Month 2 with specific trust stage goals

## Stage 4: Growth Phase (100 → 1,000)

**Objective**: Measure trust pathway transitions. Build memorable minutes. Scale outreach.

### Monthly Actions:
1. **Trust Pathway Measurement** (Workflow 02): Track audience movement between stages. Where are people dropping off?
2. **Content Bridges**: Design specific pieces that move people from Attention → Connection and Connection → Trust
3. **Forgettable/Memorable Split**: Audit your portfolio (Workflow 04). Ensure >50% production investment in memorable minutes
4. **Outreach Scaling**: Expand from 3 to 10 dream connections. Work layers systematically
5. **Revenue District Planning**: Begin Workflow 09 when trust signals are strong enough

## Stage 5: Scale Phase (1,000 → 10,000+)

**Objective**: Full media company operations. Revenue architecture. Team/systems.

### Quarterly Actions:
1. **Media Company Blueprint** (Workflow 07): Full operational transition
2. **Revenue District Activation** (Workflow 09): Launch product ladder
3. **Competitive Analysis** (Workflow 12): Map rival cities, find white space
4. **Brand Voice Districts** (Workflow 10): Formalize voice per trust stage
5. **Content Sprint Planner** (Workflow 11): Systematize production
6. **Episodic Engine** (Workflow 13): Launch recurring shows
