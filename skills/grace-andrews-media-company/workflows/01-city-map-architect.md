# City Map Architect

> **Produces**: Complete content transit system blueprint  
> **Used When**: Starting a brand's content strategy, quarterly planning, combating "what should I post?" paralysis  
> **Time**: 45-90 minutes  
> **Genius Patterns**: Media Company Default, City Architecture, Niche Precision Despite Breadth

---

## Pre-Flight Check

Before starting, confirm:
- [ ] You've loaded `genius.md` for quality ceiling calibration
- [ ] You understand the user's business model (product/service, revenue source, target audience)
- [ ] You know whether this is a zero-to-one build or a rebuild of existing content

---

## Step 1: Grand Central Declaration

Ask the user to articulate their central mission — not an "I help X do Y" statement, but the WHY that every piece of content routes through.

**Prompt the user**:
> "Forget what you sell. What is the ONE non-negotiable belief or mission that every single piece of content — podcast, tweet, newsletter, video — should route through? This isn't your offer. It's your editorial north star."

**Quality gate**: Grand Central should be:
- ✅ Emotionally resonant (not corporate)
- ✅ Broad enough to sustain years of content
- ✅ Specific enough to exclude topics that don't fit
- ❌ NOT "I help [X] do [Y]" — that's an offer, not a mission

**Examples**:
- DOAC: "Helping people become the best version of themselves"
- A fitness brand: "Making strength culture accessible to people who never felt welcome in a gym"
- A SaaS founder: "Proving that bootstrapped companies build better products than venture-backed ones"

---

## Step 2: Destination Mapping

Define 2-4 **destinations** for the current quarter — the specific business outcomes content should drive toward.

**Prompt the user**:
> "What are the 2-3 specific business outcomes you need your content to drive this quarter? Not vague goals like 'grow audience.' Specific: 'Add 1,000 newsletter subscribers,' 'Fill 20 seats in my cohort,' 'Get 3 brand partnerships.'"

**Quality gate**: Each destination must be:
- ✅ Measurable (number, date, outcome)
- ✅ Connected to revenue (even indirectly)
- ✅ Achievable within the quarter
- ❌ NOT vanity metrics ("get more views")

---

## Step 3: Line Design

Map 2-4 **content lines** — the channels/formats through which content travels.

For each line, define:
1. **Format**: Podcast, newsletter, short-form video, blog, social carousel, etc.
2. **Frequency**: How often this line runs
3. **Trust pathway stage**: Which stage of Attention → Discoverability → Connection → Trust → Conversion does this line primarily serve?
4. **Destination link**: Which destination(s) does this line connect to?

**Quality gate**: Your line map should:
- ✅ Cover at least 3 of the 5 trust pathway stages
- ✅ Have at least one "memorable minutes" line (long-form)
- ✅ Not exceed 4 lines (overextension kills consistency)
- ❌ NOT be all short-form (no trust pathway progression)

---

## Step 4: Passenger Profiling

Define **passengers** for each line — the specific audience segments riding each route.

**Prompt the user**:
> "For each content line, who specifically is this for? Not demographics. A person. Give them a name. What keeps them up at night? Why are THEY on this line?"

**Quality gate**: Each passenger profile must:
- ✅ Be a specific person (real or realistic), not a demographic
- ✅ Have a defined pain point that connects to your Grand Central Station
- ✅ Differ between lines (different passengers ride different routes)

---

## Step 5: Transit Map Assembly

Assemble the complete City Map:

```
Grand Central Station: [Mission statement]

DESTINATION 1: [Business outcome + metric]
  └── LINE A: [Format] @ [Frequency] → [Trust stage]
       └── Passengers: [Name + profile]
  └── LINE B: [Format] @ [Frequency] → [Trust stage]
       └── Passengers: [Name + profile]

DESTINATION 2: [Business outcome + metric]
  └── LINE A: (cross-line connection — same line serves multiple destinations)
  └── LINE C: [Format] @ [Frequency] → [Trust stage]
       └── Passengers: [Name + profile]
```

---

## Step 6: Validation Check

Run these checks:
1. [ ] Does every line route through Grand Central?
2. [ ] Does every destination have at least one line connecting to it?
3. [ ] Are at least 3 trust pathway stages covered?
4. [ ] Is there at least one memorable-minutes line?
5. [ ] Can you explain the map in 60 seconds to someone who's never heard of your brand?

If any check fails, iterate on the relevant step.

---

## Output Format

Deliver the completed City Map as:
1. **Visual transit diagram** (text-based)
2. **One-page strategic brief** with Grand Central, destinations, lines, and passengers
3. **First 30-day content calendar** with each piece tagged by line, trust stage, and destination served
