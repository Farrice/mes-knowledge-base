# Content Series Architect

> **Produces**: Multi-episode content series plan with narrative arc, episode structure, cross-platform distribution  
> **Used When**: Building a recurring content format, launching a themed series, or creating Netflix-style episodic content  
> **Time**: 60-90 minutes  
> **Genius Patterns**: City Architecture, Forgettable/Memorable Split, Consistency × Experimentation  
> **Cross-Stack**: Shaan Puri (story hooks), Kallaway (retention psychology), Kieran Flanagan (content operations)

## Step 1: Series Concept Mining

Identify the series concept using Grace's "bus route" metaphor — a series is a named line on your city transit map that runs on a regular schedule.

**Prompt the user**:
> "What recurring question, argument, or curiosity does your audience keep coming back to? Not a one-off topic — something with enough depth to sustain 4-12 episodes. Think: 'Each episode is a new stop on the same bus route.'"

Mine for concepts using three lenses:

| Lens | Question | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Tension-Based** | What ongoing debate or tension does your audience live inside? | "Is AI replacing creative jobs or creating them?" |
| **Journey-Based** | What transformation can be serialized into stages? | "Month 1 through Month 12 of building a media company" |
| **Anthology-Based** | What category can hold infinite unique episodes? | "How [Famous Brand] built their content city" |

**Quality gate**: Series concept must:
- ✅ Sustain 6+ episodes without running dry
- ✅ Create anticipation — each episode makes the next one desirable
- ✅ Connect to a trust pathway stage on your City Map
- ❌ NOT be a topic dump dressed up as a "series" (that's just a content calendar)

## Step 2: Narrative Arc Design

Design the series arc using a 3-act structure adapted for episodic content:

### Act 1: The Hook (Episodes 1-2)
- **Episode 1 (The Pilot)**: Establish the core tension/question. Make the audience feel the problem at a visceral level. End with an implicit promise: "If you stick with me, I'll show you the answer."
- **Episode 2 (The Deepen)**: Reveal the first unexpected insight. Show that the answer is more complex/interesting than the audience assumed. Create the "wait, really?" moment.

### Act 2: The Journey (Episodes 3 to N-2)
- Each episode explores one dimension of the central question
- **Callback rule**: Every episode must reference at least one element from a previous episode — creates continuity and rewards loyal viewers
- **Cliffhanger rule**: End each episode with an unresolved question that the NEXT episode addresses (not a cheap "tune in next time" — a genuine intellectual cliffhanger)

### Act 3: The Payoff (Final 1-2 episodes)
- **Synthesis episode**: Pull all threads together into a unified framework, answer, or revelation
- **Implications episode** (optional): "Now that we know X, here's what changes about how you should operate"

**Quality gate**: Each episode must:
- ✅ Stand alone (someone can watch just this one and get value)
- ✅ Build on what came before (loyal viewers get MORE value)
- ✅ End with forward momentum (the viewer feels pulled toward the next one)

## Step 3: Episode Blueprint

For each episode, create a blueprint:

```
EPISODE [#]: [Title]
  └── Core Question: [What does this episode answer?]
  └── Key Insight: [The one thing the audience should remember]
  └── Callback: [Reference to previous episode — which element?]
  └── Cliffhanger: [Unresolved question that pulls toward next episode]
  └── Trust Stage: [Which city district does this serve?]
  └── Format: [Interview, solo, investigation, case study, etc.]
  └── Hook (first 30 seconds): [Exact opening line or concept]
  └── Memorable Moment: [The one scene/segment designed to be shared or quoted]
```

**Cross-stack enrichment** — For each episode hook, run through:
- Shaan Puri's "curiosity gap before answers" principle
- Kallaway's "anticipation loop" — the hook should create a dopamine promise
- Apply Luke Iha's Vicious Hook test: does the hook contain specificity, conflict, or proof?

## Step 4: Cross-Platform Distribution Map

Map how each episode travels across platforms using Grace's line metaphor:

| Platform | Role in Series | Format Adaptation | Timing |
|----------|---------------|-------------------|--------|
| **Anchor platform** (YouTube/Podcast) | Full episode | Long-form — the complete experience | Release day (e.g., Tuesday) |
| **Newsletter** | Episode preview + exclusive insight | Written companion piece with one extra insight not in the video | Release day or day before |
| **LinkedIn** | Episode highlight | 1-2 post-worthy insights extracted as standalone posts | Day of + day after |
| **Short-form** (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) | Episode trailer + hook clips | Top 3 most shareable 30-60 second moments | Day before (teaser) + day after (clips) |
| **Community** (Discord/Skool) | Episode discussion | Discussion questions, polls, behind-scenes | Day after release |

**Quality gate**: Distribution map must:
- ✅ Create a "content orbit" — each platform points back to the anchor
- ✅ Adapt natively (not just repost — rewrite for the platform's grammar)
- ❌ NOT require more than 2x the production effort of a single episode

## Step 5: Series Bible Assembly

Create the Series Bible — the reference document that keeps the series coherent over time:

### Series Identity
- **Series Name**: [Catchy, memorable, brandable]
- **Grand Central Connection**: [How this series routes through your core mission]
- **Tagline**: [One sentence that communicates what the series is about]
- **Visual/Audio Identity**: [Consistent branding elements — intro, color, thumbnail style]

### Recurring Elements
- **Opening ritual**: [How every episode begins — creates Pavlovian anticipation]
- **Signature segment**: [A recurring segment within episodes — e.g., "The Hot Take," "The Case Study," "The Mailbag"]
- **Closing ritual**: [How every episode ends — creates emotional closure + forward pull]
- **Vocabulary**: [Recurring terms, inside jokes, or frameworks that reward loyal viewers]

### Production Standards
- **Minimum quality bar**: [What must be true for an episode to ship?]
- **Kill criteria**: [When do you kill an episode that isn't working?]
- **Guest criteria** (if applicable): [What makes someone worth featuring?]

---

## Step 6: Pilot Episode Deep Design

Design the pilot episode in full detail — this sets the quality ceiling for the entire series:

1. **Cold open** (0-30 sec): Hook that establishes the series tension
2. **Setup** (30 sec - 3 min): Context — why this series, why now, why should the audience care
3. **Core content** (3 min - end minus 3 min): The meat — first dimension explored
4. **Memorable moment**: The single segment designed to be clipped, shared, or quoted
5. **Cliffhanger close** (final 2-3 min): The unresolved question that makes Episode 2 irresistible
6. **CTA**: Subscribe prompt tied to series (not generic — "You do NOT want to miss Episode 2 because...")

---

## Output Format

Deliver:
1. **Series Concept Brief** — one-page summary: name, tagline, arc, target audience, trust stage served
2. **Full Episode Blueprint Map** — every episode with core question, insight, callback, cliffhanger, and hooks
3. **Series Bible** — identity, recurring elements, production standards
4. **Cross-Platform Distribution Matrix** — how each episode travels across platforms
5. **Pilot Episode Script/Outline** — detailed structure for Episode 1
6. **30-Day Launch Calendar** — production timeline from pilot recording through first 4 episodes
