# Workflow 14: Virality Autopsy

> **Genius Patterns**: GP-11 (Virality Skepticism) + Hidden Knowledge HK-2 (Virality Come-Down Tax)
> **Purpose**: When content goes viral, the instinct is to celebrate and replicate. This workflow forces a disciplined post-viral analysis that separates genuine trust gains from hollow attention spikes — and prevents the "come-down tax" from warping your next 30 days of strategy.

## Pre-Flight Check

Before starting:
- [ ] A specific viral piece triggered this workflow (defined as 5x+ your normal reach)
- [ ] You have access to performance data (views, engagement, follows, unsubscribes, conversion metrics)
- [ ] The city map / trust pathway exists as baseline context (Workflow 01/02 output)
- [ ] genius.md has been read this session

---

## Step 1: Viral Spike Forensics

Collect raw data on the viral piece:

```
VIRAL PIECE PROFILE
Title/Topic: [what went viral]
Platform: [where it spiked]
Normal Baseline: [your typical reach/engagement on this platform]
Viral Reach: [actual numbers]
Spike Multiplier: [viral reach / normal baseline]
Time-to-Spike: [how fast it took off]
Viral Mechanics: [what shared it — algorithm, shares, reposts, press, influencer amplification?]
```

## Step 2: Trust-Stage Classification

What trust stage was this piece designed for? Where did the new audience land?

```
TRUST STAGE AUDIT
Intended Trust Stage: [Attention / Discoverability / Connection / Trust / Conversion]
Actual New Audience Entry Point: [Where did new followers actually enter your ecosystem?]
Trust Pathway Advancement: [Did ANY new audience advance beyond the entry stage? How many? What %?]
```

**Quality gate**: If <5% of new audience advanced beyond the attention stage → this was pure attention inflation, not growth.

## Step 3: The Come-Down Tax Diagnosis

Grace's HK-2 insight: virality creates a psychological anchor that makes "normal" performance feel like failure.

```
COME-DOWN TAX ASSESSMENT
Pre-Viral Baseline: [your normal metrics before the spike]
Post-Viral "Normal": [your metrics in the 7-14 days after the spike faded]
Psychological Anchor: [What numbers do you now "expect" that are unrealistic?]
Strategy Distortion Risk: [Are you tempted to replicate the viral format instead of following your city map?]
```

**Red flags** (any "yes" = come-down tax is active):
- [ ] You've compared subsequent content's performance to the viral piece (unfavorably)
- [ ] You've considered pivoting your content strategy to chase the viral format
- [ ] You feel like your content is "underperforming" even though it matches pre-viral baselines
- [ ] You've delayed publishing because "nothing feels big enough"
- [ ] You've considered doing something sensational/controversial to recapture the spike

## Step 4: Trust Damage Assessment

**The Attention-Trust Inversion** (HK-1): High-attention content often builds the LEAST trust.

```
TRUST DAMAGE REPORT
Unsubscribe Rate (post-spike): [Did email/newsletter unsubscribes increase?]
Engagement Quality Shift: [Are new followers engaging deeply or just lurking?]
Comment Quality: [Are new comments adding to discourse or generic ("great post", emoji-only)?]
DM Quality: [Are you getting more DMs? Are they substantive or low-effort?]
Conversion Rate Change: [Did your conversion rate on products/services go UP or DOWN?]
Audience-Content Fit: [Are the new followers actually your target audience?]
```

**If conversion rate DROPPED post-viral**: Your audience diluted. You added attention without trust. This is the most common viral damage.

## Step 5: Recalibration Protocol

Based on Steps 2-4, choose one of three responses:

### Response A: "Beneficial Viral" (Rare)
> New audience advanced along trust pathway. Conversion rate stable or improved. Audience-content fit is good.
- **Action**: Study what made this piece work. Extract the principle (not the tactic). Add ONE element to your experimentation rotation. Do NOT make it your new "consistency" baseline.

### Response B: "Neutral Viral" (Common)
> Attention spiked but trust pathway unchanged. No damage, no advancement.
- **Action**: Acknowledge the spike, don't chase it. Return to your city map. File the piece as a data point. Continue your planned content strategy without modification.

### Response C: "Damaging Viral" (More common than people admit)
> Unsubscribes increased, conversion rate dropped, audience diluted, or you're in come-down tax distortion.
- **Recovery Protocol**:
  1. **Immediately**: Stop comparing to viral numbers. Reset your psychological anchor to pre-viral baseline
  2. **Next 7 days**: Publish 3 pieces specifically designed for your EXISTING trust audience (Connection/Trust stage content). Prioritize depth over reach
  3. **Next 30 days**: Track whether your original metrics return to or exceed pre-viral baseline
  4. **Anti-pattern**: Do NOT try to "convert" the new audience. They came for something different. Focus on the audience that was already on your trust pathway

## Quality Gate

The autopsy is complete only when:
- ✅ Raw viral data collected and compared to baseline
- ✅ Trust stage classified (intended vs. actual)
- ✅ Come-down tax diagnosed (with red flag checklist)
- ✅ Trust damage assessed with specific metrics
- ✅ Response classification chosen (Beneficial / Neutral / Damaging)
- ✅ City map recalibrated or explicitly confirmed unchanged
