# Trust Pathway Planner

> **Produces**: Trust pathway content plan with stage-tagged calendar  
> **Used When**: Building a trust-based conversion pipeline from content to business outcomes  
> **Time**: 60-90 minutes  
> **Genius Patterns**: Trust Pathway Sequencing, Forgettable/Memorable Split, Business Outcome Anchoring, Emotional Residue Engineering

---

## Pre-Flight Check

- [ ] City Map is drawn (run Workflow 01 first if not)
- [ ] Business outcomes for the quarter are defined
- [ ] You've loaded `genius.md` for quality ceiling calibration

---

## Step 1: Trust Stage Inventory

Audit current content (or planned content) against the 5 trust stages:

| Stage | Definition | Content Types That Serve This Stage | Current Coverage |
|-------|-----------|--------------------------------------|-----------------|
| **Attention** | They notice you exist | Short-form clips, ads, guest appearances, viral moments | [ ] Covered / [ ] Gap |
| **Discoverability** | They find you through multiple touchpoints | SEO content, collaborations, platform-native discovery, shares | [ ] Covered / [ ] Gap |
| **Connection** | They feel personal resonance | Stories, values-driven content, vulnerability, behind-scenes | [ ] Covered / [ ] Gap |
| **Trust** | They believe your recommendations | Deep tutorials, case studies, consistent value, long-form | [ ] Covered / [ ] Gap |
| **Conversion** | They act on your offer | Product launches, CTAs, testimonials, proof-stacked offers | [ ] Covered / [ ] Gap |

**Quality gate**: At least 3 of 5 stages covered. If 2+ are gaps, prioritize filling them before adding volume to existing stages.

---

## Step 2: Forgettable / Memorable Classification

For each planned content piece, apply the 48-Hour Memory Test:

> "Will someone remember this specific piece in 48 hours? Will they tell someone about it?"

- **Both NO** → Forgettable Seconds (entry point only — invest 20% production quality)
- **Either YES** → Memorable Minutes (trust builder — invest 80% production quality)

**Target**: Content portfolio should be >50% memorable minutes by production investment.

---

## Step 3: Trust Pathway Mapping

Design explicit **bridge content** — pieces that move audiences from one stage to the next:

| Bridge | From → To | Content Type | Example |
|--------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Entry Bridge** | Attention → Discoverability | Cross-platform clips with searchable titles + show notes | Podcast snippet → full episode |
| **Resonance Bridge** | Discoverability → Connection | Values-driven content, personal stories, behind-the-scenes | Newsletter revealing why you do this |
| **Depth Bridge** | Connection → Trust | Long-form tutorials, case studies, consistent value delivery | 90-min deep-dive episode |
| **Action Bridge** | Trust → Conversion | Testimonials, proof stacking, limited offers, natural CTAs | Client results + "doors open" |

---

## Step 4: Business Outcome Linkage

For every content piece in your plan, complete this trace:

```
CONTENT PIECE: [Title/concept]
  └── Trust Stage: [Which stage does this serve?]
  └── Bridge Role: [Does this move someone to the next stage? If so, which?]
  └── Business Outcome: [Which destination on your City Map does this connect to?]
  └── Measurement: [How will you know this did its job?]
```

**Quality gate**: Every piece must be traceable to a business outcome within 2 steps. If the trace breaks, the piece doesn't have a job.

---

## Step 5: Calendar Construction

Build a 30-day content calendar with each piece tagged:

| Date | Content Piece | Trust Stage | Bridge? | Format | Memorable/Forgettable | Destination |
|------|--------------|-------------|---------|--------|----------------------|------------|
| [Date] | [Title] | [Stage] | [Y/N → which] | [Format] | [M/F] | [Which goal] |

---

## Step 6: Relief-First Check

Run Grace's hidden rule: does your content plan satisfy the audience's **relief/entertainment need** BEFORE serving business outcomes?

For each content piece, verify:
- [ ] Opens with value, not a pitch
- [ ] Provides genuine relief, entertainment, or insight
- [ ] Business outcome is a natural endpoint, not a forced insertion
- [ ] Audience feels served, not sold to

---

## Output Format

Deliver:
1. **Trust Stage Coverage Map** — visual showing current gaps and planned fills
2. **Bridge Content Blueprint** — specific bridge pieces connecting stages
3. **30-Day Tagged Calendar** — every piece with stage, bridge, format, memorable/forgettable, and destination labels
4. **Measurement Dashboard** — what metrics prove each stage is converting to the next
