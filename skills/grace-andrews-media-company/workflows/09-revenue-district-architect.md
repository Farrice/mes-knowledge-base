# Revenue District Architect

> **Produces**: Revenue layer architecture with product ladder, trust thresholds, and conversion pathways mapped to content  
> **Used When**: Content is generating trust but not revenue, or revenue is fragile and single-stream  
> **Time**: 60-90 minutes  
> **Genius Patterns**: Business Outcome Anchoring, Trust Pathway Sequencing, Media Company Default  
> **Cross-Stack**: Nicolas Cole (product vehicles), Monk.AI (offer pyramid), Dan Koe (one-person business model), Vincent Hu (growth ecosystem)

## Step 1: Revenue Reality Check

Map what exists today — brutally honest:

| Revenue Stream | Monthly Revenue | % of Total | Trust Stage Required | Founder-Dependent? |
|---------------|-----------------|-----------|---------------------|-------------------|
| [Stream 1] | $[amount] | [%] | [Which stage?] | [Yes/No] |
| [Stream 2] | $[amount] | [%] | [Which stage?] | [Yes/No] |
| [Stream 3] | $[amount] | [%] | [Which stage?] | [Yes/No] |

**Red flags**:
- ⚠️ Any stream >60% of total = fragile
- ⚠️ Revenue only at Trust/Conversion stage = no ladder (cliff instead of stairs)
- ⚠️ All streams founder-dependent = not a media company, it's a freelance gig
- ⚠️ Zero revenue = not a business yet, don't pretend otherwise

## Step 2: Trust-to-Revenue Threshold Mapping

For each trust stage, define what revenue is APPROPRIATE — not what you wish people would pay:

| Trust Stage | Audience Relationship | Revenue Appropriateness | Examples |
|-------------|----------------------|------------------------|---------|
| **Attention** | "I just found you" | $0 — earn the right to exist in their world | Free content, platform ads (passive, they don't pay YOU) |
| **Discoverability** | "I've seen you around" | $0-low — tiny commitments | Email opt-in (free), $7-19 digital download, tip jar |
| **Connection** | "I feel like I know you" | Low-Medium — they'll invest if you ask right | $29-99 course, workshop, paid newsletter |
| **Trust** | "I believe your recommendations" | Medium-High — they WANT to buy | $100-500 program, coaching, premium membership, consulting |
| **Conversion/Advocacy** | "I recommend you to others" | High — premium willingness | $500-5K+ high-ticket, partnerships, brand deals, licensing |

**Grace's rule**: "If you're asking for $500 at the Attention stage, you're not selling — you're begging. Revenue is a function of trust earned, not price points chosen."

## Step 3: Product Ladder Design

Design a product ladder where each rung matches a trust stage:

### Rung 0: Free Value (Attention → Discoverability)
- **Product**: Free content that demonstrates expertise
- **Function**: Proves you're worth someone's time
- **Quality bar**: Must be BETTER than most people's paid content
- **Conversion metric**: [Views] → [Follows/Subscribes]

### Rung 1: Micro Commitment ($7-29 — Discoverability → Connection)
- **Product**: [e.g., PDF guide, template pack, mini-course, paid newsletter tier]
- **Function**: Converts passive consumer into active participant
- **Trust threshold**: They've consumed 3-5 free content pieces and feel resonance
- **Conversion metric**: [Subscribers] → [First-time buyers]

### Rung 2: Core Offer ($50-299 — Connection → Trust)
- **Product**: [e.g., full course, workshop series, membership, digital product]
- **Function**: Delivers a meaningful transformation — this is where most revenue lives
- **Trust threshold**: They've consumed 10+ content pieces, opened 5+ emails, see you as an authority
- **Conversion metric**: [Email subscribers] → [Core customers]

### Rung 3: Premium Experience ($300-2000 — Trust → Deep Trust)
- **Product**: [e.g., cohort-based course, group coaching, consulting, premium community]
- **Function**: High-touch, transformation-oriented — they're investing in the outcome
- **Trust threshold**: They've bought Rung 2 OR consumed deeply for 3+ months without buying
- **Conversion metric**: [Core customers OR long-term free audience] → [Premium customers]

### Rung 4: Inner Circle ($2000+ — Deep Trust → Advocacy)
- **Product**: [e.g., 1:1 coaching, mastermind, done-for-you, retainer, licensing]
- **Function**: Access to you/your team at the highest level
- **Trust threshold**: Results from Rung 3 OR significant brand alignment
- **Conversion metric**: [Premium customers] → [Inner circle]

## Step 4: Content → Revenue Pathway Design

For each product rung, design the content pathway that leads to it:

```
PRODUCT: [Rung X — Product Name]
  └── Content that CREATES the demand:
      └── [Content piece/type that surfaces the problem this product solves]
      └── [Content piece/type that demonstrates the authority to solve it]
      └── [Content piece/type that provides partial solution (the "taste")]
  └── Content that CAPTURES the demand:
      └── [CTA embedded in trust-stage content — natural, not forced]
      └── [Launch content — testimonials, case studies, behind-the-scenes]
      └── [Limited-time or cohort-based urgency — if applicable]
  └── Content that RETAINS post-purchase:
      └── [Onboarding sequence]
      └── [Community/ongoing content for buyers]
      └── [Results showcase — turns buyers into advocates]
```

**Quality gate**: Every product must have specific content pieces that create, capture, and retain demand. If you can't name the content pieces → the product isn't integrated into your city map.

## Step 5: Revenue Resilience Test

Stress-test your revenue district:

| Test | Question | Status |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Single-Point Failure** | If your biggest revenue stream disappeared tomorrow, could you survive 3 months? | ✅ / ❌ |
| **Founder Dependency** | Can revenue continue if the founder takes 2 weeks off? | ✅ / ❌ |
| **Platform Risk** | If YouTube/LinkedIn/podcast platform changes algorithms, does revenue survive? | ✅ / ❌ |
| **Ladder Integrity** | Can someone enter at any rung, or do they HAVE to start at Rung 0? | ✅ / ❌ |
| **Trust Match** | Is every product sold at the APPROPRIATE trust stage? No overreach? | ✅ / ❌ |
| **Content Link** | Can you trace content → trust → revenue for EVERY product? | ✅ / ❌ |

**If 2+ tests fail**: Fix those before adding new revenue streams.

## Output Format

Deliver:
1. **Revenue Reality Dashboard** — current state audit with red flags identified
2. **Product Ladder Blueprint** — every rung with product, trust threshold, pricing, and conversion metric
3. **Content → Revenue Pathway Maps** — specific content pieces that create, capture, and retain demand per product
4. **Revenue Resilience Report** — stress test results with fix priorities
5. **90-Day Revenue Action Plan** — quarterly milestones for activating the next revenue rung
