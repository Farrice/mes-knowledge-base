# Phil M Jones - Conversational Influence

This skill turns high-stakes conversation moments into usable scripts, question chains, and follow-up moves. It is built from Phil M Jones' *Exactly What to Say* video reading and application session, with the source mechanics converted into practitioner workflows for sales, leadership, client work, content, and everyday asks.

## Core Idea

People rarely resist the person speaking. They resist pressure, confusion, poor timing, and poorly framed words. The Phil M Jones method fixes the moment of influence by choosing a small sequence of words that gives the other person safety, clarity, a mental picture, and an easy next decision.

## Quick Start

1. Run `/pmj-decision-map` on the conversation or offer.
2. Run `/pmj-magic-words` to pick the phrase stack.
3. Run `/pmj-opener` or `/pmj-mental-picture` depending on whether the main barrier is rejection or imagination.
4. Run `/pmj-objection-softener` for known objections.
5. Run `/pmj-follow-up` after any meeting, proposal, or unanswered message.
