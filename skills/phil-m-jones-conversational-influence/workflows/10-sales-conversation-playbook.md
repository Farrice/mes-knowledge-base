# Sales Conversation Playbook

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this for consultative sales calls, offer conversations, discovery calls, proposal calls, or renewal conversations.

## Skill Acquisition

Load:
- `genius.md`
- `references/phrase-library.md`
- `references/stacking-guide.md`
- Optional stack: `skills/jeremy-miner-identity-persuasion/genius.md`

## Inputs

- Offer
- Prospect type
- Current problem
- Desired decision
- Known objections
- Call stage: cold, discovery, proposal, close, renewal

## Execution

1. **Map the call stages**: opener, context, problem, future picture, option design, objection handling, close, follow-up.
2. **Write the opener**: reduce rejection and earn permission.
3. **Write diagnostic questions**: discover knowledge gaps, emotion, timing, and authority.
4. **Build the future picture**: show gain and cost of inaction.
5. **Present three options**: make the choice easier.
6. **Prepare objection branches**: use diagnostic questions and conditional close.
7. **Close with next action**: state what happens next and ask an easy question.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Channel | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Live call | Include stage directions and pauses. |
| Zoom sales call | Add screen-share moments and chat prompts. |
| Proposal review | Put phrases in slide or doc talk track. |
| Renewal | Focus on future picture and enough threshold. |
| High-ticket | Add more diagnostic depth before phrase deployment. |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- Full conversation flow
- Exact scripts by stage
- Objection branch map
- Follow-up messages
- One-page rep cheat sheet

## Quality Gate

Reject scripts that sound like a monologue. The buyer should be speaking often.
