# Content Hook Conversation Engine

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this for LinkedIn posts, short-form scripts, emails, ads, captions, and community prompts where the goal is a reply, comment, booking, or decision.

## Skill Acquisition

Load:
- `genius.md`
- `references/phrase-library.md`
- `references/stacking-guide.md`
- Optional stack: `skills/persuasion-story-code/genius.md`

## Inputs

- Topic
- Audience
- Desired conversation
- Offer or next step
- Platform
- Tone

## Execution

1. **Choose the decision barrier**: curiosity, certainty, imagination, timing, objection, or ask avoidance.
2. **Write hook families**: create hooks using open-mindedness, soft certainty challenge, future feeling, or social category contrast.
3. **Build the post/script body**: make one idea easy to understand and picture.
4. **Write conversational CTAs**: ask for reply, comment, DM, share, call, or small favor.
5. **Add follow-up replies**: prepare responses to likely comments and DMs.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Channel | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Hook, body, comment CTA, DM follow-up. |
| Short-form video | Spoken hook, scene beats, closing ask. |
| Email | Subject, opener, story bridge, CTA. |
| Ad | First line, objection line, action line. |
| Community | Prompt that earns specific replies. |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- 10 hooks
- 3 finished content drafts
- CTA bank
- Comment/DM follow-up scripts
- Phrase mechanics used

## Quality Gate

Reject hooks that are only clever. They must open a real decision conversation.
