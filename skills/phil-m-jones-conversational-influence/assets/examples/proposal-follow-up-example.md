# Example Output - Proposal Follow-Up

## Context

A prospect received a proposal five days ago and has not replied. The goal is to restart momentum without blame.

## Output

**First follow-up**

I am guessing you have not had a chance to sit with the proposal yet. The part that matters most is whether the first 30 days feel like the right starting point.

When would be a good time to compare notes for 10 minutes?

**If they say they need more time**

Of course. Before you make your mind up, would it be useful to look at the three paths in front of you so you can decide whether this is a now, later, or not-right-fit decision?

**If they say price is the issue**

What makes you say that?

If the investment could be shaped around the first milestone, would you want to move forward, or is there something else you are weighing?

## Mechanics Used

- Face-saving follow-up
- Assumptive timing
- No-to-maybe recovery
- Objection diagnosis
- Conditional commitment test
