# Decision Framework

## Routing By Conversation State

|---|---|---|---|
| New idea, possible rejection | "They may shut this down fast." | `03-permission-based-opener.md` | 1, 2, 11 |
| False certainty | "They think they already know." | `02-magic-words-selector.md` | 3, 8, 17 |
| No emotional pull | "They understand it but do not care." | `04-mental-picture-builder.md` | 4, 5, 12 |

## Three-Part Script Shape

1. **Safety frame** - Lower pressure or restore dignity.
2. **Decision frame** - Give them a picture, choice, or diagnostic question.
3. **Easy next step** - Ask something factual, schedulable, or small.

## Ethical Gate

Do not use a phrase if it creates false urgency, hides material information, implies a fake norm, or traps the person into a choice that does not serve them.
