# Hidden Knowledge

## Words Are Tools, Not Spells

The phrase does not create the result by itself. The result comes from matching words, timing, tone, relationship, and intent.

## Tone Carries the Safety Signal

The same words can sound curious or confrontational. Phil M Jones mechanics require a listener to feel that they still have agency.

## Prepared Does Not Mean Fake

A script is not inauthentic when it helps the speaker say something truthful with clarity. The worst time to invent the words is usually the moment they are needed.

## Simple Is Not Easy

The phrases are simple enough to remember, but they need repetition in low-stakes moments before they become natural under pressure.

## No Often Means Not Enough Yet

Delay, objection, and no are often incomplete information. The practitioner asks one better question before accepting or fighting the surface answer.

## The Next Question Should Be Easy

After a large decision frame, the next question should be small: time, address, preference, option, date, or reply.

## Dignity Restarts Momentum

Follow-up works better when it lets the other person save face. Shame may get a response, but it damages the relationship.

## People Decide Twice

They first make a decision in imagination, then in action. If they cannot picture the changed state, the real-world decision stalls.
