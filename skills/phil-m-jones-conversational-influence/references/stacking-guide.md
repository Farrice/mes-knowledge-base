# Stacking Guide

## Sales Conversations

- **Phil M Jones x Jeremy Miner**: Use Phil for the exact phrase mechanics and Jeremy for identity-based question architecture.
- **Output**: A sales script where the buyer reaches the decision through low-pressure questions and identity-safe frames.

## Conversion Copy

- **Phil M Jones x Joanna Wiebe**: Use Phil for spoken decision phrases and Joanna for persuasion hierarchy, trigger placement, and copy craft.
- **Output**: Landing page, email, or DM copy that feels conversational without losing conversion intent.

## Belief Change

- **Phil M Jones x David McRaney**: Use Phil for the next words and McRaney for safety, identity protection, and belief-change conditions.
- **Output**: Influence scripts that lower resistance instead of forcing agreement.

## Behavioral Choice Design

- **Phil M Jones x Rory Sutherland**: Use Phil for the words and Rory for context, option design, and perceived value.
- **Output**: Choice architecture that changes the decision environment and the sentence.

## Story and Content

- **Phil M Jones x Persuasion Story Code**: Use Phil for hooks and CTAs, then use story code for proof, reassurance, and objection stories.
- **Output**: Content that opens a conversation, creates a picture, and moves to a reply or call.
