# Michael Connelly Vivid Writing

Write prose where a single detail does the work of five paragraphs, where momentum is sacred, and where every element exists to deliver character.

> *"Instead of five details, you pick the one that says something about the situation, the character."* — Michael Connelly

## Tier 3 — Cross-Domain

Connelly's craft translated into the converting-copy, brand, social, and ghostwriting lanes — each composes with the existing engine it names and never manufactures a claim (honesty spine: details/proof stay real).

| Command (/slug) | Produces | Use When | Stacks With |
|-----------------|----------|----------|-------------|
| /connelly-copy-detail | Ad copy / VSL / landing lines where ONE concrete, true detail implies the feature set — replacing abstract benefit lists with a single particular the reader's mind builds the rest from, with a per-detail honesty-spine confirmation that every detail is real/substantiated | A piece of converting copy reads as an abstract benefit list ("save time, reduce stress, scale faster") and you need one concrete detail that makes the reader INFER the benefit (which they trust more than being told it) — applied to VSL leads, fascination bullets, landing-page proof, or email body | luke-iha-copy-blocks (proof architecture + telling detail = copy that proves without sounding like it's convincing) and copy-engine (Connelly supplies the detail-selection layer inside copy-engine's offer/proof structure — he tunes which particular survives, never manufactures the claim). Optional subtext pass from subtext-dialogue-builder. |
| /connelly-brand-momentum | A marked-up brand/content narrative with every speed bump flagged (pauses, re-reads, comfortable stopping points), each paragraph-ending checked for forward pull, density-threshold violations split, and a momentum verdict — the reader cannot find a place to stop | A brand story, About page, content series, manifesto, or long-form narrative loses the reader's forward energy — it has "good places to stop," exposition dumps, or paragraphs the eye drifts away from before the point lands | stanton-clamp-audit (Stanton's clamp closes each unit's tension loop; Connelly's momentum audit ensures the loops chain so the reader can't disengage between them — clamp = per-unit grip, momentum = inter-unit pull). Also pairs with real-world-anchor for believability and character-through-place for depth without slowing. |
| /connelly-content-slingshot | A short-form opener (social post / Substack lede / email first lines) built on the slingshot mechanic: empathic strike + the trigger moment that "drops the car into drive," anchored on one telling detail, with zero comfortable exit before the reader is committed | You need an opening that converts a passive scroller/skimmer into a committed reader — a LinkedIn/X hook, a Substack edition lede, or the first three lines of an email — and the current open states a claim or "sets the scene" instead of pulling the trigger | parallax (Substack edition ledes — slingshot supplies the drop-into-drive open under Parallax's zeitgeist-first structure), linkedin-daily (daily post hooks), and hook-forge (Connelly's slingshot = the narrative-momentum variant in the hook toolkit; telling-detail open vs pure-curiosity open). Composes after connelly-copy-detail when the open needs a concrete particular. |
| /connelly-ghostwrite-economy | A ghostwritten client voice cut for economy: dialogue/voice halved with zero information loss, the nod-over-yes principle applied (what the client leaves unsaid characterizes them), the insider-access / outsider-belonging gap defined as the voice's engine, and a subtext check on the lines that must persuade without a hard sell | Ghostwriting in a client's voice and the draft is flabby, over-explained, or generically "professional" — the client says too much, every claim is spelled out, and the voice has no edge or distinct economy | nicolas-cole-sentence-craft (Cole supplies sentence rhythm + structural patterns; Connelly supplies what to LEAVE OUT and the economy that makes a voice distinct — Cole fills the frame, Connelly implies it) and ghostwrite (Connelly's economy/voice layer inside the ghostwriting pipeline). Optional outsider-lens-filter for sharpening the access/belonging gap. |

### Cross-Domain Reach (Tier 3)

The four Tier 3 workflows extend Connelly's craft outward into the conversion, brand, social, and ghostwriting engines without ever rebuilding them — Connelly is the detail/momentum/economy layer slotted *inside* the engine that owns the structure, and the honesty spine holds (he tunes which true particular survives; he never manufactures a claim).

| Cross-Domain Stack | What You Get |
|--------------------|-------------|
| **Luke Iha Copy Blocks + copy-engine** (via /connelly-copy-detail) | Converting copy where one concrete, substantiated detail implies the whole feature set — the reader infers the benefit (and trusts it) instead of being told it. Connelly supplies detail-selection inside copy-engine's offer/proof structure. |
| **stanton-clamp-audit** (via /connelly-brand-momentum) | Per-unit grip (Stanton's clamp closes each tension loop) chained into inter-unit pull (Connelly's momentum keeps the loops linked) so the reader of a brand story / About page / series can't find a place to stop. Pairs with real-world-anchor + character-through-place. |
| **parallax + linkedin-daily + hook-forge** (via /connelly-content-slingshot) | Drop-into-drive short-form openers: the slingshot becomes the narrative-momentum variant in the hook toolkit (telling-detail open vs. pure-curiosity open), supplying Parallax ledes, LinkedIn/X hooks, and email first lines. Composes after /connelly-copy-detail when the open needs a concrete particular. |
| **nicolas-cole-sentence-craft + ghostwrite** (via /connelly-ghostwrite-economy) | A client voice cut for economy — dialogue/voice halved, nod-over-yes restraint, the insider-access / outsider-belonging gap defined as the voice's engine. Cole fills the frame with rhythm; Connelly decides what to leave out. Optional outsider-lens-filter sharpens the access/belonging gap. |
