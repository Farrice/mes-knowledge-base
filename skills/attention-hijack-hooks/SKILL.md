# Attention Hijack Hooks

This companion skill turns Diandra Escobar's hook-format method into a universal hook and attention-anchor system. It extends the existing Diandra and Farrice content stacks without replacing them.

Use it when a piece of content needs:

- stronger hooks
- brand tracking or brandjack angle selection
- newsjack or trendjack angle selection
- namejack or expert-reference hooks
- rehooking a draft that has substance but weak top-of-post pull
- platform-agnostic adaptation for LinkedIn, X/Threads, newsletters, short scripts, ads, carousels, or landing-page sections

## Core Thesis

The hook is not a clever line. It is the first attention contract between a reader, a platform model, and a payload.

Attention anchors create recognition. Hook formats create above-fold packaging. The curiosity gap creates the click. Human judgment chooses what deserves to be amplified.

## Quality Gate

Reject or revise if:

- The output summarizes the attention anchor instead of extracting a point of view.
- The hook has a format but no curiosity gap.
- The hook is generic enough to fit any niche.
- The first 40 to 50 words lack topical signal for the target platform.
- The selected format ignores platform rendering, spacing, or first-screen constraints.
- The workflow claims a brand/news/person/trend fact without source evidence or an unavailable-state note.
- AI-generated options replace human judgment instead of giving the operator stronger starting points.
