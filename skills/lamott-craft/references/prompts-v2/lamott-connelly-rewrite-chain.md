## Role & Activation

This is a cross-expert stack. Anne Lamott runs the first three passes (Child → Adult → Dental) to build emotional truth and honest voice. Then Michael Connelly enters for the surgical precision pass — cutting to the bone, strengthening detail economy, and deploying the Connelly anchor (verifiable real-world details that make writing feel documentary). The stack works because Lamott builds what's *true* and Connelly makes what's true *precise*. Use it when a draft needs to feel like memoir but read like a thriller — both vulnerability and control.

## Input Required

1. **[DRAFT]** — raw material at any stage: notes, a Draft 1, or a messy document
2. **[CONTENT TYPE]** — essay, memoir section, long-form article, newsletter, chapter
3. **[TARGET LENGTH]** — final word count

**Pre-Flight Gate**: this is a 5-pass workflow. Block at least 2 sessions — Lamott passes in session 1, Connelly pass in session 2, ideally after sleep. Do not compress into one sitting.

## Execution Protocol

### Passes 1-3 — Lamott Domain
Run the full Three-Draft Personality System:
- **Pass 1 (Child)**: everything, all tangents, way too long, permission to be awful.
- **Pass 2 (Adult)**: firm but friendly — structure, cut darlings, anti-literary filter, nickel words.
- **Pass 3 (Dental)**: tooth by tooth, read aloud, find the real ending.

**Handoff check** before passing to Connelly — verify:
- Emotional truth is established: the piece has a beating heart.
- Voice is warm and human: sounds like a person, not a writer.
- The piece says what it needs to say, even if imprecisely.

### Pass 4 — Connelly Surgical Pass
Switch experts. Deploy Connelly's craft.
1. **Detail audit**: every descriptive detail must do work. Cut atmospheric filler. Keep only details that reveal character, advance the story, or anchor the reader in reality.
2. **Connelly anchor**: add 1-2 verifiable real-world details per page — street names, specific car models, actual restaurant names, real weather on that date. These make the reader trust everything else.
3. **Momentum check**: find and fix speed bumps — any place the reader's eye pauses or re-reads gets smoothed or cut.
4. **Dialogue tightening**: combine Lamott's "said only" with Connelly's newspaper economy — cut dialogue in half; what remains carries double weight.
5. **Slingshot opener**: if the opening still feels soft after the Lamott passes, apply Connelly's slingshot — the opening image that launches the reader forward.

### Pass 5 — Integration Check
1. Voice continuity: did the Connelly pass preserve Lamott's warmth? If the piece now sounds cold or clinical, the surgery was too aggressive — restore warmth where needed.
2. Heart + precision: the final piece should read like a personal letter written by someone with a detective's eye for detail.
3. Final read-through: the piece should move like a river — steady current, no eddies, arriving somewhere inevitable.

## Output Contract

1. **Final Draft** — the integrated 5-pass piece
2. **Pass Log** — what each pass contributed and removed
3. **Stack Assessment** — did the Connelly pass improve or damage the Lamott foundation? Note for future calibration.

## Quality Gate

1. Is emotional truth intact from the Lamott passes — does the piece still have a heart?
2. Is detail economy present from the Connelly pass — zero filler details remaining?
3. Is the voice warm AND precise, not one at the expense of the other?
4. Does the piece pass a read-aloud test with zero stumbles?
5. Are at least 2 Connelly anchors (verifiable real-world details) present?

## Creative Latitude

The handoff between experts is where judgment matters most: if the Lamott passes produced something raw but real, resist the temptation to over-polish it into Connelly's precision at the cost of the vulnerability that made it worth reading. The Connelly anchors should be chosen for what they specifically unlock about this piece's world — not generic verisimilitude details bolted on for their own sake. If Pass 5 reveals the surgery killed something essential, say so plainly in the Stack Assessment rather than declaring success by default; an honest "the Connelly pass over-corrected" is a more useful outcome than a falsely clean verdict.

## Deploy When

- A draft is emotionally honest but structurally loose
- The piece needs to feel like memoir but read like a thriller
- The writer has completed the Three-Draft Rewrite and the result still feels imprecise or padded
