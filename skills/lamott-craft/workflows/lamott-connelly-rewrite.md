# Lamott → Connelly Rewrite Chain

## Role
This is a cross-expert stack. Anne Lamott runs the first three passes (Child → Adult → Dental) to build emotional truth and honest voice. Then Michael Connelly enters for the surgical precision pass — cutting to the bone, strengthening detail economy, and deploying the Connelly anchor (verifiable real-world details that make fiction feel documentary).

The stack works because Lamott builds what's *true* and Connelly makes what's true *precise*.

## When to Use
- You have a draft that's emotionally honest but structurally loose
- You need writing that FEELS like memoir but READS like a thriller
- The piece needs both vulnerability and control

## Input Required
1. **[Draft]**: Raw material — any stage (notes, Draft 1, or messy document)
2. **[Content Type]**: Essay, memoir section, long-form article, newsletter, chapter
3. **[Target Length]**: Final word count

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: This is a 5-pass workflow. Block at least 2 sessions (Lamott passes in session 1, Connelly pass in session 2 after sleep). Do NOT compress into one sitting.

## Workflow

### Pass 1-3: Lamott Domain (Execute `/lamott-drafts`)
Run the full Three-Draft Personality System.
- **Pass 1 (Child)**: Everything. All tangents. Way too long. Permission to be awful.
- **Pass 2 (Adult)**: Firm but friendly. Structure. Cut darlings. Anti-literary filter. Nickel words.
- **Pass 3 (Dental)**: Tooth by tooth. Read aloud. Find the real ending.

**Handoff Check**: Before passing to Connelly, verify:
- Emotional truth is established — the piece has a beating heart
- Voice is warm and human — sounds like a person, not a writer
- The piece says what it needs to say — even if imprecisely

### Pass 4: Connelly Surgical Pass
Switch experts. Now deploy Connelly's craft.

1. **Detail Audit**: Every descriptive detail must do work. Cut atmospheric filler. Keep only details that reveal character, advance story, or anchor the reader in reality.
2. **Connelly Anchor**: Add 1-2 verifiable real-world details per page — street names, specific models of cars, actual restaurant names, real weather on that date. These make the reader trust everything else.
3. **Momentum Check**: Apply Connelly's "find and fix speed bumps" — any place the reader's eye pauses or re-reads is a speed bump. Smooth it or cut it.
4. **Dialogue Tightening**: Lamott's "said only" + Connelly's "newspaper economy" — cut dialogue in half. What remains must carry double weight.
5. **Slingshot Opener**: If the opening still feels soft after Lamott's passes, apply Connelly's slingshot — the opening image that launches the reader forward.

### Pass 5: Integration Check
1. **Voice Continuity**: Did the Connelly pass preserve Lamott's warmth? If the piece now sounds cold or clinical, the surgery was too aggressive. Restore warmth where needed.
2. **Heart + Precision**: The final piece should feel like reading a personal letter written by someone with a detective's eye for detail.
3. **Final Read**: One complete read-through. The piece should move like a river — steady current, no eddies, arriving somewhere inevitable.

## Output Contract
1. **Final Draft**: The integrated 5-pass piece
2. **Pass Log**: What each pass contributed and removed
3. **Stack Assessment**: Did the Connelly pass improve or damage the Lamott foundation? Note for future calibration.

## Quality Gate
1. Emotional truth intact from Lamott passes — the piece has a heart
2. Detail economy from Connelly pass — zero filler details
3. Voice is warm AND precise — not one or the other
4. Read-aloud test passed with zero stumbles
5. At least 2 Connelly anchors (verifiable real-world details) present

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: If the Connelly pass killed the vulnerability, the stack failed. Connelly's precision serves Lamott's truth — not replaces it. The piece should feel like a conversation with someone who notices everything.
