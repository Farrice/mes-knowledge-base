# Parasite SEO Playbook

Execute Darrel Wilson's parasite SEO strategy for affiliate and service-based content — publish on high-DA platforms (Medium DA95, LinkedIn DA98, Reddit DA97) to rank on Google within 2-4 weeks instead of 6-12 months on your own domain.

## Input Required

- **Niche**: What topic/industry are you targeting?
- **Monetization**: Affiliate links, service promotion, or lead generation?
- **Target Keywords**: Specific keywords you want to rank for (or keyword research needed?)
- **Content Capacity**: How many articles per week can you publish?

## Execution

### Step 1: Keyword Qualification

Find low-competition keywords where parasite content can rank:

**Criteria for Parasite SEO Keywords:**
- Keyword Difficulty (KD) < 30 (Ahrefs/Ubersuggest)
- Monthly search volume > 200
- Commercial or informational intent (not navigational)
- No existing Medium/LinkedIn results dominating (if they do, you're competing against yourself)

**Keyword Research Process:**
1. Seed keyword brainstorm (your niche + buyer questions)
2. Run through Ahrefs/Ubersuggest for KD and volume
3. Check SERP manually — are there .medium.com or linkedin.com results ranking?
4. If yes → that keyword is PROVEN for parasite SEO
5. If no → check if the top results are thin/outdated → opportunity

### Step 2: Platform Selection Matrix

| Platform | DA | Article Length | Affiliate Links | Best Content Type |
|----------|-----|---------------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Medium** | 95 | 1,500-2,500 words | ✅ Yes (in body) | Guides, how-tos, comparisons |
| **LinkedIn Articles** | 98 | 1,000-2,000 words | ✅ Yes (in articles) | B2B insights, professional guides |
| **Reddit** | 97 | 300-1,000 words | ⚠️ Careful (value-first) | Answer-style, community help |
| **Quora** | 93 | 500-1,500 words | ⚠️ Subtle (in thorough answers) | Q&A responses with tool mentions |
| **HubPages** | 91 | 1,500-3,000 words | ✅ Yes (Amazon focused) | Product-adjacent informational |

### Step 3: Article Architecture (SEO-Optimized)

```
TITLE: [Primary Keyword] + [Value Proposition]
  Example: "Best Currency Exchange Apps in 2026 (Compared & Tested)"

H1: [Primary Keyword]

INTRO (100-150 words):
  - Hook with empathy for the reader's problem
  - Establish credibility (brief personal experience)
  - Preview what the article covers

H2: [Section 1 — Most Important Topic]
  - 200-400 words of genuine value
  - Natural affiliate link placement at decision point
  
H2: [Section 2 — Supporting Topic]
  - Include secondary keywords naturally
  
H2: [Section 3 — Comparison/How-To]
  - This is where affiliate links convert best
  - "To get started with [Product], click here"

H2: FAQ (Schema Markup-Friendly)
  - 3-5 real questions from "People Also Ask"
  
CONCLUSION:
  - Summary of key takeaways
  - Clear CTA with affiliate link or service pitch
```

### Step 4: Cross-Linking Strategy

Build a network of parasite content that supports itself:
1. **Hub Article**: Long-form comprehensive guide (2,500+ words on Medium)
2. **Spoke Articles**: 3-5 shorter articles on related subtopics
3. **Cross-Links**: Each spoke links back to the hub; hub links to spokes
4. **Platform Spread**: Hub on Medium, spokes on LinkedIn + Reddit + Quora

This creates topical authority even on platforms you don't own.

### Step 5: Publication Cadence

| Week | Platform | Content Type | KW Target |
|------|----------|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | Medium | Hub article (comprehensive guide) | Primary keyword |
| 1 | LinkedIn | Insight article (professional angle) | Secondary keyword |
| 2 | Reddit | Value-first answer in relevant subreddit | Long-tail question |
| 2 | Quora | 3-5 detailed answers | FAQ-style queries |
| 3 | Medium | Spoke article #1 | Supporting keyword |
| 3 | LinkedIn | Spoke article #2 (different angle) | Supporting keyword |
| 4 | Medium | Spoke article #3 + update hub with cross-links | Long-tail keyword |

### Step 6: Performance Tracking

Track each piece of content:
- **Google Search Console**: Add Medium/LinkedIn URLs to track impressions and clicks
- **Affiliate Dashboard**: Tag links with UTM parameters per article
- **Ranking Tracker**: Monitor keyword positions weekly
- **Content Performance**: Views, reads, claps/reactions per platform

**Success Metric**: A well-executed parasite SEO article should rank page 1 within 2-4 weeks and generate consistent traffic for 6-12 months.
