# AI Website Sales Pipeline

Build a systematic pipeline for finding local businesses with terrible websites, generating AI-powered replacement demos, and closing recurring-revenue web design deals. Based on Darrel Wilson's "finished product" close method and Google Maps prospecting system.

## Input Required

- **Target Niche**: What type of local businesses? (Dentists, gyms, restaurants, lawyers, salons)
- **Geographic Area**: What city/region are you targeting?
- **AI Builder**: Hostinger Horizons, Lovable, Bolt.new, or custom?
- **Pricing Comfort**: What's your target price point?
- **Scale Goal**: How many clients per month?

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: Before executing, run the **Decision Framework** in `genius.md` § Decision Framework. Confirm all diagnostic questions are answered.


## Execution

### Step 1: "Ugly Website" Prospecting

Systematically find businesses whose current websites are candidates for replacement.

**Google Maps Method:**
1. Open Google Maps for your target area
2. Search: "[niche] near [city]" (e.g., "dentist near Nashville")
3. Click through the top 20-30 results
4. Visit each website and score on the Ugly Website Scorecard:

**Ugly Website Scorecard:**

| Criteria | Score 1-5 | Red Flag Signals |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Mobile responsiveness** | | Broken layout on phone, tiny text |
| **Load speed** | | 3+ second load, no optimization |
| **Design era** | | Looks like 2010, Flash elements, clip art |
| **Content quality** | | Lorem ipsum, stock photos, broken links |
| **Contact functionality** | | No form, broken phone link, no booking |

**Threshold**: Total score ≤ 12/25 = strong prospect. Screenshot their current site.

**Alternative Prospecting Channels:**
- Yelp listings with "no website" badges
- Chamber of Commerce directories with broken links
- Facebook business pages with no website linked
- Industry-specific directories (Healthgrades, Avvo, OpenTable)

### Step 2: AI Demo Site Generation

Build a replacement demo in under 30 minutes using AI:

1. **Gather Intel**: Business name, logo (screenshot from Google/Facebook), services, hours, location
2. **Generate Site**: Use AI builder with prompt: "[Niche] business website for [Business Name] in [City]. Services: [list]. Modern, mobile-first, booking-enabled."
3. **Brand It**: Add their logo, business name, and real address/phone number
4. **Add Key Pages**: Home, About, Services, Contact, Booking/Inquiry form
5. **Deploy to Preview URL**: Get a shareable link they can click

**The Psychology**: You're NOT selling a concept. You're showing a finished product vs. their current embarrassment. The visual gap closes the sale.

### Step 3: Pricing Architecture

**The Darrel Wilson Model**: Low upfront + monthly recurring.

| Package | Upfront | Monthly | Includes |
|---------|---------|---------|----------|
| **Starter** | $200 | $47/month | AI-built site, hosting, SSL, basic maintenance |
| **Professional** | $500 | $97/month | Custom design, booking integration, monthly updates |
| **Premium** | $1,000 | $197/month | Custom features, SEO optimization, analytics, priority support |

**Why this works:**
- **Low upfront** removes the "too expensive" objection
- **Monthly recurring** builds a portfolio of passive income
- **5 Starter clients** = $235/month × 5 = $1,175/month recurring before new sales
- **20 clients in 6 months** = $940-$3,940/month recurring (depending on mix)

### Step 4: Outreach Templates

**Cold Email Template:**

```
Subject: I redesigned your website (see what you think)

Hi [First Name],

I was looking at [Business Name]'s website and I think you could be
getting a lot more customers from it.

I actually went ahead and built a redesign concept — here's what it
could look like: [PREVIEW LINK]

It's mobile-optimized, has a booking form built in, and loads in under
2 seconds.

If you're interested, I can have this live on your domain by Friday.
The setup is just $[PRICE] and includes hosting and maintenance.

No obligation — just wanted to show you what's possible.

[Your Name]
```

**Key Principles:**
- SHOW, don't tell — the preview link does the selling
- No jargon, no technical language
- Specific deliverable ("live on your domain by Friday")
- Low pressure ("no obligation")

**DM Template (Facebook/Instagram):**

```
Hey [Name]! I saw [Business Name] on Google Maps and I love what you're
doing. I actually redesigned your website for fun — check it out:
[PREVIEW LINK]

If you want it, it's yours. Takes 24 hours to go live. Just lmk! 🙌
```

### Step 5: Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I already have a website" | "You do! But check your current one on your phone right now... and then look at this one. Which would you rather your customers see?" |
| "I can't afford it" | "The setup is just $200 — less than one lost customer. And the monthly is like a Netflix subscription." |
| "My nephew does my website" | "That's great for basic updates. This is specifically designed to get you more bookings/calls. Your nephew can still manage the content." |
| "I need to think about it" | "Totally understand. The demo stays live for 7 days — here's the link. The offer is $200 setup this week, but I can only hold that for a few clients." |
| "What if I want changes?" | "Included in the monthly plan. Just email me what you need and I'll update it within 48 hours." |

### Step 6: Scaling to Agency

Once you have 5+ clients, systematize:

1. **Template Library**: Build 5-10 niche-specific templates that can be customized in 15 minutes
2. **Prospecting VA**: Hire a VA ($5/hour) to run the Ugly Website Scorecard on 50 businesses/day
3. **Outreach VA**: Hire a VA to send customized emails with preview links
4. **Fulfillment**: Use AI builders for 90% of site generation; manual polish for 10%
5. **Revenue Math at Scale**: 20 clients × $97/month = $1,940/month recurring + $4,000-10,000/month from new sales
