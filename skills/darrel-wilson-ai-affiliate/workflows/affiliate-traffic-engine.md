# Affiliate Traffic Engine

Build a multi-channel traffic strategy that generates consistent affiliate commissions using Darrel Wilson's traffic hierarchy: long-form video (primary), parasite SEO (secondary), and short-form syndication (tertiary). Based on Wilson's $50K/month commission approach.

## Input Required

- **Niche/Industry**: What are you promoting?
- **Affiliate Programs**: Which programs are you driving traffic to?
- **Current Assets**: Existing YouTube channel? Website? Social following?
- **Content Capacity**: How many pieces per week can you produce?
- **Revenue Target**: Monthly commission goal

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: Before executing, run the **Decision Framework** in `genius.md` § Decision Framework. Confirm all diagnostic questions are answered.


## Execution

### Step 1: Traffic Channel Selection

Rank channels by conversion ROI for the user's specific niche:

| Channel | Conversion Rate | Revenue Potential | Effort | Best For |
|---------|----------------|-------------------|--------|----------|
| **Long-form video** | ~5% | $50K+/month at scale | High (production) | Tech, tutorials, complex products |
| **Parasite SEO** | ~3% | $2-5K/month | Medium (writing) | Any niche with search demand |
| **Short-form video** | ~2% | $500-2K/month | Low (repurposing) | Physical products, impulse buys |
| **Email sequences** | ~4% | Compounds with list size | Medium (setup) | All niches (amplifier) |
| **Social media** | <1% | Branding only | Low | Awareness, not conversion |

### Step 2: Long-Form Content Strategy

**The Money Channel** — This is where $50K/month comes from.

**Content Type Matrix:**

| Type | Example | Promotion Style | Conversion Mechanism |
|------|---------|----------------|---------------------|
| **Tutorial** | "How to Build a Website with WordPress" | Required tool (hosting affiliate = prerequisite) | Must buy to follow along |
| **Problem-Solution** | "3 Emails That Make Us $500/Day" | Indirect mention (~10 seconds) | Product solves a friction point |
| **Comparison** | "Shopify vs WordPress for Beginners" | Balanced review both ways | Links to both in description |
| **Use Case** | "How I Use Google Flow for AI Videos" | Incidental mention (NordVPN for geo-restriction) | Product removes a blocker |

**Production Cadence**: 1 video every 2 weeks minimum. Quality > quantity. A single video with 282K views earns for years.

**Description Optimization**:
- Affiliate link in first 3 lines (above the fold)
- Clean PrettyLinks format: `yourdomain.com/go/[product]`
- Brief value prop next to each link
- Don't overdo it — 3-5 links max per description

### Step 3: Parasite SEO Strategy

**The Quick Win Channel** — Rank on Google in weeks, not months.

**Platform Selection:**

| Platform | DA | Best Content Type | Affiliate Rules |
|----------|-----|-------------------|----------------|
| **Medium** | 95 | Long-form guides, reviews | Links allowed in body |
| **LinkedIn** | 98 | Professional insights, case studies | Links in articles, not posts |
| **Reddit** | 97 | Community answers with resource links | Subreddit rules vary; add value first |
| **Quora** | 93 | Question-answer with tool recommendations | Subtle links in thorough answers |

**Keyword Strategy for Parasite SEO:**
1. Find low-competition keywords (KD < 30) using Ahrefs/Ubersuggest
2. Check if Medium/LinkedIn articles already rank for those terms
3. Write 1,500-2,000 word articles targeting those keywords
4. Embed affiliate links at natural decision points
5. Cross-link between your parasite content pieces for authority

**Cadence**: 1 article per week across platforms.

### Step 4: Short-Form Syndication

**The Awareness Channel** — Gets eyeballs, not purchases directly.

**Cross-Platform Distribution:**
1. Create one short-form piece (60-90 seconds)
2. Post to: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Pinterest Video
3. All point back to long-form content or utility site (bio link)

**Content Formula for Shorts:**
- Hook (first 2 seconds): Visual surprise or bold claim
- Value (30-60 seconds): One actionable tip or demonstration
- CTA (last 5 seconds): "Full tutorial in my latest video" — NOT "buy this product"

**Niche Fit Assessment:**
- ✅ Physical products (Amazon hauls, product demos) — shorts work well
- ✅ Lifestyle/travel (quick tips, location reveals) — shorts work well
- ❌ Complex tech tutorials — shorts don't convert, use for awareness only
- ❌ Services/SaaS — too nuanced for 60 seconds

### Step 5: Content Calendar Generator

Produce a monthly content calendar:

| Week | Long-Form | Parasite SEO | Short-Form | Email |
|------|-----------|-------------|------------|-------|
| 1 | [Tutorial: Required Tool type] | Medium article | 2 shorts from tutorial clips | Welcome sequence |
| 2 | — (editing/production) | LinkedIn article | 2 shorts from behind-scenes | Value email |
| 3 | [Problem-Solution type] | Quora answers (3-5) | 2 shorts from results/demos | Soft pitch |
| 4 | — (editing/production) | Medium article | 2 shorts from tips | Case study |

### Step 6: The Indirect Promotion Playbook

**Never make the affiliate product the star.** Wilson's hierarchy:

1. **Level 1 — Required Tool**: Product is necessary to follow the content. Highest conversion.
2. **Level 2 — Friction Remover**: Product solves a problem encountered during the content. Brief mention, ~10 seconds.
3. **Level 3 — Comparison Context**: Product appears alongside alternatives. Balanced, honest assessment.
4. **Level 4 — Direct Review**: Full review of the product. Lowest trust, lowest conversion in 2026. Avoid.

## Output Schema

A complete multi-channel traffic strategy, delivered as these fields:
- **Channel Priority Ranking** (table): channel name, conversion %, revenue potential, effort tier — ranked specifically for the user's niche, not the generic hierarchy
- **Long-Form Content Plan** (list of items): topic, promotion style (Required Tool / Friction Remover / Comparison / Direct Review), affiliate integration point
- **Parasite SEO Article Plan** (list of items): platform, target keyword, article angle, link placement
- **Short-Form Syndication Schedule** (list of items): platform, cadence, content formula (hook / value / CTA)
- **Monthly Content Calendar** (4-week table): week × long-form/parasite-SEO/short-form/email
- **Indirect Promotion Level Assignment** (table): affiliate product → Level 1-4 (Required Tool → Friction Remover → Comparison → Direct Review)
- **Revenue Projection** (numeric): traffic × conversion rate × commission = $/month estimate per channel

## Creative Latitude

The traffic hierarchy is proven by Wilson's $50K/month results. But niche-specific opportunities (emerging platforms, untapped keywords, cross-promotion partnerships) should be surfaced wherever creative intelligence detects them. The best traffic strategies exploit asymmetries nobody else sees.
