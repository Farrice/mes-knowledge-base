# Darrel Wilson — AI-Powered Affiliate Marketing

Darrel Wilson is a 10-year affiliate marketing veteran averaging $50-60K/month in commissions who has evolved from traditional affiliate marketing into AI-augmented revenue systems. His core innovation is the **utility-first affiliate model** — building functional AI tools (currency converters, crypto analyzers, file converters) that monetize through natural affiliate integration instead of the dying review-blog paradigm. He layers n8n automation workflows, AI website generation, and parasite SEO to create scalable income systems.

## Core Genius

Wilson's signature move: **build the tool, not the review.** In a post-AI-search world where ChatGPT answers "best VPN review" before anyone clicks, the only affiliate sites that survive are ones that provide genuine utility. Wilson builds them using AI, embeds affiliate links as natural next steps, and drives traffic through long-form video content (5% conversion rate — industry best).

## Key Principles

1. **Utility over opinion** — Build tools people use, embed affiliate links as natural solutions
2. **Indirect over direct** — Never make the product the subject; make the viewer's problem the subject
3. **Long-form over short-form** — 5% vs 2% conversion rate; $50K/month vs branding
4. **Recurring over one-time** — Monthly subscriptions compound; one-time sales don't
5. **AI-augmented over manual** — n8n workflows, AI builders, automated link placement
6. **Marketplace over direct** — Join via Impact/Awin for payment protection
7. **Anti-freemium** — Avoid programs with free tiers; cookies expire before conversion
