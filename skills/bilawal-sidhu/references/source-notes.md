## 3. Fidelity assessment — read this before trusting any workflow here

**Honest verdict: the philosophy is course-deep; the greybox procedure is post-deep.**

The brief anticipated a thread-deep corpus and permitted shipping smaller. What was actually found is
lopsided rather than thin, and the two halves need different levels of trust:

**Deep and directly quotable (high confidence).** The spatial-control doctrine — viewport-before-render,
explicit vs. implicit, the reproducibility gate, create-once, spatial memory as the operator's job,
retrieval-over-recall, annotation-as-camera-channel, previz/finish split, route-by-department. This is
argued at length, from multiple angles, across four 2026 video essays totalling ~1h 20m, with hands-on
demonstration. `genius.md` Groups A, C, D and F rest on this and are quote-backed throughout.

**Shallow in the primary source (flagged wherever it appears).** The greybox→reskin **procedure** exists
in exactly one public artifact: a 2025-01-06 post of three numbered steps plus three observations
[S1]. There is no public step-by-step, no software walkthrough, no worked example with settings, no
teaching of what makes a greybox good. Everything in `workflows/01-greybox-reskin-shot.md` beyond those
three steps and three notes is one of:

- **(a) Sidhu-derived** — extended from his stated practice elsewhere in the corpus. Tagged inline.
- **(b) Standard previz/look-dev practice** — not his, not invented, and marked as craft-general.

**It is never presented as "Bilawal Sidhu's greybox framework," because he never published one.**

**Not in this corpus at all — route elsewhere:**
- Story, script, character, performance, edit rhythm → `skills/dave-clark/`.
- Prompt syntax, style codes, image art direction → `skills/nick-st-pierre/`, `skills/rory-flynn/`.
- Colour, grade, delivery specs, chain of title → `skills/dave-clark/workflows/02-hybrid-pipeline-plan.md`.
- Any specific 3D application's operation. He names software; he never teaches it.

**One structural caveat on the man himself.** His 2026 output has moved substantially toward
spatial-intelligence and OSINT journalism — of the 30 most recent uploads on the main channel,
roughly a third concern creative tooling and the rest concern satellites, mapping, surveillance and
defence [channel page, 2026-08-02]. The Substack has moved almost entirely that way [S10]. He is an
active practitioner-analyst of spatial computing who *applies* it to creative work, not a full-time
creative director. The skill is scoped to match.

**Prompt count: three, not eight.** One per deliverable the corpus can honestly carry. Per the fidelity

## 4. Method notes on the extraction itself

- X.com blocks automated reads (402 on WebFetch; every Nitter mirror tried was behind a bot wall). The
  syndication endpoint `cdn.syndication.twimg.com/tweet-result?id=…` returned the root tweet verbatim;
  the author's own LinkedIn cross-post carried the complete text. **Tweets 2/5–5/5 of the original
  thread were not recoverable** — their content is presumed to be the walkthrough shown in the attached
  video. This is a known gap; if the thread becomes readable later, re-check whether it adds procedure.
- Playwright was available but in use by a concurrent extraction; the browser was deliberately not
  seized. All retrieval here was done through free, non-gated routes.
- No paid research API was called. No cost gate was triggered.
