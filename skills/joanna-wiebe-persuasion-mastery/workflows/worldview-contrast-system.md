# Joanna Wiebe — Worldview Contrast System

## Role

Turn accurate but generic positioning into a viewpoint the audience can see through, then create contrast without scapegoating. This is the first two layers of Belief Community Architecture.

## Input Required

- audience and chosen identity
- current category assumption
- offer/product truth
- evidence and counterevidence
- audience language or explicit VOC gap
- protected people/groups and claim constraints

## Execution

1. **Map the default lens:** state what the audience currently assumes, why it persists, and which decisions it creates.
2. **Generate five reframes:** use `Most people think __. Here is what is actually true __.` Reject feature claims and adjectives.
3. **Select by consequence:** choose the lens that changes proof, behavior, or comparison criteria in at least three downstream contexts.
4. **Build the honest devil:** name the behavior, belief, broken status quo, or institution preserving the default; explain the mechanism and consequences.
5. **Protect agency:** name what is not being attacked, where the brand may be wrong, and what evidence would revise the lens.
6. **Write finished language:** produce a founder thesis, homepage contrast, campaign line, and content-series premise from the same lens.

Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/worldview-contrast-system.md` — honor its Output Contract.

## Output Contract

- default-lens diagnostic
- five candidate lenses with evidence labels
- selected lens and decision delta
- honest devil architecture
- protected boundary and disconfirming evidence
- four finished language expressions
- unresolved evidence gaps

## Quality Gate

- Selected lens changes judgment, not merely wording.
- Contrast targets a mechanism, never a person, demographic, critic, customer, or competitor's users.
- No fabricated VOC or proof.
- Finished lines remain clear without framework labels.
- Disconfirming evidence is explicit.
