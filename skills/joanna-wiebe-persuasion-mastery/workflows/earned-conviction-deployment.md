# Joanna Wiebe — Earned Conviction Deployment

## Role

Turn an existing five-layer belief system into conviction that sounds certain because it is earned. Refuse bravado, hidden uncertainty, and causal claims the evidence cannot support.

## Input Required

- approved lens, honest devil, symbol, ritual, and language
- verified proof, likely evidence, unknowns, counterevidence
- desired future and decision
- channels and permission state

## Execution

1. **Audit prerequisites:** stop or return gaps if any of the first five layers is only decorative.
2. **Build the evidence ladder:** VERIFIED, LIKELY, UNCONFIRMED, UNTESTED, NO EVENT, NO PERMISSION.
3. **Write the future three ways:** conservative, supported, and frontier. The frontier version may be visionary but must remain visibly a thesis unless verified.
4. **Select the strongest honest line:** compress to one portable declaration; attach its evidence label and disconfirming condition.
5. **Deploy by channel:** founder writing, content refrain, campaign promise, landing-page thesis, offer ritual, community declaration, and live talk track.
6. **Run the certainty audit:** remove verbal hedges that obscure thinking, then reduce claim strength where evidence is weak.

Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/earned-conviction-deployment.md` — honor its Output Contract.

## Output Contract

- prerequisite audit
- evidence ladder
- three future declarations
- selected proof-bounded declaration
- seven-channel conviction map
- claim and permission boundaries
- next evidence needed

## Quality Gate

- All five prerequisite layers exist and reinforce one another.
- Certainty matches evidence; tone never upgrades proof state.
- No inevitability, false causal claim, or private-state diagnosis.
- The line is portable and specific without hype.
- NO PERMISSION channels remain prepared but unsent/unpublished.
