# Belief Community Architecture — Source-Grounded Reference

## Source

Joanna Wiebe, *How To Command Belief Like a Cult Leader* (2026-08-20), local package: `extractions/video-context/Tnv9PuMo84E/`.

## Dependency chain

`lens -> honest devil -> symbol -> ritual -> language -> earned conviction`

Treat the chain as a progression, not a checklist. A later component cannot compensate for a missing earlier one.

## Mechanism cards

### 1. Shiny New Lens

- **Question:** What default assumption keeps the audience judging this through the wrong frame?
- **Build:** `Most people think [default]. Here is what is actually true: [evidence-backed reframe].`
- **Pass:** the reframe changes choices, proof standards, or what the audience notices.
- **Fail:** a feature claim or premium adjective wearing worldview clothes.

### 2. Honest Devil

- **Question:** What broken behavior, belief, status quo, or institution keeps the old lens alive?
- **Build:** `We exist because the normal way of [activity] is broken: [mechanism]. We refuse to [complicity].`
- **Pass:** specific, falsifiable, non-human target; audience ego protected.
- **Fail:** a person, demographic, customer, critic, competitor's users, or fabricated conspiracy.

### 3. Belief Symbol

- **Question:** What visible or holdable signal would let another believer infer the worldview?
- **Build:** color, phrase, mark, tool, proof label, object, or repeated visual treatment.
- **Pass:** carries belief even when the logo is removed.
- **Fail:** merchandise whose only meaning is brand affiliation.

### 4. Participation Ritual

- **Question:** What useful action should people perform repeatedly to experience the worldview?
- **Build:** cue -> participant action -> immediate value -> visible receipt.
- **Optional earned threshold:** a reversible, proportionate contribution that creates value or demonstrates readiness.
- **Pass:** voluntary, useful, accessible, and easy to exit.
- **Fail:** secrecy, humiliation, pain, collateral, arbitrary scarcity, exploitative lock-in, or exit penalty.

### 5. Shared Language

- **Question:** What does the audience feel but struggle to name?
- **Build:** 1–3 short phrases, each tied to the lens and a real situation.
- **Pass:** audience-readable, portable, meaning stays stable across contexts.
- **Fail:** corporate jargon, borrowed public phrase with no new meaning, or language that suppresses critical thought.

### 6. Earned Conviction

- **Question:** What future does the evidence already make reasonable to declare?
- **Build:** one repeatable future line plus evidence label and disconfirming condition.
- **Pass:** confidence matches verified proof; no hedging used to hide weak thinking.
- **Fail:** bravado, inevitability, private-state diagnosis, or certainty without evidence.

## Evidence ladder

| State | Allowed expression |
|---|---|
| VERIFIED | direct declaration within the verified boundary |
| LIKELY | calibrated declaration plus why it is likely |
| UNCONFIRMED | hypothesis or question, not a fact |
| UNTESTED | design intent; no efficacy claim |
| NO EVENT | do not imply audience adoption, conversion, or revenue |
| NO PERMISSION | prepare privately; do not publish, send, or deploy externally |

## Adoption events

Count only observable behavior:

- audience uses a phrase without prompting;
- participant completes the ritual voluntarily;
- buyer asks for the proof ritual or compares alternatives through the lens;
- another person recognizes the symbol and accurately explains its meaning;
- community member helps another member enact the belief.

Brand repetition, internal enthusiasm, and asset completion are not adoption events.
