## Visual evidence — what 28 frames of the 2025-02-17 Promise reel actually show

Read directly with the Read tool (no transcript exists for this source). This is the empirical half of the
flat-vs-cinematic axis; the interviews supply the *why*, the frames supply the *what*. Crucially, **this is
model-independent evidence** — it is composition, light and grade, none of which belongs to any generator.

Recurring across every frame regardless of genre (WWII swamp, WWII naval, sci-fi mech, cyberpunk alley,
school brawl, black pyramid, astronaut):

1. **One dominant motivated source, behind or beside the subject.** Not one frame of 28 is front-lit.
   Rim/edge separation carries the subject off the background; in several shots the face is the *least*
   lit part of the frame (t=00:12 swamp soldier, t=00:26 astronaut).
2. **A real black point.** Shadows land on actual black, not lifted grey. Highlights are allowed to clip at
   source (fire, flare, rim). This is the register he names verbatim as *"whites are really white and blacks
   are really black"* [EVERY 2024-02-28 @ 27:43].
3. **Atmosphere in the mid-ground on every shot** — mist, water haze, smoke, dust, rain, spray, sparks.
   Nothing is photographed through clean air.
4. **Desaturated cool base + exactly one warm accent family.** Teal/slate/graphite base; the accent is fire,
   sodium, or a red practical (t=00:16 mech + fire pillar, t=00:35 black pyramid with red running lights,
   t=00:39 cyberpunk alley with CRT glow). Never two competing warms in one frame.
5. **Foreground occlusion.** The camera looks *through* something — a post, doorway, shoulder, gate
   (t=00:39 alley occluded across the left third by a dark vertical). Depth is built from planes, not blur.
6. **A capture-artifact layer.** t=00:19 (naval) carries a full telecine/gate burn-in — timecode and edge-code
   numerals top and bottom of frame. He is deliberately layering the evidence of a physical camera over
   footage that never had one. Corroborates the global-capture-layer testimony in [FWAI 2023-11-18 @ 16:13].
7. **Anamorphic-wide framing, human small in field.** Letterboxed ~2.39; the environment is a character.

None of these seven is stated as a rule in any interview. They are the **observed** signature, tagged
`[REEL-obs]` wherever used, never presented as quoted doctrine.

## Fidelity assessment

**Overall: HIGH for the durable layer (judgment, direction discipline, pipeline architecture,
edit-survivability). MEDIUM for edit-layer craft. DELIBERATELY LOW / QUARANTINED for tool mechanics.**

**What the corpus genuinely supports (extracted at full depth into the core method):**
- The taste/judgment layer: generator-as-camera specification discipline, shot-list-before-prompt,
  take-not-prompt, model-manager framing, feel→mechanism reference debugging
- Selection discipline and its diagnosis of flatness
- Edit-layer craft as *principles*: cadence variation, composite-the-take, coverage pairs, a global capture layer
- The 2025–26 studio-grade layer: drift as an architecture problem, colour-space compliance as the release gate,
  provenance as a craft requirement, hybrid-crew doctrine
- The observed visual signature (model-independent: light motivation, black point, atmosphere, palette
  discipline, occlusion, capture layer, framing)

**What the corpus does NOT support — and was therefore NOT written:**
- ❌ Any lighting **ratio**, f-stop, colour-temperature number, LUT, or grading value. He never gives one.
  The reel shows the *result*; nobody states the recipe. Inventing one is exactly the fabrication the
  fidelity rule forbids.
- ❌ A lens/focal-length table. He says "wide shot, medium shot, close-up" and "extreme close-up" and stops.
- ❌ Current-model guidance. The tool names in [FWAI]/[EVERY] are historically accurate and operationally
  dead. Quarantined to Appendix A with a verify-before-use banner.
- ❌ A named "flat vs cinematic" framework. **He has never published one.** The eight-cause diagnostic in
  `workflows/01-flat-to-cinematic-audit.md` is assembled by this extraction from stated doctrine + the
  observed reel signature, with per-check source tags so the seam stays visible. Presented as *Clark-derived*,
  never *Clark-stated*.
- ❌ Prompt syntax libraries or style codes. That is Nick St. Pierre's and Rory Flynn's lane (hunt #1, #3).
  He is deliberately upstream of prompt syntax.

**Prompt count decision: 3, not 6.** One per genuinely distinct deliverable the corpus can carry
(diagnostic audit / hybrid pipeline plan / shot-list-to-generation brief). A fourth on "lighting design" or
"prompt syntax" would have required invention. Three honest prompts beat twelve padded ones.

**Recommended top-up.** The gap is a *current* dense process breakdown. At extraction time the 2025–26
surface was: the reel (no audio), a 74-second Cannes soundbite, and two text interviews. If a 2026 Promise
process video appears, re-watch it and promote whatever it corroborates out of Appendix A into the core method.
