# Niche Evidence Research

**Execution prompt:** `../references/prompts-v2/niche-evidence-research.md`

## Purpose

Turn available behavior and market context into comparable niche candidates without laundering weak evidence into certainty.

## Protocol

1. Define the decision and minimum evidence needed.
2. Collect owned evidence: past posts, replies, saves, subscriptions, calls, proposals, delivery results, repeat requests, and payments.
3. When current market facts could change the recommendation, research authoritative primary sources and observable competitor/customer behavior.
4. Normalize candidate evidence across: creator interest, audience interest, problem urgency, outcome clarity, delivery advantage, information advantage, and commercial event.
5. Separate `VERIFIED`, `SELF-REPORTED`, `DERIVED`, `UNTESTED`, and `NO EVENT`.
6. Rank no more than three candidates. Name the weakest assumption and the cheapest test that could reverse the ranking.

## Output

Evidence inventory, comparison table, ranked candidates, recommendation, dissenting case, and next test.

## Quality Gate

No score may substitute for a real event. Views are content signal; replies are problem signal; deposits and payments are commercial signal.
## Output Schema

Evidence inventory, comparison table, ranked candidates, recommendation, dissenting case, next test.

## Quality Gate

The result must preserve source-versus-derived boundaries, label uncertainty, name one executable next test, and avoid treating clarity or engagement as payment proof.
