# 30 Pieces in 30 Days Discovery

**Execution prompt:** `../references/prompts-v2/thirty-in-thirty-discovery.md`

## Purpose

Generate enough publishing behavior to discover a creator niche when no reliable audience signal exists.

## Protocol

1. Select three plausible topic territories the creator can sustain.
2. Define ten pieces per territory or another balanced 30-piece design.
3. Keep format and distribution sufficiently stable to make topic response comparable.
4. Track reach, completion, saves, replies, follows/subscriptions, qualified conversations, and creator energy.
5. At day 30, identify two or three topics with roughly 2× relative lift on decision-relevant signals.
6. Turn the strongest topic into the next experiment by varying audience, problem, and outcome.
7. Repeat monthly until a durable signal or a clear rejection appears.

## Output

30-piece matrix, measurement sheet, weekly learning questions, day-30 decision rule, and month-two variation plan.

## Negative Control

Do not call a one-off viral post a niche. Do not call low absolute metrics failure when relative lift is meaningful. Do not call engagement payment.
## Output Schema

Thirty-piece matrix, measurement sheet, weekly questions, day-30 decision rule, month-two variations.

## Quality Gate

The result must preserve source-versus-derived boundaries, label uncertainty, name one executable next test, and avoid treating clarity or engagement as payment proof.
