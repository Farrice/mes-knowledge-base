# Niche Brief

**Execution prompt:** `../references/prompts-v2/niche-brief.md`

## Purpose

Package a niche decision so an operator, team, or client can use it for offers, research, content, and evaluation.

## Required Sections

1. Decision and confidence.
2. Route and why it fits.
3. Evidence ledger.
4. Audience, problem, desired outcome, and vehicle.
5. Specialist advantage and information advantage.
6. Tip of spear plus constellation.
7. Content and commercial tests.
8. Refusal/capacity rules.
9. Risks, dissent, confirmation signals, and reversal conditions.

## Quality Gate

The brief must distinguish what is known, inferred, and untested. It must be specific enough to guide action but reversible enough to survive new evidence.
## Output Schema

Decision, route, evidence, audience, problem, outcome, vehicle, advantage, constellation, tests, risks, reversal.

## Quality Gate

The result must preserve source-versus-derived boundaries, label uncertainty, name one executable next test, and avoid treating clarity or engagement as payment proof.
