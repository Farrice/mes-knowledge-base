# Commit or Pivot Audit

**Execution prompt:** `../references/prompts-v2/commit-or-pivot-audit.md`

## Purpose

Determine whether a niche should be continued, refined, or abandoned—and prevent a missing skill or overloaded delivery system from masquerading as a niche failure.

## Protocol

1. Reconstruct the test: volume, duration, audience, content quality, distribution, offer, sales motion, delivery, and refusal discipline.
2. Identify the actual failure layer: category legibility, content, funnel, sales, delivery, capacity, or market response.
3. Separate `NO EVENT` from negative evidence.
4. Test whether the service/topic received a fair repetition window.
5. Choose one verdict: `COMMIT`, `REFINE`, `RETEST`, or `PIVOT`.
6. Name the preservation lock—the skills, proof, audience knowledge, and assets that transfer even if the vehicle changes.

## Output

Verdict, evidence, primary failure layer, preservation lock, next test, stop condition, and reversal trigger.

## Quality Gate

A pivot requires evidence against the current vehicle or a superior opportunity—not boredom, comparison anxiety, or one weak post.
## Output Schema

Verdict, evidence, failure layer, preservation lock, next test, stop condition, reversal trigger.

## Quality Gate

The result must preserve source-versus-derived boundaries, label uncertainty, name one executable next test, and avoid treating clarity or engagement as payment proof.
