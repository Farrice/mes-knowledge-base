# Niche Discovery System

**Execution prompt:** `../references/prompts-v2/niche-discovery-system.md`

## Purpose

Route a creator or service operator to the smallest evidence-producing path and return a decision—not an identity exercise.

## Protocol

1. Classify the objective: creator audience, service clients, or hybrid.
2. Inventory existing evidence: content performance, qualified replies, client history, delivery strength, buyer language, sales, and payments.
3. Assign one state: `COLD START`, `SIGNAL EXISTS`, `SPECIALIZATION READY`, or `PIVOT QUESTION`.
4. Route:
   - cold-start creator → 30 Pieces in 30 Days;
   - cold-start service operator → Service × Industry Specialization;
   - signal exists → Niche Evidence Research;
   - pivot question → Commit or Pivot Audit.
5. After evidence, produce a Niche Brief and optionally a Content Constellation Plan.

## Output

Decision state, selected route, evidence labels, missing evidence, immediate action, success signal, and reversal condition.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before invented buyer demand, external outreach, publication, or a permanent niche claim.
## Output Schema

Decision state, selected route, evidence labels, missing evidence, immediate action, success signal, reversal condition.

## Quality Gate

The result must preserve source-versus-derived boundaries, label uncertainty, name one executable next test, and avoid treating clarity or engagement as payment proof.
