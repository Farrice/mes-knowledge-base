# Content Constellation Plan

**Execution prompt:** `../references/prompts-v2/content-constellation-plan.md`

## Purpose

Translate a niche into repeatable content that tests audience, problem, and outcome while compounding an owned library.

## Protocol

1. Start from the Niche Brief's tip of spear.
2. Define three to five content pillars tied to specific audience questions, problems, or desired outcomes.
3. Build one flagship series per pillar and a small matrix varying audience × problem × outcome.
4. Assign each idea a claim, mechanism, proof object, format, call to action, and learning objective.
5. Allocate most output to core signal, a smaller share to adjacent signal, and a bounded share to experiments.
6. Define save-back: what result, language, proof, or objection enters the owned content library.

## Output

Pillars, series, 30-day calendar, experiment matrix, measurement rules, and save-back plan.

## Negative Control

Do not generate thirty interchangeable topic titles. Every piece must test or compound something identifiable.
## Output Schema

Pillars, flagship series, experiment matrix, calendar, measurement rules, save-back plan.

## Quality Gate

The result must preserve source-versus-derived boundaries, label uncertainty, name one executable next test, and avoid treating clarity or engagement as payment proof.
