# Hidden Knowledge

## Discovery and Positioning Are Different Jobs

Discovery produces evidence; positioning makes evidence legible. A cold-start creator should create signal before polishing a permanent-sounding category.

## Creator and Service Paths Start Differently

Publishing creates creator signal. Repeating one service creates delivery and buyer signal. Forcing both through one specificity interview destroys this distinction.

## Specificity Is an Operational Constraint

It improves comprehension, repetition, and adjacent inference. It is not proof that a market will buy.

## A Niche Is Also a Capacity Design

Every accepted topic or client shapes the factory: process reuse, information advantage, delivery time, pricing power, and what the operator must refuse.

## Pivots Preserve Assets

A vehicle can change while skills, proof, audience language, content, and delivery learning transfer forward. The audit must name that preservation lock.
