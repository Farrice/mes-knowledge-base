# Nicolas Cole — Niche Discovery and Content Positioning

This is the deployable owner for Cole's niche system. It does not assume a niche is a permanent identity. It treats a niche as a reversible vehicle for building skill, signal, pricing power, and a recognizable body of work.

## Recognition Test

Load for niche discovery, content focus, service specialization, niche briefs, niche constellations, repeated niche switching, or deciding what to publish and double down on.

## Core Routing Rule

1. **No meaningful evidence + creator path** → `02-thirty-in-thirty-discovery.md`.
2. **No meaningful evidence + service path** → `03-service-industry-specialization.md`; choose the service before the industry.
3. **Existing audience, content, or client evidence** → `01-niche-evidence-research.md` before recommending a niche.
4. **Mixed or unclear path** → `00-niche-discovery-system.md` to choose the route and name missing evidence.
5. Use specificity and Category of One only as sharpening tools after the route is known—not as automatic discovery gates.

## When Not to Use

- Not as proof of buyer demand; niche selection is not payment behavior.
- Not to force permanent identity language or promise a forever niche.
- Not to declare a winner from vanity metrics alone.
- Not to treat Cole's current service examples as timeless market recommendations without fresh research.
- Not to replace macro brand strategy, offer economics, claim review, or platform-specific craft owners.
- Not to publish, contact prospects, or change client positioning without approval.

## Output Standard

Every recommendation contains the route, evidence inventory, audience/problem/outcome/vehicle, one tip of spear, bounded adjacent constellation, smallest test, capacity/refusal rule, and confirmation/reversal conditions.
