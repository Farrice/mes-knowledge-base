# Luisa Zhou — Coaching Business & Client Delivery

Luisa Zhou builds coaching businesses on a simple inversion: qualification comes from experience delivering a specific result, not credentials — her first offer was Microsoft Excel coaching, no certification, and it grew into a business that has trained 3,500+ coaches. Her delivery system centers on "coach-sulting" (guide clients to their own answers AND tell them what works from your experience) and a pre-session preparation discipline that makes the very first call feel like the best investment the client ever made. The whole system is held together by anti-perfectionism: 3-month packages at $1,500, taster sessions instead of pitches, Google Docs instead of fancy platforms, 45-minute content deadlines instead of 3-hour polish sessions.

## Quick Reference

- **Coach-sulting**: Blend coaching (client finds their own answers) with consulting ("this is exactly what you should do — borrow my experience"). Pure question-asking reads as a scam to modern clients.
- **The Three S's** (first session): **Share** questionnaire insights → **Strategy** (tentative month-by-month plan) → **Specific actions** with real dates. Every call ends with action items.
- **Welcome questionnaire**: completed 2+ business days before the first call. Goals at 1/3/6 months, weaknesses, belief stories, coaching-style preference, anticipated obstacles, strengths.
- **Starter package**: 3 months, weekly calls + one async channel, $1,500. Optimizing for learning, not profit.
- **Taster session**: 30-60 min free session, laser-focused on ONE valuable takeaway, expectations set up front ("at the end, if helpful, I'd love to share how I can help further").
- **Three sustainability pieces**: teach clients to be coachable (partnership, not done-for-you) · systematize knowledge in simple docs (today's client doc = tomorrow's group program) · set boundaries (defined channel, response time, check frequency — boundaries improve client results).
- **Content engine**: take inspiration (don't wait for it) · one idea → many pieces · one takeaway per piece · Hook/Meat/CTA · fixed time + place · 45-minute hard deadline · draft out loud like talking to a best friend.
