# Reader Access Rewrite

## Pre-Flight Gate

Require a protected original, target reader context, target feeling, and permission to rewrite. Read the full Seven-Part Reader Bridge in `../genius.md`.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../references/rubric_v1.md`, then the structural-pull execution prompt.

## Execution

1. Diagnose the highest causal layer.
2. Lock what cannot be lost: emotional fact, strange particular, native cadence, factual constraints.
3. Select no more than three interventions.
4. Rewrite the complete requested unit with mechanics invisible.
5. Compare before and after for comprehension, felt pressure, surprise, rhythm, and residue.
6. Revert any change that only makes the work safer or smoother.
7. Produce a concise causal change ledger and a stop decision.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Scene/chapter | Structural cause before line craft |
| Essay/newsletter | Preserve the writer’s voice and concrete incident |
| Brand narrative | Claims and brand facts retain veto power |
| Short post | Limit to one emotional movement and one residue ending |
| Synopsis/pitch | Simplify the carrier without spoiling the mystery |

## Output Requirements

Protected original; causal diagnosis; finished rewrite; change ledger; preserved elements; before/after comparison; evidence state; stop decision.

Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/structural-pull-edit.md` — honor its complete-rewrite branch.

## Quality Gate

- Did the highest causal problem change?
- Is the output a finished rewrite rather than advice?
- Does at least one productive irregularity remain?
- Are factual or voice constraints preserved?
- Is the result more accessible without becoming more generic?
