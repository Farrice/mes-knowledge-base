# Simple Word Residue

## Pre-Flight Gate

Require an existing passage and its intended feeling. Simplicity may not erase technical precision, legal meaning, cultural specificity, or the writer’s native voice. Read `../genius.md` Patterns 6, 8, and 9.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../references/rubric_v1.md` and the simple-word prompt.

## Execution

1. Mark abstraction, prestige vocabulary, emotional explanation, and smooth-but-dead lines.
2. Identify the concrete image, action, or object underneath each marked phrase.
3. Choose the smallest accurate word; keep specialized language where accuracy requires it.
4. Preserve meaningful irregularity and native cadence.
5. Rewrite the passage.
6. Generate three endings: image residue, shared-word residue, unresolved-action residue.
7. Select the ending that continues working without a summary sentence.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Fiction | Favor image, action, and sensory consequence |
| Essay | Keep necessary concepts; concretize their stakes |
| Technical/claim-safe | Precision outranks simplicity |
| Brand voice | Voice Card outranks Haig-derived cadence |
| Short-form | Final five words carry disproportionate weight |

## Output Requirements

Abstraction map; protected vocabulary; revised passage; three ending options; selected ending; residue rationale.

Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/simple-word-residue-edit.md` — honor its Output Contract.

## Quality Gate

- Is every replacement more accurate, not merely shorter?
- Did specialized language remain where needed?
- Did native rhythm survive?
- Does the ending avoid summarizing the feeling?
- Was productive oddity preserved?
