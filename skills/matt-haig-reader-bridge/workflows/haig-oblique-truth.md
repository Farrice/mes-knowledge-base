# Oblique Truth

## Pre-Flight Gate

Require the emotional fact and what feels unsafe, embarrassing, exposed, or overexplained in literal form. Never infer trauma or private facts. Read `../genius.md` Patterns 16 and 17.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../references/exemplars.md` and the oblique-truth prompt.

## Execution

1. State the emotional fact without plot detail.
2. Separate real danger, privacy, and consent constraints from ordinary creative exposure.
3. Generate three distance transformations using different levers: identity, world/species, time/place, or relationship role.
4. Preserve pressure, desire, contradiction, and consequence across all three.
5. Select the form that admits the most specific truth with the least self-explanation.
6. Produce a premise and one finished scene in that form.
7. Check that distance increased honesty rather than hiding the issue.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Fiction | Full speculative or identity transform |
| Memoir | Composite or framing device with disclosure clearly labeled |
| Essay | Third-person or object-mediated scene |
| Brand story | Metaphor may carry tension but cannot falsify facts |
| Sensitive material | Consent, safety, and privacy override craft ambition |

## Output Requirements

Emotional fact; safety boundary; three transformations; comparison; selected premise; finished scene; honesty check.

Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/oblique-truth-transformation.md` — honor its Output Contract.

## Quality Gate

- Is the emotional pressure preserved?
- Did the transformation produce more specific truth?
- Were privacy and consent treated as vetoes?
- Is the result distinct from cosmetic genre swapping?
- Does it avoid imitating *The Humans* or any Haig plot?
