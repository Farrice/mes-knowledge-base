# Structural Pull Edit

## Pre-Flight Gate

Require the marked passage plus enough surrounding material to inspect scene purpose, motive, sequence, and premise. Preserve the original. Read `../genius.md` Patterns 3, 4, and 5.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../references/rubric_v1.md` and the structural-pull prompt.

## Execution

1. Describe the visible symptom without prescribing a fix.
2. Test wording: is the sentence itself unclear or inert?
3. Test scene function: does the scene change pressure, knowledge, or choice?
4. Test motive: does the character want or fear something legible?
5. Test sequence: does this arrive too early, too late, or without setup?
6. Test premise: is the story asking the wrong dramatic question?
7. Repair the highest supported layer, then revise the local passage.
8. Compare against the protected original for access, surprise, rhythm, and charge.
9. Stop, continue, or revert.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Scene/chapter | Full causal ladder |
| Essay | Paragraph function → section logic → governing question |
| Post | Hook → turn → payoff, without inflating scope |
| Brand story | Claim → proof → sequence → positioning premise |
| Synopsis | Premise and causal sequence outrank sentence craft |

## Output Requirements

Symptom; causal ladder; root cause; structural patch; local patch; before/after comparison; stop decision.

Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/structural-pull-edit.md` — honor its Output Contract.

## Quality Gate

- Was every layer tested before choosing the cause?
- Did one higher-level repair resolve multiple symptoms?
- Is the local edit downstream of the structural change?
- Did charge survive?
- Is uncertainty named where surrounding material is insufficient?
