# Authentic Optimism

## Pre-Flight Gate

Require the passage or arc, the real damage or cost, and the current ending. Do not use this workflow to override factual, medical, grief, or safety reality. Read `../genius.md` Pattern 12.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../references/rubric_v1.md` and the authentic-hope prompt.

## Execution

1. Inventory the darkness: loss, uncertainty, damage, constraint, and what cannot be restored.
2. Mark false hope: reassurance, rescue, lesson, or neat closure unsupported by events.
3. Find one credible affordance: choice, relationship, endurance, responsibility, or next action.
4. Rebuild the arc so possibility emerges through behavior rather than commentary.
5. Preserve unresolved cost.
6. Write the finished passage or beat sequence.
7. Test whether hope changes action without claiming the pain was necessary or solved.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Fiction | Hope appears as consequential choice |
| Memoir | No retroactive meaning is imposed on pain |
| Essay | Possibility is evidence-bounded and specific |
| Brand/client | No healing, transformation, or outcome claim beyond proof |
| Short-form | One credible next possibility; no inspirational slogan |

## Output Requirements

Cost inventory; false-hope diagnosis; chosen affordance; revised arc/passage; unresolved truth; action test.

Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/authentic-hope-revision.md` — honor its Output Contract.

## Quality Gate

- Does darkness retain its full cost?
- Does hope emerge through action or relationship?
- Was neat closure resisted?
- Are sensitive claims bounded?
- Does the ending create possibility without reassurance theater?
