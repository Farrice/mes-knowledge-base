# Exemplars and Anti-Exemplar

## 1. Local Note → Structural Rebuild

An editor marks a small area; Haig searches for the larger cause and performs the bigger deletion or rebuild. Calibration: a good diagnosis reduces several symptoms with one causal intervention.

## 2. Personal Truth → Alien Mask

*The Humans* becomes deeply personal through a fantastical outsider. Calibration: changed form is valid when emotional specificity increases.

## 3. Adult Complexity → Magical Train

An overloaded adult AI/dystopian concept becomes a children’s-story engine for *The Midnight Train*. Calibration: simplification must restore action and image, not merely shorten the pitch.

## Anti-Exemplar — Trend-Compliant Prestige Draft

A current-market premise, literary-status language, and repeated smoothing produce a competent object with no protected necessity or odd life. It fails even when every sentence is polished.
