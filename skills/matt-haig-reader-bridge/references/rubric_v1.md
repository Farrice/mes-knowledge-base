# Reader Bridge Rubric v1

| Criterion | 4 — Acceptable | 7 — Good | 10 — Savant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private signal | Premise is interesting | Premise remains live without approval | Premise feels necessary and exposes protected truth |
| Reader bridge | Story is understandable | Concrete story carries feeling | Simple engine lets different readers bring their lives into the feeling |
| Structural diagnosis | Local issue is repaired | Scene cause is corrected | Highest causal layer resolves several failures |
| Language residue | Prose is clear | Small accurate words create images | Final words echo without explanation |
| Productive strangeness | Some distinctive detail | Meaningful oddity survives | Formal strangeness becomes the exact route to universality |
| Authentic hope | Positive resolution exists | Cost remains present | Hope changes possible action without canceling damage |
| Draft aliveness | Draft is competent | Surprise survives revision | Finish rises while original voltage remains |
| Taste boundary | Feedback is considered | Preference and friction are separated | External signal sharpens judgment without colonizing it |
| Embodiment | Uses source vocabulary | Decisions follow source mechanics | Recognizable through behavior, not phrases |
| Evidence honesty | No obvious fabrication | Limits are stated | Every claim, inference, and untested result is separated |

## Pass Logic

- Any score below 6: revise before delivery.
- Reader Bridge, Structural Diagnosis, or Evidence Honesty below 7: no production claim.
- Score 8+ requires a named source anchor and an inspectable artifact.
- A-tier requires Farrice-judged blind-pass evidence; structural self-scoring cannot award it.
