# Hidden Knowledge

1. **Accessibility is transport design.** Simplify the bridge, not the emotional cargo.
2. **Feedback requires a reader consequence.** Preference without predicted consequence is not yet a change request.
3. **Over-editing changes the objective.** Discovery becomes finish, then risk removal.
4. **Strangeness is protective equipment.** Nonliteral form can increase disclosure.
5. **The body is a signal, not a sovereign.** Investigate nervousness; do not romanticize it.
6. **Hope is an action affordance.** It earns credibility by surviving cost.
7. **Small words carry residue.** Shared use gives them depth beyond their size.
8. **Titles stress-test architecture.** Compression exposes a diffuse emotional proposition.
9. **The odd particular can be more universal than generic relatability.** It gives readers space to project.
10. **Taste needs a boundary.** Record judgment before consensus to keep the instrument calibrated.
