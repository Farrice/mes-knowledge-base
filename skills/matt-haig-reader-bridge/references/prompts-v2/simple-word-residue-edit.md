# Simple Word Residue Edit

## Role & Activation

Execute precision through small words and concrete residue. Remove abstraction, prestige performance, and emotional explanation only when accuracy and reader access improve. Protect specialized language, native cadence, and productive irregularity.

## Input Required

- `[PASSAGE]`
- `[INTENDED FEELING]`
- `[VOICE OWNER]`
- `[WORDS OR FACTS THAT MUST STAY]`
- `[FORMAT AND LENGTH]`

## Execution Protocol

1. Mark abstraction, status vocabulary, summary, and smooth-but-dead language.
2. Identify the concrete image, action, object, or consequence underneath.
3. Replace each marked phrase with the smallest accurate language; retain technical or cultural specificity where required.
4. Preserve irregular rhythm that carries the writer’s presence.
5. Produce the revised passage.
6. Generate image-residue, shared-word-residue, and unresolved-action endings.
7. Select the ending that echoes without explaining itself.

## Output Contract

Return: abstraction map; protected vocabulary; finished passage; three ending options; selected ending; residue explanation; evidence state.

## Output Skeleton

```markdown
# Simple Word Residue Edit
## Abstraction map
| Original | Concrete meaning | Action |
## Protected language
## Finished passage
## Ending options
1. Image residue
2. Shared-word residue
3. Unresolved-action residue
## Selected ending
## Evidence state
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Every shorter choice is also more accurate.
- [ ] Necessary technical, legal, or cultural language survives.
- [ ] The writer’s cadence remains recognizable.
- [ ] The final line does not summarize the feeling.
- [ ] No uniform smoothing has occurred.

## Creative Latitude

Small language does not require small thought. Keep the rare or long word when it is exact. Let one unexpected concrete detail carry more than an explanatory paragraph.

## Deploy When

Use for `/haig-simple-word-residue` when prose is abstract, over-literary, overexplained, or ending without echo.
