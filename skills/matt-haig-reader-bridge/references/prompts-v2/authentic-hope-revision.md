# Authentic Hope Revision

## Role & Activation

Execute hope that survives darkness. Preserve damage, uncertainty, and cost; remove reassurance theater; create one credible possibility through behavior, relationship, or choice.

## Input Required

- `[PASSAGE OR ARC]`
- `[REAL COST OR DAMAGE]`
- `[CURRENT ENDING]`
- `[FORMAT AND LENGTH]`
- `[FACTUAL OR SENSITIVITY LIMITS]`

## Execution Protocol

1. Inventory what was lost, damaged, constrained, and cannot be restored.
2. Mark every unsupported reassurance, rescue, lesson, and neat closure.
3. Choose one action affordance that the story has earned.
4. Rebuild the emotional sequence so possibility appears through conduct or relationship.
5. Keep unresolved cost visible.
6. Produce the finished revision.
7. State what action now feels possible and what remains unsolved.

## Output Contract

Return: cost inventory; false-hope diagnosis; selected affordance; revised arc or passage; unresolved truth; action-possibility test; claim boundary.

## Output Skeleton

```markdown
# Authentic Hope Revision
## Cost that remains
## False hope removed
## Credible affordance
## Revised arc / passage
## What remains unsolved
## Action now possible
## Claim boundary
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] No loss is retroactively justified.
- [ ] Hope emerges through behavior or relationship.
- [ ] The ending retains a real cost.
- [ ] Sensitive or outcome claims remain bounded.
- [ ] No inspirational slogan substitutes for story.

## Creative Latitude

Hope may be tiny, ambivalent, comic, relational, or unresolved. Choose the possibility the material earns, not the one that produces the neatest uplift.

## Deploy When

Use for `/haig-authentic-optimism` when the work is bleak, sentimental, falsely reassuring, or emotionally airless.
