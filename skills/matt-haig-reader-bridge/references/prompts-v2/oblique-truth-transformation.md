# Oblique Truth Transformation

## Role & Activation

Execute protective distance as a truth technology. Change the mask—identity, world, species, age, place, time, or relationship role—while keeping emotional pressure intact. Privacy, consent, and factual integrity retain veto power.

## Input Required

- `[EMOTIONAL FACT]`
- `[LITERAL VERSION]`
- `[WHAT FEELS GUARDED]`
- `[PRIVACY AND CONSENT LIMITS]`
- `[FORMAT AND LENGTH]`

## Execution Protocol

1. Separate the emotional fact from literal biography.
2. Distinguish real danger/privacy constraints from ordinary exposure.
3. Build three materially different transformations using different distance levers.
4. Carry the same desire, contradiction, consequence, and pressure through each.
5. Compare which form admits the most specific truth with the least self-explanation.
6. Select one and produce a premise plus finished scene.
7. Verify that distance increased honesty rather than merely decorating the plot.

## Output Contract

Return: emotional fact; safety boundary; three transformations; comparison table; selected premise; finished scene; preserved-pressure ledger; honesty verdict.

```markdown

## Emotional fact
## Safety boundary
## Three masks
| Mask | Changed surface | Preserved pressure | New access | Risk |
## Selection

```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] All three options change more than cosmetic genre details.
- [ ] The emotional pressure remains constant and inspectable.
- [ ] Privacy and consent constraints are honored.
- [ ] The selected form produces more specificity, not less.
- [ ] No Haig plot, character, phrase, or voice is imitated.

## Creative Latitude

Use unexpected formal distance if it creates safer, sharper disclosure. The transformation may be subtle or fantastical. Its value is measured by truth admitted, not novelty displayed.

## Deploy When

Use for `/haig-oblique-truth` when literal telling is guarded, overexplained, or unsafe.
