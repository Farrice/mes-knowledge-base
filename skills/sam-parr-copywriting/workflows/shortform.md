# /parr-shortform — The Short-Form Video Script Engine

Write short-form video scripts (Reels, TikTok, Shorts, LinkedIn video) by translating classic direct-response into modern attention mechanics. **This is the roster gap** — nobody else owns classic-DR-meets-short-form-video. Sam's onion-dad video: raw, 4M views, 80% 3-sec stop rate.

## Pre-Flight Gate
Load `genius.md` (Patterns 3, 5, 15; the retention benchmarks; the guardrail). Confirm:
- The topic and the ONE behavior (follow, click, buy, share).
- Platform (Reels/TikTok/Shorts/LinkedIn) and target length (default 45-60s).
- The frame-breaking premise: what surprising truth defies the category's expectations?
- Any factual claim → verification queue.

## Execution
1. **Frame-breaking premise.** Lead with the surprising truth (the $10M onion business that's one guy + two phones). The premise IS the retention engine.
2. **The 3-second hook.** Punch line one — spoken, visual, fourth-wall break. Target ≥50% stop rate (80% = elite). Pair words with what's ON SCREEN.
3. **Open-loop spine.** Map the script to ~8-second beats. Each beat ends on a fresh open loop so the next 8s pays off the last and opens a new one. Stack loops; never let tension fully resolve until the end.
4. **Retention-graph design.** Pre-empt the known drop-off points (3s, 12s, 30s) with a re-hook: a new curiosity gap, a pattern interrupt, or a tangible reveal (`/parr-tangible`).
5. **Rhythm for the ear.** Spoken cadence — short, punchy, varied. Read it aloud; cut a third (`/parr-rhythm-edit`).
6. **Quick CTA.** One specific action at the end; don't over-explain.
7. **Output the shot/text notes.** For each beat: spoken line + on-screen text/B-roll cue + the loop it opens.

## Quality Gate (from genius.md)
- **Curiosity gap / open loops** ≥8: a fresh loop ~every 8s mapped to drop-off.
- **Hook punch** ≥8: line one engineered for ≥50% 3-sec stop.
- Auto-fail: no frame-break, slow warm-up open, loops resolve too early, unverified stat.
