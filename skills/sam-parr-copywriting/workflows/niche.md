# /parr-niche — The Niche Call-Out Engine

Win the click by naming the exact reader. Sam Parr: "Niches make riches." "50 reasons you know you're from Denver" → 30% of Denverites click. Homesick candles sold the smell of your home state. Specificity of WHO beats breadth of how-many.

## Pre-Flight Gate
Load `genius.md` (Pattern 7). Confirm:
- The product and its highest-intent audience segment(s).
- Available targeting dimensions: identity, geography, income, role, life-stage, behavior, belief.
- **Note**: for full awareness-ladder targeting or paid-media funnel structure → defer to **Luke Iha / Sabri Suby**. This is the call-out *headline*, not the funnel.

## Skill Acquisition
- `genius.md` — Niches Make Riches (7).
- `references/swipe-file-and-frameworks.md` — BuzzFeed, Delta, Homesick Candles examples.

## Execution
1. **Pick the call-out axis.** Which single dimension most sharply identifies the buyer? (Income: "If you make over $3M/year…" / Identity: "For the founder who…" / Geography / Life-stage / Belief.)
2. **Name them so precisely it feels like eavesdropping.** The reader should think "that's literally me." Use the income figure, the role, the specific situation.
3. **Layer the expansion play** (the Homesick move): can the same call-out also capture adjacents? ("from Washington" → also "moved away from Washington, buy it for your mom"). Note secondary audiences.
4. **Generate 6-10 call-out headlines** across axes — vary the dimension, not just the words.
5. **Pair with a curiosity gap.** The best call-outs combine WHO + an open loop ("If you make over $3M/year, you've felt this exact thing — and it's not what you think").
6. **Pick the top 3** with the segment + estimated resonance noted.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Format | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Ad headline | One axis, razor-specific; pair with the gap |
| Email subject | Identity call-out + curiosity |
| Landing H1 | Name the reader; subhead begins the slippery slope |
| Community/high-ticket | Income/role qualification doubles as a filter (Hampton) |
| Newsletter growth | "X reasons you know you're a [identity]" listicle hook |

## Output Requirements
- 6-10 call-out headlines grouped by targeting axis.
- Top 3 with segment + expansion-play note.
- Each paired with or noting a curiosity gap.

## Quality Gate (from genius.md)
- **Targeting specificity** ≥8: the exact reader called out by identity/income/situation — feels like eavesdropping.
- Auto-fail: "anyone who…" breadth; generic demographic with no identity hook.
