# /parr-shortform-to-ad — Organic Winner → Paid Ad

Turn a short-form video that already won organically into a paid ad. Sam Parr's exact move: his onion-dad video hit 4M views organically, then he wrapped it into one of Hampton's best-performing ads — *"This video got 3 million views. If you're a business owner, you're going to understand why. Watch this…"*

## Pre-Flight Gate
Load `genius.md` (Patterns 8, 15; the onion-video exemplar). Confirm:
- The winning organic video (link/transcript) + its metrics (views, stop rate, retention).
- The product/offer to attach (and the action).
- Why it won organically — the frame-breaking premise that made it spread.
- Verification queue for any claim added in the ad wrapper.

## Skill Acquisition
- `genius.md` — Story = hero/guide (8), Short-Form Retention (15), the onion-video atomization.
- **Stacks**: `/parr-shortform` (the source script logic), **Lara Acosta** (platform hook), `/parr-story-ad` (the wrapper narrative).

## Execution
1. **Diagnose the organic win.** Name the frame-breaking premise and the retention mechanic that made it spread. That's the asset — don't dilute it.
2. **Build the ad wrapper.** Front-load social proof FROM the organic performance: "This got [X] views. Here's why it matters to YOU." Use the view count as the hook (proof + curiosity gap).
3. **Bridge to the offer (the guide turn).** Connect the video's premise to the product as the guide: "My post went viral because it was frame-breaking. People realize there are hundreds of ways to build a company. That's why founders get value from [Hampton]…" Customer = hero, product = guide.
4. **Keep the raw asset intact.** Don't re-edit the organic video to look "produced" — its rawness is why it converts. Wrap it, don't polish it.
5. **Quick CTA.** One action, fast (Sam: "some quick CTA too").
6. **Whitelist/format note.** Note whether to run as-is from the creator's account (whitelisting) or as a branded ad.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Source | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Founder/personal video | Lean into the personal story; product as the lesson learned |
| Customer/UGC win | Social-proof wrapper; "real customer, real result" |
| Educational reel | Wrap with "save this / here's the deeper version inside [offer]" |
| Parallax video clip | Bridge the essay's premise to the newsletter/offer; keep interiority |
| Talking-head | Restaurant-owner energy; view-count hook |

## Output Requirements
- The ad wrapper script (hook using the view count → bridge → CTA).
- A note on what NOT to change (the raw asset that won).
- Whitelisting vs. branded recommendation.
- Verification Queue for any added claim.

## Quality Gate (from genius.md)
- **Story structure** ≥7: the bridge makes the customer the hero, product the guide.
- The organic asset's frame-break is preserved, not diluted.
- Auto-fail: over-produced re-edit that kills the rawness; product leads instead of the proof/premise.
