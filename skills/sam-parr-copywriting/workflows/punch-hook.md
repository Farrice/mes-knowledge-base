# /parr-punch-hook — The Hook & Headline Engine

Produce headlines and opening lines that grab the collar. Sam Parr: "The first sentence needs to punch you." Headlines get 80% of the spend (5× more read the headline than the body) — so this is where the leverage is.

## Pre-Flight Gate
Load `skills/sam-parr-copywriting/genius.md` (Patterns 3, 5, 7; the guardrail). Confirm:
- What's the ONE behavior the reader should take? (Pattern 1 — engineer backward from it.)
- Who is the EXACT reader? (Pattern 7 — name them.)
- Format: headline / email subject / ad opener / reel hook / landing H1?
- Any factual claim in the hook → flag for verification (guardrail).

## Skill Acquisition
- `genius.md` — Mind the Gap (3), Punch in the Face (5), Niches Make Riches (7).
- `references/swipe-file-and-frameworks.md` — classic hooks + phrase frameworks to steal-and-twist.

## Execution
1. **Define the punch type.** Pick the strongest fit: (a) fourth-wall break ("You're reading this because I want you to"), (b) bold/contrarian claim, (c) visceral physical image (Tangibility — see `/parr-tangible`), (d) niche call-out ("If you make over $3M/year…"), (e) curiosity gap ("They laughed when I sat down at the piano…").
2. **Open the loop.** The hook must create a question the reader needs answered. Withhold the resolution. State the tension, not the payoff.
3. **Steal & twist.** Pull a proven skeleton from the swipe file; twist ~3% to the brand/reader.
4. **Generate a ladder of variants.** Produce 8-12 hooks across the punch types — not minor rewordings. Vary the mechanic, not just the words.
5. **Stress-test on the stranger test.** For each: would a cold reader stop on this line alone, with zero context? Cut any that need warm-up.
6. **Pick the top 3** and say why each works (which pattern, what gap it opens).

## Content Type Adaptations
| Format | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Email subject | ≤9 words; curiosity gap or niche call-out; never describe the body |
| Reel/Short hook | Spoken cadence; pair with a frame-breaking premise; must land in <3s |
| Landing H1 | Pair the punch with a one-line subhead that begins the slippery slope |
| Cold DM/outreach | Restaurant-owner energy — talk like you already know them; time-constraint snap |
| LinkedIn (Parallax) | Punch + interiority; defer to Lara Acosta's 8-word rehook for platform-native formatting |

## Output Requirements
- 8-12 hook variants grouped by punch type.
- Top-3 picks with a one-line rationale each (pattern + gap).
- Any factual claim labeled VERIFIED / LIKELY / UNCONFIRMED.

## Quality Gate (from genius.md)
- **Hook punch** ≥7: stops a stranger cold on line one.
- **Curiosity gap** ≥7: a clear, resolvable gap opened.
- Auto-fail: warm-up opener, vague "anyone who…" targeting, unverified stat in the hook.
