# /parr-objection — The Objection-Handling Engine

Disarm the reader's doubts before they can raise them. Sam Parr's model is the 8 Mile freestyle: Eminem names every insult about himself first, so the opponent has nothing left. Then defuse the biggest objection with a concrete anecdote, not a claim.

## Pre-Flight Gate
Load `genius.md` (Pattern 14). Confirm:
- The product/offer and the reader.
- Known objections (price, trust, "not for me," "won't work," timing). If unknown, infer the top 3 from the reader's state.
- Any proof claim → verification queue.

## Skill Acquisition
- `genius.md` — Objection Handling / 8 Mile (14); the indirect-anecdote move ("I wore them in the shower").
- For deep voice-of-customer objection mining → defer to **Joanna Wiebe**.

## Execution
1. **List the top 3-5 objections** in the reader's own words ("This is too expensive." "Who even is this person?" "I've tried things like this." "I don't need it right now.").
2. **8 Mile the biggest one.** Voice it out loud, first, before the reader does: "I know what you're thinking — who is this guy, why should you trust him?" Owning it removes its power.
3. **Defuse with anecdote, not claim.** Don't assert "it's durable" — tell the story that proves it indirectly ("I accidentally wore them in the shower"). Show, don't claim.
4. **Reframe price as value/experience** where relevant ("people who buy experiences are happier — and this one happens to save you money").
5. **FAQ container.** For objections that don't fit the narrative flow, write 3-5 FAQ Q&As — the FAQ is where objections go to die without breaking the story.
6. **Place them.** Map each handled objection to where it goes (early for trust, mid for "won't work," late/FAQ for logistics).

## Content Type Adaptations
| Format | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Sales page | 8 Mile the top objection in-copy; FAQ block near the CTA |
| Ad | One objection, defused via anecdote, woven into the story |
| Email sequence | One objection per email across the sequence |
| Video script | Voice the objection to camera ("I know what you're thinking…") |
| High-ticket (Hampton-style) | Trust + "am I qualified" objections first; price as the bonus |

## Output Requirements
- Ranked objection list (reader's words).
- The top objection handled 8 Mile-style (in-copy).
- 2-3 anecdote-based defusals for the rest.
- An FAQ block (3-5 Q&As).
- Placement map. Verification Queue for proof claims.

## Quality Gate (from genius.md)
- **Objection handling** ≥8: top objections voiced before the reader, defused via anecdote/FAQ.
- Auto-fail: objections ignored or only countered with bare claims; unverified proof.
