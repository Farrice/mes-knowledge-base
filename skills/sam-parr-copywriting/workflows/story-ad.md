# /parr-story-ad — The Story Ad Engine

Produce a complete direct-response story ad. Sam Parr: "Story, story, story, story. There's no such thing as too long, just too boring." The customer is the hero; the brand is the guide. Build desire FIRST, reveal the product LAST.

## Pre-Flight Gate
Load `genius.md` (Patterns 2, 8, 12; the guardrail). Confirm:
- The product/offer and the single action (buy, subscribe, book).
- The EXACT reader (Pattern 7) and their current state / pain.
- The transformation: who do they become after the product?
- Length latitude: long-form is fine (long converts) — the only enemy is boring.
- **Every factual claim → verification queue (guardrail). Sam improvises fake stats to show structure; we ship verified ones.**

## Skill Acquisition
- `genius.md` — AIDA spine (2), Story = hero's journey (8), Bury the Product + absolve guilt (12), Slippery Slope (4).
- `references/swipe-file-and-frameworks.md` — WSJ "Tale of Two Boys" exemplar.
- If full VSL needed → hand off to **Luke Iha**. If pure brand story → **Donald Miller (StoryBrand)**.

## Execution
1. **Punch hook (Attention).** Open the loop. Often: a relatable moment of the hero stuck in their problem. (Use `/parr-punch-hook`.)
2. **Name the problem (Interest).** Make the reader feel seen. Plant an early YES — a head-nod line they can't disagree with (Pattern 4). Specificity = recognition.
3. **The failed-solution / absolve-guilt beat.** "You tried X — it didn't work. That's not your fault. You were missing [mechanism]." Removes shame, opens them to the new way.
4. **Build desire (Desire).** Tell the story: hero lost → encounters the guide (the product as mechanism) → overcomes → returns transformed. Tension in the middle. Make the mechanism tangible (use `/parr-tangible`). **Bury the product here as the guide — do NOT lead with it.**
5. **Proof.** Stack the strongest proof: before/after, social proof ("trusted by X just like you"), specific results. (Proof > claims.)
6. **Handle the top objection** (use `/parr-objection` — the 8 Mile move or an anecdote).
7. **The ask (Action).** One clear action, framed as the obvious next step in the hero's journey. Quick, specific CTA.
8. **Rhythm + cut a third.** Run `/parr-rhythm-edit` — vary sentence length, 7th-grade level, delete 33%.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Format | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Meta/paid social ad | Front-load the punch + early yes; mobile rhythm (short paragraphs); CTA in first + last |
| Long-form sales page | Full slippery slope; section subheads as mini-hooks; proof blocks throughout |
| Email | One story per email; the P.S. restates the open loop |
| VSL script | Hand to **Luke Iha** for VSL architecture; bring this story as the narrative spine |
| Newsletter (Parallax) | Memoir-grade interiority over hard sell; brand-as-guide stays subtle |

## Output Requirements
- A complete, deployable story ad mapped to AIDA beats (label each beat).
- The product buried past the desire build (never in the first third).
- A **Verification Queue**: every stat/claim listed with VERIFIED / LIKELY / UNCONFIRMED. Unverified claims flagged, not shipped.

## Quality Gate (from genius.md)
- **Desire-before-offer** ≥8: product fully buried; reader wants it before knowing what it is.
- **Story structure** ≥8: full hero's-journey with tension; customer=hero, brand=guide.
- **Objection handling** ≥7. **Rhythm** ≥7.
- Auto-fail: product in the first third, no story, fabricated unverified stat, complete-but-boring.
