# /parr-tangible — The Tangibility Engine

Convert any dry claim, stat, or benefit into a physical object the reader can SEE. Sam Parr: "Imagine storing your food in the toilet bowl." A brick of fat = movie popcorn. The deconstructed Coke bottle = 40% sugar. Abstraction doesn't convert; images do.

## Pre-Flight Gate
Load `genius.md` (Pattern 11; the Caraway exemplar; the guardrail). Confirm:
- The claim/stat/benefit to make tangible.
- Whether it's true → **if it's a real stat, verify it before deploying the image (guardrail).** If illustrative, label it clearly.

## Skill Acquisition
- `genius.md` — Tangibility Engine (11), Caraway/Tupperware exemplar.
- `references/swipe-file-and-frameworks.md` — the visceral-comparison framework.
- Stacks with **Harry Dry** (zoom-in) for precision.

## Execution
1. **Strip the claim to its core number/benefit.** ("Less bacteria." "150g of fat." "40% sugar.")
2. **Find the visceral anchor.** Ask: what disgusting, vivid, or surprising physical object makes this real? Reach for the body, the home, food, money — things the reader touches daily. Disgust and surprise out-convert neutral.
3. **Write the comparison as an image, not a number.** "More E. coli than your toilet bowl." "A brick of fat the size of your fist." Lead with the image; the stat supports it.
4. **Punch it into the first sentence** where possible (pairs with `/parr-punch-hook`).
5. **Generate 3-5 tangibility options** at different intensities (visceral → surprising → elegant) so the brand can pick the right register.
6. **Visual companion (optional).** If a literal image would amplify it (deconstructed product, before/after object), note it for `/parr-shortform` or a poster via `fantastic-posters`.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Use | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Ad headline | Lead with the most visceral image; one line |
| Reel/video | Show the physical object on camera (brick of fat, the Coke pour) |
| Landing page | Image + caption near the proof block |
| B2B/finance | Dial to "surprising" not "disgusting" — money/time comparisons (e.g., "that's a Tesla every year in fees") |
| Health/CPG | Full visceral register works; verify the underlying claim first |

## Output Requirements
- 3-5 tangibility options, ranked by intensity, with the register noted.
- The top pick written as a deployable opening line.
- Underlying stat labeled VERIFIED / LIKELY / UNCONFIRMED, or marked "illustrative."

## Quality Gate (from genius.md)
- **Tangibility** ≥8: the reader can physically picture it; it's unforgettable.
- Auto-fail: still an abstract benefit; a "real" stat used without verification.
