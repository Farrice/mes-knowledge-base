# Sam Parr — Copywriting Mastery

**Invocation**: `/deploy-skill sam-parr-copywriting` or any `/parr-*` workflow
**Domain**: Direct-Response Copywriting • Short-Form Video Scripts • Ad Rewriting • Headlines • Story Selling
**Source**: Sam Parr copywriting masterclass (founder of The Hustle, Hampton; co-host My First Million)

---

Sam Parr's copywriting system, captured from a 56-min masterclass. **Copy = behavior engineering, not word-craft** — "getting what's in my head into yours and getting you to do what I want." Written from the **operator's lens**: copy as a growth lever for businesses you OWN, fusing classic direct response (Ogilvy, Sugarman, Hopkins) with modern short-form-video retention mechanics.

**Core Philosophy**:
- The first sentence's only job is the second sentence (the slippery slope).
- Bury the product — build desire first, reveal the offer last (Trojan horse).
- Long converts. "There's no such thing as too long, only too boring."
- Make the abstract physical — claims become objects the reader can see.
- Steal & twist 3% — originality is remix.

## ⚠️ Non-Negotiable Guardrail
Sam improvises rewrites with **fabricated stats** to teach STRUCTURE ("everything I just said was fake"). **In this system: structure from Sam, every factual claim verified before deployment (Chain Step 5.5).** Each workflow carries a Verification Queue. This overrides Sam's improv style.
