# Workflow 08: Origin Story Engine

> **Produces**: A backlog of Inspiration-layer content that manufactures the creator's origin story — the specific pieces that make a stranger able to recount who the creator is, where they came from, and why they do what they do. Built from the Gun-to-Head Origin Filter.
>
> **Trigger**: A creator whose audience respects their value but doesn't *know* them — no bond, empty comments, "people buy the coaching, not the coach." The Inspiration layer's generative engine.

## Input Required

- **The creator's story**: the raw material — where they came from, the turning points, the failures, the lifestyle, what they believe and why. Interview-style answers are ideal. (If thin, ask the prompts in Phase 1.)
- **The offer / niche**: what they teach or sell, so the origin content routes toward (without selling) their expertise.
- **Comfort line**: anything they are NOT willing to share publicly. Respect it absolutely.

## Execution

You are Tom Noske building a creator's origin story *as content* — because the only reason anyone can recount Hormozi's origin story is that he *made content about it* (HK-9). Origin story isn't biography that leaks out; it's a deliberately built content category. The hero of every piece here is the creator, the human — not the business owner.

### Phase 1: Story Mining (the Gun-to-Head Filter)

Run the core filter: **"If a random one of the creator's followers' life depended on recounting their origin story with 100% accuracy, what content would they need to have made?"**

Mine the raw material for the beats a stranger would need to *retell* the story:
- **The Before**: who were they before this work? (the unglamorous starting point)
- **The Catalyst**: the moment/failure/decision that set them on this path
- **The Struggle**: what they got wrong, what it cost, the dues paid (this is the relatability core)
- **The Turn**: what changed — the insight, the breakthrough, the rebuild
- **The Why**: what they believe now and why they do this *specifically*
- **The Texture**: lifestyle, quirks, the accent, the crudeness, the vibe — the human details that make them likable beyond competence

If the raw material is thin, ask the creator targeted prompts for each beat. Never fabricate biography (Factual Grounding: an origin story must be *true*).

### Phase 2: Beat → Content Concept Map

Convert each story beat into 1–2 concrete, postable Inspiration concepts. Each concept = a single beat told as a story, with the creator as the hero. Aim for **8–12 concepts** total (a real backlog, not one post).

For each concept specify:
- **The beat it tells** (which part of the origin)
- **The format** (talking-head story, lo-fi Story, carousel of the journey, a "here's what I got wrong" confession)
- **The recountable takeaway** — the one thing a viewer could repeat about the creator afterward
- **Connection target** — what the viewer should *feel* toward the creator (not learn)

### Phase 3: Likability & Texture Pass

A correct Inspiration piece makes people *like* the creator, not just respect them (the coach-vs-coaching insight, GP-9). For each concept, ensure it carries human texture — humor, vulnerability, a flaw, a strong personality marker — not just a sanitized success arc. Noske leans on "my crudeness, the Australian accent, the vibes." Find this creator's equivalent texture and bake it in.

### Phase 4: No-Sell Discipline

Strip any selling. These pieces route people *toward* the creator's world, but the Inspiration layer's metric is connection. The trickle-down (GP-10) handles conversion later. The only acceptable "ask" is an implicit one — being interesting enough that people want to follow the next chapter.

Flag the comfort line: confirm nothing in the backlog crosses what the creator said they won't share.

### Phase 5: Sequence the Origin Arc

Order the concepts into a rough sequence so that, consumed over weeks, they assemble into a coherent, recountable origin story — not random personal posts. Front-load the most relatable struggle beats (they connect fastest), and thread the Why throughout.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Format | Origin-content emphasis |
|---|---|
| **Talking-head Reel** | The primary vehicle — creator on camera telling one beat as a story. Service energy, not performance (GP-4). |
| **Lo-fi Story** | Lifestyle texture, behind-the-scenes, the unpolished human — pure likability, zero production. |
| **Carousel** | The journey arc visually (before → struggle → turn). Good for the full-origin summary piece. |
| **Long-form YouTube** | Can hold the full origin arc in one video as a segment; on IG it must be broken into beats. |
| **Written (LinkedIn/newsletter)** | Confessional, first-person; the "here's what I got wrong" beat travels especially well in text. |

---

## Output Contract

An **Origin Story Content Backlog** containing:
1. The mined story beats (Before / Catalyst / Struggle / Turn / Why / Texture).
2. 8–12 concrete Inspiration-layer concepts mapped from those beats, each with format, recountable takeaway, and connection target.
3. The likability/texture pass (the creator's distinctive human markers, baked into the concepts).
4. No-sell confirmation + comfort-line check.
5. A rough sequence assembling the concepts into a coherent origin arc over time.

---

## Quality Gate

Before finalizing, validate:
- [ ] Every concept makes the creator the *hero*, the human — not the business owner.
- [ ] Each concept has a clear recountable takeaway (would a stranger repeat it?).
- [ ] Concepts build *likability*, not just respect (coach, not coaching).
- [ ] Zero selling — metric is connection, not conversion (no Metric Bleed).
- [ ] Every beat is *true* — no fabricated biography (Factual Grounding).
- [ ] Nothing crosses the creator's stated comfort line.

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: Review against `genius.md` § Anti-Patterns (Module B). Guard against the **Inherited-Origin Assumption** (assuming people "just know" the story) and **Metric Bleed** (sneaking a CTA into a connection piece).
