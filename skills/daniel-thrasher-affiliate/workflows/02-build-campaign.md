# Build the Campaign

## Role

You are building the middle of Thrasher's affiliate funnel: copy that sells the click (skill #2), a bridge page that is the campaign's only owned asset (skill #3), and a single traffic channel commitment (skill #4). The discipline throughout: one action per funnel stage. The copy does not sell the product — the seller's pitch page does that. Every element you produce earns exactly the next click.

## Input Required

1. **Validated offer** — output of `01-select-offer.md` or equivalent: product, network, target avatar, pain points
2. **Seller assets** — the offer's affiliate tools page and sales page content (pain points, benefits, declared target audience, approved creatives)
3. **Voice-of-customer material** — forum threads, community posts, or reviews of this or similar products (real audience language)
4. **Operator's channel skills** — which traffic channels they have genuine experience with
5. **Page-building setup** — landing page tool available (builder, HTML/CSS, etc.)

## Workflow

### Phase 1 — Pain-to-Promise Copy Bank

1. Harvest pain points: seller's affiliate page and sales page first (it's pre-done research), then forums/communities/reviews for the audience's own words. List 5+ pain points (e.g., for a diet offer: low energy, brain fog, high blood sugar, slow metabolism).
2. Map each pain to the offer's corresponding promise/benefit — the transformation, stated concretely.
3. Choose the lead angle: the most universal or most motivating benefit for the specific avatar the seller identified.
4. Write 5-10 headline variants for the lead angle using Thrasher's two formulas as starting points:
   - **[Result] in [timeframe] without [objection]** — "Reclaim your youthful energy in 30 days without hating the food you eat"
   - **Do [this], not [that] / [mistake to fix]** — "Eat healthy Mediterranean foods — no need to starve yourself"
5. Test each headline against the "feel seen" bar: does it demonstrate you understand the pain as well as or better than the reader does? If they believe that, they'll trust you have the solution.
6. Define the single action for each funnel stage: traffic source (click the ad/link), bridge page (click the CTA), then the seller's page takes over.

### Phase 2 — Bridge Page Spec

Build to the five-element minimum — nothing else:
1. **Headline + subhead** — from the variant bank; big, bold, promise or curiosity hook
2. **Body copy** — brief; extends the pain-to-promise line, sells the click-through only
3. **Hero image** — congruent with the angle and avatar
4. **CTA button** — one, with the affiliate tracking link
5. **Constraints**: no navigation menu, no sidebar, no popups, no other distractions; everything visible above the fold; designed mobile-first

Then enforce **message match**: the page must feel continuous with the traffic source topically AND visually — if the ad tells a personal energy story, the page reflects that same story. Read ad and page back-to-back as one journey.

### Phase 3 — Traffic Channel Commitment

1. List channels where (a) the operator has real experience and (b) the target avatar is demonstrably present. Check the seller's tools page for channels they say work.
2. Commit to ONE primary channel — the intersection of skill and audience. Mastery means maximum qualified traffic at lowest cost, and it takes sustained focus on the same platform, tactic, and audience.
3. Optionally name one secondary channel to layer in ONLY after the primary plateaus (e.g., SEO blog now, Pinterest later).
4. Specify the first concrete traffic play on the primary channel (first ad set, first content cluster, first pin board — sized to the channel).

## Output Contract

- **Copy bank**: pain-point list with sources, pain→promise map, lead angle, 5-10 headline variants, and the one-action-per-stage funnel definition
- **Bridge page spec**: all five elements written out (headline, subhead, body copy, hero image direction, CTA text), constraint checklist confirmed, message-match notes
- **Channel commitment**: primary channel with skill+audience rationale, deferred secondary, and the first traffic play
- All in the avatar's language, not marketer jargon

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Every headline traces to a documented pain point in real audience or seller language
- [ ] At least 5 headline variants exist before one is recommended
- [ ] Bridge page contains exactly one action and zero distraction elements (no nav/sidebar/popups)
- [ ] Message match verified: ad and page tell the same story topically and visually
- [ ] Exactly one primary traffic channel committed, justified by operator skill AND audience presence
- [ ] No element of the copy tries to close the sale — every stage sells only the next click
