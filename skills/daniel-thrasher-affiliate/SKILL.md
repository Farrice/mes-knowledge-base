# Daniel Thrasher — Affiliate Campaign Skills

Daniel Thrasher runs affiliate education at ClickBank and has spent five years watching what actually separates top affiliates from the rest: skill, acquired in the right order. His core claim is that success in affiliate marketing is inevitable if you can execute every step of a campaign well — so the leverage is a priority-ranked skill ladder, not tactics. This skill covers the full campaign stack: three-filter offer selection (demand, competition, competency), sell-the-click copywriting, bridge page building with CRO, single-channel traffic mastery, tracking-ID analytics, email as a second-act channel, and AI/automation as amplifiers rather than substitutes.

## Quick Reference

- **Genius Context**: [genius.md](genius.md) — load before any workflow
- **The ladder (in order)**: 1) Offer selection → 2) Copywriting → 3) Bridge page + CRO → 4) Traffic channel mastery → 5) Analytics → 6) Email marketing → 7) AI + automation. One skill at a time; each applied to a live campaign before moving on.
- **Core reframe**: The affiliate's job is not selling the product (that's the seller's pitch page). The affiliate matches a pain point to a credible promise for one specific person, and sells the click — one action per funnel stage.
- **Lane boundary**: Campaign/funnel model (traffic source → bridge page → seller sales page). For hobby-niche blog/SEO authority sites, that is Adam Enfroy's lane (`skills/adam-enfroy-affiliate-marketing/`).
