## The 12 Patterns (compressed — dossier has full versions)

1. **Categorize Before You Optimize** — first order of business in any account: tag every dollar awareness / consideration / conversion / retention. Imbalance is visible before performance data is.
2. **Signals Over Perfection** — "I don't need perfect, I need signals." Chase the right confidence level and "by the time you get it, it's over." Decision latency in days.
3. **Fake-ROAS Detection** — platform ROAS is contaminated: the $3 Meta claims "would have happened without the ad as well." Never rank spend by platform ROAS alone.
4. **ROAS × New-to-Brand Cross** — the poor man's incrementality: high ROAS + high NTB% ≈ probably incremental. Triage all spend through this before any test infra exists (a 2-year MMM "will never get me where I need to be").
5. **Geo Holdout Fundamentals** — spend in NY, go dark in matched LA, read the retail sales delta. Experts will scoff ("what is he talking about?"); it's the fundamentals, and it works at small budgets.
6. **Incremental ROAS North Star** — models now return incrementality in weeks; rank budget by iROAS, re-derive quarterly. (fidelity: low — destination named, stack not disclosed.)
7. **Cost-per-PDP-View Chain** — CTR misleads: CTR × CPM = CPC → click-to-PDP drop-off (leaving-site popups, loops) → **cost per product page view** is the mid-funnel price. Test each lever separately.
8. **Home-Run-Only Testing** — only run tests that could move a metric ≥20%. Big effects reach significance fast: "four days in and still not conclusive — the outcome will never be big. Just move on." 5% wins are for share-defending incumbents.
9. **Channels Are Lever Sets** — never "where are my customers?" (everywhere); ask "what lever does this platform surrender?" Awareness needs **frequency control** → social is NOT awareness, period (algo feeds 1–1.5x/week inside an infinite feed; nobody remembers you); CTV/radio/podcast are. Consideration needs click-out optimization + retargeting → Meta qualifies there. Conversion lives where people convert (amazon.com, walmart.com). "If they don't let me control it, I won't touch it."
10. **One Job Per Creative** — "you cannot have everything in a single creative." Awareness unit's only job: remember the name (Liquid Death's lane: comedy — hard lane, memorable). Consideration: link the remembered name → benefits → where to buy. A funny ad that also sells benefits and retailer "is not going to happen."
11. **Message Laddering by Buyer State** — retarget with a NEW angle ("if they clicked and didn't convert — why?"); lapsed one-time buyers get yet another. States: never-seen → seen-not-clicked → clicked-not-bought → bought-once-lapsed → repeat. No state sees a repeated default.
12. **The Golden Core** — THE metric destined to fail you: CAC:LTV chased from day one. Every product has a fixed-size core of fast converters ($500K–$3M revenue); past it CAC inverts and the marketer says "the Meta algorithm is not working anymore." No — everyone outside the core needs the full funnel. Conversion-only spend is "counterintuitively false — you'd get a lot of that revenue anyway."

## Hidden Knowledge (tacit — the apprentice layer)

- **The funnel never collapsed** — "people trying to sound cool" say it did. Aware→consider→buy always exists; only its speed varies per person.
- **Meta sees one fragment** — the deep reason to refuse algo-picked creative: the platform optimizes its fragment and "completely distracts my full funnel." Creative-to-stage consistency > per-impression optimization.
- **Big-brand measurement is anti-startup** — adopting MMM/95%-confidence rituals at a growth brand is a category error, not diligence.
- **Investor pressure manufactures the failure** — "investors are good investors, not good marketers"; CAC interrogation pushes founders into golden-core strip-mining.
- **The system is tedious; the shoots are a rounding error** — "the collision point of data and taste." System that marches daily > campaign that launches.
- **Control-comfort bias** — "where you get the most scale is mostly where you get the least amount of control." Pick first steps by where you want to end, not by what you control.
- **Platform incentives are conditionally aligned** — use each platform only where your incentives and theirs coincide.
- **Frequency is the physics of awareness** — memory needs several exposures/week; 1–1.5x/week in an infinite feed can't create it at any budget. Arithmetic, not opinion.

## Decision Framework (pre-flight for every workflow)

1. What funnel stage is this dollar/creative/channel serving? (No stage = stop.)
2. What's the fastest honest signal available? (Signal > perfection.)
3. Could this test/change move the metric ≥20%? (No = don't run it.)
4. What lever must I control here, and does the platform surrender it?
5. Is this the golden core talking, or real incremental growth?
