# Lulu Cheng Meservey — Cross-Domain Patterns

> How the Go Direct genius transfers OUT of founder-PR into the domains this
> repo actually ships in. The genius is domain-agnostic: it operates on
> **belief, attention, and authority**, not on startup press releases. This
> file is the concrete before→after layer beneath the summary table in
> `genius.md § Cross-Domain Applications` — read that table for the one-line
> map, read this for the worked examples.

## The one nerve that carries across every domain

Every transfer below survives or dies on the same test from `genius.md`:
**would Lulu recognize this as someone speaking with conviction from a real
position — or as someone running a play?** The patterns are not domain-bound;
the *failure* is not domain-bound either. A LinkedIn post, a landing page, a
ghostwritten essay, and a newsletter all detonate the same way the instant the
seam shows. "You get one bullet, you get one shot." So the cross-domain rule
is: **port the move, never port the fakeness.** Authenticity engineering only
ever amplifies a thing that is already true.

The five moves that travel the furthest, in rough order of portability:

1. **Candy coating** (Pattern 3) — lead with their world, not your offer. Works in *every* domain.
2. **Naming-gives-shape** (Pattern 15) — name the felt-but-unsaid; let them spread it in their own words.
3. **Pressure over surface area** (Pattern 12) — one audience, one thing, repeated.
4. **Experience monopoly** (Pattern 16) — say the thing only you lived; opinions are commodities, experiences are not.
5. **Trajectory arbitrage** (Pattern 14) — play where the puck is going; retire burned templates at the half-life.

## 1. Social media (LinkedIn / X / short-form)

**Moves that earn their place:** Naming-gives-shape (15) · Candy coating (3) · Trajectory arbitrage (14) · Slogan ubiquity (13) · Cringe-vs-resentment decoder (10) · Negative-space reading (TK#5).

**The transfer:** A social post is a comms campaign at 1/100th scale. The same triad runs — message first (the words people latch onto), then medium (the right pocket of the feed, not the biggest), then messenger (you, in unmistakable first person). The post fails for the same reason a launch fails: it hit a wall, not a wound — no charged receptor was open for it.

**Before → After**

| | Before (generic) | After (Lulu move) |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | "Here are 7 lessons I learned scaling a coaching business." | "Every fitness coach I know is quietly terrified of the same thing, and nobody will say it out loud." *(names the felt-but-unsaid; opens a receptor — Pattern 15 + TK#5)* |
| **Caption open** | "Excited to share my new client onboarding system!" | "Most onboarding makes the client feel processed. Here's the moment that decides whether they stay." *(candy coating — leads with THEIR obsession, the offer comes later or not at all — Pattern 3)* |
| **Topic timing** | Posts about "founder mode" three weeks after it peaked. | Reads the trajectory, sees the format is burned, posts the *next* contrarian read on it — or skips it. "The post that flopped, by definition, nobody saw." *(Pattern 14 + TK#2)* |
| **Recurring phrase** | A different clever framing every week; nothing sticks. | The same 3-5 words anchored across every post ("brands first, not coaching"), stories around them vary. Audience repeats the phrase back unprompted. *(Pattern 13)* |
| **Reading reception** | Counts likes. | Checks WHO went quiet. Supportive voices silent = cringe territory. Detractors loud = you mattered. *(Pattern 10 + TK#5)* |

**Anti-pattern to kill on sight:** the engagement-farm template ("I spent 487 hours so you don't have to / 7 ChatGPT hacks"). It reads as bot-bait and "disgusts on a visceral level" (AN-5). Never ask people to share/reply — that's the al-Qaeda-hostage-video energy (AN-4); give them something they already wanted to say instead.

**Routes here:** `lulu-zeitgeist-content` (calendar + hooks + ready posts), `lulu-viral-trajectory` (which topic, what week), `lulu-erogenous-zones` (the upstream receptor scan).

## 2. Marketing / brand

**Moves that earn their place:** Gerrymandered line (6) · Cultural erogenous zone (8) · Candy coating (3) · "What you stand for = who they want to be" · Deterrence score (5).

**The transfer:** A brand that stands for nothing is a fungible commodity competing on price and features. A brand that stands for something is a *flag* — the product becomes who the customer gets to be seen as. The amateur version of "take a stand" splits the base (Coke-vs-Pepsi civil war among your own customers). The master version gerrymanders the district so every group you depend on lands inside the line and the only thing outside is an external "old way."

**Before → After**

| | Before (generic) | After (Lulu move) |
|---|---|---|
| **Positioning** | "The most affordable, full-service strength-coaching platform." | "We coach the people the fitness industry gave up on — and we think the industry's 'transformation' theater is a lie." *(gerrymandered line: customers + staff inside, the external "old way" outside — Pattern 6)* |
| **Campaign frame** | "New feature: AI-powered meal plans!" | "Everyone in performance nutrition is exhausted by tracking apps that make eating feel like accounting. Here's the version that doesn't." *(candy coating + attaching to a charged zone — Pattern 3 + 8)* |
| **Tagline** | "Innovating wellness for a better tomorrow." | One fixed phrase that names the tribe and gets repeated everywhere until it feels inevitable. *(Pattern 13)* |
| **Claim credibility** | "Trusted by thousands." | A visible track record of announced→delivered that compounds: "We said we'd X by Q2. We did. We said Y. We did." *(deterrence score — Pattern 5)* |

**The enemy rule (critical):** the "old way" you draw the line against is a *practice or trend*, never a named competitor and never a political party. Anduril didn't pick a competitor; it picked an *attitude* (squeamishness about defense). Pick a trend nobody on your side will defend.

**Routes here:** `lulu-line-in-sand` (the stance), `cultural-positioning-differentiation` (positioning + zeitgeist roadmap), `lulu-reality-architect` (category creation), `build-bos` / `oren-brand` for the broader brand-OS wrap.

## 3. Copywriting

**Moves that earn their place:** Belief architecture (conviction copy) · Candy coating (3) · Pressure over surface area (12) · Saxon conviction-over-polish (genius.md "How to Use") · Experience monopoly (16).

**The transfer:** Most copy *tells the reader things* and argues with the conscious brain. Conviction copy installs beliefs in the order a human accepts them — recognition first ("finally, someone gets it"), then the crack, then the new frame, then the identity bridge, then the proof. The architecture is the skeleton; the conviction is what actually moves the reader, and it's the exact layer a model sands off into "textbook correct and dead."

**Before → After**

| | Before (generic) | After (Lulu move) |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead** | "Our program helps you build sustainable strength." | "You've started over so many times you've stopped believing the next start will be different. That belief is the actual problem — not your discipline." *(recognition hook → the crack — belief architecture)* |
| **Promise count** | Five benefits, three audiences, two CTAs. | One promise, one audience, one thing to remember. "Do you want them to remember one thing or zero things?" *(Pattern 12)* |
| **Proof** | "Studies show resistance training works." | "I coached 40 people through the exact wall you're at. Here's the one that made me change how I do this." *(experience monopoly — only this author lived it — Pattern 16)* |
| **Voice** | Grammatically immaculate, emotionally dead. | Keeps the slightly-wrong, unmistakably-human pressure. "If the writing is bad, it's better for it to be bad and honest." *(genius.md conviction layer)* |

**Guard:** candy coating in copy means the recognition hook leads with the reader's world; the offer segues in later. AN-3 (self-interest-first / "I'm excited to announce") is the cardinal copy sin — count sentences about *us* vs. sentences that make the *reader* feel understood.

**Routes here:** `lulu-conviction-copy` (belief install + conviction layer). Lulu supplies the *belief architecture* layer INSIDE `copy-engine` / `luke-iha-*` proof-and-offer structure — she is the architect of what the reader believes, not the writer of every line.

## 4. Ghostwriting

**Moves that earn their place:** First-person vision monopoly (1) · Experience monopoly (16) · Signature voice · Authenticity engineering (TK#4) · "Is this me?" filter.

**The transfer:** Ghostwriting is the single most dangerous domain for this genius, because the whole point of going direct is that "the crux has to come directly from you speaking in first person" — and a ghostwriter is by definition not that person. The resolution: the ghostwriter does not invent the voice or the experience, they *engineer the conditions where the client's real voice surfaces*, then select. Record the client talking unscripted, extract the genuinely good moments, present those as the prepared piece. The authenticity is real; the selection is strategic (TK#4).

**Before → After**

| | Before (generic) | After (Lulu move) |
|---|---|---|
| **Sourcing** | Writer drafts from a content brief and a few bullet points. | Writer records a 30-min unscripted call, mines the three sentences only this person could have said, builds the piece around those. *(authenticity engineering — TK#4)* |
| **Detachment test** | Polished post any VP of marketing could have published. | Strip the client's name and personal stakes — if nothing is lost, it's AN-1 (founder-detached). Inject origin + conviction + specifics only they know. *(detection from AN-1)* |
| **Authority** | "Here are best practices for X." | "When I lost the account that was paying my rent, the thing I did next is the thing I'd tell you to do." *(experience monopoly — uncopyable — Pattern 16)* |
| **Final filter** | "Does this read well?" | "Would the client say this at the bar after two drinks? Is the delta between them-on-camera and them-in-person zero?" *(authenticity engineering target)* |

**Guard:** never fabricate the experience — engineer the moment where the real one emerges. The instant the lived-experience claim is invented, the piece is AN-5 (fake UGC) and you have spent the one bullet.

**Routes here:** `founder-narrative-voice-architecture` (voice DNA + first-person manifesto), `lulu-authenticity-engineering` (the truth gate → strip corporate signals → inject spontaneity), `ghostwrite` / `nicolas-cole-*` / `lara-acosta-*` for the broader ghostwriting OS.

## 5. Entrepreneurship / founder visibility

**Moves that earn their place:** Go direct · Ship-to-yap ratio (2) · Deterrence score (5) · Message→Medium→Messenger (11) · Business-goal gravity well (TK#6).

**The transfer:** This is the home domain, so the transfer here is about *discipline* rather than translation. Going direct is a standing posture, not a stunt. The founder owns the relationship and the crux (speaks the vision in first person) — it does NOT mean writing every tweet personally, boycotting the press, or talking constantly. Every word is backed by a recent ship; over years the announced→delivered record hardens into a deterrence score where "if he says it'll happen, it will."

**Before → After**

| | Before (generic) | After (Lulu move) |
|---|---|---|
| **Cadence** | Goes quiet, surfaces for the launch, harvests dopamine, goes quiet. | Ships first, then yaps about the ship. Every week of visibility backed by something tangible. *(ship-to-yap — Pattern 2)* |
| **Promises** | Announces ambitious roadmap to generate buzz. | Only announces what's committed; builds a years-long announced→delivered record. Observers cite past follow-through to predict the next move. *(deterrence score — Pattern 5)* |
| **Channel** | Hires a PR firm to place the story. | Knows the reporter and the regulator on a texting basis; goes direct *to* them, not *around* them. *(go direct, correctly understood)* |
| **Goal discipline** | "We want more awareness." | Every comms decision is pulled toward exactly ONE of recruiting / fundraising / sales / regulatory. *(business-goal gravity well — TK#6)* |

**Routes here:** `lulu-founder-brand-os` (the standing 5-subsystem OS — the headline workflow for this domain), `lulu-m3-matrix` (the load-bearing campaign engine underneath any single push).

## 6. Content / media (newsletters, podcasts, series)

**Moves that earn their place:** Trajectory arbitrage (14) · Naming-gives-shape (15) · Experience monopoly (16) · Template half-life (TK#2) · Strategic wrongness.

**The transfer:** A content franchise is territory. The play is to claim intellectual ground *early* — "the writing on the wall" before the crowd — so by the time the mainstream arrives you own the language. Speak from lived experience for an uncopyable monopoly (a smaller audience is fine: 100% of the people who care about *that* thing have to come to you). Retire formats at the 6-8 week half-life, and remember the fear-killer: the iteration that flops, by definition, nobody saw.

**Before → After**

| | Before (generic) | After (Lulu move) |
|---|---|---|
| **Topic selection** | Writes about what's trending right now. | Forms a thesis on where the conversation is *heading*, plants the flag early, owns the term before the crowd. *(trajectory arbitrage — Pattern 14)* |
| **Newsletter angle** | "5 productivity tips for creators." | Names a feeling the audience already has but can't articulate ("the consistency tax"), gives it catharsis and permission, not instruction. *(naming-gives-shape — Pattern 15)* |
| **Differentiation** | Curates other people's takes. | Recounts the thing only the writer lived through; the issue could only have been written by this one person. *(experience monopoly — Pattern 16)* |
| **Format cadence** | Same format for six months. | Tracks the half-life; rotates before fatigue; exits a format when 3+ others are running it. *(template half-life — TK#2)* |
| **Contrarian take** | Edgy hot take for engagement. | Right-before-it's-safe-to-be-right, with the conviction backing to hold it the months until consensus catches up. *(strategic wrongness)* |

**Routes here:** `lulu-strategic-wrongness` (stake the early position), `lulu-viral-trajectory` (timing), `lulu-zeitgeist-content` (the production engine / calendar), `lulu-reality-architect` (install a new operating truth in the field), `parallax` / `nicolas-cole-newsletter-flywheel` for the broader media OS.

## Cross-domain composition notes

- **Candy coating (3) is the universal opener.** In every domain above, the highest-leverage single edit is: cut the self-interest-first lead, replace it with a statement about the audience's world. If you change one thing, change this.
- **Naming-gives-shape (15) + experience monopoly (16) is the uncopyability pair.** Naming gives them words to spread; experience gives you the monopoly on saying it. Together they are why "AI can't do this part."
- **The honesty spine binds all six domains.** Every move amplifies a claim that is already TRUE. The angle, the slogan, the candy coating, the conviction layer ring *louder* — they never make a missing claim exist. Point any of these at a hollow thing and you've built the most efficient possible machine for getting caught.
- **Decision Framework gates the port.** Before deploying any move in any domain, run `genius.md § Decision Framework` Q1 (names a business goal?), Q2 (passes the two comms tests?), Q7 (real enough to survive the seam showing?). A move that fails these in founder-PR fails identically in social, copy, or content.
