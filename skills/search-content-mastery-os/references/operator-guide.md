## Portable Project Pack

Every project carries:

1. entity truth;
2. brand, offer, audience, and voice;
3. source policy;
4. claim policy;
5. competitor patterns;
6. search inputs;
7. operator taste;
8. execution, outcome, and recommendation ledgers.

Resume must validate the saved manifest without asking the operator to re-enter project name or vertical.

## SearchBrief Gate

A brief is not a keyword. It must name:

- target query and intent;
- audience;
- information-gain requirement;
- source receipts;
- proven structural pattern;
- channel;
- CTA;
- risk boundary;
- prediction, primary outcome stage, observation window, and falsifier.

Only `APPROVED` briefs enter production. Approval covers a local handoff, not publishing.

## Scoring Rule

Score these independently:

- intent fit;
- information gain;
- source quality;
- technical/on-page readiness;
- AEO/GEO readiness;
- human usefulness;
- format fit;
- conversion alignment;
- claim risk;
- measurement readiness.

Deterministic checks show contract coverage. Expert judgment shows meaning and craft. Farrice's override is a third layer and must preserve the original composite and reason. No score is an outcome event.

## Outcome Rule

Append stages independently:

`PREDICTED → PUBLISHED → INDEXED → RANKED → CITED → TRAFFIC → CONVERTED → COLLECTED`

The arrow shows a useful diagnostic sequence, not a guarantee that every asset advances. `CITED` can occur without `RANKED` or `TRAFFIC`. Do not infer a missing stage.

## Import Rule

V1 is local and import-first. Preserve the raw file, hash, date range, schema mapping, normalized rows, and receipt. Reject partial, malformed, duplicate, unknown, or conflicting inputs. Never guess a field mapping.

If no dated import or manual observation exists, run the recommendation review and expect zero proposals. “No evidence” is `NO EVENT`, not zero performance.

## Health And High-Risk Content

- Separate ingredient evidence from finished-product evidence.
- Inventory express and implied claims across the full impression.
- Name the qualified reviewer and approval state for real work.
- Treat schema and crawl access as eligibility, not results.
- Final medical, legal, regulatory, merchant, and platform decisions remain with the client's qualified owners.

## Service Boundary

The service may promise scoped research, artifacts, evidence handling, implementation specifications, scoring, and measurement cadence. It may not promise rankings, citations, traffic, leads, conversions, or revenue.

No scheduler, external publishing, outreach, connector write, payment creation, live API, paid generation, deployment, push, merge, or automatic skill mutation belongs to V1.

## Stop Conditions

Stop and surface the gate when:

- the project lacks entity or source truth;
- the target query or intent is only a guess and the decision depends on demand;
- a brief is not approved;
- the content invents facts or requires qualified review;
- publication or paid generation lacks permission;
- an import schema is unknown or incomplete;
- a score is being presented as market proof;
- the requested service requires an outcome guarantee.
