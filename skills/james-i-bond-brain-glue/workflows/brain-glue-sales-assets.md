# Sales Asset Builder

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this when the user needs sales copy for an offer, landing page, email sequence, ad, VSL, sales call, pitch, or objection-handling asset.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../genius.md`, `../references/genius-patterns.md`, and `../references/implementation.md`. Stack with proof, offer, or direct-response skills when needed.

## Execution

1. **Clarify the offer**: Buyer, problem, promise, mechanism, proof, CTA.
2. **Set expectation**: Open with the frame that makes the rest easier to believe.
3. **Simplify the mechanism**: Use analogy, perspective, or demonstration.
4. **Create buying memory**: Add a sticky line that repeats through the asset.
5. **Add trust through honesty**: Include a true limitation, fit statement, or qualifying line.
6. **Intensify the key moment**: Use trigger words, sensory detail, or odd/unexpected phrasing where doubt appears.
7. **Write the asset**: Produce deployable copy in the requested format.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Asset | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Landing page | Hero, mechanism, proof, fit, CTA |
| Sales page | Sticky campaign spine repeated across sections |
| Email | One problem, one sticky phrase, one action |
| VSL | Visual metaphor, spoken proof, memorable promise |
| Sales call | Honest opener, understanding check, rejection line |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- **Offer Clarity Snapshot**
- **Sticky Sales Spine**
- **Finished Asset Copy**
- **Objection Lines**
- **Fit / Not-Fit Language**
- **CTA Options**

## Quality Gate

The sales asset must make buying feel safer and easier, not merely louder.
