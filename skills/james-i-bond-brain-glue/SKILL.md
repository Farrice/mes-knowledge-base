# James I. Bond — Brain Glue

Brain Glue turns a logical idea into a memorable, emotionally charged buying idea. It works by making the prospect **desire**, **understand**, and **remember** the message through right-brain triggers: expectation, tribe, simplicity, metaphor, anchoring, voice, humor, rhyme, alliteration, chiasmus, trigger words, surprise, selective rejection, and sensory language.

Use this skill when the message is true but forgettable, useful but hard to explain, or valuable but not converting.

## Operating Rule

Brain Glue is not decoration. Do not add cute wording after the strategy is done. First identify the buying idea, then build the sticky form that makes the prospect feel it, remember it, and repeat it.
