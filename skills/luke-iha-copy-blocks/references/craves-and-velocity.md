# CRAVES, Copy Velocity & Power Words

> The finishing layer. Once every block is present (the Two-Move Diagnostic step 1), CRAVES strengthens each block (step 2), velocity tunes the rhythm, and power words supply the expressive charge. Source: Copy Blocks Training (Director's Cut).

---

## CRAVES — the Universal Block Strengthener

Two headlines can contain the *exact same blocks* and score 6/10 vs 10/10 — the difference is CRAVES. Apply to any block, line, or whole piece. First three = clarity/logic trio; second three = emotion/vividness trio.

| | Dimension | Diagnostic question | Strengthening move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C** | **Clear** | Can they instantly understand it? | Remove ambiguity, jargon-creep, tangles. Clarity is King — this wins all ties. |
| **R** | **Relevant** | Is it what *this* market actually wants? | Match the real, segment-specific desire (more money vs. more relationship). |
| **A** | **Accurate** | Is it precise to their reality? | Sharpen to the true nuance of what they want / feel. |
| **V** | **Visual** | Can they *picture* it? (dimensionalize) | "rewires your **mind, body & emotions**" > "clears hidden roots" — they can see/feel electrical rewiring. |
| **E** | **Expressive** | Does it carry emotional charge? | Power words + emotionally-charged language. The "**Latina effect**" — Sofia Vergara vs. Ben Stein telling the *same* story; the expression makes a subway-sandwich sale sound like the world is ending. Everything at the apex of importance. |
| **S** | **Specific** | Named mechanism? Concrete symptoms? | "bizarre arctic berry" > "nature's candy"; "clinical studies prove" > "doctors say"; problem-tokens: "can't sit with your legs crossed without your gut bunching up" > "weight loss." |

### Worked CRAVES example (same blocks, saturated)
> **Weak:** "The nature's candy that doctors say is the reason Danish people have low blood sugar even while eating breads and potatoes all day."
> **Strong:** "The **bizarre arctic berry** that **clinical studies prove** is why Scandinavians have **three times lower** rates of high blood sugar — despite stuffing their faces with **mountains of carbs** like fresh-baked pastries, warm white bread, and creamy potatoes all day."

Same idea, same blocks. The second is more *specific* ("bizarre arctic berry," "clinical studies," "3× lower"), more *visual/expressive* ("stuffing their faces with mountains of carbs," "fresh-baked pastries, warm white bread, creamy potatoes"). That's the entire delta.

> "**Cheat sheet**" >> "**PDF**" — same deliverable; "cheat sheet" implies forbidden value you shouldn't have access to. Word choice is never neutral.

## Copy Velocity

Variable-speed block cycling IS the engagement mechanism. The density move: say the same thing in fewer words.

**Copy Block Score (CVS):** `(# of copy blocks ÷ # of words) × 100`.
> Luke: "completely fake and made up — but a powerful *thinking tool*." Do **not** report a CVS to a client or run around calculating it. Use it only to *feel* whether copy is dense or padded.

**Velocity rules:**
1. No single block dominates **4+ consecutive sentences** (velocity drop kills engagement).
2. **Interleave, don't sequence**: Pain → Curiosity → Promise → Pain(new angle) → Proof beats P-P-P-PR-PR.
3. Each block **reappearance uses a fresh angle** (the 3rd pain hit ≠ the 1st).
4. **Opening velocity is highest** — first ~100 words hit 3–4 different blocks.
5. **Pre-CTA velocity drops deliberately** — slow the rhythm before the close to add weight.

**Compression example (ad open):**
> Before (CVS ≈ 8): sprawling, filler-laden multi-sentence open.
> After (CVS ≈ 26): "Can't lose weight no matter what? Harvard scientists are shocked at how this **ancient tribal dragon berry** reignites the stubborn metabolism practically overnight."
Same information, far fewer words — 9 times out of 10, that speed (as long as it stays *clear*) converts better.

## Power Word Banks (the Expressive layer)

Every word carries emotional baggage. People who say "don't use power words" are wrong — *every* word is a power word; the skill is wielding the charge deliberately. Banks by emotional driver:

- **Greed / Desire:** more, double, unlock, secret, hidden, exclusive, breakthrough, windfall, jackpot, untapped.
- **Lazy / Ease:** lazy, easy, instant, quick, effortless, simple, fast, hack, cheat sheet, shortcut, done-for-you, autopilot, while-you-sleep.
- **Lust:** ache, naked, wild, forbidden, obsession, intense, ecstasy, crave, seductive, desire.
- **Safety:** safe, guarantee, protected, proven, risk-free, backed, certified, verified.
- **Terror / Fear:** terror, blood, nightmare, agony, disaster, panic, destroy, death, crisis, warning, threat, robs, forces, trapped.

Observe your *own* reaction when you read these — if a word moves you, it moves the market. Match the bank to the **Core Wound** (terror/loss for joint pain's "trapped," safety/ease for an overwhelmed market). Don't over-stack — clarity and believability still govern.
