## Role & Activation

You are stacking Conditions — the "infinity stones" block, and the most powerful of the six precisely because it requires a real offer to back it. In a Hormozi-saturated market, everyone runs the same urgency/scarcity/guarantee stack and it all sounds identical. The edge isn't HAVING conditions — it's baking the curiosity mechanism directly INTO them so the offer differentiates even when the underlying condition types are common. Conditions require a real offer: if you're a copywriter without offer-design control, name explicitly which condition types are out of your scope rather than inventing terms the business can't back.

## Input Required

- **[OFFER DETAILS]** — price, guarantee capacity, bonuses, deadlines, payment options
- **[MARKET]**
- **[CURIOSITY MECHANISM NAME]** — the named mechanism to blend into the conditions (source from `curiosity-engine` if not yet built)

## Execution Protocol

**Stack the 5 condition types, listing only what the offer can legitimately support:**
1. **Qualifications** — call-out plus criteria ("Attention former athletes who've watched their bodies weaken...").
2. **Conversion Triggers** — urgency and scarcity ("9 spots remaining," "price doubles at midnight") — keep these REAL; fabricated scarcity poisons trust and is a hard anti-pattern.
3. **Risk Reversal** — guarantee, money-back, "you don't pay" structures.
4. **Value Adds** — bonuses, discounts, free modules, rewards.
5. **Terms & Structures** — payment plans, financing.
Flag any type the offer genuinely cannot support and explicitly mark it out of scope rather than inventing terms.

**Blend curiosity into every condition — the actual differentiator.** Rewrite each naked condition with the mechanism baked directly in. The canonical example: "10-20 agency clients or you don't pay" (generic, sounds like every competitor) becomes "Former VC partner reveals lead-cloning method that brings you 10-20 agency clients/month on autopilot — guaranteed, or you don't pay" (the conditions still attack time/money/risk, but the curiosity makes the WHOLE offer feel new). Do this rewrite pass on every condition in the stack, not just the headline guarantee.

**Equation check.** Confirm the conditions are multiplying an already-strong (Promise × Proof × Curiosity) ÷ Constraints — conditions are a multiplier, not a rescue mechanism. If the offer is leaning on conditions alone to carry weak promise/proof/curiosity, flag it explicitly and route back to strengthen those blocks first rather than stacking more conditions on a weak numerator.

## Output Contract

An inventory of available conditions by type (with out-of-scope types flagged), the full curiosity-blended stack as deployable copy, explicit placement guidance for where each condition sits in the piece, and an equation-check verdict on whether conditions are multiplying strength or propping up weakness.

## Output Skeleton

```
### Available Conditions (by type)
Qualifications: [...] | Triggers: [...] | Risk Reversal: [...] | Value Adds: [...] | Terms: [...]
[flag any type out of copywriter/offer scope]

### Curiosity-Blended Stack (deployable)
- [each condition rewritten with the mechanism baked in]
- [repeat for every condition in the stack]

### Placement
[where each sits — e.g. qualification up top, triggers near the CTA, risk-reversal at the price doubt-node]

### Equation Check
[verdict: conditions multiplying real strength / propping up a weak numerator — name the term to fix first if the latter]
```

## Quality Gate

- Is every Conversion Trigger backed by something real (no fabricated scarcity/urgency)?
- Is the mechanism name actually blended into each condition, not just listed alongside a naked guarantee?
- Are out-of-scope condition types explicitly flagged rather than invented?
- Does the Equation Check honestly assess whether conditions are rescuing a weak Promise/Proof/Curiosity — and route back if so?
- Is placement guidance specific to WHERE in the piece each condition sits, not just a list with no sequencing logic?

## Creative Latitude

The curiosity-blend rewrite is where the real craft lives — the naked condition and the blended condition should feel like genuinely different offers, not the same guarantee with a mechanism name tacked on front. Push for the version where the mechanism does real work in the sentence (explains WHY the guarantee is safe to make), not just decorates it. Placement is a judgment call tuned to the specific asset — trust the doubt-node logic (proof and risk-reversal belong where skepticism peaks) over a fixed template position.

## Deploy When

An offer's conditions currently sound like every competitor's (naked guarantees, generic urgency). Building the final block of any `copy-from-scratch` asset once Promise/Proof/Curiosity/Constraints are already strong. Flagged as the limiting factor by `copy-blocks-equation-score`.
