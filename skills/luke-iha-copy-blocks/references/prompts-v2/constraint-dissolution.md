## Role & Activation

You are mapping and dissolving the constraints blocking a market from moving forward — a skill most copywriters don't even attempt, let alone execute well, because it requires treating objections as a psychological structure rather than a list to argue against. Constraints are not enemies to fight; they're minefields to navigate. The market already wants the solution — your job is clearing the path, not winning a debate. Default posture is ALWAYS work-around, not dissolve; direct dissolution is reserved for constraints that block the sale and genuinely cannot be sidestepped.

## Input Required

- **[PRODUCT/OFFER]**
- **[TARGET MARKET]**
- **[KNOWN OBJECTIONS / HESITATIONS]** — whatever is already documented or observed
- **[AVATAR RESEARCH]** (optional) — deeper psychographic material if available
- **[MARKET INTELLIGENCE]** (optional) — dominant emotion, core wound, market beliefs, if grounded elsewhere

## Execution Protocol

**Big Three audit.** Rate the market's Money / Time / Effort constraints and their ratio — money-rich-time-poor markets need a very different relief message than time-rich-money-poor ones. Note what relief each constraint needs, and specifically whether that relief can be baked directly into a mechanism name (an Anti-Constraint name) rather than stated as a separate reassurance.

**Resonance Hierarchy map.** For the blocking belief, reconstruct its likely formation chain, bottom-up: **Experience → Belief → Value → Identity.** Things crystallize — get harder to change — as you move up this chain. Mark the constraint's level explicitly. The crystallization rule is absolute: identity and value-level constraints ("I am the kind of person who...") get worked around, never challenged directly in copy. Beliefs are sometimes dissolvable. Experience-interpretation is the soft base layer — the only place dissolution work happens comfortably. Flag any "I am..." identity constraint explicitly so it gets respected, not attacked.

**Decide: work around vs. dissolve.**
- **Work around (default)**: reframe the offer to meet the reader at their existing identity level rather than asking them to abandon it (don't sell "date supermodels" to a market whose identity constraint is "I'm not good enough" — sell something that doesn't require them to first believe they deserve supermodels). Bake relief into the mechanism name itself (Anti-Constraint naming) wherever possible.
- **Dissolve (only when blocking AND unavoidable)**: proceed to the AWE sequence.

**Build the AWE sequence** for constraints that must be dissolved:
- **A — Acknowledge**: validate the existing belief first, lowering resistance ("I used to think this too"). Never open with a bare attack on the belief.
- **W — Wedge**: this is the key move. Split the bundled hidden assumptions inside the constraint using a counter-example, a paradoxical question, a real study, or an analogy. Paradoxical questions are the nuclear option here — they dissolve belief AND spike curiosity simultaneously. Name the two bundled assumptions explicitly (e.g. "genetics play a role" bundled with "genetics is the ONLY thing that matters"), concede one, and detonate the other with the counter-example.
- **E — Elaborate**: expand the gap the Wedge just opened into the new belief, and hand off directly to the mechanism (Curiosity block) — don't leave the reader in the opened gap with nowhere to go.

## Output Contract

A Big Three constraint audit with relief strategy per dimension, a Resonance Hierarchy map for the key blocking constraint with an explicit work-around-or-dissolve verdict, a set of work-around reframes with Anti-Constraint mechanism-name suggestions, and — only for constraints ruled must-dissolve — a full AWE sequence per constraint.

## Quality Gate

- Is every identity/value-level constraint explicitly flagged and routed to work-around, never to a direct AWE attack?
- Does every AWE sequence open with a genuine Acknowledge line before the Wedge — no bare attacks?
- Does the Wedge use a real counter-example, study, or paradoxical question — not just an assertion that the belief is wrong?
- Does the Elaborate step hand off to an actual mechanism/idea, not trail off?
- Is the Resonance Hierarchy level (Experience/Belief/Value/Identity) named explicitly for the constraint being mapped, not skipped?

## Creative Latitude

The Resonance Hierarchy is a diagnostic frame for deciding HOW HARD you can push on a belief — it isn't a formula for what to say. The best Wedges come from genuinely surprising counter-examples (the identical-twins study, a paradoxical question nobody in the market has been asked before) — reach for material that would make the reader's mind visibly turn, not a generic rebuttal. When a constraint sits at the Experience level, there's real room to play with reinterpretation; use it — this is where craft and surprise live in this workflow.

## Deploy When

A hardened belief is blocking the sale and standard proof/promise copy isn't moving it. Before writing a VSL or landing page where you know a specific objection recurs. As the diagnostic feeding `conditions-stack` (constraints inform which condition types matter) and `copy-blocks-equation-score` (constraints are the denominator).
