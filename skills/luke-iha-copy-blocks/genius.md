## Core Philosophy

Copy isn't art and it isn't science — it's **architecture that's 80% science, 20% art**. The 6 blocks are your materials. Velocity is your blueprint. The Curiosity Quadrant is your idea engine. CRAVES is your finishing pass. You never stare at a blank page because you have a SYSTEM that generates infinite variations from structural principles — and you never second-guess, because every decision has a stated reason behind it. ### The 3 Core Operating Principles

1. **Clarity is King.** Every other rule bows to this. You can do everything right and lose by burying the meaning. When copy gets over-engineered, strip it back until it makes sense — "copy laundering": rewrite it almost identically, just make it make sense. A reader who is confused does not buy.
2. **80/20 — Science over Art.** ~80% of copy is procedure you can write down and replicate (blocks, velocity, CRAVES, the Quadrant). ~20% is judgment — *how much* proof, *what* ratio, *which* mechanism. Master the science so the art is the only thing left to spend yourself on. ("How many blocks do I need in a sentence?" is the wrong question — it means you're missing principle #2: **there is no right answer**, follow ~80% of the science and use judgment for the rest.)
3. **Throw Away the Ladder.** Internalize until intuitive, then stop consciously running the frameworks. The frameworks exist to be outgrown.

## The 6 Copy Blocks — P3C2 + 1

The mnemonic is **P3C2**: three P-blocks (**P**ain, **P**romise, **P**roof) and two C-blocks (**C**onstraints, **C**uriosity). Plus the bonus sixth: **Conditions**.

| Block | Function | What it answers | Color (Luke's system) |
|-------|----------|----------------|----------------------|
| **Pain** | Surface/amplify the problem | "Do they understand me?" | — |
| **Promise** | Show the outcome | "What do I get?" | — |
| **Proof** | Provide a reason to believe | "Why should I believe you?" | Blue |
| **Constraints** | Name + dissolve what blocks them | "What's stopping me?" | Gray |
| **Curiosity** | Bridge pain→promise with a new idea | "How does it work?" | Yellow |
| **Conditions** | Make acting a no-brainer | "Why act now / is this for me?" | — |

**The Ascension.** Pain and Promise are the most *static* and intuitive — you don't control them, you present them. The magic — and your flexibility — lives in **Proof, Constraints, and Curiosity**. Curiosity is the single most important block (it's where the marketing actually happens — your mechanism, your differentiation) and the one most copywriters are weakest at. Constraints and Curiosity are "the twin C's": a strong curiosity block can dissolve a constraint automatically.

### Pain — the Pain Chain, the Pain Matrix, the Core Wound

- **Pain Chain** (escalation ladder): *General → Specific → Cinematic (dimensionalized, a movie of their life) → Emotional → Core Wound.* Luke's standard VSL move: open with an overarching description, name 3 specific aspects, paint the cinematic picture, hit the emotion, briefly touch the core wound.
- **Pain Matrix** (10 dimensions that shape how a market experiences pain): chiefly **psychological vs physiological** (physical pain is felt *now* — don't over-remind it; psychological pain must be re-triggered through story/imagination because they only feel it at "the party"), **measurable vs not**, **social stigma**, and others. Don't obsess over the matrix — use it to (a) calibrate your approach and (b) judge whether a market you're *swiping from* even matches the market you're *writing to*. Swiping a physiological structure into a psychological market falls flat.
- **Core Wound** — the one deep existential fear at the heart of a market: death, annihilation, abandonment, total loss. Skincare = *invisibility*. Joint pain = *loss of freedom / trapped in your body*. The Core Wound is **nuclear**: deploy sparingly, weave it in *indirectly* (never "Do you feel invisible?"), warm up to it, use **softening/hedging language** ("I don't know if you've felt this, but…") so non-resonators tune out instead of feeling judged. Make it your **marketing DNA** — a recurring subtle theme, not a callout.

### Promise — the Promise Ladder, Promises With Balls, the Identity Runway

- **Promise Ladder** (mirror of the Pain Chain): *General → Specific → Cinematic → Emotional → Core Transformation.* There's always one core transformation the market wants — make it the dominant theme. Feature the promise **prominently and early** — it's why they're reading. Hard to overdo promises; most people drastically under-promise. Err toward too much.
- **Promises With Balls** = NOT a bigger number. It's **specificity + emotion + conviction**. Same "$10k/month" promise told as a sketch vs. an 8K hidden-camera video of their actual life. Paint it like there's zero doubt in your mind they'll achieve it.
- **The Identity Runway is shorter than the Belief Runway.** This is the master calibration. People's *identity* ("am I someone who could make $1M?") runs out before their *belief* that it's possible. So pitch the promise to the **edge of their identity constraints**, not the edge of believability. Copywriter market identity ceiling ≈ $10–20k/mo — promise there even if you can deliver $50k.
- **Gradualization.** Once you've anchored a believable core promise, build a *ladder* to the bigger one for the segment that wants it: "It'll do $2–3k/mo on autopilot… some people double down and scale it to $30–50k." Never open with the big number that triggers the BS detector.

### Proof — the Proof Braid, the Feeling of Truth (compact; deep-dive → `luke-iha-proof-mechanisms` / `luke-iha-proof-ladder`)

- **Proof is about the FEELING of truth, not winning a logical battle.** Once pain + promise land, the prospect is *on your team* — they *want* it to be true. You're not convincing; you're giving them enough to relax and move forward. **Overdoing proof reads as trying too hard and lowers trust.** The maturity arc: no proof → prove everything logically/legally → create the feeling of trust.
- **The Proof Braid** (the "Siamese twin"): every claim carries a proof element, intertwined until the proof is nearly invisible. Mental model — the **Belief Bank Account**: every claim withdraws belief; every proof deposits it. Drain the account and they stop paying attention without knowing why. Best move: **proof-as-promise** — instead of "you'll make $X," show a testimonial of someone who already did (an implied promise nobody can call BS on).
- **Never make your claim bigger than your proof** (the BS card-game rule): back every claim and eventually they stop second-guessing you, which buys you latitude for bigger claims later. **Proof Balance Scale**: big promise → big proof; small promise → small proof (don't cite meteorologists to prove the sky is blue).
- **5 categories / 22 types** (compact): **Psychological** (technical jargon drop, candid/damaging admission, explanatory reasoning — "because", logical *frame* even when logic is broken, compliance, guarantees) — *most underutilized, best pound-for-pound*; **Experiential** (demonstration, testable proof, trials/samples, challenges, comparisons, before/afters) — *hardest to get, most convincing*; **Empirical** (studies/science); **Credible** (authority, endorsements, press, awards, track record); **Social** (testimonials, case studies, reviews, metrics). Rules of thumb: harder-to-fake = stronger; experiential wins; psychological is the hidden edge.

### Constraints — the Big Three, the Resonance Hierarchy, AWE

- Constraints = whatever **blocks the market from moving forward**. Not objections you fight — minefields you navigate. They *want* your solution; clear the path.
- **The Big Three (always present): Money, Time, Effort.** "Can't afford it / no time / too hard." Markets vary in ratio (money-rich/time-poor vs. the reverse).
- **The Resonance Hierarchy** — how constraints form, bottom-up: **Experiences → Beliefs → Values → Identity.** Things *crystallize* (get harder to change) as you rise. **Don't challenge identity or values in a sales letter — work around them.** You *can* play with their *interpretation of experiences* (the soft base layer).
- **Dissolving constraints — the AWE Framework**: **A**cknowledge (validate the belief, lower resistance — "I used to think this too") → **W**edge (the key move: counter-examples / paradoxical questions / anecdotes that drive apart the bundled hidden assumptions and open a new option) → **E**laborate (build out the new belief that fits your message). Paradoxical questions are nuclear for the wedge — they dissolve belief *and* generate curiosity at once.
- **Curiosity dissolves constraints.** The ideal: bake the constraint-relief *into* the curiosity mechanism — "**whisper** command stops dog barking" (gentle, value-safe), "**lazy** mom's 10-minute trick" (effort-safe), "works **while you sleep**" (effort + time safe).

### Curiosity — the gap, the Idea + the Frame, the Quadrant, the 4 Tools (deep-dive → `references/curiosity-engine.md`)

- **Curiosity = the bridge across the gap between Pain and Promise**, usually filled with *information* (sometimes action). Whatever closes that gap IS the curiosity block. Used heavily up front (hooks, openings, fascinations, mechanisms, open loops), then phased out once "the cat's out of the bag" and you shift to selling.
- Two components: **the Idea** (what you say) and **the Frame** (how you present it — 58 rhetorical frames live in AI Email Mastery; paradoxical question, pop-quiz, etc.).
- **The Curiosity Quadrant** generates the Idea: a 2×2 of **Problem vs Solution** × **Market's view (external) vs Your view (internal / UMP·UMS)**, each splittable general/specific. Every interesting thing you can say lives in one cell: *here's why what you think the problem is isn't the real problem · here's the real problem · here's why everyone else's solutions are BS · here's why mine is the one.*
- **Insight = the quality that makes an Idea worth caring about** (the aha/epiphany). Four qualities: **Simple, Easy, Explanatory, Novel** — the two that matter most are **Explanatory** (it explains your past failures and others' success; it reframes reality) and **Novel** (genuinely new). The single most valuable business skill.
- **The 4 Thinking Tools** for strengthening the Idea: **Epiphany Threshold** (0–10 believability/novelty; 1–5 too obvious, 6–9 the goldilocks zone, 9–10 unbelievable), **Evocative Naming** (a specific named mechanism tied to the problem — "hidden cartilage destroyer" beats "hidden cause"; the magnet tension of knowing-enough-but-not-enough), **Intuition Pumps** (natural/mechanical metaphors + forces + strong associations — "off-market dating system" borrows real-estate hidden-value), **Idea Caricature** (exaggerate the surprising/taboo/weird facet — kidney-bean extract → "eat this **white carb before bed** to burn fat").

### Conditions — the 6th block (the "infinity stones")

- Covers urgency, scarcity, guarantees, free offers, discounts, qualifications. The most *powerful* block — but it requires a real offer to deliver, and in a Hormozi-saturated market everyone's conditions sound identical. **The edge: blend curiosity INTO the conditions** so the offer differentiates ("former VC partner reveals **lead-cloning method** that brings 10–20 clients/mo, guaranteed — or you don't pay" >> "10–20 clients or you don't pay").
- **5 types:** Qualifications (call-out / criteria — "attention former athletes…"), Conversion Triggers (urgency, scarcity), Risk Reversal (guarantees, money-back, "you don't pay"), Value Adds (bonuses, discounts, rewards), Terms & Structures (payment plans, financing). Use as many as the offer allows.

## CRAVES — the Universal Block Strengthener

Once every block is present, elevate *any* block (or any line) through six dimensions. First three = the clarity/logic trio; second three = the emotion/vividness trio.

| | Dimension | Test | Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C** | Clear | Can they instantly understand it? | Strip ambiguity |
| **R** | Relevant | Is it actually what THIS market wants? | Match the real desire |
| **A** | Accurate | Is it precise to their reality? | Sharpen specificity of meaning |
| **V** | Visual | Can they picture it? ("dimensionalize") | "rewires your mind, body & emotions" > "clears hidden roots" |
| **E** | Expressive | Does it carry emotional charge? (the "Latina effect") | Power words — every word carries baggage; Sofia Vergara vs Ben Stein telling the *same* story |
| **S** | Specific | Named mechanism? Problem tokens? | "bizarre arctic berry" > "nature's candy"; "can't sit with legs crossed" > "weight loss" |

Two headlines can contain the *exact same blocks* and differ only in CRAVES quality — that's the gap between a 6/10 and a 10/10. Power-word banks (Greed / Lazy / Lust / Safety / Terror) live in `references/craves-and-velocity.md`. ("Cheat sheet" >> "PDF" — same deliverable, implied forbidden value.)

## Copy Velocity

Variable-speed block cycling IS the engagement mechanism. **Density move** — express the same idea in fewer words: `Copy Block Score = (# blocks ÷ # words) × 100`. (Luke: "completely fake and made up, but a powerful thinking tool" — do NOT report a CVS to clients.) Rules:
1. No block dominates 4+ consecutive sentences (velocity drop).
2. Interleave, don't sequence: Pain→Curiosity→Promise→Pain(new angle)→Proof beats P-P-P-PR-PR.
3. Every reappearance uses a FRESH angle.
4. Opening velocity is highest — first ~100 words hit 3–4 blocks.
5. Pre-CTA velocity drops deliberately to create weight.

## The Copy Blocks Equation (the Hormozi value-equation parallel)

$$\text{Value} = \frac{\text{Promise} \times \text{Proof} \times \text{Curiosity}}{\text{Constraints}} \times \text{Conditions}$$

- **Promise** = dream outcome · **Proof** = perceived likelihood of achievement · **Curiosity** = the vehicle/mechanism · **Constraints** = everything blocking (time, effort, money — *and* values/identity) · **Conditions** = the multiplier/supercharger.
- Diagnostic power: a $20k/mo, guaranteed, zero-effort promise still fails if it violates a **value constraint** (e.g. "scam grandmothers") — the denominator blows up. Find the limiting factor and fix *that* block.

## The Canyon / Helicopter Metaphor (teaching model)

Prospect stands in **Pain Land**, wants the **Promised Land** across the canyon. The **helicopter = Curiosity** (the vehicle). The **pilot = Proof** (they won't board unless they trust the pilot). Their **baggage = Constraints** (must be dropped to board). A **stick of dynamite counting down = Conditions/urgency** (board now or it blows). Every block has a role in getting them across.

## Hall of Fame Exemplars

**1. The Core-Wound Weave (Arthur Z. joint-pain control)**
> "I've seen firsthand the destruction caused by joint problems… how it cruelly *robs* you of everyday joys right at the moment when you should be enjoying life the most. How it *forces* you into the sidelines of your own life… or how it can make you feel old before your time — forced to give up everything you love most, like golf or hiking or afternoon walks with your spouse."
- **Why it's excellent**: The core wound (loss of freedom) is *victimization-framed and indirect* — "I've seen how it happens to *other people*," never "you." Verb choice (*robs, forces, give up*) embeds the existential theme without naming it. Then the promise mirror: "every possibility is opened back up to you" pops the same wound positively.

**2. The Belief-Wedge (AWE, eye-bag skincare)**
> "It's definitely true that genetics play a role when it comes to eye bags. *(Acknowledge)* But then I saw this twin study — two twins, same genetics, same diet, exercise, sun exposure… just look at the difference in their eye bags. Turns out there's one thing scientists found that made the difference — and it wasn't their skincare, genetics, or lifestyle… *(Wedge — drives apart 'genetic component' from 'genetics is the ONLY thing that matters')*"
- **Why it's excellent**: Lowers resistance before the wedge, uses a paradoxical real-world counter-example (identical twins, different result) that simultaneously dissolves the constraint and opens a curiosity loop. The reader's mind starts turning *for* you.

**3. The Curiosity-Baked Constraint (made-up, dating)**
> "Divorced dad discovers **lazy man's dating system**, finds high-quality matches in 37 days while working 6+ hours a week — without expensive dating apps, time-consuming coffee dates, or energy-draining dating games."
- **Why it's excellent**: The mechanism name ("lazy man's") *is* the effort-constraint relief; every clause after "without" dissolves a Big-Three constraint (money/time/effort). Curiosity and constraints fused into one line — the ideal state.

**4. Anti-Exemplar: the Flat Feature Dump**
> "Our new software has many features. It has a dashboard, reporting tools, and integrations. It is good for businesses. Buy it now."
- **Why it fails**: No blocks (no pain, no proof, no curiosity, no constraint relief), zero velocity (a list), zero CRAVES (not visual/expressive/specific). Fails every layer of the system.

## Signature Moves

- **The Two-Move Diagnostic.** Look at any copy and run: (1) *Does it have all 6 blocks? Add the missing ones.* (2) *Look at the blocks it has — which is weakest? Strengthen via CRAVES.* → This IS how Luke reviews copy live. Deploy on any audit/feedback request.
- **Copy Feedback Only in the Form of Copy** (Kat Merrit's rule). Never say "add more proof" — write the better line. If you can't produce a better suggestion *as copy*, your critique is "a good opinion, not a great one." Always pair a flagged weakness with a rewritten alternative.
- **Curiosity-Baked Constraints.** When naming a mechanism, choose a name that pre-dissolves the dominant constraint (whisper/lazy/while-you-sleep).
- **Proof-as-Promise.** Replace a claim with a testimonial of someone who already got the result — an implied promise immune to "BS."
- **The Softened Core-Wound Tap.** Touch the existential wound with hedging language so non-resonators exit gracefully and resonators attach.
- **The Identity-Calibrated Promise.** Pitch to the edge of identity, not belief; gradualize upward for the ambitious segment.
- **The Smart-Bomb Jargon Drop.** 1–2 sentences of dense unexplained technical jargon to confer authority, then immediately back to plain language (Gary Halbert water ad).
- **The Little Big Idea.** Fuse what they want (promise) + what they won't do (constraints) + what's unique (curiosity/mechanism) into one core idea — "Lunch Break Millionaire," "Wild Card Copywriter." Does enormous heavy lifting.

## Decision Framework (Pre-Flight)

Before producing, answer:
1. **Market reality** — what's the dominant **Core Wound** and **Core Transformation**? Where's the **identity ceiling** for the promise?
2. **Pain dimension** — psychological or physiological? (Determines whether you re-trigger pain via story or just acknowledge it.)
3. **Awareness/constraints** — what are the Big Three ratios and the top identity/value constraints to *work around* (not challenge)?
4. **The Idea** — which Curiosity Quadrant cell is the play? What's the Evocative Name for the mechanism?
5. **Proof level** — does proof balance the promise? Is the heaviest proof placed at the biggest doubt-node?
6. **Velocity & CRAVES** — opening hits 3+ blocks? Each block CRAVES-strong? Conditions blended with curiosity?

## Anti-Patterns (Quality Gate)

- ❌ **Visible architecture** — block names, "CASH," CRAVES, or the Quadrant surfacing in customer-facing copy.
- ❌ **Sequential blocks** — P→PR→PF→CTA in rigid order. Interleave.
- ❌ **Direct core-wound callout** — "Do you feel invisible?" Weave indirectly with softening.
- ❌ **Challenging identity/values head-on** — work around them; only play with experience-interpretation.
- ❌ **Claim bigger than proof** — drains the belief bank.
- ❌ **Over-proofing** — 27 studies past the point of trust reads as desperation.
- ❌ **Promise to the edge of belief instead of identity** — triggers the BS detector.
- ❌ **Generic mechanism name** — "hidden root" usable in any market = no specificity, no curiosity. (Magnet test: too far apart = no tension; name must create knowing-but-not-knowing.)
- ❌ **Conditions naked** — relying on guarantee/urgency alone in a saturated market.
- ❌ **Critique without a rewrite** — "add curiosity" is bozo feedback. Show the line.
- ❌ **Clarity sacrificed for density** — velocity never beats comprehension. Clarity is King.

## Patterns from claude.ai export — Collaborative Copywriting & Marketing Maestro

Two net-new capabilities harvested from a collaborative-copy custom-instruction prompt. Both add a *layer above* the solo block-composition engine: block composition stays the same; these change *who reasons* about the blocks and *how the feeling is sequenced* across the whole piece.

- **Panel-to-Block Mapping.** *Execute:* Instead of one author assembling all 6 blocks, stand up a 3-7 expert panel tailored to *this* objective and assign each expert the block(s) they own (psychology→Pain/Constraints, idea→Curiosity, proof-craft→Proof, voice→AI-tell removal across all). One expert is the **spine-keeper** who owns coherence + velocity and can *veto* any block rewrite that breaks the single-author heartbeat. *Success Metric:* every weak block has a dedicated owner, every critique arrives as a rewritten line (not an opinion), and the final draft reads as ONE coherent voice — not concatenated fragments (the Frankenstein failure). → `expert-panel-deliberation`.
- **User-Gated Deliberation Loop.** *Execute:* Run refinement one block at a time as a stop-and-check dialogue: owning expert diagnoses (weakest CRAVES dimension) → rewrites as copy → one cross-lens dissent → spine-keeper adjudicates with a stated reason → present before/after → **wait for the user** before the next block. Collaboration is a checkpointed loop, not a one-shot dump. *Success Metric:* no round proceeds without a user decision; the deliberation ledger shows real dissent and reasoned verdicts, not rubber-stamps.
- **Emotional-Arc Mapping (funnel-level).** *Execute:* Before drafting, map the ordered sequence of *feelings* the reader travels first-line-to-CTA (Recognition→Descent→Hope-crack→Relief→Desire→Resolve), name the specific **trigger** that fires each shift, and assign every beat to the block that produces it. This is the whole-piece companion to the single-block Pain Chain. *Success Metric:* no orphaned feelings (a felt shift with no causing block) and no emotionally-silent blocks (present but producing no feeling); the fear→hope pivot is explicitly placed and carried by a real mechanism.
- **Emotional Continuity (each feeling earns the next).** *Execute:* Check every adjacent beat pair — does emotion N make emotion N+1 believable? You can't jump Descent→Desire without the Curiosity/Proof bridge; the reader won't feel the turn. Write each block to *produce* its assigned feeling (Expressive/Visual CRAVES on the descent; intensity dropped on Proof so it reads calm, not desperate), not merely to convey information. *Success Metric:* the piece reads as a felt journey, not a block stack; every transition is earned, never jumped. → `emotional-journey-map`.

> **Boundary note:** These do NOT replace the solo engine or the system-wide `/convene` council. `/convene` deliberates cross-domain *strategy*; this panel is *copy-blocks-specific* — experts are mapped to the 6 blocks and constrained by block grammar + velocity. Use the panel when a solo pass has plateaued, stakes are high, or the client wants the reasoning made visible.

## Patterns from claude.ai export — Luke Iha conversations (2026-07-01)

> Source: "6 Advanced Marketing Lessons $100MM Copywriters" (Genesis certainty-call recording). Line-craft patterns only — the strategy-layer lessons from the same recording (Seven-Layer Decision Stack, Reverse Beat Map, Character Casting, Micro-Lead Multiplication) live in `luke-iha-creative-strategy`.

- **Ego/Id Double Talk.** *Execute:* For every promise, write two versions: the **ego version** the reader can admit wanting ("restore love to your loveless marriage") and the **id version** they actually want ("a husband absolutely obsessed with you"). STATE the acceptable one; then paint the picture that delivers the id one — the reader makes the connection privately without you ever claiming it. You're talking to the mindset with acceptable beliefs and to the deep emotional id in the same passage. *Success Metric:* the id desire is never named in a claim, yet a reader could describe it back after one pass.
- **Specificity as Spice.** *Execute:* Specificity is cayenne pepper, not a universal good — dose it. Avoid raw numbers/percentages early (processing load). "87%" gives the skeptic wiggle room; "nearly 9 out of 10 people" rounds to *everyone* in the reader's head and stays true. ALWAYS attach a source to a stat ("studies have shown...") — an unsourced number is a question, not proof. Inversion works too: "true for only 1 out of 10 people." Save hard precision (87.3%) for proof stacking, where the precision itself signals rigor. *Success Metric:* every number has a source attached or is converted to a concrete-fraction frame; no bare percentages in the lead.
- **The "But First" Friction Purge.** *Execute:* Hunt every transition that halts momentum — "but first, I need to tell you about John" is a speed bump at 60mph, a commercial inside your commercial. Rewrite as forward-pull connectives: "and in order to get the benefit of what I just showed you, you need to understand who John is." Every segue must feel like a consequence of what the reader is already invested in (Sugarman's slippery slide). *Success Metric:* zero "but first"-class transitions; each section opens by referencing the reader's existing investment.
- **Possibility Hedging.** *Execute:* Trade certainty for possibility in two places. (1) **Big-enemy claims**: never assert the conspiracy — stack the data and hedge ("am I saying they're manipulating this? Honestly, I don't know — but look at the numbers, and consider the profit incentive"). Certainty splits the audience into believers and eye-rollers; possibility captures both (Blair Warren: people do anything for those who encourage their dreams, justify their failures, allay their fears, confirm their suspicions, and help them throw rocks at their enemies). (2) **Assumptive future-pacing**, especially in trust-sensitive/female markets: soften "your husband is going to obsess over you" to "chances are, after a little while, you might find he starts texting you more... maybe it even feels like he's obsessed." Softness = tone × assumptiveness. *Success Metric:* skeptics can't dismiss the enemy passage as conspiracy-theory; future-pace lines carry a possibility marker wherever the market skews trust-sensitive.
- **One Dominant Mechanism.** *Execute:* Three-part mechanisms ("there are actually THREE causes and you must fix all three") consistently underperform — people are simple. Pick ONE dominant problem-solution pair as the spine; secondary causes may appear but stay explicitly subordinate. *Success Metric:* a reader asked "why does this work?" answers with one cause, not a list.
- **Riff-then-Filter.** *Execute:* Run the mechanical pipeline (blocks, bots, frameworks) to a complete draft — that's ~85%. Close the gap with state, not procedure: internalize one section, get connected to the reader, turn on a transcriber, and riff aloud — angles, emotion, lines you "don't know where they came from." Feed riff transcript + mechanical draft back to AI: "here's me riffing, here's what I like — weave it into the structure." Finish with a seamlessness edit pass. *Success Metric:* the final draft contains lines that exist in the riff transcript but in no framework output, while the mechanical spine's block order survives intact.

### Hidden Knowledge
- **Insight:** Every solution creates a new problem — that's the physics of upsells. The upsell script does two jobs in order: kill buyer remorse ("you just made a great decision") THEN introduce the successor problem. And the upsell should be MORE of what they just bought (done-for-you, faster, amplified) — a lateral jump (book → course) breaks the demonstrated-preference chain. *Deploy:* any upsell/bump script; cross-wire with offer design in `luke-iha-creative-strategy`.
- **Insight:** Riffing a section is easy; writing the whole thing is hard — live-riffed feedback impresses because it's local. Never mistake a brilliant riff for an architecture: riffs are amplifiers laid onto a mechanical spine (the same lesson as one-coherent-author — escalating experts degrades a sound body). *Deploy:* when tempted to rebuild a working draft around one great riffed line — graft the line, keep the spine.
