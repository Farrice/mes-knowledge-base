# Hidden Knowledge — Diagnostic Boundaries

1. A high non-follower share can prove distribution beyond the existing audience; it cannot prove buyer fit.
2. A high profile-visit rate with no clients makes “get more views” a poor first prescription, but it does not uniquely identify Offer, Trust, Profile, or CTA without more evidence.
3. A vague bio and aggressive DM ask can create different leaks: weak self-selection and excess commitment. Test them separately.
4. Voiceless content is a trust hypothesis, not a universal defect. Demonstrations, artifacts, and customer proof can transfer trust without a talking head.
5. Low views in health, weight-loss, GLP-1, supplement, or regulated contexts warrant policy/account-status checks; they do not prove a shadowban.
6. Empty competitor whitespace is worthless unless it matches an audience need and a creator strength.
7. The lowest-ticket product can hide a high-margin offer by adding friction before qualification; price architecture must be inspected as a path, not isolated SKUs.
8. `$20k+` is a commercial target. The operating ledger distinguishes reported revenue, sold revenue, and collected revenue.
