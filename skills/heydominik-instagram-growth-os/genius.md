# Reverse-Funnel Instagram Growth — Genius Context

## Operating thesis

Reach is the last thing to fix, not the first. Read the account backward from revenue, determine which handoff failed, and repair forward from the earliest broken dependency.

## Five decisions that matter

1. **Desire before distribution.** If the offer does not specify buyer, painful/urgent problem, credible transformation, vehicle, proof, price logic, and low-friction next step, stop attraction work.
2. **Telemetry is not causality.** Non-follower share, profile visits, follows, DMs, link clicks, calls, and sales are evidence of handoffs. They do not by themselves explain why the handoff failed.
3. **Trust is not polish.** Trust comes from specific voice, visible judgment, proof with boundaries, honest opinions, and repeated competence. Generic face time is not trust; silent B-roll is not automatically distrust.
4. **A profile is a routing surface.** In two to five seconds, a cold visitor must recognize who it is for, what useful change is offered, why to believe, what to binge, and where one next step goes.
5. **Attraction is qualified reach.** The first 0.5 seconds orient the right person; the next three seconds create a curiosity gap that the body actually closes.

## Audit/repair duality

The diagnostic reads `Offer → Attraction → Profile → Trust → Offer visibility` because the account's visible metrics reveal where attention stopped. The repair sequence is `Offer → Trust → Profile → Attraction` because each later layer depends on the prior one.

## Scoring discipline

- Scores are evidence summaries, not scientific predictions.
- Every score row cites a screenshot, metric, content sample, offer statement, or `MISSING` field.
- Do not average away a hard veto. Missing offer clarity, rights, substantiation, or regulated-category review can stop downstream generation even when the composite looks high.
- Prefer account-relative baselines to universal benchmarks. If baseline and target are both missing, report the ratio and label the verdict `UNVERIFIED`.

## Hook mechanics

- **0.5-second clarity:** first frame identifies topic, object, person, or stakes without audio.
- **Three-second curiosity:** language creates one specific unanswered question.
- **Visual handoff:** B-roll/context may lead before the face when it clarifies faster; face appears when identity or trust adds value.
- **Caption hierarchy:** high contrast, few words, underline only the decision word; accessibility outranks decoration.
- **Native variants:** remove watermarks; build a Trial Reel test and a feed version from the same truthful premise.

## Trust reform mechanics

Run five checks: voice, format range, gap, opinion, and synthetic slop.

- Voice: does the creator say something only they could credibly say?
- Format: can the account prove competence across explanation, example, demonstration, story, and offer?
- Gap: what useful belief/format is absent from the niche?
- Opinion: is there a defensible judgment, not generic advice?
- Slop: are claims, anecdotes, specificity, or emotions fabricated to sound human?

## Compliance mechanics

Classify jurisdiction, organic/ad/branded status, category, product, claims, visuals, CTA, and external destination. Route health/supplement claims through `claim-safe-health-marketing`. Treat recommendation eligibility, ad approval, community standards, and law as separate layers. A rewrite can reduce risk; it cannot guarantee platform approval.

## Surpass advantage

The AI advantage is coverage and memory: read many evidence rows consistently, produce multiple testable variants, retain a dated baseline, and re-run the same decision rules after every sprint. It is not phantom access, unlimited scraping, conversion prophecy, or replacing human permission and taste.
