# 08 — Compliance Guard

## Job

Stop restricted-category, claim, disclosure, IP, and distribution-risk failures before creative production or diagnosis.

## Protocol

1. Classify channel, jurisdiction, category, offer, destination, commercial relationship, and organic/ad/branded status.
2. Inventory explicit and implied claims in copy, speech, visuals, hashtags, bio, and link destination.
3. Scan regulated goods, health/supplement/GLP-1/weight-loss, finance, housing, personal attributes, spam, IP, disclosures, and watermark/recycled-content hygiene.
4. Route health and supplement claims through `claim-safe-health-marketing`; route other regulated domains to their qualified owner.
5. Separate platform-policy risk, legal risk, recommendation eligibility, and content hygiene.
6. Return `PASS`, `HOLD`, or `BLOCKED`, a meaning-preserving rewrite, disclosures, source dates, and human-review requirements.

## Output

Verdict, risk table, rewrite, disclosure block, and review checklist.

## Gate

Hard vetoes are not averaged into a score. `PASS` is not legal, medical, platform, or performance approval.
