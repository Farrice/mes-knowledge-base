# 09 — Monetization Linker

## Job

Carry one promise from content through one link, one conversion event, and an attributable payment receipt.

## Protocol

1. Select one primary destination and conversion event from the Offer Canvas.
2. Map content promise → landing promise → CTA → fulfillment → sales event → collected revenue.
3. Remove competing destinations, including a YouTube link, unless that destination is the declared conversion event.
4. Draft a tool-neutral comment/DM flow with consent, opt-out, handoff, and failure states.
5. Use a calculator only when inputs, formulas, caveats, and resulting claims are auditable.
6. When relevant, draft a Framer brief, ManyChat mapping JSON, calculator specification, and cohort outline.
7. Define attribution events and distinguish sold from collected.

## Output

One-link landing outline, automation JSON, DM copy, calculator spec, curriculum outline, and attribution plan.

## Gate

One CTA path; reproducible math; consent and opt-out; drafts only until external-write approval and schema validation.
