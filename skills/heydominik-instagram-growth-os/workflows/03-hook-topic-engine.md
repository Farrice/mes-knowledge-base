# 03 — Hook & Topic Engine

## Job

Reach the right non-followers with content that is instantly legible and earns three more seconds.

## Protocol

1. Verify Offer, Trust, and Profile gates. If any fails, return `PREMATURE` and the upstream repair.
2. Use only permissioned account/competitor evidence; record sample dates and denominators.
3. Calculate account-relative outliers where real performance data exists.
4. Cluster topic, verbal hook, first visual, format, proof mechanism, and audience signal.
5. Identify gaps that match audience need and the creator's sustainable advantage.
6. Generate 30 cards: 0.5-second clarity frame, three-second curiosity line, B-roll-before-face transition, selective underline, high-contrast caption note, body payoff, CTA, Trial version, and feed version.
7. Assign one variable and one falsification threshold to each test family.

## Output

Thirty topic/hook cards, gap clusters, shot notes, caption hierarchy, and test matrix.

## Gate

Muted first frames remain understandable; the body pays off the hook; no watermark, copied premise, phantom scrape, or auto-publish.
