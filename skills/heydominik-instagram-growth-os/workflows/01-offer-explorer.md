# 01 — Offer Explorer

## Job

Make the intended buyer want the next step before adding reach.

## Protocol

1. Ask the 12 offer questions: buyer, trigger, present pain, desired change, failed alternative, urgency, vehicle, delivery, time-to-value, proof, friction, and economic limit.
2. Tag every answer `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`, or `CONTRADICTED`.
3. Analyze only supplied or connector-authorized comments and competitor offers. A smaller sample is disclosed, not inflated to 100.
4. Synthesize the unmet transformation gap and the strongest competing explanation.
5. Draft three ladders: low-friction diagnostic/tripwire, core outcome delivery, and premium acceleration. Suggested price bands are hypotheses constrained by delivery cost and buyer evidence.
6. Select one ladder for validation; define a behavioral demand test and failure threshold.

## Output

Offer Canvas, price architecture, proof-gap map, three ladders, selected offer, and next demand test.

## Gate

Do not pass until buyer, costly problem, transformation, vehicle, proof state, price logic, and next action are explicit. Revenue remains `NO_EVENT` without payment evidence.
