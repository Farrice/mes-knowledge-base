# 05 — Funnel Diagnostician

## Job

Name the first material leak without confusing symptoms for causes.

## Protocol

1. Check offer clarity before interpreting traffic.
2. Inspect Attraction using reach/views and non-follower percentage.
3. Compute only available handoffs: profile visits ÷ reach, follows ÷ profile visits, DMs ÷ profile visits, calls ÷ qualified leads, sales ÷ calls, and collected revenue ÷ sales.
4. Inspect Profile evidence, then Trust evidence, then offer visibility and conversion continuity.
5. Compare alternative explanations: wrong audience, weak promise, weak proof, profile friction, trust deficit, invisible offer, or restricted-category risk.
6. Name exactly one primary Breaking Point, confidence, missing evidence, and three next fixes in forward repair order.

## Output

Breaking Point, ratio table, competing explanations, confidence, missing-evidence list, and three fixes.

## Gate

Denominators are shown; unavailable ratios are `UNVERIFIED`; low views never prove a shadowban.
