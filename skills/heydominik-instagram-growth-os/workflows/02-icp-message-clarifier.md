# 02 — ICP & Message Clarifier

## Job

Make a cold visitor understand who this is for, what changes, why to believe it, and the low-friction next step within two to five seconds.

## Protocol

1. Load the approved Offer Canvas and proof inventory.
2. Extract five message units: buyer, outcome, mechanism, evidence, and next step.
3. Generate 20 meaning-distinct bios across three hypotheses: outcome-led, mechanism-led, and proof-led.
4. Score each for specificity, evidence match, readability, persona language, and CTA friction. Scores rank clarity; they do not predict DMs.
5. Reject fabricated proof, universal claims, clutter, and a hard DM ask before trust exists.
6. Select three variants and pair each with one primary-link landing outline and a live-test window.

## Output

Three bios, score table, one-link outline, and A/B test plan.

## Gate

All variants fit the supplied character limit and can be paraphrased accurately by a cold reader.
