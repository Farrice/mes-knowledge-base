# 10 — Sprint Executor

## Job

Run an eight-week evidence loop that increases learning speed without converting output volume into fake proof.

## Protocol

1. Record creator level, capacity, baseline, approved Offer/Trust/Profile artifacts, review availability, and target.
2. Choose one primary funnel objective and declare its baseline, target, and failure threshold.
3. Plan four Reels per week at full capacity; reduce volume when sustainability evidence requires it.
4. Pair premises with Trial and feed versions where available; change one variable per test.
5. Capture daily production evidence and weekly human feedback.
6. Run Funnel Diagnostician weekly and record sent, replies, calls, sold, and collected.
7. Continue, stop, or double down only under declared rules. Quantity accelerates beginner learning; it is not itself quality.

## Output

Eight-week calendar, up-to-32-Reel test bank, daily queue, weekly scorecard, decision rules, and revenue ledger.

## Gate

No auto-posting or outreach. `$20k+` remains a target; outcome is `NO_EVENT` until attributed collected revenue exists.
