# 07 — Trust Content Reformer

## Job

Turn voiceless or generic content into attributable judgment, proof, and presence.

## Protocol

1. Audit the asset for voice, face/presence, format, gap, opinion, proof, specificity, and AI-slop.
2. Preserve the truthful premise and discard unsupported claims.
3. Draft a creator-specific voiceover from supplied voice samples and opinions.
4. Place face cutaways where identity, conviction, demonstration, or proof matters; B-roll may lead for immediate clarity.
5. Add high-contrast captions and underline only decision-bearing keywords.
6. Produce a Trial Reel variant and a feed variant that differ by one declared test.

## Output

Voiceover, edit decision list, caption file, Trial plan, feed plan, and trust rationale.

## Gate

One sourced opinion and one bounded proof object; no invented anecdote; audio-on and muted versions both work. Voice cloning or synthetic faces require explicit consent and rights.
