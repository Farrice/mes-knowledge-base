## Primary sources (all accessed 2026-08-02)

### A. The two campaign case studies — the strongest evidence in this extraction
Because the **same four-move process appears in both, in the same order, with the same stage
vocabulary**, it is a repeatable operating chain rather than a retrofitted story. This is the single
highest-confidence structural finding here.

1. `https://www.domestika.org/en/projects/1855562-onalaja-x-fashion-coupids-instagram-campaign-for-zaza-bag`
   - Concept, verbatim: *"a woman running through the city, a metaphor for the fast-paced rhythm of
     modern life"* — with the message that stylish women stay stylish however busy their schedule.
   - Step 1 Research: Onalaja's aesthetic (*"bold patterns, sculptural silhouettes"*) + brand
     positioning → **mood and storyboards**.
   - Step 2 Midjourney: iterative prompt refinement exploring **lighting, urban environments,
     dynamic poses**.
   - Step 3 Photoshop: **the bag's texture and colour accuracy**, model polish, seamless backgrounds.
   - Step 4 Lightroom: cohesive colour grading, contrast, palette matched to the brand's aesthetic.
2. `https://www.domestika.org/en/projects/1855557-versace-x-fashion-coupids-greca-goddess-bag-campaign`
   - Concept, verbatim: blending *"fashion luxury with surrealist storytelling"*, models in dreamlike
     environments that elevate the accessory into an **artistic centerpiece**.
   - Step 1: research Versace brand identity / colour palettes / aesthetic, then *"a moodboard and
     **loose storyboard** in Canva that captured the intended tone, colors, materials, and
     surrealistic themes."*
   - Step 2 Midjourney: prompt variations across **lighting, poses, styling, surreal backgrounds**.
   - Step 3 Photoshop: compositing/retouch, layer masking + colour adjustment to editorial quality.
   - Step 4 Lightroom: grade to *"unify the series and elevate the atmosphere."*
