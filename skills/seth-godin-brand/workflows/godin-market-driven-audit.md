# Godin Market-Driven Audit

> **Expert**: Seth Godin | **Skill**: seth-godin-brand | **Tier**: Foundation
> **Produces**: Market-Driven Diagnostic + Realignment Plan
> **Slash Command**: `/godin-market-driven-audit`

---

## Purpose

Diagnose whether your organization is **marketing-driven** (run BY the marketing department) or **market-driven** (exists TO SERVE the market). Then map every decision that touches the market and ensure marketing has input on all of them.

---

## Inputs Required

1. **Organization** — Company or business being audited
2. **Marketing Team Scope** — What does the marketing team currently own?
3. **Recent Decision Examples** — 3-5 recent business decisions that affected customers

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: The George Distinction

> **Marketing-driven** = The marketing department runs the show
> **Market-driven** = The company exists to serve the market

These sound similar. They're opposites.

**Quick Diagnostic**: Who made the last 5 decisions that affected how customers experience your business?
- If mostly marketing → You might be marketing-driven (department-centric)
- If cross-functional → You might be market-driven (customer-centric)
- If engineering/finance alone → You're neither — you're operations-driven, which means marketing is absent from decisions that ARE marketing

### Step 2: The VW Diesel Test

Godin's example: Five engineers and accountants decide to cheat emissions tests. $1B in fines. Was it a marketing move? Absolutely — it touched the market. Was marketing in the room? No.

**Apply to your organization**: For each major decision in the last year, ask:
1. Did this decision touch the market (customers, community, perception)?
2. Was anyone with marketing judgment in the room?
3. If not — what was the cost?

### Step 3: The "Touches the Market" Map

Godin: "Everything your company does that touches the market — how you design things, how you answer the phone, how much you charge, what you dump in the river — those are ALL marketing decisions."

Map every touchpoint:

**Pre-Purchase**:
- [ ] How prospects discover you
- [ ] What your website/profile communicates
- [ ] How you respond to inquiries
- [ ] What your pricing signals

**During Purchase**:
- [ ] The buying experience
- [ ] Onboarding/first interaction
- [ ] How expectations are set

**Post-Purchase**:
- [ ] Support/service quality
- [ ] Follow-up communication
- [ ] How mistakes are handled
- [ ] How referrals are earned

**Invisible Marketing**:
- [ ] Employee behavior that customers see
- [ ] Environmental/community impact
- [ ] Partner/vendor relationships visible to market
- [ ] Internal decisions that leak externally

For each: Who owns it? Is it consistent with the brand promise?

### Step 4: Market-Driven Realignment

For every touchpoint where marketing judgment is absent:

1. **Identify the gap** — What market-touching decision lacks marketing input?
2. **Assess the risk** — What's the VW Diesel equivalent? What could go wrong?
3. **Design the intervention** — How does marketing perspective get inserted?
4. **Define ownership** — Not "marketing owns everything" but "marketing has input on everything that touches the market"
