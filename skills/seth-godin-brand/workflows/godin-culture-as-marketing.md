> **Expert**: Seth Godin | **Skill**: seth-godin-brand | **Tier**: Practitioner

## Purpose

Audit and redesign your organization's culture so that every behavior, every process, and every decision IS the marketing — not just "aligned with" marketing. Culture isn't something you tell people about. It's what people experience.

---

## Inputs Required

1. **Organization** — What entity are we auditing?
2. **Size** — Solo/small team/large team
3. **Current Culture Statement** — If one exists (mission, values, etc.)
4. **Customer Complaints** — Top 3 things customers complain about

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: The "Everything Touches the Market" Audit

> Godin: "Everything your company does that touches the market — how you design things, how you answer the phone, how much you charge, what you dump in the river — those are ALL marketing decisions."

Map every behavior that a customer, prospect, or community member could ever see, hear about, or experience:

**Visible Behaviors**:
- [ ] How you answer the phone/messages
- [ ] How quickly you respond
- [ ] What your workspace/office looks like
- [ ] How employees talk to each other in front of customers
- [ ] How you handle mistakes publicly
- [ ] Your social media tone and content

**Invisible-But-Leaking Behaviors**:
- [ ] How you treat vendors and partners
- [ ] How you handle employee disagreements
- [ ] What you say about customers when they're not in the room
- [ ] What you optimize for internally (speed? cost? quality?)
- [ ] How you handle firing/letting go of clients

For each, score: **Does this behavior reinforce or erode the brand promise?**

### Step 2: The Employee Optimization Audit

> Godin: "Mostly, we're keeping track of what will I tell my boss. Will this get me in trouble? Is this going to be a hassle? Am I taking a risk? What am I getting measured on?"

**For each team member (or yourself), ask**:

| Question | Current Answer | Ideal Answer |
|----------|---------------|-------------|
| What do I optimize for daily? | | |
| What will I tell my boss? | | |
| What gets me in trouble? | | |
| What am I measured on? | | |
| Am I avoiding risk? | | |

**The Misalignment Signal**: If employees optimize for "not getting in trouble" instead of "serving the customer," culture and marketing are opposed.

### Step 3: The "Mom Is Watching" Redesign

> "Nothing's off the record. Everyone is watching all the time. How do we want to behave when we know our mom is watching?"

For every behavior identified in Step 1:
1. Would we do this if mom was watching?
2. Would we do this if our best customer was watching?
3. Would we do this if our competitor was watching?
4. Would we do this if it was recorded and posted online?

**If the answer changes based on audience → that behavior is inauthentic (in the BAD sense) and needs redesign.**

### Step 4: The Culture = Marketing Alignment

Design the culture-as-marketing system:

**Principle**: "If we always [behave as if everyone is watching], we never have to worry about keeping our story straight."

**For each misaligned behavior, define**:
1. The current behavior
2. The brand promise it violates
3. The replacement behavior
4. How to make the replacement the default (systems, not willpower)
