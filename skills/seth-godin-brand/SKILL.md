## Skill Overview

This skill operationalizes Seth Godin's brand-building methodology — the layer between philosophy (why you create) and viral mechanics (how it spreads). It covers **what you actually build**: brand promise architecture, trust engineering, consistency systems, remarkability design, metric integrity, culture alignment, and AI-era permission strategy.

**Core Thesis**: A brand is a promise, not a logo. Trust = keeping that promise, especially when it's hard. Marketing = everything that touches the market. Better > louder. Consistency > authenticity. AI should deepen permission, not cheapen service.

## Key Concepts Reference

- **Brand = Promise**: Nike vs. Hyatt test
- **False Proxy**: Easy to measure, harmful to optimize for
- **Marketing-Driven vs. Market-Driven**: Department-centric vs. customer-centric
- **Consistency > Authenticity**: George Clooney model, boiler repairman standard
- **Remarkability Engine**: Carmine's three-mechanism blueprint
- **Better Not Louder**: Gajist model ($30M, 130K subscribers, closed signups)
- **AI Permission Deepening**: Techlas vision, cost reduction vs. permission
- **Culture = Marketing**: Everything that touches the market IS marketing
