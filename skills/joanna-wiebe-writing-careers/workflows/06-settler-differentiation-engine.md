# Workflow 06: Settler Differentiation Engine

> **Produces**: A complete differentiation strategy for a writing practice or a client's brand — pioneer's-curse market scan, resource-matched pathway selection (better brand / better product / oppositional positioning / bold voice), and an execution brief for the chosen pathway.
> **Use When**: You (or a client) are stuck chasing an "original idea," entering a saturated market, or losing to a market leader you can't out-spend or out-feature. Answers "how do we stand out?" without requiring novelty.

---

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: Before executing, run the **Decision Framework** in `genius.md` § Decision Framework. Confirm all diagnostic questions are answered.

## Phase 2: Execution

### Step 1 — Pioneer's Curse Market Scan

**Input Required**: The market/category. The current leader(s). Your (or the client's) honest resource picture: budget, product control, marketing strength, timeline.

**Execute**:

Replace the originality question with the settler question:

```
BANNED QUESTION: "Is this idea original?"
SETTLER QUESTION: "How can I stand out with an idea that's already proven to work and make money?"

MARKET SCAN:
- Proven concept: [What has the market already been educated to want? Who paid that education cost?]
- The pioneers: [Who took the arrows — what did their failures teach for free?]
- The leader: [Who currently owns the default-choice position?]
- The complaint mine: [What do users/buyers actually complain about in the leader? Verbatim, from reviews/forums — this is VOC work, not guessing]
- The emotional gap: [What does the leader's brand make people FEEL — and what's missing from that experience?]
```

### Step 2 — Pathway Diagnosis

**Execute**:

Match resources to pathway. One pathway. Half-measures across all four is the losing position.

```
PATHWAY DECISION MATRIX:

→ Better Product (TikTok method)
  Requires: product control + build resources
  Signal: the complaint mine is full and specific (missing features, clunky UX, poor execution)
  Test: can you be GENUINELY superior on the top 3 complaints — not a coat of paint?

→ Better Brand (Starbucks method)
  Requires: marketing/storytelling strength; product parity is fine
  Signal: the emotional gap is large (leader is functional but feels like nothing)
  Test: can you name the feeling the leader fails to deliver?

→ Oppositional Positioning (Apple method)
  Requires: a well-known leader whose awareness you can borrow
  Signal: the leader has a clear, ownable opposite (enterprise→personal, feature-heavy→simple, corporate→rebellious)
  Test: does "we're not [leader]" need zero explanation?

→ Bold Brand Voice (Liquid Death method)
  Requires: almost nothing — voice is completely in your control
  Signal: you can't change the product and can't outspend anyone
  Test: are you willing to alienate a real slice of the market to be remembered by yours?
```

For a WRITER'S OWN PRACTICE: the same matrix applies. Proven offer done poorly = pathway 2 (out-execute it). Crowded service category = pathway 3 (position against the "content mill" default) or pathway 4 (a voice no other consultant would dare use).

### Step 3 — Execution Brief for the Chosen Pathway

**Execute**:

Build the pathway-specific brief:

```
IF BETTER BRAND:
- The feeling the leader fails to deliver: [from Step 1 emotional gap]
- Story, positioning, and identity moves that fill it
- What "third place"-style ecosystem extensions are available beyond the core product?

IF BETTER PRODUCT:
- Top 3 complaints → the genuinely-superior spec for each
- Proof plan: how superiority gets demonstrated, not claimed

IF OPPOSITIONAL POSITIONING:
- The leader's message: [their actual tagline/positioning]
- Your explicit opposite: [claim the other side of the landscape]
- Contrast plan: make the opposition impossible to miss in every asset
- Underdog framing: how the outsider identity gets owned, not apologized for

IF BOLD VOICE:
- Archetype selection: [hero / outlaw / caregiver / explorer] — chosen from what the target audience already responds to, not from taste
- Full commitment spec: voice rules, banned neutral phrasings, sample rewrites of 3 existing assets
- Alienation budget: who this voice will repel, stated explicitly and accepted
```

### Step 4 — The Neutrality Audit

**Execute**:

Whatever pathway was chosen, run the final invisibility check on all messaging produced:

```
NEUTRALITY AUDIT:
- Could the leader's name be swapped into this messaging without anyone noticing? → FAIL, still neutral
- Does this have a point of view someone could disagree with? → required for PASS
- Would a competitor be able to copy the product but NOT this? → the moat test (Brand Voice Consistency Test)
- Risk reframe delivered: alienation vs. invisibility — invisibility always loses
```

---

## Phase 3: Output Contract

**Deliver**:

1. **Market Scan**: Proven concept, pioneer lessons, leader profile, complaint mine (verbatim VOC), emotional gap
2. **Pathway Verdict**: The ONE chosen pathway with the resource-based reasoning, and why the other three were rejected
3. **Execution Brief**: The pathway-specific brief from Step 3, concrete enough to hand to whoever executes
4. **Neutrality Audit Results**: Pass/fail on all four checks with fixes applied

---

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Was the originality question replaced — is the strategy built on a PROVEN concept?
- [ ] Is exactly ONE pathway chosen (no half-measures across pathways)?
- [ ] If Better Product: is superiority genuine (top-3 complaints answered), not cosmetic?
- [ ] If Oppositional: does the positioning borrow the leader's awareness without needing to explain who they are?
- [ ] If Bold Voice: is the alienation budget stated and accepted — or did the voice get sanded back toward neutral?
- [ ] Does the final messaging pass the moat test — copyable product, uncopyable voice/position?
- [ ] Is the complaint mine built from verbatim customer language, not invented complaints?
