# Avery Chen — Cold-Start Personal Brand Creative Direction System

## Behavior Proof

- **Input tested:** a synthetic health-performance expert with accurate but interchangeable talking-head tips, minimal production resources, and no supplied origin story or performance data
- **Weakness diagnosed:** one person is implicitly expected to strategize, operate, produce, and direct; the content has topics but no recurring story system or recognizable world
- **Source mechanics used:** Strategy / Operations / Direction separation; World Over Account; biweekly conversational mining; Topics × Formats × Stories; visual grammar; styling as narrative; distribution as world surface; campaign sequencing
- **Output produced:** bounded 90-day system, three pilot briefs, and explicit specialist handoffs
- **Behavior delta:** "post more evidence-based tips" became an authored field-lab world with repeatable formats, a source-rich Idea Room, an execution owner map, and no-action boundaries
- **Validation run:** `python3 skills/oren-repositioning/tests/verify_personal_brand_creative_direction_system.py`
- **Remaining risk:** founder history, cultural roots, human taste, live performance, collaborator fit, and market outcomes remain `UNKNOWN` or `UNTESTED`

## Executive Verdict

Avery does not need more topics. Avery needs a recognizable operating premise: **the person who takes performance claims out of the abstract and pressure-tests what can survive real implementation.** The current blank-wall tip format signals generic educator. The direction should feel like a calm field lab—evidence on the table, behavior in the room, and every recommendation forced through real constraints.

This is a proposed direction, not a claim about Avery's private identity. The founder must confirm or replace it before production.

## Biweekly Idea Room Slate

The first live session must collect real language and stories. Until then, concepts that require founder history remain blocked.

### Priority

1. **The Claim-to-Constraint Test** — Topic: evidence quality × Format: desk teardown × Story: a supplied public claim, not a client result. Authority job: show how Avery decides what survives implementation.
2. **The Adherence Autopsy** — Topic: behavior change × Format: whiteboard case pattern × Story: a synthetic or permission-cleared scenario. Authority job: reveal where good protocols fail in the handoff to real life.
3. **What I Would Measure First** — Topic: recovery × Format: walk-through at a gym or home office × Story: a public product/protocol example. Authority job: replace certainty theater with decision discipline.

### Middle

- **One / Three / Five Years Ago:** `BLOCKED PENDING FOUNDER HISTORY`.
- **The Tool I Changed My Mind About:** requires a real supplied product and reason.

### Reject

- "Five hacks to recover faster" — naked-tip format, no source story, no distinct judgment.
- AI-written founder confession — invented history and unreviewed voice.

## Personal Brand World Kit

### World Sentence

Evidence leaves the slide deck and meets real constraints.

### Set Logic

- **Primary:** warm home-office field lab; one physical evidence object on the table; paper annotations visible; no sterile neon health-tech look.
- **Alternate:** gym or recovery environment used only when the location materially changes the decision being explained.

### Design Grammar

- Warm neutral field-note base, one high-contrast annotation color, compact serif for claims, utilitarian sans for decisions.
- Recurring on-screen structure: `CLAIM -> CONSTRAINT -> DECISION`.
- Silent-thumbnail test: one evidence object, one handwritten or diagrammatic annotation, one composed human frame; no logo required.

### Styling Axis

Proposed axis: **precise / non-clinical**. Recurring signals: structured dark overshirt, clean neutral base, one practical watch or notebook. No-go: lab-coat cosplay, luxury-performance costume, trend-led gymfluencer uniform. `HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED`.

### Collaborator and Surface Map

- A coach or practitioner can supply the implementation constraint.
- A researcher can clarify the evidence boundary.
- A founder can supply the business tradeoff.
- Owned briefing library preserves the work beyond social accounts.
- Small workshops and recorded constraint sessions create physical/participatory surfaces.
- Outreach, booking, paid amplification, and collaboration contact are `NO PERMISSION`.

## Three Pilot Briefs

### Pilot 1 — The Claim-to-Constraint Test

- **Source:** one public, fully cited claim supplied before production
- **Hook:** "This result is real. The way brands repeat it usually isn't."
- **Authority job:** demonstrate Avery's decision filter
- **Format:** 60-90 second desk teardown
- **Visual premise:** claim printed on paper; constraints annotated in frame; `CLAIM -> CONSTRAINT -> DECISION` lockup
- **Assets:** claim source, printout, notebook, three cutaway annotations
- **Owner:** Avery on camera; editor assembles; human creative owner approves visual choices
- **State:** `DRAFT ONLY`

### Pilot 2 — The Adherence Autopsy

- **Source:** synthetic scenario clearly labeled or a permission-cleared case
- **Hook:** "The protocol didn't fail at the hard part. It failed at Tuesday."
- **Authority job:** show behavior-level diagnosis
- **Format:** whiteboard sequence
- **Visual premise:** timeline with one failure point circled; no generic workout montage
- **State:** `DRAFT ONLY`

### Pilot 3 — What I Would Measure First

- **Source:** supplied public protocol or product example
- **Hook:** "Before I change the plan, I need one signal."
- **Authority job:** model disciplined sequencing
- **Format:** environment walk-through
- **Visual premise:** one real location, one measurement object, one decision rule
- **State:** `DRAFT ONLY`

## 90-Day Direction Plan

### Days 1-30 — Recognition

- Run two Idea Rooms.
- Produce the three pilots.
- Test the set, annotation system, and styling axis across two formats.
- Human review: keep/change/kill on recognition and authority—not views alone.

### Days 31-60 — World

- Add one permission-cleared practitioner conversation.
- Turn one pilot into a four-beat Tease -> Unveil -> Launch -> Sustain sequence.
- Publish only after explicit approval; otherwise maintain `DRAFT ONLY`.

### Days 61-90 — Compounding

- Keep one recurring format, one collaborator surface, and one owned briefing artifact.
- Review production friction, recognition, qualified response, and claim safety separately.
- Market performance remains `UNTESTED` until approved live evidence exists.

## Human Approvals and No-Action Boundaries

- Founder history and cultural roots: approval required.
- Styling and final visual taste: approval required.
- Medical or performance claims: source and claim-safety review required.
- Collaborator outreach, booking, purchases, paid tools, production, and publishing: `NO PERMISSION`.
- Founder voice: no autonomous generation or publishing.

## Proof Plan and Remaining Risk

This cold-start proves the source mechanics change the structure and specificity of the output while preserving unknowns. It does not prove Avery would prefer the direction, that the assets can be produced at the available budget, or that any live audience would respond. Those require founder review, production, approved publication, and measured outcomes.
