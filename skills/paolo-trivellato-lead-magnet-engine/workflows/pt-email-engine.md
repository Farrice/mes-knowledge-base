# /pt-email-engine — Welcome Sequence + Weekly Rhythm

"Once they opt in, they enter the welcome sequence — which is where the real conversion starts." Goal: from *I like your posts* to *I paid you and I trust you with my business*.

## Pre-Flight Gate

- Opt-in flow live or spec'd (`/pt-comment-capture`). Email without the capture front-end is a list of nobody.
- The two CTA destinations exist: a low-cost workshop (~$99) and/or a booking link. Neither → `/pt-workshop-ascension` first or use call-only CTAs.

## Skill Acquisition

- `genius.md` — NURTURE step + eleven-email effect
- `references/source-quotes.md` — E3/E4/E5 subjects verbatim; follow-up angles

## Execution

1. **Welcome sequence — 5 emails over week 1:**
   - **E1 Delivery + orientation**: resource link, short use instructions, one sentence on who you are, soft "here's what's coming."
   - **E2 Your story** (next day): brief background, the moment you saw the pain you now solve, a couple of lessons. **No pitch.**
   - **E3 Quick win** — subject "Fix this in 30 minutes this week": one golden nugget actionable in 24h (dashboard template, one onboarding line that lifts retention) — the 20% for 80%. Mention the workshop as "the next level."
   - **E4 Case study** — subject "From [messy starting point] to [clear outcome] in 60 days": one client story in detail — decisions, mindset, what they feared, who they'd talked to before — so the reader sees themselves. End: check the workshop page.
   - **E5 Clear invite** — subject "Want me to help you implement this": recap who you help, 3 bullets on what you fix, two ways forward (workshop / reply-book a call). Hard CTA.
2. **Weekly rhythm** (after E5): 3 emails/week, same days (e.g. Tue/Thu/Sat) to build habit; 2 value (stories, breakdowns, small frameworks) + 1 direct sell; same tone of voice; every email has a CTA. Early-list stage: shift further toward value.
3. **Every CTA = one of two doors**: $99 workshop ("a lead magnet times five") or book a call / reply ("times ten — direct support, personalized"). Stack perceived value before each sell.
4. **Ascension follow-up** (workshop buyers who didn't upgrade — 3–5 emails over weeks): content upgrade ("here's what you've got, here's how to use it") → social proof ("one workshop client cut their workweek by ten hours") → new capacity ("opening three new spots this month") → new case study ("just got a message from a client...") → honest scarcity (real limit + deadline, never manufactured). Warmest leads: ≥2 personal invitation emails, hand-personalized. **Most upgrades happen here.**

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| No workshop yet | Both doors → call/audit; add workshop when built |
| Newsletter-first operator | Weekly rhythm = the newsletter; E-sequence still fires per new magnet |
| Client-ghostwritten | Story/case-study emails from onboarding interview + client transcripts |
| Farrice | VOICE-CARD + dial; no cheap question sign-offs (feedback memory) |

## Output Requirements

E1–E5 written in full (subjects + bodies), weekly-rhythm template calendar, the 5 ascension-follow-up emails outlined (angles + subjects), CTA map.
Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/welcome-sequence.md — honor its Output Contract.

## Quality Gate

E2 pitch-free? · E3 win achievable in 24h? · E4 reader-sees-themselves detail (decisions/fears, not tools)? · Scarcity claims all true? · Same-days habit rhythm set? · Every email CTA'd to one of the two doors?
