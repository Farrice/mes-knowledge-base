# /pt-content-rotation — Awareness-Stage Content Rotation

The daily-post layer that builds the trust lead magnets alone can't ("you'd only build surface-level trust"). Plans by how the audience FEELS, never by topic pillars.

## Pre-Flight Gate

- Pain map + desire/fear map must exist (from `/pt-lead-engine` MAP or equivalent ICP work). No map → do MAP first; rotation planned without it is reactive content.
- Commitment honest-check: one post per day, **weekdays AND weekends, no exceptions** — Sundays are a top-performing day. If the operator won't commit daily, scale the plan to reality but say the tradeoff out loud (slower format testing → slower growth).

## Skill Acquisition

- `genius.md` — ROTATE step, Emotion Over Information, Decision Framework
- `references/source-quotes.md` — V2 10:15 (anti-pillars), 12:30 (objection-as-hook), 16:23 (emotion)

## Execution

1. **Stage-map the ICP**: problem-unaware / pain-aware / solution-aware (split further if useful). For each: what they don't know yet, what it's costing them, what proof moves them.
2. **Set the mix**: default 20% personal / 40% authority / 30% educational / 10% social proof — or the interview variant (1 lead magnet + 2 posts per awareness stage per week, cycled). Pick per niche sensitivity.
3. **Fill a 7-day calendar** with per-post jobs:
   - *Personal (20%)*: founder journey, values, lessons — relevant to the ICP's world; the unreplicable layer (Hormozi balance: a little, not a lot).
   - *Authority (40%)*: proven frameworks, client-result insights, market opinions — ride industry moments ("Sequoia released a paper → here's why agencies should implement AI"). Go deep, be specific, share the actual system.
   - *Educational (30%)*: teach what they don't fully know; name problems they have but haven't named; awareness-stage mapped.
   - *Social proof (10%)*: transformation arc (before → what was implemented → after); value withheld — "if you want the value, work with me"; DM/profile CTA.
4. **Objection harvesting**: every sales-call objection becomes a solution-aware hook in quotation marks → answered in the post.
5. **Idea sourcing loop**: top-3 competitor scan (last 30 days, top performers — extract underlying principles, never copy) + own-feed/X trend scan 15 min each morning.
6. **Every post's third job**: drive profile views (profile must already be rebuilt — `/pt-profile-funnel`).
7. **Weekly review**: which formats hit → double down (daily posting is the testing cadence).

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| No sales calls yet | Objections from competitor comments + research calls |
| Ghost-written client | Tone-of-voice + stories from onboarding interview; asset library first |
| Farrice's own account | VOICE-CARD + dial mandatory; I-narrative framing per feedback memory |
| Low-frequency niche | Keep mix ratios, shrink volume; never drop personal to 0% |

## Output Requirements

7-day calendar with per-post: type, awareness-stage job, hook draft (objection-hooks in quotes), CTA, profile-drive note. Plus the objection→hook bank (≥5) and competitor-principles notes.
Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/awareness-week-plan.md — honor its Output Contract.

## Quality Gate

Every post has a stage job (no "just value" orphans)? · Sunday filled? · Mix within ±10% of target? · Hooks emotional not informational (desire/fear traceable)? · Zero topic-pillar planning residue?
