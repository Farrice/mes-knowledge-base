# /pt-x-acosta-reach — Reach × Conversion Handshake

Source 2 IS this pairing: Lara Acosta interviewing Paolo because her reach mechanics and his conversion mechanics complete each other. Lara alone: viral posts, thin capture ("I just send them to an email funnel... I don't do DMing"). Paolo alone: booked calls on 40-comment posts, but formats a Lara pass would make travel 10×. Together: her post, his funnel.

## Pre-Flight Gate

- A lead-magnet campaign is planned (pain chosen, resource in build). This handshake styles and converts it — it doesn't pick the pain.
- Both skills load or no handshake: `lara-acosta-*` (4-3-2-1 system, rehook architecture, F-shape formatting) + this skill's genius.md.

## The Handoff Contract

**Paolo's pass outputs** (conversion spine — fixed):
```
AUTHORITY LINE: [≤8 words, full stop]
PAIN (ICP-verbatim): [the one pain + its first-person quotes]
RESOURCE PROMISE: [bad state → desired state without annoying-thing]
FOMO STACK: [1–2 honest lines]
GATE: [keyword] · CAPTURE: [opt-in flow + DM scripts] · SEQUENCE: [E1–E5 ready?]
```
**Lara's pass consumes that spine and returns** (craft layer):
```
HOOK REWRITE: [rehook-architecture pass on line 1–2 — authority line survives verbatim]
BODY FORMAT: [F-shape/visual formatting, edu-telling translation]
FORMAT CALL: [which of her proven viral formats carries this]
```
The conversion spine is non-negotiable: craft may reshape everything EXCEPT the authority line's position, the one-pain rule, the keyword gate (no links), and the FOMO honesty. If a craft suggestion breaks a spine element, the spine wins and the dissent is logged.

## Execution

1. Build the spine via `/pt-lead-magnet-post` steps 1–6 (draft grade).
2. Load `skills/lara-acosta-linkedin-growth/` (or `-content-system`) genius; run her craft pass over the draft per the contract.
3. Reconcile: apply craft, restore any broken spine element, log dissent side-by-side (one author per body — never stitch; pick the winning take per feedback memory).
4. Ship through `/pt-comment-capture` unchanged.
