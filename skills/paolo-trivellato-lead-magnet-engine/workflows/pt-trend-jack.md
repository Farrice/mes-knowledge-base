# /pt-trend-jack — Name-Jacking Lead Magnet

"Using something which already has thousands of eyeballs on it to your advantage, and then tying it into what you sell." His agency's AI-creative-strategy magnet: ~3,000 comments; ~7,000 across 4 variations (self-reported).

## Pre-Flight Gate

- **The trend must be live NOW** (attention pool full: LLM launch, viral tool, major funding/acquisition in the ICP's industry). Stale trend → normal `/pt-lead-magnet-post` instead. LinkedIn lags X — if it's already saturated on LinkedIn, you're late.
- **The tie-in must be real**: one step of YOUR actual service delivery must genuinely map to the trend. Forced tie-in = ChatGPT-slop territory.

## Execution

1. **Trend intake**: what launched, why the ICP cares, the 1-line mechanism of the attention pool (new + relevant + everyone's testing it). Radar: key X accounts 15 min each morning; company announcements; funding rounds. (Harness shortcut: `/hunt-trends`, `/last30days`, daily zeitgeist briefs.)
2. **The recipe** (his, verbatim logic): pick ONE prompt/SOP step that automates part of your service delivery — you already have these → package it as the giveaway → **name the trending tool in the hook** ("mentioning Claude in the hook, mentioning how much time it saves — overpowered").
3. **Write the post** via `/pt-lead-magnet-post` mechanics: authority line intact, pain still ICP-verbatim, trend named in hook, keyword gate. The trend buys reach; the 3 ingredients still do the converting.
4. **Resource** via `/pt-lead-magnet-resource`: the actual prompt with input slots + 3-line usage guide + example output. Copy-paste ceiling is mandatory here — instant output IS the trend's promise.
5. **Variation plan**: if it hits, ship 2–3 back-to-back variations (his 7,000-comment pattern) before the pool drains.
6. **Capture unchanged**: full `/pt-comment-capture` flow — trend traffic is colder; the email sequence does the warming.
