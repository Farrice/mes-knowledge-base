# /pt-boring-offer-audit — Engine Gap Audit

Scores an existing LinkedIn motion against the eight spine components and the Kyle before-state ("great offer, invisible founder, zero predictable inbound"). LENS mode: verdicts on THEIR asset, not a method lecture.

## Pre-Flight Gate

- Access to the actual profile + last 30 days of posts (screenshots, exports, or live view). No access → request it; never audit from imagination.
- Establish the offer + ICP in one line each first — verdicts are relative to who they sell to.

## Skill Acquisition

- `genius.md` — full spine + Anti-Patterns + Quality Rubric
- `references/kyle-hunt-case-study.md` — before-state checklist

## Execution

1. **Before-state check** (Kyle baseline): presence? content system? audience? inbound pipeline? mechanism to surface expertise? Mark each.
2. **Component-by-component verdict** (KEEP / FIX / MISSING, with the one-line reason and the strongest evidence quoted from their actual material):
   - MAP: is content traceable to real ICP pains in ICP language?
   - PROFILE: headline formula? button? featured funnel? About sales letter + one-word CTA? Generic-flashy phrases present?
   - ROTATE: daily? Sundays? mix vs 20/40/30/10? awareness-stage jobs or topic pillars?
   - MAGNET: 3 ingredients present in recent magnets? one-pain rule? FOMO honest?
   - GATE: comment keyword vs links-in-post?
   - CAPTURE: opt-in interposed or resource dumped in DMs? list growing?
   - NURTURE: welcome sequence? weekly rhythm? two doors?
   - ASCEND: low-ticket narrow promise? follow-up window worked?
3. **Anti-pattern scan**: flag every § Anti-Patterns hit with the specific post/line.
4. **Fix order**: rank by revenue proximity — capture leaks before content polish (a viral post with no capture = "your audience is dead"). Name the top 3 moves with expected mechanism.
5. **Dissent preserved**: where the operator's current approach plausibly beats Paolo's default for their niche (e.g. no-DM operators funnel straight to email — Lara's model), say so explicitly rather than forcing the full engine.
