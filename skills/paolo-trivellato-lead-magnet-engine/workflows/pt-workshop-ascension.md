# /pt-workshop-ascension — Low-Ticket → Consulting Trust Stack

The monetization spine: $100 workshop filters and pre-pays trust; consulting upgrade happens in the follow-up window. "The upgrade conversation is not a sales call — the experience did the selling."

## Pre-Flight Gate

- A high-ticket backend must exist (DWY/DFY at $k's/mo). Workshop with no backend = a $100 product business, different economics.
- **One narrow problem picked** — a sliver of the ICP, ideally the main vertical. "Solve one narrow problem, not everything."
- Price sanity: low enough the qualified buyer doesn't think hard; high enough to filter non-payers ($100 logic: who won't pay $100 won't pay $1,000).

## Execution

1. **Write the One Narrow Promise** in the exact-wording form: "In [timeframe] I will help you [build one concrete artifact] so you never [named pain emotion] again." Bar: *"In 2 hours I will help you build a simple weekly dashboard so you never feel blind about your agency performance again."* Clear before-state and after-state named.
2. **Spec the front offer**: ~$100; what they come in with → what they walk out with (the system, the resource, the personalized plan — list it).
3. **In-workshop arc**: deliver the promised outcome LIVE with clear templates → show what "doing this every week" looks like long-term → Q&A on their specific situations (also your market-research goldmine) → **naturally point out where more help would speed them up** → 2-minute pitch max ("if you want support building out the rest... here's how we work together") → follow-up email same day.
4. **Backend framing**: "I solved this one thing for you. Want me to solve the other 10 things in the same department?" DWY/DFY, few $k/mo. Upgrade conversation = diagnosis ("what else are you dealing with?"), never a pitch.
5. **Trust-stack check** (why it converts): already paid → already trust → already saw the methodology deliver → results surface new problems → door open. Even without instant results, real operators foresee the ROI of an implemented system.
6. **Follow-up sequence** (where most upgrades happen): 3–5 emails over weeks per `/pt-email-engine` step 4; warmest leads get personal invitations with matched case studies.
