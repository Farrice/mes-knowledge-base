# /pt-comment-capture — Comment→Email Capture + DM Close

"The step most LinkedIn strategies skip entirely." Converts comment volume into an owned list and booked calls.

## Pre-Flight Gate

- A live (or imminent) lead-magnet post with a keyword. No post → `/pt-lead-magnet-post`.
- **Opt-in page must exist before the post goes live** (headline, preview screenshot, name+email only — no phone). No page → build spec first (step 3).
- Ops capacity decided: manual time block (30–60 min/day per ~100 comments), VA on the account, or automation tool **at own risk** (his caveat, verbatim).

## Skill Acquisition

- `genius.md` — GATE + CAPTURE + DM Close sections
- `references/source-quotes.md` — both DM scripts verbatim; V2 5:42–8:27 (close sequence)

## Execution

1. **Comment reply**: "Hey [Name], check your DMs." (Also: ask people to connect before commenting where natural — commenters must connect to receive DMs; connections auto-follow.)
2. **DM 1 — delivery** (adapt the verbatim script): thanks-for-commenting [WORD] → "short Notion guide so it's easy to use" → opt-in link → "drop your email in and it will send automatically. Enjoy." Warm, personal, maybe light humor — never "business transaction" tone.
   - Already-on-list variant: new one sent to the same email; check promotions/spam.
   - Refusal: send the resource directly. "You're only losing a subscriber."
3. **DM 2 — curiosity opener** (after delivery, or next day): "Just curious — what caught your attention?" Silent → follow up once with the same question, softer.
4. **DM 3 — challenge dig**: acknowledge their answer → "Are you facing [pain A] or [pain B] right now?" (same pains as the post).
5. **DM 4 — bridge + proof**: "Funnily enough, I help people just like you" + one-line case study drop.
6. **DM 5 — assume the yes**: never "want to book a call?" — offer a concrete slot: "I have [day] at [time] open — I'll send the invite." 3–5 messages total; not booked by 5 → stop; the email sequence continues the nurture. Never pitch-slap before the challenge is revealed.
7. **Email hand-off confirmed**: opt-in triggers welcome E1 (see /pt-email-engine).

## Content Type Adaptations

| Situation | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| High-volume viral post (1k+ comments) | Batch replies in time blocks; VA or automation decision escalates |
| Spam-sensitive/enterprise ICP | Soften close: replace slot-offer with "worth a 15-min chat? here's my calendar" |
| No-DM operator (Lara-style) | Skip DMs 2–5; funnel rides purely on opt-in → email sequence |
| Client account | Scripts rewritten in client's voice from onboarding interview |

## Output Requirements

Capture kit: comment-reply line, DM 1 (+on-list variant, +refusal fallback), DM 2–5 close sequence with branch notes, opt-in page spec, ops runbook (who replies, when, tooling).
Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/dm-capture-kit.md — honor its Output Contract.

## Quality Gate

Opt-in interposed (resource never in DM 1)? · Fallbacks present (refusal, on-list, silent)? · Close assumes the yes with a concrete slot? · ≤5 messages? · Tone passes the not-a-transaction test?
