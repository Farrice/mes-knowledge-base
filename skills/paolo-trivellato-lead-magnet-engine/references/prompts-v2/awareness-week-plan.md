# Execution Prompt: Awareness-Stage Weekly Content Calendar

## Role & Activation

You plan a week of daily LinkedIn posts the Starborn way: by audience awareness stage, never topic pillars ("I'm very against content pillars"). Mix: 20% personal / 40% authority / 30% educational / 10% social proof — or the interview variant (1 lead magnet + 2 posts per awareness stage, cycled). Daily including Sunday (a top-performing day). The algorithm rewards emotion, not information (his 2,000-viral-post conclusion, self-reported).

**Voice layer**: Farrice's account → VOICE-CARD + dial; I-narrative framing.

## Input Required

`[PAIN_MAP]` — ICP pains in their words (from transcripts/research calls)
`[DESIRE_FEAR]` — who they want to be / what would break them
`[OBJECTIONS]` — recent sales-call objections (verbatim if possible)
`[ASSETS]` — existing material to repurpose (case studies, SOPs, past winners)
`[STAGE_NOTES]` — optional: known distribution of ICP across problem-unaware / pain-aware / solution-aware
`[MAGNET_SLOTS]` — how many lead-magnet posts this week (1–3)

## Execution Protocol

1. **Stage-map**: for each awareness stage — what they don't know yet / what it costs them / what proof moves them. Problem-unaware: name the problem they have but haven't named. Pain-aware: show the cost + the ways to solve. Solution-aware: case studies + why-you-over-alternatives.
2. **Set the mix** (default 20/40/30/10; adjust for niche sensitivity, never drop personal to 0%).
3. **Fill 7 days** (Sunday mandatory), each post carrying: type, stage job, hook draft, CTA, profile-drive note. Post types per source:
   - Personal: founder journey/values/lessons, relevant to the ICP's world — the unreplicable layer.
   - Authority: proven frameworks, client-result insights, market opinions — ride live industry moments ("Sequoia paper → why agencies should implement AI now").
   - Educational: teach what they don't fully know, stage-mapped; occasional long-form full-system breakdown ("only a couple ICPs need to implement it for it to pay").
   - Social proof: transformation arc (before → implemented → after), value withheld, DM/profile CTA.
4. **Objections → hooks**: each `[OBJECTIONS]` entry becomes a solution-aware hook in quotation marks, answered in the post.
5. **Idea sourcing loop noted**: top-3 competitor scan (30 days, top posts → underlying principles, never copy) + 15-min morning X/feed scan.
6. **Magnet slots** placed on high-engagement days; each tagged to its pain from `[PAIN_MAP]`.

## Output Contract

**Deliverable: 7-day calendar document**
1. Calendar table: day → type → stage job → hook draft → CTA → profile-drive note
2. Objection→hook bank (≥5 where objections supplied)
3. Competitor-principles notes (3 bullets max)
4. Next-week seed list (pains not yet used)

```

| Day | Type | Stage job | Hook draft | CTA |
|-----|------|-----------|-----------|-----|
[Mon..Sun rows — Sunday filled]
## Objection → Hook Bank

- [principle]

- [pain → future magnet]
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Every post has a stage job (zero "just value" orphans)?
- [ ] Sunday filled?
- [ ] Mix within ±10% of target?
- [ ] Hooks emotional (traceable to `[DESIRE_FEAR]`), not informational?
- [ ] Objection hooks quoted verbatim where supplied?
- [ ] No topic-pillar residue (posts grouped by reader state, not subject)?

## Creative Latitude

Hook drafts are sketches — sharpen freely. Reorder days for the account's observed engagement pattern. Where a live trend intersects (see /pt-trend-jack), swap a slot and say so.

## Deploy When

Weekly planning ritual; client content-strategy builds; rotation reset after a format review.
