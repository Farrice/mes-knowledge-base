# The Kyle Hunt Case Study — Full Documented Funnel

The forge's calibration case: video 1 (`mJYiaQDoLVk`) documents Starborn's build for Kyle Hunt end-to-end, with on-screen artifacts. All numbers self-reported by Paolo (Stripe/analytics screenshots shown on stream are creator-supplied).

## Before (on-screen "Kyle's Starting Point" card, verbatim)
- Zero LinkedIn presence · No content system · No audience · No inbound pipeline
- "Offer is genuinely unsexy — no flashy income claims, no viral hooks baked into the product"
- "Had expertise and results but no mechanism to get those results in front of the right people consistently"
- Core problem line: **"great offer, invisible founder, zero predictable inbound"**
- Context: ex-COO of a very successful (8-figure) agency; selling agency operations — "the most boring niche on the internet" (his own phrase). Was closing a few hundred-dollar/mo community deals.

## After (on-screen results card)
- **$88,984.92 in one month** · **93% margins** · **+129%** vs previous period ($38,859.36) — "not a spike, a system that compounds" · **$0** paid ads, zero cold outreach
- Packages $3k–30k, sold to people who had only seen LinkedIn posts
- Closing line on the card: *"An operations consultant. A genuinely unsexy offer. $89k a month with 93% margins. The offer did not change. The system did."*
- End-state color: Kyle asked them to STOP posting lead magnets — capacity full, protecting family time.

## Who this transfers to (on-screen qualification card)
1. An agency operator or consultant · 2. Selling something "boring" — systems, operations, finance, process · 3. Relying on LinkedIn for leads · 4. Already good at what you do but not getting enough qualified inbound.
Best-fit target industries on LinkedIn: software, agencies, e-commerce, coaches/consultants.

## The five system components (as delivered)
1. **Lead Magnet System** — "turned likes and comments into booked revenue"; 2–3 magnets/week at scale; single posts at 1,000–4,000 comments; one post ≈ month's pipeline when it hits.
2. **Post Planning** — planned in advance against Kyle's ICP pain-point map, "not reactive."
3. **Resource Structure** — must be (a) genuinely valuable and (b) built to convert (CTAs inside).
4. **Comment → Email** — every commenter moved onto an owned email list, then into paid offers.
5. **30-Day Action Plan** — the DIY replication path (the skill's `pt-lead-engine` workflow).

## Kyle's ICP pain map (each became its own lead magnet — ICP-verbatim)
- "I cannot step out of client work without things breaking."
- "Delivery is messy and every client gets a different experience."
- "Our margins disappear as soon as we grow past five or six team members."
- "I have no weekly dashboard, so I'm flying blind."

## The hook system
Authority line opener on 10–15+ consecutive magnets: **"I am an eight-figure agency COO."** (full stop) → e.g. "You can scale to 500K a month and remove yourself from the business." → pain descent in ICP dialect ("Right now you're probably trying to track dashboards, but you're not able to...").

## Economics note (his paid-ads comparison)
One post reached 1.8M impressions (self-reported). His argument: matching even 10% of that (~180k ICP impressions) on LinkedIn ads — one of the most expensive platforms — costs "easily multi five figures."
