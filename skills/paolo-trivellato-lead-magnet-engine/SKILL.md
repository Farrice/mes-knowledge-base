## Quick Reference (the spine)

**MAP → PROFILE → ROTATE → MAGNET → GATE → CAPTURE → NURTURE → ASCEND**

- Authority line first, ≤8 words, full stop ("I am an eight-figure agency COO.")
- One resource = one main pain (plaster model); pains in the ICP's first-person words.
- Copy-paste resource shape; 20% that gets 80%; Claude-prompt = current ceiling.
- Keyword comment gate, no links until taper; commenters connect → auto-follow.
- DM the opt-in page, never the resource; refusal → send it anyway.
- Assume the yes with a concrete slot; 3–5 messages max.
- Daily posts incl. **Sundays**; 20/40/30/10 mix; awareness stages, not pillars.
- $100 workshop, One Narrow Promise exact wording; upgrades land in the follow-up window.

## Anti-Patterns (reject on sight)

Sexy-hook cosplay on boring offers · broad "ultimate guides" · 50-page PDFs · ChatGPT-slop giveaways · links in fresh posts · resource in the DM · pitch-slapping · asking instead of assuming the yes · >5-message threads · topic pillars · skipped Sundays · manufactured scarcity · free-trial clients · hard-selling inside the workshop · self-reported numbers presented as verified.
