## Core Truth

**A boring offer is a credibility asset — if you replace the viral hooks it can't use with a complete conversion machine.** The offer doesn't change; the system does. Every post has a funnel job; the money is made in the steps AFTER the post: DM script → opt-in page → email sequence → $100 workshop → consulting. "Take someone from *I like your posts* to *I paid you and I trust you with my business*."

## The Spine

**MAP → PROFILE → ROTATE → MAGNET → GATE → CAPTURE → NURTURE → ASCEND**

1. **MAP** — ICP pains from call transcripts / 50×15-min paid research calls. Each pain, in the ICP's first-person words, becomes its own future resource. Also map desire (who they want to be) and fear (what would break them): the algorithm rewards emotion, not information (his conclusion from 2,000+ viral posts, self-reported).
2. **PROFILE** — rebuild as a landing page. Headline = outcome + timeframe + mechanism + authority statement ("Ex 8-figure agency COO — helping agencies fix operations and scale to $10M/yr"). Custom button (Premium) → calendar = highest-visibility element. Featured section → opt-in/lead magnet for the curious. About = mini sales letter: ICP call-out → pain → mechanism → proof bullets (client quotes, numbers) → **one-word DM CTA** (About can't hold clickable links — zero-friction CTA only). Two paths: high-intent → button; curious → featured → email. "A broken landing page just burns the audience you are building."
3. **ROTATE** — one post daily, **no exceptions including Sundays** (a top-performing day; everyone else skips it). Mix: 20% personal (founder journey, values, relevant life — the unreplicable layer; B2B readers buy from people), 40% authority (proven frameworks, client-result insights, market opinions — e.g. ride a Sequoia paper into "here's why agencies should implement AI"), 30% educational (teach what they don't fully know, mapped to awareness stages), 10% social proof (transformation arc, value withheld: "if you want the value you need to work with me" + DM/profile CTA). Anti-content-pillars: plan by **awareness stage** (problem-unaware / pain-aware / solution-aware), not topic. Objections from sales calls become solution-aware hooks in quotation marks. Daily posting = testing cadence: faster format discovery → double down.
4. **MAGNET** — 1–3 lead-magnet posts/week. **Three ingredients, in order**:
   - **Authority line** (≤8 words, full stop, rare + relevant): *"I am an eight-figure agency COO."* Used on 10–15+ consecutive magnets; named the #1 success factor.
   - **Pain description** in ICP dialect until "this person is in my head": *"Right now you're probably trying to track dashboards, but you're not able to. Right now you're probably having to put out fires every week."*
   - **Copy-paste solution offer**: "I put the solution into a simple guide they can use today." One resource = ONE main pain (plaster-on-scar model). Resource shape: blueprint/template/prompt — the 20% that gets 80%; nobody reads 50 pages of plain text. Claude-prompt magnets are the current viral ceiling (lazy-user physics: paste → instant output).
   - Then **perceived-value/FOMO stack** just before CTA (honest, followed-through): "we only give this to paid clients" / "my team won't be happy" / "taking it down in 24 hours."
   - **Post skeleton (verbatim on-screen template)**: *"I spent X years as [role] at a [size] agency. The [stage] was [pain]. So I wrote a step by step [resource] that takes you from [bad state] to [desired state] without [annoying thing]. Comment [WORD] and I will send it."*
5. **GATE** — one keyword comment CTA, **no link while the post climbs** (links throttle reach; add the link only after virality tapers, to convert stragglers). Comment physics: each comment shows the post to that commenter's network (spider-web); commenters must **connect to receive the DM** → connections auto-follow → highest follower-converting content type. Reply to each comment "check your DMs" (30–60 min daily time block, VA, or automation at own risk).
6. **CAPTURE** — DM the **opt-in page, not the resource**. Word-for-word script (on-screen artifact): *"Hey [Name], thanks for commenting 'KYLE' on my post. I put the breakdown into a short Notion guide so it's easy to use. You can grab it here: [opt-in link]. Once you drop your email in it will send the guide automatically. Enjoy."* Already-on-list variant: *"Just saw you already grabbed one of my resources before. The new one has been sent to the same email. If you do not see it, check promotions or spam."* Opt-in page: headline + resource preview screenshot + name/email only. Refusal → send the resource directly ("you're only losing a subscriber"). Rationale: LinkedIn is rented, email is owned; 30k followers ≠ 30k views, email = guaranteed delivery.
7. **NURTURE** — welcome sequence, 5 emails / first week: **E1** delivery + orientation (resource link, how to use, one line on who you are, what's coming). **E2** your story (trust; relevant background; **no pitch**). **E3** quick win — subject: *"Fix this in 30 minutes this week"* — one actionable nugget, result within 24h. **E4** case study — subject: *"From [messy starting point] to [clear outcome] in 60 days"* — decisions, fears, mindset (reader sees themselves in the client). **E5** clear invite — subject: *"Want me to help you implement this"* — recap who you help, 3 bullets, two ways forward (workshop or call). Then weekly rhythm: 3 emails/week, same days (e.g. Tue/Thu/Sat), 2 value + 1 direct sell. Every CTA → $99 workshop (filter) or book a call. Two weeks on the list ≈ 11 emails → "the sale is almost made before the pitch lands."
8. **ASCEND** — **$100 workshop → core consulting.** Front offer: $100 workshop solving ONE narrow operations problem — "low enough that people do not overthink it, high enough to filter out anyone who just wouldn't pay" ("who won't pay $100 won't pay $1,000" — and paying once makes paying again easy). **One Narrow Promise, exact-wording bar**: *"In 2 hours I will help you build a simple weekly dashboard so you never feel blind about your agency performance again."* (timeframe + artifact + named emotion + "again"). Inside: deliver the outcome live with templates → show what "doing this every week" looks like → naturally point out where more help would speed them up → 2-min pitch + email after. **The Trust Stack**: they've already paid, already trust, already saw the methodology deliver; once they get a result, new problems appear → door opens for ongoing consulting; "the upgrade conversation is not a sales call — the experience did the selling." Follow-up: 3–5 emails over weeks — content upgrade / social proof ("one workshop client cut their workweek by ten hours") / new capacity ("three new spots this month") / new case study / **honest scarcity** (real capacity limit + deadline, "not manufactured"). Warmest leads: ≥2 personal invitation emails. **Most upgrades happen in this follow-up window**, not at the workshop's end.

## DM Close (assume the yes)

Opener after delivery: *"Just curious — what caught your attention?"* (72 calls in 26 days for a cold-email-agency client, self-reported; follow up once if silent). → one deeper question on their current challenge → *"Funnily enough, I help people just like you"* + case study → **assume the yes**: don't ask for a call, offer a concrete slot/date. 3–5 messages total, never 10. Fear check: if you're actually good, selling = helping.

## Trend/Name-Jacking

Ride attention pools that are already full: LLM launches, funding rounds, tool trends. Recipe he gives away: take one prompt that automates a step of YOUR service delivery (you already have these) → package as a giveaway tied to the trending tool → mention the tool in the hook ("overpowered"). Trend radar: follow key X accounts 15 min every morning (X moves faster than LinkedIn); his agency uses 50 client home pages as sensors — solo operators use their own feed. Case-study lead magnet paradox: lowest virality, highest revenue/impression ("tens of thousands of impressions on a sales asset is unheard of").

## Cold-Start Doctrine

Treat yourself as your first client (self-serving proposition — your account answers "how do I know this works?"). First case study > early revenue. Never free-trial clients (uncommitted, won't continue); **pay-on-performance**: "$100 per meeting booked" or "free until 1,000 followers / 10 meetings, then $X/mo retainer." A B2B meeting is worth $50–300. Put the first case study literally everywhere: website, headline, featured pin, post, AND a lead-magnet breakdown of how you achieved it. Conviction transfers on sales calls — the case study funds the conviction.

## Decision Framework

- Offer boring / no income claims available? → lean INTO it: authority line + specificity (never manufacture sexy hooks).
- Lead magnet broad? → cut to ONE pain. The other pains are next week's magnets.
- Resource >20% overhead for the 80% result? → compress to blueprint/prompt/template.
- Link in a fresh post? → never. Keyword comment gate; link after taper only.
- DM contains the resource? → wrong: opt-in page first, resource by email, direct-send only on refusal.
- Asking "want to book a call?" → wrong: assume the yes, give a slot. Past message 5? → stop; nurture by email.
- Selling the consulting on the workshop call? → wrong: 2-min pitch max; the follow-up emails do the ascension.
- Scarcity claim you can't honor? → cut it. FOMO must be true or become true.

## Quality Rubric

| Criterion | 4 | 7 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authority line | present | rare + relevant, ≤8 words, full stop | reader stops scrolling ("8-figure agency COO") |
| Pain | named | ICP dialect, present tense | transcript-verbatim, "in my head" |
| Resource | useful | step-by-step blueprint | copy-paste, win in 24h |
| FOMO stack | CTA present | 1–2 honest exclusivity lines | true AND followed through; skeptics comment anyway |
| Funnel | post only | comment→DM→resource | comment→connect→DM→opt-in→email→sequence, scripted |
| Ascension | offer mentioned | narrow $100 promise, before/after | exact-wording promise; upgrades land in follow-up |
| Humanity | no AI tells | desire/fear mapped | personal layer makes the B2B reader buy the person |

## Anti-Patterns (reject on sight — each anchored to source, both videos 2026-07 capture)

- **Broad "ultimate guide" magnets** — violates the one-pain rule: "One resource should solve exactly one painful problem. Pick one. Ignore everything else for that week." (V1 ~13:10 on-screen Rule card)
- **50-page plain-text PDFs / ChatGPT-slop giveaways** — "Nobody is going to go through like 50 pages of just plain text" (V2 3:28); "people are just giving away ChatGPT slop or irrelevant info — there's just not enough quality" (V2 2:44).
- **Links in fresh posts** — "links are literally detrimental... you can put a link in only after it's gone viral" (V1 17:25).
- **Resource sent in the DM (no capture)** — the DM carries the opt-in page, not the resource; "you don't want to be that guy who has a viral post and all of a sudden you don't have any contact info and your audience is dead" (V1 3:21).
- **Pitch-slapping / asking for the call** — "it's still going to feel like you're pitch-slapping them if you do it too early" (V2 7:43); close by assume-the-yes with a slot, "three to maximum five messages" (V2 8:13).
- **Content pillars by topic** — "I'm very against content pillars... think about the awareness stage of your audience instead" (V2 10:15).
- **Skipping Sundays** — "Sundays are actually a huge day for engagement... definitely do not miss out on Sundays" (V1 5:35).
- **Manufactured scarcity** — follow-up scarcity email spec reads "States that clearly with a deadline. Not manufactured." (V1 ~29:00 on-screen card); FOMO claims must be followed through (V1 16:59).
- **Free-trial clients** — "you get people which are not committed, they don't care about you... do pay on performance" (V2 25:15).
- **Hard-selling inside the workshop** — pitch is "maybe like a 2-minute pitch" at the end; "the upgrade conversation is not a sales call — the experience did the selling" (V1 26:55, ~28:00 card).
- **Generic-flashy profile phrases** — "if you say that you're a dad, a serial entrepreneur, or any of these flashy generic phrases... it's really not going to help you" (V1 12:21).
- **Presenting self-reported numbers as verified** — quarantine above (extraction decision 2026-07-21, per jeremy-haynes precedent).
