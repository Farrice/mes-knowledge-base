# AI Readiness Scorecard

> Load `genius.md` before executing. This is the "lighter" version of the full diagnostic — a pipeline builder, not a product.

## When to Use
- A prospect is interested but not ready to commit to a 2-week audit
- You want to offer a free or low-cost ($0-$5K) entry point for lead generation
- Testing a new vertical before investing in a full vertical playbook
- A warm contact asks "what would working together look like?"
- You want to productize this as a self-serve digital product ($497-$997)

## Steps

### 1. Scorecard Setup

**Format**: 60-90 minute structured interview OR self-serve questionnaire (for productized version).

**Six Assessment Dimensions** (each scored 1-10):

| Dimension | What It Measures | Key Questions |
|-----------|-----------------|---------------|
| **1. Data Foundation** | Is data centralized, clean, and accessible? | "How many systems hold customer data? Could you pull all of it into one spreadsheet?" |
| **2. Process Maturity** | Are workflows documented and standardized? | "If your best employee quit tomorrow, could someone replicate their daily tasks from documentation alone?" |
| **3. Technology Stack** | Are current tools connected and modern? | "How many tools does your team use daily? How many of them talk to each other automatically?" |
| **4. Team Readiness** | Is the team open to AI adoption? | "On a scale of 1-10, how would your team react to 'we're implementing AI next month'?" |
| **5. Leadership Alignment** | Is there an AI champion with budget authority? | "Who in your organization would own an AI initiative? Do they have budget authority?" |
| **6. ROI Opportunity** | How much waste exists in current operations? | "What's the one process your team complains about most? How many people touch it daily?" |

### 2. Conduct the Assessment

**For live interviews (recommended for $2K-$5K engagements):**

Spend 10-15 minutes per dimension. Use Andrew's "Then What?" cascade for Dimension 2 and 6 — these are where the gold is.

**Scoring Rubric:**

| Score | Level | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1-3 | **Foundation** | Significant gaps. AI projects will fail without foundational work first |
| 4-6 | **Developing** | Some readiness. Quick wins are possible but major implementations need prep |
| 7-8 | **Ready** | Strong foundation. Can move directly to diagnostic + implementation |
| 9-10 | **Advanced** | Already leveraging AI. Opportunity is in optimization and scaling |

### 3. Calculate the Readiness Score

**Weighted Formula:**

```
Readiness Score = (Data × 0.25) + (Process × 0.20) + (Tech × 0.15) +
                  (Team × 0.15) + (Leadership × 0.15) + (ROI Opportunity × 0.10)
```

Weights reflect reality: without data foundation and process maturity, nothing else matters.

**Interpretation:**

| Score Range | Grade | Recommendation |
|-------------|-------|---------------|
| 1.0 – 3.0 | **F – Not Ready** | Foundation building required (data centralization, process documentation). Engagement: consulting on fundamentals, not AI |
| 3.1 – 5.0 | **D – Early Stage** | Quick wins possible in isolated processes. Full diagnostic recommended to identify low-hanging fruit |
| 5.1 – 7.0 | **B – Ready** | Strong candidate for diagnostic + implementation. Multiple ROI opportunities exist |
| 7.1 – 9.0 | **A – Advanced** | Ready for full transformation. Focus on optimization and scaling existing AI |
| 9.1 – 10.0 | **S – Leading** | Advisory relationship — help them stay ahead. Fractional AI strategist role |

### 4. Quick ROI Estimate

Even in 90 minutes, surface ONE quantifiable waste. Use the ROI formula:

```
[Identified Process] × [People Involved] × [Minutes Wasted/Day] × [Working Days/Year] × [Hourly Cost/60]
= Annual Waste on THIS ONE PROCESS
```

Example output:
```
QUICK ROI ESTIMATE
Process: Manual lead qualification
People: 6 sales reps
Time Wasted: 45 min/day per rep
Working Days: 260
Hourly Cost: $35 (loaded)

Annual Waste: 6 × 45 × 260 × ($35/60) = $245,700/year

Note: This is ONE process. A full diagnostic typically reveals 5-8 similar opportunities.
```

**Output**: 2-3 page report (not a deck — reports feel more substantial for a lightweight assessment).

```
OVERALL SCORE: [X.X / 10] — Grade: [Letter]

DIMENSION SCORES:

■■■■■■□□□□  Technology Stack: 6/10
■■■■■□□□□□  Team Readiness: 5/10
■■■■■■■■□□  Leadership Alignment: 8/10
■■■■■■■■■□  ROI Opportunity: 9/10

[One sentence — the biggest insight from the assessment]

QUICK ROI ESTIMATE:
[The waste figure from Step 4]

RECOMMENDATION:
[One of the 5 grade-based recommendations from Step 3]

[Specific: "A 2-week diagnostic sprint focused on [department/process]
would map all opportunities and build a prioritized implementation roadmap.
Investment: $[X]. Expected ROI: $[X] within 12 months."]
```

### 6. Conversion Bridge

The scorecard's purpose is to convert to a full diagnostic. The conversion mechanism:

- **Score 1-3**: "You need foundation work first. I can scope a data centralization project."
- **Score 4-6**: "There's clear opportunity here. A full diagnostic would map every dollar you're leaving on the table. Based on the quick ROI estimate, the diagnostic pays for itself 3-5x."
- **Score 7-9**: "You're ready to move. I'd recommend jumping straight to a diagnostic + quick win implementation."
- **Score 9-10**: "You don't need a diagnostic — you need a strategic advisor. Let me show you our advisory retainer structure."

**Conversion stats (research-validated):**
- Scorecard → Full Diagnostic: ~65-70% conversion when quick ROI estimate exceeds $100K
- Free Scorecard → Any Paid Engagement: ~40-50% conversion
