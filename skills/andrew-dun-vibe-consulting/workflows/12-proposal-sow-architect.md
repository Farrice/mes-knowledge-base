# Proposal & Scope of Work Architect

> Load `genius.md` before executing. Research: phased proposals close at 65% vs. 30% for flat quotes.

## When to Use
- After delivering a diagnostic presentation (Workflow 05) and the client says "let's move forward"
- When a prospect asks for a "proposal" or "quote"
- When scoping a new engagement from scratch
- When converting a free/backend diagnostic into a paid implementation contract

## Steps

### 1. Engagement Intelligence Intake

Before writing a single word, gather:

```
CLIENT: [Company name]
DECISION MAKER: [Name, title]
BUDGET SIGNAL: [What have they said about budget? "A couple thousand" vs. "we have budget"]
URGENCY: [Timeline pressure? Fiscal year? Board deadline?]
DIAGNOSTIC FINDINGS: [Link to completed audit or key findings]
TOP 3 PAIN POINTS: [From the diagnostic, ranked by annual waste $]
AI CHAMPION: [Who will push this through internally?]
COMPETITORS: [Are they talking to other consultants?]
```

### 2. Engagement Architecture (Phase Design)

**Never propose a single monolithic engagement.** Break every proposal into 2-4 phases with independent value at each stage.

**Phase Framework:**

| Phase | Name | Duration | What Client Gets | Price Range |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|-------------|
| **0** | AI Readiness Scorecard | 2-4 hours | Maturity score + top 3 opportunities + go/no-go recommendation | $0–$5K (or completed) |
| **1** | Diagnostic Sprint | 1-2 weeks | Full process audit + ROI quantification + opportunity matrix + roadmap | $15K–$35K |
| **2** | Quick Win Implementation | 2-4 weeks | Deploy #1 highest-ROI solution + measurable results + team training | $25K–$50K |
| **3** | Full Transformation | 2-6 months | Phased implementation of top 3-5 solutions + change management | $75K–$150K |
| **∞** | Advisory Retainer | Ongoing | Monthly optimization + quarterly reviews + trend filtering | $8K–$15K/mo |

**Key principle**: Each phase delivers standalone value. The client can stop after any phase and still have a complete deliverable. This eliminates risk perception.

### 3. Write the Proposal Document

**Structure** (this order is deliberate — it mirrors a persuasion sequence):

#### Section 1: Current State (The Pain)
- Summarize the diagnostic findings in 3-5 bullet points
- Lead with the BIGGEST annual waste figure
- Use direct quotes from operator interviews (anonymized)
- Tone: factual, not alarmist. The numbers speak for themselves

```
Example:
"Your sales team currently spends 2.3 hours per rep per day on manual data entry
across 4 disconnected systems. Across 12 reps, this represents $298,000 in annual
labor waste — before counting the downstream impact on response time and close rates."
```

#### Section 2: Target State (The Vision)
- Paint the picture of what "solved" looks like
- Be specific: "Response time drops from 4 hours to 12 minutes"
- Include 3 scenarios: Conservative / Moderate / Aggressive ROI projections
- Frame in PROFIT terms (genius.md: "Bottom-Line Focus")

#### Section 3: Engagement Structure (The How)
- Present the phased approach from Step 2
- For each phase:
  - **Deliverables** (specific, tangible outputs)
  - **Timeline** (start date → end date)
  - **Investment** (fixed price, not hourly)
  - **Success Criteria** (measurable outcomes)
  - **Your Commitment** (what you will do)
  - **Client Commitment** (access, time, champion)

#### Section 4: Investment & ROI (The Math)
- Total investment across all phases
- Total projected annual savings/revenue impact
- **Payback period** — frame against the 12-month benchmark (genius.md)
- "For every $1 invested, you recover $X within Y months"
- Include the Success Fee option if appropriate: "Base fee + 20% of verified savings"

#### Section 5: Risk Mitigation (The Safety Net)
- "Phase 1 is independent — if the diagnostic doesn't reveal significant opportunity, we stop"
- "All ROI projections use conservative estimates (actual results from similar engagements exceeded projections by 40%)"
- "Implementation includes change management and team training — not just technology deployment"
- Reference the AI Champion requirement

#### Section 6: About [Your Name/Firm] (Brief — 1 paragraph)
- 2-3 sentences positioning statement
- Relevant vertical experience
- "We specialize in finding where businesses bleed money — and proving the ROI before asking you to invest"

#### Section 7: Next Steps (The Close)
- One clear action: "Sign this proposal and we begin Phase 1 on [date]"
- Include a decision deadline (not pressure — "This pricing is held for 14 days")
- Offer a 15-min call to discuss questions

### 4. Pricing Strategy Selection

Choose the pricing model based on client signals:

| Model | Use When | Structure |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| **Fixed Phase** | Most engagements. Clear scope, clear deliverables | $X per phase, defined outputs |
| **Success Fee Hybrid** | High-ROI opportunities where you're confident in outcomes | Base ($40K) + 20-30% of verified savings |
| **Retainer + Project** | Established relationship, ongoing needs | $X/mo retainer + project pricing for implementations |
| **Diagnostic-Only** | New relationship, building trust | $15K-$35K for diagnostic, implementation priced separately |

**Never use hourly billing.** Research shows hourly caps at $50K/year and creates perverse incentives (slower = more revenue).

### 5. Internal Review Gate

Before sending, run the proposal through these checks:

| Check | Question | Pass? |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **ROI Clarity** | Can the client see the payback period in under 10 seconds? | ☐ |
| **Risk Mitigation** | Does the client feel safe saying yes to Phase 1 without committing to all phases? | ☐ |
| **No Jargon** | Could a non-technical CEO understand every sentence? | ☐ |
| **Specificity** | Are all deliverables tangible (not "strategic recommendations")? | ☐ |
| **Dollar Anchoring** | Does the annual waste figure appear before the investment figure? | ☐ |
| **Champion Reference** | Is the AI Champion named or referenced? | ☐ |
| **Deadline** | Is there a decision timeline? | ☐ |

### 6. Follow-Up Sequence

If no response within 3 business days:

- **Day 3**: Brief email — "Just checking if you have questions about the proposal"
- **Day 7**: Value-add email — share a relevant case study or industry insight
- **Day 14**: "I'm holding this pricing through [date]. After that, I'll need to re-scope based on current availability"
- **Day 21**: "I understand timing may not be right. I'll check back in [30/60/90 days]. In the meantime, here's [resource]"

**Never chase. Always add value.**

## Output Schema

The deliverable is a **Proposal & SOW document** with exactly the 7 sections from Step 3, in that fixed order (the sequence is the persuasion structure — do not reorder):

| # | Section | Required Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Current State (Pain) | 3-5 bullets, biggest annual waste figure first, anonymized operator quotes |
| 2 | Target State (Vision) | Specific before/after claim + 3-scenario ROI projection, profit-framed |
| 3 | Engagement Structure | 2-4 phases, each with Deliverables/Timeline/Investment/Success Criteria/Both parties' commitments |
| 4 | Investment & ROI | Total investment, total projected impact, payback period vs. 12-month benchmark |
| 5 | Risk Mitigation | Phase-independence statement, conservative-estimate disclosure, AI Champion reference |
| 6 | About | 1 paragraph, 2-3 sentences |
| 7 | Next Steps | One clear action, decision deadline, call offer |

Pricing model selected from Step 4's table must be named explicitly in Section 3 — never left implicit.

## Quality Gate

| Criterion | Pass Threshold |
|-----------|---------------|
| Annual waste figure appears before investment | YES |
| Payback period is under 12 months | YES |
| Each phase has standalone value | YES |
| Zero technical jargon in executive sections | YES |
| Success criteria are measurable (not subjective) | YES |
| Follow-up sequence loaded | YES |
