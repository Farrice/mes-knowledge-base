# Workflow 08: Maintenance & Advisory Retainer Builder

> Design recurring revenue structures that create MSP-style lock-in.

## Prerequisites
- **Load**: [genius.md](../genius.md)
- **Input Required**: Completed implementation (or near-completion), client relationship context
- **Best Stacked With**: @soowei (consulting leverage architecture for scaling retainers)

## Produces
1. Advisory retainer proposal with scope definition
2. Maintenance scope and SLA framework
3. Advisory cadence (monthly/quarterly touchpoints)
4. Upsell pathway from retainer to expanded engagement
5. Client retention metrics and churn prevention triggers

---

## Step 1: Define the Retainer Value Proposition

```
WHY RECURRING ADVISORY EXISTS

The pitch to the client:
"AI technology evolves faster than any other technology in history.
What we built 3 months ago already has better options available.
Without someone filtering the noise, you'll either:
(a) miss opportunities that your competitors catch, or
(b) waste money chasing shiny objects that don't matter.

I act as your AI advisor — I filter the noise, surface what's relevant
to YOUR business, maintain what we've built, and tell you when it's
time to upgrade."

KEY INSIGHT: This mirrors the IT Managed Service Provider (MSP) model.
Once you've built a company's AI infrastructure, switching costs are
prohibitive. They stay for YEARS.
```

---

## Step 2: Design the Retainer Tiers

```
RETAINER TIER ARCHITECTURE

TIER 1: MAINTENANCE ONLY ($2K-$3K/month)
├── Monthly system health check (all implemented workflows)
├── Bug fixes and minor adjustments
├── Quarterly performance review (is it still saving what we projected?)
├── Email support for issues (48hr response SLA)
└── Deliverable: Monthly maintenance report

TIER 2: MAINTENANCE + ADVISORY ($4K-$6K/month)
├── Everything in Tier 1
├── Monthly 60-min advisory call (AI landscape briefing)
├── Quarterly opportunity audit (new processes to automate)
├── Technology trend filtering (what's relevant to YOUR business)
├── Priority support (24hr response SLA)
└── Deliverable: Monthly advisory brief + maintenance report

TIER 3: FRACTIONAL AI OFFICER ($8K-$15K/month)
├── Everything in Tier 2
├── Weekly 30-min check-in with CEO/leadership
├── Attend quarterly strategic planning sessions
├── Own the company's AI roadmap end-to-end
├── Vendor evaluation and selection for new tools
├── Team training and upskilling program design
├── Same-day support for critical issues
└── Deliverable: Weekly status update + monthly strategic brief

RECOMMENDATION: Start most clients at Tier 2. Graduate to Tier 3
after 6+ months when they see you as indispensable.
```

## Step 3: Monthly Advisory Cadence

```
MONTHLY ADVISORY TOUCHPOINTS

WEEK 1: System Health Check
- Review all implemented automations
- Check error logs, failure rates, performance metrics
- Document any issues or degradation
- Produce: Maintenance Report

WEEK 2: AI Landscape Scan
- Review relevant AI developments from the past month
- Filter for what actually matters to THIS client's industry
- Identify potential upgrades or new opportunities
- Produce: "What's New & Relevant" brief (1-2 pages max)

WEEK 3: Advisory Call (60 min)
- Review maintenance findings
- Present relevant technology updates
- Discuss any new business challenges or expansion plans
- Identify next optimization opportunity
- Produce: Call summary with action items

WEEK 4: Performance Review
- Compare actual savings against projected ROI
- Calculate month-over-month efficiency gains
- Flag any processes where adoption has dropped
- Produce: Monthly performance dashboard
```

## Step 5: Churn Prevention

```
CHURN PREVENTION TRIGGERS

Monitor for these warning signs:

| Signal | Risk Level | Response |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Client stops attending advisory calls | HIGH | Personal outreach — "Is the format working? Let's adjust." |
| New leadership/ownership change | HIGH | Re-run diagnostic discovery to prove value to new stakeholders |
| Client starts asking about competitors | MEDIUM | Proactive competitive comparison — show your unique value |
| Implementation adoption drops below 70% | MEDIUM | Change management intervention — re-engage AI Champion |
| Client requests budget reduction | MEDIUM | Offer tier downgrade rather than cancellation |
| Client says "we've got it from here" | HIGH | Present the "what happens without maintenance" scenario with specific examples |

BEST DEFENSE: Monthly ROI reporting that shows ongoing value.
"Last month your automations saved [X] hours and $[X].
Over the past 6 months, total value delivered: $[X]."
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Retainer positioned as protecting investment, not new cost
- [ ] Protection ratio calculated (retainer cost vs. savings protected)
- [ ] Month-to-month terms (reduces buyer friction)
- [ ] Clear deliverables per month (not vague "advisory")
- [ ] Churn prevention triggers documented and monitored
- [ ] Upsell pathway from Tier 1 → Tier 2 → Tier 3 mapped
- [ ] Quarterly scope review built in (keeps engagement fresh)
