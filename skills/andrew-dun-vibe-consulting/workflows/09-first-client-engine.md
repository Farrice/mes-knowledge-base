## Step 1: Define Your Positioning

Before any outreach, crystallize what you do in one sentence:

```
POSITIONING BUILDER

Template: "I help [WHO] find where they're [LOSING/WASTING] [WHAT]
and fix it with [METHOD], so they can [OUTCOME]."

EXAMPLES:
- "I help service businesses find where they're wasting $100K+ per year
  on broken processes and fix it with AI-native workflows, so they can
  add that directly to their bottom line."

- "I help companies with 10-50 employees find where their teams are
  doing robot work and replace it with actual automation, so owners
  can stop paying humans to do computer tasks."

YOUR VERSION:
"I help __________ find where they're __________ and fix it with
__________, so they can __________."

VALIDATION: Does this pass the non-technical test?
- Would a business owner with ZERO tech knowledge understand this? [Y/N]
- Does it lead with their PROBLEM, not your solution? [Y/N]
- Does it imply a dollar amount or concrete outcome? [Y/N]
```

## Step 3: The Backend Deal Offer (Zero-Risk First Client)

This is Andrew's exact strategy for the first engagement:

```
THE BACKEND DEAL — FIRST CLIENT SCRIPT

"Hey [Name],

I've been building an AI consulting practice focused on helping businesses
find where they're losing money on broken processes. I'm looking for
ONE company to do a deep diagnostic audit for.

Here's what I'd do:
- Interview you and 2-3 of your team members
- Map out your core business processes step by step
- Identify exactly where you're wasting time and money
- Calculate the actual dollar amount you're losing per year
- Give you a prioritized roadmap to fix it

Here's the deal: If you're satisfied with what I find, it'll be
a couple thousand dollars. If you're not satisfied, all I'd ask for
is a case study, a testimonial, and permission to use you as a reference.

Either way, you walk away with a complete picture of where your
business is bleeding money. No risk to you.

Would next [day] work for a 30-minute conversation?"
```

**Why This Works**:
- Zero financial risk for the client
- You get paid OR get the three assets worth more than cash (social proof)
- The diagnostic almost always overdelivers (client feels "guilty for not paying more")
- Creates the springboard for the full paid engagement

## Step 4: Outreach Execution Plan

```
5-DAY OUTREACH SPRINT

DAY 1: Send messages to Tier 1 contacts (5 messages)
- Use the Backend Deal script above
- Personalize the opening with something specific about their business
- Don't attach anything — keep it conversational

DAY 2: Follow up on Day 1 (if no response) + send Tier 2 introductions (5 messages)
- For Tier 2: "Hey [Connector], do you know anyone who runs a business
  with 10-50 employees who's overwhelmed? I'm doing free AI audits for
  a limited time."

DAY 3: Send to Tier 3 contacts (5 messages)
- LinkedIn DMs work well here
- Lead with something you noticed about their business specifically

DAY 4: Follow up on all non-responses
- "Just bumping this up — no pressure. Figured it couldn't hurt
  to have someone map out where you're leaving money on the table."

DAY 5: Schedule all conversations booked
- Prepare pre-interview intelligence briefs (Step 1 of Workflow 01)
- Set discovery calls for the following week

TARGET: 3-5 conversations booked from 20 outreach messages = 15-25% response rate
```

## Step 5: Social Proof Capture Plan

```
AFTER THE FIRST ENGAGEMENT — CAPTURE EVERYTHING

1. CASE STUDY (for your portfolio):
   - Problem: [What they were struggling with]
   - Discovery: [What you found — include dollar figures]
   - Solution: [What you recommended/implemented]
   - Result: [Measurable outcome — time saved, money saved, efficiency gained]
   - Format: 1-page PDF + LinkedIn post version

2. VIDEO TESTIMONIAL (most valuable):
   - Ask: "Would you be willing to do a 2-minute video call where I ask you
     3 questions about working with me?"
   - Questions:
     a. "What was the problem before we worked together?"
     b. "What did you discover through the audit?"
     c. "Would you recommend this to other business owners? Why?"

3. REFERENCE (for future sales calls):
   - "Can I give your name to future clients who want to hear about
     your experience? I'll always ask before sharing your info."
   - This is worth more than the engagement fee for your second client.

4. LINKEDIN POST (immediate social proof):
   - Post about the engagement (with client permission)
   - Include specific numbers: "Found $X/year in process waste"
   - Tag the client if appropriate
```

## Step 6: First-Client-to-Second-Client Bridge

```
BRIDGE STRATEGY

FROM FIRST CLIENT:
1. Referral Ask: "Who else do you know who might benefit from this?"
   (Ask AFTER delivering the audit, when satisfaction is highest)

2. Case Study Deployment:
   - LinkedIn post about the engagement
   - Website/portfolio update
   - Include in all future outreach: "I just completed an audit for
     [Company] and found $[X] in annual waste..."

3. Price Escalation:
   - First client: Backend deal ($0-$2K)
   - Second client: $3K-$5K (with case study proof)
   - Third client: $5K-$10K (with two references)
   - Fourth client: Full market rate ($10K-$15K)

4. STACK WITH @LINDSAY:
   - Use Lindsay's Multi-Channel Outreach Playbook (Workflow 02)
   - Apply Andrew's positioning + Lindsay's acquisition engine
   - Scale from warm network to cold outreach with social proof
```
