# Workflow 05: Diagnostic Presentation Architect

> Build the client-facing audit deck that makes business owners say "I feel guilty for not paying you more."

## Prerequisites
- **Load**: [genius.md](../genius.md)
- **Input Required**: Discovery notes (Workflow 01), ROI calculations (Workflow 03), Opportunity Matrix (Workflow 04)
- **Dependencies**: This is the capstone of the audit — requires all preceding workflows

## Produces
A presentation-ready diagnostic deck (10-15 slides) containing:
1. Executive summary with total annual waste headline
2. Process-by-process findings with before/after mapping
3. ROI quantification per bottleneck
4. Prioritized opportunity matrix
5. Phased implementation roadmap with investment/return projections

## Presentation Delivery Notes

**Andrew's Principles for the Presentation Meeting**:
- Present to the business owner/CEO directly — not a committee
- Walk through EVERY finding, don't rush the numbers
- Let the dollar figures do the selling — you don't need to "close"
- When they ask "what would this cost?" — the ROI already answered that
- If they're surprised by the waste figure, you've done your job
- The goal: client says "when can we start?" not "let me think about it"

**Non-Technical Language Rules**:
- Never say: "We'll implement an N8N workflow with API integrations"
- Always say: "We'll automate this so your team never has to do it manually again"
- Never say: "This uses GPT-4 with RAG retrieval"
- Always say: "This gives your team instant access to all your company knowledge"
