## Step 1: Choose Your Partnership Model

```
PARTNERSHIP TIER DECISION MATRIX

| Model | Your Role | Their Role | Your Margin | Best When |
|-------|----------|-----------|------------|-----------|
| REFERRAL | Introduce client, step back | Full implementation + relationship | 10-15% | Starting out, low risk, building network |
| WHITE-LABEL | Own client relationship, manage project | Technical implementation (invisible) | 40-50% | You have client trust, need technical execution |
| FULL-STACK | Everything | N/A | 100% | You have technical skills or in-house team |

DECISION CRITERIA:
- Your technical ability: [None / Basic / Intermediate / Advanced]
- Client relationship depth: [New / Established / Deep Trust]
- Project complexity: [Simple / Moderate / Complex]
- Your bandwidth: [Limited / Available / Dedicated]

RECOMMENDATION FOR BEGINNERS: Start with REFERRAL for first 2-3 projects.
Graduate to WHITE-LABEL after you've seen how implementations work.
```

## Step 3: Contract Structure

```
PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT ESSENTIALS

FOR REFERRAL MODEL:
- Commission: 10-15% of implementation contract
- Payment: Upon client payment (not upon signing)
- Duration: Per-project basis
- Client relationship: Partner owns going forward

FOR WHITE-LABEL MODEL:
- Your client-facing rate: $[X]/hour or $[X] project fee
- Partner's rate: $[Y]/hour or $[Y] project fee
- Your margin: $[X-Y] per hour or flat project margin
- Client communication: ALL through you (partner is invisible)
- IP ownership: You own the client relationship, partner owns the code
- NDA: Partner cannot approach client directly

KEY CLAUSES:
□ Non-solicitation (partner can't poach your client)
□ Quality standards and revision policy
□ Timeline and milestone obligations
□ Payment terms (you pay partner within X days of client payment)
□ Confidentiality (client data protection)
```

## Step 4: Quality Control Protocol

```
MANAGING THE INVISIBLE PARTNER

WEEKLY CHECK-INS:
- Monday: Review week's deliverables and timeline
- Wednesday: Mid-week status check (any blockers?)
- Friday: Demo of completed work before presenting to client

CLIENT COMMUNICATION:
- You attend ALL client meetings
- Partner attends technical reviews (introduced as "my technical team")
- All client emails go through you
- Partner sends you updates, you relay to client in non-technical language

QUALITY GATES:
Before presenting ANY deliverable to client:
□ You've personally tested/reviewed the implementation
□ It matches the specification from your audit findings
□ Documentation exists (how it works, how to maintain)
□ Partner has conducted their own QA
□ You can explain it to the client in non-technical language
```
