# Industry Vertical Playbook Builder

> Load `genius.md` before executing. Vertical specialization is the #1 differentiator for $500K+ consultants.

## When to Use
- Preparing to enter a new industry vertical (manufacturing, BFSI, healthcare, retail, professional services, real estate, creative agencies)
- Before a diagnostic engagement in an unfamiliar vertical
- Building authority content for a target vertical
- Pricing and scoping vertical-specific engagements

## Steps

### 1. Vertical Selection & Intelligence Gathering

**Choose ONE vertical to build the playbook for.** Input: industry name.

Research (use Perplexity or existing knowledge):
- **AI maturity level**: Where is this industry on the AI adoption curve? (Early/Growth/Mature)
- **Regulatory landscape**: What compliance/governance requirements affect AI deployment?
- **Common tech stack**: What CRMs, ERPs, and tools dominate this vertical?
- **Decision-maker profile**: Who buys AI consulting? (CEO, COO, CTO, VP Ops?)
- **Budget benchmarks**: What do companies in this vertical spend on technology consulting?

Produce a Vertical Intelligence Card:
```
VERTICAL: [Name]
AI Maturity: [Early / Growth / Mature]
Typical Company Size: [$XM–$XM revenue]
Decision Maker: [Title]
Tech Stack: [3-5 dominant tools]
Regulatory Gates: [Key compliance requirements]
AI Budget Range: [% of revenue or $ range]
Competition: [Who else serves this vertical?]
```

### 2. Top 5 Pain Point Mapping

For the selected vertical, identify the **5 most common operational pain points** that AI can solve. For each:

```
PAIN POINT #[N]: [Name]
Department: [Where it lives]
Current State: [How it's done today — manual, fragmented, etc.]
Waste Estimate: [Using ROI formula: Time × People × Days × Cost]
AI Solution Category: [Automation / Prediction / Knowledge Management / Agent]
Quick Win Potential: [HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW]
Typical ROI Timeline: [Days to measurable result]
```

**Sources for pain points:**
- Industry trade publications and forums
- Competitor case studies
- Andrew's 4-Question AI Suitability Filter (genius.md)
- Client interviews from similar verticals

### 3. Vertical-Specific Discovery Questions

Build a bank of 15-20 discovery questions tailored to this vertical. Organize by role:

**For Executives (5-7 questions):**
- Questions about strategic goals, competitive pressure, compliance burden
- Example: "What's your biggest operational cost that you believe is just 'the cost of doing business'?"

**For Operators (8-12 questions):**
- Questions about daily workflows, tool friction, manual handoffs
- Example (Manufacturing): "Walk me through what happens between a customer order and production starting."
- Example (BFSI): "Show me the steps from loan application received to first human review."

**For IT/Tech (3-5 questions):**
- Questions about data architecture, integration readiness, security constraints
- Example: "If I asked you to pull all customer interaction data from the last 12 months into one spreadsheet, how many systems would you need to touch?"

### 4. Vertical Solution Library

Map the top 10 AI solutions proven in this vertical. For each:

| # | Solution | Pain Point Solved | Complexity | Typical ROI | Implementation Time |
|---|----------|------------------|------------|-------------|---------------------|
| 1 | [Name] | [Pain #] | [Low/Med/High] | [$ or %] | [Weeks] |

**Include 3 tiers:**
- **Quick Wins** (3-4): Deploy in 1-2 weeks, prove ROI fast
- **Core Implementations** (3-4): 4-8 weeks, major efficiency gains
- **Transformation Plays** (2-3): 3-6 months, competitive advantage

### 5. Pricing & Packaging for Vertical

Build vertical-specific pricing tiers:

| Package | What's Included | Price | Timeline |
|---------|----------------|-------|----------|
| **Readiness Scorecard** | 2-hour assessment + maturity score + top 3 opportunities | $2K–$5K | 1 session |
| **Full Diagnostic** | Executive + operator interviews + process mapping + ROI report + roadmap | $15K–$35K | 2 weeks |
| **Diagnostic + Quick Win** | Full diagnostic + implement #1 quick win | $25K–$50K | 4 weeks |
| **Full Transformation** | Diagnostic + phased implementation + change management | $75K–$150K | 3-6 months |
| **Advisory Retainer** | Monthly optimization + trend filtering + quarterly reviews | $8K–$15K/mo | Ongoing |

Adjust ranges based on vertical benchmarks from Step 1.

### 6. Authority Content Blueprint

Design 10 content pieces that establish vertical authority:

1. **POC Teardown** — Publicly audit a common vertical problem (no client names)
2. **ROI Calculator Post** — "Here's what [vertical] companies waste on [process]"
3. **Case Study Template** — Before/after for the most common quick win
4. **Industry Trend Analysis** — "3 AI shifts [vertical] leaders are ignoring"
5. **Myth Buster** — "Why [common AI misconception in vertical] is costing you $X"
6. **Process Decomposition** — "You think [task] is one step. It's actually 7."
7. **Benchmark Report** — "AI maturity scores across 50 [vertical] companies"
8. **Interview Excerpt** — Anonymized operator quotes that reveal hidden waste
9. **Quick Win Walkthrough** — Step-by-step of the #1 quick win (no tools, just outcomes)
10. **Cost of Inaction** — "Every month you wait costs $X" with real math

### 7. Compile Vertical Playbook

**Output**: A single document containing all 6 sections above.

```
VERTICAL PLAYBOOK: [Industry Name]
Created: [Date]
Version: 1.0

1. Vertical Intelligence Card
2. Top 5 Pain Points (with ROI estimates)
3. Discovery Question Bank (by role)
4. Solution Library (tiered by complexity)
5. Pricing & Packaging
6. Authority Content Blueprint
```

## Output Schema

The deliverable is a single **Vertical Playbook** document (Step 7 compile format) with exactly 6 numbered sections, each populated (not left as a section header):

| Section | Required Content |
|---|---|
| 1. Vertical Intelligence Card | All 7 fields filled (AI Maturity, Company Size, Decision Maker, Tech Stack, Regulatory Gates, AI Budget Range, Competition) |
| 2. Top 5 Pain Points | Exactly 5, each with department, current state, ROI-formula waste estimate, AI solution category, quick-win potential |
| 3. Discovery Question Bank | 15-20 questions split Executive (5-7) / Operator (8-12) / IT (3-5), vertical-specific |
| 4. Solution Library | 10 solutions across 3 tiers (Quick Wins/Core/Transformation) with complexity, ROI, timeline |
| 5. Pricing & Packaging | 5-tier table (Scorecard → Full Diagnostic → Diagnostic+Quick Win → Full Transformation → Advisory) with vertical-adjusted $ ranges |
| 6. Authority Content Blueprint | 10 titled content pieces, each producible in <2 hours |

## Quality Gate

| Criterion | Pass Threshold |
|-----------|---------------|
| Pain points have dollar figures | All 5 quantified |
| Discovery questions are vertical-specific (not generic) | 80%+ couldn't apply to another vertical |
| Solution library cites real tools/approaches | All 10 have specific tech referenced |
| Pricing aligns with vertical benchmarks | Within 20% of research ranges |
| Content blueprint is actionable | Each piece could be produced in <2 hours |
