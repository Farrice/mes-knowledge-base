# Andrew Dun — Vibe Consulting Mastery

> Prescription without diagnosis is malpractice. Map the disease before prescribing the cure.
This skill captures Andrew Dun's complete consulting delivery methodology — the 6-step diagnostic framework that turns AI skeptics into long-term advisory retainer clients. He built and exited a multi-7-figure consulting firm by treating businesses like patients: interview, diagnose, quantify, prescribe, implement, maintain. **v2.0** adds practice operations: proposals, pipeline, vertical specialization, readiness scoring, and live value proof.

**Expert**: Andrew Dun — AI Consulting ("Vibe Consulting") | Non-technical strategist who commands premium fees | Exited multi-7-figure firm
**Domain**: Process Diagnosis + ROI Quantification + Prescriptive AI Strategy + Advisory Retainers + Practice Operations
