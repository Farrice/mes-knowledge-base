## 23 Genius Patterns

### P1: Position Before Content
Never content before positioning. Complete: "I help [WHO] achieve [WHAT] through [HOW] so they can [OUTCOME]"

### P2: Dual-Persona Content Design
Every post serves TWO audiences: **ICP** (who pays) + **IFP** (who shares). Always ask "Who will share this AND who will buy from this?"

### P3: The 4-3-2-1 Content Ratio
4 posts/week. Three types: Educational, Storytelling, TAM (trend/news commentary). Calendar: Mon(educate) Wed(story) Thu(TAM) Sun(flex). Applied version, verbatim: "Split them into 1 Education, 1 Storytelling, 1 'Edu-Telling' (both combined), and 1 Education (again)" against 3 pillars — TAM, Growth, Sales (`extractions/lara-acosta-content-system/extraction-report.md:22`, 2026-03-02).

### P4: First-Principle Hook Testing
Hook must work if reader has no idea who you are. **Test**: Remove name/avatar — does it still stop scrolling? Success signal, verbatim: "a 3x higher click-through-rate on the 'see more' expansion" (`extractions/lara-acosta/extraction-report.md:24`, 2026-03-02).

### P5: Authority Trigger Stack
Stack: specific numbers > vague claims, named clients > "clients", timeline specificity > "eventually", process transparency > black-box results.

### P6: Social Proof Archaeology
Dig for existing proof: testimonials in emails/DMs, before/after transformations, small specific results, borrowed authority. Lara's own proof: she "made $10K passively from comments (linking to a Gumroad product in replies)" (`extractions/lara-acosta/2026-linkedin-playbook-extraction.md:113`, 2026-03-05).

### P7: Content Pillar Excavation
Three pillars from expertise intersection: (1) What you DO, (2) What you BELIEVE, (3) What you ENABLE. Applied version, verbatim: "Define the TAM topics (AI, Productivity, Remote Work, LinkedIn), Growth topics (building a SaaS, quitting 9-to-5), and Sales topics" (`extractions/lara-acosta-content-system/extraction-report.md:53`, 2026-03-02).

### P8: The SLAY Framework
**S**tory → **L**esson → **A**pplication → **Y**our turn (CTA/question). Success metric, verbatim: "High comment volume and high average read time (time-on-post)" (`extractions/lara-acosta/extraction-report.md:34`, 2026-03-02).

## Signature Moves

1. **Double-Tap Hook** — 8-word hook + line break + 8-word rehook, forces "see more" → Deploy on first two lines of any post
2. **Engagement Sprint** — 30-60 min active replying post-publish, never post and ghost → Deploy immediately after posting
3. **Audience Funnel Weave** — Broad hook → narrow pain → niche advice (Broad-Narrow-Niche) → Deploy on educational/"how I" posts
4. **Relatable Win Reframing** — Shift wins from "I/we" to impact on others/humble origin → Deploy when sharing achievements
5. **Visual Scan Path** — Single-sentence lines, double line breaks, F-shape reading → Deploy on all post body formatting

## Anti-Patterns

What Lara Acosta would never do — sourced against the raw extraction material, not inferred. Full audit trail in `references/source-ledger.md` and `PROVENANCE-2026-07-17.md`.

- **Never schedule-and-ghost** — "If you can't be there to engage, don't post at all during that day." LinkedIn distributes based on network engagement; zero engagement in the first hour reads as a dead post to the algorithm. (2026-03-05, `extractions/lara-acosta/2026-linkedin-playbook-extraction.md:84`)
- **Never lean on AI-written comments** — "Don't use AI comments" — the market penalizes obviously automated engagement, and it undercuts the ghostwriting-service positioning itself. (2026-03-05, `extractions/lara-acosta/2026-linkedin-playbook-extraction.md:137`)
- **Never open with "How to"** — "'How to' is AI slop. ChatGPT writes 'How to.' True experts write 'How I.'" Swap the pronoun or the post reads generic instead of battle-tested and proprietary. (2026-03-02, `extractions/lara-acosta/extraction-report.md:63`)
- **Never tag an authority you haven't back-channeled** — "DO NOT physically tag their @ handle in the post unless you have back-channel confirmation they will comment within 30 minutes" — an unanswered tag reads as spam and the algorithm suppresses the post. (2026-03-02, `extractions/lara-acosta/extraction-report.md:50`)
- **Never niche the hook down to the buyer only** — "'My business gets 10 leads every week without outbound' outperforms 'If you're a B2B business owner making over $10M a year, here's how...'" — the second version segments ruthlessly and kills shareability. (2026-03-05, `extractions/lara-acosta/2026-linkedin-playbook-extraction.md:117`)
- **Never announce a win as raw "I"** — the Humble Brag pattern exists because unfiltered success framing risks "100% positive sentiment in comments, avoiding the 'LinkedIn Lunatics' backlash" being the exception, not the default; route wins through family, team, or origin instead. (2026-03-02, `extractions/lara-acosta/extraction-report.md:46`)
- **Never let content try to sell** — "Content's job: attract the right people. NOT to sell." Selling inside the post breaks the Revenue Bridge; email closes, LinkedIn only attracts. (2026-03-05, `extractions/lara-acosta/2026-linkedin-playbook-extraction.md:100`)
