## Lenses

### Position Before Content
Never content before positioning. Complete: "I help [WHO] achieve [WHAT] through [HOW] so they can [OUTCOME]."
**Test:** The statement is complete before any post is written.

### Dual-Persona Content Design
Every post serves TWO audiences: ICP (who pays) + IFP (who shares). IFP-designed content grows reach 3-5x vs ICP-only.
**Test:** Always ask "Who will share this AND who will buy from this?"

### The 4-3-2-1 Content Ratio
4 posts/week. Three types: Educational, Storytelling, TAM (trend/news commentary). Applied verbatim: "Split them into 1 Education, 1 Storytelling, 1 'Edu-Telling' (both combined), and 1 Education (again)" against 3 pillars — TAM, Growth, Sales.

### First-Principle Hook Testing
Hook must work if reader has no idea who you are.
**Test:** Remove name/avatar — does it still stop scrolling? Success signal: "a 3x higher click-through-rate on the 'see more' expansion."

### Authority Trigger Stack
Stack: specific numbers > vague claims, named clients > "clients", timeline specificity > "eventually", process transparency > black-box results.

### Social Proof Archaeology
Dig for existing proof: testimonials in emails/DMs, before/after transformations, small specific results, borrowed authority.

### Content Pillar Excavation
Three pillars from expertise intersection: (1) What you DO, (2) What you BELIEVE, (3) What you ENABLE.

### The SLAY Framework
Story → Lesson → Application → Your turn (CTA/question).
**Test (success metric, verbatim):** "High comment volume and high average read time (time-on-post)."

### Voice Extraction Protocol *(LIKELY — mechanism grounded, 5-part taxonomy is house synthesis)*
Capture: signature phrases, sentence rhythm, emotional intensity moments, passion topics, humor patterns. Grounding anchor: "we focused a lot on your story and what you specifically educate on and your social proof… That is the main thing that will keep you authentic."

### Zero-to-Authority Roadmap
Days 1-30: Foundation (positioning, pillars, first 30 posts). Days 31-60: Momentum (consistency, engagement, lead magnets). Days 61-90: Authority (thought leadership, speaking, press).

### Lead Magnet Virality
Must do both: capture email AND go viral as content. Design as standalone content pieces. "It doesn't need to be a hyperdesigned thing." Conversion is driven by solving one key problem well — not production quality.

### Format Emulation
"Find a viral post from an unrelated industry. Strip away the niche-specific words, keep the formatting... and inject your own industry's context." Then test with your hook.

### Algorithm Optimization
LinkedIn rewards: early engagement (first hour critical), dwell time, native content (no external links), authentic voice, conversation starters. "LinkedIn distributes based on network engagement. No engagement = content dies." 80% of reach is determined in the first 60 minutes.

### Ghostwriting Voice Deployment *(UNCONFIRMED — no transcript support for the 4-stage pipeline)*
Voice profile → topic approval → authenticity check → iteration loop. Never sanitize voice — amplify patterns.

### Authority Acceleration
Fast-track: borrow authority (interview experts), stack micro-proof, niche down aggressively, daily consistency signal. Guardrail, verbatim: "DO NOT physically tag their @ handle in the post unless you have back-channel confirmation they will comment within 30 minutes."

### Client Attraction Content *(UNCONFIRMED — no transcript support for buyer-awareness staging)*
Problem-aware → solution-aware → decision-stage → trust-building. Map posts to buyer journey stages.

### The Founder's Content Gap
Founders: know too much (curse), assume context, skip "obvious", fear being salesy. First 30 posts address all four.

### Attention Arbitrage Economics
LinkedIn = 1B users consuming, almost none creating. One post (20 min) can reach 10K qualified ICPs.

### Three Growth Shapes
Steady (reliable, slow). Exponential (hockey stick at content-market fit). Identity Crisis (grow→pivot→reset→repeat). If identity crisis: STOP pivoting — "the slowest because you reset every time you change direction."

### Profile-as-Algorithm-Signal
Headline = pain+audience+proof. Banner = social proof. Featured = proof of work. All aligned to single niche.
**Test (Mental Folder Test):** "Could a friend describe what I do in one sentence?"

### The 1+3 Comment Formula
1 substantive comment + 3 replies to OTHER people's comments on the same post = 4x profile views daily. Replying to others' comments shows you to their network. Comments > posts for revenue: $10K passively from comment links.

### Post & Ghost Kill Switch
Never post and disappear. Protocol: 20 min engaging BEFORE posting → reply to every comment in first 30 min. Can't engage? Don't post.

### The Revenue Bridge Architecture
Content → Profile → Lead Magnet → Email List → Sales (attract / convert / capture / nurture / sell). 70-80% of revenue from email, not LinkedIn. Content's job: attract, NOT sell. One lead magnet post/week + permanent on profile. Content→client journey = 90-180 days; most quit at 60.

### Signature Moves
- **Double-Tap Hook** — 8-word hook + line break + 8-word rehook forcing "see more"; deploy on first two lines of any post.
- **Engagement Sprint** — 30-60 min active replying post-publish.
- **Audience Funnel Weave** — Broad hook → narrow pain → niche advice (Broad-Narrow-Niche) on educational/"how I" posts.
- **Relatable Win Reframing** — shift wins from "I/we" to impact on others/humble origin.
- **Visual Scan Path** — single-sentence lines, double line breaks, F-shape reading on all post bodies.

### Savant bar (from rubric)
8-word hook + 8-word rehook creating open loop · seamless S→L→A→Y with high-engagement CTA · universal hook → smooth narrow → deep niche value · success routed through family/origin/client-impact lens · 1-2 sentence blocks, double breaks, mobile scan · explicitly designed for sharing by amplifiers · every comment answered for 30-60 min.

## Anti-patterns / calibration
- **Never schedule-and-ghost** — "If you can't be there to engage, don't post at all during that day."
- **Never lean on AI-written comments** — "Don't use AI comments."
- **Never open with "How to"** — "'How to' is AI slop. ChatGPT writes 'How to.' True experts write 'How I.'"
- **Never tag an authority you haven't back-channeled** — an unanswered tag reads as spam and the algorithm suppresses the post.
- **Never niche the hook down to the buyer only** — "'My business gets 10 leads every week without outbound' outperforms 'If you're a B2B business owner making over $10M a year, here's how...'" — the second segments ruthlessly and kills shareability.
- **Never announce a win as raw "I"** — route wins through family, team, or origin; avoid the "LinkedIn Lunatics" backlash.
- **Never let content try to sell** — "Content's job: attract the right people. NOT to sell." Email closes; LinkedIn only attracts.
- **Calibration:** patterns are intuition primitives, not a checklist. Never stamp "S: … L: … A: … Y:" labels or announce mechanisms ("Hook Line 1 (max 8 words)") — execute the move, never announce it. Texture is spoken-then-formatted, not corporate: "How I," not "How to"; direct, declarative, a little blunt. Consultant hedging ("it's important to note that...") is the tell that breaks the spell.
