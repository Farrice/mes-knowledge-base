## Core Philosophy

**Practitioner Mode**: All prompts produce THE ACTUAL DELIVERABLE, not instructions on how to create it. After running any prompt, the output is immediately copy-paste deployable.

**Key Principles**:
1. **Funnel Width Consciousness** - Strategy calibrated to wide (mass) vs. narrow (niche) market reality
2. **The Two-Max Rule** - Maximum 2 new initiatives per 6 months regardless of team size
3. **Normie-Fringe Navigation** - Pre-position initiatives for approval difficulty
4. **$30-50M Wall Recognition** - Diagnose when "measurement addiction" caps growth
5. **Preparation Over Speed** - Success in cultural moments comes from preparation, not reaction
6. **Bottom-Funnel Intensity** - Sophisticated brands often need to ask for money more aggressively

## Key Frameworks (Quick Reference)

### Three-Stage Funnel
- **Awareness** (Top) → **Consideration** (Middle) → **Conversion** (Bottom)
- Grade each stage A-F, identify weakest link
- Funnel width varies: mass market (wide) vs. niche/enterprise (narrow)

### Normie-Fringe / Sales-Awareness Matrix
- X-axis: Normie (easy approval) ↔ Fringe (career bet)
- Y-axis: Sales (direct revenue) ↔ Awareness (brand building)
- Most brands over-indexed on Normie/Sales, under-indexed on Fringe/Awareness

### Content Pillar Architecture
- Pillar = Format + Topic combination repeated regularly
- Excavate existing pillars first (most brands have 3-5 never consciously designed)
- Map each pillar to funnel stage, grade A-F effectiveness

### Campaign Architecture
- Structure: Teaser → Unveil → Sustain
- Balance: 50/50 Internal (owned) vs. External (earned/partner) promotion
- Day-by-day execution calendars with preparation timelines

### The Two-Max Rule
- Hard cap of 2 new initiatives per 6 months regardless of team size
- More initiatives = diluted execution
