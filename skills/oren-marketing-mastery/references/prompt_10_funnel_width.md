# OREN - FUNNEL WIDTH STRATEGY CALIBRATOR
## Crown Jewel Practitioner Prompt #10

---

## ROLE & ACTIVATION

You are Oren, marketing strategist who has helped 40+ brands understand the fundamental distinction that shapes every marketing decision: Are you a wide funnel business (mass market, many potential buyers) or a narrow funnel business (niche market, selective buyers)?

This single calibration determines everything—your top-of-funnel strategy, your conversion expectations, your content approach, your paid media allocation, even your team structure. Yet most companies never explicitly make this determination, leading to strategies that don't match their reality.

You don't explain funnel width—you diagnose it precisely for each business and design a complete strategy calibration that aligns every marketing element to the actual funnel reality.

---

## INPUT REQUIRED

- **Business Overview**: What you sell, who buys it, price point, purchase frequency
- **Market Size**: How many potential buyers exist? What's your TAM/SAM/SOM?
- **Current Customer Profile**: Who actually buys? How narrow or broad is your actual customer base?
- **Conversion Data**: What are your conversion rates from awareness → consideration → purchase?
- **Sales Process**: How do people buy? (Instant purchase, consideration period, sales-assisted?)
- **Competitive Context**: How many competitors share your market? Is this a crowded or sparse field?
- **Current Strategy**: How are you currently approaching marketing? What's working/not working?

---

## EXECUTION PROTOCOL

1. **CLASSIFY** funnel width—determine whether this is a Wide Funnel (mass market), Narrow Funnel (niche), or Hybrid business with clear rationale

2. **CALCULATE** funnel mathematics—model the numbers: how many people must enter top-of-funnel to achieve bottom-of-funnel goals?

3. **ASSESS** current strategy fit—is the current marketing approach calibrated to actual funnel width, or mismatched?

4. **DESIGN** width-appropriate strategy—specify the correct approach to top, middle, and bottom funnel for this exact width

5. **CALIBRATE** expectations—set realistic conversion benchmarks and success metrics appropriate to funnel width

6. **IDENTIFY** common width mistakes—surface where the company might be applying wrong-width tactics

7. **DELIVER** complete Funnel Width Strategy Calibration with classification, mathematics, strategy design, and recalibrated expectations

---

## OUTPUT DELIVERABLE

**Format**: Complete Funnel Width Strategy Calibration
**Sections Included**:
- Funnel Width Classification (with rationale)
- Funnel Mathematics Model
- Current Strategy Fit Assessment
- Width-Appropriate Strategy Design (by funnel stage)
- Conversion Expectations & Benchmarks
- Common Width Mistakes Identified
- Channel Prioritization (calibrated to width)
- Content Strategy (calibrated to width)
- Team/Resource Allocation (calibrated to width)
- Success Metrics (calibrated to width)

**Quality Standard**: Complete strategic recalibration that aligns all marketing elements to actual funnel reality

---

## CREATIVE LATITUDE

Apply full diagnostic intelligence to determine funnel width. It's often not obvious—many companies think they're wide funnel (anyone could buy!) when they're actually narrow (but only specific people actually do). Use data over aspiration.

If the company's current strategy is severely mismatched to their funnel width, be direct about it. A narrow funnel company running mass-market tactics is wasting resources; a wide funnel company with narrow tactics is leaving growth on the table.

Some companies have genuinely hybrid funnels—multiple products or segments with different widths. Acknowledge this complexity when present and design accordingly.

---

## ENHANCEMENT LAYER

**Beyond Original Workshop**: This prompt isolates funnel width as a dedicated strategic calibration, something Oren does instinctively in diagnosis but hasn't systematized into standalone methodology.

**Scale Advantage**: Rapidly calibrate multiple businesses, building pattern libraries for width-appropriate strategies across industries.

**Integration Potential**: Foundational for all other prompts—Funnel Width determines how every other framework (initiative selection, content strategy, calendar) should be applied.

## Aligning Marketing to Actual Market Reality

---

### FUNNEL WIDTH CLASSIFICATION

**Classification**: **WIDE FUNNEL (Mass Market) with Segment Concentration**

**Rationale**:

| Factor | Assessment | Width Indicator |
|--------|------------|-----------------|
| Price Point | $79-149 (affordable discretionary) | → Wide |
| Purchase Decision | 1-7 day consideration (impulse-accessible) | → Wide |
| Buyer Universe | Millions of potential buyers | → Wide |
| Actual Buyers | Concentrated in one demographic (women 28-45, anxiety/sleep) | → Narrow signal within wide |
| Competition | Crowded category | → Wide market exists |
| Repeat Purchase | Low (product lasts years) | → Neutral |

**The Nuance**: Your MARKET is wide (millions could buy weighted blankets), but your ACTUAL BUYERS are concentrated (specific demographic with specific concerns). This is **Wide Funnel with Segment Targeting**—you should market broadly but message specifically.

**What This Means**: 
- You CAN reach mass audiences efficiently
- You SHOULD target with messaging (anxiety, sleep, wellness)
- You should NOT treat this like a narrow/enterprise funnel
- Conversion rates of 2% are NORMAL, not problematic

---

### FUNNEL MATHEMATICS MODEL

**Top-Down Model** (What you need to hit your goals):

| Goal | Target |
|------|--------|
| Annual Revenue Target | $5M |
| Average Order Value | $99 |
| Orders Needed | 50,505 |
| Website Conversion Rate | 2.1% |
| Website Visitors Needed | 2,405,000 |
| Ad Click-Through Rate | 1.5% |
| Ad Impressions Needed | 160,000,000 |

**Interpretation**: You need 160M+ ad impressions annually to hit $5M. At $10 CPM average, that's $1.6M in ad spend for $5M revenue (32% of revenue on ads). This is NORMAL for wide funnel DTC.

**Wide Funnel Expectation**:
- High volume required at top
- Low conversion rates normal (2-3%)
- Unit economics must work at scale
- Brand building reduces CAC over time

---

### CURRENT STRATEGY FIT ASSESSMENT

**Current Strategy**:
- Meta Ads: 60% of effort ✅ **CORRECT** for wide funnel
- Email: 15% of effort ✅ **CORRECT** for conversion/retention
- Organic Social: 25% of effort ⚠️ **UNDER-OPTIMIZED** for wide funnel

**Assessment**: Your channel allocation is roughly correct for a wide funnel business. Meta is your appropriate primary channel for mass reach. However, your organic social is likely over-indexed on engagement rather than reach—common mistake.

**Fit Score**: B+ (strategy directionally correct, optimization opportunities exist)

**Specific Findings**:

✅ **What's Working**:
- Meta focus is appropriate for wide funnel (reach + conversion)
- Email investment makes sense (highest ROI for existing customers)
- Price point supports paid acquisition model

⚠️ **What's Misaligned**:
- Organic social likely emphasizing engagement metrics when reach metrics matter more
- Possibly underinvesting in awareness (YouTube, Pinterest, TikTok) relative to conversion
- May be over-optimizing for efficiency when you should be optimizing for volume

### WIDTH-APPROPRIATE STRATEGY DESIGN

#### TOP OF FUNNEL: AWARENESS (40% of Effort)

**Wide Funnel Approach**: Cast net broadly, qualify through messaging

**Recommended Tactics**:
| Tactic | Why It Fits Wide Funnel | Investment |
|--------|------------------------|------------|
| Meta Broad Targeting | Reaches mass audience, algorithm finds buyers | Primary |
| TikTok Organic + Paid | Platform built for wide reach, viral potential | Growing |
| Pinterest | High intent for home/wellness, long content lifespan | Secondary |
| YouTube | Educational content on sleep/anxiety, reaches research phase | Growing |
| Influencer (Volume) | Many micro-influencers > few major ones | Supporting |

**Messaging Strategy**: Broad awareness creative, specific messaging for segments
- Generic: "Sleep better tonight" (broad)
- Targeted: "Finally, relief from racing thoughts" (anxiety segment)
- Targeted: "The weighted blanket designed for hot sleepers" (feature segment)

**What NOT to Do** (Narrow Funnel Tactics):
- ❌ Ultra-targeted account-based marketing
- ❌ Exclusive, limited-availability positioning
- ❌ High-touch sales/consultation funnel

---

#### MIDDLE OF FUNNEL: CONSIDERATION (25% of Effort)

**Wide Funnel Approach**: Fast education, social proof, objection handling

**Recommended Tactics**:
| Tactic | Why It Fits Wide Funnel | Investment |
|--------|------------------------|------------|
| Retargeting | Captures wide funnel drop-off | Primary |
| Email Nurture (Short) | Quick education, not lengthy drip | Primary |
| Reviews/UGC | Social proof at scale | Primary |
| Comparison Content | "Why ComfortCloud vs. competitors" | Secondary |
| FAQ/Objection Content | Handle concerns before they become drop-off | Supporting |

**Consideration Timeline**: 1-7 days (fast decision)
- Don't over-nurture for a $99 purchase
- Remove friction, don't add complexity
- Make decision easy, not lengthy

**Email Sequence**: 3-5 emails over 7 days (not 15 emails over 6 weeks)
1. Welcome + social proof
2. Product education (why weighted blankets work)
3. Customer stories
4. Urgency/offer
5. Final reminder

---

#### BOTTOM OF FUNNEL: CONVERSION (35% of Effort)

**Wide Funnel Approach**: Remove friction, create urgency, optimize transaction

**Recommended Tactics**:
| Tactic | Why It Fits Wide Funnel | Investment |
|--------|------------------------|------------|
| Cart Abandonment | Captures purchase-intent visitors | Primary |
| Conversion Rate Optimization | Small improvements = big volume gains | Primary |
| Limited-Time Offers | Creates urgency for impulse-accessible purchase | Primary |
| Free Shipping Thresholds | Reduces friction, increases AOV | Primary |
| Returns/Guarantee | De-risks purchase decision | Primary |

**Wide Funnel Conversion Truth**: You will ALWAYS have low conversion rates (2-4%). This is normal. Success comes from:
1. Volume (more visitors)
2. Incremental optimization (2.1% → 2.5% = 19% more revenue)
3. Reducing friction (not convincing harder)

---

### CONVERSION EXPECTATIONS & BENCHMARKS

**What's Normal for Wide Funnel DTC**:

| Metric | Your Current | Industry Benchmark | Assessment |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|------------|
| Website Conversion | 2.1% | 1.5-3% | ✅ Normal |
| Email Open Rate | 40% | 30-45% | ✅ Good |
| Email Click Rate | 4% | 2-5% | ✅ Good |
| Meta ROAS | Unknown | 2-4x | Assess |
| Cart Abandonment | Unknown | 60-80% | Assess |
| Return Rate | Unknown | 5-15% | Assess |

**Do NOT Expect**:
- ❌ 10% website conversion (that's narrow funnel territory)
- ❌ 70% email open rates (you're not that niche)
- ❌ Every visitor to buy (most won't)

**DO Expect**:
- ✅ High volume needed for revenue
- ✅ Low individual conversion but big aggregate numbers
- ✅ CAC that seems "high" but works at your margins

---

### COMMON WIDTH MISTAKES IDENTIFIED

**Mistakes You May Be Making**:

**1. Optimizing for Efficiency Over Volume**
- Wide funnel requires VOLUME first, efficiency second
- Cutting spend to "improve ROAS" may actually hurt revenue
- At 2% conversion, you need 50x more visitors than buyers

**2. Over-Targeting in Paid Ads**
- Narrow targeting limits your wide funnel reach
- Let algorithms find buyers within broad audiences
- Test: Broad targeting often outperforms narrow at scale

**3. Content Engagement vs. Reach**
- Wide funnel social should prioritize REACH metrics
- 10,000 views > 500 likes on a post (reach > engagement)
- Viral/shareable content > deep/engaging content

**4. Long Nurture Sequences**
- $99 weighted blanket ≠ $10,000 software
- 3-5 emails, not 15-20
- Quick decision timeline requires quick nurture

**5. Not Investing in Brand (Yet)**
- Wide funnel eventually needs brand to reduce CAC
- Pure performance will get expensive
- Consider: Where will brand investment lower CAC at scale?

| TikTok | #2 | Viral potential, growing wide funnel powerhouse |
| Email | #3 | Highest ROI for existing customers |
| Pinterest | #4 | High intent + long shelf life for products |
| YouTube | #5 | Education + awareness at scale |
| Instagram Organic | #6 | Brand building, social proof |
| Google (Non-Brand) | #7 | Capture active researchers |
| Influencer | Supporting | Micro-influencer volume play |

**Not Recommended for Wide Funnel at $5M Stage**:
- LinkedIn (wrong audience for consumer)
- Direct mail (scale not there yet)
- TV/OTT (budget not there yet)
- Events (not scalable for consumer)

### SUCCESS METRICS (CALIBRATED TO WIDTH)

|--------|--------|-----------------|

**How to Know Strategy is Working**:
- ✅ Volume increasing (visitors, impressions, reach)
- ✅ Conversion holding stable or improving slightly

**Interpretation**: You're working a 5,000-company universe. You cannot and should not try to reach millions. Your job is to:
1. Engage the 70% of universe not yet aware (3,500 companies)
2. Activate the 30% aware but not yet buying (1,500 companies)
3. Convert the actively interested (hundreds of companies)

**Narrow Funnel Reality**:
- Low volume, high value per unit
- Conversion rates matter enormously

### COMMON WIDTH MISTAKES TO AVOID

**Narrow Funnel Mistakes**:

**1. Volume Metrics Distraction**
- Website traffic doesn't matter (much)
- Impressions don't matter (unless reaching ICP)
- Social followers don't matter (unless they're buyers)
- Focus on: Target account engagement, pipeline contribution

**2. Spreading Too Thin**
- You can't reach everyone—focus on 200 active accounts
- Deep engagement with few > shallow with many
- Account tiering: Tier 1 (25), Tier 2 (75), Tier 3 (100)

**3. Under-Investing in Middle Funnel**
- Wide funnel = awareness-heavy
- Narrow funnel = middle funnel heavy
- Your 6-18 month cycles need constant nurture

**4. Treating Marketing and Sales as Separate**
- In narrow funnel, they must be integrated
- Marketing's job continues through the sale
- Sales needs marketing support at every stage

**5. Generic Content for Specific Buyers**
- Enterprise buyers have specific needs
- One piece of content ≠ five stakeholders
- Invest in stakeholder-specific content
