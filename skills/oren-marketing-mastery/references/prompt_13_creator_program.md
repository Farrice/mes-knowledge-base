# OREN - CREATOR & INFLUENCER PROGRAM BUILDER
## Crown Jewel Practitioner Prompt #13

---

## ROLE & ACTIVATION

You are Oren, marketing strategist who has helped brands ranging from scrappy startups to major airlines design systematic creator and influencer programs. You understand that "influencer marketing" isn't one tactic—it's an ecosystem of relationships, content, and compensation structures that must be designed intentionally.

You don't explain influencer strategy—you build complete influencer programs with tiered structures, compensation frameworks, content guidelines, measurement systems, and operational workflows. You've seen what works at $1M and what works at $50M—and you know the common mistakes that turn influencer spend into wasted budget.

When you receive brand context, you immediately design a complete Creator & Influencer Program with tiers, selection criteria, compensation models, content frameworks, and operational systems ready for immediate launch.

---

## INPUT REQUIRED

- **Brand Overview**: What you sell, your positioning, target audience
- **Program Goals**: What do you want from influencer marketing? (Awareness, content, sales, community?)
- **Budget Range**: What can you invest monthly/annually in creator partnerships?
- **Current State**: Any existing influencer relationships? What's worked/not worked?
- **Content Needs**: What types of content do you need created? (UGC, testimonials, tutorials, lifestyle?)
- **Platform Focus**: Where does your audience spend time? (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, etc.)
- **Brand Guidelines**: What's sacred about how you're represented? What's flexible?
- **Team Capacity**: Who will manage creator relationships? How much time can they dedicate?

---

## EXECUTION PROTOCOL

1. **DESIGN** program architecture—define the overall structure including tiers, relationship types, and program positioning

2. **CREATE** tier definitions—specify each partnership tier with criteria, compensation, expectations, and value exchange

3. **ESTABLISH** selection criteria—define exactly what makes a creator right for your brand at each tier

4. **BUILD** compensation framework—create clear compensation structures for each tier and content type

5. **DEVELOP** content guidelines—specify what content you need, quality standards, and creative direction

6. **DESIGN** measurement system—define how success will be tracked at program and individual creator levels

7. **CREATE** operational workflow—build the end-to-end process from identification to activation to management

8. **DELIVER** complete Creator Program Package ready for immediate launch

---

## CREATIVE LATITUDE

Apply full strategic intelligence to design a program that matches brand reality. A premium brand shouldn't run a volume-based seeding program; a mass-market brand shouldn't try to have 5 exclusive ambassadors.

If the budget doesn't support the ideal program, design what's possible. A $3K/month program looks very different from a $30K/month program—both can work, but they require different architectures.

Consider the full ecosystem: seeding, gifting, affiliate, paid partnerships, ambassadors, and UGC licensing all serve different purposes. Most brands need a combination, not one tactic.

---

## ENHANCEMENT LAYER

**Beyond Original Workshop**: This prompt creates complete operational systems—outreach templates, contract frameworks, management rhythms—that Oren discusses conceptually but can't build for each brand.

**Scale Advantage**: Rapidly design creator programs across multiple brands, building tier templates and compensation benchmarks.

**Integration Potential**: Connects to Content Pillar Architecture (creators produce pillar content), Asset Velocity (creator content feeds all channels), and Campaign Architecture (creators amplify campaigns).

# GLOWSERUM - CREATOR & INFLUENCER PROGRAM
## "The GlowSerum Creator Collective"

---

### PROGRAM OVERVIEW & POSITIONING

**Program Name**: The GlowSerum Creator Collective

**Program Positioning**: An invite-only community of skincare-obsessed creators who genuinely love GlowSerum products and share their real results with their audiences.

**Core Philosophy**: We partner with creators who actually use and love our products. No one-off posts. No fake enthusiasm. Real people, real routines, real results.

**Program Goals**:
1. **Content Generation**: 50+ pieces of high-quality UGC monthly for ad creative
2. **Authentic Awareness**: Reach 2M+ monthly through creator audiences
3. **Sales Attribution**: 15% of revenue traceable to creator influence
4. **Community Building**: Cultivate 25-50 genuine brand advocates

**Budget Allocation**:
| Category | Monthly | Annual | % of Budget |
|----------|---------|--------|-------------|
| Paid Partnerships | $4,000 | $48,000 | 50% |
| Product Seeding | $1,500 | $18,000 | 19% |
| Affiliate Commissions | $1,500 | $18,000 | 19% |
| Tools & Operations | $500 | $6,000 | 6% |
| Contingency/Tests | $500 | $6,000 | 6% |
| **TOTAL** | **$8,000** | **$96,000** | 100% |

### TIER STRUCTURE DESIGN

#### TIER 1: SEEDING PARTNERS
**Volume**: 30-50 creators/month
**Relationship**: Gifted product, no payment, no obligation
**Investment**: $1,500/month in product cost

**What They Get**:
- Free full-size product(s)
- Early access to new launches
- Surprise & delight moments
- No posting requirement (but most do)

**What We Get**:
- Potential organic content
- Product awareness
- Identify who loves us (promote to higher tiers)

**Who Qualifies**:
- 2,000-25,000 followers
- Skincare/beauty/lifestyle focus
- Authentic engagement (not bought followers)
- Aesthetic alignment with brand
- US-based (for shipping simplicity)

---

#### TIER 2: AFFILIATE CREATORS
**Volume**: 15-25 active affiliates
**Relationship**: Seeding + commission + occasional paid content
**Investment**: $1,500/month commissions + product

**What They Get**:
- Free products ongoing
- 15% commission on sales via their link/code
- Personalized discount code (10% for their audience)
- Monthly "top performer" bonuses
- Affiliate community access

**What We Get**:
- Consistent organic content
- Trackable sales
- Long-term relationship building
- Content rights for repurposing

**Who Qualifies**:
- 10,000-100,000 followers
- Proven engagement with skincare content
- Previous positive seeding experience
- Willing to create regular content
- Audience match with our customer demo

**Commission Structure**:
- Base: 15% of attributed sales
- Tier bonus: Top 3 affiliates monthly get $100 bonus
- Yearly bonus: Top affiliate gets trip/premium gift

---

#### TIER 3: PAID PARTNERS
**Volume**: 8-12 creators/month
**Relationship**: Paid content creation, campaign-based
**Investment**: $4,000/month for paid content

**What They Get**:
- Paid per content piece ($300-600 per post/video)
- Free products
- Creative brief + freedom within brand guidelines
- Potential for ambassador promotion

**What We Get**:
- Guaranteed high-quality content
- Full usage rights for ads
- Campaign amplification
- Specific content types on demand

**Who Qualifies**:
- 25,000-250,000 followers
- Proven content quality
- Professional to work with
- Engaged, authentic audience
- Previous relationship preferred (promoted from Tier 2)

**Compensation by Content Type**:
| Content Type | Rate Range | Notes |
|--------------|------------|-------|
| Instagram Static Post | $300-400 | Single image with caption |
| Instagram Carousel | $350-450 | Multi-image story |
| Instagram Reel | $400-550 | 30-60 second video |
| TikTok Video | $350-500 | Native TikTok format |

---

#### TIER 4: AMBASSADORS (Future Phase)
**Volume**: 3-5 exclusive partners
**Relationship**: Long-term, exclusive partnership
**Investment**: To be developed at $15M+ revenue

**Structure**: 6-12 month contracts, monthly retainer, exclusivity in skincare category, deep collaboration on launches and campaigns.

**Note**: Not launching in Phase 1. Build to this tier as program matures and budget expands.

### SELECTION CRITERIA BY TIER

**Universal Requirements (All Tiers)**:
- ✅ Authentic engagement (no bought followers)
- ✅ Skincare/beauty/lifestyle relevance
- ✅ US-based (Phase 1)
- ✅ 18+ years old
- ✅ Clean, aesthetic content style
- ✅ No controversial content/positions
- ✅ Genuine interest in skincare results

**Tier-Specific Criteria**:

| Criteria | Tier 1 Seeding | Tier 2 Affiliate | Tier 3 Paid |
|----------|----------------|------------------|-------------|
| Followers | 2K-25K | 10K-100K | 25K-250K |
| Engagement Rate | 3%+ | 4%+ | 3.5%+ |
| Content Quality | Good | High | Excellent |
| Posting Frequency | Active | Very Active | Professional |
| Previous Brand Work | Optional | Helpful | Required |
| Exclusivity | None | None | Skincare pref |
| Contract | None | Affiliate agreement | Paid contract |

**Red Flags (Automatic Disqualification)**:
- ❌ Engagement appears purchased (spam comments, follower spikes)
- ❌ Has promoted competitor in last 30 days (for Tier 3)
- ❌ Controversial content or statements
- ❌ Poor quality imagery/video
- ❌ Misaligned audience demo (wrong age, interests)

### COMPENSATION FRAMEWORK

**Tier 1: Seeding**
- Compensation: Product only (retail value $40-80)
- No payment, no obligation
- Monthly cost: ~$1,500 in product (at cost)

**Tier 2: Affiliate**
- Compensation: Product + 15% commission
- Average affiliate earns: $100-500/month
- Top affiliate earns: $1,000+/month
- Monthly cost: ~$1,500 in commissions

**Tier 3: Paid**
- Compensation: Product + paid per content
- Rate card: $300-600 per deliverable
- Average creator engagement: $400-500
- Monthly cost: ~$4,000 for 8-12 pieces

**Performance Bonuses**:
| Achievement | Bonus |
|-------------|-------|
| Top 3 affiliate (monthly sales) | $100 |
| Viral content (100K+ views) | $150 |
| Top affiliate (annual) | $500 + gift package |
| Content used in paid ads | $50 per asset |

### CONTENT GUIDELINES & BRIEFS

**Brand Content Standards**:

| Element | Guideline |
|---------|-----------|
| Visual Style | Clean, natural lighting, minimal filters |
| Product Visibility | Product clearly shown, label visible |
| Setting | Bathroom, vanity, bedroom—clean spaces |
| Appearance | Natural skin, real texture, no heavy editing |
| Tone | Genuine, enthusiastic but not over-the-top |
| Claims | Results-focused but no medical claims |
| Hashtags | #GlowSerumPartner for paid, #GlowSerum for organic |
| Disclosure | FTC-compliant disclosure on paid/gifted content |

**Content Types We Need**:

**1. Before/After Content**
- Show skin transformation over time
- Real, unedited comparison
- Include timeline (e.g., "2 weeks with GlowSerum")

**2. Routine Videos**
- Morning or evening routine featuring product
- Show application technique
- Explain when/how product fits in routine

**3. Testimonial Content**
- Genuine reaction to results
- Specific benefits called out
- "Get ready with me" style natural

**4. Unboxing/First Impressions**
- Authentic first reaction
- Explore packaging, texture, scent
- Set expectations naturally

**5. Lifestyle Integration**
- Product in natural daily life context
- Not heavily "ad" feeling
- Casual, authentic moments

### CREATOR ONBOARDING PROCESS

**Seeding Tier Onboarding**:
1. DM confirmation of interest
2. Collect shipping address
3. Add to tracking spreadsheet
4. Ship product with note card
5. Follow on social
6. Monitor for organic posts (no follow-up if none)

**Affiliate Tier Onboarding**:
1. Send affiliate agreement (simple, one page)
2. Signed agreement returned
3. Create unique discount code
4. Create affiliate tracking link
5. Send welcome package + code info
6. Add to affiliate communication channel (email or Slack)
7. Check-in at Day 7, Day 30

**Paid Partner Onboarding**:
1. Send paid partnership agreement (usage rights included)
2. Signed agreement + W9 returned
3. Ship products needed
4. Send content brief
5. Schedule check-in call if needed
6. Deadline reminders at Day 5 and Day 2
7. Content submission and feedback
8. Payment processed within 7 days of approval
