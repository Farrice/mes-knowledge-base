# Articulation Mastery Sprint

> **Produces**: Complete articulation transformation — content that went from flat to gripping, from monotone to rhythmic, from informative to irresistible. The full stack applied in sequence.

## When to Use
- Need maximum transformation of any content piece
- Starting with a rough draft and want professional-grade articulation
- Combining all Word Mastery techniques into a single comprehensive pass
- Preparing a flagship piece (pillar content, launch post, sales page, keynote)
- Demonstrate the full articulation difference for before/after comparison

## Input Required
1. **Content to transform** — any draft, at any quality level
2. **Target outcome** — what this piece needs to accomplish (engage, persuade, teach, inspire)
3. **Target tone** — the desired register (or blend)
4. **Voice reference** (optional) — sample of the author's natural voice
5. **Proof inventory** (optional) — stats, results, data available for injection

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: Before executing, run the **Decision Framework** in `genius.md` § Decision Framework. Confirm all diagnostic questions are answered.


## Execution Protocol

### Pass 0: Pre-Flight (Anxiety Physics)
Before touching the content, calibrate:

**Throwaway dump**: If the content feels stiff or cold-started, write 200 words of throwaway content on the topic. Discard. This warms up the creative pathways.

**Friend Photo**: Identify the single person this piece is FOR. Name them. Write to them. Not a persona — a real person.

**HTBT Energy Gate**: Does the writer's energy come through? If the content was written under obligation, no amount of editing fixes the underlying energy deficit. Flag this honestly.

```

────────────────

Energy State: [HTBT / Neutral / Forced]
Voice Pocket Match: [Natural / Slightly Off / Foreign]
Proof Available: [Yes — list / No]
Estimated Transformation Potential: [Moderate / High / Complete Overhaul]
```

### Pass 1: Rhythm Architecture
Execute the full [Rhythm Rewrite](rhythm-rewrite.md) protocol:
1. Sentence compression (target ≤15 words average)
2. Breather injection (3-5 per 100 words)
3. Downward inflection engineering (70% ratio)
4. Anti-dropping phrase fix
5. Variance engineering (mix of 5-word and 15-word sentences)

### Pass 2: Tone Calibration
Execute the [Tone Calibration Engine](tone-calibration-engine.md):
1. Diagnose current register
2. Shift to target register using the linguistic toolkit
3. Inject 1-2 code-switches for texture
4. Apply the Likable Expert formality-trust calibration (informal delivery + formal proof)

### Pass 3: Grip & Tension
Execute the [Grip & Tension Engine](grip-and-tension-engine.md):
1. Engineer the opening grip (first sentence demands the second)
2. Build tension thread architecture (unresolved question pulling through)
3. Paragraph-to-paragraph pull (every paragraph creates demand for the next)
4. Insight landing architecture (build-up → breather → insight → space)
5. Closing impact engineering (resonant ending)

### Pass 4: Likable Expert Filter
Execute the [Likable Expert Filter](likable-expert-filter.md):
1. Proof-heavy framing (specific evidence for every claim)
2. Admission line injection (1-2 genuine vulnerabilities with resolution)
3. HTBT energy verification
4. Usefulness-first reframe (service energy, not extraction energy)

### Pass 5: Believability Polish
Execute a condensed [Believability Audit](believability-audit.md):
1. Scan for template language and replace with original phrasing
2. Check sophistication variance (not robotically uniform)
3. Verify voice pocket alignment
4. Add idiosyncratic details for texture

### Pass 6: Final Assembly & Quality Gate
Reassemble all passes into the final piece. Run the complete quality audit:

## Quality Gate (Non-Negotiable)
- [ ] Average sentence ≤ 15 words?
- [ ] 70%+ downward inflection?
- [ ] First sentence demands the second?
- [ ] Tension thread runs through the piece?
- [ ] Every claim has proof within 2 sentences?
- [ ] At least one genuine admission with resolution?
- [ ] HTBT energy is palpable?
- [ ] Zero template phrases?
- [ ] Voice pocket alignment ≥ 8/10?
- [ ] Reader cannot stop reading mid-piece?
- [ ] Overall quality score ≥ 8/10?
