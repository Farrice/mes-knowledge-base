# Rhythm Rewrite

> **Produces**: Content with engineered pacing — every sentence optimized for cognitive processing, attention direction, and authority signaling

## When to Use
- Content reads flat despite strong ideas
- Sentences all feel the same length and energy
- Reader attention drops mid-paragraph
- Key insights don't "land" with impact
- Content sounds like a lecture, not a conversation

## Input Required
1. **Content to rewrite** — any draft, article, post, or script
2. **Format context** — where this will appear (affects rhythm targets)
3. **Key insights** (optional) — which ideas need to hit hardest

## Execution Protocol

### Step 1: Rhythm Diagnostic
Run the content through a 4-dimension rhythm analysis:

```
RHYTHM AUDIT
────────────
Avg Sentence Length: [X words] (target: ≤15)
Sentence Length Variance: [low/medium/high] (target: high)
Breather Count: [X per 100 words] (target: 3-5)
Downward Inflection Ratio: [X%] (target: 70%)
Dropping Phrases: [count of sentences where energy fades before end]
Longest Sentence: [X words — flag if >25]
Shortest Sentence: [X words — need some ≤5]
```

### Step 2: Sentence Compression Pass
Apply GP-WM-06:
- **Cut every sentence over 20 words in half** unless there's a deliberate reason for length
- **6 words > 15 words** — if you can say it shorter, say it shorter
- **Target**: No sentence over 25 words. Average under 15
- **The brain processes in chunks of 3-4 items** — fewer words per chunk = more comprehension

**Before**: "The reason that most content creators struggle to keep their audience engaged throughout the entire piece is that they don't understand the cognitive limitations of their reader's working memory."
**After**: "Most creators lose their audience halfway through. The reason is simple. The brain has limits. Respect them."

### Step 3: Breather Injection
Apply GP-WM-07:
1. Identify the single most important word in each key sentence (the "insight carrier")
2. Place a pause immediately after that word: period, em-dash, line break, or paragraph break
3. Let it breathe — the reader's working memory needs 1-2 seconds to consolidate before processing new input

**The principle**: Strategic pauses create 15-25% comprehension gain in dense content. Breathers direct attention to what matters most.

**Before**: "The key to great content is understanding that rhythm and pacing are what separate good writers from great ones because without it everything runs together."
**After**: "Rhythm. That's what separates good from great. Without it — everything runs together."

### Step 4: Downward Inflection Engineering
Apply GP-WM-08:
- **Audit the last 3 words of every sentence** — if they're qualifiers, hedges, or weak connectors, rewrite
- **Target 70% downward-ending sentences**: "This works." "That's the difference." "It compounds."
- **Allow 30% rising patterns** for engagement: questions, open loops, ellipses
- **Eliminate written upspeak**: "I think this could potentially work for some people…" → "This works."

**Power-word endings**: Final word of each sentence should be a strong noun or verb. "The problem isn't effort." Not: "The problem isn't about the amount of effort you put in."

### Step 5: Anti-Drop-Phrase Fix
Apply GP-WM-09:
- **Scan every sentence for trailing energy loss** — strong opening that dribbles into a weak ending
- **The fix**: Cut at the energy peak

**Before**: "This incredible framework will completely transform your ability to potentially start thinking about ideas in a more structured way going forward."
**After**: "This framework transforms how you think."

The power died at "ability." Everything after: energy leak. Cut.

### Step 6: Variance Engineering
After compression, breathers, and inflection — check the rhythm variance:
- **Mix sentence lengths**: 5 words. Then 12. Then 8. Then 20 (with purpose). Then 3.
- **Monotone length = monotone reading experience**
- **Short sentences create urgency. Longer sentences create flow and allow for nuance — when used deliberately.**
- **One-word sentences create emphasis.** Sparingly.

### Step 7: Reassembly & Polish
Reassemble the content with all rhythm changes applied. Read the full piece aloud (or simulate reading aloud mentally). Check:
- Does every sentence feel fresh?
- Do key insights land with weight?
- Is there a pull through each paragraph?
- Does the piece end on the strongest possible note (downward inflection)?

## Output Specification

**DELIVERABLE**: Fully rhythm-rewritten content plus:
1. **Before/After rhythm metrics** (sentence length avg, variance, breather count, inflection ratio)
2. **5 key interventions annotated** — the most impactful rhythm changes with reasoning
3. **Read-aloud test verdict** — does the piece flow when spoken?

## Quality Gate
- [ ] Average sentence length ≤ 15 words?
- [ ] No sentence > 25 words without deliberate purpose?
- [ ] 3-5 breathers per 100 words?
- [ ] 70%+ sentences end with downward inflection?
- [ ] Zero dropping phrases (energy consistent start-to-finish)?
- [ ] Sentence length variance is high (mix of short and medium)?
- [ ] Key insights hit with weight — reader can identify the thesis of each paragraph?
