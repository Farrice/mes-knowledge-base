# Code-Switch Architect

> **Produces**: Content with deliberate tonal texture — the entertainment layer that makes educational content feel like a conversation with the smartest, most interesting person you know

## When to Use
- Content is monotone — same energy and register from start to finish
- Educational content needs an entertainment layer without sacrificing authority
- Writing needs to feel dynamic and alive, not like a lecture
- Building content for platforms where engagement requires holding attention (LinkedIn, newsletters, YouTube scripts)
- Need to make dense, expert-level material ENJOYABLE to read
- Creating flagship content that showcases range

## The Insight

Expert writers shift between registers WITHIN a single piece. The switch itself creates energy. Monotone register — even a GOOD register — becomes forgettable after 200 words. The human brain is wired to notice pattern breaks. Code-switching IS the pattern break.

**The 5 registers** (from genius.md):
- **Casual**: Contractions, short, direct, first-person, active voice
- **Professional**: Formal vocabulary, complex structures, measured pacing
- **Entertaining**: Story-driven, varied lengths, rhetorical questions
- **Comedic**: Punchy fragments, hyperbole, unexpected juxtapositions, rule-of-three
- **Authoritative**: Precise language, declarative, data anchors, downward inflection

**The Kallaway solution**: You don't pick one. You SEQUENCE them.

## Input Required
1. **Content** — any draft (or topic to build from scratch)
2. **Primary register** — what the piece should feel like overall
3. **Emotional arc** — what should the reader feel at the beginning vs. end?
4. **Platform** — where this will live (determines register range)

## Execution Protocol

### Step 1: Register Audit
Map the current register across the entire piece:

```
REGISTER MAP
────────────
Para 1: [Casual / Pro / Entertaining / Comedic / Authoritative]
Para 2: [...]
Para 3: [...]
...
Register Diversity Score: [1-5] (1 = monotone, 5 = full range)
Switch Points: [count]
Emotional Arc: [Flat / Rising / Arc / None]
```

### Step 2: Emotional Arc Design
Before deciding WHERE to switch, decide the EMOTIONAL JOURNEY:

**Classic arcs for content**:

| Arc | Sequence | Best For |
|-----|----------|----------|
| **Hook → Teach → Punch** | Casual → Authoritative → Comedic | LinkedIn posts, hot takes |
| **Story → Insight → Action** | Entertaining → Authoritative → Casual | Newsletter essays, blog posts |
| **Challenge → Evidence → Revolution** | Authoritative → Professional → Casual | Thought leadership, contrarian takes |
| **Confession → Lesson → Manifesto** | Casual (intimate) → Entertaining → Authoritative | Personal brand content, vulnerability pieces |
| **Comedy → Depth → Revelation** | Comedic → Professional → Authoritative | YouTube scripts, podcast intros |
| **Whisper → Build → Thunder** | Casual (quiet) → Entertaining → Authoritative (full power) | Keynote content, flagship pieces |

### Step 3: Switch Point Engineering
WHERE you switch registers matters as much as WHICH registers you use:

**The 3 natural switch points**:
1. **After the hook** — reel them in casual, then shift to authority to keep them
2. **Before the key insight** — switch to a different register to signal "pay attention, this is the important part"
3. **At the close** — shift to the register that creates the emotional response you want them to leave with

**Switch mechanics**:

**The Hard Cut**: Abrupt register change via line break. Maximum impact.
```
"I spent six figures learning this the hard way.

Here's the data.

The average content creator..."
```
(Casual → Authoritative — the hard cut signals a gear shift)

**The Fade**: Gradual transition over 2-3 sentences. Subtle, sophisticated.
```
"Look, I get it — nobody wants to hear about algorithms. But here's the thing about
algorithmic distribution that most casual observers consistently misunderstand..."
```
(Casual fading into Professional)

**The Puncture**: A single sentence in a completely different register, surrounded by the main register. Creates a surprise.
```
"The research demonstrates clear correlational patterns between early-stage engagement
metrics and long-tail content distribution outcomes.

Translation: post early, or nobody sees it.

To quantify this relationship..."
```
(Professional → Comedic puncture → Professional)

### Step 4: The Likable Expert Blend
The master code-switch: informal delivery + formal proof. Sounds like a friend who happens to be the world's leading expert.

**How to blend**:
- Sentence STRUCTURE stays casual (short, direct, active voice)
- Sentence CONTENT carries formal-level precision (specific data, exact numbers, precise terminology)
- Think: texting style with PhD-level information

**Before** (pure formal): "Research indicates a statistically significant correlation between sentence brevity and reader comprehension metrics."

**After** (Likable Expert blend): "Shorter sentences literally make your brain work less. The research is wild — 50-130 characters per sentence and comprehension jumps 15-25%."

Same information. One sounds like a paper. The other sounds like a friend who reads papers.

### Step 5: Entertainment Injection Points
Identify where the piece needs ENERGY and inject register-appropriate entertainment:

- **Comedic relief after dense material**: One punchy, self-aware observation after a heavy paragraph
- **Story mode before a data dump**: Narrate how you discovered the data before presenting it
- **Aside energy**: Parenthetical observations in a different register than the main text (this is where personality lives)
- **The "let me be real" shift**: Drop from formal to raw casual for one sentence — signals authenticity

### Step 6: Coherence Check
Ensure switches ENHANCE rather than confuse:

- [ ] Does each switch feel intentional, not accidental?
- [ ] Is the primary register clear despite the switches? (Reader should know what KIND of piece this is)
- [ ] Do the switches serve the emotional arc?
- [ ] Does the piece feel like ONE voice with range, not multiple authors patched together?
- [ ] Are there at least 2 switch points but no more than 5 per 1,000 words?

## Output Specification

**DELIVERABLE**:
1. **Code-switched content** — the piece with deliberate register orchestration
2. **Register map** — before/after showing the emotional arc via registers
3. **Switch point annotations** — where each switch happens, what type, and why
4. **Entertainment density report** — where energy was injected and what type
5. **Primary register + range** — the tonal identity and its creative extensions

## Quality Gate
- [ ] Minimum 2, maximum 5 register switches per 1,000 words?
- [ ] Emotional arc is deliberate and discernible?
- [ ] Switches feel natural — ONE voice with range, not patchwork?
- [ ] Entertainment moments land without undermining authority?
- [ ] The Likable Expert blend is present in at least 30% of the piece?
- [ ] Reader is both entertained AND educated?
- [ ] Content would survive a "read this out loud" test without tonal whiplash?
