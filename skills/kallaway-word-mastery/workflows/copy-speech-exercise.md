# Copy-Speech Exercise

> **Produces**: Annotated model analysis + extracted rhythm template + mirror-written original piece using the model's structural DNA

## When to Use
- Building articulation muscles — want to internalize another writer's rhythm
- Admire a specific writer's style but can't pinpoint WHY their writing works
- Trying to level up your writing quality by studying masters
- Need a structured practice exercise for articulation development

## Input Required
1. **Model piece** — a specific piece of writing you admire (URL, pasted text, or title)
2. **Your topic** — what you want to write about using the model's rhythm
3. **What you admire** — specifically what draws you to this piece (rhythm? grip? tone? all?)

## Execution Protocol

### Step 1: Model Selection & Framing
Confirm the model piece and frame the exercise:

```
COPY-SPEECH SETUP
─────────────────
Model: [Title/Author/Source]
Model's Domain: [What they write about]
Your Domain: [What you'll write about — must be DIFFERENT]
What to Extract: [rhythm / tempo / emphasis / tone / all]
Exercise Goal: Internalize the model's structural DNA, not their voice
```

### Step 2: Deep Model Analysis
Analyze the model piece across 5 rhythm dimensions:

**A. Sentence Length Map**
Chart the sentence lengths through the piece. Where are the short punches (≤5 words)? Where are the flowing passages (15-25 words)? What's the rhythm pattern — does it accelerate, alternate, or pulse?

**B. Breather Map**
Identify every strategic pause — where the writer creates space for a word or idea to land. Mark them with [BREATHE]. What pattern emerges?

**C. Inflection Map**
Mark each sentence: ↓ (downward/authoritative) or ↑ (upward/curious/opening). What's the ratio? How does it create the overall tone?

**D. Tension Thread Trace**
What's the main unresolved question? Where does the writer add friction? Where does resolution land? Map the tension arc.

**E. Energy Flow**
Where is the energy highest? Lowest? How does it shift? Is there a consistent pulse or deliberate variation?

### Step 3: Rhythm Template Extraction
From the analysis, extract a structural template:

```
RHYTHM TEMPLATE
───────────────
Pattern: [e.g., "Short-Short-Medium-Short-Long-Short-Breathe"]
Inflection Ratio: [e.g., "70% down, 30% up"]
Breather Frequency: [e.g., "Every 3-4 sentences"]
Tension Pattern: [e.g., "Open loop → 4 paragraphs of friction → resolve"]
Signature Move: [e.g., "One-word sentences for emphasis after a build-up"]
Energy Shape: [e.g., "High-dip-escalate-peak-resolve"]
```

### Step 4: Mirror-Writing Exercise
Write your own piece on YOUR topic using the model's rhythm template:

1. **Follow the sentence length map** — match the pattern, not the words
2. **Place breathers at the same structural points** — same rhythm, different content
3. **Match the inflection ratio** — same authority/curiosity balance
4. **Build a parallel tension thread** — different question, same arc shape
5. **Echo the energy shape** — highs and lows in the same places

**Critical**: You are absorbing the *rhythm*, not the *voice*. The content, vocabulary, and personality should be entirely yours. Only the structural bones are borrowed.

### Step 5: Detachment Check
After writing, read your piece without reference to the model:
- Does it sound like YOU, not them?
- Would someone familiar with the model recognize the rhythm influence?
- Does the rhythm serve YOUR content, or feel forced?

If it sounds too derivative → loosen the template. Keep the macro rhythm, vary the micro.

### Step 6: Pattern Embedding Assessment
After 3-5 copy-speech exercises with the same model, the patterns naturally embed. Test:
- Write freely on a new topic without reference to any template
- Check: do the model's rhythm patterns appear naturally in your writing?
- If yes → the muscle memory has locked. Move to a new model.

## Output Specification

**DELIVERABLE**:
1. **Full model analysis** with annotated rhythm map across 5 dimensions
2. **Extracted rhythm template** — the structural DNA as a reusable pattern
3. **Mirror-written original piece** on your topic using the model's structure
4. **Detachment audit** — confirming the piece sounds like you, not them

## Quality Gate
- [ ] Model analysis reveals non-obvious structural patterns, not just "good writing"?
- [ ] Rhythm template is specific enough to reproduce, abstract enough to transfer?
- [ ] Mirror-written piece is genuinely original content with borrowed structure?
- [ ] Detachment check passes — sounds like your voice, not the model's?
- [ ] The exercise produces a reusable rhythm template for future writing?
