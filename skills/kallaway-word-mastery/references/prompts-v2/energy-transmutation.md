## Role & Activation

You are transmuting extraction energy into service energy — flipping content from "asking for a favor" to "doing you a favor." There are exactly two energy polarities in content: extraction energy (the writer wants something FROM the reader — views, follows, clicks, purchases; readers sense this within seconds even when well-disguised, and it creates the subtle pressure of talking to someone at a party who keeps looking over your shoulder) and service energy (the writer wants to be USEFUL TO the reader; this creates magnetic pull, like talking to someone who makes the reader feel like the most important person in the room). The key mechanism: extraction energy cannot be edited out — it can only be transmuted by genuinely shifting the writer's intention. This workflow changes the intention embedded in the prose, not just the words.

## Input Required

1. **[CONTENT_TO_TRANSMUTE]** — any draft
2. **[WHAT_THE_WRITER_ACTUALLY_WANTS]** — honest statement (followers? revenue? authority? all three?)
3. **[WHAT_THE_READER_ACTUALLY_NEEDS]** — the genuine value in this content
4. **[THE_RELATIONSHIP]** — first-touch audience or existing audience

## Execution Protocol

### Step 1 — Energy Polarity Diagnosis
Map every paragraph: Extraction / Service / Neutral, with the specific phrases as evidence. State the overall polarity (Extraction-Heavy / Mixed / Service-Heavy), run the Favor Test (does this read like doing a favor or asking for one), and detect any hidden agenda leaking through unspoken.

### Step 2 — Identify Extraction Markers
Scan for the specific linguistic signatures:

| Marker | Example | Feels Like |
|---|---|---|
| CTA-first framing | Opens with or centers "subscribe/follow/buy" | "This exists to convert me" |
| Engagement bait | "Comment below!" without genuine curiosity | "They need my engagement, not my opinion" |
| Manufactured urgency | "You NEED to know this" / "Don't miss this" | "They're afraid I'll scroll past" |
| Authority posturing | Leading with credentials rather than demonstrating expertise | "They're trying to convince me they're qualified" |
| Hedge-free certainty | Zero nuance, absolute claims, no admitted complexity | "They're performing confidence" |
| Value extraction structure | Free content is a trailer for paid content, not standalone value | "This is an ad disguised as a post" |
| Metric broadcasting | "I made $X / grew Y followers" as proof, not story | "They're flexing, not teaching" |

### Step 3 — Service Energy Injection
Apply the transmutation to each marker found:
- **CTA-first → Value-first**: restructure so complete value lands before any ask; the reader should feel served whether or not they take the CTA
- **Engagement bait → Genuine invitation**: replace "Comment below!" with an observation that naturally invites response ("I've noticed people split into two camps on this — curious where you land")
- **Manufactured urgency → Genuine enthusiasm**: urgency says "you'll lose something"; enthusiasm says "I found something amazing and want to share it" — rewrite urgency as discovery-energy
- **Authority posturing → Authority demonstration**: replace "I'm an expert in X" with specific, surprising observations only an expert would make — show, don't claim
- **Metric flexing → Metric contextualizing**: "I grew to 47K followers" becomes "At 2,000 followers, I tried X. By 47K, I'd learned Y. Here's the specific thing that changed."

### Step 4 — HTBT Energy Gate
Check whether the content was created with Happy-To-Be-There energy: can the reader sense enthusiasm without the writer saying "I'm excited"? does the content feel like it was created by choice or obligation? is there genuine curiosity or discovery energy in the prose? If HTBT is missing, no amount of editing fixes it — flag honestly and offer options: identify the one insight in the piece that genuinely excites the writer and restructure around it; add personal context that reconnects the writer to why they care; in extreme cases, recommend shelving the piece until HTBT is authentic.

### Step 5 — The Favor Test
Final verification across three questions: does this feel like the writer is doing the reader a favor by sharing, or asking for one? would the writer share this with a friend for free, enthusiastically, or only if there was a business reason? if the CTA were stripped entirely, would this piece still feel complete, or does it feel like a trailer?

### Step 6 — Energy Coherence Audit
Ensure the energy is consistent start to finish: opening energy equals middle energy equals closing energy (no dropping from service to extraction at the CTA); any CTA present should feel like an extension of the service, not a pivot to extraction ("If this was useful, here's the deeper version" beats "Now buy my course"); emotional temperature stays consistent with no sudden shift from warm to transactional.

## Output Contract

- **Transmuted content**: service-energy prose that pulls rather than pushes
- **Energy polarity map**: before/after for every paragraph
- **Extraction markers**: the specific phrases/structures found leaking extraction energy
- **Favor test results**: before/after comparison across all three questions
- **HTBT assessment**: honest evaluation of the underlying energy state
- **Transmutation notes**: patterns to watch for in future writing

## Quality Gate
- [ ] Every extraction marker identified in Step 2 has a corresponding transmutation applied, not just relabeled?
- [ ] Metric flexing (if present) is contextualized into a specific story rather than deleted outright — the proof value is preserved, only the polarity changes?
- [ ] If HTBT energy is genuinely absent, is that flagged honestly rather than manufactured?
- [ ] Any CTA in the final piece reads as an extension of value, never a pivot?
- [ ] Energy coherence holds from the first sentence to the last, including the close?

## Creative Latitude

The extraction-marker taxonomy names the failure patterns, but the actual rewrite of each flagged line is where the craft lives — the goal is genuine, specific, felt enthusiasm on the page, not a formulaic swap of "you need this" for "here's something cool." Where the source content's real personal stakes and honest motivations are, lean into those rather than inventing generic discovery-energy language. If the content genuinely can't be transmuted without HTBT energy present, say so plainly rather than forcing a polished-sounding rewrite over a hollow core.

## Deploy When

Content feels subtly "salesy" even when meant to be educational; the writer is creating under obligation, not enthusiasm; content passes technical quality checks but readers don't engage; the content is sensed to be "asking" rather than "giving"; AI-generated content needs the human energy layer injected; launching into a monetization phase and content needs to sell without selling.
