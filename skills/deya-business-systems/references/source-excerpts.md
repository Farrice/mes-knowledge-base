## §4 — Beta Price Ladder
> "...initial pricing of my product I think I started it cheaper I think it was like $5 for the first 10 people then $10 for the next 20 people and now it's $15..."
> "...I always recommend just beta test your service give it to a few people maybe the first person gets it for free let the market tell you how much your thing should cost and increase the price point with each additional client so that's what I did... I increased my price point by like 10 to 15 to 20% with each additional client..."

## §11 — Anti-Pattern: 50-page plan / planning theater
> "I've started two six figure businesses of my own and helped clients scale to seven figures and I have never once written a 50-page business plan I think they are a huge waste of time and get you stuck in that planning and thinking phase instead of taking action which is the most important thing if you want to start a business."

## §12 — Anti-Pattern: build before validating
> "...this is another huge mistake I see business owners do is they just they're like I have a good idea I'm just going to do it and then they start making the thing without even checking that the market wants to pay money for this thing and we can't forget that we operate in a market which means supply and demand is a real thing."

## §13 — Anti-Pattern: vague problem definition
> "...your problem has to be ultra specific these things I listed here are not specific enough problems lack of clarity is not specific enough overwhelm is not specific enough being stuck in business is not specific enough if it's not specific it's just not going to succeed."
