# Deya — Business Systems & Validation OS

Deya (creator, digital business manager, founder of DBM Bootcamp) built two six-figure businesses and helped clients scale to seven figures without ever writing a 50-page business plan. Her system replaces planning theater with a 1-page, 2-step engine: validate the Person-Problem-Product triad, then run a simplified sales funnel — optimizing for learning velocity, not planning completeness. The core question shifts from "Is this a good idea?" to "Will a specific named person pay for this today?"

## Quick Reference

- **The 1-page plan has 2 steps**: (1) Is the product idea worthwhile? — Person, Problem, Product. (2) Simplified sales funnel — Traffic, Nurture, Sales.
- **Name a specific person** — a real individual you know or follow, never "women 20-35." Do not pass go until you truly understand this person.
- **Problem severity 1-10** — 1 = pen-cap annoyance, 10 = keeps them up at 2 a.m. Severity sets pricing power. Low-severity problems are fine for low-priced products.
- **Dual gate** — the problem must be worth paying to solve AND the person must be able to afford the solution. Both or kill.
- **Customer voice** — capture how the person describes the problem in their own words; those words become the marketing and sales copy.
- **Passion floor** — founder must care 6-7/10 minimum about solving the problem or they won't survive the growing pains.
- **Beta price ladder** — start low ($5 first 10, $10 next 20...), collect testimonials, raise price with each validation cycle; freelancers +10-20% per new client.
- **Specificity beats saturation** — resonate 100% with a small market rather than 0.001% with a huge one. Generic = replaceable (AI demolished the copying barrier).
- **One platform, all in** — pick where your person actually is; master it before expanding.
- **Touch points scale with price** — $15 product needs 2-3 content touches; high-ticket retainers need case studies, email nurture, and a named diagnostic call.
- **Validation = transactions, not compliments** — talk to 5-10 versions of the person, fake landing pages, pop-up taste tests, beta sales. "I paid for it — boom, validated."
