# /jensen-acosta-growth — Authentic Growth Stack

## Purpose
Combine insider algorithm psychology with Acosta's SLAY execution for growth that's platform-aligned, not platform-fighting.

## Stacking Partners
- **Jessica Jensen** — Platform Intelligence (WHY the platform works this way)
- **Lara Acosta** — SLAY Framework (HOW to execute for growth)

## When to Use
- When deploying Acosta's tactical playbook and wanting to ensure platform alignment
- When growth has plateaued and the question is "am I fighting the algorithm?"
- When building a LinkedIn growth strategy from scratch with maximum efficiency
- When reconciling conflicting advice from multiple LinkedIn practitioners

## Inputs
- `[CREATOR]` — Who is growing their LinkedIn presence
- `[CURRENT_STATE]` — Follower count, engagement rate, content frequency, growth trajectory
- `[GOALS]` — Specific growth targets (followers, engagement, leads, revenue)
- `[TIMEFRAME]` — Target timeline

## Steps

### Step 1 — Jensen Platform Intelligence Layer
Run `/jensen-platform-intel` diagnostic on the creator's current strategy:
- Supply-demand dynamics in their niche
- AI authenticity risk assessment
- Format alignment with platform preferences
- Comment Jedi compliance score

### Step 2 — Acosta SLAY Calibration
Load Acosta's SLAY framework and calibrate each element against Jensen's insider intelligence:

| SLAY Element | Acosta Tactic | Jensen Calibration |
|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **S — Story** | Personal narrative hooks | Must pass AI authenticity gradient — stories are your moat |
| **L — Lift** | Content repurposing and distribution | Optimize for dual distribution (feed + LLM citation) |
| **A — Audience** | ICP targeting and engagement | Factor 50% job-seekers and 95/5 out-of-market ratio |
| **Y — Yield** | Conversion and monetization | Design for 7-month B2B cycle, not immediate conversion |

### Step 3 — Growth Architecture (Calibrated)
Build the unified growth plan:

**Content Engine** (Acosta framework + Jensen alignment)
- Content mix: Acosta's format variety, weighted toward Jensen's platform-preferred formats (video priority)
- Voice: Acosta's engagement optimization + Jensen's AI authenticity gate
- Cadence: Sustainable posting rhythm (Acosta's consistency) without AI-content flooding

**Engagement Engine** (Comment Jedi + Acosta's engagement tactics)
- 50%+ time on commenting (Jensen's Comment Jedi Principle)
- Strategic comment targets per Acosta's authority-building methodology
- Response velocity optimization (first 2 hours critical)

**Distribution Engine** (Revenue Bridge + Dual Distribution)
- Acosta's Revenue Bridge (Content → Profile → Email → Sales) 
- Jensen's dual distribution layer (feed engagement + LLM citation)
- Content designed for both immediate and persistent discovery

### Step 4 — Authenticity-Growth Balance
Apply the critical tension management:
- Acosta optimizes for GROWTH metrics → Jensen ensures growth doesn't sacrifice authenticity
- Jensen prioritizes PLATFORM alignment → Acosta ensures alignment doesn't sacrifice momentum
- The balance point: "Platform-aligned growth with human voice intact"
