# /jensen-platform-intel — Platform Intelligence Grounding

## Purpose
Ground any LinkedIn strategy in insider understanding of algorithm philosophy, feed dynamics, and platform direction. The "why behind the why" for every tactical decision.

## When to Use
- Before deploying ANY LinkedIn-focused workflow from another expert (Acosta, Escobar, Clark, Alic, Sanders)
- When a client asks "why isn't my LinkedIn working?"
- When evaluating conflicting LinkedIn advice from practitioners
- When planning quarterly LinkedIn strategy

## Inputs
- `[STRATEGY]` — The LinkedIn strategy, tactic, or advice being evaluated (optional — if blank, generate a full platform intelligence briefing)
- `[CONTEXT]` — Business context: B2B/B2C, industry, audience size, current performance

## Steps

### Step 1 — Load Platform Intelligence
Load `skills/jessica-jensen-platform-intelligence/genius.md` (Genius Patterns 1, 7, 9) and `references/platform-data.md`.

### Step 2 — Algorithm Reality Check
Map the strategy against Jensen's Supply-Demand Feed Model:
1. **Content supply dynamics**: 41% content growth in 3 years → organic reach compression is mathematical, not punitive
2. **AI content detection**: Is the strategy AI-dependent? Flag authenticity risk (Genius Pattern 10)
3. **Format alignment**: Does it use platform-preferred formats? (short-form video, captions, visual hooks)
4. **Engagement economics**: Does it prioritize commenting (50%+ time) or just posting? (Comment Jedi Principle)

### Step 3 — Platform Direction Alignment
Score the strategy against where the platform is HEADING, not just where it IS:
- **LLM citation layer**: Is content structured for dual distribution (feed + LLM retrieval)?
- **Video investment**: Is there a short-form video component?
- **Events integration**: Are LinkedIn Events being used? (20K/week, massively underexploited)
- **AI authenticity gradient**: Will this content survive increasing AI detection?
- **Portfolio career signals**: Does it accommodate multi-identity profiles?

### Step 4 — Institutional Intelligence Briefing
Produce a grounding report:

```
## Platform Intelligence Briefing

### Algorithm Reality
[Supply-demand dynamics for this specific niche/audience]

### What the Platform Rewards (and Why)
[Behavior-level explanations, not "tips"]

### What the Platform Suppresses (and Why)
[Suppression signals with institutional reasoning]

### Direction Vectors (Next 6-12 Months)
[Where the platform is heading — design for THIS, not yesterday]

### Grounded Recommendations
[Strategy adjustments based on insider understanding]

### Risk Flags
[Where the strategy fights the platform instead of riding it]
```

## Output Format
Institutional intelligence briefing (not tips list). Every recommendation grounded in "here's why the platform works this way" rather than "here's what to do."
