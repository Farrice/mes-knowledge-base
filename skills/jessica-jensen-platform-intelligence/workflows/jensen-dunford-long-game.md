# /jensen-dunford-long-game — B2B Long Game

## Purpose
7-month buying cycle strategy combining Jensen's platform intelligence with Dunford's positioning for B2B trust engineering on LinkedIn.

## Stacking Partners
- **Jessica Jensen** — 95/5 Patience Principle, Song Sheet Architecture, B2P Reframe
- **April Dunford** — Positioning, Competitive Alternatives, Category Design

## When to Use
- When a B2B company needs LinkedIn strategy designed for long buying cycles
- When "product-push" LinkedIn content isn't converting
- When positioning and platform strategy are misaligned
- When designing B2B thought leadership that compounds over 6-12 months

## Inputs
- `[COMPANY]` — B2B company/product
- `[POSITIONING]` — Current positioning (if Dunford positioning has been done) or market context
- `[BUYING_CYCLE]` — Average sales cycle length
- `[LINKEDIN_PRESENCE]` — Current LinkedIn strategy and performance

## Steps

### Step 1 — The 95/5 Reality Map
Apply Jensen's 95/5 Patience Principle (Genius Pattern 7):
- 95% of the target market is NOT buying today
- Average B2B buying cycle: 7 months (Jensen's data)
- "That product message you're ramming down people's throats is wasted on 19 out of 20 people"

Map the company's current content: What percentage speaks to the 95% vs. the 5%?

### Step 2 — Dunford Positioning → LinkedIn Song Sheet
Translate Dunford's positioning into Jensen's song sheet format:

| Dunford Element | Song Sheet Translation |
|----------------|----------------------|
| Competitive alternatives | "Unlike [alternatives]..." → contrast message |
| Unique attributes | Core value props per segment |
| Value themes | Emotional resonance anchors (B2P) |
| Best-fit customers | Song sheet audience segments |
| Market category | Content authority territory |

Produce ONE song sheet line per audience segment that embeds Dunford's positioning.

### Step 3 — Long-Cycle Content Architecture
Design the 7-month content engine:

**Month 1-2: Category Education** (95% audience)
- Educate the market on the PROBLEM (not your solution)
- Dunford's "set the context" approach via LinkedIn thought leadership
- Jensen's B2P reframe: make the problem emotionally resonant

**Month 3-4: Authority Building** (95% audience)
- Establish expertise through frameworks, insights, data
- Jensen's LLM citation optimization for persistent discovery
- Comment Jedi engagement in industry conversations

**Month 5-6: Differentiation** (shifting 95→5)
- Begin surfacing unique perspective (Dunford's competitive alternatives)
- Case studies and proof points (for those entering buying mode)
- Song sheet messaging becomes more specific

**Month 7+: Conversion** (5% audience)
- Direct value propositions for active buyers
- Retargeting content for LinkedIn audience
- Sales enablement content for direct outreach

### Step 4 — B2B Trust Engineering
Design trust-building mechanisms for long cycles:
- **Consistency signal**: Regular posting cadence (trust accrues over time)
- **Vulnerability signal**: Behind-the-curtain content (Jensen) showing real process
- **Authority signal**: Deep expertise content with Dunford's positioning embedded
- **Social proof signal**: Customer stories, data, third-party validation
