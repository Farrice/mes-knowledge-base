# /jensen-b2b-creative — B2B Creative Distinctiveness Audit

## Purpose
Audit any B2B creative against Jensen's "forklift in the meeting room" / "office vampire" standard. Break through B2B boredom using the B2P emotional resonance test.

## When to Use
- When B2B content or advertising feels generic, boring, or interchangeable
- When reviewing ad creative, brand campaigns, or content series
- When a B2B company says "our content isn't working" but the problem is creative, not targeting
- Before launching any B2B campaign on LinkedIn

## Inputs
- `[CREATIVE]` — The B2B creative to audit (ads, posts, campaigns, landing pages)
- `[BRAND]` — Company/product being promoted
- `[AUDIENCE]` — Target B2B buyer personas
- `[OBJECTIVE]` — Campaign goal (awareness, lead gen, conversion, recruitment)

## Steps

### Step 1 — The B2B Boredom Diagnostic
Score the creative against Jensen's hierarchy of B2B creative quality:

| Level | Description | Example | Score |
|-------|-------------|---------|-------|
| **1 — Invisible** | Blends with every other B2B ad in feed | Stock photo + "Transform your business" | 1-2 |
| **2 — Competent** | Communicates value clearly but forgettably | Product screenshot + feature list | 3-4 |
| **3 — Engaging** | Stops the scroll, earns attention | Unexpected visual + clear message | 5-6 |
| **4 — Memorable** | Lives in memory after seeing | AI billboard on 101 freeway mockery | 7-8 |
| **5 — Remarkable** | People share, screenshot, talk about | Jensen in flamingo sunglasses in a ball pit | 9-10 |

**Score the current creative. Anything below 5 requires intervention.**

### Step 2 — The B2P Emotional Resonance Test
Apply Jensen's B2P Reframe (Genius Pattern 2):

**Rational Layer Check**
- [ ] Value proposition is clear
- [ ] Differentiation from competitors is stated or implied
- [ ] Proof points are present (data, testimonials, case studies)

**Emotional Layer Check** (most B2B creative fails here)
- [ ] Appeals to a HUMAN emotion (ambition, fear, curiosity, humor, pride)
- [ ] Would work as a consumer ad with product swap? ("Doritos or SaaS" test)
- [ ] Creates a feeling, not just communicating information
- [ ] The person seeing this would FEEL something before they THINK something

**B2P Verdict**: Is this speaking to a job title or to a person?

### Step 3 — The Forklift Standard
Apply Jensen's "forklift in the meeting room" creative benchmark:

Jensen's exemplars of B2B creative that works:
- **AI Billboard Mockery**: LinkedIn billboards mocking incomprehensible AI ads on the 101 → Humor + relevance + self-awareness
- **Office Vampire**: B2B ad featuring a vampire in an office setting → Unexpected character in expected setting
- **Behind-the-Curtain BTS**: Casual, unpolished photos of real work → Authenticity > production value

**The Forklift Test**: Would someone screenshot this and send it to a colleague? Would they remember it tomorrow? Would they describe it at dinner?

### Step 4 — Creative Transformation Prescription
For creative scoring below 5, apply Jensen's transformation framework:

**Intervention 1: Inject the Unexpected**
- What visual, character, or scenario would be SURPRISING in this business context?
- The "flamingo in the ball pit" principle: Combine absurdity with legitimate expertise

**Intervention 2: Lead with Emotion**
- Rewrite the headline to trigger a feeling before communicating a benefit
- "Instead of 'Improve pipeline efficiency by 40%' → 'Your sales team is drowning. Here's the life raft.'"

**Intervention 3: Apply Behind-the-Curtain**
- Replace stock imagery with real, unpolished visuals
- Replace corporate copy with human language
- Show the making-of, not just the finished product

**Intervention 4: The Song Sheet Anchor**
- Does the creative connect to ONE crisp message? (Genius Pattern 6)
- If the message isn't clear enough to repeat in an elevator, fix the message before fixing the creative

## Output Format
Scored audit with specific transformation prescriptions and revised creative direction.

## Quality Gate
- [ ] Boredom score applied honestly (most B2B creative scores 2-4)
- [ ] B2P emotional layer tested (not just rational/functional)
- [ ] Forklift standard applied (screenshot-worthy?)
- [ ] Transformation includes specific examples, not just principles
- [ ] Song sheet alignment checked (clear message before creative execution)
