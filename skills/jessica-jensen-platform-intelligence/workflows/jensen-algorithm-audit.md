# /jensen-algorithm-audit — Algorithm Psychology Audit

## Purpose
Diagnose content underperformance through platform dynamics (supply/demand, content volume growth, AI detection, timing variance) rather than surface-level metric chasing.

## When to Use
- When LinkedIn content performance has declined and the reason isn't obvious
- When a creator says "the algorithm changed" or "reach is dying"
- When conflicting advice from practitioners creates confusion
- Before making drastic strategy changes based on short-term metric dips

## Inputs
- `[CONTENT_SAMPLES]` — 5-10 recent posts (or descriptions of content approach)
- `[METRICS]` — Available performance data: impressions, engagement rate, follower growth, profile views
- `[TIMEFRAME]` — When performance changed or concern began
- `[NICHE]` — Industry/topic vertical

## Steps

### Step 1 — Supply-Demand Diagnosis
Apply Jensen's Supply-Demand Feed Model (Genius Pattern 1) to the specific niche:

**Supply Side Analysis**
- Niche content volume: Has competition increased in this topic space?
- AI content flooding: Are competitors publishing AI-generated content at volume?
- Format saturation: Has the dominant format (text posts, carousels) become oversaturated?

**Demand Side Analysis**
- Audience growth rate: Is the addressable audience expanding or stagnant?
- Attention competition: Are new formats (video, newsletters) splitting attention?
- Seasonal patterns: Are there cyclical dips in this industry?

**Diagnosis**: Is decline from supply compression, demand shifts, or content quality?

### Step 2 — AI Content Detection Assessment
Audit content against Jensen's AI Authenticity Gradient (Genius Pattern 10):

| Signal | Risk Level | Indicator |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Generic openings ("In today's fast-paced world") | HIGH | AI template language |
| Perfect grammar, zero personality | MEDIUM | Lacks human fingerprint |
| Interchangeable perspective | HIGH | "Could 50 other people's AI have written this?" |
| No specific anecdotes or experiences | MEDIUM | Missing lived-experience markers |
| Consistent posting cadence with uniform quality | LOW-MEDIUM | Algorithmic suspicion trigger |

Score: How many posts would survive LinkedIn's growing AI detection? Flag the percentage at risk.

### Step 3 — Engagement Pattern Analysis
Apply the Comment Jedi Principle (Genius Pattern 4) diagnostic:
- **Post-to-comment ratio**: What percentage of LinkedIn time goes to commenting vs. posting?
- **Comment quality**: Substantive value-add vs. "Great post!"
- **Comment targeting**: Engaging with peers in niche vs. random engagement?
- **Response velocity**: Replying to comments on own posts within first 2 hours?

### Step 4 — Format-Platform Alignment Check
Audit format choices against Jensen's insider signals:
- Short-form video (with captions + visual hooks): Platform-preferred
- Text posts: Highest volume, most competition
- Carousels: Strong for saves, but format may be saturating
- Newsletters: Growing distribution, underexploited
- Events: 20K/week, massive untapped opportunity
- Live audio/video: Platform investment signal

### Step 5 — Algorithm Psychology Report
Produce the diagnostic:

```
## Algorithm Psychology Audit

### Root Cause Diagnosis
[Supply compression / AI detection / engagement pattern / format misalignment / content quality]

### Platform Dynamics Affecting This Niche
[Specific supply-demand dynamics]

### AI Authenticity Risk Score
[X/10 posts at risk of AI detection suppression]

### Engagement Architecture Grade
[Comment Jedi compliance: A/B/C/D/F]

### Format Alignment Score
[Current mix vs. platform-preferred formats]

### Prescription
[Prioritized list of 3-5 changes, grounded in platform dynamics]

### What NOT to Do
[Common practitioner advice that would FIGHT the platform here]
```

## Output Format
Diagnostic report with root cause, scoring, and platform-grounded prescription.

## Quality Gate
- [ ] Diagnosis grounded in supply-demand dynamics, not "algorithm punishing you"
- [ ] AI authenticity gradient applied to actual content
- [ ] Comment Jedi compliance measured
- [ ] Format alignment checked against platform-preferred signals
- [ ] Prescription explains WHY (platform dynamics), not just WHAT (tactics)
