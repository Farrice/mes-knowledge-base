# /jensen-brand-physics — Personal Brand Physics

## Purpose
The anxiety-dissolution → authentic expression pipeline. Deploy Jensen's anti-anxiety framework + Comment Jedi principle + behind-the-curtain test for anyone building a LinkedIn presence.

## When to Use
- When someone says "I know I should be on LinkedIn but..."
- When a creator's content feels forced, corporate, or performative
- When someone is stuck between wanting to post and fear of judgment
- When onboarding any client to LinkedIn content strategy
- Before tactical workflows (Acosta, Escobar) when the barrier is emotional, not tactical

## Inputs
- `[PERSON]` — Who is building (or rebuilding) their LinkedIn presence
- `[CURRENT_STATE]` — Where they are now: lurker / sporadic poster / active but stale / executive afraid to start
- `[ANXIETY_SIGNALS]` — What they've said about their hesitation (capture exact language)
- `[PROFESSIONAL_IDENTITY]` — Their role, expertise, and what they'd LIKE to be known for

## Steps

### Step 1 — Anxiety Diagnosis
Map the person's specific anxiety against Jensen's taxonomy:

| Anxiety Type | Signal Phrases | Jensen Antidote |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Judgment Fear** | "What will people think?" | "People are not judging you as much as you think. I promise." |
| **Perfectionism** | "My content isn't good enough" | Behind-the-curtain test — unpolished BTS outperforms polished |
| **Imposter Syndrome** | "Who am I to post about this?" | Network-first discovery — you don't need authority to engage |
| **Time Scarcity** | "I don't have time" | Three-Minute Video Challenge — start with phone + 3 minutes |
| **Corporate Risk** | "What if my employer sees?" | Creator-company interplay — your brand helps their brand |
| **Content Paralysis** | "I don't know what to post" | Comment Jedi first — build confidence through engagement before creation |

Identify the PRIMARY and SECONDARY anxiety. Address PRIMARY first.

### Step 2 — Anxiety Dissolution Sequence
Deploy Jensen's anxiety-first methodology (Genius Pattern 3):

**Phase 1: Normalize**
- Share Jensen's own journey: CMO of LinkedIn was nervous about posting on LinkedIn
- "Even I get nervous" — the platform owner's vulnerability
- Reframe: LinkedIn is a professional community, not a performance stage

**Phase 2: Lower the Bar**
- First 30 days: No posting required. Network-first (Genius Pattern 11)
- Follow 20 experts in your field. Connect with 10 peers. Comment on 5 posts/day.
- The Comment Jedi path: build presence through engagement, not creation

**Phase 3: Behind-the-Curtain Entry**
- First content = BTS, not thought leadership
- "Here's what I'm working on" / "Here's what I learned today" / "Here's my desk"
- Apply the Producer James exemplar: casual > polished

### Step 3 — Authentic Expression Architecture
Once anxiety is dissolved, build the expression system:

**Voice Discovery**
- Apply Jensen's Improv Comedy Filter (Genius Pattern 5): What's the intersection of your expertise AND your personality?
- "Just because LinkedIn is professional doesn't mean you can't be fun"
- Find YOUR flamingo sunglasses equivalent — the unexpected personal detail

**Content Physics**
- **Gravity content** (pulls people in): Stories, vulnerability, behind-the-scenes
- **Authority content** (earns respect): Expertise, frameworks, insights
- **Engagement content** (builds community): Questions, debates, celebrations
- Ratio: 40% gravity / 40% authority / 20% engagement

**The Comment Jedi System**
- Allocate 50%+ of LinkedIn time to commenting
- Comment strategy: Add value, share a related experience, ask a genuine question
- Target: Comments on posts in your niche from people 1-2 levels ahead
- "Most people post and sit there and watch. The true Jedi engage."

### Step 4 — Progressive Confidence Ladder
Design a 90-day progressive confidence plan:

| Week | Action | Confidence Builder |
|------|--------|--------------------|
| 1-2 | Follow, observe, optimize profile | "I belong here" |
| 3-4 | Comment substantively 5x/day | "People value my input" |
| 5-6 | Share a curated article with 2-sentence take | "I have a perspective worth sharing" |
| 7-8 | Post first original content (BTS or lesson learned) | "I can create" |
| 9-10 | Post 2x/week with growing personality | "This is my space" |
| 11-12 | Experiment with video or carousel | "I'm getting good at this" |

### Step 5 — Authenticity Maintenance System
Design guardrails to prevent drift back into corporate-speak or anxiety:

- **Weekly behind-the-curtain check**: Is my content getting too polished?
- **AI authenticity gradient**: Could AI have written this for 50 other people? If yes, rewrite.
- **Flamingo test**: When was the last time I showed personality, not just expertise?
- **Comment ratio audit**: Am I still spending 50%+ time on engagement?

## Output Format
Personalized anxiety-dissolution plan with progressive confidence ladder and authenticity maintenance system.
