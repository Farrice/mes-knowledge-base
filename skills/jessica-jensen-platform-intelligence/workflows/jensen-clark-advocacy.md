# /jensen-clark-advocacy — Founder Advocacy

## Purpose
Founder-led content leveraging Jensen's creator-company interplay insights with Clark's stealth hook engineering for maximum founder authority on LinkedIn.

## Stacking Partners
- **Jessica Jensen** — Creator-company interplay, anxiety dissolution, platform intelligence
- **Tommy Clark** — Stealth hooks, founder-led content, social media engineering

## When to Use
- When a founder or CEO needs to become the face of company content on LinkedIn
- When company page content underperforms and personal presence is the growth lever
- When founding team members resist posting ("I'm a builder, not a content creator")
- When designing a founder content program that scales beyond the founder

## Inputs
- `[FOUNDER]` — Founder name, company, and role
- `[COMPANY_STAGE]` — Pre-revenue / growth / scaled
- `[FOUNDER_COMFORT]` — Content comfort level: terrified / hesitant / willing / active
- `[COMPANY_GOALS]` — What the company needs from founder presence: hiring, fundraising, sales, brand

## Steps

### Step 1 — Founder Anxiety Dissolution
Apply Jensen's Personal Brand Physics (Genius Pattern 3) specifically for founders:

| Founder Objection | Jensen + Clark Response |
|-------------------|----------------------|
| "I should be building product, not posting" | Your content IS product marketing — personal reach 5-10x company page |
| "I'm not charismatic enough" | Behind-the-curtain authenticity > charisma (Jensen). Stealth hooks don't require charm (Clark). |
| "My board/investors won't like it" | Investor-facing content is a separate lane — and most investors actively want founder visibility |
| "I don't know what to say" | You know more about your problem space than 99.9% of LinkedIn. That's the content. |
| "I'll say the wrong thing" | Guardrails + topic lanes > silence. Jensen: "Guidelines + encouragement > prohibition" |

### Step 2 — Creator-Company Interplay Architecture
Design the founder-company content relationship using Jensen's model:

**Founder Personal Brand → Company Brand**
- Founder posts about industry → establishes thought leadership → company benefits
- Founder posts about building → humanizes company → talent attraction
- Founder posts about customer problems → demand generation → pipeline

**Company Brand → Founder Personal Brand**
- Company milestones → founder narrative content
- Customer wins → founder credibility content
- Team achievements → founder leadership content

**The Flywheel**: Founder visibility drives company → company growth fuels founder content → repeat

### Step 3 — Clark Stealth Hook Integration
Apply Tommy Clark's hook engineering to Jensen's authenticity framework:

**Hook Types for Founders** (Clark's methodology)
- **Contrarian hook**: Challenge industry orthodoxy (what you see from the inside)
- **Behind-the-scenes hook**: Real building stories (Jensen's behind-the-curtain)
- **Data hook**: Proprietary data or insights from your business
- **Vulnerability hook**: Honest failure or struggle stories (Jensen's anxiety dissolution makes this safe)

**Authenticity Filter** (Jensen)
- Every hook must pass the AI Authenticity Gradient
- Founder voice = ultimate competitive moat (no AI can replicate YOUR building experience)
- The flamingo test: unexpected personality moments build the strongest founder brands

### Step 4 — Content System Design
Build the founder content operating system:

**Weekly Rhythm**
- Monday: Industry perspective post (authority)
- Wednesday: Building/BTS post (authenticity)
- Friday: Team/customer celebration or engagement post (community)
- Daily: 15 minutes Comment Jedi engagement (growth)

**Content Production**
- Capture: Voice memos during the day → transcribe → edit
- Three-Minute Video Challenge for video content
- Monthly strategy session for topic planning
- Ghostwriting support for polish (maintaining founder voice)

**Scaling Beyond the Founder**
- Phase 1: Founder posts alone (months 1-3)
- Phase 2: Founder + 2-3 leadership team members (months 4-6)
- Phase 3: Full advocacy program (Jensen's /jensen-advocacy workflow)
