# /jensen-advocacy — Creator-Company Advocacy Engine

## Purpose
Design employee/leader advocacy programs that benefit both individual careers and company brands. The "you have 3,000 other people" playbook.

## When to Use
- When a company wants to scale LinkedIn presence beyond the brand page
- When executives resist personal branding ("that's for influencers")
- When employee advocacy programs exist but feel forced or underperforming
- When designing founder-led content programs for startups

## Inputs
- `[COMPANY]` — Company name, size, and industry
- `[ADVOCATES]` — Target advocates: executives, SMEs, sales team, all employees
- `[COMPANY_GOALS]` — What the company needs from advocacy: hiring, brand awareness, lead gen, thought leadership
- `[ADVOCATE_GOALS]` — What individuals gain: career growth, visibility, network expansion

## Steps

### Step 1 — The Creator-Company Interplay Map
Apply Jensen's creator-company advocacy model:

**Company Benefits**
- Amplified reach: 1 company page vs. N personal profiles (personal profiles get 3-5x more engagement)
- Authentic voice: Employee perspective > corporate messaging
- Talent magnet: Visible, posting employees signal great culture
- Sales enablement: Trusted advisors, not cold outreach

**Individual Benefits**
- Career insurance: Personal brand survives job changes
- Network value: Professional connections compound over career
- Skill development: Content creation as leadership skill
- Visibility: "People inside your company see what you're doing"

Map the specific interplay for THIS company's situation.

### Step 2 — Anxiety Dissolution for Executives
Apply Jensen's Personal Brand Physics (Genius Pattern 3) specifically for corporate advocates:

| Executive Objection | Jensen Response | Action |
|--------------------|-----------------|--------|
| "I'm not an influencer" | "You're not performing. You're sharing expertise." | Frame as knowledge sharing, not self-promotion |
| "What if I say the wrong thing?" | Guidelines + encouragement > prohibition | Provide clear lanes, not restrictions |
| "I don't have time" | Three-Minute Video Challenge | Start with 3-min phone video weekly |
| "Our legal/comms team won't allow it" | "Companies that restrict lose. Companies that enable win." | Pre-approved topic lanes, not post approval |
| "It won't impact business" | Employee posts drive 8x engagement vs. brand posts | Share the data |

### Step 3 — Advocacy Program Architecture
Design the tiered program:

**Tier 1: Leadership Circle (C-suite + VPs)**
- Commitment: 2 posts/week + 5 strategic comments/day
- Content: Vision, industry perspective, culture moments
- Support: Ghostwriting assistance, content calendar, quarterly strategy sessions
- Measurement: Impression share, inbound leads, talent pipeline

**Tier 2: Subject Matter Experts (Directors + Specialists)**
- Commitment: 1 post/week + daily commenting
- Content: Deep expertise, how-we-built-it, industry analysis
- Support: Topic suggestions, posting templates, monthly coaching
- Measurement: Engagement rate, profile views, speaking invitations

**Tier 3: Amplification Network (All Employees)**
- Commitment: Engage with company + leadership content weekly
- Content: Reshares with personal commentary, milestone celebrations
- Support: Easy-share content library, recognition program
- Measurement: Participation rate, amplification reach

### Step 4 — Song Sheet Alignment
Ensure all advocacy content maps to the company's song sheet (Genius Pattern 6):
- Each advocate's content lane connects to one segment's core message
- Personal stories and perspectives are the VEHICLE for the company message
- The message is embedded, not stated — advocacy, not advertising
