## Algorithm Philosophy

- **Living organism**: Constantly adjusting based on trends, behavior, topics, connections
- **Supply/demand**: As content and users increase, organic reach compresses (standard social platform economics)
- **AI detection**: Actively identifying and likely suppressing AI-generated posts
- **Demographics**: Gender, race, and demographic factors are NOT factored into the algorithm
- **Timing variance**: Same post at different times can get radically different results due to real-time supply/demand dynamics
- **Content format preference**: Short-form video with captions and visual hooks (CMO-recommended = platform signal)

## Product Direction Signals

- **AI Job Search**: Natural language job matching ("I want to work in environment, have graphic design background, used to work in banking") — widens opportunity discovery
- **Brand Link**: Creator partnership advertising program — buy ads with leading creator voices
- **Thought Leadership Ads**: Promote individual expert content, not just company pages
- **Events Platform**: 20K events/week — audience building, lead gen, community
- **Learning Resources**: Copious AI skill training and professional development

## Labor Market Signals

- **AI creating more jobs than eating** (interest rates are the primary compression driver)
- **Entrepreneurship blossoming**: People "betting on themselves" in uncertain markets
- **Trades resurgence**: Younger workers choosing AI-proof careers (plumbing, electrical, healthcare)
- **Portfolio careers**: People doing 3-4 different jobs simultaneously
- **AI literacy**: Becoming table stakes across all professional fields
- **Human skills premium**: Creativity, judgment, strategic thinking, clear writing, collaboration — value increasing
