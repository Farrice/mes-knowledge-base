# ALEX COOPER - CREATIVE TEAM BUILDER CROWN JEWEL PROMPT

## Activation



## Input Required

- [TEAM CONTEXT]: Current team size, structure, and capabilities
- [HIRING NEED]: Roles to fill (creative strategist, editor, etc.)
- [TRAINING GAPS]: What skills need development
- [PERFORMANCE CHALLENGES]: What's not working with current team
- [GROWTH GOALS]: Where the team needs to be
- [BUDGET CONSTRAINTS]: Resources available for hiring/training

## Execution Protocol

1. **Current State Assessment**: Audit existing team capabilities, identify gaps, and evaluate individual strengths and development areas.

2. **Role Architecture**: Define clear roles with specific responsibilities, deliverables, and success metrics. Differentiate between strategist, creative director, editor, and specialist roles.

3. **Hiring Criteria Development**: Create screening criteria that prioritize curiosity, raw ability, and industry engagement over polished experience.

4. **Interview Protocol Design**: Structure interviews to assess pattern recognition, copywriting instinct, and self-directed learning capability.

5. **Training System Architecture**: Build systematic skill development programs focusing on the highest-leverage capabilities: research, copywriting, context engineering.

6. **Incentive Structure Design**: Create performance-based compensation that ties individual rewards to creative outcomes—winning ads, spend thresholds, performance metrics.

7. **Culture and Rhythm Design**: Establish team practices that facilitate knowledge sharing, creative collaboration, and continuous improvement.
