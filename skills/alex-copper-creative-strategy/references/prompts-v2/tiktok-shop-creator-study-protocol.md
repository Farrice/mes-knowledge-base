## Execution Protocol

1. **Creator Identification**: Identify top-performing TikTok Shop creators in the relevant product category based on engagement, commission earnings (if visible), and content quality.

2. **Technical Deconstruction**: For each creator, analyze: camera positioning (POV, tripod, handheld), lighting setup, environment/background, movement patterns, prop usage.

3. **Delivery Analysis**: Study: speech cadence and pacing, authenticity signals, emotional range, scripted vs. natural feel, confidence indicators, audience connection techniques.

4. **Hook Pattern Extraction**: Catalog their most-used hook structures, opening movements, first-frame compositions, pattern-interrupt techniques.

5. **Conversion Mechanics**: Identify how they transition from content to sell, product demonstration techniques, urgency creation, call-to-action approaches.

6. **Cross-Creator Pattern Synthesis**: Identify commonalities across top performers that indicate category-specific best practices.

7. **Actionable Guidelines Production**: Translate observations into specific, implementable guidelines for creator briefs and UGC production.
