# Justin Welsh — Solopreneur Operating System

Justin Welsh built $8M+ in cumulative revenue as a literal one-person business (90%+ profit margin, ~$600/month expenses, zero ads, zero employees) by teaching corporate professionals to identify, develop, and monetize skills they already have. His system inverts the standard playbook: the niche emerges from audience dialogue rather than upfront selection, content multiplies from one idea into 10+ pieces rather than being invented daily, and the business is designed backward from the lifestyle ("build a lifestyle that generates income, not a business that generates money"). Every tactic rests on a practitioner-first ethic — do the thing, get the result, then teach it, because "announcing it doesn't make it true."

## Quick Reference

- **Think once, publish 10 times** — hub piece (newsletter/essay) → chop into 6-12 micro posts through format lenses (observation, story, contrarian take, future prediction, tactical how-to) → cross-post with platform-native context → the winning micro piece expands back into the next hub.
- **Trailer / Call-to-Conversation / Meat** — post anatomy in priority order: first 300 characters are a movie trailer, one specific ask, then deliver the meat so the hook isn't clickbait.
- **Profile-as-landing-page** — banner states the problem you solve, tagline says why to follow, featured section carries the conversion. Clear beats clever.
- **Data × passion intersection** — every 90 days: 5-7 topics the data says resonate × 5-7 topics you love talking about; write only at the crossings.
- **Unique knowledge through obsession** — get obsessed, run the experiment, name the framework (Creator Funnel, hub-and-spoke). Owned language is the moat.
- **Practitioner gate** — help someone make $100, then $1,000, then $100,000 before you claim the positioning.
- **Buffer rule** — 30 ideas before launching a newsletter; stay ~30 issues ahead so consistency never depends on inspiration.
- **Write like you talk** — read it aloud; if you trip, rewrite. Grammar serves feel ("I make the rules. This is my content.").
- **Medium-fit** — if you've hated writing after 10-20 honest tries, switch to the medium you enjoy; consistency follows enjoyment.
- **Meet readers where they are** — never gate "free" content behind an email barter; generosity and kindness are competitive advantages.
