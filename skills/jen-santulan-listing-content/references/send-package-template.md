## The rules under the skeleton

1. **One reel gets filmed.** 5 options, one marked top pick. Never present a per-buyer content calendar as the send.
2. **The HOOK carries Jen; the TOUR carries the facts.** Her audience is warm/parasocial — anticipation and rarity hooks ("i've been waiting to show you this one") need zero verification and sound like her. Fact-in-tension material mined by the hook engine goes into TOUR, cover text, and the caption's contrast line — where it's checkable.
3. **The CLOSE is a lead-capture unit.** Name + brokerage + direct CTA, every option, filmed as its own take.
4. **Cover text must be provable by its photo.** Two lines, each pair mapped → to a real shot.
5. **Fair housing is structural, not a footnote.** Never target the creative at families/kids; schools stay off camera; the don't-say set above ships in every package.
6. **Caption = walk-in story → ✔️ checklist → one contrast line → DM CTA → attribution + DRE.** Complete enough that no buyer needs to hunt; hashtags reach into the richer adjacent cities.
7. **Voice authority:** Farrice's lived sense of Jen's register outranks the scraped @_jiing profile where they conflict (emoji density, #realestatewithjing).
