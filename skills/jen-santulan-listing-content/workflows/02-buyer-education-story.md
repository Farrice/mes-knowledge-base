# Workflow 02 — Buyer Education Story Engine

> FTHB-focused educational Reels and Stories. The content that turns midnight-Zillow scrollers into Jen's DM list. NOT property tours — this is the trust layer that earns the right to show listings.

- Jen needs Story / Reel content that ISN'T tied to a specific listing
- Educational angle: explain a program, debunk a myth, teach the math

## The Educational Story Architecture

Education content has a different shape than listing hooks. Listings are pull (lifestyle dream). Education is push (you don't know what you don't know — let me show you).

### Universal Structure (3 acts, regardless of format)

**Act 1 — Pain Naming (10-15% of length)**
Name the EXACT midnight thought. The thing the buyer says to themselves on Zillow at 11pm. This is the same FTHB Permission move from genius.md Section 8 — but stretched into a longer educational frame.

**Act 2 — The Reframe + Education (70-80% of length)**
The substance. Show the math. Explain the program. Walk through the comparison. This is where Jen earns trust — by being the agent who actually TEACHES instead of upselling.

**Act 3 — Action with Permission (10-15% of length)**
NOT "DM me to qualify." Always: "Save this for later" or "Send this to your partner." The action is the relationship deposit, not the conversion.

---

## 5 Educational Content Angles (Rotate Across the Calendar)

Each angle has its own structure. Rotate to avoid topic fatigue.

### Angle 1: Program Explainer
**When**: New program announcement, Dream For All lottery dates, HOP funding refresh
**Hook formula**: "[Program name] just opened — and most people don't know they qualify."
**Example beats**:
- Open: "If you're a first-generation buyer in LA, this is your week."
- Educate: 3 slides — what it is, who qualifies, how much you can get
- Specific: One real number, one real address range, one real eligibility filter
- Close: "Save this. The lottery opens [date]."

### Angle 2: Rent-vs-Mortgage Math (FTHB Niche Killer)
**When**: Anytime — this is Jen's evergreen authority move
**Hook formula**: "Your rent is higher than this mortgage. Let me show you."
**Example beats**:
- Open: "$3,200/month rent in Sherman Oaks. You think buying is impossible."
- Educate: Show a real listing under $800K, calculate PITI with 5% down + DPA, compare net monthly
- Reveal: "The mortgage is $3,400 — and you OWN it. The difference is $200/mo."
- Close: "This is why I tell my clients: get the math BEFORE you give up."

### Angle 3: Myth Debunk
**When**: Recurring buyer myths surface in DMs or comments
**Hook formula**: "Three things you've been told about [topic] that are wrong."
**Common myths to debunk**:
- "I need 20% down" → FHA at 3.5%, DPA programs at 0% down available
- "First-time means never owned" → DEFINITION includes anyone who hasn't owned in 3 years
- "I make too much for first-time programs" → Income limits go up to 120-160% AMI in many cases
- "The Valley is just as expensive as Westside" → Reseda median is $750K vs Brentwood $2M+

### Angle 4: Neighborhood Comparison (FTHB Lens)
**When**: Buyer is stuck on one neighborhood that's outside their budget
**Hook formula**: "If you can't afford [aspirational neighborhood], here's where I'd look."
**Example beats**:
- Open: "Everyone wants Encino. Here's why my FTHB clients buy in Reseda first."
- Compare: Median price, commute to Encino amenities, school ratings, 5-year appreciation
- Reframe: "Buy here, build equity, move there in 5 years. That's the play."
- Close: "Send this to anyone who's frustrated by the price tag in their dream neighborhood."

### Angle 5: First-Year-as-Homeowner Reality
**When**: New listing closed, anniversary of a buyer's purchase, address common post-buy fears
**Hook formula**: "What no one told my clients before they closed."
**Example beats**:
- Open: "I had a client text me at month two: 'Why didn't anyone warn me about THIS?'"
- Educate: 3 honest realities — closing costs sting, first-month maintenance hits, property tax is real
- Reframe: "But here's why they'd still do it: month 12 they had $40K in equity vs $0 from rent."
- Close: "Save this for when you're ready. I'd rather you go in eyes open."

## Format-Specific Adaptations

### Reels (60-90 seconds spoken)
- Open with Act 1 pain naming in first 5-8 words (scrollstop)
- Compress Act 2 education into 2-3 numbered points (not 5+)
- Use on-screen text for numbers — listener can re-watch to verify
- Close with Act 3 in last 5-10 seconds with clear save trigger

### Story Sequences (3-7 slides)
- **Slide 1**: Pain naming (text-heavy, single statement)
- **Slides 2-5**: Educational content, one idea per slide, clear visual hierarchy
- **Slide 6**: Specific proof (screenshot of program page, real comp, MLS detail)
- **Slide 7**: Close with "Save this Story" or DM trigger

### Captions (paired with Reel or photo post)
- Caption is the LONG-FORM version of what the Reel shows in 60s
- 100-200 words; carries depth the Reel can't fit
- Always end with #realestatewithjing + 1 topic hashtag

## Voice Quality Gate

Before publishing, run against genius.md Section 10 quality bar.

**Education-specific MUST PASS:**
- ☐ Pain named in Act 1 — not jumped over
- ☐ At least one specific number, address, or program detail in Act 2 (not generic)
- ☐ Math shown, not implied
- ☐ Action in Act 3 is "save / send / take" — NOT "DM" or "click"
- ☐ Banned phrases absent (no "stunning," "don't sleep," "you got this!")
- ☐ FTHB program named correctly if mentioned (Dream For All, HOP80/120, LIPA, Greenline, MyHome)

**Anti-pattern check (auto-reject if present):**
- Generic motivation language ("manifest your dream home," "you got this," "trust the process")
- Selling the agent rather than the information
- "Click the link in bio" closer
- Made-up urgency ("only this week!" without real program deadline)

**What makes this excellent**:
- **Pain naming is exact**: "Got the email. Not selected. Again." — that's the actual text in the user's head
- **No motivation slop**: doesn't say "don't give up" — says "you weren't unqualified, the lottery is just the lottery"
- **Educational surplus**: explains 4 specific programs the buyer probably didn't know existed
- **Math transparency**: real example with real numbers ($145K HHI → $100K assistance → $720K Reseda → $3,400/mo)
- **Action is relational**: "Save this Story" — not "DM me to qualify"
- **SFV-specific**: Reseda named, Burbank named — geographic specificity = trust
- **Voice match**: contractions throughout, exclamations earned, single emoji at emotional beat
