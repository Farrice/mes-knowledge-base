# Fraser Cottrell — Paid Ads Creative Strategy

Fraser Cottrell is co-founder of Fraggle, a performance ad creative studio producing 8,000+ creatives per month for seven-, eight-, and nine-figure DTC brands, tied to roughly $300-450M in Meta ad spend across 15 years in performance marketing. His core thesis: media buying and optimization are fully automated now, so creative strategy is the only lever left — and a creative strategist is a director, not an ideas person, owning research through production through the data readout. This skill packages his three engines: the research-first strategy process, the 5-part villain-vs-hero video script formula, and the 6-format static ad library that actually holds spend.

## Quick Reference

- **The last lever**: media buying and optimization are automated — the thought that goes into the ad is the only remaining differentiator. Creative strategy decides whether an ad makes $10 or $100,000.
- **Research > writing**: spend more time in the customer's shoes than in the doc. Never blindly trust the brand's own read of who buys — research surfaces segments the brand never saw (the one-handed bra demo came from discovering a disability audience).
- **Strategist = director**: understand editing, lighting, design, and camera movement well enough to know what's possible and to review drafts against the vision — without becoming the editor or UGC creator.
- **Script skeleton (5 parts)**: Hook (visual + verbal, curiosity-driven) → Villain build (twist the knife) → Hero introduction (your product saves the day) → Demo/proof (never claim without showing) → CTA (tell them exactly what to do). Inject a benefit block that answers the top comment-section objection.
- **Hook relevance check**: a great hook rate with a terrible hold rate means the hook was irrelevant bait — people came for the hook, not the product.
- **Static formats that held spend**: color-block SKU callout, AI headline-image/mascot, social-proof comment frame, headliner, collage/grid, Instagram organic disguise. Color stops the scroll before copy is ever read.
- **Demographic mirror**: the hands/person in the image must look like the target customer's own. A woman's hand holding the product tells women "this is for me."
- **Test like a scientist**: one variable at a time (same message, different production vehicle isolated a 47.5% vs 34% hook-rate gap). Metric-to-fix: weak hook rate → new hook; weak CTR → better CTA; high production flop + UGC win → production style was the problem, not the message.
- **Storytelling now wins**: AI slop flooded the feed; a real founder story is the separation a strategist should deploy.
