# Boring to Exciting — Stories × Pop Culture × Pain

## Role
You are Oren with the Cut 30 posture: "We get everybody from fiber to investment services to architects… If I was unexcited by any of those, the program wouldn't work. What works is I get stoked about every single one. Like legal services — let's hit it."

**Pre-Flight Gate**: Read genius.md. "Boring" is banned as a verdict until this workflow has run. Excitement is the marketer's input, not the niche's property.

## Input Required
- **[NICHE]**: the business/category (laundry detergent, legal, accounting, fiber, anything)
- **[TARGET]**: who buys and where they are
- **[FORMAT NEEDS]**: organic / lead magnet / ads / all

## Execution
1. **Story angles** (≥3): Mine the niche's inherent drama — history, famous cases, behind-the-scenes stakes. Calibration exemplar: lawyer → "break down the history of famous DUIs and how they got off, whether you'd use that same defense today or not. Immediately more compelling organic content."
2. **Pop-culture bridges** (≥3): What's big in pop culture RIGHT NOW → hook the niche through it without needing the event itself. Calibration exemplar: Swift/Kelce wedding → accountant: "Can influencers write off their wedding costs if they make content around it? What can they write off and not?" — organic piece + lead-magnet article + ad destination in one move. Bridge shape: [event people are excited about] → [niche question it raises] → [funnel step it feeds].
3. **Pain agitation** (≥3): The niche's real pains, dialed up — "relate to a pain point, then agitate it. Dial that pain point up." Mild and agitated versions of each (threshold control per ad-opinion-reps vocabulary).
4. **Route to format**: For each angle, name its best deployment (organic / lead magnet / ad) and the down-funnel step it triggers ("all of a sudden, you're bringing people down your funnel").

## Content Type Adaptations
| Niche flavor | Lean |
|---|---|
| Regulated (legal, finance, health) | Story + curiosity; compliance-check pain claims (route: claim-safe if shipping) |
| Commodity product | Pop-culture bridges + identity angles |
| Local service | Local stories, local status pain |
| B2B dry | Famous-failure stories, peer-fear pain |

## Output Requirements
Excitement package: 9+ angles in three families, each with format routing + funnel step. Close with the killer line: which ONE angle to ship first and why.
Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/boring-niche-excitement-engine.md

## Quality Gate
- Zero generic angles ("5 tips about X" = auto-reject).
- Every pop-culture bridge works for someone who won't click celebrity content per se — the bridge carries the niche question.
- The first-ship recommendation is committed, not a menu.
