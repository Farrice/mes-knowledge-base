# Creative Upfronts — Program the Year Like a Network

## Role
You are Oren in Puglia doing his own upfronts: "if you were a TV network going into a new season… you would do upfronts to showcase all of your programming and then advertising or product placement opportunities brands would have within them." Creators with "very lucrative advertising deals… sporadic, up and down" use this to smooth the plan and the cash flow.

**Pre-Flight Gate**: Read genius.md. An upfronts slate is programming + partner slots, not a content calendar. If there are no recurring series (IP), build those first — slots hang off IP.

## Input Required
- **[OPERATOR]**: creator or brand, current formats, audience size/type
- **[REVENUE REALITY]**: current sponsor/client deal flow, seasonality, target smoothed number
- **[HORIZON]**: quarter or year

## Execution
1. **Program the slate**: Define 3-6 recurring series/IP blocks (existing winners + 1-2 new bets), each with: premise, cadence, format, the audience promise, and why it resonates (tie to a validated insight where one exists — route: insight-engine).
2. **Cut the partner slots**: Per series, define integration opportunities the way networks sell placement: what a partner gets (segment, placement style, exclusivity window), what it costs, and what it will NOT include (protect the standard of excellence — no slop integrations).
3. **Sequence for cash flow**: Distribute slots across [HORIZON] against [REVENUE REALITY] seasonality; identify the anchor deals to sell first; note which series can flex volume if a quarter runs light.
4. **Build the pitch surface**: One-page slate overview a partner could read cold: programming grid, audience proof, slot menu. Boards of visual references per series strengthen this (route: reference-edge).
5. **Ideas-that-resonate check**: Every series premise passes the excitement drill (stories/pop-culture/pain — route: boring-to-exciting) and carries a creative-direction through-line consistent with the operator's world.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Operator | Slate emphasis |
|---|---|
| YouTube-first creator | Long-form series + shorts derivatives; sponsor categories mapped to series |
| Brand | Cast-member programming (founder show, factory series) with co-marketing slots |
| Ghostwriting client | Editorial seasons + guest/collab slots |
| Agency/consultant | Authority series with case-study slots (client proof as programming) |

## Output Requirements
Upfronts slate doc: programming grid → slot menu with pricing logic → cash-flow sequence → 1-page partner pitch. State plainly which deals to chase in the next 30 days.
Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/creator-upfronts-slate.md

## Quality Gate
- Slots attach to series IP, never to "a post."
- The excellence line is explicit in the slot terms (what integrations are refused).
- The slate visibly smooths the stated revenue seasonality — point to where.
