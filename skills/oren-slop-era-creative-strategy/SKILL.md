# Oren — Slop-Era Creative Strategy

**Invocation**: `/deploy-skill oren-slop-era-creative-strategy`
**Domain**: Era Diagnosis • Premium Brand Building • Consumer Insight • Reference Curation • Marketer Training
---
Expansion of the Oren corpus with his freshest (2026) layer: WHY everything feels the same (four machines), HOW premium gets built anyway (four pillars), and the daily regimen that trains a marketer's brain (insight validation, reference edge, opinion reps). "Every time there's one of these shifts, there's a bunch of personal brands, creators, brands, services… that basically blossom out of it."

**Core Philosophy**:
- Understanding before tactics — name the machine before prescribing the move
- Behavioral evidence beats stated preference; profiles beat counts
- Owned references and a held standard are the only uncopyable moats
- Excitement is the marketer's input, not the niche's property
