# Text Hook Rules Card (quick reference)

The on-screen designed text (NOT captions). Most important hook component — eyes hunt text faster than they comprehend images. Full context: genius.md Patterns 3–6.

## Substance (what it says)

**Formula: Subject Repeater + ONE payload**

| Element | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Subject Repeater | Proper noun / technical term / jargon naming the thing. Capitalize names — attention magnets. | "ChatGPT…", "People pleasing…" |
| + Pain-Point Reminder | Name the pain being solved | "…avoid hook mistakes" |
| + Dream Outcome Teaser | Tease the wanted outcome | "…make your hooks unskippable" |
| + Future State Change | The field just changed | "Claude just changed social media forever" |

Curiosity = knowledge gap (today's knowledge vs. tomorrow's outcome). The payload IS the gap.

## Rules (how it's written)

1. **Specificity**: 22 days > "a few weeks"; $2,700 > "a few thousand"; "$97,000 in 13 days on Instagram" > "several thousand in a few weeks."
2. **Power words** for pain/outcome framing: 1–3-word heat-seeking bundles ("social media machine," "scientifically impossible to skip").
3. **Two lines max** (system font, hard cap). Premium typography: 3 lines allowed ONLY with one dominant + accent lines.
4. **Line break follows spoken phrasing**: "ChatGPT just pioneered / the future of work" — never split a read-together phrase.
5. **No punctuation** except quotes and parentheses.
6. **No compound/transition words**: and, or, that, but, therefore. You're writing trigger words, not a sentence.

## Design (how it looks & behaves)

- **Fork**: system font (CapCut/Reels raw) = text separate from word-for-word captions · premium typography = may BE the word-for-word line. Never word-for-word in plain system font (too boring).
- **Placement**: top third or upper-middle; wherever eyes go quickest; never blocking eyes/mouth (above the head if face fills frame).
- **Hold ≥3 seconds** — eye path: eyes → text (catch power words) → visuals → re-read → decide. Cut early = effect lost.
- **Calibrate by landscape scan**: top 15–20 creators in niche, 30–50 outliers curated, remix the aesthetic — never copy.
