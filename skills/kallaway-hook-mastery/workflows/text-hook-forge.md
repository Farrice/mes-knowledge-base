# Text Hook Forge

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: genius.md Decision Framework. Confirm: is this the designed on-screen text (not captions)? What's the audience's wanted outcome? Do we have 30–50 curated winners to calibrate against (if not, note cold-start or run `hook-landscape-scan` first)?

## Skill Acquisition
`genius.md` Patterns 3–6 + `references/text-hook-rules-card.md` (binding rule set).

## Input Required
- **[TOPIC + SUBJECT NAME]**: the thing, with its proper noun / jargon term
- **[PAYLOAD ANGLE]**: pain point being solved, dream outcome, or state change (pick or request all three lanes)
- **[SPOKEN HOOK]** (if it exists): for alignment + the word-for-word/premium fork decision
- **[NICHE EXAMPLES]** (optional): winning title texts to mine

## Execution

1. **Name the Subject Repeater.** The proper noun/technical term, capitalized. If the topic has no name, coin or borrow one — names are attention magnets.
2. **Write all three payload lanes** (pain reminder · outcome teaser · state change), then keep the lane(s) matching [PAYLOAD ANGLE]. Bake the knowledge gap: today's state vs. tomorrow's outcome.
3. **Compress with power words.** Replace every flat phrase with a 1–3-word amplifier. Specificity pass: every vague quantity becomes a number ($ figures, day counts, percentages).
4. **Enforce the six micro-rules** (rules card): specificity · power words · 2-line cap with phrasing-true break points (mark as `line1 / line2`) · no punctuation but quotes/parens · no and/or/that/but/therefore · design-fork correctness (system font ≠ word-for-word; premium may be word-for-word).
5. **Spec the physical layer**: placement (top third / upper-middle, never over eyes/mouth), font fork recommendation, ≥3-second hold.
6. **Alignment check** against [SPOKEN HOOK] and planned visual: does the text predict them?

## Content Type Adaptations
| Surface | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Reels/TikTok/Shorts | Full spec as above |
| YouTube thumbnail | Same substance formula; 2–4 words max; power word mandatory |
| LinkedIn post line 1 | Rules apply minus font/placement; break = the "see more" fold |
| Carousel cover | Subject Repeater dominant line + accent line (premium 3-line pattern) |
| Email subject | Substance formula, ≤6 words, no punctuation rule holds |

## Output Requirements
3–5 options, each: text with break markup · lane used · power words flagged · font fork + placement + hold spec · one-line rationale tracing to a rule or a mined winner. State length held. Feedback Triad.

Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/khm_text_hook_forge.md

## Quality Gate
Every option passes all six micro-rules (list any deliberate exception with its reason). Two-question test on the text alone: subject AND payoff visible without the video. Anti-patterns: 3+ equal lines, sentence punctuation, split phrases, word-for-word system font.
