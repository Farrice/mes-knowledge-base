# Kallaway Hook Mastery — Interrupt Theory OS

**One-line thesis**: a hook is an engineered orienting response — it must answer *what is this about?* and *what do I get?* in ≤3 seconds, across three aligned channels, then EARN the stay in the lock-in zone. Reflex first, trust second, everything else third.

## Quick Reference (the whole system in nine lines)

1. Feed = highway; you're engineering a freeze (orienting response), not persuading.
2. Two questions, instantly answered: what's it about + what do I get.
3. 4 S's or it doesn't hook: Subject, Stakes, Speed (compression ≤12 words), Super Clear (one interpretation).
4. Three channels — text, spoken, visual — each must predict the others. Misalignment is the #1 killer.
5. Text hook is the most important channel; copywriting it is the highest-leverage lever.
6. Text = Subject Repeater + (pain | outcome | state change), specific numbers, power words, 2 lines, no sentence grammar, held 3 seconds.
7. Power words: mine winners, validate (2+ appearances = banger), twist, recombine. Remix, never rip.
8. Visual hooks last: one small aligned move beats a gimmick circus.
9. The hook is the appetizer — the lock-in zone (confirm claim + trust anchor + four levers) makes them stay.
