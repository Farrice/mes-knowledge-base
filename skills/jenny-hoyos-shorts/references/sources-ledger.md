## Anti-Patterns Documented (What NOT to Do)

Extracted from Hoyos' explicit kill criteria and failure cases mentioned in interviews:

1. **"And Then" Storytelling** (Kill Signal)
   - Source: Philipp Humm interview — "storytelling is just cause and effect" (implies zero "and then" beats)
   - Example: "I boil the water, AND THEN add pasta, AND THEN add salt" = retention poison
   - Fix: Every beat must connect through but/so, never and-then
   - **Status**: VERIFIED from explicit interview statements

2. **Announcement Frames** (Hook Killer)
   - Source: Philipp interview — "people are starting to catch on to these like hookie hooks... just start the video with you're already driving"
   - Kill: "Today I'm going to show you..." / "Let me explain..." / "This is crazy because..."
   - Fix: Open mid-action, viewer joins a scene already happening
   - **Status**: VERIFIED from hook-engineering section

3. **Confusion (Top Hidden Kill Switch)**
   - Source: 1of10 Podcast — mentioned as #1 failure reason for otherwise high-production shorts
   - Kill: Viewer doesn't know what's happening by frame 5, or swipes
   - Fix: Frame 1 must be immediately legible (muted, no audio, what's on screen is obvious)
   - **Status**: VERIFIED as explicit kill criterion

4. **Dread/Conclusional Language Mid-Video** (Swipe Trigger)
   - Source: Philipp interview — "finally," "sit back, this is going to take a while" are documented swipe triggers
   - Kill: Any phrase that signals the video is ending before it's halfway done
   - Fix: Sweep script before production; remove conclusional language until the actual ending
   - **Status**: VERIFIED from retention-graph analysis

5. **Flat Dead Endings** (Rewatch Killer)
   - Source: Loop-Ending Engineering pattern — "it has to flow with what the hook is going to be"
   - Kill: Final frame is static, final line doesn't resolve the opening question, no loop mechanism
   - Fix: Either stitch the ending back to the hook, or plant a "wait, what about ___?" second question
   - **Status**: VERIFIED from rewatch physics (100% retention target)

6. **Off-Avatar Ideation** (Algorithm Confusion)
   - Source: 1of10 Podcast — "the algorithm is this audience" — if idea is off-avatar, algorithm doesn't know who to show it to
   - Kill: Idea works for a general audience but not for the named single viewer
   - Fix: Filter every idea through the single named avatar first; cut ruthlessly off-avatar ideas
   - **Status**: VERIFIED from channel-stage-specific distribution patterns
