# Neural Marketing Trigger Map (NMTM) — Reference

> Per Omar's Neural Marketing Trigger Map signature move: "I need to know exactly per avatar what is their trigger category, what is the specific trigger, the intensity, how to deploy in an ad, and then the funnel stage."

The NMTM bridges customer research → creative production. It ensures every ad has an identified psychological lever, not vibes.

---

## The 6 Trigger Categories

### 1. Fear-Based
**Mechanism**: Loss aversion, threat avoidance, status protection
**When it lands**: Problem-aware audiences, early-stage funnels, identity-protection contexts
**Example specifics**:
- Fear of missing the moment ("Limited release — never again")
- Fear of social embarrassment ("Don't be the one who didn't know about this")
- Fear of decline / aging ("Your 30s are when this matters most")
- Fear of regret ("People who waited wished they hadn't")

**Intensity scale 1-10**: 1 = soft mention; 10 = visceral fear of imminent loss
**Funnel stage best fit**: Top-of-funnel (problem awareness), bottom-of-funnel (urgency drivers)

### 2. Desire-Based
**Mechanism**: Aspiration, identity attainment, lifestyle visualization
**When it lands**: Solution-aware and product-aware audiences, identity-led brands
**Example specifics**:
- Aspirational lifestyle ("Wake up to a body that feels 10 years younger")
- Identity attainment ("Become the version of yourself you've been trying to be")
- Sensory desire ("That first cold plunge moment of clarity")
- Status desire ("The watch worn by the people who run things")

**Intensity scale 1-10**: 1 = subtle aspiration; 10 = visceral lifestyle imagery
**Funnel stage best fit**: Mid-funnel, retargeting, premium positioning

### 3. Social Proof
**Mechanism**: Validation through others' choices, herd intelligence, FOMO
**When it lands**: All stages, especially when skepticism is high
**Example specifics**:
- Authority endorsement ("Doctors recommend...")
- Volume proof ("Over 50,000 customers")
- Peer proof ("Used by people like you")
- Publication proof ("As seen in...")
- UGC / testimonial ("Sarah's transformation in 30 days")

**Intensity scale 1-10**: 1 = single testimonial; 10 = stacked authority + volume + peer
**Funnel stage best fit**: All stages, especially mid-to-bottom

### 4. Cognitive Bias Activation
**Mechanism**: Triggers a specific bias to bypass rational evaluation
**When it lands**: Sophisticated audiences who would otherwise over-analyze
**Example specifics**:
- Anchoring (high original price → discounted price)
- Reciprocity (free gift before ask)
- Commitment + consistency (small Y → bigger Y)
- Scarcity / loss aversion ("Only 7 left")
- Social proof (see above)
- Authority (see above)
- Liking (similarity / familiarity / attractiveness of source)

**Intensity scale 1-10**: 1 = subtle bias activation; 10 = stacked multiple biases
**Funnel stage best fit**: Bottom-of-funnel, conversion optimization

### 5. Urgency
**Mechanism**: Time-pressure forces decision before deliberation
**When it lands**: Decision-stalled audiences, retargeting
**Example specifics**:
- Time scarcity ("Sale ends Sunday")
- Inventory scarcity ("Only 23 units left at this price")
- Cohort scarcity ("Closing applications Friday")
- Seasonal urgency ("Before summer, you have 6 weeks")
- Identity-deadline urgency ("This is the year you...")

**Intensity scale 1-10**: 1 = soft date mention; 10 = countdown timer + scarcity stack
**Funnel stage best fit**: Bottom-of-funnel, abandoned-cart, retargeting

### 6. Trust
**Mechanism**: Reduces perceived risk through credibility signals
**When it lands**: Skeptical audiences, sophisticated markets, premium price points
**Example specifics**:
- Founder transparency ("Hi, I'm [name], here's why I started this")
- Process transparency ("How it's made")
- Money-back guarantee
- Third-party certifications (USDA Organic, FDA registered, B-Corp)
- Long-form education content (signals "we have nothing to hide")
- Negative reviews displayed prominently (counterintuitively builds trust)

**Intensity scale 1-10**: 1 = generic guarantee; 10 = stacked transparency + founder + certifications
**Funnel stage best fit**: All stages — required for premium price points

## NMTM Matrix Template

For each avatar, build the following matrix:

| Trigger Category | Specific Trigger | Intensity (1-10) | Ad Deployment | Funnel Stage |
|------------------|------------------|-------------------|---------------|--------------|
| Fear | [specific fear] | [intensity] | [hook example] | [stage] |
| Desire | [specific desire] | [intensity] | [hook example] | [stage] |
| Social Proof | [specific proof] | [intensity] | [hook example] | [stage] |
| Cognitive Bias | [specific bias] | [intensity] | [hook example] | [stage] |
| Urgency | [specific urgency] | [intensity] | [hook example] | [stage] |
| Trust | [specific trust signal] | [intensity] | [hook example] | [stage] |

**Per avatar**: Select the TOP 3 triggers by intensity × stage-fit. These become the primary creative levers for that avatar.

## Worked Example — Marcus, the Recovery-Focused Creator

| Trigger Category | Specific Trigger | Intensity | Ad Deployment | Funnel Stage |
|------------------|------------------|-----------|---------------|--------------|
| Fear | Overtraining without knowing | 7 | Hook: "By the time your body tells you, you've already lost 2 weeks" | Top-of-funnel |
| Desire | High-performer identity reinforcement | 9 | Lifestyle imagery of cold plunge + readiness score | Mid-funnel |
| Social Proof | Niche micro-influencer endorsement | 8 | "Used by [niche performance creators]" | All stages |
| Cognitive Bias | Authority + scarcity stack | 6 | "Recommended by sleep researchers — limited Q1 availability" | Bottom-funnel |
| Urgency | Identity-deadline ("This is the year you...") | 5 | "2026 is your highest-performing year — set the foundation now" | Retargeting |
| Trust | Founder + R&D transparency | 8 | Founder ad: "Why I built this for myself" + lab process video | All stages |

**Top 3 for Marcus**: Desire (9) + Trust (8) + Social Proof (8)

These three triggers drive his creative portfolio. Hook bank generation focuses on these three categories.

## Hook Bank Generation Brief (use after NMTM is built)

```
You are a creative strategist building a hook bank for [BRAND] targeting [AVATAR NAME].

Avatar context: [paste 1-page avatar]
Top 3 triggers: [paste from NMTM]

Generate 20 scroll-stopping hooks for static and video ads.

Distribution requirement:
- 7 hooks deploying Trigger 1 ([trigger name])
- 7 hooks deploying Trigger 2
- 6 hooks deploying Trigger 3

Each hook must:
- Use verbatim customer language from the avatar's "Their language" section
- Be 6-12 words for static / 1-2 sentences for video
- Pass the "would my avatar stop scrolling for this?" test
- Tag which trigger it deploys (Fear / Desire / Social Proof / etc.)

Output as a markdown table: Hook | Trigger | Format (static/video) | Why it works for this avatar
```

## Anti-Patterns

❌ **Trigger-stuffing** — using all 6 triggers in one ad. Pick 1-2 dominant triggers per creative.

❌ **Generic triggers** — "fear of missing out" applied to every brand. The trigger must be SPECIFIC to the avatar's context.

❌ **Wrong stage triggers** — using bottom-of-funnel urgency on top-of-funnel cold traffic. Match trigger to funnel stage.

❌ **Inconsistent intensity** — pairing intensity-9 fear with intensity-2 trust. Calibrate for emotional coherence.

❌ **Skipping the matrix** — going straight to hooks without psychological architecture. The hooks become guesswork.
