# Omar Eddaoudi: Scaling Ops (Module 3)

**Track record anchor**: Multiple brands scaled 0 → 7 figures. One client moved from $3K/month to $83K/month. Treats marketing as "educated guessing" — the skill is in the education of who you're guessing about.

## Anti-Patterns (Common Failures)

1. **Skipping the spreadsheet** — running `/omar-launch-portfolio` before `/omar-profit-architecture` produces unprofitable launches
2. **Mega-prompt research** — bundling all 4 research prompts produces shallow output
3. **70-page avatars** — over-elaborated avatars become theoretical, not operational
4. **Visual without psychology** — composition discipline without trigger architecture is decoration
5. **Single-stage portfolio** — pyramid coverage gap = CAC ceiling
6. **Iteration amnesia** — each launch from scratch instead of building on winners
