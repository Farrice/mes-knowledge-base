## What This Skill Does

Takes **any founder, brand, or organization** from invisible to narrative-dominant in 30 days by chaining six expert skill sets through the a16z kingmaker methodology:

1. **Diagnose** the target's current media posture (old vs. new media physics)
2. **Position** the founder for go-direct communication (Joe Rogan CEO test)
3. **Architect** the content city with oral/written culture awareness
4. **Arm** every position with proof (proof ladder across platforms)
5. **Launch** with platform-native campaigns designed for viral impact
6. **Protect** with OODA-speed narrative control and crisis protocols
