# Workflow 02: Narrative Warfare — OODA-Powered Competitive Dominance

## Purpose
Win any narrative war permanently. Combines OODA loop speed advantage with proof-stacked content and flood-zone tactics to ensure you are NEVER responding to competitors — they are always responding to you.

## Input Required
- Your brand/founder/organization
- 2-5 key competitors (with their public channels listed)
- Your current OODA loop speed (or "unknown")
- Active narrative battles (if any)
- Available publishing channels and team capacity

## Step 1: Intelligence Gathering

### 1a: Competitor OODA Assessment
**Load**: `skills/andreessen-horowitz-new-media/genius.md` → OODA Loop Media Warfare pattern

For each competitor, research and estimate:
- How often do they publish? (frequency = proxy for loop speed)
- How fast do they respond to industry events? (measure in hours from event → their post)
- What platforms do they dominate? Tag each as oral/written culture
- What's their crisis response pattern? (fast flood vs. slow defense vs. silent)
- Who speaks — brand account or founder personally?

| Competitor | Est. OODA Speed | Dominant Platforms | Culture Modes | Crisis Pattern | Founder Go-Direct? |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|---------------|----------------|-------------------|

### 1b: Narrative Map
Map the current narrative landscape:
- What are the 5 dominant narratives in your space right now?
- Who OWNS each narrative? (first mover advantage)
- Which narratives are contested? (multiple voices competing)
- Which narratives are UNOWNED? (white space)

## Step 2: Speed Architecture

### 2a: Your OODA Loop Design
Build each stage for minimum cycle time:

**Observe** (target: real-time)
- Set up monitoring: competitor content feeds, industry news, audience signals
- Decide: RSS, social listening, manual checks — whatever your capacity allows
- Key question: "How quickly do I KNOW when the landscape shifts?"

**Orient** (target: <1 hour)
- Pre-built orientation templates for top 10 scenario categories
- Each template includes: "This is happening → This is what it means for us → This is our position"
- Categories:
  1. Competitor product launch
  2. Industry trend shift
  3. Regulation/policy change
  4. Viral moment in our space
  5. Direct competitor attack on us
  6. Customer win/loss
  7. Technology breakthrough
  8. Hiring/talent market shift
  9. Media/press coverage of our space
  10. Cultural moment we can authentically tie to

**Decide** (target: <30 minutes)
- Decision authority: Who decides? (ideally: founder alone)
- Decision framework: Is this worth responding to? (filter: does it affect our narrative?)
- If yes → which pre-built template? Which platform? What culture mode?

**Act** (target: <2 hours from observation)
- Publishing protocol: draft → one review → publish
- NO multi-layer approval. That's old media.
- Written-culture response: Substack/essay for complex positions
- Oral-culture response: Tweet thread or short video for simple reactions

### 2b: Speed Dominance Ratio
Calculate: Your loop speed ÷ competitor loop speed

| Competitor | Their Loop | Your Loop | Ratio | Status |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-------|--------|
| | X hours | Y hours | X/Y | Dominant / Contested / Behind |

Target: minimum 2:1 ratio (you cycle 2x faster than nearest competitor)

## Step 3: Proof-Armed Positions
**Load**: `skills/luke-iha-proof-copy/SKILL.md` → proof ladder methodology

For each of your top 5 narrative positions:
- **Claim**: Your position stated clearly
- **Proof Stack**: 3+ proof types supporting it
  - Personal proof (your experience/results)
  - Social proof (customer results, testimonials)
  - Third-party proof (industry data, research)
- **Platform Deployment Plan**: Which culture mode gets which proof type?
  - Oral platforms: demonstration proof, before/after, social proof clips
  - Written platforms: statistical analysis, case studies, research citations
- **Vicious Hook**: Generate 5 hooks per position using Luke Iha methodology

This creates a library of pre-armed positions that can be deployed instantly when the OODA loop triggers.

## Step 4: Offensive Disruption Calendar
**Load**: `skills/grace-andrews-media-company/workflows/11-content-sprint-planner.md`

Design a 4-week proactive disruption plan (not reactive — PROACTIVE):

| Week | Disruption Play | Platform | Culture Mode | Proof Type |
|------|----------------|----------|-------------|------------|
| 1 | Contrarian take on industry consensus | Long-form first | Written | Research + personal |
| 2 | Exclusive data/insight reveal | All platforms, simultaneous | Cross-mode | Statistical |
| 3 | Founder vulnerability piece (why we almost failed) | LinkedIn + Substack | Hybrid/Written | Experiential |
| 4 | Industry prediction with evidence | X thread → YouTube deep dive | Oral → Written | Third-party + analysis |

Each disruption is designed to FORCE competitors into your OODA loop — they must respond to YOUR narrative, not the reverse.

## Step 5: Defensive Flood Protocol
**Load**: `skills/andreessen-horowitz-new-media/references/prompts/05-flood-the-zone-crisis-protocol.md`

Pre-build the crisis arsenal:
- **Content Bank**: 10 "in the can" interesting pieces ready for emergency publication
  - 3 data-driven insights pieces (always relevant)
  - 3 founder story/vulnerability pieces
  - 2 customer success showcases
  - 2 industry analysis/prediction pieces
- **One-Touch Templates**: Pre-drafted contextual addresses for top 5 attack vectors
- **Ally Activation List**: 5-10 allies with pre-coordinated rapid response agreement
- **Cascade Protocol**: Minute-by-minute flood deployment sequence

## Step 6: Monthly Warfare Review

Monthly check-in protocol:
1. Recalculate competitor OODA speeds (have they compressed?)
2. Update narrative map (any new unowned narratives?)
3. Refresh proof assets (any new case studies, data, testimonials?)
4. Replenish content bank (anything deployed last month needs replacing)
5. Score: Are competitors responding to US or are we responding to THEM?

## Output
- **Competitive OODA Intelligence Dashboard** (table of speeds, ratios, vulnerabilities)
- **Pre-Armed Position Library** (5+ positions with proof stacks and hooks)
- **4-Week Disruption Calendar** (proactive offensive)
- **Crisis Arsenal** (10 content pieces + templates + ally list)
- **Monthly Review Protocol** (recurring dominance maintenance)
