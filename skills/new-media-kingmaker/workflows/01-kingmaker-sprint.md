# Workflow 01: Kingmaker Sprint — Invisible to Dominant in 30 Days

## Purpose
End-to-end transformation of any founder, brand, or organization from media-invisible to narrative-dominant using the full a16z kingmaker methodology cross-pollinated with 5+ expert skill sets.

## Input Required
- Target: founder name, company, industry
- Current media presence (or lack thereof)
- Key competitors with public presence
- Core product/service offering
- Founder's strongest opinions and beliefs (the ones they suppress)
- Available team resources and weekly capacity

## Phase 1: Diagnostic & Positioning (Days 1-3)

### Step 1: New Media Audit
**Load**: `skills/andreessen-horowitz-new-media/workflows/01-new-media-audit.md`

Execute the full audit:
- Old/New Media Classification: Score every current practice
- OODA Loop Speed: Measure current decision-to-publish cycle (in hours)
- Oral/Written Culture Map: Classify every active platform
- Joe Rogan CEO Test: Score founder 1-10

**Output**: Diagnostic report with scored rebuild prescription

### Step 2: Founder Voice Mining
**Load**: `skills/andreessen-horowitz-new-media/workflows/04-founder-go-direct.md`
**Support**: `skills/nicolas-cole-ghostwriting/SKILL.md` (voice capture methodology)

Execute founder positioning:
- Original Idea Inventory: Mine 3-5 ideas from founding insight
- Unscripting Protocol: Kill list of old-media conditioning
- Controversy Calibration: Map ideas on consensus → third rail spectrum
- Voice Capture: Nicolas Cole methodology to document authentic voice patterns

**Output**: Founder positioning brief + voice document + first 3 content outlines

## Phase 2: Content Architecture (Days 4-7)

### Step 3: City Blueprint with Culture Layer
**Load**: `skills/grace-andrews-media-company/SKILL.md` + `genius.md`
**Load**: `skills/grace-andrews-media-company/workflows/01-city-map-architect.md`
**Inject**: Oral/written culture matrix from a16z frameworks

Build the content city:
- Grand Central Station: Editorial mission anchored in founder's #1 original idea
- Content Lines: Mapped to oral vs. written culture modes (NOT just platforms)
- Trust Pathways: Attention → Connection → Trust → Conversion with culture-mode awareness
- Each content line tagged: oral-native or written-native

**Critical addition**: Enforce written-first pipeline. All complex positions established in long-form (Substack, YouTube, podcast) BEFORE oral extraction to short-form.

### Step 4: Competitive OODA Benchmarking
**Load**: `skills/andreessen-horowitz-new-media/workflows/02-ooda-media-warfare.md`

For each competitor identified:
- Estimate their OODA loop speed (how fast do they publish after a narrative shift?)
- Map their content to oral/written modes
- Identify their slowest stage (where they're most vulnerable)
- Calculate speed dominance ratio (your target loop ÷ their loop)

**Output**: Competitive intelligence dashboard with speed asymmetry mapped

## Phase 3: Arsenal Loading (Days 8-14)

### Step 5: Proof Ladder Deployment Across Platforms
**Load**: `skills/luke-iha-proof-copy/SKILL.md` + `genius.md`
**Load**: `skills/luke-iha-proof-copy/workflows/proof-ladder-builder.md`

For each platform in the content city:
- Identify the proof types that work in that platform's culture mode
  - Oral platforms (tweets, reels, shorts): Social proof, demonstration proof, before/after
  - Written platforms (Substack, essays, YouTube long-form): Statistical proof, case study proof, third-party proof
  - Hybrid platforms (LinkedIn): Proof braids combining both modes
- Build proof stacks per content line
- Generate 10+ vicious hooks per platform (culturally native — not reformatted)

### Step 6: LinkedIn Authority Architecture
**Load**: `skills/lara-acosta/SKILL.md`

Build the LinkedIn power base:
- Profile conversion audit using Lara Acosta methodology
- Social proof tagline engineering
- First 10 LinkedIn posts mapped to trust pathway stages
- Each post tagged for oral/written culture mode appropriate to LinkedIn (hybrid)
- Revenue bridge design: attention → connection → conversion path on LinkedIn specifically

### Step 7: Email Conversion Architecture
**Load**: `skills/cardinal-mason/SKILL.md`

Build the conversion backend:
- 5-email welcome sequence (for new audience captured during launch)
- Proof-braided throughout using Luke Iha methodology
- Segmentation plan: oral-culture audience vs. written-culture audience get different sequences
- Bridge email that moves people from short-form consumption to long-form trust building

## Phase 4: Launch Campaign (Days 15-21)

### Step 8: Platform-Native Launch
**Load**: `skills/andreessen-horowitz-new-media/references/prompts/06-platform-native-launch-service.md`

Design the launch-as-a-service campaign:
- **Platform-native content slate**: Different content per platform, not reformatted
  - X: Oral-culture spike (provocative thread w/ vicious hooks)
  - YouTube long-form: Written-culture canonical reference
  - YouTube Shorts/TikTok/Reels: Oral-culture extraction of one "whoa" moment
  - LinkedIn: Hybrid-mode founder personal story
  - Substack/Blog: Written-culture manifesto
- **Founder go-direct activation**: Founder is the face, not the brand
- **Cascade sequence**: Pre-launch tease → launch spike → sustain wave → long-tail
- **Ally amplification**: 10-20 allies coordinated for independent content creation

### Step 9: Vicious Hook Sprint for Launch
**Load**: `skills/luke-iha-proof-copy/workflows/vicious-hook-sprint.md`

Generate 30+ hooks specifically for the launch:
- Hooks per platform (culturally native)
- Hooks per trust stage (awareness → conversion)
- Scored using Luke Iha's 8 Vicious Hook Principles
- Top 5 A-tier hooks marked for founder personal use

## Phase 5: Dominance & Protection (Days 22-30)

### Step 10: OODA Loop Establishment
Build the permanent speed advantage:
- Observation system: What are you monitoring? (competitors, industry, audience signals)
- Orientation template: Pre-built position templates for top 10 scenarios
- Decision framework: Who decides? How fast? (target: hours, not days)
- Action protocol: Publish chain from decision to live content
- Measure and compress: Weekly OODA speed audit

### Step 11: Crisis Protocol Installation
**Load**: `skills/andreessen-horowitz-new-media/references/prompts/05-flood-the-zone-crisis-protocol.md`

Pre-build the crisis response system:
- Identify top 5 most likely attack vectors
- For each: pre-drafted contextual address (long-form)
- Flood content bank: 10 "in the can" interesting pieces ready for emergency publication
- Ally activation checklist with contact info and talking points
- 48-hour assessment protocol
