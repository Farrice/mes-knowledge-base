# Workflow 03: Platform-Native Launch — Launch-as-a-Service Campaign

## Purpose
Design and execute a product/company/feature launch that generates maximum impact by treating every platform as a different MEDIUM requiring different content, different taste, and different creative execution. This is a16z's "Launch as a Service" product cross-pollinated with Luke Iha hooks, Grace Andrews trust pathways, and proof-stacked copy.

## Input Required
- Product/company/feature being launched
- Target audience segments
- Available platforms and team resources
- Founder/CEO's capacity for direct involvement
- Existing assets (demo videos, screenshots, data, customer stories)
- Launch date and timeline
- Key competitors for competitive positioning context

## Step 1: Core Launch Concept
**Load**: `skills/andreessen-horowitz-new-media/references/prompts/06-platform-native-launch-service.md`

Identify THE single unifying insight that makes this launch genuinely interesting:
- What's the most extreme or surprising metric? (e.g., "12 hours → 30 seconds")
- What's the founder story that makes people FEEL something?
- What's the competitive gap that this fills?
- What's the "whoa" moment in the product demo?

The core concept must be strong enough that people share because they CAN'T NOT share.

## Step 2: Platform-Native Content Slate
**Load**: `skills/andreessen-horowitz-new-media/genius.md` → oral/written culture matrix

For EACH platform, design genuinely DIFFERENT content (NOT reformatted):

### X / Twitter (Oral Culture)
- **Type**: Provocative announcement thread optimized for retweets
- **Structure**: Lead with most counterintuitive stat → demo GIF → how it works → personal hook → CTA
- **Hooks**: Generate 5+ tweet-native hooks using Luke Iha methodology
- **Mode**: Burst, emotional, identity-triggering

### YouTube Long-Form (Written Culture)
- **Type**: Canonical reference video (10-20 min)
- **Structure**: Founder on camera → origin story (90 sec) → live demo → specific wins → customer clip → invitation
- **Mode**: Depth, analytical rigor, evidence-based
- **This is the piece people send to colleagues**

### YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels (Oral Culture)
- **Type**: The single most "whoa" moment, 30-60 seconds
- **Design**: One visual/concept so compelling it stands alone
- **Generate 3 separate shorts**, each highlighting a different angle
- **Mode**: Interpersonal, campfire energy, shareability

### LinkedIn (Hybrid Mode)
- **Type**: Founder personal story with announcement embedded in narrative
- **Load**: `skills/lara-acosta/SKILL.md` for LinkedIn-native formatting
- **Structure**: Personal story of WHY → what you built → specific result → invitation
- **Mode**: Hybrid — personal vulnerability + professional authority

### Substack / Blog (Written Culture)
- **Type**: Full vision manifesto — WHY this matters
- **Structure**: Industry problem (historical) → why previous solutions failed → your breakthrough → implications → future vision
- **Mode**: Logical, rigorous, long-form persuasion
- **This establishes the full argument before any oral extraction**

### Email Sequence (Written Culture → Conversion)
- **Load**: `skills/cardinal-mason/SKILL.md`
- **Type**: 3-email launch sequence for existing subscribers
- **Email 1**: Pre-launch tease (24h before — build anticipation)
- **Email 2**: Launch announcement (hour 0 — link to all assets)
- **Email 3**: Social proof follow-up (48h after — early results, reactions)

## Step 3: Proof Loading Per Platform

| Platform | Culture Mode | Best Proof Types |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| X Thread | Oral | Demonstration, before/after, social proof |
| YouTube Long | Written | Statistical, case study, third-party |
| Shorts/Reels | Oral | Visual demonstration, extreme metrics |
| LinkedIn | Hybrid | Personal experience + customer results |
| Substack | Written | Research, analysis, competitive proof |
| Email | Written | Testimonials, results data, urgency |

## Step 4: Founder Go-Direct Activation
**Load**: `skills/andreessen-horowitz-new-media/workflows/04-founder-go-direct.md`

The founder is the FACE of the launch. Design their specific deliverables:
- Which tweets they post from their PERSONAL account (not brand)
- Which video they appear in (on camera, raw, not overproduced)
- Which LinkedIn post they publish personally
- Which podcast they appear on during launch week (pitch 2-3)
- What podcast talking points to prepare (interesting, not safe)

## Step 5: Cascade Sequence Design

Design the minute-by-minute launch cascade:

### Pre-Launch Tease (72 hours before)
- Day -3: Subtle teaser on the founder's personal channels (curiosity, not announcement)
- Day -2: Behind-the-scenes content (the human story of building)
- Day -1: Email to subscribers with "Tomorrow, everything changes" energy

### Launch Spike (Hour 0-12)
- Hour 0: Simultaneous cross-platform publishing
- Hour 0: Ally amplification activation (see Step 6)
- Hour 1-4: Founder engages in real-time replies (oral culture mode)
- Hour 4-8: Publish additional reaction/behind-the-scenes content
- Hour 8-12: Monitor spike trajectory, feed winning platforms

### Sustain Wave (Hour 12-72)
- Follow-up content: Q&A, customer reactions, behind-the-scenes
- Repurpose any organic viral moments (real-time OODA loop)
- Second LinkedIn post from founder with early results

### Long-Tail (Day 3-14)
- Podcast appearances (pre-booked)
- In-depth review embargoes lifted
- Customer case study content begins
- Newsletter deep-dive edition

## Step 6: Ally Amplification Network

Identify and pre-coordinate with 10-20 allies:

| Ally | Platform | Their Angle | Assets to Provide |
|------|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| [Investor] | X | Investment thesis | Product data, demo access |
| [Customer] | LinkedIn | Results story | Metric highlights, quotable results |
| [Industry KOL] | YouTube | Analysis | Early access, interview slot |
| [Partner] | Substack | Integration story | Technical specs, API benefits |

- They create content in THEIR voice, on THEIR platforms
- Provide talking points + assets, NOT pre-written posts
- Coordinate timing (all within first 24 hours)

## Step 7: Real-Time OODA During Launch

Run the OODA loop at maximum speed during launch (hours 0-72):

**Observe**: Real-time monitoring of all platforms — what's spiking, what's flat?
**Orient**: Which platform is generating the most organic amplification? Any unexpected reactions?
**Decide**: Feed more content to winning platforms. Redirect resources from underperforming channels.
**Act**: Publish follow-up content within hours of observing traction.
