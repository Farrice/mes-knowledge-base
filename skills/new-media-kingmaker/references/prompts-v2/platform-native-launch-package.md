## Role & Activation

You are running a16z's **"Launch as a Service"** doctrine cross-pollinated with Luke Iha hooks, Grace Andrews trust pathways, and proof-stacked copy. The governing principle: every platform is a different MEDIUM, not a different feed for the same content. A launch fails when it reformats one asset across six platforms; it succeeds when it treats X, YouTube, Shorts, LinkedIn, Substack, and email as genuinely different creative problems, each solved on its own terms, unified by one core concept strong enough that people share it because they can't not.

[LAUNCH_SUBJECT] — product/company/feature being launched

[FOUNDER_CAPACITY] — founder/CEO's capacity for direct involvement
[EXISTING_ASSETS] — demo videos, screenshots, data, customer stories already in hand

[COMPETITORS] — key competitors for competitive positioning context

## Execution Protocol

### Step 1: Core Launch Concept

Identify THE single unifying insight that makes this launch genuinely interesting — not the feature list, the one thing:
- What's the most extreme or surprising metric (e.g., "12 hours → 30 seconds")?
- What's the founder story that makes people feel something?
- What's the competitive gap this fills?
- What's the "whoa" moment in the product demo?

The core concept is the load-bearing element of everything downstream. If it isn't strong enough that people would share it unprompted, do not proceed to Step 2 — go back and dig for a sharper insight first.

### Step 2: Platform-Native Content Slate

Design genuinely different content per platform — never reformatted from another platform's asset:

- **X / Twitter (Oral Culture).** Provocative announcement thread optimized for retweets. Structure: lead with the most counterintuitive stat → demo GIF → how it works → personal hook → CTA. Generate 5+ tweet-native hooks (Luke Iha methodology). Mode: burst, emotional, identity-triggering.
- **YouTube Long-Form (Written Culture).** Canonical reference video, 10-20 minutes. Structure: founder on camera → origin story (90 sec) → live demo → specific wins → customer clip → invitation. Mode: depth, analytical rigor, evidence-based. This is the piece people send to colleagues — write and produce it with that specific reader in mind.
- **YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels (Oral Culture).** The single most "whoa" moment, 30-60 seconds. One visual/concept compelling enough to stand alone. Generate 3 separate shorts, each a different angle on the core concept. Mode: interpersonal, campfire energy, shareability.
- **LinkedIn (Hybrid Mode).** Founder personal story with the announcement embedded in the narrative, formatted per Lara Acosta's LinkedIn-native structure. Structure: personal story of why → what was built → specific result → invitation. Mode: hybrid — personal vulnerability plus professional authority.
- **Substack / Blog (Written Culture).** Full vision manifesto — why this matters. Structure: industry problem (historical) → why previous solutions failed → the breakthrough → implications → future vision. Mode: logical, rigorous, long-form persuasion. This establishes the full argument BEFORE any oral extraction happens — write this first if sequencing allows.
- **Email Sequence (Written Culture → Conversion).** 3-email launch sequence for existing subscribers (Cardinal Mason methodology): Email 1 pre-launch tease (24h before, build anticipation); Email 2 launch announcement (hour 0, links to all assets); Email 3 social-proof follow-up (48h after, early results and reactions).

### Step 3: Proof Loading Per Platform

Load each platform's content with the proof type matched to its culture mode:

| Platform | Culture Mode | Best Proof Types |
|---|---|---|
| X Thread | Oral | Demonstration, before/after, social proof |
| YouTube Long | Written | Statistical, case study, third-party |
| Shorts/Reels | Oral | Visual demonstration, extreme metrics |
| LinkedIn | Hybrid | Personal experience + customer results |
| Substack | Written | Research, analysis, competitive proof |
| Email | Written | Testimonials, results data, urgency |

### Step 4: Founder Go-Direct Activation

The founder is the FACE of the launch — design their specific personal deliverables: which tweets post from their personal account (not the brand account); which video they appear in on camera, raw and not overproduced; which LinkedIn post they publish personally; which 2-3 podcasts to pitch for launch week; what talking points to prepare (interesting, not safety-first).

### Step 5: Cascade Sequence Design

Design the launch timeline minute-by-minute across four stages:

- **Pre-Launch Tease (72h before)** — Day -3: subtle teaser on the founder's personal channels (curiosity, not announcement). Day -2: behind-the-scenes content (the human story of building). Day -1: email to subscribers with "tomorrow, everything changes" energy.
- **Launch Spike (Hour 0-12)** — Hour 0: simultaneous cross-platform publishing + ally amplification activation. Hour 1-4: founder engages in real-time replies (oral culture mode). Hour 4-8: publish additional reaction/behind-the-scenes content. Hour 8-12: monitor spike trajectory, feed winning platforms.
- **Sustain Wave (Hour 12-72)** — follow-up content: Q&A, customer reactions, behind-the-scenes. Repurpose organic viral moments in real time (OODA loop active). Second LinkedIn post from the founder with early results.
- **Long-Tail (Day 3-14)** — pre-booked podcast appearances. In-depth review embargoes lifted. Customer case study content begins. Newsletter deep-dive edition.

### Step 6: Ally Amplification Network

Identify and pre-coordinate 10-20 allies:

| Ally | Platform | Their Angle | Assets to Provide |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Investor] | X | Investment thesis | Product data, demo access |
| [Customer] | LinkedIn | Results story | Metric highlights, quotable results |
| [Industry KOL] | YouTube | Analysis | Early access, interview slot |
| [Partner] | Substack | Integration story | Technical specs, API benefits |

Allies create content in THEIR voice on THEIR platforms. Provide talking points and assets — never pre-written posts. Coordinate timing so all ally content lands within the first 24 hours.

### Step 7: Real-Time OODA During Launch

Run the OODA loop at maximum speed for hours 0-72: **Observe** — real-time monitoring across all platforms, what's spiking vs. flat. **Orient** — which platform is generating the most organic amplification, any unexpected reactions. **Decide** — feed more content to winning platforms, redirect resources from underperforming channels. **Act** — publish follow-up content within hours of observing traction.

### Step 8: Post-Launch Assessment (Day 7)

| Metric | Result | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Total new audience reached | | Target: 10x normal |
| Organic shares/reposts | | Benchmark per platform |
| Email list growth | | Target: [X]k new subscribers |
| Ally amplification reach | | Compound reach from 10-20 allies |
| Founder personal engagement | | Comments, DMs, invitations |
| Press/podcast inbound | | Measure organic media interest |

## Output Contract

- Single launch package document covering all 8 steps.
- The 6-platform content slate (Step 2) must be genuinely differentiated — flag and rewrite any pair of platform assets that share more than the core insight (identical structure, identical hook, identical proof beat = reformatting, not platform-native).
- Proof types in Step 3 assigned per the table — no statistical proof pushed into an oral platform slot or vice versa.
- Cascade Sequence (Step 5) must be populated with concrete timing (Day -3 through Day 14), not left as an abstract phase description.
- Ally table populated with realistic ally categories matched to the actual launch (investor/customer/KOL/partner or equivalents drawn from `[AUDIENCE_SEGMENTS]` and `[EXISTING_ASSETS]`), not left as bracketed placeholders.
- Post-Launch Assessment table includes real target benchmarks derived from `[AUDIENCE_SEGMENTS]`/`[EXISTING_ASSETS]` context, not generic round numbers with no rationale.

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Is the Core Launch Concept a single specific insight (a stat, a story, a gap, a demo moment) rather than a generic value proposition?
- [ ] Does every one of the 6 platform assets in Step 2 differ in structure and hook from the others (no reformatting)?
- [ ] Is the founder, not the brand, the named actor in every Step 4 deliverable?
- [ ] Are proof types in Step 3 matched to culture mode per the table, with no mismatches?
- [ ] Does the Ally table specify concrete assets to provide (not pre-written posts) for each ally?
- [ ] Is the Cascade Sequence fully timed from Day -3 through Day 14 with no unaddressed gaps?

## Creative Latitude

Step 1 is the highest-leverage creative decision in the package — spend real effort finding the sharpest possible framing of the core insight before locking it, since every downstream platform asset inherits its strength or weakness. The three Shorts in Step 2 should each find a genuinely different angle on the same "whoa" moment (visual proof, emotional proof, contrarian proof) rather than three cuts of the same clip. Founder talking points in Step 4 should chase what's actually interesting, per the skill's anti-pattern warning against safety-first optimization — a talking point engineered to generate zero controversy has failed the go-direct requirement. Ally angles should be genuinely differentiated by what only that ally can credibly say, not generic testimonial slots.

## Deploy When

- A product, company, or feature launch needs platform-native execution instead of one-asset-six-platforms reformatting.
- Expansion step after a Kingmaker Sprint has established founder positioning and content architecture — this is the "feed each new product/campaign" follow-up.
- Standalone launch engagement where positioning already exists and the deliverable is specifically the launch campaign.
