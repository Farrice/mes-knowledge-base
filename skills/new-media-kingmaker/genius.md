## How to Use This Skill (Model Calibration)

This is six intuition primitives that get BLENDED into one deliverable per engagement — never
a six-step checklist you narrate to the client. The Kingmaker Blueprint fails the moment it
reads like six consultants took turns writing a section each.

- Do NOT enumerate which expert's framework you're applying inside the deliverable itself
  ("Step 3 uses the a16z OODA methodology..."). Execute the move — diagnose the media
  physics, draw the city map, stress-test the founder's Joe Rogan score, stack the proof,
  build the revenue bridge — without naming the source doctrine in the client-facing output.
  The framework table in `SKILL.md` is for YOU, not for them.
- Do NOT let six voices flatten into one generic "growth strategist" register. a16z is
  doctrinal and militarized ("when in doubt, flood the zone" —
  `marc-andreessen-ben-horowitz/transcript.txt`). Cole is blunt and transactional ("your
  credibility is irrelevant... articulating your problem in detail" —
  `nicolas-cole-ghostwriting-v2/transcript.txt`). Luke Iha is visceral and Germanic-rooted on
  purpose — he explicitly teaches "sweat" over "perspire" because the Germanic word "just has
  this earthy, raw kind of feel to it" (`luke-iha-hooks/transcript.txt`). Lara is
  metrics-first and funnel-literal ("70 to 80% of my own revenue comes from email, not
  LinkedIn directly" — `lara-acosta/2026-linkedin-playbook-transcript.txt`). Grace is
  architectural and metaphor-driven (the transit-system framing, LIKELY). Cardinal Mason's
  layer is sequencing-obsessed — named prompt architecture, not prose voice. A Kingmaker
  Blueprint that reads as one bland voice across all 12 steps of the sprint has failed all
  six inputs at once.
- **The polish-is-the-tell class failure mode here is specific and compounding**: a 30-day
  plan that looks like a consulting deck — executive summary, SWOT-shaped diagnostic,
  generic "content pillars," a founder quote that could belong to anyone — has been run
  through corporate sanitization at every one of the six handoff points. This is the exact
  failure a16z defines itself against: a founder who "would come off stage having
  deliberately said absolutely nothing" (paraphrase of the transcript's vanilla-CEO critique,
  carried forward from the sibling `andreessen-horowitz-new-media/genius.md`, itself
  UNCONFIRMED-in-this-pass per that skill's own provenance trail) is the anti-exemplar this
  entire skill exists to prevent. A hook that could run on any brand's account has failed
  Luke Iha's viciousness test before it failed anyone else's.
- **The recognition test**: would a16z recognize this as their own physics (binary
  commitment, volume-over-defense, the 36-hour decay window) and not generic "post more"
  advice? Would Nicolas Cole recognize this as his own case-study-of-one method (never
  testimonials-led)? Would Luke Iha recognize this as genuinely vicious — "the vicious
  version is always the most specific, most embodied, most uncomfortable, and most charred
  version" (`luke-iha-hooks/transcript.txt`) — not merely punchy? Would Lara Acosta recognize
  this as her own revenue bridge (content → profile → lead magnet → email → sales), sequenced
  in that literal order? If any one of the four VERIFIED experts would shrug and say "this is
  just content marketing," rebuild that layer before shipping the blueprint.

## Signature Moves (Compound Service)

1. **The Binary Commitment Check** — before any Kingmaker Blueprint ships, ask: does any
   section hedge between old-media-safe and new-media-interesting? a16z's "half and half"
   physics says the plan already failed if yes (verified quote above).
2. **The Proof-Fakeability Sort** — before deploying proof per platform, sort every proof
   asset by Luke Iha's ladder (resume/samples at the bottom, hardest-to-fake at the top);
   lead every pitch with the hardest-to-fake asset available, not the easiest to produce.
3. **The Vicious Hook Pass** — score every generated hook against Iha's eight principles;
   any hook that "sounds reasonable" on a first read has already failed his standard.
4. **The City Map Draw** — before tactical content planning, draw Grand Central + 2-3
   Destinations + Lines + Passengers (Grace Andrews, LIKELY-labeled framework).
5. **The Revenue Bridge Sequence** — content → profile → lead magnet → email → sales, in
   that literal order, per Lara Acosta's own naming; skipping the lead magnet step is the
   single most common reason a "voice-perfect, hook-perfect" launch never converts.
6. **The Email Sequence Handoff** — once the launch spike lands new subscribers, route them
   through the Cardinal Mason-derived Email Sequence Executioner pattern (LIKELY) rather than
   improvising a generic welcome series.

## Anti-Patterns — Sourced

- ❌ **Hedging a founder's public position between "safe corporate" and "authentic founder."** a16z: "you can't be half and half... the whole motion of the old world will kill you in the new world" (`marc-andreessen-ben-horowitz/transcript.txt`).
- ❌ **Responding directly to a media attack instead of flooding the zone.** a16z doctrine, verbatim: "when in doubt, flood the zone" (`marc-andreessen-ben-horowitz/transcript.txt`).
- ❌ **Writing a hook that "sounds reasonable" or opens with three to four sentences of throat-clearing before the point.** Luke Iha: "most people start their ad three to four sentences in... a vicious hook goes right for the gut" (`luke-iha-hooks/transcript.txt`).
- ❌ **Leading proof deployment with the easiest-to-fake asset (a resume or a portfolio sample) instead of the hardest-to-fake tier.** Luke Iha's proof ladder ranks these explicitly bottom-to-top by fakeability (`luke-iha/transcript.txt`).
- ❌ **Leading a client pitch with a portfolio or testimonial instead of a free custom sample.** Cole ran 300+ ghostwriting clients on "Zero. actually zero." testimonials (`nicolas-cole-ghostwriting-v2/transcript.txt`).
- ❌ **Selling directly from LinkedIn posts instead of building the revenue bridge.** Lara Acosta: "LinkedIn is a relationship platform. People buy from people they know, like, and trust" (`lara-acosta/2026-linkedin-playbook-transcript.txt`).
- ❌ **Cross-posting identical content across platforms instead of native-designing per culture mode.** Verified in the sibling `skills/andreessen-horowitz-new-media/genius.md` "Platform-Native Obsession" pattern, imported here per this skill's own `SKILL.md` framework table.
- ❌ **Treating the Grace Andrews city-map layer or the Cardinal Mason email-sequence layer as verified doctrine rather than a LIKELY synthesis.** No raw transcript exists for either — see `references/source-ledger.md`, repair pass dated 2026-07-18.

## Expert-Specific Quality Rubric

| Criterion | Score 4 (Acceptable) | Score 7 (Good) | Score 10 (Savant) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Media Physics (a16z) | Founder posts consistently on 1-2 platforms | Long-form-first pipeline established, crisis plan exists | Founder scores 8+/10 on the Joe Rogan CEO Test; a real attack gets flooded within 36 hours |
| Hook Viciousness (Iha) | Hooks are on-topic but polite | Hooks use charged language and an open loop | A close reader flinches, gasps, or feels personally called out in the first 10-20 words |
| Voice Capture (Cole) | Generic interview, no artifact | Voice document + client's own content analyzed | A colleague can't tell the ghostwriter wrote it |
| City Architecture (Grace, LIKELY) | Content calendar with topics | City Map drawn (Grand Central + destinations + lines) | Full trust-pathway-tagged content with measured stage transitions |
| Revenue Bridge (Lara) | Posts get engagement, no funnel | Lead magnet exists somewhere in the profile | 70%+ of tracked revenue traceable to content → profile → lead magnet → email → sales |
