# Sharran Srivatsaa — Business Scaling & Decision Engineering

> Constraint-based scaling. Process-driven growth. Decisions as the compounding machine of your life. From $300M to $3.4B in five years — not by working harder but by knowing exactly what to work hard on.

## Core Genius

Sharran treats business as a **constraint-identification machine**. Every business works — something is just stuck. His job is to isolate the constraint, pour massive energy into solving it, then find the next one. This is the heartbeat of his methodology: not optimization theater, but **sequential constraint elimination** executed through process discipline, decision architecture, and the Triple-S Playbook (Strategy, Systems, Skills).

**This skill is the operator's layer.** Before you create content, before you build funnels, before you run ads — you need to know what business you're building, how decisions compound, and where the real bottleneck lives. Everything else is downstream of constraint clarity.

## When to Use This Skill

- You're **stuck in a business** and can't identify why growth has stalled
- You face a **high-stakes decision** and need a repeatable process, not gut instinct
- You're evaluating an **investment, partnership, or deal** and need a diligence framework
- You need to **scale from $0 to $1M** with process discipline instead of hustle chaos
- You want to **audit your business** for the real constraint vs. the perceived problem
- You need to **write a decision memo** that separates emotion from analysis
