# 10X Growth Diagnostic

> Load `skills/sharran-srivatsaa-scaling/genius.md` before executing.

## Inputs Required
- **Business overview** (What it does, when founded, team size)
- **Revenue trajectory** (Current revenue, 12-month trend, target)
- **Operational snapshot** (Key metrics: leads, close rate, churn, capacity, profit margin)
- **CEO's time allocation** (Where do they spend 80% of their hours?)
- **Optional**: Prior coaching/consulting engagements and their outcomes

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: This diagnostic is for businesses aiming to scale — not side hustles or pre-revenue ideas. If pre-revenue, route to `/constraint-audit` for initial constraint identification first.

## The Process

### Step 1 — Phase Identification

Every business passes through four phases. Each has different constraints, different leverage points, and different failure modes.

| Phase | Revenue Range | Primary Job | Typical Constraint |
|-------|--------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **STANDARDIZE** | $0 – $250K | Get the machine working once | Can't describe the business model on a napkin. No repeatable process. |
| **OPTIMIZE** | $250K – $1M | Make the machine efficient | CEO trapped in fulfillment. No systems. Tribal knowledge dependency. |
| **GROW** | $1M – $5M | Add fuel to the working machine | People constraint. Can't hire fast enough or well enough. Leaders missing. |
| **SCALE** | $5M+ | Multiply the machine | Strategy constraint. Need new channels, markets, or product lines. CEO becomes bottleneck as chief decision-maker. |

**Phase Diagnosis Questions:**

**Are you in STANDARDIZE?**
- [ ] Have fewer than 3 customers/clients
- [ ] Can't explain the business model to a stranger in 60 seconds
- [ ] Revenue is unpredictable month to month
- [ ] No documented process for delivering the product/service

**Are you in OPTIMIZE?**
- [ ] Revenue is growing but the CEO does most of the work
- [ ] If the CEO took 2 weeks off, revenue would drop
- [ ] There's a working model but no documented systems
- [ ] Hiring feels "too expensive" but the CEO is drowning

**Are you in GROW?**
- [ ] Systems exist but depend on specific people
- [ ] Revenue growth has plateaued despite effort
- [ ] The team needs mid-level leaders but can't find or afford them
- [ ] Multiple revenue streams create complexity without proportional returns

**Are you in SCALE?**
- [ ] The business runs without CEO daily involvement
- [ ] Growth requires new markets, channels, or products — not more of the same
- [ ] The constraint is strategic — knowing WHAT to build next
- [ ] Decision-making is the CEO's primary bottleneck

### Step 2 — Phase-Specific Constraint Map

Based on the phase identified, map the constraints:

**STANDARDIZE Constraints:**
| Constraint | Diagnostic Question | Action if Present |
|-----------|--------------------|--------------------|
| No repeatable offer | Can you deliver the same thing to 10 consecutive customers? | Standardize the deliverable |
| No pricing clarity | Do you know your cost per delivery + target margin? | Build the simple spreadsheet |
| No customer acquisition process | Can you generate 5 leads this week on purpose? | Build one acquisition channel |
| No artifacts | Is anything written down? | Write the first artifact of success |

**OPTIMIZE Constraints:**
| Constraint | Diagnostic Question | Action if Present |
|-----------|--------------------|--------------------|
| CEO in fulfillment | Does the CEO deliver the product/service? | Hire/automate delivery |
| No SOPs | If the CEO disappeared, could someone replicate the process? | Document the top 3 processes |
| Revenue ceiling | Is revenue flat because the CEO is at capacity? | Calculate: cost of first hire vs. CEO hours freed |
| No tracking | Do you know your numbers with precision, or are you "estimating"? | Build the dashboard |

**GROW Constraints:**
| Constraint | Diagnostic Question | Action if Present |
|-----------|--------------------|--------------------|
| People constraint | What's the ONE hire that would unlock the most growth? | Make that hire |
| Leadership vacuum | Are there people who can make decisions without the CEO? | Develop or recruit mid-level leaders |
| Channel dependency | Does >80% of revenue come from one channel? | Build a second acquisition channel |
| Complexity drag | Do multiple revenue streams create more work than revenue? | Kill the underperformers |

**SCALE Constraints:**
| Constraint | Diagnostic Question | Action if Present |
|-----------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Strategy ambiguity | Does the team know what to build next? | Run `/decision-map` on the top strategic choice |
| Decision bottleneck | Do all important decisions route through the CEO? | Build the Decision Mapping process for the team |
| Daisy chain failure | Has health/relationships/personal stability been sacrificed for growth? | Address the weakest life domain |
| Market expansion | Has the current market been saturated? | Research adjacent markets or verticals |

### Step 4 — Growth Architecture

Produce the 10X growth plan:

**Current Phase**: [STANDARDIZE / OPTIMIZE / GROW / SCALE]

**The Constraint** (one sentence):
> [Specific, measurable bottleneck]

**Triple-S Layer**: [Strategy / Systems / Skills]

**90-Day Focus:**
| Priority | Action | Expected Result | Measurement |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------------|
| 1 | [The constraint-solving action] | [What changes] | [How we'll know] |
| 2 | [Supporting action] | [What it enables] | [Metric] |
| 3 | [Preparation for next phase] | [What it sets up] | [Milestone] |

**The "Stop" List:**
- [Activity that feels productive but doesn't address the constraint]
- [Energy leak to eliminate]
- [Project to kill or pause]

**Next Phase Preview:**
When the current constraint is solved, the business will enter [next phase], where the likely constraint will be [prediction].

## Quality Gate
- [ ] Phase correctly identified with diagnostic evidence (not assumption)
- [ ] Phase-specific constraint map completed for the identified phase
- [ ] Constraint stated in one sentence with measurement where possible
- [ ] 37 Lessons scan completed (at least top 10 lessons checked)
- [ ] 90-day plan has specific actions, expected results, AND measurements
- [ ] "Stop" list names at least 2 things to eliminate
- [ ] Next phase predicted with likely constraint

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: Most businesses self-identify one phase too high. A business that "feels like GROW" but has no SOPs is actually in OPTIMIZE. Be honest about the phase — the actions are completely different.
