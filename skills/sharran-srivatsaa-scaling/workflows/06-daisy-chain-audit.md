# Daisy Chain Audit

> Load `skills/sharran-srivatsaa-scaling/genius.md` before executing.

## Inputs Required
- **Current business situation** (Revenue, growth rate, hours worked per week)
- **Honest self-assessment** across life domains (see framework below)
- **Recent sacrifices** (What have you given up in the last 90 days to work on the business?)
- **Optional**: Feedback from partner/family/close friend on perceived balance

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: This audit triggers when any of these signals appear:
> - Working 60+ hours/week consistently
> - Health metrics declining (sleep, exercise, stress)
> - Key relationships showing strain
> - Burnout symptoms (cynicism, exhaustion, reduced effectiveness)
> - Business growing but founder is miserable
> - "I'll rest when we hit [milestone]" language

## The Concept

Sharran's **Daisy Chain Principle**: Life domains are linked like a daisy chain — pull one, and the rest follow. You can't 10X your business while your health, relationships, spiritual life, or personal development collapse. The chain breaks at the weakest link, and the weakest link ALWAYS becomes the constraint on business growth eventually.

The opposite is also true: strengthening the weakest domain often unlocks business growth that no amount of business tactics could achieve.

## The Process

### Step 1 — Domain Scoring

Rate each domain honestly (1-10). Then identify the TREND (improving, stable, declining):

| Domain | Score (1-10) | Trend | Evidence |
|--------|-------------|-------|----------|
| **HEALTH** (sleep, energy, exercise, nutrition, stress) | | ↑ → ↓ | |
| **RELATIONSHIPS** (partner, family, friendships, social life) | | ↑ → ↓ | |
| **BUSINESS** (revenue, growth, team, impact) | | ↑ → ↓ | |
| **PERSONAL GROWTH** (learning, skills, hobbies, creativity) | | ↑ → ↓ | |
| **SPIRITUAL/PURPOSE** (meaning, alignment, values, inner peace) | | ↑ → ↓ | |

**Chain Health**: Sum of all scores = ___/50

| Score | Diagnosis |
|-------|-----------|
| 40-50 | **Thriving**: Sustainable pace. Optimize, don't overhaul. |
| 30-39 | **Functional**: Working but with cracks. Address the weakest domain. |
| 20-29 | **Strained**: One domain is dragging the chain. Urgent attention needed. |
| Below 20 | **Breaking**: Multiple domains failing. Business growth will stall regardless of tactics. |

### Step 2 — Weakest Link Identification

The weakest scored domain with a declining trend is the constraint on everything — including business growth.

**The Weakest Link:**
> Domain: [___] | Score: [___] | Trend: [↓]

**How It's Constraining Business:**
Every weak domain has a specific mechanism by which it drags business performance:

| Weak Domain | Business Impact Mechanism |
|-------------|-------------------------|
| Health declining | Decision quality drops. Energy for sales/creativity depleted. Reactive instead of strategic. |
| Relationships strained | Emotional bandwidth consumed. Guilt drives distraction. Support system erodes. |
| Personal Growth stalled | Skills plateau. Content/ideas become stale. Imposter syndrome intensifies. |
| Spiritual/Purpose lost | Motivation becomes extrinsic only. Burnout inevitable. Can't inspire team. |

### Step 3 — The 10X Asymmetry Check

Sharran's insight: "You can't just 10X the business. EVERY domain has to level up."

| Domain | Current Level | What 10X Business REQUIRES of This Domain | Gap |
|--------|--------------|-------------------------------------------|-----|
| Health | | (10X business demands more energy, not less) | |

Identify what the business is TAKING from other domains, and set boundaries:

## Quality Gate
- [ ] All 5 domains scored with specific evidence (not aspirational guesses)
- [ ] Trends identified for each domain (not just current state)
- [ ] Weakest link identified with explicit business impact mechanism
- [ ] 10X Asymmetry Check completed (what does growth DEMAND of each domain?)
- [ ] Daily non-negotiable is <30 minutes and embarrassingly simple
- [ ] Protection boundaries are specific (times, days) not vague ("more balance")
- [ ] 90-day milestone is measurable

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: The most common failure mode is treating this as a "nice to have" and skipping back to business tactics. Sharran's 37 Lessons are clear: the daisy chain IS the business strategy. A broken chain = a broken engine, regardless of how good the tactics are.
