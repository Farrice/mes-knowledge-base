# Decision Mapping Engine

> Load `skills/sharran-srivatsaa-scaling/genius.md` before executing.

## Inputs Required
- **The decision** (What are you trying to decide?)
- **Stakeholders** (Who is affected? Who has input?)
- **Timeline** (When must the decision be made?)
- **Optional**: Prior decisions in the same domain (for pattern recognition)

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: Confirm the decision is worth structured analysis. Trivial/reversible decisions don't need this framework. Threshold: >$5K impact OR >30 days of trajectory shift.

## The Process

### Step 1 — Understand the Context

Do NOT solve. Do NOT prescribe. Zoom out.

Ask EVERY clarifying question, even if you believe you know the answer:
- Why are we talking about this right now? What triggered it?
- What are the 3 things that happened that make this important TODAY?
- Who is involved and what are their positions?
- What don't we know yet?
- What assumptions are being made that haven't been verified?

Run the 5W diagnostic:
| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Why** are we facing this decision? | |
| **What** exactly is at stake? | |
| **Who** is affected / has input? | |
| **Where** does this sit in the business? | |
| **When** is the decision deadline? | |

**Einstein Check**: "If I had an hour to solve this problem, I'd spend 55 minutes thinking about the problem." Have we spent enough time on context, or are we jumping to solutions?

### Step 2 — Isolate the Issue

Write the problem in **one sentence**. If you can't, you haven't isolated it.

Diagnostic questions:
- Is this the REAL constraint, or a symptom of a deeper constraint?
- If we solved this, would the business actually move? Or would we discover the real problem is elsewhere?
- What is really holding us back?
- Is this a Strategy failure, a Systems failure, or a Skills failure? (Triple-S diagnosis)

**The One-Sentence Constraint Test:**
> "[Insert one sentence that names the specific bottleneck with zero buzzwords]"

❌ Bad: "We need to improve our marketing."
❌ Bad: "Our growth is stagnating."
✅ Good: "We lose 73% of booked demos because we have no 48-hour follow-up sequence."
✅ Good: "We can't hire fast enough because our job posts attract unqualified candidates and we have no screening filter."

### Step 3 — Accept the Risk

Every decision is a trade-off. Name what you're cutting off.

| If we say YES to... | We say NO to... | Risk of that NO |
|---------------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| [Option A] | [What it displaces] | [Consequence] |
| [Option B] | [What it displaces] | [Consequence] |

**Inversion Audit** (Charlie Munger):
- What guarantees this decision FAILS?
- Are any of those conditions currently present?
- What is the worst-case scenario, and can we survive it?

**The Trade-Off Declaration:**
> "I am accepting the risk that [specific consequence] because the upside of [specific benefit] outweighs it."

### Step 4 — Map the Decision

A decision without action is just a thought. Close with:

**Decision Statement:**
> "We have decided to [specific action] because [one-sentence rationale]."

**Next Steps:**

| What | Who | By When |
|------|-----|---------|
| [Action item 1] | [Owner] | [Date] |
| [Action item 2] | [Owner] | [Date] |
| [Action item 3] | [Owner] | [Date] |

**Review Checkpoint:** When will we evaluate if this decision worked? [Date + success criteria]

## Output Format

### Decision Memo: [Decision Title]

**Decision Date**: [date] | **Decision Maker**: [name] | **Impact Level**: [High/Critical]

**CONTEXT**
[2-3 paragraph summary of the situation, including all 5W answers]

**THE CONSTRAINT** (one sentence)
> [The isolated issue]

**RISKS ACCEPTED**
[Trade-off table + Inversion Audit results]

**THE DECISION**
> [Decision statement with rationale]

**NEXT STEPS**
[Action table with owners and dates]

**REVIEW DATE**: [Date] | **Success Criteria**: [Measurable outcome]

---

## Quality Gate
- [ ] Context section answers all 5W questions without assumptions
- [ ] Constraint is stated in one sentence with zero buzzwords
- [ ] At least 2 trade-offs named with specific consequences
- [ ] Inversion audit completed (failure conditions checked)
- [ ] Decision statement is specific and actionable (not "we'll think about it")
- [ ] Next steps have owners AND deadlines
- [ ] Review date and success criteria defined

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: If the constraint reads like a wish ("improve marketing," "grow faster"), restart Step 2. If no trade-offs are named, the decision hasn't been made — restart Step 3.
