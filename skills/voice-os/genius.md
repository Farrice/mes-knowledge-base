## How to Use This Skill (Model Calibration)

These are intuition primitives, not a checklist. Absorb them, then write — never narrate them.
If the output announces "here's the confession pivot" or visibly checks off Identity Spine
anchors in order, you have failed the assignment before a single sentence lands.

The recognition test: would Farrice read this and feel it's HIS voice engine, not a generic
brand-voice tool? If it reads like it could be re-skinned for any other confessional founder by
swapping the anecdotes, it is wearing Farrice's vocabulary without earning his rhythm — rebuild.

Specifically:
- Do NOT label sections "confession pivot here" or "showing the image that names the thing" —
  execute the move, never announce it.
- Do NOT quote VOICE-CARD.md or the calibration bank back at a reader, or narrate "per my voice
  guidelines" inside delivered text. The card is grounding to absorb, never a script to cite.
- His texture is generated long, then cut for punch — not built short from the first draft. The
  measured stylometric fingerprint (§3 below) is a *tightening-pass* signature, not a first-draft
  one. Writing short-choppy from the start produces the wrong kind of short.
- **Polish is the tell-class here, inverted from most voices**: Farrice's own approved corpus
  runs 2-4 em dashes per ~1,000-1,200 word edition before final audit (VOICE-CARD.md §3), never
  zero from the first pass — a draft that arrives too clean, too metronomic, too evenly punctuated
  reads as performed, not lived. The wince test (VOICE-CARD.md §2) exists precisely because a
  technically flawless draft can still fail: "picture a close friend who'd notice if he were
  performing instead of writing. If they'd wince, it's wrong."
- The bimodal taste signature (`user_taste-calibration-signature.md`, folded into VOICE-CARD.md
  §2) means there is no safe "pretty good" zone — a 7-8/10 draft is *more* suspicious to Farrice
  than an obvious miss, because "almost there" usually means the confession pivot got
  intellectualized instead of felt. Aim for a clean pass, expect a clean fail on a swing, and
  don't smooth a draft into the mushy middle to avoid risk.

## The Core Engine: Two-Stage Rhythm — Generate Long, Then Cut for Punch

The measured stylometrics in VOICE-CARD.md §3 are not a style to imitate directly; they are the
*output* of a two-stage process. Across three directly word/sentence-counted pieces — Ed 01
Manifesto (129 sentences, mean 10.4 words, stdev 8.4), Ed 02 Coachella (107 sentences, mean 11.0
words, stdev 8.3), and the unpolished Ed 04 JJ draft (76 sentences, mean 13.9 words, stdev
9.7) — the *unpolished* draft runs longer and less punchy than the two *tightened* editions. The
finding, stated directly in VOICE-CARD.md §3: "the tightening pass, not the raw draft, is where
the short-sentence density gets dialed in." Roughly a third of every polished piece (33% in Ed
01, 30% in Ed 02) is one-to-five-word sentences functioning as punches — "I nodded. I paid him. I
tried." — landing after 15-45-word pressure-building sentences, never as the starting register.

**In practice**: draft first without forcing staccato rhythm, then cut. A draft that opens
choppy and stays choppy is imitating the surface pattern without the pressure that makes it read
as earned compression rather than a stylistic tic.

## Signature Move: The Confession Pivot

An unexpected private detail dropped mid-paragraph, not signposted. Real example from the
system's own production corpus: *"I have a son. He's two. I'm building this in his naptime."*
This is the move behind the highest-scored 2026-07-16 rewrite in `calibration-log.md` — a draft
that opened "For eighteen years I was the best trainer in every gym I worked at. You've never
heard of me." earned a direct Farrice verdict — "I love the direction of this flip! It really
works really well" — specifically because the credential (eighteen years) gets punctured in the
same breath by the confession (you've never heard of me), rather than sitting there as a status
claim. The workflow's own audit note on this line: "the undercut is the engine of the whole
post; softening it would kill the contradiction gap" (`skills/kallaway-content-psychology/
workflows/batched-content-testing.md` house-style aside — the same undercut logic is native to
Farrice's own confession-pivot pattern, confirmed independently in `deliverables/linkedin/
2026-07-16-jenny-transfer-post-v2-i-narrative.md` line 11).

## Voice Law: The Wince Test Beats the Slop Detector

`prose_classifier.py` catches phrase-level tells (5+ signals caps Expert Standard at 6 per the
root Quality Gate). It does not catch everything. VOICE-CARD.md §6 documents a FAIL line that a
generic checker would likely pass clean: "taking the thing that makes an expert dangerous in a
room and making it visible to strangers who've never been in that room" — Farrice's own verdict,
recorded 2026-04-25: "I don't know anyone who talks like that." No banned phrase triggered; it
still failed, because forced jargon that sounds smart and means nothing (§5 item 9) is a
*register* failure, not a lexical one. Two checks run in sequence before any MIRROR/BLEND draft
ships (`skills/voice-os/references/prompts-v2/post-draft-voice-verification-pass.md`): the
deterministic classifier, then a manual read of the draft's strongest lines against VOICE-CARD.md
§6's PASS and FAIL columns — the second catches what the first structurally cannot.

## Calibration Behavior: The Bimodal Grader

Per `user_taste-calibration-signature.md`, folded into VOICE-CARD.md §2: Farrice's felt scoring
is bimodal — clear PASS or clear FAIL, a narrow marginal band. A 7.0-8.0 "almost there" score is
"actively suspicious to him." On genuine failures he runs roughly one point harsher per dimension
than auto-seeded scores; on genuine wins his gut matches the score. A concrete instance:
`calibration-log.md`'s 2026-05-05 entry logs Variant C of the JJ-manipulation post at a scored
2/10 — a harsh, non-marginal fail, confirmed in the log as "harsh-fail confirmed bimodal taste
signature" — because the draft "traded personal vulnerability for detached marketing-craft
commentary" (it became a lecture about marketers instead of a reflection on his own experience).
Structure without heartbeat caps at 5/10 regardless of how clean the sentences are.

## Channel Register: The Same Fingerprint, Different Finish

Real numbers, not estimates. A Substack/Parallax edition runs 1,000-1,300 words with `---` scene
breaks (never headers) and 2-4 em dashes measured pre-audit (target zero). A LinkedIn post caps
at 3,000 characters with only 2-3 lines visible before "see more," a documented 52% conversion
penalty on question-hooks versus statement-hooks (`NOTES_TRAILER_PLAYBOOK.md`, cited in
VOICE-CARD.md §4), and CTAs that land in the first comment, never in-body. A Substack Note has a
hard 500-character database limit, a practical target of 32-63 words / 180-380 characters, and a
documented zero-em-dash target (max 1) — the tightest register in the whole system. Per
`PLATFORM-NARRATIVE-CARD.md` §2, X/Twitter is the one surface that **inverts** the whole engine:
info-first, numbered, no I-narrative arc at all, because "long personal narrative is a liability
unless they already know you" (flagged LIKELY confidence, not yet locked against shipped
results).

## Identity Floor: Privacy Law and the Practitioner Test

Two hard constraints that never lift, in any dial mode (VOICE-CARD.md §1, §7): family specifics —
father, mother, brother — are Farrice's to tell and are explicitly **not cleared**; public work
may dramatize the *pattern* (suppression, dimming, fear of being seen at full size) but never
narrate the family autobiography itself. Second, the Practitioner Test (POV anchor #6): "actively
doing it → share it. Not doing it → don't post it." Combined with POV anchor #8 — "never
manufacture urgency that doesn't exist" — these two rules are why Farrice's proof claims run on
specific, checkable numbers (18 years, 1,000+ client transformations, $14,000 in courses) rather
than aspirational framing.

## Anti-Patterns

- **"Here's what/why/how..." openers / "Here's the thing"**: VOICE-CARD.md §5 item 1, cross-
  referenced against `directives/ai-slop-detector.md` — the single most recognizable AI tell on
  social; Farrice's writing states the insight, it never announces it's coming.
- **"It's not X. It's Y." negation-reveal**: VOICE-CARD.md §5 item 2 — documented as appearing
  "15+ times in rejected Parallax drafts," the single most reliable structural tell across this
  system's own drafting failures.
- **More than 2 em dashes per piece**: VOICE-CARD.md §3, measured directly — 2 em dashes in Ed 01
  (~1,050 words) and 4 in Ed 02 (~1,180 words), both flagged for a pre-publish audit pass toward
  zero. Confirmed independently in `directives/ai-slop-ban-bank.md` line 133: "em-dash count > 0
  (Farrice tell; default ZERO, max 1)."
- **Cheap question signoffs** ("What's your version?" / "Who was it actually for?"): locked as a
  system-wide ban on 2026-05-05 per `calibration-log.md` — "Asking questions is good, but it
  feels cheap now" — optimizes for comment-count over recognition. Confirmed in
  `deliverables/linkedin/2026-07-16-jenny-transfer-post-v2-i-narrative.md` line 59 as still
  actively checked against on 2026-07-16 drafts.
- **Detached marketing-craft commentary standing in for personal reflection**: `calibration-
  log.md`, 2026-05-05, Variant C of the JJ-manipulation post ("If I talked to JJ like marketers
  talk to ICPs...") scored 2/10 — "traded personal vulnerability for detached marketing-craft
  commentary; became a lecture about marketers instead of a reflection on what he was doing/
  feeling."
- **Whole-post "you"-framed dictation register**: `calibration-log.md`, 2026-07-16 FAIL entry on
  a ring-light LinkedIn draft ("Watch what happens the next time you sit down to fix it...") —
  Farrice's own words: "people don't like being yelled at or dictated to anymore," logged
  alongside `feedback_i-narrative-hero-guide-framing.md` as the rule-change that retired
  you-dictation posts in favor of I-narrative arcs.
- **Under-bridged payoff transitions**: `calibration-log.md`, 2026-07-17 FAIL entry — "So I
  stopped typing and started talking. A client talked to me about her work for forty minutes. I
  turned twenty seconds of it into a post." Farrice's verdict: the transition and payoff "feel
  disjointed, abrupt," because the twenty-second receipt needed to be defined and bridged before
  it lands, not just juxtaposed against the setup.

## Recognition Test

The recognition test for anything produced under this skill: would Farrice read this and feel
it's HIS voice engine, not a generic brand-voice tool — the way he'd distinguish a line that's
merely using his vocabulary (anime references, dollar figures, a confession dropped in) from a
line that earns his actual rhythm (long build, short cut, the image anchored to a specific scene,
never a portable maxim manufactured to be quotable)? A draft that hits the surface markers but
skips the two-stage rhythm, or states a POV anchor instead of dramatizing it, is wearing Farrice's
vocabulary without recognizing what makes it his.
