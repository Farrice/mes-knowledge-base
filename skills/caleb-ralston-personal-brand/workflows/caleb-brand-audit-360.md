# 360° Brand Diagnostic — Complete Brand Health Audit

> Audit against all 24 Caleb Ralston patterns, score the full quality rubric, diagnose what's broken, and route to the exact fix workflow.

---

## When to Use

- Something feels "off" with your brand but you can't articulate what
- Engagement is high but revenue is flat (or vice versa)
- You haven't done a brand health check in 90+ days
- You're getting views but not the RIGHT audience
- You feel "guru-glaze" creeping in or burnout approaching
- Before any major pivot or relaunch

## What You'll Produce

1. **Pattern Coverage Scorecard** — how well your brand executes each of the 24 patterns
2. **Quality Rubric Score** — scored against the 9-dimension quality rubric (4/7/10 scale)
3. **Weakness Diagnosis** — top 3-5 areas bleeding trust, attention, or revenue
4. **Fix Route Map** — exact workflow to deploy for each weakness
5. **30-Day Recovery Calendar** — prioritized actions to address the most critical gaps

### Phase 3: Weakness Diagnosis

From the pattern audit and quality rubric, identify the **top 3-5 weaknesses** ranked by impact.

**Impact ranking logic:**
1. **Revenue-blocking weaknesses first** — if DMs are zero, fixing intros doesn't matter
2. **Trust-eroding weaknesses second** — if content feels inauthentic, volume makes it worse
3. **Growth-limiting weaknesses third** — if format is stale, new eyeballs won't stick

For each weakness:

```
### Weakness #[N]: [Name]
- **Symptoms**: [What you're seeing]
- **Root Cause**: [Which pattern(s) are being violated]
- **Impact**: [Revenue / Trust / Growth]
- **Severity**: [Critical / High / Medium]
- **Fix Route**: [Exact workflow to deploy]
```

### Phase 4: Fix Route Map

Map each weakness to its treatment:

| Weakness | Fix Workflow | Command | Priority |
|----------|-------------|---------|----------|
| Trust foundation weak | `authority-foundation-blueprint` | `/caleb-brand-build` Phase 2 | Critical |
| Content misaligned with customer pain | `trust-based-content-engine` | `/caleb-brand-build` Phase 4 | High |
| AI-sounding content | Pluribus rewrite sessions | Manual rewrite pass | High |
| Platform confusion | `content-format-strategy-engine` | `/caleb-format-strategy` | Medium |
| Stale packaging | `wrapping-paper-library-builder` | `/wrapping-paper-library` | Medium |
| Weak video intros | `4c-intro-content-architect` | `/caleb-4c-intro` | Medium |
| No revenue path | `growth-revenue-activation` | `/caleb-brand-build` Phase 6 | Critical |
| Inauthenticity debt | Authenticity reset protocol (Pattern 16) | Manual | High |

### Phase 5: 30-Day Recovery Calendar

Build a recovery plan that addresses weaknesses in priority order:

**Week 1: Stop the Bleeding**
- Fix the most critical weakness (typically trust or revenue)
- Immediate content adjustments (no new production, fix existing pipeline)
- Run quick Pluribus pass on next 5 scheduled pieces

**Week 2: Rebuild Foundation**
- Deploy the highest-priority fix workflow end-to-end
- Refresh customer pain map if content is misaligned
- Update wrapping paper library if packaging is stale

**Week 3: Implement New Systems**
- Put the fix into your weekly content sprint
- Test new approaches via accordion (short-form first)
- Track lead indicators: DMs, saves, watch time

**Week 4: Validate & Lock In**
- Review metrics from Week 3 changes
- If improving → lock in as permanent process change
- If not → diagnose deeper (did we fix symptoms not causes?)
- Schedule next audit for 90 days out

## Output Contract

A complete **360° Brand Health Report** containing exactly these five artifacts, in this order:
1. **Pattern Coverage Scorecard**: All 24 patterns scored 1-10 with evidence, grouped by the 7 sections above (Trust Foundation, Content Strategy, AI Authenticity, Platform Intelligence, Packaging Quality, Intro Effectiveness, Growth Health) — every score needs a specific evidence citation (which content piece, what it showed), not a bare number.
2. **Quality Rubric Average**: 5 recent pieces scored across all 9 rubric dimensions from genius.md, averaged.
3. **Top 3-5 Weaknesses**: Each in the `### Weakness #[N]` block format (Symptoms / Root Cause / Impact / Severity / Fix Route) — ranked Revenue-blocking > Trust-eroding > Growth-limiting, never listed in discovery order.
4. **Fix Route Map**: Each weakness mapped to exactly one deployable workflow or manual protocol, never a vague "work on this."
5. **30-Day Recovery Calendar**: Populated week-by-week per the Phase 5 template — no placeholder weeks.

Use the `## Output: Brand Health Report` template below as the literal document skeleton.
