# The Wrapping Paper Library Builder

> Systematic packaging inspiration collection — build a cross-niche library of hooks, formats, and structures you can wrap your expertise in.

---

## When to Use

- You're creating content but your packaging (hooks, thumbnails, structures) looks like everyone else in your niche
- You want to find fresh formats without copying competitors
- You need a repeatable system for spotting outlier packaging across platforms
- You're spending time scrolling but not capturing or organizing what you find

## What You'll Produce

1. **Platform Collection System** — save folders, screenshot albums, and Notion boards organized by hook formats vs content formats
2. **Cross-Niche Source Map** — 10+ niches outside yours to mine for packaging inspiration
3. **Outlier Detection Criteria** — your specific threshold for what's worth saving (2.5x+ above channel average)
4. **Wrapping Paper Catalog** — 30+ saved formats organized by type (hooks, structures, packaging) with source and outlier score
5. **Weekly Scroll Protocol** — 1-hour weekly session structure for intentional inspiration mining

---

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Gift Clarity (Your Expertise Content)

Before collecting wrapping paper, clarify what you're wrapping.

**Inputs needed from user:**
- Niche / subject matter
- Top 5 painful problems your customers face (from Caleb's Customer Pain Mapping)
- Your unique solution for each problem
- Current platforms (YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, etc.)

**Output:** A "Gift List" — 10-15 content concepts (problem + unique solution pairs) ready to be wrapped.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The gift is your expertise. The wrapping paper is how you deliver it. Never confuse these. You choose wrapping paper AFTER you know the gift.

### Phase 2: Platform Setup

For each active platform, create the collection infrastructure:

**Instagram:**
- Create a saved folder called "Wraps" (or user's preferred name)
- Split into sub-collections: "Hook Formats" and "Content Formats"
- Reset algorithm or create burner account for cross-niche discovery
  - Method 1: Use Instagram's built-in algorithm reset
  - Method 2: Go to Explore → tap into non-niche content → let it rewire
  - Method 3: Separate phone/account for pure discovery

**YouTube:**
- Install 1of10 Chrome extension for outlier detection
- Set outlier threshold (recommend 2.5x-5x above channel average)
- Create a playlist or Notion board: "YouTube Wraps"
- Split into: "Packaging Formats" (title/thumbnail) and "Content Formats" (video structure)

**LinkedIn:**
- Create a screenshot album (phone) or Notion board
- Save full post text + engagement metrics
- Focus on: hook structures, formatting patterns, carousel structures

**Cross-Platform Notion Board (recommended):**
```
| Source | Platform | Type | Outlier Score | Niche | Screenshot/Link | Notes |
```

### Phase 3: Cross-Niche Source Mapping

Identify 10+ niches OUTSIDE yours to mine. The more unrelated, the better.

**Source Niche Selection Criteria:**
- Zero overlap with your topic
- High production quality in content
- Active format innovation
- Strong visual/packaging culture

**Default niche suggestions (customize to user):**
1. Gaming/esports channels
2. Cooking/food content
3. Travel vloggers
4. Tech review channels
5. Fitness transformation content
6. Music production tutorials
7. Camping/outdoor adventure
8. Interior design/home renovation
9. Automotive enthusiasts
10. True crime/mystery storytelling
11. Science explainer channels
12. Fashion/style content

**For each niche, identify 3-5 creators who consistently produce outlier packaging.**

### Phase 4: Collection Sprint (Initial Build)

Run a focused 2-hour sprint to seed the library with 30+ items.

**For each item saved, note:**
1. What caught your attention (hook word? visual? structure?)
2. Is this a **hook format** or a **content format**?
   - Hook format: How they opened / grabbed attention
   - Content format: How they structured the information delivery
3. Outlier score (how much did it outperform the creator's average?)
4. Source niche (so you know it's NOT from your niche)
5. Translation potential: Can you see your gift wrapped in this?

**Outlier Detection Method:**
- Go to the creator's page
- Check their last 15 posts
- Calculate rough average engagement
- If the piece you noticed is 2.5x+ above average → save it
- Tools: 1of10 (YouTube), VidIQ, ViewStats (Mr. Beast's tool)

### Phase 5: Translation Protocol

Take 5 of your best saved wrapping papers and translate them to your niche.

**For each translation:**
1. Strip the original topic completely — extract pure skeleton/structure
2. Map your gift (problem + solution) onto the skeleton
3. Write a draft hook/outline using the new wrapping
4. **Litmus test**: Would someone in the original niche recognize this as copied? If yes, strip more. If no, you're good.
5. **Caleb test**: Does this solve my customer's problem? If no, it's wrapping paper with no gift inside — kill it.

### Phase 6: Weekly Scroll Protocol

**Structure a 1-hour weekly session:**

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 0-20 min | Scroll cross-niche feeds (Instagram Explore, YouTube 1of10, LinkedIn) |
| 20-35 min | Save + tag anything that catches attention with outlier score |
| 35-50 min | Review last week's saves — which still feel fresh? Which feel stale? |
| 50-60 min | Translate top 1-2 fresh finds → draft hook or outline for upcoming content |

**Rules:**
- Never scroll when you NEED a video idea — scroll when you DON'T
- Quantity over quality in the save phase — curate later
- If you haven't saved anything in 20 minutes, you're in the wrong niche feeds — reset
- Share the library with your content team (Trevor model: shared Notion board)

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Library has 30+ items minimum
- [ ] Items span 5+ niches outside user's own
- [ ] Each item tagged as hook format OR content format
- [ ] Each item has outlier score (2.5x+ threshold)
- [ ] 5+ translations completed with litmus test passed
- [ ] Weekly scroll protocol scheduled on calendar
- [ ] Collection infrastructure set up on all active platforms

## Caleb Principle

> "We're developing this wrapping paper library. So, when we do have a video that we do know what the problem and solution are, we then look at what wrapper is going to wrap this best to make it most appealing to my audience."

The library is never the starting point. The customer's problem is. The library is how you make the solution irresistible.
