# Micro-Fame Authority Density Companion

## Function

This workflow extends the Kallaway Content OS with one job: prevent audience growth from being mistaken for authority growth.

It uses the source package at `extractions/video-context/1ilMGCxJBQY/`. Creator outcomes and platform claims retain the proof labels in that package. The workflow adopts the source's authority-density tension, positioning lenses, 3-2-1 portfolio, controlled-chaos slot, and four-rep review. It does not adopt a universal score, automatic bucket killing, or a seven-box positioning worksheet.

## Activate When

- follower or view growth may be attracting the wrong audience;
- the user wants micro-fame or category authority rather than general visibility;
- a personal-brand position sounds correct but interchangeable;
- broad and narrow content need different jobs and scorecards;
- a 3-2-1 batch has enough evidence for a keep, modify, kill, or inconclusive decision.

Do not activate for a single script or hook unless the user is asking what strategic job that piece should perform.

## Inputs

- category in which unaided recall matters;
- high-value viewer or buyer;
- offer, next action, or commercial destination;
- creator capabilities, proof, access, constraints, and preferred delivery;
- current content and outcome data, if available;
- competitor or category evidence, if available;
- available production capacity.

If category or buyer is missing, request only that decision. If analytics are missing, continue with a provisional test labeled `UNTESTED`; do not fabricate authority evidence.

## Source Rules

- Load `video-context-ledger.md` and `uncertainty-report.md` before using this source.
- Keep reach, audience fit, trust behavior, and commercial action separate.
- Keep creator-reported results `SELF_REPORTED_UNCONFIRMED` unless independently verified.
- Sandcastles is an optional implementation. Preserve a tool-neutral research path.
- Treat 25K–250K accounts, the 2% engagement filter, and a 50-video batch as source heuristics, not universal requirements.

## Stage 1: Define the authority job

Write one sentence:

> Become the trusted name among [HIGH-VALUE AUDIENCE] for [CONSEQUENTIAL DECISION OR PROBLEM], evidenced by [OBSERVABLE TRUST OR COMMERCIAL BEHAVIOR].

Report available evidence under four headings:

1. **Reach:** who saw it and how widely it traveled.
2. **Fit:** whether the right people entered or engaged.
3. **Trust:** behavior that indicates reliance, not passive attention.
4. **Commercial action:** calls, proposals, deposits, purchases, referrals, or another declared destination.

Do not collapse these into one authority-density number. The tension between them is the diagnosis.

Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/authority-density-diagnostic.md` — honor its Output Contract.

## Stage 2: Find the decisive contrast

The source names seven positioning lenses: topic, substance depth, stories/scenarios, avatar specificity, delivery style, storytelling format, and visual format. Use them as **search lenses**, not fields to complete or score.

### Contrast diagnosis

1. Establish the category's default promise and default execution.
2. Search the seven lenses for credible differences tied to the creator's real capabilities.
3. Name the **controlling contrast**: the one difference most likely to change why the intended buyer chooses or remembers this creator.
4. Run a blind distinction test against three nearby alternatives.
5. If the position is still interchangeable, add one supporting contrast. Test again.
6. Stop once the distinction is clear. Park every unused lens.

The output may contain one contrast, occasionally two, and rarely three. A completed seven-axis grid is a failure unless the user explicitly asks for research notes.

Prefer differences that require judgment, proof, access, accumulated examples, or embodied delivery. Reject differences that can be copied from a screenshot.

Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/positioning-contrast-brief.md` — honor its Output Contract.

## Stage 2.5: Create the authority transfer

For narrow authority or educational content, check whether the draft merely explains the evidence or changes the reader's mental model. When it is only explanatory, use `luke-iha-insight-vectors` as a bounded upstream depth layer.

Work backstage:

1. State the audience's current reasonable model.
2. Find one grounded structural error: a missing bridge, missing variable, hidden condition, false causal link, or mistaken control point.
3. Build one perspective shift from the evidence. Do not invent a mechanism or force novelty.
4. Give the reader a portable decision rule they can apply to the next example without the creator present.
5. Inject that shift into the piece without naming the vector taxonomy or adding a visible worksheet.

Use at most one primary shift in a short piece. If the evidence cannot support a real shift, keep the piece useful and factual rather than manufacturing an aha moment.

**Authority-transfer test:** after reading, can the intended buyer inspect the next claim, campaign, or decision differently? If not, the piece may be credible but it has not yet transferred authority.

## Stage 3: Build the authority portfolio

Choose:

- **one broad acquisition bucket** that recruits category-relevant attention;
- **two narrow authority buckets** tied to high-value buyer decisions, pains, or outcomes;
- **one chaos slot** for a transposed mechanism, a credible new topic, or a third attempt on a winner.

The source cadence is two posts per core bucket plus one chaos post each week. When capacity is lower, preserve the portfolio logic and extend the time window. Do not lower the evidence floor.

Precommit different measures:

- broad bucket: qualified reach, target-account follows, and entry into the intended category;
- narrow buckets: saves, qualified replies, referrals, buyer conversations, proposals, deposits, or attributed action;
- narrow authority quality: whether a reader can repeat and apply the perspective shift to a new decision;
- chaos: whether the experiment earns enough evidence to enter a core-bucket trial.

Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/authority-321-batch-plan.md` — honor its Output Contract.

## Stage 4: Apply the four-rep evidence floor

Four meaningfully different executions per core bucket are the **minimum evidence floor**, not an automatic verdict timer.

After the floor is met:

- `KEEP`: the bucket performs its declared job with credible evidence.
- `MODIFY`: the job is promising but topic, hook, format, or audience fit is suppressing it.
- `KILL`: four or more fair executions fail the precommitted job with no countervailing high-value signal.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: evidence is noisy, confounded, missing, or split across reach and authority signals.

Extend the test when the four reps were not fair or comparable. A factual, claim-risk, privacy, or permission veto can stop an execution before four reps; ordinary underperformance cannot.

The chaos slot must also reach four fair reps before promotion into a core bucket. Early strong signals justify continued testing, not instant promotion.

Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/four-rep-authority-review.md` — honor its Output Contract.

## Stage 5: Transfer the learning

Record:

- authority job;
- controlling contrast and any supporting contrast;
- bucket and job;
- topic, hook, format, story, and visual treatment;
- reach, fit, trust, and commercial action;
- decision and controlling evidence;
- exact change to the next batch.

The run is incomplete until the next batch changes because of the evidence.

## Output Contract

Return the smallest complete set required by the user's stage:

1. authority-density diagnosis;
2. decisive positioning contrast brief;
3. 3-2-1 authority batch plan;
4. four-rep review and next-batch decision.

Do not force all four deliverables when the user only needs one. Every output must expose proof states and the human judgment still required.

## Quality Gate

- Does the work distinguish reach from fit, trust, and commercial action?
- Did the contrast diagnosis surface only the differences that change choice or recall?
- Is every chosen difference backed by a real creator capability?
- Does narrow authority content change the reader's mental model and leave one portable decision rule?
- Do broad and narrow buckets have different jobs and measures?
- Is four reps treated as a floor rather than a mechanical kill rule?
- Does the decision change the next batch?

## Human Checkpoint

Human taste owns the final contrast, acceptable evidence proxies, chaos promotion, and any decision where commercial-quality signals conflict with distribution. External publishing, paid tools, and claim-sensitive content retain their existing approval gates.
