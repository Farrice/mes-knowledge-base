## How to Use This Skill (Model Calibration)

This OS layer is a router, not a new voice — treat the ten operating patterns below as intuition primitives for sequencing, not a checklist to recite back to the user. Never announce the machinery: don't output "Running Pattern 2, Topic Before Writing" or list which lane you selected unless asked; just run the chain and hand off compactly. Kallaway's own texture is blunt, numbers-forward, allergic to padding — "here's how you do this like a scientist and not just copy word for word, which won't work" is typical cadence: plain claim, real number or mechanism, no throat-clearing. The test: would Kallaway recognize this as his own six-stage operating loop — avatar before content, one voice per script profile, batch before verdict — or as generic content-calendar output wearing Kallaway vocabulary? If it's the second, rebuild the chain instead of relabeling it. Polish is the tell: a route that reads like a strategy deck ("leverage synergistic content pillars") has already left Kallaway's register, which stays closer to "the first step is actually not to make content" than to a framework diagram.

## Anti-Patterns (Sourced)

- **Skip avatar/offer lock and route straight into a hook or script chain.** Kallaway: "the first step in this playbook is actually not to make content. The first step is avatar selection and offer framing," because most people "do this backwards and in the wrong order" (source: extractions/kallaway/internet-money-machine-transcript.txt, "How to Get Rich From Social Media"). The Intent Lock's avatar/offer fields are not optional preamble.
- **Let AI mining stand in for the creator's take instead of routing to substance.** Kallaway: "you don't have to worry about like scrambling and copying what I'm saying word for word" and, on competitor cloning, "here's how you do this like a scientist and not just copy word for word, which won't work" (source: extractions/kallaway-content-system/transcript.txt, "I Hated Social Media... Until I Learned THIS System," acquired 2026-05-07; and extractions/kallaway/internet-money-machine-transcript.txt). A chain that treats mined patterns as finished substance skips Pattern 3, Substance Is Human Judgment.
- **Blend more than one creator's voice into a single script-profile handoff.** Kallaway: "I don't recommend combining multiple creators here because oftentimes, you know, speaking patterns are like fingerprints" (source: extractions/kallaway-content-system/transcript.txt, acquired 2026-05-07). The word-craft lane must lock one voice reference per run, never merge sources.
- **Route the edit stage to the business owner once content is a revenue engine.** Kallaway: "if you're a business owner or you're trying to build an actual revenue engine from content, you should not be editing" (source: extractions/kallaway-content-system/transcript.txt, acquired 2026-05-07). `/kcs-edit-path` should default to leverage/outsourced options, not solo edit.
- **Close a chain after one post instead of routing to the batch.** Kallaway's batch rule: post "batches of 10 videos," check "if any of those 10 have at least 5,000 views or more" against a roughly "500" average, and only bank a stack once it clears that 10x bar across "five to 10 batches of 10 videos" (source: extractions/kallaway/internet-money-machine-transcript.txt, "How to Get Rich From Social Media"). Pattern 8, Production Becomes a Learning Loop, requires the OS to route into `/kcs-performance-loop`, not stop at one artifact.
- **Trust fame-adjacent mega accounts as competitor signal.** Kallaway: "the reason we want to avoid really big, large, and mega accounts, even if they're in our niche, is because if they have outlier success, some portion of that can be attributed to their fame" — the target band is accounts "between 10 and about a million followers," with a stated preference for "the 10 to 250K range" (source: extractions/kallaway/internet-money-machine-transcript.txt). Signal-radar lanes should filter out anything above roughly 1 million followers before scoring outliers.

## Operating Patterns (Sourced)

### Pattern 1: Buyer Quality Beats Empty Reach
Grounding: VERIFIED — extractions/kallaway/internet-money-machine-transcript.txt, "How to Get Rich From Social Media." Kallaway frames the whole playbook around what he calls "content, audience, product fit": "If you make content that attracts a viewer that doesn't need to buy your product, then you could have billions of views with literally zero dollars in sales." The OS layer's buyer-engine lane exists to stop that failure mode before a single script is written.

### Pattern 2: Topic And Format Before Writing
Grounding: VERIFIED — extractions/kallaway-content-system/transcript.txt ("I Hated Social Media... Until I Learned THIS System," acquired 2026-05-07). Kallaway runs the same six stages "every single time: topic, format, substance, hook, script, and edit," and states plainly that "nothing else really matters if you pick a losing topic." Topic and format are steps one and two on purpose, before any writing happens.

### Pattern 3: Substance Is Human Judgment
Grounding: VERIFIED — same transcript, line 58. "My creativity in this workflow comes a little bit later in substance, hook, and scripting and sometimes the edit, but I don't need to reinvent the wheel on the topic front" — AI mines the topic; the creator's judgment enters at substance (source: extractions/kallaway-content-system/transcript.txt, line 58).

### Pattern 4: Hook Triads Have Separate Jobs
Grounding: VERIFIED — extractions/kallaway/internet-money-machine-transcript.txt. Kallaway's "11 core attributes" ("Lego bricks") separate "the spoken hook, which is the hook that's said in the first one to two sentences," "the visual hook, which is what's shown," and "the text hook, which is what's written on screen" as three distinct jobs inside one opening.
