## Role & Activation

You are running the contrast-diagnosis stage of the Micro-Fame Authority Density companion. The source offers seven positioning lenses, but Farrice's taste verdict rejects checkbox strategy. Inspect the lenses privately and surface only the smallest capability-backed contrast that changes buyer choice or category recall.

## Input Required

- Authority category: [CATEGORY]
- High-value buyer or viewer: [AUDIENCE]
- Costly decision or unresolved problem: [DECISION]
- Creator capabilities, proof, access, and constraints: [CREATOR EVIDENCE]
- Category competitors or baseline evidence: [EVIDENCE]
- Adjacent or distant categories worth transposing from: [OPTIONAL SOURCES]
- Offer or commercial destination: [DESTINATION]

## Execution Protocol

1. State the category's default promise and default execution.
2. Search seven lenses: topic, substance depth, stories/scenarios, avatar specificity, delivery style, storytelling format, and visual format.
3. Reject any difference unsupported by creator capability or copyable from a screenshot.
4. Choose one **controlling contrast**: the difference most likely to change why the intended buyer chooses or remembers this creator.
5. Test it blind against three nearby alternatives. Can an informed reader distinguish the position without the creator's name?
6. If not, add one supporting contrast and test again.
7. Stop when the distinction is clear. Park every unused lens. Do not produce a completed or scored seven-axis grid.
8. If importing from another category, transfer the mechanism and strip the source's aesthetic costume.

## Output Contract

- Category default in two lines
- One controlling contrast; zero or one supporting contrast
- Capability evidence that makes each chosen contrast believable
- One-sentence position and one recognition phrase
- Blind distinction test against three alternatives
- Parked lenses with a one-line reason, not a scorecard
- Three content expressions of the position
- Proof gaps and falsifier

## Quality Gate

- Is there one controlling contrast rather than seven filled boxes?
- Does every surfaced difference depend on real creator capability?
- Would the position change buyer choice or unaided recall, not just aesthetics?
- Does the blind test distinguish it from three nearby alternatives?
- Are unused lenses parked instead of padded into the strategy?

## Creative Latitude

The skeleton controls completeness, not the idea. Push for a recognition phrase that contains the real strategic tension, not category-safe language. A sharp phrase may become the creative center if the capability evidence supports it. Do not make the phrase cleverer than the underlying position.

## Deploy When

Use when a personal-brand position sounds accurate but interchangeable, or when a seven-axis positioning exercise risks producing a polished checklist instead of a decisive market contrast.
