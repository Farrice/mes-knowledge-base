# Kallaway Content Operating System - Hidden Knowledge

## 1. The OS Layer Is A Router, Not A New Expert

The implementation is valuable because it prevents the user from manually choosing between Kallaway components. It should stay compact and route to the right component instead of becoming another huge skill.

## 2. Full Stack Does Not Mean Every Component Every Time

The orchestrator should choose the smallest complete chain for the job. A hook request may only need hook triad plus word opener; a content business request may need buyer strategy, content system, obsession, and monetization.

## 3. The First Artifact Must Be Operational

Avoid analysis-only outputs. A run should produce a content blueprint, one-rep production brief, hook suite, script package, batch plan, audit, or content-to-revenue map.

## 4. Source Packages Are Confidence Anchors

The source videos are evidence packages, not context to preload blindly. Use their `analysis.md`, `video-context-ledger.md`, and `uncertainty-report.md` only when the lane requires them. Exact presence and uncertainty remain governed by the evidence map and source ledger.

## 5. OCR Is Not Available In The Current Evidence Batch

The full-context packages produced transcript and sampled visual evidence. OCR rows are unavailable because the local OCR dependency is not configured. Do not claim on-screen text unless it appears in an observed row or another reviewed source.

## 6. Kallaway Runs On Compound Handoffs

Good handoffs are short: evidence used, component used, output produced, next input, validation, and open risk. Long upstream context makes the OS brittle.

## 7. Buyer Intent Is The Tie Breaker

When content options are equally plausible, choose the option more likely to attract qualified buyers or create monetizable trust.

## 8. The Batch Loop Is The Compounding Mechanism

The system is incomplete if it only makes one piece and never converts the result into a learning rule for the next batch.

## 9. Authority Density Is A Tension, Not A Magic Score

The useful move is to keep reach, fit, trust, and commercial action visible when they disagree. Combining them into one number hides the decision and creates false precision.

## 10. The Seven Axes Stay Backstage

The axes help search for contrast. They should not appear as a completed worksheet unless the user explicitly asks for the research notes. The decision surface is one controlling contrast, occasionally one supporting contrast, and the unused lenses parked.

## 11. Minimum Evidence Is Not Mechanical Certainty

Four reps prevent one-post overreaction. They do not make noisy or confounded evidence conclusive. The operator must be able to say `INCONCLUSIVE` and specify the next fair test.

## 12. The Chaos Slot Protects Discovery

The core portfolio exploits the current model; chaos explores beyond it. Early strong signals earn more testing, not instant promotion. The same four-rep floor applies before chaos becomes a core bucket.
