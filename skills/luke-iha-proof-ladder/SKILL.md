# Luke Iha: Proof Ladder Architecture

Luke Iha's Proof Ladder is the meta-framework that sits *above* the 22-type proof arsenal. While `luke-iha-proof-mechanisms` teaches you WHICH proof weapons to use, the Proof Ladder teaches you HOW to architect them — the progressive hierarchy from weakest to strongest, the belief hardening model that governs dissolution, and the systematic trust compression that turns cold strangers into buyers at unprecedented speed.

This skill also uniquely addresses **proof as client acquisition** — the progressive portfolio strategy that transforms freelancers from commodity vendors into "selecting" authorities.

## Master Workflow: Proof Copy Engine

The **`/proof-copy-engine`** is the end-to-end master workflow for producing the highest-level copywriting across ALL platforms — YouTube scripts, LinkedIn posts, Instagram captions, articles, Substack, newsletters, emails, ads, sales pages, Twitter threads, and carousels. It braids three pillars simultaneously: **Proof** (5-tier architecture), **Story** (character → conflict → transformation), and **Mechanism** (named frameworks that make promises believable). Use this when you need masterful, publishable copy with maximum persuasive power.
