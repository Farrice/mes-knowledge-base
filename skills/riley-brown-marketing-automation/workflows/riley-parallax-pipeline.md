# /riley-parallax-pipeline — Research → Voice → Design → Distribute (Farrice)

Riley's skill-chaining doctrine (Pattern 8: "the goal then is that you use these skills together") applied to Farrice's Substack + LinkedIn. Riley supplies the *exemplar-retrieval input layer*; Farrice's voice + Satori + Parallax supply the craft. Every stage terminates in an editable artifact behind Farrice's approval — never auto-published.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `genius.md` first. Proceed if:
- The piece is going out under **Farrice's name** → VOICE-CARD + dial is mandatory (binding `farrice_voice_alignment`).
- There's a real topic/market to research (not a cold-start with no exemplars).
- Farrice wants the full chain; otherwise use the individual workflows.

## Skill Acquisition

- `genius.md` — Patterns 1 (examples-over-instructions), 8 (chaining), 9 (draft terminus)
- Voice: `_active/farrice-brand/voice/VOICE-CARD.md` + `skills/voice-os/SKILL.md` (BLEND default)
- Chain partners: `/parallax`, `/satori-frontend-flow`, `/scrape-creator`, `/extract`

## Execution

1. **Scrape the market's best (input layer).** `/riley-scrape-to-skill` steps 1–2 on 3–5 top creators in the topic space — verified, non-sponsored winners with lens-grounded "why it works" verdicts. This is Riley's retrieval layer: the essay is grounded in *what actually landed*, not training-memory vibes.
2. **Find the angle.** Synthesize the exemplars into the gap — Farrice's differentiated take (I-narrative, reader-as-hero per feedback memory), not a summary of what exists.
3. **Write in Farrice's voice.** Load VOICE-CARD (BLEND) as a layer, then run the Parallax/content workflow. First take is a candidate ship, not scaffolding. Reader-contract three dials (payoff/pull/recognition) apply.
4. **Verify (Step 5.5).** Real people/dates/claims → isolated fact-verifier subagent before delivery (Parallax Edition 02 shipped 7 fabrications when this was skipped).
5. **Design.** `/satori-frontend-flow` for layout/visual hierarchy where the piece warrants it.
6. **Stage for distribution.** `/riley-distribution-ops` / `/post-scheduler` — draft/stage the Substack + LinkedIn + X derivatives; **Farrice ships from the UI.** Nothing auto-publishes.
7. **Close.** Next Moves block + Operator Lesson (steering loop); offer `/jam` on taste-bearing takes.
