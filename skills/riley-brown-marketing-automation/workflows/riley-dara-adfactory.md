# /riley-dara-adfactory — Ad-Spy Intel → Original Static Ads

The stack `vision.md` names explicitly: "Riley × Dara Denney: longest-running-ad intel feeds `/dara-static-engine` production." Riley supplies the *what's-winning* signal (durability proxy); Dara supplies the static-ad craft (format selection, hooks, objection engine). Together they turn competitor intel into original on-brand creative — not clones. Direct revenue line: the $2,500 10-day sprint for supplement/performance brands (`project_proof-to-market-path-a.md`).

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `genius.md` first. Proceed if:
- You've run (or will run) `/riley-ad-spy` on the brand's #1 competitor.
- You have brand truth to build on (DESIGN.md or `/riley-brand-scrape`).
- The floor holds: **original creative on a proven structure**, never a competitor clone, never a carried byline.

- `genius.md` — Patterns 6 (longest-running), 13 (template-steal), Exemplar 2

- Live infra: `/ad-spy`, `/creative-from-winners`

## Execution

1. **Spy (Riley).** `/riley-ad-spy` on the competitor set → longest-running ads, ranked, with inference-labeled "why it survived" analysis. The #1 is the strongest free signal.
2. **Read the winners as strategy input, not templates to copy.** Extract offer / hook / mechanism / CTA from the durable ads. For the client-facing sprint, hand them the same read on themselves (start with their #1 competitor before pitching).
3. **Feed Dara's engine.** `/dara-static-engine` — format selection, winning-hook patterns, objection engine, comparison/founder/transformation formats. Riley's floor: "we would change it more than this." The competitor's runtime tells you the *angle* works; Dara builds the *original* execution.
4. **Layer buyer-trigger / copy lenses** (options): `meg-heckman` for trigger audits on the scraped ads; `luke-iha` for the persuasion pass on copy.
5. **Produce + test plan.** `/creative-from-winners` production step → Canva/Fantastic Studio; Dara's test plan for what to run. Zero real names/bylines from the source (the Dr. Fahim Hussain gate).
6. **Close the loop.** Variants + test plan back to the Notion record; offer `/jam` on picks.
