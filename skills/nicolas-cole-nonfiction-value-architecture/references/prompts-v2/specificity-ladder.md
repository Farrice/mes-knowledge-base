## Role & Activation

You are working in Nicolas Cole's nonfiction value architecture, applying the principle that "writing skill is not a mystery gap between bad and genius. It is the writer's ability to speak with increasing specificity, objectivity, actionability, and instruction" (genius.md, Specificity as Proficiency). This deliverable converts advice that sounds true but does not feel useful into concrete, measurable moments.

## Input Required

- `[LIST OF CLAIMS, TIPS, SUBHEADS, OR DRAFT SECTIONS]` — if a full draft is provided, extract the weakest broad claims first
- `[AUDIENCE]` — needed to judge what already counts as specific vs. still-bundled for this reader

## Execution Protocol

1. **Identify bundled terms** — words that name a category but do not tell the reader what to do: love, push, accountability, consistency, mindset, discipline, authenticity, trust, authority, confidence, value, quality, and similar broad words (genius.md, Bundled Term Detection).

2. **For each bundled term, ask: "What does this look like in a literal moment?"** (Moment Translation, genius.md pattern 6). This is the core move of the whole ladder.

3. **Convert the broad advice into an observable action.**

4. **Add a measurable or judgeable standard** where possible (Measurable Helpfulness, genius.md pattern 7) — something the reader could count or verify.

5. **Rewrite the line at three levels:**
   - Level 1: broad claim (as given)
   - Level 2: concrete action
   - Level 3: concrete action plus measurable standard

6. **Keep the version that gives the reader the most immediate instruction without overexplaining.** Not every claim needs to reach Level 3 — stop when the line delivers instruction without padding.

Worked model (Hall of Fame exemplar, genius.md): the move being applied —

> Weak: "Love your kids. Push them. Hold them accountable."
> Stronger: "No phones at the dinner table. When your child fails, do not give them an excuse to quit; require one more attempt. When your child breaks a promise, create a consequence tied to the action."

Why it works: the same high-level idea becomes visible, measurable, and executable. This is the shape of the move — never copy this example's content into unrelated material; find the moment specific to the input at hand.

### Content Type Adaptations

| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Newsletter | Upgrade section subheads and bullets before drafting |
| LinkedIn post | Upgrade the hook promise and numbered points |
| Client thought leadership | Preserve client phrasing only if it remains specific |
| YouTube script | Translate broad beats into scenes, moments, or examples |
| Lead magnet | Turn each broad rule into a checklist item |

## Output Contract

- Bundled-term audit — every flagged term with the line it appears in
- Specificity ladder table — Level 1 / Level 2 / Level 3 for each flagged claim
- Rewritten points — the kept version (best level reached) for each claim
- Measurability notes — what can be counted, observed, or judged, and how
- Remaining claims that still need evidence or examples this workflow alone cannot close

## Output Skeleton

```
BUNDLED-TERM AUDIT:
- Term: [word] — Found in: "[original line]"
- Term: [word] — Found in: "[original line]"

SPECIFICITY LADDER:
Claim [n]:
  Level 1 (broad): [original claim]
  Level 2 (concrete action): [rewrite]
  Level 3 (concrete + measurable): [rewrite]
  Kept version: [Level 2 or Level 3, whichever is warranted]

...

MEASURABILITY NOTES:
- [claim] → measurable via: [count/observation/standard]

REMAINING CLAIMS NEEDING EVIDENCE OR EXAMPLES:
- [claim] — needs: [stat / example / proof this workflow doesn't supply]
```

## Quality Gate

- Does every claim marked "fails" meet the workflow's own fail condition — reader could nod but still not know what to do?
- Does every kept version meet the pass condition — reader can imagine, measure, or execute it?
- Is every bundled term identified in the audit matched to a literal-moment rewrite (no term left unaddressed)?
- Does no rewritten line introduce a claim, statistic, or specific detail not present in or fairly inferable from the source material?

## Creative Latitude

The literal moment chosen for each bundled term is the taste call in this workflow — the same word ("discipline," "trust") can translate into dozens of different concrete moments, and the value is in picking the one most vivid and true to this specific input, not reaching for a generic stock example. The dinner-table exemplar above is a demonstration of the MOVE (bundled term → literal, measurable moment), never a template whose content should be reused or echoed in unrelated material.

## Deploy When

A draft, outline, or set of claims sounds true but reads as vague, generic, or hard to act on — before subhead work or full drafting, whenever advice needs to earn its usefulness.
