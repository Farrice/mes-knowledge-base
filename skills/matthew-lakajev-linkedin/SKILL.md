# Matthew Lakajev — LinkedIn Revenue Architecture

Matthew Lakajev (Seven Figure Creators) built a $3.5M+ business through organic LinkedIn: 5,000+ sales calls booked, 2,000+ founders coached, no ads, no virality — "Just talk to people. That's it." Under the tactics sits the real engine: a founder whose nervous system is calm enough to act immediately and whose self-trust survives the reps.

## Quick Reference

- **Offer Viability = Latent Demand × Category Belief × Outcome Observability × Payment Normalization** — multiplication; any zero kills it.
- **Category of one** = 2-3 identity layers intersected + "I am one of you." Language bank mirrored across every surface.
- **The whole formula**: 3-5 posts/week + 10 DMs before 10am + 5 engagers/day. "But still people don't do it."
- **Volume = time spent, not touches sent.** Five personalized beats 100 generic.
- **Never pitch, only invite.** The ladder: opinion ×3 → free gift → week of silence → scarcity invite. Exception: live hand-raise = book now.
- **Closed-lost monthly**: "Where did we drop the ball?" — 500+ people, real scarcity, manufactured compelling event.
- **Six Gates**: real → for me → snowflake → risk → worth-it-now → part-of-me → identity ally. Fix the earliest failing gate first. Trust is lane-scoped.
- **97/3**: market to the 97% AND the 3% by starting conversations; KPI = conversations, never impressions.
- **Twin rule**: next best client = twin of best current client, reached lukewarm through the graph.
- **Win online by going offline.** In person: 1-2 meetings to close vs 4-5 on Zoom.
- **Email = nugget of goodness, not newsletter.** Plain text, random timing, story-first.
- **State before tactics**: no-input mornings; big decisions only in a calm state; missed day = state event, no shame debt.
