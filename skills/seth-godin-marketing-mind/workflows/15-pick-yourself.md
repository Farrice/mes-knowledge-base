# Pick Yourself — Permission Audit + Smallest Shippable Unit

> **Expert**: Seth Godin | **Skill**: seth-godin-marketing-mind | **Tier**: Practitioner
> **Produces**: Pick-Yourself Plan — the permission being waited for, named; a PDF-to-20-class unit shipped this week as a gift
> **Slash Command**: `/gmind-pick-yourself`
> **Source**: Mel Robbins interview Part 2 (2026-07-16)

---

## Purpose

For the thing that's been waiting — the book, the channel, the offer, the change — where the honest blocker is that no one has granted permission yet. Godin's verdict on that arrangement: "No one's forcing you to not wait. You're choosing to wait. What a safe, lovely place to hang out... Congratulations. You've built a perfect place to hide." This workflow names the permission structure, shrinks the thing to its smallest shippable unit, and ships it as a gift — attachment removed by design.

---

## Inputs Required

1. **The Waiting Thing** — what's been sitting, in the operator's words, verbatim.
2. **The Permission Story** — who or what is supposed to say yes first (a publisher, a boss, an audience size, "feeling ready").
3. **How Long** — months or years it's been waiting. No judgment; it calibrates the on-ramp.

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Diagnose the Hiding Structure

Before prescribing anything, name what the waiting has been FOR. Godin's cases: the novel "submitted to 20 great publishers and they all turned me down. Okay, you're off the hook. How safe could that be?" — the TED talk that only exists "as soon as they call" — the influencer with "the wideband hat... the lighting... I just don't have my 300,000 followers yet. And now you've found another safe place to be stuck." Each is a completed, successful construction project whose product is safety. State it flat: what has this person built, and what does it protect them from? (Usually: a transaction with somebody who might say no — cross-ref `06-fear-isolate` when the fear is the dominant note.)

### Step 2: Name the Picker

Who exactly is supposed to call? "The idea that someone else will pick you and authorize you. They will call you up and they say, 'Mel, we know you have a novel in you. Would you please write the novel and we will publish it?' The end." Write the picker's name down — publisher, algorithm, boss, credential board, future confident self. Then the verdict: the dominant system wants the waiting; the picker is not coming. "Pick yourself" is the entire alternative.

### Step 3: Shrink to the Smallest Shippable Unit

Two shrink dials from the source:
- **Smallest viable audience** — "what's the smallest group of people that if you made an impact on them, it would be enough. So if it's a household, one kid, that would be enough."
- **Smallest viable art** — the 20-year nursing-school paralysis becomes "go to the local senior home... and volunteer for one afternoon a week"; "my boss won't let me" becomes "start a book group over lunch on Fridays and invite five people... if it works you get to do it more and if it doesn't work you can stop."

The canonical unit: "Make it into a PDF, email it to 20 people, ask them to share it with others for free. If it spreads, your phone will ring... If it doesn't spread, write something better. The end." Design THIS person's equivalent: one unit, ≤20 recipients, free, shippable within seven days.

### Step 4: Remove the Attachment

The unit ships as a gift or it will re-create the hiding. "Here, I made this — but it's not followed by and I need you to like it. It's just here, it's a gift... Not and you owe me a thank you note... because now I'm attached and I'm trying to control it." The physics: roped swimmers — "we're both going to drown because you can't survive that way."

Two design moves:
1. **Zero-invisible launch** (the mom exemplar): structure it so a silent result is private — "if no one comes, no one will know that no one came." Her next day: 5,000 people in line. The design freed her to hold the event at all.
2. **Name the catastrophe chain** out loud before shipping: "I will make a mistake. It will cause me shame. I will be ostracized from my community. I will be alone. And then I will die... It's so absurd that lipstick on your teeth is going to cause you to be dead."

### Step 5: Set the On-Ramp

If the name-attached ship is still too heavy, use the assumed-name ramp: "start a blog and if you want to do it under an assumed name, I don't care. But after 100 days, you'll want to put your name on it... You're going to be so proud that this work is there." The ramp is legitimate; permanent anonymity is just the hiding structure with better decor — set the 100-day check now.

### Step 6: The Becoming Line

Close with the mechanism that makes repetition the point: "We become what we do. If you want to be a truthful person, start telling the truth." One shipped unit is one rep of the identity being built. "You are not behind, you are becoming."

## Quality Gate

| Dimension | Minimum Standard |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Hiding named flat | The structure is stated as a completed project ("congratulations"), never as a character flaw |
| Picker named | A specific waiting-for entity, not "fear" in the abstract |
| Unit is small enough | Shippable in ≤7 days to ≤20 people at $0 — if it reads like a launch plan, shrink again |
| Gift integrity | No success metric that depends on recipients' reactions; zero-invisible design present |
| Not a pep talk | Every step carries a designed mechanism, no "believe in yourself" language |
