# Kallaway Illusion of Novelty — User Guide

How to use the manufactured-novelty skill: what it does, the highest-payoff entry points, and where it stops. Companion: `OPERATORS-GUIDE.md` (how the system runs it underneath you). Updated 2026-07-13 after the 13-workflow benchmark, the flagship run, and the enrichment pass.

## The one-line version

This skill manufactures the FEELING of novelty for topics that are objectively old, boring, or saturated, so they earn attention anyway. Five components do the work: New Reveal + Outcome Mapping (makes them look, then stay), Contrast Framing (opens the "I might be wrong" gap), honest Urgency (optional turbo), Bullseye Proof (climb the Trust Ladder toward the viewer), Protect the Illusion (commit, whisper, never show the mascot). One line is non-negotiable: the facts, urgency windows, and proof are always REAL. Only the *novelty* is manufactured. Fabrication is an auto-fail.

## Three canonical modes

**1. Gut Check (`/novelty-audit`).** Hand it a draft; get a scored 3-questions × 5-components read plus a fix list. Honest and restrained: in the 26-run benchmark it never forced a component it didn't believe in, and six workflows returned clean "ship it" verdicts on strong prose. Known blind spot below.

**2. Co-Writer (`/novelty-forge`).** Topic in, finished asset out: all five components assembled, component map attached, rubric self-scored. The full build path when you're starting from a dead topic.

**3. Pattern Analyst (`/novelty-pattern`).** Winner/loser extraction on your own published content. Converts the one-size framework into your niche-specific execution rules (words, structures, topics that separate your winners). Needs real performance data; it correctly refuses to run at N=1.

**Front door: `/novelty-engine`.** The conductor over all 13 workflows. It routes by job (full asset, angles, hooks, audit, retention handoff, campaign, calibration) and carries a live-research grounding phase (Phase 0.5) so reveals, urgency windows, and proof come from verified current sources, never training memory. The flagship post proved the pattern: every factual claim in it traces to a VERIFIED source (`_active/linkedin-launch/04-deliverables/content-os/novelty-engine-flagship/FLAGSHIP.md`).

## The benchmark lesson (read this before trusting a score)

The Gut-Check scorecard is honest inside its lane and blind outside it. In the 2026-07-13 benchmark it scored an original post and its rewrite both 10/10 while Farrice ranked them clearly apart; the deciding axis was body coherence and loop closure, which no component measures. **Never rank two rival versions on the novelty scorecard alone.** A tie at the ceiling means the card cannot decide; route the call to `/novelty-to-addictive` (the only workflow that caught the rewrite's dropped narrative loop) or a prose/writers-room lens. Full card: `docs/solutions/2026-07-13-novelty-scorecard-lane-blind-to-coherence.md`.

## The highest-payoff plays (proven)

1. **`/novelty-forge` via `/novelty-engine` for the $5K sprint.** The linkedin-launch flagship hit the 10/10 ceiling with a real dated urgency window and all-VERIFIED claims. This is the pattern for every sprint post on a "saturated" topic.
2. **`/novelty-to-addictive` on any hook-strong, body-flat piece.** It found the single best fix in 26 benchmark runs (the abandoned friend-loop) and independently matched Farrice's felt "flat" verdict.
3. **`/novelty-angles` before declaring a client topic dead.** Wellness, performance, and real-estate topics are all Path B: old stuff with unmined angles. For Jen's listings, the "new aspect + wanted outcome" formula is the listing-hook machine.
4. **`/novelty-proof` to re-aim third-party stats at the reader.** The benchmark's surviving fix on both specimens: same verified numbers, one rung closer ("if it happened to the giant, it happens to you").
5. **`/novelty-protect` as the last pass on anything that sells.** Mascot reveals (hedging, false modesty, "you've probably heard this") and town-crier tone are the two silent killers; this pass scrubs both.

## What it's NOT for (honest limits)

- **Not a coherence or retention engine.** It manufactures the LOOK; kallaway-addictive-storytelling owns the STAY. Body problems route to `/novelty-to-addictive`.
- **Not a fact source.** Phase 0.5 research and `fact-verifier` own truth; this skill only decides how truth gets framed.
- **Not a voice.** Tone guidance stops at whisper-vs-crier; the line belongs to VOICE-CARD, Roth, or the client's rules.
- **Not for content that's already novel.** A genuinely new thing is Path A: just reveal it. Running the full engine on real news over-engineers it.
- **Weakest fit:** ranking two refined rivals (the lane-blindness above) and any piece where the honest answer is "skip urgency" but you're tempted not to. Restraint is a feature here.

## Three quick-start recipes

1. **Dead topic to posted:** `/novelty-engine` with the topic + audience → review component map + source table → BLEND voice pass → ship.
2. **Draft nobody engages with:** `/novelty-audit` → run the routed fix workflow → `/novelty-protect` as the final scrub.
3. **Client category entry (wellness/performance):** `/novelty-angles` on their stale topic → `/novelty-forge` the best angle → `/novelty-campaign` to atomize across platforms.
