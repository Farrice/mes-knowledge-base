# `/kallaway-x-jenny-hoyos`: Kallaway × Jenny Hoyos, Novelty-Sustained Retention

The compound output: Kallaway's 5-component novelty engine stops being a hook-only trick and becomes a per-beat gate, wired into Hoyos' retention-graph diagnosis. Kallaway alone manufactures attention at second 1. Hoyos alone diagnoses structural retention failures (hook, progression, loop) but has no vocabulary for staleness re-creeping mid-video. Together: a saturated topic that still feels new all the way to the payoff, with dips diagnosed on both a structural axis and a novelty-decay axis.

## Stacking Partners
- **Kallaway (Illusion of Novelty)**: the 5-component engine (New Reveal + Outcome Mapping, Contrast Framing, Urgency, Bullseye Proof, Protect the Illusion), applied **per beat** across the whole runtime instead of spent once in the hook zone
- **Jenny Hoyos (Shorts)**: but/so conflict-chain storytelling, the A/B/C retention-graph diagnostic (early exodus, point drops, slow slope), payoff spacing (answer as literal last word), and loop physics

## When to Use
- A beat sheet or script already has a working hook, but the topic is saturated enough that by beat 3-4 it reads as "another one of those." The hook novelty doesn't survive the body.
- Diagnosing an underperforming short where the hook passed (viewed-vs-swiped ≥70%) but retention still decays mid-video with no obvious Hoyos-named cause (no conclusional language, no confusion, no missing progression mechanism). The missing variable is stale novelty, not structure.
- Building new content in a genuinely dead-saturated niche where even a strong hook can't carry five beats of sameness on its own.

## Not This
`/omar-kallaway-pattern-interrupt` already owns Kallaway × short-form **hook** engineering: the first-frame/thumbnail scroll-stop, visual and verbal, before the viewer commits to watching. **This crossing starts after the hook lands.** It owns the rest of the curve: mid-video retention, but/so conflict chains, payoff spacing, and dip diagnosis on the retention graph, wrapped in manufactured novelty so a stale topic keeps *feeling* new at second 20, not just second 1. Hooks and first-frame work route to `omar-kallaway-pattern-interrupt`. Body, payoff, and retention work stay here.

## Inputs
- `[SCRIPT_OR_TREATMENT]`: beat sheet, script, or treatment with rough timestamps
- `[RETENTION_DATA]` (optional): viewed-vs-swiped %, retention graph shape or cliffs, if diagnosing a shipped video
- `[TOPIC_SATURATION]`: how saturated or stale this topic is in the niche (informs how hard the per-beat novelty check needs to work)
- `[AVATAR_HELD_BELIEF]`: what the avatar already believes about this topic (required for Contrast Framing at every beat, not just the hook)

## Execution

### Step 1: Beat-Map the Retention Curve (Hoyos)
Break the script/treatment into its VIRAL-framework beats (V-I-R-A-L, or the existing beats if a draft exists). For each beat, capture: timestamp, what happens, the **but/so connector** into it (never "and then"), and, if retention data exists, the actual retention % at that point.

### Step 2: Per-Beat Novelty Check (Kallaway, run at EVERY beat, not just the hook)
This is the fused move: re-apply the novelty engine as a gate at each beat, not just once at the hook. For every beat, ask:
- **Fresh reveal still present?** Is there a small new angle, detail, or twist at this beat, or is it coasting on the hook's novelty?
- **Contrast still live?** Is it still positioned against the avatar's held belief, or has it decayed into "yeah, I already knew that" (the gap closed)?
- **Proof climbing or flat?** Is Bullseye Proof escalating toward the viewer, or stuck at the same rung as the hook?
- **Delivery still whisper, not crier?** Has the tone drifted town-crier as the hook's initial excitement wears off?
Score each beat: **fresh, decaying, or stale.**

### Step 3: Dip Diagnosis (Hoyos graph reading, novelty-informed)
Where retention data exists, classify each drop per Hoyos' A/B/C (early exodus, point drops, slow slope), scrubbing 2-5 seconds *before* the drop per her method. **Fused addition**: for every drop or decay zone, ask both questions.
1. *Hoyos' structural question*: conclusional language, dread language, confusion, or a missing progression mechanism sitting before the drop?
2. *Kallaway's novelty question*: did the illusion collapse here? A hedge that admits the angle is old, a contrast that stopped feeling like a gap, proof that went flat, or delivery that turned salesy?
Staleness-collapse is a **4th drop cause** Hoyos' framework doesn't name on its own. This crossing adds it alongside her three.

### Step 4: Payoff Spacing + Novelty Reserve
Hoyos: the answer must land as the literal last word; nothing trails it. Kallaway layer: reserve **at least one fresh-reveal beat for the back third**, a twist or detail saved rather than fully spent in the hook, so the payoff itself still carries novelty instead of resolving a question the viewer assumes they already know the shape of.

### Step 5: Revised Beat Sheet
Output the full beat sheet with, per beat: original content, but/so connector, novelty verdict, the fix if stale, and an updated retention prediction.

## What This Replaces
Replaces treating novelty as a hook-only expense: `novelty-forge` builds one strong opener, then the body gets scripted on Hoyos mechanics alone with no staleness check. Also replaces running `/03-diagnose-retention` as a purely structural audit blind to novelty decay. Neither skill alone catches the specific failure mode this crossing targets: a video with a genuinely strong hook and technically correct but/so beats that still bleeds viewers mid-video, because the exact manufactured-novelty trick that won the hook was never refreshed for the body. The beats are structurally sound and simultaneously stale.

## Quality Gate
- [ ] Every beat, not just the hook, scored for novelty-component presence (fresh/decaying/stale)
- [ ] Any retention drop diagnosed on both axes: Hoyos structural cause and Kallaway novelty-decay cause
- [ ] At least one fresh-reveal beat identified and reserved in the back third
- [ ] Differentiation preserved: no first-frame/thumbnail/visual-hook work here; that routes to `omar-kallaway-pattern-interrupt`
- [ ] Honesty spine intact: refreshing novelty per beat never fabricates a fact, urgency window, or proof point
- [ ] Contrast Framing at each beat is anchored to the avatar's actual held belief, not a strawman re-used from the hook
