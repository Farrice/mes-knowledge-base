# /novelty-protect — Protect the Illusion (Mascot Audit + Gossip-Whisperer Rewrite)

The fifth and final pass over any piece already built with `/novelty-forge` (or imported from anywhere). It produces a scrubbed draft plus a flagged-line ledger: every hedge that takes the head off the mascot is committed, and every billboard/salesy line is dropped into a whisper. Fire it on any draft that *feels* almost right but reads slightly apologetic, slightly loud, or slightly "you've heard this before."

The first four components build the illusion. This one keeps you from tearing it down yourself. Audiences rarely catch you. You hand them the mascot head.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `../genius.md` if it is not already hot — the mascot rule (Step 5a), the gossip-whisperer (Step 5b), Genius Patterns 8 and 9, and rubric criteria 6 and 7 are the operating spec for this pass.

From the Decision Framework, this workflow specifically needs:
- **What is the *old frame* this piece dressed up?** (You must know it to detect when the draft accidentally confesses it. If the angle is genuinely new — Path A — the mascot risk is near zero; the tone pass still runs.)
- **Where was the writer tempted to hedge?** (Question 6 of the framework. Pre-identify the likely apology points before scanning so you read with suspicion.)
- **Who is the avatar, and do they already know the old frame?** (If the entire audience already knows the old name, commitment is harder and you may need a *different* angle — escalate back to `./novelty-forge.md` rather than scrub a doomed one.)

If those are unanswerable, the draft was built without an avatar. Stop and route to `./novelty-forge.md` Step 1 before protecting anything.

## Skill Acquisition

- **Always:** `../genius.md` (mascot rule, gossip-whisperer, Hidden Knowledge bullets on false modesty and structural whisper, anti-pattern list).
- **Stacks with (load when scrubbing tone heavily):** `../../kallaway-word-mastery/workflows/tone-calibration-engine.md` — the Gossip Whisperer is a named tone target there; hand the scrubbed draft over for a full register sweep on long-form. And `../../kallaway-word-mastery/workflows/believability-audit.md` — mascot-reveal scrubbing is the structural twin of its believability pass; run it after this when claims density is high.
- **Upstream:** `./novelty-forge.md` produced the draft. This workflow never re-builds components; it only protects what forge built. If the audit finds the *contrast* or *proof* is broken (not just hedged), kick back to forge — protection cannot fix a missing component.

## Execution

Run three parts in order. Part A finds the leaks; Part B fixes the volume; Part C decides whether to hand off. Every flag carries a committed rewrite, never just a note.

### PART A — The Mascot-Reveal Audit

A mascot reveal is any line that tells the brain "this isn't actually new / isn't actually a big deal / you already know this." It collapses the open loop the first four components built. Scan the draft for the five leak families below. For each hit: quote the exact phrase, name the family, write the committed replacement.

| Leak family | What it sounds like | Why it kills the illusion |
|---|---|---|
| **Hedge-to-old** | "this is really just X," "it's basically circadian biology," "fundamentally nothing new" | Confesses the new frame is the old thing in costume — head off the mascot |
| **Everyone-knows** | "people have known this for decades," "this is common knowledge," "nothing groundbreaking here" | Tells the brain it's familiar → autopilot resumes → scroll |
| **False modesty** | "I'm no expert but," "this might be obvious but," "you've probably heard this," "take this with a grain of salt" | De-authorizes the reveal and pre-labels it as un-new |
| **Magnitude downplay** | "small thing," "minor tweak," "honestly not a huge deal," "won't change your life or anything" | Shrinks the gap; intrigue is *proportional* to the gap |
| **Pre-emptive apology** | "sorry if this is too long," "I know everyone says this," "not trying to oversell" | Signals the writer doesn't believe the frame → reader won't either |

**The committed-rewrite move.** Replace each hedge with the same fact stated as if it is genuinely the first time anyone has framed it this way — because for the slice of the audience who doesn't hold the old frame, it is. Commit to the storyline. The fix is almost always *delete the hedge clause and let the claim stand*, or *flip the apology into a confidence signal*.

**The ethics reframe (state it once, internally, so you scrub without flinching).** Committing is not lying. You are manufacturing the illusion of *novelty* — never of facts. The fact stays true; only the framing is new. Anyone who doesn't know or remember the old frame experiences the new frame as genuinely new, so commitment deceives no one. The line you may never cross is the Honesty Spine: never fake the reveal, the urgency, or the proof. Scrub the hedge; never invent the fact.

**Audit output:** a numbered ledger — `#N · [family] · "exact phrase" → "committed rewrite"`.

### PART B — The Gossip-Whisperer Rewrite

Same information, opposite trust outcome depending on register. Convert every Town Crier line into a Gossip Whisperer line. Scan for the salesy tells: ALL-CAPS, exclamation points, "HUGE / massive / game-changer / this changes everything," "you NEED to," "pay attention NOW," billboard declaratives that announce instead of confide.

**The conversion.** A town crier *announces from a stage*; a gossip whisperer *leans in and lets you behind a curtain you weren't supposed to see*. Lower the voice. Under-claim the magnitude in the words while over-delivering the substance underneath. Lowercase conspiratorial energy beats uppercase urgency.

Show every fix as a before/after pair:

| Town Crier (before) | Gossip Whisperer (after) |
|---|---|
| "HUGE NEWS — this changes everything." | "ok so… most people don't know this yet." |
| "You NEED to hear this right now." | "here's the part nobody mentions." |
| "This is a total game-changer!!" | "this quietly broke the thing everyone still does." |
| "Pay attention — this is massive." | "i probably shouldn't be telling you this, but—" |
| "Don't scroll past, this is important." | "stick with me for one second, this one's a little strange." |

> All pairs above are illustrative — vary, never verbatim. The target is *register*, not a phrasebook.

**The 2026 sales-resistance mechanism (Genius Pattern 9 — state it so you scrub the register, not just the words).** The salesy *tone itself* is now a disqualifier, independent of content quality. Audiences are trained to distrust and dismiss anything that sounds like a billboard; the moment a line reads as a sale, trust drops regardless of how true or good the offer is. The whisper wins every time and it is not close. "In 2002 that would have worked. 2026, not a chance." Selling *to* someone triggers the resistance; sharing *with* someone bypasses it. The structural tells: lowercase energy, conspiratorial framing, magnitude under-claimed verbally.

**Whisper-without-lying guard.** Lowering the volume does not lower the truth. If a claim is large, you may *under-state the announcement* but never under-state to the point of misleading, and never inflate to fill the quiet. The whisper is a tone, not a license.

### PART C — Stack Decision

After the two passes, decide handoff:
- **Tone is fully fixed in-house** for short assets (a post, a script, an email) → close here, return the scrubbed draft.
- **Long-form / high register-stakes** (VSL, landing page, thought-leadership essay) → hand the scrubbed draft to `../../kallaway-word-mastery/workflows/tone-calibration-engine.md` for a full sweep; this workflow only catches the gross town-crier leaks, not subtle rhythm.
- **Claims-dense** (numbers, mechanisms, named results) → run `../../kallaway-word-mastery/workflows/believability-audit.md` next; a believable claim that's hedged is a wasted claim, and an unhedged claim that isn't believable is a different failure.
- **Audit found a *structural* hole** (contrast missing, proof fabricated or absent, urgency faked) → kick back to `./novelty-forge.md`. Protection cannot patch a missing component.

### Worked Mini-Example — Gutter Cleaning (fresh topic)

Avatar: suburban homeowner, 40s, dreads the once-a-year ladder ritual. Old frame the draft dressed up: "gutter guards / mesh screens" (a product that has existed for decades). Outcome wanted: never get on the ladder again. Draft handed up from forge:

> **HUGE for any homeowner who hates ladders!!** There's a new self-clearing gutter system that means you basically never clean gutters again — and you'll never climb a ladder in October again. You've spent every fall scooping wet leaves out by hand, but turns out that whole ritual is obsolete now. *Honestly this is really just a fancy mesh screen, people have been using these for years, so I'm no expert here.* A neighbor down my street, same kind of house as yours, had it installed and texted me a photo of his clean gutters after the first big storm. **You NEED to look into this before the leaves drop!!**

**Part A — mascot ledger (3 reveals):**
1. *Hedge-to-old* · "this is really just a fancy mesh screen" → delete clause; let the self-clearing claim stand on its own.
2. *Everyone-knows* · "people have been using these for years" → delete; replaced framing: "most homeowners on your street still don't know the install got this simple."
3. *False modesty* · "so I'm no expert here" → flip to confidence: "and I've watched a dozen of these go in this season."

**Part B — town-crier ledger (2 lines):**
1. "HUGE for any homeowner who hates ladders!!" → "ok, so if you're the one who always gets stuck doing the gutters in your house—"
2. "You NEED to look into this before the leaves drop!!" → "worth knowing before the leaves come down, since installers book up fast that month." *(Note: the urgency clause here is real — installer scheduling tightens in autumn — so it survives the whisper as a fact, not a fabricated deadline. Had it been invented, it would have been cut, not whispered.)*

**Scrubbed draft:**

> ok, so if you're the one who always gets stuck doing the gutters in your house — there's a self-clearing gutter system now that means you basically never get on the ladder again. you've spent every fall scooping wet leaves out by hand, but that whole ritual is obsolete. most homeowners on your street still don't know the install got this simple, and i've watched a dozen of these go in this season. a neighbor down the block, same kind of house as yours, had one put on and texted me a photo of clean gutters after the first big storm. worth knowing before the leaves come down — installers book up fast that month.

Same facts, every one intact. Three confessions committed, two billboards lowered to a whisper. The loop the forge opened stays open through delivery.

## Content-Type Adaptations

The audit and rewrite logic is identical across assets; *where the leaks hide* and *how loud "town crier" registers* change by format. Apply per row.

| Asset | How /novelty-protect's output changes |
|---|---|
| **Short-form video script** | Mascot reveals hide in the *spoken aside* mid-script ("I mean this is basically just…") and in the throwaway outro. Whisper is mostly vocal-delivery direction — annotate the script with `[lower, conspiratorial]` cues; cut any line the VO would "announce." |
| **LinkedIn post** | False modesty lives in the opener ("Probably obvious but…") and the humble-brag close. Whisper = lowercase first line, no "🚨 BIG NEWS." Scrub the "agree?" town-crier CTA into a confided observation. Keep one quiet line of white space before the reveal. |
| **X/Twitter thread** | Each tweet is a fresh chance to leak; audit *every* tweet's opener, not just tweet 1. Town-crier lives in caps and "THREAD 🧵" hype. Whisper = the thread reads like a DM you're forwarding. The mascot most often appears in the final "just my 2 cents" tweet — cut it. |
| **Email** | Subject line is the loudest town-crier zone (kill "HUGE:", "URGENT:", "Don't miss"). Mascot reveals hide in the P.S. ("None of this is new, of course…"). Whisper = a subject that reads like a friend's note, body that lets them behind a curtain. |
| **Ad / VSL** | Highest sales-resistance surface — town-crier register is fatal here. Scrub every hype superlative; the whisper *is* the conversion mechanism in 2026. Mascot reveals in a VSL hide in the "to be fair / full disclosure" beat — commit instead. Real disclaimers stay (legal); manufactured modesty goes. |
| **Sales / Landing page** | Headlines and section banners are town-crier magnets (all-caps value props, "GAME-CHANGING"). Rewrite to confided specificity. Mascot reveals hide in the FAQ ("Isn't this just X?" answered with a confession) — answer with commitment to the new frame instead. |
| **Long-form article** | Hedges multiply over length ("of course, none of this is groundbreaking," "experts have long known"). Audit per-section. Whisper is harder to sustain across thousands of words → hand to `tone-calibration-engine.md` after this pass. Magnitude-downplay is the dominant leak in essays. |
| **Ghostwritten thought-leadership** | The principal's *voice* may include reflexive false modesty — preserve genuine humility, cut illusion-killing hedges (a real distinction: "I got this wrong once" is voice; "this is nothing new" is a mascot reveal). Whisper aligns naturally with authority-by-confiding. Flag any line that lets the principal accidentally confess the framing. |

## Output Requirements

Return three artifacts:

1. **Mascot-Reveal Ledger** — numbered list: `#N · [leak family] · "exact phrase from draft" → "committed rewrite"`. Empty if clean (state "no mascot reveals found — illusion intact").
2. **Town-Crier Ledger** — before/after table of every salesy line converted to gossip-whisperer register. Flag any urgency clause as REAL (survives) or FABRICATED (cut, not whispered).
3. **Scrubbed Draft** — the full piece, every fact preserved, every hedge committed, every billboard whispered, in the original asset format. If a structural hole was found, append `KICK-BACK: route to /novelty-forge — [missing component]` instead of pretending protection fixed it.

## Quality Gate

Score against `../genius.md` rubric. This workflow lives or dies on two criteria:
- **Criterion 6 — Illusion Intact:** zero mascot reveals after the pass. One surviving hedge / false-modesty / "everyone knows" = automatic ≤5. The whole point of the workflow is reaching zero.
- **Criterion 7 — Whisper Test:** delivery reads as secret-sharing, not billboard. Any surviving salesy register = cap at 6 regardless of substance.

Anti-patterns this pass must eliminate: hedging, false modesty, "you've probably heard this," "this is just X that's existed forever," town-crier / exclamation-point sales tone. Anti-patterns it must NOT introduce: do not "fix" a hedge by inventing a bolder fact; do not whisper a claim into something untrue.

**Honesty Spine (non-negotiable).** The illusion is of NOVELTY only. Every fact, every urgency window, every proof point in the scrubbed draft must remain REAL and unchanged from the source. Committing to the new frame is permitted because it deceives no one who doesn't hold the old frame; fabricating a fact, a deadline, or a result is never permitted. If protecting the illusion would require inventing anything, stop — that is a forge failure, not a protection task.

**One-line self-check:** every confession is now a commitment, every billboard is now a whisper, and not one fact moved.
