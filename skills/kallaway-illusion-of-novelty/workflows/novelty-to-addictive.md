# /novelty-to-addictive — Novelty → Addiction Pipeline

Produces a finished script or asset where the Illusion of Novelty manufactures the *look* (attention captured) and Kallaway's Four-Step Addiction Loop manufactures the *stay* (attention held to the end). Fire this when a single hook is not enough — the topic is boring AND the piece is long enough that a viewer who stops scrolling can still bounce thirty seconds in.

The split: Illusion of Novelty owns the **front** (open). The Addiction Loop owns the **body** (everything after the open). The two skills share a spine — the novelty hook becomes the Loop's opening Stakes and Big Question, so the handoff is seamless rather than two bolted-together halves.

---

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `../genius.md` if it is not already hot, then answer the Decision Framework questions this pipeline depends on:

1. **Old or genuinely new topic?** Default Path B (mine a new angle). Path A only if a real new tool/study/method exists.
2. **What does the avatar already believe about this topic?** (Feeds Contrast AND becomes the Loop's overturned assumption.)
3. **What outcome does the avatar actually want?** (Feeds Outcome Mapping AND becomes the Loop's Stakes payoff.)
4. **Is there an HONEST urgency window?** Yes → Turbo. No → skip; that is correct, not a gap.
5. **Highest honest Trust-Ladder rung available?** Bullseye if a viewer-mimic example or self-demo exists.
6. **Where will I be tempted to hedge?** Pre-commit against the mascot reveal — this matters double here, because a Head Fake in the body is the single most likely place a writer accidentally reveals the mascot.

If the avatar's held belief and wanted outcome cannot be named, stop and load `kallaway-audience-obsession` (or run the quick 2-question avatar mini-canvass from `./novelty-forge.md`) before proceeding. Both halves of this pipeline collapse without a precise avatar.

## Execution

The pipeline runs front-end → handoff → reconcile → assemble. Do not skip the reconcile step; it is where most amateur "novelty + retention" mashups break.

### Step 1 — Manufacture the front-end (Illusion of Novelty)

Either run `./novelty-forge.md` and accept its output, or build the five components directly:

- **New Reveal + Outcome Mapping** — `reveal new aspect of a thing + tie it to an outcome you know the viewer wants`.
- **Contrast Framing** — anchor the reveal against the avatar's *actual held belief* as a true opposite.
- **Urgency** — only if a real window exists; else skip.
- **Bullseye Proof** — the highest honest Trust-Ladder rung (viewer-mimic example beats a study).
- **Protect the Illusion** — commit to the storyline; Gossip Whisperer delivery.

Output of this step: a dense 1–2 line hook (carrying Reveal + Outcome + Contrast, plus Urgency if real) and a proof beat staged for the body. Hold these. They become the raw material for the Loop.

### Step 2 — Handoff: map novelty components onto the Addiction Loop

Load `skills/kallaway-addictive-storytelling/genius.md` and `skills/kallaway-addictive-storytelling/workflows/addiction-loop-architect.md`. The Four-Step Loop is **Stakes → Big Question → Head Fake → Rehook**. Map, do not duplicate:

| Novelty component (front) | Maps onto Loop component (body) | The move |
|---|---|---|
| New Reveal + Outcome Mapping | **Stakes** — what the viewer gains/loses by knowing this | The wanted outcome from Mapping becomes the explicit stake: "get this right and you get *Y*; miss it and you keep losing." |
| Contrast Framing | **Big Question** — the open loop the body promises to close | The gap between old belief and new reality *is* the Big Question. Phrase the contrast as an unresolved question, not a settled fact, so the loop stays open. |
| (body content / mechanism) | **Head Fake** — the redirect that resets the prediction | Tease a resolution, then redirect to a deeper layer before paying off. The mechanism behind the new angle is your richest Head Fake material. |
| Bullseye Proof + open follow-on | **Rehook** — the next loop that pulls them past the proof | After proof lands, the proof itself raises a second question ("if it worked for *them*, what does that mean for *you*?") → opens the next stake. |

Concretely: the novelty hook is not discarded after the open — it seeds the entire body. The Outcome becomes the Stakes engine; the Contrast gap becomes the Big Question that the body spends its runtime answering; the mechanism explanation becomes one or more Head Fakes; the proof beat becomes a Rehook into the next loop or the close.

Design at least one full loop in the body. Long pieces stack loops: each Rehook seeds the next Stakes. Short pieces run a single tight loop and resolve.

### Step 3 — Reconcile (the step that saves the piece)

The retention edits from the Loop can quietly violate the novelty discipline. Audit the assembled draft against three reconcile checks:

1. **No mascot reveal introduced by a Head Fake.** Head Fakes redirect to a *deeper layer of the same new frame* — never to "here's the boring real reason this works, which everyone's known forever." A Head Fake that exposes the old mechanism as old is a mascot reveal wearing a retention costume. Redirect deeper into the illusion, not behind the gate.
2. **Gossip Whisperer survives the loop's stake-raising.** The Addiction Loop wants escalating tension; that pressure tempts town-crier register ("and THIS changes EVERYTHING"). Keep the secret-sharing tone through every Rehook. Escalate the substance, not the volume.
3. **Contrast integrity holds across the Big Question.** When the contrast becomes the Big Question, confirm it is still the avatar's true held belief as a true opposite — not softened into a strawman to make the loop easier to close.

If any check fails, fix the body, not the front. The novelty front-end is load-bearing; the loop bends to it.

### Step 4 — Assemble + dual map

Produce the complete script/asset with a two-column map showing which lines carry novelty components (front) and which carry Loop components (body), so the operator can see the seam and edit either layer independently.

### Worked mini-example — Gutter cleaning (a different boring topic, end to end)

*Avatar:* suburban homeowner, 35–55, owns the house, dreads a roof job. *Held belief:* "you clean your gutters in the fall, after the leaves drop." *Wanted outcome:* never deal with a flooded basement or a four-figure repair bill.

**FRONT — Illusion of Novelty hook (vary, never verbatim):**
> "Turns out the season everyone cleans their gutters is the exact wrong one — and the timing is what's quietly rotting the wood behind your fascia."
- New Reveal: timing, not the cleaning itself, is the variable. · Outcome Mapping: avoid the hidden rot / the repair bill.
- Contrast: against "clean them in fall after the leaves drop" (true opposite — a *different* season, not an unrelated claim).
- Urgency: skip unless there's a real seasonal window ("this only matters before the first hard freeze" — use only if honestly true for the climate).

**BODY — Addiction Loop, seeded from the hook:**
- **Stakes** (from Outcome Mapping): "Get the timing right and the system drains itself all winter. Get it wrong and you're paying to replace soffit and fascia next spring — that's the four-figure number, not the cleaning."
- **Big Question** (from Contrast gap): "So if fall is the wrong time, when *is* the right time — and why has every checklist had it backwards?"
- **Head Fake** (mechanism, redirected deeper — NOT a mascot reveal): "You'd assume it's about getting ahead of the leaves. It's not. It's about the freeze-thaw cycle behind the gutter, which most people never see…" (redirects into a deeper layer of the *same* new frame; never says "this is just basic drainage, every pro knows it").
- **Bullseye Proof → Rehook:** "I had a homeowner your age, same split-level, who'd cleaned every October like clockwork — still got the rot. He moved one job to the right week and the staining stopped cold." Then the Rehook: "Which raises the part nobody on those checklists tells you about *how* to check your own timing window…" → next loop or close.
- **Protect:** delivered as a peek behind the curtain ("not many homeowners know this yet"); never hedged into "of course this is obvious if you think about it."

Reconcile check passes: the Head Fake redirects deeper rather than exposing the mechanism as old; the whisper holds through the stakes; the contrast stays a true opposite. The facts (rot mechanism, repair cost, the example) must all be REAL — manufacture the novelty, never the numbers or the homeowner.

## Content-Type Adaptations

| Asset | How the novelty→addiction pipeline changes |
|---|---|
| **Short-form video script** | Front-load Reveal+Outcome+Contrast in the first 1–2 lines as the open; run ONE tight loop (Stakes→Big Question→single Head Fake→proof as the close). No Rehook into a second loop — runtime won't support it. Whisper register from frame one. |
| **LinkedIn post** | Hook line = the novelty open; line break; Stakes + Big Question in the first two paragraphs (above the "see more" fold). One Head Fake mid-post as a contrarian turn, proof as a short relatable beat, Rehook as the closing line that earns the comment. No emoji-shouting; conspiratorial-confidential tone. |
| **X/Twitter thread** | Tweet 1 = densest novelty hook (Reveal+Outcome+Contrast). Each subsequent tweet closes the prior loop and opens the next (every tweet ends on a Rehook). The thread structure IS loop-stacking — map one loop per 1–3 tweets. Final tweet resolves the Big Question. |
| **Email** | Subject line carries the Contrast gap as a Big Question (curiosity, not clickbait). Open with Stakes; body runs 1–2 loops; the Head Fake is the "but here's what I didn't expect" pivot. Proof = a Bullseye reader story. CTA replaces the final Rehook — the open loop the click closes. |
| **Ad / VSL** | Open with the densest novelty hook for the cold scroll-stop; Stakes within the first 5–8 seconds. Stack loops aggressively (VSL retention lives on Rehooks). Head Fakes redirect toward the mechanism reveal; proof escalates Trust-Ladder rungs as the VSL runs. Hard discipline on Gossip Whisperer — paid traffic is the most sales-resistant audience; town-crier register tanks watch time. |
| **Sales / Landing page** | Headline = novelty Reveal+Contrast. Subhead = Stakes. Section structure = stacked loops, each section's header is a Big Question the section body closes, each section ends seeding the next (Rehook). Proof modules placed at the Trust-Ladder rung the section's claim earns. Protect the illusion = zero hedging copy, no "of course this is nothing new" disclaimers. |
| **Long-form article** | Lede = novelty hook; nut graf = Stakes + the governing Big Question. Each section is a loop; subheads phrased as Big Questions; transitions are Rehooks ("which raises…"). Head Fakes appear as "the obvious answer is X — it isn't" turns. Sustained whisper across length; proof climbs toward Bullseye in the cases section. |
| **Ghostwritten thought-leadership** | The novelty hook = the proprietary *named* frame the byline owns (naming is a Step-1 novelty lever and a thought-leadership asset). Stakes framed around the leader's worldview; Big Question = the contrarian thesis. Head Fakes = the "everyone assumes X" reversals that signal expertise. Proof = the leader's own first-hand result (self-demo Bullseye). Whisper register reads as earned authority, not pitch. Reconcile hard: the named frame must never be hedged as derivative — that collapses both the novelty and the authority. |

## Quality Gate

Score against `../genius.md` rubric. This pipeline must specifically pass:

- **Three-Question Pass** — Relevant / Novel / Interesting all earn YES, and the body's Loop keeps the YES alive past the open (a hook that lands then bores = front-end success, pipeline failure).
- **Hook Density** — the open carries ≥2 components in ≤2 lines AND cleanly seeds the Loop's Stakes + Big Question.
- **Contrast Integrity** — true opposite of the avatar's real held belief, preserved when it becomes the Big Question.
- **Urgency Honesty** — any urgency window is real; fake/bolted-on = automatic ≤4.
- **Trust-Ladder Height** — proof as close to the viewer as the honest facts allow; Bullseye preferred.
- **Illusion Intact** — zero mascot reveals, with extra scrutiny on every Head Fake (a Head Fake that exposes the old mechanism = automatic ≤5).
- **Whisper Test** — Gossip Whisperer register survives the Loop's stake-escalation; any town-crier drift = cap at 6.
- **Domain Fit** — output adapted to the specific asset row above, not generic.

**HONESTY SPINE (non-negotiable):** the illusion is of NOVELTY only. Every fact, statistic, urgency window, proof example, and outcome in both the front-end hook and the body loops must be REAL. The Loop's appetite for escalating stakes and dramatic Head Fakes is the most common pressure to invent — resist it. Manufacture the *feeling* of new; never manufacture the substance. Any fabricated fact = automatic fail, no matter how addictive the piece.

**Self-check (one line):** Does the front make them LOOK, does the body make them STAY, and is every word of it true — and did no retention edit hand the viewer the mascot head?
