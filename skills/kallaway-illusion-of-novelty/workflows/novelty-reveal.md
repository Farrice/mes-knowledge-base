# /novelty-reveal — New Reveal + Outcome Mapping

Produces the first and most load-bearing layer of the Illusion of Novelty: a new-feeling angle on an old topic, mapped to an outcome the avatar demonstrably wants, expressed as 6-10 hook variants. Fire this whenever a piece needs an opener that makes a saturated/boring subject earn focus. It is the front door to the whole `/novelty-*` stack — almost everything downstream consumes its output.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `../genius.md` if it is not already hot. Then answer (these come from the genius Decision Framework — `/novelty-reveal` specifically needs 1, 2, 3):

1. **Is the topic genuinely new, or old?** Genuinely new (a real new tool/study/method) -> Path A. Old (the default for ~90% of boring niches) -> Path B. When in doubt, Path B.
2. **What outcome does the avatar actually want?** Name it as a concrete, felt result — not a category ("better health") but a specific state the avatar can picture ("more energy at 3pm than your second coffee gives you"). If you can't name it, load the avatar before proceeding (see Skill Acquisition).
3. **Who exactly is the avatar?** Outcome Mapping is only as sharp as the avatar. Vague avatar -> vague outcome -> weak reveal.

Do **not** resolve Contrast (Step 2's held-belief), urgency, or proof here. Those are downstream workflows. This file owns reveal + outcome + hooks only — though you should note the held belief if it surfaces, so `/novelty-contrast` inherits it cleanly.

## Skill Acquisition

- Always: `../genius.md` (the IP anchor — patterns 1, 3, 10 and the "naming is a Step-1 power tool" hidden-knowledge note are the operative ones here).
- Avatar/outcome precision (when the wanted outcome is fuzzy): `../../kallaway-audience-obsession/` — load the avatar payload so Outcome Mapping has a real target.
- Held-belief sourcing (optional, only if you want to pre-stage Contrast): `mcraney-deep-canvass` or the project ICP file — but resolving Contrast itself belongs to `./novelty-contrast.md`.
- Hand-off siblings this feeds: `./novelty-contrast.md` (next step), `./novelty-forge.md` (full assembly), `./novelty-hook.md` if a larger hook volume is needed.

## Execution

### 1. Determine the path

- **Path A (genuinely new):** the thing itself didn't exist or just changed in substance. Your reveal is simply "this exists / this changed." Rare. If you're on Path A, confirm the newness is *real* — fabricating a "new" thing violates the Honesty Spine.
- **Path B (old topic — default):** the topic is permanent and saturated. You are not inventing a new topic; you are finding a **new door into the old one**. The angle comes from one of five new-aspect levers:

| Lever | What you reveal | Example shape |
|---|---|---|
| New **frame** | A different lens on the same thing | "It's not a X problem, it's a Y problem" reframed as discovery, not slogan |
| New **name** | Coin a proprietary name for an old mechanic | Naming = the cheapest novelty lever (genius hidden-knowledge note) |
| New **update** | A recent change/version/method | "the new way to do X" |
| New **finding** | A specific, surprising sub-fact | "it's the *timing*, not the amount" |
| New **mechanism** | The hidden how-it-actually-works | "the reason X works has nothing to do with what you think" |

> Default to Path B. The skill of this workflow is *angle-mining*, not waiting for new topics. Supply of angles is infinite; supply of genuinely-new topics is near zero (genius pattern 1).

**Naming as a power tool:** if no obvious new aspect exists, *name* one. Giving an old mechanic a memorable proprietary name manufactures novelty by itself and makes old mechanics feel like discovered IP. Use this when the levers above come up dry.

### 2. Map the outcome

State the outcome the avatar demonstrably wants, then build the bridge from the new aspect to it. The bridge sentence answers the avatar's silent "what do I get?" Two checks:

- **Demonstrably wanted** — is this an outcome the avatar already chases (you have evidence: their searches, complaints, purchases), or one you're projecting? Project nothing.
- **Tied, not adjacent** — the new aspect must *cause* or *unlock* the outcome, not merely sit near it. "New finding about X, and separately X is good for Y" fails. "New finding about X *is* what gets you Y" lands.

> The new reveal makes them LOOK; the outcome mapping makes them STAY. A reveal with no wanted outcome is still novel-but-irrelevant, and irrelevant gets scrolled (genius pattern 3).

### 3. Apply the formula

```
reveal a new aspect of the old thing  +  tie it to an outcome the avatar demonstrably wants
```

Compress both halves into the hook so the opener does at least two jobs (New Reveal + Outcome) in 1-2 lines. A single-job hook caps the whole piece at rubric 5.

### 4. Generate 6-10 hook variants

Run the two base templates below — **vary, never verbatim** (Kallaway's explicit instruction: do not have thousands of people ship the same line). Rotate which new-aspect lever each hook leans on so the set gives the operator real choice, not six rewordings of one.

- Template 1 (vary): "*X* new thing just changed the way you get *Y*."
- Template 2 (vary): "You've heard of *X* — well, *Y* just happened that changed how you do *X* to get *Z*."

Additional vary-shapes to keep the set diverse:
- "Turns out the part of *X* that actually drives *Y* isn't the part anyone focuses on."
- "There's a newer way to handle *X* that gets you *Y* without *[the old cost]*."
- "Nobody talks about the *[new aspect]* of *X* — and it's the whole reason some people get *Y*."
- "*X* has a *[named mechanism]* most people never use, and it's what separates *Y* from *[default bad outcome]*."

Keep the tone **gossip-whisperer, not town-crier** even at the hook stage — no exclamation-point sales energy, no "HUGE." A hook that reads salesy is already losing trust before the body starts.

### 5. Self-check each hook (the two-question gate)

For every variant, confirm:
- **Does it make them LOOK?** (Is there genuine novelty — a real new aspect, not just rephrased common knowledge?)
- **Does it give a reason to STAY?** (Is the wanted outcome present and tied, so it's relevant?)

A hook that fails either half is cut, not shipped. Keep the 6-10 that pass; mark the 2-3 strongest for the operator.

### Worked mini-example — Gutter cleaning (fresh; not water/root-canal)

**Topic:** residential gutter cleaning. About as inert as it gets.

**Path:** B (old topic — gutters have been cleaned the same way forever).

**Avatar:** a homeowner, 40s, owns their house, hates ladders, has been ignoring a vague "I should deal with the gutters" guilt for two seasons.

**New aspect (lever = new finding + new mechanism):** the damage gutters cause isn't the clog you can see — it's the slow foundation seepage that starts the season *before* the clog is visible. The cleaning *timing* (late fall vs. the spring most people default to) is what actually prevents the expensive part.

**Outcome the avatar demonstrably wants:** not "clean gutters" — they want to *not* get hit with a four-figure foundation or basement-water surprise, and to stop the nagging chore-guilt.

**Bridge:** the new finding (timing-before-visible-clog) *is* what prevents the foundation bill -> directly ties the new aspect to the wanted outcome.

**Hook variants (6, varied):**
1. "Everyone cleans their gutters in spring. Turns out that's the one season it barely matters — and the timing most people skip is what saves them a five-figure foundation repair."
2. "There's a newer reason to deal with your gutters that has nothing to do with the leaves you can see, and it's the thing that quietly keeps water out of your basement."
3. "The damage from clogged gutters doesn't start at the clog. It starts a season earlier, and almost nobody handles it then."
4. "You've heard 'clean your gutters' your whole life — what changed is *when*, and the new window is the difference between a $200 job and a $9,000 one."
5. "Nobody mentions the timing of gutter cleaning, and it's the whole reason some houses never get water in the foundation and others do."
6. "Gutters have a 'pre-clog' phase most people never act on — handle that, and you skip the expensive part entirely."

Each makes them LOOK (a new finding / new mechanism, real if the cause-chain is true) and gives them a reason to STAY (avoid the foundation bill / the guilt). Held belief surfaced for `/novelty-contrast`: *"gutter cleaning is a spring chore about visible leaves."* Pass that forward.

> Honesty note on this example: ship it **only** if the seepage-timing cause-chain is factually true for the operator's region/climate. If it isn't, the *reveal* is fabricated and the whole thing fails the Honesty Spine. Manufacture the novelty, never the fact.

## Content-Type Adaptations

The reveal + outcome + hook is the same engine; how it surfaces changes per asset.

| Asset | How `/novelty-reveal` output changes |
|---|---|
| **Short-form video script** | The hook IS the first 1-2 spoken lines; reveal + outcome must land in the opening ~3 seconds before a thumb moves. Pick the single densest variant; the rest become alt-takes for re-cuts. Verbal gossip-whisper delivery, not announced. |
| **LinkedIn post** | Lead line = the chosen hook, sized for the "see more" cutoff so reveal + outcome both clear the fold. Slightly more setup allowed than video; the new-aspect lever can be a named frame the post then unpacks. No hashtag-spam, no hook stacked with emojis. |
| **X/Twitter thread** | Tweet 1 = hook carrying reveal + outcome at maximum compression (character-limited, so favor the new-finding/new-name levers — they shorten well). Hold a second variant as the quote-tweet/re-hook. Brevity forces the densest phrasing. |
| **Email** | Subject line = a hook variant tuned for inbox skim (curiosity + outcome, low-key register so it doesn't read as a blast); first line = a *different* variant so subject and open don't repeat. The reveal can build across the first two sentences rather than one. |
| **Ad / VSL** | Hook becomes the scroll-stopper / first frame; reveal + outcome must pre-frame the entire pitch. Choose the variant whose wanted-outcome most directly maps to the offer. Keep whisper register — salesy hooks tank ad trust fastest. Pair tightly with the proof step downstream. |
| **Sales / Landing page** | Hero headline = the reveal-as-discovery; subhead = the outcome bridge. The new-aspect lever often becomes a *named mechanism* the page sells around (naming power tool). Above-the-fold must answer LOOK + STAY before the CTA. |
| **Long-form article** | Headline + dek carry reveal + outcome; the lever frequently becomes the article's organizing frame or coined name. The opener can afford a beat of context, but the new aspect must appear in the title, not buried in paragraph three. |
| **Ghostwritten thought-leadership** | Reveal is framed as *the author's* observed insight or coined frame (first-person discovery), not reported news. Lower-key, more authoritative whisper; the new-name lever is especially strong here — a leader naming a phenomenon reads as IP. Tie to the outcome *their specific audience* wants, sourced from the client's avatar, not a generic one. |

## Output Requirements

Return:
1. **Path** (A or B) with a one-line justification.
2. **New aspect** — the specific lever used (frame / name / update / finding / mechanism) and the one-sentence reveal.
3. **Wanted outcome** — named concretely, with the one-sentence bridge tying aspect -> outcome.
4. **6-10 hook variants**, each labeled with the lever it leans on; the 2-3 strongest flagged.
5. **Held belief surfaced** (one line, passed forward to `/novelty-contrast.md`), if one emerged.
6. **Honesty flag** — one line naming any factual claim in the reveal that must be verified-true before shipping.

Keep it scannable. No prose padding around the deliverables.

## Quality Gate

Score against `../genius.md` rubric. The criteria this workflow must pass:

- **#2 Hook Density** — opener carries New Reveal + Outcome (>=2 components) in <=2 lines. Single-job hook caps at 5.
- **#7 Whisper Test** — every hook is gossip-whisperer, not town-crier. No exclamation-point sales register, no "HUGE NEWS." Salesy tone caps at 6.
- **#9 Domain Fit** — hooks are adapted to the actual topic and asset, not generic template fills.
- **#1 Three-Question Pass (partial)** — this step must deliver Relevant + Novel honestly; Interesting is Contrast's job, so don't over-claim it here.

Anti-patterns that auto-fail this file: naked new claims with no wanted outcome attached; single-job hooks; six rewordings of one hook instead of a varied set; town-crier/exclamation tone; assuming an outcome the avatar doesn't demonstrably want.

**Honesty Spine (non-negotiable):** the illusion is of **novelty only**. The new aspect must be a *real* aspect of the topic — facts, findings, mechanisms, and any implied urgency or proof must be true. Manufacture the *feeling* of new; never manufacture the *fact*. A fabricated reveal is an automatic fail no matter how good the hook reads.

**One-line self-check:** *Does each shipped hook make a stranger LOOK (real novelty) and give them a reason to STAY (a wanted outcome), in a whisper, without a single invented fact?*
