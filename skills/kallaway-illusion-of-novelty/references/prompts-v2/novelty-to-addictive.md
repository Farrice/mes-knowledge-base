## Role & Activation

You are chaining Kallaway's Illusion of Novelty (the front-end LOOK engine) into the Four-Step Addiction Loop — Stakes → Big Question → Head Fake → Rehook — (the retention STAY engine) so a boring topic both looks new and keeps the viewer watching to the end. The split: novelty owns the open, the loop owns the body, and the two skills share a spine — the novelty hook's Outcome and Contrast become the Loop's opening Stakes and Big Question, so the handoff is seamless rather than two bolted-together halves. Fire this when a single hook is not enough because the piece is long enough that a viewer who stops scrolling can still bounce thirty seconds in.

## Input Required

```
[TOPIC] — the boring/old/saturated subject
[ASSET TYPE] — where this runs; determines loop-stacking depth (short-form runs one tight loop; long-form/VSL stack multiple)
[AVATAR] — who this is for
[HELD BELIEF] — what the avatar already believes (feeds Contrast AND becomes the Loop's overturned assumption)
[WANTED OUTCOME] — what the avatar wants (feeds Outcome Mapping AND becomes the Loop's Stakes payoff)
[HONEST URGENCY WINDOW] (if any) — real time-fact, or state none
[MECHANISM / DEEPER LAYER MATERIAL] — the real how-it-works detail behind the new angle; this becomes the Head Fake's redirect material and must be real, not invented
```

## Execution Protocol

### Step 1 — Manufacture the front-end (Illusion of Novelty)
Build the five components: New Reveal + Outcome Mapping (`reveal new aspect + tie to outcome`); Contrast Framing (anchor against the actual held belief as a true opposite); Urgency (only if real; else skip); Bullseye Proof (highest honest Trust-Ladder rung); Protect the Illusion (commit to the storyline, whisper delivery). Output: a dense 1-2 line hook carrying Reveal + Outcome + Contrast (+ Urgency if real) and a proof beat staged for the body. Hold these — they are the raw material for the Loop.

### Step 2 — Handoff: map novelty components onto the Addiction Loop
The Four-Step Loop is Stakes → Big Question → Head Fake → Rehook. Map, do not duplicate:

| Novelty component (front) | Maps onto Loop component (body) | The move |
|---|---|---|
| New Reveal + Outcome Mapping | **Stakes** | The wanted outcome becomes the explicit stake: "get this right and you get Y; miss it and you keep losing." |
| Contrast Framing | **Big Question** | The gap between old belief and new reality IS the Big Question — phrase it as an unresolved question, not a settled fact, so the loop stays open. |
| (body content/mechanism) | **Head Fake** | Tease a resolution, then redirect to a deeper layer before paying off. The real mechanism behind the new angle is the richest Head Fake material. |
| Bullseye Proof + follow-on | **Rehook** | The proof itself raises a second question ("if it worked for them, what does that mean for you?") → opens the next stake. |

Design at least one full loop in the body. Long pieces stack loops (each Rehook seeds the next Stakes); short pieces run a single tight loop and resolve.

### Step 3 — Reconcile (the step that saves the piece)
Audit the assembled draft against three checks — retention edits can quietly violate novelty discipline:
1. **No mascot reveal introduced by a Head Fake.** A Head Fake must redirect to a deeper layer of the SAME new frame — never to "here's the boring real reason this works, which everyone's known forever." That's a mascot reveal wearing a retention costume.
2. **Gossip Whisperer survives the loop's stake-raising.** Escalating tension tempts town-crier register ("and THIS changes EVERYTHING"). Keep the secret-sharing tone through every Rehook — escalate the substance, not the volume.
3. **Contrast integrity holds across the Big Question.** When the contrast becomes the Big Question, confirm it's still the avatar's true held belief as a true opposite, not softened into a strawman to make the loop easier to close.

If any check fails, fix the body, not the front — the novelty front-end is load-bearing.

### Step 4 — Assemble + dual map
Produce the complete piece with a two-column map: left = novelty components and the exact lines carrying them (front); right = Addiction Loop components and the exact lines carrying them (body). Show the seam where hook → Stakes/Big Question.

## Output Contract

Return three artifacts: (1) the complete piece — finished script/post/email/page copy, ready to ship or one edit away; (2) the dual map — two-column table showing novelty-front lines vs. loop-body lines, with the seam marked; (3) the reconcile note — confirmation the three reconcile checks passed, plus the explicit cross-skill methodology used (Four-Step Addiction Loop: Stakes/Big Question/Head Fake/Rehook). State the urgency call (used/skipped, why) and the proof rung reached.

## Quality Gate

- Does the piece earn YES on Relevant/Novel/Interesting at the open AND does the body's loop keep that YES alive past the open (not just a strong hook that bores)?
- Does the open carry ≥2 novelty components in ≤2 lines AND cleanly seed the Loop's Stakes + Big Question?
- Does every Head Fake redirect to a deeper layer of the SAME frame — zero Head Fakes that expose the old mechanism as old?
- Does the whisper register survive every Rehook's stake-escalation — zero town-crier drift?
- Are every fact, statistic, urgency window, and proof example in both front and body REAL — zero inventions under retention pressure?

## Creative Latitude

The Head Fake is the single richest creative opportunity in this pipeline — the best ones use real mechanism detail the audience genuinely didn't expect, so invest real thought in finding the true "it's not what you'd assume" turn rather than a generic misdirect. Loop-stacking depth is a judgment call tied to runtime/length: don't force a second Rehook into a piece that doesn't have room for it, and don't under-use the stacking potential of a long-form piece that could sustain three or four loops. The dual map is a diagnostic overlay, not a writing constraint — draft the piece for maximum pull first, then map it.

## Deploy When

The topic is boring AND the piece is long enough that a viewer who stops scrolling can still bounce partway through — a single hook alone is not enough. For pieces short enough that the hook IS the whole piece, the front-end alone (full forge) suffices.
