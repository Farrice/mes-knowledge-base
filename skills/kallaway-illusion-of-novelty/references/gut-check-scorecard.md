# The Gut-Check Scorecard (canonical audit instrument)

> Source-of-truth: `./illusion-of-novelty-doc.md` Section 8. Use this whenever you score a hook/script/asset for novelty (it is what `./../workflows/novelty-audit.md` runs). Score each component 0–2 for a /10, then run the two overrides. Honesty spine is non-negotiable: only the *novelty* is manufactured — facts, urgency windows, and proof stay REAL.

## The scoring table (0–2 each, 10 total)

| # | Component | 0 points | 1 point | 2 points |
|---|-----------|----------|---------|----------|
| 1 | **New Reveal** | Topic presented as-is; nothing framed as new or changed | Something framed as new, but vague or buried late | Clear reveal of a new thing OR new angle, in the hook zone |
| 2 | **Outcome Mapping** | Reveal not tied to anything the viewer wants | Outcome implied but generic ("this is great") | Reveal explicitly tied to a specific outcome the viewer wants |
| 3 | **Contrast Framing** | No old belief referenced | Old way mentioned, but not a true opposite / not next to the new angle | Old belief and new angle in the same breath, true opposites |
| 4 | **Urgency** | FAKE urgency present (worse than none — flag it) | No urgency, and none was genuinely available (fine — take the point) | Genuine recency or closing window, with a "when" |
| 5 | **Bullseye Proof** | No proof at all | Cold third-party proof only (study/stat) | Warm-crowd or bullseye proof — someone the viewer sees themselves in |

### The counter-intuitive rule that matters most (Urgency)
An **honest skip earns 1 point, not 0.** A piece with no genuine urgency window caps at **9/10** — and that is *correct*, not a deficiency. Only **fake/bolted-on urgency scores 0** (it is worse than none). Never coach a writer to invent a window to chase the 10th point.

## Override 1 — Illusion Integrity check (PASS/FAIL, overrides the number)
A high score means nothing if the illusion leaks. Two binary gates:
- **Mascot showing?** Does ANY line hedge, downplay, or admit the idea is old ("this is really just…", "as you probably know…", "this has been around forever, but…", "to be fair, this isn't new…")? → **FAIL.** Locate the line, cut it, re-score.
- **Town Crier delivery?** Does it announce like a billboard ("HUGE NEWS, this changes EVERYTHING") instead of whispering a secret? → rewrite those lines to the Gossip Whisperer register.

## Override 2 — The 3-question sanity test (Section 1)
Would the *target viewer* say YES to all three: something they **care about**, something they **haven't heard before**, something they **want to learn more about**? Any NO points straight at the weak component.

## How to deliver an audit (the canonical output order)
1. **Score table first** — the 5-row table with each component's 0/1/2 and the /10 total. (In Gut Check mode, always lead with this, before any commentary.)
2. **Illusion Integrity verdict** — PASS/FAIL + any mascot/town-crier lines quoted.
3. **The 1–2 highest-leverage fixes only** — not a laundry list. Fix the biggest leaks first.
4. **Side-by-side rewrites** — weak line → rewritten line, originals preserved. Do NOT rewrite the whole script.

## Score reading
- **9–10 (+ Integrity PASS):** ship it. (9 is the honest ceiling when no real urgency window exists.)
- **6–8:** one or two components are carrying 1s — route each to its repair workflow (`./../workflows/novelty-reveal.md`, `-contrast`, `-urgency`, `-proof`, `-protect`).
- **≤5, or any Integrity FAIL:** the piece is leaking or under-built — rebuild the failing zone before polishing anything else.

## Relationship to the deeper rubric
This scorecard is the fast, canonical gut-check. `../genius.md`'s 9-criterion rubric adds supplementary checks the source scorecard doesn't grade explicitly — hook *density* (≥2 components in ≤2 lines), domain-fit (adapted to the actual asset), and the standalone honesty-spine veto. Use the scorecard for the go/no-go; use the rubric when you want depth on *why* a 1 is a 1.
