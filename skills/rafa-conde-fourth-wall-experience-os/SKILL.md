# Rafa Conde: Fourth-Wall Experience OS

This companion skill turns Rafa Conde's Metal Gear Solid / Psycho Mantis insight into a deployable creative operating system: build moments where the audience realizes the experience can see beyond its expected frame. The goal is not "storytelling." The goal is perspective shift: the product, content, agent, presentation, or client experience briefly acknowledges the user's real context, medium, behavior, assumptions, or environment in a way that feels surprising, tasteful, and true.

Use this skill when an idea needs to feel alive, authored, human, and impossible to ignore.
