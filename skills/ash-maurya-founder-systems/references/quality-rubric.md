# Quality Rubric

| Criterion | 4 - Usable | 7 - Strong | 10 - Mastery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem Specificity | Names a market pain | Anchors pain in recent behavior | Shows a costly, triggered, repeated struggle with current workarounds |
| Early Adopter Clarity | Defines a broad ICP | Defines a reachable first segment | Identifies customers with strongest push, active search, and commitment potential |
| Customer Forces | Lists pros and cons | Maps push, pull, inertia, friction | Shows which force must be amplified or reduced to trigger switching |
| MVP Quality | Reduces features | Defines the 5P decisions | Makes problem, prospect, promise, price, and packaging mutually coherent |
| Experiment Design | Suggests research | Tests one assumption at a time | Uses the smallest behavior test with clear pass/fail thresholds |
| Offer Strength | Makes a pitch | States outcome, demo, price | Breaks the old way, proves the new path, and removes the biggest anxiety |
| Evidence Discipline | Uses anecdotes | Uses behavioral interviews and commitments | Separates opinion, intent, behavior, and paid commitment with no signal inflation |
| Commercial Leverage | Produces advice | Produces a validation plan | Produces a path to paying customers before overbuilding |
| Question Framing | Asks broad or leading questions | Uses past-tense behavioral prompts | Reconstructs trigger, journey, chosen solution, rejected alternatives, and tradeoffs |
| Old-Way Mining | Names competitors | Studies current alternatives | Extracts pet peeves, struggles, workarounds, and switching triggers from existing solutions |
| Buying Criteria | Lists desired features | Identifies stated selection criteria | Predicts buying by mapping what was hired, fired, feared, and paid for |
| Insight Grounding | Interprets loosely | Quotes customer facts | Audits every decision against transcript-grounded facts and repeated patterns |
