# Ash Maurya: Founder Systems Mastery

Ash Maurya's core move is turning entrepreneurship from inspirational guessing into a staged learning system. The business model is treated as the product, customer behavior is treated as truth, and the founder's job is to move from assumptions to commitments before overbuilding.
