# Tom Segura: Observational Comedy & Storytelling Craft

Extracted from Tom Segura's "How I Write" deep-dive interview. Segura is a working A-list observational/storytelling comedian (*Completely Normal*, *Sledgehammer*, *Teacher*; creator of Netflix's *Bad Thoughts*; author). This skill captures the **practitioner's process** — where material comes from, how a notebook fragment becomes a finished bit, and how it's performed.

> **Load [genius.md](genius.md) before any workflow.** It holds the 15 genius patterns, 10 hidden-knowledge insights, exemplars, signature moves, and the quality rubric every workflow references.

## Positioning vs. Robert Mack (no overlap)
- **Robert Mack** = comedy *mechanics for business deployment* (joke architecture, virality, humor-in-marketing). Assumes you already have the bit.
- **Tom Segura** = the *upstream craft* — the antenna that finds material, the story "way in," the long-to-tight edit, playing-it-straight, performance presence. **Where the bit comes from.**
- **They stack.** See `comedy-stack` (T3).

## The One-Line Thesis
*Comedy is the discipline of noticing what everyone has experienced but no one has articulated — then earning the right to say it, grounding the absurd in the real, and holding the tension until it breaks.*
