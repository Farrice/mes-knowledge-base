## TOP 3 — "get it out, someone's going to say this" (digs expanded)

### 🥇 #3 — The real tell is the confidence (Dev 34)
**Surface complaint** (dead): "AI writing has tells — em-dashes, emojis, 'delveّ.'"
**The dig** (Hidden 3 — past the obvious to the irrational core): Everyone's hunting the *cosmetic* tells. The real tell is that every AI post is **certain**. It states. It never says "I think," "maybe," "I'm still figuring this out." Why does that bother me? Because **real expertise is mostly doubt** — the expert you trust is the one who says "it depends." AI made everyone sound like they've got it figured out, so the one signal that used to mark a real expert — *visible uncertainty* — now reads as weakness, and we trained ourselves to delete the exact thing that made us trustworthy. **The irrational core: we're using a confidence machine to erase the doubt that was our credential.** The most human thing you can post now is "I'm not sure yet." *(Ties directly to Farrice's "say I don't know" value + the Invisible Expert ICP.)*

### 🥈 #11 — Everyone got jacked in the same month (Dev 34)
**Surface complaint** (dead): "AI improved everyone's writing."
**The dig**: Notice nobody *mentions* it. There's a collective, unspoken non-aggression pact — *I won't point out your writing transformed in March if you don't point out mine did.* Why does that bother me? **The pretense is the tell, not the tool.** Nobody's embarrassed about *using* AI; we're embarrassed about the silent agreement to pretend we don't — and the energy spent maintaining the fiction (the de-slopping, the "make it sound human" prompts) is more work than just saying it. **The irrational core: the whole feed is in on a secret everyone already knows, building elaborate theater to hide a thing that isn't even shameful.** The funniest framing: imagine if everyone's body got ripped the same month and we all just... didn't bring it up.

### 🥉 #1 — The anti-slop post is the slop (Dev 33)
**Surface complaint** (dead): "Ironic that 'avoid AI tells' posts sound AI-written."
**The dig**: It's not irony — it's a trap. "11 prompts to sound human" *is* the slop: listicle, hook, framework, almost certainly AI-assisted. Why does this bother me? Because **outrage at sameness is the most same content on the platform**, and it out-engages original work — so the market is rewarding meta-slop over actual voice, the exact thing everyone claims to want. You can't sell "be original" as a framework; the moment it's a framework, it isn't original. **The irrational core: the only un-fakeable move left is to post something so specifically *you* that no prompt could generate it — and almost nobody does, because that's scarier than complaining.**
