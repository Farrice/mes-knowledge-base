# /segura-arc — Earned Provocation Arc

Takes provocative material plus its container (set, essay, thread) and orders it so the audience is on your side *before* the dangerous beat lands. Deploys **Pattern 7 (Earned Provocation Arc — Build Trust, Then Risk)** and **Hidden Knowledge 5 (Provocation as Strategic Asset)**: front-load goodwill, escalate risk only once trust is banked ("minute 25, not minute 1"), and for a single hot take, state the unacceptable line *first*, then drag them to it with jokes.

## When to Use
- A piece carries a genuinely risky angle, hot take, or line you know the audience will resist.
- Material is already drafted/generated but lands flat or alienating because the risk fires too early.
- A set, essay, or thread needs sequencing across multiple beats of escalating provocation.

## When NOT to Use
- The material has no risk to spend — pure recognition or warm autobiography. Route to the relevant production workflow instead.
- The bit doesn't exist yet. Develop it first (long-version-first pipeline), then sequence here.

## Pre-Flight Gate
Load genius.md. If the provocation isn't yours (a derivative hot take), apply **Pattern 13 (Story Ownership)** and pivot to the unrepeatable autobiographical version before sequencing.

## Execution
1. **Isolate the risk.** Generate a one-line statement of the most provocative beat — the exact line they "can't believe you'd say." Mark this the **RISK-SPENT** moment. Everything else sequences around it.
2. **Build the trust runway.** Produce 2-4 front-loaded goodwill beats — recognition, warm autobiography, the disarming calm of **Pattern 14**. These bank rapport before any risk fires. Mark the point where the account is full: **TRUST-BUILT**.
3. **Choose the structure.** For a multi-beat arc, escalate risk gradually past TRUST-BUILT toward RISK-SPENT (the minute-25 principle). For a single hot take, switch to **statement-first**: render the unacceptable line cold up front (so it can't read as your real position), then deploy the supporting jokes that drag them to it.
4. **Dig the support.** Behind RISK-SPENT, install **The Annoyance Dig** — expand on *why* the line is true past the obvious, to the irrational core. The jokes that follow the statement are what flip resistance into reluctant agreement (**Pattern 6**, the against-judgment laugh).
5. **Install the way in.** Apply **Pattern 4**: front-load the reason-for-telling so the audience is invested before the risk arrives. Cut any beat that spends risk before trust is banked.
6. **Render the beat map.** Produce the ordered sequence with each beat numbered, its function labeled, and **TRUST-BUILT** + **RISK-SPENT** explicitly marked. Confirm no risk fires before TRUST-BUILT.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Content Type | How this shifts |
|---|---|
| Stand-up / spoken | Literal minute-25 placement; trust runway = warm opening bits, silence held for authority (Pattern 14). |
| Essay / newsletter (Parallax) | Trust runway = recognition + memoir interiority; risk lands in the back third after the way-in earns it. |
| LinkedIn / social | Compress: 1-2 goodwill lines, then statement-first for the hot take so the scroll-stopper line opens cold. |
| Sales / DR copy | Trust = proof and shared frustration; the provocative claim sits after credibility, never in the lead. |
| Video / sketch script | Front-load rapport beats; hold the real moment before the risk (Pattern 9) so duration banks tension. |

## Output Requirements
- **Format**: Ordered beat map — numbered beats, each with a function label, plus a one-line note on which structure (escalation vs statement-first) was chosen and why.
- **Must include**: An explicit **TRUST-BUILT** marker and an explicit **RISK-SPENT** marker; the isolated risk line; the supporting dig behind it.
- **Ends with**: The biggest beat — confirm the risk pays off rather than alienates, and that nothing risky precedes TRUST-BUILT.
