# /segura-develop — Long-First Edit

Runs a raw idea or rough draft through Segura's four explicit rounds — fat version, cut, insert, end-on-biggest — producing a finished bit/essay/script where nothing is removable and the ending is the strongest beat. Deploys **Pattern 10: Long-Version-First, Edit-Second** as the spine.

## When to Use
- A raw idea, voice note, or rough draft exists and needs development into finished, tight material.
- The piece feels bloated, shapeless, or front-loads its best line.
- Punches are clustered or absent and the ending fizzles.

## When NOT to Use
- No raw seed exists yet — route to the Antenna/ideation workflow (Pattern 1, Pattern 3) first.
- Refining a single line's word choice only — route to the polish workflow (Pattern 11).
- Sequencing multiple finished bits into a set — route to the arc workflow (Pattern 7).

## Pre-Flight Gate
Load genius.md. This workflow is governed by **Pattern 10 (Long-First, Edit-Second)**, **Pattern 5 (Just Say Them All)**, **Pattern 6 (Surprise)**, and **Hidden Knowledge 2 (The Performance Co-Authors the Material)**; primary move is **Long-First, Edit-Second**. Disqualifier: if the input has no autobiographical or observational charge and is a derivative take on a crowded topic, halt and route to ownership-checking (Pattern 13) — developing a derivative seed only produces a polished derivative.

## Skill Acquisition
- Load: skills/tom-segura-comedy-storytelling/genius.md — Patterns **5, 6, 10**; Hidden Knowledge **2**; Moves **Long-First Edit-Second**, **Just Say Them All**, **The Annoyance Dig**.

## Execution
1. **Round 1 — Generate the FAT version.** Produce the bloated 7–10-minute / full-length telling with **zero precommitment to shape** (Pattern 10). Dump every thought including the embarrassing ones (Pattern 5: Just Say Them All) and write it as if performing aloud, letting the live register surface lines the desk wouldn't (Hidden 2). Do not edit while generating.
2. **Round 2 — Find the payoff, then CUT to it.** Identify the single biggest beat the piece is actually walking toward. Cut everything the payoff doesn't need — repeated setups, hedges, tangents, throat-clearing. Where a complaint sits flat, dig past the surface to its irrational core before cutting (Move: The Annoyance Dig). Stop when nothing left is removable.
3. **Round 3 — INSERT punches at the gaps.** Walk the cut spine and mark dead air between laughs. Install a punch at each gap, building anticipation one direction then swerving (Pattern 6: Surprise). Where possible, engineer the against-judgment laugh — the one they don't want to give. Distribute punches; no two-minute dry stretch.
4. **Round 4 — END on the biggest beat.** Confirm the strongest punch lands last (Pattern 10). If the biggest beat sits mid-piece, restructure so the climb runs uphill to it. Render the final line for maximum surprise.
5. **Stage-test pass.** Read the result aloud end-to-end. Wherever the live read coughs up a better line than the page (Hidden 2), swap it in. Re-confirm the ending still hits hardest.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Content Type | How this shifts |
|---|---|
| Stand-up / spoken | Round 1 is literally told aloud and transcribed; Round 4 ends on the tag, not the premise. |
| Essay / newsletter (Parallax) | Fat version = the over-written draft; Round 3 punches = recognition turns and surprise reversals, not jokes; ends on the line that reframes. |
| LinkedIn / social | Compress all four rounds into one post; Round 2 cut is brutal (kill the second half); the biggest beat becomes the last line before the CTA. |
| Sales / DR copy | Punches in Round 3 are proof and objection-flips; the biggest beat is the offer's sharpest claim, placed last. |
| Video / sketch script | Round 2 cut respects held-real beats (don't trim the tension); biggest beat = the final reveal shot. |

## Output Requirements
- **Format**: The finished piece, plus a one-line tag per round noting what was generated, cut, inserted, and confirmed-as-ending.
- **Must include**: A distributed punch map (no dry stretch), at least one swerve the reader won't see coming (Pattern 6), and a visible biggest-beat ending.
- **Ends with**: The single strongest beat in the piece. Never a question signoff, never a recap.

## Quality Gate
Score against the genius.md rubric (name the anchor for any score ≥8). Reject if any of these anti-patterns are present: **Comprehensive but dead** (everything said plainly, no surprise/stakes), **Premature editing** (filtered in round one before the fat version existed), **Stopping at the obvious** (surface complaint instead of the dig), or **Bloated, no shape** (Edit-discipline failure — something is still removable, punches clustered, or the ending isn't the biggest beat). Retry the weakest round once before delivery.
