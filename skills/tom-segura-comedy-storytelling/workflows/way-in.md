# /segura-way-in — The Way In

Produces an engineered opening that installs the *reason-for-telling* and stakes ahead of the events, turning a flat anecdote into a leaning-in one. Deploys **Pattern 4: The "Way In"** — "the reason for telling it is just as important as the story itself."

## When to Use
- A true story, anecdote, case study, or founder narrative that lands as "yeah, that's cool" instead of pulling the reader in.
- An essay or post that opens cold on events with no frame or stakes.
- Any piece where the facts are good but the audience arrives uninvested.

## When NOT to Use
- The angle itself is dead (no opinion, no temperature) — route to `/segura-dig` or the Annoyance Dig first.
- The story isn't yours / is derivative — route to ownership work (Pattern 13) before framing.
- The opening already lands and just needs polish — route to the Word Swap / `/segura-words`.

## Pre-Flight Gate
Load genius.md. This workflow is governed by **Pattern 4 (The "Way In")**, **Exemplar 1 (Subway spokesman)**, **Exemplar 4 (apartment-smells-like-weed stakes transfer)**, and the **Move: The "Way In" Install**. Disqualifier: if the input has no stakes available even when interrogated (nothing was actually at risk, nothing changed, nobody cared), it cannot be framed — surface that and route to a different premise. If the problem is a weak *angle* rather than a weak *opening*, this is the wrong workflow.

## Execution
1. **Interrogate the reason.** Ask the input one question: "Why am I telling this, *right now*?" Produce 2-3 candidate answers (what was at risk, what it proved, what it threatened, who was watching). Keep the one with the most personal exposure.
2. **Locate the stakes.** Name what the narrator stood to gain or lose *before the events* — the relationship, the self-image, the proof they were chasing. Per Exemplar 4, the same facts in a different stakes-universe become a different story.
3. **Render the flat version.** Write the anecdote opening cold on events, no frame. This is the control — label it **FLAT**.
4. **Install the Way In.** Write the frame that precedes the first event: gratitude/context, the thing that mattered, the expectation about to be tested. Mirror Exemplar 1's move — never open "one time I almost was the Subway spokesman"; open with "I thought the highlight of my career was..." Then narrate. Label it **FRAMED**.
5. **Cut the frame to its load-bearing minimum.** Strip any setup sentence the stakes don't need. The Way In installs *weight*, not throat-clearing.
6. **Render side by side.** Present FLAT and FRAMED together so the lift is visible, then state in one line what the frame bought (investment before event one).
7. **Verify the swerve survives.** Confirm the events still pay off the installed reason. Per the rubric, the audience must be invested before the first event lands; if removing the Way In doesn't measurably deflate the open, dig deeper into stakes and re-install.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Content Type | How this shifts |
|---|---|
| Stand-up / spoken | Frame is delivered as confession/gratitude before the bit; the Way In buys silence to lean into (Pattern 14). |
| Essay / newsletter (Parallax) | **Primary mode.** Open on the stakes-paragraph (Exemplar 4 apartment open), not the scene. Interiority replaces the spoken aside — the reason-for-telling becomes the first beat. |
| LinkedIn / social | Compress the Way In into the hook line: the thing at risk *is* the scroll-stopper, events follow below the fold. |
| Sales / DR copy | The Way In = why this story matters to the *reader's* outcome; install the reader's stakes, not just the narrator's. |
| Video / sketch script | Open on the frame as a held real beat (Pattern 9) before the inciting event; let the stakes sit before the cut. |

## Output Requirements
- **Format**: Two labeled blocks — **FLAT** (control) and **FRAMED** (engineered) — followed by a one-line lift note.
- **Must include**: An explicit answer to "Why am I telling this, right now?"; the named stakes; the load-bearing frame preceding event one.
- **Ends with**: The FRAMED opening, ready to narrate from — not commentary about it.

## Quality Gate
Score against the genius.md rubric (name the anchor for any score ≥8 — for **The Way In**, the anchor is "reason-for-telling installed; audience invested before the first event"). Reject if any present: **the flat anecdote** (events with no Way In, Anti-Pattern 2); **comprehensive but dead** (stakes stated plainly with no investment, Anti-Pattern 9); **the derivative middle** (a frame that isn't unrepeatably yours, Anti-Pattern 7). Retry the weakest element once before delivery.
