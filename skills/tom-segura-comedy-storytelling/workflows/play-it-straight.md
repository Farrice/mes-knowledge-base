# /segura-straight — Play It Straight

Produces the played-straight version of an absurd premise: the more ridiculous the idea, the MORE sincere the delivery. Deploys Pattern 8 (Play It Straight — Ground the Absurd) and Pattern 9 (Hold On The Real) so the deadpan realness, not the winking, does the work.

## When to Use
- An absurd, exaggerated, or satirical premise that the draft is currently over-selling with jokey signaling.
- Sketch / scene / video where casting and pacing decide whether the bit lands.
- Any content "so big it begs to be over-sold" — and you want to do the opposite.

## When NOT to Use
- The premise is grounded/mundane and needs a reason-for-telling → route to `/segura-way-in` (Pattern 4).
- The annoyance hasn't been mined to its irrational core → route to `/segura-dig` (Hidden 3).
- The piece needs structural development from raw → route to `/segura-develop` (Pattern 10).

## Pre-Flight Gate
Load genius.md. This workflow is governed by **Pattern 8 (Play It Straight)** and **Pattern 9 (Hold On The Real)**, calibrated against **Exemplar 3** (dramatic-actor casting + 25-second door hold). Disqualifier: if the input premise is realistic rather than absurd, there is nothing to ground straight — re-route. The operating law: **"it's funnier if they're not trying to be funny."**

## Skill Acquisition
- Load: skills/tom-segura-comedy-storytelling/genius.md — Patterns **8, 9**; Move **Play It Straight**; Exemplar **3**.

## Execution
1. **Isolate the absurd core.** Name the single most ridiculous element in one flat sentence (the "comedian sells inconvenient merch" equivalent from Exemplar 3). This is what gets played straight.
2. **Produce the over-sold version FIRST.** Render the premise the way a draft instinctively does it — winking narration, jokey adjectives, signaling "this is a joke." Label it clearly. This is the foil, per **Anti-Pattern 5 (Over-selling the absurd)**.
3. **Cast the dramatic register.** Per **Exemplar 3**, swap the comedy voice for the dramatic one — deliver the absurd material as the most sincere thing said all day. Strip every wink, every nudge, every "obviously this is bonkers" tell.
4. **Render it grounded (Pattern 8).** Generate the played-straight version: real stakes, real cadence, sincere conviction. The character believes this completely. No line may signal "joke."
5. **Hold the real beat (Pattern 9).** Find the moment instinct says to cut to the payoff, and instead HOLD — extend the unhurried real beat past comfortable (the 25-second door hold). Mark the held beat explicitly so a performer/reader sits in the tension.
6. **Run the wink-strip diagnostic.** Scan the straight version line by line; cut any word, aside, or stage direction that betrays the bit is a bit. If a line tries to be funny, neutralize it.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Content Type | How this shifts |
|---|---|
| Stand-up / spoken | Deliver the absurd as confession, not punchline. Hold the pause where the laugh wants to start. |
| Essay / newsletter (Parallax) | Write the ridiculous premise in earnest, interiority-grade prose — no irony tags. The straight face is on the page. |
| LinkedIn / social | Drop the absurd claim in flat, sincere founder-voice. Resist the emoji-wink and the "lol" deflate. |
| Sales / DR copy | State the outlandish promise with total conviction; let proof (not jokey hedging) carry the weight. |
| Video / sketch script | Cast the dramatic actor for the dumbest material; write the long-held shot (coffee one-shot / door hold) into the directions. |

## Output Requirements
- **Format**: Two labeled blocks — **OVER-SOLD (foil)** then **PLAYED STRAIGHT (ship this)** — plus a one-line note on which beat to hold and for how long.
- **Must include**: The dramatic-register casting/voice call; at least one explicitly marked held beat (Pattern 9); a wink-strip confirmation.
- **Ends with**: The straight version standing alone, signaling nothing — the deadpan IS the joke.

## Quality Gate
Score against the genius.md rubric (name the anchor for any score ≥8 — e.g. **Realness/Grounding 10**: "played 100% straight; the real beat held longer than comfortable"). Reject if any of these anti-patterns are present: **#5 Over-selling the absurd** (winking, playing for the joke), **#6 Rushing the beat** (cutting away before the real tension builds), **#9 Comprehensive but dead** (everything stated plainly with no surprise or grounding). Retry the weakest element once before delivery.
