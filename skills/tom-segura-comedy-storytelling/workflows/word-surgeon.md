# /segura-words — Word Surgeon

Produces the finished, polished passage by replacing flat words with the funniest-*sounding* specific and rendering characters' actual speech instead of paraphrase. Deploys **Pattern 11: Word-Choice as Sound Engineering** and **Pattern 12: Voices & Accents Paint the Picture** via the **Move: The Word Swap**.

## When to Use
- A draft is **structurally done** (stakes, dig, shape all set) and needs the last-mile sound/voice polish.
- Verbs read flat ("pulled," "hit," "said") and the line should *sound* funnier, not just mean the same thing.
- A story contains **other people** whose speech is currently summarized ("she asked if I needed help").

## When NOT to Use
- Structure is still soft (no "way in," no dig). Route to the development/edit workflow first; sound polish cannot rescue a dead structure.
- Pure factual/technical copy with no comedic or character surface.

## Pre-Flight Gate
Load genius.md. This workflow runs **only Patterns 11 and 12** plus the **Word Swap** move. Disqualify the input if structure is incomplete (Pattern 4 "Way In" missing, Hidden 3 "Dig" not done); sound is the *final* pass, never a substitute for shape. A bloated or shapeless draft routes back to **Long-First, Edit-Second** before returning here.

## Skill Acquisition
- Load: `skills/tom-segura-comedy-storytelling/genius.md` — Patterns **11** (Word-Choice as Sound Engineering), **12** (Voices & Accents Paint the Picture); Moves **The Word Swap**.

## Execution
1. **Inventory the flat words.** Scan the draft and extract every pivotal verb, noun, and reported-speech line that is doing comedic or pictorial work. Flag the limp defaults: "pulled," "hit," "grabbed," "said," "told me," "asked."
2. **Swap for the funniest-sounding specific** (Pattern 11). Replace each flat word with the higher-energy specific where the *sound* lands: "pulled" → "**yanked**"; "hit" → "**clipped**" / "**tugged**"; "walked fast" → "**book it**." Test the **sound**, not just the meaning. Mine vivid phrasings the way Segura banks lines he overhears off-stage.
3. **Apply the deflation test** (Pattern 11 success metric). For each swap, mentally swap the word *back* to the boring option. Keep the swap **only if the line measurably deflates** without it. Cut any swap that's just a flashier synonym carrying no extra charge.
4. **Render the voices** (Pattern 12). Find every paraphrased character line and rewrite it as the person's **actual speech, accent, and cadence**. "She asked if I needed help" → "'**Are you looking for something?**'" in her real register. Render specific speech; do not summarize it.
5. **Generate the swap table.** Produce a table (*flat → funnier → reason*) logging each word/sound change and each voice render, with the one-line reason (sound, picture, deflation).
6. **Rewrite the passage clean.** Produce the final passage with every kept swap and rendered voice installed, reading as finished delivery-ready prose.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Content Type | How this shifts |
|---|---|
| Stand-up / spoken | Maximize sound; rehearse swaps aloud. Voices performed in full accent/cadence. |
| Essay / newsletter (Parallax) | Voices in italic dialogue; swaps serve rhythm + picture, restraint over slapstick. |
| LinkedIn / social | One or two surgical swaps per post; render the one key quoted line; keep it clean. |
| Sales / DR copy | Swap flat verbs for high-energy specifics in the proof beats; render the customer's actual words. |
| Video / sketch script | Voices become character lines with delivery notes; swaps land in the spoken beat, not stage directions. |

## Output Requirements
- **Format**: Swap table (flat → funnier → reason) followed by the fully rewritten passage.
- **Must include**: At least one word/sound swap that passes the deflation test, and at least one paraphrased line converted to rendered speech (if characters are present).
- **Ends with**: The rewritten passage as delivery-ready prose, biggest sound/voice beat positioned last.

## Quality Gate
Score against the genius.md rubric (name the anchor for any score ≥8; for this workflow, the **Word/Sound precision** row: "every pivotal word is the funniest-sounding option; voices rendered, not summarized"). Reject if any of these anti-patterns are present:
- **Flat verbs / paraphrased speech** carried into the final pass (Anti-Pattern: Word/Sound failure mode).
- **Sound-polish substituting for structure**: applying swaps to a draft missing its "way in" or dig (Anti-Pattern 2 / 4).
- **Synonym inflation**: a swap that fails the deflation test (a flashier word with no added charge).
Retry the weakest swap or voice render once before delivery.
