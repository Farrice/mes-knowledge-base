# /segura-essay — Recognition Essay

Produces a finished memoir-grade essay that starts from a "that's a thing" universal, installs the way-in/stakes, develops long-then-tight, and lands the recognition where the reader thinks "why didn't *I* say it?" Flagship STACKING workflow: Segura's observation engine (Patterns 3, 4, 10) carries the recognition; Parallax interiority carries the depth.

## When to Use
- A universal experience nobody named precisely, and the writer has lived it.
- Parallax-grade newsletter editions where recognition is the payoff, not argument.
- Personal-brand essays needing autobiography no one else can repeat (Pattern 13).

## When NOT to Use
- Pure argument/thesis content with no lived recognition core — route to `/insight-brief`.
- Stand-up bit from a single premise — route to a Segura bit workflow.
- LinkedIn-length scroll-stoppers — route to `/ghostwrite` or Lara Acosta.

## Pre-Flight Gate
Load genius.md. This workflow is governed by **Pattern 3 (Universal-but-Unarticulated)**, **Pattern 4 (The Way In)**, and **Pattern 10 (Long-First Edit)**, stacked with **Move: The Annoyance Dig** and **Move: The Way-In Install**. Disqualify the input if the premise is merely "relatable" rather than unarticulated (route to Cole atomic essay), if it is invented rather than reported (Pattern 13 violation — kill it), or if it carries no temperature (Pattern 2 — the writer must care).

## Skill Acquisition
- Load: skills/tom-segura-comedy-storytelling/genius.md — Patterns **3, 4, 10**; secondary **2, 11, 12**; Moves **The Annoyance Dig, The Way-In Install, The Word Swap, Long-First Edit**.
- Load the stacking partner for interiority: `.agent/workflows/parallax.md` (memoir-grade interiority + Phase 2.5 GROUND if any external factual surface). For deeper scene-level interiority, also load `skills/wright-thompson-mastery/` via `/wright-interiority`.

## Execution
1. **Dig the universal.** Generate 10+ candidate "that's a thing" observations the writer noticed "60, 70 times" but never voiced (Pattern 3). Apply **The Annoyance Dig** — push each past the surface gripe to its irrational core (Hidden 3). Select the one with the strongest "of course" charge and a temperature (Pattern 2).
2. **Install the Way In.** Before narrating anything, write the reason-for-telling and the stakes that precede the events (Pattern 4 / Exemplar 4). Render the same facts flat, then framed; keep framed. The reader invests *before* the first event lands.
3. **Tell the fat version.** Draft the bloated, over-long essay with zero precommitment to shape (Pattern 10 Round 1; Pattern 5). Report; do not invent (Pattern 13). Let lived detail run long.
4. **Cut to need.** Round 2 — remove everything the recognition payoff does not require (Pattern 10). Hold the one real beat longer than comfortable (Pattern 9) instead of rushing it.
5. **Stack interiority, render voices.** Braid Parallax/Wright memoir interiority into the held beats — the internal weather Segura's performance would carry. Render any person in actual speech/cadence, not paraphrase (Pattern 12).
6. **Insert punches, end biggest.** Round 3 — distribute surprise turns across the gaps (Pattern 6). Run **The Word Swap**: flat verbs become the funniest-sounding specific (Pattern 11). Land the close on the strongest recognition beat (Pattern 10 Round 4).

## Content Type Adaptations
| Content Type | How this shifts |
|---|---|
| Stand-up / spoken | Way-in spoken aloud; hold the beat with silence (Pattern 14); voices performed |
| Essay / newsletter (Parallax) | Full interiority stack; route external claims through Parallax 2.5 GROUND |
| LinkedIn / social | Compress to universal + one held beat + turn; way-in becomes the first two lines |
| Sales / DR copy | Universal becomes the prospect's unspoken problem; stakes = cost of staying there |
| Video / sketch script | Play the recognition 100% straight (Pattern 8); hold duration on camera (Pattern 9) |

## Output Requirements
- **Format**: Finished essay (700-1500 words for Parallax), way-in first, never a flat anecdote.
- **Must include**: Named unarticulated-universal, installed stakes, at least one held real beat, distributed surprise turns, rendered voices over paraphrase.
- **Ends with**: The recognition beat — the line that earns "why didn't *I* say it?" Never a rhetorical-question signoff.

## Quality Gate
Score against the genius.md rubric (name the transcript-anchored behavior for any score ≥8). Reject if any of these anti-patterns are present: **the neutral take** (no temperature, Pattern 2); **the flat anecdote** (no way-in, Pattern 4); **stopping at the obvious** (no dig, Hidden 3); **comprehensive but dead** (everything said plainly, no surprise/stakes/ownership). Retry the weakest element once before delivery.
