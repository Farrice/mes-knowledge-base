# Ross Minchev — Digital Products & Niche Selection

Ross Minchev is a performance marketer who builds $10K/month digital products by solving the niche-selection problem first: drill any broad category three levels deep until the niche title itself names a passionate, budget-carrying audience, then validate with external data (trend trajectory, competition, live affiliate offers, active ads) before building anything. Monetization is always dual-path — create your own AI-assisted product or promote an existing affiliate offer — chosen per niche by speed-to-revenue and margin, never by default. His differentiator is packaging: "people are tired of buying lazy products," so every AI-generated asset gets refined, visually unified, and loaded with countable value before it ships.

## Quick Reference

- **Drill three levels, always**: broad → sub-niche → micro-niche. "Mushroom coffee immunity" — a niche of a sub-niche of a sub-niche. The broader the niche, the more competition and saturation.
- **Title-as-targeting**: the micro-niche title IS the audience ("Air fryer recipes for college students in dorms"). One element does five jobs: positioning, ad targeting, SEO keyword, sales hook, content brief.
- **Validate before you build**: trend trajectory → competition level → live affiliate offers → active ads. Stop at the first failed gate.
- **Active ads = market proof**: someone spending ad money on a niche right now is stronger evidence than any keyword estimate.
- **Dual-path**: crowded-but-proven niche with strong affiliate offers → promote (zero build, ~50-60% commissions on digital). Hidden-gem micro-niche → create your own product.
- **Commission consciousness**: compute actual dollars (60% of $50 = ~$30/sale), never raw percentages.
- **Never ship raw AI**: text-on-white-pages ebooks fail. Refine, add style-consistent visuals, name the countable value (45 recipes, 85 pages, 12,000 words).
- **Speed-to-demonstration**: complete one full niche→product→sale cycle before analyzing further. Execute, don't estimate.
