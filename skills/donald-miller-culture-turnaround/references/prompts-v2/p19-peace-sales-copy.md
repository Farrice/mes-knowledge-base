# PEACE Framework Sales Copy Architecture (Synthesis Prompt)

Apply Miller's PEACE framework to sales copy structure.

---

## Role & Activation

You are applying Donald Miller's PEACE framework to create sales pages and long-form copy. Problem → Empathy → Answer → Change → End Result provides complete psychological coverage.

Each section of sales copy maps to a PEACE element, creating belief-installing copy structure.

---

## Input Required

- **[OFFER]**: What's being sold?
- **[TARGET AUDIENCE]**: Who's buying?
- **[TRANSFORMATION]**: What change does the offer create?
- **[COMPETITOR LANDSCAPE]**: What alternatives exist?

---

## Execution Protocol

1. **DESIGN** Problem Section - hook by naming unacceptable reality
2. **CRAFT** Empathy Section - demonstrate understanding of pain
3. **BUILD** Answer Section - present offer as the path forward
4. **CREATE** Change Section - declare the identity shift available
5. **ARCHITECT** End Result Section - paint vision of transformed state
6. **PRODUCE** complete sales copy structure with section drafts

---

## Output Contract

- Five labeled copy sections, one per PEACE element, in Problem → Empathy → Answer → Change → End Result order.
- Each section: drafted prose (not an outline), sized to its word-count recommendation, written for the stated [TARGET AUDIENCE].
- At least one sound bite embedded per section — short, repeatable, identity-bearing phrases (not generic taglines).
- A closing word-count table mapping recommended length to each section, calibrated to the offer's price point and format (page vs. email vs. script).

---

## Output Skeleton

```
## Problem Section
[Name the unacceptable reality the audience is living in. State the losing belief this section attacks. Word count target: ___]
Sound bite: [short line naming the problem in the audience's own words]

## Empathy Section
[Demonstrate specific understanding of the pain — what it costs them daily, what they've already tried. Word count target: ___]
Sound bite: [short line signaling "I understand this exact pain"]

## Answer Section
[Present [OFFER] as the bridge from problem to resolution — mechanism, not just claim. Word count target: ___]
Sound bite: [short line naming the offer as the answer]

## Change Section
[Declare the identity shift the audience makes by taking this offer — who they become, not just what they get. Word count target: ___]
Sound bite: [short line declaring the new identity]

## End Result Section
[Paint the transformed state — vivid, specific, tied to [TRANSFORMATION]. Word count target: ___]
Sound bite: [short line anchoring the end-state identity]
```

---

## Quality Gate

- [ ] All five PEACE sections are present, in order, and each is drafted prose — not a bullet outline.
- [ ] Each section contains at least one identity-bearing sound bite distinct from the others.
- [ ] The Problem section names a specific losing belief, not a generic pain point.
- [ ] The Change section states an identity shift ("who they become"), not just a feature or benefit.
- [ ] Word-count recommendations are present for every section and match the stated offer/format.
- [ ] No claim, statistic, or case study appears that wasn't supplied via [OFFER], [TARGET AUDIENCE], [TRANSFORMATION], or [COMPETITOR LANDSCAPE].

---

## Success Metric

Sales copy that installs belief changes and creates identity shifts, not just describes features.
