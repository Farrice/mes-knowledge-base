# Sound Bite Brand Positioning Engine (Synthesis Prompt)

Create belief-installing sound bites for brand positioning and differentiation.

---

## Role & Activation

You are applying Donald Miller's sound bite architecture to brand positioning. Brands need repeatable phrases that install identity beliefs about who they are for, what they stand against, and why they matter.

Position brands through sound bites that destroy competitor narratives while installing preference beliefs.

---

## Input Required

- **[BRAND]**: What brand needs positioning?
- **[TARGET AUDIENCE]**: Who should believe in this brand?
- **[COMPETITORS]**: What narratives need destroying?
- **[DIFFERENTIATION]**: What makes this brand unique?

---

## Execution Protocol

1. **MAP** Competitor Narratives - what beliefs currently exist
2. **DESIGN** Destruction Sound Bites - phrases that attack competitor positioning
3. **CREATE** Installation Sound Bites - phrases that build brand identity
4. **BUILD** PEACE Framework for Brand - brand's own transformation narrative
5. **DEVELOP** Repetition Strategy - where and how to deploy
6. **PRODUCE** complete brand sound bite arsenal

---

## Output Contract

- 5-9 core sound bites total, each a short, repeatable, identity-bearing phrase (not a tagline or slogan in the ad-agency sense).
- A competitor narrative destruction map: each existing competitor belief paired with the sound bite designed to attack it.
- A full PEACE framework pass (Problem, Empathy, Answer, Change, End Result) applied to the brand's own transformation story.
- A deployment matrix separating internal (team-facing) sound bites from external (audience-facing) sound bites, with venue recommendations for each.

---

## Output Skeleton

```

| Existing Competitor Belief | Destruction Sound Bite | What It Attacks |
|---|---|---|
| [belief held by market about [COMPETITORS]] | [phrase] | [specific losing belief targeted] |
[repeat for each competitor narrative in scope]

## Installation Sound Bites (Brand Identity)
1. [Sound bite] — installs: [belief this builds]
2. [Sound bite] — installs: [belief this builds]
[3-7 more, total 5-9 core sound bites across destruction + installation]

## PEACE Framework for Brand Story
- Problem: [the unacceptable reality [BRAND] exists to end]
- Empathy: [how the brand demonstrates it understands the audience's pain]
- Answer: [the brand as bridge/mechanism]
- Change: [identity shift the brand offers its audience]
- End Result: [the transformed state the brand promises]

## Sound Bite Function Map
| Sound Bite | Installs / Destroys | Primary Audience |
|---|---|---|

## Deployment Matrix
| Sound Bite | Internal or External | Venue(s) | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|

## Internal Alignment Sound Bites
[subset of the arsenal reserved for team-facing use]

## External Marketing Sound Bites
[subset of the arsenal reserved for audience-facing use]
```

---

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Total core sound bites falls within the 5-9 range specified by the framework.
- [ ] Every destruction sound bite is mapped to a named competitor belief it's designed to attack.
- [ ] The PEACE framework section covers all five elements for the brand's own story, not just the offer.
- [ ] Internal and external sound bite lists are explicitly separated, not just implied by tone.
- [ ] Deployment matrix names actual venues (not "various channels") for each sound bite.
- [ ] No fabricated competitor names, market-share figures, or customer quotes appear — only what was supplied via [BRAND], [TARGET AUDIENCE], [COMPETITORS], [DIFFERENTIATION].

---

## Success Metric

Brand associated with specific sound bites that customers repeat and identify with.
