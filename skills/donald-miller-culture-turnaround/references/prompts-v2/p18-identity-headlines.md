# Identity-Installing Headline System (Synthesis Prompt)

Create headlines and subject lines that install identity shifts, not just capture attention.

---

## Role & Activation

You are deploying Donald Miller's identity-first methodology to headlines and subject lines. Headlines don't just hook—they begin the identity installation process from word one.

The sound bite principles applied to attention-capture: every headline is a micro-sound-bite that programs belief.

---

## Input Required

- **[CONTENT TOPIC]**: What is the content about?
- **[TARGET IDENTITY SHIFT]**: What identity belief should headline begin installing?
- **[AUDIENCE]**: Who will read this?
- **[FORMAT]**: Email subject, blog title, ad headline, etc.?

---

## Execution Protocol

1. **IDENTIFY** the Identity Shift embedded in the content
2. **DESIGN** Headline Variations that begin installing belief
3. **APPLY** Sound Bite Principles (memorable, repeatable, identity-forming)
4. **TEST** Against Miller Criteria (does it challenge old identity?)
5. **PRODUCE** 10+ headline options ranked by identity installation power

---

## Output Contract

A headline set containing exactly these five components, in order:

1. 10 or more headline/subject-line options
2. Ranking of all options by identity installation power (highest to lowest)
3. Rationale for the top 3, specifically
4. The identity belief each of the top 3 begins installing
5. An A/B Testing Recommendation for which to test first and why

## Output Skeleton

```

## Headline Options (10+)
1. "[headline]"
2. "[headline]"
3. "[headline]"
[... through at least 10]

2. [headline #]
[... full ranked order]

### #1: "[headline]"
[why this ranks highest — what old identity it challenges]
### #2: "[headline]"
[rationale]
### #3: "[headline]"
[rationale]

## Identity Belief Each Top-3 Headline Installs
| Headline | Identity Belief Installed |
|---|---|
| "[top 1]" | [belief] |
| "[top 2]" | [belief] |
| "[top 3]" | [belief] |

## A/B Testing Recommendation
[which headline to test first, against which alternate, and why]
```

## Quality Gate

- At least 10 distinct headline options are produced, not variations that only swap one word
- Every headline in the set is ranked — no unranked leftovers
- The Rationale for Top 3 explains what old identity/belief each headline challenges, not just why it's "catchy"
- The Identity Belief table names a specific belief per top-3 headline, not a repeated generic answer
- The A/B Testing Recommendation names a specific comparison (headline vs. headline), not just "test a few and see"
