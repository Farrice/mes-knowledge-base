# Causal Chain Content Strategy System (Synthesis Prompt)

Create content strategies that target identity-level change, not just surface engagement.

---

## Role & Activation

You are applying Donald Miller's causal chain to content strategy. Content should trace: Results ← Performance ← Behavior ← Identity ← Language.

Each piece of content intervenes at the language level to install identity beliefs that cascade to behavior and results.

---

## Input Required

- **[AUDIENCE]**: Who is the content for?
- **[DESIRED BEHAVIOR]**: What should they do differently?
- **[IDENTITY SHIFT NEEDED]**: Who must they become?
- **[CONTENT FORMATS]**: What types of content are possible?

---

## Execution Protocol

1. **MAP** Desired Results backward through causal chain
2. **IDENTIFY** Identity Beliefs that must shift
3. **DESIGN** Content Pillars that install each belief
4. **CREATE** Content Calendar targeting identity shifts
5. **BUILD** Measurement Framework (is identity shifting?)
6. **PRODUCE** complete causal chain content strategy

---

## Output Contract

- A full causal chain map for [AUDIENCE], showing Results ← Performance ← Behavior ← Identity ← Language with the specific belief at each link (not a generic restatement of the chain's five labels).
- A prioritized list of identity beliefs to install, each mapped to at least one content pillar.
- One content pillar per identity belief, each pillar naming its format(s) from [CONTENT FORMATS].
- A 30-90 day content calendar (cadence stated explicitly) that sequences content against the causal chain, not just a posting schedule.
- A measurement framework defining leading indicators for identity shift (language/behavior change), distinct from lagging output metrics.

---

## Output Skeleton

```
## Causal Chain Map for [AUDIENCE]
Language: [specific words/phrases to install] →
Identity: [specific belief/self-concept this creates] →
Behavior: [DESIRED BEHAVIOR] this identity produces →
Performance: [what improves once behavior shifts] →
Results: [the outcome this chain is built to produce]

## Identity Beliefs to Install (Prioritized)
1. [Belief] — priority rationale: [___]
2. [Belief] — priority rationale: [___]
[repeat, ranked highest-leverage first]

## Content Pillars (One Per Belief)
| Pillar | Belief Installed | Formats (from [CONTENT FORMATS]) |
|---|---|---|

## Content Calendar (30-90 Days)
| Timeframe | Pillar | Content Piece | Sound Bite Embedded |
|---|---|---|---|
[cadence stated: e.g., weekly / bi-weekly per pillar]

## Format Recommendations per Belief
[for each belief, which format from [CONTENT FORMATS] carries it most effectively and why]

## Sound Bites to Embed per Content Piece
[short, repeatable phrase per content piece tying back to the identity belief it installs]

## Identity Shift Metrics
[leading indicators: language changes, self-reported identity, behavior signals — NOT vanity engagement metrics]

## Cascade Predictions
[if the identity shift installs, what behavior change follows, and on what approximate timeframe — stated as a hypothesis to test, not a guarantee]
```

---

## Quality Gate

- [ ] The causal chain map names a specific belief/phrase at each of the five links for [AUDIENCE] — not a restatement of the framework's labels.
- [ ] Every identity belief in the prioritized list has at least one content pillar mapped to it.
- [ ] The content calendar states an explicit cadence and spans the stated 30-90 day window.
- [ ] Identity Shift Metrics are leading indicators (language/behavior) distinct from lagging output or vanity metrics.
- [ ] Cascade Predictions are framed as testable hypotheses, not guaranteed outcomes.
- [ ] No fabricated engagement numbers, case studies, or named brands appear — only what was supplied via [AUDIENCE], [DESIRED BEHAVIOR], [IDENTITY SHIFT NEEDED], [CONTENT FORMATS].

---

## Success Metric

Content that changes audience identity over time, evidenced by language and behavior shifts.
