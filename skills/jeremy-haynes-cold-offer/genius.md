## The Core Truth

**The offer is a living stack, not a launch artifact.** Every component is *derived* — read off the ICP's umbrella narrative (problems → circumstances → desired outcomes) — and the stack re-composes as the audience migrates colder (in-market → needs-convinced → mass-market) and as objection data accumulates. "An offer is ideally dynamic." Where Hormozi gives a value equation and Suby a nurture ladder, Haynes gives the **re-composition engine**.

Cold-first validation is the asymmetry that anchors everything: "Anything that works for cold audiences has the highest odds of working for warm audiences, whereas vice versa is not true."

## The Method (8-step spine)

1. **GROUND** — field-research the ICP before composing: AI agents scraping Reddit/X/IG/TikTok + literal conversations with prospects ("a very undermined tactic"). Harvest verbatim language. "You never want to pull the narrative out of your ass."
2. **NARRATE** — write the umbrella narrative: who the buyer is, what they've been through (especially failure history — burned by agencies, failed hires, failed programs), decomposed into **problems / circumstances / desired outcomes**. Drawn as an arc over the three variables.
3. **CLASSIFY** — in-market (actively pursuing the solution) or needs-convinced (must be persuaded)? "Completely different offer stacks" — even for the same product, framed differently. Audience state also dictates funnel type downstream.
4. **COMPOSE** — generate stack components 1:1 from narrative elements. Two-cliffs frame: current problems on one cliff, desired outcomes on the other, the offer is the bridge — and they only care about the bridge AFTER you've articulated both cliffs. Absorb the **next problem** (what the buyer must solve right after purchase — the deal dies there: cat-piss-mansion construction loan).
5. **LAYER** — split core offer (what the majority buyer needs: the steak) from contextual **offer editions** (reserve additions closers deploy on commitment signals: the truffle butter, the Miami Ferrari tit job). "If I tried to sell every steak with black truffle butter, I wouldn't sell the people who just want a steak."
6. **INSTRUMENT** — read funnel stats as *offer telemetry*: "The show rate stats represent the enthusiasm that your leads actually have." Misaligned offer cooks every stat at once; re-alignment improves everything "instantaneously." Step-function = alignment event; single-stat increment = funnel tuning.
7. **AUGMENT** — AI analysis over ALL sales-call recordings → objection + delay inventory (categorically different) → **fragmented feedback pie chart** → majority slices become core components, fragments stay as editions. Funding objection → capital partners inside the stack.
8. **MIGRATE** — at scale the in-market pool exhausts and leads arrive colder. Diagnose via lead-temperature drift (sales floor reports curious-but-unsold prospects). Re-compose frame/stack first; change the product only when drift is confirmed after cheaper fixes fail. Scale THROUGH the ROAS dip ("10:1 to 3:1 and work your way back up to 7:1").

## Decision Framework (pre-flight for every workflow)

- **Is the narrative field-grounded?** If problems/circumstances/desired outcomes weren't sourced from real market language, STOP — run GROUND first (`/jh-umbrella-narrative` or `/avatar-machine`).
- **Which audience state?** In-market vs. needs-convinced must be declared before any composition or audit. Unknown = classify first.
- **Offer problem or funnel problem?** Step-function decay across all stats + colder leads = offer alignment. Isolated stat decay = funnel. Never rebuild an offer on one loud anecdote; never tune a funnel through an alignment failure.
- **Core or edition?** Majority-frequency needs go in core; fragment-frequency needs stay reserve. Pie chart before either.
- **Would this survive a 30-second cold read?** Cold audiences "have literally just assumed they've never heard of you." If the articulation doesn't instantly let a stranger recognize their situation, it fails regardless of internal elegance.

## Signature Moves (behavioral DNA)

- **Narrative-before-stack**: refuses component talk until problems/circumstances/outcomes are on the board.
- **"What have they already tried and failed?"**: mines buyer scar tissue; the highest-converting components answer scars (never sell done-for-you to someone burned by five agencies).
- **Component challenge — "not random"**: every component must name the narrative element it neutralizes; orphans get cut.
- **AI transcript sweep before opinion**: data → pie chart → action; never anecdote → redesign.
- **Reserve the editions**: contextual additions live in the closer's pocket, deployed only on commitment signals — never in the opening pitch.
- **Secret-shop the buyer's seat**: burner contact info, walk the funnel cold, judge against a five-star-hotel standard.

## Hidden Knowledge

- **Offer upstream of funnel**: audience state "is also going to dictate the type of funnel." Funnel debugging before offer-alignment checking has the causality backwards.
- **Friction buys intent**: "Higher the friction is, the higher the intent" — typed applications over multiple choice. Add friction to buy lead quality.
- **Real urgency = quantified cost of inaction** (from the buyer's circumstances). Countdown-timer urgency banned.
- **Anti-value-stack**: rejects inflated "$14,997 value" anchoring lists. Stack = problem-aligned components only.
- **Stage-gated depth**: scanner mode (pre-commitment: recognition only) vs. consumption mode (post-commitment: hammer with depth so "the sales call's actually a sales call"). Depth itself is a paid-stack component (his YouTube = 10-20% of what he knows).
- **Alignment beats fulfillment** (diagnostic, not license): terrible businesses scale on narrative alignment alone — so a badly-fulfilling winner = a well-mapped market to steal the map from and out-fulfill.
- **The narrative migrates; the product may not**: $300K→$2.5M case kept one $2,500 product across all three stages — stack/frame/belief-content mutated. Product change only on confirmed temperature drift. Knowing which lever = mastery.

## Hall of Fame Exemplars (calibration anchors — full versions in extraction report)

1. **Athena Reframe** — $3K/mo EA service outselling commodity VAs because every component (pre-vetted placement, ongoing training, AI training, vacation temp-swap, manager layer) answers a specific scar in the burned VA-buyer's history. "They've successfully charged a premium... all they've done is just updated their offer to what their ideal ICP is actively going through."
2. **Cat Piss Mansion** — his own $3.8M home purchase: deal survives ONLY because the post-purchase problem ($1.5M construction) has a path. "You can deal with the next set of problems before the current problem is done by adding it to the original offer."
3. **Inner Circle Autopsy** — his own stack reverse-engineered aloud, each component tied to buyer failure history, punctuated by the refrain: "This isn't random shit that's included in the offer stack."

**Anti-exemplar**: the set-in-stone offer ("there's nothing I'm going to change about that") + churning through campaigns/angles/funnels/teams while leads arrive colder — or the classic inflated value-stack webinar pitch. Both violate dynamism + traceability.

## Quality Rubric (score deliverables against this; anchors at 4/7/10 in extraction report)

1. **Narrative grounding** — field-sourced verbatim language, not demographic sketch
2. **Component traceability** — 1:1 both directions, failure-history components present
3. **Audience-state fit** — stack declared for in-market or needs-convinced; migration path designed
4. **Cold-stranger legibility** — 30-second cold read passes; specificity qualifies AND disqualifies
5. **Dynamic augmentation** — objection pie chart feeding core-vs-edition decisions
6. **Next-problem absorption** — post-purchase kill-points absorbed into the offer
7. **Urgency integrity** — quantified inaction cost only; zero manufactured scarcity
8. **Proof specificity** — before/after + strategy + measurable outcome; pattern proof; profession-matched

## Voice Notes (when writing AS Haynes)

Direct, conversational, "bro"-adjacent but data-first. Concrete receipts over adjectives. Vivid vulgar memory hooks carrying structural lessons (cat piss mansion, tit job Ferrari). Recurring cadence: "Okay?" / "You understand?" / "I want to be really clear/direct when I say this." Never hype — his whole messaging doctrine is "substance, specificity, and credibility" over excitement. Preserve spoken texture; polish is the tell.

## Anti-Patterns (what Haynes would reject — each anchored to source)

- **Components brainstormed instead of derived** — "This isn't random shit that's included in the offer stack" (source video kiWQ3M6fiH4, Inner Circle walkthrough, 2026)
- **Warm-audience validation used as cold-traffic evidence** — "When you have an offer that's working really well for warm audiences, that does not by any means validate that it's probable to work with cold traffic" (source video, opening)
- **Funnel/team/creative churn while the offer stays frozen** — "They tried different campaign structures, different ad angles... different marketers... nothing was working until they made an offer change" (source video, $1.6–1.7M/mo client story)
- **The set-in-stone offer** — "'This is what I'm selling. Like, there's nothing I'm going to change about that.' And that can't be true. An offer is ideally dynamic" (source video)
- **Inflated value-stack price anchoring** — he recommends "never using a value stack" in webinar pitches (jeremyhaynes.com, webinar-scaling article, fetched 2026-07-15)
- **Fake urgency** — countdown timers that reset, "limited spots" repeated for months; real urgency = quantified cost of inaction like "losing fifteen thousand dollars a month" (jeremyhaynes.com, sell-high-ticket-without-hype article)
- **Front-loading education to scanner-mode prospects** — depth belongs post-commitment: "Get the education part of the sales process out of the way before the sales call. That way, the sales call's actually a sales call" (jeremyhaynes.com, pay-in-full article)
- **Acting on one loud objection instead of the pie chart** — "If I tried to sell every steak with black truffle butter, I wouldn't sell the people who just want a steak" (source video, Mastros example)
- **Selling done-for-you to the done-for-you-burned** — "I'm not going to sell them done-for-you agency services if they've worked with five agencies and been burnt" (source video, Inner Circle narrative)
- **Narratives pulled from assumption** — "You never want to pull the narrative out of your ass" (source video, market-research close)
