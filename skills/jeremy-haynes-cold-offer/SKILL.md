**The gap this fills**: cold-traffic offer architecture at the mechanism level. "Anything that works for cold audiences has the highest odds of working for warm audiences, whereas vice versa is not true." ## Quick Reference (the spine)

GROUND → NARRATE → CLASSIFY → COMPOSE → LAYER → INSTRUMENT → AUGMENT → MIGRATE

- Every component names its narrative element or gets cut ("not random").
- In-market vs. needs-convinced = different stacks, same product possible.
- Show rate is offer telemetry: all-stats step-function = alignment event.
- Majority objections → core components; fragments → closer-held editions.
- The deal dies at the NEXT problem — absorb it into the current offer.
- Urgency = quantified cost of inaction, nothing manufactured.

## Anti-Patterns (reject on sight)

Brainstormed components · warm-traction as cold evidence · funnel churn over a frozen offer · inflated value-stack anchoring · fake urgency · education front-loaded to scanner-mode prospects · one loud anecdote redesigning the offer · done-for-you sold to the done-for-you-burned.
