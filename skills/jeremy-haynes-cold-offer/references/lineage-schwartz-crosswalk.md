# Lineage Crosswalk — Haynes Audience States ↔ Schwartz Awareness Stages

Honesty anchor: Haynes' audience-temperature model is Eugene Schwartz's awareness ladder (*Breakthrough Advertising*, 1966) operationalized into an offer-composition SOP with AI tooling. Name the lineage in strategy work; don't present the split as novel theory. Haynes' novelty is the **operational layer**: what the offer STACK does at each stage, the migration triggers, and the feedback machinery.

| Schwartz stage | Haynes state | What the offer stack does | Migration trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Most Aware + Product-Aware | **In-market** — "actively, literally pursuing it and open to the idea of buying it" | Components differentiate and de-risk the decision (proof, guarantees, next-problem absorption). $2–7 opt-ins, immediate profitability. | This pool is finite. Scale exhausts it. |
| Solution-Aware + Problem-Aware | **Needs-convinced** — "open to the idea of being sold... but they needed a lot more convincing" | Stack rebuilds around education + belief installation: training components over done-for-you, mechanism articulation, backend selling system carries the persuasion load. Expect ROAS dip; scale through it ("10:1 → 3:1 → back to 7:1"). | Sales floor reports leads arriving curious-but-unsold = drift confirmed. |
| Unaware | **Mass market** — cold general population | Belief-installation content becomes a paid line item ("handed them beliefs to think with"); brand/omnipresence subsidized by front-end economics; core product can stay constant while frame + content mutate. | Macro-trend dependence: "when that macro trend reverses, they're cooked." |
