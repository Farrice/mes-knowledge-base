# /jh-temp-migrate — Audience-Temperature Migration Plan

When the in-market pool exhausts, the same product needs a different stack and frame: "You can technically sell the same thing but frame it differently to sell to one demographic over the other." Frame/stack first; product change only when confirmed necessary.

## Pre-Flight Gate

- Migration must be EARNED: `/jh-plateau-diagnostic` verdict (or equivalent evidence of temperature drift) required. Migrating a working in-market offer prematurely burns profitable pool.
- Lineage honesty in strategy docs: this is Schwartz's awareness ladder operationalized — see `references/lineage-schwartz-crosswalk.md`.

## Skill Acquisition

- `genius.md` — Method step 8, hidden knowledge "the narrative migrates, the product may not"
- `references/lineage-schwartz-crosswalk.md` (stage table)

## Execution

1. **Name the stages**: current stage and target stage (in-market → needs-convinced, or needs-convinced → mass-market), with the drift evidence.
2. **Re-narrate**: rewrite the umbrella narrative for the colder buyer — they are NOT pursuing the solution; they're curious, education-seeking, "open to the idea of being sold." Their problems may match; their circumstances and self-story differ.
3. **Re-compose the stack**: education/belief components move to the front (training over done-for-you, mechanism articulation, proof aimed at category skepticism, not vendor comparison). Persuasion load shifts from the buyer's own intent onto the backend selling system (pre-call VSLs, breakout videos, hammer sequences — depth AFTER commitment, recognition before).
4. **Re-frame, keep the product** (default): draft the needs-convinced articulation of the SAME product. Escalate to product/offer change ONLY if the diagnostic showed frame-level fixes already failed (the $1.6–1.7M/mo case).
5. **Mass-market stage additions** (if migrating to stage 3): belief-installation content becomes a budget line ("handed them beliefs to think with"); front-end economics must subsidize omnipresence; macro-trend dependence flagged with a reversal watch-signal.
6. **Set the dip expectation**: pre-write the ROAS trajectory ("might go from 10:1 to 3:1 and work your way back up to 7:1") with the MSI floor — the minimum scaling indicator below which you stop, above which you scale through the dip.

## Output Requirements

Migration Plan: stage declaration + evidence, re-narrated umbrella, stack diff (kept/moved/added/cut), needs-convinced articulation draft, dip expectation + MSI floor, mass-market additions if applicable.

## Quality Gate

- Migration evidence cited (never migrate on hunch)
- Product held constant unless escalation criteria met and documented
- Dip expectation + MSI floor stated numerically
- Depth staging preserved (recognition pre-commitment, education post)
