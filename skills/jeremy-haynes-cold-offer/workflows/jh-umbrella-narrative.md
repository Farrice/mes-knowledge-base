# /jh-umbrella-narrative — Umbrella Narrative Map

The generative grammar for everything else in this skill. Output feeds `/jh-offer-stack`, `/jh-offer-audit`, `/jh-offer-to-copy`.

## Pre-Flight Gate

- Is there field data (scraped language, reviews, call notes, real conversations)? If none and none can be gathered → mark the narrative DRAFT-UNGROUNDED and say so; never present an assumed narrative as researched.
- Deep identity-level work needed (resistance, worldview)? Stack with `mcraney-deep-canvass` or `/icp-deep-dive` — Haynes' map is behavioral/situational; McRaney goes identity-level.

## Skill Acquisition

- `genius.md` — Method steps 1–2, signature moves (narrative-before-stack, scar-tissue mining)

## Execution

1. **Gather**: research agents against the platforms where the ICP actually talks (Reddit/X/IG/TikTok/reviews/communities). Harvest verbatim phrases for pains, situations, wants. Real conversations trump everything — include any prospect/client call notes available.
2. **Story first**: write the narrative as one flowing paragraph in near-first-person — where they are in their story, what they've tried, what failed, what they now believe about their own knowledge ("they know officially that they don't know enough"), what specific outcome they name in their own words.
3. **Decompose** under the umbrella arc:
   - **Problems** — the named frustrations (in market language)
   - **Circumstances** — what those problems CREATE in their life (the pretty-girl-Uber move: same problem, different circumstance per persona)
   - **Desired Outcomes** — specific, named, in their vocabulary (not "success" — "crack a million a month")
4. **Failure history**: list what this buyer has already bought/tried that failed them. Flag component types that are now radioactive (burned by agencies → no done-for-you).
5. **Exploitables**: close with "the whole bunch of individual things that become exploitable and obvious" — the 5–10 component/messaging openings the narrative reveals.

Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/umbrella-narrative-map.md — honor its Output Contract.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Multiple personas | One umbrella per persona — circumstances diverge even when problems match |
| B2B | Narrative carries both the operator's business story AND their personal stakes |
| Existing avatar doc | Ingest via `/jh-avatar-bridge`; this workflow fills narrative gaps only |
| Cold outreach lists | Compress to the recognition line: the one sentence that makes the ICP say "that's me" |

## Output Requirements

Umbrella Narrative Map: grounding sources listed, story paragraph, three decomposed lists with verbatim-language markers, failure-history register, exploitables list, grounded/DRAFT-UNGROUNDED status.

## Quality Gate

- Verbatim market language present (quoted), not paraphrase-only
- Circumstances distinct from problems (the consequence layer exists)
- Failure history captured with radioactive component types flagged
- Outcomes specific enough to disqualify non-buyers
- Grounding status honest
