# Bill Browder — Use Cases & Workflow Routing

> Specific "I have *this* situation" scenarios mapped to the exact `browder-` workflow that handles it. Use this to pick the right entry point instead of loading the whole skill and guessing.
>
> **The 11 workflows fall into three tiers:**
> - **Single-pattern tools** (surgical — fix one thing): next-sentence-test, cliffhang-spine, place-as-character, show-then-nail, drama-excavation, stakes-architecture, villain-evidence, dig-below-surface, mini-book-outliner.
> - **Composers** (Tier 3 — stack multiple patterns + other skills into one publish-ready deliverable): founder-warstory, cause-campaign.
> - **The architecture-first move**: mini-book-outliner runs *before* drafting; everything else operates on or produces drafts.
>
> **Honesty-spine gate runs across all of them.** Workflows marked HARD GATE will refuse or flag manufactured stakes / unsupported accusations.

---

## Routing decision tree (start here)

1. **Do you have a draft already, or are you starting from zero?**
   - Starting a *large* piece (book, season, campaign, pitch) → **`/browder-mini-book-outliner`** (architecture before color).
   - Starting from research overload / can't find the angle → **`/browder-dig-below-surface`**.
   - Have a draft → go to step 2.

2. **Is the problem the whole piece, or one specific failure?**
   - One specific failure → use the matching single-pattern tool (table below).
   - The whole piece needs to become a thriller (founder origin, cause) → use a **composer** (`/browder-founder-warstory` or `/browder-cause-campaign`).

3. **Single-pattern symptom → tool** (the diagnostic table):

| Symptom in the work | Workflow |
|---|---|
| "There are comfortable places to stop / it loses me." | `/browder-next-sentence-test` |
| "It's serial but each part resolves tidily / nobody continues." | `/browder-cliffhang-spine` |
| "The dry/technical material is a boring exposition dump." | `/browder-drama-excavation` |
| "The setting is over-described / flat / a speed bump." | `/browder-place-as-character` |
| "The point is *told*, not landed; no quotable line." | `/browder-show-then-nail` |
| "The bad thing doesn't land / nobody cares when it happens." | `/browder-stakes-architecture` |
| "The adversary reads as a strawman / the claim could get sued." | `/browder-villain-evidence` |
| "I'm drowning in material and everyone's overcomplicating the angle." | `/browder-dig-below-surface` |
| "I need to resolve the structure of a big piece before writing." | `/browder-mini-book-outliner` |

## Use cases by workflow

### `/browder-next-sentence-test`
*A line-by-line grip audit. Mark every comfortable stopping point, re-engineer each marked beat to pull forward, end with a "no place to stop" pass.*

**Deploy when:**
- A finished essay/post/page "reads fine" but isn't propulsive — you suspect lines that exist to be admired, not to grip.
- A landing page or VSL has a bounce/drop-off problem and you need to find *where* readers leave.
- Auditing the first 3 lines of a social post (the stack-or-scroll decision zone).
- Final QA pass on any draft a reader can abandon — i.e., everything.
- **Pairs after** any composer or drafting workflow as the closing grip gate.

---

### `/browder-cliffhang-spine`
*Re-architect a serial/multi-part piece as a miniseries: each unit ends on a live uncertainty; outputs rewritten unit-endings + an open-loop map.*

**Deploy when:**
- An email sequence/nurture flow has low open rates after email 1 (each email resolves comfortably).
- A content series or newsletter run has subscriber decay between editions.
- A multi-part thread where each tweet ends on a tidy complete thought.
- A book/long-form whose chapters/sections let the reader put it down.
- An episode/edition run (e.g., a Parallax-style series) that needs miniseries pull.

---

### `/browder-drama-excavation`
*Take the dullest necessary material, interrogate it for buried human jeopardy BEFORE dressing it up, name the conflict/rival/threat, re-tell the dry artifact THROUGH that conflict. Honesty spine: drama must be real and found.*

**Deploy when:**
- Translating a dry B2B/technical/finance/legal/compliance domain into narrative (the "make the Chinese wall exciting" problem).
- A SaaS feature, insurance clause, tax structure, or integration spec that must read as story, not spec.
- A case study or category campaign that's currently a feature/exposition dump.
- Any piece where the necessary material is boring but cannot be skipped.

---

### `/browder-place-as-character`
*Render a setting through the 2–3 most distinctive recognizable sensory details, then stop. A hard restraint gate: forces a cut-to-3, delivers rendered passage + cut-list of removed decoration.*

**Deploy when:**
- A scene/setting is over-described (4+ descriptors, speed bumps).
- A location reads flat and generic — carries no felt atmosphere.
- A founder story, memoir, or case study where a room/place should be a character but currently isn't.
- Tightening any narrative where description is slowing momentum.

---

### `/browder-show-then-nail`
*Dramatize the meaning so the reader infers it, then drive ONE earned hard sentence at the end. Delivers rendered passage + the single nail-line + an earned-not-crutch check.*

**Deploy when:**
- A section/chapter/post *tells* the lesson instead of dramatizing it (AN-6).
- An ending falls flat — you need the quotable, earned landing line.
- A founder/origin piece that states the moral ("taught me perseverance") instead of landing it.
- Any dramatized point that needs a clean, quotable close (used once, only at the end).

---

### `/browder-stakes-architecture`
*Size the fall by the caring built first. Engineer investment (who, what they want, why root for them) BEFORE the threat. Outputs a staged investment-then-jeopardy beat-map + worked sample. HARD GATE (AN-2): jeopardy must be real AND earned.*

**Deploy when:**
- A case study leads with the problem and the crisis doesn't land (build investment in the client first).
- A founder story drops a "tragedy" before the reader cares about anyone.
- Advocacy/mission content where a loss or threat must be *felt*, not asserted.
- Any narrative where a consequence currently arrives un-earned.

---

### `/browder-villain-evidence`
*Render a real adversary through their OWN words/actions/documents — never adjectives. Clear good-guy/bad-guy structure that survives a hostile lawyer. Outputs evidenced villain portrait + accusation routed through receipts + caricature-check. HARD honesty/legal GATE (AN-4).*

**Deploy when:**
- Positioning against an incumbent/competitor (render them through their own pricing page, docs, SLA — not slander).
- Investigative or advocacy content naming a real adversary.
- A founder origin with a real antagonist (the partner, the gatekeeper, the system).
- Any explosive/contestable claim that must withstand a hostile reader or legal scrutiny.
- Persuasion with a clear "enemy" (the status quo, the broken way) that's currently a strawman.

---

### `/browder-dig-below-surface`
*When drowning in surface material and everyone's too smart for their own good: hunt the one thing that "doesn't make sense," build a source-it-from-people plan, ask the dumb obvious question experts skip. Outputs the anomaly + people-plan + why it was missed. Honesty spine: a real overlooked truth, never a manufactured contrarian one.*

**Deploy when:**
- Research overload — too much material, no clear angle.
- Competitive intelligence / market discovery: finding the non-obvious edge.
- A content angle hunt where everyone is producing the same "comprehensive" take.
- Investigative work where the truth is buried under expert overcomplication (the "preferred shares" situation).
- **Runs at the front** of a piece, before mini-book-outliner, when the angle itself is unknown.

---

### `/browder-mini-book-outliner`
*Write a substantial architectural draft (mini-chapter per unit: spine, drama, challenge, resolution, end-of-unit uncertainty) + the single core-thread sentence — resolving structure and stakes BEFORE prose.*

**Deploy when:**
- Beginning any large/high-stakes deliverable: book, long-form, content season, major campaign, pitch.
- You have more material than the format can hold and need to find the spine + cut the rest.
- De-risking a big project before committing prose-time (Browder sells on the mini-book alone).
- **Runs first**, before any drafting; the architecture other workflows then operate on.

---

### `/browder-founder-warstory` *(composer, Tier 3)*
*Render a founder/brand origin as a high-stakes thriller, not a chronological bio. Composes drama-excavation + core-thread + stakes-architecture + villain-through-evidence + show-then-nail into one publish-ready origin narrative. HARD honesty GATE (AN-2). Stacks `build-bos` / `oren-brand` / `stanton-brand-origin` (Browder runs the thriller spine; brand OS supplies positioning, voice, visual).*

**Deploy when:**
- A founder/brand needs an origin story that grips (about page, keynote, brand film, investor narrative, LinkedIn founder arc).
- A chronological "I was born / I founded / we grew" bio needs to become a thriller.
- You want the full stack in one pass rather than running five single-pattern tools.
- **Stack it**: run alongside `/build-bos`, `/oren-brand`, or `/stanton-brand-origin` — Browder owns the narrative spine; those own positioning/voice/visual. Do not rebuild brand strategy inside this workflow.

---

### `/browder-cause-campaign` *(composer, Tier 3)*
*Engineer an advocacy/mission narrative that mobilizes: book-as-delivery-mechanism logic (a story built to travel and recruit beyond the room), relatable anchors, real stakes built before the fall, an evidence spine, publicity-as-safety framing. Outputs a campaign narrative + spread mechanics. Honesty spine: real cause, real stakes.*

**Deploy when:**
- An advocacy/mission/movement needs a narrative that spreads without the author in the room.
- A cause that's "righteous but not traveling" — needs to be re-engineered to recruit strangers.
- A campaign requiring relatable anchors for broad identification + an evidence spine the accusation rides on.
- High-stakes public positioning where publicity itself is a shield (the Browder safety logic).

## Common multi-workflow sequences

- **Big new piece from scratch**: `/browder-dig-below-surface` (find the angle) → `/browder-mini-book-outliner` (architecture) → draft → `/browder-next-sentence-test` (grip gate).
- **Fix a flat founder story**: `/browder-founder-warstory` (composer) → `/browder-place-as-character` (tighten settings) → `/browder-next-sentence-test`.
- **Fix a dry B2B case study**: `/browder-drama-excavation` (find buried jeopardy) → `/browder-stakes-architecture` (build caring first) → `/browder-show-then-nail` (land it).
- **Fix a leaking email sequence / series**: `/browder-cliffhang-spine` (open-loop every unit) → `/browder-next-sentence-test` (grip per email).
- **Position against a competitor honestly**: `/browder-villain-evidence` (route accusation through their own receipts) → `/browder-next-sentence-test`.
- **Launch an advocacy campaign**: `/browder-cause-campaign` (composer) → `/browder-cliffhang-spine` (if serial) → `/browder-next-sentence-test`.

## Final gate (every deployment)

Before delivery, run the genius.md Decision Framework — especially #4: *Are the stakes real, and have I built the caring before the fall?* The HARD-GATE workflows (stakes-architecture, villain-evidence, founder-warstory) enforce this mechanically, but every Browder output is subject to the honesty spine: real jeopardy, real receipts, restraint over decoration, and no comfortable place to stop.
