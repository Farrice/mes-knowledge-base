# Exemplars and Anti-Exemplar

These are mechanism demonstrations, not reusable plot material.

## Birds Defeat Porcupines
A draft contains recurring birds and one beautiful porcupine incident. Revision strengthens the governing family and cuts the lovely orphan. Recurrence becomes architecture only when selection has a cost. Source §1.

## Suffering Character, Comic Narrator
The character experiences disaster without knowing its eventual proportion. A later narrator carries comic safety while respecting immediate pain. Comedy can live between consciousnesses. Source §6.

## Recovered Setup
A late pass finds an object or joke placed without conscious planning. Revision searches backward, prepares the promise, and earns the payoff. Discovery becomes craft after the fact. Source §§16,18.

## Human Trace
Instead of scenic cliché or its showy opposite, record the worn, chosen, resisted, or improvised detail that implies a person. Source §19.

## More Mermaid
A strange ending does not fit. Compare cut, move, transform, prepare, and intensify, then rebuild earlier material if the ending is the revelation. Source §22.

## Anti-Exemplar — Respectable Flattening
An editor calls the odd ending confusing. The revision deletes it, smooths transitions, explains the theme, and produces competent category sameness. It solves the symptom by destroying the revelation.
