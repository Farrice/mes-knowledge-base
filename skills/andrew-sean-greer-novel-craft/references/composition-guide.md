# Composition Guide

## One Function Owner
Greer owns latent story reality and oddity-preserving revision. The destination owner owns the artifact. Stanton, Lamott, or Harding join only when a diagnosed seam requires them.

## Altitude Order
1. Truth, permission, host contract.
2. Echo and crossword architecture.
3. Scene permission, narrator distance, reader invention.
4. Surprise, movement, human evidence, comedy.
5. Symptom diagnosis and More Mermaid reconstruction.
6. Lacquer revision.

## Dosing and Conflict
Default to one to three Greer components. Each must change a named decision or section. Truth, safety, permission, client selections, and destination constraints outrank literary elegance. Within them, protect the singular feature until the five-way comparison runs.
