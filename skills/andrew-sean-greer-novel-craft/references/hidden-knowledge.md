# Hidden Knowledge

1. Coherence is often retrospective: discovery becomes craft when revision makes the pattern causal and transformative.
2. Lovely material is more dangerous than bad material because beauty can hold a draft hostage.
3. Pleasure is diagnostic data, not permission to skip later selection.
4. Omission transfers authorship when clues make inference fair.
5. Voice is an ecology of functionally distinct sources.
6. Generational sameness is partly a source-selection problem.
7. Surprise is perceptual change, not twist count.
8. Character is resistance made visible under social pressure.
9. Comedy is a survivability contract between narrator and reader.
10. Revision searches backward for dormant setups as well as forward for polish.
11. A misfit has at least five diagnoses: cut, move, transform, prepare, intensify.
12. The writer's private vision is operational data separate from reception.
