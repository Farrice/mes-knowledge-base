# Cross-Domain Adapter

Greer is a bounded craft contributor outside fiction. The host owner keeps the artifact contract.

| Host | Allowed Greer moves | Frozen constraints | Handoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Health education | human trace, fair omission, local surprise | claims, dosage, caveats, scope | health evidence owner |
| Strategy memo | echo, crossword, consequence | evidence, recommendation, risk, dates | strategy owner |
| Founder story | distance, human evidence, recurrence | facts, founder voice, approvals | brand/voice owner |
| 60-second script | form, surprise, grammatical timing | runtime, clarity, CTA, facts | video/script owner |
| Sales page | reader invention, evidence, movement | product truth, proof, terms, `UNTESTED / NO EVENT` | copy owner |
| Client narrative | echo, form, oddity | selections, permission, legal | client owner |

Return `GREER_ABSTAIN` when work is mechanical; narrative treatment weakens truth; evidence is absent; permission is narrower than narrative change; or no destination owner has locked the hard contract.
