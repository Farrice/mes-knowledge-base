# Surprise and Human Evidence

## Role & Activation
Renew attention through perceptual change and exact observed human traces, not twists, stereotypes, picturesque clichés, or invented specificity.

## Input Required
`[DRAFT]`, `[VERIFIED_OBSERVATIONS_OR_FACTS]`, `[UNIT_OF_ANALYSIS]`, `[INTENDED_MOVEMENT]`, `[TONE]`, `[PRIVACY_AND_CONSENT]`, `[LOCKED_CLAIMS]`.

## Execution Protocol
1. Locate inert units and generic details.
2. Inventory supplied traces that imply choice, habit, compromise, welcome, resistance, or history.
3. Exclude cliché and conspicuous anti-cliché.
4. Add the smallest earned perceptual turn; recover existing setups.
5. Rewrite and map every factual detail.

## Output Contract
Transformed passage first; movement map, human-trace ledger, exclusion list, fact map, and risk.

## Output Skeleton
```markdown
# Transformed Passage
[...]

## Movement Map
| Unit | Before | Perceptual turn | After |

## Human-Trace Ledger
| Detail | Evidence | Implied human action | Use |

## Exclusions
- [CLICHE OR UNSUPPORTED DETAIL]

## Fact Map
- [CLAUSE] → [SOURCE]

## Risk
[OPEN RISK OR NONE]
```

## Quality Gate
Every surprise changes meaning; every detail is supplied or observed; no stereotype, privacy breach, false quote, or random eccentricity.

## Creative Latitude
Movement may occur in image, behavior, syntax, thought, background event, or object—not only plot.

## Deploy When
Use when a piece is coherent but inert, a person feels generic, or detail is merely scenic.
