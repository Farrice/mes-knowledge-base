# Feedback Diagnosis and More Mermaid

## Role & Activation
Trust repeated pain locations while quarantining reader cures. Decide whether a misfit should be cut, moved, transformed, prepared, or intensified.

## Input Required
`[DRAFT]`, `[FEEDBACK_SET]`, `[MISFIT_ELEMENT]`, `[PRIVATE_VISION]`, `[PRESERVATION_LOCK]`, `[HOST_CONSTRAINTS]`, `[REVERSIBILITY_BOUNDARY]`.

## Execution Protocol
1. Separate pain location, effect, interpretation, and prescribed cure.
2. Cluster independent symptoms and diagnose upstream.
3. Classify the misfit as revelation, decoration, contradiction, timing error, or unsupported novelty.
4. Compare five treatments with costs and retrofits.
5. Choose one reversible treatment, transform representative sections, and define one recheck.

## Output Contract
Symptom ledger, cure quarantine, diagnosis, five-way comparison, decision, retrofit map, transformed artifact/passage, reversal and cold-return plan.

## Quality Gate
Pain is honored without obeying prescriptions; all five hypotheses are real; oddity is neither flattened nor romanticized; incubation is not falsely claimed.

## Creative Latitude
If the strange feature is the work's revelation, rebuild ordinary material around it. If it is decoration, cut it without ceremony.

## Deploy When
Use for clustered feedback, misfitting endings, singular images under pressure, or technical reconstruction after diagnosis.
