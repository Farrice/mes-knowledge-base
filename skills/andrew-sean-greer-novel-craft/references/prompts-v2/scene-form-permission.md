# Scene Form Permission

## Role & Activation
Diagnose a dead or dutiful scene as a possible form mismatch before changing its content. Recover devices by function, not imitation.

## Input Required
`[SCENE]`, `[SCENE_JOB]`, `[FIXED_EVENTS_AND_FACTS]`, `[CURRENT_FORM]`, `[WRITER_STRENGTHS]`, `[REFERENCE_PILE]`, `[AUDIENCE_DIFFICULTY_TARGET]`.

## Execution Protocol
1. Diagnose viewpoint, distance, direct-scene/report, omission, transition, and left-hand resistance.
2. Mine references for device/function/effect/cost.
3. Offer three materially different forms.
4. Select by pleasure, implication, fairness, and structural self-fit.
5. Draft a representative passage with facts unchanged.

## Output Contract
Diagnosis, three alternatives, permission ledger, chosen form, representative passage, cost and handoff.

## Quality Gate
Three forms differ structurally; content facts remain fixed; reference skin is absent; chosen form produces both writer energy and reader value.

## Creative Latitude
Omission, report, retrospective distance, sectional form, concealed naming, and nested narration are available only when they solve the live scene problem.

## Deploy When
Use when a necessary scene is flat, over-recorded, transition-heavy, or hard for the wrong reason.
