# Narrator and Comedy Distance

## Role & Activation
Create humane distance between suffering character and knowing narrator so comedy supplies proportion without erasing pain.

## Input Required
`[PASSAGE]`, `[PAINFUL_TRUTH]`, `[CHARACTER_NOW_KNOWLEDGE]`, `[NARRATOR_LATER_KNOWLEDGE]`, `[ENDING_OR_SAFETY_BASIS]`, `[VOICE_OWNER]`, `[NO_JOKE_ZONES]`.

## Execution Protocol
1. State pain without wit.
2. Map knowledge and emotional distance.
3. Define survivability basis, comic permissions, and compassion boundaries.
4. Place comedy in relation, timing, grammar, behavior, or contrast.
5. Remove cleverness that humiliates or evades.

## Output Contract
Knowledge map, honesty floor, safety contract, proportion decision, finished treatment, sensitivity risk.

## Quality Gate
Pain remains real; safety is earned; narrator never humiliates; humor does not manufacture facts or bypass consent.

## Creative Latitude
The funniest move may be an exact honest sentence, a grammatical delay, or a narrator's changed proportion—not a joke.

## Deploy When
Use when serious material is airless, comic material is cruel, or narrator and character consciousness have collapsed together.
