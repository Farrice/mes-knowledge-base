# Narrator Distance

## Input Required
Character-now truth, narrator-later knowledge, pain boundary, voice owner, ending knowledge, and excerpt.

## Hard Stop / Refusal
Do not let the narrator humiliate the character, deny pain, or imply safety the story has not earned.

## Diagnose Before Treat
Map what character and narrator each know, believe, misread, and may joke about. Locate merged consciousness or superior contempt.

## Procedure
Set temporal/emotional distance; define comic permission and compassion limits; rewrite the passage; test whether pain and survivability coexist.

## Output Contract
Knowledge table, distance contract, comic permission, compassion boundary, revised passage, and risk note.

## Quality Gate
The character's trouble stays real; narrator knowledge adds proportion, not ridicule.

## Handoff
Pass the distance contract to the prose owner or `comedy-through-fire.md`.
