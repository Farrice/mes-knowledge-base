# Comedy Through Fire

## Input Required
Painful event, facts, narrator relation, audience safety boundary, comic intent, and locked lines.

## Hard Stop / Refusal
Do not joke around active danger, humiliate the suffering person, deny loss, or use wit to evade truth.

## Diagnose Before Treat
State the pain plainly; identify who can know the outcome, where safety comes from, and which current joke violates proportion.

## Procedure
Set honesty floor and survivability contract; choose proportion; place comedy in narration, timing, grammar, behavior, or contrast; rewrite; remove defensive cleverness.

## Output Contract
Pain statement, safety contract, proportion decision, timing map, and finished treatment.

## Quality Gate
The reader touches the serious material long enough for it to become real and is carried rather than tricked past it.

## Handoff
Pass to voice/domain owner for sensitivity and truth review.
