# Crossword Assemble

## Input Required
Unordered scene cards/fragments, host contract, chronology facts, preservation lock, and optional echo map.

## Hard Stop / Refusal
Do not preserve every fragment, fabricate connective facts, or produce chronology filler.

## Diagnose Before Treat
For each scene, extract characters, objects, promises, time, unresolved pressure, and tonal obligations. Mark contradictions and orphan cards.

## Procedure
Build a Scene Lattice; infer adjacency and dependencies; specify the smallest missing scenes; compare sequences; choose one with reasons; name cuts and unanswered blanks.

## Output Contract
Scene Lattice, obligations, missing-scene briefs, proposed sequence, cut/hold list, and revised reconstruction package.

## Quality Gate
Existing scenes constrain the blanks; no invented truth; every new scene pays at least one named obligation.

## Handoff
Pass the lattice to `form-permission.md` or the fiction owner.
