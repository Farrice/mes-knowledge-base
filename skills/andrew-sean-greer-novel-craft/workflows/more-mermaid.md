# More Mermaid

## Input Required
Misfit element, surrounding draft, feedback symptoms, private-vision statement, host constraints, and reversibility boundary.

## Hard Stop / Refusal
Do not default to deletion, protect oddity merely because it is odd, or violate facts/permission to make it fit.

## Diagnose Before Treat
Ask whether the element is revelation, decoration, contradiction, timing error, or unsupported novelty. Preserve the current version.

## Procedure
Compare `CUT`, `MOVE`, `TRANSFORM`, `PREPARE`, `INTENSIFY`; state costs and required retrofits; choose one; revise representative sections; test retrospective inevitability.

## Output Contract
Five-way comparison, decision, retrofit map, transformed passage/artifact, and reversal plan.

## Quality Gate
The chosen treatment serves the private vision and the whole work; useful oddity is earned, useless oddity is not romanticized.

## Handoff
Pass operations to `lacquer-revision.md` and the destination owner.
