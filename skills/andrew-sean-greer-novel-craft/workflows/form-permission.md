# Form Permission

## Input Required
Scene job, fixed events/facts, current form, writer's generative strengths, source pile or references, and host contract.

## Hard Stop / Refusal
Do not alter content merely to create novelty, imitate reference skin, or treat difficulty as artistic proof.

## Diagnose Before Treat
Identify whether resistance comes from viewpoint, temporal distance, direct scene/report, omission, transition burden, or a disowned easy capability.

## Procedure
Mine references for device/function/cost; propose three materially different forms; test pleasure, implication, fairness, and self-fit; choose one; draft a representative passage; record the device ledger.

## Output Contract
Form diagnosis, three alternatives, permission/device ledger, selection rationale, representative passage, and self-fit risk.

## Quality Gate
The form solves a live problem, creates reader value, and does not sound like one reference.

## Handoff
Pass chosen form and locked content to the scene or destination owner.
