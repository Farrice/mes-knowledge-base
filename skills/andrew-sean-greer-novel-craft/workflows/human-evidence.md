# Human Evidence

## Input Required
Observed notes or verified facts, character/person function, cliché boundary, consent/privacy constraints, and excerpt.

## Hard Stop / Refusal
Do not invent observation, stereotype, expose private detail, or replace a cliché with its conspicuous opposite.

## Diagnose Before Treat
Separate generic scenery/biography from traces that imply choice, habit, compromise, welcome, resistance, or history.

## Procedure
Inventory exact traces; filter cliché and showy anti-cliché; select only supported details; rewrite around behavior under pressure; preserve uncertainty.

## Output Contract
Human-trace inventory, exclusion list, character-capture sentence, revised passage, and fact map.

## Quality Gate
Each detail is observed or supplied, socially revealing, privacy-safe, and non-decorative.

## Handoff
Pass fact-mapped prose to the host owner.
