# Reader Invention

## Input Required
Excerpt, audience, payoff, difficulty target, facts required for fairness, and host constraints.

## Hard Stop / Refusal
Do not remove safety, causal, product, or factual information; do not equate ambiguity with sophistication.

## Diagnose Before Treat
Label each element `SAY`, `SHOW`, or `WITHHOLD`; for every withheld element, name its clue and intended inference.

## Procedure
Set difficulty; preserve required facts; remove redundant explanation; seed fair clues; test a literal and inferential reading; restore any missing causal bridge.

## Output Contract
Say/show/withhold map, clue ledger, difficulty setting, revised passage, and failure risks.

## Quality Gate
Readers can infer the intended line without guessing unsupported facts; effort is proportionate to reward.

## Handoff
Pass revised passage to destination owner with the locked fact list.
