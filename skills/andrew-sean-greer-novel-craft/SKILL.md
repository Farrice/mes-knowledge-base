# Andrew Sean Greer Novel Craft

This is a method system, not a voice clone. It translates one reviewed interview into twelve bounded procedures. Greer owns latent story reality and oddity-preserving revision; the destination expert owns truth, safety, conversion, platform, client intent, and final form.

## Status and Proof Boundary

- `COLD_BUILD_COMPLETE`: internal files and frozen proof fixtures exist.
- `ORCHESTRATOR_ATTESTED`: candidates were built and checked in this implementation context.
- `HUMAN_BLIND_VERDICT: NO EVENT`.
- `REGISTRATION: NOT AUTHORIZED`; `CONDUCTOR_WIRING: NOT AUTHORIZED`; `DEPLOYMENT: NOT AUTHORIZED`.
- Passing fixtures show bounded behavior on frozen examples. They do not prove universal improvement, market effect, or Greer-equivalent judgment.

## When to Use

- Unordered scenes or fragments need reconstruction without a generic beat sheet.
- Repeated images seem meaningful but the governing pattern is unclear.
- A scene is dutiful because its point of view, temporal distance, or reporting form is wrong.
- Explanation has displaced reader participation.
- Comedy either denies pain or becomes too grim to carry the reader.
- Feedback correctly locates pain but proposes a cure that would flatten the work.
- A strange ending or image seems misfit and deserves a cut/move/transform/prepare/intensify comparison.
- A story-bearing health, strategy, founder, video, or sales artifact needs lived human reality without changing frozen truth.

## When Not to Use

- Formatting-only, citation correction, tiny direct answer, transcription, arithmetic, or factual-summary work.
- Medical, legal, financial, safety, dosage, or scientific claims without their domain owner and evidence packet.
- A proof-poor offer: retain `UNTESTED / NO EVENT`; do not invent buyers, outcomes, or specificity.
- Client-selected language where permission is limited to punctuation or formatting.
- A draft whose premise, audience pull, or destination mechanics are the real failure; hand off to the relevant owner.

## Required Host Contract

Every run needs: `[ARTIFACT]`, `[OBJECTIVE]`, `[AUDIENCE]`, `[DESTINATION_FORMAT]`, `[SOURCE_OF_TRUTH]`, `[LOCKED_MATERIAL]`, `[VOICE_OWNER]`, `[HARD_CONSTRAINTS]`, `[FELT_STANDARD]`, and `[SCOPE]`. Missing truth or permission yields `STOP_FOR_CONTEXT`, not invented continuity.

## Route Selection

1. Lock the host contract and diagnose before treatment.
2. Classify the state: `GENERATE`, `ASSEMBLE`, `REVISE`, `DIAGNOSE_FEEDBACK`, or `REALITY_PASS`.
3. Choose one to three components by default. A full novel reconstruction may use the ordered route.
4. Work in altitude order: latent architecture → scene form → page pleasure → grammatical timing.
5. Hand to the destination owner, then run only the relevant Greer reality checks.

| structure set; revision needed | `lacquer-revision.md` | operations plan + cold-return protocol |

Never run all twelve merely because they exist. Each selected workflow must change a named decision or passage.

## Recognition Test

The system is working when the artifact feels discovered rather than imposed: early details gain later force, omission is fair, surprise changes perception, characters leave exact human traces, comedy preserves suffering, and the strangest useful material is either earned or cut for a named reason. The machinery should be invisible in the finished work.

## Quality Gate

- Diagnose-before-treat is visible in the receipt, not in the prose.
- Every Greer mechanism resolves to `references/source-ledger.md`.
- Factual clauses remain inside the supplied source-of-truth packet.
- Omission has sufficient clues; surprise is earned; oddity is integrated rather than decorated.
- One function owner remains clear and locked material survives byte-for-byte when required.
- The result never claims registration, deployment, market proof, or A-tier embodiment.
