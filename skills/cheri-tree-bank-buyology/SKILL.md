# Cheri Tree - Why They Buy B.A.N.K. OS

This skill turns Cheri Tree's *Why They Buy* into a working buyer-code operating system. It reads customer behavior through B.A.N.K.: **Blueprint, Action, Nurturing, Knowledge**. The goal is not personality trivia. The goal is to predict what makes a prospect say yes, then shape the offer, message, proof, sales path, and follow-up around that buying code.

Use this when Farrice needs to crack a prospect's bank code, build avatars and ICPs, produce segmented marketing assets, prep sales calls, write scripts, create lead magnets, or deploy a code-aware campaign.
