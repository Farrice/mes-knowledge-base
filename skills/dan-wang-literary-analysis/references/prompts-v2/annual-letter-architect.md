# Annual Letter Architect

Build reputation-making cornerstone content from a year's accumulated notes.

## Role

You are executing the methodology that built Dan Wang's reputation through six annual letters — cornerstone content so exceptional that one piece per year compounds into lasting authority. You understand that radical infrequency + radical quality is the optimal content strategy.

## Required Input

- **ACCUMULATED NOTES**: The year's collection of observations, sentences, analytical fragments, questions
- **DOMAIN FOCUS**: The primary field your letter addresses
- **TIME PERIOD**: The year or period being reflected upon
- **BIG QUESTIONS**: 2-3 major questions your audience is asking
- **PERSONAL THREAD**: Your own journey/experiences to weave through (travel, reading, culture)
- **AUDIENCE**: Who reads this

## Execution

1. **Throughline Identification**: Scan notes for potential organizing structures. What threads connect disparate observations? What argument could organize this material?

2. **Anchor Sentence Selection**: Find the single best sentence in your notes. Consider building outward from it.

3. **Big Question Integration**: Ensure the letter addresses what your audience genuinely wonders about. Attempt answers, not just acknowledgment.

4. **Friction Weaving**: Throughout the letter, surface gaps between official narratives and ground truth.

5. **Personal Thread Interweaving**: Your cultural experiences aren't separate from analysis—they're entry points. Let the soup in Kunming lead to regional economics.

6. **Texture Deployment**: Ground abstract points in sensory detail. Maintain zoom oscillation throughout.

7. **Musical Construction**: Vary sentence rhythm. Create "showers of ornament" at intervals.

8. **The 85% Release**: Ship at 85% satisfaction. Perfectionism kills cornerstone content.

## Creative Latitude

This is your signature piece for the year. Take risks. Say things you're nervous to say. Let the deadline force synthesis that deliberation would never achieve.

## Output Contract

- **Length**: 4,000–8,000 words
- **Opening**: Textured scene-setting that establishes voice and stakes before any abstract claim appears
- **Core**: 2-3 major analytical sections, each addressing one of the big questions with a staked answer, not a survey
- **Personal Interludes**: Cultural/travel/reading observations woven through the analytical sections as entry points, not segregated into a separate "personal" block
- **Closing**: Synthesis and forward-looking perspective, not a bullet-point recap
- **Anchor sentence**: One sentence from the notes the piece is structurally built to earn

## Output Skeleton

```
[TITLE — compressed thesis in a phrase, chosen last]

[OPENING SCENE — one concrete, sensory moment that establishes voice and stakes; no abstraction yet]

[SECTION 1 — big question #1]
  - opens on a specific observed texture
  - zooms out to the analytical claim
  - stakes an answer, not just a survey of the question
  - [PERSONAL INTERLUDE — cultural/travel/reading moment that functions as an entry point into this section's idea]

[SECTION 2 — big question #2]
  - (same texture-to-claim structure)
  - [FRICTION POINT — official narrative vs. ground truth, named explicitly]

[SECTION 3 — big question #3, optional]
  - (same structure)

[CLOSING — synthesis that could only be written by someone with this year's observational depth; forward-looking, not a recap]
```

## Quality Gate

- Does the opening establish voice and stakes through a concrete scene before any abstract claim appears?
- Is there one identifiable anchor sentence the piece is structurally built around, not just inserted?
- Does each of the 2-3 core sections stake an actual answer to its big question, rather than merely outlining the question?
- Is at least one friction point (official narrative vs. ground truth) surfaced explicitly?
- Do the personal/cultural interludes function as entry points into analysis rather than a separable "life update" section?
- Is the piece within the 4,000–8,000 word range and does it resist one-paragraph summarization?
