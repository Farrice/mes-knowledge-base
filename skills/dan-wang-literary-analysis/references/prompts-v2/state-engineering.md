# State Engineering Mantra

Create and deploy identity-trigger mantras for accessing optimal creative state under deadline pressure.

## Role

You are applying Dan Wang's "qualm-collected-Canadian" technique — using personal identity mantras to access specific psychological states regardless of external circumstances.

## Required Input

- **DESIRED STATE**: The psychological/creative state you need to access (calm under pressure, bold creativity, disciplined execution)
- **CURRENT CHALLENGES**: What typically disrupts this state for you
- **IDENTITY ELEMENTS**: Aspects of your background or aspirations that could anchor this state

## Execution

1. **State Definition**: Precisely define the psychological state you need. Not "focused" but "calmly persistent despite uncertainty."

2. **Identity Archaeology**: What aspects of your background, heritage, role models, or aspirations embody this state? Wang uses "Canadian" for its connotation of calm, steady, polite-but-persistent.

3. **Compression**: Condense into 3-5 words that evoke the full psychological profile. The mantra should feel slightly unusual—that strangeness aids memory.

4. **Trigger Testing**: Say the mantra when resistance appears. Does it shift your state? Refine until it reliably triggers the desired psychology.

5. **Deployment Protocol**: Embed the mantra in your work routine—written on monitor, said before writing sessions, repeated when deadline pressure appears.

## Output Contract

- **Mantra**: A 3-5 word phrase, specific to the user's own identity elements — never a generic productivity slogan
- **Invocation explanation**: What psychological profile the mantra compresses and why those specific words were chosen
- **Deployment moments**: The concrete situations in the user's actual routine where the mantra will be used
- **Refinement guidance**: How to tell if the mantra has stopped working and what to adjust

## Output Skeleton

```
## Your Mantra
"[3-5 word phrase]"

## What It Invokes
[the precisely-defined psychological state (not a vague word like "focused") and the identity element(s) it draws on]

## Why These Words
[why this specific compression was chosen over alternatives — what makes it memorable/strange enough to stick]

## Deployment Moments
- [specific recurring situation where resistance appears]
- [specific recurring situation]
- [where/how it's embedded in the routine — monitor, pre-session ritual, etc.]

## Refinement Notes
[how to detect the mantra has stopped shifting state, and what to try next]
```

## Quality Gate

- Is the desired state defined precisely (a specific compound description), not a generic word like "focused" or "productive"?
- Does the mantra draw on an identity element genuinely supplied by the user, rather than a stock phrase?
- Is the mantra 3-5 words and does the output explain why that specific compression was chosen?
- Are the deployment moments concrete situations in the user's actual routine, not generic "when needed" language?
- Does the output include a real test for whether the mantra is working, not just an assertion that it will?
