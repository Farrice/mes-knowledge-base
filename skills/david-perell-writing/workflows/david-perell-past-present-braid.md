# Past-Present Braid

## Pre-Flight Gate

Read `genius.md` and the Past-Present Braiding pattern. Require grounded historical material, a grounded present observation, thesis, sources, target format, and output mode. Missing thesis or present observation returns `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`; unsupported load-bearing claims return `HOLD`. A full narrative needs `/shaan-story-deploy` for story dosage. Multi-expert finished prose routes through `how-i-write-os`. When text ships under Farrice's name, load `_active/farrice-brand/voice/VOICE-CARD.md` and declare the dial mode.

## Input Required

1. Historical material and sources.
2. Present observation and sources.
3. Thesis and intended reader shift.
4. Target format.
5. Output mode: `COMPLETE OUTLINE` or `ONE DRAFTED SECTION`.
6. Optional story-dosage receipt.

## Procedure

### 1. Lock the Braid Thesis

State what the past should reveal about the present and which modern assumption has become normalized.

### 2. Build the Source Ledger

Separate verified history, present observation, interpretation, and analogy. Record attribution, dates, proof states, and unsupported bridges.

### 3. Atomize the Material

List historical beats, present observations, continuities, contrasts, and unresolved tensions.

### 4. Design Revelation Crossings

For each crossing, specify the historical beat, supported bridge, present return, temporal regrounding, and revelation delta—what the reader understands now that they did not before.

### 5. Sequence by Insight

Do not alternate mechanically. Remove a present return that merely repeats the thesis or exists only as transition.

### 6. Render and Handoff

Produce the selected outline or one section. Preserve sources and unresolved claims. Route story dosage to Shaan, deeper analysis to Dan Wang, larger literary architecture to Susan Orlean, and multi-expert composition to How-I-Write OS.
