## Input Required

1. Voice note, transcript, or full source expression.
2. Core idea and desired reader takeaway.
3. Audience.
4. Allowed duration targets; default 60, 20, and 10 seconds.
5. Optional actual read-aloud timings.

## Procedure

### 1. Preserve the Full Version

Retain the source expression before editing. Mark unsupported claims and unresolved ambiguity.

### 2. Freeze the Core Lock

Record the invariant claim, necessary support, nuance that cannot be removed, audience outcome, and voice constraints.

### 3. Compress Downward

Create the 60-second version first, then 20, then 10. Remove repetition before removing logic. Introduce no new claim or evidence. If the source is already shorter than a target, do not pad it.

### 4. Compare Each Transition

Record meaning loss, certainty drift, causal drift, scope drift, and voice drift. Duration is `ESTIMATED` unless actual read-aloud evidence exists.

### 5. Decide

- `READY`: every version preserves the Core Lock.
- `REWORK`: a shorter version distorts meaning or becomes a generic slogan.
- `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`: the invariant claim cannot be frozen honestly.

### 6. Route

A selected sentence needing a memorable image may proceed to `02-compress-to-memorable`. That workflow produces a Buffett-style conceptual ladder; it is not the same artifact. Re-expansion after compression is Nathan Barry/Ultraspeaking, not part of David's verified 60-20-10 sequence.
