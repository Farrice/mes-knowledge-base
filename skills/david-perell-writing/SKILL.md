# David Perell Writing — Idea to Culture

This skill decides whether writing needs a better idea, a more honest supported claim, better craft, a truthful cultural carrier, or protection from attention drift. It preserves the original POP system—Personal, Observational, Playful—while adding an upstream and downstream decision layer grounded in David Perell's 2026 Nathan Barry interview.

## Start Here

For a substantial writing request, begin with [Idea-Courage-Craft Triage](workflows/david-perell-idea-courage-craft-triage.md). Do not assume the ideas are fine. The triage returns one primary route:

- `IDEA`: mine observations, then refine the strongest bit.
- `COURAGE`: surface the feared supported sentence and return a human decision.
- `CRAFT`: use the smallest preserved POP workflow.
- `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`: hold and request the missing discriminating input.

Timing requests must pass [Current-Fit Diagnostic](workflows/david-perell-current-fit-diagnostic.md) with a sourced date and expiry before any timely frame is produced.

## When NOT to Use This Skill

- Do not use it to manufacture a topic, hot take, personal memory, location, emotion, dialogue, or controversy from missing evidence.
- Do not use it for live trend discovery, analytics, publication, outreach, or external action.
- Do not use a timely shell when the signal packet lacks a source date, fit, or expiry.
- Do not use POP repair when triage finds an idea or courage problem.
- Do not use the 2026 interview as evidence for POP; that framework remains in the older 2026-07-01 evidence lane.
- Do not claim reach, recognition, conversion, sales, or revenue without a real event.

## Operating Invariants

- Expand before compressing.
- Separate idea, execution, current, audience fit, reach, conversion, and revenue.
- Freeze the timeless thesis before changing the why-now shell.
- Use only supplied lived detail in placeful work.
- Keep `HOLD`, `EXPIRED CURRENT`, `NO EVENT`, `NO PERMISSION`, and `UNTESTED EFFECT` distinct.
- When writing ships under Farrice's name, load `_active/farrice-brand/voice/VOICE-CARD.md` and apply an explicit dial mode before drafting.
