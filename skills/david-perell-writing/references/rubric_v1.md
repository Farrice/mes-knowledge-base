# David Perell Idea-to-Culture Rubric v1

Score 0–10. Pass requires overall at least 8, every critical criterion at least 7, and factual grounding at least 6. Criteria 1–5 are critical.

| # | Criterion | 4 — weak | 7 — deployable | 10 — exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Source fidelity and attribution | Speakers blur | David, Nathan, third parties, and synthesis stay separate | Every material mechanic has an auditable timestamp and boundary |
| 2 | Bottleneck routing | Lists all three | Chooses one primary with evidence | Holds under ambiguity and sequences only necessary support |
| 3 | Timeless-core preservation | Frame roughly resembles core | Thesis, claims, evidence, and conclusion survive | Strong timely frame and fallback with a complete delta receipt |
| 4 | Current integrity and expiry | Names an event | Records source, date, fit, and expiry | Rejects a tempting false current and restores a better evergreen path |
| 5 | Truthful placefulness | Adds generic detail | Uses only supplied details and names gaps | Sparse true detail makes the source unmistakable without invention |
| 6 | Distinctiveness without imitation | Recycles surface style | Transfers a structure across context | Produces an original result with clear source distance |
| 7 | POP provenance discipline | Treats new interview as POP evidence | Preserves the older evidence lane | Routes into POP while explicitly denying new-source verification |

| 11 | Learning-loop quality | Calls a receipt improvement | Each rep yields a named observation or next behavior | Repeated evidence shows a real change while market effects stay bounded |

## Recognition Gate

Would David Perell recognize the result as developing a worthwhile idea, stating it honestly, and finding a truthful cultural carrier—or as generic growth content with his nouns pasted on? Polish does not rescue a wrong route. Timing does not rescue a false claim. A clean shell with thesis drift fails.

## Proof Labels

- `STATIC_CONTRACT_PASS`: files encode the required behavior.
- `RUNTIME_OBSERVED`: a fresh run produced the required artifact on a held-out input.
- `HUMAN_RECOGNITION_PASS`: Farrice judged a side-by-side result indistinguishable or preferred.
- `MARKET_OBSERVED`: a real external event produced attributable behavior.

These labels are cumulative only when their separate evidence exists.
