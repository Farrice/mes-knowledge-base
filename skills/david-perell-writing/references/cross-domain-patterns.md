# Cross-Domain Boundaries and Stacking

One owner decides each stage. Supporting systems supply bounded inputs or downstream craft; they do not overwrite the Perell judgment artifact.

| Need | Owner | Handoff into or out of David Perell | Non-ownership rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live current discovery | `attention-hijack-hooks` | Sourced, dated signal packet | Perell never invents or live-searches a signal |
| Current lifecycle | `lulu-cheng-meservey-communications` | Trajectory, expiry support | Perell decides payload fit, not lifecycle analytics |
| Queue, publishing, metrics | `kieran-flanagan-content-engine` / content ops | Authorized asset or response evidence | Perell never publishes, queues, or calls reach quality |
| Cross-domain recombination | `dan-koe-multipassionate-mastery` | Developed idea candidates | Perell does not run broad recombination |
| Formal taste training | `oren-taste-development` | Critique vocabulary or taste verdict | Perell placefulness is a diagnostic, not a taste curriculum |
| Format transfer/testing | `tim-danilov-niche-bending`, `sky-tan-format-engine` | Locked claim and selected form | Format owners cannot change the thesis |
| Analytical / literary depth | `dan-wang-literary-analysis`, Susan Orlean | Braid map or evidence-safe draft | Perell owns the braid judgment, not full literary finishing |
| Placeful line repair | `ocean-vuong-perceptual-writing`, `high-taste-writing-os` | Missing-evidence list and safe details | Repair cannot invent lived material |
| Platform-native production | Lara Acosta, Diandra Escobar | Locked shell and target platform | Platform formatting cannot mutate claims |
| Multi-expert composition / story dosage | `how-i-write-os`, `/shaan-story-deploy` | Selected Perell artifact | Conductors compose; they do not absorb the Perell method |

## Required Handoff Objects

1. **Signal Packet:** source, date, observed signal, audience relevance, expiry estimate, uncertainty.
2. **Idea Card:** claim, evidence, tension, repeatable core, unknowns.
3. **Core Lock:** invariant thesis, claims, evidence, conclusion, prohibited distortions.
4. **Learning Receipt:** event state, observation, bounded hypothesis, confidence, next rep.
5. **Placefulness Packet:** supplied facts, forbidden inventions, missing evidence, safe edits.

A missing required object returns HOLD or routes upstream. It never triggers creative fabrication.
