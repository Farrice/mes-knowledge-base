# 01 — Messaging Evolution Audit

The orchestrating diagnostic for this skill. Most messaging audits grade a *single, current* message. This one grades the message *as a living system* — is it still evolving, has the market drifted the meaning of its key words, and is there a testing loop keeping it alive? Miller's frame: a brand that stops evolving its words *"would be antiquated Blockbuster."*

## Pre-Flight Gate

Run this audit when:
- A brand "tested fine" but growth has plateaued and nobody can say why.
- The same tagline has run unchanged for 2+ years.
- A key word in the positioning may have drifted in meaning (e.g., "virtual," "AI," "agency").
- Before any rebrand or major message change — establish the baseline first.

**Do NOT** run this to fix a single sentence — that's `donald-miller-cognitive-load/01-cognitive-load-autopsy`. This audits the *system*, not the sentence.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `genius.md` (this skill) — Core Thesis + Patterns 3 (lane), 6 (testing loop) + the Quality Rubric. Optionally cross-load `donald-miller-cognitive-load/genius.md` if individual sound bites also need scoring.

## Execution

**Step 1 — Identity vs. Expression split.** Write two columns: (a) the brand's **identity** (the things it must never change — its lane, who it is, what it refuses to be) and (b) its **expression** (every word/tagline/message it currently uses). Miller's test: Belay held identity (*fractional, human, US-based, not full-time, not on-site*) while changing expression hundreds of times. If the user can't articulate column (a) crisply, that is finding #1 — there's no fixed identity to defend.

**Step 2 — Lifecycle stage diagnosis (the S-curve).** Miller's named model (*How to Grow Your Small Business*): every brand hits the **S-curve** — *"the business begins to grow... then things take a turn. The business owner is pulled out of their sweet spot... managing problems rather than continuing to create the magic that grew the company."* Messaging is an early casualty of the S-curve: when the founder leaves the sweet spot, nobody owns the words, and the message ossifies. Place the brand:
- *Generalist* — young, "everyone wears 10 hats," one general message for all. Correct early; a liability past ~$5–10M.
- *S-curve onset* — growing, but the founder is now firefighting; the message that grew the company is running on autopilot. **The danger zone for messaging drift.**
- *Specializing* — segmenting messages and teams (see `05-message-siloing-system`).
- *Drifting* — chasing adjacent revenue, lane blurring (see `04-lane-discipline-diagnostic`).
- *Stalled / Blockbuster* — message unchanged while market moved; key words decaying.
The cure Miller names is to *"professionalize your operation"* — install the systems (and the message ownership) that let the words keep evolving without the founder. Name the stage and the single transition the brand is overdue for.

**Step 3 — Word-drift scan.** For each load-bearing word in the positioning, ask: *has the culture changed what this word means since we adopted it?* (Belay: "VA" → "bot," forcing a return to "EA.") Flag every word whose connotation has inverted or eroded. These are the re-test priorities.

**Step 4 — Testing-posture check.** Score the brand's *abracadabra loop*: Is anyone actively AB-testing wording? What % of leadership/marketing attention goes to "how do we word that"? (Miller's benchmark: CEO ~20–25%, marketing team's primary job.) A brand with zero live message tests is by definition stalled.

**Step 5 — Output the verdict + next move.** One stage diagnosis, a ranked list of drifted words to re-test, and the single highest-leverage next workflow in this skill.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Asset | Audit emphasis |
|---|---|
| Website / homepage | First-date message integrity (is it one idea?) + drifted hero words |
| Sales deck / call script | Internal cognitive load — is one rep carrying all segments? |
| Brand name / tagline | Identity-vs-expression split + 3-year rebrand exposure |
| Marketplace (two-sided) | Audit BOTH demand and supply-side messages as separate systems |
| Category-creation product | Word-drift + bridge-word presence (is the new category trust-bridged?) |

## Output Requirements

- **Identity / Expression table** (what's fixed vs. disposable).
- **Lifecycle stage** with the one overdue transition named.
- **Drifted-word list**, ranked by re-test priority.
- **Testing-posture score** + the abracadabra loop to install.
- **Routing line**: which of workflows 02–06 to run next, and why.

## Quality Gate

Reject the audit if it (per `genius.md` anti-patterns):
- Grades the current message without diagnosing whether the *system* is still evolving.
- Treats a stale word as sacred identity (word-clinging).
- Recommends a rebrand without pricing it at 3 years (`06`).
- Ships a "fixed" message instead of a testing loop.
