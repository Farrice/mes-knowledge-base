# 06 — Rebrand-Patience Protocol

Renaming or merging brands has a fixed, brutal recovery cost most leaders underestimate. Miller: *"When you make a decision like that, you should not expect the ship to be righted for 3 years — and 3 years only if you're aggressive, only if you're constantly saying it."* Belay's "Mission Possible" merger (EA Help + Mag Bookkeeping → Belay, 2017): growth slowed, *"everything felt heavy,"* ~18 months to recover, years before the market connected "Belay" to "EA Help."

## Pre-Flight Gate

Run this when:
- A rename, merger, or brand consolidation is on the table or underway.
- Two brands with separate sites/teams/leadership are being unified into one.
- Leadership is mid-rebrand and panicking at a growth dip (the predictable 12–18 month trough).
- A bridge-word or category rename (`03`) is large enough to function as a rebrand.

**Do NOT** use this for a tagline tweak or message iteration — those carry no recovery cost. This is for *name/identity* changes only.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `genius.md` Pattern 5 (Rebrand Patience) + the "rebrand impatience" anti-pattern. Cross-load `donald-miller-culture-turnaround/stakeholder-alignment-plan` — internal alignment is the gating dependency here.

## Execution

**Step 1 — Price the rebrand at 3 years.** Set explicit expectations: the brand will not be fully recovered for ~3 years, with a visible trough around 12–18 months (Belay: ~18 months to regain traction). Put this in writing *before* launch so the inevitable dip isn't misread as failure and the clock isn't reset by a panic-reversal.

**Step 2 — Sequence internal alignment FIRST.** The non-negotiable dependency: *"Your team has to be on board... when you realize they're not, it's very possible we can't get this done."* Employee confusion *"resonates out there."* Before any external launch:
- Confirm leadership owns multiple units under one identity (not running parallel fiefdoms).
- Unify processes, branding, and vernacular internally.
- Get certified partners / front-line staff genuinely bought in — not just informed.
Map who is NOT yet on board; that list is the launch blocker.

**Step 3 — Build the aggression plan.** The 3 years is a floor *only under constant repetition.* Silence resets the clock. Specify the drumbeat: how often, on how many channels, the brand will *say the new name and connect it to the old* ("Belay was EA Help"). Aggression = the accelerant; under-saying it is the most common cause of a 5-year recovery instead of 3.

**Step 4 — Plan the old→new bridge.** How will you carry recognition across the gap? Options: explicit "[New] was [Old]" messaging, a bridge word (`03`), parallel-running the old name during transition. Budget for the fact that *"even if I say it today, people don't realize Belay was EA Help."*

**Step 5 — Set the dip dashboard.** Define the metrics that will dip (brand search, traction, inbound) and the recovery thresholds, so the trough is monitored against plan rather than reacted to emotionally.

**Step 6 — Output the protocol.** A 3-year timeline with the trough marked, an internal-alignment gate (who's not on board), the aggression/repetition cadence, and the old→new bridge plan.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Rebrand type | Patience emphasis |
|---|---|
| Simple rename | Aggression cadence + old→new bridge dominate |
| Two-brand merger | Internal alignment is the hard part — sequence it first, expect operational "madness" |
| Category rename (via `03`) | Bridge word lowers the recovery cost; still price at 3 years |
| Acquisition integration | Map two cultures onto one identity before external launch |
| Sub-brand consolidation | Decide P&G/J&J separation vs. unification (see `05`) before renaming |

## Output Requirements

- **3-year timeline** with the 12–18 month trough explicitly marked and pre-justified.
- **Internal-alignment gate**: who must be on board, who isn't yet, and the plan to fix it *before* launch.
- **Aggression cadence**: the repetition drumbeat that keeps the clock at 3 years, not 5.
- **Old→new bridge plan** (explicit "[New] was [Old]" / bridge word / parallel-run).
- **Dip dashboard**: which metrics dip, expected depth, recovery thresholds.

## Quality Gate

Reject if (per `genius.md`):
- It expects recovery in months, or treats the 12–18 month dip as failure.
- It launches externally before internal alignment is secured.
- It assumes the new name will stick without an aggressive repetition plan (silence resets the clock).
- No old→new bridge — recognition is left to evaporate.
