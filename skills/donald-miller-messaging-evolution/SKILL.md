# Donald Miller — Messaging Evolution & Positioning Discipline

The three other Donald Miller skills capture the **static** message — how to *score* it (`cognitive-load`), *structure* it as story (`storybrand`), and *align a team* around it (`culture-turnaround`). This skill captures the **dynamic** message: how positioning evolves over a company's entire lifecycle to drive growth, and the discipline to keep evolving without losing the lane.

The organizing paradox: **change your words constantly; never change your lane.** Belay Solutions changed its messaging hundreds of times (EA Help → VA → EA → Belay; "modern staffing" → "accomplish more, juggle less" → "the right hire right now") on its way from $0 to $100M, but never changed who it was (fractional, human, US-based, not full-time, not on-site). Evolution of expression, constancy of identity. A brand that stops evolving its words *"would be antiquated Blockbuster."*

> **Sources** (three): **(1)** StoryBrand Podcast — "How Messaging Grew a $100M Brand" (Miller × Trisha Shurtino, CEO Belay) — the longitudinal case study. **(2)** "How to Grow Your Small Business" interview (Miller, Kathy Heller show). **(3) PRIMARY:** the published book *How to Grow Your Small Business* (HarperCollins Leadership, 2023) — verbatim grounding for the 6-part sales color-key (Wf 02), the rule of three / rule of proportion / "kill your darlings" / product brief (Wf 04), and the S-curve (Wf 01) — plus StoryBrand collateral PDFs (*Close the Client Kit*, *Fully Booked*). Scoped expansion: only frameworks absent from the other three Don Miller skills; the book's ops system (cash flow, meetings, airplane diagnostic) is intentionally out of scope. ## Available Workflows

### Tier 1 — Foundation

| # | Workflow | Produces | Use When |
|---|---------|----------|----------|
| 01 | [Messaging Evolution Audit](workflows/01-messaging-evolution-audit.md) | Lifecycle-stage diagnosis + drifted-word list + testing loop to install | A brand "tested fine" but plateaued; the same tagline has run 2+ years; before any rebrand |
| 02 | [Date-Sequence Architect](workflows/02-date-sequence-architect.md) | A date map — each touchpoint assigned first/second/third/fourth-date + objection chain | A landing page is overloaded; conversion drops between click and call; front-loading suspected |
| 03 | [Bridge-Word Engine](workflows/03-bridge-word-engine.md) | The single word that ports old-category trust into a new one + 2 runners-up | Your category triggers a quality objection ("AI isn't as good as human", "fractional isn't as good as full-time") |

### Tier 2 — Practitioner

| # | Workflow | Produces | Use When |
|---|---------|----------|----------|
| 04 | [Lane-Discipline Diagnostic](workflows/04-lane-discipline-diagnostic.md) | Hold / Silo / Refuse verdict on an expansion, via the net test | New revenue "smells like money"; positioning is blurring; getting leads you can't serve |
| 05 | [Message-Siloing System](workflows/05-message-siloing-system.md) | Silo matrix — segment → pain → message → specialist team → channels | One message/team serves multiple distinct buyer pains; a marketplace needs demand + supply messages |
| 06 | [Rebrand-Patience Protocol](workflows/06-rebrand-patience-protocol.md) | 3-year timeline + internal-alignment gate + aggression cadence + old→new bridge | A rename or brand merger is on the table or mid-flight (and leadership is panicking at the dip) |
