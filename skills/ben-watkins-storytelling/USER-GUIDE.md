# Ben Watkins — User Guide

A practical guide to the commercial-storytelling skill: what it does, how to route into it in 30 seconds, the three plays that earn most of its keep, and where it stops.

## The one-line version

Watkins is your **pitch-room + selling-through-story** layer. "People buy based on emotion, not logic" — so this skill engineers the emotion: the four-pillar anatomy (attention → feel → teach → leave wanting), the 3 C's Titanic stack, the subtraction discipline ("it's the ability to know what to leave out"), and the live-sell psychology no one else on the roster touches (infect-with-purpose, read-the-room, truncate-on-buy-in, riff-for-ownership). Front doors: `/bw-story-selling-system` (deploy one message everywhere) and `/bw-anatomy-audit` (diagnose anything already written).

## Mental model: the showrunner who sells

Watkins runs two rooms at once and refuses to separate them. The writers' room builds the story on four pillars and one governing purpose; the pitch room sells it live to an executive who's tired, skeptical, and on their sixth pitch of the day. Reach for this skill whenever the job is *selling an idea* — to a decision-maker, a client, or a thumb mid-scroll — and the current draft is betting on facts to close a decision emotion makes.

## The three most common plays

**1. Audit, then elevate.** Point `/bw-anatomy-audit` at anything already written — a Parallax edition, a LinkedIn post, a listing sheet, a sales page. It names the failing pillar (most content dies at Pillar 1 or Pillar 4) instead of guessing at line edits. Then `/bw-elevate` attacks from two directions — a scalpel pass that keeps the spine and a blank-page reimagining that tries to beat it — and lays the takes side-by-side for your gut to judge. Elevate expects the audit to exist first, so run them in this order. This pair is the highest value-per-minute in the skill, and no piece is too good for it.

**2. One message, everywhere.** When you have a core message and need brand, pitch, and content shipped coherently, `/bw-story-selling-system` conducts the rest: north star locked, log line cut, brand-world built, pitch staged, hook bank forged. Don't hand-assemble the pieces — the conductor sequences them and keeps the promise consistent across every surface.

**3. The pitch prep.** Real meeting on the calendar: `/bw-north-star` (lock the promise) → `/bw-pitch-room` (build the modular pitch: infect with purpose, plan the truncation points, plant the overlap) → `/bw-subtraction` (cut it to momentum and seeds). The KPI isn't the yes — it's whether they leave the room and immediately call their boss or their spouse about what they just heard.

## The flow layer (why outputs read the way they do)

Every workflow here ships prose through the Flow Gate in `references/flow-and-cadence.md`, because the skill's one confirmed failure mode lives *between* the sentences: pieces that pass every deterministic gate and still read jarring and jolty — subject-hops with no bridge, causal links severed by compression, "open loops" that are really just mentions. The pillars decide what the story does, subtraction decides what stays, and the flow layer governs how the surviving sentences hand off to each other: varied contour, known-to-new handoffs, engineered loops (promise + withhold + stake), a cold read-aloud pass, and cuts that never sever the but/so/which-is-why chain. If an output feels tight but choppy, that reference is the repair manual.

## What it's NOT for (route elsewhere)

- **A fixed brand-message template.** That's Donald Miller — `/storybrand` or `donald-miller-storybrand` supplies the 7-part frame (customer as hero, brand as guide). Stack: Miller frames the message → Watkins sells it in the room and hooks it on the feed.
- **Story-uncovering and engagement craft.** Don't know what a piece is *about*, or need the exact beats where attention drops? That's Stanton — `/stanton-premise-sentence`, `/stanton-clamp-audit`. Stanton architects and clamps → Watkins pitches and hooks.
- **Written direct-response copy.** Awareness ladders, proof mechanisms, VSL blocks belong to Luke Iha and the copy roster (`copy-engine`, `luke-iha-vsl-leads`). Watkins sets the emotional arc above the copy; they write the converting lines inside it.
- **Content psychology and viral compression.** Dopamine loops and novelty are Kallaway; the 5-second moment of change is Shaan Puri. Stack them on the hook; Watkins holds the anatomy and the sell.
- **Org-level belief change** (Margolis) and **identity-level resistance mapping** (`mcraney-deep-canvass`, `icp-deep-dive`) — Watkins turns their profiles into the line that lands, not the other way around.
- **Fact-checking or research.** Nothing here verifies claims. Run `execution/research.py` separately, and keep the attribution line clean: but-therefore is Parker/Stone, log line and Save the Cat are Snyder, world-building is Ralston.
