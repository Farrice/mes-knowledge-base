# Anatomy Audit (Ben Watkins)
This is the table read Watkins runs on his own pages before anyone else sees them — hold the piece up to the four pillars and find out what it actually does, not what it meant to do. "I don't want you to just see my resume. I want you to see me": the audit asks whether the piece makes the reader *feel* or merely describes at them, and it answers with proof lines from the page, never with vibes. And because the master's secret "is the ability to know what to leave out," the X-ray also reads what was left IN between the sentences — CS-02 proved a piece can pass every deterministic gate and still land jolty, so this audit reads flow as hard as it reads pillars. The protocol underneath is fixed: acknowledge what's firing first, case-study what scores high, and leave every elevation door open. A high score is a record, never a lock.

## Pre-Flight
Read before executing:
1. `skills/ben-watkins-storytelling/genius.md` (voice + Decision Framework + Anti-Patterns + § Expert-Specific Quality Rubric — the scoring instrument)
2. `skills/ben-watkins-storytelling/references/source-quotes.md` (calibration anchors — hold scores against the verbatim lines)
3. `skills/ben-watkins-storytelling/references/flow-and-cadence.md` (the connective-tissue layer + § The Flow Gate — Step 3 runs it)
4. `skills/ben-watkins-storytelling/references/case-studies.md` (CS-01/CS-02 entry format — Step 5 writes into it)

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: run the **Decision Framework** in `genius.md § Decision Framework` — but in reverse. You are not asking "what should this piece do"; you are asking "what does this piece already do." If you cannot reverse-engineer even a candidate answer to question 1 (say / why / who / promise), the input is material, not a story — say so as the headline finding and route toward `/bw-north-star` before any rewrite is attempted.

## Input Required
- The piece, verbatim and complete — post, edition, pitch script, sales page, thread set, deck voiceover
- **Where it runs** (LinkedIn, Substack, Threads/X, live room, VSL) — the surface changes what Pillar 1 and the caps demand
- Any known reception: felt verdicts, performance numbers, a client's reaction (the felt verdict outranks the gates — CS-02 is the standing proof)
- Who it was for, if known — otherwise the audit infers it

## Workflow

### Step 1: Reverse-engineer the North Star
Watkins starts every story here: "the first place you start is what I call principles of messaging, which is what are you trying to say? Why are you saying this? Who is this for? And what are you promising the audience?" You run it backward — read the piece cold and infer the four answers *from what is on the page*, not from what the author intended. This is a litmus, not a wish (the Stanton discipline): cite the line that proves each answer.

| North Star question | Inferred answer | Line that proves it |
|---|---|---|
| What is it saying? | | |
| Why is it saying it? | | |
| Who is it for? | | |
| What is it promising? | | |

An unanswerable cell is itself a finding — most drifting pieces have a sayable "what" and an empty "promise."

### Step 2: Score per-pillar, every score with a proof line
Score against `genius.md § Expert-Specific Quality Rubric` — the six ledger criteria below (the case-study ledger's canonical set, so Step 5 compresses 1:1), 1-10. Two hard rules: **every score cites a verbatim line from the piece** (the line that earns it or the absence that caps it), and **any score ≥8 names the rubric anchor it matches** — can't name the anchor, lower the score.

| Criterion | Score | Proof line from the piece | Anchor (if ≥8) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attention (Pillar 1) | /10 | | |
| 3 C's (Pillar 2) | /10 | | |
| Teach (Pillar 3) | /10 | | |
| Leave-wanting (Pillar 4) | /10 | | |
| Subtraction | /10 | | |
| Recognition | /10 | | |

**North Star is not a scored row** — it's a pass/fail four-for-four check: did Step 1 prove all four answers (say / why / who / promise) from the page? Record PASS or FAIL, the way CS-01 logs "clean four-for-four."

Calibrate against the source: Pillar 2 means content × character × circumstance *simultaneous* — "If you get all those three working, then you're gonna have Titanic" — two out of three is a fact, not a feeling. Pillar 3 means "take a world people are familiar with and show them parts of it that they didn't know were there" — a 10 reveals a mechanism, a hidden room, inside a world the reader thought they knew, so they leave fuller than they arrived (substance, not empty calories). Pillar 4 means a question, not a mention: "I actually don't approach it as cliffhangers. I actually approach it as questions."

### Step 3: The flow read
Pillars can pass while the piece still jolts — CS-02's five posts came back classifier-CLEAN and Farrice's felt read was "jarring and jolty." So run the Flow Gate from `references/flow-and-cadence.md § The Flow Gate` as a diagnostic sweep: map the sentence-length contour (mark L/M/S, flag runs of 3+), audit every handoff (does each sentence open on something the reader already holds?), test every loop against promise + withhold + stake (a mention is not a loop), and **read the piece aloud, cold** — the read-aloud is the instrument, not a formality. Record each of the five checks as PASS/FAIL with the line where it breaks.

### Step 4: Name what's FIRING — before anything weak
Acknowledgement is step one of the protocol, not a courtesy. List the moves that are working, each with its verbatim proof line and the mechanism it embodies — the way CS-01 logs `browser of 47 tabs` as recognition at savant level. A rewrite that doesn't know the thousand right micro-decisions tramples them; this list is what makes any later elevation informed instead of blind.

| Firing move | Proof line (verbatim) | Why it fires |
|---|---|---|
| | | |

Only after the firing list is complete: name the weak points, each tied to the pillar or Flow Law it violates, each with its proof line.

### Step 5: Case-study the high scorers
If the felt/gate score lands **9+** (or gate 9+ with Farrice's nod), compress this audit into a ledger entry per the `references/case-studies.md` format — *what it is · composite + per-pillar scores · what's firing (verbatim proof lines) · transferable moves · elevation doors left open* — and note that it should be appended there and cross-linked from the project. CS-01 (the Parallax manifesto) is the model; CS-02 shows failures earn entries too when the lesson is structural.

### Step 6: Leave every elevation door open
List the surgical options — the specific seed to withhold, the loop to thread, the handoff to front — routed to the right scalpel: `/bw-elevate` for the felt-verdict rewrite (takes side-by-side, gut decides), `/bw-subtraction` when the weight is bloat, `stanton-clamp-audit` when the slack is engagement tension. **And state plainly that a full reimagining is equally legitimate.** A 9 is not a do-not-touch sign; the ledger exists so the next pass can pick up a scalpel or a blank page with equal legitimacy.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Format | Adaptation |
|--------|-----------|
| LinkedIn / short-form | Weight Pillar 1 and Recognition heaviest — the open is a third of the piece. One off-pillar line is a material fraction; flag it by position. |
| Substack / long-form | Weight Pillar 3 (teach inside the familiar) and Subtraction; run the flow read per section — contour drift hides in the middle third. |
| Threads/X (char-capped) | Flow read is the primary lens: audit per `flow-and-cadence.md § Platform Notes` — one subject thread per post, every post stands alone, no amputated triads. CS-02 is the reference wound. |
| Sales page / VSL | Emotional Ignition and Open Loop carry the score — "People buy based on emotion, not logic." Flag every stretch that bets on specs; flag over-explaining past the close. |
| Live pitch / deck script | Audit for truncation points (where buy-in likely lands, is there a cut planned?) and withheld seeds the room could ask about; a script with total clarity has no conversation designed in. |
| Brand / campaign asset | Audit against the inferred promise: an asset that scores high while proving a different north star is the one to flag, however good it is. |

## Quality Gate
> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: review against `genius.md § Anti-Patterns` + § Expert-Specific Quality Rubric before delivering.
- Every score carries a **verbatim proof line** from the piece; every ≥8 names its rubric anchor. A number without a line is an opinion, not an X-ray.
- The **firing list precedes the weak list** and is at least as specific — acknowledgement is step one of the protocol, not garnish.
- The north star was **reverse-engineered from the page** (litmus), not transcribed from the author's intent (wish).
- No elevation door was closed: a 9+ produced a case-study entry AND open doors, including the full-reimagining route. "Do not touch" appears nowhere.
- The audit's machinery stays in the audit — if any of this feedback is folded back into the piece itself, the pillars and laws are never named on the page.

**FLOW GATE** (from `references/flow-and-cadence.md § The Flow Gate` — Step 3 must have run all five; one fail = flagged in the audit):
- [ ] **Contour varies.** No run of 3+ same-length sentences; the piece uses short, medium, AND long; long sentences arrive only after the reader is rested.
- [ ] **Every sentence hands off.** Each opens with something the reader already holds (echo word, bound pronoun, named relationship) and ends on the new thing; no subject swap without a bridge; every *it/this/that/all three* binds instantly inside the piece.
- [ ] **Loops are engineered, not asserted.** Each loop = promise + withhold + stake, primed with real information first; 3-persona prediction test run; every opened loop resolves honestly in the same piece.
- [ ] **Survives read-aloud.** Read cold, out loud, at the reader's pace — zero stumbles, stalls, or backtracks. Not performed silently.
- [ ] **Compression kept causality.** Every cut preserved the but/so/which-is-why chain; no garbled fusions, wrong-antecedent pronouns, or amputated lists; each trimmed line still parses first-pass.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Leading with what's broken.** The audit becomes a teardown and the thousand right micro-decisions get trampled in the rewrite. Recovery: stop, complete the firing table with proof lines, then return to the weak list.
- **Scores without proof lines.** "Recognition 8" with no cited line is taste posing as measurement, and the next session can't verify or reuse it. Recovery: for each naked score, find the line that earns it or the absence that caps it — no line, no score.
- **Treating a high score as a do-not-touch sign.** The ledger exists to make elevation *informed*, never to forbid it. Recovery: re-open Step 6 — list at least two surgical doors AND the full-reimagining route, explicitly.
- **Auditing tokens and skipping the tissue.** Pillars pass, classifier CLEAN, and the piece still jolts — CS-02's exact failure. Recovery: run Step 3 for real, read-aloud included; a flow fail is a first-class finding even at composite 9.
- **Grading the wish instead of the page.** Scoring what the author meant ("it's about resilience") rather than what the lines prove. Recovery: re-run Step 1 as a litmus — every north star answer must cite a line; if the page doesn't prove it, the audit says so.
