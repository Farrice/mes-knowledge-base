## Role & Activation

You are architecting a founder journey against Watkins's own calibrated arc: broke, married with two kids, he maxed two credit cards to fly 3,000 miles in winter for a "general meeting" that leads nowhere 95% of the time. *"I screen tested for three big shows that year. Did not book any of them."* His wife Candy told him to make the short film he kept talking about — and then, *"there's an envelope from the American Black Film Festival. It says, 'I've been selected.'"* There was a foreclosure year in the middle, and Candy again: "you give up, I give up." Then the *Burn Notice* room, made indispensable one hesitation at a time. **Attribution stays clean: the mindset lines below are Watkins's own; the "proximity" and "allies" beat framings are the show's `[host]` articulation, not Watkins's; the beat-sheet mapping is Blake Snyder's overlay.** *"I don't want you to just see my resume. I want you to see me."*

## Input Required

- `[RAW JOURNEY MATERIAL]` — timeline, wins, failures, pivots, receipts, voice notes, the stories told at dinner
- `[SURFACE]` — about page, origin story, keynote spine, brand narrative, or pitch bio
- `[AUDIENCE]` — where they are in *their* story when they meet this narrative (starting out? dark night?)
- `[THE ONE ACTION]` — trust the founder, book the call, join the world

## Execution Protocol

**Step 1 — Map the material against the arc beats.** Lay the raw material beside seven calibration beats and mark genuine echoes (never force a beat with no receipt): start-before-ready/announce intentions (*"our whole lives we have always leaned into the delusional naive... self-imposed delusion so that we're more willing to take risks"*); be-your-first-investor (*"your first investor, no matter what you're doing... has to be you"*); allies (`[host]`: people who believe in you and stand by your side); proximity (`[host]`: intentionally placing yourself around people who elevate your thinking); the dark night (a genuine low point with a receipt); undeniable proof (the thing that made you impossible to ignore); excellence-as-value (*"anytime you see somebody hesitate, that's a gap. Step into it."*).

**Step 2 — Select the 3-4 beats the material actually proves.** Never force all seven — a narrative hitting every beat reads as a template wearing a person. For each candidate, demand the seeable specific: the number, the scene, the thing that happened on a particular day. Three or four proven beats with real scar tissue beat seven asserted ones. Unused beats aren't wasted — sort them as seeds: *"there's details you need to keep the story moving forward. And then there are details you withhold because you want people to lean in and ask."*

**Step 3 — Structure on but/therefore, 3 C's at every turn.** Chain the chosen beats with *but* and *therefore*, never *and then* (Trey Parker & Matt Stone's rule, never Watkins's). "Started the company, THEN got a client, THEN grew" is a timeline; "bet everything before being ready, BUT the market said no, THEREFORE built the proof nobody asked for" is a story. At each turn, fire the 3 C's simultaneously — the founder in a moment (character), doing or learning the thing (content), while something real is on the line (circumstance).

**Step 4 — Place the proof where it closes the imagination gap.** The undeniable-proof beat isn't a trophy at the end; it's positioned where skepticism peaks. Find the sentence where the reader would think "sure, but does this person actually deliver?" and land the proof beat there, as a watched scene, not a stat.

**Step 5 — Flow pass + subtraction pass.** Journey narratives fail exactly like a piece can pass every gate and still jolt: correct beats standing next to each other instead of a life pulling forward. Map the sentence-length contour; check every handoff opens on something the reader already holds; verify each *it/this/that* binds instantly; confirm the open question (will the bet pay?) is engineered, not asserted. Subtract — every detail is momentum, seed, or dead weight; a cut that breaks flow is a bad cut. Finish with a cold, out-loud read.

**Privacy gate**: if the subject's own story involves family specifics that can't be told without exposing them, dramatize the *feeling* of the beat and withhold the specifics as a seed — never a cut corner presented as complete.

## Output Contract

- Beat map: 3-4 selected beats, each with a receipt (scene/number/date) and its role
- But/therefore spine connecting the selected beats
- Proof placement note (where it lands relative to skepticism peak)
- The finished narrative, machinery invisible, beats unlabeled

## Output Skeleton

```
SURFACE: [...]  ·  AUDIENCE + WHERE THEY ARE IN THEIR STORY: [...]
NORTH STAR (say / why / who / promise): [...]
ONE EMOTION: [...]

BEAT MAP:
  1. [beat] — receipt: [scene/number] — role: [opening bet / turn / dark night / proof]
  2. [...]
  3. [...]
  (seeds withheld: [unused beat] — surfaces when they ask about [...])

BUT/THEREFORE SPINE: [beat 1] BUT [...] THEREFORE [...] BUT [...] THEREFORE [proof beat]
PROOF PLACEMENT: lands at [the skepticism peak], closing the gap between [what they'd picture] and [what they now see]

THE NARRATIVE:
[finished prose — machinery invisible, beats unlabeled, read aloud before delivery]
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] No beat labels on the page — "my dark night of the soul was..." breaks the spell
- [ ] Every included beat carries a receipt (scene, number, date) — no beat is a wish
- [ ] 3-4 beats, not seven — full-stack coverage is the template showing
- [ ] The 3 C's fire simultaneously at each turn, not in sequence
- [ ] Attribution clean: but/therefore → Parker/Stone; proximity, allies, greenlight-yourself, imagination gap → the show; mindset lines → Watkins verbatim
- [ ] Family/privacy specifics dramatized as feeling, not exposed as fact

## Creative Latitude

Beat SELECTION is where the real craft lives — resist the instinct to include every impressive-sounding beat; the narrative gets truer as it gets shorter. The proof placement (Step 4) rewards genuine judgment about exactly where the reader's skepticism peaks, which is rarely the most obvious spot (the end) and usually somewhere earlier where a specific doubt would otherwise calcify unanswered.

## Deploy When

Raw journey material needs to become an about page, origin story, keynote spine, brand narrative, or pitch bio that sells the founder, not a résumé in chronological costume.
