## CS-01 — Parallax Edition 01: "The Manifesto" (2026-07-10 X-ray)

**What it is:** Farrice's Parallax launch edition — "I was told to pick one thing. I picked all of them." (`_active/farrice-brand/content/substack-v2-drafts/01-manifesto.md`). Farrice's felt verdict: one of the most accurate and powerful pieces we've generated together.

**Scores (Watkins rubric):** ~8.5 composite · Attention 9 · 3 C's 8 · Teach 9 · Leave-wanting 7.5 · Subtraction 7.5 · **Recognition 9.5** · North Star: clean four-for-four.

**What's firing (with proof):**
- **Recognition at savant level** — the superpower of the piece. `browser of 47 tabs`, `a bookshelf that makes no sense to anyone else`, `success felt like a costume they couldn't take off`, `if you understood every word in this newsletter, we should be friends`. Precise interior mirrors; the reader feels named without a name.
- **Contrarian-truth open + emotional cause** — `$14,000 in courses I'd never finish` + the coach who said it `the way you'd tell someone to put down the fork`. Surprise, emotion, and recognition all in the first beat.
- **Teach inside the familiar** — the psychology *under* "niche down": `a cage with better lighting`, the filing-cabinet-vs-falling-in-love mechanism, `you are the niche... to the bone`.
- **3 C's coincidence point** — character (the performer), content (wholeness is magnetic), circumstance ($14K, two years, the dead-on-arrival business) converge at `the ones struggling hardest are the ones pretending to be less than they are`.
- **Named promise** — a 15-minute micro-transformation per edition. Most manifestos never name their promise; this one closes on a contract.

**Transferable moves (reuse anywhere):** the fork simile pattern (borrowed physical gesture to make advice *felt*); belief-list-as-identity-signal; the "we should be friends" close (recognition as a bookend); teaching the mechanism under the cliché instead of arguing with the cliché.

**Elevation doors left open (not obligations):** thread one forward-pull question after `a cage with better lighting`; test a 5-line cut of the belief list (two beliefs are truer as withheld seeds); anchor the "when you truly know yourself" stretch to a micro-scene. A full reimagining is also legitimate — run `/bw-elevate`, takes beside the original, Farrice's gut decides.

## CS-02 — DWA Threads Launch Set (2026-07-10) — **FAILURE CASE STUDY (felt verdict overruled the gates)**

**What it is:** 5 Threads posts for the DWA affiliate engine, produced net-new by `/bw-hook-forge` + `/bw-story-anatomy` inside the locked Frame 1 (anti-guru, credential-led, SAHD-as-texture).

**Gate scores vs. felt verdict — the calibration event:** prose_classifier CLEAN (0/10 AI-score) · ≤500 chars · fully compliant. **Farrice's felt verdict: jarring, jolty — "sentence formations and content do not flow well, they jump from one thing to another and don't create real open loops or curiosity gaps. They just create a little bit of confusion."** The felt verdict wins. Every gate passed and the pieces still failed — proof again that gates measure the floor, not the ceiling.

**What still worked (keep these moves):**
- **Recognition hook against the spine pain** — `You bought the courses. You know what to do. You still haven't done it.`
- **Name-the-controversy as trust mechanic** — honest disqualification as the conversion lever.
- **Uncopyable metaphor** — progressive overload as income logic, native to Farrice, alien to 40,000 competing affiliates.

**Root-cause diagnosis (why they failed):**
- **Subtraction cut connective tissue, not just fat.** Three char-cap cut passes removed the transitions and causal links between sentences; what remained was correct clauses standing next to each other instead of a story pulling forward. A cut that breaks flow is a bad cut, no matter how many characters it saves.
- **Sentence-length monotony + subject-hopping.** Compressed lines all landed at similar mid-length weight, each starting a new subject — no rhythm contour, no breath, no thread the reader rides.
- **Open loops asserted, not engineered.** The posts *mentioned* the mechanic quickly instead of opening a genuine gap the reader needs closed — curiosity was claimed, not caused.

**The repair (what this failure bought us):** the flow/cadence research layer + `references/flow-and-cadence.md` + Flow Gates baked into the /bw workflows (2026-07-10 amplification). Rule now standard: **subtraction sorts details; it never severs the connective tissue between the ones that stay.**

**Elevation doors:** the flow-layer rewrite shipped 2026-07-10 (v2, side-by-side with these originals — one subject thread per post, known-new handoffs, loops staged in-post, connectors protected through every trim; all ≤500 chars, classifier CLEAN). Awaiting Farrice's felt verdict on v2 — that verdict, not the gates, closes this entry. Still owed: the DWA engine's 3 human gates before posting.

## CS-03 — “The second price” (2026-08-26 anatomy audit) — **9.1 CRAFT / PERMISSION GATE OPEN**

**What it is:** A flagship LinkedIn teardown of Ritual's subscription price versus its Costco price (`_active/farrice-brand/content/vault/2026-08-16-the-second-price-post.md`). It turns a shopper's arithmetic into a message-strategy problem: once retail publishes a second price, the subscription needs a reason to exist beyond price.

**Scores (Watkins rubric):** **9.1 composite** · Attention 9.5 · 3 C's 9 · Teach 9 · Leave-wanting 9 · Subtraction 9 · Recognition 9 · North Star: clean four-for-four · Flow Gate: PASS.

**What's firing (with proof):**
- **A complete story in one opening line** — `A woman in a Costco aisle is doing arithmetic on her phone.` Character, action, location, and an unanswered question arrive together.
- **The hidden business mechanism** — `Landing a retail account does not add a channel. It publishes a second price for the same product.` Distribution becomes a message decision, not a logistics update.
- **The question migrates into the reader's building** — `Someone in the building has to decide what the subscription is for now.` The post answers its own case while opening a live decision the operator still has to make.
- **Runnable residue** — `Open your subscription page next to your retail price and write the sentence that survives both.` The close creates Monday-morning behavior instead of asking for engagement.

**Transferable moves:** shopper arithmetic as strategy scene; concede the legitimate channel economics before naming the message cost; turn a two-price comparison into a one-sentence survival test; make the buyer's next thought (`We have not written that sentence`) the real conversion event.

**Evidence boundary:** the source file records both prices as verified against Ritual's live pages on 2026-08-16, but prices can drift. Refresh before publication. The file also binds the named-brand Pillar-1 teardown protocol: **send to Ritual before publishing.** A 9.1 craft score does not waive permission or freshness.

**Elevation doors left open:** a scalpel pass could reduce the explanatory concession by one sentence; a blank-page take could stay entirely inside the loyal subscriber's point of view. Neither should replace this version without Farrice's felt verdict and the named-brand decision.
