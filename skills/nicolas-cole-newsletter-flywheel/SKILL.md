# Nicolas Cole — Newsletter Flywheel

Newsletter architecture using Cole's two rules: (1) It's a book that never ends, (2) The never-ending component is a tangible, repeatable asset.

## Expert Context

Nicolas Cole runs **Write With AI** (#1 paid education newsletter on Substack, ~$70-100K/mo) and **Start Writing Online** (free newsletter funneling to digital products). His methodology reduces all newsletter success to two binary rules, eliminating the overthinking that kills launches.

**Core thesis**: People don't subscribe to newsletters — they subscribe to a *faucet of tangible assets they never want to turn off*. The newsletter is just the vehicle.

## Genius Context

- **Book That Never Ends** — Newsletter = infinite book the reader wished kept going

## Quick Reference

**The Two Rules** (run BEFORE anything else):
1. Is this a book that never ends?
2. Is the never-ending component a tangible, repeatable asset?

**The Faucet Test**: "Do you ever want this faucet to turn off?" If yes → redesign.

**The Wine Club Test**: "It's like a _____ club but for _____." If blank ≠ physical object → redesign.

**The Serial Investment Test** (run for Edition 2+): "Would a subscriber who read the previous edition find THIS edition meaningfully more valuable than someone reading cold?" If no → redesign the inter-edition mechanics.
