# Newsletter Social Proof — Cross-Platform Amplification

Converts each newsletter edition into LinkedIn content, ghostwriting portfolio proof, and case study material. Every post does triple duty.

## Prerequisites
- Load `nicolas-cole-newsletter-flywheel` skill
- A published or ready-to-publish newsletter post

## Process

### Step 1: LinkedIn Post Extraction
From the newsletter post, generate 3 LinkedIn post variants:

**Variant A — The Insight Post**:
- Extract the core insight/lesson from the newsletter
- Reframe for LinkedIn's audience (broader, professional context)
- Hook → Insight → "I break this down in detail in my newsletter [link]"
- Format: Short paragraphs, line breaks, 150-300 words

**Variant B — The Tangible Asset Teaser**:
- Show a preview of the tangible asset (e.g., 1 of 5 prompts, partial template)
- "I created this [prompt/template/framework] for my newsletter subscribers"
- CTA: "Subscribe to get the full version every [frequency] → [link]"

**Variant C — The Story Post**:
- Extract the story/observation from the newsletter opening
- Tell it as a standalone LinkedIn narrative
- End with "I wrote about this deeper in my latest newsletter"

### Step 2: Ghostwriting Portfolio Proof
If the user offers ghostwriting or content services, convert the newsletter into portfolio evidence:

1. **Process documentation**: "Here's how I designed this newsletter concept":
   - The Two Rules validation
   - The tangible asset identification
   - The content structure choices
   
2. **Results framing**: Track and document metrics over time:
   - Open rates
   - Subscriber growth
   - Engagement (replies, comments)
   - Revenue generated (if applicable)

3. **Case study template**:
   ```
   CLIENT/PERSONAL: [Newsletter name]
   CHALLENGE: [What the newsletter needed to solve]
   APPROACH: [Two Rules + Tangible Faucet methodology]
   RESULT: [Metrics — subscribers, open rates, revenue]
   TANGIBLE ASSET: [What subscribers receive]
   ```

### Step 3: Service Demonstration
Convert the newsletter edition into a "proof of methodology" for potential service clients:
- "This is exactly what I'd build for you"
- "My newsletter uses the same flywheel I install for clients"
- Shows the methodology in action, not just claims it works

### Step 4: Content Calendar Integration
Map the outputs to a weekly content schedule:
- **Monday**: LinkedIn Variant A (Insight) — builds authority
- **Wednesday**: LinkedIn Variant B (Teaser) — drives subscribers
- **Thursday**: Newsletter publishes on SubStack
- **Friday**: LinkedIn Variant C (Story) — extends reach

### Output
- **3 LinkedIn posts** (ready to publish, formatted for platform)
- **Case study snapshot** (if applicable)
- **Weekly content calendar** showing how newsletter + LinkedIn work together
- **Portfolio proof format** for ghostwriting/service pitches

[methodology-in-action pitch]

## Quality Gate

- [ ] All 3 LinkedIn variants are genuinely distinct in angle and format, not the same post reworded?
- [ ] Each variant is platform-native (short paragraphs, line breaks) not a pasted newsletter excerpt?
- [ ] Case study (if included) uses real metrics, never placeholder numbers presented as real?
- [ ] The weekly calendar sequences LinkedIn and newsletter so neither cannibalizes the other's reason-to-click?
