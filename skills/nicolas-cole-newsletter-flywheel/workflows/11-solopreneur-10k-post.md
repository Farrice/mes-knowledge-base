# Solopreneur 10K Post — Farrice's SubStack Engine

Produces a publish-ready SubStack post for multi-passionate solopreneurs working toward their first $10K by monetizing expertise. Uses the prompt-as-tangible-asset format.

## Prerequisites
- Load `nicolas-cole-newsletter-flywheel` skill
- FARRICE.md loaded for voice and brand alignment
- Topic/angle provided by user (or generate via `/trend-to-newsletter`)

## Audience Profile
**Who**: Multi-passionate entrepreneurs/solopreneurs — creators, coaches, consultants, freelancers
**Where**: Pre-$10K or early-stage revenue
**Pain**: Overwhelmed by too many ideas, struggling to focus, unclear on how to monetize their expertise
**Desire**: A clear, repeatable path to their first $10K that doesn't require them to abandon their many passions
**Tangible asset**: Step-by-step coaching prompts — "extensions of Farrice" that coach the reader through specific business-building decisions

## Process

### Step 1: Topic → Pain → Prompt Mapping
1. Take the edition topic
2. Map it to a specific pain the $0-$10K solopreneur faces
3. Design the coaching prompt that solves that pain
4. The prompt IS the tangible asset — the centerpiece of the edition

### Step 2: Post Architecture
Write the post using this structure:

**Subject Line**: Hook that promises the tangible outcome + signals the prompt is inside
- Pattern: "[Outcome they want] — The Prompt That Gets You There" or "I Built This Prompt So You Don't Have to [painful thing]"

**Opening (2-3 paragraphs)**:
- Start with a story or observation that names the pain
- Bridge to why THIS prompt exists
- "By the end of this edition, you'll have [specific tangible output]"

**The Setup (2-3 paragraphs)**:
- Why most solopreneurs get this wrong
- The hidden blocker they don't see
- Farrice's perspective (commentary layer) — earned from experience

**The Prompt (the tangible asset)**:
- Formatted in a clear, copy-pasteable block
- Input fields the reader fills in from their own situation
- Expected output described
- "Run this in ChatGPT, Claude, or your preferred AI tool"

**The Commentary (2-3 paragraphs)**:
- What the prompt reveals that most people miss
- What to do with the output
- One specific action item for this week

**Close**:
- Tease next edition's prompt
- Soft CTA to share/forward
- "If this is the kind of thing you never want to stop receiving..."

### Step 3: Voice Check
Run through FARRICE.md voice markers:
- Authentic, not performative
- Direct, not motivational-spammy
- Multi-passionate energy — celebrates range, doesn't apologize for it
- Practical over theoretical — always "here's the thing you DO"

### Step 4: Polish
- Apply `nicolas-cole-sentence-craft` for sentence-level optimization
- Format for SubStack (headers, blockquotes, prompt highlighted)
- Write LinkedIn teaser (3-4 line hook + "Read the full prompt in my newsletter [link]")

### Output
- **Complete SubStack post** (ready to copy-paste)
- **LinkedIn teaser post** (2-3 variants)
- **Subject line variants** (3 options)
