# Newsletter Review Cycle

Monthly content system audit — review newsletter performance, re-validate Two Rules, diagnose tangible asset health, and prescribe adjustments.

## When to Use

- Monthly newsletter health check
- Subscriber growth stalling or declining
- Engagement metrics dropping (open rate, click rate, saves)
- Uncertainty about whether to pivot the tangible asset
- Pre-planning for next month's content calendar

## Inputs Required

- Newsletter name and platform (SubStack, Beehiiv, etc.)
- Last 4-8 editions (titles, topics, tangible assets delivered)
- Available metrics: open rate, click rate, subscriber count trajectory, churn rate, save/bookmark signals
- Unsubscribe survey data (if available)
- Creator's gut feeling ("what felt right, what felt off?")

## Execution

### Phase 1: Two Rules Re-Validation

Re-run the Two Rules against the newsletter AS IT CURRENTLY EXISTS (not as it was designed):

**Rule 1 — Book That Never Ends:**
- Review last 8 editions. Does the sequence feel like chapters in an infinite book?
- Or has it drifted into random topic coverage with no through-line?
- Test: Could someone read editions 1-8 in order and feel momentum building?

**Rule 2 — Tangible Faucet:**
- Categorize each edition: Did it deliver a specific tangible asset?
- Count: How many of the last 8 editions delivered a noun (prompt, template, recipe, framework) vs. an essay/opinion?
- Faucet Health Score: (editions with tangible asset ÷ total editions) × 100
  - 80%+ = Healthy faucet
  - 50-79% = Dripping — some editions are just essays
  - Below 50% = Broken faucet — redesign the tangible asset

### Phase 2: Metric Diagnosis

Map metrics to root causes:

| Symptom | Likely Root Cause | Diagnostic Question |
|---------|------------------|-------------------|
| Open rate declining | Subject lines OR subscriber-topic mismatch | Are subject lines promising the tangible asset? |
| Click rate low | Tangible asset not compelling enough | Would you save/bookmark this? |
| Churn rising | Faucet problem (Hidden Knowledge #3) | Do subscribers ever want this faucet to turn off? |
| Growth stalled | Acquisition problem, not content problem | Is the newsletter being promoted on other channels? |
| Engagement flat | Commentary layer missing (Hidden Knowledge #6) | Are you delivering assets WITH expert perspective? |

### Phase 3: Tangible Asset Evolution Scan

The Infinite Repeatability Engine says the domain evolves and generates content for you. Audit whether the creator is riding that wave:

1. What changed in the domain in the last 30 days? (New tools, research, trends, events)
2. Did the newsletter editions reflect those changes?
3. Are there 3+ upcoming domain shifts that guarantee fresh material for the next quarter?
4. Is the tangible asset format still the best format? (Maybe prompts should evolve to templates, or recipes should evolve to meal plans)

### Phase 4: Subscriber Desire Audit

Run the Faucet Test retrospectively:
- Pick the 3 best-performing editions (by open rate or engagement)
- Pick the 3 worst-performing editions
- What tangible asset did each deliver?
- Pattern: Best performers likely had the strongest tangible assets. Worst performers likely drifted toward essays.

### Phase 5: Prescriptions

Generate a prioritized list of adjustments:

**If faucet is broken (below 50%)**:
→ Redesign tangible asset. Run `/tangible-faucet` with current audience data.

**If faucet is dripping (50-79%)**:
→ Create a "Tangible Asset Checklist" for every edition: Before publishing, confirm the subscriber receives a saveable, shareable, collectible object.

**If faucet is healthy but growth stalled**:
→ Problem is acquisition, not content. Run `/newsletter-growth-audit`.

**If commentary layer is missing**:
→ Each edition needs: tangible asset + creator's specific perspective on WHY it works and WHAT most people get wrong.

**If format needs evolution**:
→ Design 2-3 new tangible asset formats and A/B test over the next month.

## Output Schema

```markdown
# Newsletter Review — [Name] — [Month Year]

## Two Rules Status
- Rule 1 (Book That Never Ends): PASS / FAIL — [diagnosis]
- Rule 2 (Tangible Faucet): PASS / FAIL — Faucet Health: [X]%

## Metric Diagnosis
[table of symptoms → root causes → fixes]

## Domain Evolution
[3+ upcoming shifts that fuel the next quarter]

## Top Prescriptions (Ranked)
1. [Highest-impact fix]
2. [Second fix]
3. [Third fix]

## Next Month's Tangible Asset Calendar
[10 edition concepts with specific tangible assets named]
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Two Rules re-validated against current state (not original design)?
- [ ] Metrics mapped to specific root causes (not generic "try harder")?
- [ ] Domain evolution scan identified 3+ fuel sources for next quarter?
- [ ] Prescriptions are specific actions (not vague recommendations)?
- [ ] Next month's calendar has named tangible assets for every edition?
