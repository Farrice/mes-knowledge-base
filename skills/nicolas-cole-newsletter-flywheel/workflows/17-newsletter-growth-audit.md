# Newsletter Growth Audit

Design a subscriber acquisition strategy using multi-expert stacking. The extraction focused on what the newsletter IS — this workflow handles how subscribers FIND it.

## When to Use

- Newsletter validated (Two Rules pass) but subscriber count isn't growing
- Pre-launch: designing the acquisition funnel before publishing edition 1
- Scaling: newsletter has product-market fit but needs distribution
- Churn diagnostic says the problem is acquisition, not content

## Inputs Required

- Newsletter name, tangible asset, audience description
- Current subscriber count and growth rate
- Active social platforms (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, YouTube, etc.)
- Existing content assets (blog posts, LinkedIn posts, lead magnets)
- Budget for paid acquisition (if any)
- Creator's existing audience size on other platforms

### Phase 2: Tangible Asset as Acquisition Hook

The tangible asset isn't just the retention mechanism — it's the ACQUISITION hook.

**The Signup Promise Framework:**
Don't pitch the newsletter. Pitch the tangible asset.

❌ "Subscribe to my newsletter about AI"
✅ "Get a new AI prompt every week that you can copy-paste into ChatGPT"

❌ "Join my Substack for marketing tips"  
✅ "Get a ready-to-deploy marketing template every Wednesday"

Write 5 variations of the signup promise, each leading with the tangible asset noun.

### Phase 3: Channel-Specific Acquisition Strategies

**LinkedIn → Newsletter (Lara Acosta stack):**
1. Profile optimization: Newsletter link in featured section + headline
2. Content strategy: Every 3rd LinkedIn post references the tangible asset
3. "Trailer posts": Posts that deliver 1/3 of a newsletter edition with CTA "Full version in my newsletter"
4. Carousel format: Visualize tangible assets as carousels, link to newsletter for the complete version
5. DM funnel: When someone engages with content, "You'd love what I send every [day]..."

**Twitter/X → Newsletter:**
1. Thread strategy: Turn newsletter tangible assets into threads with CTA at end
2. Pinned tweet: Signup promise + link
3. Quote-tweet strategy: Share subscriber testimonials about the tangible assets

**YouTube → Newsletter (if applicable):**
1. In-video mention: "I created a [tangible asset] for this — link in description"
2. End screen: Newsletter signup as the CTA instead of "subscribe to the channel"
3. Community tab: Share newsletter tangible assets as community posts

**SubStack Network (organic):**
1. Recommended newsletters: Find complementary (not competing) newsletters for cross-recommendation
2. SubStack Notes: Post tangible asset previews as Notes
3. Guest posts: Write for larger newsletters, include your tangible asset as the embedded proof

**Referral System:**
1. SubStack referral rewards: Design reward tiers aligned with the tangible asset
   - 1 referral: Bonus tangible asset
   - 5 referrals: Premium tangible asset bundle
   - 10 referrals: Access to an exclusive tangible asset library

### Phase 4: Hook Engineering for Signup CTAs

Stack Luke Iha hook principles on the signup CTA itself:

Generate 10 signup hooks using the tangible asset:
- **Curiosity**: "The AI prompt that got 47 coaches their first $10K client"
- **Specificity**: "Every Tuesday: 1 copy-paste prompt + the exact scenario to use it in"
- **Proof**: "Join 2,000+ [audience] who get a new [asset] every week"
- **FOMO**: "Last week's [asset] generated $X for a subscriber. Next one drops Tuesday."
- **Identity**: "[Audience descriptor] get a new [asset] every week. You in?"

Score each hook 1-10 on Vicious Hook Principles. Deploy top 3.

### Phase 5: 90-Day Growth Roadmap

```
Month 1 — Foundation:
Week 1: Optimize signup promise, deploy top 3 hooks
Week 2: Implement LinkedIn content strategy (trailer posts)
Week 3: Launch referral system
Week 4: First cross-promotion outreach

Month 2 — Acceleration:
Week 5-6: Twitter thread strategy launch
Week 7-8: Guest post on 2-3 complementary newsletters
Milestone: 2x subscriber count from Month 1

Month 3 — Scale:
Week 9-10: Evaluate paid acquisition (if budget allows)
Week 11-12: Launch "best of" tangible asset lead magnet for new subscribers
Milestone: Sustainable weekly growth rate of X%
```
