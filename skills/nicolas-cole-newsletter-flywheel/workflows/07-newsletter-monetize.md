# Newsletter Monetize — Revenue Architecture

Designs the complete revenue pathway from newsletter to money.

## Prerequisites
- Load `nicolas-cole-newsletter-flywheel` skill
- Load `nicolas-cole-digital-products` skill (for Vehicle Framework)
- Business model fork completed (`/newsletter-biz-model`)

## Process

### Path A: Free Newsletter → Product (Recommended for most)
1. **Product Identification**: Using the Vehicle Framework from `nicolas-cole-digital-products`:
   - What $49-$350 product does the newsletter naturally sell?
   - Apply the Problem-Completion Test: Does the product help the reader DO the thing?
   - Select vehicle type: Written Guide / Templates / Course / Workshop

2. **Education-to-Purchase Arc**:
   - Map how each newsletter edition moves the reader from "learning" to "ready to buy"
   - Design the "if this is free, the paid thing must be incredible" effect
   - Every tangible free asset should demonstrate ONE slice of the paid product's value

3. **CTA Architecture**:
   - **Weekly soft CTA**: 1-2 sentences at the end ("Want the full system? [link]")
   - **Monthly launch sequence**: One dedicated edition per month that's a deeper sell
   - **Quarterly campaign**: Full launch event with bonuses, urgency, social proof

4. **Pricing Strategy**:
   - Apply the $350 threshold: below = impulse, above = experience required
   - Start at the TOP of the range ceiling (Higher Price = More Revenue)
   - Design the value stack that justifies the price

### Path B: Paid Newsletter (Subscription)
1. **Pricing Tier Design**:
   - **Free tier**: What do free subscribers get? (The "taste" — enough to prove the Two Rules work)
   - **Paid tier ($5-15/mo)**: Full tangible asset delivery every issue
   - **Premium tier ($25-50/mo, optional)**: Community access, Q&A, bonus assets

2. **Conversion Architecture**:
   - Free → Paid conversion targets: 2-5% is healthy
   - Design the "paywall moment": What specific thing makes the free reader say "I need more"?
   - Saunders model: Free = the story. Paid = the commentary layer.

3. **Retention Engineering**:
   - Retention is solved at conception (Hidden Knowledge #3) — if the tangible asset is right, churn is minimal
   - Monthly churn target: <5%
   - Retention levers: consistency of delivery, asset quality, community (if applicable)

### Output

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Path A or Path B chosen explicitly, not both pursued half-heartedly?
- [ ] Pricing recommendation is tied to a specific threshold or logic (the $350 ceiling, or a stated churn/conversion target), not a round number picked arbitrarily?
- [ ] Revenue projections show 3 months out AND the specific math connecting subscribers → conversion → dollars?
- [ ] For Path B, the "paywall moment" is named as a specific asset or edition, not a vague "when they're ready"?
- [ ] The $1K/mo milestone is a concrete subscriber-count target, not an aspiration?
