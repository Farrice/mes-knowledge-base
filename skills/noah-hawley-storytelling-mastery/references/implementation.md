# Implementation — Methodology & Workflow Chaining

How Hawley's process actually runs, and how the 20 workflows chain into it. Load when you need sequence and stacking, not the patterns themselves.

---

## The Hawley Build Sequence (any story, any medium)

1. **Seed** — Start from a question or a question-bearing image (Pattern 2). Name the theme as a question you can't yet answer.
2. **Destination** — Name the ending (Pattern 3). The ending gives the whole its meaning; lock it before the middle.
3. **Assumption audit** — Ask "what am I taking for granted?" (Pattern 6). Find the medium/format/frame to convert into a tool.
4. **Break by theme** — Populate from the theme outward (Pattern 1). Characters/beats/sections are angles on the question, not plot dominoes.
5. **Tonal architecture** — Map the tension→release machine across the whole (Pattern 4). Decide where the gray zone lives.
6. **Draft at velocity** — Sprint enough "there there" to survive a gap (Pattern 11). Discover the middle.
7. **Withhold pass** — Find where to *not* explain, *not* score, *not* show (Patterns 7, 16). Leave space.
8. **Coherence cut** — Broom anything serving an asset rather than the story (Pattern 9).
9. **Mirror check** — Is the audience made complicit anywhere it should be (Pattern 5)?
10. **Calibrate to the moment** — Most-advanced-yet-acceptable for this audience, this era (Pattern 12).

The workflows below each enter this sequence at a specific point.

## Stacking Protocols

**Hawley → Roth.** Hawley sets the season/piece architecture and theme spine; hand each scene/edition to Roth's scene-level craft (visual prose, character wallets, off-center subtext) for the line work. Hawley decides *why the scene exists*; Roth writes it.

**Hawley → Wright Thompson.** For long-form narrative non-fiction, Hawley supplies theme-first + ending-first; Thompson supplies reporting depth, interiority, and the structural hammer. Note the myth/reality inversion: Thompson excavates real truth, Hawley can manufacture a "true story" frame — choose deliberately per piece.

**Hawley → Pressfield.** Hawley's theme-as-question + Pressfield's narrative physics (gravitational structure, all-is-lost → epiphany) compound for manifestos and sales narratives.

**Hawley → Connelly / Segura.** For short-form: Hawley's tension→release machine sets the shape; Connelly's telling detail and Segura's comic timing sharpen the build and the release.

**Order matters.** Hawley runs *first* (architecture, theme, ending, tone), then the scene/detail/timing experts run *inside* his frame. Inverting the order produces well-crafted scenes with no spine.

## Quality Gates (per workflow)

Every workflow ends against `genius.md` § Anti-Patterns and § Quality Rubric. The non-negotiables:
- Plot-listing without a theme underneath → fail (return to `/hawley-theme-engine`).
- Emotionality (telling the audience what to feel) → fail (apply Dry-Then-Score).
- Service creep → fail (apply `/hawley-coherence-cut`).
- Named machinery on the page → fail (the architecture stays invisible).
