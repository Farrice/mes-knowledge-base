# Solopreneurship Adapter

## Practice Type

`SOLOPRENEURSHIP`

## Owner Fork

- `skills/dan-koe-multipassionate-mastery/`: use when the work centers on personal monopoly, multipassionate synthesis, or an intellectual-product practice.
- `skills/justin-welsh-solopreneur/`: use when the work centers on a narrow niche, repeatable content/business system, or a data-led revenue stack.

Select one. Do not blend the niche philosophies by default.

## Shared Gates

- Credible expertise or a named mechanism.
- One buyer, problem, and bounded result.
- One paid founding unit and one acquisition path.
- Actual demand and capacity evidence.
- No content machine before the offer and proof loop are defined.

## Forbidden Routes

Andrew Dun, Luisa Zhou, simultaneous Dan/Justin ownership, Growth Ecosystems before repeatability, and revenue-stack forecasts before paid evidence.
