# Economics Activation Contract

No annual or monthly economics workflow exists in the cold build.

## Three Required Gates

Economics implementation requires all three:

1. Detached `RUNTIME_OBSERVED` behavior proof.
2. Stage-appropriate operating inputs rather than aspirations.
3. Explicit human approval to build the economic module.

## Annual Practice Inputs

Stage 1 evidence must include actual pricing, per-unit delivery and support hours, working weeks, sales capacity, acquisition events, collections, retention where relevant, costs, margin, and life constraints.

## Monthly Company Inputs

Stage 2 or 3 evidence must add team roles, compensation, utilization, sales capacity, acquisition cost and payback, delivery ratios, QA, escalation, rework, management load, retention, churn, expansion, cash runway, and non-founder delivery proof.

## Cold Response

Any cold or Stage 0 request for economics returns:

```yaml
economics_status: LOCKED_PRE_RUNTIME
terminal_decision: STOP_OR_HOLD
terminal_reason_code: HOLD_PREMATURE_SCALE_MODEL
model_output: null
```

No income guarantee or source-reported business result may become a forecast assumption.
