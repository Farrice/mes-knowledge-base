# Expert Practice OS

Expert Practice OS is a thin intake-and-routing spine for coaching, consulting, solopreneurship, and other qualified expert practices. It universalizes the Packet, risk, proof, capacity, stage, and handoff layers. It never universalizes the public positioning, protocol, promise, delivery, or evidence.

The first candidate public configuration is Farrice's Final 10% Diagnostic, routed internally to Andrew Dun's consulting discipline. Life coaching and solopreneurship remain separate cold adapters. A fresh run cannot borrow Farrice's offer or proof.

This skill is local and loadable by exact path for diagnostic testing. It is not registered, command-invokable, automatically routed, or approved for client use. A future broad registry sync could discover any folder under `skills/`; do not run registry sync until `RUNTIME_OBSERVED` proof and explicit registration approval. ## When to Use

- A practitioner has expertise or a protocol but needs the correct business lane and proof-stage next step.
- Farrice wants to apply the POP method to himself or a client without imposing one niche or offer on everyone.
- A request mixes coaching, consulting, solopreneurship, content, delivery, or scale and needs one owner.
- A revenue target must be held until proof, capacity, and operating inputs support it.

## When NOT to Use

| Situation | Route instead |

| The practice lane and requested deliverable are already clear | Load that lane owner directly |

## Operating Rules

1. Complete or explicitly hold the Practitioner / Protocol Packet.
2. Clear qualification, scope, claims, privacy, and authorization before lane work.
3. Classify achieved proof separately from requested next stage.
4. Select one scalar lane owner. Supporting functions stay bounded and sequential.
5. Load the conductor plus only the selected owner and exact current workflow.
6. Produce a route receipt or a hold receipt. Do not perform lane work.
7. Keep economics `LOCKED_PRE_RUNTIME` throughout the cold build.
8. Take no external action.
