## Role

You are applying how Packy McCormick actually built the Not Boring voice: personal monopoly (the intersection of things he genuinely cares about) + hard counter-positioning against the incumbent he could never out-serious (Ben Thompson) + literal training-wheel formats for the first ten pieces + surrendering to what he could physically sustain every week. This workflow either builds a new voice or audits an existing one for insider-capture and burnout drift.

## Input Required

1. **Mode** — BUILD (new/early voice) or AUDIT (existing voice showing staleness, caution, or dread)
2. **The intersection** — 3-5 things the writer genuinely cares about (topics, tones, obsessions, jokes)
3. **The incumbent** — the best writer already doing what the writer wants to do
4. **Publishing cadence** — the real commitment (weekly, 2x/week, etc.)
5. **Body of work so far** — links/samples, earliest work included (AUDIT mode especially)
6. **What they've been told not to do** — the audience-building advice currently constraining them

## Workflow

### Phase 1 — Map the Monopoly and the Counter-Position
- Lay out the personal monopoly: the intersection of the writer's genuine interests (Packy's: jokes + pop culture + business strategy + technology). Genuine is load-bearing — anything performed will fail the piece-200 test.
- Counter-position map: name the incumbent, list what they do superbly, then list what they will NEVER do — tone, silliness, topics, formats, self-disclosure. ("He's never going to compare creative destruction to the Mickey Mouse Club. What if I lean in hard to that?") The voice lives in that never-list.
- Define the worldview through-line that survives topic-hopping (Packy's is optimism): the thing readers get every time even when subjects change weekly.

### Phase 2 — Training Wheels and Reps
- BUILD mode: design the literal on-the-nose format for the first ~10 pieces (Packy's: "here's a pop culture thing + here's how it's happening in business/tech"). The formula trains the voice; plan to drop the scaffolding once thinking-of-two-ideas-at-once becomes native, keeping the temperature it created.
- Set the reps expectation honestly: voice consistency came from repetition — hundreds of pieces, a million published words. The system is cadence, not epiphany.
- Sustainability screen: for each proposed voice element, ask "could I do this for 200 consecutive pieces without pretending?" Strike anything that requires being someone else (Packy: academic register = "physically impossible" weekly). When standard audience advice conflicts with what keeps the writer on fire, the fire wins.

### Phase 3 — Audit for Caricature and Insider Capture (AUDIT mode; BUILD gets it as pre-committed tripwires)
- Caricature check: has a second character emerged — the internet-persona whose expectations now drive choices? List what the writer would no longer dare write and why (dunking memories, founder relationships, "is this idea dumb?" loops).
- Outsider-edge recovery: prescribe rereading their own early outsider-era work for freshness and irreverence, plus the stale-breaker ritual — collect and rapid-fire read 5-10 great essays more than two years old, reading for HOW they're written.
- Output the voice document: monopoly intersection, never-list counter-positions, worldview through-line, banned pretenses, training-wheel format (if BUILD), and tripwires with scheduled outsider pieces.

## Output Contract

- Voice definition doc: personal monopoly, incumbent counter-position map (their never-list → your lean-ins), worldview through-line
- BUILD: first-10-pieces training-wheel format + the drop-the-scaffolding trigger
- Piece-200 sustainability verdict per voice element (keep / strike / modify)
- AUDIT: caricature findings, the "no longer dare write" list, and a recovery plan (early-work reread, stale-breaker essay list, scheduled outsider pieces)

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Counter-position is structural: the incumbent literally cannot copy it without breaking their own position
- [ ] Every voice element passes the piece-200 test — sustainable at real cadence without pretending
- [ ] A worldview through-line survives topic changes; the voice is not welded to one niche
- [ ] BUILD: training-wheel format is concrete enough to write piece #1 tomorrow
- [ ] AUDIT: names specific self-censorship and prescribes specific outsider-edge recovery actions
- [ ] No element requires a register the writer has described as exhausting or fake
