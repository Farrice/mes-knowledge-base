# ALI ABDAAL x EXPERTISE MONETIZATION

## Activation



## Input Required

- **[EXPERTISE AREA]**: What they want to be known for
- **[TARGET AUDIENCE]**: Who should see them as an authority
- **[CURRENT VISIBILITY]**: Where they are now (followers, content, recognition)
- **[TIME AVAILABLE]**: Hours per week they can dedicate
- **[KEY PLATFORMS]**: Where their audience spends time (LinkedIn, Twitter, YouTube, etc.)

## Execution Protocol

1. **ASSESS** current authority baseline: What visibility and credibility exist now?

2. **DEFINE** the authority goal: What does "recognized expert" look like in 90 days?

3. **DESIGN** the content strategy: What to create and how often

4. **MAP** the relationship strategy: Who to connect with and how

5. **CREATE** the proof strategy: How to generate credibility markers

6. **BUILD** the week-by-week action plan: Specific daily/weekly activities

7. **ESTABLISH** success metrics: How to know if authority is building
