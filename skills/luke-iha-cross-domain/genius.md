## How to Use This Skill (Model Calibration)

These four workflows are sequencing disciplines, not fill-in-the-blank templates. The failure mode is mechanical: running Phase 1, discarding its output, and free-associating Phase 2 from scratch. Absorb the chain logic below, then execute — never narrate it.

- Do NOT announce which phase or skill you're "now running." Execute the phase; never print "Now invoking the Mechanism Discovery Engine."
- Do NOT let a checkpoint pass silently. If the mechanism can't be found by name in an ad script, that script is broken — rewrite it, don't ship it with a note.
- Luke Iha's own standard for a portfolio piece is blunt: "the work itself IS the pitch" (extractions/luke-iha/video-2-creative-strategy/extraction-report.md — Nuclear VSL pattern). Apply the same bar to every cross-domain output — if the package needs a cover memo explaining why it's good, the chain broke somewhere upstream.
- Polish-is-the-tell class warning: a campaign package where all 5 ad scripts read like variations of one script (same opening beat, same CTA cadence) has failed the "5 distinct lead angles" checkpoint even if each individual script is well-written. Sameness under polish is still sameness.

## The Core Engine: Six-Skill Traceability

Luke Iha built his own track record — "Over $100M generated with VSLs" (extractions/luke-iha/extraction-report.md, expert byline) — on chains where a single strategic decision (the mechanism, the positioning) shows up unchanged in every downstream asset. The SIN framework enforces this at the mechanism layer: "Total < 21 = mechanism needs work. < 15 = abandon and start over" (extractions/luke-iha/video-7-million-dollar-mechanisms/extraction-report.md, § 1.2). The cross-domain orchestrator's job is to enforce the equivalent discipline at the pipeline layer — a phase that fails its checkpoint doesn't get papered over, it gets rerun.

## Pattern: Checkpoint Discipline (Phase Gates)

Each workflow's checkpoints are load-bearing, not decorative. `full-stack-ad-campaign.md` requires the validated mechanism to score SIN ≥ 21 before Phase 2 opens, and requires 17+ of the eventual hooks to be "demonstrably mechanism-driven (removing the mechanism collapses the hook)" — a direct application of HK1: "Without a mechanism, every promise is just another claim" (extractions/luke-iha/video-7-million-dollar-mechanisms/extraction-report.md, Hidden Knowledge). Skipping a checkpoint to hit a word count is the single most common way these pipelines produce polished, disconnected output.

## Pattern: The Nuclear VSL as Portfolio Capstone

`client-portfolio-builder.md` is built on Iha's Platform Leverage Ladder, where premium positioning is earned, not claimed: "Don't try to charge premium rates until you have 10+ reviews. Don't leave the platform until you have a direct client pipeline" (extractions/luke-iha/video-2-creative-strategy/extraction-report.md, Genius Pattern 5). The Nuclear VSL piece itself follows the Over-Delivery Flywheel logic — "Take a $500 job. Deliver $3,000 of value" — applied to spec work instead of paid work: rebuild a real brand's worst asset well enough that the work needs no pitch deck around it.

## Pattern: Agency Ladder → Category of One

`client-portfolio-builder.md`'s positioning statement and Phase 5's strategic wrapper in `full-stack-ad-campaign.md` both draw on the Category of One formula: "❌ 'I'm a copywriter' (category: millions) … ✅ 'I write VSLs for supplement brands doing $1-5M/yr that need to scale past the $5M ceiling' (category: YOU)" (extractions/luke-iha/video-6-offer-cycling/extraction-report.md, § 1.3). A cross-domain package that ends in generic positioning ("I help brands grow") has failed this pattern even if every individual asset is strong.

## Anti-Patterns (Sourced)

- **Generic hook bank decoupled from the mechanism** — a CASH matrix or Hook Forge burst that would survive if you swapped in a different mechanism has already failed; the SIN Framework exists precisely because "Total < 21 = mechanism needs work" (source: extractions/luke-iha/video-7-million-dollar-mechanisms/extraction-report.md, § 1.2, video ID 3B3nQNDWVWk).
- **Charging premium/positioning as an expert before the Portfolio Builder's proof exists** — Iha's own ladder is explicit that reviews and reps come first: "Don't try to charge premium rates until you have 10+ reviews" (source: extractions/luke-iha/video-2-creative-strategy/extraction-report.md, Genius Pattern 5, § Platform Leverage Ladder).
- **Abstract or clinical mechanism naming inside any cross-domain output** — Iha's Characterization principle ranks "'Cortisol Belly' > 'Hormonal Metabolic Dysfunction'" (source: extractions/luke-iha/video-7-million-dollar-mechanisms/extraction-report.md, § 1.4, Naming Principles).
- **Explaining the mechanism instead of simplifying it toward intuition** — "Most mechanism failures are complexity failures, not accuracy failures" (source: extractions/luke-iha/video-7-million-dollar-mechanisms/extraction-report.md, Hidden Knowledge HK3).
- **Hypothetical or invented brands in `client-portfolio-builder.md`** — the workflow's own checkpoint requires "All 6 pieces use real brands (not hypothetical)," matching Iha's Nuclear VSL instruction to "Pick your dream client's worst-performing asset. Rebuild it from scratch" (source: extractions/luke-iha/video-2-creative-strategy/extraction-report.md, Genius Pattern 1, The Nuclear VSL).
- **Broad, undifferentiated positioning in the Phase 5 strategic wrapper** — violates the Category of One formula, which explicitly rejects "'I'm a copywriter' (category: millions)" in favor of a specific skill + niche + outcome stack (source: extractions/luke-iha/video-6-offer-cycling/extraction-report.md, § 1.3, dated extraction video ID 2aEmSn7sypE).

If it's the second, rerun the failed checkpoint before delivery.
