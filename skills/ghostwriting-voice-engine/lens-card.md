# LENS CARD — Ghostwriting Voice Engine (unified 5-expert voice capture-embody-crystallize-refine-elevate system)
> Compiled 2026-07-27 from genius.md (canonical). FIRING RULE: this card decides WHETHER this lens has something to say about a draft line; when a lens fires, READ the matching genius.md section before treating. Never treat from the card alone.

## Lenses

### Voice Extraction (Acosta)
- **5-Dimension Voice Scan** — analyze every client across: (1) Vocabulary Patterns, (2) Sentence Architecture, (3) Emotional Range, (4) Perspective Markers (I/you/we ratios, question frequency), (5) Unique Identifiers (phrases only THEY use). **Test:** profile covers all 5 dimensions with specific examples, not vague descriptions.
- **Signature Phrase Extraction** — highlight phrases appearing 2+ times across 3-5 samples; categorize as openers ("Look—"), transitions ("Here's the thing—"), intensifiers ("Dead wrong"), closers ("Your call."). **Test:** library of 10-15 phrases tagged with deployment context.
- **Rhythm Fingerprinting** — map the dominant rhythm pattern (e.g., "Short-short-short-LONG explanation-short punch"); count average sentence length; measure fragments; note acceleration (passion) and deceleration (teaching). **Test:** rhythm describable as a pattern formula (S-S-S-L-S punch).
- **Emotional Range Mapping** — default mode, passion triggers, intensity markers (ALL CAPS, dashes, clustering), humor style. **Test:** documented floor, ceiling, and the triggers that move between them.
- **Voice Discovery Interview** — when <3 samples: "What do you believe that your industry gets wrong?" "How do you explain what you do to a stranger?" "What topic could you rant about for an hour?" "What phrases do your team/friends mock you for repeating?" Analyze response patterns, not just content.
- **Dual-Persona Lens** — for every voice element: "Would this resonate with someone who PAYS them?" AND "Would this make someone SHARE their content?"

### Voice Embodiment (Albom)
- **Voice Archaeology** — study recurring argument structures, belief assumptions under word choices, what they emphasize vs skip, where language gets precise (passion zones) vs vague (discomfort zones). **Test:** you can predict how they'd approach a topic they've never written about.
- **Belief Mapping** — map what they believe about their domain, audience, success, failure. **Test:** you can finish their sentences.
- **BECOME, Don't Imitate** — before every writing session, re-read their best samples until you hear their voice; write from inside the perspective, not while looking at a checklist. **Test:** writing feels generated FROM their identity, not applied TO generic text.
- **Creative Latitude Doctrine** — you are not a transcription service; find what they're TRYING to say and say it with their voice at its sharpest. Elevate without distorting. **Test:** client says "I wish I'd written this," not "This sounds like me."
- **Contrast Mapping** — where they sound like Naval Ravikant = derivative; where they sound like nobody else = original. Amplify the original, reduce the derivative.
- **Validation Through Anticipation** — **Test:** "If I showed them a controversial take in their domain, could I predict their response?" If no, return to Belief Mapping.

### Voice Crystallization (Mallet)
- **6-Component Voice DNA** — document: Sentence Architecture · Vocabulary Fingerprint · Emotional Range (4 spectrums: Reserved↔Expressive, Clinical↔Personal, Serious↔Playful, Detached↔Vulnerable) · Humor Integration · Structural Signatures (setup/payoff, list builder, story frame, myth bust, direct hit) · Specificity Patterns. **Test:** another writer could use the guide without meeting the client.
- **4-Axis Voice Spectrum** — mark position on Formality (contractions, sentence length), Intensity (qualifiers, hyperbole tolerance, certainty language), Pacing (length variation, white space, transition density), Intimacy (self-disclosure, second person, vulnerability). **Test:** each axis has a marked position + 3-4 measurable indicators.
- **Signature Template Library** — Opening Signatures (Hook/Story/Direct/Question/Scene), Transition Signatures, Closing Signatures (CTA/Reframe/Question/Callback/Permission), each with client-specific examples. **Test:** 15+ templates, 2-3 examples each.
- **Do/Don't Guardrails** — ALWAYS DO (contractions, specific numbers, their metaphor family, rhythm match) / NEVER DO (corporate jargon they'd hate, hedging that weakens authority, engagement bait foreign to their voice, off-domain topics). **Test:** scannable in 60 seconds.
- **Voice Drift Warnings** — document this client's 4-5 drift patterns: formality creep, specificity loss ("many people" instead of numbers), teaching-mode creep, over-qualifying ("I think maybe this might be..."). **Test:** writer catches drift before the client does.

### Voice Refinement (Cole)
- **Voice-First Optimization** — before ANY edit, tag voice markers (unusual word choices, rhythm breaks, quirks) as PROTECTED; optimize everything else. Voice markers survive even when they violate "rules."
- **Terminal Word Power Placement** — flag any sentence ending in: is, are, was, were, it, this, that, the, a, an. Restructure so the final word is a power noun or strong verb. **Test:** zero important sentences end on empty words.
- **Three-Pass Economic Compression** — Pass 1 write freely; Pass 2 circle every word under 4 letters and remove as many as possible; Pass 3 reorder for flow. **Test:** word count drops 20-40% with zero meaning loss and all voice markers intact.
- **Vocabulary Calibration** — 4th-grade anchor with strategic elevations; maximum ONE elevated word per paragraph; jargon follows client's balance (e.g., 70/30). **Test:** Flesch-Kincaid within 1 grade of client's existing content.
- **Rhythm Engineering** — restructure paragraphs to the client's rhythm pattern; introduce variation where lengths cluster; respect their fragment preference. **Test:** reading aloud reproduces their natural speaking cadence.

### Voice Elevation (Farnsworth) — precision instruments, deploy surgically, never as defaults
- **Saxon Punch** — build 1-2 sentences of Latinate abstraction, end with short, stony, hard-consonant Saxon words; the transition must feel like a physical drop. Max 1-2 per post. **Test:** the final phrase is impossible to ignore.
- **Chiasmus Reversal (ABBA)** — "[A] does not dictate [B]. [B] dictates [A]." Max 1 per post. **Test:** feels like closed-loop wisdom admitting no interruption.
- **Epistrophe** — 2-3 consecutive clauses ending on the single most important word. **Test:** the repeated word feels like the inescapable conclusion.
- **Anaphora** — repeat a short opening phrase 3+ times with escalating content, building toward a crescendo. **Test:** rising energy toward the final clause.
- **Two Languages Translation** — state the idea first in conceptual language (intellect), then bluntly in concrete Germanic words (gut). **Test:** intellectual validation followed instantly by an emotional gut-punch.

### The 10-Point Voice Authenticity Standard (every piece, before delivery)
1. **Say-It-Out-Loud Test** — read aloud in their voice; where you stumble, the voice is wrong.
2. **Signature Element Verification** — ≥2 signature phrases, rhythm match, metaphor family — checked against the profile, not memory.
3. **Voice Marker Protection** — no marker "optimized" away.
4. **5-Dimension Profile Compliance.**
5. **Emotional Range Accuracy** — intensity where they'd be intense, calm authority where measured; not stuck in one mode.
6. **Red Line Check** — zero phrases they'd never use, opinions they don't hold, emotions they don't express, off-domain topics.
7. **Voice Drift Scan** — formality creep, specificity loss, teaching-mode creep, over-qualifying, warmth loss.
8. **Terminal Word Audit.**
9. **Register Variation Check** — at least one register shift (conceptual→visceral, calm→intense, serious→wry). Flat = lifeless.
10. **The Anticipation Test** — shown without attribution, would the client claim it? If you hesitate, return to embodiment.

### Client Acquisition Layer (Cole OS — upstream of voice craft)
- **Farrice ICP Triad** — target one profile per campaign: B2B SaaS founders/VPs (Series A-C), executive coaches for tech leaders, biotech/healthtech (Series A-C) — each with defined win signals. **Test:** every prospect maps to a profile with ≥2 documented win signals.
- **Information Advantage Audit** — inventory 1,000+ hour domains, strip peer anchoring, apply the affluence filter; write "I help [ICP] achieve [outcome] because I know [asymmetric thing] they don't."
- **Leaks & Faucets Warm Network** — 50 contacts tagged LEAK (referrer) or FAUCET (client), one specific ask each (never "let me know if you know anyone"); exhaust warm before cold.
- **Personalized Loom Outreach** — 2-4 min: warm open, 2-3 observations proving homework, 2 quick wins, one soft CTA. Never needy, never a hard pitch.
- **Education-as-Sales Problem Script** — Problem → Root cause → Quantified consequence → Emotional hook → Solution → Positive outcome. **Test:** prospect articulates the problem back in their own words before you present the offer.
- **Problem-First Discovery Call** — rapport → agenda → teach 2-3 tailored Problem Scripts → outline solution → let THEM ask → price confidently → proposal within 24h. **Test:** prospect asks about working together before you pitch; close rate trends toward 50%.
- **Outcome-Based 3-Tier Pricing** — Foundation / Authority (recommended) / Executive; anchored in asset language + ROI vs full-time hire, agency, DIY; never deliverable-hours.
- **Free Work Accelerator (guard-railed)** — only with finite scope, feedback-for-testimonial trade, and a pre-agreed conversion path. Never open-ended.
- **Land-First-$10K Sequence** — Advantage Audit → 50-contact list → 3 spec pieces → 15 Looms week 1 → 3-5 discovery calls → 3-tier proposals → close 1-3 clients at $3K-$5K/mo.

## Anti-patterns / calibration
- **The Portfolio Paradox** — never lead a pitch with credentials/case studies/testimonials: "The only thing the client cares about is you educating them on a problem they know they have... or a problem they don't even know they have."
- **The Pricing Floor** — "You should never be charging less than $3,000 for a project or per month." Hard floor, not a negotiating opener.
- **Free Work Without a Boundary** — "Don't look at it as free work. Look at it as a marketing cost."
- **Case-Study-Only Content** — proof-of-work feeds cap out well short of a real discovery mechanism; one of the biggest mistakes Acosta names.
- **Voice Fidelity Without a Distribution Strategy** — layer platform-native mechanics on top of the voice, not instead of it.
- **Losing the Reader** — "the worst thing you want to hear as a writer is I tried to read it but I got lost." Worse than an unpolished line.
- **Anaphora Run the Whole Length** — "you don't want the entire speech to be this kind of repetition"; the commentator only gets loud on the approach to goal.
- **Hidden-knowledge calibrations:** voice lives in the "imperfections" — when something looks like a mistake, check if it's a voice marker before removing it. Flattened emotional range = "them on a sedative" — map the full range and its transitions. The ear detects difference, not absolute level — if everything is loud, nothing is loud. Limitations you accept become signatures readers love — amplify what's there, don't add what's missing. Repetition is the ghostwriter's hidden enemy — one clear statement > two fuzzy restatements. The piece's fate is set in the first hour after publishing — run the First-Principle Hook Test (remove name/avatar; does the hook still stop scrolling?).
- **Operating principles:** Voice Profile Before Content — Always (the profile IS the product). Embodiment Over Imitation (goal: "sounds like them on their best day"). Protect Voice Markers With Your Life. Elevate Surgically, Not Systematically (the reader should feel the impact without detecting the technique). The Client's Limitations Are Features (voice is defined as much by what someone WON'T say).
- **Model calibration:** the five layers are intuition primitives, not a checklist to march through. Never narrate machinery ("Now applying Terminal Word Power Placement...") or label dimensions in the deliverable — the 10-point standard is a private editing pass, not section headers. The recognition test: would the client recognize this as something they'd write on their sharpest day — or as ghostwriting-voice-engine vocabulary? Polish is the tell-class failure: a post that reads a little TOO even, TOO balanced, TOO smoothly-paced is more likely wrong than one with a rough edge matching a documented voice marker.
