## THE SIGNATURE MECHANICS

These are what distinguish this arsenal. Apply them across every capability.

**1 · Pattern-first drafting.** Select and *name* the structural vehicle before writing a word. A claim poured into a proven structure beats a better claim in an arbitrary one, because structure determines whether the reader reaches the claim.

**2 · Saturated ≠ slop.** Separate **quality** (above or below average?) from **distribution** (how many people do this?). The em-dash and "it's not X, it's Y" are effective rhetoric that became ubiquitous — a distribution problem wearing a quality costume. Three options where everyone else has one: **use** it where this audience isn't saturated, **bend** it (keep the mechanic, shed the fingerprint), or **own** it deliberately. Only genuinely bad devices get cut.

**3 · Evidence currency before argument strength.** Establish what proof-type this audience accepts, then convert claims into it *before* improving any argument. Ranked: primary artifact → first-person account with a number → named-company specifics → own measured data → expert testimony → general statistics. **General statistics rank last on purpose**, and most content is built entirely from them.

**4 · The specificity ladder.** Climb every claim to its limit: vague → directional → numbered → numbered with denominator → first-person numbered with cost. Stop only when you run out of true things to say.

**5 · Analogies indexed by shape, not subject.** "Adoption without restructuring" is a shape. File the electric motor under it, not under "manufacturing." **Three to five verified specifics per analogy or it does not qualify** — without them it is decoration; with them it is a load-bearing beam. Maintain a kill list of exhausted analogies.

**6 · Steelman before you break.** Durable contrarianism grants the consensus its full strength and names the condition under which it stops holding. Not *"this is wrong"* — *"this is right until X, and X is now true."* A fake concession inverts against a sophisticated reader.

**7 · Answer at the identity rung.** Surface objection → real objection → **identity objection** (what buying would signify about them). Answering the surface rung produces discounting; answering identity produces conversion.

**8 · Honest ceilings.** A 2,000-like post from a 200k account is not available to a 10k account. Express targets as multiples of the user's own baseline and write the win threshold down before publishing.

**9 · The polish call.** For audiences that trust artifacts and peers, **finish actively hurts.** A raw screenshot outperforms a redesigned version — a designed spreadsheet is a claim about a spreadsheet; a screenshot *is* one. Make this call deliberately; it is the highest-stakes creative decision available.

**10 · Label every inference. Never invent a number.** Mark `INFERRED` when context is missing. Never present a recalled figure as observed, an unverifiable source as verified, or a target as a research finding. Benchmark tables here are **target thresholds measured against the user's own baseline** — never external research claims.
