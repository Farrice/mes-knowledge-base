# Unique Promise Spine

## Role

Choose one governing promise that the available evidence can carry. Do not invent a mechanism backward from the headline the user wants.

## Source and Ownership

- **Kyle:** mechanism/durable-force/result (`SL-009`), opportunity catalyst/pattern/path (`SL-014`), promise-above-tactics hierarchy (`SL-015`).
- **Matthew:** evidence-derived rather than backward-invented mechanism (`SL-018`). Retain Matthew ownership.
- **NESB restraint:** `SL-013`, `SL-017`; emotions must emerge from evidence.

## Input Required

1. Verified Product Truth Packet and prohibited-claims list.
2. Audience Card and awareness level.
3. Valid Swipe Packet with one primary.
4. One verified mechanism and causal bridge, or one verified catalyst and recurring pattern.
5. One supported result with evidence IDs and qualifiers.
6. Desired action, format, voice owner, and domain constraints.

## Hard Stop / Refusal

Return `BLOCK_PROMISE` when:

- the mechanism exists only as a desired label;
- a causal arrow is plausible but unverified;
- the catalyst/pattern is invented or third-party without validation;
- the result is unsupported, overbroad, or regulated without review;
- two promises remain equally load-bearing and no human decision resolves them;
- the Swipe Packet failed or is absent.

Route uncertain mechanism/proof architecture to the appropriate Luke owner. Do not disguise uncertainty with adjectives.

## Procedure

### 1. Classify the Spine

Choose one:

- `MECHANISM_LED`: verified mechanism → durable force/process → supported result.
- `OPPORTUNITY_LED`: verified catalyst → recurring pattern → bounded way to exploit → supported result.

If neither graph is evidence-complete, stop.

### 2. Build the Evidence Graph

For each node and arrow, cite Product Truth IDs. Mark `VERIFIED`, `QUALIFIED`, or `MISSING`.

### 3. Draft Three Promise Candidates

Each candidate must contain:

- one reader-relevant result;
- one reason it can happen;
- the necessary qualifier;
- no extra mechanism, outcome, or proof claim.

### 4. Reject Competing Promises

Name why two candidates lose: weaker relevance, weaker evidence, bundled outcomes, duplicated control language, or unsupported causality.

### 5. Run NESB Restraint

For new/easy/safe/big intent, cite the evidence that creates the emotion. Delete any category that rests only on an adjective.

### 6. Lock One Spine

State the promise in one sentence, then specify the claim it must not expand into.

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Exactly one spine type and one locked promise were selected.
- [ ] Every node and causal arrow has Product Truth support.
- [ ] No mechanism, catalyst, authority, or outcome was invented.
- [ ] Rejected alternatives remain unblended.
- [ ] NESB effects come from evidence, not adjectives.
- [ ] The promise sits above beats and techniques rather than listing them.
- [ ] The result and qualifiers survive unchanged into the handoff.

## Handoff

Pass a valid Promise Card, primary swipe ID/path, evidence IDs, voice constraints, and one open risk to `03-four-beat-opening-builder.md`. An invalid card goes to Luke or the factual/domain owner, not the opening builder.
