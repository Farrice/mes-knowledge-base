# Proof Texture Dimensionalizer

## Role

Make existing credibility concrete enough to answer one active skepticism. This workflow can dimensionalize verified proof; it cannot manufacture, independently verify, or certify proof.

## Source and Ownership

- **Kyle:** known credibility pattern restraint (`SL-030`), skepticism design (`SL-037`), resource/action/change texture (`SL-039`), specificity and room for proof (`SL-057`, `SL-058`).
- **Illustrative lock:** company, amount, authority, celebrity, and result examples in `SL-040` and `SL-059` are hypothetical.
- **Contribution language:** `SL-031`; underlying performance in `SL-032` remains self-reported.

## Input Required

1. One exact claim and its Product Truth ID.
2. The reader's expected skepticism.
3. Proof ledger with source, truth status, owner, date, permissions, and qualifiers.
4. Audience, placement, voice constraints, and desired action.
5. Domain and implied-endorsement constraints.

## Hard Stop / Refusal

Return `PROOF_GAP` when:

- the proof is missing, self-reported without permitted disclosure, or unverifiable;
- a quote, amount, action, customer, authority, or changed position lacks a source;
- wording would imply endorsement or sole causation;
- the proof does not resolve the skepticism created by the claim;
- regulated evidence lacks domain review;
- the request asks this workflow to certify truth.

Do not fill gaps with source-video examples or control-swipe authority.

## Procedure

### 1. Name the Skeptical Gap

Write the reasonable reader response to the claim in one sentence.

### 2. Classify Available Proof

Mark each proof item as one or more:

- verified datum or comparison;
- relevant quote;
- documented action/resource commitment;
- authority cluster with explained relevance;
- changed position;
- contribution evidence;
- demonstration/result;
- unavailable or prohibited.

### 3. Test Truth and Relevance

For every usable detail record:

`evidence ID → exact fact → owner → qualifier → skepticism answered → permitted wording`

### 4. Create 3–5 Texture Options

Each option may rearrange existing facts but may not add a new fact. Keep claim, evidence, and qualifier adjacent.

### 5. Select One

Choose the option that resolves the active skepticism with the least unsupported implication. Preserve rejected options separately; do not blend them.

## Quality Gate

- [ ] One claim and one skeptical gap control the packet.
- [ ] Every concrete detail maps to a proof-ledger ID.
- [ ] No quote, number, authority, endorsement, action, or result was invented.
- [ ] Hypothetical source examples did not transfer.
- [ ] Contribution is not rewritten as sole causation.
- [ ] Selected option remains qualified and claim-matched.
- [ ] The workflow does not certify truth or publication readiness.

## Handoff
