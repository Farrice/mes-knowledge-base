|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| `KM-02` | 5-3-1 | Writer wants to draft before comparing relevant promotions | Read five, break down three, choose one primary | Swipe Packet and borrowing boundary | `SL-023` |
| `KM-03` | Promise hierarchy | Beats and phrases compete without one governing idea | Lock one supported promise above tactics | Promise Card | `SL-009`, `SL-014`, `SL-015` |
| `KM-04` | Mechanism-led spine | Verified mechanism connects to a durable force and result | Narrate the durable force without inventing facts | One mechanism-led promise | `SL-009` |
| `KM-05` | Opportunity-led spine | Catalyst and recurring pattern are verified | Connect catalyst → pattern → way to exploit | One opportunity-led promise | `SL-014` |

| `KM-07` | First-four gate | Early lines do not explain relevance, benefit, or why to continue | Advance, repair, or restart at first failure | Gate verdict | `SL-045` |

| `KM-14` | Do-the-least restraint | Writer adds complexity to display cleverness | Choose the smallest proven move | Fewer unsupported moves | `SL-021` |
| `KM-15` | NESB evidence translation | New/easy/safe/big appear as adjective stacks | Express emotional category through evidence | Specific comparative copy | `SL-013`, `SL-017` |

| `KM-18` | One-technique practice | A writer tries to learn all moves in one draft | Isolate one technique per practice rep | Deliberate-practice handoff | `SL-041` |

## Cross-Cutting Distinctions

- `Four Punches` (`SL-022`) is the vocabulary of bounded moves. The `four-beat opening` (`SL-038`, `SL-047`) is a specific sequence. A system that merges them fails source fidelity.
- NESB is an emotional diagnostic, not a claim generator.
- Proof texture can make existing evidence more concrete; it cannot create or certify evidence.
- Negative-space diagnosis chooses one repair. It is not permission to orchestrate all eight workflows.
