# Kyle Milligan Copy Chief

This is a method skill, not a Kyle persona or voice clone. It converts one reviewed video into eight bounded procedures with explicit source ownership, truth limits, and handoffs. It can produce a source-grounded opening or one copy-chief repair order. It cannot prove market performance, certify a claim, or reproduce Kyle's full embodied judgment from one interview.

## Domain

Direct-response opening architecture, research-to-promise compression, early-line continuation, evidence texture, concept continuity, and highest-level copy-chief diagnosis.

## When to Use

- The user supplies verified offer facts, a defined audience, one desired action, evidence constraints, and five relevant comparable promotions.
- The task is a narrow direct-response opening, not a full VSL or sales letter.
- The user explicitly asks for Kyle's `5-3-1`, unique promise spine, separate four-beat opening, first-four-lines test, thumbtack continuity, proof texture, mumbo-jumbo pruning, or negative-space diagnosis.
- A draft feels wrong and the useful question is “What is the first structural failure or missing move?” rather than “Rewrite everything.”
- One evidence-backed repair must be chosen before a current production owner resumes the work.

## When NOT to Use

- **Full VSL lead or body:** use `luke-iha-vsl-leads` or the selected copy-production owner.
- **Mechanism or proof architecture:** use the appropriate Luke Iha mechanism/proof workflow; this skill rejects invented mechanisms.
- **General full-copy review:** use `matthew-volkwyn-copywriting`; Kyle's negative-space entry point only chooses the highest repair level.
- **Voice capture or imitation:** use the documented voice owner. This source is not a voice corpus.
- **Daily copywork:** use `sam-parr-copywriting-mechanics`; 5-3-1 here is assignment research.
- **VOC mining:** use the existing Joanna Wiebe/customer-research owner.
- **Generic hook batches:** use the existing hook-production route. The four-beat builder makes three evidence-bounded openings only.
- **Health, finance, legal, or other regulated claims without domain review:** stop and hand off to the factual/compliance gate.
- **Missing Product Truth:** do not “make reasonable assumptions.” Return the exact missing fields.

## Required Input Packet

Every run needs:

1. **Product Truth Packet:** offer, capabilities, mechanism/catalyst status, supported result, proof IDs, prohibited claims, and domain constraints.
2. **Audience Card:** reader, awareness, situation, felt problem, and what cannot be assumed.
3. **Desired Action:** one action only.
4. **Format and Voice Constraints:** where the copy appears and whose voice owns it.
5. **Work Artifact:** draft/excerpt for audit, or five accessible comparable promotions for build mode.

Build mode additionally needs five relevant swipes with provenance. Proof-texture work needs an evidence ledger. A missing required field is a stop condition, not a creative invitation.

## Runtime Modes

`Swipe Packet → Promise Card → Three Openings → First-Four Gate → existing body-copy owner`

After the body owner works, this skill may perform at most one targeted repair before the Matthew audit and factual/publishable gate. Do not keep Kyle loaded as a co-author of the body.

### Audit Mode

Start with `08-negative-space-copy-chief.md`. Preserve passing material, choose one highest-level failure, dispatch one procedure or existing owner, recheck once, then stop.

### Direct Procedure Mode

When the user explicitly names one mechanic, load only that workflow, its same-named prompt, the relevant `genius.md` rows, and its source anchors.

## Routing Rule: One Failure, One Owner

Use this order because lower-level edits cannot repair a higher-level failure:

1. Missing truth, audience, action, proof, provenance, or domain review → **STOP_FOR_CONTEXT**.
2. Broken or unsupported promise/mechanism → `02-unique-promise-spine.md` or Luke handoff.
3. Missing opening architecture → `03-four-beat-opening-builder.md`.
4. Weak first four lines → `04-first-four-lines-audit.md`.
5. One carried concept breaks across adjacent sentences → internal `05-thumbtack-continuity-audit.md`.
6. Verified proof is true but thin → `06-proof-texture-dimensionalizer.md`.
7. Two or more undefined concepts compete → internal `07-mumbo-jumbo-pruner.md`.
8. The failure level is unknown or an important move is absent → `08-negative-space-copy-chief.md`.

Never run all eight automatically. The output of one procedure may hand off to one next owner. A second dispatch requires a new human or workflow decision.

## Cross-Cutting Rules

### Four Punches Is Not the Four-Beat Opening

Four Punches is the source's bounded language of known copy moves. The separate four-beat opening is `interrupt → consequential claim → credibility → demonstrated result`, with credibility and result allowed to swap. Calling one the other fails source fidelity.

### NESB Is a Restraint Lens

New, easy, safe, and big describe selling emotions. They do not authorize adjective stuffing. Translate emotional intent into relevant evidence; never add unsupported superlatives.

### Truth Classes Control Use

- `OBSERVED` method rows may ground procedures.
- `SELF_REPORTED` rows may supply source context only; never offer proof or role credentials.
- `ILLUSTRATIVE` rows may demonstrate a technique only; never become facts.
- `THIRD_PARTY` rows retain their owner and cannot become Kyle doctrine.
- `INFERRED` synthesis must cite observed anchor rows and cannot be quoted as spoken.
- `UNRESOLVED` attribution cannot ground doctrine.

Matthew-owned items remain Matthew-owned. In particular, the repair taxonomy and the phrase “compensation copy” are not Kyle-only inventions.

## Handoff Schema

Every handoff contains only:

```yaml
artifact: <named output and path or inline block>
decision: <one locked choice>
evidence_used: <PT IDs and source-ledger row IDs>
preserve: <passing material that must not be rewritten>
next_owner: <one workflow or existing skill>
next_task: <one bounded action>
validation: <PASS, FAIL, or BLOCKED with reason>
open_risk: <one unresolved issue or none>
```

Pass cards, decisions, evidence IDs, and paths—not the full transcript or another expert's entire output.

## Quality Gate

A run passes only when:

- one route and one output contract were selected;
- every factual clause maps to supplied evidence;
- no control, swipe, source-video, credential, or illustrative fact transfers into the offer;
- the highest-level failure is handled before line polish;
- attribution is intact and feedback diagnoses the artifact rather than attacking the writer;
- a stop condition stops generation;
- the result states its proof boundary;
- no full-body authorship, proof certification, publication approval, or market-performance claim is implied.
