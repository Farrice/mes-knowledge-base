# Yann Martel Hidden Knowledge

## Research Has to Become Touchable
The working problem is not storing notes. It is forcing notes into relationship. Discrete fragments make choices visible.

## Reader Labor Creates Possession
Readers remember what they helped create in their own imagination.

## Allegory Needs Distance
Hard material often becomes more precise after the surface form changes.

## A Character Can Be Too Specific
If the story's main force is pressure, excessive characterization can block reader entry.

## Description Exaggerates Quickly
Physical traits become cartoonish when over-named. Use selective cues.

## The Ending Is the Compass
An opening can be imperfect. A weak ending changes the memory of the whole work.

## Mystery Is Not Confusion
Mystery remains after coherent closure. Confusion appears when the reader never had enough structure.

## One Rule Break Needs Several Handrails
Experiment works when readers still know how to move through the piece.

## AI Is a Trust Question
The key distinction is not whether AI can produce fluent prose. It is whether the audience expected a human gift.

## Reviews Belong to Readers
Interpretation is part of co-creation. The author can learn, promote, or ignore, but cannot own the reader's response.
