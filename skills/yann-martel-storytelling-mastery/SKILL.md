# Yann Martel: Storytelling Mastery

> A story is prepared like a journey, written like a discovery, and finished like a question the reader can keep carrying.

## Available Workflows

| # | Workflow | Produces | Use When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Envelope Architecture Builder](workflows/01-envelope-architecture-builder.md) | Research-to-scene envelope map | You have notes, facts, or fragments but no story path |
| 02 | [Co-Creation Gap Editor](workflows/02-co-creation-gap-editor.md) | Restraint edit and reader-work map | A draft explains too much or feels over-supplied |
| 03 | [Scene Card Orchestration](workflows/03-scene-card-orchestration.md) | Scene deck with movement and surprise | You need to turn material into scenes |
| 04 | [Satisfying Mysterious Ending](workflows/04-satisfying-mysterious-ending.md) | Ending design with closure and afterglow | The piece ends flat, too neatly, or too abruptly |
| 05 | [Animal Allegory Translator](workflows/05-animal-allegory-translator.md) | Animal or mask-based allegory route | A heavy subject needs distance and entry |
| 06 | [Ordinary Vessel Pressure Test](workflows/06-ordinary-vessel-pressure-test.md) | Character-vessel design | The character must carry extraordinary pressure |
| 07 | [Punctuation Rhythm Score](workflows/07-punctuation-rhythm-score.md) | Sentence tempo and punctuation edit | Prose feels monotone, breathless, or choppy |
| 08 | [Rule Break Calibration](workflows/08-rule-break-calibration.md) | Deliberate experiment plan | You want to break form without losing the reader |
| 09 | [Genre Contract Mapper](workflows/09-genre-contract-mapper.md) | Reader-expectation contract | A story mixes genre, literary, or product forms |
| 10 | [Sublime Question Engine](workflows/10-sublime-question-engine.md) | Moral and spiritual question map | A piece needs awe, depth, or a larger question |
| 11 | [Authenticity Boundary Audit](workflows/11-authenticity-boundary-audit.md) | AI/human authorship trust audit | You need to know whether AI help damages sincerity |
| 12 | [Gift Release Review System](workflows/12-gift-release-review-system.md) | Launch and review detachment protocol | You are preparing to publish or share personal work |
