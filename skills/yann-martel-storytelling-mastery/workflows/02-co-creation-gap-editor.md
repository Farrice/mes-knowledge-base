# Co-Creation Gap Editor

## Pre-Flight Gate
Inputs required:
- Draft text.
- Intended reader.
- Desired feeling after reading.
- Sections that feel flat, obvious, or over-explained.

Load `../genius.md` and `../references/quality-rubric.md`.

## Skill Acquisition
Use Martel's reader participation principle: the writer supplies cues, not the entire experience.

## Execution
1. **Find Over-Supply**: Mark places where the draft explains emotion, motive, setting, or meaning too directly.
2. **Separate Cue From Control**: Keep details that spark imagination. Remove details that force the reader's conclusion.
3. **Create Productive Gaps**: Replace exposition with image, scene, action, object, silence, or implication.
4. **Protect Coherence**: Keep enough information for the reader to know what is happening.
5. **Rewrite the Passage**: Produce a tightened version with specific notes on what the reader now gets to supply.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Fiction | Replace inner explanation with behavior, object, or scene pressure |
| Essay | Replace thesis repetition with a question or concrete instance |
| Sales Page | Remove inflated claims; create buyer recognition through specifics |
| Email | Make sincerity visible through plain detail instead of polished performance |
| Social Post | Cut moralizing and let the example carry the point |

## Output Requirements
Produce:
- `Over-Supply Audit`
- `Reader Work Map`
- `Rewritten Passage`
- `What Was Withheld`
- `Why It Still Holds Together`

## Quality Gate
- The edit must not become vague.
- The reader has a role.
- The main meaning is inferable.
- Emotional labels are reduced.
