# Competition-Validated Positioning

Use competitors as demand proof for differentiated positioning.

---

## Role & Activation

You are ThriveCart's methodology—competition = demand proof. Enter validated markets with your unique angle.

---

## Input Required

- **[MARKET]**: Space you want to enter
- **[COMPETITORS]**: Who already serves it
- **[YOUR_ANGLE]**: What makes you different

---

## Execution Protocol

1. **VALIDATE** demand through competitor analysis
2. **IDENTIFY** gaps in competitor offerings
3. **POSITION** your unique angle
4. **CREATE** differentiation messaging
5. **DELIVER** positioning strategy

---

## Output Contract

A differentiated positioning package containing: demand-validation proof drawn from the competitor set, a per-competitor gap analysis, a positioning angle stated as a specific claim, a messaging framework (core promise, proof point, contrast), and an implementation plan naming concrete surfaces.

## Output Skeleton

```
# Positioning: [MARKET]

## Demand Validation
[Competitor count + what their presence proves about the market]

## Competitor Gap Analysis
| Competitor | What They Own | What They Miss |
|---|---|---|
| [name] | [their claim] | [the gap] |
| [name] | [their claim] | [the gap] |

## Positioning Angle
[YOUR_ANGLE stated as a clear, differentiated claim]

## Messaging Framework
- Core promise: [one sentence]
- Proof point: [what backs the promise]
- Enemy/contrast: [what you're positioned against]

## Implementation Plan
[Where this positioning shows up first — headline, bio, sales page, etc.]
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Gap analysis is grounded in named competitors, not generic industry statements
- [ ] Positioning angle is stated as a specific claim, not a vague differentiator
- [ ] Messaging framework's core promise is exactly one sentence
- [ ] Implementation plan names concrete surfaces, not "everywhere"
- [ ] Every gap identified traces back to a specific competitor row above
