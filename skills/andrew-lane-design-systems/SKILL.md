# Andrew Lane — Design Systems & The Mood Board Method

Andrew Lane is a professional designer with 15+ years of experience who has built multiple seven-figure brands from scratch and taught thousands of entrepreneurs to build profitable online brands. His Mood Board Method is a complete system for building a visual brand identity inside an AI chat tool: get the vibe right, create visual mockups, solidify into written guidelines — so that every future asset (website, funnel, social content, thumbnails, products) starts from a trained foundation instead of a blank page. His companion framework separates business decisions (brain: what you sell, price, delivery) from branding decisions (heart: style, voice, energy, the feelings people associate with you).

## Quick Reference

- **The Mood Board Method (core loop)**: Brand Vibe Report (written feeling) → mood board images (unlimited variations until it feels right) → written visual brand guidelines → upload all three into a persistent AI project → create anything on-brand by conversation.
- **Vibe before specs**: never start with fonts and colors — start with "what is the feeling I'm trying to capture?" Emotional clarity drives visual coherence.
- **The blind-designer test**: guidelines must be written as an email to a design team that will never see any visuals. If the words alone can't reproduce the style, the guidelines aren't done.
- **Layers are the product**: extract backgrounds, textures, and elements from the mood board as standalone layers. Reusable components are why you never start from scratch.
- **Business vs branding**: business decisions = logical (offer, price, delivery); branding decisions = emotional (style, voice, relationship). Same business + different branding = different customers. Branding is what escapes the race to the bottom.
- **AI mocks up, human executes**: use AI for concepts, direction, and layers; keep human control of final assembly and messaging.
