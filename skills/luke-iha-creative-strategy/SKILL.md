# Luke Iha: Creative Strategy & Freelance Mastery v2.1

## What This Skill Does

This skill operationalizes Luke Iha's creative strategy methodology — the upstream thinking that PRECEDES copywriting. While most practitioners jump straight to writing, this skill forces strategic clarity first: audience selection, angle identification, competitive positioning, and proof-of-competence architecture.

It also deploys his complete freelance business system: the 7-step Upwork mastery sequence, the Nuclear VSL technique (portfolio pieces so good they eliminate objections), the Over-Delivery Flywheel, and — new in v2.0 — the **Offer Cycling Engine** for detecting, packaging, and rotating hot market opportunities, the **Agency Ladder** for career evolution from task vendor to growth partner, and the **12 Types of Leverage** for scaling beyond time-for-money.

Version 2.1 adds the **Profit-Finder Opportunity Scan** one decision upstream of the Creative Strategy Brief. It fingerprints the current strategy, distinguishes cosmetic variation from a supported strategic change, compares three opportunity lanes, and selects one hypothesis or holds before the brief develops the message.

## When to Use

- Before writing ANY sales asset (run a creative strategy brief first)
- When launching or scaling a freelance practice
- When positioning as a creative strategist (not just a copywriter)
- When Upwork or platform-based client acquisition is the channel
- When building a portfolio from zero
- When detecting hot market opportunities and cycling offers *(v2.0)*
- When assessing career stage and planning the next Agency Ladder rung *(v2.0)*
- When reviewing/diagnosing any marketing asset or "should I do X?" question — locate it on the decision stack first *(2026-07)*
- When a challenger asset must beat an existing control *(2026-07)*
- When current creative changes words, formats, or styling while preserving the same audience and buying situation *(2026-08)*
- When the correct use case, adjacent surface, or language/geography hypothesis must be chosen before the target market is locked *(2026-08)*
- When translation is proposed as market entry and demand, native-language evidence, rights, operations, or economics are not yet cleared *(2026-08)*

## When NOT to Use

- The target market and buying situation are already locked and the need is hooks, copy, concepts, or a finished asset brief: use the existing **Creative Strategy Brief Generator** and downstream production skills.
- The need is full voice-of-customer or avatar construction: use `luke-iha-avatar-machine` or Dara Denney, then return the evidence here.
- The request is direct translation or international launch execution without demand, native review, rights, operations, and economics: return `HOLD` and route validation to April Dunford plus the required native, legal, and operations owners.
- The request requires product-specific health, clinical, financial, or regulatory claims: clear those claims with the appropriate owner before creative work.
