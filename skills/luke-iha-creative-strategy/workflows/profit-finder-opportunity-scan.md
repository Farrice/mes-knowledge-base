# Luke Iha: Creative Strategy — Profit-Finder Opportunity Scan

## Role

Operate as the Luke Iha creative-strategy function owner. Decide what strategic variable deserves a test before a copywriter or creative team develops the message. A bounded Adil Amarsi source delta supplies the missing-market, product-use-case, adjacent-surface, and language-market mechanics; it does not create a fused Luke/Adil persona.

This workflow owns the diagnosis and locked hypothesis. It does not write hooks, produce ads, build personas, localize copy, contact partners, or run live media.

**Before executing:** Read `genius.md`, especially the Profit-Finder Opportunity Layer, then read `references/source-delta-zX61pyC1vLM.md`.

## Input Required

1. **[Product / offer]:** What is sold, price, offer, destination, and desired action.
2. **[Substantiated product truths]:** Specifications, mechanisms, delivery facts, and claim support.
3. **[Current buyer and strategy]:** Audience, situation, message, channel, geography, language, and current assets.
4. **[Customer evidence]:** Reviews, calls, surveys, support language, usage observations, and provenance.
5. **[Competitor or category sample]:** Named assets and sample boundary, if available.
6. **[Adjacent surface evidence]:** Communities, channels, partners, permissions, and reachable entry points.
7. **[Geography / language readiness]:** Demand, native-language evidence, rights, compliance, operations, and economics.
8. **[Test parameters]:** Channel, control, available sample/window, primary metric, guardrail, and owner-set thresholds.
9. **[Constraints]:** Brand, production, legal, regulatory, budget, and timing limits.
10. **[Prohibited claims / unknowns]:** What may not be asserted or inferred.

## Pre-Flight Gate

1. If the target market and buying situation are already locked and the request is for hooks, copy, concepts, or an asset brief, route directly to `creative-strategy-brief.md` or the relevant production owner.
2. If product truth or buyer evidence is too thin to distinguish a real strategic change, return `EVIDENCE HOLD` plus the shortest discovery queue.
3. If a decisive product claim is regulated or unsupported, return `CLAIM HOLD` and route it to the relevant legal, clinical, or compliance owner.
4. Treat every selected opportunity as `UNTESTED` until a real controlled event occurs.

## Workflow

### Phase 0: Strategy Fingerprint

Record the current coordinates:

- product truth or mechanism;
- audience or segment;
- triggering situation and consequence;
- desired outcome;
- channel or encounter surface;
- geography and language;
- offer and price;
- proof burden;
- post-click destination.

Then classify the proposed variation:

- **COSMETIC:** Only wording, syntax, tone, headline formula, story format, image styling, or production polish changes.
- **STRUCTURAL:** At least one strategic coordinate changes, evidence supports the change, the learning question changes, and the new proof or destination burden is named.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE:** A strategic coordinate appears to change, but the input cannot support the decision honestly.

Do not count a story, meme, static, UGC treatment, or direct translation as a strategic change by itself.

### Phase 1: Evidence Receipt and Scope

For every material fact or claim, record:

- the statement;
- its source;
- one evidence label: `SUPPLIED`, `SOURCE-REPORTED`, `INFERRED`, `UNAVAILABLE`, or `PROHIBITED`;
- what it may support;
- what it does not establish;
- the missing proof or owner.

When exact customer language is supplied, preserve it verbatim in the evidence receipt and carry the exact attributed language into the selected hypothesis's available-proof field. Do not normalize, paraphrase, or strengthen it as proof.

Classify any claimed absence:

- **Account whitespace:** Absent from the inspected brand assets.
- **Category whitespace:** Absent from a named, bounded competitor sample.
- **Market whitespace:** Wider evidence supports both demand and underserved supply.

Account absence is not category absence. Category absence is not market demand. None proves profit.

### Phase 2: Three-Lane Scan

Produce at most one candidate in each lane. Any lane may return `HOLD`.

#### Lane A: Product / Use Case

Intersect substantiated product truths with observed buyer situations, life stages, jobs, or consequences that current strategy does not address. Reject any use case that depends on invented product intent, prevalence, or performance.

#### Lane B: Adjacent Identity / Community / Distribution

Look for the buyer under another identity, ritual, community, channel, or encounter environment. Require evidence of buyer overlap and a reachable, permission-aware entry path. Interest adjacency alone is not demand.

#### Lane C: Language / Geography

Treat another language or geography as a market-entry hypothesis, not a translation task. Hold unless the input supports demand, native-language voice of customer, cultural ownership, rights, product and claim clearance, fulfillment, returns, duties, taxes, payments, support, landed economics, and an accountable owner.

For each candidate, state:

- the strategic coordinate changed;
- supporting evidence and scope;
- buyer and buying situation;
- reachable surface or destination;
- dependency and missing proof;
- excluded audience or collateral risk;
- state: `TEST HYPOTHESIS` or `HOLD`.

Never manufacture three positive opportunities to fill the lanes.

### Phase 3: One-Hypothesis Lock

Compare the candidates without fake numeric precision:

- evidence strength;
- product and buyer fit;
- degree of structural difference;
- reachability;
- testability;
- operating burden;
- claim, trust, and exclusion risk.

Lock one hypothesis only when it is more defensible than the others. Record why each alternative was rejected or held. If no candidate clears the evidence floor, return `NO EVIDENCE / HOLD`.

Every lock must name a falsifiable learning question. “This could be interesting” is not a decision.

### Phase 4: Creative Strategy Brief Handoff

Pass only the compact decision packet:

- hypothesis ID and state;
- audience and triggering situation;
- substantiated product truth;
- changed coordinate and held constants;
- channel, locale, and destination;
- angle direction, not finished hooks;
- available and missing proof;
- excluded claims and audiences;
- falsifier;
- fields the Creative Strategy Brief may improve;
- fields it may not reopen without new evidence.

The existing Creative Strategy Brief owns psychographics, positioning, emotional journey, hooks, proof architecture, category disruption, and the finished asset brief.

### Phase 5: Smallest Valid Test

Design the smallest controlled event that can answer the learning question:

- control and variant;
- one primary strategic coordinate changed;
- necessary fixed adaptations that do not become extra test variables;
- channel and destination;
- supplied sample/window parameters, or `owner input required`;
- one primary metric and one guardrail;
- owner-set stop rule;
- falsifying result;
- next branch for support, contradiction, or inconclusive evidence.

Do not invent traffic, budget, statistical thresholds, expected lift, CPA, ROAS, or profit. Designing a test is not executing one.

If the sample/window, primary decision metric, or owner-set stop rule is missing, keep the controlled-test shell but label it `TEST DESIGN: HOLD (owner input required)`, list the missing fields, and do not present the test as executable.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Required adaptation |
|---|---|
| DTC physical product | Require substantiated product properties and observed use-case evidence. |
| Service or consulting | Require a buyer trigger, current alternative, and reachable acquisition surface; do not invent case-study proof. |
| B2B or SaaS | Require role, triggering event, current alternative, buying committee constraints, and destination continuity. |
| Creator or content | Treat an adjacent audience as a gateway hypothesis, not automatic audience transfer. |
| Regulated health or finance | Hold unsubstantiated claims and route them to the relevant review owner before downstream creative. |
| Cross-language or geography | Hold until native evidence, cultural ownership, rights, operations, economics, and policy gates are present. |

## Output Contract

Produce one **Profit-Finder Opportunity Packet** containing:

1. evidence receipt;
2. current strategic fingerprint and `COSMETIC`, `STRUCTURAL`, or `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE` verdict;
3. account, category, and market evidence-scope table;
4. three lane decisions, each allowed to hold;
5. one selected hypothesis or `NO EVIDENCE / HOLD`, with rejection reasons;
6. strategic delta showing changed and held-constant fields;
7. compact Creative Strategy Brief handoff;
8. smallest valid test with falsifier and owner-set parameters;
9. proof-state footer.

## Quality Gate

1. **True-variable test:** Does the selected hypothesis change a strategic coordinate rather than words or format alone?
2. **Evidence honesty:** Is every decisive statement labeled, bounded, and traceable?
3. **Scope honesty:** Are account, category, and market evidence kept separate?
4. **Hold discipline:** Can any lane or the whole scan return `HOLD` without filler?
5. **Selection integrity:** Is one hypothesis locked with named rejection reasons and a falsifier?
6. **Handoff integrity:** Can the existing brief proceed without reopening the decision or inventing proof?
7. **Test integrity:** Does the test isolate one primary strategic coordinate and avoid invented thresholds or results?
8. **Ownership discipline:** Does the output avoid hooks, assets, localization, outreach, or live execution?

> **Recognition test:** Would Luke Iha recognize a strategist deciding what to test and why before the copy starts, or only someone generating more creative-strategy ideas?
