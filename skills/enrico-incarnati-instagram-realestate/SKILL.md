# Enrico Incarnati — Instagram for Real Estate

## Expert Identity

Enrico Incarnati is an Instagram growth coach who works exclusively with real estate agents. His approach rejects the "billboard" model of social media (posting listings with prices) and replaces it with a personality-driven, platform-native content system that generates leads through entertainment, education, and lifestyle storytelling.

**Core Philosophy**: "If your name were to be removed from the content, would it change at all? If the answer is 'no,' you don't have a brand — you have a billboard."

## When to Load This Skill

- Any request involving Instagram strategy for real estate agents
- Content planning, scripting, or production for real estate social media
- Profile optimization for agents
- Lead generation via Instagram (ManyChat, keyword triggers, DMs)
- Personal branding for individual agents or teams/brokerages
- Consulting service design for real estate teams

## Key Patterns (from genius.md)

1. **Lifestyle Intersection Method** — Find the format at the crossroads of agent personality × buyer demographic
2. **Three-Function Architecture** — Every piece must: bring new eyes, qualify leads, build attractive character
3. **Content Expansion Pack** — One topic → 5 formats (Visual Math, Green Screen, Bracket, Ranking, Clipboard)
4. **Proximity Play** — Hyperlocal "mayor of your city" authority positioning
5. **Digital Curb Appeal** — SEO name, bio architecture, link store, pin posts, stories system
6. **Friday Night Strategy** — Weekly stories-based lead capture using ManyChat keyword triggers
7. **ManyChat Lead Engine** — Keyword-triggered DMs → PDF delivery → email capture → CRM routing
8. **Show Not Feed** — Content as episodic entertainment, not broadcast
9. **Attractive Character** — Personality-driven branding that can't be copied
10. **Listing Showcase Transformation** — Specs → lifestyle storytelling
11. **Team Scalability** — Individual format discovery + shared asset infrastructure
12. **Save-Magnet Architecture** — Digital Clipboard format optimized for bookmarks
