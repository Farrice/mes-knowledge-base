```
   Issue: [Inactive, all business, no personality]
```

```
SEO NAME REDESIGN
```

### Step 3: Bio Rewrite

Produce 3 bio options following the architecture:

```
BIO OPTION 1 (Authority-Led)
Line 1: Helping [buyer type] find their dream home in [City]
Line 2: [Team name] @ [Brokerage] | [Social proof — "50+ families helped"]
Line 3: Your neighborhood expert for [specific areas]
Line 4: 👇 Free [resource] + Book a call

BIO OPTION 2 (Personality-Led)
Line 1: [Personality hook — "Your favorite realtor's favorite realtor"]
Line 2: Making [City] home-buying actually fun
Line 3: [Team/niche] | DM me "[keyword]" for my free guide
Line 4: 👇 [CTA]

BIO OPTION 3 (Niche-Led)
Line 1: [Specific niche] specialist in [City]
Line 2: From house-hunting to keys-in-hand 🔑
Line 3: [Social proof or credential]
Line 4: 👇 Grab my [lead magnet] below
```

### Step 4: Link Store Setup

```
LINK STORE ARCHITECTURE
───────────────────────
Platform: Stan Store (recommended) or Linktree

LINK ORDER (top to bottom):
1. 🎁 [Lead Magnet Name] — FREE
   "[Title of checklist/guide]"
   → Links to: PDF download or email opt-in page
   
2. 📞 Work With Me
   "Ready to [buy/sell]? Let's talk"
   → Links to: Booking page (Calendly) or application form

3. 🏠 Current Listings
   "See what's available in [City]"
   → Links to: Personal listing page (NOT brokerage homepage)

4. 📊 Market Update — [Month Year]
   "What [City] homes are selling for right now"
   → Links to: Latest market report or video

5. 📧 Newsletter (optional)
   "Weekly [City] real estate intel"
   → Links to: Email signup
```

### Step 5: Pin Post Strategy

Design 3 strategic pin posts:

```
PIN POST 1: MARKET UPDATE (Swap Monthly)
────────────────────────────────────────
Purpose: Prove you know the market better than anyone
Content: "[City] Market Update — [Month Year]"
Include: Average price, days on market, inventory levels, YOUR take on what it means
Format: Carousel or Reel with data overlays
CTA: "Comment 'update' to get my monthly market report DM'd to you"
Design: Professional, data-heavy, updated look (not a random old post)

PIN POST 2: CLIENT SUCCESS STORY
─────────────────────────────────
Purpose: Social proof that you deliver results
Content: Real client story — their challenge, your process, the result
Include: Before/after, timeline, client quote (with permission)
Format: Carousel telling the story in 5-7 slides
CTA: "Ready for your success story? DM me or click the link in bio"
Design: Warm, personal, shows the HUMAN behind the transaction

PIN POST 3: LEAD MAGNET
────────────────────────
Purpose: Generate leads on autopilot
Content: "[N] things every [buyer type] needs to know in [City]"
Include: Teaser of the checklist, why it matters, what they'll learn
Format: Reel or carousel that previews the value
CTA: "Comment '[KEYWORD]' and I'll send you the full guide"
Design: Clean, actionable, clear value proposition
ManyChat: Trigger word auto-sends PDF + captures email
```

### Step 6: Stories Framework

```
STORIES OPERATING SYSTEM
────────────────────────
DAILY MIX (3-5 stories minimum):

PROFESSIONAL (70%):
- Document showing a property
- Quick market stat or tip
- Behind-scenes of a closing
- Answering a DM question on camera
- Reposting your latest reel with commentary

PERSONAL (30%):
- Morning routine
- Coffee shop you're working from (tag it)
- Your dog, family, hobbies
- Weekend activities
- Something funny that happened today

WEEKLY SIGNALS:
Monday → Market stat + "What are you looking for this week?"
Thursday → Educational tip with poll ("Did you know this?")
Friday → FRIDAY NIGHT STRATEGY (see below)
Sunday → Personal lifestyle content

FRIDAY NIGHT STRATEGY SCRIPT:
──────────────────────────────
Story 1 (Text card): "Going to open houses this weekend? 🏠"
Story 2 (Face-to-camera): "Do NOT put in an offer until you check these 12 things. Most people miss them and it costs thousands. Reply 'checklist' and I'll send it over."
Story 3 (Text card): "Seriously — I've seen people miss #7 and lose $20K. Reply 'checklist' — it's free."

ManyChat Config:
- Trigger: "checklist" 
- Auto-DM: "[PDF link] + Here's your cheat sheet! Save it on your phone and take it with you. 🏡"
- Follow-up (24h later): "Hope the showings went great! Saw anything you loved? I can pull comps if you want a second opinion."
```

## Quality Gate

Before delivering, verify:
1. **No "link in bio" survives anywhere in the deliverable.** Anti-Pattern 6 is a hard fail — replace with a Stan Store/link-store architecture or a ManyChat keyword trigger, per source: "please stop telling people to click the link in your bio. It is killing your engagement."
2. **SEO name passes the search test.** The recommended name must follow Name + City + Real Estate Agent exactly as taught ("Stop being cute with your name... Make it searchable") — a clever tagline instead of a searchable name is a fail.
3. **Pin posts function as billboards, not favorites.** Per source, the three pins solve three specific problems (market authority, social proof, lead capture) — three "best-performing reels" pinned instead is a fail, even if engagement was high.
4. **Friday Night Strategy script is copy-paste ready**, three sequential story beats with the exact keyword-reply mechanic ("Reply 'checklist' and I'll send you my cheat sheet") — a paraphrase or summary instead of the verbatim script is incomplete.
5. **Recognition check**: would Enrico Incarnati recognize this as "digital curb appeal" — profile as the conversion layer, sequenced BEFORE content strategy — or does it read like a generic Instagram-bio-writing checklist that happens to mention real estate?
