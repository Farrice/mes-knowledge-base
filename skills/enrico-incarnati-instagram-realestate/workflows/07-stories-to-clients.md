# /enrico-stories — Stories-to-Clients Engine

Deploy Instagram Stories as a trust-building and lead generation machine. Reels get you discovered — Stories get you hired. Uses the Friday Night Strategy and day-in-life templates to convert followers into clients.

## Usage

```
/enrico-stories [agent name] [optional: specific week/event]
```

Examples:
- `/enrico-stories @_jiing`
- `/enrico-stories @_jiing "open house weekend"`
- `/enrico-stories "new agent" "first week"`

## Steps

### Step 1: Stories Strategy Foundation

```
STORIES OPERATING PHILOSOPHY
─────────────────────────────
"Reels = DISCOVERY. Stories = HIRING."

Your Reels bring strangers to your profile.
Your Stories convince them you're the agent they want.

RULES:
1. Post 3-7 stories per day MINIMUM (Instagram rewards consistency)
2. Mix: 70% professional, 30% personal
3. Use interactive features in AT LEAST 1 story per day (polls, questions, sliders)
4. Never go 24 hours without stories — your profile goes "dark" to followers
5. Always tag locations and businesses (algorithmic reach + partnerships)
```

### Step 2: Weekly Stories Calendar

Produce a complete, plug-and-play weekly stories calendar:

```
STORIES CONTENT CALENDAR
─────────────────────────

📅 MONDAY — "Market Monday"
Story 1: Quick stat with text overlay ("3 homes sold above asking in [area] last week")
Story 2: Poll — "Are you surprised? 😱 / Not surprised 😎"
Story 3: Share your latest Reel from the feed with commentary

📅 TUESDAY — "Behind the Scenes"
Story 1: Morning routine shot (coffee, car, office)
Story 2: At a showing or meeting — "Taking [clients] to see their dream home today 🏠"
Story 3: Post-showing debrief — "They LOVED the kitchen. Might be putting in an offer 🤞"

📅 WEDNESDAY — "Local Love"
Story 1: Visit a local business — "Working from [coffee shop] today ☕"
Story 2: Tag the business + short review ("Best cortado in [City]")
Story 3: Optional: Ask followers "What's YOUR go-to lunch spot? 👇"

📅 THURSDAY — "Teach Something"
Story 1: Quick tip — face-to-camera, 15-30 seconds
   Examples: "One thing most buyers forget to negotiate..." / "The document nobody reads but should..."
Story 2: Question box — "What real estate questions do you have? I'll answer them live"
Story 3: Answer 2-3 questions from the box throughout the day

📅 FRIDAY — "FRIDAY NIGHT STRATEGY" 🔥
Story 1 (5 PM): Text card — "Going to open houses this weekend? 🏠"
Story 2 (5:30 PM): Face-to-camera — 
  "If you're seeing homes this weekend, do NOT put in an offer 
   until you've checked these 12 things. 
   Most people miss these and end up losing thousands.
   Reply 'checklist' and I'll send my cheat sheet right now."
Story 3 (6 PM): Text card reminder — 
  "Seriously — #7 alone has saved my clients $15K+. 
   Just reply 'checklist.' It's free."

ManyChat Auto-DM on "checklist":
  "Here's your checklist! 📋 [PDF link]
   Save it to your phone and take it to every showing this weekend.
   DM me after if you find a home you love — I'll pull comps for free. 🏡"

📅 SATURDAY — "In the Field"
Story 1: At an open house or showing — "Live from [address]"
Story 2: Show the best feature — "Would you look at this kitchen 😍"
Story 3: Community activity — farmers market, kids' sports, neighborhood event

📅 SUNDAY — "Real Life"
Story 1: Morning — personal content (family breakfast, workout, hobbies)
Story 2: Afternoon — relaxed lifestyle content
Story 3: Evening — "Week ahead preview: I've got [X] showings, [Y] closings — let's go 🚀"
```

### Step 3: Event-Triggered Story Sequences

Pre-built story sequences for common real estate events:

```
🏠 OPEN HOUSE DAY
─────────────────
Pre-event (morning): "Open house today at [address]! Come say hi 👋"
During (arrival): "Setting up — look at this [feature] 😍"
During (guests): "Great turnout so far! [natural moment]"
After: "That was amazing — [X] groups came through. DM me if you missed it!"

🔑 CLOSING DAY
──────────────
Pre-closing: "Closing day for [client first names]! 🎉"
At the table: "It's official — they got the keys! 🏡🔑"
Keys photo: Post the keys-in-hand photo (classic but effective)
Celebration: "From house hunting to homeowners in [X] weeks. So proud of them ❤️"

📊 RATE CHANGE
──────────────
Story 1: "BREAKING: Rates just [dropped/changed] — here's what it means"
Story 2: Face-to-camera — 30 second explainer for your market
Story 3: "If you've been waiting, this changes things. DM me 'rates' to discuss"

📝 NEW LISTING
──────────────
Story 1: "NEW LISTING ALERT 🚨 — [Address/Neighborhood]"
Story 2: Walkthrough clip — 15 seconds of the best feature
Story 3: "Full video dropping tomorrow. DM 'tour' if you want first access"
```

### Step 5: Stories Analytics Framework

```
TRACK WEEKLY:
─────────────
1. Average story views (benchmark: 5-10% of followers)
2. Reply rate (benchmark: 1-3% of viewers)
3. "Checklist" keyword volume (Friday Night Strategy)
4. Profile visits from stories
5. Link clicks (if using link sticker)
6. Poll participation rate

OPTIMIZE:
- If views dropping → Post more personal content, fewer sales-y stories
- If no replies → Ask more direct questions, use question box
- If Friday Night Strategy flat → Test different trigger words, different resources
- If poll participation low → Make questions more controversial/fun
```

## Quality Gate

Before delivering, verify:
1. **The Reels-vs-Stories distinction is structurally honored.** Per source: "If reels are how you get discovered, your stories are how you get hired." Stories content must show personality/process/trust-building — a calendar that just reposts Reel content as Stories fails this check.
2. **Friday Night Strategy is verbatim, not paraphrased.** The 3-story sequence must match the source mechanic exactly: timed post → "don't put in an offer until you check these things" → reply keyword → cheat sheet. A generic "post a story reminding people about open houses" version is incomplete.
3. **70/30 professional/personal ratio is actually represented** in the 7-day calendar, not just stated as a rule and ignored in the content.
4. **Every business/location tag opportunity is called out explicitly** — per source, tagging drives reshares and algorithmic reach; a calendar with zero tagged locations/businesses misses this mechanic.
5. **Recognition check**: would Enrico Incarnati recognize this as the trust-building "hiring" layer distinct from Reels — or does it read like a generic "post stories daily" content calendar with real estate flavor text?
