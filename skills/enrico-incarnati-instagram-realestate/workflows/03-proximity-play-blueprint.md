# /enrico-proximity — Proximity Play Blueprint

Build a complete hyperlocal content strategy that positions a real estate agent as THE authority on their city/neighborhood — not just homes, but the entire lifestyle. Based on Incarnati's "Mayor of Your City" framework.

## Usage

```
/enrico-proximity [city/market area]
```

Examples:
- `/enrico-proximity "Palmdale, CA"`
- `/enrico-proximity "North Dallas"`
- `/enrico-proximity "Brooklyn, NY"`

### Step 1: Market Intelligence Scan

Research the target city/area using Perplexity or web search. Gather:

```

───────────────────

Population: [Number]

Competitors: [Other agents heavily active on Instagram in this market]
```

### Step 2: Content Pillars — The "Mayor's Agenda"

Design 5 hyperlocal content pillars:

#### Pillar 1: Local Business Spotlights
```
CONCEPT: Interview owners of beloved local businesses
FORMAT: Mini-vlog (60-90 seconds) or Instagram Reel
FREQUENCY: 1x per week

FIRST 5 BUSINESSES TO FEATURE:
1. [Best coffee shop] — Why: [Everyone goes here]
2. [Favorite restaurant] — Why: [Local institution]
3. [Unique local business] — Why: [Story-worthy]
4. [Family-friendly spot] — Why: [Buyer demo match]
5. [New business opening] — Why: [News value]

INTERVIEW TEMPLATE:
"Hey [Name], how long have you been here?"
"What do you love about [City]?"
"What's your favorite thing on the menu?"
"If someone just moved here, what's the first thing they should try?"

PARTNERSHIP ANGLE:
- Tag the business (they'll reshare = instant new audience)
- Offer to cross-promote (their stories mention you)
- Create a "Best of [City]" highlight reel on your profile
```

#### Pillar 2: Local News & Development Coverage
```
CONCEPT: Be the first to report on changes coming to the area
FORMAT: Green Screen Reaction to local news articles
FREQUENCY: 1-2x per week (as news breaks)

CONTENT IDEAS:
1. New businesses opening in the area
2. Road/infrastructure improvements
3. School rating changes or new school openings
4. Housing development announcements
5. Major employer hiring or relocating
6. Community events and festivals
7. Crime stats improvements or neighborhood watch updates
8. Property value trends specific to this area

WHY THIS MATTERS:
"People want to know what is happening in their area.
And ultimately — does this affect the property value of their home?"
```

#### Pillar 3: Neighborhood Lifestyle Vlogs
```
CONCEPT: Day-in-the-life content focused on the NEIGHBORHOOD, not just you
FORMAT: Vlog-style Reels (60-120 seconds)
FREQUENCY: 1x per week

VLOG TEMPLATE:
"A morning in [Neighborhood]"
Scene 1: [Coffee spot — where you start your day]
Scene 2: [Walking/driving the area — what it FEELS like]
Scene 3: [Lunch spot or park — the lifestyle]
Scene 4: [Something unique — the thing only locals know]
Scene 5: [The home — tie it back to real estate]

LIFESTYLE ELEMENTS TO CAPTURE:
- The commute (how easy/scenic is it?)
- The gym or fitness scene
- Where families go on weekends
- The nightlife or dining scene
- Parks and outdoor spaces
- The "hidden gems" that don't show up on Zillow
```

#### Pillar 4: Community Interviews
```
CONCEPT: Interview real residents about why they love living here
FORMAT: Quick street-style interviews (30-60 seconds)
FREQUENCY: 2x per month

INTERVIEW QUESTIONS:
"How long have you lived in [City]?"
"What's your favorite thing about living here?"
"What would you tell someone thinking about moving here?"
"What's the one thing you wish you'd known before moving here?"

WHY THIS WORKS:
- Real people are more believable than agent testimonials
- Shows actual community, not just marketing
- Each interviewee becomes a potential content sharer
```

#### Pillar 5: "What's Coming to [City]" Series
```
CONCEPT: Future-focused content about area development
FORMAT: Carousel or Reel with visual breakdown
FREQUENCY: Monthly

TEMPLATE:
"5 things coming to [City] in [Year]"
1. [New development] — What it is + how it affects property values
2. [New business] — What it means for the area
3. [Infrastructure change] — Impact on commute/accessibility
4. [Community project] — Lifestyle improvement
5. [Wild card] — Something exciting/unexpected

RESEARCH SOURCES:
- City planning commission websites
- Local newspaper development sections
- Commercial real estate databases
- City council meeting minutes
- Local Facebook groups and community boards
```

### Step 4: Partnership & Cross-Promotion Strategy

```
LOCAL PARTNERSHIP FRAMEWORK
───────────────────────────
For each business spotlight:

BEFORE:
- DM business owner: "I feature local businesses my followers love. Can I stop by?"
- Most say yes — it's free marketing for them

DURING:
- Film 60-90 second interview
- Get their Instagram handle for tagging

AFTER:
- Post + tag them + tag location
- DM them the post to reshare on their stories
- Their audience discovers you → new followers in the area
- Save their info for future collaborations (open house catering, event partnerships)

ADVANCED PARTNERSHIPS:
- Co-host a local event (open house + local food truck)
- Create a "New to [City] Welcome Package" with local business coupons
- Partner with movers, contractors, lenders for cross-referrals
```

### Step 5: Property Value Connection Bridge

Every proximity play piece must connect back to real estate value:

```
THE BRIDGE FORMULA:
[Local content] + "And here's what this means for property values..."

EXAMPLES:
- "New Whole Foods opening → property values in a 1-mile radius typically increase 5-10%"
- "School rating went from 7 to 9 → this is the #1 factor for family buyers"
- "Goldman Sachs office opening → expect an influx of high-income renters-turned-buyers"
- "Best coffee shop in town → homes within walking distance sell 15% faster"
```
