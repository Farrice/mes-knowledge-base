# Digital Curb Appeal — Profile Optimization Checklist

## Complete Instagram Profile Audit Framework

Use this checklist to audit and optimize any real estate agent's Instagram profile for lead conversion.

---

## 1. SEO Name (Searchability)

- [ ] Name follows format: `[First Last] | [City] Real Estate Agent`
- [ ] Name includes the specific market/city they serve
- [ ] Name is searchable (not just a cute handle or brand name)
- [ ] If searching "[City] Realtor" on Instagram, does this agent appear?

**Good Examples**:
- `Adrienne | First-Time Buyer Specialist — Dallas`
- `Tom | Luxury Homes — Scottsdale AZ`
- `Jiing | MyHouseSellers — [City] Real Estate`

**Bad Examples**:
- `Jiing ✨ Living My Best Life`
- `MyHouseSellers`
- `[Brokerage Name] Agent`

---

## 2. Bio Architecture

- [ ] Line 1: Clear value proposition (who you help + how)
- [ ] Line 2: Specific market/niche identifier
- [ ] Line 3: Social proof or credential (if relevant)
- [ ] Line 4: Call to action (what to do next)
- [ ] Emoji usage is intentional, not random

**Bio Template**:
```
Helping [buyer type] find their dream home in [city/area]
[Specific niche — first-time buyers, luxury, relocation, etc.]
[Social proof — "Helped 50+ families in 2025" or team affiliation]
👇 Free [resource] + Work With Me
```

---

## 3. Link Store (Not Brokerage Website)

- [ ] Using a mobile-optimized link store (Stan Store, Linktree, etc.)
- [ ] NOT linking to a generic brokerage website
- [ ] Top link = best free resource / lead magnet
- [ ] Second link = "Work with me" / application / booking
- [ ] Links are visually clear and ordered by priority
- [ ] Each link has a clear, action-oriented title

**Link Priority Order**:
1. 🎁 Free lead magnet (checklist, guide, calculator)
2. 📞 Book a call / Apply to work with me
3. 🏠 Current listings / Market update
4. 📧 Newsletter signup (if applicable)

---

## 4. Pin Posts (3 Billboards)

- [ ] Pin 1: **Market Update** — swap weekly or monthly. Establishes you as the go-to source for local market intelligence
- [ ] Pin 2: **Client Success Story** — social proof. A real transformation story (before/after, challenge/solution)
- [ ] Pin 3: **Lead Magnet** — drives people to comment a keyword for a checklist, guide, or resource

**Pin Post Design Principles**:
- Thumbnail/cover must be visually distinct from regular feed
- Each pin serves a different function (trust, proof, capture)
- Update Pin 1 regularly to show active market knowledge
- Pin 2 should feature real client names/photos (with permission)

---

## 5. Stories Strategy

### Principle: "Reels = Discovery. Stories = Hiring."

- [ ] Posting 3-5 stories per day minimum
- [ ] Mix of personal (30%) and professional (70%)
- [ ] Using polls, question boxes, and emoji sliders for engagement
- [ ] Documenting real work (putting up signs, client meetings, showings)
- [ ] Tagging local businesses and locations
- [ ] Sharing behind-the-scenes of the real estate process
- [ ] Running the Friday Night Strategy weekly

### Friday Night Strategy Template

```
FRIDAY EVENING (5-7 PM):

Story 1: "Going to open houses this weekend? 🏠"

Story 2: "Don't put in an offer until you check these 12 things.
Most people miss these and it ends up costing them thousands."

Story 3: "Reply 'checklist' and I'll send you my cheat sheet
you can take with you this weekend."

→ ManyChat auto-DM triggers on "checklist" keyword
→ Sends PDF guide + collects email
```

### Stories Content Ideas (Weekly Rotation)

| Day | Story Type | Example |
|-----|-----------|---------|
| Monday | Market Monday — quick stat or trend | "3 homes sold in [neighborhood] this week above asking" |
| Tuesday | Behind the scenes | Document a showing, staging, or client meeting |
| Wednesday | Local spotlight | Tag a local business, share a lunch spot |
| Thursday | Tip/education | Quick tip in text or selfie video |
| Friday | Friday Night Strategy | Lead capture (see above) |
| Saturday | Open house/showing content | Live from an open house |
| Sunday | Personal/lifestyle | Show your life outside real estate |

---

## 6. Highlights Organization

- [ ] Highlights are organized by topic (not random)
- [ ] Cover images are designed and consistent
- [ ] Essential highlights are:

| Highlight | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| 🏠 Listings | Current and recent properties |
| ⭐ Reviews | Client testimonials |
| 📊 Market | Market updates and data |
| 📍 [City] | Local area content (proximity play) |
| 💡 Tips | Educational content |
| 🎉 Sold | Celebration posts (social proof) |
| 👋 About Me | Introduction / personal content |

---

## 7. Grid Aesthetic

- [ ] Feed has visual consistency (colors, style, energy)
- [ ] Reel covers are designed (not random frame captures)
- [ ] There's a recognizable visual pattern when someone visits the profile
- [ ] Content mix is visible at a glance (listings, education, local, personal)
- [ ] No stretches of identical-looking content
