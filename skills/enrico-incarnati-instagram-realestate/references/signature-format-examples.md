# Signature Format Examples — Reference Library

## Proven Signature Formats (From Source)

### 1. The Disruptor (Trent Miller)
- **Format**: Sprint through homes while showing features in one take
- **Why it works**: High energy, cinematic, impossible to scroll past
- **Personality fit**: Athletic, high-energy, competitive agents
- **Content cadence**: 2-3 listings/week

### 2. Gamified Home Tours (Lisa Dubois)
- **Format**: Hide and seek in the home — viewer plays along
- **Why it works**: Interactive, shows every room naturally, creates engagement
- **Personality fit**: Playful, creative, family-oriented agents
- **Content cadence**: 1-2 listings/week + behind-scenes stories

### 3. The Funniest Friend (Tampa Brie)
- **Format**: Comedy-first walkthroughs where you never know what she'll say next
- **Why it works**: Unpredictability creates watchability, humor builds trust
- **Personality fit**: Naturally funny, quick-witted, unfiltered agents
- **Content cadence**: 3-4 short-form reels/week

### 4. Cool Aunt Energy (Margie Morasco)
- **Format**: Viral trend leverage + immediate trust signals
- **Why it works**: Trends bring distribution, personality builds connection
- **Personality fit**: Stylish, culturally aware, relatable agents
- **Content cadence**: 2-3 trend-adapted reels/week

### 5. Clear 3-Second Hooks (Eric Thurwood)
- **Format**: All qualifying information delivered in the first 3 seconds
- **Why it works**: Respects viewer time, self-qualifies leads, clean format
- **Personality fit**: Data-driven, efficient, professional agents
- **Content cadence**: 4-5 listings/week (high volume, tight format)

## Signature Format Discovery Framework

### The Lifestyle Intersection Method

```
Step 1: "What do you love OUTSIDE of real estate?"
→ Answer: [hobby/passion/interest]

Step 2: "Who buys your homes?"
→ Answer: [buyer demographic]

Step 3: "Do THEY [your hobby]?"
→ If YES: That's the format.
→ If NO: Find the overlap between your interest and their lifestyle.

Step 4: Design the format
→ How does [your hobby] physically integrate into a home walkthrough?
→ What recurring bit or angle does this create?
→ Is it repeatable across every listing?
```

### Examples of Lifestyle Intersections

| Agent's Passion | Buyer Demo | Format Concept |
|----------------|-----------|----------------|
| Golf | Dads/families | Walk through with putter, test carpet, show ceiling height with full swing |
| Cooking | Young couples | Cook a meal in the kitchen during the tour, rate the kitchen |
| Dog lover | Pet owners | Bring your dog on every tour, rate the yard and pet-friendliness |
| Fitness | Health-conscious | Rate the home gym potential, test the garage for CrossFit space |
| Wine | Luxury buyers | Pair each room with a wine recommendation, build a "wine cellar tour" |
| Music | Creative professionals | Rate the acoustics of each room, test the studio potential |
| Hiking/outdoors | Active families | Start each video from a nearby trail, show walkability and nature access |
| Fashion/style | Design-conscious | Rate the closet and style potential of each home, "would I live here?" lens |
| Comedy | Everyone | Improv bits during walkthroughs, unexpected commentary, character creation |
| Cars | Gear heads | Rate the garage, driveway, and parking situation as the main feature |

## Format Design Checklist

Every signature format must satisfy ALL THREE functions:

- [ ] **Brings in new eyes** — Is this entertaining/interesting enough for non-buyers to watch and share?
- [ ] **Qualifies leads** — Does it attract the RIGHT type of buyer for your market?
- [ ] **Makes you an attractive character** — Does it showcase your unique personality in a way nobody can copy?

If any checkbox is missing, the format is incomplete. Redesign.
