# Content Expansion Pack — Templates & Prompts

## The 5-Format Remix System

Take ANY real estate educational topic and produce 5 distinct content pieces.

---

### Format 1: Visual Math

**What it is**: Someone walks into your office/space and asks a real question. You crunch the numbers live.

**Template**:
```
HOOK: "[Person] walks in and asks: Can I buy a $[X] home making $[Y]/hour?"
BODY: Walk through the actual math on whiteboard/paper/screen
- Monthly income: [calc]
- DTI ratio: [calc]
- Down payment needed: [calc]
- Monthly mortgage: [calc]
PAYOFF: "So here's the real answer..."
CTA: "Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you my affordability calculator"
```

**Topics this works for**:
- "Can I afford this home?"
- "How much do I need for a down payment?"
- "What do closing costs actually look like?"
- "Is it cheaper to rent or buy right now?"
- "How much equity do I build in year 1?"

---

### Format 2: Green Screen Reaction

**What it is**: Find a real headline/article. Use Instagram's green screen feature. React and explain.

**Template**:
```
HOOK: "I saw this headline and I need to talk about it..."
[Show headline on green screen]
BODY: "Here's what this actually means for you if you're buying/selling..."
- What the headline says (surface)
- What it actually means (your expertise)
- What to DO about it (action)
PAYOFF: "So stop panicking. Here's what I'd do..."
CTA: "Save this for when you hear people talking about [topic]"
```

**Topics this works for**:
- Interest rate changes
- Market predictions
- New housing regulations
- Economic news affecting real estate
- Local development announcements

---

### Format 3: This vs. That Bracket

**What it is**: Compare two options head-to-head. Tournament-style. 6-8 rounds to an ultimate winner.

**Template**:
```
HOOK: "Which renovation ACTUALLY adds value? Let's find out."
ROUNDS:
- Round 1: Kitchen remodel vs. Bathroom remodel → Winner: [X]
- Round 2: New roof vs. Landscaping → Winner: [X]
- Round 3: Basement finish vs. Deck addition → Winner: [X]
- Round 4: Smart home upgrades vs. Energy efficiency → Winner: [X]
FINAL: "[Winner] is the #1 renovation for home value"
CTA: "Do you agree? Fight me in the comments 👇"
```

**Topics this works for**:
- Renovations that add value
- Best neighborhoods in [city]
- Renting vs. buying scenarios
- New build vs. resale
- Different loan types compared
- Best time of year to buy/sell

---

### Format 4: S-Tier Ranking

**What it is**: Rank items from S tier (best) to F tier (worst). Visually satisfying, highly shareable.

**Template**:
```
HOOK: "I'm ranking [topic] from S tier to F tier and I know you'll disagree"
RANKING:
- S Tier: [Your top pick + why]
- A Tier: [Strong choice + why]
- B Tier: [Decent + why]
- C Tier: [Meh + why]
- D Tier: [Skip it + why]
- F Tier: [Waste of money + why]
CTA: "What's YOUR S tier? Comment below"
```

**Topics this works for**:
- Home renovations
- Neighborhoods in your city
- First-time buyer mistakes
- Home features (pool, basement, garage, etc.)
- Real estate apps/tools
- Open house red flags

---

### Format 5: Digital Clipboard

**What it is**: Walkthrough video with overlaid checklist. Engineered for SAVES.

**Template**:
```
HOOK: "[N] things you MUST check before closing on a home"
VIDEO: Property walkthrough
OVERLAY LIST:
1. Check water pressure in every bathroom
2. Test ALL light switches
3. Open and close every window
4. Run the dishwasher
5. Check the water heater age
6. Look for foundation cracks
7. Test the garage door
8. Check cell service in every room
9. Visit at night to check noise levels
10. Ask about HOA rules
11. Check the roof age
12. Test the HVAC
CTA: "Comment 'checklist' and I'll DM you the full PDF you can take to your next showing"
```

**Topics this works for**:
- Pre-closing inspection checklist
- First-time buyer preparation list
- Things to ask at an open house
- Red flags when house hunting
- Moving day essentials
- Staging checklist for sellers
