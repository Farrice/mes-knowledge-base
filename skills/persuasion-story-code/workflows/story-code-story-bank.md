# Story Code Story Bank

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load:
- `skills/persuasion-story-code/genius.md`
- `skills/persuasion-story-code/references/story-taxonomy.md`
- `skills/persuasion-story-code/references/quality-rubric.md`

Use when the user wants a durable library of stories for content, sales, launches, websites, video, email, and conversations.

## Input Required

- Business context.
- Founder background.
- Offer/product/service details.
- Customer results or proof.
- Common objections.
- Audience language or research if available.

## Execution

1. **Inventory raw material**: Founder moments, customer wins, failures, discoveries, proof, demos, market changes, objections.
2. **Sort by story type**: Assign each usable moment to the taxonomy.
3. **Create story cards**: Write compact reusable cards, not full essays.
4. **Add usage tags**: Sales page, email, LinkedIn, short video, webinar, pitch, FAQ, DM, onboarding.
5. **Flag evidence gaps**: Note where proof, permission, numbers, or customer approval is needed.
6. **Prioritize creation**: Pick the highest-value missing stories.

## Output

Produce a **Reusable Story Bank**:

- **Story Inventory Table**: title, type, source material, persuasion job, channel tags, proof status.
- **Finished Story Cards**: 10-20 concise story cards when enough material exists.
- **Missing Story List**: what to collect next.
- **Interview Prompts**: questions to elicit founder and customer stories.
- **Content Repurposing Map**: which cards can become posts, emails, scripts, sales sections, or pitch answers.

## Quality Gate

- No invented stories.
- Each card has a clear use case.
- Stories are compact enough to be reused across formats.
