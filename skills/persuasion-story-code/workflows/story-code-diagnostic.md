# Story Code Diagnostic

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load:
- `skills/persuasion-story-code/genius.md`
- `skills/persuasion-story-code/references/story-taxonomy.md`
- `skills/persuasion-story-code/references/quality-rubric.md`

Use when the user has raw material, an offer, a draft, a launch, a sales page, a content plan, or a product and needs to know which stories would make it more persuasive.

## Input Required

- Business, offer, product, service, or content context.
- Target audience.
- Current asset or raw notes if available.
- Desired action: buy, book, reply, share, apply, watch, subscribe, trust, or understand.

## Execution

1. **Identify the persuasion moment**: Name whether the audience is facing confusion, uncertainty, objection, low trust, low desire, low familiarity, or weak proof.
2. **Map the story jobs**: Assign the needed story categories: origin, prospect experience, future, reassurance, explanation, trust, or objection.
3. **Select exact story types**: Choose the best specific types from the taxonomy and reject poor fits.
4. **Find source material**: Extract available facts, moments, proof, customer language, founder memories, product details, and missing evidence.
5. **Prioritize**: Rank the top stories by business impact and ease of creation.
6. **Specify placement**: Place each story in the asset: opening, proof block, CTA lead-in, FAQ, sales call answer, email, post, or video beat.

## Output Schema

Produce a **Story Opportunity Map**:

- **Audience State**: current beliefs, desire, doubts, objections.
- **Primary Barrier**: the one barrier that matters most.
- **Story Priority Table**: story type, job, available raw material, missing proof, placement, urgency.
- **Top 3 Stories To Create First**: exact working titles and why.
- **Data To Collect**: interviews, testimonials, screenshots, metrics, demos, support logs, reviews.
- **Do Not Use Yet**: stories that would require invented or weak claims.

## Quality Gate

- Every recommended story has one job.
- No story depends on facts the user has not supplied or can not collect.
- The output tells the user what to make first, not just what is possible.
