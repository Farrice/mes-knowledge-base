# Story Code Conversational Story

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load:
- `skills/persuasion-story-code/genius.md`
- `skills/persuasion-story-code/references/story-taxonomy.md`
- `skills/persuasion-story-code/references/quality-rubric.md`

Use when the user needs one finished persuasion story for a post, email, sales call, page section, webinar, pitch, DM, or video.

## Input Required

- Raw event, anecdote, proof point, idea, feature, objection, testimonial, or founder note.
- Audience and desired action.
- Channel and length target.
- Any facts that must be included or avoided.

## Execution

1. **Name the story job**: credibility, familiarity, future desire, reassurance, explanation, trust, or objection.
2. **Choose the story type**: Select one exact story type from the taxonomy.
3. **Build the truth inventory**: Separate verified facts from assumptions.
4. **Compress to three beats**: Before/state, event/action, after/meaning.
5. **Write conversationally**: Use familiar words, specific images, and clean sequence.
6. **Adapt to channel**: Shape for post, email, pitch, sales page, DM, or video.
7. **Audit**: Remove inconsistency, vagueness, exaggeration, and stiff language.

## Output Schema

Produce:

- **Story Strategy**: story type, story job, audience barrier, placement.
- **Finished Story**: ready to use.
- **Short Version**: 1-3 sentences.
- **Medium Version**: 1-3 paragraphs.
- **CTA Bridge**: one natural next line after the story.
- **Truth Notes**: claims that need proof, qualification, or legal review.

## Quality Gate

- The finished story sounds natural aloud.
- It is short enough for the channel.
- The story persuades by showing, not by explaining the method.
