> **Use When**: Transitioning an org from traditional marketing to content-first media company

## Step 1: Starting Position Assessment

Run `/oren-media-org-diagnostic` if not already completed. If completed, load the scorecard.

Establish:
1. **Current tier** — iPhone / Mid / Enterprise
2. **Target tier** — where the org wants to be in 90 days
3. **Available budget** for the transformation
4. **Existing team** — who stays, who gets reassigned, who gets hired
5. **Founder commitment level** — weekly hours the founder will dedicate to content

## Step 2: Phase Gate Design

### Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-30)

**Objective**: Build the minimum viable content engine

| Week | Action | Deliverable | Gate Check |
|:-----|:-------|:-----------|:-----------|
| 1 | Define POV + editorial standards | Brand Content Manifesto (1-page) | Can articulate POV in one sentence? |
| 1 | Select project management tool | PM workspace configured | Tool live with team access? |
| 2 | Hire/designate Strategist (Pod 1) | Strategist onboarded | First brief produced? |
| 2 | Define content pillars (3-5) | Pillar framework documented | Each pillar has 10+ concept ideas? |
| 3 | Hire/designate Creator (Pod 1) | Creator onboarded, gear acquired | First content pieces shot? |
| 3 | Set up analytics tracking | Dashboard live | Baseline metrics captured? |
| 4 | First week of full production | 10 concepts produced | Output meets quality standard? |

**Phase 1 gate**: Pod 1 is producing 10 concepts/week at acceptable quality. Analytics are being tracked. Do NOT proceed to Phase 2 without this.

### Phase 2: Velocity (Days 31-60)

**Objective**: Build the feedback loop and expand the content world

| Week | Action | Deliverable | Gate Check |
|:-----|:-------|:-----------|:-----------|
| 5 | First analytics review cycle | Performance report | What worked, what didn't — documented? |
| 5 | Begin external creator sourcing | 3-5 creator candidates identified | Using miniocial/Super Affiliate/Outer Signal? |
| 6 | Launch pillar testing system | 4-6 test episodes of signature format | Data on which format gets traction? |
| 6 | Connect organic → paid pipeline | First organic posts boosted/sparked | Organic-to-paid workflow documented? |
| 7 | Onboard first external creator | First external content delivered | Brief → delivery → publish pipeline smooth? |
| 8 | Lock signature series format | Format bible documented | Clear repeatable format with consistent output? |

**Phase 2 gate**: Signature series format locked. At least 2 external creators producing. Organic-to-paid pipeline flowing. Analytics review is weekly habit.

### Phase 3: Integration (Days 61-90)

**Objective**: Close the flywheel circuit and build organizational DNA

| Week | Action | Deliverable | Gate Check |
|:-----|:-------|:-----------|:-----------|
| 9 | Connect content to email/SMS | Content-first email templates live | Same faces/voice across channels? |
| 9 | Evaluate agency partners against flywheel | Agency scorecard | Do they "get" the creator flywheel? |
| 10 | Launch founder content (if applicable) | Founder content calendar live | Founder producing 2-3 pieces/week? |
| 10 | First monthly recap to leadership | Media company progress report | Clear ROI storytelling to stakeholders? |
| 11 | Assess Pod 2 readiness | Pod 2 business case | Volume + quality + demand justify expansion? |
| 12 | Full flywheel audit | Flywheel health scorecard | All 5 nodes + 5 connections scoring 3+? |

**Phase 3 gate**: Complete flywheel circuit operational. Content-first DNA embedded in daily operations. Pod 2 decision made.

## Step 4: Risk Mitigation

For the top 5 transformation risks, pre-build the response:

1. **Founder loses interest at week 3** → Pre-commitment contract, show 90-day patience data
2. **Creator quits early** → Have backup sourcing channels documented from Day 1
3. **Content quality drifts** → Weekly creative review is non-negotiable (Pattern 5)
4. **Agency resists the model** → Evaluate against Pattern 12 criteria; replace if needed
5. **Leadership demands ROI at Day 14** → Educate on the 90-day commitment (Pattern 3, Gate 2)
