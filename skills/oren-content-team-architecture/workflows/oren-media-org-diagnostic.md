# Media Org Readiness Diagnostic

> **Expert**: Oren — Content-Team Architecture
> **Produces**: Transformation Readiness Scorecard + Gap Roadmap
> **Use When**: Auditing whether a brand is ready to become a content-first organization
> **Load First**: [genius.md](../genius.md) — Patterns 3 (Media Company Diagnostic), 7 (Exemplar Matrix), 10 (Anchor Strategy)

---

## Step 1: Intake — Org Context

Collect the following (ask the user if not provided):

1. **Industry & size** — What does the brand sell? Revenue range? Employee count?
2. **Current marketing structure** — Existing team roles, agency partners, budget allocation
3. **Content status quo** — What content exists today? Who makes it? What platforms?
4. **Ambition level** — iPhone-tier startup or enterprise multi-pod buildout?
5. **Existing content samples** — Links to 3-5 pieces of current content (if available)

---

## Step 2: Five-Gate Audit

Score each gate on a 1-5 scale with specific evidence.

### Gate 1: Point of View (POV)
- What is the brand saying beyond "buy our product"?
- Is there a lifestyle, function, humor, or key insight that differentiates?
- **Pass criteria**: Can articulate a clear, defensible POV in one sentence

### Gate 2: Patience Capacity
- Has leadership committed to 90+ days of consistent publishing before judging results?
- History of "starting and stopping" content initiatives?
- **Pass criteria**: Written commitment to minimum 90-day content sprint

### Gate 3: Editorial Standards
- Can anyone on the team articulate why a piece of content is "good enough" or "not good enough"?
- Is there a reference library or benchmark set?
- **Pass criteria**: At least one person with taste-level judgment on the team

### Gate 4: Process Readiness
- Are characters, production workflows, approval chains, and publishing cadence defined?
- Is there a project management tool in use?
- **Pass criteria**: Documented production workflow exists (even if basic)

### Gate 5: Product-Content Integration
- Is the content team in the room during product development?
- Does a content roadmap exist alongside the product roadmap?
- **Pass criteria**: Content team has visibility into upcoming product launches 30+ days in advance

---

## Step 3: Exemplar Match

Based on the scores, match the org to the appropriate tier from Pattern 7:

| Score Range | Tier | Model |
|:------------|:-----|:------|
| 5-10 | iPhone-tier | Cheese Store / Chunky Fit — start with founder + iPhone + one person |
| 11-18 | Mid-tier | Flamingo Estate / Tracksmith — build first pod with strategist + creator |
| 19-25 | Enterprise-tier | Represent / Ladder — multi-pod with shared resources |

---

## Step 4: Gap Roadmap

For each gate scoring below 3, generate:
1. **Specific gap description** — what's missing and why it blocks progress
2. **Remediation action** — the exact next step to close the gap
3. **Timeline** — estimated days/weeks to close
4. **Dependency** — what else must be in place first

---

## Step 5: Output Schema — Readiness Scorecard

Produce a single-page scorecard:

```
BRAND: [Name]
DATE: [Date]
OVERALL READINESS: [Score/25] — [Tier Match]

GATE 1 — Point of View:       [X/5] — [Evidence]
GATE 2 — Patience Capacity:   [X/5] — [Evidence]
GATE 3 — Editorial Standards: [X/5] — [Evidence]
GATE 4 — Process Readiness:   [X/5] — [Evidence]
GATE 5 — Product-Content:     [X/5] — [Evidence]

TOP 3 GAPS:
1. [Gap] → [Fix] → [Timeline]
2. [Gap] → [Fix] → [Timeline]
3. [Gap] → [Fix] → [Timeline]

RECOMMENDED NEXT WORKFLOW:
→ [Specific workflow from this skill domain]
```

---

## Quality Gate

- [ ] All 5 gates scored 1-5 with cited evidence, never assumed from a single answer
- [ ] Overall readiness score correctly maps to the Step 3 tier band (5-10 iPhone / 11-18 Mid / 19-25 Enterprise)
- [ ] Every gate scoring below 3 carries a Step 4 gap-roadmap entry (gap, remediation, timeline, dependency)
- [ ] Gate 2 (Patience Capacity) only passes with a WRITTEN commitment to a 90-day sprint — a verbal one fails the gate
- [ ] Recommended next workflow is named specifically, never left generic ("more work needed")
