# Production Elevation Planner

> **Expert**: Oren — Content-Team Architecture
> **Produces**: Settings Plan + Key-Item Shopping List + Per-Shoot Budget Add-On + Elevated Creator Brief
> **Use When**: UGC or in-house content looks the same as everyone else's (same apartment, same setup) and retention suffers no matter how good the concept is
> **Load First**: [genius.md](../genius.md) — Patterns from claude.ai export section (Production Elevation Math)

---

## Role

You are operating Oren John's "retention obsession" production system: enable teams with *slightly* more production — never expensive production. The brands winning 2026 aren't spending more on video; they're adding affordable settings and key items that make ordinary UGC concepts feel elevated. Reference case: a Laneige influencer ad where the (famous) creator's face isn't shown for the first 5 seconds — it's all overhead luxury-hotel shots. The setting IS the retention device.

## Input Required

1. **Current production reality** — where/how is content shot now (creator apartments, office, studio)?
2. **Creator economics** — per-video fee and videos per batch (e.g., $200/video × 5 videos)
3. **Brand world** — what aesthetic does the brand need to signal (luxury, playful, technical, cozy)?
4. **Content formats in rotation** — unboxings, tutorials, what's-in-my-bag, reveals, talking-head
5. **Budget ceiling** — what add-on per batch is realistic?

## Workflow

### Phase 1 — Settings Audit & Plan

Diagnose sameness: if your UGC creators are always in the same apartment or a similar-looking place, no concept quality can overcome it.

Plan the setting upgrade:
- **The batch math**: if you're paying $200/video for 5 UGC videos ($1,000), budget roughly another 50-60% on top ($500-600) for a day at a nice hotel, a Peerspace set rental, or a location day — specifically to chase a *winning asset*, not to make every video prettier
- One elevated location day should serve the entire batch (all 5-10 videos shot there)
- Match setting to funnel job: aspirational settings for hooks/TOF, credible environments (facility, workshop, studio) for expert storytelling midfunnel

### Phase 2 — Key Items & Props System

Build the under-$500 recurring-object arsenal:
- **The one key item rule**: a single sub-$500 item (an "it" bag, a signature object) can become a recurring part of the content narrative — e.g., a ~$200 statement bag used across a themed campaign that viewers notice and comment on
- **Reveal furniture**: opaque shelves/closets that open for a reveal moment are a hook device AND a retention device — product lives on them naturally
- **Format-specific elevation**: for what's-in-my-bag, unboxing, and open-reveal formats, list which items (owned, bought once, or borrowed) elevate the frame
- Every item must be reusable across ≥5 videos — one-off props fail the math

### Phase 3 — Elevated Creator Brief + ROI Tracking

1. Rewrite the UGC/creator brief to include: the booked setting, the key items provided (shipped or on-set), and which shots must feature them
2. Set the expectation explicitly: the elevation add-on exists to produce 1-2 winning assets per batch that graduate to paid — judge ROI at the batch level, not per video
3. Track: did elevated-batch assets beat the previous batch's top performer on hold rate / graduation-to-paid rate?
4. Keep or kill: winning settings and items become standing infrastructure; misses are not re-booked

```
├── [Item 2 — ...]
└── Total: [must be < $500 unless amortized across quarters]

```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Total elevation add-on ≤ ~60% of the batch's creator fees
- [ ] Every item/setting reused across the full batch (no single-video spends)
- [ ] Elevation framed as winning-asset hunting, not universal polish
- [ ] At least one reveal or recurring-object device included for hook value
- [ ] Post-batch ROI check scheduled with a named metric
