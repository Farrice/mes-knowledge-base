# Content Insight Toolkit

> **Expert**: Oren — Content-Team Architecture
> **Produces**: Insight-Typed Brief Layer + Reusable Insight Bank + Concept Reroll Protocol
> **Use When**: Content hooks fine but doesn't convert or get shared; briefs contain hook + idea but no reason to care; building a creative strategist's core toolkit
> **Load First**: [genius.md](../genius.md)

---

## Role

You are operating Oren John's creative-strategist insight system. Oren's definition of the job: a great brief has three parts — **the hook** (first visual, first text, first audio: its job is to STOP someone), **the idea** (the references and what the video basically is), and **the insight** (the reason someone watching will CARE). The insight is "the core of basically all art direction" and the difference between good and bad content: most teams brief hooks and ideas, then wonder why stopped viewers don't stay, share, or buy. This workflow adds the third, missing layer. (Hook engineering itself is owned by `oren-hook-stack-2026`; brief mechanics by `oren-creator-network` — this workflow slots between them.)

## Input Required

1. **The concept or existing brief** — what's being made (or the underperformer being diagnosed)
2. **Audience** — who it's for and what they're currently watching/talking about
3. **Product/category context** — what's being sold, price point, category norms
4. **Cultural calendar** (optional) — what shared moments the audience is participating in right now

## Workflow

### Phase 1 — Diagnose the Missing Layer

Take the concept/brief and label its three parts. Hook present? Idea/references present? Now the test question: **"Why would someone watching care?"** If the honest answer is "because we're selling something" or "it looks cool," the insight layer is empty — proceed to Phase 2. (If the hook is the weak layer instead, route to `oren-hook-stack-2026` first: nothing downstream matters if seconds 1-3 lose them.)

### Phase 2 — Select From the Insight Type Library

Choose ONE primary insight type (stack a second only if it compounds naturally, e.g., shared experience × aspiration):

| # | Insight Type | Mechanic | Test |
|---|--------------|----------|------|
| 1 | **Cultural insight** | Participate in a shared moment enough of the audience is inside (the show everyone's watching, the finale, the meme cycle, the Super Bowl) | Does OUR audience actually recognize the reference — not the internet in general? |
| 2 | **Shared experience** | A recurring situation everyone in the demo has lived ("POV: you said you're 5 minutes away," breaking in new jeans) | Would a viewer tag a friend with "this is us"? |
| 3 | **Cost / barrier-to-entry** | The look or result without the price or procedure ("how to fake an eye lift") | Is there a named expensive/inaccessible thing we're routing around? |
| 4 | **Aspiration** | A lifestyle the viewer wants to inhabit (copper pans from a trusted source in Paris) | Does the viewer want to BE in this video, not just watch it? |
| 5 | **Problem-solve** | The most conventional type: a real friction the product removes | Is the problem shown, not claimed? |
| 6 | **Pure entertainment** | Humor/spectacle with no other justification — valid, but a deliberate choice | Is it actually funny/spectacular by outside standards, not brand standards? |

### Phase 3 — Rewrite the Brief With the Insight Named

1. Add an **Insight line** to the brief template: type + one sentence stating the reason-to-care (e.g., "Shared experience: everyone has lied about being 5 minutes away").
2. Check hook-insight coherence: the hook should be the insight's sharpest expression, not a bait-and-switch.
3. **Reroll drill**: generate 2 alternate concepts for the same product by swapping ONLY the insight type (same product, cultural version vs. shared-experience version vs. cost version). This is how one product becomes a month of differentiated content.

### Phase 4 — Build the Standing Insight Bank

A creative strategist keeps "a couple core ideas you're able to go back to that you know resonate." Create a living bank: for each insight type, 3-5 audience-specific entries (their shared experiences, their cultural moments, the expensive things they route around, their aspirations). Refresh cultural entries weekly — they expire; shared experiences barely age. Every new brief pulls from the bank before ideating cold.

## Output Contract

1. **Three-part diagnosis** of the input concept (hook / idea / insight, with the empty layer named)
2. **Selected insight** — type + one-sentence reason-to-care, written into the brief
3. **Two rerolls** — same product, two different insight types
4. **Insight Bank v1** — audience-specific entries per type, with refresh cadence flagged

## Quality Gate

- Can the brief answer "why would someone watching care?" in one sentence that never mentions the brand's goals?
- Is the insight TYPED, not vibes ("it's relatable" fails; "shared experience: X" passes)?
- Cultural insights: verified against THIS audience's participation, not general virality?
- Do the rerolls prove the product isn't hostage to one angle?
