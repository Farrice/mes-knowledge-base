> **Use When**: Designing the founder/CEO's personal content presence as part of the brand media company
> **Load First**: [genius.md](../genius.md) — Patterns 1 (Pod Architecture), 10 (Anchor Strategy), 11 (Content-Product Integration)

---

## Step 1: Founder Assessment

Collect:
1. **Founder's name and role** — CEO, co-founder, etc.
2. **On-camera comfort level** (1-10) — are they natural or terrified?
3. **Available time** — weekly hours the founder will dedicate to content
4. **Existing presence** — any social accounts, podcast appearances, talks?
5. **Anti-patterns** — what the founder REFUSES to do (e.g., dancing, scripted reads)
6. **Brand relationship** — how closely tied is the founder to the brand identity?

---

## Step 2: Founder Content Archetype

Map the founder to one of four content archetypes:

### The Craftsman
- Deep product knowledge, passionate about the details
- Content: How it's made, design decisions, product philosophy, factory tours
- Energy: Calm, authoritative, detailed
- Example: Aimé Leon Dore's Teddy Santis, Flamingo Estate's Richard Christiansen

### The Evangelist
- Industry POV, challenger mentality, willing to be controversial
- Content: Hot takes, industry commentary, behind-the-strategy, interviews
- Energy: High, opinionated, provocative
- Example: Represent's founders, Tracksmith's leadership

### The Storyteller
- Personal narrative intertwined with brand mission
- Content: Origin stories, customer stories, day-in-the-life, vulnerable moments
- Energy: Warm, personal, relatable
- Example: Chunky Fit's founder

### The Operator
- Business-building content, hiring/scaling transparency
- Content: Revenue updates, hiring posts, lessons learned, operational decisions
- Energy: Strategic, transparent, educational
- Example: DTC founders sharing growth metrics

**Most founders are a blend of 2 archetypes.** Identify the primary and secondary.

---

## Step 3: Content Format Design

Based on archetype and comfort level, design 3-5 signature formats:

### Low Comfort (1-4/10)
- Voice-over product shots (founder speaks but isn't on camera)
- Written posts with founder's voice (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, newsletter)
- Podcast appearances (easier than solo content)
- Behind-scenes iPhone footage (casual, not polished)

### Medium Comfort (5-7/10)
- Talking head with teleprompter or bullet points
- Sit-down interviews (someone asks questions, founder answers)
- Walk-and-talks (moving through office/workshop/store)
- Reaction videos (founder reacts to customer content, competitor moves)

### High Comfort (8-10/10)
- Direct-to-camera opinion pieces
- Unscripted vlogs
- Live streams
- Podcast hosting
- Conference-style presentations

## Step 4: Content Calendar Architecture

### Weekly Founder Content Cadence

```
FOUNDER CONTENT TARGETS:
├── Minimum: 2 pieces/week
├── Target: 3-5 pieces/week
└── Maximum: 7 pieces/week (content-first founder)

WEEKLY RHYTHM:
├── Monday: Content strategist sends founder brief for the week
│   (3-5 concepts with hooks, talking points, references)
├── Tuesday or Wednesday: Production block (60-90 min)
│   (Shoot 3-5 concepts in one batch session)
├── Thursday: Strategist sends rough cuts for review
├── Friday: Publishing begins (staggered across next week)
└── Ongoing: Founder captures 2-3 spontaneous moments/week (iPhone, voice memo)
```

### Content Pillar Distribution

```
PILLAR 1: Product/Craft (30%)
→ Design decisions, making-of, quality details, why this matters

PILLAR 2: POV/Industry (30%)
→ What most people get wrong, hot takes, trends the founder sees

PILLAR 3: Behind-the-Curtain (20%)
→ Day-in-the-life, team moments, building in public, real challenges

PILLAR 4: Customer/Community (20%)
→ Customer stories, community moments, responding to feedback
```

## Step 5: Production Protocol

### The Founder Content Pod

```
THE FOUNDER POD:
├── Pod Strategist (can be shared with Brand Pod)
│   → Writes briefs, manages calendar, reviews edits
├── Founder
│   → Shows up for production blocks, captures spontaneous content
└── Videographer/Editor
│   → Shoots production blocks, edits to platform spec
    → May be shared resource across pods
```

### The Production Block

**Duration**: 60-90 minutes (never more than 90)
**Frequency**: Once per week
**Output**: 3-5 raw concepts minimum

**Block structure**:
1. 5 min — Review today's concepts, get founder aligned
2. 15 min — Concept 1 (2-3 takes)
3. 15 min — Concept 2 (2-3 takes)
4. 15 min — Concept 3 (2-3 takes)
5. 10 min — Bonus concept or B-roll
6. 5 min — Quick debrief, any spontaneous ideas

**Rules**:
- Never exceed 90 minutes (founder fatigue = bad content)
- Have everything set up BEFORE the founder arrives
- The strategist directs, the creator shoots — the founder just talks
- If a take isn't working after 3 attempts, move on
- Capture genuine reactions and off-script moments

## Step 6: Anchor Strategy

The founder's content acts as the brand's "anchor show" — the narrative backbone that all other content orbits around.

### How Founder Content Feeds the Flywheel
1. **Organic social** — Founder content gets highest engagement (people follow people)
2. **Paid amplification** — Founder testimonial-style content performs in ads
3. **Email** — Founder voice in email sequence builds trust
4. **Website** — Founder video on About page and PDPs increases conversion
5. **PR** — Founder content creates a media-ready library for press opportunities
6. **External creators** — Creator content can reference/respond to founder content
