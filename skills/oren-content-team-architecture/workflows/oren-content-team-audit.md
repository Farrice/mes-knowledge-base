# Content Team Performance Audit

> **Expert**: Oren — Content-Team Architecture
> **Produces**: Team Performance Scorecard + Structural Diagnosis + Optimization Plan
> **Use When**: An existing content team is underperforming, or quarterly review is due
> **Load First**: [genius.md](../genius.md) — Patterns 4, 5, 6, 12 (team structure, cadence, standards, agency)

---

## Step 1: Audit Scope

Collect:
1. **Team roster** — every person involved in content production (internal + external + agency)
2. **Current output** — weekly/monthly content volume by platform
3. **Performance data** — last 90 days of analytics (views, engagement, conversion, ad performance)
4. **Budget** — total monthly spend on content team + ads + tools
5. **Goals** — what the team is supposed to achieve (awareness, leads, revenue)
6. **Complaints** — what leadership/stakeholders say isn't working

---

## Step 2: Six-Dimension Audit

### Dimension 1: Volume
- Are they hitting the 10-concept/week standard?
- If not, where is the bottleneck? (ideation, production, editing, approval?)
- Volume trend: increasing, flat, or declining over last 90 days?

**Score 1-5**: 5 = 10+ concepts/week consistently, 1 = fewer than 3/week

### Dimension 2: Quality
- Pull 10 random pieces from the last month
- Grade each against the brand's editorial standards
- Distribution: How many are Ship / Fix / Kill quality?
- Quality trend: improving, flat, or declining?

**Score 1-5**: 5 = 80%+ Ship quality, 1 = majority need Fix or Kill

### Dimension 3: People
- Is each role filled correctly? (strategist ≠ creator ≠ editor)
- Are people in their zone of genius or covering for gaps?
- Is the strategist genuinely strategic or just a social media manager with a new title?
- Is the creator actually creating or drowning in admin?
- When was the last hire? The last fire?

**Score 1-5**: 5 = right people in right roles, 1 = role confusion and coverage gaps

### Dimension 4: Process
- Does a weekly cadence exist? Is it followed?
- Are briefs being written before production?
- Is there a creative review process?
- Is performance data reviewed weekly?
- Is there a documented workflow from idea → publish?

**Score 1-5**: 5 = documented + followed, 1 = ad hoc chaos

### Dimension 5: Integration
- Is organic feeding paid?
- Is content voice consistent across email, social, ads, website?
- Is the content team connected to product/launch calendars?
- Are external creators integrated into the flywheel?

**Score 1-5**: 5 = fully connected flywheel, 1 = siloed channels

### Dimension 6: Agency Alignment (if applicable)
- Does the agency understand the content-first model?
- Can the agency articulate which specific creatives drive results?
- Is the agency proactively sourcing creative insights or just executing?
- Does the agency collaborate with the internal pod or operate independently?
- Would removing the agency improve or hurt the flywheel?

**Score 1-5**: 5 = agency amplifies the pod, 1 = agency is a bottleneck

**Agency Red Flags**:
- Reports on campaign metrics but not creative-level metrics
- Can't name their top 3 performing creatives this month
- Takes 2+ weeks to launch new creative
- Doesn't attend weekly pod creative reviews
- Charges for "strategy" that's just media buying

## Step 4: Root Cause Diagnosis

For the lowest-scoring dimensions, diagnose root cause:

### Common Root Causes
1. **Volume low because quality expectations too high** → Lower the bar for test content, separate "hero" from "volume" tracks
2. **Quality low because no editorial standards** → Define the Ship/Fix/Kill rubric first
3. **People miscast** → Strategist doing creator work, or vice versa → restructure roles
4. **Process absent** → No cadence = no accountability → implement `/oren-pod-cadence`
5. **Integration broken** → Silos between organic, paid, email → run `/oren-content-flywheel`
6. **Agency misaligned** → They optimize media, not creative → renegotiate scope or replace

---

## Step 5: Optimization Plan

For each dimension below target, produce:

1. **Diagnosis** — one sentence on what's wrong
2. **Fix** — specific action to take
3. **Owner** — who executes
4. **Timeline** — days to implement
5. **Success metric** — how you know it worked
6. **Workflow to run** — which Oren workflow addresses this gap
