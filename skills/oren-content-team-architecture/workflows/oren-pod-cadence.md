# Pod Operating Rhythm Engine

> **Expert**: Oren — Content-Team Architecture
> **Produces**: Weekly + Monthly Operating Cadence + Meeting Architecture + Review Protocols
> **Use When**: A pod exists but lacks operational rhythm, or when building a new pod's operating system
> **Load First**: [genius.md](../genius.md) — Patterns 5 (Cadence Architecture), 6 (10-Asset Standard), 9 (Paid Creative Ops)

---

## Step 1: Pod Context

Collect:
1. **Pod composition** — strategist, creator(s), external contributors, shared resources
2. **Platforms** — which channels does this pod own?
3. **Output target** — concepts per week (default: 10)
4. **Current cadence** — any standing meetings or rituals already in place?
5. **PM tool** — what project management system is active?

---

## Step 2: Weekly Operating Rhythm

### Monday — Strategy + Planning
**Who**: Strategist + Creator(s)
**Duration**: 60-90 minutes
**Agenda**:
1. Review last week's analytics (top 3 performers, bottom 3)
2. Review cultural moments, competitor moves, trending formats for the week
3. Present this week's brief stack (10 concepts minimum)
4. Assign concepts to creators
5. Confirm production schedule (shoot days, edit timelines, publish slots)

**Output**: Weekly content calendar populated in PM tool. Every concept has: hook, format, platform, creator assigned, publish date.

### Tuesday-Thursday — Production
**Who**: Creator(s)
**Cadence**: Strategist checks in via Slack/PM tool twice daily
**Rules**:
- Creator shoots in batches (never 1 piece at a time)
- Strategist reviews rough cuts same day or next morning
- Feedback is specific and actionable ("the hook is weak because X" not "this doesn't feel right")
- External contributor briefs delivered by Tuesday for Thursday deliveries

### Friday — Creative Review + Publishing
**Who**: Strategist + Creator(s) + Creative Director (if enterprise tier)
**Duration**: 45-60 minutes
**Agenda**:
1. Review all content produced this week
2. Grade each piece: Ship / Fix / Kill
3. Schedule next week's publishing queue
4. Surface any creative patterns or breakthroughs
5. Document learnings in the shared playbook

**Output**: Next week's publishing queue locked. Underperforming concepts analyzed. Pattern log updated.

---

## Step 3: Monthly Operating Rhythm

### Monthly Creative Audit (First Monday of Month)
**Who**: All pod members + Creative Director + stakeholders
**Duration**: 90-120 minutes
**Agenda**:
1. Month-in-review: analytics deep dive
2. Content pillar performance — which pillars drove results?
3. Format performance — which formats are earning attention?
4. Creator performance — individual and external contributor scorecard
5. Competitor content review — what are they doing that's working?
6. Next month's strategic focus — any shifts in editorial direction?
7. Budget review — spend vs. output vs. results

**Output**: Monthly content report. Next month's editorial direction locked. Budget adjustments proposed.

### Monthly Cross-Pod Sync (If multi-pod, first Wednesday of month)
**Who**: All strategists + Creative Director
**Duration**: 60 minutes
**Agenda**:
1. Shared learnings across pods — what worked in Pod A that Pod B can adopt?
2. Resource sharing opportunities — editors, designers, locations
3. Cross-pod narrative alignment — are we telling a coherent brand story?
4. External creator performance across pods
5. Flywheel health check — any broken connections?

---

## Step 4: Meeting Architecture

Every meeting must have:
1. **Agenda** — shared 24 hours before
2. **Timer** — hard stop, no meeting runs over
3. **Decision log** — every decision documented in PM tool
4. **Action items** — assigned with owner + deadline
5. **No spectators** — if someone doesn't contribute, they don't attend

### Anti-Patterns to Prevent
- ❌ "Creative reviews" that become critique sessions with no clear standards
- ❌ Weekly meetings that drift into status updates instead of strategic decisions
- ❌ Production schedules that flex because "we'll figure it out"
- ❌ Monthly reviews that don't tie content to business outcomes

## Step 5: The 10-Asset Standard

Every pod targets 10 concepts minimum per week. Break down:

```
WEEKLY CONCEPT ALLOCATION (default):
├── Platform-native organic:    5 pieces
├── Ad concepts (paid-ready):   3 pieces
├── Founder/behind-scenes:      1 piece
└── External creator brief:     1 piece
                                ──────
                                10 concepts

ALLOCATION FLEX:
- Early stage: 7 organic / 3 ad
- Scaling: 5 organic / 5 ad
- Launch week: 3 organic / 7 ad
```

For each concept, the brief must include:
- Hook (first 3 seconds / first line)
- Format type
- Target platform
- CTA (if any)
- Reference/inspiration link
- Creator assigned
- Due date
