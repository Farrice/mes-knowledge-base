# Jay Sun Films — Video Craft (Observed)

Bio verbatim: "Real Estate Videographer | LA — Helping agents sell homes w/ clean video 🎥 Reels • Listings • Walkthroughs 📩 DM to book." 192 followers; his portfolio is effectively the Jen catalog. "Clean video" is accurate: the craft is exposure and movement discipline, not effects.

## Observed patterns (VERIFIED from frames)

1. **Agent-forward opens.** Every reel opens on the agent, full-body, centered, at the property exterior — lav mic on, architecture framing her (stone facade, porch lines). The agent is the brand; the house is the set.
2. **Exposure discipline.** Balanced window exposures indoors (view visible AND interior correctly lit), twilight exteriors with warm porch light against blue-hour sky, night interiors balancing warm lamps with cool downlights. This is the single clearest "hired a pro" signal vs. phone footage.
3. **Detail-first reveals.** Low-angle tilt-ups on ceiling beams and light fixtures; slow push-ins on vanities and built-ins; symmetrical compositions. Details before room wides — the walkthrough teases, then satisfies.
4. **Movement grammar.** Stabilized continuous motion (gimbal-grade), walking-and-talking tracking shots of the agent moving toward camera, including a night pool-deck tracking shot. Motion carries pace so cuts stay few.
5. **Three formats in rotation:** ~85s full walkthrough (day), ~84s twilight lifestyle showcase, ~20s kinetic hook reel (agent walking + big animated text).
6. **Blue-hour scheduling.** Twilight and night shoots are deliberate — that's a scheduling choice, not luck, and it's what makes $2M-lane listings read premium.
