## Genius Patterns

### Pattern: Seven-Law Decoding
Every spiritual text, every business plateau, every relationship pattern reads to him as "oh, that's the law of this and this combined — a permutation of the seven." Mentalism, Correspondence, Vibration, Polarity, Rhythm, Cause & Effect, Gender. Fifteen years in, this is his instant diagnostic layer for any situation.
**Execute**: Take the presenting problem and translate it out of story-language into law-language. Ask: which law is being violated, ignored, or ridden here? A father estranged for years reconciling the day after embodied surrender = Mentalism (his thoughts recast) + Correspondence (internal vibration shifted, external world re-projected) + Cause & Effect (new input, new output).
**Success Metric**: The person stops describing the problem in circumstance-language and can name the 1-3 laws generating it — and the intervention becomes obvious from the law itself.

### Pattern: Oblique Intention Engineering
A NASA-engineer Kabbalist gave him the physics metaphor he now teaches everywhere: light hitting a surface at an angle produces more energy than hitting it directly. Direct goals ("lose 10 pounds") trap you on the pendulum of the problem — every rep of the goal reminds you that you're 10 pounds overweight. A peripheral intention ("be more connected and mindful with food") delivers the outcome as a guaranteed side effect: eat slower → feel full sooner → eat less → lose the weight without ever thinking about weight.
**Execute**: For any target outcome, ask his master question: *"What is the thing I can focus on — in a story, in a relationship to that thing — that guarantees me this other thing I want, in a way that reveals who and what I am even more?"* Then set ONLY the peripheral intention.
**Success Metric**: The person never rehearses the deficit state again, and the original target arrives measured as a side effect.

### Pattern: Six-Word Elimination Protocol
"We are programmed to exist in a context of judgment." His cleanest hack: you cannot judge without six words — good, bad, right, wrong, positive, negative. Remove them from active vocabulary and judgment becomes mechanically impossible.
**Execute**: Ban the six words for 30 days. When anger, jealousy, or grief arises, it is no longer "bad" or "negative" — so there is space to actually feel it and meet it with love instead of managing it.
**Success Metric**: Emotions get felt within seconds of arising instead of suppressed and story-fied; self-talk audits show zero instances of the six words.

### Pattern: Love-Both-Poles Neutrality
Neutrality is not choosing zero. Choosing zero is avoidance, and the avoided poles keep manufacturing situations in your life. Real neutrality: 100% love fear AND 100% love courage — the two dissolve back into one, and situations requiring either simply stop showing up.
**Execute**: Name the pendulum (fear/courage, healing/wounding, success/failure). Check for fake-neutrality (suppressing both). Then deliberately embrace and appreciate each pole fully — what it gave you, what it protects, what it reveals.
**Success Metric**: The polarity stops recurring in circumstances; the person reports the theme "just doesn't come up anymore" rather than "I handle it better now."

### Pattern: Identity-Before-Effort Reprogramming
Seven years identified as a "hard worker" = 60-80 hour weeks and treating patients in his dreams. He switched identity to "problem solver" — hours dropped, but life became relentlessly problematic (a problem solver needs problems). The identity dictates the experience it must generate to stay true.
**Execute**: Diagnose the operating identity behind the workload or struggle ("What must be true about who I am for this pattern to make sense?"). Then choose an identity whose side effects are the life you want — his endpoint was "empty vessel," which out-produced a decade of striving in two years.
**Success Metric**: Behavior change happens without willpower because the new identity makes the old pattern incoherent.

### Pattern: 100% Trust of Knowings

Intuition doesn't speak in sentences — it arrives as half-second knowings, emotions, images, "glimpses of knowings." Objects and situations communicate in feelings, not words.

**Success Metric**: Latency between knowing and action approaches zero; the person builds an evidence log that trusting beat deliberating.

### Pattern: Vibration-First Causation

### Pattern: 0.1-Level Emotional Awareness
"The key is to be aware of the 0.1 level of anger or the 0.1 level of grief." Society trains repression from childhood ("stop crying"), so most adults only notice emotions at level 7+. He was "deeply intellectual simply so I don't feel all the pain" — intellect as anesthesia.
