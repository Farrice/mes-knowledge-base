# Jay Hiette — Coaching Positioning & Belief-Shifting Offers

Jay Hiette went from a burned-out marketing agency operator doing 80-hour weeks of cold outreach to ~$20K/month personal profit from an online coaching business run three days a week. His core inversion: strategy is not the bottleneck — messaging and positioning are. Marketing that only educates is "handing books to someone with blurred vision"; shift the belief first (the optometrist model) and the same content suddenly converts. The Reality Matrix operationalizes this in four moves: Dream Client → Vision Assets → Reality Shift → Deal Flow.

## Quick Reference

- **Reality Matrix (4 steps)**: 1) Dream Client, 2) Vision Assets, 3) Reality Shift, 4) Deal Flow. Foundations and message BEFORE ads and scaling.
- **One Niche Trinity**: You (values, story) + Mechanism (named process, visualized) + Outcome (the unique identity your clients achieve). Compress into an IEL statement for the bio.
- **Core claim formula**: "People think X, which causes Y, but the truth is Z" — anchored in a mechanism, never a random opinion.
- **The 4 belief shifts**: problems → symptoms · goals → priorities · dreams → milestones · education → belief shift.
- **Optometrist model**: correct the prescription (belief) before handing over content (books). Education teaches; belief shifts transform — and transformation makes you the only choice.
- **Symptom language**: describe the moment they can't ignore ("every time you walk in the kitchen, you overeat"), not the label ("emotional eating"). Symptoms are felt, not debated.
- **Deal flow**: profile-visit ads (~$10/day) grow a qualified audience → one VSSL (golden YouTube video) nurtures → email community → nano offer repairs trust → high-ticket ascension. Target 5-10x ROAS; attention compounds over months, never ad-to-book-a-call.
- **Belief-shift metric**: people repeating your wording back to you in DMs = the message is landing.
- **Idea sources**: life snapshots, calendar activities, conversations & media → captured via 5-minute daily "splurge" (no backspaces) → hook / story / value-lesson.
- **Proof point**: 800K-follower fitness coach couldn't convert (funny content, no clear message); Yip with a few hundred followers made EUR 8K in 3 weeks off clear positioning + unique mechanism. Followers don't convert — beliefs do.
