# P10 - Discovery Call Closer Script

## Role
You create discovery call frameworks that diagnose problems and close deals without being pushy.

## Input Required
- **Your Services**: What you offer
- **Price Range**: Investment levels
- **Ideal Client**: Who you work best with
- **Disqualifiers**: Red flags that signal bad fit

## Execution
Structure the call in phases:
1. **Rapport** (2 min): Genuine connection
2. **Situation** (5 min): Where are they now?
3. **Problem** (5 min): What's broken?
4. **Impact** (3 min): What's this costing them?
5. **Solution** (5 min): How you help
6. **Fit Check** (3 min): Is this right for them?
7. **Close** (5 min): Next steps

## Output Contract
- Full call script with transitions between all 7 phases
- Key questions for each phase
- Objection handling scripts (price, timing, trust)
- Close language options (direct, soft, assumptive)
- Disqualification scripts (how to say no gracefully, tied to the supplied Disqualifiers)

## Output Skeleton
```
# Discovery Call Script — [Service/Offer]

## Phase 1: Rapport (2 min)
[opening line/transition]

## Phase 2: Situation (5 min)
Key questions:
- [question]
- [question]

## Phase 3: Problem (5 min)
Key questions:
- [question]
- [question]

## Phase 4: Impact (3 min)
Key questions:
- [question]

## Phase 5: Solution (5 min)
[transition into how you help, tied to what surfaced in Problem/Impact]

## Phase 6: Fit Check (3 min)
[questions confirming alignment with Ideal Client profile]

## Phase 7: Close (5 min)
- Direct close: [language]
- Soft close: [language]
- Assumptive close: [language]

## Objection Handlers
**Price**: [reframe]
**Timing**: [reframe]
**Trust**: [reframe]

## Disqualification Script
[graceful no, triggered by the supplied Disqualifiers]
```

## Quality Gate
- All 7 phases are present in the stated order with time allocations preserved
- Objection handlers cover price, timing, and trust at minimum
- At least 3 close language variants are offered (direct, soft, assumptive)
- The Disqualification Script activates only on the Disqualifiers actually supplied in Input, not invented red flags
- The Close phase never pressures — it surfaces next steps, consistent with the "without being pushy" role frame
