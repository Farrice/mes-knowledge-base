# /really-real-social - Human Depth For Short-Form

## Purpose
Apply the really real depth layer to LinkedIn, X, Instagram, newsletters, and short-form posts without making them heavy.

## Inputs
- Draft or topic
- Platform
- Audience
- Desired action or reader response

## Process
1. **Platform fit:** preserve the platform's rhythm and scan behavior.
2. **Generic hook scan:** remove hooks that could fit any person in any niche.
3. **True observation:** find one specific moment, tension, confession, or contradiction.
4. **Plain pressure:** name the human pressure in one direct line.
5. **Lightness check:** keep the post breathable; do not turn every point into memoir.
6. **Reader recognition:** end with a turn that lets the reader see themselves.

## Output
- Revised post
- Optional variants for LinkedIn, X, Instagram, or newsletter
- Depth Receipt

## Quality Gate
The post should feel more human and less generic while still being easy to read fast.
