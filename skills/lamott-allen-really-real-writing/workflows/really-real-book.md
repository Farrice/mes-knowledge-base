# /really-real-book - Book, Memoir, Essay, And Scene Depth

## Purpose
Apply the craft to novels, memoir, essays, chapters, scenes, and book concepts.

## Inputs
- Scene, chapter, essay, memoir passage, or book concept
- Genre
- Character or narrator state
- Reader promise

## Process
1. **Book promise:** name why the reader would keep turning pages.
2. **Hard-stuff gate:** identify the emotional or existential pressure the draft avoids.
3. **Scene embodiment:** replace summary with one lived moment where possible.
4. **Music diagnostic:** repair melody, rhythm, or harmony.
5. **Silence and stakes:** add one implied beat and one clear consequence.
6. **Reader care:** orient the reader enough to follow the deeper turn.
7. **Ending or exit:** stop at an earned recognition, image, or question.

## Output
- Scene/chapter/essay diagnosis
- Revised passage or outline
- Next craft recommendation

## Quality Gate
The work must gain depth through story pressure and specificity, not author explanation.
