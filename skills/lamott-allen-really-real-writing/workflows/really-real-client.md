# /really-real-client - Trusted Advisory Prose

## Purpose
Deepen client-facing deliverables, strategy docs, audits, and personal brand assets without making them overwrought.

## Inputs
- Client-facing draft or brief
- Client/audience
- Decision the prose should support
- Evidence available

## Process
1. **Decision lock:** name what the client needs to understand or decide.
2. **Consultant-language cut:** remove vague optimization, alignment, unlock, and transformation phrases unless they are concretely defined.
3. **Hard truth with care:** name the real issue without shaming the client.
4. **Evidence anchor:** tie the claim to observed behavior, metric, quote, or example.
5. **Plain recommendation:** state the next move in direct language.
6. **Reader trust pass:** make the prose calm, specific, and easy to act on.

## Output
- Revised client-facing passage
- Advisory diagnosis
- Evidence gaps
- Remaining risk

## Quality Gate
The client should feel clearly advised, not performed at.
