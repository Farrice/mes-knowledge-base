# /really-real-attention - Source Material Builder

## Purpose
Build source material through attention, aliveness, porousness, and noticed detail before drafting.

## Inputs
- Topic, scene, client context, personal memory, brand idea, or content pillar
- Target medium

## Process
1. **Attention field:** list what can be noticed: objects, gestures, words, setting, tension, silence, contradiction.
2. **Human pressure:** name the fear, hope, want, shame, tradeoff, or ordinary ache underneath the topic.
3. **Specificity gather:** collect five concrete details, but keep only the one or two that imply the most.
4. **Reader recognition:** ask what a real reader would privately recognize in this.
5. **Plain sentence:** write the central observation in one direct sentence.
6. **Draft seed:** create 3 opening lines or scene starts.

## Output Schema
- Attention inventory
- One really real sentence
- Three draft seeds
- Recommended next workflow

## Quality Gate
The material must include observed specificity, not only concepts or claims.
