# /really-real-reader-trust - Reader Safety And Follow-Through

## Purpose
Make the reader feel safely held, emotionally honored, and willing to follow the piece to the end.

## Inputs
- Draft or section
- Reader type
- Reader skepticism, fear, or sensitivity

## Process
1. **Reader state:** name what the reader brings into the piece: hope, doubt, pain, urgency, skepticism, fatigue, or curiosity.
2. **Trust leaks:** find confusion, overclaiming, judgment, emotional pressure, missing context, or unsupported certainty.
3. **Reader care repairs:** add orientation, humility, plain stakes, or specific proof.
4. **Anti-manipulation pass:** cut lines that use pain without honoring the person carrying it.
5. **Continuation check:** ensure each paragraph earns the next one.
6. **Ending check:** leave the reader with earned clarity, not forced uplift.

## Output Schema
- Trust diagnosis
- Revised passage
- Reader Trust Score: 1-10
- Remaining risk

## Quality Gate
The reader should feel respected even if they disagree.
