# /really-real-rewrite - Truth And Trust Rewrite

## Purpose
Rewrite a draft so it carries more truth, intimacy, clarity, and reader trust while preserving the writer's core meaning.

## Inputs
- Draft
- Audience
- Medium
- Desired level of transformation: light, medium, deep

## Process
1. **Preservation lock:** state the meaning, voice traits, and constraints that must survive.
2. **Really real target:** identify the plain truth underneath the current polish.
3. **Cut first:** remove overproof, throat-clearing, status phrases, and fake closure.
4. **Replace abstractions:** choose direct words and vivid verbs where the prose floats.
5. **Add lived specificity:** add one concrete detail, scene, pressure, or felt moment.
6. **Reader trust pass:** clarify context and remove manipulation, judgment, or confusion.
7. **Read-aloud rhythm:** tighten sentences that cannot be spoken naturally.

## Output
- Rewritten draft
- Optional: before/after change notes
- Depth Receipt

## Quality Gate
The rewrite should sound like the writer became more honest and clearer, not like another writer took over.
