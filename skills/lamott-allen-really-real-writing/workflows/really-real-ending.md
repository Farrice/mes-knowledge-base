# /really-real-ending - Humane Ending Finder

## Purpose
Find the ending that feels inevitable, humane, and emotionally fair.

## Inputs
- Draft or outline
- Desired reader state
- Medium

## Process
1. **Arc check:** state where the reader began and where they now are.
2. **Earned recognition:** name the truth the piece has actually earned.
3. **False closure scan:** remove summary, moralizing, forced uplift, and clever last lines that dodge the truth.
4. **Restraint pass:** test ending one beat earlier.
5. **Plain final sentence:** create three ending options:
   - quiet
   - direct
   - image/detail
6. **Fairness check:** choose the ending that honors the reader's emotional journey.

## Output
- Ending diagnosis
- Three ending options
- Recommended ending with rationale

## Quality Gate
The ending should feel discovered, not pasted on.
