# /really-real-writing - Flagship Depth Pass

## Purpose
Turn any draft or writing goal toward truth, clarity, reader trust, and human resonance without making it heavy or pretentious.

## Inputs
- Draft or writing goal
- Audience
- Medium
- Desired behavior from the reader
- Optional: voice constraints, client constraints, conversion goal, or story context

## Process
1. **Function lock:** identify whether the run is audit, rewrite, source-building, stakes, ending, social, marketing, book, or client.
2. **Reader promise:** state what the reader should feel, know, or do by the end.
3. **False-note scan:** find snark, loftiness, generic polish, overproof, vagueness, and status language.
4. **Really real target:** name the human truth the draft is circling.
5. **Sentence care:** repair dull verbs, abstract words, dead rhythm, and next-sentence drop-off.
6. **Hard-stuff pass:** add one clear sentence or scene beat that touches the real pressure.
7. **Restraint pass:** cut the extra explanation and let one detail or pause carry implication.
8. **Reader trust pass:** make sure the reader is never confused, judged, tricked, or emotionally abandoned.

## Output
- Revised draft or directed writing plan
- Short Depth Receipt:
  - weak link
  - source mechanic used
  - behavior change expected
  - remaining risk

## Quality Gate
The result must feel more true, not merely more emotional. It must fit the medium and preserve the user's intent.
