# /really-real-silence - Restraint, Omission, And Subtext

## Purpose
Add subtext, restraint, omission, quiet, and reader-completed force without becoming vague.

## Inputs
- Draft, scene, paragraph, post, email, or client passage
- What the reader must still understand

## Process
1. **Clarity first:** confirm the reader can follow the basic meaning.
2. **Overproof scan:** mark repeated explanation, emotional labeling, apology, and defensive setup.
3. **Implied meaning:** choose what the reader can infer.
4. **Detail carrier:** let one concrete detail, pause, or line break carry the omitted feeling.
5. **Cut the label:** remove the sentence that tells the reader what to feel.
6. **Safety check:** restore context if the omission creates confusion.

## Output Schema
- Marked cuts
- Revised passage
- Note on what the silence now lets the reader feel

## Quality Gate
The omission must increase force. If the reader loses the thread, restore clarity.
