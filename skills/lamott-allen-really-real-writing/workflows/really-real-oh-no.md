# /really-real-oh-no - Stakes Moment Builder

## Purpose
Create the reader's "oh no" moment: stakes that make them stop, care, and continue.

## Inputs
- Draft, scene, campaign idea, offer, post, or chapter
- Current stakes
- Reader or character desire

## Process
1. **Desire lock:** name what someone wants.
2. **Threat lock:** name what could be lost, exposed, delayed, broken, or made impossible.
3. **Human scale:** translate abstract stakes into a concrete consequence.
4. **Delay the reveal:** build one beat of attention before naming the consequence.
5. **Plain line:** write the sentence that lets the reader feel the pressure.
6. **Care check:** make sure stakes do not exploit pain or overstate the problem.

## Output
- Stakes diagnosis
- "Oh no" moment
- Revised opening, scene beat, or copy section
- Remaining risk

## Quality Gate
The reader must care because something human is at risk, not because the prose shouts.
