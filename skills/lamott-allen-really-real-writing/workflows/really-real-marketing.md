# /really-real-marketing - Truthful Conversion Layer

## Purpose
Add human truth to ads, emails, landing pages, offers, and brand writing without weakening conversion.

## Inputs
- Draft copy or offer brief
- Audience
- Product/service
- Conversion goal
- Proof available

## Process
1. **Conversion owner:** preserve the main copy structure and conversion goal.
2. **Truth audit:** find fake empathy, inflated promise, vague transformation, or pressure tactics.
3. **Reader reality:** name the buyer's real frustration, fear, hope, or tradeoff in plain words.
4. **Bound the promise:** make the claim smaller if the proof cannot carry it.
5. **Concrete proof:** add specific proof or cut unsupported certainty.
6. **Trust repair:** make the offer feel easier to believe and easier to act on.
7. **Voice pass:** keep it persuasive, not pious.

## Output
- Revised marketing copy
- Trust/claim notes
- Remaining proof gaps

## Quality Gate
The copy must become more believable and still sell.
