# Anne Lamott + Neal Allen Really Real Writing

## Role
This skill is the dedicated "really real" depth layer from Anne Lamott and Neal Allen's conversation on writing toward compassion, clarity, and the real. It keeps `skills/lamott-craft` intact as the broader Lamott process base, while this package owns the narrower job of making a piece feel true, humane, and trusted.

## Core Thesis
Good writing earns the reader twice:

1. Neal Allen earns the next sentence through plain, vivid, economical craft.
2. Anne Lamott earns the reader's trust through compassion, hard truth, heart, and human contact.

The output should not sound like a technique stack. It should feel more honest, clearer, warmer, more specific, and less defended.

## Use When
Trigger this skill when the user asks for:
- "real," "true," "really real," "soulful," "deep," "human," "heart," or "voice"
- "less AI," "less generic," "less performative," "less fake," or "less polished"
- "make people feel it," "resonant," "relatable," "reader trust," or "emotional truth"
- "novel quality," "literary but not pretentious," "memoir," "scene depth," or "personal essay"
- social, marketing, client, or book writing that needs human depth without losing function

Do not trigger this layer automatically for fast tactical copy, simple line edits, formatting, research synthesis, or purely structural writing unless the user asks for depth or truth.

## Direct-Use Instructions
When invoked directly:

1. Identify the function owner:
   - rewrite, audit, source material, stakes, ending, social, marketing, book, or client.
2. Load `genius.md`, then the matching workflow file.
3. Ask only if missing context changes the output. Otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
4. Preserve the user's core meaning and voice.
5. Apply depth through craft moves, not visible labels.
6. End with a short "Depth Receipt" naming:
   - weak link found
   - source mechanic used
   - behavior change expected
   - remaining risk

## Required Quality Gates
- Reader Continuation: does each sentence create enough desire for the next?
- Compassion Target: has the draft moved away from snark, loftiness, or status performance?
- Plain Force: did abstract/fancy language become more direct where needed?
- Hard Thing: did the piece touch the real issue without melodrama?
- Reader Trust: is the reader held with clarity, context, and emotional fairness?
- Restraint: were overproof, apology, throat-clearing, and fake closure removed?
- Medium Fit: did the result stay appropriate for social, marketing, client, personal, or book use?

## Stacking Guide

| If You Need | Function Owner | Depth Slot |

| Cinematic structure or thematic residue | `eric-roth-writing-mastery` | Use this skill to keep resonance human rather than grandiose |
| Copy that converts without feeling manipulative | `copy-engine` or `proof-copy-engine` | Use this skill as the depth/truth pass after offer logic is sound |
| Social posts with more humanity | platform owner such as Lara Acosta or Diandra | Use `/really-real-social` after platform structure is clear |
| Book atomization or chapters | `book-atomize`, Roth, Connelly, or Lamott | Use `/really-real-book` to protect emotional truth and reader care |

## Composition Rule
Use one function owner per run. This package occupies the depth/truth slot. Do not crowd output with many named experts; integrate the move into the prose and show evidence through changed writing.
