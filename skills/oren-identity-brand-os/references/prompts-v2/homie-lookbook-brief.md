## Role & Activation

You are building Oren John's Homie Lookbook — casting real community over agency models. "Their lookbooks were what we now call the homie lookbook, where it's your friends and members of your community versus traditional models" [07:39]. The companion craft-level teardown sharpens the filter: "it's not just hey I'm going to use my friends in my photos versus employing a real model — it's more of, is there a story that we can tell underneath that" [companion 11:56–12:32]. This prompt builds the casting and shot brief through that filter, not a looks check.

## Input Required

- [COMMUNITY POOL]: the friend/collaborator/customer pool available for casting
- [CAMPAIGN CONCEPT]: the product or campaign the shoot is built around
- [EXISTING THEMES]: any recurring visual themes the brand already owns

## Execution Protocol

**Step 1 — Long-List the Cast.** Pull candidates from [COMMUNITY POOL] — real community, never a casting agency.

**Step 2 — Run the Story-Underneath Filter (companion source, ~11:56–12:32).** For every candidate, answer: what did this person actually do that's interesting? Model: **Brigade NYC** — a garment designed *because of* a specific friend's specific interesting activity, with the lookbook built around that story [companion 11:21–13:07]. Cut anyone who passes only on looks.

**Step 3 — Physical Membership Token (optional).** If budget allows, propose something marking belonging the way Stussy's International Tribe received personalized varsity jackets carrying their own names [VERIFIED] — converts a shoot into an ongoing identity relationship.

**Step 4 — Shot Logic.** Real locations, outdoor/in-motion bias. Apply [EXISTING THEMES] or the recurring-theme toolkit where it fits: urban action-and-people (place tells the story), vehicle-as-frame (shoot through/around, never centering the vehicle), or a non-serious recurring bit. Avoid posed studio setups and direct-eye-contact catalog framing.

**Step 5 — UGC-Look Production Notes.** Favor real, uncontrolled light over studio lighting; keep motion and candid framing over posed symmetry; avoid over-retouching. If a collaborator/partner is involved, source them from their actual scene — the way the Dries Van Noten collab drew from Flea's real world, not a stock celebrity booking [companion 10:46].

## Output Contract

- Cast long-list with story-underneath answer per person, cut list noted with reason
- Physical-token recommendation (yes/no, and what)
- Shot-logic brief: location, motion bias, recurring-theme device if any
- UGC-look production notes
- Explicit flag on whether any collaborator/celebrity cameo was sourced from a real scene

## Quality Gate

- Did every retained cast member pass the story-underneath filter, not just a looks check?
- Is the shot logic outdoor/in-motion biased, avoiding posed studio catalog framing?
- Was any collaborator cameo sourced from a real scene, not a stock celebrity booking?
- Does the brief avoid distance-based aspiration (polished, generic-model casting)?

## Creative Latitude

The story-underneath filter is the floor; the strongest lookbooks find a genuinely surprising story per cast member, not a rote "they're into hiking" answer. The recurring-theme device (Step 4) is where a brand's specific visual world lives — push for something no other brand in the category would land on.

## Deploy When

Any content, campaign, or lookbook casting decision where the brand wants peer-proximity belonging over aspirational distance.
