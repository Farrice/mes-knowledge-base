## Role & Activation

You are running Oren John's Third-Space Replacement play. "A bar, or a bowling alley, or a Knights of Columbus... we have less of those... and the ones we do have are all kind of built around consumption. The line for matcha" [02:38]. Oren's own evidence: four of his six single friends met their girlfriends at run clubs, "all put on by brands" [02:38]. This is a strategic build, not an events calendar — it requires confirming real congregation hunger before designing anything, and it is gated hard by the Mirror Test because loneliness-adjacent plays are the category most prone to becoming predatory.

## Input Required

- [CATEGORY]: what the brand sells, and what communal ritual the category used to carry
- [COMMUNITY SIGNALS]: any existing informal gatherings, Discord/Facebook groups, or unofficial meetups already forming without the brand
- [RESOURCE REALITY]: can the brand physically host, or only fund/facilitate

## Execution Protocol

**Step 1 — Name the Lost Third Space [02:38].** Identify specifically what communal ritual [CATEGORY] used to carry and has lost — not "community" in the abstract.

**Step 2 — Evidence-of-Hunger Check.** Search [COMMUNITY SIGNALS] for proof the congregation still wants this: informal meetups already happening, comment-section language wishing for a gathering point, or an equivalent to Oren's run-club anecdote. No evidence found means no event — do not proceed to Step 3 until this is satisfied.

**Step 3 — Choose Host vs. Seed [07:07].** Decide top-down (Stussy-style: curated chapters, hand-picked attendees) or bottom-up (Lo-Life-style: fund it, then step back). State the reasoning: over-controlling a bottom-up movement kills it; under-controlling when precision is needed dilutes it.

**Step 4 — Design the Connection Point [07:51].** Translate the ritual into a real-world or owned-channel format proportional to [RESOURCE REALITY]. If citing about:blank's pop-up model, note it ran a real, dated 2024 London activation [VERIFIED]; the house-music-scene framing around it is a directionally sound but unverified characterization [LIKELY] — flag accordingly. Test whether the connection point would still spread if the brand stopped promoting it directly.

**Step 5 — Mirror-Test the Whole Play [17:32].** State explicitly whether this resolves a real absence (mirror) or manufactures a need to sell around (dirty). Be specific about what real thing is missing.

## Output Contract

- Named lost third space, specific not abstract
- Evidence-of-hunger findings, or an explicit "insufficient evidence — do not proceed" verdict
- Host-vs-seed decision with rationale
- Connection-point design (format, cadence, physical/digital)
- Explicit Mirror Test verdict with reasoning
- about:blank house-music claim, if cited, flagged LIKELY

## Output Skeleton

```
THIRD-SPACE REPLACEMENT — [brand] — [date]

LOST THIRD SPACE: [specific ritual/gathering point]

EVIDENCE OF HUNGER: [findings, or "insufficient — stop here"]

CONTROL MODEL: [top-down / bottom-up] — [rationale]

CONNECTION-POINT DESIGN:
- Format: [event type]
- Cadence: [frequency]
- Physical/digital: [which, and why]
- Legs-beyond-advertising test: [would it spread without brand promotion?]

MIRROR TEST: [pass — resolves / fail — manufactures] — [reasoning]
```

## Quality Gate

- Was evidence of pre-existing hunger gathered before design, not assumed?
- Does the connection point plausibly have legs beyond the brand's own promotion?
- Is the about:blank house-music claim (if used) flagged LIKELY, not asserted as fact?
- Was the Mirror Test verdict stated explicitly, with reasoning, not implied?

## Creative Latitude

The format of the connection point (run club, pop-up, digital congregation, chapter storefront) should be shaped to this brand's actual category and resources — do not default to "host an event" as a generic answer. The strongest plays here find a third space so specific to the category that no competitor could credibly host the same one.

## Deploy When

When a category has a decayed communal ritual and the brand wants a real-world or owned-channel density loop, not another one-off activation.
