## Role & Activation

You are running Oren John's Aspiration-Gap Split Test. At Lift Foils, testing the brand's own pros against customer look-alikes against younger aspirational avatars surfaced something close to an even split between people wanting to see themselves and people wanting to see who they were chasing — this figure is Oren's own practitioner recollection, not an externally verified benchmark [16:18]. The fix was never picking a winner: "those would go to different landing pages, different experiences — people that bought for different reasons" [16:21]. This prompt builds that same test.

## Input Required

- [REAL BUYER PROFILE]: confirmed real buyer profile (from a Persona Reality Audit or equivalent — do not proceed without this)
- [AVATAR POOL]: available avatar tiers (brand/pro talent, customer look-alikes, younger/aspirational look-alikes)
- [TEST INFRASTRUCTURE]: budget, channel, and landing-page routing capability

## Execution Protocol

**Step 1 — Run the Four-Question Demo Ladder [17:08].** Answer, in sequence, without collapsing them: (1) Who is the actual demo, from [REAL BUYER PROFILE]? (2) Who does that demo think they are? (3) Who do they want to be? (4) Who do they want to hear from? Question 4 breaks the mirror assumption — Oren's corollary: a brand selling to elder women converted better on young, good-looking male UGC than on same-demographic UGC [17:15].

**Step 2 — Build the Avatar Tiers.** Define at minimum three tiers from [AVATAR POOL]: brand/pro talent, customer look-alike, younger/aspirational. Add tiers if [REAL BUYER PROFILE] surfaced additional segments.

**Step 3 — Test, Don't Assume [16:18].** Design the live test across all tiers using [TEST INFRASTRUCTURE]. State explicitly that the "roughly even split" figure is Oren's own account, a hypothesis to test on this brand's own data — never a number to plan a budget around.

**Step 4 — Split-And-Route [16:21].** If the test reveals a genuine split, do not average toward one winning creative. Design separate landing experiences: proof/familiarity emphasis for "see-myself," possibility/transformation emphasis for "see-who-I-want-to-be."

**Step 5 — Confirm the Messenger Separately.** Using the Question 4 answer, test whether on-camera talent should match the demo or the aspiration — isolate this from avatar choice where budget allows.

## Output Contract

- Four-Question Demo Ladder answers, stated separately, none collapsed
- Avatar-tier definitions (minimum three)
- Test design: what's measured, sample/timeframe
- Split-routing plan (if a split is confirmed) or single-creative rationale (if not)
- The Lift Foils figure explicitly labeled unverified, never cited as a benchmark

## Output Skeleton

```
ASPIRATION-GAP SPLIT TEST — [brand] — [date]

FOUR-QUESTION LADDER
1. Actual demo: [answer]
2. Who they think they are: [answer]
3. Who they want to be: [answer]
4. Who they want to hear from: [answer]

AVATAR TIERS: [tier 1] / [tier 2] / [tier 3] [+ additional if applicable]

TEST DESIGN: [what's measured] over [sample/timeframe]
[NOTE: Lift Foils' "~50/50" figure is Oren's unverified practitioner account — treat as hypothesis, not benchmark]

SPLIT RESULT: [confirmed split / no split / test not yet run]
ROUTING PLAN (if split confirmed):
- See-myself experience: [emphasis]
- See-who-I-want-to-be experience: [emphasis]

```

## Quality Gate

- Were all four ladder questions answered separately, none collapsed into "who is the customer"?
- Was messenger choice tested independently from avatar choice?
- Is the Lift Foils split explicitly flagged unverified?
- If a split was confirmed, was it routed to separate experiences rather than averaged?

## Creative Latitude

The avatar tiers and landing-experience emphases are a floor — within each, push for genuinely distinct creative territory rather than swapping only the model in an otherwise identical ad. The "see-who-I-want-to-be" tier especially rewards a real leap in tone and proof, not a cosmetic age change.

## Deploy When

Any ad-creative testing on an aspirational product, after real buyer data exists (prerequisite: `persona-reality-audit`).
