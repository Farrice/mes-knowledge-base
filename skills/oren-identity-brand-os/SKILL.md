# Oren — Identity Brand OS

The identity-first layer that answers "who am I as a public identity, and why would a stranger bond their self-concept to this brand?" before any archetype, content, or platform decision. It treats a product as a recognition token between lonely strangers first and a utility second, then builds that attachment on purpose: excavate the zero-point identity, diagnose the Five Identity Drivers, engineer true-fan density toward 1,000 true fans, and scale any creator or founder toward their mission. Everything already owned by the Oren arsenal (archetype selection, repositioning, team scaling, luxury positioning, taste QC) routes out. This OS is thin orchestration plus the net-new zero-point, cult-following, and generalized-intake gaps.

**Front door:** `/identity-brand-os` runs founder intake, stage diagnosis (zero / launching / scaling), and a routed mission plan.

**Load [genius.md](genius.md) before any workflow.** It carries the 13 patterns, the exemplars, the rubric, and the mirror-test ethics gate.

## Disambiguation (binding)

The **Five Identity Drivers** in this OS (Belonging, Better-than-the-others, Rebel, Standout, Subculture-pride) are consumer-side motives for self-association. They are **never called "archetypes."** The word "archetypes" belongs to `oren-brand-archetypes`, whose five creator archetypes (Oracle, Performer, World Builder, Catalyst, Helper) answer a different question: what functional content role a brand plays as a creator. The two frameworks are complementary and disjoint. A brand can be a Rebel (Identity Driver) executed through the Performer archetype (content role). Never merge the two five-part lists.

## Quick Reference

**The Five Identity Drivers** (why a consumer bonds self to brand; pick a primary plus secondary per segment, never a pile):
- **Belonging:** I want to fit in with people like me (sneakers).
- **Better-than-the-others:** subtle superiority, more tasteful than my friend group (The Row).
- **Rebel:** I don't play by your rules (Liquid Death).
- **Standout / flare:** the I'm-special call, it stands out on a feed.
- **Subculture-pride:** if you know, you know (geography, race, hobbies, gender).

**The four testing questions** (the demo ladder): who is the actual demo, then who does that demo think they are, then who do they want to be, then who do they want to hear from. The fourth breaks the mirror assumption: the messenger they want is often not their own demographic.

**The mirror test (ship gate):** a play that shows the buyer a mirror of something that resolves a real weak point ships; a play that feels dirty, or manufactures a weak point it then sells against, is rejected outright.
