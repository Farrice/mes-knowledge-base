# Identity Weak-Point Map

"If people have a weak point in their internal identity, how can you help resolve it?" [14:31]. Lift Foils didn't sell aging athletes a watercraft — it sold them "that feeling of flight, of exhilaration... in this really modern way" [15:14], and Drew Brees on Ellen worked "because of what it solved," not celebrity gloss [15:09]. This workflow is distinct from every social-signaling driver in the OS: it maps identity that's private, not performed, and it carries the strictest dignity rule in the whole system.

## Pre-Flight Gate

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: cite genius.md **Pattern 9 — Identity Beyond Affiliation** and **Signature Move 6 — Reframe-The-Wound-As-A-Feeling**. Before naming any weak point, confirm it is a real, resolvable feeling the product can actually restore — not a manufactured insecurity invented to create demand. If the product doesn't genuinely resolve anything, this workflow does not apply; route back to `identity-driver-diagnostic` for a social-signal driver instead.

## Input Required

- The product's actual capability (what it restores, repairs, or makes possible again)
- The candidate audience segment believed to carry this internal weak point
- Any existing advocacy assets (testimonials, influencer/celebrity partners) being considered

## Execution Steps

**Step 1 — Name the Private Weak Point [14:31].** What internal identity gap does this audience carry that isn't about how they're seen by others? Lift Foils' example: aging athletes who still feel 22 internally but can't perform like it. Be specific to a feeling, not a demographic label — "aging" is not specific enough; "still thinks of themselves as an athlete but the body has changed" is.

**Step 2 — Map Features to Resolutions.** For every product feature or capability, ask: what identity gap does this specifically close? Build a table — feature on one side, the internal feeling it restores on the other. Reject any feature that only maps to a functional benefit with no identity resolution; this workflow is about the feeling, not the spec sheet.

**Step 3 — Reframe the Wound as a Feeling [15:14].** Rewrite every weak-point statement so it never names a deficiency. Never: "you're getting older." Always: "you can still have that feeling of [X]." This is Signature Move 6 and it is non-negotiable — a deficiency-framed weak point fails the dignity rubric regardless of how accurate it is.

**Step 4 — Choose Advocacy That Proves the Resolution [15:09].** If using a testimonial, athlete, or celebrity, the proof has to be *what it solved*, not *who's holding it*. Brees-on-Ellen worked because the audience watched him regain competence and play, not because he's famous. Screen any candidate advocate against this: does the footage show the resolution happening, or just an endorsement?

**Step 5 — Mirror-Test the Map [17:32].** Run Pattern 13 explicitly: does this play resolve a weak point the audience already carries (mirror), or does it manufacture insecurity to sell against (dirty)? This is the single hardest gate in the OS because weak-point marketing is the category most prone to becoming exploitative. If any doubt remains, do not ship the play as drafted — narrow the claim until it's clean.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Format | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Physical product (sport/fitness/wellness) | Direct feature-to-feeling map, as written |
| Service / coaching offer | Map program milestones to identity restoration points instead of product features |
| B2B / professional tool | Weak point = professional competence or relevance anxiety; advocacy = peer case study, not celebrity |
| Content / education brand | Weak point = knowledge or confidence gap; resolution = demonstrated competence gained, shown not told |

## Output Requirements

- Named weak point, stated as a feeling never a deficiency
- Feature-to-resolution mapping table
- Reframed language for every weak-point claim in the deliverable
- Advocacy screening notes (what-it-solved test, pass/fail per candidate)
- Explicit Mirror Test verdict with reasoning

## Quality Gate

> Cite genius.md **Expert-Specific Quality Rubric** row **Weak-point dignity** (score 10 = face-saving reframe, "still have that feeling") and the **Mirror test** row (a play that fails this is rejected outright regardless of the rest — the minimum bar in this OS).
- Is every weak-point statement framed as a feeling still available, never a deficiency?
- Does every feature in the map trace to a real identity resolution, not just a spec?
- Was the advocacy candidate screened for "what it solved" over "who's holding it"?
