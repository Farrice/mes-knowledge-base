# /identity-brand-os — Identity Brand OS (Front Door)

Diagnose ANY founder or creator's raw material, place them on the zero/launching/scaling ladder, and route them into the right workflow — inside this skill or across the Oren arsenal. This is the intake, not the build; it produces a mission plan, not a finished identity.

## Pre-Flight Gate (genius.md decision framework)

- Is this a NEW identity (no public presence yet) or an EXISTING brand needing diagnosis/repair? Both enter here — the intake questions are the same, the stage answer diverges.
- Does the founder already have an archetype selected (via `oren-brand-archetypes`) or content system running? If yes, this workflow still runs first — per vision.md's binding sequencing, identity work precedes archetype/content work, even retroactively (flag the sequencing gap rather than skip it).

## Execution

1. **Raw Material Inventory.** Collect five inputs, refusing to proceed on vague answers:
   - **Origin** — birthplace, formative displacement, family trade, what shaped them before the business existed
   - **Place** — the geography/scene they physically inhabit now
   - **Obsessions** — what they can't stop consuming or talking about outside the work
   - **Credibility assets** — track record, results, receipts (not aspirational claims)
   - **Existing audience** — size, platform, and whether the relationship is direct (owned) or borrowed (rented)
   This is the **Start-With-What-You-Have** signature move applied at intake — "location/origin as identity seed" [09:30]: an outdoors brand can build a unique identity off NW-vs-SW geography alone; a racing company off Americana-vs-British-vs-Chinese heritage. Never invent a seed the founder doesn't already own.

2. **Stage Diagnosis.** Score the inventory against three stages:
   - **Zero** — no defined public identity, no meaningful owned audience. Raw material exists but unexcavated.
   - **Launching** — some content history or audience exists, but the identity is borrowed, generic, or was never deliberately chosen.
   - **Scaling** — real traction and a working identity; the bottleneck is team, positioning sophistication, or premium repositioning, not identity formation.
   State the stage explicitly with the evidence that produced it — never leave it implied.

3. **Five Identity Drivers Pre-Read.** Do a fast gut-check across **the Five Identity Drivers** (Belonging / Better-than-the-others / Rebel / Standout / Subculture-pride) using the raw-material inventory — this is a hypothesis, not the diagnosis; the full per-segment scoring happens in `identity-driver-diagnostic`. Flag a likely primary candidate and move on.

4. **Routed Mission Plan.** Route by stage:
   - **Zero** → `identity-zero-point` (excavate the identity seed) → `identity-driver-diagnostic` (confirm the driver) → `true-fan-density-engine` (build the tribe mechanics)
   - **Launching** → `identity-driver-diagnostic` first (existing identity is often misaligned to the real driver) → Tier 2 workflows in this skill (signature-design, persona-reality, weak-point mapping — see SKILL.md for status) once published
   - **Scaling** → route OUT per arsenal-map §5: archetype/content roadmap → `oren-brand-archetypes`; team/pod scaling → `oren-content-team-architecture`; premium repositioning → `oren-luxury-psychology`; taste QC → `oren-taste-development`; full brand-doc system → `/build-bos`
   Never run a Tier 2/3 workflow before the founder has a locked identity seed and a confirmed primary driver — that sequencing violation is the single most common way this OS gets misused.

Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/identity-brand-os.md — honor its Output Contract.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Founder Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Solo creator, zero audience | Full intake; expect thin credibility-asset column, lean harder on origin/obsessions |
| Existing brand, borrowed identity | Intake still runs; flag which inputs were reverse-engineered from a competitor rather than owned |
| Client engagement (agency use) | Intake becomes the discovery-call script; document gaps as `[NEEDS CLIENT]`, never invent |
| High-credibility expert, no content history | Credibility-asset column is strong — route faster to `identity-driver-diagnostic`, zero-point excavation still mandatory |

## Output Requirements

Mission Plan: Raw Material Inventory table, Stage Diagnosis with evidence, Five Identity Drivers pre-read (candidate primary, flagged as hypothesis), routed next-workflow sequence with rationale per hop.

## Quality Gate

- Real-buyer grounding: inventory answers are the founder's actual material, never invented seeds
- Driver focus: pre-read names a candidate driver with a receipt, not a vague label
- Stage diagnosis carries explicit evidence, not a guess
- Routing sequence never skips zero-point/driver-diagnostic ahead of archetype/content decisions for a Zero or Launching founder
