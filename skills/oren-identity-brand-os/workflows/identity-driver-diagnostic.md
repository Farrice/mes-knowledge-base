# /identity-driver-diagnostic — Five Identity Drivers Diagnostic

Diagnose why a buyer self-associates with a brand — a motive diagnostic, never a demographic segmentation. A single person flips between drivers per purchase; this workflow finds the primary + secondary per segment, not a fixed customer type.

## Pre-Flight Gate (genius.md decision framework)

- Does an Identity Seed Document exist (from `identity-zero-point`) or equivalent brand-identity material? If the founder is at zero and hasn't run zero-point yet, recommend it first — driver diagnosis on an unexcavated identity produces guesses, not receipts.
- **Naming discipline, binding:** these are the Five Identity Drivers — NEVER "archetypes." That term is owned by `oren-brand-archetypes`' five CREATOR archetypes (Oracle/Performer/World Builder/Catalyst/Helper), which answer a completely different question (how the founder produces content, not why the buyer self-associates). Keep the two vocabularies disjoint in every output.
- Farrice fast-path: Authority Flywheel's Invisible Expert ICP likely skews **Subculture-pride** (practitioner-not-guru) primary and **Better-than-the-others** (taste/rigor versus the guru economy) secondary — treat as a hypothesis to confirm with Step 2 receipts, not a shortcut past the diagnostic. `FARRICE-MASTER-CONTEXT.md` §5.6–5.7 (the Practitioner Test, the named Forever-Learning Industrial Complex enemy) is real receipt material.

## Execution

1. **Segment mapping.** List every distinct buyer segment for the brand — a segment is a purchase context or motive cluster, not a demographic bucket. The same person can occupy multiple segments across different purchases.

2. **Per-segment driver scoring.** For each segment, score across all five drivers using the verbatim anchors as the diagnostic questions:
   - **Belonging** [12:41] — "I want to fit in with people like me"
   - **Better-than-the-others** [13:13] — subtle superiority, "a bag that not everyone would know"
   - **Rebel** [13:32] — "I don't play by your rules"
   - **Standout / flare** [13:47] — "I know I can go on a podcast and it'll stand out on a feed"
   - **Subculture-pride** [14:03] — "if you know, you know"
   Ask, per segment: what is this purchase *for*, socially or privately? Score plausibility per driver rather than assuming one.

3. **Primary + secondary selection.** Per the failure mode Oren names explicitly — "naming three at once" — pick exactly one primary and one secondary per segment. No pile of drivers, no unranked list.

4. **Receipt requirement.** Apply **Receipt-Or-It-Didn't-Happen**: every driver claim needs a named-brand or named-behavior receipt, never an abstract label. "This segment skews Belonging" is not a receipt; "this segment buys the cohort badge because it gets them into the private Discord where their people are" is.

5. **Driver → creative implications map.** Translate the primary/secondary pair into concrete direction:
   - Belonging → homie-lookbook casting, community-proof content
   - Better-than-the-others → subtlety/color-ownership signature design, insider register, "if you know" copy
   - Rebel → counter-convention hooks, anti-establishment framing
   - Standout / flare → bold visual signature, "stands out on a feed" creative
   - Subculture-pride → geography/hobby/identity-marker-specific language and imagery

6. **STP-trap adversarial check.** Explicitly answer: "is this just segmentation repackaged?" The answer must demonstrate per-purchase motive logic — the same buyer choosing Rebel today and Belonging tomorrow — not a fixed demographic bucket. If the output reads like an STP grid, redo Step 2.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Deliverable | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Ad creative | Primary driver leads the hook; secondary drives the CTA or proof beat |
| Social/organic content | Alternate primary/secondary across a content calendar rather than forcing both into one piece |
| Product packaging / signature design | Feeds directly into the sophistication axis (logo/iconography for younger-skewing Belonging or Standout drivers; color/pattern for Better-than-the-others or Subculture-pride) |
| Email / owned media | Driver determines "show a life" content style — aspirational-lookbook for Standout, insider-register for Better-than-the-others |

## Output Requirements

Driver Diagnostic Report: segment table (segment, primary driver + receipt, secondary driver + receipt), creative-implications map per segment, explicit STP-trap self-check verdict.

## Quality Gate

- Driver focus: primary + secondary named per segment with per-purchase motive logic, not vague single-driver labeling
- Receipt density: every driver claim pinned to a named brand or named behavior
- Naming discipline: zero instances of "archetype" used for the Five Identity Drivers
- STP-trap check present and answered, not skipped
