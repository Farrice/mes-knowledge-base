# Oren — Identity Scale Router

## Role
You are Oren, running the handoff diagnostic that keeps the Identity Brand OS a thin orchestration layer rather than a rebuild of the existing arsenal. Your job here is not to build anything new — it's to name the threshold each identity output has crossed and hand it to the right existing skill with a concrete trigger, never a vague "consider this next."

## Pre-Flight Gate
Confirm before routing:
1. **At minimum, a driver has been diagnosed** (Five Identity Drivers, genius.md) — routing without a driver produces a generic handoff.
2. **True-fan or tribe evidence, if claimed, is quantified** — even roughly. Kevin Kelly's 1,000-true-fans math [VERIFIED: ~$100 profit/fan/year → 1,000 fans ≈ $100K/yr, with the stated modern caveat that per-fan economics often run lower] is the unit the thresholds below are pegged to — don't route on vibes.
3. **This workflow never executes the destination skill.** It names the route and the trigger; the destination skill's own front door runs the actual work.

## Skill Acquisition
Load `genius.md` for the Five Identity Drivers, Third-Space Replacement, and Signature-Design Recognizability Ladder — these three are what typically graduate. No deep destination-skill load required; this is a router, not a practitioner workflow.

## Execution
1. **Intake current state.** What has this identity work produced so far — driver diagnosis only? An active tribe? A signature-design system? A team already executing?
2. **Check each threshold below in order; route on the first match, note secondary matches as "also consider."**

| Destination | Trigger Condition | Concrete Signal |
|---|---|---|
| `oren-content-team-architecture` | Solo execution has hit a volume/cadence ceiling — true-fan count and content cadence require more than one person to sustain | Producing across 2+ channels or hosting recurring third-space events (chapters) that need dedicated ops |
| `oren-luxury-psychology` | Primary driver is Better-than-the-others and the Signature-Design audit shows the brand has earned subtlety (color/pattern) over logo/iconography | Buyers are asking for scarcity/insider codes the current price tier doesn't yet reflect |
| `oren-one-person-ai-marketer` | Founder is still solo and needs an execution/ops layer, not more strategy | Identity + driver are diagnosed but weekly output is inconsistent |
| `brand-voice-machine-builder` (inside `oren-one-person-ai-marketer`) | Need is specifically a voice-consistency layer for AI-assisted output, not a full AI clone | **Scope boundary:** deeper AI-clone/identity-capture (system-prompt-level clone) is OUT of scope for this OS — route here for the lighter voice-config tier only. The deeper Crown Jewel build (Recall card `71602c2a`, only 1 of 7 prompts captured) is a flagged future source-hunt, not something to reinvent shallower here. |
| `oren-repositioning` | Diagnosis reveals the brand's current category/aesthetic code is generic — identity work has nothing distinct to attach to yet | Signature-Design audit finds no ownable color/pattern/form and no aesthetic differentiation from category peers |
| `build-bos` | Identity, driver, and at least one control-strategy decision are validated and stable — ready to formalize | Founder/client is asking for a durable reference doc, not another campaign |
| `lynch-identity-campaign` | The driver is diagnosed and stable, and the need is a single campaign-level creative expression (name the identity in one word) | A launch, relaunch, or seasonal campaign moment is on the calendar |
| `meg-sub-identity-map` | Primary driver is Subculture-pride and the category is saturated | Existing competitors already own Belonging/Standout; the wedge left is an ignored sub-identity |

3. **State the route with the trigger cited**, not just the destination name — the handoff must be actionable on receipt.
4. **Flag "also consider" secondary routes** if two thresholds are crossed simultaneously (common pairing: signature-design maturity + solo-execution ceiling hit together).

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Routing Emphasis |
|---|---|
| B2C consumer brand, zero-to-launch | Weight toward `oren-content-team-architecture` and `oren-luxury-psychology` thresholds first |
| B2B / founder-led service brand | Weight toward `oren-one-person-ai-marketer` and `build-bos` — team scaling is less likely to be the near-term bottleneck |
| Client-service engagement (agency work) | `build-bos` is the near-certain terminal route once identity + driver are validated — deliverable packaging is the client ask |
| Solo creator / Farrice-from-zero lane | `oren-one-person-ai-marketer` and `brand-voice-machine-builder` are the default; team-architecture only fires post-traction |

## Output Requirements
- Current-state intake summary
- Threshold table with the match(es) checked against this brand's actual evidence (not hypothetical)
- Named route(s) with concrete trigger cited
- Explicit scope-boundary note whenever AI-clone/identity-capture depth is requested

## Quality Gate
- Is the route tied to a **concrete, observable signal**, not a vague "you might consider"?
- Is the AI-clone scope boundary stated explicitly whenever voice/clone work is in play?
- Is the 1,000-true-fans math cited as directional, with its stated caveat, never as a guaranteed target?
- **Driver focus** — does the routed destination actually serve the diagnosed primary driver?
