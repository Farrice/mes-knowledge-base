## Fidelity assessment

**Overall: HIGH.** Three primary sources totalling ~2.5 hours of him speaking, one of them a stage talk
with readable slides, one an unrehearsed hour-long working session. Every named framework in this skill —
Image Elements, Simple Prompt Formula, Keep/Change, the six-slot system prompt, the frozen backbone, the
feeder image, the scale ref, the null-prompt sweep, the solo→stack ladder — is his, in his words, and is
quoted with a timestamp.

**Thin spots, honestly marked:**

1. **The photorealism cheat sheet itself is not in this skill.** The LinkedIn post exists; its contents are
   an image behind a login. What IS here is the doctrine and the taxonomy he teaches on camera
   (nine Image Elements, the formula, photography terminology as the photorealism trigger, two-to-three
   words per description). No token list has been invented to fill the gap. If the PDF is later obtained,
   `photorealism-language.md` is the file it extends.
2. **Style codes vs. moodboards.** He treats `sref` codes, `--p` profile codes and moodboards as three
   handles on the same problem (reusable style direction without retyping). His deepest recorded material
   is on **moodboards**; his `sref`/style-code material is mostly LinkedIn posts whose bodies are images.
   `style-code-library.md` is therefore built on the operation he demonstrates — build, characterize,
   name, weight, retrieve — and not on a claimed taxonomy of code types.
3. **"Moodboard sweep" is my name for his operation, not his term.** He never calls it that. What he
   demonstrates and narrates is the operation: run the same prompt against global-profile-only, then each
   board solo, then stacked one at a time, then diff. The name is a label on real behaviour; the ledger
   says so out loud so nobody later mistakes the label for a quote.
4. **All ROI figures are self-reported** and are presented as such wherever they appear.
5. **Tool-era drift — handled structurally, not by disclaimer.** [MJM] is 2024 Midjourney v6-era (Discord
   `/describe`, alpha site pre-launch, InsightFace, Magnific); [MOOD] is 2025 v7-era (moodboards, profile
   codes); [CONFIG] is 2026 (Figma Weave node graphs, Nano Banana). Rather than sprinkling caveats, every
   era-bound mechanic from all three has been **moved out of the teaching body into
   `genius.md` Appendix A**, tabulated by era with its date, each row paired with the durable principle it
   implemented. The main body of `genius.md`, the SKILL, and all four execution prompts teach only what
   survives a model swap. This is his own instruction turned on his own corpus: *"screw the models… if you
   build structured systems, you can just swap tools in"* [CONFIG @ 20:32, 2026].

   **Consequence for anyone extending this skill:** if a new Rory Flynn source arrives, sort it first.
   Doctrine goes in the body. Buttons go in Appendix A with a date. Never the reverse.
