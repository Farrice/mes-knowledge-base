# Chase Hughes — Context Engineering

The operating layer for the one inversion underneath all of Chase Hughes's work: **stop engineering outcomes — engineer the context where the outcome is inevitable.** Behavior is downstream of permission, permission of context/category, context of perception (the **PCP** formula). The amateur reaches for a script or a close; if you are good, what you engineer are *conditions*, and you build the perfect recipient first so the behavior falls out for free and feels self-chosen. The planning verb is always **"what is upstream of the thing I want?"** and its sharp form **"what context makes the behavior I want automatic?"**

This skill turns that inversion into a deployable system — for marketing, content, offers, leadership, coaching, self-change, and cognitive defense.

**Two modes.** *Design* (`/ce-design`, `/ce-pcp`, …) produces the context spec and hands off. *Production* (`/ce-write`, `/ce-offer`) fuses the psychology with the craft experts and produces **finished, publishable work** across seven verticals — social, content, media, storytelling, marketing, copywriting, ghostwriting — plus rebuilds weak offers. Both pass the deterministic ethics gate, so finished output is also defensible.

## When to Use

- Designing an offer / funnel / launch / onboarding where the action should feel self-chosen, not pushed
- A direct ask would trigger resistance and the real lever is the context, not the copy
- "Agreed but didn't act" responses (the outcome was pushed; the recipient was never built)
- Building genuine on-camera / pitch / leadership presence at the cause level
- Reading a person, transcript, or pitch for stress and change (not "lie detection")
- Surfacing a real objection or hidden truth ethically (sales discovery, coaching)
- Defending yourself / a team / kids against the FEAR loop, fractionation, and engineered division
- Content that needs the emotional substrate (shame, never-being-seen, emotional debt) — memoir-grade

## When NOT to Use This Skill

- ✅ Helping people reach conclusions and take actions that genuinely serve them
- ✅ Designing contexts where the outcome stands on its own merits, stripped of the engineered receptivity
- ✅ Reducing an audience's destabilization and supplying genuine clarity
- ✅ Defensive cognitive education (self, kids, teams)
- ❌ Manufacturing chaos/fear/scarcity you then sell the cure to (the ethics gate BLOCKS this)
- ❌ Running the SMRP honesty protocol on someone who cannot freely exit (employee, subordinate, partner mid-conflict) — coercive regardless of intent (the ethics gate flags this for REVIEW)
- ❌ Engineering conclusions that contradict observable reality

## Quality Bar

Hughes-grade context-engineering passes one test: **the target performs the behavior and experiences it as their own choice, because the context made it the obvious move — and you could defend the full design if they saw it.** If the design only works by hiding what it's doing, or by manufacturing a problem to sell the solution, it fails the Defense/Ethics Gate. The defense is the receipt. See `genius.md` for the full rubric + the offense-without-defense VETO.
