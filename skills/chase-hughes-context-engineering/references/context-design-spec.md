# Context-Design Spec — The Operating-Layer Output Contract + Ethics Gate

This is the contract that turns Chase Hughes's PCP spine into a repeatable artifact. Everything upstream of it (the named frameworks, the force-library, the behavioral-reading layer) exists to be *compiled* into one document: a **Context-Design Spec**. The persona designs the context; the spec is what it ships; the Defense/Ethics Gate is the blocking stage every spec passes through before it leaves the building.

**Hughes's one move, restated as a build instruction:** stop engineering the outcome, engineer the conditions upstream of it, and the outcome becomes an automatic, self-chosen byproduct. The planning verb is always **"What is upstream of the thing I want?"** and its sharper form **"What context makes the behavior I want automatic?"** The spec is the place you answer those two questions on paper, then hand the answer to a production expert who writes *into* the designed context instead of pushing the outcome directly.

> Milgram is the proof the whole contract is built on. Hughes's stated figures: *"people will shock strangers what they think is to death in about 47 minutes. At a 70% success rate… they didn't have a script… It's just a dude in a lab coat. And all they did in the Milgram experiment is engineer the conditions that make it okay."* **Hughes's stated figures (canonical Milgram is ~65% obedience; there is no standard 47-minute figure).** The lab coat supplied Permission, the manufactured situation supplied Context, and no script was needed. A Context-Design Spec is the deliberate, ethical, logged version of that lab coat.

## 1. The INPUT → OUTPUT Contract

A spec is cheap to request and expensive to fake. The input is three fields; the output is the eight-section document below.

**INPUT (three fields, all required):**

| Field | What it is | Failure mode if missing |
|---|---|---|
| **Desired end-state** | The one behavior you want to become automatic (one sentence, a verb the target performs) | Vague "more engagement" → no recipient to build, no upstream to trace |
| **Target** | Who the recipient is — their current perception, current category, their installed source-code if known | You design for a generic human and the resonance is flat |
| **Channel** | Where this runs — landing page, email sequence, video, room, 1:1, feed | You can't grade followability or hesitation without a delivery surface |

**OUTPUT:** a Context-Design Spec — eight sections, in order, each section a real design decision and not a description. A spec that *describes* the target instead of *deciding* the context shift is a failed spec. The test: hand the spec to a production expert who has never met the client and they can write the asset without asking you a single question.

**The contract's one inviolable rule:** the spec never contains the ask phrased as a push. The ask is *deferred to* a touchpoint (Section 5) and arrives after the recipient is built, so it lands as the recipient's own conclusion. If the spec's headline move is "say the thing more persuasively," it has not understood Hughes. *"if you're good, what you engineer are conditions. And if I can engineer the right conditions, I can get you to do anything… make the person the perfect recipient for what I need to give them."*

## 2. THE CONTEXT-DESIGN SPEC FORMAT — 8 Sections

Copy this skeleton verbatim. Fill every field. An empty field is a decision you skipped, not a field that didn't apply.

### Section 1 — Desired End-State (one sentence)
The single behavior, phrased as a verb the target performs, not an outcome you receive. **"Reader books the discovery call"** not **"more bookings."** One sentence. If it needs two, you have two end-states and you owe two specs. This sentence is the only thing Section 7's followability notes and Section 6's outcome-on-merits test are allowed to reference — it is the anchor the whole document is graded against.

### Section 2 — Condition Chain (upstream)
Trace backward from the end-state through the chain of conditions that make it automatic. Use the planning verb at each rung: *"What is upstream of this?"* Stop when you hit a condition you can actually build through the channel.

```
END-STATE: [the verb]
  ↑ upstream: what permission must already be live for that verb to feel obvious?
  ↑ upstream: what category/context makes that permission make sense?
  ↑ upstream: what perception shift puts them in that category?
  ↑ upstream: what state must they be in to accept that perception shift?
  ↑ upstream: what installed source-code (childhood app→source-code) does that state ride on, if known?
```

This is the **"Upstream Engine"** — *a synthesis coinage, not a name Hughes uttered.* Hughes's actual contrast is verbatim: *"if you're good, what you engineer are conditions."* The chain is the operational form of that contrast. Build the lowest rung you can reach first; the higher rungs fall out for free. **The absence of an early ask in the chain is correct, not weak** — the master-operator signature is how long you can defer the ask while the chain installs.

### Section 3 — Ambient Force-Map (what is already acting)
Before you add a single force, map the forces already in the room. The target is never a blank slate; they arrive mid-fractionation, mid-feed, pre-categorized by something. Name each ambient force that is *already* shaping the recipient, because your design either rides it, fights it, or accidentally amplifies it.

Check each, mark PRESENT / ABSENT / UNKNOWN, and note the direction it's pushing:

- **FEAR loop** (Focus → Emotion → Agitation → Repetition). Is the channel itself a fractionation surface (a feed, a high-pressure sales floor)? *"focus, emotion, agitation, and repetition… That process creates a blank slate in people… that is exactly what social media is using."*
- **Fractionation** (Erickson). Is the target arriving wrung-out from up-down cycling elsewhere — more GABA, higher theta, pliable? *"there's no such thing as depth in hypnosis. What they essentially mean is you'll have more GABA… and a higher degree of theta wave brain state."* Note: the claim that fractionation "works through a screen with no hypnotist present" is a **synthesis inference at the mechanism layer (LIKELY, not Hughes-verbatim)** — Hughes implies it via the feed analogy but does not state the chemical-therefore-no-hypnotist logic explicitly.
- **Engineered division / prepackaged enemy.** Has the target already been handed an enemy and told how to feel? *"when a population is destabilized and something clear and logical is presented… you're 10 times more likely to accept it… humans do not ever follow the best leader… They follow the most followable."* (Hughes hedged the source: *"I think it's called Unrestricted Warfare"*, attributed to "two Chinese intelligence officers." Carry the hedge.) Destabilization drops critical thinking ~50% and raises suggestibility ~10x — the thornbush-grab state.
- **Algorithm** (Stuart Russell / *Human Compatible*). Is a recommender nudging the target's preferences toward predictability, not just serving clicks? *"it's not necessarily radicalization to an extreme of one particular worldview, it's an extreme of predictability."* "The feed gets me so well" is the symptom of capture.
- **Installed source-code.** What childhood app-turned-source-code is already running? Diagnostic: *"Look at your 8-year-old self. What did you do to get friends, safety, rewards?"* Concealment is "more mentally taxing than calculus"; the most concealment-burdened recipient in the room is the most pliable.

The force-map's job is honesty: if the target arrives *already destabilized*, your spec must declare whether it **reduces that chaos and supplies clarity (help)** or **rides the chaos to sell the cure (BLOCK at Gate test 5)**.

### Section 4 — PCP Design (Perception → Context → Permission)
This is the engine. Three sub-fields, in Hughes's order. *"All I have to do is it's a PCP formula. I change your perception about the situation… if I shift category and context, that changes what you think you're allowed to do… Permission is that final thing that says, 'Oh, in this context I'm completely allowed to do this and it makes perfect sense.'"*

- **Perception shift:** what does the target currently believe is happening / possible, and what is the new perception? State the before and the after.
- **Category word:** the single loaded word that reclassifies the situation. Category beats argument — one word ("mortal threat," "being helped," "reclaiming your mornings") rewrites permission faster and more completely than any reasoning. **Pick the one word. Write it down.** The pub operator's ethical version is the canonical flip: *"he shifted the context to her being helped instead of controlled."* The off-duty-cop story is the warning of how total a category flip is — *"context can dictate your behavior no matter what."*
- **Permission statement:** finish the sentence the recipient should be able to say in their own head — *"in this context I'm completely allowed to ____ and it makes perfect sense."* If you can't write that sentence cleanly, the category word is wrong.

### Section 5 — Recipient-Build Sequence (touchpoints + install order)
The build order. List the touchpoints in the order they fire, and at each one declare **which layer it installs — belief, identity, or permission — and confirm the ask is NOT here.** Then name the single touchpoint the ask is *deferred to*.

```
Touchpoint 1 (channel: ___) → installs: [belief] → ask present? NO
Touchpoint 2 (channel: ___) → installs: [category/context] → ask present? NO
Touchpoint 3 (channel: ___) → installs: [identity — "you are the kind of person who…"] → ask present? NO
Touchpoint 4 (channel: ___) → installs: [permission] → ask present? NO
THE ASK → deferred to: [touchpoint #] → fires only after permission is live; phrased as the recipient's own next obvious step
```

Install belief before identity, identity before permission, permission before the ask. The ask never precedes the build. *"your first goal is being able to engineer and build the perfect client."* Rapport, trust, and "warmth" are byproducts of this sequence — never engineer them as outcomes (*"rapport is a byproduct of that"* and "a little bit overrated").

Two rapport accelerants Hughes names explicitly belong in the early touchpoints (folded from the verification's Missing Material): **(1) a vulnerable admission others would be embarrassed to make** — the fastest trust accelerant; **(2) genuine ignorance + fascination about a thing the target prides themselves on knowing.** Both install belief/identity without an ask in sight.

### Section 6 — Defense / Ethics Verdict
The output of Section 9's gate. One line: **PASS** or **BLOCK**, plus the named technique and the one-sentence reason. A spec with no verdict is not shippable — the verdict is the receipt that the gate ran. (See Section 9 for the five tests and Section 10 for the deterministic backstop that logs this verdict whether or not the persona remembers to.)

### Section 7 — Followability Notes (delivery state)
The spec is inert until someone delivers it, and delivery is where authority is won or destroyed. *"Our brain's shortcut is that we follow someone who is probably loudest, clearest, and has no hesitation in their behavior… micro hesitations are the fastest way to destroy authority."* Specify the delivery state:

- **State to speak FROM** (resonance): you induce the state you broadcast from. *"wherever you're speaking from is where you're going to speak to in other people. Where you speak from, you will speak to."* The piano C-string: strike middle C, only the C strings on every other piano vibrate. Speak from genuine confidence (its two real elements: **willingness to receive social injury** + a **fuzzy belief things work out okay**), not from insecurity, not from hierarchy/status thinking (which "pushes your awareness back behind your eyes").
- **Hesitation kills:** flag every micro-hesitation, hedge, or apology in the asset and cut it. Hesitation-free ≠ correct (see Gate), but hesitation present = authority leaked.
- **Grade level:** write low. Lower-grade-level speakers win debates ~35% more often — *Hughes hedged this: "I think like 35%."* Carry the hedge; write at a low grade level anyway.
- **Picture-painting:** the message must paint a picture (Shane Gillis's Baghdadi bit "painted a picture in your head"). Abstract = forgettable = unfollowed.
- **Gratitude / discipline cues:** present-moment thankfulness reads as emotional stability; visible (not performed) discipline reads as trustworthy. Lead with them.

### Section 8 — Handoff (which production expert, what format)
The spec is a brief, not a deliverable. Name the production expert it routes to and the format they produce. The category word, the permission statement, and the recipient-build sequence are the constraints they write *into*. Validate every named partner against the live roster before routing — phantom routing is a failure (the registry has no visible "April Dunford" skill, for example; do not route to it without confirming it exists).

Real stacking partners confirmed in-roster: **Luke Iha** (copywriting — writes the line-level craft inside the designed context), **David McRaney / `/mcraney-deep-canvass`** (belief-change open, structurally identical to recipient-building), **`/drk-identity`** (identity install in Section 5), **Connelly / `/connelly-subtext`** (component-placement, no stated conclusion), **`/caleb-4c-intro`** (cold-audience video front-end). The existing `chase-hughes-conversational-influence` skill is the line-level companion: PCP (here) sets the macro-context; engineered-self-conclusion (there) is the move *inside* it.

## 3. THE DEFENSE / ETHICS GATE — 5 Tests (MANDATORY, BLOCKING)

This gate is the single non-negotiable of the domain. It is faithful to Hughes's own Plato's-Cave stance — teach offense and defense as one act, and admit knowing the technique does not immunize you: *"I bought the dumbest shit in the world on Instagram… a good well-informed victim."* Every spec passes all five before Section 6 can read PASS. **Any single FAIL blocks the spec.** Defense and positive-deployment notes must be sharper, not softer, than the offensive mechanic — the verification flagged that the interrogation cluster and the FEAR "positive deployment" are exactly where optimism launders an exploitative mechanic, so the gate polices those hardest.

**Test 1 — Name the technique honestly.**
Write the real mechanic in plain words, with Hughes's real name where one exists (PCP, FEAR, COPE, SMRP, the five trust factors, emotional debt) and the synthesis coinage marked where it is the extractor's (Upstream Engine, Evidence Dilemma, Pressure Mechanics, the wall map, the SMRP+AQ 5-step). If you cannot name it without flinching, that flinch is the finding. Euphemism here is itself a FAIL.

**Test 2 — Defensive mirror.**
If this exact spec were run on *you*: could you detect it? At which touchpoint? And the decisive question — would you feel **manipulated** or **helped**? Run PCP in reverse on your own design: *"What context am I being told I'm in, and who chose it for me?"* The tell of an over-engineered context is permission arriving without the recipient having reasoned to it — *"it just makes perfect sense"* is the signature. If the mirror reads "manipulated," BLOCK.

**Test 3 — Surface test.**
Would you defend this design if the target saw the full spec — the category word, the recipient-build order, the deferred ask — laid out on the table in front of them? If the design only works while hidden, it fails. A spec that survives full disclosure to its target is an ethical spec.

**Test 4 — Outcome-on-merits.**
Strip away all the engineered receptivity. With the perception shift, the category flip, and the recipient-build gone — judging the end-state cold, on its merits alone — does it genuinely serve the target? Hughes's discipline: *judge the eventual outcome on its merits, not on how natural it felt.* If the end-state only serves them *because* it felt natural, the naturalness was the product, and that is a BLOCK.

**Test 5 — Destabilization check.**
The bright line. **Reducing existing chaos + supplying genuine clarity and a followable path = help. Manufacturing chaos in order to sell the cure = BLOCK.** This test reads directly off Section 3's force-map: if the spec's own design *adds* a FEAR loop, *manufactures* an enemy, or *fractionates* the audience into a buying state, it is selling the cure for a disease it caused. The thornbush rule, inverted: do not be the cliff that makes them grab your branch. This test must fire inside any content workflow that touches tension-and-release rhythm, not only inside `/ce-design` — the line between "tension-and-release storytelling" and "fractionating your audience into a buying state" is exactly this test.

**Consent / power-asymmetry caveat (interrogation cluster).** When the spec touches SMRP, the alternative question, the bait/Evidence-Dilemma question, or the punishment question, add an explicit consent check: running shame-dissolution on someone who **cannot freely exit the conversation** (an employee, a subordinate, an intimate partner mid-conflict) is where "benevolent" tips into coercive *regardless of intent*. Free exit is a precondition for PASS on any interrogation-derived spec.

## 4. DETERMINISTIC BACKSTOP — `execution/context_ethics_gate.py`

The gate cannot be a persona the model merely remembers to run. The system bans **AI-memory-dependent observability** — any always-on safety check that depends on Claude choosing to invoke it will silently no-op the first time the model is busy. So the gate is paired with a deterministic primitive.

**Division of labor:**
- **The persona runs the judgment.** Tests 2, 3, 4, and the human reading of Test 5 require taste and context the script cannot have — the persona answers them.
- **The primitive logs every verdict and runs the structural checks deterministically.** `execution/context_ethics_gate.py` records each PASS/BLOCK with the named technique, scans the spec for the structural FAIL signatures (a category-escalation word with no Section 6 verdict; an ask that appears in Section 5 before the permission rung; a force-map that marks a FEAR loop PRESENT while the design *adds* one; an interrogation-derived spec with no free-exit declaration), and refuses to emit a clean spec when a structural check trips.

Because the primitive logs unconditionally, the gate **cannot silently no-op** — a missing verdict is itself a logged failure. This satisfies the codebase's deterministic-backstop requirement (pair every observability surface with a deterministic backstop; never ship infra requiring manual Claude invocation). The persona supplies judgment; the script supplies the receipt and the structural floor.

## 5. STANDALONE vs CHAIN Modes

The spec runs two ways, and the mode determines which steps of The Chain fire.

**STANDALONE — `/ce-design` emits a spec.**
The user wants the design, not the asset. `/ce-design` takes the three input fields and returns the eight-section spec. It still runs the full Chain on itself: a spec naming a category-flip word and a recipient-build sequence is expert-domain output with a live ethics surface, so **Step 5.5 (verify) and Step 6 (finalize) fire normally** — exempting a spec from the gate would remove the one check meant to catch a manipulative design. Section 6's verdict is not a substitute for `finalize`; it is an input to it.

**INTEGRATED — the spec is the Chain's front-end.**
Here the spec slots in as **Step 3.5 / 4**: after routing (Step 3) and during load (Step 4), the persona produces the Context-Design Spec, then **hands Section 8 to the routed production expert**, who writes the actual asset *into* the designed context. Step 5 (produce) is the production expert's job; Step 5.5 (verify) and Step 6 (finalize) then run on the finished asset exactly as on any deliverable. The spec is the brief that makes Step 5 build the recipient instead of pushing the outcome.

**Precedence rule (resolves the routing ambiguity):** a request like "design a campaign where buying feels automatic" routes to `/ce-design` **first** to emit the spec, which then hands off to the production binding (copy → Luke Iha; LinkedIn → Lara Acosta; multi-deliverable → `/supercomputer`). `/ce-design` produces the *context*; the production workflow produces the *asset*. This precedence must be mirrored in `routing_enforcer.py` BINDINGS, not just asserted here.

## 6. STATION 1 TEMPLATE — the `/ce-defend` Output Format

Hughes's own news project supplies the ready-made template for the defensive media-literacy layer. He described "Station 1" as a **president's-daily-brief format** for news: *"here's how the three separate stories are actually connected… what to look out for in the next 72 hours."* That single sentence is the inverse of engineered division — where division hands you an enemy plus three "separate" stories and never the connective tissue, Station 1 *supplies* the connective tissue and the forward read. It is the deployable form of the sharpest defensive heuristic in the transcript: *"If you're watching the news and you don't hear nuance, you are being manipulated."*

**`/ce-defend` emits a Station 1 brief — fixed four-part format:**

1. **The three (or N) stories, stated flat.** No enemy assigned, no "how to feel" attached. Just the events.
2. **How they're actually connected.** The connective tissue the division-machine deliberately omits. Track **pressure** — oil, trade, financial, emotional — not the money or the personalities: *"tracking pressure is always more revealing than tracking the money."*
3. **The prepackaged enemy being offered, named as such.** Identify the caricature each tribe is being fed and the totalizing judgment it manufactures. Refuse to grab the thornbush: *"The first solid object that touches your body, you're going to instinctively grab onto it. Even if it's a thorn bush or barbed wire."* Name the branch before the reader grabs it.
4. **What to look out for in the next 72 hours.** The forward read — what the FEAR loop will do next (the ad at the emotional trough is the tell), where the next category-escalation word will land, which clean-and-followable "solution" is being staged for a destabilized audience.

**Why this is the defensive flagship:** media-literacy content that *names the offense honestly* while supplying the antidote is high-share and rarely produced, because almost no one pairs the two. Station 1 is that pairing as a repeatable format. It is also the structural mirror of the Defense/Ethics Gate's Test 5 — it reduces the reader's destabilization and supplies genuine clarity, which is the definition of help.
