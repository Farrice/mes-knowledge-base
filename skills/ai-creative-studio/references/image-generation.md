## Professional Prompt Architecture

Crafting professional-grade AI image prompts requires understanding each compositional layer. Think of prompting as building a specification document for a skilled photographer—each component controls a distinct visual dimension.

The most effective prompts combine **specificity with brevity**. Avoid vague generalities; replace them with precise technical language. Precision in camera specifications, lighting setup, and material description directly correlates with output quality.

### The Universal Prompt Formula

Structure every prompt using this architecture:

```
[Subject/Action] + [Environment/Setting] + [Camera/Lens] + [Lighting] + [Composition] + [Mood/Grade] + [Quality Anchor] + [Negative Prompts]
```

**Component Breakdown:**

- **Subject/Action**: The primary visual focus. Be specific about pose, expression, activity. Example: "A woman with red curly hair, laughing, holding a coffee cup" vs. vague "a woman"

- **Environment/Setting**: Context and location. Include textures, time of day, season, architectural elements. Example: "Modern minimalist loft with floor-to-ceiling windows, concrete floor, morning sunlight"

- **Camera/Lens**: Critical specification that defines perspective, depth of field, and professional quality. Include camera body, lens focal length, aperture. Example: "Shot on Hasselblad X2D with 80mm f/1.9 lens"

- **Lighting**: Specific light source position, quality, color temperature, and mood. Example: "Rembrandt lighting with warm golden hour backlight, deep shadows, rim lighting on hair"

- **Composition**: Frame arrangement, depth, positioning within canvas. Example: "Rule of thirds, subject positioned left-center, leading lines through foreground"

- **Mood/Grade**: Emotional tone, color palette, post-processing aesthetic. Example: "Warm nostalgic mood, desaturated earth tones, slight cross-processing effect"

- **Quality Anchor**: Reference standard or artistic style to elevate output. Example: "Editorial quality as seen in Vogue, award-winning photography"

- **Negative Prompts**: Explicit exclusions preventing unwanted artifacts. Example: "No watermark, no artificial look, no oversaturation, no extra fingers"

**Complete Example Prompt:**

"A ceramic artist at a pottery wheel, hands caked with clay, concentrated expression, in a bright studio with wooden shelves of finished pieces. Shot on Canon EOS R5 with 35mm f/1.4 lens, soft window lighting from left, butterfly lighting setup, shallow depth of field with blurred background. Centered composition with subject in rule of thirds. Warm cream and terracotta color palette, natural skin texture, shot by Annie Leibovitz style. Award-winning editorial quality. No watermark, no artificial look, no plastic skin, no oversaturation."

## Lighting Terminology

Lighting is the language of professional photography. Master these setups and reference them precisely in prompts. Each setup creates distinct emotional and technical results.

### Classic Portrait Lighting Setups

**Rembrandt Lighting**
- Characteristics: Triangular shadow on one cheek, dramatic three-dimensional modeling, traditional and timeless
- Best for: Classic portraiture, serious editorial, dramatic character studies
- Prompt language: "Rembrandt lighting with dramatic side modeling, small triangle of light on shadowed cheek, deep dimensional shadows, studio setup"
- Positioning: Main light 45° to camera, high enough to create shadow under nose and cheekbone triangle
- Example prompt: "Businessman in sharp suit, serious expression. Rembrandt lighting creating dramatic facial modeling, single hair light creating rim, dark moody background. Shot on Canon EOS R5 with 85mm f/1.4. Professional commercial headshot quality."

**Butterfly/Paramount Lighting**
- Characteristics: Symmetrical, flattering, small shadow under nose, traditional glamour look
- Best for: Beauty shots, glamour photography, commercial headshots, red carpet style
- Prompt language: "Butterfly lighting with symmetrical key light, small shadow directly under nose, flattering highlight on cheekbones, beauty photography standard"
- Positioning: Main light centered above camera, creates butterfly shadow under nose
- Example prompt: "Woman with flawless skin, looking directly at camera, natural smile. Butterfly lighting for maximum flattery, soft fill light, professional makeup lighting. Shot on Hasselblad X2D with 80mm f/1.9. Beauty photography quality, Vogue editorial standard."

**Loop Lighting**
- Characteristics: Subtle nose shadow, flattering but with dimension, most versatile everyday setup
- Best for: Commercial portraits, corporate headshots, friendly approachable look
- Prompt language: "Loop lighting with subtle nose shadow, three-dimensional modeling without drama, professional studio setup"
- Positioning: Main light 40° from camera, slightly higher than subject
- Example prompt: "Marketing professional, warm friendly expression. Loop lighting creating gentle dimension, soft catch lights in eyes, neutral background. Shot on Sony A7R V with 70mm f/2.8. Corporate headshot quality, professional polish."

**Split Lighting**
- Characteristics: Dramatic half-lit effect, one side illuminated one side in shadow, high contrast and bold
- Best for: Character-driven portraits, horror/dark narrative, artistic experimental
- Prompt language: "Split lighting dividing face into lit and shadowed halves, dramatic contrast, artistic tension"
- Positioning: Main light 90° from camera at subject's side
- Example prompt: "Mysterious character in profile, half-lit by single dramatic light source. Split lighting creating stark division, deep shadows, cinematic mood. Shot on RED V-Raptor with anamorphic lens. Dark cinematic quality, narrative intensity."

**Clamshell Lighting**
- Characteristics: Beauty-focused, no shadows, perfectly flattering, professional beauty standard
- Best for: Beauty products, cosmetics, glamour, maximum flattery photography
- Prompt language: "Clamshell lighting setup with main light above and fill light below, zero shadows on face, magazine beauty standard"
- Positioning: Main key light above and in front, fill light below reflecting into shadows
- Example prompt: "Flawless face, radiant skin, professional makeup. Clamshell lighting with no facial shadows, perfect beauty lighting, catch lights in eyes. Shot on Nikon Z9 with 85mm f/1.2. Cosmetics advertising quality, magazine beauty standard."

### Advanced Lighting Techniques

**Rim/Edge Lighting**
- Characteristics: Backlit outline, subject separation, dimensional depth, professional separation
- Best for: Editorial, fashion, luxury product, creating subject pop
- Prompt language: "Strong rim lighting creating luminous outline around subject, backlit edge separation, dimensional depth"
- Use: Combine with main light for professional dimensional look
- Example prompt: "Elegant woman in white dress against dark background. Strong rim lighting around hair and shoulders creating luminous edge, three-point lighting with main and fill, professional fashion editorial lighting. Shot on Hasselblad X2D. Editorial fashion standard."

**High Key Lighting**
- Characteristics: Bright overall, minimal shadows, clean commercial aesthetic, light and airy

## Composition Directives

Composition controls how the eye moves through an image and what creates visual impact. Reference these directives explicitly.

### Rule of Thirds

- Divide frame into 9 equal sections (3x3 grid)
- Position subject at intersections or along lines for dynamic balance
- Prompt language: "Rule of thirds composition with subject positioned at right intersecting point, leading eye through frame"
- Example prompt: "Surfer catching wave at sunset. Rule of thirds composition with surfer positioned at right third intersection, horizon along upper third line, dynamic diagonal action. Shot on Sony A7IV with 24mm. Vibrant action sports quality."

### Golden Ratio / Fibonacci Spiral

- Position subject following natural spiral progression
- More sophisticated alternative to rule of thirds
- Prompt language: "Golden ratio composition following Fibonacci spiral, subject positioned along natural spiral progression, mathematically perfect balance"
- Example prompt: "Spiral staircase descending into darkness. Golden ratio composition following spiral architecture, natural mathematical balance, eye drawn through frame. Shot on Hasselblad X2D with 45mm. Architectural editorial quality."

### Centered / Symmetrical Composition

- Subject directly centered, balanced both sides
- Often associates with controlled formal aesthetic (Wes Anderson style)
- Prompt language: "Centered symmetrical composition, subject in exact frame center, perfectly balanced both sides, formal geometric precision"
- Example prompt: "Woman in bright red coat centered in frame, facing camera. Perfectly centered symmetrical composition, balanced background on both sides, formal geometric precision. Shot on Leica Q3. Wes Anderson-style composed geometric quality."

### Leading Lines

- Use natural lines in environment to guide viewer's eye
- Lines converge toward subject or point of interest
- Prompt language: "Strong leading lines pulling eye through composition toward subject, perspective lines converging on focal point"
- Example prompt: "Train tracks disappearing into distance with lone figure standing on platform. Leading lines of tracks pulling viewer's eye through composition toward figure, perspective depth, cinematic depth. Shot on Sony A7R V with 35mm. Cinematic narrative quality."

### Frame Within Frame

- Natural frame created by environmental elements (doorways, windows, trees)
- Creates depth and draws focus to subject within nested frame
- Prompt language: "Frame within frame composition with natural environmental frame drawing focus to subject, nested compositional layers"
- Example prompt: "Woman sitting by window looking out, framed by window panes and curtains. Frame within frame composition using window as natural frame, nested layers of depth, intimate contemplative mood. Shot on Sony A7IV with 50mm. Intimate editorial quality."

### Negative Space / Minimalist

- Subject small within large empty space
- Creates isolation, scale emphasis, contemplative mood
- Prompt language: "Minimalist composition with subject small in frame, expansive negative space, contemplative isolation, breathing room"
- Example prompt: "Lone figure walking across vast snow field at dusk. Minimalist composition with figure small in frame, massive negative space creating scale and isolation, contemplative mood. Shot on Canon EOS R3 with 24mm. Minimalist conceptual quality."

### Dutch Angle / Tilted Horizon

- Frame tilted off-horizontal, creates tension and energy
- Conveys unease, action, dynamism
- Prompt language: "Dutch angle composition with tilted horizon line, dynamic tension and energy, psychological unease"
- Example prompt: "Man falling through air during action sequence. Dutch angle tilted composition creating dynamic tension, sense of uncontrolled motion, dramatic energy. Shot on RED V-Raptor with anamorphic lens. Cinematic action quality."

### Bird's Eye / Overhead View

- Shoot from above looking down
- Creates flattened abstract perspective, emphasizes patterns and geometry
- Prompt language: "Bird's eye overhead composition looking down on subject, flattened perspective, emphasis on geometric patterns"
- Example prompt: "Colorful food arranged on marble table. Bird's eye overhead composition showing geometric arrangement and colors, flat design perspective, pattern emphasis. Shot on Sony A7R V with 50mm. Styled flat lay quality."

### Low Angle Hero Shot

- Shoot from below looking up at subject
- Creates heroic scale, power, dramatic imposing presence
- Prompt language: "Low angle hero shot looking up at subject, dramatic scale emphasis, heroic powerful presence"
- Example prompt: "Corporate executive standing in sleek modern office. Low angle hero shot looking up, dramatic power and scale, imposing professional presence. Shot on Canon EOS R5 with 35mm. Executive power portrait quality."

### Diagonal Composition

- Position key elements along diagonal line across frame
- Creates dynamic movement and visual flow
- Prompt language: "Diagonal composition with subject positioned along strong diagonal line, dynamic movement through frame"
- Example prompt: "Staircase with person at bottom corner ascending to top. Diagonal composition along staircase line, dynamic movement and visual flow through frame. Shot on Hasselblad X2D with 40mm. Architectural editorial quality."

## Negative Prompt Patterns

Exclude unwanted artifacts and qualities that detract from professional results.

### Universal Negative Prompts

Apply to every prompt for clean baseline:

```
no watermark, no text overlay, no artificial look, no oversaturation,
no plastic rendering, no uncanny valley, no distorted proportions
```

- **no watermark**: Prevents copyright logos and photographer marks
- **no text overlay**: Removes unwanted text layers
- **no artificial look**: Prevents fake digital appearance
- **no oversaturation**: Prevents garish color pollution
- **no plastic rendering**: Prevents rubber-skin artificial appearance
- **no uncanny valley**: Prevents slightly-wrong creepy look

### Portrait-Specific Exclusions

```
no extra fingers, no distorted features, no unnatural skin texture,
no warped hands, no incorrect anatomy, no floating features
```

- **no extra fingers**: Prevents polydactyl errors
- **no distorted features**: Prevents face warping
- **no unnatural skin texture**: Prevents plastic or mottled skin
- **no warped hands**: Prevents hand mutation artifacts
- **no incorrect anatomy**: Prevents impossible proportions

### Product Photography Exclusions

```
no reflections of studio equipment, no color cast,
no lens distortion, no motion blur, no dust particles
```

- **no reflections of studio equipment**: Prevents unwanted technical reflections in product
- **no color cast**: Prevents color contamination
- **no lens distortion**: Prevents perspective warping
- **no motion blur**: Prevents unsharp areas
- **no dust particles**: Prevents unwanted texture

### Environmental/Landscape Exclusions

```
no blurry background, no artifacts, no unnatural colors,
no clipped highlights, no crushed shadows
```

- **no blurry background**: Prevents overly soft unfocused areas
- **no artifacts**: Prevents glitch and AI errors
- **no unnatural colors**: Prevents weird color combinations
- **no clipped highlights**: Prevents blown-out white areas
- **no crushed shadows**: Prevents lost shadow detail

### Compositional Exclusions

```
no centered subject, no awkward framing, no distracting background,
no unbalanced composition, no harsh shadows
```

- Custom to prompt based on desired composition
- Specify what NOT to do compositionally

### Quality Exclusions by Platform

**For realistic/photographic prompts:**
```
no illustration, no sketch, no painted look, no cartoon,
no stylized, no low quality, no blurry
```

**For stylized/artistic prompts:**
```
no photorealism, no hyperdetail, no noise, no grain,
no imperfections
```

## Hybrid Reality Asset Workflow

Blend AI-generated elements seamlessly with real photography for maximum flexibility and control.

### Workflow Overview

1. **Generate AI Base Image**
   - Create main AI composition using platform-appropriate techniques
   - Export at maximum available resolution (typically 2048px or higher)
   - Maintain proper aspect ratio for intended use

2. **Inpainting & Refinement**
   - Use inpainting tools to refine specific areas
   - Fix problematic elements (hands, details, faces)
   - Adjust composition subtly if needed
   - Re-run specific regions for quality improvement

3. **Outpainting Extension**
   - Expand canvas to add environmental context
   - Extend backgrounds or additional subjects
   - Adjust composition by extending borders

4. **Export for Compositing**
   - Save at highest resolution available
   - Export with transparency if needed
   - Create mask/alpha channel for edges if compositing with photography

5. **Photo Compositing & Integration**
   - Import AI element into photo editing software (Photoshop, Affinity)
   - Composite over real photography background
   - Use blending modes and masks for natural integration
   - Refine edges with feathering and anti-aliasing

6. **Color Grading Matching**
   - Match AI element color temperature to photographic background
   - Apply consistent color grading to both elements
   - Ensure lighting direction matches between AI and photo elements
   - Add subtle grain and texture for cohesion

7. **Subtle Aging & Texture**
   - Add film grain consistent across entire composite
   - Apply subtle vignette if appropriate
   - Add light dust or texture overlay for authenticity
   - Ensure no hard digital edges remain

### Quality Assurance Checklist

- [ ] Color temperature matches between AI and real photography
- [ ] Lighting direction consistent (shadows point same direction)
- [ ] No visible blending boundaries or hard edges
- [ ] Film grain and texture applied uniformly
- [ ] Aspect ratio and composition finalized
- [ ] Export resolution appropriate for intended use
- [ ] File format appropriate (PSD for editing, PNG/JPG for delivery)

### Example Hybrid Workflow

**Scenario**: Create hero image of product in lifestyle environment combining real product photography with AI-generated setting.

1. Photograph real product on neutral background at studio
2. Generate AI image of dream lifestyle environment where product would be used
3. Use inpainting to create "space" in AI image where product will be placed
4. Export both high-resolution images
5. Composite real product into AI environment in Photoshop
6. Match lighting and color grading between real product and AI environment
7. Blend edges using layer masks and feathering
8. Add consistent film grain and subtle vignette
9. Final color grade across entire composite
10. Export final deliverable

## Common Pitfalls & Fixes

Address frequent issues systematically for consistent quality improvement.

| Problem | Typical Cause | Recommended Fix | Example Fix Prompt Addition |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|---------------------------|
| Plastic-looking skin | Over-smoothed rendering, excessive AI beautification | Add natural skin texture specifications | "natural skin texture with visible pores, authentic skin micro-texture, no plastic skin appearance" |
| Wrong hand count (extra fingers) | AI hand generation limitation | Specify exactly five fingers, regenerate multiple times | "five fingers clearly visible on each hand, proper hand anatomy, no polydactyl" |
| Generic stock-photo look | Vague undescriptive prompt, missing specific details | Add precise camera, lens, lighting, and style details | "Shot on Hasselblad X2D with 80mm f/1.9, Rembrandt lighting, editorial quality as seen in Vogue" |
| Oversaturated colors | Default processing, insufficient color direction | Specify color palette and desaturation levels | "natural color palette, muted earth tones, desaturated warm aesthetic, no oversaturation" |
| Inconsistent style across prompts | Mixed references, vague style direction | Commit to single photographer or publication style per series | "Peter Lindbergh minimalist portrait style applied consistently" |
| Text rendering errors | Platform limitation, unrealistic expectation | Use Ideogram 3 or GPT Image specifically for text-heavy | Switch to text-optimized platform or remove text requirement |
| Distorted facial features | Model misinterpretation of complex descriptions | Simplify description, reference specific photographer style | "clean simple portrait, Annie Leibovitz style, natural undistorted features" |
| Awkward hand/arm positioning | Anatomical generation challenge | Specify hand position clearly, use positive examples | "hands relaxed at sides, natural arm positioning, anatomically correct" |
| Unnatural eye appearance | Eye rendering difficulty, uncanny valley | Add eye-specific details | "warm natural eye expression, genuine eye contact, natural eye color and lighting" |
| Distracting background | Insufficient background specification | Explicitly define background element and its treatment | "blurred bokeh background, shallow depth of field, background completely soft and non-distracting" |
| Color mismatch between generations | Lack of color reference | Lock color palette across prompts | "consistent warm golden tone palette, match previous generation color temperature" |
| Composition feels off | Vague framing specification | Reference specific composition technique | "Rule of thirds with subject at right intersection, leading lines pulling through frame" |
| Subject looks too artificial | Generic prompt without personality | Add emotional and contextual depth | "genuine warm smile, natural relaxed posture, authentic human presence, not digital-looking" |
| Lighting looks flat | Insufficient lighting specification | Specify detailed light setup with position and quality | "Rembrandt lighting with triangle cheek shadow, rim light from back creating edge separation, three-point setup" |
| Image too contrasty/harsh | Over-specified lighting, crushed blacks/blown highlights | Add softening specifications | "soft diffused studio lighting, gentle gradation, lifted blacks for flattering rendering" |
| Details too crunchy/noisy | Overly detailed prompt creating visual chaos | Simplify, focus on key elements only | Reduce adjective count, remove unnecessary detail specifications |
| Unnatural material appearance | Insufficient material specification or wrong material language | Use precise material terminology | "brushed aluminum with visible grain, matte contemporary finish, not reflective" |
| Subject looks cut off or awkward | Poor composition framing specification | Specify framing and breathing room | "full-body frame with comfortable space above head, not cropped at edges" |

### Systematic Improvement Process

When output quality falls short:

1. **Diagnose the issue**: Compare output to specific quality anchor or reference
2. **Identify the cause**: Determine if cause is prompt ambiguity, platform limitation, or specification gap
3. **Implement focused fix**: Add specific language addressing identified issue
4. **Regenerate and compare**: Create new version with fix applied
5. **Document the fix**: Record what language successfully addressed the issue for future use
6. **Build prompting muscle memory**: Incorporate successful fixes into standard prompt structure

### Pro Tips for Consistent Quality

- **Establish baseline templates**: Create standardized prompt structures for each common use case (portrait, product, lifestyle, etc.)
- **Reference specific standards**: Always include specific magazine, photographer, or quality standard reference
- **Build progressive complexity**: Start with simple strong prompt, incrementally add specifications only if needed
- **Test camera/lens combinations**: Master 5-10 favorite camera/lens combinations that consistently produce great results
- **Maintain color consistency**: Lock color palette and tone across series of related images
- **Prioritize over-specification**: Include too much detail rather than too little; platforms handle excess gracefully
- **Use negative prompts strategically**: Include only negative prompts addressing known challenges; generic negatives dilute specificity
- **Iterate with intention**: Each regeneration should test specific hypothesis, not just "try again"
- **Document successful prompts**: Maintain database of prompts that produced excellent results for reference and iteration
