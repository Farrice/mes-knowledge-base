## Portrait & Headshot Prompts

### Executive / Professional Headshot

```
[GENDER] [AGE RANGE] professional, [ETHNICITY/FEATURES], wearing [ATTIRE], shot on Canon EOS R5 with 85mm f/1.4 lens, shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh. Rembrandt lighting with warm fill. [BACKGROUND: modern office / neutral gradient / outdoor urban]. Natural skin texture with subtle retouching. Confident, approachable expression. Editorial quality, LinkedIn-ready. Corporate photography style.
```

**Key customization points:**
- [GENDER]: Male, Female, Non-binary
- [AGE RANGE]: 30s, 40s, 50s, etc.
- [ETHNICITY/FEATURES]: Specific facial features, ethnicity, distinguishing marks
- [ATTIRE]: Business suit, blazer and open collar, formal dress, etc.
- [BACKGROUND]: Choose one or specify custom setting

---

**Key customization points:**

## Tips for Maximum Effectiveness

1. **Specificity is Power**: The more specific your [PLACEHOLDERS], the more accurate the AI's output. Instead of "professional," use "55-year-old female architect in tailored charcoal blazer."

2. **Technical Details Matter**: Include camera model, lens, f-stop, and lighting setup. These technical specs drive hyperrealistic output.

3. **Reference Studios**: Mention prestigious publications and studios (Vogue, ArchDaily, Behance, Awwwards) to influence quality and style.

4. **Combine Multiple Styles**: Use slashes (/) to give AI options: "shot on Hasselblad X2D / Phase One IQ4" for variation.

5. **Color Grading Language**: Use specific color grading terms: "desaturated earth tones," "rich jewel tones," "high contrast black and white," "warm color grade."

6. **Lighting Vocabulary**: Master these lighting terms to control mood:
   - Rembrandt lighting (classic, flattering)
   - Rim lighting (dramatic, separation)
   - Butterfly lighting (glamorous)
   - Three-point lighting (professional)
   - Natural golden hour (warm, authentic)

7. **Material & Texture Specificity**: Instead of "nice surface," specify "brushed walnut with visible grain" or "matte ceramic with subtle glaze texture."

8. **Composition Precision**: Use photography terminology: "rule of thirds," "leading lines," "depth layers," "negative space," "framing," "shallow depth of field."

9. **Iterate and Refine**: Use successful prompts as bases. Adjust one element at a time to see what changes output.

10. **Platform Requirements**: Remember aspect ratios and design constraints specific to each platform (Instagram 1:1, YouTube 16:9, TikTok 9:16).
