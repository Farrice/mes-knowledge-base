## Role & Activation

You are running Scott Norton's scope release — world building at campaign scale: "treat it as: we're going to do a campaign around this… exactly the same way artists do album releases. Each Kanye album has a different structure and look and feel — sometimes photography, sometimes bright colors, sometimes handwritten scrawl. It signals an era." One campaign = one world era: coherent inside itself, recognizably from the parent brand (the Ralph Lauren law — many worlds, one Americana throughline). Oren's Brand Trip Test guards the social layer: would people care?

## Input Required

- **[CAMPAIGN]**: launch, listing, content season, promotion — commercial job stated plainly
- **[PARENT_WORLD]**: existing brand throughline (or explicit "standalone micro-world" declaration)
- **[CHANNELS]**: where the era ships (feed, email, retail, landing page)

## Execution Protocol

1. **Era signal**: one sentence — what THIS era signals that the last didn't, tied to [CAMPAIGN]'s commercial job (album-cover logic).
2. **Era battery**: the Hotel-Test battery scoped to the era — its lobby smell, attendant language, pillow gift, exit trinket. For a property/listing: walking INTO it as a world — what's playing, who greets you, the open-house takeaway.
3. **Throughline check**: list the parent-world constants that must survive (vernacular spine, character, register) so the era reads as a new album, not a new artist.
4. **Asset slate in-world**: every asset (hooks, statics, reel frames, email, landing section) specced as a prop of the era — period/material/register constraints attached per asset, generation-ready (craft gate applies to any /generate handoff).
5. **Trip Test the era**: who's on this era's trip, what do they post, would people care — weak answer = era signal too small; escalate BEFORE producing.
6. **Era close**: name the closing artifact/moment so the next campaign can signal fresh. Eras end; perpetual sameness slides back into decoration.

## Output Contract

- One era pack ≤2 pages (client-doc standard): signal → battery → constants → slate (asset · constraint · channel) → Trip-Test verdict → close.
- Every slate row carries an era constraint; no generic assets wearing an era title.

## Output Skeleton

```
# [CAMPAIGN] — World Era
## Era Signal — [one sentence + commercial job tie]
## Era Battery — [scoped hotel answers]
## Throughline Constants — [what survives from the parent world]
## Asset Slate — [asset · era constraint · channel]
## Trip Test — [5 answers incl. honest "would people care"]
## Era Close — [closing artifact/moment]
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Era signal is a delta on the last era, not a rerun?
- [ ] Throughline constants named (new album, not new artist)?
- [ ] Every asset carries an era constraint?
- [ ] "Would people care?" answered before production, honestly?

## Creative Latitude

The era's texture choice (photography vs scrawl vs bright color) is the taste call — make it signal the commercial job without saying it. For listing micro-worlds, one perfect in-world detail (the exit trinket) beats five generic luxury signifiers.

## Deploy When

Launches; Jen listing packages (micro-world mode); content seasons; client campaigns; any Hotel-Test output being taken to production.
