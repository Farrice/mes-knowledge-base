# Genius Patterns — Scott Norton × Oren John

Canonical evidence and full explanations: `../../../extractions/oren-norton-brand-world-building/extraction-report.md`. Use this reference for rapid pattern retrieval; load the source packet before quoting externally.

1. **Category psychology before aesthetics** — excavate the category’s emotional history and reclaim an abandoned feeling [01:32–02:22].
2. **Transportive setting** — specify time, place, class, technology, and camera-holder; a mood is not a setting [02:42–03:08].
3. **Take it seriously at 11/10** — totality reaches retail ads, packing slips, errors, and back-office objects [03:12–04:55].
4. **Growth by initiation** — confer a specific playable role and credential instead of buying attention with a discount [05:10–07:44].
5. **Commit to the bit** — the institutional register survives every surface; breaking character becomes conspicuous [04:43–07:01].
6. **Setting → institutions → props** — fictionalized adjacency and implied history turn art direction into a living world [09:46–12:11].
7. **Props, artifacts, and totems** — every object must pass the zoom-in test and make ownership feel like participation [11:28–12:11; 25:35–27:51].
8. **Customer as hero** — the brand supplies the setting, costume, supporting cast, and transformation detail, not the hero role [13:01–14:24].
9. **Fantasy engine** — name a competitor-excluding fantasy that predicts what participants display and post [28:36–35:47].
10. **World before product** — oppose the category king axis by axis; let lore make product decisions derivable [17:17–20:24].
11. **Play as generative state** — canon the spine of the lore and improvise the flesh [20:27–20:47].
12. **Humanize** — personify values, cast the team, and make internal commitments physical and ceremonial [20:52–22:40].
13. **Artist–craft–hack calibration** — preserve both story legibility and the customer’s job-to-be-done; then commit one notch harder [22:53–24:22].
14. **Shelf-rooted diagnostics** — use the shelf angle, Double-Take Test, Hotel Test, and Brand Trip Test to make the world falsifiable [24:24–26:25; 37:41–40:18].

## Recognition condition

The method is present only when a decision in one layer derives another: fantasy predicts setting; setting implies institutions; institutions issue roles and artifacts; artifacts produce observable participant behavior. A decorated moodboard fails even if it looks excellent.
