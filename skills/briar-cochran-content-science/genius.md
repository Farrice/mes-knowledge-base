## Decision Framework (run before any deliverable)

1. **The Idea Venn** — a great idea sits in ALL THREE circles; center = psychological alignment
   ("their ears perk up"):
   - **Proper TAM**: audience breadth sized to THIS video's declared goal, not to max reach.
     His receipt: "unlock creativity" ~250K views earned less than "build a media team" 10K views
     — both correct for their goals.
   - **Specific Psychology**: level-correct insider language. The wrestler test: "teach you a
     takedown" outs you; "90-minute carb-load blueprint after a 5-lb weight cut" ignites.
     Every audience has 3-5 things they think about daily — find those, compress to 1-3 word
     triggers ("yapping" is alive; "viral" is burned).
   - **Unique/Novel**: target emotion = "I've never heard it that way" — felt-but-never-
     articulated. Verbatim ripping caps a personal brand.
2. **Signal check** — name the data source before production (subreddit thread, outlier,
   sales-call FAQ, comment cluster). No source = you're guessing at zero.
3. **Outlier math** — inspiration only counts as signal at ≥3-5× the creator's OWN baseline
   average (click the profile, compute it). 100K views on a 200K-baseline account is a flop.
4. **Transfer filter** (borrowed ideas) — 4 layers: **Following / Strategy / Niche / Creator**.
   Subtract what you can't replicate (their reach, their goal, their audience psychology, their
   charisma/looks); twist what remains; only then produce.

## The Five-Input Ideation Hour (weekly, sacred, keywords-not-scripts)

| # | Input | Protocol |
|---|-------|----------|
| 1 | Inspiration list | 15-20 creators on a burner account, in AND out of niche; scroll, jot keywords; baseline-check every outlier |
| 2 | Reddit | 15-20 subreddits ≥50-100K members, sort by top (week/month/year); paste whole threads into Claude: "what are people talking about here?" — two-sided topics reproduce their argument in your comments |
| 3 | YouTube thumbnails | Outlier finders (1of10/TubeLab/TubeBuddy) on 5-10 niche keywords. YT is click-and-watch → outlier performance attributes to packaging → thumbnails are pre-isolated topic data |
| 4 | External signal | Sales-call FAQs, top email CTRs, winning paid ads, community call questions |
| 5 | Internal signal | Own comments, DMs, view outliers |

Yield: 2 ideas per input = 10 data-backed ideas/week minimum.

## The Arsenal Loop (Chris Rock, broken and rebuilt)

Rock tests jokes club-by-club until MSG is all-killer. Content has NO single MSG — "we play
Madison Square Garden every night" — so the loop is infinite:

- Test every new idea **3-6 times** before discarding.
- Winners bank into a persistent **arsenal** (goal: 12+ banked by week 8, not 7).
- Weekly ratio ≈ **4 proven : 3 tests**; never fall below a **70/30** winner/test split — and
  never run an all-winners week.
- **Deliberately bench** winners (run 4 of 6): content lifecycle is 4-6 weeks; repetition
  converts winners into auto-scroll triggers ("shadow banned by the follower"). The arsenal is a
  RATIONING device, not a trophy case.

## The Four-Question Win Audit (strict order)

Was it the **Topic** → the **Format** → the **Hook** (incl. location/wardrobe/color) → the
**Body** (a 30-second story/statistic driving comments)? Isolate the variable; write the verdict
into the matching arsenal — four ledgers: topics, formats, hook principles, body principles.
His own: topics = counter-positioning, data analytics, posting frequency; formats = basement
yap, green screen (dead: object lessons, brackets); hooks = blue, basement, thinking nonverbal;
body = Liquid Death, Cashvertising (woven ~30s deep as engineered comment bait).

## Follower-Quality Economics (account layer)

- Value audiences in **$/follower**, not size. His client receipts: influencer 400K followers →
  $0.06/follower; niche educator 13K → $3.46; sales creator 700 followers → $1M+/mo months.
  Pure niche + education + high-ticket offer beats influencer scale ~50×.
- **CCN layers** (via Paddy Galloway): core / casual / new — ladder top/middle/bottom-funnel
  content on ONE through-line (Gatsby green-light party metaphor). Off-through-line virality =
  poisoned follower quality.
- The follower graph is the algorithm's first jury: ~67% of first-3-hour views are followers;
  they decide whether non-followers ever see it. Quality killers: bad paid ads ("paying someone
  for their love"), niche swaps, too-high TAM, off-through-line content, over-posting (fatigue →
  auto-scroll).
- Track: follower-view ratio (first 3h), returning-viewer %, story views ≥10% of followers,
  inbound DMs, $/follower trend.

## Anti-Patterns (what Briar rejects — all source-anchored)

- **The verbatim rip** [V1, 2026-07-23]: "Female creator wearing a swimsuit talking about
  motherhood should not be contextualized over and stolen as a hook for a dude in sales" — all
  four transfer layers failed at once.
- **Raw-view worship** [V1, 2026-07-23]: "Just because a video gets 100,000 views does not mean

  watch time ratios... that stuff's whenever you are truly crushing content, you're averaging

  this video is a good idea. I'm going to go make it, which is a bad idea" — no named signal, no

## Recognition Test

Before shipping any output from this skill, ask: **would Briar recognize this as his own
system?** Concretely: is the baseline math shown, is the signal source named, is the goal
declared before TAM, is exactly one primary variable isolated, is something benched or killed?
If a deliverable could have been produced by a generic "content tips" model — no ledger, no
ratios, no insider read-aloud — it fails the recognition test regardless of polish.
