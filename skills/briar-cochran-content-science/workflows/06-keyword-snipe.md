# Keyword Snipe

"Find these keywords that specifically snipe the soul of your audience." Every audience has 3-5
things they think about daily; the job is finding the exact words that make ears perk up.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `../genius.md` → Specific Psychology. Required: niche + the practitioner LEVEL of the
target audience (beginner language and expert language are different dialects — the wrestler
takedown test). Gather any raw audience language available (comments, DMs, subreddit threads,
sales calls).

## Skill Acquisition

- `../genius.md` (keyword sniping, insider read-aloud, exemplars)
- `../references/source-quotes.md` (wrestler calibration, yapping-vs-viral, Kolby cascade)
- Tier 1.5 Recall grounding fires for voice/brand domains — inject matching cards

## Execution

1. **Daily-thoughts hypothesis**: List the 3-5 things this audience thinks about every day (not
   what they SHOULD think about — what actually loops in their heads).
2. **Language harvest**: From raw audience sources, extract candidate words/phrases (1-3 word
   units). Anonymous sources (Reddit) outrank public-performance sources.
3. **Insider read-aloud**: Say each candidate as a target-level practitioner would hear it.
   Cut anything that outs you as an outsider; cut burned words (the "viral" problem); keep words
   currently alive in the niche (the "yapping" pattern).
4. **Temperature tag**: HOT (perking right now) / STABLE (evergreen identity words) / BURNED
   (retired by overuse). Note replacement candidates for burned words.
5. **Deploy map**: For each keeper — where it works (topic, title, spoken first line, DM,
   keyword-cascade candidate) and the emotional register it triggers.

## Output Requirements

Niche keyword ledger: 10-20 entries — word · temperature · source evidence · insider verdict ·
deploy map. Plus the 3-5 daily-thoughts list it derives from.
Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/keyword-ledger.md — honor its Output Contract.

## Quality Gate

Every keyword has source evidence (no invented jargon). Insider read-aloud verdict recorded.
Burned words identified, not just keepers. Level-match explicit (who exactly perks up).
