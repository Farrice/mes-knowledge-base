# Contextualization Filter

"You have to untangle the reasoning why they posted that video in the grand scheme of their
account." The journey: their account → their video → WHY it worked → what survives → your video.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `../genius.md` → Decision Framework §4. Required: the source video/post (verified as a true
outlier via /bc-outlier-scan — if not verified, do that first), the source account's context,
and your/target account's context (size, strategy, niche, creator profile).

## Skill Acquisition

- `../genius.md` (transfer bridge, anti-exemplar)
- `../references/frameworks.md` §3
- Deeper forensics on the source: stack `skills/alex-content-science/` Detail Stack

## Execution

Walk the four layers; verdict each TRANSFERS / SUBTRACT / TWIST:

1. **Following**: Did size buy them slack (personal-story content, casual posts)? A 3M-follower
   move rarely ports to a 2K account (Hormozi-pregnancy anti-pattern).
2. **Strategy**: What was the video FOR in their account's game — growth, monetization, testing
   (book-title hooks)? Does your goal match?
3. **Niche**: Does the psychology port across tribes (money-motivated vs granola; guy-in-sales
   vs motherhood)? Check keyword temperature in YOUR niche (/bc-keyword-snipe if unsure).
4. **Creator**: Be honest — was it their charisma, intellect, or looks? Subtract what you can't
   replicate.

Then: **Twist engineering** — with non-transferable layers subtracted, design what remains into
YOUR version (new mechanism, extended model, opposite stance, your account's through-line).
If nothing survives all four layers: KILL, with the reason banked so it isn't re-borrowed.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Type | Adaptation |
|------|-----------|
| Cross-platform ports | Add a 5th check: does the platform's attribution model match? |
| Client accounts | Client's strategy layer comes from their funnel doc, not intuition |
| Farrice's voice | Surviving idea still passes VOICE-CARD + reader-contract dials |
| Cross-niche steals | Expect TWIST on niche layer by default; verbatim = automatic fail |

## Output Requirements

Four-layer verdict table + subtract list + engineered twist + final adapted idea (or KILL with
banked reason).
Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/contextualization-verdict.md — honor its Output Contract.

## Quality Gate

All four layers verdicted (no skipped honesty on the Creator layer). Twist is specific and
producible, not "make it your own." Verbatim rips impossible by construction. KILL is a
legitimate output.
