# Proper-TAM Calibration

"Go for the proper TAM based off of your specific goals." The 250K-view video that earns less
than the 10K-view video is the point, not the problem.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `../genius.md` → Decision Framework §1a. Required: the idea + the account's business model
+ the DECLARED GOAL for this piece. If the goal is unstated, derive candidates from the funnel
(growth / nurture / revenue) and force a choice.

## Skill Acquisition

- `../genius.md` (proper TAM, follower-quality economics)
- `../references/frameworks.md` §2, §6

## Execution

1. **Size it**: Who can this piece be FOR? Order-of-magnitude bands (billions / millions /
   hundreds-of-thousands / thousands / hundreds).
2. **Goal match**: Growth goals tolerate high TAM *on the through-line*; monetization goals
   want narrow TAM (the media-team pattern); nurture targets core/casual only.
3. **Through-line check**: High-TAM ideas must still sit on the account's through-line (Gatsby
   party test: is the ONE person you want inside this big net?). Off-through-line virality =
   poisoned follower quality (dog-driving-car anti-pattern).
4. **Follower-quality forecast**: Who follows from this? Would they watch video 2, 3, 4? Predict
   the first-3h follower jury effect of accumulating this audience.
5. **Verdict**: MATCHED / RESIZE UP / RESIZE DOWN / OFF-THROUGH-LINE-KILL, with the reworded
   idea at the correct TAM where resizing is possible.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Type | Adaptation |
|------|-----------|
| Funnel content | Map verdicts to top/middle/bottom (CCN) explicitly |
| Client work | Goal comes from the paying funnel, not the client's vanity |
| Launch content | Temporarily higher TAM acceptable; note the reversion date |
| Local/service biz | "Narrow" may mean geographic + situational (the ASU Pilates example) |

## Output Requirements

TAM band + goal statement + through-line verdict + follower-quality forecast + final verdict
with resized wording where applicable.
Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/idea-gate-scorecard.md (TAM section) — honor its Output Contract.

## Quality Gate

Goal declared before sizing. Through-line test run on every high-TAM idea. No "bigger is
better" reasoning anywhere. Verdict names the funnel layer it serves.
