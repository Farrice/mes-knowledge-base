# Signal Mine

"You could literally go into your last 100 emails, find the headline that has the highest
click-through rate, turn that into a hook, and make a video about it." Inputs 4 and 5 of the
ideation hour, run standalone at depth.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `../genius.md` → Five-Input table rows 4-5. Inventory available signal surfaces: sales
calls/transcripts, email stats, paid-ad performance, community/consulting call questions,
comments, DMs, own view outliers. No surfaces available = route to /bc-ideation-hour inputs 1-3
instead.

## Skill Acquisition

- `../genius.md` (signal doctrine, win-audit body arsenal)
- `../references/frameworks.md` §5

## Execution

1. **External sweep** (per available surface):
   - Sales calls: the 3-5 most-repeated questions/objections → one video each.
   - Email: top subject-line CTRs → hooks; top-clicked links → topic demand.
   - Paid ads: best performer → organic version.
   - Community: "what do I feel like I'm always answering?" → video.
2. **Internal sweep**: comment clusters (repeated questions, arguments), DM themes, own videos
   beating own baseline (compute multiples).
3. **Convert**: each signal → idea card (keyword · idea · signal source · strength: how many
   independent occurrences · proposed goal). Multi-source convergence = flag as priority test.
4. **Body-arsenal harvest**: recurring references that spike comments (the Liquid Death pattern)
   → bank as 30-second-weave candidates, distinct from full ideas.

## Output Requirements

Idea cards (signal-sourced, strength-rated, goal-tagged) + body-arsenal candidates + the
priority-test shortlist (multi-source convergence first).
Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/ideation-idea-cards.md — honor its Output Contract.

## Quality Gate

Every idea traces to a countable occurrence (n named). Convergence flagged where present.
Body-weave candidates separated from full ideas. No signal surface invented or assumed.
