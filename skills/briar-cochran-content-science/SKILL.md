# Briar Cochran — Content Science

> Content is not a game of guessing, it's a game of knowing. Start at one, not zero.

## Core Genius

Briar treats content as an **evidence pipeline, not a creativity lottery**. Every idea arrives
with signal attached, survives a three-way psychological-alignment gate (Proper TAM × Specific
Psychology × Unique/Novel), and then earns permanent placement in a testing arsenal that is
deliberately rationed — winners get benched — so the audience never fatigues. Everyone else
optimizes the artifact; Briar optimizes the portfolio.

## When to Use This Skill

- You need **content ideas backed by data**, not vibes (weekly practice or one-off)
- You found an inspiring video and want to know **whether it's real signal** and **what survives
  transfer** to your account
- You're planning a **weekly content slate** and need the winners/tests ratio managed
- A post won (or flopped instructively) and you want to know **which variable did it**
- You're **installing a content OS** on a new account or client
- You want a **keyword-cascade belonging piece** for a niche

## Quick Reference

- **The Venn**: Proper TAM (sized to goal) × Specific Psychology (insider perk-up words) ×
  Unique/Novel ("never heard it that way") — all three or rework.
- **Outlier math**: views ÷ creator's own baseline; act at ≥3-5×.
- **Transfer filter**: Following / Strategy / Niche / Creator → transfers / subtract / twist.
- **Arsenal rules**: 3-6 attempts per test · ≈4:3 winners:tests · ≥70/30 floor · bench surplus
  winners · 4-6 week content lifecycle · never an all-winners week.
- **Win audit order**: Topic → Format → Hook → Body → bank into the matching arsenal.
- **Claim hygiene**: Briar's self-reported stats are UNCONFIRMED — deploy methodology, never
  cite his numbers as facts.
