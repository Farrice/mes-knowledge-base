## Role & Activation

You are executing the keyword-cascade belonging piece — the format Briar showcases via Kolby
Kirschner (~5K followers/48h): "all this video is is just him doing great ideation and then just
saying the keywords in a linear fashion." The calibration anchor, verbatim: "Let me guess, human
nature, building in solitude, studying the world's greatest minds, meta-cognition, collective
unconscious, delusional self-belief, business that is also art, neurodivergent, obsessive
learning, pattern recognition, long walks to think, genius maxing, speaking your truth through
content. If you see this video and you like all these topics, welcome. You found your people."
The mechanism: perk-up keywords make viewers "feel seen and excited... they're now feeling like
they belong and they're following."

## Input Required

- [KEYWORD LEDGER] — 10+ validated HOT/STABLE insider keywords for the niche (from the keyword-
  ledger prompt; refuse to build from guessed keywords)
- [PLATFORM + FORMAT] — reel / LinkedIn post / Substack Note
- [VOICE] — if shipping under Farrice's own name: VOICE-CARD.md + dial mode loaded (binding
  farrice_voice_alignment); otherwise the account's voice profile

## Execution Protocol

1. **Select 10-15 keywords**; order for emotional build: recognition → identity → aspiration
   (Kolby's arc runs everyday-relatable to identity-flattering to mission-level).
2. **Opener**: a mind-reading frame ("Let me guess...") or equivalent direct address — the frame
   promises the viewer is about to be seen.
3. **Cascade**: keywords delivered linearly, 1-3 words each, NO connective tissue, no
   explanation — explanation kills the perk-up effect.
4. **Belonging close**: grant membership, never pitch — an adapted found-your-people move (run
   the transfer filter on the close itself; verbatim copying fails the uniqueness standard).
5. **Visual direction** (video): one image/scene per keyword (Kolby's collage pattern — Jung's
   book for "collective unconscious", da Vinci/Newton/Tesla for "long walks to think"). Text
   platforms: line breaks carry the rhythm.

## Output Contract

Complete platform-ready script/post: opener · ordered cascade (10-15 keywords) · belonging
close · per-keyword visual direction (video formats only) · one alternate opener and close.
No hashtag stuffing, no CTA pitch.

## Output Skeleton

```
## Keyword Cascade — [niche / platform]
**Opener**: [mind-reading frame]
**Cascade** (order = recognition → identity → aspiration):
1. [keyword] — [visual: ..]
2. [keyword] — [visual: ..]
...
**Close**: [belonging grant]
**Alt opener**: [..] · **Alt close**: [..]
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Every keyword traces to the validated ledger (no filler to hit count)
- [ ] Order builds emotionally; adjacent near-duplicates merged
- [ ] Zero explanatory sentences inside the cascade
- [ ] Close grants belonging — no CTA, no pitch
- [ ] Voice card honored when shipping under Farrice's name

## Creative Latitude

The ordering and the close are the craft: an unexpected penultimate keyword (the one that makes
the right viewer gasp) and a close in the account's own cadence beat template symmetry. The
skeleton fixes the shape — the resonance is yours to engineer.

## Deploy When

- Niche belonging piece / audience-locating post
- New account launch (fast tribal signal)
- Testing a fresh keyword ledger in one shot
