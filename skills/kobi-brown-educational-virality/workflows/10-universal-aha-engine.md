# Universal Aha Engine

## Role
This is the domain-agnostic front door for the whole Kobi Brown system. It applies the educational-virality method to **any** task with educational or entertainment value — a LinkedIn post, a client memo, a newsletter, a sales page, a course module, a deck, a video script, a talk, a tweet, even an internal doc — and re-architects it so the audience leaves **changed**, not just informed or entertained.

Use it when the objective isn't "make educational content go viral" specifically, but the broader goal: *make this thing produce an aha, a perspective shift, or an identity shift — and not be AI slop or noise.* If the task is clearly one of the nine specialist workflows (a full script, a legitimacy ladder, a visual explainer), route there instead. The Aha Engine is the universal lens; the nine are the specialist tools.

## Pre-Flight Gate (from `genius.md` Decision Framework)
Answer before producing:
1. **Goal:** durable authority / real change, or a short-term spike? (Sets every constraint.)
2. **The true idea:** what should the audience actually *understand* — not just consume?
3. **The wrong belief:** what does the audience currently believe, assume, or overlook that this will correct or reframe? *(This is the aha seed.)*
4. **The uncopyable layer:** what about your version can't a copycat or an AI fake — access, data, lived proof, method, relationship?
5. **The line:** what accuracy/integrity boundary won't you cross for attention?

## Input Required
- The task/draft/idea (or just the topic + intent).
- Audience and where it will appear (platform/format/context).
- What you want the audience to think, feel, or do afterward.
- Any proof, access, data, or experience you actually have.

## Execution
1. **Find the payload.** Name the one true, valuable idea. Pressure-test it: is it worth making even with an average hook? If it's thin, fix the *idea* before anything else (Core Idea Over Hook).
2. **Locate the aha.** Identify the misconception, blind spot, or unexamined assumption the audience holds. The aha is the gap between what they believe and what's true. Name the exact "you think X — actually Y."
3. **Build the doorway.** Choose the entertainment/curiosity entry that earns attention *without distorting the idea*: a question, anomaly, contradiction, scale shift, stakes, or striking image. Lead with appetite, never with "here's what I'll explain." (The Vegetable Smuggle.)
4. **Choose the proof form.** Pick at least one: simulation, scale comparison, physical analogy, step-by-step demo, first-hand access, or story. Make the proof *carry the truth*, not decorate it. (Visual Accuracy as Epistemic Discipline.)
5. **Insert earned legitimacy.** At the moment the claim gets hard to believe, show — don't assert — why you're trustworthy: process, access, data, lived result, verification. Never "trust me, I'm an expert."
6. **Make the Restraint Cut.** Identify the line you won't cross and the cheap version you're refusing (the extra views, the spicier claim, the milked follow-up). Name it explicitly.
7. **Set the differentiation move.** State what makes this uncopyable. If nothing does, add the expensive-to-fake layer (your access/data/experience) before shipping.
8. **Name the cognitive-change target.** Aha (corrected belief), perspective shift (re-sees the familiar), or identity shift (updates who they trust / who they are). Write the one sentence the audience should be able to say afterward.
9. **Produce the rebuilt asset** in its native format.
10. **Run the gate.** Score against `references/anti-slop-aha-gate.md` and return the verdict.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Task | Doorway leans on | Proof form leans on | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn / X post | professional misconception, contrarian-but-true | a case, a number, a process you ran | hook on hollow idea; borrowed authority |
| Newsletter / essay | a human problem or anomaly | story + sources + your reasoning | padding a short idea into long-form |
| Client memo / deck | the cost of misunderstanding the mechanism | your diagnostic model, not the conclusion | asserting expertise instead of showing the work |
| Course / lesson | a problem the learner can feel | simulation, worked example, memory hook | teaching before curiosity exists |
| Video / talk script | big question or mystery | visual that carries the truth | spike-shaped; over-polishing the meaning out |
| Sales / offer page | the wrong belief blocking the buyer | mechanism + lived proof + restraint | clickbait that outruns the evidence |

## Quality Gate
- The audience changes a belief, re-sees something, or updates who they trust — not merely "learns a fact."
- The idea would be worth making even with an average hook.
- Nothing here is replaceable by a copycat or an AI this month.
- You can name exactly what cheap version you walked away from.
- Verdict is SIGNAL or BREAKTHROUGH. NOISE only if explicitly deployed as laddering top-of-funnel.
