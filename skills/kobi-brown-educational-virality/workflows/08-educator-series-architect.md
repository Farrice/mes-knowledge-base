# Educator Series Architect

## Role
You build a multi-part educational series that turns a topic into trust over time. Two engines drive the arc (genius.md #11, #12): the **misconception correction** ("what most people think X is, isn't what it is," walked step by step — Kobi's *"Could the Big Bang happen again?"*) creates the *aha*; the **meaning piece** (emotional, perspective-shifting, allowed to stay rough-around-the-edges — Kobi's *"The stars are disappearing"*, filmed under New Zealand's darkest skies) creates the *perspective shift*. Together they move the audience aha → re-seeing → identity shift (coming to trust how you think). The series should also contain at least one **flagship arc you'd make even if it flopped** (genius.md #12: Kobi's ~2-year Voyager golden-record project) — built because it *should exist*, beyond the algorithm.

## Input Required
- Topic territory and audience.
- Platform(s) and series length.
- Desired business or learning outcome.
- Proof assets, field access, constraints.

## Execution
1. **Define the transformation**: What should the audience be able to *explain, re-see, decide, or believe* by the end — not just consume?
2. **Pick the two aha forms per arc**:
   - **Correction beats** — list the widely-held wrong beliefs in the territory. Each becomes a "you think X, it's actually Y" episode, walked step by step (history, mechanism, reveal). This is the *aha* engine.
   - **Meaning beats** — find the 1-2 ideas that matter *emotionally*. Let them breathe; don't over-polish the authenticity out (rough = organic = trusted). This is the *perspective-shift* engine.
3. **Map the misconception ladder**: Order the beliefs so each correction depends on the previous one being dissolved.
4. **Build episodes**: Each gets a doorway, payload, visual anchor (genius.md #9 — make the visual carry the truth: ugly-accurate Python-sim first, beautiful animation second), proof, and bridge.
5. **Assign short/long roles** (genius.md #8): short-form as cheap hypothesis + top-of-funnel; long-form as the legacy resolution. Don't stretch a short idea into a long one (#7).
6. **Reserve a flagship arc** (genius.md #12): the definitive piece only you can make — the niche's missing artifact, backed by access/data/relationships a copycat can't fake (the commoditization escape, #5). Pair it with consistent baseline output (Kobi's 500-day streak).
7. **Add conversion or trust bridge**: connect the series to the offer, newsletter, product, or client path.

> Every episode and the flagship must clear `references/anti-slop-aha-gate.md` — verdict SLOP / NOISE / SIGNAL / BREAKTHROUGH. Target genuine cognitive change (aha → perspective → identity shift), not reach for its own sake.

## Quality Gate
- Series has an arc, not a topic list — later corrections depend on earlier ones.
- Each correction beat resolves one "you think X, it's actually Y"; each meaning beat earns a perspective shift.
- At least one meaning piece is allowed to stay rough/organic, not over-produced.
- The flagship arc is something a competitor would need years + access to replicate.
- Every piece clears the anti-slop/aha gate (BREAKTHROUGH or SIGNAL, never NOISE).
- The series builds trust in *how you think*, toward a clear next step.
