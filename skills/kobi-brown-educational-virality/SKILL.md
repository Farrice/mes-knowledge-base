# Kobi Brown Educational Virality

## Core Thesis
Educational content breaks out when it treats entertainment as the attention doorway, not the payload. The payload stays rigorous: verified, visually understandable, emotionally alive, and paced so the audience leaves *changed* — an aha, a perspective shift, eventually an identity shift — instead of merely amused or informed. In Kobi's words: *"sneak the vegetables in."*

The deeper move, and the reason this work survives a feed full of AI slop and copycats, is **commoditization escape**: when your craft edge gets copied or automated, migrate your differentiation to what can't be faked — earned access, original data, lived proof, verification. This is the spine that turns "educational content" into durable authority.

Kobi Brown's AstroKobi method adds a missing capability to Antigravity: true education that can scale. It is built for educators, coaches, consultants, advisors, founders, analysts, and knowledge workers who need their ideas to travel without turning into shallow edutainment — and, via `/aha-engine`, for *any* task where the goal is genuine cognitive change instead of noise.

## When To Use
- An educational idea is valuable but skippable.
- A coach or consultant needs content that proves expertise without lecturing.
- A knowledge-work topic feels complex, abstract, or "too smart for social."
- The goal is saves, shares, trust, leads, client education, or authority, not empty reach.
- The user wants to connect short-form attention with long-form depth.
- The content needs accuracy, restraint, and legitimacy instead of clickbait.

## When Not To Use
- Pure entertainment with no learning payload.
- Direct-response copy where the main job is immediate purchase conversion.
- Platform-only LinkedIn growth without an educational method problem.
- Content that depends on claims the user cannot verify or defend.

## Master Front Doors
- **`/aha-engine`** — the **universal lens**. Use on any task with educational or entertainment value (yours, client, consulting, social, writing) when the goal is genuine cognitive change, not just an educational deliverable. It architects the doorway, payload, proof, legitimacy, restraint, and the target shift, then returns an anti-slop verdict (SLOP / NOISE / SIGNAL / BREAKTHROUGH) against `references/anti-slop-aha-gate.md`. It hands specialist jobs to the nine workflows.
- **`/educational-breakout-system`** — the **educational router**. Use when the objective is specifically educational/coaching/consulting content and you want the system to pick the right one of the nine specialist workflows. Routes raw or non-educational-framed tasks into `/aha-engine`.

## Quality Bar
Every output must pass:
- Accurate enough to defend.
- Interesting before it teaches.
- Clear enough for a smart beginner.
- Specific enough to feel earned.
- Visualizable, demonstrable, or analogy-backed.
- Legitimacy-building, not attention-harvesting only.
- Platform-native without losing the core lesson.
