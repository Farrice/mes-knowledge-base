# Cross-Domain Deployment Map

> **Universal entry:** for ANY task in this skill, start with `/aha-engine`. Every row below is the same engine pointed at a different surface.
>
> **The outcome, not the format:** the goal is never reach — it's genuine cognitive change. Aim each deliverable up the ladder: **aha** ("what most people think the Big Bang is isn't what it actually is") → **perspective shift** ("the stars are disappearing") → **identity shift** (the audience comes to trust how *you* think). Kobi: *"sneak the vegetables in"* — entertainment is the doorway, a true hard idea is the payload. Run any output through `references/anti-slop-aha-gate.md` (SLOP / NOISE / SIGNAL / BREAKTHROUGH); ship SIGNAL or better.
>
> **Commoditization Escape (the spine of every row):** when your edge gets copied or AI-lowered, migrate differentiation to what's expensive to fake — original access, lived proof, proprietary data/method, real relationships. Kobi did this consciously: *"my distinguishing factor was visuals and communication style... with generative AI even those barriers are being lowered, so it was very much a conscious thing"* → he stopped commentating and started showing up at NASA/ESA/CERN. Ask each row: what here could a copycat or model replicate this quarter? Move your moat below that line.

## Coaching
- Doorway: the question that exposes the client's hidden pattern before you name it.
- Payload (aha): correct the misconception step by step — "what you think the problem is isn't the problem."
- Proof: a story, analogy, or 60-second demo that makes the pattern *visible*, not asserted.
- Legitimacy: show access + verification (the cases you've actually closed), never "trust me, I'm a coach."
- Escape: your moat is the diagnoses only you've earned through reps, not a repeatable framework.

## Consulting
- Doorway: the painful false belief the org is operating on.
- Payload (perspective shift): convert abstract strategy into one visible operating mechanism.
- Proof: proof of *process*, not generic thought leadership; show the cost of misreading the mechanism.
- Restraint: bound the claim, name the line you won't cross for the easy sell — that restraint *is* boardroom trust.
- Escape: original engagement data and first-hand operator access an AI deck can't fabricate.

## Education And Courses
- Doorway: a problem the learner can *feel* before any concept arrives.
- Payload (aha): the real, undiluted mechanism — keep the hard physics, just drop the insider jargon.
- Proof: the "ugly-accurate then beautiful" pipeline — build the correct version first (Kobi's Python sims), then make it engaging without breaking it.
- Sizing: let small ideas stay short; reserve full modules for ideas that carry the weight.
- Escape: short-form/lessons as cheap hypotheses; graduate the signal into the definitive long-form only you can make.

## Advisory And Client Work
- Doorway: pre-sell *how you think*, not the conclusion.
- Payload: show the diagnostic model, walked back to first principles.
- Proof + restraint: accuracy restraint (the honest version once, then "leave it there") earns high-trust relationships.
- Legitimacy: pair every credential signal with an act of verification.
- Escape: real proximity to the decision — you were in the room — beats anyone commentating from outside.

## Offers
- Doorway: the educational asset that reveals the problem the offer solves.
- Payload (perspective shift): teach the mechanism behind the transformation, undiluted.
- Proof: legitimacy proof (access + verification) *before* the CTA, not after.
- Restraint: don't milk one winning angle into five thin follow-ups — name the views/revenue you forgo.
- Escape: a proprietary method or proof portfolio a competitor can't clone by next quarter.

## Knowledge Products
- Doorway: short-form to test which ideas carry real demand (the lab).
- Payload (identity shift): bundle winning ideas into a deeper map; the audience trusts the whole worldview.
- Proof: visual/structural explanation as the curriculum spine — the visual carries the truth.
- Sizing: idea-first; match each idea to its natural container (post vs. essay vs. course vs. book).
- Escape: build the thing the niche is *missing* — the asset that needs years and access to replicate (Kobi's 2-year Voyager project).

## General Writing / Any Knowledge Work
- Doorway: open with the question or anomaly the reader didn't know they had — never "Today I'll explain X."
- Payload (aha): pressure-test the *idea* before the hook; a great hook on a thin idea is still thin. *"What actually makes a good idea fundamentally"* is the real work.
- Proof: one concrete demonstration — example, scale comparison, before/after — that lets the reader redraw the point from memory.
- Restraint + verification: claims bounded, labeled, sources real; correct, then leave it there.
- Escape: original synthesis and lived evidence, not the rephrasable take an AI produces in one prompt.

## Personal Brand / Social
- Doorway: a striking fact or tension that earns the scroll honestly (no clickbait/conspiracy/divisive bait unless that's your *defined* goal — it wasn't Kobi's).
- Payload (identity shift): every post should compound *who they trust*, not just spike a number.
- Proof: show up and document the work — *"earn legitimacy by showing up and no longer just commentating."* Consistency compounds (Kobi's 500-day streak), but reach is top-of-funnel, never the end state.
- Restraint: cover a hot trend truthfully once; refuse the milking sequence even when the math says it works.
- Escape: your real proximity, taste, and track record — the things that don't reduce to a prompt.

## Platform Adaptation
| Platform | Doorway | Payload | Proof | Cognitive-change target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube | Big question or mystery | Complete, undiluted explanation | Visual + story + source depth | Belief corrected, worldview re-seen |
| Shorts / Reels / TikTok | One striking fact or tension | One mechanism (don't stretch) | Fast visual or analogy | One clean aha; funnel to long-form |
| LinkedIn | Professional misconception | Practical insight | Process, case, or earned-access signal | Trust in how you think |
| X | Compressed contrarian claim | Threaded mechanism | Examples + crisp definitions | A reframe that sticks |
| Newsletter | Human story or felt problem | Full lesson | Links, examples, reflection | Perspective shift; identity over time |
| Webinar / Talk | Painful false belief | Framework + application | Live demo, case, Q&A | Decision changed; commitment earned |

> Every cell answers to the gate: a piece can nail doorway + payload + proof and still be NOISE if it produces no aha/perspective/identity shift and rests on a commoditizable edge. Verdict it against `references/anti-slop-aha-gate.md` before shipping.
