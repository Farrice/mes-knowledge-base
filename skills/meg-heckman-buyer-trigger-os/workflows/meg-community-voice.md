# The Community Voice System

Micro-moment #2 as an operating system. The farmers-market frame: two stalls, same product — one vendor silent, one mid-conversation laughing with a customer. "You already know which line you are joining." The comment section under your ads is that stall, and it's the FIRST place a stranger meets the brand — before the store, before the list. Bonus mechanics: platforms reward engaged ads, so the conversations literally make the ads cheaper to run.

## Pre-Flight

1. `skills/meg-heckman-buyer-trigger-os/genius.md` — Trust Mechanics moment 2 + operating notes (named human voice, one-reader-mass-audience), Exemplar 7 ("-Eric" replies)
2. The brand's last 2 weeks of real comments (ads + organic), unanswered included

> **Pre-Flight Gate**: ads or posts must exist with real comments. Nothing to reply to → route to `/meg-factory-loop` (test infrastructure first) or `/meg-micro-moments` (full audit).

## Input Required

- Brand, sub-identity, named human who signs replies
- Voice source (founder's actual texts/replies) or 3 voice decisions
- The comment backlog (paste or screenshots)

## Workflow

### Step 1: THE VOICE CARD
One card the operator (or an LLM in production) can reply from: 3 voice adjectives · energy-matching rule (banter answers banter, sincerity answers sincerity) · signature sign-off ("-Eric" pattern) · never-do list (no "Thanks for reaching out!", no deflect-to-DM except money/private, no punching down — "the joke is WITH the niche") · 3 worked reply pairs stolen from the founder's real register.

### Step 2: CLEAR THE BACKLOG
Reply to EVERY comment from the last week as the brand. Tag-a-friend threads and arguments about the niche's trivia (SHC: trail snacks) are the highest-value threads — join them, don't moderate them. Each reply written for that one person, knowing hundreds of strangers read it deciding if you're real.

### Step 3: THE STANDING SYSTEM
Daily 15-minute reply block on the calendar · LLM-assisted drafting from the voice card (Meg: "ChatGPT and Claude have made this so easy... literally copy and paste") with the named human approving sends · escalation line (complaints/money → human immediately, in the same voice).

## Content Type Adaptations

**Paid ads (core case)**: engagement compounds ad efficiency — track CPM drift on high-reply ads. **Organic social**: same card; replies are the content strategy for accounts under 1k followers. **LinkedIn (Farrice/clients)**: banter dial lower, specificity dial higher; sign with initials. **Marketplace listings (Etsy/Amazon)**: reviews are comments — reply to every one in voice.

## Output Schema

```
COMMUNITY VOICE — [brand] — [date]
VOICE CARD: [adjectives / energy rule / sign-off / never-do / 3 worked pairs]
BACKLOG CLEARED: [N replies drafted — full text, threaded]
STANDING SYSTEM: [daily block, LLM workflow, escalation line]
```

## Example Output

**Context**: MyBPM (EDM streetwear), signer Farrice. Comment: "bro these prices for a tee 💀"

**Reply (voice card: playful, direct, never defensive)**:
> "fair — it's not a $12 blank. it's the tee you wear to the function AND the airport after. but if it's not your moment yet, the group-chat screenshots are free 😂 -F"

**What makes this excellent**: matches the commenter's energy (emoji banter answered in kind), defends with identity ("the function AND the airport") instead of specs, exits warm with a joke that spectators screenshot — written for one skeptic, performed for every stranger scrolling the thread.

## Quality Gate

- Voice card has all five elements; worked pairs come from the founder's real register, not template-speak
- 100% of the backlog answered — skipping the hard ones is the tell
- No reply reads support-desk; energy matching visible
- Escalation line preserves the voice (no "please contact our team")
