# Avatar Bridge

Meg's layer finds the person through what they buy: the casual hiker "genuinely proud" of a 30-minute stroll revealed herself through a shirt, not a survey. Wallet-validated — but purchase psychology is not the full psyche. A shirt tells you what someone wants said about them — not what they fear at 3am, which purchases failed them, or where self-story contradicts behavior. When a brand finds its person through product work and now needs copy, offers, or ads, this workflow converts the sub-identity into avatar-manifold seed material without flattening the behavioral moments in transit.

## Pre-Flight
Read these files before executing:
1. `skills/meg-heckman-buyer-trigger-os/genius.md` (Market Mechanics; Hidden Knowledge 2, 3, 6)
2. `skills/luke-iha-avatar-machine/SKILL.md` (what the manifold needs; Phase 0 GROUND contract)
3. The source artifact — the completed dossier or audit, in full

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: The source must contain at least three behavioral moments and one named belonging community. Demographics where moments should be ("women 25–34 who hike") = not bridge-ready — run `/meg-sub-identity-map` first. You cannot translate what was never captured.

## Input Required
- A completed sub-identity dossier (`/meg-sub-identity-map` output) — the standard input — OR `/meg-trigger-audit` results with behavioral moments and mirror/poster verdicts
- Downstream objective (VSL, ads, email, offer) — the `--objective` flag
- Sales evidence if any — what sold, forward signals, repeat buyers (belonging retains, HK-6)
- Own brand, or client discovery (their product sells and nobody knows why)?

## Workflow

### Step 1: Inventory the Source Artifacts (verbatim)
Quote from the dossier/audit — never paraphrase: the three behavioral-moment scenes, the quiet pride nobody validates, the inside jokes and belonging language, every mirror-vs-poster verdict, the familiar seed/bank, every involuntary-forward target ("I need to send this to Sarah"). "Financially ruined by my golden retriever" loses everything as "pet owners with high spending" — the specificity IS the cargo.

### Step 2: Run the Translation Table
Six rows, each mapping a concrete Meg artifact to the concrete avatar-machine input it seeds:

| Meg artifact (quoted from source) | Avatar-machine input | Translation rule |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioral moments (dossier scenes 1–3) | `/pain-matrix` seeds + day-in-the-life scenes | Each moment names a pain dimension to plot; the scene is day-in-the-life raw footage, kept first-person |
| The quiet pride nobody validates | `/core-wound` adjacency + resonance material | "They're genuinely proud of that" — the pride IS the unmet recognition need; dismissed pride points at the wound without claiming excavation |
| Belonging-community language (inside jokes, phrases) | `/market-pickup-lines` material + voice-of-customer floor (`/buyer-sourcer`) | Their phrases become hook candidates; floor: no manifold language may be LESS specific than the community already is |
| Mirror-vs-poster verdicts | `/resonance-hierarchy` placement | Mirrors = Identity-layer resonance — wallet-backed "that's me" tops the pyramid; failed posters = low-resonance themes the lead never opens with |
| Familiar bank / familiar seed | Cultural-context inventory | What the buyer already knows = the manifold's reference world + the familiar half of Epiphany Threshold calibration |
| Involuntary-forward targets ("send this to Sarah") | Relationship-map nodes | Who validates, who gifts, who gets tagged — the forward is the leading indicator (HK-3); these names are the avatar's social graph |

Row discipline: every row cites its source line; rows without a quotable artifact are deleted, not invented.

### Step 3: Flag the Gaps (what this seed does NOT contain)
Purchase psychology ≠ full psyche. Name what Meg's layer never captured — avatar-machine's job, not a dossier defect:
- **Deep fears / Core Wound depth.** The pride points AT the wound; only `/core-wound`'s Ontological Resource excavation establishes the refraction (annihilation/abandonment/death). GROUND note: the belonging community is the dig site — scrape vent threads, not celebration threads.
- **Failed solutions history.** Merch shows what resonates, never what disappointed — prior purchases, why they failed, who gets blamed: `/dissolution-forge` material. GROUND note: competitor-offer reviews, refund stories, "I tried everything" posts.
- **Identity contradictions.** Say-versus-do never surfaces in a purchase — buyers show only the self they want billboarded. GROUND note: compare community self-description against complaint language; the gap is the inventory.

### Step 4: Note the Reverse Path (one paragraph, pointer only)
The bridge runs both directions. An existing avatar manifold can seed `/meg-concept-sprint`: read Pain Matrix dimensions and Identity-layer resonance back out as behavioral moments, feed them to the familiar/twist machinery, and get concepts the manifold predicts will land. Copy-side engagements that later need merch should not rebuild the buyer — translate backward. Pointer only; the sprint owns execution.

### Step 5: Assemble the Handoff Package
Produce the seed doc plus the invocation note: `/avatar-machine "[market]" "[product]" --objective "[objective]"`. Phase 0 GROUND auto-fires and stays ON — the seed enriches cold-start research, never replaces it. It is prior context, not a `--voc-file` — it does not qualify for the `--no-ground` import path. The manifold must still earn its own evidence.

## Quality Gate
> **🛡️ Bridge Integrity Check**: review before handing off.
- Every translation row maps a CONCRETE Meg artifact (quoted from source) to a CONCRETE avatar-machine input (named workflow or manifold section) — no vibes rows.
- Gap flags present and honest — no core-wound depth, failed-solution history, or contradiction inventory claimed from product data; the pride-to-wound row reads adjacency, never excavation.
- Avatar-machine's Phase 0 GROUND explicitly preserved in the invocation note; the package never recommends `--no-ground`.
- Behavioral-moment language survived verbatim — if "the one at the back of the group" became "casual outdoor enthusiasts" in transit, the bridge failed (HK-2).
- Her numbers, if cited, carry UNCONFIRMED labels.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Treating the bridge as the avatar.** A six-row seed is not a manifold — it is one input among many GROUND gathers. Recovery: re-read the gap flags; three load-bearing layers are explicitly absent.
- **Flattening behavioral moments into personas during translation.** Manifold vocabulary (dimensions, hierarchies, archetypes) invites abstraction, and abstraction is how mirrors die. Recovery: Step 1's verbatim rule — quoted scenes in, quoted scenes out.
- **Skipping the research phase because "we already know the buyer."** You know what they buy, not what they fled. Recovery: GROUND stays on; the seed makes research sharper, not optional.
- **One-way thinking.** Manifold-first engagements that later need products rebuild the buyer from scratch. Recovery: Step 4 — read the manifold back out as behavioral moments and sprint.
