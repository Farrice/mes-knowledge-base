# Hidden Knowledge (HK-1 … HK-12) — Meg Heckman

Tacit insights she demonstrates but never teaches directly. Workflows cite these by number.

- **HK-1 — Craft quality inversely predicts POD conversion.** "As a piece of art, design A is the better design... wins every design competition." It sold zero (claim). Optimizing for beauty manufactures posters. Implication: aesthetic review criteria actively mislead; trigger criteria only.
- **HK-2 — Identity is coded behaviorally, never demographically.** Never "hikers" — "the one who's always at the back of the group, the one who needs a water break every 10 minutes." The unit of targeting is a recognizable moment, not a census field.
- **HK-3 — The involuntary forward is the leading indicator.** "You don't just think, 'I want that.' You think, 'I need my friend to see this.'" The send-to-Sarah reflex predicts scale before sales data exists. Score it by naming the recipient.
- **HK-4 — The trust gate is the cart, not the checkout.** Initiate-checkout (5-6%) is where legitimacy collapses: shipping shock, missing reviews, absent return policy. Fix the cart page's proof before touching checkout flow.
- **HK-5 — ROAS 2.0 is a floor, not a win.** Break-even, not success. Scaling triggers at 2.5+. Recalibrates what "ads are working" means.
- **HK-6 — Humor acquires; belonging retains.** "Belonging and pride layered underneath the humor... turns a one-time buyer into somebody who comes back." Repeat buyers ("five, six, seven times" — claim) ARE the business model at 10-20% margins.
- **HK-7 — Identity resonance overrides UX friction.** "Objectively terrible websites... convert like crazy because people identified with the product so strongly." Sequence: identity fit first, infrastructure polish second.
- **HK-8 — Benchmark against Gap/Nike/REI, not other POD stores.** Incumbents set buyer expectations (drops, seasons, collections). Measuring against Etsy peers measures the wrong thing.
- **HK-9 — The unprofitability window is calibration cost.** Two unprofitable months on a brand she already knew how to run (claim) — "we dialed in the audience, the designs, the funnel." Early loss ≠ broken model; it's tuition with a planned end.
- **HK-10 — The OS does not change at scale.** Same 5 steps at $0 and at $861K/30d (claim). Scale adds volume, never process complexity. Resist the redesign instinct at growth inflections.
- **HK-11 — Mobile CTA position is a revenue metric.** "Where my add to cart button actually lands on a mobile device" — audited inside conversion diagnostics, not design preference.
- **HK-12 — Humor embeds at brand-architecture level.** Sloth Hiking Club IS the joke (slow animal, fast sport). The name does trigger work before any design exists; every design inherits the twist.
