# Genius Patterns (16) — Meg Heckman

Numbered references used by workflows ("Pattern 7" = this file). Full treatment: `extractions/meg-heckman/mastery-extraction.md`. Layers: 1 MARKET · 2 DESIGN · 3 FACTORY · 4 DIAGNOSTICS.

## The Six Triggers (Layer 2 core — vocabulary contract)

**1. Identity Beats Utility (Identity Signal)** — "People do not buy the shirt, they buy what the shirt says about them." Execute: replace "is it pretty?" with "what does this let the buyer say about themselves?" — demand a first-person sentence. Deploy: first gate on any product/design/offer/headline.

**2. Recognition Creates Emotion (Recognition Speed)** — "People scroll fast. They do not decode designs... they simply react." Execute: "make the right person feel seen faster," never "make the customer think harder." Needs-explanation = kill. Deploy: concept review, cold-traffic surfaces.

**3. Specificity Sells (Specificity)** — "Dog mom is broad. Financially ruined by my golden retriever is very specific... It turns a topic into a person." Execute: narrow past comfortable; the buyer must feel it was made FOR them. Deploy: niche, briefs, copy, hooks.

**4. People Buy Social Currency (Social Currency Moment)** — "Beginners design for product pages, operators design for the moment after the shirt arrives." Execute: test for the future moment — who laughs, who says "that is SO you," who receives it as a gift. "A product sits on a page. A conversation starter moves through people." Deploy: concept scoring, spend decisions.

**5. Familiar Plus Unexpected Wins (Familiar/Twist Pair)** — "Only familiar = boring. Only unexpected = confusing. Both = stop." Execute: start from what the buyer already knows, twist sharper/truer — "thought it before, never seen it on a shirt." Deploy: generation, hooks, thumbnails.

**6. Emotion Beats Logic (Emotion-First Reason)** — "The feeling comes first and then the reason comes second." Execute: create the feeling; let the buyer self-supply justification ("I've been meaning to buy another hoodie anyway"). Deploy: listing copy, offer framing, pricing courage.

## Design Mechanics (Layer 2 extensions)

**7. Mirror vs. Poster** — "Design A is a poster. Design B is a mirror." Execute: classify before critiquing; posters (category descriptions) die or get revised toward mirrors (lived behavioral moments). Deploy: THE briefing gate, all formats.

**8. The 50ms Gate** — "10 things fighting for your eye... in 50 milliseconds, that's chaos." Execute: 1-2 focal elements; describable in 5 words; "one thing loudly rather than three things quietly." (Her stat: 1-2-element designs = 56% of sales — UNCONFIRMED.) Deploy: pre-upload, ad creative, heroes.

**9. Conceptual Interrupt (Orienting Response)** — "It's called the orienting response, and it's involuntary." Execute: surprise in the IDEA, execution stays in-world; "the interrupt has to lead somewhere... it's the door. It's not the room." Deploy: pattern-interrupt design, hooks. Anti-case: AI 27-finger weirdness.

**10. Layered Emotion** — "Funny and it belongs and it's pride all layered together." Execute: name layers 2-3 of every humorous concept; humor acquires, belonging+pride retain. Deploy: concept upgrades, brand personality.

## Market Selection (Layer 1)

**11. Saturation = Demand Proof** — "A saturated niche... has proven demand." Pizza analogy. Execute: enter proven markets deliberately; 0.01% TAM framing dissolves scarcity panic. Deploy: niche selection, client positioning.

**12. The Sub-Identity Gap** — "We went after somebody completely different, the casual hiker... genuinely proud of that." Execute: map the dominant served identity; build for the adjacent ignored person. "Winning brands put identity on their shirts" (vs decoration). Billboard test. Deploy: brand architecture, market entry.

## Factory (Layer 3)

**13. The 5-Step Loop** — "Generate, test, scale or cut, email, repeat. Five things. That is the entire operating system." Execute: paid tests (48h verdicts), kill-by-data ("the market does not care what you love"), email 2-3×/week (list = pre-paid traffic), identical loop at every scale. Deploy: brand ops install.

**14. Design Volume Math** — "Your one breakout design might be design 91 or 312 or 800." Execute: depth before breadth (one product type); treat early failure as sampling error; never stop generating. Deploy: cadence planning, quit-prevention.

## Diagnostics (Layer 4)

**15. Metric-as-Single-Question** — "CPC is really just one question: does this creative make people stop?" Execute: 6 metrics, 6 questions, 6 stage-fixes (card: `references/metric-thresholds.md`); ignore the rest. Deploy: weekly review, store audits.

**16. Symptom Displacement Diagnosis** — "Most ROAS problems are actually Shopify problems disguised as ad problems." Execute: trace upstream first; mockup swap before targeting; cart trust before checkout; cohesion before upsells. Deploy: funnel debugging.
