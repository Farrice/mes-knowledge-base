## Worked Transfers (from source + production)

**Offers / landing pages** — Generic: "we help businesses use AI." Trigger-transferred: **"Your AI is producing. Your buyers are not moving."** Names the private failure → recognition before explanation. (Production proof, Codex prior art.)

**Newsletter/content positioning** — The sub-identity gap = the reader nobody writes for. Hiking's "casual hiker" ≈ the Invisible Expert in the expertise niche: already excellent, no external signal, quietly proud. Belonging is the retention engine; the subscription says something about the subscriber.

**Email subject lines** — 50ms = one concept per line; interrupt must deliver in the body or it buys unsubscribes, not opens ("the door must lead to a room").

**SaaS** — "Crowded category" = proven demand; serve the team identity incumbents ignore. Trial funnel = her 6 stages renamed (activation = add-to-cart; upgrade-page visit = initiate checkout; "pricing page UX problem disguised as acquisition problem").

**Coaching/services** — Decoration vs identity: generic frameworks are decoration; the coach who IS the methodology is identity. Repeat clients = belonging renewals, not tactic novelty.

**Programmatic content/SEO** — Design volume math is structurally identical: "publish 500 pages, find the 12 that rank"; the breakout might be page 312.

**Real-estate / local services listings** — Mirror-vs-poster on property copy: "3BR family home" (poster) vs the behavioral moment of the buyer's Saturday morning in it (mirror). Emotion first, square footage last.
