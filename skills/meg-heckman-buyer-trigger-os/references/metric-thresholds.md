# The 6-Metric Diagnostic Card — Meg Heckman

> **Calibration defaults** (POD / Meta ads / her suppliers, 2026) — NOT laws. Recalibrate per category, channel, and margin structure. "The rest of the metrics I simply ignore."

| # | Metric | Healthy | The single question | When weak → fix THIS stage first |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **CPC** | $0.55–0.75 | Does this creative make people stop? | Presentation: MOCKUP SWAP first ("the exact same design on a different mockup and the CPCs immediately started to come down"), targeting last |
| 2 | **ROAS** | 2.0 = break-even FLOOR · 2.5–3.5 = scale trigger | How many dollars back per dollar spent? | Usually displaced: "Most ROAS problems are actually Shopify problems disguised as ad problems" — check conversion layer before ad account |
| 3 | **Add-to-Cart** | 7–8% | Do they actually want the product? | Product images, description, pricing, mobile CTA position (HK-11) |
| 4 | **Initiate Checkout** | 5–6% | Does this store feel trustworthy? | CART-page trust (HK-4): shipping cost clarity, delivery windows, reviews, return policy, sizing confidence |
| 5 | **Conversion** | 3–4% | How easy is it to click buy? | Checkout friction: load speed, flow confusion, payment options, mobile. "The closest thing Shopify gives you to a real-time profit indicator" |
| 6 | **AOV** | $45+ | How much profit per order? | Collection cohesion + shipping arbitrage ("$4.75 for the first item, then $1.99 for the second" — supplier-specific) BEFORE upsell apps. "AOV is where the margin lives" |

**Diagnosis order**: funnel order, first failing stage wins, everything downstream is noise until it's fixed. One stage, one fix — never a list of ten.

**Scale call**: all stages healthy + ROAS ≥2.5 → "I'm bumping my budgets." All stages healthy + no profit → the problem is upstream of the funnel: the design has no trigger → `/meg-trigger-audit`. A funnel cannot save a poster.
