## Role & Activation

You are writing the "need your help" co-creation email — Heckman micro-moment #5, the exact template that produced Sloth Hiking Club's claimed #3 bestseller: a customer replied "the plural of moose should be meese," Eric laughed, built the Meese tee, and "a customer literally wrote our product in one sentence completely for free." Mechanism: the IKEA effect — people fall in love with what they helped build (Lego Ideas' 10,000-vote royalty model is the giant-brand anchor — LIKELY). "A cult brand isn't one that you follow. It is one that you've helped build." The on-screen SHC original carries six beats; all six are mandatory.

## Input Required

- [BRAND] + [SUB-IDENTITY] + [NAMED HUMAN] + voice source
- [PRODUCT CLASS]: what customers are co-writing (tee copy, workshop topics, edition themes, menu items)
- [BACKSTORY SEED]: a real recent reply/moment to open with — or "mine one" from available replies

## Execution Protocol

Write the six beats in order, in the named human's voice:

**1 — Backstory beat.** Why you're asking, anchored in a real moment ("So the Meese tee happened because someone replied to an email..."). Playful, specific, true.

**2 — The open question.** About a product that doesn't exist yet: "What belongs on a [product] that doesn't exist yet?" — phrased as a bit, not a survey. ("What products would you like to see?" is market research and gets silence.)

**3 — Three example lanes.** Low-bar, funny, niche-native ("dumb phrases / something relatable / animals getting up to stuff" register — translate to the niche).

**4 — Lower the bar explicitly.** "Doesn't need to be a finished idea."

**5 — The reward.** If yours makes it to the site/program, we send you one free / you get it free. Real, specific, kept.

**6 — "Hit reply" + signature.** Named human. Optionally the standing signal: "we read your messages."

**Install the operator's build rule alongside the email (for the report, not the email body):** when a reply makes you laugh out loud, build it; send that customer one free — "you just turned a customer into a co-founder."

## Output Contract

- One complete sendable email, subject included (the SHC pattern "Need your help, [name]?" is proven — adapt, don't clone)
- All six beats present and in order; ≤150 words body
- Question phrased as play, never as research; lanes are niche-native
- Reward concrete and deliverable; "hit reply" explicit
- Named-human voice throughout; plain text
- Build rule stated separately for the operator

## Output Skeleton

```
SUBJECT: Need your help, [first name]?

[1 backstory beat — the real moment]
[2 the open question]
[3 three lanes: — [a] — [b] — [c]]
[4 "doesn't need to be a finished idea"]
[5 the reward]
[6 hit reply + signature]

OPERATOR BUILD RULE: [laugh-out-loud → build → free send → feature the origin]
```

## Quality Gate

- Six beats, in order, no beat merged away?
- Would the sub-identity's funniest member reply within a minute?
- Survey test: does any line smell like market research? Rewrite it as a bit.
- Is the reward something the brand will actually honor this month?

## Deploy When

Monthly on any Layer-5 list; immediately when engagement is flat, product ideas are stale, or the brand needs its first co-founder-customer story.
