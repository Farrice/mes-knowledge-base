## Role & Activation

You are converting Meg Heckman's product-purchase-psychology findings into avatar-manifold seed material. Her layer finds the person through what they buy — the casual hiker "genuinely proud" of a 30-minute stroll revealed herself through a shirt, not a survey. Wallet-validated, but purchase psychology is not the full psyche: a shirt tells you what someone wants said about them, not what they fear at 3am, which purchases failed them, or where self-story contradicts behavior. This workflow converts a sub-identity into avatar-manifold seed material without flattening the behavioral moments in transit.

## Input Required

- [SOURCE ARTIFACT]: a completed sub-identity dossier (standard input) OR trigger-audit results with behavioral moments and mirror/poster verdicts, in full
- [DOWNSTREAM OBJECTIVE]: VSL / ads / email / offer / content strategy
- [SALES EVIDENCE]: what sold, forward signals, repeat buyers, if any
- [ENGAGEMENT TYPE]: own brand, or client discovery (product sells and nobody knows why)

## Execution Protocol

**Pre-flight gate**: the source must contain at least three behavioral moments and one named belonging community. Demographics where moments should be ("women 25–34 who hike") are not bridge-ready — flag that a sub-identity map needs to run first. You cannot translate what was never captured.

**Step 1 — Inventory the Source Artifacts (verbatim).** Quote from the dossier/audit directly, never paraphrase: the three behavioral-moment scenes, the quiet pride nobody validates, the inside jokes and belonging language, every mirror-vs-poster verdict, the familiar seed/bank, every involuntary-forward target ("I need to send this to Sarah"). "Financially ruined by my golden retriever" loses everything as "pet owners with high spending" — the specificity IS the cargo. Paraphrasing at this step is the failure mode this workflow exists to prevent.

**Step 2 — Run the Translation Table.** Map six concrete Meg artifacts to six concrete avatar-machine inputs, each row citing its source line:

| Meg artifact (quoted from source) | Avatar-machine input | Translation rule |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioral moments (dossier scenes 1–3) | Pain-matrix seeds + day-in-the-life scenes | Each moment names a pain dimension to plot; the scene is day-in-the-life raw footage, kept first-person |
| The quiet pride nobody validates | Core-wound adjacency + resonance material | "They're genuinely proud of that" — the pride IS the unmet recognition need; dismissed pride points at the wound without claiming excavation |
| Belonging-community language (inside jokes, phrases) | Market pickup-line material + voice-of-customer floor | Their phrases become hook candidates; floor rule — no manifold language may be LESS specific than the community already is |
| Mirror-vs-poster verdicts | Resonance-hierarchy placement | Mirrors = Identity-layer resonance — wallet-backed "that's me" tops the pyramid; failed posters = low-resonance themes the lead never opens with |
| Familiar bank / familiar seed | Cultural-context inventory | What the buyer already knows = the manifold's reference world + the familiar half of downstream calibration |
| Involuntary-forward targets ("send this to Sarah") | Relationship-map nodes | Who validates, who gifts, who gets tagged — the forward is the leading indicator; these names are the avatar's social graph |

Row discipline: every row cites its source line; rows without a quotable artifact are deleted, not invented.

**Step 3 — Flag the Gaps (what this seed does NOT contain).** Purchase psychology is not the full psyche — name what Meg's layer never captured explicitly, as the downstream avatar work's job, never claimed here: (a) Deep fears/core-wound depth — the pride points AT the wound but only dedicated wound-excavation work establishes the refraction; GROUND note: the belonging community is the dig site, scrape vent threads, not celebration threads. (b) Failed-solutions history — merch shows what resonates, never what disappointed (prior purchases, why they failed, who gets blamed); GROUND note: competitor-offer reviews, refund stories, "I tried everything" posts. (c) Identity contradictions — say-versus-do never surfaces in a purchase, buyers show only the self they want billboarded; GROUND note: compare community self-description against complaint language, the gap is the inventory.

**Step 4 — Note the Reverse Path (one paragraph, pointer only).** The bridge runs both directions. An existing avatar manifold can seed concept generation: read pain-matrix dimensions and identity-layer resonance back out as behavioral moments, feed them to the familiar/twist machinery, and get concepts the manifold predicts will land. Copy-side engagements that later need merch should not rebuild the buyer from scratch — translate backward. State this as a pointer only; downstream concept work owns the execution.

**Step 5 — Assemble the Handoff Package.** Produce the seed doc plus the invocation note pointing at the downstream avatar-building process with the stated market, product, and objective. GROUND research must stay ON and explicit in the note — the seed enriches cold-start research, never replaces it; it is prior context, not a substitute for primary research. The manifold must still earn its own evidence.

**Content Type Adaptation**: Merch brand → full avatar (standard path) — full workflow as-is, dossier is primary source, sales data weights resonance placement. Client discovery (product sells, nobody knows WHY) — audit the winning catalog FIRST to recover behavioral moments from what's selling; reverse-engineer the person from its mirrors; gap flags become the discovery agenda. Content positioning (newsletter/LinkedIn lane) — sub-identity → audience avatar; belonging language → hook bank; forward targets → shareability; objective = content strategy, not VSL.

## Output Contract

- Six-row translation table, each row citing its source line verbatim
- Three gap flags stated explicitly with GROUND notes for each — never claimed as covered
- Reverse-path note included as a pointer
- Invocation note preserving GROUND research as ON, never recommending a no-research shortcut
- Behavioral-moment language preserved verbatim through translation — no abstraction into generic personas

## Quality Gate

- Does every translation row map a CONCRETE Meg artifact (quoted from source) to a CONCRETE avatar-machine input — no vibes rows?
- Are gap flags present and honest — no core-wound depth, failed-solution history, or contradiction inventory claimed from product data?
- Is downstream GROUND research explicitly preserved in the invocation note, with no recommendation to skip it?
- Did behavioral-moment language survive verbatim — did "the one at the back of the group" stay intact rather than becoming "casual outdoor enthusiasts" in transit?
- Are her numbers, if cited, carrying UNCONFIRMED labels?

## Deploy When

Feeding a product/niche angle into avatar-manifold work — a merch or POD brand that found its person through sales data and now needs copy, offers, or ads built on that person.
