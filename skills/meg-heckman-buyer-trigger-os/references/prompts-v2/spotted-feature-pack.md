## Role & Activation

You are producing Heckman micro-moments #6-7 as one pack: the customer-feature "spotted" email plus the never-skip-a-week rhythm installation. "Your customer's photo is a review with a face" — Nielsen's 92% peer-trust finding (per Meg — LIKELY) is the mechanism; GoPro's Million Dollar Challenge (42,000 customer clips as the yearly ad — LIKELY) is the giant-brand anchor, but "you don't need 42,000 clips. You need one photo of one customer celebrated properly." Sloth Hiking Club's version: "sloths have been spotted" — real customers in tees on real trails, whole message "You wore them, we noticed. Tag us and you'll be featured next." And the rhythm that keeps all of it believable: "Trust is rhythm. A brand is like a heartbeat. Nobody notices it until it stops" — weekly drop, zero missed weeks, shipped even when nobody claps (Costco $1.50 hot dog since 1985 as the unbroken-promise anchor — LIKELY).

## Input Required

- [BRAND] + [SUB-IDENTITY] + [NAMED HUMAN]
- [CUSTOMER EVIDENCE]: photos/UGC on hand, or the last 5 reachable customers to request from
- [CADENCE STATE]: current drop/posting rhythm, last drop date, realistic weekly capacity

## Execution Protocol

**1 — The spotted email.** Feature ONE real customer properly: name (permission + thank-you noted as required), where the product was spotted, what it says about THEM. The customer is the message; the product is set dressing — inverting this produces a catalog with faces, which is a fail. Close with the standing invitation: "You wore them, we noticed. Tag us and you'll be featured next."

**2 — The harvest kit.** The 5-customer photo-request DM (short, warm, named-human voice, zero "UGC campaign" smell) + one review-flow tweak that rewards photo/video submissions.

**3 — The rhythm install.** Named drop day on the calendar · what ships weekly (size explicitly declared irrelevant: "the size of the drop matters way less than the fact that it never misses") · the no-applause clause in writing: "the drop happens anyways" · a 10-minute fallback version of the weekly artifact for overloaded weeks · the abandoned-store warning for the client brief: "Would you put your credit card into an abandoned store?"

**4 — Wire the flywheel row.** One weekly operating line: [drop day] — [what ships] — [which customer gets celebrated] — [where new photos come from].

## Output Contract

- Spotted email: complete, sendable, customer-centered, signed; permission/thank-you flagged
- Photo-request DM ≤50 words + review-flow tweak, both in voice
- Rhythm: named day + weekly artifact + written no-applause clause + 10-min fallback
- Flywheel row connecting features → photos → next features
- All stats/anchors labeled LIKELY; SHC figures UNCONFIRMED


## Quality Gate

- Is the customer the hero and the product set dressing? (Count product mentions vs customer mentions.)
- Would the featured customer screenshot the email and send it to someone?
- Does the rhythm survive a bad week — is the fallback genuinely 10 minutes?
- Permission + thank-you flagged before any name is used?

## Deploy When

A brand has ≥1 real customer and either zero UGC motion or a broken posting rhythm — and monthly thereafter as the feature cadence.
