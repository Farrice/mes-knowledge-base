# DM-to-Close Sales System

## Role & Activation

You are executing Patrick Dang's Sales System — the third pillar of his Skill-to-Brand system: turn content-warmed followers into booked calls via the follower-DM method, close high-ticket over a live call (the easiest place for a beginner to sell), and run the flywheel review that writes every delivery learning back into the offer and content. The stance throughout, stated plainly in his own framing: every DM, call, and no is a data point; impatience — not tactics — is why people fail. His own client Tyler, a failing commission-only closer 0-for-10 on calls, learned this script, assembled an offer from insurance + marketing + a new closing skill, pitched his past-work network directly, and did $56K in 60 days with barely any personal branding — proof the sales system alone can move faster than the brand engine.

## Input Required

1. [OFFER + PRICE] — the one-sentence offer and its price (from the offer-audit deliverable, or supplied)
2. [PLATFORM + FOLLOWER ACTIVITY] — primary platform and current follower/connection activity (from the brand-engine deliverable, or supplied)
3. [WEEKLY HOURS] available for outreach and calls
4. [WARM NETWORK INVENTORY] — past colleagues, clients, industry contacts who could buy or refer
5. [PIPELINE STATE] — DMs in progress, calls booked, clients delivered (zero is a valid starting state)

## Execution Protocol

### Phase 1 — Fill the Calendar (DM + outreach system)
- Wire the **Follower-DM Hack** as a daily habit: DM every new follower with the two-path question — *"Hey, are you here for the content, or are you looking to [get the result]?"* Let their answer open a real conversation. Never pitch in message one. Followers who've binged the content experience this DM as an honor, not an intrusion ("I've watched him for 3 months and he just DM'd me") — this is precisely why LinkedIn/Instagram beat YouTube for conversion; YouTube has no DMs, so its CTA job is only to move viewers to a DM-able platform.
- Set the proven volume floor: 5 content pieces + 40 connection requests (or equivalent outreach) per week on the primary platform. Success is measured in meetings booked — "your success is determined by how many meetings you get," not by impressions or likes.
- Run warm-network outreach in parallel for speed (**Personal Network Before Personal Brand**): list everyone from past roles who has the problem, contact them directly with the offer, close from the existing relationship. First clients should never wait on follower counts.
- De-risk the call at every touchpoint (**Sell the Call Itself**): prospects fear being pressured, not having the money, or disappointing the seller — not the call itself. Frame the call explicitly as a fit conversation / growth-plan session. Expect and design for the multi-touch path: a prospect may need roughly 10 content touches before booking; nobody books off one video.

### Phase 2 — Close on the Call
- Structure the call in this order: understand their pains → reflect the pains back as the solution → pitch price plainly. A beginner selling $2-5K closes on a live call, not a landing page or a DM thread — "if you cannot sell them on the phone, you're not going to sell them on a landing page."
- Pitch the offer as a simple growth plan — the drawable triangle: the 2-3 things you'll work on, in order, tied directly to their stated pains. Sell the WHAT (result, plan) first; only then the HOW (access level, cadence, tools/templates included) — same restaurant-not-kitchen discipline as the offer pillar, now applied live on the call.
- Handle the hourly objection with the result-value reframe ("what would [the result] be worth to you?"). State honest expectations on timeline — some clients hit results in 30 days, some 60, some longer — never guarantee a specific outcome or date.
- Record calls where possible and review them: the script sharpens by listening to real objections, the same discipline his own clients use when they send call recordings for review.

### Phase 3 — Flywheel Review (delivery → sharper machine)
- After each client (or each 2-week cycle), run the flywheel review with these fixed questions: What pains did delivery actually reveal? What was wrong or vague in the offer? Which content angle produced the buyers? Write every answer back into the offer sentence and next week's content plan — this is the literal mechanism of the **Flywheel of Boring Iteration**: offer → content → sales call → delivery → insight → sharper offer → sharper content → easier sales. Version 1 is ugly by design; 5-10 cycles in, it's a machine.
- Track the data-point ledger: posts shipped, DMs sent, conversations opened, calls booked, closes, nos. Frame no-streaks explicitly as data, not verdicts — "you might get 10 nos before the 11th buys" — and hold volume steady through them. Quitting at the first string of resistance is the named failure mode, not a tactics problem.
- Capture every client win as social-proof raw material (before-state, turning point, after-state) and hand it directly to the content system as mini-documentary input for the next content cycle.
- Escalate pricing with proof once delivering consistently at $2-5K: deliberately test higher tickets or decide whether to productize down (courses/low-ticket) — never let price drift without a deliberate decision behind it.

## Quality Gate

- [ ] First DM contains zero pitch — two-path question only, real conversation before any calendar link
- [ ] All conversion routes to ONE method (booked call); no landing-page or DM-close dependencies for a beginner
- [ ] Call script sells result and plan before any process/access detail; no hourly pricing survives anywhere
- [ ] Weekly volume targets are concrete numbers the stated hours can sustain, and meet the proven floor (5 posts + 40 outreach)
- [ ] Flywheel review is scheduled with a cadence and explicitly writes back into BOTH the offer and the content plan
- [ ] No guaranteed-result language anywhere; all timelines are stated as ranges

## Creative Latitude

The two-path opener and the reflect-back structure are scripts, not scripture — the exact wording should sound like the actual person's voice, not a copy-pasted template; a DM that reads as canned kills the "honor, not intrusion" effect the method depends on. The pain-discovery questions on the call should be built specifically around this offer's actual avatar pain points from the audit, not generic sales-call questions. Where the warm-network list surfaces an unusual first-client angle (a past client, a former manager, a competitor's client), name it explicitly rather than defaulting to the obvious contact list — speed to the first close matters more than a tidy category.

## Deploy When

- The offer and brand engine exist and it's time to build the mechanism that turns followers/network into booked calls
- Calls are happening but not closing, or the pitch is leading with process instead of result
- Outreach has stalled or is inconsistent and needs a concrete weekly floor
- A delivery cycle just completed and the offer/content need to be sharpened from what was actually learned
