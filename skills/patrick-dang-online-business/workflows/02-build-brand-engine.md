# Build the Personal Brand Engine

## Role

You are executing Patrick Dang's Personal Brand Engine: a deliberately constrained content system (one traffic source, one conversion method, one offer) that attracts the ideal buyer — not just followers — using contrast positioning, an uncopyable origin story, and a weekly rhythm across problem, framework, and social-proof content. Offer clarity is a prerequisite: content built before the offer attracts the wrong people.

## Input Required

1. The one-sentence transformation offer (from workflow 01, or supplied)
2. Avatar and where they hang out (B2B professional vs. consumer niche)
3. Available weekly hours for content (be honest — the system must survive a 9-5)
4. The person's real backstory: before-state, failures along the way, turning point, current after-state
5. The incumbent voices in the niche: who currently teaches this, and what they look/sound like
6. Comfort with video (long-form on camera vs. text/short-form first)

## Workflow

### Phase 1 — Platform Constraint + Positioning
- Apply the One-One-One constraint: select ONE primary platform matched to the avatar (LinkedIn for B2B, Instagram for B2C consumer niches), ONE conversion method (book a call — no website required, just a calendar link), ONE offer. Reject any multi-platform plan from zero; each platform is its own skill and even the biggest brands staff one specialist per platform.
- If hours allow, sanction the only approved expansion — the two-platform stack: one long-form YouTube video per week (trust engine, evergreen compounding, binge path) + five short-form posts per week on the DM-able platform (discovery + conversion). All CTAs funnel to the same call link; YouTube CTAs push viewers to the DM-able platform since YouTube has no DMs.
- Build the contrast positioning: profile the incumbent archetype (age, distance from the trenches, tone, flash level), then position this person at the contrast points — younger/closer to the work, different background, different look, and deliberately anti-flash if the avatar is a skilled professional repelled by Lambo marketing. Add the taste layer as a named advantage: fonts, thumbnails, lighting, site aesthetics tip decisions between otherwise-equal options.

### Phase 2 — Origin Story Construction
- Draft the origin story with the three required components: (1) clear before/after transformation that hits the avatar's exact pain points, (2) specific relatable failures on the path — named, concrete, slightly embarrassing (the matcha-shop class of story), (3) belief transfer — the story must end with the reader feeling "I can do this too," because a viewer who doesn't believe in themselves never books the call.
- Verify the past-self avatar link: the story should make the ideal client recognize their current situation in the storyteller's past. This is the uncopyable moat — bigger competitors cannot replicate a personal history.
- Compress into three lengths: a 2-3 sentence bio version, a 60-second short-form version, and a long-form keystone version for the platform's flagship content.

### Phase 3 — Weekly Content System
- Build the weekly plan on the three content types: **problem content** (agitate the avatar's pain and solve a slice of it), **framework content** (show the named unique process for solving it), **social-proof content** (mini-documentary style transformation stories — "30 days ago X couldn't do this; now they're at Y" — never boring testimonial interviews).
- Set the volume floor by his client-proven cadence: 5 posts/week on the primary platform (+1 long-form weekly if running the two-platform stack). Every piece must pass the day-one offer filter: "Does this attract someone who might buy the offer?" — followers without buyers is the named failure pattern.
- Design keystone long-form topics for search + recommendation (the "how to X" queries the avatar actually types), since one evergreen video can book calls for years.
- Wire the belief-transfer selling style into all teaching content: give real value, show it's possible, build the viewer's self-belief — subtle selling, never "limited time" pressure.

## Output Contract

- **Platform decision**: primary platform + optional two-platform stack, with the rejected alternatives and why
- **Positioning brief**: incumbent archetype profile, contrast points, flash/anti-flash call, taste-layer checklist
- **Origin story**: full version + 60-second version + bio version, annotated for the three required components
- **Weekly content plan**: cadence, the three content types with 5+ concrete titled ideas each, keystone long-form topics, and the single CTA/conversion path
- **Offer-alignment check**: one line per content pillar stating which stage (discovery/trust/conversion) it serves

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Exactly one traffic source (two max: long-form + short-form), one conversion method, one offer — no platform sprawl
- [ ] Every content idea traceable to the offer and avatar; no generic "grow your audience" content
- [ ] Origin story contains all three components: before/after on avatar pain, specific relatable failures, belief transfer
- [ ] Positioning is contrast-based against named incumbents, not "be better/post more"
- [ ] Weekly volume is realistic for the person's stated hours AND meets the 5-posts/week floor
- [ ] Social-proof content is story-arc format, not testimonial quotes
