# Red-Flag Word Bank — Banned Terms and Compliant Swaps

> Load for `/claim-audit`, `/compliant-rewrite`, `/claim-safe-hooks`. Organized by risk category. Every swap traces to genius.md GP-07 and the FTC/FDA sources in the source-ledger.

## Disease-Name Tokens (Amazon/platform scanners flag these regardless of surrounding sentence — genius.md GP-06)

Cancer, diabetes, Alzheimer's/dementia, arthritis, anxiety (as a diagnosed condition), depression, COVID-19/coronavirus, herpes, HIV/AIDS, lupus, Parkinson's, heart disease, ADHD, autism. **Rule**: if a disease name must appear at all (e.g., citing that a category of research exists), it must be nowhere near a treatment verb in the same sentence or paragraph, and ideally routed through legal review rather than copy at all.

## Treatment/Cure Verbs → Structure/Function Swaps

| Banned | Compliant | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cures / cure | Supports [normal function] | Never pair with any disease token |
| Treats / treatment | Supports / promotes | |
| Prevents / prevention | Supports [immune/metabolic/etc.] health | "Prevents" is a disease-claim verb even with a vague object |
| Heals / healing | Soothes / supports comfort | |
| Diagnoses / diagnostic | (remove entirely) | Supplements cannot make diagnostic claims, period |
| Reverses [condition] | Supports healthy [function] as part of a balanced routine | "Reverses" implies existing pathology |
| Remedy / remedies | Supports | |
| Eliminates [symptom/craving] | Supports a healthy response to [trigger] | See Rejuvica/Sobrenix anti-exemplar (genius.md) |

## Outcome-Guarantee Language → Realistic-Expectation Swaps (genius.md GP-07 #4)

| Banned | Compliant |
|---|---|
| "You WILL sleep better" | "Most customers notice a difference in sleep quality within the first week" |
| "Guaranteed results" | "[N]% of customers report [specific, typical, disclosed result] within [timeframe]" |
| "Works instantly" | "Formulated for [mechanism]; many customers notice [typical, non-disease effect] within [realistic timeframe]" |
| "Melts fat away" | "Supports healthy weight management as part of a balanced diet and exercise routine" (must be paired with the lifestyle qualifier per FTC Gut Check — genius.md GP-09) |

## Evidence-Strength Language (must match actual evidence tier — genius.md GP-02)

| Banned unless Tier-5 product-specific RCT evidence exists | Compliant fallback if evidence is weaker |
|---|---|
| "Clinically proven" | "Formulated with clinically studied ingredients" (only if the INGREDIENT, not necessarily the product, has Tier 4-5 evidence — and copy must not blur ingredient vs. product) |
| "Scientifically proven" | "Backed by research on [specific ingredient/mechanism]" |
| "Doctors recommend" (without substantiation of an actual survey/panel) | "Formulated by [credentialed role, named and verifiable]" |
| "#1 doctor-recommended" | Remove unless a verifiable, disclosed survey exists (FTC treats this as a substantiated-claim, not puffery) |

## Category-Specific High-Risk Terms (Dougherty's "especially sensitive" list)

Inflammation, pain, immune-boosting, cholesterol, blood pressure, testosterone/hormones, weight loss, blood sugar. Any claim in these categories needs a Tier 4-5 evidence check before shipping — do not default to Tier 2/0 evidence for these categories even if the language feels mild.

## Comparison/Substitution Language (never permitted regardless of evidence — genius.md Anti-Pattern #3/#4)

- "Alternative to [named drug]"
- "Works like [prescription name]"
- "Skip the prescription"
- Any framing that positions the supplement as a substitute for a recognized medical treatment.

## Platform-Specific Additional Bans

| Platform | Additional banned/restricted terms (beyond FTC/FDA baseline) |
|---|---|
| Meta | Second-person symptom framing ("Sick of your anxiety?", "Struggling with joint pain?") — banned under Personal Attributes policy even if the underlying claim is compliant |
| TikTok | "Weight loss," "muscle gain," "GLP-1," "Ozempic," any diet-drug-adjacent term as the CENTRAL claim of a supplement ad |
| Amazon | Any of the disease-name tokens above appearing ANYWHERE in listing copy, including in unrelated context (e.g., "not for people with diabetes" still trips the scanner) |
