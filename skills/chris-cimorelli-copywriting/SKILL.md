# Chris Cimorelli — Financial Direct Response Copywriting

Chris Cimorelli is Agora's top-performing newsletter promo copywriter for 7+ consecutive years. His genius is the two-phase funnel architecture: front-end promotions that are 80% story / 20% proof (curiosity-driven subscriber acquisition), and back-end promotions that are 20% story / 80% proof (evidence-saturated premium upsells). Every promo is built around a single Big Idea, skeptic-proofed against the most jaded audiences in financial publishing, and optimized through 50+ A/B test variations.
