# Blind Copy Sprint

Sam Parr's core training method. You learn taste by COPYING — word for word, pixel for pixel, note for note. No creative license. No "getting inspired." Exact reproduction.

This builds what Sam calls "texture" — the tacit knowledge that only comes from your hands having made the thing.

## Pre-Flight Gate

- User MUST have completed `/taste-declare` (or equivalent identity declaration) first
- Read `genius.md` — focus on Pattern 4 (Blind Copy Protocol), Pattern 5 (Texture Discovery), Pattern 6 (Feed Purge & Rewire)
- User must provide: Their domain and identity statement from `/taste-declare`

## Execution

### Step 1: Feed Purge
Design the exposure re-engineering protocol from genius Pattern 6:
- Identify the user's primary discovery platform for this domain (Instagram, Dribbble, Pinterest, Spotify, YouTube, bookshelf, etc.)
- Prescribe the purge: unfollow/remove everything in the domain
- Prescribe the rebuild: follow/add 15-30 exemplars that align with the identity statement
- Let the algorithm rewire for 3-5 days before proceeding

### Step 2: Exemplar Collection
Build the copying curriculum — 15-20 specific pieces to reproduce:
- Source from the curated feed, plus research of the domain
- Each exemplar must:
  - Align with the identity statement from `/taste-declare`
  - Represent genuine quality (not just popularity)
  - Vary in sub-style (within the identity, show range)
- Label each: **What specifically makes this one worth copying?**

### Step 3: Copy Protocol Design
Create the specific copying instructions per domain:

| Domain | What "Exact Copy" Means | Tool/Medium |
|--------|------------------------|-------------|
| Fashion | Photograph existing outfits piece by piece, list every item with brand/fit/material notes | Camera + spreadsheet |
| Web design | Reproduce the full page in Figma — every typeface, spacing, color, layout | Figma/design tool |
| Writing | Hand-copy full paragraphs or pieces — on paper, not typing | Pen + paper |
| Music | Learn to play/sing the exact piece, or map out the full arrangement note by note | Instrument or DAW |
| Brand | Recreate the full brand system — logo, colors, typography, voice samples | Design tool + writing |
| Naming | Write out 50+ names from the domain, letter by letter, noting sounds and patterns | Paper |

### Step 4: 30-Day Schedule
Build the sprint calendar:
- **Days 1-5**: Feed purge + algorithm rewire
- **Days 6-10**: Copy exemplar #1-#3 (one every 1-2 days, slow and careful)
- **Days 11-20**: Copy exemplar #4-#10 (one per day, building speed)
- **Days 21-28**: Copy exemplar #11-#15 (faster — texture is building)
- **Days 29-30**: Review all copies side by side. Journal: What patterns do you notice? What do all the good ones have in common? What feels different now?

### Step 5: Texture Journal
After each copy session, the user answers:
1. What surprised you — what did you notice only after your hands made it?
2. What was harder than expected?
3. What do all your copies have in common that you didn't notice before?
4. What's the "rule" you think you're learning?

This is the raw material for the next workflow (`/taste-rules`).

## Content Type Adaptations

| Content Type | Sprint Adaptation |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Fashion | Build a "copied outfit log" — photograph, inventory, and wear the style for 30 days |
| Web design | Build a Figma library of 15 pixel-perfect reproductions |
| Writing | Fill a physical notebook with hand-copied passages — aim for 15 complete pieces |
| Naming | Create a naming bible — 100+ copied names clustered by domain, sound pattern, etymology |
| Brand | Build a brand kit reproduction portfolio — 5 complete brand systems recreated |

## Output Requirements

1. **Feed Purge Plan** — what to unfollow, who to follow, platform-specific instructions
2. **Exemplar Library** — 15-20 specific pieces with quality annotations
3. **Copy Protocol** — exact instructions for how to reproduce in this domain
4. **30-Day Calendar** — day-by-day sprint with time commitments
5. **Texture Journal Template** — daily reflection questions

## Quality Gate

Reference genius.md quality rubric:
- **Score 4**: User is "inspired by" exemplars but hasn't reproduced them
- **Score 7**: User has copied 5-10 exemplars with reasonable fidelity
- **Score 10**: 15+ exemplars reproduced with full texture journal
- **Minimum to pass**: Score 7. Less than 5 copies = the texture hasn't formed yet.
