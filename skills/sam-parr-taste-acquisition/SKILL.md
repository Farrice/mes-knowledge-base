# Sam Parr — Taste Acquisition Mastery

**Invocation**: `/deploy-skill sam-parr-taste-acquisition`  
**Domain**: Taste Development • Identity Architecture • Creative Direction • Brand Aesthetics • Competitive Positioning

---

Sam Parr's complete system for manufacturing taste in 3-5 months in any domain. Contains 12 workflows spanning identity declaration, blind copying, rule extraction, history mining, and cross-expert stacking. Positions taste as "the biggest moat" in the AI era.

**Core Philosophy**:
- Taste is a learnable 4-step process, not an innate gift
- Identity drives aesthetic choices — decide what you want to say BEFORE copying
- History provides constraints; good taste = play within them; great taste = break them intentionally
- 3-5 months of deliberate practice puts you in the top 10%

**How This Differs from Oren (Taste Development)**:
- Oren = taste EVALUATION (CEV matrix, connoisseurship, insider codes)
- Sam Parr = taste ACQUISITION (Decide → Copy → Rules → History)
- Sam Parr is the on-ramp. Oren is the highway. Use sequentially.

## Available Workflows

### Tier 1 — Foundation

| # | Workflow | Produces | Use When |
|---|---------|----------|----------|
| 1 | [Taste Identity Declaration](workflows/taste-identity-declaration.md) | A clear "what I want to say" statement with values tracing and cultural lineage | You're starting any creative project, brand, or aesthetic direction and haven't articulated what you're trying to communicate or why |
| 2 | [Blind Copy Sprint](workflows/blind-copy-sprint.md) | A 30-day copying schedule with 15-20 specific exemplars to reproduce | You've declared your identity but haven't built the muscle memory of quality through hands-on reproduction |
| 3 | [Rule Extraction Engine](workflows/rule-extraction-engine.md) | The explicit rules underneath what you copied — named, sourced, and actionable | You've copied enough to feel the texture but can't articulate WHY things work |
| 4 | [History Lineage Map](workflows/history-lineage-map.md) | A traced lineage from current inspiration back 3+ generations through history | You know what you like and the rules it follows, but lack the historical depth to operate with authority |
