## Execution

### Step 1 — Re-read everything; extract the single truth

Load all five state files end-to-end. The blueprint is not a stapler: find the ONE through-line the engagement proved — the sentence connecting who the buyer is → the open lane nobody holds → the buckets that reach and convert them → the vehicles that carry it → the money it feeds. Write that sentence first. Every section of the blueprint either serves it or gets cut ("comprehensive" is failure; density over completeness).

### Step 2 — The Executive Verdict page (≤2 pages, leads the document)

1. **The one-line verdict** — the through-line sentence.
2. **The positioning call** — the UPA paragraph, with its confirmed/contested/redirect tally as the evidence score ("of the seven claimed edges: N confirmed, N contested, N redirected").
3. **The money map** — the three buckets with their offer linkage and "working =" thresholds in one table: which bucket makes money, how we'll know by batch 3.
4. **The bet register** — every LIKELY and UNCONFIRMED claim the strategy leans on, in one honest list with what would harden each (this is what makes the document trustworthy to a sophisticated buyer — the negative space section, adopted and promoted to the front).
5. **Data provenance line** — pack date, coverage scope, data tier, refresh command.

### Step 3 — The five chapters (compression, not repetition)

Each chapter compresses its state artifact to decision-weight and links/embeds the full artifact as the appendix — the living files remain the depth; the blueprint is the argument:

| Chapter | Carries | Compression rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Who we're for | Avatar portrait + identity layer + top-5 pains (verbatims, sourced) | The chair-test portrait survives whole; everything else in decision-weight bullets |
| 2 · The open lane | Wheel summary + the whitespace entries we're claiming, each with its receipts + decision-ready move | Only claimed lanes; the full grid is appendix |
| 3 · Where we aim | Ring ladder + 3-2-1 trio + revenue overlay + chaos slot | The overlay table verbatim — it IS the money argument |
| 4 · What we make | The 3 buckets w/ mechanisms + top-10 of the top-50 (struck rows kept — the pedagogy travels) | Mechanisms named; the 50 is appendix |
| 5 · How it's made | The 2–3 format picks w/ mechanism cards + production recipes | Recipes at constraint level; databases are appendix |

### Step 4 — The 90-day batch plan

Concrete calendar: **batches 1–12/13 (7 videos/week per the 3-2-1 mix)** with per-batch composition, the calibration checkpoints (after batches 2–3: first kill/keep pass in "working =" units; the 24-hour and 2–3-batch maturity rules printed), the chaos-reserve draw order (whitespace-tagged candidates first), the hero-format decision point (~batch 3: testing ends, reps begin), and the refresh cadence: dossier ~90d · whitespace ~90d · scan ~45d · `gb-refresh` monthly + on any positioning change. Wave-2 handoff named as an option: production loop via the `kallaway-*` roster (hook/script/retention substance) until the native production tier ships.

### Step 5 — Verification-label audit (the ship gate that is ours)

Sweep the assembled document: every claim carries VERIFIED / LIKELY / UNCONFIRMED; every number traces to a receipt, a stated basis, or a `[NEED]`; every UNCONFIRMED appears in the bet register. Anything failing gets fixed or labeled — never silently shipped. Then the prose gate (ban bank), then — self-run only — the voice layer.

### Step 6 — Render, save, hand off

Write `growth-blueprint.md`; render the client form via `render_brief.py --client` → `exports/growth-blueprint-client.html` (Premium Minimal — this is the artifact a $2,500 engagement hands over; the outward form is the brand canon, full stop) + PDF export. Update `manifest.json` (deps: all five; inherited data tier), snapshot priors to `history/`. Close: one-line state + the standing loop (`gb-refresh` monthly; `gb-orchestrate` for what's next).
