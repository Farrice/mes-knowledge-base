# Growth Blueprint OS — Bullseye + Revenue Overlay

## Pre-Flight Gate

- **Read state:** `positioning-dossier.md` (the avatar IS the center — confirm in one reflect-back line, don't re-interview) and `whitespace-map.md` (chaos pre-screening + bucket candidates read it). Dossier missing → route to `gb-interview`, or rough out from a two-line version without blocking.
- **Offer map present?** The revenue overlay needs the dossier's pain→offer wiring and price points. Missing → one question, not an interrogation.
- **Pack state:** classify FRESH / STALE / ABSENT — governs Stage 2 anchoring and Stage 3 competition columns.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `genius.md` (§2.1 constraint-relaxation rings, §2.2 3-2-1 + chaos, §2.3 two-zone sourcing, §2.4 menu-not-verdict, §1.3 revenue wiring). Load `references/artifact-design-language.md` before rendering (same-geometry-different-legend is this workflow's signature move).

## Execution

### Stage 1 — Center Statement

`[specific person] who [specific situation] and struggles with [specific problem]` — drafted from the dossier's Chair Test portrait, confirmed in one line. Person-level specificity or it isn't a center. **Viewer = buyer holds:** the center is the buyer; viewer-heavy rings serve reach, the center serves revenue.

### Stage 2 — Derive the rings by constraint relaxation (the load-bearing mechanic)

Exactly **5 rings, numbered inside-out.** Each ring outward relaxes **ONE named constraint** versus the ring inside — geography, income, purchase history, intent, profession — until Ring 5, where the constraint column reads "everything," which is precisely why Ring 5 is a trap, not a tier. Do the abstraction work yourself; present all five with the relaxed constraint named per ring; adjust on feedback. "One ring broader" is a vibe; "drop the income filter" is a decision — the named-constraint ladder is what makes the ring set auditable.

Per ring, three meters, each labeled with the *business* meaning: relative audience size, **conversion proximity (how close to a $-defined next step — name the actual step and price from the offer map)**, competition density.

**Anchor with real proxies (data step — degradation tiers apply):**
- FRESH pack → competition per ring = actual watchlist channels operating at that ring (named, from `channels[]` + their `ranked_videos` topics); audience-size direction sanity-checked against ring-level view volumes. Anchored estimates ship as LIKELY with their basis stated; named competitor placements as VERIFIED w/ specimens.
- STALE → same anchors, date-stamped "as of `<generated_at>`", refresh command quoted.
- ABSENT → estimates ship as **directional, UNCONFIRMED, and say so on the meters themselves** — honest directionality beats fake precision, but unlike his version we say which number a refresh would harden and quote the command.

**Ring 5 trap list (adopted — negative-example pedagogy):** generate 2–3 personalized video ideas that would LOOK reasonable to a beginner but are Ring-5 traps for this niche, each with its failure logic. Teach the causal story once, in plain cause-and-effect: these can get views, but the viewers don't match the buyer — and the algorithm learns from whoever engages, so one viral Ring-5 hit teaches it to send more of the wrong people. Ring 1's inverse trap: it's the person you *speak to*, not a content lane.

### Stage 3 — The 3-2-1 mix + bucket menu

**3 buckets × 2 videos + 1 chaos dart = the 7-video batch.** Placement rules: 2 narrow buckets at Rings 2–3 (trust + conversion engines), 1 broad bucket at Ring 4 (reach engine — broad in audience, still delivered in the operator's voice to the avatar), buckets never at Ring 1 (too narrow to sustain a theme) or Ring 5 (generic; the algorithm never learns who it's for).

**Menu, not verdict:** draft **8–10 candidate buckets** from the dossier (pains, expertise) and the whitespace map (open lanes become bucket candidates — this is where whitespace converts to strategy). Per candidate: plain-language name (proposed + alternate — the name slot cannot be empty), ring, strategic job, 2 example topics. Recommend a starting trio (2 narrow + 1 broad) with reasons; keep the rest as the labeled **bench** — the operator's swap beats our ranking, and the bench is `gb-topic-scan`'s cross-check list.

### Stage 4 — The revenue overlay (ours — the surpass layer)

Every bucket — picks and bench — gets an economics row:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Offer fed | Which named offer this bucket's viewers are closest to (from dossier pain→offer wiring) |
| Funnel role | Trust / conversion / reach / proof — named, one per bucket, not a blur |
| Lead value | Estimated $ value of one qualified lead from this bucket, with its basis (offer price × plausible close rate, or the operator's real numbers). No basis → [NEED], never a plausible invention |
| **"Working ="** | A dollar-or-count threshold: e.g. "≥2 DM inquiries mentioning <offer> per batch" — observable, not vibes |
| Kill/keep rule | The decision answerable **by batch 3**: below X across 2–3 batches → swap for the named bench challenger; above Y → third video slot |

Reach and conversion will point at different buckets — that is by design; the overlay is what keeps calibration deciding in dollars while reach does its job. **The surpass bar this stage answers:** "which bucket makes this business money, and how do you know by batch 3?" — answered in writing, per bucket.

### Stage 5 — Chaos reserve (designed experiment, pre-screened)

Generate **4–5 chaos candidates, each wild in a different way**: one deeper-than-Ring-1 insider probe, one format experiment nobody in the niche runs, one personality/behind-the-scenes swing, one trend hijack re-aimed at Rings 2–3, one pure gut punt. Each carries: the idea in one line, its kind-of-wild, and **"a win teaches you"** — one orthogonal question per candidate, so a weekly rogue slot compounds into a designed experiment. **Our addition — pre-screen against the whitespace map:** each candidate tagged with which whitespace entry it probes (or "none — pure exploration"), so the rogue slot doubles as a positioning probe. Rings 1–4 only; never Ring 5. Operator picks one for batch 1; the rest bank in the reserve.

### Stage 6 — Sourcing rules + sample batch + calibration tracker

- **Two-zone sourcing rule (adopted IN FULL — all three of his doctrines):** topics come from the ring your bucket sits on; craft — formats, hooks, storytelling, editing — from anywhere on the board including Ring 5 and other niches. (1) For the narrow buckets, source topics from Rings 1–3 — drift is deadliest here, stay tight, and **Ring 1 creators, if any exist, are topic GOLD** even though you'd never build a bucket there. (2) For the broad bucket, source from Ring 4 — **then always re-aim the delivery at the avatar**: a broad topic in your voice for your person, never generic content. (3) **An empty ring is not a problem — it's your opening**: pull from the adjacent ring and re-aim. Ring 5 is craft-only; sourcing topics there is the specific act that drifts a channel generic.
- **RING INTELLIGENCE (the sourcing map's content contract — every ring/zone card carries ALL of these, register per genius.md Register Contract; exemplars: `.scratch/kallaway-sandcastles-forge/content-standard-exemplars.md`):**
  1. **Pillar** — the content lane that lives at this ring, with its evidence built into the sentence (whitespace verdicts, pool stats — quietly linked, never narrated).
  2. **Which bucket sources here** — named, so the map answers "who do I study, and for what."
  3. **What to make** — 3–5 concrete example topics (titles, not categories), drawn from the dossier's pain bank verbatims and the whitespace moves.
  4. **Who to study, and for what** — named channels from the watchlist, each tagged `topic inspiration` or `craft inspiration only` (his `Use for` column — mandatory), with the reason stated ("for how they talk about launches, not for topics").
  5. **The trap at this altitude** — stated where the reader acts on it (person-winning-not-topic, drift risk, wrong-avatar pull).
  6. Per-ring **search terms (2–3)** survive as ONE field among six, feeding `gb-whitespace` and `gb-format-find` — never as the card's content.
  Rings render individually (never collapse rings into one card); Rings 2–4 `warn`/guidance fields are NEVER empty; size/competition cells speak audience reality, never pipeline state.
- **Sample batch of 7:** slot → bucket → ring → job → one example topic; chaos pick in slot 7.
- **Calibration tracker (live, not a blank template):** columns batch # / videos per bucket / reach per bucket / **conversions per bucket in the "working =" unit** / decision. Rules printed on it: no verdict at 24 hours; no demotion before 2–3 batches; the numbers — in dollars — decide, not the vibes.

### Stage 7 — Visualize + save

Three renders per `references/artifact-design-language.md`, all reusing the SAME ring geometry with different legends (the signature move — one mental model, three read-outs): (1) **The Bullseye** — rings + constraint labels + the three meters per ring card, warning bands on Ring 5 ("too broad — viral with the wrong avatar") and Ring 1 ("too narrow"); (2) **The 3-2-1 Mix** — bucket zones + numbered darts + candidate card rack with live bench-swap + the revenue overlay surfaced on each bucket card; (3) **The Sourcing Map** — same rings, craft-zone band, per-ring search terms. Premium Minimal semantics throughout. Save `bullseye.md`, update `manifest.json` (deps: [positioning-dossier, whitespace-map]), snapshot priors. One-line state + next: `gb-topic-scan`.
