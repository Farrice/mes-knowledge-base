## 🧬 CORE METHODOLOGIES & FRAMEWORKS (the secret sauce)

1. **Name-the-Problem-Own-the-Solution.** Coin a name for the *problem* the operator solves (the authority paradox model). Naming the affliction makes the operator the default cure.
2. **The Paradox/Tension Formula.** The stickiest names hold a tension: two words that shouldn't sit together (authority *paradox*, algorithm *audition*, total addressable *content*). Tension = memorability.
3. **The Proprietary-Mechanism Frame.** Name the *method*, not just the outcome — a named mechanism ("the trend rider") sounds like IP, invites curiosity, and differentiates from generic "frameworks."
4. **The Boomerang Test.** A name works when prospects repeat it back unprompted ("are you the two-lift person?"). Engineer names to be *sayable* and *self-explaining enough to travel.*
5. **The Renewable-Engine Property.** Every named concept spawns content: the origin of the name, the symptoms, the cure, the case studies, the contrarian defense. One name = months of posts.
6. **Verbal Identity System.** Names ladder into a coherent lexicon (a family of terms) so the operator's whole worldview sounds like one owned language.

## 📊 EXAMPLE OUTPUT 1: Sales-productivity consultant → the "Pipeline Mirage" lexicon (with viral metrics)

**Context:** Consultant who fixes bloated-but-empty CRM pipelines. ICP = VPs of Sales. Core problem: pipelines that *look* healthy but don't convert.

**THE ACTUAL DELIVERABLE:**

**Problem-Name (winner):** **"The Pipeline Mirage"** — the illusion of a full pipeline that evaporates at forecast time.
*Runners-up:* "Ghost Pipeline," "Forecast Fiction."

**Method-Name (winner):** **"The Deal-Reality Audit"** — the proprietary method that strips a pipeline down to deals that will actually close.
*Runners-up:* "Pipeline Deflation," "The Honest Forecast Method."

**Lexicon (term family):**
- *Mirage Deals* — deals in the CRM that no rep actually believes will close.
- *Reality Rate* — % of pipeline that survives the audit.
- *The Deflation* — the (healthy) shrinking of pipeline to truth.
- *Forecast Floor* — the number you can actually promise the board.

**Definitions + origin (excerpt):** *Pipeline Mirage:* "When I audit sales teams, 40–60% of 'pipeline' is deals reps keep open to look busy. I started calling it the Pipeline Mirage because it's water that isn't there — and every VP who's missed a forecast knows the feeling."

**Renewable-content map (excerpt):** (1) "Signs you have a Pipeline Mirage." (2) "Why your reps build Mirage Deals (it's your fault, not theirs)." (3) "The Deal-Reality Audit in 5 questions." (4) "What Reality Rate is 'good'?" (5) Case study: "We deflated a $4M pipeline to $1.6M — and hit forecast for the first time in a year." (6) Hot take: "A shrinking pipeline is the healthiest thing that can happen to your quarter."

**Viral result + formula:** Posted #6 as a hot take — *"The healthiest thing that happened to my client's sales team was watching their pipeline shrink 60%."* The named concept ("Pipeline Mirage") gave the take a memorable handle; the comment section split (reps defending optimism vs VPs nodding). **Realistic: ~180 comments, ~31K impressions (~10× baseline); within weeks prospects DMed "do you do the mirage audit thing?"** — the boomerang confirming ownership. **Formula:** `name the buyer's private shame ("my pipeline is fake") → the name makes it sayable → post the counterintuitive cure as a hot take → the name boomerangs back in DMs as the inbound trigger.`
