## 🧬 CORE METHODOLOGIES & FRAMEWORKS (the secret sauce)

1. **Reverse-Order Composition.** Draft the full asset first (story, numbers, framework), then mine the sharpest line already doing the heavy lifting and pull it to the top. Never write the hook first.
2. **The 50-Word Audition.** Engineer the opening ~50 words to clear the algorithm's retrieval gate — concrete, specific, tension-loaded — before any human decides.
3. **The Four Hook Formats.** Dense · Punchy + Context · Single-Line Bomb · Stacked — every headline/lead mined from the asset in the best-fitting shape.
4. **Proof-Stacking.** Every claim carries a specific number or named example (specificity = credibility). No adjective survives without a receipt.
5. **Problem → Solution → Proof → CTA.** The load-bearing structure for long-form (about sections, sales pages, emails): name the pain, position the fix, back it with numbers, tell them what to do next.
6. **Buyer-Language Fidelity.** Write in the exact words the buyer uses and searches — never "big, hard, complicated words to sound fancy."
7. **The Post-Quality Gate.** If no line is worth pulling to the top, stop — fix the argument, don't rescue a hollow asset with a clever hook.

## 📊 EXAMPLE OUTPUT 1: Full launch copy kit — B2B onboarding-software startup (with viral post + metrics)

**Context:** Offer = onboarding software cutting new-hire ramp time. ICP = People/HR leaders at 200–2000 person companies. Proof: cut ramp from 90→45 days at a 600-person client; 3.2× faster time-to-productivity.

**THE ACTUAL DELIVERABLE (excerpt — kit includes landing page, email, ad, post):**

**Landing-page headline (Single-Line Bomb, mined from proof):**
"Your new hires are unproductive for 90 days. We cut that to 45."
*Variants:* (Dense) "New hires cost you full salary for 3 months of half output — we've cut ramp time in half for teams up to 2,000." (Punchy+Context) "Ramp time is a silent payroll leak. One 600-person client cut it 90→45 days."

**Lead (Problem→Solution→Proof→CTA, 50-word audition-passing):**
> "Every new hire you make is a 90-day payroll loss you've accepted as normal. Full salary, half output, while they hunt for answers across 11 tools. It's not an onboarding problem — it's a *findability* problem. We cut one 600-person company's ramp from 90 to 45 days: 3.2× faster time-to-productivity. See the teardown ↓"

**Launch post (reverse-order composition, viral-engineered):**
> "We cut a 600-person company's new-hire ramp time from 90 days to 45.
>
> Here's the uncomfortable part: we didn't improve their onboarding. We deleted it.
>
> Their 'onboarding' was a 40-page doc, 11 tools, and a Slack channel where new hires were afraid to look dumb. New people weren't ramping slowly because they were slow. They were ramping slowly because the answers were *hidden.*
>
> We replaced the doc with one searchable place that answers 'how do I…' in 10 seconds. Ramp time halved. Time-to-first-real-contribution dropped 3.2×.
>
> Your onboarding isn't too short or too long. It's too *hard to search.*
>
> Reply 'RAMP' for the 45-day ramp framework."

**Viral result + formula:** Mined hook (the 90→45 line) pulled the whole post; the "we deleted it" turn created the split (HR traditionalists vs reformers), and the reframe ("too hard to search") made it saveable. **Realistic: ~150 comments, ~26K impressions (~9× baseline), 70 "RAMP" replies.** **Formula:** `lead with the proof number → subvert the expected cause ("we deleted onboarding") → reframe the real problem in the buyer's words → single-word CTA converts comments.`

## 📊 EXAMPLE OUTPUT 2: Cold email + ad rewrite — B2B recruiting agency (with metrics)

**Context:** Offer = niche engineering recruiting. ICP = eng VPs who can't fill senior roles. Proof: filled a 6-month-open staff role in 19 days; 82% offer-accept rate.

**Cold email (proof-stacked, Problem→Solution→Proof→CTA):**

**Ad rewrite (Single-Line Bomb + proof):**

**Viral/performance result + formula:** The rewritten ad — leading with the specific 19-day proof instead of a generic promise — is engineered to out-convert because specificity *is* the credibility. **Realistic uplift pattern: reply rate on the proof-stacked cold email materially higher than the generic version because "19 days / 82%" survives the skeptic's filter where "great engineers faster" doesn't.** **Formula:** `open with the prospect's exact stuck situation → one hyper-specific proof number → an honest, low-pressure CTA ("I'll tell you if it's not a fit").` The proof-stacking discipline is the entire mechanism: replace every adjective with a number and the same offer converts dramatically better.
