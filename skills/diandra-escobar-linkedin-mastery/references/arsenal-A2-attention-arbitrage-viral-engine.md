## 🧬 CORE METHODOLOGIES & FRAMEWORKS (the secret sauce)

**The Five Arbitrage Formats** — each borrows a different pool of existing attention:

1. **Brand Jacking** — frame your insight around a credible brand's move; the brand carries reach, your analysis carries value.
2. **Newsjacking** — be the *first credible voice* to explain what breaking industry news *means for the reader* (not a summary).
3. **Memejacking** — reference a *person* your audience already follows; add something real to the conversation.
4. **Hot Takes** — a genuinely-held belief stated plainly enough that people *must pick a side* — engineered so the comment section splits and distributes.
5. **Trend Writing (Trend Rider)** — pour expertise into a spreading *post format* while the window is open; substance makes it land.

**The virality mechanics under all five:**
- **Comment-section-as-distribution:** design the post so half agree / half defend, because argument = reach.
- **Percentile-native engineering:** optimize *engagement quality* that moves you up a percentile (real replies, saves, debate) — pods don't move percentiles.
- **The 50-word audition:** the first ~50 words must clear the algorithm's retrieval gate before a human ever votes.
- **The short window:** ride a trend while it's climbing; once everyone copies it, it's dead.

## 📊 EXAMPLE OUTPUT 1: B2B cybersecurity founder — newsjacking a breach (viral, with metrics)

**Context:** Founder of a mid-market security startup, ICP = IT directors at 200–1000 person companies. Live pool: a major retailer just disclosed a breach dominating the news cycle.

**THE ACTUAL DELIVERABLE:**

**Format: Newsjacking** *(pool: the breach news cycle)*
> "[Major retailer] didn't get breached because of a sophisticated attacker.
>
> They got breached because of a checkbox nobody owned.
>
> I've now read the disclosure three times. The 'advanced threat' framing is PR. The actual failure was a third-party vendor with standing access that no one had reviewed since 2023.
>
> Here's the uncomfortable part for every IT director reading this: you have that same vendor. You just haven't had your name in a headline yet.
>
> The fix isn't a bigger security budget. It's a 90-minute access review you keep postponing because nothing's on fire. Do it before the fire.

**Spread mechanism (mapped):** Rides the breach news pool (massive existing attention) → reframes from "what happened" to "what it means for *you*" (the newsjack move) → the line *"you have that same vendor"* creates personal threat = saves + shares → the "reply ACCESS" turns comments into a distribution + lead-capture loop.

**Viral result + replicable formula:** Posted within 6 hours of the disclosure (temperature intact), the post pulled **realistic outcome: 140+ comments, ~28K impressions vs his ~3K baseline (~9× lift), 60+ "ACCESS" replies → 60 warm leads in one post.** **Formula:** `breaking news in niche → reframe cause from "sophisticated" to "boring + preventable" → make the reader the next headline → single-word comment CTA → comments become a lead list.` Timing rule: publish inside the first 12 hours or the pool drains.

## 📊 EXAMPLE OUTPUT 2: B2B SaaS marketer — brand jack + hot take combo (viral, with metrics)
