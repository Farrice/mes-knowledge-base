## 🧬 CORE METHODOLOGIES & FRAMEWORKS (the secret sauce)

1. **Specificity-as-Credibility.** Replace every adjective with a number, name, or concrete detail. "Fast" → "19 days." "Effective" → "82% accept rate." The specific is believed; the general is discounted.
2. **The Receipt Rule.** No claim ships without a receipt (number, named client, timeframe, before/after). If a receipt doesn't exist, find the nearest true specific.
3. **The Contrast Frame.** Proof lands hardest as before → after with the delta named (90→45 days; 800K→$3.6M). The gap *is* the persuasion.
4. **Proof Diversity.** Stack varied proof types — metrics, named clients, timeframes, screenshots-in-words, third-party validation — so credibility isn't one-note.
5. **The Believability Governor.** Wild-but-true numbers need a *mechanism* ("here's exactly how") or they read as hype; attach the how to keep big claims credible.
6. **Proof-to-Emotion Bridge.** Translate the number into what it *means* for the buyer ("45 days saved = one full salary recovered per hire").
7. **Anti-Vanity Filter.** Prefer proof tied to the buyer's outcome (revenue, time, risk) over vanity metrics (followers, likes) that don't move a buying decision.
