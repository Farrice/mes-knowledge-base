# /eli5 output shape

At most 20 lines. No headers. Plain words. Every line has a source in the collector's JSON.

## The five parts

```
Here's what we're doing and why: <current ask or top piece>. <goal, in plain words>.

Where each piece is:
  ⛔ <thing>: stuck. <blocker, and for how long>
  🙋 <thing>: waiting on you. <one thing it needs from you>
  🔄 <thing>: working. <step in motion>
  ✅ <thing>: done. <what shipped>

What only you can decide:
  1. <question>  A) <option>  B) <option>  → I'd pick A because <plain reason>.

If you do nothing: <what stays stuck, what keeps waiting, what comes due>.

Next thing I'll do unless you say otherwise: <one thing this seat can do now>.
```

With three or more pieces, the "Where each piece is" block becomes a table:

```
| thing | state | what it needs |
|---|---|---|
| linkedin-800k-os | ⛔ stuck | a human look at the corrected profile, 9 days waiting |
| farrice-final-10 | 🙋 waiting on you | four calls: offer name, father-wound post, ship audit, discovery calls |
| jen week 2 | 🔄 working | rendering the reels |
```

## Empty state

One line, nothing else:

```
Nothing is open right now. Nothing is waiting on you.
```

## A feed I can't read

One extra line, plain, never zeroed:

```
I can't see the missions log. It isn't there.
```

## Before / after: the bar

Before (a real handoff line, verbatim):

> ⧗ unfinished: Awaiting Farrice raw take before the cook (§7); HALT mode, no --auto

After (`/eli5`):

> ⛔ linkedin-daily: stuck. It has waited 79 days for your raw take on the "AI slop" post. Nothing runs until you give it.

Before (a real sweep lede, verbatim):

> This thread is the sprint's engine and it has quietly stopped producing new mouth-to-market motion — every mission on the board is marked done, the assets exist, and nothing in the record shows a post going live or a reply coming back.

After (`/eli5`):

> 🙋 linkedin: waiting on you. The posts are written. None has gone live. No reply has come back. This is the only thread that touches buyers.

Same facts. Fewer words. Nothing thinned that changes what he would do.
