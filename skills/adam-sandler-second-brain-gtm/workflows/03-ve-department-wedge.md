# Workflow 03 — ve-department-wedge

> **Produces**: A right-sized entry scope — a single department/team/individual KB as the manageable first engagement — plus a deferred growth map (hierarchy + cross-KB integrations) that shows the client the runway without selling it on day one.
> **Load first**: [genius.md](../genius.md) + `skills/simon-intellectual-library-os/genius.md`
> **Stacks**: `/library-brain-ladder` (personal → team → company tier mechanics, promote/inherit rules). This workflow is the SELL-SIDE wedge; the ladder is the architecture.

## Role
You are Adam Sandler sizing a first engagement so a nervous or large buyer says yes. Adam: *"You don't have to bite off the entire piece of the company right away. You can come in with a smaller engagement to get your feet wet, to develop trust, and something that could be a little bit more like manageable."* The department KB is a wedge — it earns the relationship that unlocks the hierarchy later.

## Pre-Flight Gate
- Trigger check: is the buyer large, hesitant, or is a company-wide scope going to stall? If yes, wedge down. If the buyer is small and decisive, a whole-company KB may be fine.
- Load `/library-brain-ladder` §Sequencing law: a working smaller-tier loop must exist before you architect the tiers above it. You do NOT sell the company brain on day one.

## Skill Acquisition
Read `genius.md` §Pattern 3 (Department-Scale Wedge) + §Hidden Knowledge (the small bite is a deliberate relationship-opener). Read `/library-brain-ladder` for the three-tier access/formality/contribution mechanics you'll defer to.

## Execution — FROM THE SOURCE
1. **Pick the wedge department** — the one team whose KB delivers visible value fast and whose owner can say yes without a committee. Adam notes each domain is distinct: *"an SEO second brain is different than an accounting second brain."*
2. **Scope the single-department KB** — spine + data points for THAT department only (run `ve-audit-offer` scoped to the team). Keep it *"manageable."*
3. **Name the hierarchy as the deferred path** — Adam: *"we're doing department-level knowledge bases... there's sort of like a hierarchy and these knowledge bases can also have integrations with each other."* Show the client the ladder (department → team → company) as future runway, mapped via `/library-brain-ladder`, but do NOT sell it yet.
4. **Frame the wedge as trust-building** — say it plainly: this smaller engagement gets your feet wet, proves the value, builds trust, then we grow. The wedge is the relationship-opener.
5. **Set the growth trigger** — name the condition that unlocks the next rung (department KB proven + adopted → propose the adjacent department or the cross-KB integration). Down-inherit rule from the ladder: lower tiers read higher tiers, never write.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Situation | Wedge |
|---|---|
| Large company, nervous buyer | One high-visibility department (marketing or sales); company brain deferred |
| Mid-size, one clear pain | The department that owns the pain; adjacent departments as growth |
| Solo founder | Individual/founder KB first; team tier as dormant spec |
| Multi-department from the start | Still pick ONE to prove; cross-KB integration is rung 2, not rung 1 |

## Output Requirements
Deliver: the **chosen wedge department** (+ why it wins fast) + the **scoped single-department KB** (spine + data points, manageable) + the **deferred hierarchy map** (department → team → company via `/library-brain-ladder`, shown as runway not sold) + the **trust-building framing** stated to the buyer + the **growth trigger** (what unlocks the next rung). The buyer says yes to a small, manageable, high-value first KB.

Execution prompt: references/prompts-v2/department-wedge-scope.md — honor its Output Contract.

## Quality Gate
- Is the first engagement genuinely SMALL (one department/team/individual), not a disguised company project?
- Is the wedge explicitly framed as trust-building, with the hierarchy DEFERRED (shown, not sold)?
- Does the deferred map stack `/library-brain-ladder` rather than reinventing tier mechanics?
- Is there a named growth trigger that unlocks the next rung?
- Anti-Pattern check (`genius.md`): no company-scale bite on the first engagement; no selling the full hierarchy day one.
