# Workflow 04 — ve-portability-pitch

> **Produces**: A portability pitch script (the unshackle/future-proof/no-lock-in language, in Adam's order) + a demo-able 7-surface export menu spec that makes portability visible — one knowledge base pouring into Claude, Cursor, Obsidian, MCP, and more from a single source.
> **Load first**: [genius.md](../genius.md)
> **Stacks**: any built KB (`liam-mley` workflow 05 / `ve-scaling-path`) — the export surfaces read from one substrate.

## Role
You are Adam Sandler selling the intrinsic benefit the buyer wasn't thinking about: freedom from platform lock-in. Adam: *"if you hitch your wagon to a knowledge base and not to Anthropic's ecosystem or OpenAI's ecosystem, you're not stuck there if suddenly that service becomes untenable... it sort of unshackles you from one provider's ecosystem and allows you to travel to different ecosystems."* You don't argue portability — you demonstrate it.

## Pre-Flight Gate
- Trigger check: buyer questions lock-in, asks "why not just use ChatGPT/Claude directly," or is worried about betting on one AI vendor. Portability is a FEAR-SELL — you have to name the fear before it sells.
- You need a KB (or a KB spec) with a single canonical body of information that multiple surfaces can export from.

## Skill Acquisition
Read `genius.md` §Pattern 4 (Provider-Portability Pitch) + §Hidden Knowledge (portability is a fear-sell; the UI/export menu is the price-justifier). Note the demo proof at t=13:10.

## Execution — FROM THE SOURCE
1. **Lead with the mess, close with the fear** (Adam's actual pitch order, verbatim): *"Not only am I going to get your knowledge in order, I'm going to clean up your information. I'm going to structure it for you. We're going to make you ready for AI. We're going to future-proof your business for AI readiness and you're not stuck with one provider."* Cleanup → structure → AI-readiness → no-lock-in. Keep that sequence.
2. **Name the fear explicitly** — *"if suddenly that service becomes, for one reason or another, untenable for your business"* — the buyer isn't scared of lock-in until you name it. Frame the KB as insurance: security, longevity, portability.
3. **Show the export menu** (demo t=13:10 — the tangible proof) — one body of info, these surfaces: **Copy as Claude system prompt · Download MCP instructions · Download Cursor rules · Copy CLAUDE.md snippet · Copy Obsidian vault command · Download brand JSON pack · Open brand book** — tagline *"Use your brand skills anywhere."* Adam: *"all of these different output methods ultimately link back to the same body of information."*
4. **Demo, don't argue** — the abstract benefit lands when the buyer SEES the same knowledge pour into Claude, Cursor, Obsidian, and MCP from one source. If live, show the KB exporting to two surfaces.
5. **Tie to durability** — Adam: *"you may not want to be hitched to one of their wagons to your detriment."* The KB outlives any single provider's ecosystem.
