# `/vince-guard-down-vssl-architect` — Guard-Down VSSL Architecture

Vince's preferred format for top-of-funnel scale: 3-5 minute VSSLs that capture attention with their guard down. The viewer is hooked by a relatable scenario, not a brand reveal — they're emotionally engaged before they realize they're watching an ad.

## Genius Context (Load First)

Read `genius.md`. Internalize:
- **Pattern 6: Guard-Down Attention Capture**
- **Pattern 5: Emotional Marketing — Angles AS Emotions**
- **Pattern 8: Top-of-Funnel AI / Middle-of-Funnel Real Creators**

Then read `references/emotional-angle-library.md` (especially multi-emotion stacking section).

## When to Run

- Top-of-funnel VSSL needed for cold acquisition
- New brand entering scale phase ($1M+ ready to push to $10M+)
- Existing brand whose top-of-funnel is too "ad-shaped" (high-skip, low-retention)
- Hero campaign for product launch
- Repurposing a winning emotion concept into VSSL format

## Input Required

- **Primary emotion** for the VSSL (single)
- **Data bank source quote(s)** anchoring the emotion
- **ICP profile**: who specifically should "feel like the ad is about them"
- **Product mechanism**: how the product solves the pain (in 1 sentence)
- **Hero claim**: the one promise the brand makes (in 1 sentence)
- **B-roll inventory**: what visual assets exist + what gaps need filling
- **Length target**: 3 min / 4 min / 5 min (longer = harder retention but deeper conversion)

## Execution

You are Vince Nijhof's senior strategist architecting a top-of-funnel VSSL. Your discipline: the viewer doesn't know it's an ad until 60+ seconds in. Brand reveal is delayed. Story comes first. Emotion is the spine.

### Step 1: The Guard-Down Open (0:00-0:15)
The first 15 seconds CANNOT feel like an ad. Required structure:

- **Visual**: Real human in a relatable scenario. NO logos, NO brand colors prominent, NO product visible.
- **Audio**: Voice that sounds like a friend telling a story, NOT a brand voice. Specific tone: warm, conversational, lower-energy than typical ad.
- **Hook line**: First sentence is a scenario or feeling statement, NOT a benefit claim. Examples: "I haven't slept through the night in 6 months" / "My hairdresser stopped mentioning it after I started doing this" / "I used to dread mornings"
- **Pattern interrupt**: Something visually unexpected — a moment of stillness, a quiet reveal, a contrast to typical ad cadence

### Step 2: The Identification Beat (0:15-0:45)
The "that's me" moment. Viewer recognizes their own pain in the speaker's story.

- **Specific scenario**: NOT "I had bad sleep." YES "I'd lie there at 3am scrolling Reddit because my mind wouldn't stop."
- **Sensory detail**: temperature, sound, body sensation, specific time
- **No solution yet**: keep the viewer in the pain. Resist the urge to introduce the product.
- **Use data bank quote language**: lift specific phrases customers have used in reviews

### Step 3: The Discovery Beat (0:45-1:30)
The pivot. Speaker discovers the solution. STILL not heavy-handed brand reveal.

- **Mechanism reveal**: HOW the discovery happened — friend, doctor, accidental find, late-night search
- **Skepticism beat**: "I didn't believe it would work" — pre-empts viewer skepticism
- **First experience**: brief, specific, sensory ("First night I used it...")
- **Soft brand introduction**: product/brand named, but as a character in the story not a sales pitch

### Step 4: The Outcome Beat (1:30-2:30)
The emotional payoff. The viewer feels the relief / confidence / belonging the speaker feels.

- **Specific outcome**: time, frequency, comparison ("I used to..., now I...")
- **Emotion language**: from data bank — verbatim
- **Social proof beat (optional)**: "My partner noticed before I said anything" / "I caught myself smiling"
- **Identity shift**: "I feel like the version of me I'd been waiting to be"

### Step 5: The Differentiation Beat (2:30-3:30)
Now the viewer is sold on the emotion — bridge to why THIS product specifically.

- **Mechanism explainer**: how does it actually work? (Product science, ingredient story, design principle)
- **Comparison to alternatives**: what others tried and why they failed (loss aversion)
- **Proof beat**: specific stat / before-after / trust signal
- **Hero claim delivery**: the brand's one promise, clearly stated

### Step 6: The CTA Beat (3:30-end)
Direct ask, low-pressure, time-sensitive.

- **Outcome restatement**: not features — restate the emotional win
- **Specific offer**: discount, bundle, guarantee
- **Time pressure**: limited stock, sale window, shipping deadline
- **Final hook**: callback to opening line — "If you're done with [opening pain]..."

### Step 7: Multi-Emotion Stacking Check
For VSSLs (uniquely vs. shorter formats), you can stack emotions in sequence:

- Beats 1-2: **Loss** (the pain you've been carrying)
- Beats 3-4: **Curiosity** (wait, there's a way)
- Beats 5-6: **Relief** (it actually worked)
- Beats 7-8: **Confidence** (now I'm the version of me I wanted to be)

ONE primary emotion still anchors. Others are the journey TO that emotion.

## Quality Gate

Score against `genius.md` rubric. Critical for this workflow:
- **Customer Voice Grounding** (9+ required): hook line + identification beat lift from data bank
- **Emotion Specificity** (9+ required): single primary anchor, even if stacking
- **Operational Realism** (8+ required): production requirements match pod capacity
- **Intent-First Discipline** (8+ required): differentiation from top performer is specific

If guard-down open feels like an ad in first 15 seconds → automatic rework. The whole architecture depends on the open.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Length | Beat compression |
|---|---|
| **3 min** | Beats 1+2 = 30 sec (compress identification); Beat 5 differentiation = 30 sec; CTA = 15 sec |
| **4 min** | Standard structure as written |
| **5 min** | Expand Beat 4 outcome (45 sec); add Beat 5b proof gallery (30 sec) |
| **2 min "VSSL-light"** | Beats 1+2+4+6 only (cut discovery + differentiation) — for retargeting warm audience |
| **YouTube ad version** | Same beats, but Beat 1 must be skip-resistant in first 5 seconds — pattern interrupt required |
| **TikTok adapt** | Compress to 60-90 sec; Beat 1 = 3 sec hook; Beats 2+3+4 collapsed to single arc; Beat 6 CTA |
