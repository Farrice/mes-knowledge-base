# /velocity-pivot — Infrastructure-Based Pivot Methodology

> When markets shift, don't rebuild from scratch. Map your existing infrastructure, identify the highest-TAM adjacent market, and enter in weeks using Danny Yeung's 5-company pivot methodology.

## When to Use
- Current market is declining or saturating
- Product-market fit isn't working and you need to redirect
- A massive new opportunity appears in an adjacent space
- Company has infrastructure assets (tech, team, distribution, regulatory) being underutilized
- Preparing for a post-funding or post-IPO pivot

## Inputs Required
1. Current business model and infrastructure inventory
2. Why the current market isn't working (decline, saturation, disruption)
3. Team capabilities beyond their current roles
4. Regulatory approvals, licenses, or certifications held
5. Distribution channels and customer relationships
6. Available capital and runway

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: Infrastructure Asset Mapping
Inventory everything you already have that transfers:

| Asset Category | What You Have | Transfer Potential (1-10) |
|---|---|---|
| **Technology** | Software, platforms, APIs, algorithms | |
| **Lab/Manufacturing** | Physical production capabilities | |
| **Team Expertise** | Skills beyond current job descriptions | |
| **Distribution** | Channels, logistics, fulfillment | |
| **Customer Base** | Existing customers who trust you | |
| **Brand/Credibility** | Market reputation, press, partnerships | |
| **Regulatory** | Licenses, approvals, certifications | |
| **Data** | Customer data, market intelligence, research | |
| **Capital** | Cash reserves, credit facilities, investor relationships | |

**Danny Yeung's pivot history**:
- Groupon competitor → used e-commerce infrastructure + team → Circle DNA
- Circle DNA → used lab infrastructure + genomics team → COVID PCR testing
- COVID testing → used brand credibility + capital ($100M+) + team → IM8

Every pivot reused 3+ asset categories.

### Step 2: Adjacent Market Scan
Find markets where your infrastructure creates unfair advantage:

**Scan criteria**:
1. **TAM** > $1B (not worth pivoting for small markets)
2. **Infrastructure fit** — at least 3 asset categories transfer directly
3. **Speed-to-entry** — can you ship in <30 days using existing assets?
4. **Competitive gap** — incumbents are slow, complacent, or non-existent
5. **Unit economics** — can you see a path to 3.0x+ LTV:CAC?

**Red flags** (don't pivot here):
- Requires entirely new team (infrastructure doesn't transfer)
- Regulatory approval timeline >6 months
- Existing competitors with 10x your resources AND speed
- Market requires brand you don't have and can't bootstrap

### Step 3: Pivot Speed Assessment
Danny Yeung's "In Three Weeks" standard:

| Phase | Timeline | Activities |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1** | Days 1-7 | Team redeployment, MVP scoping, first 10 customer conversations |
| **Week 2** | Days 8-14 | Build/adapt product, set up sales channel, initial creative |
| **Week 3** | Days 15-21 | Launch, first sales, immediate feedback loop |
| **Week 4+** | Ongoing | Scale what works, kill what doesn't, expand channel mix |

**If your pivot can't launch in 30 days**: Either you're rebuilding too much (use more existing infrastructure) or the market isn't actually adjacent (scan again).

### Step 4: Survival Economics
Manage cash through the transition:

- **Rule 1**: Never sell equity at the bottom (Danny held through $1B → $40M market cap crash)
- **Rule 2**: Let people go fast and with dignity — "Letting someone free early is way better"
- **Rule 3**: Cut non-essential burn immediately — speed of cost reduction = survival
- **Rule 4**: Revenue from new market must appear within 30 days — not 6 months
- **Rule 5**: If you have more cash than market cap → investors are wrong, not you. Be patient.

### Step 5: The Commitment Signal
Once the pivot decision is made:
- **All-in within 48 hours** — no "let's test this for a quarter"
- **Team messaging** — explain the pivot with full context (why, what, how)
- **Public narrative** — frame the pivot as evolution, not desperation
- **Founder goes first** — be the first person doing the new work

## Output Format
```
## PIVOT ARCHITECTURE — [Company] → [New Market]

### Infrastructure Asset Map
[9-category inventory with transfer scores]

### Target Market Analysis
[TAM, fit, speed-to-entry, competitive gap]

### 3-Week Launch Plan
[Day-by-day execution plan using existing infrastructure]

### Survival Economics
[Cash management + team restructuring plan]

### Commitment Signals
[First 48-hour actions that make the pivot irreversible]
```

## Quality Gate
- Must reuse at least 3 infrastructure asset categories (no greenfield pivots)
- Target market TAM must exceed $1B
- Launch timeline must be <30 days (with explanation if longer)
- Survival economics must show positive cash flow path within 90 days
- Must include specific team restructuring decisions (not vague "we'll adjust")
