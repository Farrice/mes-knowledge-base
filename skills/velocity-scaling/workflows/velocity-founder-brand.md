- Want to build a personal brand that accelerates business outcomes (not vanity metrics)
- Need to attract celebrity/influencer partnerships through founder visibility
- Transitioning from "company sells product" to "founder sells vision"

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: The Founder Credibility Inventory
Before building the brand, inventory what makes this founder investable/partnerable:

**Yeung's Operator Credibility Stack**:
1. **The Number** — One metric that proves execution ability
2. **The Pivot** — Evidence of adaptation under pressure
3. **The Speed** — "We built X in Y weeks" (compressed timelines)
4. **The Sacrifice** — Personal risk taken (not just time, but capital, reputation, comfort)
5. **The Conviction** — Skin in the game (equity held, salary deferred, personal investment)

**Rate each layer 1-10**. Anything below 5 = needs building before broadcasting.

### Step 2: Junyuh's "I Am The Niche" Applied to Founders
Build the founder's identity position — not a niche, but a category of one:

**The Identity Equation**:
```
[Founder Name] = [Unique Background] × [Industry] × [Operating Philosophy]
```

**Examples**:
- Danny Yeung = Serial HK entrepreneur × Supplement DTC × Speed-as-Moat
- Your equation: ____________ × ____________ × ____________

**Junyuh's Pillar Architecture for Founders**:

| Pillar | Purpose | Content Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| **Builder** | Show the work — behind-the-scenes of building | 40% |
| **Operator** | Share the operating principles (speed, delegation, decisions) | 25% |
| **Human** | Vulnerability, failures, lessons, personal life | 20% |
| **Visionary** | Where the industry is going, contrarian takes | 15% |

### Step 3: Noske's Authenticity Physics for Founder Content
Apply the energy audit and Dave Ramsey Effect:

**Energy Audit**: What content format lets the founder be most NATURAL?
- Video (talking head)? Voice memo? Written? Live Q&A?
- Yeung is natural in conversation → long-form podcast/interview format
- Match the format to the founder's authentic communication style

**Noske's Dave Ramsey Effect**: The most powerful founder content is the LEAST produced.
- No teleprompter
- No script
- No perfect lighting
- Raw thoughts from the founder's actual day
- "I just left a meeting where X happened. Here's what I learned."

**The 60-30-10 Rule for Founder Content**:
- 60% authentic/raw (phone recordings, voice memos, quick takes)
- 30% structured (weekly reflections, lesson-based posts)
- 10% produced (high-quality pieces for key moments — launches, partnerships, milestones)

### Step 4: The Founder Brand Content Engine (Weekly Cadence)

| Day | Content | Platform | Yeung Principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | "This week I'm building X" (Builder pillar) | LinkedIn + Twitter | Speed-as-Moat visibility |
| Tuesday | Operating principle deep-dive (Operator pillar) | Newsletter/LinkedIn | Decision velocity philosophy |
| Wednesday | Industry contrarian take (Visionary pillar) | Twitter + LinkedIn | Positioning as thought leader |
| Thursday | Behind-the-scenes decision or failure (Human pillar) | Instagram Stories / Short-form | Vulnerability = trust accelerator |
| Friday | Customer/team win celebration (Builder pillar) | All platforms | Proof of execution |

**Volume**: 5 posts per week minimum. Noske's principle: Consistency beats quality at the founder stage. Ship raw over not shipping at all.

### Step 5: The "Jockey Signal" Distribution Strategy
Make sure the RIGHT people see the founder brand:

**For Investors**:
- LinkedIn long-form posts showing operator credibility
- Podcast appearances on investor-facing shows
- Twitter threads on unit economics and growth decisions
- "Building in public" content showing speed and transparency

**For Celebrity/Influencer Partners**:
- Instagram showing product usage and lifestyle alignment
- Short-form video demonstrating genuine product conviction
- Tag potential partners' content with authentic engagement (not thirsty)

**For Customers**:
- Founder origin story (Junyuh's Problem → Pursuit → Payoff framework)
- "Why I built this" video on product pages
- Monthly founder letter to subscribers (email)

### Step 6: Founder Brand Measurement (Not Vanity)
Measure founder brand against business outcomes, not follower counts:

| Metric | Vanity Version (Ignore) | Business Version (Track) |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Total followers | Inbound investor/partner inquiries per month |
| Engagement | Likes and comments | DMs from potential customers or collaborators |
| Content performance | Viral posts | Posts that generated revenue or meetings |
| Authority | Speaking invitations | Partnership offers citing founder brand as reason |
| Trust | Testimonials about the founder | Customer retention correlated with founder content consumption |

**Yeung's test**: Does the founder brand make the business move FASTER? If it's generating vanity metrics but no velocity — it's a distraction, not an asset.

## Quality Gate
- Credibility stack must contain at least 3 layers scoring 7+ (if not, build credibility before broadcasting)
- Identity position must be a "category of one" (not generic "entrepreneur" or "CEO")
- Content format must match founder's natural communication energy (Noske mandate)
- 60%+ of content must be raw/authentic (not produced)
- Every piece must map to a business outcome (investor, partner, or customer acquisition)
- Follower count is NEVER a success metric — velocity impact is
