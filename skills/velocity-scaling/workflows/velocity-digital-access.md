# /velocity-digital-access — Digital Access Value Layer Engineering

> Transform a commodity product into an irresistible subscription by bundling expert access, masterclasses, and community — Danny Yeung's "lifestyle, not product" architecture.

## When to Use
- Subscription churn is too high (product alone isn't sticky enough)
- Need to justify a premium subscription tier
- Have celebrities, experts, or advisors whose access could be monetized
- Want to differentiate from competitors who sell the same functional product
- Building community as a retention flywheel

## Inputs Required
1. Product/service and current subscription tiers
2. Current churn rate and average subscription duration
3. Advisory board / experts / celebrities associated with the brand
4. Current content production capacity
5. Target audience and their aspirations beyond the product

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: Access Asset Inventory
Map every person whose expertise or access creates subscriber value:

| Person | Role | Domain Expertise | Availability | Content Potential |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Name] | Celebrity Partner | [Field] | X hrs/month | Masterclass, Q&A |
| [Name] | Medical Advisor | [Specialty] | X hrs/month | Wellness content |
| [Name] | Performance Coach | [Sport/Domain] | X hrs/month | Training programs |
| [Name] | Founder | Business/Lifestyle | Always | Behind-the-scenes |

### Step 2: Value Layer Architecture
Design the "beyond product" subscription value:

**Tier 1 — Product Only** (Base):
- Product at standard price
- Basic shipping
- No access to digital content

**Tier 2 — Product + Digital Access** (Premium):
- Product at slight discount (quarterly commitment)
- Exclusive monthly masterclass (live Zoom, recorded replay)
- Private community access
- Expert Q&A sessions (quarterly minimum)
- Behind-the-scenes content

**Tier 3 — All-Access** (VIP):
- Full product range
- All digital access from Tier 2
- Live group coaching sessions with experts
- Early access to new products
- Exclusive events or meetups

**Pricing psychology**: Tier 2 should be priced so that the digital access feels "free" vs. buying the product alone. "We don't want to just sell our product."

### Step 3: Content Production Calendar
Design the recurring content cadence:

| Frequency | Content Type | Producer | Platform |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly | Expert masterclass (live) | Celebrity/Expert partner | Zoom → subscriber portal |
| Bi-weekly | Q&A session | Rotating expert | Zoom or community forum |
| Weekly | Behind-the-scenes | Founder/team | Video + community post |
| Daily | Community thread | Community manager | Community platform |
| Quarterly | Special event | Full team + partners | Live event or webinar |

### Step 4: Community Architecture
Build the retention flywheel:

1. **Platform selection** — Private community (Discord, Circle, or custom app)
2. **Onboarding flow** — New subscriber immediately joins community + gets welcome from expert
3. **Engagement triggers** — Weekly challenges, progress sharing, peer support
4. **Expert presence** — Partners/experts post or respond at least monthly
5. **Success stories** — Highlight member results to create aspirational retention

### Step 5: Churn Reduction Metrics
Measure the digital access impact:

| Metric | Before Digital Access | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly churn rate | X% | -30% or more |
| Premium tier adoption | N/A | 50%+ of new subscribers |
| Masterclass attendance | N/A | 40%+ of eligible subscribers |
| Community daily active % | N/A | 15%+ |
| NPS score | X | +20 points |

## Quality Gate
- Must include at least 3 unique access assets (people whose expertise creates value)
- Premium tier must offer perceived value 3x the price difference over base tier
- Content calendar must show monthly minimum cadence (not "when we feel like it")
- Community architecture must include specific engagement triggers
- Churn reduction target must be quantified
