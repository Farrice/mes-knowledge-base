# /velocity-to-content — Danny Yeung × Kallaway: Content-to-Subscription Funnel

> Chain Danny Yeung's subscription economics with Kallaway's content psychology to build content funnels that acquire subscribers with world-class CAC/LTV ratios.

## When to Use
- Have a subscription product but content isn't converting to paid subscribers
- Want to use organic content as a customer acquisition channel
- Need to reduce paid CAC by building organic flywheel
- Combining content strategy with subscription unit economics

## Prerequisites
- Load: `skills/velocity-scaling/SKILL.md` (Danny Yeung — subscription economics)
- Load: `skills/content-psychology/SKILL.md` or Kallaway genius patterns (content architecture)

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: Reverse-Engineer the Content-to-Subscriber Funnel
Map the conversion chain from content view → subscription:

```
Content View (organic)
  → Click/Follow (2-5% of views)
    → Landing Page Visit (20-30% of followers)
      → Free Trial or Sample (10-15% of visitors)
        → Paid Subscription (30-50% of trial)
          → 3-Month Subscription (60%+ of paid)
```

### Step 2: Content Architecture Using Kallaway's Lego Bricks
Design each piece of content as a subscription funnel entry point:

**Content Types by Funnel Stage**:

| Stage | Kallaway Brick | Danny Yeung Economics |
|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | "Non-Obvious" angles + "Ugly Numbers" proof | Showcase the RESULTS the product creates |
| **Interest** | "Expert Breakdown" + "Contrarian Take" | Educate on subscription value (digital access, community) |
| **Desire** | "Case Study" + "Behind-the-Scenes" | Show celebrity partnerships, expert access, production quality |
| **Action** | "Direct CTA" + "Limited Offer" | 3-month subscription offer with specific AOV/discount |

### Step 3: Content-Driven CAC Calculation
Measure organic content as an acquisition channel:

```
Organic Content CAC = (Content production cost + Team time) / New subscribers from content
Blended CAC = (Paid CAC × Paid %) + (Organic CAC × Organic %)
Target: Organic content reduces blended CAC by 30-50%
```

### Step 4: The Subscription Content Calendar
Merge content psychology cadence with subscription economics:

| Week | Content Focus | Subscription Tie-In |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Behind-the-scenes product quality | "This is what subscribers get" |
| Week 2 | Celebrity/expert masterclass clip | Digital access value layer proof |
| Week 3 | Customer transformation story | 3-month subscription results window |
| Week 4 | Founder story / mission | Brand identity → subscription commitment |

### Step 5: Retention Content Strategy
Content that keeps subscribers subscribed:

- **Subscriber-only content** — Weekly drops that non-subscribers can't access
- **Progress tracking** — "Your 90-day journey" content within subscription portal
- **Community highlights** — Share member wins to reinforce belonging
- **Expert previews** — Tease upcoming masterclasses/Q&As
