# /velocity-operating-rhythm — Speed-as-Moat Founder Operating System

> Build and deploy Danny Yeung's founder operating system where speed IS the competitive advantage — compressed decision cycles, velocity-filtered teams, and AI-native operations.

## When to Use
- Building a founding team for a new venture
- Current team or organization is moving too slowly
- Need to redesign decision-making processes for speed
- Implementing AI-native operations across a team
- Preparing for a high-velocity growth phase

## Inputs Required
1. Current organizational structure and team size
2. Decision-making flow (who approves what)
3. Average time from idea to execution
4. Current AI tool usage across the team
5. Founder's current involvement level across departments

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: Decision Architecture Redesign
Map and compress every decision chain:

**Danny Yeung's Decision Hierarchy**:
| Decision Type | Who Decides | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Product changes | Founder (with full context) | Same day |
| Hiring/Firing | Founder + direct manager | 48 hours |
| Creative direction | Founder reviews + trusts team | Same day |
| Budget under $50K | Department lead | Same day |
| Budget over $50K | Founder | 24 hours |
| Regulatory/Legal | Legal team → Founder | 48-72 hours |
| Partnership deals | Founder | Same week |
| Pivots | Founder | "In three weeks" |

**Key principle**: "I always try to provide a lot of context. I never tell someone 'just do this.' I always give the reason why." Context-rich delegation eliminates back-and-forth.

### Step 2: Team Velocity Filter
Audit existing team and design future hiring through Danny Yeung's filter:

**Hiring Screen** (Non-Negotiable Gates):
1. Tell them the truth: "I will text you at midnight, 2am, 6am. Is that okay?"
2. Screen for response — recoil = disqualified, lean-in = proceed
3. Ask about the hardest thing they've done that took relentless effort
4. Test for growth mindset: "What did you learn from your biggest failure?"
5. Binary decision: "Would I want this person next to me in a trench at 3am?"

**Existing Team Audit**:
- Identify velocity blockers — who slows everything down?
- Is it skill? (trainable) or mindset? (not trainable)
- If mindset: "Letting someone free as early as possible is way better for them"
- Zero tolerance for velocity blockers. One slow person contaminates the chain.

### Step 3: Founder-in-the-Weeds Protocol
**Not micromanaging. Decision-accelerating.**

Design the founder's "touch points" across all departments:
- **Daily**: 30-minute morning standup (all departments, 5 min each)
- **Weekly**: Deep dive into 1 department (rotating)
- **Real-time**: Founder accessible for any urgent decision via direct message
- **Monthly**: Full company metrics review + course correction

**Context maintenance**: The founder reads every Slack channel, reviews every dashboard, knows every major customer complaint. Not to control — to have full context when a decision needs to happen in minutes, not weeks.

### Step 4: AI-Native Operations Mandate
"Everyone moving forward has to be AI native."

**Implementation**:
1. **Tool selection** — Choose 3-5 AI tools the entire company will use daily
2. **Training sprint** — 1 week intensive, showing each role how AI accelerates their work
3. **Usage tracking** — Minimum 1 hour/day AI usage per team member
4. **Output benchmark** — Measure output per person before and after AI adoption
5. **Non-compliance protocol** — Clear expectation: AI adoption is not optional

**Danny's personal cadence**: 2-3 hours/day using AI for strategy, creative review, and communications.

### Step 5: The "I'll Go First" Leadership Protocol
Speed culture starts at the top.

**Rules**:
1. Founder does the uncomfortable thing first — always
2. Never ask the team to work hours you won't work
3. Never ask the team to take risks you won't take
4. Share failures publicly before celebrating wins
5. When the team sees the founder going door-to-door in COVID zones for 8 hours, they know the standard

### Step 6: Launch Cadence Installation
Target: Idea → Revenue in 3 weeks.

Design the 3-week sprint:
- **Days 1-3**: Scope, build MVP, identify first 10 customers
- **Days 4-10**: Build, test, iterate (no perfection, just function)
- **Days 11-15**: Launch, first sales, customer feedback
- **Days 16-21**: Iterate based on feedback, scale what works

## Quality Gate
- Decision architecture must show <24hr cycle for non-regulatory matters
- Team audit must name specific velocity blockers (not vague "we're slow")
- AI mandate must include specific tools and usage minimums
- Launch cadence must show a concrete 3-week timeline for next initiative
- Founder protocol must include at least 2 "I'll go first" commitments
