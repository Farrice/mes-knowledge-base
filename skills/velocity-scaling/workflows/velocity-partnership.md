# /velocity-partnership — Celebrity/Influencer Equity Partnership Architecture

> Structure celebrity and influencer partnerships that actually work — equity-based, long-term, authenticity-verified. Danny Yeung's Beckham Partnership Model.

## When to Use
- Seeking a celebrity or major influencer partnership for a product/brand
- Evaluating an incoming partnership opportunity
- Restructuring an underperforming celebrity deal
- Building the approach strategy and pitch for a target partner

## Inputs Required
1. Product/brand and its current market position
2. Target celebrity/influencer (or criteria for ideal partner)
3. Product's clinical/performance evidence (if any)
4. Current brand strength independent of any celebrity
5. Budget and equity available for partnership

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: Partner Selection Criteria
Score potential partners on Danny Yeung's 5 non-negotiables:

| Criterion | Requirement | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Authentic User** | Must be a genuine, daily user of the product (or category) | "People just see right through" transactional deals |
| **3-Year Minimum** | Willing to commit for 3+ years | Brand building requires time. Short deals are wasted money |
| **Equity Alignment** | Willing to take equity (not just cash) | Equity creates alignment. Cash creates transactions |
| **Reputational Risk Tolerance** | Product must be safe enough for partner's reputation | "Everyone's going to go after [the celebrity]" — they need to feel SAFE |
| **Brand Independence** | Product must stand alone beyond the partner | Structural dependency = structural fragility |

### Step 2: The Power User First Pathway
Danny Yeung's preferred acquisition method — let the product sell itself:

1. **Seed the product** with the target's inner circle (coach, trainer, assistant, spouse)
2. **Never pitch** — let results create the inbound
3. **Measure** — if possible, have the target's team track performance metrics
4. **Wait** — the best partnerships are ones where the celebrity approaches you
5. **If no inbound after 90 days** → the product isn't right for them. Move on.

**Alternate path** (when you can't seed organically):
1. Research the target's actual routines/interests/needs
2. Identify a genuine product-fit (not just "they're famous")
3. Approach through the target's business manager (not creative agent)
4. Lead with product quality and clinical evidence, NOT exposure metrics
5. Solve for their reputational risk FIRST

### Step 3: Deal Structure Architecture
Design the Danny Yeung-grade deal:

**Equity Component**:
- Meaningful equity stake (not token)
- Vesting over the partnership term (3-year cliff or annual)
- Upside tied to company performance, not guaranteed payout

**Cash Component**:
- Below-market guarantee (equity compensates)
- Performance bonuses tied to measurable outcomes
- No guaranteed minimums that exceed realistic projections

**Obligations**:
- Weekly calls or bi-weekly check-ins
- Product development input (taste testing, feedback loops)
- X content pieces per year (appearances, social, events)
- Exclusive digital access (masterclasses, subscriber Q&As)

**Protections**:
- Morality clause (standard)
- "Brand beyond celebrity" clause — brand assets built during partnership remain with company
- Performance review at 12-month intervals
- Clear exit terms that don't destroy the brand

### Step 4: Digital Access Value Engineering
Transform the partnership into subscriber value:

- **Exclusive masterclasses** — Partner teaches their domain to subscribers
- **Live Q&A sessions** — Direct access (even occasionally) creates massive perceived value
- **Behind-the-scenes content** — Training routines, recipes, processes
- **Co-created products** — Partner involved in product development, not just endorsement

This transforms the celebrity from "face on the box" to "reason I subscribe."

### Step 5: The Reputational Risk Pitch
Frame the entire pitch around making the celebrity feel SAFE:

1. Lead with **clinical evidence** and third-party validation
2. Show **product independence** — "This brand works without you"
3. Demonstrate **operational integrity** — supply chain, quality control, testing
4. Provide **exit clarity** — "Here's exactly how this ends if it's not working"
5. Reference **existing credible partnerships** as social proof

## Quality Gate
- Partner must score 4/5 or higher on selection criteria
- Deal must include equity component (no pure-cash deals pass this gate)
- Digital access plan must include at least 3 subscriber-facing activities
- Must address reputational risk explicitly
- "Brand beyond celebrity" structural independence must be demonstrated
