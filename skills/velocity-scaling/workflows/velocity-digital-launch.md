### Step 2: The $350 Threshold × Subscription Play
Merge Cole's pricing psychology with Yeung's subscription architecture:

**Cole's rule**: Below $350 = impulse purchase zone. Above $350 = deliberation zone.

**Yeung's subscription overlay**:
- **One-time $47-$97**: Loss-leader. Goal is customer acquisition, not profit.
- **One-time $197-$347**: Sweet spot. High enough for quality signal, low enough for impulse.
- **Subscription $29-$99/mo**: Yeung's 3-month subscription play → offer quarterly at discount.
- **High-ticket $997+**: Only if you have proof. Cole's "context multiplier" repackages the same IP.

**The velocity play**: Launch at the $47-$97 loss-leader price to acquire customers fast. Then ascend them into the subscription or high-ticket backend.

### Step 3: Guerrilla Customer Acquisition (Yeung's Playbook Applied to Digital)
Adapt Yeung's physical-world guerrilla tactics to digital product launches:

**The Digital Starbucks Play** (Loss-Leader Brand Building):
Instead of buying Starbucks vouchers → buy ads promoting someone ELSE's free content that's adjacent to your product. When they associate your brand with curated quality → present your product.

Portfolio approach:
1. Curate 5 free resources from respected names in your space
2. Build a landing page: "[Topic] Starter Kit — 5 best free resources + ours"
3. You're borrowing credibility by association (same as Yeung's Starbucks move)
4. Run this at a loss. Goal: email capture + brand association.

**The Digital Cold-Call Bypass**:
- Identify 20 newsletter/podcast/community owners in your niche
- Don't pitch through their "partnerships" page (gatekeeper)
- Find the founder's email/DM directly
- Send a personalized message with specific value ("I noticed your audience asks about X. I built a tool that solves it. Happy to give your readers free access.")

**The Digital Midnight Deal Drop** (Create Shopping Behavior):
- Publish one piece of free, high-value content at the same time every day for 30 days
- Doesn't matter if no one reads it at first
- Consistency creates expectation → expectation creates habit → habit creates audience
- On day 31 → launch the product to the habit you built
