# /velocity-access-product — Danny Yeung × Tom Noske: Irresistible Subscription Access Layers

> Chain Danny Yeung's "Digital Access Value Layer" (GP-9) with Tom Noske's 7-ingredient product architecture to engineer subscription tiers that feel irresistible — not just "product + discount."

## When to Use
- Have a subscription product but churn is high (>10% monthly)
- Subscription feels like "auto-ship" instead of a membership
- Need to add perceived value without increasing COGS
- Want to transform a commodity product into an experience
- Building tiered subscription pricing

## Prerequisites
- Load: `skills/velocity-scaling/SKILL.md` (Danny Yeung — digital access, subscription economics)
- Load: Tom Noske genius patterns (7-ingredient product, Promise-Payoff, authenticity physics)

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: The Value Layer Diagnosis
Audit current subscription against Danny Yeung's "access value" benchmark:

**Yeung's principle**: "We don't want to just sell our product. It's about the lifestyle, the community, how we enrich people with knowledge."

| Value Layer | Present? | Quality (1-10) |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical product** (the thing they receive) | __ | __ |
| **Expert access** (masterclasses, Q&As, live sessions) | __ | __ |
| **Community** (private group, member interactions) | __ | __ |
| **Content** (exclusive education, behind-the-scenes) | __ | __ |
| **Progress tracking** (measurement, milestones) | __ | __ |
| **Identity** (member status, badges, recognition) | __ | __ |
| **Surprise** (unexpected bonuses, early access) | __ | __ |

**Score**: 5+ layers present and scoring 7+ = "irresistible zone."
Below 4 layers = commodity subscription.

### Step 2: Noske's 7-Ingredient Architecture Applied to Subscription
Map each subscription tier against Noske's product architecture:

**Ingredient 1: The Promise** (Why subscribe?)
- Not "get product monthly" → "Become [identity] within 90 days"
- Yeung's 3-month subscription is the PERFECT promise window
- Promise must be specific, measurable, and tied to transformation

**Ingredient 2: The Vehicle** (How does it get delivered?)
- Physical product + digital access layer + community
- Each delivery touchpoint reinforces the promise

**Ingredient 3: The Proof** (Why should they believe?)
- Yeung's celebrity partnerships = authority proof
- Customer transformation data = results proof
- Clinical testing = scientific proof

**Ingredient 4: The System** (What's the step-by-step?)
- 90-day journey map: Week 1-4, Week 5-8, Week 9-12
- Each phase has clear actions and expected milestones
- Progress tracking makes the journey visible

**Ingredient 5: The Community** (Who's on this journey with them?)
- Private member group (not public social media)
- Weekly wins thread
- Monthly member spotlights

**Ingredient 6: The Access** (What can't they get elsewhere?)
- Yeung's digital access layer: live expert masterclasses
- Monthly Q&A with advisory board / celebrity partner
- Early access to new products before public launch

**Ingredient 7: The Identity Shift** (Who do they become?)
- Member vs. customer language
- Milestone celebrations
- "You're not buying supplements — you're joining a performance protocol"

### Step 3: Tiered Subscription Design
Build 3 tiers using Yeung's economics + Noske's value architecture:

| Tier | Name Pattern | Contains | Pricing Psychology |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Base** | "[Product] Monthly" | Product only, auto-ship | Lowest friction entry |
| **Core** | "[Brand] Member" | Product + digital access + community | 2-2.5x base price, highest margin |
| **Elite** | "[Brand] Inner Circle" | Core + 1:1 expert access + priority everything | 4-5x base, scarcity-capped |

**Yeung's 3-month play**: Offer all tiers as quarterly subscriptions with a per-unit discount that makes 3-month the obvious choice. Ship everything at once. Recognize revenue immediately.

### Step 4: The 90-Day Retention Architecture
Map the first 90 days (one 3-month subscription cycle) to maximize renewal:

| Week | Product Touchpoint | Access Touchpoint | Emotional State |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unboxing + getting started guide | Welcome message from founder | Excited |
| 2 | First usage milestone | Community introduction | Curious |
| 3-4 | First results window | First expert masterclass | Hopeful |
| 5-6 | Habit formation period | Progress check-in email | Committed |
| 7-8 | Visible results expected | Member spotlight opportunity | Proud |
| 9-10 | "You've changed" moment | Celebrity/expert Q&A access | Belonging |
| 11-12 | Renewal preview + upgrade offer | "What's coming next quarter" reveal | Anticipation |

### Step 5: Noske's Promise-Payoff Audit
Score the subscription against authenticity physics:

- **Promise delivered within the subscription window?** (If 90-day promise needs 6 months → broken trust)
- **Proof visible to the subscriber?** (Can they SEE their own progress?)
- **Access genuinely exclusive?** (If non-members can get it → perceived value drops)
- **Community alive?** (Dead community = worse than no community)
- **Identity shift real?** (Do members talk differently about themselves?)

## Quality Gate
- Subscription must include 5+ value layers scoring 7+ each
- 3-month quarterly subscription must be the default/prominent option
- Retention architecture must cover all 12 weeks of the first cycle
- Digital access must feature real experts (not generic content)
- Promise must be achievable within the subscription window
- Noske's authenticity physics must pass — no performative community
