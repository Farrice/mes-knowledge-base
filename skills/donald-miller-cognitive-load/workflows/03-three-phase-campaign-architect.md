# Workflow 03: Three-Phase Campaign Architect

> **Produces**: Full Curiosity → Enlightenment → Commitment campaign blueprint
> **Use When**: Building the complete messaging campaign structure from sound bites through collateral
> **Genius Context**: Load `genius.md` before executing
> **Prerequisite**: Run `02-peace-soundbite-generator` first — this workflow requires existing PEACE sound bites

## Pre-Flight

**Required Inputs:**
- Completed PEACE Sound Bite System (from Workflow 02)
- Business name, product, and target customer
- Available channels (website, social, email, ads, sales team, events — which are active?)
- Budget tier: Bootstrap (free/low-cost channels only) | Growth (paid ads + content) | Scale (full omnichannel)

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: PEACE sound bites must exist. This workflow does not create sound bites — it architects the campaign that deploys them. If no sound bites exist, redirect to Workflow 02.

## Execution

You are Donald Miller architecting the Three-Phase Messaging Campaign. You are building the house — the front steps (curiosity), front porch (enlightenment), and front door (commitment). Every piece of collateral you design embeds the PEACE sound bites.

### Step 1: The House Diagnostic

Draw the current state of the business's messaging house:

```

     │   (Commitment)      │

     │   (Enlightenment)   │

     │   (Curiosity)       │

     Verdict: [Welcoming House / Haunted House / Porch-Only / Door-Only]
```

Map what currently exists across all three phases. Identify gaps.

**Output**: House diagnostic with gap analysis.

### Step 2: Phase 1 — Curiosity Architecture (Front Steps)

Design the curiosity layer — the first-contact messaging that makes strangers say "Wait, tell me more."

**Collateral Plan**:
For each available channel, specify which PEACE sound bite is deployed and how:

| Channel | Sound Bite | Format | Frequency |
|---------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Website hero | Problem | H1 header | Permanent |
| Social media posts | Problem + End Result | Text posts | 3x/week |
| Ad headlines | Problem | Single-sentence ads | Ongoing |
| Email subject lines | Problem or End Result | Subject line | Per send |
| Business cards | Answer | Single line | Permanent |
| Elevator pitch | Full PEACE chain | 30-second script | On demand |

**The Rule**: Curiosity collateral uses ONLY the 5 PEACE sound bites. No new language. No creative variations. Verbatim deployment.

**Output**: Complete Phase 1 collateral plan.

### Step 3: Phase 2 — Enlightenment Architecture (Front Porch)

Design the enlightenment layer — material that earns the right to sell by building confidence. Minimum 3 pieces. Ideal: 300+.

**Required Elements** (design at least 3):

1. **Lead Generator** — A free asset that delivers value while embedding sound bites
   - Title: [based on Problem sound bite]
   - Format: PDF / checklist / video / quiz
   - Sound bites embedded: [which ones and where]

2. **Explanatory Content** — Blog post, video, or social series that teaches while reinforcing sound bites
   - Topic: [derived from Problem + Change]
   - Format: Long-form / video / carousel
   - Sound bites embedded: [which ones and where]

3. **Trojan Horse Content (GP9)** — Entertainment that secretly carries sound bite principles
   - Hook: [celebrity, trend, or curiosity gap]
   - Payload: [which PEACE principles are embedded]
   - Format: Social post / video / newsletter edition

4. **Process Explainer** — How it works, what it costs, what the risk is
   - Steps: [3-step process derived from Answer sound bite]
   - Risk reversal: [guarantee, trial, or refund]
   - Sound bites embedded: [which ones and where]

5. **Social Proof Compilation** — Testimonials and results structured around the PEACE narrative
   - Before state: [echoes Problem sound bite]
   - After state: [echoes End Result sound bite]
   - Transformation: [echoes Change sound bite]

**Output**: 3+ enlightenment pieces fully designed with sound bite integration.

### Step 4: Phase 3 — Commitment Architecture (Front Door)

Design the commitment layer — incentives that remove the last barrier. Minimum 3 incentives.

**Required Elements** (design at least 3):

1. **Impulse Buy Stimulator** — A time-limited or value-added reason to act NOW
   - Incentive: [bonus, bundle, discount, or exclusive access]
   - Deadline mechanism: [real scarcity, not fake urgency]
   - CTA: [derived from Answer sound bite]

2. **Risk Eliminator** — Something that makes "no" harder than "yes"
   - Format: Free trial / money-back guarantee / first-session-free / pay-after-results
   - Connection to PEACE: [which sound bite does this reinforce?]

3. **Community/Belonging Play** — An invitation to join something
   - Format: Private group / early access / founding member / VIP tier
   - Connection to Change sound bite: [how does membership = identity upgrade?]

**Output**: 3+ commitment incentives fully designed.

## Quality Gate

- [ ] PEACE sound bites are embedded verbatim in every phase — no creative variations
- [ ] All three phases have minimum 3 pieces of collateral
- [ ] Phase sequence is preserved: Curiosity BEFORE Enlightenment BEFORE Commitment
- [ ] Enlightenment material includes at least one Trojan Horse piece (GP9)
- [ ] Commitment incentives include at least one risk eliminator
- [ ] Timeline maps realistic deployment cadence
- [ ] No phase is skipped — all three are fully designed
- [ ] Sound bites appear word-for-word in every collateral piece

**ENFORCEMENT**: Skipped phases = FATAL (haunted house). Creative sound bite variations = FATAL (GP6 violation). Commitment before Curiosity = structural failure, resequence.

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: Verify against GP7 (Haunted House), GP9 (Trojan Horse), GP6 (Decade Repetition) in `genius.md`.
