> **Produces**: 3+ enlightenment pieces (lead gens, blogs, video scripts, social) with sound bites embedded
> **Use When**: Building "front porch" material that earns the right to sell
> **Genius Context**: Load `genius.md` before executing
> **Prerequisite**: PEACE sound bites (from Workflow 02) must exist

## Pre-Flight

**Required Inputs:**
- Completed PEACE Sound Bite System
- Business name, product, target customer
- Preferred content formats (blog, video, social, email, podcast, PDF — pick 2-3)

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: PEACE sound bites must be locked. Enlightenment material EMBEDS sound bites — it doesn't replace them.

### Step 2: Lead Generator Design

Design the primary lead generator — the free asset that converts strangers into leads while embedding sound bites.

**Title**: Derived from Problem sound bite (e.g., Problem = "Worried about money" → Lead Gen = "5 Signs You'll Never Stop Worrying About Money")

**Format**: [PDF guide / checklist / quiz / video mini-course / template]

**Structure**:
- Opening: Problem sound bite (verbatim) — "You're reading this because [Problem]."
- Body: 3-7 actionable insights that demonstrate expertise
- Each insight: Embeds one or more PEACE sound bites naturally
- Close: Answer sound bite (verbatim) — the CTA to take the next step
- Final line: End Result sound bite (verbatim) — the vision of life after

**Cognitive Load Check**: Every sentence in the lead gen must score 0. A lead gen that requires effort to read will not be consumed.

**Output**: Complete lead generator outline with sound bite placement markers.

**Output**: 2-3 Trojan Horse pieces designed.

### Step 4: Process Explainer

Design the "how it works" piece that removes uncertainty:

**Structure**:
1. **Opening**: Problem sound bite — name the pain
2. **Step 1**: [Simple first action] — derived from Answer sound bite
3. **Step 2**: [What happens next] — demonstrate competence
4. **Step 3**: [The result] — Change + End Result sound bites
5. **Risk Reversal**: What makes this safe to try
6. **CTA**: Answer sound bite — verbatim

**Format**: Landing page section / standalone page / video script / email

Every step must score 0 cognitive load. No jargon. No "our proprietary methodology." Plain language.

**Output**: Complete process explainer.
