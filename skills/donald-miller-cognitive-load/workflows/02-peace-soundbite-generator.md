# Workflow 02: PEACE Sound Bite Generator

> **Produces**: 5 interlocking zero-load sound bites (Problem → Empathy → Answer → Change → End Result)
> **Use When**: Creating the messaging foundation for any business, product, or campaign
> **Genius Context**: Load `genius.md` before executing

## Pre-Flight

**Required Inputs:**
- Business name and what it sells
- Target customer (who they serve)
- The outcome or transformation the product delivers
- Any existing messaging (optional)

If inputs are sparse, ask:
1. "What specific problem does your customer have RIGHT BEFORE they find you?"
2. "What does your customer's life look like 90 days after using your product?"
3. "If your customer described what you do to their friend in 10 words, what would they say?"

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: You must name ONE specific problem. If multiple problems are named, force the One-Hole Lock (GP2) — one problem, no commas.

## Execution

You are Donald Miller building a PEACE Sound Bite System. This messaging foundation will be repeated verbatim across all channels for 10+ years. Target: zero cognitive load on every sound bite.

### Step 1: Problem Excavation — Own One Hole

Identify ONE specific problem. Not two. Not a category. One felt, visceral "hole."

**Tests**:
- Uses a comma? → Too many problems. Pick one.
- Applies to any business? → Too generic.
- Triggers survival pattern-match? → If no, it's not a real problem.

**Survival Categories**: Financial safety | Social belonging | Health | Competence | Emotional security

Generate 3-5 candidates. Score each for cognitive load. Select the one that scores zero AND triggers the strongest survival response.

**Output**: Selected Problem sound bite + scoring rationale.

### Step 2: End Result Mirror — The Bookend Flip

Write the End Result IMMEDIATELY after the Problem. The End Result is the Problem's positive mirror.

**Bookend Rule (GP3)**: "Worried about money" → "Never worry about money again." Same words. Flipped polarity.

Generate 2-3 candidates. Score each.

**Output**: Selected End Result sound bite + bookend confirmation.

### Step 3: Empathy — The Guide's Voice

Position the brand as guide OUTSIDE the hole — Gandalf, Yoda, Haymitch. Strong AND empathetic. 3-10 words max.

**Anti-Patterns**:
- ❌ "We've been there too" → puts you in the hole
- ❌ "We understand your challenges" → generic
- ✅ "We know how that feels" → specific emotional acknowledgment
- ✅ "That frustration is real" → validates without claiming victimhood

Generate 3 candidates. Score each.

**Output**: Selected Empathy sound bite.

### Step 4: Answer — The Rope

The product appears NOW — after problem, empathy, and end result. The simplest possible action.

**Rules**: One verb + one noun max. No features. No technology. No methodology. 5 words or fewer.

Generate 3 candidates. Score each.

**Output**: Selected Answer sound bite.

### Step 5: Change — The Identity Upgrade

Not just problem solved — identity transformed. Who they BECOME.

**Rules**: Personal, not business-level. Achievable, not fantasy. Increases urgency via identity stakes.

**Examples**: "Get good with money" | "Become the leader your team deserves" | "Finally feel like you know what you're doing"

Generate 3 candidates. Score each.

**Output**: Selected Change sound bite.

Compile all 5 sound bites:


### Step 7: Stress Tests

**Test 1 — Standalone (GP5)**: Each works as a headline, social post, or billboard independently.
**Test 2 — Chain**: All 5 chain into a natural 30-second pitch read aloud.
**Test 3 — Decade (GP6)**: Exact words repeatable for 10+ years. No trends, years, or current events.

**Output**: Pass/fail on all three tests.

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Problem is singular — one hole, no commas
- [ ] Problem triggers survival-brain response
- [ ] Problem and End Result perfectly bookend
- [ ] Empathy positions brand OUTSIDE the hole
- [ ] Answer is 5 words or fewer
- [ ] Change addresses identity, not just problem resolution
- [ ] Every sound bite scores exactly 0 cognitive load
- [ ] All three stress tests pass
- [ ] A 12-year-old could understand every sound bite

**ENFORCEMENT**: Multi-problem = FATAL (One-Hole Lock). Non-zero load = rewrite. Broken bookend = rewrite End Result. Brand as hero = restructure guide position.

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: Verify against GP2, GP3, GP5, GP6 in `genius.md`.
