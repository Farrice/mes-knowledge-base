# Cross-Domain Patterns — Sam Vander Wielen

*Where these mechanics transfer outside "$2K course launch." The physics, not the skin (MES 3.0 Anti-Pattern Lock).*

## The transferable physics

Strip the course-launch skin and three mechanics remain domain-agnostic:

1. **Permission inverts status.** Asking to sell, then narrowing who may buy, converts a pitch into an audition. Works anywhere the seller has more demand than capacity — or can credibly act as if they do.
2. **Personal contact at volume beats personalization at scale.** 583 two-minute videos outperform any merge-tag sequence, because the cost is legible to the recipient. The mechanic is *visible expenditure of a scarce resource*.
3. **Patience is a conversion asset.** A seller who is demonstrably not going anywhere can decline urgency. The six-month window isn't tolerated — it's spent, buying relaxed confidence.

## Transfers into Farrice's live lanes

### Proof-to-Market ($2,500 sprint, supplement/performance brands)
- **Consent-then-disqualify** → the discovery call open. Ask permission to make a recommendation at the end; then name who this sprint is wrong for, with a self-interested reason (*"I have to deliver this personally, so I need a brand that can actually ship the fixes"*).
- **The gap-naming close** (Exemplar 2) → the teardown-to-offer bridge. The free teardown delivers real value on the visibility problem; the paid sprint answers the question the teardown just opened.
- **Order-bump stack** → sprint add-ons for the more advanced client the core sprint deliberately underserves.

### LinkedIn Cash Launch (active campaign)
- **Newsletter-as-magnet + forecast post** → the weekly LinkedIn post that trails the next deliverable, with exclusivity + urgency dials, no lead magnet required.
- **Reverse voice-of-customer** → post asking what the audience wants covered, then build the Angle Map angle from the answers. Doubles as demand evidence for the $750 offer.
- **Pause-and-redo CTA** → record once, capture the DM-keyword CTA and the link CTA in the same session.

### Parallax (Substack)
- **Subscriber-as-hero subject lines** → drop-in, this week, zero build.
- **The "got it" reply hook** → welcome sequence email 1; lifts deliverability and opens conversations.
- **Shoulder issues** → the reason Parallax can hold an audience that has already extracted what it needed from any single essay.

### Jen Santulan (listings)
- **Personal video at volume** → a 2-minute video per inquiry on a $2M listing makes the "two minutes for a $2,000 sale" arithmetic look conservative.
- **Handwritten note + flywheel question** → post-close, with a question that routes to Instagram. Fits the luxury register directly.
- ⚠️ **Do not transfer the disqualification move into housing copy without fair-housing review.** "Who this isn't for" language carries regulatory risk in real estate that it does not carry in course sales.

## Transfers by business shape

| Shape | What ports cleanly | What breaks |
|---|---|---|
| **Agency / services** | Consent-disqualify, personal video, gap-naming close, buyer-temperament targeting | Order-bump stack (no checkout); the 4-day window (services sell on pipeline, not launch) |
| **SaaS** | Newsletter-as-magnet, reverse VoC, "got it" hook, shoulder issues | The unscalable 5% at self-serve price points — the Two-Minute Test fails below ~$500 ACV |
| **Physical product / DTC** | Handwritten note flywheel, organic-tested ad creative, real-time newsjacking | Webinar architecture; the 6-month patience posture (inventory cost forbids it) |
| **Local service** | Personal video, note flywheel, temperament targeting, replay-as-audio | Multi-bump checkout; the list-scale ramp |
| **Newsletter / media** | Every email mechanic, shoulder issues, exclusivity+urgency, forecast post | The high-ticket close (needs a $1K+ product to justify the 5%) |

## Where the Two-Minute Test fails

The non-scalable layer is not universal. Sam's arithmetic — *"two minutes for a $2,000 sale"* — assumes:
- **A price point ≥ ~$1,000.** At $97, two minutes per prospect destroys margin.
- **A founder who is the trust asset.** If the buyer doesn't care who you are, the personal video is noise.
- **A machine underneath.** She can do this only because *"there was nothing else for me to do."* Bolting the 5% onto an un-automated business produces a burned-out founder, not a launch.

**Diagnostic before recommending the unscalable layer**: state the per-unit time cost against per-unit revenue out loud. If the sentence sounds absurd, cut the act or batch it down.

## Domain transfer — non-obvious

- **Conference / workshop sales**: the consent ask maps exactly onto a keynote that ends in an offer.
- **Fundraising**: the disqualifier ("we're not the right fit for every donor") plus visible patience is unusually strong in nonprofit major-gift work.
- **Hiring**: the personal-video-at-volume mechanic transfers directly to candidate experience at companies competing on culture rather than comp.
- **Community / membership renewal**: the note-with-a-question flywheel is a retention mechanic, not just an acquisition one.
