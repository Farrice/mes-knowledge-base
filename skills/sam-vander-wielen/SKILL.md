# Sam Vander Wielen — Live-Webinar Launch Systems

**$500,000+ in under four days. From a 50,000-person list. With one full-time employee. On an eight-year-old product she didn't change.** $198,000 of it in about five minutes, live on the webinar — 128 sales at $2,000–2,400 each. Three months earlier, the same slides did $438,000.

Sam Vander Wielen is an attorney turned entrepreneur selling legal education to online business owners. Her thesis: *"I am proof that courses are not dead, webinars are definitely not dead, email marketing is not dead either, and AI isn't killing me."*

## The Two Engines

**1. Engineered non-scalability.** She automates 95% of the launch *specifically so she can afford the 5% that doesn't scale* — 583 personalized videos and 329 handwritten notes in a single launch. *"There was nothing else for me to do because my launches are such well-oiled machines."* Her arithmetic: **"Two minutes for a $2,000 sale is not bad."**

**2. Consent-and-disqualify selling.** She asks permission to pitch, then actively narrows who's allowed to buy — with a reason that costs her something real (*"I know I'm going to have to deal with you on the back end"*). Attendees report: **"I didn't feel like I had to buy. I wanted to."**

## The Recognition Test

> **"Would a registrant who did not buy still feel like they got the better end of the deal?"**

Every asset in this system passes it before shipping. Full rubric, anti-patterns, and voice in **`genius.md` — load it before any workflow.**

## When NOT to Use

| Situation | Go here instead |
|---|---|
| **Product doesn't exist / unvalidated** | `/pat-flynn-validate-with-one-person`, `/ash-problem-validation`. This model *relaunches* — it does not launch net-new. |
| **The offer itself is the problem** | `/offer-redteam`, `/jh-offer-audit`. No launch mechanic fixes a broken offer. |
| **Manufacturing demand / scarcity positioning** | `/oversubscribed-launch-sales-system` (Priestley). Sam supplies the room's mechanics; Priestley engineers the demand. Stack them. |
| **Evergreen funnel, no live component** | `/suzuki-product-funnel`, `/russell-brunson-funnels` |
| **Sub-$500 product** | Most of this survives, but the non-scalable layer fails the Two-Minute Test. Take `/sv-subject-line-hero` and `/sv-newsletter-magnet` only. |
| **Show-rate diagnosis, not repair** | `/jh-show-rate-diagnostics` first, then `/sv-showup-engine` to fix it. |
| **General newsletter growth strategy** | `/newsletter-growth-audit`, `/matt-mcgarry-newsletters`. This skill covers only her three distinct mechanics. |
| **Writing the emails themselves** | `/copy-engine`, `/writers-room` — load `references/source-quotes.md` as the voice anchor. |
