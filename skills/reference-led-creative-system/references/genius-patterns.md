# Genius Patterns

These patterns are the reusable craft logic behind the Reference-Led Creative System. They preserve the conditions that produced the Performance Evidence Journal without turning its appearance into a house style.

## 1. Separate taste research from identity ownership

Refero supplies evidence about composition, hierarchy, density, media, and interaction. The active brand or product design system owns typography, color, components, and identity. A reference may suggest a move; it does not inherit authority over the brand.

## 2. Make difference structural

Three options are only useful when they differ across at least four consequential axes: composition, density, typography behavior, media strategy, surface treatment, motion, or signature device. Color swaps and renamed mood boards are one direction pretending to be three.

## 3. One primary, bounded borrowing

Each direction gets one primary reference. Borrow at most one or two named moves from supporting references. This preserves a coherent point of view and prevents the averaged, generic middle produced by unrestricted mood-board blending.

## 4. Local visibility before preference

A visual choice is valid only when the reviewer can inspect every option. Remote URLs remain provenance; local previews and a local contact sheet are the decision surface. Missing visibility is a failed run, not a minor rendering issue.

## 5. Lock the source logic before production

The reference lock records what to preserve, what may be borrowed, what is forbidden, which existing tokens remain authoritative, and which surface will test the direction. It turns taste from a vague mood into a falsifiable production contract.

## 6. Prove on the smallest useful surfaces

Apply the lock to one or two representative surfaces before expanding. Choose surfaces that reveal hierarchy, evidence treatment, conversion logic, and media behavior. Expansion before proof multiplies drift.

## 7. Preserve the decision sequence, not the costume

The golden run is evidence for the process: distinct research territories, a visible choice, one recommendation, a lock, bounded application, and visual QA. Its colors, type scale, evidence cards, and editorial tone are not defaults for unrelated work.

## 8. Name the proof state honestly

Local capture proves visibility. A verifier proves file integrity and replayability. Human review proves taste approval. Neither proves market performance, conversion lift, or brand fit without the corresponding evidence.
