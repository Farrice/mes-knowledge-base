## Original route and context

1. Install and authorize Refero through OAuth.
2. Load Refero Design and its visual workflow.
3. Frame Health Performance as premium, evidence-led, and distinct from biohacker luxury, supplement hype, and hospital sterility.
4. Search five visual angles: scientific editorial, cinematic athletic, private longevity, evidence-first health data, and high-fashion sports performance.
5. Retrieve strong preview and full-style evidence.
6. Synthesize three territories with one primary reference and bounded borrowing.
7. Recommend the territory that extends Premium Minimal instead of requiring a rebrand.
8. Create the reference lock before building.
9. Build one master-brand hero and one Angle Map decision page.
10. Validate visually and mechanically.

## Why the output worked

- The territories were strategically different, not three luxury-wellness variants.
- The winner made Farrice's actual advantage—edited judgment and inspectable proof—visible.
- Alpine Bio owned mood and density; Operate owned ledger structure; Integrated Biosciences owned metadata cadence.
- Borrowed colors, pills, rounded cards, biotech imagery, and extra type systems were rejected.
- Premium Minimal stayed the identity owner, so the vertical felt new without becoming a second brand.
- The journal feeling came from information behavior, not biotech decoration.
- The build tested two surfaces before system expansion.
- The preserved core stops at the hero and decision page; later channel production is not required to reproduce the creative-system result.

## Regression guard

- Remote preview URLs are provenance only.
- No visual verdict before local images and contact sheet pass.
- The process may repeat; the journal aesthetic may not repeat by default.
- One existing identity owner must be named before references are translated.
- One or two surfaces must prove the lock before expansion.
- Channel adaptations and unrelated project tests do not become part of the golden run by proximity.
