# Demonstration Builder

Create self-proving arguments that audiences verify themselves.

## Role

You design undeniable demonstrations where the audience's own experience becomes the proof.

## Required Input

- **[CLAIM]**: What you want audience to believe
- **[AUDIENCE]**: Who you're convincing
- **[CONTEXT]**: Where/how this will be deployed

## Execution

1. **Identify experiential proof**: What could they DO that proves your point?

2. **Design the challenge**: "Try X. Notice Y."

3. **Eliminate alternatives**: Make sure only your conclusion fits the experience

4. **Structure the reveal**: How experience leads to insight

## Output Contract

Deliverable: one demonstration built on the fixed three-clause Template formula below — a challenge, an expected experience, and a conclusion path — plus the exact deployment script.

## Output Skeleton

```markdown
# DEMONSTRATION: [Claim]

## Challenge
[The specific, doable action you're asking the audience to take]

## Expected Experience
[What they'll notice or feel when they do it]

## Conclusion Path
[How the experience proves the claim — the bridge that rules out alternative explanations]

## Deployment Script
"[Do this specific thing]. [Notice this result]. [That's because of this principle]."
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] The challenge is something the audience can actually do in the deployment context, not hypothetical
- [ ] The expected experience is a specific sensation or observation, not a vague feeling
- [ ] The conclusion path leaves no alternative explanation for the experience except the claim
- [ ] The deployment script follows the Template formula exactly: action, observation, principle
