## Voice Consistency Check (Tone Stays Authentic)

### Hook Lines Read-Aloud Test

- [ ] Read aloud the hook line from each video
  - Test: Does it sound like Jen speaking, or like a teleprompter?
  - ❌ BAD: "Are you considering purchasing a property in the San Fernando Valley?"
  - ✅ GOOD: "So you want to buy in SFV but you're not sure if $800K is enough?"

### Beat Outlines Make Sense

- [ ] For each video, the beat outline follows the hook promise (hook says X, beats deliver on X)
  - ❌ BAD: Hook promises "best neighborhoods for first-time buyers," but beats talk about financing
  - ✅ GOOD: Hook promises "neighborhoods," beats deliver 3 neighborhood examples
- [ ] Beats are not filler (each beat answers a question or proves a point)

### Register Consistency

- [ ] If video is FTHB-focused, tone is warm-friend (from VOICE.md register 1)
- [ ] If video is luxury-focused, tone is authority POV (from VOICE.md register 2)
- [ ] No mixing of registers within a single video

## Fair-Housing Audit (Screening for Violations)

### No Demographic Steering

- [ ] Hook lines: no "safe neighborhood," "family-friendly," "great for young families," etc.
- [ ] Beat outlines: no description of neighborhood demographics (age, race, family size, disability status)
- [ ] Recording notes: no direction like "film in diverse neighborhood" or "capture family moments"

### Housing-Stock / Commute / Amenities Only

- [ ] Hooks/beats focus on: housing stock (age, bedrooms, square footage), commute time, price, amenities (yard, garage, updates)
- [ ] NO reference to: schools, safety, religious institutions, family-friendly, walkability to (kids') activities

### Current-Event / Financial Program Language OK

- [ ] If beats mention FTHB programs (HOP80/120, LIPA, Greenline, MyHome), that's on-brand and fair-housing OK
- [ ] If beats mention rate environments or affordability trends, that's factual and OK

**Fair-Housing Scan Result:**
- [ ] All 20 video hook lines pass fair-housing lint
- [ ] All beat outlines pass fair-housing lint
- [ ] All recording notes pass fair-housing lint

## If Changes Needed (Recovery)

### Theme / Voice Changes

| Feedback | Recovery |
|----------|----------|
| "This doesn't feel like me" | Review VOICE.md section (registers, signature phrases); rewrite hook lines to match her actual voice |
| "Week 2's theme feels forced" | Check demand report; is there a different angle for the same audience? Or split into two themes? |
| "I would never say that" (specific hook) | Replace hook with alternative from the same demand question; ensure it's speakable to her |
| "The CTA tone is off" | Check VOICE.md CTA phrasing; ensure all 20 CTAs use her authentic language (not generic sales talk) |

### Filming Feasibility Changes

| Feedback | Recovery |
|----------|----------|
| "FILM THESE THREE FIRST aren't actually quick wins" | Pick 3 simpler videos (location she goes to regularly, minimal setup); move current 3 to later weeks |
| "I can't shoot Location A" | Substitute with location Jen uses (office, coffee shop, a regular client listing); regroup batch 1 accordingly |
| "I don't have time for 3 filming sessions" | Compress: can Batch 1 + 2 happen same afternoon? (different rooms/areas, same location?) Or combine Batch 2 + 3 if locations are close? |
| "Wardrobe is too varied" | Reduce outfit changes; could she wear same blazer across multiple weeks? Different shirt underneath? |
| "I don't have the props listed" | Remove props or substitute with things she has; simplify the visual concept if needed |

### Fair-Housing Changes

| Feedback | Recovery |
|----------|----------|
| Fair-housing lint flags "family-friendly" language | Remove from hooks/beats; replace with specific housing-stock detail (e.g., "has a yard for outdoor space" instead of "great for families") |
| Lint flags demographic targeting | Rewrite beat to focus on housing situation (price, size, age) not demographics; or remove the video entirely |
| Lint flags school references | Remove "near good schools"; replace with "short commute to DTLA" or "neighborhood has [specific amenity]" |

### Demand Traceability Changes

| Feedback | Recovery |
|----------|----------|
| A video doesn't trace to demand report | Go back to DEMAND-REPORT.md; find a demand phrase that matches this video's topic; update the calendar to cite it, OR remove the video and replace with one from top-5 unanswered Qs |
