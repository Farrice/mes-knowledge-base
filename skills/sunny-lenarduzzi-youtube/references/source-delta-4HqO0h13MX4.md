## Evidence Classes

Keep these separate in every run:

1. **Source evidence:** what the Authority.io source teaches.
2. **Practitioner evidence:** the operator's experience, credentials, protocol, and limits.
3. **Demand evidence:** dated buyer events and collected payment.
4. **Delivery evidence:** proof that the promised unit occurred and what it revealed.
5. **Outcome evidence:** permissioned client change inside the measured scope.
6. **Repeatability evidence:** comparable clients, stable delivery, capacity, acquisition, retention, and margin.

## Source-Reported Claims That Cannot Transfer

- Authority.io reach, niche count, revenue, client count, and business-scale claims.
- Presenter close rates, time-to-result, monthly revenue, and delegation results.
- Client-program revenue and outcome examples.
- Causal claims that POP, community, content, or a specific sequence guarantees a result.
- Any price, call quota, cohort size, profit margin, or revenue target not supported by the current practitioner's actuals.

These claims may be described only as `SOURCE_REPORTED`. They cannot become a forecast, stage gate, practitioner credential, testimonial, or guarantee.

## Additive Capability Test

The delta changes behavior only when a run produces all of the following:

- a specific transformation and disqualification boundary,
- one paid founding-offer contract,
- one primary acquisition path,
- capacity-aware sales and delivery limits,
- live delivery appropriate to the practice type,
- separate evidence and permission ledgers,
- actual `sent / held / sold / collected` counters, and
- exactly one terminal verdict: `ADVANCE_TO_REPEATABILITY`, `REVISE_POP`, or `STOP_OR_HOLD`.

No automatic move to scale is allowed.
