# Profitable Offer Prototype

## Role

Operate the Authority.io / Sunny-led paid POP method as captured in source `4HqO0h13MX4`. Move a credible transformation into one paid founding engagement, learn through live delivery, and make the next decision from evidence. Do not import the source's revenue examples as practitioner proof or require a course, cohort, or community when the practice does not need them.

## Input Required

1. Practitioner identity, relevant experience, credentials, scope, exclusions, and protocol.
2. One buyer, current problem, desired outcome, failed alternatives, and disqualifiers.
3. Current offer hypothesis, price status, terms status, and proof stage.
4. Actual source, practitioner, demand, delivery, outcome, and repeatability evidence.
5. Permission status for measurement, quotation, anonymization, and reuse.
6. Sustainable weekly sales calls; delivery and support hours; operational weeks; and concurrency.
7. Exactly one proposed acquisition path.
8. Dated `sent / held / sold / collected` counters.
9. Authorized scope and forbidden external actions.

Use `UNKNOWN`, `UNTESTED`, `NO_EVENT`, or `NO_PERMISSION` instead of filling an evidence gap with an assumption.

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Prove the Right to Offer

- State the buyer, zero state, desired state, and bounded outcome.
- Tie the transformation to practitioner experience or a qualified protocol.
- Record who should not be served and why.
- Stop when credentials, scope, mechanism, claims safety, or buyer specificity is inadequate.

### Phase 2: Build the Validation Bridge

- Design interviews around exact problem language, failed attempts, investment conditions, desired outcome, fit, and no-fit signals.
- Separate relationship-driven interest from problem-driven demand.
- Treat interviews, positive comments, and stated interest as research. They do not increment `sold` or `collected`.
- Preserve the diagnostic conversation when moving to sales. Add an offer only when fit exists.

### Phase 3: Contract One Paid Founding Unit

Define:

- buyer and problem,
- observable outcome and exclusions,
- scope, format, access, and duration,
- price status and payment terms,
- what is built before sale versus during delivery,
- the exact event that increments `collected`, and
- cancellation, refund, confidentiality, and evidence-permission status.

One founding unit must be understandable without a complete curriculum, funnel, content engine, or automated delivery system.

### Phase 4: Fit Sales and Delivery to Capacity

- Choose exactly one primary acquisition path.
- Set a sustainable sales-call limit and delivery ceiling.
- Select delivery components by function. Curriculum supplies a route; coaching adapts it; community may provide peer reinforcement. Omit any component without a necessary job.
- For consulting diagnostics, use the actual diagnostic functions. Do not force coaching or community.

### Phase 5: Run the Evidence Loop

Prepare separate ledgers for:

- source evidence,
- practitioner and protocol evidence,
- demand events,
- delivery events and friction,
- client outcomes, and
- repeatability evidence.

For each live unit, capture questions, missing steps, wrong sequence, adherence, decisions, wins, adverse events, method changes, and permission. A testimonial or case study requires genuine satisfaction and explicit permission.

### Phase 6: Issue One Terminal Decision

- `ADVANCE_TO_REPEATABILITY`: collected payment, completed delivery, fit, capacity, method learning, and permissioned evidence support another comparable run.
- `REVISE_POP`: a paid or delivered unit exposed a correctable buyer, scope, mechanism, delivery, price, or capacity defect.
- `STOP_OR_HOLD`: payment has not been collected, the unit has not been delivered, claims or scope are unsafe, the buyer is vague, the mechanism is not credible, or authorization is missing.

No verdict automatically authorizes scale or registration. It also does not authorize outreach, publication, payment setup, or economic implementation.

## Output Contract

### Routed branch

Produce one POP decision packet containing:

1. Transformation and practitioner-fit record.
2. Validation and no-fit interview plan.
3. Paid founding-offer contract.
4. One acquisition path plus sales and delivery capacity limits.
5. Delivery-component map with a named job for each included component.
6. Evidence and permission ledger.
7. Dated `sent / held / sold / collected` counters.
8. One terminal decision with its evidence, proof gaps, and next checkpoint.

### Hold branch

When practitioner authority, scope, buyer, outcome, mechanism, paid-unit definition, claims safety, or authorization fails, stop before offer construction. Return only the fit verdict, accepted and rejected evidence, exact missing fields, forbidden moves, terminal hold reason, and next human checkpoint. Do not invent the founding offer, acquisition plan, delivery components, or economics.

Both branches keep `economics_status: LOCKED_PRE_RUNTIME`, registration false, and external actions empty throughout the cold build.
