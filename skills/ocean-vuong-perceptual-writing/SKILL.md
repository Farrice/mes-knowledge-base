# Ocean Vuong Perceptual Writing

Deploy the perceptual writing intelligence of a MacArthur "Genius Grant" Fellow across **every** writing domain. This is the skill of *re-seeing* — rescuing familiar subjects from automatized perception and producing sentences the species has never had. Now with the **Haunting > Capturing** deployment system: content optimized for permanent habitation, not attention capture.

> *"Has the species had this sentence yet? If it has, I keep going."* — Ocean Vuong
