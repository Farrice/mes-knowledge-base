## Fidelity assessment

**Doctrine: HIGH.** Every pattern in `genius.md` carries a verbatim quote and a date. Nothing was
paraphrased into a claim the source does not make. The two Substack posts were captured in full
(not summarized), and the Forbes article was captured in full body text rather than via an
abstracting fetch.

**Procedure: LOW — and both execution prompts are marked `fidelity: low` in frontmatter.**
The reason is specific and structural, not a hedge:

> **Liu published a doctrine, not a method.** She names the three layers and states what originates
> in each. She does **not** publish how she runs any of them. Her actual procedure is taught live,
> privately, in a small paid cohort, and no part of it is public.

Therefore, in both prompts:
- The **layers, tests, tiers, travel mechanics and every quote** are hers, sourced and dated.
- The **ladder order, the stopping rule, the defaults inventory as a counted artifact, the brief's
  eight-section shape, and the audit's output contract** are composed by this house from her stated
  principles. Both prompts state this in-body and require the output to state it in a fidelity note.

**What is absent from the skill because it is absent from the sources — do not backfill:**
- Any Machine Layer content: models, parameters, prompt syntax, workflows, post-production process.
- Any lighting, camera, lens, colour or grading vocabulary. She uses none in public.
- Any prompt formula, style code, or reference-writing convention.
- Any procedure for running the Translation Layer. Load `nick-st-pierre` or `rory-flynn` for that
  layer's actual craft — that is precisely the seam this skill is designed to sit above.
- Any second worked example. Only the Gucci Primavera piece is documented end-to-end, and it is
  labelled in `genius.md` as **observed**, not as a taught method.

**Anti-inflation note.** The brief permitted 1–2 prompts and warned against padding. Two were
forged because two genuinely distinct deliverables exist in the corpus — a diagnosis (backward-looking,
locates a failure) and a direction brief (forward-looking, prevents one). A third would have
required inventing methodology. Per `directives/prompt-forging-spec.md`: *"A skill with 3 honest
prompts beats one with 12 padded ones."*
