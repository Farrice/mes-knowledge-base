# Reference — Cross-Domain Patterns (Where Alex's Frameworks Transfer)

> Alex's frameworks are domain-named (Meta ads) but mechanism-general. This reference catalogs how each pattern transfers cleanly outside ads — into LinkedIn, email, Substack, sales pages, cold outreach, career architecture, and client retention for any service business.

---

## Why The Frameworks Transfer

Alex built the CES through atomic decomposition of 50,000+ Meta ads. The artifacts (Creative Pyramid, Content Grid, Vacation Test, Care Square, 5-Step Wealth Loop) are domain-tagged but operate at the level of mechanism, not surface. The mechanism — "how do humans pay attention, recognize repetition, allocate trust, and convert" — is the same wherever attention is being competed for.

---

## Pattern 1 — Creative Pyramid → LinkedIn Content Variation

**Original (Meta ads)**: Avatar / Idea-Style-Hook / Volume-Diversity-Relevance.

**Transfer (LinkedIn)**:
- **Avatar** = which reader-state are you posting for? (Aware-but-stuck, Newly-curious, Active-shopper.)
- **Idea** = the post's central claim or insight.
- **Style** = format (carousel, single image, text-only, story-arc, listicle, contrarian-take, behind-the-scenes).
- **Hook** = the first 2 lines (LinkedIn truncates after ~210 chars on mobile).
- **Volume** = posts per week (audit: are you taking enough shots?).
- **Diversity** = Vacation Test on your last 10 posts. Do they read as 10 distinct experiences or 10 versions of the same post?
- **Relevance** = does the language match what your ICP actually says about the problem? (Apply IVOC mining: comments on competitor posts, niche subreddits, YouTube replies.)

**Use when**: planning a content series, auditing why your LinkedIn is flat, briefing a ghostwriter.

## Pattern 2 — Content Grid → Email Subject Line Testing

**Original**: Ideas (X) × Styles (Y) = ad concept matrix.

**Transfer (email)**:
- **X-axis**: 5 message angles (curiosity, urgency, social-proof, contrarian, direct-benefit).
- **Y-axis**: 5 hook types (question, declarative, "here's what...", numerical, fragment).
- Each intersection = one subject line concept.
- 25 subject lines from one grid.

**Vacation Test transfer**: review your last 10 sent subject lines. Would a subscriber recognize them as 10 distinct emails or 10 versions of the same email? If the latter, your open rate has a structural ceiling Andromeda-style.

## Pattern 3 — Vacation Test → Any Content Series

**Original**: imagine viewer goes on vacation, comes back, sees the next ad — "I've seen this" or "this is new"?

**Transfer (universal)**: applies to any content where the same audience sees consecutive pieces:
- **Substack editions** — would a subscriber who read Edition 02 think Edition 03 is "more of the same" or "new"?
- **Sales emails in a sequence** — does email 4 feel different from email 3?
- **YouTube uploads** — across your last 10 thumbnails + titles, do they read as 10 distinct experiences?
- **Twitter/X thread arcs** — does each thread offer a fundamentally different shape from the last 5?

**Failure mode is the same everywhere**: surface variation (different topic, different headline) without fundamental variation (different STYLE, different angle of attack) = audience fatigue, declining engagement, "everyone unfollowed me" surprise.

## Pattern 4 — Care Square → Any Service Business

**Original (DTC creative agency)**: Results / Perception / Relationship / Efficiency.

**Transfer (ghostwriting, advisory, consulting, SaaS CSM, real estate agent, coaching)**:

| Dimension | Ghostwriting example | Advisory example | Real estate example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Results** | Engagement, follower growth, attributed leads | KPI movement on the metric they hired you for | Sale price, days on market, multiple-offer count |
| **Perception** | The post they show their CEO, the "voice doc" they show peers | The deck they show their board | The MLS listing copy, the open-house staging photos |
| **Relationship** | Quote their podcast back to them, send a hamper on a deal-close, kid-name memory | Birthday card, "just thought of you" resource sends | Anniversary of their move-in, holiday card, kid graduation note |
| **Efficiency** | Reply within 4hrs, AI-augmented metrics dashboard, weekly summary | Same-day turnaround on urgent asks, async-default communication | Showings within 24hrs of request, automated update emails |

**Universal insight that transfers**: clients leave service providers who deliver Results without Perception. Build all four. Audit monthly.

## Pattern 5 — 5-Step Wealth Loop → Any Revenue Architecture

**Original**: Identify problem → Solve → Systematize → Delegate → Sell.

**Transfer (career, business, content, product)**:

| Step | Career path | Content business | Product launch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Identify | Name the bottleneck (not "I need clients" but "I can't get on the call") | Name the audience problem (not "people want growth" but "people want X without Y") | Name the unmet need precisely |
| 2. Solve | Build the specific solution to the named bottleneck | Build the specific content/lead-magnet/offer | Build the specific feature |
| 3. Systematize | Document as a repeatable process | Document content production SOP | Document operational SOP |
| 4. Delegate | Hire to operate the system | Hire VA / contractor / writer | Hire ops manager |
| 5. Sell | Sell yourself, then license, then content | Sell direct, then productize, then course | Sell direct, then channel, then platform |

**Universal use**: any time you're tempted to skip from Identify to Sell without Systematize. The middle steps are where the leverage compounds.

## Pattern 6 — Land & Expand → Any Client-Facing Offer

**Original (creative strategy)**: front-end with current buzzword (creative strategy), expand to email/retention/funnel/media buying = $20K/month per account.

**Transfer (Farrice's authority-flywheel work)**:
- **Front-end** = whatever the coach/founder is currently anxious about (right now: AI, authority, ghostwriting, podcast presence, Substack).
- **Expand** = once inside, surface adjacent problems: "your LinkedIn is great, what's happening with your email list?" → "your newsletter is converting, want to design the offer it sells into?" → "the offer is working, want me to script the VSL?"

**Pricing math**: $3K front-end → $7K expansion #1 → $10K expansion #2 → $15K full retainer. 12-18 month arc per client.

## Pattern 7 — Hyperlocal Outreach Specificity → Any Cold Outreach

**Original**: pharmacy on Hagley Road next to bigger Boots → "we help independents capture more of [town]'s market share."

**Transfer (any cold target)**:
- For a strength coach: "I noticed you train hybrid athletes in [city] — most of your competitors are positioning as physique coaches. Want to talk about content that pulls in your ideal client?"
- For a Substack writer: "Edition 04 of [their newsletter] mentioned you're testing paid tiers. I work with newsletters at the conversion stage — happy to send a 1-page audit of your free→paid funnel."
- For a SaaS founder: "I saw your case study with [specific customer] — the activation gap they mentioned is exactly what we solve for B2B teams in [their vertical]."

**Mechanism**: name 1-2 things only THIS recipient has. Demonstrate research before asking for time. Reply rate >5x generic.

## Pattern 8 — 2 Atoms of Attention → Any Hook (Any Format)

**Original**: Relevance OR Difference. Both = best.

**Transfer (universal)**:
- **Hook writing** (any platform) — force-classify every hook attempt as Relevance (audience already cares) or Difference (cuts through the pattern).
- **Headlines** — same test.
- **Cold subject lines** — same test.
- **Talk titles for podcasts/YouTube** — same test.
- **Thread openers / first lines of LinkedIn posts** — same test.

If neither, it's noise. Both = best. Stop arguing about "intrigue" — it's a derivative.

## Pattern 9 — Three S's → Any Operator's Calendar

**Original**: System / Selling / Strategy = founder focus. Execution gets delegated.

**Transfer (any operator phase)**:
- **Solo freelancer year 1-2**: 70% Execution is fine. You're learning by doing.
- **Solo freelancer year 3-5**: 50% Execution + 50% Selling. Building book of business.
- **Sole-operator with retainers**: 30% Execution + 30% Selling + 30% Strategy + 10% System. Begin systematizing.
- **First hire (VA/contractor)**: 20% Execution + 30% Selling + 25% Strategy + 25% System.
- **Small team (3-5 people)**: 10% Execution (chosen, not default) + 30% Selling + 30% Strategy + 30% System.

**Audit cadence**: quarterly. Tag last 4 weeks. Adjust hiring/delegation accordingly.

## Pattern 10 — Atomic Decomposition → Any Stuck System

**Original**: "What is this in itself?" applied to every component of a stuck process.

**Transfer (universal diagnostic for any operator)**:
- **Stuck content workflow** — list every action in your last successful content piece. For each, write a 1-sentence first-principle. Group. The shared roots are your delegate-able system.
- **Stuck client acquisition** — decompose "I can't get clients" into atomic steps (find prospect → research → reach out → respond → call → propose → close). Identify which atom breaks.
- **Stuck pricing decisions** — decompose "is this priced right?" into (cost to deliver / value to client / market comparable / your time-leverage). Score each. Identify the binding constraint.
- **Stuck creative blocks** — decompose "I don't know what to write" into (no audience clarity / no point-of-view / no insight stockpile / no production system). Identify which is empty.

**Master move**: when in doubt, decompose. The act of decomposition is the work; the framework is the artifact.
