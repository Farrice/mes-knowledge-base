# Workflow 08 — Land-and-Expand Pitch

> **Tier 2 — Practitioner.** The client-acquisition + revenue-expansion architecture. Use for new client outreach, upsell conversations, positioning audits, sales-page H1 updates. Two clients = $20K/month freelance business.

---

## Pre-Flight Gate

- [ ] You have a target client profile (specific, not "anyone")
- [ ] You know what the market is currently obsessing over (the live buzzword)
- [ ] You have skills/services that go BEYOND the front-end (so the expansion path is real)

**If you only have one skill**: this workflow won't help. Build skill breadth first via `/myatt-decompose` on "I'm too narrow to expand inside accounts."

## Execution

You are Alex Myatt designing a Land-and-Expand engagement. The front-end mechanism is whatever the market is currently buying. Don't argue about whether you "really do" the buzzword — adopt it as a positioning tool, then expand inside.

### Step 1 — Market Buzzword Surf (5 min)

```
CURRENT MARKET BUZZWORD AUDIT

What is the audience SPENDING against right now?
- Top 3 buzzwords currently dominating their conversations: [list]
- Which one is at peak (most-said, most-paid for): [primary]
- Which one is rising (will be peak in 6-12 mo): [secondary]
- Which one is fading (was peak last cycle): [avoid]

YOUR FRONT-END BUZZWORD CHOICE: [primary or rising — your call]

WHY THIS ONE: [what evidence shows it's where the spend is]
```

**Examples by domain (2025-2026 snapshot — refresh per use)**:
- DTC ecom: Creative Strategy / AI ad generation
- B2B SaaS: AI agents / RevOps / GTM efficiency
- Authority/Coaching: Authority engineering / Substack growth / Personal brand systems
- Real estate: AI-powered marketing / video listing content
- Local services: AI receptionist / AI lead gen / Done-for-you content

### Step 2 — Front-End Offer Design

```
FRONT-END OFFER

Title: [the buzzword + your specific angle]
- Example: "Andromeda-Era Creative Strategy Sprint"
- Example: "Authority Flywheel Sprint for Coaches"
- Example: "Substack-to-Revenue System for Newsletter Operators"

Promise (in 1 sentence): [outcome the buyer can taste]

Format: [sprint / audit / monthly retainer / done-for-you / done-with-you]

Duration: [time-boxed for low commitment]

Price point: $[X]
- Front-end pricing rule: high enough to feel real, low enough to land 1-2 calls per week
- Typical range: $2K-$5K for sprints, $3K-$7K/mo for entry retainers

What's included: [3-5 deliverables, dense, specific]

What's deliberately EXCLUDED: [the things you'll upsell to in expansion]
```

### Step 3 — Expansion Path Map

```
EXPANSION PATH (90-180 days post-landing)

EXPANSION 1 (~Day 30-60): [adjacent problem revealed during front-end work]
- Trigger: [the moment in delivery when you can naturally surface this]
- Offer: [packaged solution]
- Price: $[X]
- New monthly run-rate: $[front-end + expansion 1]

EXPANSION 2 (~Day 60-120): [next adjacent problem]
- Trigger: [delivery moment]
- Offer: [package]
- Price: $[X]
- New monthly run-rate: $[front-end + 1 + 2]

EXPANSION 3 (~Day 120-180): [full retainer / ownership]
- Trigger: [usually end of front-end + expansion 1+2 success window]
- Offer: [full marketing function ownership]
- Price: $[X / month]
- New monthly run-rate: $[full retainer]

12-MONTH ACCOUNT VALUE: $[total]
```

**Math check**: 2 clients on full retainer at $10K-$20K/month each = $20K-$40K/month freelance business. This is the path Alex describes.

### Step 4 — Outreach Architecture

```
OUTREACH MESSAGE (for cold or warm)

Use Hyperlocal Specificity pattern (see /myatt-grid or genius.md):
- Open with named-detail recognition specific to the recipient
- Connect to the buzzword they're currently anxious about
- Offer the front-end as a low-commitment sprint/audit
- NO pitch slap — make the front-end so cheap and clear that "yes" is easy

TEMPLATE (adapt per recipient):
"Hey [name] — noticed [specific detail about their business / a recent post / a gap I can see].
I work on [buzzword] specifically for [their type of business]. Most operators I see are [common problem].
I do a [front-end sprint name] — [specific deliverable + timeline + price]. Worth a 15-min call to see if it's a fit?"

OUTREACH CADENCE:
- Initial send: [day]
- Follow-up 1: [+3 days, value-add not just bump]
- Follow-up 2: [+7 days, alternative angle]
- Move to "long-term nurture" after: [3 attempts]
```

### Step 5 — Discovery Call Script (the close)

```
DISCOVERY CALL — 30 MIN STRUCTURE

Min 0-5: Rapport + their context
- "Tell me what you've been working on this quarter"
- Listen for: which buzzword they care about, what they've tried, what's frustrating

Min 5-15: Diagnose (this is the value moment)
- Run a mini-version of the relevant Alex audit (Andromeda audit / Care Square / etc.)
- They should leave the call with 1-2 actionable insights even if they don't sign

Min 15-25: Propose the Front-End
- Connect their stated problem to your front-end offer
- Time-box, price, deliverable
- "I think this is a 4-6 week sprint to get [specific outcome]. Sprint fee is $[X]. Want me to send the proposal?"

Min 25-30: Next steps
- Proposal sent within 24hrs
- Decision deadline: 5 business days
- If yes → kickoff call scheduled in 7 days
- If no → schedule follow-up in 60 days (markets change)
```

### Step 6 — Title Refresh

If your current LinkedIn headline / sales-page H1 / email signature uses LAST cycle's buzzword, swap it:

```
TITLE REFRESH

Current title: [whatever]
New title: [current buzzword + your specific angle]

Where to update:
- [ ] LinkedIn headline
- [ ] LinkedIn About first line
- [ ] Email signature
- [ ] Sales page H1
- [ ] Twitter/X bio
- [ ] Calendar booking page
- [ ] Speaking bio
```

**Mechanism reminder**: titles are positioning tools, not identity statements. Swap when the market swaps.
