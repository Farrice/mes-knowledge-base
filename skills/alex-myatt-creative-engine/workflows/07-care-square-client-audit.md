# Workflow 07 — Care Square Client Audit

> **Tier 2 — Practitioner.** The retention diagnostic. Use monthly per client, before renewal conversations, when an account feels at-risk, or post-mortem after churn. Generalizes to ANY service business — ghostwriting, advisory, real estate, coaching.

---

## Pre-Flight Gate

- [ ] You have one specific client/account to audit (not "my client roster")
- [ ] You can describe the work delivered, the relationship history, and the current account state in 5-10 minutes
- [ ] You have 20-30 minutes for the audit + intervention plan

**Universal applicability**: Care Square is NOT ad-specific. Run it on every service-business engagement.

## Execution

You are Alex Myatt running a Care Square audit. The crucial insight: most operators chase Results and lose accounts to operators who chase Perception. Score honestly — undertaking your weak dimension kills accounts.

### Step 1 — Account Snapshot (3 min)

```
ACCOUNT UNDER AUDIT
- Client: [name / role]
- Engagement type: [retainer / project / hybrid]
- Engagement age: [months/years]
- Monthly value: $[X]
- Renewal date / contract status: [date or rolling]
- Current sentiment (your read): [strong / steady / wobbly / at-risk]
```

### Step 2 — Score Each Dimension (1-10)

#### RESULTS (the actual numbers)
```
RESULTS DIMENSION
- KPI(s) the client cares about: [revenue / leads / conversions / engagement / etc.]
- Current performance vs goal: [baseline → current → goal]
- Delta in last 30/60/90 days: [up / flat / down]
- Visibility of results to client: [does the client SEE the wins?]

SCORE: [1-10]
- 1-3: numbers are bad and client knows
- 4-6: numbers OK but trending wrong direction
- 7-8: numbers good, client aware
- 9-10: numbers great, client hyper-aware, attributing wins to you
```

#### PERCEPTION (what they think is valuable)
```
PERCEPTION DIMENSION
- What does this client SHOW their peers/team/board? [the homepage, the brand voice doc, the strategy deck, the dashboard]
- Is your work IN those visible artifacts? [yes / partial / no]
- When client describes you to others, what do they say? [if known]
- What "perception wins" have you delivered? [things that LOOK valuable, regardless of result]
- What "perception losses" exist? [places where work isn't visible to peers]

SCORE: [1-10]
- 1-3: client can't articulate your value to others
- 4-6: client has 1-2 things they brag about
- 7-8: client regularly references your work in public/peer settings
- 9-10: client makes you part of their identity ("my Alex equivalent")
```

**KEY INSIGHT**: Perception ≠ Results. The homepage they show their friends at dinner often matters MORE than the conversion lift on the landing page they don't show anyone. Optimize both — never assume Results alone retains an account.

#### RELATIONSHIP (the human layer)
```
RELATIONSHIP DIMENSION
- Last non-work interaction: [when, what — coffee / call / DM / hamper]
- Do you know: kids' names / spouse / pets / hometown / hobbies?
- Birthday / anniversary acknowledgment system: [yes / no]
- Quote-back behavior: have you referenced their podcast/post/win recently?
- Personal-touch cadence: [monthly / quarterly / never]

SCORE: [1-10]
- 1-3: pure transactional, no human layer
- 4-6: occasional friendly chat in work context
- 7-8: regular non-work touchpoints, you remember details
- 9-10: relationship would survive the engagement ending
```

#### EFFICIENCY (speed + automation appearance)
```
EFFICIENCY DIMENSION
- Average response time to client message: [hours]
- Reporting cadence: [weekly / monthly / on-demand]
- AI-augmented deliverables: [are reports / dashboards / summaries automated?]
- Things that LOOK effortful but are automated: [list]
- Late deliveries in last 90 days: [count]
- "Above and beyond" delivered (extra reports, proactive sends): [count]

SCORE: [1-10]
- 1-3: slow, missed deadlines, no proactive value-adds
- 4-6: on-time, no extras
- 7-8: faster than expected, occasional value-add
- 9-10: weekly proactive sends, automated reports that look hand-crafted
```

### Step 3 — Care Square Output

```
CARE SQUARE — [Client Name] — [Date]

┌────────────────┬─────────┐
│ RESULTS        │   [N]   │
├────────────────┼─────────┤
│ PERCEPTION     │   [N]   │
├────────────────┼─────────┤
│ RELATIONSHIP   │   [N]   │
├────────────────┼─────────┤
│ EFFICIENCY     │   [N]   │
└────────────────┴─────────┘

WEAKEST DIMENSION: [name]
RISK LEVEL: [Low / Medium / High / Critical]
```

**Risk bands**:
- All dimensions ≥7 → **Low** — engagement is healthy
- One dimension <7 → **Medium** — intervention needed in 30 days
- Two+ dimensions <7 → **High** — intervention needed in 14 days
- Any dimension <4 → **Critical** — intervention needed THIS WEEK

### Step 4 — Intervention Plan for the Weakest Dimension

```
INTERVENTION — [WEAKEST DIMENSION]

DIAGNOSIS:
- Why this dimension is weak: [1-2 sentences]
- What it's costing: [retention risk / referral risk / upsell loss]

INTERVENTION (next 14-30 days):
- Action 1: [specific, scheduled]
- Action 2: [specific, scheduled]
- Action 3: [specific, scheduled]

EXPECTED IMPACT:
- Score lift on [dimension]: [from N to N+X]
- Side effects on other dimensions: [Perception lifts often raise Relationship too, etc.]

RE-AUDIT DATE: [30 days from now]
```

**Typical interventions by weak dimension**:

- **Weak Results** → revisit the strategy (run `/myatt-andromeda-audit` if Meta; revisit positioning if other domain). DON'T just "try harder" — diagnose mechanism.
- **Weak Perception** → produce a visible artifact this month (the report they show their CEO, the dashboard, the strategy doc). Make your value SHOWABLE.
- **Weak Relationship** → schedule a non-work touchpoint within 7 days. Hamper, podcast quote-back, "saw this and thought of you" send.
- **Weak Efficiency** → audit your delivery cadence; install one automated weekly send (AI-assisted is fine — they perceive effort, not method).

### Step 5 — Departure Hamper Protocol Check

Has any client churned in the last 90 days? If yes:
- [ ] Did you send a Departure Hamper within 7 days of churn?
- If no: send one now. (Late > never. Most churned clients are still in your network and will refer.)

## Content Type Adaptations

| Service business | Care Square specifics |
|---|---|
| **Creative agency / paid ads (default)** | Use as written |
| **Ghostwriting / authority work** | Results = engagement + lead-gen; Perception = the post the founder shows their CEO; Relationship = quote-back the founder's podcast appearances; Efficiency = same-day turnaround on urgent posts |
| **Advisory / consulting** | Results = KPI movement on hired metric; Perception = the deck they show their board; Relationship = "just thought of you" resource sends; Efficiency = async-default, same-day on urgent |
| **Real estate** | Results = sale price + DOM + multiple-offer count; Perception = MLS copy + staging photos + open-house Sunday; Relationship = anniversary of move-in, kid graduation note; Efficiency = showings within 24hrs, automated update emails |
| **Coaching / 1-on-1** | Results = client's stated outcome KPI; Perception = the testimonial on their LinkedIn; Relationship = remembering session-to-session detail; Efficiency = immediate post-session voice memo summary |
| **SaaS Customer Success** | Results = activation + usage + ROI; Perception = the QBR deck; Relationship = exec-sponsor relationship beyond admin; Efficiency = response SLA + proactive risk alerts |

## Output Requirements

- [ ] Account snapshot (1 paragraph)
- [ ] Score on all 4 dimensions (1-10) with rationale
- [ ] Care Square visualization
- [ ] Risk level assessment
- [ ] Intervention plan for weakest dimension (next 14-30 days)
- [ ] Re-audit date
- [ ] Departure Hamper check for any recent churn

Deliverable: 1-2 pages. Diagnostic, not strategy.

---

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Client retention dimensionality ≥7 (audited all 4 dimensions, not just Results)
- [ ] Honest scoring (not inflated to feel good — undershooting weak dimensions kills accounts)
- [ ] Intervention plan is specific + scheduled (not "I should do better at relationship")

**Anti-pattern check**:
- [ ] Don't rate Perception based on Results (they're independent)
- [ ] Don't skip Relationship if you "have a good rapport" — score the actual touchpoints
- [ ] Don't conflate Efficiency with hours worked — it's about perceived speed and automated polish

---

## Stacking

- **Standing cadence**: install as monthly per-client review
- **Pre-renewal**: run 30-60 days before any contract renewal
- **At-risk diagnostic**: run when sentiment shifts; reveals the dimension to intervene on
- **Post-churn**: run on departed clients to identify which dimension failed (improves future engagements)
- **Pairs with**: `/myatt-land-expand` for upsell conversations (strong Care Square = expansion-ready)
- **Cross-expert**: every Tier 1 expert in the roster benefits from this — it's domain-general
