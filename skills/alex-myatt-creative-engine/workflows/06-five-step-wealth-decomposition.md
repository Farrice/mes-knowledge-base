# Workflow 06 — 5-Step Wealth Decomposition

> **Tier 2 — Practitioner.** The Socratic atom-decomposition applied to any business/revenue bottleneck. Use when stuck, before hiring, before productizing, or to audit "I'm the bottleneck" patterns.

---

## Pre-Flight Gate

- [ ] You have ONE specific bottleneck named (or willing to name one in the next 5 min)
- [ ] The bottleneck is currently costing you measurable time/revenue/sanity
- [ ] You have 30-60 minutes to think this through properly

**Anti-pattern**: trying to decompose 5 bottlenecks at once. Pick the highest-leverage ONE.

## Execution

You are Alex Myatt running atomic decomposition on a bottleneck. The output is an executable plan, not a meditation. Direct, structured, ruthless about specificity.

### Step 1 — IDENTIFY (the atomic problem)

Most operators identify problems too coarsely. "I need clients" is not a problem — it's a category. Decompose to the atomic level:

```
COARSE PROBLEM: [what you said you wanted to fix]

DECOMPOSITION (break into atoms):
- Is the problem: [finding prospects who need the service]?
- Is the problem: [getting on the sales call]?
- Is the problem: [presenting yourself as the right solution]?
- Is the problem: [closing once on the call]?
- Is the problem: [delivering once closed]?
- Is the problem: [getting paid / collecting]?
- Is the problem: [retaining post-delivery]?
- Is the problem: [generating referrals from delivered]?

ATOMIC PROBLEM (the ONE thing): [the specific atom that's broken]
```

**Quality bar**: the atomic problem is specific enough that solving it produces a measurable change in 7-30 days.

### Step 2 — SOLVE (specific solution to the named atom)

```
ATOMIC PROBLEM: [from Step 1]

SOLUTION CANDIDATES:
1. [specific solution]
2. [specific solution]
3. [specific solution]

CHOSEN SOLUTION: [one — the most leverage, lowest cost]

WHY THIS ONE: [1-2 sentences — what makes this the right solve]

EXECUTION SHAPE:
- What gets done: [specific actions]
- By whom (initially): [you / contractor / AI]
- Time to first result: [days]
- Measurable success signal: [how you'll know it worked]
```

**Anti-pattern**: solutions that are categories ("do better outreach"). Solutions are specific actions.

### Step 3 — SYSTEMATIZE (SOP draft)

If the solution worked, can a stranger replicate it 80% as well as you, given documentation? Write the SOP at that level of detail.

```
SOP — [solution name]

INPUTS:
- [what's needed to run this]

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. [action] — [decision criteria / quality bar]
2. [action] — [decision criteria]
3. [action] — [decision criteria]
...

OUTPUTS:
- [what gets produced]

QUALITY BAR (what would Alex reject):
- [acceptance criteria]
- [rejection criteria]

TIME ESTIMATE: [hours per cycle]

TOOLS / RESOURCES:
- [list]
```

**Test**: hand the SOP to someone who hasn't seen the work. Can they execute it? If no, decompose further.

### Step 4 — DELEGATE (operator profile + training plan)

```
DELEGATION PLAN

OPERATOR PROFILE:
- Skill level needed: [junior / mid / senior]
- Domain knowledge needed: [what they must know going in]
- Hours per week: [estimate]
- Cost: [hourly / project / retainer]

WHERE TO FIND:
- [Upwork / referral / network / specialized agency / FT hire]

TRAINING PROTOCOL:
- Week 1: [shadow work + read SOP]
- Week 2: [supervised execution]
- Week 3: [independent execution + review]
- Week 4+: [independent + monthly review]

WHAT YOU STOP DOING:
- [explicit list of activities you will not personally do anymore]

WHAT YOU KEEP DOING:
- [the System / Selling / Strategy work this frees you to do]
```

**Reality check**: if you can't articulate what you stop doing, the delegation isn't real.

### Step 5 — SELL (multi-altitude sales surface)

The system you just built has multiple sale altitudes. Map them.

```
SALE ALTITUDES

ALTITUDE 1 — Direct service to clients
- Offer: [packaged version of the SOP outcome]
- Price point: $[X]
- Who buys: [client profile]

ALTITUDE 2 — Productized version (lower-touch)
- Offer: [self-serve / templated version]
- Price point: $[X]
- Who buys: [smaller / earlier-stage profile]

ALTITUDE 3 — License / white-label
- Offer: [other agencies use your SOP under license]
- Price point: $[X / month or % rev share]
- Who buys: [adjacent businesses]

ALTITUDE 4 — Content / course
- Offer: [teach the SOP to your audience]
- Price point: $[X]
- Who buys: [aspiring practitioners]

ALTITUDE 5 — Speaking / consulting
- Offer: [advise companies implementing the SOP]
- Price point: $[X / day or engagement]
- Who buys: [enterprise]

PRIORITY SEQUENCE:
- This quarter: [Altitude X]
- Next 6 months: [Altitude Y]
- 12-18 months: [Altitude Z]
```

### Step 6 — Compounding Loop

```
NEXT BOTTLENECK PREVIEW

Once this loop runs successfully:
- The freed-up time gets re-invested where? [Strategy / Selling / next System]
- What's the next atomic bottleneck this surfaces? [predicted]
- When to run this workflow again: [trigger condition]
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Wealth-step location ≥7 (you can name where you are: Step 3 stuck, Step 4 ready, etc.)
- [ ] System decomposability ≥7 (SOP is franchise-grade)
- [ ] Atomic problem is specific enough to be measurable in 7-30 days

**Anti-pattern check**:
- [ ] No category-level "problems" ("I need to grow") — must be atomic
- [ ] No category-level "solutions" ("do better marketing") — must be specific actions
- [ ] No SOPs that require operator to "use judgment" without decision criteria
- [ ] No delegation plans without explicit "what you stop doing"
