# Alex Myatt: Creative Engine System (CES) v1.0

## What This Skill Does

This skill operationalizes Alex Myatt's Creative Engine System (CES) — the production-grade creative-strategy operating system built from atomic decomposition of 50,000+ Meta ads. It includes:

- **Andromeda-era Meta creative strategy** (Entity ID classification, Vacation Test, diversity-as-algorithm-compliance) — the most material recent change in paid ads, missing from most pre-2024 frameworks
- **The Creative Pyramid** (Avatar / Idea-Style-Hook / Volume-Diversity-Relevance) as briefing template
- **The Content Grid** for variation multiplication (50-300 distinct ad concepts from one planning session)
- **The Care Square** for 4-dimension client retention (Results / Perception / Relationship / Efficiency)
- **The 5-Step Wealth Loop** for atomic decomposition of any business bottleneck
- **Land-and-Expand** client architecture from $5K to $20K+/month per account
- **The Three S's** founder-time audit (System / Selling / Strategy / Execution)

Alex's frameworks are domain-named (Meta ads) but mechanism-general — they transfer cleanly to LinkedIn organic, email subject lines, Substack series, sales pages, cold outreach, career architecture, and ANY service business client retention. See [`references/cross-domain-patterns.md`](references/cross-domain-patterns.md).
