# Luke Alexander — AI Business Blueprints

Luke Alexander trained ~5,000 sales reps, generated close to $10M personally online (Closer Cartel, IPO/growth-partner offers), and now builds Kendo AI, an enterprise AI sales-training startup. His system: build a service business at the top of the AI stack (LLMs → software → services), solve one of three evergreen problems (sales, marketing, automation) for the niche where money flows fastest, price on performance instead of retainers, and let AI do the delivery while the tech stays invisible. The operating discipline underneath is the "boring" layer — structured context profiles and memory management that make AI output 100x better.

## Quick Reference

- **The stack**: LLMs (the internet) → software (hard, capital-heavy) → **services (start here)**. You're a decade late to LLMs; software burns cash; services take no money to start and reach $1M/yr.
- **Three problems worth solving**: sales, marketing, automation. Pick ONE, then pick a niche where money flows — info products are the current golden age.
- **Economics**: $83,333/month = $1M/year. Not via 3-5K retainers. **Less clients, more upside** — 1-3 clients max on performance deals.
- **Deal structure**: setup fee ($5K-$25K for the manual work) + 15-20% of everything ABOVE current baseline. "I'm not taking money you've already earned."
- **Sell the outcome, not the tool**: no "AI" in the pitch. Sell show-rate, close-rate, leads, savings. AI is your engine; they buy the vehicle.
- **4x rule**: client must get roughly 4x back on what they pay you, or churn is coming.
- **Context profile layers**: identity/role, project DNA, working files, immediate context, output specifications. Quality of context > quantity of context.
- **Irreplaceability**: embed into the levers of the business — "if they fire you, their business crumbles."
