## THE CORE EQUATION

> **Niche = Market × Format**

Markets are **discovered** — finite, ancient, pre-validated by their own existence. You cannot invent one.
Formats are **invented** — thousands spawn daily. This is the only variable you get to bet on.

**Nichebending** is the deliberate act of taking a format proven in one market and applying it to a market where it has never been used. The market pre-validates demand. The format pre-validates delivery. The intersection carries **no demand risk — only execution risk.**

You are never wrong about whether people want it. You are only ever wrong about how you served it.

## THE NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE

> **Never bend a format you cannot fill with genuine expertise.**

A dentist ranking toothpaste on a gaming tier list works, because the dentist is a real dentist. An operator with no financial credentials borrowing a finance format fails — and no amount of effort repairs it.

Read the rule forward, not as a caution: **the format is the free half; the fill is the scarce half.** Anyone can copy a format. Almost nobody can fill it with high-value knowledge. That asymmetry is not a constraint on nichebending — it is the entire reason nichebending pays the operator with real depth and not the copyist.

**The second, unstated gate:** donor-market *fluency*. Speak the borrowed market's dialect natively or the bend reads as a tourist in costume. **Gate A cannot be borrowed. Gate B can be hired.**

## THE BEND-DEPTH LADDER

Depth is the moat. Danilov demonstrated this ladder; he never named it.

| Layer | What Is Borrowed | Imitator Copy Time | Durability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Packaging** | Title architecture, visual grammar | An afternoon | Weeks |
| **2 — Structure** | The asset's shape: tier list, patch notes, countdown-elimination, POV, draft, bracket | A weekend | Months |
| **3 — Lexicon** | The vocabulary itself — adaptation becomes *play style*, extinction becomes *nerf*, habitat becomes *server* | Weeks, and only with donor fluency | A year |
| **4 — Ontology** | The donor market's entire worldview applied to the host subject — a coherent frame that generates assets on its own | Requires rebuilding the worldview *and* holding the host expertise | Ownership |

**Race on formats. Build on squares.** Formats decay with saturation. An occupied square — a market permanently associated with your bent frame — appreciates.

## THE SEARCH HEURISTIC NOBODY STATES

Empty squares are rarely empty for lack of demand. They are empty because of **cultural distance** — nobody in zoology hangs out in competitive gaming, so nobody made the connection.

**The further apart the two markets' audiences, the emptier and more defensible the square.** Rank candidate donors by distance, not by similarity.
