# Depth — Converting Copy

Most copy that gets "deepened" gets killed. A writer falls in love with a sentence, an earned moment of interiority swells, and three lines later the reader can no longer find the next step. That is not depth — that is a copywriter winning an argument with the buyer. This workflow exists for the opposite outcome: copy that reads more human, more specific, and more believed *while converting at least as well as it did before* — usually better, because conviction and a concrete image sell harder than a stacked claim ever did.

The single job this workflow owns: take ad / VSL / landing / email / offer copy and run the full deepen stack at the COPY dose — depth poured *into* the conversion skeleton, never around it. The offer logic, the CTA, the proof, and the clarity-to-action are load-bearing walls. We add conviction, specificity (Connelly's telling detail), and a signature voice; we call `/really-real-marketing` as the truth slot so warmth is earned, not asserted. Then we prove, on the page, that every conversion wall is still standing. Literary flourish never beats clarity-to-action. If a move blurs the next step or buries the offer, the move is wrong no matter how good the sentence sounds.

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: Before touching a word, run the **Decision Framework** in `genius.md § Decision Framework` (Q1 *did I diagnose first?*, Q2 *did the user ask for this scope?*, Q3 *which dose?* — Copy is MEDIUM, deepen-in-skeleton). STOP condition: if you have not scored the 8 deficits AND written down the exact conversion mechanics to protect (offer, CTA, proof, clarity-to-action), you cannot deepen — you are about to refine slop on a misdiagnosed draft and risk breaking the one thing this copy must keep doing. Diagnose and inventory the mechanics first.

## Input Required
- **The draft copy** — paste the ad / VSL script / landing page / email / offer in full. Partial copy diagnoses partially.
- **The conversion mechanic to protect** — the single next action this copy must drive (click, buy, book, opt-in) and the proof it leans on. This becomes the PRESERVE contract the deepen pass cannot break.
- **The offer** — what's being sold, the price/terms, and the core promise. Depth that drifts off the offer is decoration.
- **The buyer (ICP) and awareness stage** — who reads this and how aware they already are (the more aware, the more #5 over-explained is the likely deficit; the less aware, the more #2 hollow/conviction).
- *(Optional)* The channel inside copy — VSL vs. landing vs. cold email vs. offer page. Narrows the PRESERVE list (a VSL protects the open-loop sequence; a landing protects scan-path + CTA placement; an email protects the single ask).
- *(Optional)* Existing performance signal — what's converting / where readers drop. A known drop point tells DIAGNOSE where to look first.
- *(Optional)* Scope — diagnosis only, a surgical inject on one section, or a full deepen pass. Never full-rewrite when an inject would do.

## Workflow

This is the DEEPEN LOOP (diagnose → select+order → apply → receipt) specialized for converting copy. The non-negotiable difference from every other `/depth-*` workflow: a **mechanics inventory** is taken *before* the loop and **verified preserved** *after* it.

### Step 0 — Inventory the conversion mechanics (do this before diagnosing)
Before any depth work, lock the load-bearing walls so you can prove later they still stand. Write them down verbatim:

| Mechanic | Capture from the draft | Why it's load-bearing |
|---|---|---|
| **Offer logic** | The promise, the price/terms, the value stack, the risk reversal. | If depth drifts off the offer, the copy is now beautiful about the wrong thing. |
| **CTA** | The exact next action + where it appears + its wording. | The reader must still know precisely what to do, and it must still be reachable. |
| **Proof** | Every claim, mechanism, stat, testimonial, and credibility marker. | Conviction added on top must never quietly delete the proof underneath it. |
| **Clarity-to-action path** | The scan path / open-loop sequence / single ask that carries a reader to the CTA. | This is the spine of conversion. Earned interiority that breaks the path defeats the purpose. |

This inventory is the PRESERVE contract. Every move in Step 3 is checked against it; Step 4's receipt re-verifies it line by line.

### Step 1 — DIAGNOSE (score the 8 deficits, name the weakest links)
Score the draft 0/1/2 on each of the 8 deficits using the rubric in `references/depth-deficit-taxonomy.md`. Diagnose before treating — never refine slop on a misdiagnosed draft. For converting copy, the deficits that matter most (per the Copy row in `references/vertical-dosing.md`) are usually:

- **#2 Hollow / generic** — vague benefit, could-be-any-product claim. The proper-noun-swap test passes (swap your brand for a competitor's and nothing breaks). Owner: Connelly telling-detail + Lamott-Allen specificity.
- **#4 No signature voice** — sounds like every ad ever written; default "explainer" cadence, AI-tells firing. Owner: ghostwriting-voice-engine + Cole.
- **#3 Emotionally unearned** — sentiment / urgency asserted without proof underneath ("life-changing," "you deserve this"). Owner: Lamott-Allen really-real (`/really-real-marketing`) + Roth erosion.
- **#5 Over-explained / bloated** — common on high-awareness copy that re-proves what the buyer already trusts; ~25–30% cuttable. Owner: Cole compression + Roth erosion. Here, depth = the cut.

Name the **1–3 highest-scoring (weakest-link) deficits** as the treatment target. A 0 is left alone — routing an owner at a 0 over-deepens and risks the function. If the user asked for diagnosis only, STOP here and return the scores + named weakest links + the mechanics inventory; do not load owners or write prose.

### Step 2 — SELECT + ORDER (only confirmed deficits, sequenced by the Ordering Law)
Pick *only* the owners for the confirmed deficits from `references/routing-map.md`. Then sequence them by the **Ordering Law** (architecture → scene/detail → line/rhythm → truth/voice last) — table number is an index, not an apply order. For copy this typically resolves to:

1. **Architecture** — usually already implicit in the funnel logic; do NOT manufacture a spine a converting page doesn't need. Touch only if #1 scored 1–2 (e.g. a landing page with no through-argument). Owner: `skills/noah-hawley-storytelling-mastery` → `/hawley-theme-engine`.
2. **Scene + telling detail** — #2 / #7. Replace one abstract benefit with one concrete, photographable detail the reader can picture. Owners: `skills/michael-connelly-vivid-writing` → `/telling-detail-engine`; `skills/eric-roth-writing-mastery` → `/visual-prose-for-copy`.
3. **Line / rhythm + the cut** — #5 / #6. Compress what's over-proven, set cadence, give the CTA terminal punch. Owners: `skills/nicolas-cole-sentence-craft` → `/atomic-compression-density-audit`, `/terminal-power-rhythm-engineering`; `skills/eric-roth-writing-mastery` → `/content-erosion-protocol`.
4. **Truth + voice (last)** — #3 / #4. CALL `/really-real-marketing` (`skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing`) so the conviction is earned, not asserted; set the signature voice via `skills/ghostwriting-voice-engine` → `/voice-capture` and `skills/nicolas-cole-sentence-craft` → `/terminal-power-rhythm-engineering`. The marketing-belief reframe via `skills/rory-sutherland-marketing` → `/conspiratorial-reframe-engine` is available when the deficit is a flat positioning rather than a flat sentence.

Set the dose: **MEDIUM — deepen inside the skeleton.** You are adding conviction, specificity, and voice to a frame; you are never re-architecting the funnel. Resist treating deficits the copy doesn't have.

### Step 3 — APPLY (move the craft into the prose; check every move against PRESERVE)
For each confirmed deficit, load the owner's `genius.md` + the single named command and apply the move INTO the copy, in the Order from Step 2. Two rules hold throughout:

- **Integrate invisibly.** No expert names, no technique labels in the copy itself. Experts are named only in the Receipt.
- **Check each move against the PRESERVE contract (Step 0).** If a move touches the offer, the CTA, the proof, or the clarity-to-action path, it must *strengthen* that mechanic or it does not ship. A more specific benefit must still point at the same offer. A more rhythmic sentence near the CTA must not soften the ask. Earned emotion must sit *on top of* the proof, never replace it.

The bar from the source: make it read more honest, clearer, more believed, less defended — with the machinery invisible. For copy, add the one telling detail that makes the claim un-fake-able, the one voice move that makes it sound like a person who means it, and cut the three sentences re-proving what the buyer already granted. Conviction and concreteness sell; a stacked claim does not.

### Step 4 — RECEIPT + verify the mechanics survived
End with the Depth Receipt (see Output Format). For this workflow the receipt carries a mandatory extra block — the **Conversion-Mechanics Survival Check** — that walks the Step 0 inventory item by item and confirms each is intact or strengthened. If any mechanic was weakened, the move that weakened it is reverted before delivery. A deepened draft that converts worse is a failed deepen, regardless of how the prose reads.

| Book/long-form | Out of scope — route to `/depth-book` (FULL stack, architecture-first, truth slot `/really-real-book`). Long-form sales letters that are essays-with-an-offer still run COPY here; true long-form prose runs `/depth-book`. |

## Output Format
Deliver the deepened copy first (clean, no annotations inside it), then the Depth Receipt with its mandatory survival check. If scope was diagnosis-only, deliver Steps 0–1 (inventory + scores + named weakest links) and STOP — no prose, no receipt.

```
[THE DEEPENED COPY — full, clean, ready to ship. No expert names, no technique labels, no inline commentary. CTA reachable, offer intact, proof present.]

---

DEPTH RECEIPT
- Weakest link found: [the 1–3 confirmed deficits, by name + severity score]
- Moves applied:
    [deficit fixed] -> [move in plain craft terms] -> [expected reader/buyer effect] -> [source principle]
    (one line per move; experts named ONLY here, never in the copy above)
- Dose / vertical fit: COPY = MEDIUM, deepen-in-skeleton — [why this dose; what was added vs. cut]
- Truth slot: /really-real-marketing called for [which beat] — [what it earned, not asserted]
- Remaining risk: [what still could underperform — e.g. a claim that needs a real proof asset, a CTA worth A/B testing]

CONVERSION-MECHANICS SURVIVAL CHECK (mandatory for this workflow)
- Offer logic: [INTACT / STRENGTHENED] — [the offer/price/terms/promise still reads as before or better]
- CTA: [INTACT / STRENGTHENED] — [exact action + placement + reachability confirmed]
- Proof: [INTACT / STRENGTHENED] — [every claim/stat/testimonial/mechanism still present]
- Clarity-to-action path: [INTACT / STRENGTHENED] — [reader can still find and reach the next step; scan-path / open-loop / single ask preserved]
- Verdict: [SHIP / REVERT] — [if any mechanic weakened, name it and the reverted move]
```

## Quality Gate
> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: review the output against `genius.md § Anti-Patterns` and the § Decision Framework before delivering. Flag and fix any violation first.
- **No lengthening-as-deepening.** Deepen ≠ lengthen. If the copy got longer without a confirmed #2/#3/#4 fix earning the words, you padded — re-run DIAGNOSE for #5 and cut. For high-awareness copy the deepest move is usually the cut. Rebuild if the word count rose with no functional gain.
- **No expert name-drops or technique labels in the copy.** Experts and moves live only in the Receipt. If a craft term leaked into the prose, strip it and re-integrate the move invisibly.
- **No function sacrificed for feel.** Run the Conversion-Mechanics Survival Check honestly — offer, CTA, proof, and clarity-to-action each INTACT or STRENGTHENED. If a gorgeous sentence blurred the next step or buried the offer, REVERT it; clarity-to-action beats literary flourish every time. This check is the veto.
- **No manufactured sentiment.** Conviction is earned via `/really-real-marketing`, not asserted with intensity words. If warmth/urgency was added without proof or a real specific detail underneath it, it's melodrama — pull it.
- **Ordering Law respected.** Architecture (if present) before scene before line/rhythm before truth/voice. Confirm you didn't set voice or run the truth pass before the detail and structure held. Re-sequence if inverted.
- **Only confirmed deficits treated.** A 0 was left alone. If you routed an owner at a 0 "while you were in there," you over-deepened — revert that move.
- **Truth slot called, not re-implemented.** `/really-real-marketing` was *invoked* as Order Step 4; its logic was not copied into this pass (Anti-Duplication Contract). If you re-taught really-real craft locally, delete it and route to the command.

## Common Pitfalls
- **CTA buried under earned interiority.** A vivid, honest passage swelled near the close and pushed the ask down or softened it. Recovery: hold the PRESERVE contract as a hard wall — the survival check is the backstop; revert the move and re-place the CTA where the scan-path expects it, then add the detail somewhere that doesn't compete with the ask.
- **Diagnosed as copy, was actually social or long-form.** You ran the MEDIUM deepen-in-skeleton dose on a 120-word promo post (should be `/depth-social`) or an essay-with-an-offer (should be `/depth-book`). Recovery: re-confirm the vertical and the function to protect before selecting moves; the dose is set by the vertical, not the draft's literary ambition.
- **Proof quietly deleted while "tightening."** The #5 compression pass cut a sentence that was actually carrying a stat or credibility marker, not bloat. Recovery: the Step 0 inventory exists for this — diff the deepened copy against the captured proof list; restore any claim/mechanism that vanished.
- **Conviction faked instead of earned.** You added urgency and stakes words to fix #3 instead of calling `/really-real-marketing` to ground them in a real specific. Recovery: route the truth slot; find the concrete detail or proof that earns the feeling, or leave the line plain — asserted emotion converts worse than honest plainness.
- **Over-deepened a clean draft.** The copy scored mostly 0s and you treated deficits anyway, crowding lines and threatening the CTA. Recovery: trust the diagnosis — fix only confirmed weakest links; a copy that's already lean and specific ships as-is with a short receipt saying so.
