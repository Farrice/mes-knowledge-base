# Depth Gate (Composition Decision-Tree)

The writing-depth-layer ships twelve workflows across three tiers: entry points (`/deepen`, `/depth-social`, `/depth-copy`, `/depth-marketing`, `/depth-stack`, `/depth-audit`, `/depth-inject`) and specialist layers (`/depth-line`, `/depth-voice`, `/depth-gate`). Choosing wrong is how a near-final post gets dragged through a full-stack pipeline until its scroll-stopping shape drowns, or a high-stakes essay gets a quick polish when it needed the whole architecture-to-truth ladder, or a voice-only deficit gets treated with a full deepen when `/depth-voice` is surgical. The wrong door is its own depth failure: over-treatment buries function, under-treatment ships competent prose with no spine. This workflow is the triage desk that routes to the right door.

It is a decision tree, not a treatment. `/depth-gate` reads the draft's stakes, vertical, and scope-of-ask; runs a fast severity read (or consumes a prior `/depth-audit`); and returns a one-screen routing recommendation — *which* workflow, *which* owners in *which* order, at *what* dose, with the function to preserve named. It loads no owner to apply a move and rewrites not one sentence. The output is a routing slip a human (or the next workflow) executes. If you find yourself improving a line, you have left the gate and entered the treatment — stop.

## Pre-Flight
Read these files before routing:
1. `skills/writing-depth-layer/genius.md` (the composition brain — § 3 The 8 Depth Deficits, § 5 The Ordering Law, § 8 Per-Vertical Dosing, § 11 Decision Framework — item 2 *scope-of-ask* is the heart of this gate).
2. `skills/writing-depth-layer/references/depth-deficit-taxonomy.md` (the per-deficit detection signals + the 0/1/2 severity rubric — the instrument for the fast read; full scoring lives in `/depth-audit`).
3. `skills/writing-depth-layer/references/vertical-dosing.md` (the dose dial, the deficits-that-matter-most-per-vertical priors, the truth slot, and the function to PRESERVE — this picks the vertical door).
4. `skills/writing-depth-layer/references/routing-map.md` (deficit → owner → real path → real command + the Ordering-Law sequence) — consult to *name* owners/commands for the slip; do NOT load any owner skill on a routing run.

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: Before routing, run the **Decision Framework** in `genius.md § 11`, items 2 and 3 — confirm (a) the user's actual scope-of-ask (diagnosis-only / surgical inject / full deepen / maximum-depth) and (b) the vertical that sets the dose. STOP condition: this workflow NEVER rewrites prose and NEVER loads an owner skill to apply a move. Naming the destination workflow, its owners, and its commands is the whole job; treating is the next workflow's job. If the user already wants the draft deepened and the vertical is unambiguous, route straight to that workflow — don't add a gate step they didn't ask for.

## Input Required
- **The draft (or its description)** — the text to be deepened, pasted inline, or a clear description of what it is and how near-final it is. A full draft lets the gate do a fast severity read; a description routes on vertical + stakes + scope alone (say which you got).
- **The vertical** — social / copy / marketing / book-long-form / client-personal. This picks the vertical door and sets the dose ceiling. If ambiguous, ask; the route is wrong if the vertical is wrong.
- **The stakes** — how high the bar is: a routine post vs. a flagship essay vs. a sales page the quarter rides on. Stakes decide depth ceiling and whether `/depth-stack` (maximum) is warranted over `/deepen`.
- **The scope-of-ask** — does the user want *diagnosis only*, a *surgical inject* (one or two weakest links, prose mostly untouched), a *full deepen*, or *maximum depth*? When unstated, infer from "near-final / just one thing" (inject) vs. "make this exceptional / it has to land" (stack) and confirm.
- *(Optional)* **A prior `/depth-audit` scorecard** — if one exists, the gate consumes it instead of re-reading; the routing slip then carries the audit's named weakest links directly.
- *(Optional)* **Known constraints** — length ceiling, banned claims, channel — flagged on the slip so the chosen workflow honors them.

## Workflow
The gate runs a one-pass DEEPEN loop specialized to routing: **diagnose (fast read) → select+order (pick the door + chain) → apply (nothing — emit the slip) → receipt (no Depth Receipt; this is routing).** The "apply" step of a router is to hand off, not to treat.

### Step 1: Read stakes, vertical, and scope-of-ask (set the door's coordinates)
Three readings set which door you're pointing at before you look at a single deficit. Get these wrong and the right deficits route to the wrong workflow.

| Reading | The question to answer | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Vertical** | Which of the five rooms is this playing in? | Named, not assumed — social / copy / marketing / book / client. Sets the dose ceiling and the vertical door (`vertical-dosing.md`). |
| **Stakes** | How high is the bar — routine, important, or must-be-exceptional? | A routine social post and a flagship long-form essay route differently *even at the same scope*. High stakes raises the depth ceiling toward `/depth-stack`. |
| **Scope-of-ask** | Did the user ask for diagnosis, a surgical inject, a full deepen, or maximum depth? | Matched to the ask, not over-scoped. "Near-final, fix one thing" ≠ "make this exceptional." Over-scoping is the anti-pattern this gate exists to prevent (`genius.md § 9`). |
| **Function to protect** | What is the one thing the chosen workflow must not break? | Concrete for *this* piece — CTA unmissable, hook still stops the scroll, spine carries 4,000 words. Carried onto the slip as PRESERVE. |

### Step 2: Fast severity read (lightweight — or consume a prior audit)
This gate needs only enough diagnosis to *route*, not the full evidence-quoted scorecard `/depth-audit` produces. If a prior `/depth-audit` scorecard was supplied, skip the read and use its named weakest links.

- Run a LIGHT pass on the 8 deficits using the detection signals in `depth-deficit-taxonomy.md` — looking for "which 1–3 are clearly weakest," not a defensible 0/1/2 on all eight.
- Lead with the vertical's deficits-that-matter-most prior (`vertical-dosing.md`): social → #2/#6/#5; copy → #2/#4/#3; marketing → #2/#3/#4; book → #1/#7/#3/#8; client → #1/#8/#5. A prior, not a verdict.
- **Escalation rule:** if the fast read is ambiguous, the stakes are high, or three-plus deficits look severe, the gate's recommendation is to **run `/depth-audit` first** — a thorough diagnosis, then re-gate. Do not guess a chain on a high-stakes piece off a glance.
- If nothing looks worse than a single light deficit, the routing answer may be **no deepen pass** (or a single surgical inject). A clean near-final draft routed into a full pipeline gets worse, not deeper.

### Step 3: Select the door (the decision tree)
Map vertical × stakes × scope-of-ask to exactly one destination workflow. This is the core routing logic. Match top-to-bottom; the first row that fits wins.

| If… | Route to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| User asked for **diagnosis only** (no rewrite wanted) | `/depth-audit` | Scores all 8 with evidence quotes, recommends a chain, touches no prose. The gate's job ends by pointing here. |
| Fast read is **ambiguous / high-stakes / 3+ severe** | `/depth-audit` **first**, then re-gate | Never guess a full chain on a high-stakes piece. Diagnose thoroughly, then route on the scorecard. |
| **Only Deficit 4** (signature voice), structure sound, all others at 0 | `/depth-voice` | Surgical voice-as-music pass; restores the rhythmic fingerprint so prose stops sounding like AI/anyone. No architecture work needed. |
| **Only Deficits 5–6** (compression / weak rhythm), architecture + scene confirmed at 0 | `/depth-line` | Surgical line-craft pass; sentence rhythm, vivid verbs, read-aloud cadence, density. Structure and voice are solid; prose just needs the technical tuning. |
| **Exactly one deficit at severity 1**, nothing at severity 2, **near-final draft** | `/depth-inject` | Surgical single-move: one telling detail / recognition beat / hard-truth line / voice tweak. Minimal-touch rewrite + one-line Receipt. No full chain, just the one weakest link. |
| **Social** vertical (LinkedIn / X / IG / short newsletter), any normal stakes | `/depth-social` | Light + fast, 1–2 deficits max, hook/brevity/shape preserved. The lightest door — deepest move is often a cut. |
| **Copy** vertical (ads / VSL / landing / email / offers) | `/depth-copy` | Depth inside the conversion skeleton; offer logic / CTA / proof / clarity-to-action verified intact. |
| **Marketing / brand** vertical (brand narrative, manifesto, about page, campaign) | `/depth-marketing` | Humanity + belief + specificity; Rory Sutherland reframe available as the belief layer; positioning preserved. |
| **Book / long-form / novel** vertical | `/depth-stack` | Full Ordering Law as four explicit staged passes; architecture (Hawley) weighted heaviest. The deep, layered door. |
| **Client / personal-brand** vertical, **high stakes** (flagship essay, the piece must land) | `/depth-stack` | Maximum depth with restraint — argument architecture (Fareed) + credibility, staged and shown. |
| **Client / personal-brand** vertical, **normal stakes** | `/deepen` | Measured full deepen; the flagship one-tool conductor handles a single advisory piece without the staged ceremony. |
| Vertical is **mixed / unclear**, or it's one piece needing the standard treatment | `/deepen` | The flagship one-tool conductor — diagnoses + fixes the 1–3 weakest links at the inferred vertical dose. The default when no specialized door clearly wins. |

Resolve `/deepen` vs. `/depth-stack` by stakes, not vertical alone: `/deepen` is the standard single-piece conductor; `/depth-stack` is the staged maximum-depth pipeline reserved for high-stakes / book / long-form pieces that must be exceptional and benefit from each stage shown explicitly.

### Step 4: Name the chain, order, dose, and truth slot (fill the slip)
For the chosen workflow, pre-name what it will run — owners, sequence, dose — so the routing slip is immediately actionable. *Name* from `routing-map.md`; load nothing.

| Slip element | What to specify | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Destination workflow** | The single `/depth-*` (or `/deepen`) command Step 3 selected. | Step 3 decision tree |
| **Owners (chain)** | One owner per confirmed weakest link only — never for a clean deficit. | `routing-map.md` Deficit → Owner table |
| **Order** | Sequence the owners by the **Ordering Law**, not by deficit number: architecture → scene/detail → line/rhythm → truth/voice. | `genius.md § 5` / `routing-map.md § Ordering Law` |
| **Commands** | The real `/command` per owner (verified roster only). | `routing-map.md` REAL command column |
| **Dose** | Light / medium / heavy per the vertical and the inferred scope, with the depth ceiling the stakes set. | `vertical-dosing.md` Dosing Table |
| **Truth slot** | The vertical's `/really-real-*` pass the destination workflow will CALL (social→`/really-real-social`, copy & marketing→`/really-real-marketing`, book→`/really-real-book`, client→`/really-real-client`). Name it; the destination calls it — this gate does not. | `vertical-dosing.md` Truth slot column |
| **PRESERVE** | The function the destination workflow is forbidden to break. | Step 1 / `vertical-dosing.md` PRESERVE column |

The slip is the route made executable. It ends here — the gate points; the named workflow treats. No move is applied on this run.

## Output Format
This workflow produces a one-screen routing slip. **No rewritten prose, and therefore no Depth Receipt** (the Receipt belongs to every prose-changing workflow — `/deepen` and all `/depth-*` except `/depth-audit` and this one).

Vertical: [social / copy / marketing / book / client] · Stakes: [routine / important / must-be-exceptional]

## FAST READ — likely weakest link(s)
[The 1–3 deficits the light pass flagged, in Ordering-Law order — e.g. "#1 No architecture, #6 Weak rhythm."]
[Or, if escalating:] "Ambiguous / high-stakes / 3+ severe → recommend running /depth-audit FIRST, then re-gate."
[Or, if clean:] "Nothing above a single light deficit — recommend a surgical inject only, or NO deepen pass."

## ROUTE  →  [/depth-<workflow> or /deepen]

## PRE-NAMED CHAIN (the destination workflow will load + run these; the gate loads none)

  1. [Deficit #] → [Owner] (`skills/...`) → run [/command] — [dose note]
  2. [Deficit #] → [Owner] (`skills/...`) → run [/command] — [dose note]

## Quality Gate
> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: review output against `genius.md § 9 Anti-Patterns` and `genius.md § 10 Anti-Duplication Contract`. Flag and fix any violation before delivering — no lengthening-as-deepening, no expert name-drops in prose (N/A here — there is no prose), no function sacrificed, no manufactured sentiment, no craft re-taught, no over-scoping.
- **Zero prose changed.** Not one sentence of the draft is rewritten, injected, or "lightly polished." If any output line is altered draft text, this stopped being a gate — delete it. Routing only.
- **No owner loaded, no move applied.** Owners and commands are *named* from `routing-map.md`; none were loaded to treat. A loaded owner on a routing run is scope creep — rebuild as a pure slip and hand off.
- **Exactly one destination door.** The slip routes to a single `/depth-*` or `/deepen` (plus an optional "/depth-audit first" escalation). A slip that hedges between two doors hasn't routed — re-run Step 3's decision tree top-to-bottom and commit to the first matching row.
- **Scope matched, not over-scoped.** A near-final / "fix one thing" ask routes to a LIGHT inject (vertical door or `/deepen` at inject dose), never `/depth-stack`. A must-be-exceptional / book piece earns `/depth-stack`. If the routed depth exceeds the ask, return and dial down — over-treatment is the failure this gate prevents.
- **Ordering Law respected in the pre-named chain.** The sequence runs architecture → scene/detail → line/rhythm → truth/voice — by the law, not deficit number. A chain that pre-names rhythm before a recovered spine is wrong; re-sequence.
- **Only confirmed weakest links in the chain.** No owner pre-named for a deficit the fast read found clean. The chain is the 1–3 weakest links, capped by the vertical's dose (social: 1–2). A "comprehensive" eight-owner chain is a routing failure — cut to the weakest links.
- **No Depth Receipt.** This workflow produces a routing slip, not deepened prose; the Receipt block belongs only to rewrite/inject workflows. If a Receipt appears here, remove it.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Gate drifts into treatment.** You "couldn't help" rewriting the worst line while routing. Recovery: stop at the slip — the gate's entire value is that it touches no prose. Hand the named workflow the chain and let it treat.
- **Over-treatment routed.** You sent a near-final post into `/depth-stack` because more depth felt safer. Recovery: re-read the scope-of-ask — "near-final / fix one thing" routes to the vertical door at LIGHT/inject dose, never the maximum-depth pipeline. Over-scoping is the exact failure this gate exists to prevent.
- **Under-treatment routed.** You sent a high-stakes flagship essay to a quick `/deepen` (or worse, a single inject) when it needed the full staged ladder. Recovery: high stakes + book/long-form (or high-stakes client) routes to `/depth-stack` — let the bar the piece must clear raise the door, not just the vertical.
- **Guessed a chain on a high-stakes glance.** The fast read was ambiguous and you committed to a full chain anyway. Recovery: when the read is ambiguous, the stakes are high, or 3+ deficits look severe, the correct route is `/depth-audit` FIRST, then re-gate on the evidence-quoted scorecard. The gate is allowed to say "diagnose harder before treating."
- **Two doors on one slip.** You hedged between `/depth-copy` and `/depth-marketing` instead of deciding. Recovery: re-confirm the vertical (is this converting copy with a CTA, or brand media staking a position?) — the vertical is the door, and the vertical is singular. Commit to one.
- **Wrong vertical, wrong door.** You gated converting copy as if it were a Substack essay and routed `/depth-stack`, which would bury the CTA. Recovery: re-confirm the vertical and the function to protect before routing; both the door and the dose ceiling are set by the vertical, not the draft's literary ambition.
- **Over-routed a surgical single-move to a full pipeline.** You had exactly one deficit at severity 1 (near-final draft) but routed to `/deepen` or a full-stack door instead of surgical `/depth-inject`. Recovery: if severity reads 1 / 0 / 0 / 1 (one deficit at 1, nothing at 2), the slip should name `/depth-inject` + one owner + one move. Over-treating a single-move ask is the inverse of under-treatment.
