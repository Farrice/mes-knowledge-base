# Depth — Client / Personal Brand (Writing Depth Layer)

Client-facing prose fails in the opposite direction from social and copy. It rarely fails by being thin; it fails by being *bloated, over-explained, and quietly untrustworthy* — proving what the reader already grants, throat-clearing before it makes a claim, hedging until no position survives, or worst, manufacturing a vulnerable confession to seem "relatable" when the reader came for a credible advisor. The depth a client piece is missing is almost never more words. It is a spine the argument is built to deliver, the restraint to under-claim, and the discipline to earn the reader's trust line by line. This workflow's single job is to deepen client deliverables and personal-brand assets into prose that reads like a trusted advisor — by giving the argument a real architecture (Fareed Zakaria), grounding authority in fair, accurate, well-reasoned cases instead of hype, and earning whatever warmth appears instead of asserting it (the truth slot) — without ever overreaching, padding, or sacrificing clarity for ornament.

It is a full-stack orchestrator dosed for one vertical, MEASURED toward restraint. It does not re-teach argument architecture, sentence craft, or really-real craft. It diagnoses what depth the client piece is missing, composes the confirmed owners in the Ordering Law, and hands back a deepened draft plus a change-map. Two bright lines govern this vertical: **density over completeness** — a client doc earns trust by being tight, not comprehensive; deepening here is usually *cutting* — and **authority is quiet** — depth shows as clarity and a fair case, never as confession or flourish. Manufactured vulnerability on a piece that needs to read *credible* is the signature failure this dose exists to prevent.

## Input Required
- **The draft** — the advisory memo, positioning document, founder essay, thought-leadership piece, proposal, client-facing strategy brief, executive summary, or personal-brand asset to deepen. Paste it in full.
- **The single argument** — the one claim this piece is built to land (claim → evidence → so-what). If the user can't state it, that *is* the diagnosis (#1 No architecture) — surface it before deepening, do not paper over it with prettier sentences.
- **The audience & their existing trust** — who reads this, what they already grant the author, and what they are skeptical of. This sets the restraint dial: the more the reader already trusts, the less you prove (#5 Over-explained is the usual offender), and the more credibility hinges on not overreaching.
- **The voice / credibility constraints** — the author's authority register, the claims they can legitimately make, the lines / stats / sources that are real, and any words or postures forbidden (no hype, no manufactured intimacy). The deepened draft must read as *the same advisor*, clearer — never a louder one.
- **The scope asked for** — diagnosis only, a surgical inject (one section/argument/paragraph), or a full rewrite. Never full-rewrite when an inject would do; client docs especially reward the smallest true change.
- *(Optional)* The length ceiling — most client docs should stay ≤2 pages (density over completeness). If a ceiling exists, treat it as a hard PRESERVE constraint and deepen by cutting.
- *(Optional)* The medium inside the vertical — advisory memo vs. positioning doc vs. founder essay vs. proposal vs. LinkedIn thought-leadership — which narrows the PRESERVE list and the dose.

## Workflow
Run the **DEEPEN LOOP** (diagnose → select+order → apply → receipt), specialized for client/personal-brand. The dose is MEASURED — trusted-advisory prose with restraint. Depth shows as authority and clarity, not ornament; the moves run *inside* the existing argument and authority register, and they more often subtract than add.

1. **DIAGNOSE — score the 8 deficits, name the 1–3 weakest links (this vertical's usual suspects first).** Score the draft against all eight deficits in `genius.md § The 8 Depth Deficits`, but look hardest where client prose fails: **#1 No architecture** (the argument has no spine — claims and evidence in a pile, no claim → evidence → so-what throughline), **#8 No reader trust** (throat-clearing, hedging until no position survives, unfairness to the counter-argument, fake closure, overreach the reader can smell), and **#5 Over-explained / bloated** (proving what the reader already grants — usually 25–30% is cuttable without loss). Then check **#2 Hollow / generic** (could-be-any-consultant abstraction with no concrete stakes). Name the confirmed deficits explicitly. Diagnose before treating; never refine a client piece on a misdiagnosed draft — and on this vertical especially, confirm a deficit is *real* before treating it, because adding depth a tight doc doesn't need reads as bloat, not authority.

2. **SELECT + ORDER — pick only the owners for confirmed deficits, sequence by the Ordering Law.** Load *only* the routing-map rows for the confirmed set (Load Discipline: one `genius.md` per owner, Tier 2, plus the single named command). Sequence by `genius.md § The Ordering Law`, not by deficit number:
   - **(argument architecture — spine first, if #1)** `skills/fareed-zakaria-writing-mastery` → `/high-stakes-argument-architecture` — give the piece a real claim → evidence → so-what spine before any line work. This is the home owner for this vertical: it builds the case the whole document is built to deliver. Where the deficit is specifically a missing reason-this-exists / theme rather than argument logic, `skills/noah-hawley-storytelling-mastery` → `/hawley-theme-engine` is the available spine owner.
   - **(public-intellectual voice / authority register, if #4)** `skills/fareed-zakaria-writing-mastery` → `/public-intellectual-voice-narrative` — set the trusted-advisor register: confident without hype, narrative without ornament. The voice that earns being believed.
   - **(scene + telling detail, if #2 / #7)** `skills/eric-roth-writing-mastery` → `/visual-prose-for-copy` and `skills/michael-connelly-vivid-writing` → `/telling-detail-engine` — replace the abstract could-be-anyone claim with the one concrete, accurate detail that makes the case credible and specific. On this vertical, the detail must be *true* and *verifiable*, never decorative — specificity serves credibility.
   - **(line / rhythm — the dominant move here, if #5 / #6)** `skills/nicolas-cole-sentence-craft` → `/atomic-compression-density-audit`, `/terminal-power-rhythm-engineering` and `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing` → `/really-real-silence` — cut everything the reader already grants, kill the throat-clearing, and set the argument to land on strong terminal beats. For client docs, the deepest move is almost always *subtraction* in service of density over completeness.
   - **(reader trust, if #8)** `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing` → `/really-real-reader-trust`, `/really-real-ending` — make the piece fair to its counter-argument, stop the hedging, and close on an earned conclusion rather than a fake-comprehensive summary.
   - **(truth + voice, LAST)** the truth slot below + the advisor's authority fingerprint — earned warmth (only if the draft has earned it) and signature voice go last, onto a draft that is already argued, scened, and tight. On this vertical the truth pass usually *restrains* rather than warms.

3. **APPLY — run each owner's move INTO the prose, invisibly, inside the argument and authority register.** Load each confirmed owner's `genius.md` + the single named command and apply the move directly into the draft. Preserve the author's core argument, position, and credibility register — the output is *this advisor, clearer and tighter*, not a new one. Integrate every move invisibly: no expert names, no technique labels, no "claim → evidence:" scaffolding visible in the prose. Hold the PRESERVE constraint as hard: every move must *strengthen* the argument architecture (claim → evidence → so-what), the credibility / accuracy (no overreach, no manufactured or unverifiable stat), and the restraint that signals a trusted advisor rather than a hype machine — or it does not ship, no matter how good the sentence sounds. If a deepening move adds length without adding trust, it loses to the cut: density over completeness.

4. **TRUTH SLOT — CALL `/really-real-client` (do not re-implement it).** As the final earned-emotion / earned-authority pass, **run `/really-real-client`** (`skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing`) on the deepened draft. This is the truth slot for the client/personal-brand vertical: it earns whatever heart or conviction the piece carries and enforces the restraint that keeps an advisory voice trustworthy. Pass it the single argument and the concrete, accurate detail you grounded in Step 3 so the conviction attaches to something real and the warmth never tips into manufactured vulnerability. Do NOT re-teach really-real craft here; orchestrate the pass and take its output back. (Per the Anti-Duplication Contract, this workflow composes the truth slot owner; it never duplicates it.)

5. **RECEIPT — end with the Depth Receipt.** Emit the verbatim Depth Receipt block (Output Format below). Name the experts ONLY here, never in the prose. State which dose you used and why, and the remaining risk — for client work, usually: did the piece stay tight (density over completeness), is every claim still defensible (no overreach introduced), and does the authority still read *quiet* rather than performed?

## Quality Gate
> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: review the output against `genius.md § Anti-Patterns` and `references/vertical-dosing.md` (Client / Personal-brand row + Common Pitfalls) by name. Flag and fix any violation before delivering.
- **No lengthening-as-deepening — density over completeness.** Comprehensive = failure, and on client work especially. Confirm the deepened piece is tighter, clearer, and more defensible — not longer or more "thorough." A strong client pass almost always *cuts* (#5). If it grew without earning every added word in trust, rebuild from the cut and respect the length ceiling.
- **No expert name-drops or technique labels in the prose.** Experts and moves live in the Depth Receipt ONLY. If "argument architecture," "claim → evidence → so-what," "telling detail," or any expert name appears in the deepened draft, strip it.
- **Function not sacrificed — the argument and credibility survived.** The PRESERVE constraint held: the claim → evidence → so-what spine is sharper, no overreach or unverifiable stat was introduced, and the restraint that signals a trusted advisor stayed intact. If a deepening move produced a gorgeous sentence that softened the argument or made a claim the author can't defend, return it — clarity and credibility beat flourish, every time.
- **No manufactured sentiment / vulnerability.** Authority is quiet. Any warmth or conviction is earned from a real, specific, accurate thing on the page — never confession or false intimacy bolted on for "relatability." If the heart isn't earned, leave the restraint; on this vertical, restraint *is* the depth.
- **Ordering law respected, only confirmed deficits treated.** Architecture (argument spine) → authority register → scene/detail → line/cut → reader trust → truth/voice, in that sequence. Confirm you treated *only* the diagnosed weakest links and did not crowd an already-tight draft with depth it didn't need.
- **Truth slot CALLED, not re-implemented.** `/really-real-client` was actually run as the truth pass; this file did not re-teach really-real craft. If you re-implemented it, you violated the Anti-Duplication Contract — route to the real command and re-run.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Deepened into confession.** Chasing "relatability," you injected vulnerability or personal intimacy onto a piece the reader came to for a credible case. Recovery: this is the #1 fail mode the client dose exists to prevent — strip the manufactured heart, return to restraint, and let the argument and a true specific detail carry the trust. Depth here is quiet authority, not performed openness.
- **Lengthened to "deepen."** You added paragraphs, qualifications, and context to make the doc feel thorough and blew past the density-over-completeness line. Recovery: re-run DIAGNOSE for #5 (over-explained) and cut what the reader already grants; for client work the deepest move is usually subtraction. Hold the length ceiling as a hard PRESERVE constraint.
- **Overreach introduced.** A line-craft or truth pass sharpened a sentence into a claim the author cannot actually defend, or a stat that isn't verified. Recovery: hold credibility/accuracy as a PRESERVE constraint — every claim must be defensible and every stat real; pull the overreach and re-state the claim at the size the evidence supports. (Cross-check Factual Grounding before delivery.)
- **Argument polished before it existed.** You ran line-craft and trust passes on a pile of true-but-unspined claims, producing clean sentences with no case. Recovery: obey the Ordering Law — establish the claim → evidence → so-what spine (Fareed `/high-stakes-argument-architecture`) first, then re-sequence the line work onto it.
- **Wrong vertical, advisory stack run.** You ran the measured client stack on a converting proposal and buried the next-step, or on a brand manifesto and starved the belief. Recovery: re-confirm the vertical and the function to protect — route to `/depth-copy` (conversion), `/depth-marketing` (belief/brand), or `/depth-social` (post) when those are the real verticals.
- **Truth slot re-implemented instead of called.** You re-taught really-real craft inline instead of running `/really-real-client`. Recovery: delete the inline craft, invoke the real command, take its output back into the draft.
- **Over-scoped the ask.** The user asked for a diagnosis and got a full rewrite, or asked for a one-section inject and got a top-to-bottom rebuild. Recovery: match the scope — diagnosis-only returns the scored deficits and the route and stops; an inject changes only the named section. Client docs especially reward the smallest true change.
