# Depth — Book / Long-Form

A thin chapter is almost never thin because the sentences are weak. It is thin because nothing underneath the sentences is holding them up — no spine, no reason this scene exists, no question the reader is leaning toward. So the instinct to "polish the prose" is exactly backwards: you can make every line beautiful and still hand back pages that mean nothing and go nowhere. This is the one vertical where that mistake is fatal, because long-form gives the failure room to compound across an arc. A book earns the reader page by page, and a page with no architecture under it is a page the reader puts down.

This workflow is the conductor for the heavy end of the dial. It runs the full Ordering Law top to bottom — architecture before scene, scene before line, truth last — because in long-form the spine is load-bearing for everything above it. It owns the order and the dose; it owns no craft. Architecture loads Hawley, scene loads Roth and Connelly, line loads Cole and the Lamott-Allen technical-craft module, and the emotional-truth pass is *called*, not re-taught, by running `/really-real-book` as the truth slot. The result reads more honest, clearer, warmer, more specific, less defended — with a spine you could name in one sentence and the machinery nowhere on the page.

## Pre-Flight
Read these files before executing:

2. `skills/writing-depth-layer/references/depth-deficit-taxonomy.md` (the 0/1/2 severity rubric + detection signals — score against this, especially Deficit 1 architecture and Deficit 8 reader trust, which carry the most weight in long-form)
3. `skills/writing-depth-layer/references/routing-map.md` (the deficit → owner table; the REAL skill paths and commands — never reference a path not in this map)
4. `skills/writing-depth-layer/references/vertical-dosing.md` (the **Book / Long-form / Novel** row: FULL STACK dose, truth slot `/really-real-book`, the PRESERVE list — spine, narrative continuity, earned trust across the arc)
5. Then, and ONLY for the confirmed deficits, each owner's `genius.md` + the single named command from the routing map — starting with Hawley's `genius.md` for the architecture pass. Do not pre-load the whole roster; load the spine owner first because everything above it depends on it.

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: Before touching the prose, run the **Decision Framework** in `genius.md § Decision Framework` — all five questions. STOP condition: if you cannot name 1–3 confirmed deficits from an actual score (not a hunch), or you have not confirmed the architectural spine of the larger work this passage belongs to, you are about to polish sentences onto a draft with no center. That is the signature long-form failure and the one unrecoverable error. If the user asked for diagnosis ONLY, do not run this workflow — route to `/depth-audit`.

## Input Required
- **The passage** — the chapter, scene, essay, or excerpt to deepen, pasted in full. Deepen works on what's on the page, never on a synopsis of what the chapter is "supposed" to do.
- **The architectural context** *(infer if unstated, confirm if risky)* — what the larger work is *about* underneath its plot (the theme), where this passage sits in the arc, and what it must accomplish for the spine. Long-form is the only vertical where a single passage cannot be deepened in isolation; a scene's depth is set by its job in the whole.
- **The reader promise / stakes** — why a reader keeps turning pages here: the question being held open, the cost on the line, the recognition being built toward. This is the PRESERVE column made concrete for this passage.
- **The narrator / character state** *(Optional)* — the interiority the scene is rendered through; sharpens the scene and earned-emotion passes.
- **The scope** *(Optional, defaults to full-stack rewrite of the passage)* — surgical inject (one or two confirmed deficits, prose mostly intact) vs. full deepening. Never full-rewrite when an inject would do; but note that long-form drafts more often genuinely need the full stack than any other vertical.

## Workflow

The DEEPEN LOOP, run end to end at the **FULL-STACK** book dose: **DIAGNOSE → SELECT + ORDER → APPLY → RECEIPT.** Architecture is not one pass among four here — it is the foundation the other three are built on.

### Step 1: DIAGNOSE (score the 8, name the weakest links, weight architecture)
Diagnose before treating — always. Read the passage once for what it's *about* underneath its events, then score all eight deficits on the 0/1/2 rubric in `references/depth-deficit-taxonomy.md`. Score against the detection signals, not against a vibe. In long-form, two deficits carry extra weight: **#1 No architecture** (a passage with no job in the spine is the deepest possible miss) and **#8 No reader trust** (long arcs punish confusion, unfairness, and fake closure harder than any short form).

| Deficit | The one question that scores it (book lens) | Severity anchors (0 / 1 / 2) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — No architecture | Can I name what this passage is *about* and what job it does for the book's spine, in one sentence? | 0: spine nameable, scene earns its place, opening/closing connect to the throughline · 1: theme reachable but the scene's job is fuzzy · 2: scene is freely reorderable, no reason-to-exist, plot without "why" |
| 2 — Hollow / generic | If I swapped this character/setting for a generic one, would the scene survive? | 0: particular, this-and-no-other · 1: hollow patches between concrete beats · 2: could-be-any-character, any-place throughout |
| 3 — Emotionally unearned | Is the feeling *built* through scene and cost, or *labeled* (melodrama or flatness)? | 0: earned through lived moment · 1: one over-/under-claimed beat · 2: pervasive melodrama or affectless flatness |
| 4 — No signature voice | Strip the byline — does the narration carry a recognizable fingerprint? | 0: unmistakable narrator voice · 1: thin/intermittent, default-novel cadence in stretches · 2: anyone's prose, no fingerprint |
| 5 — Over-explained / bloated | Can I lift ~25–30% — a summarized scene, a re-explained motive — with no loss? | 0: lean, each beat earned once · 1: 10–25% cuttable · 2: ≥25–30% cuttable, motives/themes re-stated |
| 6 — Weak rhythm | Read a paragraph aloud — does it ride the mouth or fight it? | 0: varied length, terminal punch · 1: flattens in stretches · 2: monotone throughout, fails read-aloud |
| 7 — Missing telling detail | Am I told the character felt X, or shown the image that makes me feel it? | 0: shows, reader concludes · 1: mixed, key moments summarized · 2: tells throughout, nothing rendered |
| 8 — No reader trust | Across the arc, am I held — or rushed, confused, judged, handed fake closure? | 0: held start to finish, turns land fair · 1: one trust wobble or unearned exit · 2: lost/managed/cheated, reader bails |

Output one integer per deficit in Ordering-Law order (1→8). Then name the **1–3 weakest links** — the highest-scoring deficits — as the treatment target, with #1 architecture promoted to first treatment whenever it scores 1 or 2. A passage scored clean on architecture (a 0) does NOT load Hawley, however tempting the spine work looks; say so rather than manufacturing a rebuild. *(This is the same diagnostic instrument as `/depth-audit`; the difference is `/depth-book` continues past the score into the prose.)*

### Step 2: SELECT + ORDER (set the full-stack dose, pick owners, sequence spine-first)
Three sub-decisions, in this order:

1. **Confirm the FULL-STACK dose — from the vertical, locked before any owner is chosen.** Open `references/vertical-dosing.md` to the **Book / Long-form / Novel** row. The dose is heavy and the whole Ordering Law runs by default — but "heavy" still bows to two laws: architecture-first (spine before any sentence, every time) and deep ≠ long (a deepened chapter often *cuts* a summarized scene rather than adding one). Lock the truth slot (`/really-real-book`) and the PRESERVE list: the spine/theme once set, narrative continuity, and the reader's earned trust across the arc.
2. **Select only the owners for confirmed deficits.** From `references/routing-map.md`, load exactly the rows for the named weakest links — nothing more. A deficit scored 0 gets no owner; routing one risks over-deepening a passage that was already doing its job.
3. **Order by the Ordering Law — architecture is the foundation, not a peer.** Sequence the selected owners:

| Order | Layer | Owners (load `genius.md` + named command) | Treats deficits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | **Architecture (spine first — load-bearing)** | Noah Hawley — `skills/noah-hawley-storytelling-mastery` → `/hawley-theme-engine`, `/hawley-ending-first` (voice spine via `/hawley-voice-bible` when the narrator fingerprint is the architectural problem) | 1 |
| 2nd | **Scene + telling detail** | Eric Roth — `skills/eric-roth-writing-mastery` → `/visual-prose-for-copy`; Michael Connelly — `skills/michael-connelly-vivid-writing` → `/telling-detail-engine`, `/slingshot-opener` | 2, 7 |
| 3rd | **Line / rhythm** | Nicolas Cole — `skills/nicolas-cole-sentence-craft` → `/atomic-compression-density-audit`, `/terminal-power-rhythm-engineering`; Lamott-Allen technical-craft — `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing` → `/really-real-silence`, `/really-real-attention` | 5, 6 |
| 4th | **Truth + voice (LAST — call, don't re-teach)** | Lamott-Allen really-real → **`/really-real-book`** (the book truth slot — run it, do not duplicate it); voice owners (`skills/ghostwriting-voice-engine` → `/voice-capture`; `skills/noah-hawley-storytelling-mastery` → `/hawley-voice-bible` for narrator voice) | 3, 4 |

If the confirmed set is, say, Deficits 4 and 1, you still run architecture (1st) before voice (4th). On a book draft this is not a stylistic preference — a narrator voice set before the spine exists has nothing to be the voice *of*.

### Step 3: APPLY (run each owner's move INTO the prose, spine-first, in sequence)
Work down the ordered layers from Step 2. For each confirmed deficit: load that owner's `genius.md` + the single named command, and apply *their* move into the passage — never re-implement it here. Preserve the author's core meaning, narrator, and voice throughout; you are deepening their book, not rewriting it as yours.

- **Set the architecture first and hold it.** If Deficit 1 is confirmed, run the Hawley pass before any line is touched — name the spine, confirm what this passage does for the arc, ensure its opening and exit connect to the throughline. Once the spine is set, it joins the PRESERVE list: no later line- or truth-pass may drift the throughline.
- **Build scene on the spine, not instead of it.** Roth/Connelly render the concrete lived moment — but in service of the scene's job in the architecture, not as decoration. A vivid scene that doesn't advance the spine is a beautiful detour; cut or repurpose it.
- **Hold the PRESERVE list as a hard constraint** at every move: the spine/theme, narrative continuity, and the reader's earned trust across the arc. A move that breaks continuity or cheats the reader of an earned turn doesn't go in, however good the sentence sounds.
- **Call the truth slot — never duplicate it.** When Order Step 4 fires, run **`/really-real-book`** for the earned-emotion pass — its book-promise, hard-stuff gate, scene-embodiment, silence-and-stakes, reader-care, and earned-exit logic. Do not re-teach really-real craft inside this workflow; compose it.
- **Deepen ≠ lengthen.** Long-form's most seductive failure is mistaking *deep* for *long*. On a Deficit 5 finding, the move is the cut — a summarized scene collapsed to one lived moment, a re-explained motive deleted. Try subtraction before addition; a deepened chapter is often shorter.
- **Integrate invisibly.** No expert names, no technique labels, no "now applying the telling-detail move" inside the prose. The reader feels the spine and the earned turn, never the machinery. Experts are named only in the Receipt.

### Step 4: RECEIPT (hand back the change-map, name the spine)
End with the Depth Receipt (verbatim block in Output Format). This is where — and the *only* where — experts and moves are named. For long-form, the receipt must additionally name the **architectural spine** the deepening was built on (the one-sentence "what this is about") and the **emotional truth protected** by the `/really-real-book` pass, so the deepening is auditable against the book's throughline.

## Quality Gate
> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: review the output against `genius.md § Anti-Patterns` and `genius.md § Anti-Duplication Contract` before delivering. Flag and fix any violation.
- **Diagnosis ran before the rewrite.** All eight scored, 1–3 weakest links named — not a hunch. No diagnosis, no rewrite. If you treated a deficit you never scored, rebuild from Step 1.
- **Architecture was set first and the spine is nameable.** If Deficit 1 scored 1–2, the Hawley pass ran before any line work, and the Receipt names the one-sentence spine. If you polished sentences onto a passage with no center, you committed the signature long-form failure — return to architecture and re-sequence.
- **Ordering Law respected.** Architecture → scene/detail → line/rhythm → truth/voice. A narrator voice or earned-emotion pass that ran before the spine existed is out of order; re-sequence.
- **Only confirmed deficits treated.** Every move maps to a deficit scored 1 or 2. A deficit scored 0 got no owner. If you "improved" a clean dimension, you over-deepened — return it.
- **Deep, not long.** Did the passage get longer by default? On a #5 finding the move should have been a cut — a summarized scene collapsed, a re-explained motive deleted. If length grew without a deficit demanding it, rebuild leaner; a deepened chapter is often shorter.
- **Function and continuity preserved.** The PRESERVE items (spine, narrative continuity, reader's earned trust) are intact or stronger. If a "literary" move broke continuity, cheated an earned turn, or drifted the throughline, it doesn't ship.
- **No machinery on the page.** No expert names, no technique labels, no manufactured trauma or false vulnerability in the prose. Experts appear ONLY in the Depth Receipt. If a reader can feel the technique stack, integrate harder.
- **Composed, never duplicated.** Every move ran through its owner's real command; the truth slot was *called* (`/really-real-book`), not re-implemented. If really-real craft got re-taught inside this file's run, route to the owner instead.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Polished before the spine existed.** Ran line-craft and earned-emotion passes on a passage with no reason-to-exist, ending with beautiful prose that means nothing across the arc. Recovery: obey the Ordering Law — run Hawley first (`/hawley-theme-engine`, `/hawley-ending-first`), name the spine, then re-run the line and truth passes on top of it.
- **Deep confused with long.** "Deepened" the chapter by adding scenes, backstory, and re-explained motive, producing a bloated draft. Recovery: re-run DIAGNOSE for Deficit 5; collapse summarized scenes into single lived moments and cut the re-explanations — depth in long-form is usually subtraction.
- **Scene that doesn't serve the spine.** Built a vivid, gorgeous scene that advances nothing the architecture needs — a beautiful detour. Recovery: test every scene against the one-sentence spine; if it doesn't earn its place, cut it or repurpose it to do the spine's work.
- **Truth slot re-implemented.** Re-taught really-real book craft (hard-stuff gate, earned exit) inside this workflow instead of calling `/really-real-book`. Recovery: enforce the Anti-Duplication Contract — run `/really-real-book` for the earned-emotion pass; compose it, never duplicate it.
- **Continuity broken for a local fix.** A deepening move to one scene contradicted an earlier or later beat in the arc. Recovery: hold narrative continuity as a hard PRESERVE constraint; check every change against the passage's place in the whole before it ships.
- **Manufactured emotion.** Injected trauma or confession the passage hadn't earned, chasing "heart." Recovery: depth is earned, never faked — run the hard-stuff gate in `/really-real-book` to find the real pressure the draft is avoiding, and build the feeling through scene and cost rather than labeling it.
- **Over-scoped the ask.** Full-stack rewrote when a surgical inject would do, or rewrote when the user asked for diagnosis only. Recovery: match scope to the ask — diagnosis-only routes to `/depth-audit`; a one-deficit fix is an inject, not a teardown.
