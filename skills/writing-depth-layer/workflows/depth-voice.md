# Voice (Voice-as-Music)

A thin draft and a voiceless draft are two different sicknesses. A thin draft is missing a spine, an image, a feeling — and the rest of the depth layer fixes those. A voiceless draft has all of that and still reads like no one: competent words, default explainer cadence, the byline interchangeable. You could swap the author and nothing would break. That is Deficit 4, and it is the one deficit you cannot fix by adding more — you fix it by finding the one human already faintly present in the lines and turning the prose *toward* them.

This workflow owns that single move. It runs the source's voice-as-music model (Lamott + Allen, the Beatles typology): every writer integrates melody, rhythm, and harmony but one center dominates — the melodist who leaps intervals (metaphor on metaphor), the rhythmist who thinks in the conversational beat, the rhythmic-melodist whose rhythm serves the meaning being sung, the harmonist whose gift is warmth and relationship. The error to avoid is homogenizing — sanding a melodist into a metronome, or forcing all three centers equally until the voice is "balanced" and dead. The job: diagnose the natural center, build on that strength, add only the single missing layer, and end by naming the voice center found in the Depth Receipt. This is a Tier 3 specialist pass — it treats Deficit 4 and nothing else. If the draft also lacks a spine or a scene, that is `/deepen`'s job, not this one.

## Input Required
- **The draft** — the prose to find and deepen the voice in, pasted in full. Voice-as-music diagnoses from what's on the page; a description of "how it should sound" is not a sample.
- **The vertical** — social / copy / marketing / book-long-form / client-personal. Sets the dose and the truth slot, and — critically — the PRESERVE list the voice pass must not break (hook/CTA/offer/spine). If ambiguous, ask.
- **A voice reference / sample of the real human** *(strongly preferred; infer from the draft if absent)* — prior posts, transcripts, voice notes, or any writing where the author already sounds like themselves. The natural center is far easier to find when there's a true sample to triangulate against; without one, the draft itself is the only evidence and the center is read from its faint best moments.
- **The author's own read of their gift** *(Optional)* — if the writer already knows they "drop metaphors" or "write the way they talk" or "lead with warmth," that names the center directly and you build on it. Don't override a self-aware writer's known strength with your guess.
- **The scope** *(Optional, defaults to surgical voice inject)* — voice work is usually an inject (turn the prose toward the center, prose otherwise intact), not a teardown. Never full-rewrite a draft whose only deficit is voice.

## Workflow

The DEEPEN LOOP, specialized to the single voice move: **DIAGNOSE the center → SELECT + ORDER (build on strength) → APPLY (turn toward the center) → RECEIPT (name the center found).**

### Step 1: DIAGNOSE — confirm Deficit 4, then find the natural center
Two reads, in order. Do not skip to picking a voice.

**Read A — confirm the deficit is real.** Score Deficit 4 on the taxonomy's 0/1/2 rubric. The byline-strip test is the fast gate: cover the author's name — would anyone who knows this writer recognize the piece as theirs? Listen for the tells in `references/depth-deficit-taxonomy.md § 4`: default explainer cadence, "Here's what / Here's why / Here's how" openers, twin-sentence endings, "It's not X. It's Y.", triple anaphora. A draft scored **0** has a fingerprint already — stop and return it. A **1** is thin/intermittent (a tell or two, cadence flattens in patches). A **2** is no recoverable fingerprint — AI/anyone throughout.

**Read B — find the dominant center.** Now read for the *gift*, not the flaw. Even a voiceless draft has best moments — the one place the writer leaned in, the one true sentence, the move they reach for under pressure. That is the center. Score the three musical registers and name the dominant one against the Beatles typology (`technical-craft-36-rules.md § Voice as Music`):

| Center | Source name | The gift to listen for | Where it shows in a draft |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Melody = the head** | Paul (pure melodist) | Leaps of interest — metaphor on metaphor, the fresh association, curiosity that makes the reader lean in | The line that surprised you; an unexpected image; a writer who *drops one metaphor after another* |
| **Rhythm = the body** | John (rhythmist) | The conversational beat (NOT iambic — the rhythms of talk), breath and pace, bending the line | A sentence that rides the mouth; punchy fragments; a writer who "writes the way they talk" |
| **Rhythmic-melodist** | Ringo (follows the voice, not the bass) | Rhythm shaped by and serving the *meaning being sung* — cadence that tracks the sense, not a fixed beat | Pacing that slows for the heavy beat and quickens for the light one; rhythm in service of melody |
| **Harmony = the heart** | George (harmonist) | Relationship and warmth — being relational, adding voices, the reader feeling *accompanied* | A line that turns to the reader; warmth under the argument; the sense of a hand on the shoulder |

Name **one** dominant center (and, if clear, a secondary). Then name the **single missing layer** — the register the draft is starving for. Most voiceless drafts are not weak in all three; they are strong-but-buried in one and flat in another: a clever (melody) draft with no warmth (harmony); a sincere (harmony) draft with no leap; a true thought with no breath (rhythm). The treatment is to *amplify the buried center* and *add just enough of the one missing layer* for the piece to come alive — never to balance all three equally.

### Step 2: SELECT + ORDER — build on the strength, set the dose
Three sub-decisions, in this order:

1. **Set the dose from the vertical (not the draft's ambition).** Open `references/vertical-dosing.md` to the confirmed row. Lock the PRESERVE list (hook / CTA / offer / proof / position / spine) and the truth slot. Voice is dosed: social = the lightest possible turn-toward-center inside the existing shape; book/long-form = a fuller voice pass across the arc. The dose caps how much of the prose the voice move may touch.
2. **Decide the move: amplify + add-one-layer, never homogenize.** From the diagnosis, the plan is fixed in shape — *strengthen the dominant center, add only the single missing layer*. A melodist is never flattened into a metronomic rhythmist; a harmonist's warmth is never traded for cleverness it doesn't have. Build on what's already strong.
3. **Order the owners by the Ordering Law — voice is LAST.** Voice/truth is Order Step 4. If Read A surfaced *other* deficits scored 1–2 (architecture, scene, line), STOP and route to `/deepen` — those must be fixed before voice, or you set a confident fingerprint on a draft with no center. Within this voice-only pass, select exactly the owners the named center needs:

| Center / need | Owner (load `genius.md` + named command) | What it does for the voice |
|---|---|---|
| **The model itself** (every run) | Lamott-Allen — `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing` → Pattern 7 Music Diagnostic (the melody/rhythm/harmony voice-as-music model; Pattern table in `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing/references/genius-patterns.md`) | The lens: names the center and the missing layer; governs "build on strength, don't homogenize" |
| **Capture the real human's fingerprint** (when a sample exists, or to define the center from the draft's best moments) | ghostwriting-voice-engine — `skills/ghostwriting-voice-engine` → `/voice-capture` (01) | Extracts the author's actual signature moves so the deepening turns toward *their* voice, not a generic "good" one |
| **Set the rhythmic fingerprint** (when the missing/weak layer is rhythm — the body) | Nicolas Cole — `skills/nicolas-cole-sentence-craft` → `/terminal-power-rhythm-engineering` | Builds the cadence and terminal punch that make the rhythm recognizable and varied |
| **Scale a captured voice into new lines** (when producing more in-voice prose, not just fixing the sample) | ghostwriting-voice-engine — `skills/ghostwriting-voice-engine` → `/content-production` (02) | Generates additional copy that holds the captured fingerprint |
| **Charisma / cadence reinforcement** *(optional — only when the fingerprint gap is specifically rhythm-charisma)* | Kallaway — `skills/kallaway-word-mastery` → `/rhythm-rewrite`, `/charisma-engineering` | Optional voice-rhythm reinforcement per the routing map; do not load unless the center is cadence-charisma |
| **Truth slot** (last, when the voice turn also needs the draft to ring true) | Lamott-Allen — the vertical's `/really-real-*` pass | **CALL it, never re-implement.** Truth and voice are the same final layer; a recognizable voice that says nothing true still fails |

Load only the rows the named center needs. A draft whose buried center is melody and whose missing layer is warmth does NOT load Cole's rhythm engine — adding rhythm work would homogenize.

### Step 3: APPLY — turn the prose toward the center
Work the voice move into the draft. Load each selected owner's `genius.md` + its single named command and apply *their* technique — never re-implement it here.

- **Amplify the dominant center first.** If melody: let the strongest existing leap stand taller and clear the flat lines that smother it. If rhythm: find the conversational beat the writer already reaches for and bend the monotone lines to it. If rhythmic-melodist: let pace track meaning — slow the heavy beats, quicken the light. If harmony: surface the one moment the writer turned toward the reader and let warmth carry the section.
- **Add only the single missing layer — just enough.** A clever draft gets one true warm turn, not a sentiment bath. A sincere draft gets one fresh leap, not a metaphor pile-up. Stop the moment the piece comes alive; over-adding re-homogenizes.
- **Kill the AI cadence as you go.** The before-snippet tells (`taxonomy § 4`) — "Here's what / Here's why / Here's how," twin-sentence endings, "It's not X. It's Y.", triple anaphora — are the opposite of a fingerprint. Replace them with the writer's actual move, not a different generic one.
- **Hold the PRESERVE list as a hard constraint.** A voice turn that strengthens the line but blurs the CTA, dissolves the position, or drifts the spine is wrong no matter how alive it sounds.
- **Call the truth slot — never duplicate it.** When the voice turn needs the draft to ring true, run the vertical's `/really-real-*` pass. Do not re-teach really-real craft inside this file.
- **Integrate invisibly.** No expert names, no "now amplifying the melodist center," no technique labels in the prose. The reader feels one specific human, never the machinery. Experts and the center are named ONLY in the Depth Receipt.

### Step 4: RECEIPT — name the voice center found
End with the Depth Receipt (verbatim block in Output Format). This pass has one signature obligation the other workflows don't: **the receipt must name the voice center found** (melodist / rhythmist / rhythmic-melodist / harmonist), the buried strength amplified, and the single layer added. That naming is the deliverable's proof — it tells the writer who they are on the page so they can keep writing toward it.

## Content-Type Adaptations
| Vertical | How `/depth-voice` adapts (dose / order / truth slot) |
|---|---|
| **Social** (LinkedIn / X / IG / newsletter short) | LIGHTEST touch. The voice turn happens *inside* the existing scroll-stopping shape — never lengthen to "add voice." Usually the move is killing the AI-cadence tells in the hook and first lines and letting the one true beat the writer already has carry it. PRESERVE the hook, brevity, scannability, platform-native shape. Truth slot: `/really-real-social`. A post that suddenly reads like an essay to "sound more like someone" is a failure. |
| **Copy** (ads / VSL / landing / email / offers) | MEDIUM — voice WITHOUT losing conversion. The fingerprint that makes copy stop sounding like every ad is the win; add the captured human's cadence and one missing layer into the conversion skeleton. PRESERVE offer logic, CTA, proof, clarity-to-action. Truth slot: `/really-real-marketing`. A recognizable voice that buries the next action is the wrong trade — clarity-to-action beats charisma. |
| **Marketing / Brand** | MEDIUM-HEAVY — the brand should sound like a human who believes something, not a category. Capture the founder's/brand's actual center and amplify it; warmth (harmony) is often the missing layer brands starve for, but add it only if the draft earns it. PRESERVE the belief/position and the strategic frame. Truth slot: `/really-real-marketing`. Generic "authentic brand voice" that could paste onto any competitor is the failure this pass exists to kill. |
| **Book / long-form / novel** | FULLER voice pass — but still LAST, after architecture/scene/line. Voice can run across the arc here, not just one section. Find the author's center and hold it consistently chapter to chapter; the missing layer (often a leap in an over-sincere manuscript, or warmth in a clever one) is added with restraint. PRESERVE the spine/theme and narrative continuity once set. Truth slot: `/really-real-book`. Don't let a voice pass drift the established throughline. |
| **Client / personal-brand** | MEASURED — voice shows as a *recognizable, credible* human, not performed personality. The center is usually rhythmic or harmonic (a trusted advisor's measured cadence, warmth under the authority); amplify that, do not bolt on cleverness the brand doesn't have. PRESERVE the claim→evidence→so-what argument, credibility, and restraint. Truth slot: `/really-real-client`. Never manufacture a "quirky" voice or false intimacy for relatability — a credible voice that under-claims beats a charismatic one that overreaches. |

## Quality Gate
> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: review the output against `genius.md § Anti-Patterns` and `genius.md § Anti-Duplication Contract`, plus the "build on the strength / add only the missing layer / don't homogenize" rule in `technical-craft-36-rules.md § Voice as Music`, before delivering. Flag and fix any violation.
- **Deficit 4 actually scored 1 or 2.** If the draft scored 0 (recognizable fingerprint already), you should have returned it, not "improved" it. Improving a clean voice is homogenization. Rebuild — return the draft.
- **A natural center was found, not invented.** The receipt names melodist / rhythmist / rhythmic-melodist / harmonist with evidence from the draft's own best moment. If the center was guessed with no anchor in the prose (and no sample), you manufactured a fingerprint — re-read for the gift before applying.
- **Built on the strength; did not homogenize.** The dominant center was amplified and exactly one missing layer added — not all three balanced into deadness, not a melodist sanded into a metronome. If you forced equal melody/rhythm/harmony, return it.
- **Voice ran LAST.** No voice fingerprint was set on a draft still missing its spine/scene/line. If other deficits scored 1–2, this should have routed to `/deepen` first. If you set a confident voice on a centerless draft, re-sequence.
- **AI cadence is gone.** The explainer tells — "Here's what/why/how," twin-sentence endings, "It's not X. It's Y.", triple anaphora — are removed, replaced by the writer's real move, not a different generic one. If any banned move survived, the voice isn't fixed.
- **Function preserved.** The PRESERVE item (hook / CTA / offer / proof / position / spine) is intact or stronger. A voice turn that blurred the next action or dissolved the position doesn't ship.
- **No machinery on the page; center named only in the Receipt.** No expert names, no technique labels, no "amplifying the harmonist" in the prose. The reader feels one human. The voice center and owners appear ONLY in the Depth Receipt. If a reader can feel the technique, integrate harder.
- **Composed, never duplicated.** The voice-as-music model was applied via Lamott-Allen Pattern 7; the truth slot was *called* (`/really-real-*`), not re-taught; rhythm work ran through Cole's real command. If craft got re-implemented inside this file, route to the owner instead.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Manufactured a fingerprint instead of finding one.** Picked a "good" voice and imposed it, so the prose now sounds like a *different* generic writer. Recovery: re-read for the draft's own best moment — the leap, the beat, the warm turn it already reaches for — and amplify *that*; if there's a real sample, run `/voice-capture` against it first.
- **Homogenized the voice.** Balanced melody, rhythm, and harmony equally until the piece is "polished" and dead — or flattened a natural melodist into a metronomic rhythmist. Recovery: name the one dominant center, amplify it, add only the single missing layer, and stop the moment the piece comes alive.
- **Set voice before the spine.** Applied a confident fingerprint to a draft that was also missing architecture/scene/line, so the voice now confidently says nothing. Recovery: voice is the LAST layer — route to `/deepen` to fix the earlier deficits first, then return for the voice pass.
- **Over-deepened to "add voice."** Lengthened a social post or copy block to make it "sound more like someone," killing the function. Recovery: voice is a turn, not an addition — re-confirm the dose, kill the AI-cadence tells inside the existing shape, and add at most one layer lightly.
- **Improved a clean voice.** Ran the pass on a draft that already had an unmistakable fingerprint (scored 0) and homogenized it. Recovery: the byline-strip test gates this — if the writer is already recognizable, return the draft untouched and say so.
- **Truth slot re-implemented.** Re-taught really-real craft inside this workflow instead of calling the matching `/really-real-*` pass for the truth layer. Recovery: enforce the Anti-Duplication Contract — compose the truth-slot owner, never duplicate it.
- **Function sacrificed for charisma.** Produced a vivid, recognizable line that buried the CTA, dissolved the position, or drifted the spine. Recovery: hold the PRESERVE column as a hard constraint — any voice move touching the protected function must strengthen it or be reverted.
