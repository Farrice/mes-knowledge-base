# Depth Deficit Taxonomy

The eight deficits every `/depth-audit` scores. This is the diagnostic instrument of the Writing Depth Layer — the conductor that DEEPENS a draft by diagnosing what depth is missing, then composing existing craft owners in the right order and dose. It does not re-teach craft; it names the absence and routes the fix.

The deficits are ordered by the **Ordering Law** (architecture FIRST → structure/scene → line/rhythm → truth/voice LAST). Read top to bottom: that is the sequence a deepen pass treats them in. Inverting it — polishing lines before there is a spine — yields well-crafted sentences with nothing underneath. Diagnose all eight, then fix only the 1–3 confirmed weakest links, in this order.

Each owner reference below uses a REAL skill path and the owner's own commands. The depth layer COMPOSES these owners — it never duplicates the craft they already do. Truth slots route to the per-vertical `/really-real-*` pass; sentence-craft knowledge lives only in `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing`.

---

## How to score each deficit (the shared rubric)

Every deficit is scored on the same 3-point scale. `/depth-audit` returns one integer per deficit, then names the 1–3 highest-scoring (weakest-link) deficits as the treatment target.

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | Absent | The deficit is not present. The draft already does this thing well enough to leave alone. Do NOT route an owner here — touching it risks over-deepening. |
| **1** | Present | The deficit is detectable: at least one clear detection signal fires, but the draft still functions. Route the owner with a LIGHT dose (often a surgical inject, not a rewrite). |
| **2** | Severe | The deficit is structural: multiple signals fire, or one fires so hard the draft fails its job (no spine, reads like anyone, melodrama, throat-clearing the reader bails on). Route the owner with a FULL dose, in Ordering-Law sequence with the other severe deficits. |

A draft is healthy when no deficit scores 2 and at most one or two score 1. "Comprehensive coverage of all eight" is not the goal — surgical fixes of the weakest links are. Per-deficit severity below restates this scale in the deficit's own terms so the audit can anchor consistently.

## 1. No Architecture

**What it is.** No spine, no theme, no reason this piece exists. There are events, claims, or beats — but no "why," nothing the whole is about. Highest in the Ordering Law: fix this first or every downstream fix decorates a draft with no center.

**Detection signals (spot these in the draft):**
- You cannot answer "what is this *about*, underneath the topic?" in one sentence after reading it.
- Paragraphs are reorderable with no loss — nothing depends on what came before; sequence is arbitrary.
- The ending summarizes or just stops; it does not *land* anything the opening set up. No through-line connects first beat to last.

**Before-snippet (deficit present):**
> Remote work has pros and cons. Some people love the flexibility. Others miss the office. Productivity studies are mixed. In the end, it depends on the person.

*(A list of true-enough observations with no spine — it is about nothing, drives toward nothing, and could end at any sentence.)*

**Owner that fixes it:** Noah Hawley — theme / ending-first.
`skills/noah-hawley-storytelling-mastery` (genius: `skills/noah-hawley-storytelling-mastery/genius.md`) · commands: `/hawley-theme-engine`, `/hawley-ending-first`.

**Severity rubric (0 / 1 / 2):**
| Score | Condition |
|---|---|
| **0** | A reader could name the spine/theme in one sentence; ending pays off the opening. |
| **1** | A theme is reachable but buried — present in one beat, absent from the structure; sequence mostly holds but a few sections float. |
| **2** | No spine recoverable; paragraphs freely reorderable; the piece is "about" its topic but about nothing underneath. Fix FIRST — nothing downstream matters until there is a center. |

## 2. Hollow / Generic

**What it is.** Abstract, could-be-anyone prose. No concrete stakes, no specific human, no detail only this writer/subject could supply. The draft describes a category instead of inhabiting an instance.

**Detection signals:**
- Swap any proper noun or product for a competitor's and nothing breaks — the copy fits anyone in the category.
- Stakes are named in the abstract ("this matters," "it's important") but never made concrete (who loses what, when, how much).
- Zero sensory or specific anchors — no object, number, name, place, or moment a reader can picture.

**Before-snippet (deficit present):**
> Our solution helps businesses improve efficiency and drive results. We're committed to delivering value and exceeding expectations for every client we serve.

*(Interchangeable with ten thousand other companies; no stake a reader can feel, no detail only this seller could write.)*

**Owner that fixes it:** Michael Connelly telling-detail + Lamott-Allen specificity.
`skills/michael-connelly-vivid-writing` (genius: `skills/michael-connelly-vivid-writing/genius.md`) · command: `/telling-detail-engine`.
`skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing` (genius: `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing/genius.md`) · Pattern 4 Plain-Force Language for grounding the abstraction in lived specificity (Pattern table in `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing/references/genius-patterns.md`).

**Severity rubric (0 / 1 / 2):**
| Score | Condition |
|---|---|
| **0** | Concrete and particular — specific stakes, at least one anchor detail only this piece could carry. |
| **1** | Mostly specific but with hollow patches — a generic claim or two where a concrete one belonged. |
| **2** | Could-be-anyone throughout; proper-noun swap test passes; no concrete stake or detail anywhere. |

## 3. Emotionally Unearned

**What it is.** Sentiment asserted instead of earned — in either direction: **melodrama** (feeling overclaimed, the draft tells you to be moved) or **flatness** (feeling that should be there is absent). Trust in the emotion is missing because the draft did not build it.

**Detection signals:**
- Emotion is *labeled* ("it was heartbreaking," "I was devastated," "this is so exciting") rather than rendered through what happened.
- Big feeling words arrive with no scene, detail, or cost underneath them — the reader is told to feel before given a reason.
- Or the inverse: a moment that should carry weight passes in a flat, reportorial sentence with the feeling drained out.

**Before-snippet (deficit present):**
> Losing the account was absolutely devastating, the most heartbreaking moment of my entire career, and I will never forget how incredibly painful it was.

*(Three intensity labels stacked; no scene, no concrete loss, nothing the reader can feel for themselves — sentiment asserted, not earned.)*

**Owner that fixes it:** Lamott-Allen really-real + Roth erosion.
`skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing` (genius: `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing/genius.md`) · commands: `/really-real-writing`, plus the vertical truth slot (`/really-real-social`, `/really-real-marketing`, `/really-real-book`, `/really-real-client`).
`skills/eric-roth-writing-mastery` (genius: `skills/eric-roth-writing-mastery/genius.md`) · command: `/content-erosion-protocol` to erode overclaimed sentiment down to the earned core.

**Severity rubric (0 / 1 / 2):**
| Score | Condition |
|---|---|
| **0** | Emotion is earned — built through scene/detail/cost; the reader feels it without being told to. |
| **1** | One overclaimed or flattened beat; the draft mostly earns its feeling but tips into asserting (or draining) it once. |
| **2** | Pervasive melodrama OR pervasive flatness; the reader is repeatedly told what to feel, or denied a feeling the material clearly holds. |

## 4. No Signature Voice

**What it is.** Sounds like AI, or like anyone. No rhythmic fingerprint, no recognizable engine — competent words with no one behind them. A reader could not pick this writer out of a lineup.

**Detection signals:**
- Read-aloud produces a default "explainer" cadence with no idiosyncratic turn, no signature move, no withholding.
- Hallmark AI-tells fire: "Here's what / Here's why / Here's how" openers, twin-sentence endings, "It's not X. It's Y." constructions, triple anaphora.
- Strip the byline and no one who knows the writer would recognize the piece as theirs.

**Before-snippet (deficit present):**
> Here's the thing about consistency. It's not about motivation. It's about systems. Here's why that matters, and here's how you can start today.

*(Default AI cadence — "Here's the thing," "It's not X, it's Y," "here's why / here's how" — zero fingerprint; this is no one's voice.)*

**Owner that fixes it:** voice-as-music model + ghostwriting-voice-engine + Cole.
`skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing` (genius: `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing/genius.md`) · Pattern 7 Music Diagnostic (the melody/rhythm/harmony "voice-as-music" model; Pattern table in `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing/references/genius-patterns.md`).
`skills/ghostwriting-voice-engine` (genius: `skills/ghostwriting-voice-engine/genius.md`) · commands: `/voice-capture` (01), `/content-production` (02).
`skills/nicolas-cole-sentence-craft` (genius: `skills/nicolas-cole-sentence-craft/genius.md`) · command: `/terminal-power-rhythm-engineering` for the rhythmic fingerprint.

**Severity rubric (0 / 1 / 2):**
| Score | Condition |
|---|---|
| **0** | Unmistakable fingerprint — recognizable cadence and moves; byline-strip test still identifies the writer. |
| **1** | A voice is present but thin or intermittent; one or two AI-tells slip in; cadence flattens in patches. |
| **2** | Reads like AI / anyone; default explainer cadence throughout; multiple banned tells fire; no recoverable fingerprint. |

## 5. Over-Explained / Bloated

**What it is.** The draft proves what the reader already trusts — second explanations, defensive caveats, throat-clearing, repeated proof. Roughly 25–30% is cuttable. Deepening here means CUTTING; deepen is not lengthen.

**Detection signals:**
- A point is made, then re-made in different words (the "in other words" / "what I mean is" restatement) where a trusting reader needed it once.
- Caveats and hedges defend against objections the reader was not going to raise.
- Estimate the cut: if you can lift ~25–30% with no loss of meaning, the deficit is real.

**Before-snippet (deficit present):**
> The first step is to define your audience. In other words, you need to know who you're writing for. This is really important because, as you can probably imagine, writing without knowing your audience tends not to work very well. So, to put it simply, define your audience first.

*(One idea — "define your audience" — stated four times; the reader trusted it after the first sentence. ~70% is cuttable.)*

**Owner that fixes it:** Lamott-Allen economize + Cole compression + Roth erosion.
`skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing` (genius: `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing/genius.md`) · Pattern 6 Anti-Overproof Cut (Pattern table in `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing/references/genius-patterns.md`).
`skills/nicolas-cole-sentence-craft` (genius: `skills/nicolas-cole-sentence-craft/genius.md`) · command: `/atomic-compression-density-audit`.
`skills/eric-roth-writing-mastery` (genius: `skills/eric-roth-writing-mastery/genius.md`) · command: `/content-erosion-protocol`.

**Severity rubric (0 / 1 / 2):**
| Score | Condition |
|---|---|
| **0** | Lean — nothing meaningful cuttable; each point earns its space and is made once. |
| **1** | Some bloat — roughly 10–25% cuttable; a few restatements or defensive caveats. |
| **2** | Heavily over-proven — ~25–30%+ cuttable; ideas re-made repeatedly, throat-clearing throughout; the bloat itself dulls the piece. |

## 6. Weak Rhythm

**What it is.** Monotone sentence length, no cadence, fails the read-aloud test. The prose may be correct and even specific, but it plods — no build-and-release, no terminal punch, nothing for the ear.

**Detection signals:**
- Sentence lengths cluster (all medium, all long); no short sentence ever snaps a passage shut.
- Read aloud, you run out of breath or stumble — the cadence fights the mouth instead of riding it.
- Paragraph endings land soft; the last word of key sentences carries no weight (no terminal power).

**Before-snippet (deficit present):**
> When you start building a habit you should try to make it small enough that you can do it every single day without much effort, and then over time you can gradually increase the difficulty so that it continues to challenge you and help you grow as a person over the long term.

*(One unbroken medium-long sentence; no short beat, no terminal punch, nothing for the ear — it reads like a held breath.)*

**Owner that fixes it:** Cole sentence craft + Lamott-Allen read-aloud.
`skills/nicolas-cole-sentence-craft` (genius: `skills/nicolas-cole-sentence-craft/genius.md`) · command: `/terminal-power-rhythm-engineering`.
`skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing` (genius: `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing/genius.md`) · Pattern 7 Music Diagnostic (read-aloud rhythm fix; Pattern table in `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing/references/genius-patterns.md`).

**Severity rubric (0 / 1 / 2):**
| Score | Condition |
|---|---|
| **0** | Reads aloud cleanly — varied lengths, build-and-release, terminal punch where it matters. |
| **1** | Mostly fine but flattens in stretches; a passage or two of monotone length; some soft endings. |
| **2** | Monotone throughout; fails read-aloud; no length variation, no cadence, no terminal power anywhere. |

## 7. Missing Telling Detail

**What it is.** Tells instead of shows. Conclusions and adjectives where there should be a concrete image. Distinct from Hollow/Generic (#2): #2 is abstraction at the level of *stake and subject*; #7 is the absence of the *specific rendered image* that would let the reader see it.

**Detection signals:**
- Adjective-driven characterization or description ("she was nervous," "the office was chaotic") with no image to back the label.
- The reader is handed the conclusion ("it was a tense meeting") instead of the detail that would produce it.
- No single load-bearing concrete object/gesture/moment a reader could quote back.

**Before-snippet (deficit present):**
> She was clearly nervous before the big presentation, and you could tell she really cared about doing a good job and making a strong impression on everyone.

*(All conclusion, no image — "nervous," "cared," "strong impression." Nothing shown; the reader is told how to read a person they never get to see.)*

**Owner that fixes it:** Connelly + Roth visual prose.
`skills/michael-connelly-vivid-writing` (genius: `skills/michael-connelly-vivid-writing/genius.md`) · command: `/telling-detail-engine`.
`skills/eric-roth-writing-mastery` (genius: `skills/eric-roth-writing-mastery/genius.md`) · command: `/visual-prose-for-copy`.

**Severity rubric (0 / 1 / 2):**
| Score | Condition |
|---|---|
| **0** | Shows through concrete, telling detail; the reader sees it and draws the conclusion themselves. |
| **1** | Mixed — some images, but key moments still told via adjective/conclusion instead of shown. |
| **2** | Tells throughout; conclusions and adjectives stand in for every image; nothing rendered. |

## 8. No Reader Trust

**What it is.** The reader is confused, treated unfairly, made to wade through throat-clearing, or handed fake closure. The piece does not hold them — they feel rushed, judged, manipulated, or abandoned. Lowest in the Ordering Law (truth/trust last), but a 2 here can sink an otherwise strong draft.

**Detection signals:**
- Throat-clearing or a slow, defended open that makes a reader work before earning their attention; the cost of staying is not yet paid.
- Confusion or unfairness — a leap the reader cannot follow, a claim that judges or manipulates them, an unanswered "wait, what?"
- Fake closure — a tidy moral, a summary bow, or a resolution the piece did not earn, leaving the reader feeling managed rather than met.

**Before-snippet (deficit present):**
> Before we get into it, let me just say that this is something I've thought about for a long time and that I think is really worth your attention if you'll bear with me. Anyway. The point is, everything happens for a reason, and once you realize that, all your problems basically solve themselves.

*(Throat-clearing open that makes the reader wait, then a fake-closure bow — "everything happens for a reason… problems solve themselves" — that the piece never earned. The reader feels managed, not held.)*

**Owner that fixes it:** Lamott-Allen reader-trust.
`skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing` (genius: `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing/genius.md`) · command: `/really-real-reader-trust` (Pattern 9 Reader-Trust Repair; Pattern 11 Humane Ending for fake closure — Pattern table in `skills/lamott-allen-really-real-writing/references/genius-patterns.md`).

**Severity rubric (0 / 1 / 2):**
| Score | Condition |
|---|---|
| **0** | The reader is held start to finish — earns attention fast, never confused or judged, ending is fair and earned. |
| **1** | One trust wobble — a slow open, a single confusing leap, or a slightly unearned close — but the reader stays. |
| **2** | The reader is lost, judged, manipulated, or handed fake closure; throat-clearing or unfairness makes them bail. |

## Audit output contract

`/depth-audit` emits one severity integer (0/1/2) per deficit, in Ordering-Law order (1→8), then names the **weakest link(s)** — the 1–3 highest-scoring deficits — as the treatment target. The deepen pass then SELECTS only those owners, SEQUENCES them by the Ordering Law (architecture → scene/detail → line/rhythm → truth/voice), sets the dose by vertical, and ends with a Depth Receipt.

Reminders the audit must honor:
- **Diagnose before treating.** Never refine on a misdiagnosed draft. A wrong weakest-link call sends the wrong owner.
- **Fix only confirmed deficits.** A 0 is left alone — routing an owner at a 0 risks over-deepening (turning a 150-word post into an essay).
- **Order is non-negotiable.** A 2 on #1 (No Architecture) is always treated before a 2 on #6 (Weak Rhythm). Line-craft on a spineless draft produces well-crafted nothing.
- **Owners are composed, never duplicated.** The names above appear only in the Depth Receipt — never inside the deepened prose. Integrate the moves invisibly.
- **Deepen ≠ lengthen.** Deficit #5 is the standing reminder: the most common deepening move is the cut.
