# Customer Improvement Loop

Use this after a first buyer, paid pilot, client, or product customer exists.

## Input Required

- Offer promise, scope, price, buyer, and delivery record
- Payment or deposit evidence
- Customer communications and consent state
- Observed result, missing value, confusion, and objections
- Current marketing and acquisition source

## Protocol

1. Verify that a payment, deposit, or contracted pilot exists. If not, mark `NO EVENT` and return to the live-offer gate.
2. Ask where the buyer found the offer, why they bought, what was confusing, what was missing, what they would pay more for, and what outcome they achieved.
3. Preserve exact language with source and consent metadata.
4. Classify each finding as:
   - core promise repair,
   - delivery/experience repair,
   - adjacent upsell,
   - unsupported or out of scope.
5. Choose one version change and one next measurement.
6. Request a testimonial only after real value has been experienced; keep private research separate from public permission.
7. After repeatable delivery, choose either traffic or LTV as the next scale lever—not both by default.

## Output Contract

Produce the payment state, interview guide, language ledger, classification table, version decision, testimonial boundary, next measurement, and one scale hypothesis or explicit reason to wait.

## Quality Gate

- Payment evidence is distinct from delivery success
- Testimonial request follows experienced value
- Consent and exact wording are recorded
- Core repair and upsell are not conflated
- One version change has a measurable outcome
- No scale recommendation before repeatable delivery
