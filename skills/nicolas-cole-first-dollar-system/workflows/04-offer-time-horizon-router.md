# Offer Time-Horizon Router

Use this when choosing between services, digital products, subscriptions, communities, coaching, courses, or software.

## Input Required

- Cash deadline and target amount
- Existing skills and work the operator is willing to repeat
- Buyer access, audience, email list, and demand evidence
- Delivery capacity, proof, tools, and permissions
- Desired leverage and complexity tolerance

## Protocol

1. State the cash clock: urgent (0-30 days), near-term (31-90 days), or patient (90+ days).
2. Score each plausible offer on time to buyer decision, number of buyers required, current skill fit, delivery risk, proof burden, and leverage.
3. Apply the default branch:
   - urgent cash -> one bounded service, paid audit, or sprint;
   - near-term -> service-first with a reusable delivery asset or a pre-sold small product;
   - patient -> product or recurring model only when demand/distribution evidence exists.
4. Reject models selected mainly because they appear passive, fashionable, or scalable.
5. Write the smallest paid test and the event that would falsify the choice.
6. Hand urgent cash to `/first-10k`, service mechanics to `nicolas-cole-client-acquisition`, and product mechanics to `nicolas-cole-digital-products`.

## Output Contract

Produce a decision table, verdict, chosen offer hypothesis, first paid test, proof requirement, delivery boundary, kill condition, and handoff.

## Quality Gate

- Cash deadline materially affects the verdict
- Price is a hypothesis, not a source-derived default
- First version is deliverable by the current operator
- No large audience is assumed
- One paid evidence event is named
- Scalable expansion is parked until proof exists
