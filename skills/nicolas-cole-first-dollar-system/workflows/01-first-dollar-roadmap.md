# First-Dollar Roadmap

Use this as the conductor when the user needs the full source-to-payment path or when the bottleneck is unclear.

## Input Required

- Current knowledge, skills, proof, audience, email list, and offers
- Buyer or problem hypotheses
- Cash deadline and weekly delivery capacity
- Existing replies, calls, deposits, payments, and customer evidence
- Claim, permission, publishing, outreach, and spend boundaries

## Protocol

1. **Name the proof state.** Mark each of audience, demand, offer, delivery, and payment as `VERIFIED`, `LIKELY`, `UNCONFIRMED`, `UNTESTED`, or `NO EVENT`.
2. **Find the current gate.** Use the seven steps as dependencies, not a ritual calendar. Do not prescribe downstream work when an earlier commercial gate is missing.
3. **Run the cash-clock branch.** If meaningful cash is needed inside 30 days, favor a bounded service and hand off to `/first-10k`. If the operator can wait and has distribution or direct demand, evaluate a small digital product or recurring model.
4. **Preserve congruence.** Keep the same named buyer problem from public proof through owned follow-up and paid offer.
5. **Define the smallest live offer.** State buyer, problem, promise, deliverable, price hypothesis, delivery limit, proof burden, and explicit non-promises.
6. **Choose one next evidence event.** Outreach, publishing, pricing, and payment collection require approval. Preparation may continue locally.
7. **Pre-wire the learning loop.** Name what will be asked after the first paid result and how core fixes will be separated from upsells.

## Output Contract

Produce:

1. Current commercial state
2. Chosen route and why
3. Parked routes and why
4. One smallest live offer
5. One next evidence event
6. Proof and permission gates
7. Handoff summary

## Quality Gate

- One current gate, not seven simultaneous projects
- Cash clock explicitly changes or confirms the route
- No audience-building prerequisite when a service can test the problem faster
- No revenue claim without reply, call, deposit, or payment evidence
- One function owner and bounded handoffs
- External action remains approval-gated
