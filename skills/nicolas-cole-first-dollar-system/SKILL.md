# Nicolas Cole First-Dollar System

This is a connected orchestration skill, not a new general business expert. It turns Cole's seven-step roadmap into evidence-gated decisions and hands each craft problem to the existing owner.

## Core Decisions

1. **Start from earned context:** inventory jobs, hobbies, and lived experiences, then separate firsthand context from qualified authority.
2. **Test before committing:** rank up to five candidate topics and compare relative reader or buyer signal against the operator's own baseline.
3. **Keep one problem spine:** public content, owned email, and the paid offer should solve the same class of problem at increasing depth.
4. **Choose by skill fit and cash clock:** urgent cash routes to a bounded service and `/first-10k`; patient scale may route to a digital product or recurring model.
5. **Get to a safe live offer:** no revenue learning exists until a real buyer can say yes, no, or not yet.
6. **Let payment start the loop:** capture discovery source, missing value, confusing parts, desired outcomes, and exact customer language.
7. **Expand only after proof:** improve the core promise first; split a genuinely adjacent problem into an upsell; scale through traffic and LTV only when evidence supports it.

## Non-Negotiables

- Never guarantee that 30 or 90 publishing days will produce revenue.
- Never turn lived experience or proximity into regulated professional authority.
- Never force an urgent-cash operator to build a newsletter before testing a bounded service.
- Never treat followers, views, completed assets, or a live checkout as payment proof.
- Never invent testimonials or request them before the buyer has a real experience.
- Never add new offers merely to avoid improving the first one.
- Publishing, outreach, pricing changes, payment collection, and external actions still require the user's approval.

## Quality Test

A strong run identifies the current commercial gate, chooses one next paid-evidence action, names the exact proof object, preserves claim and permission boundaries, and hands off only the context the next owner needs. It must mark absent replies, calls, deposits, or payments as `NO EVENT` or `UNTESTED`.
