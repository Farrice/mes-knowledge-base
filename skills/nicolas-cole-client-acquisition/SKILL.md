# Nicolas Cole Client Acquisition

Land premium ghostwriting clients ($3,000+/month) starting from zero credentials, zero portfolio, zero testimonials. Built from Nicolas Cole's methodology that scaled Digital Press to 300+ enterprise clients and millions in revenue — all launched from an 8,000-follower Quora profile and an air mattress from Target.

**Core philosophy**: Client acquisition is an education problem, not a sales problem. The writer who articulates the prospect's problem better than the prospect can has already won. Portfolio, testimonials, and case studies are outputs of client work — not prerequisites for it.
