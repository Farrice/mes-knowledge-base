## Pricing Landscape

| Tier | Price Range | What's Included | Market Position |
|------|------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Commodity** | $50–$300/post | Template-based posts, AI-assisted | Race to the bottom — Upwork/Fiverr |
| **Basic Retainer** | $397–$1,000/mo | Outreach + ghostwriting add-ons | Entry agencies (Cleverly) |
| **Mid-Market** | $1,000–$2,500/mo | Voice-matched posts, 4-8/mo | Solo operators with process |
| **Premium** | $2,500–$5,000/mo | Strategy + voice engineering, 8-12/mo | Productized service operators |
| **Enterprise** | $5,000–$7,500+/mo | Full demand-gen, multi-format campaigns | Agencies (Lead Cookie, Sculpt) |

### Key Pricing Insight
The **$350 purchasing decision threshold** (Nicolas Cole's framework) maps perfectly to the market: services below $350/mo are perceived as commodities. Services structured as $2,500+/mo retainers with named methodologies command premium pricing and lower churn.

## AI Impact Assessment

| Service Type | AI Commoditization Level | Premium Defensibility |
|-------------|------------------------|----------------------|
| Template-based posts | **FULLY COMMODITIZED** | None — LinkedIn's own AI does this |
| Hook/format optimization | **HIGH** — AI excels here | Low |
| Basic voice matching | **MODERATE-HIGH** — AI hits ~90-95% | Eroding fast |
| Deep voice engineering | **LOW** — rhythm, compression, word choice | Still defensible (for now) |
| Strategic narrative design | **VERY LOW** | High — requires human judgment |
| Client systemization | **NONE** | Highest — switching costs protect you |

## Market Demand Signals

### Positive Signals
- B2B LinkedIn content demand continues to grow (thought leadership as GTM strategy)
- Agency growth implies institutional demand
- C-suite professionals increasingly need LinkedIn presence but lack time
- Average revenue impact of executive LinkedIn presence: measurable pipeline contribution

### Negative Signals
- Low-end market flooded — forum sentiment labels it "saturated grift"
- Upwork/Fiverr ghostwriting rates compressed by AI-enabled volume operators
- Client churn rates high industry-wide (agencies report frequent cancellations when ROI unclear)
- LinkedIn's native AI tools reducing perceived need for human writers
