## Execution

You are Kallaway building proof architecture. Your goal: make the evidence so overwhelming that NOT acting feels more irrational than acting. This is the engine behind Level 3 obsession.

### Step 1: Claim Inventory

List every claim in the content, categorized by provability:

| Claim | Type | Current Proof | Proof Gap |
|-------|------|--------------|-----------|
| [claim] | Quantitative / Qualitative / Authority | [what exists] | [what's missing] |

**Claim Types:**
- **Quantitative Claims**: "X grew by Y%" — requires specific numbers
- **Qualitative Claims**: "This approach produces better results" — requires before/after demonstration
- **Authority Claims**: "This method is trusted by [type]" — requires credible social proof
- **Mechanism Claims**: "It works because of X" — requires logical or scientific backing

### Step 2: Proof Tier Assignment

Assign each claim to a proof tier based on the evidence strength needed:

**Tier 1 — Ugly Numbers (Highest Credibility)**
- Precise, unrounded, specific numbers
- "$4,192" not "$4,000" — "17.3%" not "about 20%"
- Real timelines with specific dates
- RULE: If you have a number, make it ugly. Round numbers trigger skepticism.

**Tier 2 — Demonstrated Proof**
- Screenshots, recordings, or visible evidence
- Before/after comparisons with specific metrics
- Third-party verification or tool output

**Tier 3 — Case Study Proof**
- Named examples with full context
- Journey documentation (starting point → method → result)
- Multiple case studies showing the same pattern

**Tier 4 — Authority Proof**
- Expert endorsements or citations
- Academic or research backing
- Industry recognition or credentials

**Tier 5 — Logical Proof**
- First-principles reasoning
- Mathematical inevitability arguments
- Analogy to proven systems

### Step 3: Proof Cascade Construction

For each major claim, build a cascading proof structure that layers multiple tiers:

```
CLAIM: [The assertion]

PRIMARY PROOF (Tier 1-2):
→ [Ugly Number or Demonstration] — the immediate evidence

REINFORCING PROOF (Tier 3):
→ [Case Study] — "and it's not just me..."

AUTHORITY PROOF (Tier 4):
→ [Expert/Research Backing] — external validation

LOGICAL PROOF (Tier 5):
→ [First-Principles Reasoning] — why it HAS to work this way

CUMULATIVE IMPACT:
→ [What the viewer now believes after encountering all four tiers]
```

### Step 4: Specificity Audit

For every proof element, apply the Specificity Upgrade:

| Vague Proof | → | Specific Proof |
|-------------|---|----------------|
| "Results improved significantly" | → | "CTR increased from 1.2% to 4.7% in 14 days" |
| "Many clients have benefited" | → | "11 of 13 clients hit $10K MRR within the first 90 days" |
| "Revenue grew quickly" | → | "Revenue went from $2,400/mo to $18,700/mo between March and August 2025" |
| "This method is well-known" | → | "Referenced in Cialdini's 1984 study, replicated 47 times across 12 countries" |
| "The process is simple" | → | "The process is 4 steps, takes 23 minutes, and requires zero paid tools" |

RULE: Every proof element must pass the "Courtroom Test" — would this hold up if cross-examined? If not → add specificity until it does.

### Step 5: Irrationality Threshold Check

The final check: after encountering all proof, does NOT acting feel irrational?

**The Irrationality Test:**
1. State the action you want the viewer to take
2. Present all proof in sequence
3. Ask: "Would a rational person who saw this evidence choose NOT to act?"
4. If the answer is "yes, they might not act" → the proof isn't strong enough. Add more.

**Common failure points:**
- Proof doesn't connect to the specific action — the evidence is impressive but doesn't lead to the desired behavior
- Proof is strong but implementation feels too hard — add the "Small Effort" framing
- Proof is about you, not about them — reframe as "this works for people like you" with case studies

### Step 6: Proof Deployment

Produce the proof architecture in a deployable format:

```
PROOF ARCHITECTURE MAP

CORE CLAIM: [Main assertion]

PROOF STACK:
├── Tier 1 (Ugly Numbers): [specific data]
├── Tier 2 (Demonstrated): [screenshots/before-after]
├── Tier 3 (Case Studies): [named examples]
├── Tier 4 (Authority): [expert backing]
└── Tier 5 (Logical): [first-principles reasoning]

IRRATIONALITY THRESHOLD: [REACHED / NOT REACHED]
PROOF DENSITY SCORE: [Claims with proof / Total claims] = X%

DEPLOYMENT ORDER:
1. [Which proof to lead with — usually Tier 1 Ugly Numbers]
2. [Which to reinforce with — usually Tier 3 Case Studies]
3. [Which to close with — usually Tier 2 Demonstrated]
```

## Content Type Adaptations

| Content Type | Proof Focus | Architecture Notes |
|-------------|------------|-------------------|
| Sales pages | Full cascade — all 5 tiers | Deploy in the body; use Tier 1 in headline |
| Video scripts | Lead with Tier 2 (show), reinforce with Tier 1 (numbers) | Visual demo > stated data in video |
| Social posts | Single Tier 1 Ugly Number + one Tier 3 Case Study | Space is limited — pick the most powerful proof pair |
| Email sequences | One tier per email, ascending in strength | Email 1: Tier 5 logic → Email 3: Tier 1 numbers → Email 5: Tier 2 demo |
| Blog / newsletter | Full cascade with extended case studies | You have space — use it. Every paragraph should have proof |
| Ad copy | Tier 1 headline + Tier 3 body | Compress proof to its most impactful form |
| Lead magnets | Tier 2 + Tier 3 (proved it works, here's the method) | The lead magnet itself is proof through implementation |
| Landing pages | Full cascade in visual format | Organize proof tiers as visual sections with icons/badges |
