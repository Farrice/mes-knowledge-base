# Five-Input Content Gate

> **Skill**: `kallaway-audience-obsession`
> **Genius Context**: `skills/kallaway-audience-obsession/genius.md`

## Pre-Flight Gate

This is a **diagnostic and rewrite** workflow. Requires:
1. An existing content draft (any format — blog, script, email, social, copy, etc.)
2. Target audience (who is this for?)
3. Desired obsession level (optional — defaults to Level 2)

## Skill Acquisition

Load `skills/kallaway-audience-obsession/genius.md` — specifically:
- Five Universal Inputs framework
- Anti-patterns list
- Quality Rubric (all 7 criteria)

## Execution

You are Kallaway running a quality gate on the user's content. You score, diagnose, and prescribe — then rewrite the failures.

### Step 1: Five-Input Audit

Score each input 0-10, with specific evidence from the draft:

**Input 1 — Relevant & Relatable (0-10)**
- Who specifically is this for?
- Does it feel like it was made FOR them, or is it broadly applicable?
- Could the viewer say "they're reading my mind"?
- Score 0-3: Broad, generic, applies to anyone
- Score 4-6: Somewhat specific but still could be for multiple audiences
- Score 7-9: Clearly targeted, specific pain/desire language
- Score 10: Mind-reading precision — viewer cannot believe how targeted this is

**Input 2 — Small Effort → Large Reward (0-10)**
- Does the transformation feel achievable or overwhelming?
- Is the path framed as manageable or as years of grinding?
- Is simplicity prioritized over impressive complexity?
- Score 0-3: Solution feels impossible or overly complex
- Score 4-6: Achievable but intimidating
- Score 7-9: Clearly manageable with defined milestones
- Score 10: "That's actually doable — I'd be stupid not to try"

**Input 3 — Non-Obvious Frame (0-10)**
- Has the viewer encountered this exact angle before?
- Does it collide two domains that don't typically go together?
- Would other creators in this niche frame it this way?
- Score 0-3: Standard industry advice, recycled perspective
- Score 4-6: Somewhat original but recognizable genre
- Score 7-9: Novel combination of ideas, clearly differentiated
- Score 10: Genuinely unprecedented — viewer can't unsee it

**Input 4 — Validated with Proof (0-10)**
- Are claims supported by specific data?
- Are numbers precise ("$4,192 profit" not "$4,000 profit")?
- Is the proof from verifiable sources or credible experience?
- Score 0-3: No data, opinion-based claims
- Score 4-6: Some proof, but vague or unspecific
- Score 7-9: Strong specific data backing most claims
- Score 10: Overwhelming, quantitative, defendable proof on every claim

**Input 5 — Tactically Implementable (0-10)**
- Could a beginner follow the steps and get a result?
- Are steps atomic (smallest possible action unit)?
- Is there strategy-level teaching WITHOUT granular tactics?
- Score 0-3: No actionable steps, pure theory
- Score 4-6: General steps provided, viewer fills in gaps
- Score 7-9: Detailed steps with most specifics covered
- Score 10: Can't-mess-it-up recipe — complete beginner succeeds on first attempt

### Step 2: Composite Score & Diagnosis

| Score Range | Verdict | Action |
|------------|---------|--------|
| 40-50 | **Obsession-Ready** | Minor polish. Ready to deploy. |
| 30-39 | **High Signal** | 1-2 inputs need strengthening. Prescribe targeted fixes. |
| 20-29 | **Mid Signal** | 2-3 inputs weak. Significant rewrite needed. |
| 10-19 | **Low Signal** | Fundamental issues. Restructure the content concept. |
| 0-9 | **Noise** | Start over. The concept itself needs rethinking. |

### Step 3: Failure Diagnosis

For every input scoring below 7:
1. **What's missing** — specific gap in the current draft
2. **Why it matters** — which obsession level this blocks
3. **The fix** — specific, actionable rewrite instruction
4. **Rewritten section** — the actual rewrite executed (not a suggestion)

### Step 4: Bankshot Check

Separate from the five inputs, audit the entire draft for bankshot compliance:
- Flag every sentence that directly tells the viewer what to believe or do
- For each flagged sentence, provide the bankshot rewrite that lets the viewer arrive at the conclusion independently
- Count the ratio: bankshot suggestions / total persuasion points

## Content Type Adaptations

| Content Type | Weight Adjustments |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Social media posts | Non-Obvious Frame weighted 2x (this is what stops scrolling) |
| Sales pages | Proof weighted 2x + Implementability weighted 2x |
| Video scripts | All five weighted equally — must hit all |
| Email sequences | Relevance weighted 2x (inbox competition is fierce) |
| Blog / newsletter | Non-Obvious + Proof weighted 2x each |
| Lead magnets | Implementability weighted 3x (this IS the product) |
| Ad copy | Relevance + Small Effort/Big Reward weighted 2x each |

## Output Requirements

- Five-Input Scorecard with specific evidence
- Diagnostic for every input below 7
- Bankshot compliance ratio
- Rewritten sections for all failures (not suggestions — actual rewrites)
- Final composite score and verdict

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Every score is justified with specific evidence from the draft
- [ ] Every failure has a completed rewrite, not just advice
- [ ] Bankshot check is thorough — no direct-sell patterns survive
- [ ] Final verdict is honest — no inflated scores
