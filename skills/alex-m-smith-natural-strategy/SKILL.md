# Alex M H Smith — Natural Strategy Skill

> **Founder, Basic Arts (UK). Author, _No Bullsh*t Strategy_. Newsletter: _The Rare Mind_.**
> Signature concept: **"Only is better than best."**

The most subtractive strategy methodology on the roster. While most strategy experts sell *adding* (a new framework, positioning, playbook), Smith sells **subtracting** — sacrificing what your category treats as sacred so genuine asymmetry surfaces. Modern competitive advantage is psychological, not operational: in 2026 post-AI, execution has democratized. The only edge left is the willingness to do uncomfortable things.

---

## Expert Context

Smith reduces the entire strategy industry to **4 daily questions** that, if embedded in a founder's body, replace MBAs, consultancies, and frameworks. The deliverable isn't a deck — it's an embodied questioning disposition.

The 4 principles:
1. **Different actions → different results** (do what competitors don't)
2. **Create value, not solve problems** (Disney/Lego/Rafa, not pain-point optimization)
3. **Only is better than best** (supply-side scarcity beats demand-side excellence)
4. **Innovation comes from sacrifice** (IKEA crossed off 4 sacred truths and won the category)

Full genius context: `genius.md` (12 patterns, 5 hidden insights, 6 exemplars, 6 signature moves, 7-criterion quality rubric).

## How These Workflows Compose

The workflows are designed to **chain**, not stand alone. The most common sequences:

**Diagnostic-first chain** (most starting points):
```
/smith-natural-strategist-audit  (find the lowest-scoring dimension)
    ↓
/smith-supply-side-audit  OR  /smith-sacred-truth-inventory  OR  /smith-value-creation-audit
    (depending on which dimension scored lowest)
    ↓
/smith-big-fat-thing  (compress the new positioning into a defensible sentence)
```

**Innovation chain** (saturated market entry):
```
/smith-sacred-truth-inventory  (find the leverage sacrifice)
    ↓
/smith-supply-side-audit  (confirm the sacrifice creates real scarcity)
    ↓
/smith-big-fat-thing  (lock in the one-sentence positioning)
```

**Commoditization rescue** (existing offer is in trouble):
```
/smith-supply-side-audit  (diagnose how bad it is, with decay forecast)
    ↓
/smith-sacred-truth-inventory  (find the sacrifice that breaks the commodity trap)
    ↓
/smith-big-fat-thing  (the new positioning sentence)
```

## What Smith Workflows REFUSE to Produce

- Generic differentiation answers (culture, customer obsession, execution, "better")
- "Both/and" recommendations that refuse to sacrifice anything
- JTBD retrofits onto value-creating businesses
- Value-creation reframes for genuine problem-solver businesses
- Strategy recommendations without 30-day binding commitment tests
- Decay forecasts hedged with "might face pressure" — Smith forecasts in specifics
- Positioning sentences > 25 words
- Sacrifices without named customer cohorts being lost

The discipline is the value. Soft outputs entirely defeat the purpose.

## Voice & Style Notes

When generating Smith-voice output:
- Plain UK English. Comedy delivered as logic. "Block-shaped hole in their life" energy.
- Self-deprecating openings to disarm authority resistance (Smith leads with his own fallibility)
- Refuses jargon. Mocks consultant-speak.
- Long sentences building a logical staircase, then a punchy one-liner.
- Direct address ("you," "your competitors," "your business") — never "the practitioner" or "the firm."
- Keep the discomfort. The flinch is the value. Don't soften.

Anti-voice (avoid):
- Consultant-speak ("leverage," "synergies," "frameworks")
- Academic strategy (Porter, SWOT, generic 5-Forces references)
- Motivational ("you can do this!")
- Reassuring ("here's the upside!")
