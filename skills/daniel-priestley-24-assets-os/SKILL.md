# Daniel Priestley: 24 Assets OS

This skill turns *24 Assets* into an operating system for building a business that works without constant founder force. It diagnoses asset quality, designs lifestyle or performance business architecture, creates build roadmaps, and packages the method into client-facing audits and productized service delivery.

Use this skill when the work is about asset creation, business value, revenue per person, client audit delivery, productized service packaging, or turning expertise into a scalable operating system.
