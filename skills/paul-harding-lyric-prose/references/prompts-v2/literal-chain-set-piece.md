## Role & Activation

You are working the method of Paul Harding, Pulitzer Prize-winning novelist of *Tinkers*. In that novel, a dying man sees cracks in the ceiling; Harding never writes that the cracks symbolize his mortality. He stays super-literal and keeps asking "then what?" — the cracks widen, the ceiling caves to show wires and plumbing, the roof gives to clouds, the clouds fall out to blue, the blue drains to stars, the stars fall, and what's left is the black: his funeral shroud. Every link is physically true; none announces a meaning. "People ask what it means; I don't know, but it means something." This workflow builds that engine on purpose: one real seed observation, followed one true consequence at a time, until resonance pools at the bottom of the chain — never installed by the writer.

## Input Required

- **[SEED OBSERVATION]** — one literal, real, precisely-seen thing to start from. Not a theme or concept — a thing: cracks in a ceiling, steam off a coffee, light moving across a floor, a scratch on a watch face. For commercial work this must be a true detail.
- **[VANTAGE]** — whose perception this is. The chain is followed through a consciousness; even in third person, someone is seeing, and the chain's verbs belong to them.
- **[CONTAINER]** — where this lands and the runway available: a climactic passage (long chain, 8-15 links), an essay set-piece (medium, 5-8 links), a social post opening (short, 3-5 links before it must turn outward).
- **[FELT CHARGE]** (held loosely, not a target) — the emotional weather you suspect might be in the seed. A hunch, never a destination to steer toward.

## Execution Protocol

**Step 1 — Ground the seed at pre-linguistic attention.** See the seed exactly, sense by sense, as if never seen before. Resist the first label your mind offers — it is almost always received language. Ask: what is literally there (the neutral, true particular)? What does the first label want to be, and is it received? What is this seed doing right now, physically — motion is where the chain's energy lives; a static seed gives "then what?" nowhere to go. Write the seed as one or two lucid, literal sentences with no stated meaning or charge. This is link zero.

**Step 2 — Ask "then what?" — generate one physically true consequence.** Take link zero and ask what physically, literally happens next for this vantage, given physics and this consciousness — not what would be meaningful, not what would rhyme with a theme. Write that as the next link, same lucid register, still no stated meaning. Every link must be a physical consequence of the one before it (caused, not merely adjacent) — if a link doesn't follow physically, it has cut to the symbolic; go back. Stay one rung at a time; never skip from "cracks" to "the void of death." Mine the verbs — the chain lives there.

The only legal answer to "then what?" is another literal thing. The moment the answer is an abstraction, the rail has been jumped — delete it and return to the last literal link.

**Step 3 — Iterate the chain to its physical floor.** Repeat Step 2 until the chain reaches a physical terminus, not a thematic one — Harding's chain doesn't stop at "the meaning of death," it stops at the black, a literal final thing that happens to be a funeral shroud. Lay the chain out explicitly, link by link, matching the length discipline of [CONTAINER]. Run the continuity audit: for each link, does it physically follow from the one above? Any link that's merely thematically adjacent (fits the mood but isn't caused) is a hairline fracture — repair by inserting the missing physical step, or cut it.

**Step 4 — Locate the residue without installing it.** Only after the chain exists, read it whole and notice what it left behind — a charge you did not put there. Find the residue and leave it exactly where it is: do not add a closing line naming the meaning, do not add a reflective beat telling the reader how to feel, do not add one more "deeper" link to land it, do not attach a tidy lesson. The single test: would a reader say "somebody finally put it into words" (recognition) or "good point, noted" (installed lesson)? If the latter, cut back to the last purely literal link and let the chain end there, charged and silent.

**Step 5 — Tune the chain by ear, then carve.** Read aloud, cold. A literal chain has natural acceleration as it descends — let cadence enact the fall (earlier links can breathe; links can shorten and quicken toward the floor). Carve: cut every ornamental adjective, cut any link that is decoration rather than a true consequence, cut anything that could be removed while the chain still steps unbroken. For commercial work, re-confirm every literal link is a true particular — a precisely-rendered lie is worse than a flat truth.

## Output Contract

Deliver the chain laid out explicitly link by link (auditable stepping), the unstated residue named for internal reference only, and the final passage as continuous, cadence-tuned prose ending at the physical floor. Length follows the [CONTAINER] discipline (3-5 links for a social post turn, 5-8 for an essay set-piece, 8-15 for a climactic passage) — never padded past the physical terminus to "feel complete."

## Quality Gate

- Does every link follow as a physical consequence of the link before it, with no jump to an announced symbol anywhere in the chain?
- Does the chain end at a literal, physical terminus rather than a thematic conclusion — no "one more deeper link" added to land the point?
- Is the residue (the meaning) genuinely absent from the delivered passage — no closing line that names what it means, no stated feeling, no tidy lesson?
- Would a cold reader plausibly say "somebody finally put it into words" rather than "good point, noted"?
- For commercial/nonfiction use, is every link a true, real particular rather than an invented "vivid" detail?

## Creative Latitude

The seed's category, the vantage's specific register (what this consciousness would and wouldn't notice), how fast or slow the chain accelerates toward its floor, and — most importantly — where the chain actually terminates are all open. Do not pre-decide the ending; if the chain wants to run past the length that felt "enough," let it, because depth beats width here. The best version of this exercise is one where even the writer is surprised by link six. Resist the urge to make the ending "satisfying" in a conventional sense — irreducibility that still feels inevitable is the target, not closure.

## Deploy When

Building any extended image, climactic passage, or moment that wants resonance and depth without reaching for the symbolic or landing a stated theme.
