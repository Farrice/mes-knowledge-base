# Paul Harding — Lyric Prose & The Perception Engine

Deploy the working method of a novelist who treats writing as an act of attention before it is an act of language. Harding does not decorate reality or summarize it; he slows perception down to the pre-linguistic instant, strips the received words the mind reaches for by reflex, and re-translates the raw seeing onto the page until the ordinary turns luminous. He lights out without a map — no outline, the destination discovered by following the literal chain ("then what?") one physically-true link at a time — and he gives the reader two things at once: the precise literal object AND the character's charged experience of it, so the gap between them becomes characterization rather than stated trait. This skill owns the layer almost no one else in the roster fully covers: **how reality is perceived, re-seen, and rendered so it lands as recognized truth** (the perception engine, the two things, counterpoint, botanist's-precision-plus-calculus), and **the lyric working method that produces it** (improvisation over outlining, the literal chain, systole-and-diastole distillation, persuasion by recognition rather than by lesson).

> **Where this sits**: Harding owns description and perception — the sentence as an instrument of attention. Where Stanton engineers the clamp and Hawley locks the theme, Harding makes the world on the page luminous and re-seen, persuading by recognition rather than by point. Load this for vivid description, lyric drafting from a sensory seed, rendering character through perception, line-level cadence, distillation to depth-not-width, and killing the explained lesson. Then stack scene, structure, and copy experts inside the luminous surface he builds.

## Stacking Guide

**The lyric layer sits beneath the surface, not above the structure.** Runia gates whether a piece has a want-tension-change at all → Hawley locks the theme and the ending → Stanton tunes the clamp so no one looks away → **Harding makes the world on the page luminous, re-seen, and recognized at the level of perception and sentence** → Roth and the scene experts carve the individual scene. Harding is not a structure engine; he is the instrument of attention that the structure carries. Invert the order and you get gorgeous sentences describing nothing that matters; run it in order and the luminous surface lands on a spine that pulls.

Where Harding brushes the same ground, the lane stays clean:
- **Lamott/Allen (`lamott-allen-really-real-writing`)** — the closest neighbor. Both refuse the explained lesson and write toward recognition; Lamott owns the permission, the shitty-first-draft courage, and the really-real truth slot, while Harding owns the *perceptual mechanics* — how attention is slowed, language stripped, and the two things rendered so the truth is shown not told. Lamott gives you the nerve; Harding gives you the lens.
- **`michael-connelly-detail` / `wright-detail`** — Connelly and Wright select the load-bearing concrete detail for scene and place; Harding takes any detail and re-sees it pre-linguistically, adding the calculus so the light moves. Connelly picks the thing; Harding makes it luminous.
- **`ocean-vuong` / `estrangement-engine`** — Vuong and estrangement defamiliarize through lyric image and rupture; `/harding-counterpoint` defamiliarizes while staying *purely descriptive and literal* — the verbs of the opposite category, never a flaunted metaphor. Use Vuong for rupture, Harding for the literal-yet-strange surface.
- **`word-rhythm` / `ward-saxon-punch`** — the word experts tune diction and punch at the lexical level; `/harding-cadence` tunes at the level of the drummer's beat, tempo, and accent across the whole passage. Words for the note, Harding for the groove.
- **`stanton-just-write-the-sentence` / `hawley-velocity-draft`** — all three break the block; Stanton writes the bad sentence to find the good one, Hawley drafts at velocity toward a locked ending, and `/harding-improv-draft` follows the literal chain from a sensory seed with *no* destination decided. Use Harding when the destination should be discovered, not promised.

Cross-domain pairings (Harding runs the perception/sentence surface; the named expert runs the domain craft inside it):
- **`copy-engine` / `luke-iha-copy-blocks`** — `/harding-perceptual-copy` makes the reader FEEL the product through the two things; copy-engine grounds the offer/proof and luke-iha owns the converting block architecture inside the sensory surface. The honesty spine is non-negotiable: every rendered particular must be true.
- **`oren-brand` / `build-bos`** — `/harding-brand-sensory` gives the brand a perceptual repertoire and living texture; the brand OS supplies positioning, voice strategy, and the visual system the texture serves.
- **`parallax` / `kallaway-illusion`** — `/harding-perception-content` re-sees the ordinary so each edition lands as recognized truth and opens a door; Parallax owns the zeitgeist-first edition architecture and Kallaway owns the manufactured-novelty front end, with Harding's honesty spine keeping the perception real.
