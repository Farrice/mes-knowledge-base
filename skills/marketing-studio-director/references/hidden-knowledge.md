# Marketing Studio Director - Hidden Knowledge

## Platform Bias
Marketing Studio works best when one location, one product focus, and one preset grammar are clear. Product placement needs to be stated in-frame, not implied.

## Failure Mode
Reference drift is the main risk. If a product or avatar image is provided, fidelity beats style upgrades, extra claims, and aesthetic embellishment.

## Operating Rule
Write for what the model can see or hear. Avoid invisible sensory claims, off-screen transformations, and mechanical over-description.
