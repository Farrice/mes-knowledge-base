# Marketing Studio Director - Genius Patterns

## 1. Preset Is the Grammar
**Execute**: Identify or honor the Marketing Studio preset before writing. Let the preset determine camera behavior, pacing, environment, and register.
**Deploy When**: Every Higgsfield Marketing Studio prompt.
**Success Metric**: UGC feels phone-native, TV Spot feels composed, Hyper Motion is kinetic, and review/tutorial/unboxing formats stay distinct.

## 2. Fidelity Before Flourish
**Execute**: When product or avatar images are attached, preserve exact product packaging, color, logo placement, proportions, avatar face/build, and user-specified wardrobe.
**Deploy When**: Any prompt with product or avatar references.
**Success Metric**: The prompt never restyles reference assets or invents unsupported brand claims.

## 3. Visible Action Only
**Execute**: Describe what can be seen or heard. Use outcome-based action instead of biomechanics, and show state changes on camera.
**Deploy When**: Product demos, tutorials, unboxings, reviews, try-ons.
**Success Metric**: The prompt avoids invisible sensory claims and unsupported off-screen transformations.

## 4. Single-Paragraph Production Prose
**Execute**: Return one flowing paragraph with camera, action, environment, product placement, mood, and audio woven together, followed by the generation link.
**Deploy When**: Every Marketing Studio final output.
**Success Metric**: No labels, shot headers, markdown, JSON, or commentary leak into the final response.

## 5. Age-Blind Avatar Description
**Execute**: Describe avatars by appearance, wardrobe, and delivery style, never by age category.
**Deploy When**: Any prompt involving a presenter, creator, model, or wearer.
**Success Metric**: The prompt contains no age markers and still gives enough visual direction.
