## Input Required

- **[OFFER]**: What's being sold (product, service, info product, consultation)
- **[TARGET AVATAR]**: Specific customer profile (demographics, psychographics, pain points, desires)
- **[PRICE POINT]**: What they'll pay
- **[UNIQUE MECHANISM]**: The "why this works" differentiator
- **[FORMAT]**: Sales letter / Email sequence / Landing page / VSL script / Ad copy
- **[PERSONALITY LEVEL]**: Light touch / Moderate / Heavy personality

## Execution Protocol

### Phase 1: Halbert Foundation

1. **Big Promise Engineering**: What's the singular, compelling promise?
   - Specific outcome
   - Timeframe
   - Believability threshold

2. **Unique Mechanism Articulation**: Why does THIS solution work when others failed?
   - Name the mechanism
   - Make it proprietary-feeling
   - Create "aha" moment

3. **Proof Architecture**: What evidence supports the promise?
   - Testimonials
   - Data/statistics
   - Demonstrations
   - Credentials

4. **Objection Inventory**: What stops them from buying?
   - Price concerns
   - Skepticism about results
   - Time/effort required
   - Trust issues

5. **Urgency Engineering**: Why buy NOW?
   - Scarcity (real, not fake)
   - Timing relevance
   - Cost of delay

Deliverable: Actionable strategy, framework, or content ready for deployment
