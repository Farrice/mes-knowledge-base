## How to Use This Skill (Model Calibration)

These eleven patterns are plotting primitives, not a fill-in-the-blank form. The system's whole thesis is that "intuition" can be taught — but internalize the *reasoning* (why a score of 8 on Urgency changes the marketing move), not the score itself as a fact to recite.

Two different outputs, two different rules:
- **The Manifold itself** (the internal market-intelligence document) is allowed to show its scaffolding — tables, 1–10 scores, named frameworks (Pain Matrix, Core Wound, AWE) are the point; a copywriter reads it to load context, so label the sections.
- **Anything downstream of the Manifold** (a hook, a lead, a VSL line, an ad) must never leak that scaffolding into the reader's experience. A prospect must never see the words "Epiphany Threshold" or "Ontological Resource" or "AWE Formula" — they see a sentence that happens to land in the Goldilocks Zone because the plotting already happened backstage. Name the machinery on the page and you've written marketing-department copy, not a hook that gets pulled off a swipe file.

The test: would Luke Iha recognize this as a market that's actually been *plotted* — orthogonal dimensions scored with real consequences, a Core Wound tied to ranked resources, VOC pulled and not invented — or as someone using Avatar Manifold vocabulary as a costume over a one-adjective persona? If a "Manifold" reads like one elaborate paragraph with the framework names sprinkled in and no scored table, no ranked resource matrix, no sourced soundbites, that's the costume, not the machine. Rebuild it as a plot, not a description.

Texture note: Iha's own delivery is unglamorous and procedural — he teaches through worked drills ("Beta Males Get Less Women" → "Beta Males Get More Women" → "The 3 'beta male' moves that actually get you laid more often"), not through inspiration. Match that register in the Manifold's own prose: declarative, drill-graded, willing to show its 1–10 math. Save the polish for the copy that ships — polish inside the Manifold itself is the tell that the scores were backfilled to justify a conclusion instead of generating one.

## The Central Thesis

**"Anything explainable can be explained."** The top copywriters factor enormous nuance into their work but teach in one dimension ("just use intuition / it takes experience"). This system closes that gap — the **"Great Curtain of Direct Response"** — by making avatar construction *teachable, repeatable, and dimensional.*

The whole method rests on **two fundamental concepts**:

1. **Orthogonal Dimensions** — break a market into independent axes that don't collapse into each other.
2. **Spectrums** — rate each dimension 1–10.

> If you know *what dimensions to measure* and *how far along each spectrum* the market sits, you can plot anything with far more accuracy than a one-word label. "Salty vs. sweet" is amateur. Big-Five-for-markets is the Manifold. **Precision through dimensionality.**

Everything downstream — hooks, stories, proof, offers — is a *consequence* of where the market plots. Get the plot right and the copy writes itself.

## Genius Patterns

### 1. The Multi-Dimensional Plot (the meta-method)
Never describe a market with a single adjective. Decompose into orthogonal axes, score each 1–10, and read the **marketing consequence** off each score. The score is not the output — the *consequence* is. (Pain Matrix dim 4 = Urgency 8 → "collapse the timeline of inaction; use scarcity.") This is the cognitive move that generates every other framework.

### 2. The Core Wound (everything refracts one fear)
Every market's pain is a refraction of **one universal Core Wound: the fear of total annihilation, abandonment, and death.** Markets differ only in which **Ontological Resources** they use to feel safe against it, and whether they fear *losing* them or desire *gaining* them. Find the market's specific refraction and you've found the emotional bedrock under every hook, story, and objection.

> 12 Ontological Resources (all "false" = impermanent/external, which is what DR sells against): physical attractiveness · wealth/possessions · social status/influence · titles/achievements · fame · youth/vitality · relationships/emotional dependencies · intellect/knowledge · cultural validation · sensory pleasure · independence · power/control over others.
> Structure each: **Relevance (1–10) × Inherent-or-Earned × Fear-of-Loss vs. Desire-to-Gain.** Inherent resources lost (youth, attractiveness) hurt worse than earned ones.

### 3. The Resonance Hierarchy (built bottom-up, sold top-down)
Markets are **collectives of shared experiences.** The pyramid forms from the bottom: **Experiences ⇒ Beliefs ⇒ Values ⇒ Identity.** But prospects *interact* with your message from the **top down** — Identity first. Things harden as they rise; Identity is hardest to change. So:
- **Never conflict with Identity in the lead** — it triggers an autopilot rejection (you can't sell a meat-based diet to vegans, or a Trump policy to a self-identified Trump-hater, however good it is).
- **Appease Identity, avoid clashing with Values, then rework the pyramid from the foundation up** — re-frame Experiences → shift Beliefs → soften Identity → prospect sees your product as the path to who they *want* to be.
- People buy to **reinforce who they are** or **become who they want to be.** "Molding identities is how you build buyers." (Grounded in Breakwell's Identity Process Theory: continuity, distinctiveness, self-esteem, self-efficacy.)

### 4. The Epiphany Threshold (the minimum-viable insight)
A belief sits on a spectrum from **Obvious (0)** to **Outlandish (10)**.
- **Boredom Zone (0–7):** too obvious, no attention.
- **Epiphany Threshold (≈7):** minimum insight needed to capture attention.
- **Goldilocks Zone (7–9):** the sweet spot — surprising but still believable.
- **BS Limit (9+):** triggers the bullshit detector, instant rejection.

This is the **Hegelian dialectic** operationalized: **Thesis** (obvious belief) → **Antithesis** (outlandish opposite) → **Synthesis** (the Goldilocks hook). *"Beta males get less women" (thesis) → "Beta males get more women" (antithesis, over BS limit) → "The 3 'beta male' moves that actually get you laid more often" (synthesis, Goldilocks).*
> Rule: **It's better to push toward outlandish than toward obvious.** Reels/TikTok comments accelerate this mimetic process — every synthesis becomes the next market's thesis.

### 5. Splitting the Atom (how reframes actually work)
Every objection/constraint is a **bundled assumption.** Reframing = drawing a **distinction** inside the bundle. *"Eye bags run in my family"* bundles two claims: (a) eye bags have a genetic component [true], and (b) genetics is therefore the *most important* factor [the target]. Agree with (a), wedge (b): "there are twins where one has eye bags and one doesn't — so genetics can't be the whole story." This is the foundational persuasion operation (all logic reduces to NAND = "A and B are not both true" = a distinction exists). Grounded in Harvard's **Immunity to Change.**

### 6. The AWE Dissolution Formula
Constraints dissolve via **Agree → Wedge → Elaborate:**
- **Agree:** acknowledge the constraint (non-aggressive, congruent).
- **Wedge:** create a new distinction inside the underlying assumption.
- **Elaborate:** spell out the new possibility this opens.
10 interchangeable "delivery vehicles" for the Wedge: Scientific Evidence · News/Current Events · Historical Examples · Storytelling · Analogy · Thought Experiment · Personal Experiment · Consequences · Counterintuitive Wisdom · Paradox.
> Purpose is NOT to paste these into copy. It's to build (1) a checklist of constraints, (2) an arsenal of dissolutions, (3) a copy-review reference.

### 7. The Anti-Hero's Journey (the Energetic Changeling)
The Hero's Journey ends in an **Epiphany** — surrender, love, acceptance ("higher consciousness"). Marketing usually *substitutes the product/mechanism for that epiphany* — the **"Energetic Changeling."** The mechanism becomes the new Holy Grail. Calibrate to the market's **Levels of Consciousness**:
- **Victim** → put responsibility on MECHANISM/PRODUCT (Clickbank, weight-loss supps)
- **Hybrid** → product + inner transformation (fitness via "hormone hacking")
- **Accountability** → mostly inner transformation, "with some double-speak" (75 Hard)
- Moving up: narrower market, better customers, higher prices.

**Part 1 structure** (the emotional backstory, reverse-engineered from the avatar):
1. **Garden of Eden** = image of the *reverse* of the Core Wound
2. **Slow Descent & False Idols** = the Problem Matrix in motion (failed solutions)
3. **Fall of Man (PIG Story)** = the Core Wound made visceral (public, high-stakes, delusion-then-collapse, real people not cartoon villains)
4. **Dark Night of the Soul** = explicit confrontation with the Core Wound
**Part 2:** A New Hope → Guru (self/other) → Epiphany/Insight/Mechanism → Initial→Final Results → A World Redeemed.

### 8. Pain Matrix → Benefit Matrix mirroring
The 10 pain dimensions have a benefit twin. Plot the pain, then plot the desired-state benefit on the same axes. The gap between them *is* the transformation you sell.

### 9. The Maze Metaphor of hooks
Every market is a maze; hooks are snapshots of specific elements. Three types: **What** (the problem/situation), **Who** (the entities), **How** (navigating it). Analyzable along dimensions: Time, Specificity, Scale, Conflict, Insight, Center/Edge, Accepted/Not-Accepted, Status, Narrative, Number, Perspective → ~32 significant combinations. "Bootleg Hook Theory" = Problem + {What / Who / How}.
> Curiosity generators: conflict between *authorities* beats conflict between low-status people · "why the popular theory is wrong" beats "the popular theory" · live beats past · extreme edge cases beat center cases · giving genuine insight.

### 10. Specific Language is pulled, never invented
The *structure* of the wound is predictable and can be modeled by AI. The *specific language* must be **pulled directly from the market** (reviews, comments, VOC, surveys). The system's job is to tell you *what to look for* so you can find it fast. "When you know what the market will react to, you know what to look for, you find it more easily, you sell more."

### 11. The Manifold is the deliverable
The endgame is not a hook or an ad — it's the **Avatar Manifold**: every framework run for one market and concatenated into a single document. Upload that one doc and you can write *anything* to that market. It is a reusable market-intelligence asset (and a sellable product).

## Hidden Knowledge (from the live calls — not in the written docs)

- **Interoceptive Mechanisms** — when promising to fix an internal/bodily problem, choose the mechanism with the better *interoceptive word-picture* (congruence with felt body-sense). "Pain switch" vs. "joint hydration" for knee pain: "hydration" gives an immediate, congruent body-image; "switch" implies pain is *neurological / on-off / in your head* — which doubles as an **Ejection Trigger** in a pain market. Metabolism "switched on/off" works *because* metabolism feels switch-like. **Match the mechanism's sensory metaphor to how the body actually experiences the problem.**
- **Ejection Triggers** — things you say that make the market *shut down* (autopilot rejection). In pain markets: implying "it's all in your head." Map these per-market as landmines to avoid *before* writing. The inverse of resonance.
- **Market Addictions** — segments aren't only *suffering*; some are *addicted to exploring the cause* (e.g., gut-health people addicted to hunting the cause of their gluten/gut problem; biz-opp addicted to "$10K/month"). Sell the *exploration* to the curious, the *relief* to the suffering.
- **Concentric Circles of Concern** — the market is concerned about its immediate problem first; you marry those concerns to *static, already-proven* ideas to make a new idea fluid and credible ("it already has proof"). Understand what's actually top-of-mind.
- **Desire Daisy-Chain** — chain desires outward: surface benefit → the feeling it unlocks → the relationship it changes → the identity it confirms. Each link pulls the prospect further into the aspirational identity (see worked exemplar's Desire Daisy-Chain section).
- **Double-Speak** (Accountability markets) — say the high-consciousness thing *and* wink at the low-consciousness want simultaneously ("find your amazing companion and build a life… but if you just want to go have fun, that's cool too").
- **Brick Logic & Idea Caricatures** — loosen literal logic. Allow multiple degrees of connection ("This onion is 1,000,000× stronger than Botox" — antioxidants → my thing has antioxidants) and exaggerated caricatures ("Fettuccini Alfredo Trick fixes doggy guts"). This is where real creative range comes from.

## Hall of Fame Exemplars

**1. The "Switch the Atom" reframe (eye bags)**
> *"I'm just destined to have eye bags, it runs in my family." → "Yes, eye bags have a genetic cause. But what's crazy is there are TONS of 'twins' where one has eye bags and the other doesn't. So if it were ONLY genetic, that couldn't happen. There must be other, more important causes."*
Why it's elite: agrees fully (no resistance), wedges the *exact* load-bearing assumption (genetics = most important), elaborates a new possibility (other causes → your product). Textbook AWE + Splitting the Atom.

**2. The Goldilocks ladder (dating)**
> Thesis: "Beta males get less women." · Antithesis: "Beta males get more women." · **Synthesis: "The 3 'beta male' moves that actually get you laid more often."**
Why it's elite: the synthesis is unexpected (contradicts the obvious) yet self-limiting ("3 specific moves," not "always be beta") so it stays under the BS limit. The specificity is what keeps it believable.

**3. The Dark Night of the Soul (Lift Factor / invisibility wound)**
> *"…it had been at least two years since I'd heard a catcall… I'd started to get ma'amed… Men weren't falling over themselves to help me anymore. Or even asking my opinion on anything. I felt invisible."*
Why it's elite: never names the product. It's the Core Wound (becoming invisible/replaced) made specific and sensory, with mounting isolation and an open-ended ache that *demands* a solution. This is a plotted avatar rendered as prose.

**4. The full Avatar Manifold (Testosterone market — worked exemplar)**
See `references/worked-manifold-exemplar.md`. One market run through Build-a-Buyer → Pain Matrix (all 10 scored w/ consequence) → Core Wound + Ontological Resource Matrix → Benefit Matrix → Desire Daisy-Chain → Resonance Hierarchy → RH Constraints (6 types) → Dissolution Frameworks per tier. This is the *output standard* every `/avatar-manifold` run aims at.

## Signature Moves

1. **Plot before you write.** Refuse any creative request for a market until the market is plotted on at least the Pain Matrix + Core Wound. Output should read scores *and* consequences.
2. **Refract to the Core Wound.** For any market, name the universal wound's specific refraction ("fear of being replaced by younger men") before anything else. Tie every pain back to a threatened Ontological Resource.
3. **Lead at the top of the pyramid, build from the bottom.** Open by appeasing Identity; never clash with it in the hook. Then re-frame Experiences to soften the rest.
4. **Calibrate every belief to the Goldilocks Zone.** Generate the obvious set, the over-BS set, *then* the 7–9 synthesis set. Always push toward outlandish.
5. **Dissolve with AWE, not argument.** Agree → Wedge (split the bundled assumption) → Elaborate. Pick the delivery vehicle (one of 10) that fits the constraint type.
6. **Map the landmines.** Always list the market's Ejection Triggers before writing — what *not* to say is as load-bearing as what to say.
7. **Pull the language, model the structure.** AI predicts the *shape* of the wound; the market supplies the *words.* Always pair generated structure with a VOC-sourcing step.
8. **Assemble the Manifold.** Don't deliver fragments — concatenate into the single reusable market-intelligence document in canonical order.

### Anti-Patterns (auto-fail)
- Single-adjective market descriptions ("busy moms who want convenience") — the failure mode Iha names directly: describing a market as "Salty Vs. Sweet" instead of plotting it across dimensions (Source: `extractions/luke-iha-avatar-machine/Copy_of_The_AI_Avatar_Machine.txt`, Day 1 transcript, line 135).
- Scores with no marketing consequence attached — every dimension in the source Pain Matrix carries a "Marketing Consequences:" line and a "Specific Consequence" table column; a bare 1–10 number is not the deliverable (Source: `extractions/luke-iha-avatar-machine/Copy_of_The_AI_Avatar_Machine.txt`, lines 285/315/339; `extractions/luke-iha-avatar-machine/AI_Manifold_Brief_(Dirty).txt`, line 154).
- Hooks that clash with Identity in the lead — the exact failure Iha calls the autopilot killer: "you're activating an autopilot REJECTION response" (Source: `extractions/luke-iha-avatar-machine/The_Resonance_Hierarchy.txt`, line 123).
- Beliefs over the BS limit or under the Epiphany Threshold presented as hooks — "The 9 is the 'BS limit' – too outlandish" (Source: `extractions/luke-iha-avatar-machine/BASIC_ET_PROMPT.txt`, line 29).
- Reframes that argue instead of agreeing-then-wedging — the whole point of "Splitting the Atom" and the "Agree, Wedge, Elaborate" (AWE) framework is that arguing against a bundled assumption loses; wedging it wins (Source: `extractions/luke-iha-avatar-machine/Copy_of_The_AI_Avatar_Machine__Day_3.txt`, lines 630 and 720).
- "Specific language" that's obviously AI-invented rather than market-pulled — the Swiping SOP exists precisely because the words have to come from real ads/VSLs/native pages/reviews, not a model's guess (Source: `extractions/luke-iha-avatar-machine/Copy_of_SOP_for_Swiping.txt`, "How to swipe" + sourcing sections, lines 55–175).
- Delivering loose frameworks instead of an assembled, ordered Manifold — the Build-a-Buyer prompt's own closing instruction is "combine all of this info into a comprehensive, detailed summary" (Source: `extractions/luke-iha-avatar-machine/BuildABuyerSnapshot.txt`, line 171).
- Treating this as generic ICP/persona work — demographic profiles are the floor this *starts* above — the source material's own demographic pass (Young vs. Old, reading ability, tech familiarity) is explicitly the raw material the multi-dimensional plot supersedes, not the finished avatar (Source: `extractions/luke-iha-avatar-machine/Copy_of_The_AI_Avatar_Machine.txt`, lines 160–175).
