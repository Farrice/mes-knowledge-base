# Suffering-Archetype Typer

Types a market into one of the 9 Archetypes of Suffering — a fast read on tone, approach, and how much accountability the market can hold. Derived from positions across stigma, control, urgency, measurability, emotional intensity, and causal clarity.

## Pre-Flight Gate
- Market named; a Pain Matrix sharpens the read (the archetypes are emergent from dimension positions).
- The archetype is a *lens*, not a cage — markets can straddle two. Pick the dominant, note the secondary.

## PHASE 0 — GROUND (none — pure derivation)
Per `references/research-spine.md`. This stage types the market from the *already-grounded* Pain Matrix dimension positions — it fires NO new research. If no Pain Matrix exists yet, run `/pain-matrix` (which grounds) first; the archetype read is reasoning over real scores, not a fresh data pull.

## Skill Acquisition
Load `references/framework-library.md` § B (the 9 archetypes). Cross-reference § A if you have the Pain Matrix.

## Execution
1. **Read the dominant archetype** from the market's pain profile:
   Shadow (hidden/high-stigma) · Warrior (control + causal understanding) · Guardian (protecting others, urgency, measurable) · Phoenix (intense + stigma, rising) · Explorer (complex/unclear/low-visibility) · Sisyphus (recurring, can't sustain) · Olympian (measurable goal, high control) · Alchemist (charged + stigma, self-mastery) · Seeker (unclear cause, no direction).
2. **Justify** with 2–3 dimension reads (e.g., "Stigma 8 + Emotional Intensity 9 + rising → Phoenix").
3. **Note the secondary** archetype if the market straddles.
4. **Translate to directives** — tone, empathy level, proof strategy, locus-of-control handling, and what to avoid for that archetype.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Archetype | Tone directive |
|---|---|
| Shadow | Maximum confidentiality + non-judgment; "you're not alone" |
| Warrior / Olympian | Empower, give tools, measurable milestones |
| Guardian | Reciprocity + urgency for the protected one |
| Phoenix / Alchemist | Honor the transformation; dignity over shame |
| Explorer / Seeker | Simplify the cause; offer a map/diagnostic |
| Sisyphus | Remove the "willpower" frame; start-small mechanics |

## Output Requirements
- Dominant archetype + justification + secondary + a 4–5 line tone/approach/proof directive.

## Quality Gate
Archetype is justified by specific dimension reads, not vibes; directives are actionable. Auto-fail: archetype with no justification; ignoring an obvious straddle.
