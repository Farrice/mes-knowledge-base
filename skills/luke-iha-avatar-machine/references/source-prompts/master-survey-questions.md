**Master Survey Questions**  
**(AI Avatar Manifold)**

PROBLEM QUESTIONS:

1. How would you describe the main problem or challenge you're facing related to this issue?  
2. To what extent do you feel this problem is caused by factors within your control?   
3. How often do you experience this problem? (Frequency →)  
4. How comfortable are you discussing this problem with others? (1-10)  
5. How would you describe this problem to a close friend or family member?  
6. What specific aspects of your life have been most affected by \[problem/challenge\]?  
7. How has \[problem/challenge\] impacted your self-confidence and self-image?  
8. Besides yourself, who is most directly affected by this problem?  
9. What does a typical day look like for you while dealing with \[problem/challenge\]? How does this compare to your ideal day?  
10. What does a typical week look like for you while managing \[problem/challenge\]? How does this compare to your ideal week?  
11. What is your biggest fear or concern related to \[issue\] if it doesn’t get better?

SOLUTION QUESTIONS:

12. If you could wave a magic wand and solve this problem, what would your life look like?  
13. What are the top 3 other benefits you hope to gain by resolving this issue?  
14. What would your closest loved ones say if you were able to successfully overcome \[problem/challenge\]?  
15. What would it mean for your sense of identity to successfully overcome \[problem/challenge\]?

PAST EXPERIENCE QUESTIONS:

16. What solutions or approaches have you tried in the past to solve \[problem/challenge\]? What were the outcomes?  
17. What did you like or dislike about the previous solutions you've tried for \[problem/challenge\]?  
18. How long have you been actively trying to solve \[problem/challenge\]? How has your approach evolved over time?  
19. What lessons have you learned from your past attempts at solving \[problem/challenge\]? What would you do differently now?

BELIEF QUESTIONS:

20. How knowledgeable do you feel about the underlying causes or factors contributing to \[problem/challenge\]?  
21. What beliefs do you have about the ideal solution for this issue? (Beliefs)

CONSTRAINT QUESTIONS:

22. What has prevented you from solving this problem so far? (Constraints)  
23. What resources or support do you feel you need to effectively address this issue? (Constraints)  
24. What do you feel is the missing piece or key to finally achieving a lasting solution to \[problem/challenge\]?  
25. What do you believe are the unique challenges of addressing \[problem/challenge\] at this stage of your life?  
26. How much time and energy are you realistically able to invest each week into solving \[problem/challenge\]?
