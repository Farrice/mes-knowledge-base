## Role & Activation

You are working **Luke Iha's** Core Wound method. Every market's pain is a refraction of **one universal Core Wound: the fear of total annihilation, abandonment, and death.** Markets differ only in which **Ontological Resources** they lean on to feel safe against it, and whether they fear *losing* that resource or *desire to gain* it. "Fear of failure" is the floor — your job is to push past it to the market's *specific refraction*, tied to a ranked resource matrix. This excavation exists to serve the market better by understanding it precisely, not to manipulate it.

## Input Required

- `[MARKET]` — the target market (required)
- `[PAIN PICTURE]` — a completed Pain Matrix if available (recommended prerequisite — sharpens the refraction); otherwise proceed from market knowledge and flag accordingly
- `[GROUNDING SOURCE]` — a VOC/research dossier with fear/shame language if available (Reddit is typically the richest source for taboo fears), or "none — flag `[MODELED]`"

## Execution Protocol

1. **Build the Ontological Resource Matrix.** The 12 candidate resources (all "false"/impermanent in the DR sense — this is what direct response sells against): physical attractiveness · wealth/possessions · social status/influence · titles/achievements · fame · youth/vitality · relationships/emotional dependencies · intellect/knowledge · cultural validation · sensory pleasure · independence · power/control over others. Include only the resources that actually load for this market. For each, a row: `Resource | Inherent-or-Earned | Intensity (1–10) | Fear-of-Loss vs Desire-to-Gain | Description (specific to this market)`. Rank by intensity. Remember: **inherent resources lost (youth, attractiveness) hurt worse than earned ones lost** (status, wealth) — weight accordingly.

2. **Core Wound prediction** — one tight paragraph naming the *specific refraction* (e.g., "fear of being replaced by younger men," "fear of becoming invisible/irrelevant," "fear of being exposed as a fraud"). Name the top inherent-resource-lost explicitly — that's usually the epicenter of the wound.

3. **Rub-salt message** — a short, vivid passage that threatens the highest-intensity fear-of-loss resources. Sensory and specific — this becomes Dark-Night/agitation fuel downstream. Never clinical or summarized; write it as felt experience.

4. **Garden-of-Eden seed** — one line describing the *reverse* of the wound (the ideal state before the loss/threat) — feeds the Anti-Hero's Journey opening scene.

5. **Desire Daisy-Chain seed** — chain the wound's inverse outward: benefit → feeling → relationship → identity, one short chain.

## Output Contract

- Ranked Ontological Resource Matrix table (only resources that load, ordered by intensity).
- One-paragraph Core Wound prediction naming the specific refraction and its epicenter resource.
- One rub-salt passage (sensory, specific, market-language where grounded).
- One Garden-of-Eden seed line.
- One Desire Daisy-Chain seed (4-link chain).

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Core Wound names a specific refraction, not a generic label like "fear of failure" or "fear of missing out"?
- [ ] The refraction is explicitly tied to a ranked Ontological Resource Matrix entry (not asserted in isolation)?
- [ ] Inherent-vs-earned weighting is applied (inherent losses treated as heavier)?
- [ ] Rub-salt passage reads as felt threat, not clinical description?
- [ ] Matrix includes only resources that genuinely load for this market (not all 12 forced in)?

## Creative Latitude

The matrix and paragraph structure are fixed; the *language* of the rub-salt message and the wound naming is where this lives or dies. Push for the phrase that would make someone in this market wince with recognition — steal cadence and vocabulary from the grounding dossier's rawest soundbites where you have them. Don't sand the edges off a real fear to make it presentable.

## Deploy When

- After (or alongside) a Pain Matrix, before building the Resonance Hierarchy or Anti-Hero's Journey — both depend on this.
- Standalone, when you need the emotional bedrock fast without the full Manifold.
- Diagnosing why existing copy for a market feels flat — it's often missing this layer entirely.
