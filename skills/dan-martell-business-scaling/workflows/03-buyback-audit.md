## Execution

### Phase 0: Delegation Timing Calculus

Before classifying tasks by $/hr, calculate the **break-even revenue threshold** for each potential delegation. This prevents the two scaling killers:
- **Premature Delegation**: Hiring at $2K/month when you're making $4K burns 50% of revenue on a function you can still handle. Cash death.
- **Late Delegation**: Doing $10/hr work when you're at $15K/month means you're losing $100+/hr in opportunity cost. Founder death.

**The Delegation Break-Even Formula:**
For each task you might delegate:
1. **Monthly delegation cost** = hours/week x 4.3 x hourly rate for replacement
2. **Monthly revenue unlocked** = hours freed x your realistic $/hr on revenue work
3. **Break-even revenue** = the monthly revenue at which delegation cost < 15% of gross revenue AND revenue unlocked > 2x delegation cost

**Build the Delegation Sequence Table:**

| Function | Hours/Week | Delegation Cost/Mo | Revenue Unlocked/Mo | Break-Even Revenue | Current Revenue | Status |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|--------------------|--------------------|-----------------|--------|
| [task] | [hrs] | [$cost] | [$unlocked] | [$threshold] | [$current] | TOO EARLY / NOW / OVERDUE |

**Status logic:**
- **TOO EARLY**: Current revenue < break-even threshold. Do it yourself. Every dollar to delegation is a dollar away from survival.
- **NOW**: Current revenue is within 20% of break-even OR you've crossed it. The delegation window is open. Delay costs more than action.
- **OVERDUE**: Current revenue > 2x break-even. You're burning opportunity cost every day you hold this. Emergency delegate.

**The Scaling Sequence:** Sort the table by break-even revenue ascending. This IS your delegation timeline — the exact order things should be handed off as revenue grows. Not by importance. Not by annoyance. By financial readiness.

### 2. The Runner Test
Dan's brother was spending 12 hours a day running around getting supplies for his home-building crew. The fix: hire a runner. Cost: $15/hr. Revenue unlocked: selling houses instead of hauling bricks.

For every $10/hr or $100/hr task identified:
- What does it cost to outsource? (hourly rate)
- What would the founder do instead? (revenue-generating activity)
- What's the ROI? (revenue gained vs. cost of delegation)
- What's the break-even revenue? (from Phase 0 table — when does this delegation pay for itself?)

### 3. The Vacation Test
"Imagine you took a 4-week vacation. No Slack. No email. What would break?"

List every item that would fail. Rank by severity:
- Red: **Business stops** (must systematize immediately)
- Yellow: **Quality drops** (needs SOP + delegation)
- Green: **Nobody notices** (you were doing it for no reason — stop)

### 4. Delegation Roadmap (Revenue-Gated)
Build a phased delegation plan **gated by revenue milestones**, not arbitrary timelines:

**Phase 1: $0-$3K/month — Founder Does Everything (Except...)**
- Only delegate tasks where delegation cost < $200/month AND you can immediately redirect that time to revenue
- Tools: AI automation, free/cheap templates, batch processing
- The rule: If you can't point to the revenue the freed time produces, don't delegate yet

**Phase 2: $3K-$8K/month — First Hire Window**
- Tasks that crossed break-even in the Timing Calculus table
- Typically: admin, scheduling, basic content formatting, customer onboarding emails
- Budget ceiling: 15% of gross revenue on delegation
- Hire type: VA or part-time contractor (not full-time)

**Phase 3: $8K-$15K/month — Systems Handoff**
- Tasks requiring SOPs + skilled execution
- Typically: content production, client delivery components, bookkeeping
- Write the SOP, train the person, verify for 1 week, then release
- Budget ceiling: 20% of gross revenue on delegation

**Phase 4: $15K-$25K+ — Strategic Replacement**
- Functions that require a skilled hire who can own outcomes, not just tasks
- Typically: sales calls, client strategy, team management
- Interview process, KPIs, SLA structure, output-only compensation
- This is where you transition from "delegating tasks" to "delegating outcomes"

### 5. Vacation-Readiness Score
Rate the business 1-10:
- **1-3**: Founder IS the business. Takes vacation = revenue stops.
- **4-6**: Some systems. Could take 1 week off with anxiety.
- **7-8**: Solid delegation. 2 weeks possible. Some check-ins.
- **9-10**: McDonald's-level. 4 weeks, no contact, nothing breaks.

**Revenue-Readiness Alignment Check:**
- Is the founder delegating functions they can't yet afford? (cash danger)
- Is the founder hoarding functions they passed break-even on months ago? (opportunity danger)
- What's the NEXT delegation that will unlock, and at what revenue?
