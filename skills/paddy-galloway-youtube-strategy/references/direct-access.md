# Your Paddy Galloway content-strategy desk

Say **“Paddy, help me with…”** and describe the content decision. You do not need
to select other experts, start the video analyzer or remember every command.

**Whole project:** `/paddy-galloway` with your goal, or `/pg-creative-sprint`.
**Learn a principle:** “Paddy, teach me how you judge whether an idea is big enough.”
**One specific job:** use the menu below.

| What you need | Direct command | What you receive |
|---|---|---|
| Find what is holding my channel back | `/pg-channel-diagnosis` | Goal, audience, idea and execution diagnosis |
| Make ideas work for fans and newcomers | `/pg-ccn-slate` | Audience map and content slate |
| Study content that appears to be winning | `/pg-reference-analysis` | Reference breakdown with comparison evidence |
| Choose which idea deserves production | `/pg-idea-funnel` | Idea pool, elimination reasons and selected concept |
| Write stronger titles | `/pg-title-lab` | Ten title options with a reasoned choice |
| Plan thumbnails people understand quickly | `/pg-thumbnail-concepts` | Three distinct thumbnail briefs |
| Know what to shoot for the title and thumbnail | `/pg-packaging-capture` | Capture list before filming |
| Make the opening keep the promise | `/pg-intro-rewrite` | Finished opening and transition into the video |
| Give viewers a reason to care what happens | `/pg-stakes-design` | Relevant consequences and narrative stakes |
| Decide what success should look like | `/pg-launch-experiment` | Expectations and a release-test plan |
| Find where people stop watching | `/pg-retention-diagnosis` | Evidence-based diagnosis, or risk review if analytics are absent |
| Adapt a lesson to short-form content | `/pg-platform-transfer` | Original platform-appropriate short-form script |
| Build a complete content concept | `/pg-creative-sprint` | Idea, title, thumbnail briefs, capture plan, intro and test |
| Keep useful references so I can reuse them | `/pg-learning-bank` | Reference bank separating performance, taste and mechanism |

In Codex, `$paddy-galloway` and `$pg-title-lab` are skill names; the same names
with `/` are Claude commands and workspace workflow requests. Skill discovery
may refresh on the next task turn. All entry files point to the same Paddy skill
and its original source references; no global installation is required here.

## What this contains

The complete interview supplied 24 operational patterns, eight labeled deeper
interpretations and fourteen focused workflows with their own execution prompts.
Methods cover audience fit, ideation, research, titles, thumbnails, filming
preparation, openings, stakes, measurement, adaptation and repeated learning.
This models what was taught in one source; it does not claim to contain everything
Paddy knows or guarantee the same results as hiring him.

The Gemini experiment and Paddy's extracted knowledge are separate. Gemini's
answer needed corrections. The skill uses the preserved full captions and
source checks. Using this knowledge does not repeat the paid video analysis.
