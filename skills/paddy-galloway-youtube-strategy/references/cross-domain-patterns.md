# Cross-domain transfer and ownership

| Input / trigger | Owner and handoff | What is carried | What is not assumed |
|---|---|---|---|
| References need baseline comparison | Briar Cochran content science → Paddy | observed outlier multiple, audience, access limits | raw views prove a winning mechanism |
| A selected long-form idea needs packaging | Paddy workflows 05–07 → existing thumbnail/visual craft owner | question, title, three composition briefs, source shots | Paddy’s name authorizes a renderer or paid asset generation |
| Long-form idea becomes a Reel/Short | Paddy workflow 12 → Jenny Hoyos / Kallaway appropriate existing route | premise, intrinsic stakes, first-frame promise | a click-based thumbnail or 45-second intro transfers to a served feed |
| Final prose ships as Farrice | creative owner + VOICE-CARD + anti-slop | promise, proof, structure constraints | mixing expert voices creates Farrice’s voice |
| Channel package needs metadata | existing mkt-youtube-content-package after Paddy decisions | locked title/thumbnail promise, actual timeline | vendor skill can rewrite the strategic premise silently |

Reconcile overlap: Briar’s CCN summary maps toward a funnel, but Paddy’s original categories are viewing familiarity. Preserve both dimensions separately. Briar’s outlier thresholds and test cadence are his practices; Paddy’s 80% overlap heuristic is not the same as a winner/test ratio. Do not merge them into an invented universal law.

Safe cross-domain transfer is a hypothesis: attention question, intelligibility, contextual stakes and continuity can carry; numeric thresholds, audience behavior, thumbnail-click mechanics and measured success need platform-specific evidence. No global hook, recurring scan or automatic paid call is added.
