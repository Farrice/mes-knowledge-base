# Paddy Galloway — Genius Patterns
Source: Z2uoA3bhJT0. These are operational paraphrases; numbers are his heuristics or attributed cases, not guarantees.

## P01 — Click, watch, satisfaction
Source: 00:01:00–00:04:09; calibration anchor: Red Bull.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Separate the choice to click, the experience after clicking, and the reason to return; diagnose the failed stage before changing production.
- **Deployment context:** Long-form channel diagnosis
- **Success metric:** A revision addresses the observed bottleneck.
- **Failure / exception:** Served short-form has no identical thumbnail-click gate.

## P02 — Three ceilings, not one quality score
Source: 00:09:03–00:13:10; calibration anchor: astrophotography.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Distinguish available audience, the subset interested in this idea, and execution capture. Widen the concept through a understandable comparison while preserving niche substance.
- **Deployment context:** A polished video stalls
- **Success metric:** Name which ceiling changed and what stays valuable to core viewers.
- **Failure / exception:** A bigger audience may be the wrong audience for a specialist business.

## P03 — Floor from committed viewers
Source: 00:12:30–00:15:20; calibration anchor: video one.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Estimate the loyal audience that would show up for an unremarkable upload; treat it as a rough diagnostic, not a guaranteed minimum.
- **Deployment context:** Channel baseline
- **Success metric:** Separate core demand from idea upside.
- **Failure / exception:** Do not publish junk to measure the floor; seasonality and distribution vary.

## P04 — Advantage meets demonstrated demand
Source: 00:15:20–00:18:20; calibration anchor: 100-subscriber channel.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Intersect personal skill/access/interest with demand, then study small channels overperforming their own history.
- **Deployment context:** Choosing a niche
- **Success metric:** At least one relevant advantage and a named demand signal.
- **Failure / exception:** Subscriber ratios are rough intuition, not performance targets.

## P05 — Core, casual, new at every boundary
Source: 00:21:02–00:29:17; calibration anchor: Jesser.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Specify what loyal viewers get, why occasional viewers return, and why a stranger understands the promise. Test all three in idea, package, and body.
- **Deployment context:** Growth-oriented concept selection
- **Success metric:** All three have a distinct reason to watch without alienation.
- **Failure / exception:** Business-purpose or relationship videos can intentionally favor a narrower group.

## P06 — Through-line before expansion
Source: 00:29:17–00:33:24; calibration anchor: Forrest Galante.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Start with a tractable niche within a niche; expand through adjacent audience interests rather than the operator’s unrelated hobbies.
- **Deployment context:** Channel expansion
- **Success metric:** An existing viewer can recognize the reason for the next topic.
- **Failure / exception:** Topic labels such as fitness or gaming do not prove audience overlap.

## P07 — The glance test
Source: 00:33:24–00:35:55; calibration anchor: one second.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Reduce the concept to one understandable sentence and a legible image; inability to package is evidence against the idea itself.
- **Deployment context:** Preproduction idea gate
- **Success metric:** A stranger can restate the premise after a quick look.
- **Failure / exception:** Word count is subordinate to an interesting, comprehensible promise.

## P08 — New-viewer context without reintroductions
Source: 00:35:50–00:38:45; calibration anchor: Katie.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Seed relationships and required knowledge inside action; remove dependence on the previous episode without boring regulars.
- **Deployment context:** Script and intro review
- **Success metric:** A newcomer can follow each consequential relationship.
- **Failure / exception:** A full repeated biography solves context at the cost of momentum.

## P09 — An unanswered question worth interrupting for
Source: 00:38:45–00:40:11; calibration anchor: five NBA games in 50 hours.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Make the package pose a concrete unresolved outcome; ask whether it could interrupt an already-busy viewer.
- **Deployment context:** Promise design
- **Success metric:** There is an answer the video can actually deliver.
- **Failure / exception:** Do not fabricate outcomes or omit the promised answer.

## P10 — Internal, external, innovation
Source: 00:40:11–00:49:04; calibration anchor: 3x outlier.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Mine prior wins, compare external outliers to their ordinary neighbors, and reserve room for new combinations. In a new channel, begin with external evidence.
- **Deployment context:** Idea supply
- **Success metric:** Each idea has a source class, source link or an explicit innovation label.
- **Failure / exception:** An outlier is a clue, not causal proof that its thumbnail drove performance.

## P11 — Borrow components, preserve the mechanism
Source: 00:49:04–00:52:31; calibration anchor: Noah Kagan.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Combine a door-answer thumbnail situation, a money-question title structure, and a street-interview premise into a distinct story with its own source assets.
- **Deployment context:** Cross-niche adaptation
- **Success metric:** Name the borrowed mechanisms, the changed premise, and the missing access.
- **Failure / exception:** Do not copy the example’s identity, screenshots or unearned credibility.

## P12 — 80% audience overlap heuristic
Source: 00:52:31–00:55:40; calibration anchor: 80% strategy rule.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Ask whether most viewers of the previous idea would want the next; keep a minority experimental lane. Treat 80% as his planning heuristic, not an analytics measurement.
- **Deployment context:** Slate planning
- **Success metric:** State the shared viewer motive and the experimental slot.
- **Failure / exception:** Do not equate topic similarity with measured overlap or confuse this with Pareto allocation.

## P13 — 100 → 10 → 1
Source: 00:55:40–01:02:42; calibration anchor: five elimination criteria.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Generate a broad raw pool, eliminate with roughly four or five channel-specific criteria, develop ten one-page concept cards, then choose one strong bet.
- **Deployment context:** High-stakes production choice
- **Success metric:** Feasibility, packageability, target audience and goal are visible in the cuts.
- **Failure / exception:** Thirty survivors suggests loose filters; one or two can mean excessive restriction; these are calibration heuristics.

## P14 — Nonlinear returns and time allocation
Source: 01:03:20–01:07:30; calibration anchor: Gabe.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Treat packaging as a possible distribution multiplier; allocate deliberate research and concept effort, often 10–20% of production time in his practice.
- **Deployment context:** Budgeting craft effort
- **Success metric:** Time goes to competing concepts and research, not just polishing one image.
- **Failure / exception:** A reported 40x case is not a forecast or a measured 20% quality scale.

## P15 — Prior satisfaction is part of packaging
Source: 01:07:30–01:10:30; calibration anchor: Outdoor Boys.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Account for recognizable creator identity and the previous viewing experience before replacing a familiar thumbnail style.
- **Deployment context:** Established channel redesign
- **Success metric:** Test a change while preserving identifiable cues.
- **Failure / exception:** A successful screenshot thumbnail does not prove intentional packaging is unnecessary.

## P16 — Click to unpause
Source: 01:10:10–01:14:20; calibration anchor: Red Bull F1 drone.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Show a poised action or a person mid-thought that invites the viewer to start the event. Remove celebrity faces when the scene already carries the story.
- **Deployment context:** Thumbnail concepting
- **Success metric:** The central action and question are legible without clutter.
- **Failure / exception:** The race example’s access, cost and brand audience do not transfer automatically.

## P17 — Title → brief → shoot → design
Source: 01:14:20–01:20:20; calibration anchor: 10 titles and 3 thumbnails.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Write at least ten title alternatives; brief genuinely different thumbnail concepts before filming; capture angles and microexpressions; finish at least three alternatives.
- **Deployment context:** Packaging production
- **Success metric:** The shoot list covers the images required by all concepts.
- **Failure / exception:** Three shirt colors on the same shot are one concept, not three.

## P18 — Expectations before publication
Source: 01:20:20–01:27:42; calibration anchor: one out of ten.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Record expected first-hour/day/week performance before release. Compare actual results to that expectation and similar uploads; test distinct thumbnails with one initial title.
- **Deployment context:** Launch and diagnosis
- **Success metric:** A change follows a documented expectation miss.
- **Failure / exception:** A 2/10 can disappoint an expected 1/10; an 8/10 can be intentional; small samples need caution.

## P19 — Good evidence versus no evidence
Source: 01:23:30–01:27:42; calibration anchor: thumbnail experiment.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Keep a well-performing package; consider an untried alternative when current evidence is poor. Change one thing deliberately and record confounders.
- **Deployment context:** Repackaging decision
- **Success metric:** Each change has a reason, timestamp and comparison window.
- **Failure / exception:** Rapid uncontrolled swaps destroy attribution; passage of time can mimic an improvement.

## P20 — Titles for human curiosity
Source: 01:27:42–01:38:10; calibration anchor: Porsche GT3.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Use familiar, quickly read words; complement rather than repeat the thumbnail. Add a real contradiction or story when it makes a longer title more compelling.
- **Deployment context:** Title writing
- **Success metric:** The package offers one clear unresolved question with an honest payoff.
- **Failure / exception:** A strict character cap or SEO score cannot outrank comprehension; specialist language may serve qualified buyers.

## P21 — Views plus curve shape, with context
Source: 01:38:10–01:44:10; calibration anchor: lemonade stand.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Use views to see reach and retention-curve shape to locate departures; examine distribution context before interpreting aggregate CTR or average watch time.
- **Deployment context:** Analytics review
- **Success metric:** A diagnosis distinguishes broader reach from degraded content.
- **Failure / exception:** Without private analytics, do not invent a retention curve or claim a specific edit caused growth.

## P22 — Promise → intrigue → seamless flow
Source: 01:44:10–01:52:40; calibration anchor: FBI chase.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Confirm the click promise immediately, open answerable questions with only necessary context, then bridge straight into the first substantive beat.
- **Deployment context:** Intro rewrite
- **Success metric:** The first seconds reassure; the bridge starts the work without a reset.
- **Failure / exception:** Under 45 seconds is a default, not a law; a strong podcast montage can serve the promise.

## P23 — Stakes must belong to the viewer
Source: 01:57:26–02:02:10; calibration anchor: paper-ball demonstration.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** Make the consequence matter to this audience: uncertainty, opposition, time, reward or cost of ignorance. Use truthful stakes intrinsic to the topic.
- **Deployment context:** Story design
- **Success metric:** The viewer can say why the outcome matters to them.
- **Failure / exception:** Slime punishment in an investment explanation is incongruent; illustrative money claims are not evidence.

## P24 — Quality pendulum and learning bank
Source: 02:02:10–02:45:08; calibration anchor: 3–5 hours of study.
- **Observed behavior / executable action:** First make the video watchable, then intensify idea/packaging work, then raise production quality. Keep separate reference banks for outliers, titles, thumbnails and intros; retrieve the mechanism when needed.
- **Deployment context:** Capability building
- **Success metric:** One stage-appropriate craft priority and reusable notes tied to evidence.
- **Failure / exception:** A brand needs commitment and an empowered strategist; a stored summary is not repeatable market success.
