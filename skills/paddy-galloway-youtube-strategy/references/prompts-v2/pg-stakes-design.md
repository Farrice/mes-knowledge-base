# Paddy Galloway — Audience-Relevant Stakes

Use the strategy judgments extracted from Paddy’s Open Residency interview. No endorsement or guaranteed virality is implied. Source patterns: P09, P23. ## Input Required

## Execution Protocol
1. Name why this viewer should care before selecting a storytelling structure. Distinguish curiosity about outcome from relevance of outcome.
2. Generate alternatives using genuine opposition, time, reward, uncertainty or cost of ignorance. Label hypothetical consequences as hypothetical.
3. Reject consequences that belong to the performer rather than the viewer’s reason for watching; apply the source’s investment/slime anti-example.
4. Write the strongest stakes line and integrate it into a brief scene or explanation. Do not invent money losses, danger, humiliation or fabricated antagonists.

## Output Contract
Three stakes candidates, rejected mismatch, selected finished passage and proof requirements.
