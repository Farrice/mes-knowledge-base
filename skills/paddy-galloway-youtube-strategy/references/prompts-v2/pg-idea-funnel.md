## Input Required

## Execution Protocol
1. Build a diverse raw pool from internal wins, external signals and explicitly labeled innovation. Use local reasoning; never create a paid loop to fill a quota.
2. For a full production decision, aim for 100 distinct premises. If evidence or scope supports fewer, show the actual count and call it a reduced run; never label 12 as 100.
3. Define roughly four or five elimination criteria: audience fit, clear packaging, feasibility, credible outcome potential and project-specific constraint. Calibrate overly loose or strict filters.
4. Develop ten survivors as one-page concepts: title options, rough thumbnail composition, logline, body skeleton, source hypothesis and cost/access needs. Reduced runs preserve the decision sequence with disclosed counts.
5. Choose one bet with a concrete reason and a falsification test. Declare expected performance as a hypothesis; do not invent historical support.

## Output Contract
Raw idea pool, elimination ledger, finalist concept cards and one selected production bet with counts.
