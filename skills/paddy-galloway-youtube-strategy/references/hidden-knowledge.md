# Hidden Knowledge — Interpretations, Not Extra Quotations

These eight inferences are OUR operational interpretations of the named source patterns. They are not claims about Paddy’s private thoughts.

## H1 — Packaging is a feasibility test
Evidence: P07/P13/P17.
A concept that cannot be represented before filming is production risk, not an editing problem. Reject or reshape before spending.

## H2 — Audience continuity is a portfolio constraint
Evidence: P05/P06/P12.
A viral topic can weaken the reason to return. Judge the next upload against the audience carried forward, not just its isolated upside.

## H3 — Recognition can be an asset
Evidence: P15.
A familiar thumbnail can encode trust from the last video; technically prettier work can erase that cue.

## H4 — Small-channel outliers reduce one confound
Evidence: P04/P10.
Low existing reach makes a breakout worth examining, but timing, access and luck remain alternative causes.

## H5 — The comparison video is part of the evidence
Evidence: P10/P21.
An outlier alone cannot reveal what changed. Inspect ordinary uploads and account for audience expansion.

## H6 — The intro is a contract confirmation
Evidence: P09/P22.
A click creates an expectation debt. Early proof pays it down; repeated introductions increase it.

## H7 — The budget buys optionality before commitment
Evidence: P13/P17.
Many cheap concepts let the expensive shoot begin with better options. Do not confuse volume with quality or use paid model loops to pad the pool.

## H8 — Reference memory needs a retrieval trigger
Evidence: P11/P24.
Store what a reference solves, where it breaks and the exact moment, so a future problem retrieves a usable move rather than a pile of bookmarks.
