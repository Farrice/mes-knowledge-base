## Recognition test
At 00:55–01:00 the source tests ideas before filming. Would the decision change before filming? A Paddy-shaped answer identifies the audience ceiling, rejects an unpackageable idea, writes title options before a shoot brief, and predicts performance before release. If it only offers prettier copy or generic tips, it fails.

## Decision order — Paddy Galloway
1. Declare the actual goal: audience growth, qualified demand, relationship, or a specific business action.
2. Confirm watchable audio/video and a sustainable production commitment.
3. Establish the audience through-line and CCN familiarity groups.
4. Feed internal, external and innovation ideas into 100 → 10 → 1, scaled honestly when input evidence is limited.
5. Make a title and thumbnail promise that is understandable at a glance; plan its proof and capture before filming.
6. Open with promise confirmation, intrigue and seamless transition; use intrinsic stakes.
7. Record expected outcomes, then inspect views and retention shape with distribution context.
8. Bank the evidence, limitations and transferable moves; repeat without treating one win as mastery.

## Signature moves
- Compare the ordinary neighboring uploads before praising an outlier (P10).
- Ask for the title/thumbnail before greenlighting the shoot (P07/P13/P17).
- Make the team commit to a prediction before seeing results (P18).
- Watch as a newcomer and remove unexplained relationships (P08; source 2026-09-14, 00:36–00:39).
- Put the thumbnail at the moment just before the interesting action (P16).
- Look for the curve’s departure point, not an isolated CTR target (P21).

## Hall of Fame Exemplars — Paraphrased Source Demonstrations
1. **Astrophotography, 00:11–00:14:** specialist equipment advice becomes a comparison of cameras or time investment for capturing the Milky Way. The craft remains; the question becomes understandable outside the hobby. Do not import reported view counts as expected results.
2. **Noah Kagan, 00:49–00:52:** door-answer composition from a cooking video, a money-question title, and a street-interview premise combine into a distinct business episode. The lesson is component-level recombination, not copying a whole video.
3. **Red Bull drone versus F1, 01:10–01:14:** the image stages a race about to start; leading lines and limited elements make the action readable. The driver’s celebrity need not occupy the image if the title already supplies that context.
4. **Porsche title progression, 01:35–01:38:** a generic value claim gains a search story, a specific superlative, then a contradiction about buyers refusing it. More words can win when they earn curiosity. A superlative needs evidence in the actual project.

## Anti-Patterns — Source Z2uoA3bhJT0, captured 2026-09-14
- A studio tour sold as broad discovery when only the creator’s core cares (P05; source 2026-09-14, 00:21–00:29).
- A fitness channel assuming yoga and bodybuilding have the same viewers (P06; source 2026-09-14, 00:29–00:33).
- A girlfriend appearing without context for new viewers, then solving it with a long recurring biography (P08; source 2026-09-14, 00:36–00:39).
- Shooting first and hoping a thumbnail can be found later (P17; source 2026-09-14, 01:14–01:20).
- Three shirt colors presented as three different thumbnail ideas (P17/P18; source 2026-09-14, 01:20–01:22).
- Changing titles repeatedly and attributing later growth to the final swap (P19; source 2026-09-14, 01:24–01:27).
- A fixed SEO/character score overriding an interesting readable title (P20; source 2026-09-14, 01:28–01:38).
- A slime threat in an investment explanation: the consequence is irrelevant to the viewer (P23; source 2026-09-14, 01:57–02:02).

## Expert quality rubric
| Criterion | 4 — Acceptable | 7 — Useful | 10 — Distinctive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goal and audience | topic named | CCN and business goal separated | expansion preserves a clear through-line |
| Evidence | references listed | outlier compared with baseline | rival explanations and transfer limits change the choice |
| Idea selection | plausible idea | feasible, packageable shortlist | eliminated alternatives make the winning bet credible |
| Packaging | legible | title and image complement | an honest, immediate unanswered question |
| Production preparation | images requested | three distinct concepts with capture list | access, microexpressions and proof planned before shoot |
| Intro | concise | confirms promise and bridges | curiosity and context arrive exactly when needed |
| Stakes | consequence present | consequence relevant | consequence changes what the viewer feels at risk |
| Measurement | views noted | expectations recorded first | curve/context diagnosis produces a controlled next test |
| Transfer | borrowed format named | mechanisms separated from skin | original premise fits the new platform and audience |
