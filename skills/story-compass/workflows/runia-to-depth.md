# /runia-to-depth — The Depth Escalation Pipeline

Take a completed compass sentence and feed it into the Antigravity cinematic storytelling experts for maximum narrative depth. This is the chain: Runia finds the story → Connelly/Wright/Roth make it unforgettable.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `skills/story-compass/genius.md` — Pattern 6 (Compass Sentence), Stacking Guide.
Load target expert genius AFTER selecting the depth track.

## Input Required

- **Compass sentence**: Completed "I wanted ___, but ___, until ___"
- **Content type**: Long-form article, video essay, email, profile, social post
- **Depth track** (choose one or chain):
  - **Connelly Track**: Detail, subtext, momentum, outsider lens
  - **Wright Track**: Gap, complication, universal resonance, theme
  - **Roth Track**: Cinematic prose, visual depth, erosion, residue

## Execution

### Step 1: Verify the Compass

Confirm the compass sentence passes the story test at strength 7+. If below 7, refine first with `/runia-compass`.

### Step 2: Decompose for Depth

Break the compass into the three raw materials each depth expert needs:

| Compass Element | Connelly Needs | Wright Needs | Roth Needs |
|----------------|---------------|-------------|-----------|
| **Want** | The outsider's desire — what makes this person's want SPECIFIC | The gap between want and reality — where the story lives | The visual world of the want — what it looks/feels/sounds like |
| **Tension** | The surgical detail that makes tension visceral | The complication beneath the surface tension | The cinematic scene where tension is SHOWN, not told |
| **Change** | The subtext of change — what's NOT said at the resolution | The universal meaning — why does this change matter to everyone? | The residue — what haunts the reader after the change |

### Step 3: Route to Depth Expert

**Connelly Track** → Load Connelly genius → Execute:
1. `/connelly-detail` — Replace descriptions with 1-2 surgical details
2. `/connelly-subtext` — Build what's unsaid underneath the change
3. `/connelly-momentum` — Find and fix speed bumps in the narrative

**Wright Track** → Load Wright genius → Execute:
1. `/wright-gap` — The tension between public surface and private truth
2. `/wright-complication` — The real story beneath the surface story
3. `/wright-universal` — Make the specific resonate universally

**Roth Track** → Load Roth genius → Execute:
1. `/roth-visual-prose` — Cinematic scene construction
2. `/roth-erosion` — Daily rewrite for density and precision
3. `/roth-residue` — Engineer the moments that stay with the reader

### Step 4: Compass Integrity Check

After depth escalation, verify the compass still holds:
- Has the want been deepened without being changed?
- Has the tension been enriched without being muddied?
- Has the change been elevated without being overcomplicated?

If the depth process reveals a BETTER compass sentence → update it.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Content Type | Best Depth Track | Why |
|-------------|-----------------|-----|
| **Personal essay/memoir** | Connelly | Detail + subtext = intimacy |
| **Profile/case study** | Wright | Gap + complication = revelation |
| **Video narrative** | Roth | Visual prose + residue = cinematic |
| **Newsletter** | Connelly → Wright | Detail hooks → universal meaning resonates |
| **Long-form article** | Wright → Roth | Complication anchors → visual prose elevates |

## Output Requirements

```
COMPASS SENTENCE: [original]

DEPTH TRACK SELECTED: [Connelly / Wright / Roth / Chain]

DEPTH-ESCALATED OUTPUT:
[The content piece with depth applied — either the enriched narrative or specific depth elements to integrate]

COMPASS UPDATED?: [Yes — new sentence / No — original holds]

DEPTH AUDIT:
- Want deepened: [how]
- Tension enriched: [how]
- Change elevated: [how]
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Depth enhances the compass — doesn't replace it
- [ ] Specificity increased at every level
- [ ] The story is richer but not more complicated
- [ ] The compass sentence still works as structural GPS
- [ ] The depth expert's methodology is properly deployed (not generic)
