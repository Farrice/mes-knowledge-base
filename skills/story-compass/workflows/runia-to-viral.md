# /runia-to-viral — The Viral Optimization Pipeline

Take a completed compass sentence and feed it into the Antigravity viral and audience psychology experts for maximum reach, saves, and conversion. This is the chain: Runia builds the story → Puri/Kallaway make it spread and convert.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `skills/story-compass/genius.md` — Pattern 6 (Compass Sentence), Stacking Guide.
Load target expert genius AFTER selecting the viral track.

## Input Required

- **Compass sentence**: Completed "I wanted ___, but ___, until ___"
- **Platform**: YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X
- **Goal**: Reach / Saves / Conversion / All three
- **Viral track** (choose one or chain):
  - **Puri Track**: Frame engineering, emotion transfer, viral hooks
  - **Kallaway Track**: Audience obsession, bankshot suggestion, dopamine psychology

## Execution

### Step 1: Verify the Compass

Confirm the compass sentence passes the story test. Viral optimization on top of a weak story = polished mediocrity.

### Step 2: Extract Viral Fuel from the Compass

| Compass Element | Puri Extracts | Kallaway Extracts |
|----------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Want** | The FRAME — what lens makes this want universally relatable? | The HOOK — what signal word triggers audience obsession? |
| **Tension** | The EMOTION — what feeling transfers from content to viewer? | The BANKSHOT — how does the tension make the viewer arrive at the conclusion themselves? |
| **Change** | The INSIGHT — what shareable realization makes this worth passing along? | The SAVE TRIGGER — what makes this too valuable to scroll past? |

### Step 3: Route to Viral Expert

**Puri Track** → Load Shaan Puri genius → Execute:
1. Frame the compass through the most compelling lens
2. Engineer emotion transfer — the viewer should FEEL the tension
3. Design the hook that stops the scroll using the compass
4. Structure for shareability — the insight must be quotable

**Kallaway Track** → Load Kallaway genius → Execute:
1. Identify the obsession level (Signal → Belief → Action)
2. Apply bankshot suggestion — let the audience arrive at the conclusion
3. Engineer save triggers — make the framework too useful to forget
4. Design the dopamine arc — curiosity → investment → payoff → share

### Step 4: Platform Optimization

| Platform | Hook Format | Story Delivery | CTA Style |
|----------|-----------|----------------|-----------|
| **YouTube** | Tension as thumbnail + title | Full compass arc in video | Subscribe + next video |
| **LinkedIn** | Counterintuitive frame from compass | Text-based compass narrative | Comment engagement |
| **Instagram** | Visual tension as first frame | Carousel = compass beats | Save + share |
| **TikTok** | Pattern interrupt from tension | Speed-compressed compass | Stitch/duet invitation |
| **Twitter/X** | One-line compass as hook tweet | Thread = compass expanded | Retweet + bookmark |

### Step 5: Compass Integrity Check

After viral optimization, verify:
- The story hasn't been simplified to the point of losing its compass
- The hooks serve the narrative, not replace it
- The viral mechanics enhance the story rather than distorting it

## Output Requirements

```
COMPASS SENTENCE: [original]

VIRAL TRACK: [Puri / Kallaway / Both]

VIRAL-OPTIMIZED ELEMENTS:
- Hook: [the scroll-stopper derived from the compass]
- Frame: [the lens that makes this universally compelling]

PLATFORM-SPECIFIC OUTPUT:
[Content optimized for the target platform]

COMPASS INTEGRITY: ✓ Story preserved / ⚠ Story simplified
```

- [ ] Story compass preserved — viral optimization enhances, doesn't replace
- [ ] Hook connects to the tension (not clickbait unrelated to content)
- [ ] The change/insight is the share trigger (not the hook)
- [ ] Platform-native formatting applied
- [ ] Audience obsession mechanics properly deployed
