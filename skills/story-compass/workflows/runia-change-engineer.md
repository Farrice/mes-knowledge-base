# /runia-change-engineer — The Change Mechanism Builder

When you have a clear want and strong tension but the ending falls flat, this workflow engineers meaningful change that completes the story. The most common failure: change that just restates the want.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `skills/story-compass/genius.md` — Pattern 5 (Change Requirement), Pattern 10 (Dig Protocol), Anti-Pattern: Circular Change.

## Input Required

- **The want**: What the person/audience desires
- **The tension**: What stands in the way
- **The weak change** (if any): The current ending that doesn't work

## Execution

### Step 1: Diagnose the Change Failure

| Failure Type | Signal | Example |
|-------------|--------|---------|
| **Circular** | Change = want restated | "I wanted to start" → "I started" |
| **Vague** | No specifics, just feeling | "Things got better" / "I grew" |
| **Missing mechanism** | What happened, but not WHY | "I finally did it" (but what shifted?) |
| **Too small** | Change is real but trivial | "I adjusted the temperature" (for a story about transformation) |
| **Too big** | Change is unearned by the tension | "I completely transformed my life" (from a pasta video) |

### Step 2: Run the Change Excavation

For each probe, push deeper:

1. **What was the realization?** — Not what you DID, but what you UNDERSTOOD that made action possible
2. **What did you do differently?** — The specific behavioral shift, not "I tried harder"
3. **How did you see it in a new way?** — The frame shift, the perspective change
4. **What was the smallest action that broke the pattern?** — Often the mechanism is tiny but decisive
5. **What would you tell someone else who's in the tension right now?** — This often reveals the change

### Step 3: Classify the Change

**Internal Change**: A realization, a fear faced, a belief shifted, a perspective altered
**External Change**: A visible result, a situation transformed, a measurable outcome
**Both**: Internal shift enables external result (strongest structure)

### Step 4: Test the Change

The change must pass three checks:
- [ ] **Non-circular**: Clearly different from the want
- [ ] **Has mechanism**: Contains the WHY or HOW, not just the WHAT
- [ ] **Proportional**: Matches the scale of the tension (small tension → small but meaningful change)

### Step 5: Produce the Complete Compass

With the engineered change, write the full compass sentence:

> "I wanted [WANT], but [TENSION], until [CHANGE with mechanism]."

## Content Type Adaptations

| Content Type | Change Sweet Spot | Mechanism Style |
|-------------|------------------|----------------|
| **Personal story** | Internal realization that enabled action | "I realized that..." |
| **Tutorial** | Specific technique or insight that solved the problem | "I discovered this one thing..." |
| **Case study** | Measurable result with the pivot point | "The shift was..." |
| **Sales copy** | Your product/method as the change mechanism | "Until I found/built/designed..." |
| **Thought leadership** | Counter-intuitive insight | "Until I understood that the opposite was true..." |

## Output Requirements

```
CHANGE DIAGNOSIS:
[Failure type identified] — [why current change doesn't work]

ENGINEERED CHANGE:
[New change with mechanism] — [Internal/External/Both]

COMPASS SENTENCE:
"I wanted ___, but ___, until ___."

CHANGE STRENGTH: [1-10] — [rationale]
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Change is non-circular (clearly different from want)
- [ ] Change contains mechanism (not just "I did it")
- [ ] Change is proportional to tension
- [ ] The compass sentence now makes someone want to hear the full story
