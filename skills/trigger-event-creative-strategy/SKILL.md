# Trigger-Event Creative Strategy

A trigger event is not a benefit, pain-point label, persona adjective, or purchase trigger inferred by a marketer. It is the concrete moment when the problem stopped being tolerable: the 2:00 a.m. paw chewing, the dog refusing another bowl, the morning a symptom disrupted work.

This skill turns exact customer evidence into testable creative strategy. It does not copy Alex Cooper's tools or commercial system. It preserves the underlying mechanics and connects them to Antigravity's existing review miner, creative strategists, finalize path, outcome tracker, and governed memory.

## Evidence Standard

- Verbatim customer language carries its source and row, URL, timestamp, or artifact path.
- Paraphrases are labeled.
- An inferred trigger event is marked `UNCONFIRMED` and cannot become a customer quote.
- Generic benefit language fails the intolerance test.
- Commercial tools, ad-library impressions, and creator claims are directional evidence, not conversion proof.

## Quality Bar

A stranger should be able to trace every event to evidence, see why it marks a transition, deploy the narrative without inventing a life story, and test one changed variable at a time.
