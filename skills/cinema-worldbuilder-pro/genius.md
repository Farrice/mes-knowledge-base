## Anti-Patterns (Sourced)

- **Style prefix at the top of the prompt** ("cinematic, moody film-noir style:" before Scene & Mood). Reversed by the Distributed Style lock: "No style header at the top of the prompt. Style isn't a single object — it splits across many aspects, and each aspect belongs inside the block that carries it. Putting a style prefix on the prompt scatters the model's attention." — `skills/cinema-worldbuilder-pro/SKILL.md`, line 190.
- **Millimeters alone as the lens spec** ("50mm dialogue two-shot") instead of the FOV-degree anchor. "Seedance latches onto FOV in degrees as a snap value... Millimeters read as suggestion; degrees read as instruction." — `skills/cinema-worldbuilder-pro/SKILL.md`, line 211. Corroborated independently in Joey's own notes, harvested 2026-07-13: "FOV in degrees, not just millimeters. Seedance treats degrees as a discrete snap value and treats millimeters as a suggestion. 47° (50mm) holds. 50mm drifts." — `extractions/joey-cinema/notion-cinema-claude-skills-v3.md`, line 69.
- **Re-describing wardrobe already visible in the reference image** inside a Subject Lock block. "Trust the reference for wardrobe... Wardrobe details visible in the reference are NOT re-described." — `skills/cinema-worldbuilder-pro/SKILL.md`, line 472 (Universal Prompt Rule 20).
- **Mood-word abstractions** ("she looks stressed," "the alley feels dangerous," "fast bike chase") instead of measurable physical cues. "Seedance is a physics engine, not a mood board. It renders things it can see and count. Mood words evaporate." — `skills/cinema-worldbuilder-pro/SKILL.md`, line 30.
- **Negative prohibitions** ("the camera doesn't shake," "no other people in the shot") instead of positive locks. "State what happens. Do not state what shouldn't. Negative language weakens the signal — the model sees the noun and rounds toward it." — `skills/cinema-worldbuilder-pro/SKILL.md`, line 52 (POSITIVE PHRASING, LOCKED).
- **Letting the environment plate stand in for a named subject's canonical reference** when that subject is already visible in the plate. "Every named subject that appears in a Seedance scene gets its canonical reference tagged separately — even if that subject is also visible in the rendered environment plate... No exceptions." — `skills/cinema-worldbuilder-pro/SKILL.md`, line 100 (Canonical-over-plate rule, HARD LOCK).
- **Skipping the pre-prompt confirmation on a genuinely new scene** to move faster. "For new scenes, confirmation is mandatory. Never assume runtime — ask." — `skills/cinema-worldbuilder-pro/SKILL.md`, line 148.
- **Defaulting to a locked-off tripod** instead of handheld. "Default camera energy is handheld with breath, drift, organic operator movement... Locked-off tripod is OPT-IN ONLY." — `skills/cinema-worldbuilder-pro/SKILL.md`, lines 384–386.
