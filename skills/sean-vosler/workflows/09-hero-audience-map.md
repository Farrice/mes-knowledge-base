# 09 — Hero Audience Map (The Unlikely Hero, Phases 1–2)

## Pre-Flight Gate

1. **Belief in the product.** Vosler's Phase-1 close: if you don't believe in it, refine it or find another — taking any paying client degrades copy and burns you out.
2. **Depth calibrated before questions.** Step 1 runs first, or you'll build $20K-coaching research for a $12 thermos.
3. **Dissonance preconditions checkable** (Pattern 3, hard gate): the map must be able to name *perceived choice* and *meaningful stakes*. Factual lock (contract, physiology) → walk away.
4. **A mining source exists** (Hard Veto 1): answers come from interviews, reviews, community posts, or client raw language. Best-guess-with-empathy is the disclosed fallback, never the default.

## Execution

**1 — Depth calibration (three variables, in writing).** Score *familiarity* · *active search* · *bottom-line impact*. High familiarity + searching + low impact = shallow map; low familiarity + not searching + high future impact = deep map. A $12 thermos needs nothing like the world-building a $20,000 coaching program needs; broad audience × high price = more nurturing. Record the score — downstream workflows read it to route reveal-depth.

**2 — Frame the two worlds.** Not "day-to-day" — **accepted world vs. dream world**. "If bliss is reaching a dream world, most people are lost in the opposite: a world of distraction and denial." One line each, in their words.

**3 — The 6-question bank** (`link.sean.co/hj-1`), every answer sourced:
1. What dream worlds could the hero be harboring? (health / wealth / happiness; giving more to community or family; more time with loved ones — *how much*?)
2. Why are they putting off pursuit? (waiting for "the right time"? believe they lack resources? waiting on a trigger — age, money, mindset? afraid of judgment? unaware it's possible for people "like them"?)
3. What emotional fears hold them back? (too shy / too outgoing? lacking patience, knowledge, experience? scarred by overconfidence-then-failure? told their whole life they don't have what it takes?)
4. What deep hopes do they hold unconsciously, or that you'd wish for them? (what could they attain? who could they become? how would they experience the world differently?)
5–6. What past positive experiences have they had with your industry / product / service? *(printed twice in the source — one question, note the duplication rather than inventing a sixth.)*

Twin goal: they understand where they are vs. where they want to be, and they're woken to possibilities they didn't know existed.

**4 — Character Sketch + Day-in-a-Life** (~30 min, `link.sean.co/hj-2`). 2–3 paragraphs of narrative, not bullets: life **BEFORE** the product · life **AFTER** · the **IN-BETWEEN**, the buying experience itself. Prose forces empathy a grid hides.

**5 — Values pass** (deep maps). Heroes pursue unnamed higher-level values (~50-term list, `link.sean.co/100-values`). Pick the 3 they hold; state how the offer aligns. Seeds The Value Battle enemy in §3.

**6 — Extract the truths (Phase 2, "stoke the nagging voice of change").** You're joining a conversation already running in their head. Two categories, **drawn from the Step 3–4 answers, not researched fresh**: **Category 1** — the dream is real and reachable for YOU, even if they don't believe it; **Category 2** — what has actually been holding you back, internal and external: the villain. Rule: the harder a truth is to accept, the stronger the bond when it's accepted. Keep the monsters real.

**7 — Change psychology, applied to every truth.** Sell **addition over change** (adding one food costs less than changing a diet). **Hope > fear** — both drive change, hope builds the durable connection. **Don't ask for too much change early**; a reader who rationalizes "not for me" means the core argument needs re-examining, not a louder ask. Trust = give them a usable truth: win the heart with emotion, the mind with logic, the sale with both.

**8 — Extend to any phase via the master 4-item template (p.239):** (i) their internal and external world at that phase · (ii) your role as guide there · (iii) emotions to encourage, emotions to mitigate · (iv) tactical assets and where they enter the funnel. Segments sit at different phases simultaneously — tag each. Guardrail: not every page needs the whole journey.

## Output Requirements

One **Audience Hero Map**: **(1)** depth score, three variables rated, resulting depth stated; **(2)** Accepted World / Dream World, one mined line each; **(3)** the six answers, each source-tagged (interview / review / community post / client raw text / `EMPATHY-GUESS`); **(4)** Character Sketch + Day-in-a-Life, before / after / in-between; **(5)** values shortlist + alignment sentence (deep maps); **(6)** Truth Bank — Categories 1 and 2, each traced to its source question; **(7)** change-psychology notes: addition-framing, hope frame, size of the first ask; **(8)** per-phase extension table with segments tagged, if used; **(9)** open questions only a real customer can answer.

## Quality Gate

- **Provenance** (rubric: Language provenance). Every truth and dream-world line traces to a mined artifact; unsourced lines labeled `EMPATHY-GUESS`. Violation = **Hard Veto 1, No blank-page copy** — fail.
- **Stakes.** Category 2 names a real villain with real consequences, not inflated risk. Violation = **Hard Veto 2, No fear-mongering** — fail.
- **Barnum check.** Any "you're the kind of person who…" line is high-probability-true for the target, letting non-targets self-select out. Violation = **Hard Veto 4, No flattery-Barnum** — fail.
- **Layer-3 discipline.** Freedom / time / family held for imagination triggers, never asserted downstream. Violation = **Hard Veto 5** — fail.
- **Preconditions named** in one sentence each. Missing = fail (Pattern 3 is a hard gate).
- **Claim hygiene.** Statistics labeled VERIFIED / LIKELY / UNCONFIRMED. Violation = **Hard Veto 6** — fail.
- **Ask-size sanity.** The first ask is an addition, not a wholesale change — or the exception is argued. Unexamined = fail.
