## Pre-Flight Gate

1. **SMART objective exists** (Pattern 7): "In this [medium] I want to convince my reader that [feature] is better for [task] than their current method…" No objective = optimizing for cleverness.
2. **Argument stands before scaffold** (Pattern 2): the contention fits one sentence. A formula sequences an argument; it can't manufacture one.
3. **Mining pass done** (Hard Veto 1): headline and bullet banks exist. Formulas are slots; mined language fills them.
4. **Depth score inherited** from workflow 09 — email → straight to CTA · sales page → benefit outline then buy-CTA · content → tactical depth then soft-sell.

## Execution

**1 — Classify the deliverable → shortlist.** He groups by *what are you writing today*: long-form page → **Frey 12-Step** (default) · Stone **Seven-Step** · **AICPBSAWN** · **Belcher 21-Part** (only 1–10 printed) · **HELLYEAH**. Short/low-ticket → **SLAP** (Stop→Look→Act→Purchase; his own aside: act and purchase arguably aren't separate steps). Desire beat collapsing → **AIDPPC** (Collier): Attention→Interest→**Description**→**Persuasion**→**Proof**→Close, AIDA's vague Desire split into three workable slots. Lead-gen page → **Lacey's 5 questions**: what will you do for me if I listen · how · who's responsible for these promises · who have you done this for · what will it cost me.

**2 — Frey 12-Step, mapped to the 7 objection types.** Steps: **1** Get Attention → **2** Identify the Problem → **3** Provide the Solution → **4** Present Credentials → **5** Show Benefits → **6** Give Social Proof → **7** Make Your Offer → **8** Inject Scarcity → **9** Give a Guarantee → **10** Call to Action → **11** Give a Warning → **12** Close With a Reminder. Write each step *at the objection it answers*:

| Reader's line | Type | Steps carrying it |
|---|---|---|
| "You don't understand my problem" | empathy | 2, 6 (proof from someone like them) |
| "How do I know you're qualified?" | authority | 4 |
| "I don't believe you" | logical | 6, 5 (benefits made provable) |
| "I don't need it right now" | urgency | 8, 11 |
| "It won't work *for me*" | ego | 6 (segment-matched), 5 |
| "What if I don't like it?" | risk | 9 |
| "I can't afford it" | value | 7 + Trivialize Price |

Motivation: buy on emotion, justify with logic after; only gain or loss motivates, and **loss is stronger** (Frey's 5:1 "Stop Your Divorce" claim is unattributed lore — label **UNCONFIRMED**). Beneath both, the 8 universal motivations: wealthy, good looking, healthy, popular, secure, inner peace, free time, fun. His annotation is the operating instruction — *"DIG DEEP into each step"*; every label is a sub-exercise, not a checkbox.

**3 — Canonical offer close** (prints twice; his default): **Here's What You Get → Establish Value → Conditional Bonus → Price → Trivialize Price → Guarantee → Risk Reversal → Scarcity.** Trivialize against a daily habit; the price reveal is where §10's anchoring two-stack belongs.

**4 — VSL.** *Edwards 10-step*: shocking statement → problem and why it matters → agitate → push agitation to deep emotion → solution → credibility → proof → biggest things they get → reasons not to procrastinate → close asking for the purchase. *12-step 3-CTA*: greeting → problem + promise → **scarcity established early, before the pitch** → agitate → your solution as THE solution → features+benefits → **CTA #1: desire** → credentials/proof/testimonials → guarantee → **CTA #2: logic** → deadline reprise → **CTA #3: FOMO**. *P.A.S.T.O.R.*: Person/Problem/Pain → Amplify consequences → Story, Solution, System → Testimonials → **Offer — ~80% of it on the transformation, not the mechanics** → Response.

**5 — Email drips.** *Walling 5-Day*: Day 0 on signup (welcome + what the drip covers + reply-CTA) → D1 educate, end on an action → D2 theory via story, CTA in the P.S. → D3 actionable tips → D4 case study with real numbers + CTA. *(Printed day offsets are internally inconsistent around D3–D4; transcribe and flag, don't silently fix.)* *6-Email Nurture*: welcome + support contact → free offer → case study → ROI examples → testimonial video → FAQs. *Wishpond 5-Part*: warm hello → transparent case study → personal business story → case study → free trial or soft sell. *PASOP* is the chaining engine: each email runs Problem → Agitation → Solution → Outcome → **new Problem**, and that problem opens the next; email 3 collapses to PAS and links the page. Use when sequence completion matters more than any single email.

**6 — Ads.** *ERERS*: Emotional → Rational → Emotional → Rational → Social proof. *MECLABS*: `ea = 2at + i + as` — attention weighted double against interest and the click-ask; budget line-count accordingly. Structural rule: Headline = attract attention (be relevant) · Description 1 = generate interest (be useful) · Description 2 = ask for the click (show value).

**7 — Blog.** *Hyatt 6-Part*: headline → lead hook → relevant image → personal story → scannable body → open-ended question. *HIPASI*: Headline → Image → Problem → Agitation → Solution → Invitation.

**8 — SCAMPER remix pass** (on anything that exists — winners, swipes, a dead draft): **S**ubstitute a ho-hum phrase for something surprising · **C**ombine elements from two or more sources · **A**dapt a winner from a different category · **M**odify, minify or magnify one element · **P**ut it to use with unexpected people · **E**liminate what's seen better days · **R**earrange, reverse or redefine any part. A mutation tool, not a from-scratch generator.

**9 — Support assets.** *Testimonials*: run **Before-After-Experience** as the literal interview script (hesitations before? what did you discover after? what did it feel like?), bar-check with **4 Ss** (Specific, Short, Sizzling, Signed) and **TEASE** (tactful? emphasizes a strength? authentic? short and sweet? engages?). *Value prop*: "For {target} who {need}, {Name} is a {category} that {benefit}", or **VAD** (Verb–Application–Differentiator): "We do X, but the difference is {differentiator}" / "We help X do Y by doing Z."

**10 — Word banks, at point of use only.** Resistance (Imagine, Suppose, Picture, Remember, Realize…), Influential (Discover, Proven, Guaranteed, Results…), Power (~40: breakthrough, sure fire, insider, the truth about…), Subconscious phrases (Just Pretend, The More You, What Would It Be Like If…). Power words with no argument under them is Pattern 8's failure mode.

**11 — A FOREST ship-check**: **A**lliteration · **F**acts · **O**pinions · **R**epetition · **E**xamples · **S**tatistics · **T**hree. Missing registers get one targeted addition each, never a rewrite.

## Quality Gate

- **Argument survives formula deletion.** Strip the mnemonic and the contention still closes — structural form of **Hard Veto 3** — fail otherwise.
- **Provenance.** Every filled slot traces to a mined artifact. Violation = **Hard Veto 1, No blank-page copy** — fail.
- **Emulation legality.** Form only; no competitor's words and structure copied together — fail on lifted phrasing.
- **Bullet craft.** Feature + Benefit + Benefit-of-that-benefit, layer 3 as imagination trigger. Stated layer 3 = **Hard Veto 5** — fail.
- **Real stakes.** Warning and scarcity steps describe genuine consequences and genuine limits. Manufactured urgency = **Hard Veto 2** — fail.
- **Claim labeling.** Borrowed statistics (the 5:1 claim, MECLABS' heuristic, third-party open rates) carry VERIFIED / LIKELY / UNCONFIRMED. Violation = **Hard Veto 6** — fail.
