## Pre-Flight Gate

1. **SMART objective exists** (Pattern 7 — Objective Before Tactics): "In this [medium] I want to convince my reader that [specific feature] is better for accomplishing [specific task] than their current method…" No objective, no formula selection — you'd be optimizing for cleverness.
2. **The argument stands before the scaffold.** Pattern 2: you can state the piece's single contention in one sentence. A formula sequences an argument; it does not manufacture one. If there's no Acknowledgement/Aggravate pair, go back to workflow 02 (Contrarian Copy Structure).
3. **Mining pass done** (Hard Veto 1): headline and bullet banks exist from real sources. Formulas are slots; mined language is what fills them.
4. **Depth score inherited.** Pull the depth calibration from workflow 09 — it decides reveal-depth by medium (email → straight to CTA · sales page → benefit outline then buy-CTA · content → tactical depth then soft-sell).

## Execution

**Step 1 — Classify the deliverable.** Vosler groups by *what are you writing today*, not by persuasion theory. Route:

- Long-form sales page → **Frey 12-Step** (default) · Bob Stone **Seven-Step** · **AICPBSAWN** · **Belcher 21-Part** (only steps 1–10 printed; 11–21 gated on his site) · **HELLYEAH**
- Short page / low-ticket → **SLAP** (Stop → Look → Act → Purchase; Vosler's own aside: "act" and "purchase" arguably shouldn't be separate steps)
- Desire beat keeps collapsing → **AIDPPC** (Collier): Attention → Interest → **Description** → **Persuasion** → **Proof** → Close — AIDA's vague "Desire" split into three workable slots
- Lead-gen page → **Jack Lacey 5 Questions**: What will you do for me if I listen? · How will you do it? · Who's responsible for these promises? · Who have you done this for? · What will it cost me?
- VSL → **Jim Edwards 10-step** · **12-step 3-CTA template** · **P.A.S.T.O.R.**
- Email drip → **Walling 5-Day** · **6-Email Nurture** · **Wishpond 5-Part** · **PASOP**
- Ads → **ERERS** · **MECLABS** · Loud.Relevant.Engaging · Wordstream lines
- Blog / content → **Hyatt 6-Part** · **HIPASI**
- Testimonials → **Before-After-Experience** interview + **4 Ss** + **TEASE**
- Value proposition → the positioning template + **VAD**

**Step 2 — Long-form: Frey 12-Step mapped to the 7 objection types.** Frey's premise is that copy exists to clear buying resistance, and resistance arrives as seven internal monologues. Steps: **1** Get Attention → **2** Identify the Problem → **3** Provide the Solution → **4** Present Your Credentials → **5** Show the Benefits → **6** Give Social Proof → **7** Make Your Offer → **8** Inject Scarcity → **9** Give a Guarantee → **10** Call to Action → **11** Give a Warning → **12** Close With a Reminder.

Objection mapping — write each step *at the objection it answers*, not generically:

| Objection (reader's line) | Type | Steps that must carry it |
|---|---|---|
| "You don't understand my problem" | empathy | 2 (Identify the Problem), 6 (Social Proof from someone like them) |
| "How do I know you're qualified?" | authority | 4 (Credentials) |
| "I don't believe you" | logical | 6 (Social Proof), 5 (Benefits made specific and provable) |
| "I don't need it right now" | urgency | 8 (Scarcity), 11 (Warning) |
| "It won't work *for me*" | ego | 6 (Social Proof matched to their segment), 5 |
| "What happens if I don't like it?" | risk | 9 (Guarantee) |
| "I can't afford it" | value | 7 (Offer), plus Trivialize Price from the offer-close |

Motivation theory attached: we buy on emotion and justify with logic *after*. Only two things motivate — promise of gain or fear of loss — and **loss is stronger** (Frey's supporting 5:1 "Stop Your Divorce" vs. "Improve Your Marriage" claim is unattributed industry lore; label **UNCONFIRMED** if you repeat it). Beneath both sit the 8 universal motivations: be wealthy, be good looking, be healthy, be popular, have security, achieve inner peace, have free time, have fun. Vosler's own annotation is the operating instruction: *"The deceptive simplicity of this framework is what excites me… DIG DEEP into each step."* Each label is a sub-exercise, never a checkbox.

**Step 3 — Close with the canonical offer stack.** The same 8-step sequence appears twice in the book under two names; treat it as Vosler's default: **Here's What You Get → Establish Value → Conditional Bonus → Price → Trivialize Price → Guarantee → Risk Reversal → Scarcity.** Trivialize against a daily habit ("less than a latte"), and note that the price reveal is where the anchoring two-stack from §10 belongs.

**Step 4 — VSL routing.** *Jim Edwards 10-step*: shocking statement → problem and why it matters → agitate → push agitation to deep emotion → solution → credibility → proof → biggest things they get → reasons not to procrastinate → close asking for the purchase while reinforcing benefits. *12-step 3-CTA template*: greeting → problem + promise → **scarcity established early, before the pitch** → agitate → your solution as THE solution → features+benefits → **CTA #1 keyed to desire** → credentials/proof/testimonials → guarantee → **CTA #2 keyed to logic** → deadline/FOMO reprise → **CTA #3 keyed to FOMO**. *P.A.S.T.O.R.*: Person/Problem/Pain → Amplify consequences → Story, Solution, System → Testimonials → **Offer — spend ~80% of this section on the transformation, not the mechanics** (Vosler's inline tip: "Good tip.") → Response.

**Step 5 — Email drips.** *Walling 5-Day*: Day 0 on signup (welcome + what the drip covers + a reply-CTA) → Day 1 educate, end on an action → Day 2 theory via story, CTA in the P.S. → Day 3 batch of actionable tips → Day 4 case study with real numbers, CTA to the solution. *(The printed day offsets contain an internal inconsistency around Days 3–4; transcribe as printed and flag rather than silently correcting.)* *6-Email Nurture*: welcome + support contact → free offer → case study → ROI examples → testimonial video → FAQs. *Wishpond 5-Part*: warm hello → transparent case study/finding → personal business story → case study → free trial or soft sell. *PASOP* is the chaining engine: each email runs Problem → Agitation → Solution → Outcome → **new Problem**, and the new problem opens the next email; email 3 collapses to PAS and links the sales page. Use PASOP whenever sequence completion matters more than any single email.

**Step 6 — Ads.** *ERERS*: Emotional → Rational → Emotional → Rational → Social proof. *MECLABS*: `ea = 2at + i + as` — effectiveness equals twice attention plus interest plus the click-ask; the formula's argument is that the attention element is worth double either other component, so budget your line-count accordingly. Structural rule for search/display: Headline = attract attention (be relevant) · Description 1 = generate interest (be useful) · Description 2 = ask for the click (show value).

**Step 7 — Blog / content.** *Hyatt 6-Part*: headline → lead paragraph hook → relevant image → personal story → scannable body → open-ended question. *HIPASI*: Headline → Image → Problem → Agitation → Solution → Invitation.

**Step 8 — SCAMPER remix pass** (run on anything that already exists — winners, competitor swipes, a dead draft): **S**ubstitute a ho-hum phrase for something surprising · **C**ombine successful elements from two or more sources · **A**dapt a winning headline/button/offer from a different category · **M**odify, minify or magnify one element · **P**ut it to use with unexpected people · **E**liminate an element that's seen better days · **R**earrange, reverse or redefine any part of the copy or funnel. SCAMPER is a mutation tool, not a from-scratch generator.

**Step 9 — Support assets.** *Testimonials*: run **Before-After-Experience** as the literal interview script (what hesitations before? what did you discover after? what did it feel like?), then bar-check with the **4 Ss** (Specific, Short, Sizzling, Signed) and **TEASE** (tactful? emphasizes a particular strength? shares an authentic part of the experience? short and sweet? engages the reader?). *Value prop*: "For {target} who {need/opportunity}, {Name} is a {category} that {benefit}", or **VAD** (Verb–Application–Differentiator): "We do X, but the difference is {differentiator}" / "We help X do Y by doing Z" / "{Proven industry example} for {new domain}."

**Step 10 — Word banks at point of use only.** Resistance words (Imagine, Suppose, Picture, Remember, Realize…), Influential (Discover, Proven, Guaranteed, Results…), Power (~40: breakthrough, sure fire, insider, the truth about…), Subconscious phrases (Just Pretend, The More You, What Would It Be Like If…). Drop them at the slot that needs them; a page salted with power words and no argument is Pattern 8's failure mode.

**Step 11 — A FOREST ship-check.** Before delivery, verify the piece hits: **A**lliteration · **F**acts · **O**pinions · **R**epetition · **E**xamples · **S**tatistics · **T**hree (rule of three). Missing registers get one targeted addition each — never a rewrite.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Deliverable | Primary formula | Objection load | Ship-check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long-form sales page (cold) | Frey 12-Step + canonical 8-step offer close | All 7 types explicitly slotted | A FOREST + staged FAQ near the close |
| VSL / webinar close | Edwards 10-step or 12-step 3-CTA; PASTOR when short | Risk + urgency carried by CTAs 2–3 | A FOREST; 80%-transformation rule on the offer |
| Email drip | PASOP (chained) or Walling 5-Day | Empathy first, value last | Each email ends on a live open loop |
| Paid ad | ERERS; MECLABS weighting | Ego + logical, compressed to one line | Attention element double-weighted |
| Blog / content marketing | Hyatt 6-Part or HIPASI | Empathy + authority only | Open-ended question present |
| Lead-gen page | Jack Lacey 5 Questions | Authority + value | All five questions answered in order |
| Short / low-ticket page | SLAP | Value only | One decision, no tiers past three |
| Testimonial set | Before-After-Experience + 4 Ss + TEASE | Ego + logical | Signed, specific, short |

## Quality Gate

- **Argument survives formula deletion.** Strip the mnemonic scaffolding and the contention still closes. Failure here is **Hard Veto 3, No bias play without a standalone message** in its structural form — fail.
- **Provenance.** Every filled slot traces to a mined artifact (review, subtitle, live ad, client raw language). Violation = **Hard Veto 1, No blank-page copy** — fail.
- **Emulation legality.** No competitor's words and structure copied together — form only. Fail on any lifted phrasing.
- **Bullet craft (rubric).** Bullets read Feature + Benefit + Benefit-of-that-benefit, with layer 3 delivered as an imagination trigger. A stated layer-3 benefit = **Hard Veto 5** — fail.
- **Stakes are real.** Warning and scarcity steps describe genuine consequences and genuine limits. Manufactured urgency = **Hard Veto 2, No fear-mongering** — fail.
- **Claim labeling.** Every borrowed statistic (the 5:1 loss-aversion claim, MECLABS' heuristic, third-party open-rate numbers) carries VERIFIED / LIKELY / UNCONFIRMED. Violation = **Hard Veto 6** — fail.
