# 05 — Headline Forge (The Imitation Game)

## Pre-Flight Gate

1. **Hard Veto 1 — no blank-page copy.** A mining pass must exist before a single headline is drafted. Aggregator captures, Amazon sub-titles, "most helpful" reviews, or live long-running ads — one of these surfaces, in a doc, with sources. No mining artifact = stop and mine. Client's own raw language counts (the "message in a bottle" method: copy is drawn out of existing material by a good question, never invented).
2. **Objective before tactics (Pattern 7).** The SMART objective sentence exists in writing before line one: *"In this [medium] I want to convince my reader that [specific feature] is better for accomplishing [specific task] than their current method. To do this I will show specific evidence, share specific case studies, and appeal to [xyz] emotions."* Free-write first if stuck, then throw it away and write the objective fresh.
3. **Format routed before drafting (Hidden Knowledge — format governs depth-of-reveal).** Email / sales page / content piece decided now, because it sets what the post-headline intro does.
4. **Ask is a family, not a line.** If the brief says "give me a headline," you deliver the family anyway (Signature Move: modifier swap on one skeleton).

## Execution

**Step 1 — Brief.** Write the objective (above) plus the desired action in one line: what the reader DOES after reading. Name the audience identifier you'll use — the literal words the target would use to describe themselves, pulled from mined language, not from a persona doc.

**Step 2 — Research the aggregators.** digg, reddit, news.google, medium, buzzfeed, cracked. **Mine STRUCTURE, not topic** — "focus less on what's being said, and more on how it's structured." Capture 10-20 headlines verbatim into the working doc with source links. Always mine through a ranking lens (front page, top-voted, best-seller rank, ad run-length) — recency alone is not validation.

**Step 3 — Formulate the skeleton.** Strip a captured headline to its slot grammar. Vosler's worked example: `"The [noun] Who Perfected The [topic]"`. Sub-title variant: `"A [adjective] Approach to [enticing promise]"`. Keep the grammar, discard every word of the original — emulate FORM, never words and structure together.

**Step 4 — Develop (interrogate why it works).** For each skeleton, answer in one sentence: does it conjure curiosity, confirm a suspicion, open a loop, promise powerfully, question reality, provoke thought? Name the mechanism. An un-interrogated skeleton gets discarded — you can't swap modifiers on a machine you can't explain.

**Step 5 — Adapt.** Plug your audience and topic into the skeleton. Check against the **master formula (p37): Attention Grabbing Headline = "Audience Identifier" + "Hook" + "Benefit"** — all three present, or the line is not finished.

**Step 6 — Expand ("feed the need").** Apply **Modifier = End Result + Time Frame** to the modifier slot, then bolt on a consequence clause answering "what's in it for them if they keep reading." Vosler's chain: *"A Counterintuitive Approach to Living a Good Life"* → `"A [adjective] Approach to [enticing promise]"` → *"A [Uniquely Powerful] Approach To [Making Millions Short Selling Homes]"* → *"…And Why Many Agents Are Missing Out Big Time."*

**Step 7 — Mint the family (the modifier swap).** One skeleton, four keyed variants, each labeled with its lever:
- **Base** — End Result + Time Frame (the clean promise).
- **Fear of Loss** — what the reader forfeits by not acting; loss-flavored rewrite ("How to Avoid Easy-to-Miss Writing Mistakes That Are Costing You Sales").
- **Fear of Change** — disarms the cost of switching; frames the offer as an addition, not a replacement (Hidden Knowledge: don't sell change, sell addition).
- **Fear of Unknown + Hope** — names the murk, then points at the lit path.
Deliver all four. Each carries its psychological lever named in-line so the test is readable.

**Step 8 — Sub-title mining variant (run when the aggregator pass is thin).** Amazon best-sellers in-niche → collect sub-titles (the promise carrier — "Don't judge a book by its cover… judge by its subtitle") → tag each by persuasion mode: Contrarian / Intriguing / Inspiring / Powerful Promise → abstract to skeleton → repopulate → expand with the consequence clause. Tag the mode on each output so the family spans registers, not just modifiers.

**Step 9 — Volume pass (optional, on request or when the family stalls).** Run the **32-prompt Imitation Game worksheet** against the niche: Ask a question / Reference current events / Create Your Own Terms / Reveal News / Command / Give Stats / Comparison / Promise Useful Info / Direct Offer / Quick Story / Recommendation / State Benefit / Testimonial / Arouse Curiosity / Reveal a Secret / Ultra Specific / Target Segment / Time Based / Urgency-Scarcity / Good News / Challenge Reader / Guarantee / Price-as-benefit / Contradiction / Address Objection / "As Crazy As It Sounds" / Promised Land / Demonstrate ROI / Reason Why / List-Answer Questions / Cost Saving / Cost of Mistakes / Reader's Goals. One line per prompt; harvest the survivors back into Step 7.

**Step 10 — Write the sub-headline and the Even-If opener.** Jobs are split (p29): **Headline = Capture Attention + Frame Problem · Sub-headline = Hint at Solution + Address Main Objection.** Then open the intro with the literal words **"Even if…"** answering the jugular objection — the one that loses the most audience — before the illusory-superiority reflex ("maybe for them, but not me") can fire. Route the post-intro: Email → straight to CTA · Sales page → benefit outline → buy CTA · Content → tactical depth → soft-sell CTA.

**Step 11 — Function checklist.** Every shipped headline does ≥1 of: Conjure Curiosity / Provoke Thought / Confirm Suspicion / Create Intrigue / Promise Powerfully / Question Reality — **and** pulls the reader down the page. A headline that scores on the list but doesn't pull is a slogan, not a headline.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Content type | Skeleton source to prefer | Modifier emphasis | Post-headline routing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales page | Amazon sub-titles + long-running ads | End Result + Time Frame, then Fear of Loss | Even-If intro → benefit outline → buy CTA |
| Cold email / subject line | Aggregator structures (curiosity + open loop) | Fear of Unknown + Hope; keep the line short | Even-If intro → straight to CTA |
| Content piece / blog | Aggregator structures; 32-prompt "Reveal a Secret" / "Contradiction" | Base + Fear of Change (low-pressure) | Tactical depth → soft-sell CTA |
| Paid social ad | Live ads sorted engagement-then-Running-Longest | Fear of Loss primary, loss-framed rewrite | Compressed: hook + one benefit + CTA |
| Webinar / registration page | Sam-Ovens-style hook bullets from review mining | Base + Fear of Loss; open-loop, unresolved | Bullet hooks that only close on registration |
| Lead magnet / opt-in | Review-mined self-talk statements | Fear of Change (addition, not replacement) | Even-If intro → single soft CTA |

## Output Requirements

1. The SMART objective sentence and desired action, stated up top.
2. The mining artifact: captured sources with links, and the extracted skeleton(s) with the "why it works" sentence for each.
3. **The headline family** — minimum 4 variants off one skeleton, each with (a) the psychological lever named, (b) audience identifier / hook / benefit labeled inline, (c) the function-checklist item it hits.
4. Sub-headline for each shipping candidate (hints at solution + addresses the main objection).
5. The Even-If opener paragraph for the intro that follows, with the jugular objection named.
6. Any 32-prompt run attached as an appendix, not as the deliverable.
7. Provenance line: every headline traced to its mined artifact.

## Quality Gate

- **PASS/FAIL — Hard Veto 1 (no blank-page copy):** every headline traces to a named mined artifact. Zero invented persona language. One untraceable line fails the batch.
- **PASS/FAIL — Family, not a line:** ≥4 modifier-keyed variants off one skeleton, each with its lever named. A single "best" headline delivered alone is a failed run.
- **PASS/FAIL — Master formula (rubric: Argument spine / Language provenance):** Audience Identifier + Hook + Benefit all present and labelable in every shipping candidate.
- **PASS/FAIL — Function checklist + pull:** each headline hits ≥1 of the six functions AND pulls down the page.
- **PASS/FAIL — Hard Veto 2 (no fear-mongering):** Fear-of-Loss and Fear-of-Unknown variants name REAL, likely risks. Manufactured or improbable doom = rejected variant, rewritten toward the deeper real risk ("a lifetime of unfulfillment in a dead-end job," not "you might fail"). Keep the monsters real.
- **PASS/FAIL — Hard Veto 6 (no unverified claims as fact):** any number, stat, or timeframe inside a headline is VERIFIED, or it comes out. Vosler flags his own unsourced stats in print; channeled output does the same.
