# Sean Vosler — Named Frameworks & Skeletons (verbatim-faithful reference)
> Every skeleton below is decoded from *7 Figure Marketing Copy* V.5. Page cites = printed footers. Full context: `extractions/sean-vosler-7fmc/harvest/`.

## 1. Headline Machinery (Imitation Game, Methods 1-2)

**Headline/Sub-headline jobs (p29)**: Headline = Capture Attention + Frame Problem · Sub-headline = Hint at Solution + Address Main Objection.

**Master formula (p37)**: Attention Grabbing Headline = "Audience Identifier" + "Hook" + "Benefit".

**6-Step Forming Headlines process (p30-35)**: 1 Brief (objective + desired action) → 2 Research aggregators (digg, reddit, news.google, medium, buzzfeed, cracked — mine STRUCTURE not topic) → 3 Formulate (extract the skeleton, e.g. "The [noun] Who Perfected The [topic]") → 4 Develop (interrogate WHY it works: curiosity? belief-confirmation? open loop?) → 5 Adapt (plug audience + topic) → 6 Expand ("feed the need": **Modifier = End Result + Time Frame**; variants keyed to Fear of Loss / Fear of Change / Fear of Unknown + Hope → one skeleton yields a testable headline FAMILY).

**Headline function checklist (p37-38)**: must do ≥1 of Conjure Curiosity / Provoke Thought / Confirm Suspicion / Create Intrigue / Promise Powerfully / Question Reality — AND pull the reader down the page.

**Sub-title mining (pp44-47)**: Amazon best-sellers in-niche → collect subtitles → tag persuasion mode (Contrarian / Intriguing / Inspiring / Powerful Promise) → abstract to skeleton ("A [adjective] Approach to [enticing promise]") → repopulate + expand with consequence clause.

**The Even-If opener (p40)**: intro starts literally "Even if [jugular objection]…" — disarms illusory-superiority reflex before it fires. Post-intro routing: Email → straight CTA · Sales page → benefit outline → buy CTA · Content → tactical depth → soft-sell CTA.

**32-prompt Imitation Game worksheet (p301+)**: run all 32 mechanism prompts against one niche until reflexive (Ask a question / Reference current events / Create Your Own Terms / Reveal News / Command / Give Stats / Comparison / Promise Useful Info / Direct Offer / Quick Story / Recommendation / State Benefit / Testimonial / Arouse Curiosity / Reveal a Secret / Ultra Specific / Target Segment / Time Based / Urgency-Scarcity / Good News / Challenge Reader / Guarantee / Price-as-benefit / Contradiction / Address Objection / "As Crazy As It Sounds" / Promised Land / Demonstrate ROI / Reason Why / List-Answer Questions / Cost Saving / Cost of Mistakes / Reader's Goals).

**SMART objective template (p42)**: "In this [medium] I want to convince my reader that [specific feature] is better for accomplishing [specific task] than their current method. To do this I will show specific evidence, share specific case studies, and appeal to [xyz] emotions." Ritual: free-write → trash it → one objective → fresh draft.

## 2. Research Arbitrage (Methods: Amazon R&D, Community Arbitrage, Advertising Anatomy)

**Community Arbitrage (pp59-67)**: 1 Pick adjacent bestseller on Amazon → 2 Read "most helpful" reviews (top ~10 into a doc) → 3 Two-color highlight (YELLOW = inner dialogue/self-talk; RED = emotional pull) → 4 Extract each Statement → rephrase as (a) Statement hook, (b) empathy bullet: "[Empathetic question echoing their self-talk]? [Solution] helps you [benefit], without [objection/cost]." → 5 Assemble: Headline + Soft CTA sub-head + 4 bullets + Hard CTA (+ authority quote). Rule: "If it's true, and it's a benefit to the reader, it's worth sharing."

**Bullet formula (p60)**: **(Feature) + (Benefit) + (Benefit of that benefit)**. Hook-bullet variant for register pages: short open-loop curiosity bullets (Sam Ovens style).

**Advertising Anatomy (pp49-57)**: objective (SMART) → AdvertSuite keyword search (Ad Text / Comments / Landing Page Text) → sort by engagement then Running Longest → filters progressively → capture per ad (6-item checklist: full screenshot, verbatim text doc, landing link, landing screenshot, more-info screenshot, top comments) into Trello (columns=keywords, cards=ads, params logged) → run the **30-question Advertising Emulation checklist** per ad (target, funnel goal, price, attention grabber, benefits, engagement driver, mood, dream inspired, thesis, story, contrarian view, emotions, lessons, CTA, warnings, facts, authority, desires, enemy, pains, entertainment, objections handled, repetition, associations, fears+hopes, effective/ineffective…). Legal rule: emulate FORM, never words+structure together.

## 3. Persuasion Architecture (The A's of Influence + Craft of Persuasion)

**A's spine (p126)**: Appeal-powered **Attention → Attitude → Arousal → Action**; 15-term glossary as levers: Attention, Appeal, Acknowledgement, Arousal, Angst, Aspirations, Attitude, Authority, Ambiguity, Ascension, Affirm, Associate, Aggravate, Animate, Action. Use as diagnostic checklist on any existing piece.

**Craft of Persuasion — 5 Foundations (p138, adapted from Blair Warren)**, each mapped to an A:
1. **Fan The Flame** ~ascension: fan the dream-gap, pair belief with education + tools ("inspire rebellion against self-doubt… no need to promise the world, just solve their problem"). Positive-emotion levers: increase understanding (esp. about themselves), support passionate views, paint vivid possibility, pair fear with the relief of knowing.
2. **Forgive Faults** ~aspirations: 4-step forgiveness script (acknowledge false self-belief → context why not alone → forgive: situational ≠ identity → invite to leave it behind). Ego-morphing: every purchase reinforces an ego facet at every price point — "Do Not Assume You And Your Customer Have All The Same Motivations or Desires." Lighthouse mini-template: define the storm / the light / the path.
3. **Defeat Demons** ~aggravate: **Myth Repellant 6-step** (empathize both views → claim → evidence → manage objections → beneficial conclusion → repeat). **6 Socratic Frames** for copy: Clarification / Probing Assumptions / Probing Reasons+Evidence / Analyzing Perspectives (riskiest) / Probing Consequences (branching storyline) / Questioning the Question (own your self-interest, then the bigger motive). Evidence standard: "if you make a claim, you need at least one convincing piece of logic or fact"; bigger claim → more evidence. "Bring them to a conclusion, not give them one."
4. **Prove Presumptions** ~acknowledgement (teased; treated via evidence standards above).
5. **Attack The Enemy** ~animate: three enemy types — **The Struggle** (4-step: list ALL features → connect each to a struggle → write the felt experience → rank), **The Value Battle** (Apple "Get a Mac": define enemy → shared context + values foundation + self-selecting buyers; pick 3 brand values), **The Boogie-Man** (template: "The [problem with][common belief] of x is not y, it's z." — new enemy from the dark). Tension rule: escalate local → universal (Master Plots). "Keep the monsters real."

**Mistakes Research Document (pp155-158)** — standing artifact, 5 steps: DEFINE THE MISTAKES (≥3 harmful beliefs; search "[keyword] mistakes") → RAISE THE STAKES (cascading consequences) → THE FORGIVENESS (internal + external reasons; fallback "they just didn't know") → GRAND ILLUMINATION (immediate benefit → extrapolate) → WHAT'S NEXT (steps; ≥1 ties to offer; depth governed by format).

## 4. The Contrarian Copy Structure (Framework 2 — his flagship)

**7 beats (p186)**:
1. **ATTENTION** — claim + hook; position the problem; allegory/metaphor into the reader's head.
2. **ACKNOWLEDGEMENT** — what most people think (need not be wrong — "just not as good as your option").
3. **AGGRAVATE** — the truth/claim that cannot coexist with the old belief ("a match to gasoline").
4. **AUTHORITY** — why it's true: scientific research, logical examples, emotional examples (emotional evidence counts).
5. **ANGST** — damage of the false belief; "problem of the problem" list; where continued belief leads.
6. **AMBIGUITY** — the fix: what to do now, specific steps, beliefs changed, how to self-remind in hard times.
7. **APPEAL** — offer transition; benefits summary + CTA.

**Preconditions (p192)**: perceived choice + meaningful stakes (both required). **Epiphany chain (p188)**: acknowledge current perspective → introduce deeper perspective → [belief disharmony] → realization → lasting trust. **Resolution tree (p194)**: change belief (→ behavior = win / "right but don't care" = missing empathy or stakes) · dispute (→ revise argument) · import outside ideas (→ pre-refute). **Applications (p185)**: sales pages, emails (~1,000 written, "trackable 40m"), webinars, content marketing, cornerstone content, social long-form, ads. **Benchmark**: 9.4% conversion, 5,687 cold visitors.

**4-Gate Diagnostic Toolkit (p200)**: stakes → logic → agency (sub-argument vs. no-control belief) → counter-evidence; clear all four = belief internalized.

## 5. Storytelling (Framework 3 — "let me tell you a story…")

Story = **experiential transference** ("a multiplication of information"); use for cold/uninterested audiences (facts serve the pre-interested). Governing rule: audience is the protagonist; brand/product are characters in THEIR narrative. Rip-tide beat structure: sudden threat → failed struggle → escalating danger → climax tied to the practical prop → remembered-wisdom resolution → named payoff ("you've given them wisdom, not knowledge"). NOTE: the copy-application pages (printed 212-225) are absent from the V.5 PDF — do not invent Vosler-attributed application mechanics beyond this.

## 6. The Unlikely Hero (Framework 4 — audience research)

Reframe: "You are not the hero, your audience is." Two uses: research tool + campaign generator (segments sit at different phases simultaneously). **Depth calibration (3 variables)**: familiarity, active search, bottom-line impact ($12 thermos vs. $20K coaching). **Master per-phase template (p239)**: audience's internal/external world → your guide role → emotions to encourage/mitigate → tactical assets for the funnel. **Phase 1 Ordinary World**: accepted world vs. dream world; 6-question bank (dream worlds harbored / why putting off / emotional fears / unconscious hopes / past positive experiences) + Character Sketch & Day-in-a-Life exercise (before/after/in-between, 2-3 paragraphs). **Phase 2 Call to Adventure**: "stoke the nagging voice of change"; put TRUTHS in front of the hero — Category 1 (the dream is reachable for you) / Category 2 (what's actually been holding you back — the villain); drawn from Phase-1 answers. Change psychology: sell addition over change; hope > fear; don't ask for too much change early.

## 7. Teach, Transform, Transact (Framework 5) + KLTCC

TEACH (challenge + educate; neuroplasticity grounding — unlearn the defective belief) → TRANSFORM (the eureka moment; "You don't need a website, you need an online lead generation funnel"; be the last vendor) → TRANSACT (benefit-of-the-benefit; imagination trigger — never state layer 3). **Bloom's Taxonomy as outline tool**: diagnose audience level → one deliverable-outcome sentence per level ascending → evaluate the draft ascended. **Know. Like. Trust. Challenge. Convert.** — and the **5-email KLTCC sequence** (pp267-271): each email = worksheet questions + worked example; beats: Know (welcome, humanize, value-first video, cliffhanger PS) → Like (universal problem, vulnerability, withhold solution) → Trust (raw low-point story → contrast → mindset shift → engagement CTA) → Challenge (persona parable "Sarah" → self-diagnostic choices → systematize reframe) → Convert (resolve cliffhanger → smarter-solution triplet → social-proof stack → trivialized price + scarcity + bonus stack + #1-reason block + risk reversal).

## 8. Research Pipeline ("Gold Ring", pp277-283)

Research = "distilling data into knowledge, knowledge into principles, principles into actionable wisdom." 5 stages: mine broadly (no filtering) → first separation (relevant vs. adjacent-for-later) → smelt with a PRE-SET standard (guards: research-loop time-sink, missed vitals, disinformation) → refine into structure/outline → craft into a purpose-matched, brand-packaged format ("a gold ring crafted by Cartier is worth much more than the raw gold" — format & brand multiply the same research).

## 9. Pricing & Positioning (feast. + Coal Calling + Value Ascension)

**Vendor→growth-partner reframe**: "Good clients don't care what you do, they care about how 'what you do' affects their bottom line"; everything traces to increasing sales or reducing risk; "what are they actually buying?" **Do-the-math**: baseline → growth delta → ~10% share of the DELTA = your worth → fee lands trivial ("Would you be comfortable charging $20,000+ if you knew it had a $1M upside?"). **Coal Calling**: "Sell the right thing to the right person and you won't have to sell at all" — math-makes-sense filter before pursuit; position before pitch. **Value Ascension Strategy**: P1 Tactical Guide ($24-49, one narrow problem) → P2 Strategic Guide ($199-499, transformation); target math: 1,000 visits/wk × 8% × $24 + 20% take × $199 = $5,104/wk ≈ $20,416/mo. 4 steps: Compass (quality commitment) → Map (reusable asset/marketing list) → Vessel (credibility, partners, voice/brand, distribution — "rowboat is fine") → Execute. **Reverse Product Development**: write the marketing BEFORE building the product; floor: "great marketing applied to a crappy product is just adding saffron to a turd." **Dung-beetle doctrine**: "The Right Strategy Can Beat The Biggest Advantages" — match strategy to resources (capital / distribution / time+experience).

## 10. Bias Arsenal (Cognitive Manipulation, decoded set)

Governing rule: **biases are an accelerant, not the engine.** Ethical gate: only if you believe in the offer's real value.
- **Affect Heuristic**: swing valence, land net-positive right before CTA; negatives attach to competition/inaction, positives to the offer; subtle > overt.
- **Choice Overload + Rule of Two**: ≤3 tiers, visual options, minimal form fields; never one option — frame the conscious binary ("Sign up today" vs. "No, I'm not interested in…").
- **Forer/Barnum**: "you're the kind of person who…" (must be true of the target; non-targets self-select out) + Tests & Surveys segmentation (quiz → 3 tiers → tailored validate-then-pitch message).
- **Framing — 7 tactics**: Contextual Savings ("less than a cup of coffee") · Picking the Right Number (good→big frame, bad→small frame; "80% fat-free") · Savings or Penalty (both at once) · Good vs. Evil · Us vs. Everyone Else · Unique in a Sea of the Same · "Because I'm Worth It".
- **Prospect Theory / Loss Aversion**: losses ≈ 2x gains; primary emphasis on losses of inaction; tactics: kill unused coupon fields, loss-frame bonuses, live scarcity tech, order bumps, strikethrough features, loss-flavored rewrites ("How to Avoid Easy-to-Miss Writing Mistakes That Are Costing You Sales"); **2:1 benefit-to-risk evidence ratio**; **Staged FAQ**: validate → answer → reframe.
- **Anchoring two-stack**: expensive familiar comparator → "even at that price it's justified" → reveal.
- **Bandwagon**: phrase bank ("Thousands Have Figured Out…"), testimonials everywhere, live social proof.
- **Commitment/Choice-supportive**: pre-CTA testimonials, post-sale affirmation ("Great Choice!"), unannounced bonus near refund deadline.
- **Pro-Innovation**: [Feature]+[Benefit]+[Innovation framing]; calibrate "innovative" to the audience's frame, not the market's.
- **Mere Exposure**: IMPACT + FREQUENCY + TIME; retargeting, tolerate frequency, singular big idea repeated; devices: Anaphora/Epistrophe (MLK/Dickens/Churchill models).
- **Nostalgia ("Pain from an Old Wound")**: 3 levels — company branding (narrow-cohort only, heritage brands), product branding (vintage treatment; buyer needn't have lived the era), campaign level (visuals/music + the **Invoking Shared Nostalgic Values** 6-line story skeleton: "A lot has changed, but, like you, we still believe…" → "that ___ is still true" → "It may seem old fashioned…" → "Remember when…?" → "Wouldn't it be great if…?" → "Well that's why we…"). Handle with respect — deep nostalgia craft, not retro-font pandering.
