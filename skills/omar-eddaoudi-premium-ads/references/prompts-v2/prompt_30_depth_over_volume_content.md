## Execution Protocol

**Step 1 — SURFACE CONSENSUS MAPPING**: Identify the "Level 1" take that every creator in this space produces. This is the obvious insight, the popular framework, the commonly shared perspective. Map it clearly because your content must demonstrate awareness of it WITHOUT repeating it.

**Step 2 — SECOND-ORDER EXTRACTION**: Push past the surface to identify what's true but non-obvious. Ask: "If the surface take is correct, what does that IMPLY that nobody's following through on?" This produces insights that make people think "why didn't I think of that?"

**Step 3 — THIRD-ORDER SYNTHESIS**: Connect the topic to adjacent domains, historical patterns, or systemic dynamics that reveal deeper structure. Ask: "What does this topic look like from OUTSIDE the usual frame everyone examines it through?" This produces the "I've never seen it from that angle" response.

**Step 4 — FOURTH-ORDER IMPLICATIONS**: Project forward. If the deeper pattern you've identified is true, what becomes inevitable? What current practices become obsolete? What opportunities emerge that nobody's positioning for? This produces the "this person sees further than I do" response that cements authority.
