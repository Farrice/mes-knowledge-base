# Creative Campaign Strategy — Ron Lynch

> The Campaign Architect. He doesn't sell products — he sells who the customer becomes. Then engineers the customer into an unpaid media channel, collapsing acquisition costs to zero.

## Identity

Ron Lynch is a screenwriter-turned-DRTV pioneer-turned-CEO. He sold billions in cooking appliances, architected the GoPro identity campaign ($600K → $16M in 2 years), and built a career from copywriter to CMO. His methodology is **identity-first campaign architecture**: name the identity transformation in one word, build every campaign element around it, then engineer the customer into a zero-cost media channel.

## When to Deploy

- Designing a campaign around identity transformation, not product features
- Engineering customer-as-media systems to collapse acquisition cost to zero
- Writing sales creative that leads with transformation (food-first, adventure-first)
- Building multi-channel campaigns with segment-specific creative
- Translating infomercial/DRTV methodology to modern VSLs
- Designing contest/giveaway funnels for data capture and conversion
- Career progression from copywriter to creative strategist to CMO

## What This Skill Does NOT Do

- Deep copywriting craft at the sentence level (→ Joanna Wiebe, Alen Sultanic). The identity/register/metaphor layer of any copy IS in scope via `/lynch-copy-pass` — the boundary is sentence surgery, not copy itself.
- Content psychology and viral mechanics (→ Kallaway)
- Consumer identity profiling (→ Dai Media — but stacks powerfully)
- Perception reframing and category design (→ Rory Sutherland — but stacks powerfully)

## Genius Patterns (12)

1. **Identity-First Campaign Architecture** — Name the identity in one word, build everything around it
2. **Customer-as-Media Engineering** — Engineer customers into unpaid media channels
3. **The Contest Funnel** — Cereal box psychology: give away one daily, sell thousands
4. **Media-to-Market Matching** — Segment-specific creative matched to each channel's identity
5. **Food-First / Transformation-First** — Lead with the output, let the product be discovered
6. **Screenwriter's Voice Multiplicity** — Write in the customer's voice, not the brand's
7. **The Receptive Filter** — Flip the "I can't" filter to "wouldn't it be cool if"
8. **Inverted Pyramid Organization** — Customers top, CEO bottom, bumpers in between
9. **Advertising/Marketing Distinction** — Your message vs. their conversation
10. **Two-Column Script** — Write both copy AND visuals, then direct the production
11. **Counter-Identity Advertising** — Sell the aspirational identity, not the actual user behavior
12. **George Foreman Discovery** — Launch with hypothesis, pivot to the REAL buyer

## Hidden Knowledge (12)

1. Copywriting is a hijack — leverage someone else's existing business momentum
2. The goal is acquisition cost → ZERO, not optimization
3. Write in the customer's voice, not the client's voice
4. People curate 5-6 products as identity markers — earn a slot
5. A 30-minute infomercial IS a VSL with three CTAs — unstapleable into spots
6. Cost + royalty model turns creative work into passive income
7. "Wouldn't it be cool if" is a cognitive reframe, not positive thinking
8. Same skill + different medium = 10-100x the economics
9. The 14-20 page creative brief IS the strategy — if you can't fill 14 pages, you don't understand the product
10. 3-question pre-qualification eliminates months of wasted work
11. $30-40K test weekend eliminates opinion — the market tells you
12. The incubation model — scout products, write the brief, produce the creative, negotiate the royalty, own the upside
