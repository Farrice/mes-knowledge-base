# /lynch-royalty-model — Royalty-Based Creative Pricing

> Structure creative compensation as royalties (2-5% of attributed sales) rather than fixed fees. Align incentives so the creative team profits when the campaign works.

## When to Use
- Pitching creative services to a brand or client
- Structuring compensation for a campaign you designed
- When fixed-fee pricing undervalues your creative contribution
- When you want to prove your creative delivers measurable revenue
- When transitioning from agency model to performance-based model

## Inputs Required
1. Campaign type and scope (what you're creating)
2. Client's projected revenue or sales volume
3. Attribution model (how creative contribution will be tracked)
4. Your current pricing model and rates
5. Client relationship history and trust level

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: Value Mapping
Map the creative's revenue contribution using Ron Lynch's framework:
- What is the product's total addressable revenue?
- What percentage of sales can be DIRECTLY attributed to creative?
- What percentage can be INDIRECTLY attributed (brand lift, CAC reduction)?
- Total creative attribution = direct + (indirect × discount factor)

### Step 2: Royalty Structure Design (GP-8)
Design the royalty tier:

| Sales Volume | Royalty Rate | Your Revenue |
|---|---|---|
| First $1M | 5% | $50K |
| $1M-$5M | 3% | $120K |
| $5M-$10M | 2% | $100K |
| $10M+ | 1.5% | Variable |

- Include a minimum guarantee (base fee + royalty upside)
- Cap the royalty at a defined ceiling if needed for client comfort
- Include audit rights and transparent reporting

### Step 3: Attribution Framework
Define exactly HOW revenue will be attributed to creative:
- Direct response: Track clicks, conversions, attributed sales
- Brand campaigns: Agree on lift metrics and measurement periods
- Hybrid: Base attribution percentage + performance bonus triggers

### Step 4: Client Pitch Structure
Frame the royalty model as CLIENT-advantageous:
- "I only get paid more when YOU make more" (aligned incentives)
- "My base is lower than my standard fee" (reduced upfront risk)
- "I'm betting on my own creative" (confidence signal)
- "You get a creative partner, not a vendor" (long-term alignment)

### Step 5: Contract Mechanics
Specify the contract elements:
- Reporting frequency (monthly/quarterly)
- Payment terms (30/60/90 days after reporting)
- Term length and renewal conditions
- Exclusivity clauses (if applicable)
- Termination and tail provisions

## Output Format
```
## ROYALTY MODEL PROPOSAL — [Client/Campaign]

### Value Map
[Total addressable revenue + creative attribution percentage]

### Royalty Structure
[Tiered rate table with projected revenues]

[Base fee + royalty calculation]

### Attribution Framework
[How revenue is tracked and reported]

### Client Pitch Deck
[4-5 slides: aligned incentives, reduced risk, confidence signal, partnership]

### Contract Terms Summary
[Reporting, payment, term, exclusivity, termination]

### Risk Scenarios
[Best case, expected case, worst case revenue for both parties]
```

## Quality Gate
- Royalty rate must be between 1-5% (Ron Lynch's proven range)
- Must include a minimum guarantee — never pure royalty
- Attribution framework must be specific and auditable
- Client pitch must frame royalties as CLIENT-advantageous
- Risk scenarios must be modeled for both parties
- Score ≥7 on Commercial Architecture in the Ron Lynch Quality Rubric
