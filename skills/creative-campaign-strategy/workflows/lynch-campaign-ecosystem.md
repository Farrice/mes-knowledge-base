# /lynch-campaign-ecosystem — Full Campaign Ecosystem Design

> Design a full campaign ecosystem where identity → contest → media-matched creative → customer-as-media tools form a self-reinforcing loop. The GoPro playbook.

## When to Use
- Building a comprehensive campaign from scratch (not a single ad)
- When you need all elements to compound and reinforce each other
- Product launches where the goal is exponential growth, not linear spend
- After running `/lynch-identity-campaign` and wanting the full system

## Inputs Required
1. Identity word (from `/lynch-identity-campaign`)
2. Product details (cost of goods, retail price, margin)
3. Available channels and media budget
4. Customer-as-media feasibility assessment
5. Timeline and growth targets

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: Ecosystem Architecture
Map all four layers of the Ron Lynch campaign ecosystem:

```
Layer 1: IDENTITY (one-word transformation)
    ↓
Layer 2: CONTEST (cereal box psychology — data + education + conversion)
    ↓
Layer 3: MEDIA-MATCHED CREATIVE (segment-specific for each channel)
    ↓
Layer 4: CUSTOMER-AS-MEDIA TOOLS (templates, music, handles)
    ↓
[Loop back to Layer 1 — each customer reinforces the identity for others]
```

### Step 2: Identity Layer
Confirm or establish the one-word identity:
- Identity word: ___
- Product naming/framing aligned to identity
- Visual language defined
- Voice calibration complete

### Step 3: Contest Layer (GP-3)
Design the daily giveaway mechanism:
- Product giveaway: 1 unit/day at cost of goods (365/year)
- Data capture: Email, preferences, demographics
- Product education: After entry, show full product range
- Conversion psychology: "I'll never win but I really want this"
- Economics: 365 × cost of goods = total contest investment

### Step 4: Media-Matched Creative Layer (GP-4)
Design segment-specific creative for each channel:
- Identify 5-10 audience identity segments
- Create a unique creative execution for each segment
- Match the story/activity/lifestyle to the channel's audience
- The viewer sees THEMSELVES before they see the product

### Step 5: Customer-as-Media Layer (GP-2)
Design the tool kit:
- Templates for customer content creation
- Licensed audio/visual assets
- Professional reference material
- Branded distribution channels
- Recognition/featuring mechanism

### Step 6: Compounding Economics
Model how the layers compound:
| Layer | Cost | Value Generated |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Creative development time | Brand differentiation |
| Contest | 365 units at cost of goods | Data + education + direct sales |
| Media creative | Production × number of segments | Per-channel ROAS uplift |
| Customer-as-media | Tool kit development | Zero-cost acquisition volume |

Calculate: At what point does the ecosystem become self-sustaining?

## Quality Gate
- All four layers must be present and interconnected
- Contest economics must be calculated (not estimated)
- At least 5 segment-specific creative briefs included
- Customer-as-media tool kit must have 3+ concrete items
- Must show how each layer feeds the others (not independent tactics)
- Score ≥7 on Campaign Ecosystem in the Ron Lynch Quality Rubric
