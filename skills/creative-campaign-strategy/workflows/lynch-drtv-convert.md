# /lynch-drtv-convert — DRTV-to-Digital Conversion Engine

> Take the proven principles of direct response television (sell the result, demo the transformation, compress the proof) and convert them into digital-native creative formats.

## When to Use
- Converting DRTV methodology into digital ads (Meta, YouTube, TikTok)
- Learning from infomercial structure for VSL or landing page design
- When digital creative lacks urgency, demonstration, or proof density
- When the product benefits from live demonstration or before/after

## Inputs Required
1. Product/service to sell
2. Target platform (Meta, YouTube, TikTok, landing page, VSL)
3. Available demonstration footage or capability
4. Existing proof/testimonials
5. Price point and offer structure

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: DRTV Principle Extraction
Identify which DRTV principles apply to this product:
- **Demonstration supremacy**: Can you SHOW the transformation?
- **Problem agitation → solution reveal**: Can you make the pain vivid?
- **Compressed proof**: Can you stack 3-5 testimonials in rapid sequence?
- **Price anchoring**: Can you anchor against a higher perceived value?
- **Urgency mechanics**: What legitimate scarcity or time pressure exists?

### Step 2: Format Translation (GP-5)
Translate DRTV structure into digital format:

| DRTV Element | Digital Translation |
|---|---|
| 30-min infomercial | 3-5 min VSL or long-form YouTube ad |
| Live demonstration | Product demo clip (15-60 sec) |
| Testimonial montage | UGC compilation or story-format testimonials |
| "But wait, there's more" | Offer stack reveal on landing page |
| "Call now" CTA | Click-through urgency with countdown |
| Price drop reveal | Strikethrough pricing with anchored comparison |

### Step 3: Attention Architecture
Design the digital attention curve:
- **Hook (0-3 sec)**: Transformation result shot (the "food," not the "appliance")
- **Agitation (3-15 sec)**: The problem they're experiencing now
- **Demonstration (15-45 sec)**: Show the product creating the transformation
- **Proof (45-75 sec)**: Compressed testimonial stack
- **Offer (75-90 sec)**: Price reveal with anchor + CTA

### Step 4: Platform-Specific Adaptation
Adjust the structure for the target platform:
- **Meta (15-30 sec)**: Hook → demo → CTA (skip agitation)
- **YouTube (60-180 sec)**: Full DRTV structure, compressed
- **TikTok (15-60 sec)**: Hook → result → "link in bio"
- **VSL (5-15 min)**: Full DRTV structure with identity layer
- **Landing page**: DRTV structure as scrollable page sections

### Step 5: Digital-Native Enhancement
Add what DRTV couldn't do:
- Retargeting sequences (segment by watch time)
- Dynamic creative testing (swap hooks, demos, CTAs)
- Pixel-based attribution (know exactly what converts)
- Comment section social proof (real-time testimonials)
- Influencer/creator versions (third-party credibility)

## Quality Gate
- Must identify at least 3 DRTV principles that apply
- Digital structure must have specific timestamps, not vague descriptions
- Platform adaptations must be genuinely different (not the same ad resized)
- At least one digital-native enhancement must be included
- Script must follow transformation-first structure (no feature-leading)
- Score ≥7 on Channel Translation in the Ron Lynch Quality Rubric
