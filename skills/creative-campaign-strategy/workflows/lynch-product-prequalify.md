# /lynch-product-prequalify — 3-Question Product Pre-Qualification

> Before investing 40+ hours in a creative brief, run Ron Lynch's 3-question triage to determine if a product is worth your creative firepower. Most creatives waste months on products that fail question one.

## When to Use
- Before accepting any new client engagement
- Before writing a creative brief for any product
- When a potential client pitches you their product
- When scouting products for incubation (Bonfire Enterprises model)
- When deciding between multiple opportunities

## Inputs Required
1. Product/service description (what it does, how it works)
2. Current pricing (retail, wholesale, cost of goods)
3. Any existing marketing (website, ads, testimonials)
4. Competitive alternatives (what else solves this problem)
5. Demo potential (can you SHOW it working?)

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: The 3-Question Gate

Answer each question with brutal honesty. All three must pass.

**Question 1: Is the USP "Super Unique"?**
Not "kinda different" — SUPER unique. The bar is:
- Can someone describe what makes this different in ONE sentence?
- Would a stranger watching a demo say "I've never seen anything like that"?
- Does the differentiation survive the "So What?" test three times?
  - "It does X." → "So what?" → "That means Y." → "So what?" → "That means Z."
  - If Z isn't compelling, the USP isn't unique enough

Score: Pass / Conditional / Fail

**Question 2: Does the pricing support media economics?**
The math must work at scale:
- What's the retail price?
- What's the cost of goods (COGS)?
- What margin remains after COGS? (Need ≥60% for DRTV, ≥70% for digital)
- Can you afford $30-60 per customer acquisition and still profit?
- Does the price point support impulse buying ($19.99-$99.99) or justify consideration ($100+)?
- If subscription/recurring: what's the projected LTV at 3, 6, 12 months?

Score: Pass / Conditional / Fail

**Question 3: Does it need explanation that a demonstration can provide?**
This is the hidden qualifier — if the product is self-explanatory (commodity), you don't need a creative strategist. If it's confusing (too complex), demo won't help.
- Can you demo the product in under 90 seconds?
- Does the demo produce a visible "wow" moment?
- Is the demo result BETTER than the customer expects?
- Would the demo work on a skeptic? (Not just an enthusiast)

Score: Pass / Conditional / Fail

### Step 2: The Red Flag Scan

Even if all 3 questions pass, check for deal-killers:
- **Supply chain fragility**: Can they actually fulfill at scale?
- **Regulatory risk**: FDA, FTC, or industry compliance issues?
- **Founder ego**: Will the client let you control the creative, or do they want to "approve every word"?
- **Capital position**: Can they fund a $30-40K test weekend?
- **Competition timing**: Is a funded competitor about to launch something similar?

### Step 3: Market Potential Sizing

For products that pass the gate:
- **Addressable market**: How many people could buy this?
- **Category velocity**: Is this category growing, flat, or declining?
- **Identity slot potential**: Could this product earn one of the customer's 5-6 identity slots? (HK-4)
- **Media channel fit**: Which channels match this product's identity? How many?
- **Repeat purchase**: Is this a one-time or recurring purchase?

### Step 4: Engagement Recommendation

Produce one of four verdicts:

| Verdict | Meaning | Next Action |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **GREEN LIGHT** | All 3 pass, no red flags, strong market | → `/lynch-customer-voice-mine` then `/lynch-creative-brief` |
| 🟡 **CONDITIONAL** | 2/3 pass or minor red flags | Specify exactly what must change before proceeding |
| 🔴 **PASS** | Any critical fail or major red flags | Decline politely. Don't invest creative capital |
| ⚪ **INCUBATION** | Strong product, weak business — YOU bring the strategy | → Structure as equity/royalty deal via `/lynch-deal-structure` |

## Output Format
```
## PRODUCT PRE-QUALIFICATION — [Product Name]

### 3-Question Gate
1. USP Uniqueness: [PASS/CONDITIONAL/FAIL] — [evidence]
2. Pricing Economics: [PASS/CONDITIONAL/FAIL] — [margin analysis]
3. Demo Power: [PASS/CONDITIONAL/FAIL] — [demo assessment]

### Red Flag Scan
[List any flags found, or "Clear"]

### Market Potential
- Addressable market: [size]
- Category velocity: [growing/flat/declining]
- Identity slot potential: [high/medium/low]
- Media channel fit: [channels identified]
- Repeat purchase: [yes/no + frequency]

### VERDICT: [GREEN LIGHT / CONDITIONAL / PASS / INCUBATION]
[Rationale for verdict]

### Recommended Next Step
[Specific workflow to chain, or specific conditions to revisit]
```

## Quality Gate
- All 3 questions must have explicit PASS/CONDITIONAL/FAIL with evidence — no ambiguity
- Red flag scan must be completed even if all 3 questions pass
- Market potential sizing must include at least addressable market and identity slot potential
- Verdict must be one of the four defined categories with clear rationale
- If CONDITIONAL, must specify exact conditions for advancement
- Must recommend next workflow in chain
