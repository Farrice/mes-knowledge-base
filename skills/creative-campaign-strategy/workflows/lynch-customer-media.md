# /lynch-customer-media — Customer-as-Media Engineering

> Engineer the customer into an unpaid media channel. Design the shareable moment, remove broadcast friction, provide tools. Goal: acquisition cost → zero.

## When to Use
- After identity is established (post `/lynch-identity-campaign`)
- When customer acquisition costs are unsustainably high
- When the product has naturally visible/shareable usage
- When building a UGC-driven growth engine
- When transitioning from paid-media dependency to organic growth

## Inputs Required
1. The identity word from `/lynch-identity-campaign`
2. Product usage description (what does the customer physically DO with it?)
3. Current customer acquisition cost and channel mix
4. Existing UGC or organic mentions (if any)
5. Budget for tool/template creation

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: Shareable Moment Design
Identify what the customer will DO with this product that others want to see:
- What's the natural post-purchase behavior?
- Is it visually interesting? Emotionally resonant? Identity-reinforcing?
- If usage isn't naturally shareable → DESIGN the shareable moment

Rate the shareable moment on Ron Lynch's spectrum:
- **10/10**: Usage IS the content (GoPro extreme sports footage)
- **7/10**: Usage produces shareable output (cooking appliance → food photos)
- **4/10**: Usage must be framed as shareable (SaaS → share the result/dashboard)
- **1/10**: Usage is private/invisible → requires significant engineering

### Step 2: Friction Audit
Map every barrier between experience and broadcast:

| Barrier | Solution Design |
|---|---|
| "I don't know how to create content" | Templates |
| "I don't have music/assets" | Licensed asset library |
| "It doesn't look professional" | Professional reference examples |
| "Where would I post?" | Branded hashtags + submission mechanism |
| "Why would I bother?" | Contest + identity reinforcement + featuring |

### Step 3: Tool Kit Design
Specify exactly what the brand must provide:
- **Templates**: What visual/editing templates do customers need?
- **Audio**: What music/sound can be licensed for customer use?
- **References**: What professional examples can customers model?
- **Distribution**: What hashtags, handles, and submission channels exist?
- **Recognition**: How will top UGC be featured/rewarded?

### Step 4: Contest/Incentive Layer (GP-3)
Design the cereal box psychology mechanism:
- Daily giveaway at cost of goods (365 units/year)
- Entry gated behind data capture + product range exposure
- "I'll never win but I really want this" conversion psychology
- Contest entries double as content submissions

### Step 5: Flywheel Mapping
Map the self-reinforcing loop:
```
Purchase → Use → Create content → Post → New customer sees → Purchase
```
Calculate: At what UGC volume does organic > paid acquisition?

## Quality Gate
- Must identify a specific shareable moment, not a generic "share your experience"
- Tool kit must have at least 3 concrete items (templates, audio, references)
- Friction audit must address every barrier between experience and broadcast
- Flywheel economics must project specific CAC reduction timeline
- Score ≥7 on Customer-as-Media in the Ron Lynch Quality Rubric
