# /lynch-identity-campaign — Identity Transformation Campaign

> Design a campaign around an identity transformation — not product features. Name the identity in one word, build all creative around it.

## When to Use
- Launching a new product or brand campaign
- Repositioning an existing product from feature-selling to identity-selling
- Entering a commoditized market where the product alone doesn't differentiate
- Before any other Lynch workflow (this is the foundation)

## Inputs Required
1. Product/service description (what it does, who it's for)
2. Current positioning (how it's currently sold/described)
3. Target audience (who buys it now, who SHOULD buy it)
4. Competitive landscape (what alternatives exist)
5. Any existing customer testimonials, reviews, or UGC

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: Identity Audit
Map the customer's aspirational self-image using Ron Lynch's framework:
- What 5-6 products currently define their social media persona?
- What identity GAP exists that this product could fill?
- What does the customer BECOME when they use this product?
- What would they put in their social media bio after using it?

### Step 2: One-Word Naming
Reduce the identity transformation to a single word:
- List 10 candidate identity words
- Score each on: aspirational pull (1-10), brand fit (1-10), one-word clarity (1-10)
- Select the highest-scoring word — this becomes the creative North Star
- Test: Does every creative decision become OBVIOUS when filtered through this word?

### Step 3: Counter-Identity Check (GP-11)
Map reality vs. aspiration:
- What does the TYPICAL user actually do with this product?
- What does the TOP 5% of users do?
- Design the identity around the top 5%, not the median
- The gap between reality and aspiration = where the sale lives

### Step 4: Identity Cascade
Build the full cascade from the one-word identity:
- **Product naming/framing**: How does the identity word reshape what we call this?
- **Visual language**: What imagery reinforces this identity?
- **Copy voice**: What words does someone with this identity use?
- **Social proof**: What customer stories reinforce this identity?
- **CTA framing**: What action feels like JOINING this identity?

### Step 5: Identity Validation
Stress-test the identity against Ron Lynch's quality rubric:
- Would the customer post about this product? (If not → identity too weak)
- Could a competitor claim this same identity? (If yes → not specific enough)
- Does the identity make the product SECONDARY to the transformation? (Must be yes)
- Can every team member explain the identity in one word? (Must be yes)

```

### Identity Audit

[The gap this product fills]

### Counter-Identity Analysis

- Aspirational identity: [top 5% behavior]
- The gap that sells: [reality → aspiration]

### Identity Cascade
- Product framing: [how to name/describe it]
- Visual language: [imagery direction]
- Copy voice: [tone, vocabulary, POV]
- Social proof: [what stories to tell]
- CTA: [action as identity joining]

### Campaign Elements (3-5)
[Specific creative concepts that reinforce the identity]

### Recommended Next Workflow
[/lynch-campaign-ecosystem, /lynch-media-match, or /lynch-customer-media]
```

## Quality Gate
- Identity MUST be a single word — not a phrase, not a tagline
- The product must be secondary to the transformation in every creative element
- Counter-identity analysis must show the gap between reality and aspiration
- Must recommend the next Lynch workflow to chain
- Score ≥7 on Identity Clarity in the Ron Lynch Quality Rubric
