# /lynch-identity-validated — Identity-Validated Campaign (Lynch × Dai Media)

> Stack Ron Lynch's identity-first campaign architecture with Dai Media's Consumer Posture Framework. Build campaigns grounded in WHO the customer IS, not who you WISH they were.

## When to Use
- When the identity word from `/lynch-identity-campaign` needs psychological validation
- When the campaign targets a niche where assumptions about the customer are risky
- When previous campaigns have underperformed due to wrong audience assumptions
- When building a campaign for a new market with limited customer data

## Inputs Required
1. Identity word (from `/lynch-identity-campaign`)
2. Product/service description
3. Target audience (broad definition)
4. Any existing customer data, surveys, or feedback
5. Competitor positioning (who else targets this audience?)

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: Consumer Posture Profile (Dai Media)
Build the 3-dimensional consumer posture:
- **Occupation dimension**: What they do for work and how it shapes identity
- **Activity dimension**: What they do for leisure and how it signals identity
- **Thought Process dimension**: How they make decisions and what they value

Apply the Kristen Stewart Test: Can you name 5-6 products this person buys that DEFINE them on social media?

### Step 2: Identity Validation
Cross-reference the Lynch identity word against the posture profile:
- Does the identity word match what the consumer ALREADY aspires to?
- Or is it an identity the BRAND wants to impose?
- If misaligned → revise the identity word to match the posture

### Step 3: Counter-Identity Mapping (GP-11 + Dai Media)
Combine Lynch's counter-identity check with Dai Media's radical individual:
- **The real person**: What does the TYPICAL customer actually do?
- **The posture**: What image do they PROJECT to their network?
- **The aspiration**: What identity GAP exists?
- **The campaign opportunity**: The gap between posture and aspiration

### Step 4: World Design (Dai Media)
Build the Dai Media "World" around the validated identity:
- What WORLD does this product invite the customer into?
- Is this world designed around emotional outcome, not problem-solving?
- Does the world feel like a place the customer ALREADY wants to be?

### Step 5: Campaign Build (Lynch)
With validated identity and designed world, build the campaign:
- Identity cascade using the VALIDATED identity word
- Media-matched creative using the consumer posture segments
- Customer-as-media tools designed for the world (not just the product)
- Contest/incentive layer aligned with posture-appropriate motivation

## Quality Gate
- Consumer posture MUST use all 3 Dai Media dimensions
- Identity word must be VALIDATED (not assumed) against posture data
- Counter-identity map must show real behavior vs. projected posture
- World design must be emotion-first (not problem-solution)
- Both expert contributions must be explicitly documented
- Score ≥7 on both Identity Clarity AND Audience Truth
