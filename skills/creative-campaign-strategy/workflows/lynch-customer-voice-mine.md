# /lynch-customer-voice-mine — Customer Voice Mining Engine

> "How do I write inside the head of the one receiving this?" Ron Lynch's entire methodology hinges on writing in the CUSTOMER'S voice — not the brand's voice, not the expert's voice. This workflow extracts the raw material you need before writing a single word of the creative brief.

## When to Use
- Before writing any creative brief (MANDATORY pre-step)
- Before any Lynch copywriting or campaign workflow
- When existing copy "sounds like the brand" instead of "reading my mind"
- When you don't know how your customer ACTUALLY talks about their problem
- When customer research feels surface-level (demographics, not psychology)

## Inputs Required
1. Product/service to sell
2. Target customer description (even rough)
3. Access to ANY of: customer reviews, testimonials, support tickets, Reddit threads, Amazon reviews of competitors, YouTube comments, forum posts, social media comments
4. Competitor ads/marketing (what's the existing voice in this market?)
5. Any customer interviews or survey data

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: The Voice Source Harvest

Mine real customer language from these sources (minimum 3):

| Source | What You Extract | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Amazon reviews (3-star)** | Nuanced complaints and praise | 3-star reviews are the most honest — they liked it enough to use it but have real complaints |
| **Reddit/forum threads** | Unfiltered language, slang, metaphors | People write to peers, not brands — this is how they ACTUALLY talk |
| **YouTube comments** | Emotional reactions, "this is me" moments | Video comments reveal identification patterns |
| **Support tickets/complaints** | Pain language, frustration vocabulary | The words they use when they're ANGRY reveal their deepest needs |
| **Social media comments** | Identity performance, aspiration language | What they POST reveals who they want to BE |
| **Competitor reviews** | Unmet needs, switching triggers | Why they left the last solution = why they'll come to yours |
| **Interview transcripts** | Pauses, self-corrections, tangents | Where they stumble = where the real emotion lives |

### Step 2: The Character Wallet

Build a psychological profile of ONE specific customer (not a segment — a person):

**External Layer (What They Show)**
- Name (create a specific person): ___
- Age, location, occupation: ___
- Social media persona (what 5-6 products define their identity?): ___
- How they describe themselves to strangers: ___
- The image they're curating: ___

**Internal Layer (What They Think)**
- Internal monologue when they encounter THIS problem: ___
- The words they use when complaining to a friend (not a brand): ___
- The metaphors they reach for: ___
- What they've already tried that didn't work: ___
- What they SECRETLY believe about why nothing works: ___

**Aspiration Layer (What They Want to Become)**
- Who do they want to be in 6 months?: ___
- What would they post on social media if this problem was solved?: ___
- What identity would they claim? (GP-1): ___
- What would make them tell a friend? (GP-2): ___
- What's the "wouldn't it be cool if" fantasy? (GP-7): ___

### Step 3: The Voice Extraction Matrix

From your harvested sources, extract and categorize:

**Pain Vocabulary** (exact phrases they use)
- Top 5 phrases describing the problem: ___
- Top 3 emotional words (frustrated, exhausted, embarrassed, etc.): ___
- The ONE sentence that captures their pain most vividly: ___

**Desire Vocabulary** (exact phrases for what they want)
- Top 5 phrases describing the ideal outcome: ___
- Top 3 aspiration words (confident, free, effortless, etc.): ___
- The ONE sentence that captures their desire most vividly: ___

**Skepticism Vocabulary** (how they express doubt)
- Top 3 "I've heard that before" phrases: ___
- What they think when they see an ad for this category: ___
- The internal objection they'd never say to a salesperson: ___

**Sophistication Level** (Ron's chess-to-checkers principle)
- How much do they already know about this category? (Naive / Aware / Sophisticated)
- What jargon do they use vs. what jargon confuses them?
- Are they buying for the first time or replacing something?

### Step 4: The Voice Match Test

Write 3 test sentences — one pain, one desire, one skepticism — in the customer's voice. Then score:

| Test | Sentence | Score (1-10) |
|---|---|---|
| **Pain** | "[Your sentence in their voice]" | Would they say "that's EXACTLY how I feel"? |
| **Desire** | "[Your sentence in their voice]" | Would they say "that's EXACTLY what I want"? |
| **Skepticism** | "[Your sentence in their voice]" | Would they say "that's EXACTLY what I'm worried about"? |

If any score is below 7, return to Step 1 and harvest more source material. The voice isn't ready.

### Step 5: The Brief-Ready Voice Card

Package the voice into a reference card that fuels the creative brief:

## Output Format
```
## CUSTOMER VOICE CARD — [Product/Campaign Name]

### The Character
[Name, age, occupation, identity stack]

### Their Internal Monologue
[3-5 sentences written AS the customer, capturing their thought process when they encounter this problem]

### Voice DNA
- Pain vocabulary: [top 5 phrases]
- Desire vocabulary: [top 5 phrases]
- Skepticism vocabulary: [top 3 phrases]
- Sophistication level: [naive/aware/sophisticated]
- Metaphors they use: [the analogies they reach for]
- Words they NEVER use: [jargon that would break the voice]

### The One Sentence
[The single most vivid sentence that captures this customer's inner world — pain AND desire — in their exact language]

### Voice Match Scores
- Pain match: [score]/10
- Desire match: [score]/10
- Skepticism match: [score]/10

### Identity Opportunity (→ /lynch-identity-campaign)
- The identity they want to claim: [one word]
- What they'd post if transformed: [the social media moment]
- The 5-6 product stack slot this could earn: [where it fits]

### Recommended Next Workflow
[/lynch-creative-brief — Voice Card is now the #1 input]
```

## Quality Gate
- Minimum 3 voice sources harvested with actual customer language (not made up)
- Character wallet must be a specific person, not a demographic segment
- Voice extraction matrix must use REAL phrases from harvested sources
- All 3 voice match test sentences must score ≥7/10
- The "One Sentence" must be writable in the customer's voice, not the brand's voice
- If the voice match scores are below 7, the workflow LOOPS — do not proceed to the brief
- Sophistication level must be explicitly identified (naive/aware/sophisticated)
- Must chain to `/lynch-creative-brief` as next step
