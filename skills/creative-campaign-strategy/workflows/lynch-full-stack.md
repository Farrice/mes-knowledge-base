# /lynch-full-stack — Full-Stack Campaign Deployment (Lynch × Cole × Wiebe)

> Stack Ron Lynch's campaign ecosystem with Nicolas Cole's product ladder and Joanna Wiebe's conversion copy. Build a campaign that attracts, converts, AND monetizes across multiple price points.

## When to Use
- Deploying a complete go-to-market campaign (not just creative)
- When the campaign needs to drive revenue across a product ladder
- When creative, copy, and commerce must work as a unified system
- Maximum deployment: the culmination of all Lynch workflows

## Inputs Required
1. Full campaign ecosystem (from `/lynch-campaign-ecosystem`)
2. Product ladder (from Cole methodology or existing)
3. Conversion copy needs (landing pages, emails, sales pages)
4. Revenue targets and timeline
5. Available resources (team, budget, tools)

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: Campaign Ecosystem Review (Lynch)
Confirm all four layers are active:
- ✅ Identity (one-word transformation)
- ✅ Contest (data capture + education + conversion)
- ✅ Media-matched creative (segment-specific)
- ✅ Customer-as-media tools (templates, assets, handles)

### Step 2: Product Ladder Integration (Cole)
Map the campaign to a multi-tier product ladder:

| Tier | Product Type | Price | Campaign Layer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Lead magnet / contest entry | $0 | Contest layer |
| Low | Digital download / starter kit | $27-97 | Post-contest offer |
| Mid | Course / membership / service | $197-497 | Nurtured email sequence |
| High | Premium service / coaching | $997+ | Identity-qualified leads |

- Each campaign layer feeds the NEXT tier of the ladder
- Contest entries → low-ticket → mid-ticket → high-ticket

### Step 3: Conversion Copy Layer (Wiebe)
Write conversion copy for each transition point:
- **Contest → Low-ticket**: Wiebe Level 1-2 copy (desire + trust)
- **Low → Mid-ticket**: Wiebe Level 3-4 copy (mechanism + urgency)
- **Mid → High-ticket**: Wiebe Level 5 copy (full persuasion hierarchy)

Apply Wiebe's voice-of-customer methodology:
- Mine contest data and UGC for exact customer language
- Use customer words in copy (not brand voice)
- Match sophistication level to each tier's audience

### Step 4: Revenue Architecture
Design the revenue flow:
```
Campaign Impressions → Identity Attraction → Contest Entry
    → Data Capture → Product Education → Low-Ticket Conversion
    → Email Nurture → Mid-Ticket Conversion
    → Qualification → High-Ticket Conversation
    → Customer Success → UGC → [Loop to Campaign Impressions]
```

Calculate:
- Expected conversion rate at each tier
- Revenue per 1,000 campaign impressions
- Customer lifetime value across the full ladder
- Payback period on campaign investment

### Step 5: Deployment Timeline
Phase the deployment:
- **Week 1-2**: Identity + contest launch
- **Week 3-4**: Media-matched creative deployment
- **Week 5-6**: Low-ticket conversion optimization
- **Week 7-8**: Email nurture sequence activation
- **Week 9-12**: Mid/high-ticket conversion + UGC flywheel scaling

## Quality Gate
- All four Lynch ecosystem layers must be present and active
- Product ladder must have at least 3 tiers with specific products and prices
- Conversion copy must use Wiebe's persuasion hierarchy (not generic copy)
- Revenue architecture must include specific conversion rate assumptions
- 12-week deployment must be phased (not "launch everything at once")
- All three expert contributions must be explicitly documented
- Score ≥7 across Identity, Conversion, and Commercial Architecture
