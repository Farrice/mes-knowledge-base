# /lynch-strategist-career — Creative Strategist Career Architecture (Lynch × Cole × Wiebe)

> Full career architecture from copywriter → creative → creative director → creative strategist → CMO/founder. The Ron Lynch career path with Nicolas Cole's product thinking and Joanna Wiebe's writing career economics.

## When to Use
- When a copywriter wants to evolve into a creative strategist or CMO
- When transitioning from freelance writing to strategic creative leadership
- When building a portfolio that demonstrates campaign thinking, not just copy chops
- When pricing creative services and considering the cost + royalty model
- When the career feels stuck at "good copywriter" and needs the next level

## Inputs Required
1. Current career position (copywriter, creative, director, strategist, other)
2. Current skill set (what you CAN do vs. what you're PAID to do)
3. Portfolio inventory (what work demonstrates which level?)
4. Income structure (flat fees, hourly, retainers, royalties)
5. Career ambition (where you want to be in 2-5 years)

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: Career Level Diagnosis (Lynch)
Identify your current level on the Ron Lynch progression:

| Level | Title | What You Do | What You Write | Income Model |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Copywriter** | Write words for others' ideas | Left column only (copy) | Per-word / per-project |
| 2 | **Creative** | Write words AND visuals | Both columns (copy + visual direction) | Per-project, higher rate |
| 3 | **Creative Director** | Direct the production of your scripts | Both columns + direct shoots | Day rate + project fee |
| 4 | **Creative Strategist** | Design entire campaign ecosystems | Campaign architecture, not just scripts | Retainer + performance |
| 5 | **CMO / Founder** | Own the business outcome | Strategy + P&L ownership | Cost + royalty / equity |

Honest assessment: Where are you? Where does your PORTFOLIO prove you are?

### Step 2: Skill Gap Analysis — "Write Both Columns" (Lynch GP-10)
The critical unlock from Level 1 → Level 2:

| Skill | Level 1 (Copy Only) | Level 2 (Both Columns) | Your Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visual thinking** | "Someone else handles visuals" | "I direct what the camera sees" | ✅/❌ |
| **Production knowledge** | "I don't know how shoots work" | "I write shot lists and direct" | ✅/❌ |
| **Format fluency** | "I write long-form copy" | "I write scripts, storyboards, social, email" | ✅/❌ |
| **Business acumen** | "I don't know the margins" | "I design creative around unit economics" | ✅/❌ |
| **Voice multiplicity** | "I write in my voice" | "I write in the customer's voice" | ✅/❌ |

Each ❌ = a specific skill to develop before advancing.

### Step 3: Business Acumen Layer (Lynch — Required for Levels 4-5)
The creative strategist/CMO must understand the business:
- **Sales mix**: Which products generate what percentage of revenue?
- **Margin analysis**: What's the gross margin on each product?
- **Unit economics**: What's the CAC, LTV, and payback period?
- **Supply chain basics**: What are the cost-of-goods drivers?
- **Labor economics**: What does production actually cost?

Ron Lynch's rule: "You can't be a CMO if you don't know the P&L. The creative strategist who understands sales mix, margin, and supply chain can run 4-5 companies simultaneously."

### Step 4: Portfolio Architecture (Lynch + Cole)
Design a portfolio that PROVES your level:

| Level | Portfolio Piece | What It Demonstrates |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Individual copy samples | "I can write" |
| 2 | Two-column scripts with visual direction | "I can create complete creative" |
| 3 | Campaign case studies with production notes | "I can direct creative teams" |
| 4 | Full campaign ecosystems with results | "I can design systems that sell" |
| 5 | Business outcomes tied to creative decisions | "My creative generates P&L impact" |

Apply Nicolas Cole's vehicle selection: Your portfolio IS your product. Package it as:
- **Free**: 1-2 case studies on LinkedIn/personal site
- **Mid-ticket**: Full campaign breakdown as a consulting deliverable
- **High-ticket**: The campaign ecosystem as a retainer service

### Step 5: Income Architecture (Lynch + Cole + Wiebe)
Design the income evolution:

**Level 1-2: Wiebe's writing career economics**
- Position as a specialist, not a generalist
- Solve measurable business problems (funnel fix, not "brand voice")
- Price based on business impact, not word count

**Level 3-4: Cole's product thinking**
- Productize your methodology (campaign frameworks, audit processes)
- Build a "tangible faucet" — repeatable deliverables that scale
- Create IP that generates recurring revenue (templates, courses, frameworks)

**Level 5: Lynch's royalty model**
- Cost + royalty structure: "I'll do it for [base] and a little royalty"
- Client thinks it's cheap. You get 2-5% of attributed sales
- One 4-day shoot → $20-30K/month for 5 years
- Negotiate audit rights and transparent reporting
