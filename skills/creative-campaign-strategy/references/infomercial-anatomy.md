# The 28:30 Infomercial Anatomy — Ron Lynch (Source 2, 2026-07-19)

The complete structure of a long-form show, from `extractions/ron-lynch/transcript-2-dOM.txt` (Marketing Misfits). This is the skeleton under `/lynch-drtv-vsl` and `/lynch-modular-script` — the show is a legal case argued to a channel-surfing jury.

## The Clock (28 minutes 30 seconds)

| Time | Block | Function |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-2:00 | **The Opener / Tease** | "I'm going to show you what I'm going to show you" — the lawyer's opening statement: the problem, the promised proof, the crazy stuff coming |
| 2:00-8:00 | **Act One** | Host + co-host introduce problem, solution, 2-3 demonstrations |
| ~6:30-8:00 | **Testimonials (90s)** | The relay (see below) |
| 7:00-8:00 | **First commercial (3 min)** | Retells the story, presents offer + bonuses, CTA |
| 8:00-21:00 | **Act Two** | Start over for channel-surfers, but drill DEEPER: the second layer of objections, more demos, more proof — "It's just a legal case" |
| 21:00 | **Same commercial again** | Identical 3-min offer block, CTA |
| ~25:00 | **The Radical Demonstration** | The over-the-top payoff they stayed for (Billy Mays doctrine: "make it over the top and outlandish cuz that's good TV") |
| 27:00-28:30 | **Wrap + 30s commercial** | "By now we've told you everything we can tell you" + final CTA |

Design constraint: viewers arrive mid-show while channel-surfing — every act must work as an entry point, and staying longer must keep raising purchase probability (the sunk-time close, HK-13).

## The Demonstration Doctrine

- Sequence: **benefit with wonder → tinged by desire → promise of proof**. (Ultimate Chopper: guacamole/salsa side-by-side → omelet → grate cheese → then grind concrete, glass, bricks to prove toughness.)
- Start from the FOOD (the result), walk the common-use cases, END on the outlandish proof demo.

## The Testimonial Relay (GP-14)

Five people, one story, told in snippets: cape → basket → woods → house → wolf. Sequential fragments read as unanimous experience. Full workflow: `/lynch-testimonial-relay`.

## Translation Map (then → now)

- 30-min show, unstapled = 12 standalone spots (GoPro) = today's TikTok/Reels clip stack
- The 3-min commercial block = the offer stack section of a VSL
- Infomercial = VSL with three CTAs; remove the middle two for modern VSL (HK-5)
- The tease = the VSL lead; Act Two objection-drilling = the VSL's middle third
- 1-800 number = the URL/code — which is also the attribution instrument for royalty deals
