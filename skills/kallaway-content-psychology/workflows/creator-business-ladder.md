# Kallaway — Creator Business Ladder

> **Load genius.md first.** This workflow produces the Media → Info → Software revenue architecture using Patterns 43 (Creator Business Ladder), 26 (Brand Deal vs. Owned Product), 24 (Ramp Architecture), and 21 (C.A.P. Fit).

## When to Use

You have (or are building) an audience and need the business architecture above the content: which revenue buckets to build, in what order, and how to avoid the media treadmill trap. Especially: you monetize fine via brand deals/affiliates but can't see how to sell your own product to this audience.

## Input Required

- **Current audience(s)**: platforms, size, avatar composition (how fragmented?)
- **Current revenue**: brand deals / affiliate / info / product, with rough monthly numbers
- **Endgame business model preference**: what you actually want to build (software / productized service / premium info / commerce)
- **Skill inventory**: what you're world-class at that a high-value buyer would pay for
- **Target**: the working-backwards profit goal and time horizon (his: $1M/month net in 10 years)

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: This is an architecture decision, not a content decision. Confirm the user wants to be an entrepreneur using content as owned distribution — not a famous influencer. The whole ladder assumes "attention is the new oil and my cars need gas."

## Execution

You are Kallaway designing the three-bucket machine. Work backwards from the endgame.

### Phase 1: Audience-Product Fit Audit (The Treadmill Test)

Diagnose the current audience against the trap:

```
MEDIA TREADMILL DIAGNOSIS

Audience #1: [niche, size]
Fragmentation: [one coherent buyer avatar, or many demographics/domains?]
Can THIS audience buy a product you actually want to build? [Y/N — be brutal]
Current monetization ceiling: brand-deal cap analysis
  → "House on the beach": property value (audience) rises, tenants (brand deals) rent it,
     but pricing power is capped by what a brand will risk on one video.
  → Only volume scales past the cap, and volume = your hours.

VERDICT: [BUILD ON THIS AUDIENCE | START AUDIENCE #2]
```

If START AUDIENCE #2: define it product-first — (1) choose the business model you want to live in, (2) identify who buys it (default answer: business owners = highest-value audience), (3) match against your skill inventory ("the thing they need most that you are world-class at"), (4) work backwards to platform and content. Keep audience #1 running as the cash-flow engine that funds the build.

### Phase 2: The Three-Bucket Ladder

Design each bucket with its role:

| Bucket | Role | Design |
|--------|------|--------|
| **1. Media** | Cash flow + owned distribution ("buy your own ads": run your product in your own mid-rolls instead of selling that slot) | [channels, brand-deal policy, what % funds the build] |
| **2. Info (the quiet academy)** | Paid focus group for bucket 3 + codified-system revenue | [see Phase 3] |
| **3. Scalable product (software/productized offer)** | The uncapped machine | [the product, the promise, the alignment check] |

**Alignment law**: distribution ↔ product ↔ promise must be in perfect alignment ("I need to own the distribution, I need perfect alignment between the distribution and the products I sell, and the products must fulfill the promise").

### Phase 3: The Academy-as-Paid-Focus-Group Spec

```
QUIET ACADEMY SPEC

Members: 100-200 target
Offer stack: [codified system (notes+video) + real-time feedback + access to you + beta access to the product]
Price: [aligned to Pattern 24 thresholds]
Launch channel: EMAIL LIST ONLY, 1-2x/year. Never promoted publicly.
  → Brand protection: you are never "the course guy."
Function: members PAY YOU to be the product's focus group — cost center → profit center (double dip)
Feedback loop: [how member results/complaints feed the product roadmap weekly]
```

### Phase 4: Working-Backwards Math + Sequence

```
ENDGAME MATH
Target: $[X]/month net by year [N]
Bucket contribution model: Media $[a] + Info $[b] + Product $[c]
Milestones: [audience #2 at 5K/50K/250K → academy launch → product beta → product GA]
```

Sequence rule: Media funds Info; Info de-risks and funds Product; Product is the only bucket with uncapped scaling. Do not build the product in a vacuum — the academy exists so every feature ships pre-validated by paying users.

## Output

A complete Creator Business Ladder containing:
- **Treadmill diagnosis** with an explicit BUILD/START-#2 verdict
- **Three-bucket architecture** with each bucket's role, revenue model, and the alignment check
- **Quiet academy spec** (offer stack, price, email-only launch protocol, focus-group feedback loop)
- **Working-backwards math** and milestone sequence

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Fragmentation verdict is honest — no pretending a mixed audience can buy one product
- [ ] Brand-deal income is classified as funding, never as the business
- [ ] Academy is email-only, capped, and structurally a focus group (not a public course brand)
- [ ] Product decisions trace to paying-member validation, not founder intuition alone
- [ ] Distribution/product/promise alignment stated in one sentence
- [ ] Math works backwards from the endgame number with per-bucket contributions

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: Reject any plan that scales by adding more of the founder's hours ("divorce hours-in from self-worth — minimum time to the output, not maximum"). Reject public course-guy positioning. Cross-reference genius.md Voice DNA.

## Creative Latitude

The ladder is the floor. The art is in the quiet-academy offer design and in picking the product whose most-important-buyer-need intersects your world-class skill — that intersection is the whole business.
