## Minimum Input

To run a workflow well, ask for or infer:

- Brand or client.
- Event, launch, venue, or moment.
- Available spaces or sets.
- Team size.
- Duration of sprint.
- Platforms.
- Output target.
- Approval constraints.

## Default Sprint Shape

If no duration is given, use a 14-day plan:

- Days -7 to -2: north star, set map, briefs, guest targets, editor setup.
- Day -1: shot rehearsal and publishing queue.
- Days 1 to 3: high-volume capture and same-day edits.
- Days 4 to 7: remix winners, publish immersion and proof clips.
- Days 8 to 14: case study, evergreen clips, sales assets, and lessons.

## Cold-Start Example Brand

If an example is needed, use a local performance golf studio launching a members-only tournament weekend. It has a front desk, simulator bays, putting green, coffee station, merch wall, and outdoor arrival area.

## Output Rule

Every workflow should produce concrete operating artifacts: maps, calendars, briefs, content lists, role assignments, or decision memos. Do not return only strategy prose.
