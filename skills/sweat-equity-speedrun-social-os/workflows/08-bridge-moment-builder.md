# Bridge Moment Builder

## Use When

The sprint has celebrities, customers, partners, creators, athletes, guests, staff, or local personalities whose presence can bridge into new audiences.

## Inputs

- Guest or partner list.
- Their audience/context.
- Brand constraints.
- Available sets.
- Time available with each person.
- Collab posting options.

## Process

1. Identify each bridge person's audience and story.
2. Decide why they would say yes.
3. Match them to a set, prop, or action that makes them look good.
4. Design a 20-second ask:
   - What they do.
   - What they say, if anything.
   - Why it fits them.
   - How fast it is.
5. Add risk checks:
   - Brand safety.
   - Sponsor conflicts.
   - Permission and usage.
   - Collab post feasibility.
6. Prioritize the bridges with the highest audience fit and lowest friction.

## Quality Gate

The plan fails if it treats guests as props. The clip must also make the bridge person look good or serve their story.
