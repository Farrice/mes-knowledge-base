# In-The-Moment Capture Loop

## Use When

A better live idea appears: unexpected guest, prop, action, line, visual setup, audience reaction, weather, conflict, or cultural detail.

## Inputs

- Current plan.
- Live opportunity.
- Available people and props.
- Time available.
- Brand constraints.
- Approval constraints.

## Process

1. Identify the opportunity in one sentence.
2. Score it:
   - Brand fit.
   - Audience interest.
   - Visual specificity.
   - Speed.
   - Guest friction.
   - Risk.
3. If the idea passes, run the level-up pass:
   - Level 1: obvious shot.
   - Level 2: add action, prop, or context.
   - Level 3: add story compression, background detail, or micro-conflict.
4. Assign one owner to execute.
5. Capture fast.
6. Send to editor with a short brief.
7. Decide publish now, hold, remix, or kill.

## Quality Gate

The loop fails if it says "be creative" without a decision rule for when the team should stop, shoot, improve, or kill the moment.
