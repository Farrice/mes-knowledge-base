# Social Narrative North Star

## Use When

A brand, event, venue, launch, or client activation needs one content filter before the team starts generating ideas.

## Inputs

- Brand or client.
- Event or sprint moment.
- Existing brand belief or positioning.
- Target audience.
- Desired outcome.
- Tone constraints.

## Process

1. State the larger brand narrative in one sentence.
2. Translate it into the temporary event narrative.
3. Name the audience feeling the sprint must create.
4. List 5 things the content must make visible.
5. List 5 things the content must not do.
6. Convert the narrative into a decision filter:
   - Does this idea make the audience feel invited?
   - Does this idea make the place or moment legible?
   - Does this idea respect the brand's tone?
   - Does this idea create a repeatable content lane?
7. Produce a one-line north star and a 5-rule content filter.

## Output Schema

```markdown
# Social Narrative North Star

Brand:
Sprint moment:
Audience:
Outcome target:

## Larger Brand Narrative

## Temporary Sprint Narrative

## Audience Feeling

## Content Must Show

## Content Must Avoid

## Decision Filter

## North Star
```

## Quality Gate

The north star fails if it sounds like generic brand strategy, cannot reject weak ideas, or does not clearly connect the event to the brand's larger story.
