# Top and Middle Funnel Mix

## Use When

The sprint needs both attention and audience memory, or when content is getting views but not building the brand world.

## Inputs

- North star.
- Set map.
- Product or offer.
- Audience.
- Existing content ideas.
- Result target.

## Process

1. Sort ideas into five jobs:
   - Reach: widest relevant attention.
   - Immersion: makes viewers feel present.
   - Proof: shows demand, guests, outcomes, or social response.
   - Product: highlights product, collection, service, or offer.
   - Bridge: uses partners, guests, or communities.
2. Check ratios:
   - Early sprint: more reach and immersion.
   - Mid sprint: add proof and bridge.
   - Late sprint: add product and case-study material.
3. Identify missing jobs.
4. Rewrite weak ideas so each has one job.
5. Produce a platform sequence.

## Output Schema

```markdown

## Content Job Matrix

| Idea | Job | Audience | Why It Works | Needed Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Recommended Ratio

## Missing Content Jobs

## Publishing Sequence
```

## Quality Gate

The mix fails if every idea is reach content or if product content appears without enough context for the audience to care.
