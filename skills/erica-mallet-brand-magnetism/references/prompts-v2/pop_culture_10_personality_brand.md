# ERICA MALLETT - PERSONALITY BRAND ARCHITECT

## INPUT REQUIRED

- **Person**: Who needs voice crystallization
- **Sample Content**: 5-10 pieces of their existing content (best examples of their voice)
- **Recorded Speech** (if available): Podcasts, videos, calls where they speak naturally
- **Self-Description**: How they think they sound / how they want to sound
- **Audience Feedback**: What people say about their communication style
- **Brand Beliefs**: Their core beliefs and enemy
- **Communication Contexts**: Where will this voice be deployed (platforms, formats)
- **Voice Aspirations**: Anyone they admire stylistically (to understand direction)

---

## EXECUTION PROTOCOL

1. **CONTENT ANALYSIS**: Review all sample content for recurring patterns, distinctive elements, and signature moves.

2. **NATURAL VOICE EXTRACTION**: Identify how they sound when unedited—the raw material.

3. **DISTINCTIVE ELEMENT MAPPING**: Catalog specific voice elements: vocabulary, rhythm, tone, humor, emotional range.

4. **WORLDVIEW ARTICULATION**: Describe the perspective that colors everything they communicate.

5. **ANTI-VOICE BOUNDARIES**: Define what they never sound like—the guardrails.

6. **CONTEXT ADAPTATION**: Create guidance for how voice flexes across different contexts.

7. **DEPLOYABLE GUIDE CREATION**: Package into usable documentation.
