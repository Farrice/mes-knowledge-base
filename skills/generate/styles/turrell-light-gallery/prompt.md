Photograph of a concrete gallery interior at dusk. Shot on medium format Hasselblad 500CM with Kodak Ektar 100 film — visible film grain, slight color shift in the shadows, subtle chromatic aberration. The concrete shows real texture: formwork marks, hairline cracks, mineral deposits; poured floor with imperfect patches. A single slightly-blurred figure far right for scale, dark clothing, long exposure. Dust particles visible in the light beams, natural lens flare from the bright center. The image should feel like an architecture photographer documenting a James Turrell installation — not rendered, not digital, not perfect; shot on film, printed in a darkroom.
