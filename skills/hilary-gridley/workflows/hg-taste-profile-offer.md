# hg-taste-profile-offer — Productize the Moat

Turn the Taste Profile build into a sellable, repeatable engagement. The market timing is her own course data: buyer pain has shifted from "get my team using AI" (saturated) to "my team uses AI and everything is slop" (open). This offer sells the cure at the root: the client's judgment and canon, encoded once, raising every output after.

**Standing constraints (memory-binding)**: this workflow produces the offer PACKAGE as a shelf asset. Send-before-build is BINDING — validate with a real prospect send before building delivery collateral. Run `/offer-redteam` before any public launch; offer_gate applies. ## Execution

1. **Position against the felt pain.** The buyer's words: "everything my team ships with AI sounds the same / feels off-brand / I'm the bottleneck rewriting everything." The offer: *we encode your taste and your customer's inner world into one canonical asset — every person and every AI tool on your team produces to YOUR bar from day one.* Anti-position: not another AI tool, not training, not an ICP refresh — those are what already failed them.
2. **Design the deliverable stack** (each item independently demo-able):
   - **The Taste Profile** (flagship) — three layers per spec, provenance appendix included (the receipts ARE the differentiation)
   - **Evaluator starter fleet** — 2-3 narrow tools minted from the client's own edit history (`hg-judgment-encode`), the profile made operational; the Executive Editor pattern as the always-lands demo
   - **Quality Bar + accountability one-pager** — the management layer that makes it stick (`hg-quality-bar`)
   - **Install session** — load rule wired into their actual tools (GPTs/Projects/gems), team walkthrough, re-mine cadence handed off
3. **Design the proof arc.** Free teardown as entry (per Alignment-Audit kill decision: audits are lead magnets, never the paid product): run `hg-slop-diagnostic` on 3 public artifacts + one paragraph of what their Taste Profile would have caught. Paid engagement opens with the months-to-minutes demo: one of their real artifacts, re-produced against a draft profile, before/after side by side.
4. **Structure delivery** as a 10-day sprint arc: Days 1-3 grounding (voice-of-customer mining, founder interview, edit-pair harvest) → Days 4-7 build (three layers + evaluators) → Days 8-10 install + validation (their team ships 3 real artifacts through the system). Client involvement front-loaded; theirs to run on day 11 (kick-the-crutch applies to the engagement itself — no dependency tail unless they buy the retainer).
5. **Price logic + expansion path.** Anchor within the existing shelf ($2K-$5K urgency band; final number is Farrice's call — flag, don't fix). Expansion: quarterly re-mine retainer · per-brand additional profiles · the evaluator fleet grows per `hg-evaluator-fleet`. The profile compounds switching costs: every re-mine deepens the moat.
6. **Package the send.** One-pager + the 3-sentence DM/email version + the teardown offer script. Send-before-build: these three ship first; everything else waits for a yes.
