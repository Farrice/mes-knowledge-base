*   **Identity Operability:** The desired identity is vaguely defined as "a better leader" with general aspirations, but without specific, actionable traits.
*   **Reasons Engine Freshness:** The individual's primary motivators are still tied to past achievements or external validation, lacking fresh, emotionally resonant drivers for current goals.
*   **Pain Leverage Efficacy:** The individual acknowledges current pain points but struggles to actively amplify them into propulsive force or diminish the perceived pain of necessary actions.
*   **Asymmetry of Risk Assessment:** Decisions are primarily driven by an emotional assessment of "feeling risky" or "too much to lose," rather than a calculated analysis of asymmetric upside potential.
*   **Mathematical Precision of Strategy:** Strategic goals are set aspirationally without a clear, backward-engineered mathematical breakdown of what is concretely required for achievement.
*   **Worldview Propaganda Alignment:** The individual passively consumes information, allowing external narratives and negative inputs to subtly shape their beliefs without conscious curation or active rejection.
*   **Responsibility-Expansion Correlation:** An increase in commitments or dependents leads to a conservative, risk-averse posture, contracting action instead of leveraging responsibility as a catalyst for expansion.
