# Framework Creation Workflow

## Overview
Turn your existing internal processes into coined, visual, memorable frameworks that signal obsession and expertise. The person who appears most obsessed gets the business.

## Step 1: Extract Internal Processes
Ask yourself:
- "What steps do I consistently take clients through?"
- "What do I find myself explaining over and over?"
- "What's my process for [core thing I do]?"
- Write out the raw steps. Don't filter. 3-7 steps is ideal.

## Step 2: Name It
Apply these techniques:
- **Alliteration** — Same starting letter (Crispy Content Checklist, Business Blueprint)
- **Same suffixes** — Matching endings (-tion, -ment, -ity)
- **Acronyms** — If letters spell something memorable
- **AI assist** — "Give me a P word that means consistent" → "Persistent"

Test: Can someone hear the name once and remember it tomorrow?

## Step 3: Visualize It
Draw it. Options:
- Sad face → arrow → happy face (before/after)
- Numbered steps in circles
- Pyramid / triangle
- Quadrant (2×2 grid)
- Cycle / loop
- Ladder / staircase

"Frameworks communicated visually signal: I've thought about this before you have."

## Step 4: Validate
- [ ] Can you teach it in under 5 minutes?
- [ ] Does it cover the full journey (start → finish)?
- [ ] Is the name memorable after one hearing?
- [ ] Does the visual make it clearer than words alone?
- [ ] Does it apply to more than one person? (Principle vs. Theory check)

## Step 5: Deploy
Use across:
- Social media posts (teach one element per post)
- Web class / challenge (teach the full framework)
- Sales conversations ("Here's my [Framework Name]...")
- Coaching calls (reference by name consistently)
- Bio / about page ("Creator of the [Framework Name]")

## Pro Tips
- **One framework doesn't have to cover everything.** Omar has the Business Blueprint Framework, the Go-To Expert Framework, the Five F's — multiple frameworks for multiple angles.
- **Preaching technique:** Great preachers create memorable points using alliteration and structured delivery. Study sermon structures for framework inspiration.
- **Obsession signal:** "The person who's obsessed is the person who gets the business." Frameworks prove obsession.
