# Objective-by-Context

Omar gives away the entire play on YouTube and loses nothing. People flew in from Florida, drove from California and Utah to sit in a live recording they *knew* would be posted free the next week. He explains why with one line: *"the context in which the content is consumed determines the value of the content."* The information on the feed and the information in the paid room is identical. What changes is the receiver's attention. *"If you pay them, you'll pay attention more."* So the question that paralyzes most experts — *how much do I give away?* — is the wrong question. It produces gatekeeping anxiety and no answer.

This workflow replaces that question with a better one: *what's the context, and what's the objective for THIS context?* For every channel the user actually publishes on, it fixes a single objective, the target feeling, how much to give, the WHAT-vs-HOW line, and the one next step that hands off to the channel below it. The disclosure then falls out automatically. The user leaves with a filled matrix for their real channels and a one-line disclosure rule per channel they can tape above their desk.

## Input Required
- The channels the user actually publishes on (or plans to within 90 days): IG feed, IG stories, YouTube long-form, podcast, email, webinar, challenge, sales call, paid program — list the real ones, skip the rest
- The ONE paid container money flows into (program, coaching, course, intensive) — the bottom of the funnel
- The expertise / topic, in the receiver's words not the user's jargon
- Optional: a current symptom — "I give away too much and no one buys," "my free stuff converts but my paid feels thin," "every post sounds the same" — sharpens which leak the matrix is fixing

## Workflow

### Step 1: Order the channels into one funnel (not a list)
List the user's real channels top (most transient) to bottom (most committed). Read them as a single descent — each channel's next step must be the entry to the channel below it (Pattern 2). If two channels claim the same objective, one is redundant; collapse or re-aim it. If there's a gap (a free channel whose next step has nowhere to land), flag it now — that's a broken handoff, not a styling problem.

### Step 2: Set the SINGLE objective per channel
For each channel, write one objective only — borrow from Tool 1's column 2, then make it specific to this user's topic. Transient channels aim at *recognition and trust*; committed channels aim at *belief-shift and enrollment*; paid channels aim at *fulfillment and ascension*. Omar's own YouTube objective is the anchor for any long-form row: become undeniable, *"so that you undeniably know who I am"* — *"you can't fake who you are for a long time"* (Pattern 9). One objective per channel. A channel doing two jobs does neither.

### Step 3: Name the TARGET FEELING (before the content)
For each channel, write what the receptive person should FEEL at the end — not what they should know (Pattern 3). *"The goal of teaching is to create the feeling in a person to take the next step."* Feed = "that's me, this person sees my problem." Long-form = "I got more than I paid for, and I trust this person." Webinar = "it's possible, it's easy, and I trust them to walk me through it." If you can't name the feeling, the row isn't ready.

### Step 4: Set disclosure depth + the WHAT-vs-HOW line (HOW-reconciliation)
For each channel, decide how much to give and where the WHAT/HOW line sits. Apply the rule (Patterns 6, 7):
- **Free / transient** (feed, stories, YouTube, podcast, email): give the WHAT generously. *"Don't teach how... teach what or what not, when or when not, why or why not, where or where not."* Title can pose a "how to"; the body delivers the WHAT. The HOW stays out — not to gatekeep, but because the attention can't hold it.
- **Webinar / challenge** (semi-committed): enough WHAT to prove it's real and EASY; the implementation HOW is the paid promise, withheld. Make-it-easy IS the close.
- **Paid container** (program, coaching, intensive, bootcamp): HOW is fully allowed — *"when somebody pays you, they'll be more receptive of the how-to,"* delivered *"in the right context and in a way that anyone can do it."*

State it as a one-liner per channel: e.g. "YouTube: WHAT generously, title-in-HOW, deliver-WHAT, zero step-by-step." Reserve every actual HOW for the paid row. Calibration, never gatekeeping.

### Step 5: Write the ONE next step per channel (the handoff)
Each channel gets exactly one next step, and it must be the entry to the row below (Step 1's funnel). Feed → follow/save. Stories → tap link / DM. YouTube → free asset / subscribe / "watched till the end" DM. Webinar → enroll / book. Challenge → ascend. Paid → stay / ascend / become a testimonial that feeds lead gen. Then add the self-selection honesty for the conversion rows: *"99.99% of you guys won't even click the link... and that's totally fine. But for the 0.1%, let's go"* (Pattern 9). One CTA per row. Three CTAs = zero decisions.

### Step 6: Pressure-test against the leak
Re-read the matrix against the user's stated symptom (or scan for the two classic leaks): (a) HOW leaking into a free/transient row — pull it down to the paid row; (b) the WHAT being withheld behind the paywall while free content is thin — push it up, the free channels are *supposed* to be generous. Confirm no row carries two objectives and no next-step points nowhere. Then collapse the whole thing into one disclosure rule per channel — the tape-above-the-desk line.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Channel / Format | Objective | Disclosure rule (one line) |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| **Instagram feed** | Identity recognition — name the need they KNOW they have | One belief-shift per post; WHAT/WHY only; title-in-HOW ok; over-deliver value, never volume |
| **IG / Reels stories** | Proximity & pattern — daily presence, tease the room | A glimpse + WHY-this-matters; presence over instruction; point to the long-form |
| **YouTube long-form** | Become undeniable — "you can't fake who you are for a long time" | Generous WHAT, ~one core teach, title-in-HOW deliver-WHAT, HOW stays out; over-deliver so they rewatch |
| **Podcast** | Depth of relationship over hours | WHAT + WHY through story, not curriculum; "everything is a reference point" |
| **Email** | Re-open the loop, move toward the room | One idea per send; WHAT/WHY; single CTA to the next channel |
| **Webinar / masterclass** | Shift possible · easy · trust; create the felt impulse to enroll | Enough WHAT to prove it's real and EASY; HOW = the paid promise; make-it-easy IS the close |
| **5-day challenge** | Foundations + a small implemented win | More WHAT sequenced daily, lightest HOW that produces a same-day win; one move per day |
| **Sales call** | Confirm fit + remove the last belief block | Diagnose, don't teach; tiny WHAT to prove understanding; HOW is what they're buying — don't give it free |
| **Workshop / bootcamp / paid program** | Transformation via done-with-you; fulfillment-as-teaching + ascension | HOW fully allowed, calibrated to their grow gap; consolidate; post-transaction attention can hold it |

## Quality Gate
> 🛡️ **Anti-Pattern Check**: review against `genius.md § Quality Rubric` (Context-Calibrated Disclosure, WHAT-not-HOW Discipline, Feeling-Over-Information) before delivering.
- Every channel carries exactly ONE objective and ONE next step — no row doing two jobs, no CTA stacking (Rubric: Feeling-Over-Information anchor 10).
- The HOW lives in exactly one place: the paid container. No step-by-step HOW leaked into any free/transient row (Rubric: Context-Calibrated Disclosure — "WHAT generously in free, HOW reserved for paid").
- Free/transient rows give the WHAT *generously* — the matrix is not an excuse to gatekeep; it's calibration (Pattern 6).
- Every row names a target FEELING, not just an information goal (Pattern 3).
- The funnel actually connects: each channel's next step is the entry to the row below. No dead ends.
- Self-reported numbers (Omar's revenue, the live-recording attendees, Paige) carry UNCONFIRMED labels if cited.
- Faith grounding (treasure/heart) stated as principle first, optional — the matrix is fully usable secular.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Gatekeeping disguised as calibration.** The user withholds the WHAT to "protect value." Recovery: the value is the CONTEXT (committed attention), not the withheld information — push the WHAT up into the free rows where it belongs; people pay for the room, not the secret.
- **HOW bleeding into free content.** A YouTube row that secretly ships step-by-step mechanics overwhelms and converts no one — *"when you start teaching how, you will start to lose people."* Recovery: run that asset through `/oe-what-not-how`; relocate the HOW to the paid row.
- **One objective smeared across every channel.** Same teach, same depth, same CTA everywhere — so nothing escalates. Recovery: force one *distinct* objective per row; if two match, collapse or re-aim.
- **A free channel with no landing.** The next step points at a paid container that doesn't exist yet. Recovery: name the intended paid container in Pre-Flight; until it exists, the top-of-funnel is just brand-building, not a funnel — say so honestly.
- **Building for the 99.99%.** The matrix gets optimized for mass reach instead of the receptive fraction. Recovery: re-anchor the conversion rows on self-selection — *"for the 0.1%, let's go"* — and design the depth for the people who actually move.
