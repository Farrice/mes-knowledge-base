# Mindset Over Tactics

A client emails: "Skip the philosophy, just give me the hook formula." Omar's read on that whole reflex is blunt — broke people chase tactics, and the people who've actually made it tell you it was *always* mindset. Why the gap? *"You've got scales over your eyes."* Experience removes them. The tactic the client begs for isn't useless; it's just inert on its own, because *"we don't have an information problem. We have an application problem."* This workflow does not refuse the tactic. It hands the tactic back wrapped in the one belief that makes it fire — and then runs Omar's test for whether anything actually moved: did they think about the thing differently, *and then act on it?*

This is the Tier-3 depth layer. Where the other exterior-game workflows shape the asset, this one shapes the person receiving it — and bridges back to the Interior Game, because *"all you're doing is you're revealing and projecting who you are."* The package can never out-run who someone is becoming. Run this when a deliverable is technically correct and still won't land.

- Owner intent / context: is this advice to a client, a sales-call moment, content, or copy? (Changes the wrap, not the method.)

## Workflow

### Step 1: Take the request literally, then re-diagnose
Restate the tactical ask exactly as given. Then re-read the stall through Pattern 10's lens: this is almost never an information gap. Classify it:
- **Application problem** — they have the info, aren't doing it. ("We don't have an information problem. We have an application problem.")
- **Consolidation problem** — drowning in unconsolidated inputs; ten frameworks, zero installed.
- **Belief problem** — a "scale over the eyes" that makes the tactic feel impossible, hard, or untrustworthy for *them specifically.*

PRODUCE: one sentence naming which problem this is, and why the tactic alone won't fix it.

### Step 2: Locate the belief in the way
Find the single belief that, if it stays in place, neuters the tactic. Probe against the Possible-Easy-Trust triad (Pattern 5) — the stall is usually a hole in one:
- **Possible** — "this works for other people, not someone like me."
- **Easy** — "this is more than I can sustain / too complicated for where I am."
- **Trust** — "I don't believe this person / this method actually delivers."

Pull the matching belief from the Interior Game reservoir where one fits (e.g. money-fear → `$100K Devaluation`; perfectionism-freeze → `Do It for the Data`; chasing-new → `I Am Boring`). 

PRODUCE: the belief-in-the-way, stated as the client would feel it (first person), plus the named Interior-Game belief that answers it.

### Step 3: Package the tactic INSIDE the mindset
Build the deliverable as belief-first, tactic-embedded — never the tactic as a standalone hack. Structure: **belief-shift → the tactic delivered cleanly → the belief restated as the thing that makes it work.** Omar's instruction is exact: *"give the tactic INSIDE a belief-shift — package the how-to, deliver the mindset."*

The how-to does not get withheld or watered down here (that would be gatekeeping, not calibration — see Pattern 6/7). It gets *framed*. The belief is what lets the receiver actually use it.

PRODUCE: the reframed deliverable — the same tactic the client asked for, wrapped so the belief does the lifting.

### Step 4: Run the true-influence test
Score the wrapped deliverable against Omar's definition: *"true influence is when someone thinks about the thing differently than they did after they met you... and then they act on it."* Two gates, both required:
- **Think differently** — after this, does the receiver hold a *different belief* about the thing than before? Name the before → after shift.
- **Then act** — does the deliverable make a specific next action obvious and feel-able? (Feeling-first, Pattern 3 — *"people do things... because they feel like it."*) Name the one action.

A deliverable that shifts thinking but produces no action is half-influence — incomplete. One that drives action without shifting belief is a hack that won't survive the first obstacle. Both gates or revise.

PRODUCE: the before→after belief shift + the single next action the receiver feels like taking.

### Step 5: Apply the two ride-along laws + the revealer bridge
Two checks before delivery, both from Pattern 10:
- **"The way you sell is the way you buy."** If you're modeling the tactic for a creator, make sure the wrap doesn't teach cheapness or discount-chasing — *"cheap buyers become cheap sellers."* The frame should model the buyer/seller you want them to become.
- **Teaching is a revealer.** The ceiling check: *"focus on who you're becoming. We don't become what we don't believe... we live in the lies we take on."* The deliverable can't out-run the person. If the tactic requires the receiver to embody something they don't yet believe about themselves, flag the identity work — the asset alone won't close that gap.

PRODUCE: a one-line "becoming" note — who this person has to be *becoming* for the tactic to hold, and whether that's in range or needs Interior-Game work first.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Format | How the wrap changes |
|--------|----------------------|
| Client advice / coaching | Full method; deliver the becoming-note out loud — "you'll be able to run this when you're showing up as the person who already charges this." |
| Sales call | Belief-shift live, in their language; the "act" gate IS the close. Make the easy-belief explicit (Pattern 5): "if it feels this clear here, that's what working together feels like." |
| Content (IG / YouTube) | Lead the post/video with the belief-shift, embed the tactic mid, restate belief on the out. Free/transient = give the WHAT generously (Pattern 6); the wrap is the differentiator competitors can't copy. |
| Email | One belief, one tactic, one action. The CTA is the "act" gate — if the belief didn't shift above it, the click won't happen. |
| Webinar / web class | The whole teach is the wrap: belief-shift across the body, tactic as the demonstration, next-step offer as the engineered action (Pattern 3 feeling-first). |
| Course / fulfillment | Tactic delivered in full (paid context, Pattern 6 — depth is allowed here); each module still opens with the belief that makes the module's how-to land. |

## Quality Gate
> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: review against `genius.md § Expert-Specific Quality Rubric` — esp. *Identity-First Framing*, *Belief-Shift Primacy*, *Feeling-Over-Information*.
- The tactic was DELIVERED, not withheld — this is mindset-wrapping, not gatekeeping (Pattern 10 vs. 6/7).
- A specific belief-in-the-way was named in first person; the wrap attacks that belief, not a generic one.
- The true-influence test cleared BOTH gates — thinking shifted AND a single next action is feel-able (Pattern 3). Half-influence was flagged, not shipped.
- The deliverable leads with belief and closes on belief — the tactic sits inside, never naked.
- The becoming-note is present: the ceiling check ("the package can't out-run the person") was actually run, not assumed.
- Any self-reported numbers (Omar's revenue, the Paige / $110K cases) carry UNCONFIRMED labels.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Lecturing instead of wrapping.** Replacing the tactic with a mindset sermon and never delivering the how-to. That's the opposite failure — and it teaches cheapness. Recovery: re-run Step 3; the tactic must be physically present and clean.
- **Generic-belief substitution.** Wrapping in "you've got this!" instead of the specific scale over *this* person's eyes. Recovery: re-do Step 2 against the triad until the belief is first-person and precise.
- **Stopping at "think differently."** A clever reframe that produces no action is half-influence — Omar's bar is think AND act. Recovery: name the single feel-able next action or the deliverable fails the gate.
- **Skipping the becoming-note.** Shipping a tactic the receiver can't embody yet, then blaming the tactic when it doesn't hold. Recovery: flag the identity gap; route Interior-Game work before the asset is expected to perform.
- **Forcing a wrap on a clean execution ask.** Sometimes "just give me the format" is genuinely that. Recovery: honor the Pre-Flight Gate — no located belief, no forced reframe; route to the craft workflow instead.
