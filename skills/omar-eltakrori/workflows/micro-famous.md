# Micro-Famous Strategy Workflow

## Overview
Being micro-famous in your sector > trying to be mega-famous. Broad appeal with a niche business is the game.

## Step 1: Define Your Sector
- What specific niche do you serve?
- Who are the other known names?
- Where does your audience congregate? (platform + communities)

## Step 2: Build Know-Like-Trust

### Trust (Share Educational Content)
- Be the source of updated, relevant information in your niche
- Post consistently (1 long-form/week + 5 feed posts/week)
- Teach principles, not just tips
- Use frameworks to signal obsession

### Know (Be Involved & Visible)
- Show up where your audience is
- Comment on other creators' content
- Attend industry events (virtual and in-person)
- Collaborate with peers (podcasts, joint lives, guest posts)
- "Intentional consistency leads to indirect opportunity"

### Like (Open Up Your Life — Five F's)
Share content across all five categories:

| F | What to Share | Content Ideas |
|---|--------------|---------------|
| **Faith** | Belief system, values, what drives you | Morning routine, gratitude posts, value statements |
| **Family** | Relationships, personal moments | Behind-the-scenes with family, lessons from parenting |
| **Food** | What you eat, cook, enjoy | Coffee reviews, restaurant visits, cooking while teaching |
| **Fashion** | Style, gear, aesthetics | What you wear to events, gear reviews, workspace tours |
| **Fitness** | Physical pursuits, health | Gym sessions, golf rounds, runs — pair with business tips |

## Step 3: Content Strategy for Micro-Fame

### Two-at-Once Technique
Every piece of lifestyle content should communicate two things:
1. A business/expertise insight
2. A lifestyle/personality element

Example: Share a business framework while at the golf course. Teach a sales principle while making coffee.

### Classified Content
- Scroll your phone album weekly
- Find 3-5 photos that tell a story
- Add copy overlay
- Post as feed content (dwell time = algorithm boost)

### Live Streaming
- 25-minute Instagram lives
- Teach and get to the point
- Focus on the replay audience (more people watch replay than live)
- Repurpose as podcast episodes

## Step 4: Local → Global Content
You don't need millions of followers. Example: Tommy Oswald (Homeboy coffee shop, Australia):
- Vlogs about his container coffee shop
- Gets hundreds of thousands of views
- Grows his LOCAL business through GLOBAL content
- Niche business, broad audience appeal

## Step 5: Measure Micro-Fame
Signs you're becoming micro-famous:
- [ ] People reference your frameworks by name
- [ ] You get invited to podcasts/stages without pitching
- [ ] Strangers mention your content when you meet them
- [ ] Inbound leads increase without increasing ad spend
- [ ] Industry peers recognize your name
- [ ] "I feel like I already know you" from new contacts

## Key Principles
- "Visibility beats ability" — but ability catches up. Be both visible AND good.
- "The riches are in the niches, but that's more about the business you offer"
- "Having broad appeal with a niche business is really the game"
- "The person who appears most obsessed with a topic gets the business"
