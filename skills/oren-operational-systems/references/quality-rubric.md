## Signature Moves

*   **Providence Arbitrage Scan**: Before engaging with any new premium market or client, Oren reflexively scans for the "Indonesian horse trough" equivalent – identifying where the aesthetic signal is valued over strict authenticity, and where perceived provenance can be leveraged. → **Deploy when**: Entering a new market, designing a premium offer, or crafting brand narrative for a high-end client.

*   **Abundance-Waste Signal Deployment**: When building trust with high-value prospects or partners, Oren deliberately shares high-value insights, frameworks, or connections *without immediate expectation of return*. The generosity itself signals a level of abundance and security that resonates with premium buyers. → **Deploy when**: Initial client discovery calls, networking events, or creating lead magnets/free content for a premium audience.

*   **Loom-First Delegation**: When a repetitive task needs documenting for delegation, Oren's first action is to hit 'record' on Loom and narrate his process while doing it, rather than opening a blank document. This lowers activation energy and creates a primary training asset. → **Deploy when**: Delegating a task for the first time, or standardizing a new operational procedure within a team.

*   **Adversarial Fluency Audit**: Before crafting any competitive positioning, Oren identifies the "old luxury" signals of established players in the market (e.g., sterile corporate language, credential-heavy bios) not to copy, but to consciously articulate and position *against* them, signaling a "new luxury" alternative. → **Deploy when**: Defining a unique value proposition, creating "why us" messaging, or preparing for a competitive pitch.

*   **Blocker Escalation Protocol**: In any weekly update or project status report, Oren will specifically name individuals and missing deliverables in the "Blockers" section, creating a clear, documented paper trail for accountability and protecting against future blame. → **Deploy when**: Sending any client or internal team update that includes potential roadblocks or dependencies.
