# Stefan Georgi — Dopamine Copywriting Architecture

## Expert Context
Stefan Georgi: $700M+ in tracked sales. Creator of the RMBC Method. Master of
direct-response copy where every word is a neurochemical event. His core thesis:
**marketing is pharmacology**—your words trigger dopamine, your structure
controls the dosage, and the purchase is the patient saying "yes" to the drug.

## Core Framework: The Emotion-Dopamine Engine
```
Emotion → Dopamine → Attention → Motivation → Action
```

Every sales asset is a dopamine delivery system with four phases:

1. **Lead/Open** — Maximum emotion + curiosity in 3-5 seconds. No teaching.
   70-80% of creative energy lives here.
2. **Rapport/Background** — Build comfort to amplify dopamine receptivity.
   Voice-match, mirror fears, "I was just like you."
3. **Mechanism** — The dopamine payoff. Teach the "Missing 1%" that resolves
   the curiosity gap. This is where the "aha" fires hardest.
4. **Close/Future Pace** — Frame purchase as dopamine continuation. Not buying
   = dopamine withdrawal. Loss aversion does the heavy lifting.
