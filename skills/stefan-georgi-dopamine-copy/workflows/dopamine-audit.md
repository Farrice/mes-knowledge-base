# Dopamine Audit

> Audit any existing copy for dead zones, dopamine sequencing failures,
> premature education, curiosity leaks, and emotional conversion gaps.
> Turns mediocre copy into neurochemically optimized persuasion.

## Expert
Stefan Georgi — Dopamine Copywriting Architecture

## Inputs Required
1. **The Copy**: Paste or link to the asset being audited
2. **Asset Type**: Sales letter | VSL | Email | Ad | Landing page | Social post
3. **Performance Data** (optional): CTR, conversion rate, drop-off points

## Phase 1: Structural Scan

Map the copy's current architecture against the Dopamine Funnel:

```
IDEAL:  Lead (emotion/curiosity) → Rapport → Mechanism → Close
ACTUAL: [map what the copy actually does]
```

### Structural Violations Checklist
- [ ] **Education-First Violation**: Does the copy teach before building curiosity? (Pattern 9)
- [ ] **Premature Mechanism Reveal**: Is the mechanism revealed before rapport is established?
- [ ] **Missing Emotional Ignition**: Does the lead fail to trigger emotion within 3-5 seconds?
- [ ] **Flat Rapport Section**: Does the rapport section lecture instead of mirror?
- [ ] **Weak Close Architecture**: Does the close rely on logic instead of dopamine continuation?

## Phase 2: Dead Zone Mapping

Read every sentence. Classify each as:

| Classification | Definition | Action |
|---------------|------------|--------|
| **🟢 Active** | Builds curiosity, triggers emotion, or resolves a pre-built gap | Keep |
| **🟡 Passive** | Provides context but doesn't trigger dopamine | Rewrite with emotional or curiosity charge |
| **🔴 Dead** | Neither curiosity, emotion, nor gap-resolution | Delete or completely rebuild |

### Dead Zone Density Score
- **0 dead zones**: Elite — ship as-is
- **1-3 dead zones**: Revise targeted sections
- **4-7 dead zones**: Significant rewrite needed
- **8+ dead zones**: Full rebuild recommended

## Phase 3: Curiosity Leak Detection

Identify points where curiosity gaps are prematurely resolved:

1. **Early Answer Leaks**: Where does the copy give away information that should be teased?
2. **Resolution Without New Gap**: Where does a gap close without opening a new one?
3. **Clarity That Kills Curiosity**: Where is the copy too clear too early?

For each leak, prescribe:
- What to withhold
- Where to tease instead
- What new gap to open at the resolution point

### Emotional Failures
- **Emotion Crash Points**: Where does emotional intensity DROP?
- **Single-Emotion Flatline**: Does the copy rely on only one emotion throughout?
- **Generic Emotion Language**: Does the copy SAY emotions ("you'll feel great") instead of TRIGGERING them through stimulus?

## Phase 5: Prescription

For each identified issue, provide:
1. **The Problem**: Specific sentence or section + which pattern it violates
2. **The Fix**: Exact rewrite or structural change
3. **The Principle**: Which genius pattern governs this fix

### Prioritize fixes by impact:
- **Priority 1**: Lead failures (3-5 second rule violations)
- **Priority 2**: Dead zones in the first 30% of copy
- **Priority 3**: Curiosity leaks that deflate tension
- **Priority 4**: Close architecture weaknesses
- **Priority 5**: Rapport and transition failures

## Output Contract
- Structural map (ideal vs. actual funnel)
- Dead Zone Density Score with sentence-level classification
- Curiosity Leak Report with prescriptions
- Emotional Conversion Audit with gap analysis
- Prioritized fix list with exact rewrites for top 5 issues
- Overall Dopamine Sequencing Score (1-10 per genius rubric)
