# Dopamine Copy Engine

> Full sales asset creation through the Stefan Georgi dopamine funnel.
> Produces complete copy that engineers a neurochemical journey from
> attention through arousal through motivation through action.

## Expert
Stefan Georgi — Dopamine Copywriting Architecture

## Inputs Required
1. **Product/Offer**: What's being sold + core promise
2. **Target Audience**: Demographics, psychographics, awareness level
3. **Asset Type**: Sales letter | VSL script | Email sequence | Ad | Landing page
4. **Emotional Profile**: Primary emotions to target (reference Ekman's 7)
5. **Mechanism**: The unique mechanism or discovery (if known; will build if missing)

## Phase 1: Emotional Conversion Mapping
Map the prospect's journey across 4 emotional states:

1. **Entry State**: What emotion does the prospect carry INTO the piece? (frustration, skepticism, hope, fear)
2. **Arousal State**: What emotion will the LEAD trigger? (curiosity, nostalgia, anger, surprise)
3. **Resolution State**: What emotion does the MECHANISM produce? (relief, excitement, vindication)
4. **Action State**: What emotion drives the PURCHASE? (urgency, hope, fear of loss)

> **Gate**: If you cannot name specific Ekman emotions for each state, stop and deploy `/georgi-emotion-map` first.

## Phase 2: Lead Construction (70-80% of Creative Energy)

Build the opening using ONE of these architectures:

### Option A: Nostalgic Lead (35+ audiences)
- Open with specific sensory childhood memory
- Fire 3 Ekman emotions through hippocampus trigger
- Bridge from memory to product context
- Zero product mentions in first 40% of lead

### Option B: Curiosity/Paradox Lead (all audiences)
- Open with paradoxical question or counterintuitive fact
- Stack 2-3 nested curiosity gaps before resolving any
- Each gap creates cognitive debt that demands continuation
- Zero teaching or education in the lead

### Option C: Emotion-Bomb Lead (high-urgency products)
- Open with a vivid scenario that triggers fear, anger, or disgust
- Immediately establish "this is happening to YOU"
- Transition from negative emotion to curiosity about the solution
- The emotion must be specific and visceral, not generic

**Lead Quality Gate (Pattern 15 — 3-5 Second Rule)**:
- [ ] Dopamine is flowing within 3-5 seconds / 10-20 words
- [ ] Zero educational content in the lead
- [ ] At least 2 Ekman emotions activated through identifiable pathways
- [ ] Reader cannot determine the conclusion without continuing

## Phase 3: Rapport/Background Story

Build the comfort layer that amplifies dopamine receptivity:

1. **Voice Match**: Mirror the prospect's vocabulary, sentence rhythm, and worldview
2. **"I Was Just Like You"**: Establish shared experience or shared enemy
3. **Credibility Without Teaching**: Demonstrate authority through story, not instruction
4. **Comfort Escalation**: Each paragraph should increase the prospect's sense of "they get me"

> **Mandatory**: No mechanism reveal during this phase. The reader should feel understood but still curious.

## Phase 4: Mechanism Reveal (The Dopamine Payoff)

Deploy the mechanism using the "Missing 1%" architecture:

1. **Problem Mechanism First**: "Here's the ONE thing that's been sabotaging you..."
2. **Why Nothing Else Worked**: Frame all previous failures as missing this piece
3. **Solution Mechanism**: Reveal the missing piece as a discovery/breakthrough
4. **The "Aha" Moment**: Reader should feel relief + excitement simultaneously

> Cross-stack with David Deutsch's mechanism architecture if available.

## Phase 5: Close/Future Pace

Engineer the purchase as dopamine continuation:

1. **Pain of Without**: Vivid description of life continuing without the solution (dopamine contrast)
2. **Pleasure of With**: Sensory future-pace of life after the solution
3. **Loss Aversion Frame**: Not buying = losing the emotional state you've built
4. **CTA**: The purchase is saying "yes" to keeping the dopamine flowing

## Phase 6: Dead Zone Audit (Pattern — Dopamine Audit)

Read every sentence. For each, answer: **Is this building curiosity, triggering emotion, or resolving a pre-built gap?**

- If NONE → it's a dead zone. Delete or rewrite.
- Target: ZERO inert sentences in the final asset
- Every line is either priming, triggering, or resolving

## Output Contract
- Complete copy asset in the requested format
- Emotional journey map (entry → arousal → resolution → action)
- Dead zone audit results (0 dead zones = ship; 1-2 = revise; 3+ = rebuild)
- Dopamine sequencing score (1-10 using genius rubric)
