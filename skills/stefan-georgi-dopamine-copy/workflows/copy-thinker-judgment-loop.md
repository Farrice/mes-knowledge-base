# Copy Thinker Judgment Loop

> Georgi's AI-era production method: AI wins on outputs, humans win on
> judgment. Generate wide with AI, select via pattern recognition grounded
> in ad-library evidence, then run the congruence-edit critique loop. The
> writing is the cheap step — the paid skill is choosing.

## Expert
Stefan Georgi — Dopamine Copywriting Architecture (RMBC 2.0 layer)

## Preconditions
- Unified research document exists (run `awareness-distribution-map` first, or supply equivalent). **AI is an amplifier, not an originator** — "write me a headline" with no research amplifies nothing.
- You know the asset's funnel context: traffic source, position in journey, what it hands off to. No asset gets written in a vacuum.

## Phase 1: Generate Wide (AI's Job)

With the research document loaded, have AI produce volume: 50-100 hooks, 5-10 mechanism candidates, or multiple full-draft variants. Expect ~10-15% to be genuinely good. Do not polish anything at this stage.

## Phase 2: Judge Narrow (Your Job)

Testing 100 hooks is prohibitively expensive; judgment replaces budget. Score candidates against **pattern-recognition evidence**, not taste:

1. **Ad-library recurrence check**: Search the ads libraries (Meta Ad Library; Kalodata for TikTok; native spy tools) for the underlying pattern of each shortlisted hook. A pattern that keeps recurring for high-spend, high-test-volume brands (e.g., "don't make this mistake…" formats) is pre-validated — weight it up.
2. **Awareness fit**: Does the hook meet the target segment at ITS awareness level (from the distribution map)?
3. **Big-idea differentiation**: Against the 5 competitor big ideas — is this different, or the same promise in new words?

Greenlight rule: for every candidate you advance, you can name WHERE you've seen the pattern win before. If you can't, it goes to the "unproven — small test budget only" pile.

(Optional AI assist: "Here are the 5 candidates I like most. Based on this research, rank 1-5 by probability of success and why.")

## Phase 3: Draft via AI, Structure via You

Have AI assemble the full asset from the winning idea + research document. Your role during drafting is structural: does each section fit the journey, is the length calibrated to awareness + price point, does the piece hand off cleanly to the next funnel step?

## Phase 4: The Congruence-Edit Critique Loop

Georgi's actual editing pass on AI drafts is not line polish — "most editing is removing the fluff that's getting in the way of the big idea." Iterate with directives at the section level:

- **Congruence**: "We said [X] above — this section contradicts/ignores it. Fix."
- **Meat**: "We glossed over [section] but it's actually the important part. Expand with substance from the research doc."
- **Fluff**: "This paragraph is in the way of the big idea. Cut it."
- **Metaphor audit**: "This metaphor is stupid / doesn't map. Replace or delete."

Repeat until every section either advances the big idea or is gone. (Then, and only then, run the dopamine-audit workflow on the surviving copy.)

## Phase 5: Feedback Loop (Judgment Compounding)

Judgment is trained, not innate. Keep the loop live:
- Log which greenlit patterns actually won in-market; your Phase 2 weighting improves each cycle
- Periodically buy competitor front-ends ($200-300) and walk the full checkout → upsell path — ads libraries show the front door, purchases show the money architecture
- Cheap live reps (e.g., TikTok Shop-style rapid posting) give faster hook feedback than any swipe file

## Quality Gate
Before advancing any candidate past Phase 2, verify:
- [ ] The unified research document was loaded before generation (Phase 1) — not zero-context prompting
- [ ] Every greenlit candidate has a named pattern-evidence source (ad library, spy tool, or a prior in-market win) — "I like it" is not a pass
- [ ] Awareness fit and big-idea differentiation were checked against the research doc, not against taste alone
- [ ] The congruence-edit changelog shows both cuts AND expansions — a changelog that's only cuts means the "meat" directive (Phase 4) was skipped
- [ ] A judgment log entry is written before delivery (pattern bet + prior evidence), so this cycle compounds the next one

Ship only when every greenlit candidate can answer "where has this pattern won before?"

## Output Contract
- Shortlist of greenlit candidates, each annotated with its pattern-evidence source
- Final asset drafted by AI, structurally directed by you
- Congruence-edit changelog (what was cut as fluff, what got meat, what was incongruent)
- Judgment log entry: patterns bet on + where they'd won before (graded later against results)
