# Mechanism Architect

> Build unique mechanisms using the "Missing 1%" framework. The mechanism
> is where curiosity resolves into dopamine payoff — it must feel like
> a discovery, not an instruction.

## Expert
Stefan Georgi — Dopamine Copywriting Architecture

## Inputs Required
1. **Product/Offer**: What's being sold
2. **Core Ingredient/Process**: The actual thing that makes the product work
3. **Audience's Failed Attempts**: What have they tried before? Why did it fail?
4. **Competitive Mechanisms**: What mechanisms do competitors claim? (to avoid duplication)

## Phase 1: Problem Mechanism Construction

Build the "why everything else fails" narrative:

### Step 1: The Hidden Saboteur
Identify the ONE invisible factor that explains all previous failures:
- It must be something the audience has never considered
- It must be verifiable (or at least plausible with research backing)
- It must reframe ALL past failures as "you were close, but missing this one thing"

### Step 2: The Paradigm Shift Statement
Compress the problem mechanism into one sentence:
> "The reason [past approach] hasn't worked isn't [what they think] — it's [hidden factor]."

### Step 3: Evidence Architecture
Build the proof stack for the problem mechanism:
- Research citation (study, paper, expert quote)
- Analogy that makes the mechanism visceral ("It's like trying to fill a bathtub with the drain open")
- Counter-argument preemption ("You might think it's [obvious thing], but...")

## Phase 2: Solution Mechanism Construction

Build the "here's the missing piece" reveal:

### Step 1: The Discovery Frame
Position the solution as a FINDING, not a product:
- "Scientists at [institution] discovered..."
- "A [nationality] [profession] stumbled upon..."
- "After [N years] of research, a team finally identified..."

### Step 2: The Mechanism Name
Create a proprietary name that:
- Sounds scientific but is accessible (e.g., "Cellular Reset Protocol")
- Contains the benefit implied in the name
- Is NOT the product name — it's the PROCESS name
- Passes the "cocktail party test" — someone could explain it in one sentence

### Step 3: The "Aha" Architecture
Structure the mechanism reveal for maximum dopamine:
1. **Tease**: "And when they looked closer, they found something remarkable..."
2. **Build**: "This one [compound/process/discovery] was responsible for..."
3. **Reveal**: "They called it [Mechanism Name] — and it works by [simple explanation]"
4. **Proof**: "In a [study/trial], participants who [used mechanism] saw [specific result]"
5. **Implication**: "Which means that if YOU [do mechanism], you could..."

## Phase 3: Mechanism Uniqueness Audit

Score the mechanism against the market:

| Criterion | Score 1-10 |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Novelty**: Has this mechanism been used before by competitors? | |
| **Believability**: Would a skeptic find this plausible? | |
| **Specificity**: Is the mechanism precise enough to visualize? | |
| **Explanatory Power**: Does it explain why EVERYTHING else failed? | |
| **Memory Stickiness**: Can someone repeat the mechanism name + concept 24h later? | |

**Ship at 35+. Rebuild below 25.**

## Phase 4: Mechanism-to-Product Bridge

The mechanism must connect to the product without feeling like a "bait and switch":

1. The product is the **delivery vehicle** for the mechanism
2. The mechanism is the **reason** the product works
3. The conversation shifts from "should I buy?" to "how do I access this mechanism?"

### Bridge Template:
> "Now, the challenge with [Mechanism Name] is that it's not easy to get on your own.
> You'd need to [difficult/expensive alternatives]. That's why [product name] was created —
> it's the first [product type] specifically designed to [deliver the mechanism]."

## Quality Gate
Before delivering the mechanism, verify:
- [ ] The Mechanism Uniqueness Audit (Phase 3) totals 35+ — the workflow's own ship threshold; 25-34 needs targeted rework, below 25 needs a full rebuild
- [ ] The Problem Mechanism's paradigm-shift statement follows the exact template ("The reason [past approach] hasn't worked isn't [what they think] — it's [hidden factor]") without lapsing into a generic claim
- [ ] The Mechanism Name passes the cocktail-party test — write the one-sentence explanation and confirm it could be repeated by someone else without notes
- [ ] The Solution Mechanism's "Aha" Architecture hits all 5 steps (Tease/Build/Reveal/Proof/Implication) in order — no skipped steps
- [ ] The Mechanism-to-Product Bridge does not read as a bait-and-switch — the product must read as the delivery vehicle for the mechanism, not an unrelated add-on

Do not hand off a mechanism scoring below 35 to any downstream copy workflow.

## Output Contract
- Complete Problem Mechanism with paradigm shift statement + evidence
- Complete Solution Mechanism with proprietary name + "aha" architecture
- Mechanism Uniqueness Score (1-50)
- Mechanism-to-Product Bridge copy
- One-sentence "cocktail party" explanation of the full mechanism
