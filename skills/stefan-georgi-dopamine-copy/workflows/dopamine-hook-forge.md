# Dopamine Hook Forge

> Generate 10-20 dopamine-triggering hooks for any product or offer.
> Each hook is engineered to get dopamine flowing within 3-5 seconds
> using emotion, curiosity, or both.

## Expert
Stefan Georgi — Dopamine Copywriting Architecture

## Inputs Required
1. **Product/Offer**: What's being sold
2. **Target Audience**: Who sees this hook
3. **Platform**: Where the hook will appear (email subject, ad headline, video intro, social caption, VSL open)
4. **Awareness Level**: Unaware | Problem-aware | Solution-aware | Product-aware

## Phase 1: Emotion-Curiosity Matrix

For the given product, map available triggers:

| Emotion | Stimulus Pathway | Hook Potential |
|---------|-----------------|----------------|
| Fear | Physical event / Imagined future | ? |
| Anger | Social interaction / Status threat | ? |
| Surprise | Counterintuitive fact / Paradox | ? |
| Enjoyment | Remembered event / Nostalgia | ? |
| Disgust | Physical event / Moral violation | ? |
| Sadness | Loss / Time passage | ? |
| Contempt | Social comparison / Hypocrisy | ? |

Fill in the "Hook Potential" column with specific angles for THIS product. Mark the top 3 most potent combinations.

## Phase 2: Hook Generation

Generate 10-20 hooks across these categories:

### Category A: Paradoxical Hooks (3-5 hooks)
Use Pattern 5 templates:
- **French Paradox**: "Why does [Group A] do [unhealthy thing] yet [healthy outcome]?"
- **Pimsleur Paradox**: "Why is [easy thing] so hard for [capable people]?"
- **Lifespan Paradox**: "Why does [inferior entity] outperform [superior entity]?"

### Category B: Nostalgic Hooks (2-4 hooks)
Fire hippocampus-mediated memory through specific sensory details from ages 12-19:
- Open with a question that triggers episodic memory
- Include at least one sensory detail (smell, sound, texture)
- Bridge from memory to product territory

### Category C: Incomplete/Ambiguous Hooks (2-4 hooks)
Use Pattern 16 — Strategic Incompleteness:
- Captions where the viewer cannot determine the conclusion
- Statements that break mid-thought
- Questions with implied answers that contradict expectations

### Category D: Emotion-Bomb Hooks (3-5 hooks)
Pure emotional detonation within 3-5 seconds:
- Each hook fires 2+ Ekman emotions simultaneously
- Must create an involuntary emotional response before intellectual processing
- Fear + Curiosity and Anger + Surprise are the highest-performing combinations

## Phase 3: Scoring & Tiering

Score each hook 1-10 on three dimensions:

| Hook | Dopamine Speed (3-5 sec?) | Curiosity Debt (must they continue?) | Emotional Charge (involuntary response?) | TOTAL |
|------|--------------------------|--------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|-------|

**Tier the results**:
- **Tier 1 (Score 25-30)**: Ship immediately — these are weapons
- **Tier 2 (Score 18-24)**: Strong — test in rotation
- **Tier 3 (Score <18)**: Rebuild or discard

## Phase 4: Platform Adaptation

For Tier 1 hooks, create platform-native variants:
- **Email Subject Line**: ≤50 characters, curiosity-first
- **Facebook/Instagram Ad**: First line must fire before "See More" truncation
- **Video/Reel**: 3-second spoken version + visual pairing
- **YouTube Thumbnail**: Caption that forces click-to-resolve

## Quality Gate
Before delivering the hook set, verify:
- [ ] 10-20 hooks generated across all 4 categories (Paradoxical, Nostalgic, Incomplete/Ambiguous, Emotion-Bomb) — not concentrated in one category
- [ ] Every hook is scored on all 3 dimensions (Dopamine Speed, Curiosity Debt, Emotional Charge) — no blank cells in the scoring table
- [ ] Tier 1 hooks (score 25-30) have platform-native variants for at least 2 platforms
- [ ] The recommended A/B test pair names WHY the two hooks are a meaningful contrast (e.g., paradox mechanism vs. emotion-bomb mechanism), not two hooks that feel interchangeable
- [ ] No hook resolves its own curiosity gap within the hook itself — a hook that answers its own question isn't a hook

Ship only Tier 1 and Tier 2 hooks; Tier 3 hooks (score <18) go back for rebuild, not into the deliverable.

## Output Contract
- 10-20 scored and tiered hooks
- Emotion-Curiosity Matrix for the product
- Top 5 hooks with platform-native variants
- Recommended A/B test pairs (pair highest-scoring paradox vs. highest-scoring emotion-bomb)
