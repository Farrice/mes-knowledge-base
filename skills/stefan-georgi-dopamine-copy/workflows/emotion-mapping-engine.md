# Emotion Mapping Engine

> Map any product, offer, or idea to Ekman's 7 universal emotions with
> specific stimulus pathways. Produces the emotional ammunition that
> feeds every other Georgi workflow.

## Expert
Stefan Georgi — Dopamine Copywriting Architecture

## Inputs Required
1. **Product/Offer**: What's being sold + core promise
2. **Target Audience**: Demographics, psychographics, current emotional baseline
3. **Competitive Landscape** (optional): What emotions are competitors already using?

## Phase 1: Ekman Emotion Inventory

For each of the 7 universal emotions, identify ALL available stimulus pathways for this product:

### Emotion: Fear
- **Physical Event**: What physical consequence does the audience fear?
- **Social Interaction**: What social rejection or status loss do they fear?
- **Remembered Event**: What past failure or trauma relates?
- **Imagined Event**: What future scenario terrifies them?
- **Reenacted Experience**: What repeated pattern haunts them?

### Emotion: Anger
- **Who/what are they angry AT** about this problem?
- **What injustice** surrounds their situation?
- **What hypocrisy** from authorities/experts fuels rage?
- **What systemic failure** keeps them stuck?

### Emotion: Surprise
- **What counterintuitive fact** about their problem exists?
- **What hidden truth** contradicts common advice?
- **What unexpected connection** between seemingly unrelated things?
- **What research finding** would shock them?

### Emotion: Enjoyment
- **What past success** can they remember and re-experience?
- **What nostalgic memory** connects to the product domain?
- **What sensory pleasure** does the solution promise?
- **What social enjoyment** (belonging, praise) awaits?

### Emotion: Sadness
- **What have they lost** because of this problem?
- **What time has passed** that they can't get back?
- **What relationship damage** has occurred?
- **What potential went unrealized?**

### Emotion: Disgust
- **What about their current situation is revolting?**
- **What moral violation** surrounds the problem?
- **What "gross" reality** are they avoiding?

### Emotion: Contempt
- **Who do they look down on** in this space?
- **What lazy/dishonest behavior** triggers their contempt?
- **What false expertise** can be exposed?

## Phase 2: Stimulus Pathway Scoring

For each emotion × pathway combination, score:
- **Intensity (1-10)**: How strongly does this trigger fire?
- **Universality (1-10)**: What % of the audience shares this trigger?
- **Novelty (1-10)**: Has this trigger been overused in the market?

**Priority Matrix**: Plot Intensity × Universality. High-High quadrant = primary ammunition.

## Phase 3: Emotional Sequence Architecture

Design the optimal emotional journey for a full sales asset:

```
Entry Emotion: _________ (what they feel arriving)
     ↓
Disruption Emotion: _________ (what jolts them into attention — surprise, fear, anger)
     ↓
Rapport Emotion: _________ (what makes them feel understood — sadness, enjoyment)
     ↓
Resolution Emotion: _________ (what the mechanism creates — surprise, enjoyment)
     ↓
Action Emotion: _________ (what drives the purchase — fear of loss, enjoyment of gain)
```

**Rule**: Never use the same emotion twice in sequence. The journey must modulate.

## Phase 4: Emotional Ammunition Cards

For the top 6 emotion × pathway combinations, create Ammunition Cards:

```
EMOTION: [Name]
PATHWAY: [Physical / Social / Remembered / Imagined / Reenacted]
TRIGGER SENTENCE: [One sentence that fires this emotion]
INTENSITY: [1-10]
BEST POSITION: [Lead / Rapport / Mechanism / Close]
STACKING PARTNER: [Which other emotion pairs well?]
```

## Quality Gate
Before delivering the emotional ammunition, verify:
- [ ] All 7 Ekman emotions have at least one populated stimulus pathway for this specific product — a blank emotion row is a missed pathway, not evidence the emotion doesn't apply
- [ ] Priority Matrix scores (Intensity × Universality) are filled for every emotion × pathway combination carried into Phase 2, not just the obvious ones
- [ ] The Emotional Sequence Architecture (Phase 3) never repeats the same emotion twice in sequence
- [ ] All 6 Ammunition Cards have a complete, specific Trigger Sentence — not a placeholder describing what the sentence should do
- [ ] The competitive emotion gap analysis names at least 1 emotion pathway competitors are currently leaving unused

An emotion map with unscored pathways or a placeholder trigger sentence is not ready to feed downstream Georgi workflows.

## Output Contract
- Complete 7-emotion inventory with all stimulus pathways mapped
- Scored priority matrix (Intensity × Universality)
- Emotional sequence architecture for the sales asset
- 6 Ammunition Cards ready for deployment in any Georgi workflow
- Competitive emotion gap analysis (what emotions aren't being used by competitors)
