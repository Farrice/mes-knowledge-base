# Post-Purchase Dopamine Drip

> Build post-purchase email sequences and upsell architecture that
> prevents the neurochemical crash after buying. The anticipation
> (wanting) was the dopamine peak — the purchase resolution creates
> a crash that causes refunds and upsell rejection if not managed.

## Expert
Stefan Georgi — Dopamine Copywriting Architecture

## Inputs Required
1. **Product Purchased**: What they just bought
2. **Upsell Product(s)**: What's being offered next (if applicable)
3. **Delivery Timeline**: When does the customer receive/access the product?
4. **Average Order Value**: For calibrating reaffirmation intensity

## Phase 1: The 15-Minute Reaffirmation Email

Send within 15 minutes of purchase (before the dopamine crash sets in):

### Structure:
```
SUBJECT LINE: [Reaffirmation — "You just made the best decision of [year]"]
     ↓
PARAGRAPH 1 (200+ words of genuine praise):
- "You're among the smartest X% who actually took action..."
- Specific praise for their decision-making quality
- Social proof: "You're joining [number] of others who..."
- Zero selling. Pure reaffirmation.
     ↓
PARAGRAPH 2 (New curiosity/emotion hook):
- "But here's something most people don't know about [product]..."
- Open a NEW curiosity gap about the product they just bought
- This gap can seed the upsell without explicitly selling
     ↓
PARAGRAPH 3 (Anticipation builder):
- Create anticipation for what's coming next
- "In the next [timeframe], you're going to experience..."
- Future-pace the positive emotions of using the product
```

**Critical Rule**: 200+ words of reaffirmation BEFORE any selling or teasing. This is dopamine maintenance, not a sales pitch.

## Phase 2: The Upsell Dopamine Bridge

If there's an upsell, introduce it using the Dopamine Bridge:

### The Bridge Architecture:
1. **Extend the emotional state** from the main purchase reaffirmation
2. **Create a NEW curiosity gap**: "But there's one thing [product] doesn't include that could [amplify benefit]..."
3. **Small mechanism tease**: Not a full mechanism — just enough to create the itch
4. **Urgency through loss**: "This is only available for the next [timeframe] because..."
5. **Frame as enhancement, not separate purchase**: "Think of this as the [turbo/premium/missing piece] version"

### Upsell Emotional Sequence:
```
Reaffirmation (elevated dopamine from purchase validation)
     ↓
New Gap (dopamine stays elevated because new curiosity opens)
     ↓
Enhancement Frame (the upsell is MORE of what made them feel good)
     ↓
Loss Aversion (not getting the upsell = incomplete experience)
```

## Phase 3: 48-Hour Dopamine Drip Sequence

Design a 3-email sequence over 48 hours that maintains elevated emotional state:

### Email 1 (15 minutes post-purchase)
- Pure reaffirmation + new curiosity gap
- Seed upsell if applicable

### Email 2 (6-12 hours post-purchase)
- "Here's something I forgot to mention..."
- Deliver a genuine useful insight about using the product
- This trains the brain: "Emails from this brand = dopamine"
- Close with anticipation for Email 3

### Email 3 (24-48 hours post-purchase)
- Social proof from other buyers ("Look what [Name] achieved in just [timeframe]...")
- Trigger enjoyment + hope through results stories
- If upsell: final close with enhanced loss aversion
- If no upsell: plant seeds for future purchase through forward-looking curiosity gap

## Phase 4: Refund Prevention Architecture

The post-purchase crash is structural — prevent it with:

1. **Immediate Value Delivery**: First actionable tip within 15 minutes
2. **Progress Anchoring**: "You're already ahead of 90% of people because you [action they took]"
3. **Community Proof**: Show activity from other buyers (even if it's just testimonials)
4. **Sunk Cost Activation**: "You've already unlocked [benefit 1] — wait until you see [benefit 2-5]"
5. **Anticipation Loops**: Always end each touchpoint with "coming next..."

## Phase 5: Sequence Quality Gate

Before deploying, verify:

- [ ] 200+ words of reaffirmation before ANY selling in Email 1
- [ ] No dead zones in any email (every sentence builds curiosity, triggers emotion, or resolves a gap)
- [ ] Dopamine state is maintained or elevated across all 3 emails (no crash points)
- [ ] Loss aversion framing is present in upsell sections
- [ ] Each email ends with an open loop or anticipation builder
- [ ] Timing is calibrated (15 min → 6-12 hours → 24-48 hours)

## Output Contract
- Complete 3-email post-purchase sequence
- Upsell dopamine bridge (if applicable)
- Refund prevention architecture
- Emotional state map across the 48-hour sequence
- Quality gate pass/fail for each email
