# Paradox Generator

> Build paradoxical question architecture that creates mandatory cognitive
> engagement. Paradoxes trigger the brain's contradiction-detection system,
> producing curiosity that cannot be voluntarily suppressed.

## Expert
Stefan Georgi — Dopamine Copywriting Architecture

## Inputs Required
1. **Product/Offer**: What's being sold
2. **Core Benefit**: The primary outcome or transformation
3. **Audience Beliefs**: What does the audience currently believe about the problem?
4. **Industry/Niche**: The domain for research-based paradox sourcing

## Phase 1: Paradox Mining

Identify real-world contradictions related to the product's domain:

### Mining Method A: The Comparison Paradox
Find two entities where outcomes contradict expectations:
- Same inputs, different outputs → "Why does A get [result] but B doesn't?"
- Different inputs, same outputs → "How do both A and B achieve [result] from opposite approaches?"
- Inferior entity, superior result → "Why does [smaller/weaker] outperform [bigger/stronger]?"

### Mining Method B: The Belief Paradox
Find facts that contradict common audience beliefs:
- "Everyone knows X is true, but research shows Y"
- "The group doing [bad behavior] has [good outcome]"
- "The expert advice says A, but the data says B"

### Mining Method C: The Timeline Paradox
Find temporal contradictions:
- "Ancient people did X that modern science just discovered"
- "This was known in [year] but forgotten until [trigger event]"
- "The solution existed for [decades] but was suppressed/ignored because..."

Generate 5-8 raw paradoxes per method. Total: 15-24 candidates.

## Phase 2: Paradox Architecture

For the top 5 paradoxes, build the full curiosity architecture:

### Structure Template
```
[Paradoxical Question — 1-2 sentences]
     ↓
[Amplification — deepen the contradiction with specifics]
     ↓
[Emotional Layer — add jealousy, fear, or anger to the curiosity]
     ↓
[Implied Promise — hint that the resolution is connected to the product]
     ↓
[Open Loop — do NOT resolve. Leave the gap open.]
```

### Emotional Layering Guide
Layer emotions ONTO the curiosity gap:
- **French Paradox structure** → add jealousy (they get to enjoy AND be healthy)
- **Pimsleur Paradox structure** → add status threat (if this is easy, why can't I do it?)
- **Lifespan Paradox structure** → add fear (am I aging because of something I don't know?)

## Phase 3: Paradox Stacking

Build 2 "Paradox Stack" sequences for leads — 2-3 nested paradoxes where:
1. Paradox 1 opens a curiosity gap
2. Before resolving Paradox 1, Paradox 2 opens a second gap
3. Paradox 3 compounds the cognitive debt
4. The reader now has 3 unresolved contradictions — the dopamine debt forces continuation

**Stack Rule**: Each paradox must ESCALATE the stakes or specificity. Don't repeat the same emotional register.

## Phase 4: Validation

Score each paradox 1-10:
- **Contradiction Strength**: How clearly do the facts contradict each other? (1-10)
- **Verifiability**: Can the reader fact-check this and find it's true? (1-10)
- **Emotional Amplification**: Does the paradox trigger emotion beyond curiosity? (1-10)

**Ship paradoxes scoring 24+. Rebuild those below 18.**

## Quality Gate
Before delivering the paradox set, verify:
- [ ] 15-24 raw candidates generated across all 3 mining methods (Comparison, Belief, Timeline) — not concentrated in one method
- [ ] The top 5 fully architected paradoxes each complete the full 5-step structure template (Question → Amplification → Emotional Layer → Implied Promise → Open Loop)
- [ ] Every paradox is validated on all 3 dimensions (Contradiction Strength, Verifiability, Emotional Amplification) — no partially-scored candidates in the final set
- [ ] Both Paradox Stack sequences (Phase 3) escalate stakes or specificity across their nested paradoxes — no repeated emotional register between stacked paradoxes
- [ ] The workflow's own ship threshold is enforced: only paradoxes scoring 24+ advance; sub-18 candidates are cut, not softened into the deliverable

A paradox that isn't fact-checkable (fails Verifiability) is a lie, not a hook — cut it regardless of its other scores.

## Output Contract
- 15-24 raw paradox candidates across 3 mining methods
- Top 5 fully architected paradoxes with emotional layering
- 2 complete Paradox Stack sequences ready for lead insertion
- Validation scores for all candidates
