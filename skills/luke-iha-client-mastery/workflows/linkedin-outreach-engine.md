# LinkedIn CS Outreach Engine

## Purpose
Build a LinkedIn-specific client acquisition system for creative strategists. Combines profile positioning, content-driven lead generation ("Trapdoor Posts"), and systematic 5-10 daily outreach.

## When to Use
- When LinkedIn is your primary or secondary client acquisition channel
- When you want to generate inbound leads through content
- When you need to complement Upwork with a professional presence

---

## Step 1: Profile Optimization for Creative Strategists

### Completion Target
LinkedIn profile that functions as a landing page, not a resume.

### Process
1. **Headline formula**: "[Result you deliver] for [specific client type] | [Proof point]"
   - Example: "I write ads that scale DTC brands past $1M/month | $4.2M in tracked ad spend"
   - Example: "Creative Strategist for supplement brands | 3.2x avg ROAS across 12 accounts"
2. **Banner image**: Portfolio showcase — 3-4 winning ad screenshots or results
3. **About section**: Problem → Your approach → Proof → CTA
   - Paragraph 1: The problem DTC brands face (creative fatigue, Andromeda demands)
   - Paragraph 2: Your system (AI-orchestrated production + human judgment)
   - Paragraph 3: Proof (results, client names, testimonials)
   - Paragraph 4: CTA ("DM me 'creative' if you need a strategist")
4. **Featured section**: Pin 2-3 "How I" case study posts + portfolio link
5. **Experience**: Frame past roles through the creative strategy lens

## Step 2: Trapdoor Post Strategy (Content-Driven Lead Gen)

### Completion Target
A repeatable system for generating inbound leads through high-value LinkedIn posts.

### Process

**The Trapdoor Post Formula**:
1. **Hook**: Teach something specific about your process (an ad breakdown, a framework, a result)
2. **Body**: Deliver genuine value — show your thinking, not just your conclusions
3. **Trapdoor**: Offer a downloadable asset in exchange for engagement
   - "Comment 'PLAYBOOK' and I'll send you my complete creative diversity framework"
   - "DM me 'FRAMEWORK' for the actual brief template I use with clients"
4. **Asset**: The downloadable serves as a mini-portfolio — it showcases your expertise and drives discovery calls

**Post Types That Work for CS**:
| Post Type | Example | Frequency |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| Ad Breakdown | "I broke down why this ad spent $340K. Here's what I found:" | 2x/week |
| Framework Share | "The 6 copy blocks that improve every ad (with examples):" | 1x/week |
| Result Story | "How I cut CPA by 42% for [brand] in 3 weeks:" | 1x/week |
| Market Insight | "Meta Andromeda changed everything. Here's what DTC brands need now:" | 1x/week |

**Engagement Cadence**:
- Post 4-5x per week
- Respond to every comment within 2 hours
- DM everyone who engages with the Trapdoor within 24 hours
- Follow up on DM conversations within 48 hours

## Step 3: Outreach System (5-10 Daily Actions)

### Completion Target
Systematic daily outreach that compounds over time.

### Process

**Daily 5-10 Actions**:
1. **3-5 Connection Requests**: Target DTC brand founders, marketing directors, agency owners
   - Always include a personalized note referencing something specific
   - Never pitch in the connection request
2. **2-3 Engagement Actions**: Comment substantively on prospects' posts
   - Show your expertise by adding value ("Great point. I'd add that Andromeda also rewards...")
   - Comment BEFORE you connect — warm the relationship
3. **2-3 Direct Outreach**: Message warm connections or respond to job posts
   - Template: "Hey [name], I noticed [specific thing about their brand/ads]. I actually wrote some ads for a similar brand and [brief result]. Would it be cool if I shared what I put together?"
   - **Never**: Send a cold pitch without prior engagement

**Tracking System**:
| Metric | Daily Target | Weekly Target |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| Connection requests sent | 3-5 | 15-25 |
| Substantive comments | 2-3 | 10-15 |
| DM conversations started | 2-3 | 10-15 |
| Discovery calls booked | — | 2-3 |
| Proposals sent | — | 1-2 |

## Step 4: DM-to-Call Conversion Sequence

### Completion Target
User has a natural conversation flow from DM to discovery call.

### Process

**Message 1 (Value Lead)**:
"Hey [name], I saw your brand running some solid ads. I actually broke down what's working in [their niche] and put together some angles I think could be strong for [brand name]. Want me to send over what I found?"

**Message 2 (After they say yes — Value Delivery)**:
Share 2-3 specific ad concepts or a mini-audit of their current creative. Not a sales pitch — genuine value.

**Message 3 (Bridge to Call)**:
"Happy this was helpful! I have a few more ideas that are more specific to your funnel. Would a quick 15-min call make sense? I can show you the system I use to [specific result]. No pitch — just want to see if there's a fit."

**On the Call**:
1. Ask about their current creative process and pain points (5 min)
2. Show your system and how it addresses their specific gaps (5 min)
3. Propose an entry offer: "What if I wrote 5 ads for free, and if they perform, we talk about a monthly arrangement?" (5 min)
