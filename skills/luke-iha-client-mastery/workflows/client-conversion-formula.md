# Client Conversion Formula

## Purpose
Deploy Luke Iha's CR × OU formula to diagnose and fix client acquisition bottlenecks. Every freelance business problem reduces to either a conversion rate problem or a volume problem — this workflow identifies which one and prescribes the fix.

## When to Use
- When outreach isn't producing clients and you don't know why
- When you're "doing everything right" but still not getting hired
- When you need a diagnostic framework before panicking about positioning or skill gaps
- When coaching someone who's stuck in freelance acquisition

## Step 1: The Formula

### Completion Target
User understands the two variables and can diagnose their bottleneck.

### The Equation
```
Clients = Conversion Rate × Outreach Units
```

**Outreach Units (OU)**: Total number of outreach actions per day.
- Upwork proposals sent
- LinkedIn connection requests + DMs
- Cold emails sent
- Engagements that lead to conversations
- **Luke's minimum**: "5-10 per day across Upwork, LinkedIn, and cold email"

**Conversion Rate (CR)**: Percentage of outreach that converts to paying clients.
- Measured as: Clients won ÷ Total outreach actions
- Healthy range: 2-5% for cold outreach, 10-20% for warm referrals
- CR is determined by the **POP Stack**: Positioning × Offer × Proof

### The Diagnostic
"Every time I talk to a freelancer who's struggling, this is how I think about it:

1. Are you actually doing enough outreach? (Volume)
2. Is your outreach converting? (Conversion)

It's always volume. They're like, 'Oh I do like two.' Every other day. So they're completely lying to themselves about their effort."

## Step 3: The POP Stack Audit (Conversion Rate Fix)

### Completion Target
User has identified which element of CR is broken and has a fix plan.

### P — Positioning
**Definition**: "The story you tell yourself, what clients think of you."

**Diagnostic questions**:
- Can you state in one sentence what you do, for whom, and why they should care?
- Would a prospect immediately know if you're the right fit for their specific problem?
- Are you positioned as a "creative strategist" or a generic "copywriter"?

**If broken**: Run `/creative-strategist-launcher` Step 3 for positioning statement work.

### O — Offer
**Definition**: The structure of what you're selling and how they buy it.

**Diagnostic questions**:
- Are you offering entry-level access (free sample, trial, low-risk project)?
- Do you have a clear escalation path (trial → project → retainer)?
- Is your pricing transparent or is it a mystery?

**If broken**: Structure a 3-tier offer:
| Tier | What | Price | Purpose |
|------|------|-------|---------|
| Entry | 3-5 free/spec ads | $0 | Prove your skill |
| Project | 10-15 ads for one campaign | $500-1,500 | Build track record |
| Retainer | Monthly creative strategy | $2-5K/month | Recurring revenue |

### P — Proof (80-90% of CR)
**Definition**: Evidence that you can deliver what you promise.

**Diagnostic questions**:
- Where are you on the 7-rung Proof Ladder?
- What's your highest completed rung?
- Would a stranger believe your claims based on your portfolio alone?

**If broken**: Run `/proof-ladder-sprint` immediately. "By far 80-90% of this depends on your proof."

**Luke's critical insight**: "Your proof is coupled to your skill. There are ways to fake it short term. But getting really good and getting hard-to-fake proof is the cheat code."

**The uncomfortable truth**: "People who just get more and more proof but skill doesn't catch up... they usually make more money." Proof outweighs skill in the short term. But skill + proof is the long game.

## Step 4: Daily Outreach System

### Completion Target
A sustainable, trackable daily outreach routine.

### The 60-Minute Power Block
| Time | Action | Volume |
|------|--------|--------|
| 0-15 min | Upwork: Find 3 jobs + send customized proposals | 3 proposals |
| 15-30 min | LinkedIn: Send 3 connection requests + 2 DMs to warm connections | 5 actions |
| 30-45 min | LinkedIn/Community: Substantive comments on 3 prospects' posts | 3 engagements |
| 45-60 min | Follow-ups: Bump all active conversations (proposals, DMs, testimonial asks) | Variable |
| **TOTAL** | | **11+ actions** |

### Sustainability Rules
1. **Same time every day** — habit > motivation
2. **Track everything** — spreadsheet or CRM, no guessing
3. **Never zero days** — even 3 actions > nothing
4. **Weekend optional** — but catching up on Sunday is smart
5. **Review weekly** — what's working? What channels? What messages?

---

## Step 5: Bottleneck Escalation

### Process
After 2 weeks of tracked data:

**If volume was low and now it's high** → Give it 2 more weeks. Volume takes time to compound.

**If volume is high but CR is <1%** → Your POP stack needs work. Prioritize:
1. First: Proof (80-90% of the issue)
2. Second: Positioning (are you reaching the right people?)
3. Last: Offer (are you making it easy to say yes?)

**If volume is high and CR is 2-5%** → You're on track. Focus on:
1. Increasing close rate on discovery calls
2. Improving proposal conversion specifically
3. Getting more referrals from current clients (highest CR channel)

**If CR is >5%** → You're doing great. Focus on:
1. Raising prices (you're undercharging)
2. Reducing volume to prevent burnout
3. Systemizing to scale (hire a VA for outreach, focus on judgment)
