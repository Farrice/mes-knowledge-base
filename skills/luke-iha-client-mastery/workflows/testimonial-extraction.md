# Testimonial Extraction Engine

## Purpose
Systematically collect testimonials from past/current clients using Luke Iha's ghost-write method, relentless follow-up cadence, and fear-override psychology. Testimonials are Rung 4 on the Proof Ladder and the first "unfakeable" proof.

## When to Use
- When you've done work and haven't asked for testimonials yet
- When running `/proof-ladder-sprint` and hitting Rung 4
- When client responses have stalled and you need follow-up scripts
- When starting from zero and need character testimonials

---

## Step 1: Testimonial Target List

### Completion Target
Comprehensive list of every person who could give you a testimonial.

### Process
1. **Past clients** (any paid work — ads, writing, design, consulting)
2. **Pro-bono/free work** recipients
3. **Colleagues** who've seen your work quality
4. **Mentors/professors** who can speak to your ability
5. **Community members** who've benefited from your advice
6. **Friends/family who know your work ethic** (last resort — character testimonials only)

**Target**: Minimum 10-15 people on the list. You'll get 5-10 to respond.

---

## Step 2: Ghost-Write the Testimonial

### Completion Target
Pre-written testimonials ready to send for approval.

### Template
```
Before working with [your name], [before state — be specific]:
"I was struggling with [specific problem]. We were [measurable before metric]."

After working with [your name], [after state — tangible]:
"[Specific result or change]. We went from [before] to [after]."

What it was like working with [your name]:
"[Working relationship quality — reliable, creative, proactive]. 
I'd recommend [your name] to [specific type of person/business]."
```

### Examples

**For a DTC brand owner**:
"Before working with Alex, we were spending $5K/month on ads but creative was stale and ROAS was declining. After Alex came on board, they produced 15 new ad variations in the first week alone and identified two winning angles we hadn't considered. Our ROAS went from 1.8 to 3.2 in six weeks. Alex is that rare combination of creative and analytical — they'll tell you what to test AND why. I'd recommend them to any DTC brand that takes ad performance seriously."

**For a zero-client beginner (character testimonial)**:
"I've known Sarah for two years through [community/context]. What stands out is her obsessive attention to detail and her ability to break down complex marketing concepts into actionable strategies. She helped me restructure my ad campaigns for free just because she was passionate about it. If Sarah is applying for your project, hire her — she'll over-deliver."

### Rules
1. Make it realistic — "not too insanely glowing, but exactly what it is that you want"
2. Include specific metrics when possible
3. Include a working relationship quality statement
4. Include a recommendation statement
5. Keep it 50-100 words

## Step 3: The Ask (Outreach Message)

### Completion Target
Sent the ghost-written testimonial to all targets.

### Message Template
```
Hey [name]! 

Hope you're doing great. I'm building out my portfolio and 
would love to include a testimonial from our work together.

I know you're busy, so I actually drafted something based on 
our experience — you can edit it, add to it, or just give me 
the okay to use as-is. Zero pressure either way.

Here it is:

[Paste ghost-written testimonial]

Would this work? Feel free to change anything. Thanks so much! 🙏
```

### Psychology Notes
- **Write it FOR them**: Removes the blank-page burden — they just need to approve
- **"Zero pressure"**: Reduces defensiveness
- **"Edit it or add to it"**: Gives them ownership without forcing creation
- **Short message**: Respect their time. The ask should take 30 seconds to process.

---

## Step 4: The Follow-Up Cadence (4-5 Bumps)

### Completion Target
Systematic follow-up system that gets to 80%+ response rate.

### Schedule

**Day 0**: Send initial ask
**Day 3**: Bump 1 (gentle)
```
Hey [name], just bumping this up! No rush at all — 
whenever you get a sec 😊
```

**Day 6**: Bump 2 (with humor)
```
Hey! Just floating this back up one more time. 
Feel free to just give me a 👍 if the draft works! 
[Include a funny GIF or meme]
```

**Day 10**: Bump 3 (direct but warm)
```
Hey [name], I know you're super busy. I'll only message 
you one more time about this. If the testimonial draft 
looks good, just let me know and I'll use it as-is. 
Thanks either way! 🙏
```

**Day 14**: Final bump
```
Last bump, I promise! 😄 Here's the draft one more time:
[Re-paste testimonial]
A simple "looks good" is all I need. Thanks [name]!
```

### Fear Override
Luke's key insight: "If you're a sane person and somebody is reaching out with a gentle request, chances are you're not really going to get mad."

Most people don't follow up because they fear annoying the person. The reality:
- People are busy and forget — your follow-up is a reminder, not an annoyance
- Most will feel slightly guilty for not responding — the follow-up helps them resolve that guilt
- The worst case is silence. Nobody is going to burn a relationship over a testimonial ask.

## Step 5: Video Testimonial Upgrade (Optional)

### Process
Once you have written approvals, offer the video upgrade:
```
Thanks so much for the written testimonial! 
Would you also be open to a quick 60-second video version? 
I can send you 3 bullet points to cover — literally just 
record on your phone. No pressure if not!
```

**Bullet points to send**:
1. What was the problem before we worked together?
2. What changed after we started working together?
3. Would you recommend me — and to whom?
