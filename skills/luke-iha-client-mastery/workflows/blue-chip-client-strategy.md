# Blue-Chip Client Strategy

## Purpose
Land a recognizable brand client — Proof Ladder Rung 7, the highest rung. Exploits the psychological halo effect: name recognition > raw performance numbers from unknown brands. One blue-chip client on your portfolio changes everything.

## When to Use
- When you've built track record and case studies but outreach still isn't converting
- When running `/proof-ladder-sprint` and hitting the final rung
- When you need the "stamp" that signals authority to premium prospects

## Step 1: Blue-Chip Target Identification

### Completion Target
List of 10-15 recognizable DTC brands to target.

### Where to Find Targets
1. **FunnelOfTheWeek.com** — "Most DTC brands are probably following Funnel of the Week. If you could work for ANY of the brands listed on there, even if it's for a month..."
2. **Facebook Ad Library** — Brands running high-volume creative = brands that NEED creative strategists
3. **DTC Twitter/X** — Brands frequently discussed by DTC founders
4. **Shopify Plus merchants list** — Recognizable ecom brands
5. **DTC newsletters** (Nik Sharma, Moiz Ali) — Brands they feature

### Selection Criteria
| Factor | Weight | Why |
|--------|--------|-----|
| Name recognition in DTC community | High | The entire point is the name |
| Active ad spending (visible in Ad Library) | High | Means they need creative and have budget |
| Creative gaps visible | Medium | Your "in" — solve something they're missing |
| Reasonable size (not Nike, not micro-brands) | Medium | $1-50M revenue brands are accessible |
| Industry you can speak to | Low | You can research any niche in 2 hours |

## Step 2: The Free Pitch Framework

### Completion Target
Ready-to-send pitch packages for top 5 blue-chip targets.

### Process (Per Brand — 2-3 Hours Each)

**Research (45 min)**
1. Audit their Facebook Ad Library (all active ads)
2. Note: What formats are they running? What's missing?
3. Identify the gap — do they lack statics, UGC, advertorials, awareness-level diversity?
4. Check their site — what's the mechanism, proof, promise?

**Create (60 min)**
1. Write 3-5 ads that fill the identified gap
2. Use AI to generate variations, then curate using the Feeling Test
3. Package with strategic rationale ("Why these ads, why now")
4. Make everything upload-ready — headlines, body copy, images

**Pitch (15 min)**
```
Hey [name/brand],

I've been studying your ad creative and I'm impressed — 
specifically [reference a specific winning ad]. 

I noticed a gap: you're running [what they have] but don't 
have [what's missing — e.g., awareness-level diversity / 
static interpretations of your video winners / advertorial 
content for problem-aware audiences].

I put together [3-5 ads] specifically for [brand name] that 
address this gap. I had them reviewed by [peer/community] and 
I have high conviction they'd perform.

Here they are — ready to upload, zero friction on your end:
[Attach or link to ads]

If they work, I'd love to chat about doing more together. 
If not, no hard feelings — hope the ideas are useful either way.

[Your name]
[Your one-line positioning + proof point]
```

## Step 3: Leverage Multiplication (Post-Win)

### Completion Target
System for extracting maximum value from the blue-chip relationship.

### Immediate Actions (Week 1)
1. Get explicit permission to reference the brand name
2. Request a testimonial using the ghost-write method (`/testimonial-extraction`)
3. Create a case study using the "How I" structure (`/case-study-how-i`)
4. Update every profile headline: "Creative Strategist for [Blue-Chip Brand] + X others"

### Amplification Actions (Month 1)
1. LinkedIn post: "What I learned in my first month working with [brand]" (value-first, not bragging)
2. Upwork portfolio: Blue-chip case study as the FIRST item
3. Proposals: Lead with the blue-chip name in every outreach
4. **The halo effect**: "Most companies trying to hire you will be more impressed by the names of who you work for — which is just a psychological weakness of humans. But it's very, very powerful."

### Long-Term Strategy
- Keep producing for blue-chip even at discounted rates → ongoing permission to reference
- Use the relationship to get introductions to similar-tier brands
- Stack 2-3 blue-chip names and you move to "selecting" mode — clients come to you

### Process
After 1-2 months of free or discounted work with results:
```
Hey [client], I've loved working on [brand]. The numbers

Option B: [X ads/month + strategy calls] — $[price]
Option C: Full creative strategy retainer — $[price]

I'm flexible on structure. What works for you?
```
