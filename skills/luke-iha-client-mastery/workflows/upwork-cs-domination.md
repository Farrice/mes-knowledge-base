# Upwork CS Domination

## Purpose
Upwork-specific mastery for creative strategists — distinct from the general `/upwork-domination` workflow. This focuses on DTC-specific proposals, ad-spend portfolio showcasing, and positioning as a creative strategist (not a generic copywriter).

## When to Use
- When using Upwork as a client acquisition channel for creative strategy work
- When you're applying to DTC/ecom brand jobs specifically
- When you need to differentiate from the thousands of "copywriters" on Upwork

## Step 1: Profile Setup — Creative Strategist Positioning

### Completion Target
Upwork profile that immediately signals "creative strategist" not "copywriter."

### Process
1. **Title**: "DTC Creative Strategist | AI-Orchestrated Ad Production | [Result]"
2. **Overview**: Lead with the problem DTC brands face (creative fatigue, Andromeda algorithm demands, volume requirements), then position your solution (AI production + human judgment curation)
3. **Skills tags**: Creative Strategy, Direct Response Copywriting, Facebook Ads, Ad Creative, DTC Marketing, Video Script Writing, Meta Advertising
4. **Portfolio items**: 5-7 pieces organized by brand, each with strategic rationale
5. **Certifications display**: All 3-5 certifications visible

### Differentiation Strategy
Most Upwork copywriters say: "I write compelling copy that converts."
You say: "I orchestrate AI-powered ad creative testing systems for DTC brands — generating 20+ variations and curating the 3-5 that deserve budget."

## Step 2: Job Identification — DTC Signal Detection

### Completion Target
System for finding the right jobs to apply to daily.

### Process

**Search terms** (set up saved searches):
- "Creative strategist DTC"
- "Ad creative ecommerce"
- "Facebook ad copy DTC"
- "Performance creative"
- "Ad copywriting Shopify"
- "Direct response creative"

**Green flags** (apply immediately):
- Mentions ROAS, CPA, or specific ad metrics
- References DTC or ecommerce brands
- Asks for volume creative (10+ ads)
- Mentions Meta, Facebook, or Instagram ads
- Explicitly uses "creative strategist" title

**Red flags** (skip):
- Vague "content writing" without ad specificity
- Very low budget (<$500 for a project)
- Wants "branding" without performance metrics
- No mention of paid advertising

## Step 3: Proposal Template — CS-Specific

### Completion Target
Customizable proposal template that converts at 10%+ response rate.

### Template Structure

**Line 1 — Specificity Hook**:
"Hey [name], I noticed you're looking for someone to handle [specific thing from their job post]. I just finished a similar project for a [similar brand type] where I [specific result]."

**Paragraph 2 — The Diagnosis**:
"Looking at your current ad library, I can see [1-2 specific observations about their ads]. The gap I'd fill is [specific insight — e.g., 'you're running mostly UGC but missing static variations for retargeting']."

**Paragraph 3 — The Proof Stack**:
"Here's what I've done in similar accounts:
- [Result 1 — quantified]
- [Result 2 — quantified]  
- [Testimonial snippet — 1 sentence]"

**Paragraph 4 — The Easy Next Step**:
"I have high conviction I can [specific deliverable] for [their brand]. Want me to put together 3 ad concepts as a sample? No charge — if they're not strong, no hard feelings."

**Closing**:
"Looking forward to connecting, [Your name]"

### Rules
1. NEVER send a generic proposal — reference their specific job post AND their actual ads
2. Always include quantified proof — even from spec work
3. Always offer something free — lowers their risk
4. Keep it under 200 words — respect their time

## Step 4: Portfolio Presentation for Upwork

### Completion Target
Portfolio that proves you can think, not just write.

### Process
**For each portfolio piece include**:
1. The brand name and brief
2. The strategic rationale ("WHY I wrote this ad this way")
3. The ad creative itself
4. The awareness level it targets
5. Results if available (or "spec work" if not)

**Organization**: By brand, not by ad type. Clients hire for brand-level thinking.
