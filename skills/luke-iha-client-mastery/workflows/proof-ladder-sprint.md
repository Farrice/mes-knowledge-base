# Proof Ladder Sprint

## Purpose
Execute Luke Iha's 7-rung Proof Ladder at maximum speed — moving from zero proof to blue-chip client in 30-90 days with daily actions and clear exit criteria for each rung.

## When to Use
- When starting from scratch as a creative strategist or copywriter
- When you have some proof but gaps in your ladder
- When clients aren't responding to outreach (proof is the bottleneck)
- When running the `/client-conversion-formula` reveals proof as the blocker

---

### Completion Target
User knows exactly which rungs they've completed and which need work.

### Process
Rate each rung: ✅ Complete | 🔶 Partial | ❌ Missing

| Rung | What Counts | Status |
|------|------------|--------|
| 1. Resume | Professional resume highlighting relevant smartness/experience | |
| 2. Samples | 3-5 brands × 3-5 ads each in portfolio format | |

**Sprint rule**: Start at your lowest incomplete rung. Don't skip ahead.

## Step 2: Rung 1 — Resume (1-2 Hours)

### Completion Target
Professional resume that makes you sound smart and interesting.

### Process
1. **Identity anchor**: What's your "this guy is the ___ guy" hook? Engineer? SAT tutor? Economics major? Bartender who studied persuasion?
2. **Smart signals**: Education, past roles, anything analytical or creative
3. **Structure**: One page. Name, identity anchor, 3-5 bullet points of relevant experience, skills, certifications (even if blank for now)
4. **AI assist**: "Write me a creative strategist resume emphasizing my background as [X]. Make me sound smart but not pompous."
5. **Format**: PDF + LinkedIn-ready version

### Exit Criteria
- [ ] Resume exists as PDF
- [ ] Identity anchor is clear and memorable
- [ ] Resume is uploaded to Upwork and LinkedIn

---

## Step 3: Rung 2 — Samples (1-2 Hours)

### Completion Target
Portfolio of 15-25 spec ads across 3-5 brands.

### Process
1. **Brand selection**: Go to Facebook Ad Library → find 3-5 DTC brands that are actively spending
2. **Ad creation**: For each brand, produce 3-5 ads using AI:
   - 2 static image ads (headline + body + CTA)
   - 1-2 video scripts (30-60 seconds)
   - 1 advertorial lead (if applicable)
3. **Quality filter**: Apply the Headline Feeling Test to each. Only include pieces that produce bodily amplitude.
4. **Presentation**: Google Drive folder or Notion portfolio page, organized by brand
5. **Bonus**: Include brief strategic rationale for each ad ("I wrote this for [awareness level] because [insight]")

### Exit Criteria
- [ ] 15-25 ad samples across 3-5 brands
- [ ] Organized in shareable portfolio format
- [ ] Strategic rationale accompanies at least 3 samples

## Step 4: Rung 3 — Certifications (1-2 Days)

### Completion Target
3-5 relevant certifications displayed on profiles.

### Process
1. **Ask AI**: "List the best free or low-cost certifications for creative strategists and copywriters in 2026"
2. **Priority stack**:

3. **Complete and display**: Add to LinkedIn, Upwork, and resume
4. **Commitment signal**: "What it demonstrates is, hey, at least this person is committed to their own education."

### Exit Criteria
- [ ] 3-5 certifications completed
- [ ] All displayed on LinkedIn and Upwork profiles
- [ ] Resume updated with certifications

## Step 5: Rung 4 — Testimonials (1-7 Days)

### Completion Target
5-10 testimonials from real humans who can vouch for your work or character.

### Process

**If you have past clients:**
1. List every person you've done work for (any kind of work — not just ads)
2. Ghost-write a testimonial for each using the template:
   ```
   Before working with [you]: [before state — the problem they had]
   After working with [you]: [after state — tangible results]
   What it was like: [working relationship — reliable, creative, went above and beyond]
   ```
3. Send: "Hey [name], I'm building my portfolio. I wrote a draft testimonial based on our work together — you can edit it, add to it, or just approve it as-is. Here it is: [testimonial]. Would this be okay to use?"
4. Follow up: Day 2, Day 4, Day 6, Day 8 — gentle bumps with GIFs/memes
5. **Luke's exact follow-up script**: "Hey, I know you're super busy. I just wanted to bump this. I'll only message you a couple more times — just want to make sure you've seen this."

**If you have ZERO past clients:**
1. Join communities (Discord, Reddit, Facebook groups for DTC brands)
2. Offer free work: "I'll write 5 ads for your brand for free in exchange for a testimonial"
3. Upwork micro-gigs: Even $50-100 jobs count for testimonials
4. Luke's extreme method: "I used to tell people on Upwork, 'I'll pay YOU $50 to work for you.'" The testimonial > the payment.
5. Character testimonials: Ask colleagues, mentors, professors — anyone who can speak to your diligence, creativity, and character

### Exit Criteria
- [ ] 5-10 testimonials collected (written or video)
- [ ] Displayed on LinkedIn, Upwork, and portfolio
- [ ] Follow-up system for any outstanding requests

## Step 6: Rung 5 — Track Record (1-4 Weeks)

### Completion Target
Verifiable ad spend numbers attached to your name.

### Process

**Path A — Agency Work (Fastest)**
1. Apply to DTC ad agencies as a freelance creative strategist
2. Agencies want margin-positive talent → they'll pay less but give you VOLUME
3. Your ads get real spend behind them → you get track record numbers
4. "Some of my students work with agencies, get spend behind their ads, then use those to pivot to their own work"

**Path B — Free Work with Value**
1. Find a brand currently spending on ads (you can tell by their Ad Library volume)
2. Write ads specifically designed to fill a gap in their current creative
3. Pitch: "Hey, I know you're busy. Your ads are really awesome. I love [specific ad]. I wrote these to fill [specific gap]. I got them reviewed — I have high conviction they'll work. Here are the static images, headlines, body copy, ready to upload."
4. Make it EASY — they should be able to test your ads with zero friction

**Path C — Partner/Barter**
1. Find a DTC brand owner in a community
2. Offer: "I'll be your creative strategist for 30 days. You pay nothing. I just need access to your ad account to build my track record."
3. Over-deliver → they'll likely want to continue paying you

### Exit Criteria
- [ ] At least one documented ad spend number (e.g., "$5,000 spent on my ads")
- [ ] Screenshots or reports proving the spend
- [ ] Permission to share the results publicly

## Step 7: Rung 6 — Case Studies (1-4 Weeks)

### Completion Target
2-3 "How I" narrative case studies with quantified results.

### Process
→ Use the `/case-study-how-i` workflow for full case study creation.

Quick version:
1. Pick your best results from track record work
2. Frame as "How I [achieved result] for [brand]"
3. Structure: Before → My Diagnosis → My System → Iterations → Results
4. Include mistakes — proves it was real work, not luck
5. Quantify everything: ROAS, CPA, revenue, ad spend, conversion rate

### Exit Criteria
- [ ] 2-3 complete "How I" case studies
- [ ] Published on LinkedIn and portfolio
- [ ] Each includes quantified results + process narrative

## Step 8: Rung 7 — Blue-Chip Client (1-4 Weeks)

### Completion Target
One recognizable brand name on your client list.

### Process
→ Use the `/blue-chip-client` workflow for the complete strategy.

Quick version:
1. Go to funneloftheweek.com → identify brands the DTC community recognizes
2. Target 5-10 blue-chip brands for free/discounted work
3. Even 1-2 months of working with them = permanent portfolio halo
4. "Most companies will be more impressed by the names of who you work for — which is just a psychological weakness of humans. But it's very, very powerful."

### Exit Criteria
- [ ] At least one blue-chip brand relationship (paid or free)
- [ ] Permission to reference the brand name in portfolio
- [ ] At least 1-2 winning ads from the engagement
