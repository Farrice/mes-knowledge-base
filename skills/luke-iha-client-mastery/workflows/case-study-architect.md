# Case Study Architect

## Purpose
Transform raw track record data into compelling "How I" narrative case studies — Proof Ladder Rung 6. Case studies are the inflection point where proof becomes persuasion — they show your THINKING, not just your results.

## When to Use
- When you have track record numbers that need narrativizing
- When running `/proof-ladder-sprint` and hitting Rung 6
- When building LinkedIn content from client results
- When proposals need a "see my work" attachment

---

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: Before executing, run the **Decision Framework** in `genius.md` § Decision Framework. Confirm all diagnostic questions are answered.


## Step 1: Case Study Selection

### Completion Target
2-3 strongest results selected and mapped for case study development.

### Selection Criteria (Rank by Priority)
1. **Recognizable brand name** > unknown brand (even with lower numbers)
2. **Clear before/after delta** > impressive but contextless numbers
3. **Process was interesting** > numbers were large
4. **You can get permission** > technically better results without permission

### Minimum Requirements
- [ ] You wrote the ads (not just consulted)
- [ ] You have before and after metrics
- [ ] You can describe your strategic decisions
- [ ] You have permission (or can anonymize the brand)

---

## Step 2: The "How I" Narrative Structure

### Completion Target
Complete narrative case study following the 5-section template.

### Section 1: The Situation (Before State)
"When I came on board, [brand] was..."
- Current ad spend and performance (the mess you inherited)
- What they'd tried before (establishes difficulty)
- Why the obvious solutions weren't working
- **Emotional texture**: Paint the frustration, not just the numbers

### Section 2: My Diagnosis (What Others Missed)
"The first thing I noticed was..."
- What specific insight did YOU see that nobody else did?
- What data did you look at? What patterns emerged?
- Why did the existing approach fail (be specific)?
- **This is the most important section** — it proves you THINK differently

### Section 3: The System I Built (Your Approach)
"Here's exactly what I did..."
- Step 1, 2, 3 of your strategy (be specific enough to be credible)
- What ads did you write and why?
- What awareness levels did you target?
- What creative formats did you use and why?
- **Show your frameworks** — reference copy blocks, mechanisms, proof layering

### Section 4: The Iterations + Mistakes (Reality Proof)
"The first round of ads didn't work because..."
- What failed? What did you learn?
- How did you adjust?
- This section PROVES it was real work, not luck
- **Swarm enrichment**: This is what differentiates a "How I" narrative from a results screenshot — it shows the MESSY MIDDLE

### Section 5: The Results (Quantified)
"After [timeframe], here's what happened..."
- Primary metric: ROAS, CPA, revenue, ad spend scaled
- Secondary metrics: CTR, conversion rate, cost per click
- Timeline: How long did it take?
- Ongoing status: Are these ads still running?

---

## Step 3: Format Variations

### Completion Target
Case study adapted for 3 formats: portfolio, LinkedIn, and proposal.

### Portfolio Version (Full — 500-800 words)
Complete narrative with all 5 sections. Include ad screenshots, before/after metrics table, and strategic rationale.

### LinkedIn Version (Post — 200-300 words)
Hook: "How I [specific result] for [brand]"
Body: Compressed version of Diagnosis + System + Results
CTA: "Want my complete case study? Comment [keyword] and I'll send it."

### Proposal Version (Snippet — 100-150 words)
One paragraph: "[Brand] was struggling with [problem]. I [specific action] and [specific result] in [timeframe]. See the full case study here: [link]."
