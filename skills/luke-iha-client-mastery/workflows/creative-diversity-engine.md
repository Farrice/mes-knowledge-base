# Creative Diversity Engine

## Purpose
Build a systematic creative diversity pipeline that feeds Meta's Andromeda algorithm the variety it rewards. Moves beyond "creative testing" to a matrix-based system that ensures continuous fresh creative across compositions, vibes, and awareness levels.

## When to Use
- When managing ad accounts and creative fatigue is killing performance
- When Meta performance is declining despite good creative
- When you need to produce high-volume diverse creative for DTC clients
- When setting up a creative production system for a new client engagement

## Step 1: Understand the Andromeda Shift

### Completion Target
User grasps why creative diversity is now mandatory, not optional.

### The Three Convergent Forces
1. **AI made production easy** → overabundance of ad creative in the marketplace
2. **Meta's Andromeda algorithm** → optimizes creative distribution, rewards diversity, penalizes repetition
3. **Creative IS targeting** → "Back in the day you had the copywriter and the media buyer. Now the actual creative messaging IS the targeting. Meta's algorithm sends it to the people it thinks are most likely to convert."

### What Changed
- Old model: Find one winning ad → scale spend → ride it until fatigue
- New model: Maintain a constant pipeline of diverse creative → algorithm surfaces the right ad to the right person
- **Creative monogamy is dead.** The algorithm punishes accounts that rely on 1-3 ads.

## Step 2: The Composition × Vibe Matrix

### Completion Target
A creative diversity matrix mapping all format/tone combinations.

### Compositions (Ad Formats)
| # | Composition | Description |
|---|------------|-------------|
| 1 | Static Image | Single image with headline + body copy |
| 2 | Video (30s) | Short-form video with hook + body + CTA |
| 3 | Video (60s) | Medium-form with more room for story/mechanism |
| 4 | Carousel | Multi-image with sequential narrative |
| 5 | UGC-Style | User-generated-content aesthetic (phone camera, casual) |
| 6 | Founder/Expert | Authority figure speaking directly to camera |
| 7 | Advertorial | Long-form native content style |
| 8 | Before/After | Visual transformation proof |
| 9 | Testimonial | Customer story format |
| 10 | Mashup | Multiple testimonials/clips edited together |

### Vibes (Tonal Approaches)
| # | Vibe | Description |
|---|------|-------------|
| 1 | Educational | "Here's how it works" — mechanism-driven |
| 2 | Emotional | "Feel this" — pain/desire/transformation |
| 3 | Urgent | "Act now" — scarcity/FOMO |
| 4 | Social Proof | "Look at all these people" — herd behavior |
| 5 | Contrarian | "Everything you thought was wrong" — pattern interrupt |
| 6 | Humorous | "This is funny AND relevant" — entertainment-first |
| 7 | Raw/Authentic | "No BS, here's the truth" — anti-marketing |

### Building the Matrix
**Create a grid**: Compositions (rows) × Vibes (columns) = 70 possible combinations. You don't need all 70 — but you should have creative in at least 15-20 cells.

**Priority cells** (start here):
- Static × Educational (always works for cold traffic)
- UGC × Emotional (highest-performing format for many DTC brands)
- Video-30s × Contrarian (great for unaware audiences)
- Testimonial × Social Proof (warm audience conversion)
- Advertorial × Educational (retargeting/mid-funnel)

## Step 3: Awareness-Level Segmentation Layer

### Completion Target
Each creative piece tagged by awareness level for algorithmic efficiency.

### Awareness Level → Creative Mapping
| Level | Audience State | Creative Goal | Best Compositions |
|-------|---------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Unaware | Doesn't know they have a problem | Interrupt + surface the problem | Contrarian video, UGC, meme-style |
| Problem Aware | Knows the problem, not the solution | Amplify pain + introduce category | Educational static, emotional video |
| Solution Aware | Knows solutions exist | Differentiate your mechanism | Advertorial, founder-led, before/after |
| Product Aware | Knows YOUR product | Overcome objections with proof | Testimonial, mashup, before/after |
| Most Aware | Ready to buy | Remove friction + urgency | Social proof static, discount/offer |

### Rule
Never run only one awareness level. The matrix should have creative for at least 3 awareness levels at all times.

## Step 4: Creative Rotation Calendar

### Completion Target
A weekly/monthly production schedule that prevents creative fatigue.

### Weekly Cadence
| Week | Focus | Output |
|------|-------|--------|
| 1 | NEW: 3-5 new ad angles from matrix gaps | Fresh creative enters testing |
| 2 | ITERATE: Top performers get 3-5 variations each | Winners get extended life |
| 3 | EXPLORE: Test a new composition or vibe you haven't tried | Expand the matrix |
| 4 | AUDIT: Kill underperformers, promote winners, update matrix | System optimization |

### Monthly Cadence
- Weeks 1-2: Production sprint (15-25 new pieces)
- Week 3: Performance analysis + iteration
- Week 4: Matrix audit + next month planning

### Kill Criteria
- Ad below CPA threshold after $X spend → pause
- Ad with declining CTR over 3 days → pause
- Any ad older than 45 days → review for fatigue

## Step 5: AI-Powered Volume Production

### Process
1. **Use the matrix as a creative brief**: "Write a [composition] ad with a [vibe] tone for a [awareness level] audience selling [product]"
2. **Generate 5-10 variations per cell** using AI tools
3. **Apply the Feeling Test** from `/paid-to-feel` to curate each batch
4. **Format for upload**: Ensure everything is platform-ready
5. **Test in batches**: 3-5 new pieces per week, rotating through matrix cells
