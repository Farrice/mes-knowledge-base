# Track Record Builder

## Purpose
Build verifiable ad spend track record (Proof Ladder Rung 5) using agency partnerships, strategic free work, and systematic documentation. Track record is the first "hard to fake" proof — making it the conversion multiplier for outreach.

## When to Use
- When samples and testimonials aren't enough to close clients
- When clients ask "how much ad spend have your ads generated?"
- When running `/proof-ladder-sprint` and hitting Rung 5
- When you need to bridge from spec work to real performance data

## Step 1: Path Selection

### Completion Target
User selects and commits to one or more track-record-building paths.

### Three Paths (Not Mutually Exclusive)

**Path A — Agency Work (Fastest, Least Glamorous)**
- Apply to DTC ad agencies as a freelance creative strategist
- Agencies have volume and budgets — your ads get real spend
- You earn less per ad BUT get volume track record fast
- "Some of my students work with agencies, get spend behind their ads, then pivot"
- **Timeline**: 1-2 weeks to get placed, 2-4 weeks for meaningful spend data

**Path B — Free Work with Value (Best for Blue-Chip Chances)**
- Find brands currently spending on ads (visible in Facebook Ad Library)
- Write ads that fill a specific gap in their current creative mix
- Pitch with Luke's exact approach:
```
Hey, I know you're busy. Your ads are awesome — specifically 
[reference a specific ad]. I noticed you're running mostly 
[format type] but don't have [missing format]. 

I put together these [3-5 ads] and had them reviewed by 
[peer/community]. I have high conviction they'd perform. 

Here they are ready to upload — headlines, body copy, 
static images. Zero friction on your end. If they work, 
maybe we can chat about doing more together.
```
- **Timeline**: 1-2 weeks for the pitch, 1-4 weeks for spend data

**Path C — Partner/Barter (Best for Long-Term Relationships)**
- Find a DTC brand owner in a community (Discord, Reddit, masterminds)
- Offer 30-day free creative strategy in exchange for ad account access
- Over-deliver → transition to paid retainer
- **Timeline**: 1 week to find a partner, 4 weeks for the trial

## Step 2: Documentation System

### Completion Target
System for capturing and presenting track record data in real-time.

### What to Capture (Per Ad)
| Data Point | Source | Example |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Ad creative (screenshot) | Facebook Ads Manager | Static image, video thumbnail |
| Total spend | Ads Manager | "$4,200 spent" |
| Results | Ads Manager | "142 purchases at $29.50 CPA" |
| ROAS | Ads Manager | "3.2x ROAS" |
| Duration | Ads Manager | "Ran for 42 days" |
| Brand name (with permission) | Client | "Supplement brand X" |

### Presentation Format
1. **Portfolio page**: Organized by brand with total spend across all ads
2. **One-liner summary**: "My ads have generated $X in tracked ad spend across Y brands"
3. **LinkedIn headline update**: Include cumulative spend figure
4. **Proposal proof block**: "[Brand] — $X spend, Y ROAS, Z weeks"

### Running Total Tracker
Keep a running Google Sheet:
| Brand | Ad | Spend | CPA | ROAS | Dates | Permission? |
|-------|-----|-------|-----|------|-------|-------------|

## Step 3: The "Hey I Know You're Busy" Pitch Framework

### Completion Target
Ready-to-send pitch for any brand visible in Facebook Ad Library.

### Process
1. **Research**: Spend 20 minutes in their Ad Library — note what they're running, what's missing
2. **Create**: Write 3-5 ads that fill a gap (different format, different awareness level, different angle)
3. **Package**: Make it upload-ready — headlines, body copy, images, suggested targeting
4. **Send**: Use Luke's "already done the work" approach — no asking for permission to pitch, just deliver value

### What Makes This Work
- You've already done the work → no risk for them to test it
- You've identified a specific gap → shows strategic thinking, not just writing
- You've packaged it ready to upload → zero friction
- You're not asking for money → removes their primary objection

## Step 4: Agency Application System

### Completion Target
Applications sent to 5-10 DTC ad agencies.

### Where to Find DTC Agencies
- LinkedIn search: "DTC ad agency" or "performance creative agency"
- Upwork: Search for agencies hiring creative strategists
- Industry lists: "Top DTC marketing agencies 2026"
- Conferences: Agency booths at DTC conferences

### Application Approach
1. Position as a volume creative producer
2. Lead with writing samples, not strategic frameworks (agencies want executers)
3. Offer a trial: "Give me a brand brief and I'll produce 10 ads. If 3+ are strong, let's talk."
4. Accept lower rates initially — you're paying for track record, not income
