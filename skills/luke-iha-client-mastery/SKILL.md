# Luke Iha: Client Mastery & Business Operations

This skill operationalizes the *business side* of being a creative strategist — not what to write, but how to get hired, build proof, acquire clients, and leverage AI-era judgment as a monetizable skill. While `luke-iha-creative-strategy` handles upstream strategic thinking, this skill handles the downstream business engine: client acquisition formulas, proof ladder sprinting, testimonial extraction, and the "Paid to Feel" methodology that makes the creative strategist role AI-proof.

## Core Frameworks

- **CR × OU Formula** — Clients = Conversion Rate × Outreach Units. The only two variables.
- **POP Stack** — Positioning + Offer + Proof (80% of conversion = proof)
- **7-Rung Proof Ladder** — Resume → Samples → Certifications → Testimonials → Track Record → Case Studies → Blue-Chip Client
- **Paid to Feel** — Monetizing nervous-system judgment in an AI production world
- **Creative is the New Targeting** — Post-Andromeda creative diversity mandate
