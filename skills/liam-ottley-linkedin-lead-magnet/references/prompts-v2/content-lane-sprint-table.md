## Role & Activation

You are operating in the frame Liam Ottley teaches in "How to Turn LinkedIn into a Personal Brand Lead Magnet with AI." Lanes are recurring routes; ideas are one-time executions. The lane set must stay smaller than the idea set, or the plan collapses into a list of one-off posts with no repeatable system behind it. This prompt turns the strategic arbitrage angles into a structured, sequenced first sprint — the validation batch the rest of the system is tested against.

## Input Required

- Strategic arbitrage map (10-15 angles with attention carrier / buyer relevance / owned insight / commercial bridge): [ARBITRAGE MAP]
- Lead magnet concept (if already chosen): [LEAD MAGNET CONCEPT OR "not yet chosen — content should still tease toward one"]
- Posting cadence or timeframe the expert wants to hit: [CADENCE OR "no constraint given"]

## Execution Protocol

1. **Define 3-5 content lanes** from the source method — do not invent lane names outside this set unless the input material demands it:
   - **Lead Magnet Lane**: posts that tease, validate, or explain the opt-in asset.
   - **Authority Lane**: posts that prove expertise and make the lead magnet desirable.
   - **Story Lane**: personal moments linked back to the business problem.
   - **Arbitrage Lane**: trends or authority references reframed through the expert's method.
   - **Conversion Lane**: direct but useful posts that invite a DM, checklist, audit, or call.

2. **Build the 20-post sprint table** with exactly these columns: `#`, `Lane`, `Angle`, `Hook`, `Rehook`, `Structure`, `Lead Magnet Bridge`, `Success Signal`.

3. **Hit the minimum distribution from the source method** — the sprint is not evenly split, it is weighted toward authority and story:
   - 5 authority posts
   - 5 story/SLAY posts
   - 4 strategic arbitrage posts
   - 3 conversion/PAS posts
   - 3 lead magnet teaser posts

4. **For every row, apply hook and rehook standards from the source material**: the hook must use specific raw material (exact result, specific number, named mechanism, "how I" rather than generic "how to", buyer pain with commercial consequence, or a timely trend translated into a useful take) — not a generic list-opener. The rehook must extend the hook with specificity, proof, tension, or an outcome; a rehook that just restates the hook or jumps straight into a list fails this standard.

5. **Assign a Structure to each post** — SLAY (Story/Lesson/Advice/You) for authority, founder-story, or educational posts that need retention before the teaching begins; PAS (Problem/Agitate/Solution) for posts that must sell or route directly into the lead magnet.

6. **Name a Success Signal per post** — what would indicate this post worked (saves, DMs, comments-with-intent, lead magnet clicks) — not a vanity metric alone.

## Creative Latitude

The eight columns and the lane-distribution minimums are the floor that keeps the sprint from collapsing into 20 interchangeable posts. Inside that floor, the angle and hook language should carry the owned insight from the arbitrage map — push for hooks that could only come from this expert's specific proof, and vary sentence rhythm and hook length post-to-post rather than settling into one template repeated 20 times. Story Lane posts should genuinely put the reader in the story (what they see of themselves), not the creator as the hero — that reframe is explicit in the source material.
