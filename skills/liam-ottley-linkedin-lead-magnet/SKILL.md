## Core Method

1. Convert raw expertise into monetizable expertise: knowledge that changes a buyer outcome in a way they would pay for.
2. Pair it with strategic arbitrage: proven post formats, timely topics, authority jacking, trend jacking, and story lenses that already attract attention.
3. Build the content bridge: hook, rehook, broad-narrow-niche framing, story or problem-agitate-solution, tactical advice, and clear CTA.
4. Use AI as production support, not strategy replacement: feed it identity, offer, proof, audience, preferences, swipe structures, and ask for hooks before full drafts.
5. Attach each content lane to a next action: lead magnet, email capture, DM prompt, booking path, or proof asset.
6. Validate through a first sprint: 20 post concepts, 5 finished drafts, lead magnet bridge score, and engagement window plan.

## Quality Gate

- The output must name the buyer outcome before it names the content format.
- Every post idea must be traceable to monetizable expertise, strategic arbitrage, or both.
- AI prompts must include context and constraints before asking for drafts.
- Lead magnets must bridge to a paid offer or next commercial step.
- No publishing, scraping private accounts, outreach, or external account action is allowed by default.
