# Workflow 01: New Media Strategy Audit

## When to Use
Any organization, brand, or individual needs their communication posture diagnosed. You suspect old-media instincts are holding them back. A competitor is outpacing them in narrative control. Growth has stalled despite "doing social media."

## Input Required
- Organization/brand/individual being audited
- Current communication channels and practices
- Recent crises or missed opportunities (if any)
- Industry and competitive landscape

## Steps

### Step 1: Old/New Media Classification
Scan every communication practice and tag it:

**Old Media Signals** (score -1 each):
- [ ] PR firm reviewing public statements
- [ ] Legal approval gates on social media
- [ ] "What will journalists think?" checkpoints
- [ ] Cross-posting identical content across platforms
- [ ] Corporate voice abstracted from real people
- [ ] Defensive posture / "say nothing" strategy
- [ ] Monthly editorial calendar (in a 36-hour viral cycle world)
- [ ] Company social accounts with no founder presence
- [ ] Press releases as primary announcement channel
- [ ] Measuring success by "no bad press"

**New Media Signals** (score +1 each):
- [ ] Founder/CEO speaking directly on personal accounts
- [ ] Platform-native content (different per channel)
- [ ] Decision-to-publish under 4 hours
- [ ] Long-form anchor content (podcast, Substack)
- [ ] Interesting > safe as the quality gate
- [ ] Crisis response via volume, not defense
- [ ] Platform-obsessed specialists per channel
- [ ] Personality-first brand identity

**Score**: Sum all. Negative = old media dominated. Positive = new media oriented. Zero = dangerous hybrid.

### Step 2: OODA Loop Speed Measurement
Map the current decision-to-publish cycle:
1. **Observe** — How fast does the org detect market events, competitor narratives, sentiment shifts? (Measure in hours)
2. **Orient** — How fast do they contextualize against their position? (Measure in hours)
3. **Decide** — How fast do they choose a content response? (Measure in hours)
4. **Act** — How fast do they publish? (Measure in hours)

Total = OODA loop time. Compare against estimated competitor loop times.

### Step 3: Oral/Written Culture Map
For each active platform, classify by cultural mode:
- **Oral**: Short tweets, TikTok, Reels, Stories — burst emotionality, interpersonal, triggering
- **Written**: Podcast, Substack, long YouTube, blog — depth, abstraction, evidence, analytical
- **Hybrid**: LinkedIn posts, email newsletters, medium YouTube

Flag mismatches: analytical content in oral formats, emotional content in written formats, cross-posted content native to neither.

### Step 4: Joe Rogan CEO Test
Score the leadership 1-10: Could they hold 3 hours unscripted and be genuinely interesting?
- 1-3: Invisible or actively boring
- 4-6: Competent but not magnetic
- 7-8: Interesting with room to grow
- 9-10: Would be one of the best episodes

Diagnose WHY: Fear? Polish-addiction? Lack of stories? Suppressed opinions? (Rarely: genuine lack of original thinking.)

### Step 5: Structural Rebuild Prescription
Deliver the exact changes:
1. **Channel architecture** — which channels, who owns each, what cultural mode each serves
2. **Staffing model** — platform-native obsessives, not generalist social media managers
3. **Content production system** — written-culture-first flow, platform-native extraction
4. **Crisis response protocol** — flood the zone, never defend
5. **Founder go-direct motion** — personal accounts, podcast, long-form, unscripting protocol
