# Workflow 04: Founder Go-Direct Positioning

## When to Use
A founder/CEO needs to go from invisible to magnetically interesting. They're letting a PR firm speak for them. Their competitor's CEO has a podcast and is winning mindshare. They have original ideas but have been trained to suppress them in public.

## Input Required
- Founder/CEO to position
- Company, industry, and stage
- Origin story and founding insight
- Strongest opinions (including suppressed ones)
- Current public presence (if any)
- 2-3 competitor CEOs for comparison

## Steps

### Step 1: Joe Rogan Test (Current Score)

Score 1-10: Could they hold 3 hours unscripted and be genuinely interesting?

**Diagnostic Questions:**
- What do they say in private that they'd never say publicly? (These are the gold)
- Do they have a PR firm? (Liability signal)
- Do they have scripted talking points? (Old media conditioning)
- Can they tell stories that have nothing to do with their product? (Range test)
- Do they have strong opinions about their industry? (Originality test)
- Are they funny in person? (Personality suppression test)

**Score Interpretation:**
- 1-3: Actively boring (usually PR-trained into boringness — the personality exists, it's buried)
- 4-6: Competent but not magnetic (needs unscripting, not personality development)
- 7-8: Interesting with room to grow (needs volume and format, not transformation)
- 9-10: Already magnetic (needs channel architecture and team, not coaching)

### Step 2: Original Idea Inventory

Every successful founder has at least ONE original idea (that's why they founded a company). Mine for 3-5:

| Original Idea | Controversy Level (1-5🔥) | Interestingness (1-10) | Deployment Priority |
|--------------|--------------------------|----------------------|-------------------|

**Mining Questions:**
- "Why did you start this company?" (The founding insight is always interesting)
- "What does your industry get fundamentally wrong?"
- "What's a belief you hold that would get you in trouble if you said it publicly?"
- "What technology trend is everyone excited about that you think is overhyped?"
- "What's the counterintuitive truth about your market that outsiders don't understand?"

### Step 3: Unscripting Protocol

Remove old-media conditioning:

| Old Media Training | New Media Replacement |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| "Stay on message" | "Say what you actually think, with full context" |
| "Bridge back to talking points" | "Follow the interesting thread wherever it goes" |
| "Avoid controversy" | "Take strong positions with long-form explanation" |
| "Corporate voice" | "Your actual voice — humor, tangents, real emotion" |
| "No comment" | "Here's exactly what I think and why" |
| "We're excited to announce" | "We built this because X is broken — here's how" |

### Step 4: Controversy Calibration

Map positions on the spectrum:

**Consensus Positions** (safe to say anywhere, any format):
- [List positions that are industry-standard views]

**Mild Contrarian** (say with brief context):
- [List positions where you disagree with mainstream but the argument is obvious]

**Strong Contrarian** (say with FULL long-form context):
- [List positions where the argument needs extensive evidence and reasoning]
- Each one gets a Context Package: the full argument, evidence, anticipated objections, and responses

**Third Rail** (say on podcast with 1+ hour of context, never in tweets):
- [List positions that require the full relationship and context that only long-form provides]

### Step 5: 30-Day Go-Direct Launch Plan

**Week 1: The Foundation**
- Kill or restrict the PR firm (if applicable)
- Set up founder personal accounts on X, LinkedIn (if not active)
- Day 1-3: Record first podcast episode (guest on industry podcast, NOT mainstream)
- Day 4-7: Write first Substack essay (the #1 original idea from Step 2, in full long-form)

**Week 2: First Public Positions**
- Publish first Substack essay (written-culture anchor)
- Extract 5 tweets from the essay (oral-culture extraction)
- Post first LinkedIn piece (hybrid: personal story + industry insight)
- Day 1 of daily X activity: 2-3 reactive takes on industry news

**Week 3: Build the Rhythm**
- Record second podcast (this time, founder's OWN podcast or long video)
- Publish second essay — the #2 original idea
- First Twitter thread (7 tweets building an argument)
- LinkedIn: 3 posts this week

**Week 4: The Multiplier**
- Podcast interview with a customer or industry peer
- Third essay — the strong contrarian (#3 original idea)
- Enough content exists that short-form extraction becomes self-sustaining
- Assess: are people engaging? Which positions generated the most response?

## Quality Gate

Before delivering, verify:
- [ ] The Joe Rogan Test score is diagnosed with a WHY (fear, polish-addiction, lack of stories, suppressed opinions) — never a bare number
- [ ] The Original Idea Inventory contains ideas mined from the founder's actual founding story and private opinions — never generic industry positions dressed up as "original"
- [ ] Every Strong Contrarian and Third Rail position carries a Context Package (argument, evidence, anticipated objections) — a bold claim with no context package is a liability, not a deployment
- [ ] The Unscripting Protocol replaces specific old-media habits this founder actually has, not the full generic table verbatim regardless of input
- [ ] Week 1 of the 30-Day Plan starts the written-culture anchor (podcast or Substack) before any oral-mode extraction — the Cardinal Rule from Workflow 03 is never violated here either
- [ ] The plan never recommends "no comment," bridging to talking points, or PR-firm review as the response to a hard question — that is precisely the old-media conditioning this workflow exists to remove
