# Workflow 02: OODA Loop Media Warfare

## When to Use
You need to build a rapid-response communication system. Competitors are controlling the narrative. Your decision-to-publish cycle is measured in days. You want sustained speed dominance where competitors are permanently inside YOUR loop.

## Input Required
- Organization/brand deploying the system
- Current market landscape and key competitors
- Active or emerging narrative threats (if any)
- Available channels and team capacity
- Current decision-to-publish cycle time

## Steps

### Step 1: Measure Existing Loop
Map each OODA stage in hours:

| Stage | What It Measures | Current Time |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Observe** | Time from market event to team awareness | ___ hours |
| **Orient** | Time from awareness to contextualized understanding | ___ hours |
| **Decide** | Time from understanding to approved response plan | ___ hours |
| **Act** | Time from approval to published content | ___ hours |
| **TOTAL** | Full OODA cycle | ___ hours |

Identify the bottleneck stage (usually Orient→Decide — review/approval cycles).

### Step 2: Benchmark Competitors
For each key competitor, estimate their OODA loop:
- How fast do they publish after a market event?
- Do they have a founder/CEO who posts directly?
- Do they have real-time monitoring?
- What's their typical response lag?

Calculate the **dominance ratio**: Your loop ÷ Their loop. If > 1, they're inside your loop. If < 1, you're inside theirs.

### Step 3: Compress Each Stage

**Observe (target: <30 min)**
- Set up real-time X lists for competitors, industry terms, brand mentions
- Google Alerts for industry news
- Subscribe to competitor channels (podcast, newsletter)
- Join industry Discord/Slack channels
- Assign one person as narrative watch with push notifications

**Orient (target: <45 min)**
- Pre-build position templates for top 20 most likely narrative scenarios
- Categories: competitor attack, industry trend, regulation change, viral moment, product comparison, market shift
- Each template: pre-drafted thesis + talking points + evidence links
- When something happens, you pull a template and customize — not start from scratch

**Decide (target: <15 min)**
- Eliminate per-event approval gates
- Pre-authorize via guardrails: "The founder can publish anything within these boundaries without review"
- Define the boundaries once, then trust the system
- For truly sensitive topics: 15-minute Slack thread with one approver, not a meeting

**Act (target: <30 min)**
- Raw > polished. A 5-min video response beats a 5-day blog post
- Platform-native production: tweet threads, video responses, LinkedIn posts — formats that can be produced in 30 min
- Long-form follow-up queued for 24-48h later (podcast episode, Substack essay) — but the FIRST response ships fast

### Step 4: Build the Crisis Protocol (Flood Variant)

For active threats, deploy the accelerated loop:

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 0-1 hr | Founder records direct video/audio response (raw, authentic, no script). Publish on primary channel |
| 1-4 hr | Team publishes 2-3 threads/posts on OTHER topics — genuinely interesting content that fills audience feeds |
| 4-12 hr | 2-3 allies (investors, partners, friendly KOLs) publish their own supportive/unrelated content |
| 12-36 hr | Long-form piece (Substack/podcast) with the complete argument. Then 2+ more interesting pieces on new topics |
| 36 hr+ | The attacking narrative is buried. Resume normal cadence |

### Step 5: Design the Proactive Disruption Calendar

Once operating at sustained speed advantage, shift from reactive to proactive:
- Identify the top 5 industry conversations your competitors haven't addressed yet
- Schedule one "perspective bomb" per week — a strong take published while competitors are still forming opinions
- The goal: competitors are always responding to YOUR narratives
