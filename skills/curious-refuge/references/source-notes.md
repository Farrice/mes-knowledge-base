## Fidelity assessment — what the corpus carries, and what it does not

### Fully carried (dense, multi-source, safe to build on)
- **Reference-asset architecture** — character sheets, location angle banks, style anchors, voice
  beds. Present in three of four videos, with prompt-level detail and a stated budget rule.
- **The shot-list floor and the sketch-board rationale.** [FILM26] states both, and [ANIME26]
  revises the board half in a way that is itself well-reasoned and quotable. The resulting
  composition question (board it when composition is load-bearing) is **Ward-derived** by this
  extraction from the two statements plus his stated reason — labelled as such in `genius.md`.
- **VO directing.** [VOICE26] is a complete ladder with failure modes, an honest verdict and a
  stated ceiling. This is the strongest single-topic source in the corpus.
- **Prompted coverage and rolling-reference chaining.** [ANIME26], with verbatim prompts and a
  worked cost.
- **Judgment vocabulary.** Fifteen dated critique verdicts, enough to assemble the quality rubric
  without inventing a criterion.

### Partially carried (built, but bounded)
- **Hybrid AI + live pipelines** — the dossier's phrase. What the corpus actually contains is one
  crisp, load-bearing hybrid rule (**AI sets timing, human matches it in ADR**, [ANIME26] 08:49–09:13,
  reinforced at 18:43) plus a finishing seam (source separation → editor + voice actor; up-res, film
  stock, grain). It does **not** contain live-action plate photography, on-set/generated compositing,
  or an actor-on-camera pipeline. Workflow 03 builds exactly the seam he teaches and says so.
- **Dynamic storyboards** — carried, but note the honest complication: *"dynamic"* is his adjective
  for the **character sheet** ([ANIME26] 06:03), not for the storyboard. His storyboards are
  deliberately *static and rough*. Workflow 02 preserves the distinction rather than smoothing it.

### NOT carried — deliberately not built
- **Pitch trailers.** The task brief proposed a `pitch-trailer-build.md` workflow. **No 2025–26
  source in this corpus teaches pitch-trailer structure.** The only trailer-build video on the
  channel (*Create a Video Game Trailer Using AI*, 2025-05-21) predates the multi-shot-coverage shift
  that [ANIME26] documents, so building trailer doctrine from it would have imported era-bound
  mechanics straight into the core. **Workflow dropped; replaced by 03 (voice & performance
  direction), which is the dossier's own "VO directing" claim and is the densest verified material
  in the corpus.** FIDELITY RULE applied: thin → smaller honest skill.
- **Screenwriting method.** He explicitly declines to teach it on camera twice ([FILM26] 02:10,
  [ANIME26] 03:27), routing to a paid course. Nothing invented to fill the gap.
- **Look/grade craft** — colour temperature, ratios, LUTs, film-stock choice by name. He names a
  plugin and a halation control; he never teaches a look. That layer belongs to `skills/dave-clark/`.
- **Editing craft** — cadence, cut rhythm, sound design. Mentioned, never taught. Also Clark's.
