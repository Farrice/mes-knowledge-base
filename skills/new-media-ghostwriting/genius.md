## Nicolas Cole — Voice Capture as Case-Study-of-One

Cole's ghostwriting-client-acquisition doctrine (not a polished framework document — raw
talked-through method) is built on one refusal: never lead with portfolio, case studies, or
testimonials. Verified directly from his own words:

> "As a ghost writer, the nature of the work that I do... is confidential. So I'm not at
> liberty to share who I work with... I can tell you that I've worked with people just like
> you." — `extractions/nicolas-cole-ghostwriting-v2/transcript.txt`

He states the volume behind this refusal explicitly: **"we worked with over 300 different
industry leaders, Silicon Valley founders, investors, executives of publicly traded
companies... 300 plus clients, we didn't use a single case study or testimonial. Zero.
actually zero."** (same source). His agency, Digital Press, specialized narrowly: **"we
wrote 800word thought leadership articles... at the height of our agency, we were
ghostwriting more thought leadership articles for CEOs and founders per month than Inc.
Magazine and Forbes combined,"** with **"23 plus full-time employees"**
(`extractions/nicolas-cole-ghostwriting-v1/transcript.txt`).

His pricing floor is stated as a non-negotiable, not a suggestion: **"You should never be
charging less than $3,000 for a project or per month... if you are charging less than
$3,000, that means on some level you have a faulty belief"** (same source). And his voice-
capture technique for landing a client without a portfolio is literally to point at himself:
he pitched CEOs at age 26, living in an **"$800 a month studio apartment,"** pointing to his
Quora profile with **"like 8,000 followers"** as proof the method worked
(`nicolas-cole-ghostwriting-v2/transcript.txt`). Later in the same transcript he states the
underlying principle directly: **"your credibility is irrelevant and I can demonstrate
credibility much better by articulating your problem in detail"**
(`nicolas-cole-ghostwriting-v2/transcript.txt`, ~char 19,193). The deployment rule for THIS
skill: the voice-capture phase of any engagement should produce a document the ghostwriter
can point to as evidence — not a survey, an artifact.

## a16z (Marc Andreessen & Ben Horowitz) — The Physics of New Media

Verified directly against `extractions/marc-andreessen-ben-horowitz/transcript.txt`
(confirmed present, 55,427 bytes):

> "You can't be half and half... because the whole motion of the old world will kill you in
> the new world and vice versa."

test. The crisis-response doctrine is equally direct:

> "We can go on... 30 podcast[s], all of which get a much bigger audience than any of the
> publications... and just basically... erase that from everybody's memory very quickly...

The founder-positioning standard — the Joe Rogan CEO Test — is named explicitly in the
transcript: **"Jordy from TBPN has this concept of a Joe Rogan CEO — someone interesting
enough to go on Joe Rogan for three hour[s]... someone like an Alex Karp, someone like a
Palmer Luckey."** These three verified quotes are the load-bearing physics this skill
imports from a16z: binary commitment, volume-over-defense in a crisis (the 36-hour viral
decay window), and the 3-hour-unscripted-conversation standard for founder magnetism.

## Signature Moves (Compound Service)

1. **The Confidential-Client Deflection** — when a prospect asks for case studies, deflect
   to method not proof: "I can tell you I've worked with people just like you" (Cole,
   verified above), then immediately offer a free custom sample instead of a testimonial.
2. **The Binary Commitment Check** — before any engagement plan ships, ask: does any section
   hedge between old-media-safe and new-media-interesting? If yes, a16z's "half and half"
   physics says the plan already failed (verified quote above).
3. **The 36-Hour Flood Window** — crisis protocol timing is not arbitrary: a16z's own viral-
   post lifecycle (12h up, 24h down, 36h forgotten) sets the flood-zone content production
   deadline at under 36 hours from any attack.
4. **The City Map Draw** — before tactical content planning, draw Grand Central + 2-3
   Destinations + Lines + Passengers (Grace Andrews, LIKELY-labeled framework).
5. **The Revenue Bridge Sequence** — content → profile → lead magnet → email → sales, in
   that literal order, per Lara Acosta's own naming (verified above); skipping the lead
   magnet step is the single most common reason a "voice-perfect" LinkedIn engagement never
   converts to revenue.
