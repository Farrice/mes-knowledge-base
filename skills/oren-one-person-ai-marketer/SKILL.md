# Oren — The One-Person AI Marketing Machine

**Invocation**: `/deploy-skill oren-one-person-ai-marketer`
**Domain**: Solo/Lean Marketing Operations • Funnel Flywheel Architecture • Capture-to-Call • Offer Ladder and Economics Gate • AI-as-Strategically-Framed-Multiplier • Anti-Slop Taste Gate • Weekly Time-Block OS • Word-of-Mouth • MESSAGES • INFO-RELEASE

The AI-execution layer beneath Oren's six strategy skills. Oren runs all marketing for a multi-million-dollar womenswear brand solo, in a few deliberate hours a week, for six-plus years. This skill is the system that makes that possible — and the discipline that keeps it from producing slop.

**The One Principle**: One operator WITH TASTE produces a full marketing team's output by framing AI strategically — personas + named copywriting frameworks + a persistent Project substrate — *never paste-and-pray*. AI is **right** for scaling approved copy, aggregating research, and clerical ops; **wrong** for personal-brand and original voice (it "all sounds alike / midbaseline / clutters everything with noise"). Taste is the guardrail at the **input** (framework selection) and the **output** (the "don't look foolish" review gate). **Every workflow carries both an AI-leverage mechanic AND a taste gate — both, always.** That dual requirement is the skill.

## The Skill at a Glance

- **THE THREE-AXIS MACRO FRAME** (beneath all tactics): you are **better, faster, or cheaper**. Locate yourself; all messaging/creative/positioning derives from that one placement.
- **THE WEEKLY OS** (4.5-day fixed budget — *caps matter more than allocations*): 8h New Creative → 2× capped 2h Performance (set a timer, leave when it rings) → 4h Funnel → 4h Collateral+Email → 1 full Influencer day → ~8h Logistics (meetings ~1h/day) → last 4h Organic (idea-VALIDATION only). Each block is secretly a future job description.
- **THE THREE MONTHLY MECHANICS**: (1) Word-of-mouth before any paid (4-axis virality audit + completion-moment referral); (2) MESSAGES (value-props mined monthly from 4 friction channels + 1 trending hook → list that gates all creative); (3) INFO-RELEASE (fan each message to 6 surfaces, prioritizing LLM-scraped ones = AEO).
- **THE ANTI-SLOP LOCK**: AI's failure mode is regression-to-the-mean and the mean is rising. Class A output (collateral/ads/landing/SEO/cart-flows) = AI-assisted; Class B (founder POV, LinkedIn/IG, plain-text founder email) = human voice only. Diagnostic before automating anything: *"Is sameness acceptable here?"*
- **THE FUNNEL FLYWHEEL**: buyer/job/offer lock → route → capture/conversation → qualified sale/payment → fulfillment → retention/referral → retained learning. Pages and tools are handoffs; proof state and economics veto govern every claim.
