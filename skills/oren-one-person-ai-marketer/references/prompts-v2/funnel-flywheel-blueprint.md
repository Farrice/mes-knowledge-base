## Role & Activation

Use Oren's source-derived definition of a funnel as the customer's journey from attention to sale, expanded through the existing Antigravity retention and proof gates. Architect the complete journey; do not mistake pages, bookings, or automation for commercial proof.

## Input Required

1. **[BUYER_AND_PURCHASED_JOB]**
2. **[PRIMARY_OFFER_AND_BOUNDARIES]**
3. **[PRICE_COMMITMENT_AND_CURRENT_PROOF_STATE]**
4. **[TRAFFIC_OR_ATTENTION_SOURCES]**
5. **[SALES_MOTION_AND_HUMAN_CAPACITY]**
6. **[PROOF_ASSETS_AND_CLAIM_BOUNDARIES]**
7. **[FULFILLMENT_AND_FOLLOW_UP_CAPACITY]**
8. **[KNOWN_ECONOMICS]** — CAC, conversion, fulfillment cost, repeat purchase, LTV; mark unknowns
9. **[PERMISSION_BOUNDARY]** — draft-only or explicitly authorized actions

## Execution Protocol

1. Lock buyer/job/offer and label the commercial state.
2. Map `UNSEEN → ATTENTION → KNOWN LEAD → QUALIFIED → CONVERSATION/CHECKOUT → PAID → FULFILLED → RETAINED/REFERRED`.
3. For every transition name trigger, asset/conversation, one action, owner, evidence, measurement, and failure state.
4. Select one primary route and at most one supporting route; state rejected alternatives.
5. Specify capture, confirmation, qualification, booking/checkout, show-up, nurture, and CRM handoffs.
6. Connect only justified ladder rungs; keep future offers behind evidence triggers.
7. Apply the economics veto and permission veto.
8. End with the smallest decision-bearing test and retained-learning destination.

## Output Contract

Deliver a Route Card, Customer-Journey Map, Capture/Conversation Sequence, Offer Ladder, Automation Handoff, Measurement Plan, Assumption Ledger, and Next-Test Queue. Label SOURCE-DERIVED, SYSTEM ENHANCEMENT, CONDITIONAL SUPPORT, UNVERIFIED HEURISTIC, and UNTESTED claims where relevant.

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Buyer/job/offer and current proof state are explicit.
- [ ] Every transition has one action, owner, measurement, and failure state.
- [ ] Route choice includes rejected alternatives.
- [ ] Missing economics cannot produce profitability language.
- [ ] No external action is implied beyond the permission boundary.
- [ ] One next test changes a real decision.

## Creative Latitude

The journey shape may depart from the skeleton when the buyer's real behavior demands it. Preserve the named outputs and truth gates; do not force unnecessary pages or rungs just to make the blueprint look complete.

## Deploy When

Use when a business needs the complete acquisition-to-retention system or the existing four-hour Funnel block needs a concrete operating plan.
