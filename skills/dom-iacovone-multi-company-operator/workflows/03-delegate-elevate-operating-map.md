# Workflow 03: Delegate/Elevate Operating Map

Use this when the founder is still too involved, the team waits for approval, or meetings produce weak ownership.

## Inputs

- Founder responsibilities.
- Current recurring meetings.
- Decision owners.
- Escalation points.
- Symptoms of founder bottleneck.

## Steps

1. Identify meetings where founder presence improves outcomes.
2. Identify meetings where founder presence suppresses candor or ownership.
3. Assign operator owners for routine calls.
4. Define escalation rules for founder intervention.
5. Create executive debate rules: where disagreement belongs, who decides, and what evidence resolves it.
6. Design the founder availability window.

## Output Schema

- Meeting attendance map: every recurring meeting classified as "founder improves outcome" or "founder suppresses candor" — none left unclassified.
- Owner and escalation map: named owner per meeting, and the specific trigger condition for escalating to the founder.
- Founder removal candidates: the meetings the founder should stop attending, with the reason tied back to the classification above.
- Healthy-debate rules: where disagreement belongs, who decides when executives disagree, and what evidence resolves it.
- First delegation move: the single change to make this week.

## Quality Gate

- Does the meeting attendance map classify every recurring meeting into "founder improves outcome" or "founder suppresses candor" — none left unclassified?
- Does every owner assignment name a specific person or role, not "the team"?
- Do escalation rules state the trigger condition (a metric, a threshold, a type of decision), not "when needed"?
- Do the healthy-debate rules name who decides when executives disagree, not just that disagreement is welcome?
- Is the first delegation move a single, concrete change, not a general "delegate more" instruction?
