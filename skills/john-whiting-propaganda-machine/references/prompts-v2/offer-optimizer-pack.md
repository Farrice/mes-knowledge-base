## Role & Activation

You are building John Whiting's **Offer Optimizer** — the avatar-language answer key that gets re-read before every piece of content, copy, or collateral gets drafted. Whiting's framing: *"It's like if you're going to go take a test, you have two options. You can study real hard and guess... or you can just go to the teacher and get the answer key."* This industrializes the One Best Client's language into an always-on qualified-survey engine, compressed into a three-page document with hard word caps.

## Input Required

- `[OPERATOR/OFFER]` and the `[ONE BEST CLIENT]` — name, and their onboarding form/call transcripts/DMs if available (if no named best client exists, run the One Best Client Brief deliverable first)
- `[AUDIENCE/LIST]` — an email list, client base, or warm following to survey (if zero audience, seed from the One Best Client's artifacts alone and mark the survey layer "install at first promotion")
- `[QUALIFICATION FIELDS]` — the 1-2 radio-button fields that separate qualified from unqualified respondents for THIS operator's market (e.g., business type + revenue level)
- `[BIG DESIRE CANDIDATE]` — a first-pass guess at the ≤4-word big desire, to be refined against real language

## Execution Protocol

**Pre-Flight Gate.** Confirm: (1) a One Best Client is on file — the optimizer is seeded from their real artifacts; (2) a list or audience exists to survey, or seed from the best client alone; (3) the operator will actually enforce qualification filtering — averaging in every response regresses the language bank to the broad market; (4) run the Ethics Gate intent check — the mechanisms attached to the harvested language must be real and deliverable.

**Step 1 — Install the survey instrument.** One form, deployed at every promotion/onboarding/launch, incentivized (discount, priority access, bonus): identity fields (name, email, phone); radio-button qualifiers (the 1-2 fields that separate qualified from not — these exist to FILTER, not to be polite); three long-form text questions in their own typed words: (1) "When it comes to [big goal], what are your biggest goals?" (2) "...biggest challenges?" (3) "Top 3 things you want to learn as it relates to [big goal]?"

**Step 2 — Filter, then compress.** Cut every unqualified response BEFORE synthesis. Dump the qualified set into an LLM with an explicit instruction to extract the most frequent verbatim phrases for desires/challenges/learning wants, preserving exact wording — no paraphrase, no marketing-speak, ranked by frequency. Merge with the One Best Client's own verbatim phrases (their words outrank the collective's on ties).

**Step 3 — Page 1: the offer grid (word caps are load-bearing).** Big desire ≤4 words ("seven figure freedom"). Top-3 obstacles in their phrasing ("no time," "stressed out," "inconsistent profit"). Each obstacle flipped into a want, capped ~6/3/4 words, punchy and concise. Per want: a named mechanism → outcome → benefit, with every chain flowing visibly back to the big desire. A mechanism that doesn't ladder up to the big desire is a feature, not an offer component — cut or re-chain it.

**Step 4 — Page 2: you and the company.** North-star question (Whiting's: "what does the data say to do?"), what you're known for, mission, vision, thesis/main argument, what you're FOR and AGAINST (the polarization spec — repel lines live here), and verifiable credibility. This page is why the same beliefs recur across every asset.

**Step 5 — Page 3: avatar motivations, now/future.** A 2×2 in their words: what they want today / are frustrated by today / fear tomorrow if unchanged / aspire to tomorrow. Collateral rule: lead with today's felt problem, sell tomorrow's aspiration + averted fear.

**Step 6 — Install it as the answer key.** This document is a pre-write ritual, not a filed deliverable: reviewed before every piece of content so the operator only ever writes to one real person. Wire it into the content SOP (and into the objection arsenal + content cadence engine as the language source). Refresh the language bank at every survey deployment; re-run the grid if the One Best Client changes.

## Output Contract

Deliver an **Offer Optimizer Pack**:
1. The survey instrument — fields, qualifiers, the three long-form questions phrased for this market, the incentive, deployment points
2. The language bank — qualified-only, verbatim phrases ranked by frequency, One Best Client phrases flagged
3. Page 1 grid — big desire (≤4 words), 3 obstacles → wants (capped), mechanism/outcome/benefit chains, each traced back to the desire
4. Page 2 — north-star question, known-for, mission, vision, thesis, for/against, credibility (verifiable only)
5. Page 3 — the now/future motivation 2×2
6. The install rule — where the pre-write ritual lives, refresh cadence. Ethics Gate sign-off.

## Quality Gate

- Does every cell trace to a verbatim source (survey response, onboarding form, transcript), not an operator guess "because it sounded right"?
- Was the qualification filter actually applied before synthesis, or did unqualified responses get averaged in?
- Do Page 1's word caps hold (≤4 / ~6 / ~3 / ~4), with no cell ballooning into template slop?
- Does Page 2's AGAINST column actually repel (specific, not generic "we're against mediocrity")?
- Does the thesis (Page 2) and the big desire (Page 1) read as the same belief from two sides?
- Are Page 2's credibility claims real and verifiable, with no borrowed or inflated authority?

## Creative Latitude

The mechanism names on Page 1 and the FOR/AGAINST lines on Page 2 are where craft matters most — a mechanism name that's generic ("our coaching program") does nothing; a named, ownable mechanism does real positioning work. Push to find the sharpest, most specific way to name each mechanism and each AGAINST line using the survey's own harvested language rather than reaching for marketer-speak. The word caps are a floor for concision, not a ceiling on creativity — the tightest phrasing that still carries the real language wins.

## Deploy When

- Content/copy keeps guessing at the avatar's language instead of using it
- A One Best Client is identified and there's a list or client base to survey
- Before any major content or sales-page push, as the pre-write reference document
