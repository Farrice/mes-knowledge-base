## Pre-Flight Gate

From the genius.md decision framework — confirm before you spend a dollar:

1. **Is there an asset army to distribute?** This workflow moves assets through tiers; it does not create them. If there is no objection inventory and no belief-shift library, route to `jw-objection-arsenal` first. Empty pipes carry nothing.
2. **Is there real borrowed authority for the TOF?** Tier 1 runs on testimonial ads from recognizable names. If the only proof is staged or thin, do NOT fake it — fall back to a results-led or mechanism-led TOF and flag it. Borrowed authority is fragile capital: "one wrong move and those same people start saying bad things." Phantom proof fails the Ethics Gate.
3. **Can the operator fund a feed, not just a buy?** TOF liquidates ~30% of spend — it is built to break-roughly-even and FEED the warm audience, not to profit. If the budget needs the front end to be cash-flow positive on day one, the math is wrong and this funnel will read as a loss. Reset the expectation or change the vehicle (`jw-vehicle-engineer`).
4. **Run `jw-ethics-gate` intent check.** Reversible respect: would you be comfortable if the prospect saw the entire machine — the frequency caps, the tier logic, the assumptive close? If the funnel only works in the dark, kill it.

If all four pass, build.

## The Build (6 steps)

### Step 1 — Define the audiences and size them
You are building three concentric rings, warmest at the center. Name each, define its membership rule, and estimate its size:
- **TOF (cold/just-warmed):** anyone who hit the front end — book/SLO landing page visitors, ad engagers, video viewers (3s+). This is the intake.
- **MOF (~1,000 people):** the engaged warm — completed-play viewers, multi-touch engagers, email opens, the people who consumed more than one asset. The belief-shift happens here.
- **BOF (~600 warmest):** the bought-in but not-yet-paid — offer-doc / sales-page viewers, book BUYERS, application starters, DM repliers. These are the people one decision away.
Whiting's own ratios: ~1,000 in MOF feeding ~600 in BOF. Use his numbers as the shape, not gospel; scale to your traffic. The sizing tells you the spend per tier.

### Step 2 — Map every asset to a tier
An asset's tier is set by the JOB it does, not its format:
- **TOF assets = testimonial ads led by recognizable names.** Borrowed authority does the heavy lifting: higher AOV, longer to convert, but it frames how the market sees you by association. Route to a book or SLO (self-liquidating offer). The TOF's only job is to manufacture a warm audience the back tiers can work on.
- **MOF assets = the ~50 belief-shift pieces** from the arsenal. Objection-killers, big-domino installs, mechanism reveals, polarization/self-selection content. This is where you change what they believe.
- **BOF assets = logistics + assumptive-close onboarding clips.** "Here's what happens next… looking forward to our initiation call." Run pieces of your actual onboarding as if they have already joined. Pattern #6: when they feel like they are already a client, the only thing left to do is become one.

### Step 3 — Set the frequency cap per tier (this is the lever)
Frequency is the throttle. Too low and the belief never installs; too high and you burn the asset and the audience. Whiting's caps:
- **TOF:** standard prospecting frequency. Let the algorithm optimize reach; you are buying intake, not saturating.
- **MOF: ~2× per 7 days.** Enough repetition to install belief without fatigue. ~50 assets at this cap is what manufactures the 7 controlled hours over weeks.
- **BOF: ~5× per day.** The warmest ~600 are cheap to saturate because the audience is tiny. Heavy frequency here is the force-feed that closes. This is why a small warm ring is the most valuable real estate you own.
Write the caps into the build sheet as hard numbers per tier. Hard caps because number-of-times-over is what builds certainty — repetition installs the belief. This is the force-feed mechanic of Pattern #5: ~50 assets at controlled frequency over weeks, hammered in front of the warm ring until the 7 hours accumulate.

### Step 4 — Set the optimization objective per tier
Each tier optimizes for a DIFFERENT thing, because each does a different job. Optimize on the wrong objective and the data lies to you:
- **TOF → liquidation / purchase (book or SLO).** Target ~30% spend recapture. You are measuring whether the front end feeds the back end affordably, NOT ROAS. A "losing" TOF that fills MOF cheaply is a winning TOF.
- **MOF → completed plays / thru-play.** You want belief installed, which means the asset got WATCHED, not just served. Optimize for completed video plays, not clicks. Track time-on-content toward the 7-hour target.
- **BOF → leads / conversations / purchases.** This ring is close to the money; optimize for the action that precedes payment. Cheap saturation + assumptive frame = self-close.

### Step 5 — Plan the feed (TOF warms MOF warms BOF)
The tiers are not parallel — they are a pipeline. Each ring's job is to graduate people into the next ring:
1. TOF buys cold attention and converts a slice into book/SLO buyers and engaged viewers → those people now meet the MOF membership rule.
2. MOF runs ~50 belief-shift assets at ~2×/7days until a viewer has consumed enough and taken a warm action (offer-doc view, book buy, application start) → they graduate to BOF.
3. BOF saturates the warmest ~600 with logistics + assumptive-close clips at ~5×/day until they self-close.
Total controlled exposure across the pipeline = the **force-fed 7 hours**. Map the graduation triggers explicitly (what action moves someone from ring to ring). A funnel with no graduation logic is three disconnected ad sets, not a machine.

### Step 6 — Set the throughput expectations and the fragility flags
- **Time-to-close ~67 days (and shrinking).** Tell the operator this up front (pre-frame inoculation, signature move #3). A 67-day cycle reads as "broken" to anyone expecting a 7-day funnel — set the expectation so the wait becomes proof the machine is working, not a complaint.
- **Borrowed-authority fragility.** The recognizable-name testimonials that power TOF also bind your reputation to theirs. One bad public move and the same names become liabilities. Build a contingency TOF (results-led or mechanism-led) so the funnel does not collapse if the borrowed authority sours.
- **Verify efficiency before adding volume.** Volume is usually the real answer — but only after the tier conversion rates check out. Before scaling spend, confirm MOF→BOF graduation and BOF self-close are working. Scaling a leaky funnel just buys more leaks.

## Content Type Adaptations

The tier logic is universal; the distribution mechanism and the spine-translation change by channel.

| Deliverable | How the funnel adapts |
|---|---|
| **Coaching / high-ticket service, no calls (Authority Flywheel — $2K–$5K)** | Hero use case. TOF = a low-cost book or offer-doc led by your two best named results. MOF = ~50 belief-shift assets killing the "I need a call first" objection. BOF saturates offer-doc viewers with onboarding clips at ~5×/day. The 67-day cycle replaces the sales call entirely. Mode B spine: *"If you need a call to be convinced, you'll need a call to stay convinced. I built this so you can decide on your own time — that's the respect."* (Wrong-fit reader squirms, right-fit reader feels seen.) |
| **LinkedIn-native (× Lara Acosta)** | No paid retargeting — frequency is engineered through posting cadence + algorithm warm-audience + DM funnel. TOF = reach posts with named proof; MOF = belief-shift posts to engagers; BOF = DM sequence with assumptive-close language. "Frequency cap" becomes posting consistency hitting the same warm followers repeatedly. |
| **Newsletter / Substack (× Nicolas Cole)** | The send IS the frequency mechanism; the list is the warm audience. TOF = lead magnet / book funnel growing the list. MOF = belief-shift editions on a cadence, the big domino as the through-line. BOF = an assumptive-close segment (high-engagement readers) gets the "here's what happens when you join" sequence. |
| **Paid ads (× Luke Iha / Sabri Suby)** | Full paid build as written. Luke's vicious hooks + proof ladders dress the MOF assets; Sabri's halo-offer / SLO math powers the TOF liquidation target. Optimize TOF on liquidation, MOF on thru-play, BOF on leads — exactly as Step 4. This is the funnel at full instrumentation. |
| **Low / no ad budget (organic-only)** | No paid frequency control — organic carries distribution and the time-to-close lengthens past 67 days. Lean harder on polarization for reach (self-selection content travels). "BOF saturation" becomes a manual warm-list sequence (DMs, email, retargeting only the warmest if any budget exists). Pre-frame the longer cycle aggressively. |

## Output Requirements

Deliver a **Retargeting Build Sheet** the operator can hand to a media buyer and run:
1. **Audiences:** the three rings named, each with its membership rule and an estimated size (anchor on ~1,000 MOF / ~600 BOF, scaled to actual traffic).
2. **Assets-per-tier:** every asset mapped to its ring by the job it does — TOF testimonial/borrowed-authority set, MOF ~50 belief-shift pieces, BOF logistics + assumptive-close onboarding clips.
3. **Frequency caps:** hard numbers per tier — TOF standard, MOF ~2×/7days, BOF ~5×/day.
4. **Optimization objective per tier:** TOF = liquidation (~30% recapture), MOF = completed plays, BOF = leads/conversations/purchases.
5. **The feed map:** graduation triggers that move a person TOF→MOF→BOF, totaling the force-fed 7 hours.
6. **Throughput + risk notes:** ~67-day time-to-close (pre-framed), borrowed-authority fragility + contingency TOF, "verify efficiency before adding volume."
7. **Ethics Gate sign-off** on the whole funnel (reversible respect on the frequency logic especially).

## Quality Gate

Score against the genius.md **Expert-Specific Quality Rubric** (1–10). The dimensions this workflow lives or dies on:

- **Reality-grounded? (Rubric #1)** — Are tier sizes, caps, and the ~30% liquidation target real numbers tied to the operator's actual traffic, or hopeful guesses? No "I feel like 2× is enough."
- **Leveraged? (Rubric #6)** — Once built, does the funnel run on frequency and graduation logic without the operator manually nudging it? A funnel that needs daily hand-holding is not leveraged.
- **One big domino? (Rubric #8)** — Do the MOF assets all drive the ONE core belief, or scatter across disconnected points? Scattered MOF = no compounding.
- **Edge intact? (Rubric #5)** — Run the spine test on every client-facing asset that flows through the tiers. A testimonial ad or onboarding clip sanded into agreeable mush stops self-selecting and the funnel converts no one.
- **Ethics Gate passed? (Rubric #7)** — All four gates. Watch two failure modes specific to THIS workflow: (a) **phantom borrowed authority** — staged or implied recognizable-name proof violates gates #1–2; (b) **frequency that only works in the dark** — if 5×/day BOF saturation would make the prospect feel ambushed rather than served once they understood it, it fails reversible respect. Kill it.

**Funnel-specific failure modes:** parallel ad sets with no graduation triggers (three buckets, not a pipeline); optimizing every tier on the same objective (the data lies); a TOF expected to profit (kills the feed); MOF assets that scatter instead of compounding the one domino.

If any dimension <6, rebuild that step once and re-score.
