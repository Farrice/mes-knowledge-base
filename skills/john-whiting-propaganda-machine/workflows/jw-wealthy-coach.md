# The Wealthy Coach — Revenue-to-Wealth Engineering

> "The freedom number is the number you can accumulate in liquidity that you can draw down 4% of and live your ideal lifestyle." — John Whiting (V3)

Revenue is not wealth. A coach pulling $80K/mo with no allocation system is a high-income employee of his own business, one bad quarter from broke. This workflow reverse-engineers wealth from the life you actually want, then back-calculates the daily KPIs and the dollar-by-dollar allocation that funds it. Genius pattern #11. *Not financial advice — see disclaimer at the bottom; carry it on every output.*

---

## Pre-Flight Gate

Before running the math, confirm via the genius.md decision framework:

1. **Is the business profitable and tracked?** Wealth math runs on real after-tax profit, not topline. If the operator can't state last month's actual profit and tax set-aside, route to `jw-record-month-formula` + `jw-data-bottleneck` first. *"We make business decisions based on what made stats go up,"* not on a topline they've never net out.
2. **Has the operator defined Point B, or just "more"?** The Freedom Number requires a priced-out lifestyle, not a vibe. If the destination is vague, this is a `jw-the-map` Destination problem before it's a money problem. The bar for success is hilariously low: *"all you have to do is get clear on what you want and write it down"* — and 99.9% never do.
3. **Are they chasing exit or lifestyle?** A coaching business is a lifestyle business by definition. If the operator is optimizing for a fictional acquisition, name it — this workflow builds for lean profit and draw-down, not a multiple.
4. **Ethics Gate (intent):** No fabricated returns, no "you'll be a millionaire in a year" math. Every number we install is one we'd defend under oath. If the plan only works on fantasy compounding, kill it.

## The Engine (7 steps)

### Step 1 — Price the life, then build the Freedom Number
Define exactly how you want to live: housing, vehicles, travel, schooling, giving, the works. Price it to a **monthly spend**. Most coaches underestimate by 30–50% — they forget taxes-on-the-life, healthcare, lumpy annual costs (insurance, tuition) amortized monthly, and the cost of the team that buys back their time. Inflate the honest number; do not flatter it.
- **Freedom Number = (ideal monthly spend × 12) ÷ 0.04.** A $50K/mo life needs a **$15M** liquid portfolio throwing off 4%/yr without touching principal. $20K/mo → $6M. $30K/mo → $9M.
- This is the **Destination** on the map. Everything downstream reverse-engineers from it. *"The idea is to get your gut to where it matches what the data says to do. That's where ultimate confidence comes from."*

### Step 2 — Back-calculate the KPIs (the GPS)
The Freedom Number is the output. Now solve for inputs. Pick a target accumulation date → required portfolio → required annual invested capital (after expected return, not aspirational) → required after-tax profit → required revenue → required leads/calls/closes at the operator's *actual, historical* conversion rates. This is the GPS: **targets in, daily/weekly/monthly KPIs out.** Don't argue with the GPS. If the daily input is impossible at current price/conversion, the lever is price or niche, not heroics (the "747-sized opportunity").

### Step 3 — Allocate every dollar of after-tax profit
The wealth happens here, not in the topline. *"You get rich by spending less than you earn and investing the difference."* Default split of after-tax profit:
- **≤50% — lifestyle** (this is the cap, not the target; under it is better).
- **~10% — education / masterminds** (write-off + ROI; the highest-return asset for an operator who is still the bottleneck).
- **~10% — treats** (guilt-free, named in advance, so discipline doesn't snap from deprivation).
- **Remainder (~30%+) — invested** down the ladder in Step 5.
Disorganized money repels money. Name every dollar a job before it lands.

### Step 4 — "If you can't buy it twice, don't buy it once"
Every lifestyle purchase gets matched with an equal investment deposit. *"If you can't buy it twice, don't buy it once."* — he matched a $142K Audi R8 purchase with $45K down AND $45K invested. The rule is a brake disguised as permission: if the matching deposit would hurt, you can't actually afford the toy yet. Wealth and the dopamine hit move in lockstep, so the lifestyle never outruns the engine that funds it.

### Step 5 — Invest down the priority ladder (lowest → highest risk)
Order is non-negotiable; you don't fund a later rung until the earlier ones are saturated:
1. **Self** — skills/health that raise the operator's own throughput (highest ROI while you're the bottleneck).
2. **Systems** — automation/SOPs that buy back time and de-risk the business.
3. **Marketing** — proven channels at known CAC:LTV.
4. **Team** — only after the operator has mastered the role (`jw-figure-it-out-yourself`; never abdicate).
5. **Index funds** — the boring, compounding base. *"Asset allocation is the key to growing wealth, not income."*
6. **Speculation — ≤5%** of investable capital, hard cap. Crypto, angel, moonshots live here and nowhere else.

### Step 6 — Structure the accounts so money flows to an organized place
- **Separate business and personal** accounts — no commingling, ever.
- **Tax set-aside** swept on every deposit (an account you pretend doesn't exist).
- **S-corp**: pay a reasonable W2 salary + take distributions (self-employment tax efficiency). Confirm specifics with a CPA — this is structure, not tax advice.
- A **3-month operating cash buffer** held separately. *"Money likes to flow to organized places."* Structure precedes inflow.

### Step 7 — Compound gradually; "things die as fast as they grow"
No moonshots. *"Get rich slow and stay rich forever."* Dollar-cost-average into the ladder on a fixed schedule, rebalance quarterly, never time the market. Treat the 3-month buffer as **the new zero** — you are "broke" at one month of runway, not at $0, which keeps you out of forced-sell decisions. Compounding does the heavy lifting (Richard Johnson: $14/hr UPS driver, invested the difference 40 years → ~$70M). The operator's only job is to not interrupt it.

## Output Requirements

Deliver a **Wealthy Coach Plan**:
1. **Priced lifestyle → Freedom Number** (the filled calc: monthly spend × 12 ÷ 0.04, with the underestimate audited).
2. **Reverse-engineered KPIs** (target date → required portfolio → annual invested capital → after-tax profit → revenue → daily/weekly inputs at *historical* conversion).
3. **Allocation plan** (the ≤50% / 10% / 10% / invested split applied to actual after-tax profit, in dollars).
4. **Investment priority ladder** (specific allocation across the 6 rungs, speculation ≤5% enforced).
5. **Account structure checklist** (separation, tax sweep, S-corp note-to-CPA, 3-month buffer = new zero).
6. **Cadence**: DCA schedule + quarterly rebalance + the "buy it twice" rule stated for the next planned purchase.
7. **The disclaimer**, verbatim, on the document.

## Quality Gate

Score against the genius.md **8-dimension rubric**. Failure modes specific to this workflow:
- **#1 Reality-grounded?** Built on the operator's *actual* after-tax profit and *historical* conversion rates — not topline, not aspirational returns. A plan running on 20% annual returns or unaudited spend fails here.
- **#2 Reverse-engineered?** Starts from the priced Freedom Number and works backward to daily inputs. If it flies forward from "save more," fail.
- **#5 Edge intact?** The revenue≠wealth confrontation survives translation (Mode B spine test). "We recommend a balanced approach to saving!" is sanded-down mush — kill it.
- **#4 Self-selecting?** When shipped as content (esp. the Flywheel row), the Freedom-Number math should make the wrong-fit prospect math-shame themselves out and the right-fit prospect feel called in — the gap does the qualifying, no pitch required. If everyone nods along comfortably, it isn't sorting.
- **#7 Ethics Gate?** No fabricated returns, no fake compounding miracles, no "get rich quick" framing. The plan must work in the daylight; you'd be fine if the buyer saw every number behind it.
- **#8 One big domino?** Revenue ≠ wealth — installed and reinforced so every objection ("I'll save later," "I just need a bigger month") becomes irrelevant.

If any dimension <6, rebuild that step once and re-score.
