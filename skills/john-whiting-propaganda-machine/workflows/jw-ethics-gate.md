# Ethics Gate — The Audit That Runs on Everything

> "When I brainwash somebody, their business doubles. When the government brainwashes people, they take vaccines and die. You can use those same principles to create or destroy." — John Whiting

The mechanism is amoral. The frequency, the retargeting, the belief-installs, the self-selection knife — none of it has a conscience. The operator supplies the morality, and supplies it deliberately, before the asset reaches a human. This atom is the gate. Every other `jw-` workflow ships its output through here first. No exceptions.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Per genius.md Decision Framework: every deliverable that installs or reinforces a belief runs the Ethics Gate before shipping. Confirm before auditing:

1. **Is there an actual asset to audit?** A finished or near-finished piece that installs a belief — ad, post, email, clip, landing page, onboarding sequence. Not a vibe, not an outline. If it's still abstract, the gate has nothing to bite on. Build the asset, then return.
2. **Do you know the exact belief each asset installs?** Name it in one sentence: "After this, the buyer believes ___." If you can't name the belief, you can't audit whether it's true, proven, or good for them. Run `jw-big-domino` first.
3. **Is the operator willing to kill?** The gate only works if a fail means death. An operator who'll "tweak it a little" past a hard fail has defeated the gate. Confirm the kill is on the table before you start.

## The Gate (run every asset through all four)

Confrontation note: the gate is itself a Whiting move. It confronts the operator the way his content confronts the prospect. You are not looking for reasons to pass. You are looking for the lie, because the lie is where the asset becomes manipulation. Reality over comfort applies to your own work first.

For each asset, score each gate **PASS / FAIL**, and on any fail write **the fix or the kill**.

1. **TRUE CLAIMS ONLY.** Inventory every claim, number, promise, result, and scarcity statement in the asset. For each: would you defend it under oath, with documentation, in front of someone hostile? "$300K/month" — real and provable? "Spots close Friday" — actually closing Friday, or invented urgency? Any fabricated proof, invented result, or fake deadline = FAIL. Fix: replace with a true claim, or cut it. There is no "directionally true."

2. **REAL PROOF.** Every testimonial is a real person with a real, attributable outcome. Every borrowed-authority play (recognizable name, logo, "as seen in") is earned, not staged. Stock-photo "clients," composite testimonials, screenshots you can't source, authority you rented but didn't earn = FAIL. Whiting's own warning applies: borrowed authority is fragile capital — "one wrong move and those same people start saying bad things." If you can't put the named human on a call to confirm it, it doesn't ship. Fix: swap in proof that survives a phone call, or cut the claim it props up.

3. **BUYER'S GENUINE INTEREST.** The belief-shift this asset installs — does it move the buyer toward something genuinely good *for them*, or only good for your bank account? This is the gate that separates the cold shower from the con. Whiting's defense of his own work is the bar: "when I do it, their business doubles." If the honest answer is "this belief makes them buy but doesn't make them better off," = FAIL. The bait can be their surface desire; the switch has to deliver something real. Fix: re-aim the belief at an outcome you'd want for your own family, or kill the offer the asset serves.

4. **REVERSIBLE RESPECT.** Would you be comfortable if this exact prospect saw the entire machine — the frequency cap, the retargeting sequence, the assumptive-close clips, the self-selection knife, the strategy doc — and understood precisely what you were doing to them and why? If the asset only works because they can't see the mechanism, = FAIL. No move that dies in the light. The tell: a mechanism you'd hide from the person it's used on is manipulation by definition. Fix: redesign the move so it survives full disclosure, or cut it.

**Verdict logic.** Pass all four = SHIP. Fail any one = the asset is manipulation, not persuasion. Apply the fix and re-run the gate, or **KILL the asset.** A fix is not a pass; the re-run is the pass. Document every fail, every fix, every kill — the kill log is the operator's conscience made legible, and it compounds taste across future builds.

A note on the fix, voice-wise: when a soft-fail forces a rewrite, restore the confrontational spine (Mode A raw or Mode B translated), do not hedge your way to safety. A true, polarizing claim passes the gate. A sanded-down, "for everyone," hedged claim fails Rubric #5 even when it clears all four gates. Edge and ethics are not in tension. The honest version is usually the sharper one.

## Output Requirements

Deliver a **Gate Scorecard**, one block per asset:

1. **Asset ID + the belief it installs** (one sentence: "After this, the buyer believes ___").
2. **Four-gate table:** Gate 1 / 2 / 3 / 4, each marked PASS or FAIL.
3. **For every FAIL:** the specific claim/proof/belief/mechanism that failed, plus **the fix applied OR the kill decision** (no blanks).
4. **Verdict:** SHIP (all four pass) · SHIP-AFTER-FIX (failed, fixed, re-run clean) · KILLED (failed, unfixable).
5. **Kill log entry** (if killed): one line on what it would have manipulated and why it couldn't be saved — so the pattern doesn't recur.
6. **Factual Grounding label** on every retained claim: VERIFIED / LIKELY / UNCONFIRMED. Any UNCONFIRMED claim that survives must be flagged in the asset or cut.

## Quality Gate

This atom IS the gate, so the rubric scores how rigorously it ran. Score against genius.md rubric:

- **Ethics Gate passed (Rubric #7)** — the headline dimension. All four gates explicitly scored on every asset, every fail resolved by a documented fix-and-rerun or a kill. A scorecard with no fails on a real propaganda asset is itself suspect — re-audit, you went too easy.
- **Reality-grounded (Rubric #1)** — Gate 1 and Gate 2 are the persuasion-domain teeth of Factual Grounding. Every number and testimonial sourced or cut.
- **Edge intact (Rubric #5)** — confirm no fix sanded the spine into mush. Run the spine test on every rewritten line: does it still make the wrong-fit reader uncomfortable and the right-fit reader feel seen?

Failure modes specific to this workflow:
- **Rubber-stamping.** Marking PASS to avoid the work of a kill. The gate that never fails anything isn't a gate.
- **Tweak-not-kill.** Treating a hard fail as a copy edit. A fabricated result is not a wording problem.
- **Auditing the vibe, not the claims.** Passing an asset on tone while leaving an UNCONFIRMED number inside it. Gate 1 is line-item, not impression.
- **Over-cleaning under cover of ethics.** Using a Gate-3 rewrite as an excuse to hedge and please everyone. Restoring honesty is not the same as removing edge.

If Rubric #7 scores <6 on any asset that nonetheless got marked SHIP, the scorecard is invalid. Re-run the gate before anything ships.
