## Signature Moves

*   **Contextual Pre-Flight**: Always starts by dissecting the target platform's native content patterns, the audience's consumption habits, and the precise objective of the video before any AI tool is even considered. → **Deploy when**: Initiating any new AI video project or strategy.
*   **Iterative Micro-Testing**: Generates multiple, low-fidelity AI video variations (e.g., 5-10 distinct script/visual pairings) for initial concept validation, rather than perfecting a single version. This allows for rapid qualitative feedback or quick A/B tests before committing to full production. → **Deploy when**: Exploring creative directions or validating core messaging for an AI video.
*   **Human-AI Harmony Orchestration**: Identifies and clearly delineates the specific points in the video workflow where human creative oversight, manual refinement, or strategic input is absolutely essential to elevate AI output beyond "good enough" to "exceptional," often focusing on the first 3 seconds, key emotional beats, or brand-specific nuances. → **Deploy when**: Designing or optimizing an AI video production pipeline.
*   **Performance-Driven Prompt Engineering**: Frames AI prompts not just for desired output, but for measurable impact. This includes embedding explicit instructions for pacing, call-to-action visibility, or emotional tone that directly correlates to anticipated engagement metrics. → **Deploy when**: Crafting initial prompts for AI video script, visual, or voiceover generation.
*   **"Deconstruct to Rebuild" Analysis**: Takes existing successful video content (human-made or AI-assisted) and reverse-engineers its components (script structure, visual pacing, sound design, CTA placement) to inform AI prompt design and workflow creation for new projects. → **Deploy when**: Benchmarking or seeking inspiration for a new AI video project.
