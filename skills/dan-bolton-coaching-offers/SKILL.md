# Dan Bolton — AI-Proof Coaching Offers

Dan Bolton built a multi-million-dollar online coaching business by abandoning the "selling secrets" model before the market forced him to. His system replaces information-scarcity coaching with **co-creation offers** — positioned between do-it-yourself courses and done-for-you agency work — delivered through the Three I Framework: the *right* information (micro, actionable), build-once infrastructure (custom GPTs, templates, dashboards that serve clients forever), and structured implementation support. His client acquisition runs on a single 15-25 minute mini-VSL that every touchpoint in his world points to: one asset, ~10,000 views, hundreds of clients. Underneath the tactics sits a governing conviction: everything works, but not everything works for *you* — strategy comes from outside, but is only confirmed by intuition.

## Quick Reference

- **The shift**: "I have the secrets" is dying — AI made information abundant. Reposition from *I have the information* to *I will do the work WITH you*.
- **Co-creation position**: coaching = do-it-yourself; agency = done-for-you (all responsibility, no client investment); co-creation sits in the middle and outsells both.
- **Three I's**: Information (micro-modules, action not education) + Infrastructure (build once, works forever) + Implementation (structured co-building, async feedback).
- **The subtraction question**: not "how much of my knowledge can I put in?" but "how *little* can I put in while people still get amazing results?"
- **Boredom = productization signal**: giving the same feedback repeatedly? Build a GPT/tool that gives it for you.
- **Mini-VSL**: 15-25 minutes (not 90). Structure: who it's for/not for → your story (ethos) → old way vs. new way (pathos) → simple CTA (logos, DM or call).
- **Neon-sign rule**: every YouTube description, email PS, post keyword, story, and retargeting ad points to the ONE video. Retargeting yes, cold traffic no.
- **Coach the gut**: follow every piece of advice with "but what do you think? What is your gut saying?" — clients who coach themselves into alignment get better results.
- **Benchmark proof points**: client into $1,400/mo offer at $150-200 acquisition; client Jason at $103K cash month on $10/day ad spend; one GPT with 700+ active client chats.
