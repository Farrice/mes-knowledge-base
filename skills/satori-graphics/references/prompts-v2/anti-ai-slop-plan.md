## Role & Activation

You are running Satori's **Anti-AI-Slop via Human Imperfection** discipline. AI layouts default to too-perfect — symmetrical, evenly spaced, polished into a template. The human moat is to "ruin the perfection in a bring-it-to-life way" — not chaos, but deliberate, rent-paying imperfection. You inject a minimum of three human-imperfection moves into any AI-generated or AI-assisted layout heading toward delivery.

> "AI layouts in 2025 are almost just too perfect for their own good… everything starts looking like a default template, which to be fair, that's exactly what it is." — Satori
> "You, the human designer, are supposed to ruin that template style perfection. And I don't mean in some kind of chaotic way, more like in a bring it to life way." — Satori
> "The brands or businesses that hire actual human designers do so for their thinking and their psychological experience in the design space." — Satori

## Input Required

- **[LAYOUT]** — the AI-generated, AI-assisted, or generic-feeling human layout heading to delivery
- **[CONTEXT]** — editorial/streetwear/artistic, brand/hero-key-visual, product/e-commerce hero, standard ad creative, corporate/financial, tech/SaaS landing, or children/family/hospitality (drives the imperfection quota)
- **[TRUST/CLINICAL CONSTRAINTS]** if the brief calls for clinical sterility (medical UI, financial dashboards, government forms) or AI-aware audiences that want the AI aesthetic — name these explicitly, since they change or disable the workflow

## Execution Protocol

### Step 1 — Diagnose the AI Tells

| AI tell | Symptom | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Perfect symmetry | Center-aligned everything; mirror-balance dominates | High |
| Even spacing | All gaps identical; no rhythm variation | High |
| Default template feel | Looks like 100 other layouts | High |
| Evenly distributed elements | Equal weight across all quadrants | Medium |
| Stock-photo cleanliness | Imagery feels untouched, generic | High |
| Generic palette | "Professional blue" / "modern gray" without specificity | Medium |
| Vector-perfect curves | No hand-feel; mathematically smooth | Medium |
| Rigid grid adherence | No breakage; everything in cells | High |
| Headline-deck-body-CTA stacking | Default hierarchy, no creative interpretation | High |
| Standard aspect ratios | All elements at default 1:1/16:9/golden ratio | Low |

Count high-severity tells. 3+ = significant slop signal requiring strong intervention.

### Step 2 — Lock the Imperfection Quota

| Context | Quota |
|---|---|
| Editorial / streetwear / artistic | 5-7 |
| Brand work / hero key visual | 4-5 |
| Product / e-commerce hero | 3-4 |
| Standard ad creative | 3 |
| Corporate / financial | 2-3 (within trust constraints) |
| Tech / SaaS landing | 3-4 |
| Children / family / hospitality | 4-5 (warmth requires hand-feel) |

Minimum: 3. Below 3, the AI-tell signal still dominates.

### Step 3 — Choose Imperfection Moves

**Compositional**: element creep (one element crosses over another), subtle blend (logo/motif at low opacity across background), connecting line (hand-drawn/thin line between distant elements), controlled crop (defy symmetry), asymmetric breathing (more white space on one side, with reason), off-rotation (2-7° on a glyph/motif).

**Surface**: tapered gradient (fades off-axis or stops short of an edge), hand-drawn element (one drawn line/scribble/annotation in a clean composition), texture overlay (subtle grain/paper/risograph), imperfect alignment (4-8px off the grid baseline, deliberate not accidental).

**Rhythm**: rhythm break (one one-line paragraph, one full-bleed image, one section that breathes), type-size variance (non-standard jumps — 14/22/52 instead of 14/24/48), color punctuation (one color in only ONE place), negative-space asymmetry (empty space concentrated on one side).

**Restraint**: subtraction (remove one element the AI insisted on), single-job element (replace a multi-job element with a single-job one), resist-the-balanced-corner (leave the symmetry-demanding corner empty).

### Step 4 — Validate Each Imperfection Pays Rent

Each move needs a concept, hierarchy, or psychology reason. Imperfection without rent is chaos, not character — reject and pick another.

### Step 5 — Sequence the Imperfections (distribution rule)

Don't cluster. Distribute: ONE at or adjacent to the leverage point (anchors the human feel), ONE in a secondary zone (carries the feel through the journey), ONE+ elsewhere (texture-level human signal).

### Step 6 — Stress-Test Against Sterile Baseline

Compare mentally: the AI-perfect baseline vs. the anti-slop version. The anti-slop version must still pass legibility/function, still pass a LIFT-style composition check, still land the correct next-emotion, and feel **more alive, not chaotic**. If it feels chaotic, reduce the imperfection count.

## Output Contract

An Anti-Slop Spec: AI-tell diagnosis with severity, the locked quota (3+ minimum), each injected move with type/implementation/rent-reason/location, a distribution audit (leverage/secondary/texture), a stress test (legibility/composition/empathy/alive-vs-chaotic), executable directives, and an anti-pattern checklist.

## Quality Gate

- Minimum 3 imperfections injected
- Every imperfection pays rent (concept / hierarchy / psychology) — none is decoration
- Distribution is intentional, not clustered in one zone
- Legibility remains fully intact
- The result reads as alive, not chaotic

## Creative Latitude

The move library is the toolkit; the craft is choosing the moves that are specific to *this* layout's content rather than the generic default (a gradient tapered "somewhere" vs. a gradient that tapers off exactly where the leverage point needs isolation). Push toward moves that a genuinely skilled human designer would make out of instinct — the ones that look inevitable in hindsight — over moves that merely check the "3 imperfections" box.

## Deploy When

A layout was AI-generated (Midjourney, DALL-E, Nano Banana, Skywork, Canva AI); a human-made layout feels template/generic; a final layout is heading to delivery and needs the human-quality differentiator; or the brief explicitly demands a hand-crafted feel. Do not use when the brief calls for clinical/systematic sterility, in accessibility-first contexts where imperfection adds cognitive load, or when the audience explicitly wants the AI aesthetic.
