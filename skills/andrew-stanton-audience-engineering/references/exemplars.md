## The Stanton-Grade Gate (run on every /stanton-* output before delivery)

The embodiment gate from the solution card, tuned to this skill. A draft FAILS if any of these is true — fix or regenerate, never ship past it:

1. **Topic, not premise.** The one-sentence premise is a subject ("a post about magnesium forms") instead of character + conflict + conclusion ("trust in the label's number denies a smart buyer the dose she paid for"). No true premise = keep digging.
2. **Handed them 4.** The conclusion, emotion, or mechanism is stated where the reader should assemble it (the 2+2 — `references/ted-talk-layer.md` T4). The tell: a line that tells the reader what to feel or what it means.
3. **Machinery named on the page.** "Here's the tension," a labeled theme, an announced withhold. The architecture must be felt, never seen.
4. **A spine-less protagonist.** The person/brand/customer is a list of behaviors with no governing wiring + one-liner, or a want with no need underneath it.
5. **Static beat survives.** Any beat where nothing changes — no shift in feeling, fact, or position — and no live pull to the next. The cable car disconnected and it shipped anyway.
6. **Vague where specific belongs.** "Results," "a lot," "quickly," "a guide" where a number, a named mechanism, or a spec'd deliverable should sit. Specificity is grip; never strip it to feel safe (compliant-grip rule: move specificity off risky claims onto outputs/time/skills, don't delete it).
7. **Ending merely stops.** Not surprising, or not inevitable, or a tidy takeaway that flattens the run into advice.
8. **The promise never paid.** The opening pulled back a slingshot the piece never releases — an opened debt with no payoff beat.

Pass = none of the eight fire, plus the rubric's ≥8 anchors are nameable. This gate is what "Pixar-level" means operationally; a piece can be clean, verified, and on-voice and still fail it.

## Exemplar 1 — The Clamp Audit on a strong post (diagnostic tier)

**Run**: `/stanton-clamp-audit` on LinkedIn post P1-1 "Why your magnesium does nothing" (Content OS, 2026-07-13 session).
**Why it's Stanton-grade**: on a genuinely strong post (8 of 10 beats held) it found the ONE real slip — beat 7 re-explained the mechanism *after the best image had already landed* ("swallowing a chemistry lesson every night and grading herself on the wrong test"). Root diagnosis, not symptom: the "after the best line" slip, where the reader feels permission to stop. Prescription was a ~40% trim of one beat, not a rewrite.
**Rubric anchors hit**: Clamp/Engagement 9 (walked cold at reader pace, named the exact glance-up), Telling Quality 8 (the fix was restraint, not addition).
**The transferable move**: on strong material the audit's job is *the one slip*, and it usually hides directly after the piece's best moment.

## Exemplar 2 — The portfolio-level escalation find (architecture tier)

**Run**: `/stanton-series-escalation` across all 14 Content OS posts (same session).
**Why it's Stanton-grade**: it read 14 pillar-filed standalone posts and uncovered (not invented) the series premise already latent in them — *"confidence is the costume an empty claim wears, and the operator who learns to ask for the receipt stops paying for it"* — plus a 5-rung stakes ladder the pillar-ordering was hiding: a bottle lies to you ($20) → the machine repeats the lie → your body lies → your instinct lies → the receipt that saves you from the regulator is the same one that wins AI search ($53,088). Diagnosis: "a FOLDER, not a SERIES" — zero inter-post clamp, finale-grade material buried mid-list.
**Rubric anchors hit**: Premise Clarity 9 (the sentence was proven across 14 artifacts), Ending Surprising+Inevitable 9 (the reader came for supplement tips, leaves with a business thesis — and every prior post was secretly teaching it).
**The transferable move**: run escalation on any content BATCH before sequencing; the series premise is usually already in the work, hidden by topic-bucket ordering.

## Exemplar 3 — The 30-second arc (compression tier)

**Run**: `/stanton-30sec-arc` for MyBPM (same session). Full script in the session record; the load-bearing beats:
> [0:00] dead-quiet elevator, earbuds in, blank face — *"Nobody in this elevator knows the drop just hit."* … [0:12] the body finally GOES — *"Then for four minutes, you're not."* … [0:18] product revealed only now, mid-motion … [0:26] back to the blank face, tiny knowing half-smile — *"MyBPM. For the ones somewhere else."*
**Why it's Stanton-grade**: one 5-second change carries the whole arc; the product is WITHHELD until the change lands (2+2 — the viewer assembles "the shirt = the feeling" before being told); the close re-reads the opening image (surprising + inevitable: the calm face was never empty).
**Rubric anchors hit**: Clamp 9 (the gap between still body and inner state opens in line one), Telling Quality 9 (the reveal order IS the message), Premise 8 ("the world's silence denies the feeler a witness — until the shirt").
**The transferable move**: in compressed formats, the reveal ORDER does the work prose can't afford — place the product/offer/point on the change beat, never before it.

## Exemplar 4 — The brand manifesto by negation (positioning tier)

**Run**: `/stanton-brand-origin` for Andrea/Resonance (same session). Load-bearing excerpt:
> "Somewhere we agreed that to really let go, you had to leave a little… Resonance starts from the opposite. Daylight. Clear heads… It sounds like a subtraction. Take away the bar, the dark, the haze, and what's left? The first time you're in it, you find out it was never the bar doing the work."
**Why it's Stanton-grade**: pure define-by-negation (TED T5 — the "no songs" move: daytime/sober against the entire grammar of nightlife); the opposites engine renders it concrete (daylight vs. 2am, full-volume-self vs. numbed); the "what's left?" question is a 2+2 the reader answers one beat before the copy does.
**Rubric anchors hit**: Spine 8 (the dancer who wants the floor but needs to be met without the bar between), Truth vs. Forced 8 (no invented facts; the wonder beat — "how loud sober gets" — is earned from the mechanism, not asserted).
**The transferable move**: when a brand's category has a mandatory grammar, the origin story writes itself from the negation list — name what everyone assumes is load-bearing, remove it, and let the reader discover it was never doing the work.

## Anti-Exemplar — what fails the gate even when it's "good"

A polished, verified, on-voice sales page section that walks: claim → citation → benefit → next claim. Every sentence clean, every fact labeled. It fails Gates 2, 5, and 8: each unit resolves itself (handed them 4), nothing changes beat to beat (static), and no debt is ever opened (no promise). This is the "polished but flat" failure the engines' wiring exists to catch — competence is not clamp. The fix is never "write better sentences"; it's withhold the mechanism one beat, open a debt before each section, and put the biggest reveal ON a change.
