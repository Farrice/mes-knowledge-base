# Implementation — Build + Stacking Guide

How to load this skill, where Stanton sits in the story stack, and which `/stanton-*` command to reach for. Stanton owns one lane: uncover the story already in the marble, then keep the audience clamped to the cable so they never look away. He does not gate whether a story exists, he does not architect a ten-hour season, and he does not write the final line. Stack him with the experts who do.

---

## Load Order

Read top to bottom; stop at the level the task needs. Never produce Stanton-domain output without `genius.md` loaded first — the voice lives there, and a workflow run without it stamps vocabulary instead of doing the work.

1. **`genius.md`** — always. The 15 operating principles, the architect-who-digs paradox, the seven-criterion rubric, the Decision Framework, and the Anti-Patterns. The grounded quotes ("fear denies a good father from being one," the cable car, "fit the dinosaur") all live here. This is the cognitive signature; load it before anything else.

The patterns give the move in full; the hidden-knowledge notes carry the non-obvious mechanics (the premise-sentence is a *litmus*, never an outline; patience beats the forced premise; fit-the-dinosaur needs structural permission to survive). Each workflow's Pre-Flight names the specific patterns and HK entries it leans on — load only those.
3. **The specific `/stanton-*` workflow** — the numbered steps, the fill-in tables, the Output Format, the Quality Gate. The workflow assumes the voice and the patterns are already in context.

> If a workflow is hot in this conversation (loaded earlier), skip back up to genius.md only for a refresher quote. Do not re-read the whole stack.

## The Story Stack — Order Is Load-Bearing

Four experts touch narrative. They run in one direction. Inverting the order means crafting lines for a story that has no premise, or breaking a season's theme before anyone confirmed there was a story to break. Run them in sequence; a deliverable can enter at any rung, but never above its own.

| Order | Expert | Owns | The question it answers | Hands off when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — gate** | **Tim Runia / `/story-compass`** | story-vs-topic | Is there a story here at all? (want → tension → change) | A want, a tension, and a change exist. If not, it's a topic — stop, or reframe before any craft. |
| **2 — architecture** | **Noah Hawley** (`/hawley-theme-engine`, `/hawley-content-season`) | arc + theme across the whole work | Why does this exist, and what question does it answer across all its parts? | The theme-question and the ending are locked; the arc is broken into angles. |
| **3 — uncover + engage** | **Andrew Stanton** (`/stanton-*`) | the premise-sentence + the clamp | What is this *really* about, in one true line — and does every beat pull the audience forward? | The premise-sentence rings true, the spine governs the protagonist, and the clamp holds beat to beat. |
| **4 — craft** | **Eric Roth** (`/roth-*`) | the scene and the line | Does each scene and sentence carry subtext, character, and visual prose? | The page is finished. |

**Why this order, and never inverted:**
- Runia runs **first** because everything downstream is wasted if there's no want, no tension, no change. Stanton *assumes* the story exists — he supplies the dig and the clamp, not the gate. Skip Runia and you can polish a topic for hours and never feel the floor give way.
- Hawley runs **above** Stanton on anything multi-part (a season, a series, a campaign, a multi-edition essay arc). Hawley decides why the whole thing exists and locks the ending; Stanton then tunes the engagement *inside* the arc Hawley set. Reverse them and you clamp beats that serve no theme.
- Stanton sits **between** architecture and craft because the premise-sentence is the bridge: one altitude up (what is this about) and one altitude down (where did attention drop) at the same time. He hands Roth a story that is uncovered, spined, and clamped — Roth makes the lines worthy of it.
- Roth runs **last** because line-craft on an unstructured story is decoration. Surprising-and-inevitable lives at Stanton's altitude; a beautiful sentence in a slack beat is still a slack beat.

> Single-author voice rule (project standard): one coherent author writes the body. The stack sets *structure and engagement*; it does not stitch four experts' prose into one piece. Stanton audits and uncovers; he does not co-write the sentence with Roth in the same paragraph.

## Stacking Recipes — The Four Tier-3 Deployment Workflows

Tier-3 workflows are deployment surfaces. Each one runs the Stanton spine (premise-sentence → spine → clamp → change → close), then hands the finished arc to the engine that executes the surface's conventions. Stanton sets the arc; the execution expert lands the sentences and the format.

### `/stanton-sales-arc` → copy-engine / Stefan Georgi / Jason Fladlien
A sales page or VSL is an engagement arc with a customer as protagonist.

1. **`/stanton-premise-sentence`** — write the offer's true one-liner: *customer + conflict + transformation.* Not "the product does X" — the felt conflict the customer is denied a resolution to.
2. **`/stanton-spine`** — give the *customer* a Weston spine and a one-liner (their want vs. their need). The page is their movie, not the brand's.
3. **`/stanton-clamp-audit`** — walk every section for the beach-ball drop; manufacture anticipation mingled with uncertainty so no reader unclamps between the lead and the close.
4. **`/stanton-change-engine`** + **`/stanton-surprising-inevitable`** — stage the fundamental change and land a close that was inevitable once you arrive yet not telegraphed at the top.
5. **Hand to `/copy-engine` (Ground Once, Refine Free), `/stefan-georgi`, `/jason-fladlien`, `/luke-iha-*`** — they write the headline, the bullets, the proof ladder, the offer mechanics on top of the locked arc. Stanton does not write the copy; he hands them a clamped structure to write into.

### `/stanton-brand-origin` → build-bos / Donald Miller StoryBrand
A brand or founder origin is a character with a spine, an opposites-driven conflict, and a real change.

1. **`/stanton-spine`** — give the brand or founder a fixed inner wiring + one-liner (the central complication they solve in everything).
2. **`/stanton-opposites-engine`** — set the conflict from opposing agendas (box vs. circle); opposition is the drama engine, not a tagline.
3. **`/stanton-change-engine`** — name the change the brand exists to create in the world.
4. **`/stanton-invoke-wonder`** — place one wonder beat (the involuntary "oh") so the origin earns feeling, not just facts.
5. **Hand to `/build-bos` and `/donald-miller-storybrand`** — Miller frames the *customer* as hero in the brand template; `/build-bos` codifies voice, positioning, and the brand operating system. Stanton supplies the living spine and the forward pull that Miller's frame holds. (`/oren-brand` for luxury/repositioning surfaces.) Real-world deploys: MyBPM, Andrea/Resonance, Jen Santulan.

### `/stanton-30sec-arc` → Seena Rez / Dara Denney / Shaan Puri
An ad or Reel is a full engagement arc compressed inside the pre-skip window.

1. **`/stanton-change-engine`** — pick the single five-second moment of change (Stanton cites Puri's "story is a five-second moment of change" by name; this is the seam between them).
2. **`/stanton-anticipation-uncertainty`** — clamp line one *before* the skip point; the first frame must already be uncertainty in motion.
3. **`/stanton-opposites-engine`** — one opposite for instant drama; there's no room for a slow build.
4. **`/stanton-clamp-audit`** — cut every frame that doesn't serve the clamp; the beach ball cannot drop once in thirty seconds.
5. **Hand to `/seena-rez` (short-form), `/dara-denney` (paid ads), `/shaan-puri`** — they own the hook conventions, the platform mechanics, the viral compression of that single change into a postable unit. Stanton hands them an arc that already earns the watch.

### `/stanton-series-escalation` → Hawley content-season / parallax / linkedin-daily
A content series is an arc across units, each raising the engagement and the change toward a finale.

1. **`/hawley-content-season` runs first** — Hawley breaks the *season-level* theme as angles and locks the finale (architecture above Stanton, per the stack).
2. **`/stanton-series-escalation`** — write the series-level premise-sentence, give each unit its own five-second change, and escalate the clamp so every edition ends pulling into the next.
3. **`/stanton-surprising-inevitable`** — design the finale change so the series resolves surprising-yet-inevitable, not merely stops.
4. **Hand to `/parallax` and `/linkedin-daily`** — Parallax runs the edition pipeline (research → briefing → draft → prompt pack → cross-promo); `/linkedin-daily` runs the zeitgeist-gated daily production. Stanton supplies the cross-edition pull; the engines ship each unit in its native voice.

Grouped by tier. One-line trigger each. Foundation builds the structure; Practitioner diagnoses and rescues existing work; Cross-domain deploys to a surface.

| Command | Trigger — reach for it when… |
|---|---|
| `/stanton-premise-sentence` | …it's unclear what a piece is *really* about, or a draft feels unfocused — distill to one true line (character + conflict + conclusion) and audit against it. |
| `/stanton-spine` | …a character, brand, founder, or customer feels generic or inconsistent — give them a fixed Weston wiring + one-liner. |
| `/stanton-anticipation-uncertainty` | …you're building a piece from scratch and need every beat to pull forward — engineer the cable-car clamp. |
| `/stanton-surprising-inevitable` | …the ending merely stops, or feels arbitrary or telegraphed — make it surprising in the moment, inevitable in retrospect. |
| `/stanton-change-engine` | …a piece has no center of gravity — name the single fundamental moment of change the whole thing leads to. |

### Tier 2 — Practitioner (diagnose, rescue, sharpen existing work)

## How `/stanton-*` Slots Into the Engines

Stanton is an enrichment layer, not a replacement engine. He sharpens what the user's existing engines already produce — he sets structure and engagement, they execute their surface. Never rebuild an engine; insert Stanton at the named seam.

| Engine | Where Stanton inserts | The seam |
|---|---|---|
| **`/copy-engine`** | Before the copy is written. `/stanton-sales-arc` sets the customer spine + clamp + change; copy-engine grounds once and refines the headline, bullets, and offer on top of the locked arc. Stanton structures; copy-engine writes. |
| **`/parallax`** | Inside the edition pipeline, after research, before drafting. `/stanton-premise-sentence` sharpens what the edition is *really* about (vs. topic-first drafting); `/stanton-series-escalation` pulls each edition into the next. Hawley's season theme sits above. |
| **`/build-bos`** | At the brand-story layer. `/stanton-brand-origin` gives the brand a living spine + one-liner and an opposites conflict; build-bos codifies it into the operating system. Miller frames customer-as-hero; Stanton makes the hero alive. |
| **`/linkedin-daily`** | Per-post, after the zeitgeist gate. `/stanton-clamp-audit` lifts a flat post's retention; `/stanton-change-engine` finds the five-second change a post is built around. Runs inside the daily engine, not instead of it. |
| **`/deepen` + `/depth-*`** | As the engagement-deficit slot. When `/deepen` diagnoses a "flat attention" or "no forward pull" deficit, it composes `/stanton-clamp-audit` and `/stanton-anticipation-uncertainty` into the depth roster (alongside Hawley → Roth/Cole → Lamott-Allen → voice). Stanton is the clamp slot; he doesn't reimplement the depth conductor. |
| **`/convene`** | As a council voice on story, engagement, or rescue questions. Stanton deliberates with the roster (cross-talk, genius-loaded); his distinctive contribution is the dig, the clamp-audit, and "fit the dinosaur" late-pivot courage that no other seat carries. |
| **`/writers-room`** | As a structural-pass voice, not a body co-author. The room catches AI structural tells; Stanton's seat audits the premise-sentence and the clamp on the existing draft. He diagnoses why the reel isn't holding — he does not stitch his prose into the single author's body copy. |

> Routing note: Runia (`/story-compass`) runs *first* on any story-vs-topic ambiguity, Hawley runs *above* on multi-part arcs, Roth runs *after* for line-craft. When in doubt about ordering, read the Story Stack table above — the direction never reverses.
