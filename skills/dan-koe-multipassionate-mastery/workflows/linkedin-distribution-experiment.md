# LinkedIn Distribution Experiment

## Pre-flight and skill acquisition
Load `../references/linkedin-source-method.md` and the completed post package. Inputs: goal, audience, author capacity, approved assets, baseline if available. Before recommendations, check current LinkedIn primary guidance. Posts and replies remain drafts until authorized for sending. No account scraping, automated comments or paid tools by default.

## Execute
1. Define the outcome: qualified follower, substantive reply, relevant profile visit, qualified conversation or revenue. Keep these separate. No baseline means UNTESTED, not zero performance.
2. Preserve Dan's source proposal as an option: roughly ten quality replies daily within thirty minutes and credited commentary two or three times weekly. Fit the operator's actual capacity; do not turn the counts into engagement chores.
3. Find relevant conversations from user-provided links/captures or permitted read-only public research. For each candidate, draft an adjacent insight, practical step or truthful personal connection. Omit generic praise and responses that only restate the parent.
4. If using credited commentary, explain the unique addition, retain attribution and choose an owned or cleared visual. Relevant credit does not guarantee a creator response; do not build forecasts around a repost.
5. Plan a small matched comparison: comparable topic/audience/proof, same measurement age, image+caption versus text-only or a current baseline. Label organic comparisons observational; topic, timing and exposure remain confounders.
6. Precommit an operator-chosen time budget and decision rule. Suggested first pilot: six posts in three matched pairs over two weeks, measured at 72 hours; continue the format only if it wins the chosen meaningful metric in at least two pairs without raising production time beyond the cap. This is our practical pilot rule, not statistical significance or Dan's prescription.
7. Log impressions, substantive replies, relevant follows, qualified conversations, production minutes and actual attributable revenue when available. Unknown stays blank, never becomes zero. A zero event may be recorded only after measurement.
8. On review, decide continue, alter or park. Name the weak link and next test. A single viral post cannot establish a reusable advantage. Update the guide only after repeated evidence or explicit taste feedback.

## Adaptations
| Goal | Primary metric |
