1. No references: use the source demo for a labeled practice run; never invent a swipe file or baseline. References present: separate observed structure from hypotheses about why it performed.
2. No seed: retrieve existing ideas/notes first, then ask for missing personal material. Seed present: skip the interview and draft distinct angles.
3. No outcomes: return a test plan. Outcomes present: compare at the same age, distinguish attention from qualified actions, and update only a supported weak link.

## Boundaries that improve the source

These are our adaptations: comparable ordinary-post controls, source rights check, no fake metrics/badges, qualified-action measurement and time cost. Do not attribute them to Dan.

LinkedIn says AI assistance can be used while preserving the author's viewpoint; generic content may receive less distribution ([June 2026](https://news.linkedin.com/2026/keeping-conversations-real-on-linkedin)). It prohibits engagement pods and automated comments ([March 2026](https://news.linkedin.com/2026/authentic-content-and-conversations)). Check current primary guidance again when deploying later. ## Failure and recovery

Copied voice -> reload author voice card and actual raw phrasing. Repeated image/caption -> supply an application or consequence. Celebrity-only sample -> mark transfer uncertainty and collect comparable references. Oversized guide -> retain a short decision guide plus cold source evidence. Poor performance -> inspect audience, topic and exposure before blaming wording. No paid tool is required by this method.
