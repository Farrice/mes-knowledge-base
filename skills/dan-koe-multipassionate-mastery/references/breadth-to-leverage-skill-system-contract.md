# Breadth-to-Leverage Skill System Contract

## Capability delta

The existing Dan Koe owner begins with “you are the niche” and proceeds into personal monopoly, content, product, and community design. The source-backed gap is a **pre-prescription diagnostic**: several operators can have many interests for different reasons, and each reason requires a different focus mechanism.

This subsystem adds one decision before expansion: identify the likely behavior mechanism, choose one execution arena, and define the proof event that must occur before more breadth is activated.

## Source authority

- Primary: `extractions/video-context/UnXm_R1APxo/` — six multi-interest archetypes and mechanism-specific prescriptions.
- Supporting: `extractions/video-context/YNJ18ggqwqM/` — five-step market-interest sequence used only after focus is locked.
- Existing owner: `skills/dan-koe-multipassionate-mastery/` — personal monopoly and multi-interest monetization.
- Existing downstream supports: Dan Koe Identity Polarization Engine and Joanna Wiebe persuasion workflows.

## Intake contract

Required:

- Active interests and current projects.
- Last 90 days of starts, switches, completions, and abandoned opportunities.
- Current buyer/problem arena or explicit absence of one.
- Visible outputs and external evidence separated by proof state.
- Constraints, anti-goals, and permission boundary for any market action.

Missing evidence produces `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`, not a flattering archetype.

## Deliverable contract

The system produces a **Breadth-to-Leverage Decision Brief** containing:

1. Evidence inventory.
2. Primary, secondary, and rejected archetype hypotheses.
3. Mechanism-specific prescription.
4. One destination arena and one hub skill.
5. Interest roles: lead, supporting R&D, maintenance, parked.
6. One visible-output commitment.
7. Honest proof ladder and 30-day behavior experiment.
8. Optional handoff into strategic interestingness only after the focus lock passes.

## Routing contract

- Use `/breadth-to-leverage` when too many interests, repeated switching, identity labels, or framework accumulation precede an execution decision.
- Route to Personal Monopoly Foundation only after the focus decision exists.
- Route to Identity Polarization or Joanna persuasion only after arena, buyer, problem, and proof artifact exist.
- Do not route traditional single-vertical niche strategy here.

## State model

`EVIDENCE INCOMPLETE` → `WORKING HYPOTHESIS` → `FOCUS LOCKED` → `OUTPUT SHIPPED` → `EXTERNAL SIGNAL` → `COMMERCIAL PROOF`

No state may be inferred from a later-looking artifact. A polished framework remains `WORKING HYPOTHESIS` until it changes behavior.

## Permission boundaries

- Diagnosis, local drafting, and proof-packet preparation are allowed.
- Outreach, publishing, connector writes, spending, and live business changes require explicit permission.
- “Polymath” is withheld unless at least two domains have inspectable contribution evidence.

## Proof contract

Structural PASS requires source packages, workflow, born-v2 prompt, cold-start receipt, negative control, and natural-language routing. Behavior proof requires a real run that narrows scope. Commercial proof requires response, sale, or collected payment and remains separate from artifact quality.

## No-regression clause

This subsystem may not delete or weaken the skill’s core multi-interest premise. It adds diagnostic precision before expansion; it does not turn the skill into conventional “pick one niche forever” advice.
