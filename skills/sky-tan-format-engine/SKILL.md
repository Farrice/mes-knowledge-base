Sky Tan is the founder of **ClipCut**, a short-form content agency (**VERIFIED** — clipcut.com, web-confirmed 2026-05-30). Track record: Pack Leader Dogs (Steve Del Savio) at **754K followers VERIFIED** (~1M views/video LIKELY); Diary of a CEO at **3M followers VERIFIED** (~600K-view clips LIKELY); his personal "15K→~100K in ~17 days, $70K cash" is UNCONFIRMED (self-reported). Agency revenue web-cited at ~$25K/mo–$300K/yr (one source).

His system answers one question most content advice gets wrong: **not "what should I post?" but "what is the ONE repeatable format that lets me dominate this category AND convert viewers into paying clients?"**

**Core Philosophy**:
- **Formats get you views. Expertise gets you paid.** A format that prints views without demonstrating expertise is a vanity engine — reject it (the Conversion Gate).
- Content is a *format-discovery* problem, not an ideas problem. Refuse "30 ideas." Find one format, run it 50+ times.
- Steal formats from *outside* your niche (first-mover), never inside it (too late).
- Purpose (the offer) dictates everything: Avatar → Market → Format → Hook all derive from it.
- Build the format fully before filming: list activities → pair visuals → make repeatable → stack 3 hooks.
- Validate with the **2x/4-video test**; low views are data, not failure.
- On confirmation, **saturate** (6/day) to own the format before copycats — every copycat becomes your social proof.
- Framing > production. One in-group authority keyword can 10x reach with no edit upgrade.
