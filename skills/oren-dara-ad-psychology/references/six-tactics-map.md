# The Six Tactics — One-Page Map

| # | Tactic | Psychological mechanism | Frame-verified exemplar | Failure mode (named in source) | Best for | Workflow | Vessel handoff (dara-denney-meta-ads) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lean Into the Taboo | Mirroring + safe space — customer's innermost secret thoughts, said for them | Grüns: organic "waist-slimming diarrhea episode" → yapper "For the girls and madams who need to poop… Scouts honor" | Shock-for-shock; male-gaze review pick (URO "We are having sex all over the house") | Health, body, money, politics-adjacent categories with unsaid truths | 02 | Yapper (`/dara-yapper-script`), TikTok Short |
| 2 | Selfish Desires / Justification Math | Purchase justification — "check a box: I can justify this purchase" | Bed sheets "$1 per week" + "We're not cheap because we don't want to be"; Patek anchor; girl math "$6 a brush" | Over-catering to the price objection (kills the status filter) | AOV ≥ $100, price = #1 objection, luxury/masstige | 03 | Objection engine (`/dara-objection-engine`), Headliner static |
| 3 | Armchair Investigation | Unfairness agitation + curiosity + virtue activation; comments carry the ad | AI mechanic: "same car, same problem, three different mechanics… $480 vs $1,800" | Mistaking format for mechanism — it works because comments get infuriated | Rip-off-energy categories; drama/true-crime-overlap audiences | 04 | Test plan (`/dara-test-plan`), AI production notes |
| 4 | Prove It | Claim → demonstration in-frame ≤60s | Shot on iPhone anchor; Laura Geller Arizona-chef ad; 400-tomatoes knife | Stating the claim instead of staging it | Any demonstrable value prop (hold, durability, speed, capacity) | 05 | Format swap, hooks (`/dara-winning-hooks`) |
| 5 | Us-vs-Them + Visual Psychology | Comparison leveling + eye mechanics: multiples stop scroll; eye trails Z/F on grids | Kettle & Fire vs Nutra Organics: "dirty dishwater," daughter blind-taste, stacked boxes = space savings | All messaging, no visual layer; making claims (stay opinion-based) | Solution-aware buyers comparing options; multi-SKU | 07 (+04) | Comparison callout (`/dara-comparison-callout`), Grid static |
| 6 | Credible Explainer | Authority + scarcity of expert access — "people just want to be told what to do" | Bag-brand artisan, grizzled hands, "why this stitch is better"; founder-warehouse | Fake authority ("do not put on a white coat"); expert staged in a car/UGC | Trust-gap categories: medical-adjacent, technical, craft | 06 | Founder ad (`/dara-founder-ad`), authority statics |

**Cross-cutting loops**: organic→paid flywheel (09) feeds ideation in; comment virtue-mining (08) feeds iteration out; the 6-part process (10) is the cadence that runs both.

**Tactic selection heuristics** (from source):
- Unsaid customer truths in reviews/group-chat register → 1
- Price objection dominates → 2
- Category has rip-off/unfairness energy → 3
- Claim is physically demonstrable → 4
- Buyer is comparing alternatives / value prop is spatial-visual → 5
- Category needs telling-what-to-do trust → 6
- Multiple match: pick the one whose *comment section* you can predict most vividly (comments are the multiplier in 1, 3, 6).
