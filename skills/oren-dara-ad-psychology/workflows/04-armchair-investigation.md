# `/adpsy-armchair-investigation` — The Armchair Investigation

> The ad: "I took the same car, same problem, three different mechanics, and I let them tell me what was wrong. And I filmed it because I knew… First shop… O2 sensor, quoted me $480… Second shop… $1,800 for the same car."

Dara's team's top performer — 100% AI, TikTok-native, and honest about why it works: *"It works really well because people are getting infuriated in the comments."* The format is an investigation; the mechanism is **unfairness agitation + designed comment fury + virtue activation**.

## Pre-Flight Gate

- Does the category carry genuine rip-off/unfairness energy the audience already feels? Manufactured outrage about a non-issue fails and burns trust.
- Legal line: the scenario must be experience/opinion-based — dramatize what happens, don't fabricate claims about named competitors (the Kettle & Fire rule: "we're not technically making any claims… it was opinion-based").

## Skill Acquisition

Read `genius.md` (Tactic 3, Rage-bait is load-bearing, Demographic Puncture, AI disclosure calculus) + `references/source-quotes.md` Tactic 3 block.

## Input Required

- **[BRAND + PRODUCT]** and the unfairness it resolves
- **[CORE DEMO]** + the adjacent segment you want delivery in (for casting)
- **[AUDIENCE DRAMA DIET]**: what this demo watched 10 years ago (true crime, soap opera, Maury, court TV) — Oren's Study-Their-Old-TV move
- **[REAL PAIN EVIDENCE]**: comments/reviews where people describe getting ripped off

## Execution

1. **Pick the injustice.** From real pain evidence, choose the unfairness scenario (rip-off, nepotism, information asymmetry). Quote the customer language that proves it's felt.
2. **Structure it as an investigation.** Cold open with the experiment premise ("same X, same problem, three different Y — and I filmed it because I knew") → escalating findings with concrete numbers → the reveal. Borrow the pacing grammar of the demo's drama diet.
3. **Cast for puncture.** Persona spec: default = the adjacent segment's face ("a younger woman who looked like the daughters of our core demographic"). If AI: demographic-tell check ("we knew for the demographic they would not be able to tell"), and craft gate — native fonts, native formats, "well done AI." Sloppy AI reads as slop; care scales.
4. **Design the comment fight.** Write the 3 predicted comment factions (e.g., "the first guy was also lying to you"), and one deliberate imperfection/ambiguity that gives commenters something to correct or argue. This is the engagement anchor — design it, don't hope for it.
5. **Plan the virtue-mine loop.** Schedule the `/adpsy-comment-mine` pass at day 7-14: what virtue is the audience revealing (Dara's team found *honesty* — Bible verses in the comments), and pre-commit to shipping virtue-led iterations within days of the read.
6. **Vessel + test handoff**: `/dara-test-plan` for the matrix; production notes for AI pipeline.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Services (repair, finance, legal) | Multi-quote experiment structure translates directly |
| CPG | Investigation = ingredient/label comparison, opinion-framed |
| B2B | "I asked 3 vendors for the same scope" — pricing-opacity agitation |
| No AI budget | Yapper-told scenario ("I was on a date with X and Y…") — pre-AI form Oren names |

## Output Requirements

Investigation Package: injustice + evidence quotes · beat-by-beat scenario script with concrete numbers · casting spec (human/AI, puncture target, craft gate) · comment-engagement design (factions + planted ambiguity) · virtue-mine schedule · legal framing note · test handoff.

Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/04-armchair-investigation.md`

## Quality Gate

Rubric: mechanism + predicted comment behavior must both be explicit (≥7 comment design); numbers concrete; legal framing opinion-based. Automatic fail: fabricated claims about named competitors, outrage without evidence the audience feels it, AI casting that fails the demographic-tell or craft check.
