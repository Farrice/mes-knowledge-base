# `/adpsy-taboo-ad` — Lean Into the Taboo

> Dara: "really think about your customers' innermost secret thoughts and how certain creators can actually create more of that community in a safe space for them to actually say the thing that they don't really feel like they should say."

Not shock value. The Grüns play: an organic creator says the unsayable ("How life feels after having the most waist-slimming diarrhea episode"), the brand harvests it into a paid yapper ("For the girls and madams who need to poop… Scouts honor"), and the comments become the community — "you're saying the thing that I wish I could say."

## Pre-Flight Gate

- Does this category actually carry an **unsaid customer truth** (health, body, money, politics-adjacent, spirituality)? If the "taboo" is only edgy to the brand, stop — that's shock-for-shock.
- Is there real customer voice to mine? The taboo must be found, not invented.

## Skill Acquisition

Read `genius.md` (Tactic 1 + vetoes) and `references/source-quotes.md` (Tactic 1 block — the Grüns pair and the URO anti-exemplars are the calibration set). If a yapper vessel is likely, pre-read `skills/dara-denney-meta-ads/workflows/03-yapper-script.md` for the handoff.

## Input Required

- **[BRAND + PRODUCT]** and category
- **[PERSONA]** incl. gender/age register — the veto depends on it
- **[CUSTOMER-VOICE MATERIAL]**: reviews, comments, group-chat-register organic content the audience shares
- **[ORGANIC INSPIRATION]** (optional): a specific organic post/creator that already says the unsayable in this niche

## Execution

1. **Find the taboo, don't pick it.** From the material, list candidate unsaid truths in the customer's exact register. Map each to Oren's category table: politics / spirituality / health / money-honesty ("I blew my money on this purchase").
2. **Run the lived-experience veto on every candidate** — Dara's test, verbatim: *"I actually don't think a woman who has this problem would ever think to say that. I could see a young male creative strategist looking through the reviews and being like, that's the one."* For each candidate: would the actual customer say this out loud, in their world, to their friend? Kill everything that reads like the strategist's golden-nugget review (URO: "We are having sex all over the house" / "My vagina feels like silk"). If the persona's lived experience differs from yours, say so and flag for a human check from someone with it (cultural-connotation rule).
3. **Frame the safe space.** Write the concept so the creator says it *for* the customer — friend-talking-to-friend, "super relatable… not a whole scripted bit." Vulnerability must be real; if it feels forced, that's the "mask of authenticity" cringe — cut it.
4. **Design the comment payoff.** Predict and write the comment thread you're engineering: the "oh my god, you're saying the thing I wish I could say" replies. Add one explicit comment prompt in the script.
5. **Check the deeper level.** Oren's counter-positive: the yoga-pants ad wasn't about sex — "it's about attention… the dynamic." Name what the taboo is *really* about one level down, and make sure the concept lands there.
6. **Hand off the vessel.** Default: yapper (`/dara-yapper-script`) or TikTok Short. Pass the chosen line, register notes, and comment design.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Intimate/body categories | Veto is decisive; prefer creator-said over brand-said |
| Money/finance | Honesty-immediacy framing: "I have zero dollars in my bank account, but I'm about to do X" |
| Politics-adjacent | Values-mirroring without party language ("back to the earth" register) |
| Static vessel | Taboo line becomes the headliner; route `/dara-static-copy` mechanic #3 |

## Output Requirements

Taboo Ad Concept: the found taboo (with source quote) · veto verdict log (candidates killed and why) · safe-space framing + creator direction · script beats or headline · comment-design block (predicted thread + prompt) · vessel handoff note.

Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/02-taboo-ad-concept.md`

## Quality Gate

Rubric: voice authenticity must score ≥7 (survives the veto cold); inner-thought rooting ≥7; comment design present. Automatic fail: any line the actual customer wouldn't say (URO test), forced vulnerability, or a taboo that serves shock rather than recognition.
