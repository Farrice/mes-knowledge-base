# `/adpsy-organic-flywheel` — The Organic→Paid Flywheel

> Dara: "we don't look at our competitor ads anymore. We only look at what our audience loves on organic and try to replicate more of those formats moving forward… almost a learning flywheel."

Competitor ad libraries are lagging indicators — by the time a format is visible there, the arbitrage is closing. The audience's organic behavior is the leading indicator: the Grüns yapper came from an organic creator's post; the armchair investigation came from organic inspiration + an accidental true-crime overlap that became deliberate.

## Pre-Flight Gate

- Is ideation currently competitor-library-first? That's the pattern this replaces. (Libraries stay useful for *vessel* reference — the ban is on using them as the idea source.)
- Access check: can you actually see what the audience saves/shares (brand organic, creator accounts they follow, community spaces)?

## Skill Acquisition

Read `genius.md` (Pattern 6, Tactic 3 origin story) + `references/source-quotes.md`. Cross-load `skills/kallaway/SKILL.md` or `tess-barclay` when trend-carrier depth is needed.

## Input Required

- **[BRAND + PERSONA]**
- **[ORGANIC SURFACES]**: the brand's organic accounts, creators the audience follows, community spaces (subreddits, groups), comment sections
- **[AUDIENCE MEDIA DIET]**: incl. Oren's question — what did this demo watch on TV 10 years ago?
- **[PAID LEARNINGS TO DATE]** (optional): winners/losers so far

## Execution

1. **Map the organic sources.** List 5-10 places this audience's genuine enthusiasm is visible. Rank by signal quality (their OWN engagement, not follower counts).
2. **Harvest loved formats.** From those surfaces: which posts does the audience save/share/comment-flood? Capture format grammar (structure, register, pacing), not topics. Include old-media formats from the diet question (true crime, court TV, soap opera, Maury-style confrontation).
3. **Cross-reference the overlap.** Look for audience-interest overlaps a soulless media plan would miss (core demo × true crime). Name 1-2.
4. **Build the replication shortlist.** 3-5 organic formats → paid replication briefs: source example, format grammar, which of the six tactics it naturally carries, vessel recommendation.
5. **Close the loop.** Cadence spec: weekly organic scan → monthly format review → every winner's comments mined (`/adpsy-comment-mine`) → learnings logged → next scan. That's the flywheel — each turn compounds.
6. **Handoff**: shortlist briefs → `/adpsy-tactic-select` seeds or straight to `dara-denney-meta-ads` vessel workflows.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| No brand organic presence | Mine creators the audience follows + community spaces; the flywheel can start before the brand posts |
| Client engagement | The source map + cadence is a sellable research system (production-sheet format) |
| B2B | LinkedIn-native formats + industry community threads replace TikTok surfaces |
| Personal brand | Same loop; saves/DM-shares are the strongest signal |

## Output Requirements

Flywheel Package: ranked organic source map · loved-format harvest (grammar-level) · overlap discoveries · 3-5 replication briefs (source, grammar, tactic, vessel) · loop cadence spec.

## Quality Gate

Rubric: inner-thought rooting ≥7 (formats harvested from real audience behavior, cited); overlap section non-empty or honestly marked "none found." Automatic fail: competitor ads as the idea source, invented "trends," follower-count-ranked sources.
