# `/adpsy-credible-explainer` — The Credible Explainer

> Oren: "with so much noise and so much advice, people just want to be told what to do. And people will never admit it… They just want someone credible to just tell them the answer."

The scarcity driver: nobody gets enough time with real experts — so the brand whose credible person explains and *answers comments* becomes the anchor. Credibility is scavenged, not hired: "Do you have a board? Do you have investors? People inside really technical roles with any bit of charisma? Inside your warehouse or factory?"

## Pre-Flight Gate

- Is there a trust/authority gap in this purchase (medical-adjacent, technical, craft, high-consideration)? Low-consideration impulse buys don't need this.
- **No fake authority.** Dara: "A real doctor, ideally, yes. Do not put on a white coat." If no real credibility exists anywhere in the chain, do not manufacture it — this workflow ends with "not viable."

## Skill Acquisition

Read `genius.md` (Tactic 6, Set-as-Credibility, Mine-The-Chain) + `references/source-quotes.md` Tactic 6 block. Calibration: the bag-brand artisan — imperfect English, grizzled hands, "Why this stitch is better than the other?" — credibility carried by craft and set, not polish.

## Input Required

- **[BRAND + PRODUCT]** and the trust gap (what the buyer is afraid of getting wrong)
- **[THE CHAIN]**: founder, board, investors, advisors, technical staff, factory/warehouse people, credentialed partners — everyone within reach
- **[EXISTING ORGANIC PRESENCE]**: does any credible person already post?
- **[BUYER QUESTIONS]**: the questions customers actually ask (comments, support tickets, sales calls)

## Execution

1. **Mine the chain.** List every real-credibility candidate with: credential/craft, charisma signal, availability. Rank. Remember the surprise picks — the artisan beat the executive.
2. **Stage the set.** Match person → environment that proves them: lab (fragrance testers everywhere), warehouse, workshop, office. Never a car, never generic UGC framing — "if that person's talking in a car… that's not there."
3. **Write the beats.** Credential open ("I've been doing this for 14 years. I work with brands like X and Y") → straight into the buyer's actual problem → tell them what to do, plainly. The register is expert-answering-a-question, not presenter-performing.
4. **Design the access loop.** The scarcity payoff: this person answers comments. Specify the cadence — "I can comment on this, I might be able to get a response, and that's going to be the anchor of a brand."
5. **Sequence organic → paid.** Establish the person on organic first; when they enter the ads, "people in your ASC likely already have seen their content organically — those begin to be really high performers." Write the 2-4 week pre-seed plan.
6. **Layer the authority ladder** (Dara): founder slides in first; then the "outside verified check mark" voices. Note which rung this ad occupies.
7. **Vessel handoff**: founder rung → `/dara-founder-ad` (6 plays, 7-beat script); expert statics → `/dara-static-engine`.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Medical-adjacent | Real credentials only; scrubs only on actual clinicians; compliance check |
| Craft/manufacturing | Hands + process close-ups; imperfect speech is a feature, not a bug |
| B2B | Technical staff explaining the unglamorous detail buyers actually fear |
| Local/service | The proprietor in the actual shop; proximity is the credential |

## Output Requirements

Authority Plan: ranked chain-mining table · set/staging spec per candidate · script beats · comment-access loop cadence · organic pre-seed sequence · authority-ladder rung note · vessel handoff.

Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/06-credible-explainer-plan.md`

## Quality Gate

Rubric: credibility staging ≥7 (real authority + real set); if organic pre-seeding is skipped, say why. Automatic fail: manufactured credentials, credible person mis-staged (car/UGC), polish that erases the craft texture, or presenting the doctor-visit statistics as verified fact (they're speaker-attributed).
