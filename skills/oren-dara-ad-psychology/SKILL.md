# Oren John × Dara Denney — Ad Psychology

> "They're all rooted in something that a consumer wants to do, how they think, some of the psychology of it, and some of the things that actually work in performance and organic." — Oren

The layer UNDER format selection. An ad converts cold traffic when it is the **safe** (taboo/mirroring), **justified** (selfish desires), **credible** (authority), or **proven** (demonstration) container for something the customer already thinks, wants, or says privately. Six tactics, each with a real scaled ad and a named failure mode — plus the operating loop that feeds them (organic mining in, comment mining out).

**Position in the stack**: run this skill to choose the psychological mechanism and concept → hand the concept to [`dara-denney-meta-ads`](../dara-denney-meta-ads/SKILL.md) for vessel, hooks, statics, test plan, and production. Fidelity note: single-source extraction (6.9k words); everything is quote-grounded in [`references/source-quotes.md`](references/source-quotes.md) — never invent past it.
