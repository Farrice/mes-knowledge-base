## Evidence Classes

- **SOURCE-VERIFIED:** the idea or demonstration appears in the timestamped source. This does not independently verify every external fact stated by a speaker.
- **CORPUS-GROUNDED:** the capability already existed in the local Rory corpus before this source arrived.
- **OPERATIONAL SYNTHESIS:** Antigravity converted source mechanics into a repeatable workflow, command name, rubric, or output contract. The name is not presented as Rory’s published terminology.
- **UNAVAILABLE:** the full text of *Alchemy*. Farrice owns the Kindle edition, but it was not available as a local readable source.

## Claim Corrections

1. S8 verifies the Guidara, Buc-ee’s, Moxy, and Uber examples used in the reverse-benchmarking layer. It does not blanket-promote unrelated examples from older files.
2. S8 grounds Thaler-style portfolio risk pooling and Bezos-style fat-tail upside. It does **not** establish that Rory teaches Bezos’s “two-way door” framework. v5.0 therefore centers the asymmetric-bet workflow on portfolio incentives and treats reversibility as an Antigravity test aid.
3. S8 grounds horsepower as buyer-native translation. The workflow name `Adoption Unit` is operational synthesis.
4. Progress cues must be truthful, estimated, or explicitly figurative. The source’s discussion of placebo controls does not authorize fabricated operational status.
5. Psychology cannot overrule a real product defect, safety concern, legal rule, or factual error.
