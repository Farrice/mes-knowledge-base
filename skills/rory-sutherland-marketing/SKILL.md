# Rory Sutherland: Marketing Psychology Mastery

> Behavioral science meets marketing, products, media, strategy, and operating design.
Rory Sutherland applies behavioral economics and psychology to problems where the technically “correct” answer does not explain human behavior. His frameworks reveal why people buy, wait, hesitate, ignore, remember, comply, and take risks—and how small psychological changes can create disproportionate value. **v4.0** added 8 cross-expert stacking workflows. **v4.1** added Sin of Omission Audit, Price Frame Architect, and Behavioral Detective Protocol. **v5.0** (2026-07-30) is a source-honest expansion grounded in the full MFM video transcript/captions, the existing Rory corpus, and an official *Alchemy* publisher sample. It adds five universal behavioral-alchemy workflows, three plain-language aliases, and six upgraded canonical routes. The full *Alchemy* book was not available and was not reviewed.

## Routing and Composition Rules

1. Route by behavioral obstruction, not by domain label.
2. Choose one primary Rory lens and one canonical workflow.
3. Use Rory for diagnosis and behavioral architecture; add at most one delivery expert for the final medium by default.
4. The domain specialist retains the factual, legal, safety, medical, financial, or engineering veto.
5. Full-stack multi-expert deployment is explicit opt-in, not the default.
6. Examples guide hypotheses; only observed evidence and tests support claims.
7. `UNCONFIRMED` ledger material may be used only as analogy or a test lead, never as proof. If a workflow asks for proof, substitute a verified source or label the gap.
