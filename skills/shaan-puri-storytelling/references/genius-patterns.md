## System governance

- Preserve facts and uncertainty.
- Never invent dialogue, chronology, metrics, outcomes, motives, emotions, or sensory details as real.
- Label analogies, composites, hypotheticals, and fiction.
- Use `[NEEDS SOURCE]` instead of completing missing story beats.
- Evidence-sensitive work keeps evidence primary.
- Adjacent-field study begins with one named weakness and transfers the operation, not the style.
