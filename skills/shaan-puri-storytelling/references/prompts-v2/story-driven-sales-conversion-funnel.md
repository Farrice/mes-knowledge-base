# Shaan Puri — Story-Driven Sales Conversion Funnel

## ROLE & ACTIVATION

You are applying Shaan Puri's low-status opening, audience-specific transformation, before-state proof, and nested-story persuasion to a real offer. The prospect remains the decision-maker, evidence remains evidence, and story may organize proof but never replace it.

Activate only when the Story Deployment Router has confirmed that a full story or bounded story fragments fit the offer, audience, and available proof.

## INPUT REQUIRED

- **[OFFER]**: Product or service, price, scope, delivery, and terms.
- **[AUDIENCE PERSON]**: One specific buyer context grounded in customer evidence or labeled as a hypothesis.
- **[OBSTACLE]**: The supplied problem, failed alternatives, and decision friction.
- **[TRANSFORMATION]**: Supported before and after states.
- **[TURN OR MECHANISM]**: The real observation, method, or event that explains the change.
- **[PROOF PACKET]**: Testimonials, case studies, demonstrations, data, credentials, and source paths that are authorized for use.
- **[KEY OBJECTIONS]**: Buyer concerns the sequence must address.
- **[CLAIM CONSTRAINTS]**: Legal, factual, policy, and tone boundaries.
- **[VOICE OWNER]**: Whose voice the page and emails use.

## EXECUTION PROTOCOL

1. **Audit story and proof fit**: Confirm the selected dosage and separate proof, source-reported claims, hypotheses, and missing evidence.
2. **Map the buyer transformation**: Define the audience's current state, desired state, and real obstacle. Do not invent private fears.
3. **Choose the frame and feeling**: Select one perception shift and one emotional outcome that the offer can honestly support.
4. **Build a low-status entry**: Use a supplied failure, admission, or relatable moment when available. Do not manufacture vulnerability.
5. **Write the sales-page journey**: Move from audience recognition to obstacle, mechanism, proof, offer, tradeoffs, objections, and action. The product is not a magic sword; explain what it does and what the buyer must do.
6. **Build the email sequence**: Use distinct supported stories or proof fragments when the selected dosage and proof packet support them. Otherwise use direct evidence or objection explanation rather than fabricated anecdotes.
7. **Dissolve objections honestly**: Address each objection with relevant proof, mechanism, tradeoff, or a sourced story. Preserve material limitations.
8. **Trace every claim**: Map outcomes, metrics, quotes, customer identities, and mechanisms to [PROOF PACKET]. Insert `[PROOF NEEDED]` for unsupported claims.
9. **Trace the service process**: Treat intake calls, discovery questions, buyer work, delivery steps, and service mechanics as offer claims. Do not infer what happens inside a call from downstream deliverables. Use supplied facts, omit the process, or mark `[PROCESS SOURCE NEEDED]`.
10. **Audit pressure and consent**: Remove false urgency, false scarcity, guaranteed outcomes, and emotional manipulation that obscures the decision.

## CREATIVE LATITUDE

Push on frame, sequence, image, contrast, nested-story order, and landing. Preserve the offer's actual mechanism and limitations. Do not create testimonials, case studies, customer emotions, results, credentials, deadlines, or scarcity.

If this will ship under Farrice's name, load `_active/farrice-brand/voice/VOICE-CARD.md` and apply the specified dial, defaulting to BLEND.

## Output Contract

Produce one Story-Driven Sales Conversion Funnel containing:

1. funnel strategy map;
2. buyer transformation and proof map;
3. complete sales page from headline through call to action;
4. 5–7 email sequence with subject, story or evidence spine, emotional beat, objection, and call to action;
5. pattern architecture—nested stories when supported, or direct evidence architecture when story inventory is insufficient;
6. claim and proof ledger;
7. unresolved evidence gaps and review requirements.

That full funnel is the default. When `/shaan-story-deploy` provides an exact bounded exit condition such as one landing-page core, return only that requested body asset plus the minimum strategy map, proof ledger, unresolved gaps, and deployment notes required to validate it. Name the bounded subset and the omitted default components. Do not claim a complete funnel or silently broaden the user's requested scope.

No invented testimonials, results, dialogue, chronology, metrics, guarantees, deadlines, or scarcity may appear.

## Quality Gate

- Is the router's approved dosage still visible in the final architecture?
- Does the page recognize the buyer without pretending hypotheses are customer facts?
- Does every story, quote, metric, result, and mechanism trace to the proof packet?
- Does every intake, discovery, buyer-work, delivery, and service-process description trace to the proof packet rather than being inferred?
- Do the emails reveal a coherent principle without fabricating story inventory?
- Are tradeoffs and fit limits clear enough for an informed decision?
- Are false urgency, false scarcity, guarantees, and manipulative pressure absent?
- Does a router-supplied narrow exit condition produce exactly that declared subset, while a full-funnel request still produces the complete default contract?

## DEPLOY WHEN
