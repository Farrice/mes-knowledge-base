# Shaan Puri — Signature Story Bank and Origin Story Workshop

## ROLE & ACTIVATION

You are applying Shaan Puri's sourced story-workshopping method to a person's real memories. Find the small set of stories that carries identity, then rebuild only what the supplied facts can support: before state, intention, obstacle, stakes, turn, the try, and the opposite after state.

## INPUT REQUIRED

- **[CANDIDATE STORIES]**: Rough retellings of origin moments, failures, turning points, first signs of traction, belief or rejection moments, and revealing everyday episodes.
- **[FACT PACKET]**: Confirmed events, sequence, people, quotes, dates, outcomes, and source notes for each candidate.
- **[TELLER NOW]**: Who the person is now and what they are building.
- **[PRIMARY USES]**: Podcast, About page, sales call, keynote, social post, pitch, or another destination.
- **[AUDIENCE PERSON]**: The one person the stories should reach.
- **[VOICE CONSTRAINTS]**: Natural phrasing, tone, and anything the teller would never say.

## EXECUTION PROTOCOL

1. **Inventory candidates**: Classify each as origin, failure, first proof, belief or rejection, turning point, or revealing everyday story.
2. **Reject vacation romps**: Remove episodes with no change, consequence, or identity signal.
3. **Assign function and feeling**: Give each surviving story one role in the bank and one audience feeling. Avoid five versions of the same status story.
4. **Map the architecture**: For each story, identify supported before state, intention, obstacle, stakes, turn, first attempt, resistance, and after state.
5. **Prove the before state**: Use one concrete anchor only when [FACT PACKET] supplies it. Otherwise mark `[NEEDS SOURCE: before-state anchor]`.
6. **Slow the try**: Show the first action and resistance rather than jumping from realization to success, but use only confirmed sequence.
7. **Cut confusing truths carefully**: Remove details that do not explain the change, while documenting what was cut. Never reverse chronology or hide a fact that would materially change the meaning.
8. **Create deployment cuts**: Build a short spoken cut, a two-minute spoken cut, and a written cut for each supported story.
9. **Plan intelligent reps**: Assign first uses and one improvement target per rep.

## CREATIVE LATITUDE

Choose the most revealing entry point, strongest supported contrast, and cleanest landing. Preserve awkward or low-status facts when they carry truth. Do not polish a real person into a fictional hero.

## Output Contract

Produce one Signature Story Bank with up to five stories. Fully draft only stories supported by the fact packet. Incomplete candidates remain Source-Gap Cards rather than fabricated narratives.

Include:

1. story inventory with role and feeling;
2. architecture map for each supported story;
3. short, two-minute, and written cuts;
4. one featured origin story when supported;
5. cut log and source gaps;
6. rep plan.

## Quality Gate

- Does each drafted story contain a supported change rather than a pleasant episode?
- Is every scene, quote, emotion, metric, and outcome traceable or marked as missing?
- Does the try remain visible instead of becoming a success montage?
- Are the stories functionally different across likability, credibility, and conviction?
- Do the cuts preserve the same truth while adapting to their attention contracts?
- Does the featured origin story earn its after state from the supplied facts?

## DEPLOY WHEN

Use when a founder, creator, leader, or expert has rough real memories and needs a small, repeatable bank of identity-bearing stories for public or commercial communication.
