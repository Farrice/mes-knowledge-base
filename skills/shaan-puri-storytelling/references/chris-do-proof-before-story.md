## Source-Derived Decision

Chris Do diagnoses an experiential travel offer before prescribing content. He
first separates the claimed quality of the experience from what a prospect can
actually inspect. At `00:08:30-00:09:32`, he asks for photos, videos, written and
video testimonials, illustrations, and audio, then names the audience's
"imagination gap" and says the proof must be documented. At
`00:10:35-00:11:25`, he rejects a promotional video in favor of a sourced
before-and-after transformation.

Operational consequence:

1. A claim such as "our experience is different" is not story evidence.
2. If promotion depends on a transformation the audience cannot yet inspect,
   the current publishable asset is `NO STORY`.
3. Route to `/proof-portfolio-builder` to acquire and organize the missing
   receipts.
4. Return to `/shaan-story-deploy` only when the raw material contains a real
   before state, obstacle or tension, observable after state, and enough
   evidence to locate the turn.

This preserves Shaan as narrative-dosage owner and Luke Iha's proof portfolio as
proof-acquisition owner. Chris Do contributes the source-grounded trigger and
handoff, not a competing production method.

## Proof-Capture Handoff

Pass the smallest useful packet to `/proof-portfolio-builder`:

- **Claim to replace:** what the business currently asks the audience to
  imagine.
- **Current proof inventory:** named assets that already exist, separated from
  opinions and unverified claims.
- **Before state:** the customer's own sourced words, behavior, or observable
  starting condition.
- **Obstacle:** the real constraint, fear, friction, or situation that made the
  result uncertain.
- **After state:** the observable change, result, artifact, or customer report.
- **Turn evidence:** what proves how or when the change occurred; use
  `[NEEDS SOURCE]` when unknown.
- **Capture forms:** written testimonial, video testimonial, photo, video,
  audio, artifact, or metric as appropriate to the claim.
- **Permission boundary:** consent, confidentiality, and allowed downstream use.

The returned proof portfolio is material, not permission to publish. Shaan
still chooses `FULL STORY`, `STORY FRAGMENT`, or `NO STORY`, and exactly one
production owner writes the body.

## Negative Controls

- A status, incident, procedure, specification, calculation, or direct decision
  remains `NO STORY` and routes to its direct domain owner. Missing narrative
  movement does not make proof capture useful.
- Health, finance, legal, safety, research, and policy work keeps its evidence
  spine. Narrative cannot upgrade a claim or turn source uncertainty into a
  transformation.
- A documented before and after with an unknown turn remains a
  `DOCUMENTED CONTRAST` or story fragment; no conversion scene is invented.
- Generic aspirations, polished testimonials without provenance, stock footage,
  and illustrative B-roll do not prove chronology or causality.
- Existing seeable proof does not force a full story. Shaan's dosage decision
  and `NO STORY` path remain authoritative.
