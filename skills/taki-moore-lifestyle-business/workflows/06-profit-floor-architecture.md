# Workflow 06: Profit Floor Architecture

> **Load genius context first**: Read `genius.md` before executing this workflow.

## Purpose
Design a **Profit Floor** — the minimum sustainable revenue layer that runs WITHOUT founder time — so the lifestyle business stays profitable even when the founder is fully living their life. Prevents the two failure modes that kill lifestyle businesses: (1) lifestyle eroding profit because the founder disengages, and (2) profit eroding lifestyle because the founder "just does one more thing."

## The Problem This Solves
Most lifestyle business advice says "design the life first." Correct. But it stops there. Over time:
- Revenue dips → founder panic → takes on extra clients → lifestyle collapses
- Founder protects boundaries → revenue stagnates → anxiety grows → worse decisions
- No system distinguishes "this constraint makes me more profitable" from "this constraint is costing me money"

**The Profit Floor** is the revenue layer below which the business CANNOT fall without the founder having to work more. It's the financial boundary that PROTECTS the lifestyle boundary.

## Input Required
- **Current revenue model**: What generates money today? (coaching, courses, community, done-for-you)
- **Founder time audit**: How many hours/week does the founder ACTUALLY work? On what?
- **Revenue-per-hour map**: Which activities generate revenue? Which don't?
- **Lifestyle non-negotiables**: From the Life Design Canvas (Workflow 01)
- **"Panic number"**: Below what monthly revenue does the founder start compromising lifestyle?

## Execution

You are Taki Moore deploying a layer that doesn't exist in the standard playbook — the financial architecture that makes lifestyle constraints GENERATIVE rather than merely protective.

### 1. Revenue Autopsy: What Runs Without You?

Categorize every revenue stream by **Founder Dependency Score (FDS)**:

| FDS Level | Description | Examples |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **FDS-0** | Runs with zero founder hours | Evergreen digital product, automated upsell, affiliate revenue |
| **FDS-1** | Runs with <2 hours/week founder time | Community with moderators, cohort with guest coaches, templated service |
| **FDS-2** | Requires founder presence but is time-boxed | Group coaching calls, monthly cohort intake, content recording |
| **FDS-3** | Requires founder for every dollar | 1:1 coaching, done-for-you, custom work |

**The Profit Floor = total revenue from FDS-0 and FDS-1 sources.**

If the Profit Floor < the Panic Number, the lifestyle business has a structural vulnerability. The founder's lifestyle is one bad month away from collapse.

### 2. Profit Floor Engineering

For each FDS-2 and FDS-3 revenue stream, run this diagnostic:

**The Constraint Conversion Question**: "If I could NEVER do this activity again, how would I still capture this revenue?"

Options (deploy using GP-11 DAFY thinking):
- **Productize**: Turn the 1:1 insight into a self-serve tool, template, or course module
- **Delegate**: Train someone to deliver 80% of the value (not the founder's genius — the repeatable parts)
- **Automate**: Replace the human touchpoint with a DAFY tool, AI workflow, or self-serve system
- **Eliminate**: If it can't be converted, ask: "Is this revenue worth the lifestyle cost?" If no, kill it.

For each conversion, calculate:
- **Revenue retained**: What % of current revenue survives the conversion?
- **Hours reclaimed**: How many founder hours return to the lifestyle?
- **Profit Floor increase**: How much does the floor rise?

### 3. The Lifestyle Constraint Audit

Not all lifestyle constraints are equal. Some make the business BETTER (forcing focus, creating scarcity, improving decision quality). Others are just leaving money on the table.

**Generative Constraints** (KEEP — they make you more profitable):
- "I only take 20 clients/month" → creates genuine scarcity (GP-06), increases demand
- "I don't do sales calls" → offer doc system closes better AND saves 10+ hrs/week (GP-04)
- "I only work Tue-Thu" → forces batching, eliminates context-switching, increases output quality
- "I don't customize" → standardized delivery scales, reduces support load

**Degenerative Constraints** (EXAMINE — they might be costing you):
- "I don't look at metrics" → can't distinguish what's working from what's dying
- "I don't do any marketing" → relying on hope, not a trust economy engine
- "I refuse to hire anyone" → bottleneck that caps revenue at founder-hours ceiling
- "I don't raise prices" → leaving money on the table out of fear, not strategy

**The test**: For each constraint, ask: "Does this constraint force a BETTER business decision, or does it just avoid an uncomfortable one?"

### 4. The Three-Number Dashboard

Every lifestyle business needs exactly three numbers, checked monthly:

1. **Profit Floor**: Revenue from FDS-0 + FDS-1 sources. Must be > Panic Number.
2. **Founder Freedom Ratio**: (Total revenue - Profit Floor) / Total revenue. This is the % of revenue that depends on the founder showing up. Target: < 40%.
3. **Constraint ROI**: For each lifestyle constraint, the revenue impact (positive or negative). Positive = generative. Negative = degenerative. Review quarterly.

### 5. The Profit Floor Ratchet

Once established, the Profit Floor only moves UP:
- Every new product/offer gets evaluated on FDS before launch
- Every process improvement that reduces FDS = Profit Floor increase
- Every quarter, convert ONE FDS-2 stream to FDS-1 or FDS-0
- The ratchet ensures that as the founder takes MORE lifestyle freedom, the business gets MORE stable, not less

This is the anti-pattern to "lifestyle business = eventually broke": each lifestyle upgrade MUST come with a corresponding Profit Floor upgrade.

## Output

**THE PROFIT FLOOR BLUEPRINT** — containing:

1. **Revenue Autopsy**: Every stream categorized by FDS, current Profit Floor calculated
2. **Conversion Plan**: For each FDS-2/3 stream, the specific conversion strategy (productize/delegate/automate/eliminate) with revenue retention estimates
3. **Constraint Audit**: Each lifestyle constraint classified as generative or degenerative, with recommended actions
4. **Three-Number Dashboard**: Current Profit Floor, Founder Freedom Ratio, Constraint ROI for top 3 constraints
5. **Quarterly Ratchet Plan**: Which FDS-2 stream converts next, and how

**Format**: Operational document with specific numbers. Not aspirational — mechanical. The founder should be able to look at this and know exactly what to do next month.

## Quality Gate
- [ ] Is the Profit Floor > the Panic Number? If not, is there a clear 90-day path to get there?
- [ ] Does every lifestyle constraint have a classification (generative vs. degenerative)?
- [ ] Can the founder take a 2-week vacation without revenue dropping below the Profit Floor?
- [ ] Is there a clear quarterly ratchet — one specific FDS conversion per quarter?
- [ ] Would Taki say "this protects the life AND the business" — not just one or the other?

> **🛡️ Anti-Pattern Check**: The Profit Floor is NOT about maximizing revenue. It's about making the lifestyle SUSTAINABLE by ensuring the business doesn't depend on the founder's constant presence. If the blueprint feels like a "scale plan," it's wrong.
