# Tess Barclay — Social Media Content System

Tess Barclay (founder of Busy Blooming, social media strategist and creator-economy analyst) builds creators around one inversion: a niche is an AUDIENCE, not a topic. Her system replaces the dying aesthetic-influencer playbook with "polished casual" content — high production quality, casual human substance — positioned where AI cannot compete: connection, journey, opinion, and personality rather than commoditized education. The operating unit is a "corner of social media" for one niche audience, run like a workplace ritual (weekly formats, community language, a business connected to the content).

## Quick Reference

- **Niche = audience, not topic.** Build a dynamic multi-topic brand for ONE niche audience. Brands now discover creators by audience interest, not demographics.
- **Polished casual = quality x authenticity as independent axes.** "Sitting in your apartment in a sweatsuit — but in 4K." Back camera, high quality, casual human substance.
- **AI-proof positioning:** education migrates to AI; connection appreciates. Lead with journey, mistakes, opinions, hot takes, personality — value through connection, not instruction.
- **Older-sibling authority:** not the expert ("here's what you'll do"), not chaotic — "I've tried everything to clear my skin; these are the 5 products I'm obsessed with."
- **Platform bet:** YouTube long-form sit-downs (~15 min) as the weekly theme + Instagram carousels/short-form extended from it; Stories/lives for real-time connection.
- **Ritual infiltration:** a fixed weekly live/Q&A so you enter the audience's routine; community language and a signature series create belonging.
- **Income = content + connected business** (subscription community or coaching), never brand deals/AdSense alone.
