## Workflow

### Phase 0: Algorithmic Arbitrage Detection (Format Window Intelligence)

Before selecting ANY format, systematically diagnose which formats are currently underpriced by the algorithm. Format arbitrage windows open and close — what worked 6 months ago may be saturated now. First-movers in emerging formats get 3-10x the reach of late adopters.

**Step 1: Format Saturation Inventory**
Audit the target niche's LinkedIn feed for the last 30 days. For each major format type (text-only, carousel/document, selfie/photo, video, poll, newsletter, collaborative article, live event, audio), estimate:
- **Volume**: How many creators in this niche are using this format? (Low / Medium / High)
- **Quality ceiling**: What's the best execution you've seen? (Poor / Competent / Exceptional)
- **Engagement-to-effort ratio**: How much reach does a median post in this format get relative to effort? (Declining / Stable / Rising)

**Step 2: Arbitrage Window Classification**
Classify each format into one of four states:
- **OPEN WINDOW** (Low volume + Rising engagement): First-mover territory. The algorithm is rewarding this format but few creators are using it. Deploy aggressively.
- **CLOSING WINDOW** (Medium volume + Stable engagement): Early majority is arriving. Still profitable but differentiation within the format now matters more than the format itself.
- **SATURATED** (High volume + Declining engagement): The algorithm has normalized this format. Engagement-to-effort ratio is falling. Avoid unless you have a genuine format innovation (not just "better carousel").
- **DORMANT** (Low volume + Low engagement): Either the algorithm doesn't reward this format, or no one has cracked the execution. Investigate whether this is a dead format or an undiscovered opportunity.

**Step 3: First-Mover Opportunity Mapping**
For each OPEN or DORMANT-with-potential format, answer:
1. **What adjacent industry is already using this format successfully?** (The Niche Bending source)
2. **Why hasn't this niche adopted it yet?** (Identify the adoption barrier — is it skill, awareness, or perceived inappropriateness?)
3. **What would exceptional execution look like in THIS niche?** (Describe the specific content that would make people stop and think "I've never seen an S&C coach do THIS on LinkedIn")
4. **What's the expertise transfer value?** When this window closes, what skill or audience relationship persists?

**Step 4: Format Arbitrage Scorecard**
Rank the top 3 opportunities by:
- **Window Size** (how long before saturation): Months, not weeks = better
- **Execution Moat** (how hard to copy well): Higher = better
- **Expertise Transferability** (what you keep when the window closes): Skills and audience trust that compound
- **Niche-Format Fit** (how naturally the format serves the expertise): Forced = abandon, intuitive = pursue

*Quality Gate*: If you cannot identify at least ONE format in OPEN WINDOW state, the niche may be format-saturated. In that case, the arbitrage opportunity is in FORMAT INNOVATION within a closing-window format (new execution pattern, not new format type). Document this pivot explicitly.

> **Transition**: Phase 0 output (ranked format opportunities with arbitrage scores) feeds directly into Phase 1. The top-ranked format becomes the Niche Bend target. If the top opportunity is NOT a carousel or selfie, adapt the Phase 2/3 execution to match the winning format.

### Phase 1: The Niche Bend & Hook Engineering
Apply the **Niche Bending Formula** (`Niche = Format + Market`) to the user's core topic. We are not inventing a market; we are wrapping established demand in a novel format.
1.  **The Trapdoor Hook (Zeigarnik Effect)**: Craft two distinct 3-line hooks—one for the Carousel and one for the Selfie.
    *   *Rule*: The thought must remain unresolved at the 3rd line to force the "See More" click.
    *   *Pattern*: Line 1: Universal Problem. Line 2: The Shocking Cost. Line 3: The "Anti-Guru" Pivot... [Truncation]

### Phase 2: The Apex Predator (10-Slide Document Carousel)
Construct an 8-10 slide PDF blueprint designed for the **Sunk-Cost Swipe**. Every slide must hold a 24.42% higher engagement potential by delivering a complete, chunked idea.
1.  **Slide 1: The Cover**: Use negative urgency. What does it cost them NOT to know this?
2.  **Slide 2: The Retention Bridge**: Insert an ego challenge. (e.g., "90% of consultants fail at Step 3. Here is why you won't.")
3.  **Slides 3-8: The Core Payload**: 1 Idea Per Slide. Use the **1-3-1 Structural Rhythm** within the slides. Maximize white space.
4.  **Slide 9: Transformation Summary**: A high-contrast "Before vs. After" visual projection.
5.  **Slide 10: The Exit & Save Trigger**: Explicitly command the "Save" action to signal high value to the algorithm.

### Phase 3: The Costly Signal (Contextual Selfie)
Counteract AI fatigue using **Costly Signaling Theory**. This post builds the "Anti-Guru" positioning by showing the "messy middle" of real work.
1.  **Visual Directive**: Describe the "Proof-of-Work" photo.
    *   *Constraint*: No stock photos. Must include specific environmental artifacts (coffee cups, messy wires, toddler drawings, actual code/spreadsheets on screen).
2.  **The Anti-Guru Manifesto**: Write the post text attacking the **Guru Trope**.
    *   *Structure*: Hook line -> The Evidence of Failure -> The Pivot to the Photo -> The Re-Frame.
    *   *Tone*: Grounded, contrarian, high-leverage.

### Phase 4: The Velocity Engine (Self-Pinned Comments)
Generate two **T-Zero Pinned Comments** to seed the conversation and drive the **Comment-to-Download Flywheel**.
1.  **Carousel Comment (Archetype: Behind the Scenes)**: Share a specific client win or a "making of" detail that proves the framework works in the wild.
2.  **Selfie Comment (Archetype: Soft Funnel)**: Provide a low-friction entry point to the lead magnet using a qualifying question to force a reply (spiking the 60-minute Velocity Score).
