# Geoff Woods — AI Thought Partner OS

Geoff Woods is the founder of AI Leadership and author of the #1 bestseller *The AI-Driven Leader*. Before that he co-founded the training and consulting company behind *The ONE Thing* with Gary Keller and Jay Papasan, and was a C-level exec at a public company based in India (external bios identify it as Jindal Steel & Power, CGO, with a $750M → $12B market-cap arc — bio claims, not from the interview; see `references/source-ledger.md`). His system inverts the default AI relationship: **"You are the thought leader. AI is your thought partner."** Instead of asking AI questions like it's Google, he makes AI interview *him* — one question at a time — to pull context out of his head, elevate his thinking, and only then produce. Every output is "the bad answer" until the Feedback Triad has run. This skill encodes his full operating system, and its Tier O layer (CO-CREATION-CARD.md) installs the posture as this workspace's always-on default.

## Quick Reference

- **The Three Skills**: (1) target a 20% use case ("if you aim it at something that doesn't matter, it doesn't matter"), (2) communicate via CRIT, (3) remain the thought leader (skeptical, iterating, never submitting slop).
- **CRIT**: **C**ontext (verbose, unedited, voice-dump; "when you think you've given enough, assume you have not — ask 'what else?'") → **R**ole (vivid specific expert, never a category label) → **I**nterview (the most important part: "ask me one question at a time, up to five, to gain deeper context") → **T**ask (request the DELTA: non-obvious strategies, what-I-don't-know).
- **The Feedback Triad**: "Here's what I like / what I don't like / the top changes." Run it on every output; a prompt becomes a conversation.
- **"This is the bad answer"**: the pre-read ritual. Every output is a first draft regardless of how good it sounds.
- **Persona flip**: at plateau ("best I can do ≠ best that can be done") rotate: Challenger → ideal customer → aggressive growth-minded board member.
- **Dominoes**: agentic AI is the 18th domino. Three Skills daily × 30 days first ("you haven't seen color yet"). Agents only for documented, repetitive, 20%-tied tasks — one at a time, made excellent.
- **Job = skills + processes**: anything undocumented in your head → CRIT interview → markdown → agent replicates it.
- **Cognitive sovereignty**: AI that answers your questions replaces your thinking; AI that questions your answers strengthens it. Self-test: "Can I still solve problems without it?" Pen-and-paper thinking time upstream of AI, always.
- **AI-repricing rule**: if AI is 50% as good as you at a task today, start handing it over — in 12 months it runs away with it. Durable human skills: strategic thinking, problem solving, communication, collaboration (people + machines), creativity.
- **Conductor creativity**: "You're no longer the player of one instrument. You're the conductor" — creativity relocates to vision-casting, role-casting, task design.
- **Claims quarantine**: his case-study outcomes ($300M save, board turnaround, $750M→$12B) are HIS stated figures — cite as his claims, never as verified facts.
