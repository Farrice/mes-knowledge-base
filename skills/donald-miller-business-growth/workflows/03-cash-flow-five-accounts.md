# 03 — Cash-Flow: The Five Accounts

The fuel tanks. *"Without fuel, the plane crashes no matter how well it is designed."* This installs Miller's five-checking-account system — designed for owners who are *"more wired to make money than manage it"* — giving real-time optics and never running out of cash.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Run this when:
- Business and personal money are commingled; the owner can't say what's profit.
- Tax Day is a recurring ambush; there's no reserve fund.
- The fuel tanks scored low in the flight-plan diagnostic — **start here if cash is the fire.**
- The owner identifies as a moneymaker, not a money-manager.

**Do NOT** treat this as accounting/tax advice — it's a cash-flow *operating system*. Pair with a real accountant for tax specifics.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `genius.md` Pattern 7 (Five-Account System) + the "commingled money" anti-pattern. (Aligned with Mike Michalowicz's *Profit First*; note Miller's boil-over tweak below.)

## Execution

**Step 1 — Open (or rename) five checking accounts, all visible in one portal:**
1. **Operating** — all revenue flows in; all bills (incl. your fixed salary) flow out.
2. **Personal** — receives your fixed salary; you live off this. Never pull from Operating for personal wants.
3. **Business Profit** — profit + rainy-day reserve.
4. **Tax** — set aside as you earn, so taxes are never a surprise.
5. **Investment Holding** — money that buys money-making assets.

The visibility in one portal is the point: *"see the overall health of my company in an instant."*

**Step 2 — Pay yourself a fixed salary.** Automate a bi-monthly/monthly transfer Operating → Personal. *"You must live off of a fixed salary so the business can establish a predictable rhythm."* Raise it deliberately as the business grows — never raid Operating for a car/watch/"albino tiger."

**Step 3 — Set Operating's high-water mark.** ≈ enough to cover the largest single monthly hit (e.g., if payroll is $25K/2wks, keep Operating ≥ ~$35K). High enough to be safe; low enough that excess cash doesn't sit there inviting spend.

**Step 4 — Sweep the boil-over.** When Operating exceeds its high-water mark, transfer the excess — **split it between Business Profit and Tax** (e.g., $25K over → $12.5K Profit + $12.5K Tax). *Miller's tweak vs. Profit First:* take profit when Operating **boils over** (his revenue spikes sporadically with big deals), rather than a fixed % up front. Bonus: you'll over-fund Tax and move the surplus to Profit at year-end — the opposite of the usual Tax-Day panic.

**Step 5 — Set Business Profit's high-water mark + sweep to Investment Holding.** Business Profit's mark ≈ **6× Operating's** (≈ 6 months of overhead = the reserve fuel to "circle the airport" in a crisis — survive a downturn without layoffs). When Business Profit overflows that, sweep the excess to **Investment Holding** — the account whose *name pressures you to invest rather than splurge.* From there: real estate, stocks, retirement, giving. *"Your business is a machine that makes you money so you can buy another machine that makes you money."*

**Step 6 — Note the reverse-transfer rule + raise marks as you grow.** If you must move money *back* from Business Profit to Operating, you can move the same amount back from Tax (it's no longer taxable income). Raise both high-water marks as the business's expenses grow.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Business shape | Cash-flow emphasis |
|---|---|
| Side hustle ($20K/yr) | Pay yourself little; still separate the 5 accounts for the habit |
| Lumpy revenue (big deals) | Boil-over method shines — sweep profit on the spikes |
| Steady recurring revenue | Either boil-over or fixed-% (Profit First) works; pick one |
| Partners / investors | Split Investment Holding per an agreed cadence |
| High-tax-exposure | Sweep a higher Tax share; treat Tax as a secondary safety buffer |

## Output Requirements

- **The five named accounts** + what flows in/out of each.
- **A fixed owner salary** figure + the auto-transfer cadence.
- **Operating high-water mark** + the boil-over split rule.
- **Business Profit high-water mark** (≈6× Operating) + the Investment-Holding sweep.
- A one-line **portal habit**: log in, read the plane's health at a glance.

## Quality Gate

Reject if (per `genius.md`):
- Business and personal money stay commingled, or the owner takes a variable draw instead of a fixed salary.
- No high-water marks (profit never gets swept; cash sits in Operating and gets spent).
- No Tax account (Tax-Day ambush remains).
- No reserve target (~6 months) — the plane can't circle the airport in a crisis.
- It's presented as tax/accounting advice rather than a cash-flow operating system.
