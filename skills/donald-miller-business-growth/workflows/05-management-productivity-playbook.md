# 05 — Management & Productivity Playbook

The body. The #1 source of overhead bloat is **labor** — and the fix is rarely layoffs. It's **re-pointing labor at the three economic priorities** so team energy flows to the wings/engines instead of busywork in the body. The system is five meetings, run by an operator.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Run this when:
- "Too many people wearing too many hats"; the culture feels like organized chaos.
- Team members aren't sure what they're supposed to be working on (someone spent a week on a cancelled project).
- The owner is pulled into firefighting (S-curve); meetings are many and unproductive.
- The body scored heavy in the flight-plan diagnostic (Wf 01).

**Requires** a defined Mission Statement with three economic priorities (Wf 02) — the playbook exists to focus labor *on those priorities*. Run Wf 02 first if it's missing.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `genius.md` Pattern 6 (Management & Productivity) + the "firing first" and "founder-as-operator" anti-patterns.

## Execution

**Step 1 — Assign an operator (the key move).** Three leader types: the **artist** (obsesses over product/vision), the **entrepreneur** (smells revenue/growth), the **operator** (organizes chaos into repeatable systems). **Only an operator installs/runs this playbook** — *"if you hand this assignment to an artist, they will forget to show up at 75% of the meetings."* Find a process-driven, encouraging truth-teller (title: COO/President/President/EA — don't overthink titles). This person will effectively run the company day-to-day, freeing the founder to be the artist/entrepreneur.

**Step 2 — Install the five meetings, in order, one at a time:**
1. **All-Staff** (weekly, ~45–60min) — align everyone on the 3 economic priorities; share dept updates; **publicly honor** team members advancing the mission. Start here; run for a few months before adding more.
2. **Leadership** (weekly, right after All-Staff, ~30–60min) — dept heads cover initiatives + roadblocks against the economic objectives.
3. **Department Stand-up** (daily, <15min, led by dept directors) — set the day's objectives, surface blockers. (Five priorities / what I completed / what's next / where I'm blocked.)
4. **Personal Priority Speed Check** (weekly 1:1, ~15min, led by dept head) — individual coaching/feedback; team member fills the template first. Drives morale; the owner does NOT run these.
5. **Quarterly Performance Review** (quarterly) — assess performance honestly (both fill the template); answer the team's #1 question, *"am I doing a good job?"*; tie Q4 to compensation.

**Step 3 — Use templates, every time.** Each meeting has a template that keeps it focused on the economic priorities. *"If you do not use the template, the conversation during the stand-up will wander in unproductive directions."*

**Step 4 — Tie compensation to performance (as you scale past ~10 people).** A simple, fair, scalable model: 1–3% raise by performance; 1–3% (of salary) bonus tied to hitting the company revenue goal, up to 5% at the stretch goal. Requires revenue + stretch-revenue goals. Removes the precedent-trap of discretionary raises.

**Step 5 — Output the install plan.** Which meetings to launch and when, who runs each (mostly NOT the owner), the templates, the operator hire, and the comp model — framed as *converting body weight into thrust/lift* (same payroll, more output aimed at the priorities).

## Content Type Adaptations

| Business shape | Management emphasis |
|---|---|
| Solopreneur | Skip most meetings; the Personal Priority Speed Check becomes self-accountability |
| <6 people | All-Staff (a few times/week) may suffice; combine with Leadership |
| Departments forming | Add Department Stand-ups; appoint dept directors to run them |
| Remote/distributed | Templates matter more (no hallway alignment); stand-ups over video |
| Scaling past ~10 | Add Personal Priority Speed Checks + Quarterly Reviews + comp model |

## Output Requirements

- **The operator** — who runs the playbook (and why they're an operator, not artist/entrepreneur).
- **The five-meeting cadence** — which, when, who leads, with the owner attending only ~3.
- **Templates** for each meeting (or a pointer to build them).
- **A labor-refocus statement** — how this re-points existing labor at the 3 economic priorities (body → thrust/lift).
- **Comp model** (if >10 people) tied to revenue goals.

## Quality Gate

Reject if (per `genius.md`):
- It recommends layoffs before re-pointing labor at the economic priorities.
- The playbook is assigned to the artist/entrepreneur founder instead of an operator.
- Meetings run without templates (they'll wander).
- There are no economic priorities to focus labor on (run Wf 02 first).
- The owner is scheduled to attend all five meetings (defeats the purpose).
