# Donald Miller — Small Business Growth & Operations

The four other Donald Miller skills own **messaging** (`storybrand`, `cognitive-load`, `culture-turnaround`, `messaging-evolution`). This skill owns the **business operating system** underneath it: how to engineer a small business like an airplane — leadership/mission, products, overhead/operations, and cash flow — so it scales without crashing.

Miller's airplane has six parts. Two — the **right engine (marketing)** and **left engine (sales)** — are pure messaging, already built to mastery in the other skills. **This skill does NOT rebuild the engines.** It owns the other four parts and routes the engines out. The meta-thesis, from a mentor's driveway advice: ***"professionalize your operation"*** — a business that runs on the founder's improvisation has a ceiling; one that runs on installed systems does not.

> **Source (PRIMARY)**: *How to Grow Your Small Business* (Donald Miller, HarperCollins Leadership, 2023, ISBN 978-1-4002-2695-5), verbatim. Collateral: StoryBrand *Fully Booked* + *Close the Client Kit*. See [references/source-quotes.md](references/source-quotes.md).

## Stacking Guide — the engine hand-offs (anti-hoarding)

This skill flies the plane; it does **not** build the engines. Route the two messaging parts out:

| Airplane part | Owned here | Route to |
|---|---|---|
| Cockpit (Leadership/Mission) | ✅ Wf 02 | — |
| **Right Engine (Marketing)** | ❌ | `donald-miller-storybrand` (BrandScript, one-liner, funnel) + `donald-miller-cognitive-load` (score/simplify) |
| **Left Engine (Sales)** | ❌ | `donald-miller-messaging-evolution/02` (6-part color-key pitch) + `donald-miller-storybrand` |
| Wings (Products) | ✅ Wf 04 | new-product *positioning* → `messaging-evolution/04` (lane discipline) |
| Body (Overhead/Ops) | ✅ Wf 05 | — |
| Fuel Tanks (Cash Flow) | ✅ Wf 03 | — |
