# Baldacci Craft → Non-Fiction / Marketing / Digital-Product Transfer Map

How each fiction mechanic deploys outside the novel. This is the layer that makes the skill earn its keep across Farrice's domains (ebooks, sales copy, email, LinkedIn, client work).

| Fiction mechanic | Transfer principle | Deployments |
|---|---|---|
| **Big Pop** (plant assumptions, shatter them) | Cold audiences pre-judge; let them bet and lose in the first lines and they surrender attention | Sales-page leads, email subject→open contrast, LinkedIn hooks, VSL cold opens, ebook chapter 1 |
| **Premise as plant food** (jacket-copy test) | If the one-paragraph premise doesn't contain every thread, the asset stalls mid-build | Offer one-liners, ebook back-cover copy, course promises, landing-page headlines — write the jacket copy BEFORE the product |
| **Baggage unrelated to plot** | The ongoing wound/quest, not the individual piece, is the subscription engine | Creator narrative arcs, newsletter serialization, brand founder story, series content (each post = plot; the arc = baggage) |
| **Motivation-magnitude matching** | Claims need proof equal to their size or plausibility snaps | Copy claims vs. proof stack; testimonial weight vs. promise size; case-study framing |
| **Gettysburg standard** (100→10) | Compression budget = brand equity; established voices can spend it | Editing pass on any draft; CTA lines; bio copy; the one-liner that closes a post |
| **Two-purpose scene rule** | Every section must advance, deepen, or arm — or die | Content audits, course modules, funnel steps, deck slides, email paragraphs |
| **99% research cut** | One eyewitness detail beats a data dump | Research-heavy briefs, authority content, niche SEO pages — plant the mechanical cowboy, delete the rest |
| **Slow-motion peak** | Finite go-for-it moments; decelerate at the payoff so consequence lands | Launch reveals, case-study turns, webinar pitches, testimonial sequences, storytelling posts |
| **Pen endings** | Placeholder + disassociation beats forced closure | Stuck endings/CTAs/names: draft the plausible version, walk away, amalgamate |
| **Percolation gate** | Excitement is the leading indicator of output quality | Content calendar triage; kill trend-chasing pieces with an empty tank |
| **Knowledge pots** | Originality = YOUR unique combination + the unanswered question | Ideation for books/series/offers; "what question did the big story leave open?" |
| **One-day eyewitness research** | A single day-in-the-life yields 90% of the world | ICP research (walk the avatar through one day), client discovery, niche immersion |
| **Author economics** | Map money flow, build leverage, raise the deal | See `business-of-publishing.md` transfer table |
| **Find readers where they are** | Product ≠ package; format follows audience | Repurposing strategy, ebook vs. audio vs. thread, platform selection |
| **Game-film the book** | Reader-reaction data outranks author intent ("if enough people say this is not how they saw it, that's my problem") | Post-launch retros, content analytics review, A/B learnings, testimonial mining |
