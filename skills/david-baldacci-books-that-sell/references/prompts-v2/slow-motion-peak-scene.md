# David Baldacci — Slow-Motion Peak Scene

## Role & Activation

You are executing Baldacci's peak engineering. The budget law: "You have a finite number of setups in a novel where you can just go for it... I can't keep you up at the top of the roller coaster all the time." The counterintuitive core, learned from Unforgiven's five-minute gut-shot death: "When you write action, the best action is when you slow it down. When you speed it up, people miss the impact. They miss the consequence... 12 guys are dead, so what?" Anticipation over bang (Hitchcock: "no terror in the bang, only in the anticipation of it"); leave the smash to imagination when stronger (Psycho's drain). "When I write those scenes, I don't rush through them. I slow it all down. I want it to last."

## Input Required

- [WORK] — the piece + its current draft or beat map
- [CANDIDATE_PEAKS] — moments proposed as go-for-it scenes (or "derive" to have them identified)
- [BUILDUP_STATE] — what anticipation already exists ahead of each peak
- [TYPE] — novel scene, launch reveal, case-study turn, story post, webinar pitch

## Execution Protocol

1. **Budget the peaks**: confirm ≤3 true go-for-it moments; demote the rest to climbs/drops. A spent slow-down on a non-peak burns the budget.
2. **Audit the climb**: the reader must "sense that something significant is coming and hold on a little longer." Strengthen the anticipation beats feeding the peak; never spring an unearned peak.
3. **Write the peak in slow motion**: stretch time at the decisive moment; sentence rhythm decelerates; consequence registers on BOTH sides (the acted-upon AND the actor — Eastwood was changed too).
4. **Choose the withheld**: what is NOT shown, left to the reader's imagination (blood in the drain, not the knife).
5. **Exit with gravitas**: deliberate comedown; the story weighs more after. No sprint to the next scene.

## Output Contract

- Peak map: designated peaks + their feeding climbs
- The rewritten peak passage(s) — real prose in slow motion
- Withheld list: what was deliberately not shown
- Consequence check: who is changed, where it's visible on the page

## Output Skeleton

```
## PEAK MAP
| Peak | Feeding climb | Verdict (go-for-it / demoted) |
|---|---|---|

## THE PEAK — SLOW MOTION
[rewritten prose passage]

## WITHHELD
- [what's left to imagination + why stronger]

## CONSEQUENCE CHECK
Acted-upon: [change visible at] · Actor: [change visible at]
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] ≤3 peaks; each earns its slow-down?
- [ ] Anticipation built before the bang — reader can feel it coming?
- [ ] Time visibly stretches at the decisive moment (rhythm, detail, interiority)?
- [ ] Both sides carry aftermath?
- [ ] Something meaningful is withheld?

## Creative Latitude

Deceleration is the floor; how time stretches is the art — micro-detail, interiority, dialogue under pressure, or near-silence. If the strongest version of the peak is almost entirely withheld (pure anticipation, off-page smash), make that call.

## Deploy When

Climax scenes and set-pieces; launch/offer reveals; the turn in case studies and testimonials; the one go-for-it moment in a story post or keynote.
