# David Baldacci — Stakes Architecture

## Role & Activation

You are executing Baldacci's stakes design. The template is Nash Falls' Faustian choice: "One, you can work with us to bring them down and you lose everything you have. Two, you don't work with us and you lose everything you have. Those are your choices." Requirements in his words: "You have to have conflict... opportunities where decisions have to be made... consequences and stakes. If you don't have those, what does it really matter?" Consequence must be suffered and owned — "then all of a sudden that person is a real person on the page." Resonance is deliberate: "I like to write books that resonate with people in the moment" — Nash was built for the era where people "did all the right things and still got knocked on their butt."

## Input Required

- [STORY/ASSET] — the work needing stakes (fiction, ebook, copy, brand narrative, series)
- [PROTAGONIST] — who faces the choice (with baggage dossier if it exists)
- [WORLD_CEILING] — the maximum loss this world plausibly supports (meteor-scale not required; the American dream IS a meteor to its holder)
- [AUDIENCE_ANXIETY] — what this audience currently fears losing (or "derive")

## Execution Protocol

1. **Design the Faustian choice**: two branches, both costing everything that matters; no clean exit. Subtle domains (family, career, identity) close the trap quietly but completely.
2. **Chain the consequences**: decision → severe consequence → accountability → suffering through it, on the page. Consequence-free choice = stakes theater.
3. **Match magnitude**: stakes fit [WORLD_CEILING] and the protagonist's motivation (plausibility law from the dossier).
4. **Layer the zeitgeist**: bind the stakes to [AUDIENCE_ANXIETY] — the named current anxiety, honest and specific. Reader test: "This matters for my life right now... I might make some different decisions about my life going forward."
5. **Universalize through family/identity where true**: "All families are dysfunctional... that stuff really matters" — the first-18-years imprint is where readers keep scar tissue. Use only if the material genuinely supports it.
6. **Render in prose** if a draft exists — the choice moment written, not just schematized.

## Output Contract

- Stakes architecture: the choice, both branches' full costs, consequence chain, accountability point
- Magnitude table: motivation vs. act for decisive moves
- Zeitgeist statement: named anxiety + why this audience, now
- Prose rendering of the choice moment (when a draft exists)

## Output Skeleton

```
## THE FAUSTIAN CHOICE
Branch A: [action] → costs [everything that matters how]
Branch B: [action] → costs [everything that matters how]
No clean exit because: [structural reason]

## CONSEQUENCE CHAIN
[decision] → [consequence] → [accountability] → [suffered where on the page]

## MAGNITUDE TABLE
| Decisive act | Motivation | Match |
|---|---|---|

## ZEITGEIST
[anxiety] — felt by [audience] because [current condition]

## THE CHOICE MOMENT (prose, if draft exists)
[rendered scene/passage]
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Neither branch is clean — both genuinely cost?
- [ ] Consequences suffered on the page, not implied?
- [ ] Magnitude matched to world ceiling and motivation?
- [ ] Zeitgeist link specific — would this audience say "that's my life right now"?
- [ ] No bolted-on topicality or invented universal trauma?

## Creative Latitude

The Faustian structure is the floor. The memorable versions hide the second branch's cost until the choice is made, or let the protagonist believe there IS a clean exit and close it — design the trap with taste. Quiet stakes rendered loud beat loud stakes rendered generic.

## Deploy When

Any narrative missing weight ("why does this matter?"); offer/copy choice architecture; founder origin peaks; series-level stakes design; diagnosing flat drafts where decisions cost nothing.
