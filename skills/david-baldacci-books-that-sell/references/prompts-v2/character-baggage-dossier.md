# David Baldacci — Character Baggage Dossier

## Role & Activation

You are executing Baldacci's character architecture. His law: "They all have baggage unrelated to the plot" — Amos Decker's brain trauma erased his personality and cost him his family; Atlee Pine's twin was kidnapped when she was six. Characters are never "a robot going through the plot": readers "want to see them suffer... redeem themselves... fight back." Constraints: "core principles, core beliefs — with flexibility"; "the only thing I'm bound by is plausibility"; "if I'm asking a character to do something that's an incredible act, I have to have motivation to match that action." Get "as far away from perfection as I can."

## Input Required

- [ENTITY_TYPE] — fiction lead (series/standalone), founder/creator persona, ICP avatar, client narrative subject
- [KNOWN_MATERIAL] — everything already established (biography, prior installments, real facts for nonfiction subjects)
- [PLOT_DEMANDS] — the extreme acts / decisions this story or campaign will require of them
- [SERIAL_SCOPE] — standalone, or number of planned installments

## Execution Protocol

1. **Assign baggage unrelated to the plot** — a wound predating page 1 that would exist without this story. For real subjects: locate it in [KNOWN_MATERIAL], never invent trauma.
2. **Define core-with-flexibility**: core principles + the pressure conditions under which each could bend. The reader must later "see how they got there."
3. **Build the value collision**: two good values the story forces into competition (loyalty vs. justice model); the choice reveals the TRUE core.
4. **Motivation-magnitude ledger**: for each act in [PLOT_DEMANDS], write the motivation and verify it matches the act's size. Reader standard: "Would I have made that decision? Maybe not. But I understand why it's plausible."
5. **Imperfection pass**: vulnerable, fragile, uncertain, self-doubting. Design where they go THROUGH the wall — "the real character comes out of how they respond."
6. **Two-layer plan**: plot layer vs. baggage unpeeling. Serial: ration reveals across [SERIAL_SCOPE] (Pine got four books).
7. **Deepen the peripherals**: one humanizing line each for secondary cast — "the more you deepen all of the characters... the more forceful the read."

## Output Contract

- Full dossier: baggage, core + flex conditions, value collision, motivation ledger, imperfection profile
- Two-layer plan with reveal schedule (if serial)
- Peripheral-cast depth notes
- Plausibility verdict per extreme act

## Output Skeleton

```

## CORE WITH FLEXIBILITY

|---|---|---|

## IMPERFECTION PROFILE
[vulnerabilities, doubts, the through-the-wall moment]

## TWO-LAYER PLAN
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Baggage exists independent of this plot?
- [ ] Every extreme act's motivation matches its magnitude?
- [ ] Value collision is between two goods (not good vs. evil)?
- [ ] Character is meaningfully far from perfection?
- [ ] Real-person subjects: nothing invented, all sourced from [KNOWN_MATERIAL]?

## Creative Latitude

The architecture is the floor. Surprising baggage-to-plot resonance (unrelated wound that thematically rhymes with the case), counterintuitive flex conditions, or a peripheral who quietly carries the theme — take those swings. For avatars/brands, translate freely: baggage = identity-level resistance; the collision = the buying decision.

## Deploy When

Designing any lead or series character; founder/creator narrative arcs; ICP avatars needing identity depth; auditing a flat character ("robot walking a plot"); planning multi-installment emotional reveals.
