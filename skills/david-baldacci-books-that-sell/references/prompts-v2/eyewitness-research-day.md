# David Baldacci — Eyewitness Research Day

## Role & Activation

You are executing Baldacci's one-day research method. His bound: "I put Archer to bed and I woke him up in the morning and I took him through one day... clothes, food, how he got from point A to point B, the stores, the streets... That gave me pretty much 90% of the stuff I needed to create that world." Primary artifacts only — the 1958 Esso map bought on Etsy ("my Bible geographically"), the 1953 Vegas dashcam film studied "frame by frame" until "I was an eyewitness... walking down that street." Hardware (do the thing) + software (face-to-face whys; "body language counts... I just assumed everybody was lying to me"). Exit discipline: "You have to leave 99% of it out."

## Input Required

- [SUBJECT] — the world/era/niche/audience to research
- [POV_ENTITY] — the character or avatar whose day is walked
- [DESTINATION] — the work this research feeds (sets what details matter)
- [ARTIFACT_ACCESS] — what primary sources are reachable this run (archives, footage, forums, listings, people, places); flag what isn't

## Execution Protocol

1. **Frame one day**: wake [POV_ENTITY] and walk them to bed — clothes, food, transit, streets, stores, work, money, media, night. No research outside the day's demands.
2. **Consult primary artifacts only** from [ARTIFACT_ACCESS]: period maps, raw footage, archives, the audience's actual forums/reviews/DMs, real listings, real places. Third-hand summaries are contamination — a researcher "could tell me their perspective... totally different from what I would feel important."
3. **Frame-by-frame notes**: granular, unfiltered, noting what draws the eye — that's the differentiated material.
4. **Split hardware/software**: what was done/observed directly vs. what humans said about WHY (Iraq-volunteer standard: get under the decision, not the fact).
5. **Harvest mechanical cowboys**: extract the 3-5 details so specific they buy suspension of disbelief alone (the cigarette-raising robot dismantled for scaring people).
6. **Enforce the 99% cut**: deliverable carries the day-map + planted details; everything else is logged as withheld. Final-prose budget: 3-4 sentences of description per pass.

## Output Contract

- The day-map: hour-by-hour with sourced texture
- Primary-artifact log (only what was ACTUALLY consulted; inaccessible sources flagged, never faked)
- Mechanical-cowboy shortlist (3-5, deployment-ready)
- Withheld note: what was learned and deliberately left out

## Output Skeleton

```
## DAY-MAP — [POV_ENTITY], [setting]
| Hour | What they do | Texture (sourced) |
|---|---|---|

## PRIMARY-ARTIFACT LOG
- [artifact] — [what it yielded] · Not reachable this run: [flagged]

## MECHANICAL COWBOYS
1. [detail] → deploy at [destination point]

## WITHHELD
[one paragraph: the 99% left in the notebook]
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Research bounded to the single day — zero sprawl?
- [ ] Every load-bearing detail traces to a consulted primary artifact?
- [ ] No phantom research — inaccessible sources flagged, not simulated?
- [ ] 3-5 details pass the mechanical-cowboy bar?
- [ ] The 99% cut is explicit?

## Deploy When

Period/setting work; ICP one-day-in-the-life research; client discovery; niche-authority content; local/listing work where lived detail IS the copy.

## Creative Latitude

The day-frame is the floor. Which details qualify as mechanical cowboys is pure taste — favor the odd, human, slightly absurd specifics over the merely accurate. If the artifact trail surfaces a better POV day than planned, switch.
