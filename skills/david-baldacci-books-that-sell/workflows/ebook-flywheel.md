# /baldacci-ebook-flywheel — Zero-to-Sellable Ebook Sprint (Stacking)

**The deployment Farrice named**: "I could go from nothing right now to selling and making a few hundred bucks as soon as tomorrow." This workflow chains Baldacci's craft + business layers over the existing publishing/product machinery.

## Pre-Flight Gate (genius.md Decision Framework)
- Passion tank + knowledge pots: the ebook must sit at the intersection of what Farrice (or the client) knows uniquely AND finds genuinely interesting. Trend-chasing with an empty tank is the named anti-pattern.
- Speed honesty: "tomorrow money" means a SHORT asset done excellently, not a 200-page book done fast.

## Execution
1. **Seed** (`/baldacci-premise-seed`): knowledge-pot inventory → the unanswered question this author can answer → premise with all its food → jacket copy FIRST. The jacket copy is the go/no-go: if it doesn't sell cold, don't write the book.
2. **Scope to short-story weight.** "Would you do an 800-lb squat on your first day?" First product = the 5-page-story equivalent: a tight, single-promise ebook (typically 20-40 pages) that fully pays off its jacket copy.
3. **Draft in the trenches** (`/baldacci-trenches-draft`): linear, chapters as connected short stories, no outline paralysis, speed as the health signal. Pen the ending if stuck; shower; amalgamate.
4. **Open with a Big Pop** (`/baldacci-big-pop`): shatter the buyer's first assumption about the topic in chapter 1 — that's what screenshots, reviews, and word-of-mouth quote.
5. **Edit passes**: `/baldacci-scene-audit` (two-purpose rule per section) + `/baldacci-dialogue-economy` (100→10). Density is the product: "skim at your peril" is a FEATURE buyers feel.
6. **Price with the money-flow map** (`/baldacci-author-economics`): platform royalty tiers → per-unit net → price point. Know exactly what each sale nets before launch.
7. **Launch where the readers are** (`/baldacci-find-your-readers`): channel by audience evidence; listing copy = jacket copy × buyer triggers; design the next-purchase path (this ebook → the next asset/offer) from day one.
8. **Game-film at 7 days** (`/baldacci-game-film`): reader reactions vs. intent; feed corrections into the next asset. The flywheel is the SERIES: baggage-layer continuity across ebooks makes buyers of book 1 buy book 2.
