# /baldacci-find-your-readers — Reader Location & Format Strategy

## Pre-Flight Gate (genius.md Decision Framework)

- Never force a format the audience has left.

## Execution
1. **Locate the readers.** Where does THIS audience actually consume now — audio, ebook, print, threads, video, newsletter? Evidence over habit: the market moved to audio/Spotify while print-first authors weren't looking. List channels by where the audience IS, not where the creator is comfortable.
2. **Separate product from package.** The writing/content never changes for the format; the packaging, marketing, and delivery do. Define the master asset and its format derivatives.
3. **Time the release.** Window analysis: who else launches then (Christmas = heavy hitters; get creamed or move), seasonal fit to the content, the creator's own cadence (Baldacci: spring + fall, 16 years). Pick the window and defend it.
4. **Handle the table stakes.** Metadata, categories, keywords, jacket/listing copy (route to `/baldacci-premise-seed` output), the path from this purchase to the NEXT one.
5. **Install the accountability loop.** Annual/quarterly channel audit using the Baldacci questions: why hasn't the plan changed; why not those outlets; why this date; why the decline; "you're marketing me as X when I'm Y." Calendar it.
6. **Plan the fan-base compounding.** The 10-year truth compressed: say yes to rooms, own your own publicity, build the base that becomes negotiating leverage (feeds `/baldacci-author-economics`).
