# /baldacci-premise-seed — Premise as Plant Food + Jacket Copy

## Pre-Flight Gate (genius.md Decision Framework)
- Has the idea percolated? "Pulled the trigger too soon" = wrote into a wall. If the pull isn't physical yet, hold.
- Passion tank: is Farrice/the author fascinated? If not, no premise engineering saves it.

## Skill Acquisition
- Load `genius.md` (patterns 11, 12; hidden knowledge: Premise as Plant Food; exemplar 2)
- Load `references/source-quotes.md` § Premise & Packaging, § Endings & Process

## Execution
1. **Seed from knowledge pots.** Inventory the 3+ unrelated knowledge stores this author uniquely holds; find the combination "nobody else's pots" produce. Alternatively/additionally: name the big true story and the question it left unanswered ("what do you do with $30 trillion?").
2. **Assemble the premise engine** — the Absolute Power anatomy: **conflict/crime** (the disruption) + **maximal stakes** (highest plausible power/loss level) + **witness/protagonist** (the unlikely person wedged in). Check: does this one paragraph create "every facet you need to write the story"?
3. **Run the plant-food test.** List every thread the full work will need (plot lines, emotional layer, theme). Each must be present or seeded in the premise. A thread with no food in the premise = future page-50 stall.
4. **Layer the zeitgeist.** Why does this matter to a reader's life RIGHT NOW? (Nash Falls: "did everything right and still got knocked on his ass.") Name the current anxiety the premise resonates with.
5. **Distill to jacket copy** — 40-80 words, best face forward, ending on the destabilizing hook ("But one unlikely witness saw everything"). Rewrite until it doubles as the sales asset.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Novel/fiction | Full anatomy as above |
| Nonfiction ebook | Conflict = the reader's unsolved problem; stakes = cost of staying stuck; witness = the author's unlikely vantage |
| Digital product/offer | Premise = offer one-liner; jacket copy = landing-page above-the-fold |
| Content series | Premise = series spine; each episode draws food from it |
| Client brand story | Conflict/stakes/witness mapped onto founder narrative |
