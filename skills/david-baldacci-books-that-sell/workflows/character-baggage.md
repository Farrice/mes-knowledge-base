# /baldacci-character-baggage — Character Baggage Architecture

## Pre-Flight Gate (genius.md Decision Framework)
- Is this character asked to carry a plot? A "robot going through the plot" is the failure state this workflow exists to prevent.
- Series or standalone? Series = ration the baggage unpeeling across installments.

## Skill Acquisition
- Load `genius.md` (patterns 4, 5, 6; hidden knowledge: Baggage Is the Series Fuel)
- Load `references/source-quotes.md` § Character

## Execution
1. **Assign baggage UNRELATED to the plot.** A wound from before page 1 that would exist without this story (Decker: brain trauma erased his personality + lost family; Pine: twin kidnapped at 6). The plot tests the baggage; it doesn't create it.
2. **Define core-with-flexibility.** Who they are, what they stand for — plus the latitude to change. Write the core principles AND the pressure conditions under which each could bend.
3. **Build the value collision.** Two good values the story will force into competition (loyalty vs. justice). The choice under pressure reveals the TRUE core principle.
4. **Run motivation-magnitude matching.** For every extreme act planned: does built motivation equal the act? Reader standard: "Would I have made that decision? Maybe not. But I understand why it's plausible."
5. **Get away from perfection.** Vulnerable, fragile, uncertain, self-doubting. Put them THROUGH the wall, not against it — "the real character comes out of how they respond."
6. **Plan the two layers.** Layer 1: this story's plot. Layer 2: the baggage unpeeling — what gets revealed here, what's held back (series: schedule reveals across books; Pine got four books of unpeeling).
7. **Deepen the peripherals.** Even NPCs get a look, a background beat, a chance to be understood — "it makes a deeper, more forceful read."

## Content Type Adaptations
| Type | Adaptation |

| Fiction lead (series) | Full system; reveal schedule across installments |
| Fiction standalone | Baggage + collision + arc compressed to one book |
| Founder/creator brand | Baggage = the ongoing wound/quest (the subscription engine); posts are plots |

## Output Requirements
- Character dossier: baggage (pre-plot wound), core principles + flex conditions, value collision, motivation ledger for extreme acts
- Two-layer plan: plot layer vs. emotional unpeeling (with reveal schedule if serial)
- Peripheral-cast depth notes (one line each)
