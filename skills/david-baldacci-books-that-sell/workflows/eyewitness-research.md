# /baldacci-eyewitness-research — One-Day Eyewitness Research

## Pre-Flight Gate (genius.md Decision Framework)
- Scope check: this method exists to PREVENT research overwhelm. If the ask is "learn everything about X," reframe to one day-in-the-life first.
- Output destination known? Research feeds a specific work; 99% will be cut. No destination = no research pass.

## Execution
1. **Frame the one day.** Put the character/avatar to bed; wake them up; walk them through ONE full day: clothes, food, transit, streets, stores, work, money, media, night. "That gave me 90% of the stuff I needed."
2. **Go to primary artifacts — never third-hand.** Someone else's interpretation flattens what matters: "I've got to do it all myself." Hunt period maps (the 1958 Esso map from Etsy), raw footage (1953 Vegas dashcam, studied frame by frame), archives, real locations, listings, forums where the audience actually talks.
3. **Split hardware and software.** Hardware = do/observe the thing (ride-alongs, walk the site, use the product). Software = face-to-face conversation about WHY ("you're 18, we're at war — why volunteer?"). Body language counts; assume polish is concealment.
4. **Take frame-by-frame notes.** Granular, unfiltered, your own eye. Note what draws YOUR attention — that's the differentiated material.
5. **Harvest the mechanical cowboys.** From the notes, extract the 3-5 details so specific they buy total suspension of disbelief on their own.
6. **Enforce the 99% cut on exit.** Deliverable carries the day-map + the planted details. Everything else stays in the notebook. Description budget in final prose: 3-4 sentences max per pass.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Period/setting fiction | Full protocol (era maps, footage, artifacts) |
| ICP research | Walk the avatar through one real day; primary artifacts = their actual forums, reviews, DMs, spaces |
| Client discovery | Hardware = use their product/service yourself; software = face-to-face founder/customer interviews |
| Niche content authority | One day inside the niche's actual channels; harvest insider details generics can't fake |
| Real-estate/local (Jen) | Walk the neighborhood day; the mechanical-cowboy details ARE the listing copy |

## Output Requirements
- The day-map: the character/avatar's single day, hour by hour, with sourced texture
- Primary-artifact log: what was consulted, firsthand
- The planted-detail shortlist (3-5 mechanical cowboys) ready for deployment
- Cut confirmation: what was learned and deliberately left out
