# /baldacci-stakes-forge — Stakes & Zeitgeist Architecture

## Pre-Flight Gate (genius.md Decision Framework)
- Character/entity built? Stakes only weigh what the reader already cares about.
- Honest resonance available? Zeitgeist layering must be real ("I feel like a lot of people felt like they'd done all the right things and still got knocked on their butt") — never bolted-on topicality.

## Execution
1. **Design the Faustian choice.** The Nash Falls template: both options cost everything ("One, you work with us and you lose everything you have. Two, you don't and you lose everything you have"). No clean exit. Subtle stakes work the same way — family, career, identity — the trap just closes quieter.
2. **Make consequences real and owed.** Decisions → severe consequences → accountability → suffering through them. "If you don't have those, what does it really matter whether someone makes a decision or not?"
3. **Match magnitude.** Stakes size must fit the world and the character's motivation (the plausibility law). A meteor isn't required; losing the American dream is a meteor to the person living it.
4. **Layer the zeitgeist.** Name the current collective anxiety this maps to (Nash: "you did everything right and it was ripped apart, having nothing to do with you at all" — the AI-displacement era). The reader test: "This matters for my life right now. I might make different decisions after reading this."
5. **Universalize through family/identity.** "All families are dysfunctional... that stuff really matters." The most transferable stakes live where readers already have scar tissue — siblings, parents, the first-18-years imprint.

## Output Requirements

- Magnitude check: motivation vs. act table for the decisive moves
- Zeitgeist line: the named current anxiety + why this audience feels it now
- The stakes rendered in prose where a draft exists (not just the schematic)
