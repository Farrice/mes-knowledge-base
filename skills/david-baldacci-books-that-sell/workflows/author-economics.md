# /baldacci-author-economics — The Author-as-Partner Deal Pass

## Pre-Flight Gate (genius.md Decision Framework)
- Real numbers available? This workflow runs on actual money flows, never estimates presented as facts. Missing numbers get labeled UNCONFIRMED and listed as questions to ask.
- Leverage stage honest? Pre-audience creators get the leverage-building plan, not the renegotiation script — sequence is non-skippable.

## Execution
1. **Map the money flow end-to-end.** Retail price → who receives what at each hop → creator's actual per-unit net. Expose the royalty illusion (15% of list = ~30% of publisher net; they keep ~70%). Advance/threshold mechanics included: what must earn out before anything flows?
2. **Score against the mantra.** "No one should make more off the book than the person who wrote it." Who currently makes more? By how much?
3. **Locate the leverage stage.** Fan base size, track record, replaceability. Pre-leverage → output is the leverage-building plan (build audience, say yes to rooms, own your publicity — the 10-year compression). Post-leverage → proceed to raise.
4. **Design the raise.** The partnership reframe: "We're not doing royalties anymore. You get a [X]% return on your money; I get the rest." Work the vectors: contract scope (books/deliverables per term), advance vs. back-end, genre/product-adjusted pricing, release timing, positioning. "They have no chance to agree to it if you never raise it."
5. **Write the accountability questions.** The annual distributor audit: why hasn't the marketing plan changed; why am I not in those outlets; why this date; why the decline. Questions the creator "didn't know to ask" are how careers go down the toilet.
6. **Check the partnership balance.** Reciprocity plan: what the creator gives back (the go-to-Iowa clause). Fairness protects the prose.
