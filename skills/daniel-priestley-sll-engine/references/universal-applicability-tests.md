# Universal Applicability Acceptance Tests

These tests prevent the SLL engine from drifting back into a fitness, coaching, founder-brand, or single-platform template. They test the stable kernel while allowing domain-specific adapters.

## Required Inputs

Every valid run must identify:

1. stakeholder or buyer;
2. desired change or prize;
3. current pain;
4. obstacle or problem;
5. credible proof source;
6. next measurable commitment;
7. owner and response time for that commitment.

If one is absent, mark it as missing or hypothesis-grade. Never invent it.

## Cross-Domain Matrix

| Domain | Buyer or stakeholder | Proof form | Next commitment | Valid adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B2B service | Operations leader | Before/after workflow evidence | Diagnostic or fit call | Standard LAPS |
| Ecommerce | Shopper with a decision problem | Product test, review, demonstration | Guide, quiz, sample, purchase | “Process” becomes selection and use |
| SaaS | Functional user and economic buyer | Usage evidence, case study, benchmark | Trial, demo, activation | Separate user pain from buying obstacle |
| Local professional practice | Local client with a consequential need | Credentials, permissioned case, public guidance | Assessment or consultation | Add jurisdiction and claims checks |
| Nonprofit/community | Donor, volunteer, attendee, or beneficiary | Outcome evidence and transparent reporting | Donate, attend, volunteer, subscribe | Replace sale with named commitment |
| Internal initiative | Employee or executive stakeholder | Pilot result, adoption data, operational evidence | Apply, enroll, approve, activate | Replace public platform with internal channel |

## Negative Controls

Reject or repair an output when:

- it mentions training, coaching, strength, health, founders, or LinkedIn without those appearing in the inputs;
- “process” defaults to coaching calls when the business delivers a product, software, event, practice, or internal program;
- the CTA defaults to “book a call” when a lower-friction or non-sales commitment fits better;
- testimonials, credentials, customer counts, outcomes, or trends are invented;
- engagement metrics substitute for the named commitment;
- the workflow is used for an operational task with no demand-generation job.

## Pass Standard

Run workflows 01–05 conceptually against at least three unlike rows in the matrix. A pass requires:

- all required output sections remain meaningful;
- no unprompted domain leakage;
- the same Short → Long → Lead kernel remains visible;
- proof and CTA adapt without changing factual or permission boundaries;
- workflow 05 measures the chosen commitment rather than likes alone.
