## Cold-Start Domain Runs

### B2B workflow consultancy

- **Buyer:** operations leader managing a fragile manual handoff
- **Pain / Prize / Problem:** reconciliation burden / reliable flow / proposals that replace the stack before diagnosing the handoff
- **Proof:** measured before-and-after steps with human-review boundaries
- **Long-form process:** map, prototype, acceptance-test, hand off
- **Lead commitment:** Workflow Friction Score, followed by a bounded fit call
- **Audit signal:** qualified assessments and held calls, not post engagement
- **Verdict:** PASS. All five workflow outputs remain meaningful without coaching language.

### Independent ecommerce brand

- **Buyer:** shopper who cannot judge fragrance strength online
- **Pain / Prize / Problem:** overpowering product / right atmosphere / poetic product names hide decision information
- **Proof:** room-size product test, verified reviews, demonstration
- **Long-form process:** select by room and sensitivity, use safely, adjust placement
- **Lead commitment:** scent-strength guide or quiz; purchase remains downstream
- **Audit signal:** guide completions, quiz completions, and attributed purchases
- **Verdict:** PASS. “Process” adapts to product selection and use; the CTA does not default to a sales call.

### Community nonprofit

- **Stakeholder:** local resident who wants to help but cannot commit every week
- **Pain / Prize / Problem:** concern without a clear entry point / visible neighborhood result / volunteering framed as an indefinite obligation
- **Proof:** permissioned outcome story plus transparent project reporting
- **Long-form process:** choose a one-day project, complete orientation, attend, see the outcome report
- **Lead commitment:** register for one event or sponsor one project
- **Audit signal:** registrations, attendance, repeat participation, and donations
- **Verdict:** PASS WITH ADAPTATION. LAPS becomes commitment follow-up, and the adaptation must be labeled because the objective is participation rather than a commercial sale.

## Boundary

The system is universal across **demand-generation contexts**, not every business function. It should route out when the task is product engineering, finance, hiring operations, fulfillment, or support and there is no content-to-commitment job. This boundary keeps the framework useful instead of turning it into a vague universal hammer.
