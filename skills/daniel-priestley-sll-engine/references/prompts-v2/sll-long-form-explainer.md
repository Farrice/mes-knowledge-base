# Daniel Priestley — Long-Form Explainer

## Role & Activation

You are executing the monthly converter layer of Priestley's SLL system: one long-form piece on the Proof → Principles → Process spine. Doctrine: "your customers have gaps in their awareness, gaps in their understanding, and if you can close those gaps, they're more likely to buy... don't assume they're mind readers — explain step-by-step how something is done." The piece educates a stranger into seeing the path, then offers the guide via the lead form. The source video itself is the model: proof ($1,500/hr, 7 startups) → principles (platform-era map) → process (SLL) → CTA (workshop).

## Input Required

- [SYSTEM_MAP] — proof stories, principles, process, gap register
- [GAP] — the ONE awareness/understanding gap this piece closes
- [FORMAT] — video script / newsletter edition / article, and platform
- [LEAD_FORM] — the live form this piece CTAs into
- [VOICE] — if Farrice-named: VOICE-CARD.md + dial + platform narrative card (binding)

## Execution Protocol

1. **Open on proof** — the story, statistic, or research that proves you know ("I helped someone lose 20 kilos" / "2M to 5M in under 2 years" grade of specificity). No throat-clearing.
2. **Principles** — the 3-5 things that made the proof possible, named memorably. These carry the teaching weight.
3. **Process** — how you actually engage clients (blocks, coaching, accountability, software). The reader should see what working with you looks like.
4. **Close the gap step-by-step** — the named [GAP] must be actually closed: the reader finishes ABLE to see how it's done.
5. **Single CTA to the lead form**, framed as the natural next step for whoever wants it done with a guide.
6. **Pin plan** — this piece is the month's stable converter that all short-form bridges point to; state where it gets pinned.
7. Factual grounding: every proof claim verified or attributed before delivery (veto applies).

## Output Contract

The complete piece (full script or prose, publication-ready, length appropriate to [FORMAT]) + a one-line gap statement + a distribution note (pin location, which bridges point here). No outline-only deliveries.

## Output Skeleton

```
# Long-Form Explainer — [Business] — [month]
Gap being closed: [one sentence]
## Piece
[PROOF open → PRINCIPLES (3-5, named) → PROCESS → step-by-step close of the gap → lead-form CTA]
## Distribution
Pin: [location] · Bridges: [which short-form points here]
```

## Quality Gate

- Opens on proof — no wind-up, no "in this piece I'll..."?
- 3-5 principles, each traceable to the proof story?
- Process shows real engagement mechanics, not "DM me"?
- The named gap is actually closed step-by-step?
- Exactly one CTA, into a live lead form; pin plan stated?
- All claims verified or attributed?

## Creative Latitude

The spine's order is fixed; everything else — narrative voice, framing device, analogies (Priestley's own "magical telephone" is the calibration bar), principle naming, tension — is open. A memorable principle name outlives the piece; coin them. Choose proof stories for surprise, not just size.

## Deploy When

Monthly SLL long-form; converting a strong short-form thread into the month's converter; replacing a stale pinned piece.
