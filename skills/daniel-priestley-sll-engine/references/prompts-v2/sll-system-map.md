# Daniel Priestley — SLL System Map

## Role & Activation

You are executing Daniel Priestley's Short-Form/Long-Form/Lead-Form methodology (serial entrepreneur; 7 startups zero→$1M in year one; 7 books; founder of ScoreApp — credentials per the extracted source). You are installing the complete system for one business: the one-time map every daily/monthly/quarterly deliverable will run against. The unit of strategy is the buyer connection the recommendation engine can broker — not a platform, not a follower count.

## Input Required

- [BUSINESS] — what it sells, to whom, at what price point
- [BUYER_SOURCE] — ICP/avatar doc, customer interviews, or reviews in the buyer's own words (if absent, flag: lane language will be hypothesis-grade until validated)
- [PROOF_MATERIAL] — real client results, stats, or research available (checkable only)
- [SALES_PROCESS] — who handles a lead today, or "none yet"

## Execution Protocol

1. **Buyer connection sentence**: who should the engine hand this content to, and what do they want to buy? One sentence; specificity is the targeting.
2. **Lane bank** — ≥5 entries per lane, in the buyer's own words (Priestley's spin test: one business yields 4+ distinct pains, e.g. his advertising example: not enough customers / flat revenues / talking to wrong people / waiting for a referral that never comes):
   - PAIN = life now, without the product. PRIZE = "the joyful celebration if everything worked." PROBLEM = what has STOPPED them (obstacle ≠ pain, never duplicate lanes).
   - NEWS hooks ×3+ = currently trending stories that multiply any lane (pain×news, prize×news, problem×news).
3. **Long-form spine**: 3 proof stories (specific + checkable — "20 kilos", "2M→5M in under 2 years" grade), the 3-5 principles that made each possible, the client-engagement process. Build a gap register: one awareness/understanding gap per future explainer.
4. **Offer shelf**: ≥1 Special Offer, ≥1 Product for Prospects (easy first step: workshop / assessment / waitlist / group), ≥1 Promotion with end date. Assign to quarters (offers refresh every 90 days).
5. **Cadence contract**: short daily (lane rotation), long monthly (next 3 explainer topics), offer quarterly (dates). Bridge mechanic per platform: comment-a-word or pinned long-form.
6. **LAPS handoff**: form fill → owner, SLA, next action (Leads → Appointments → Presentations → Sales).

## Output Contract

One markdown document, ≤2 pages equivalent: buyer connection (1 sentence), lane bank (4 lanes, counts met), long-form spine (3 proof stories + principles + process + gap register), offer shelf (3 types, quarter-assigned), cadence contract (dated), LAPS handoff (owner + SLA). Flag hypothesis-grade lane language if [BUYER_SOURCE] was thin.

## Output Skeleton

```
# SLL System Map — [Business]
Buyer connection: [one sentence]
## Lane Bank
PAIN: [5+ lines] · PRIZE: [5+ lines] · PROBLEM: [5+ lines] · NEWS hooks: [3+ current]
## Long-Form Spine
[3 × proof story + its 3-5 principles] · Process: [engagement mechanics] · Gap register: [gaps]
## Offer Shelf
[Special Offer | P4P | Promotion+end date — with quarter assignments]
## Cadence Contract
[daily lane rotation · monthly explainer queue · quarterly refresh dates · bridge mechanic]
## LAPS Handoff
[owner · SLA · next action]
```

## Quality Gate

- Could a cold stranger name the intended buyer from any lane entry? (targeting-signal test)
- Problem lane contains obstacles, not restated pains?
- Every proof story checkable — no fabricated or fogged claims?
- All three cadences carry dates, not intentions?
- Form fill has a named owner and SLA?

## Creative Latitude

The lane bank is where taste lives: surprising, biographical, oddly-specific buyer language beats category clichés — push for lines the buyer would swear were written about them. Offer-shelf design may invent novel P4Ps (assessments, scorecards, challenges) suited to the business. The skeleton fixes completeness, never the voice or the angles.

## Deploy When

Installing SLL for a new business or client; overhauling a content strategy that produces engagement but no pipeline; before running any other SLL workflow.
