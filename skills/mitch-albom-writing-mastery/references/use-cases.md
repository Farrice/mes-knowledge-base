# Use Cases — Mitch Albom Writing Mastery

> Specific deployment scenarios mapped to the matching `albom-*` (and companion) workflow. Find the row that matches what's in front of you, run the workflow, load [genius.md](../genius.md) first. The 13 workflows live across three tiers: Foundation (architecture), Practitioner (technique), and Cross-Domain (commercial/social formats). When a scenario spans tiers, the **Run order** column shows the chain.

The single qualifying question for *any* Albom deployment: **does this piece need to move a reader, not just inform or impress one?** If yes, there's an Albom workflow for it. If the job is purely transactional/informational with no human truth at stake, route elsewhere — Albom's engine is overkill and will read as forced sentiment.

## Practitioner — Technique Scenarios

| You're facing this... | Run this | Why this one | Run order / stacks with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Building a **personal brand voice** or ghostwriting for a client who needs a soulful, humble, long-horizon presence | `/signature-voice-legacy-identity` | Produces the full Voice Brand Guide: voice-DNA, gravedigger framework + anchor images, emotional-architecture map, legacy sample passages, 98%-for-readers checklist. | Stacks with `ghostwrite`, `ghostwriting-voice`, `voice-document`, `oren-brand` |
| A **big, important topic** that everyone is covering the obvious way, and your draft reads grand/declarative/abstract | `/albom-gravedigger-angle` | Finds the one peripheral figure whose small story carries the whole truth — so the reader feels the theme more than the grand take ever could. | After `/albom-theme-first-engine`; stacks with `wright-thompson-mastery`, `story-compass` |
| A draft about **death / grief / love / loss is trying too hard** — stacked adjectives, narrator telling us how sad it is, flowery phrases | `/albom-restraint-pass` | The *Tuesdays with Morrie* move: cut the reached-for feeling back to what-happened; say the one point in one sentence; re-earn the feeling by withholding it. | Emotion in the piece reads as **undifferentiated "sad"**; or a love story only covers the meet-cute | `/albom-loss-architecture` | Classifies the exact loss — life / love-while-present / path-not-taken — and picks the right yearning + images, and the flame choice (combustion vs nurturing). | Stacks with `/albom-restraint-pass`, `character-psych`, `eric-roth-screenwriting-mastery` |
| **Multi-thread / multi-timeline / multi-voice** work that drags or feels monotone | `/albom-pin-and-crosscut` | Pins charged threads, cuts away leaving the reader impatient, ties each cut to the next scene, places breathers, distributes the crescendo. | Stacks with `/stanton-anticipation-uncertainty`, `runia-anticipation`, `serial-arc` |
| A **heavy run** where the reader is getting overwhelmed and needs an exhale; or a piece that **back-loads all its payoff** | `/albom-pin-and-crosscut` | Counter-Action Heartbeat pacing (Pattern 21) — the White-Tower breather where the heartbeat spikes; never peak too soon, never hoard the finale. Fixes AN-7. | Stacks with `universal-narrative-blueprint` |

## Multi-Workflow Chains (common real jobs)

**Job: A Substack/LinkedIn post that must genuinely move people, from a raw memory or news clipping.**
`/albom-theme-first-engine` (lock the truth + ending) → `/albom-gravedigger-angle` (find the margin figure) → `/albom-content-emotion` (build the post) → `/albom-meaning-hook` (sharpen the open) → `/albom-restraint-pass` (strip reached-for feeling). Then route through `really-real-social` / `linkedin-daily` for platform fit.

**Job: A long-form narrative (book / memoir / big essay), zero to publication-ready.**
`/albom-theme-first-engine` → `/universal-narrative-blueprint` (full architecture) → `/albom-loss-architecture` + `/albom-pin-and-crosscut` (tune emotional core + pacing) → draft (with `eric-roth-screenwriting-mastery` / `roth-visual-prose` writing scene & line inside the theme) → `/high-density-manuscript-polish` + `/albom-restraint-pass` (final cut so it cuts the flesh).

**Job: A product launch that connects emotionally rather than transactionally.**
`/albom-theme-first-engine` (campaign theme + tether) → `/albom-brand-story` (origin spine, if needed) → `/story-driven-marketing-engine` (full funnel) → `/albom-email-narrative` (nurture/launch sequence) → `copy-engine` / `luke-iha-vsl-leads` grounds and writes the proof/mechanism/offer *inside* the emotional spine.

**Job: A founder/personal brand voice for the long horizon (e.g., a client's authority presence).**
`/signature-voice-legacy-identity` (Voice Brand Guide) → `/albom-brand-story` (the origin that carries the voice) → `ghostwrite` / `ghostwriting-voice` produce in the matched cadence. Read every important draft aloud (the wife-around-the-corner test) before ship.

## When NOT to Use Albom (route elsewhere)

- **Pure information / reference / spec** with no human truth at stake → the Albom engine will manufacture forced sentiment (AN-1). Use a plain informational workflow.
- **A story doesn't even exist yet** (no want → tension → change) → gate it through `runia-story-test` *first*; Albom decides the *truth* a story serves, not whether one exists.
- **The premise/clamp needs auditing for focus or "looking away"** → that's `/stanton-premise-sentence` / `/stanton-clamp-audit`. Albom owns the *destination* (the one human truth), not the focus litmus.
- **The job is structural inevitability of an ending for plot reasons** → `/hawley-theme-engine` / `hawley-ending-first`. Albom locks the ending so the whole piece is a run-up to one *earned feeling*, a different question.
- **Already-elevated content that's working** → do NOT rebuild it through Albom (or any engine). Preserve the spine; run at most a surgical `/albom-restraint-pass` on a single high-feeling section. Rebuilding good work degrades it.

## The Bar (applies to every use case)

Not "what an artist with words" — Albom rejects that goal. The bar is **"I couldn't stop thinking about it — it made me think about my own life."** Whatever workflow you ran, before you ship, ask: will the reader think about *their own life*, or admire the writing? If "admire the writing," the theme isn't universal enough or the voice is performing — re-run `/albom-theme-first-engine` and reselect.
