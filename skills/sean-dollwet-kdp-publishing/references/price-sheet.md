# The KDP Price & Economics Sheet

Every figure frame-verified across the five Dollwet source videos. Use this to populate any DIY-vs-outsource decision, royalty projection, or portfolio math. The governing doctrine: every step is a **time-or-money trade** — DIY to save money, outsource to save time; "the more money you invest, typically the more time you save."

## Launch pricing (price low to harvest proof, then raise)

- **Ebook: $0.99**
- **Paperback: $5.99–$6.99**
- Raise price only after sales and reviews accumulate. Early goal is copies + reviews + social proof, not margin.

## Book spec (production targets)

- High-content nonfiction: **30,000 words · 5x8–6x9 trim · ~150–200 pages.**
- First book / minimum viable: **10,000–15,000 words.**
- Low/medium content: model competitors' trim + page count (e.g. coloring 8.5x8.5, ~80pp), then **offer slightly more** (40 pages → 45).
- Value = highest value *per page*, never page count.

## Platform facts

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Amazon US book market share | **~74–78%** (book units ~74% vs Apple 11%; eBook units 78% vs Apple 11%, B&N 8%, Kobo 3%, Google 2%) |
| Marketplaces / reach | **19+ marketplaces / 19+ countries**, print-on-demand |
| KDP signup cost | **$0** — no monthly or signup fee; Amazon takes a cut only after a sale clears (zero downside cost) |
| Upload → live | **1–3 days** for Amazon review/approval |
| Fulfillment | Print-on-demand: Amazon prints and ships only after a sale — no inventory, no shipping, no customer service |
| Formats from one upload | ebook, paperback, hardcover, audiobook |

## Portfolio & exit math

- **The $3–5k/mo target:** at the honest median of $300–400/book/month, a portfolio of **8–12 books ≈ $3,000–$4,000+/month.**
- **The ladder:** master the single-book pipeline to $1,000/mo, then "rinse and repeat" — $1k → $10k → $100k is duplication of a proven unit, not new tactics. Plant flags across **2–5 niches in 6 months.**
- **The exit (catalog as sellable asset):** Dollwet sold his first KDP account for **$820,000** on Empire Flippers (Aug 24 2021, Listing #32231). Live account proof: **$526,055.73 across 364 books** (Dec 2021–Jun 2026) and **$495,736.13 across 183 books** (Dec 2021–Feb 2026). A KDP catalog lists on Empire Flippers for six figures routinely — the endgame is equity, not just royalties.

## Review-acquisition economics

- **Book Reverb:** bid-based from ~$6/review, fast, mostly *unverified* (reader gets a PDF, not an Amazon purchase); now also does audiobook reviews.
- **Book Bounty:** ~$25/mo points system, triangulated (A→B→C→A, never a direct swap), can request *verified-purchase* reviews and target specific marketplaces (e.g., Amazon Germany).
- **Free lane:** niche Facebook-group script (see prompt-chain / launch workflows) — costs time, not money.
- Gate: **minimum 15 reviews before any marketing**; target 50–100 honest reviews in the first 90 days.

## Coaching / self-investment (his own path, disclosed honestly)

Course → first dollar → **$6,000 for 10 coaching sessions ($600/hr, "a little too much")** → first $10k month in ~10 months → sold that account for $820,000. His advice: start with a structured course, add a coach who can see your actual books once earning, don't over-invest before your first dollar.
