Checked 2026-08-04. Re-check current KDP pages and obtain fresh quotes before a spend or upload. This file separates official rules, source-reported examples, and Book One assumptions.

## Book One economics row

Record:

`format, marketplace, list_price, royalty_option, gross_royalty_formula, delivery_or_print_cost, tax_assumption, refund_assumption, editing_cost, design_cost, tool_cost, marketing_cost, time_cost, net_per_unit_estimate, evidence_date, official_source, uncertainty`

Before a sale, every result is `UNTESTED`. After a sale, distinguish gross royalty, direct costs, allocated costs, refunds, taxes, and `NET_COLLECTED` cash.

## Zero-budget pilot rule

Book One uses operator time and current workspace tools. Paid ads, review services, vendors, premium software, and KDP Select enrollment are parked. If a later stage needs spend, gather current quotes and stop for approval.

## Pricing hypothesis

Choose price from:

- current KDP eligibility and royalty rules;
- delivery or print cost;
- category price range for comparable reader value;
- format and marketplace;
- reader promise and sample quality;
- tax/refund assumptions;
- explicit conversion and net-cash test.

No review target or low-price period is a prerequisite for organic marketing.

## Review economics boundary

The approved review budget is zero. No money, points, gift cards, reciprocity, close relationships, sentiment filtering, or other value may be tied to a review. A free/discounted copy is allowed only when a review is neither required nor influenced. See [KDP Customer Reviews](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202101910).

## Format expansion

Print, audio, translation, bundle, and wide distribution each require new current cost, rights, quality, reader-demand, and KDP Select compatibility evidence. A surface becomes an income stream only after `NET_COLLECTED`.
