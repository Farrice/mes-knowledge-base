# Organic Book Content — Source Taxonomy and Test Rules

Primary source: [Sean Dollwet's no-paid-ads video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CZgZmPd-ETY). View counts, conversion claims, channel rankings, and case-study revenue are `SOURCE_REPORTED`; they show what to test, not what Book One will reproduce.

## Seven source-reported format ideas

| # | Format | Core composition | Best-fit hypothesis |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | No-talk showcase | Music/ambient sound, text, cover/interior | Visual or calming topic; privacy-first |
| 2 | Talking-head lesson | One useful concept, natural book reference | Operator has credible teaching material |
| 3 | Behind-camera book demo | Hold/show the book and explain one use | Physical book or proof copy exists |
| 4 | Page flip-through | Show interior and narrate | Journal, workbook, illustrated book |
| 5 | One-page lesson | Teach one approved page/tool | Useful nonfiction, prompt, exercise |
| 6 | Process video | Coloring, worksheet, design, annotation | The process itself is visually useful |
| 7 | Image/carousel explainer | Rights-cleared visuals plus numbered ideas | Faceless and digitally native |

These are optional ingredients. The target reader's current attention behavior decides channel and format.

## Safe content rules

- Use only approved, verified, rights-cleared book material.
- Do not expose private interview material or unpublished sensitive sources.
- Do not use shame, diagnosis, trauma, health, money, or identity claims merely for clicks.
- Do not manufacture personal results, reader outcomes, reviews, or urgency.
- Add new value when repeating a format; avoid recycled near-duplicates.
- Posting and outreach are external actions and need approval.

## Explore/exploit loop

Start with three genuinely different format hypotheses. Record audience tension, useful idea, hook, asset source, channel, effort, approval, and attribution tag. Repeat an observed winner only with a new example, tool, question, or reader job. Retire a format when its predefined test window ends without the intended signal.

## Measurement ladder

`POSTED → REACH OBSERVED → ENGAGEMENT OBSERVED → DISCOVERY OBSERVED → SALE ATTRIBUTED → NET COLLECTED`

Each arrow needs evidence. Views are not Amazon demand; an Amazon search is not a sale; a royalty dashboard is not net cash.

## Repurposing

Repurpose when the target reader, format conventions, rights, and incremental effort still fit the next channel. Reposting is not assumed free, and TikTok is not automatically first.

## Book One default

Organic only, no paid ads. Produce a thirty-experiment backlog but do not publish it until the listing and content assets are approved.
