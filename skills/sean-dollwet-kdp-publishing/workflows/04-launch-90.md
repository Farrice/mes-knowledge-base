# Launch 90 — Honeymoon + 90-Day Review Sprint

## Pre-Flight Gate

Run this the moment a manuscript is uploaded and live — uploading is not launching. Amazon gives new books extra visibility in the first **30 days** (the honeymoon) and rewards velocity across the first **90 days** — an audition you actively pass, not a passive grace period (Pattern 13, Hidden Knowledge 9). Reviews come FIRST because they remove the buyer's purchase hesitation; a zero-review book barely sells.

Run this when: the book is published and you need the review + pricing + ads sequence.

Do NOT: market before the review floor, arrange direct swaps, or use close friends/family (KDP-prohibited). Anti-patterns killed (genius.md): **no-review launch** (wasting the honeymoon with upload-and-pray), **direct review swaps / family reviews**, **assuming reviews are unbeatable** (the "3,000 reviews" paralysis is your opening). Sequencing law (Rubric 7): validate → build → **15 reviews → market** → multiply. Nothing markets before reviews.

## Execution

### Step 1 — Work the 30-day honeymoon: reviews FIRST
Amazon boosts new books for ~30 days; the goal is to enter that window already accumulating social proof. **Hard gate: minimum 15 reviews before any marketing spend.** Target **10–15 reviews in the honeymoon month**, all honest. Never close friends or family (prohibited); use acquaintances and friend-of-a-friend, not your inner circle.

**Free lane — the verbatim Facebook-group script (deep-notes-qn6VasGSexI):**
1. Search the book's keyword → **Groups** → join active groups (many members, many daily posts).
2. Engage genuinely for a while first — don't cold-drop.
3. Post the ask, verbatim: *"Hey guys, I just published a book on [topic]. I would love to give out a free copy of my book in exchange for your honest feedback. Who's interested?"*
4. DM each commenter a **PDF** of the book.
5. Follow up **1–2 weeks later**: if they liked it, ask for an Amazon review and send the listing link.
Costs time, not money (Pattern 17).

**Paid lane — triangulated services (never direct swaps, Hidden Knowledge 2):**
- **Book Reverb:** bid-based from **~$6/review**, fast; mostly *unverified* (reader gets a PDF, not an Amazon purchase); also does audiobook reviews. Budget math = target reviews × bid.
- **Book Bounty:** **~$25/mo** points system, triangulated (A→B→C→A — no two authors ever review each other, so it stays inside KDP rules); can request **verified-purchase** reviews and target specific marketplaces (e.g., Amazon Germany for a German edition).
State the time-or-money trade explicitly and pick by budget/speed.

### Step 2 — Set launch pricing (price low to harvest proof)
The early goal is copies + reviews + social proof, not margin (Pattern 15):
- **Ebook: $0.99**
- **Paperback: $5.99–$6.99**
Raise price only after sales and reviews accumulate. If you ever price above category norm, the listing must *visibly* convey the extra value (stronger cover, denser subtitle, more reviews than page-one peers — Hidden Knowledge 3) or it won't sell.

### Step 3 — Run $3–5/day Amazon ads as DIAGNOSTIC data
Only after the 15-review floor is cleared. A tiny ad budget is not for profit — it's a controlled test isolating where the funnel leaks (Pattern 14):
- Run Amazon Ads at **$3–5/day** on the book's keyword set.
- **Read impressions vs. clicks.** High impressions + low clicks = the *listing* is the problem (cover, title, or reviews) — go fix cover/title/reviews, not the budget. (Cross-check: is the target keyword actually in the title/subtitle? Does a subtitle exist? Is the book ranking #1 for a keyword nobody buys — a demand failure, not a marketing failure?)
- If it's **converting**, scale budget — but only up from a demonstrated profitable baseline.
Every self-driven sale is also an algorithm deposit that triggers Amazon's organic push.

### Step 4 — Sprint the 90-day review window (50–100 reviews)
Amazon rewards momentum across the first 90 days, so keep the review engine running past the honeymoon: **sprint to 50–100 honest reviews in the first 90 days** using the same free + triangulated sources. This is the **door-close mechanic** (window-thesis.md): you already beat incumbents on cover + title + content (workflow 02, Pattern 12); now stacking reviews fast means you hold BOTH quality and social proof — "no weak spot to attack," and the same trick can't be run on you. "AI gives you the quality. Speed gives you the reviews… The door closes behind you." Miss this window and quality alone can't dislodge entrenched positions later.

### Step 5 — Hand off to the organic layer
Once the review base is building and the listing is diagnosed clean, the durable free-traffic engine is high-volume short-form video (the volume game, faceless-preferred). Point to **workflow 06** (organic marketing) for the 7-type video taxonomy, the clone-the-winner cadence, and the 1-page = 1-video content math. Anchor all expectations to the honest median: **$300–$400 per book per month** (Hidden Knowledge 13) — no revenue promises.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Content type | Review sourcing | Pricing | Ads diagnostic |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High-content nonfiction** | Facebook-group script + Book Bounty verified; also audiobook reviews via Book Reverb | $0.99 ebook / $5.99–6.99 paperback | Full impressions-vs-clicks read; unlocks audiobook stream later |
| **Low-content journals** | PDF-share harder (visual product) — favor Book Bounty verified-purchase + niche groups | Paperback-led; model category norm | Same $3–5/day diagnostic |
| **Medium-content coloring/activity** | Reviews on finished physical copy; niche groups + Book Bounty | Model niche ceiling (coloring ~$9.99) | Diagnostic still applies; cover trend matters most |
| **Sprint book** | Same 15-review gate — no shortcut | Same launch pricing | Same |
