# Outsource Desk — Buy a Bounded Result, Keep the Evidence

## Pre-Flight Gate

Book One is zero-budget by default. Use this workflow only after a separate spend decision or to prepare future briefs. Review acquisition services are excluded; current review policy has no points, compensation, or triangulation loophole.

## Execution

For editing, fact review, sensitivity/qualified review, formatting, cover design, illustration, narration, or translation:

1. Name the approved input artifact and the one result the vendor owns.
2. Set scope, deadline, revision count, budget ceiling, and acceptance tests.
3. Require confidentiality and deletion/return treatment for private inputs.
4. Require work-for-hire/assignment or an explicit license covering formats, territories, term, commercial use, modifications, and attribution.
5. Require source files, font/stock/license receipts, and AI tool/asset disclosure.
6. Prohibit plagiarism, competitor imitation, fabricated sources, undisclosed subcontracting, and unauthorized AI training/reuse.
7. Keep final approval with Farrice; a vendor delivery is `DRAFT`, not `PASS`.

## Output Requirements

- DIY versus vendor decision with real current quotes or `UNCONFIRMED` estimates.
- One bounded brief per selected vendor role.
- Rights, privacy, AI, source-file, and acceptance clauses.
- Review checklist and rejection/remediation path.
- Spend and external-contact approval boundary.

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Review acquisition is not outsourced or purchased.
- [ ] Every brief has one owner, one result, and measurable acceptance tests.
- [ ] Rights, confidentiality, AI disclosure, source files, and licenses are explicit.
- [ ] Prices are current evidence or labeled unconfirmed.
- [ ] No vendor is contacted and no money is spent without approval.
