# Outsource Desk — Time-or-Money Decision + Vendor Briefs

## Pre-Flight Gate

Run this when the operator needs to decide **who does the work** — DIY-free or paid-outsource — for one or more production steps (cover, interior/writing, formatting, coloring, narration, reviews), and wants briefs ready to send.

Do NOT run this when:
- The topic isn't validated. Never spend on a book whose demand isn't proven (Anti-Pattern: write-first-validate-never). Route to `01-validate-book-topic`.
- The operator wants to outsource *everything* pre-first-dollar. Dollwet's own disclosed path: don't over-invest before the first sale (price-sheet, coaching note). Flag it, then apply the beginner-default lanes below.

Governing doctrine (Pattern 17): **you are always paying money to save time, or paying time to save money — pick the lane deliberately, never drift.** "The more money you invest, typically the more time you save." Every step has a free path and a cheap-outsource path; the desk's job is to make each choice a *stated decision that names the cost of the other lane*, not an accident.

## Execution

### Step 1 — Fix the operator's default lane
Two postures, two defaults:
- **Beginner-default (pre- or near-first-dollar):** DIY-free wherever the work is learnable and the cost is only tedium (Pattern 16 — the tedious steps are the differentiation). Outsource only the one step that most blocks shipping. Do not over-invest before the first sale.
- **Scaling-default (proven unit, replicating across niches):** outsource to buy time; run both organic and paid lanes; a ghostwriter or full-book gig is justified because you're duplicating a proven earner ($1k→$10k→$100k is duplication, not new tactics). "If scaling seriously, run both lanes."

### Step 2 — Assign a lane per production step (the decision matrix)
For each step, present both lanes with real prices, and make the call by the operator's posture, budget, and speed need. Name the cost of the lane not chosen.

| Step | DIY-free lane (time) | Outsource lane (money) | Notes |
|------|----------------------|------------------------|-------|
| **Cover** | Canva or an image model (ChatGPT/GPT-Image, Gemini — never Claude for images) | **$10–$20 budget → more at scale**; Fiverr (real gig: Lesia S, 4.9/5,864, Basic $10, 3-day, unlimited revisions, 3D mockup) | Must *stand out*, never clone a competitor. Cheap DIY loses the 3-second snap-test line-up — this is the step most worth outsourcing early. |
| **Formatting** (ebook + paperback) | Reedsy (free) | **$50–$100** freelancer | Low skill, high tedium; DIY is fine for beginners. |
| **Full low-content book** (cover + interior) | Build in Canva | **$75** (Fiverr: Mohammed H, 100pp, 7-day, 2 revisions, 3D mockup) | The one-gig shortcut for journals/planners at scale. |
| **Coloring / illustrated pages** | Draw/AI-generate yourself | **$10 per page** interior art | Per-page rate; the escape hatch for the high-effort coloring niche. |
| **Writing (high-content)** | Write it with AI + humanize (Pattern 6): outline → one ~1,250-word subchapter at a time → edit + story + fact-check + Grammarly | **Ghostwriter $1,000–$2,000/book**; or purpose-built book-AI tool ~$149/$224/$749-mo (1/3/10 books) | DIY-with-AI is the middle lane between free and ghostwriter. Accept only work you're happy with. |
| **Audiobook narration** | (none — narration is specialist) | **~$200 one-time** via ACX for a 30k-word book | ~$3.40 royalty per sale; only for proven high-content sellers. |
| **Reviews** | Facebook-group script (free, time) | **Book Reverb** (~$6+ bid, fast, mostly unverified) / **Book Bounty** (~$25/mo points, triangulated, verified + marketplace-targetable) | Never direct swaps or family (KDP-prohibited). 15-review gate before marketing. |

### Step 3 — Enforce the always-paying-something doctrine
No step is "free of cost" — the free lane costs *time* (tedium), the paid lane costs *money*. Make the operator name which currency they're spending on each step, deliberately. Drifting into DIY-everything because "it's free" ignores the time cost; drifting into outsource-everything pre-first-dollar ignores the over-investment risk. The desk forces a conscious pick.

### Step 4 — Fill the vendor briefs
For every step assigned to the outsource lane, produce a ready-to-send brief:

**Cover designer brief** — niche + target reader; 2–3 comp covers from page-one (as *reference for the bar*, explicitly "do not clone"); exact title + subtitle; **text-dominance requirement** (text-forward covers convert better in self-help — "make the text the main focus, images/vectors complimentary"); contrast/standout requirement (must win a 3-second snap-test line-up against the comps); trim size; 3D mockup requested; revisions expected.

**Ghostwriter brief** — the validated topic + one-problem/one-audience framing; the full outline (from TOC-stalking + review-mining, not blank-slate); spec (**30,000 words, 5x8–6x9 trim, ~150–200pp**, or 10k–15k for a first book; ~1,250 words/subchapter); voice/style ("practical advice, use stories, don't write in list style"); fact-check and originality expectation (copyright-eligible, humanized — not raw AI paste).

**Narrator brief (ACX)** — sample/audition criteria (tone match to the book's register, clear diction, pacing); ~$200 one-time budget frame; per-finished-hour vs royalty-share decision; the audition-first process (post for audition, pick from samples).

### Step 5 — Set the quality bar + revision standard per deliverable
Standing rule across all vendors: **never accept work you're not happy with** — use the revisions you paid for. Per-deliverable checks:
- **Cover:** wins the snap-test against page-one comps; text-dominant; stands out (doesn't blend); no clone of a competitor design.
- **Full book / interior:** value-per-page (no fluff-padding), organized, delivers the promised transformation; interior not raw-AI-flat.
- **Ghostwritten manuscript:** on-spec word count/trim; humanized (stories, not list-slop); fact-checked; copyright-eligible.
- **Narration:** sample matches the book's tone; clean audio; correct pacing before committing the full ~$200.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Type | Adaptation |
|------|------------|
| High-content (nonfiction) | Writing lane is the big decision: DIY-with-AI+humanize (free-ish, tedious) vs ghostwriter $1–2k. Narration lane unlocks (ACX ~$200) only once proven. |
| Low-content (journals/planners) | The $75 full-book gig or Canva-DIY covers most of it; no ghostwriter, no narration (low-content can't audiobook). Cover + formatting are the only real lanes. |
| Medium-content (workbooks, prompt books) | Interior design matters — lean toward the $50–100 formatting lane; writing is lighter (prompts/exercises), so DIY-with-AI usually beats a ghostwriter on cost. |
| Coloring niche | Coloring pages $10/page is the defining lane; the colorist/illustrator-who-films route is the organic escape hatch. Cover $10–20. |
| Sprint vs standard | Sprint: assign lanes for the 2–3 blocking steps + fill only those briefs. Standard: full matrix across every step + all applicable briefs + quality bars. |

## Output Requirements

Deliver:
1. **Posture call** — beginner-default or scaling-default, with the reasoning.
2. **Chosen lanes** — a per-step table showing the lane picked, the price, and *the named cost of the lane not chosen* (money saved or time saved).
3. **Filled vendor briefs** — one ready-to-send brief per outsourced step (cover / ghostwriter / narrator / formatting / coloring as applicable).
4. **Quality bar + revision standard** — the pass/fail check per vendor deliverable.

No fabricated prices — every figure traces to `references/price-sheet.md`; flag anything niche-specific as an estimate.

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Every step framed as an explicit time-or-money trade with the cost of the *other* lane named (Pattern 17, Rubric 3).
- [ ] Posture (beginner vs scaling) set before lanes are assigned; no over-investing pre-first-dollar (price-sheet coaching note).
- [ ] Real prices only, traced to the sheet — cover $10–20, formatting $50–100, full low-content $75, coloring $10/page, ghostwriter $1–2k, narration ~$200 (Rubric 2, thresholds explicit).
- [ ] Cover brief demands text-dominance + snap-test standout + no-clone (Anti-Patterns: cheap DIY covers, ripping off).
- [ ] Ghostwriter brief carries the full outline + 30k/5x8–6x9/150–200pp spec + humanize/fact-check requirement (Pattern 6, no raw AI paste).
- [ ] Review lane never uses direct swaps or family; 15-review gate stated (Insight 2, Anti-Pattern: direct swaps).
- [ ] Revision standard ("never accept work you're not happy with") applied per deliverable.
- [ ] Outsourcing never treated as free — the money cost is named (Anti-Pattern: treating outsourcing as free).
