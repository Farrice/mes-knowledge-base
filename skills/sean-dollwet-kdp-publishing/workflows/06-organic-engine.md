# Organic Engine — The No-Ads Short-Form System

## Pre-Flight Gate

Run this when the operator wants distribution **without ad spend** — no budget, or wants to run organic *alongside* ads (serious scaling runs both). The trade is explicit and honest (Pattern 17): **organic costs time and saves money; ads cost money and save time.** "You're saving money by investing a lot more time." Never sell organic as a free lunch.

Do NOT run this when:
- The book has fewer than 15 reviews. Virality drives traffic to a listing that must already convert; a no-review listing wastes the spillover. Clear the review gate in `03-launch-and-multiply` first (Anti-Pattern: no-review launch).
- The topic was never validated. Views cannot rescue dead demand — route to `01-validate-book-topic`.
- The operator wants a "content strategy" full of blogs, a Substack, or a podcast. Those are the kill list (below) — this workflow refuses them.

Governing frame: individual short-form views are **near-random**. You don't predict winners; you buy lottery tickets in bulk and let the feed surface the hit. **Judge the account, never the video** (Pattern 19).

## Execution

### Step 1 — State the trade and kill the useless tactics
Open with the doctrine: this is the time-lane, not the money-lane. Then remove the dead tactics before building anything — **podcasts, Substack, and blogging/website blogs are "a complete waste of time" for selling books.** 100% of free effort routes to **short-form video, TikTok-first, faceless-preferred.** Everything downstream lives inside that one channel.

### Step 2 — Assign content pillars from the 7-type taxonomy
Pick 2–3 formats to run as pillars, biased toward the lowest-effort, highest-converting types. The counterintuitive law: the single best *converter* (type 3) is the *lowest-effort* one.

| # | Type | Composition | Effort | Evidence | When |
|---|------|-------------|--------|----------|------|
| 1 | ASMR / no-talk showcase | Music + text overlay + cover showcase, ~30s | Low | default filler | Faceless volume; calming niches |
| 2 | Talking-head topic + pitch | Speak one concept, then pitch; overlay hook question | Medium (on-camera) | @theinnerwork — 154.5K likes | On-camera comfort / teaching angle |
| 3 | **Book-showcase behind camera** *(best converter)* | Hold book to camera, talk, no editing | Very low | Jeffrey Mason "I Want to Hear Your Story" → **10M** | **Always — highest ROI** |
| 4 | Page flip-through | Turn camera on, flip pages, narrate | Very low (easiest to shoot) | @heeryourstory → "millions" | Journals / prompt books |
| 5 | One-page-read | Point at / read ONE page; hand + pen on interior | Very low | Shadow Work Journal → **70M** | Unlocks 1-page engine; best-selling journals |
| 6 | Coloring / interior process | Color a page on camera or flip-through | High (or outsource) | Coco Wyo → 28K–106K/video | Coloring niche |
| 7 | AI image carousel | Auto-scroll images + numbered overlays; CTA comment | Low, fully online | Hayden Fox → 34K saves | Refuse to show self OR book |

Default every operator to **faceless** (type 3 + type 5). The book, not the creator, is the product; faceless lowers friction AND raises virality (Jonathan's viral hits are all faceless — 4.7M, 1.4M, 861.4K; his face videos average hundreds).

### Step 3 — Mine the book: 1 page = 1 video (Pattern 20)
Derive the calendar from the book's own pages, never invent from scratch. A **100-page book = 100 videos**; when the pages are exhausted, restart at page one with new framing → unlimited inventory. This is the answer to "I'll run out of ideas." Best paired with type 5 (one-page-read): hand + pen on one interior page, read it aloud (the Shadow Work inner-child wound page did 70M this exact way).

### Step 4 — Model competitors in the niche (recreate, don't rip)
Find accounts already working in or adjacent to the niche (the taxonomy's cases are the template — @mindfulmindspublishing, @cocowyocoloring). Identify their winning *formats* and recreate the format with your book and subject — model Coco Wyo's kawaii "Bold-Easy" design language into a new subject ("Ballerina Bold-Easy Coloring Book"), never clone the exact video or design (Anti-Pattern: ripping off vs modeling).

### Step 5 — Write hooks as second-person wound-accusations (Insight 12)
Every viral overlay is a "you" statement naming a painful self-recognition — shame-relief as the scroll-stop. The overlay text *is* the product; the visuals barely matter. Verified pattern:
- *"You say you love your family… but you snap at them… and then hate yourself after"* → 1.4M
- *"You're not broken. That's your inner child still carrying pain no one helped you process"* → 1.4M
- *"What if you stopped trying to change people and instead changed how you react?"* → 4.7M

Write 10+ hooks in this register per book, each naming a hidden wound the book addresses.

### Step 6 — Post daily, then run the winner-detection loop
- **Cadence:** multiple short-form videos **every single day**, TikTok-first. Set the expectation up front: **most videos get a couple hundred views — that is normal and profitable.** Mindful Minds earns ~$40K/mo on a barbell of couple-hundred-view clips (666, 395, 622, 597, 465, 383, 1054…) over a handful of mega-hits. Reach (volume), not per-video engagement (~2–4% even on the hits), drives revenue.
- **Detect the outlier:** watch the *account*, not any single video, for the format that breaks out.
- **Clone the winner 10+ times** (exploit) while still testing ~3 new ideas in parallel (explore). Jonathan's sitting-with-book format has "more than 10" clones, "many did really well." Once the feed reveals a hit, the *format* — not luck — is the asset.

### Step 7 — Repurpose waterfall + monetization ladder
- **Waterfall (marginal cost ~$0):** post to TikTok first (highest reach) → repurpose to Instagram (fewer views) → repurpose to YouTube. Every extra platform is free incremental sales. Coco Wyo runs all three and "does really well on YouTube."
- **Ladder (graduate by earned proof):** (1) viral spillover — a viral book video creates its own Amazon search demand; **no link needed** for spillover ("I've seen this happen multiple times"). (2) link-in-bio once you have followers. (3) TikTok Shop once views are reliable ("goes hand in hand with it").

### Step 8 — Escape hatch for high-effort content
Coloring/interior-process video (type 6) is the only high-effort format. If it's the niche's pillar, offer the outsource lane: hire a colorist, ship pages to an illustrator who films, buy the markers ($10/page interior art rate, Ohuhu markers) — this exits the free frame and becomes a money-lane cost. State the trade explicitly.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Type | Adaptation |
|------|------------|
| High-content (nonfiction) | Pillars = type 2 (talking-head one concept) + type 3 (book-showcase). 1-page mining works on chapters/passages. Wound-accusation hooks pull directly from the book's pain points. |
| Low-content (journals/planners) | Pillars = type 4 (flip-through) + type 5 (one-page-read) — the 70M Shadow Work path. Faceless, fastest to shoot; the interior IS the content. |
| Medium-content (workbooks, prompt books) | Type 5 (read one prompt/exercise aloud) + type 4. Each prompt = one video; the book's structure is the calendar. |
| Coloring niche | Type 6 is the pillar; default to the outsource escape hatch unless the operator enjoys coloring. Model Coco Wyo's Bold-Easy language; run the full TikTok→IG→YouTube waterfall (their strongest surface is YouTube). |
| Sprint vs standard | Sprint: 1 pillar format + first 15 videos + hooks. Standard: 2–3 pillars, first 30 videos mapped to book pages, full winner-detection loop + ladder. |

## Output Requirements

Deliver a per-book content system:
1. **Trade statement** — time-lane vs money-lane, named explicitly; kill list confirmed.
2. **Pillars** — 2–3 chosen formats from the taxonomy with the effort/converter rationale, faceless default.
3. **First 30 videos** — derived from the book's own pages (1 page = 1 video), each with a second-person wound-accusation hook.
4. **Posting cadence** — daily volume plan, TikTok-first, with the "most videos get hundreds of views, that's normal" expectation set.
5. **Winner-detection loop** — how to spot the outlier, the 10+-clone rule, the parallel 3-new-ideas test.
6. **Repurpose waterfall + monetization ladder** — platform order and the spillover→link→Shop graduation.
7. **Modeled competitor accounts** — named, with which format to recreate (never rip).
