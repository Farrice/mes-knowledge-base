# Organic Engine — Experiments, Not Guarantees

## Pre-Flight Gate

Run after the book promise, sample, cover, metadata, and rights are approved. Review count is not a prerequisite. The listing must be compliant and reader-ready. Load `references/organic-taxonomy.md`, the target-reader dossier, approved pages, and proof ledger.

## Execution

1. **Find the reader's attention surfaces.** Compare current evidence for short video, YouTube, Instagram, search, newsletter, podcast, community, or other relevant channels. Do not declare a channel mandatory or useless without audience evidence.
2. **Choose three format hypotheses.** Sean's seven short-form types are a source-reported idea bank, not law. Select formats that fit reader, topic, assets, privacy, skill, and time.
3. **Mine approved book value.** Turn pages, tools, questions, examples, and diagrams into standalone useful pieces. Never post unverified claims, copyrighted source material, sensitive interviews, or a recycled full book.
4. **Build thirty controlled experiments.** Each row contains audience tension, useful idea, hook, format, channel, asset source, CTA, rights check, publication approval, and attribution tag.
5. **Run explore/exploit.** Test varied concepts first; repeat an observed winner with meaningful new value. Do not spam near-duplicates.
6. **Measure honestly.** Record post, reach, engagement, profile/listing visit where observable, discovery event, attributed sale, and uncertainty. Platform views are not book demand; sales are not net cash.
7. **Repurpose selectively.** Reuse only when channel format and rights still fit. Marginal effort is measured, not assumed free.

## Output Requirements

- Audience/channel evidence table.
- Three format hypotheses with why/why-not.
- Thirty-experiment backlog tied to approved book material.
- Rights and publication-approval field per asset.
- Explore/exploit and stop rules.
- Content → discovery → sale attribution ledger.

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Channels were selected from audience evidence, not doctrine.
- [ ] Content uses approved, rights-safe, verified material.
- [ ] Thirty experiments are genuinely distinct and useful.
- [ ] No unsupported wound, health, or income claim is used as a hook.
- [ ] Repetition adds new value rather than creating recycled spam.
- [ ] Views, discovery, sales, refunds, and net cash remain separate.
- [ ] Posting or outreach waits for external-action approval.
