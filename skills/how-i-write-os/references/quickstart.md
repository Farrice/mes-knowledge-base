# Quickstart — `/how-i-write` in 6 Worked Invocations

> One page. Six real intents, each shown as **intent → chosen stack → gates → what comes back**. The OS reads your intent, finds the lane in `composition-map.md`, runs 3–6 experts (never all 10) in order, opens on the Runia story gate, closes on the truth/clamp/slop gate. Stacks below are quoted from the composition map — `[VOICE]` marks the single body-voice owner. When a deploy workflow already owns a pipeline (`/parallax`, `/copy-engine`, `/ghostwrite`), the OS supplies craft *inside* it and never rebuilds it.

---

### 1. "Write a Parallax Substack edition on why we trust machines we don't understand."
**Lane:** §5 Substack / newsletter edition (zeitgeist-first, voice-driven, re-readable).
**Stack (5):** Runia `/runia-story-test` → Hawley `/hawley-theme-engine` → Wang `/wang-friction-map` + `/wang-newsletter-essay` (the gap that *is* the story) → **[VOICE]** Connelly `/connelly-content-slingshot` (lede drop-into-drive) → Ward `/wang-anchor-sentence` + `/ward-aphorism-forge`.
**Gates:** OPEN Runia story-test → CLOSE `/really-real-social` + `prose_classifier.py check`.
**Route note:** an *actual* Parallax edition runs through `/parallax` (it owns the edition pipeline); this OS feeds the craft layers into it. Omits Browder (not investigative), Lulu (no launch).
**Comes back:** a publish-ready edition with a stated theme, a sharp friction/gap as the spine, one coherent Connelly-voiced body that drops the reader mid-motion, and the single anchor sentence the piece is built to earn — slop-checked and truth-passed.

### 2. "Write a founder origin story for a wellness brand — make it feel like jeopardy, not a résumé."
**Lane:** §2 Founder origin story.
**Stack (5):** Runia → Stanton `/stanton-brand-origin` (living founder character + premise) → Browder `/browder-founder-warstory` (real jeopardy, adversary-through-evidence) → **[VOICE]** Albom `/albom-brand-story` (the one human truth) → Ward `/ward-saxon-punch` (the close).
**Gates:** OPEN Runia → CLOSE `/really-real-writing` + `verify` (evidence-spine claims must carry receipts).
**Branch:** if the founder is being *positioned* publicly (contrarian thesis, go-direct), add Lulu `/founder-narrative-voice-architecture` at Layer 6.
**Comes back:** an origin told as a character in genuine jeopardy — the dry "started in 2019" excavated into stakes, an evidenced adversary, one human truth that lets a customer see their own life in it, a Saxon-hard close, every factual claim verified before delivery.

### 3. "Write a high-stakes investigative piece on a fintech that misled its customers."
**Lane:** §3 High-stakes nonfiction / investigative (must grip AND survive scrutiny).
**Stack (5–6):** Runia → Stanton `/stanton-spine` → Browder `/browder-drama-excavation` + `/browder-stakes-architecture` + `/browder-villain-evidence` (lane owner) → **[VOICE]** Connelly `/connelly-rewrite` (economy/momentum) → Ward `/browder-show-then-nail` close.
**Gates:** OPEN Runia → CLOSE `/really-real-writing` + **`verify` / `wright-truth-audit` MANDATORY** — every claim routed through receipts.
**Deploy point:** `/browder-investigative-content` for Substack length.
**Comes back:** a piece where the villain is built from evidence (not adjectives), stakes architected so the next sentence is unskippable, Connelly economy keeping it lean, and a hard truth-audit that blocks delivery if any claim can't survive scrutiny. Omits Orlean (subject is dangerous, not overlooked).

### 4. "Write a contemplative LinkedIn post about noticing your kid grow up."
**Lane:** §11 Contemplative / wonder piece, deployed at social length (§10 cadence rules apply).
**Stack (4):** Runia → Shukman `/shukman-mythos-logos` (transmit an experience, not info) + `/shukman-let-it-through` (surrendered first draft) → **[VOICE]** Shukman `/shukman-concrete-doorway` + `/shukman-embodied-word` (vastness through the smallest true thing).
**Gates:** OPEN Runia → CLOSE `/shukman-dont-cut-live-flesh` + `/really-real-writing` + slop gate.
**Critical:** do NOT bolt Stanton `/stanton-invoke-wonder` on top — that *engineers* the feeling; Shukman supplies the genuine article and vetoes faked mystery. Shukman's honesty spine wins any conflict.
**Route note:** `linkedin-daily` owns daily cadence; the OS supplies the voice layer. Defer to `/depth-social` if it needs deepening (preserves scannability).
**Comes back:** a short, sincere post that reaches the large feeling through one small concrete doorway — no performed awe, no hook gimmickry, brevity preserved, live flesh uncut.

### 5. "Write a VSL for my 1:1 strength-coaching offer."
**Lane:** §8 VSL / converting copy (jeopardy and proof must be real).
**Stack (5–6):** Runia → Stanton `/stanton-sales-arc` (the arc) → Browder `/browder-high-stakes-vsl` (real jeopardy, proof-as-narrative) → Lulu `/lulu-conviction-copy` (belief sequence) → **[VOICE]** `/copy-engine` as the body engine + Connelly `/connelly-copy-detail` (one true detail implies the feature set) → Ward `/ward-copy-rhetoric` + `/ward-saxon-punch` (device per high-stakes line, Saxon close on the CTA).
**Gates:** OPEN Runia → CLOSE **hard honesty-spine gate** + slop gate + `/really-real-marketing`.
**Route note:** `/copy-engine` owns the converting body; the OS wraps narrative + rhetoric + conviction around it — it does not rewrite the copy engine.
**Comes back:** a VSL with a real sales arc, jeopardy and proof rendered as narrative (not hype), a belief sequence that earns the ask, one true detail that implies the whole offer, rhetorical device on the load-bearing lines, and an honesty-spine gate that strips any claim the offer can't back.

### 6. "Ghostwrite a thought-leadership post for my client, in her voice."
**Lane:** §12 Ghostwriting a client voice (one coherent author — never a multi-expert composite).
**Stack (3–4):** Runia → Albom `/signature-voice-legacy-identity` (voice-DNA, if soulful) OR Connelly `/connelly-ghostwrite-economy` (if the voice needs edge + economy) → **[VOICE]** the single chosen expert holds the ENTIRE body — the strictest single-author lane → Ward (the client's quotable lines, à la carte only).
**Gates:** OPEN Runia → CLOSE `voice-audit` + `/really-real-writing` + slop gate.
**Critical lesson:** never stitch multi-expert bodies for voice. The Sandwich tested 4/10 disjointed; the single-author draft won (`feedback_diandra-hooks-only-separation.md`). Other experts are à la carte amplifiers — never a second body-voice. Hard rule.
**Route note:** `/ghostwrite` (Lara/Cole pipeline) owns the workflow; the OS supplies the voice-craft layer inside it.
**Comes back:** one indistinguishably-in-her-voice post written end-to-end by a single author, with optional Ward-sharpened quotable lines, passed through a voice-audit that confirms it can't be mistaken for anyone else.

## How to read what comes back (every invocation)
- A **stack receipt**: which experts ran, in what order, and — explicitly — which were *deliberately omitted* and why.
- The **draft**, with the `[VOICE]` owner holding one coherent body voice (never a stitched composite).
- **Gate results**: Runia story verdict at the open; `really-real` truth pass + Stanton clamp-audit + `prose_classifier.py` slop check at the close; plus any lane-specific gate (`verify` on investigative/origin, honesty-spine on VSL, `voice-audit` on brand/ghostwriting).

## If the intent is ambiguous (selection logic)
1. **Market to move?** Yes → Lulu enters at Layer 6. No → omit Lulu.
2. **Material dangerous/dry, overlooked, analytical, or invented?** → Browder / Orlean / Wang / (Pressfield·Wright). Pick exactly one.
3. **Target feeling: meaning, wonder, momentum, or strangeness?** → Albom / Shukman / Connelly / Ocean. Pick exactly one as `[VOICE]`.
4. **A single line must be remembered?** Yes → Ward, one device. No → skip the rhetoric layer.
5. **Always** open on Runia, close on really-real + clamp + slop gate.

> Stack over 6 experts = a lane was double-picked. Cut to one per altitude band. Full routing table: `composition-map.md`. Lane disambiguation: `genius.md § The Lane Map`.
