# Seth Godin — Strategic Philosophy

> The operating system for overcoming resistance, shipping work that matters, and serving the smallest viable audience with consistency and generosity.

This skill captures Seth Godin's complete philosophy of creative self-leadership — the engine that powers everything from writing to marketing to living. Not viral mechanics (see `seth-godin-ideavirus` for that), but the human operating system that makes any creative or professional work possible.

**Expert**: Seth Godin — Marketing Visionary, 20+ bestselling books, Marketing Hall of Fame inductee
**Domain**: Strategic Philosophy + Shipping + Self-Leadership + Meaningful Work
