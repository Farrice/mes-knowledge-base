# Workflow 03: Strategic Clarity Engine

> **Prerequisite**: Load [genius.md](../genius.md) before running this workflow.
> **Uses**: Patterns 3, 5, 7, 10 + Tacit Knowledge 1, 2

## When to Use
- Starting something new and need clarity on who you serve
- Feeling scattered — serving "everyone" and resonating with no one
- Need to define or refine positioning before building
- Stuck because you can't separate fixable problems from unchangeable situations

## Produces
1. Smallest Viable Audience (SVA) definition
2. "Who's it for / What's it for" lock
3. Problems vs Situations triage
4. Positioning statement
5. First generous action

---

> **🔒 Pre-Flight Gate**: Before executing, run the **Decision Framework** in `genius.md` § Decision Framework. Confirm all diagnostic questions are answered.


## Phase 1: Who's It For?

### Step 1: Reject "Everyone"
If your answer to "who is this for?" includes the word "everyone," "anyone," or "people who," you're hiding. Mass appeal is a way to avoid the vulnerability of specific commitment.

### Step 2: Find Your Smallest Viable Audience (Pattern 5)
Answer these in order, each one smaller than the last:

1. What general category of human needs this? (e.g., "entrepreneurs")
2. What specific subset? (e.g., "solo entrepreneurs in their first year")
3. What narrower slice? (e.g., "solo entrepreneurs in their first year who are terrified of selling")
4. What single person embodies this? (e.g., "My friend Sarah who just quit her job to start a coaching business and freezes every time she has to pitch")

**The SVA is Level 3 or 4.** Not Level 1. Not Level 2.

```
SVA DEFINITION
══════════════
Broad category: [Level 1]
Specific subset: [Level 2]
Narrow slice: [Level 3 — THIS IS YOUR SVA]
Single embodiment: [Level 4 — optional but powerful]
```

### Step 3: The "Enough" Test
"What's the smallest group of people that if I made an impact on them, it would be enough?"

Write the number. Is it 10? 50? 500? Not 10,000. Not "a million." What's ENOUGH?

---

## Phase 2: What's It For?

### Step 4: Lock the Change (Pattern 7)
Every piece of work exists to create a specific change in a specific person. Lock it:

```
CHANGE STATEMENT
════════════════
Before: [What does this person believe/feel/do BEFORE encountering your work?]
After: [What do they believe/feel/do AFTER?]
The change: [One sentence: "This moves [SVA] from [before state] to [after state]"]
```

### Step 5: The Generous Act (Pattern 10)
Is this work a gift or a performance?

| Gift (Generous Act) | Performance (Ego Act) |
|--------------------|-----------------------|
| "Here, I made this for you" | "Look what I can do" |
| Detached from metrics | Measured by applause |
| Serves the SVA | Serves your reputation |
| Would you make it if no one clapped? | Only exists for recognition |

If it's a performance, redesign it as a gift. The generous ruckus is "work that matters for people who care" — not work that makes you look good.

## Phase 3: Triage the Obstacles

### Step 6: Problems vs Situations (Pattern 3)
List every obstacle between you and the SVA receiving this work. Then classify:

| Obstacle | Problem or Situation? | Evidence | Action or Acceptance |
|----------|----------------------|----------|---------------------|
| [Obstacle 1] | | Why you classified it this way | [If problem: specific solution / If situation: acceptance statement] |
| [Obstacle 2] | | | |
| [Obstacle 3] | | | |

**Classification Rules**:
- If the solution exists but you don't like it → **Problem**
- If it's unchangeable (like age, physics, the past) → **Situation**
- If you're unsure → Default to **Problem** and find the solution you're avoiding
- "I don't know how" = **Problem** (learning is a solution)
- "I can't afford it" = often **Problem** (money allocation is a choice)
- "I'm not credentialed" = **Problem** (pick yourself — Pattern 6)

### Step 7: Story Mode (Tacit Knowledge 1)
Which story are you in right now?

- **Architect**: "The circumstances aren't ideal AND I can design around them"
- **Victim**: "This is happening TO me and I can't change it"

If victim → rewrite your three biggest obstacles using "and" instead of "but":
- "I want to launch AND I don't have a following" (both true — now design for both)
- "I'm passionate about this AND the market is crowded" (both true — niche down)

---

## Phase 4: Lock the Positioning

### Step 8: Positioning Statement
Combine everything into one working statement:

```
POSITIONING LOCK
════════════════
I make [type of work]
for [SVA — Level 3 specificity]
that moves them from [before state]
to [after state].

The problems I'm solving: [list problems only — not situations]
The situations I'm accepting: [list situations — no emotional energy wasted here]

This is a generous act because: [how it serves them, not you]
```

### Step 9: Invisible Failure Test (Tacit Knowledge 2)
Before you over-think the launch:
"If I try this and it doesn't work, who will actually know?"

Write the actual names of people who would know about your failure. Usually it's 0-5 people. The catastrophe you're imagining has an audience of almost nobody.

---

## Phase 5: First Action

### Step 10: Design the Smallest Generous Act
What's the tiniest version of this work you could deliver to ONE person in your SVA within 48 hours?

```
FIRST GENEROUS ACT
══════════════════
What: [The micro-deliverable]
For whom: [One named person from your SVA]
Delivered how: [Email / DM / In person / Published]
By when: [Within 48 hours]
Success = : [They received it. That's it. Not "they loved it."]
```
