# Objection By Detail Pass

## Use When

The reader has a predictable doubt that the copy does not handle naturally.

## Inputs

- Draft section.
- Audience.
- Likely objection.
- Proof, example, test, or use detail.
- Desired action.

## Steps

1. Name the private objection in plain language.
2. Find the exact moment where that doubt appears.
3. Choose a detail, anecdote, comparison, or test that answers it indirectly.
4. Insert the detail without sounding defensive.
5. Remove over-explaining.
6. State the objection pressure before and after.

## Quality Gate

Fail if the copy debates the reader or makes the objection feel bigger.
