# Story Desire Pass

## Use When

The product, offer, or CTA appears before the reader wants the result.

## Inputs

- Draft.
- Offer.
- Audience.
- Desired action.
- Proof assets.
- Situation or story material.

## Steps

1. Find where the product first appears.
2. Ask whether the reader wants the outcome by that point.
3. Add situation, contrast, stakes, or story before the product appears.
4. Place proof inside the story where possible.
5. Reintroduce the product as the natural next step.
6. State what desire changed.

## Output Schema

```markdown
## Story Desire Pass
- **Product entry point:**
- **Desire gap:**
- **Story or contrast added:**
- **Proof placement:**
- **Revised product entry:**
- **Behavior delta:**
- **Remaining risk:**
```

## Quality Gate

Fail if the story entertains but does not increase desire for the outcome.
