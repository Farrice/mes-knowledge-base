# Story Desire Objection Pass

## Use When

The draft explains the product before the reader has enough desire or trust.

## Inputs

- Current draft.
- Product or offer.
- Audience.
- Desired action.
- Proof assets.
- Biggest likely objection.

## Steps

1. Identify where the product first appears.
2. Ask whether the reader already wants the outcome at that point.
3. Add or strengthen the situation story before the product appears.
4. Add one proof object inside the story.
5. Handle the biggest objection through a detail, anecdote, comparison, or test.
6. Reintroduce the product as the natural next step.

## Output Schema

```markdown
## Story Desire Objection Pass
- Current product-entry point:
- Desire gap:
- Story insertion:
- Proof insertion:
- Objection handled:
- Revised product-entry line:
- Remaining risk:
```

## Quality Gate

Fail if the story entertains without increasing desire, or if the objection handling sounds defensive.
