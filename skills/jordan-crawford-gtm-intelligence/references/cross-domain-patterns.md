# Cross-Domain Patterns and Handoffs

## Jordan -> April Dunford

Transfer observed alternatives, problem consequences, best-fit evidence, customer language, and unknowns. April owns the positioning synthesis. She may reframe the market; she may not convert inferences into source facts.

## Jordan -> Offer architecture

Transfer the worst-version problem, qualification chain, PVP, proof objects, evidence strength, and explicit commercial unknowns. The offer owner decides packaging, scope, price, and risk reversal.

## Jordan -> Luke Iha / copy expert

Transfer recipient evidence, their language, current beliefs, failed alternatives, information value, and proof. The copy expert owns persuasion and sequence. If the message needs invented pain or trivia personalization, return upstream.

## Jordan -> Content system

Repeated dossier clusters become content territories. Quotes retain provenance; content themes cannot be reported as market demand without outcome evidence.

## Jordan -> RevOps

Evidence types, provenance, conflicts, unknowns, and review triggers become implementation requirements. Do not turn the evidence contract into mandatory-field bureaucracy that encourages fabrication.

## Jordan -> Agentic engineering

Unitize only a proven step. Retrieval finds, models judge, humans approve ambiguity-heavy or irreversible actions. Every unit returns evaluation evidence and exceptions to the shared truth base.

## Composition rule

Jordan is the function owner until a source-locked evidence packet exists. One downstream expert receives one bounded slot. Integration evidence is a changed artifact with preserved provenance—not a list of expert names.
