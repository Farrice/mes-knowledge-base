# Hidden Knowledge

1. **Difficulty is a diagnostic.** If GTM is persistently hard, treat width and invisibility as hypotheses before adding channels or copy.
2. **Simplicity is earned upstream.** A short message is strong when evidence made it obvious, not when nuance was deleted.
3. **Prediction is a stop rule.** Discovery saturation is demonstrated by anticipating answers and edge cases, not completing an interview quota.
4. **Unknown is a protected state.** It blocks false confidence and tells the system what to research next.
5. **Manual work is design research.** Known-good examples reveal which judgments are stable enough to automate.
6. **Good research becomes invisible.** The recipient receives the conclusion or asset, not a performance of personalization.
7. **Exceptions preserve intelligence.** Dissent rows keep the dossier from collapsing into a persona average.
8. **Internal truth must cross the boundary.** Information asymmetry creates growth only when it becomes useful to the next customer.
9. **Market selection is a leverage decision.** A high-demonstrability finite universe can outperform a bigger market with weak traces.
