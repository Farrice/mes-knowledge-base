# Message-Market-Fit Test

## Pre-Flight Gate

Require a qualified or explicitly provisional universe, evidence-backed PVP, its Research Receipt, and explicit authorization before any send. This workflow designs and reviews a test; it does not perform outreach. Opens and generic replies are not fit.

## Skill Acquisition

Load patterns 2, 10, 11, and 16. Test three gates separately: right recipient, disproportionate information value, and customer-native language.

## Input Required

- PQS and selected universe slice
- PVP and proof object
- Customer language with provenance
- Channel and permission constraints
- Current offer/action, if any
- Commercial baseline and desired learning

## Execution

1. Freeze one segment/problem/PVP hypothesis and its proof state; do not vary everything at once. A provisional PQS makes the test a discovery exposure, not a validation exposure.
2. Create a message shell that re-describes the targeting evidence and leads to the useful object.
3. Score qualification, value, and language independently; repair only the failed gate.
4. Define counters: qualified exposures, meaningful responses, problem confirmations, requested next steps, held calls, paid conversions, money collected.
5. Define `NO EVENT`, `NO PERMISSION`, and disqualification states.
6. Set exposure batch, follow-up rule, promote/iterate/kill thresholds, and evidence capture.
7. Run a receiver-frame pretest with real customer language; simulated councils remain predictive, not market proof.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Warm network | Small direct batch; relationship context explicit |
| Cold outbound | Legal/channel review and tighter evidence bar |
| Landing page | Qualified traffic source becomes part of the test |
| Sales call opener | Count problem confirmation and next-step commitment |

## Output Requirements

Frozen hypothesis, three-gate scorecard, message shell, proof object, counters, batch design, thresholds, permission state, and learning-capture plan. Use `references/prompts-v2/message-market-fit-test.md`.

## Quality Gate

- One hypothesis is frozen; variants have line-level deltas.
- Fit counters include commercial outcomes, not activity alone.
- `NO EVENT` and `NO PERMISSION` are explicit.
- Research status is explicit; `NO RESEARCH EVENT` cannot support qualification or language claims.
- Simulations are not reported as market proof.
- No send or publish action occurs inside the workflow.
