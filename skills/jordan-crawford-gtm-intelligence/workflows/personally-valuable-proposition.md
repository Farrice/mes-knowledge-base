# Personally Valuable Proposition

## Pre-Flight Gate

Require a PQS and recipient/company evidence. If qualification is weak, route upstream. A PVP is not a personalized compliment, offer discount, generic audit, or disguised sales pitch.

## Skill Acquisition

Load patterns 1, 2, 10, 11, and 16. The PVP converts information asymmetry into a decision advantage the recipient can use even if they decline the offer.

## Input Required

- Qualified recipient or recipient class
- Problem evidence and consequence
- Internal information advantage
- External evidence available for this recipient
- Decision/action the recipient could improve
- Proof and privacy constraints

## Execution

1. State what the recipient likely knows, does not know, and must decide; label uncertainty.
2. Identify the intersection of internal customer knowledge and recipient-specific external evidence.
3. Generate three possible value forms: diagnosis, benchmark/comparison, or opportunity/risk map.
4. Reject any idea that depends on trivia, unverified claims, exaggerated precision, or purchase to become useful.
5. Select one PVP and build its proof chain.
6. Apply the independent-value and proud-to-receive tests.
7. Define the smallest delivery shell; keep selling language out of the value object.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | PVP forms |
|---|---|
| B2B software | workflow diagnosis, peer pattern, implementation risk map |
| Agency/service | evidence-backed opportunity map, teardown, decision memo |
| Local business | local demand/operations signal, reputation or conversion gap |
| Existing customer | expansion insight or preventable-risk brief |

## Output Requirements

Recipient truth, information gap, three candidates, rejection notes, selected PVP, proof chain, independent-value test, delivery shell, and unknowns. Use `references/prompts-v2/personally-valuable-proposition.md`.

## Quality Gate

- The recipient can act or decide better without buying.
- Every material claim has evidence or an uncertainty label.
- No scraped-trivia personalization.
- The PVP is specific enough for one recipient/class but not falsely precise.
- Sales copy is deferred until the value object is sound.
