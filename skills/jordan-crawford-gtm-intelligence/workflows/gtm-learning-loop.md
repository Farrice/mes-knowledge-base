# GTM Learning Loop

## Pre-Flight Gate

Require actual exposure or a clearly labeled no-event state. Predictive reviews and simulated buyers are not market outcomes. Do not overwrite prior evidence; append and reconcile.

## Skill Acquisition

Load patterns 1, 3, 4, 9, and 15. Actions outrank interpretations. Replies, silence, calls, losses, wins, and collected revenue carry different proof weight.

## Input Required

- Frozen test hypothesis and counters
- Exposure log and permission state
- Replies, calls, objections, actions, commercial outcomes
- Prior dossier/PQS/universe/PVP versions
- Confounds and deviations

## Execution

1. Reconstruct what actually happened: exposures, responses, actions, held events, sales, and money collected.
2. Separate `NO EVENT`, `NO PERMISSION`, disqualification, message signal, problem signal, and commercial proof.
3. Convert new material into typed evidence rows with provenance.
4. Compare predictions with outcomes; preserve surprises and dissent.
5. Update beliefs separately from evidence and version the affected dossier sections.
6. Decide `PROMOTE`, `ITERATE ONE HYPOTHESIS`, `HOLD`, or `REJECT`.
7. Choose the single next experiment that resolves the highest-value uncertainty.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Evidence | Weighting |
|---|---|
| Silence | Weak unless delivery/attention is known |
| Reply | Language/problem signal; not payment proof |
| Held call/demo | Behavioral interest signal |
| Paid conversion | Commercial evidence for exact offer/context |
| Money collected | Strongest realized commercial proof |

## Output Requirements

Event ledger, proof-state table, prediction errors, evidence delta, belief delta, version changes, verdict, and one next experiment. Use `references/prompts-v2/gtm-learning-loop.md`.

## Quality Gate

- Activity, language signal, problem confirmation, and revenue remain separate.
- Previous evidence is preserved with version history.
- Confounds and deviations are named.
- Verdict follows predeclared thresholds or explains the exception.
- Exactly one next hypothesis is selected.
