# Zero-Data Discovery

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use only when customer evidence is absent or unusable. An idea, founder intuition, or model-generated persona is a hypothesis—not proprietary data. Read `references/research-tool-contract.md`, partition `PRIVATE_CONTEXT` from `PUBLIC_QUERY`, and record permission state. External outreach remains approval-gated.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `genius.md` patterns 5, 7, 8, and 9. The objective is to reach prediction saturation through known-good examples and the Five Cs, not to fill a persona template.

## Input Required

- Product/idea and hypothesized problem
- Initial market boundary
- Founder observations and why they may be biased
- Public-research permissions and interview access
- Time/budget constraint

## Execution

1. Write the current belief ledger: hypotheses, evidence, and unknowns.
2. Select three to five known-good examples likely to experience the worst version of the problem; require an inspectable case receipt for a named company or label it `MODELED ARCHETYPE`, and state why each is only provisional.
3. Write an answer contract for each Five-Cs question, then research customer, company, category, competition, and context through the allowed tool ladder. Attach a Research Receipt; engine failure is `NO RESEARCH EVENT`.
4. Derive candidate observable indicators and disqualifiers.
5. Design problem interviews around past behavior, triggers, alternatives, consequences, and language; do not pitch.
6. After each interview or research cycle, predict the next answer and record surprises.
7. Stop when predictions repeatedly hold across distinct examples, the customer-pattern evidence floor is met, and new evidence changes no material qualification rule; otherwise continue or narrow.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Pre-seed B2B | Manual company/person dossiers and problem interviews |
| New segment | Begin with existing-customer analogues, then out-of-sample cases |
| Local business | Public reviews, local directories, calls, observable operating traces |
| No interview access | Public evidence only; result remains `PROVISIONAL` |

## Output Requirements

Discovery charter, known-good case list, Five-Cs matrix, interview guide, prediction log, saturation verdict, and next truth layer. Use `references/prompts-v2/zero-data-discovery.md`.

## Quality Gate

- No invented customer quote, behavior, or market size.
- Known-good cases have observable reasons, not demographic convenience.
- Named cases have inspectable receipts; otherwise they remain `MODELED ARCHETYPE`.
- Stop rule is behavioral and testable.
- `PROVISIONAL` remains visible until direct evidence exists.
- Public traces without direct customer/action evidence cannot satisfy prediction saturation.
- Research Receipt and failed-search record are present.
- No external contact occurs inside this workflow.
