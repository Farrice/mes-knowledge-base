# `/dara-denney-creative-format-intelligence` — Format Portfolio and Mechanism Transfer

Use this when the task is broader than choosing a Meta video archetype: building a creative portfolio, deciding which formats deserve production, translating a winning ad mechanism into content or strategy, or rejecting familiar formats that do not fit the job.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Read:

1. `../references/creative-format-intelligence.md`
2. `../genius.md` — Format-then-Messaging Separation, Annual Arbitrage Hunting, and Partnership Ads as Scale Divider
3. the source ledger only when a tier, exception, or exact rationale is material: `../../../extractions/dara-denney/meta-ad-creative-format-intelligence-2026/source-ledger.md`

Treat every tier as a dated expert prior. Live account evidence can overrule it; unsupported optimism cannot.

## Input Required

- **Objective**: acquisition, reach, trust, education, proof, conversion, sale urgency, retention, or learning
- **Domain/platform**: Meta, LinkedIn, YouTube, email, sales enablement, client strategy, or another named container
- **Audience/persona**: stage, beliefs, objections, native behaviors, and awareness
- **Offer/topic**: what must be understood, believed, or done
- **Available access**: founder, employees, authorities, creators, customers, product feed, locations, proof assets
- **Economics**: budget, timeline, production capacity, and test runway
- **Evidence baseline**: current winners, losers, reach, spend, or `NO LIVE EVIDENCE`
- **Constraints**: rights, claims, approvals, platform rules, and brand/taste boundaries

## Execution

1. **Diagnose the job before the vessel.** Name the primary job and one secondary job. If the ask says "winning creative" without a job, default to a portfolio—not one universal format.
2. **Run the seven gates.** Evaluate evidence, access, funnel, friction, category/claims, and durability. Record disqualifiers before ranking.
3. **Build the portfolio.** Select one scale-anchor hypothesis, one trust/education play, one supporting-cast play, and at most one bounded challenger. If the budget cannot support four cells, reduce to anchor versus control.
4. **Pair message separately.** For every selected vessel, name the message/angle axis and persona independently. Do not mistake format variety for strategic diversity.
5. **Name holds and rejects.** Return at least two tempting formats that should not consume production now, with the exact veto or missing input.
6. **Transfer mechanisms when the destination is not Meta.** Preserve the job and mechanism; choose a platform-native surface. Reject cosmetic remixes.
7. **Route construction.** Hand each chosen format to the correct Dara production workflow or bounded specialist. Dara owns format strategy; claim, copy, design, and channel owners certify their own craft.
8. **Design the proof.** State the control, first cells, learning question, promotion/demotion evidence, and stop condition. No performance promise.

## Context Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Health/performance | Claim gate precedes script or visual production; authority is not evidence; VSL needs source-level substantiation |
| B2B/service | Investigation, founder, authority, commentary, and challenging-beliefs mechanisms usually transfer better than DTC product-feed formats |
| Personal brand | Translate to origin, commentary, question reply, belief challenge, education, and collaboration—not fake DTC ads |
| Organic content | Optimize for native consumption and relationship, not only paid conversion; keep the same strategic job |
| Sale period | Grid, founder letter, ugly/handwriting, and warehouse may rise because urgency and inventory become the job |
| No live data | Label all selections `HYPOTHESIS`; use the source tier only as a prior and design the cheapest discriminating test |

## Quality Gate

- Job diagnosed before format selection.
- Source tier labeled as a dated prior, not a promise.
- At least three distinct jobs represented or a named budget-based reduction.
- Message, persona, and format remain separate variables.
- Access, rights, claims, and production friction can veto a high tier.
- At least two holds/rejects have real reasons.
- Cross-domain adaptations preserve mechanism and become platform-native.
- Proof plan names control, promotion, demotion, and stop evidence.

## Stop Conditions

- No audience or objective -> produce a provisional eligibility map, not recommendations.
- Missing rights or claim evidence -> hold the affected format and continue with the nearest safe eligible play.
- User asks for production or spend -> stop at the brief unless image generation, publishing, outreach, or spend is explicitly approved.
