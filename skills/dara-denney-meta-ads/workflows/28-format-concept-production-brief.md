# `/dara-format-concept-production-brief` — One Format, One Integrated Production Packet

Use this after `/dara-denney-creative-format-intelligence` selects a format. Dara remains the format and integration owner. Content strategy, copy, visual direction, and claim safety occupy bounded craft slots; they do not independently re-strategize the vessel.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Read only the current step's owner surface:

1. The `Format Decision Handoff` from `../references/creative-format-intelligence.md`
2. Dara's matching format-construction workflow, when one exists
3. Content strategy: `../../kallaway-content-psychology/references/prompts-v2/kallaway_domain_08_creative_brief.md`
4. Copy: `../../stefan-georgi-dopamine-copy/SKILL.md` plus one route: `/georgi-copy`, `/georgi-social`, or `/georgi-hook`
5. Visual direction: `../../creative-direction/SKILL.md` plus `/art-direct`, `/storyboard`, or `/design-spec`
6. Claim-sensitive work: `../../claim-safe-health-marketing/SKILL.md`, then `/claim-safe-claim-substantiation-map` before copy and `/claim-audit` after integration

Pass summaries and paths between steps. Do not preload every skill's `genius.md` into one context.

## Input Required

- Locked format, source tier/date, portfolio job, and eligibility rationale
- Objective, platform, funnel stage, category, and named persona
- Message/angle axis and awareness state
- Offer/topic, proof held, evidence gaps, and claim jurisdiction
- Brand voice, visual system, people/assets/access, rights, budget, timeline, and production capacity
- Test hypothesis, control, primary metric, thresholds, and intended outcome-ledger IDs

## Composition

| Slot | Owner | Bounded contribution | May not change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spine and integration | Dara format intelligence | Format, mechanism, job, hypothesis, production fit, final packet | Source/live-evidence boundary |
| Content strategy | Kallaway content psychology | Platform-native wrapper, viewer state, retention/share logic, distribution role | Selected format or conversion claim |
| Copy craft | Stefan Georgi dopamine copy | Hook set, copy spine, proof placement, CTA states | Approved claim strength or format |
| Visual craft | Creative Direction | Visual hook, emotional core, cultural anchor, layout/scenes, asset and production specs | Message promise or proof boundary |
| Risk gate | Claim-Safe Health Marketing | Claim inventory, substantiation state, safer language, final SHIP/HOLD/BLOCKED | Commercial strategy outside claim risk |

`/publishable-copy-gate` remains the final copy gate for public or revenue-facing assets. It is a gate, not another strategy owner.

## Execution

1. **Dara locks the decision.** Restate format, job, mechanism, message, persona, source prior, live evidence, control, and what the format will not do.
2. **Run claim preflight when needed.** In health, wellness, supplement, or any claim-sensitive category, map every intended express and implied claim before copy or visual work. Mark `APPROVED`, `HOLD`, or `OUT OF SCOPE`.
3. **Content-strategy pass.** Kallaway returns the platform-native content job, viewer entry state, first-value moment, retention question chain, share/save reason, distribution role, and repurposing boundary. No full script.
4. **Copy pass.** Choose one route by surface: `/georgi-copy` for conversion-bearing long-form, `/georgi-social` for native organic content, or `/georgi-hook` for hook-led/static work. Return the primary hook, two alternates, beat/copy spine, proof placement, CTA states, and copy do-not-cross lines.
5. **Visual-direction pass.** Use `/storyboard` for sequenced video, `/art-direct` for campaign concepts, or `/design-spec` for static/layout work. Return the visual hook, emotional core, cultural anchor, composition or shot sequence, casting/props/location, typography/motion/sound, platform specs, and required source assets. Do not render.
6. **Integrate and claim-audit.** Dara resolves conflicts into one concept. Claim Safety audits the whole main impression and returns `SHIP`, `HOLD`, or `BLOCKED` with exact affected lines or frames.
7. **Build the production packet.** Name owner, deliverables, dependencies, rights, budget/cost gate, review checkpoints, production sequence, QA criteria, version plan, and stop conditions.
8. **Register the test cell.** Include one ready-to-fill `dara_format_outcome_ledger.py record` command and the explicit evidence required before any `promote`, `demote`, or `retire` decision.
9. **Write the Composition Ledger.** Show what each owner changed, what was preserved, and which overlapping routes were skipped.

## Quality Gate

- One format, message, and persona remain independently identifiable.
- The concept is specific enough to shoot or design without a new strategy meeting.
- Content strategy changes channel behavior rather than rewriting the ad.
- Copy and visual hooks reinforce the same first promise.
- Claim mapping happens before copy in claim-sensitive work and the integrated main impression is audited afterward.
- Production plan names people, assets, rights, specs, dependencies, cost gates, QA, and stop conditions.
- Ledger registration preserves category, persona, message, hook definition, conversion evidence, and fatigue.
- Composition Ledger proves integration; expert names alone do not count.

## Stop Conditions

- Missing locked format/message/persona -> return to `/dara-denney-creative-format-intelligence`.
- Claim remains `HOLD` -> continue only with a nearest-safe claim or hold the affected concept.
- Missing rights, source assets, or production economics -> mark the brief `HOLD`, do not invent access.
- Rendering, publishing, outreach, rights acquisition, paid tools, or spend -> stop for explicit approval.
