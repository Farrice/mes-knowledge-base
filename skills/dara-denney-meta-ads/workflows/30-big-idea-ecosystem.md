# `/dara-big-idea-ecosystem` — Evidence to Creative Ecosystem

Use this when the question is not merely “which format?” but “which idea has earned the next format, and how should we validate and expand it?”

## Pre-Flight Gate

Read:

1. `../references/big-idea-evidence-ecosystem.md`
2. the available Mission Doc, persona research, gap audit, recent roadmap, and outcome scoreboard
3. `../genius.md` only for the matching format/construction patterns after the idea decision

For health/performance work, map claim boundaries before recommending a message transplant or production asset.

## Input Required

- **Objective and decision:** what must be learned, chosen, or scaled
- **Brand/offer and platform:** product, funnel, destination, category
- **Evidence inventory:** account history, metric definitions, top cohort, reviews/comments, organic examples, competitor examples, or `NO LIVE EVIDENCE`
- **Personas:** observed buyers, intended recent-ad targets, and researched opportunities kept separate
- **Access/economics:** founder, internal team, creators, partnership rights, budget, timing, capacity
- **Proof/claims/rights:** substantiation, approvals, prohibited implications, jurisdiction, source assets
- **Current learning state:** prior format/message/persona IDs, fatigue, outcome decisions, and control

## Execution

1. **Choose the evidence branch.** Use Branch A for comparable first-party performance, Branch B for thin data or net-new persona exploration, or `HYBRID` with the evidence types kept separate.
2. **Build big-idea cards.** For each candidate record source asset, surface treatment, portable idea, persona, tension, promised shift, proof/mechanism, format, and evidence state. The portable idea cannot contain a format label.
3. **Mine patterns.** Keep message, persona, proof, visual, setting, creator, format, and outcome columns independent. Name recurrence and contradictory evidence.
4. **Triangulate personas.** Compare review/comment-derived buyers, intended audiences in recent creative, and organic opportunity personas. Name the gap; never merge cohorts into one avatar.
5. **Rank evidence.** Assign the highest defensible Dara evidence rung, receipt, date, confidence limit, and any human `JUDGMENT` override. Competitor/organic evidence remains a prior.
6. **Choose the cheapest discriminating test.** State the uncertainty, early vessel, why it can answer the question, control, metric definition, threshold, and higher-lift follow-up. Claims/rights may veto the cheapest vessel.
7. **Design the ecosystem.** For ideas that already have conversion evidence, map distinct jobs across native formats and creators. For hypotheses, map only the staged path, not a fake full rollout.
8. **Name holds and rejects.** Reject cosmetic remixes, competitor copies, production-heavy ideas without precursors, and persona claims without receipts.
9. **Route production.** Send each selected early vessel to `/dara-format-concept-production-brief`; use `/dara-denney-creative-format-intelligence` only if the portfolio job remains unresolved.
10. **Register learning.** Preserve format/message/persona/category IDs, big-idea label, evidence rung, control, metrics, promotion/demotion evidence, and stop condition for `/dara-format-outcome-ledger`.

## Quality Gate

- Big idea and surface treatment are distinct.
- Every idea has a source receipt or explicit `NO EVIDENCE` label.
- First-party, organic, competitor, and judgment evidence are not blended.
- Persona cohorts remain separate and the gap is traceable.
- The early vessel can answer the same uncertainty as the proposed higher-lift expression.
- Ecosystem rows have different jobs, not cosmetic variety.
- Health/performance messages stay inside approved claims and proof.
- No winner, scale, or performance language outruns an explicit outcome decision.

## Stop Conditions

- No usable evidence and no research permission -> produce an evidence-inventory and cheapest collection plan, not an ecosystem recommendation.
- Metric definitions are not comparable -> split cohorts; do not rank by blended performance.
- Claim/rights evidence is missing -> hold the affected message or creator route and continue with the nearest safe test.
- User asks for production, spend, or publishing -> stop at the brief unless explicitly authorized.
