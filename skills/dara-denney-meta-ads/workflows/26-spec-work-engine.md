# `/dara-spec-work-engine` — The Spec-Work Flywheel

How she got her first agency job, and how a candidate got an instant interview: **demonstrate the process ON the target's own brand before anyone pays.** "You do not need paying clients to start. You can analyze real ads that are live right now… I would do blog posts breaking down the funnels of brands I wanted to work with — more than 75% of the time, the brand would reach out." And: "A creative strategist dropped me five AI static ads for brands we actually run and said 'test these, and if it performs, reach out' — I immediately took an interview." This is a trust-based job; the flywheel manufactures trust with receipts.

## Genius Context (Load First)

Read `genius.md` — Creative Strategy OS layer:

- **Pattern 11**: the spec teardown IS a timeboxed reputation analysis
- The Grüns precedent: full research SOP on a non-client → public feedback from the brand itself
- Positioning rules: strategist FIRST (know your lane) · name admired PERFORMANCE brands (never Nike/Lululemon) · voice a different perspective ("'nothing is wrong' = red flag; a different perspective is a gold mine")

## Input Required

- **Dream-client list**: 3-10 brands (beachhead-aligned; for Farrice: wellness/fitness/supplement DTC)
- **Channel**: where the thinking gets published (LinkedIn / X / Substack / direct send)
- **Capacity**: teardowns per week you can sustain (consistency beats volume)

## Execution (per target brand)

1. **One-Sitting teardown** — timeboxed `/dara-reputation-analysis` (Reddit + YouTube comments + Meta Ad Library recon prioritized) + mini persona read + the pre-pitch move: infer their REAL top performer past the impressions ranking.
2. **Find the different perspective** — the one thing they're doing wrong or missing (a gap, a persona, a format unlock like Rhode's zero partnership ads). No perspective = pick a different target.
3. **Run the evidence-to-meaning pass** — do not stop at research reporting. Move only as deep as the evidence permits: VERIFIED observation → material pattern or contrast → labeled interpretation with one plausible alternative → decision delta → cheapest falsifying test. A clever story is not more evidence. If the interpretation would not change what gets made or tested, the target is not ready for a proof note.
4. **Run the bounded copy-elevation bench** — Dara owns the testable strategy and integrates the final artifact. The analyst's truth standard supplies the evidence-to-meaning bridge. Luke Iha's Vicious Hook pass gets one job: turn the chosen tension into six emotionally specific, consequence-first openings. Stefan Georgi's Copy Thinker/Dopamine pass gets one job: remove dead zones and make the script or sample lines create recognition, curiosity, or payoff. Neither specialist may change the hypothesis, invent buyer emotion, promote an inference to fact, or soften a proof boundary. Skip either specialist when that artifact does not contain their surface.
5. **Produce two different surfaces**:
   - **Internal Creative Test Autopsy** — exhaustive diagnosis, alternatives, two concepts, hook pool, complete script/static brief, test sequence, proof ledger, and account-input gaps.
   - **Client Proof Note** — one arresting interpretation, the evidence that makes it defensible, two concept territories, six sample openings, and an explicit line showing where public evidence stops. It proves the quality of thought without handing over the complete production and testing engagement.
6. **Ship the public breakdown** — the thinking, published: what's working (name the mechanism), the gap, what you'd test first and WHY (gap-traced). Voice layer: VOICE-CARD + BLEND dial for Farrice's channels. Tag nobody-or-the-brand deliberately per channel norms.
7. **Optional escalation — the spec drop**: 3-5 spec concepts (or produced AI statics via workflows 08-15) sent direct: "test these; if they perform, reach out." Production cost ≈ $0 on our stack.
8. **Community loop** — contribute the same thinking in practitioner communities (contribute first, THEN ask — her explicit sequencing). Smaller brands/agencies take newer strategists.
9. **Track the flywheel** — per target: teardown shipped → response? → conversation? → engagement? Feed `revenue_tracker.py`; feed learnings back into target selection.

## Output Schema

Per target: internal Creative Test Autopsy + client Proof Note + optional spec-concept pack + outreach note (≤120 words, self-interest-first, no cheap question signoff). The client Proof Note contains a labeled strategic read, two concept territories, six sample openings, evidence links, visible unknowns, and the paid-work boundary. Portfolio index page grows one entry per shipped teardown — this IS the portfolio.

## Quality Gate

- Breakdown demonstrates PROCESS (journey stations, receipts, gap-tracing) — hot takes without receipts = fail.
- The different perspective is specific and defensible; flattery-only teardowns are skipped.
- Reporting-only briefs fail. The client-facing read must name what the evidence means, one credible alternative, what decision changes, and what result would weaken or reverse the interpretation.
- Facts are not hooks. Each sample opening must create private recognition, tension, curiosity, or consequence while remaining traceable to the chosen hypothesis. Generic feature restatements fail even when accurate.
- Internal and client surfaces must stay distinct. The free Proof Note may show six sample openings, but the full script/brief batch, account-informed diagnosis, claim clearance, test matrix, and post-test interpretation stay inside paid work unless a full specimen was explicitly requested.
- Run `/publishable-copy-gate` on client-facing copy. A clean classifier cannot overrule Farrice's felt verdict; user calibration controls the taste judgment until market evidence exists.
- Public claims about real brands pass Step 5.5 verification (VERIFIED/LIKELY labels; brand facts checked) before publishing.
- Spec drops are clearly speculative gifts — no invoice-shaped surprises.
- Consistency plan realistic (capacity-matched), because the flywheel compounds through repetition.

## When to Return
