# `/dara-strategist-package` — The Sellable Creative Strategy Offer

Turns the OS layer into revenue. Dara's economics, verbatim: never below **$2K/mo per brand** ("even if it's a small brand" — 5 clients = $10K/mo); average agency strategist $4-5K/mo; leadership $8-10K/mo; veterans charge **$8-10K per brand × 3-4 brands** "painfully easily." The price driver is positioning: **"The brands who pay you the most recognize the strategist is OWNING THE OUTCOME, not just submitting deliverables. If you want to charge more, bring more value."** Sequencing discipline (hers, and Farrice's standing rule): results first, then the ask — proof-before-pay.

## Execution

1. **Package the deliverables** (from `/dara-research-sop`) into named tiers:
   - **Persona Intelligence Brief** (standalone, the moat as a product): persona deck + desire segments + activation picks. The natural lead offer / lead magnet at teardown depth.
   - **Creative Strategy Sprint** (fixed scope): full One-Sitting package — reputation analysis → deck → gaps → mission doc → roadmap. The $2K-floor unit.
   - **Creative Strategy Retainer** (own the outcome): package + monthly roadmap governance + test readouts + quarterly big swings. The $4-10K/brand band.
2. **Price by the ladder + proof state** — no proof yet: free/discounted sprints ONLY as explicitly-framed proof-building (n capped, testimonial + case-study rights required — never quietly cheap). With receipts: floor $2K, target the $4-5K band, veterans' band comes with owned outcomes on record.
3. **Position as outcome ownership** — the offer names the business outcome (persona-led growth, CAC/AOV movement, net-new audiences), the governance rhythm (monthly roadmap, quarterly swings), and what you're accountable for. Deliverables are evidence of the system, not the product.
4. **Differentiate on the moat** — evidence-ranked personas + gap traceability is the demo: "every concept we ship answers *why this creative, why this persona, why this angle* — here's the receipt trail." AI-leverage is internal efficiency, not the pitch.
5. **The process proof** — buyers (like Dara hiring) trust PROCESS: show the research process, the performance-tank triage process, the onboarding process. One page each, in the package appendix.
6. **Sequence the go-to-market** — spec-work flywheel first (`/dara-spec-work-engine`), communities second, network announcement third. Route the actual offer copy through `/copy-engine`/`/jh-offer-stack` if a full offer build is wanted (compose, don't force).

## Quality Gate

- Every tier answers "what do I get?" from the buyer's self-interest first (three-beat copy standard).
- Prices sit ON the ladder with the proof-state rationale stated — no aspirational veteran pricing without veteran receipts, no quiet underpricing either.
- Outcome ownership named; a deliverables list priced as a package = fail.
- Free/discount work is explicitly proof-building with caps and rights.
- Offer-shaped output → flag for `/offer-redteam` before it goes out (anti-echo-chamber loop).

## When to Return

- At every proof-state change (receipts earned → re-price) · quarterly · when a new segment opens.
