# Big-Idea Evidence and Creative-Ecosystem Intelligence

Source boundary: Dara Denney, YouTube `p4yXXdnCgWg`, published 2026-08-26. Full preserved evidence lives at `extractions/dara-denney/winning-meta-ads-p4yXXdnCgWg/`.

## The Decision Unit

A **big idea** is the portable compelling concept beneath an asset's surface treatment. Record both:

- **Surface treatment:** visible vessel or convention, such as get-ready-with-me, static grid, founder monologue, or partnership clip.
- **Portable idea:** persona + tension + promised shift + proof/mechanism, stated in one sentence without format labels.

Do not transfer a winner until the portable idea is defensible. Format, message, persona, and proof remain separate fields.

## Two Evidence Branches

### Branch A — First-Party Account Evidence

Use when comparable performance history exists.

1. Select the strongest available cohort over a meaningful period. Dara's source uses the top 20 over a year for large accounts; adapt cohort size to the available data and preserve metric definitions.
2. Extract surface treatment, big idea, message, persona, proof, format, spend/conversion evidence, fatigue, and date from each asset.
3. Identify recurring ideas and components across independently performing assets.
4. Choose a new native vessel for the most defensible idea.
5. Test the transplant before assuming the whole ecosystem scales.

### Branch B — Organic Proxy and Persona-Gap Evidence

Use when first-party history is thin or when seeking net-new personas.

1. Start from a candidate idea or persona problem, not a generic trend search.
2. Mine TikTok/Reels or other native organic surfaces.
3. Capture recurring **phrases, visuals, settings, and creator types**.
4. Prefer cross-account recurrence and smaller-account breakouts over one large-account hit.
5. Compare three cohorts: review/comment-derived buyers, intended audiences in recent ads, and organic opportunity personas.
6. Label every output `ORGANIC PROXY` or `PERSONA RESEARCH`; paid promotion still requires a discriminating test.

## Dara's Evidence Ladder — 2026-08-26 Source Prior

High to low confidence:

1. Pressure-tested past performance through iterations.
2. New formats for proven messaging.
3. Partnership ads for proven performance and messaging.
4. New insights or hooks from proven personas.
5. New personas from thorough research.
6. Competitor evidence that the idea is working.
7. A competitor's longest-running ad.
8. A competitor did it.

This ladder sets a starting confidence prior. It does not replace category, claim, rights, economics, attribution, fatigue, or outcome evidence. A human may make a judgment override, but must label it `JUDGMENT`, name the owner, and preserve the reason.

## Cheapest Discriminating Test

Choose the least expensive eligible vessel that can answer the actual uncertainty.

| Uncertainty | Early vessel | Higher-lift follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Does the message stop and earn a click? | Static or simple internal-creator asset | Creator or partnership expression |
| Does the founder tension build trust? | Phone-first founder clip | Produced founder narrative |
| Does a persona recognize itself? | Direct callout static/short | Persona-matched creator system |
| Does the proof explain the shift? | Simple explainer or proof static | Full VSL or authority-led asset |

Cheap does not mean claim-light, rights-blind, or incapable of testing the same idea. If the cheap vessel changes the mechanism, it is a different test.

## Creative Ecosystem Map

Expand only after the idea earns evidence.

| Job | Native expression | What stays fixed | What changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fast learning | Static/internal creator | Big idea, persona, proof boundary | Hook/layout |
| Explanation | Creator or founder | Big idea, mechanism | Voice, sequence, setting |
| Scale/reach | Partnership or distributed creator | Proven message and persona | Creator, opening, native context |
| Trust/proof | Founder, customer, authority, case evidence | Claim boundary and proof | Narrator and evidence presentation |
| Supporting repetition | Static, carousel, email, native content | Idea spine | Platform-native vessel and job |

An ecosystem is not ten cosmetic variants. Each asset needs a distinct journey or learning job.

## Claim and Outcome Boundaries

- Organic virality is not paid-conversion proof.
- Competitor longevity is not verified profitability.
- Self-reported source examples establish methodology, not guaranteed outcomes.
- Health/performance ideas require claim mapping before copy or visual expansion.
- Hook rate can diagnose the opening but cannot promote an idea without the declared conversion evidence.
- Register format, message, persona, category, evidence rung, and the big-idea label in the brief even when the deterministic ledger does not yet have a dedicated `big_idea_id` field.
