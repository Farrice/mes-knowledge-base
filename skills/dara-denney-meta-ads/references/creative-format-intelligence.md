## Seven-Gate Selection Model

Run the gates in order. A high tier does not bypass eligibility.

1. **Job gate** — Choose the actual job: net-new scale, trust, education, objection resolution, proof, sale-period urgency, awareness/traffic, or cheap learning.
2. **Evidence gate** — Separate this source prior from live account evidence. A demonstrated brand winner outranks a generic tier; a single noisy result does not.
3. **Access gate** — Confirm the format's scarce input: founder, credible authority, creator audience, two-person cast, warehouse, product feed, customer progression, or real podcast appearance.
4. **Funnel gate** — S/A does not mean top-of-funnel by default. Before/after and objection handling often do mid-funnel work; partnership and founder can open net-new reach.
5. **Friction gate** — Price production load, creator sourcing, script looseness, asset count, feed setup, rights, permissions, and approvals before ranking.
6. **Category and claims gate** — Check category-native behavior, platform expectations, substantiation, testimonial/likeness rights, and disclosure requirements.
7. **Durability gate** — Distinguish a temporary pocket from a durable format. AI novelty, tired UGC, and trend-native UI formats can spike without sustaining spend.

## Portfolio Rule

Do not return "the best format." Return a portfolio with different jobs:

- **1 scale-anchor hypothesis** — eligible S/A play designed to reach or convert new demand.
- **1 trust/education play** — authority, VSL, educational infographic, founder, or investigation depending on access.
- **1 supporting-cast play** — efficient B-tier vessel that fills a funnel or persona gap.
- **1 bounded challenger** — a cheap C/D experiment only when a category-specific reason changes the prior.
- **2 explicit rejects** — tempting formats excluded because of job, rights, fatigue, claims, or production economics.

If budget or time cannot support four cells, run one scale-anchor hypothesis against one supporting-cast control. Do not create false variety by changing the skin while keeping the same message, persona, and funnel job.

## Mechanism Transfer Across Domains

Transfer the **attention and persuasion mechanism**, not the surface aesthetic.

| Meta format | Portable mechanism | Cross-domain adaptations |
|---|---|---|
| Founder's Ad | human why + shared problem | origin post, keynote opening, sales deck narrative, welcome email |
| Partnership Ad | borrowed distribution + persona trust | co-created post, guest newsletter, webinar partner, creator-led launch |
| VSL | sequenced education + conversion | webinar, sales-page explainer, YouTube essay, onboarding walkthrough |
| Amateur Investigation | discovery journey + earned conclusion | teardown, audit, case study, research article, field test |
| David & Goliath | specific enemy + challenger proof | positioning memo, category POV, brand manifesto, comparison story |
| Authority Ad | credible trust transfer + objection relief | expert FAQ, evidence-led carousel, analyst note, client education |
| Greenscreen Commentary | borrowed context + interpretation | reaction video, annotated post, news commentary, live teardown |
| Educational Infographic | useful information that earns attention | chart post, carousel, sales slide, client brief, internal training |
| Comment Reply | real question + direct answer | FAQ content, support macro, sales enablement, objection email |
| Challenging Your Beliefs | named limiting belief + counter-evidence | thought-leadership post, workshop exercise, nurture email, strategy memo |

### Transfer vetoes

- Do not translate a paid-social native look onto a platform where it reads as imitation.
- Do not use partnership/authority skin without real access, permission, and credibility.
- Do not carry health or performance claims across formats without re-running the claim gate.
- Do not assume an S-tier acquisition vessel is the right organic-content vessel; preserve the job, then choose a native container.
