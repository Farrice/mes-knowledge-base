## The Strategy Doctrine (transcript-grounded)

- Worst: copy competitors (even cleverly, via impressions-ranked ad libraries). Slightly better but still wrong: format-chasing. **Real strategy = the layer before format selection**: personas · content pillars/angles (2-3 pillar pain-point ideas per persona — "angles, not concepts") · platform trends (macro: EGC, partnership ads).
- **Three gaps** every strategy must audit vs the LIVE account: persona gaps · awareness-level gaps (classic: over-indexed MOF/BOF, winners are offer-oriented → build TOF) · creative diversity gap (personas × 2-3 angles × awareness range + creator diversity: lifestyle/gender/age/jobs/vibes).
- Validation: a strategy (vs a creative) is right when it echoes in organic; single creative tests can't validate strategy.
- Cadence: quarterly planning → monthly roadmap → weekly execution. The weekly-sprint-forever trap kills big swings.
- Closing questions for any concept: **why this creative? why this persona? why this angle? which gap does it fill?**

Pre-pitch move: 5 minutes in Meta Ad Library, read past the impressions ranking to the real top performer.
