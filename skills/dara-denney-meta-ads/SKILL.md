# Dara Denney — Meta Ads Creative Strategy

> "In 2026, formats alone are not enough." — D.D.

A completion engine for DTC Meta ads creative strategy — **video and static**. Built from Dara Denney's annual format review (thousands of cross-brand tests) and her static-ad masterclass "How I Make AI Static Ads (in minutes)". Operates on her core principle: **format × messaging are independent test axes** — you engineer, test, and grade each axis on its own.

## Expert Context

Dara Denney is a DTC creative strategist running an agency that has produced 20,000+ Meta ads across hundreds of brands. She publishes weekly on YouTube for performance creative practitioners. Her annual format reviews are arbitrage maps — she identifies which creative archetypes are performing in early-mass-adoption (before saturation) and pairs them with messaging strategies that survive 2026 ad fatigue.

**Her methodology** treats Meta ad format trends as a quarterly arbitrage discipline, separates format from messaging as two test axes, runs format theses across multi-brand portfolios for confidence, and operates at the script-distribution-architecture level (not single-creator level). She publicly admits when her taste is wrong — "I bet against this and was proved wrong" — and ships ads she personally finds embarrassing if pattern signal contradicts her gut.

## When to Use This Skill

- Building any DTC Meta ads creative strategy from scratch — video or static
- Diagnosing why current ads are underperforming (likely a format-messaging mismatch, not a creative quality issue)
- Selecting formats for a new campaign — funnel position × audience age × ops maturity × budget × brand stage
- Constructing high-leverage video formats: David & Goliath, yapper, objection-handling, founder ads
- Building a static from a brand + persona: 3-layer system → one of 7 formats → 8 copy mechanics → 1-second comprehension gate → AI production
- Productizing a paid static-ad sprint (5–10 concepts, research-grounded, delivery package)
- Designing 30-day test plans as format × messaging × persona matrices
- Swapping a proven winner between static and video without re-researching

## Recommended Workflow Sequence

2. **For each recommended format**: Run the construction workflow (`/dara-david-goliath`, `/dara-yapper-script`, `/dara-objection-engine`, or `/dara-founder-ad`).

2. **Build the spec**: `/dara-static-engine` (08) for the full 3-layer build — *or* jump straight to a format builder if you already know the play: `/dara-educational-infographic` (12), `/dara-transformation-static` (13), or `/dara-comparison-callout` (14).

## Static Ads

> Source of truth: `references/static-ad-exemplars.md` — every exemplar below was **watched frame-by-frame** from Dara's masterclass, not paraphrased. Never invent a headline; a converting static is one you can name against these seven patterns.

### The 3-Layer Static System

- **Layer 1 — Strategy** (button it up *before* a line of copy): pick ONE specific goal (offer / education / target a problem-aware buyer — never one ad doing two jobs) → a SPECIFIC persona (stage + objection, not "small business owners") → the FORMAT → a matched LEVEL OF AWARENESS (the bridge from persona+goal to framework).
- **Layer 2 — Design**: visual hierarchy — headline generally FIRST (it does the targeting) → product/key visual → supporting elements; the focal point sits on the messaging ~9 times out of 10. Then the **1-second comprehension test** — *if a stranger can't tell what you're selling in one second, kill it.* Clarity always beats creativity.
- **Layer 3 — Copy** (the most important layer): the 8 mechanics below.

### The 7 Format Archetypes (real exemplars — never invent past these)

1. **Educational infographic** — Sweetgreen **"The Economics of $15 Salads"** (Chartr-style bar chart). Masquerades as organic; leads with education/value; TOF workhorse.
2. **Headliner** — Happy Tuesdays **"The cheat code to your big weekend."**; Wandering Bear **"SO GOOD IT SHOULD BE BAD FOR YOU"**; TIME/supplement **"…the closest we've gotten to a fountain of youth"** + TIME logo + **"USE CODE TRY25 FOR 20% OFF"**. Ogilvy print lineage; the message is the focal point.
3. **Benefits callout** (Ogilvy "features callout" renamed) — headline at top; test it two ways: tap the **core desire** vs a generic line, or slide a **golden-nugget testimonial** into the headline.
4. **Comparison / us-vs-them** — **"GRO Shampoo & Conditioner"** vs **"Other Hair Growth Products"**, green ✓ / red ✗ rows ("No harmful side effects," "Hormone free," "Visible results in 90 days," "Certified vegan & clean"). "Still cranking." Negative marketing baked in.
5. **Transformation** — **"My secret for getting rid of dandruff"** before/after split (flaky scalp BEFORE / clean AFTER 2 USES) + creator holding two bottles (lo-fi creator). Biggest gap + biggest opportunity for problem/solution-aware buyers ("they're just looking for proof").
6. **Grid static** — **"MEET THE Cook & Bake Set"** struck-through $1,090 → $632, "STORAGE INCLUDED," ~9 product tiles. OG print-era; apparel / multi-SKU; sales periods. (Laura Geller top performer follows a similar grid pattern.)
7. **Text-only** — totallee **"iPhone Cases Are Weird."** founder's-letter long text. Borrows the organic founder's-letter format; founders' letters do well.

### The 8 Copy Mechanics (Layer 3)

1. **Be specific** — name the demographic, give exact numbers, lead with a number communicating time/effort/cost ("five minutes," "under $5," "2 inches in just one use").
2. **Call out the audience by name** — self-selection is a copy mechanic; it improves targeting.
3. **Lean into the taboo** — say what competitors are too polite to say.
4. **Tap a primal desire** — status, sex, belonging, safety, approval. Product is the mechanism; the DESIRE is the headline.
5. **Open a curiosity loop** — show the setup, hide the payoff through the click; satisfy it on the landing page.
6. **Negative marketing** — say it in a negative aspect; name what the audience is afraid of.
7. **Borrow from customers** — run a CSV of reviews through your LLM and find the "golden-nugget testimonials."
8. **Show the transformation** — don't just describe the after state, show it (before/after). Before/afters are NOT illegal; cosmetic / weight-loss categories carry more restriction — claim only what the frame shows.

### The 3 Production Levels

- **Graphic-style** — infographics, comparison grids, product grids, feature callouts.
- **Hi-fi production** — premium / luxury / B2B positioning.
- **Lo-fi creator** — phone-shot, founder/transformation, real-person content. **The biggest current gap AND her current needle-mover in client accounts.** Match the audience's expectation, not aspirational polish.

### AI Production Pipeline (tool-agnostic)

Claude builds the **brand brain** (voice/style/constraints as one reference doc, added to the generator's context) → **research first** (sub-agents scan competitor ad libraries — she scanned Native, Every Man Jack, Harry's — scored winning ads ~62–80) → **creative gap analysis** (which awareness levels / personas / formats are missing → the real failure is lack of creative diversity) → generate in **3-variation batches** ("I often like to stick to about three in the beginning") → **edit-to-refine loop** (natural-language edits: "remove the m dash," "change the background to beige," "make the t-shirt smaller," "make me five more variations for a solution-aware audience"). She demoed on SuperScale (paid sponsor) but the **method is tool-agnostic**.

### Her Taste Calibration (verbatim)

- **Rejects**: em dashes; misspellings ("Deodorant spelled wrong — please spell it correctly"); "too much going on" / no focal point ("less is more"); review-collage / quote-heavy social-proof statics.
- **Accepts**: visceral — Dr. Squatch **"Blame your D.O., not your shirts"** / "Your Deo is ruining your shirts," a "disgusting closeup." She advised leaning **more** visceral, not "clean and safe."

## Video Quick Reference

### The 8 Video Format Archetypes (2026)
1. David & Goliath (enemy callout → contrast → science proof)
2. Obvious AI Slop (outrageous animated characters; permission via cringe)
3. TikTok Love Letter (long text on short video; target callout)
4. TikTok Short (1-2 line POV opener; rapid iteration)
5. "We're Not Cheap" (objection-as-positioning)
6. "We're Sorry" (emotional pattern interrupt)
7. Listical (N reasons × multi-persona script distribution)
8. Yapper (creator-led rant; operationally hardest, highest arbitrage)

### Format → Messaging Pairing
- **David & Goliath** ↔ Enemy + science + customer benefit
- **AI Slop** ↔ Outrageous absurdity + product utility
- **TikTok Love Letter** ↔ Target audience callout + insight
- **TikTok Short** ↔ POV-opener + 1-line payoff
- **We're Not Cheap** ↔ Objection + reframe + proof
- **We're Sorry** ↔ Emotional pattern interrupt + offer
- **Listical** ↔ N reasons + persona × script distribution
- **Yapper** ↔ Creator-led rant + flexible script + native vernacular

### The 4-Layer Hook Anatomy (first 3 seconds)
Text overlay + sound + visual + vibe — all four engineered per hook. Visual layer moves performance most; best hooks show, don't tell. Winning verbal families: investment, scam, give-me-time, POV/"POV: you hate X", founder origin, founder self-intro, in-action creator, emotional cliffhanger, "why did no one tell me", "if you…". Details: `genius.md` export-patterns section + `/dara-winning-hooks`.

### Founder Ads (desert-island format)
Six plays: problem-solution VSL, objection handler, product demo, educational, sales-announcement POV, high-production (only after UGC traction). 7-beat script spine; keep the self-intro (split-tested winner). Camera-shy → interview-style shoot. Details: `/dara-founder-ad`.

### 5-Question Selection Tree
1. Funnel position?
2. Audience age?
3. Operational maturity (script-flex)?
4. Creative budget?
5. Brand maturity (6/7/8/9 figure)?

### 5 Signature Moves (Video)

- **Copy architecture**: the 8 copy mechanics apply universally; the headline sells a desire/outcome, not a spec
- **Design gate**: the visual hierarchy (headline first, focal point on the message ~9/10) and the 1-second comprehension test are format-agnostic QA
- **Cross-format research reuse**: message architecture (goal / persona / objection / awareness / proof mechanism) is format-agnostic; only the production vessel changes (static headline+hierarchy ↔ video hook+sequence) — that's `/dara-format-swap`
- **Production efficiency**: production-level choice (lo-fi / graphic / hi-fi) compounds with awareness level — match the audience's expectation, over-index toward lo-fi creator

### Stacking Pattern: Video ↔ Static
1. Lock the winning spec in one vessel (video: 8 formats / 6 founder plays; static: 3-layer system / 7 archetypes).
2. Extract the message architecture (goal, persona objection, awareness level, proof mechanism, format).
3. Run `/dara-format-swap` — reuse the research, produce the paired spec in the other vessel.
4. A/B test (same budget, ~30 days) — measure which vessel wins for this ICP.
5. Document the winning awareness-level × format × objection combo (feeds `evolution_store/ground_truth/`).
