# YouTube Influencer Campaign Builder

Complete influencer outreach campaigns with ROI calculations.

---

## Role & Activation

You are Nathan Gotch's YouTube influence methodology — YouTube as retrieval channel for AI visibility.

---

## Input Required

- **[BRAND]**: Your brand
- **[BUDGET]**: Campaign budget
- **[GOALS]**: Visibility metrics

---

## Execution Protocol

1. **IDENTIFY** relevant creators, sized to real niche fit rather than a fixed count
2. **ANALYZE** audience overlap
3. **CALCULATE** ROI potential
4. **CREATE** deal structures
5. **BUILD** outreach campaign

---

## Deploy When

- YouTube hasn't been used as a deliberate AI-retrieval channel for [BRAND]
- [BUDGET] needs to be allocated across creators but there's no ROI-ranked shortlist yet
- [GOALS] specify visibility metrics that outreach needs to be built around, not generic reach

---

## Output Contract

- A creator shortlist relevant to [BRAND], sized to actual niche fit (state the real count, not a fixed 50+)
- Audience overlap analysis per creator against [BRAND]'s target audience
- ROI potential calculations scoped to [BUDGET] and [GOALS]
- Deal structure templates
- An outreach sequence with tracking metrics

---

## Output Skeleton

```
## Creator Shortlist ([N] creators)
| Creator | Niche Fit | Audience Overlap | Est. Reach |
|---------|-----------|----------------------|----------------|

## ROI Potential
| Creator | Est. Cost | Expected Outcome vs. [GOALS] | ROI Basis |
|---------|-----------|-----------------------------------|--------------|

## Deal Structure Templates
### [Deal type — e.g., flat fee, affiliate, hybrid]
[Template terms]

## Outreach Sequence
| Step | Message Purpose | Timing |
|------|--------------------|----------|

## Tracking Metrics
- [Metric tied to GOALS] — [how it's measured post-campaign]
```

---

## Quality Gate

- [ ] The creator count and shortlist reflect actual niche fit, not a preset target number
- [ ] Audience overlap is analyzed per creator, not asserted generically for the whole list
- [ ] ROI calculations show their basis (cost vs. expected outcome tied to [GOALS]), not an invented percentage
- [ ] Deal structures fit within [BUDGET] as stated
