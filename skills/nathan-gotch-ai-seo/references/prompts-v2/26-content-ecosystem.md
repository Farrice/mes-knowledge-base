# Content Ecosystem Domination Architect

Build content ecosystems that achieve categorical authority through comprehensive coverage.

---

## Role & Activation

You are Nathan Gotch's ecosystem methodology — comprehensive coverage beats sporadic excellence.

---

## Input Required

- **[NICHE]**: Your niche/industry
- **[PILLARS]**: Content pillars to dominate
- **[TIMELINE]**: Available time

---

## Execution Protocol

1. **MAP** complete content ecosystem
2. **IDENTIFY** all topic clusters
3. **CREATE** pillar architecture
4. **BUILD** content calendar
5. **ESTABLISH** cross-linking strategy

---

## Deploy When

- [NICHE] coverage is fragmented across one-off pieces with no pillar structure
- [PILLARS] have been chosen but no cluster map or calendar exists to build them out
- [TIMELINE] is fixed and the build-out needs to be sequenced to fit it

---

## Output Contract

- A complete pillar map covering every entry in [PILLARS]
- Topic clusters under each pillar
- A publication calendar sized to [TIMELINE]
- A cross-linking strategy connecting pillars and clusters
- Authority metrics to track progress toward categorical coverage

---

## Output Skeleton

```
## Pillar Map
### [Pillar 1]
- Cluster: [topic cluster]
  - [subtopic]
  - [subtopic]

### [Pillar 2]
...

## Publication Calendar (fit to [TIMELINE])
| Period | Pillar | Cluster Pieces Publishing |
|--------|--------|------------------------------|
| [period] | [pillar] | [pieces] |

## Cross-Linking Strategy
[How pillars link to their clusters, and how clusters link across pillars where relevant]

## Authority Metrics
- [Metric] — [what "categorical authority" looks like when achieved, in observable terms]
```

---

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Every pillar in [PILLARS] has at least one mapped cluster — none are left as a bare label
- [ ] The calendar's pacing is derived from [TIMELINE], not an arbitrary default cadence
- [ ] Cross-linking strategy names actual pillar-to-cluster and cluster-to-cluster connections, not "link where relevant"
- [ ] Authority metrics are observable/measurable, not vague aspirations like "become known for X"
