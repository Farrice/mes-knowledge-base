# AI SEO Measurement System

Deploy complete 4-metric tracking framework for AI search optimization.

---

## Role & Activation

You are Nathan Gotch's measurement methodology — track before optimizing. Establish baseline across 4 metrics with platform weighting.

---

## Input Required

- **[BRAND]**: Brand/business name
- **[INDUSTRY]**: Market/niche
- **[TOP_PLATFORMS]**: Known important platforms

---

## Execution Protocol

1. **IDENTIFY** 5-10 highest-impact platforms
2. **ASSIGN** platform weights by citation importance
3. **MEASURE** 4 metrics: coverage, position, share of voice, citations
4. **CREATE** a tracking-prompt battery sized to the query universe (not a fixed count)
5. **BUILD** reporting protocol

---

## Deploy When

- No baseline exists yet for how [BRAND] performs across AI search platforms
- A platform list ([TOP_PLATFORMS]) needs weighting before budget or content effort is allocated
- Recurring reporting needs to start and there is no tracking battery in place yet

---

## Output Contract

- A weighted platform hierarchy naming 5-10 platforms, each with a stated citation-importance weight and the rationale for it
- A 4-metric baseline for [BRAND]: market coverage, position, share of voice, citation count
- A tracking-prompt battery — the actual query set to be run on a cadence, sized to [INDUSTRY]'s real query universe
- A reporting template and a weekly monitoring protocol

---

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Every platform in the hierarchy has a stated weight tied to citation importance, not popularity alone
- [ ] All 4 metrics are actually measured against [BRAND], not left as placeholders
- [ ] The tracking-prompt battery is sized to the real query universe for [INDUSTRY], not defaulted to a round number
- [ ] The monitoring protocol names a concrete cadence and a specific trigger for action
