# Nathan Gotch — Search Content Production and Readiness Review

Create or review one asset without confusing a polished draft with eligibility, usefulness, conversion readiness, or market results.

## Input Required

- **[PROJECT_PACK]**: validated project truth
- **[OPPORTUNITY]**: one promoted source-labeled opportunity
- **[LIVE_PATTERN_EVIDENCE]**: current traditional and retrieval sources showing viable structure
- **[ASSET]**: optional existing draft for review
- **[CHANNEL]**: article, local service page, ecommerce page, LinkedIn post, video script, visual brief, or audio script

## Workflow

### 1. Eligibility and route fit

For owned pages, inspect response, crawl/index/canonical/retrievability and current route. For other channels, verify format eligibility and production constraints. Record unknowns instead of assuming pass.

### 2. Intent and pattern

Name the dominant buyer/job and the proven structural pattern. Split only when the buyer, decision stage, location, or required answer materially differs.

### 3. SearchBrief mechanics

Specify target query, audience, information gain, source receipts, structural pattern, channel, CTA, risk boundary, and falsifiable measurement hypothesis. Desired positioning is a hypothesis, not a fact.

### 4. Production handoff

Send only an approved brief to `/create` and the appropriate writing/media craft owner. Paid generation and external action remain approval-gated.

### 5. Review layers

Keep these separate:

- deterministic eligibility/on-page checks;
- Nathan expert judgment on intent, information gain, search structure, and conversion continuity;
- Farrice's override with original score preserved;
- later observed outcomes, attached only through independent events.

### 6. Action verdict

Return approve, revise, hold, consolidate, redirect, or reject with the changed decision and remaining proof gap. Never call a readiness score a ranking prediction.

## Output Requirements

- SearchBrief-ready mechanics
- Production route and approval boundary
- Ten-dimension readiness review or score handoff
- Original, expert, override, and observed-outcome layers kept distinct
- Changed-decision receipt and next test
- Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/37-search-content-production-readiness.md`

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Current intent and source receipts shape the asset
- [ ] Information gain is specific, not “make it better”
- [ ] CTA path and risk boundary are explicit
- [ ] Human fact/claim and taste review remains mandatory
- [ ] No outcome is inferred from the readiness score
