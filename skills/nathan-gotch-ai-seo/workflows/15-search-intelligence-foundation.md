# Nathan Gotch — Search Intelligence Foundation

Build the truth and evidence layer required before search strategy or content production. This is the Nathan mechanics component inside `/search-content-mastery foundation`; the conductor owns the portable records and runtime.

## Input Required

- **[ENTITY]**: brand, people, products/services, locations, and ownership facts
- **[OFFER_AUDIENCE_VOICE]**: current offer, customer, language, objections, tone, proof, and policy
- **[SOURCE_SET]**: primary sources, SME calls/transcripts, approved research, customer evidence
- **[SEARCH_INPUTS]**: dated GSC/GA4/GBP/YouTube/rank/citation exports or `NONE`
- **[OPERATOR_DECISIONS]**: Farrice's taste, exclusions, priorities, and overrides
- **[PROJECT_PACK]**: existing `/search-content-mastery` project pack or requested destination

## Workflow

### 1. Truth inventory

Separate verified facts, unresolved facts, hypotheses, prohibited claims, and time-sensitive fields. Do not let model memory fill blanks.

### 2. Source shaping

Preserve raw evidence, then distill only the retrieval-ready artifacts the work needs: entity truth, brand/offer/audience/voice, source rules, claim rules, competitor patterns, search imports, and operator taste.

### 3. Retrieval probe

Ask specific questions of the assembled context: what is sold, to whom, why it is different, what can be claimed, what requires review, and what evidence is missing. A plausible answer without a source receipt fails.

### 4. Search surface selection

Activate universal surfaces (traditional and AI/retrieval observation) plus only relevant vertical branches: local/GBP, ecommerce/marketplaces, YouTube/social, directories/publishers, or app search.

### 5. Foundation handoff

Return the populated fields and unresolved queue to `/search-content-mastery foundation`. Do not create strategy, publish, or promote the project from this workflow.

## Output Requirements

- Truth inventory with evidence state per field
- Seven portable context payloads or explicit missing-field queue
- Retrieval probe results with source references
- Active and parked search surfaces with reasons
- Foundation handoff for `SearchProjectManifest`
- Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/35-search-intelligence-foundation.md`

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Raw evidence remains linked; no transcript dump becomes the active context by default
- [ ] Facts, hypotheses, operator taste, and claim restrictions are separate
- [ ] Every active surface has a project-specific reason
- [ ] Missing evidence stays visible
- [ ] No ranking, citation, traffic, conversion, or revenue result is implied
