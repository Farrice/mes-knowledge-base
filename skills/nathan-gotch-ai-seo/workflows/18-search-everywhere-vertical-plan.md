# Nathan Gotch — Search-Everywhere Vertical Plan

Extend one source-grounded commercial topic across the search surfaces that actually matter for this project. This is an adaptation plan, not permission to publish or distribute.

## Input Required

- **[PROJECT_PACK]**: entity, offer, audience, voice, claims, and operator decisions
- **[PRIMARY_ASSET]**: approved service/category/answer asset or approved SearchBrief
- **[SURFACE_EVIDENCE]**: dated traditional, AI citation, local, video, directory, marketplace, or social observations
- **[VERTICAL]**: local service, ecommerce, expert/personal brand, B2B, or other
- **[EXTERNAL_ACTION_STATE]**: defaults to `NO PERMISSION`

## Workflow

1. Benchmark owned rank, third-party presence, AI mention/citation, video/social presence, traffic, and conversion separately.
2. Confirm the primary asset serves one buyer intent and conversion goal.
3. Build supporting owned coverage around that same coherent cluster before adding adjacent geographies/categories.
4. Activate vertical branches only when evidence supports them:
   - local: real entity/location, GBP/local-pack eligibility, service/location pages, selective directories;
   - ecommerce: one category, awareness/intent map, comparison ladder, marketplace and creator surfaces;
   - video/social: channel-native formats built from the evidence spine;
   - third party: recurring non-competitor citation/publisher surfaces as opportunity records.
5. Adapt topic, proof, format, CTA, and audience posture per channel. Do not syndicate unchanged copy.
6. Separate owned build tasks from approval-bound profiles, reviews, outreach, sponsorships, distribution, and publishing.
7. Attach a per-surface measurement hypothesis and independent outcome stage.

## Output Requirements

- Surface benchmark and active/parked branch map
- Primary asset plus supporting intent architecture
- Channel adaptation matrix
- Third-party opportunity ledger with no implied authorization
- Per-surface measurement hypotheses and proof gaps
- Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/38-search-everywhere-vertical-plan.md`

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Primary owned answer exists before multiplication
- [ ] Local pack is not substituted by content where eligibility/proximity is absent
- [ ] Ecommerce scope remains category-bounded
- [ ] Recurring sources are evidence, not automatic outreach targets
- [ ] Paid or external actions remain disclosed and approval-bound
- [ ] No fixed asset quota or result guarantee survives
