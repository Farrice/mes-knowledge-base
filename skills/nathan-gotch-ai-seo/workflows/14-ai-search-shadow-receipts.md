## Workflow

### Phase 1: The Pull (Pattern 16, $0 variant)
1. Execute [PULL_PROTOCOL] per query per platform. Capture dated screenshots — these are the receipts.
2. Build the citation sheet manually (Keyword | URL | Platform | Brand mentioned?).

### Phase 2: The Count (Patterns 15-16)
1. Mention count: X of Y citations, self-serving separated. Same count per competitor.
2. Traditional check for contrast — the "you rank #N on Google, but…" line is the hook.
3. Carousel autopsy: whose products get recommended instead, with prices visible.

### Phase 3: The Receipts Deck (Pattern 17 framing)
1. Page 1: the split in one sentence + the two counts.
2. The consensus story in buyer language: "When your buyer asks ChatGPT for [query], it recommends [competitors] — because across the {Y} sources it reads, you appear {X} times."
3. Screenshot exhibits, dated. 4. The stake in revenue terms ("missing out on sales right now by not being recommended"). 5. The fix preview: one paragraph on the source-occupation path (mining → ladder → echo) — enough to see the path, priced work to walk it.

### Phase 4: Verification (Chain Step 5.5)
Every claim in the deck traces to a screenshot or counted row — this deck is fact-verifier
territory before it ships (real brand names, real counts).
