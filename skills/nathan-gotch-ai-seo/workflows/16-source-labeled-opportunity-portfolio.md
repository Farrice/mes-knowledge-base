# Nathan Gotch — Source-Labeled Search Opportunity Portfolio

Turn first-party data, live discovery, current result surfaces, and citation observations into a decision database. Volume and tool scores are inputs, not verdicts.

## Input Required

- **[PROJECT_PACK]**: validated Search Content Mastery project
- **[CATEGORY_OR_CLUSTER]**: one commercial category, service/location, or coherent topic family
- **[IMPORTS]**: dated GSC and other local exports, with source/mapping receipts
- **[LIVE_OBSERVATIONS]**: current SERP, AI answer/citation, video, directory, marketplace, or social evidence
- **[BUSINESS_CONSTRAINTS]**: relevance, offer fit, capacity, risk, and channel limits

## Decision Record

Every opportunity must contain:

- source and observation date;
- cluster and target query/topic;
- audience and intent;
- current page/position when observed;
- visible search surfaces and result types;
- demand/value proxies with their provenance;
- page- and domain-level feasibility evidence;
- business relevance and operator judgment;
- opportunity class: improve, consolidate, create, support, distribute, observe, or reject;
- confidence, proof gap, and measurement hypothesis.

## Workflow

1. Normalize imports through the strict profile and retain raw hash, date range, and mapping.
2. Add first-party discovery, current suggestions/questions, recent channel outliers, competitor/citation sources, and manual observations. Label their origin.
3. Deduplicate synonymous queries while preserving materially different buyers, jobs, locations, and decision stages.
4. Diagnose existing-position buckets as review queues, never automatic actions. A weak position can mean intent mismatch, thin support, poor relevance, authority gap, or the wrong page.
5. Inspect current result composition across traditional, local, video, third-party, commerce, and AI surfaces.
6. Score value, relevance, feasibility, information-gain potential, risk, and measurement readiness separately. Keep the component values visible.
7. Select one bounded cluster. Mark emerging/zero-volume ideas as hypotheses with a dated falsifier.

## Output Requirements

- Source-labeled opportunity table
- Deduplication and intent-split decisions
- Surface-by-surface feasibility notes
- Selected cluster plus explicit parked/rejected opportunities
- Measurement hypothesis per promoted opportunity
- Execution prompt: `references/prompts-v2/36-source-labeled-opportunity-portfolio.md`

## Quality Gate

- [ ] No priority exists without a source field and date
- [ ] CPC, volume, difficulty, and tool scores remain proxies
- [ ] One coherent cluster is selected
- [ ] Zero-volume and AI-demand claims are labeled hypotheses
- [ ] Missing mappings or fields are rejected, not guessed
