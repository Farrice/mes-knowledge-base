# Nathan Gotch AI SEO

Evidence-bounded search mechanics across traditional search, retrieval/citations, local, ecommerce,
on-page, audits, production, measurement, and search-everywhere planning. 39 prompts; the original
2026-07-19 category/citation layer now sits inside an eleven-source portfolio captured 2026-08-04
(see `references/source-ledger.md`).
Nathan Gotch's genius: AI SEO operates at the **retrieval layer**, not the ranking layer. Most optimize for what users see—Gotch optimizes for what AI sees *before* it generates answers.
**Core Insight**: Decisions happen at the retrieval layer, not the visibility layer — and the AI decides by **consensus across its sources**, so the unit of work is a third-party mention.
