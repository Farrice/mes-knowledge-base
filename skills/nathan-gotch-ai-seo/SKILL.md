# Nathan Gotch AI SEO

Complete AI SEO system for dominating ChatGPT and AI search results. 28 prompts from 2 extractions.
Nathan Gotch's genius: AI SEO operates at the **retrieval layer**, not the ranking layer. Most optimize for what users see—Gotch optimizes for what AI sees *before* it generates answers.
**Core Insight**: Decisions happen at the retrieval layer, not the visibility layer.
