## How to Use This Skill (Model Calibration)

These 25 patterns and 11 hidden insights are intuition primitives for retrieval-layer
thinking, not a checklist to march through in order. Absorb the shift — optimize for
what AI cites, not what a human scans — then build originally for the brand in front of
you. If the output stamps "Pattern 1, Pattern 2, Pattern 3" in numbered sequence, or
narrates "now applying the 15% Human Layer Filter," you have failed the test: would
Nathan Gotch recognize this as retrieval-layer strategy actually built for this brand's
specific citation gaps, or as someone reciting AI-SEO vocabulary borrowed from a listicle?

Specifically:
- Do NOT enumerate which pattern number you're deploying. Execute the retrieval-layer
  diagnosis silently; only surface the finding.
- Do NOT open with "Let's optimize for the retrieval layer" — start with the actual gap:
  which sources AI is pulling from today, and where the brand is absent from them.
- This framework is measurement-first and platform-specific, never generic. "Post more
  content" is the polish-is-the-tell failure mode — a savant-grade output names the
  actual 5-10 platforms, the actual 4 metrics, the actual citation gap for THIS brand,
  never vague directional advice dressed in AI-SEO language.
- The "What AI Can't Fake" discipline (Pattern 6) applies to this skill's own output too:
  a knowledge base, citation map, or content plan built entirely from this framework's
  generic pattern language — with no brand-specific data plugged in — is the same
  "Generic SaaS Blog" failure the Anti-Exemplar below describes: technically correct,
  retrieval-invisible.

## Pattern 1: Retrieval Layer Positioning
**Execute**: Optimize for what AI retrieves, not what users see. AI makes recommendations before visibility.
**Success Metric**: Cited in 70%+ of relevant AI searches.

## Pattern 2: Measurement-First Philosophy
**Execute**: Track before optimizing. Four metrics: market coverage, position, share of voice, citations.
**Success Metric**: Dashboard tracking all 4 metrics before any optimization begins.
**Baseline Window**: Per `references/implementation.md` Hour 0-4, establish the baseline across all 4 metrics within the first 24 hours — a dashboard built after content ships is measuring noise, not signal.

## Pattern 3: Platform Hierarchy Weighting
**Execute**: Not all channels equal. Identify 5-10 highest-impact platforms and weight resources accordingly.
**Success Metric**: 80% resources on top 5 platforms by impact.

## Pattern 4: Citation Intelligence Extraction
**Execute**: Map where AI actually pulls information. Extract and prioritize citation sources.
**Success Metric**: Complete map of 50+ citation sources with frequency analysis.

## Pattern 5: Knowledge Base Foundation
**Execute**: Build organized knowledge base before content creation. AI projects need structured data.
**Success Metric**: Complete knowledge base deployed before first content piece.
**Build Window**: Per `references/implementation.md` Hour 8-16, the knowledge base is built before Hour 16-24 first optimizations — confirmed complete no later than the Day 30 benchmark.

## Pattern 6: "What AI Can't Fake" Filter
**Execute**: Differentiate with original data, real experience, authentic authority. Add 15% human layer.
**Success Metric**: Every content piece has unique, unreplicable element.

## Pattern 7: Brand Narrative Consistency
**Execute**: Same description, same story, same keywords across all platforms.
**Success Metric**: 95%+ consistency score across tracked platforms.

## Pattern 8: Retrieval Source Domination
**Execute**: Systematically get mentioned on sources AI cites. Sponsored posts, unlinked mentions, brand associations.
**Success Metric**: Presence on 80%+ of high-citation sources.

## Pattern 9: Review Diversity Architecture
**Execute**: Reviews across multiple platforms, not just one. Diversity signals legitimacy.
**Success Metric**: Active reviews on 5+ platforms.
**Cross-Reference**: Ties to the Day 30 benchmark's "10+ optimized platforms" target in `references/implementation.md` — review diversity is one slice of that broader platform count.

## Pattern 10: Lead Domino Sequencing
**Execute**: Identify single action that unlocks all others. Knowledge base unlocks content which unlocks citations.
**Success Metric**: Clear lead domino identified for every initiative.
**Worked Example**: The clearest lead domino in this framework is the knowledge base itself (Pattern 5, Hidden Knowledge 3) — sequenced first in `references/implementation.md`'s Hour 8-16 build window, before any of the 20+ AI-assisted content pieces the Day 30 benchmark targets.

## Pattern 11: AI Coding Leverage
**Execute**: Build custom tools using AI coding (Replit, Cursor). Small team ships like large team.
**Success Metric**: 1+ custom tool deployed per quarter.
**Cadence**: "1+ custom tool deployed per quarter" (~90 days) syncs with three consecutive 30-Day Transformation cycles in `references/implementation.md`.

## Pattern 12: Rapid Iteration Protocol
**Execute**: Ship fast, measure, iterate. Don't perfect before deploying.
**Success Metric**: Iteration cycle under 48 hours.

## Pattern 13: Competitor Citation Analysis
**Execute**: Map where competitors get cited. Those are your target sources.
**Success Metric**: Complete competitor citation matrix with gaps identified.
**Cross-Reference**: Uses the same source set as the "AI's Brain Scan" signature move below — the top 50+ sources AI cites for the niche — mapped against competitor presence on each.

## Pattern 14: Brand Association Strategy
**Execute**: Associate with well-known brands for trust transfer. "As seen on" = citation fuel.
**Success Metric**: 3+ notable brand associations documented.

## Pattern 15: Visibility Split Diagnosis *(primary-sourced, 2026-07-15 video)*
**Execute**: Decompose visibility into traditional vs AI components before prescribing anything. A #3 organic rank can coexist with 0% AI mentions — "doing good SEO doesn't always drive more AI visibility" (0:00). **Success Metric**: Per-category split scorecard exists before any work is prescribed.

## Pattern 16: Citation Autopsy First *(primary-sourced)*
**Execute**: "Go to the AI citations — this is most of the work that we do these days" (2:50). Extract every source AI cites for the query set; count real third-party brand mentions, separating self-serving citations. Receipt: 4 of 82 citations, 3 self-serving → "they basically have one" (3:00).
**Success Metric**: A counted mention number, not a vibe, is the diagnosis.

## Pattern 17: Consensus Engineering *(primary-sourced)*
**Execute**: The AI "is working through these different sources and then looking for consensus" (3:40). The unit of AI SEO work is a third-party source mention, not an on-site optimization. Fix absence-from-sources before touching the site.
**Success Metric**: Rising mention share across the citation set over scan history.

## Pattern 18: Export-and-Mark Opportunity Mining *(primary-sourced)*
**Execute**: Run multiple keyword reports, export ALL traditional + AI citations to one sheet (Keyword / URL / Platforms / Avg. position / Opportunity-Y), hand-classify every URL: earned (blogs, press, affiliates), owned (FB/IG/YT in retrieval), distribution (marketplaces in retrieval). ~20 earned opportunities per few topics; ×10-20 topics = hundreds (4:16-7:40).
**Success Metric**: Every citation URL classified into a bucket with an action.

## Pattern 19: Retrieval-Informed Distribution *(primary-sourced)*
**Execute**: Marketplace domains inside the citation set (Amazon/Target/Walmart) that the brand isn't on = double-value targets — sales channel AND citation source (12:45).
**Success Metric**: Presence on every retrieval-cited marketplace relevant to the category.

## Pattern 20: Competitor Comparison Ladder *(primary-sourced)*
**Execute**: Seed "[Competitor] alternatives" → expand "[X] vs [Y]" → "[X] vs [Y] vs [us]" — "kills two birds with one stone: you rank for the competitor queries, then you slap yourself in there" (10:27). Template: honest Quick Picks (self takes ONE credible slot), competitor strengths conceded, criteria disclosed. Repeat for every retrieval-cited competitor.
**Success Metric**: Full ladder per competitor; pages survive an honesty sniff-test.

## Pattern 21: Home Base → Echo Expansion *(primary-sourced)*
**Execute**: Build topic authority on the website first; once tapped out, echo the SAME topics to YouTube (long-form first; Shorts secondary), then Facebook/Instagram — "make sure the content format matches whatever distribution channel you're using" (12:10). Influencer outreach on the same channels is the second front (5:50).
**Success Metric**: Each tapped-out topic exists channel-native on every owned channel appearing in retrieval.

## Pattern 22: Benchmark-Annotate-Scan *(primary-sourced)*
**Execute**: "No point tracking performance every day if you aren't working on that category" (13:50). Benchmark snapshot → annotate each shipped asset (dated, categorized) → run scan → read movement against the annotation timeline (14:00-14:30).
**Success Metric**: Every scan preceded by ≥1 dated work annotation; zero always-on tracking spend on unworked categories.

## Pattern 23: Category-Focused Linkbait Prompting *(primary-sourced)*
**Execute**: Prompt at category granularity, never brand granularity — "get real focused on just that one category cuz you get way better ideas" (15:20). Demand data-driven, statistics-driven, research-grounded angles serving three jobs at once: backlinks, PR outreach, social distribution. Generate 25, prioritize 5.
**Success Metric**: Ideas carry named data sources + explicit PR hooks (verbatim prompt: `references/linkbait-prompt-bank.md`).

## Pattern 24: Research → Asset → Design-Agent Chain *(primary-sourced)*
**Execute**: Deep research the chosen angle (his run: 12m 10s) → review limitations → adopt the most defensible lead statistic → convert research into a structured asset (never publish raw research) → generate a design-agent prompt → full visual asset system in the brand's design system (16:17-18:00).
**Success Metric**: Flagship ships with defensible lead stat + complete visual system.

## Pattern 25: Magic-in-the-Edit *(primary-sourced)*
**Execute**: "You don't ever need to start with a blank canvas basically ever. The magic is in the edit… true with design, AI content, AI coding" (18:10). Generate fast at full scope (skip clarifying questions), concentrate human hours on the edit pass — hallucination check, refinement, taste.
**Success Metric**: Human time is post-generation, not pre-generation.

## Hidden Knowledge

11 tacit insights for AI SEO domination.

---

## 1. Retrieval ≠ Ranking
**Insight**: AI search works fundamentally differently—it retrieves and synthesizes rather than ranks. Optimizing for ranking misses the retrieval layer entirely.
**Deploy**: Before any SEO work, map the retrieval layer first.
**Cross-Reference**: See Pattern 1's Success Metric — cited in 70%+ of relevant AI searches is the retrieval-layer target; a ranking-layer metric (SERP position) does not measure it.

## 2. Unlinked Mentions Count
**Insight**: Traditional SEO valued backlinks. AI SEO values mentions—even without links. Brand mention = citation fuel.
**Deploy**: Pursue unlinked mentions as actively as backlinks.
**Cross-Reference**: See Pattern 8's Success Metric — presence on 80%+ of high-citation sources, which counts unlinked brand mentions alongside linked ones.

## 3. Knowledge Base Is the Lead Domino
**Insight**: Everything else (content, citations, tools) depends on organized knowledge. Without it, AI can't help you scale.
**Deploy**: Build knowledge base BEFORE creating content.
**Cross-Reference**: See `references/implementation.md` Hour 8-16 (Knowledge Base Build) — sequenced before Hour 16-24 (First Optimizations).

## 4. Platform Weights Are 80/20
**Insight**: 5-10 platforms determine 80%+ of AI's citation decisions. Most optimization wastes resources on irrelevant platforms.
**Deploy**: Identify top 5-10 platforms, ignore the rest.

## 5. 15% Human Layer Is Non-Negotiable
**Insight**: AI content is detectable. The "what AI can't fake" layer (original data, real experience) is what AI engines trust.
**Deploy**: Every piece needs 15% uniquely human content.

## 6. Coding Is the Multiplier
**Insight**: AI coding (Replit, Cursor) lets small teams ship tools that previously required developers. This is leverage at scale.
**Deploy**: Invest in AI coding skills or hire for them.
**Cross-Reference**: See Pattern 11's Success Metric and the ~90-day quarterly cadence for shipping at least one custom tool.

## 7. One Category, 90-180 Days *(primary-sourced, 2026-07-15)*
**Insight**: "We're usually focused on one category for anywhere between 90 days to 180 days… to make sure we absolutely cover it to the fullest extent. Most businesses never do this. You have to go a mile deep" (0:55). Category focus is the enabling constraint — "If you try to be super broad, it's just too hard" (18:30).
**Deploy**: Pick ONE category; refuse breadth until it's tapped out.

## 8. Press Releases Get Retrieved *(primary-sourced)*
**Insight**: A BusinessWire release appears inside AI-mode citations — "We can run these ourselves, too. The fact that it's getting picked up as a citation tells us what we need to know" (5:06).
**Deploy**: When a wire release shows in the category's citation set, run your own.

## 9. The Gaps Are Always More Granular *(primary-sourced)*
**Insight**: The brand covered sugar-free/keto/grass-fed; the gaps were paleo, weight loss, bodybuilding, zinc/magnesium/vitamin D — "all separate dedicated intents that you'd want to build out assets for" (8:58). Pipe GSC when available for even richer granular intent.
**Deploy**: When coverage looks complete, drop one granularity level and look again.

## 10. Strategy Is Easy; Execution Is the Moat *(primary-sourced)*
**Insight**: "You should be able to develop a strategy in one day… It's easy to develop the strategy. It's hard to execute" (0:40, 13:10). The strategy artifact is one page + one visual map.
**Deploy**: Cap strategy at a day; spend the quarter executing.

## 11. Grounded Ideation Only *(primary-sourced)*
**Insight**: "They're grounded, too, cuz you know that they're grounded because it actually did research" (15:55). Ungrounded idea lists don't ship.
**Deploy**: Every ideation output must name its data sources before it earns a build slot.

*   **Anti-Exemplar: "Generic SaaS Blog" by TechSolutions Inc.**
    TechSolutions Inc. invested heavily in a blog producing 50+ articles monthly, optimized for long-tail keywords using traditional SEO tools. Their content was well-written, covered trending topics, and acquired backlinks. While some articles ranked well for specific keywords, they were rarely cited or synthesized by AI models because the content offered no unique insights, wasn't structured for easy AI retrieval, and wasn't present on the specific external platforms AI used for citations.
    *   **What makes this mediocre**: Fails on Hidden Knowledge 1 (Retrieval ≠ Ranking) by focusing on rankings over AI retrieval. Neglects Pattern 6 ("What AI Can't Fake" Filter) by not providing unique data, and ignores Pattern 8 (Retrieval Source Domination) by not strategically placing content on AI-preferred citation sources.

## Signature Moves

*   **The "AI's Brain Scan"**: Before any content or optimization, the expert initiates a deep scan of AI's current retrieval layer for the target niche. This involves identifying the top 50+ sources AI models frequently cite, the data structures they prefer, and the specific entities they recognize. → **Deploy when**: Starting any new AI SEO project or entering a new market.
*   **The "Unfakable Core" Design**: For every piece of content or knowledge base entry, the expert explicitly defines and integrates a "15% human layer" of original data, proprietary research, or unique experiential insights. This element is designed to be impossible for generic AI to replicate. → **Deploy when**: Planning content creation, knowledge base development, or authority building.
*   **The "Citation Domino Strategy"**: The expert identifies the single highest-leverage external platform or content type that, once established, will naturally lead to citations across multiple other AI-preferred sources. Resources are then hyper-focused on dominating that "lead domino." → **Deploy when**: Prioritizing resource allocation for content distribution or brand association efforts.
*   **The "AI Toolsmith Protocol"**: Upon identifying a repetitive or scalable task (e.g., competitor citation analysis, knowledge base structuring, content generation with unique data integration), the expert immediately scopes out a custom AI-powered tool or script to automate and enhance the process. → **Deploy when**: Encountering any bottleneck or opportunity for exponential leverage.
*   **The "Platform Power Play"**: The expert conducts an exhaustive analysis to identify the top 5-10 external platforms that account for 80% or more of AI's citation activity in a given niche. All subsequent content distribution, partnership, and brand association efforts are then aggressively weighted towards these identified platforms, with minimal effort on others. → **Deploy when**: Formulating a content distribution strategy or assessing competitor presence.
