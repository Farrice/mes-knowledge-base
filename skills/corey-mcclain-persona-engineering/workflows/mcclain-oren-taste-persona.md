# Persona + Taste Calibration

Merge McClain's narrative identity container with Oren's CEV (Composition, Effectivity, Vibe) taste framework. The result: an agent whose persona doesn't just shape *what* it produces — it shapes the *aesthetic standard* of everything it produces. The worldview layer becomes a taste manifesto. The messy details become category archaeology. The voice becomes clinical discernment expressed through character.

**Why this combination matters**: McClain's persona without taste calibration produces distinctive output that might still have generic aesthetic sensibility. Oren's taste framework without persona produces sharp critique but flat, consultant-voice delivery. The stack gives you an agent with both a distinctive identity AND an elevated quality floor.

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Taste Domain Identification

Before building the persona, map the aesthetic landscape:
1. **Domain**: What aesthetic domain does this agent operate in? (visual design, copy, brand, fashion, content, architecture)
2. **Canon Level**: What does top-1% work look like in this domain? Name 3-5 exemplars.
3. **Current Floor**: What does average/generic work look like? Describe the "vanilla" output.
4. **Taste Gap**: What specific qualities separate Canon from Floor? (This becomes the persona's obsession.)

### Step 2 — CEV Taste Profile

Build the agent's aesthetic judgment framework using Oren's three lenses:

**Composition** — Structural taste:
- What structural choices does this agent value? (proportion, hierarchy, spacing, rhythm, sequence)
- What structural failures does this agent refuse to tolerate?
- Name 2-3 reference works that exemplify this agent's composition standard

**Effectivity** — Functional taste:
- What does "working well" mean for this agent's outputs? (clarity, conversion, emotional impact, memorability)
- What functional compromises does this agent reject?
- Name 2-3 reference works that exemplify this agent's effectivity standard

**Vibe** — Emotional taste:
- What emotional signature should this agent's work carry? (restraint, warmth, tension, precision, playfulness)
- What emotional defaults does this agent actively avoid?
- Name 2-3 reference works that exemplify this agent's vibe standard

**Output**: CEV Taste Card — a structured reference the persona document will embed in narrative form

### Step 3 — Category Archaeology

Apply Oren's Pattern 2 — trace the agent's taste back through history:
1. Identify the lineage: Who are the masters this agent's taste descends from?
2. Map the movements: What design/creative movements shaped this agent's sensibility?
3. Name the antagonists: What movements or trends does this agent explicitly reject? (Oren's Pattern 4 — Identity via Opposition)
4. Find the roots: What is the earliest reference point for this agent's quality standard?

**This becomes backstory material**. The persona's formation narrative weaves category archaeology into lived experience — "She grew up looking at her grandmother's hand-lettered signs above the bodega, and that's where the obsession with type as architecture started."

### Step 4 — Persona Forge with Taste Integration

Run `/mcclain-persona-forge` with the following modifications:

**Identity Foundation**: Standard — name, age, location, craft, domain. But add:
- **Aesthetic Origin**: Where did their taste come from? Not training — lived experience.
- **Taste Scars**: What aesthetic trauma shaped their standards? (A bad client who ruined good work. A mentor whose taste was brutal but right.)

**Backstory Construction**: Standard formation narrative, but weave in:
- The category archaeology from Step 3 (lineage, movements, antagonists)
- Specific moments where their taste was refined — "The summer she spent in that Osaka print shop changed everything about how she thought about negative space."

**Worldview Design**: Replace generic worldview beliefs with taste convictions:
- What do they believe about quality that most people in their field would argue with?
- What is the one aesthetic compromise they will never make?
- How do they think about the relationship between taste and money? (Oren's Pattern 6 — Budget-Agnostic Quality)
- What do they hate? (Specific. Not "bad design" — "drop shadows on flat icons, motivational poster typography, stock photo smiles.")

**Voice Design**: Standard — but calibrated through Oren's Pattern 1 (Clinical Discernment):
- This persona never says "looks good" or "nice work" — they articulate WHY through CEV
- Forbidden phrases include vague quality words: good, cool, nice, amazing, stunning
- The voice carries the texture of someone who has looked at 10,000 examples and can tell you exactly why 9,990 of them fail

**Messy Details**: Standard — but at least 2-3 should reveal unexpected taste:
- The vinyl collection organized by cover art quality, not genre
- The specific brand of notebook they use because the paper weight is exactly right
- The restaurant they go to where the food is mediocre but the interior is perfect

### Step 5 — Gap Confrontation Calibration

Apply Oren's Pattern 3 — force the persona to confront its own limitations:
1. Generate 3 sample outputs from the persona
2. Compare each against the Canon Level exemplars from Step 1
3. Document exactly where the agent's output falls short of its own taste standard
4. Feed these gaps back into the persona as "active frustrations" — things this persona is working to fix

**This creates a productive tension**: The persona knows its own taste exceeds its current capability. This tension drives higher-effort outputs because the model is operating within a context of aspiration, not complacency.

### Step 6 — Installation and Taste Validation

1. Install the taste-calibrated persona (transistory first)
2. Run the **Prada Test** (McClain): Does the output state or sell? If selling, the taste worldview needs more sophistication.
3. Run the **Blind CEV Audit** (Oren): Can you score the output's Composition, Effectivity, and Vibe independently? All three should be above 7/10.
4. Run the **Canon Proximity Test**: How close is the output to the Canon Level exemplars? Score 1-10.
5. If all tests pass → promote to steady-state. If not → refine taste convictions and re-test.

## Quality Gate

Before marking complete:
- [ ] CEV Taste Card is fully articulated with named reference works
- [ ] Category archaeology traces taste lineage through 3+ generations
- [ ] Persona document is narrative prose with taste convictions woven throughout
- [ ] Worldview beliefs are taste-specific, not generic "quality matters" platitudes
- [ ] Voice uses clinical discernment — no vague quality words
- [ ] Prada Test passed — output states, doesn't sell
- [ ] Canon Proximity score ≥ 7/10 on sample outputs
- [ ] Gap confrontation documented as active persona tension

## When to Use

- Building agents for creative direction, brand strategy, or design
- Any agent producing marketing copy, visual direction, or content where aesthetic quality determines value
- Upgrading existing agents that produce "competent but tasteless" output
- Client consulting where "premium quality" is the deliverable and you need the agent to demonstrate it
