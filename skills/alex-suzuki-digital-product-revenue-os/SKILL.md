# Alex Suzuki Digital Product Revenue OS

## Domain
Digital product revenue systems for ebooks, courses, templates, communities, services, and info products — sold through high-specificity posts, comment/like triggers, trust containers, automation, and checkout or application paths. Scales from a single tweet to a multi-account portfolio, and starts from cold even with no product, proof, or following.

## Owner Role
This skill owns the final output for Suzuki-style digital product selling, scaling, and cold-start. Other experts can support product economics, sales copy, or platform adaptation, but the Suzuki OS decides the final asset structure, funnel, and proof/compliance gate.

## Core Thesis
The sales post is only the public tip of the machine. The real system is:

`specific result post -> comment/like trigger -> DM/free asset -> trust container -> checkout or conversation -> reply sprint -> proof loop`

The operating edge is not broad reach. It is a high-fit audience taking a small public action around a product-relevant promise.

At scale, the full blueprint adds two layers: a **cold-start path** (sell a partner's real proof or an affiliate product when you have none) and an **automation/portfolio layer** (generate + schedule + opt-in auto-DM across many accounts). See `references/scaling-mechanics.md`.

## Floor-Raise Standard

## Required Output Floor
Every public-facing run must produce:
1. Buyer readiness diagnosis.
2. Purchase-adjacent free asset.
3. Direct checkout, application, or conversation lane.
4. First-use or first-7-day path after purchase.
5. Proof ladder with concrete proof or explicit missing proof.
6. Reply/DM routing by intent level: cold, warm, ready, wrong fit.
7. Launch path (sprint or evergreen) with replay templates, not only one post.
8. Compliance ledger when any claim, borrowed proof, affiliate, or automation is involved.

If the output resembles a question map only, neutral report, or "comment for guide" asset without purchase motion, automatically run the Launch Debugger before delivery.

## Quality Gate
Every output must include: source mechanic used; finished buyer-facing asset; funnel step after the post; buyer readiness diagnosis; purchase-adjacent free asset; direct or conversation lane; first-use/first-7-day path; proof gaps or uncertainty; one next action.

Block delivery if:
- Results are promised as guaranteed.
- The product lacks a clear buyer and outcome.
- The CTA has no concrete free asset or next step.
- High-ticket selling is forced through a thin self-serve checkout.
- The post attracts spectators instead of likely buyers.
- The free asset only creates caution or research without helping the buyer decide.
- Ready buyers have no permission-based path to checkout, link, application, or conversation.
- **Borrowed/partner proof is used without disclosure, an affiliate link lacks disclosure, an income figure is presented as guaranteed, or auto-DM would fire without an opt-in trigger** (see `references/compliance-gate.md`).

## Suzuki Output Rubric
Minimum pass: buyer recognizes themselves quickly; the asset makes the paid step feel like the next tool in a plan; CTA separates curiosity from purchase intent; DM flow routes cold/warm/ready/wrong-fit differently; checkout/application path matches price and risk; proof is concrete or explicitly missing; buyer sees what to do first after purchase; launch plan includes reusable replay templates; all claims/disclosures pass the compliance gate.
