# Voice & Texture — How He Actually Writes

Distilled from watching the real posts (`real-post-teardowns.md`). This is the embodiment layer: the difference between *"a summary of what Suzuki does"* (5/10, generic, unusable) and *a post that actually pulls qualified buyers into the DMs.* Load this before writing any post. **The failure mode is vagueness. The cure is specificity + mechanism + a payload freebie.**

## The 9 signature moves (every strong post uses 5+)
1. **Transformation headline, two hard numbers, line 1.** `"I helped a [buyer] go from [X] to [Y]…"` The first line is a before→after with concrete figures, not a topic.
2. **The enemy line.** One sentence naming the lazy/default thing everyone else does, so the reader feels the contrast. *("most founders are still fumbling around with cloud-code and buying something stolen.")*
3. **Invented proprietary mechanism.** Coin your own named system + jargon — "Business Aerodynamics," "Drag Map," "the Aerodynamics Audit." A named method reads as IP you own, not advice anyone could give. **This is the single most-missed move.**
4. **Before → strip → After, bulleted.** Show the messy before (bulleted specifics), what you removed/built (bulleted), and the after (bulleted, with the metrics). Never a paragraph where bullets would hit harder.
5. **The payload freebie.** The CTA gives away a *numbered stack of named deliverables* (aim 3–5). Each one gets a full sentence with a **number, a scale, a duration, or a sample size** ("75-question diagnostic, scores 0–100, takes 60 minutes, based on 30+ builds"). A vague "free guide" is an instant fail.
6. **Extreme concrete specificity.** Every claim carries a number, percentage, count, timeframe, or named artifact. Round-number vagueness ("a lot," "quickly," "results") is banned.
7. **Mechanical CTA.** One keyword, one action stack: `Comment "KEYWORD" (must follow + RT so I can DM)`. The RT is free distribution; the follow is the list; the comment is the opt-in.
8. **Quality-over-reach targeting.** Written for a small, high-fit, capitalized buyer — not the masses. "Audience quality is a reflection of the copy": the copy itself filters.
9. **The 10-minute rule + retweet boost.** Post → reply-sprint the first ~10 minutes → retweet from bigger accounts to manufacture reach. (Ops, not copy — but it's why the posts land.)

## The specificity gate (fail the draft if any is true)
- [ ] Line 1 is a topic or a question instead of a transformation with numbers → **rewrite**
- [ ] No invented/named mechanism anywhere → **add one**
- [ ] The freebie is one vague thing ("free guide/course") instead of a numbered stack of spec'd deliverables → **rebuild the freebie**
- [ ] Any sentence uses "a lot / quickly / results / a bunch / some" where a number belongs → **replace with the number**
- [ ] No enemy/contrast line → **add one**
- [ ] CTA has no keyword + follow/RT mechanic → **fix the CTA**
Three-plus unchecked = the post is generic. Do not ship it.

## Compliant-grip: keeping the texture WITHOUT income claims
Suzuki's real posts are drenched in income/valuation claims. For affiliate/make-money niches (DWA) those are **banned** (FTC). The mistake I made the first time was stripping the claims *and* the grip. Keep the grip by moving specificity off money and onto **skills, outputs, time, and process**:

| His move (income-based) | Compliant version (same grip, no income claim) |
|---|---|
| "went from $493K to $1.8M valuation" | "went from a blank page to a published 6-piece content system in 9 days" |
| "$100K/month posting simple tweets" | "publishes in ~20 min/day using 5 repeatable post skeletons" |
| "$27K from one post" | "one post → 40 saved templates → a finished landing page in an afternoon" |
| Income-screenshot proof | Process/artifact proof: the actual templates, the build, the before/after *of the work* |
| "Aerodynamics Audit" (invented mechanism) | Invent your OWN mechanism — "The Faceless Skill Stack," "The 5-Signal Content System" — fully allowed and encouraged |

The invented mechanism, the payload freebie, the extreme specificity, the enemy line, the before/after — **all of these are 100% compliant** and are what create grip. Only the *money claims* get swapped for *skill/output/time* specifics.

>

>
> I'm giving the whole starter kit away free (today):
> **1. The Skill Stack Map** — the 5 skills in the exact order to learn them, with the one project that proves each (so you're building, not just watching).
> **2. The 9-Day Publish Plan** — a naptime-sized daily checklist that ends with your first live page and first 12 posts.
> **3. The 5 Post Skeletons** — fill-in-the-blank templates that make a post a 15-minute job, not a blank-page stare.
> **4. The "Is this you?" Fit Check** — an honest 10-question screen so you know before you spend a cent whether this is your lane.

> No income promises — this is a skill and reps, not a lottery ticket.

Notice: named mechanism (Faceless Skill Stack), transformation-in-outputs (blank page → live page in 9 days), enemy line (6 months learning, never publishing), 4-part spec'd freebie (each with specifics), extreme concreteness (12 posts, 3-email, 9 days, 10 questions) — and **zero income claims**, with disclosure. *That* is usable.

## Next-Step Over Flex (skeptical / burnt-out niches)
For affiliate + "make money online" + info-product niches, the audience's question has flipped from aspiration ("how do I make $X") to **verification + execution** ("has a normal person done this, and what do I do on day one?"). The lifestyle/income flex is burnt out — it *triggers* the scam-reflex. Win by doing the opposite of the category:
- **Answer the execution question everyone dodges.** End every post with ONE concrete action a beginner could do in 20 minutes — not a promise.
- **Show the reps, not the results.** A filmed boring loop ("kid down at 1pm, one post, 3 DMs, done by 1:30") out-converts an income screenshot with a skeptical buyer — and a flexer can't fake it.
- **Honest curve, not the dream.** "Month one is $0–100; first sale ranges days-to-months; count reps; first target = $1,000." Naming the unglamorous truth IS the differentiation.
- **Disqualify.** Tell the wrong-fit buyer not to buy — a real coach turns people away.
This is the compliant-grip rule taken to positioning: specificity lives in the *next step*, trust is earned by answering what the flexers can't. Applied engine: `_active/dwa-affiliate-battle-test/04-deliverables/09-question-led-content-engine.md`.
