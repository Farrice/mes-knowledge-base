# Funnel Map Reference

## Low-Ticket Digital Product Path
Use for roughly `$30-$500` products when trust and proof are enough for direct checkout.

1. Post or thread.
2. Comment/reply keyword.
3. Reply sprint and DM handoff.
4. Free asset: sample page, checklist, calculator, teardown, or mini lesson.
5. Checkout page with fit, outcome, proof, table of contents, FAQ, and refund rule.
6. 3-7 day email follow-up.
7. Before/after or proof post retargeting organic readers.

Minimum decision motion:
- Value lane sends the free asset, score, sample, or first-use path.
- Purchase lane runs a quick fit check, then sends checkout by permission.
- First 24 hours after purchase are mapped so the buyer can start.

## High-Ticket Product Path
Use when price, complexity, or buyer risk requires conversation.

1. Post or thread.
2. Comment/reply keyword.
3. DM to free asset/community.
4. Trust container: email, Telegram, Discord, webinar, or private resource hub.
5. Application or diagnostic questions.
6. Call, text conversation, or closer flow.
7. Guarantee or risk reversal.
8. Proof follow-up and objections.

Minimum decision motion:
- Value lane sends proof bundle and diagnostic.
- Purchase lane routes to application, questions, call, or private trust container.
- First 7 days after purchase or acceptance are mapped so the buyer sees support and implementation.

## Intent Lane Map

| Intent | Public Signal | DM Job | Next Step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold | Comments for free asset only | Deliver value, ask one context question | Invite score/context reply |
| Warm | Shares score, use case, or objection | Interpret fit, point to first-use path | Offer link/application only if relevant |
| Ready | Asks for link, price, checkout, application, or "can I buy?" | Disclose, fit-check, confirm permission | Send checkout/application/call link |
| Wrong fit | Wants guarantee, urgent rescue, or shortcut | Protect trust, explain mismatch | Give no-cost next move or wait path |

## Purchase-Adjacent Free Asset Options

| Asset | Best For | Must Include |
|---|---|---|
| Readiness score | Affiliate course, high-risk purchase, cautious buyer | Score, lanes, link criteria, wrong-fit note |
| First-use map | Course, ebook, template, community | Day 1-7 or first 3 uses |
| Sample plus worked example | Ebook, template, small course | Sample, example, paid-product bridge |
| Decision map | Saturated or controversial offer | Fit lanes, proof, risk note, next step |
| Proof bundle | Buyer doubts value | Before/after, FAQ, first step, proof limits |

## Trust Container Options
| Container | Best For | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Email | Low-ticket and repeat launches | Slower feedback |
| Telegram | Fast response and warm group energy | Can feel thin without moderation |
| Discord | Community, modules, coaching, cohort | Setup and upkeep burden |
| Checkout page | Simple ebooks/templates | Needs enough proof on page |
| Application | High-ticket or limited access | Adds friction |

## First-Use Path
Every funnel needs a post-purchase plan:

- Low-ticket: first 30 minutes, first 24 hours, or first 3 uses.
- Course/community: first 7 days.
- High-ticket: onboarding, diagnostic, first call, first implementation, proof collection.

## Minimum Checkout Copy
- Headline: buyer + outcome.
- Subhead: what the product helps them do.
- Bullets: concrete assets inside.
- Proof: sample, beta feedback, case, or demo.
- Fit: who it is for and not for.
- FAQ: price, time, access, refund, support.
- CTA: direct purchase.
- First-use path: what to do immediately after buying.

## Minimum Application Or Conversation Copy
- Fit promise: who should apply or ask for the link.
- Diagnostic questions: buyer, problem, readiness, constraints.
- Proof: what can be shown safely.
- Boundary: what is not promised.
- Next step: call, application, community, or reply.
