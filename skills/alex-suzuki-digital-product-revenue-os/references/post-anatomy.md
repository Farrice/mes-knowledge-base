## The Three Invariants (what carries every sale)
1. **Qualified result hook** — first line names a specific buyer + a specific result/asset + a number or time frame. It *repels* the wrong reader on purpose.
2. **Proof-bearing body** — short detail of what was built/done + a concrete proof cue (owned facts when revenue proof is thin: page count, module count, sample, turnaround, beta use).
3. **Opt-in, multi-freebie CTA** — "like/comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send [concrete free asset]." The like/comment is a public micro-commitment that becomes a permissioned DM (and, at scale, an auto-DM). See `scaling-mechanics.md` and `compliance-gate.md`.

Everything else (formatting, length, emojis) is style. These three are the machine.

## The Simple-for-Scale Variant (video 3) [`00:04:30-00:05:30`]
At portfolio scale, posts are deliberately simple — header + a few detail lines + CTA — typed in ~15-20 seconds so they can be **AI-generated and scheduled by the hundred**. Simplicity is not laziness; it is what makes volume + automation possible.

```
[Eye-catching header: buyer + outcome/number]
[2-4 plain detail lines: what it is, why it works, one proof cue]
[CTA: like/comment KEYWORD → free guide/course; ready buyers → link]
```
Trade-off: simple posts lean harder on **audience quality + offer + auto-DM** to convert. Keep the three invariants even when short.

## Hook Bank (15, by type)
**Result/number**
1. `I made [$X] from one [asset] for [buyer]. Here's the build.`
2. `[Number] [buyer]s asked how to [outcome], so I built [asset].`
3. `[Buyer]: I turned [messy input] into [paid output] in [time].`

**Asset/owned-proof (use when revenue proof is thin)**
4. `I wrote a [page-count]-page [topic] playbook for [buyer]. Free sample below.`
5. `[Module count] lessons that take a [buyer] from [before] to [after].`
6. `The exact [system/template] I use across [n] accounts — packaged for [buyer].`

**Problem/contrarian**
7. `[Buyer] are losing [cost] to [problem]. Here's the fix I built.`
8. `Everyone tells [buyer] to [common advice]. It's why they stay [stuck]. Do this instead.`
9. `You don't need [expensive thing] to [outcome]. You need [your asset].`

**Curiosity/mechanism**
10. `The [adjective] reason [buyer] can [outcome] with almost no [resource].`
11. `I found a way for [buyer] to [outcome] without [hated behavior].`

**Proof/case (partner or self)**
12. `How I helped [partner type] do [claim-labeled result] in [time].`
13. `[Buyer] went from [before] to [after] using this [asset]. Steps below.`

**Offer-led (low-ticket direct)**
14. `[$price] [product]: [specific promise] for [buyer]. Free sample first.`
15. `If you're a [buyer] who wants [result], this [product] is the next tool. Comment [KEYWORD].`

## Swipe Templates (5 reusable skeletons)
1. **Build reveal** — Hook(result) → "here's what's inside" bullets → proof cue → opt-in CTA → reply-sprint plan.
2. **Problem→asset** — Hook(problem+cost) → why common fixes fail → the asset → CTA → ready-buyer lane.
3. **Case study** — Hook(claim-labeled result) → 3-step how → "I packaged this" → CTA → disclosure.
4. **Free-first** — Hook(free valuable thing) → what they get free → "the paid next step is X" → two-lane CTA.
5. **Offer-direct (low-ticket)** — Hook(price+promise) → bullets → sample CTA → purchase CTA → first-use line.

## AI-Generation Prompt Pack (volume, claim-safe)
Use to batch-draft, then **human-edit every post against `compliance-gate.md` before scheduling**.
- *Bulk hooks:* "Generate 20 first-line hooks for [buyer] selling [product]. Each must include a specific buyer + a result/asset + a number or time frame. No income guarantees. No invented results."
- *Bodies:* "For hook [X], write 3 bodies: context, what's inside (bullets), one owned-proof cue (page/module/sample/turnaround). Under 60 words. No earnings claims."
- *CTAs:* "Write 5 opt-in CTAs: a value keyword for the free asset + a purchase keyword for ready buyers. Include the free asset name."
- *Variation:* "Rewrite this winning post 10 ways, changing proof, buyer situation, and free asset — keep the structure."
Guardrail: AI drafts are raw material. Claims, proof, and disclosures are a human responsibility.

## Scroll-Stop / Buyer-Quality Test (run before publishing)
| Check | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Stops the right buyer | The named buyer recognizes themselves in line 1 |
| Repels the wrong reader | A non-buyer scrolls past — that's correct |
| Result/asset is concrete | A number, page count, or specific deliverable is present |
| Proof is real | Owned fact or claim-labeled — never invented |
| CTA is opt-in + specific | Keyword + a concrete free asset; ready-buyer lane exists |
| Generatable at scale | Could 50 variants be made without lying |

Three or more fails → run `/suzuki-launch-debugger` before posting.
