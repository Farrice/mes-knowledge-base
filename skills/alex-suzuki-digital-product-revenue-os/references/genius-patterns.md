# Genius Patterns Reference

## Source-Grounded Patterns
1. Specific first line: numbers, time frame, buyer, result.
2. Detail body: problem, action, proof, output, result.
3. CTA with concrete freebies and comment keyword.
4. First 10 minutes: replies and DM prompts are operational work.
5. Buyer quality over raw views.
6. Comment-to-DM-to-free-asset path.
7. Low-ticket direct checkout; high-ticket conversation path.
8. Account trust and profile readiness before launch.
9. Repeat formats that already attract qualified buyers.
10. Avoid outcome guarantees when using claims.

## Transfer Principle
The reusable genius is not the exact wording. It is the sequence:

`specific proof -> public micro-action -> private trust handoff -> purchase path -> proof loop`

## Output Bar
A Suzuki OS output is good enough when a buyer can answer:

- Is this for me?
- What result or asset do I get?
- Why should I believe it?
- What do I do next?
- What happens after I comment, reply, or click?

## Scaling & Cold-Start Patterns (video 3 — `YaonYaeQlA4`)
11. Borrowed proof: partner with a proven operator, run their content, split revenue.
12. Affiliate path: sell an already-validated product; skip creation and demand-testing.
13. The account is the scaling unit: add accounts (niche × proof source), not just posts.
14. Manufacture first distribution: a retweet/boost network makes a cold account viral.
15. Automation = create + schedule + auto-DM (opt-in only), as three separate jobs.
16. Qualified comments × price beats views; each like/comment is a lead.
17. High-ticket routes free → application → founder call → close, not self-serve checkout.
18. Aligned profit-split self-selects committed buyers and raises success rate.
19. The 6-section mirrorboard is a reusable high-ticket sales asset.
20. Posts are simple on purpose so they can be generated and scheduled at volume.

## Scaling Transfer Principle
The reusable genius at scale is the operating split:

`proof source (own / partner / affiliate) -> account -> generated posts -> scheduled -> auto-DM (opt-in) -> checkout or application -> proof loop -> add account`

Every revenue figure is a creator claim. The mechanics transfer; the numbers do not. See `compliance-gate.md`.
