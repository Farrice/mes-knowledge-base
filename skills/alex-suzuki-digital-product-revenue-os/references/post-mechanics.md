# Post Mechanics

## The 7-Part Sales Post
1. **Qualified result hook**: name buyer, result, asset, or proof in the first line.
2. **Context**: why this mattered or what problem it solves.
3. **Build details**: what was made or changed.
4. **Concrete outputs**: bullets with deliverables, numbers, examples, or modules.
5. **Proof cue**: result, sample, page count, beta use, teardown, or working example.
6. **Decision asset CTA**: comment, reply, or DM keyword for a specific free asset that helps the buyer choose a lane.
7. **Purchase lane**: a separate keyword, reply path, or permission step for buyers already asking for link, checkout, application, or details.
8. **Handoff promise**: what the user receives, how the DM routes them, and what happens after purchase or application.

## Hook Formulas
- `I built [specific asset] for [buyer] who [pain].`
- `[Number] [buyer type] asked me how to [outcome], so I made [asset].`
- `I turned [messy input] into [concrete paid output] in [time].`
- `[Price] product: [specific promise] for [buyer]. Free sample below.`
- `If [buyer] wants [outcome] without [hated behavior], use this [asset].`

## CTA Formulas
- `Comment [KEYWORD] and I will DM [specific free asset] plus [proof sample].`
- `Reply [KEYWORD] and I will send the checklist and the first worked example.`
- `Want the sample before buying? Comment [KEYWORD].`
- `If you are [buyer] and want [result], comment [KEYWORD].`
- `Comment [VALUE KEYWORD] for the map. Comment [BUYER KEYWORD] if you are already considering the paid step and want the fit check first.`
- `Comment [PLAN] for the first-use path. Comment [LINK/APPLY] if you already want the next step and are okay with a quick fit check.`

## Decision-Asset Rules
The free asset must do more than create caution. It must give the buyer a useful next move.

Strong assets:
- Scorecard plus lane.
- Sample plus worked example.
- Decision map plus first 7 days.
- Proof bundle plus FAQ.
- Mini teardown plus buyer-specific next action.

Weak assets:
- Questions only.
- Generic checklist.
- Awareness guide with no paid path.
- Link promise with no fit check or proof.

## Intent Routing
Use different public replies or DMs for:

| Buyer Intent | Signal | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Cold | Likes the topic but asks broad questions | Send the value asset and ask one context question |
| Warm | Shares problem, score, or specific use case | Send asset, first-use path, and invite a next-step reply |
| Ready | Asks for price, link, application, or "how do I buy?" | Disclose, run fit check, then send checkout/application by permission |
| Wrong fit | Wants guaranteed result, urgent rescue, or shortcut | Give safer next move and do not push purchase |

## Buyer-Quality Test
Before publishing, score the post:

| Signal | Pass Condition |
|---|---|
| Buyer named | A likely buyer recognizes themselves immediately |
| Problem named | The pain is active, costly, or urgent |
| Free asset fit | The free asset previews the paid result |
| Proof cue | There is a believable reason to trust the offer |
| CTA action | Comment/reply keyword is easy and specific |
| Follow-up path | DM, email, checkout, or application is ready |
| Link lane | Ready buyers have a permission-based paid next step |
| First-use path | Buyer knows what to do after buying |

Do not publish if three or more signals fail.

## OS Floor Test
Fail the draft and run Launch Debugger if it only produces:
- A question map with no score, lane, or first-use plan.
- A post with one value CTA and no purchase-intent path.
- A DM that sends a guide but never asks for score, context, or readiness.
- A checkout link with no fit, disclosure, proof, or support boundary.
