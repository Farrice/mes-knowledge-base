# Portfolio Scale Engine

## Use When
The user wants to scale beyond one account — design or expand a multi-account portfolio, add an account, or plan first-distribution. The scaling unit is the account (niche × proof source × offer), not the post.

## Load First
- `../genius.md`
- `../references/scaling-mechanics.md`
- `../references/compliance-gate.md`
- `../references/composition-ledger.md`

## Pre-Flight Gate
Confirm: do you have a proof source for each new account (own, partner, or affiliate)? If not, route to `/suzuki-product-source` first. Multi-account is platform-risk-bearing — load `compliance-gate.md` and keep accounts genuinely distinct.

## Steps
1. **Define the unit(s)**: for each account name niche × proof source × offer.
2. **Niche selection**: meta-niche ("teach how to make money") or an adjacent topic with capitalized buyers; quick demand read (are people already selling here = demand proof).
3. **Proof source per account**: own / partner / affiliate. Missing → `/suzuki-product-source`.
4. **First-distribution plan**: retweet/boost network or paid boost for each new account's first 1-3 posts (treat as a required input).
5. **Automation handoff**: per-account create→schedule→auto-DM (`/suzuki-automate`).
6. **Cadence + saturation guardrails**: distinct copy/identity per account; no duplicated posts; no fake engagement.
7. **Unit economics target**: qualified comments × price per account; kill/keep rule.
8. **Compliance pass**: multi-account ToS + claim ledger.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Mode | Focus |
|---|---|
| Single-niche scale | One niche, multiple accounts/angles; watch saturation |
| Multi-niche portfolio | Several niches, one proof source each |
| Partner network | One account per partner; deal terms + disclosure per account |
| Affiliate stack | One account per proven product; FTC disclosure per account |

## Output Format
```markdown
## Account Map
| Account | Niche | Proof Source | Offer | First-Distribution |
## Distribution Plan (boost/retweet per new account)
## Per-Account Economics Targets (qualified comments × price, kill/keep)
## Saturation + Compliance Note (distinct identity, ToS, claims)
## Next Account To Launch
```

## Quality Gate
- Every account has a real proof source, an offer, and a distribution plan.
- Accounts are genuinely distinct (no duplicated copy/identity, no fake engagement).
- Economics target and kill/keep rule are explicit.
- Claims labeled; multi-account ToS risk noted with the safer alternative.
