# Product Funnel Builder

## Use When
The product needs the system behind the post: offer, checkout, email, DM, trust path, and proof.

## Load First
- `../references/funnel-map.md`
- `../references/revenue-mechanics.md`

## Steps
1. **Price path**: decide direct checkout, trust container, application, or call.
2. **Offer spec**: define deliverables, buyer outcome, price, bonus, and risk reversal.
3. **Buyer readiness diagnosis**: define cold, warm, ready, and wrong-fit lanes.
4. **Free asset bridge**: design the purchase-adjacent free asset that previews the paid outcome and helps the buyer decide.
5. **Intent routing**: create value-lane and purchase-lane DM paths.
6. **Checkout or application copy**: create the paid path that matches price and trust.
7. **First-use path**: map what the buyer does after purchase or application.
8. **Email/DM follow-up**: write a 3-message sequence for day 1, day 3, and day 7.
9. **Proof gate**: list missing proof and what to collect.
10. **Replay hooks**: give 3 post frames that can retarget objections or proof.

## Quality Gate
- Path matches price and trust level.
- Free asset leads toward a decision and purchase path.
- Cold, warm, ready, and wrong-fit buyers are routed differently.
- Checkout copy includes fit and FAQ.
- Paid path includes first-use plan.
- Follow-up is concrete enough to send.
