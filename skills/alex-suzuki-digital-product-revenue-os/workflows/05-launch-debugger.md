# Launch Debugger

## Use When
A digital product post or funnel is not converting, or the user wants to diagnose a weak post before publishing.

## Load First
- `../references/post-mechanics.md`
- `../references/funnel-map.md`
- `../references/hidden-knowledge.md`
- `../references/revenue-mechanics.md`

## Diagnostic Grid
| Layer | Failure Question |
|---|---|
| Buyer | Does the right buyer recognize themselves? |
| Hook | Is there a specific result, asset, time frame, or pain? |
| Proof | Is there a believable reason to act? |
| Free asset | Does it preview the paid result and help the buyer decide? |
| CTA | Does it separate value seekers from purchase-intent buyers? |
| Follow-up | Does the comment/reply lead to an intent-routed DM path? |
| Price path | Does price match trust level? |
| Audience | Did it attract buyers or spectators? |
| First-use | Does the buyer know what happens after purchase? |
| Replay | Is there a 7-day or repeatable launch path after the post? |

## Steps
1. Score each layer as pass, weak, or fail.
2. Identify whether the draft is V1/V2 failure class: question-map-only, caution-only, single CTA, no link lane, no first-use plan, or neutral report.
3. Identify the primary bottleneck.
4. Rewrite the post or funnel section that caused the bottleneck.
5. Add buyer readiness lanes and a direct or conversation paid path.
6. Add missing proof or safety language.
7. Add first-use path and replay template.
8. Produce a before/after behavior delta.

## Output Format
```markdown
# Suzuki Launch Debugger

## Diagnosis
| Layer | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|

## Primary Bottleneck
[One bottleneck]

## Repaired Asset
[Rewritten post, CTA, funnel, DM, or checkout section]

## Buyer Routing Repair
[Cold, warm, ready, wrong-fit DM/reply routing]

## First-Use Or Launch Replay Repair
[First-use path or 7-day replay sequence]

## Behavior Delta
[What changed and why it should move buyer behavior]

## Proof Gaps
[What remains unproven]
```

## Quality Gate
- Choose one primary bottleneck.
- Repair the artifact, not just the idea.
- State the behavior change.
- If the asset creates safety but no desire, say that plainly and add decision motion.
- If comments exist but sales do not, test audience quality, free-asset fit, and purchase-lane friction before blaming reach.
- Do not pass a repaired draft that lacks a paid-intent lane.
