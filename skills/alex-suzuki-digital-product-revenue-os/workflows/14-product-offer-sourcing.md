# Product & Offer Sourcing

## Use When
The operator needs something to sell. Three paths: **Build** (AI-assisted from owned expertise), **Partner** (borrowed proof — sell a proven operator's offer), or **Affiliate** (sell an already-validated product). This is the cold-start product solve.

## Pre-Flight Gate
Diagnose starting position: Do you have your own product? Your own proof? Decision rule — **no product + no proof → Partner.** **want a ready offer fast → Affiliate.** **have expertise/proof → Build.**

## Steps
1. **Diagnose + choose path** using the decision rule.
2. **Path A — Build**: scope a fast digital product from owned expertise (ebook/course). Spec page/module count, sample, outcome, price; plan claim-safe proof (owned facts + beta).
3. **Path B — Partner**: define the ideal partner (real documented proof + a real offer + no time). Draft the outreach frame and deal structure (split %, attribution, disclosure). Proof must be theirs and real.
4. **Path C — Affiliate**: select a product with proven sell-through; define the buyer-outcome angle; add FTC disclosure.
5. **Offer ladder**: tripwire → mid-ticket → high-ticket (application) matched to price/risk.
6. **First-use path** for the chosen product.
7. **Compliance**: proof real (own/partner) or product-validated (affiliate); disclosures; no income guarantees.

## Output Format
```markdown
## Starting Position + Path Chosen (why)
## Offer Spec  /  Partner Brief  /  Affiliate Brief
## Offer Ladder (tripwire → mid → high-ticket application)
## Proof Plan (real proof or product validation)
## Disclosure Block (partnership / affiliate / earnings)
## Next Action (today)
```

## Quality Gate
- A concrete sellable offer exists at the end (not "ideas").
- Proof is real or a real plan to get it; affiliate demand is validated.
- Disclosures present for partner/affiliate/earnings.
- Offer ladder matches price and buyer risk; first-use path included.
