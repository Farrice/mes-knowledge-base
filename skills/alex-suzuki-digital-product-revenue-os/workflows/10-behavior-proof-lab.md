# Behavior Proof Lab

## Use When
The system needs to prove that a weak post or funnel has been transformed into a sales-capable asset.

## Load First
- `../references/post-mechanics.md`
- `../references/funnel-map.md`
- `../references/hidden-knowledge.md`
- `../references/revenue-mechanics.md`

## Steps
1. Capture the before asset.
2. Identify the behavior failure.
3. Rewrite the asset using Suzuki mechanics.
4. Map each change to expected buyer behavior.
5. Run the OS floor test: buyer readiness, purchase-adjacent asset, paid-intent lane, first-use path, proof ladder, intent routing, and replay sprint.
6. List remaining proof gaps.

## Output Format
```markdown
## Before
[Original post/funnel]

## Failure Class
[Buyer, hook, proof, CTA, funnel, price path, audience]

## After
[Rebuilt asset]

## OS Floor Test
| Criterion | Pass/Fail | Evidence |
|---|---|---|

## Behavior Delta
| Change | Expected Behavior |
|---|---|

## Remaining Proof Gaps
[Gaps]
```

## Quality Gate
- The after asset must be publishable or close to publishable.
- The after asset must beat question-map-only output.
- The after asset must include value and purchase-intent lanes.
- The after asset must show first-use or first-7-day path.
- The behavior delta must be specific.
- Proof gaps must be named plainly.
