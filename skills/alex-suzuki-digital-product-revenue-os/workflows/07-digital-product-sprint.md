# Digital Product Sprint

## Use When
The user wants a 7-day or short launch sprint for a digital product.

## Load First
- `../references/funnel-map.md`
- `../references/revenue-mechanics.md`
- `../references/post-mechanics.md`
- `../references/composition-ledger.md`

## Steps
1. Define launch goal and path.
2. Choose launch asset sequence: proof, decision asset, main post, purchase-lane post, adaptation, objection, first-use, final push.
3. Assign daily work blocks.
4. Add first 10-minute reply sprints and DM windows.
5. Add cold/warm/ready/wrong-fit routing prompts.
6. Add proof collection and checkout/application updates.
7. Add replay templates so winning posts can be repeated with new proof.

## 7-Day Sprint Template
| Day | Build | Publish | Follow-Up |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Proof bank and buyer map | Teaser or proof post | Gather questions |
| 2 | Decision asset | Sample/freebie post | DM interested people and ask for score/context |
| 3 | Checkout or application | Objection post | Add FAQ |
| 4 | Main sales post | X/primary platform post | 10-minute reply sprint plus ready-buyer lane |
| 5 | Platform adaptation | LinkedIn/email/Instagram asset | DM and email follow-up |
| 6 | Before/after proof | First-use or transformation post | Answer objections |
| 7 | Final reminder | Deadline, recap, or support-boundary post | Collect learnings and replay winners |

## Output Format
```markdown
## Launch Goal
[Goal]

## Asset Stack
[Post, free asset, checkout, email, proof]

## 7-Day Calendar
[Day-by-day table]

## Daily Reply Windows
[Times and scripts]

## Intent Routing
[Cold, warm, ready, wrong-fit prompts]

## Replay Templates
[3-5 reusable frames with new proof/objection/first-use angle]

## Success Signals
[What to watch]

## Stop/Adjust Signals
[What means the launch needs repair]
```

## Quality Gate
- The 7-Day Calendar has a Build/Publish/Follow-Up entry for every day 1-7, matching the template shape — no skipped or merged days.
- Day 4 (main sales post) explicitly carries the 10-minute reply sprint and a ready-buyer lane, not a generic "post and monitor" note.
- Replay Templates give 3-5 genuinely different reusable frames (new proof angle, new objection, new first-use story), never the same post copy-pasted.
- Intent Routing has a distinct prompt per lane (cold, warm, ready, wrong-fit) — a shared script for all four fails this gate.
- Stop/Adjust Signals name a real, checkable trigger (e.g., comments with no DMs by hour 2, DMs with no checkout clicks by day 3), not a vague "if it's not working."
- No day's Publish column implies guaranteed sales; proof-bank claims stay labeled per `revenue-mechanics.md`.
