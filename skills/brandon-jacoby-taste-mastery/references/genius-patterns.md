## Extracted Genius Patterns

### 1. Pattern/Invention Discernment
The taste master knows when to follow established patterns and when to create a new path. This is not a preference for novelty. It is judgment about context, user trust, and consequence.

### 2. Requirement Questioning
Inherited rules become design gravity. Brandon's X experience turned every requirement into a candidate for interrogation, including the rule that blocks the questioning.

### 3. Decisiveness Under Abundance
AI increases the number of possible ideas and micro-decisions. The scarce skill becomes decisive selection, not access to options.

### 4. Tool-Agnostic Design Identity
Great design is not tied to Figma, code, or AI. The designer's job remains judgment, feeling, empathy, and product-shaping even as tools change.

### 5. Build the Missing Tool
AI is most creatively empowering when it lets designers build custom tools for their exact creative constraint.

### 6. Tinker to Discover
Taste is created in the in-between moments: dragging, testing, moving, making bad artifacts, and stumbling into the strange third option.

### 7. Visual Feeling as Product Logic
Alignment, hierarchy, density, and visual fundamentals change how people feel. A product's emotional state is not decoration; it is functional design.

### 8. Taste as Journey, Not Surface
Taste is not the final minimal interface or fashionable style. It is the journey of choices, restraint, invention, and lived curiosity that produced the result.

### 9. Craft Meets Consequence
Craft and business impact are not enemies. The strongest products make craft consequential and make consequence crafted.

### 10. Founder Thought Acceleration
Bad or wrong artifacts help founders discover their own beliefs. A designer is not only making screens; they are creating thinking surfaces.

### 11. Accessible Greatness
Great products can bring previously rare or high-end experiences to many people. Accessibility and high craft can reinforce each other.

### 12. Signature Against Abundance
In a world where software is easy to create, the defensible mark is the human signature: a point of view that fights average output.
