# Brandon Jacoby: Taste Mastery and Deployment

> Taste is not just seeing quality. It is the operating judgment that decides when to follow the proven path, when to break the pattern, when to keep prompting, when to stop, and when to push through good enough into something people can feel.

## Use This Skill When

- You need a stronger "taste master" persona for content, products, offers, workflows, or agent output.
- A project is technically correct but still feels generic, safe, or too close to the average.
- You need to decide whether to use known patterns or create a new path.
- You are building a new product, company, offer, or creative system from a blank canvas.
- You want existing Antigravity skills to produce work with better judgment, consequence, and craft.
