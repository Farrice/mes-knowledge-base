# Marisa Murgatroyd — Experience Product Design

Marisa Murgatroyd is the founder of Live Your Message, creator of the Experience Formula, and builder of Experiencify (a gamified LMS). Her core thesis: "It's not about the information, it's about the transformation" — courses fail because they are information dumps sequenced by logic instead of experiences sequenced by motivation. Her programs hit 59% completion against a 3-7% industry average, produced ~3,000 testimonials from ~7,000 students, and support $2,497+ price points wrapped in an ironclad, behavior-anchored guarantee.

Use this skill to design a new experience product, re-architect an underperforming course, craft a product mission statement that doubles as the sales headline, or launch a Minimum Viable Offer with zero audience via chatterboxing and the five conversational pivots.

## Quick Reference

- **The Mission test**: the finish line must be so specific you could *photograph someone crossing it*. "Remove your limiting beliefs" fails; "launch your MVP and make your first sale in 12 weeks" passes.
- **Mission Possible template**: "Your mission, should you choose to accept it, is [specific, time-bound outcome]" — forces you into the participant's shoes.
- **Sequencing rule**: order by motivation, not logic (human-focused design, not function-focused). First win must be foolproof, in the first hour.
- **The cycle**: watch → do → win, escalating — never learn → learn → give up.
- **Flow channel**: match challenge to skill; too hard = anxiety, too easy = boredom. Start video-game easy.
- **Guarantee**: anchor it to behavior (use coaching + submit milestones on time), then stand behind the mission with money.
- **Completion economics**: consumption → completion → results → testimonials → referrals → lifetime value. Completion rate is the business model.
- **MVO reframe**: the minimum offer is not a discount — it's a premium, high-touch founding-member experience with direct access and co-creation.
- **Everything is experiencifiable**: even taxes. Macro (whole program) and micro (every training, email, call) get the same formula.
