# Resistance-Matched Proof Prescription

**Produces**: A proof strategy document specifying exactly which proof types to deploy against which resistance type, followed by finished proof-fortified copy.

> **Load before executing**:
> 1. `skills/david-mcraney-belief-change/genius.md`
> 2. `skills/luke-iha-proof-ladder/SKILL.md`

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Phase 1 fires Perplexity research to ground resistance diagnosis in real audience language. Misdiagnosing the resistance type produces proof that BACKFIRES.

## When to Deploy

- Your copy has proof but isn't converting (wrong proof type for the resistance type)
- Building proof sections for any sales page, ad, VSL, or email
- When you know the audience is skeptical but don't know WHY they're skeptical
- Prescribing proof for a client or creative team

## Inputs Required

- **Product/Offer**: What are we proving?
- **Audience**: Who is resisting?
- **Copy Type**: Ad / Sales page / Email / VSL / Landing page
- **Existing Copy** (optional): Copy that needs proof injection
- **Known Objections** (optional): Any resistance you already know about

## Phase 1: Resistance Diagnosis 🔍

**Uses**: McRaney Pattern 25 (Rebuttal Type Detection), Pattern 23 (Sunk-Cost Awareness)

### Research Step 🔬

Fire Perplexity query:

```
Search for: [audience] skepticism about [product/offer/category].
Find: Reddit posts, reviews, forum comments where [audience] EXPLAINS their skepticism.
Specifically looking for:
- Do they question the METHOD of selling? ("another guru", "just a sales tactic", "red flags")
- Or do they question the CLAIMS? ("the numbers don't add up", "no proof", "sounds too good")
- Or both?
Also search for: What [audience] has tried before that failed. Prior investments in similar solutions.
Return: 10-15 skepticism quotes with source attribution.
```

### Classification

Analyze the research to classify resistance:

**Technique Rebuttal** — Resistance to the METHOD of persuasion:
- Signal language: "This is just marketing", "I can see what they're doing", "Another guru selling a course", "Red flags everywhere", "Sounds like a scam"
- Root cause: Pattern recognition from past manipulation
- Emotional driver: Self-protection, "I won't be fooled again"

**Topic Rebuttal** — Resistance to the CONTENT of claims:
- Signal language: "Where's the proof?", "The numbers don't add up", "I'd need to see data", "Show me it works", "Sounds too good to be true"
- Root cause: Information deficit or contradictory evidence
- Emotional driver: Rationality, intellectual honesty

**Sunk-Cost Anchoring** — Resistance rooted in prior investment:
- Signal language: "I've already invested in [alternative]", "I've been doing it this way for years", "Why would I switch now?", "I've already spent [time/money] on [competitor]"
- Root cause: Loss aversion + identity investment in prior choice
- Emotional driver: Protecting past decisions from being "wasted"

**Mixed** — Multiple types co-occurring (most common in practice)

### Output

```
## Resistance Diagnosis

Primary Type: [Technique / Topic / Sunk-Cost / Mixed]
Secondary Type (if mixed): [type]
Confidence: [High / Medium / Low]

Evidence:
1. "[quote]" → [type] — [source]
2. "[quote]" → [type] — [source]
3. "[quote]" → [type] — [source]

Prior Investment Scan:
- Have they invested in alternatives? [Yes/No — specifics]
- Sunk-cost risk level: [Low / Medium / High]
```

## Phase 2: Proof Type Matching

**Uses**: McRaney Pattern 25 × Luke Iha proof ladder

### Execute

Based on the diagnosed resistance type, prescribe MATCHED proof:

### If TECHNIQUE REBUTTAL (defending against manipulation):

| Priority | Proof Type | Purpose | Example |
|----------|-----------|---------|---------|
| 1 | **Transparency Proof** | Disarm suspicion by acknowledging the pitch openly | "Yes, I'm selling you something. Here's why it's worth your money..." |
| 2 | **Process Proof** | Show your method, not just your results | Behind-the-scenes, methodology explanation |
| 3 | **Permission Proof** | Lower pressure, increase control | "Take 30 days. If it's not for you, full refund, no questions." |
| 4 | **Anti-Selling Proof** | Paradoxically increase trust by advising AGAINST purchase for wrong fit | "If you're [wrong fit], don't buy this." |
| ❌ | ~~Testimonial Stacking~~ | BACKFIRES — confirms "sales machine" suspicion | |
| ❌ | ~~Scarcity/Urgency~~ | BACKFIRES — triggers "manipulation detection" alarm | |

### If TOPIC REBUTTAL (disputing claims):

| Priority | Proof Type | Purpose | Example |
|----------|-----------|---------|---------|
| 1 | **Empirical Proof** | Data, studies, specific numbers that can be verified | "In 2024, 847 students completed the program. 73% achieved [result] within 90 days." |
| 2 | **Demonstration Proof** | Show it working, don't just claim it works | Live walkthrough, case study with timestamps, before/after |
| 3 | **Third-Party Proof** | Independent verification they can check themselves | Press mentions, industry awards, independent reviews |
| 4 | **Concession Proof** | Acknowledge where they're RIGHT, then redirect | "You're right that [point]. Where the data diverges is..." |
| ❌ | ~~Transparency about method~~ | BACKFIRES — looks defensive, like you have something to hide | |
| ❌ | ~~Emotional testimonials~~ | WEAK — they want data, not feelings | |

### If SUNK-COST ANCHORING:

| Priority | Proof Type | Purpose | Example |
|----------|-----------|---------|---------|
| 1 | **Investment-Acknowledgment Proof** | Validate their prior spend, don't invalidate it | "Everything you learned from [competitor] still applies. This builds on it." |
| 2 | **Bridge Proof** | Show transition path that PRESERVES prior investment | "Your experience with [old method] is actually an advantage here..." |
| 3 | **Opportunity-Cost Proof** | Show what staying costs vs what switching costs | "Each month you wait costs approximately [X] in [missed opportunity]" |
| 4 | **Gradual-Migration Proof** | Reduce switching cost to near-zero | "You don't have to abandon [old system]. Start with [small step]." |
| ❌ | ~~Comparison attacks~~ | BACKFIRES — attacking their prior choice attacks their judgment | |

3. [Proof Type] — [specific execution]

- [Type] — [why]

## Phase 3: Proof Sequencing

**Uses**: McRaney Pattern 16 (Staged Delivery)

### Execute

Sequence the prescribed proof types for maximum dissolution effect:

1. **Opener** — Lowest-friction proof first (the one that meets them where they are)
2. **Credibility Builder** — Escalate to proof that earns the right to make bigger claims
3. **Anchor Point** — The single strongest proof point (specific, verifiable, emotionally resonant)
4. **Reinforcement** — Stack supporting proof around the anchor
5. **Permission Close** — Final proof element that REDUCES pressure rather than applies it

### Route-Matched Sequencing:

- **Central route audience** → Lead with empirical, build with demonstration, anchor with third-party
- **Peripheral route audience** → Lead with authority signals, build with social proof, anchor with credibility cues

## Phase 4: Copy Production

### Execute

Produce the proof-fortified copy:

1. **If writing new copy** → Build proof architecture into the structure from scratch
2. **If injecting proof into existing copy** → Identify every "naked claim" (claim without proof) and attach the matched proof type

### Proof Braiding (apply to every section):

For every claim in the copy:
- Is it proven? If not → attach matched proof
- Is the proof type matched to the resistance type? If not → swap
- Is the proof sequenced correctly (escalating credibility)? If not → reorder
