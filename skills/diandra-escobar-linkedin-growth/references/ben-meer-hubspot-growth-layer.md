# Ben Meer and HubSpot growth layer

Use this reference with workflow 23. Source package: `extractions/video-context/GKbNTGLfd34/`.

## Required additions

- Build the account profile before the posting calendar.
- Add 20 ideas every two weeks and score attention potential plus time to create from 1-5 each.
- Treat 6 as the default minimum combined score; permit a documented strategic override.
- Draft the body first, then test three or more hook candidates against the source’s ten hook families.
- Make the post surprising or actionable without inventing personal experience, statistics, proof, or urgency.
- Maintain a private peer-feedback group for learning. Never prescribe coordinated likes, comments, reposts, automated engagement, or quid-pro-quo distribution.
- Record impressions, saves, reposts, comments, profile views, followers, DMs, leads, and attributed revenue.
- Review every 10 posts; complete a top-10 pattern review every 100 posts.
- Treat newsletters as an optional owned-depth layer, not a magic algorithm hack. One newsletter article can feed several source-linked feed posts.

## Hook-family safety

Only use a family when its placeholders can be filled with true, sourced material. Result hooks require real proof. “Secret,” elite-percent, time-to-result, and first-person frames fail when they overstate what the author knows or did.

## Outcome boundary

“840K” names the source case and ambition. The system may target it but cannot promise it. Report `NO EVENT` until live account data exists, then report the measured change without causal overclaim.
