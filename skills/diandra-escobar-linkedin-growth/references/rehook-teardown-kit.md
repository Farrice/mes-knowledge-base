A productized service you can sell this week. The engine is [workflow 21](../workflows/21-rehook-teardown.md). This kit holds the offer, the pricing ladder, the outreach copy, and the deliverable template the client receives.

**Sections marked 🔒 INTERNAL are for you. Sections marked 📤 CLIENT-FACING are what the buyer sees.**

---

## 🔒 INTERNAL — The Offer Logic

**What it is**: You take posts a creator already published that underperformed, and prove — post by post — that the content was good and the *hook* leaked the reach. Then you rebuild the hooks. The proof is the product.

**Why it sells (the three beats):**
- *What do I get?* My old posts turned into reach I already paid to produce, plus the exact reason my hooks fail.
- *Why you?* A pixel-width validation model and a 131-hook benchmark almost no ghostwriter uses. The teardown shows receipts, not opinions.
- *Why now?* Every week those posts sit un-rehooked is reach left on the table, and the algorithm rewards the creators who fix this first.

**Why it's the perfect wedge for the Authority Flywheel:** it requires zero trust to start (you work on posts that already exist), it demonstrates competence visibly, and it ends inside the exact problem a retainer solves. The free version is a lead magnet. The paid version funds the pipeline. The retainer is the destination.

**Delivery time**: 10-post audit = under an hour of your time with workflow 21. That margin is the point.

## 🔒 INTERNAL — How to Run It

1. Collect their posts (ask for text, not screenshots — see below).
2. Run: `/diandra-rehook-teardown "[paste posts]" --register [formal|informal] --client "[name]"`
3. The workflow produces the report below. Review the picks, sanity-check the top fix, ship it.
4. For the free version: tear down their single best-but-underperforming post. Pick one with a genuinely good insight and an obviously weak opener — the contrast sells.

**The text-not-screenshots line** (paste to client): *"Send me the full text of 5–10 posts that underperformed but you felt should've done better. Text, not screenshots, so I can rebuild the exact lines."*

## 🔒 INTERNAL — Quality Bar Before You Send
- Every rebuild passed the validation pass (char + width). No broken counts visible.
- Each teardown proves a *buried gap* — never "your hook was weak, here's better."
- The cross-post section lands on ONE habit, not a list.
- It reads like a paid diagnostic. No "as an AI," no hedging, no exposed scaffolding.
- Nothing fabricated — real post text, real metrics, or explicitly marked as hook-mechanics-only.
