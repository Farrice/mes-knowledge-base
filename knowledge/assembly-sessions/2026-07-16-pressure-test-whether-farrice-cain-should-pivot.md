### The Principle

**Being early is only a moat when the receipt is aimed at a market you can win: the timestamp does the positioning, the vertical does the converting.**

A dated artifact (git commit, hook log, ledger entry) proves *when* you started — that's free, undeniable, and needs no claim. But proof alone doesn't sell; it only converts when it's pointed at an audience where you already hold trust and a sellable offer. Chasing the category the artifact "belongs to" (AI second brain, in general) taxes you with Dunford's category-creation problem — a fight only the Halligans and Forte's of the world can win. Pointing the same artifact at the vertical you already dominate (wellness/fitness) skips that tax entirely. The wound resolves not by relitigating the category, but by re-routing the receipt.
