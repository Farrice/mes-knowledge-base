# Single Truth Convergence

## Cross-Domain Pattern
The instinct in every creative domain is to ADD — more features, more points, more proof, more value. The masters in every domain converge on the opposite: reduction to ONE essential truth. Not because simplicity is elegant, but because human attention and belief crystallize around a single point. Everything that dilutes that point weakens the work. This is the most dangerous failure mode in the Antigravity system — "structurally sound but flat" outputs almost always tried to cover everything instead of finding the one thing.

## How It Manifests

### In Screenwriting (Connelly + Vuong)
Connelly's Vending Machine Principle: the object that opens a scene must close it. One image, carrying the entire weight of transformation. Not a subplot list — a single thread that runs through and emerges changed. Vuong's Species Test: one sentence that has never existed. Not a paragraph that covers the topic — a single line that is irreducibly itself. Both masters practice radical reduction. Connelly deletes until what remains cannot be deleted. Vuong writes one sentence and asks "has the species had this before?"

### In Copywriting (Luke Iha)
The Little Big Idea: the ONE intersection of product truth × audience desire. Not a feature list. Not a benefit stack. One idea that sits at the exact overlap. The CASH Method's Concept layer (the C) is specifically "the emotional territory" — singular, not plural. "Concept ≠ Product" is the foundational rule. The moment a copywriter starts listing features, they've lost the concept. Hooks get 80% of time allocation — not because hooks are 80% of the work, but because finding the ONE right hook is the single highest-leverage decision.

### In Brand/Sales (Dai Media + Godin)
Dai Media: one core identity assertion worth repeating. The Kristen Stewart Test is binary — yes or no. Not a spectrum, not a matrix, not a multi-axis model. One identity question. Godin: resistance as single compass. Not a framework of 12 strategic priorities — one compass that points to the thing you're avoiding, which is the thing that matters. "Pick Yourself" is a single truth that generates an entire philosophy.

## Contradiction or Tension
This pattern contradicts the system's natural tendency toward thoroughness. The Chain requires 6 steps. Expert loading has 4 tiers. The quality gate scores 3 dimensions. The evolution engine tracks 10+ constraints. The SYSTEM is complex by design. But the OUTPUT should be simple. The tension is real: complex process → simple result. The system's complexity exists to find the single truth, not to produce comprehensive output.

## Actionable Implication
- **For the quality gate**: Add "Single Truth Test" — can you state the piece's core truth in one sentence? If not, the piece is trying to cover too much. This is the fastest diagnostic for "structurally sound but flat."
- **For the writers' room**: The first question in every writers' room should be: "What is the ONE thing?" Not "what are the key points?" The room's job is to find the single truth, then build everything around it. Points that don't serve the single truth get cut.
- **For content creation**: The failure mode memory from 2026-03-11 ("structurally sound but flat" = 5/10 max) is THIS pattern in action. Every flat draft was a draft that tried to cover everything. Every 9/10 draft found one truth and committed to it.
- **For routing**: When the system loads multiple experts, the risk of "too many frameworks" increases. The routing layer should explicitly flag: "3 experts loaded — what is the SINGLE TRUTH they agree on?" Multi-expert doesn't mean multi-truth.
