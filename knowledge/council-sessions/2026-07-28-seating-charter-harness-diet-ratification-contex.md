Hop 1: consumed by a process whose output a human receives (Opus/Sonnet production; Fable dispatches here). Hop 2+: consumed only by another subsystem — archived. The spark that made it net-new: Meg's receipts fused with Boris's latent-demand mining to invert enforcement's *direction* — receipts are MINED from observe logs (observed, repeated, costly behavior), never manufactured on a calendar, which killed the probation-tracker failure mode (a new tracker to track the trackers) before it was born.

## The Principle

**The Grounding Chain with two-sided provenance.** Every expenditure in a system — a premium-model turn, a hook injection, a log line, a resident rule — must point FORWARD at a named consumer (`# consumer:` — code proves a reader) or BACKWARD at a dated scar (`# scar: 2026-07-27 eight-headline-rounds` — prose proves a wound), and its consumption chain must reach a human within two hops. A line with neither is decoration: archive it wholesale, no triage — because triage IS the maintenance loop you're exiting, and archival is a delist-with-restock (the break is the ticket back), not a funeral. Corollary: scars are mined from logs of real breaks, never written on a schedule; scars formally outrank consumers in conflicts (never trade a wound for telemetry). One grep yields the archive list, the misseat audit, and the rule triage as a single mechanical pass.

Transferable anywhere resources compound into self-serving machinery: org charts (does this role's output reach a customer in two hops?), content calendars (does this post point forward at a named reader or backward at a proven demand signal?), personal routines (does this habit serve a life outcome or only another habit?).
