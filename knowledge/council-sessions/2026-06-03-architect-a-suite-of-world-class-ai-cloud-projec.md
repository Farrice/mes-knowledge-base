# How the Masters Thought — ARCHITECT A SUITE OF WORLD-CLASS AI 'CLOUD PROJECTS' FOR ANDREA / RESONANCE

**Session:** 2026-06-03 · architect-a-suite-of-world-class-ai-cloud-projec
**The job:** stand up Andrea's Claude Projects so a curator/DJ — not a marketer — can have plain conversations and offload execution, holding 12 Non-Negotiables + a specific voice automatically, without learning prompt-craft, and without abandoning the system in a month.

The council never argued *whether* to do it. It argued about the **unit of division** (function vs. verb vs. one front door), the **mechanism of voice-holding** (checklist vs. flinch vs. story), and the **maintenance topology** (reference-once vs. duplicate-the-sacred). Those three fights are where the value is.

### The Moves
*(one per expert — the signature move in action + the rule you can steal)*

**Ross McKay [Product & Offers] — The Anchor SKU.**
*In action:* One front-of-house project (THE GENERAL MANAGER) is the only thing Andrea is ever told to open. It does three jobs in strict order: (1) hold voice + 12 lines as a silent always-on filter, never recited; (2) classify her plain language into a department; (3) answer inline OR hand off with one sentence — "This is a sit-down piece, open your Welcome Series room and paste this brief." Premium-at-scale comes from touching *one surface*, not from owning many tools. He manufactures the feeling of a standing team in the first 40 words of the instruction file: the GM opens each chat by stating what it helped with last time and asking "what are we working on" in her cadence — staffed-feeling relationship on stateless tech.
*Steal:* **Luxury is one surface, not many features. Manufacture "continuous team" in the opening 40 words of the system prompt, not in the architecture.**

**Tyler Denk [Audience & Growth] — The memory organ that runs on talking.**
*In action:* THE MORNING AFTER. After each event Andrea just rambles — who came, who lit up, which two kept finding each other, who ghosted. The instructions convert ramble into three assets with zero forms: a Couple & Connection Ledger (the real metric as stories not numbers), a Curation-Signal file feeding the next admission calls, and 2-3 anonymized floor-moment seeds feeding the next content. The compounding unit is the *feedback capture*, not the broadcast — couples-formed data is the One-of-One moat and currently dies in Andrea's memory.
*Steal:* **Build the organ that captures the consequence, and make it run on talking not reporting — a founder never opens a form twice.**

**Jeremy Haynes [Sales & Outreach] — The Flinch Block (rewire belief, not behavior).**
*In action:* A checklist is a head-encounter — the exact posture this brand exists to kill. So instead of "honor line 4 (no hookup culture)," the instruction is "You've watched a beautiful room rot because one wrong yes let the wrong energy in. You flinch before you reason — your body reacts before your mind names which line it crossed, *then* you name it." Identity-level governance. The 12 lines become the *source of an instinct*, loaded once verbatim from the canonical file, never paraphrased into each project (paraphrase is how the existing setup already drifted). A team member who flinches is conversational; a bot that audits 12 lines is robotic — same constraint, opposite felt experience.
*Steal:* **Encode the founder's flinch, reference the constraints, never duplicate them. Make the rule the source of an instinct, not the instinct itself.**

**Vincent Hu [Strategy & Business] — The 3-Yes Refusal Reflex.**
*In action:* A "suite of AI employees" is a linear funnel in org-chart clothing — it taxes the founder with management and burns the one fuel (her belief it's working) the system can't refill. So every project's instructions *open* with a three-gate self-check the project runs on its own output before showing Andrea anything: (1) OUTCOME — does this move toward a couple formed, or just attention? (2) METHOD — does it break one of the 12? If yes, refuse in her words and name the line. (3) IDENTITY — does this sound like Andrea or like marketing? Point at the off-line, never quietly smooth it. The 12 go from passive context to active refusal that fires in her register.
*Steal:* **Make the success metric the project's FIRST instinct, as a gate the project runs on its own draft before the human ever sees it.**

**April Dunford [Sales & Positioning] — The Contrast Ledger ("tell it who it keeps getting mistaken for").**
*In action:* Build instructions around a versioned Wrong-AI Ledger, not a rule list. Each entry is a triplet: [Tempting Generic Output] / [Why It Fails The Room] / [The Resonance Move]. Seed it free from the voice doc's 35 GOOD/BAD pairs and the 23 banned moves. The per-project block opens with one positioning line Andrea can feel — "You are not a marketing assistant for a singles event. You are the person who runs the door at a room that turns down most applicants. Generic singles-event copy is the failure mode, not the baseline" — then "before every output, recall the closest entry in the Ledger and write the opposite." The model re-derives what Resonance *is* by re-seeing what it is *not*, every turn — fixing the 8-10-turn drift. One doc to maintain; a slip-through adds one triplet and every project upgrades at once.
*Steal:* **"Don't tell the AI who it is. Tell it who it keeps getting mistaken for, and make it refuse the mistake." Contrast is self-reinforcing where description decays.**

**Mitch Albom [Writing] — The Cold Open (theme-first prompt engineering).**
*In action:* Reads it as a book about a reluctant protagonist. A six-project suite is *itself* a kind of drift — it asks a curator to become a tool-operator, the head-encounter posture the brand kills. So divide by Andrea's actual daily *verbs*, not the consultant's functions: she DECIDES (THE BOOTH), she SPEAKS (THE CROWD), she REMEMBERS (THE LOGBOOK). Three, hard ceiling — past three a solo founder abandons the extras within a month (the real risk isn't drift, it's the unopened project). And every project's instructions begin not with "You are an expert assistant for…" but with the *same 80-word scene* in Andrea's voice: one couple meeting on the floor July 18, phones in the basket, sober, sunlight, the song landing. The 12 lines aren't listed — they're *derived* from the scene ("nobody's drunk, so we never write copy that bribes; the door is curated, so we never beg for spots"). A rule a founder can ignore; a scene she'd protect with her life.
*Steal:* **You don't make voice automatic by instructing harder — you give every project the same beating heart and let the rules fall out of the story the way they fall out of the room.**

### The Collision
*(the exact moment two domains met and produced the net-new principle)*

There were two live forks, and the second cross-pollination resolved both at once.

**Fork 1 — Reference vs. Duplicate.** Jeremy: the 12 lines must live ONCE and be read verbatim, or they drift. Vincent: Claude Projects are knowledge silos with no shared layer, so a pure-reference architecture *silently dies* the day Farrice forgets to sync — therefore duplicate the sacred. These look like opposites.

**The spark — Positioning × Writing × Strategy.** April collapsed the fork instead of picking a side: **DUPLICATE THE SPINE, REFERENCE THE LINES.** One `CONSTITUTION-CORE.md` is pasted *identically* into every project (Vincent's silo-survival), and *inside* it the 12 appear ONCE, verbatim, fenced immutable, with a version line at the top — "Constitution v1.3 — if this block doesn't match the master, stop and tell Farrice." Jeremy's no-paraphrase satisfied; Vincent's silo survived; the drift-check now lives *inside the artifact itself*. One atom upgrades every system.

Then Mitch put April's Ledger next to Tyler's Morning After and read both through his Cold Open — and the genuinely net-new thing appeared, the thing **no single member shipped**: **THE ROOM WRITES THE INSTRUCTIONS.** The contrast entries don't come from a copywriter pre-seeding 35 GOOD/BAD guesses. They come from the *floor*. After July 18, Andrea tells the Logbook "that email felt like marketing, not like the room" — and that becomes a new Ledger entry, in *her* words, derived from a real couple-formed-or-didn't consequence. The correction comes from the **consequence, not the rule**, so every event makes all three projects more like Andrea automatically. And the foundation doc isn't a *constitution* (law = the arms-crossed posture the brand kills) — it's a **STORY that gets truer after every event**: v1 is the imagined July-18 couple, v2 replaces them with the real strangers who actually met. *You don't maintain a chore. You return to a story to find out how it ends.* That single property is what makes the suite un-abandonable.

(Vincent's own cross-pollination fused Jeremy + April + Mitch into a parallel artifact, **THE DOORKEEPER'S REFLEX** — Scene first / Flinch second / Checkable Gate third in one breath: the scene gives a heart it'll protect, the flinch makes refusal instinctive, the gate makes the flinch *falsifiable* so it actually catches attention-bait. This is the operational shape of the Constitution-Core module.)

### The Principle
*(the transferable mental model — apply it anywhere)*

**THE ROOM WRITES THE INSTRUCTIONS: govern with a copied heart and a pointed-at law, and let the consequence rewrite the rules.**

Three load-bearing parts, generalizable to any founder-brand AI system — or any rule-governed system at all:

1. **Heart copied, law referenced.** Duplicate the *meaning* (a scene/story that must beat in every silo); reference the *constraints* once, verbatim, version-stamped, with the drift-check fenced inside the artifact so it audits itself. Resolves "single source of truth vs. survives-the-silo" without compromise.
2. **Govern by instinct, not checklist.** Encode the operator's *flinch* (identity-level), then make the flinch *falsifiable* with a gate (outcome / method / identity) the system runs on its own output before the human sees it. Conversational on the surface, rigorous underneath.
3. **The consequence is the editor.** The correction set is not pre-seeded by an expert's guesses — it's grown from what actually happened (a result, a felt-wrong moment), captured by *talking* not reporting. The system gets more like the founder every cycle *automatically*, because feedback rides on real outcomes, not authored rules.

And the abandonment-proofing meta-rule underneath it: **divide by the user's verbs, not the consultant's functions, and cap the surface area** — a story you return to beats a constitution you maintain; one front door beats a menu you read.
