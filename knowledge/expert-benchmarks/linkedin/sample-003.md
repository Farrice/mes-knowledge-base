# LinkedIn Post: The Delegation Illusion

---

Most founders don't have a hiring problem.

They have an identity problem.

Here's what I mean:

I coached a founder last year doing $74K/month. Smart. Driven. Miserable. She answered every client email personally. Reviewed every deliverable. Sat in on every sales call.

When I asked why, she didn't say "quality control."

She said: "What am I if I'm not the one doing the work?"

That sentence broke something open.

Because delegation isn't an operational decision.
Delegation is an identity decision.

You can't hand off work you've welded to your self-worth.

The fix isn't a VA. The fix isn't a system. The fix is separating WHO YOU ARE from WHAT YOU DO — then building the system around the gap.

Three things that changed for her once she made that separation:

1. She hired an ops lead and gave her decision-making authority (not just tasks)
2. She stopped reviewing deliverables and started reviewing frameworks
3. Her revenue went up 23% in 90 days — because she spent that time selling instead of managing

The bottleneck was never capacity.
The bottleneck was ego dressed as diligence.

If you're doing $30K+ per month and still answering every email yourself — the constraint isn't your team.

It's your definition of indispensable.
