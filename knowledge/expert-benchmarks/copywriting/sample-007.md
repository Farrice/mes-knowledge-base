# Meta Ad Set: SaaS Email Re-Engagement Campaign

## Client: B2B Project Management Tool ($49/mo)
## Objective: Reactivate churned trial users (30-60 days inactive)

---

### Ad 1: The Spreadsheet Graveyard (Video Script — 30s)

**Hook (0-3s):** [Screen recording: scrolling through a chaotic spreadsheet with 47 tabs]

"You didn't quit our tool because it was bad."

**Body (3-20s):**
"You quit because setting it up felt like a second job. I get it. You went back to the spreadsheet. It's comfortable. It's familiar. It's also why your team spent 11 hours last week searching for information that should take 11 seconds."

**CTA (20-30s):**
"We rebuilt onboarding from scratch. What used to take a weekend now takes 22 minutes. Your old account is still there. Your data is still there. Come back and see what changed."

**[Button: See What Changed →]**

**Headline:** Your spreadsheet misses you. Your team doesn't miss the spreadsheet.
**Primary text:** We made onboarding 94% faster. Your trial account is waiting — data intact.

---

### Ad 2: The Meeting That Should've Been a Dashboard (Static Image)

**Visual:** Split screen. Left: conference room with 6 exhausted people around a table, laptops open, pizza boxes. Right: single laptop screen showing a clean project dashboard, person holding a coffee, smiling.

**Caption under left:** "Monday's 90-minute status update"
**Caption under right:** "Monday's 90-second status check"

**Primary text:**
Your team doesn't hate meetings. They hate meetings that exist because nobody can find the information.

1,247 teams switched from "status update" meetings to async dashboards last quarter. Average time saved: 4.3 hours per week per team.

Your trial still has your old projects loaded. The new onboarding takes 22 minutes.

**Headline:** Kill the meeting. Keep the alignment.
**[Button: Reactivate Free Trial →]**

---

### Ad 3: The Honest One (Text-heavy, no image)

**Primary text:**

Honest ad from a project management company:

We know you tried us and left. Probably during onboarding. Our onboarding used to be bad. Dense. Confusing. Required a tutorial video that was somehow 45 minutes long.

We fixed it.

New onboarding: 22 minutes. No video. Guided setup that asks you 5 questions, then builds your workspace around your answers.

Your old account? Still there. Your data? Untouched. Your team's Slack complaints about the spreadsheet? Probably still happening.

One ask: give us 22 minutes. If it still feels like a chore, we'll delete your account permanently and stop emailing you. Deal?

**Headline:** We were bad at onboarding. We fixed it. Give us 22 minutes.
**[Button: 22-Minute Setup →]**

---

### Performance Notes:
- All three ads use specific numbers (11 hours, 22 minutes, 94%, 1,247 teams, 4.3 hours) — never rounded
- Buyer's language throughout ("second job," "comfortable," "familiar," "chore")
- Benefits-first: what it does FOR them, not what the tool IS
- The Honest One deploys radical candor — admitting the old product was bad builds trust faster than pretending it wasn't
- Zero clichés: no "streamline," "optimize," "game-changer," "unlock"
