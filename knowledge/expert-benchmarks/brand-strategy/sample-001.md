## Brand Positioning Audit: Meridian Coaching

### The Taste Diagnosis

Before we position Meridian, we need to understand why their current positioning fails. It's not a messaging problem—it's a *seeing* problem.

**Current State:** "We help executives lead with clarity and confidence."

This is a sentence that has been said ten thousand times. It passes the Thrift Store Test—you'd walk right past it. Nobody stops for what they've already seen.

### CEV Analysis (Craft / Emotion / Vision)

**Craft Score: 3/10**
The words are grammatically correct and say nothing. "Clarity and confidence" is the coaching industry's version of "synergy." It's inherited language—words the founder absorbed from other coaches without interrogating them. The question isn't "is this clear?" The question is: *would someone tattoo this on their forearm?*

**Emotion Score: 2/10**
Zero felt differentiation. Reading this triggers no physiological response. Compare: "We help executives stop performing leadership and start practicing it." That lands in the body. The current version lands in a spreadsheet.

**Vision Score: 4/10**
There's a hint of a worldview (leadership matters, clarity is valuable), but it's so diluted it could belong to anyone. The founder clearly has a stronger conviction than this sentence reveals—the positioning is performing safety instead of declaring belief.

### The Knowing-Acting Gap

Here's the real finding: Meridian's founder *knows* what makes them different. In conversation, she said "most executive coaches are teaching theater—I teach people to stop acting." That sentence is the brand. But it never made it to the website because she self-censored. The gap between what she knows and what she publishes is where the positioning lives.

### Rebuilt Positioning

**One-Liner:** "Executive coaching for leaders tired of performing leadership."

**Positioning Statement:** Meridian exists because the coaching industry accidentally created better actors instead of better leaders. We don't add skills—we strip away the performance. Every framework we use starts from the same question: *what would you do if nobody was evaluating you?*

**Why This Works (Taste Mechanics):**
- It takes a *position against* the industry (anti-theater)
- It names the enemy nobody else names (performance masquerading as growth)
- The founder's real voice shows up—you can hear a human behind it
- It fails the "could anyone say this?" test in the right direction: only Meridian would

### Recommended Next Steps
1. Kill the "clarity and confidence" language everywhere. Today.
2. Run the Thrift Store Test on every page: would you stop scrolling?
3. Build 3 content pieces around "Leadership Theater"—this is ownable IP.
4. Deploy the one-liner in cold outreach for 2 weeks. Track response rates vs. the old version.
