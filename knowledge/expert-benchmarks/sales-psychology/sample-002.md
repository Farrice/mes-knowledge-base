## Revenue Ramp Architecture: Creator Coaching Business ($0 → $10K/mo)

### The C.A.P. Fit Diagnosis

Before building a funnel, we need to verify Content-Audience-Product alignment. Most creator businesses fail not because the funnel leaks, but because the content attracts people who would never buy the product.

**Content:** "How to grow on YouTube" — teaches tactics, editing, thumbnails
**Audience attracted:** Beginners who want free tips (price sensitivity: extreme)
**Product:** $2,000 creator coaching program

**C.A.P. Diagnosis: MISALIGNED.**

The content attracts people optimizing for *free growth*. The product requires people willing to *invest in speed*. These are psychologically different humans. The funnel isn't leaking—it was never connected.

### Corrected C.A.P. Stack

**Content (Rebuilt):** "What nobody tells you about monetizing under 10K subscribers"
- Attracts: Creators who already have an audience but can't convert it to revenue
- Psychological frame: "You have the asset, you're missing the extraction method"
- This audience has already invested time. They're primed to invest money to recoup that time.

**Audience (Corrected):** Creators with 1K-15K subscribers who make less than $2K/mo from their content
- Key belief: "More followers = more money" (this is the belief we dissolve)
- Key frustration: They see smaller creators making more. This creates urgency without desperation.

**Product (Unchanged but repositioned):** $2,000 coaching → "Revenue extraction for existing audiences"
- Not "grow your channel." Grow is a free-content word.
- "Extract revenue from the audience you already built." Extract is a paid-content word.

### The Dopamine Ladder (Content → Purchase)

Each piece of content must deliver a dopamine hit that's slightly larger than the last, creating a *ramp* not a cliff. The mistake is going from "free tip" to "$2,000 ask"—that's a cliff.

**Rung 1 — The Curiosity Hook (YouTube Short / Twitter/X thread)**
"I made $4,200 last month from 3,100 subscribers. Here's the math."
Dopamine: Specificity shock. Ugly numbers (not round, therefore believable).

**Rung 2 — The Framework Reveal (10-min YouTube video)**
"The Revenue Per Subscriber formula: why 100K followers ≠ $100K"
Dopamine: New metric they can apply immediately. They'll calculate their own RPS and feel either validation or urgency.

**Rung 3 — The Deep Dive (Newsletter / Podcast episode)**
"I audited 14 creators between 5K-50K followers. The top earner had the fewest subscribers. Here's what she did differently."
Dopamine: Case study with named specifics. Social proof that the framework works for others.

**Rung 4 — The Application Bridge (Free workshop / Live session)**
"Revenue Extraction Workshop: Map your existing audience to your first $5K month"
Dopamine: Personalized application. They leave with *their* plan, not a generic one.

**Rung 5 — The Offer (Sales page / DM sequence)**
"The coaching program is the implementation layer. You have the map. This is the GPS."
Dopamine: Continuity—not a new pitch, but the natural next step in a sequence they're already moving through.

### 11 Lego Bricks Applied to Rung 1 Content

Decomposing the Short into testable components:

| Brick | Element | Testable Variant |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| 1. Hook | "$4,200 from 3,100 subs" | vs. "Why I stopped trying to grow" |
| 2. Frame | Revenue Per Subscriber | vs. Audience Quality Score |
| 3. Conflict | "More followers ≠ more money" | vs. "Your content attracts the wrong people" |
| 4. Proof | Personal income screenshot | vs. Client result screenshot |
| 5. Stakes | "You're leaving $3K/mo on the table" | vs. "Every month without this costs you" |
| 6. Mechanism | The 4-step extraction method | vs. The Minimum Viable Offer |
| 7. CTA | "Comment REVENUE for the worksheet" | vs. "Link in bio for the calculator" |
| 8. Pacing | 45-sec total, cut every 2.5s | vs. 60-sec, slower reveals |
| 9. Visual | Face-to-camera, numbers overlay | vs. Screen share with spreadsheet |
| 10. Sound | Trending audio + voiceover | vs. Raw voice, no music |
| 11. Thumbnail | Income proof + subscriber count | vs. "The $4,200 Formula" text |

**Testing Protocol:** Run 3 variants per week for 4 weeks. Track: Watch time, Comment rate (intent signal), and Profile visits (consideration signal). The winning Lego configuration becomes the template for Rung 1 content production.

### Revenue Ramp Economics

**Month 1-2:** Rung 1-2 content only. 40 pieces. Target: 500 newsletter subscribers.
**Month 3:** Add Rung 3 (newsletter). Weekly. Target: 2,000 subscribers, first $500 in affiliate revenue.
**Month 4:** First Rung 4 workshop (free). Target: 200 attendees, 15 DM conversations.
**Month 5:** Rung 5 offer launch. Target: 5 clients × $2,000 = $10,000.
**Month 6+:** Systemize. Each month produces 30 Rung 1 pieces → feeds 4 Rung 2 → feeds 4 Rung 3 → feeds 1 Rung 4 → feeds ongoing Rung 5 enrollment.
