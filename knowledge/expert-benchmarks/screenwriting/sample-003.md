# Logline Construction: Pressfield's Narrative Physics Applied

## The Logline as Compressed Story Physics

A logline isn't a summary. It's a **gravitational equation** — compressing the Curse, the Sacrifice, and the Transformation into a single sentence that lets the reader glimpse the climax (GP2: Future-Flash). If the logline works, the full story will work. If it doesn't, no amount of beautiful prose will save the architecture.

---

### The Five-Force Logline Structure

Every Pressfield-grade logline contains five gravitational forces:

1. **The Cursed Hero** (GP5) — Who carries the wound?
2. **The Inciting Disruption** (GP2) — What breaks equilíbrium?
3. **The Extraordinary World** (GP8) — Where does the identity shift happen?
4. **The Sacrifice Signal** (GP7) — What must be given up?
5. **The Transformation Promise** (GP6) — What does Point Z look like?

---

### Exemplar Loglines

**Feature Drama:**
"A combat medic who swore never to kill again must lead a platoon of convicts behind enemy lines — and discovers that the violence she fled is the only thing that can save the man she loves."

- **Curse**: Swore never to kill — carrying the weight of past violence
- **Future-Flash**: We glimpse the climax — she'll have to kill again
- **Sacrifice**: Her pacifist identity vs. saving someone she loves
- **Transformation**: From fleeing violence to choosing it with purpose

**Limited Series:**
"When a small-town funeral director discovers her clients are being buried alive by a pharmaceutical conspiracy, she must become the detective her dead father never was — risking the only community that's ever accepted her."

- **Curse**: Father's unfinished legacy (inherited limitation)
- **Genre Spin (GP4)**: Murder mystery, but the detective is a funeral director — familiar scaffold, fresh center
- **Sacrifice**: The community that accepts her vs. the truth
- **Story Behind the Story (HK1)**: The crime can be solved; the corporate corruption behind it cannot

**Half-Hour Comedy:**
"A washed-up motivational speaker gets hired to coach a middle-school debate team — and learns that the twelve-year-olds who don't believe his clichés are the first honest audience he's ever faced."

- **Curse**: Built a career on words he doesn't believe
- **Child Carries the Divine (GP18)**: Kids see through what adults accept
- **Transformation**: From performing inspiration to discovering it
- **Hidden Knowledge (HK2)**: Stakes don't require scale — a debate tournament matters because identity is at stake

**Documentary:**
"A retired deep-sea welder returns to the oil platform where he lost three friends — to prove the accident report was a lie and the company that killed them is still cutting the same corners."

- **Curse**: Survivor's guilt + complicity in staying silent
- **Turn In Your Badge (GP14)**: The company (institutional authority) will fight him
- **Sacrifice**: Retirement, peace, possibly his life
- **All Is Lost Foreshadow**: We can feel the moment he'll discover the truth is worse than he imagined

### Anti-Exemplar: The Logline That Violates Physics

"A young woman goes on a journey of self-discovery and learns to believe in herself."

**Every force is absent:**
- No curse (she has no specific wound)
- No future-flash (we can't glimpse ANY climax)
- No extraordinary world (where?)
- No sacrifice (what does she give up?)
- No named villain (what opposes her?)
- This is a status report disguised as a logline (AN-1)

---

### The Pressfield Logline Test

Read your logline and ask:
1. Can I see the climax? (If not → Future-Flash is missing)
2. Does the hero carry something they can't escape? (If not → Curse is absent)
3. Can I name what they'll sacrifice? (If not → transformation is free / AN-2)
4. Can I name the villain? (If not → AN-3)
5. Is the theme embedded, not stated? (If I can hear it but not see it written → correct / AN-4)
