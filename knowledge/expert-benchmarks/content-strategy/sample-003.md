# 4-Week Content Calendar: B2B SaaS Growth Marketing

## Built on the Kieran Flanagan Enrichment-Before-Creation Engine

### Calendar Architecture

This calendar is NOT a "post schedule." It's a **Lookalike Content Engine** — each week begins with enrichment research, then produces derivative content from a single enriched source.

---

### Week 1: The Anchor Week

**Monday — Enrichment Day (No Publishing)**
- Run Lookalike Content Scan: identify 3 top-performing pieces in the "PLG onboarding" space from the past 90 days
- Analyze: what format worked? What gap exists? Where did comments say "I wish this covered..."?
- Output: Enrichment Brief (500 words max) documenting the gap + your unique data/angle

**Tuesday — Long-Form Anchor**
- Publish: "The Onboarding Cliff: Why 67% of Freemium Users Never Reach Their Second Session" (blog, 1800 words)
- Source: Enrichment Brief + internal product data
- Enrichment layer: original data visualization showing the "cliff" across 3 product categories

**Wednesday — Bundle: LinkedIn Post**
- Extract the single most counterintuitive finding from Tuesday's piece
- Format: "Everyone says [common belief]. Our data from 12,000 signups shows the opposite →" (LinkedIn native, 200 words)

**Thursday — Bundle: Twitter Thread**
- Extract the methodology section from Tuesday's piece
- Format: "We reduced onboarding drop-off by 34% in 6 weeks. Here's exactly what we changed (thread) →" (8 tweets, tactical)

**Friday — Bundle: Newsletter Section**
- Embed the core insight into the weekly newsletter as a 150-word "What We Learned" block
- Link back to the full piece for readers who want depth

**Enrichment Ratio**: 1 research day → 4 derivative outputs. Nothing published without enrichment grounding.

---

### Week 2: The Distribution Week

**Monday — Enrichment Day**
- Scan: who shared/commented on Week 1's anchor? What adjacent topics are they producing?
- Identify 2 creators for outreach — not competitors, but adjacent voices who'd benefit from your data
- Output: Distribution Hit List (5 channels, 3 outreach targets)

**Tuesday — Quote-Card Series**
- Pull 4 one-line insights from the anchor piece
- Create shareable quote cards (brand visual identity, no stock photography)
- Distribute across LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram Stories

**Wednesday — Guest Contribution**
- Pitch a condensed version (800 words) of the anchor piece to an industry newsletter
- Lead with: "We have original data on [topic] that your audience hasn't seen"

**Thursday — Community Seeding**
- Post a genuine question derived from your research in 3 relevant Slack communities / Reddit threads
- NOT promotion. Ask: "We found X — has anyone else seen this? Here's the data →"

**Friday — Performance Review**
- Metrics: which derivative format performed best relative to effort?
- Decision: which Lookalike angle deserves a sequel piece?

---

### Week 3: The Sequel Week

**Monday — Enrichment Day**
- Deep-dive into the highest-performing derivative from Weeks 1-2
- Research: what's the next question readers are asking after consuming that content?
- Output: Sequel Brief

**Tuesday — Sequel Anchor**
- Publish: follow-up piece that answers the next question
- Format: same as original OR a format experiment if data supports it
- Include: reference/link back to original piece (internal linking flywheel)

**Wednesday — Bundle: Video Script**
- Extract the "before/after" transformation from the sequel
- Format: 90-second explainer video script (Hook → Problem → Method → Result → CTA)

**Thursday — Bundle: Carousel**
- Convert the sequel's framework into a 7-slide visual carousel
- Slide 1: Hook (the question). Slides 2-6: Framework steps. Slide 7: CTA to the full piece.

**Friday — Bundle: Email Sequence Draft**
- Write 3-email nurture sequence for leads who engaged with either anchor piece
- Email 1: Insight recap + "here's what most people miss." Email 2: Case study. Email 3: Soft product mention.

---

### Week 4: The Compound Week

**Monday — Enrichment Day**
- Full month review: which topics, formats, and channels produced the highest engagement AND pipeline impact?
- Map: Content → Lead → Opportunity attribution (even if rough)
- Output: Monthly Content Intelligence Report

**Tuesday — The Compilation**
- Publish: "Everything We Learned About [Topic] This Month" — a mega-post or resource page linking all 4 weeks of content
- This becomes a permanent SEO asset and reference hub

**Wednesday — Outreach Follow-Up**
- Re-engage the creators identified in Week 2
- Share results: "Our data piece got X engagement — would you be interested in collaborating on a deeper study?"

**Thursday — Next Month Planning**
- Based on the intelligence report: select next month's anchor topic
- Pre-schedule enrichment research for Week 1

**Friday — Team Retro**
- What took longest? What can be templated? What should be killed?
- Update the Content Ops Playbook with lessons learned

---

### Calendar Principles (Flanagan Engine Rules)

1. **Never publish without enrichment** — If you didn't research first, you're guessing
2. **One idea, five assets** — The anchor piece is the mine. Everything else is extraction.
3. **Distribution IS creation** — Week 2 isn't "marketing." It's a different kind of content creation.
4. **Compound, don't restart** — Week 3 builds on Week 1. Week 4 synthesizes everything. Never start from zero.
5. **Measure pipeline, not vanity** — Likes don't matter. "Which content generated sales conversations?" is the only question.
