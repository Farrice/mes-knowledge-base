## 1. Streetwear Brand Graphic Design

`[Subject/Scene] + [Action/Vibe] + [Artform/Medium] + [Technical Stylization] + [Brand Aesthetic/Reference] + [Negative Constraints]`

Present the system on a single A3-sized moodboard, including mockups of the logo on a black t-shirt and a beanie. ## 3. Cinematic Video Production

### Creative Director's Mindset
Cinematic AI video is not about generating random motion; it's about directing a scene. You must control the camera, the lighting, the pacing, and the emotional arc. Using the Higgsfield production chain allows you to lock in your vision step-by-step, ensuring consistency and narrative power.

### The Universal Cinematic Video Prompt Formula
`[Shot Type & Framing] + [Subject & Action] + [Camera Movement] + [Lighting & Atmosphere] + [Technical Specs (Lens/Film Stock)] + [Duration & Pacing]`

### Elite Prompts

**1. The Establishing Shot: "Neon Dystopia"**
*Platform: Higgsfield Popcorn (for keyframe) → Veo 3.1 (for video)*
> **Popcorn Prompt:** Wide cinematic establishing shot of a sprawling, rain-soaked cyberpunk metropolis at night. Towering skyscrapers are illuminated by massive, flickering neon billboards in cyan and magenta. Flying vehicles streak through the smog-filled air. Shot on 35mm film, anamorphic lens, deep depth of field, moody and oppressive atmosphere, inspired by Blade Runner 2049.
> **Veo 3.1 Prompt:** A slow, deliberate crane shot descending through the neon-lit smog of a cyberpunk city. The camera pushes forward smoothly as rain lashes against the lens. The massive holographic billboards flicker and glitch. The atmosphere is heavy, wet, and oppressive. Cinematic pacing, 24fps, anamorphic lens distortion.

**2. The Emotional Close-Up: "The Realization"**
*Platform: Higgsfield Popcorn → Seedance (for micro-motion)*
> **Popcorn Prompt:** Cinematic extreme close-up of a young woman's face, illuminated by the warm, golden hour light filtering through a dusty window. Her expression is one of sudden, heartbreaking realization. A single tear forms in her eye. Shot on an 85mm portrait lens, f/1.4 for extremely shallow depth of field, soft focus on the background, Kodak Portra 400 film stock, intimate and melancholic tone.
> **Seedance Prompt:** Slow camera dolly in, subtle eye movement, tear slowly falls.

**3. The High-Octane Action Sequence: "The Chase"**
*Platform: Sora 2 (via Higgsfield)*
> A single continuous cinematic shot. The camera is mounted on a low-angle tracking rig, following a sleek, matte-black sports car tearing down an empty desert highway at midday. The harsh sun casts sharp, high-contrast shadows. Suddenly, the car swerves violently to avoid debris, tires smoking. The camera maintains a smooth, high-speed tracking motion, capturing the intense motion blur and the heat distortion rising from the asphalt. Shot on a 24mm wide lens, high shutter speed, gritty action-film aesthetic, no cuts.

**4. The Character Transformation: "The Infection"**
*Platform: Higgsfield Popcorn → Veo 3.1 → Recast*
> **Popcorn Prompt:** Cinematic medium shot of a man in a tailored suit standing in a sterile, brightly lit laboratory. He looks directly at the camera with a confident, slightly arrogant expression. Cold, clinical lighting, 50mm lens, sharp focus.
> **Veo 3.1 Prompt:** The man in the suit stands still, his confident expression slowly faltering. He begins to twitch, his eyes widening in panic as he looks at his hands. The camera slowly pushes in.
> **Recast Prompt:** Replace the man with a terrifying, decaying zombie with glowing white eyes and torn flesh, maintaining the exact lighting, suit, and twitching motion.

**5. The Atmospheric B-Roll: "The Forge"**
*Platform: Higgsfield Popcorn → Seedance*
> **Popcorn Prompt:** Macro cinematic shot of glowing, molten steel being struck by a massive industrial hammer in a dark, cavernous forge. Sparks explode outward in a shower of bright orange light. Deep shadows, high contrast chiaroscuro lighting, gritty industrial texture, shot on a 100mm macro lens.
> **Seedance Prompt:** Slow motion, sparks flying outward, subtle camera shake on impact.

### Pro Moves
1. **The Higgsfield Chain:** Never try to generate a complex scene in one prompt. Use Popcorn to lock the composition and lighting, Seedance for subtle motion, Veo 3.1 for performance, and Recast for character consistency.
2. **The "One Primary Motion" Rule:** Do not confuse the AI with contradictory camera movements (e.g., "pan left while zooming in and rotating"). Stick to one primary motion per prompt (e.g., "slow dolly-in") for professional, artifact-free results.
3. **Direct the Lighting:** Lighting creates the "expensive" look. Always specify the lighting setup (e.g., "volumetric fog with hard morning sidelight," "three-point softbox setup").

Overcast daylight, gritty urban documentary style.

**4. The Product Demonstration Loop**

*Platform: Higgsfield Talking Avatar / Lipsync Studio*

1. **Engineer Imperfections:** Real UGC isn't perfect. Prompt for "handheld smartphone camera movement," "slight motion blur," or "harsh ring light" to sell the illusion of authenticity.

3. **Maintain Character Consistency:** When creating a series of UGC ads with the same "creator," use Higgsfield's Recast or Soul ID Character tools to ensure the face and identity remain identical across different shots and environments.

## 9. B-Roll Creation & Stock Footage

### Creative Director's Mindset
B-roll is the connective tissue of any great video. It sets the mood, establishes the location, and provides visual breathing room. When generating AI B-roll, you must think in terms of "modules"—separating the environment, the lighting, and the specific camera motion to create clips that are stable, cinematic, and highly usable in an edit.

### Pro Moves
1. **The "One Motion" Rule:** The biggest mistake in AI video is asking for too much movement. Choose *one* camera motion (e.g., "slow push-in" OR "pan right") and stick to it.
2. **The "Static Camera" Trick:** For highly detailed, stable B-roll (like rain falling or a record spinning), explicitly prompt for a "static camera locked on a tripod." This forces the AI to focus on the internal motion of the scene rather than moving the virtual camera.
3. **Generate for the Edit:** Think about how the clip will be used. If you need a transition, generate an abstract light leak or a fast whip-pan. If you need an establishing shot, generate a slow drone push-in.

## 11. The Master Prompting Frameworks (Summary)

To operate at a savant level, you must internalize the underlying frameworks that power these prompts.

### The Nano Banana Pro Framework (The Logical Anchor)
Nano Banana Pro excels at spatial reasoning and complex instructions.
1. **Structure:** Define the layout (e.g., "Logo in the top left, product in the center").
2. **Logic:** Use JSON or highly structured text to assign specific attributes to specific elements.
3. **Multi-Step:** Give the AI a sequence of events or a complex hierarchy of visual importance.

### The Venice.ai Six-Layer Video Framework
1. **Subject & Action:** Who/what, movement, emotional state.
2. **Shot Type & Framing:** Wide/medium/close-up, angle (eye level, low, high).
3. **Camera Movement:** Static, tracking, panning, dolly (slow = cinematic).
4. **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Golden hour, blue hour, studio, fog, rain.
5. **Technical Specs:** Lens (35mm wide, 50mm natural, 85mm portrait, macro), f-stop, film grain, color palette.
6. **Duration & Pacing:** 3-10 seconds, slow motion, time-lapse, transitions.

### The Kittl AI Building Blocks
1. **Set the Scene:** The 5 Ws (Who, What, Where, When, Why).
2. **Add Details:** Use highly specific adjectives (e.g., "weathered," "iridescent," "brutalist").
3. **Define Output Style:** Vector, photorealistic, risograph, 3D render.
4. **Incorporate Negatives:** What must *not* be in the image.
