## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC PROMPTING SYSTEMS

### Higgsfield — The Cinematic Reasoning Engine

**Architecture:** Higgsfield uses a multi-model pipeline where different AI models handle different stages of production. The platform's Cinema Studio 3.0 features a "cinematic reasoning engine" that interprets natural language prompts with deep understanding of film grammar.

**The Logical Anchor System (Nano Banana Pro):**

Structure every Higgsfield prompt using this framework:

```
[SUBJECT with specific details] + [ACTION with physics-aware motion] + [ENVIRONMENT with atmospheric details] + [CAMERA: shot type, movement, lens] + [LIGHTING: setup, quality, direction] + [MOOD/TONE: emotional target] + [STYLE REFERENCE: specific film/director/aesthetic]
```

**Critical Higgsfield Rules:**
1. **Be specific about physics:** "Hair flows in the wind" beats "windy scene." The model needs physical anchors.
2. **Name the camera:** "35mm lens, dolly forward" gives the model a concrete visual framework.
3. **Reference real cinematography:** "Roger Deakins lighting" or "Emmanuel Lubezki long take" activates trained aesthetic patterns.
4. **Describe motion, not just position:** "A woman turns to face the camera, her expression shifting from contemplation to resolve" beats "a woman looking at the camera."
5. **Use temporal language:** "As the sun sets..." or "In the moment before..." gives the model narrative context.

**The 9-Step Higgsfield Solo Studio Pipeline:**

| Step | Tool | Purpose | Key Settings |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Pre-Viz | Sketch-to-Video | Rough composition from hand-drawn sketch | Upload sketch, describe intended motion |
| 2. World Building | Image Generation (9 refs) | Create 9 reference images for world consistency | Generate establishing shots, character refs, detail shots |
| 3. Core Generation | Cinema Studio 3.0 | Primary video generation with cinematic reasoning | Full prompt with logical anchors, select aspect ratio |
| 4. Genre Presets | Preset Library | Apply genre-specific color grading and pacing | Action, Horror, Comedy, Noir, Sci-Fi, Romance, Documentary |
| 5. Speed Ramps | Timing Control | Control pacing and dramatic timing | Flash In, Flash Out, Slow Build, Pulse, Heartbeat |
| 6. Enhancement | Sora 2 Enhancer | Upscale quality and add detail | Apply after core generation for maximum quality |
| 7. Upscale | Higgsfield Upscale | Resolution enhancement | 2x or 4x upscale for final delivery |
| 8. Character Lock | SoulID | Maintain character consistency across shots | Upload reference face, apply to all character shots |
| 9. Export | Asset Standardization | Final format and specs | 1080p/4K, frame rate, codec selection |

**Higgsfield Genre Presets:**

| Genre | Color Profile | Pacing | Sound Design | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Action | High contrast, teal/orange | Fast cuts, speed ramps | Bass impacts, whooshes | Product launches, hype content |
| Horror | Desaturated, cold blue-green | Slow build, sudden cuts | Low drones, silence breaks | Tension, mystery reveals |
| Comedy | Warm, saturated, bright | Snappy timing, holds for beats | Upbeat, playful | Lifestyle, casual brand content |
| Noir | High contrast B&W or muted | Deliberate, slow reveals | Jazz, rain, city ambience | Luxury, editorial, moody |
| Sci-Fi | Cool steel, neon accents | Measured, epic reveals | Synth, electronic, atmospheric | Tech, futuristic, innovation |
| Romance | Warm pastels, soft focus | Flowing, gentle transitions | Piano, strings, ambient | Beauty, fashion, emotional |
| Documentary | Natural, minimal grading | Observational, steady | Natural sound, minimal score | Authenticity, BTS, testimonial |

### Kittl — The Infinite Canvas Design System

**Architecture:** Kittl operates on an infinite canvas with two primary boards: the AI Image Board (for generating and iterating on images) and the AI Video Board (for animating designs into video). Kittl Flows connects these boards into node-based pipelines.

**The Kittl Building Block System:**

Structure every Kittl prompt using these building blocks:

```
[SUBJECT] + [STYLE/AESTHETIC] + [COMPOSITION] + [COLOR PALETTE] + [TEXTURE/MATERIAL] + [TYPOGRAPHY INTEGRATION] + [BACKGROUND]
```

**Critical Kittl Rules:**
1. **Define the Allowed AND the Locked:** Tell Kittl what to generate AND what to preserve. "Generate a vintage distressed texture WHILE KEEPING the existing logo placement unchanged."
2. **Use style keywords Kittl recognizes:** "Screen-printed," "letterpress," "risograph," "hand-drawn illustration," "vector flat design."
3. **Specify print-readiness:** "300 DPI, CMYK-safe colors, bleed area included" for production output.
4. **Layer your iterations:** Start broad (concept), then narrow (detail), then polish (production). Never try to get everything in one prompt.

**The Kittl Blueprint Production Pipeline:**

| Step | Board | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Reference Photo | Image Board | Generate or upload a photorealistic base image | Clean product/subject photo |
| 2. Outline Extraction | Image Board | Convert photo to clean line art / outline | Vector-ready outline drawing |
| 3. Style Application | Image Board | Apply target aesthetic to the outline | Styled illustration/design |
| 4. Multi-Angle Generation | Image Board | Generate 3-5 angles of the styled design | Complete product view set |
| 5. Detail Enhancement | Image Board | Generate close-up detail shots | Texture and detail reference |
| 6. Mockup Placement | Image Board | Place design onto product mockups | T-shirt, hoodie, poster, packaging mockups |
| 7. Video Animation | Video Board | Animate the mockup with camera movement | Product reveal video |

**Kittl Video Engine Comparison:**

| Engine | Strength | Best For | Duration | Motion Quality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Veo 3.1 | Cinematic quality, natural motion | Hero shots, brand films | 5-8s | Excellent |
| Seedance 1.5 Pro | Fast generation, good for iteration | Social content, quick edits | 3-5s | Good |
| Kling 3.0 | Character consistency, face quality | People-focused content | 4-6s | Very Good |
| Wan 2.1 | Stylized motion, artistic | Abstract, artistic content | 3-5s | Good |

**Kittl Video Prompt Structure (Modular):**

```
CAMERA: [movement and framing]
ACTION: [what happens in the scene]
AUDIO: [sound design direction]
TEXT: [any on-screen text or title cards]
```

### Midjourney v6 — The Aesthetic Image Engine

**Prompting Formula:**

```
[SUBJECT DESCRIPTION], [ENVIRONMENT/SETTING], [LIGHTING], [CAMERA/LENS], [STYLE/AESTHETIC], [MOOD], [QUALITY MODIFIERS] --ar [RATIO] --v 6 --s [STYLIZE] --c [CHAOS]
```

**Key Parameters:**
- `--ar`: Aspect ratio (16:9 cinematic, 3:2 editorial, 1:1 social, 9:16 mobile, 2:3 portrait)
- `--s` (Stylize): 0-1000. Low = literal interpretation. High = artistic interpretation. Default 100.
- `--c` (Chaos): 0-100. Higher = more unexpected/varied results. Use 20-40 for creative exploration.
- `--no`: Negative prompt. Exclude unwanted elements: `--no text, watermark, blur`
- `--q`: Quality. 0.25 (draft), 0.5 (half), 1 (standard), 2 (high detail)

**Midjourney Style Modifiers That Work:**
- Photography: "shot on Hasselblad," "Kodak Portra 400," "35mm film grain," "DSLR, shallow DOF"
- Cinematic: "anamorphic lens flare," "cinematic color grading," "film still from [director name]"
- Illustration: "editorial illustration," "gouache painting," "risograph print," "screen-printed"
- 3D: "octane render," "unreal engine 5," "isometric," "clay render"

### Flux Pro — The Photorealistic Engine

**Prompting Formula:**

```
[DETAILED SUBJECT], [PRECISE ENVIRONMENT], [SPECIFIC LIGHTING with direction], [CAMERA SPECS: lens, aperture, ISO], [COLOR TEMPERATURE], [MOOD/ATMOSPHERE]
```

**Flux Pro Strengths:** Photorealism, text rendering, hands/fingers accuracy, architectural precision.

**Critical Flux Rules:**
1. **Be hyper-specific:** Flux rewards precision. "A 28-year-old woman with shoulder-length auburn hair" beats "a young woman."
2. **Specify camera technically:** "Canon EOS R5, 85mm f/1.4, ISO 200, 1/250s" produces more photorealistic results than generic "portrait photo."
3. **Describe light direction:** "Key light from upper-left at 45 degrees, soft fill from right, no harsh shadows" gives Flux physical lighting data.
4. **Use real-world references:** "Shot in a Brooklyn brownstone apartment" beats "indoor setting."

## NARRATIVE ARCHITECTURE: THE TRAILER FRAMEWORK

### The 4-Act Trailer Structure

Apply this to any video sequence, social content, or visual narrative:

| Act | Duration | Purpose | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Act 0: Cold Open** | 0-5 seconds | Pattern interrupt — stop the scroll | Maximum visual/auditory intensity, no context needed |
| **Act 1: Setup** | 5-30 seconds | Establish world, character, stakes | Wide shots, world-building, introduce the question |
| **Act 2: Escalation** | 30-60 seconds | Build tension, raise stakes, montage | Increasing pace, music build, conflict introduction |
| **Act 3: Climax + Button** | 60-90 seconds | Peak intensity + memorable final beat | Money shot, title card, release date, final twist |

### The 7 Trailer Archetypes

| Archetype | Structure | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **The Mystery Box** | Question → clues → deeper mystery → no answer | Intrigue-driven, luxury, tech reveals |
| **The Spectacle** | Quiet → build → EXPLOSION of visuals | Action, blockbuster, product launches |
| **The Emotional Journey** | Normal life → disruption → struggle → hope | Brand stories, testimonials, cause marketing |
| **The Slow Burn** | Whisper → whisper → whisper → BANG | Horror, suspense, premium/luxury |
| **The Montage Machine** | Rapid-fire highlights, music-driven | Sports, events, year-in-review, hype |
| **The Character Study** | One person's world, intimate and specific | Documentary, personal brand, founder story |
| **The Contrast Cut** | Beautiful ←→ Ugly, Calm ←→ Chaos alternating | Social commentary, before/after, transformation |

## PROMPT QUALITY CHECKLIST

Before generating any prompt, verify it includes:

- [ ] **Specific subject** (not generic — age, clothing, expression, pose)
- [ ] **Defined environment** (not "background" — specific place, weather, time of day)
- [ ] **Lighting setup** (not "good lighting" — named setup, direction, quality)
- [ ] **Camera specification** (shot type, angle, movement, lens focal length)
- [ ] **Color/grade direction** (not "colorful" — specific palette or grade name)
- [ ] **Mood/atmosphere** (emotional target, not just visual description)
- [ ] **Style reference** (specific director, film, photographer, or aesthetic movement)
- [ ] **Platform-appropriate syntax** (--ar for Midjourney, logical anchors for Higgsfield)
- [ ] **Physics-aware motion** (for video — describe HOW things move, not just that they move)
- [ ] **Negative space** (what to EXCLUDE — "no text, no watermarks, no distortion")
