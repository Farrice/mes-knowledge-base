A 92/100 content score can be useful.

It can also be the most convincing lie in the report.

Because the score can tell a founder deciding whether to trust an SEO content score that the page is ready.

It cannot tell them what happened next.

Our analysis keeps four truths apart:

1. The deterministic checks passed.
2. The expert still made a judgment call.
3. The operator overrode one decision for a stated reason.
4. A dated market observation either happened or it did not.

That is the information gain: A concrete separation of readiness, judgment, override, and observed outcomes.

For example, `CITED` is not `TRAFFIC`, and `CONVERTED` is not `COLLECTED`.
