# Visual Brief — SEO Outcome Stages Explained

Format: visual brief. Structure: one spine -> eight independent proof cards -> boundary footer.

## Visual hook

**Composition:** A horizontal evidence rail with eight cards. No automatic arrows. Each card has its own receipt icon, date slot, and proof label.

**Palette:** Neutral graphite for unknown; blue for an observed stage; amber for a prediction. Never use green merely because a score is high.

## Information gain

A visual system that prevents readiness and later outcome stages from collapsing into one score.

1. PREDICTED — score or hypothesis.
2. PUBLISHED — live URL receipt.
3. INDEXED — dated index evidence.
4. RANKED — query, engine, location, and position evidence.
5. CITED — engine, query, answer, URL, and timestamp.
6. TRAFFIC — analytics import.
7. CONVERTED — defined conversion event.
8. COLLECTED — cleared revenue receipt.

**Example:** show CITED and TRAFFIC as neighboring cards with no causal arrow.

**Source footer:** https://example.com/visual-source-receipt [1].

## Boundary footer

No arrow may imply that one outcome stage automatically causes or proves the next.

Prediction: The visual may improve correct stage labeling in the next internal review. Primary stage: PREDICTED. Observation window: 7 days. Falsifier: The next internal review still collapses two or more outcome stages after 7 days.

Log the stage you can actually prove.
