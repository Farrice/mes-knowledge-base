# After-Action Review: Dhar Capability Restoration

## Mission

`Dhar Post-Extraction Restoration: Capability Without RBR Shackles`

- **Date:** 2026-08-04
- **Scale:** STRIKE TEAM
- **Duration:** ~14-minute instrumented implementation-to-receipt window;
  preceding diagnosis and redirection were not time-instrumented.
- **Agents deployed:** Main-thread System Audit + Repeatability Spine owner;
  Kepler, depth-prompt cold test; Mill, universal-depth stress and Anti-Shackle
  design; Cicero, protocol-overlap audit and independent cold validation.
- **Workstreams:** 6: freeze the good state; prove the regression; restore
  Launchpad, High-Taste, and Farrice Content OS; remove duplicated Dhar tiers
  and schema; demote RBR to an optional claim-local practice; independently
  verify capability preservation.

## What Worked

- The Preservation Lock separated the harvested Dhar capability from the later
  enforcement layer, so restoration did not become deletion.
- Test-first repair moved from expected red to 6/6 safe controls, all four hard
  vetoes, 3/3 capability-preservation surfaces, and an unchanged historical
  V1 hash.
- The Dhar factory remained at nine workflows, ten prompts, intact manifests,
  and honest workflow-only readiness.
- System Audit isolated active behavior from ambient worktree drift;
  Repeatability Spine protected the known-good post-extraction state.
- Mill converted Farrice's anti-shackle intent into OPEN/SHADOW/ENFORCE rules
  and evidence-earned promotion criteria.
- Cicero's cold review caught four stale V1 documents that still looked like
  current authority; they were marked superseded before closeout.
- The patch remained local and did not alter Mission, global Codex, source
  corpora, manifests, or plugin-readiness claims.

## What Did Not Work

- The preceding integration promoted an optional source-depth practice into
  broad keyword and route activation, mandatory fields, artifact tiers, and
  whole-artifact authorization before creative-range preservation was proven.
- Initial operator preflight misrouted the repair to Bloat Optimizer and
  required a manual owner override.
- The first repair pass covered active surfaces but not every historical
  authority-looking document; independent semantic review closed that gap.
- Ambient checks mixed isolated-worktree omissions and old cohesion-state drift
  with the targeted patch until their proof states were separated.
- The frozen V1 helper remains directly executable historical code, although no
  active workflow imports or calls it.

## User Redirections

Four substantive redirections occurred across the integration-to-restoration
arc; none occurred after the final Preservation Lock:

1. Farrice questioned whether the integration had overbuilt and reduced
   intuitive capacity.
2. He specified shadow mode with only truth, proof, privacy, and permission
   vetoes.
3. He proposed returning to the post-extraction state.
4. He clarified that restoration must retain and broadly deploy the harvested
   storytelling, media, packaging, production, and depth expertise.

## Effective Pairings

- System Audit + Repeatability Spine: isolate active failure while protecting
  the known-good state.
- Universal-depth stress + main-thread integration: turn philosophy into
  executable contracts and fixtures.
- Semantic cold review + deterministic verifiers: catch both runtime regression
  and authority-document drift.
- Anti-Shackle verifier + Dhar factory verifier: prove constraints were removed
  without losing capability.

## Learnings Captured

- Harvested expertise should amplify judgment and creative range, not become
  front-door governance.
- "Go back" meant restore behavior while retaining learned capability, not
  erase the extraction.
- Source-integrity enforcement stays claim-local and limited to truth, proof,
  privacy, and permission; quality and taste remain advisory.
- Structural proof does not earn automatic enforcement. Promotion requires
  production receipts, blind comparison, zero safe-task false blocks, no
  creative-range loss, and explicit approval.
- Dhar remains at `54/100`; the readiness verdict stays `keep as workflow`
  changes the readiness state.
