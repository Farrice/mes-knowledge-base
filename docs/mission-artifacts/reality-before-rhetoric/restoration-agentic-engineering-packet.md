## Preservation Lock

- **Keep:** all Dhar extraction sources, manifests, workflows, prompts, expert
  surfaces, production operations, David Bayer composition routes, FP-001
  learning, truth boundaries, and readiness limits.
- **Change:** RBR activation, whole-artifact authorization, duplicated packet
  fields, and mandatory content-card propagation.
- **Do not disturb:** unrelated dirty files, global Codex instructions, Mission,
  router ownership, registries, source history, or plugin-readiness score.
- **Risk:** a broad restore could erase the extracted capability together with
  the later integration.
- **Gate:** targeted patch only; frozen V1 hash preserved; Dhar, High-Taste,
  Launchpad, skill-system, and control-plane checks rerun.

## Behavioral Invariants

1. Exploration is open by default.
2. Reality Before Rhetoric is optional and claim-local, not a front-door gate.
3. No keyword or route name may activate enforcement.
4. Fabricated facts, proof promotion, private-material exposure, and
   permissionless use remain hard claim/material vetoes.
5. A veto affects the smallest unsafe unit. Supported adjacent work continues.
6. No RBR fields are mandatory in Launchpad packets, content cards, story
   briefs, or receipts.
7. One domain owner still composes the artifact.
8. No private source is automatically persisted or reused.
9. The Dhar system stays workflow-only until real production evidence earns a
   different readiness decision.
