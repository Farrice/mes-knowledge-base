## What the benchmark measures

Each fixture tests:

- whether Reality Before Rhetoric activates or bypasses;
- the separate `source_status` and `authorized_artifact_tier` decisions;
- whether drafting is authorized;
- exact, accumulated, missing, or non-applicable hinge handling;
- offer claim class or system proof state when relevant;
- retrieval versus lived interview versus external/runtime acquisition;
- the one-question, non-leading, five-question ceiling;
- required and forbidden behavior frozen for future cold-start evaluation.

The runtime benchmark executes the deterministic activation boundary, evidence-profile decision, and Co-Creative Launchpad handoff. It freezes conversational required/forbidden behavior as the regression contract; it does not claim to be a market-performance or prose-quality experiment.

## Domain findings

### Personal content

Reality Before Rhetoric does not equate depth with confession or a dramatic epiphany. It distinguishes an absent hinge from an accumulated one and permits a transformation only when a witnessed counterchoice or attributable evidence earns it.

### Offer copy

An untested offer is not automatically blocked. It may ship mechanism, testable hypothesis, and attributable buyer language while demonstrated outcomes and case-study transformations remain unauthorized. When bounded delivery proof exists, the system allows the demonstrated result without laundering it into revenue attribution.

### Strategy

The protocol treats a real tradeoff as strategy's hinge. Missing resource or downside constraints pause recommendation; complete constraints authorize one chosen path, explicit rejection, accepted cost, milestone, and falsifier.

### Research

The system acquires public evidence itself instead of interviewing Farrice about retrievable facts. Conflicting primary evidence does not force a false synthesis; it authorizes a narrower confidence update with unresolved uncertainty intact.

### System repair

Structural presence never becomes runtime proof. The protocol requires a reproduced before-state and observed after-state for `behavior-delta-verified`, while keeping the claim scoped to the tested fixture and bypass boundary.

## Proof boundary

This benchmark proves the scoped workspace-local activation, decision vocabulary, consumer handoff, and frozen regression behavior. It does not prove:

- higher engagement, sales, or audience transformation;
- that every future model response will execute the conversational protocol perfectly without a live cold-start evaluation;
- global availability or plugin portability;
- permission to store or reuse interview material;
- readiness beyond the specific tested workflows and fixtures.
