## Problem

The first Creative Test Autopsy pack preserved sources, claims, unknowns, and test logic, but its hooks and script often restated facts. Farrice scored the Rello work 5/10: solid, safe, and commonplace rather than surprising enough to make a founder want the strategist on the team.

## Root Cause

The workflow jumped from reporting to concepts. It did not require the strategist to identify a pattern, interpret what it might mean, preserve a credible alternative, name the decision delta, or define a reversal condition. Copy specialists therefore received product facts rather than a human tension worth dramatizing.

## Approach That Worked

1. Preserve the source/proof/test spine, then add an evidence-to-meaning pass: VERIFIED observation -> material pattern -> labeled interpretation -> plausible alternative -> decision delta -> falsifier.
2. Keep Dara Denney as integration owner. Give Luke Iha only the six-hook tension pass and Stefan Georgi only the script/dead-zone pass. Split the exhaustive internal autopsy from the bounded client Proof Note and state exactly where paid account-informed work begins.

## Dead Ends

Adding stronger adjectives or more experts would have produced louder fact recitation or expert soup. Machine-clean copy was also insufficient evidence because the user's felt verdict exposed a taste failure the classifiers could not see.

## Verification

Six revised JSON sources parse, six share-safe HTML briefs render, all contain two concept territories and six sample openings, share redaction passes, and the authored Rello copy passes prose, structural-finish, grounding, and claim-risk checks. Rello and GateDrop were inspected in the local browser. The revised Dara workflow records the sequence and the client/paid boundary. No outreach or publishing occurred.

## Weaker-Model Trap

Do not translate "add emotion" into invented pain, melodrama, or more forceful feature claims. The emotional scene must descend from the interpretation, and the interpretation must remain falsifiable. Do not let a copy specialist change the hypothesis or upgrade public evidence into account knowledge.
