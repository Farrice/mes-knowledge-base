# Solution: Jeremy Haynes Cold Traffic Offer Mastery Extraction

## Problem

Cold-traffic offer mastery was absent from the harness. The system had offer-building frameworks (Hormozi value equation), positioning frameworks (Dunford, Fladlien), but lacked the re-composition engine — the methodology for building offers that work for cold audiences, then adapting them as the audience migrates colder (in-market → needs-convinced → mass-market).

**Gap**: "Anything that works for cold audiences has the highest odds of working for warm audiences, vice versa not true." The asymmetry wasn't systematized.

## When to Use

**Start here**: `/jh-umbrella-narrative` if building a cold-traffic offer from scratch. Ground the ICP narrative before composing anything.

**Route by problem**:
- Offer underperforming cold traffic → `/jh-offer-audit` (diagnose)
- Growth stalled at scale → `/jh-plateau-diagnostic` (offer vs. funnel)
- Need to scale to colder audiences → `/jh-temp-migrate` (recompose)
- Sales conversations show persistent objections → `/jh-objection-mine` (augment core)
- Composed offer ready for copy team → `/jh-offer-to-copy` (hand off to copywriters)

## Key Principles (Never Violate)

1. **Narrative first, stack second**: Never compose before narrative is grounded. GROUND step is non-negotiable.
2. **1:1 component traceability**: Every component must name exactly one narrative element. Orphans get cut ("not random").
3. **Cold-audience asymmetry**: Anything that works for cold audiences has highest odds of working for warm audiences. Validate cold-first.
4. **Offer is dynamic**: Not a launch artifact. Re-composes via objection mining (AUGMENT) and temperature migration (MIGRATE).
5. **Audience state dictates stack**: In-market vs. needs-convinced = completely different stacks, even for the same product.
6. **Show rate = offer signal**: Misaligned offer cooks every funnel stat at once. Step-function decay = alignment event, not tuning issue.
7. **Urgency = quantified inaction cost**: Never manufactured urgency. Timer resets and "3 spots left" are banned. Real urgency is cost-of-inaction from the buyer's circumstances.

Cold-traffic offer mastery fills a structural gap in the harness. Positioning frameworks tell you *how* to frame your uniqueness. Offer frameworks (Hormozi) tell you *what components* matter. The re-composition engine — especially objection mining (AUGMENT) and temperature migration (MIGRATE) — is how real businesses scale beyond their initial audience.

This extraction unlocks: offer audits for stuck deals, migration planning for plateaus, and dynamic augmentation workflows for scaling cold traffic.
