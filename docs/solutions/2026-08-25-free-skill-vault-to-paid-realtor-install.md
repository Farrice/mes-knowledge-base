# Free skill vault to paid realtor install

## Problem signature

A high-distribution expert gives a large library of realtor AI skills away for free. Farrice has stronger orchestration and vertical-specific assets but no comparable distribution. Selling “better prompts” would compete against free in a category where buyers cannot judge quality before purchase.

## Evidence

- Mike Sherrard's August 24, 2026 video demonstrates eight realtor skills and offers a 40-skill course free for a comment.
- Early inspected metadata showed 2,674 views, 169 likes, and 235 comments. The incentive makes this an interest signal, not payment proof.
- The prior realtor offer red-team found exact one-time-install demand thin, agency-first targeting structurally wrong, and the Listing Launch System stronger as a proof mechanism than as a complete offer.
- Existing Antigravity capabilities already cover the Agent Brain and Listing Launch jobs. Local Authority is partial; a realtor-specific Lead Reactivation module remains a build-and-test gap.

## Solution

Move one step downstream from the free files. Sell a bounded, personalized installation that produces real work inside the buyer's own business. Keep four modules only: Agent Brain, Local Authority, Listing Launch, and Lead Reactivation. The files are the take-home asset; installation, source grounding, first outputs, and independent rerun earn the price.

Do not modify the canonical Mike Sherrard skill from this one source. Preserve the video as a dated extraction record and keep the new offer as a proposal until payment evidence exists.

## Proof boundary

- Source interest: VERIFIED, incentive-driven.
- Generic skill-file paid demand: NO EVENT.
- Four-module offer demand: UNTESTED.
- $750 founding price and $250 deposit: UNTESTED.
- Deliverability: PARTIAL, with two ready modules, one packaging task, and one missing reusable module.

## Cheapest test

After the two missing prototype modules pass a real Jen run, send ten owner-approved warm introductions through Jen's network. Proceed only with at least two fit calls and one paid $250 deposit inside 72 hours. Calls without a deposit trigger one-variable iteration. Weak replies stop the build.

## Reuse rule

When a creator's free library appears to validate a paid product, separate four layers: category interest, distribution advantage, backend monetization, and the exact paid transaction. Compete at the first step the free offer does not complete.
