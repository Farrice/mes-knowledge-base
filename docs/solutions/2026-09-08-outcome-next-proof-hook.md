## Limits and acceptance

The observer reports `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `NO_FLAG`, `UNOBSERVED`, or `SKIP`.
It never reports quality PASS. It recognizes bounded text cues and matched
successful retrieval calls, not the semantic sufficiency of research. Wrapped
tool calls it cannot interpret remain unobserved; false positives and false
negatives are possible. Human-quality improvement is UNTESTED until inspected
in ordinary work. The instruction is preventive; the observer is advisory.

It reads only a bounded transcript tail, resets evidence on the current user
turn, ignores reasoning/commentary as action proof, and writes hashes/flag names
to the existing observation log rather than copying conversation content.
