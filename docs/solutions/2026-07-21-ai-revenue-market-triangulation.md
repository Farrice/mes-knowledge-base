## Problem

The operator needed a current, source-grounded answer to whether AI consulting, AI creative strategy, training, LinkedIn ghostwriting, and copywriting still contain real buyer demand. The answer also had to choose one practical offer without misrepresenting category demand as proof that the exact offer would sell.

## Root Cause

The apparent binary, “AI is booming” versus “AI is saturated,” mixes three different markets: commodity production, implementation and adoption work, and outcome-led authority services. Public demand data can validate a category, but it cannot validate a specific buyer, promise, price, or close rate.

## Approach That Worked

1. Compile the raw concern through the revenue bridge, then split research into AI adoption and implementation, LinkedIn and content demand, and adjacent productized-service opportunities. Verify the strongest claims against direct reports and label platform or vendor limitations.
2. Rank opportunities by demand, budget, competitive room, proof burden, access, speed, recurrence, and operator fit. Preserve the existing Authority Flywheel spine, narrow it to a paid pilot, and use payment as the exact-offer validation event.

## Dead Ends

Broad “AI consultant” positioning, generic prompt training, bulk AI-written post packages, and a large transformation engagement all create more proof burden than the operator can support today. Marketplace growth rates also cannot be treated as a census of the economy or evidence that a particular $2,000 pilot will close.

## Verification

The research artifact passed the strict claim audit, export-format guard, and research quality gate at 90/100 with 11 URLs from 11 domains. The content finish gate returned WARN, not FAIL, because its structure detector counts the report's research tables and lists. The exact offer remains explicitly UNCONFIRMED until payment.

## Weaker-Model Trap

A weaker model will either declare the AI market dead from social sentiment or recommend a generic AI agency from high-level adoption statistics. Both moves skip buyer access, commercial trigger, proof burden, and the distinction between category evidence and cash validation.
