## Problem

The Jun Story Engine had strong source, component, prompt, and negative-control proof, but Farrice had not seen it operate across the real states in his own material. A later Bayer language pass became the visible result and made the source-built engine seem grandiose and abstract even though the failure lived after extraction.

## Root Cause

Structural proof, personal behavior proof, prose taste, and market proof were allowed to blur together. The initial field proof used one documented commercial contrast, not a full personal story. Natural-language routing also failed to put Jun first for one cold lived-material query.

## Approach That Worked

1. Preserve Jun's truth and privacy decisions before changing anything; classify the Bayer pass as failed experiment evidence rather than engine evidence.
2. Test four source-backed Farrice outcomes separately: a full seminar story, a method candidate, a documented fragment, and a strong fact that remains `NEEDS SOURCE`.
3. Add only narrow lived-material routing phrases and lock three cold queries into the connected verifier.
4. Run anti-slop, voice, content-finish, grounding, routing, and control-plane checks; repair only the repeated-`I` cadence the finish gate rejected.

## Dead Ends

- Treating clean prose classification as proof that the writing matched Farrice's taste.
- Adding Bayer, Saxon, or another language owner before Jun had made the story decision.
- Turning the 90+ agent fact into a cinematic naptime scene without a supplied Problem, Pursuit, or Turn.
- Using the generic `validate_skill.py` on a `.agents/skills/` command bridge; the dedicated connected verifier owns that surface.

## Verification

- `verify_jun_story_engine.py`: 32/32 required files, 6/6 story fixtures, method and angle controls, all sabotage cases, and 3/3 cold routing queries PASS.
- Google Operator Core and system control-plane checks pass after the bounded routing addition.
- All four personal asset files return anti-slop `CLEAN` at `0/10`.
- The full story and documented fragment return no deterministic Farrice voice tells.
- Content Finish Gate rejected repeated `I` openings, then passed after one rhythm-only repair.
- Grounding and export-format guards pass. Audience and market performance remain untested.

## Weaker-Model Trap

A weaker model will rewrite the failed prose harder, mine the most painful private material, or create a heroic origin around an impressive result. The correct move is to separate engine, route, voice, and market proof; select the smallest truthful story state; and let `NEEDS SOURCE` or `NO STORY` remain successful outcomes.
