## Problem

A one-hour Nicolas Cole video contained a useful first-dollar sequence, but its component capabilities already existed across niche positioning, library-first writing, newsletters, digital products, and client acquisition. A literal extraction would have produced a duplicate mega-skill or forced every operator through a slow audience-first calendar.

## Root Cause

The source mixed three layers: a personal origin story, durable sequencing mechanics, and conditional business-model choices. The visuals added almost no process evidence, so the spoken architecture had to be timestamped and separated from source-reported outcomes. Existing Antigravity capabilities owned the craft; the missing capability was orchestration across evidence state and cash horizon.

## Approach That Worked

1. Capture the complete transcript and scene-aware visual sample, build an observed-spoken ledger, and label source-reported outcomes separately from verified implementation claims.
2. Add one Nicolas Cole orchestration skill that owns sequence, cash-clock branching, congruence, and post-payment learning while handing niche, content, newsletter, product, service, and fast-cash execution to existing owners.
3. Encode the crucial negative control: urgent cash plus no audience routes service-first to `/first-10k`; newsletter and course paths are parked, and revenue remains `NO EVENT` until payment evidence exists.

## Dead Ends

The default YouTube watcher entered a PO-token retry loop. The bounded fallback used `yt-dlp --ignore-config --no-plugin-dirs` with the embedded web client, then ran the local watcher on the downloaded file. A repository-wide prompt-pointer refresh also exposed unrelated stale changes; those diffs were removed from the lane. The archived behavior-contract verifier resolves its root from the archive folder and produces false missing-file failures.

## Verification

- Video package: 1,780 timestamped transcript segments, 11,980 clean words, and 16 observed-spoken ledger rows.
- Connected system: five workflows, five structure-pure prompts, eight source anchors, three command surfaces, and one urgent-cash negative control all pass the local deterministic verifier.
- Prompt audit: 3,958 of 3,958 v2 prompt files pass.
- Routing: `/nicolas-cole-first-dollar` ranks first for the broad connected job; `/first-10k` remains the stronger direct route when urgent cash is explicit.
- Harness: Codex harness passes; Operator Core reports 14 of 14 aligned.
- Commercial outcome: `NO EVENT`. No outreach, buyer reply, deposit, or payment was created by this build.

## Weaker-Model Trap

Do not summarize the seven steps into a standalone skill and do not call a 30- or 90-day publishing calendar universally necessary. Preserve the demand-first origin mechanic, route by cash clock, distinguish information advantage from regulated authority, and keep implementation proof separate from buyer proof.
