# Solution Card — Two-Gate Dispatch: reachability is a channel variable, not a lane variable

**Date:** 2026-07-21 (night — third adversarial pass of the day)
**Domain:** Cold outreach / offer channel strategy / pricing posture
**Trigger:** Farrice: "Are the coaches in this industry actually on LinkedIn, and are they reachable? Do they actually want this offer? We need to pressure test... I don't want to be a cheap option either."

## Method
Three parallel legs, then synthesis: (1) evidence agent on channel truth, (2) cold buyer gauntlet — standing panel built at `councils/buyers/practitioner-founders.md`, walked through the REAL assets in realistic order (DM → profile → post), (3) /assemble panel (Ross McKay × Lara Acosta × Oren × Farrice-as-Function-Owner) deliberating lane + pricing. Full panel pin: `.agent/handoffs/assemble-pressure-test-farrice-s-signal-pilot-practitione.md`.

## Findings
- **Channel truth:** fitness core = PARTIAL, tilting off-platform (the bigger the fitness brand, the deader the LinkedIn: Rusin 20K certs/418 followers, Hanson 338). Exec coaches + B2B consultants = LinkedIn-native with proven $2-5K/mo ghostwriting spend. Menopause/women's-health fitness sub-niche IS LinkedIn-native. Cold-PITCH DMs are dead per 2026 playbooks; spec-FIRST DMs still convert.
- **Cold gauntlet:** current assets = "polite maybe with one live door," not an easy yes. Spec-sample DM is the strongest cold asset; profile stalls click-throughs on zero service-proof. WINCEs: unverifiable "1,000+", the free-year story next to a missed number (boundary-risk read), guru-cadence closer. $2K + free spec + 69 followers reads scraping-by WITHOUT an anchor.
- **Panel net-new principle (THE keeper):** **Reachability is a per-prospect CHANNEL variable, not a lane variable — the lane question dissolves.** Two independent gates per prospect: (a) ACTIVITY (posted/replied on LinkedIn last 30 days) gates the send channel; (b) AUTHORITY-ASYMMETRY (big off-platform proof, thin LinkedIn) gates the flagship bet. Fail (a) → don't drop the prospect or the lane; re-route delivery to where they're demonstrably alive (IG DM, their funnel), demote LinkedIn to credibility page. Never pitch "your LinkedIn is neglected" INTO a neglected LinkedIn inbox.

## Resolutions (Farrice's operating spec)
1. **Lane:** stay fitness for cycle one under two-gate dispatch. Flagship day-1 sends = dual-clear actives (Rosner, Broxterman; Morrison on the activity gate). Dormant giants (Rusin, Hanson) = cycle-two re-channeled touches, only after a logged flagship send exists. LinkedIn-native expert-service re-aim: written now, shelved, fires ONLY on the standing kill condition (day-7 <3 fit-conversations → teardown side-bet primary; 20 convos/0 pilots → /offer-redteam).
2. **Pricing:** hold $2,000. Verbatim frame: *"Founder pricing for the first three pilots is $2,000 prepaid — less than what you charge one cohort of your own program. It goes up the day the first case study exists."* Premium = refusal (5-condition checklist stated aloud) + capacity (3 seats) + anchor to the BUYER'S own verified economics. **Never cite Cooz $3K as a receipt** (panel veto: doesn't survive checking) and never put the free-year story in sales copy (gauntlet: boundary-risk read).
3. **Copy fixes applied same night** to PROFILE-REBUILD: 1,000+ removed, cold start owned ("building the system in public"), scoped guarantee replaces promise-story, capped-lane added to Post 1, guru-cadence closer cut.

## Contradiction resolved on record
Gauntlet suggested anchoring price to the Cooz $3K precedent; panel vetoed it as unverifiable. Resolution: buyer's-own-economics anchor wins (checkable beats private precedent). Both were right about the problem (naked $2K reads cheap); the panel's anchor survives diligence.

## Operator lesson
A channel diagnostic and a lane diagnostic feel identical from inside ("nobody's answering = wrong market"). They're not. Sort prospects on activity and authority as independent axes BEFORE concluding anything about the lane — most "dead lane" verdicts are dead-channel verdicts wearing a bigger costume.

## Re-solve guard
Before any "re-aim the offer / wrong niche" decision: (1) has every prospect been tagged ACTIVE/DORMANT on the actual send channel? (2) were dormant prospects re-routed to their live channel before being counted as evidence? (3) is the pitch itself being delivered into the channel it criticizes? If any answer is no, it's not a lane problem yet.
