## Compliance & Risk Map (FTC Boundary)

### Green Light (Defensible)
- "Supports liver function"
- "Contains ingredients studied for liver health"
- "Milk thistle has been traditionally used to support liver health"
- "Clinical evidence shows silymarin helps liver cells resist oxidative stress"

### Yellow Light (Risky, Needs Legal Review)
- "Liver cleanse"
- "Detoxifies the body"
- "Removes toxins"
- "Supports natural detoxification"
- "Helps your body cleanse itself"

### Red Light (FTC Flag, Avoid)
- "Cures liver disease"
- "Treats hepatitis"
- "Reverses liver damage"
- "Detoxes you in 7 days"
- "Removes all toxins"

**Key Rule:** Use "supports" + ingredient name. Avoid "cleanse," "detox," "remove toxins," and disease claims.
