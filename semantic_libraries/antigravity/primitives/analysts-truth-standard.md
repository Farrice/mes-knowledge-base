# Analyst's Truth Standard

Status: `ACTIVE ADVISORY`. This is a companion intelligence primitive, not an expert,
agent, skill, command, router, score, schema, template, or blocking gate.

## Purpose

Prevent an accurate answer from being mistaken for a useful one. The standard
helps an existing owner move from facts to meaning and action only when that
extra depth changes a real decision, artifact, test, or confidence state.

The source spine is the Marketing Specialist Workbench package at
`extractions/video-context/SupWhagSCm8/`, especially `deep-extraction.md`,
`behavior-proof.md`, and `system-contract.md`. Its transcript-backed lesson is
artifact production plus decision support; Antigravity adds evidence states,
causal restraint, one craft owner, and a falsifiable next test. The source does
not prove that model-generated diagnoses are true or that marketing performance
will improve.

## The Depth Ladder

1. **Reporting: what is known.** State the verified observation, retrieved
   material, or result. Preserve unknowns and provenance.
2. **Analysis: what matters in the evidence.** Identify a relationship,
   contrast, pattern, break, or constraint without upgrading correlation into
   cause.
3. **Diagnosis: what may explain it.** Name the most plausible mechanism as a
   labeled inference, keep credible alternatives visible, and state confidence
   in proportion to the evidence.
4. **Decision intelligence: what changes.** Name the decision delta, why it is
   the nearest sensible move, and what evidence would confirm, weaken, or
   reverse the conclusion.

Stop at the earliest layer that fully serves the requested job. Deeper is not
automatically better.

## Activation

Apply quietly when both are true:

1. the work is synthesis, evaluation, strategy, creative ideation or review,
   research, a system readout, or a consequential decision; and
2. moving one layer deeper can materially change what the operator should
   believe, choose, make, test, inspect, or stop doing.

Skip direct factual answers, simple retrieval, translation, mechanical work,
and pure production unless the user asks for interpretation. Do not add an
analysis appendix merely to prove the companion ran.

## Internal Truth Map

Use only the cells needed for the job. Observation carries a `VERIFIED`,
`LIKELY`, `UNCONFIRMED`, `UNTESTED`, `NO EVENT`, or `NO PERMISSION` state plus
its source or gap. Inference names the proposed relationship or mechanism as an
interpretation, not a fact. Alternatives keep other plausible explanations in
view. Decision delta states what changes compared with a reporting-only answer.
Falsification identifies confirming and disconfirming evidence, including the
condition that would reverse the decision.

These are internal reasoning handles, not mandatory visible headings or fields.

## Creative Ideation Lens

For content and concept work, reporting-only failure often looks like a generic
topic list. Decision-grade creative intelligence may draw on an observed
audience, market, or cultural signal; the tension, mechanism, contradiction, or
desire inside it; a differentiated angle or surprising frame; why the angle
matters to this audience now; the proposed artifact, concept, scene, or format;
and the smallest test that can reveal resonance or misunderstanding.

Preserve divergence. Several strange, defensible directions may be better than
one optimized answer. A weak signal can justify a hypothesis or exploration,
not a confident audience diagnosis. The native creative owner retains voice,
taste, form, pacing, and the right to break the internal lens.

## Causal Restraint

Observation can support analysis without supporting diagnosis. Diagnosis needs
a mechanism, plausible alternatives, and calibrated confidence. Low-evidence
work may name an evidence gap, a hypothesis, or a reversible test; it may not
assert an exact cause. Recommendations based on uncertain causes should be
reversible and should name a stop, rollback, or reconsideration condition when
consequence warrants. A compelling story is not extra evidence.

## Visible Result Filter

Retain only material that presents evidence, states a meaningful labeled
inference, changes a decision, delivers the requested artifact, or defines a
test or next action.

This is a deletion rule, not an instruction to add five sections. Do not show
private chain-of-thought, generic framework teaching, unused alternatives, or a
ceremonial trace.

## Preservation Boundaries

The companion must preserve the user's approved premise, requested artifact,
scope, and length; the native owner and specialist definition of good; voice,
surprise, creative range, and useful ambiguity; factual, proof, privacy, safety,
cost, destructive-action, and permission boundaries; and the nearest reversible
action when evidence is incomplete.

It may not reroute the task, add required questions, block safe work, create a
universal score, force visible sections, expand the artifact, invent causality,
or silently replace production with analysis.

## Owner Integration

The current route or craft owner decides the artifact shape. Operating
Alignment carries the pointer-level activation sniff. Marketing Specialist
Workbench remains the source-grounded example, not the universal owner. System
Audit verifies structural wiring and firing behavior. No new hot route is
created.
