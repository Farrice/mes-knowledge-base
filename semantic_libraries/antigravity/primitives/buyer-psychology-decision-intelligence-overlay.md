# Buyer Psychology Decision Intelligence Overlay

**Status: SHADOW**
**Mode:** cold, conditional, advisory
**Source context:** Jason Fladlien practitioner corpus, independently calibrated where primary research is available

## Purpose

This companion helps the active function owner notice the one buyer or reader decision that could materially improve an audience-facing artifact. It is a mechanism selector, not a copy template, a scientific authority, or a replacement owner.

It may sharpen copy, content, marketing, offers, and bounded audience-facing knowledge work. The native owner still decides what to write, what evidence is sufficient, and whether the intervention improves the artifact.

## Activation

Cold-load this overlay only when both conditions are true:

1. A human receiver must understand, remember, evaluate, choose, adopt, or act.
2. One of the eight decisions below could materially change the artifact.

Select one primary decision. A second decision is allowed only when it resolves a clearly different weak link. Return to the native owner after the smallest useful change.

## Skip Conditions

Stay silent for:

- tiny or mechanical edits;
- neutral summaries, evidence ledgers, status reports, specifications, or code;
- research records where persuasion would blur uncertainty or claims;
- already-clear artifacts where no decision would materially change;
- any task where the layer would add questions, ceremony, or latency merely to show it ran.

## Evidence Language

Keep these labels distinct:

- `SOURCE-OBSERVED`: present in a locally captured Jason transcript or performance.
- `PRIMARY-CORROBORATED`: the underlying phenomenon has support in a cited primary or authoritative source; this does not validate Jason's application, causal story, numbers, or business result.
- `OPERATIONAL-SYNTHESIS`: an Antigravity decision label or deployment rule derived from sources and existing system needs.
- `UNVERIFIED`: a causal, numerical, revenue, conversion, or universality claim not independently demonstrated.

Repeated claims across Jason videos establish practitioner-corpus consistency, not independent scientific corroboration.

## Eight Decisions

The decision labels are Antigravity operational synthesis. The source names remain attached so the translation is auditable.

| Decision | Jason source mechanic | Seed anchor | Diagnostic question | Smallest useful move | Countercondition |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **Belief** | Belief perseverance | `01:03` | Is the artifact arguing head-on with a belief the audience is likely to defend? | Acknowledge the current logic, expose its limit with evidence or a question, then offer a testable alternative. | Do not invent the belief, shame the reader, or manufacture discomfort. Hand evidence-based belief change to David McRaney. |
| **Focus** | "Attentional bias" in Jason's phrasing; working-set and chunking in this overlay | `03:54` | Is the receiver being asked to hold too many equal-weight ideas? | Name a short roadmap, group details, and make the next decision visually obvious. | Do not treat three or four items as a universal cognitive law. Use the Low-Cognitive-Load Message Gate when clarity is the whole job. |
| **Recognition** | Barnum effect / artful vagueness | `06:51` | Can a truthful shared experience create recognition without pretending to know the individual? | Use a supported market pattern, then move quickly to a specific observable scene or proof. | No fake personalization, cold-reading posture, generic flattery, or unsupported psychographics. |
| **Priority** | Law of triviality | `11:51` | Is attention being spent on easy minor details while the consequential decision is buried? | Name the high-consequence question first and park reversible details. | Do not dismiss material objections as trivial or use priority framing to avoid disclosure. |
| **Fit** | "Bounded reality" in Jason's phrasing; bounded rationality and satisficing in research | `15:37` | Does the receiver need an objectively exhaustive solution, or a credible best-fit path under real time, knowledge, and effort constraints? | Define the fit criteria and show the simplest sufficient route. | Do not lower quality, conceal tradeoffs, or label a weak offer "good enough." |
| **Choice** | Paradox of choice | `18:11` | Are options increasing fit or merely increasing decision burden? | Recommend a default, group alternatives by use case, and preserve material options and terms. | Choice-overload effects are context-dependent; do not assume fewer choices are inherently better. |
| **Congruence** | Cognitive dissonance / campaign congruence | `21:52` | Do claim, proof, design, price, maturity, voice, and CTA tell the same story? | Repair the smallest cross-surface mismatch. | Reality can be messy; do not fabricate consistency or polish away uncertainty. |
| **Affect** | Affect heuristic | `24:41` | Is felt risk or benefit distorting what the receiver can evaluate? | Name legitimate emotion, make risk concrete, and pair feeling with verifiable evidence and reversible action. | Affect is not evidence. Never exploit fear, identity, trauma, health anxiety, financial distress, or status vulnerability. |

## Truth and Ethics Guard

Truth, proof, privacy, safety, permission, legal and claim-safety requirements, and Farrice's approved voice outrank persuasion.

The overlay may not invent or intensify audience beliefs, emotions, identities, urgency, scarcity, threat, status, proof, outcomes, or psychographics. It may not hide options or material terms, exploit a vulnerability, diagnose a buyer, use affect as proof, or add coercive pressure to medical, legal, financial, employment, housing, or other consequential decisions.

Jason explicitly presents the seed material as business practice rather than clinical or cognitive-psychology expertise. The schema source is context for empathy only; it is not permission to diagnose, segment, or target buyers by clinical labels.

## Cold Context Policy

The hot pointer contains only the activation boundary and this file path. Once activated:

1. Load this primitive.
2. Load `skills/jason-fladlien-marketing/references/buyer-psychology-decision-layer.md` only if source detail is needed.
3. Load one relevant source ledger or transcript slice, not the full corpus.
4. Return the selected decision and proposed change to the native owner.

Do not load Jason's full 38-workflow package, 33 deterministic `prompts-v2`, or compatibility prompt library merely because this overlay activates.

## Compact Handoff

Use plain prose when possible. When a visible handoff helps, report:

- selected decision and observed friction;
- evidence label and source anchor;
- proposed smallest change;
- preservation lock;
- native owner and remaining proof gap.

This is a decision receipt, not an extra scorecard.

## Promotion Gate

Remain in SHADOW after structural and fixture validation. The eight source-grounded decisions may be used conditionally now; the five research-derived candidate decisions remain development-only.

Three prospective receipts, a two-of-three blind craft win, and six safe controls are only an **early SHADOW-continuation check**. Passing that check permits more cold observation; it does not authorize a candidate, hot, global, or mandatory route.

Candidate promotion requires the frozen protocol in `extractions/jason-fladlien/buyer-psychology-intelligence-layer/mastery-benchmark-protocol.md`: `30/30` eligible preservation passes; at least `21/30` human-majority wins with no more than three losses and at least ten material wins; all `18/18` holdout controls; fifteen complete real-task receipts across the five eligible lanes; at least ten prospective blind comparisons meeting the protocol's win, loss, and materiality bounds; `12/12` prospective controls; zero hard preservation, question-burden, or false-block failures; and Farrice's explicit approval. This authorizes only a limited opt-in candidate route.

Broader or hot consideration requires the protocol's separate thirty-task probation. A workspace authority pointer, global pointer, hot route, gate, or enforcement change remains a separate promotion decision with separate explicit approval.

## Kill or Keep Cold

Keep the overlay cold or remove its owner pointers if it produces generic psychology labels, applies several decisions mechanically, sounds manipulative, duplicates existing owners, slows clear tasks, hides material information, or changes no material decision.
