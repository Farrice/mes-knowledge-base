# Reality Before Rhetoric: Optional Depth Practice

## Purpose

Reality Before Rhetoric is an optional co-creative depth practice for recovering
or calibrating one consequential source, scene, bridge, or claim. It is not a
router, preflight gate, artifact-authorization system, writer, content factory,
or mandatory schema.

The operating insight is:

> A missing bridge becomes retrieval, one question, a visible placeholder, or
> a smaller claim. Never prettier connective tissue.

Use it when Farrice explicitly invokes it or accepts one quiet recommendation
at a genuine source gap. Do not invoke it merely because the work concerns
story, content, offers, strategy, research, or system repair.

This practice is subordinate to
`semantic_libraries/antigravity/primitives/anti-shackle-constitution.md`.

## Modes

### OPEN

Explore freely. Generate hypotheses, structures, metaphors, alternatives,
counterfactuals, prototypes, fictional possibilities, and questions. Label
uncertainty; do not confuse exploration with a publishable factual assertion.

### SHADOW

Quietly inspect a specific proposed claim, scene, causal bridge, outcome, or use
of private material. Name evidence debt without blocking, interrupting,
rewriting, creating mandatory fields, or persisting source material.

### ENFORCE

Apply only at the smallest unsafe claim, material, or action. Remove, relabel,
narrow, redact, or withhold that unit. The supported adjacent work continues.

The four enforcement families are:

1. a fabricated fact, scene, quotation, causal bridge, or lived experience
   presented as real;
2. proof promotion beyond the available outcome, causal, buyer, research, or
   runtime evidence;
3. unauthorized exposure, persistence, or reuse of private material;
4. use, reuse, publication, or action without permission.

A full publishable artifact holds only when the violating unit is its essential
premise and no honest smaller form survives. Exploration and source acquisition
may continue.

## Source Modes

- `LIVED`: Farrice's real experience or memory.
- `OBSERVED`: a witnessed audience, customer, team, market, or category pattern.
- `ARTIFACT`: an inspectable draft, message, object, decision, dataset, source,
  transcript, trace, or receipt.

Use the least invasive source mode that can honestly support the intended
claim. Depth does not require confession.

## Optional Co-Creative Loop

### 1. Retrieve before asking

Search the current workspace, approved memory, source files, receipts, voice
evidence, and public evidence Codex can responsibly retrieve. Ask Farrice only
for lived truth, private meaning, felt judgment, taste, permission, or a
decision that cannot be retrieved.

### 2. Name the exact unit

Identify the one claim, scene, transformation beat, recommendation, research
conclusion, or readiness statement whose authority is uncertain. Do not turn a
general desire for quality into a source ritual.

### 3. Diagnose the earliest gap

Inspect only what materially supports that unit:

- load-bearing particular;
- pressure, contradiction, or constraint;
- observable cost or consequence;
- hinge or evidence update;
- first counterchoice, tradeoff, or verified state change;
- aftermath, proof limit, privacy, and permission.

Treat a personal hinge as `exact`, `accumulated`, or `missing`. Real life does
not owe the artifact a cinematic epiphany. When change accumulated, use the
first observable counterchoice rather than inventing a revelation.

### 4. Acquire adaptively

Retrieve public or runtime evidence directly when possible. When the missing
source lives only with Farrice, ask one non-leading question at a time. Continue
only while each answer materially raises the source ceiling and Farrice wants
to continue. Do not run a questionnaire or steer toward a preferred thesis.

### 5. Choose the smallest honest disposition

- `use`: the source supports the proposed unit;
- `label`: the unit is opinion, hypothesis, scenario, fiction, or uncertainty;
- `narrow`: a smaller claim is supported;
- `omit`: the unit is unnecessary and unsupported;
- `acquire`: the unit is worth additional retrieval or interview;
- `block`: a hard veto prevents the unit from shipping in its current form.

The disposition applies to the affected unit, not automatically to the whole
artifact.

Do not store a full interview transcript by default.

## Domain Adaptation

- Personal content: protect lived provenance while allowing reflection,
  placeholders, accumulated change, and unresolved truth.
- Offer copy: distinguish mechanism, testable hypothesis, buyer observation,
  demonstrated outcome, and permissioned case study.
- Strategy: ground the chosen path in real constraints and tradeoffs without
  blocking option generation.
- Research: distinguish source-backed conclusion, confidence update, and open
  question.
- System work: distinguish structural evidence, runtime observation, and
  behavior-delta proof.

## Composition Boundary

- The domain workflow owns the artifact.
- Reality Before Rhetoric may recover or calibrate one source-dependent unit.
- High-Taste craft may improve expression but may not invent facts, causal
  bridges, outcomes, private authority, or permission.
- Dhar may design structure and transformation craft around supported material,
  honest placeholders, or a smaller claim.
- Farrice owns lived truth, felt meaning, privacy, permission, and final taste.

No downstream craft pass may overrule a hard claim/material veto. No advisory
may suppress supported adjacent work.

## Promotion Boundary

This practice remains manual and optional. Three independent production
receipts, a blind comparison with the post-extraction workflow, and no material
creative-range loss are required before any automatic advisory is proposed.
Enforcement may never be inferred from a keyword or route name.

The frozen V1 activation and artifact-tier corpus remains historical evidence.
It is not the active behavior contract.

## Validation

Run:

```bash
python3 execution/verify_reality_before_rhetoric.py
```

Structural validation proves restoration and preserved boundaries. It does not
prove higher engagement, sales, audience transformation, or future model
behavior. Those require real production receipts.
