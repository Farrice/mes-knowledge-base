# Layout & Composition Intelligence Contract

Status: **SHADOW companion**  
Craft owner: **Satori Graphics**  
Human taste owner: **Farrice**

## Purpose

Stop taste-bearing design work from jumping directly from a vibe or content list into styling, prompting, generation, or code. When composition is material, produce a small decision packet that makes the hierarchy and viewing experience executable.

This companion advises existing design routes. It is not a new router, universal blocker, scoring theater, or permission to replace the native craft owner.

## Activate When

Activate when the task contains a meaningful arrangement problem: several elements competing for attention, a high-taste layout request, an AI-generated design, a web/page/slide/poster/social composition, or one identity adapting across formats.

## Skip When

Skip for logo-only work, palette-only work, pure typography selection, a non-layout image, or an exact mechanical/pixel edit whose hierarchy is locked. A short `Composition: SKIPPED — [reason]` is enough when the surrounding workflow expects a receipt.

## Composition Intelligence Packet

Keep this compact and executable:

1. **Leverage point** — the one element that must win and the mechanism: scale, contrast, position, or isolation.
2. **Eye path** — first, second, and final stop; add at most two subordinate micro-routes and say how they rejoin.
3. **Grid commitment** — grid family, what it organizes, and one deliberate break or `none`.
4. **Movement and time** — primary movement level (1–6); for time-bearing work map `Punch → Slow → Pull → Release`.
5. **Friction budget** — the purposeful pause, the meaning it serves, and the flow/release zone after it.
6. **Transfer adaptations** — how leverage, path, and friction change across the required formats; this is adaptation, not cropping.
7. **Generation/build constraints** — three `must preserve` and three `must avoid` instructions.

## Source-Faithful LIFT

- **L — Leverage Point:** one dominant element.
- **I — Internal Rhythm:** trusted spacing, alignment, and contrast; eye choreography is the mechanism.
- **F — Friction & Flow:** purpose-led resistance plus readable release.
- **T — Transferability:** identity and hierarchy survive size, background, and format changes.

Use LIFT as a design sequence before production and an anchored audit after production. Do not treat a high score as proof of taste.
