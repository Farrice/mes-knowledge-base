# Signal Fidelity Minimal Contract

Status: `SHADOW / BOUNDED`. This is a silent loss detector, not a writing
format, workflow, command, router, skill, agent, hook, score, or universal
receipt.

## Purpose

Preserve a few consequential facts when work crosses into AI execution without
making human communication or public work feel machine-shaped.

The governing rule is simple:

> If the native owner already carries the meaning, Signal Fidelity says
> nothing.

## Eligible Surfaces

### AI-agent execution handoff

Use an explicit capsule only when an AI agent could otherwise lose a material
execution boundary. The capsule contains exactly:

1. `Goal`
2. `Must survive`
3. `Constraints`
4. `Proof state`
5. `Owner`
6. `Stop when`

This is the only routine visible Signal Fidelity surface.

### Consequential strategy compression

When a strategy is being compressed and one consequential boundary would
otherwise disappear, an owner may use exactly three lines:

```text
Decision: ...
Must not lose: ...
Proof boundary: ...
```

This is optional and on demand. It is not a required footer or default memo
shape.

### Human collaborator

Give the collaborator the natural handoff. Do not show a Signal Fidelity
capsule, receipt, score, or explanation. If the native handoff is unclear,
repair the handoff in the human owner's voice.

### Public reader

Give the reader the native content unchanged. Signal Fidelity has no public
composition authority and may not add labels, caveats, audit notes, or receipt
language merely to prove a check ran. Truth and claim-safety remain with their
existing owners.

## What Counts As Material Loss

Intervene only when at least one of these would change execution or the
truth-status of the result:

- a limit is detached from the promise it bounds;
- a prepared or assumed claim is upgraded into proof;
- a must-survive instruction or approval boundary disappears;
- an AI agent cannot identify the human owner or stop condition.

Different wording, tone, metaphor, compression, deliberate reframing, and
owner-approved evolution are not loss by themselves.

## Silence Rule

No material loss means no Signal Fidelity output. The layer may not generate a
question, block safe work, require a form, rewrite an artifact, or announce
that it ran.

## Ownership

- Skill System owns recipient-appropriate handoff design.
- Agentic Engineering owns the explicit AI execution capsule and recovery
  boundary.
- Strategy, content, proof, voice, and human collaboration retain their native
  owners.
- Farrice retains promotion, enforcement, and taste authority.

## Source And Evidence Boundary

The source insight comes from Lena Hall's talk, *The Signal Layer: What to Build
When Anything Can Be Built*:
`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1KOdiGgMtpY`. The compact source boundary is
stored at
`extractions/video-context/1KOdiGgMtpY/final-minimal/source-insight.md`.

Local tests prove only that the four recipient modes obey this contract. They
do not prove trust, audience response, adoption, creative quality, or
commercial effect.

## Status Decision

The broad prototype on `codex/signal-fidelity-shadow` is parked and remains
unmerged. Its receipts are retained as negative-control evidence. Only this
minimal existing-owner extension is a candidate for integration.

## Removal

Remove the contract and its two owner pointers if the AI capsule is not useful
in real handoffs, the strategy spine becomes routine ceremony, or either owner
starts exposing framework language to human collaborators or public readers.
