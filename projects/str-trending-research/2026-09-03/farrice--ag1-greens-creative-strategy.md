## Key Findings

AG1 runs one of the highest-volume paid creative engines in D2C nutrition and has repeated the same three claims through every format change. Challengers now attack the gaps in that argument (vitamin D, probiotic viability, price per serving) and a February 2026 class action targets the subscription funnel itself. The buyer's live fear is retention (will I still drink this in month three), which the ads do not argue for.
