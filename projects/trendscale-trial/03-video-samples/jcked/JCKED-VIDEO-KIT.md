## 6. Assembly Runbook

**Edit order per variant:** Hook → B1 (the lock) → B2 (proof) → B3 (the right key) → B4 (close). Cut on VO sentence boundaries, not a fixed frame count. Match frame rate (24-30fps) across all clips before cutting — mixed rates stutter at the join.

**Captions:** burned-in, Inter Tight (fallback Söhne), bottom third, max 6 words/frame, manual placement, no auto-caption tool. Amber reserved for "CPT-1" and dose/date numbers (4,000mg, 500mg, 365-day) only; all other text in steel/white.

**Music:** sparse low pulse at −18dB under body beats, silence on the final CTA line, "Go open it."

**Export:** 1080×1920, H.264, consistent 24-30fps, stereo audio −1dB peak ceiling.

**60-second pre-send QA checklist:**
1. Product fidelity — bottle label/cap match the PDP exactly, no invented text.
2. No age markers leaked into any frame or caption.
3. No banned phrases in VO or captions — check the antislop list; no "unlock/harness/elevate."
4. VO is verbatim against `MASTER-COPY.md`, word for word.
5. Captions match the on-screen text column in Section 2.
6. No reflection shots slipped through (mirrors, glass, screens).
7. No before/after, scale, doctor-in-coat, or urgency-stinger visuals.
8. "Top of the studied range," never "the clinical dose."
9. No em-dashes in any caption or on-screen text.
10. Music drops to silence exactly on the final CTA line.
