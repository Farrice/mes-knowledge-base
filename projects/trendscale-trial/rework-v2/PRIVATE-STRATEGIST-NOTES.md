## 2. A/B test-cell design (the logic behind the "alternate hooks")
- Each brief = 3 hooks × 1 shared body = a hook test. You learn which psychological entry wins before spending on the wrong angle.
- JCKED levers: contrarian/blame-removal, pain/self-recognition, mechanism-curiosity. Puravita levers: curiosity/analogy, authority/contrarian-proof, partner-POV. No two share a lever, so the test isolates the entry, not just the wording.
- CTA is its own variable: harder direct-response close is the default cell; the softer "decide for yourself" close is the A/B. Bet: this skeptical buyer may convert better on restraint, but you prove it rather than assume it.
- Kill/scale logic to bring to a call (from the original decision rules): kill a hook below ~20% hook-rate after ~$200; scale at >30% hook-rate and >1.2% CTR; iterate the middle in the next round.

## 3. Sourcing & accuracy decisions (so you can defend any line)
- **Dose comparison kept, framed honestly.** "2,000 to 4,000mg" matches the meta-analysis band the original cited (Talenezhad 2020, 37 RCTs / 2,292 people); 4,000mg = top of the studied range; "most products stop at 500" is the market contrast the client already saw. Avoided the absolute "the clinical dose" phrasing.
- **Age angle tightened.** The original's "carnitine declines with age" is weakly sourced (Chee/Aging Cell 2021 doesn't establish decline). Replaced with the verified finding from the same trial: it still moved fat oxidation ~20% in older adults ("held up in men well into their sixties"). Same "why now" feeling, defensible source.
- **"12 forms" exclusivity dropped.** Original said "the only formula with all twelve." Stated as a product fact instead, no "only" claim, unless the brand can substantiate exclusivity.
- **"80% magnesium loss since 1900" dropped.** Hard to source. The defensible version (if ever needed) is USDA on grain refining: milling whole wheat to white flour removes ~80% of its magnesium.
- **Magnesium prevalence:** using "~48% below the estimated average requirement" (NHANES/NIH ODS). The original said "over half consume less than the RDA," which is looser; ~48% is the defensible figure.
- **Authority beat (Huberman/Attia):** both are accurate public statements, neither framed as endorsing Puravita. Name-free fallback is in the brief Notes for a more conservative cut.
- Science spine verified to primary sources: CPT-1 rate-limiting (Frontiers in Pharmacology 2023), serum magnesium <1% of body stores (NIH ODS), 600+ Mg-dependent reactions (de Baaij 2015).

## 5. Founder-call talking points (this is where the hire is won)
A tough performance founder reading this will want proof you optimize for his ROAS, not your taste. Get ahead of it:
- **Kill criteria are in the briefs now.** Each hypothesis states what would prove the angle wrong ("if the lock-and-key hook does not clear the willpower control on hook rate within ~$2K, we cut it"). Say out loud: "I run tests, I do not decorate hypotheses."
- **The ugly control is in, on purpose.** Hook 4 in each brief is the loud, dumb, pattern-interrupt version, running against the three sophisticated hooks. The line that lands the job: "I built the smart version and the loud version, and I am completely fine if the loud one wins. I ship what the data picks." That answers the one doubt a performance founder has about a 'taste' hire.
- **Risk reversal is the biggest CVR lever left.** This buyer has stopped trusting the category. The mechanism removes intellectual risk; a guarantee or free-shipping line at the CTA removes financial risk. Flag that you would A/B the risk-reversal itself, and ask what the brand can offer.
- If asked "why so cerebral / will this stop the scroll on cold," answer with the control cell: that is exactly why Hook 4 exists, and the test settles it.
