# Instagram Content Strategy: "The Fasting Executive"

**Framework:** Grace Andrews Media Company Architecture
**Goal:** Build a "Trust Pathway" via Instagram to funnel high-ticket leads and sell the $7 Fasting Pre-Flight Checklist.
**Primary Culture Mode:** Oral Culture (Reels/Stories) + Short Written Culture (Carousels)

---

## 1. The City Map (Instagram Architecture)

### **Grand Central Station (The Core Narrative)**
*“You don’t need more time to get in shape; you need metabolic efficiency.”*
Positioning Ken as the high-achiever’s guide to effortless fat loss and limitless energy. 

### **The Trust Pathway**
1. **Attention (Reels):** Vicious hooks breaking industry myths about dieting and time management.
2. **Connection (Stories):** Behind-the-scenes of Ken’s life. Showing the "Messy Middle"—drinking black coffee, working hard, eating massive but healthy meals during the eating window.
3. **Trust (Carousels):** Deep-dive, visually appealing step-by-step guides (Written culture adapted for IG). Case studies.
4. **Conversion (Bio Link & DM Automations):** "Comment FAST to get my $7 Pre-Flight Checklist."

---

## 2. Content Pillars & Cadence (Consistency × Experimentation)

**Posting Cadence:** 4x Reels/Week, 1x Carousel/Week, Daily Stories.

### **Pillar 1: The "Myth-Buster" (Attention) - Reels**
*Hook-driven short videos designed for Top-of-Funnel reach.*
- **Concept A:** "Why eating 6 small meals a day is making you fat and tired."
- **Concept B:** "The 'healthy' breakfast habit that destroys your executive focus by 11 AM."
- **Execution:** Fast cuts, B-roll of Ken in a professional/gym setting, on-screen text matching oral delivery.

### **Pillar 2: The "Fasting Blueprint" (Trust) - Carousels**
*High-dwell-time educational sliders.*
- **Concept A:** "What biologically happens when you don't eat for 16 hours." (Slide-by-slide timeline)
- **Concept B:** "The 3 things you CAN consume during a fast without breaking it."
- **Execution:** High-contrast designs, bold typography, simple diagrams. CTA on the last slide to the $7 guide.

### **Pillar 3: The "Lifestyle Proof" (Connection) - Stories & Reels**
*Showing that the system works in the real world.*
- **Concept A:** "How I break a 16-hour fast at a business lunch." (Vlog style)
- **Concept B:** Showcasing a massive dinner (Burger/Steak) highlighting that restriction isn't necessary if the window is right.
- **Execution:** Raw, unpolished, iPhone aesthetic. Authenticity > High Production.

---

## 3. The 30-Day Launch Sprint

**Week 1-2: Awareness & Proof Stacking**
- Deploy 8 Reels attacking common dieting pain points for busy professionals.
- **Proof Ladder:** Share 2 client transformations (Before/After with an emphasis on *how much time they saved*).
- **CTA:** "Follow to learn the 16:8 Executive Protocol."

**Week 3: The $7 Output Launch**
- Transition CTA to the Digital Product.
- Run the "DM to Auto-Reply" play. 
- Example Reel: *“I get asked every day how to start fasting without getting headaches or giving up coffee. I built a $7 Pre-Flight checklist that acts as your exact roadmap. Comment 'START' and I’ll DM it to you.”*

**Week 4: The Upsell Bridge ($47-$97)**
- Once the $7 product purchasers are in the email sequence, Instagram stories transition to highlighting the "next step" (e.g., The 30-Day Custom Protocol).
- Use IG Stories to share screenshots of DMs from people who bought the $7 guide and loved it.

---

## 4. Quality Gate Checklist (Pre-Publishing)
Before any piece of content goes live, it must pass this gate:
1. **The 'Joe Rogan' Test:** Does this sound like a human talking, or a corporate brochure?
2. **The Vicious Hook Check:** Does the first 3 seconds promise a specific payoff or challenge a specific belief?
3. **The Proof Braid:** Are we demonstrating expertise (visuals of fitness/success) rather than just stating it?
