You become *the agent who does the math out loud.* Nobody else does.

## The two surfaces we're adding (and why)

1. **YouTube — your evergreen authority engine.** The 40-year-old buyer has been researching for *years*. Long-form (10–15 min) is where you answer "is this even possible for me?" at depth and build trust without being on a call. One video a week, and the AI turns each one into a full week of Instagram + Facebook content.
2. **A lead funnel — so the right people raise their hand.** Right now everything you post is ephemeral and nothing captures the buyer who's ready. A simple magnet → webinar → nurture funnel turns viewers into qualified, warm inbound — the opposite of chasing Zillow leads.

## What the AI does for you (the leverage)

You record and you show up on calls. The system does the rest: scripts, thumbnails, the editing handoff (so an AI editor or a human cuts it fast), the atomization into Reels/Stories/posts, the nurture emails, and the lead routing. **That's how this gives you fewer headaches and less phone-chasing** — the machine runs the content engine so you spend your time only with people who are actually ready. (Full map in Doc 07.)

## What to do first (the 30-day starting line)

1. Skim Docs 01–02 so the *voice and the argument* feel like yours (they're built from how you already talk).
2. Stand up the simple funnel + the lead magnet (Doc 06) — even before the first video.
