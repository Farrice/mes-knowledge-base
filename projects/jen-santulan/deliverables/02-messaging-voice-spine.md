# Jen Santulan — Messaging & Voice Spine
*The one language system. Every YouTube video, reel, email, and webinar runs through this so it all sounds like one Jen, making one argument.*

**The one argument, in one line:** *the down payment isn't the wall — income and access are. and you're closer than you've been told.*

Everything below serves that sentence. If a piece of content doesn't reinforce it, it doesn't ship.

---

## 1. Voice DNA — The Rules

**Rule 1 — lowercase, best-friend register.** writes like she's texting her sister at 11pm, not addressing a room.
- ✅ "i've had buyers wait years because they thought they needed perfect credit."
- ❌ "Many of my clients have delayed their purchase due to misconceptions about credit requirements."

**Rule 2 — story first, listing second.** the hook is a person, a feeling, or a moment. the property is the proof, never the opener. (top post: 1,169 likes, Coachella with a toddler. listing posts: 20–51.)
- ✅ "she cried in the kitchen. not because it was perfect — because it was hers."
- ❌ "JUST LISTED: 3BD/2BA in Reseda, $679K, motivated seller."

**Rule 3 — every adjective pays rent in a number, name, or detail.** show, don't tell, at the sentence level.
- ✅ "quartz counters, the pendant lights you'd save to a moodboard, and a backyard with the kids' bikes already in it."
- ❌ "stunning, move-in-ready dream home."

**Rule 4 — emoji at the emotional beat, 1–2 max.** placed where the feeling is. 🔑 💫 🥹 at the win. never 🔥🔥🚨💯.
- ✅ "and just like that, they got the keys 🔑"
- ❌ "NEW LISTING 🔥🔥🔥 DON'T MISS 🚨🚨💯"

**Rule 5 — permission, not pressure.** "you can" beats "you should," always. when scarcity is fake, say "hidden gem," not "act now."
- ✅ "you're not disqualified. let me show you the actual math."
- ❌ "act now — this won't last!"

**Rule 6 — celebrate the win as shared joy.** client wins are her wins. self-deprecating about herself, generous about them.
- ✅ "real estate hallelujah 🥹 these two waited three years and i'm not crying you're crying."
- ❌ "Another successful closing. Proud to deliver results for my clients."

**Rule 7 — show the math out loud, like a transparent friend.** never hide behind "great deal." do the arithmetic on screen.
- ✅ "your $3,000 rent vs roughly $3,758 all-in on an entry condo. yes it's more right now — here's what the extra $750 is actually buying you."
- ❌ "buying is cheaper than renting, trust me!"

**Rule 8 — close with "let's," never "DM me."** the invite is a doorway, not a conversion ask. the relationship is the CTA.
- ✅ "want me to run your real numbers? let's do it — no pressure, just clarity."
- ❌ "DM me 'HOME' to get started!! link in bio!!"

## 2. The Belief-Dissolution Map (McRaney)

You don't argue them out of a belief — arguing hardens it. Reframe so the old belief no longer fits, then show a real person like them who walked through (backward dissolution).

**Belief 1 — "i need 20% down and perfect credit, or i'm not ready."**
- *Don't say* "that's a myth!" (calls them dumb).
- *Reframe:* "the 20% rule was true for your parents. it stopped being the rule and nobody sent the memo." then move the wall: "the down payment isn't the thing standing between you and the keys. income and access are — and those are solvable in ways 20%-in-cash never was."
- *Proof:* about 1 in 5 first-time buyers (22%) used a gift or loan from family/friends (NAR 2025). MyHome can cover up to 3.5% of the down payment. **almost nobody is putting 20% down.**

**Belief 2 — "i waited too long. i missed it. i'm 40 and renting."**
- *Don't say* "it's never too late!" (the poster they distrust).
- *Reframe:* "40 isn't late. 40 is the new normal — you're not behind, the whole map redrew." the shame is comparing to a 1981 timeline that no longer exists.
- *Proof:* in 1981 the typical first-time buyer was 29. today it's 40 — a record high (NAR 2025 Profile). **"you didn't fall behind. the average buyer is exactly where you are."**

**Belief 3 — "buying makes no sense vs renting. the math doesn't work."**
- *Don't say* "buying is cheaper!" (they ran the numbers; lying loses them).
- *Reframe:* tell the truth no agent tells them. "you're right — buying costs more per month right now. anyone who says otherwise is selling something." then reframe the premium: "you're not paying more for the same thing. you're paying to stop rent from rising on you forever and to start owning the asset instead of renting it."
- *Proof:* 2BR rent ~$3,000/mo; entry condo ~$3,758 all-in. ~$750 more — and that $750 locks your housing cost and builds equity. **honesty IS the proof. she's the only agent who admits the number, so she's the one they believe.**

**Belief 4 — "the programs aren't for someone like me. those lotteries never hit."**
- *Don't* oversell the programs (they've been burned by "assistance!" videos that ignored the income wall).
- *Reframe:* "you're half right. one program — Dream For All — really is a lottery, and that window's closed right now. but it was never the only door, and the agents who hyped it skipped the part that matters: assistance gets you past the cash wall. your income gets you the home. that's the part you can plan around."
- *Proof:* the real menu, honest — MyHome (up to 3.5% down help, open now), LIPA (up to $161K, City of LA only), Dream For All (first-gen, watch for the next round). **naming what's open AND closed is what makes the open doors believable.**

## 4. The FTHB Permission Mechanic v2 (refreshed for the 40-yo)

**NAME** the specific midnight pain (the exact 1am thought) → **FLIP** with one real proof/number/program (never bare encouragement) → **INVITE** softly (a doorway, never a hard "DM me").

**Example 1 — the "i missed it" buyer**
> "you're 40, you've rented for a decade, and somewhere you decided you missed your shot.
> here's the part your parents' timeline missed: in 1981 the average first-time buyer was 29. today it's 40. you didn't fall behind — the whole timeline moved, and you're standing right where everyone else is.
> want me to show you what 40 can actually afford in the Valley right now? let's run it 🥹"

**Example 2 — the "20% down" buyer**
> "you've got decent income and you're still waiting, because the number in your head is 20% down. for a $600K condo that's $120K in cash, and who has that.
> the part your parents' rule never accounted for: almost nobody puts 20% down anymore. about 1 in 5 first-time buyers used a gift or loan from family, and MyHome can cover up to 3.5% of your down payment. the cash wall is the solvable part.
> let's figure out your real number — no pressure, just clarity 🔑"

**Example 3 — the "math doesn't work" buyer**
> "you ran the numbers yourself: $3,000 rent vs almost $3,758 to own. so you stayed put. smart, honestly.
> i'm not going to tell you buying is cheaper — it isn't right now, and any agent who says otherwise is selling you something. but that extra ~$750 is doing one thing your rent never will: it stops going up on you, and it builds something that's yours.
> if you want, i'll build the honest side-by-side for your actual income. let's do it."
