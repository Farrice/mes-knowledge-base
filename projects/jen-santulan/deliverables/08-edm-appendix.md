# EDM / Festival Expansion Appendix
### a warm network you already belong to — seed it now, build it later

*Companion to the Repositioning Brief. Secondary lane, not the main engine. Read after the FTHB foundation is producing leads.*

---

## 1. The reframe: you're not breaking in, you're already in

every other realtor who wants the festival crowd has to cold-pitch their way into a room that doesn't trust them. you walk in and someone already saved you a spot. that's the whole thing. you're team mybpm. you've been to edc, camp edc, coachella-with-a-toddler (1,169 likes, the most-loved post on your feed by a mile). in your own words: *"music has always been something farrice and i both loved… we were going to shows and music festivals long before we even met."* you don't have to manufacture belonging. you have it.

so the question is never "how do i enter this niche." it's "how do i quietly become the realtor the people i already party with think of first." 🔑

the reason this is a gift and not a gimmick is simple: it's a community that travels together, spends on what it loves, and trusts its own over any outsider. the warmth that already does your relationship work in your DMs does it here too, except the trust is pre-installed. better still, a lot of these people *are* your primary buyer wearing different shoes. mid-30s, established, dual-income, renting somewhere in the valley, financially serious six days a week and free on the seventh. the festival is the day off; the spreadsheet runs the rest of the week. you're one of the few people who genuinely sees both.

---

## 2. positioning seed for the EDM lane

the say-out-loud sentence for this lane (a sibling to your main one, not a replacement):

> *"i'm the realtor who actually gets how festival people earn and live, the 1099 years, the irregular-but-real income, the asset-light life, because i'm one of you. let's see what owning a piece of the valley actually looks like for someone like us."*

what makes it credible is that you're not cosplaying the lifestyle to win the lead. you'd be at edc whether or not a single attendee ever bought a home. that's the difference between you and the agent who slaps a festival hashtag on a listing post. the lane is honest home math for people whose income and life don't fit the bank's default form. same wedge as your main engine, the down payment isn't the wall, income and access are, just aimed at someone whose income is real but reads "messy" to a lender who's only ever seen W-2s.

---

## 3. first moves — 5 low-key actions, zero salesy energy

1. **show up as you, document nothing-special.** keep posting the festival moments you'd post anyway. no listing tag, no pitch. you're just being a person in your community, and that's the asset working. (your numbers already prove this; the lifestyle posts beat the listings ~10x.)

2. **drop one soft "festival people buy homes too" piece, once.** a single quiet story, not a campaign. something like the honest version of: *you can love edc every may and still close on a place in reseda. i did the math for someone in exactly that life last month and it surprised both of us.* story first, no math dump, no CTA harder than "let's talk if that's you."

3. **find your lender before you need them.** the one real homework item. build a relationship with a loan officer who actually knows non-W-2 / 1099 / irregular-cashflow qualifying (bank statement loans, two-year averaging, the real paperwork). when a festival friend finally asks "could i even qualify," you want a name ready, not a shrug. ask marty's team who they trust for self-employed buyers.

4. **be useful in the group chats you're already in.** when the mybpm / camp threads drift toward "rent went up again," don't sell, just be the friend who knows things. one honest sentence about what's actually happening in valley condo inventory (it's up ~31% YoY, verify same-day before you say a number) lands harder than any ad.

5. **collect proof quietly.** the first festival-adjacent client who closes is your whole future content library for this lane. celebrate them like you celebrate everyone, shared joy, their win, their keys 🔑, and that one real story does more recruiting than a year of posting.

---

## 4. first targets — who's already in your orbit

start where the warmth is highest and the income is most "normal," and only climb the ladder later:

- **camp members + the mybpm fam.** your inner ring. mid-30s, established, many renting in the valley right now. this is your primary ICP in festival fits, so start here.
- **vendors + event staff.** the people who *work* the scene: production, ops, hospitality. steady-ish income, deeply networked, undersold to. natural fit for the honest-math conversation.
- **mid-tier DJs + working artists.** real 1099 income, no W-2, often told by lenders they're "complicated" when they're just unfamiliar. you plus the right lender become the people who finally make it make sense for them.

then, the **aspirational ladder**, and this is a *later* step, not step one. above the working tier sit the festival execs, label folks, the pasquale-rotella / insomniac orbit. that's a real ceiling worth knowing exists, but you earn your way up to it on the backs of the working-tier wins, not by reaching for the top first. the camp member who closes introduces you to three more. that's how you climb: referral by referral, not cold pitch to the top.

---

## 5. develop-later note

this is a seed, on purpose. the core FTHB engine (youtube, the lead magnet, the "real programs, real math" series) comes first and proves out before this lane gets real build hours. don't split your focus while the main engine is still spinning up. the moves above cost you almost nothing (you're already at the festivals, already posting, the only real lift is the lender relationship), so plant them now and let them compound quietly in the background. when the core engine is producing leads and you've got room, we come back and build this out properly: a dedicated content angle, a festival-income lead path, maybe a "buying a home on irregular income" long-form. for now, just keep being you in a community that already loves you, and let one real story tell you when it's time. let's plant it and let it grow. ✨
