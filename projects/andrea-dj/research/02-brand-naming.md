# 02 — Brand Naming Guide
## 15 Name Options for Your Event Brand

---

**What this is:** 15 researched name options for your event brand, tested against two critical phrases: *"We met at ___"* and *"Are you going to ___ this Friday?"*

**What to do with it:** Pick your top 3. Sit with them for a few days. Say them out loud. Check if the Instagram handle is available. Then commit.

---

## Should You Use Your Name in the Brand?

**Recommendation: Use a standalone brand name, with you as the visible founder.**

Here's why:
- It scales — if you ever expand to multiple cities or other DJs, "Andrea's Nights" limits you
- It feels like a movement, not a party series
- Daybreaker's founder Radha Agrawal is well-known, but the brand is Daybreaker — not "Radha's Mornings"
- You stay visible as "Andrea, founder of [Brand]" on all marketing

## Top 3 Recommendations

### 1. PULSE

- **Why:** Best balance of dance energy, connection metaphor, and shareability. Your heartbeat. Two hearts synchronizing. The pulse of the music. The pulse of the room.
- **"We met at Pulse"** — feels like a genuine love story moment
- **"Are you going to Pulse this Friday?"** — mysterious, intriguing
- **Instagram:** @pulseevent, @pulsenights
- **Spiritual alignment:** Life force, presence, connection at the core

### 2. WILDHEARTS

- **Why:** Most culturally resonant. Feels like a movement, not just an event. Warm, romantic without being cheesy, speaks to authenticity and uninhibited joy.
- **"We met at Wildhearts"** — warm, memorable, romantic
- **"Are you going to Wildhearts?"** — high frequency, fun to say
- **Instagram:** @wildheartsco, @wildheartsevents
- **Spiritual alignment:** Authenticity, untamed spirit, heart-centered living

### 3. SPARK

- **Why:** One syllable. Immediate. Speaks to attraction and ignition. Daybreaker-level simplicity. Works effortlessly in conversation.
- **"We met at Spark"** — efficient, romantic, clear
- **"Are you going to Spark?"** — extremely natural
- **Instagram:** @sparkevent, @spark_nights
- **Spiritual alignment:** Life force, creation, divine timing

## How to Decide

**Step 1:** Say each of your top 3 out loud in these sentences:
- "We met at ___"
- "Are you going to ___ this Friday?"
- "Have you heard of ___? It's this event where..."
- "Welcome to ___" (what you'd say on stage)

**Step 2:** Check Instagram handle availability for your top 3

**Step 3:** Ask 5 friends which one they'd be most curious about if they saw it on Instagram

**Step 4:** Pick one. Don't overthink it. The name matters less than the experience you create under it.

---

## Sub-Branding (Once You Pick a Name)

Individual events can have themes under the main brand:

- **"Pulse: First Light"** (the launch event)
- **"Pulse: Full Moon"** (monthly themed night)
- **"Pulse: Intimate"** (small premium events, 20-30 people)
- **"Pulse at [Venue Name]"** (location-specific)

This gives you flexibility without diluting the main brand.
