## 1. What Resonance is (read this first)

> **Resonance is a daytime, sober, curated dance party in Chicago for people who want to meet a partner.** Heart encounters, not head encounters. The music does the emotional labor so the people don't have to. We count the couples, not the followers.

Three words run everything. If a design ever drifts from one of them, it's wrong:

- **Daytime** — Saturday afternoon, real sunlight. Never night, never club.
- **Sober** — no bar, no drinks in frame, ever. But we never *preach* sobriety either.
- **Curated** — every guest applied and was hand-picked. It's a room with a door, not an open party.

**The one visual law:** *If a photo could have been taken at 11pm in a club, it fails.* Daytime, real room, real bodies — or it doesn't ship.

## 3. The fonts

| Role | Ideal (licensed) | Free substitute (use these) |
|---|---|---|
| **Display / headlines** | GT Sectra (A) · Tiempos/Canela (B) · Domaine (C) | **IBM Plex Serif** (A) · **Fraunces** (B) · **Cormorant Garamond** (C) — all free on Google Fonts |
| **Body / labels** | — | **Inter** (free, Google Fonts) — every variant |
| **Signature only** | — | **Caveat** (A) / **Homemade Apple** (B, C) — used ONLY for Andrea's handwritten signature, never body |

**Rules that never bend:**
- Serif carries headlines. Sans (Inter) carries body. **Never stack two serifs.**
- **Never use a weight above 500.** Size does the work, not bold. (Variant C goes even lighter — 400.)
- Hand-script appears **max once** per design, and only as a signature.

## 4. Photos — the discipline

The photograph is the protagonist. Type is the caption to it. Get the photo right and the design is 80% done.

**DO:**
- Real daytime light — sun on a wood floor, big windows, overcast Chicago afternoon (flat grey is fine, it's real)
- Real adults ~30–38, mixed race / body / gesture, casual-elevated (linen, sundresses, dark jeans, clean sneakers)
- Candid moments — a body mid-laugh, two people leaning in, a hand on a record, shoes by the wall
- Leave the photo alone: **filter = None, all sliders at 0.** It was already color-corrected.

**KILL on sight:**
- Anything that could be 11pm / a club / a bar
- Flash, club lighting, neon, strobe, candlelight, black-and-white
- Drinks, wine glasses, bottles anywhere
- Phones in hands
- AI-generated faces or stock "dancers"
- Canva auto-filters (Vintage, Cinematic, Drama), Color Pop, duotone, drop shadows, "photo to cartoon"
- "Smile at the camera" group portraits; everyone facing the DJ in a lineup

If you don't have a real daytime photo yet, use a clean tonal block in the cream/terracotta family as a placeholder rather than a wrong photo. A placeholder is honest; a night-coded stock photo is brand damage.

## 5. Canva setup — two ways (do both)

### Path 1 — Self-contained (start today, any Canva tier)
You can build everything from the hexes and font names above. Quick setup:

1. **Brand Kit → Colors:** add the 10 hex codes from the chosen variant (Section 2), named exactly as listed. Delete any Canva-suggested pinks/purples/gradients.
2. **Fonts:** if you're on Canva Pro, upload the display font for the variant (IBM Plex Serif / Fraunces / Cormorant Garamond) + Inter + the signature font. On free Canva, all four are available in the font picker — just search them.
3. **Photo treatment:** when you drop in a photo → Filter: **None** → Adjust: **all sliders 0** → Effects: **none**. (Brightness +5 only if the import looks dark on screen.)
4. **Turn off Magic Write for copy.** Captions and headlines come from Andrea / the briefs, not Canva AI. (Canva AI is fine for background-remove and resizing only.)

### Path 2 — Full fidelity (Andrea adds you to the live Brand Kit)
For exact fonts and locked templates, Andrea can add you as a **collaborator on the Resonance Canva Pro Brand Kit** (Canva → Brand Hub → Share). That gives you the uploaded GT-Sectra-tier fonts and the 5 saved templates so you never start from a blank canvas. **Ask Andrea to send the Brand Kit collaborator invite to your Canva email.** Until then, Path 1 has you fully covered.

The 5 reusable templates to build/inherit are spec'd in `03-evergreen-templates.md`.

## 6. Quick Do / Don't card

**DO**
- Let the photo own 65–75% of the surface
- One accent color per layout (the terracotta, or whatever the variant's accent is)
- Name the city and date explicitly: *"Chicago. July 18, 2026."*
- Point the CTA to the **application**, not to "buy tickets"
- Leave generous white space — depth comes from cream tones, never drop shadows

**DON'T**
- No QR code as a focal element (tiny, bottom corner, under 0.5" — or skip it)
- No hashtags on the flyer (those live in the IG first comment)
- No sponsor logos, ever
- No "limited spots, going fast!" urgency
- No more than 2 em-dashes in any caption
- No emoji-stuffed CTAs ("DM us YES 🌹", "tag a friend")

## 7. Before you send anything back — the 5-second check

Read the asset, then ask:

1. **Daytime?** Could this be 11pm? If maybe → kill the photo.
2. **Would Andrea say this to a friend over coffee?** If it sounds like marketing → rewrite.
3. **One accent, real photo, city + date present?**
4. **No banned phrases?** (manifest, sacred container, high-vibe, conscious singles, "intentional" as filler, "find your person," tribe, sober-curious *community*)
5. **CTA points to applying, not buying?**

Yes to all → it's on-brand. Send it.
