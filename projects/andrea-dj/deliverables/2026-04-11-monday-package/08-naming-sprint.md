### The One Out-of-Market Risk That Matters

**Resonance (resonancematch.com) — Boston, MA.** This is a live, national singles-event brand. Their tagline: *"Curated, IRL singles events."* Same name, same positioning, same category. They're not in Chicago yet.

- If they have a registered trademark in Class 41 (entertainment/events) or Class 45 (social introduction/dating services), Andrea's brand could be infringing — regardless of geography.
- If they don't, we're in good shape but should move fast before they file.
- **Lawyer task (before the first public post):** USPTO TESS search for "RESONANCE" live marks in Classes 41 + 45, and a specific check on whether resonancematch.com has a pending filing. 30 minutes of a lawyer's time. ~$150–300.

Reference: [resonancematch.com/events](https://www.resonancematch.com/events)

### SEO Pollution Level: **MEDIUM-HIGH**

Google search for "Resonance Chicago" surfaces (in order): Resonance Massage & Bodywork, MCA Chicago's 2024 series, Resonance Arts. A new singles brand won't rank organically for "Resonance Chicago" for months — maybe ever — without dedicated SEO work. **This isn't fatal**: the event will be discovered through IG, word-of-mouth, and the newsletter, not Google. But it means the IG handle and the direct-URL become more important because SEO can't carry load.
