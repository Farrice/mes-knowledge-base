More importantly, it proves the unit economics work, which makes Event #2 venue conversations radically easier ("our last event sold out, here's the revenue, let's talk").

The break-even line

12 tickets at $35 average covers all costs. That's the floor. If Andrea can sell 12 tickets (which is half of her own 20-person invite list), Event #1 does not lose money. That's the psychological baseline for her to hold through the nervous middle weeks.

Andrea's up-front outlay

~$225 before first tickets sell. (Venue deposit if any, insurance, printing, initial supplies.) Every dollar after that comes from ticket revenue.
