### Path B — Arts Studio (Humboldt Park or similar)

**Source**: Andrea mentioned loving the arts-studio energy of the Humboldt Park scene. Needs 2 quoted leads this week.

| Factor | Arts Studio Path |
|---|---|
| **Venue cost** | $500–1,500 for a 4-hour window (daytime weekend rates typically gentler) |
| **Capacity fit** | Most accommodate 50–100. Good room is a feature. |
| **Sound** | Studio may have rig; budget $100–250 rental if not. |
| **Alcohol (optional)** | **Controlled bartender** ($200–400 incl. bartender fee + BYOB or licensed). Slow pour, limited SKUs, intentional presence. Alcohol as curation instrument, not crutch. |

| **Risk** | Availability for June 20, deposit terms, timing restrictions, loading logistics. |
| **Aesthetic** | Designed for events — photographs well. Strong IG content default. |
| **Discoverability** | Higher than house — visible address, taggable location. Good for Phase 2 growth signal. |

**Total cost estimate: $1,200–2,100** depending on bartender choice and rental tier.

### Comparison — Break-Even at 50 Attendees

Using tiered pricing ($25 early-bird / $35 standard / $45 late):

| Scenario | Cost | Revenue at 50 × $35 avg | Net |
|---|---|---|---|
| **House path** | ~$500 | $1,750 | **+$1,250** |
| **Studio (no bar)** | ~$1,300 | $1,750 | **+$450** |
| **Studio (w/ bartender)** | ~$1,800 | $1,750 | **–$50** (tighten) |

**Reality check on studio-with-bartender**: break-even requires either higher ticket avg (~$40) or 55 attendees. Viable, but thinner margin. The bartender is a narrative choice, not a financial one.

**Start with Path A (house).** It's the purest proof-of-concept — highest margin, lowest risk, naturally aligned with the "we don't need alcohol here" posture. If the friend-offer confirms June 20 availability and the space fits 50, lock it by Week 2.

**Run Path B in parallel as backup.** Get 2 studio quotes this week with June 20 availability. If the friend-offer falls through or the space is too small, we have a path ready.

**What determines the pick**: friend-offer confirmation + space scout (Andrea + Farrice walk-through). If both succeed → House. If either fails → Studio.
