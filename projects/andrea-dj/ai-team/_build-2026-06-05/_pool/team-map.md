# Resonance Team Map

*Your six AI projects and what each one is for. When you're not sure, open Planning & Strategy first — it'll point you to the right one.*

---

## The Six Projects

| # | Project | Use it for | Model |
|---|---------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | **Planning & Strategy** | Thinking, planning, deciding what to do next. Start here when you're not sure where to go. | Opus |
| 2 | **Content & Social** | Anything the world reads: IG posts, reels, stories, captions, emails. | Sonnet |
| 3 | **Applications & Vetting** | Who gets in: DMs, applications, accept/decline, sponsor and partner offers. | Opus |
| 4 | **Partners & Outreach** | Working with people: venues, press, DJs, sponsors, outreach messages. | Sonnet |
| 5 | **Music & The Floor** | The music and the room: the set, tempo, the floor, day-of flow. | Sonnet |
| 6 | **Event Memory** | *(Turn on after July 18.)* Couples who met, voice corrections, lessons from each event. | Sonnet |

---

## How to Choose

Ask one question about what's in front of you:

- **Am I deciding who's in the room?** (a DM to read, an application to judge, a sponsor offer to weigh) → **Applications & Vetting**
- **Am I speaking to the world?** (a post, reel, story, caption, email) → **Content & Social**
- **Am I working with someone?** (a venue, a journalist, a DJ, a partner) → **Partners & Outreach**
- **Am I moving the room?** (the set, the tempo, the floor, the DJ brief for JR) → **Music & The Floor**
- **Am I capturing what happened?** (a couple who met, a story, a lesson) → **Event Memory** *(after July 18)*
- **None of the above / I'm thinking or planning** → **Planning & Strategy** — it's also your front door; it can do a little of everything and hand you off when something needs the specialist.

---

## The One Rule

Every project carries the same heart: daytime, sober, curated, the twelve non-negotiables, and your voice. They differ only in the job they're best at. You never have to remember the system — Planning & Strategy does that for you.
