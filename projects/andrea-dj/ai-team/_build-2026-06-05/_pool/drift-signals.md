# 03 — Drift Signals

*Andrea's early-warning system. Read these whenever you're about to make a decision you're unsure about. Source: Andrea's Internal Anchor §7. Verbatim.*

*Heart encounters, not head encounters. Daytime. Sober. Curated.*

---

## The 10 Drift Signals

If any of these are true, **stop**. Re-read the Non-Negotiables and the Brand Bible. Then decide.

1. **I am watching follower count more than the floor.**

2. **The music has gotten safer.** My sets are crowd-pleasing in a way that costs the curation edge.

3. **The room is becoming a "scene"** — people coming to be seen instead of to meet.

4. **Resonance is becoming more about personal growth** than finding a committed partner.

5. **Resonance is becoming a dance party for dance party's sake.**

6. **I am spending more energy on the brand than on the room.**

7. **Sponsorship conversations are taking up more time than the event itself.**

8. **Press coverage is becoming a goal in its own right.**

9. **I am being asked to soften daytime, sober, or curated admission, and I am considering it.**

10. **A venue, a partner, or a promoter is starting to shape the event more than I am.**

---

## The Readback Ritual

Before any uncertain decision — sponsor offer, format change, content tone shift, hire, partnership, schedule pressure — Andrea reads these out loud.

Reading aloud matters. It engages the body that the brand exists to honor. The signals you'd let slide on a screen, you can't let slide when you hear yourself say them.

If even one signal is firing, the decision goes on hold for 24 hours minimum. Sleep on it. Re-read. Then decide.

---

## What To Do When a Signal Fires

For each signal, the operational response:

### Signal 1: Watching Followers More Than the Floor

- Stop the IG metrics check for 7 days.
- Spend the recovered time talking to past attendees and asking what they remember.
- Re-anchor: *the metric is couples formed, not followers gained.*

### Signal 2: Music Has Gotten Safer

- Cut one safe song from the next set; replace with a track only Andrea curates.
- Do a 30-min "what would I play if nobody were judging" set in the studio.
- Re-anchor: *crowd-pleasing is a form of drift when the crowd isn't yet the room.*

### Signal 3: Room Becoming a "Scene"

- Re-tighten the curation filter — invite-only ratio bumps up, public tickets shrink.
- Decline 1-2 high-profile attendees who'd come "to be seen."
- Re-anchor: *meeting matters; being seen does not.*

### Signal 4: Becoming About Growth, Not Partnership

- Audit the last 3 pieces of content. What % is partner-seeking framing vs personal-growth framing?
- Rebalance to ≥70% partner-seeking framing in next 3 pieces.
- Re-anchor: *Resonance is for people who want a partner. If they want growth, there are 1,000 other rooms.*

### Signal 5: Becoming a Dance Party for Dance Party's Sake

- Re-read Andrea's Anchor §1 ("The dancing is the medium. The meeting is the point.").
- Add a curation step: every event includes an introduction structure (not a forced icebreaker, but a reason to talk to one new person before the floor opens).
- Re-anchor: *the floor is the protocol of meeting, not the entertainment of an evening.*

### Signal 6: More Energy on Brand Than on Room

- Audit last 7 days of Andrea's calendar. What % was room-shaping work vs brand-shaping work?
- Cap brand work at 20% of weekly Resonance time until next event ships.
- Re-anchor: *brand serves the room, not the other way around.*

### Signal 7: Sponsor Conversations Eating Time

- Triage sponsor pipeline against Non-Negotiable #12.
- Decline anything that's not a clear yes. Don't "explore" anything.
- Re-anchor: *if sponsor work isn't compounding into the room, it's a leak.*

### Signal 8: Press as Goal in Itself

- Pause active press outreach for 14 days.
- If a journalist asks, deliver the press one-sheeter and end the conversation.
- Re-anchor: *press serves the room. Press as a metric is vanity.*

### Signal 9: Asked to Soften Daytime, Sober, or Curated

- Read Non-Negotiable lines 1, 2, 3 out loud.
- Decline the ask, named: "Resonance is daytime/sober/curated. Variations on that require a different name and aren't this brand."
- Re-anchor: *the lines are the brand. Without them, this is a generic singles event, which the market has and rejected.*

### Signal 10: Venue/Partner/Promoter Shaping Event More Than Andrea

- Identify exactly which decisions in the last cycle weren't Andrea's.
- Reclaim each one explicitly, even if it costs the relationship.
- Re-anchor: *founder-curated always (Non-Negotiable #5).*

## The Annual Reckoning

Once a year (anniversary of the first event), Andrea reads:

1. The Internal Anchor (full document)
2. This Drift Signals doc
3. The 12 Non-Negotiables

She names which signals fired in the last year, which lines bent, what got named at the time, what didn't. The point is not punishment — it's calibration. A founder who can't honestly say "this drifted" can't course-correct.
