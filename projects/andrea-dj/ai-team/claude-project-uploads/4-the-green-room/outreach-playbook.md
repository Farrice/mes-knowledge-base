## Friend Filter (5 Yes/No — Ask Before Approaching)

| Question | Must-Have? |
|----------|-----------|
| Real daylight windows in the main room at 2pm? | Yes |
| Holds 40-50 people comfortably dancing (not standing-room)? | Yes |
| Friend is comfortable with ~50 vetted strangers knowing the address? | Yes |
| Neighborhood works: parking or transit, neighbors tolerate 3 hours indoor-volume music, safe arrival? | Yes |
| Friend will hold the curation — won't insist on inviting their own crowd? | Yes |

**Decision rule**: 4-5 yes → reach out. 3 or fewer → skip, or ask them for an introduction to someone else.

## When a Friend Says Yes with Caveats

- **They want to add alcohol** → No. "Sober is the format. Want to come as a vetted attendee instead?"
- **They want to invite their crowd** → No. "Curated room means every person comes through the gate. Can I add 2 names from your circle to the applicant list?"
- **They say no but offer an intro** → Yes. "Thanks. Mind connecting me to {{NAME}} directly so I can ask?"
