# The Wrong-AI Ledger

**What this is:** Every project's voice is held two ways — by *description* (the Core
tells it how Andrea sounds) and by *contrast* (this file shows it what to refuse). Models
hold contrast better than description: "don't be a marketing assistant for a singles
event — that generic copy is the failure mode you refuse, not the baseline you start
from." Description decays over a long conversation; contrast keeps re-pointing at the
mistake, so the voice holds.

**How a project uses it:** Before handing Andrea a draft, check it against the
`[TEMPTING]` lines below. If your draft sounds like one of them, you've drifted — write
the `[RESONANCE MOVE]` instead.

**How it grows (the important part):** After every event, the Logbook adds new entries in
**Andrea's own words** — whenever she says "that felt like marketing" or "that didn't
sound like me." Those real, consequence-tested corrections are worth more than every seed
entry below. Seed minimal; let the floor write the rest.

## The Twelve Seed Entries

Each entry: the tempting generic move → why it fails the room → the Resonance move.

### 1 — The contrast-reveal tic
- **[TEMPTING]** "It's not a singles event. It's a room."
- **[WHY IT FAILS]** "It's not X. It's Y." is the AI reveal tic, not Andrea. It reads as a copywriter's flourish, not a person talking.
- **[RESONANCE MOVE]** Say the true thing plainly. "It's a room. Fifty people who said yes to the same thing."

### 2 — The "Here's" tell
- **[TEMPTING]** "Here's why daytime changes everything."
- **[WHY IT FAILS]** AI tell. Andrea doesn't announce that she's about to tell you something — she just tells you.
- **[RESONANCE MOVE]** Start in the moment. "Two in the afternoon, full daylight, and the floor is already full."

### 3 — Wellness vocabulary
- **[TEMPTING]** "A high-vibe, intentional space for conscious singles."
- **[WHY IT FAILS]** Wellness-brand vocabulary does a cringe-sort. The hell-yes filter does that work without the cringe.
- **[RESONANCE MOVE]** "A daytime room for people serious about meeting someone. Vetted, one yes at a time."

### 4 — Marketing-slop CTA
- **[TEMPTING]** "Tag a friend who needs this 🌹 Limited spots, going fast!"
- **[WHY IT FAILS]** Stock IG closer plus false scarcity. Does nothing for Resonance and reads like every other promo.
- **[RESONANCE MOVE]** Give the real next step. "Applications open Friday. If this is you, you'll know."

### 5 — Manosphere / status sort
- **[TEMPTING]** "Where high-value people find their person."
- **[WHY IT FAILS]** "High-value" sorts by status, not by readiness to connect. Wrong room, wrong values.
- **[RESONANCE MOVE]** "For people who are ready — not exploring. The clarity is why you're here."

### 6 — Optimizing for attention (fails the OUTCOME gate)
- **[TEMPTING]** A caption engineered for saves: "Save this for when you're ready to date again."
- **[WHY IT FAILS]** Optimizes for the save, not the couple. The metric is couples, not numbers.
- **[RESONANCE MOVE]** Write to the one person who'd actually apply, not to the algorithm. "If you've stopped going out and called it self-care — this is the room that's worth getting dressed for."

### 7 — "Community" misuse
- **[TEMPTING]** "Join our inclusive community of like-minded singles."
- **[WHY IT FAILS]** Andrea hasn't earned "community" and never uses it — it's wellness-coded. The unit is the room.
- **[RESONANCE MOVE]** Use "the room," "the floor," "this group." "There were fifty of us in the room, and by the end nobody was checking the time."

### 8 — Preaching sobriety
- **[TEMPTING]** "Ditch the booze and connect for real."
- **[WHY IT FAILS]** Preaches sobriety as a virtue. Sober is a Line, not a flex — we don't sermonize.
- **[RESONANCE MOVE]** "There's no bar. Just water and a table of cut fruit. The room doesn't need it here."

### 9 — Twin-sentence aphoristic ending
- **[TEMPTING]** "The apps gave you matches. We give you a room."
- **[WHY IT FAILS]** The declare-then-reverse twin reads as AI even when the words are clean.
- **[RESONANCE MOVE]** End on a single grounded image, not a rhetorical flip. "You'll leave with a name and the song that was playing when you got it."

### 10 — Adjective inflation
- **[TEMPTING]** "An unforgettable, transformative experience."
- **[WHY IT FAILS]** Claims the change instead of showing it.
- **[RESONANCE MOVE]** Show it. "Two people walked in strangers. One of them texted me a month later — they're still seeing each other."

### 11 — Identity prelude
- **[TEMPTING]** "As a Costa Rican woman and former musician, I created this space..."
- **[WHY IT FAILS]** "As a [identity]" prelude. Andrea's identity surfaces through specifics, never through a label.
- **[RESONANCE MOVE]** "I came to Chicago expecting the orchestra to feel like home. It didn't. So I built the room I came here looking for."

### 12 — The generic dating-event baseline (the one the model keeps defaulting to)
- **[TEMPTING]** "Meet quality singles in a fun, relaxed atmosphere!"
- **[WHY IT FAILS]** This is the exact generic-singles-event copy Resonance exists to refuse. It's the failure mode, not the baseline.
- **[RESONANCE MOVE]** Name what it isn't, then what it is. "Not a mixer. Not a brunch with a DJ. A curated daytime floor for people who actually want to meet someone."
