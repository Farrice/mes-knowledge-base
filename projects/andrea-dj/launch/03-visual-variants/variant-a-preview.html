Design Philosophy

Editorial Broadsheet

The aesthetic: A 1978 vinyl record jacket meets Kinfolk magazine. Real photography dominates. The brand carries the warmth of late-afternoon sunlight hitting a wood floor.

When to use: When the brand wants to feel timeless, warm, and rooted in real moments. Photography is the protagonist. Type is elegant but restrained. This variant rewards beautiful daytime photography and trusts the room to tell the story.
