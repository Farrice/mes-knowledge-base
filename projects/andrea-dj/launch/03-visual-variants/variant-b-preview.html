Design Philosophy

Latin-American Modernism

The aesthetic: Architectural, grounded, rooted in place. Deep earth tones. Bold, confident typography. The room is the second protagonist—architecture visible, intentional, unmovable. Photography emphasizes architectural frame and human connection as equals.

When to use: When the brand wants to feel permanent, architectural, place-specific. The room itself carries cultural weight. Typography is bold and confident, not whisper-quiet. This variant emphasizes that Resonance is a *place* with character, not just an event.
