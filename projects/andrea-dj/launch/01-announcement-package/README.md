## Style notes for Andrea

- Voice register: conversational for founder/origin pieces; polished for hero/announcement
- Banned phrases: "vibes," "community," "intentional," "authentic," "energy," "showing up," "conscious singles," "It's not X. It's Y.", "Here's what/why/how", "tag a friend who needs this"
- Hell-yes filter appears in any "who this is for" post — warm in invitation, clear in filter, never hostile
- Em dashes ≤ 2 per piece. Where Andrea would say a line differently to a friend over coffee, change it — her mouth is the final filter.
- Live counts ("forty-eight," "eighteen applications") are illustrative — swap the real number at posting time, or use the fallback phrasing in the Note column.
