# Section A — Strategy

## A.1 The two-stage announcement

Resonance can't ship a complete event announcement yet. Venue is open, JR is open, the date is a default (July 18) with a backup (July 25). What can ship is **the brand**: the founder, the format, the philosophy, the room — everything that doesn't require knowing the building's name.

So the launch is two announcements stacked into one funnel.

**Phase 1 — Brand-alive (ships Week 2, runs 4–5 weeks).** The brand goes public. Andrea introduces herself, the room, the mechanic, and the waitlist. There is no date in the CTA, no venue, no lineup. The ask is: read the manifesto, join the list, apply when the application opens.

**Phase 2 — Event announcement (ships the day venue + JR lock).** The full announcement drops. Date, neighborhood (not address yet — address goes out the day before per the operational protocol Andrea has already published), format, spot count, application open. Press teaser goes live the same week.

**Phase 3 — Pre-event ramp (final two weeks before event).** Countdown without the countdown energy. Room-curation behind-the-scenes. Final 50 spots → 30 → 12. 48-hour pre-event email to confirmed RSVPs.

## A.2 What lives in which phase

| Phase | Window | Posts | Reels | Stories | Substack Notes | Emails | Total surfaces |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 (brand-alive) | 6/3 – ~7/1 | 3 | 2 | 5-frame sequence | 3 trailers | 0 | 13 |
| Phase 2 (announcement) | venue + JR lock | 1 hero + supports | 1 | 5-frame sequence | (rolls into IG/Substack) | 1 | 9 |
| Phase 3 (ramp) | final 2 weeks | 3 | 2 | daily cadence guide | (organic notes) | 1 (48hr) | ~12 |

Total cadence is steady, never frantic. Resonance posts in the morning and on weekend afternoons. It doesn't post at night, and it doesn't carpet-bomb the feed when the venue locks.

## A.3 The venue gate / lineup gate callout

The single risk of launching brand-alive without an event lock is the perception that Resonance is vapor. Two protections.

**Protection 1 — Operational specificity carries trust without a venue name.** Andrea has already said publicly: doors at 2pm, no bar, the address goes out the day before, the application takes about four minutes. That's the room described. Readers don't need the building name to believe the room exists — they need the protocol described well enough to picture themselves inside it. Phase 1 leans on that.

**Protection 2 — The waitlist itself is proof.** When the application opens (Phase 1, Week 3 target, dependent on Tally + Beehiiv being live), every applicant who fills the form is the room declaring itself. Resonance can post the waitlist count once it crosses a threshold worth posting (suggested: 40 applications) without naming applicants. That's social proof from the room, not from invented testimonials.

If JR doesn't confirm by ~7/1, the Phase 2 announcement ships with **"Andrea on the decks. Anchor DJ TBA."** Andrea is the brand's DJ — she does not need a guest to ship. JR is upside, not floor.

If a venue isn't locked by ~7/1, the date moves to the July 25 backup and Phase 2 ships then. Phase 1 extends by one week with one extra Reel.

### Note 1 — Counterintuitive Truth — Wed 6/4

```
The most-asked question I get about Resonance is not about the daytime or the sobriety or even the curation.

It's about the music.

The assumption is that a sober dance party at 2pm is somehow easier to DJ than a 1am club — that the crowd needs less, that the room is forgiving, that the songs can carry less weight.

The opposite is true. Sober adults at 2pm hear everything. The kick drum has nowhere to hide. The transition has to be earned. The song that bends the room toward someone you wouldn't have noticed an hour ago has to do that work without alcohol in the room thinning anyone's attention.

The bar at 11pm forgives a lot of bad music. Daytime, sober, curated does not.

That's the actual mechanic underneath Resonance, and the part that took me longest to figure out.
```

**Archetype.** Counterintuitive Truth. **What.** Sober daytime DJing is harder than club DJing. **How.** Concrete description of the audio difference. **Why.** It reframes the format as a discipline, not a softening.
