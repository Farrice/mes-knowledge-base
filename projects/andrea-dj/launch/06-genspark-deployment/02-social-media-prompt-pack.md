## Strategy in One Paragraph

Resonance's social feed runs on **two visual modes**: (1) **typographic carousel slides** with brand-spine copy on cream backgrounds — generated by GPT Image 2 (text rendering wins) or Nano Banana Pro, (2) **photo-led posts** featuring the v3 hero shots or fresh daytime daylight stills — generated by Nano Banana Pro for series continuity. Video content uses the I2V routing matrix in `01-image-to-video-prompts.md`.

## ASSET 7 — Substack-Specific Hero Headers

**Format**: Substack post hero (wide 1456×819) at top of each long-form post
**Model**: Nano Banana Pro
**Cost**: FREE on Pro

### Why a different aspect ratio
Substack post heroes are 1456×819 (close to 16:9). Different from IG. Don't reuse IG square crops — they letterbox badly on Substack and look amateur.

### Generic Substack hero prompt template

```
Wide 1456x819. Real photography: [scene description specific to the post topic — wood floor + record + daylight, OR Andrea's hand on a turntable, OR an empty Chicago loft, OR a hand on a notebook with a track name].

35mm film grain, late-afternoon Chicago overcast register, documentary photography. NOT staged, NOT Pinterest.

Composition: subject in lower or right third, generous negative space for Substack post title overlay if Andrea wants to add one in-platform.

The image evokes the post's subject — concrete, lived, NOT decorative.

Negative: no AI faces, no club, no neon, no golden hour, no Pinterest aesthetic, no warm-orange filter, no decorative typography, no stylized filter.
```

### Variations to swap in
- For founder origin posts: Costa Rica register (warm-but-overcast morning, ceramic + book + light)
- For "the room" posts: empty Chicago loft, wood floor, daylight, NO people
- For "the music" posts: vinyl + turntable + hand detail
- For "the application" posts: notebook + handwriting, neutral surfaces

## Voice + Visual Integrity Checklist

Before generating any Resonance social asset, confirm:

- [ ] **Voice document is in scope**: copy in the asset matches `voice-document.md` Section 6 paste-in
- [ ] **No banned phrases**: nothing on the asset uses "Here's what / why / how", "vibes", "community" (unless meant), "energy", etc.
- [ ] **Banned visual descriptors absent**: no "golden hour", "stadium", "neon", "club lighting" in prompt
- [ ] **Negative tail applied**: standard Resonance negative prompt at end of every generation
- [ ] **Reference image used** for series consistency: same hero shot referenced across related assets
- [ ] **Single model per series**: all carousel slides through GPT Image 2, all photo posts through Nano Banana Pro — don't mix mid-series
