# 02 — Hook Library

*The strategic hook bank. Paste-in seeds for every Resonance post, story, reel, email, and caption.*
*Last updated: 2026-05-04. Pairs with `01-content-pillars.md`. Inputs: Voice Document §2 (six patterns), §4 (voice examples), §5 (banned moves); ICP Master (Nora / Imani / Marcus); A1 Reconciliation §4 (crystallized phrases).*

> *Resonance is heart encounters, not head encounters... a daytime, sober dance party in Chicago for people who want to meet a partner. The mechanic is body-first: the music does the emotional labor so the people don't have to. The metric is couples, not followers.*

---

## Section 1 — How To Use This Library

This library is built to be paste-in. Andrea picks a pillar, scans hooks, lifts the one that resonates with the day's idea, adapts it for the asset type. Hooks are seeds, not finished posts — every hook below has a sample fill demonstrating one way it could become a caption, but the same hook can adapt across IG main, story, reel, email, or DM with different fills. The pattern stays. The fill flexes.

Each hook is tagged for pattern (which of the six voice patterns it leans on), profile fit (which ICP profile it targets — Nora, Imani, Marcus, or All), and audience state (pre-contemplation / contemplation / preparation / mixed). Use the tags to triage: if a week leans pre-contemplation, lift hooks tagged that way. If a week is targeting Imani specifically, scan for her name. The tags exist so Andrea isn't deciding fit from scratch every time.

## Section 3 — Cross-Pillar Hooks (the universal-utility hooks)

These five hooks come from Andrea's manifesto and anchor. They are so resonant they can open content under any pillar — the work is choosing which pillar's lens to write the body through.

---

**Hook**: *If you've asked yourself why it's so hard to meet a good person, the answer is not another app.*
**When to use**: Any pillar, leans Singles Reality + Spine. Strongest as an opener for pre-contemplation content.
**When NOT to use**: When the body of the post is going to be about logistics, music, or Chicago specifically — the hook is too heavy for those bodies.
**Sample fill**: *If you've asked yourself why it's so hard to meet a good person, the answer is not another app. The answer is also not another bar. The answer is a different room. Daytime. Sober. Curated. Chicago. June.*

**Hook**: *We count the couples, not the followers.*
**When to use**: Spine + Curation pillars. Closing line for any piece that named the metric question. End-of-thread punch.
**When NOT to use**: Story pillar — Story content shouldn't restate the metric, it should demonstrate it.
**Sample fill**: *We count the couples, not the followers. The first time you read that, it sounds like a brand line. The fifteenth time you read it, you realize we mean it operationally. We genuinely do not know our follower count tonight. We genuinely do know that two people met on a song last week and have been on three dates since.*

**When to use**: Spine pillar primarily. Hero copy. About sections. The four-word version of the brand.

## Section 4 — Hook Anti-Patterns

Hooks that look like they should work but fail the brand. Each fails for a specific structural or register reason. Catalog this list and check against it before any post ships.

- **"5 reasons Resonance is the best singles event in Chicago."** Listicle clickbait register, plus *singles event* is a wince word for Nora and Imani. Voice doc bans the listicle frame outright for Resonance.
- **"Imagine a room where the music does the work and the people don't have to..."** *Imagine a room* is an AI-tell + a wellness register. The voice doc bans the *imagine* opener because it puts the reader in fantasy mode when the brand is built on showing real moments.
- **"What if I told you there's a different way to meet someone?"** AI-tell + condescending. The implied *what if I told you* posture talks down to the reader. Voice doc bans this exact opener.
- **"Did you know Chicago invented house music?"** Trivia register. Diminishes the brand by making the city's lineage a fun fact instead of a load-bearing inheritance. The Chicago pillar uses lineage as ground, never as trivia.
- **"Here's why we don't allow alcohol at our events..."** Banned *Here's why* opener + justification posture. The brand doesn't justify Line 2; it lives it. *We don't allow* also misframes — alcohol isn't allowed-or-not, it's not part of the format.
- **"Are YOU ready to find your person?"** Caps + hyper-direct invasion. The voice doc bans *find your person* as a promise (it's a wish, not a guarantee, and the grammar of promise activates the ICP's bullshit detector). Caps for emphasis is its own register failure.
- **"The dating event that's taking Chicago by storm."** Press-release register + adjective inflation. The brand has no *taking by storm* energy. Also *the dating event* is the exact category-trap the brand was built to escape.
- **"Stop swiping. Start dancing."** Twin-aphorism reveal pattern (banned in voice doc § 5). Reads as AI even when the words are clean. The pairing of two short imperatives is the AI-rhythm tell.

## Section 5 — Hook Test (the gate before posting)

Five-question gate. Run before any post ships. If any answer is no, revise.

1. **Pattern named?** Can you name which of the six voice patterns the hook leans on? If no — the hook is stylistically vague and likely drifting toward AI register. Pattern up.
2. **Profile-fit clear?** Could you tag this hook *Nora / Imani / Marcus / All*? If you can't, the hook is targeting a generic ICP that doesn't exist. Sharpen the target.
3. **Voice test passed?** Would Andrea say this sentence to a friend over coffee? If no — rewrite. The voice doc's non-negotiable test.
4. **No banned phrases or moves?** Scan for the voice doc § 5 ban list: *Here's what / why / how / the thing*, *vibes* unsaid, *community* unsaid, *conscious singles*, *high-vibe*, *quality over quantity*, *DM us to learn more*, *tag a friend who needs this*, *imagine a room*, the *It's not X. It's Y.* twin-aphorism. Em-dashes ≤ 2.
5. **Specific image/concept available to pair?** Hook + visual is the IG unit. If the hook lands but you can't picture a specific photo or graphic to run alongside it, the hook is verbal-only and likely too abstract. Either the hook gets sharper or the visual gets more specific. Don't ship until both line up.
