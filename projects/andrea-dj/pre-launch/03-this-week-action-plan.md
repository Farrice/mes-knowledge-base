## The Posture for This Week

Week 1 is the week the brand stops being a document and starts being a thing in the world. You're not trying to do everything. You're trying to do the three moves that make every following week possible: lock the date that anchors the calendar, put the first venue outreach in market so a room can say yes back, and stand up enough of a public presence that anyone curious about Resonance can find a place to land. Everything else this week is supporting work. The trinity above is what makes Week 2 possible.

1. **Lock the event date** (Andrea, by Friday). Without it, venues can't say yes and the public copy can't name a Saturday.
2. **Get the first venue outreach in market** (Farrice, by Wednesday). The room is the highest-stakes variable — venue cost is the difference between Lean-profitable and break-even.
3. **Set up the public-facing presence** (both, IG bio + first post live by Monday 5/26). The brand becomes legible to anyone Andrea mentions Resonance to between now and Event #1.

If those three land, Week 1 was a win. If two land and one slips, Week 2 absorbs the third without resetting the whole sprint.
