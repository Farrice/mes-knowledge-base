# Venue Cold Pitch Email — Tier 2 Outreach

*Part of the Pre-Launch Command Center (see `00-command-center.md`). Decision deadline: 2026-06-02 venue lock.*

*The workhorse. Andrea sends 8-10 of these to Tier 2 venues (dance studios, photo studios, art galleries, indie performance venues, lofts under $1,500 day rate). Adapt per venue — never send identical copy at scale.*

---

## Subject line bank (5 options, ranked by recommendation)

Pick by venue type. Each subject line ties to a different opener strategy.

### Subject line 1 (top pick — specific-asks opener)
**`Daytime dance partnership — Sat July 18 or 25, 50 people`**

Best for: Tier 2 venues with public events calendars (dance studios, photo studios with active booking pages). Specificity gets opened.

### Subject line 2 (slot-focused)
**`A 2pm Saturday slot — mid-to-late July`**

Best for: art galleries / arts spaces where mission-fit matters more than spec. The slot framing reads as a partnership inquiry, not a rental request.

### Subject line 3 (capacity + venue-name)
**`Hosting a 50-person daytime event at [Venue Name]`**

Best for: photo studios / dance studios where capacity is the gating spec. Naming the venue in the subject increases open rate.

### Subject line 4 (relational opener — when there's a soft connection)
**`Andrea (Resonance) — partnership inquiry, mid-July daytime`**

Best for: when there's a soft connection (Andrea's been to a show there, follows them on IG, has a mutual). The branded subject earns the open.

### Subject line 5 (mechanic-led — format as filter)
**`Saturday afternoon, no bar, 50 vetted attendees`**

Best for: venues whose calendar fills with bar-driven events. The format itself sorts them — they either lean in or pass quickly.

## Body (300-450 words)

**Adapt the opener per venue.** Either name a specific observation (a show you've been to there, a feature of the space) OR — if pure cold — name a specific architectural detail (the light at 2pm, the floor, the proportions). Generic openers get archived.

---

Hi [Name or "team at Venue"],

[Opener: Name the specific observation if you have one. Examples: "I've been to two openings at [Venue] and the room stays with me." Or: "[Venue]'s 2pm light through the west windows is the kind of room I'm trying to build for."]

I'm Andrea, a DJ in Chicago, launching Resonance — a daytime, sober dance event for adults who want to meet a partner. The first event is mid-to-late July, Saturday 2pm to 5pm, about 50 people. The room is small on purpose, vetted before the address goes out, on the floor by 2:15 and off by 5:15.

The format is built differently. Daytime, no bar, music curated by me beat to beat, phones in a basket at the door. Three hours of presence in real daylight. We don't sell drinks — that part isn't a constraint, it's the design.

What we'd need from [Venue]:
- A 3-hour event window (12pm-6pm building access for setup + breakdown, 2-5pm event)
- Real daylight in the main room
- About 800 sq ft of open floor that survives 50 people dancing
- Bathroom capacity for 50 attendees + 8-12 staff
- Loading access for DJ gear (turntables, mixer, PA if you don't have one)
- One power circuit on a dedicated breaker

What we bring:
- Certificate of insurance documented and provided 14 days before event date
- Hard cap on body count (50 attendees + 8-12 staff/photographer)
- Zero liquor liability for [Venue]
- Sound capped at your dB ceiling (we self-monitor to respect neighbors / building code)
- Post-event promotion: IG tag, professional photographer shots shared with permission, recap article that names the space

Could we tour the space sometime this week or next? A fifteen-minute call works too if that's easier. We're targeting July 18 or July 25 — venue availability decides the lock.

If [Venue]'s not the right room for this, a one-line no is plenty and I appreciate the consideration.

Best,
Andrea
Resonance · @resonance.chicago · [phone or email]

*PS — I grew up at a music school in Costa Rica, then trained in a national youth orchestra where curated admission made the room work. I came to Chicago expecting that room and didn't find it. So I'm building it. No alcohol service required from your side; that part is just how we built it.*

## Personalization checklist (do not send without these confirmed)

- [ ] Opener mentions a specific venue detail or observation (not generic flattery)
- [ ] `[Venue]` placeholder replaced everywhere
- [ ] Subject line picked per venue archetype (see bank above)
- [ ] Recipient name confirmed (not "team at")
- [ ] CTA dates match latest internal lock (July 18 / July 25)
- [ ] Sign-off has working contact info (phone OR email — pick one consistent)

## Voice-check notes (do not include in the sent version)

- 2 em-dashes total in body, 1 in PS (cap of 2 per piece met across the email)
- Zero banned phrases swept ("amazing space," "passionate about," "intentional," "container," "energy," etc.)
- Mechanic-as-sentence pattern carries the format paragraph (Voice Pattern 6)
- Frame-then-sharpen pattern in opening Resonance frame (Voice Pattern 2)
- Compressed founder paragraph in PS (~60 words including the no-bar tag)
- "Room" used consistently, not "community" or "container"
- Low-friction CTA preserves venue dignity ("a one-line no is plenty")

## Flagged items to confirm before sending the first batch

1. `[Andrea has been DJing in Chicago for X years; networks include Y]` — currently not in the body (kept the body lean). If a venue requests social proof, add this in the follow-up email rather than front-loading.
2. COI bound? (If not, bind this week before sending Tier 2.)
3. Contact info: phone or email at sign-off? Andrea's preference — pick the one she'll check fastest.
