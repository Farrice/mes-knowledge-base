Voice: Andrea-texting-a-friend. Lowercase. No formal openers. The friend already knows what Resonance is, or close enough.

## EMAIL version (300-500 words — for friend-of-friend introduction chains)

Voice: warm but slightly more polished than DM. Lands when a mutual connector has set up the introduction and the recipient is opening cold but expecting your name. Uses the v1.1 compressed founder paragraph.

---

**Subject:** Daytime room for ~50 — Resonance, mid-to-late July Saturday

Hi [Name],

Sam mentioned you might be the right person to talk to about [Venue]. I'm Andrea, a DJ in Chicago, and I'm launching Resonance — a daytime, sober dance event for adults who want to meet a partner. First event is mid-to-late July, Saturday 2pm to 5pm, about 50 people.

I grew up at a music school in Costa Rica, then trained in a national youth orchestra where curated admission made the room work. I came to Chicago expecting that room and didn't find it. So I'm building it.

The format is simple in the right way. Saturday afternoon, 2pm to 5pm. Fifty people on the floor by 2:15 and off by 5:15. No alcohol service from your side. Sound is mine — I bring the PA if you don't have one. Phones go in a basket at the door. The room is small on purpose, vetted before the address goes out.

What we'd need from [Venue]:
- A 3-hour event window (12pm-6pm building access for setup, 2-5pm event)
- Real daylight in the room (windows)
- About 800 sq ft of open floor that survives 50 people dancing
- Bathroom capacity for 50
- Loading access for DJ gear

What we bring:
- Certificate of insurance documented in advance
- Predictable body count (hard cap at 50 + 8-12 staff)
- Zero liquor liability (we don't serve)
- Post-event promotion: IG tag, photographer shots shared with permission, recap that names the space

If [Venue] could work for July 18 or July 25, I'd love a fifteen-minute call this week or a tour of the space, whichever's easier. If it's not your room, a one-line no is plenty and I appreciate the consideration.

Best,
Andrea
Resonance · @resonance.chicago · [phone or email]

**Voice-check notes (do not include in the sent version):**
- 2 em-dashes total (cap met)
- Zero banned phrases ("intentional," "container," "community," "vibes," etc.)
- Mechanic-as-sentence pattern carries the format paragraph (Voice Pattern 6)
- Compressed founder paragraph (~40 words) does the autobiographical work without overwhelming a venue inquiry
- Low-friction CTA ("a one-line no is plenty") respects the venue's time
- "[Venue]" placeholder gets replaced with the actual venue name per send
