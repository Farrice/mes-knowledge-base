## 48-Hour Follow-Up Template

Send 48 hours after the original cold/warm pitch if no response. Reply to the original thread (preserves subject line + context).

> Just bumping this back up in case it got buried. Same ask — Saturday daytime mid-to-late July, about 50 people, no bar. Happy to send the one-pager if helpful, or jump on a quick call.
>
> Andrea

(Under 80 words. One em-dash. Pass.)

**Variation for warm contacts (more casual):**

> hey [name] — bumping this in case it got lost. same ask, sat daytime mid-to-late july. lmk?
> andrea

---

## 7-Day No-Response Protocol

If 7 days pass with no response from the venue, send ONE more short note with a sharper close. Then move on.

> Hi [Name],
>
> Following up one last time on the daytime event inquiry from last week (Saturday mid-to-late July, ~50 people).
>
> If [Venue]'s not the right room for this, a one-line no is plenty and I'll stop bothering you. If it is, I'd love to find a window this week.
>
> Best,
> Andrea

(Under 80 words. Two em-dashes. Pass.)

After this final send, no more follow-ups. Move venue to "no response" and shift focus to remaining outreach. Do not chase. The brand carries; chasing erodes it.

## Decision Tree for Venue Replies

Every reply scenario you'll encounter. Each branch tells you what to do.

### Branch 1 — Venue asks for revenue share

**Example reply:** *"We'd love to host but we work on a 70/30 ticket revenue split."*

**Defense:** BOS Lines 3 (Curated admission) + 5 (Founder-curated, always). Revenue share creates pressure on door curation we don't carry. The room rots when economics pressure who gets in.

**Response:**

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for considering it. We work on a flat rental model rather than revenue share — revenue share creates pressure on door curation we don't carry, and the room curation is what makes this work.
>
> If [Venue] can do a flat rate for the 3-hour window, I'd love to keep the conversation going. If the rev-share model is non-negotiable on your side, totally understand — and would love to come back to you for a different Resonance variant down the line.
>
> Best,
> Andrea

**Counter-offer:** flat fee within the Lean Path ceiling ($700) or Standard Path range ($1,000-1,200). Andrea adjudicates per Command Center Section 4.

### Branch 2 — Venue requires alcohol service

**Example reply:** *"Our license requires us to run the bar during all events."*

**Defense:** BOS Lines 1 (Daytime) + 2 (Sober). Non-negotiable.

**Response:**

> Hi [Name],
>
> Appreciate the consideration. The flagship Resonance event is sober — that's a structural part of the format, not a preference, so we won't be able to make it work for this one.
>
> We're building a Resonance Series variant later in the year that's structured differently — would love to revisit your space for that. I'll keep you in the loop.
>
> Best,
> Andrea

Mark the venue for the future Resonance Series outreach list. Don't burn the relationship.

### Branch 3 — Venue offers weeknight only

**Example reply:** *"We don't rent on Saturdays but Thursday evenings are open."*

**Defense:** BOS Line 1 (Daytime). The flagship is Saturday daytime. Non-negotiable.

**Response:** Same template as Branch 2 (Resonance Series bridge for future). Daytime Saturday is the format; alternates are sub-brands per the BOS variations-as-sub-brands doctrine.

### Branch 4 — Venue offers Sunday daytime

**Example reply:** *"Saturday's booked but Sunday 2-5pm is open."*

**Decision:** ACCEPT if mid-to-late July Sunday works for Andrea's calendar.

**Reasoning:** Sunday daytime still honors Line 1 (Daytime). The "Saturday" framing was preference, not a Line. Sunday afternoons can read as MORE intentional than Saturdays in Chicago (less competing weekend events, attendees are in a different headspace).

**Response:**

> Hi [Name],
>
> Sunday's actually a strong option — let me check the calendar and get back to you by [tomorrow / specific date]. Could we tour the space [proposed date/time]?
>
> Andrea

Then check Andrea's calendar + JR/DJ availability for that Sunday before confirming.

### Branch 5 — Venue offers 3pm-6pm or 1pm-4pm time slot

**Example reply:** *"We can do 3pm-6pm instead of 2pm-5pm."*

**Decision:** ACCEPT. Within the flagship daytime window.

**Reasoning:** The 2pm-5pm anchor was preference. 3pm-6pm is daytime (real light through ~5pm in July, golden hour by 6). 1pm-4pm is daytime (full daylight). Both work.

**Adjustments needed:** update doors-open/doors-close in all post-confirmation copy. Other format mechanics unchanged.

### Branch 6 — Venue rental is $3,000 or higher

**Example reply:** *"Our 3-hour Saturday afternoon rate is $3,500."*

**Decision:** HALT. Cross-reference Command Center Section 4 (break-even math).

**Math:**
- At $3,500 venue + ~$1,200 variable costs = $4,700 total costs
- 50 attendees × $40 ticket = $2,000 revenue. Loss of $2,700.
- 50 attendees × $55 ticket = $2,750 revenue. Loss of $1,950.
- 50 attendees × $70 ticket = $3,500 revenue. Loss of $1,200.
- 50 attendees × $100 ticket = $5,000 revenue. Profit of $300.

Premium ticket ($75+) is hard sell for Event #1. The math says NO unless the venue is exceptional AND Andrea wants to invest profitability in the launch.

**Response if declining:**

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for the breakdown. The $[number] rate puts us above our Event #1 budget — we're targeting under [Standard cap, $1,200] for the first event to keep ticket pricing accessible.
>
> If your rate flexes for first-time partnerships, I'd love to keep talking. If not, totally understand and would love to revisit for Event #3 or #4 when the model is proven.
>
> Best,
> Andrea

**Response if accepting (only with Andrea's explicit sign-off):**
- Decision flagged to RISKS.md as a budget exception
- Ticket pricing reverse-engineered: $75 ticket × 50 = $3,750 → breaks even at $3,750 venue + $0 other costs; not viable. Must be $90+ to clear $1,000 profit.
- Andrea adjudicates: is the venue prestige worth a higher ticket price + lower margin Event #1?

### Branch 7 — 14 Days Pass with No Tier 1 or Tier 2 Lock

**Decision:** Activate Tier 3 (house party). Plan B is real.

**Tier 3 activation steps:**

1. **Pick the friend.** 1-2 candidates identified during Week 1 framework exercise. Must have: 50-person capacity, real daylight, accessible location, willing host, neighbors won't call noise complaint.

2. **Reach out:**

> Hey [friend] — bigger ask incoming. Venue search hasn't landed in time. Could your place host Resonance Event #1 on Saturday July 18 (or 25)? 50 people, 2-5pm, no bar, music curated by me, all vetted attendees. We carry insurance + handle setup/breakdown. Happy to thank you with [donation / dinner / $200 thank-you fee per Profile C from BOS venue-pitch brief].

3. **Confirm format adjustments for Tier 3:**
   - Capacity may need to flex down to 35-40 if the house can't hold 50 comfortably
   - Sound levels capped lower (respect neighbors)
   - Possible address-not-disclosed-until-RSVP confirmation
   - Photographer briefed on residential context (no exterior shots showing address)

4. **Update Command Center Section 4** with Tier 3 numbers:
   - Venue: $0-200 (donation or thank-you fee)
   - Reduces total costs to ~$830 floor
   - At $40 ticket × 50 attendees = $2,000 revenue → $1,170 profit (highest margin Path)
   - At $40 ticket × 35 attendees = $1,400 revenue → $570 profit (still healthy)
