## Section 7 — Voice Rules Quick-Reference (apply to every post)

From `00-foundation/03-voice-document.md`. Print this and tape it next to the computer if needed.

- Show, don't tell — lead with the specific moment, not the abstraction
- Mechanic-as-sentence — daytime/sober/curated written as sentences with stakes, not feature bullets
- Crystallized phrases — short period-stacked phrases that read like a creed
- Out-loud-asking — use the ICP's actual sentences as recognition triggers
- Frame-then-sharpen — clean declarative, then 2-3 named enemies
- Hell-yes filter — invite the right, filter the unsure
- "Here's what / why / the thing..." openers — banned
- "It's not X. It's Y." reveal pattern — banned
- Em-dashes more than 2 per piece — banned
- "Vibes," "community," "intentional," "container," "sacred," "high-vibe," "conscious singles," "manifest," "energy," "journey," "soul," "embodied" — banned
- "Tag a friend who needs this" / "Drop a 🌹" / stock IG closers
- Cheap question signoffs ("What's your version?", "Anyone else?", "Drop a 🌹 in the comments...") — banned
- Posed group shots, looking-at-camera-smiling, branded alcohol, nightclub aesthetic

The voice test: *"Would Andrea say this to a friend over coffee?"* If no, rewrite.
