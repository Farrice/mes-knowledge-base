**DISSENT**:
While many providers tout "low latency," there's a significant nuance often overlooked: the *type* of latency and its *relevance* to a trader's specific strategy. A contrarian view is that for many retail traders, especially those not scalping or engaging in high-frequency trading, the difference between 1ms and 10ms latency is negligible in terms of actual trading profitability, yet the price difference can be substantial. Generic cloud providers, while requiring more technical setup, might offer a better "raw compute per dollar" for traders whose primary need is 24/7 uptime for less latency-sensitive strategies, rather than absolute sub-millisecond execution. The "premium" narrative can sometimes oversell the necessity of extreme latency for the average user, creating an articulation gap where traders feel they *must* pay top dollar when a more affordable, reliable option would suffice for their actual needs.
