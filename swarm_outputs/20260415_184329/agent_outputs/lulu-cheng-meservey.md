**SUMMARY**

Visual Crossing offers a compelling free tier that includes 1,000 daily records for both historical and forecast data, uniquely permitting commercial use. Beyond this, their pricing scales through a pay-as-you-go metered plan at $0.0001 per record and various monthly subscriptions starting around $35, catering to diverse usage needs and high-concurrency demands.

**KEY FINDINGS**

*   **Generous Free Tier with Commercial License:** Visual Crossing provides 1,000 free weather records per day, a significant differentiator as it explicitly allows commercial deployment and access to full 15-day forecasts and 50 years of historical data. This positions them as a truly risk-free entry point for developers and small businesses.
*   **Flexible Metered and Subscription Models:** Beyond the free tier, users can opt for a pay-as-you-go "Metered" plan at $0.0001 per record for unlimited usage and concurrency. For higher volumes, monthly subscriptions (e.g., Pro, Corporate, Timeline LLX) offer cost efficiencies, with a Pro plan estimated around $35/month for 350,000 records and a Corporate plan around $150/month for 1.5 million records.
*   **Strategic Competitive Positioning:** Visual Crossing actively highlights its free commercial tier as a superior alternative to competitors like Weather Underground, which have eliminated their free offerings. This "line-in-the-sand" positioning directly targets a segment of developers and businesses seeking affordable, unencumbered access to weather data.
