## Who buys
Established supplement, recovery, functional-nutrition, or performance brand, roughly $1M–$50M revenue, founder-led voice or one drowning marketing lead, no senior creative-strategy seat. Already selling, already running ads (Meta for the Control-Beater), with approved evidence and a team or agency ready to produce. They have a dated occasion inside 60–90 days: launch, retail push, reformulation, new ingredient, repositioning, or a creative cycle where the control is dying.

| Brand size | Who signs | Door |
|---|---|---|
| Sub-$1M | Founder / CEO-as-CMO | Founder DM |
| $1M–$20M | CMO / VP Marketing / Head of Growth | Named-role DM; media buyer is gatekeeper and referral source |
| Scaled | In-house team | Not the buyer; anchor content only |

## Avatar 1 — "Dana Reyes", brand decision-maker (primary)
Head of Brand / Head of Content / VP Growth at a $3M–$40M DTC wellness brand, 34–44, owns $15K+/mo paid budget, retainer authority to ~$5–8K/mo. Carries the number, sees the dashboard turning red, will get blamed.
- **Midnight fear:** "If the founder types 'best magnesium for sleep' into ChatGPT tonight and sees three competitors named and not us — that's my job. And I won't even be able to explain why, because I did everything right."
- **What winning feels like:** typing the category question in front of the founder, seeing the brand named, saying "I built that."
- **Relationship to AI:** overuser getting nervous; needs permission to use it as research engine, not voice, and someone to take the shame off the table.

## Avatar 2 — "Marcus Vale", founder / expert (secondary)
Founder, formulator, or credentialed face of a founder-led brand, 33–50. Real domain credibility, total decision authority, near-zero time. The founder account is now the brand account and that terrifies him.
- **Midnight fear:** "I'm the most knowledgeable person in this category and I'm invisible — and some kid with half my expertise and twice the posting cadence is becoming the name people associate with this."
- **What winning feels like:** known for the work, in his own voice, without becoming someone he doesn't respect.

## Awareness ladder
Bulk (~40%) is Problem-Aware and thinks it is an AEO problem. Meeting them at "more schema" loses. Meeting them at "your proof is good, it just isn't getting carried, and in this category that's a two-gate problem" wins. That is his home stage.

## The one belief every piece must shift
"Our copy problem is a writing/volume problem" → **"It's an unmade decision: what we prove, who must believe it, what we'll defend."**

## Purchase triggers (time outreach to these)
1. LTV:CAC collapse below 3:1
2. Scaling wall: hero-product brand at $1–5M cannot expand portfolio or retail
3. New exec or funding announcement: first 60–90 days; response halves after day 90

## Yes-signals for a no-logo outsider [V]
Published, dated, named teardown work · the $300–500, 90-minute paid case test they already use · permissionless audit of their brand · niche compliance fluency · a credible "what AI does well and where it fails" POV

## Disqualifiers
No real revenue yet · >$1M/mo ad spend with in-house creative team · a product he wouldn't defend
