# Farrice Cain — Visual Identity

## One-paragraph summary

A commissioned decision object translated into a personal brand: quiet, exact, contemporary, decisive. The work should feel expensive because the thinking has been edited, not because the surface is decorated. Warm off-white canvas, near-black ink, fine graphite rules, a strict twelve-column grid, at least a third of every surface left open, and one dark recommendation per sequence. Helvetica Neue is the only face. The buyer should come away believing: "This person can reduce ambiguity without manufacturing claims."

## Mission

Stronger arguments for honest products. Farrice Cain helps established supplement and performance brands decide what the product should argue before they fund more creative: what to say, what approved proof supports it, and why the buyer should care. He does not make weak products louder, and he does not turn a fairy-dust dose into a heroic headline.

## Values

- Honest products deserve stronger arguments
- Decide the message before the creative
- Proof you can inspect, not claims you have to trust
- Restraint is the expensive part

## Three mood words

**Restrained. Contemporary. Decisive.** A choice that serves none of them gets removed.

## Where this identity sits

- **Closer to** Kith-like restraint applied to business communication; a commissioned field guide; a scientific blueprint with a bounded ledger grid (the Performance Evidence Journal mode).
- **Further from** management-consultancy sterility, anonymous template shops, agency-bro or creator-lifestyle branding, black-and-gold luxury theater, faux-lab or stock-supplement imagery, dashboard density.

## Two modes, one system

- **Master-brand mode.** Masthead `FARRICE CAIN`, descriptor `CREATIVE STRATEGY FOR SUPPLEMENT + PERFORMANCE BRANDS`. Content is category point of view, lived observation, teardown, framework. The offer appears only in a restrained footer or final frame.
- **Offer mode.** Masthead `THE ANGLE MAP`, author `FARRICE CAIN`. Content is three campaign arguments and one recommendation. Route grammar appears only when the content genuinely compares choices.

## Performance Evidence Journal (vertical Health Performance mode)

Not a parallel brand system. Inside Premium Minimal, for health and performance marketers who buy edited judgment and inspectable proof: scientific blueprint structure, a bounded ledger grid, evidence labels set in Helvetica caps. Preserve the canvas, the face, the open space, the proof boundaries, and the single dark recommendation. Add no new palette. The rejected directions (dark cinematic motion photography; linen, brushed metal, amber warmth) require a rebrand and risk category sameness.

## Proof surfaces

Evidence crops, source labels, proof-status fields (supported / qualified review / outside supplied proof), and annotated margin notes live one click deeper, in Featured content and proof artifacts. The top-of-profile scan leads with the decision, never the documentation. No warning colors, shields, checks, badges, or compliance theater.

## Portrait

Only the original supplied portrait from the private identity add-on. Crop and uniform scale only. No regeneration, retouching, filtering, or AI upscaling. Portraits are an occasional authority or story device, never required.

## What we stand against

Serif type, italics, outlined type, decorative quotation marks, faux small caps, luxury tracking. Gradients, shadows, rounded pills, badges, seals, ornamental icons, contours, card-heavy layouts. Decorative route colors. Stock supplement imagery as credibility. Stale approval labels on public assets. Logos for the deliverable parts (Decision Book, Angle Cards, Build First Card). Route lines used as decoration when no choice is being compared. Any fall-through to a default palette.
