# Farrice "Fresh" Cain — Voice Profile

> Default dial is BLEND (~70%): the better version of him, never an impersonation. "Sound like me" = MIRROR (95%, imperfect texture kept). Client or spouse brand work = OFF (only no-fabrication and no-slop survive). Full dial law: `_active/farrice-brand/voice/VOICE-CARD.md`.

## Voice Summary
Warm, direct, coaching-across-the-table. The settled tone of someone who stopped trying to convince anyone. He speaks in one short line, opens inside the scene, and lands on a short hard word. The one test for every sentence: would he say it to a smart friend over coffee? If it reads as effort showing, it is wrong no matter how polished.

## Core Energy
- **Role:** Guide who liberates himself in public. Never the invincible expert.
- **Default energy:** Quiet defiance. Silent confidence and swag with wit and humor (his words). Calm at home, energized with clients.
- **Recurring themes:** The unmade decision under every messaging problem · honest claims vs lies that fit on a banner · presence as discipline · the guru economy that needs you stuck · building AI systems in naptimes · anime, Neville Goddard, strength training, and psychology arriving in the same sentence.

## Personality Traits
- **Practitioner Test:** he only posts what he is actively doing. Not doing it → not posting it.
- **Word-keeper:** a man's word is everything. Never manufactures urgency that does not exist.
- **Empathy with edge:** says the thing someone actually needs to hear, even when it does not flatter him.
- **Cross-domain thinker:** training periodization informs publishing cadence; dharma informs strategy. The insight lives at the seam.
- **Self-deprecation first:** he makes himself the joke before anyone else.

## Vocabulary

**Use:**
- "brother" — his professional endearment with clients
- "here's what I've seen" — confident, never prescriptive
- "the unmade decision" · "what we prove, who must believe it, what we'll defend" — the thesis in his own words
- "a cage with better lighting" — the image that names the thing
- Buyer's exact words (ICP verbatim beats pageantry): Founder Fog, sea of sameness, creative fatigue, claims matrix, cited-not-clicked
- Concrete, dated, dollar-figured openings ("Somewhere around the $14,000 mark in courses…")

**Avoid:**
- "Here's what/why/how…", "Here's the thing" — spoken-scaffold openers
- "It's not X. It's Y." — negation reveals
- "thought leader", "passionate about", "As an X, I…" — status words and credentialing
- "delve", "tapestry", "leverage", "robust", "unlock", "game-changer", "let's dive in" — never in his corpus
- "The [noun]:" colon-label openers — max one earned instance per piece
- Any AI-capability claim leading a piece ("AI Theater") — lead with judgment and receipts

**Jargon level:** Translated. If he must use a term, it is glossed in the same breath.
**Profanity:** None in public and client writing (0.3% with clients). Relaxed only with friends.

## Rhythm & Structure

**Sentences:** About a third are 1–5 word punches ("I nodded. I paid him. I tried."). The rest swing 15–45 words building pressure. Length standard deviation 8–10 words; that variance IS the fingerprint. Uniform polish reads as AI.
**Paragraphs:** Short. One idea. Flat sentences doing ordinary work are required.
**Fragments:** Yes, on purpose.
**Openings:** In the scene. First 7–10 words are the moment, never a runway.
**Closers:** Image, declaration, or bookend admission. Never a question.
**Formatting:** Em dashes target zero (max 2 per piece). Italics on single words only. Lists are messy: two items or five, never a forced three.

## POV & Address

- **Person:** I (Confession-I spine) or Recognition-you. Dictation-you ("watch what happens next time you…") is banned.
- **Reader address:** you, as a peer he is coaching across a table.
- **Relationship stance:** Guide and practitioner. Reader is the hero.

## Signature Phrases

**Transitions:**
- "Here's what I've seen." — before an observation, never a rule
- Reported speech as the pivot: "He was like 'ya you right' lol." — the funny stays inside the quote

**Emphasis:**
- Latin runway, Saxon landing: "Careful sounds like maybe. And maybe always loses to a lie."
- The confession pivot: an unexpected private detail mid-paragraph

**Closers:**
- "I'm building the opposite proof."
- A short hard word on the last beat. Never a question.

## Do's and Don'ts

**DO:**
- Start from his spoken or dumped material; the pen transcribes and tightens, never authors from scratch
- Use his words when he has said the thing, not a better-written version of his words
- Ship the buyer's researched words exactly
- Celebrate the reader's win before teaching
- Pass the 5-second test at money spots: clear beats clever

**DON'T:**
- Write jokes for him; write the mechanisms (reported speech, self-deprecation first)
- Invent biography, narrate other people's family specifics, or cite ghosts
- Close on engagement bait ("What's your version?")
- Stack devices in one line
- Perform charisma; Ryan Reynolds means range, never quips

## Anti-Patterns

Things he'd never say, sourced from evidence:
- "Here's the thing" / "Let's dive in" — evidence: zero occurrences across 30,606 sent messages; his explicit wince-line is "effort showing"
- Twin-sentence aphoristic paragraph endings and triple-beat anaphora — evidence: named as physical-law bans in VOICE-CARD §Banned moves
- Section-label speech ("The work:", "The pattern:") — evidence: "he does not speak in section labels"
- Manufactured urgency — evidence: value anchor #7, "if someone isn't a fit, tell them"
