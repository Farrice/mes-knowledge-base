## The job
Take a coaching client's neglected website and a dormant lead-referral relationship to two things he can act on: a premium, mobile-first redesign preview of the client's site (experiential, scroll-animated, "instant trust" register) and a win-win referral proposal for the domain owner who is still passing leads — with the voice memo and the current site's real numbers read first, and nothing spent, sent, or published without his say.

## Sub-jobs / lanes
- L1 Recover the ask — pull the client's voice memo and last messages (iMessage on his Mac, `imessage` tooling if present; else he pastes); write the client's own words as the brief [parallel]
- L2 Site as it stands — crawl the client's live site: pages, load, mobile render, copy, offers, CTAs, analytics if reachable; Lighthouse-style numbers with receipts [parallel]
- L3 Referral site as it stands — the domain owner's site: domain rating, traffic estimate, lead paths, what a lead sees; the finder's-fee arrangement as he described it [parallel]
- L4 Deal shapes — three win-win structures for owner + client (+ him): lease/rev-share, managed-lead-gen for a fee, buy-later option; unit math on each with the client's lifetime value; the one to propose and why [after L3]
- L5 Design direction — load the design skills (`/mkt-visual-identity`, reference-led creative system, Style Vault) and the client's brand; one locked direction with two references, mobile-first, scroll motion named [after L1, L2]
- L6 Preview build — a static preview of the redesigned home + one service page (HTML, no CMS), on a local port with a mobile render; scroll animation working; copy in the client's voice [after L5]
- L7 Proposal packet — one page each: the client-site redesign scope + what it costs him (time, tools), and the referral proposal for the domain owner in plain words; both stop before send [after L4, L6]

## Ask me first
- Q: Which is first for the client — the site redesign or the referral deal? · look first: the voice memo (L1) usually says
- Q: The referral arrangement's numbers — finder's fee amount and the client's lifetime value per signed lead? · look first: L1 messages; if absent, ask in one line
- Q: Are we designing inside the client's current platform or replacing it? · look first: L2 (platform detection)
- Q: Does he want the domain-owner proposal to include him as a party or only the client? · look first: his ask (2026-09-10: "a way for us all to win") — confirm only if the deal shapes differ

## Handles alone
Crawls, measurements, domain and traffic lookups, design-skill loading, the preview build on a local port, unit math, drafting both proposals.

## Comes back when
- The voice memo is unreachable by tooling (packet: paste it, or grant access)
- Deal math says no structure is win-win at the stated numbers (packet with the gap)
- The design direction is a taste fork (two references, one tap)
- Anything that would contact the client or the domain owner

## Needs approval
Sending anything to the client or the domain owner · any paid tool (ads, hosting, stock, scraping credits) · publishing the preview anywhere public · touching the client's live site.

## Needs
iMessage access or the pasted memo · the client's site URL and platform · the domain owner's site URL · design skills on disk · a local port for the preview.

## When it breaks
| Failure | Keep-going move | Ask when |
|---|---|---|
| iMessage not readable | proceed on L2/L3/L5 with his ask as the brief; L1 blocked with a packet | immediately, one line |
| site blocks the crawl | screenshot pass via the browser pane; numbers marked UNCONFIRMED | never |
| domain metrics tool unavailable | use free DR estimate + his description; label LIKELY | never |
| design skill missing | fall back to reference-led-creative-system alone; name the gap in the receipt | never |
| deal math has no win-win | present the closest two with the gap named | always — that is the packet |

## Done means
Voice memo transcribed and quoted in the brief · site numbers with receipts · domain-owner site numbers with receipts · one recommended deal shape with math · a preview he can open on his phone · two one-page proposals, unsent · trace shows what was measured and what was skipped.
