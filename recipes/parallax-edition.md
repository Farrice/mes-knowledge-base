## The job
From a raw idea or nothing at all to a publish-ready Parallax edition — essay, prompt pack, Notes trailer batch, LinkedIn cross-promo — anti-slop certified, nothing sent to Substack without him.

## Sub-jobs / lanes
- L1 Research — trending-topic research per `.agent/workflows/parallax.md` Phase 1 [parallel]
- L2 Brief — his raw take captured before drafting [after: L1]
- L3 Draft — 800 to 1200 word edition, Parallax voice, anti-slop certified Phase 3 [after: L2]
- L4 Prompt pack — single-prompt coaching session, Nate B Jones / IP Flywheel standard, Phase 4 [after: L3]
- L5 Notes — 5 Substack Notes trailer batch, Phase 4.5 archetypes [after: L3]
- L6 LinkedIn cross promo — 3 posts (insight variant, prompt teaser, output screenshot), Phase 5 [after: L3]
- L7 Package — canonical ship file `parallax-packages/0N-[slug].md`, Phase 5.5 [after: L4, L5, L6]

## Ask me first
- Q: Topic already decided, or should research surface the opportunity? · look first: `.agent/workflows/parallax.md` Quick Start flags
- Q: His raw take on the thesis (the brief HALT) · look first: `_active/farrice-brand/` thought-bank inbox

## Handles alone
Research, anti-slop drafting, prompt pack engineering, Notes batch, LinkedIn cross-promo drafts, package assembly, `execution/prose_classifier.py check`.

## Comes back when
- The raw-take HALT before drafting (mandatory, once)
- Two rejected drafts of the essay → the brief is the problem, no third take
- A private fact or client-identifying detail would appear in the edition

## Needs approval
Publishing the edition to Substack · posting the LinkedIn cross-promo · sending any Note.

## Needs
`.agent/workflows/parallax.md` · `_active/linkedin/CAMPAIGN.md` (cross-promo queue) · `directives/ai-slop-ban-bank.md` · `execution/prose_classifier.py`.

## When it breaks
| Failure | Keep-going move | Ask when |
|---|---|---|
| research thin or stale | fall back to `--quick` with his raw take only | never invent a trend |
| draft fails anti-slop check twice | back to the brief, not a third take | second miss |
| LinkedIn cross-promo has no open queue slot | hold as a draft, note it in the receipt | never auto-post |

## Done means
`parallax-packages/0N-[slug].md` complete and one-scroll reviewable · `execution/prose_classifier.py check` clean · Notes and LinkedIn drafts staged, nothing sent.
