## HEAT MANAGEMENT PROTOCOL

Weekend 2 is HOT. This is your primary risk, not dust.

### The 30-Minute Rule
Every 30 minutes in direct sun, do a quick check:
1. **Is he drinking?** Offer water every time you check
2. **Is his skin cool to the touch?** If hot/dry, wet a cooling towel immediately
3. **Is he peeing normally?** If no wet diaper in 2+ hours in this heat, he's dehydrating
4. **Is he irritable beyond normal?** Heat exhaustion makes toddlers go quiet or inconsolable

### Shade & Cooling Strategy
No shade canopies allowed — but parasols ARE. Your shade and cooling sources:
- **Parasol** (officially allowed — your #1 portable shade tool)
- **Stroller canopy** (secondary shade)
- **Inside the tented stages** (Sahara, Yuma, Mojave, Gobi are all tented — cooler inside)
- **The Bunker** (near Sahara Tent) — AIR CONDITIONED. Movie screenings inside. This is your emergency cool-down spot on Saturday when you're in the south zone
- **Camp Lounge** — AC, nap area, Wi-Fi. Great for a toddler reset
- **Red Bull Mirage Club** — misting fans
- **Art installations** with overhead elements
- **Building structures** near food vendors and restrooms
- **Your blanket** (lay it in the shadow of any structure)
- **Water refill stations** — free, multiple locations. Fill up and use water misters

**Pro tip for EDM day**: Sahara and Yuma are TENTED stages. You're naturally in shade while watching sets. This is another reason to stay in the southern EDM zone on Saturday — you're literally inside structures.

### Emergency Signs (Leave Immediately)
- He stops sweating despite being hot
- Vomiting
- Confusion or unusual lethargy
- Rapid breathing
- Skin that's hot and red

These are heat emergency signs. Don't wait. Go to a medical tent (there are several) or leave.

## CROWD MANAGEMENT — Staying on the Perimeter

You mentioned wanting to stay back from the crowd. Here's how:

### The "Back 100" Rule
Stay at least 100 feet back from any stage. At this distance:
- **Sound is still great** but not damaging (even with ear muffs on him)
- **You have space** — the crowd thins dramatically past the first 50 feet
- **You can exit in any direction** without pushing through people
- **He can stand/walk/dance** without getting bumped

### Where Crowds Are Thinnest
1. **Rear-left or rear-right of any stage** — most people cluster center
2. **Between stages** during set transitions — the fields empty as crowds move
3. **Early afternoon everywhere** (1-4pm) — lowest attendance of the day
4. **Yuma tent rear** — Yuma draws a more chill, dance-focused crowd. Less pushing
5. **Art installation areas** — people stop briefly and move on. Rarely crowded

### Where to AVOID with a Stroller
- **Front 50 feet of Sahara at night** — densest, most chaotic crowd on the grounds
- **The Main Stage → Sahara walking path after 9pm** — bottleneck, packed, slow
- **Any stage during headliner transitions** — everyone moves at once
- **DoLab during surprise guest rumors** — can go from empty to packed in 5 minutes

5. **"Phone battery protocol."** Agree to keep phones above 50% at all times. You need them for: finding each other if you split up, calling rideshare in emergency, GPS to find the car, photos. Bring a portable charger.

## THE HONEST EXPECTATIONS

You two are seasoned. You know what Coachella is. Here's what changes with a 2.5-year-old:

**You will see 3-5 acts per day, max.** That's a great day. Don't stack the schedule or you'll spend the whole time speed-walking between stages with a screaming toddler in a stroller.

**Art installations > stages for him.** Giant glowing sculptures, moving parts, colors, textures — this is toddler Disneyland. Budget more time here than you think. His favorite memory (and yours, honestly) will probably be him touching some art piece and losing his mind with joy.

**Saturday is YOUR day. Sunday is HIS day.** Protect this distinction. Saturday you're optimizing for EDM. Sunday you're optimizing for family magic. Friday is the heat day — play it conservative.

**The food lines are long and expensive.** Budget $50-80/day for food inside. Lines are 15-30 minutes. Eat during less popular set times, not during transitions when everyone else is eating.

**He will get dirty.** Accept this. Embrace this. He's at Coachella. Let him be dirty.

**One of you will have a moment.** A set you've waited years to see, playing 200 feet away while you're pushing a stroller in the other direction because he's done. Name it now, grieve it for 10 seconds, and keep moving. This trip isn't about seeing everything. It's about experiencing something together that you'll talk about for years.

**The best moments will be unplanned.** Him dancing to something random. The sunset hitting the art while Bedouin plays in the distance. Jen's face when she hears her favorite drop. You'll know them when they happen. Put the phone down for those.
