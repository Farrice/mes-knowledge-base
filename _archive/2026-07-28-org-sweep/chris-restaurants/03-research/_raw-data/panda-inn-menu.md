## Critical Observations
1. **Two-portion menu structure** — Most entrees offer "small/large" or "regular/family" sizes (referenced in Allmenus as "$17/$33" for Mongolian Beef, "$21/$40" for Honey Walnut Shrimp). Delivery apps hide this by showing "Priced by add-ons."
2. **Hot & Sour Soup is the anchor item** — Most-ordered, smallest ticket ($5.50), gets called out by name in 9 out of 30 review tags.
3. **Heavy Wagyu/USDA Prime call-outs** — Asian Spiced Beef Ribs (USDA prime), Wagyu Chowfun upcharge — premium proteins are present but underpromoted in delivery.
4. **No clear lunch special pricing on delivery apps** — In-store has dedicated lunch specials (per pandainn.com/menu_type/lunch-specials/), but they're not surfaced on Uber Eats/Postmates. This is a delivery channel monetization gap.
5. **No Peking Duck on the live menu** — Despite being a heritage Chinese-American positioning play, Panda Inn Glendale does NOT offer Peking Duck. It offers Tea-Smoked Duck and Mango Duck Salad instead.
6. **Catering menu exists** but items not exposed publicly — separate B2B/event channel.
