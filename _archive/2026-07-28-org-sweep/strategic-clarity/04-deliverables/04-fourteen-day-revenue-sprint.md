# 14-Day Revenue Sprint Plan
*Rebuilt 2026-03-30 — Grounded in Perplexity Deep Research*

## The 3-Front Attack (Validated by Research)

Based on [windmillgrowth.com](https://windmillgrowth.com/blogseo/linkedin-ghostwriter-cost) and solopreneur launch case studies from [financialmodelslab.com](https://financialmodelslab.com/blogs/operating-costs/professional-ghostwriting):

| Front | Target Revenue | Channel | Conversion Data |
|-------|---------------|---------|----------------|
| **LinkedIn DM Bundles** | $4,000–$6,000 | LinkedIn outreach | 100+ targeted DMs → 2–3 closes at $2K starter retainer |
| **Platform Launches** | $2,500–$5,000 | Upwork/Reedsy | Fixed-scope productized listing → 1–2 closes in 10 days |
| **Proof Run Demos** | $1,000–$2,000 | Personal network + LinkedIn content | 2–4 proof runs at $500 each |

**Total sprint target: $5,000–$10,000 in 14 days**

---

## Day-by-Day Execution

### Days 1–3: Foundation
**Goal: Build the machine before turning it on**

- [ ] **Day 1**: Write 3 "Demo-in-Public" LinkedIn posts showing your AI ghostwriting process (screen recordings of Antigravity producing voice-matched content)
- [ ] **Day 1**: Create $500 Proof Run landing page (simple Notion page or single-page site)
- [ ] **Day 2**: Build prospect list — 100 coaches on LinkedIn earning $100K+ (look for: active posting but inconsistent quality, 500+ followers, coaching certification in bio)
- [ ] **Day 2**: Create Upwork profile optimized for "LinkedIn ghostwriting for coaches" with 3 portfolio samples
- [ ] **Day 3**: Write 5 "unsolicited demo" posts — take real coaches' public content and rewrite it in a better version (anonymized), showing before/after

### Days 4–7: Outreach Blitz
**Goal: Generate 10–15 warm conversations**

- [ ] **Day 4**: Send 25 personalized LinkedIn DMs to prospect list. Template: *"I noticed your posts about [coaching topic]. I built an AI system that writes in coaches' voices — here's what your content could look like: [link to unsolicited demo]. Would a free sample be useful?"*
- [ ] **Day 5**: Send 25 more DMs. Post another Demo-in-Public post.
- [ ] **Day 6**: Send 25 more DMs. Apply to 5 Upwork coaching/ghostwriting gigs with customized proposals.
- [ ] **Day 7**: Send 25 more DMs (total: 100). Follow up on Day 4 conversations. Post Demo-in-Public #3.

**Expected conversion**: 100 DMs → 15–20 responses → 5–8 interested → 2–3 Proof Run sales
**Source**: LinkedIn DM bundles converting at 2–3% for ghostwriting services ([windmillgrowth.com](https://windmillgrowth.com/blogseo/linkedin-ghostwriter-cost))

### Days 8–10: Delivery & Conversion
**Goal: Deliver Proof Runs so well they can't say no to a retainer**

- [ ] **Day 8**: Deliver first Proof Run (voice capture → 4 posts in 48 hours using Antigravity)
- [ ] **Day 9**: Deliver second Proof Run. Post client reaction screenshot (with permission) as social proof.
- [ ] **Day 10**: Pitch retainer to Proof Run clients. Framework: *"You saw what I delivered in 5 days. At $3K/month, you get 12–16 posts like these, every month, without lifting a finger."*

### Days 11–14: Close & Compound
**Goal: Convert trials to retainers + close Upwork/platform deals**

- [ ] **Day 11**: Follow up on all open conversations. Send 3 more Upwork proposals.
- [ ] **Day 12**: Close first retainer (target: $2,500–$3,000/month). Celebrate. Post about it.
- [ ] **Day 13**: Deliver any remaining Proof Runs. Follow up on Upwork applications.
- [ ] **Day 14**: Revenue check. Retarget remaining warm leads with a time-limited "Founding Client" rate (10% discount for 3-month commitment).

## Revenue Math

**Conservative scenario** (2 proof runs + 1 retainer + 1 Upwork gig):
- 2 × $500 Proof Runs = $1,000
- 1 × $2,500 retainer = $2,500
- 1 × $2,000 Upwork project = $2,000
- **Total: $5,500**

**Aggressive scenario** (4 proof runs + 2 retainers + 1 Upwork):
- 4 × $500 = $2,000
- 2 × $3,000 = $6,000
- 1 × $2,500 = $2,500
- **Total: $10,500**

## The Key Lever: "Results in 30 Days or Refund"

Research shows the single highest-converting element for new ghostwriters is the **money-back guarantee**. Mid-tier ghostwriters offering "Results in 30 days or refund" close at **2–3x the rate** of those without ([windmillgrowth.com](https://windmillgrowth.com/blogseo/linkedin-ghostwriter-cost)).

Offer this on Tier 2 retainers for the first 3 clients. You'll never have to honor it if the Proof Run already demonstrated quality.
