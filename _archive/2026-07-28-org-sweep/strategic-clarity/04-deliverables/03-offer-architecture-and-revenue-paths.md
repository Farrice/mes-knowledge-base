## Pricing Architecture: Research-Validated Tiers

Based on [windmillgrowth.com](https://windmillgrowth.com/blogseo/linkedin-ghostwriter-cost), [financialmodelslab.com](https://financialmodelslab.com/blogs/operating-costs/professional-ghostwriting), and [scribemedia.com](https://scribemedia.com/how-much-does-ghostwriting-cost):

### Tier 1 — Proof Run (Entry Point)
**$500 one-time** | Timeline: 5 days

What they get:
- 5-dimension voice profiling (1 hour voice capture)
- 4 LinkedIn posts written in their voice (2 text, 1 carousel script, 1 story-format)
- Voice fidelity scorecard showing how close the match is

Why this works:
- Research shows coaches' #1 fear is "will it sound like me?" — the Proof Run eliminates that risk
- At $500, it sits below even the lowest monthly retainer ($1,500), making it a no-brainer trial
- Converts to Tier 2 at 40–60% based on Nicolas Cole's free-consulting methodology

---

### Tier 2 — Monthly Retainer (Core Revenue)
**$2,500–$4,000/month** | Minimum 3-month commitment

What they get (at $3,000/mo):
- 12–16 LinkedIn posts per month (voice-matched, psychology-informed)
- Content strategy aligned to coaching sales pipeline
- Monthly performance review with engagement-to-inquiry tracking
- Commenting strategy guide (not execution — that stays with them)

Why this pricing:
- Market benchmark for mid-tier LinkedIn ghostwriters: **$2,000–$4,000/month for 12–16 posts** ([windmillgrowth.com](https://windmillgrowth.com/blogseo/linkedin-ghostwriter-cost))
- You deliver at the same price point but with coaching psychology depth that generic ghostwriters can't match
- At $3K/mo × 4 clients = **$12K/month recurring revenue** — sustainable solo-operator income

---

### Tier 3 — Premium Authority Package
**$5,000–$7,500/month** | For coaches at $250K+ revenue

What they get:
- 16–20 LinkedIn posts per month
- Weekly newsletter (ghostwritten, voice-matched)
- Quarterly content strategy refresh
- AI-powered content repurposing (posts → email sequences → carousel scripts)
- Priority response (24-hour turnaround)

Why this tier:
- Coaches at $250K+ are spending **$4,000–$7,500/month** on content already ([Perplexity research, cultureimpact.co.uk](https://www.cultureimpact.co.uk))
- They're currently splitting that across 2–3 providers (ghostwriter + VA + strategist) — you consolidate into one
- 2 Premium clients = **$10K–$15K/month**

---

### Tier 4 — AI Systems Consulting (Add-On)
**$2,500–$5,000 one-time project** | 2-week delivery

What they get:
- Custom AI content workflow built on their tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Notion)
- Voice-tuned prompt library for self-serve content between retainer deliverables
- Training session (recorded) on how to use the system
- 30 days of async support for troubleshooting

Why this works:
- Coaches are spending $50–$200/month on AI tools with no strategy — this is a productized "intelligence upgrade"
- Positions you as a strategic partner, not just a writer
- Can be offered as an upsell to Tier 2 clients

## Revenue Path Comparison

| Path | Time to First Dollar | Monthly Revenue at 3 Months | Scalability | Risk |
|------|---------------------|---------------------------|-------------|------|
| **Proof Runs only** | 1–2 weeks | $2K–$4K (4–8 proof runs) | Low ceiling | Low |
| **Retainers (Tier 2)** | 2–4 weeks | $6K–$12K (2–4 clients) | Medium — capped at 5–6 clients solo | Medium |
| **Retainers + Premium** | 4–8 weeks | $10K–$20K | High — fewer clients, higher revenue | Medium |
| **Full stack (all tiers)** | 4–8 weeks | $12K–$25K | Highest — diversified, recurring + project | Requires strong pipeline |

**Recommended path**: Start with Proof Runs → convert to Tier 2 retainers → add Tier 3 as portfolio builds → layer Tier 4 consulting when demand exists.
