## The One Filter: "Does This Generate Coaching Client Revenue?"

After 18+ sessions of research, extraction, and system-building, the clutter isn't in your system — it's in your attention. Your Antigravity system works. Your skills work. Your research is deep. What's been missing is a **decision filter** that prevents you from starting new workstreams before the current one generates money.

### The 3-Question Gate

Before starting ANY new work session, ask:

1. **Is this a Proof Run, retainer delivery, or outreach?** → Do it immediately.
2. **Is this preparing for a Proof Run, retainer, or outreach?** → Do it, but timebox to 1 hour.
3. **Is this neither?** → Write it down and do it *after* you have 3 paying clients.

That's it. Everything else is a distraction dressed as progress.

## Daily Operating Rhythm (1–3 Hour Windows)

Based on your compressed work window reality:

### Morning Block (30 min)
- Check LinkedIn DMs and engagement on yesterday's post
- Respond to all warm conversations within 2 hours of waking
- Review today's task from the sprint plan

### Execution Block (60–90 min)
This block does ONE of these — never more than one:

| Priority | Activity | Frequency |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| **P1** | Deliver client work (Proof Runs, retainer posts) | Daily when clients exist |
| **P2** | Outreach (LinkedIn DMs, Upwork proposals) | Daily until 3+ clients |
| **P3** | Content creation (Demo-in-Public posts) | 3x per week |
| **P4** | System improvement (new workflows, extractions) | 1x per week MAX |

### Close Block (15 min)
- Update sprint tracker (what got done today)
- Queue tomorrow's single priority
- Post or schedule one LinkedIn post

```
│   ├── content/          ← Content strategy, workflows, drafts
│   ├── offers/           ← All offer architecture, scripts, playbooks
│   ├── research/         ← Market research, competitive intel
│   └── icp-intelligence/ ← Client avatar work
│
├── linkedin-launch/      ← Your LinkedIn execution engine
│   ├── posts/            ← Actual post drafts
│   ├── outreach/         ← DM templates, prospect lists
│   ├── voice-captures/   ← Voice profiling work
│   └── [etc.]
│
└── strategic-clarity/    ← These 5 documents (you are here)
```

### Rules for keeping it clean:

1. **New personal work goes in `_active/`** — always in the right subfolder
2. **Completed work gets moved to `_archive/`** with a date stamp
3. **System work (skills, agents, directives) stays where it is** — never mix personal outputs with system architecture
4. **One project at a time in your execution block** — don't context-switch between writing client content, doing outreach, AND building new workflows in the same session

## The Metric That Matters

Track **one number** daily: **warm conversations generated**.

Not posts created. Not followers gained. Not workflows built. Not sessions completed.

**Warm conversations** — because they're the leading indicator of revenue. If you're having 3+ new conversations per day about your ghostwriting service, the revenue will follow. If you're having zero, no amount of system-building will save you.
