## Day-by-Day Execution

### DAYS 1-2 (March 30-31): LAUNCH ALL CHANNELS

**Morning (1 hour):**
- [ ] Set up 3-4 Fiverr gigs: LinkedIn posts, brand messaging, content strategy, AI market research
- [ ] Create Upwork profile → apply to 10+ relevant jobs (ghostwriting, content strategy, LinkedIn)
- [ ] Create the ICP Deep Dive offer page (simple landing page or PDF)

**Afternoon (1 hour):**
- [ ] LinkedIn: Identify 20 target coaches using criteria:
  - 2K-30K followers
  - Posts inconsistently or at B-level quality
  - Real methodology visible
  - Health/wellness, business, career, or leadership coaches
- [ ] Run first 2 unsolicited demos (rewrite their weakest posts using GVE)

**Evening (30 min):**
- [ ] DM 10 coaches using warm outreach scripts
- [ ] Post on own LinkedIn about the ICP Deep Dive service
- [ ] Apply to 10 more Upwork jobs

### DAYS 3-4: DEMO BLITZ + FOLLOW-UP

**Morning (1 hour):**
- [ ] Run 3 more unsolicited demos (5 total)
- [ ] Send demos via DM: "I rewrote a couple of your posts — want to see?"

**Afternoon (1 hour):**
- [ ] Respond to Fiverr/Upwork inquiries
- [ ] Start delivering any won platform projects
- [ ] Post ICP offer on Twitter/X, Reddit (r/Entrepreneur, r/smallbusiness), Facebook groups

**Evening (30 min):**
- [ ] DM 5 more coaches
- [ ] Follow up on Day 1-2 DMs (CRITICAL — 80% of sales happen on follow-up)
- [ ] Apply to 10 more Upwork jobs
- [ ] Cold email 25 coaches using Apollo.io free tier

### DAYS 5-7: CLOSE + DELIVER

**Each day (2-3 hours total):**
- [ ] Deliver first Proof Run or freelance project
- [ ] Close ICP research clients
- [ ] Cold email 25 more coaches (50 total by Day 7)
- [ ] Follow up ALL previous DMs and emails (second touch)
- [ ] Accept every reasonable freelance project — no cherry-picking this week

**Revenue checkpoint by Day 7:** $1,500-$3,000 collected or invoiced

### DAYS 8-10: DELIVER + SCALE OUTREACH

**Each day (2-3 hours):**
- [ ] Deliver remaining Proof Runs and ICP research packages
- [ ] Request payment upfront via Stripe/PayPal/Venmo before calls
- [ ] Send third follow-up touch to all prospects who haven't responded
- [ ] Post methodology demonstrations on own LinkedIn (show the work)

### DAYS 11-14: CLOSE + COLLECT

**Each day (2-3 hours):**
- [ ] Close remaining deals
- [ ] Deliver final projects
- [ ] Convert successful Proof Runs to "The Voice" retainer proposals ($2,500/mo)
- [ ] Collect all payments

## Non-Negotiable Rules for 14 Days

1. **Ship 80% quality in 2 hours, not 100% quality in 8 hours.** Perfection is the enemy of revenue.
2. **Payment upfront.** No "pay after review." PayPal, Venmo, Stripe, CashApp.
3. **Run all 3 fronts simultaneously.** Don't put all eggs in one basket.
4. **Follow up relentlessly.** DM once → follow up Day 3 → follow up Day 5.
5. **Leverage Antigravity for speed.** AI does 70%, you do the 30% requiring human judgment.
6. **Say yes to everything reasonable.** $200 for 2 hours of work? Do it. Volume this week.
7. **Track every dollar.** Create a simple spreadsheet: Prospect | Offer | Status | Amount | Date Collected.

## Key Insight

> **The math works: 3 Proof Runs ($1,500-$2,250) + 2-3 ICP packages ($1,000-$2,250) + 3-5 freelance gigs ($500-$1,500) = $3,000-$6,000. Your AI system is the force multiplier — you can deliver in hours what takes competitors days. The constraint isn't capability. It's outreach volume. Spend 60% of your time on outreach and 40% on delivery for the first 7 days, then flip to 40/60 for days 8-14.**
