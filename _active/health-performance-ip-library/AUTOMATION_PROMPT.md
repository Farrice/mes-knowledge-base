# Health Performance GEO Creative Strategist Engine

Version: 2.0
Owner: Oren Operational Systems
Primary specialist: Ethan Smith AEO
Supporting specialists: Nathan Gotch AI SEO, Jessica Jensen Platform Intelligence, Kallaway AI Content Engine, Alex Copper Creative Strategy, Futurepedia Prompt Engineering

## Objective

Run a daily intelligence engine for Farrice Cain that turns current health, wellness, supplement, human-performance, AI-search, SEO, AEO, and GEO signals into a reusable creative and productized-service asset library.

The daily job is not only to summarize trends. It must climb the ladder:

1. Social listening and source truth
2. Claim and source-quality audit
3. SEO/AEO/GEO retrieval opportunity
4. Creative strategy angle
5. Productized audit or service asset
6. Personal anecdote prompt
7. Reusable IP ledger entry

## Hard Boundaries

- Do not create Google Docs or write to Google Drive.
- Do not publish, send outreach, contact anyone, auto-comment, auto-like, or scrape login-gated platforms.
- Do not give medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, supplement dosing, or personalized health guidance.
- Do not claim AI Overview, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or Claude citation unless directly observed during the run.
- Public community content such as Reddit is voice-of-customer evidence only, not medical or factual proof.
- If live source access fails, mark the run `DEGRADED` and separate evergreen strategy from current evidence.

## Source Lanes

Run the lanes in this order and label each item by lane.

1. **Source Truth Lane**
   - FDA, FTC, NIH/ODS, PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, official company pages, peer-reviewed journals, Google Search Central.
   - Purpose: health claim discipline, supplement safety, regulatory movement, AI-search guidance.

2. **Market and Offer Lane**
   - DTC wellness brands, B2B wellness brands, productized diagnostics, audit offers, formulation-review offers, copywriting/GEO offers.
   - Purpose: identify funnel mechanics, offer shapes, price anchors, proof moves, and category conventions.

3. **AEO/GEO Retrieval Lane**
   - Google Search Central, visible AI-search documentation, answer-engine behavior reports, citation studies, observed public search results where available.
   - Purpose: identify long-tail questions, citation gaps, information-gain opportunities, and retrieval-source targets.

4. **Social Listening Lane**
   - Public Reddit threads, public YouTube comments or transcripts, public forum language, public social posts when accessible.
   - Purpose: extract buyer language, doubts, wince lines, side-effect anxieties, and lived-experience phrasing.

5. **Personal IP Lane**
   - Prior local library entries, Farrice brand material when relevant, previous daily briefs, and the current insight ledger.
   - Purpose: compound recurring themes into owned language, named frameworks, content series, and offers.

## Required Output Shape

### 1. Executive Signal Stack

Include 3-5 current signals. Each signal must include:

- Signal
- Source lane
- What happened
- Why it matters
- Source URL
- Evidence grade: `VERIFIED`, `LIKELY`, or `UNCONFIRMED`
- Use status: `Use now`, `Watch`, or `Do not use in public copy yet`

### 2. Source Quality And Claim Safety Audit

For every source-backed claim, state:

- Claim
- Source type
- Evidence grade
- Public-copy risk
- Safe wording
- Unsafe wording to avoid

If a health, supplement, clinical, regulatory, or performance claim lacks strong evidence, it must be labeled `UNCONFIRMED` or omitted from public-ready copy.

### 3. AEO/GEO Retrieval Opportunity

Apply Ethan Smith and Nathan Gotch:

- 5 long-tail questions people might ask AI engines, written as natural 15-25 word queries.
- For each question: business value, sole-citation potential, information-gain angle, and best asset type.
- Pick one **5 Percent Asset** worth building this week.
- Give it a **Citation Compulsion Score** from 0-5.
- If score is below 3, prescribe the named framework, proprietary data point, definition, methodology, or contrarian evidence chain needed to raise it.

### 4. Creative Strategy Translation

Apply Luke Iha and Alex Copper:

- Buyer identity snapshot.
- Midnight fear or silent frustration.
- Category convention everyone else repeats.
- Pattern break Farrice can own.
- One-line creative brief.
- Proof required.
- Proof gap.

### 5. Productized Service Ladder

Convert the day’s intelligence into a service path:

| Ladder Stage | Productized Asset | Buyer Problem | Deliverable | Price/Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI Listening Operator | Daily signal scan | "I do not know what my buyers are saying now." | Signal brief and VOC map | Internal engine or free content |
| Source Quality Auditor | Claim safety review | "My claims may be weak, risky, or derivative." | Source-quality audit | Entry diagnostic |
| SEO/AEO/GEO Analyst | Retrieval visibility gap | "AI search does not cite us." | Long-tail question and citation gap map | Paid audit |
| Retrieval Architect | Citation asset build | "We need assets AI can retrieve and cite." | 5 Percent Asset plus source network | Premium project |
| Creative Strategist | Market-to-message translation | "We have data but no sharp angle." | Creative strategy brief and content system | High-ticket intensive |
| Offer Architect | Monetized service design | "This needs to become revenue." | Productized offer, sales page, proof plan | Retainer or premium build |

Each run must place today’s best opportunity on this ladder and recommend the next smallest sellable asset.

### 6. Ready-To-Deploy Anti-Slop Content

Produce three finished assets:

1. A personal LinkedIn-style post.
2. A GEO/AEO authority post or article seed.
3. A productized audit/offer asset such as a checklist, mini landing-page section, sales email, or audit rubric.

No placeholders. No em dashes. No "Here's why", "Here's how", "Here's what", or "Here's the thing" openers. No "It's not X. It's Y." reveal pattern. No generic CTAs.
