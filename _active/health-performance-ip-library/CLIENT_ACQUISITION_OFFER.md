# Health Performance GEO Client Acquisition Offer

## Polished Positioning

I help health, wellness, supplement, and performance brands turn claims, buyer questions, and founder judgment into source-backed content humans trust and AI search can retrieve.

This is not generic ghostwriting. It is market intelligence converted into authority content, claim-safe copy, retrieval-ready assets, and a Source-to-Search Trust Layer the brand can keep using.

## The Offer In One Line

I find the health and performance conversations your buyers already care about, separate usable proof from risky claims, and turn the sharpest signals into content, copy, GEO/AEO assets, and source-to-search trust assets that make your brand easier to trust, quote, and buy from.

## The Problem It Solves

Most health brands have three content problems hiding inside one another:

1. Their buyers are reacting to new health trends faster than the brand's content team can interpret them.
2. Their strongest claims are either under-supported, over-softened, or too generic to create authority.
3. Their website, LinkedIn, and long-form content are not structured for AI search to retrieve, cite, or preserve nuance.

The result is content that stays busy but does not create demand.

## The Method

### 1. Ear-To-The-Ground Signal Scan

Current health, wellness, performance, supplement, AI-search, social listening, and buyer-language signals become a daily intelligence layer.

### 2. Claim And Source Safety

Each usable claim gets a receipt: source, evidence grade, limitation, safe wording, unsafe wording, and public-copy risk.

### 3. Creative Strategy Translation

The signal becomes a sharp angle: audience, category convention, pattern break, emotional lever, proof gap, and one-line creative brief.

### 4. Copywriting And Ghostwriting

The angle becomes founder POV, LinkedIn posts, article seeds, email angles, sales-page copy, and perspective-changing educational content.

### 5. AEO, SEO, And GEO Buildout

The best insights become retrieval-ready assets: long-tail question maps, citation gap maps, 5 Percent Assets, source-backed answer pages, and LinkedIn reinforcement.

### 6. Source-To-Search Trust Layer

The brand's scattered proof becomes a reusable operating layer: claim receipts, source hierarchy, brand entity file, founder judgment rules, examples, counterexamples, quality tests, failure modes, and update rules. Publicly, this is the trust layer. Warm buyers can understand it as an agent-readable system.

## Quality Bar

A usable daily output must pass these gates:

- It identifies one real buyer tension.
- It names one source-backed proof lane.
- It avoids medical advice and unsupported health claims.
- It contains one non-obvious perspective shift.
- It creates one public content asset.
- It creates one private audit or offer bridge.
- It shows how the insight could become SEO, AEO, or GEO surface area.
- It gives Farrice at least one post he could publish with minimal rework.
