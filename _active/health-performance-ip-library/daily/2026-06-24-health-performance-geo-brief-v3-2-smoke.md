# Make The Caveat Travel

Health content has a new problem.

A source somewhere on the page no longer carries the whole burden.

The caveat has to travel with the claim.

A wellness brand can publish a careful article about appetite, protein, energy, strength, recovery, or GLP-1-adjacent behavior.

The page can have sources.

The language can sound responsible.

Then an AI answer, affiliate summary, creator post, or buyer screenshot can carry the useful part and drop the limit.

That is the risk.

A citation-fidelity block fixes the page before the page gets summarized.

Every high-stakes health page should include:

1. Claim
2. Source
3. What the source supports
4. What the source does not prove
5. Wording the brand will not use
6. When the buyer should ask a clinician, pharmacist, or qualified professional
7. Quote-safe sentence

The useful frame is source design.

If the caveat cannot travel, the claim is not ready.
