So the same swarm of reviews, comments, and distributor posts that can get a brand sued can also be the exact material an AI answer engine treats as the truth about that brand.

Most wellness founders think their proof problem is "we need more reviews" or "we need creators talking about us."

The real problem is that nobody at the company knows what the swarm is currently claiming on their behalf, to humans or to machines.

Three questions I would start with:

What do your top ten reviews, distributor posts, or creator captions actually claim, in plain words?

Which of those claims would you be comfortable defending to the FTC tomorrow?

If an AI agent summarized your brand using only public reviews and comments, what would it say your product cures?

Most brands cannot answer any of the three. That is the gap.

The proof surface is now everyone talking about you that you never hired a lawyer to review, not just your product page.
