## 1. The Pick

Most health and wellness brands are still optimizing to be found by a search engine their customer has quietly stopped asking first.

Line Farrice could say out loud: "You spent two years building an SEO strategy for a search engine your customer stopped using."

Buyer who would care today: a founder, content lead, or growth lead at a supplement, wellness clinic, diagnostics, or performance brand with a mature website and content library who has never checked what an AI assistant actually says about them.

Why this is more useful than the other researched signals: this is a structural shift in the retrieval layer itself, not one company's legal exposure or one ingredient's safety question, and it converts directly into a live, runnable audit instead of another warning post.

Picture it: a founder opens ChatGPT in the middle of a client meeting to make a point, types the company's own name, and reads the answer out loud to the room. Nothing comes back. Then someone types a competitor's name, and three confident paragraphs show up, product named twice.

That gap between "we rank" and "we get repeated back" is the actual content opening.

## 2. Why It Has Juice

**Visual scene:** a founder, phone or laptop open in a real meeting, typing their own brand's name into an AI assistant and getting silence where a paragraph should be, then typing a competitor's name and watching the assistant name the product twice without hedging.

**Buyer tension:** the brand did everything the old playbook rewarded, reviews, backlinks, keyword-dense FAQ pages, and the criteria that actually earns a mention inside an assistant's answer moved to freshness and clear structure, without anyone sending a memo.

**Belief shift:** ranking on Google's blue links and getting repeated back by an AI assistant used to be roughly the same job. In 2026 they split into two different jobs, and most brands have only done one of them.

**What Farrice can say that the category usually will not:** most agencies will keep selling more blog posts and more backlinks through the rest of 2026, because that is what they know how to scope and bill, while the thing that actually decides who gets named is a structure and freshness audit on the content that already exists, not more volume.

**Plain-English versions of the strategic language:**
- "Citation" means the AI assistant repeats your brand's name back to someone who asked a real question.
- "Structured content" means the page is organized in plain question-then-answer chunks an assistant can lift cleanly, not a wall of paragraphs written for a crawler.
- "Freshness signal" means how recently the page was actually rewritten, not just republished with a new date stamp.
