# Health Performance GEO Client Acquisition Service Ladder

This ladder turns the daily automation from a listening report into a client-acquisition content and offer engine.

Core positioning:

> I help health, wellness, supplement, and performance brands turn claims, buyer questions, and founder judgment into source-backed content humans trust and AI search can retrieve.

| Stage | Capability | Buyer Problem | Productized Asset | Price/Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI Listening Operator | "I do not know what my buyers are saying now." | Daily signal brief, VOC map, and zeitgeist read | Internal engine or free content |
| 2 | Brand And Content Auditor | "Our content is active, but it is not creating authority or demand." | Public/private teardown, angle audit, and content gap map | Free proof asset or entry diagnostic |
| 3 | Source Quality Auditor | "My claims may be weak, risky, or derivative." | Source-quality audit, claim receipt, and safe wording map | Entry diagnostic |
| 4 | Copywriting And Ghostwriting Partner | "We have things to say, but our POV sounds generic." | Founder LinkedIn posts, article seeds, sales-page copy, email angles, and ghostwritten POV | Monthly retainer or sprint |

| 8 | Creative Strategist | "We have signals, proof, and data but no sharp creative direction." | Creative strategy brief, campaign narrative, content system, offer bridge, and trust-layer roadmap | High-ticket intensive |

## Offer Progression

1. **Signal post:** publish the insight with a sharp point of view.
2. **Audit hook:** turn the insight into a brand/content/claim/retrieval audit lens.
3. **Proof asset:** build a public teardown, rubric, checklist, carousel, or source-backed article.
4. **Entry diagnostic:** sell the audit as a compact paid review.
5. **Execution sprint:** convert findings into copywriting, ghostwriting, LinkedIn, sales-page, or email work.
6. **Retrieval build:** convert findings into AEO/SEO/GEO ghostwriting and citation assets.
7. **Trust-layer build:** convert claims, sources, buyer questions, and founder judgment into reusable source-to-search assets.
8. **Strategy intensive:** convert the whole system into creative strategy, campaign narrative, and offer architecture.

## Daily Promotion Rule

Each brief must choose one signal and promote it one rung up the ladder.

Examples:

- A regulatory warning becomes a `Source Quality Auditor` checklist.
- A brand sameness pattern becomes a `Brand And Content Auditor` teardown.
- A Reddit tension becomes a `Creative Strategist` pattern break.
- A long-tail AI-search query becomes an `AEO/SEO/GEO Ghostwriter` 5 Percent Asset.
- A founder POV gap becomes a `Copywriting And Ghostwriting Partner` offer.
- A repeated AI-search citation gap becomes an `AEO/SEO/GEO Ghostwriter` asset.
- A scattered proof pattern becomes a `Source-To-Search Trust Layer Architect` sprint.
- A repeated buyer fear becomes an `Offer Architect` diagnostic promise.

## Weekly Promotion Rule

Every Friday, choose the strongest repeated theme and package it into one sellable asset:

- mini audit

- LinkedIn article
- source-quality rubric
- GEO/AEO visibility checklist
- Source-to-Search trust layer snapshot
- LinkedIn authority post pack
- founder ghostwriting angle map
