## The Positioning Spine

**Headline wedge**: GEO/SEO ghostwriting that makes a health/wellness/
supplement brand the one AI search *names* — in a regulated category, by
someone who has actually lived the physiology (18 years human performance,
NASM CPT/CES/PES).

**The moat**: the credential isn't the pitch. It's the reason the
claim-safety read and the citation-worthiness read are credible coming from
one person — someone who can read the actual evidence behind a physiology
claim, not just reformat it.

**The Bridge Message** (spine of every asset — content, audit, DM, brief):

> "Your proof is good. It just isn't getting carried — and in a regulated
> category, being the source AI names is now a two-gate problem only a real
> expert can clear."

Route by avatar:
- **Dana** (brand lead — Head of Brand/Content, VP Growth): *"You did
  everything right. The machine just isn't carrying your proof — and the
  brands getting cited aren't bigger, their proof is organized so the
  machine can carry it. Let's make you the source it names, without a
  claim blowing up."*
- **Marcus** (founder/formulator, credentialed face): *"AI search is
  finally built to reward the real ones. Let's make sure it's carrying
  your name — in your voice — before the category names someone else."*
- **Cold-open/audit**: *"AI search doesn't read your marketing — it reads
  your proof. And ChatGPT and Google don't even agree on whose proof
  counts. If your authority only lives on your own site, you're invisible
  to both."*

**Voice law**: lead with **named / cited / carried**. "GEO/AEO" is
introduced second, never as the lead. Reframe, never shame — "not because
you're small, because your proof isn't organized for the machine to
carry." Banned register: "personal brand," "thought leader," "influencer,"
"hack," "10x," "go viral," "more content."

**The empty quadrant Farrice owns**: AI-native GEO × lived body expertise ×
claim-safety, fused, pointed at $1M–$50M DTC health brands. Competitors
(KeyGrow, Pennock, 95 Projects, Sudhakar Senthilkumaran, David Sautter/Write
Fit, Toni Bubb) each have one or two of the three legs — none fuses all
three.

## Mapping Daily-Brief Signals to This Ladder

When a daily brief signal implies a client-acquisition angle, map it to a
specific pillar and, where possible, a specific brand from the 20-brand
shortlist (`ACQUISITION_TARGETS.md`) — not a generic "DTC brand" reference.

- A regulatory/enforcement signal (FDA/FTC) → **Pillar 2** angle: "this is
  exactly the claim-safety gate the Audit grades."
- An AI-citation/GEO-visibility signal → **Pillar 2 or 3** angle: "this is
  the citation gap the Audit surfaces / the retainer closes."
- A competitive/brand-move signal (funding, repositioning) → **Pillar 4**
  angle for larger, better-funded brands moving upmarket; **Pillar 3** for
  smaller founder-led brands.
- Always check whether the target brand is Dana-led or Marcus-led (see
  `ACQUISITION_TARGETS.md`) before drafting outreach language — the two
  avatars take different openers (see Bridge Message routing above).
