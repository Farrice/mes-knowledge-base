# Health Performance GEO Service Ladder

This ladder turns the daily automation from a listening report into a productized service engine.

| Stage | Capability | Buyer Problem | Productized Asset | Price/Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI Listening Operator | "I do not know what my buyers are saying now." | Daily signal brief and VOC map | Internal engine or free content |
| 2 | Source Quality Auditor | "My claims may be weak, risky, or derivative." | Source-quality and claim-safety audit | Entry diagnostic |
| 3 | SEO/AEO/GEO Analyst | "AI search does not cite us." | Long-tail question and citation gap map | Paid audit |
| 4 | Retrieval Architect | "We need assets AI can retrieve and cite." | 5 Percent Asset and source network | Premium project |
| 5 | Creative Strategist | "We have data but no sharp angle." | Creative strategy brief and content system | High-ticket intensive |
| 6 | Offer Architect | "This needs to become revenue." | Productized offer, sales page, proof plan | Retainer or premium build |

## Daily Promotion Rule

Each brief must choose one signal and promote it one rung up the ladder.

Examples:

- A regulatory warning becomes a `Source Quality Auditor` checklist.
- A Reddit tension becomes a `Creative Strategist` pattern break.
- A long-tail AI-search query becomes a `Retrieval Architect` 5 Percent Asset.
- A repeated buyer fear becomes an `Offer Architect` diagnostic promise.

## Weekly Promotion Rule

Every Friday, choose the strongest repeated theme and package it into one sellable asset:

- mini audit
- landing-page section
- LinkedIn article
- source-quality rubric
- GEO/AEO visibility checklist
- premium-service pitch angle
