## Executive Verdict

The health, wellness, supplement, and performance offer should keep the public front door simple:

> I help health, wellness, supplement, and performance brands turn claims, buyer questions, and founder judgment into source-backed content humans trust and AI search can retrieve.

The deeper productized module is the **Source-to-Search Trust Layer**.

This is the bridge between creative strategy, claim-safe copy, AEO/SEO/GEO, and the agent-readable systems Farrice already built. It turns claims, source receipts, buyer language, founder judgment, long-tail questions, and brand rules into assets that humans can inspect and AI systems can parse without flattening the nuance.

Buyer-facing language should say "trust layer," "source-backed answer assets," "claim receipts," and "AI-search retrieval readiness." Warm-buyer language can say "agent-readable trust assets."

## First Proof Demo

### GLP-1 Movement Receipt

Use the June 23 daily brief direction as the first proof demo:

> A claim-safe content rubric for wellness brands entering GLP-1-adjacent conversations without pretending to give medical advice.

The demo should show:

1. The trend signal: appetite-change medication is shifting buyer questions around movement, energy, strength, food, supplements, and identity.
2. The risk: brands want to join the conversation but can imply too much with soft wording.
3. The receipt: source, evidence grade, limitation, safe wording, unsafe wording, and buyer net impression.
4. The retrieval asset: a dated, source-backed answer page with long-tail questions and a clear claim boundary.
5. The agent-readable primitive: purpose, inputs, source of truth, decision rules, examples, counterexamples, quality tests, failure modes, and update rule.
6. The offer bridge: sell a GLP-1 Adjacent Claim-Safety Snapshot before pitching the full trust-layer sprint.
