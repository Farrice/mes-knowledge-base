# Red-Team: "God Agent Builds for Businesses"

**Date:** 2026-08-06 · **Discipline:** /offer-redteam (prosecutor / defender / evidence run in one seat; dissent preserved) · **Grounding:** `01-research/god-agent-delta.md`, `extractions/riley-brown/mes-extension-god-agent.md`, `_active/linkedin-launch/CAMPAIGN.md`, `skills/nick-saraev-agentic-workflows/` (offer-selection workflow, 900-deal dataset)

## 1. The offer, steel-manned

**Name:** The Company Agent Install. **Price:** $10,000 build + $2,000/mo agent-tending retainer. **Buyer:** owner-operator of a 5–50 person business already paying humans for repeatable knowledge work (support triage, content ops, reporting). **Promise:** in 30 days your company has one agent front door — a router over specialized sub-agents carrying *your* operating knowledge as encoded skills, wired to real event triggers (Stripe, inbound email, calendar), with falsifiable verification instead of vibes. **Delivery shape:** week 1 extraction interviews (the forge applied to the client's own expertise), weeks 2–3 build on an agent framework, week 4 evals + handoff; retainer = "working on the agent" (Rauch's meta-work doctrine) as a monthly service.

Pricing is grounded, not aspirational: Saraev's dataset runs $200 disasters to $60k+ enterprise systems, with nearly every $20k+ win carrying recurring revenue and a $2.5k/mo retainer worth $30k in year one. A $10k+retainer install sits mid-ladder for a builder with receipts. The differentiator is real: incumbents sell Zapier-grade plumbing; Farrice's forge encodes named-expert domain judgment into skills, and his own harness (conductor ladder, mission queue, event listener, blind-bar verification) is a production instance of the architecture Vercel calls a god agent. That is the strongest honest version. Now the attacks.

## 2. Kill shots

**K1 — Zero external receipts.** He has built a god agent for exactly one customer: himself. No client build, no handoff, no uptime record, no case study, no named business that will take a reference call. Rauch's own framing raises the buyer's bar: "hundreds of millions of dollars of revenue are dependent on the well-being of this agent" — a buyer installing a company nervous system asks who else trusts you with theirs. The self-referential demo ("look at my harness") reads as hobbyist proof to a check-writer. **Survives?** No. This is the exact shape the 2026-07-21 red-team killed: a cold offer with no proof ladder. The Alignment Audit died at $400; this asks for $10,000.

**K2 — The scar pattern, live and in progress.** This document exists because a compelling video landed *today* while the active campaign sits at 0 sends, $0 collected, with five DMs finalized and waiting on a payment link. Designing offer #2 before offer #1 has a single send is not adjacent to the offer-jumping pattern — it is the pattern, executing. Send-before-build is binding (Sprint Spine 2026-07-21), and CAMPAIGN.md explicitly parks product expansion behind daily sends. **Survives?** No, as a now-offer. The only honest disposition is sequencing.

**K3 — Solo-operator delivery trap.** Vercel runs a dedicated V team to keep one internal agent alive. An installed company agent is not a 48-hour artifact like the Angle Map; it is integration, permissioning, event plumbing, drift, and an implicit on-call promise. Two retainer clients plus the campaign plus Jen plus content is a capacity lie; the retainer that funds the offer is the same retainer that eats the calendar. **Survives?** Only at 1–2 clients with brutally scoped SLAs — which caps it as a side revenue line, not a spine.

**K4 — Platform compression.** Eve's pitch is that an agent is "a folder with an instructions.md file," deployable from eve.dev, connected to Slack in an afternoon. The vendors are racing the build fee to zero; every month the plumbing gets cheaper to self-serve. What survives compression is the layer platforms cannot ship: extracted domain judgment, taste, verification. **Survives?** Partially — but only if the offer is sold as encoded expertise ("your best operator's brain, on call") and never as "I build agents." Sold as builds, it is a melting asset.

**K5 — ICP fracture.** His active buyer is a supplement/performance founder with a proof-and-positioning problem. The god-agent buyer is an ops-minded SMB owner with a labor problem. Speaking to both from one LinkedIn profile mid-campaign blurs the one thing the campaign spent five weeks sharpening: what Farrice is *for*. **Survives?** Not during the campaign window. Post-validation, agentic content can widen the moat; today it splits the signal.

## 3. The content-as-distribution claim, tested

"I articulate it and they come to me to build it" holds where the content *is* the service — ghostwriting, positioning, copy. It fails where the content merely gestures at the service: a post about god agents proves fluency, not delivery. Fluency attracts DIY-curious operators and other AI people, not check-writers. Saraev, the incumbent proof case, built his agency on outbound and delivered systems, then used content to amplify receipts — not to substitute for them. For a buyer to move, the content would need to carry: (1) a named business running his agent with a measured before/after (hours saved, response time, revenue touched); (2) a visible demo a prospect can poke; (3) a reference human. None exist. The nearest legitimate receipt is in his own house: Jen's real-estate operation as first installed client, with real numbers. Until a receipt like that exists, "content as distribution" for this offer is a belief, not a channel. What content *can* honestly do now is compound authority for later — which is a sequencing argument, not a selling one.

## 4. Relationship to Proof-to-Market

Not standalone (K2, K5 kill it), not replacement (abandoning a campaign at 0 sends for a shinier offer is the failure mode itself, named). **Backend upsell — chosen.** The sprint delivers a proof system; the natural continuation for a satisfied sprint client is "the agent that runs your proof engine" — signal listening, claim-ledger upkeep, content ops on triggers. Same ICP, no positioning fracture, delivery scoped to a system Farrice already built once, and the sprint relationship supplies the trust a cold agent-install can never buy. The upsell inherits the campaign's receipts instead of demanding its own from zero.

## 5. VERDICT

**SEQUENCE-BEHIND-FIRST-COLLECTED-PROOF-TO-MARKET-DOLLAR.** The offer is architecturally real and eventually his to own, and it may not be sold, packaged, or content-launched until the active campaign has collected money. **Single next falsifiable step:** execute Mission 2b — send the five finalized DMs and log them (payment link first). That send is the gate this offer waits behind; no god-agent artifact, post, or page before it.
