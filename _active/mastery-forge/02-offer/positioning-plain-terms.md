# The God Agent Offer: Plain-Terms Positioning
**Expert loaded:** Donald Miller, StoryBrand one-liner (prompts-v2 contract honored) · **Produced:** 2026-08-06 · **Status:** UNCAGED, Farrice overrule on record
**Naming note:** "God agent" is our internal word. It never reaches a business owner. The customer-facing frame is **an AI employee that knows your whole business.**

---

## 1. The Core One-Liner

**Most owners ARE the system. Every order, question, and follow-up runs through their head and their phone. We install an AI employee that learns how your business actually works and handles the repeat work every night, so nothing falls through and you get your evenings back.**

- Problem (internal): *I can never step away. Everything depends on me remembering.*
- Solution mechanism: an installed AI employee trained on YOUR operation. Not a chatbot, not an app subscription.
- Result: nothing falls through, and you get time back. Identity shift: from "the system" to "the owner."

## 4. The Need-Not-Want Argument (owner terms, honest)

- **The believable version:** a business that encodes how it operates into an AI employee compounds. Every week it knows more, catches more, drafts more. A business that doesn't will be answering leads on Tuesday that a competitor answered Sunday night. This is the website-in-2005 curve, the POS-system curve: optional until the guy across the street has it.
- **What we do NOT claim:** that AI replaces staff, that it runs unattended from day one, or that every business needs it today. Overclaiming is how this market burns trust. The offer's credibility IS the falsifiable checking layer.

## 5. Per-Instance Variants (the warm three)

**Chris, three restaurants (Wasabi CityWalk · Yakiya Pasadena · Panda Inn Glendale).**
The hook is the receipt that already exists: "Remember the menu analysis I did for you in April? Imagine that running every night. Competitor prices in the 5-mile radius, review responses drafted for all three spots, and a one-page report to the owner every Monday morning." Problem: three locations, one brain. Result: the owner reads one page Monday and knows what changed and what to do.

**Dog business.**
"Every no-show you forgot to chase, every review you didn't answer, every 'do you have availability Saturday?' at 9pm. The AI employee drafts all of it overnight, and you approve it with your coffee." Problem: bookings leak. Result: fuller calendar, zero late-night phone admin.

**Micheaux, title/law shop.**
"Half that office's day is chasing status: where's the doc, who signed, what's outstanding. The AI employee runs the checklist, drafts the status-chase emails, and flags what actually needs a human." Problem: deadline-critical follow-up lives in inboxes. Result: files move without anyone babysitting them.

## 6. First Instance: Chris, the 2-Week Proof Install

**Why Chris first:** trust exists, the April audit is a delivered receipt, and Chris has the owner's ear across all three restaurants. This is a deploy, not a sale. Price is $0; the payment is a named outcome we can say out loud.

**Scope (small on purpose):**
1. **Nightly market watch.** Competitor menu and price scan in each location's radius. Extends the April audit; the machinery pattern already exists in this repo.
2. **Weekly owner page.** Every Monday: what changed, what it means, one recommended move. This page is the artifact the owner falls in love with.
3. **Review-response drafts.** All three locations, drafted in the house tone, human approves before posting.

**Success bar (falsifiable):** by day 14, the owner has read two Monday pages and asked at least one "can it also…" question. That question is the sales conversation. We never have to open one.
