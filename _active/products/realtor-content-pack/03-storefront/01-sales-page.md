## Questions agents actually ask

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Cancel in one click from your account page, no email required, no retention call. You keep access through the end of your billing period, and everything already delivered stays licensed to you.

**Do you do refunds?**
Because content is delivered and licensed the moment your billing period starts, monthly fees are not refundable. That is exactly why canceling is one click and there is no contract: the exit is easy, so you never need to gamble on a long commitment. Try one month at $49 and judge it on the work.

**What does the license let me do, in plain words?**
Practitioner: use everything in your own marketing, on any platform, edited however you like, for as long as you were a member when it was delivered. You cannot resell the templates or share them with other agents. White-Label Agency: everything above, plus you may rebrand the content and deliver it to your own agents or clients as part of your service. Neither tier may resell the raw template library as a competing product.

**Is this MLS and brokerage compliant?**
Templates ship with slots for your brokerage name, license number, and fair-housing-safe language. Final compliance responsibility sits with you and your broker, same as any marketing you run, but the templates are built so filling the slots keeps you inside the lines.

**I'm in a small market. Will this feel generic?**
Every template has fill-in slots for your market's numbers, neighborhoods, and price points. The frameworks are what you are buying; the local specifics are yours, and they take minutes to add.

**What if I already use a template pack?**
Keep whatever works. The difference to check for: does your current pack hand you a coordinated per-listing launch sequence, or just a stack of posts? The drop replaces the stack. The launch system is the upgrade.

**How is this made?**
By a working listing-marketing team that builds and runs listing content in the field, then turns the winning formats into templates. You are buying the system that already worked, not experiments.
