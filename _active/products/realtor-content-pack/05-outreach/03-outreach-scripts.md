# Outreach Scripts: Realtor Content Pack

Product facts used below: agent tier $49/mo (monthly ready-to-post social content pack for real estate agents), agency white-label tier $149/mo (same library, their branding, use across their realtor clients). Founding rate is whatever you decide to honor for the first cohort; the field is a merge slot so you can change it without rewriting scripts.

**Global rule:** every merge field must be filled from the prospect's actual page (the `source_url` in the contact list). If you can't name a real post or a real detail, don't send that message; move to the next row. No invented compliments.

---

## AGENTS

### 1. Cold DM (59 words)

> [FIRST_NAME], that [SPECIFIC_POST] post was the good kind of local content. The rest of the grid looks like it's fighting the calendar, which is normal when you're also selling houses. I make a $49/mo content pack for agents: 30 days of posts, captions, reel scripts, done. First month at [FOUNDING_RATE] for founding subscribers. Want the sample?

**Fill instructions (one line each):**
- `[FIRST_NAME]`: their first name as written on their own site, not the brokerage roster.
- `[SPECIFIC_POST]`: name one actual post you watched (e.g. "the Bucktown sold-story reel"); if you can't describe it in five words, you didn't watch it.
- `[FOUNDING_RATE]`: the founding-cohort price you're honoring this week (e.g. "$29").

### 2. Cold email

**Subject:** your [MONTH] content calendar, already done

> [FIRST_NAME],
>
> I found you through [WHERE_FOUND] and went through your [PLATFORM]. The [SPECIFIC_POST] post is doing real work. The gaps between posts are the tell that content is competing with actual deals for your time, and losing.
>
> I build a monthly content pack for agents: 30 days of posts, captions, and reel scripts written for [MARKET] realtors, delivered on the 1st, ready to post or hand to an assistant. $49/mo, cancel whenever. Founding subscribers get their first month at [FOUNDING_RATE].
>
> Reply "sample" and I'll send this month's pack so you can judge the quality before paying anything.
>
> [YOUR_NAME]

(Body: 109 words.)

**Fill instructions:**
- `[WHERE_FOUND]`: the honest path ("a Dallas agents roundup", "your site"); honesty here is what kills the spam smell.
- `[PLATFORM]`: the one channel they actually post on (Instagram / YouTube / blog), from their own site links.
- `[SPECIFIC_POST]`: same rule as the DM: real post, five-word description minimum.
- `[MARKET]`: their metro as they name it themselves ("SLC", "Tampa Bay", "DFW").
- `[FOUNDING_RATE]`: current founding price.
- `[YOUR_NAME]`: sign as a person, no title block on a cold send.

### 3. Follow-up bump (send 3–4 days later, same thread)

> [FIRST_NAME], closing the founding rate to the first [COHORT_SIZE] agents and I'd rather one of the spots go to someone already posting than someone starting from zero. The sample offer stands: reply "sample," judge the pack, then decide. If content is handled some other way, tell me and I'll stop here.

**Fill instructions:**
- `[COHORT_SIZE]`: the true cohort cap (e.g. "20"); only use a number you will actually enforce.

## AGENCIES

### 4. Cold email (white-label pitch)

**Subject:** your content production cost, cut to $149

> [FIRST_NAME],
>
> You're producing realtor content by hand at [AGENCY_NAME]. I read the [SERVICE_PAGE_DETAIL] on your site. That model works, and it eats hours that don't scale.
>
> I run a white-label content pack for exactly your situation: every month, a full library of realtor posts, captions, and reel scripts, delivered unbranded. You put your logo on it, adapt it per client, and resell at your margin. It's $149/mo flat, whether you run it under four clients or fifteen. One client at [CLIENT_PRICE_POINT] covers it several times over; the rest is margin your team didn't have to write.
>
> Want this month's library free to pressure-test against your own output? Reply "library."
>
> [YOUR_NAME]

(Body: 118 words.)

**Fill instructions:**
- `[AGENCY_NAME]`: their name as styled on their site (BSREM vs Beth Sutton Real Estate Marketing matters).
- `[SERVICE_PAGE_DETAIL]`: one concrete thing from their service page ("the $325–$700 calendar tiers", "the 4-posts-per-network package"); proves you read it.
- `[CLIENT_PRICE_POINT]`: their own published per-client price if visible, otherwise "$400/mo" as the category norm.
- `[YOUR_NAME]`: a person, not a brand.

### 5. Agency follow-up (send 4–5 days later, same thread)

> [FIRST_NAME], quick math and then I'll leave it: at [CLIENT_COUNT] realtor clients, in-house content runs you roughly [ESTIMATED_HOURS] hours a month. The white-label library is $149 and replaces the blank-page part of that, so your writers edit instead of originate. The free month is still open if you want to test it on one client account first.

**Fill instructions:**
- `[CLIENT_COUNT]`: their visible client count if published, otherwise a conservative "5–10".
- `[ESTIMATED_HOURS]`: your honest estimate at ~2 hrs per client per week (e.g. "40+"); round down, never up.

## Register notes (why these read the way they do)

- Every opener touches something real from their page; the merge-field rules force the research, so familiarity is earned, never faked.
- One offer, one reply word ("sample" / "library"). Low-friction self-selection instead of a pitch to book a call.
- The follow-ups give an explicit out ("tell me and I'll stop here"). Prospects who use it save your send list's reputation.
- Scarcity appears only where it's true (cohort cap you enforce, founding rate you actually retire). If it isn't true, delete the sentence rather than send it.
