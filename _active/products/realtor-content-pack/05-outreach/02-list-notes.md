# Contact List Notes — Realtor Content Pack Outreach

**Built:** 2026-08-24 · **Rows delivered: 50 of 50 target (30 agents/teams · 20 agencies)** — every row verified against a page actually loaded that day.

## Sourcing method

1. **Discovery layer** (WebSearch): metro-by-metro "agents to follow on Instagram" roundups, agency-category searches ("social media management for realtors," city + "real estate social media agency"), and VA-firm searches for the white-label side. Roundups were used only to surface names, never as a source of record.
2. **Verification layer** (WebFetch, one load per row minimum): every contact was confirmed on a **business-published page** — the agent's own website/contact page, a brokerage roster or bio page, the agency's own service page, or the person's own public link page (Linktree/hoo.be). The `source_url` column is the exact page loaded.
3. **Integrity rules applied:**
   - No fabricated contacts. If a site masked its email behind cloaking (renders as `[email protected]`), the email was **excluded** rather than guessed; the row carries phone, contact form, or IG handle instead.
   - Business channels only — no personal cells, personal emails, or home addresses. All phones/emails are the ones each business publishes for inquiry.
   - Candidates whose pages could not be loaded were **dropped, not guessed**: Social Espresso (domain now parked/for sale), Beyond Business VA (expired domain), Social Behavior Houston (expired SSL cert), Bern McGovern (cert mismatch), Monica Church (DNS dead), Riley Smith Group (403), Dami Olatunde (site misrendered + HAR 403), Ashley Alvarez (429/403 on all pages). That is why some big-name social agents are absent.

## Fit logic

- **Agents (30):** teams and individual agents who demonstrably already invest in content — linked IG/YouTube on their own site, blogs, reels, content funnels. Mix of mid-tier personality brands (the natural $49/mo buyer) and larger teams (multi-seat potential). A few mega-luxury names (Shelton Wilder, Mahsheed) are included as stretch targets; the core ICP is the 3K–50K-follower agent whose posting is active but inconsistent.
- **Agencies (20):** shops that hand-produce social content for realtor clients today — their fulfillment cost is the wedge for the $149/mo white-label tier. Best-fit are the small/solo studios (Beth Sutton, Finally Social, Just Pended Denver, Realty Studio Design, The Williams Agency, Sellwell) and the VA firms (YesAssistant, EasyOutsource) where one subscription arms many client accounts. Thrive/LYFE/Chatter Buzz are larger; kept as volume plays but expect procurement friction. Direct content-pack competitors (Agent Crate, Coffee & Contracts, The Social Broker, Agent Nest) were deliberately excluded — they are rivals, not prospects.

## Market clusters worth noting

- **Florida is the densest cluster** (11 rows: Miami ×4 agencies, Tampa/St. Pete ×5, Fort Lauderdale, Orlando) — both sides of the market are content-hungry there; consider a geo-batched send.
- **Texas** (8 rows: Dallas ×5, Houston ×2, Austin) and **the Carolinas** (3 Charlotte rows) are the next-deepest.
- **Two Kansas City teams share one brokerage** (KW KC North: MoJo + Nelson Home Group) — don't send identical copy to both.
- Agencies cluster in **Denver ×2, Miami ×3, DFW ×2, Georgia ×3** — referrals travel inside those scenes; land one, name-drop nothing, but expect word to move.
