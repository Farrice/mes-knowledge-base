# Realtor Local-Signal System: Behavior Proof

## Verdict

**Behavior-changing proof: PASS (9/9 cards; 8/8 negative controls).**

The system transforms interchangeable Realtor topics into content built from a local signal, one target person's tension, the agent's supported point of view, a feasible production plan, and a human conversation path.

This is artifact proof, not market proof. Nothing in this fixture was published. Attention, conversations, appointments, clients, and revenue remain `NO EVENT`. View, lead, and client anecdotes from the source interview remain `UNCONFIRMED` without first-party analytics and CRM evidence.

## Card 1: Privacy starts before the front door

- **Intended local person:** An LA buyer or seller trying to understand what privacy actually looks like in a luxury property.
- **Recognizable tension:** Tall fences are marketed as privacy even when the street still feels present everywhere on the lot.
- **Source / evidence:** Jen calibration log; approved 5200 Armida authority-POV hook; `VERIFIED / HISTORICAL`.
- **Original POV:** In LA, privacy is not a finish or a fence. It is the amount of city that disappears before you reach the house.
- **Hook:** “True privacy in LA doesn't mean building a taller fence. It means the arrival starts before the house does.”
- **Beat map:** Walk the approach → show distance and sight lines → explain why arrival changes the feeling → identify what a buyer should inspect at a private showing.
- **Visual plan:** Jen walking the driveway, then quiet property B-roll; elegant short captions.
- **Low-pressure CTA:** “If privacy is high on your list, send me the word PRIVATE. I'll share the three things I check before I call a property secluded.”
- **Human response path:** Jen asks whether the person is comparing homes now or simply building a future wish list, then answers personally.
- **Real-estate memory:** Demonstrates how an experienced agent evaluates privacy beyond marketing language.
- **Attention metrics:** Local reach, completion, saves, shares, profile visits.
- **Pipeline metrics:** Relevant DMs, privacy-criteria conversations, appointments, represented clients, collected revenue.
- **Receipt:** Originality PASS; voice supported by approved Jen calibration; factual property details require day-of-use verification; fair-housing PASS; `NO EVENT`.

## Negative controls

| Control | Rejection receipt | Safe continuation |
|---|---|---|
| Exact competitor hook and script copied word for word | `verbatim_imitation` | Extract only the visible format; write a new local premise and agent POV |
| “This project will raise nearby values 12%” with no source | `unsourced_factual_claim` | Report proposal status and verification questions; remove causal prediction |
| “The perfect safe neighborhood for young families” | `fair_housing_steering` | Describe property, place, access, and observable amenities without saying who belongs |
| Viral mansion tour from another country used as a local topic | `wrong_market_or_audience` | Keep only a transferable visual format if it fits a sourced local premise |
| Agent assigned an angry anti-development take absent from their Fit Card | `artificial_opinion` | Ask for the agent's real view or use neutral source explanation |
| “Comment GUIDE” when no guide or person owns the reply | `cta_without_human_response_path` | Prepare the asset and response owner before publishing |
| Restaurant montage with no reason to follow a Realtor | `lifestyle_without_real_estate_memory` | Add a neutral location-evaluation method or skip the real-estate claim entirely |
| Daily calendar imposed on an agent capped at two posts weekly | `forced_daily_cadence` | Select the two highest-fit signals and preserve sustainable capacity |
