# Reel Scripts — 8 Templates

Rules that apply to every script: the hook lands in the first 2 seconds, no walk-up, no "hey guys." Put the hook on screen as text, word for word. Use your platform's native captions. 20–40 seconds. Film the close as its own take so you can reuse it. Speak numbers cleanly ("seven forty-nine," not "$749,000 dollars").

---

## Reel 1 — Listing Tease: The Objection Flip

Use when your listing has a feature that beats a common hesitation (busy street but huge private lot, dated exterior but renovated interior, further-out neighborhood but more house).

- **HOOK (≤8 words, to camera):** "Saw [THE OBJECTION, e.g. 'the street'] and almost scrolled? Watch."
- **On-screen text:** [NEIGHBORHOOD] · $[PRICE]
- **BEAT 2 (walking through the feature that flips it):** "[Name the hesitation honestly in one line.] Then you [step through the gate / walk inside / turn the corner] and it flips: [THE PROOF — 2 or 3 concrete specs from the MLS sheet. Square footage, renovation year, the lot, the layout]."
- **BEAT 3 (the frame):** "At $[PRICE] in [CITY], you can pay for the address, or you can pay for [what this house gives: space, condition, the second unit]. This one picked [X]."
- **CLOSE (separate take, best spot on the property):** "[LISTING ADDRESS or NEIGHBORHOOD]. $[PRICE]. I'm [AGENT NAME] with [BROKERAGE]. DM me '[KEYWORD]' and I'll send the full details and get you in this week."

## Reel 2 — Listing Tease: The Fast Checklist

Highest save-rate format. Whip-cuts, each line lands with its shot.

- **HOOK:** "The whole checklist, fast. [NEIGHBORHOOD]."
- **VO + matching cuts:** "[FEATURE 1 — the rarest one first]. [FEATURE 2]. [FEATURE 3]. [FEATURE 4]. [BEDS] bed, [BATHS] bath, [SQFT] square feet."
- **On-screen:** a checkmark stamping per line; price stays on screen the whole time: $[PRICE]
- **BEAT (slow down for one beat, to camera):** "The one that matters: [THE SINGLE BEST FEATURE, one sentence on why it's rare at this price]."
- **CLOSE:** "$[PRICE], [NEIGHBORHOOD]. I'm [AGENT NAME] with [BROKERAGE]. DM '[KEYWORD]' for the full sheet. Save this if you're touring this weekend."

## Reel 3 — Objection Flip: "I'm waiting for prices to drop"

- **HOOK:** "Waiting for prices to drop? Run this first."
- **BEAT 2 (to camera or walking):** "Maybe waiting is right for you. But make it a math decision, not a headline decision. While you wait, three meters run: your rent, the rate you'd lock, and prices in [CITY], which are currently [WHAT YOUR MLS DATA ACTUALLY SHOWS: 'up X% over last year' / 'flat' / 'down X%' — pull from your board's report and say the source]."
- **BEAT 3:** "So the real question: if you wait 12 months, what do you save, and what does the waiting cost? That's one spreadsheet. I'll build it with your actual rent and budget."
- **CLOSE:** "I'm [AGENT NAME] with [BROKERAGE]. DM me '[KEYWORD]' and I'll run your wait-or-buy math. If waiting wins, I'll tell you that too."

## Reel 4 — Objection Flip: "I don't have 20% down"

- **HOOK:** "Nobody told you this about down payments."
- **BEAT 2:** "20% down does one thing: it removes mortgage insurance. It was never the minimum. There are conventional loans from 3% down, FHA from 3.5, and zero-down options if you qualify for VA or USDA."
- **BEAT 3:** "Mortgage insurance is a real cost. Sometimes renting longer to avoid it is the right call, sometimes it isn't. It depends on your rent, your market, your timeline. That's a 20-minute conversation with a lender, and it's free."
- **CLOSE:** "I'm [AGENT NAME] with [BROKERAGE]. DM me '[KEYWORD]' and I'll connect you with a lender who'll give you the real number, not a rule of thumb."

## Reel 5 — Market Explainer: Translate the Stat

Facts tell, stories sell. Never post a stat without saying who it's good for.

- **Data to pull first:** ONE current number from your MLS market report. Days on market, months of inventory, or sale-to-list ratio. Note the source and month.
- **HOOK:** "[THE STAT] in [CITY]. Here's who wins."
- **On-screen:** [THE STAT] · source: [MLS/BOARD], [MONTH]
- **BEAT 2 (for buyers):** "If you're buying: [what this number means for how you should behave — how fast to move, how hard to negotiate, one concrete instruction]."
- **BEAT 3 (for sellers):** "If you're selling: [what it means for pricing and timing, one concrete instruction]."
- **BEAT 4:** "The market is always good for somebody. The job is knowing whether it's you."
- **CLOSE:** "I'm [AGENT NAME], [BROKERAGE]. I break down [CITY]'s numbers like this every month. Follow for the next one, or DM '[KEYWORD]' for the full report."

## Reel 6 — Market Explainer: What "Days on Market" Hides

- **HOOK:** "This number lies to buyers constantly."
- **BEAT 2:** "Average days on market in [CITY] is [DOM AVERAGE] right now. But averages blend two markets: turnkey homes at the right price go in [FAST BAND] days, and everything else sits."
- **BEAT 3:** "So when a listing you like has been sitting for 40 days, that's not automatically a red flag. It might be a pricing mistake you can negotiate against. The sitting inventory is where deals live."
- **BEAT 4:** "And if you're selling: you don't want to be in the sitting pile. The first two weeks decide which pile you're in."
- **CLOSE:** "I'm [AGENT NAME] with [BROKERAGE]. DM '[KEYWORD]' and I'll send you the listings in [CITY] that have been sitting long enough to talk."

## Reel 7 — Day in the Life: The Honest Version

People connect with people, not properties. This is a trust post, not a lead post.

- **HOOK:** "What a [DAY OF WEEK] actually looks like in this job."
- **BEATS (b-roll of each, VO over):** "[TIME]: [real task — coffee + MLS hot sheet]. [TIME]: [real task — walkthrough before photos at LISTING or 'a listing in NEIGHBORHOOD']. [TIME]: [real task — inspection negotiation call, no client details]. [TIME]: [real task — showing tour, driving b-roll]. [TIME]: [the unglamorous one — writing an offer at the kitchen table at 9pm]."
- **BEAT (to camera, one line):** "The job is mostly [your honest one-liner about what the work really is]. The keys photo is the last 1%."
- **CLOSE:** "I'm [AGENT NAME]. I sell homes in [CITY] and show the real version of it here. Follow along."

## Reel 8 — Behind the Scenes: "What I do after your offer is accepted"

Converts the process into proof that hiring you matters.

- **HOOK:** "You went under contract. My job just started."
- **BEATS (VO over b-roll: phone calls, checklist, site visits):** "Day one: I calendar every deadline in your contract, because missing one can cost you your deposit. Week one: I'm at your inspection, flagging what's negotiable. Week two: I'm negotiating repairs with the other agent. Weeks three and four: I'm chasing your lender, the appraiser, and escrow so nothing stalls quietly. Final week: walkthrough with a checklist, then keys."
- **BEAT (to camera):** "Roughly [N] tasks between 'accepted' and 'keys.' You'll see maybe five of them. That's the point."
- **CLOSE:** "I'm [AGENT NAME] with [BROKERAGE]. Buying or selling in [CITY], DM me '[KEYWORD]' and I'll walk you through exactly what working together looks like."
