# Standalone Captions — 15 Templates

Hook is always the first line (it's all anyone sees before "more"). Labeled by job: AUTHORITY (proves you know the market), ENGAGEMENT (starts conversations), LEAD-GEN (gives a reason to DM). Pair each with a photo/b-roll note. Hashtags: 5–10, your city + adjacent neighborhoods + format tags. Skip generic ones like #realestate #dreamhome.

---

## Authority

**1. The translated stat**
*Pair with: a simple graphic of the one number, or you at a listing.*
[STAT]. That's [CITY]'s [metric name] as of [MONTH], per [SOURCE].
Plain English: [one sentence on what it means for buyers]. And if you're selling: [one sentence for sellers].
Numbers without a translation are just decoration. This is the one worth knowing this month.

**2. The pricing truth**
*Pair with: photo of you at a kitchen table with a laptop/comps.*
What you paid for your house has nothing to do with what it sells for.
Neither does what you spent on it, or what the app says.
It sells for what the last three buyers paid for the three most similar homes near you, adjusted for condition. That's the whole science. The strategy part is timeline and negotiation, and that's where it gets personal.

**3. The micro-market callout**
*Pair with: street-level photo in the neighborhood.*
[NEIGHBORHOOD] and [ADJACENT NEIGHBORHOOD] are ten minutes apart and behave like different markets.
Right now: [NEIGHBORHOOD] median is $[MEDIAN PRICE] with homes going in about [DOM] days. [ADJACENT NEIGHBORHOOD]: $[COMP MEDIAN], [COMP DOM] days. (Source: [MLS/BOARD], [MONTH].)
The difference is [the concrete reason: housing stock, lot sizes, commute]. If you're shopping one, you should at least tour the other.

**4. The inspection story (anonymized)**
*Pair with: photo of an inspection detail — attic, panel, crawlspace.*
The best $[INSPECTION COST] my buyer spent this year.
[2–3 sentences: what the inspection found on a recent deal — no address, no client details — and what it saved or how it reshaped the negotiation.]
I will never let a client skip the inspection to "strengthen the offer" without knowing exactly what that trade costs. There are safer ways to compete.

**5. The "what I'd do" take**
*Pair with: to-camera photo or b-roll.*
If I were buying in [CITY] this month, here's exactly what I'd do.
1. [Concrete move tied to current conditions]
2. [Concrete move]
3. [Concrete move]
Not advice for everyone. It depends on your timeline and your cash. But that's my honest read of this exact market, and I'll update it when the market changes.

## Engagement

**6. The either/or**
*Pair with: split photo of two listings or two rooms.*
Same budget, two doors. $[PRICE] gets you either:
A) [Concrete option: more space, further out — 2 specifics]
B) [Concrete option: smaller, in the core — 2 specifics]
There's no wrong answer, but everyone has one instantly. A or B in the comments. I'm keeping score.

**7. The local favorite**
*Pair with: photo at the actual spot.*
Unofficial [CITY] census: where's the best [coffee / tacos / breakfast] in town?
My vote is [YOUR PICK] and I'm prepared to defend it.
Winner gets added to the relocation guide I give every buyer moving here.

**8. The first-home memory**
*Pair with: throwback photo or a front-door shot.*
Tell me about your first place. The worse, the better.
Mine: [2–3 sentences, your real story — the rent, the flaw, the thing you loved anyway].
Everyone starts somewhere. Drop yours below.

**9. The trade-secret admission**
*Pair with: to-camera photo.*
An opinion most agents won't say out loud: [YOUR REAL CONTRARIAN TAKE, e.g. "open houses mostly serve the agent, not the seller" or "the 'perfect starter home' is a myth that keeps renters renting"].
[2 sentences defending it honestly, including the case where you're wrong.]
Plenty of agents disagree with me on this one. Comments are open.

**10. The guess-the-price**
*Pair with: carousel of 3–4 photos of a PUBLIC active or sold listing (yours, or with the listing agent's permission and credit).*
[BEDS] bed, [BATHS] bath, [SQFT] sqft in [NEIGHBORHOOD]. [ONE FEATURE]. No filters on the photos.
Guess the [list/sold] price. Closest without going over gets bragging rights. Answer's in the comments tomorrow.

## Lead-Gen

**11. The buyer checklist offer**
*Pair with: photo of the actual checklist/guide.*
I made a one-page checklist for buying in [CITY]: every step from "thinking about it" to keys, with the local costs filled in.
It's the same one my clients use. No signup page, no drip campaign. DM me "[KEYWORD]" and I'll just send it.

**12. The home-value offer (sellers)**
*Pair with: photo of you outside a recent listing.*
The online estimate hasn't been inside your house. I haven't either, but I've been inside the ones it's comparing yours to.
If you want a real range (actual recent sales on and around your street, with the adjustments explained), DM me "[KEYWORD]" with your neighborhood. Takes me a day, costs you nothing, and no, it doesn't obligate you to list.

**13. The sitting-inventory list**
*Pair with: b-roll touring a listing.*
Some of the best buys in [CITY] right now are the listings everyone scrolled past 30 days ago.
Sitting doesn't mean broken. It usually means priced wrong at launch, and that's negotiable now.
DM me "[KEYWORD]" and I'll send you the current sit-list for [CITY]: what's been on 30+ days and what I'd offer on each.

**14. The relocation guide**
*Pair with: your best skyline/landmark shot of [CITY].*
Moving to [CITY]? I built the guide I wish existed: neighborhoods compared honestly, commute realities, where locals actually eat, and what each area costs right now.
DM me "[KEYWORD]" and it's yours. And if you just want to ask a human questions about living here, that works too. That's kind of the whole job.

**15. The open-house follow-through**
*Pair with: photo from this weekend's open house.*
If you walked through [LISTING ADDRESS / "my open house in NEIGHBORHOOD"] this weekend: the full info sheet, the disclosure package, and the payment breakdown are all one DM away. Send "[KEYWORD]."
If you missed it: the next showing window is [DAY/TIME], and yes, private tours are a thing. Same DM.
