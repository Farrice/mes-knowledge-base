# Faceless / B-Roll Voiceover Scripts — 2

For weeks you can't film yourself. Record the voiceover on your phone in a quiet room; lay it over b-roll (your own listing footage, neighborhood driving shots, or licensed stock). On-screen text carries every number. 60–90 seconds each. Listing-agnostic: nothing here depends on having an active listing.

---

## Script 1 — "The real monthly cost of a $[PRICE] home" (~75s)

**Prep:** get one payment scenario from your lender partner at today's rate. Label everything an estimate. Fill every bracket before recording.

| Beat | VO | On-screen / b-roll |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | "Everyone talks about the price. Nobody talks about the payment. Let's fix that." | Text: THE REAL MONTHLY COST · $[PRICE] HOME. B-roll: neighborhood exterior. |
| 5–20s | "Take a $[PRICE] home in [CITY], [DOWN]% down, at [RATE]% — this month's rate, and rates move, so check the date on this video. Principal and interest: about $[P&I] a month." | Payment stack graphic building line by line. |
| 20–35s | "Now the lines the calculators skip. Property taxes: around $[TAX ESTIMATE] a month here in [COUNTY]. Insurance: call it $[INSURANCE ESTIMATE]. Putting less than 20 down? Mortgage insurance adds roughly $[MI ESTIMATE]." | Each line stamps onto the stack. Source line: "estimates · [MONTH YEAR] · your numbers will differ" |
| 35–50s | "All-in: about $[TOTAL] a month. Which is either terrifying or surprisingly close to your rent — and that's exactly the comparison worth making. Part of that payment pays down your own balance every month. Rent doesn't." | Total circled. Side-by-side: $[TOTAL] vs "your rent." |
| 50–65s | "One more real cost: upkeep. A common rule of thumb is setting aside about 1% of the home's value a year. Nobody escrows it for you. Budget it anyway." | Text: + upkeep fund |
| 65–75s | "Want this exact breakdown with your numbers instead of mine? DM me the word '[KEYWORD]' and I'll run it with a local lender. Free, and you keep the spreadsheet. I'm [AGENT NAME] with [BROKERAGE] in [CITY]." | End card: handle, name, brokerage, license #, keyword. |

## Script 2 — "How to read a listing like an agent" (~80s)

Educational, evergreen, works with any b-roll of listing photos/screens (use your own listings or generic footage, not a competitor's live listing).

| Beat | VO | On-screen / b-roll |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | "Agents read listings differently than buyers do. Here's the decoder." | Text: READ A LISTING LIKE AN AGENT. B-roll: scrolling a listings app. |
| 5–20s | "First: days on market, not just price. Fresh listing at a fair price, expect company. Thirty-plus days, you've got negotiating room, and the only question is why it sat: price, condition, or photos. Two of those three are fixable at the offer stage." | Highlight DOM field. |
| 20–35s | "Second: the price history tab. A cut tells you the seller's getting realistic. Relisted after a fell-through deal? Ask your agent to find out what happened — sometimes it's the buyer's financing, sometimes it's the inspection. Very different answers." | Highlight price history. |
| 35–50s | "Third: what the photos avoid. Count the rooms in the photos against the bedroom count. No shot of the front of the house, the backyard, or one of the baths is usually an answer in itself. That's not a reason to skip it. It's a reason to tour it." | B-roll: photo grid, one slot conspicuously empty. |
| 50–65s | "Fourth: the words. 'Cosmetic updates' means bring paint money. 'Sold as-is' means the seller won't negotiate repairs, not that you can't inspect. 'Motivated seller' means write the offer." | Terms flash with translations. |
| 65–80s | "The listing is marketing. The disclosures, the reports, and a walkthrough are the truth. Want a second set of eyes on something you've been watching? Send me the address — DM '[KEYWORD]'. I'm [AGENT NAME] with [BROKERAGE], and I do this all day in [CITY]." | End card: handle, name, brokerage, license #, keyword. |
