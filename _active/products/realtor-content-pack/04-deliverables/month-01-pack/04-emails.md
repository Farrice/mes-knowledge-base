# Email Templates — 2

Send from your normal address, plain formatting, like a person wrote it. One link maximum per email. Short paragraphs win on phones.

---

## Email 1 — Sphere-of-Influence Monthly Update

Send to: past clients, friends, anyone who knows you. Monthly, same week each month. The job is staying the person they think of first, not selling.

**Subject options (pick one, rotate monthly):**
- What's actually happening in [CITY] real estate ([MONTH])
- [CITY] market, translated ([MONTH])
- The [MONTH] numbers, without the spin

**Body:**

Hi [FIRST NAME],

Quick [MONTH] read on the [CITY] market. Three things worth knowing:

**1. [THE HEADLINE NUMBER].** [Median price / DOM / inventory — one current stat from your MLS report, with source and month.] In plain English: [one sentence on what it means for regular people, not agents].

**2. [SOMETHING YOU SAW FIRSTHAND].** [2–3 sentences from your actual month: a multiple-offer situation, a deal that fell apart and why, a negotiation that worked. No names, no addresses. This is the part no portal can give them.]

**3. [SOMETHING LOCAL, NOT REAL ESTATE].** [One sentence: a restaurant opening, an event coming up, a road project everyone's grumbling about. You're a local resource, not a listing feed.]

If you're wondering what all this means for your own place, I'll run a quick value update whenever you want one. No listing appointment attached, ever.

And as always: if someone you know is thinking about buying or selling, I promise to take absurdly good care of them.

[AGENT NAME]
[BROKERAGE] · [LICENSE #] · [PHONE]

*P.S. [One line, personal: what you're reading, where you'll be this weekend, your team's game. The P.S. gets read more than paragraph two.]*

## Email 2 — Post-Open-House Follow-Up

Send to: everyone who signed in. Within 24 hours, same-day evening is better. One email, two audiences: people who liked the house, and people who were really shopping the market.

**Subject options:**
- Everything on [LISTING ADDRESS] (from Sunday's open house)
- The info sheet you didn't have to ask for

**Body:**

Hi [FIRST NAME],

Thanks for coming through [LISTING ADDRESS] this weekend. As promised, everything in one place:

- **The numbers:** $[PRICE] · [BEDS] bed / [BATHS] bath · [SQFT] sqft · [ONE HEADLINE FEATURE].
- **The full package:** disclosures, reports, and the detailed sheet are ready. Reply "send it" and it's in your inbox today.
- **Payment reality check:** if you want to know what this house actually costs per month with your down payment, my lender partner can run it in about 10 minutes, no credit pull. Reply "numbers."

If this one wasn't it, that's genuinely useful too. Tell me the one thing that didn't fit (size, street, layout, price) and I'll flag you when something closer hits the market. I see them before the portals show them.

Either way, no drip campaign follows this email. It's just me, and I answer.

[AGENT NAME]
[BROKERAGE] · [LICENSE #] · [PHONE]

*P.S. Offers [are being reviewed on DATE / are welcome as they come]. If you're more interested than you let on Sunday, let's talk this week rather than next.*

**Customization note:** the P.S. must reflect the real offer situation. Never imply deadlines or competing offers that don't exist.
