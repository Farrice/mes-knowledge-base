# Realtor AI Revenue Desk: Founding Pilot

**Status:** Proposal for warm-market validation. Does not replace `OFFER.md` until Farrice approves the chassis and a buyer pays.

## Decision

Do not launch the $49 content subscription. Do not sell a 40-skill download. Do not send the stale storefront or outreach copy.

Test a bounded one-time installation for active agents and small teams. The buyer pays for personalization, installation, verified workflows, and first usable outputs. The skill files are what they keep.

## Who it is for

An active residential agent or small team who:

- controls at least one listing going live within 30 days;
- has a database of past, quiet, or active leads;
- already uses or is willing to use Claude Desktop on a paid plan;
- wants repeatable work without hiring another assistant;
- agrees that founding work can become a named case study after broker and client-safe review.

Not for agencies, template collectors, brand-new agents with no live inputs, or anyone expecting autonomous sending, guaranteed rankings, or guaranteed leads.

## One-line promise

In one bounded install, your Claude workspace learns your voice and market, then produces three pieces of real work from your business: a source-grounded local brief, a launch for one live listing, and a value-led follow-up sequence for quiet leads.

## What gets installed

### 1. Agent Brain

One editable context file containing positioning, market, audience, voice, proof, calls to action, prohibited claims, and broker/compliance boundaries. Every downstream module reads this file.

### 2. Local Authority Desk

Produces one current market brief with source links, three local content angles, and one publishable draft. MLS-only fields remain owner-verification slots.

### 3. Listing Launch Desk

Uses the existing golden-reference Jen system to produce a shoot sheet, coming-soon or just-listed sequence where permitted, reel structure, caption, and seller-forwardable update for one real listing.

### 4. Lead Reactivation Desk

Produces a three-email and two-text value-led sequence for one owner-approved segment of quiet leads. Nothing sends automatically. The client and broker own final review and sending.

### Take-home package

- Four installed skill modules
- Agent Brain file
- One-page command card
- One real local-market brief
- One real listing launch package
- One real lead-reactivation sequence
- One 75-minute activation call
- One 30-minute debrief
- 14 calendar days of bounded email support
