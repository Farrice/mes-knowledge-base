## Executive Summary

Insight Vectors is Luke Iha's **meta-methodology** — the thinking engine that GENERATES mechanisms, hooks, and copy angles rather than just deploying them. While `luke-iha-million-dollar-mechanisms` teaches you HOW to engineer mechanisms (Matrix, SIN filter, naming), Insight Vectors teaches you WHERE mechanisms COME FROM by reverse-engineering the structure of human epiphany itself.

**Core insight**: People walk around with incomplete mental models (maps) of how the world works. An Insight Vector is a specific type of **systems-thinking exploitation** that identifies a structural weakness in those mental models and introduces a new idea into that gap. When the idea takes hold, it rewrites the map — and the person acts (buys, subscribes, engages).

## Core Framework Extracted

### The Systems Grammar (12+ Insight Vector Types)

Luke identifies that mental models can be analyzed through **systems thinking** categories. Each category produces a distinct type of insight vector:

| Category | Insight Vector Type | Example Pattern |
|----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Causality** | Reverse Causation | "You're not fat because of slow metabolism — your fat slows your metabolism" |
| **Causality** | Multiple Causes | "Weight gain isn't calories. It's sleep + hormones + inflammation together" |
| **Causality** | Hidden Condition | "Writing goals works — but ONLY if they stay visible (commitment evaporation)" |
| **Patterns** | System Archetypes | "What's your metabolism TYPE?" (people love classification) |
| **Patterns** | Virtuous Cycles | "Once you have energy → you exercise → sleep improves → more energy" |
| **Patterns** | Vicious Cycles | "Quick-fix diets → muscle loss → slower metabolism → harder next time" |
| **Control Points** | Leading Indicators | "Morning temperature predicts weight gain 3-4 weeks before the scale" |
| **Control Points** | Hidden Constraints | "The real limit isn't willpower — it's cellular inflammation blocking everything" |
| **Control Points** | False Assumptions | "You think more A = more B. But subtracting C gives you more B" |
| **Control Points** | Missing Variables | "Everyone tracks X but nobody measures Y, which actually matters more" |
| **Model Structure** | Model Limitations | "Fewer Lego pieces → more creative structures (constraints = liberation)" |
| **Model Structure** | Structural Revelations | "Your body runs a 'night shift' crew — eat late and they stop burning" |

### The 8-Fold Elaboration Structure

Once you have a raw insight vector, Luke's 8-beat structure makes it deployable:

1. **Paradoxical Question** — hooks attention by violating expectations
2. **UMP/UMS** — mechanism that explains WHY the insight is true
3. **Trigger** — the emotional moment that creates urgency
4. **Testable Proof** — evidence that validates the insight
5. **Intensifiers** — elements that make it feel more urgent/relevant
6. **Myths & Mistakes** — wrong beliefs the insight corrects
7. **Cause** — the root explanation
8. **[Implied: The Resolution]** — what to do about it (your product)
