## Quality Gate

Before using any option publicly, confirm:

- Cooz is comfortable with the likeness and visual tone.
- The banner does not imply a real client/session unless verified.
- The copy is readable on desktop and mobile crop zones.
- The asset feels serious, premium, and grounded.
- The asset does not feel like commodity gym marketing, wellness branding, or fake luxury.
