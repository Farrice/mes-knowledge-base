## Uniqueness Audit: What Sam Parr Does That Nobody Else in the Roster Does

Oren teaches taste as **evaluation** (CEV matrix, connoisseurship progression, insider codes). Sam Parr teaches taste as **acquisition** — a 4-step manufacturing process anyone can execute in 3-5 months.

| Dimension | Oren (Existing) | Sam Parr (New) |
|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| **Core metaphor** | Sommelier — refining what you already sense | Guitar student — learning from zero through copying |
| **Entry point** | You already have taste, let's articulate it | You don't know what you like yet, let's find out |
| **Method** | CEV evaluation, insider codes, quality curation | Decide → Copy → Rules → History (4-step process) |
| **Time horizon** | Lifelong journey, weekly practice doses | 3-5 months to "better than 90%" |
| **Identity relationship** | Taste expresses identity | Identity DRIVES taste choices (the root) |
| **History usage** | Canon appreciation (know the classics) | Lineage tracing (follow the thread backward) |
| **Breaking rules** | Not emphasized | Explicit progression: good taste → great taste = break rules |
| **Practical focus** | Fashion, luxury, brand | Fashion, web design, music, naming, products |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The stacking opportunity is massive.** Oren answers "How do I evaluate quality?" Sam Parr answers "How do I develop the ability to evaluate quality in the first place?" They're sequential — Sam Parr is the on-ramp, Oren is the highway.

## Gap Fill Analysis

**Before Sam Parr**: The Antigravity system can evaluate taste (Oren), execute with taste (various experts), and apply taste to brand (Oren luxury). But it has **no system for BUILDING taste from scratch** — no on-ramp.

**After Sam Parr**: The system gains a concrete, time-boxed, domain-agnostic taste acquisition methodology. Any user can go from "I know what I like but can't articulate why" to "I can speak the language of quality in any domain" in 3-5 months.

**Critical gap this fills**: The `/design-taste-gate` workflow currently evaluates taste quality but has no pathway for users who FAIL the gate. Sam Parr provides the remediation path.
