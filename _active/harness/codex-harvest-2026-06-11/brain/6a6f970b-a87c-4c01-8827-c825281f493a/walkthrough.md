## Key Design Decisions

1. **Sam Parr = acquisition, Oren = evaluation** — explicitly separated to prevent confusion. Sam Parr is the on-ramp; Oren is the highway.
2. **14 genius patterns from a single source** — high density because Sam's presentation is systematically structured (4-step process with clear examples at each level).
3. **Tier 3 stacking with Oren** — the `/taste-cev` workflow chains the two experts sequentially rather than blending them.
4. **Domain-agnostic foundation** — Tier 1 workflows work for fashion, web design, writing, music, brand, naming, or any creative domain. Tier 2 includes domain-specific variants (web, naming).
