# 48-Hour Audit Customer Runway Sprint

## Verdict

Do not spend the $100 on ads, a polished website, or a digital product funnel first.

The highest-likelihood path to $200-$300 in 48 hours is one paid, fast-turnaround audit sold through direct outreach to people who already have enough business activity to value a quick outside diagnosis.

The offer:

> I will review your website, profile, offer, follow-up path, and visible content, then send a 24-hour action plan with the 5 fastest fixes to improve trust, clarity, and lead flow from what you already have.

## Success Target

| Target | Number |
|---|---:|
| Cash target | $200-$300 |
| Paid customers needed | 1 |
| Price | $200 standard, $150 emergency close, $300 strong fit |
| Outreach volume | 40-60 direct asks |
| Required conversations | 5-8 |
| Delivery window | 24 hours after payment |

## $100 Budget

Spend as little as possible until a buyer is active.

| Use | Amount | Rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Payment link / invoice | $0 upfront | Use Stripe Payment Links, PayPal, Square, or Cash App depending on what the buyer already trusts. |
| Domain / landing page | $0 | Use a one-page Google Doc, Notion page, or simple markdown/PDF proof page first. |
| Lead research | $0-$20 | Only spend if it speeds up finding contact info for warm or obvious-fit prospects. |
| Loom / screen recording | $0 | Use free recording if needed for a sample teardown. |
| Emergency reserve | $80-$100 | Keep this for life logistics, not speculative traffic. |

Hard rule: no paid ads until at least one person has paid or three qualified buyers have explicitly asked for the offer link.

## Best Buyer Today

Prioritize buyers with visible business activity and obvious customer-facing leaks:

- local service businesses with weak websites or Google profiles
- consultants, coaches, and boutique agencies with unclear offers
- realtors, med spas, contractors, wellness businesses, photographers, trainers, dentists, accountants, attorneys, clinics
- founder-led experts already posting on LinkedIn but not converting attention into clear next steps
- anyone in your warm network who owns or directly supports a business

Avoid:

- broke creators
- pre-revenue founders
- people who want free brainstorming
- big companies with slow approvals
- businesses that need deep analytics access before anything useful can be said

## The First 4 Hours

### Hour 1: Set The Checkout And Proof

Create:

1. One payment link for `24-Hour Business Asset Audit - $200`.
2. One short proof/sample page with:
   - who it is for
   - what they get
   - turnaround time
   - what to send you
   - one mini example of the kind of fix you spot

Do not build a website unless the payment link or doc is impossible to share.

### Hour 2: Build The First 40

List 40 prospects:

- 15 warm contacts
- 10 referral sources
- 15 cold-but-obvious local or LinkedIn prospects

Each prospect needs:

- name
- business
- contact path
- one visible issue
- price to offer
- follow-up time

### Hours 3-4: Send 40 Direct Asks

Send warm asks first. Then referral asks. Then cold DMs or emails with one specific observation.

Minimum standard: no generic "do you need marketing help?" messages. Every message needs one visible reason you thought of them.

## Scripts

### Warm Contact

```text
Hey [Name], quick ask.

I am trying to generate quick runway this week, so I am selling a few useful 24-hour business audits instead of building a long funnel.

For $200, I review a business's website/profile/offer/follow-up path and send a short action plan with the 5 fastest fixes to improve trust, clarity, and lead flow from what is already there.

I thought of you because [specific reason].

Would you want me to do one for [business/project] today?
```

### Referral Ask

```text
Hey [Name], do you know one business owner who would pay $200 for a useful 24-hour outside review of their website, Google/profile, offer, and follow-up?

I am doing a few Business Asset Audits this week. It is practical: 5 fixes, 3 content/outreach ideas, and 1 clearer offer recommendation.

If someone is a fit, I can turn it around in 24 hours.
```

### Cold But Specific

```text
Hi [Name], I noticed one thing on [business/profile]:

[specific visible issue].

That can cost trust because [plain consequence].

I am doing 24-hour Business Asset Audits this week for $200. I review the website/profile/offer/follow-up and send 5 priority fixes, 3 content or outreach ideas, and 1 offer improvement.

Want me to send what I would review for [Business]?
```

### Interested Reply

```text
Perfect. Send me the best website/profile links and anything specific you want me to check.

The audit is $200. I can start once payment is sent and deliver it within 24 hours.

[payment link]
```

### Hesitation Reply

```text
Totally fair.

The reason I am keeping it small is so it does not become a big consulting engagement. You will get a concrete action plan either way: 5 fixes, 3 content/outreach ideas, and 1 offer improvement.

If it feels useful after delivery, we can talk about implementation. If not, you still have the action plan.
```

## Delivery Promise

Deliver a simple document, not a giant report.

Sections:

1. Executive snapshot
2. Biggest trust or clarity leak
3. Best hidden asset
4. 5 priority fixes
5. 3 content or outreach ideas
6. 1 clearer offer line
7. optional implementation next step

The document is good enough when the buyer can implement at least one fix within 24 hours.

## 48-Hour Schedule

### Day 1

| Time | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1 hr | payment link + proof page | link ready |
| 1-2 hr | build 40-prospect list | tracker ready |
| 2-5 hr | send first 40 asks | conversations started |
| 5-7 hr | follow up warmest replies | payment ask made |
| evening | deliver if paid | first audit shipped |

### Day 2

| Time | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| morning | follow up every non-response | second touch |
| midday | send 20 more targeted asks | new conversations |
| afternoon | send one public proof post | inbound/referral signal |
| evening | deliver/close/upsell | cash + next step |

## Public Proof Post

```text
I am doing a few 24-hour Business Asset Audits this week.

The pattern I am looking for:

- the website says what the business does, but not why someone should trust it now
- the offer is real, but buried
- the profile has activity, but no clear next step
- follow-up depends on memory instead of a simple path

I am reviewing websites/profiles/offers and sending a short action plan:

5 fixes. 3 content/outreach ideas. 1 clearer offer line.

If you want one, send me your business link.
```

## Kill Criteria

If 25 direct asks produce zero replies, the message is too vague or the prospect list is too cold.

Fix by:

- adding a specific visible observation
- lowering the ask to "want me to send what I would review?"
- contacting warmer/referral sources before more cold outreach

If replies happen but no payment happens, the close is too soft.

Fix by:

- restating the deliverable
- asking for payment before starting
- offering $150 for the first same-day slot only

## Next Upsell

If the audit lands well, offer one implementation sprint:

> I can implement the top 1-2 fixes for $300-$500 and have it done this week.

Only offer this after the paid audit or when the buyer asks for help implementing.
