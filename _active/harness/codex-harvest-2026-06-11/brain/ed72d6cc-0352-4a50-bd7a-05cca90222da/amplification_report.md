## Scan C: Top Stacking Chains

### 1. `/design-system-forge` × Oren `/taste-cev`
**What it produces:** A DESIGN.md that has passed taste calibration — not just technically correct but aesthetically excellent by expert judgment standards.
**Deploy when:** Building DESIGN.md for a premium brand where "good enough" isn't acceptable.
**Example chain:** Forge draft DESIGN.md → Run CEV Matrix evaluation → Score Craft/Effort/Vision → Iterate on lowest dimension → Re-score until all dimensions ≥8.

### 2. `/brand-dna-extraction` × Grace `/grace-city-blueprint`
**What it produces:** Full city-level media brand with extracted visual identity from the local market.
**Deploy when:** Building a new-media brand for a specific geographic market (real estate, local business).
**Example chain:** Extract brand DNA from top 3 local competitors → Grace maps media landscape → Forge DESIGN.md that differentiates from extracted competitor patterns.

### 3. `/website-build` × Sean Kochel `/design-first-build`
**What it produces:** A research-validated, conversion-optimized website with professional design system underneath.
**Deploy when:** Building a client-facing website where conversion matters as much as aesthetics.
**Example chain:** Kochel runs market research → DESIGN.md forged from findings → Website built from DESIGN.md → Anti-slop audit → Iteration loop.

### 4. `/presentation-build` × Tim Runia `/story-compass`
**What it produces:** Presentations with narrative structural integrity — not just beautiful slides but a story that transforms the viewer.
**Deploy when:** Any presentation meant to persuade, sell, or educate.
**Example chain:** Story compass diagnoses Want-Tension-Change → Presentation build uses narrative arc as structural skeleton → Each slide maps to a story beat.

### 5. `/anti-slop-audit` × Kallaway `/five-input-content-gate`
**What it produces:** A design that passes both visual quality AND content psychology gates.
**Deploy when:** Any design asset that needs to convert (landing pages, sales presentations, product pages).
**Example chain:** Anti-slop audit passes visual quality → Five-Input gate ensures the content within the design hits all 5 universal inputs → Combined score determines ship-readiness.

### 6. `/design-skill-enshrine` × Nick Saraev `/self-evolve`
**What it produces:** Design skills that self-improve over time through automated annealing.
**Deploy when:** A design skill has been used 5+ times and should auto-optimize based on accumulated feedback.
**Example chain:** Enshrine the initial skill → After 5 uses, run self-evolve → Compare outputs against initial benchmarks → Update the enshrined skill with learned improvements.

### 7. `/multi-format-deploy` × Greg Hoffman `/emotional-value`
**What it produces:** Cross-format brand expression that doesn't just look consistent but *feels* emotionally consistent across every touchpoint.
**Deploy when:** Deploying a brand across channels where emotional resonance matters (Nike-style, not just template consistency).
