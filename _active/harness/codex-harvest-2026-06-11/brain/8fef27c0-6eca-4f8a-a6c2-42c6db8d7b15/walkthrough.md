## Architecture Decision: Why "Brand Operations"

The existing Seth Godin skills covered **philosophy** (shipping, resistance, SVA) and **mechanics** (viral engineering, sneezer strategy). This source material filled the operational middle — the day-to-day brand-building discipline that connects "why" to "how":

- Brand promise definition and maintenance
- Metric hygiene (false proxy purge)
- Consistency as a discipline, not a feel
- AI integration without trust erosion
- Culture as the marketing engine

This creates a complete three-skill stack: **Philosophy → Brand Operations → Viral Engineering**.
