# Profile DM-Readiness Checklist

## Purpose

Use this before Cooz starts warm outreach, connection requests, referral DMs, or follower-list conversations.

The profile has to do enough work that the DM does not feel random.

## 3-Second Profile Test

A visitor should understand these fast:

| Question | Must Be Clear |
|---|---|
| What does Cooz do? | Private strength + energy coaching. |
| Who is it for? | Professionals over 40. |
| What problem does he solve? | Their body is starting to fall behind their life. |
| Where/how does he deliver? | Burbank private coaching and remote daily support. |
| Why trust him? | Some clients stay 3-9 years. |
| What is the next step? | Triage Audit. |

If any answer takes more than a few seconds, fix the profile before sending DMs.

## Cognitive Load Gate

The first screen should not force the visitor to decode:

- Resurrection
- Brave Choice
- every offer path
- every client proof name
- Mari's 16-lb result
- ESA or executive body language
- a long list of services

The first screen should show:

- strength
- energy
- consistency
- professionals over 40
- body falling behind life
- Burbank + remote
- Triage Audit

## Attention Gate

The profile should create recognition without clickbait.

Passes:

- "Your body can start falling behind before your life looks like a problem."
- "The same Monday restart keeps showing up."
- "The gym keeps losing to the calendar."
- "The body you used to trust starts feeling less familiar."

Fails:

- "Unlock your best self."
- "High performers need optimization."
- "Transform your life in 90 days."
- "The Resurrection Coach will change everything."
- "Are you ready to level up?"

## Proof Safety Gate

Before publishing, answer:

- Are named clients approved for public use?
- Are years accurate?
- Are job titles and industries approved?
- Is Mari's measurement method verified?
- Are exceptional results labeled as exceptional?
- Is any client identifiable from a story they did not approve?

Default safe proof:

> Some clients have stayed 3-9 years because the work keeps paying them back.

Use named proof only after permission.

## Featured Section Gate

Featured should answer three jobs:

1. What do I do next?
2. What useful thing can I inspect now?
3. Why should I trust this person?

Recommended order:

1. Start With The Triage Audit
2. The 4 PM Fog Audit
3. Why Clients Stay 3-9 Years

Do not lead Featured with a brand manifesto.

## DM Alignment Gate

The first DM should echo the profile, not introduce a new frame.

Good DM language:

```text
I am reaching out because I work with professionals over 40 whose strength, energy, or consistency is starting to slip under a real schedule.
```

Good soft ask:

```text
If this is not you, no pressure. But if someone came to mind, I would appreciate the intro.
```

Good direct ask:

```text
If that pattern sounds familiar, I can start with a Triage Audit. I listen, diagnose what is breaking, and recommend one path.
```

Avoid:

- pitching all offers
- starting with price
- using "Resurrection" before context
- pretending intimacy
- generic "checking in"
- asking for a call before giving the reason

## Final Go / No-Go

Go if:

- Headline names buyer, problem, and delivery.
- About section is scannable.
- Featured has one clear next step.
- Proof is permission-safe.
- No public claim overreaches.
- A warm referral would understand what Cooz does.

No-go if:

- The profile sounds like generic fitness.
- The profile opens with brand language before category.
- The reader must decode multiple frameworks.
- The profile leans on unverified body-change claims.
- There is no obvious next step.

## Scorecard

Score each from 1-5.

| Category | Score |
|---|---:|
| Cold clarity | /5 |
| Attention | /5 |
| Buyer recognition | /5 |
| Proof safety | /5 |
| Premium differentiation | /5 |
| DM support | /5 |
| Triage Audit clarity | /5 |

Minimum launch score: 30/35.

If below 30, fix headline, About opening, Featured order, or proof section before outreach.
