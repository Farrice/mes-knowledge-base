# Coach Cooz LinkedIn Profile V4 - Three Variants

## How To Use These Variants

The launch-ready V4 profile is the recommended version.

These three variants are for comparison, testing, or future rotation.

Use them in this order:

1. Variant A if clarity is the priority.
2. Variant B if attention and emotional recognition are the priority.
3. Variant C if premium differentiation and Triage Audit conversion are the priority.

## Variant A - Plain Problem-Led

### Best For

Warm outreach, referrals, and profile visitors who need immediate clarity.

### Headline

> I help professionals over 40 rebuild strength, energy, and consistency when life keeps breaking the plan | Burbank + remote

### Banner

```text
When life keeps breaking the plan, rebuild the plan around life.

Strength + energy coaching for professionals over 40
Burbank private coaching + remote daily support
```

### About Opening

```text
Most people who find me are not beginners.

They know how to work hard.
They know they need to train.
They know the basics.

That is not the problem.

The problem is that the plan keeps losing to the week they actually have.

Work gets loud.
Travel moves the schedule.
Sleep gets thin.
The 4 PM fog comes back.
Monday becomes the restart again.

I help professionals over 40 rebuild strength, energy, and consistency when life keeps breaking the plan.
```

### Why It Works

- Lowest cognitive load.
- Most direct fit for DMs.
- Strongest for referral language.
- Less emotionally rich than Variant B, but easier to understand fast.

## Variant B - Body Falling Behind Life

### Best For

Attention, emotional recognition, and profile visitors coming from a strong post.

### Headline

> Private strength + energy coach for professionals over 40 whose body is starting to fall behind their life | Burbank + remote

### Banner

```text
Your life got bigger.
Your body needs support that can keep up.

Private strength + energy coaching
Burbank + remote daily support
```

### About Opening

```text
Your body can start falling behind before your life looks like a problem.

You still work.
You still show up.
People still count on you.

But you know something changed.

You do not recover like you used to.
The calendar wins more often.
The mirror is harder to ignore.
The body you used to trust feels less familiar.

I coach professionals over 40 who want to rebuild strength, energy, and consistency before the diminished version becomes normal.
```

### Why It Works

- Strongest emotional tension.
- Best match for the V3 avatar.
- Keeps Resurrection energy without making the cold buyer decode the word.
- Slightly heavier than Variant A, but more attention-grabbing.

## Variant C - Diagnosis Before Training

### Best For

Premium buyers, skeptics, and people comparing Cooz to trainers, gyms, apps, or online programs.

### Headline

> Strength + energy coaching after 40 | I diagnose what keeps breaking before I write the plan | Burbank + remote

### Banner

```text
Do not buy another plan until you know what keeps breaking.

Triage Audit first
Private strength + energy coaching after 40
```

### About Opening

```text
If the same restart keeps failing, the first job is not another workout.

The first job is diagnosis.

What is actually breaking?

Training?
Recovery?
Food?
Sleep?
Travel?
Pain?
Accountability?
The way the week is built?

I coach professionals over 40 who want to rebuild strength, energy, and consistency with a plan that fits the life they actually have.
```

### Why It Works

- Strongest premium differentiation.
- Makes the Triage Audit feel necessary.
- Helps justify price by moving the buyer out of "workout" comparison.
- Less warm than Variant B, but stronger for skeptical buyers.

## Variant Recommendation

Use Variant B as the public launch base.

It has the best balance:

- enough attention to stop the right person
- clear category
- simple buyer language
- emotional tension without confusion

Borrow from Variant C for Featured and DM follow-up because diagnosis is the strongest conversion frame.

Borrow from Variant A for referral asks because plain language gets named prospects faster.
