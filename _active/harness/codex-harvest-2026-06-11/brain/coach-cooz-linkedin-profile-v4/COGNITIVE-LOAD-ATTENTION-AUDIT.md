# Cognitive Load And Attention Audit

## Executive Read

The V3 profile is strong, but it asks a cold visitor to process too much too soon.

The copy has truth. The issue is sequence.

The reader has to understand:

- who Cooz is for
- what he does
- whether this is local or remote
- why this is not commodity training
- what "Resurrection" means
- what Brave Choice means
- who the proof names are
- what the Triage Audit is
- whether they should reach out

That is too much for the first profile visit.

V4 should reduce the first-screen job to five things:

1. This is strength and energy coaching.
2. It is for professionals over 40.
3. The problem is the body starting to fall behind the life.
4. He works in Burbank and remote.
5. The next step is a Triage Audit.

Everything else earns its place after that.

## Current Profile Friction

| Triage Audit appears late | Strong conversion doorway. | The reader may not know the next step soon enough. | Bring it into the About earlier and Featured first. |

## First 3-Second Test

The visitor should understand this without opening every section:

> Coach Cooz helps professionals over 40 rebuild strength, energy, and consistency when their body is starting to fall behind their life.

If the visitor only remembers one thing, make it that.

## Attention Strategy

The profile should not try to be loud.

It should create a quiet stop:

> "That is the thing I have been minimizing."

The strongest attention lines are not clever. They are recognition lines:

- Your body can start falling behind before your life looks like a problem.
- You can still be performing and still know something is off.
- The old restart stops working when the week you have is not the week the plan was built for.
- The third Monday restart is not a character flaw. It is data.

## What To Cut Or Move Lower

Cut from launch profile:

- "Mari gained 16 lbs..." until measurement context is confirmed.
- Full list of named clients unless permission is cleared.
- "Executive Body System" as public language.
- Too many offer names in the Experience section.
- Any language that makes the buyer decide whether this is fitness, therapy, spirituality, or executive coaching.

Move lower:

- Brave Choice.
- Resurrection.
- Long proof list.
- Offer architecture details.

Keep high:

- professionals over 40
- strength, energy, consistency
- body falling behind life
- Burbank private coaching
- remote daily support
- Triage Audit

## V4 Profile Sequence

Use this order:

1. Headline: clear category plus buyer pain.
2. Banner: emotional promise plus delivery.
3. About opening: recognizable tension.
4. About body: what Cooz does and how.
5. Proof: long-arc client relationships, permission-safe.
6. Method layer: Resurrection and Brave Choice, briefly.
7. CTA: Triage Audit.
8. Featured: audit first, useful diagnostic second, proof third.

## V4 Standard

The final profile should feel:

- plain
- serious
- specific
- emotionally alive
- premium without saying "premium"
- easy to scan
- easy to DM from

It should not feel:

- poetic before clear
- overexplained
- like a generic trainer bio
- like a wellness profile
- like a performance-optimization funnel
- like AI polished it
