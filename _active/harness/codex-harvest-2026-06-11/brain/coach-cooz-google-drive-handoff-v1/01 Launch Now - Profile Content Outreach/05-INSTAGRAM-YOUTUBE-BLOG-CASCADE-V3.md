6. How many alcohol nights this week?

Track these five lines for seven days:

coffee
protein
water
alcohol
bedtime

Save this before the next week starts.

## IG Reel 1: Your Plan Only Works On Your Best Week

Goal: shares  
Length: 45-60 sec  
Format: face-to-camera, Cooz in gym or office, no motivational music required

### Script

```text
If your workouts keep disappearing by Wednesday, I would not start by attacking your discipline.

I would look at the plan.

Most people build the plan for the week they wish they had.

Good sleep.
Open calendar.
Groceries in the house.
No travel.
No kid schedule.
No late meeting.

Then their real week shows up and the plan has no answer.

The fix is simple, but people resist it:

Write the minimum version before the week gets messy.

Bad sleep version.
Travel version.
Long workday version.

Most people are stuck between perfect and nothing.

The middle option is where consistency gets built.
```

### Caption

```text
The minimum version is not the failure version.

It is the version that keeps the thread.

Send this to the person who keeps restarting every Monday.
```
