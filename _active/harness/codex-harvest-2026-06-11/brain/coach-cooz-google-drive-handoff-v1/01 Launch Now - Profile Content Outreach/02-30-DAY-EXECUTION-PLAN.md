# Coach Cooz V6.1 - Corrected 30-Day Execution Plan

Purpose: Prove the acquisition loop before expanding channels.

Default focus for 30 days:

1. Long-arc referrals
2. Google Business Profile/local proof
3. LinkedIn profile and 3 posts/week
4. Triage Audit conversion tracking

Do not launch YouTube, blog, podcast expansion, broad Meta ads, or a `$197` trial during this 30-day validation sprint.

## North Star

By Day 30, the plan must prove whether Cooz can reliably turn trust into booked Triage Audits and paid clients.

Minimum proof threshold:

| Metric | Day 30 Target |
|---|---:|
| Referral conversations completed | 20 |
| Named prospects identified | 12+ |
| Actual warm intros sent | 4+ |
| Triage Audits booked | 3+ |
| Clients closed | 1+ |
| Serious active follow-ups | 2+ if 0 closed |
| New Google reviews live | 4-6 |
| LinkedIn posts shipped | 12 |
| LinkedIn baseline dashboard complete | Yes |
| Audit hours per close tracked | Yes |

If these thresholds are missed, do not add channels. Diagnose the broken link.

## Week 1 - Foundation And Baselines

### Referral track

Run 5 referral conversations with the highest-trust long-arc clients first: Mike, Robin, Corey, Sam, Carron.

Use two scripts so the positioning can be tested.

Script A - brand-led:

> I'm bringing on a small number of new clients for the work I've been calling the Resurrection rebuild: real change in 90 days, with the kind of long-arc support that's kept clients here for years. Who do you know who has built a strong life or career, but their body, energy, or strength is starting to fall behind it?

Script B - plain-problem-led:

> I'm looking for two people who are probably 40-plus, successful, and quietly frustrated that their strength, energy, sleep, or consistency is not where it used to be. Not someone looking for a cheap trainer. Someone who would value serious, high-touch help. Who comes to mind?

Track:

- Which script was used
- Names suggested
- Whether the intro was actually sent
- How the referrer described Cooz in their own words

### GBP/local track

Capture baseline before changing anything:

- Search from Burbank/SFV for:
  - personal trainer Burbank
  - private personal training Burbank
  - strength coach Burbank
  - fitness coach Burbank
  - online fitness coach Los Angeles
- Screenshot ranking positions.
- Record current review count, review recency, primary category, services, photos, business hours, website link, and booking/contact path.

Then refresh:

- Confirm primary category matches the real local search target.
- Add or clean up services around private personal training, strength coaching, online coaching, and nutrition/accountability if accurate.
- Upload 4-6 real current photos.
- Reply to all existing reviews if not already done.
- Request 8 reviews, aiming for 4-6 live by Day 30.

### LinkedIn track

Deploy only the conversion-critical profile updates:

- Headline
- Profile image/banner if ready
- Featured Slot 1: Triage Audit or contact path
- Featured Slot 3: Brave Choice lead magnet if it exists
- About section test version

Use a shorter cold-visitor About for the first test:

> I help professionals over 40 rebuild the strength, energy, and consistency their life now demands.
>
> Most people who come to me are not beginners. They have built careers, families, companies, shows, practices, and reputations. The problem is that the body carrying all of it is starting to fall behind.
>
> My work is high-touch coaching for that gap: training, nutrition, recovery, and the daily choices that make the rebuild hold.
>
> Real change can show up in 90 days. The reason clients stay for years is that the work keeps paying them back.
>
> Mike has been with me 9 years. Robin 8. Corey 4. Carron 3. Mari gained 16 lbs in 3 months in one of the fastest transformations I have coached.
>
> If your strength, energy, or body confidence is starting to slip under the life you built, book the Triage Audit. I listen, diagnose, and recommend one pathway. No menu pitch.

Post 3 times this week. Track every post:

- Impressions
- Reactions
- Comments
- Saves
- Profile views
- DMs
- Featured clicks
- Audit bookings

## Week 2 - Expand Referral Sample And Test Positioning

### Referral track

Run 5 more referral conversations. Include clients, peers, family-adjacent relationships, and professional contacts.

Goal by end of Week 2:

- 10 conversations completed
- 8 named prospects identified
- 3 actual intros sent

If people compliment the positioning but do not name anyone, the language is not referral-operational yet.

### LinkedIn track

Run the first positioning test in posts/DMs:

- Version 1: "The Resurrection Coach"
- Version 2: "strength and energy rebuild for professionals over 40"

Measure which one creates replies, profile clicks, and plain-language recognition.

Do not judge by likes alone.

### Triage Audit track

If audits are booked, track the true labor cost:

- Intake review minutes
- LinkedIn/profile research minutes
- Call minutes
- Follow-up/proposal minutes
- Close result
- Channel source
- Price/pathway recommended

If audit hours per closed client exceed 8-10, gate or charge for the audit.

## Week 3 - Add Diagnostic Utility And Run Calls

### Referral track

Run 5 more referral conversations. Follow up with all unsent intros from Weeks 1-2.

Target:

- 15 total conversations
- 10+ named prospects
- 4+ intros sent
- 2+ Triage Audits booked

### LinkedIn track

Add one save-worthy diagnostic post this week. Use practical structure, not only story.

Recommended diagnostic post:

> The 4 PM fog audit
>
> Before you assume you need more discipline, check these five inputs for seven days:
>
> 1. Last caffeine time
> 2. First real protein meal
> 3. Water before coffee
> 4. Alcohol nights
> 5. Bedtime consistency
>
> If all five are unstable, the problem is not motivation. The body is running on bad inputs and asking for more output.
>
> Fix the inputs first. Then train harder.

Track saves and profile clicks. If diagnostic utility drives warmer DMs than proof stories, shift the Month 2 content mix toward diagnostic posts.

### Access track

Confirm one real access path:

- WGF registration
- IATSE guest invite
- Corey/Sam/Buzz intro to a named person
- Mike intro to a real executive group contact
- Chamber event registration

Do not count "maybe" as a channel.

## Week 4 - Decision Review

### Complete the 20-referral sample

Run the final 5 referral conversations.

By the review, the referral test should answer:

- Which language produced names?
- Which referrers actually sent intros?
- Which buyer lane appeared most: executive, Hollywood creative, athlete, family/aging, remote?
- Did referrals convert to audits?

### LinkedIn conversion review

Review 12 posts and profile metrics:

- Which posts produced profile views?
- Which posts produced DMs?
- Which posts produced audit bookings?
- Did the shorter About create more clarity?
- Did first-comment links help, hurt, or make no difference?
- Did proof posts attract buyers or spectators?
- Did diagnostic posts get saved?

### Sales review

Review all Triage Audits:

- Source
- Fit
- Recommended pathway
- Objections
- Close status
- Hours spent
- Follow-up required

Decision rule:

- If 3+ audits and 1+ close: continue referrals + LinkedIn + GBP for 30 more days.
- If audits happen but do not close: diagnose Triage Audit, offer clarity, pricing, and fit.
- If no audits happen: do not add channels; fix CTA, audience, referral language, or lead magnet.
- If referrals produce intros but LinkedIn does not: use LinkedIn as trust reinforcement, not primary lead-gen.
- If LinkedIn produces DMs but no audits: fix CTA and booking friction.

## Proof Discipline Checklist

Before any post or profile claim ships:

- Mari claim: body composition method confirmed or softened.
- Named client: permission confirmed or anonymized.
- "Last week" claim: literal and permission-safe, or rewritten without recency.
- Client result: tied to the right buyer lane.
- No universal promise from one exceptional case.
- No "online alone" projection stated as fact.
- No `$10k-$15k/mo` math without pathway mix.

## ESA Validation

Do not build more ESA collateral yet.

Run 5 founder/VP conversations with this question:

> If your body, energy, recovery, training, nutrition, schedule, and accountability were rebuilt as one operating support system for the next 12 months, would that be a `$5k/mo` problem for you, or does that still feel like personal training?

Track:

- Exact language they use
- Whether they compare it to executive coaching, medical concierge, personal training, or life coaching
- Whether `$5k/mo` feels reasonable, high, or confusing
- What proof they need to believe it

ESA becomes real only if at least one buyer asks for a next step or two buyers clearly validate the value frame.
