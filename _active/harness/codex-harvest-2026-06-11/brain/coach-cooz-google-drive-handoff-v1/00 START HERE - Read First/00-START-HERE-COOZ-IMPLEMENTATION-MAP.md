# Coach Cooz Implementation Map

Purpose: This is the first document to read. It explains what matters now, what can wait, and how to start implementing without getting buried in strategy.

## The Short Version

The plan is simple for the first 30 days:

1. Make the LinkedIn profile clear.
2. Post useful content 3 times per week.
3. Comment and message on LinkedIn every weekday.
4. Use the Triage Audit as the only doorway.
5. Track proof, calls, DMs, referrals, and closes.

Do not try to launch every channel at once.

Do not rebuild the brand again before testing the market.

Do not lead cold buyers with "The Resurrection Coach" before they understand what you do.

## What You Do

You help professionals over 40 rebuild strength, energy, and consistency when life keeps breaking the plan.

The deeper brand layer is Resurrection:

Coming back to a body you recognize before the diminished version becomes normal.

The practical doorway comes first.

The deeper language comes after the buyer understands the work.

## What Matters Most Right Now

### 1. LinkedIn Profile

Make the profile clear enough that a cold visitor understands:

- who you help
- what you help them rebuild
- why their old restart keeps breaking
- how the Triage Audit works
- how to take the next step

Start with the docs in:

`01 Launch Now - Profile Content Outreach`

### 2. Triage Audit

The Triage Audit is not a free consultation.

It is where you listen, name what is breaking, and recommend one path.

The diagnosis chooses the path.

Use:

- `TRIAGE-TO-CLIENT-CONVERSION-SYSTEM`
- `OFFER-ARCHITECTURE`
- `SERVICE-DESIGN-DELIVERY-MAP`

### 3. Content

Your content should not just prove you are good.

It should help the reader leave with:

- a diagnosis
- a distinction
- a checklist
- a question
- a belief shift
- a minimum action

The reader should feel:

1. "He sees the thing I have been minimizing."
2. "He can name the pattern without shaming me."
3. "There is a practical next move."

### 4. LinkedIn Distribution

Posting is not enough.

Every weekday:

- leave 10 useful comments
- send 5 connection notes
- send 3 warm DMs or referral follow-ups when there is context

Use the LinkedIn Distribution Gem so this does not become spammy or awkward.

### 5. Proof Safety

Strong proof is one of your best assets.

But proof has to be handled carefully.

Before using a client name, result, number, quote, profession, photo, video, or timeline, check:

- Is it true?
- How do we know?
- Does the client approve?
- Is the measurement credible?
- Is it typical or exceptional?
- Is there a safer way to say it?

Use:

- `PROOF-CLAIMS-VALIDATION-MAP`
- `COOZ-PROOF-CLAIMS-GEM`

## What To Ignore For Now

Do not prioritize these in the first 30 days:

- paid ads
- big YouTube push
- full blog engine
- a new brand rebuild
- public ESA launch
- complex automation
- a large content calendar that does not create calls

Use YouTube and blog as support assets only.

The first proof gate is not reach.

The first proof gate is:

- warm intros
- booked audits
- serious follow-ups
- closes

## What Needs Farrice Review Before Public Use

Cooz can execute directly:

- rough post drafts before final polish

## The Most Important Operating Rule

If the content only says "look at me," rewrite it.

If the content helps the right reader name something they have been minimizing, keep going.

The work is not to sound impressive.

The work is to be useful enough that the right person trusts you with the first conversation.
