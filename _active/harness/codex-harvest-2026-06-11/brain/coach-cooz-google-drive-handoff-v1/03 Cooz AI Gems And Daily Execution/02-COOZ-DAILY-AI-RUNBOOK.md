# Coach Cooz Daily AI Runbook

Purpose: Give Cooz a simple way to use the Gems without deciding from scratch every day.

## Daily Rule

Use AI to reduce friction, not to outsource judgment.

Before posting, messaging, or sending a client note, ask:

1. Does this sound like me?
2. Does this help the right person?
3. Is the next step clear?
4. Is every claim safe?

## Monday: Diagnostic Content

Use: `Cooz Content Flywheel`

Prompt:

```text
I need one LinkedIn post and one Instagram carousel for Monday diagnostic content.

Topic:
[one issue: 4 PM fog, under-recovered, travel week, Monday restart, minimum version]

Buyer:
Professionals over 40 whose body is starting to fall behind the life they built.

Reader value:
Give them a diagnosis or checklist they can use this week.

Proof status:
[none / client-safe / anonymized / permission needed]

Create:
1. LinkedIn post
2. IG carousel slide copy
3. Reel hook
4. CTA
5. quality gate notes
```

Then run the output through: `Cooz Proof And Claims Guard`.

## Tuesday: LinkedIn Distribution

Use: `Cooz LinkedIn Distribution`

Daily target:

- 10 comments
- 5 connection notes
- 3 warm DMs or referral follow-ups

Prompt:

```text
Here is the post or profile I want to respond to:

<context>
[paste post/profile snippet]
</context>

Give me 3 comment options in my voice.

Rules:
- no pitch
- no fake agreement
- one useful observation
- sound like a serious coach, not a marketer
- keep it under 90 words
```

## Wednesday: Proof With Transfer

Use: `Cooz Content Flywheel` and `Cooz Proof And Claims Guard`

Prompt:

```text
I want to turn this client-safe proof idea into content without bragging.

Story:
[paste story or voice memo]

What I know is verified:
[list]

What needs permission or softer language:
[list]

Create:
1. LinkedIn post with proof transfer
2. IG Story sequence
3. one DM follow-up line if someone responds
4. proof risk notes
```

## Thursday: Service Ops

Use: `Cooz Service Ops`

Prompt:

```text
Help me prep client check-ins for this week.

Client context:
[paste client-safe notes]

What happened:
[training, energy, missed sessions, travel, food, sleep]

Need:
1. what pattern to read back
2. what to change this week
3. what minimum version to assign
4. one plain message I can send
5. any proof note worth capturing
```

## Friday: Belief Or Identity Post

Use: `Cooz Content Flywheel`

Prompt:

```text
I need a Friday belief-shift post.

Core tension:
[self-reliance, third Monday restart, waiting, diminished version becoming normal, brave choice]

My real story or client-safe moment:
[paste]

Reader value:
Give them a question or distinction that changes how they see the pattern.

Create one LinkedIn post and one Reel script. Keep it plain and serious.
```

## Before A Triage Audit

Use: `Cooz Service Ops`

Prompt:

```text
Prep me for this Triage Audit.

Intake:
[paste answers]

Give me:
1. likely buyer lane
2. likely proof analogue
3. phrases to listen for
4. likely objection
5. likely recommended path
6. five questions I should ask
7. what not to diagnose too early
```

## After A Triage Audit

Use: `Cooz Service Ops`

Prompt:

```text
Help me write the follow-up from this Triage Audit.

Notes:
[paste notes]

Recommended path:
[path]

Decision state:
[ready / needs time / objection / no fit]

Write:
1. short read-back
2. path recommendation
3. next step
4. no-pressure decision question
5. what to track in my CRM
```

## Before Anything Public Ships

Use: `Cooz Master Brand Brain`

Prompt:

```text
Audit this asset against the Coach Cooz standard.

Asset:
[paste]

Check:
1. cold-buyer clarity
2. reader value
3. Cooz voice
4. proof safety
5. CTA fit
6. what to rewrite
```

Then use: `Cooz Proof And Claims Guard` if the asset includes a client, result, number, timeline, or sales claim.

## Weekly Review

Use: `Cooz Master Brand Brain`

Prompt:

```text
Here are this week's posts, DMs, comments, audits, and client notes:

[paste]

Review:
1. what sounded most like me
2. what sounded generic
3. what got real buyer response
4. which content lane worked
5. what proof we can safely reuse
6. what to make next week
```
