# Positioning And Category

## Category

24-hour business asset audit for local service businesses.

## Plain-Language Positioning

> I review the parts of your business that customers see before they contact you, then send a short action plan showing the fastest fixes to increase trust, clarity, and lead flow.

## What This Is Not

- Not a full marketing agency package
- Not a long strategy deck
- Not an AI gimmick
- Not a retainer pitch
- Not a promise of instant sales

## What This Is

- A fast external review
- A scored customer-facing asset check
- A list of practical fixes
- A low-risk first step before bigger implementation

## Alternatives

| Alternative | Why Buyer Chooses It | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Do nothing | No time or money spent | Problems keep leaking trust |
| Ask a friend | Free | Usually vague and not systematic |
| Hire agency | Full support | Too expensive and slow for today |
| Buy template/course | Cheap | Not specific to their business |
| DIY audit | Control | Hard to see own blind spots |

## Differentiated Value

Speed + specificity + low-risk entry.

The buyer does not need to commit to a big project. They get a useful diagnostic in 24 hours and can decide later whether to fix it themselves or hire help.

## Proof Standard

Every outreach message must include one specific observation. That observation is the first proof.
