# Today Checklist

## Vet And Bill Pressure

- [ ] Call vet using `VET-BILL-CALL-SCRIPT.md`
- [ ] Ask for written estimate
- [ ] Ask what must happen now vs later
- [ ] Ask about CareCredit/Scratchpay
- [ ] Ask about direct nonprofit/crowdfund payment
- [ ] Prepare dog photo and story
- [ ] Prepare RedRover / Frankie's Friends / Waggle materials
- [ ] Contact 211 or utility providers if bills are due soon

## Revenue Sprint

- [ ] Fill 15 warm contacts in `PROSPECT-TRACKER.csv`
- [ ] Fill 10 referral sources
- [ ] Fill 15 cold prospects
- [ ] Send 15 warm messages
- [ ] Send 10 referral asks
- [ ] Send 15 cold messages
- [ ] Follow up with all replies within 10 minutes
- [ ] Ask for payment before starting
- [ ] Deliver first paid audit using `AI-BUSINESS-ASSET-AUDIT-TEMPLATE.md`

## Minimum Winning Day

The day is successful if:

- [ ] Vet payment path is clearer than this morning
- [ ] Grant/crowdfund materials are ready
- [ ] 40 prospects/referral sources are listed
- [ ] 40 messages are sent
- [ ] At least 3 conversations are active
- [ ] At least 1 payment is requested directly

## If It Is Already Evening

Do not try to do everything. Do this:

1. Call or message the vet if still open.
2. Send 15 warm/referral messages.
3. Build 10 cold prospects.
4. Send 10 cold messages.
5. Stay available for replies for 2 hours.
