# Vet Bill Call Script

## Goal

Get a written cost estimate, reduce non-urgent spend, and confirm every payment path before the appointment.

## Call Script

Use this word for word:

```text
Hi, I have an appointment coming up for my dog and I need to plan payment before I come in. I have limited funds right now, but I want to make sure my dog gets the care they need.

Can you help me with five things?

1. Can I get a written estimate before treatment?
2. Can the estimate separate what must be done immediately, what can safely wait, and what is optional?
3. Do you accept CareCredit or Scratchpay?
4. Does your clinic have a hardship fund, rescue partner, nonprofit fund, or payment assistance list?
5. If a nonprofit or crowdfunding platform pays the clinic directly, will you accept that payment?
```

If they say they cannot estimate before seeing the dog:

```text
I understand. Can you tell me the exam fee, the most common diagnostic range for this situation, and what payment options are available if the treatment cost is higher than I can pay today?
```

If they mention a treatment plan:

```text
Can you please write the diagnosis, treatment plan, prognosis, and cost estimate in a document? Some grant programs require those exact details and pay the clinic directly.
```

If they push for full payment upfront:

```text
I understand your policy. If I can pay part today and have a grant or crowdfunding platform send payment directly to the clinic, is there any way to proceed with the urgent portion first?
```

## Ask For These Documents

- Written estimate
- Diagnosis or suspected diagnosis
- Treatment plan
- Prognosis with treatment
- Clinic contact information
- Invoice format accepted by grant/crowdfund platforms

## If The Cost Is Too High

Ask:

```text
Is there a lower-cost clinic, nonprofit clinic, veterinary school, rescue partner, or urgent care option you would trust for this case?
```

Then:

```text
Can you email me the estimate so I can apply for assistance today?
```
