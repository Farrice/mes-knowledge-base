# Sales Proof Kit

## Proof Position

This sprint starts with low formal proof, so the offer must prove itself through specificity.

Do not say:

```text
I can transform your business.
```

Say:

```text
I noticed your homepage says what you do, but it does not give a clear next step for a first-time visitor. That is the first thing I would check.
```

## Proof Types Available Today

| Proof Type | How To Use |
|---|---|
| Specific observation | Put one real issue in every message |
| Process proof | Show the audit sections before payment if asked |
| Time proof | Promise 24-hour delivery and meet it |
| Risk reversal | Refund or redo if no 5 useful fixes |
| Demonstrable proof | Send a screenshot or short note showing the issue |

## Sales Call Flow

Use this for a 10-minute call:

1. "What do you want more of right now: calls, bookings, trust, or clearer offers?"
2. "Where do most new customers first see you?"
3. "What have you been meaning to fix but have not had time for?"
4. "I can review those assets and send the top fixes in 24 hours."
5. "It is $200. Want me to do it today?"

## Objection Responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| Send me info | "Sure. The short version: 1-page audit, 5 fixes, 3 content/outreach ideas, 1 offer improvement, delivered in 24 hours." |
| I need to think | "Makes sense. I can only hold the 24-hour slot once payment is sent." |
| Too expensive | "I can do the tighter top-5-fixes version for $150." |
| Can you guarantee leads? | "No. This is a diagnostic to fix visible trust and clarity issues. I will not promise lead volume from a 24-hour audit." |
| I already have someone | "Good. This can give them a prioritized fix list so they know where to start." |

## Delivery Message

```text
Here is the audit. I kept it focused on the fixes most likely to improve trust, clarity, and response from the assets you already have.

If you want help implementing the top fixes, I recommend starting with [specific option]. I can do that for [$X] and turn it around by [date].
```
