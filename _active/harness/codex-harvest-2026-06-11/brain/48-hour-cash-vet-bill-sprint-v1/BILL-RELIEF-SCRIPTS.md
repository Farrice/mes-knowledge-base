# Bill Relief Scripts

## Purpose

Move non-vet bills out of the immediate danger zone so the vet appointment is not competing with every other payment.

## 211 Script

Call 211 or use https://www.211.org/get-help/utilities-expenses.

```text
Hi, I need emergency help finding local bill assistance. I have an urgent veterinary expense and only about $300 available, and I am trying to avoid falling behind on utilities or basic bills.

Can you help me find programs for utility assistance, emergency grants, payment relief, food support, or local nonprofit help in my ZIP code?
```

Write down:

| Program | Phone/Link | What They Cover | Deadline | Documents Needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
|  |  |  |  |  |

## Utility Company Script

```text
Hi, I am calling before missing a payment. I have an urgent veterinary expense and limited funds right now. I want to keep my account in good standing.

Do you have a hardship extension, payment arrangement, temporary discount, shutoff protection, or nonprofit assistance program I can apply for?
```

If they offer a plan:

```text
Can you email or text the terms so I have the due date and amount in writing?
```

## Phone / Internet Bill Script

```text
Hi, I need a short-term hardship option. I am trying to cover an urgent vet bill and need to reduce or delay this payment if possible.

Can you check for extensions, lower-cost plans, loyalty discounts, temporary suspension, or hardship credits?
```

## Credit Card / Loan Script

```text
Hi, I am calling before my payment becomes a problem. I have an urgent medical expense for my dog and limited funds this week.

Do you have a hardship program, payment deferral, late fee waiver, lower minimum payment, or due date change available?
```

## Rules

- Call before the due date whenever possible.
- Ask for written confirmation.
- Do not agree to a payment date you cannot meet.
- If they say no, ask: "Is there a supervisor or hardship department that handles exceptions?"
