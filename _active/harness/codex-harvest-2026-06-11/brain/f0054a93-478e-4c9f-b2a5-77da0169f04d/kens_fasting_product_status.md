## The Pivot (Critical Context)

The swarm research **unanimously** found:

1. **No credible evidence** for the original "exact electrolyte formulas" or "guaranteed Day 3 transformation" claims
2. **Significant physiological risks** — dangerous electrolyte imbalances, metabolic stress
3. **"Guarantee" language is unacceptable** — must be removed immediately

**The solution adopted:** Hybrid approach — **Responsible Guide** substance (safety-first, physician consultation gate, clear "eject button" signs) wrapped in **Identity Engineering** marketing (fasting as a "rite of passage," personal "How I Learned" narrative).

---

## Product Architecture (Post-Pivot)

### The $7 Product: "The 72-Hour Fasting Pre-Flight Checklist"
- **Module 1:** The Danger Zone (why most fail at 48 hours)
- **Module 2:** The Pre-Flight Checklist (risk assessment + physician gate)
- **Module 3:** Hydration Basics (safe practices, NOT magic formulas)
- **Module 4:** The Eject Button (when to stop immediately)

### The Upsell: "30-Day Fasting Lifestyle Blueprint" ($47-$97)
- Triggered post-purchase via Day 3 check-in email
- Checkout bump option at 50% off
- Covers meal-break protocols, social survival guides, metabolic flexibility training
