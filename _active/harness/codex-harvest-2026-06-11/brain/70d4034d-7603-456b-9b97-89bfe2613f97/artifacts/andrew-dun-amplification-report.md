## Scan A: Gap Scan — What's Missing?

### MISSED 1: Industry Vertical Playbook Architecture
- **Source**: Research confirms top AI consultants specialize by vertical — BFSI, manufacturing, retail, healthcare each have distinct AI maturity levels, compliance requirements, and ROI levers
- **Why it matters**: A consultant who walks into a manufacturing plant with a generic framework loses to one who already knows that predictive maintenance is the #1 quick win and compliance gates are low. Vertical-specific playbooks compress discovery time by 60-70%
- **Workflow potential**: **HIGH**
- **Proposed name**: `11-industry-vertical-playbook.md`

### MISSED 2: Proposal & Scope of Work Architect
- **Source**: Research shows high-ticket consultants use phased roadmap proposals (not flat quotes). Structure: Phase 1 Assessment → Phase 2 POC → Phase 3 Roadmap. Fixed pricing per phase. 65% close rate vs. 30% for generic proposals
- **Why it matters**: The gap between "great diagnostic presentation" (Workflow 05) and "signed contract" is the proposal. No workflow currently bridges this. The proposal IS the conversion mechanism
- **Workflow potential**: **HIGH**
- **Proposed name**: `12-proposal-sow-architect.md`

### MISSED 3: AI Readiness Scorecard (Pre-Diagnostic)
- **Source**: Research identifies "AI Readiness Assessment" as the most common entry deliverable — maps tech stack, data quality, team skills, and produces a maturity score. This is a *lighter* version of the full diagnostic that can be delivered in 2-4 hours for $0 (lead gen) or $2K-$5K (paid mini-audit)
- **Why it matters**: The full diagnostic (Workflow 01) requires 1-2 weeks. Many prospects aren't ready to commit that. The Readiness Scorecard is the top-of-funnel entry point that leads to the full audit
- **Workflow potential**: **HIGH**
- **Proposed name**: `13-ai-readiness-scorecard.md`

### MISSED 4: Consulting Pipeline & Revenue Dashboard
- **Source**: Research confirms 5% failure mode is "ignoring sales — delivery-focused, no pipeline." 80% of $300K+ solos spend 8 hrs/week on acquisition across 1-2 channels. Pipeline tracking is essential
- **Why it matters**: None of the 10 workflows address *running the practice itself*. Workflow 09 covers first-client acquisition but nothing about managing a pipeline, forecasting revenue, or tracking client lifecycle
- **Workflow potential**: **HIGH**
- **Proposed name**: `14-consulting-pipeline-dashboard.md`

### LATENT 1: Value Demonstration Engine (Live ROI Proof)
- **In genius.md**: The ROI formula exists. The diagnostic deck exists. But research reveals top performers end every engagement with a **live dashboard showing $X saved** — Streamlit dashboards, Notion trackers, automated ROI calculators
- **Why it matters**: The difference between "trust our numbers" and "see it in real-time" is the difference between a one-off client and a retainer client. Live value demonstration is the #1 retainer lock-in mechanism
- **Workflow potential**: **HIGH**
- **Proposed name**: `15-value-demonstration-engine.md`
