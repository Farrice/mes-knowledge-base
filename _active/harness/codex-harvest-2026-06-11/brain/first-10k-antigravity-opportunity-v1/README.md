# First $5K-$10K Antigravity Opportunity

## Core Recommendation

Use Antigravity to sell a narrow paid diagnostic plus implementation sprint, not a generic AI consulting offer and not a software product.

The strongest first offer is:

**AI Business Asset Audit + Implementation Sprint**

For expert-led service businesses, consultants, creators, and small agencies that already have knowledge, clients, or content but do not have clean reusable business assets.

The paid outcome:

- diagnose the biggest missing or weak business assets
- package the buyer's offer and positioning
- build the first reusable sales, content, proof, or delivery assets
- leave the buyer with templates, reports, scripts, and operating cadence they can reuse

## Why This Fits The Workspace

The workspace is strongest at:

- routing a vague business problem to the right expert method
- loading relevant frameworks and prior knowledge
- producing structured artifacts instead of loose advice
- turning founder knowledge into reusable assets
- applying quality and proof checks before delivery

The Daniel Priestley 24 Assets workflow is the cleanest commercial spine because it asks the right question: did this work create an asset, or did it only create activity?

## First Revenue Math

Simplest path to the first $5K-$10K:

- 5 to 10 paid audits at $500-$1,000
- or 2 implementation sprints at $2,500-$5,000
- or 1 audit-to-sprint conversion plus 3 paid audits

The first public promise should not be "I use AI to help your business."

It should be closer to:

> I help expert-led service businesses turn scattered knowledge, content, and delivery into reusable business assets that improve sales, delivery, and founder independence.

## Recommended Offer Ladder

| Level | Price | Promise | Delivery |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Diagnostic | $500-$1,000 | Find the 3 highest-value missing business assets | Intake, evidence review, scorecard, 60-minute debrief |
| Sprint | $2,500-$5,000 | Build the first 3-5 reusable assets | 1-2 week implementation sprint |
| Retainer | $1,500-$3,000/mo | Keep improving assets and campaigns | Monthly audit, build queue, QA, iteration |

## Immediate Build Priorities

1. Create a `/first-10k` meta-workflow that runs: niche selection, paid pain selection, offer design, sales asset pack, outreach plan, delivery package, outcome tracking.
2. Create a client-facing 24 Assets audit template.
3. Create a one-page offer page and proposal template.
4. Create a simple pipeline tracker for prospects, calls, audits sold, sprint conversions, and revenue.
5. Human-calibrate the eval harness so quality scores become trustworthy.
6. Benchmark only the revenue-critical skills first: `daniel-priestley-24-assets-os`, `monk-ai-offer-architecture`, `nicolas-cole-client-acquisition`, `luke-iha-creative-strategy`, `lindsay-ai-consulting`, and `ai-chris-lee-zero-testimonial-sales`.

## Current Workspace Gaps

- Too many capabilities are exposed at once: 223 skills, 522 knowledge files, and almost 2 million knowledge-base words.
- Quality evaluation is not yet load-bearing: 12 eval entries, 0 human-calibrated.
- The harness is better at producing artifacts than tracking whether those artifacts create revenue.
- The acquisition path is less deterministic than the production path.
- The skill audit is useful, but only 5 grounded skills are currently marked in the evolution orchestrator.
- There is no single "commercial cockpit" for first-revenue execution.

## Operating Rule

For this stage, judge every workflow by one question:

**Did this create a reusable asset that helps get a buyer, convert a buyer, serve a buyer, or prove a result?**

If not, it is secondary.
