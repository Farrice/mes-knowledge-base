# Coach Cooz Master Visual Direction Brief

## Strategic Job

Make Cooz look like the private strength and energy coach a serious professional over 40 would trust when their body is no longer keeping up with their life.

The design should not look like fitness motivation. It should look like diagnosis, calm authority, and practical reconstruction.

## Audience Signal

Primary viewer:

Professionals over 40 in the Burbank/LA orbit or remote professionals with demanding calendars. They are not beginners. They are successful enough to pay for premium support, but their body, energy, consistency, travel schedule, sleep, or recovery has started to slip.

They do not want:

- hype
- shame
- bro-fitness
- wellness fluff
- another generic plan
- loud transformation marketing

They respond to:

- being seen clearly
- private seriousness
- calm competence
- a coach who diagnoses before prescribing
- a body-focused message that respects their real life

## Core Creative Idea

Your calendar is not going to calm down.

Your body still has to carry it.

This is the visual and verbal center. Every asset should orbit this idea.

## Three Visual Directions

### Direction 1: The Body Carries The Calendar

Thesis: Use symbolic strength imagery and restrained copy to make the buyer feel the pressure of a full life on an under-supported body.

Best for:

- LinkedIn banner
- carousel covers
- YouTube thumbnails
- first-launch public surface

Source images:

- `approved-selects/06_kettlebell_24kg_still_life_16x9.png`
- `approved-selects/11_seated_dark_authority_portrait.png`
- `approved-selects/19_city_standing_black_tshirt_upscaled.png`

Visual language:

- dark charcoal backgrounds
- controlled amber edge light
- one strong object or one serious face
- large negative space
- no clutter

### Direction 2: Diagnosis Before Another Plan

Thesis: Position Cooz as a premium diagnostic coach by making the design feel like a serious assessment, not a workout pitch.

Best for:

- Triage Audit assets
- LinkedIn featured graphics
- IG carousels
- service explainer visuals

Source images:

- `approved-selects/07_gym_teaching_wide_16x9.png`
- `approved-selects/14_gym_teaching_portrait_clean_necklace.png`
- `needs-review/20_gym_triage_conversation_16x9.png` as concept only

Visual language:

- gym environment, quiet not gritty
- subtle grid or diagnostic linework
- text that feels like a finding, not a slogan
- one visual focal point

### Direction 3: Private Local Authority

Thesis: Make Cooz feel local, premium, human, and direct enough for warm referrals.

Best for:

- LinkedIn profile photo
- IG feed posts
- website about assets
- local referral graphics

Source images:

- `approved-selects/12_city_standing_black_tshirt.png`
- `approved-selects/18_city_arms_crossed_henley_clean_watch.png`
- `approved-selects/05_premium_blazer_headshot.png` with restraint

Visual language:

- warm city light
- black clothing
- natural authority
- no luxury signals
- no fake corporate boardroom energy

## Recommended Direction

Lead with Direction 1 for public profile surfaces.

Why:

- It has the strongest emotional hook.
- It differentiates him from trainers showing exercises.
- It speaks to professionals over 40 without saying too much.
- It allows the profile copy to do the explanatory work.

Use Direction 2 for conversion assets once someone is closer to action.

Use Direction 3 for warm trust and local referral contexts.

## Palette

Use:

- Charcoal: `#111111`
- Black: `#050505`
- Bone: `#F3EDE3`
- Warm gray: `#A69A8D`
- Brass/copper: `#B98245`
- Deep brown: `#2A211B`
- Muted cream: `#D8CBB8`

Avoid:

- neon
- bright blue fitness tech
- purple gradients
- beige wellness minimalism
- glossy luxury gold
- loud red challenge graphics

## Typography Direction

Use clean, adult typography.

Recommended:

- Primary: strong grotesk or neo-grotesk sans.
- Secondary: neutral sans for body and small labels.
- Optional: restrained serif only for editorial emphasis, never as the whole identity.

Rules:

- Large type, few words.
- High contrast between headline and support line.
- No paragraph overlays on images.
- No tiny mobile text.
- No aggressive gym fonts.
- No handwritten motivation fonts.

## Composition Rules

- One focal point per asset.
- Leave enough negative space for the buyer to feel the weight of the message.
- Use warm edge light and dark surfaces.
- Keep copy in safe crop zones.
- Let faces look direct, serious, and calm.
- Let objects feel symbolic, not decorative.
- Use subtle texture/grain only if it adds depth.

## Copy Rules

Preferred copy fragments:

- `Your calendar is not going to calm down.`
- `Your body still has to carry it.`
- `Private strength + energy coaching after 40`
- `Diagnosis before another plan.`
- `Start with the Triage Audit.`
- `Monday is not a plan.`
- `The 4 PM fog is usually traceable.`
- `Do not buy another plan until you know what keeps breaking.`

Avoid:

- crush your goals
- unlock your potential
- elite performance
- biohacking
- get shredded
- no excuses
- age is just a number
- transformation challenge

## Proof And Likeness Guardrails

- Do not imply a second person is a real client unless verified.
- Do not imply a generated scene is documentary proof.
- Do not create fake before/after imagery.
- Do not use body composition claims unless measurement method and permission are validated.
- Do not make Cooz look like a different person.
- Any edited or generated likeness must be approved by Cooz before public use.

## Anti-Slop Gate

Reject the output if it looks like:

- a gym ad template
- a motivational quote card
- a generic personal trainer graphic
- an executive-coach headshot package
- a wellness coach brand
- fake luxury
- AI-polished sameness
- a poster for a fitness challenge
