# Instagram Social Suite Design Brief

## Asset Job

Instagram should make Cooz feel useful, real, and premium without becoming a quote-card account.

The goal is not to resize LinkedIn posts. The goal is to package one clear insight so someone can save it, share it, or DM Cooz because it named something they are living.

## Required Outputs

- Feed post: 1080x1350
- Carousel cover: 1080x1350 or 1080x1080
- Carousel slides: 1080x1350 preferred
- Reel cover: 1080x1920
- Story frame: 1080x1920

## Visual Rules

- One idea per asset.
- Large readable text.
- Strong image or object, not decoration.
- Dark/warm premium palette.
- No generic motivational language.
- No tiny body copy.
- No stock-looking gym graphics.

## Carousel Cover System

Recommended source:

`approved-selects/06_kettlebell_24kg_still_life_16x9.png`

Purpose:

Make one insight saveable.

Strong cover hooks:

```text
Monday is not a plan.
```

```text
Do not buy another plan yet.
```

```text
The 4 PM fog is usually traceable.
```

```text
Your calendar is data.
```

Carousel structure:

1. Cover: name the pattern.
2. Slide 2: tell them what they are misreading.
3. Slide 3: show the hidden inputs.
4. Slide 4: give the diagnostic checklist.
5. Slide 5: practical next move.
6. Close: light CTA.

Example carousel: `The 4 PM Fog Is Usually Traceable`

Slide copy:

1. `The 4 PM fog is usually traceable.`
2. `Most people blame discipline first.`
3. `But the fog usually has inputs.`
4. `Check sleep, food timing, caffeine, training load, and stress.`
5. `Do not solve fatigue with harder training until you know which input is breaking.`
6. `If you want a second set of eyes, DM "audit."`

Prompt:

```text
Design an Instagram carousel template for Coach Cooz.

Canvas: 1080x1350 per slide, 6 slides.

Goal: saveable diagnostic content for professionals over 40 whose strength, energy, and consistency are slipping.

Visual style: dark charcoal, warm brass accent, bone text, clean adult typography, subtle texture, no clutter.

Use the uploaded kettlebell still-life reference as the cover visual anchor. Make the kettlebell symbolic, not decorative.

Slides:
1. "The 4 PM fog is usually traceable."
2. "Most people blame discipline first."
3. "But the fog usually has inputs."
4. "Check sleep, food timing, caffeine, training load, and stress."
5. "Do not solve fatigue with harder training until you know which input is breaking."
6. "If you want a second set of eyes, DM audit."

Each slide should have one thought, large readable text, and strong spacing. Avoid quote-card templates, stock fitness icons, before/after claims, fake proof, neon, and wellness beige.
```

## Story Frame System

Use for daily thought or soft CTA.

Story layout:

- top: small label `Coach Cooz`
- middle: one diagnostic sentence
- bottom: light CTA

Example:

```text
The skipped workout is not always the problem.

The problem is the system that lets one skipped workout become three weeks.

DM "audit" if you want me to look at what keeps breaking.
```

## Instagram QA

Pass only if:

- the asset gives the viewer a useful distinction
- the visual can be understood in under 2 seconds
- text is readable on a phone
- the design does not feel like a quote card
- the image does not imply fake proof
- the tone matches serious private coaching
