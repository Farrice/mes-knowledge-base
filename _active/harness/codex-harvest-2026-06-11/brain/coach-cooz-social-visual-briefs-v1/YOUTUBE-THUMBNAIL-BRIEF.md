# YouTube Thumbnail Brief

## Asset Job

YouTube thumbnails should make the topic clear in under 2 seconds.

They should signal:

- serious coach
- diagnostic insight
- strength and energy after 40
- no hype
- no fake expression

## Required Output

- Format: PNG
- Dimensions: 1280x720
- Text: 3-5 words maximum
- One face or one symbolic object
- High contrast
- Readable on mobile

## Thumbnail System A: Kettlebell Symbolic

Visual direction:

- kettlebell large left or center-left
- text on dark negative space
- warm edge light
- brass accent
- no extra icons

Prompt:

```text
Create a YouTube thumbnail for Coach Cooz using the uploaded kettlebell still-life reference.

Canvas: 1280x720 PNG.

Goal: serious diagnostic video for professionals over 40. It should feel premium, grounded, and useful, not like a fitness influencer thumbnail.

Text:
"4 PM Fog"

Use the kettlebell as the symbolic focal point. Keep dark charcoal background, warm controlled light, high contrast, and large readable type. Add a restrained brass accent if needed.

Avoid open-mouth influencer styling, arrows, neon, fake urgency, transformation claims, before/after visuals, and cluttered text.
```

## YouTube QA

Pass only if:

- text is readable at small size
- the topic is clear in under 2 seconds
- there are no more than 5 words
- the image feels serious and grounded
- no fake client proof is implied
- the thumbnail does not look like a commodity fitness channel
