# LinkedIn Banner Design Brief

## Asset Job

The LinkedIn banner should make Cooz's profile understandable before someone reads the About section.

It should create recognition and tension without adding cognitive load.

## Required Output

- Format: PNG
- Dimensions: 1584x396
- Platform: LinkedIn banner
- Mobile-safe: keep critical text away from the far left and bottom-left profile-photo overlap
- Text: minimal, high contrast, readable at small size

## Concept A: Body Carries The Calendar

Recommended default.

Source:

`approved-selects/06_kettlebell_24kg_still_life_16x9.png`

Message:

```text
Your calendar is not going to calm down.
Your body still has to carry it.
```

Support line:

```text
Private strength + energy coaching after 40
```

Layout:

- Kettlebell or dark textured gym surface on left/mid-left.
- Text on right-center.
- Large two-line headline.
- Small support line beneath.
- Optional small location line: `Burbank + remote`.
- Keep all essential text inside center-right safe zone.

Visual direction:

- dark charcoal
- warm brass accent line
- restrained grain
- no logo needed
- no quote-card box

Prompt:

```text
Design a LinkedIn banner for Coach Cooz using the uploaded kettlebell still-life reference.

Canvas: 1584x396 PNG.

Goal: serious private strength and energy coach for professionals over 40. The visual should feel grounded, premium, and diagnostic, not like a gym ad.

Use the kettlebell as a symbolic strength object. Keep the image dark, warm, cinematic but not fake. Add subtle negative space on the right for text.

Text:
"Your calendar is not going to calm down."
"Your body still has to carry it."

Small support line:
"Private strength + energy coaching after 40"

Optional small line:
"Burbank + remote"

Use bone/off-white text, charcoal/black background, and one restrained brass/copper accent. Keep text readable on desktop and mobile. Avoid fake luxury gold, neon, aggressive gym fonts, motivational slogans, busy overlays, and tiny text.
```

Reject if:

- it looks like a gym challenge banner
- text is too small
- it feels like generic motivation
- the kettlebell is decorative instead of symbolic

## Concept B: Private Strength + Energy After 40

Source:

`approved-selects/07_gym_teaching_wide_16x9.png`

Message:

```text
Private strength + energy coaching after 40
Burbank + remote daily support
```

Layout:

- Cooz in the coaching/gym environment.
- Text in open negative space.
- Keep profile-photo overlap in mind.
- Make the image feel like real coaching, not public performance.

Prompt:

```text
Design a LinkedIn banner for Coach Cooz using the uploaded gym teaching reference image.

Canvas: 1584x396 PNG.

Goal: clear profile positioning. He should read as a serious private coach, not a fitness influencer.

Text:
"Private strength + energy coaching after 40"
"Burbank + remote daily support"

Use a dark gym environment with warm controlled light. Place text in a clean safe zone with strong contrast. The photo should feel like private coaching and diagnosis, not a loud workout ad.

Use clean adult sans typography, bone/off-white text, charcoal background, and a restrained brass accent. Do not add fake proof, fake client claims, before/after language, neon, hype graphics, or overdesigned wellness styling.
```

Reject if:

- it feels too busy
- Cooz's face/body is awkwardly cropped
- text collides with LinkedIn profile-photo zone
- it feels like a class/gym promotion instead of private coaching

## Concept C: Diagnosis Before Another Plan

Use only when the Triage Audit is the main call to action.

Source:

`needs-review/20_gym_triage_conversation_16x9.png`

Important caution:

Use as concept imagery only unless Cooz verifies the session context. Do not imply the second person is a real client.

Message:

```text
Diagnosis before another plan.
Start with the Triage Audit.
```

Layout:

- Consultation/gym setting, dark and serious.
- Text center-right or clean negative-space area.
- Subtle diagnostic line or mark is acceptable.
- No fake testimonial/proof cues.

Prompt:

```text
Design a LinkedIn banner for Coach Cooz using this uploaded triage/conversation reference as concept imagery only.

Canvas: 1584x396 PNG.

Goal: communicate diagnosis before prescription. This should feel like a serious private coaching assessment, not free consulting or a sales call.

Text:
"Diagnosis before another plan."
"Start with the Triage Audit."

Optional small line:
"One clear path. No menu pitch."

Use dark charcoal, warm light, bone text, and one restrained brass accent. Keep the second person visually secondary and do not make the image imply a verified client testimonial or real documented outcome.

No fake proof. No transformation claims. No before/after language. No medical-looking design. No hype.
```

Reject if:

- it looks like proof of a real client session
- it feels too clinical or medical
- it becomes a sales webinar banner
- it creates doubt about authenticity

## Final Banner QA

Pass only if:

- a cold visitor understands Cooz's category in 3 seconds
- text is readable on mobile crop
- it does not fight the profile photo
- visual tone is premium but grounded
- no unsupported claims appear
- no generated person is implied to be a real client
