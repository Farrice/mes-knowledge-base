# First-Screen And DM-Readiness Test

## Purpose

Use this before Cooz updates LinkedIn and starts outreach.

The profile has to make the DM feel natural.

If someone receives a message, clicks his profile, and cannot quickly understand what he does, the outreach loses force.

## First-Screen Test

A new visitor should understand these within a few seconds:

| Question | Must Be Clear |
|---|---|
| What does Cooz do? | Private strength + energy coaching. |
| Who is it for? | Professionals over 40. |
| What problem does he solve? | Their body is no longer keeping up with their life. |
| Where does he work? | Burbank and remote. |
| Why is this different? | Diagnosis before prescription, real-schedule support, long-arc relationships. |
| What is the next step? | Message "audit" or start with the Triage Audit. |

## Cognitive Load Gate

The first screen should not make the reader decode:

- Resurrection
- Brave Choice
- all offer paths
- client names
- Mari's result
- ESA
- executive body language
- long methodology explanations

The first screen should show:

- strength
- energy
- consistency
- professionals over 40
- body no longer keeping up
- Burbank
- remote
- Triage Audit

## Attention Gate

The profile passes if one of these lines feels like it could stop the right buyer:

- Most people do not notice the slide all at once.
- They notice it in pieces.
- Your calendar is not going to calm down. Your body still has to carry it.
- The diminished version can become normal quietly.
- The same restart is not a character flaw. It is data.

The profile fails if it sounds like:

- generic personal training
- motivational content
- executive optimization
- wellness coaching
- therapy copy
- a clever brand manifesto

## DM Alignment Test

The DM should echo the profile.

Good warm DM:

```text
I am reaching out because I work with professionals over 40 whose strength, energy, or consistency is no longer keeping up with the life they built.

If that sounds familiar, I usually start with a Triage Audit: I listen, diagnose what is breaking, and recommend one path.
```

Good referral DM:

```text
Quick ask.

I am looking for professionals over 40 who are successful on paper, but privately frustrated that their strength, energy, or consistency is slipping.

Not beginners. Not people looking for a challenge.

People whose calendar is real and whose old restart is not holding anymore.

If someone comes to mind, I would appreciate the intro.
```

Good connection note:

```text
I work with professionals over 40 rebuilding strength, energy, and consistency under real schedules. Connected through the Burbank/LA orbit and wanted to connect here.
```

Avoid:

- pitching all offers
- using "Resurrection" cold
- pretending intimacy
- asking for a call before context
- saying "checking in"
- sounding like a mass DM

## Featured Section Test

Featured should answer:

1. What should I do next?
2. Can I get something useful now?
3. Why should I trust him?

Recommended order:

1. Start With The Triage Audit
2. The 4 PM Fog Audit
3. Why Some Clients Stay 3-9 Years

## Proof Safety Test

Before publishing, check:

- Are named clients approved?
- Are client years accurate?
- Are job titles approved?
- Is any story identifiable?
- Is Mari's measurement method verified?
- Are exceptional results labeled as exceptional?

Default public proof:

> Some clients have stayed 3-9 years because the work keeps paying them back.

## Final Launch Scorecard

Score each from 1-5.

| Category | Score |
|---|---:|
| Cold clarity | /5 |
| Attention | /5 |
| Buyer recognition | /5 |
| Premium differentiation | /5 |
| Proof safety | /5 |
| DM support | /5 |
| Triage Audit clarity | /5 |

Minimum launch score: 31/35.

If below 31, fix the headline, banner, About opening, or Featured section before outreach starts.
