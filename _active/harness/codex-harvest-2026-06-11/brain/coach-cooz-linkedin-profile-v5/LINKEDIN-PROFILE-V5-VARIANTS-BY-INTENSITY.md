# Coach Cooz LinkedIn Profile V5 - Variants By Intensity

## How To Use These

V5 uses intensity levels instead of unrelated variants.

The strategy stays the same.

The difference is how much pressure the copy puts on the buyer's private tension.

Use:

1. Variant 1 if Cooz wants maximum clarity.
2. Variant 2 if Cooz wants the best launch balance.
3. Variant 3 if Cooz wants the profile to feel more premium and diagnostic.
4. Variant 4 if Cooz wants the sharpest attention version for testing.

## Variant 1 - Clear And Referral-Safe

### Headline

> I help professionals over 40 rebuild strength, energy, and consistency when life keeps breaking the plan | Burbank + remote

### Banner

```text
When life keeps breaking the plan,
rebuild the plan around life.

Strength + energy coaching after 40
Burbank + remote daily support
```

### About Opening

```text
Most people who find me are not beginners.

They know how to work hard.
They know they need to train.
They know the basics.

That is not the problem.

The problem is that the plan keeps losing to the week they actually have.

Work gets loud.
Travel moves the schedule.
Sleep gets thin.
The 4 PM fog comes back.
Monday becomes the restart again.

I help professionals over 40 rebuild strength, energy, and consistency when life keeps breaking the plan.
```

### Best Use

Use this for referral asks and warm DM support.

It is the easiest version for someone else to repeat.
