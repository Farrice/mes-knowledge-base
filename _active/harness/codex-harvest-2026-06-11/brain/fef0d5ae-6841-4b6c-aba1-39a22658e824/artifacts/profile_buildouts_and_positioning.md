## B.E.C. Accelerator (Building Evidence + Confidence)

> "B.E.C." = the certainty of the quality standard you can produce. Here's how to build it fast.

### The 5-Rep Protocol

Before you send a single cold DM, do these 5 practice reps to build muscle memory and confidence:

| Rep | What You Do | Workflow | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pre-qualify a brand you love | `/lynch-product-prequalify` | 30 min | Pass/fail on 3 questions |
| 2 | Mine customer voice for that brand | `/lynch-customer-voice-mine` | 45 min | Voice data extraction |
| 3 | Write a 2-page mini-brief | `/lynch-creative-brief` (mini mode) | 90 min | The pitch document |
| 4 | Score it against the rubric | `/lynch-brief-audit` | 20 min | Score + fix list |
| 5 | Rewrite based on the audit | Manual revision | 45 min | Ship-ready mini-brief |

**Total time: ~4 hours for your first complete rep.**

After 3 reps, you'll know:
- Your average brief score
- How long each step actually takes you
- Which brands trigger the best thinking
- What your "floor" quality looks like (spoiler: it's higher than you think)

> [!TIP]
> Keep the first 3 practice briefs. They become your first "unsolicited" sends AND your first portfolio pieces. Nothing is wasted.

## Mini-Brief Shipping Workflow (The Fast-Path)

This is the exact sequence you run every time you want to ship a mini-brief:

```
Step 1: /lynch-product-prequalify → Pass? Continue. Fail? Pick another brand.
Step 2: /lynch-customer-voice-mine → Extract voice from Amazon, Reddit, forums
Step 3: Write the 2-3 page mini-brief:
        - The identity word (1 paragraph)
        - The transformation angle (1 paragraph)
        - ONE customer-as-media mechanism (1 paragraph)
        - The economics opportunity (1 paragraph)
Step 4: /lynch-brief-audit → Score it. Fix anything below 7/10 per dimension.
Step 5: Send via LinkedIn DM or cold email with:
        "I wrote this because your customers are already saying something
        your marketing isn't. No ask — just wanted you to see what's possible."
Step 6: Log the send in your tracking sheet. Follow up in 5 days if no response.
```

> [!NOTE]
> **Lynch GP-6 deployed in the outreach message**: The old outreach said "your product deserves better marketing" — which subtly insults the recipient's team. The new version says "your customers are already saying something your marketing isn't" — which positions the brief as a listening exercise, not a critique. It meets Jordan's internal monologue exactly: "why does word-of-mouth work but paid doesn't?"

**Target: 2 mini-briefs per week = 8 per month = 1-2 conversations guaranteed.**
