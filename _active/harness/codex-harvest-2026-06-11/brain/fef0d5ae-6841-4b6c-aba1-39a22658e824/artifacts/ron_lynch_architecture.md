## Workflow Architecture — 12 Workflows in 3 Tiers

### Tier 1 — Foundation Workflows (Core Methodology)

| # | Workflow | Slash Command | Description | Key Pattern |
|---|----------|---------------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | `lynch-identity-campaign` | `/lynch-identity-campaign` | Design a campaign around an identity transformation — not product features. Name the identity in one word, build all creative around it. | Identity-First Campaign Architecture |
| 2 | `lynch-customer-media` | `/lynch-customer-media` | Engineer the customer into an unpaid media channel. Design the shareable moment, remove broadcast friction, provide templates/tools. Goal: acquisition cost → zero. | Customer-as-Media Engineering |
| 3 | `lynch-transformation-sell` | `/lynch-transformation-sell` | Write sales creative that leads with the OUTPUT (the food, the adventure, the result) and positions the product as the "oh, you need this" discovery. Never sell features. | Food-First / Reverse Sell |
| 4 | `lynch-campaign-ecosystem` | `/lynch-campaign-ecosystem` | Design a full campaign ecosystem where identity → contest → media-matched creative → customer-as-media tools form a self-reinforcing loop. The GoPro playbook. | Campaign Ecosystem Design |

### Tier 2 — Practitioner Workflows (Granular Deployment)

| # | Workflow | Slash Command | Description | Key Pattern |
|---|----------|---------------|-------------|-------------|
| 5 | `lynch-media-match` | `/lynch-media-match` | Create segment-specific creative matched to each channel's audience identity. Not one ad everywhere — 10 executions, each speaking the identity language of that segment's media environment. | Media-to-Market Matching |
| 6 | `lynch-modular-script` | `/lynch-modular-script` | Write one comprehensive long-form script, then "take the staple out" to produce 5-12 standalone spots, social clips, email sequences, and VSL sections. | Modular Script Architecture |
| 7 | `lynch-counter-identity` | `/lynch-counter-identity` | Audit a product's REAL user behavior, then design the aspirational identity that advertising should sell. The gap between reality and aspiration = the sale. Coca-Cola principle. | Counter-Identity Advertising |
| 8 | `lynch-discovery-protocol` | `/lynch-discovery-protocol` | Launch with your best hypothesis audience, monitor where ACTUAL sales come from, pivot creative to match the real buyer. The George Foreman Grill protocol. | George Foreman Discovery |
