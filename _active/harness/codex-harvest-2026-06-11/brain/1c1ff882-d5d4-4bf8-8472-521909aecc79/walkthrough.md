## Key Production Decisions

- **Character wallets** include Voice Isolation Tests (3 scenarios each) so Josh can perform the characters live in meetings
- **Cold opens** are written as shootable scenes, not summaries — these can double as spec pages for a writing sample
- **Loglines** are organized by context (elevator → general → written → social → cocktail party) so Josh always has the right version
- **Adversarial test** arms Josh with pre-loaded rebuttals for the 5 most likely objections per concept

## The Triple-Pitch Line

> "I have three shows. One is about a music teacher running an unlicensed therapy practice in South Central. One is about the biggest movie star alive trying to survive fourth grade. And one is about the most invisible famous person in Hollywood trying to get fired and failing. All three are about the same thing: what happens when the role you're playing isn't the person you are."
