## 1. The Three Questions Positioning Lock

> [!IMPORTANT]
> These three sentences control every subsequent decision. Every edition, every Note, every design choice answers to these.

*   **Question 1 — What?** Parallax is a weekly personal essay + prompt pack from the intersection of psychology, AI, fatherhood, anime, and spirituality — the mind garden of a multi-passionate person who stopped pretending to fit in one box.

*   **Question 2 — Who?** For the ambitious outsider who has 47 browser tabs, a bookshelf that makes no sense to anyone else, and a quiet suspicion that their range is the asset — not the liability.

*   **Question 3 — What-Next?** Readers build a worldview alongside Farrice, run AI-powered micro-transformations (prompt packs) that shift something real in 15 minutes, and over time develop the psychological trust that makes working with him professionally feel inevitable — not pitched.

## 2. Name Audit: "Parallax"

*   **Say it out loud test:** Pass. Clean, one-word, easily recognizable.
*   **Searchability:** Pass. Stylized as "Parallax by Farrice Cain" in Substack to dominate the exact phrase. Live URL: `parallaxletter.substack.com`.
*   **Conveys the promise:** Strong Pass. Parallax = the displacement in the apparent position of an object viewed along two different lines of sight. It communicates the core promise: you see depth that a single vantage point can never reveal. Edition 01 defined it explicitly: *"Named for what happens when you look at the same object from multiple positions."*

## 9. Visual Identity (Summary from Creative Rebrief)

> Full specifications: [parallax-creative-rebrief.md](file:///Users/farricecain/Google%20Antigravity/_active/farrice-brand/content/parallax-creative-rebrief.md)

**Core principles:**
- Typography IS the identity. Color is the accent. Negative space is the confidence.
- AI generation is the seasoning, never the main course.
- Build everything in Canva. Zero AI generation for core identity assets.

**Color system (2 colors):**

| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Primary | Deep Ink | `#1C1C1E` |
| Accent | Parallax Violet | `#7B61FF` |
| Text (on dark) | Warm White | `#F5F0EB` |

**Wordmark**: "PARALLAX" in Space Grotesk Bold, all caps. The second "L" is set in Parallax Violet. Every other letter is warm white. One letter, different color. That's the 3% (Abloh rule).

**Typography stack**: Space Grotesk (headlines) · Source Serif 4 (body) · JetBrains Mono (code/prompt packs)
