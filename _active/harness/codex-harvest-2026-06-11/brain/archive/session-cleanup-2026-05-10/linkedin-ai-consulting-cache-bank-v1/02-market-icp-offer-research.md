## Market Thesis

The market no longer needs to be convinced that AI can create output.

The market needs help with a harder question:

> Which parts of the work can AI execute, and which parts still depend on judgment that has never been made visible?

That is the opening.

Generic AI consultants sell automation.

Farrice should sell the missing operating layer between creative strategy and AI execution:

- customer language
- proof hierarchy
- offer angle
- taste boundaries
- source-of-truth rules
- CTA timing
- escalation triggers
- "do not invent" boundaries
- work primitives AI can inherit

## Primary ICP

### Founder-Led Expert Paying The Marketing Rescue Tax

They run a consulting, coaching, advisory, agency, expert service, or premium local/service business.

They already use AI. The problem is not adoption. The problem is trust.

Private sentence:

> "It is close, but I still cannot ship it."

### What They Use AI For

| Workstream | AI Output | What Goes Wrong |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn/content | Posts, carousels, hooks, newsletters | Correct topic, wrong judgment; sounds generic; misses customer language. |
| Sales | Follow-ups, proposals, discovery summaries | Misses nuance, urgency, objections, or tone. |
| Delivery | SOPs, checklists, client docs | Human-readable but not agent-readable. |
| Offers | Landing copy, service descriptions, packages | Promise gets vague; proof is weak; CTA comes too early. |
| Operations | Task workflows, templates, automations | Speeds up unclear work and increases review load. |

### Buy Triggers

- They rewrote the same AI output multiple times.
- A team member used AI and created more review work.
- They need content or sales assets but cannot risk generic output.
- They know the correction instantly but cannot explain it as a reusable rule.
- They are considering AI workflows, agents, or automation but do not know what is safe to delegate.

### Buyer Language

- "It looks done, but I still cannot send it."
- "It is technically right, but not what we mean."
- "I keep fixing the same kind of thing."
- "AI made us sound like everyone else."
- "The prompt works until the situation changes."
- "The SOP makes sense to us, but AI misreads it."
- "I thought this would save time, but now I am reviewing everything."

## Flagship Offer Copy

### Free Tool

Paste one AI-assisted post, proposal, SOP, prompt, or workflow.

The tool will show where the output was forced to guess:

- buyer language it never heard
- proof rules it skipped
- CTA timing it rushed
- taste boundaries it crossed
- source-of-truth rules it needed

### Paid Bridge

If the report shows a repeated pattern, the next step is an AI Misfire Map.

I will take one real artifact and map:

1. where AI guessed,
2. what judgment or source of truth was missing,
3. what rule, example, boundary, or primitive would prevent the same rescue work next time.

### Implementation Bridge

If the same problem keeps happening across a workflow, the fix is not another prompt.

The fix is a marketing system primitive:

> a compact operating document that makes customer language, proof rules, offer logic, CTA timing, and quality standards clear enough for AI to use without inventing the work.
