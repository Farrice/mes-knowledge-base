### Profile/Featured Line

Paste one AI-assisted post, proposal, SOP, prompt, or workflow. Get an instant map of where AI guessed, what judgment was missing, and what rule would make the work reusable.

## The New Lead Magnet

The strongest asset is not a static PDF.

It is an instant experience:

1. Paste a real AI output.
2. Say what the output was supposed to do.
3. Say what felt off.
4. Get a Misfire Map.
5. See the first agent-ready marketing rule the system needed.
6. Leave an email to receive or continue the report.

This works because it lets the buyer feel the transformation before buying.
