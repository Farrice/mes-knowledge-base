01 Research Evidence Ledger

Research Spine

The 2026 market foundation is:

AI adoption is moving from experimentation into workflow redesign, but trust, governance, context, buyer clarity, and proof are now the bottlenecks.

This supports AI Misfire Express only if translated into plain buyer language:

AI is already in your workflow, but you still do not fully trust the output.

The buyer does not need a lecture on agentic AI. The buyer needs a diagnosis of the point where AI is guessing.

Freshness Rule

Primary evidence should be from 2026 and dated on or before May 8, 2026. Older sources may be used only as background. Future-dated sources should be excluded.

Useful Public Language

Use:

  • "AI output that looks finished but still needs rescue."
  • "Where AI is being forced to guess."
  • "The judgment behind the work."
  • "The correction is the data."
  • "Make one workflow clear enough for AI to execute."
  • "Prompt, SOP, workflow, content process, sales follow-up, proposal, or failed AI output."

Use only after buyer comprehension:

  • "context engineering"
  • "agent-readable work"
  • "semantic document library"
  • "work primitive"
  • "runtime governance"
  • "agent-ready operating system"

Avoid as first-touch hooks:

  • "AI-native documentation"
  • "semantic work primitive"
  • "context infrastructure"
  • "agentic governance layer"
  • "operating-model redesign"

What The Evidence Proves

The evidence proves:

  • AI is moving into workflows.
  • Trust, quality, context, and governance matter.
  • Professional-service buyers are adopting AI but still need validation.
  • Buyers are skeptical and need proof before large commitments.

The evidence does not yet prove:

  • founder-led experts will pay $500-$1,000 for this diagnostic
  • LinkedIn will convert the CTA
  • "AI Misfire Express" is the best offer name
  • the first audience should remain founder-led experts after live signal

Red-Team Notes

Do not claim the offer is validated. The market category is validated; the buyer response is not.

Do not let the research become the copy. Sources are the foundation, not the post.

Do not shame buyers for using AI poorly. The stronger frame is:

Your correction is not a nuisance. It is the missing operating rule.
