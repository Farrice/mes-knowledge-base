Farrice Voice + Proof Style Card

The readable standard for keeping AI Misfire content human, specific, proof-backed, and unmistakably Farrice.

Core Standard

Do not let AI finish the task while missing the work.

No clean educational post is good enough unless it carries lived tension, human judgment, proof, rhythm, and a line only Farrice would write.

Active Position

Public wedge: Creative strategist + AI operating partner for founder-led experts whose best judgment is still trapped in their head.

Cash/signal offer: AI Misfire Express.

Buyer translation: Your AI keeps guessing because the judgment behind the work is still trapped in your head.

Voice DNA

  • Sharp friend energy, not guru energy.
  • Dad under real pressure, not a content machine.
  • Pattern-recognition nerd connecting AI, parenting, training, gaming, and business.
  • Warm but direct. Premium by restraint, not polish.
  • Specific enough to sting a little.

Proof Rules

  • Personal proof: "I caught this in my own content."
  • Process proof: before/after diagnosis, correction notes, internal audit.
  • Lived proof: fatherhood, compressed work window, personal training, coaching, creative strategy.
  • Market proof: current source or observable platform behavior.
  • Demonstrable proof: Loom, audit map, post teardown, client artifact, workflow example.

Post Architecture

  1. Scene, confession, contradiction, or specific moment.
  2. Reader mirror.
  3. Hidden mechanism.
  4. Proof or lived example.
  5. Perspective shift.
  6. Small next action or open loop.

Voice Moves

Self-indicting opener

"I almost published the exact kind of AI content I warn people about."

Painfully specific detail

"It sounds like a person who read your About page once and then got promoted to CMO."

Soft knife

"If you keep rewriting the same AI output, you do not have a prompt problem yet. You have an invisible judgment problem."

Reject If

  • It opens with a generic AI trend.
  • It explains the mechanism before the reader feels the problem.
  • It uses "semantic documents" or "context engineering" before buyer recognition.
  • It ends every post with a DM keyword.
  • It could be posted by any AI consultant.

Luke + Rory Addition

Insight vector: The output is not generic because AI touched it. It is generic because the judgment that would make it specific was never made visible.

Perception reframe: In 2026, polished content no longer automatically signals quality. It can signal sameness. The new quality signal is visible judgment.

Mechanism name: The Finished-Looking Misfire.
