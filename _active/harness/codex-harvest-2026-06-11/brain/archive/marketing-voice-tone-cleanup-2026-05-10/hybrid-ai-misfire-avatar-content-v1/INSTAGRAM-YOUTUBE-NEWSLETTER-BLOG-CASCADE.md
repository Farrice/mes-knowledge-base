# Instagram YouTube Newsletter Blog Cascade

## Cascade Principle

Every platform should repeat the same market truth in a native way:

> AI did the task. It did not understand the work.

Do not copy-paste LinkedIn posts everywhere.

Use LinkedIn for conversation and DMs. Use Instagram for recognition. Use YouTube/Loom for proof. Use newsletter/blog for authority and durable search/referral assets.

## Core Asset To Cascade First

LinkedIn flagship:

> The most dangerous AI output is not the bad one. It is the one that looks finished.

- Instagram carousel
- 45-second reel
- 5-minute Loom

- blog article

## Instagram Carousel 2

Title:

> Prompt fix or workflow fix?

Slides:

1. If one output is bad, maybe the prompt is bad.
2. If the same correction keeps happening, the workflow is unclear.
3. Prompt issue: missing task, format, tone, or constraint.
4. Workflow issue: missing source of truth, decision rule, example, or quality test.
5. Prompt issue: easy to patch.
6. Workflow issue: needs a clearer operating layer.
7. Send the misfire. Diagnose before you build.
