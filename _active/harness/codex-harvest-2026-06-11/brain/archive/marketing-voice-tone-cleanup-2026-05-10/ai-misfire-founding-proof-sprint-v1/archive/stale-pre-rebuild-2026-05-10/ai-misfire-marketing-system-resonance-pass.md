# AI Misfire: Marketing System Resonance Pass

> Consumer perception and insight-vector upgrade that turns AI Misfire from a narrow output audit into the doorway for a marketing/content operating system.

## Intent Lock

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| Correction | AI Misfire should not sell as a standalone prompt/output problem. It should reveal the missing marketing system underneath the output. |
| Finished Product | A $300 proof-building diagnostic that maps where AI guessed because customer perception, proof logic, offer judgment, or content rules were not captured. |
| Positioning | Creative Strategist + AI Operating Partner building marketing systems from customer language, founder judgment, proof, and agent-ready work primitives. |
| Buyer Felt Moment | "This looks usable, but I still know it will not move the right buyer." |
| CTA | Send one AI-assisted marketing artifact you had to rescue. I will show where the marketing system was missing. |

## Perception Posture Profile

This is the one radical individual the offer should speak to.

| Dimension | Profile |
|---|---|
| Occupation | Founder-led expert who is still the final taste, customer-language, and proof authority for marketing work. |
| Activity | Rewrites posts, proposals, offers, DMs, sales pages, and content plans after AI or junior help makes them "fine" but not buyer-moving. |
| Thought Process | "I know the idea is good. Why does the output sound like every other consultant?" |
| Proxy Evaluation | They judge help by whether it catches the wrong customer language, vague proof, premature CTA, and missing belief shift. |
| Transaction Feeling | Buying should feel like commissioning a sharper strategic map, not paying someone for prompt tweaks. |
| Costly Signal Need | The offer must signal taste, restraint, and strategic discernment. No desperate availability language. No generic automation promise. |
| Feels-Like Reality | "I am carrying the marketing system in my nervous system, and AI keeps exposing that." |

## Unarticulated Buyer Feeling

The buyer is not only thinking:

> "AI got this wrong."

They are thinking:

> "My marketing only works when I am personally there to rescue the meaning."

That is the real resonance layer.

## Perception-Model Map

| Old Mental Model | Perceptual Distortion | Corrected Model |
|---|---|---|
| "I need better AI prompts." | The prompt is treated as the work. | The prompt is only the request. The marketing system is the context underneath it. |
| "AI content sounds generic." | Generic is seen as a writing problem. | Generic is often a missing customer-perception problem. |
| "I need more content output." | Volume is mistaken for power. | Content only compounds when the belief shift, proof logic, and CTA logic are reusable. |
| "The founder is the bottleneck." | Founder judgment is treated as inefficiency. | Founder judgment is the asset. The system must make it reusable. |
| "Automation should save time." | Efficiency is judged before trust. | Automation that misses perception creates review tax and brand risk. |

## Corrected Offer Frame

### Before

```text
I diagnose one AI output, prompt, SOP, or workflow and show where AI was forced to guess.
```

### After

```text
I use one AI-assisted marketing artifact as an x-ray for your content system.

I map where AI was forced to guess because the customer language, belief shift, proof logic, offer judgment, or production rules were still trapped in your head.
```

## Product Architecture

| Layer | What The Diagnostic Looks For |
|---|---|
| Customer Perception | What does the buyer already feel but cannot articulate? |
| Offer Judgment | What promise, scope, CTA, or objection logic was missing? |
| Content System | What repeatable content primitive should exist underneath this output? |
| Proof Architecture | What evidence would make the claim believable? |
| Founder Taste | What "we would never say it that way" rule needs to become reusable? |
| Agent-Ready Workflow | What inputs, rules, examples, boundaries, and quality tests would let AI produce better work next time? |
