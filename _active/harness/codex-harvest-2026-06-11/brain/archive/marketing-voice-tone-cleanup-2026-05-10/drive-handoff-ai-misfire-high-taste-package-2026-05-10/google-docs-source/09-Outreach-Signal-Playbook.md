# Outreach Signal Playbook

## Boundary

Manual outreach only.

Do not:

- scrape LinkedIn
- auto-DM
- auto-comment
- auto-like
- fake engagement
- imply a relationship that does not exist
- publish or contact externally without explicit approval

## Outreach Goal

Create qualified conversations that lead to one of three outcomes:

1. A submitted prompt, SOP, workflow, proposal, sales follow-up, content process, or failed output.
2. A paid AI Misfire Express audit.
3. A proof candidate for anonymized before/after content.

## Qualification Criteria

Strong fit:

- founder-led expert/service business
- already using AI
- visible content, offer, or delivery workflow
- likely quality/taste/judgment bottleneck
- has a repeatable workflow
- can benefit from a small diagnostic before implementation

Weak fit:

- only wants generic prompts
- no real workflow
- no urgency
- wants free consulting
- wants full automation before diagnosis
- cannot share any example

## Prospect Segments

### Founder-Led Experts

Examples:

- consultants
- coaches
- small agencies
- expert service businesses
- advisors
- course/community operators

Angle:

> Where is AI already close, but still wrong enough that you have to rescue it?

### AI-Forward Agencies

Examples:

- automation agencies
- AI consultants
- workflow builders
- small dev shops

Angle:

> Are clients buying automation before their workflows are clear enough for agents to execute?

### Premium Local/Service Businesses

Examples:

- real estate teams
- professional services
- agencies
- home services with sales/admin workflows

Angle:

> What part of sales follow-up, intake, proposal, or client delivery still needs a senior person to fix the AI output?

## Comment-To-DM Flow

### Step 1: Comment With Value

Use one useful distinction:

```text
The correction after the AI draft is often the most valuable data in the workflow.
```

or:

```text
Before automating this, I would want to know where a senior person still has to rescue the output.
```

### Step 2: DM Only If There Is A Reason

```text
Appreciate your comment on that.

Quick question: where does AI already get close in your business, but still need you or someone senior to fix it before it is usable?
```

### Step 3: Ask For One Artifact

```text
That is exactly the kind of thing I am mapping.

If you send one example - prompt, SOP, workflow, or output - I can point out the first places AI is being forced to guess.
```

### Step 4: Mini-Diagnosis

```text
Got it. I am going to look for three things:

1. where the AI guessed
2. what judgment or source of truth was missing
3. whether this is a prompt issue, SOP issue, or deeper workflow issue
```

### Step 5: Paid Bridge

```text
This looks bigger than a prompt tweak.

The useful next step is the AI Misfire Express audit. I will map the full guess pattern, missing context, and first operating-layer fix.

For this sprint, the audit is [$500/$750/$1,000 depending on turnaround and depth].
```

## Cold Connection Message Options

### Founder-Led Expert

```text
Noticed your work around [specific offer/topic].

I am studying where AI output gets close but still needs expert rescue before it can be trusted.

Curious if that shows up anywhere in your content, sales, or delivery workflow.
```

### AI Agency

```text
Saw your work around AI workflows.

I am focused on the layer before implementation: where prompts/SOPs force AI to guess because the work is not clear enough yet.

Curious how often you see that with clients.
```

### Local/Service Business

```text
Saw your work in [industry].

I am mapping where AI can help with follow-up, intake, proposals, and client communication without creating more cleanup.

Curious where AI is already close but not quite usable for you.
```

## Follow-Up Sequence

### Follow-Up 1

```text
One useful way to spot it:

look at the last AI output someone had to rewrite manually.

That correction usually reveals the hidden rule.
```

### Follow-Up 2

```text
No pressure either way.

If you do not have a workflow in mind yet, the easiest starting point is a draft that looked fine but still felt wrong.
```

### Follow-Up 3

```text
Closing the loop here.

If this becomes relevant later, send the misfire first. I can tell pretty quickly whether it is a prompt tweak or a deeper workflow issue.
```

## Intake Questions

Ask only what is needed:

1. What was AI supposed to do?
2. What did it produce?
3. What felt wrong?
4. What did you have to correct?
5. How often does this happen?
6. What does this cost you: time, trust, quality, speed, or sales?

## Mini-Diagnosis Template

```text
AI Misfire Map for: [asset/workflow]

1. Where AI guessed
   - [specific moment]

2. What was missing
   - [source of truth / rule / example / customer language / boundary]

3. Why it matters
   - [time / trust / quality / revenue / risk]

4. First fix
   - [prompt tweak / SOP cleanup / work primitive / not ready to automate]

Recommendation:
   [free note / paid audit / implementation sprint / no fit]
```

## Paid Audit Fit Gate

Offer paid audit only when:

- the workflow is real
- the misfire repeats
- the problem affects time, trust, quality, revenue, or delivery
- the buyer can share enough source material
- the fix is not obviously a one-line prompt tweak

Do not offer paid audit when:

- the buyer has no artifact
- the problem is too vague
- the buyer wants free strategy
- the workflow is not important
- the buyer expects full implementation for audit price

## Signal Score

Score each conversation 1-5:

1. Vague interest, no workflow.
2. Specific problem, no artifact.
3. Artifact available, weak urgency.
4. Artifact plus repeated pain.
5. Artifact, repeated pain, buyer asks price or turnaround.

Priority:

- Follow up on 4-5.
- Nurture 3.
- Do not chase 1-2.
