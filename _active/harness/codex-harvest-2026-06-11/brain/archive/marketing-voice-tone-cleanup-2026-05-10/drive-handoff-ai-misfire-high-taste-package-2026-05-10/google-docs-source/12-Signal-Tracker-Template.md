## Daily Use

1. Log every published post, strategic comment, contextual DM, and marketplace response.
2. Copy exact buyer language into `Pain Phrase`.
3. Mark `Proof Candidate` only when the interaction gives you a real teardown, quote, objection, or artifact.
4. Move the strongest proof candidates into the next post, demo, or paid bridge.

## Active Tracking Rule

Every real market signal goes here before it becomes content, outreach, proof, or a paid-offer bridge.

| Signal Type | Log It When | Required Fields | Follow-Up Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reply | Someone responds to a post, comment, DM, marketplace proposal, or payment/offer touch | Date, platform, person/company, source URL, exact pain phrase, status, next follow-up | If the reply names a repeated or costly problem, send a 3-bullet mini diagnosis and bridge to the paid Founding Proof only if the gap is deeper than a prompt tweak |
| Comment | You leave a strategic comment or someone comments on your content | Date, platform, asset/touch, lane, person/company, source URL, qualified comment count, notes | If the thread shows buyer fit, follow with a contextual DM that asks for one concrete artifact |
| Artifact Submission | Someone sends a prompt, workflow, SOP, failed AI output, content draft, proposal, or process doc | Date, platform, person/company, artifact type in notes, pain phrase, artifact submitted = true, proof candidate status | Cap free help at a 3-bullet diagnosis; if the artifact reveals a marketing-system or judgment void, bridge to the paid audit |

## Capture Standard

- Use the buyer's exact words whenever possible.
- Do not summarize pain into clean consultant language until after the raw phrase is logged.
- Leave `Paid Audit Interest` as `false` until the buyer shows explicit intent, asks for help, or accepts the paid bridge.
- Mark `Proof Candidate` as `true` only when the signal can become a teardown, before/after, quote, objection, or fulfillment example.
- If I am helping you operate LinkedIn or marketplaces in-session, I should update this tracker after each meaningful signal before moving to the next touch.
