# Coach Cooz - Codex Triage And Referral System

Purpose: Turn trust into qualified calls without making Cooz sound desperate or overbuilt.

## The Core Principle

Do fewer calls with better fit.

The Triage Audit should not become a free 90-minute consultation for anyone curious. It is a premium diagnostic for people who are close enough to action that a real recommendation would help.

## Referral Sprint

Run 20 asks in 30 days.

Start with the highest-trust five, then expand to current clients, former clients, peers, family-adjacent contacts, local professionals, and credible gym/community relationships.

Use this first.

```text

Not someone looking for a cheap trainer.

Someone who would value serious, high-touch help rebuilding the body that has to carry the life they built.

Who comes to mind?
```

## Triage Audit Gate

### Who gets a free audit

- Direct warm referral from a trusted person
- Existing relationship with Cooz
- Clear high-fit LinkedIn/GBP inbound with budget and urgency

### Who fills an application first

- Cold LinkedIn traffic
- GBP/search traffic
- Social lurkers
- Anyone asking price before context

### When to charge

If after 5 audits:

- show-up quality is weak
- prospects are not budget-fit
- calls take 3+ hours including prep/follow-up
- audit-to-close is below 20%

Then charge for the diagnostic and credit it toward enrollment.

## 90-Minute Call Structure

### Minutes 0-25 - Listen

Goal: hear the real pattern.

Prompts:

- What made you book this now?
- What has changed in your body or energy over the last year?
- What have you tried that worked for a minute and then stopped?
- Walk me through a normal Tuesday.
- What are you tired of hearing yourself say?

Do not diagnose early.

### Minutes 25-40 - Read Back

Use their words:

```text
Here is what I am hearing.

You said [exact phrase].

You said [exact phrase].

And the pattern I hear is this: the body carrying [career/family/life] is starting to fall behind it.

You have tried [alternatives].

Some worked for a minute.

None became something you could keep.
```

If they correct you, good. That means they are collaborating.

### Minutes 40-60 - Diagnose

Pick only 2-3 patterns. Do not perform the whole framework.

Diagnostic patterns:

1. Under-recovered, not under-trained
2. Inputs unstable before training problem
3. Calendar winning because practice is not installed
4. Self-reliance turning into blind spot
5. Wrong category: sprint solution for long-arc problem
6. Body signaling, not failing

### Minutes 60-80 - Recommend One Path

Use this structure:

```text
Based on what you told me, I would not recommend all options.

I would recommend [Path].

Here is why.
```

Then explain only that path.

### Minutes 80-90 - Close Or Next Step

If fit is clear:

```text
If you want to do this, the next step is simple.

We pick the start date, set the first 12-week target, and I send the agreement.

Do you want me to hold the spot for you?
```

If fit is not clear:

```text
I do not think you should decide from pressure.

I want you to decide from clarity.

Here is the one thing I want you to think about before we talk again: are you looking for another restart, or are you ready to build the structure that makes restarting unnecessary?
```

## Objection Handling

### "It is expensive."

```text
It is.

And I do not want to make it sound cheaper than it is.

The question is whether this is a training expense or whether the body falling behind your life is already costing you more than that.
```

### "I need to think about it."

```text
That is fair.

Think about the right thing, though.

Not whether this is more than a gym membership. It is.

Think about whether the pattern you described is likely to change without a different structure around it.
```

### "Can I start next month?"

```text
Maybe.

But I want to be honest: next month is also the story that has kept this alive for a while.

If next month is real, we can set the date now.

If next month is a way to avoid deciding, it is better to name that.
```

### "Can I do fewer sessions?"

```text
Maybe, but I would not recommend less support if the diagnosis says the support is the missing piece.

The goal is not to buy the smallest version.

The goal is to choose the version that actually has a chance of holding.
```
