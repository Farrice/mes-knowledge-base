# Coach Cooz - Analyze Intent Report

Workflow used: `analyze-intent`
Date: 2026-05-03

## Summary

The highest immediate search intent is local fitness language, not "executive coach" language.

Use "executive" and "professional" in copy to qualify the buyer. Use personal training, private coaching, strength coaching, and Burbank terms for search/discoverability.

## Keyword Intent Reports

### Keyword: personal trainer Burbank

Intent class: Transactional
Value score: 88/100

Reasoning: The phrase names a service and location. Searchers are likely comparing nearby providers or ready to contact someone.

SERP validation:

- Ads detected: Search results surfaced local providers and directory-style pages; ad presence should be checked manually from Burbank because local ads vary by geography.
- Top competitors/examples found: Body Engineering by JD, The Strength Code, Soto Performance, High Voltage Fitness, FitWorks Burbank.

Verdict: ATTACK

Use in:

- GBP services
- Website title/meta
- Local landing page
- LinkedIn profile delivery line

### Keyword: executive coach Burbank

Intent class: Commercial investigation
Value score: 45/100 for Cooz

Reasoning: The buyer may have money, but search intent likely maps to business coaching, leadership coaching, or career coaching. It can confuse the offer.

Verdict: AVOID AS PRIMARY

Use "executive" only as qualifier inside body copy, not as the main local SEO target.

## Platform Findings

### LinkedIn

LinkedIn's own help says the feed considers context, profile, network, activity, and whether content provides knowledge or advice. LinkedIn's 2026 product updates also emphasize authentic engagement, less generic content, and reduced reach for automated or inauthentic activity.

Implication for Cooz:

- The profile and posts must use consistent language: strength, energy, consistency, body, private coaching, Burbank, remote support.
- Posts need useful knowledge or advice, not only client stories.
- Automated or templated engagement should be avoided.
- DM outreach should feel hand-written and specific.

Sources:

- [LinkedIn Help: How the Feed ranks content](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a9554004)
- [LinkedIn News: Authentic content and conversations](https://news.linkedin.com/2026/authentic-content-and-conversations)
- [LinkedIn News: Improving the Feed](https://news.linkedin.com/2026/ImprovingTheFeed)

### Instagram

Instagram continues to reward original content and is expanding protection against reposted/unoriginal photos and carousels. For Cooz, this means original gym moments, client-safe stories, Cooz's own face/voice, and practical carousels should beat generic quote cards or repurposed LinkedIn screenshots.

Implication for Cooz:

- Use original photos/video, not stock-like assets.
- Turn one LinkedIn diagnostic into a carousel.
- Turn one client-safe idea into a Reel.
- Use DMs for warm conversation, not "DM keyword" on every post.

Sources:

- [TechCrunch: Instagram restricts reach of content aggregators](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)
- [Digital Camera World coverage of Mosseri update](https://www.digitalcameraworld.com/tech/social-media/two-years-later-instagram-is-finally-giving-photographers-the-same-protection-as-videographers-with-this-key-change)

### Google Business Profile

Google states local ranking is based mainly on relevance, distance, and prominence. Complete information, accurate categories, reviews/replies, and photos/videos matter.

Implication for Cooz:

- Build for local service relevance first.
- Use complete services and real photos.
- Get specific client reviews.
- Reply to reviews.
- Do not expect the description alone to rank.

Source:

- [Google Business Profile local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en)
