# Coach Cooz - Comparison Guide

Use this to compare the Codex version against Antigravity's V6.

## Core Difference

| Area | Antigravity V6 | Codex Version |
|---|---|---|
| Primary brand lead | The Resurrection Coach | Strength and energy rebuilds for professionals over 40 |
| Role of Resurrection | Front-door brand | Warm-buyer identity layer |
| Revenue target | 3-4 clients by Week 8, income spine | `$6k-$10k/mo` in 90 days; `$10k-$15k/mo` more credible by 6 months unless ESA closes |
| Channel strategy | Parallel multi-channel rollout | 30-day proof sprint: referrals, GBP, LinkedIn, Triage Audit only |
| Offer structure | Four locked pathways | Three public paths plus one invite-only premium pilot |
| LinkedIn content | Strong story/proof package | More diagnostic utility and less repeated brand phrasing |
| Sales motion | Free 90-minute Triage Audit as universal entry | Free for warm trust; gated/application for cold traffic |
| Paid ads | Optional Week 5 trial | Hold until organic/referral loop proves fit |
| Hollywood/Burbank | Referral source/geography | Same, but only counted once named access is confirmed |

## What Codex Keeps

- Long-arc client proof as the trust spine.
- Brave Choice as the core methodology.
- Triage Audit as the central conversion mechanism.
- Burbank/Hollywood as legitimate referral context.
- Premium pricing, as long as delivery and fit justify it.

## What Codex Changes

- Moves "Resurrection" out of the cold headline and into the warm identity layer.
- Makes the first 30 days a validation sprint instead of a media rollout.
- Refuses to count channels that are not owned yet.
- Adds stricter sales labor tracking.
- Uses simpler buyer-facing language.
- Treats ESA as a pilot to validate, not a product to assume.

## What To Compare In Practice

The winner is not the prettier document. The winner is the version that produces:

- More named referrals
- More booked Triage Audits
- Better-fit calls
- Less sales labor per close
- More confidence at premium price
- Clearer buyer language from real prospects

## 30-Day Head-To-Head Test

If testing both versions, split the test this way:

| Test | Antigravity V6 | Codex Version | Winning Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Referral language | "Resurrection Coach" ask | Plain-problem ask | Named intros sent |
| LinkedIn headline | Resurrection-led | Strength/energy-led | Profile views to DMs/bookings |
| Content | Story/proof dominant | Story + diagnostic utility | DMs and saves, not likes |
| Triage | Universal free audit | Warm free, cold gated | Close rate and hours per close |
| Channel expansion | Multi-channel rollout | Core loop only | Booked audits by Day 30 |

## My Bet

Antigravity's V6 is stronger as a brand world.

Codex's version is stronger as a 30-day sales test.

The best final system may combine them: Antigravity's emotional depth after Codex's plain-language front door proves which buyers actually move.
