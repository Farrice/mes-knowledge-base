## Purpose

This package is the active front-facing authority for Farrice's Creative Strategist + AI Operating Partner work.

> AI slop is the visible symptom. The real problem is that the founder's judgment, customer language, proof logic, and taste rules have not been turned into a marketing operating system yet.
