## Controlled Demo

### Prompt

```text
Write a LinkedIn post for a consultant launching an AI-assisted content system for founder-led service businesses. Make it persuasive and end with a CTA to book a call.
```

### Polished-But-Wrong Output

```text
AI is changing how founder-led service businesses create content.

Instead of spending hours brainstorming, writing, and editing, you can now use AI to create consistent, high-quality content faster.

The key is having the right system.

With an AI-assisted workflow, you can turn your expertise into LinkedIn posts, emails, and sales content without starting from scratch.

If you want help building an AI content system, send me a message and let’s talk.
```

### Misfire Map

| Visible Problem | What AI Guessed | Missing Marketing Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Generic promise | “consistent, high-quality content faster” | The buyer’s private sentence is closer to: “AI gets me close, then I still have to rewrite the part that would make someone care.” |
| Wrong enemy | “spending hours brainstorming” | The real enemy is finished-looking AI that hides missing judgment. |
| Empty mechanism | “the right system” | Show the rule the system uses: buyer language, proof hierarchy, CTA timing, taste boundary, source of truth. |
| Premature CTA | “book a call” | Ask for one artifact first. Prove the diagnostic lens before asking for time. |
| No operating primitive | The model had no durable rules to inherit. | Build a Content Rescue Rulecard the agent can use next time. |
