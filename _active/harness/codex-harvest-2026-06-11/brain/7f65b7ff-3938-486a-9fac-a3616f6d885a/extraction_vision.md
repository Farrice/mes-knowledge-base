## Source Summary

This is a live keynote where Jun Yuh walks 300+ attendees through his **Creator Vision Framework** in real time — the same framework captured at a conceptual level in `creator-vision-matrix.md`, but here with **significantly more operational detail**:

1. **The Framework is What/Who/Why** — not What/Who/Uniqueness as the personal-brand skill captures it. The seminar uses **Skills, Perspective, Lifestyle** as the three content pillars (not Skill, Mindset, Lifestyle, Passions — the existing 4-pillar model). This is either an evolution or a parallel formulation.
2. **Hook Template × Creator Vision Permutation Engine** — this is **entirely new material**. Jun Yuh demonstrates how to combine hook templates with Creator Vision branches to generate infinite content ideas through systematic permutation. This is the operational "never run out of ideas" mechanism.
3. **Problem/Pursuit/Payoff (The 3 Ps)** — captured loosely in existing genius.md as "Problem, Pursuit, Payoff origin story," but the seminar goes deeper: showing multiple origin stories per person, and demonstrating how each individual branch generates its own content permutations.
4. **Psychographic > Demographic** — explicitly taught with examples. The existing skill captures this conceptually, but the seminar gives the executable diagnostic: "What they struggle with, what are their desires."
5. **Live Audience Workshop** — Jun Yuh runs the framework in real time with audience members, showing the coaching methodology.
