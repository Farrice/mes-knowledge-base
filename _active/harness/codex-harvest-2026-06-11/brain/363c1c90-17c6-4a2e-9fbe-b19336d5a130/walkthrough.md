## Genius Patterns Extracted

5 core patterns distilled from ~60 min of source material:

| Pattern | Core Insight |
|---------|-------------|
| **Paid to Feel** | AI commoditizes production; judgment becomes the scarce asset. The body knows before the mind. |
| **Proof > Skill** | 80-90% of conversion = proof. Proof coupled to skill is the long game, but proof alone outperforms skill alone short-term. |
| **CR × OU** | Every acquisition problem reduces to 2 variables: Conversion Rate × Outreach Units. It's always volume. |
| **Orchestrator Paradigm** | Creative strategist → orchestrator + judge. AI writes, human curates via nervous-system judgment. |
| **Halo Effect** | One blue-chip name > a portfolio of unknown brands. Psychological weakness of humans — exploit it. |
