## Why a New Skill (Not Updates to Existing)

The existing `luke-iha-creative-strategy` skill focuses on *strategic thinking upstream of copy* — creative briefs, Nuclear VSL portfolio pieces, offer cycling, and the Agency Ladder. These two new videos introduce a **distinct domain**: the *business operations of being a creative strategist* — client acquisition mechanics, proof portfolio sprinting, the "Paid to Feel" judgment methodology, and the Meta Andromeda ad diversity system. This warrants its own skill rather than bloating the existing one.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The new skill `luke-iha-client-mastery` is the **business engine** complement to the existing `luke-iha-creative-strategy` (the **strategic thinking** skill). Together they form a complete system: strategy → client acquisition → delivery → growth.
