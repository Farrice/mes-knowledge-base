# Coach Cooz 30-Day Validation Test Plan

Status: launch validation plan, May 3, 2026.

## Purpose

This plan tests the V3 strategy against reality.

The goal is not to prove the plan right. The goal is to find out which parts of the plan create actual buyer movement:

- named referrals
- profile views
- replies
- booked Triage Audits
- serious follow-ups
- closed clients
- useful objections
- service insights

## 30-Day Verdict Standard

At the end of 30 days, classify the strategy:

- Keep: clear traction and conversion signal.
- Revise: attention exists but positioning, offer, or follow-up needs work.
- Pause: activity happens but no serious buyer movement.
- Rebuild: the market does not understand or care about the current frame.

Expected current outcome: revise with stronger proof.

## Week 1: Referral And Positioning Test

### Objective

Find out whether the plain problem language creates named prospects.

### Actions

Send 20 warm referral asks in two versions.

Version A: Brand-led

> I’m helping Cooz build the next version of his work around Resurrection: helping people come back to a body they recognize before the diminished version becomes normal. Who comes to mind who might need that kind of support?

Version B: Plain-problem-led

> Who do you know who is 40-plus, successful, and quietly frustrated that their strength, energy, sleep, or consistency is not where it used to be?

### Measure

- Replies
- Named people
- Intros actually sent
- Audit interest
- Which phrase got the clearest “I know someone” response

### Pass Gate

By end of Week 1:

- 20 asks sent
- 8+ replies
- 4+ named prospects
- 2+ intros sent

If people give encouragement but no names, the message is not yet referral-clear.

## Week 2: LinkedIn Reader-Value Test

### Objective

Find out whether content creates recognition, saves/comments, profile views, and DMs.

### Cadence

Post 3 times:

1. Diagnostic utility post
2. Proof-with-transfer post
3. Belief shift post

Comment daily:

- 10 useful comments per weekday
- 3 local/SFV/Burbank
- 3 client/referral/peer network
- 3 ICP-adjacent professionals
- 1 authority post where Cooz can add a practical observation

### Post Quality Gate

Each post must give one of these:

- diagnosis
- distinction
- checklist
- question
- minimum action
- proof transfer

No self-proof without reader payoff.

### Measure

- impressions
- reactions
- meaningful comments
- saves if visible
- profile views
- new connection requests
- DMs
- audit clicks/bookings
- lines people quote back

### Pass Gate

By end of Week 2:

- 6+ meaningful comments or DMs across posts
- 10+ profile views tied to posting/commenting
- 1+ Triage Audit inquiry or warm conversation

If impressions happen without comments/DMs, revise the hooks and reader payoff.

## Week 3: Triage Audit And GBP Test

### Objective

Turn attention into diagnosis calls and local intent.

### Triage Audit Standard

Call length:

- 45 minutes standard
- 60 minutes max if pre-qualified

Call structure:

1. Why now?
2. What changed in the body?
3. What has been tried?
4. What breaks first: sleep, food, calendar, travel, pain, accountability?
5. What does success need to protect?
6. Fit / non-fit decision
7. One recommended path
8. Next step

Do not pitch a menu.

### GBP Actions

- Confirm categories and services.
- Add/update photos.
- Add service descriptions using plain terms:
  - private personal training Burbank
  - private strength coaching
  - strength and energy coaching after 40
  - remote fitness coaching Los Angeles
- Capture baseline rankings manually for:
  - personal trainer Burbank
  - private personal training Burbank
  - strength coach Burbank
  - fitness coach Burbank
  - online fitness coach Los Angeles
- Ask 5 proof-safe clients for Google reviews if appropriate.

### Measure

- GBP search terms/rank snapshot
- calls/clicks/messages
- review count and recency
- audits booked
- audit completion rate
- recommended path
- objections

### Pass Gate

By end of Week 3:

- 3 booked audits total from all channels
- 2 completed audits
- 1 offer recommendation made
- GBP baseline documented

## Week 4: Close Quality And Offer Validation

### Objective

Find out whether people understand the offer and whether price resistance is category confusion or true affordability.

### Actions

- Follow up with all audits within 24 hours.
- Use one-path recommendation.
- Run 5 premium remote/pilot conversations if candidates exist.
- Interview 5 current/past clients for buyer language and proof.

### Follow-Up Template

> Based on what you shared, I would not recommend a menu of options.
>
> The main break appears to be [diagnosis].
>
> The path I would recommend is [one path] because [reason].
>
> The first 30 days would focus on [first layer].
>
> If you want to move forward, the next step is [simple next step].

### Objection Sorting

When someone does not buy, classify the reason:

- unclear value
- price resistance
- timing
- spouse/partner
- needs medical care first
- not enough trust
- wrong offer path
- wants commodity training
- wants app/program only
- not urgent

### Pass Gate

By end of Week 4:

- 4 actual warm intros
- 3 booked audits
- 1 close or 2 serious follow-ups
- 10+ buyer/client interview insights
- clear top 3 objections

If this gate is missed, do not expand channels. Revise positioning, proof, or referral language first.

## Quality Gates

### Reader Value Gate

Every post must answer:

> What does the reader get before Cooz asks for anything?

Acceptable answers:

- a diagnosis
- a distinction
- a checklist
- a question
- a minimum action
- a proof lesson

### Proof Safety Gate

Before publishing, ask:

- Is the claim true?
- Is the client identifiable?
- Do we have permission?
- Is the measurement method known?
- Is this a single-client result being implied as universal?
- Is “real call” language literal?

### Offer Clarity Gate

Before every audit follow-up:

- Did Cooz recommend one path?
- Did he say why?
- Did he avoid a menu pitch?
- Did the buyer get a clear next step?
- Did he avoid free consulting overload?

### Revenue Math Gate

Do not say:

> 3-4 normal clients create `$10k-$15k/mo`.

Use:

> `$10k/mo` likely requires 6-8 standard A/B clients or one premium pilot plus base clients.
>
> `$15k/mo` is a 6-month target unless the premium pilot validates faster.

## Decision Rules After 30 Days

### Keep

Use if:

- 3+ audits completed
- 1+ close or 2 serious follow-ups
- referral language produces names
- LinkedIn creates DMs/profile views
- objections are specific and fixable

Next:

- continue same channel stack
- sharpen proof assets
- build second 30-day sprint

### Revise

Use if:

- people recognize the pain but do not book
- referrals give encouragement but few names
- posts get likes but no conversations
- audits happen but price/value is unclear

Next:

- tighten profile and CTAs
- improve Triage Audit framing
- add proof with transfer
- test alternate positioning statements

### Pause

Use if:

- high activity produces no serious conversations
- GBP has no signal
- referrals produce no named prospects
- LinkedIn creates no profile views or DMs

Next:

- pause channel expansion
- run 15 buyer interviews
- rewrite offer/category language

### Rebuild

Use if:

- buyers do not understand the problem
- the audience does not see urgency
- premium pricing is rejected even after category framing
- Cooz cannot deliver the service standard

Next:

- narrow ICP
- simplify offer
- choose local-only or remote-only for a cycle

## The First 30-Day North Star

The goal is not content volume.

The goal is proof of market movement:

> Can a clear profile, useful content, warm DMs, referrals, GBP, and a diagnostic audit create real buyer conversations and at least one premium close?

If yes, keep building.

If no, the data will show whether the problem is language, channel, proof, price, or offer fit.
