## 25 GAP QUESTIONS — Organized by Consumer Posture Dimension

### 🔵 OCCUPATION (Their Role in Your Brand's World) — 8 Questions

| # | Gap Question | Your Knowledge | Gap Level | Business Consequence |
|---|-------------|---------------|-----------|---------------------|
| 1 | **What do they call what they do when they're NOT pitching?** (Not "leadership coach" — what do they say at a dinner party?) | ❌ Unknown | 🔴 Critical | Your copy sounds like THEIR marketing copy, not how they actually talk. They scroll past. |
| 2 | **What's their relationship to the word "expert"?** (Do they own it? Cringe at it? Feel like a fraud saying it?) | ❌ Unknown | 🔴 Critical | If they have imposter syndrome about the word "expert" and you lead with "you're an expert," you trigger shame, not resonance. |
| 3 | **Where do they sit in the hierarchy of their own industry?** (Are they the respected veteran? The rising challenger? The quiet hidden gem?) | 🟡 Assumed | 🟠 Moderate | You're writing as if they're all "hidden gems." Some are frustrated veterans. Some are early-career. The energy is different. |
| 4 | **What do they secretly believe about people MORE visible than them?** (Jealousy? Contempt? "They're not even that good"? Inspiration?) | ❌ Unknown | 🔴 Critical | This reveals whether your "you deserve to be seen" messaging triggers aspiration or disgust. Both are possible. |
| 5 | **What professional achievement makes them proudest — and who knows about it?** | ❌ Unknown | 🟠 Moderate | The gap between what they're proud of and what's visible online IS the content strategy. You can't extract what you don't know exists. |
| 6 | **What kind of clients do they REFUSE?** | ❌ Unknown | 🔴 Critical | Their refusals reveal their identity more than their offers. You need to know who they don't want to attract. |
| 7 | **When they see a bad practitioner in their field get famous, what's the visceral emotion?** | ❌ Unknown | 🟠 Moderate | This is the co-conspirator fuel. If it's rage, you have a "fight the frauds" narrative. If it's sadness, you have a "restore the craft" narrative. |
| 8 | **Do they see "building a personal brand" as an extension of their mission or a distraction from it?** | 🟡 Assumed | 🔴 Critical | You're assuming brand-building is something they WANT. For many experts, it feels like selling out. Your positioning must address this tension head-on. |
