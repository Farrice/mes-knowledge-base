# Coach Cooz Voice Memo Intake Prompts

Purpose: Give Farrice a repeatable way to pull real stories, real language, and real Cooz energy before creating future content batches.

## How To Use

Ask Cooz for one 5-10 minute voice memo per prompt.

Do not ask him to write a post.

Ask him to talk.

The best memo contains:

- one real scene
- one client or self-observed moment
- one feeling he had in the room
- one thing he noticed
- one lesson the buyer can use
- one phrase that sounds like him

## The Capture Rule

Every content batch needs at least one of these:

- client moment
- Triage Audit moment
- training floor moment
- personal confession
- proof story
- Brave Choice decision moment

If the input is only a topic, the output will sound like content.

If the input is a moment, the output can sound like Cooz.

# Prompt Set 1: The Buyer Recognition Prompts

Use these when content needs more emotional truth.

1. Tell me about a client who said something ordinary, but you heard the deeper thing underneath it.
2. Tell me about the last time someone blamed discipline and you thought, "That is not the real issue."
3. What is a sentence you hear from clients that usually means something bigger?
4. What do people over 40 minimize when they first talk to you?
5. What is the moment when you can tell someone is tired of hearing themselves say "I'll get to it"?
6. What is a private fear your clients almost never say directly, but you can feel it in the room?
7. What does the 4 PM fog actually look like in a real person's life?

## Follow-Up Probes

- What did they say exactly?
- What did you notice in their body language?
- What did you want to say but did not say yet?
- What did you say instead?
- What would a bad coach have missed?

# Prompt Set 2: The Diagnostic Utility Prompts

Use these for save-worthy LinkedIn posts, IG carousels, and blog tools.

1. Walk me through the first five things you check before changing someone's training.
2. What are the signs someone is under-recovered, not under-trained?
3. What do you ask when someone says their energy crashes in the afternoon?
4. What do you ask when someone says travel ruins their training?
5. What are the most common leaks you find in a busy professional's week?
6. What is a simple test someone can run for seven days?
7. What is one thing you would cut before adding more workouts?

## Follow-Up Probes

- What would they normally assume is the problem?
- What do you look at first?
- What is the smallest useful action?
- What does this tell you after seven days?
- When is self-correction enough, and when do they need coaching?

---

# Prompt Set 3: The Proof With Transfer Prompts

Use these when client proof needs to teach the reader something.

1. Tell me the Mike story again, but focus on what his nine years prove to someone who keeps restarting.
2. Tell me the Corey travel story and what most coaches misunderstand about people with moving schedules.
3. Tell me the Sam go-go boots story. What does that story prove about joy and long-term consistency?
4. Tell me the Carron story. What did matching inputs to outputs actually change?
5. Tell me the Robin Brave Choice story. What did he name that you had not named yet?
6. Tell me the Mari story carefully. What can we say with certainty, and what needs measurement context?
7. Tell me about a client whose result was not flashy but mattered deeply.

## Follow-Up Probes

- What was the exact moment?
- What number or detail makes the story real?
- What is the lesson for someone who is not that client?
- What should we not overclaim?
- What would you want the reader to do differently after reading this?

---

# Prompt Set 4: The Brave Choice Prompts

Use these for Friday belief/identity posts.

1. Tell me about a small Brave Choice you watched a client make recently.
2. Tell me about a time you made the comfortable choice and knew it was protecting an excuse.
3. What is the difference between a brave choice and a dramatic choice?
4. Which of the three Brave Choice questions do clients resist most?
5. What does a 6:14 AM Tuesday choice look like in real life?
6. What is a decision people make at night that ruins the next day?
7. What is a decision people make in the morning that protects the next five years?

## Follow-Up Probes

- Where was the person?
- What was the comfortable option?
- What did they choose instead?
- What did it cost in the moment?
- What did it protect later?

---

# Prompt Set 5: The Triage Audit Prompts

Use these to make sales content feel safer and more specific.

1. What do you listen for in the first 30 minutes of a Triage Audit?
2. What is a phrase someone said on a call that told you the real issue?
3. What does it sound like when someone is not a fit?
4. What is the difference between selling a menu and recommending one path?
5. What does a good read-back sound like?
6. What do people misunderstand about the Triage Audit before they book?
7. What would make you tell someone not to hire you?

## Follow-Up Probes

- What did they say when you read it back?
- Which path did you recommend and why?
- What did you avoid saying too early?
- What proof story matched their pattern?
- What would have made the call feel like a pitch?

---

# Prompt Set 6: Instagram And Video Prompts

Use these when the output needs to be visual.

1. Show me one thing in the gym that represents the work this week.
2. What is a movement, setup, or coaching cue that would make a good 30-second video?
3. What is one client-safe moment you can describe without exposing them?
4. What object tells the story: kettlebell, notebook, barbell, coffee, shoes, travel bag?
5. What would you say to camera if a client told you they keep restarting?
6. What would you say to camera if someone says they are too busy to train?
7. What would you say to camera if someone says they are too old to rebuild?

## Follow-Up Probes

- Where should you be standing?
- What should be in the frame?
- What line opens the video?
- What is the one action the viewer should take?
- What should the caption add that the video does not say?

# Weekly Voice Memo Workflow

## Sunday

Cooz records one memo from Prompt Set 2 or 3.

Goal: practical or proof content for Monday/Wednesday.

## Tuesday

Cooz records one memo from Prompt Set 1 or 4.

Goal: emotional/belief content for Friday.

## Optional Thursday

Cooz records one quick video memo from Prompt Set 6.

Goal: Instagram Reel or YouTube cold open.


## Voice Filter

Keep:

- short, plain sentences
- real scenes
- slight humor when true
- direct diagnostic language
- "I" when Cooz actually observed it
- "you" only near the end

Cut:

- generic coaching advice
- technical language the buyer would not use
- inflated stakes
- therapy language
- bro-fitness language
- polished AI cadence
- repeated slogan endings

## The One Question Before Writing

Before turning a memo into content, answer:

> What does the reader get from this besides knowing Cooz is good?

If the answer is weak, do not write yet. Ask for one more detail.
