## PHASE 2: THE ABOUT SECTION

### Mobile-First Formatting Rules (Lara Acosta SLAY Overlay)
- First 3 lines = visible before "See More" on mobile
- Line 1 = HOOK (must create cliffhanger)
- Empty line after Line 1 (visual tension)
- Short paragraphs (2-3 lines max)
- Bullets for scanability
- ONE CTA at the end — no competing links
