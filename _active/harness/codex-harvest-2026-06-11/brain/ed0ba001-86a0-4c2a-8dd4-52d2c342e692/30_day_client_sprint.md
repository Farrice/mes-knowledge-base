## PHASE 3: COLD OUTREACH (Days 11-25)

### The Daily 60-Minute Block

Every morning. Non-negotiable. This is the work.

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| **0-15 min** | Find 4-5 prospects on LinkedIn. Look for: coaches, consultants, course creators with 500-5K followers who have expertise but bad content |
| **15-50 min** | Write 4-5 personalized outreach messages (15 min research per prospect) |
| **50-60 min** | Follow up on all previous contacts (5x cadence) |

### How to Find Cold Prospects

Search LinkedIn for:
- Coaches with "I help" in their headline but generic content
- Consultants with impressive backgrounds but 5-10 likes per post
- Course creators who have a Kajabi/Teachable but no LinkedIn strategy
- Anyone who posts motivational quotes or reshares articles (= no content system)

### The AI-Powered Free Custom Sample

This is your nuclear weapon. Most writers take 2 hours. You take 20 minutes. Here's the process:

1. **5 min** — Scan their LinkedIn (last 10 posts, about section, headline, featured)
2. **5 min** — Feed their voice/content into Antigravity: "Analyze this person's voice, expertise, and positioning gaps"
3. **10 min** — Generate ONE killer post in their voice, on a topic they should be talking about but aren't
4. **Send with this message:**

> Hey [Name] — I've been following your work on [specific thing they do]. You clearly know your stuff.
>
> I actually wrote a LinkedIn post for you as an example of what I do. It's based on your voice and expertise. No strings — if it resonates, I'd love to talk about doing more.
>
> [Attach or paste the post]
>
> I help experts like you build content systems that attract clients. I use AI to deliver in days what takes most strategists weeks. Want to jump on a 15-min call?

**Volume: 2-3 custom samples/day. 10-15/week.**

At a 20% response rate and 10% close rate, 15 samples = 1-2 clients.

### Pitch-in-Public: The CMO-for-a-Day Post

Once per week, do a deep audit of a prospect and publish it:

**Title:** *"If I ran [type of expert]'s LinkedIn for 30 days, here's what I'd change..."*

**Structure:**
1. What they're doing now (specific observations, never mean)
2. What's missing (content strategy, hook architecture, positioning clarity)
3. What you'd build (content system, posting cadence, voice extraction)
4. Results you'd expect (based on your experience + industry benchmarks)

**End with:** *"If your LinkedIn looks like this and you want help, that's literally what I do. DM me for a free 15-min audit."*

## PHASE 4: CLOSE (Days 26-30+)

### The Sales Call Talk Track

When prospects say yes to a call, here's the play:

**1. Open with curiosity (first 2 min):**
> "Tell me what you're trying to accomplish with your LinkedIn. What would 'winning' look like for you?"

**2. Listen. Don't interrupt. Take notes. (2-3 min)**

**3. Play back their problem with MORE precision (2 min):**
> "So what I'm hearing is you know your stuff, but your content isn't doing the heavy lifting of attracting clients. And what that means is you're spending time on sales calls with people who aren't pre-sold — or worse, you're not getting enough conversations at all. The real cost isn't just the revenue — it's the frustration of being an expert who feels invisible."

**4. Add problems they didn't mention (2 min):**
> "I also noticed [specific thing from their LinkedIn — bad headline, no hook architecture, inconsistent posting, generic content]. Most [their type] don't realize that's actually costing them 50-100 profile views per week that should be turning into DMs."

**5. The soft close (1 min):**
> "Would it be helpful if I showed you what solving this would look like?"

**6. If they ask for proof you don't have — The NDA Deflection:**
> "The content work I do is typically confidential — my clients' audiences think MY writing is THEIRS. That's the point. But I can show you my own LinkedIn, where I practice everything first. You're not the experiment — I am."

## PSYCHOLOGY MANAGEMENT

### The Idiot-Genius Roller Coaster

You WILL experience this pattern:
- **Days 1-3**: Excitement. "This is it."
- **Days 4-7**: First silence. No responses. "This doesn't work for me."
- **Days 8-14**: First conversation or two. "Maybe this works?"
- **Days 15-20**: Either a win (genius!) or sustained silence (idiot!).
- **Days 21-30**: Momentum builds. Or you quit.

**The truth:** Same actions → same results. Only your psychology oscillates. The negative patterns you described — "all my negative patterns are firing, all that wants to keep me from this" — that's the Idiot phase. It's not a signal to change paths. It's a signal you're doing the right thing and the resistance is proportional to the reward.

**Your mantra (borrow from Cole):** *"I haven't built the skill of managing that voice yet. But I manage it by doing the work anyway."*

### The Jun Yuh Constraint

You don't need to produce like Jun Yuh. You need his ETHOS:
- **Designed simplicity**: One service. One platform. One daily block.
- **Maximum impact**: Every action produces something (a post, an outreach, a sample). Zero busywork.
- **Minimum input**: AI does the heavy lifting. You direct, refine, and connect. 

You're not building a content machine. You're running a scalpel.
