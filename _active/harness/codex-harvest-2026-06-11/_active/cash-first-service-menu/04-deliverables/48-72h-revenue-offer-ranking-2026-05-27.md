## Executive Verdict

[inference] The highest-probability path to 1,000 dollars in the next 48-72 hours is **not** a pure digital product, marketplace skill, or broad AI consulting offer.

[inference] The recommended path is a **service-first proof sprint** that can become a digital product later:

```text
Sell one tight diagnostic or implementation promise.
Use the delivery as proof.
Package the repeated parts afterward.
```

My strongest recommendation is to lead with:

```text
Buyer Signal Brief
```

This is the low-education version of Vibe Tax. The public promise is not "Vibe Tax" first. The buyer-facing promise is:

```text
Send me one offer, page, post, outreach message, or launch decision. I will show you what signal to trust, what signal to distrust, and what to change before you rebuild or spend more time.
```

Vibe Tax remains the category/pain name underneath it. Guessproof remains the backend implementation when the false signal is caused by a recurring AI/workflow failure.

## The Three I Would Actually Try First

### 1. Buyer Signal Brief

[inference] This is the strongest current front-door candidate because the market already buys research, competitor analysis, message testing, positioning, and landing page feedback. You do not need to educate them on "Vibe Tax" at the first touch.

Use the name like this:

```text
Buyer Signal Brief
Powered by the Vibe Tax diagnostic
```

Price:

- 300 dollars founding if you want speed.
- 500 dollars if the buyer has a live offer, launch, page, or expensive decision.
- 750 dollars only after one proof demo or paid win.

Deliverable:

- one asset or decision reviewed,
- buyer-language map,
- false-signal diagnosis,
- competitor/substitute frame,
- proof gap,
- hidden objection,
- keep/revise/stop/test verdict,
- one revised asset or next sales angle.

Why it can get to 1,000 dollars:

- three 300 dollar briefs plus one 100 dollar express audit,
- two 500 dollar briefs,
- one 500 dollar brief plus one 500 dollar implementation follow-up.

### 2. AI Workflow Misfire Teardown / Guessproof Rescue

This is the strongest backend because the buyer's pain is concrete: they are already using AI, but still rescuing the output by hand.

Price:

- 97 dollars for a tiny same-day teardown,
- 250 dollars for a Misfire Map,
- 500-750 dollars for a 48-hour Guessproof Workflow Rescue.

The key is not to sell a skill package. Sell:

```text
Send me the workflow that still needs your rescue.
I will show where AI is guessing and patch the weakest instruction layer.
```

This ladders into skill/plugin products only after the same problem repeats.

### 3. AI Workflow Integration Sprint

This is the clearest implementation offer. It is less poetic than Vibe Tax, but buyers instantly understand it.

Price:

Best examples:

## What You Might Be Missing

### 1. The research package is not "research." It is decision insurance.

Market research is already bought. Message testing is already bought. Competitor analysis is already bought. The Vibe Tax language makes it sharper, but the buyer should first hear a familiar promise:

```text
Before you rebuild the offer, I will tell you which signal is lying.
```

### 2. Your real edge is the receipt.

Most people can write a prompt, make a page, or produce a report. Your system can produce:

- source ledger,
- claim labels,
- before/after,
- quality gate,
- routing receipt,
- proof demo,
- reusable workflow,
- next validation test.

That is what lets you charge service prices instead of prompt-pack prices.

### 3. The marketplace skill idea is valid, but not for the first 1,000 dollars.

Agensi and SkillHQ prove a market exists. They also prove the low-ticket reality. The skill marketplace is a compounding asset after you discover which workflow people pay to repair.

### 4. The easiest buyers are not "AI people."

The easiest buyers are service owners with a visible asset and a live decision:

- consultants,
- small agencies,
- coaches,
- fractional operators,
- B2B founders,
- local service businesses with weak follow-up,
- creators with offers,
- AI-heavy operators with messy workflows.

### 5. A landing page is useful after proof, not before it.

For the next 48-72 hours, a one-page offer card plus direct asks is better than a polished page. A landing page can become useful once you know which offer produces asset submissions or paid questions.

## 48-72 Hour Action Plan

## Day 1: Pick One Front Door

Use this:

```text
I am testing a 48-hour Buyer Signal Brief.

Send me one offer, page, post, outreach message, or launch decision you are currently trusting.

I will map the first signal I would distrust, the hidden objection, the proof gap, and what I would keep, revise, stop, or test before you rebuild.

Founding price is 300 dollars for the first few.
```

If the person sends an AI workflow instead of a buyer/offer asset, route it to Guessproof:

```text
This looks less like a buyer-signal issue and more like a workflow-rescue issue. I can run the Guessproof version instead: where AI is guessing, what judgment is missing, and how to patch the repeatable workflow.
```

## Day 2: Send Ten Manual Asks

Prioritize:

| Prospect Type | Offer |
|---|---|
| warm consultant, coach, agency owner, creator | Buyer Signal Brief |
| AI-heavy friend/operator | Guessproof Teardown |
| service owner with repeated admin/sales task | AI Workflow Integration Sprint |
| person with visible weak page or profile | Trust Leak Audit |
| local business with stale follow-up | Email Follow-Up Rescue |

Do not send a menu. Send one offer per person.

## Day 3: Convert Response Into Proof

Every response becomes one of these:

| Response | Action |
|---|---|
| They send an asset | run a short diagnostic and ask for paid brief if the signal is real. |
| They ask what they get | send the deliverable list and price. |
| They say they need execution | route to Guessproof or AI Workflow Sprint. |
| They like the phrase but send nothing | treat as weak signal. |
| They want a digital product | offer the paid service first and promise a kit after proof. |

For the next 48-72 hours, do this:

```text
Primary: Buyer Signal Brief at 300-500 dollars.
Backend: Guessproof Rescue at 500-750 dollars when the pain is workflow failure.
```
