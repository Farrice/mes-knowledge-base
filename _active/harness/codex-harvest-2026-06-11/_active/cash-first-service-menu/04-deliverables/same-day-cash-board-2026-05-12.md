## Payment Rail Rules

Use these in order:

1. **Known or trusted buyer:** Zelle first. Zelle says enrolled users typically receive funds within minutes, directly in bank, but it should be used only with people you trust ([Zelle](https://www.zelle.com/faq/how-long-does-it-take-receive-money-zelle)).
2. **Known buyer or app-native buyer:** Cash App, Venmo, or PayPal only if the transfer is visible inside the app and instant transfer to bank is available ([Cash App](https://cash.app/learn/getting-started/how-to-send-and-receive-money), [Venmo](https://help.venmo.com/cs/articles/instant-bank-transfer-faq-vhel302), [PayPal](https://securepayments.paypal.com/us/digital-wallet/paypal-consumer-fees)).
3. **Stranger or weak-trust buyer:** avoid Zelle as the primary rail. Use a written scope, business payment path, and assume possible holds or chargeback risk ([PayPal holds](https://www.paypal.com/us/brc/article/funds-availability)).
4. **Do not count money as "today" until it is visible in the bank or spendable wallet.** Screenshots, emails, and pending notices do not count.

Source anchors:

- Zelle: enrolled recipients typically receive funds within minutes; Zelle also says payments to enrolled recipients cannot be reversed.
- Venmo: instant transfers usually go to eligible U.S. bank accounts or debit cards within 30 minutes, but reviews can delay, decline, or block transfers.
- PayPal: instant withdrawals charge 1.75%, but new sellers or risky transactions can be held ([fees](https://securepayments.paypal.com/us/digital-wallet/paypal-consumer-fees), [holds](https://www.paypal.com/us/brc/article/funds-availability)).
- Stripe: Instant Payouts can arrive within minutes for eligible accounts, but new Stripe users are not immediately eligible and first payout can take 7-14 days.
- Uber: Instant Pay can cash out up to 6 times daily after first trip for most U.S. drivers/delivery people, but bank delays can happen.
- DoorDash: Fast Pay and Crimson can be fast, but both require eligibility and approval.
- Upwork: fixed-price and bonuses clear after security periods; hourly work is usually not same-day.
- Fiverr: funds generally withdraw only after order completion and clearing.
- Etsy/Gumroad/Shopify: useful for productization, not a same-day bank plan for new sellers.
