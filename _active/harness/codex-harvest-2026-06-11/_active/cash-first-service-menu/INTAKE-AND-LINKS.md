## Universal Intake

Ask:

1. What are you selling?
2. Who is the buyer?
3. What are you trying to make easier or clearer?
4. What have you already tried?
5. What does a useful first version need to do?
6. What should I avoid?
7. What links/files/examples should I review?
8. What is the desired turnaround?

## Offer-Specific Intake

### AI Workflow Integration Sprint

- What repeated task should we fix first?
- Where does the task start?
- What output do you need?
- What tools are involved?
- Give me one example input.
- Give me one example of a good output.
- Who uses the workflow after delivery?

### Lead List + Outreach Starter Pack

- What offer are you selling?
- What buyer has paid or shown interest before?
- Who is a bad fit?
- What geography, niche, or platform matters?
- What proof can you mention?
- What is the next step you want a prospect to take?

### The Vibe Tax Brief

- What offer or idea are we testing?
- Who do you think the buyer is?
- What competitors or alternatives do they consider?
- What objections have you heard?
- What price point are you considering?
- What decision do you need the brief to support?
- What signals are you currently trusting?
- What signal might be misleading you?

## Risk Boundary

Do not accept:

- "Can you just look for free?"
- "Can you guarantee leads?"
- "Can you automate all my outreach?"
- "Can you log into everything before I pay?"
- "Can you build my whole AI system?"

Use the small paid starter project to create trust first.
