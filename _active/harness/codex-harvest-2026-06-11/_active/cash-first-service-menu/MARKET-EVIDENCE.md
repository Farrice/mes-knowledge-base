# Market Evidence - Cash-First Service Menu

Use this file to keep the offers grounded in current buyer demand instead of internal excitement.

## Evidence Anchors

| Source | Signal | Offer implication |
|---|---|---|
| Upwork 2026 In-Demand Skills | AI-enabled freelance skills grew quickly; AI integration, AI video, chatbot development, data work, social media strategy, lead generation, and marketing automation remain active categories. | Lead with implementation, workflow repair, AI-assisted delivery, and marketplace proposals. |
| Goldman Sachs 2026 Small Business AI Survey | Small businesses report AI benefits, but many still lack full integration, training, tool selection confidence, and technical support. | Sell small AI workflow installation, not abstract AI consulting. |
| Fiverr 2025 Business Trends Index | Searches rose for AI automation, AI video, faceless video, prompt engineering, social media, and platform-specific creative support. | Keep a creative/video lane, but package it as direction + usable assets, not tech demos. |
| Constant Contact 2025 Small Business Marketing Report | SMBs are using AI, video, and email but confidence in marketing effectiveness is low; many have little time and do not know what is working. | Sell follow-up, research, and simple marketing workflow fixes that reduce uncertainty. |
| MBO 2025 State of Independence | Independent workers use AI and platforms heavily; independent work and creator activity remain mainstream. | Use platforms and cold-qualified outreach as channels; do not rely on family or warm network validation. |

## Practical Read

The strongest buyer pain is not "I need AI."

It is:

- I know AI matters, but I cannot connect it to my work.
- I am doing more marketing and still do not know what is working.
- I need prospects and a first-message angle.
- I need faster research before I build the wrong offer.
- I need a simple workflow, not another strategy call.

## Claims Guardrail

Do not promise:

- more leads
- revenue growth
- viral content
- full automation
- replacement of team members
- guaranteed appointments
- guaranteed inbox replies

Do promise:

- a clear map
- a working first version
- a sharper list
- a specific set of buyer observations
- a tighter follow-up path
- a handoff someone can use tomorrow
