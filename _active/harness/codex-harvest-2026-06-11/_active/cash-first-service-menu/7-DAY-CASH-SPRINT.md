# 7-Day Cash Sprint

> Goal: first serious reply, checkout request, paid project, or implementation ask.
> Time budget: 1-4 hours/day.
> Rule: validate by buyer behavior, not internal confidence.

## Day 0 - Setup Lock

### Required Outputs

- choose the primary offer for the next 72 hours
- create or stage checkout link
- create one intake form or simple intake doc
- fill `TRACKER.md` with starting counts
- pick one buyer segment
- prepare the first five manual asks

### Stop Condition

No outreach until the buyer can say yes to a concrete next step.

## Day 1 - Workflow Proof + First Asks

### Build

Use `PROOF-DEMO-1-WORKFLOW-MAP.md` as the public/private proof sample for the AI Workflow Integration Sprint.

### Sell

- identify 10 prospects
- send 5 specific asks
- apply to 2 marketplace listings if available
- record every buyer phrase in `TRACKER.md`

### Stop Condition

Five specific asks are sent, not drafted.

## Day 2 - Lead Pack Proof

### Build

Use `PROOF-DEMO-2-LEAD-PACK.md` as the proof sample for Lead List + Outreach Starter Pack.

### Sell

- identify 10 more prospects
- send 5-10 specific asks
- follow up on Day 1 threads
- make one public/process post only if the copy passes `COPY-GATE-RESULT.md`

### Stop Condition

At least 10 total asks are logged.

## Day 3 - Vibe Tax Brief Proof

### Build

Use `_active/research-intelligence-entry-point/SAMPLE-BRIEF.md` and `LEAD-MAGNET.md` as the proof sample for The Vibe Tax Brief.

### Sell

- send the brief sample to prospects who ask "what does this look like?"
- send 5-10 new asks
- apply to 2-3 high-fit marketplace listings with `/draft-proposal`

### Review

Ask:

- which offer got the clearest replies?
- which offer was easiest to explain?
- which offer fits 48-hour delivery?
- which buyer phrase repeated?

## Day 4 - Tighten The Winner

- keep the best-performing offer as the main ask for the next 72 hours
- revise the worst opener
- create one smaller paid yes if the price is creating friction
- send 10 targeted asks
- follow up on every active thread

## Day 5 - Marketplace Push

- search for jobs around AI integration, lead generation, market research, email marketing, AI chatbot setup, and automation
- run `/draft-proposal` for each high-fit listing
- submit 3-5 proposals manually
- log platform, proposal angle, and reply status

## Day 6 - Delivery Or Diagnosis

If paid:

- fulfill using `DELIVERY-SOP.md`
- capture before/after proof
- ask for a quote
- offer the next small implementation step only after value is delivered

If not paid:

- identify the failure point: buyer, price, promise, proof, channel, or ask
- revise exactly one variable
- do not rebuild the whole system

## Day 7 - Decision Review

### Keep If

- one paid start
- checkout request
- serious sales conversation
- multiple prospects ask for examples
- repeated buyer phrase confirms pain

### Revise If

- people understand but do not pay
- replies cluster around a different scope
- buyers want a smaller first step

### Stop If

- 50 manual asks/proposals produce no serious replies
- delivery cannot fit inside 1-4 hours/day
- the only interest is unpaid advice or free audits
