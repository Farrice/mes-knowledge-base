# Proof Demo 3 - Research Intelligence Brief

## Demo Topic

AI workflow integration for solo B2B service operators.

## Buyer Snapshot

The buyer is a solo or small-team operator who:

- sells expertise, services, coaching, consulting, or implementation
- has repeatable admin, sales, marketing, or delivery tasks
- has tried ChatGPT or Claude
- still does not have AI embedded into daily work
- wants speed and clarity without a large transformation project

## Market Problem

The pain is not lack of AI awareness. The pain is the implementation gap:

```text
They have AI tools, but the tools are not connected to the work that creates money, saves time, or improves client delivery.
```

## Buyer Words To Listen For

- "I know I should be using AI more."
- "I tried ChatGPT, but I do not know where it fits."
- "I still rewrite everything."
- "My follow-up is inconsistent."
- "I need a simple system."
- "I do not need a huge automation project."
- "I just want one thing working."

## Status Quo Alternatives

| Alternative | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| buying another AI tool | tool does not install the workflow |
| watching tutorials | creates knowledge but not implementation |
| hiring an agency | too big, expensive, or slow for one task |
| asking a VA | can help with tasks but may not design the system |
| doing nothing | repeated task keeps leaking time or revenue |

## Offer Angles

1. **One Workflow, Not AI Transformation**
   - "Pick one repeated task. I turn it into a usable AI-assisted workflow in 48 hours."
2. **Stop Rewriting From Scratch**
   - "I turn your repeatable inputs into prompts, SOPs, and first drafts you can trust."
3. **Install The Missing Middle**
   - "You already have AI tools. I build the bridge between the tool and the task."
4. **Post-Call Follow-Up Rescue**
   - "Turn discovery call notes into follow-up, proposal angles, and reminders."
5. **Client Delivery Shortcut**
   - "Turn messy client inputs into summaries, action plans, and next-step checklists."

## Best First Paid Step

Sell the **$500 AI Workflow Integration Sprint** first.

Only sell deeper setup after the buyer has used the first workflow and can point to time saved, clarity gained, or implementation demand.

## Validation Plan

### Test Audience

- consultants
- agencies
- fractional operators
- coaches with high-ticket offers
- small service businesses with inquiry/follow-up workflows

### Test Ask

```text
Is there one repeated task in your business that still lives in your head, inbox, or notes even though AI should probably help with it?
```

### Keep Signal

- buyer names a specific task
- buyer asks what setup looks like
- buyer asks about price or timeline
- buyer sends example input
- buyer says "I need this for [specific process]"

### Revise Signal

- buyer says it sounds too broad
- buyer asks for examples
- buyer thinks it is prompt coaching only
- buyer wants done-for-you delivery instead of workflow setup

### Stop Signal

- buyers only want free advice
- buyers want full custom software
- buyers cannot name a repeated task
- delivery would require sensitive account access before trust exists
