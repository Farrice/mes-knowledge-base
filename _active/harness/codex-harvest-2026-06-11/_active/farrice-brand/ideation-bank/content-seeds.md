## Seed Bank

### Parenting as Ethical Persuasion

Source: [2026-05-05 - Dad, Marketing, and Cheap Dopamine](captures/2026-05-05-dad-marketing-cheap-dopamine.md)

Core idea: Parenting shows the difference between manipulation and ethical influence. The tactic is not the moral issue. The intent, awareness, and outcome are.

Best formats:

- LinkedIn story post
- Substack essay
- Short-form video monologue
- Offer philosophy section for creative strategy or AI consulting

Hooks:

- "I just manipulated my two-year-old son, and I don't know how to feel about it."
- "Parenting is the most uncomfortable marketing class I've ever taken."
- "The line between manipulation and empathy is thinner than most marketers want to admit."
- "My toddler taught me why most marketing sounds fake."
- "If you won't say it the way they need to hear it, you may not care as much as you think."

### Cheap Dopamine and the Cost of Screens

Source: [2026-05-05 - Dad, Marketing, and Cheap Dopamine](captures/2026-05-05-dad-marketing-cheap-dopamine.md)

Core idea: Screens are not just a parenting shortcut. They reveal the deeper cost of cheap dopamine and the numbness adults normalize until a child makes it visible.

Best formats:

- Personal essay
- "Letters to JJ" entry
- LinkedIn post about attention, creativity, and family-first work
- Prompt pack or reflection exercise about attention

Hooks:

- "I thought I understood cheap dopamine until I saw what it did to my son."
- "Adults are numb enough to call distraction normal. Toddlers make the cost obvious."
- "The hardest creative discipline in my life right now is being present while my son is awake."
- "I cannot keep calling it sacrifice if I'm outsourcing presence to a screen."
