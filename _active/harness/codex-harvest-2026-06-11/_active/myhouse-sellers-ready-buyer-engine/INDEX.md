## Project Purpose

This project turns Jen/Jiing's real estate growth goal into a client-facing AI-assisted business engine: fewer low-quality conversations, more qualified buyer and seller consults, clearer market authority, and a path to protect family time while building toward consistent $300K+ annual income.

The core proof point is not "AI makes content." The proof point is that one listing, one Zillow-style lead, and one Instagram comment can become:

- a buyer brief
- a lead score
- a next-best action
- a Jen-voice content asset
- a DM qualification flow
- a team handoff
- a proof metric

## Core Positioning

**The Grown-Up First Home Files**

For ready SFV and greater LA buyers with real income, real constraints, and no patience for realtor theater.

Jen's role: warm friend, serious advisor, and MyHouseSellers operator who helps people see whether buying is realistic before they waste weekends or emotional energy.

## Second-Phase Moat

**Artist Relocation Concierge**

EDM, creative, self-employed, touring, and event-industry clients are treated as a future luxury/referral lane. This lane stays tasteful and strategic until the qualified-buyer engine is producing proof.

## Proof Standard

The package is successful if Jen can recognize:

> This saves me time, helps me find serious people, and makes my expertise visible without making me sound like a generic agent.

## Guardrails

- No income guarantees.
- No protected-class targeting.
- No financial, legal, tax, lending, or buyer-agreement advice without the right professional review.
- No external publishing, paid ads, CRM changes, or live automation without Jen and broker approval.
- AI scores stated intent and behavior only; it does not infer protected traits.
