# MyHouseSellers Ready Buyer Engine

## The Big Shift

The opportunity is not to post more.

The opportunity is to build a repeatable system that helps Jen/Jiing find serious buyers and sellers earlier, filter out low-readiness conversations faster, and make her expertise visible before a client asks for a showing.

Most real estate marketing still treats first-time buyers like they are young, simple, and excited. The current buyer is different. They are often older, have been renting for years, are trying to understand payments, down payment programs, family help, buyer agreements, and whether buying in SFV or greater LA is even realistic.

That means the winning agent is not the loudest agent. It is the agent who can calmly answer:

> Based on your income, savings, timeline, target area, and the programs available, is the next 90 days realistic or should we build a smarter path?

That is the wedge.

## The Positioning

**The Grown-Up First Home Files**

For ready SFV and greater LA buyers with real income, real responsibilities, and no patience for realtor theater.

Jen becomes the agent who helps buyers understand the math before they fall in love with the house, and helps sellers see that MyHouseSellers can bring serious, prepared buyers into the process.

## The Visible AI Engine

The Ready Buyer Engine turns raw signals into clear next actions.

| Raw Signal | Engine Output | Human Action |
|---|---|---|
| A listing around $800K-$900K | Buyer fit brief, Reel hooks, seller proof angle, open-house DM flow | Jen records one warm, specific content batch. |
| A Zillow-style lead | Lead score, concern summary, next question, booking route | Jen or team responds with the right level of attention. |
| An Instagram comment like `READY` | DM reply, email capture, intake questions, score | The lead either books, nurtures, or gets routed to lender education. |
| A seller inquiry | Seller readiness brief, equity/timeline questions, consult prep | Jen enters the listing conversation with a clear plan. |
| A past client/referral | referral context, likely need, follow-up path | Jen reconnects without sounding scripted. |

AI does not replace Jen. It protects her attention, remembers the process, keeps the voice warm, and makes sure every lead gets the right next step.

## The 2-Minute Demo

Show Jen three cards.

### Card 1: Listing To Leads

Input: "Canoga Park townhome, $875K, 3 beds, updated kitchen, close to Warner Center, realistic first-home alternative for buyers priced out of Sherman Oaks."

Output:

- Buyer angle: "If Sherman Oaks feels impossible, this is the kind of Valley tradeoff worth understanding."
- Reel hook: "Before I let a first-time buyer fall in love with an $875K home, I check these five things."
- Seller angle: "This is how we position an entry-luxury listing to serious buyers, not weekend browsers."
- CTA: "Comment `MAP` for the Valley Match Map."

### Card 2: Zillow-Style Lead

Input: "Looking in Woodland Hills or Canoga Park. Budget maybe $850K. Currently renting. Wants to tour this weekend. Not pre-approved yet."

Output:

- Score: 58/100
- Route: nurture plus lender intro before any private tour
- Next question: "Have you already spoken with a lender, or would it help to compare what $850K looks like monthly before we pick houses?"
- Team handoff: lender readiness first, Jen consult if timeline and finances sharpen

### Card 3: Instagram Comment

Input: Comment `READY` on a Reel about buying in the Valley.

Output:

- DM: "I can send you the First Home Readiness Scorecard. It starts with numbers, not pressure. What price range are you trying to understand?"
- Intake path: timeline, area, rent/payment comfort, savings, preapproval status, decision-makers
- Route: book within 72 hours if score is 75+, nurture if 55-74, education only if below 55

## The Business Math

Using an $850K average price and 2.5% gross commission assumption, one closed client transaction is about $21,250 GCI before split, expenses, taxes, referral fees, and brokerage details.

| Pace | Annual Closings | Approx. Annual GCI | Strategic Meaning |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Conservative | 24 | $510,000 | 2 closings/month, likely enough to support $300K retained income only if split/expenses are favorable. |
| Target | 30 | $637,500 | 2.5 closings/month, better cushion for seasonality and expenses. |
| Upside | 36 | $765,000 | 3 closings/month, creates room for reinvestment, support, and proof-to-network. |

The goal is not just more leads. The goal is enough qualified conversations to support 2-3 closings/month without turning Jen's phone into the business.

## The Operating Promise

This pilot is designed around five outcomes:

1. **Better lead quality:** every CTA routes into a score, consult, nurture, or education path.
2. **Less chasing:** follow-up becomes scheduled, scored, and team-supported.
3. **More visible expertise:** content explains math, tradeoffs, programs, buyer agreements, and seller confidence.
4. **Protected capacity:** Jen's week has review queues, consult windows, and exception rules.
5. **Proof for scale:** after 14 days, the dashboard shows leading indicators that can become a MyHouseSellers case study.

## What This Does Not Promise

- It does not guarantee $300K income.
- It does not guarantee 2-3 deals/month.
- It does not make Zillow leads magically high-quality.
- It does not replace Jen's judgment, service, or relationships.
- It does not publish paid ads or financial claims without review.

## What It Can Prove Quickly

Within 14 days, the pilot can prove whether:

- the message makes Jen feel seen instead of boxed in
