# DM Intake Flow

## Purpose

The DM flow turns comments and casual inquiries into scored, routed next steps. It is designed to help Jen/Jiing respond warmly without giving high-touch time to low-readiness leads.

This can be implemented manually first. ManyChat or CRM automation should only be connected after Jen and broker approval.

## Keyword Map

| Keyword | Lead Magnet | Primary Route |
|---|---|---|
| `READY` | First Home Readiness Scorecard | buyer readiness score |
| `MAP` | Valley Match Map | neighborhood tradeoff nurture |
| `OFFER` | Offer-Ready Weekend Kit | buyer consult or offer prep |
| `SELL` | Seller Trust File | seller readiness score |
| `SOUNDCHECK` | Home Soundcheck Checklist | creative/EDM lifestyle bridge |

## Universal First Reply

Use this for manual DMs or as the first automation draft:

```text
I can send that over. Quick note: I use this to help people figure out whether the next 90 days are realistic, not to pressure anyone into touring.

What price range or neighborhood are you trying to understand right now?
```

## Buyer Intake Questions

Ask only enough to route the lead. Do not make it feel like a mortgage application.

1. What area are you trying to understand?
2. What price range feels realistic or confusing?
3. Are you hoping to buy in the next 90 days, 3-6 months, or later?
4. Have you spoken with a lender yet?
5. What is the main thing making buying feel uncertain right now?
6. Is anyone else part of the decision?

## Buyer Score Logic

| Answer Signal | Score Impact |
|---|---:|
| 0-90 day timeline | +20 |
| clear motivation | +20 |
| preapproval or lender willingness | +15 |
| target area and price fit | +15 |
| decision-makers identified | +10 |
| thoughtful replies or referral source | +10 |
| responsive within 24 hours | +10 |

## Buyer Route Copy

### 75+ Score: Book

```text
This sounds real enough that guessing from listings will probably waste your time.

The best next step is a short Buyer Strategy Session. We will look at your target area, payment comfort, timeline, and what sellers need to see before you spend a weekend touring.

I have [two time options]. Want one of those?
```

### 55-74 Score: Nurture Plus Partner

```text
You are not far off, but I would want the numbers clearer before we tour.

I can send the readiness scorecard and, if you want, connect you with a lender who can show what this price range actually looks like monthly. No pressure either way. The goal is clarity first.
```

### Below 55: Education Only

```text
I would start with the guide first. It will help you see what needs to be true before the search gets serious.

Save it, read through it, and if your timeline tightens or you want to check the numbers, reply READY and I will help you map the next step.
```

## Seller Intake Questions

1. What is the property address or neighborhood?
2. Are you thinking 0-90 days, 3-6 months, or just exploring?
3. What is making you consider selling?
4. Are there any known repairs, tenant issues, inherited-property issues, or timing constraints?
5. Would you need to buy before or after selling?
6. Who else needs to be part of the decision?

## Seller Route Copy

### Seller Ready

```text
That is worth a real look.

Before we talk price, I would want to understand timing, prep, buyer quality, and your move plan. I can send the Seller Trust File so you can see how our team thinks through the process before you choose an agent.

Want me to send it here?
```

### Seller Exploring

```text
That makes sense. If you are not ready to list yet, I would start with a seller readiness check instead of a listing pitch.

It looks at timing, prep, likely buyer questions, and what would need to be true for the move to feel calm.
```

## Follow-Up Rules

| Situation | Follow-Up |
|---|---|
| Lead asks for guide but does not answer question | Send guide, ask one question 24 hours later. |
| Lead answers but is not ready | Add to nurture, send one useful asset weekly for 4 weeks. |
| Lead is 75+ and does not book | Follow up within 24 hours with value restatement, not "just checking in." |
| Lead books consult | Send prep note and calendar invite immediately. |
| Lead no-shows | One warm reset, then nurture unless urgent motivation remains. |
| Lead asks legal/lending/tax questions | Route to qualified partner; do not answer beyond general education. |

## 72-Hour Booking Confirmation

```text
You are set for [day/time].

The goal of this call is simple: we will look at whether your target area, price range, timeline, and next steps actually fit. If it is not the right time, I will tell you that too and give you the path.

Before we meet, send me:
1. target area
2. rough price range
3. current rent or payment comfort
4. lender/preapproval status
5. biggest concern
```

## Nurture Path

| Week | Buyer Nurture Asset | Seller Nurture Asset |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | First Home Readiness Scorecard | Seller Trust File |
| 2 | Valley Match Map | Pre-List Room checklist |
| 3 | Offer-Ready Weekend Kit | Showing Quality Report explainer |
| 4 | Buyer agreement explainer, broker-reviewed | Offer Stack explainer |

## Human Review Queue

Every weekday, review:

- new keyword comments
- unanswered DMs
- leads over 75
- 55-74 leads needing partner route
- seller inquiries
- booked consult confirmations
- no-show risk
- stale hot leads

Jen should only personally handle:

- high-trust consult invites
- complex seller conversations
- offer/listing strategy
- sensitive client moments
- voice-note replies where warmth matters

Team or automation can handle:

- guide delivery
- intake question collection
- calendar links
- reminder tasks
- nurture sends
- partner introductions, once approved

## Compliance Guard

- Do not ask or infer protected-class information.
- Do not use age, family status, race, religion, national origin, sex, disability, or other protected-class traits for targeting or routing.
- Do not provide legal, lending, or tax advice.
- Do not state that a buyer qualifies for a program.
- Do not use private lead details in public content.
- Keep buyer-agreement and compensation language broker-approved.
