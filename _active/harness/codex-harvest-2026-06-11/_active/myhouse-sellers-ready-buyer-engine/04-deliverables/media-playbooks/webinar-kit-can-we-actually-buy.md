## Buyer Promise

Buying has not become impossible for every serious buyer. It has become more exacting.

This workshop helps buyers understand what needs to be true before touring makes sense: payment comfort, cash-to-close, financing route, co-buyer structure, family gift documentation, area tradeoffs, and decision timing.

## Who This Is For

- Buyers who have been renting for years and are tired of feeling behind.
- Buyers targeting SFV or greater LA and trying to understand the real tradeoffs.
- Buyers with a real timeline, income, savings, gift support, co-buyer plan, or lender conversation in motion.
- Buyers who want clarity before they start giving up Saturdays to homes that may not fit.

## Who This Is Not For

- People who only want a list of homes with no readiness conversation.
- Buyers who are not willing to look at the numbers.
- Anyone looking for guaranteed approval, guaranteed affordability, or financial/legal advice from a real estate webinar.

## Slide Flow

### Slide 1: Can We Actually Buy in the Valley?

Copy: If you make real money and still feel priced out, you are not imagining the pressure. The path is narrower now, which means the prep has to be better.

### Slide 2: The First-Time Buyer Is Not Who Agents Keep Marketing To

Copy: Many first-time buyers are not "just starting out." They are experienced adults with longer rental histories, real obligations, and more complicated financing questions.

Claim label: Source-backed directional claim. Use NAR first-time buyer profile for current median-age/share language.

### Slide 3: The Wrong Starting Point

Copy: Starting with "what homes do you like?" feels fun for five minutes. Then payment, cash-to-close, area tradeoffs, and loan terms walk into the room.

### Slide 4: The Better Starting Point

Copy: Before touring, answer four questions: What monthly payment is actually comfortable? What cash is available? Who is part of the decision? What tradeoffs are acceptable?

### Slide 5: The $800K-$900K Reality Check

Copy: This price band can be serious and still tight. The goal is not to make the number look pretty. The goal is to know where it works, where it breaks, and what needs review.

Review label: Lender review required for any example payments, rates, loan assumptions, or cash-to-close scenarios.

### Slide 6: Gift Money Is Not A Casual Detail

Copy: Family help can be powerful, but it needs to be documented correctly. The earlier the conversation happens, the fewer surprises show up later.

Review label: Lender review required.

### Slide 7: Co-Buying Is A Real Conversation

Copy: Buying with a parent, sibling, partner, or friend can open a door, but the exit plan matters as much as the purchase plan.

Review label: Legal/lender review required.

### Slide 8: The Valley Match Map

Copy: Neighborhood fit is more than price. Commute, noise, parking, HOA rules, family needs, resale path, and lifestyle all change the right search.

### Slide 9: The Offer-Ready Weekend

Copy: A strong weekend starts before the showing. Payment comfort, lender clarity, decision authority, and must-have tradeoffs should already be on the table.

### Slide 10: What Jen Looks For Before A Consult

Copy: Timeline, financing progress, decision authority, price and geography fit, motivation, responsiveness, and willingness to look at the math.

### Slide 11: Your Next Step

Copy: If you are ready, complete the scorecard. If you need the map, request MAP. If you are close to touring, request OFFER. If you need education, stay in the nurture path.

### Slide 12: Clarity First. Touring Second.

Copy: The goal is not to rush you into a house. The goal is to stop guessing and find the path that is honest enough to act on.
