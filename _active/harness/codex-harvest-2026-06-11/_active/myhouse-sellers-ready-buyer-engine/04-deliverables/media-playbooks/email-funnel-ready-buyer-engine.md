# Email Funnel: Ready Buyer Engine

## Asset Status

Ready to review. Not live. All finance, program, buyer-agreement, compensation, and legal language requires appropriate professional review before use.

## Funnel Job

Turn social attention and webinar interest into qualified buyer conversations without training every lead to expect instant personal access to Jen.

The funnel does three things:

1. Names the buyer's real problem: serious income, serious constraints, unclear path.
2. Moves them from curiosity to specific readiness data.
3. Routes them into consult, lender review, or nurture based on behavior.

## Segments

| Segment | Entry Trigger | Main CTA |
|---|---|---|

### 2. Invite Email: The Buyer Changed

**Subject:** The first-time buyer is not 25 anymore

The old first-time buyer story is too simple.

A lot of serious buyers now have years of renting behind them, real careers, family obligations, student loans, gift money questions, co-buyer possibilities, and a very understandable fear that they missed the window.

That does not mean the path is gone.

It means the path needs more clarity before the search starts.

In the workshop, we will look at the questions that actually matter before touring: payment comfort, cash-to-close, financing route, timeline, area tradeoffs, and who needs to be part of the decision.

**CTA:** Reserve your seat for Can We Actually Buy in the Valley?

### 3. Invite Email: Stop Starting With Listings

**Subject:** Listings are not the starting line

Listings make the search feel real.

But for serious first-time buyers in the Valley, listings can also create the wrong kind of pressure. You start comparing kitchens before you know whether the payment works, whether the cash-to-close is realistic, or whether the area tradeoff makes sense.

The better first step is not "what do you like?"

It is:

- What payment can you actually live with?
- What cash is available?
- What loan or assistance path needs review?
- What neighborhoods fit the life you are really building?

That is the work of the workshop.

**CTA:** Save a spot for the buyer math room.

The better question is: "What would make this price point responsible enough to act on?"
