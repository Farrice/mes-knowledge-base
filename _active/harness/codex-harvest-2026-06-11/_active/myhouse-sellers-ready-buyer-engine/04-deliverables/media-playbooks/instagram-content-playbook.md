## Caption Framework

Use this structure:

1. Name the tension.
2. Reject the easy but wrong starting point.
3. Show the better diagnostic.
4. Give one next step.
5. Route to one keyword.

### Example Caption

You can make good money and still feel like buying in the Valley is slipping away.

That does not mean you are unserious.

It means the old first-time buyer advice is too thin for your actual life.

Before you tour, look at payment comfort, cash-to-close, lender review, decision authority, and the neighborhood tradeoff you can live with.

Comment READY for the $800K-$900K First Home Readiness Scorecard.

## Quality Bar

- No generic "dream home journey" language.
- No random open-ended "DM me" CTA.
- No financial claims without review.
- Every public post has one buyer tension, one mechanism, and one keyword.
- The voice should feel warm, direct, and adult, not salesy.
