# Lead Magnet Kit

## Purpose

These assets are the reason the DM keywords work. They give buyers and sellers something useful before Jen spends high-touch time, and they make lead quality observable.

All payment, lending, assistance, buyer-agreement, and legal-adjacent language must be reviewed before public use.

## Asset 1: First Home Readiness Scorecard

Keyword: `READY`

Promise:

> Find out whether the next 90 days are realistic, what needs to be checked, and what to do before touring.

### Scorecard Sections

| Section | Buyer Answers | Score Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Timeline | 0-30, 31-90, 91-180, 180+ days | urgency |
| Target Area | neighborhoods, commute, must-have constraints | geography fit |
| Price Range | target, stretch, unknown | realism |
| Payment Comfort | current rent, monthly comfort, unknown | finance clarity |
| Savings / Funds | down payment, closing costs, reserves, unknown | readiness |
| Lender Status | preapproved, talked to lender, not yet | next route |
| Decision Group | spouse, partner, family, co-buyer, solo, unknown | decision authority |
| Main Concern | payment, area, competition, timing, selling first, programs | content/nurture path |

### Buyer Result Bands

| Band | Meaning | Next Step |
|---|---|---|
| Ready to Map | likely 75+ | Buyer Strategy Session. |
| Clarify First | likely 55-74 | lender/payment review plus Valley Match Map. |
| Build The Path | likely below 55 | education sequence and 30/60/90-day prep path. |

### Closing Copy

```text
This is not a pass/fail test. It is a way to stop guessing.

If your score says "Ready to Map," we can look at the next 90 days.
If your score says "Clarify First," we will check the numbers before we tour.
If your score says "Build The Path," I will still show you what needs to be true next.
```

## Asset 2: Valley Match Map

Keyword: `MAP`

Promise:

> Compare Valley options by payment pressure, commute, lifestyle, home type, and tradeoff instead of only scrolling by photos.

### Comparison Fields

| Field | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Neighborhood | Not all SFV price points mean the same lifestyle. |
| Typical fit | condo, townhome, entry SFR, move-up, seller opportunity. |
| Commute context | 101, 405, 118, Ventura Blvd, Warner Center, work route. |
| Buyer tradeoff | space, updates, HOA, school preference, parking, commute, resale. |
| Watch-outs | payment, competition, property condition, inventory, HOA, insurance. |
| Best next question | keeps DM conversation useful. |

### Example Language

```text
If Sherman Oaks is stretching the payment too far, the answer is not "give up."
The answer is to compare what you are really buying: commute, space, condition, payment, and resale path.
```

## Asset 3: Offer-Ready Weekend Kit

Keyword: `OFFER`

Promise:

> Know what to check before you spend the weekend touring or write an offer that looks weaker than it should.

### Checklist

- lender status
- payment comfort
- cash-to-close estimate
- proof of funds/preapproval packet
- buyer agreement and representation questions
- must-haves vs nice-to-haves
- commute and parking reality
- HOA or property condition watch-outs
- decision-maker alignment
- offer terms beyond price

### Jen Voice Intro

```text
I do not want you falling in love with a house and then finding out the offer was not ready.

This kit is the boring-but-important part that makes the exciting part possible.
```

## Asset 4: Seller Trust File

Keyword: `SELL`

Promise:

> See the process before choosing an agent: prep, pricing, showing quality, offer review, and calm close.

### Sections

| Section | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| Pre-List Room | repairs, presentation, photos, disclosure prep, pricing plan |
| Showing Quality Report | number of showings, buyer type, feedback themes, readiness |
| Offer Stack | price, contingencies, lender strength, close timeline, risk |
| Calm Close | what the team handles so the seller does not carry every detail |
| Team Behind The Sale | Jen's role, MyHouseSellers support, vendors, transaction coordination |

### Seller Intake Prompt

```text
Before we talk about list price, I want to understand timing, property condition, what would make the move feel calm, and what kind of buyer quality we need to attract.
```

## Asset 5: Home Soundcheck Checklist

Keyword: `SOUNDCHECK`

Promise:

> A practical home-fit checklist for creative, music, event, and after-hours people who care about how a home actually lives.

Use sparingly as the EDM/creative bridge. Keep it adult and useful.

### Checklist

- parking reality
- night feel
- noise and privacy
- hosting flow
- room separation
- storage for gear
- late-night entry/exit
- neighbor density
- commute to venues, airport, studio, or work
- morning-after practicality

### Positioning Copy

```text
Pretty photos show the room. A soundcheck shows whether the home fits your actual life.
```

## Delivery Rules

- Send the asset first, then ask one routing question.
- Do not ask seven intake questions before giving value.
- Keep the tone calm and specific.
- Mark all finance/legal claims for review.
- Track which asset creates the highest score leads.
