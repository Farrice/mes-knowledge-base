# 30-Day Content War Room

## Strategy

Every content asset must do at least one of four jobs:

1. attract ready buyers or sellers
2. qualify seriousness
3. prove Jen's trust and local judgment
4. move someone into a score, consult, nurture path, or partner route

This is not a "post every day and hope" calendar. It is a conversion room.

## Content Rules

- Every buyer post includes at least one readiness signal: price range, payment example, cash-to-close, timeline, preapproval, neighborhood tradeoff, or offer strength.
- Every seller post includes at least one process proof: prep, pricing, buyer quality, offer review, negotiation, or calm closing.
- Every CTA uses a keyword: `READY`, `MAP`, `OFFER`, `SELL`, or `SOUNDCHECK`.
- Payment examples must be lender-reviewed before public use.
- No broad "DM me for homes" CTA.
- No fake scarcity.
- Jen should sound like a warm friend with serious judgment, not a billboard.

1. "Question I keep hearing: can I actually buy in the Valley?"
2. "The answer is not yes or no. It depends on payment comfort, savings, timeline, and neighborhood tradeoffs."
3. Poll: "Would you rather check payment first or homes first?"
4. CTA: "Comment `READY` and I will send the readiness scorecard."

3. Poll: "Would you want a seller readiness check before listing?"
4. CTA: "Comment `SELL` for the Seller Trust File."

### Home Soundcheck Story

1. "Pretty photos do not tell you what a home feels like after work."
2. "I check parking, noise, flow, hosting, night feel, and neighborhood rhythm."
3. Poll: "Would this matter to you?"
4. CTA: "Comment `SOUNDCHECK` for the checklist."

## Weekly Review

Every Friday, record:

- posts published
- story replies
- keyword comments
- DMs started
- email captures
- lead scores
- consults booked
- no-shows
- signed clients
- protected-time exceptions
- proof assets created

If content creates attention but no scored leads, revise CTA and intake. If scored leads do not book, revise offer and appointment confirmation. If booked consults do not sign, revise consult structure.
