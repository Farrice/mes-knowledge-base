# Ready Buyer Engine Visual Demo

## What This Is

This is the product-ready local demo for Jen/Jiing. It turns the Ready Buyer Engine into a self-guided visual experience with:

- a workshop-funnel first screen
- webinar and landing page preview
- Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube content previews
- lead magnet routing
- webinar, email, video, Instagram, and lead magnet asset links
- annual closing and monthly pipeline planning metrics
- family-protected weekly rhythm
- compliance and review labels

## Demo Structure

1. **Webinar + Landing Page** shows the buyer workshop offer, local landing page, workshop flow, and lead magnet chain.
2. **Instagram + YouTube** shows the publishable content lanes: The Grown-Up First Home Files, Offer Room, Seller Side Receipts, Valley Match Map, and Home Soundcheck.
3. **Operating Metrics** shows annual closings, monthly leading indicators, estimated GCI planning math, and protected weekly rhythm.
4. **Media Engine Library** links to the landing page, webinar kit, email funnel, video scripts, Instagram playbook, and lead magnet assets.
