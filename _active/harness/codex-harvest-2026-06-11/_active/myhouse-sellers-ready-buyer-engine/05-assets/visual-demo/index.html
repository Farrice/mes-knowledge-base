Operating Metrics

This is not a promise and it is not realtor jargon. It is a plain planning view: how many qualified conversations, appointments, signed clients, and annual closings the system would need to support 2-3 closings per month.

Conservative

24 annual closings

Planning estimate: about $510K gross commission income at $850K average closed price and 2.5% gross commission before splits, expenses, taxes, referral fees, and brokerage details.

Target

30 annual closings

Planning estimate: about $637.5K gross commission income before business costs. This is the steadier 2-3 closing/month pace.

Upside

36 annual closings

Planning estimate: about $765K gross commission income before business costs. This requires stronger listing leverage, referrals, and team support.
