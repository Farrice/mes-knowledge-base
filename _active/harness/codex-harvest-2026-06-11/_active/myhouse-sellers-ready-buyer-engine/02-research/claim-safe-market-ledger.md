# Claim-Safe Market Ledger

## Use Rule

Only use claims that are either source-backed, partner-reviewed, or clearly labeled as a pilot hypothesis.

The client-facing plan should feel confident because the process is strong, not because the claims are overextended.

## Safe Language Bank

Use:

- "Based on current source-backed market context..."
- "This may be available for eligible buyers..."
- "A lender should verify the numbers before you rely on them."
- "This is not a promise that you qualify."
- "Let's check the math before we tour."
- "Here is what sellers and listing agents usually want to see in a serious offer."

Avoid:

- "Everyone qualifies."
- "Free money."
- "Guaranteed approval."
- "Guaranteed 2-3 deals/month."
- "This will make you $300K."
- Any targeting based on age, family status, race, religion, national origin, sex, disability, or other protected-class traits.

## Publishing Checklist

Before any public post, story, DM automation, or lead magnet goes live:

- source-backed market claims checked
- current DPA/program availability checked
- lender-reviewed payment examples
- broker-approved buyer-agreement language
- Fair Housing-safe targeting and wording
- no protected-class inference
- no income or closing guarantees
- no private client data in examples
