## Launch Decision

No public launch until all are true:

- Jen approves the voice
- Jen understands the cockpit routes
- lender reviews finance/program language
- broker reviews buyer-agreement and compliance-sensitive language
- dashboard owner is assigned
- manual fallback exists for every automation
- first proof capture template is ready

## Expansion Decision

Do not pitch this to other agents until the pilot proves at least three:

- Jen saves time or reduces low-quality calls
- qualified leads are scored and routed
- at least one consult is booked from the content/DM path
- seller proof asset is created
- Jen wants to show the system to someone else
- dashboard reveals a repeatable bottleneck
