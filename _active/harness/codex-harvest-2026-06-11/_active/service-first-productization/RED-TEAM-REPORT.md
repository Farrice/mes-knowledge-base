# Red-Team Report - AI Content Misfire Audit

## What Could Break

| Risk | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The promise sounds like guaranteed leads | High | Use "clearer conversation path," not guaranteed clients. |
| The offer sounds like a prompt pack | High | Lead with diagnosis, buyer path, and workflow repair. |
| The buyer is too broad | Medium | Contact only experts/creators with a paid offer. |
| The first delivery sprawls | High | Use the 48-hour SOP and sell implementation separately. |
| The copy gets generic | High | Keep profile/post-specific observations in every DM. |
| Long-term identity language muddies the offer | Medium | Keep Greatest Drivers and AI Operating Partner out of the front door. |

## Hard Gate

Before public launch:

- checkout works
- intake works
- sales page has no unsupported claims
- first 20 prospects are scored
- first 10 DMs have specific observations
- proof-capture template is ready

## Red-Team Verdict

The offer is worth testing if it stays narrow. The danger is not that the idea is weak. The danger is making it too impressive, too abstract, or too system-heavy before one person pays.
