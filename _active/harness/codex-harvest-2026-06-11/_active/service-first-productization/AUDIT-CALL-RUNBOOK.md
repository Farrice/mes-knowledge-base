# Audit Call Runbook - AI Content Misfire Audit

> Default delivery can be async. Use this call only when intake material is incomplete or the buyer needs live clarification.

## Call Goal

Leave with enough context to diagnose the content-to-client misfire and deliver the 48-hour fix.

## 30-Minute Structure

| Time | Segment | Prompt |
|---:|---|---|
| 0-5 | offer clarity | "What do you want the right reader to understand and do?" |
| 5-10 | current content | "Which posts feel closest to what you want more of?" |
| 10-15 | misfire | "Where does the process break: idea capture, AI draft, offer clarity, CTA, or consistency?" |
| 15-20 | voice | "What does AI or templated content keep doing that does not sound like you?" |
| 20-25 | buyer path | "What would make a reader ready to ask about your work?" |
| 25-30 | handoff | Confirm deliverables, timing, and any missing samples. |

## Do Not Drift Into

- full content calendar
- full profile rewrite
- ghostwriting package
- automation build
- unpaid consulting sprint

## Close

```text
I have enough to diagnose the leak. I will send the audit within 48 hours: the misfire, buyer-pathway map, voice/AI notes, 3 rewritten angles, simple workflow, and next CTA path.
```
