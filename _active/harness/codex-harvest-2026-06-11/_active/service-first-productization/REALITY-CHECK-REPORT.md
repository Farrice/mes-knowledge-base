# Reality Check Report - AI Content Misfire Audit

## Verdict

This is a legitimate first-revenue wedge because it is:

- small enough to sell today
- specific enough for a buyer to understand
- close to Farrice's real strengths: AI, systems, content, marketing, judgment
- deliverable without needing a large audience
- able to produce proof before a bigger category is built

## Main Risk

The offer fails if it drifts into abstract AI strategy or broad personal branding. The buyer must understand the paid problem quickly:

```text
My expertise/content/AI workflow is not creating enough trust or clear next steps.
```

## Price Reality

$250 is credible for a focused diagnostic if the buyer sells expertise and the deliverable is specific. $99 would make the offer feel like a favor or prompt pack. $750 belongs to implementation after the diagnostic, not the front door.

## Market Reality

The market is full of generic content services, ghostwriting, AI tools, and prompt products. The wedge is not "more content." The wedge is diagnosis plus a custom workflow that protects voice, clarifies the offer, and points content toward conversation.

## Kill Criteria

- 20 specific DMs produce no comprehension and no useful replies.
- Prospects consistently think it is ghostwriting or a prompt pack.
- Buyers only want follower growth.
- First delivery cannot be completed inside 48 hours.

## Continue Criteria

- 1 paid diagnostic
- 1 checkout request
- 1 serious sales conversation
- 3+ prospects repeat the pain in their own words
- 1 implementation request after diagnostic
