# 14-Day Daily Marching Orders - AI Content Misfire Audit

> Goal: validate that solo experts and creators will pay for a 48-hour content-system diagnostic.
> North star: paid yes or serious sales conversation.

## Fixed Daily Blocks

| Block | Time Cap | Work |
|---|---:|---|
| Setup/proof | 45 min | links, mini-audits, proof assets |
| Prospecting | 60 min | score prospects, comment, send DMs |
| Sales/delivery | 60-120 min | replies, checkout, fulfillment |
| Closeout | 15 min | tracker, buyer words, next actions |

## Day 0 - Setup Lock

- Create $250 checkout and optional $750 sprint checkout.
- Build intake and calendar/clarification link.
- Stage sales page.
- Fill `links.md`.
- Fill first 20 prospect board.
- Stop condition: no outreach until links work.

## Day 1 - First 10 Asks

- Build 3 mini-audit examples.
- Send 10 direct asks.
- Comment on 5-10 relevant prospect posts before pitching where possible.
- Stop condition: 10 specific asks logged.

## Day 2 - Follow-Up And Public Post

- Publish Post 1 if links are ready.
- Follow up with active/warm threads.
- Send 10 more specific asks to scored prospects.
- Stop condition: every reply has a next action.

## Day 3 - First Review

- Review comprehension:
  - Do they understand the offer?
  - Do they ask about price?
  - Do they ask for examples?
  - Do they confuse it with ghostwriting or prompt packs?
- Send checkout only to serious fits.
- Stop condition: keep/revise opener decision.

## Day 4 - Tighten

- Rewrite the worst-performing DM.
- Publish or send one anonymized mini-audit.
- Follow up with "send one strong post and one weak draft" bridge.

## Day 5 - Proof Push

- Publish Post 2.
- Send 5-10 new asks to the tightest segment.
- Offer implementation sprint only if a diagnostic buyer asks for installation.

## Day 6 - Delivery Or Diagnosis

- If sold: deliver using `fulfillment-sop.md`.
- If not sold: diagnose whether the issue is ICP, price, opener, proof, or name.

## Day 7 - Weekly Review

- Count touches, replies, serious conversations, checkout requests, and paid diagnostics.
- Extract strongest buyer phrase.
- Decide next week: continue, narrow ICP, change opener, or build more proof.

## Days 8-14 - Second Cycle

- Continue 10 scored direct asks/day.
- Publish one proof/process post after any audit or mini-audit.
- Close old threads with breakup notes.
- Capture every objection.
- Do not build a product unless there is paid proof.

## Day 14 Decision

Choose one:

- continue same offer
- narrow to one buyer segment
- change public name
- raise to $300 standard
- emphasize implementation sprint
- stop and choose a different service-first path
