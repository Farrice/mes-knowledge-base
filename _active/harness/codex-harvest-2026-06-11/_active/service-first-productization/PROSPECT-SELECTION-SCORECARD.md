# Prospect Selection Scorecard - AI Content Misfire Audit

> Purpose: stop guessing who to contact.
> Contact prospects scoring 13+.

## Primary ICP

Solo experts and creators with visible expertise, a paid offer, and a content-to-client misfire.

## Qualification Score

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Buyer type | no paid offer | hobby creator | service provider | expert/coach/consultant | expert with high-value offer |
| Visible content | none | thin profile | a few posts | consistent posts | strong ideas + clear samples |
| Misfire signal | none | unknown | generic content | strong ideas but weak path | obvious profile/content/AI leak |
| Ability to pay | unknown | low-ticket only | early offer | $1K+ offer | high-ticket service/advisory |
| Personalization hook | none | generic topic | broad match | specific post/profile gap | obvious mini-audit angle |

## Segment Priority

1. Solo consultants with good ideas but weak CTAs.
2. Creators with a paid offer and scattered content.
3. Coaches/educators experimenting with AI or content systems.
4. Fractional operators whose profile is strong but posting path is unclear.
5. Service experts with proof but no reliable buyer-pathway content.

## Ignore For Now

- no visible offer
- follower-growth-only creator
- full ghostwriting buyer
- no public material
- cannot personalize without guessing
