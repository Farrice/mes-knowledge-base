## Signal Thresholds

Green:

- 3+ real artifact submissions.
- 5+ qualified DMs.
- 1-2 paid audit conversations.
- At least one buyer asks about price, next step, or implementation.

Yellow:

- Good comments but few artifacts.
- People understand the pain but do not know what to send.
- The CTA needs more examples.

Red:

- Likes with no submissions.
- DMs ask "what is this?" after reading the post.
- People think it is a prompt pack.
- People ask for passive income templates.
- No one sends a real workflow.

## Daily Closeout

At the end of the day, answer:

1. What artifact types did people send?
2. Which misfire pattern repeated?
3. Which words did buyers use naturally?
4. Did AI Misfire land before Guessproof needed explanation?
5. Did anyone ask for the paid map?
6. What part of the CTA created friction?
7. What should tomorrow's post clarify?

## Decision Rules

If green:

> Offer the $750 AI Misfire Map to the strongest workflow fit.

If yellow:

> Publish a second post with clearer examples and a screenshot-style proof demo.

If red:

> Rewrite the public hook around the exact confusion. Do not expand the offer yet.
