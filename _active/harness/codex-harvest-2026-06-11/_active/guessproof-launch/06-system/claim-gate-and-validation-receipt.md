# Claim Gate And Validation Receipt

Run this before publishing, sending, or using any public-facing copy.

## Allowed Claims

You can claim:

- I map where AI was forced to guess.
- I identify the hidden judgment behind the rescue.
- I turn one recurring misfire into a reusable workflow kit.
- I add source-of-truth rules, decision boundaries, and validation checks.
- I tell you whether the issue is prompt, SOP, workflow, or not ready.
- I keep human review points visible.
- I deliver a 48-hour paid audit for one workflow.

## Banned Or Needs-Proof Claims

Do not claim:

- Guaranteed leads.
- Guaranteed revenue.
- Guaranteed virality.
- Guaranteed time savings.
- Fully replaces a human.
- Perfect AI behavior.
- Never rewrite AI again.
- Works for every business.
- Passive income.
- Client results that do not exist yet.
- Trademark-safe ownership of Guessproof before a search.

## Offer Acceptance Criteria

### Free Misfire Triage

Complete when:

- It gives three bullets.
- It names a concrete place AI guessed.
- It identifies missing judgment.
- It gives a fit verdict.
- It does not perform the whole repair.

### AI Misfire Map

Complete when:

- It maps at least five misfires or gives a clear not-ready verdict.
- It identifies at least three hidden-judgment rules.
- It includes one first repair or system patch.
- It includes a Guessproof Receipt.
- It recommends sprint, pause, or simplify.

### Guessproof Workflow Sprint

Complete when:

- One recurring workflow has a reusable kit.
- The kit includes source rules, decision boundaries, execution steps, and validation checks.
- Human review points are explicit.
- The buyer can test the workflow from files alone.
- The receipt shows what changed.

### Guessproof Skill Stack Install

Complete when:

- The workflow kit is installed into the buyer's actual working surface.
- Templates, prompts, SOPs, checklists, and review loops are connected.
- The buyer knows what AI can decide and what remains human-owned.
- Validation can be repeated without hidden chat context.

## Refund Stance

Use this:

> If I cannot identify a meaningful misfire pattern in the workflow during the first review window, I will tell you and refund the audit instead of forcing a vague deliverable.

Do not use:

- Satisfaction guarantees tied to taste.
- Revenue guarantees.
- Lead guarantees.
- Time-saved guarantees.
- Open-ended revision promises.

## Launch Gate

Green light when:

- Proof Demo 001 is readable in under 60 seconds.
- Public CTA asks for `RESCUE`.
- The one-page surface has no unsupported outcome claims.
- Free triage and paid audit are clearly different.
- Signal tracker is ready.

Yellow light when:

- Proof is readable but not sharp.
- CTA is clear but the profile still sounds generic.
- The paid bridge feels slightly too soft.

Red light when:

- The offer sounds like a prompt pack.
- The public page leads with skill stack language.
- The proof demo is missing.
- Copy promises outcomes that are not proven.
- No signal tracker exists before posting.
