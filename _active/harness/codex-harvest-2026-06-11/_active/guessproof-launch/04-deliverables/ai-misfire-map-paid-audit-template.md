This is the $750 paid gateway. It is a 48-hour diagnostic audit for one recurring AI-assisted workflow.

## Buyer-Facing Description

The AI Misfire Map shows where your AI-assisted workflow is forced to guess, what expert judgment is missing, and what has to be captured before the work can become reusable.

You send one recurring AI output that still needs human rescue. I return a mapped diagnosis, first workflow fix, and a Guessproof Receipt showing what changed and what still needs human review.

Ask the buyer for:

2. The prompt or instruction used.

## Deliverable Structure

### 1. Misfire Summary

One plain-language paragraph:

> This workflow is not failing because AI cannot write. It is failing because the AI is missing your decision rules around audience risk, source material, proof, and send-readiness.

### 2. Misfire Map

| Output Moment | Where AI Guessed | Missing Judgment | Fix Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Excerpt] | [Specific guess] | [Human rule] | Prompt, SOP, workflow, or review gate |

### 3. Hidden Judgment Inventory

List the expert rules that were trapped in the rewrite:

- What the buyer knows that the AI did not.
- What the buyer refused to say.
- What proof was required.
- What tone boundary protected the relationship.
- What source context was missing.
- What review check should exist before send.

### 4. First Workflow Fix

Deliver one of:

- Repaired output.
- Better instruction block.
- Source-of-truth rule list.
- SOP patch.
- Validation checklist.
- Mini execution kit.

### 5. Fit Verdict

Use one:

- Prompt issue.
- SOP issue.
- Workflow issue.
- Not ready to systemize.

### 6. Guessproof Receipt

Use [guessproof-receipt-template.md](guessproof-receipt-template.md).
