# Client Intake Form

Use this for free triage, paid AI Misfire Maps, and workflow sprint qualification.

## Short Free Triage Intake

1. Paste the AI output you had to rewrite or rescue.
2. Paste the original prompt or instruction, if available.
3. Who was this output for?
4. What was it supposed to accomplish?
5. What made the first version unsafe, weak, generic, or unusable?
6. If you edited it, paste the final version or summarize what changed.

## Paid AI Misfire Map Intake

1. What recurring workflow are we looking at?
2. How often does this workflow happen?
3. Who receives or uses the output?
4. What happens if the output goes out wrong?
5. Paste one to three AI outputs that needed rescue.
6. Paste the prompt, instruction, SOP, or workflow notes used to create them.
7. Paste the source material the AI should have used.
8. Paste the final approved version, if available.
9. What did you change manually?
10. What rules were you using when you made those changes?
11. What must always be true before this output can be sent?
12. What should AI never decide on its own in this workflow?
13. What does "good enough to send" mean here?
14. What tools, templates, or handoff points are involved?
15. What would make this workflow worth fixing now?

## Sprint Qualification

Use these after the paid audit or when a buyer is clearly ready.

1. Is this workflow tied to revenue, delivery, client trust, or team leverage?
2. Does the same misfire happen repeatedly?
3. Is there enough source material to capture the judgment?
4. Who owns final approval?
5. Where should the reusable kit live: docs, prompts, templates, CRM, project tool, or another system?
6. What would the first useful version need to do?
7. What should remain human-reviewed?
8. Who will test it?
9. What is the acceptance bar?
10. What would make the sprint a clear win?

## Privacy And Redaction Note

Use this line when asking for sensitive artifacts:

> Redact names, numbers, private client details, and anything confidential. I only need enough structure to see where the AI guessed and what judgment had to be supplied manually.
