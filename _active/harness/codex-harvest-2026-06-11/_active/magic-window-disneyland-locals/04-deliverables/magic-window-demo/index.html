Not a planning app. A local go-or-skip radar.

The winning wedge is not another map of waits. It is a fast judgment layer for people who can go often, can leave if it stinks, and hate burning a night on a packed park.

1 glance Turns schedules, event spillover, school breaks, passholder behavior, and weather into a simple local decision.
3 states Go, maybe, or skip. The app wins by removing spreadsheet planning from a weekday whim.
1 wedge Protect the magic for locals, Magic Key holders, and nearby families who already know the park.

Initial product bet

Useful as a paid niche tool if it feels more predictive and more local than generic crowd calendars.

Visual System Map

Where the product creates value
Signals Waits, blockouts, events, schools, weather, local arrival waves The raw inputs already exist in fragments. The product needs to unify them into a cleaner signal.
Model Friction score by hour, not only crowd score by day Locals care about whether a four-hour window is worth it, not whether the whole day is crowded.
Decision Go, maybe, skip, with the reason exposed Trust comes from explaining the forecast in human terms.
Money Alerts, premium calendars, family plans, creator/local bundles People pay when the app saves a wasted night or unlocks a surprisingly good one.

Current Market Shape

Existing apps prove demand, but leave a local-first opening
Official app Deep operational layer: wait times, maps, tickets, mobile order, photos, Lightning Lane. Too broad
TouringPlans Serious planner with crowd calendars and a paid subscription model. Planner-first
IsItPacked Simple crowd calendar mental model, useful for date selection. Lightweight
Thrill Data Data-rich waits, charts, history, and crowd levels. Analyst-first
Wait apps Magic Guide, MouseWait, Queue-Times style tools focus on live waits and park info. Live utility

First validation test Post a daily “Go or Skip Tonight” forecast to local Disney Facebook groups, Reddit, TikTok, and Instagram stories for 14 days. Track saves, replies, and “wish I had this app” comments.
Trust test Publish forecast receipts: what the app predicted, what waits actually did, and why it missed. Forecast transparency becomes the brand.
Payment test Pre-sell a private text alert list before building native iOS. The question is whether locals pay for a better night, not whether they admire the interface.
