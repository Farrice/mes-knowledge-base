# Deep Research OS

Deep Research OS is the local command package for research-heavy knowledge work:
market intelligence, social listening, ICP deep canvassing, product-market fit,
offer-market fit, competitive analysis, claim verification, and wide research.

- Codex wrapper: `.agents/cold-skills/source-command-wrappers/source-command-deep-research-os/SKILL.md`

## Preferred Use

Use `/virtuoso --mode research [objective]` when you want the full trace and
owner/gate system. Use `/deep-research-os [objective]` when you already know the
job is research-heavy.
