## Notion Page Copy

# The Vibe Tax Diagnostic Lab

## A 2-minute false-signal check for offers, content, and pages that look good but are not creating buyer movement.

Your strategy can look right and still lie.

The post can get likes.  
The page can sound polished.  
The offer can make sense in your head.

But if the right buyer still does not move, the signal may not mean what you think it means.

That gap is the Vibe Tax: the cost of building from confident guesses instead of buyer reality.

## What This Checks

This diagnostic looks at six places where false signal usually hides:

| Signal Area | What It Checks |
|---|---|
| Buyer Language Fit | Whether the message uses words buyers actually use. |
| Shortlist Readiness | Whether a buyer can tell why to choose you. |
| Proof Gap | Whether the claim has enough evidence to feel safe. |
| False Signal Risk | Whether you are over-trusting the wrong signal. |
| Human Resonance | Whether the right buyer feels seen before being sold. |
| Social Repeatability | Whether the core idea is easy to repeat. |

```text

```

Body:

```text

- proof that the problem is urgent,
- proof that your mechanism is different,
- proof that the buyer is not alone,
- proof that the old way is costing them,
- proof that you understand the decision they are scared to make.

The question I would ask:

What claim does your buyer most need to believe before they would take the next step?

If you cannot answer that clearly, the next move is not more content.

It is a better proof map.
```
