# Vibe Tax Content Pillars And First Tests

Local Markdown Source for the rendered Vibe Tax validation document.

## Creative Position

**The Vibe Tax Brief** is a 48-hour false-signal diagnostic for expert-led B2B operators who are about to make an offer, content, positioning, or sales decision from shaky signal.

Public promise:

```text
Find the false signals before they cost you strategy, content, or clients.
```

Practical CTA:

```text
Send me one decision you are about to make from shaky signal. I will tell you what I would trust, ignore, fix, or test.
```

## Content Pillars

| Pillar | Job | Buyer Belief To Shift | Sample Angle | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| False Signal Literacy | Name bad inputs: likes, peer praise, AI summaries, competitor noise, soft compliments. | If it got engagement, it worked. | Likes are not buyer language. | Send me the signal you are trusting. |
| Buyer Reality Receipts | Show buyer language, alternatives, hidden objections, and decision pressure. | My buyer just needs clearer explanation. | Your buyer may already have put you in the wrong category. | Send the claim you are building around. |
| Shortlist Readiness | Teach that buyers classify providers before a call. | The sales call is where the decision starts. | You can lose before the first call. | Send the page or pitch buyers see first. |
| Proof Gap Diagnosis | Show why interesting offers still feel unsafe to buy. | Better wording fixes the problem. | Interesting is not intent. | Send the offer claim that gets polite replies. |
| AI Backstage, Human Judgment Frontstage | Separate AI speed from buyer understanding. | AI output equals better strategy. | AI can polish the wrong signal. | Send the AI-assisted draft you do not trust yet. |
| Live Mini-Diagnostics | Demonstrate signal -> risk -> better check -> action. | A diagnostic is abstract until I see it. | A mini Vibe Tax teardown: liked by peers, ignored by buyers. | Send one decision and I will score the signal. |

## First 10 Post Angles

| # | Angle | First 50 Words | Next Step |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | 7 signals I would not build strategy from | A few smart comments. A flattering call. A competitor post with 400 likes. One AI summary that sounds confident. None of those are buyer evidence until they survive a better check. | Invite one decision. |
| 2 | Liked by peers, ignored by buyers | The post worked on the dashboard and failed in the business. That is the Vibe Tax: attention that feels like proof until the buyer still does not move. | Ask for one post or page. |
| 3 | Interesting is expensive | Interesting is the compliment people give when they understand enough to be polite and not enough to act. If your offer keeps getting interesting, check the proof gap. | Ask for the offer line. |
| 4 | AI missed the fear | AI can summarize your category and still miss the fear that decides the sale. That is why faster content can make the wrong signal louder. | Ask for an AI-polished draft. |
| 5 | Hidden buyer checklist | Sometimes the person who likes your pitch is not the person who kills the decision. Hidden buyers care about risk, proof, and internal justification. | Ask for one stalled deal. |
| 6 | Weak claim to buyer-ready claim | A claim can be clear and still not be safe to buy. The question is not only what it says. The question is what a buyer would need to believe before acting. | Ask for a claim to diagnose. |
| 7 | False Signal Scorecard | I am testing a 12-question scorecard for people making offer, content, or positioning decisions from noisy signals. It does not grade your talent. It grades the evidence underneath the decision. | Invite scorecard requests. |
| 8 | Competitor wallpaper | If everyone in your category says clarity, growth, strategy, AI, content, or transformation, the words stop carrying weight. Category wallpaper feels professional and gets ignored. | Ask for a category phrase. |
| 9 | When to skip the brief | Do not run the Vibe Tax Brief if you already know the buyer, the trigger, the proof gap, and the next test. Run it when you are about to act from signal you do not fully trust. | Qualify fits. |
| 10 | What I track instead of likes | Likes are not useless. They are just incomplete. For this offer, I care more about saves, sends, profile views, DMs with real decisions, diagnostic requests, objections, and paid asks. | Ask for a real decision. |

## Manual Conversion Path

1. Publish or comment with one specific false-signal insight.
2. Ask for one decision, offer claim, post, page, or pitch.
3. Give a three-bullet quick read:
   - false signal risk
   - missing evidence
   - keep, revise, stop, or test
4. Invite qualified fits into the founding Vibe Tax Brief.
5. Log the buyer phrase, objection, and next step in `TRACKER.md`.
