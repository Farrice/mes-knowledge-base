## How It Works

Full diagnostic source model: the buyer answers 12 questions. Each question is scored 0-5.

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0-1 | false signal risk |
| 2-3 | partial signal |
| 4-5 | buyer-ready signal |

Total raw score: 60. Convert to 100 if needed.

## Questions

### Channel / Context Fit

1. Are you using signals from the same context where buyers actually decide, search, compare, or pay?
2. Can you name which signals you are ignoring because they do not predict buyer action?

### Buyer Precision

3. Is your buyer tied to a real trigger, constraint, and buying moment?
4. Can you describe the decision pressure the buyer feels when the problem becomes urgent?

### Pain Point Specificity

5. Does your message name the external problem, internal frustration, and consequence of delay?
6. Does your copy use phrases buyers would actually say under stress?

### Solution Novelty

7. Does your offer reveal a non-obvious mechanism or reframe buyers remember?
8. Can a buyer explain why your approach is different from the obvious alternative?

### Walk-Away Value

9. Would the buyer leave with a clearer decision, sharper language, or visible correction?
10. Does every finding lead to keep, revise, stop, or test?

### Signal Consistency

11. Does the pattern repeat across buyer words, objections, alternatives, content response, and commercial behavior?
12. Can you show receipts or confidence labels for the signal you are trusting?
