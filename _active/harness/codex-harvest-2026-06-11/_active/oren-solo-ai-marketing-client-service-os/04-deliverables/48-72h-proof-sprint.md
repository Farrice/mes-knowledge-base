# 48-72 Hour Proof Sprint

## Service Position

This sprint helps a service business turn scattered proof, weak referrals, and inconsistent follow-up into a usable one-person marketing operating system.

The promise:

```text
In 48-72 hours, we identify the highest-leverage marketing leak, build the first proof-backed operating asset, and hand you a 7-day execution sprint.
```

## Best-Fit Clients

| Best fit | Why |
|---|---|
| Consultants and coaches | Outcomes exist but are often trapped in calls, notes, and memory. |
| Agencies and expert services | Proof, referrals, and follow-up usually leak across scattered tools. |
| Local and professional services | Referrals and trust matter more than channel sprawl. |
| B2B service businesses | Sales collateral and follow-up can directly increase qualified calls. |

## Poor-Fit Clients

Do not sell this first when:

- the offer is not defined,
- the business has no proof or client outcomes,
- the buyer is unknown,
- the client wants high-volume content before fixing follow-up,
- the work requires private data that has not been supplied,
- the client expects guaranteed revenue from a diagnostic.

## Intake

Ask for:

- service description,
- target buyer,
- 30-day sales goal,
- current lead sources,
- website or offer page,
- 2-5 testimonials, case notes, or client outcomes,
- recent sales objections,
- follow-up emails or call scripts if available,
- current content or LinkedIn examples,
- available weekly marketing hours.

## Delivery Process

| Phase | Work | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Intake Review | Review assets, offer, proof, lead sources, and follow-up state. | Evidence inventory |
| 2. Bottleneck Diagnosis | Identify the first leak: referral, proof, message, follow-up, collateral, or content validation. | Fit verdict |
| 3. Proof Asset Build | Turn one outcome, objection, or message into a usable asset. | Before/after proof artifact |
| 4. Operating Template | Fill the solo marketing rhythm for the client. | 7-day sprint and weekly cadence |
| 5. Handoff | Explain what to use, what to defer, and what the next sprint should be. | Client-facing operating handoff |

## Deliverables

### Diagnostic Sprint

- evidence inventory
- bottleneck verdict
- referral/proof/follow-up diagnosis
- one before/after proof asset
- 7-day solo marketing sprint
- do-not-do list
- recommended next implementation sprint

### Implementation Sprint

Use after the diagnostic when the client wants done-with-you or done-for-you execution.

- referral ask packet
- proof-backed landing page or PDF section
- AI copy-machine context pack
- 3-5 message board assets
- email/follow-up templates
- weekly operating calendar
- reuse loop for proof, content, and sales collateral

## Before/After Proof Shape

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Scattered testimonial or vague result | Proof-backed message with client context, problem, action, result, and next buyer question |
| Generic service page claim | Specific landing page section tied to proof and buyer objection |
| No referral process | Referral ask packet with timing, recipient list, and follow-up rule |
| Manual follow-up | 2-3 email templates matched to buyer stage |

## Pricing Hypothesis

These are directional starting points, not market guarantees.

| Package | Starting price shape | Raise when |
|---|---:|---|
| Diagnostic Sprint | $500-$1,500 | The before/after proof reliably creates follow-on work. |
| Implementation Sprint | $1,500-$4,000 | The work includes landing page, email, proof, and sales collateral implementation. |
| Monthly Operating Desk | $2,000-$6,000/month | The client needs ongoing proof, message, follow-up, and content validation support. |

## Quality Gate

The sprint fails if it ends with advice only. It passes when the client receives one usable proof-backed asset, one 7-day sprint, and one clear next implementation path.

## Upsell Path

| Finding | Natural next offer |
|---|---|
| Referral leak | Referral sprint or past-client reactivation |
| Proof leak | Case study/proof asset sprint |
| Follow-up leak | Email/CRM follow-up sprint |
| Message leak | Landing page and sales collateral sprint |
| Content validation leak | LinkedIn or platform-specific message test sprint |
