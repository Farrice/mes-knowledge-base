## Service-Business Adaptation

The original solo workflow covers founders, creators, operators, freelancers, service providers, and small businesses. This package narrows that into a client/service-business operating template where the highest-leverage early surfaces are usually:

- referral ask and timing,
- proof capture,
- sales collateral,
- email and CRM follow-up,
- landing page message match,
- LinkedIn or relevant organic validation,
- paid/creator work only after conversion paths exist.
