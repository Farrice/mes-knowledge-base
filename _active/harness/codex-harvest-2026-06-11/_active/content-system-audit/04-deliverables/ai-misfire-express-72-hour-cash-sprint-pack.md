## Objections

### "Isn't this just prompt engineering?"

No. Prompt engineering changes the request. This maps the missing operating knowledge behind the work: source of truth, authority, decision rules, examples, failure modes, and quality tests.

### "Can you just fix the prompt?"

Maybe. If the misfire is shallow, I will say that. If the issue is hidden judgment or missing source of truth, fixing the prompt alone will not hold.

### "I do not have a clean SOP."

That is fine. Messy inputs are often better because they show where the real work lives.

### "Why pay for an audit?"

Because building automation before diagnosis usually creates faster wrong output. The audit tells us whether this needs a prompt tweak, an SOP rewrite, a work primitive, or no automation at all.
