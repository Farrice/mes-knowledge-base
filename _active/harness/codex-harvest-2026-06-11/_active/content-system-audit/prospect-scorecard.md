# Prospect Scorecard - AI Content Misfire Audit

> **Purpose**: choose the first 20 prospects without guessing.
> **Rule**: contact only prospects scoring 13+ unless they are a warm referral.

## Primary Prospect

Solo experts and creators who sell expertise and have visible public material:

- independent consultants
- coaches with a paid offer
- fractional operators
- creator-educators
- copywriters, strategists, designers, or marketers with advisory/service offers
- consultants building a thought-leadership motion

## Score Each Prospect From 0-20

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Buyer type | No paid expertise offer | hobby creator | general service provider | expert/coach/consultant | expert with clear high-value offer |
| Visible material | no samples | thin profile | a few posts | consistent posts | strong posts + clear profile |
| Misfire signal | no content issue visible | content only sparse | generic advice | strong ideas but weak CTA | clear voice/offer/AI/content leak |
| Ability to pay | unknown | low-ticket only | early offer | $1K+ service/product | high-ticket consulting/advisory |
| Personalization hook | none | generic compliment | topic match | specific post/profile gap | obvious mini-audit angle |

## Segment Priority

1. Solo experts with strong ideas and weak direct asks.
2. Creators with a paid offer but scattered content.
3. Coaches/consultants using AI or posting about content friction.
4. Fractional executives whose profile is strong but posting path is unclear.
5. Service providers with public proof but no consistent buyer-pathway content.

## Send The Audit Offer When

- you can reference one specific post, profile line, offer, or draft pattern
- the person sells expertise, not just attention
- the profile/content gap is visible enough to diagnose respectfully
- the likely fix can fit a 48-hour audit
- the person would benefit from clearer client conversations, not just more impressions

## Do Not Contact Yet

- people with no paid offer
- creators who only monetize ads/sponsors
- people who want full ghostwriting
- people whose only visible goal is follower growth
- people with no public material to review
- conservative prospects who would dislike AI/content language unless warmed up first

## Outreach Note

Never say "your content is bad." Use:

```text
The useful thinking is already there. I think the leak is in the path between idea, offer, AI draft, and the next ask.
```
