# AI Slop Cleanup Offer + Proof Sprint

## Offer Frame

AI Slop Cleanup is a productized workflow-repair service for experts whose AI drafts are creating more rewriting, not less.

One-liner:

```text
I clean up the workflow behind your generic AI drafts so your next draft sounds more like you and takes less rewriting.
```

Short offer:

```text
Send me your offer, buyer, voice samples, and a few weak AI drafts. I will diagnose why the drafts feel generic, repair the constraint stack, rewrite a small set of examples, and hand back a usable-draft protocol you can reuse.
```

## Buyer And Pain

| Buyer Signal | What It Means |
|---|---|
| "ChatGPT sounds nothing like me." | Voice guardrails are missing or too generic. |
| "I still rewrite everything." | The workflow is not producing usable first drafts. |
| "The copy is fine but not sharp." | Buyer path, offer clarity, or proof is weak. |
| "It sounds too AI." | The draft has generic structure, filler, false certainty, or flattened taste. |
| "I do not know what to tell the AI." | The buyer needs a reusable constraint stack, not more random prompting. |

## No-Call Intake

Ask for:

1. What do you sell, to whom, and what do they already believe?
2. What are you trying to produce with AI?
3. Where will the output be used?
4. Paste or upload 2-5 AI drafts that feel generic or unusable.
5. Paste or upload 2-5 examples that sound like you at your best.
6. What do you keep rewriting by hand?
7. What claims, phrases, tones, or topics should be avoided?
8. What would make the cleanup useful within 7 days?

Start only when the buyer provides enough material to diagnose voice, buyer, offer, and intended use.

### 6. Usable Draft Protocol

1. Start with the buyer moment, not the topic.
2. Add the offer boundary before asking AI to draft.
3. Include one real example, objection, or client phrase.
4. Run the draft against the voice guardrails.
5. Remove unsupported claims and generic filler.
6. Keep only what a buyer could act on this week.

### 7. Next 7 Days

- Reuse the constraint stack on one new draft.
- Compare the new draft against the repaired examples.
- Save any phrase that sounds like the client.
- Mark what still needed manual rewriting.
- Bring those notes into the next cleanup or implementation sprint.

## Quality Gate

The delivery passes only if:

- the buyer can see the specific reason their AI output was generic
- the repaired examples sound less templated and more buyer-aware
- the constraint stack can be reused without Farrice in the room
- public-facing copy avoids unsupported revenue, follower, lead, or pipeline claims
- the handoff tells the buyer exactly what to do next
- the work stays inside the purchased cleanup scope

## Do Not Build Yet Criteria

Do not build a larger product, subscription, course, or automation until at least one of these is true:

- one buyer pays for the cleanup
- three qualified buyers describe the rewrite pain in similar language
- one buyer asks for implementation after seeing the diagnostic
- the same Slop Scan template works across at least three samples
- a before/after proof asset is strong enough to show without explanation
