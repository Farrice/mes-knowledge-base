# Kishotenketsu Mechanics Ledger

| Mechanic | Evidence Anchor | Deploy When | Failure Mode | Quality Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Battlefield to garden diagnostic | `00:01:37-00:02:16` | A draft forces a villain onto material that wants quiet, grief, appreciation, ritual, or internal stakes. | Removing conflict also removes pressure. | Name the real variable or contrast that now drives attention. |

## Scene Mechanics From The Source

| Mechanic | When To Use | Source Status | Applied Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Benign unknown opening | A story needs wonder without danger. | Transcript-backed | Code the unknown as delight, curiosity, or invitation instead of threat. |
| State of being before problem | The piece should invite immersion before urgency. | Transcript-backed | Establish rituals, rules, textures, and what ordinary life feels like. |
| Mundane plus mythic juxtaposition | You want iconic stillness or memorable brand texture. | Transcript-backed/inferred application | Place ordinary process beside an impossible, symbolic, or heightened element. |
| Reality intrusion | A soft world needs emotional gravity. | Transcript-backed | Let a hospital note, letter, bill, memory, or market shift change the meaning of the ordinary. |
| Dormant status contrast | Tension should hum under politeness or beauty. | Transcript-backed/inferred application | Put class, access, aspiration, or role contrast inside a smooth social scene. |
| New-axis doorbell | The story needs a hard turn without a bigger enemy. | Transcript-backed/inferred application | Introduce a new constraint or truth that changes the whole category of the scene. |
| Symbol returns transformed | Closure should land without over-explaining. | Transcript-backed/inferred application | Bring back an object or phrase so it means something different after the turn. |

## Rewrite Prompts

### Variable Over Villain

```text
Rewrite this so the engine is not an antagonist. Identify the real variable forcing adaptation, then rebuild the piece around world, process, perspective shift, and integration.
```

### Process As Plot

```text
Find the process I am summarizing. Render the sequence with sensory and emotional weight so the reader bonds with the world before the point arrives.
```

### Perspective Shift

```text
Introduce a Ten: a new point of view, time jump, memory, condition, or piece of information that changes what the earlier details mean.
```

### Integration

```text
Replace the victory/defeat ending with integration. Show what continues, what changed, and what the reader now understands differently.
```

## Acceptance Gate

A Kishotenketsu pass fails if it only labels sections as Ki, Sho, Ten, and Ketsu. It passes when the draft visibly changes how the reader moves: inhabits, deepens, reinterprets, and leaves with a changed relationship to the material.
