## What I Would Do First

Start with Avenue 1, not the broad marketplace.

Reason:

- It matches the current trend toward AI implementation and workflow repair.
- It can produce $5-$99 fastest, while still laddering to $250-$1,000.
- It uses your actual edge: skill design, orchestration, validation, routing, and quality gates.
- It creates the raw proof needed for later marketplace products.

## First 24-Hour Sequence

1. Pick one public-facing promise:

   > I find where your AI workflow is guessing and turn the fix into a reusable skill, prompt, or workflow receipt.

2. Build one demo using an existing artifact:

   - before: generic AI output, weak skill, brittle prompt, or messy workflow
   - diagnosis: where AI guessed
   - patch: rewritten trigger/instruction/checklist
   - test: three prompts that prove it works better
   - receipt: what changed and what remains human-reviewed

3. Send ten manual asks:

   > I am testing a tiny AI workflow rescue diagnostic. Send one AI output, prompt, SOP, skill, or workflow that still needs your rescue before it can go out. I will map the first three places it was forced to guess and patch the weakest instruction layer. Founding price is $49 for the first few.

4. If someone asks "what do I get?", answer:

   > A one-page rescue map: failure points, missing judgment, patched instruction layer, three test prompts, and a verdict on whether this is a prompt issue, SOP issue, workflow issue, or skill/package opportunity.

5. If someone has a serious recurring workflow, offer:

   > I can turn this into a 48-hour workflow sprint for $500: map, prompts, SOP, test checklist, and handoff receipt.

## Do Not Do Yet

- Do not build a giant skill marketplace storefront first.
- Do not sell generic prompt packs.
- Do not promise passive income.
- Do not claim top 1% quality publicly until proof examples exist.
- Do not automate outreach.
- Do not publish a marketplace package before at least one paid or serious free diagnostic produces a reusable proof artifact.
