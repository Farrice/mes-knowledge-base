Compliance Guardrail

Correct line: automate research and preparation, not LinkedIn scraping or fake activity.

LinkedIn prohibits unauthorized tools that scrape data or automate actions like messages, comments, likes, shares, or inauthentic engagement. The safe system is a research assistant, not a spam engine.
