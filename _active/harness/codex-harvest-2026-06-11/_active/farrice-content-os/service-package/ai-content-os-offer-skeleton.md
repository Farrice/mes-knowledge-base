# AI Content OS Offer Skeleton

## Offer One-Liner

Install the context, research, hook lab, writers' room, and quality gates that turn an expert's raw ideas into content that sounds like them and moves the right audience.

## Best-Fit Buyer

Visible experts, consultants, coaches, founders, and operators who already have substance but keep getting generic AI drafts, scattered posting, weak hooks, or content that does not connect to pipeline.

## Core Promise

The client leaves with a working content operating system, not a folder of prompts:

- Voice and source context base.
- Audience and market research ledger.
- Brandjack and outlier opportunity board.
- 20-post sprint.
- Weekly content batch.
- Hook Lab.
- Writers' room and anti-slop quality gates.
- Engagement list and comment strategy.
- Reusable card schema for future content.

## Intake

| Input | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Offer and ICP | Prevents content from becoming generic thought leadership |
| Existing best/worst posts | Calibrates voice and quality floor |
| Raw stories, calls, notes, talks | Gives the system real material |
| Competitors and inspiration | Supports outlier and brand research |
| Things they hate sounding like | Anti-slop and anti-voice guard |
| Proof assets | Conversion and authority content |

## Delivery Map

1. Context audit.
2. Voice and writing-floor registration.
3. Research and opportunity board.
4. Content architecture.
5. 20-card sprint.
6. Hook Room.
7. Writers' room and copy gates.
8. Weekly batch and engagement plan.
9. Proof capture and next-month loop.

## Proof Plan

| Proof Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Before/after | Generic AI draft vs treated OS draft |
| Hook improvement | 10 rejected hooks plus selected hook rationale |
| Voice evidence | Lines tied to real source material |
| Research evidence | Opportunity board with citations |
| Pipeline signal | DMs, comments, saves, calls, or profile clicks |
