## Post 4: Prompt Vs. Primitive

### Hook

A prompt asks AI to do the task.

A work primitive explains the work.

### Publishable Post

A prompt asks AI to do the task.

A work primitive explains the work.

That distinction matters if you want AI to do anything more valuable than produce a decent first draft.

A prompt says:

"Write a follow-up email."

A work primitive says:

- who the follow-up is for
- what the person already believes
- what source of truth controls the message
- what the AI may infer
- what it must not invent
- what a good response must include
- what would make the response unusable
- when the AI should stop and ask a human

The first one produces text.

The second one gives AI operating meaning.

This is why better prompts help until the work gets complex.

Then the prompt starts carrying too much weight.

It has to explain the audience, the offer, the tone, the proof, the boundaries, the examples, the edge cases, and the quality bar in one fragile instruction.

That is not a system.

That is a long request hoping nothing gets missed.

The work primitive is the upgrade:

small enough to use,
clear enough to test,
specific enough for AI to follow,
and honest enough to say where a human still needs to decide.
