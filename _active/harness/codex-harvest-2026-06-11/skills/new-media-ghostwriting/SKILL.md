Elevates ghostwriting from "write LinkedIn posts for someone" to "build their entire media empire." Combines Nicolas Cole's voice capture and ghostwriting methodology with a16z's new media doctrine to deliver a premium service that:

1. **Captures** the client's authentic voice (Nicolas Cole methodology)
2. **Positions** them as a Joe Rogan-ready founder (a16z founder go-direct)
3. **Maps** their content to oral/written culture physics (a16z culture matrix)
4. **Architects** their content city with trust pathways (Grace Andrews)
5. **Produces** platform-native content in their voice across all channels
6. **Protects** them with crisis protocols and context-length defense

## Anti-Patterns

- ❌ Writing posts in YOUR voice, not THEIRS (Nicolas Cole's cardinal sin)
- ❌ Cross-posting the same content to different platforms (a16z's cardinal sin)
- ❌ Polishing the client into blandness (unscripting, not scripting)
- ❌ Starting with short-form (always written-first, then oral extraction)
- ❌ Ignoring crisis preparation (every public figure needs a flood-zone protocol)
