# Cinematic Documentary — Skill Manifest

## Overview
Cinematic Documentary Architecture extracted from David Gelb (director of *Jiro Dreams of Sushi*, creator of *Chef's Table*). The only storytelling system in the Antigravity roster built on *directorial* architecture — structuring complete narrative arcs around real people using feature-film methodology while maintaining radical fidelity to truth.

**Core Thesis**: Documentary is character architecture, not information delivery. Build your story the way a director builds a film: character-first, emotion-over-information, with the audience as a character on a parallel transformation journey.

## Source Material
- **Primary**: YouTube interview (13,946 words) — [How I Write](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U3d0T2b_8rc&t=18s)
- **Referenced Works**: *Jiro Dreams of Sushi*, *Chef's Table* (6 seasons), *Neat* (whiskey documentary)
