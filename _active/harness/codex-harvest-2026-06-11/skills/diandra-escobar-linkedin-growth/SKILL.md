# Diandra Escobar — LinkedIn Growth Mastery

**Invocation**: `/deploy-skill diandra-escobar-linkedin-growth`

Diandra Escobar is the founder of Distinctiva, a LinkedIn growth agency that has taken clients from zero to hundreds of thousands of impressions — serving brands like Semrush, Backlinko, and HeyReach. Built to close to $1M in revenue from organic LinkedIn alone after getting fired at 23. Her current system covers five connected layers: (1) **profile and LinkedIn SEO setup** before posting, (2) **growth formats** that borrow existing attention through entities, (3) **content system infrastructure** that eliminates reliance on motivation, (4) **strategic commenting** as first-90-day distribution, and (5) a **steal-like-an-artist** methodology for infinite content sourcing.

**Core Philosophy**:
- Never create attention from scratch — redirect attention that already exists
- Post type is the #1 determinant of reach, not writing quality
- Every post has one job: Growth, Authority, Conversion, or Personal; use the video-grounded 40/30/20/10 monthly mix unless a stage-specific audit says otherwise
- Write the body first, mine for the hook second — best hooks already exist in your content
- Extract content from existing material (calls, docs, experts) — don't create from scratch
- Infrastructure (Notion + Drive + Claude) replaces motivation with process
- Strategic commenting is a distribution channel equal to posting
- 90-day sprint to content-market fit: posts 1-20 practice, 21-40 voice, 41+ momentum
- Content is the ad; the profile is the landing page. Fix headline, about, featured, services, and CTA before sending traffic there.
