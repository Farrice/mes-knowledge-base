# Kallaway Social Commerce 3.0 — SKILL.md

## Domain
Social Commerce & Distribution Economics — the monetization infrastructure that determines how much every view, follower, and piece of content is worth.

## Expert
Kallaway — Economic substrate beneath all content domains. Where Content Psychology determines *what to create* and Word Mastery determines *how to write it*, Social Commerce determines **how much each piece of content earns**. This domain answers: **"How do you turn distribution skill into $500K-$1M/year?"**

## Core Thesis
Social media has entered its third era — **Social Commerce 3.0** — where four converging tailwinds (instant affiliate infrastructure, agentic commerce, production cost collapse, AI visual recognition) are making the skill of generating attention worth **5-10x** what it was. The value isn't in the following. The value is in the **skill of building a following**. That skill is now the most investable business asset in the creator economy.

## Governing Metric
**Revenue Per View (RPV)** — dollars generated per view across all monetization rails. The thesis: RPV is about to 5-10x as monetization friction approaches zero. Strategy should maximize RPV, not just views.

## The Decision: Which Role Are You?
Every player in Social Commerce 3.0 occupies one of three roles:
- **Brand Owner**: Supply products, own the margin. Requires product development + fulfillment ops.
- **Content Creator**: Make content, tag products, earn affiliate commissions. Requires distribution skill.
- **Facilitator**: Connect brands and creators, take a cut. Requires relationships + marketplace knowledge.

Don't mix roles until you've mastered one. Each role has different skill requirements, capital needs, and scaling dynamics.
