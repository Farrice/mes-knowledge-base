# Attention Hijack Hooks - Hidden Knowledge

- A famous brand is only useful if it unlocks a specific point of view for the target reader.
- Newsjacking fails when the writer reports the news instead of naming the downstream implication.
- Namejacking works best when the referenced person carries the audience you want and the post adds a lens they would recognize as substantive.
- Trendjacking should prove pattern recognition, not trend-chasing.
- The strongest hooks usually come from the body, especially from a number, contradiction, reader confession, or proof object.
- Punchy plus Context is the safest default, but repeated use can become visual wallpaper.
- Single-Line Bomb hooks are rare because they spend the whole first screen on one line.
- Stacked hooks need one organizing axis. Random short lines are not a stack.
- Platform fit is not just length. It is width, line breaks, semantic signal, rhythm, and reader state.
