# Genius Patterns

## 1. Reveal the Frame
- **What it does**: Makes the audience aware of the medium or ritual they are inside.
- **Deploy when**: Content, launch, product, or agent workflow feels ordinary.
- **Success metric**: The audience sees the situation differently after the reveal.

## 2. Use the Real Object
- **What it does**: Turns the device, browser, package, chat box, form, camera, controller, or room into part of the experience.
- **Deploy when**: There is a truthful physical or platform context to use.
- **Success metric**: The moment could not work the same way in another medium.

## 3. Address the Real User
- **What it does**: Shifts from addressing an abstract persona to the person currently reading, clicking, waiting, scrolling, presenting, or deciding.
- **Deploy when**: The audience is hiding behind a role.
- **Success metric**: The user feels seen without feeling invaded.

## 4. Frustration Becomes Discovery
- **What it does**: Converts a small obstacle into a satisfying "I get it" moment.
- **Deploy when**: A tiny pause, puzzle, or missing piece can create earned insight.
- **Success metric**: The payoff is memorable enough to justify the friction.

## 5. Medium Betrayal
- **What it does**: Lets the format briefly violate its expected behavior.
- **Deploy when**: A page can act like a conversation, a video can notice time, or an agent can expose the workflow frame.
- **Success metric**: The break feels surprising but legible.

## 6. Creator Peeking Through
- **What it does**: Lets the user feel the person behind the work.
- **Deploy when**: A product or content asset feels too polished, generic, or AI-like.
- **Success metric**: The work feels more human without becoming self-indulgent.

## 7. Perspective Payoff
- **What it does**: Makes the surprise deliver an insight about the product, problem, audience, or belief.
- **Deploy when**: The temptation is to make a clever trick.
- **Success metric**: The user remembers the meaning, not just the trick.

## 8. Return to Utility
- **What it does**: Brings the user back to the main task after the break.
- **Deploy when**: The fourth-wall moment could derail the experience.
- **Success metric**: The user is more engaged and still knows what to do next.
