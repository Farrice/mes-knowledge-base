# Source Map

## Accepted Sources

| Source | Best Use |
|---|---|
| Article or blog post | Strongest default. Produces source-backed carousels that route back to owned content. |
| Raw idea | Fast ideation and first draft. Needs stronger review. |
| Video transcript or ledger | Good for extracting demonstrated workflows, talks, and tutorials. |
| Client insight | Good for case-study, proof, and sales enablement carousels. |
| Brand/style reference | Feeds style-board and GPT Image 2 prompt direction. |

## Evidence Rules

- Do not claim a hidden prompt was recovered.
- Label video-derived mechanics as observed or inferred.
- Keep strategic synthesis separate from direct source claims.
