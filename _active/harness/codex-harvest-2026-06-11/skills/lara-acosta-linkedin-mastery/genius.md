## 23 Genius Patterns

### P1: Position Before Content
Never content before positioning. Complete: "I help [WHO] achieve [WHAT] through [HOW] so they can [OUTCOME]"

### P2: Dual-Persona Content Design
Every post serves TWO audiences: **ICP** (who pays) + **IFP** (who shares). Always ask "Who will share this AND who will buy from this?"

### P3: The 4-3-2-1 Content Ratio
4 posts/week. Three types: Educational, Storytelling, TAM (trend/news commentary). Calendar: Mon(educate) Wed(story) Thu(TAM) Sun(flex).

### P4: First-Principle Hook Testing
Hook must work if reader has no idea who you are. **Test**: Remove name/avatar — does it still stop scrolling?

### P5: Authority Trigger Stack
Stack: specific numbers > vague claims, named clients > "clients", timeline specificity > "eventually", process transparency > black-box results.

### P6: Social Proof Archaeology
Dig for existing proof: testimonials in emails/DMs, before/after transformations, small specific results, borrowed authority.

### P7: Content Pillar Excavation
Three pillars from expertise intersection: (1) What you DO, (2) What you BELIEVE, (3) What you ENABLE.

### P8: The SLAY Framework
**S**tory → **L**esson → **A**pplication → **Y**our turn (CTA/question).

### P9: Voice Extraction Protocol
Capture: signature phrases, sentence rhythm, emotional intensity moments, passion topics, humor patterns.

### P10: Zero-to-Authority Roadmap
Days 1-30: Foundation (positioning, pillars, first 30 posts). Days 31-60: Momentum (consistency, engagement, lead magnets). Days 61-90: Authority (thought leadership, speaking, press).

### P11: Lead Magnet Virality
Must do both: capture email AND go viral as content. Design as standalone content pieces, not just gated assets.

### P12: Format Emulation
See viral format → strip topic → identify structure → apply to your domain → test with your hook.

### P13: Algorithm Optimization
LinkedIn rewards: early engagement (first hour critical), dwell time, native content (no external links), authentic voice, conversation starters.

### P14: Ghostwriting Voice Deployment
Voice profile → topic approval → authenticity check → iteration loop. Never sanitize voice — amplify patterns.

### P15: Authority Acceleration
Fast-track: borrow authority (interview experts), stack micro-proof, niche down aggressively, daily consistency signal.

### P16: Client Attraction Content
Problem-aware → solution-aware → decision-stage → trust-building. Map posts to buyer journey stages.

### P17: The Founder's Content Gap
Founders: know too much (curse), assume context, skip "obvious", fear being salesy. First 30 posts address all four.

### P18: Attention Arbitrage Economics *(2026)*
LinkedIn = 1B users consuming, almost none creating. One post (20 min) can reach 10K qualified ICPs. Unmatched attention economics.

### P19: Three Growth Shapes *(2026)*
**Steady**: reliable, slow. **Exponential**: hockey stick at content-market fit. **Identity Crisis**: grow→pivot→reset→repeat. If identity crisis: STOP pivoting.

### P20: Profile-as-Algorithm-Signal *(2026)*
LinkedIn uses domain expertise signals for distribution. Headline = pain+audience+proof. Banner = social proof. Featured = proof of work. All aligned to single niche. **Mental Folder Test**: "Could a friend describe what I do in one sentence?"

### P21: The 1+3 Comment Formula *(2026, Jasmin Alic)*
1 substantive comment + 3 replies to OTHER people's comments on same post = 4x profile views daily. Replying to others' comments shows you to their network.

### P22: Post & Ghost Kill Switch *(2026)*
Never post and disappear. Protocol: 20 min engaging BEFORE post → reply to every comment in first 30 min. Can't engage? Don't post.

### P23: The Revenue Bridge Architecture *(2026)*
```
Content → Profile → Lead Magnet → Email List → Sales
(attract)  (convert)  (capture)     (nurture)    (sell)
```
70-80% revenue from email, not LinkedIn. Content's job: attract, NOT sell. One lead magnet post/week + permanent on profile.

## Signature Moves

1. **Double-Tap Hook** — 8-word hook + line break + 8-word rehook, forces "see more" → Deploy on first two lines of any post
2. **Engagement Sprint** — 30-60 min active replying post-publish, never post and ghost → Deploy immediately after posting
3. **Audience Funnel Weave** — Broad hook → narrow pain → niche advice (Broad-Narrow-Niche) → Deploy on educational/"how I" posts
4. **Relatable Win Reframing** — Shift wins from "I/we" to impact on others/humble origin → Deploy when sharing achievements
5. **Visual Scan Path** — Single-sentence lines, double line breaks, F-shape reading → Deploy on all post body formatting

## Quality Rubric

| Criterion | 4 (Acceptable) | 7 (Good) | 10 (Savant) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Hook Virality | Vague, >8 words, no rehook | Clear <8 words, redundant second line | 8-word hook + 8-word rehook creating open loop |
| Narrative Flow (SLAY) | Facts collection, no story arc | SLAY applied, clunky transitions | Seamless S→L→A→Y with high-engagement CTA |
| Audience Progression (B-N-N) | Too broad or too niche | Hooks broadly, abrupt niche transition | Universal hook → smooth narrow → deep niche value |
| Sentiment Control | Direct success announcements | "We/team" framing, still self-centered | Success through family/origin/client impact lens |
| Readability (F-Shape) | Dense paragraphs, mobile unfriendly | Some breaks, still chunky | 1-2 sentence blocks, double breaks, mobile scan |
| IFP Activation | Written solely for buyers | IFP identified, not designed for | Explicitly designed for sharing by amplifiers |
| Engagement Protocol | Posts without engagement plan | Some comments, inconsistent first hour | Every comment for 30-60 min, active reply expansion |
