# Kallaway Content System — Hidden Knowledge

- **The system protects attention.** AI is valuable because it gives the creator more time for the take, the metaphor, the evidence, and the final judgment.
- **Validation creates courage.** A creator with a proven topic and format can push harder on the contrarian point.
- **The format can create the idea.** Restrictive formats often reveal the sharpest angle by forcing a choice.
- **Bad scripts are often upstream failures.** Generic AI writing usually means the user skipped topic, format, take, evidence, hook, and voice model.
- **Views are the fallback metric.** Conversions per view matter most; followers per view are a stronger proxy than raw views.
- **Carry-forward is selective.** A winner earns three slots in the next batch, not the entire calendar.
