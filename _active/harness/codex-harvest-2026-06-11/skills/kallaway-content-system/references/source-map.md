## Carry-Forward Rules

- **0 videos over 5x:** Rebuild the next batch from scratch and inspect obvious failures.
- **1+ videos at 5x-10x:** Carry the topic and format into three of the next ten videos with slight variation.
- **1+ videos above 10x:** Rerun the same topic and format. Treat it as a high-confidence winner.
- **Goal:** Find three differentiated winning combinations that can power future batches.
