# Authenticity Boundary Audit

## Pre-Flight Gate
Inputs required:
- Draft or planned communication.
- Relationship to recipient or audience.
- Whether AI was or may be used.
- Trust stakes: low, medium, high, intimate, public, legal, commercial.

Load `../genius.md` and `../references/source-map.md`.

## Skill Acquisition
Use Martel's authorship boundary: AI can help with instrumental text; it damages trust when human sincerity is the promised value.

## Execution
1. **Classify the Work**: Instrumental, commercial, personal, intimate, artistic, ceremonial, or hybrid.
2. **Name the Trust Promise**: What does the recipient believe they are receiving from a person?
3. **Mark Safe Assistance**: Identify research, formatting, grammar, or legal drafting tasks AI can support.
4. **Mark Human-Only Zones**: Identify sentences, memories, judgments, vows, apologies, praise, or confession that must come from the human.
5. **Rewrite for Human Proof**: Produce a version that restores visible human effort.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Contract | AI assistance is acceptable; human review is mandatory |
| Wedding Note | Human-only sincerity; AI may help organize, not author feeling |
| Founder Post | AI may structure; lived details must be human |
| Client Email | AI can clarify; relationship-specific judgment must be human |
| Art | AI assistance must be disclosed or kept outside authorship-critical choices |

## Output Requirements
Produce:
- `Work Classification`
- `Trust Promise`
- `Safe AI Zones`
- `Human-Only Zones`
- `Rewrite or Guidance`
- `Disclosure Recommendation` if relevant

## Quality Gate
- The answer is not anti-tool theater.
- The trust context drives the decision.
- Human specificity is restored where needed.
- The output would not feel like a betrayal if the process were known.
