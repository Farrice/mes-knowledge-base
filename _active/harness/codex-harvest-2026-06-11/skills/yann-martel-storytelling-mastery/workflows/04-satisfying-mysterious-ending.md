# Satisfying Mysterious Ending

## Pre-Flight Gate
Inputs required:
- Story or draft summary.
- Reader's central expectation.
- Current ending, if available.
- Desired after-feeling.

Load `../genius.md` and `../references/quality-rubric.md`.

## Skill Acquisition
Use Martel's ending standard: closure without full possession.

## Execution
1. **Name the Contract**: State what the reader expects to be resolved.
2. **Separate Closure From Explanation**: Decide what must close and what can remain alive.
3. **Find the Final Image or Action**: Choose a concrete moment that carries the ending.
4. **Remove False Goodbyes**: Cut sentimental closure that violates the truth of the story.
5. **Write the Ending Design**: Produce a final beat sequence and, if requested, a draft ending.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Fiction | Resolve arc, leave moral or symbolic mystery |
| Essay | Answer the central question, leave the larger question open |
| Speech | Give the audience a final image and next action |
| Sales Narrative | Resolve belief tension, leave future possibility |
| Memoir | Close the scene, not the entire life |

## Output Requirements
Produce:
- `Reader Contract`
- `Must Resolve / May Remain Open`
- `Final Image or Action`
- `Ending Beat Sequence`
- `Draft Ending` when enough source material exists

## Quality Gate
- The ending is not random.
- The ending is not over-explained.
- The core expectation is fulfilled.
- One meaningful mystery remains.
