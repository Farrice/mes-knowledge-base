# Sublime Question Engine

## Pre-Flight Gate
Inputs required:
- Draft, premise, or concept.
- The larger concern behind it.
- Audience belief context.
- Tone limit: quiet, comic, sacred, skeptical, commercial, or literary.

Load `../genius.md`.

## Skill Acquisition
Use Martel's pairing of art and religion as forms of magical thinking: both move beyond the merely rational to recover awe, sanity, and question.

## Execution
1. **Find the Surface Topic**: State what the piece appears to be about.
2. **Find the Larger Question**: Ask what the topic asks about life, death, love, freedom, faith, identity, or meaning.
3. **Anchor in Concrete Form**: Choose a scene, object, animal, ritual, line, or image to carry the question.
4. **Remove Sermonizing**: Cut abstract declaration and keep the question alive through story.
5. **Produce the Depth Map**: Build a usable question architecture for the piece.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Fiction | Let the question live through action and image |
| Essay | Use a concrete instance before the universal turn |
| Brand Story | Connect customer problem to a real human question |
| Speech | Give the audience a question they can carry |
| Personal Writing | Protect sincerity; avoid performance wisdom |

## Output Requirements
Produce:
- `Surface Topic`
- `Larger Question`
- `Concrete Carrier`
- `Anti-Sermon Cuts`
- `Depth Map`
- `Suggested Lines or Beats`

## Quality Gate
- The question is specific.
- The depth is earned by concrete material.
- The piece does not preach.
- Awe is present without vagueness.
