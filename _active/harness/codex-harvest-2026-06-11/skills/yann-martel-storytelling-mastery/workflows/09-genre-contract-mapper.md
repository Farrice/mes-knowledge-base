# Genre Contract Mapper

## Pre-Flight Gate
Inputs required:
- Project premise.
- Genre, market, or reader category.
- Current ending or promised outcome.
- Where the piece may violate expectation.

Load `../genius.md` and `../references/source-map.md`.

## Skill Acquisition
Use Martel's distinction between genre comfort and literary disorientation.

## Execution
1. **Identify the Contract**: Name what readers believe they are buying or entering.
2. **Separate Required Promises From Optional Patterns**: Decide what must be fulfilled.
3. **Map Possible Betrayals**: Identify twists or omissions that would feel unfair.
4. **Choose Fulfill, Bend, or Rewrite**: Decide how the piece will treat its contract.
5. **Adjust Structure and Ending**: Produce specific changes to align promise and payoff.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Genre Fiction | Fulfill the core emotional promise |
| Literary Fiction | Create a new contract through form and tone |
| Nonfiction Book | Solve the promised reader problem without becoming a pill-shaped product unless intended |
| Sales Page | Align headline promise with proof and final ask |
| Course | Make transformation promise match actual instruction |

## Output Requirements
Produce:
- `Reader Contract`
- `Must Fulfill`
- `Can Bend`
- `Would Betray`
- `Recommended Promise Strategy`
- `Ending / Payoff Adjustments`

## Quality Gate
- The core promise is explicit.
- Surprise does not become betrayal.
- The ending honors the chosen contract.
- Reader trust is preserved.
