# Yann Martel Genius Patterns

## 1. Envelope-First Architecture
Turn research into discrete, sortable fragments before drafting. The value is not paper nostalgia; it is visible composition.

## 2. Planned Journey, Live Discovery
Know the destination and major stops. Let local discoveries reshape the path during drafting.

## 3. Scene as Story Unit
Build from bounded units of time, action, place, pressure, and reader movement.

## 4. Co-Creation Through Withdrawal
Remove enough description and explanation for the reader to imagine and infer.

## 5. Animals as Prejudice-Free Masks
Use animals and symbolic figures to bypass defensive reaction and carry human meaning with distance.

## 6. Ordinary Vessel, Extraordinary Pressure
Keep the character open enough for identification when the main subject is the pressure they face.

## 7. Facts Are Not Story
Facts become powerful only when arranged into question, movement, image, or emotional consequence.

## 8. Punctuation as Tempo
Treat commas, periods, paragraph breaks, dashes, and restraint as the score that controls breath.

## 9. Rule Awareness Without Rule Slavery
Know the rule, break it for a purpose, and keep enough handrails for the reader.

## 10. Genre as Reader Contract
Reader expectations must be fulfilled, bent, or rewritten consciously.

## 11. Ending as Resolved Mystery
Close the central expectation while leaving one meaningful question alive.

## 12. Art as Gift, Not Transaction
Give full effort to the work, then release reader interpretation from author control.
