# Yann Martel Source Map

## Source Anchors

### Research Architecture
Martel describes spending years gathering material, then physically cutting notes into fragments and sorting them into envelopes. The method is not nostalgic paper craft; it is a way to make research compositional.

### Scene Units
He treats stories as scenes: bounded units of time, action, and place. Chapters are containers; scenes are the working material.

### Co-Creation
He contrasts books with movies by arguing that prose invites the reader to imagine the visuals, emotional cues, and interior experience. The reader's work creates attachment.

### Animals and Allegory
Animals lower defensive reaction and carry symbolic charge. Martel uses them as vehicles for human thought, as wild presence, and as a way to make heavy material speakable.

### Openings Versus Endings
He does not overstate opening-line rules. He cares more about whether everything works and whether the ending concludes expectations while leaving mystery.

### Punctuation
He treats punctuation as tempo. Commas create flow, periods shape sentence length, paragraph breaks control reading stamina, and exclamation marks manipulate emotion if overused.

### Rules
He resists formula but accepts rules as things worth knowing so they can be broken deliberately.

### Genre and Literary Fiction
Genre fiction fulfills a familiar product contract. Literary fiction can disorient, but must still reward attention.

### Authorship and AI
He accepts AI for instrumental work but rejects it where the value is human connection, sincerity, or creative authorship.

### Reviews
He classifies reviews by whether they are positive or negative for the right or wrong reasons, then returns to the deeper principle: art is a gift, not a demand for reciprocation.

## Deployable Translation
- Notes become envelopes.
- Envelopes become scenes.
- Scenes become a journey.
- The ending becomes the compass.
- Gaps invite co-creation.
- Animals create distance.
- Punctuation scores tempo.
- Rules become deliberate choices.
- AI is filtered by trust context.
- Review response is separated from craft.
