# Yann Martel Quality Rubric

| Criterion | 1-3 Weak | 4-6 Workable | 7-8 Strong | 9-10 Masterful |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fragment Sorting | Raw notes remain a pile | Notes are grouped loosely | Notes are sorted by story job | Every fragment has a role, and sequence is visible |
| Destination | No clear ending pressure | Ending is named but generic | Ending answers the main expectation | Ending satisfies and leaves a meaningful after-question |
| Reader Co-Creation | Everything is explained | Some restraint exists | Reader must infer and imagine | Reader feels partial ownership |
| Scene Integrity | Sections are abstract | Scenes have action or place | Scenes shift feeling or information | Scenes become the engine of thought |
| Allegorical Distance | Symbol is decorative | Symbol has surface fit | Symbol creates safer entry | Symbol makes the hard subject sharper and easier to enter |
| Character Vessel | Character is over-described or blank | Character is functional | Character is open and pressurized | Character becomes an entry point for universal pressure |
| Punctuation Tempo | Monotone or chaotic | Readable but flat | Varied breath and propulsion | Rhythm carries meaning |
| Rule Break | Experiment feels random | Experiment is interesting | Experiment serves the piece | Experiment feels inevitable |
| Genre Contract | Reader promise is unclear | Main promise is named | Promise is fulfilled or consciously rewritten | Contract is honored while adding surprise |
| Authorship Trust | Human voice feels outsourced | Human intent is present | Assistance, if any, is appropriate | The work feels like a personal gift |
