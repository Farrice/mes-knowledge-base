# Quality Rubric

| Criterion | Score 4 - Acceptable | Score 7 - Good | Score 10 - Savant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asset vs Activity Discipline | Names assets but still allows vague tasks. | Converts most work into reusable outputs. | Every recommendation leaves behind transferable value. |
| Ecosystem Thinking | Looks at one or two asset categories. | Connects weak categories to business constraints. | Shows how each asset strengthens or weakens the whole company. |
| Revenue Per Person Logic | Mentions revenue and headcount. | Uses RPP to guide lifestyle/performance choices. | Diagnoses stage, ambition mismatch, and next asset sequence from RPP. |
| Digital Scalability | Suggests documentation or content. | Turns assets into files, workflows, dashboards, media, or tools. | Designs assets that can be operated by people, AI agents, or partners without founder intervention. |
| Remarkability Standard | Improves mediocre assets. | Pushes assets toward shareable, buyer-valued quality. | Imports cross-industry standards and specifies what would make people talk about the asset. |
| Client Deployability | Produces useful advice. | Produces a client-ready report or roadmap. | Produces outputs that can be sold, delivered, reviewed, and expanded as a service. |
| Asset Zero Integration | Adds generic mission language. | Identifies a meaningful differentiator. | Finds the animating worldview or obsession that makes the whole OS feel inevitable. |
| Diligence Readiness | Mentions value or saleability. | Improves ownership, transferability, and risk visibility. | Makes the business easier to fund, value, buy, partner with, or inherit. |
| Sequencing | Lists many improvements. | Prioritizes by leverage, urgency, and dependency. | Designs a practical build sequence that raises RPP and reduces founder dependency fast. |

## Anti-Patterns

- Treating content volume as a content asset.
- Treating the founder's talent as a transferable asset without capture.
- Building all 24 assets at once.
- Recommending enterprise-grade assets for a lifestyle business.
- Recommending lightweight assets for a performance business.
- Scoring assets without evidence.
- Producing a plan that does not name owners, deadlines, or asset outputs.
- Stopping at "good enough" when the asset needs to become remarkable.
