## Scoring Scale

| Score | Meaning | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Missing | No clear asset exists. The business depends on founder memory, improvisation, or manual work. |
| 1 | Idea | The asset is discussed but not documented, reusable, or visible. |
| 2 | Draft | The asset exists but is rough, incomplete, or hard to reuse. |
| 3 | Commercial | The asset works in normal operations and supports sales, delivery, or team performance. |
| 4 | Scalable | The asset can be transferred digitally and used by others with little founder support. |
| 5 | Remarkable | People talk about it, share it, buy because of it, join because of it, or value the company more because it exists. |

## Diagnostic Tests

- **90-Day Founder Absence**: Would this asset keep creating value if the founder had limited phone and email access for 90 days?
- **Digital Transfer**: Can the asset be sent, taught, reproduced, or operated through a digital file, workflow, tool, or dashboard?
- **Buyer Value**: Would a buyer, lender, investor, partner, employee, or client value the company more because this exists?
- **Category Interaction**: Does the asset strengthen at least one other asset category?
- **Remarkability**: Would the target audience keep it, share it, reference it, or copy its standard from a different industry?
