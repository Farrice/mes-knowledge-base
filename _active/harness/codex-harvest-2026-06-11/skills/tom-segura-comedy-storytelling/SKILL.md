# Tom Segura Comedy Storytelling

Use this skill when writing needs observational humor, story-to-bit compression, a sharper reason for telling, stronger word choices, better text timing, or a diagnosis of why a comedic attempt feels flat.

This skill is grounded in `extractions/video-context/fN2lra4_mgk/` and the operating package at `_active/tom-segura-comedy-storytelling-os/`. It is a Tom-specific layer and a companion expansion to the existing comedy system. ## Core Method

1. Find the living phrase from conversation, not the polished phrase.
2. Locate the charge: annoyance, delight, shame, pressure, or strong opinion.
3. Say the obvious thing that everyone recognizes but no one has articulated.
4. Identify the reason for telling before telling the story.
5. Expand the story first, then cut it.
6. Add punch, surprise, callback, and word-level timing only after the story path is clear.
7. Encode cadence in text because the reader cannot hear delivery.
8. Send public, sales, or authority-building humor through the appropriate trust gate.

## Required Output Shape

Do not count this skill as used unless the output includes:

- audience and desired reader action or feeling
- original weak section
- weak-link diagnosis
- Tom mechanics used
- rewritten section or verdict
- before/after behavior delta
- proof gap or trust risk
- next gate

## Guardrails

- Do not imitate Tom's persona, profanity, cadence, or personal comedic subjects.
- Do not add jokes beside the draft's real tension.
- Do not use edginess as a substitute for surprise.
- Do not overuse callbacks.
- Do not treat transcript-only evidence as visual proof.
- Do not use humor that weakens trust, obscures the offer, or makes the writer seem less competent.
