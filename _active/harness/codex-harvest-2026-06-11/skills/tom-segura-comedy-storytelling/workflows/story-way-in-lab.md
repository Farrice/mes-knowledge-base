# Story Way-In Lab

## Use When

A story is interesting but the audience has no reason to care yet.

## Inputs

- Story summary.
- Audience.
- Desired reader state.
- Why the writer thinks the story matters.

## Steps

1. Write the plain event: what happened.
2. Write the pressure: what could go wrong, be proven, be lost, or be exposed.
3. Write the self-position: why this story belongs to this speaker.
4. Write three possible way-in lines.
5. Select the way-in that creates the cleanest stakes.
6. Cut the story to only the details needed for that way-in and payoff.

## Output

```markdown

- **Plain event:**
- **Pressure:**
- **Why this speaker is telling it:**
- **Way-in candidates:**
- **Selected way in:**
- **Cut story path:**
- **Potential punch/turn:**
- **Behavior delta:**
```

## Quality Gate

Pass only if the selected way-in makes the story feel necessary before the anecdote begins.
