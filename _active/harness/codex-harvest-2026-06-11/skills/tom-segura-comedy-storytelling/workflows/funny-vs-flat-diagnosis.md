# Funny-Vs-Flat Diagnosis

## Use When

A joke, hook, aside, callback, caption, or humorous passage is not landing, feels try-hard, or might weaken trust.

## Inputs

- Draft line or passage.
- Intended audience.
- Intended laugh or emotional response.
- Stakes and context.
- Brand voice or speaker identity.

## Steps

1. Decide whether the line has charge or indifference.
2. Check whether the observation is recognizable or generic.
3. Check whether the punch is expected.
4. Check whether the setup is missing, overexplained, or already obvious.
5. Check whether the risk appears before rapport.
6. Check whether word choice is accurate but dull.
7. Decide whether to keep, cut, or rewrite.
8. If rewriting, repair the smallest failing dimension first.

## Output

```markdown

- **Verdict:** Keep / Cut / Rewrite
- **Primary failure:**
- **Audience recognition:**
- **Charge level:**
- **Surprise level:**
- **Trust risk:**
- **Repaired line:**
- **Why this should land better:**
- **Next gate:**
```

## Quality Gate

Fail if the diagnosis says only "add more humor." The output must name the exact comedy failure class.
