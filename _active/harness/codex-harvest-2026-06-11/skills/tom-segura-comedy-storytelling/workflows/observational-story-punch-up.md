# Observational Story Punch-Up

## Use When

A social post, essay, script, email, or marketing draft is accurate but flat, too generic, too earnest, or missing the lived-world detail that makes readers feel seen.

## Inputs

- Audience.
- Desired reader action or feeling.
- Draft or weak section.
- Trust risk: low, medium, or high.
- Proof assets if the draft sells or claims authority.

## Steps

1. Identify the neutral or generic sentence.
2. Name the charge underneath it: annoyance, delight, embarrassment, pressure, envy, irritation, or strong opinion.
3. Find the recognition line: what the audience already knows but rarely says.
4. If a story is present, write the reason-for-telling before the anecdote.
5. Expand the relevant moment briefly, then cut back to the smallest useful path.
6. Replace one to three dull words with more vivid, accurate words.
7. Add one surprise turn or restrained callback only if it serves the point.
8. Send trust-sensitive output to `sam-parr-copywriting-mechanics` or `/publishable-copy-gate`.

## Quality Gate

Pass only if the rewrite becomes more specific, more charged, and easier to feel without imitating Tom's persona.
