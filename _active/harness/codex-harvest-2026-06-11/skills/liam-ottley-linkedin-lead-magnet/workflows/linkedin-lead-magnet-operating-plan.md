### 2. Commercial Positioning

Produce:

- One-line positioning: "I help [ICP] achieve [buyer outcome] through [method]."
- Monetizable expertise map: 8-12 knowledge assets that change a buyer outcome.
- Offer advantage map: why this expert's way is different, faster, safer, more specific, or more profitable.
- Proof inventory: results, examples, assets, client moments, personal story, or observed process.

Quality gate: every expertise item must name a buyer outcome and a commercial consequence.

### 3. Strategic Arbitrage Map

Produce 10-15 post angles across:

- trend jacking
- authority jacking
- story-led lesson
- problem-agitate-solution
- "how I" proof post
- buyer misconception
- timely industry shift
- lead magnet teaser

For each angle, identify:

- attention carrier
- buyer relevance
- owned insight
- commercial bridge

### 4. Content Lanes

Define 3-5 lanes:

- Lead Magnet Lane: posts that tease, validate, or explain the opt-in asset.
- Authority Lane: posts that prove expertise and make the lead magnet desirable.
- Story Lane: personal moments linked back to the business problem.
- Arbitrage Lane: trends or authority references reframed through the expert's method.
- Conversion Lane: direct but useful posts that invite a DM, checklist, audit, or call.

Keep the lane set smaller than the idea set. Lanes are recurring routes; ideas are one-time executions.

### 5. Lead Magnet Bridge

Choose one primary lead magnet concept. Score it:

| Gate | Pass Criteria |
|---|---|
| Buyer outcome | Solves a painful or valuable problem |
| Specificity | Names a concrete ICP, problem, or moment |
| Fast value | Delivers a quick win or diagnosis |
| Proof fit | Shows why this expert is credible |
| Paid-offer bridge | Makes the next step obvious |

If the bridge is weak, route to Stockton or B.A.N.K. before drafting more content.

### 9. Validation Checklist

Validate before treating the plan as ready:

- Does every post connect to a buyer outcome?
- Does the lead magnet bridge to the paid offer?
- Are hooks specific and short enough to create curiosity?
- Does each post have a rehook?
- Are stories linked back to the reader and business problem?
- Does the AI prompt set contain enough identity, offer, proof, and voice context?
- Is there a 30-minute post-publish engagement window?
- Is the result internal only unless the user explicitly approves publishing?
