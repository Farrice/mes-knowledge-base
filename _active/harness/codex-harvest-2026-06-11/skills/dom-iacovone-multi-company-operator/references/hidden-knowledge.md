# Hidden Knowledge

## Finance Sees The Campaign Before Marketing Admits It

The strongest truth surface is often not the campaign dashboard. It is contribution margin, revenue mix, deductions, and channel economics.

## Founder Presence Has A Shadow Cost

The founder can think they are helping while reducing dissent, creativity, and executive ownership.

## Retail Is Delayed Feedback

Retail work can be loaded months before revenue reflects it. Operators need a lag map or they misread momentum.

## Buyers Optimize For Their Shelf, Not Your Brand

A buyer can be rational and still wrong for your product. The operator's job is to translate buyer constraints without surrendering product truth.

## Deductions Compound Into Exit Value

Small percentage leaks in gross-to-net can become large enterprise-value losses when multiplied through valuation.

## Channel Logos Are Not Execution

Getting into a major retailer is not the same as having store-level execution, replenishment, visibility, and incentives.

## Relationships Become Scarcer As AI Gets Better

When execution and intelligence become more available, trusted access and offline relationships become more valuable.

## Slow Assets Need Inflection Diagnosis

A slower brand is not automatically a failed brand. Determine whether the problem is economics, positioning, timing, channel load, or founder attention.
