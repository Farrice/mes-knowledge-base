# Dom Iacovone Multi-Company Operator - Genius Context

## Core Genius

Dom's operating edge is portfolio-level execution without founder over-presence. The repeatable method is:

1. Reduce the year to a few strategic growth blocks.
2. Review financial truth weekly.
3. Gate new products through differentiation, margin, and channel fit.
4. Let strong executives argue and operate without founder suppression.
5. Treat retail, trade spend, logistics, and buyer requests as operating systems, not isolated events.

## Operating Lens

Ask these questions first:

- What is the business actually trying to grow this year?
- Which four priorities deserve the operating calendar?
- Which weekly numbers expose truth early?
- Which product ideas pass the stage gate?
- Which meetings should the founder leave?
- Which channel has the right execution incentives?
- Which leaks destroy margin or enterprise value?
- Which future distribution is already loaded but not yet visible in revenue?

## Signature Moves

- Convert chaos into four annual initiative blocks.
- Use CM1/revenue mix as early signal.
- Separate buyer input from product truth.
- Treat trade spend as valuation impact.
- Design for store execution, not only shelf access.
- Let the team argue before the founder enters.
- Preserve optionality before exit or major channel expansion.
