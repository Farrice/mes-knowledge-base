# Nicolas Cole — Digital Product Creation Strategy

Nicolas Cole has generated millions in digital product revenue across every vehicle type: ebooks, paid newsletters, cohort-based courses, communities, and high-ticket coaching. This skill encodes his product progression framework plus the course-module transformation layer: choosing the right vehicle, pricing it correctly, sequencing products, and designing modules that move buyers from intention to action.

**Core Insight**: Digital products are a game of context, vehicle selection, and action design. The product is not "what the buyer learns"; it is what the buyer can do after the product removes doubt, confusion, unanswered questions, and execution friction.
