# Implementation: Deploying Brandon Jacoby Taste Mastery

## Operating Sequence

1. **Name the consequence**: What must this work change for the user, buyer, founder, or system?
2. **Classify the defaults**: Which choices are proven patterns, inherited habits, brand constraints, or invention candidates?
3. **Make visible artifacts**: Create enough concrete options to shift the conversation from abstract opinion to evidence.
4. **Find the weird C**: Search for the route that is not the obvious A/B split.
5. **Score craft and consequence**: Require both felt quality and real impact.
6. **Use AI to remove limits**: Build or prompt the missing tool, but keep human taste in charge.
7. **Push only where it matters**: Choose the places where good enough would make the work replaceable.
8. **Simplify to access**: Remove complexity that makes the work harder to use, buy, share, or understand.

## System Deployment

Use this skill as a taste layer after a primary workflow has produced a draft:

- Product or offer draft -> `/jacoby-craft-consequence`
- Generic AI output -> `/jacoby-good-enough`
- New workflow or command -> `/jacoby-system-elevation`
- Founder idea or new product -> `/jacoby-worldbuild`
- Visual or design direction -> `/jacoby-pattern-invention` then `/design-taste-gate`
- Personal taste system -> `/jacoby-taste-profile` then `/taste-roadmap`

## Quality Markers

A Brandon-grade output has:

- A clear reason for every convention it keeps.
- A clear reason for every rule it breaks.
- At least one artifact or decision that could not have come from category average.
- A direct tie between craft and business, product, audience, or emotional consequence.
- Human judgment over AI output.
- A simple path for real users to experience the quality.

## Failure Markers

The workflow failed if:

- It praises "taste" without changing a decision.
- It adds novelty everywhere.
- It treats polish as a substitute for consequence.
- It skips artifacts and stays in advice.
- It lets AI output define the standard.
- It cannot explain where the work moved beyond good enough.
