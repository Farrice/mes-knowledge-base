# Brandon Jacoby - Good-Enough Breakthrough

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `../genius.md`. Start from the premise that good enough may be acceptable, but the user is asking for the places where average is too expensive.

## Input Required

- Draft output, screenshot, copy, strategy, workflow, or product concept
- Intended user or buyer
- Why this draft matters
- Known constraints

## Workflow

1. **State What Already Works**: Preserve the parts that are functional and clear.
2. **Find Replaceable Parts**: Identify phrases, layouts, steps, visuals, or decisions that any average tool could have produced.
3. **Locate Breakthrough Zones**: Choose the few areas where craft changes trust, desire, usability, or memorability.
4. **Generate Push Directives**: Produce specific revisions, not general critique.
5. **Add Signature**: Insert a point of view, artifact, metaphor, interaction, proof angle, or system behavior that makes the work ownable.
6. **Retest Simplicity**: Remove any elevation that makes the work harder to use.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Content | Replace generic insight with a sharper lived distinction |
| Design | Replace polish-only feedback with hierarchy, feeling, and memory directives |
| Offer | Replace broad value props with a specific buyer moment and consequence |
| Workflow | Replace instruction with an artifact-producing judgment step |

## Output Requirements

- What to preserve
- Replaceable average parts
- Breakthrough zones
- Revision directives
- Signature move
- Before/after summary

## Quality Gate

Fails if it edits for polish but does not make the work more ownable, useful, memorable, or consequential.
