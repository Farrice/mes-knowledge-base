# Oren Solo AI Marketing Machine

Use this when a founder, creator, operator, freelancer, service provider, or small business needs one person plus AI to produce serious marketing output without pretending they have a full pod.

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `../genius.md` and this workflow. If the user is designing a team with two or more dedicated content people, route to `oren-content-team-architecture` after this workflow defines the solo baseline.

Do not recommend more channels until the referral gate, monthly message loop, and current funnel are graded.

## Input Required

- Offer or business model
- Primary sales goal for the next 30-90 days
- Current channels and rough performance
- Customer/referral status
- Existing assets: testimonials, case studies, content, product photos, sales calls, emails, landing pages, FAQs
- Current funnel, email/CRM, ad, and analytics state
- Available weekly marketing hours
- Budget for creators, designers, paid media, or tools
- AI/tool access and current brand-voice material
- Creator, vendor, customer, or partner access

## Execution

### 1. Set The Solo Operator Constraint

State:

- available weekly hours,
- whether the operator is founder, employee, freelancer, or agency partner,
- what must happen for leads, sales, customers, or referrals to increase,
- what the operator cannot realistically do this week.

If the user has less than 20 hours, compress the calendar by preserving order: referral gate, messages, creative, funnel/email, then paid/creator/organic as capacity allows.

### 2. Run The Referral And Word-Of-Mouth Gate

Assess:

| Gate | Question |
|---|---|
| Inherent sharing | Does use naturally invite another person? |
| Billboard effect | Does the buyer visibly show the product, result, site, asset, or transformation? |
| Customer love | Do users talk about it without being asked? |
| Content-cycle fit | Does it appear naturally in customer posts, screenshots, routines, or stories? |
| Incentive | Is there a referral, affiliate, credit, gift, maintenance, or commission reason to share? |
| Ask timing | Is the ask made when the customer is happiest? |

Output the highest-conversion referral fix before adding channel work.

### 3. Build The Monthly Message Intelligence Loop

Mine the next month's message angles from:

- value to customer,
- trending context,
- customer support questions,
- social comments,
- competitor claims,
- sales objections,
- customer language,
- seasonal or cultural moments,
- launch or budget timing.

Convert findings into a **Message Board**:

| Message | Evidence | Buyer question answered | Surface to deploy | Test metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### 4. Set Up The AI Brand-Voice And Copy Machine

Create the minimum context pack an LLM needs before producing marketing copy:

- brand context and offer,
- customer personas or buying contexts,
- proof points and objections,
- voice references,
- conversion principles or copy standards,
- banned phrases and AI-sounding patterns,
- review checklist.

Then assign AI to draft, adapt, or pressure-test:

- homepage and landing page copy,
- ads,
- emails,
- scripts,
- sales collateral,
- FAQ and objection answers,
- creator briefs.

Human gate: every AI output must be reviewed for accuracy, specificity, voice, proof, and commercial intent before use.

### 5. Build The 40-Hour Solo Marketing Week

Default allocation:

| Lane | Hours | Job |
|---|---:|---|
| New creative | 8 | Ideate, recruit, brief, assign, follow up, and make creative for ads and organic tests. |
| Performance | 4 | Load new creative, check analytics, learn platform basics, record notes, and avoid ads-manager overwork. |
| Funnel | 4 | Improve landing pages, forms, abandoned cart, lead capture, checkout, and message match. |
| Collateral and email | 4 | Broadcast, segment, automate, build sales templates, proof docs, FAQs, and enablement assets. |
| Influencer and creators | 8 | Source creators, send outreach, handle contracts, schedule, track deliverables, and brief assets. |
| Logistics | 8 | Meetings, follow-ups, admin, approvals, asset handoffs, and project management. |
| Organic validation | 4 | Publish founder/customer/content tests that validate messages and feed the creative loop. |

Adapt hours only after naming what tradeoff changes.

### 6. Connect The Remarketing And Reuse Loop

Every winning message or asset should move through the loop:

1. Monthly message board
2. AI-assisted copy and brief
3. New creative or creator assignment
4. Paid test or organic validation
5. Landing page or website answer
6. Email/CRM sequence or sales collateral
7. Retargeting, referral, or creator reuse
8. Learning note back into next month's message board

If a message is not being reused across surfaces, the operator is leaving compounding value on the table.

### 7. Produce The First 7-Day Sprint

Output the next week as a calendar:

| Day | Focus | Actions | Ship by end of day |
|---|---|---|---|

Include no more than three measurable outcomes for the sprint.

### 8. Write The Do-Not-Do List

Kill or defer:

- channels that do not feed the current revenue path,
- meetings beyond the logistics budget,
- tool setup that does not produce assets,
- overproduced shoots before message validation,
- organic posting that does not test a message or feed paid/funnel work,
- broad brand strategy debates when the message board has unanswered buyer questions.

## Quality Gate

The workflow fails if it only gives marketing ideas. It passes when the user can open their calendar, AI project, creator tracker, landing page backlog, email plan, and ad account and know exactly what to do next.
