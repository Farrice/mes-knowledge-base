# Oren Archetype Offer Stack

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `../genius.md`. Social content should make the offer easier to understand, trust, and buy. If the offer is vague, repair the offer before scaling content.

## Input Required

- Brand or personal brand
- Selected social archetype or current content role
- Target customers/personas
- Current products/services/offers and prices
- Sales motion: self-serve, sales call, retail, wholesale, DMs, email, or enterprise
- Current CTA and landing page/email/SMS setup

## Execution

1. **Clarify The Commercial Job**
   - What should social make easier: awareness, trust, lead capture, product education, sales call, purchase, repeat buy, or referral?

2. **Package The Offer**
   - Name the core offer.
   - Define promise, timeline, deliverables, proof, and best-fit buyer.
   - Choose competitive basis: speed, price, or skill.
   - Add entry offer if the core offer is high-ticket.

3. **Align Archetype To Offer**

| Archetype | Offer Logic |
|---|---|
| Oracle | Sell expertise, curation, audit, advisory, premium product knowledge |
| Performer | Sell products through affinity, cast, recurring world, and omnipresent use |
| World Builder | Sell taste, creative direction, cultural relevance, and brand affinity |
| Catalyst | Sell transformation, training, implementation, community, and progress |
| Helper | Sell selection, convenience, practical results, and buying confidence |

4. **Map Personas**
   - Segment only where message changes.
   - Define pain, desired outcome, buying trigger, proof needed, and CTA.

5. **Build The Lander/Comms Plan**
   - Landing page headline/subhead/proof/CTA per meaningful persona.
   - Email/SMS angle by segment.
   - Paid ad angle by segment.
   - Organic CTA by content pillar.

6. **Create CTA Rhythm**
   - Profile CTA
   - Weekly story/post CTA
   - Monthly direct offer push
   - Lead magnet or entry offer
   - Proof/case study loop

## Content Type Adaptations

| Sales Motion | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Self-serve product | Use entry/core ladder, buying guides, comparison, and retargeting content |
| High-ticket service | Use diagnostic content, proof, entry workshop/audit, and repeated call CTA |
| Software | Use persona-specific landers, workflow demos, and Helper/Oracle content |
| Local service | Use locality, practical questions, before/after proof, and direct inquiry CTA |
| Creator offer | Use niche depth, personality, named package, and explicit availability |

## Output Requirements

Produce:

- **Commercial Job**
- **Core Offer Package**
- **Entry/Core/Aspirational/Halo Ladder**
- **Archetype-To-Offer Alignment**
- **Persona Message Map**
- **Landing Page Plan**
- **Email/SMS/Ad Angle Map**
- **CTA Rhythm**

## Quality Gate

The stack fails if a follower cannot tell what to buy or why the brand is qualified. It passes when content, offer, persona, proof, and CTA make one commercial argument.
