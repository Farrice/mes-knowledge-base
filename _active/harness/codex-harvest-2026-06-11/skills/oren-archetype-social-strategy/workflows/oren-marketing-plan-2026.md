# Oren Marketing Plan 2026

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `../genius.md`. Do not add more initiatives until the current funnel is graded. Most brands should improve existing channels before adding new channels.

## Input Required

- Brand/business model and annual or quarterly goal
- Current marketing channels and performance notes
- Current content pillars, if any
- Major product/service launches
- Cultural, seasonal, and industry calendar moments
- Sales history or known strong/weak periods
- Team capacity and budget

## Execution

1. **Grade The Marketing Funnel**
   - Awareness: how attention is generated.
   - Consideration: how people are educated and affinity is built.
   - Conversion: how sales, leads, demos, or purchases are asked for.
   - Grade each A-F and explain why.

2. **Map Initiative Options**
   - Plot current and potential initiatives on two axes: normie to fringe, sales to awareness.
   - Keep initiatives leadership can approve separate from career-bet experiments.
   - Select at most one or two new initiatives for the planning period.

3. **Grade The Content Funnel**
   - List existing content pillars.
   - Assign each to awareness, consideration, conversion, or retention.
   - Decide what to improve, keep, replace, or add.

4. **Build The Calendar Matrix**
   - Culture moments
   - Industry moments
   - Sales heat map
   - Social wins and weak spots
   - Launches
   - Missed opportunities from last year

5. **Plan Asset Repurposing**
   - Inventory internal assets, creator assets, customer assets, event assets, and product assets.
   - Assign where each can be reused: PDPs, landing pages, ads, email, stories, organic, sales enablement.

6. **Build Campaign Skeletons**
   - Teaser
   - Unveil
   - Proof/BTS
   - External creator/media/community involvement
   - Sustain
   - Conversion push

## Content Type Adaptations

| Planning Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Small business | Focus on existing funnel gaps, one new initiative, and bottom-funnel asks |
| Enterprise | Clarify approval-safe versus fringe bets and assign budget percentage |
| Creator/consultant | Treat offers, series, trips, services, and launches as real campaigns |
| E-commerce | Tie calendar to buying seasons, product launches, PDP assets, email/SMS, and retargeting |
| B2B/service | Emphasize consideration, sales materials, proof, lead magnets, and direct asks |

## Output Requirements

Produce:

- **Funnel Gradecard**
- **Initiative Map**
- **Selected Initiatives**
- **Content Funnel Map**
- **Calendar Matrix**
- **Asset Repurposing Plan**
- **Campaign Skeletons**
- **90-Day Action Plan**

## Quality Gate

The plan fails if it lists everything the brand could do. It passes when it names what the brand should do next, what it should not do, and why the team can execute it.
