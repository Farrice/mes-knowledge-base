# Oren Archetype Fit Map

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `../genius.md`. Do not choose the most exciting archetype first. Choose the archetype the brand can repeatedly execute with the people, proof, and tolerance it actually has.

## Input Required

- Brand, offer, and business model
- Current social channels and what is underperforming
- Target customer and buying context
- Available resources: experts, creators, designers, locations, products, proof, budget, approval tolerance
- Current content examples or notes, if available

## Execution

1. **Diagnose Current Role**
   - State what role the brand currently appears to play on social.
   - Mark whether it is coherent, accidental, mixed, or absent.
   - Identify the gap between what the brand wants to be and what the audience can currently feel.

2. **Map Resource Reality**
   - Expert depth: founder, staff expert, technical team, curator, or none.
   - Creator depth: on-camera talent, recurring cast, peer creator, customer creators, or none.
   - Visual world: product, location, process, inventory, events, community, or abstract service.
   - Production capacity: daily scrappy, weekly batch, monthly campaign, agency-supported, or low/no production.
   - Risk tolerance: safe, moderate, brave, or moonshot.

3. **Score The Five Archetypes**

| Archetype | Fit Test | Content Job | Resource Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oracle | We can teach the world better than others | Explain, compare, predict, contextualize | Real expertise or credible curation |
| Performer | We can make a show people would watch | Entertain while product stays omnipresent | Cast, location, humor, recurring premise |
| World Builder | We can create a world beyond the product | Build affinity through culture, story, art, ideas | Creative direction and risk tolerance |
| Catalyst | We can move the customer toward a better self | Inspire, prove, guide, challenge | Transformation path and community proof |
| Helper | We can make buying/doing easier | Tips, tutorials, stories, comparisons | Peer creator or practical customer knowledge |

4. **Choose Primary And Secondary**
   - Pick one primary archetype and, only if useful, one secondary.
   - Explain why the rejected archetypes are wrong now.
   - Name the resource gap that would need to close before those rejected archetypes become viable.

5. **Define The Archetype Card**
   - Role in customer world
   - Audience promise
   - Required content pillars
   - Required talent/owners
   - What the brand should stop posting
   - What proof must show up repeatedly
   - First 10 content ideas

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Founder-led brand | Weight the founder as expert, character, or operator; do not assume they must be the whole brand |
| Retail or marketplace | Favor Oracle or Helper using inventory explanations, comparisons, and buying guidance |
| B2B/service | Favor Oracle, Catalyst, or Helper with proof, process, and client-context education |
| Software | Use Helper for customer workflows, Oracle for category education, or World Builder only with creative strength |
| Local business | Use Performer for staff/location, Helper for practical buyer questions, or Oracle for craft expertise |

## Output Requirements

Produce:

- **Current Social Role Diagnosis**
- **Resource Reality Map**
- **Archetype Fit Scores**
- **Primary Archetype Verdict**
- **Secondary Archetype, If Any**
- **Rejected Archetypes And Why**
- **Archetype Card**
- **First 10 Content Ideas**
- **30-Day Next Move**

## Quality Gate

The verdict fails if it could apply to any brand in the category. It passes only if the chosen archetype clearly fits the brand's actual resources, customer role, proof, and business model.
