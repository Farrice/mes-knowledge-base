# Oren Brand Media Archetype Stack

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `../genius.md` and `../../oren-content-team-architecture/genius.md`. Choose archetype before team design. Do not copy a media-company structure that does not fit the archetype.

## Input Required

- Brand and selected archetype, or current social diagnosis
- Current marketing team and agency partners
- Current owned organic, paid, creator, landing page, and email/SMS setup
- Available internal creators, external creators, founder, experts, or locations
- Growth objective and constraints

## Execution

1. **Confirm The Archetype**
   - Run a light archetype fit check.
   - Name the primary role and the reason the customer would follow.

2. **Map The Media Flywheel**
   - Owned organic
   - Owned paid
   - Brand world / external organic
   - Conversion pages
   - Email/SMS communications
   - Content/product development connection

3. **Design The Pod**
   - Strategist/social manager: briefs, analytics, posting, calendar, coordination.
   - Creator: shooting, editing, on-camera or production.
   - External contributors: customer creators, influencers, specialist creators.
   - Shared resources: editor, designer, producer, creative director, agency.

4. **Assign Archetype-Specific Roles**

| Archetype | Pod Emphasis |
|---|---|
| Oracle | Expert access, research, explainer formats, category fluency |
| Performer | Cast, recurring premises, fast scripting, location access |
| World Builder | creative director, producer, cultural research, visual standards |
| Catalyst | proof capture, community highlights, transformation stories |
| Helper | peer creator, customer questions, tutorials, buying guidance |

5. **Set Cadence And Goals**
   - Weekly creative review
   - Weekly analytics review
   - Weekly research session
   - Friday recap
   - 10 strong concepts per week as aspirational pod standard, adjusted for capacity

6. **Define Anchors**
   - Fame, influence, story, production elements, or events.
   - Choose one or two; do not chase all five.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Org Stage | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| 1-2 people | Founder/creator plus simple cadence and one repeatable series |
| 2-5 people | One pod with internal creator plus a few external contributors |
| Multi-pod | Shared editors/designers/producers and channel-specific pods |
| Agency-supported | Agency must understand creator flywheel and paid/organic asset reuse |
| Enterprise | Add creative director and leads only after pods prove output |

## Output Requirements

Produce:

- **Archetype Confirmation**
- **Media Flywheel Map**
- **Pod Structure**
- **Role/Hiring Specs**
- **Weekly Cadence**
- **10-Concept Weekly Standard**
- **Anchor Strategy**
- **90-Day Buildout**

## Quality Gate

The plan fails if it says "hire a social media manager" as a complete solution. It passes when strategy, creation, analytics, external contributors, and conversion surfaces are connected.
