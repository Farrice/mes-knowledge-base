# Oren — Archetype Social Strategy

Build brand social systems that stop trying to be everything at once. This companion module turns Oren's archetype method into deployable workflows for choosing the role a brand plays in the customer's world, mapping the resources needed to execute that role, and connecting social content to pricing, personas, offers, and revenue.

## Core Philosophy

- Archetype is a role in the customer's world, not a vibe label.
- Strategy must fit available talent, budget, proof, and production reality.
- Strong social is not "more posts"; it is a content operating model with a clear job.
- Organic content, paid ads, landing pages, email/SMS, and offer design should use the same archetype logic.
- Solo-AI marketing is a constrained operator mode: use AI to multiply message, copy, creative, funnel, email, creator, and remarketing execution without pretending one person is a scaled pod.
