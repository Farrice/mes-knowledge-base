# Rachel Woods: AI Operations Mastery

Rachel Woods is the AI Operations Architect — founder of The AI Exchange, creator of the CRAFT Cycle methodology, and a leading voice on AI playbooking. Extracted from deep-dive sessions and playbooking research covering AI operations, AI-first company transformation, AI career positioning, and repeatable AI playbooks. Rachel's core insight: AI doesn't replace people, it replaces tasks — and the person who maps the work, defines what good means, and teaches AI the process captures the value.
