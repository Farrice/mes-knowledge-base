# Dan Martell — Business Scaling & Operational Leverage

Dan Martell is a 3x SaaS exit CEO who built $100M+ in enterprise value by doing less, not more. His core genius is **constraint-first systems building** — subtract complexity, find the single bottleneck, design flywheels that run without you. Author of *Buy Back Your Time*, advisor to hundreds of founders. Every framework prioritizes sequencing (right action, right time) over volume.
