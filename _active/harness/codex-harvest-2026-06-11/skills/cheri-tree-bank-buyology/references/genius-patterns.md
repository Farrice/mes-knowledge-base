# B.A.N.K. Genius Patterns

## 1. Code Before Pitch

The buyer's code determines the presentation. A pitch delivered before code diagnosis is a guess.

**Deploy**: Before writing copy, sending outreach, taking a call, or building a funnel.

## 2. Values Beat Demographics

Demographics describe who the buyer is. B.A.N.K. values describe why the buyer says yes or no.

**Deploy**: When building ICPs, avatars, segments, and offer angles.

## 3. One Offer, Four Doorways

The same offer can be entered through safety, victory, care, or certainty.

**Deploy**: When creating landing pages, emails, sales scripts, and content variants.

## 4. Sequence Creates Meaning

Credibility, emotion, and logic matter differently depending on the code. Wrong sequence can make true claims feel irrelevant.

**Deploy**: When writing Power Scripts, DMs, webinars, and sales presentations.

## 5. Objection as Code Mismatch

Many objections are not rejections of the offer. They are signals that the buyer has not heard the offer in their code language.

**Deploy**: When handling "too expensive," "need to think," "send info," "not sure," or "not now."

## 6. Ask When Possible, Infer When Necessary

Direct value ranking is the best path. Forensic coding is a field method when direct intake is impossible.

**Deploy**: In sales calls, networking, social research, and high-value prospect prep.

## 7. BANKify the System

B.A.N.K. is not only a sales call trick. It should shape CRM fields, list segments, emails, landing pages, proof assets, events, and follow-up.

**Deploy**: When building repeatable sales and marketing systems.

## 8. Weakest Code Becomes Blind Spot

The seller's lowest code is often where they under-communicate. Action sellers may skip details; Knowledge sellers may over-explain; Blueprint sellers may undersell excitement; Nurturing sellers may avoid decisive asks.

**Deploy**: When auditing why certain buyers are not converting.

## 9. Proof Must Match the Buyer

Blueprint wants stability proof. Action wants status and momentum proof. Nurturing wants trust and mission proof. Knowledge wants evidence and reasoning.

**Deploy**: In testimonials, case studies, decks, pages, and sales follow-up.

## 10. Translation Preserves Fit

The method should help the right buyer understand the right offer. It should not pressure a poor-fit buyer into a bad decision.

**Deploy**: As the ethical quality gate for every workflow.
