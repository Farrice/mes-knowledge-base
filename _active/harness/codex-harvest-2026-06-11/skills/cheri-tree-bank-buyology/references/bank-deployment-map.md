# B.A.N.K. Deployment Map

## Asset Translation Rules

| Asset | Blueprint | Action | Nurturing | Knowledge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lead magnet | Checklist, calculator, plan, risk audit | Challenge, shortcut, opportunity map, status score | Purpose guide, community diagnostic, relationship tool | Report, teardown, benchmark, data-backed framework |
| Landing page | Process, guarantees, proof, budget, FAQ | Big result, speed, exclusivity, social buzz | Mission, trust, story, people helped | Mechanism, data, logic, comparisons, evidence |
| Email | Step-by-step, clear next action | Fast win, urgency, bold CTA | Warm story, service, invitation | Analysis, proof, detailed reasoning |
| Sales call | Agenda, risks, budget, timeline | Bottom line, outcome, choice, momentum | Relationship, purpose, values, care | Data, assumptions, decision criteria, due diligence |
| Social content | "How to avoid..." and "Checklist..." | "Fastest way..." and "Big move..." | "For the person who cares..." | "What the data says..." and "Breakdown..." |
| Objection handling | Reduce risk and show process | Restore momentum and show upside | Rebuild trust and safety | Supply proof and clarify logic |

## Power Script Ordering

Use Aristotle's persuasion parts as ingredients:

- **Ethos**: credibility, reputation, qualifications, track record.
- **Pathos**: emotion, care, aspiration, pain, identity.
- **Logos**: logic, evidence, mechanism, numbers, reasoning.

Code ordering:

- **Blueprint**: Ethos -> Logos -> Pathos. Prove stability first, then show the process, then make the safe future feel real.
- **Action**: Pathos -> Ethos -> Logos. Lead with desire, speed, status, and winning; support with proof; keep logic brief.
- **Nurturing**: Pathos -> Ethos -> Logos. Lead with care, mission, and people; prove integrity; then show the practical path.
- **Knowledge**: Logos -> Ethos -> Pathos. Lead with the reasoning; prove expertise; keep emotion restrained and tied to rightness.

## Direct Code Collection

When possible, ask the prospect to rank the four value cards. Explain that the ranking helps you serve them better and saves time. This activates curiosity, self-interest, learning, process, relationship, and efficiency at once.

## Forensic Code Collection

When direct collection is not possible, use social profiles, websites, emails, calendar behavior, public content, call notes, purchase history, and objection style. Always separate observed evidence from interpretation.

## CRM Deployment

- `bank_secondary`
- `bank_code_confidence`
- `bank_evidence`
- `bank_next_test`
- `bank_asset_route`

Minimum automations:

- Route new leads to a code-specific welcome sequence.
- Assign sales prep notes by primary code.
- Tag content engagement by code signal.
- Store direct self-assessment results when available.
- Re-score when new evidence contradicts the current code.
