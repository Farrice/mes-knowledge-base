# Sales Education Map

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this workflow when the user has an offer, service, product, or sales asset and needs to know what the buyer must understand before they are ready to buy.

If the buyer or offer is unclear, run `/specificity-ladder` or an offer-positioning workflow first.

## Execution

1. Define the buyer, offer, current buying stage, and sales medium.
2. State the buyer's current default-no logic.
3. Map the problem side:
   - problem they know or do not know they have
   - reasons why the problem exists
   - financial, time, status, or opportunity consequences
   - second-order emotional impact
4. Map the solution side:
   - category of solution
   - why this category works
   - practical benefits of the category
   - ultimate positive outcome
5. Identify the education gaps that would stop the buyer from moving.
6. Turn each gap into a teaching beat, proof asset, question, example, or story.
7. Sequence the beats for the target medium.
8. Define the clean next step that should come after education.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Type | Education Shape |
|---|---|
| Sales call | Conversational arc with questions, screen-share proof, and buyer reflection |
| Sales page | Section-by-section problem and category education |
| Launch emails | One education beat per email, building belief over time |
| DM sequence | Short diagnostic questions and one clear teaching point per touch |
| LinkedIn content | Public education of one problem/reason/consequence at a time |
| Proposal | Problem-category recap before scope and price |

## Output Requirements

Return:

1. Buyer starting belief.
2. Eight-part sales education map.
3. Missing beliefs and likely objections.
4. Proof or teaching asset needed for each gap.
5. Recommended sequence by medium.
6. Clean next step.

## Quality Gate

Pass only if the buyer can understand the problem and category before the seller talks about themselves. If the offer appears before the category is taught, revise.
