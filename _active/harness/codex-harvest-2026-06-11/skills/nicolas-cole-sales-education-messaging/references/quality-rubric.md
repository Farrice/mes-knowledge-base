# Quality Rubric

Score each criterion from 1-5.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem awareness | Problem is vague or seller-centered | Buyer can recognize the issue | Buyer sees the exact problem in their own world |
| Reasons why | Cause is asserted | Cause is plausible | Cause is clear, specific, and tied to buyer behavior or market reality |
| Consequence | Cost is generic | Cost is relevant | Cost includes loss, opportunity cost, and decision urgency |
| Emotional impact | Emotion is melodramatic or absent | Some second-order impact | Emotional cost is grounded and buyer-specific |
| Category clarity | Offer is pitched too early | Category is named | Category is taught before the seller appears |
| Category power | Benefits are generic | Mechanism is understandable | Buyer understands why this category solves this problem |
| Objection diagnosis | Objections are rebutted | Objections are grouped | Objections become missing questions, beliefs, or comparisons |
| Proof design | Proof is thin | Proof supports main claims | Proof addresses each missing belief directly |
| Buyer agency | Copy pressures the buyer | Buyer has some autonomy | Buyer can make an informed yes or no |
| Medium fit | Same script pasted everywhere | Adapted to format | Call, page, email, DM, or content format carries the education arc naturally |

## Pass Standard

- 42+ = ready to deploy.
- 34-41 = usable, but run one more objection and proof pass.
- Below 34 = rebuild the education arc before polishing copy.
