# Tom Segura Observational Story Layer

## Purpose

Use this reference when `robert-mack-comedy-writing` or `/comedy` needs Tom Segura's source-backed observational and story-writing mechanics as a supporting layer.

Robert Mack remains the architecture owner for comedy construction. Tom's layer supplies:

- conversation-based premise discovery
- annoyance and opinion as charge
- saying the obvious before someone else owns it
- reason-for-telling before story
- long-first story compression
- word-choice timing
- callback restraint
- written-comedy timing compensation

## Composition Rule

Use Mack to ask: what kind of comedy structure is this?

Use Tom to ask: what real observation, irritation, story pressure, or word choice makes this feel lived-in?

Use Sam Parr when the humor must support proof, buyer action, or conversion.

Use `/publishable-copy-gate` before public or revenue-sensitive output.

## When To Load Tom

- The piece is a story but has no reason for telling.
- The draft is correct but emotionally neutral.
- The joke feels added from outside the material.
- The draft has an observation but no charge.
- The line is accurate but the word choice has no comedic force.
- The writing depends on delivery the reader cannot hear.

## Guardrails

- Do not imitate Tom's persona.
- Do not overfit all comedy to observational storytelling.
- Do not skip Mack's structure check when the joke architecture itself is broken.
- Do not skip Sam or publishable gates when trust, proof, or action matters.
