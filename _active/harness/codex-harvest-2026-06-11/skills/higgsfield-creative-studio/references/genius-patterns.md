# Higgsfield Creative Studio - Genius Patterns

## 1. Source Skill Sovereignty
**Execute**: Use the orchestrator for routing and stacking, but let `gpt-image-2-director` and `marketing-studio-director` control their own final prompt formats.
**Deploy When**: Every request routed through this skill.
**Success Metric**: Single-prompt outputs remain paste-ready for the target platform.

## 2. Strategy Spine Before Asset Stack
**Execute**: For full asset systems, define audience, angle, and emotional target before generating still and video prompts.
**Deploy When**: Campaign packages, product ad kits, still-to-video systems, launch creative.
**Success Metric**: Still and video prompts feel like one campaign, not two disconnected assets.

## 3. Still Locks the Visual World
**Execute**: Use GPT Image 2.0 to define key visuals, product frames, design boards, storyboards, or layout-heavy assets before video prompt generation when the user asks for a full system.
**Deploy When**: Still-to-video workflows and prompt stacks.
**Success Metric**: Video prompts inherit the same product placement, mood, palette, and visual hierarchy as the still.

## 4. Marketing Studio Converts Motion Intent
**Execute**: Translate the chosen concept into the Marketing Studio preset that best matches the ad job: UGC for trust, Tutorial for use, Unboxing for reveal, Hyper Motion for energy, TV Spot for brand, Try On for fit.
**Deploy When**: Any combined package with a video component.
**Success Metric**: The video prompt has a clear motion grammar and product placement throughout.

## 5. QA as Integration Check
**Execute**: Use Satori or Creative Review only to catch hierarchy, generic AI tells, fidelity errors, and brand drift after prompts are drafted.
**Deploy When**: Refinement requests, high-stakes ads, multi-format campaign packages.
**Success Metric**: QA recommendations are concrete and do not overwrite the source prompt-director constraints.
