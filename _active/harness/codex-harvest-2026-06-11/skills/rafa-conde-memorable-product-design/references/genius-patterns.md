# Genius Patterns

## 1. Feeling as the Design Brief
- **What Rafa does**: Starts from the emotional residue the user should carry away.
- **Executable behavior**: Name one target feeling and one remembered sentence before proposing UI or copy.
- **Deploy when**: Product direction, onboarding, launch, prototype presentation, or agent workflow design.
- **Success metric**: A stranger can describe what the product made them feel without being prompted.

## 2. Sell the Idea Artifact
- **What Rafa does**: Converts features into persuasive artifacts, often video, instead of relying on mockups.
- **Executable behavior**: Produce an ad, short demo, or narrative prototype with stakes, use context, pacing, and emotional payoff.
- **Deploy when**: A feature needs buy-in or a prototype is not self-evident.
- **Success metric**: Viewers understand why the feature matters before they ask how it works.

## 3. First-Run as Mythology
- **What Rafa does**: Treats first use as a memory-setting event.
- **Executable behavior**: Design the opening sequence to establish the product's promise, tone, and user identity.
- **Deploy when**: Onboarding, activation, product tours, waitlists, or first customer handoff.
- **Success metric**: The first session gives users a story they can retell.

## 4. Tiny Signature Detail
- **What Rafa does**: Adds small, human, often playful details that make a simple product feel authored.
- **Executable behavior**: Add one detail in motion, sound, microcopy, empty state, icon, default, or unlock moment that intensifies the core feeling.
- **Deploy when**: Product works but lacks affection or recall.
- **Success metric**: Users mention the detail without being asked.

## 5. Scope as Charm Protection
- **What Rafa does**: Protects the simple center of a product instead of treating every request as progress.
- **Executable behavior**: Reject, defer, or hide additions that dilute the main feeling or make the product harder to explain.
- **Deploy when**: A small app, feature, or agent workflow is gaining extra controls.
- **Success metric**: The product remains explainable in one sentence.

## 6. One-Step-Ahead Magic
- **What Rafa does**: Likes experiences that feel like they read the user's mind.
- **Executable behavior**: Add smart timing, defaults, handoff, or anticipation that reduces friction while preserving control.
- **Deploy when**: UX can feel more responsive, personal, or alive.
- **Success metric**: The user feels helped, not manipulated.

## 7. Pacing as Product Feeling
- **What Rafa does**: Uses rhythm, cuts, silence, motion, and timing to communicate emotion.
- **Executable behavior**: Specify the beats of an interaction or video: slow down, reveal, pause, snap, confirm, resolve.
- **Deploy when**: Launch videos, onboarding, prototype demos, and celebratory states.
- **Success metric**: The artifact has a felt rhythm, not a dump of information.

## 8. Device and Body Context
- **What Rafa does**: Shows product ideas in the situation where they will be felt.
- **Executable behavior**: Present on-device, with a hand, face, room, task, time pressure, or social context when it clarifies the experience.
- **Deploy when**: Static UI loses the real meaning of the feature.
- **Success metric**: The viewer imagines themselves using it.

## 9. Product Personality Through Defaults
- **What Rafa does**: Uses defaults, labels, unlocks, and small decisions to communicate what the product is.
- **Executable behavior**: Make defaults and settings express the product's taste, not just neutral configuration.
- **Deploy when**: Settings, onboarding choices, empty states, preferences, or paid upgrades.
- **Success metric**: The defaults feel intentional and characterful.

## 10. Remembered Feelings Library
- **What Rafa does**: Mines old interactions, games, apps, and personal moments for emotional mechanics.
- **Executable behavior**: Keep examples of moments that made you feel something, then transfer the mechanism to the current product.
- **Deploy when**: Ideation feels obvious.
- **Success metric**: New ideas come from emotional mechanics, not category copying.
