# Rafa Conde: Memorable Product Design

This skill turns Rafa Conde's "Make your designs memorable" methodology into an operating system for creating product experiences that leave emotional residue. It is not general art direction. It is for software, prototypes, demos, onboarding, launches, and agent workflows where the goal is to make the idea felt clearly enough that people remember it and want to pass it on.

Use this skill when a feature, product, workflow, prototype, pitch, or launch is technically useful but emotionally flat.

## Routing

Use `creative-direction` for visual systems, art direction, AI image/video prompts, storyboards, and design specs. Use `james-i-bond-brain-glue` or `made-to-stick-messaging` for message stickiness. Use this Rafa Conde skill when the surface is a product or workflow and the missing piece is emotional memory: first-run feeling, tiny delight, simple-product charm, demo context, or "make the idea felt" presentation.

Use companion skill `rafa-conde-fourth-wall-experience-os` when the desired move is specifically frame-breaking: the product, content, launch, client experience, or agent workflow acknowledges the user's real context, medium, platform, behavior, or hidden assumption to create surprise plus insight.
