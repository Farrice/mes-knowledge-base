# Belief Pre-Lander Builder

> **Expert**: Vince Nijhof | **Tier**: Practitioner
> **Produces**: Pre-lander architecture and copy blocks

## Inputs Required

1. Product and offer
2. VSL or ad angle feeding the page
3. Buyer objections
4. Proof assets
5. Product mechanism

## Skill Acquisition

Load:

- `genius.md`
- `references/funnel-belief-mechanisms.md`

## Execution

1. **Match the ad promise**: Page must continue the same emotion and message.
2. **Open with recognition**: Show the buyer their problem in their own language.
3. **Explain why old solutions failed**: Create contrast before pitching.
4. **Show the mechanism visually**: Make the product reason believable.
5. **Stack proof**: Reviews, demos, authority, comparisons, guarantees.
6. **Handle objections in sequence**: Cost, trust, ease, claims, delivery, fit.
7. **Send warmer traffic onward**: Product-page CTA should feel earned.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Page Type | Best Use |
|-----------|----------|
| Listicle | Multiple reasons, objections, education |
| Tutorial | Product requires explanation |
| Authority page | Real expert or publication proof exists |
| Comparison | Competitor or old solution is obvious |
| Problem explainer | Market does not yet understand the mechanism |
