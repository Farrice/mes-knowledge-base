# Top-of-Funnel VSL Engine

> **Expert**: Vince Nijhof | **Tier**: Practitioner
> **Produces**: 3-5 minute VSL concepts, scripts, proof beats, and B-roll briefs

## Inputs Required

1. Product and offer
2. ICP and customer-language bank
3. Main pain/outcome
4. Proof assets and claims constraints
5. Destination pre-lander or product page

## Execution

1. **Choose the human moment**: The opening must feel like the buyer’s life, not an ad.
2. **Trigger recognition**: Create the “that is me” moment.
3. **Build emotional tension**: Show what the pain costs in daily life.
4. **Name failed attempts**: Make the buyer feel understood.
5. **Reveal the mechanism**: Explain why this product can work.
6. **Layer proof**: Reviews, demos, visuals, authority, before/after where valid.
7. **Bridge to pre-lander**: Make the next click feel like the natural next step.
8. **Brief B-roll**: Every scene must support the emotion or proof.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Product Type | VSL Emphasis |
|--------------|--------------|
| Health/supplement | Failed attempts, mechanism, safety, compliance-safe proof |
| Sleep | Daily fatigue, sensory frustration, morning payoff |
| Beauty | Confidence, routine, social visibility, proof |
| Utility/gadget | Before/after demonstration and friction removal |
| Premium product | Taste, identity, perceived value, craft |

## Quality Gate

- The product should not appear too early unless the category requires it.
- The VSL must create fresh audience entry, not just retarget warm buyers.
- Every proof beat must be true and usable.
