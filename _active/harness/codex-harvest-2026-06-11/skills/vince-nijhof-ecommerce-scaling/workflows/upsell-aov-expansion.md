# Upsell and AOV Expansion

> **Expert**: Vince Nijhof | **Tier**: Practitioner
> **Produces**: Upsell map, bundle logic, take-rate targets, test plan

## Inputs Required

1. Main product
2. Current AOV and conversion rate
3. Product catalog or possible accessories
4. Customer use case
5. Fulfillment and margin details

## Skill Acquisition

Load:

- `genius.md`
- `references/ecommerce-economics.md`

## Execution

1. **Find complements**: Products that make the main product better, easier, safer, cleaner, more portable, or longer-lasting.
2. **Rank by natural fit**: Avoid random discounts that weaken trust.
3. **Model margin lift**: AOV, take rate, incremental margin, shipping impact.
4. **Design offer stack**: Multi-pack, accessory, warranty, refill, subscription, bundle.
5. **Design test path**: Product page, cart, post-purchase, email, pre-purchase bundle.
6. **Set targets**: Take-rate, AOV lift, CVR impact, contribution-margin lift.
