# Brand x Performance Bridge

> **Expert**: Vince Nijhof | **Tier**: Stacking
> **Produces**: Brand trust and direct-response integration plan

## Inputs Required

1. Brand positioning and site
2. Current ads and funnels
3. Claims/proof/authority assets
4. Brand risks or compliance constraints
5. Paid growth target

## Skill Acquisition

Load:

- `genius.md`
- `references/funnel-belief-mechanisms.md`

## Execution

1. **Separate brand surface from conversion machinery**: Main site can stay clean while pre-landers educate and convert.
2. **Map trust-sensitive claims**: Which claims should the brand make, and which require real third-party authority?
3. **Design direct-response pages**: Listicle, tutorial, comparison, authority page.
4. **Protect brand rules**: Voice, visuals, truth, proof, no fake experts.
5. **Connect the flow**: Ad to pre-lander to product page without message mismatch.
6. **Define brand safety checks**: Claims, screenshots, authority, testimonials, offer pressure.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Brand Type | Adaptation |
|------------|------------|
| Premium DTC | Keep main site refined; run education through controlled pages |
| Health/supplement | Authority and compliance review are mandatory |
| Utility product | Demo and problem-solution can be more direct |
| Fashion/lifestyle | Protect taste and identity cues while proving value |

- Do not make fake authority pages.
- Do not cheapen the main brand to solve funnel laziness.
- Every strong claim needs proof.
