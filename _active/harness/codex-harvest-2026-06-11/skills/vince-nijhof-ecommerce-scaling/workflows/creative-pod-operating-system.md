# Creative Pod Operating System

> **Expert**: Vince Nijhof | **Tier**: Practitioner
> **Produces**: Pod structure, roles, KPIs, workflows, incentives

## Inputs Required

1. Current team structure
2. Monthly output and winner rate
3. Production formats
4. Current bottlenecks
5. Budget for hiring/incentives

## Skill Acquisition

Load:

- `genius.md`
- `references/creative-pod-model.md`

## Execution

1. **Diagnose role drag**: Identify strategists doing admin, editors guessing intent, or coordinators making strategy decisions.
2. **Design pod structure**: Assign strategists, editors, coordinators, and shared design support.
3. **Set output standards**: Match targets to format complexity.
4. **Design brief protocol**: Make strategic intent executable.
5. **Design dashboard**: Spend, winner count, CAC/ROAS bands, CTR/CVR where useful.
6. **Design incentives**: Reward ads that scale at target economics.
7. **Install review cadence**: Weekly winner teardown, data-bank refresh, next-concept planning.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Team Stage | Adaptation |
|------------|------------|
| Solo founder | One-person version with weekly batching |
| Small team | One pod with shared freelancer support |
| Scaling brand | Multiple pods with leaderboard and lead strategist |
| Agency | Pods by client, category, or format |
