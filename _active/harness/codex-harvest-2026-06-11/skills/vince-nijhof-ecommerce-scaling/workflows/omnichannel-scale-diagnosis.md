# Omnichannel Scale Diagnosis

> **Expert**: Vince Nijhof | **Tier**: Stacking
> **Produces**: Blended channel map and scale diagnosis

## Inputs Required

1. Channel revenue and spend
2. Meta, Google/YouTube, Amazon, TikTok Shop, email, search data
3. Stockout and inventory notes
4. Branded search and marketplace presence
5. Blended MER/ROAS targets

## Skill Acquisition

Load:

- `genius.md`
- `references/ecommerce-economics.md`

## Execution

1. **Start blended**: Judge total business response, not isolated attribution.
2. **Map channel mental state**: Meta scroll, YouTube attention, Amazon purchase intent, TikTok discovery, email retention.
3. **Identify spillover**: Meta/YouTube spend lifting Amazon, search, email, direct.
4. **Check marketplace protection**: Stockouts, branded search conquesting, Amazon listing quality.
5. **Find channel constraint**: Creative, stock, offer, trust, attribution, cash, or team.
6. **Prioritize next channel**: Do not spread too early before core acquisition works.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Revenue Stage | Adaptation |
|---------------|------------|
| 0-1M | Focus on one core paid channel and proof |
| 1-10M | Improve Meta/funnel economics before adding complexity |
| 10M+ | Add marketplace, YouTube, email, TikTok Shop, search protection |
| Mature brand | Optimize blended performance and stock reliability |
