## Execution

1. **Map AI-safe tasks**: Research clustering, review mining, script drafts, B-roll tagging, support triage, competitor summarization.
2. **Map human-only judgment**: Final angle selection, claims review, taste, emotional nuance, edit approval.
3. **Design project contexts**: Separate projects for scripts, funnels, store optimization, customer support, data bank.
4. **Train the model**: Feed brand voice, winning ads, losing ads, customer language, feedback, claim rules.
5. **Create feedback loop**: Every output gets corrected and returned to the system.
6. **Install adoption standard**: Training, certificates where useful, role-specific AI expectations.
7. **Protect quality**: AI accelerates inputs; humans remain accountable for intent and truth.

## Quality Gate

- AI cannot replace source evidence.
- AI cannot approve claims.
- Model training must include feedback, not just prompts.
