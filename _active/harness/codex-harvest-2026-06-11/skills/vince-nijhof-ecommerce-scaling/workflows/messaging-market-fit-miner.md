# Messaging-Market Fit Miner

> **Expert**: Vince Nijhof | **Tier**: Foundation
> **Produces**: Customer-language data bank, emotion map, angle bank, creative brief seeds

## Inputs Required

1. Product/category
2. Customer reviews, support tickets, comments, emails, or competitor sources
3. Current ads/funnels if available
4. Target ICP

## Skill Acquisition

Load:

- `genius.md`
- `references/data-bank-sources.md`
- `references/source-quotes.md`

## Execution

1. **Filter for specificity**: Prioritize 150+ character reviews and detailed support/comment language.
2. **Extract buyer identity**: Who is speaking and what life context surrounds the purchase?
3. **Extract pain and failed attempts**: What have they tried, tolerated, or stopped believing?
4. **Extract desired outcome**: What better scenario do they want?
5. **Extract objections**: Price, trust, mechanism, usage, shipping, claims, complexity, taste, comfort.
6. **Translate into emotions**: Fear, relief, confidence, convenience, control, identity, status, pride.
7. **Create angle bank**: Convert source language into ad angles, VSL arcs, pre-lander sections, and product-page copy blocks.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---------|------------|
| Physical product | Mine use moments, objections, sensory proof, shipping worries |
| Supplement/health | Mine failed attempts, skepticism, authority, compliance-safe proof |
| Beauty/fashion | Mine identity, confidence, social comparison, routine |
| Sleep/comfort | Mine daily fatigue, sensory details, failed sleep aids |
| B2B/SaaS | Mine workflow pain, risk, adoption objections, switching cost |

## Quality Gate

- Every angle must cite source evidence or be marked as a hypothesis.
- Generic praise is not evidence.
- The output must sound like buyers, not marketers.
