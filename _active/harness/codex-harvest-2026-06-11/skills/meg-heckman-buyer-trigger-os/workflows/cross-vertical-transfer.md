# Cross-Vertical Transfer

## Input

- Original product/offer/page/content/client objective.
- Target buyer.
- Conversion surface.
- Existing copy, creative, or concept.

## Transfer Map

| Apparel Mechanic | Cross-Vertical Translation |
|---|---|
| Shirt says something about buyer | Offer/page names who the buyer becomes or what they privately know. |
| Fast visual recognition | Headline or opening moment creates immediate "that's me" recognition. |
| Specific design | Specific buyer scene, pain, desire, or inside language replaces generic market label. |
| Public social currency | Buyer imagines looking smart, prepared, funny, advanced, or understood by sharing/using it. |
| Familiar plus unexpected | Known problem/category plus unexpected truth, mechanism, or angle. |
| Feeling before reason | Copy creates desire/relief/recognition before stacking rationale, proof, or features. |

## Steps

1. Load `references/source-ledger.md` and `references/genius-patterns.md`.
2. Name the source timestamp anchors used.
3. If the request includes `--research`, current buyer insights, trends, social listening, purchase intent research, competitor evidence, or market claims, run `workflows/research-backed-trigger-run.md` first.
4. Use evidence IDs or source URLs for current buyer language, trends, marketplace signals, competitor facts, or community claims.
5. Identify the buyer self-statement.
6. Compress the recognition moment.
7. Replace broad market language with a specific person and scene.
8. Add the future social/professional reaction.
9. Build familiar/twist phrasing.
10. Put emotion before proof, then let proof support the feeling.
11. Separate `Source Mechanics`, `Live Evidence Used`, and `Domain Extrapolation`.
12. Produce a before/after transformation.

## Output

- Transfer diagnosis.
- Trigger Fit Table.
- Before/after copy or concept.
- Behavior delta.
- Risk and next gate.

## Research Guard

If the transfer depends on current-world buyer behavior, use the research package. If research status is `DEGRADED`, label the transfer as research-informed. If research status is `FAILED`, produce the evidence gap instead of a conversion recommendation.
