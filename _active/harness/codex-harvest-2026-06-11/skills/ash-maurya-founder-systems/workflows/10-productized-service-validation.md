# Productized Service Validation

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use when the user wants to sell validation as a service, audit, sprint, or workshop. The service should prove demand and create a build decision, not merely produce strategy.

Load:
- `skills/ash-maurya-founder-systems/genius.md`
- `deliverables/ash-maurya-founder-systems/productization-guide.md`

## Execution

1. **Choose The Service Lane**
   - Founder Validation Audit.
   - 48-Hour Demo-Sell-Build Sprint.
   - Offer Risk Map.
   - Business Model As Product Workshop.

2. **Define Buyer Pain**
   - Wasted build time.
   - Weak offer conversion.
   - Unclear ICP.
   - Too many ideas.
   - No proof before implementation.

3. **Package The Promise**
   - Outcome: a build/no-build decision or validated offer path.
   - Artifact: canvas, risk map, interview synthesis, offer script, roadmap.
   - Timeline: 48 hours, 5 days, 2 weeks, or 90 days.
   - Price anchor: cost of wasted build cycle.

4. **Design Delivery**
   - Intake.
   - Evidence review.
   - Customer forces interviews or analysis.
   - Offer/risk synthesis.
   - Decision workshop.
   - Next experiment.

5. **Create The Sales Asset**
   - One-sentence promise.
   - Who it is for.
   - Symptoms it solves.
   - What they get.
   - What it costs.
   - What commitment is required.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Consulting | Make the audit paid and implementation optional |
| Agency | Bundle validation before build retainers |
| Founder coaching | Use weekly evidence gates |
| AI implementation | Validate workflow demand before automation |

## Output Requirements

- Productized service spec
- Delivery outline
- Buyer-facing positioning
- Price anchor
- First sales message

## Quality Gate

Pass only if the service can be sold before custom implementation. Fail if the offer is just "strategy help."
