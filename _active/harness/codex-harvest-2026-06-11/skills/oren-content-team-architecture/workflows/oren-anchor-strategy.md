# Content Anchor Strategy

> **Expert**: Oren — Content-Team Architecture
> **Produces**: Anchor Selection + Activation Plan
> **Use When**: Identifying what makes a brand's content world hard to copy
> **Load First**: [genius.md](../genius.md) — Pattern 10 (Anchor Strategy Framework), Pattern 7 (Tiered Brand Exemplar Matrix), Pattern 9 (Founder-as-Character System)

---

## Step 1: Intake

Collect:
1. Brand, category, and primary audience.
2. Existing people, places, rituals, founder stories, customer communities, events, production spaces, and partnerships.
3. Current content formats and strongest performing examples.
4. Competitors whose content feels too easy to copy.
5. Team resources and production cadence.

---

## Step 2: Map The Five Anchor Types

Score each possible anchor from 1-5 for **distinctiveness**, **repeatability**, **audience relevance**, and **production feasibility**:

| Anchor | Evidence | Score |
|---|---|---:|
| Fame | Recognizable people attached to the brand | /5 |
| Influence | Recurring creators or partners | /5 |
| Story | Founder, origin, place, mission, transformation | /5 |
| Production Elements | Distinctive space, set, lab, studio, process, objects | /5 |
| Events | Activations, trips, community moments, recurring cultural calendar | /5 |

---

## Step 3: Choose The Defensible 1-2

Select the smallest anchor set that can carry a repeatable media world. Reject anchors that are:
- Interesting but not repeatable.
- Expensive but not distinctive.
- Distinctive internally but invisible to the audience.
- Dependent on one unavailable person.
- Easy for competitors to imitate.

---

## Step 4: Activate The Anchor

For each selected anchor, define:
- **Recurring formats** — at least 3.
- **Weekly capture ritual** — what is gathered every week.
- **Paid/organic use** — how assets move into ads or conversion pages.
- **Owner** — strategist, creator, founder, external partner, or producer.
- **Proof metric** — what shows the anchor is working.
