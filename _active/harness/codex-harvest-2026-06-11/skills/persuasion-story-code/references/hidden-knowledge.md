# Hidden Knowledge

## Persuasion Stories Are Modular

The hidden system is modular. A sales page, pitch, post, webinar, or video can contain several story fragments, each mapped to a specific prospect question.

## The Best Story Is Often Not The Most Dramatic

The story that closes distance may be a two-sentence customer moment, a short guarantee story, or a simple mechanism example. Dramatic tension is useful only when it serves business belief.

## Buyers Resist Most When They Want The Outcome

Desire and dread rise together. Trust stories and reassurance stories are not optional extras; they handle the fear that appears right before action.

## Detail Must Be Selective

More detail is not better. The right detail makes the story believable, visual, and specific. The wrong detail makes it slow.

## Similarity Creates Permission

Stories about other people using the product work because the prospect borrows the other person's courage and competence.

## Status Is Sensitive

Status stories work best when they show markers of social change instead of bluntly telling the prospect they will outrank others.

## Mechanisms Should Create Fascination, Not Homework

A mechanism story is not a manual. It makes the prospect believe the product has a real reason to work.

## Truth Needs Craft

True facts can still sound unbelievable. The craft is arranging true facts into a simple, specific, conversational sequence that a skeptical person can accept.
