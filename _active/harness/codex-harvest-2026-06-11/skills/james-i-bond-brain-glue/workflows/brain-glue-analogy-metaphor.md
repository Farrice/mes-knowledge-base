# Analogy & Metaphor Forge

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this when the user needs a memorable product name, campaign concept, category frame, explanation, comparison, or vivid pitch.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../genius.md`, `../references/genius-patterns.md`, and `../references/hidden-knowledge.md`.

## Execution

1. **Define the real thing**: State the product, service, or idea in plain language.
2. **List key traits**: Speed, strength, ease, danger, protection, transformation, rebellion, precision, warmth, status, or simplicity.
3. **Ask what it resembles**: Generate objects, scenes, animals, tools, rituals, games, movies, jobs, and everyday moments that share those traits.
4. **Create metaphor candidates**: Build names, phrases, visual hooks, and pitch frames.
5. **Create analogy candidates**: Build "it's like X for Y" explanations and longer story comparisons.
6. **Stress-test clarity**: Remove candidates that are vivid but confusing.
7. **Select the winner**: Choose the metaphor that sells the business job.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Format | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Product name | Short metaphor with category hint |
| Offer | Metaphor + promised transformation |
| Video | Visual metaphor that can be shown in first 3 seconds |
| Sales page | Hero metaphor and supporting explanation |
| Thought leadership | Analogy that reframes the problem |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- **Trait Map**
- **Metaphor Bank**
- **Analogy Bank**
- **Top 5 Candidates**
- **Winner + Risk Notes**
- **Deployment Copy**

## Quality Gate

The winner must make the idea easier to understand and more desirable. If it only sounds clever, reject it.
