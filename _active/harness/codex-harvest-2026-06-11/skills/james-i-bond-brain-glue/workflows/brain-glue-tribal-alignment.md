# Tribal Alignment Map

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this when resonance depends on audience identity: communities, founder-led brands, niche offers, creator content, consulting, high-ticket services, or polarizing positioning.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../genius.md`, `../references/genius-patterns.md`, and `../references/hidden-knowledge.md`.

## Execution

1. **Define the tribe**: Identify the audience's self-image, pride, frustrations, heroes, enemies, rituals, and status markers.
2. **Separate surface demographics from identity**: Ignore shallow labels unless they reveal belonging.
3. **Map alignment cues**: Find phrases, beliefs, examples, and symbols that show "I am one of you."
4. **Map danger zones**: Identify affiliations or claims that would repel the audience.
5. **Create belonging language**: Generate opening lines, hooks, slogans, and proof cues.
6. **Create selective rejection**: Name who this is not for without sounding contemptuous.
7. **Translate to assets**: Provide social, sales, email, and video variants.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Format | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn/X | Insider frustration + identity-based reframe |
| Community | Shared enemy, shared standard, shared ritual |
| Sales page | "Built for..." and "not for..." sections |
| Video | First-person confession or tribal observation |
| Email | Honest reason for contacting this audience |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- **Tribe Snapshot**
- **Belonging Cues**
- **Language to Use**
- **Language to Avoid**
- **Rejection Lines**
- **Deployable Asset Copy**

## Quality Gate

The message must feel specific to the audience without faking lived experience or using stereotypes.
