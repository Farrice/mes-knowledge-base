# Brand Sensory System

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this when the user wants a brand, product, offer, or campaign to feel vivid, ownable, memorable, and recognizable across media.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../genius.md`, `../references/genius-patterns.md`, and `../references/hidden-knowledge.md`.

## Execution

1. **Identify desired feeling**: Trust, power, ease, rebellion, luxury, safety, speed, precision, joy, relief, status.
2. **Map senses**: Sight, sound, smell, taste, touch, gesture, movement, and demonstration.
3. **Create sensory words**: Generate language that evokes physical experience without forcing it.
4. **Create symbols**: Suggest visual motifs, hand gestures, props, sound cues, colors, or packaging moments.
5. **Build brand phrases**: Generate lines that combine sensory cues with business value.
6. **Create usage rules**: Define where the sensory system appears and where it should not.
7. **Package the system**: Provide a compact brand memory kit.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Format | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Brand identity | Sensory palette, symbols, phrase bank |
| Product | Demonstration, packaging cue, tactile promise |
| Video | Prop, sound cue, visual metaphor, gesture |
| Copy | Sense-elevated headline and proof language |
| Live pitch | Participatory demonstration or physical anchor |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- **Feeling Target**
- **Sensory Map**
- **Symbol / Gesture Ideas**
- **Phrase Bank**
- **Usage Rules**
- **Brand Memory Kit**

## Quality Gate

The sensory system must make the offer more concrete, not more theatrical for its own sake.
