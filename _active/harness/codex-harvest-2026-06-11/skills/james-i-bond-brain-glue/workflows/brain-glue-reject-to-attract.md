# Reject to Attract

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this for premium offers, consulting, communities, waitlists, qualification pages, sales calls, launch messaging, or any brand that needs sharper fit.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../genius.md`, `../references/genius-patterns.md`, and `../references/hidden-knowledge.md`.

## Execution

1. **Define the ideal buyer**: Specify traits, context, budget, maturity, urgency, and worldview.
2. **Define the bad-fit buyer**: Specify who will fail, drain delivery, misunderstand value, or resist the process.
3. **Find the honest reason**: Explain why the offer is not for everyone.
4. **Create selective language**: Generate "not for you if," "built for," "minimum standard," and "why we may say no" lines.
5. **Add scarcity only if true**: Never invent limits.
6. **Turn rejection into attraction**: Make qualified buyers feel more certain, not insulted.
7. **Deploy in assets**: Adapt for landing page, sales call, email, social, and application form.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Format | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Landing page | Fit / not-fit section |
| Sales call | Honest qualification opener |
| Email | Selective invitation |
| Social | Identity-defining contrarian post |
| Application | Screening questions and expectation setting |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- **Ideal Buyer**
- **Bad-Fit Buyer**
- **Rejection Lines**
- **Qualification Copy**
- **Scarcity / Selectivity Notes**
- **Deployment Copy**

## Quality Gate

The output must increase qualified desire while reducing delivery risk. Do not use fake scarcity or contempt.
