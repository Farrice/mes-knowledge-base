# Humor Fit Check

## Use When

The draft may benefit from humor, warmth, or personality, but trust could suffer if the joke is wrong.

## Inputs

- Draft or proposed joke.
- Brand voice.
- Audience.
- Stakes and context.
- Desired action.

## Steps

1. Decide what the humor must prove: humanity, confidence, shared world, warmth, or pattern break.
2. Check whether the brand has earned that tone.
3. If the draft needs lived-world observation, load `skills/tom-segura-comedy-storytelling/SKILL.md` and find the charge, way in, and recognition line before adding humor.
4. Remove jokes that only prove cleverness.
5. Rewrite for warmth or personality if a joke is too risky.
6. Keep humor away from unsupported claims.

## Quality Gate

Fail if humor weakens trust, obscures the offer, or sounds imported from another voice.
