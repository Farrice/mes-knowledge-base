## Operating Rule

Use one to three patterns per pass. If more are needed, run the `weak-ad-rescue` workflow or route the asset through `/copywriting-agent` first.

## Behavior Proof Rule

The pattern was not actually used unless the output shows what changed in the copy and why that changes reader behavior.
