# Hidden Knowledge

## Transcript Evidence Is Not Video Evidence

Most YouTube extraction pipelines overtrust captions because they are easy to fetch. Captions can reveal claims and frameworks, but they do not prove what appeared on screen.

## Visual Silence Is Still Evidence

When frame extraction or OCR is unavailable, the limitation itself should appear in the ledger. This tells downstream workflows what they cannot rely on.

## OCR Is Often The Bridge For Tutorials

Screen recordings and lectures often hide the most reusable detail in UI labels, menu names, slide titles, and chart annotations. OCR rows should be treated as their own evidence class.

## Inference Belongs In Its Own Lane

Inference is useful for strategy and creative analysis, but it becomes dangerous when stored as observation. Keep it separate so later workflows can decide whether to trust it.
