# Follow-Up Momentum Engine

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this after a proposal, unanswered message, promised action, referral request, missed deadline, or meeting.

## Skill Acquisition

Load:
- `genius.md` - Face-Saving Follow-Up and Assumptive Timing Frame
- `references/phrase-library.md` - sequence families 6, 7, 16, 21, 23

## Inputs

- What was promised or expected
- How long it has been
- Relationship strength
- Desired next action
- Any deadline truth

## Execution

1. **Give them an exit from embarrassment**: assume busyness or delay before fault.
2. **Restate the shared next step**: remind them what the action unlocks.
3. **Offer two simple paths**: continue, reschedule, decide, or close the loop.
4. **Use an easy question**: make replying low effort.
5. **Add final curiosity if needed**: if they decline or delay again, ask what changed.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Channel | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Email | Subject should be clear, body under 120 words. |
| DM | One face-saving line and one question. |
| Phone voicemail | Say the next step and callback reason. |
| Client project | Include owner, asset, and date. |
| Referral | Keep the favor small and optional. |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- First follow-up
- Second follow-up
- Final close-the-loop message
- Live-call version
- Risk notes

## Quality Gate

Reject guilt, sarcasm, "just checking in," and vague nudges.
