# Mental Picture Builder

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this when the person understands the facts but has not pictured the changed future.

## Skill Acquisition

Load:
- `genius.md` - Future-Feeling Trigger and Mental Movie Installation
- `references/phrase-library.md` - sequence families 4, 5, 12

## Inputs

- Desired decision
- Positive future state
- Cost of inaction
- Time horizon
- Audience's real-world context

## Execution

1. **Pick the picture direction**: run-toward gain, run-away loss, or identity fulfillment.
2. **Write the future moment**: create one concrete scene after the decision succeeds.
3. **Write the alternative**: create one concrete scene if nothing changes.
4. **Connect emotion to action**: ask how the future would feel, then link to the next step.
5. **Keep it truthful**: remove inflated claims and fake certainty.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Channel | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Sales call | Use one vivid scene and stop talking. |
| Proposal | Put the picture before the scope or investment. |
| Email | Use two short paragraphs: future and next step. |
| Content | Turn the future scene into the hook. |
| Leadership | Focus on team reality and downstream cost. |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- Future picture script
- Inaction picture script
- Transition line
- Exact next-step ask
- Short version under 50 words

## Quality Gate

Reject vague visualization. The listener should be able to picture a real day, task, result, or avoided problem.
