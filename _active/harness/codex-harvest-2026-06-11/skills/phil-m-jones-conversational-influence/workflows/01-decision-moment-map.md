# Decision Moment Map

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this when a conversation, message, pitch, proposal, or ask is not moving. Do not start by choosing phrases. Start by locating the decision barrier.

## Inputs

- Conversation context
- Desired next action
- Audience or person
- Current blocker or last response
- Channel: live call, DM, email, content, proposal, leadership conversation

## Execution

1. **Locate the decision state**: classify the barrier as rejection, certainty, emotion, imagination, timing, follow-up, choice overload, status anxiety, objection, close friction, or post-no recovery.
2. **Name the hidden fear**: identify what the other person may be protecting: time, status, money, autonomy, certainty, reputation, effort, or control.
3. **Choose the lightest intervention**: pick one lead phrase family and one next-step phrase family.
4. **Design the easy next action**: make the final question factual, schedulable, or small.
5. **Flag risk**: note where the words could sound pushy, fake, or too scripted.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Channel | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Live call | Include tone notes and fallback questions. |
| Email | Keep the opener short and the next step explicit. |
| DM | Use one sentence per move. |
| Proposal | Map language to decision sections and follow-up. |
| Content | Convert the barrier into a hook and CTA. |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- Conversation Moment Scorecard
- Barrier diagnosis
- Hidden fear map
- Recommended phrase family stack
- Exact next-step question
- Risk warnings

## Quality Gate

Reject the output if it gives phrases before diagnosis, uses more than three phrase families, or lacks a concrete next action.
