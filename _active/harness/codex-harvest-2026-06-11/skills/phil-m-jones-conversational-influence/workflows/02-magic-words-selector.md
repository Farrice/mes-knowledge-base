# Magic Words Selector

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this when you know the conversation goal but need the exact words to say or send.

## Skill Acquisition

Load:
- `genius.md`
- `references/phrase-library.md`
- `references/decision-framework.md`

## Inputs

- Goal of the conversation
- Current listener state
- Relationship strength
- Channel
- Words already used, if any

## Execution

1. **Classify the moment**: use the decision framework to identify the barrier.
2. **Select the sequence family**: choose one primary family from the phrase library.
3. **Adapt to voice**: rewrite the phrase so it sounds like the speaker, not like a quote.
4. **Add support line**: include one clarifying sentence that explains the idea without overexplaining.
5. **Close with an easy response**: ask a question that can be answered quickly.
6. **Create alternates**: produce soft, neutral, and direct variants.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Channel | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Sales call | Add spoken pacing and a fallback if interrupted. |
| Email | Put the phrase in the first two lines. |
| DM | Keep under 60 words. |
| Landing page | Turn phrase into subhead, CTA, or objection line. |
| Leadership | Remove sales language and increase clarity. |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- Phrase Stack
- Primary script
- Soft, neutral, and direct variants
- Follow-up line
- Do-not-say version with why it fails

## Quality Gate

The final words must be speakable aloud, context-specific, and lighter than the pressure they replace.
