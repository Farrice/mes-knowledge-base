# Permission-Based Opener

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this before introducing something the listener may reject too quickly.

## Skill Acquisition

Load:
- `genius.md` - Rejection-Free Introduction and Identity-Safe Agreement
- `references/phrase-library.md` - sequence families 1, 2, 11

## Inputs

- The idea, product, service, request, or change
- Who is hearing it
- Why they may resist
- Desired next action

## Execution

1. **Remove the threat**: state the idea in a way that protects autonomy.
2. **Open the inspection loop**: make it easy to look without committing.
3. **Connect to identity**: invite openness, curiosity, or shared standards.
4. **State the possible value**: name one relevant benefit without hype.
5. **Ask for the smallest next move**: preview, reply, look, compare, schedule, or test.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Channel | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Cold DM | One permission line plus one reason. |
| Email | Subject can hint at low pressure; body carries the ask. |
| Call opener | Use slower tone and pause after the permission frame. |
| Team change | Make the shared standard explicit. |
| Content CTA | Invite consideration rather than demand action. |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- 5 opener options
- Best-fit opener
- One-sentence rationale
- Next-step question
- Version for live, email, and DM

## Quality Gate

Reject any opener that pretends uncertainty while hiding pressure. The listener must be free to say no.
