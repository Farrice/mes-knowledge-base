# Conversion Copy Phrase Injector

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this on emails, DMs, landing pages, CTAs, webinar scripts, sales pages, or proposal copy that needs clearer decision language.

## Skill Acquisition

Load:
- `genius.md`
- `references/phrase-library.md`
- Optional stack: `skills/joanna-wiebe-persuasion-mastery/genius.md`

## Inputs

- Draft copy
- Audience
- Offer or idea
- Conversion goal
- Known objections
- Channel

## Execution

1. **Audit the copy's decision points**: mark where the reader must continue, believe, imagine, choose, reply, or buy.
2. **Match phrase mechanics**: assign one phrase family per weak decision point.
3. **Rewrite in natural copy**: preserve the intent without making it sound like a quote book.
4. **Strengthen CTA**: replace vague asks with easy next actions.
5. **Remove pressure leaks**: delete pushy, fake, or overstacked phrase use.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Channel | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Email | Improve subject, opening, objection line, and CTA. |
| Landing page | Improve section transitions and CTA blocks. |
| DM | Rewrite as short conversational moves. |
| Webinar | Convert to spoken transitions. |
| Proposal | Add decision language around options and next steps. |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- Decision-point audit
- Revised copy
- Phrase mechanic annotations
- CTA variants
- Risk cleanup notes

## Quality Gate

Reject copy that sounds like it pasted phrases on top of weak thinking.
