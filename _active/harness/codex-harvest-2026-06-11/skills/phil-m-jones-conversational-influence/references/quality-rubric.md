# Quality Rubric

Score every Phil M Jones output from 1-10 on each dimension.

| Dimension | 4 - Acceptable | 7 - Strong | 10 - Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis | Identifies a generic goal | Identifies the active barrier | Names the precise decision moment and why it matters |
| Naturalness | Reads like a script | Mostly sounds speakable | Sounds like a real person with prepared clarity |
| Autonomy | Avoids pushiness | Gives the listener room | Makes the listener feel respected and helped |
| Specificity | Has a broad ask | Includes a concrete next step | Makes the next step almost effortless |
| Context fit | Uses a phrase correctly | Adapts to audience and channel | Fits relationship, stakes, tone, and timing |
| Objection handling | Responds to surface issue | Diagnoses real reason | Turns resistance into useful information without debate |
| Deployment value | Useful draft | Ready with light edits | Ready to say, send, or install in a playbook |

## Rejection Triggers

- The script sounds manipulative.
- It uses social proof without evidence.
- It argues with the listener.
- It piles on more than three phrase families.
- It gives a lecture instead of words to use.
- It lacks a specific next action.
