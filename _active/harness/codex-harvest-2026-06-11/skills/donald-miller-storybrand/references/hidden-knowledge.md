# Donald Miller StoryBrand Hidden Knowledge

## Source Package

Primary expansion source: `extractions/donald-miller/storybrand-message-clarity-system/`

## Hidden Knowledge

- Customers do not care about the business until they understand how it helps them solve a problem.
- Confusion is not a branding flaw; it is a buying blocker.
- The brand's story only works when it mirrors the customer's transformation.
- Product language is weak until the problem has created a story loop.
- Credentials belong in the guide position after the problem is clear.
- The one-liner is not a slogan; it is the compressed sales argument.
- A BrandScript should protect simple language, not make it sound more sophisticated.
- Repetition is a service to the customer because it lowers effort and increases recall.
- The best message feels obvious after it is written because the cognitive load has been removed.
- A message that cannot be deployed across channels is not yet a messaging system.

## Anti-Patterns

- Lead with company history.
- Lead with features.
- Use mission language before customer value is clear.
- Make the customer decode a metaphor before knowing the problem.
- Create a different message for every channel.
- Treat the one-liner and BrandScript as separate assets instead of one connected language system.
