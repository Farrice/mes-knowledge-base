# Sweat Equity Speedrun Social OS - Extraction Brief

## Objective

Turn the Sweat Equity Malbon Home case study into a deployed Antigravity capability for speedrunning social media around brands, events, launches, activations, local venues, creators, and client campaigns.

## Build Shape

New companion OS. Existing social skills generate posts and growth formats; this source adds production architecture: north star, set mapping, modular hooks, live opportunity capture, role design, fast editing, and event-based volume.

## Core Method

1. Name a sharp outcome and temporary content north star.
2. Map the environment as sets before writing content ideas.
3. Assign each set a repeatable content job.
4. Design modular concepts that can produce many variations.
5. Split content into reach, immersion, proof, product, and bridge roles.
6. Staff the sprint with director, shooters, editor, and guest-handler roles.
7. Keep the plan visible but allow the team to upgrade ideas in the moment.
8. Publish fast enough that the event feels alive while people still care.
9. Attribute results carefully and review what produced the biggest bridges into the brand ecosystem.

## Capability Gap Filled

This system fills the gap between "make more content" and "engineer a physical or live moment so the content engine has enough material to publish at speed." It should improve content strategy, client activations, launch planning, brand documentation, and agent workflows that need real-world production logic.
