## Durable Extraction

An AI employee is a role-scoped agentic system that:

1. Lives in the user's existing work surface.
2. Knows the relevant company/project context without leaking private context.
3. Uses integrations through explicit ownership and scope.
4. Treats ambient events as structured inputs.
5. Suggests or acts proactively only after earning trust.
6. Preserves personality and trust quality when models, tools, or prompts change.
7. Rolls out from small supervised use to broader activation.
