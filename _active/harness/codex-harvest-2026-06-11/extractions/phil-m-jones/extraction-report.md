## Hidden Knowledge

- Words are tools, not spells.
- Prepared scripts are not fake when delivered with honest intent.
- The next question should be easy after the big decision frame.
- A no can be a diagnostic asset if handled with curiosity.
- People often need dignity before they can re-engage.

## Hall of Fame Exemplars

The source repeatedly demonstrates three core moves:

1. **Rejection-free introduction** - lower pressure before presenting an idea.
2. **Assumptive timing** - ask for the right time instead of asking whether time exists.
3. **Conditional close** - test whether the objection is real before solving it.

## Signature Moves

- Diagnose the moment.
- Pick the smallest useful phrase.
- Preserve autonomy.
- Make the future visible.
- Move to an easy next step.
- Use curiosity after resistance.
