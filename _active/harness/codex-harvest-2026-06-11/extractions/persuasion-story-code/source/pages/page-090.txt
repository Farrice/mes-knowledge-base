You can put together a story like this when you know what other
choices are available, and what it is about the one you are offering that
makes the other ones inferior or undesirable, by comparison.
This kind of story requires more work than most. First, you need to have
comprehensive knowledge of competitors in the market. Next, you need
to make a list of all the reasons why what you offer makes yours a better
choice than the other ones. In detail. Finally, you need to boil it down
into a story that makes it so much easier to say yes to you than to say no,
that... well... there really is no other choice.
But remember. It's got to be your prospect's decision. Your job is to lead
them to that decision.
PROBLEM/SOLUTION STORIES
Hans Zimmer is a legendary composer for the movies. He has won two
Academy Awards and four Grammys for his work, which includes over
150 film scores.
With a track record like that, you would naturally expect Zimmer to be
the graduate of a famous music school. But he's not. His formal
education in music was shorter than two weeks, when he took piano
lessons as a kid—and promptly quit.
In an interview for the documentary "Score," about film composers, he
said that he'll often approach a violinist or trumpet player and say, "Can
we just sit down, and can you really explain to me what your instrument
can do?"
He explained, "So rather than saying I've had no education, I think
what's happening is I have an education every day. It's just not in a
formalized, structured way. It's just what I need to have at any one point
to solve a problem."
