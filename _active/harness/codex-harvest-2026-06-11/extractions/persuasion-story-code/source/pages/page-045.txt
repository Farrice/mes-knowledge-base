artificial intelligence in the field of writing:
Every time there's a technology breakthrough, waves of fear and
jolts of excitement spread through sectors of the workforce.
Today, people who churn out analysis and pump out content—
especially writers-are going through both fear and excitement.
Why? Because of recent advances in artificial intelligence. New
widely available programs seem to be able to write huge chunks
of usable content, prompted with nothing more than a simple
question, or statement.
But for the writers, there's a bigger question. Its answer will
determine their professional futures. That question is: Will Al
make my job obsolete... or will it provide previously unimagined
opportunities for me?
To put a story like this together requires two things:
1) You know what's going on in your prospect's world that they're aware
of, and
2) You know how they feel about it.
For me, this one was easy. Why? Because I'm in that world. As a writer
and copywriting coach, I wrote this at a time that people were coming at
me from all directions with questions, concerns, excitement and just
sheer curiosity about new AI programs that were said to be able to write.
But I've had other situations where I wasn't part of the world—
community, tribe-I was writing about. In that case, research and
awareness were crucial for me. And they will be for you, too, if you end
up writing this kind of story about events in that world and their current
or future impact on your prospect.
Also, knowledge of history. In this case, recent history. I can think back
over the last 20 years when there have been other technology
breakthroughs accompanied by the twin reactions of panic and
excitement. My knowledge of history allowed me to write about this
confidently. If I were to tackle a subject in a market I didn't already know
a lot about, then I would have had to research some history, as well as
the "top of mind" phenomenon.
