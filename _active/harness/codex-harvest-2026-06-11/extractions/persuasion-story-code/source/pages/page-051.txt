CHAPTER 5
STORIES THAT PREDICT THE FUTURE
A big part of persuasion involves making a promise for a better future.
Most copywriters, salespeople and other professional persuaders know
this instinctively. Promises of better outcomes for the prospect are a key
part of almost every persuasive message.
Some types of predictions work much better than others. In this chapter,
we'll look at four types of persuasion stories that work exceptionally
well.
As much as you may like all four, you can't use all of them in every
persuasion situation. Only the first one is, for all intents and purposes,
universal: The Unexpected Benefit Story. The second, The Predicted
Transformation Story, works only when you can make a legitimate claim
that by using your product or service correctly, your prospect will get a
transformation in their life. That is true for a lot of offers, but certainly
not all.
The third type of story also works only with certain types of offers: those
that can help a prospect improve their social standing, or status. And the
fourth type is mainly useful only with products that improve health.
Nevertheless, it's good to learn about all four types of stories. Why?
Because seeing a persuasion story in action-even one you can't use in
your current persuasion efforts-can spark your imagination for the
stories you are going to create and use.
