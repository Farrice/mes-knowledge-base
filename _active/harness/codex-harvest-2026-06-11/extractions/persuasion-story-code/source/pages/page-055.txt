Then, look through all your benefits and see which one is as different

startling. It should not directly contradict the main idea, but it can stand
in sharp contrast to it.
(The story above shows a way to hire a lawyer when you absolutely have
to, but still save money.)
Once you have your unexpected benefit, build your Unexpected Benefit
Story using these questions as guides:
1. What's the unexpected benefit?
2. How is it like the other benefits, but also, what makes it different?
3. What are the implications or spinoffs of this benefit? That is, by
having this benefit, what else will the prospect be able to get, to
do, or to be?
4. What does the prospect risk missing out on by not having this
benefit?
