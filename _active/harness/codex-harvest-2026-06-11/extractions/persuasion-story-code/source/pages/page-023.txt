But even as all this was happening, three questions kept gnawing at me:
What's the connection between copywriting and storytelling? How are they
similar? How are they different?

conference in Palm Springs, California, called "StoryCon: The World's
First Summit Meeting on the Art, Science and Application of Story."
Through some of my friends in the screenwriting world, I was able to get
a slot to speak there.
