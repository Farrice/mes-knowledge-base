If you live in the world of guitars, you know that session guitarists are
the best of the best. They're not famous rock stars who play in packed
stadiums. Instead, the session players are the ones who show up in the
studio and lay down the tracks that make top-of-the-charts hit records.
They are precision musicians who can learn new tunes much faster than
mere mortals, and play close to perfectly the first time out.
Or at least, that's the legend.
But it's pretty close to accurate. So, to have a session guitarist—nay, a
legendary session guitarist-endorse a guitar course is like having
Einstein endorse a physics course. If you're in the guitar world, that is.
With an expert testimonial, who signs the testimonial is actually much
more important than what they say. People automatically pay attention to
other people who they consider experts, and give weight to what they
say.
As to what they should say, it should be conversational and succinct.
Not, for example, "I think this is probably a good idea," but much more
like "This is definitely a good idea." Get your expert to make a sweeping,
authoritative, concise statement. It is their opinion, after all. So it might
as well be as favorable and absolute as they're willing to make it.
2. Results Testimonials
When most people think of testimonials, they usually think of this kind
of testimonial.
The formula for a Results Testimonial can be very simple:
- I had this problem/need / desire
- I tried this solution/ provider
- I got this result
