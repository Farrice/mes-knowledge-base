In the second paragraph, we said
you will also learn our secret method for being able to play any song

But in the third paragraph, we said
Suddenly, people see them instantly learn new tunes, the second they

The problem is: "within half an hour" is not the same thing as
"instantly."
It's close, compared to the hours, even days, it takes some musicians to
learn a tune. But it's not the same.
So, this minor inconsistency could ruin your chances of a successful
persuasion effort. Beware of inconsistencies in your stories!
2. Vagueness Version Of The Story
One more thing. Besides vastly improving your chops and making
yourself a top pick when anyone with a band is looking for a great
guitar player...
... you will also learn our secret method for doing something you
thought only the most talented guitarists can do. You've
probably heard of musicians who can do this. And you assumed it
was something you were born with, or you would never have it.
Well, we've figured out a way so that anyone can do it. People
assume they are music geniuses. But it's their secret that they are
simply students of Guitarist on Steroids!
In a misguided attempt to be tantalizing, someone who writes a story
like this saps the story of all its power.
