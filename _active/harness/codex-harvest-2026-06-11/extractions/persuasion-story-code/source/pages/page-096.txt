Tips For Case Studies
• Decide which result or superior quality you want to highlight.
• Now, list all the events that led up to that result.
• Decide which aspect of yourself, or your product, you want to
highlight in the case study. For example, you might want to highlight
that you re a consultant who can really solve the toughest marketing
problems. Or, you might want to highlight that your hammer truly is
indestructible.
• Weed out all but the most exciting, important points of your story. You
want it to end up being concise, but conversational-not terse and
stripped of emotion.
You can use a good case study just about anywhere in a sales
presentation or sales letter—at the beginning, at the end, or anywhere in-
between.
Note: If you are going to include a client in a case study, make sure you
get their written permission to do so.
