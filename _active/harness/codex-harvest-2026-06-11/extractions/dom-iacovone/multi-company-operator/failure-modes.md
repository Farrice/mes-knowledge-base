# Failure Modes And Recovery Moves

| Failure Mode | Source Signal | Antigravity Recovery Move |
|---|---|---|
| Founder stays in every meeting | Team becomes less opinionated and defaults to yes. | Remove founder from routine calls; define escalation triggers. |
| Annual plan has too many priorities | No weekly truth surface can show what matters. | Collapse to four initiative blocks and assign owners/metrics. |
| Product launches without margin or differentiation | Novelty substitutes for product reason. | Run stage-gate: sense, innovation, margin, channel fit. |
| Buyer request overrides brand truth | Retail access makes the team reactive. | Treat buyer feedback as input; preserve product/customer logic. |
| Campaign narrative beats finance | Team keeps explaining while CM1 misses. | Put finance first; decide from variance and cause hypotheses. |
| Retail timing is misunderstood | Current numbers miss already-loaded future distribution. | Build a retail lag map before judging momentum or exit timing. |
| Trade spend is treated as small expense | Deductions destroy exit value through multiples. | Convert leakage into enterprise-value impact. |
| Direct retail is mistaken for sell-through | Shelf availability lacks store-level execution incentives. | Compare direct retail, DSD, distributor, and Amazon paths. |
| AI strategy ignores relationships | Tools become commodity while access remains scarce. | Map offline relationship moats beside automation gains. |
| Slow brand is killed too early | A delayed retail inflection is mistaken for failure. | Separate weak core economics from slow channel timing. |

## Stop Conditions

Do not use this skill to:

- Give medical, legal, tax, or investment advice.
- Recommend retail expansion without channel economics.
- Treat a buyer, influencer, or partner request as automatically correct.
- Turn every business into a multi-company holding company.
- Justify adding new Antigravity commands without a routing and validation gate.
