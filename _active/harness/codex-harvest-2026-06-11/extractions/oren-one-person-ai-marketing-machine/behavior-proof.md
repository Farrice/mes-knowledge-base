# Behavior Proof

## Input Tested

Cold-start fixture: a solo founder runs a small premium service business with one core offer, light organic posting, no referral process, no paid ads, no structured email follow-up, scattered testimonials, and 40 hours per week available for marketing execution.

## Baseline Behavior

The existing `/oren-marketing-plan-2026` route can produce a funnel grade, initiative map, content funnel, calendar matrix, asset repurposing plan, campaign skeletons, and 90-day action plan.

That baseline is useful, but it can still leave the solo operator with missing execution mechanics:

- no explicit weekly time budget,
- no AI brand-voice/copy machine setup,
- no referral gate before channel expansion,
- no creator/vendor sourcing lane,
- no performance time-boxing,
- no logistics buffer,
- and no integrated remarketing/reuse loop across ads, landing pages, email, sales collateral, answers, creators, and organic validation.

## New Workflow Behavior

`/oren-solo-ai-marketing-machine` must produce:

- **Referral Gate**: visible-use, customer-love, affiliate/referral, ask-timing, and incentive actions before new channels.
- **Monthly Message Loop**: customer value, trending context, support questions, comments, competition, objections, and sales notes.
- **AI Copy Machine**: brand context, personas, voice references, conversion frameworks, prompt/project setup, and human review rules.
- **40-Hour Calendar**: 8 hours new creative, 4 performance/ad loading, 4 funnel/landing/email, 4 collateral/email, 8 influencer/creator pipeline, 8 logistics, 4 organic validation.
- **Remarketing Loop**: messages become website/landing page updates, emails, sales collateral, paid creative, creator briefs, Reddit/YouTube answers, and organic validation posts.
- **First 7-Day Sprint**: concrete actions for the next week.
- **Do Not Do List**: channels, meetings, tool fiddling, overproduction, and strategy drift to skip.

## Behavior Delta

The new workflow changes the output from a marketing plan into a solo operating system. The user no longer has to translate Oren planning advice into a week; the workflow turns the source into calendar blocks, AI setup, message deployment, asset reuse, and constraints.
