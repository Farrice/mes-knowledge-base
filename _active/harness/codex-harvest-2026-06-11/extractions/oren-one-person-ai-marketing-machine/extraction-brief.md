## Objective

Turn Oren's one-person marketing machine video into a reusable Antigravity capability that helps Farrice and Codex operate as a world-class solo AI marketing arbiter: source-grounded, commercially practical, AI-augmented, and connected to the existing Oren system.

## Build Shape Decision

This should not become a new Oren mega-skill. Existing Oren capabilities already cover archetypes, pricing, planning, pod design, paid-organic bridges, content systems, luxury psychology, repositioning, and taste. The missing capability is a bridge workflow that translates those Oren principles into a forced-lean solo operator cadence where AI fills missing capacity under human judgment.

## Operating Method

The extracted method has five layers:

1. **Referral and word-of-mouth gate**: exploit existing customers, visible usage, affiliate/referral incentives, and timely ask moments before adding more channels.
2. **Monthly message intelligence**: mine customer value, trending context, support questions, social comments, competition, objections, and sales feedback.
3. **AI copy and answer engine**: build a brand-voice machine with context, personas, voice references, conversion frameworks, and a human review gate.
4. **Solo weekly execution calendar**: allocate the 40-hour week across creative, performance, funnel, collateral/email, influencer/creator, logistics, and organic validation.
5. **Remarketing and reuse loop**: move messages and assets through website, landing pages, emails, ads, creator briefs, sales collateral, Reddit/YouTube answers, and organic validation.
