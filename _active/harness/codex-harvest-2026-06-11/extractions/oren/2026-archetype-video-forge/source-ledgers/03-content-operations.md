## Extracted Mechanics
- "Brand as media company" means content-first organizational design, not merely publishing more short-form clips.
- Content marketing should run as a flywheel: owned organic, owned paid, brand-world or external organic, conversion pages, email/SMS, then back into content.
- The core pod is strategist/social lead plus creator, supported by contributors as scale increases.
- Required operating rhythm includes weekly 1:1s, creative review, analytics review, research, project management, and Friday recap.
- A high-output pod should be capable of roughly 10 strong concepts per week across organic and paid use cases.
