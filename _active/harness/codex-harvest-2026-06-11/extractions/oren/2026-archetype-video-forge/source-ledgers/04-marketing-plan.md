## Extracted Mechanics
- Planning starts by grading the funnel: awareness, consideration, education/affinity, and conversion.
- Initiative options should be mapped across normie-to-fringe and sales-to-awareness, then limited to one or two additions per period.
- Content pillars must be defined as format plus topic and mapped to funnel role.
- A useful annual calendar tracks culture, industry moments, sales heat, social performance holes, launches, and missed opportunities.
- Campaign planning should split teaser, unveil, BTS/proof, and sustain phases, with internal and external promotion considered together.
