# Final MES Extraction — Oren Archetype Social Strategy Forge

## Mechanism
Oren's core move is to make social strategy a role-behavior system. A brand does not need infinite content ideas; it needs a clear archetype, a realistic resource map, and repeatable content behaviors that match the role it can credibly play in the audience's life.

The archetype decision is constrained by execution reality. A brand with deep expertise but no performer may be an Oracle. A brand with a magnetic character and product that can live inside entertainment may be a Performer. A brand with strong creative direction and risk tolerance may be a World Builder. A brand helping customers become aspirationally better may be a Catalyst. A brand offering practical, relatable help without heavy authority may be a Helper.

## Expert System
The extraction becomes a companion module with nine workflows:

| Workflow | Job |
|---|---|
| `oren-archetype-map` | Diagnose the best-fit social archetype from role, proof, resources, and risk tolerance |
| `oren-archetype-content-engine` | Convert an archetype into content pillars, formats, and weekly ideas |
| `oren-archetype-mismatch-audit` | Find when a brand's current content behavior contradicts its archetype |
| `oren-pricing-psychology-map` | Align pricing, product roles, and buyer signaling |
| `oren-marketing-plan-2026` | Convert archetype and funnel gaps into a 2026 marketing plan |
| `oren-short-form-revenue-map` | Connect short-form behavior to brand deals, services, or owned offers |
| `oren-creative-systems-stack` | Install the minimum systems required to repeat the archetype |
| `oren-brand-media-archetype-stack` | Connect archetype strategy to brand-as-media-company architecture |
| `oren-archetype-offer-stack` | Align social archetype, buyer psychology, and monetizable offer path |

## Source-To-Command Translation
- The archetype video becomes the strategic command layer.
- Pricing psychology becomes a product/offer support workflow.
- Content-team ops becomes cross-link and repair work for `oren-content-team-architecture`.
- Marketing planning becomes a funnel/calendar/initiative workflow.
- Short-form monetization becomes a revenue-path workflow.
- Creative systems becomes a repeatability stack workflow.

## Quality Bar
- Output must include a single recommended archetype or a ranked top-two with evidence.
- Output must separate observed evidence from inference.
- Output must include resource-fit constraints, not just aspirational brand language.
- Output must translate into weekly content behavior, not only strategy words.
- Output must include handoff paths to existing Oren packages when the ask belongs there.
