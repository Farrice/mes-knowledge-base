## Sample Input

Idea: AI workflow audit for solo consultants who know they should automate client delivery but keep losing time to manual handoffs.

Assumption to test: Consultants will pay for a small diagnostic before a full implementation.

## Sample Output

### 48-Hour Sprint Plan

**Day 0 decision:** Test willingness to pay for an AI Workflow Friction Audit before building any automation package.

**Day 1 discovery:** Interview 8-10 consultants who recently delivered a client project with manual research, handoff, reporting, or follow-up work.

Behavioral questions:

- Walk me through the last client delivery that felt too manual.
- What did you have to repeat?
- What did the delay cost you?
- What did you try to systemize already?
- What would make you trust an outside audit?

**Day 1 offer assembly:** Create a short audit preview that maps one delivery workflow, names three friction points, estimates time lost, and shows one automation path.

**Day 2 sell:** Return to qualified prospects with: "Based on what you told me, I mapped the manual steps that are probably costing you delivery time. Can I show you the audit format?"

### Mafia Offer

Promise: In 48 hours, identify the three highest-friction client-delivery steps and give you a prioritized automation roadmap.

Demo: One-page workflow map with before/after delivery path.

Price anchor: One lost client day or one missed delivery deadline.

Commitment ask:

- Level 4: pay for the diagnostic.
- Level 3: book the audit and share one delivery artifact.
- Level 2: intro another consultant with the same issue.
- Level 1: asks to stay updated.
- Level 0: compliment only.

### Build Decision

Build the implementation package only if at least 3 prospects reach Level 3 or higher, with at least 1 paid diagnostic.

## Result

Pass. The workflow is executable from the generated files alone and produces a behavior-based commitment test rather than generic startup advice.
