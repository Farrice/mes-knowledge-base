## Executive Summary

Kallaway's content system is not a content calendar. It is a production physics model. Each video is split into six stages: topic, format, substance, hook, script, and edit. AI accelerates the repetitive and data-heavy work, while the creator protects the creative edge: the contrarian take and the evidence that makes the take undeniable.

The system becomes compounding when ten videos are treated as one batch. Performance is reviewed by the strongest available business metric: conversions first, followers second, views last. Winners are carried forward by rule, not mood.

## Extracted Methodology

### The Six-Stage Rep

1. Topic: Use data and AI to find what the audience already values.
2. Format: Pick the storytelling container before substance.
3. Substance: Create the contrarian take and evidence stack.
4. Hook: Coordinate visual, text, and spoken hooks.
5. Script: Draft through a focused voice/rhythm profile.
6. Edit: Choose the edit path based on creator leverage and quality needs.

### The 10x Batch System

Run the first research pull once, then create ten videos. Post them back to back. Review the batch by conversions, followers, or views. A 5x winner shapes three of the next ten. A 10x winner gets the same topic and format rerun. The target is three differentiated winning combinations.

## Signature Moves

- **Signal First:** Never burn creative energy on a losing topic.
- **Format Lock:** Choose the structural container before writing.
- **Contrarian Take:** Find the belief most people resist at first and accept after proof.
- **Evidence Like A Lawyer:** Build proof, contrast, example, story, metaphor, psychology, and data.
- **Hook Triad:** Separate what is shown, what is written on screen, and what is spoken.
- **Script Profile:** Use 10-20 winning transcripts from one voice instead of generic AI writing.
- **Leverage Edit:** Outsource editing when content is a business engine.
- **Relative Winner Review:** Judge posts against their batch, not against emotion.
