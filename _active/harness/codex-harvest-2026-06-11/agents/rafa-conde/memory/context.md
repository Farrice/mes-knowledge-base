## Core Remembered Principles

- Make people feel something.
- Sell the idea, not just the feature.
- First-run moments set the stage.
- Tiny details can become the thing people remember.
- Protect small products from losing their center.
- Video and context are design tools.
- The best product moments are simple enough to retell.
- Fourth-wall moments work when the experience truthfully references the user's real frame: device, scroll behavior, physical object, workflow state, room, platform, or assumption.
- The Metal Gear Solid examples are mechanics, not skins: physical box as clue, controller as object, hardware port as solution, and player-role flip.

## Routing Notes

Use Rafa for product memorability, prototype presentation, onboarding feeling, small-app charm, and agent workflow UX. Use broader visual or messaging experts only after the feeling brief is clear.

Use `rafa-conde-fourth-wall-experience-os` when the request mentions breaking the fourth wall, surprise plus insight, perspective shift, audience reality, frame-aware product moments, or cross-domain creative experiences.
