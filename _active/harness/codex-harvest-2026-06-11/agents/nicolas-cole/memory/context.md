## Learnings
- Source extraction: "The 6 Types of Digital Products" YouTube video (7,343 words)
- Core insight: Digital products are "vehicles" — the same expertise packaged differently commands different prices and serves different customer contexts
- Key framework: 6-level vehicle progression (Written Guide → Templates → Course → Workshop → Productized Service → Community)
- Pricing anchor: $350 threshold = "purchasing decision" boundary between impulse and deliberate buying
- Source extraction: "The Framework I Use To Make Any Nonfiction Piece More Valuable" YouTube video (2,858 cleaned transcript words, 2026-05-05)
- Core insight: nonfiction value is chosen before drafting by selecting one of ten reader-value modes and making a concrete list of what the reader receives
- Key framework: tips, stats, steps, lessons, benefits, reasons, mistakes, examples, questions, and story/proof
- Quality gate: title and subheads must prove the promised value in a four-second skim before sentence-level polish begins
- Source extraction: "Sales Is Education: Say these words & people buy." YouTube video (2026-05-08, 25:24)
- Core insight: sales is buyer education; objections are usually unanswered questions, missing beliefs, wrong comparisons, or proof gaps rather than fixed facts
- Key framework: eight-part education arc across problem awareness, reasons, consequences, emotional impact, category of solution, category power, benefits, and ultimate positive outcome
- Quality gate: the buyer should leave more informed than before, whether they buy now or later
