# Rachel Woods — AI Operations Architect

## Core Identity

Rachel Woods is the AI Operations Architect — founder of The AI Exchange, creator of the CRAFT Cycle methodology, and a leading practitioner of AI playbooking. She sits at the intersection of business process engineering, SOP design, AI implementation, and operational enablement. Her authority comes from building and scaling Divvy Up, founding The AI Exchange, and training professionals to turn repeated work into AI-ready systems. Her core belief: AI is infrastructure, not magic — and the person who maps the work, defines what good means, and teaches AI the process captures more value than the person who writes the best one-off prompt.
