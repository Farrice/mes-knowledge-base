Required output:

### brief-demo

Required output:

- 3 hidden objections
- 3 proof gaps

- source/evidence notes

### outreach

Produce manual, qualified outreach only.

Required output:

- prospect filter
- personalization cue
- give-first line
- soft ask
- follow-up note
- no automation, scraping, or fake engagement

### daily

Produce a 1-4 hour execution loop.

Required output:

- 30 minute market scan
- 60 minute public/content or direct asks
- 60-120 minute proof/demo/delivery block
- 15 minute tracker closeout
- one next artifact

## Operating Sequence

1. **Mission package context**: run `mission_control.py context`, load the approved package files, and emit the Mission Handoff Receipt.
2. **Intent lock**: identify mode, audience, and source context.
3. **Vibe Tax frame**: apply `/vibe-tax-brief` to find the false signal, proof gap, or buyer-language issue.
4. **Expert council lock**: load the approved council order and decide which lanes are used or skipped.
5. **Research proof**: use `/research-intelligence-agent` posture for claims; cite sources or mark claims as local inference.
6. **Voice lock**: load Farrice voice evidence before drafting public copy.
7. **Content composition**: use `/farrice-content-os` as the content owner.
8. **Expert composition**: one owner, bounded expert slots, no expert soup.
9. **Quality gates**: Hook Room -> high-taste writing pass -> anti-slop audit -> `/publishable-copy-gate`.
10. **Output**: draft-only unless Farrice explicitly approves posting, outreach, or external action.

## Hard Rules

- AI stays backstage.
- Do not lead with "AI consulting."
- Do not assume prior expert agents actually ran; explicitly run or locally simulate the lane being used.
- Do not publish, DM, scrape, auto-comment, or change sharing without explicit approval.
- Do not use unverified stats in public copy.
- Do not ship public copy without Copy Gate Result.
- Keep every public asset human-to-human: make the buyer feel seen, not targeted.
- Do not use this workflow as a thin wrapper around the launch post. The approved mission package is part of the runtime context.
