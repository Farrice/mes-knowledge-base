## Step Order

1. **Evidence Gate**: load `skills/liam-ottley-linkedin-lead-magnet/genius.md`, `references/source-evidence.md`, and the uncertainty report.
2. **Positioning Gate**: define ICP, offer, buyer outcome, proof, and one-line positioning.
3. **Monetizable Expertise Map**: list expertise that changes buyer outcomes and would plausibly create demand.
4. **Strategic Arbitrage Map**: produce trend, authority, story, PAS, "how I", and lead magnet angles.
5. **Content Lanes**: choose 3-5 repeatable lanes, not a huge topic list.
6. **Lead Magnet Bridge**: define the opt-in concept and score buyer outcome, specificity, fast value, proof fit, and paid-offer bridge.
7. **AI Prompt Set**: generate prompts for identity, expertise extraction, strategic arbitrage, hooks/rehooks, SLAY drafts, PAS drafts, swipe remix, bridge, and quality audit.
8. **First 20-Post Sprint**: map lane, angle, hook, rehook, structure, bridge, and success signal.
9. **Draft 5 Posts**: produce one SLAY, one PAS, one authority framework, one strategic arbitrage, and one lead magnet teaser post.
10. **Validation**: run the checklist and name any missing inputs or weak bridge.
