# /video-frame-ledger

Sample frames through the full ledger command and inspect `frames/` plus `frame-notes.md`.

```bash
python3 execution/video_context_ledger.py "<youtube-url>" --mode full --interval 60
```

Frame rows prove a sampled image exists. They do not prove what is inside the image until reviewed by a human, OCR, or vision adapter.
