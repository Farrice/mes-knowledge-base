- A brand needs a world invitation rather than a feature list.
- Messaging or positioning needs a changed-world explanation rather than benefit soup.
- A concept, product, or service needs a lived use-case scene and boundary line.
- Ads or media need attention without fake urgency, rage-bait, or hollow cleverness.

- Copy needs desire and proof without problem-agitation relapse.
- A piece needs a perspective shift, not louder stakes.
