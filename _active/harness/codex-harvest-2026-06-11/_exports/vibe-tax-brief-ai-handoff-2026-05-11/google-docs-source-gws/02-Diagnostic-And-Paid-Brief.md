# Lead Magnet - The Vibe Tax Diagnostic

## Type

Stockton Walbeck category: **Clarifier + Sampler**

The diagnostic clarifies the buyer's hidden problem and samples the kind of insight the paid brief provides.

## Promise

```text
Find where your offer, content, or strategy is being steered by false signals.
```

## How It Works

The buyer answers 12 questions. Each question is scored 0-5.

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0-1 | false signal risk |
| 2-3 | partial signal |
| 4-5 | buyer-ready signal |

Total raw score: 60. Convert to 100 if needed.

## Questions

### Channel / Context Fit

1. Are you using signals from the same context where buyers actually decide, search, compare, or pay?
2. Can you name which signals you are ignoring because they do not predict buyer action?

### Buyer Precision

3. Is your buyer tied to a real trigger, constraint, and buying moment?
4. Can you describe the decision pressure the buyer feels when the problem becomes urgent?

### Pain Point Specificity

5. Does your message name the external problem, internal frustration, and consequence of delay?
6. Does your copy use phrases buyers would actually say under stress?

### Solution Novelty

7. Does your offer reveal a non-obvious mechanism or reframe buyers remember?
8. Can a buyer explain why your approach is different from the obvious alternative?

### Walk-Away Value

9. Would the buyer leave with a clearer decision, sharper language, or visible correction?
10. Does every finding lead to keep, revise, stop, or test?

### Signal Consistency

11. Does the pattern repeat across buyer words, objections, alternatives, content response, and commercial behavior?
12. Can you show receipts or confidence labels for the signal you are trusting?

## Score Bands

| Score | Band | Meaning | CTA |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0-29 | High Vibe Tax | Strategy is likely being steered by guesses. | Run the paid brief before rebuilding assets. |
| 30-54 | Noisy Signal | Some useful signal exists, but false positives are mixed in. | Use the brief to separate signal from noise. |
| 55-79 | Proof Gap | The idea is viable, but belief and proof are underbuilt. | Use the brief to sharpen proof and sales language. |
| 80-100 | Shortlist Ready | The foundation is strong; optimize angle and distribution. | Use the brief for campaign or content strategy. |

# Sample Brief - Vibe Tax Brief For A Solo AI Workflow Offer

## Executive Diagnosis

This offer is not suffering from low AI awareness. The likely gap is buyer specificity.

The buyer already knows AI exists. The false signal is assuming interest in AI equals readiness to buy an AI workflow sprint. The real buying trigger is a repeated task that is painful, visible, and close to money.

## Buyer Language Map

| Buyer Phrase | What It Reveals | Use In Copy/Sales |
|---|---|---|
| "I know I should be using AI more." | aspiration without task clarity | ask for the repeated task |
| "I still rewrite everything." | workflow trust gap | sell reusable prompts plus SOP |
| "My follow-up is inconsistent." | money leak | lead with sales-call follow-up workflow |
| "I just want one thing working." | scope anxiety | sell one workflow, not transformation |

## False Signal Diagnosis

| Signal | Why It Misleads | Better Signal |
|---|---|---|
| AI curiosity | curiosity does not equal buying intent | buyer names a repeated task |
| tool adoption | tools can be unused or disconnected | buyer provides example input/output |
| content engagement | peers like AI ideas | buyers ask about setup, price, timeline |

## Hidden Objections

1. "Will this become another tool I do not use?"
2. "Will I have to explain my whole business?"
3. "Can I trust the output enough to use it with clients?"

## Proof Gaps

1. Needs a before/after workflow map.
2. Needs a real example of messy input becoming usable output.
3. Needs a scoped acceptance checklist so the buyer knows what "done" means.

## Stronger Offer Angles

1. **One Workflow, Not AI Transformation**
2. **Stop Rewriting From Scratch**
3. **Install The Missing Middle**

## Sales Openers

1. What is one repeated task that still lives in your head, inbox, or notes?
2. Where does the task touch money, clients, or follow-up?
3. If that one workflow worked by Friday, what would change next week?

## Seven-Day Test

Use the one-workflow angle for 20 targeted asks. Keep the offer if buyers name a specific task, ask about timeline, or send an example input.
