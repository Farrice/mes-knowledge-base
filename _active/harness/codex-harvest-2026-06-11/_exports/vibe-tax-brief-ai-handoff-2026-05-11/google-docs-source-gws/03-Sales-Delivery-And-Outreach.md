## Social Position

Do not announce a service like a brochure.

Create tension around the false signals people already recognize:

- likes that do not become leads
- AI summaries that flatten the market
- peer praise that does not reveal buyer pain
- competitor claims everyone repeats
- content volume that hides weak buyer understanding

Core angle:

```text
Before you turn a signal into strategy, check whether it is evidence or expensive noise.
```

Primary channel: LinkedIn.

Launch-week mix:

| Mix | Role |
|---:|---|
| 40% | growth posts that name false signals |
| 30% | authority posts that show the diagnostic method |
| 20% | conversion posts that invite paid briefs |
| 10% | personal post on why signal quality matters to Farrice |

Conversion path:

```text
post/comment/DM -> send me one decision you are making -> quick false-signal read -> invite qualified fits into the paid Vibe Tax Brief
```

Do not lead with a checkout link. Lead with judgment.

## First 5 Hooks

1. You might not have a content problem. You might be paying the vibe tax.
2. Likes are not buyer language.
3. AI can summarize your market and still miss the fear that decides the sale.
4. The market is not quiet. You may be listening to the wrong room.
5. Before you rebuild your offer, check whether the signal is real.
6. Vibes have a tax. Evidence lowers it.
7. The danger is not a bad idea. It is a good-feeling signal with no proof underneath.
8. Your strategy can look right and still lie.
9. Your best comments might be lying to you.
10. I stopped trusting content that felt smart. The receipts kept disagreeing.
11. The market did not validate your idea. It validated your framing trap.
12. That viral post may have poisoned your strategy.

## First 50-Word Gate

The first 50 words must:

- name a specific false signal
- avoid "I help" opener unless the post is clearly offer-facing
- sound like a real observation, not a funnel
- create a small sting of recognition
- lead to either the free diagnostic or a direct ask

Approved first-50 opener:

```text
Your strategy can look right and still be built on a lie.

A few smart comments. A flattering sales call. A competitor post with 400 likes. One client says "this makes sense."

Those are signals. Research Intelligence sorts evidence from expensive noise.

The Vibe Tax starts when you stop checking.
```

```text
I am testing a small false-signal diagnostic for this.
```

| Day | Move | Asset | Manual Action |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define the enemy | Anchor post with the first-50 opener | Comment on 10 ICP posts before publishing; reply live for 30 minutes |
| 2 | Show the map | "5 False Signals" text post or one-page visual | DM anyone who comments with a real example |
| 3 | Build trust | Personal post: why vibes are costly when clients depend on the work | Ask 5 warm contacts what signal they over-trust |
| 4 | Convert softly | Founding brief post: "send me one decision" | Manually review 5-10 replies, invite only qualified fits |
| 5 | Borrow attention | Break down a familiar public false signal pattern | Leave 10 high-quality comments on strategy/content/operator threads |
| 6 | Prove method | Anonymized mini-diagnostic: signal -> risk -> better check | Send the demo to warm prospects from Days 1-5 |
| 7 | Recap and route | "7 signals I would not build strategy from" | Review comments, DMs, saves, profile clicks, and paid-brief asks |
 
## Signals To Track

- comments with specific examples
- DMs with real decisions
- saves
- profile clicks
- paid-brief asks
- buyer phrases
- objections

Treat likes as suspect until they produce a decision, example, objection, or buyer-language clue.

## Distribution Boundary

Manual, qualified outreach only. No auto-DMs, fake engagement, account scraping, or external publishing without explicit approval.

# Intake

## Buyer Intake Questions

1. What offer do you want diagnosed?
2. Who is the buyer?
3. What price point are you trying to sell?
4. What page, post, deck, proposal, or pitch currently explains the offer?
5. What are the most common objections or hesitations you hear?
6. What competitors, tools, templates, or substitutes do buyers compare you against?
7. What signals are you currently using to decide whether the offer is working?
8. What would make this brief useful within 48 hours?

## Acceptance Criteria

Accept the brief when:

- the buyer has a specific offer or service
- the buyer can name the market or audience
- there is at least one asset or pitch to review
- the buyer wants sharper language, proof, positioning, or validation
- the scope can be delivered without private account access

Do not accept when:

- the buyer wants guaranteed leads
- the buyer cannot name what they sell
- the buyer only wants validation
- the buyer expects a full brand strategy
- the buyer wants automation, scraping, or public posting

```text
I can help you avoid rebuilding this from guesses.

For this project, I would run a 48-hour Vibe Tax Brief: buyer language, competitor/substitute scan, hidden objections, proof gaps, false signals, and 3-5 stronger offer angles.
```

```text
It is finding the signal you should trust before you spend more time on the offer, page, content, or campaign.
```

## Prospect Scorecard

# Prospect Scorecard

## Best Prospects

| Signal | Score |
|---|---:|
| sells a service, consulting, coaching, agency, or expert-led offer | +3 |
| has visible public copy, offer page, or posts | +3 |
| uses broad language like strategy, growth, clarity, AI, authority, scale | +2 |
| gets engagement but unclear buyer conversion | +2 |
| recently launched, repositioned, or changed offer | +2 |
| talks about market confusion, buyer clarity, proof, or content not converting | +3 |
| can pay $300-$500 for a fast diagnostic | +3 |

## Skip Prospects

| Signal | Reason |
|---|---|
| no visible offer | too early |
| no business goal | diagnostic has no anchor |
| wants only free advice | low conversion |
| wants guaranteed leads | wrong promise |
| needs full implementation first | route to workflow sprint, not Vibe Tax Brief |
| highly regulated claims without access to proof | risk too high for first brief |

## Minimum Fit

Contact if score is **8+** and there is a specific asset to reference.

Prioritize if score is **12+** and the buyer has a visible offer with fuzzy language or weak proof.

# Delivery SOP

## 48-Hour Delivery Shape

### Hour 0-2: Intake And Scope

- confirm offer, buyer, asset, price point, and current goal
- define what decision the brief must support
- list available proof and known objections

### Hour 2-8: Research Swarm

- Market truth: alternatives, competitor claims, category language
- Audience truth: buyer phrases, fears, objections, desired outcomes
- System truth: current offer, copy, content, proof, funnel, social signal
- Naming/stickiness: repeatable language, false-signal frame
- Social attention: first public angle and outreach bridge

### Hour 8-16: Synthesis

- separate verified claims from assumptions
- identify false signals
- map proof gaps and hidden objections
- write buyer-language ledger

### Hour 16-24: Brief Draft

- complete `RESEARCH-BRIEF-TEMPLATE.md`
- create 3 sales openers
- create 3 offer/content angles
- create seven-day validation plan

### Hour 24-36: Expert Gates

- Market Truth Gate
- Human Resonance Gate
- Name/Stickiness Gate
- Proof Gate
- Copy Gate

### Hour 36-48: Delivery

- final brief
- short summary
- next paid or implementation recommendation only if justified

## Scope Boundary

Included:

- research, synthesis, diagnostic, and practical language
- public/current asset review
- competitor/substitute scan
- proof and objection mapping

Not included:

- guaranteed sales
- full brand strategy
- done-for-you funnel build
- account scraping or auto outreach
- publishing

# Proof Capture

## Proof To Capture On Every Brief

| Proof Type | Prompt |
|---|---|
| Before clarity | What felt unclear before the brief? |
| Surprise | What did the brief reveal that you had not seen? |
| Buyer language | Which phrase are you going to use immediately? |
| Action | What will you change this week? |
| Confidence | What decision feels easier now? |
| Outcome | What happened after applying the brief? |

## Testimonial Ask

```text
What did the Vibe Tax Brief help you see that you were missing before?
```

## Proof Artifact Checklist

- before/after offer one-liner
- buyer phrase ledger
- proof gap list
- hidden objection map
- sales opener list
- buyer quote or reaction
- implementation request

Likes and followers are weak proxies. Track replies, calls, paid asks, buyer words, and implemented changes.
