# Stickiness Gate

Council: Heath Brothers / Made to Stick, James I. Bond / Brain Glue, Jasmin Alic.

## Commander's Intent

```text
Stop making business decisions from false signals. Find what buyers already believe before you build, post, pitch, or reposition.
```

## Core Repeatable Line

```text
Vibes have a tax. This brief finds it before you pay.
```

Repeatability score: **9/10**.

Risk: "vibe" can sound casual if overused. Keep the artifact grounded with false signals, evidence, cost, confidence, and decision risk.

## Sticky Phrase Bank

- Vibes have a tax. Evidence lowers it.
- Find the false signals before they cost you.
- Stop paying for strategy built on weak signals.
- Before you trust the signal, price the tax.
- This is not more research. It is risk removal.
- We are not checking whether the idea is interesting. We are checking whether it is expensive to believe.
- The danger is not a bad idea. The danger is a good-feeling signal with no proof underneath.
- If the signal is real, we build on it. If it is false, we catch the tax early.
- You do not need louder content. You need cleaner signal.
- The market is not quiet. You may be listening to the wrong room.
- Likes are not buyer language.
- Your offer might be clear to peers and invisible to buyers.
- AI can summarize the category, but it cannot tell you what your buyer is afraid to admit.
- The Vibe Tax is the cost of building from confident guesses.
- Before you rebuild the offer, check the signal.
- If your strategy came from vibes, the bill is already coming due.

## Fail Conditions

Copy fails if:

- it explains the method before naming the pain
- it sounds like "market research" without stakes
- it uses expert language before buyer language
- it has no concrete deliverable
- it is memorable but not true
