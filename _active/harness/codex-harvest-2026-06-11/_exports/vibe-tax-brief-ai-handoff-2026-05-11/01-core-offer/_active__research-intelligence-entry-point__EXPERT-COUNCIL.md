# Expert Council

This package is not allowed to ship as a shallow research product. The council exists to keep every artifact truthful, human, memorable, and sellable.

## Council Order

| Order | Layer | Experts | What They Must Improve |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Buyer Reality | Research Intelligence Agent, Manus AI, April Dunford | claims, alternatives, shortlist logic, no-decision risk |
| 2 | Human Psychology | Rory Sutherland, Kallaway, Donald Miller, Nicolas Cole | fear, desire, story clarity, decision friction, belief gaps |
| 3 | Naming | David Placek, James I. Bond | naming tension, repeatability, sonic grip, comfort-trap avoidance |
| 4 | Stickiness | Heath Brothers, Brain Glue, Jasmin Alic | SUCCESs, metaphor, rhythm, memorable phrases |
| 5 | Lead Magnet | Stockton Walbeck, Liam lead magnet workflow | diagnostic type, free-to-paid bridge, buyer signal |
| 6 | Social Attention | Diandra Escobar, Lara Acosta, Luke Iha, Authority Hacker, Lulu Cheng Meservey | hooks, format, first 50 words, platform-native proof |
| 7 | Sales and Proof | Luke Iha Proof Mechanisms, Nicolas Cole, April Dunford | proof gaps, objections, sales openers, buyer education |

## Operating Rule

The research finds the truth. The psychology makes it felt. The naming makes it repeatable. The social layer earns attention. The sales layer turns the attention into a clean next step.

## Explicit Anti-Patterns

- Do not sell "AI research."
- Do not lead with process jargon.
- Do not create a generic market report.
- Do not make the buyer feel analyzed from a distance.
- Do not write public copy that sounds like a strategy memo.
- Do not pass copy because it is correct; it must also have tension, humanity, and a clear next action.

## Output Standard

Every artifact should make a buyer think:

```text
That is the thing I have been feeling, but I did not know how to say it.
```
