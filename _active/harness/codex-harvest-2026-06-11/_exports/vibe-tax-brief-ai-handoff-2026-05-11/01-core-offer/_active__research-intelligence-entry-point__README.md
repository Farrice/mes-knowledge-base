## The Offer

**The Vibe Tax Brief**

A false-signal diagnostic for strategy, content, and client work.

The public promise is not "I use AI." The promise is:

```text
I help you see what your buyers already believe, doubt, compare, fear, and need to hear before they ever talk to you.
```

## Why This Exists

Most solo B2B operators, consultants, creators, coaches, agencies, and expert-led service owners are making decisions from noisy signals:

- posts peers liked
- competitor claims everyone repeats
- AI summaries that sound confident but flatten nuance
- sales calls they remember selectively
- content engagement that does not turn into qualified conversations
- market language that feels smart internally but does not match buyer fear

That hidden cost is the **vibe tax**: the money, time, reputation, and momentum lost when an offer is steered by guesses instead of buyer reality.

```text
Vibes have a tax. This brief finds it before you pay.
```

## Diagnostic Action Standard

Every finding must end with one of four actions:

| Action | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Keep | evidence is strong enough to build from |
| Revise | signal exists, but wording, proof, or buyer context is off |
| Stop | likely false signal; do not keep building from it |
| Test | promising hypothesis that needs a small market check |
