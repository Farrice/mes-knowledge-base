# Copy Gate Result

## Status

Initial package gate: **PASS WITH WATCH ITEMS**

## What Passed

- The buyer-facing promise avoids leading with AI.
- The name carries tension and repeatability.
- The free diagnostic bridges directly to the paid brief.
- The copy names human fears: wasting momentum, sounding generic, choosing wrong, listening to the wrong signal.
- Scope is constrained to a 48-hour brief.
- Every finding now has a keep, revise, stop, or test action standard.
- Social deployment now leads with judgment and "send me one decision," not a checkout link.

## Watch Items

1. **Vibe Tax** needs context the first time it appears.
2. The offer can drift into abstract research language if the copy overuses "intelligence."
3. Public posts must not sound like a webinar funnel.
4. The first proof demo must show concrete buyer phrases and before/after language.
5. Scores should not be inflated. If the user says it feels generic, treat that as a failed gate.

## Banned Public Copy Patterns

- "Unlock your market potential"
- "AI-powered research"
- "data-driven insights"
- "transform your strategy"
- "take your business to the next level"
- "comprehensive market research report"
- "I help founders clarify their messaging"

## Approved Core Lines

- Likes are not buyer language.
- You do not need louder content. You need cleaner signal.
- The market is not quiet. You may be listening to the wrong room.
- Find the false signals before they cost you strategy, content, or clients.
- The Vibe Tax is the cost of building from confident guesses.
- Vibes have a tax. This brief finds it before you pay.
- This is not more research. It is risk removal.
- Your strategy can look right and still lie.
- Before you turn a signal into strategy, check whether it is evidence or expensive noise.
