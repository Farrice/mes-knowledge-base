## Executive Answer

No official Google source publishes a comprehensive list of Google Business Profile (GBP) local ranking factors, as Google only confirms three high-level pillars—**relevance**, **distance**, and **prominence**—without detailing specifics or weights[7]. This absence enables a "smoke test" using industry surveys and analyses to infer GBP field impacts, though these rely on correlations rather than Google's direct validation.

### Verified Evidence (Directly stated in sources, primarily Google's official guidance)
- Google's sole official statement on local rankings: "Local results are mainly based on **relevance** (how well a business matches the search), **distance** (proximity to searcher), and **prominence** (how well-known the business is)"[7].
- GBP serves as Google's primary data source for Local Pack and Maps results, influencing relevance via profile data[5].
- No Google documentation lists specific GBP fields (e.g., categories, reviews) or their weights as ranking factors[1][2][3][4][6][8].

### Directional Signals (Industry consensus from 2026 surveys and analyses on GBP's influence)
GBP optimization consistently ranks as the top factor (32% weight in Local Pack per BrightLocal survey), with specific fields showing positive correlations:
- **Primary category**: #1 Local Pack factor[1].
- **Proximity to searcher/search center**: #2; address in search city #4[1][6][7].
- **Keywords in business title**: #3[1][2].
- **Reviews** (quantity, recency, star rating, text): #6-9; 20% weight[1][2][3][6].
- **Additional categories**: #8[1].
- **Open status**: #5[1][2].
- **Physical address visibility/map pin**: #7, #10[1].
- Negative signals: Incorrect category, low ratings, closed status[1][2].
Industry reports (e.g., BrightLocal 2026 survey, Whitespark) aggregate SEO expert votes, showing GBP signals as 25-32% of Local Pack rankings[1][8].

### Inference (Logical deductions from evidence; testable via smoke test)
- **Smoke test viability**: Optimize isolated GBP fields (e.g., add primary category matching query, upload photos, post updates) on test profiles and monitor Local Pack position changes, as GBP is ~30% of rankings and proximity is fixed[1][2][5][9]. Control for distance/prominence by testing identical locations/queries.
- Surveys like BrightLocal's imply GBP fields proxy Google's relevance/prominence (e.g., categories signal relevance, reviews signal prominence)[1][8].
- Consistent NAP across citations boosts pack appearance by 40% (correlational)[1].

### Unverified Assumptions (Common claims lacking primary evidence)
- Exact weights (e.g., GBP 32%, reviews 20%): From expert surveys, not Google-confirmed; may reflect biases[1][8].
- Specific impacts like "GMB photos" or "posts" as top-10 factors: SEO blogs' opinions, unverified by Google[2].
- Behavioral signals (e.g., check-ins) or backlinks directly tied to GBP: Hypothesized, not proven causal[1][3][4].

Sources lack 2026 Google updates beyond [1][8]; test via controlled GBP edits for real-world validation[9].
