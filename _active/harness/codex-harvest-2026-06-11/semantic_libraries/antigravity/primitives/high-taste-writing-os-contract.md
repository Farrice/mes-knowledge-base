# High-Taste Writing OS Contract

Use this primitive when written work needs high taste, flow, reader pull, and craft beyond "correct structure."

## Core Rule

One composer, many scalpels.

The final writer owns the voice and flow. Specialist experts diagnose specific layers and hand back concise line-level changes. Do not concatenate expert outputs or let the piece become a visible stack of frameworks.

## Mandatory Sequence

1. Reader contract.
2. User-calibrated quality baseline.
3. Material ledger.
4. Architecture map.
5. Composed draft.
6. Targeted scalpel passes.
7. Taste Evidence Ledger.

## Pass Contract

Each pass returns only:

- top 1-3 issues,
- exact lines affected,
- one suggested change per issue,
- what must be preserved,
- remaining risk.

## Quality Bar

The piece must create:

- a legible reason to keep reading,
- a private-recognition moment,
- one perspective shift,
- deliberate paragraph movement,
- concrete material,
- memorable residue,
- honest scoring tied to user or market calibration.

## Failure Modes

- Expert soup: many names, no changed lines.
- Hammer pass: every framework applied everywhere.
- Score theater: high scores without proof.
- Outline logic: coherent order but no emotional flow.
- Clean wallpaper: professional language with no lived texture.
- Clever fog: hook creates confusion instead of curiosity.
