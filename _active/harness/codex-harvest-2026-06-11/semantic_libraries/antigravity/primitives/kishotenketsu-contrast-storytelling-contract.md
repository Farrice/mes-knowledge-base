# Kishotenketsu Contrast Storytelling Contract

## Purpose

Use this primitive when a writing, content, ads, media, brand, marketing, messaging, positioning, copy, ghostwriting, product/service/concept, or general narrative task needs a non-conflict-first engine: contrast, texture, process, perspective shift, and integration.

This contract is source-grounded in `extractions/video-context/0eIN-Zdt39Q/` and deployed through `skills/lucas-alpay-storytelling/workflows/kishotenketsu-contrast-story-system.md`.

## Trigger

Use this when:

- a draft forces a villain, enemy, fake obstacle, or problem-agitation onto material that wants a quieter engine
- a piece has atmosphere but no turn
- a middle feels like filler because process is being summarized
- a brand needs to feel like a world the audience can enter
- an ad, script, campaign, or media format needs attention without rage-bait or fake urgency
- messaging or positioning needs to explain a changed category, buyer reality, workflow, or culture
- a product, service, or concept needs a lived use-case scene instead of abstract feature language
- a ghostwritten lesson sounds generic because it lacks a lived scene
- a social post needs a frame shift without rage-bait

## Core Sequence

| Step | Required Move | Failure To Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Ki | Establish ground, rules, rituals, mood, texture, and state of being. | Decorative world-building. |
| Sho | Deepen through process, familiarity, and accumulated detail. | "Nothing happens" because no pressure or attachment builds. |
| Ten | Introduce a variable, memory, time jump, point of view, or information shift. | Random twist that does not recontextualize anything. |
| Ketsu | Integrate the earlier world and the shift. | Victory/defeat ending pasted onto non-battle material. |

## Output Requirements

Every run must produce:

- source mode: transcript-backed, research-backed, inference, or example
- Ki world
- Sho process
- Ten shift
- Ketsu integration
- behavior delta vs the original
- deployment surface: fiction, social, content, ads, media, creative strategy, brand, marketing, messaging, positioning, copy, ghostwriting, product/service/concept, or general narrative work
- next gate: proof, voice, conversion, audience, platform, positioning, or publishable-copy review

## Guardrails

- Do not call Kishotenketsu universal or superior.
- Do not equate non-antagonistic structure with no stakes.
- Do not translate `Ten` only as shock twist; treat it as a recontextualizing turn.
- Do not replace offer clarity, proof, voice fidelity, or audience research.
- Do not claim visual evidence from the source unless frame/OCR evidence exists.
- Do not treat expert stacking as proof; changed writing behavior is proof.

## Behavior Proof

Capability-enhancing uses must include at least one before/after proof showing:

1. More specific texture.
2. Stronger contrast.
3. Process-as-plot.
4. Meaningful perspective shift.
5. Integration that changes interpretation or action.

The model proof lab lives at `_active/kishotenketsu-storytelling-os/07-before-after-proof-lab.md`.
