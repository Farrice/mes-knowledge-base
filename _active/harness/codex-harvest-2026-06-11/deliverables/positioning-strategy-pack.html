Market Scan

Agentic AI demand is real

Gartner projections support the future-correct thesis. Enterprises are moving toward task-specific agents and AI-agent-enabled software.

Trust and governance are the choke point

Agent projects face cancellation, sprawl, and governance risk. That validates "AI is forced to guess," but punishes vague AI promises.

Workflow redesign beats tool hype

McKinsey's AI research points to workflow redesign as a key success factor. This supports selling one focused workflow audit first.

Market implication: The market is ready to buy "my AI work is almost useful but still needs rescue." It is not yet broadly ready to buy "semantic documentation architecture" as the first phrase out of your mouth.
