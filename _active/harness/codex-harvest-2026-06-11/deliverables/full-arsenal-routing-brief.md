## No-Lazy-Path Gate

Before any major artifact ships, check:

- Is this buyer-readable in the first 5 seconds?
- Does it sell a felt pain, not an abstract category?
- Is the CTA a concrete submission or decision?
- Does every claim have proof, a constraint, or a narrower wording?
- Does the copy sound like Farrice, not an AI automation vendor?
- Does it preserve human judgment instead of promising AI replacement?
- Does it avoid LinkedIn scraping, auto-DMs, auto-comments, auto-likes, and fake engagement?
