# High-Taste Writing OS

Use this pattern when writing, content, or copy is structurally correct but still generic, low-taste, poor-flowing, or not compelling to read.

## Problem

Expert stacking can fail when every expert is treated as a broad rewrite pass. The final draft becomes a visible pile of frameworks: strategically correct, but not elegant, interwoven, or alive.

## Working Solution

Use a companion OS with one composer and narrow specialist passes.

1. Lock the reader contract.
2. Start from the user-calibrated quality baseline.
3. Build a material ledger before drafting.
4. Map the architecture of reader movement.
5. Compose one draft in one voice.
6. Run only the scalpel passes that match the failure.
7. Produce a Taste Evidence Ledger showing what changed.

## Prevention Rule

Do not fix low-taste writing by adding more expert names. Require changed lines, preserved intent, and a clear reason the reader would keep consuming.

## Routing Rule

Route through `/high-taste-writing-os` before `/publishable-copy-gate` when the issue is flow, taste, reader pull, perspective shift, or "correct but I do not want to read it." When the draft is forcing fake conflict or problem-agitation, load `skills/lucas-alpay-storytelling/workflows/kishotenketsu-contrast-story-system.md` before conversion scoring. Conversion scoring comes after the piece is worth consuming.
