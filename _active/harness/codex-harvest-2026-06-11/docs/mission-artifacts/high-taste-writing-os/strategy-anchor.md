# Strategy Anchor: High-Taste Writing OS

Created: 2026-05-10
Mission: high-taste-writing-os

## Target Problem
Writing/copy outputs are structurally correct but still low-taste, generic, poor-flowing, and not compelling enough for public or revenue-critical work. The system has strong expert assets, but they are not composed into one elegant writing process.

## Guiding Bet
Create a companion OS layer that makes one writing owner integrate narrow specialist passes. This should improve all writing/copy agents by replacing "expert soup" with a scalpel sequence: reader contract, quality baseline, material ledger, architecture map, composed draft, craft passes, and evidence ledger.

## Audience
Farrice as the operator/publisher, plus all Antigravity writing, content, copy, and knowledge-work agents that need to produce better prose.

## Key Metrics Or Proof Signals
- `/high-taste-writing-os` exists as a command-invokable workflow and bridge skill.
- Writing Agent, Copywriting Agent, Publishable Copy Gate, Autopilot, and Orchestrate surface the OS for flat/generic/low-taste writing.
- The OS verifier passes and natural language router queries surface the route.
- A pilot AI Misfire rewrite demonstrates improved flow, taste, and line-level evidence.

## Active Tracks
- Track 1: build the High-Taste Writing OS workflow and bridge.
- Track 2: integrate it into writing/copy/publication routing.
- Track 3: create validation and a pilot rewrite.
- Track 4: capture reusable solution guidance.
