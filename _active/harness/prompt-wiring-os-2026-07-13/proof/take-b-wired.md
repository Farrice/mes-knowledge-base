## CONTENT OPTIMIZATION
Targets the two lowest-scoring components:
- **Social Capital (7, lowest Value)**: Don't broaden the message to chase outside-niche appeal — that dilutes the mirror per Ground's anti-poster rule. Instead raise it *within* the niche: add one specific, rave-literate detail (a genre reference, an event-scale cue) so raver viewers feel "this person really gets it," which is what earns in-group social capital.
- **Attention Expenditure (3, highest Cost)**: Lower the psychological bar to act by making the caption explicitly no-pressure — "no reply needed, just send" — so sharing doesn't feel like it obligates a response from either party.

## SHARE TRIGGER MAP
- **Primary**: Emotional Release (nostalgia/validation)
- **Secondary**: Identity Expression, Utility Transfer
- **Segments most likely to share**: (1) Literal kandi-keepers — highest propensity, the video is a direct mirror; (2) Lapsed ravers (haven't been to an event in years, still identify) — reconnection utility is highest here; (3) Rave best-friend/couple pairs — established relationship makes the share feel low-risk, not random.

## SHARING CONTEXT DESIGN (DM Share · Instagram Reels)
Reels DM previews often autoplay muted — the macro kandi shot must read and compel with sound off (visual hook carries Checkpoint 1). Script front-loads the object (kandi in hand) before the face, so the preview thumbnail alone signals "this is about kandi," not "talking head video." Caption supplies the exact send-line so the sharer doesn't have to compose one.
