# MY.BPM — Week-1 Hero Video: "I Still Have Your Kandi"

**Product**: The Kandi Keeper tee (LEAD design, scorecard 5.0) — PLUR Soul collection, `mybpm.store/collections/plur-soul`
**Duration**: 0:40 (within 30-45s brief)
**Platform**: IG Reels / TikTok (short-form, same-type native — do not cross-post to YouTube without re-cutting)
**Game selected**: Conversion (Pattern 33) — audience already understands kandi/PLUR (embedded understanding, Pattern 7), so the content skips category education and goes straight to identity recognition → single CTA
**Psychology principles activated**: Desire Mapping (belonging), Personalization (direct "you" address), Proof (real physical kandi on camera, not a claimed stat)

**Rehook map**: loop opens at 0:00 (why keep it), closes/reopens at 0:09 (who was it), closes/reopens at 0:17 (why does this matter now), closes at 0:33 (the shirt is the answer), final loop at 0:40 (tag/share). No section coasts past 8 seconds without a new beat.

**Production note**: the narrator should be the founder or a team member with real kandi and a real story — swap the specific details in Shot 2-3 to what actually happened. The proof only works if it's true; a stand-in story reads as staged the moment a real raver watches it twice.

## SHAREABILITY ENGINEERING (estimate, not measured data — engineering judgment only)

| Share Value | /10 | Reasoning |

| Utility Transfer | 3 | No practical utility; this is pure identity/emotion |
| Emotional Release | 8 | Nostalgia + validation ("finally someone said the quiet part") |

| Share Cost | /10 | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Social Risk | 2 | Low — sentimental, not polarizing |
| Effort Required | 2 | One-tap share, works muted/silent via on-screen text |
| Attention Expenditure | 3 | Minor — sharer isn't spending earned credibility, just relaying a feeling |

**SC = 25/40 ÷ 7/40 ≈ 3.6** — estimate, above the 2.0 exponential-spread threshold on the framework's scale. Primary share motivation: Emotional Release + Identity Expression, best suited to DM shares ("thought of you" to the specific person from the story) more than public feed shares.

## WHY THIS DESIGN, NOT THE OTHER TWO

Kandi Keeper is the only one of the three LEAD designs (Kandi Keeper, Stranger-to-Friend, Sunrise Survivor) built around a physical, on-camera prop. Stranger-to-Friend and Sunrise Survivor are strong for static listing copy but have no equivalent tangible object to shoot — Kandi Keeper is the one video-native concept in the slate.
