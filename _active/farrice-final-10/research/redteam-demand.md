# Red-Team: Demand & Willingness-to-Pay — "The Final 10%"

**Angle:** Is there real, paying demand for "help an established expert build an AI-powered business system + the inner-game to run it" at $5–8K sprint / $2–3K/mo advisory? Who already pays for adjacent things, at what price? Is the multi-passionate / stuck-at-90% operator a buyer or a broke tire-kicker?

**Adversarial stance:** Find the reasons this fails. Grounded in live web sources (July 2026). No fabricated stats or competitors. Confidence labels: VERIFIED / LIKELY / UNCONFIRMED.

---

## THESIS (the one truth)

**The price band is validated and conservative — but the money lives in the revenue-qualified established expert with ONE concrete bottleneck (Farrice's own deep ICP, "Dr. Maya Patel," $180–350K/yr), NOT in the "multi-passionate / forever-learning / stuck-at-90%" psychographic the SEED leads with. That psychographic is monetized by its own category leaders — Dan Koe and Emilie Wapnick's Puttylike — at ~$29/month, and it is the single highest-churn, highest-refund profile in the coaching market. Sold on the multi-passionate/course-graveyard *identity*, "The Final 10%" fills The Room with tire-kickers who don't sustain $2–3K/mo. Sold on a *named operational bottleneck* to people who already have revenue, it converts at the SEED's prices. The SEED conflates these two buyers. That conflation — not the price, not the category — is the kill-risk to fix before launch.**

Viability signal: **QUALIFIED-YES** (demand + price are real; viability is conditional on redefining the buyer by revenue + bottleneck rather than psychographic).

---

## Why this matters

Farrice's freedom math depends on filling ~25–30 seats of $2–3K/mo advisory. If the fill is drawn from the multi-passionate/stuck identity, the front-end will convert lookie-loos who cancel by month 2 (the exact "stuck at 90%" trait predicts non-follow-through). If the fill is drawn from revenue-bearing experts with a bottleneck, the same price sustains. The difference between those two buyers is the difference between a business and a churn treadmill. This is a positioning decision worth more than any asset in the build.

## Counter-reads (where the thesis could be wrong)

1. **The DFY structure genuinely defuses the "won't execute" objection.** Because Farrice *builds* the system (done-for-you), the client's chronic non-execution matters far less for the Sprint than it would for a course — you're not asking the stuck person to finish anything; you finish it for them. If the inner-game work is real and accountability-heavy (not motivation-only), the same research says completion/retention can hit 70–80%. So the churn risk is a design problem the offer is *aware of*, not an automatic kill. The thesis holds only if the buyer still has the cash and a reason to pay $5–8K while still "stuck."
2. **"Stuck at 90%" can co-exist with real revenue.** Dr. Maya Patel is both accomplished ($180–350K) *and* stuck (18 months of LinkedIn paralysis). The psychographic and the revenue-qualification are not mutually exclusive — the failure mode is only if marketing leads with the identity in a way that over-indexes on the broke, pre-revenue version of it. The fix is a qualifier ("already earning $X, one bottleneck"), not abandoning the psychographic.
3. **The $29/mo comps sell information/community; Farrice sells done-for-you + advisory — a different category.** Dan Koe/Puttylike prove the *low-ticket* ceiling for *content and community*, not for *bespoke build + advisory*. It's fair to argue they under-monetize on purpose. But the burden of proof is still on Farrice: no VERIFIED comp shows this audience paying $5–8K, so he must qualify hard on revenue to avoid inheriting the $29/mo willingness-to-pay.

**What would change my mind toward strong-yes:** a live operator selling the exact combo (>$3K, solo experts, DFY AI + structured inner-game) with public proof of retention. I could not find one. **What would confirm the kill-risk:** if Farrice's first 5 discovery calls skew pre-revenue "I've bought 12 courses" types rather than "$150K/yr, one bottleneck" experts.

## Recommended next move

**Re-cut the buyer on the SEED before any asset ships — from a psychographic to a revenue-qualified operator with a named bottleneck.** Concretely:
1. Add a hard qualifier to WHO: *"Established experts already earning ~$120K+/yr with one specific bottleneck they can name"* — keep "multi-passionate/stuck-at-90%" as the *emotional resonance layer* (how they feel), never as the *qualification layer* (who gets in). Lead marketing with the bottleneck (Maya Patel's "invisible online / referral ceiling"), carry the identity as the trust accelerator underneath.
2. Run a **5-call price-and-buyer probe** (organic, from Parallax/LinkedIn) before building: pitch the Sprint at $5–8K to 5 self-identified "stuck" experts and log (a) their current revenue, (b) whether they name a concrete bottleneck, (c) whether they'll put down a deposit. If the deposits come from revenue-bearing/bottleneck people, ship. If they come from pre-revenue course-buyers who "love the idea," the thesis is confirmed and the buyer must be re-cut harder.
3. **Do not model the freedom math on 25–30 seats until the fill model is proven.** The "front-end content → ~$2K/mo advisory, 50–60 members solo+VA" pattern referenced in the SEED ("Whiting's model") could **not be verified** against any named public operator this session — treat it as an ASSUMPTION, not a benchmark.
