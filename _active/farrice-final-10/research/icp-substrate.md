## PART 1 — CORE BELIEFS TO SHIFT (the deep-canvass layers, adapted to this offer)

Seven beliefs the buyer holds as *true* that each independently block the purchase. For every one: the belief, which McRaney layer it lives in, why it blocks, and the shift target. **A Final 10% asset that doesn't move at least one of these is decoration.**

### B1 — "I just need one more tool / course / framework, and *then* I'll break through."
- **Layer:** L5 Motivated Reasoning ("I'm not ready yet") + L1 Belief. The **forever-learning loop / course graveyard** — they've spent $5K–$20K+, can *teach* the material, can't *apply* it.
- **Why it blocks:** it reframes every offer (including this one) as "another input," and the fix for too-much-input can't itself look like more input.
- **Shift to:** the gap was never knowledge. More input *deepens* the trap — it's sophisticated procrastination with a receipt. **The Final 10% is the last thing you buy because it's the thing that makes the buying stop.**

### B2 — "This is a discipline / willpower problem. If I just pushed harder or got organized, I'd finish."
- **Layer:** L4 Identity + L2 Attitudes (frustration: "I shouldn't have to"). They read the 90%-stall as a *character* failure and white-knuckle it.
- **Why it blocks:** if it's willpower, the solution is *effort*, not *architecture* — so they don't hire an architect, they re-resolve.
- **Shift to:** you stall at 90% **by design** — it's a protection pattern, not a lazy streak. Willpower has lost this fight for years and will keep losing. The fix is a system *engineered to absorb the final 10% for you*, so execution stops depending on how you feel that day.

### B3 — "A serious expert like me should be able to figure this out myself — *especially* now, with AI."
- **Layer:** L4 Identity Entanglement ("I am the expert, NOT someone who needs help") + the **Curse of Expertise** (their depth makes their own blind spot invisible to them).
- **Why it blocks:** buying help collides with the self-image of the capable operator; with AI on the table, the "I can build this myself" story gets *stronger*, not weaker.
- **Shift to:** your expertise is *exactly why* you can't see your own 90% — you're too close to the frame you built. An architect who builds the system *around your genius* isn't a crutch, it's leverage. The most capable operators buy leverage; they don't prove independence by staying stuck.

### B4 — "Mindset / inner work is soft. I need the tactical system, not therapy I didn't ask for." (and its twin: "I've *done* the inner work and I'm still stuck.")
- **Layer:** L3 Values (substance over style; visceral distrust of anything "woo") — *or*, for the more self-aware buyer, L5 evidence-excuse ("I journal, I've read Neville, still 90%").
- **Why it blocks:** the "systems AND soul" pitch reads as either fluff (to the tactician) or *been-there* (to the seeker) — both dismiss the alignment half, which is the half that makes the system stick.
- **Shift to:** the inner game **is** the strategy — not a vibe, a mechanism. The reason the last five systems didn't stick isn't that they were bad systems; it's that nobody installed the operator who runs them. This isn't therapy and it isn't affirmations — it's closing the *alignment gap* so the architecture actually gets used.

### B5 — "Buying help means I failed. Admitting I can't do this myself is the shameful part."
- **Layer:** L2 Imposter Syndrome + L4 Identity ("I am a helper, not someone who needs helping"). Identity threat fires the same circuits as physical threat → pre-rational avoidance.
- **Why it blocks:** the purchase itself becomes the thing to avoid, because reaching for it *confirms* the feared story ("I'm the expert who couldn't").
- **Shift to:** staying stuck to protect the self-image **is** the failure — quietly, for years. Hiring an architect is what capable people do the second the cost of DIY exceeds the cost of help. The shame isn't in the ask; it's in another year at 90%.

### B6 — "An AI-powered business means generic, soulless, losing what makes me *me*."
- **Layer:** L3 Values (integrity/authenticity; visceral disgust at guru culture and "AI slop"). Their whole edge is that they're *not* interchangeable.
- **Why it blocks:** "AI-powered" pattern-matches to *commoditized* — the exact fate they fear — so the vehicle triggers rejection before the outcome is heard.
- **Shift to:** **systems AND soul.** AI built *around your genius* amplifies your voice; it doesn't average you into the feed. The point isn't more output — it's your actual thinking, running in your margins, sounding like you. "AI with Heart" is the antidote to the slop, not more of it.

### B7 — "Investing $5–8K in *myself* is risky / indulgent when I haven't proven this works."
- **Layer:** L5 Motivated Reasoning (data/evidence excuse) + Farrice-lived **scarcity programming**. Feast-or-famine pipeline makes any spend feel unsafe.
- **Why it blocks:** the price gets measured against *this month's* volatility instead of against the *cost of staying stuck*, so it always feels like the wrong time.
- **Shift to:** run the real math — another year at 90% (unshipped offers, capped income, the compounding tax of confusion) dwarfs the sprint. This is the spend that *ends* the other spending. Scarcity is the programming that keeps the 90%-pattern alive; naming it is step one of breaking it.
