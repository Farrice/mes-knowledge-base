> **Serves:** the RESHAPED offer (per `research/RED-TEAM-VERDICT.md`), not the original SEED. ## 1. THE DOMINO STATEMENT

**One belief. If it goes in, every objection to The Final 10% dies on the way to your mouth.**

### Mode A — raw internal spine (spine-check; never shipped as-is)

> You don't have a knowledge problem or a discipline problem. You have a final-10% problem — and the final 10% is the one thing you cannot see, buy, or build from inside your own head. Every course, tool, and hard week you've thrown at this was more of the 90% you're already elite at. You've been trying to finish with the exact thing that keeps you stuck.

### Mode B — deployable (Farrice's voice, the public through-line)

> **The thing standing between you and shipped was never another course, another tool, or one more disciplined month — it's the final 10%, and the final 10% is a design problem you can't solve from inside the same expertise that built the first 90%.**

**Spine test (Mode B):** the wrong-fit reader (still believes they're one tool or one willpower-surge from done) gets defensive. The right-fit reader (a drafts folder full of 90%-done builds, 18 months of "I'll do it tomorrow") feels *caught*. That is the domino working. `[LIVED]` + `[ICP-SOURCED]`

**Why this is a domino and not a benefit:** a benefit ("you'll ship faster") makes the offer attractive. This makes the buyer's *current situation untenable* and reframes every prior purchase as a category error. It sits **upstream** of the objections — it does not rebut them, it makes them irrelevant before they form.

**The inevitability chain it installs:**
1. The final 10% cannot be crossed from inside your own head. →
2. So every *inside-your-head* input — courses, tools, prompts, willpower, floating mindset work — is structurally incapable of closing it. You've proven this yourself, repeatedly. →
3. The only category that can close it is an architect from **outside** your blind spot who builds the system *with* you and installs the operator who runs it. →
4. The most capable operators buy exactly this the second DIY costs more than staying stuck. It reads as weakness only to people who have never run that math. →
5. So the diagnostic → sprint → advisory isn't a purchase to justify. It's the exit. Every objection is downstream noise.

## 3. THE INSTALL SEQUENCE (dissolve, don't argue — × McRaney)

You do not install this by asserting it louder. You surface the belief they hold, contrast it with a frame that exposes the gap, and let them *arrive* at the domino as their own conclusion. The strongest install is the one where they think they figured it out.

### Primary install — dissolving the willpower/knowledge belief

**Current belief being dissolved:** *"This is a discipline problem — if I just got organized or pushed harder, I'd finish."* `[ICP-SOURCED]` B2 + *"I just need one more tool/course and then I'll break through."* `[ICP-SOURCED]` B1

- **SURFACE (generously, so they feel seen):** "You think you're one disciplined month — or one better system — away from finally shipping this. You've thought that for a while. And here's what's true: you're not lazy. You're the most capable person in most rooms you walk into. You can *teach* the material you can't seem to finish applying."
- **CONTRAST (expose the gap):** "So explain the drafts folder. Explain the course you could teach but abandoned at module two. And the 18 months of 'tomorrow' — what discipline problem survives *that much* discipline? What knowledge problem survives someone who could stand up and teach it? What you have isn't a 90% you never reached. It's a 10% you can't *see* — because you're standing inside the exact expertise that built the first 90%."
- **ARRIVE (× Fladlien indirect — never "so buy my thing"):** "...huh. I've been trying to close the last 10% with more of the first 90% this whole time."

### Micro-install A — the DIY-with-AI operator (this buyer's sharpest resistance)

**Dissolving:** *"I can prompt my way to this myself."* `[LIVED]`

- **SURFACE:** "You genuinely can build 90% of this yourself. Not a hype line — you've *done* it. Repeatedly."
- **CONTRAST:** "Which is exactly the problem. It's *at* 90% right now, in a folder, because the part you can't prompt is the part that lives in your blind spot. The model gives you more options. An over-optioned expert doesn't need more options."
- **ARRIVE:** "Prompting was never the gap. Finishing was."

### Micro-install B — the visibility surface-entry (the researched on-ramp)

**Dissolving:** *"My work should speak for itself / I just can't seem to put myself out there."* `[ICP-SOURCED]`

- **SURFACE:** "You're brilliant in the room and invisible in the feed. Half-as-qualified, twice-as-loud people are getting found instead of you. It makes you angry, then it makes you sad, because the people who need you can't find you." (`[ICP-SOURCED]` — the wine-confession core)
- **CONTRAST:** "But notice *what's actually stuck*. It's not that you don't know what to say. The system that would make you findable — the authority engine — has been sitting at 90% in your drafts for months. Same pattern, higher stakes. You're not invisible because you can't. You're invisible because the thing that fixes it is the exact thing you keep not finishing."
- **ARRIVE:** "The visibility problem and the finishing problem are the same problem wearing two coats."

> **Ethics guardrail on the install:** the frame is *"you're blind to your own 10%, like every expert is to their own"* — NOT *"you're broken and only I can save you."* Keep the "leverage the capable buy, not a crutch for the incapable" nuance in every install, or it tips from persuasion into manipulation. (`icp-substrate` B3.)

## 5. POST-PURCHASE RECONFIRMATION (retention = reselling the domino)

The sale doesn't end the install — it raises the stakes. Every asset a *client* sees either reconfirms "I made the right call" or seeds doubt.

- **Onboarding (first sprint session):** name it out loud — "Session one is us finding and closing your actual final 10%." The buyer hears their own belief confirmed the moment they commit: *you were right, the 90% was never the problem.*
- **The Room (client-only advisory):** the domino becomes the *operating language*. Every review-the-data-direct-the-move touch opens with "where's your current 10%?" The belief runs the relationship, not just the sale.
- **Client content / community:** Farrice publicly running his *own* 10%-system reconfirms every client's decision each time they see it (self-as-case, ongoing — Ethics-Gate-clean, no client testimony required).
- **Graduation-as-proof:** a client running the system solo is the living domino. Feature the *mechanism* they now own (never invented results) so the cohort re-installs the belief off a peer, not a pitch.
