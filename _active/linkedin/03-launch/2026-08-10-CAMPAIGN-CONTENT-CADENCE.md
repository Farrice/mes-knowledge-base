## Publishing Rules (Binding)

1. **No concurrent carousel posts** — space them 2–4 days apart (avoid message stack)
2. **POV posts alternate with carousels** — rhythm pattern preserves feed diversity
3. **CTA consistency** — carousels end with "DM ANGLE", POV posts end with earned questions or declarations
4. **Prose gate** — every post classified <7 slop before publish (no off-brand tells)
5. **Content ecosystem** — no phrase/closing pattern repeats across the 7 pieces (measured against `_active/farrice-brand/voice/VOICE-CARD.md`)
6. **Publish timing** — carousels at midweek noon (higher engagement window for LinkedIn carousel format); POV posts at morning (thought-leadership beat)

## Contingency Scenarios

### If early response (Days 1–3)
- **Hold conversation live** (do not publish carousel yet; let DM thread show authority instead)
- **Resume carousel schedule once conversation resolved** or reaches "held" stage
- **Note in CASH-SCOREBOARD:** move prospect to "held" stage

### If objection on price (any day)
- **Make-right offer available:** $500 pilot + second angle free if issues found (per `02-offer/ANGLE-MAP-ACTIVATION-PACKET.md`)
- **Carousel + POV posts continue on schedule** (not affected by objections)
- **Note:** prospect moves to "held / objection managed" stage

### If no responses by Day 7
- **Light follow-up on Day 8:** "Checking in — any questions about the angles I sent?" (one message, no pressure)
- **Continue carousel schedule** (don't slow down on zero response)
- **On Day 10 (close deadline), reassess:** if still no response, move prospect to "no-response" and focus remaining energy on any "held" conversations

### If teardown verdicts are rejected
- **Pivot to published carousel stagger:** replace rejected teardowns with additional POV posts or re-publish earlier carousels
- **Minimum requirement:** maintain 1 asset publish every 2–3 days (keep feed active during sell window)
- **Adjust below; do not skip the window**
