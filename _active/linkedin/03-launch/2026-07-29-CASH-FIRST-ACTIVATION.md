# Cash-First Activation — The Angle Map

## The decision

Run one business for the next 14 selling days:

> AI-enabled Proof-to-Market implementation for established supplement and performance brands.

The entry offer is **The Angle Map — $750 prepaid**. The core conversion is the **Proof-to-Market Sprint — $2,500 prepaid**.

- Research and ICP intelligence are the engine.
- AI is implementation leverage.
- Copy, positioning, and marketing strategy are the result the buyer receives.

Everything else is parked until the decision test says otherwise.

## When the clock starts

**Day 1 starts when five qualified external messages have been sent and logged.**

Drafts, profile edits, prospect research, and payment setup do not start the clock. This keeps a missed calendar date from being mistaken for market evidence.

## Before the first send

The one-gap message system, first-wave prospect queue, pipeline, and cash scoreboard must exist. They do.

A booking tool is optional because direct two-time scheduling is ready. A real way to accept **$750 prepaid** must exist before the second touch, qualified conversation, or price handoff—whichever comes first. Do not let payment setup postpone the first five messages, and do not quote a buyer if payment cannot be accepted immediately.

Do not build a website, deck, lead magnet, course, content system, or new offer before these rails are live.

## Buyer qualification

Pursue an already-selling supplement, functional-nutrition, recovery, or performance brand when all four conditions are present:

- A visible launch, repositioning, retail expansion, new ingredient, or campaign trigger.
- A message, proof, or buyer-decision gap that can be named from public material.
- An authorized buyer: founder, Head of Brand, VP Marketing, or product-marketing lead.
- A legitimate business that can provide its approved evidence and claim boundaries.

Exclude pre-revenue wellness coaches, buyers seeking legal certification, and buyers asking for guaranteed revenue, rankings, legal clearance, or AI citations.

## Selling days 1–5

Before any writing or content work:

- Send **15 new qualified one-gap messages per day**.
- Send **five follow-ups per day** once follow-ups are due.
- Spend no more than **15–20 minutes** researching one prospect.
- Lead with a visible gap, not a biography or a free strategy document.
- Name **The Angle Map and its $750 price no later than the second message**.
- Record every send and outcome in the scoreboard.

Do not provide a deep free teardown. One useful observation is enough to earn the next conversation.

## Selling days 6–10

Continue 15 new messages and five follow-ups per day.

- Turn qualified replies into a short live conversation.
- State the price directly.
- Send the payment link during or immediately after the conversation.
- Close by Day 10 so payment-processing lag does not consume the test window.

**First acceptance threshold:** one Angle Map sold and **$750 collected**.

## Selling days 11–14

- Deliver every paid Angle Map within 48 hours.
- Capture the buyer's initial problem, the decision delivered, the buyer's reaction, and the permission level for proof.
- Offer the $2,500 Sprint only when the buyer needs the angles translated into full positioning, proof hierarchy, launch messaging, and a priority campaign asset.

**Day-14 floor:** $750 cleared.  
**Stretch:** one Angle Map plus one Sprint.

## The only scoreboard

| Measure | Counts when |
|---|---|
| **Sent** | A qualified, personalized message reaches a real authorized buyer or credible route to that buyer |
| **Held** | A qualified two-way sales conversation occurs by message, phone, or video |
| **Sold** | The buyer explicitly accepts the scoped paid offer |
| **Collected** | Cleared payment is received |

Likes, comments, profile views, documents, compliments, and “sounds interesting” do not validate the offer.

## Decision tests

| Observed signal | Diagnosis | Required response |
|---|---|---|
| Zero replies after 50 well-targeted sends | List or opening-message failure | Rewrite targeting and opener; keep the service unchanged |
| Fewer than five qualified conversations after roughly 100 sends | Reach, channel, or buyer-trigger failure | Lead more directly with the $750 offer and sharpen the trigger |
| Conversations occur but no price is given | Seller-execution failure | Require a price quote in every qualified conversation |
| 20 qualified conversations, price quoted, zero sales | Exact-offer failure | Kill or materially reposition the Angle Map |
| One paid Angle Map | Initial offer evidence | Deliver, document proof, and pursue the Sprint conversion |
| Two Angle Maps plus one Sprint conversion within 30 days | Repeatability signal | Commit to the niche for 90 days and standardize delivery |

## Scope and liability gates

- Claim-safety work is editorial evidence mapping, not legal advice.
- The client's attorney or compliance owner keeps final approval.
- AI-search is a supporting pattern read, never a ranking or citation promise.
- Decline any request for guaranteed revenue, legal clearance, rankings, or AI citations.

## The single fallback

Activate this only after the 20-qualified-conversation kill condition:

> **7-Day Research-to-Campaign AI Workflow Install — $1,500 prepaid** for 5–25-person boutique agencies.

It converts one real client's interviews, reviews, and competitive research into an ICP decision map, campaign brief, angle bank, QA workflow, and team handoff using the agency's existing tools. No custom software and no vague AI audit.

## 90-day continuation, only after a pass

1. **Days 15–30:** deliver two Angle Maps, close the first Sprint, and document one permissioned proof story.
2. **Days 31–60:** specialize the Sprint around one occasion: launch, retail expansion, or repositioning.
3. **Days 61–90:** test a $3,000–$5,000 monthly implementation layer for research updates, new angles, message deployment, and evidence/quality review.
4. **Success standard:** four paid Angle Maps, two Sprint conversions, and one recurring implementation client.
