# Carousel 01 — Transparent Labs

## Slide 1

Your product works.

Getting a stranger to believe it is a different job.

---

## Slide 2

Transparent Labs' homepage: "Hold your supplements to a higher standard. Clean ingredients. Transparent labels."

Here's what their customers actually say.

"It tastes just like chocolate ice cream."

"Mixes easy."

"So delicious."

---

## Slide 3

Their testing page says they use "an accredited lab."

Not the lab. An accredited lab.

Every competitor your customer checks on the same Friday already prints an accredited lab too.

---

## Slide 4

Transparent Labs Bulk Pre-Workout:

Eight grams of L-citrulline malate.

Four grams of beta-alanine.

2,500mg of betaine.

Every single number on the label.

---

## Slide 5

So here's the gap.

They paid for a formula.

Their customers bought a flavor.

The company is winning. The brand stays swappable with something half as good.

---

## Slide 6

If your label opens like that—

Let your hero be the numbers.

Eight grams. On a photo. One sentence underneath.

Your competitor's hero becomes a comparison the moment yours is a number.

---

## Slide 7

"An accredited lab" costs you nothing to say.

"LightLabs" costs you authority when you don't.

One sentence and one logo on your testing page. That's the difference between a promise and a receipt.

---

## Slide 8

Your reviews say:

"No digestive issues."

"Absorbs quickly."

"Helps with my recovery."

Tie each one back to a printed number. The claim stays factual. The dose does the persuading. The language stays theirs.

---

## Slide 9

The rule: dose disclosure is a fact about the product, not a health claim.

Pair a printed dose with a promised outcome, you're in structure/function territory and you need the evidence behind it.

Keep the dose factual. Let the reader draw the conclusion.

The whole campaign stays inside the fence.

---

## Slide 10

This isn't a supplement problem.

It's what happens when your real advantage is a number and your marketing is a mood.

If your product actually works and nobody can tell—

DM PROOF.
