# Carousel 02 — Momentous

## Slide 1

A brand can prove its product is safe.

Getting a buyer to feel it's worth wanting is a different job.

---

## Slide 2

Momentous has the deepest evidence surface I've read in this category.

Six meta-analyses cited on the creatine page. The testing house named, with the month. Two percent of revenue spent on third-party testing, published as a number.

---

## Slide 3

And this—the best sentence on their whole site:

"The trust of all 32 NFL teams and 200+ collegiate and professional sports teams."

Thirty-two out of thirty-two. That's not a testimonial. It's a census.

---

## Slide 4

It's on an interior page.

The homepage hero is about mixability.

---

## Slide 5

After the price, a buyer reads three bullets:

"New Signature Spec creatine standard."
"Ultrafine powder for exceptional mixability."
"Unrivaled 6-stage testing & certification."

Three lines. All three about the factory.

---

## Slide 6

None about what creatine does to a person.

On a creatine page.

---

## Slide 7

Now read what the customers wrote back.

The site's own review summary: "Customers say this creatine powder is completely tasteless and dissolves easily in various beverages, from coffee to smoothies."

Recent reviews:

"I can't even tell that it's in my tea."
"Best diluting creatine I've tried."
"Appreciated that it has no flavor too."

---

## Slide 8

One five-star review, in full: "The order arrived on time, in good shape, as described."

Another five-star: "I haven't taken it long enough to see any effects yet."

The 4.9 rating is real. It's measuring dissolution, delivery, and trust.

Effect barely shows up.

---

## Slide 9

Purity is a floor.

Nobody has ever felt a floor.

This isn't a supplement thing. Any company that wins on rigor eventually starts selling the rigor instead of the result. Enterprise software sells its SOC 2. Agencies sell their process deck.

Both are true. Both are floors. Neither is why anybody buys.

---

## Slide 10

There are thirty-two NFL locker rooms with this stuff in them.

That's the ad.

If your product actually works and nobody can tell—

DM PROOF.
