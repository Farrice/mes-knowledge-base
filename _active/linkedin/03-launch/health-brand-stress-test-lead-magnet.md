# Health Brand Launch Message Stress Test

**The 12-Question Diagnostic**

> Designed for: Founders, heads of brand, and product marketing leads at supplement, performance nutrition, wellness, or health-tech companies preparing a launch, repositioning, retail expansion, or fundraise.

> Duration: ~10 minutes  
> Scoring: Rough assessment of positioning maturity (not a full audit)  
> Purpose: Surface whether you need messaging clarity *before* you spend on creative, media, or retail  

---

## Scoring & Interpretation

**Add up all 12 scores. Your total is between 12 and 60.**

| Score | Interpretation | Next Move |
|-------|---|---|
| **12–24** | **Positioning is unsettled.** Your message is still forming. Spend 10 days clarifying before creative/media spend. Otherwise you'll waste budget proving an unresolved message. | Message clarity sprint (10 days) → then execute. |
| **25–36** | **Positioning is forming but not unified.** You have pieces (founder story, category insight, claim evidence) but they're not wired together. Your team can't pitch consistently yet. | Positioning audit + messaging architecture (5–7 days) → then test. |
| **37–48** | **Positioning is mostly locked, but gaps exist.** Your core message is clear, but competitive differentiation or claim architecture needs tightening before you scale. | Messaging refinement sprint (3–5 days) → then scale. |
| **49–60** | **Positioning is mature.** Your message is clear, defensible, and deployable. Focus shifts to execution: content, retail, media. You're ready to spend. | Execute messaging architecture; optimize media spend. |

---

## What This Test Doesn't Measure

- **Content quality** — this test is about positioning clarity, not whether your content is good
- **Market fit** — just because you're clear doesn't mean the market wants it (launch will tell you)
- **Sales capability** — clarity + execution are different problems
- **Brand recognition** — this assumes you're starting from scratch or repositioning, not defending an existing moat

---

## What to Do Next

**If your score is 12–36:** Book a 30-min call. Most brands at this stage need positioning clarity before they spend. The 10-day sprint fixes this.

**If your score is 37–48:** You're close. Could go either way — tighten up the gaps or launch and learn. A 3–5 day refinement sprint could accelerate the launch by weeks (fewer retailer objections, faster investor conviction, cleaner content brief).

**If your score is 49–60:** Ship it. Your positioning is ready. Focus on execution and measurement.
