## Part 1 — Skills

### The thread that makes this work: Consumer Insight on all four entries

One skill appears on every entry, and it is the reason the profile reads as one career instead of two.

**Consumer Insight** is what turns eighteen years of coaching into market research. Without it, a founder
scrolling your profile sees a marketing consultant on top and a personal trainer underneath, and has to do
the connecting work himself. With it tagged on Equinox, on the Vitamin Shoppe, on the coaching practice,
and on the founder role, the profile makes the argument for you — *this man has been studying buyers the
entire time.*

It also concentrates endorsements on the one skill that bridges both halves. Endorsements scatter across
fifteen skills mean nothing; forty on Consumer Insight is a signal.

### Pinned top three (search weight)

```text
1. Creative Strategy
2. Brand Positioning
3. Product Marketing
```

Unchanged from the master doc's ruling, and correct. These are what a supplement CMO types into search.

### Per-entry tagging — 5 each

**Founder & Creative Strategist**
```text
Creative Strategy · Brand Positioning · Product Marketing · Message Development · Consumer Insight
```
*Copywriting is deliberately absent here.* Your own copy says *"I don't build the ads."* Tagging Copywriting

disqualifying. Keep it in the profile list for search; keep it off this entry.

**Private Performance Coach**
```text

```
Consumer Insight leads on purpose. It stops this entry from reading as a fitness side hustle and makes it
read as what it is — your live line to the buyer.

**Equinox**
```text
Strength and Conditioning · Personal Training · Nutrition · Behavior Change · Consumer Insight
```

**The Vitamin Shoppe**
```text
Nutritional Supplements · Sports Nutrition · Consumer Insight · Market Research · Product Marketing
```

Market Research and Product Marketing on a retail job look odd until someone reads the description — then
they're the whole point. This is the entry where the tagging does argumentative work.

### Two skills to add

`Performance Coaching` and `Behavior Change` are not on the master doc's list of fifteen. They're accurate,

## Part 2 — Media

### The rule that governs this section

Josh Sanders' visitor map assigns each profile section a primary visitor to convert. **Experience converts
the Cold Outreach Target — the skeptic who scrolls all the way down looking for a reason to say no.**

That makes Experience media **verification, not promotion.** A skeptic doing a red-flag check is not
persuaded by a graphic. He is either reassured by something checkable, or he is made more suspicious by
something that looks like marketing this far down the page.

Most profiles get this backwards and staple decks to job entries. Don't.

### The single biggest gap is not an attachment

**Your banner does not exist yet.**

It's the first thing every Comment Stalker and Cold Outreach visitor sees, it has to answer *"what does this
man do"* in one visual scan, and right now it's blank or default. No Experience attachment moves the needle
the way this does. The copy hierarchy is already locked in §3 of the master doc.

**This is the #1 media priority and it isn't close.** Everything below is secondary.
