## Note on this segment vs. the invisible-expert inventory

This is a **different buyer** than `scene-inventory.md` (the invisible-expert profile). That ICP's
wound is *not being seen*; this one's wound is *being seen and not believed*. The wellness founder
already has an audience, a product, and often real proof — the failure is that the proof sits
somewhere a buyer's eye never lands (an FAQ accordion, a footnote, a QR code nobody scans). Every
scene below is built around that single structural gap: **claim vs. proof vs. placement**, the
exact shape all three read teardowns independently converge on.

## Fixed probe subject

> **A phone or tablet screen showing a collapsed FAQ accordion or small-type footnote holding the
> company's strongest claim, thumb or cursor resting above it, not yet acting on it.** (P1)

**Why this one:** every teardown read for this inventory — Puori, Create Wellness, Hilma, three
different founders, three different products — independently converges on this exact physical
shape: the real proof sitting one UI-fold below where a buyer's eye ever lands. It is the
structural finding of the whole content line (§4, every teardown: "the gap in one line"), not a
one-off image. It is also the scene most weeks of this content engine will need — a new brand,
same claim-buried-in-small-type shot — which is Flynn's test (produce most, not most interesting).
Do not change it mid-sweep.
