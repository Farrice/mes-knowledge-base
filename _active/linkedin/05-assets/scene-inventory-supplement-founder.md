## Fixed probe subject

> **A product page open beside its own review widget, the label's exact dosage circled on one tab while the reviews scroll past without a single number in them.** (P2)

**Why this one:** teardowns are this brand's highest-frequency production motion — six shipped in the two weeks preceding this inventory, on a standing weekly cadence — and every teardown runs the same mechanism P2 depicts: real proof (a dose, a study, a lab) sitting next to real customer language that never mentions it. Every future teardown recurs through this exact frame, which is what a probe subject needs — not the most interesting scene, the one this brand actually ships most weeks.

Do not change it mid-sweep. Flynn's rule: everything learned is a *difference*, and a difference requires a fixed baseline.
