3. **His $500 pilot audit is UNDERPRICED on three separate comp sets** — AI-visibility audits ($500–$2K typical, $5K deep), supplement compliance copy review *alone* ($350–$550/label; $1,500+/SKU with marketing copy), and KeyGrow's $1,800 supplement-AEO benchmark [OBSERVED]. $500 is defensible **only** as a conditioned founder-pilot (limited slots, testimonial + case-study rights required), not as the standing price.
4. **The retainer bands hold.** Boutique GEO retainers $3–6K/mo; LinkedIn ghostwriting medians $2–5K/mo (premium $5–10K+); his stacked service (ghostwriting + claim-safety + GEO assets) justifies the TOP of the ghostwriting band at minimum. The canonical's $1.5K pilot floor reads cheap for the credential — raise the floor.
5. **Budget exists.** DTC brands $5–50M spend 15–25% of revenue on marketing [LIKELY — 2026 benchmark sweeps]. A $5M brand ≈ $60–100K/mo total marketing; a $2.5–6K/mo authority/AI-visibility line is small enough to approve on one screenshot, big enough to matter to Farrice.
6. **Compliance attracts when framed as marketing-enablement, repels as legal review.** A real paid market exists for claims review (receipts above) — but it's bought as *"copy that sells without getting fined,"* not as audit/legal. The canonical's instinct (compliance baked UNDER the authority headline, never the front door) is confirmed. Draw the line explicitly: *editorial claim-risk read, not legal advice.*
7. **"AI Creative Strategist" (unified's lane) is not ownable** — it's the flooded register the ICP dossier's wince-list warns about. **"The credentialed body-expert who makes health brands the source AI names — claim-safe" is ownable** (category-of-one test: subculture [DTC supplement/wellness operators] × credential identity [18-yr NASM practitioner] × moment [the 2026 AI-search shift] — no second occupant found in two sweeps).

## 3. DEMAND SIDE — who's paying and what opens the wallet

- **Budget source:** the SEO/content line (already exists in every $1M+ brand) plus founder-discretionary at founder-led brands. This means Farrice **sells into an existing category** ("content/SEO retainer — AI-native") rather than evangelizing a new line item ("GEO") — exactly the Lakajev category-belief rule and exactly how the canonical leads (named/cited/carried first, GEO second).
- **Trigger ranking (fastest wallet-opener first):**
  1. **Visibility shock** — founder/CMO types the category question into ChatGPT and isn't named (or a smaller competitor is). Instant, personal, screenshot-able. The free 7-check test manufactures this trigger on demand.
  2. **Traffic decoupling** — rankings flat, clicks down (AIO zero-click 54%→72% [VERIFIED — SSRN RCT via dossier]). Opens the SEO-line conversation.

- **WTP shape:** cold buyers in this niche say yes first to a **low-friction paid diagnostic** (or a free teardown that earns the paid diagnostic). Straight retainer pitches from unknowns don't convert; audits→implementation is the documented motion. Confirms the ladder.
