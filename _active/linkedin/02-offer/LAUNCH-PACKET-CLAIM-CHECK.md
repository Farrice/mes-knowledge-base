## 3. LAUNCH SEQUENCE (90 minutes, in this order — the order IS the trust design)

1. **Profile live first** (~30 min): headline Option 1 + About + Featured cards per `featured-section-and-profile.md`. The artifact must never route a curious stranger to an empty profile. **No dead cards:** until the teardown and pinned-CTA posts exist, cards [2]/[3] point at their Google-Doc versions; [4] = the 7-check Doc (the artifact's no-login sibling — never remove it).
2. **Two posts up before the tool** (~20 min): ship post 1 from the starter batch now; queue post 2 for tomorrow per the interleave. A living feed answers Question #1 silently.
3. **Build + QA + publish the artifact** (~25 min): kit Steps 1–2 — the 3 test claims (expect LANDMINE / CAUTION / CLEAR), the **incognito logged-out test** (no-login fallback must render), the pre-publish checklist (real profile URL + real 7-check Doc URL replace both placeholders), phone check, Publish, copy the public link.
4. **Swap Featured card [1]** to the artifact (card copy in kit Step 3); the 7-check Doc moves to card [4] as the no-login path.
5. **Ship the launch post** (kit Step 4) + first comment with the link and: *"If your verdict says LANDMINE, DM me 'AUDIT' — the full version grades your top 8–12 and shows where AI names a competitor instead of you."*
6. **Open the DM layer** (§4 below is your response bench — read it once so nothing is improvised).

## 4. THE DM LAYER (rehearsed responses to the 3 inbound types)

- **A verdict screenshot arrives** (the flywheel's ignition — see §5): *"That [LANDMINE] is doing you a favor — [the risky word] is the exact kind of wording that costs $53,088 a violation and never makes it into an AI answer. Want me to run your other seven claims the same way? That's the audit — $500 founder slot if you want one of the three."* (Adapt the bracketed specifics to their screenshot. Never paste blind.)
- **"How does this work / who are you?"**: the one-liner, verbatim — *"AI is answering your buyers' questions with someone else's name. I fix that, with claims that can't blow up."* + one line of credential + the artifact or 7-check link, matched to whether they have a Claude login.
- **"What would it take to fix?"**: the audit close, verbatim from Creative Book §7-D. Then stop talking.

## 5. THE VERDICT FLYWHEEL (prep layer — rehearsed now, expanded only after real verdicts exist)

The loop each screenshot triggers: **verdict → rehearsed DM (§4-A) → free 3-observation read on THEIR brand → they ask → audit close → log.**

- **Log every verdict-sourced conversation** in `../05-lead-gen/pipeline.md` with stage + source=`claim-check`. This tag is how week-2 decisions get made from data instead of vibes.
- **Repurposing rule:** every interesting verdict (anonymized — category + verdict + the risky word, never the brand) is a post. One per week max, per the interleave. The tool manufactures its own content.
- **The teardown pairing:** the weekly named-brand teardown now ends with *"want your verdict? The Claim Check is in my Featured."* Teardown feeds tool; tool feeds DMs; DMs feed audits.
- **Expansion gate (deliberate):** no dashboards, no automation, no extra rungs until **10 real verdicts or 3 verdict-sourced DM conversations** exist. Then one review: what do people paste, where do they stall, which verdicts convert. The system grows from that data or not at all. (Stop-loss stands: the offer itself doesn't change inside the window.)

## 6. WEEK-1 SCOREBOARD (what "working" means before any conversion data)

- **Log daily** (`performance-log.md`): decision-maker profile views · DMs started · artifact-sourced DMs (`claim-check` tag) · audit conversations. Likes are noise.
- **Healthy week-1 looks like:** profile views from target-brand people trending up, 2–5 real DM conversations, 0–1 audit conversations. **First 1–2 paid audits land weeks 3–6** (the ratified honest ramp) — a quiet week 1 is on-plan, not a verdict on the offer.
- **The only allowed reaction to a slow week:** more §4-daily-loop reps and warm-list touches. Never a new build, never an offer change (60-day stop-loss, `../01-research/PATH-DECISION-BRIEF-2026-07-01.md`).
