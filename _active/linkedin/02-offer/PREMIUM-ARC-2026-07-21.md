## The executor screen v2 (the structural Cooz/Ken fix — 7 conditions, DM-applyable)

1. Sells a $2K+ offer already — "What's your core offer and price?"
2. Has customer proof — "Who's a recent client and what result did they get?"
3. 60-day commercial trigger — "What's happening in the next 8 weeks this has to be ready for?" (the Andrea gate)
4. Already pays for marketing/AI — "What are you currently spending on to grow?"
5. Expertise trapped in calls/voice notes — "Where does your best thinking live right now?"
6. ⭐ Execution capacity — "When we hand something over, who ships it — you, a teammate, or nobody yet?" → "nobody yet" = DFY-only rungs, never strategy-only.
7. ⭐ Past-shelf test — "Last time you paid for strategy, what got implemented — and what's the honest reason the rest didn't?" → all blame / "we got busy" with no system = non-executor flag; deposit + short application before kickoff.

Rules: conditions 6–7 override budget. Strategy-only scope is never sold to a buyer who fails 6. The offer internalizes execution (DFY) + anchors to a dated trigger + gates by application — the three fixes that compound; screening alone does not.
