### The 9-Point Scorecard

| # | Question | Yes/No |
|---|----------|--------|
| **1** | **LinkedIn Profile**: Does your headline describe the transformation you create — not your certifications? | ☐ |
| **2** | **Content Frequency**: Have you posted on LinkedIn at least 3x/week for the past month? | ☐ |
| **3** | **Methodology Deployment**: Do your posts teach YOUR frameworks — not generic S&C advice anyone could give? | ☐ |
| **4** | **Voice Authenticity**: Would a colleague recognize your posts as YOURS without seeing your name? | ☐ |
| **5** | **Story Library**: Have you published at least 3 client transformation stories (with permission) on LinkedIn in the last 90 days? | ☐ |
| **6** | **Content Repurposing**: Do you systematically convert podcast episodes, talks, or coaching sessions into LinkedIn content? | ☐ |
| **7** | **Lead Capture**: Do you have a mechanism (newsletter, email list, lead magnet) that captures attention from speaking gigs, conferences, and social content? | ☐ |
| **8** | **Digital Hub**: Does your website work, load quickly, and clearly communicate your offer with a call to action? | ☐ |
| **9** | **Inbound Pipeline**: Have you received at least 2 inbound DMs or inquiries from LinkedIn content in the last 30 days? | ☐ |

### How to Read Your Score

| Score | What It Means |

| **7–9** | **Visible Expert.** You've cracked the translation. Your digital presence matches your in-room reputation. Keep compounding. |
