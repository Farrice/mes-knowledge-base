# SECTION 2 — THE WEEKLY NAMED-TEARDOWN CADENCE (the keystone act)

The teardown is the engine of the whole motion. It is **content, portfolio, and lead-gen in one pass** — and it's the only act that proves the moat instead of claiming it. One named teardown a week = roughly **4–5 warm decision-maker leads/month** into the funnel. This is the keystone. If you do nothing else in a week, do this.

> **Two flavors, one cadence:**
> - **The DIY lead magnet** — *"The AI-Search Visibility Test"* (the free, un-named, evergreen 7-self-check asset, in the Featured section). Anyone can run it on themselves. It *creates* teardown demand ("I ran it, we're in citation 4, help").
> - **The named-brand teardown** — the 1/week public proof act below: you run the test on a *real named brand* and publish the principle version + DM the founder/lead the full custom version. **This is the keystone.**

## Step 1 — Pick the brand (15 min, every Monday)
- Pull from the Section 1 shortlist (Tier 1 first). Prioritize: (a) an engaged community, (b) a visible claim or sameness pattern in the last 5 posts, (c) a reachable Dana or Marcus.
- Run `python3 execution/brand_radar.py` if your radar is >1 week old (`daily/brand-radar-YYYY-WW.md`) for live recent posts.

## Step 2 — Run the actual test (45 min — this is the proof, don't skip)
Open ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI mode. Ask each the question their buyer asks:
- *"What's the best [their category] brand / supplement for [their use case]?"*
- *"Is [brand] worth it? What does the science say about [their key claim]?"*

Record, literally: **Are they named? In which citation slot? Who got named above them and why? What's the one claim of theirs the machine could repeat without hedging — and the one it can't?** This screenshot *is* the teardown's spine and your proof token. (This is also the Pennock play in reverse — and the same move you run on *yourself* to get Farrice cited; log every Farrice-cited screenshot to `proof-tracker.md`.)

## Step 3 — Build the teardown (90 min, the 6-part structure)
Open with **their words** (a wince line, verbatim), then pivot to the lived-expertise moat:
1. **Credit first.** What they're doing right. Remove the identity threat — Dana already feels behind, Marcus already feels exposed. *"Your proof is good"* is literally true here; say it.
2. **The gap, named better than they can.** Not "you rank low" — *"your proof isn't getting carried."* Show the citation slot, show who got named above them and the *structural* reason (legible dosing/mechanism/study, not bigger budget).
3. **The body-level truth.** The 18-year edge, deployed as evidence not résumé: *why* the claim is true at the physiology level, and where AI gets the physiology *wrong* (ChatGPT is ~57% accurate on supplement efficacy — a credentialed correction is genuinely scarce).
4. **The two-gate read.** For each top claim: is it FTC-substantiable, and is it AI-citation-worthy? Name one claim that's a warning-letter risk and one that's a citation goldmine they're sitting on. **This is the Claim-Safe Citation Audit, demonstrated for free.**
5. **3 angles I'd ship.** Concrete, true-before-scaled, claim-aware. Each tuned to a real consumer emotion + structured to be quotable by a machine.
6. **Close.** Public post = recognition line (no pitch). DM = *"Want this on your top 5 claims, scored for both gates? That's the Claim-Safe Citation Audit."*

## Step 4 — Triple-route (60 min)
1. **Public proof post** → LinkedIn. Generalize the principle; don't name-and-shame the brand in the opener. This is your portfolio. (Optionally the "free named-brand teardown" can name a brand *favorably* as a top-of-funnel proof piece, 1/week — that's the warm-DM opener.)
2. **Custom teardown DM** → the brand's Dana or Marcus. Use the Section 3 opener. Add the brand + stage to `pipeline.md` ("dm'd").
3. **Proof-tracker entry** → `proof-tracker.md` (date, brand, link, citation finding, any reply). This compounds.

## Step 5 — Wait & warm (ongoing)
- Reply → tag "replied," move to discovery-call prep.
- No reply after 5 days → comment substantively on their next brand post (warm again) before any follow-up DM. Never double-DM cold.

**Cadence target:** 1 named teardown/week → 4–5 warm decision-maker leads/month. Non-negotiable. This is the act everything else amplifies.

# SECTION 6 — THE PIPELINE TALLY (KPI order: signal over noise)

> **The order is the whole point.** You measure decision-maker *signal*, not vanity reach. A post with 200 likes and zero decision-maker profile views is a worse day than a post with 30 likes and two Danas who viewed your profile. **Reach and likes = noise. Track them last, or not at all.**

## The KPI priority ladder (read top-down — top = what matters)
```
1. SIGNED            ← the only number that pays rent
2. CALLS BOOKED      ← discovery calls with a qualified Dana/Marcus
3. DMs (qualified)   ← decision-maker replies to a teardown DM / "AUDIT" inbounds
4. DECISION-MAKER    ← Dana/Marcus profile views + saves + substantive comments
   PROFILE VIEWS        (recognition: they read you and came to look)
———————————————————————— everything below the line is NOISE ————————————————————————
5. reach / impressions
6. likes / followers
```
**Rule:** if you're tempted to celebrate a number, check which rung it's on. Anything below the line doesn't move the business — it's a leading indicator at best and a vanity trap at worst.

# SECTION 7 — THE FORK: DISCOVERY-CALL QUALIFICATION (ROLE vs CLIENT)

When a teardown DM or an "AUDIT" inbound turns into a call, the tracks diverge here. Same person, two ways to buy.

**Opening frame (both):** *"Thanks for looking at the teardown. Before I pitch anything, I want to make sure I'm pointing at the right problem — let me ask a couple things."*

**ROLE track (they're hiring / building a team):** ask whether the seat is embedded (day-to-day) or fractional (strategy + monthly direction); current content stack + who owns strategy vs. execution; what 90-day success looks like. *Your angle:* you don't need onboarding — you've already read their brand via the teardown, and you bring the one capability they'd otherwise split across three hires (body credential + AI-search + claim-safety). Routes to **Creative Strategy Intensive → embed ($4–9K/mo)**.

**CLIENT track (they want a pilot first):** ask where their content/proof breaks; what they've tried; who owns content decisions (buyer vs. influencer); whether they'd act on 8–12 claim-safe posts + AI-search assets next month. Routes to **Claim-Safe Citation Audit ($500 pilot) → GEO Authority Ghostwriting retainer ($1.5–3K/mo pilot)**.

**The bridge (both):** *"Cleanest next step either way is the Claim-Safe Citation Audit — I grade your top claims for both gates (will it survive an FTC read, will an AI engine carry it) so whether you hire or we run a retainer, we're aligned on exactly what's leaking."* The audit is the smallest sellable yes and proves the moat in one page. **Setup is always bundled into the retainer/sprint — never sold alone.**
