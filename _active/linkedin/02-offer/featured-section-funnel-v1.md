```

I've watched what moves people in a real room for 18 years.

A protein buyer doesn't switch for "flavor variety."

- "Our content gets engagement but no actual leads"
- "We don't know what angles to even try"
- "Our AI-generated content is tanking on the platforms"
```

**Q3: The AI Complaint**
```
Question type: Multiple choice (pick 1)

"When you ask ChatGPT or Claude to write about your brand, what happens?"

Options:
- A) It sounds generic — could be any brand in our category
- B) It has the facts right but sounds corporate / robotic
- C) It's actually pretty good but doesn't sound like me/our founder
- D) It gets facts wrong or missing and I end up rewriting everything
- E) I haven't tried it; I don't trust it
```

**Q4: Current Content System**
```
Question type: Multiple choice (pick 1)

"How are you currently creating content?"

Options:
- A) I'm posting sporadically (0-2x per week)
- B) We have an in-house person but they're overwhelmed
- C) We hired a ghostwriter or agency but the output isn't working
- D) We're using a template-based service / batch content tool
- E) We're using AI + light editing
- F) We're not posting; we're focusing on [other channel]
```

**Q5: Decision Timeline**
```
Question type: Multiple choice (pick 1)

"What would success look like for you in the next 90 days?"

Options:
- A) Posting consistently 3-4x per week with clear angles
- B) Generating 2-5 qualified leads per week from organic content
- C) Having a library of posts I can use and adapt
- D) Understanding what angles actually work for my brand
- E) Looking good on LinkedIn / building founder presence
- F) All of the above
```

**Q6: Budget & Constraints (Optional but Recommended)**
```
Question type: Short answer

"Any content format or topic we should avoid posting about?"

(This is compliance gatekeeping — helps you know if there's a regulatory angle 
or internal brand guideline you need to work around. Especially critical for 
supplement/wellness brands.)
```

**Q7: The Discovery Call (Optional)**
```
Question type: Short answer

"What's one question you want answered during our call?"

(Makes the call more targeted. Also surfaces the real objection/barrier.)
```

## VOICE NOTES (Why This Funnel Works)

1. **No pitch** — Every asset is proof by showing the work. Featured section doesn't say "hire me." It says "here's what I see."

2. **Reader as protagonist** — All copy assumes the viewer is a brand trying to solve a real problem (invisible to models, no resonance). Farrice doesn't center himself; the brand's problem centers everything.

3. **Statement hooks** — Opens are declarative ("Brands that win..." "The 10 brands...") not question-based. 52% higher engagement per Notes research.

4. **Specificity signals expertise** — Named brands, named decision-makers, named framework. Generic positioning gets scrolled past; specific positioning gets clicked.

5. **Price + scope visible upfront** — $500 audit, 60 minutes, 7-day delivery. Removes the "what does this actually cost?" friction that kills high-intent visitors.

6. **Body-level truth** — All the angles reference the nervous-system reason people act, not the feature/benefit. This is Farrice's unfair advantage (18 years in coaching psychology). It's the thing that makes a brand quotable.
