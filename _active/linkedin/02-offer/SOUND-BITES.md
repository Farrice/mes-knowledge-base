# The Sound Bites — say these words, in this order, everywhere, for years

> **Built 2026-07-01 (Miller: PEACE framework, zero cognitive load).** These five lines are the message. Every asset — profile, one-pager, posts, DMs, audit walkthroughs — repeats them **verbatim**. When you're sick of saying them, keep saying them. The goal isn't to inform; it's for a stranger to memorize your pitch and repeat it to a friend.
> **The rule they obey:** one problem, owned. The problem and the ending bookend each other. You are the guide outside the hole, not the hero.

> **"When your buyers ask AI about your category, somebody else's name comes back."**

> **"You didn't do anything wrong. The rules changed under you, and nobody sent the memo."**

**A — the answer (the rope, both halves of the moat in one breath):**
> **"I get your name in the answer, with claims that can't blow up."**

**C — the change (who they become — an image, not an argument):**
> **"The screenshot in the next board deck is yours: your name, in the answer."**

**ER — the end result (the bookend; physical; this is the whole sale):**
> **"Type the question in front of your founder. Watch your name come back."**

## The deployment rule

- Curiosity assets (headlines, hooks, cards, DM openers): sound bites ONLY. No mechanism, no studies.
- Enlightenment assets (About, one-pager, posts, walkthroughs): open on a sound bite, close on the End Result. At most ONE stat per asset, woven, not stacked.
- Commitment moments (audit close, install attach): the Answer line + the plan + one condition. Stop talking.
- The banned register stays banned (wince-list, AI-slop bank). These lines pass both.
