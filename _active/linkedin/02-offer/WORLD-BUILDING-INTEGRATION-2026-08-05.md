# World-Building Integration: Proof-to-Market Offer

## What just happened

The `/oren-norton` skill (Scott Norton × Oren John world-building) was deployed to the Proof-to-Market Sprint offer via three structural moves:

1. **Prop:** The deliverable becomes a named artifact (The Claim Ledger)
2. **Initiate:** The pilot conditions become membership roles (Founding Source)
3. **Institution:** The proof infrastructure gets a future name (Source Registry, parked)

None of these touch the pricing, scope, or claims. They are **pure world-building** — reframing the same offer through the fantasy of membership, not discounting.

---

## The three moves (implemented)

### Move 1: The Claim Ledger (Prop)

**What changed:**
- Deliverable name: "The Claim Ledger of [Brand]" (was: "The Proof-to-Market Blueprint")
- Visual register: Dossier-grade (was: Generic strategy deck)
- Evidence stamps: VERIFIED / LIKELY / UNCONFIRMED visible throughout (was: Buried in methodology)
- Forwarding behavior: Gets sent to cofounder/board/compliance (was: Gets filed)

**Files:**
- `PROOF-TO-MARKET-BLUEPRINT-TEMPLATE.md` — the deliverable template, frontmatter to appendix
- `CLAIM-LEDGER-ARTIFACT-DESIGN-BRIEF.md` — craft-map routing (never freehand)

**Status:** Ready to use. Before each client delivery, route the visual design through `/generate` with craft-map masters loaded.

**Anti-cringe calibration:** Intensity 6/10 for regulated-category buyers (Dana/Marcus type). Gravitas, not whimsy. Dossier register, no lore characters.

---

### Move 2: Founding Source (Initiation)

**What changed:**
- Pilot framing: "You are Founding Source № X of 3" (was: "You get a discounted pilot rate")
- Conditions: Reframed as membership requirements (was: Limitations)
- Testimonial ask: "As Founding Source, would you share your result?" (was: "Can we use this as a testimonial?")
- Access: Method updates, case-study protocol before public release (was: Nothing beyond delivery)

**Files:**
- `FOUNDING-SOURCE-ROLE-CONFERRAL.md` — full mechanic, contract language, sales frames

**Status:** Ready. Integrate into next Sprint onboarding:
- Add role-conferral language to all agreements
- Use "Founding Source № X" language in frontmatter of each Blueprint
- Include role definition in contracts and delivery emails
- Track all three slots in the client database

**What this does:** Reframes the discount (conditions) as membership (access), making the founder feel invited into the practice, not used as a beta tester. Directly implements Norton's "growth by initiation" principle.

---

### Move 3: Source Registry (Institution)

**What changed:**
- Future infrastructure: The registry will hold documented case stories and method versions (doesn't exist yet)
- Proof evolution: The Founding Sources' feedback gets built into the methodology and documented
- Archive: Public-facing (permission-based) record of how founders solved messaging problems

**Files:**
- `SOURCE-REGISTRY-INSTITUTION-PARKED.md` — design + trigger conditions

**Status:** Parked until 2+ case stories + approvals. Trigger expected September 2026.

**What this does:** Implements Norton's "institutions imply a past and a future" — the registry signals that this methodology has staying power, that it evolves, and that the Founding Sources' participation matters beyond one Sprint. Builds implied history.

## How they fit together

### At offer stage
**Prospect gets this frame:** "The Proof-to-Market Sprint is $2,500 for 10 days. I'm taking three founding pilots — each becomes a Founding Source with access to method updates and future case studies. Your Blueprint arrives as a dossier-grade Claim Ledger that you can forward to your board or compliance team."

**What they hear:** This isn't a discount. It's membership in a practice that's building something.

### At delivery stage
**The Blueprint frontmatter says:** "Your role in this proof: You are Founding Source № [X of 3] in the Proof-to-Market practice. As a named source, you have access to strategy updates, method refinements, and case-study protocol before public release."

**What they experience:** Membership language in a dossier-grade document that looks like it matters.

### At case-story stage (later)
**When we document results:** "The Source Registry now includes your case story (permission-based). Your feedback shaped version 1.1 of the method."

**What they experience:** They didn't just buy a Sprint. They helped build the practice.

## Why this matters for the campaign

**The Proof-to-Market offer was VIVID at the positioning level (positioning core rare) but NORMAL-WORLD in expression (consultant register, generic objects).**

These three moves live the positioning. They don't change the message — "AI search is finally built to reward the real ones" stays the same — but they build the world around it:

- **Prop:** A named, forwarded-able object (The Claim Ledger) that feels like proof
- **Initiate:** Role-conferral that makes conditions feel like membership, not discounting
- **Institution:** A future proof infrastructure (Source Registry) that implies staying power

Together, they answer the founder's unconscious question: "Is this person building a real practice, or just selling me a service?"

The answer is now in the objects, the membership tier, and the named institution. Not in words, but in structure.
