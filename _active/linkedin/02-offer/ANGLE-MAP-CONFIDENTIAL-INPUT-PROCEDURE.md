# The Angle Map: Confidential Input Procedure

## Purpose

This internal procedure governs unreleased launch material, substantiation, approved claim language, and other client-confidential inputs used in The Angle Map.

It does not authorize file collection, AI-assisted handling, proof reuse, payment creation, or external activation. The procedure becomes operational only when every owner-truth field below contains a tested value that Farrice can honor.

## Non-negotiable defaults

1. **Private by default.** Client material and buyer feedback are not demonstration, portfolio, testimonial, case-story, training, or public content.
2. **AI permission must be explicit.** The handling path is human-only unless the client gives separate written permission after seeing the named tools and information types.
3. **Access must be named.** Every person or service that may access the files must appear in the Ready-to-Start Receipt.
4. **Payment waits for readiness.** If confidential handling cannot be performed safely inside the 48-hour promise, decline or re-scope before payment.
5. **Proof states remain separate.** Usage, observed outcome, voluntary sharing, written permission, and external reuse are distinct events.
6. **New sources need a new decision.** A new file, evidence set, product, buyer, market, or occasion after T0 requires an explicit scope decision before use.

## AI permission language

Use only after the owner-truth gate is operational:

> Your materials are handled through the path listed in the Ready-to-Start Receipt. Human-only review is the default. If AI-assisted tools would be useful, I will name the tools and the information they would receive before use. Nothing enters those tools without your written permission. You may decline that permission. If the human-only path cannot protect the agreed scope and 48-hour delivery, I will decline or re-scope before payment.

Permission applies only to the named project, tools, files, and purpose. A new tool, information type, or use requires new written permission.

## Intake and handling sequence

1. Confirm that every owner-truth field has a tested value.
2. Send the applicable handling terms before requesting unreleased or claim-sensitive files.
3. Record the client's written handling confirmation, selected path, and any NDA state before file transfer.
4. Receive files only through the approved channel.
5. Record each file's name, version, approval status, permitted use, and claim boundary in the Ready-to-Start Receipt.
6. Keep access within the named list and handling within the approved path.
7. If a new source or permission request appears after T0, pause its use and decide whether it is a correction, a scope change, or out of scope.
8. At delivery, apply the named retention or deletion rule and record completion when required.
9. Keep all material private unless a separate permission chain reaches external reuse.

## Proof and permission chain

| Event | Required evidence | Reuse state |
|---|---|---|
| Usage | Session or delivery record | Private |
| Observed outcome | Buyer-authored report of a decision or meaningful result | Private |
| Voluntary sharing | Buyer chooses to provide a comment, decision change, or reported action | Still private |
| Written permission | Named use, audience, duration, identification, redaction, and revocation path | Limited to the grant |
| External reuse | Final artifact matches the written grant | Allowed only inside the grant |

No permission means no external reuse. A usable Angle Map, a buyer decision, or a positive comment does not prove campaign performance, revenue, conversion, retailer acceptance, rankings, citations, or exact-offer demand.

## Stop conditions

Keep the project and payment at `HOLD` when:

- a transfer, access, storage, NDA, retention, deletion, or handling field is unresolved;
- the client declines AI permission and the human-only path cannot meet the fixed scope and clock;
- the source pack includes material Farrice is not authorized or equipped to handle;
- an unlisted person or service needs access;
- the client requests reuse before a real event and specific permission exist; or
- deletion cannot be completed or confirmed under the stated procedure.

Resolve the exact field or decline/re-scope. Do not soften the issue with copy.
