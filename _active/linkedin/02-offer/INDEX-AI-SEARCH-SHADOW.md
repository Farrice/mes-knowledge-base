# AI-Search Shadow Module — Complete Index

## What This Is

A complete **proof-to-market cold-outreach system** using Ahrefs Brand Radar data to show supplement/performance brands exactly how ChatGPT and Perplexity describe (or fail to describe) their positioning.

**The thesis:** Before a human ever searches for your brand, an AI engine has already described it to them. If that description doesn't match your positioning, you're losing the sale in the machine layer.

**Your play:** Screenshot what the AI actually says, send it to the prospect's Head of Brand with a one-liner, book the discovery call, close the $2,500 Proof-to-Market Sprint.

### 4. **AI-SEARCH-SHADOW-SCREENSHOT-ANNOTATION-GUIDE.md** (The Technique)
**Purpose:** 6 ways to mark up Ahrefs screenshots so they land harder.

**Contains:**
1. **Red Arrow** (the absence)
   - Prospect brand missing from top-3 AI recommendations
   - Discomfort: Medium-High

2. **Yellow Box** (the wrong language)
   - Brand mentioned but described using competitor vocabulary
   - Discomfort: High

3. **Side-by-Side Comparison** (the competitive gap)
   - Two brands answering same query; AI describes one as innovator, other as follower
   - Discomfort: Very High

4. **Timeline Annotation** (the recency problem)
   - Brand launched something new; AI training hasn't caught up
   - Discomfort: Medium

5. **Feature Comparison** (the missing proof)
   - AI knows competitor's specific claims; describes prospect generically
   - Discomfort: Very High

6. **Null Annotation** (powerful understatement)
   - Brand is simply absent. One label. Let silence speak.
   - Discomfort: Extreme

**For each technique:**
- What it shows (the story)
- Step-by-step how to annotate
- When to use it
- Prospect discomfort level
- Color-coding standard (red, yellow, green, blue)
- Tool recommendations (Preview, Paint, Figma, Loom)

**Read time:** 12 minutes
**When:** When marking up your Ahrefs screenshots (before putting them in the DM)
**Action:** Pick one technique. Practice on 1–2 screenshots. Use it.
