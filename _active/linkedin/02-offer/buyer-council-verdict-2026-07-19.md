## Dissent Log (preserved, not averaged)
- **S1 vs S3 on the paid cold rung.** S1 (founder, personal check): the $350 friction is *trust*, not money. She'll pay $350 in a heartbeat *if* the free screenshot already proved a real gap; the block is "is this person real." S3 (CFO lens): the $350 is *category*. An un-guaranteed diagnostic from an unknown is a coin-flip expense, and coin-flips get declined regardless of size. **Do not merge:** S1 wants proof-of-competence, S3 wants downside-protection. The make-right clause answers S3; the free screenshot answers S1. Both fixes are needed; they are not the same fix.
- **S1 vs S2 on pace.** S1 books off a single strong screenshot in days. S2 (VP Brand) will not, and the DM set correctly never cold-DMs her. No conflict in the *motion*, but S2's timeline (weeks, committee) means any Monday-DM reply from a VP-scale account is a **mis-target signal**, not a win.

## Self-Selection Finding (NO-FIT seat)
**Mostly holds, minor leak.** "sub-$15M, founder-authorized, no committee" + dated-occasion framing correctly repels the $20M+ in-house-team founder at the *sprint* tier. **But** the $350 walkthrough is cheap enough to not trigger her budget-defense reflex, so a curious larger-brand founder could book it as a low-stakes lark, wasting a slot. Objection (verbatim roster): *"We already have three people whose job this is — what would you even be doing that they aren't?"* Low-stakes leak; watch, don't re-architect.

## Economic Gatekeeper Finding
**Cold tier: spend-defense holds** (walkthrough sits below the CFO approval threshold, deliberate and smart). **Warm tier: unresolved.** The transition-to-sprint script sells build-vs-diagnose but never pre-empts the part-time-hire math. The $2,500 ask meets the CFO with no bounded-vs-retainer contrast and no make-right. This is where the *warm* conversion leaks.

## Highest-Impact Revisions (ranked by predicted-outcome protected)
1. **Add a make-right clause to the $350 walkthrough. THE fix.** Answers the open haynes-geoff PoC flag (v2.1 answers it nowhere) and dissolves the single biggest cold-conversion block (S3's coin-flip, S1's trust). Draft: *"If you don't leave the hour with at least one gap worth more than $350 to fix before you spend, you don't pay."* Cheap to honor on a 60-min read; converts the paid-cold-stranger friction into asymmetric downside for the buyer.
2. **Name the walkthrough's no-pitch boundary in the DM copy**, not just the doc. One line: *"It's a diagnostic, not a sales call. The sprint only comes up if you ask."* Kills the "paid pitch in disguise" read (S1).
3. **Pre-empt the part-time-hire math in the transition-to-sprint script.** Add the bounded-fix-vs-a-month-of-salary contrast so the CFO objection dissolves inside the offer, not left for the close (jh-objection-mine: majority slice becomes core articulation, not rebuttal).
