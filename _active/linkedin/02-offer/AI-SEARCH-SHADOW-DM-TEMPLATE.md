# AI-Search Shadow — Cold DM Template

## Template 1: Repositioning Brand (Create Wellness, GNC style)

**Situation:** Brand is mid-repositioning or category-expanding. AI-search lags behind new positioning.

**DM Text:**

---

Hey [First Name],

I ran [Brand Name]'s positioning through what ChatGPT actually recommends to buyers in your space.

The gap is bigger than you think.

[SCREENSHOT: ChatGPT response to "best creatine for [buyer intent]" — your brand missing or described generically]

When someone asks the AI "what's a good creatine for [women/casual fitness/energy]," it names [Competitor 1], [Competitor 2], and [Competitor 3].

Not you.

I'm assuming you're trying to own that buyer. The machine disagrees.

Before your [next launch/campaign/retail push], we can make sure the AI reads your story the way you're writing it.

Proof is a 10-day sprint. Three things locked: what to say, what to prove, why they care.

## How to Pull the Screenshots

### Step 1: Go to Ahrefs Brand Radar
- https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar (login required; free tier may have limited Brand Radar access)
- Or use their API if you have a paid account

### Step 2: Run the Query
**Choose the query based on your ICP:**
- General: "best [category] supplement"
- Casual/women: "[category] for women"
- Outcome: "[category] for [health outcome]"
- New category: "what do I take for [specific problem]"

### Step 3: Select AI Platform
- **ChatGPT** (primary; 92% of AI-search traffic)
- **Perplexity** (growing; more transparent citation)
- **Claude** (optional; emerging)

### Step 4: Capture the Response
**Screenshot the:**
1. Direct AI text (the full response)
2. How the brands rank (top-3? buried? absent?)
3. The *language* used (generic? specific? positioning-aligned?)

### Step 5: Add Your Label
At the bottom of screenshot, add:
```
Query: "[exact buyer question]"
Platform: ChatGPT / Perplexity
Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Insight: [your one-liner on the gap]
```

## Response Rate Notes

**What to expect:**
- Cold DM open rate: ~15–25%
- Click-through on "want to see the screenshot": ~40–60% of openers
- "Book a call" conversion: ~30–50% of engaged readers

**Why it works:**
- Screenshot is *specific* (their brand named, their AI search dated)
- Slightly uncomfortable (gap they didn't know about)
- Positioned as diagnostic, not a sales pitch
- Proof object is proof (not a pitch deck or methodology)

**If no response:** Follow-up in 7 days with a slight pivot:
> "You didn't bite on the AI gap. Maybe [alternative angle: claim-safety? category-positioning? retail-readiness?]. Worth a quick call?"
