## Did the make-rights flip the two social-grounded seats?

**S1 Personal-Yes Founder: YES, flipped to SHIP (walkthrough rung).**
v1's flip condition was explicit: *"the free screenshot lands a gap she hadn't seen AND a one-line no-pitch/make-right removes the coin-flip."* Both are now in the artifact text: the make-right (DM V1, verbatim above) and the no-pitch boundary (*"no pitch riding on it"*). Corpus-grounded corroboration: her scar tissue is the agency trap, *"I wasn't paying for elite marketing performance. I was just funding their sales machine"* (raw-quotes #6) and *"any founder currently staring at a $5k to $10k proposal"* (#7). A $350 bounded diagnostic with don't-pay-unless downside is the structural anti-agency-trap; it reads as insider, not extraction. Her frame is invisibility, not muddle (zeitgeist-digest #12: *"INSANELY crowded," "get seen through the noise"*), and DM V1's *"named three competitors, not you"* meets that frame exactly. **Flip is real.**
Residual (unsanded): FU1 hands her the free self-serve diagnostic (*"ask ChatGPT … three times in a row … That one's free"*), so the $350 must out-value the free version she was just taught to run. Minor self-cannibalization; the make-right absorbs it but doesn't erase it.

**S3 CFO/Gatekeeper: PARTIALLY flipped, REVISE to SHIP-with-watch (sprint rung).**
His verbatim objection, *"Walk me through why this costs more than hiring someone part-time for a month"* (corpus-corroborated by #7's *"hire someone directly, and put the rest into your actual ad spend"*), is now answered *in copy* for the first time (transition script, verbatim above) plus a delivery make-right. On the element-as-written, that is a genuine flip. **But the guarantee is delivery-based, not outcome-based**, and his frame is comparative ROI. He can still say: *"Fine, I'm guaranteed to GET the deck on time. I'm not guaranteed the deck beats a month of a media buyer who actually spends into the market."* A delivery guarantee structurally cannot close an opportunity-cost objection. Cold tier ($350) clears him trivially (below his approval radar; make-right makes it asymmetric). **The coin-flip is improved, not eliminated. It now converts on the walkthrough-as-proof-event, not on the guarantee.**

## The binding constraint remaining

**No proof-of-work / first case study exists.** The make-right clauses are the correct *substitute* for a track record (controllable downside standing in for a receipt) and are the smartest available move pre-case-study. But they buy **conversion-at-ask** (they only fire once a founder is already in the conversation reading the offer), **not reply-rate or first-touch trust**, which is what a real receipt would move. Everything the panel still flags converges on this one lack: S2 is blocked on it, S5 wants it, S3's opportunity-cost residual only closes with ROI proof, and S1's leap from $350 walkthrough to $2,500 sprint still rides on "trust me" until one delivered walkthrough produces a receipt. **Ship one walkthrough, bank one receipt, and the remaining REVISE-watches dissolve.**
