# Demo Proof Strategy — $500 in 7 Days
## The Authority Flywheel Proof-of-Concept Sprint

> **Context**: Financial urgency — $500 minimum in 7 days. The fastest path
> is demoing the flywheel on real coaches' public content, proving the value,
> then converting to a paid Authority Sprint.

---

## The Play

**Do free work that IS the marketing.** Every demo you create serves 4 purposes:
1. **Outreach material** — "I took the liberty of running your content through my system"
2. **LinkedIn post** — before/after becomes content that attracts other coaches
3. **Featured section proof** — Loom video or article pinned to profile
4. **Portfolio piece** — builds the creative strategy portfolio for future higher-level work

## Demo Execution (Per Coach)

### Step 1: Select Target (15 min)

**Criteria:**
- S&C coach, sports scientist, or performance consultant
- Posts at least 1-2x/month (shows intent, not mastery)
- Has visible methodology (programming philosophy, signature approach)
- Content quality: ideas are A+, execution is C+ to B
- NOT already working with a content person
- Ideally someone in your warm network or 2nd-degree connection

**Priority targets (warm network first):**
1. Anyone you've already identified in the golf/S&C space
2. Coaches from the 8 you audited who scored lowest
3. NSCA conference speakers with weak LinkedIn presence
4. Podcast guests who are great on audio but don't repurpose

### Step 2: Capture Their "Voice Memo" (30 min)

Since you can't get them on a call yet, simulate it:
- Collect their last 5-10 LinkedIn posts
- Find 1-2 podcast appearances (if they exist)
- Read their About section, website bio, any published content
- Extract: their core thesis, recurring themes, signature phrases, methodology language
- Build an abbreviated Voice Profile (30-45 min)

### Step 3: Run the Flywheel Demo (1-2 hours)

Use `/authority-flywheel demo [topic]` — but frame it as THEIR content:

1. **Pick their strongest but worst-executed post** (great idea, weak delivery)
2. **Run the flywheel:**
   - Extract the core thesis from their post (as if it were a voice memo)
   - Enrich with parallel research (trending context, contrarian angles)
   - Generate 3 content angles with mini-briefs
   - Draft 1 newsletter edition (800-1,200 words) in their voice
   - Atomize into 3 LinkedIn posts

3. **Package the demo:**
   - Before: their original post (screenshot or text)
   - After: the newsletter edition + 3 posts
   - Voice analysis: "Here's what I noticed about how you think"
   - The flywheel explanation: "This is what 1 voice memo produces every week"

### Step 4: Record the Loom (15-20 min)

**Script structure:**
```
[0:00-0:30] "Hey [Coach Name], I'm Farrice. I spent 18 years in performance
coaching and now I build content systems for S&C professionals. I took the
liberty of running one of your posts through my Authority Flywheel — here's
what came out."

[0:30-2:00] Walk through the BEFORE — their original post. Be respectful.
"Your ideas are strong. The methodology is clearly deep. But the way it's
packaged doesn't match what you actually know."

[2:00-5:00] Walk through the AFTER — the newsletter edition. "I turned
your core insight into a newsletter article. Here's how it reads..."
Show the enrichment research that was added. "You wouldn't have to find
this — my system does the research automatically."

[5:00-7:00] Show the 3 LinkedIn posts atomized from the newsletter.
"One voice memo from you → this newsletter + these 3 posts. Every week.
You never write a word."

[7:00-8:00] The close: "If this resonates, I'm doing 30-day Authority
Sprints — 12 posts + a newsletter article in your voice. $1,500-$2,500
depending on scope. Happy to jump on a 15-min call to discuss. Either way,
I hope this was useful."
```

**Loom tips:**
- Keep it under 8 minutes
- Screen share the actual content (not slides)
- Webcam ON — they need to see your face
- Be conversational, not salesy
- The demo quality IS the pitch — if the content is good, the sale is made

### Step 5: Send the Outreach (5 min)

**DM (LinkedIn or email):**
```
Hey [Name] — I've been following your work in [their specific area].

I build content systems for S&C coaches and performance professionals.
I took the liberty of running one of your recent posts through my
Authority Flywheel — basically turned it into a newsletter edition
+ 3 LinkedIn posts.

Recorded a quick walkthrough: [Loom link]

No pitch attached. Just wanted to show you what's possible when
someone who speaks your language handles the content side.

If it resonates, happy to chat. If not, genuinely hope it's useful.

— Farrice
```

## The 7-Day Sprint Calendar

| Day | Action | Time | Output |
|-----|--------|------|--------|
| **Day 1** | Select 3 target coaches. Build abbreviated Voice Profiles. | 2-3 hrs | 3 profiles |
| **Day 2** | Run flywheel demo on Target 1. Record Loom. | 2-3 hrs | 1 demo + Loom |
| **Day 3** | Send DM to Target 1. Run flywheel demo on Target 2. | 2-3 hrs | 1 DM sent + 1 demo |
| **Day 4** | Record Loom for Target 2. Send DM. Start Target 3. | 2-3 hrs | 2 DMs sent + 1 demo in progress |
| **Day 5** | Finish Target 3 demo + Loom. Send DM. Post before/after on YOUR LinkedIn. | 2-3 hrs | 3 DMs sent + 1 LinkedIn post |
| **Day 6** | Follow up on DMs. Post another before/after on LinkedIn. Engage in feed. | 1-2 hrs | Follow-ups + content |
| **Day 7** | Book calls. Close 1 Authority Sprint at $500-$1,500 (pilot pricing). | 1-2 hrs | REVENUE |

**Pilot pricing note:** For the first 1-2 clients, $500-$750 Authority Sprint is acceptable to get case studies. Frame it as: "I'm building my portfolio in the S&C space. I'll do a 30-day sprint at a fraction of the normal price in exchange for a testimonial and permission to use the before/after."

## The Portfolio Angle

Every demo you produce is a portfolio piece. After 3-5 demos, you have:
- A Loom library of before/after flywheel demos
- Newsletter samples across different S&C sub-niches
- LinkedIn post examples in multiple coach voices
- Proof that the system works on anyone with deep expertise

This portfolio becomes the foundation for pitching:
- Higher-ticket Authority Autopilot retainers ($2,500-$3,500/mo)
- Adjacent niches (golf, longevity, wellness executives)
- Creative strategy roles at agencies or media companies
- Your own content consulting practice

**The $500 demo IS the portfolio.** There's no wasted work here.
