# One-Gap DM System: The Angle Map

## Objective

Start a qualified conversation with an authorized buyer who has a visible launch, retail move, reformulation, new ingredient, or repositioning.

The message is not a free teardown. It proves that the sender noticed one consequential decision in the buyer's real situation.

## Research cap

Spend no more than 15–20 minutes on a prospect:

1. Verify the named buyer is still connected to the brand.
2. Open one current trigger source.
3. Read the live product or campaign page.
4. Capture one exact source phrase.
5. Name one message, proof, or buyer-decision gap.
6. Send.

If a gap cannot be grounded in public material inside the cap, skip the prospect.

## Message-one formula

Keep it to four short moves:

1. **Specific trigger:** a launch, expansion, reformulation, campaign, or exact public phrase.
2. **One gap:** the unresolved buyer decision, not a broad brand critique.
3. **Useful interpretation:** one sentence showing why that gap matters now.
4. **Low-friction question:** ask permission to send the concise read.

Template:

> [Name], I saw [brand/trigger] and the line “[exact public phrase].”
> The gap I see is [one specific message or proof decision].
> That matters now because [occasion-specific consequence].
> Want the one-paragraph read?

Do not:

- Lead with Farrice's biography.
- Diagnose legal noncompliance.
- Invent revenue, conversion, or market-share consequences.
- Send a multi-page free teardown.
- Attach a deck in the first message.
- Claim the brand is wrong when the observation is an inference.

## Message two: price by the second touch

Send after a reply, or as the Day-2 follow-up if the opener is unanswered:

> The short read is this: [two-sentence interpretation].
> If you want the decision made rather than another loose opinion, I do that through **The Angle Map**.
> It includes a 60-minute live read and three campaign angles, with hooks, approved-proof direction, and an editorial claim-safety line for each.
> It is **$750 prepaid and delivered within 48 hours**. Worth seeing the exact scope?

This satisfies the price rule. Do not hide the price behind a call.

## Qualified-reply transition

> The first thing I need to know is the occasion and deadline.
> Is this for a live launch, retail push, reformulation, or repositioning?
> Who has final approval on the message?

If the occasion, authority, and evidence boundary are real:

> That fits the Angle Map. It is $750 prepaid. I’ll send the scope and payment link now; once payment and the approved inputs are in, the 48-hour clock starts.

## Day-5 closeout

> Closing the loop on this, [Name].
> I reached out because of the live [launch/retail/reformulation] decision, not to sell a generic brand audit.
> If settling three usable campaign angles inside 48 hours would help, the Angle Map is $750 prepaid.
> If the timing is wrong, I’ll leave it here.

After this, stop unless the prospect creates a new signal.

## Call rules

A 20-minute fit call answers only:

- What decision must be made?
- What is the deadline?
- Who can approve the work?
- What evidence and claim boundaries can the brand provide?
- Is the $750 Angle Map enough, or is the need larger?

Quote the price in every qualified conversation. A call without a price is not sales progress.

## Payment handoff

> I’ll send the $750 payment link and one-page scope now. The 48-hour window starts when payment and the source materials are in. Your attorney or compliance owner retains final approval on claim-sensitive language.

Do not say “invoice coming” unless a real payment rail exists and can be sent immediately.

## Five grounded first-wave examples

These are working drafts based on brand-supplied public announcements. Recheck the source and the buyer's current role immediately before sending. The gap sentence is an inference, not a reported company problem.

Source: [GateDrop distribution announcement, June 30, 2026](https://www.bevnet.com/pr/2026/06/30/gatedrop-accelerates-retail-expansion-with-broader-distribution-wins)

> The message now has to welcome a much broader convenience-store buyer without sanding off the literal gate-drop moment that makes the brand recognizable.
> That choice gets harder as the retail footprint moves beyond the original action-sports context.
> If useful, I can send the one-paragraph read.

## Disqualification replies

### Legal sign-off request

> I can map language to the evidence your team approves and flag the questions your reviewer should answer. I cannot certify legal compliance. Your attorney or compliance owner keeps final approval.

### Results or rankings guarantee

> I guarantee the scoped Angle Map and the 48-hour delivery window, not revenue, rankings, retailer acceptance, or AI citations.

### Pre-revenue buyer

> The Angle Map works best when a real product, proof set, and launch decision already exist. You are earlier than the scope I can responsibly sell, so I would not take the payment yet.

## Logging rule

After the message actually leaves the account:

1. Change the prospect's stage in `pipeline.md` to `dm'd`.
2. Record the date, channel, and message variant.
3. Add the daily aggregate to `CASH-SCOREBOARD-2026-07-29.md`.
4. Never mark a reply, held conversation, sale, or collection without evidence.
