# Campaign Day 3 Follow-Up — Non-Responder Light Touch

**Trigger:** Use on Day 3 (72 hours after initial send) IF the prospect has not replied  
**Tone:** Light, no pressure, leaves door open for "later"  
**Length:** ~40 words (mobile-friendly)  
**Goal:** Confirm they saw the message + offer to resend the angle if it got buried
