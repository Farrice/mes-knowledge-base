# Unified Offer Brief — Usage Guide

**Quick reference for when to use which document**

---

## The Two Documents

### `unified-offer-brief.md` (Full)
- **Length**: ~4-6 pages (319 lines)
- **Read time**: 7-10 minutes
- **Audience**: Founders doing due diligence, growth leads evaluating partnerships, internal sales team briefing
- **Use cases**: 
  - Email discovery call (paste full doc or link)
  - Sales deck companion or appendix
  - Internal alignment (onboarding new team members)
  - Long-form LinkedIn article or Substack post
  - Client qualification worksheet (send after first call)

### `unified-offer-brief-one-pager.md` (Quick Reference)
- **Length**: ~1-1.5 pages (133 lines)
- **Read time**: 2-3 minutes
- **Audience**: Time-crunched buyers, initial outreach, DM funnels
- **Use cases**:
  - DM attachments or link share (Twitter/LinkedIn DMs)
  - Print & share at events/conferences
  - LinkedIn carousel hook (break into 5-7 slides)
  - Slack/Telegram quick reference
  - Email subject line + first-glance summary
  - QR code landing page (print version)

## Sales Sequences (Copy/Paste Ready)

```

Hey [Name],

I pulled apart your last 5 posts. You've got a claim-safety gap I think you'd want to see—and 3 angles I'd ship instead.

No pitch. Just a quick read on your positioning.
```

### Sequence 2: Email Discovery Call with Full Brief

```
Subject: Your Brand Content Diagnostic (Ready When You Are)

Hi [Name],

Before our call Thursday, I wanted to get specific about what we'd audit.

I built a service ladder for health brands that goes: free teardown → diagnostic audit → proof run in your feed → full market-to-message system.

Most founders skip from "we need better content" to hiring an agency. This ladder de-risks it—you see proof before you commit.

Here's the full breakdown: [link to unified-offer-brief.md]

Quick version if you're pressed: [link to unified-offer-brief-one-pager.md]

I'm especially curious what resonates most: the free teardown angle, or jumping straight to the full strategy intensive?

Looking forward to Thursday.

—[Your Name]
```

### Sequence 3: Post-Audit Upsell to Retainer

```
Subject: Your Audit is Ready + Clear Next Steps

[Name],

Your full diagnostic is ready. Here's what we found:

1. **Sameness Problem**: [1-sentence diagnosis]
2. **Claim-Safety Gap**: [1-sentence diagnosis]

If proof-in-feed works, most founders move into the $4-9K/mo retainer for ongoing strategy.

→ See pricing & timelines in attached brief (Pillar 3)

Which direction feels right?

—[Your Name]
```

## Positioning Language (Pick One, Stick With It)

### If You Lead with Proof-First:
**"We start with free. A teardown of your brand + 3 angles I'd ship. Then you decide if the approach works before paying anything."**

→ Use: Full brief Pillar 1 + One-pager "Proof at every stage"

### If You Lead with Claim-Safety:
**"Health brands lose trust when their claims aren't backed. I audit your claims, grade your sources, and turn them into authority content."**

→ Use: Full brief Pillar 2 (Audit) + Claim-safety section

### If You Lead with Full System:
**"You don't need more posts. You need a system where every piece builds the same narrative. That's the Creative Strategy."**

→ Use: Full brief Pillar 4 + "Quick Strategy" path in comparison table

### If You Lead with Founder Voice:
**"Everyone has the same AI models. You have someone who's lived the physiology. The angle is true before it's scaled."**

→ Use: Full brief Positioning Spine + Pillar 3 "Founder-True" section

## Metrics to Track (What Works)

Once you start using these:

**One-Pager Signals:**
- Click-through rate (who clicks the link)
- Time-on-page (mobile vs. desktop)
- Scroll depth (do they read all the way?)
- Saves/downloads
- Shares (forwarded to team)

**Full Brief Signals:**
- Email open rate (if sent via email)
- Page views (if hosted)
- Scroll depth (engagement depth)
- Time spent (are they seriously reading?)
- Upsell conversion (did they book the call/audit?)

**Sequence Signals:**
- Reply rate (% of recipients who engage)
- Meeting booked (% who say yes to call)
- Audit close rate (% who buy after reading)
- Retainer upgrade rate (% of audit → retainer conversion)

## Internal Alignment (For Your Team)

If you have sales or operations people:

1. **Send them the full brief first** (they need context)
2. **Point them to Pillar 3 for common objections** (pricing, timeline, proof)
3. **Have them bookmark the one-pager** (for quick references)
4. **Review "Common Paths" section together** (so everyone knows the sequence)
5. **Share the Positioning Spine** (so everyone leads with the same hook)

---

## Feedback Loop (How to Improve)

Track which sections get the most engagement:

- **If Pillar 1 (Setup/Teardown) gets highest click-through**: Lead with free proof, skip the pitch
- **If Pillar 2 (Audit) gets most questions**: Lean into claim-safety messaging
- **If Pillar 3 (Retainer) gets highest conversion**: You're selling proof-of-concept well; upsell aggressively
- **If Pillar 4 (Strategy) gets highest value perception**: Charge more; raise minimums

Update pricing, positioning language, and messaging based on what the market responds to.
