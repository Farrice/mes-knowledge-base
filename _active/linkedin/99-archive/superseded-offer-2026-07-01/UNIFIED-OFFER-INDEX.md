## The 4 Pillars (Structure)

### Pillar 1: Setup (Free)
- **Entry Point**: Free teardown + diagnostic kickoff
- **What They Get**: Custom brand analysis + 3 angles they could ship tomorrow
- **Timeline**: 3–5 business days
- **Price**: $0
- **Next Step**: → Audit

### Pillar 2: Audit ($500–$1,500 Pilot)
- **Entry Point**: Deep diagnostic (merged from both sources' audit offerings)
- **What They Get**: Sameness diagnosis + claim-safety audit + compliance read + 3–5 divergence angles
- **Deliverables**: Brand brief + claim grading sheet + implementation roadmap + walkthrough
- **Timeline**: 5–7 business days
- **Price**: $500 (pilot) → $1,000–$1,500 (standard)
- **Next Step**: → Ghostwriting Retainer (Proof Run)

### Pillar 3: Ghostwriting Retainer ($1,500–$9K)
- **Entry Point**: Two phases (proof run → ongoing retainer)
- **Phase 1 - Proof Run (7 days)**: 8–12 founder-voice posts + 1 VSL + visual briefs
- **Phase 2 - Retainer (Ongoing)**: Weekly angle briefing + 4–8 posts/mo + monthly strategy call + claim-safety vetting
- **Timeline**: 7 days (sprint) or monthly (retainer)
- **Price**: $1,500–$3,000 (sprint) or $4–9K/mo (retainer)
- **Proof**: 40–50% proof-run-to-retainer conversion rate
- **Next Step**: → Creative Strategy or Keep Retainer

### Pillar 4: Creative Strategy ($3K–$25K)
- **Entry Point**: Two tiers (sprint vs. intensive)
- **Sprint ($3–5K)**: Market signal brief + positioning + narrative arc + content system architecture + offer bridge + 30-day calendar
- **Intensive ($10–25K)**: Sprint + 90-day calendar (pre-written) + long-tail question maps + 5–10 signature assets + monthly implementation calls + team training
- **Timeline**: 2–3 weeks (sprint) or 30–45 days (intensive)
- **Proof**: Teachable system, unified narrative, natural content-to-offer flow
- **Next Step**: → Layer retainer on top or iterate faster

### Backend Module: Source-to-Search Trust Layer
- **Entry Point**: Added during Audit, Retainer, AEO/SEO/GEO sprint, or Strategy Intensive
- **What They Get**: Claim library, source hierarchy, brand entity file, long-tail question map, answer-page plan, content decision rules, examples, counterexamples, quality tests, and update cadence
- **Use**: Turns claim-safe creative strategy into an agent-readable system without leading public sales copy with technical language
- **Best First Proof**: GLP-1 Movement Receipt

## Sales Paths (3 Common Routes)

### Path 1: Full Funnel (Most Common)
**Teardown → Audit → Proof Run → Retainer**
- Timeline: 4–6 weeks to retainer entry
- Investment: $2–4.5K to entry + $4–9K/mo ongoing
- Best for: Brands new to the system, want de-risked proof

### Path 2: Quick Strategy (High-Intent Founders)
**Teardown → Strategy Intensive**
- Timeline: 3–4 weeks
- Investment: $3–5K one-time + optional $4–9K/mo (retainer)
- Best for: Founders who already know they need a system rebuild

### Path 3: Fast-Track (Proven Founders, High-Budget)
**Teardown (1 call) → Strategy Intensive → Retainer**
- Timeline: 2–3 weeks to retainer entry
- Investment: $10–25K intensive + $6–9K/mo retainer
- Best for: $300K+/mo spend brands, urgent timeline
