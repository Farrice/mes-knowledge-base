**Category direction**: AI Creative Strategist — "AI with Heart" for brands. Systems AND soul.

Brands, meanwhile, are quietly using the most powerful divergence tool ever built to converge on the median — paying fortunes to look identical. Farrice occupies the empty middle: **a practitioner with 18+ years in human performance who uses AI to make a brand sound unmistakably like itself.** He doesn't defend AI or attack it. He demonstrates what depth looks like and lets the contrast do the arguing.

**Audience**: Primary — founders/CMOs + heads of content/brand/social at mid-size wellness/fitness/supplement/performance brands (and the creative leads/agencies they hire). Secondary — wellness/fitness experts & founders building a personal brand. Win condition is **hybrid** (role/seat OR client). See `research/wellness-supplement-brand-niche.md`.

**Bridge line**: *Your brand doesn't have a content problem. It has an averageness problem. I fix the second one.*

The performance/corrective lens (assess → inhibit → lengthen → activate → integrate) now applies to **brands**: *loading a misaligned brand with AI = barbell-squatting a herniated business; you don't scale slop, you align the brand's posture first, THEN add the load.* Rules: max 1 performance metaphor per post; passes Inclusion Insurance (a non-athlete still gets full value); never the hook two posts running.
