# 04 - Format And Platform Playbook

## Purpose

This file turns the ideas into platform-native assets. The standard is not "same content everywhere." The standard is one argument expressed in the right shape for each surface.

## LinkedIn Is The Home Base

LinkedIn is where the buyer is most likely to:

- save a useful audit lens
- check Farrice's profile
- DM after a visible proof object
- recognize that he understands brand, claim, proof, and AI search at the same time

Primary formats:

1. Text post
2. Document carousel
3. LinkedIn article/newsletter
4. Comment
5. DM follow-up
6. Profile/Featured section
7. Short direct-to-camera video

## Format 1 - Text Post

Use when:

- speed matters
- the insight is clear without visuals
- the post is a mechanism reveal or newsjack

Best structure:

1. Hook with a specific pressure.
2. Scene or buyer anxiety.
3. Turn: the visible problem is not the real problem.
4. Proof or mechanism.
5. One GET.
6. Restraint close.

Rules:

- No questions in the hook.
- No fake urgency.
- No generic "brands should."
- No long preamble.
- No every-post CTA.

## Format 2 - Document Carousel

Use when:

- the reader should save it
- the audit has stages
- the proof object needs visible structure

Best for:

- Teardown Tuesday
- AI-Search Visibility Test
- Claim Receipt
- Proof Vs Claim
- Soft-Line Audit Demo

Slide flow:

1. Cover: named problem, not vague benefit.
2. The claim or prompt.
3. What the buyer sees.
4. What the machine sees.
5. What is missing.
6. The proof boundary.
7. The stronger rewrite or asset.
8. Receipt.
9. Practical GET.
10. Optional audit bridge.

Design rule:

Keep it plain, legible, and proof-forward. Avoid decorative design that weakens the audit feel.

## Format 3 - LinkedIn Article Or Newsletter

Use when:

- the idea should become a durable proof surface
- the source trail matters
- the weekly flagship can become an answer-page seed

Best structure:

1. The buyer scene.
2. The market shift.
3. The proof problem.
4. The source-backed analysis.
5. The audit lens.
6. The offer bridge.
7. Claim ledger or source note.

Frequency:

One strong article every 2 to 4 weeks beats a weekly article that repeats the posts.

## Format 4 - Strategic Comment

Use daily.

The comment is not applause. It is a small proof of judgment in someone else's room.

Comment types:

- Claim-boundary comment
- AI-search implication comment
- buyer-language refinement
- source-quality correction
- founder-voice encouragement
- "what I would audit first" note

Good comment shape:

1. Acknowledge the real point.
2. Add one specific layer.
3. Name the decision it creates.
4. Stop.

Never pitch in the first comment.

## Format 5 - DM Follow-Up

Use only when earned by specificity.

DM opener after a public teardown:

"I pulled one small gap from your public page/content because it sits right at the claim/search boundary. No pitch. The short version: [specific gap]. The asset I would build first is [specific asset]. Happy to send the one-page read if useful."

DM after someone asks about audit:

"Send me your site, category, and one competitor. I will run the first five prompts and show you the first real visibility/trust gap I see."

DM rule:

Specificity earns the message. Generic curiosity does not.

## Format 6 - Profile And Featured Section

The profile is retrieval infrastructure.

It should make the lane obvious:

- health, wellness, supplement, performance brands
- AI search
- claims and proof
- founder/expert voice
- audit-first service

Featured section should hold:

1. AI-Search Visibility Test
2. Claim-Safe Citation Audit
3. one flagship article
4. one Soft-Line Audit Demo
5. proof tracker or proof-in-progress note

## Format 7 - Direct-To-Camera Video

Use only for trust, not volume.

Best topics:

- why Farrice refuses fake proof
- the two-gate problem in plain language
- claim boundary examples
- founder voice translation
- quick audit walkthrough

Video structure:

1. One concrete page, prompt, or claim.
2. One specific read.
3. One thing most marketers miss.
4. One practical correction.

Do not make generic motivational video.

## Repurpose Map

One flagship adjudication becomes:

1. LinkedIn text post: the sharpest thesis.
2. LinkedIn carousel: the audit object.
3. Article/newsletter: the source-backed proof surface.
4. Comment set: 5 to 10 insight comments.
5. DM opener: one specific private bridge.
6. Website/Featured section: if strong enough, archive as proof.

Repurposing rule:

Keep the argument. Rewrite the asset.

## CTA Rhythm

Default:

- 4 of 5 posts: no direct CTA.
- 1 of 5 posts: soft audit or discussion bridge.

Allowed soft CTAs:

- "Send me your site, category, and one competitor if you want the first five-prompt read."
- "This is the shape of the Claim-Safe Citation Audit."
- "I am collecting examples of brands with real proof that AI search is not carrying yet."

Avoid:

- "Book a call."
- "DM me if you want to grow."
- "Comment AUDIT" on every post.
- false scarcity.
- proof claims that do not exist yet.

## Weekly Format Mix

Starter week:

- Tuesday: carousel or strong text teardown.
- Thursday: text mechanism/newsjack.
- Saturday: founder/trust text.
- Daily: comments.

Strong week:

- Tuesday: carousel teardown.
- Wednesday: strategic comments and replies.
- Thursday: text newsjack.
- Friday: proof tracker update or article seed.
- Saturday: founder/trust post.

Do not add more channels until LinkedIn has 6 consecutive weeks of execution.
