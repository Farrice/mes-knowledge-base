## The System In One Sentence

Build a one-person media company that publicly proves Farrice can see the buyer's market, claim risk, AI-search gap, and content angle better than the buyer can, then converts that public proof into an audit, ghostwriting, and creative strategy ladder.

## The Business Spine

Offer ladder:

1. Free AI-Search Visibility Test
2. Paid Claim-Safe Citation Audit
3. GEO Authority Ghostwriting
4. Creative Strategy Intensive or embedded seat

Content must always support that ladder, but it should not behave like a pitch feed. The job of content is to make the buyer think:

"He understands our market, our claims, our proof gaps, our buyer anxiety, and the AI-search shelf better than we do."

## The Editorial Spine

The best content posture is not generic education. It is adjudication:

- Settle a live category fight.
- Show the proof boundary.
- Name the buyer's private anxiety.
- Make the machine's source logic visible.
- Give the reader one useful test, question, receipt, or decision.

The recurring word bank is: named, cited, carried, proof, receipt, claim boundary, source, mechanism, trust, AI shelf, two gates.

Do not lead with GEO/AEO. Lead with being named, cited, or carried.

## Weekly Operating Loop

Every week produces:

1. One flagship adjudication.
2. One proof or audit object.
3. One mechanism/newsjack post.
4. One founder/voice/trust post.
5. One strategic comment sprint.
6. One small DM bridge only when earned.

Minimum viable week:

- Tuesday: Teardown Tuesday.
- Thursday: AI-search or claim-boundary mechanism post.
- Saturday: founder/market/trust post.
- Daily: 15 to 30 minutes of comments on ICP threads.

## Non-Negotiables

- No fake case studies.
- No assumed AI citation claims without screenshots or prompt logs.
- No medical advice, diagnosis, dosing, or treatment recommendations.
- No "more content" as the solution.
- No every-post "DM me AUDIT."
- No generic growth-hacker tone.
- No hiding uncertainty when a source is only likely or unconfirmed.

## Operating Standard

Every asset must pass this test:

Can a head of brand, founder, formulator, or growth lead finish it and say, "This names a real pressure in my business, gives me a sharper way to see it, and proves Farrice would notice gaps my current team misses"?

If not, the piece is not ready.
