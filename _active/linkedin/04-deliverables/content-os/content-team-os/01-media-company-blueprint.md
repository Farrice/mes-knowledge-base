# 01 - Media Company Blueprint

## Purpose

This is the strategic architecture for turning Farrice's offer into a visible authority machine. The goal is not to become a general wellness creator. The goal is to become the operator-class source for claim-safe, AI-search-aware, founder-led health performance authority.

## Positioning

Farrice is the credible body-expert who makes health brands the source AI search names, with claims that can survive scrutiny.

The buyer is not buying posts. They are buying relief from three pressures:

1. The machine might name someone else.
2. Their proof might not be organized well enough to travel.
3. A strong claim might become a liability if it is not supported.

The content should make those pressures visible before the sales call.

## Audience

Primary audience: Dana, the brand/content/growth lead.

Her public problem:

- Rankings are no longer equal to demand.
- AI answers are taking the buyer conversation.
- The founder wants proof that the brand is still visible.

Her private pressure:

- "I did the responsible marketing work, but the scoreboard changed."
- "I need a new visibility system I can defend to leadership."

Secondary audience: Marcus, the founder/formulator/expert.

His public problem:

- The brand has real expertise but low content authority.
- Founder voice is needed, but he does not want to become a performer.

His private pressure:

- "People with less expertise are becoming the category name."
- "I want to be visible without becoming someone I do not respect."

## Content Promise

Farrice's content promises one useful turn:

The reader sees the difference between a claim, a proof surface, and a source the machine can carry.

Every piece should do at least one of these:

- Make a hidden buyer anxiety visible.
- Show the claim boundary.
- Show the machine's source logic.
- Convert a trend into an audit lens.
- Convert a brand's scattered proof into a stronger asset.
- Make the founder's real expertise legible.

## Content Zones

The media system has four zones.

### Zone 1 - Public Adjudication

Visible content that settles a live fight:

- Teardown Tuesday
- AI answer red-team
- claim-boundary post
- mechanism reveal
- proof vs claim post

### Zone 2 - Proof Objects

Assets that demonstrate the service:

- Soft-Line Audit demo
- Claim Receipt
- AI-Search Visibility Test
- Source-to-Search map
- named-brand teardown
- before/after proof surface rewrite

### Zone 3 - Founder Trust

Human posts that make the buyer trust Farrice:

- why he refuses fake proof
- how 18 years in performance shaped his claim read
- mistakes he sees in wellness content
- what he will not say in a category that touches bodies
- voice memo to authority examples

### Zone 4 - Conversion

Soft bridge assets:

- Featured section offer page
- occasional audit CTA
- DM follow-up
- case-study-in-progress notes
- proof tracker updates

## Channel Strategy

Primary channel: LinkedIn.

Reason: the buyer is an operator, founder, content lead, growth lead, or expert-adjacent decision-maker. LinkedIn is where a small account can still be discovered through comments, saves, and specific category language.

Secondary channels:

- Newsletter or article: durable proof surface for the strongest weekly adjudication.
- X: listening and fast testing, not primary trust channel.
- Short video: use only for trust and direct-to-camera claim-boundary reads.
- Website/offer page: convert proof rail into a simple audit path.

## The Weekly Media Product

Each week should feel like a small issue of a trade publication:

- one lead story
- one proof object
- one practical audit lens
- one founder/voice note
- one offer bridge

The audience should learn to expect a specific kind of judgment from Farrice.

## What Makes This Different

Most content systems optimize for volume. This one optimizes for remembered judgment.

The system wins when the buyer thinks:

- "I have not seen a ghostwriter think about claim risk like this."
- "I have not seen a GEO person understand the physiology like this."
- "I have not seen a creative strategist connect AI search, proof, and founder voice like this."

## Success Metrics

Track monthly, not daily:

- saves from ICP-aligned accounts
- profile views from founders, brand leads, growth leads, content leads
- qualified DMs
- audit requests
- replies to teardown posts
- named brands added to the proof tracker
- one new proof object per week
- one stronger claim-safe asset per month

Avoid overvaluing:

- raw likes
- follower count alone
- impressions without ICP fit
- viral posts that do not strengthen the offer
