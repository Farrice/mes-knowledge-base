# First 30-Day Content Sprint

## Goal

Launch the authority lane with 12 core posts, 4 Teardown Tuesday editions, 4 proof objects, and daily comment targets.

The sprint should prove the audit-first offer before pushing the retainer.

## Sprint Structure

Cadence:

- Tuesday: Teardown Tuesday
- Thursday: AI-search, claim-boundary, or mechanism post
- Saturday: founder/trust post
- Daily: strategic comments

Monthly output:

- 12 LinkedIn posts
- 4 Teardown Tuesday topics
- 4 proof objects
- 100 serious comments minimum
- 1 to 2 soft audit bridges per week only when earned
