# Weekly Launch Cockpit

## Purpose

This is the one-page operating surface for the content-team OS. Use it every Monday before writing and every Sunday before planning the next week.

## Weekly Objective

Produce one public proof asset, one audit lens, one mechanism or newsjack, one founder/trust post, and one serious comment sprint that all strengthen the same offer ladder.

## Offer Ladder

1. AI-Search Visibility Test
2. Claim-Safe Citation Audit
3. GEO Authority Ghostwriting
4. Creative Strategy Intensive or embedded seat

## Monday - Source Packet

Inputs:

- 1 live category signal
- 1 buyer pressure
- 1 claim or proof boundary
- 1 AI-search question
- 1 source-backed fact
- 1 offer rung

Output:

- weekly thesis
- 10 angle candidates
- chosen 3 assets

Gate:

If the signal cannot become an audit lens, do not build the week around it.

## Tuesday - Teardown Tuesday

Inputs:

- claim, brand archetype, AI answer, product page, or proof surface
- source notes
- claim boundary

Output:

- flagship post
- carousel or visual outline
- first-comment receipt
- one practical GET

Gate:

The teardown must feel useful, not smug.

## Wednesday - Distribution And Listening

Inputs:

- Tuesday post comments
- ICP account list
- buyer phrases from replies or comments

Output:

- 10 to 20 serious comments
- 3 saved buyer-language snippets
- 1 DM bridge only if specific value exists

Gate:

No pitching in cold comments.

## Thursday - Mechanism Or Newsjack

Inputs:

- source packet
- buyer anxiety
- one mechanism

Output:

- short LinkedIn post
- 1 practical check or question
- 5 comments on related threads

Gate:

Do not summarize news. Convert it into a buyer decision.

## Friday - Proof Rail

Inputs:

- source links
- screenshots or prompt logs, if any
- teardown notes
- comments that surfaced buyer language

Output:

- proof tracker row
- saved source packet
- audit object draft
- next asset recommendation

Gate:

No proof object gets promoted without source, standard, consequence, and next asset.

## Saturday - Founder/Trust Post

Inputs:

- one Farrice worldview line
- one thing the work refuses to say
- one founder/expert tension

Output:

- trust-building post
- no hard CTA

Gate:

The post should make the right founder feel safer being visible, not pressured to perform.

## Sunday - Review

Score:

- strongest save-worthy asset
- strongest comment quality
- best buyer phrase
- proof object created
- offer rung strengthened
- next week's repeated series

Decision:

- repeat
- revise
- kill

## Publish Gates

Before any public asset ships:

- specific buyer
- real pressure
- one useful GET
- source or limitation near every factual claim
- no fake result
- no medical advice
- no hard CTA unless earned
- one offer rung strengthened

## Weekly Scorecard

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Posts | 3 |
| Teardown Tuesday | 1 |
| Proof object | 1 |
| Serious comments | 25 minimum |
| Buyer-language snippets | 3 |
| DM bridges | 0 to 3, only when earned |
| Offer rungs touched | at least 2 |

## Weekly Kill Questions

- Did I make the offer easier to trust?
- Did I create a proof object?
- Did I name a buyer pressure better than the buyer could?
- Did any asset sound like generic content strategy?
- Did I ask for the sale before proving the read?

If the answer exposes weak proof, build a better audit object next week.
