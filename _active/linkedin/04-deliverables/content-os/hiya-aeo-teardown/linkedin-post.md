# LinkedIn Post — Hiya Answer Audit (channel cut)

USANA paid $205 million for Hiya, a kids' vitamin brand built on zero sugar. Ask an AI for the best no-sugar kids vitamin and it answers Flintstones.

I noticed the way everyone does. Instagram retargeting for health brands is so fast now it feels like your phone is listening.

So I ran a live test on July 15. Ten questions a buying parent actually asks, from "best kids multivitamin without added sugar" down to a 25-word one about a 6-year-old who only eats pasta and chicken nuggets. Two surfaces: a live answer engine and Google's results. 24 scored runs, real transcripts, every number computed from them.

What the transcripts showed:

Hiya wins the fights it already knows about. "Hiya vs Ritual" went to Hiya on both surfaces. The 2026 best-of question named them the overall pick. My worried-parent question about the pasta kid got Hiya as the first recommendation.

Then the floor drops.

The one question their whole brand was built to win went to Flintstones Sugar-Free Gummies. Twice. Hiya's own site ranked #2 on Google for the exact same words and still didn't make the AI's answer. Ranking and getting cited are different games now.

Their brand-new protein powder, launched in May, appeared in zero answers for kids protein questions. Eight weeks of invisibility on a hero launch.

And a question that describes their product almost clause by clause (4-year-old, dairy allergy, hates gummies, two cavities) went to Renzo's and EllaOla.

The fix is one page, not a content calendar. An original sugar audit across the 15 best-selling kids vitamins, including the number nobody currently publishes: what a daily gummy habit adds up to over a year. Give the answer engines a reason to cite you on your own question.

Scope check, because this matters: I measured visibility, not vitamins. 24 runs across 2 surfaces, below my own 3-surface standard since Perplexity's API was down that morning, and every claim carries a label in the full teardown.

I run one of these a week on a funded health or performance brand.
