## PHASE 2 — ASSESSMENT-LED DEMAND ARCHITECTURE

**The instrument**: **"The Ad Rejection Risk Score"** — *Is your best product losing to better marketing?* (ScoreApp or equivalent; this is your Pattern 9 X-ray and your Cool-zone intervention.)

**Landing page** (Pattern 35 anatomy): Frustration hook — "Feeling frustrated that competitors with worse formulas outsell you — even though your reviews are better?" · Subhead — "Answer 15 questions to find out why, and what to fix first." · VP — "We measure and improve three things: your angle strength, your claim safety, and your creative volume." · Credibility — 18-yr practitioner bio + the rejection/CPM stats · CTA — Start the quiz · 3 minutes · free · immediate recommendations.

**The 20 questions**:
- *8 Problem-Surface* (score-carrying): Do you know which of your ads is your control? Has Meta rejected an ad in the last 90 days? Are you running fewer than 8 creative variants per test cycle? Is your best hook a customer-POV testimonial? Do your ads lead with outcomes ("energy," "focus") rather than a named problem? Has legal/compliance ever killed a concept after it was produced? Are you re-using the same 2-3 angles for 6+ months? Do you know your creative's contribution vs your offer's?
- *6 Desire-Amplification*: What would a control-beating ad be worth per month? Which launch/occasion is on your calendar? What would it mean to walk into a retail meeting with proof your creative converts? (etc. — paint Desired Reality)
- *6 Qualification* (the Big 5 adapted): role/authority · revenue band · dated occasion inside 90 days? · monthly ad spend band · solution preference (angles only / done-for-you creative / full sprint) · open box.
**Scoring**: A = authority + occasion ≤90 days + spend ≥$10k/mo → 1:1 fit call within 24 hrs. B = authority, no dated occasion → group/content nurture + Angle Map offer. C = no authority/spend → content only.

**Signal Volume Calculator (Pattern 4, honest math)**: Sprint capacity 2/month + creative slots 16/month. Warm ratio 5:1 → ~80-90 signals/month needed at full run-rate. Your DM ceiling (~150/wk warm) yields 60-100 replies/month — **feasible at maturity, not in week one. Weeks 1-4 target: 40 signals/month, scaling as content compounds.** Never quote the aspirational number as the plan.

## PHASE 3 — THE CAMPAIGN (adapted for a Cold-zone start: 5 phases, not 4)

**Phase 3.0 — Problem-Aware Runway (Days 1-14, Zone 1→2)** *[the addition the Thermometer forces]*
Content = Proof of Problem only. No offers in the feed. DM engine runs in parallel (private ≠ public scarcity).
- 3 test posts, one per problem frame: (a) "Your best product is losing to a worse one with better ads — here's the mechanism." (b) "~30% of supplement ads die in review before spending a dollar. The claim that killed yours was avoidable." (c) "The most common supplement ad hook is also the worst-performing. The rarest one runs 2x longer." Watch which frame pulls replies/DMs — that frame leads Phase 3.1.
- Daily warm DMs (25-30) per the DM kit; Cooz expansion offer sent Day 1; Upwork's six live supplement postings applied to Day 2.

**Phase 3.1 — Anticipation (Days 15-28, Zone 2→3)** (Pattern 21: anticipation is 70% of the launch)
- Winning problem frame → a 5-post series; first transformation story (Cooz/portfolio, stakes framed); assessment goes live mid-phase ("I built a 3-minute diagnostic — it tells you which of the three failure modes your account has").
- Open the **priority notification list**: "I'm opening a small number of creative slots next month. Priority list gets first look + the founding rate."

**Phase 3.2 — Launch (Days 29-35, at Warm only)**
- Declare capacity: "4 creative slots/week. 72-hour delivery is the bottleneck — I architect every angle personally." Doors open to the priority list FIRST (waitlist-only soft launch, Pattern 29's Bookmagic move), then public.
- Logic/Emotion/Urgency alignment (Pattern 40): logic = cheaper than one test round + rejection-fix guarantee · emotion = the retail meeting where your creative is the proof, not the excuse · urgency = slots vs list size, stated transparently ONLY if real.

**Phase 3.3 — Close (Days 36-42)**: remaining-slot transparency, application step if Hot, "next intake" date set — the next round's anticipation starts the day this one closes.

**Tension Creative Brief** (campaign visuals): the psychological gap = *shelf-proud product / ad-account shame*. Palette: the locked editorial bone-and-ink from the July banner brief; imagery: product-hero minimalism vs red-flagged rejection screenshots (recreated, no platform logos); typography: the editorial serif. Never gymfluencer aesthetics — the restraint IS the differentiation.

## PHASE 4 — TENSION TRIANGLE SALES PLAYBOOK (for the 20-min fit calls + DM conversations)

**Current Reality excavation**: "Walk me through your last three ad tests — what won, and do you know why?" · "What happened the last time Meta rejected something?" · "Who writes your angles today, and what did they know about your customer before this job?"
**Desired Reality painting**: "If creative stopped being the bottleneck, what does the launch look like in 90 days?" · "What would you test if variants were free?"
**Obstacle naming**: let THEM say it (compliance fear, agency fatigue, founder-writes-everything). Then: "That's the gap the [Angle Map / Control-Beater] closes."
**Stakes-based close (price objection script)**: "The Angle Map is $750. One rejected campaign costs you the production spend, the media spend to learn it failed, and 2 weeks of a 90-day window. What's the launch worth if it slips a month?" Then silence.
**Peak-tension signals (prescribe NOW)**: they quote their own dead-spend number · they ask "how fast could you start?" · they name the occasion date unprompted. When any fires — stop diagnosing, send the payment link.
**Deposit fallback**: any stall → "$100 holds a founding slot at this rate."
