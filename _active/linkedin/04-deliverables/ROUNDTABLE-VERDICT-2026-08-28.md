# Roundtable Verdict — The Umbrella, Made Concrete (2026-08-28)

*Record of the 4-seat roundtable (Priestley/demand · Dunford/positioning · Hormozi-style offer/outcome · copy craft). Brief: `ROUNDTABLE-BRIEF-2026-08-28.md`. Ranked recommendation, dissent preserved.*

## Unanimous ground (all four seats, independent tests)

1. **The thesis line is dead as a headline.** "The best product almost never has the best marketing" — any agency can paste it (substitution fail); it diagnoses the market, and nobody buys a market diagnosis; it's a proverb that flatters the reader's product before saying what's for sale. It survives on the banner BENEATH the delivery promise, and as a content pillar.
2. **The 18 years is the only asset that is provable today, un-copyable by every alternative, and free of performance-claim exposure.** It belongs on the headline surface.
3. **The guarantee substitutes for case studies and must move to the first screen** — with zero clients, perceived likelihood is the collapsed term; the policy-rejection make-right is the only instrument he fully controls that raises it.
4. **214 chars is a trap.** Mobile shows ~45 chars. A headline the eye can finish beats one that fits. All seats landed 162-218.
5. **No scarcity/capacity language from Cold.** Hard gate.

## CANDIDATE 1 — The Crossover ★ RECOMMENDED

**Umbrella**: "I spent 18 years training the people who buy your supplement. Now I write the ads — three named angles in 48 hours, claim-safe creative in 72, and I eat the cost if Meta kills one."

**Headline (183 chars)**:
```
18 years training the people who buy your supplement. Now I write your ads. | 5 statics + 1 video in 72 hrs · 3 campaign angles in 48 | Meta policy rejection, I fix it free | DM PROOF
```

- **Hypothesis**: founder/marketing lead <$15M who has cycled through an agency/freelancer/$5-AI gig and can name what was wrong — none of them ever met the customer. Dated occasion forces the re-hire. Belief banked: *my ads underperform because nobody who wrote them was in the room when my customer told the truth.* Clause three banks: *a man who eats the rework risk knows the rules.*
- **Proof TODAY**: the credential as biographical fact; the four spec angle concepts (proof of the credential — "you didn't lose motivation, you lost blood sugar stability" reads as someone who's been in the room); the Meta-rejection make-right (fully controllable, needs no history); the Angle Map revise-until-usable make-right; Cooz = delivery receipt (adjacent category, not a supplement receipt).
- **Proof MISSING until clients land**: any evidence the credential converts. Never say "my ads beat agency hooks." Category stats stay labeled third-party (LIKELY). The guarantee is unexercised — if asked: "you'd be the first to test it — that's why it's free."
- **Outcome named**: 5 statics + 1 video in-account within 72 hrs of payment, claims screened, rejections fixed free without clock restart; or 3 named angles + verdict in 48.
- **Tie-back**: one asset priced by depth — Angle Map $750 (the customer's language as a DECISION) → Control-Beater $500 (the same language RENDERED) → Sprint $2,500 (the same language WIDENED). AI Brain stays off-profile.
- **Risk**: "Being a trainer doesn't mean you can write an ad" → countered because the outcome + guarantee land 12 words after the credential. If clause two is ever softened, this collapses into a memoir line and Candidate 2 becomes correct.

## CANDIDATE 2 — The Occasion (rank 2)

**Headline (181)**: `Your launch date is set. Your message isn't. | Supplement & performance brands: 3 named angles in 48 hrs, 5 claim-safe ads in 72 | 18 years a personal trainer before this | DM PROOF`
- Hypothesis: the committed-date founder; message indecision felt as a schedule problem; the deadline supplies urgency Cold hasn't earned. Best SALES ARGUMENT in the set — its stakes math ($750 < one $400-1,000 test round) runs the DM and fit call verbatim. Weakness: the opening eight words are pasteable by any positioning consultant — substitution fail exactly where the 5-second check happens. Cleanest ladder tie-back (the occasion is the canon's own gate).

## Forks (dissent preserved)

- **Fork 1 — credential vs occasion owns clause one (2-2, RULED for credential)**: Dunford+copy won via substitution + scan tests; Priestley+offer lost because the collapsed value term is perceived LIKELIHOOD, not desire — and only the credential+guarantee move it. The occasion frame relocates to DM opener + fit call + Featured cards.
- **Fork 2 — one deliverable or two in the headline (OPEN, operator call)**: run the 183-char two-deliverable version first; if DM replies show confusion, cut to the 154-char single-deliverable variant: `18 years training the people who buy your supplement. Now I write your ads. | 5 statics + 1 video in 72 hrs, any Meta policy rejection fixed free | DM PROOF`
- **Fork 3 — "DM PROOF" keyword vs visible price (OPEN, operator call)**: offer seat calls the keyword an effort tax; copy seat says the real failure was its placement at char 200. All candidates drop the quote marks and land PROOF 30-45 chars earlier. Keyword vs price = whether the 2-question qualifying script runs before or after the number.
- **Fork 4 — "human performance" → "personal trainer" (single seat, high conviction, touches ratified canon)**: change it and propagate — "human performance" is résumé-speak; "personal trainer" is the concrete noun and the whole asset. Canon files (PROOF-TO-MARKET-OS, Activation Packet, DM kit) need the propagation pass.

## Must-fix receipts violations on live copy (apply regardless of candidate)

1. "why my ads don't lead with miracle claims" → **"why I don't write miracle claims"** (he has no supplement ad body of work).
2. "built to beat your current control" → **"to test against your current control"** (never beaten one; offer NAME Control-Beater stays).
3. 6am/3pm drift: headline said 6am, About and all four angle concepts say 3pm → **standardize on 3pm** or use the object ("the drawer of half-finished supplements").
4. Cut "Judgment stays human" (agency boilerplate).
5. "nobody in your building owns that job" → **"nobody you've hired so far has actually owned it"** (7-person brands don't have buildings).
6. "Two ways in" followed by a third offer → **"Three doors, cheapest first."**
