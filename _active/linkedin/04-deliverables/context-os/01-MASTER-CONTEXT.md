## The positioning thesis (the argument all content makes)

**The best product in a category almost never has the best marketing.** Brands with real evidence get careful; careful sounds like maybe; maybe loses to a competitor with nothing to protect. Under every flat, hedged copy problem sits an unmade decision: *what are we proving, who needs to believe it, what will we say plainly and defend when pushed.* Writers can't fix an undecided message — that decision is the work, and it's the job nobody in the building owns. The market's own name for this problem: "Founder Fog" (73% of DTC founders can't state their positioning in one sentence).
