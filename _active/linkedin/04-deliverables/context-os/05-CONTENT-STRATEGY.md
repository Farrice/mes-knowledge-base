# Content Strategy — pillars, formats, platform mechanics

*Grounded in the 2026-07-28 zero-start research (56 sources on LinkedIn's 360Brew algorithm) + the live 14-day launch plan.*

## The two pillars (everything is one of these; a third topic is off-strategy)

1. **Proof — the teardowns.** Named-brand public messaging teardowns: credit where real first, then the gap, quoted verbatim from the brand's own surfaces, sent to the brand before publishing. The trust move is saying so. Cadence: ~1/week.
2. **The thesis argued from lived experience.** The unmade decision · careful sounds like maybe · the counter and the aisle · the Practitioner Test · claim-honesty as a growth lever. POV posts, each arguing ONE beat standalone.

Content's job by phase: weeks 1–4 it is the credibility surface a DM recipient checks (cash comes from DMs); weeks 5+ the interest graph starts delivering inbound. Judge early content by fit-conversations and saves, never reach.

## Platform mechanics that shape every piece (LinkedIn 360Brew, 2026)

- **Interest graph, not follower count**: content reaches strangers whose interests match semantically — a zero-follower account with tight niche language wins reach it hasn't "earned."
- **Expertise-match**: the algorithm cross-references post content against the profile. Stay inside the niche vocabulary (supplements, claims, positioning, DTC); topic-hopping is penalized.
- **Engagement weights**: Saves ~10x baseline · substantive comments (15+ words) ~7.5x · dwell >61s ~4x · likes ~1x. Write for save-worthiness (reference value) and dwell (depth), not reactions.
- **Golden hour**: first 60–90 minutes ≈ 70% of lifetime reach. Post, then stay on and reply to every comment.
- **The 5-6 rule**: before posting, 15–30 min of substantive comments on 5–6 target-audience posts (3+ sentences each: stop the scroll, add insight, one takeaway).
- **Cadence ceiling**: 3–5 posts/week. More is noise; the model rewards topical consistency and depth.
- Personal profile only (company pages reach ~1.6% of followers). Never engagement pods (detected; 60–90 day suppression).

## Formats

- **Teardowns → PDF carousels, 8–12 slides**: hook slide (the brand + the one-line finding) → context/symptom with verbatim quotes → the methodological read (the gap, step by step) → the fix direction → CTA slide (save + "DM PROOF"). Highest dwell + save format on the platform.
- **POV posts → text, 150–300 words**: open in the scene (first 7–10 words are the moment), one argument, Saxon close. No hashtag walls (3 niche tags max: #supplementmarketing #positioning #dtc).
- **Long-form article** (occasional): the thesis at full length, published after proof posts exist to back it.
- **DMs — the one-gap DM**: one verbatim quote from their site + one named gap + 4 sentences + no pitch, to a named person at a sub-$15M brand. Follow-up message 2 may name the $750 Angle Map. Never pitch in message one.

## Standing rules

- Every factual claim traces to 06-PROOF-LIBRARY with its label; UNCONFIRMED never ships.
- Every draft passes payoff / pull / recognition (see 04-VOICE-REGISTER).
- CTA discipline: one CTA per piece — save, DM PROOF, or the booking link. Never stacked.
- The reader is the protagonist; Farrice enters late, with receipts.
