# Offer Test Map — What to Test Under the Crossover Umbrella (living doc)

*Created 2026-08-28 post-roundtable. The umbrella is ratified ("18 years training the people who buy your supplement. Now I write your ads."). This map defines every offer that can be SIGNAL-TESTED under it without building anything — Priestley discipline: waitlist/DM signals first, deposits as truth serum, falsifiable gates set before data, kills announced publicly as research. Update in place as tests resolve.*

## The rules of the game (apply to every slot)

- **Test = a sentence in a DM or a one-line waitlist link. Never a build.** Permitted artifacts: one landing/waitlist page, a one-pager, a Stripe link. Nothing else until a gate passes.
- **Conditional frame**: "I'm adding [X] **if enough people want it** — join the list / reply INTEREST." Tests read as research under a stable umbrella, never as pivots.
- **Max 2 concurrent tests** (channel capacity: ~150 warm DMs/wk total; the CORE offers keep 70% of DM volume — tests ride along as a second question, they never displace the Control-Beater/Angle Map pitch).
- **Truth instrument**: interest ≠ demand. A slot only GOES on money signals: a deposit ($100), a prepaid, or a booked call that ends in "send the link."
- **Kill = content.** Every killed slot becomes a post: "Asked. Counted. The data said no. Doubling down on [winner]." This is the anti-guru pillar working for you.

## CORE (live, not tests — the ladder the tests must feed)
Control-Beater $500/72h · Angle Map $750/48h · Proof-to-Market Sprint $2,500/10d. Every test slot must either FEED this ladder or extend it upward. A test that creates a second unrelated business fails tie-back and gets parked.

## TEST SLOTS (ranked by test-this-first priority)

### Slot T1 — The Rejection Autopsy ($150, 24 hrs) — PRIORITY TEST
- **Hypothesis**: founders with a recently-killed ad will pay a small, fast fee for a diagnosis + compliant rewrite of the exact ad Meta rejected — because the pain is specific, recent, and cheap to fix.
- **The offer sentence**: "Send me the rejected ad. Within 24 hours: why it died, the exact claim line that killed it, and a rewritten version screened against published Meta health policy. $150."
- **Signal test**: add one line to cold DM message 2 and to the DM PROOF script: "If Meta just killed something of yours, I do a $150 rejection autopsy in 24 hrs." Zero pages needed.
- **Tie-back**: the cheapest possible proof of the umbrella's third clause; natural upsell to Control-Beater ("now let's build you five that won't die").
- **Gate**: GO = 3 paid autopsies in 21 days · KILL = 30+ DM conversations mentioning it, zero takers.
- **Why first**: lowest price, fastest delivery, 100% guarantee-compatible, and every delivery creates a before/after artifact for the portfolio — it manufactures the receipts the umbrella is missing.

### Slot T2 — The Customer-Language Bank ($350, 5 days)
- **Hypothesis**: brands will pay for the RESEARCH layer alone — 50-75 verbatim customer phrases (frustrations, objections, rituals, eye-roll triggers) for their exact category, mined and organized into an angle-ready bank — because "in the room" language is the thing their agency never had.
- **The offer sentence**: "Everything your customers actually say about [category] — 50+ verbatim phrases organized by frustration, desire, and objection, angle-ready. $350, 5 days."
- **Signal test**: conditional-frame post + DM question: "Considering productizing my research layer — the raw customer-language bank I build angles from. If I opened 3 slots, want one?"
- **Tie-back**: productizes the credential itself; feeds Angle Maps (it IS Angle Map phase one); AI stack does the mining at margin.
- **Gate**: GO = 2 prepaid in 21 days · KILL = strong interest, zero prepay (classic "sounds valuable" trap).

### Slot T3 — The Creative Pod (retainer, $1,500/mo entry)
- **Hypothesis**: a pilot buyer who got a winning pack wants continuous variants (8-12 statics/mo + monthly angle refresh) more than one-off packs.
- **Signal test**: NOT tested cold. Named in every Control-Beater delivery email: "Pilot clients get first claim on monthly pod slots when I open them." Count who asks.
- **Tie-back**: the ladder's missing recurring rung; only meaningful post-pilot.
- **Gate**: GO = 1 of first 3 pilot clients asks unprompted or converts on the delivery-email line · KILL = zero pull after 5 deliveries → stay project-based.

### Slot T4 — The Retail-Ready Pack (sell sheet + retail pitch one-pager, $750)
- **Hypothesis**: brands chasing retail placement (a named occasion in the ICP) need buyer-facing creative — sell sheet, line-review one-pager — and nobody creative-side speaks both shopper and compliance.
- **Signal test**: one question in fit calls with retail-occasion brands: "Is the retail buyer meeting creative handled, or is that a gap?" Count gaps.
- **Gate**: GO = 2 "that's a gap" + 1 deposit in 30 days · KILL = retail occasions keep converting to ad creative instead.

### Slot T5 — The Ad Rejection Risk Score as PAID audit ($97)
- **Hypothesis**: the free assessment (launch system Phase 2) can carry a paid tier — scored audit of their live ad account's claim exposure.
- **Signal test**: only after the free assessment is live and completing ≥20/week. A/B the results page: qualified scorers see "$97 full-account claim audit."
- **Gate**: GO = 5% of completers buy · KILL = completions convert straight to fit calls anyway (then the paid tier just adds friction — kill it happily).

## PARKED (fail tie-back today — revisit only on named triggers)
- **Founder-brand content/ghostwriting for supplement founders** — same industry, different job; reopens the operator-trap. Trigger to revisit: 3+ inbound asks.
- **AI Brain installs** — quiet lane, off-umbrella, inbound only (unchanged).
- **S&C-coach ghostwriting vertical** — reactivate only if the supplement umbrella fails its 150-DM falsifier.
- **Courses/templates/info products of any kind** — nothing is teachable until it's been done for paying clients (the receipts rule).

## Sequencing (rides the launch-system calendar)
- **Weeks 1-3 (runway + anticipation)**: T1 live in DM scripts from day one (it needs zero assets). T2 conditional-frame post in week 2.
- **Weeks 4-6 (launch cycle 1)**: T3 line goes in every delivery email. T4 question in every retail-occasion fit call.
- **Week 7+**: T5 only if the assessment is live and flowing. Re-read the whole map; kill or promote on gates, announce kills as posts.
- **Data capture**: every test conversation logs the Big 5 equivalents (situation / occasion / obstacle / budget signal / verbatim objection) into the objection log — that log is the input to the next map revision, not another positioning document.

## The falsifier that governs the whole map
If ~150 warm DMs return "we know our message, we need volume" → the market wants production, not strategy: promote T3-style volume offers, demote Angle Map. If they return "we can't get ads approved" → promote T1/T5 (Review Wedge wins). The DM replies ARE the market research. Priestley's method, running on your actual pipeline.
