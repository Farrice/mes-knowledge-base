## Stage 0 — Frame

- **Surface**: Ad creative / social hero — static, poster-grade. Runs as the primary Meta ad, the Email-1 header, the IG launch post, and the Shopify collection hero.
- **One-sentence brief**: *A launch hero that makes the between-raves raver feel their body never reset — so they recognize themselves in a neon feed and buy the shirt that proves it.*

## Stage 1 — Communication (WF-15)

- **Communication problem**: The rave-merch feed is wall-to-wall neon hype. In one thumbnail, this drop must read as the opposite — proof-of-presence for someone who *lived* it, not a costume for someone who thinks raves look cool. *Stated as a problem: "Make the identity-keeper raver stop and feel recognized in under 2 seconds — before they read a single word — in a feed trained to ignore neon."*
- **Target feeling** (walk-away, next-60s): quiet **recognition** — *"someone finally gets that it stayed with me."* Settled, premium, seen. **Not** the hype-spike of "RAVE!!!" (predictive-empathy: the audience isn't at the party right now; they're scrolling at night, missing it).
- **Viewing context**: ~90% mobile, in-feed, thumb-scrolling, often at night with brightness down (`01-ground.md`: "phone at night, half tired"). Recognition ladder:
  - **10 m** (thumbnail / scroll-by): a single calm **electric-blue heartbeat** on **warm off-white cotton** — instantly "not neon."
  - **5 m** (pause): the line *"your resting heart rate never went back to normal"* + the MY.BPM Week-1 frame.
  - **1 m** (close read): the BPM number, the three-tee lockup, the `MY.BPM` signature.

## Stage 2 — Concept + Hidden Truth (WF-16)

**Hidden truth (one sentence)**: *The real thing isn't the night you went out — it's that your body never fully came back. Your resting heart rate is still running the set's BPM.*

**Selected concept — "Still Synced" (one big idea, committed):**
A single horizontal **EKG line**. It begins as a **barely-visible, hand-drawn flat resting trace** (tonal ink, almost gone into the cotton), then at one point **ignites into a perfect electric-blue kick pattern** and holds. The line *is* the hero. The flat part is the "normal life"; the ignition is the body remembering the warehouse — and never going back.

- **Technique stack**: T1 Hidden Truth × T7 Emotion-over-Information (the flatline→alive reveal makes the truth *felt*, not stated) × T2 Commit (the pulse motif already threads all three tees — pushing it to "the resting line is nearly invisible; only the BPM is electric" takes the one idea to its limit).
- **The "further" move**: printing the resting-flat segment in near-invisible tonal ink so the eye is pulled to the exact **sync point** where life ignites — which is also where the CTA/product lives. The composition literally performs "the body coming alive at the BPM."

**Rejected directions** (proof the winner was chosen, not defaulted):
- *B — "Split-Screen"* (`SUN 4:00 AM ↔ MON 9:00 AM`, one unbroken pulse beneath both): strongest runner-up; clever, but reads clever-before-felt at drop level. → *Reserve as the dedicated ad for the Sunday/Monday tee.*
- *C — "Three Sync Points"* (the only color across the hero is three tiny blue sync marks, one per tee): beautiful restraint, but too quiet to win a cold Meta stop in <2 s.

## Stage 3 — Hierarchy / Leverage Map (WF-01 / GP-06)

- **Leverage point**: the **sync point** — where the flat line ignites into electric-blue kick. This is the <2-second, 10 m read.
- **Quieted / evicted** (subtraction first): no neon, no gradients, no festival imagery, no trippy texture. Resting-flat line → tonal, demoted. `MY.BPM` signature → 1 m tier (small, lower edge). Background → clean cotton, zero competing texture.
- **Amplified survivor**: the electric-blue kick segment is the **only saturated element** on the frame — isolation + contrast carry it (one blue against warm neutral = maximum pop, minimum ink).
- **Eye journey (1-2-3)**: ① blue sync-point ignition → ② headline *"your resting heart rate never went back to normal"* → ③ three-tee lockup + CTA.

## Stage 4 — Color Tokens (WF-17)

**Competitive-layer call (the strategic bet): STAND OUT.** The rave-merch monopoly is neon + black + trippy. This drop differentiates by *restraint* — natural cotton + a single electric blue + near-black ink. Refusing neon is the in-group credibility signal (`01-ground.md`: generic neon reads "I think raves look cool"; restraint reads "I lived it").

| Role | Hex | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Dominant** (~60%) | `#E9E2D2` natural unbleached cotton | The field / breathing room / premium warmth (not clinical white, not party-black) |
| **Secondary** (~30%) | `#16181D` near-black ink | Headline + micro-copy + the resting-flat line (at low opacity) |
| **Accent** (~10%, ONE zone) | `#007FFF` electric blue | The ignited kick pattern + the CTA underline — nowhere else |
| **Base/neutral** | `#5A5F66` warm graphite | 1 m micro-text only (BPM, signature) |

**WCAG**: `#16181D` on `#E9E2D2` ≈ 14:1 (AAA). Electric blue is used as **graphic/fill and underline only — never as body text** (blue-on-cotton fails small-text AA); the CTA reads in near-black with a blue underline. (Discipline inherited from WF-17: every color has a job; the accent lives in one place or it isn't an accent.)

## Stage 5 — Feeling Spec (WF-19)

Target feeling = *quiet recognition, premium, alive-not-hype.*
- **Type** = geometric sans (Inter / Helvetica Neue), **light–regular** weight, generous tracking, **sentence case** — because the feeling is calm confidence, not a shout. (Bold all-caps hype is the wrong register and is killed here.) Sizes set by the 10/5/1 ladder: pulse graphic largest → headline mid → BPM/signature smallest.
- **Color** = single electric blue on warm neutral confirmed to deliver "seen / calm / alive," not "party."
- **Layout** = asymmetric, heavy negative space (`05-design-handoff.md`: "negative space is the premium feel"). Pulse runs horizontally just above center; headline in lower-left third; three-tee lockup small, lower-right.
- **Surface** = matte, cotton-grain implication (not glossy screen); the resting-flat line carries a subtle hand tremor.

## Stage 6 — Memory Hook (WF-08 / GP-03)

- **Move**: conceptual metaphor + controlled imbalance. The resting segment reads as a **medical flatline** ("normal = flat = dead") that **ignites into a living BPM**. The viewer resolves it: *"the normal life is the flatline; the rave is where I came alive — and it stayed."* That half-second of resolution **is** the memory being formed. It whispers a question (why is the flat part barely there?) rather than handing over the answer.

## Stage 7 — Anti-Slop Moves (WF-09 / GP-11)

1. **Hand-drawn resting trace** — the flat line has a subtle tremor, not a perfect vector rule (at the leverage point; rent: "human heartbeat before the machine BPM").
2. **Uneven kick peaks** — the blue QRS spikes vary 2–4% in height, not mechanically identical (secondary zone; rent: a real body's BPM isn't a perfect waveform; escapes AI symmetry).
3. **Asymmetric tee crop** — one of the three tees bleeds slightly off the lower-right edge (element creep; rent: signals "a drop, more coming," not a single product).
4. **Negative-space asymmetry** — more air on the right than left (texture level; rent: premium restraint + pulls the eye left to the headline).

## Stage 8 — Perception Gaps (WF-18) — *resolved back into the brief*

- **Gap (10 m)**: at tiny thumbnail the tonal resting line may vanish, leaving only the blue kick → risk it reads as a generic **equalizer/soundwave** (a rave cliché — the exact thing we're avoiding). **→ Resolved**: brief the generator to render unmistakable **EKG/heartbeat morphology (sharp QRS spikes), never evenly-spaced audio bars.** The medical cue is the differentiator (folded into Stage 2 concept + the generation prompt).
- **Gap (5 m)**: "resting heart rate never went back to normal" could read as **fitness/health** out of rave context. **→ Resolved**: lock the headline tight to the three-tee lockup + `MY.BPM · WEEK 1` so the streetwear/rave frame is present *inside* the 5 m read, not deferred to 1 m (folded into Stage 3 eye-journey + layout).
- No other material gaps.
