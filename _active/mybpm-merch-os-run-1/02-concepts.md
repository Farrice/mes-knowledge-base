# Phase 1 — CONCEPT SPRINT

12 mirror-only concepts for the Raver as Identity Keeper. All 8 fields per concept.

---

## CONCEPT 1: The Kandi Keeper

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Familiar** | Trading kandi at a rave — universal ritual in rave culture |
| **Twist** | "I still have your kandi" — the emotional keeper of friendships |
| **Identity** | *I'm the kind of person who treasures the friendships I built in the dark* |
| **Emotion layers** | Belonging (we're part of the same world) + Pride (I honor my friendships) + Humor (it's just beads, but it means everything) |
| **Social moment** | Someone at a show sees the shirt, recognizes it, says "YES that's me," finds the wearer's rave friend in the crowd, trades kandi together |
| **Lead line** | "I still have your kandi" |
| **Evergreen** | ✓ Kandi trading is not a trend; it's a 20+ year practice |
| **Mirror/Poster** | Mirror — specific behavior (keeping kandi) that signals identity (loyal, emotionally aware, values connection) |
