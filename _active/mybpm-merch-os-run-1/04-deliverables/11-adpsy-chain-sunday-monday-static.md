### Ranked tactics
**1. Lean Into the Taboo / Mirroring (Tactic 1) — SELECTED.** Mechanism: the customer's innermost unsaid truth, said safely. The unsaid thing isn't scandal — it's the **double life**: a professional adult whose realest self exists at 4am and who tells no one at the office. Seed rooted in monologue #1. Predicted comments: "the Monday standup line is too real," tagging rave friends, "who else back at their desk rn," quiet saves (shame-adjacent categories save more than they tag). Failure mode to dodge: substance references (the lazy "rave taboo") — brand-banned and Meta-unsafe.
**2. Visual Psychology (Tactic 5) — LAYERED IN.** The split-screen is an us-vs-them of the *self* — comparison leveling turned inward; one unbroken pulse line is the visual value prop (continuity of identity), readable before any copy.

## Step 2 — Taboo/Mirroring Build (`/adpsy-taboo-ad`)

**The found taboo**: living a double life and being *proud* of it — "I'm a different person at 4am Sunday than at 9am Monday, and the 4am one is realer."
**One level deeper**: not partying — **continuity of identity**. The rave self doesn't switch off; it idles under the work shirt. (Oren's yoga-pants rule: it's never about the surface thing.)

### Veto log
- **KILLED** anything substance-adjacent ("rolling into Monday," "still recovering") — fails brand constraint (PLUR-violating implication), fails Meta policy, and is the young-strategist's edgy pick, not the identity-keeper's actual register. This is the URO-class kill.
- **KILLED** "Rave mode: activated" register — poster, not mirror (ground doc pre-kill list).
- **KILLED** "My boss has no idea what I do on weekends" — reads smirky/gotcha; the identity-keeper isn't hiding in shame, they're keeping something sacred. Register is quiet pride, not smirk.
- **KEPT**: "Nobody at Monday standup knows where I was at 4:00 AM Sunday." Survives the lived-experience test: it's the dawn-moment + re-entry disorientation the community actually describes, in sentence-case adult register. FLAG: sanity-check with one actual raver pre-spend (secondhand-register rule).

### Comment design
- Prompt (ad caption close): "tell me you know both versions of this."
- Predicted thread: "standup at 9, still hearing the kick," friends tagged with no caption, "the pulse doesn't flatline" — plus saves as the primary silent metric. Track saves alongside hook rate.

## Step 3 — Static Ad Spec (`/dara-static-engine`, 3-layer)

### Layer 1 — Strategy
- **Goal (one)**: first purchase of the **Sunday/Monday tee** (cold traffic → PDP).
- **Persona (stage + objection)**: The identity-keeper raver scrolling at night — "rave merch is a neon costume; I can't wear that to my actual life, and anything that says 'RAVE' out loud isn't me."
- **Awareness level**: problem-aware (feels the merch gap; doesn't know restraint-merch exists).
- **Format**: **Headliner** — chosen because the objection is "sell me *me*, not the party" — an identity/feeling sale, not a proof sale. (Wandering Bear "SO GOOD IT SHOULD BE BAD FOR YOU" lineage: the message IS the focal point.)
- **Exemplar mirrored**: Headliner (Wandering Bear / Happy Tuesdays) → borrowing message-as-focal-point; split-screen visual carries Tactic-5 self-comparison underneath.

### Layer 2 — Design (hierarchy, top → bottom)
1. **Headline** (focal point, leads): "Nobody at Monday standup knows where I was at 4:00 AM Sunday." — sentence case, near-black ink, top band.
2. **Key visual**: split field — left half deep near-black (`#16181D`, "SUN 4:00 AM"), right half natural cotton (`#E9E2D2`, "MON 9:00 AM") — with **one unbroken electric-blue pulse line (`#007FFF`) crossing both halves**, kick-pattern on the Sunday side, calm-but-alive on the Monday side. Never flatlining is the point.
3. **Supporting**: the Sunday/Monday tee flat-lay small at lower right (product must be nameable at a glance); `MY.BPM` signature lower left; CTA "Shop the Week-1 drop" near-black with blue underline.
- **Contrast + type**: inherited tokens; geometric sans, light-regular, sentence case (hero brief Stage 5 register: calm confidence, not shout). One accent zone only (the pulse + CTA underline).
- **1-second test**: stranger names "a t-shirt for people who rave" — the tee flat-lay + MY.BPM mark carry the *what*; the split + headline carry the *who*. → PASS (verify on render).
- **Differentiator at a glance**: the non-neon restraint IS the differentiator, visible before reading.

### Layer 3 — Copy
- **Headline**: "Nobody at Monday standup knows where I was at 4:00 AM Sunday."
- **Sub line** (5m read, small): "Your pulse kept the secret. The tee keeps it quieter."
- **CTA**: "Shop the Week-1 drop"
- **Mechanics used (2)**: Lean into the taboo (#3 — the unsaid double life) + tap a primal desire (#4 — belonging/identity; the desire is being *known without announcing*). Omission pass applied: no "PLUR," no "rave" spelled out — insiders don't need it; that's the self-selection.

## Step 4 — Comprehension Audit (`/dara-comprehension-audit`)
