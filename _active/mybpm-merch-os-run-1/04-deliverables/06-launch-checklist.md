## Pre-Committed Kill/Scale Rules (Operational Gates)

### Kill Rules (Stop the Test Immediately)

- **Technical failure:** Checkout flow broken → fix and re-launch (not a kill, just delay)
- **Revenue miss (72-hour mark):** If any SKU hasn't hit $50 in 3 days → pause ads (if any), audit messaging
- **Add-to-cart < 3%:** Indicates copy isn't landing → rewrite and re-test with fresh segment
- **Checkout abandonment > 45%:** Indicates price objection or shipping concern → lower price to $40, re-test

### Scale Rules (Double Down)

- **Email open rate ≥30%:** Emotion-first hypothesis confirmed → use similar subject-line framing for future announces
- **Email CTR ≥12%:** Copy is resonating → allocate $100 paid ad budget to cold traffic (next phase)
- **First-week revenue ≥$500 per SKU:** All three concepts are strong → prepare Capsule 1 Wave 2 (remaining 5 concepts)
- **AOV > $90:** Pairing strategy working (customers buying multiple SKUs) → feature "Complete the Moment" more prominently
- **Social mentions ≥5:** Organic reach confirmed → lean into user-generated content (re-post tags, feature customers)

### Revise Rules (Tweak, Don't Kill)

- **Open rate 18–23%:** Emotion-first is solid but needs context → add subject-line variant with storytelling depth
- **Revenue per SKU $75–150:** Concepts are strong, messaging may need lift → revise PDP copy (more emotional lead, less logic)
- **Checkout abandonment 35–45%:** Price sensitivity exists but not critical → test $40 variant on next wave
- **Social mentions 1–3:** Organic reach exists but low → create TikTok video of the moment (Sunday 4AM coworker recognition)

## Post-Launch Next Steps

### If Scale Rules Triggered (All Green)
→ Move to Phase 6 (DIAGNOSE) and plan Capsule 1 Wave 2 (remaining 5 concepts + Tier 2 revisions)

### If Revise Rules Triggered (Some Yellow)
→ Make one PDP copy revision per underperforming SKU, re-test with new email segment (holdout 20% from announce)

### If Kill Rules Triggered (Any Red)
→ Audit: Which metric is the blocker? Price? Copy clarity? Design? Implement one fix, re-test on cold traffic (small budget, $50 cap)
