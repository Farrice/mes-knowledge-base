# MY.BPM, Sunday/Monday Tee · Shoot-Ready Creator Script (Yapper, Mirroring Register)

> Vessel for the `/adpsy-taboo-ad` concept in `11-adpsy-chain-sunday-monday-static.md`. Yapper discipline per Dara: **beat structure, not locked dialogue**, the creator speaks their own words inside each beat. Mirroring rule per Oren: "does it seem like someone's talking to a friend versus doing a whole scripted bit?"

## Casting + set

- **Creator**: an actual raver, 25-35, who holds a normal job, the double life must be biographically true or the mirror breaks (lived-experience rule). NOT an aspirational model.
- **Set**: their real weekday morning, kitchen counter with coffee, or car before work, business-casual top half. Phone-shot, natural light. The mundane set IS the joke and the credibility (set-as-credibility inverted: normalcy is the costume here).
- **Wardrobe**: the Sunday/Monday tee under an open button-up or work-appropriate layer, revealed mid-video.

## Beat structure (30-45s)

**BEAT 1, Hook (0-3s), deadpan to camera in work clothes:**
The double-life confession, their words. Reference line (adapt, don't recite): *"I have a 9am standup in 20 minutes. Nobody there knows where I was at 4am on Sunday."*
- Text overlay layer: "POV: it's Monday and you're a completely different person" (native font, sentence case).

**BEAT 2, The unsaid thing, said safely (3-12s):**
What actually happens at 4am, but in identity terms, never party-forensics terms. Their version of: strangers who became family, the set that rewired them, walking out at dawn changed. Guardrail: **zero substance references** (brand + platform hard line, see veto log in doc 11); the register is quiet pride, not smirk, not confession-booth shame.

**BEAT 3, The mirror turn (12-20s):**
Flip from "me" to "us": their version of *"and if you know, you know, your body doesn't reset either. The pulse just idles all week."* This is the belonging beat, the viewer should feel found, not sold.

**BEAT 4, Product as the quiet proof (20-32s):**
Open the button-up: the tee. Their version of: *"That's the whole point of this shirt. It doesn't say rave. It doesn't glow. The people who know, know."* Camera pushes to the chest pulse print for ~1.5s (the 1-second product read lives here).

**BEAT 5, Close + comment prompt (32-45s):**
Back to deadpan, grabbing keys/laptop: *"Anyway. Standup in 10."* Beat. *"Tell me you know both versions of this."*
- End card: split SUN 4:00 AM / MON 9:00 AM frame from the static (asset reuse), CTA "Shop the Week-1 drop."

## Creator brief (one paragraph, send as-is)

"This isn't an ad read, it's you telling a friend the funny-true thing about your double life. Monday morning, work clothes, real kitchen/car. Say the 4am thing the way you'd actually say it (no substances on camera or implied, hard line). The shirt appears once, halfway, like a secret handshake, not a pitch. Don't perform hype; the whole vibe is 'calm person with a second heartbeat.' End by asking them to admit they're the same."

## Comment + measurement design

- Engagement anchor: the confession invites "same" testimony, replies from the brand account ask "what was the set?" (community payoff, credible-explainer access loop lite).
- Watch: saves (primary, identity content saves > tags), hook rate, hold at Beat 4 reveal. Day 7-14: `/adpsy-comment-mine`, first real customer voice for MyBPM; feed the virtue map into ad round 2.

## Quality gate

Mirroring test passed only if a cold identity-keeper viewer would say "this is me," not "nice ad." Kill takes where the creator performs (scripted-bit energy), any substance implication, any neon/festival b-roll (poster drift). Adpsy rubric: voice authenticity gated on the creator being an actual double-life raver.
