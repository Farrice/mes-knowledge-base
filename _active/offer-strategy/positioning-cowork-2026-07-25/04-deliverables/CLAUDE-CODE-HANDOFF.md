## SECTION 3 — THE CORE METHODOLOGY (Diandra Escobar's system, compressed)

### The foundational inversion
See LinkedIn **from the buyer's side, not the poster's side.** Judge every asset by one question: *where in the stranger's journey does this sit, and what is its single job here?*

### The six-stage journey (the spine of everything)
1. **Comment** — first touch. You enter rooms that already have your buyers in them.
2. **Profile** — the click. *Content is the ad; the profile is the landing page.* Most operators run ads to a broken landing page.
3. **Feed** — the algorithm walks relevant content to non-followers. Enter conversations already happening rather than starting cold ones.
4. **Hook** — two audiences, always: the human, and the **50-word algorithm audition** that decides distribution.
5. **Funnel** — the **40 / 30 / 20 / 10** mix: 40% growth, 30% authority, 20% conversion, 10% personal. No two posts do the same job.
6. **DM** — **trust has a temperature.** Reply before it cools. Harvest warm signals (repeat commenters, savers, ICP profile-viewers) rather than cold-outbounding strangers.

### The twelve decoded genius patterns
1. **Buyer's-journey inversion** — design backward from the stranger's path, not forward from your content calendar.
2. **Currency reframing** — expertise and attention are two *different* currencies. Most experts are rich in one and bankrupt in the other. This system mints attention *from* expertise.
3. **Attention arbitrage** — don't manufacture attention; redirect attention that already exists. The five formats: **brand jacking, newsjacking, memejacking, hot takes, trend writing.**
4. **Percentile-native strategy** — the 2026 unified-LLM algorithm scores you against *your own* percentile band, with ~30× better relevance matching and a structural lift for small accounts. Engagement *quality* moves percentiles; pods and raw counts don't.
5. **Landing-page mental model** — the profile is a conversion asset with a job, not a résumé.
6. **Reverse-order composition** — write the payoff first, then engineer the hook that earns it. Hooks written first are almost always weaker.
7. **Proof-stacking** — specificity *is* credibility. Every claim ships with a receipt. Vanity metrics are filtered out; a believability governor keeps claims inside what the reader can accept.
8. **POV as moat** — a contrarian, defensible point of view is the one thing AI can't generate and competitors can't honestly copy.
9. **Warm-signal harvesting** — engagement is a temperature reading. Act on it while it's hot.
10. **Temperature / decay awareness** — every trend and every warm lead has a ride window. Late is worthless.
11. **Reciprocity engineering** — give first, publicly, at scale. Comment infiltration is reciprocity at volume.
12. **Category creation by naming** — name the problem, own the solution. One coined name is a renewable content engine for months.

### The three types (positioning coordinates)
- **SME** — deep expertise, no distribution. Invisible.
- **Creator** — great distribution, an inch deep. Forgettable.
- **Thought leader** — depth *plus* distribution, anchored to a named category. **This is the target position.**

### The four hook formats
Dense · Punchy + context · Single-line bomb · Stacked. Every post's first 50 words must survive the **algorithm audition** before it ships.
