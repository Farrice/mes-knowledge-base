# The 15-Ad Creative Test Pack

## Seven-day performance creative for DTC brands that need something better to test next

## Offer state

This is a **founding-pilot hypothesis**, not a validated offer. Current market evidence supports demand for performance creative, static assets, scripts, briefs, and structured testing. It does not prove that buyers will purchase this exact package from Farrice at this price.

## Who this is for

This is designed for a DTC health, supplement, wellness, recovery, beauty, fitness, or performance-product brand that:

- Is actively running Meta ads now
- Can share recent creative-level data or at minimum its five highest-spend and five weakest ads
- Has one product or SKU it wants to push
- Has a live product page and usable brand/product assets
- Has an internal marketer, founder, agency, or media buyer who can launch the finished creative
- Is willing to test distinct messages rather than request cosmetic variations of one existing ad
- Can provide approved product claims and the evidence or legal language those claims require

### This is not for

- Pre-launch brands with no customer or performance signal
- Founders seeking a full paid-media operator
- Brands that need creator casting, filming, or video production in the first engagement
- Teams unable to share any performance data
- Buyers requesting guaranteed ROAS, CPA, revenue, or “winning ads”
- Brands expecting unlimited concepts, unlimited revisions, or same-day production
- Anyone asking Farrice to invent or strengthen unsubstantiated health claims

## The problem

Many brands have an asset pipeline but not a learning pipeline. They produce more creative without changing the audience, message, funnel job, or hypothesis. The result is volume that teaches the account very little.

The buyer does not need another brainstorm document. The buyer needs:

- A clear diagnosis of what the existing account overuses and under-tests
- Distinct creative bets tied to actual customer language and account evidence
- Finished assets its media buyer can launch
- A testing map that makes the results interpretable
- A next-step readout that turns performance into the next creative decision

No generic cost-of-inaction figure is claimed here. The relevant cost must be established from the buyer's own ad spend, production spend, launch cadence, and amount of creative discarded without learning.

## The transformation

In seven business days, the brand moves from:

> “We need more creative, but we are not sure what should be different.”

to:

> “We have five materially different creative hypotheses, 15 finished ads ready to launch, and a defined way to read the test.”

The service improves the quality and clarity of the creative test. It does **not** guarantee that any concept will beat the control.

## What the buyer receives

### 1. Creative Performance Read

A concise diagnosis of the supplied account data:

- Current winner and loser patterns
- Repeated messages, formats, personas, and funnel jobs
- Likely creative-fatigue or coverage gaps
- Unknowns that cannot be resolved from the supplied data

### 2. Customer and Category Signal File

Research drawn from authorized and public sources:

- Customer reviews and comments
- Brand-owned customer material supplied by the client
- Competitor ads and product pages
- Category objections, rituals, desired outcomes, and trust signals

Every customer-style phrase is labeled as supplied, observed, inferred, or unavailable. No synthetic testimonial is presented as customer language.

### 3. Five Creative Bets

Each bet includes:

- Target audience or situation
- Awareness level and funnel job
- Message hypothesis
- Reason the account needs this test
- Format and first-frame direction
- Required proof or claim boundary
- The single variable the test is intended to teach

### 4. Fifteen Finished Static Ads

- Five core concepts
- Three meaningful headline, proof, or framing variants per concept
- 15 ready-to-run master ads
- Exports in the agreed Meta placements using the same master composition
- Final copy embedded in the creative
- Organized filenames that map each asset to its hypothesis

“Meaningful variant” means the message or persuasion variable changes. Merely resizing one image does not count as a new variant.

### 5. Launch and Learning Map

- Recommended launch order
- Control and variable for each test
- Suggested grouping so the media buyer can preserve interpretability
- Signals to observe: spend, impressions, thumb-stop or hook signal where applicable, CTR, CVR, CPA, and qualitative comment response
- No universal benchmark is imposed without the buyer's own baseline

### 6. One Post-Launch Readout

One 30-minute review held after the brand has run the ads long enough to produce usable directional data, or an asynchronous written readout if preferred. The readout identifies:

- What the test can and cannot support
- Which message deserves iteration
- Which concepts should be stopped
- The next three creative decisions

The readout must occur within 30 days of delivery. Media buying and campaign optimization are not included.

## Timeline

### Before Day 1

- Invoice paid
- Intake complete
- Data and assets received
- One SKU selected
- Claims and required disclaimers supplied

### Days 1–2: Evidence and account read

- Review creative-level performance
- Map current account coverage
- Mine customer and competitor signals
- Identify the primary test gap

### Day 3: Test slate

- Build five creative hypotheses
- Confirm any blocking claim, asset, or audience questions
- Choose the five concepts without asking the client to perform strategy

### Days 4–6: Asset production

- Write copy and first-frame direction
- Build 15 master ads
- Run visual, message, evidence, and placement checks

### Day 7: Delivery

- Deliver organized assets and test map
- Client receives one consolidated revision request window

### Within 30 days: learning readout

- Review available results
- Deliver the next-decision memo

## What the buyer is really paying for

The buyer is not paying for 15 Canva files. The buyer is paying for a bounded learning system:

- Account evidence translated into creative decisions
- Customer language translated into testable messages
- Strategy translated into launchable assets
- Results translated into the next decision

AI may accelerate research organization, variation, image development, and resizing. Farrice remains responsible for source integrity, message selection, final copy, creative direction, and quality control.

## Risk reversal

### The Launch-Ready Clarity Guarantee

If an included asset cannot be launched because its copy is unclear, its agreed placement specification is wrong, or it fails the agreed brand/claim requirements supplied during intake, Farrice will correct it within the original scope.

This guarantee does not cover performance outcomes, platform approval, newly introduced brand rules, new legal advice, a changed product offer, or a request to replace the approved test direction.

## Natural capacity constraint

One active client at a time during validation. This is not manufactured scarcity. It protects the seven-day turnaround and gives Farrice enough time to research, write, build, and quality-check the assets personally.

## Positioning against alternatives

### Versus a pure strategy audit

The buyer leaves with assets to launch, not only recommendations.

### Versus a generic design subscription

The work begins with account evidence and a learnable test, not a request queue.

### Versus a full UGC agency

The pilot avoids creator sourcing, contracts, shipping logistics, filming, and usage-rights management. It is cheaper and operationally narrower, but it does not provide human creator footage.

### Versus AI-only bulk creative

The differentiator is not access to generation tools. It is customer evidence, concept selection, copy judgment, claim discipline, and an explicit learning loop.

## Success and kill conditions

The offer is not validated by compliments, views, local quality scores, or a polished sample.

- **First proof:** one paid pilot and collected payment
- **Stronger proof:** three paid pilots with at least two launching the assets
- **Renewal proof:** one repeat purchase without a discount or one explicit request for a recurring engagement
- **Kill/rework trigger:** 20 qualified exposures with zero buying conversations, or three deliveries that buyers consistently value as cheap design production rather than strategy-led testing. [MODELED gate]
