## The decision

# The 15-Ad Creative Test Pack

> Five distinct creative bets, turned into 15 finished Meta static ads and a launch-ready testing map in seven business days.

The buyer is an established health, wellness, supplement, recovery, beauty, or performance-product brand already running paid social. The buyer must have live creative, recent creative-level performance data, and someone who can launch the ads.

This replaces a standalone strategy document as the market-facing product. Research, angle selection, and message architecture remain inside the service, but the invoice is attached to test-ready assets.

## Why this shape

- Current employers and freelance buyers repeatedly request performance analysis, concepts, hooks, briefs, finished assets, structured testing, and iteration as one loop.

- **Price:** $1,000 prepaid founding pilot [MODELED]

5. Propose recurring work when a buyer asks for another batch or two clients buy a second pack. [MODELED renewal gate]
