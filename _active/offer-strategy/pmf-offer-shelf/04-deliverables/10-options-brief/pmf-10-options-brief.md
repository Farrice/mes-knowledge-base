**Delivery:** 6–8 hrs/audit on the current template. Pilot $500 (3-slot founder-conditioned, testimonial + case-study rights required); standard $1,500. Two audits/mo = 12–16 hrs = well inside ceiling.

**Entry / Scale:** $500 → $1,500 → $2,500 (as case studies land, path to Veza's $4,500 with named vertical proof).

**Defensibility:** The 18-yr NASM credential + the two-gate framing (visibility AND compliance in one read) is the moat. Cursor + Claude cannot solo-build the credential, and the general AEO audit market has no compliance leg. **Score 6** because the audit *format* is copyable; the *authority* isn't.

The receipt sweep confirms this is the standard entry rung. Standing price is $1,500, not $500 — the pilot is a conditioned instrument, not a permanent floor.

**Recommended stack:**

- **Lane 1 (primary): Options 1 → 2 → 3 as one ladder.** This is Path A's canonical motion. 55–70 delivery hrs/mo at target capacity.
