## The Three Amendments (receipts-driven; zero new documents)

**1. Reorder the pitch, same offer.** Lead: *"Claim-safe content that lowers your blended CAC and gets your brand cited by ChatGPT and AI Overviews — without FTC exposure."* CAC and compliance are the funded pains; AI-citation is the novelty hook and premium justifier. This is a sentence-order change in outreach and profile copy already written — permitted under the freeze (deployment, not redesign).

**2. Upgrade the Marcus probe to co-primary instrument.** The council placed one B-probe post; the receipts say founder-authority is the best-funded line in the entire study. Amendment: the probe post ships week one *as ruled*, AND the 3-5 ICP interviews (already in the protocol, days 1–5) add two questions: *"What do you currently spend on content/marketing help?"* and *"Who writes your founder's LinkedIn — and what would one attributed client be worth?"* This closes the study's two UNCONFIRMED gaps (SMB supplement budget line; fitness-operator content spend) inside the existing protocol. Wallet-segmented DM logging stays as ruled.

**3. Beachhead sub-vertical: sports nutrition / performance first.** 5WPR published a Sports Nutrition AI Visibility Index — proof of category-level buyer curiosity — and it is dead center of the 18-year S&C lane. The free wedge already exists in the repo (`lead-magnet-ai-search-visibility-test.md`, the 7-check test) and mirrors the exact tactic the market is validating. Aim the first teardowns and the 20-brand orbit at sports-nutrition/performance brands before general wellness.
