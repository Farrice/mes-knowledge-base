## WHAT NOT TO SHOW

Luke Iha's placement rule: **curate aggressively.** A proof spread with 12 artifacts looks desperate. A proof spread with 3 artifacts, each one load-bearing, looks confident.

**Artifacts NOT in the spread** (but referenced inside the case study):
- The full Grounding Document (too dense for a proof spread; it's the evidence, not the exhibit)
- The Brand Foundation voice guide (important, not visually striking)
- The intent validation document (internal tool, not proof)
- The brand intelligence dossier (redundant with the grounding doc)
- The Sunday Dinners 4-phase strategy (hinted at in the Grace City Blueprint; full version lives inside the case study)

These all live INSIDE the linked case study. The proof spread is the storefront. The case study is the backroom. Don't put the backroom on the street.
