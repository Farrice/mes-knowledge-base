## 3. MECHANISM BLOCK — The Three Lenses

**Purpose:** Differentiation layer. What do the three expert lenses actually do? This is the unique Parallax mechanism (Farrice's compression advantage — AI running three expert frameworks in parallel).

### H2

> Three lenses. One crystal.

### Three adjacent sub-blocks (card layout)

**Lens 1 — Consumer Posture (Dai Media)**
We don't ask "who's your demographic." We ask how they sit up when someone describes their life. Occupation, activity, thought process — not age, income, title. The result is a reader who *feels* specific, not one who fits a spreadsheet.

**Lens 2 — Deep Canvass (McRaney)**
We map the pain ladder — what they'll pay to solve this month vs. what they feel but can't name yet. Every piece of your publication gets a ladder rung. You stop writing into the void because you know which rung each piece is speaking to.

**Lens 3 — Language Map (Iha / Kallaway)**
We extract the verbatim words your ideal reader uses to describe themselves — and the words that make them flinch. You write in their vocabulary. Paid conversion stops being a mystery.

### Closing line (anchors the mechanism to the category-defining sentence)

> You have the work. We externalize the reader.

## 7. FAQ — pre-empts objections from language-map AVOID-words

**Purpose:** Each question here addresses an objection sourced from the language map and the avatar pain-ladder pre-contemplation rows. Answers use USE-words and avoid AVOID-words.

**Q: Isn't this just another ICP template?**
No. Templates give you blanks to fill in. The Crystal is the filled-in document — specific to your publication, your reader, your language. You don't fill in the blanks. We fill them in with real humans' names.

**Q: I've already paid for positioning help that didn't work. Why would this?**
Because most positioning help is inward-facing: "get clearer on what YOU do." This is outward-facing: "get specific about who's watching." Inward-clarity without outward-specificity is why the last document didn't move your paid conversion.

**Q: I'm not a "creator" or "solopreneur." Is this for me?**
Right — this is built for writers, consultants, and founders. No "creator economy" language anywhere in the work. If you run a publication, a practice, or a mission-driven organization, you're the buyer.

**Q: How is this different from a $497 course?**
A course teaches you the method. Parallax runs the method for you, on your specific work, and hands you the finished artifact. You're not buying education; you're buying the output.

**Q: Why not a $97 template to try the method first?**
Because Avatar 01 (our primary buyer) has already bought 2-3 ICP templates that didn't work. A $97 version of this would be the thing you're already running from. The free 20-min diagnosis IS the low-risk gateway — and it surfaces more than a template would.

**Q: Do I have to be on Substack?**
No. Substack, LinkedIn, Twitter, an independent website, or a traditional client practice — all work. The document is platform-agnostic; the calendars are platform-specific.

**Q: What if I only have 500 subscribers?**
Then Tier 1 might be early. The ICP Tell call will tell you. If the answer is "come back when you have 1,000," you'll hear it free.

**Q: Can I pay in installments?**
Tier 1: pay in full. Tier 2: 50/50 split available. Tier 3: 50/25/25 across kickoff / Day 10 / launch day. All via Stripe.

## CTA LOGIC PER TIER (conversion flow)

Every button on the page routes to the same place: the ICP Tell application form (2 questions, ~60 seconds). Tier selection happens ON the call, not on the page. This is intentional — Avatar 01 and Avatar 02 and Avatar 03 all enter the same door. Farrice triages tier fit live.

**The 2-question form:**
1. What do you do, in one sentence? (free text)
2. What's the specific moment this week that made you look for help? (free text)

The second question is the Trigger Event filter (per avatar pain ladders). A prospect whose answer is "I had a bad Sunday dashboard scroll" is Avatar 01 / Tier 1. A prospect whose answer is "a donor asked me who this is for" is Avatar 03 / Tier 3. Farrice pre-reads answers before the call and tiers the conversation correctly.
