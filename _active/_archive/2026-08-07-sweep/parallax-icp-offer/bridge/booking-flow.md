# Booking Flow — ICP Tell Application → Diagnosis → Close

**Purpose:** Filter tire-kickers before Farrice's calendar. Pre-qualify avatar fit. Ensure every call on the calendar is a prospect Farrice would recommend working with.

**Principle (Tyler Denk):** Every friction point is a qualification filter. A clean 2-question form kills 60% of bad-fit prospects and keeps 100% of good-fit ones.

**Principle (Grace):** The call is not a sales call. It's a diagnosis. The frame is set in the confirmation email before Farrice ever opens Zoom.

---

## Stage 1 — Application Form

**Trigger:** Any CTA button on the landing page, soft-CTA in Parallax emails, or LinkedIn DM link.

**Tool:** Tally (free tier) or Typeform (paid). Form embedded as a modal on the landing page so the reader never leaves. Submissions POST to a Notion database (Content Pipeline or dedicated "ICP Tell Applications" DB) for triage.

**Form title:** *Book the ICP Tell — free 20-minute diagnosis*

**Supporting line under title:**

> *Two questions. Sixty seconds. I read every submission before the call.*

### The two questions (locked — do not expand)

**Question 1:**
> *What do you do, in one sentence?*
>
> *(Free text. No character cap. Write it the way you'd say it to a friend at a dinner party.)*

**Question 2:**
> *What's the specific moment this week that made you look for help?*
>
> *(Free text. This is the question that matters most. Be specific.)*

### Contact capture (third field, not framed as a question)

- Email (required)
- First name (required)
- Optional: link to publication / website / LinkedIn

### Final button copy

**[BUTTON: Send the answers →]**

*Not "Submit." Not "Book call." "Send the answers" reinforces that this is a two-way conversation, not a funnel.*

## Stage 2 — Triage (Farrice's side, async)

**Timing:** Every Monday + Thursday morning. Batched, not continuous. Tyler Denk protocol: no inbox monitoring, no pings.

**Triage rubric:**

| Signal in Q2 answer | Avatar match | Action |
|---|---|---|
| *"bad Sunday dashboard scroll"* / *"post I was proud of got 38 likes"* / *"paid conversion is under 1%"* | Avatar 01 | Book Crystal-tier call |
| *"340 reactions and zero clients"* / *"signal bleeding to people who won't pay"* / *"Sunday dread"* | Avatar 02 | Book Architecture-tier call |
| *"a donor asked who this is for"* / *"we opened [city] and 3 people came"* / *"the pitch requires me to be in the room"* | Avatar 03 | Book Launch-tier call |
| Vague answer / "I want to grow my list" / "marketing help" | Unclear | Send a short qualifying email: *"Before we book — can you tell me the specific moment? A one-line answer is fine."* |
| Hard disqualifier: "I haven't started yet" / "I have 50 subscribers" / "I want to learn how to write" | Early-stage | Send the disqualification email (below) with resources. Do NOT book the call. |

### Disqualification email (template)

> Subject: Re: Parallax ICP Tell — one honest note
>
> Hey [first name],
>
> Thanks for writing. Based on your answer, I'd be doing you a disservice to book the call right now — you're at the stage where the Crystal would arrive too early to land.
>
> Three things that actually move the needle at this stage:
> - Ship 10-15 more posts before worrying about ICP. You need enough body of work that a reader could recognize a pattern.
> - Read [specific resource matched to their situation].
> - Come back in 3-6 months. Reply to this email and we'll book the call then.
>
> This isn't a soft no. It's a real no-for-now. No retargeting. No drip sequence. You're off my list unless you write back.
>
> — Farrice

**Why this email matters:** Tyler Denk's "One-of-One" rule applied to disqualification. A real no, delivered with actual advice, builds more referral momentum than 100 booked-but-wasted calls. The person who gets this email tells three friends. That's the zero-cost referral lever.

## Stage 3 — Pre-Call Confirmation Email

**Triggered:** Immediately when the call is booked (via Calendly/Cal.com integration with the form).

**Purpose:** Reset expectations. The frame is DIAGNOSIS, not pitch.

**Subject:** *See you [day] at [time]. Two things before the call.*

**Body:**

Hey [first name],

The call is on the calendar for [day, date, time with timezone]. 20 minutes. Zoom link below.

Two things to set expectations:

**1. This isn't a sales call.**

I read your answer to "the specific moment this week that made you look for help." I already have a hypothesis about where your reader has gone missing in your work. The first 5 minutes, I'll name it back to you and see if you agree. The next 10, we'll talk about whether Parallax is the fix. The last 5, we'll decide if we're working together — or if you're better served by something else (which I'll tell you honestly).

**2. You don't need to prepare anything.**

No slides. No brief. Just come with the URL of the work you're most proud of — a Substack post, a LinkedIn piece, a homepage, a grant document. The one you'd want a stranger to land on.

That's it. See you [day].

— Farrice

### Why this email works

**Tyler Denk "Post-Subscribe Intelligence Capture" extended:** We're not only capturing the question on the form — we're capturing the URL they'd want a stranger to see. That's a second high-intent data point. Farrice pre-reads it before the call.

**Frame reset:** "The first 5 minutes, I'll name it back to you" — this removes ambiguity about what the call is. The prospect knows exactly what's happening. No one is performing a sales role they didn't agree to play.

**Disqualification included:** "Or if you're better served by something else (which I'll tell you honestly)" — same move as the disqualification email. Trust signal embedded in the pre-call.

## Stage 4 — The Call (On-Call Structure)

**Total duration:** 20 minutes. Hard stop. Set on the Calendly event.

**Minute-by-minute structure:**

### Minutes 0-5 — The Diagnosis

Farrice opens with the question from the case study / final CTA:

> *"If a stranger landed on your homepage for five seconds, what would they say you do?"*

Whatever answer the prospect gives, Farrice's next move is:

> *"Right. And here's what I actually see on your homepage [or Substack, or LinkedIn]: [specific observation]. That gap — between the answer you just gave and the impression your work actually leaves — is where your reader is getting lost."*

One concrete micro-rewrite follows. A headline, a hero line, an About-section rewrite. Live. 90 seconds of work.

**The demo IS the product.** By minute 5, the prospect has received value — a live rewrite of their own work. This is the proof that closes the sale.

### Minutes 5-15 — The Fit Conversation

Farrice names the tier match based on what the demo revealed and the prospect's pre-call answers:

> *"What I just did is 1% of what the Crystal does across your full publication. Based on what you told me and what I see, the tier fit is [Crystal / Architecture / Launch]. Here's why."*

Then the tier-matched stakes script from `pricing-psychology.md`:

- Avatar 01 → math-the-payback-window script
- Avatar 02 → the-cost-of-wrong-ICP-content script
- Avatar 03 → the-grant-ratio script

Then: silence. Let the prospect ask questions.

### Minutes 15-20 — The Decision

Farrice closes with one of three outcomes, stated plainly:

1. **"Let's do it."** → Farrice sends the payment link + intake form in the chat. Calendar blocked for kickoff. Deal closed on the call. (Stripe invoice goes out within 5 minutes of the call ending.)

2. **"I need to think about it."** → *"Fair. Here's the one thing I'll ask: what specifically needs to be true for you to say yes? If you can tell me that, I'll send you whatever you need to answer it — not a sales email, the actual answer. Deal?"* Farrice logs the blocker in Notion. One follow-up email at 48 hours with the specific answer (not a generic "circling back").

3. **"This isn't the right fit."** → *"Got it. Totally fine. Who do you know who this would be right for? I'll take one warm intro as the value of this call."* This is the zero-cost referral lever activation. 30% of no-fit calls produce a warm intro.

## Stage 5 — Post-Call Follow-Up (48-Hour Close Window)

**If outcome was "Let's do it":**
- Hour 0: Stripe invoice sent. Intake form emailed (2 questions: "what's the one homepage/About/landing page we're rebuilding?" + "who are three readers or clients who said YES to your work — include their full name, what they do, and one quote from them").
- Hour 24: Kickoff call confirmation email with the intake form completed. "See you [day] for kickoff."

**If outcome was "I need to think":**
- Hour 48: The specific-answer email. Not a generic follow-up. Addresses the exact blocker named on the call. One email. No sequence.
- If no reply after 7 days: ONE final email. *"Want to close the loop — are we a no-for-now or just a not-yet?"* Then off the list.

**If outcome was "Not the fit":**
- Hour 0: Thank-you email. One line. *"Thanks for the 20 minutes. If someone comes to mind, just hit reply with their email or LinkedIn."*
- No further contact unless they reply.

## Stage 6 — No-Show Protocol

**If prospect doesn't join at the scheduled time:**

- 5 minutes past: Farrice stays in the room.
- 10 minutes past: Farrice sends one message in chat: *"Hey — are you stuck? I'll hold for 5 more minutes."*
- 15 minutes past: Farrice leaves the room. Sends one email:
  > *Subject: Missed you today*
  >
  > *No worries — stuff happens. If you want to reschedule, here's the link: [Calendly]. If the moment's passed, no pressure. — Farrice*
- If no reschedule within 7 days: Prospect is off the list. No re-engagement sequence.

**Rationale:** One no-show rescheduling allowed. Second no-show = prospect is signaling they're not ready. Honoring that signal preserves Farrice's calendar and reputation.

## Stage 7 — Calendar Configuration Spec

**Tool:** Cal.com (open-source, free) OR Calendly (paid tier $12/mo — use if the form integration matters).

**Event settings:**

- **Duration:** 20 minutes. Hard cap.
- **Buffer before:** 10 minutes (pre-read the form answer).
- **Buffer after:** 15 minutes (write the Notion log entry while memory is fresh).
- **Availability window:** Tues + Thurs + Fri afternoons only. No Monday mornings (protected deep work). No weekends. Max 6 calls per week.
- **Advance notice required:** 24 hours minimum.
- **Cancellation policy:** Can cancel up to 2 hours before. Auto-reschedule offered.
- **Reminders:** 24-hour email + 1-hour email. Both from Farrice's address, written in his voice.

**Integration chain:**
1. Form submission (Tally/Typeform) → Notion DB entry (ICP Tell Applications).
2. Farrice triages in Notion, approves → triggers Calendly invite.
3. Calendly booking → confirmation email auto-fires (Stage 3).
4. Post-call → Farrice logs outcome in Notion (same DB, status field).

**Notion DB fields (ICP Tell Applications):**
- Name
- Email
- Q1 answer (their one-sentence)
- Q2 answer (the specific moment)
- Work URL (from pre-call)
- Avatar match (01 / 02 / 03 / unclear)
- Tier recommendation (Crystal / Architecture / Launch / disqualified)
- Call status (booked / completed / no-show / disqualified)
- Outcome (closed / thinking / no-fit)
- Deal value (if closed)
- Source (landing page / Edition 01 PS / Edition 05 PS / LinkedIn / other)
- Notes (raw from call)

The source field is the analytics layer. Tells Farrice which channels produce closed deals vs. which produce tire-kickers. Feeds the next quarter's Trust Pathway map.

## Quality Metrics to Track (monthly)

| Metric | Target | Action if missed |
|---|---|---|
| Form submissions → calls booked | 70%+ | If <70%, Q2 answers are too vague. Tighten the form copy. |
| Calls booked → calls attended | 85%+ | If <85%, confirmation email isn't working. Rewrite. |
| Calls attended → closed deals | 40%+ | If <40%, the diagnosis portion isn't delivering value fast enough. Tighten on-call structure. |
| Closed deals → delivered without rework | 85%+ | If <85%, intake form isn't capturing enough context. Add fields. |
| Warm intros per no-fit call | 30%+ | If <30%, the "who do you know" ask isn't being made. Enforce. |

---

## One-Line Summary

A 2-question form filters for the specific moment of pain → pre-call email resets the frame from sales to diagnosis → the 20-minute call opens with the stranger-on-your-homepage question and demonstrates the fix in the first 5 minutes → close happens on the call or never → no-fit calls produce warm intros → the entire flow runs under 30 minutes of Farrice's time per prospect.
