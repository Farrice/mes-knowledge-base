## The Active-Buying vs Pre-Contemplation Split

Active-buying pain = they'll pay this month. They are actively searching for a fix, have budget, have the specific trigger firing.

Pre-contemplation pain = they feel it but can't name it. They need education before they can buy. Content has to meet them where they are.

## Non-Obvious Cross-Avatar Pattern (Kallaway lens)

All three avatars are suffering the same root wound in different disguises:

> **They have externalized every aspect of their work EXCEPT the reader.**

The Substack operator has externalized the topic, the platform, the cadence — not the reader.
The Signal Bleeder has externalized the craft, the positioning doc, the sales process — not the buyer.
The Mission Founder has externalized the program, the curriculum, the pricing — not the participant.

Parallax ICP Intelligence™ is *the act of externalizing the reader*. That is the sentence. That is the category. Everything else is implementation detail.

When this sentence shows up in sales copy across all three avatars, it hits all three. One insight, three buyers, one product. That's the compression.
