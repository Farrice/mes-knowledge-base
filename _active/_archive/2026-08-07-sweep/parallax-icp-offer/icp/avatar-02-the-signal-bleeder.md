# Avatar 02 — The Signal Bleeder

**Classification**: Adjacent. $1,497-$2,997 buyer. Slightly longer cycle (2-4 weeks).
**Working title buyer uses**: "I'm an independent consultant." / "I do [specific craft] for [vertical]."
**Working title they reject**: "Agency owner." "Thought leader." "Content creator."

The label "Signal Bleeder" is internal — they post good thinking on LinkedIn/Twitter, it resonates, and nothing monetizable happens. The signal leaves their mouth and hits open air.

---

## Patient Zero (3 real humans alive today)

1. **Ana Marshall** — Independent B2B SaaS positioning consultant, posts 3x/week on LinkedIn, ~8K followers, clear expertise (she used to run marketing at a Series B). Her posts get 40-80 reactions, 5-15 comments. She books ~1 client/month at $8K-$15K. Her inbound is almost entirely from old colleagues. Cold readers never convert. She's the exact signal-bleed pattern: quality output, audience watching, no pathway from watching to buying.

2. **Jake Kharrl** — LinkedIn handle @jakeK. Fractional CFO for bootstrapped e-commerce brands. 11K LinkedIn followers, writes well on unit economics. He offers a $6K/month fractional engagement. Pipeline is thin despite good content. His ICP sentence is "founders who want better numbers" — broad enough to be meaningless. He's about to hire someone or sell a course and will choose the wrong one without clarity.

3. **Jessica Gioglio** — Brand/content strategist, ex-TD Bank, co-author of *The Laws of Brand Storytelling*. Has a Substack *and* LinkedIn presence. The authority is real. The productized offer is missing. She's been dabbling with courses and consulting. The signal is loud; the shape is absent.

**Backstop pool**: LinkedIn "consultant" + 5K-20K followers + a specific craft in the bio (positioning / pricing / sales ops / SEO / RevOps / CX / fractional CMO / fractional CFO). About 60K-100K of these people in the US. Many in the $4K-$12K/month pipeline-anxiety zone.

## Consumer Posture (Dai Media — Occupation / Activity / Thought Process)

**Occupation (social + symbolic role)**
They are *the translator who used to be inside the system*. Former VP of something at a real company. Left 18-36 months ago to consult. Their symbolic role in the market is the *defector with receipts* — "I did the thing, now I help people do the thing without making my mistakes." That's why people follow. That's not yet why people buy.

**Activity (what they actually do)**
- Monday: schedule 5 LinkedIn posts in Hypefury/Taplio.
- Tuesday-Thursday: 2-3 calls. Half are "pick your brain" coffees disguised as discovery calls. They don't charge, they're flattered to be asked.
- Friday: write a long-form post or Substack essay — the piece they're actually proud of.
- Saturday: mentally draft a course or productized offer. Doesn't build it.
- Sunday: dread Monday's scheduled posts because some of them don't sound like them.

**Thought Process**
"My best post this year got 340 reactions and 42 comments. Zero of those comments turned into a client. I know the problem is that my audience is warm for my thinking but cold for my offer. I don't know what the offer should even be. Every time I sit down to 'define my ICP,' I produce a document with the phrase 'B2B SaaS founders $1M-$10M ARR' — and I can feel it's wrong as I'm writing it. But I don't know what the right document looks like."

## Emotional Outcome (feel, not solve)

They want to feel **"my pipeline pulls me instead of me pulling the pipeline."**

Not "more leads." Not "better marketing." The felt outcome is the nervous-system shift from *cold-call-mentality-disguised-as-content* to *I wrote this specifically for a person who is already on their way to finding me.* Content becomes a beacon instead of a broadcast.

Second-order feeling: **the end of the Sunday dread.** The Monday-morning scheduled-post no longer feels like a compromise.

That night they search one of:

## Why this avatar pays $1,497-$2,997

1. They already spend $3K-$6K/month on tooling + LinkedIn coaches + content systems. A one-time $2,997 to get the positioning layer clarified is cheap compared to a year of wrong-ICP content. They know the math.

2. The near-term *financial* downside of staying unclear is large and visible (going back to a job, raising rates and losing clients, a bad course launch). Urgency is structural, not manufactured.

3. They like receiving *documents* from smart people. The HVC-depth deliverable is a genre they recognize (McKinsey-one-pager, positioning doc) and will file as an asset.

---

## Non-Obvious Angle (Kallaway — Five-Input Gate)

> **"You are not underpositioned. You are over-positioned to *yourself* and underpositioned to *them.*"**

The Signal Bleeder has a crystal-clear internal model of what they do and who they are. That clarity is the trap. Because they see the work so clearly, they assume the buyer sees it too. Every post is written in the voice of *someone explaining to a peer*, not someone inviting a non-peer buyer to self-recognize. The fix isn't more clarity about them — it's a crystal about *the person watching*. Parallax's deliverable inverts the positioning muscle from inward to outward. This is the thing most consultants have never been told, because the whole consulting-coach industry sells them MORE inward clarity.

Ships.
