## 1. HEADLINE (≤ 60 chars visible on mobile)

**Recommended (59 chars)**:
`You have a reader. I find the reader. | Parallax ICP`

Breakdown:
- **Pain + for whom (implicit via the "you")**: The thesis is the reader hasn't named their reader. That's Avatar 01's exact felt state.
- **Proof mechanism**: "I find the reader" is the claim, verifiable in Featured.
- **Brand container**: `| Parallax ICP` signals category.
- **Character count**: 52 visible-on-mobile, fits well under the ~60 rule.

**Extended version (220-char max for SEO tail)**:
`You have a reader. I find the reader. | Parallax ICP Intelligence™ — 7-day ICP crystallization for Substack writers, independent consultants, and mission-driven founders who post, publish, and pitch into a void.`

(200 chars — SEO keywords: ICP, Substack, consultant, mission-driven founder, positioning. "Void" is an avatar USE-word per language-map.md.)

**Runner-up (57 chars)** — sharper pain, less claim:
`Your work has a reader. You haven't named them yet.`

(Use if the "I find" construction reads too claim-y in context. Slightly more about the reader's state; no benefit to me.)

**Rejected**:
- `I help Substack writers find their ICP` → "creator"/"help" slop, breaks Kristen Stewart test.
- `Ghostwriting for hidden experts` → Authority Flywheel language, explicitly off-limits per mission.
- `Parallax | Sharper positioning in 7 days` → no pain, abstract benefit.
