## Post-Publish 30-Minute Protocol (per post)

Every post gets this treatment in the first 30 minutes:

1. Reply to every comment — no canned replies, each one extends the conversation.
2. If someone quote-shares, thank them by commenting on their share (boosts both posts algorithmically).
3. DM anyone who comments substantively with the DM-script variant from `dm-followup-for-engagers.md`.
4. Check 3-5 outlier posts in the Avatar 01/02 feed and leave Jasmin-Alic-quality mini-post comments (see `comment-strategy-30day.md`).

**Non-negotiable**: do not post and leave the platform. The 30-minute window is the whole difference between 200 views and 20,000.
