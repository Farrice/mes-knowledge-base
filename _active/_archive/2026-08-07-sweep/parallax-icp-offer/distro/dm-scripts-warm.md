# Warm DM Scripts — Mutual Connection / Shared Context / Reactivation

**Premise:** Warm prospects close 3-5x higher than cold but burn bridges faster when the warmth is faked. Every script below has to earn the "warm" claim with a specific tie — not "I saw we're both connected with 47 people."

**Rules:**
- ≤4 sentences to first ask
- Name the specific tie (mutual name, shared comment thread, shared community, past contact date)
- Ask reads as re-entry, not cold approach
- First ask = ICP Tell (free 20 min) — same as cold scripts

---

## PATH 1 — Mutual Connection

**Use when:** Farrice and prospect share a specific mutual (not just any LinkedIn 2nd-degree), and that mutual would actually vouch if asked.

### Script M1 — Name-the-Mutual

> [Name], we both have [MUTUAL NAME] in common — I worked with them on [SPECIFIC CONTEXT]. I mention it because [SPECIFIC RECENT POST / ESSAY YOU PUBLISHED] is running into the exact pattern I helped [MUTUAL or OTHER CLIENT] get clear on: the reader on the other end is real to you but hasn't been externalized into a document your next five posts can actually point at.
>
> I built a 6-page ICP crystal for [CLIENT, e.g., "a Chicago nonprofit"] — Javier at HVC called it "truly magical." Happy to send the worked example.
>
> Free 20-minute ICP Tell here: [LINK]. Or just reply "send it" and I'll drop the case study.
>
> — Farrice

**Why it works:** Names the mutual first, not the pitch. The "send it" fallback is a micro-commitment — it converts at 2-3x the calendar link alone, because "send it" is friction-zero.

### Script M2 — Shared Comment Thread

**Use when:** Farrice has commented substantively (not "love this") on 2-3 of the prospect's posts over 2+ weeks, and they've liked/replied.

> [Name], I've been in your comments for a couple weeks — [SPECIFIC EXCHANGE OR QUESTION]. Moving to DM because what's stuck with me across your last three pieces is that you're writing to a very specific reader that your About page doesn't name.
>
> That gap is the entire thing I do. 6-page ICP document, real humans you could DM tomorrow, their language, your next five posts.
>
> Free 20-minute ICP Tell: [LINK]. If the honest answer is "keep commenting, don't pitch me," you'll hear that first.
>
> — Farrice

**Why it works:** The "keep commenting, don't pitch me" disarms. It signals Farrice values the peer relationship above the sale. Nate Herk's Zero Proof frame applied to warm.

## PATH 2 — Shared Context (community, cohort, alumni network)

### Script S1 — Alumni / Cohort

**Use for:** Write of Passage alumni, Foster members, Pavilion members, Futur Pro alums, Superpath members.

> [Name], fellow [COMMUNITY NAME] — [SPECIFIC SHARED CONTEXT, e.g., "Cohort 14, 2024" or "I posted in #show-and-tell last month about the HVC rebuild"]. Reaching out because your [SPECIFIC PIECE] has the exact stalled-paid-conversion shape I've been helping writers work out of post-[COMMUNITY].
>
> I built out a productized version — Parallax ICP Intelligence. 6-page document, 7 days, $1,497 at the entry tier. Community discount isn't the move; the work is.
>
> Free 20-minute ICP Tell: [LINK]. Or reply "case study" and I'll send you the HVC worked example.
>
> — Farrice

**Why it works:** Alumni networks reward "I'm one of you" framing but reject "I'm one of you AND a discount is coming" framing (it breaks trust). The "community discount isn't the move" line signals Farrice respects the network.

## PATH 3 — Reactivation (past contact)

### Script R1 — Old Contact, Clear Reference Point

**Use for:** Anyone Farrice has worked with, collaborated with, or exchanged substantial messages with 6-24 months ago.

> [Name], we connected back in [TIME, e.g., "summer 2024"] about [SPECIFIC TOPIC, e.g., "the positioning project you were thinking about post-[COMPANY]"]. Circling back because I just productized the exact method I would have used with you then.
>
> It's called Parallax ICP Intelligence — 6-page document that names the reader your work is already for. 7, 14, or 21 days depending on depth. The Javier Payano case study is worth a read either way; happy to send.
>
> Free 20-minute ICP Tell here: [LINK].
>
> — Farrice

**Why it works:** Names the past moment specifically. Doesn't pretend they've stayed in touch. Offers the free artifact before the calendar.

---

### Script R2 — Long-Dormant Contact, Soft Re-Entry

**Use for:** Contacts where it's been 18+ months, vibe is cold, no active touch points.

> [Name], been a while. Saw [SPECIFIC RECENT THING THEY SHIPPED] and it pulled me back — the shape of what you're working on right now looks like the shape my current offer is built for.
>
> I'm not going to pitch in a DM. If it's interesting, the 20-minute ICP Tell is free and one question: [LINK]. If it's not the moment, no worries, genuinely good to see you still at it.
>
> — Farrice

**Why it works:** "Not going to pitch in a DM" is Zero Proof move. "Genuinely good to see you still at it" is a warm exit that keeps the door open even on a no.

## POST-REPLY HANDLING (Warm path specifics)

1. **If they reply "who are you again"**: don't recover with resume. Give them the specific past moment + what you've built since. Then the calendar.
2. **If they reply "not a fit"**: always ask for one name. Warm networks compound on referral.
3. **If they reply positive + ask questions**: answer 1-2 questions max in the thread, then pivot to "this is calendar-shaped, let's grab 20 mins." Do NOT answer the full discovery in-DM — it kills the call momentum.
4. **If they reply with their own pitch (they're also selling something)**: respond with genuine interest, book their call, AND book the Parallax one. Mutual pipeline is warm pipeline.

---

## THE ONE WARM SCRIPT NOT TO USE

Do NOT send: "Hey! I noticed you and I are both connected to [NAME] on LinkedIn. Would you be open to a quick chat?"

This is the canonical "warm-faking-warm" script. Every experienced operator has seen it 200 times. It converts at cold rates with warm bridge-burning risk. Either name the specific tie + context, or default to the cold script with Zero Proof framing.
