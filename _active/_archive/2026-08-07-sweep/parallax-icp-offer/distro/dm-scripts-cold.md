# Cold DM Scripts — 9 Variants (3 per Avatar)

**Rules baked in:**
- Every script ≤4 sentences to first ask
- USE-words from language-map.md (verbatim)
- AVOID-words banned: no "creator," "content," "niche down," "funnel," "monetize," "thought leader," "personal brand," "non-profit marketing," "impact storytelling," "scale" as naked verb
- No "Here's what/why/how..." openers (Parallax brand rule)
- Max 1-2 em dashes per message
- First ask = free 20-min ICP Tell call. Not the sale.
- Personalization slot `[SPECIFIC THING THEY SHIPPED]` is non-negotiable — real artifact, not "I liked your content"
- Nate Herk rule: diagnosis-first, pitch-never. The call is diagnosis.
- Josh Sanders rule: profile converts before the DM lands — Farrice's profile should be tuned per `profile-conversion-farrice.md` (separate asset) before any send

## AVATAR 01 — STALLED SUBSTACK OPERATOR (3 variants)

Opens with raw honesty. Disarms. Lands because it refuses to posture.

**Script A1:**

>
> I help writers in exactly your spot — 1K-10K subs, paid tier live, conversion stalled — get a crystal on who this is for, externalized as a 6-page document. Not a course. Not a template.
>
> If you want a free 20-minute ICP Tell (one question, honest answer), here's my calendar: [LINK]. If not, I'll keep being a reader.
>
> — Farrice

**Why it lands:** "I'll keep being a reader" as the exit ramp disarms. The gap named (you-feel-the-reader vs. About-page-doesn't-show-them) is the exact pain from avatar-01. No "I help creators" language.

## POST-SEND RULES (cross-avatar)

1. **No follow-up for 72 hours after first send.** Mission founders decide slowly; forcing a second touch inside 3 days reads as desperate.
2. **If opened + no reply by Day 4**: send a "I noticed you opened this — if the reader for Parallax isn't you, the honest thing is to say so. Close the loop either way." one-liner. This is Nate Herk's disqualification bump.
3. **If replied but not booked**: respond inside 4 hours. Every conversation is PMF research (Validation-First). Capture the objection verbatim for script-evolution.
4. **If replied with hard no**: "Appreciate the honesty. If one specific writer/consultant/founder you know is in this exact spot, I'd love an intro. If not, no worries." (Referral ask embedded. Never waste a no.)
5. **If ICP Tell book happens**: send pre-call note with the HVC case study + "bring one question about your reader" framing. Raises show rate.
