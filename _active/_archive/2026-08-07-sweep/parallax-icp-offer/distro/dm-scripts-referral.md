# Referral Ask Scripts — Farrice → Existing Network

**Premise:** Vague asks produce vague results. "Let me know if you know anyone" returns zero introductions 90% of the time. Specific asks with a named constraint produce a 3-5x reply rate.

**Rules:**
- Name the avatar profile in 1-2 sentences (not a paragraph)
- Ask for 1-3 names, not "anyone who might fit"
- Make it easy to say no (one-sentence no, no guilt)
- Offer something back (reciprocity, kickback to HVC, or just reciprocal referral)

---

## ASK 1 — JAVIER (the highest-leverage referral node)

**Use for:** Co-creating the list of 3 names Javier can warmly introduce. This is the #45-47 prospect slot from `prospect-list-50.md`.

**Channel:** Text first (iMessage — Javier's default). Only fall back to email if he's slow.

### Script J1 — The Co-Creation Text

> Javier — quick one. I've productized the work we did together into an offer (Parallax ICP Intelligence, same method, three tiers) and I'm trying to get it in front of the right people the first week.
>
> You know 3 founders/nonprofit leaders/teachers-with-a-side-mission who are stuck at the exact "we have outcomes, we don't have words" place you were. Can I run the three names by you over a 10-min call this week? I'll handle the intro copy — you just confirm or pass.
>
> Also — 15% of any closed deal goes back to HVC as a donation, so this genuinely helps the org. Let me know if Thursday afternoon works.
>
> — Farrice

**Why it works:**
- Names the exact profile ("stuck at 'we have outcomes, we don't have words'") in Javier's own language
- Makes it a 10-min call, not an open-ended "who do you know"
- Embeds the HVC-donation structure from referral-ask-scripts.md (WS2) upfront — removes the "why am I helping" question
- Thursday afternoon specific — anchors a commitment window

**Follow-up if no reply in 48 hours:**

> Javier — no pressure, just a nudge on the referral call. If this week's not it, next week is fine. Also if you want the HVC case study finalized first so you have the asset to forward, I can turn that around in a day.

## ASK 2 — PAST CLIENTS / CREATIVE COLLABORATORS (specific-ask, not vague)

**Use for:** People Farrice has delivered work to or collaborated with in the past 18 months who have their own networks.

### Script P1 — Past-Client Referral Ask

> [Name], quick ask. I've turned the [PAST WORK, e.g., "positioning project we did"] kind of work into a productized offer — Parallax ICP Intelligence. 7-, 14-, or 21-day engagement, $1,497 / $2,997 / $4,997.
>
> I'm not asking you to share it broadly. Who's one person in your network running [SPECIFIC AVATAR PROFILE, e.g., "a newsletter at 2-10K subs with a paid tier that's not converting" OR "an independent consultant whose LinkedIn gets reactions but not calls"] that I could send you a short intro template to forward?
>
> One name is the ask. If none, no worries — and if you ever want a recalibration of your own, the door's open.
>
> — Farrice

**Why it works:**
- "One name is the ask" is the Priestley concentration move — asks for less, gets more
- "Short intro template to forward" means Farrice does the writing, not them — 80% of referral asks die at the "I'll write something up" step
- The reciprocity offer (your own recalibration) is specific and time-boxed

**Follow-up: send the template within 4 hours of any yes.**

---

### Script P2 — Specific Named-Target Ask

**Use when:** Farrice sees a specific prospect on LinkedIn/Substack they think their past client knows. Higher close rate than open ask.

> [Name], I noticed you're connected to [SPECIFIC NAME] on LinkedIn. I've been reading their [SPECIFIC RECENT WORK] and the pattern is exactly the one Parallax ICP Intelligence is built for.
>
> Would you be willing to make an intro? I can send you the 3-sentence template so you can just forward. If you don't know them well enough, totally fine — a "doesn't feel right" is the best answer either of us gets.
>
> — Farrice

**Why it works:** Naming the target specifically makes it a binary decision. Open asks ("who do you know") trigger cognitive load and die. Named asks ("introduce me to X") are yes-or-no.

## ASK 3 — LINKEDIN MUTUAL CONNECTIONS (scripts to ask for warm intros)

**Use for:** People Farrice has a 1st-degree LinkedIn relationship with who share a 1st-degree connection to a prospect on the list.

### Script L1 — LinkedIn Mutual Ask (Formal Tone)

> Hey [Name] — quick LinkedIn ask. You're connected to [SPECIFIC PROSPECT]. I've built a productized ICP offer that fits them specifically based on [SPECIFIC POST/WORK they published].
>
> Would you be willing to send a 3-sentence intro? I can draft the copy — you just hit forward. If it's a "don't know them well enough" or "not my vibe," no hard feelings.
>
> Either way, if your own work could ever use an ICP recalibration, I do a free 20-min diagnosis call.
>
> — Farrice

**Why it works:** LinkedIn is formality-default, so this script leans slightly more formal than the text-to-Javier script. "I can draft the copy — you just hit forward" is the friction-killer.

---

### Script L2 — LinkedIn Mutual Ask (Casual Tone, Warmer Relationship)

> [Name] — saw you're connected to [SPECIFIC PROSPECT]. I've been wanting to reach out to them — [SPECIFIC WORK they shipped] is the exact profile my current offer is for.
>
> Any chance you'd hit forward on a 3-liner from me? Happy to draft it. And if no / don't know them well, zero worries — just thought I'd ask.
>
> — Farrice

## ASK 4 — THE "WHO'S STUCK" BROADCAST (last-resort, avoid repeat use)

**Use ONCE per quarter max.** Public broadcast on Farrice's LinkedIn + Parallax Substack Notes asking for inbound.

**Script B1 — Public Broadcast**

> I'm running 4 Parallax ICP Intelligence engagements in [MONTH]. If you know a writer with 1-10K subs whose paid tier isn't converting, a consultant whose LinkedIn gets reactions but not calls, or a mission-founder whose "who is this for" sentence breaks at the edges, send them my way.
>
> The 20-minute diagnostic is free. The work is 7-21 days, $1,497-$4,997.
>
> The worked example (HVC in 9 days, "truly magical"): [LINK TO CASE STUDY]
>
> DMs open.

**Why this is a last resort:** Public asks feel like promotion, not generosity. They work once per quarter if the ask is specific enough. Overuse flattens them.

## WHAT FARRICE OFFERS IN RETURN (baked into every script)

1. **For Javier**: 15% of closed revenue to HVC as a tax-deductible donation. Structural.
2. **For past clients / collaborators**: Free 20-min ICP recalibration for THEIR work, no strings.
3. **For LinkedIn mutuals**: Same — free 20-min diagnosis for their own work if they want it.
4. **For public-broadcast responders**: Front-of-line on the monthly cohort tier ($1,197 instead of $1,497, capped at 4 per cohort per pricing-psychology.md).

## CADENCE RULES

- Ask Javier in Day 1 morning. If he says yes, block the 10-min call inside 48 hours.
- Past-client asks: send 5 per week, distributed across days. Track in `conversion-tracker.md`.
- LinkedIn mutual asks: 2 per day max (noise-floor; more reads as harassment).
- Public broadcast: Day 5 if cold outbound is <5% reply rate by Day 3 (pivot trigger).

---

## RED LINE

Do NOT ask the same person for a referral twice in 90 days unless they explicitly said "check back." Over-asking a warm network kills the network faster than a cold DM ever could.
