# DM Scripts — Content System Audit

> **Voice rules**: Anti-guru, peer-to-peer, system-builder voice. ≤80 words per DM. Personalize EVERY message — `[Name]` and `[topic]` are placeholders, not crutches.
> **Banned vocabulary**: ghostwriting, methodology extraction, "your frameworks," thought leadership, "in your voice." Use: workflow, system, architecture, protocol, "sounds like you."
> **Quota**: Never >20 cold connection requests/day on LinkedIn (anti-ban).

---

## COLD OPENERS (Variants 1-3)

For prospects you've never interacted with. Use after 5-day pre-warm protocol (follow → comment 2-3x → view profile → connect) per `_active/farrice-brand/offers/dm-script-playbook.md` §Phase 0.

### Variant 1 — Pain-pointed observation
```
[Name], noticed you went from 3 posts a week in February to one this month. Usually that means the content production is eating hours you don't have, or the AI drafts keep coming back sounding like everyone else. Built a $249 audit specifically for consultants who want to keep writing but stop losing the afternoon to it. Worth a look?
```
*Use when: their posting cadence visibly dropped in the last 30-60 days.*

### Variant 2 — Anti-AI-slop hook
```
[Name], your post on [topic] actually sounded like a human wrote it. That's rare in [their feed/industry] right now. Most of what I scroll past is the same ChatGPT cadence with different headshots. If you ever want to lock in what makes yours sound like you so the AI stops flattening it, I run a 48-hour audit for that exact problem.
```
*Use when: their voice is genuinely strong and you can point to a specific recent post.*

### Variant 3 — Diagnostic question
```
[Name], quick one. When you sit down to write a LinkedIn post, are you running a repeatable workflow or starting from a blank doc every time? No wrong answer. Asking because I built an audit for consultants stuck in the second mode who want to be in the first. Takes 48 hours, $249, you keep writing yourself.
```
*Use when: prospect feels mid-funnel — visible on platform, posting unevenly.*

## WARM OPENERS (Variants 4-6)

After connection accept OR after they engage with a Farrice post.

### Variant 4 — Reference content + soft offer
```
Thanks for connecting, [Name]. Read your piece on [topic] last week, the section about [specific point] was the part that stuck. If you're spending more than a couple hours per post to get to that quality, I run a content system audit that maps your patterns into a workflow your AI tools can actually follow. $249, 48 hours. Happy to send the one-pager.
```
*Use when: they accepted connection request AND have a recent post worth referencing.*

### Variant 5 — Reference Farrice's post they engaged with
```
[Name], saw you commented on my post about [post topic]. The pattern I described in there is the exact thing the audit fixes — most consultants have the voice but no system around it, so AI keeps producing drafts that need a full rewrite. $249, 48 hours, you walk out with a custom prompt set and content protocol. Want the breakdown?
```
*Use when: they liked or commented on a Parallax/audit-relevant post in the last 7 days.*

### Variant 6 — Trojan horse analysis
```
[Name], ran your last three posts through the diagnostic I use for the audit. Two patterns showed up consistently: [pattern 1] and [pattern 2]. Both are what makes you sound like you instead of a generic [their role]. If you want the full mapping plus a workflow that holds those patterns when you draft with AI, that's the $249 audit. 48-hour turnaround.
```
*Use when: post-connection, you've ACTUALLY run their content through your pattern check (not theoretically).*

## TRANSITION-TO-OFFER (Variants 7-9)

After they've engaged. Match their objection.

### Variant 7 — They said "I post but inconsistently"
```
That's the gap the audit is built for, [Name]. Inconsistent posting almost always traces back to no system, not no time. You sit down, the blank doc wins, the week gets away. The audit gives you a 6-step content production process tuned to your voice patterns plus a prompt library so AI drafts come back usable. $249, delivered in 48 hours. Send you the scope?
```

### Variant 8 — They said "I tried ChatGPT and it sucks"
```
Yeah, default ChatGPT will flatten anyone, [Name]. The fix isn't a better prompt, it's a workflow with constraints baked in so the model can't drift into generic mode. The audit pulls your actual voice patterns from existing content, builds the guardrails around them, and hands you the prompt set. $249, 48 hours, you keep writing — the AI just stops fighting you.
```

### Variant 9 — They said "I'm too busy"
> ⚠️ **Verification flag**: Stat "20 minutes vs 90" is UNCONFIRMED until audit clients report back. Soften to "noticeably faster" if not yet measured. After client #3-5 has data, swap in real numbers.
```
That's the case the audit is built for, [Name]. The whole point is reclaiming the 10+ hours a week consultants lose to bad AI drafts and blank-page restarts. Your time investment is one 30-minute intake call. I deliver the system in 48 hours. After that you're writing posts noticeably faster instead of 90 min restarts. $249. Want the one-pager?
```

## BREAKUP (Variant 10)

After 2-3 follow-ups go unanswered over 10-14 days. **Do not skip this** — highest-leverage message in the sequence based on existing playbook conversion data.

```
[Name], last note from me. The audit offer stays open if timing shifts later, but I'll stop the follow-ups here so I'm not adding to your inbox. If the content production thing becomes a priority in Q3 or beyond, the door's open. Either way, appreciated the connection.
```

## Pull-through ranking (master-copywriter assessment)

Strongest reply-rate variants: **2, 3, 6, 8** — each has a specific recognition moment.
Weakest if sent cold: **9** — risks reading transactional. Pair with prior warmth (connection + post engagement).
Highest-leverage: **10** (breakup) — never skip it.
