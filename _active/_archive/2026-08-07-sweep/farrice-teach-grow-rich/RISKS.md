## Open Decisions for Farrice

A short checklist. Each unchecked box is a place the draft is currently guessing.

- [ ] **Front door (the one lane).** Lead the teaching with S&C/Authority Flywheel or with AI-orchestrated systems? The other becomes proof, not a second offer. *(Resolves the expertise-centering risk.)*
- [ ] **The harvest.** Run the four-well rolodex (Clients · Failures · Wins · Yesterday) across BOTH lanes. 15-40 raw seeds. Nothing publishes before this exists. *(Resolves the first-pass-draft risk.)*
- [ ] **Personal red lines.** What's shareable now / gated / never. Suppression-wound specifics stay private until you say otherwise. *(Resolves the not-ready-to-share risk.)*
- [ ] **Pricing.** Confirm the live numbers for whichever offer fronts this — the existing stack ($1.5K-$2.5K Sprint, $2.5K-$3.5K/mo, $3.5K-$5K/mo) or revised. The "boil it down to one" rule applies to the offer too: one clear next step.
- [ ] **Proof to lead with.** The single closest-fit transformation per lane — your strongest S&C client outcome, your strongest "I built a system that did X" outcome. This is the living proof for cold prospects.
- [ ] **The minimum showing-up unit.** What you can hit on a low-suppression day (likely one voice memo). Cadence is designed around this floor. *(Resolves the consistency-dependency risk.)*
- [ ] **The two clocks.** Fastest honest path to the next $2K from the proven lane this month, kept separate from the non-panicked teaching-engine build. *(Resolves the urgency-tension risk.)*
- [ ] **HOW line for the systems lane.** Which WHAT you give freely to prove obsession; which HOW lives only in the paid container. *(Resolves the HOW-reconciliation risk.)*
- [ ] **Faith register.** Adopt / translate to secular / keep private. *(Resolves the faith-fit risk.)*

> Closing note: most of these risks are not flaws in the model — they're the places where the model is waiting for Farrice. The craft is sound; it just needs to be made *only-Farrice-could-have-made-it*. Fill the boxes and the draft stops guessing.
