# Farrice — Cold-Start Content Engine

Zero audience. No backlog. One operator who knows a great deal across strength-and-conditioning, persuasion, and AI-orchestrated knowledge systems, and has struggled to leverage any of it. This file turns that exact condition into a stocked engine Farrice can run Monday morning.

It combines three of Omar's craft workflows — `/oe-uncopyable-content-engine` (mine the only-you moat), `/oe-felt-need-titler` (name the need the right person already feels), and `/oe-what-not-how` (title in HOW, deliver WHAT). The thesis underneath all three is Omar's: *"how do you make content that no one else can copy? You make content only you can make."* Farrice's two assets are uncopyable by definition — a lived suppression-wound that produced an invisible-expert pattern, and a working AI persuasion system most people in his audience have never seen built. The engine below is built on those, not on talking points anyone could scrape.

**Audience (locked ICP — `_active/linkedin/01-research/deep-icp-profile-invisible-expert.md`):** the Invisible Expert. Accomplished, deeply knowledgeable, online-invisible. Trapped behind a *translation gap* — the distance between what they know and what they can say. Their problem is not skill; it is identity wearing a marketing costume. They say things like *"my work should speak for itself,"* *"I'm great at what I do but nobody knows,"* *"self-promotion feels like bragging."* Farrice IS one of them, one or two steps further down the road. That is the entire content edge. Do not write down to them; write across to them, from a half-step ahead.

> **HOW-reconciliation rule (governs every asset here):** title in HOW for reach, deliver WHAT / WHEN / WHY / WHERE (+ their negations) in free/transient channels, and reserve the actual step-by-step HOW for the paid container. Omar: *"you don't want to teach how-to from your point of view... but don't teach how. When you start teaching how, you will start to lose people."* This is context-calibration, never gatekeeping.

## Part 3 — The Never-Run-Out Input Regimen

The bank is a snapshot; this is the spring. Omar reframes the panic directly: *"if you feel like you're going to run out of content... congratulations. You've identified that you need to grow."* And *"if eight YouTube videos in you're like 'now what,' you maxed out"* — a ceiling Farrice specifically does not want, because his whole differentiation is range. The regimen makes the well refill faster than he draws from it.

**1. Be a learner, not a consumer.** Omar: *"stop watching Netflix... become interested in stuff. Become a learner."* Convert passive intake into reference points. [FARRICE: name the specific passive habit to swap — and the active input that replaces it. Likely candidates: replace doom-scroll with one S&C study / one persuasion essay / one AI-systems thread, read with a capture doc open.]

**2. Read often, not a lot.** Omar reads one Proverb a day, by date — *"that little five, seven minute moment... informs the way I teach."* The PRINCIPLE is a small, dated, daily input that compounds; Scripture is one valid source, not a requirement. For Farrice's secular deployment: **one short read per day**, dated — alternate across his three domains so the cross-domain moat keeps widening. [FARRICE: pick the rotation — e.g. Mon/Thu S&C, Tue/Fri persuasion-and-influence, Wed/Sat AI-systems, Sun reflection. Five to seven minutes. The point is the streak, not the volume.]

**3. Capture "yesterday."** Omar: *"stories and illustrations and things that happened to you the other day... everything is a reference point."* Keep a running inbox so the renewable well (seeds #10, #14) never dries between harvests. Practical: a single notes file or the existing `_active/farrice-brand/thought-bank/inbox/` — drop every lived moment, client line, failed rep, and system surprise the day it happens. The thought-bank is already wired; this regimen just gives it a daily reason to exist.

**4. Mine the moat on a schedule.** Once a month, re-run the four wells (Client Rolodex / Failures / Wins / Yesterday) for 30 minutes. Omar's literal instruction: *"list out every single client you had... what situation they were in, what they did, and then what happened... find the thread."* For Farrice that's S&C clients AND system-build milestones AND moments the invisible-expert pattern showed up in someone he talked to.

**5. Treat "running out" as the growth edge, never a hiatus.** When Farrice hits *"now what,"* the move is re-read + re-harvest, not pause. The wall is the signal to learn, not the signal to quit. [FARRICE: write the if-then on a sticky note — "if I feel tapped out → 20 min re-harvest + one new input, never a skip day."]

## Part 4 — First-30-Days Posting Cadence

Mapped to Omar's objective-by-context matrix — *"there's different objectives for Instagram and YouTube and having a webinar."* Set the objective per channel BEFORE posting. Cold-start, so the early objective is **become undeniable + recruit the receptive 0.1%**, not chase reach. Omar on long-form: *"so that you undeniably know who I am... you can't fake who you are for a long time."*

**Channel objectives (cold-start):**

| Channel | Objective | What ships here |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube / long-form | Become undeniable. The full WHAT, told over time, until the right person knows exactly who Farrice is. | Seeds #1, #3, #8, #13 — the throughline and the cross-maps, generous WHAT |
| Instagram (feed/carousel) | Recognition — make the Invisible Expert think *"that's me."* Recruit the receptive. | Sharp single-feeling seeds + felt-need titles; one idea, one twist |
| Instagram (story) | Trust + capture the renewable well in public. | "Yesterday" seeds (#10, #14), behind-the-build moments |
| Email | Deepen trust with the warm; the unfakeable backstory. | Seeds #2, #7, #9, #12 — failures and the wound-surface land hardest here |

**The 30-day schedule.** Cadence is deliberately survivable — Omar's discipline is *"I am boring, I just run the play."* Better a play Farrice runs all four weeks than a heroic week-one burnout. Sustainability is the strategy at cold-start.

| Week | YouTube (1×) | IG feed (3×) | IG story (most days) | Email (1×) | Objective for the week |

| **4 — Open the door** | Seed #1 deepened, or a Client Rolodex story — title #10 | Seeds #2 (variant), #5 (variant), #12 | Behind-the-build of the paid container | Seed #12 + the soft next-step (the 0.1% self-select) | Self-selection: *"99.99% won't click and that's fine. For the 0.1%, let's go."* |

**Rules that hold all 30 days:**
- **One thing per asset.** Every post teaches ONE WHAT. The fire-hose is the #1 mistake — *"boil it down to one. It's one drink at a time."* If a draft teaches three things, it's three posts.
- **Title in HOW, body delivers WHAT.** No free asset owes a step-by-step. The HOW points to the paid container.
- **Energy before script.** Omar/Interior-Game: before hitting publish, the question is *"is my energy right?"* not *"is this perfect?"* Cold-start is data collection — *"do it for the data."*
- **Don't optimize for the 99.99%.** Build for the receptive fraction who self-select. Low early numbers from high-value eyeballs beat viral noise.
- **Do not break the chain.** Missing a YouTube week is survivable; breaking the daily input regimen is not — the regimen is the engine, the posts are exhaust.

## Banned-Tells Guard (applies to every asset Farrice writes from this engine)

- No triple-anaphora, no twin-sentence endings.
