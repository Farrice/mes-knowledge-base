## PHASE 1 — Lead Generation (teach to magnetize)

**The principle:** Every asset is gatekeeping-free but never a fire hose. *"The biggest mistake people who teach online make is they tend to over-teach... if someone was thirsty and they needed a drink of water, and you came with a fire hose, you didn't serve them. You hurt them."* And the new objective Omar drills hardest: *"The goal of teaching is not to get people information. The goal of teaching is to create the feeling in a person to take the next step."* Behind it, Eileen Wilder's law via Omar: *"people do things for one reason and one reason only — because they feel like it."*

This rung has four moves and one gate. Run them in order; do not dump all four on yourself at once (that's a fire hose *on the operator*).

**1a — Build the content bank from the mined IP**
- **First action:** Convert your Phase-0 rolodex stories into a stocked bank — each row: seed (the lived story) | what it teaches | the feeling it creates | the channel. Your only-you angle is the AI-systems layer; every story should carry the residue of *"I built the system, so I know where the genius actually hides."*
- **Command:** `/oe-uncopyable-content-engine`
- **Artifact:** A stocked content bank (15–40 seeds) + a never-run-out input regimen. Omar reads *"one Proverb a day, by date"* — install your secular equivalent (one extraction-insight a day; one S&C principle re-examined through the systems lens).

**1b — Title the felt need (their words, not yours)**
- **First action:** Harvest 5+ verbatim phrases the Invisible Expert says about themselves. Yours are already documented: *"My work should speak for itself," "Self-promotion is bragging," "I'm great at my work but terrible at marketing myself," "We can sit with someone's deepest pain but freeze when asked to describe ourselves in 150 words."* That last one is HOT — they say it unprompted. Title to it. Omar's proof: he put one word — *"teacher"* — in a title and the comments wrote *"I know I'm called to teach."*
- **Command:** `/oe-felt-need-titler`
- **Artifact:** A ranked title/hook bank, each titled-in-HOW, each tagged with the exact felt need it calls and the WHAT the body delivers. Lead pick = the one where the right person silently thinks *"that's me."*

**1c — Reframe every asset to WHAT-not-HOW**
- **First action:** Take each title's body and strip out step-by-step mechanics. *"Don't teach how. Teach what or what not. Teach when or when not. Teach why or why not."* Omar's *"how to edit in Premiere Pro"* video has 2M+ views (UNCONFIRMED) and never teaches editing — it teaches what to do *before* you edit (*"create a folder on your desktop... open Premiere Pro... save as... create a sequence. Now you're ready to edit"*). Your equivalent: don't teach the prompt; teach what to do *before* you write a post — what to capture from a coaching session, what to never lead with, when an idea is ready.
- **Command:** `/oe-what-not-how`
- **Artifact:** Bodies rewritten as WHAT/WHEN/WHY/WHERE + negations, with the real HOW relocated to the paid container.

**1d — Build the undeniable long-form**
- **First action:** Pick your long-form vehicle and write its objective in Omar's frame — *"my objective with YouTube is to produce content that is very, very long form so that you undeniably know who I am... you can't fake who you are for a long time."* For you this is decisive: the Invisible Expert's deepest wound is the *translation gap*, and long-form is the one format where your range (18 years in the room + the systems mind) becomes self-evidently real. A long conversation can't be faked. This is also where Asset B stops being abstract — over an hour, people *see* the systems thinking.
- **Command:** `/oe-long-form-authority`
- **Artifact:** A long-form format plan (vehicle, objective, only-you angle, cadence, input loop) + the ascension ladder it feeds + a standing self-selection CTA (*"99.99% of you won't even click the link, and that's totally fine. But for the 0.1%, let's go"*).

**THE GATE — every asset clears this before it ships:**
- **Command:** `/oe-over-teaching-audit` on each post, email, title, and script.
- **Artifact:** A red-lined asset + a "boil to ONE thing" rewrite. The discipline phrase: *"One thing at a time. It's one drink at a time."* Insecurity is the tell — *"insecure people tend to over-teach because they are trying to prove... there's nothing to prove."* Frank Kern via Omar: *"prove to people you can help them by actually helping them."*

> [FARRICE: Your specific over-teaching risk is acute and worth naming. You know SO much across so many domains — the Invisible Expert's exact curse — that your default failure mode is the fire hose: dumping the systems thinking, the frameworks, the meta-layer. Resist it hardest here. The receptive Invisible Expert is *thirsty*, not drowning. One drink. The proof that you've mastered the craft is restraint, not volume.]

**Built when:** Content gets *movement* (saves, DMs, opt-ins, replies), not just views. Views without movement = you're not creating the feeling (Pattern 3). Don't advance to Phase 2 on views alone.

## PHASE 2 — Conversion (teach them what your business can do for them, and they buy)

**The principle:** *"Your job is for someone to think about or believe about the thing differently than they did before they met you."* You do it by making them feel three things: it's **possible**, it's **easy**, and they can **trust you**. The hidden close lives inside "easy": *"if they feel like it's easy because you said it... psychologically they're thinking to themself, it's going to be easy if I work with them."* Making it seem easy IS the close. (Case: Paige — new IG account in January, ~300K followers, $300 product, ~3,000 buyers, ~$1.2M, no sales team — *"she's helped people make like creating content could be so easy."* UNCONFIRMED, Omar's account.)

**2a — Set the conversion event's objective**
- **First action:** Decide the vehicle and set its objective *before* building it — *"there's different objectives for Instagram and YouTube and having a webinar... a master class... a workshop... a boot camp."* For you, the natural conversion event is a live teaching room (Omar's is the *Content-to-Cash Challenge, a 5-day live coaching experience on Zoom*). Yours: a short live workshop or challenge where invisible experts watch their trapped expertise become a publishable asset in real time.
- **Command:** `/oe-objective-by-context`
- **Artifact:** A per-channel objective + disclosure matrix; the answer to *"when do I teach HOW?"* (answer: here and beyond, post-commitment).

**2b — Architect the belief shift / the make-it-easy close**
- **First action:** Map the Invisible Expert's three wrong beliefs (in their first-person voice) against the possible/easy/trust triad. Possibility wound: *"This works for influencers, not serious professionals like me."* Difficulty wound: *"Translating what I know into content takes a skill I don't have."* Trust wound: *"Everyone selling this is just selling."* Then design ONE make-it-easy demonstration: take a piece of *their* trapped expertise and turn it into a finished post *live, in 90 seconds, using your system* — so they feel *"if it's this easy when he does it, it'll be easy if I work with him."* This is where Asset B closes the sale silently. EASY is installed by demonstration, never by claim.
- **Command:** `/oe-belief-shift-architect`
- **Artifact:** A belief-shift map (current beliefs → target beliefs → install mechanism → free/paid context) + the make-it-easy move with its second-order close written out.

**2c — Route into the live vehicle**
- **Command:** `challenge-model` (the 5-day challenge engine) or `personal-brand-blueprint` (the full pipeline) — existing Omar workflows, the conversion vehicle itself.
- **Artifact:** A built conversion event ready to run.

> [FARRICE: Your make-it-easy demo is your single strongest conversion asset and almost nobody can replicate it. Live, on a call, take an invisible expert's rambling answer to "what do you actually do?" and run it through your extraction system into a sharp, publishable post in real time. They will feel the easy and the trust simultaneously. Decide which ONE demonstration you'll standardize — that's the close.]

**Built when:** People buy without being pressured — and, per Omar, *thank you for selling them*: *"they will thank you for selling them something."* If you're closing by pressure, the belief shift is missing; go back to 2b.

## PHASE 3 — Fulfillment (NOW teach HOW, post-paywall, in the right context)

**The principle:** This is where the HOW finally lives — and the reason is the V1↔V2 reconciliation, the one rule you must not confuse with gatekeeping. *"The context in which the content is consumed determines the value of the content."* Free/transient context can't hold the value a paid room can, so you give the WHAT away freely *because* it's transient. The HOW belongs in the committed container: *"when somebody pays you, they'll be more receptive of the how-to,"* and it works when *"it's delivered in the right context and in a way that anyone can do it."* Hormozi's version, via Omar: *"teach how-to then sell the implementation."* And the delivery stays belief-led, because *"it's always mindset."*

- **First action for Farrice:** Build the paid container's curriculum as the actual HOW of your system — now teach the prompts, the extraction sequence, the publishing engine, the steps you reserved in Phase 1. But wrap each tactic in the belief that makes it land: a tactic handed to someone who doesn't believe *"someone like me can do this"* bounces off. *"All broke people want tactics. People who have made it will say it's mindset."*
- **Command:** `/oe-mindset-over-tactics` (run on each module before you deliver it)
- **Artifact:** Each deliverable converted from a raw hack into a belief-wrapped teach, passing the true-influence test: *"someone thinks about the thing differently than they did after they met you... and then they act on it."*

> [FARRICE: This rung is the inverse of your Phase-1 discipline. In free content you withheld the HOW; here you finally give it — generously, completely, anyone-can-do-it. The trap is delivering the system as a system (overwhelming) instead of as a guided transformation (empowering). Even here: one drink at a time, just more drinks, sequenced.]

**Built when:** Buyers get the result and don't ask for a refund out of confusion. Churn/refunds here = over-teaching in the delivery (Pattern 4), not under-delivering — diagnose accordingly.

- **First action for Farrice:** Design the next-level teach for graduates. Someone who learned to translate their own expertise into authority content (your core offer) now has a new felt need: *systematize it / scale it / build their own audience engine.* That ascension teach is where your AI-orchestration mastery (Asset B) becomes the *whole* product, not just the backstage moat — you teach them to build the system, not just use yours. The beachhead audience (S&C coaches) widens here into the full Invisible Expert market.
- **Command:** `/oe-teaching-business-os` (re-run the router to locate the graduate's new rung and design the next teach)
- **Artifact:** A defined next-level offer with its own objective + the ascension path from Phase-3 graduates into it.

> [FARRICE: This is your monetization of Asset B at full strength, and the natural moment to widen from S&C coaches to all invisible experts. Sketch the graduate's new felt need in their words before you design the offer — don't build the ascension product from what *you* want to teach next; build it from what *they* will feel like needing once they've tasted the first transformation.]

**Built when:** A measurable fraction of Phase-3 graduates ascend into the next teach without new acquisition spend.
