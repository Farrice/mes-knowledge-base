## 3. VOICE RULES

- **Show, don't tell.** Concrete scene, specific number, named moment — never a summary claim.
- **Reader as protagonist.** The buyer is the hero; the offer is the guide, not the subject.
- **No "Here's what/why/how" openers**, no throat-clearing ("Let me be honest," "The truth is"). Open in scene or with a named specific.
- **Max 1-2 em dashes per piece.** AI prose runs one every 50-80 words; human prose runs one per 500. Audit past two; replace with comma, period, or parenthesis.
- **Closes are image, declaration, or bookend — never a generic question.** No "So what will you choose?" Close on a picture, a stated fact, or a callback to the opener.
- **No banned AI moves**: "It's not X. It's Y." as closer/reveal; twin-sentence aphoristic endings; triple-beat anaphora ("You don't need X. You don't need Y. You need Z."); mic-drop-then-deflate; triple-list audience naming.
- **No AI slop vocabulary.** Kill on sight: delve, tapestry, landscape (metaphorical), leverage, robust, pivotal, realm, multifaceted, comprehensive, meticulous, beacon, intricate/intricacies. Replace by default: showcase, underscore, paramount, vibrant, unparalleled, garner, transformative, seamless, groundbreaking, game-changer, empower, streamline, elevate, optimize, synergy, foster, disruptive.
- **Anglo-Saxon over Latinate.** "Use" not "utilize."
- **Density over length.** Delete fluff, keep the fangs — virtuoso density mandate.
- **80/20 rule.** 80% core value, 20% interest flavor — if removing the flavor breaks the post, the foundation was shaky.
- **Never**: personal brand, content strategy, lead generation, influencer, growth hacking, hustle, grind, scale, funnel, monetize. **Use**: professional authority, sharing your expertise, attracting the right clients, recognized expert, building a system, sustainable practice, reach/impact, client pathway, serve/support.
- **Receipts-led, not persona-led.** Low emotional equity for personal-brand plays — every claim needs a number, name, or specific outcome attached.

## 4. FULFILLMENT ASSETS

What the system can actually do, in client-legible terms.

- **Voice-of-customer diagnostics** — pull the buyer's own words from calls, DMs, reviews, past conversations so messaging sounds like them, not marketing copy. Every quote verified against source before it ships.
- **Ideal-client mapping** — the real beliefs, fears, and 2am thoughts of one buyable client type, grounded in the coach's actual book of business, not a vague persona.
- **Offer design and pricing** — one clear, priced package with defined scope and outcome, instead of "it depends" pricing that stalls sales calls.
- **Messaging and positioning** — the one-line answer to "what do you do and why should I care," stress-tested against what the buyer resists (jargon, guru-speak) and what earns trust.
- **Copywriting for sales assets** — landing pages, DM scripts, call frameworks, follow-up sequences in the coach's real voice.
- **Content systems for visibility** — a repeatable way to post consistently that turns expertise into content without the coach becoming a full-time creator.
- **First-clients plan** — a concrete, dated plan for landing the first 2-3 paying clients from warm network and referrals, not a slow cold-funnel build.
- **Research and market grounding** — real, cited research on competitors, pricing benchmarks, and what's converting now — never guessed-at claims.
- **Ongoing execution support** — the system handles repeatable production (drafts, sequences, batches) so the human relationship stays on judgment calls and sales conversations.

## 5. COOZ FAILURE MAP

| Failure mode | What happened | Countermeasure |
|---|---|---|
| **Unbounded guarantee** | "Work with him until it happens" — no scope, no end date | Fixed-scope rungs only: Rung 0 Audit (48h), Rung 1 Sprint (~21 days). No open-ended "until it works" language. |
| **Architect did everything manually** | Farrice hand-executed every deliverable — content, strategy, outreach — no system doing repeatable work | The harness does repeatable production; Farrice's time goes to judgment and sales calls. |
| **Plane built mid-flight** | Offer, messaging, delivery model invented while the engagement was already running — no locked spine first | Strategic spine locked before outreach; refine inside it, never replace it mid-engagement. |
| **Client-reps gap** | Cooz was expected to execute reps (posting, outreach, follow-through) that never landed on his side | Rung 1 installs the first-clients plan as delivery, not homework; client's rep narrows to sales conversations only. |
