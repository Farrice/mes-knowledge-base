# WARM-DM-SCRIPTS.md — Alignment Sprint Launch, Warm Network

Six lists. Same voice on every one: a person texting a person. No pitch-slaps. The ask is always a conversation or an intro, never a close.

## The Transition Line

When any of these people ask "what have you been up to," this is the bridge — memorize it, don't read it:

> "I've started doing paid Alignment Audits — pulling coaches' real client language into one offer and one line that actually sells, 48-hour turnaround."

## The 3-Touch Cadence

- **Day 0**: the opener below.
- **Day 3**: one line, no re-pitch. Reference something they'd know you'd notice (their content, a mutual friend, the season). If they haven't answered, that's information, not an emergency.
- **Day 7**: the release touch. Give them the out. "No worries either way" is doing real work here — it's what makes the day-0 ask land as a conversation instead of a funnel.

If day 7 gets silence, let it go. Warm network isn't a sequence to exhaust; it's a relationship to keep.
