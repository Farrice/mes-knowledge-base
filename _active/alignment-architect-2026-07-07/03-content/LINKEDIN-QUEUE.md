### 4. Method — What an Alignment Audit reads (part 1: the process)

> An Alignment Audit is 90 minutes against three things you already have: real client conversations, testimonials, and call recordings. Not a hypothetical persona built from guesses.
>
> I read for the gap between what you actually do for people and what you say you do out loud. Almost every serious practitioner has one.
>
> I also read your pricing against your outcomes. "It depends" gets flagged every time it shows up on a sales call.
>
> Within 48 hours you get the diagnostic in writing: the gap, one pricing fix you can make this week, and a straight read on whether a bigger fix is worth your money.
>
> No slide deck. No pitch. Just the read.

### 6. POV/Pattern — The "It Depends" Pricing Tell

> Watch enough sales calls and the same tell shows up: the better the coach, the vaguer the price.
>
> Someone with years of real transformations behind them gets asked "what do you charge?" and answers with "it depends," followed by a string of qualifying questions before a number ever appears.
>
> The instinct makes sense. Every client really is different. But the buyer isn't shopping for nuance on a first call. They're shopping for a decision they can make today.
>
> One priced package with a defined scope moves the customization to session two, where it actually belongs, instead of stalling it on the first call.

### 12. Method — What an Alignment Audit reads (part 2: the four reads)

> What actually gets read in an Alignment Audit, in order.
>
> First, your last three client conversations. Where people said yes fast, where they stalled, and the exact words they used both times.
>
> Second, your pricing against your delivery. Every "it depends" gets circled.
>
> Third, your public language against your private language, the gap between what you post and what you actually say on a call.
>
> Fourth, your warm network, sized honestly, because the next 2–3 clients almost always come from people who already trust you.
>
> Four reads. One diagnostic. Written and in your hands within 48 hours.
