## 1. One Lane, Two Rungs

AA and Path A are not two businesses. They are two rungs of one ladder, reached through one door.

**Genuinely shared**: the vertical (health, fitness, wellness, transformation), the warm network the door opens through (Josh & Katie's gym world, Jen's brokerage contacts, Javier's circle, existing LinkedIn 1:1s), the fulfillment engine behind both, one LinkedIn presence publishing under one name, and the core insight — invisible expertise, dressed differently at each altitude. A solo coach can't say what they do. A funded brand can't say it in a way the FTC and an AI answer engine both accept. Same tear, two income brackets apart.

**Genuinely different, no rationalizing**: the buyer's wallet. AA sells to a practitioner spending their own money, $300-2,500 plus a capped $750-1,000/mo retainer, decided alone, often on one call. Path A sells to a funded brand where content, compliance, and AI-visibility sit in three separate budgets behind a champion who sells internally, at $5-25K/mo. AA closes fast on trust; Path A closes slow on procurement. Different cycle, different proof, different failure mode if the fusion pitch doesn't land.

**The bridge, stated plainly**: an AA client who lands their 2-3 clients and starts growing past solo-practitioner size is the exact profile who either raises funding, gets acquired by, or already has relationships inside a funded brand — the warm intro Path A's Warm-Network path (#2 in the PMF brief) is built to convert. AA is not a distraction from Path A. Run honestly, it is Path A's own contact-generation engine, one tier down.

## 3. Pre-Committed Conditions

**WIN**: 3+ paid audits collected, OR 1 sprint sold and collected plus 1 audit collected, inside the 30 days. If either bar clears, AA earns co-primary status alongside Path A and the council reconvenes within 5 days to re-sequence the shelf with AA no longer bounded as a test.

**KILL**: 30 days elapse, execution was real — **80%+ of the planned DMs across all four weeks (44 of 55) actually got sent** — and fewer than 2 paid audits collected. AA parks with a 90-day seal. The Path A contact protocol resumes untouched, using everything GROUND.md and OFFER.md built, no work discarded.

**The qualifier that matters**: a kill only counts if the reps happened. Under 44 DMs sent and under 2 audits sold is not a verdict on Alignment Architect — it is a verdict on whether the reps got run, and it gets logged as a different diagnosis, not a kill.
