# DELIVERY-SOP.md — Fulfillment Map: Alignment Audit & Alignment Sprint

Built to run inside 1–3 hrs/day, max 2 concurrent clients, alongside daily outreach for Farrice's own pipeline. Every hour below is real, not aspirational.

---

## Rung 0: Alignment Audit (48-hour turnaround)

| When | AI System Does | Farrice Does | Client Must Do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-book (gate) | — | — | Sends 3 real client conversations, testimonials, or call recordings. No material, no booking. |
| Day 1, morning | Ingests raw material, extracts verbatim buyer language, drafts ideal-client read, gap analysis (what they do vs. what they say), 2–3 pricing/packaging fix candidates | 15-min review of the AI draft before the call | Shows up to the 90-min session |
| Day 1, session | Structures session notes live | Runs the 90-min working session: pressure-tests the AI's read against the client, live | Participates |
| Day 1, evening | Drafts the written diagnostic from Farrice's synthesis notes | 30-min synthesis pass: picks the one sharpest gap, the one pricing fix, the Sprint-fit call — judgment, not automation | — |
| Day 2 (by 48h) | — | 30-min taste/edit pass, sends | Receives the diagnostic |

**Farrice hours per Audit: 2.75h total** (0.25 pre-call + 1.5 session + 0.5 synthesis + 0.5 edit/send), across two calendar days.

## Rung 1: Alignment Sprint (21 fixed days)

| Week | AI System Does | Farrice Does | Client Gate (pass/fail — sprint pauses if missed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Positioning** | Pulls real language from the Audit plus new material, drafts a buyer profile from the actual book of business, drafts 3–5 one-liner options stress-tested against jargon/guru-speak resistance | 30-min review of drafts + 60-min call: pressure-tests the buyer definition, picks the winning one-liner (pattern-read) | Sends warm-network list before the call. Missing it pauses the week — positioning can't lock against a buyer who doesn't exist on paper. |
| **2 — Offer** | Drafts 2–3 priced package options (scope, outcome) against researched pricing benchmarks | 45-min call: walks the options, decides, gets written approval | Approves the priced package **in writing** before any copy is built on it. No approval, no Week 3. |
| **3 — Messaging + Plan** | Drafts DM scripts, call framework, follow-up sequence, and a dated first-clients plan naming 2–3 targets from the warm list | 60-min call: reviews scripts and plan, taste-edit pass, sends the final packet + 30-min check-in on the client's sent messages | Sends the first 5 warm outreach messages themselves, using the script. The plan installs either way; the client fires the first shots. |

**Farrice hours per Sprint: 3.75h total** (1.5 + 0.75 + 1.5), across 21 days — roughly 15 minutes per business day on average.

**Gate-miss rule**: if a gate lands late, the Sprint clock pauses at that day and resumes within 24 hours of the gate arriving. No renegotiation, no chasing — the pause is written into the Sprint agreement before day one.

## The Math

Daily cap is 1–3 hrs, and that budget covers *everything*: Farrice's own outreach (warm DMs + one LinkedIn post, ~30–45 min/day) plus fulfillment for up to 2 concurrent clients.

- **Peak day** (an Audit session or a Sprint call): 1.5–2h fulfillment + 0.45h outreach = under 2.5h. Rung-0 sessions and Sprint weekly calls are calendar-staggered on booking — two live sessions never land on the same day — so the 3-hour ceiling holds even with 2 clients active at once.
- **Non-call days**: outreach only, ~30–45 min, split between AA warm DMs and Path A pipeline touches — see the carve-out below — with whatever's left going to intake a second Audit or draft ahead on an active Sprint.
- **Two Sprints running in parallel**, worst case (both in their heaviest call-week simultaneously): each client's own call still lands on a different day by design, so no single day stacks two live sessions. Weekly Farrice load across both stays under 3h combined.

The model holds at 2 concurrent clients. A 3rd concurrent client is the trigger to pause new intake, not to compress the hours below what judgment work actually takes.

**Path A carve-out, non-negotiable**: 15–20 minutes of every non-call day's outreach budget goes to Path A first — a warm-network touch, a pipeline follow-up, a piece of funded-brand outreach — before any spare minutes go to a second AA Audit. AA gets the leftover, not the other way around. Skip this and "one lane, two rungs" is a line in SCOREBOARD.md, not a fact about how the hours actually got spent.

## Exit Artifact (what the client keeps)

- **Audit**: one written diagnostic — the gap, the one fix, the Sprint-fit read. Theirs regardless of whether they book the Sprint.
- **Sprint**: the full packet — locked one-liner, priced package sheet, messaging bank (DM scripts, call framework, follow-up sequence), dated first-clients plan. The client owns it outright. Nothing in it requires Farrice to keep running for it to work.

## Anti-Burnout Clause — What Farrice Does Not Do Anymore

1. **Does not post or publish on the client's channels.** The system drafts; the client hits publish. *(Traces to: Architect did everything manually.)*
2. **Does not chase a client for a missed gate.** The clock pauses instead of Farrice absorbing the gap. *(Traces to: Client-reps gap.)*
3. **Does not offer unbounded revisions or an open-ended "until it happens" guarantee.** Bounded only — Audit fee credits to Sprint within 14 days; one defined week of refinement if the installed plan produces zero qualified conversations in 30 days. *(Traces to: Unbounded guarantee.)*
4. **Does not invent or reshape the offer mid-engagement.** The spine locks before outreach starts on any rung; new scope buys a new rung, it doesn't renegotiate the current one. *(Traces to: Plane built mid-flight.)*
5. **Does not run the client's inbox or become their content team.** Rung 2 is production support against a locked position, never a takeover of their voice or their conversations.
