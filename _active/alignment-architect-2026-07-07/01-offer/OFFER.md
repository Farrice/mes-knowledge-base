# OFFER.md — Alignment Architect Launch Offer

## The Promise

You already do the work. You change people's bodies and lives. What you don't have is the two-sentence answer to "what do you do," a priced package that makes a yes easy, or a dated plan for the next two or three clients. That's the gap your own words describe: *"I'm great at my work but terrible at marketing myself,"* *"I want a system, not another thing to figure out."* I pull your positioning, your offer, and your next-clients plan straight from your real client conversations, in your language, and install it in 21 fixed days, then step back. **You talk. We translate. Your authority grows.**

## Who This Is For

Health, wellness, and transformation experts (coaches, trainers, practitioners) with an existing book of business and a warm network, good enough at the work that clients stay, and bad enough at describing it that referrals are the only channel. You're not chasing your first client. You're tired of "it depends" pricing and shouting into the void with no read on what lands.

**Not for**: anyone starting with zero client history to pull language from. Anyone who wants a ghostwritten persona or a content factory (this installs a system, it doesn't run your inbox for you). Anyone who wants an open-ended "we'll figure it out together" engagement. Every rung here has a fixed scope and a stop date.

## The Ladder

**Rung 0: Alignment Audit ($300–500, paid to book the session).** A 90-minute working session against your real client conversations, testimonials, and calls, not a hypothetical persona. Within 48 hours you get a written diagnostic: the exact gap between what you do and what you say, one pricing or packaging fix you can make this week, and a clear read on whether the Sprint is the right next move.

**Rung 1: Alignment Sprint ($1,500–2,500, fixed 21 days, 50% deposit to start, balance due at the Week 3 sign-off call).**
- *Week 1, positioning locked.* Real language pulled from your client conversations, one buyer defined from your actual book of business, one-line answer to "what do you do" stress-tested against what your buyers resist.
- *Week 2, offer built.* One priced package, defined scope, defined outcome. No more "it depends" on sales calls.
- *Week 3, messaging bank and first-clients plan installed.* DM scripts, call framework, follow-up sequence in your voice, plus a dated plan to land your next two to three clients from people you already know.

**Rung 2: Retainer ($750–1,000/mo, capped at 3 clients, capped at 2 hours of Farrice time per client per month).** Monthly content and outreach production against the locked positioning, ongoing message refinement as real objections surface on calls, and sales-call prep before you talk to anyone. Your time stays on judgment calls and conversations; the system produces the repeatable work. Hit the 2-hour ceiling and the next month's scope gets renegotiated in writing, the same as any other rung — it doesn't quietly expand.

## Client-Does-The-Reps Gates

The sprint doesn't move without your reps. But your reps are narrow and bounded, not an open-ended job.
- **Before Rung 0**: you send 3 real client conversations, testimonials, or call recordings. No raw material, no diagnostic.
- **Week 1**: you send your warm-network list. Positioning can't lock against a buyer who doesn't exist on paper.
- **Week 2**: you approve the priced package in writing before any copy gets built on top of it.
- **Week 3**: you send the first 5 warm outreach messages yourself, using the script. The plan gets installed either way; you fire the first shots.

You do the reps. I hold the pattern.

## The Guarantee

Bounded, not open-ended. The Rung 0 Audit fee credits in full toward the Rung 1 Sprint if you book within 14 days. If you complete every rep gate on time through the Sprint and the installed first-clients plan hasn't produced one qualified sales conversation within 30 days of completion, you get one additional week of messaging refinement at no charge. One defined redo. Never "work with you until it happens."

## Offer Name Options

1. **The Alignment Sprint.** Keeps the method identity (Alignment Architect) while "Sprint" anchors it to a fixed 21-day scope, not a philosophy.
2. **The Practice Clarity Sprint.** Plainest description of the outcome, zero mystical language, easiest first read for a skeptical buyer.
3. **The 21-Day Client Pathway.** Names the timeframe and the deliverable (client pathway plan) directly in the title.

**Recommendation: The Alignment Sprint.** It carries the "Alignment Architect" identity forward without inventing a second brand to explain, and "Sprint" does the concrete work (fixed time, fixed scope, stated deliverable) that keeps the name grounded instead of mystical.
