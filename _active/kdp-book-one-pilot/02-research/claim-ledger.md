## Claim-control rules

- Every statistic keeps its population, year, reporting basis, and uncertainty.
- Recovery instructions link to the current official agency, bank, platform, or law-enforcement route and are refreshed at upload.
- No claim implies that a parent is incapable, that age causes gullibility, or that one tool guarantees safety.
- No legal, medical, investment, or account-control instruction ships without the appropriate boundary or qualified review.
- Interviews may provide scenes and language, never population statistics.
