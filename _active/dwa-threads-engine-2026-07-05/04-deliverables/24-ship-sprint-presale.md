## [Page copy begins]

### You didn't quit because you're lazy. You quit in the same week almost everyone does.

You bought the thing. Card ran, confirmation email hit, you told someone. For about four days that felt like enough.

Then came the week where nothing happened yet. No bite, no reply, no idea what to post next. The course sat there while you found a hundred reasons to open it tomorrow instead.

That week has a name in the data: **day 8 to day 10.** It's the exact window where a first-time seller is statistically most likely to go quiet and never come back. Not because the course was bad, and not because they ran out of information. They ran out of a reason to keep going before the first result landed.

**The 14-Day Ship Sprint is built to close that specific window.** Nothing else.

---

### The promise. Read it twice before you pay.

This is not an income product. It will not tell you how much you'll make, because nobody honestly can, and anyone who does is lying to you before you've even paid them.

**What it promises instead:** for 14 days, you get a located reason you're stalling, a 20-minute daily action sized to your actual calendar, and a small pod of people going through the identical week with you. So when day 8 hits and you want to quietly disappear, there's a system already built for that exact moment, instead of just your own willpower standing between you and closing the tab for good.

If you finish, you'll have shipped consistently through the week that ends most people's attempt. What that produces for you (a sale, a lead, nothing yet, a clearer no) is not something this page will promise, because the honest range in real seller data runs from days to five-plus months, and pretending otherwise is exactly the lie you've already been sold once.

### Who this is for

- You already bought something. A course, a resell license, a "start your shop" program, doesn't matter which. It's still mostly unopened, or opened once and stalled.
- You can say the skeptical thing about your own purchase out loud ("this might be a scam," "I don't know if this actually works") and you bought it anyway, cautiously, hedging. You're not naive about this. You're just stuck.
- You want a system that makes you take the next rep, not another module explaining the theory behind reps you're not taking.

### Who this is not for

- If you're looking for the next thing to learn, this isn't it. You don't have a knowledge gap, you have an execution gap, and this page won't pretend otherwise.
- If you want someone to tell you the income number this leads to, this is not your product. Nobody in this room will show you a screenshot, and if another program does, that's worth noticing.
- If you're not willing to be in a small pod with a handful of strangers doing the same 14 days (there is no video library to binge alone), this delivery format won't work for you, and buying it anyway would waste your $47.

---

### What's actually inside

**Day 0: find where you actually stalled. It starts the minute you pay.** The moment your payment clears, you get a 5-minute self-check that sorts you into one of four real stall types: blank page, tech overwhelm, fear of hitting publish, or posted-once-and-froze. Not when the pod opens. Tonight. You'll know where your last attempt actually died before you close the tab, and you get placed in a pod with people stalled the same way you are, not a generic group.

**Days 1–7: build the rep, not the willpower.** One 20-minute action block a day, scheduled against your real calendar, not a to-do list you'll ignore. And the block comes pre-loaded for your stall type: if you're blank-page, the first draft is already started and you're editing, not staring. If you're tech-stuck, the link and checkout setup is a follow-along walkthrough, not a support ticket. The 20 minutes is doing, because the part you'd normally burn 40 minutes dreading is handled before you sit down.

**Days 8–10: the danger zone, named and armed in advance.** This is the week the data says you're most likely to quietly stop. You'll know it's coming before you're in it, with a written pact and pod accountability already in place, and one rule for the moment you want out: you're allowed to quit for ten minutes — never quit for good in ten minutes.

**Days 11–14: keep the reps going, then an honest close.** No new material added this late, just continuing what's already working. Day 14 is a real check-in on where you landed, whichever way that goes.

**What day 14 does not promise:** a sale. What it does promise: you're still in this on day 14, which puts you ahead of most people who buy something and go quiet by day 10. That's the actual product.
