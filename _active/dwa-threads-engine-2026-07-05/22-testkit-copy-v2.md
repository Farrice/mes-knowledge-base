## What changed and why (read before posting)

**1. Lead product swap.** All 5 pieces now sell the **14-Day Ship Sprint ($47)**, not DWA. The verdict's own language is explicit and repeated: DWA survives *"never as cold Threads content, no content funnel built around it."* That is not softened anywhere in `21-GO-NO-GO-VERDICT.md`. So DWA does not appear in any of the 5 posts below — not as a link, not as an aside, not as a disclosed mention. If you want DWA to show up as "an option" per the original brief wording, the only verdict-compliant place for that is a **private DM/call to your named warm list of 20-40 former S&C clients and dad/mom-adjacent friends** — a separate motion this file does not script, covered by the verdict's "smallest flip test" (warm-outreach probe). Flagging this tension directly rather than quietly splitting the difference: the brief asked for "DWA as an option" inside these posts, the verdict says don't, and I followed the verdict.

**2. Belief target changed.** V1 fought the DWA skeptic's belief ("this is a pyramid, buying it makes me a fool to my network") — identity-protective, per the data resistant to cold one-way content. V2 targets **self-efficacy** ("I've started things before and not finished them — will this time be different?") — not identity-protective, not socially policed by a call-out subreddit, and per the verdict the class of belief cold content can actually move. This is an architectural bet, not a validated one — see the honesty flag below.

**3. ICP de-gendered.** V1's "Mark, 33" and "your son" framing centered a male stay-at-home-dad narrative. Real buyer-adjacent data is mom-skewed (mom-to-mom resale) and the corrected ICP is explicitly gender-neutral. Farrice's own lived detail (his son, naptime, the baby monitor) is real and kept — you can't fabricate a daughter — but every post now explicitly widens the frame to "night shift, sick parent, second job, whatever your thirty minutes looks like" so the copy doesn't silently code the reader as a dad.

**4. Credulous cohort still excluded.** The "send me the link" / zero-due-diligence cohort (confirmed bimodal in the data) is not addressed anywhere below — the Sprint explicitly disqualifies anyone chasing passive/hands-off outcomes, which filters them out by design, same as v1 intended for DWA.

**5. Real scarcity, not fake.** The 8-seat cohort cap is a real operational constraint from `24-ship-sprint-presale.md` (small-group stall-type matching breaks past 8 people) — stated as real and enforced by closing the form, never as a countdown gimmick.

**Honesty flag, carried forward from the verdict and not to be lost in the rewrite:** there is **zero direct demand evidence for the Ship Sprint** in this dataset. Its case is architectural — it fixes the mechanism-mismatch (movable belief) and the attribution-mismatch (same-session-plausible at $47) that killed DWA — not empirically validated. These 5 posts are themselves part of that validation pass, not proof it already passed. Read reactions/replies as data, not as confirmation.

**Compliance carried through every post below:** zero income claims, no screenshots, no invented guarantee, no fake scarcity (8 seats is real per the source doc), honest disqualifier present in every piece, vehicle-agnostic disclaimer where relevant, and — since the Sprint is Farrice's own product — plain "no affiliate link, no commission" stated rather than an affiliate disclosure (nothing to disclose; stating it anyway is a transparency signal).
