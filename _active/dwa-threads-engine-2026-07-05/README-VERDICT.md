## Reasoning

Four independently-run panel lenses (Lean/traction, positioning, deep-canvass persuasion, red-team) converged on PIVOT_VEHICLE via four different failure modes, which is stronger evidence than one high-confidence lens alone. I independently re-verified the two load-bearing facts myself: (1) `threads-native.txt` is a 1-line scrape-failure notice, not partial data — zero Threads-native evidence exists for the platform Farrice would actually run on; (2) grep for "dwa"/"digital wealth" across both 120-video TikTok digests returns zero hits despite ~40,000 affiliates sharing the identical link. Three fresh intel pulls stack coherently: reachability shows DWA has no visible organic footprint anywhere measurable; competitor-conversion shows the only reach-winning register in this niche (hype/comment-gate/income-flex) is the opposite of Farrice's anti-guru brand, with zero scaled anti-guru precedent anywhere in the sample; belief-movability shows the skeptic's core objection is identity-protective and socially policed (reinforced by dedicated call-out subreddits), and across 200 sampled items there is zero documented case of a cold stranger's content converting a DWA skeptic — every real conversion on record is warm/relational (mother-to-network, mom-to-mom). Session-only attribution is diagnosed as structurally blind to the one mechanism that might work (slow-burn warm conversion), meaning a working strategy and a dead one would look identical in Farrice's own reporting. This is not full NO_GO because the underlying skill match (18yr behavioral-change credential, confirmed-open anti-guru white space, correctly-identified bimodal/mom-skewed market) is real and Farrice-specific — the Ship Sprint targets a fundamentally more movable belief (self-efficacy, not identity-protective) with the exact tool he has, making this a genuine vehicle swap rather than a euphemism for no.

---

## Preserved Dissent

All four panel lenses raised the same counter-argument: the entire kill case targets COLD content specifically, but the data's own findings show the one channel that DOES convert DWA skeptics is warm/relational — exactly what Farrice has via 18 years of S&C clients and dad friends. Run as direct warm outreach (not cold Threads posts), the reach problem, the Threads-blind-spot, and the hype-wins-not-honesty problem all become moot because the play never depended on cold discovery. A handful of warm sales at $422 each could plausibly hit the low end of the $3K target. This dissent does not resolve the Incumbency Rule conflict (DWA is a new offer pre-$5K/mo) or the brand-risk of attaching Farrice's real reputation and real personal network to a scam-halo product — and a warm list is finite, it can't scale to a standing 7-12 sales/month without reverting to cold outreach. Preserved as a bounded, named exception in the reshape spec, not folded back in as the main plan.

**Note on sunk cost:** "He already has affiliate access" is not evidence of a market, and is deliberately excluded from the reasoning above. Access is a sunk asset, not a demand signal — the entire evidence base for PIVOT_VEHICLE is external reachability/belief data, not the fact that the DWA door is already open.

## Threads Caveat — Read Before Acting on Threads

Every reachability and competitor-conversion finding in this verdict is imported from TikTok/Reddit — the Threads scrape itself failed completely (`threads-native.txt` is a 1-line error notice: "Both web-actor attempts returned empty text... ZERO Threads-native data exists," verified directly, not a small sample). TikTok's algorithmic video FYP and Threads' reply/quote-driven text culture are mechanically different discovery systems. Every "confirmed" finding above should be read as **confirmed-on-TikTok-and-Reddit, unconfirmed-on-Threads** — treating cross-platform demand as proof Threads is reachable is an unearned leap the brief itself warns against. If Farrice runs anything on Threads regardless of this verdict, its first job should be generating the missing Threads-native data, not sales.

## Smallest-Flip Test

**Warm-outreach probe (~2 hrs):** Farrice directly DMs/calls 15-20 former S&C clients or dad friends about DWA — real conversation, not cold content. 2+ genuine interest signals or 1 sale within a week = real evidence the one channel this data supports (warm/relational) works for him specifically, unlocking the bounded DWA side-channel on its own terms.

**Alternative/complementary test:** Manual review of 10-20 real Threads accounts (~1-2 hrs) checking whether credentialed/anti-hype content earns reply/quote engagement there — if yes, the core "hype wins" finding (TikTok-derived) may not transfer to Threads and the reachability objection weakens.
