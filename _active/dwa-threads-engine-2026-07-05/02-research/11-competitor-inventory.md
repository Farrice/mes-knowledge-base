## The load-bearing verdict

**None of the three white-space layers is owned. Two are confirmed genuinely open; one is only thinly tested by this data.**

- **`antiguru_claimed`: false.** Every named competitor with real traction runs the standard playbook this thesis calls "burned": income-flex reveals (Marie's "$25k/week... drumroll"), faceless MRR/resell-rights kits (@faceless.boss.girl1), generic "6-figure" curiosity hype (@lavishdigitalco), or DM-gated urgency (@officialloucameron: "Comment INFO and I'll DM you the breakdown," 12/12 posts). Zero posts in either digest or the IG scrape name a coaching credential, screen buyers out, or connect the sale to a documented behavior-change mechanism. The one near-miss — @brianbrewermarketing's "$1,000/mo isn't enough for you" — is a status filter, not an integrity/readiness filter, and it actually cuts against the $1,000-first beachhead rather than validating a competitor already in that lane.
- **`sahd_claimed`: false, but weaker than the thesis assumed — the identity space is not silent, it's just not staffed by dads.** The SAHM persona is not hypothetical or occasional in this data — it's a **sustained, repeat-poster throughline**: @mamaclicks4 posts across at least 4-6 videos with `#wfhmom #workingmoms #sahmlife` as her identity, not a hashtag afterthought, and @simplykelsi1 runs `#sahmsidehustle` the same way. On the male side, the *closest* thing to a dad signal is @officialmatthenry's `#workfromhomedads` and @.loganbidwell's `#sidehustlefordads` — both single-video hashtag tags with **no caption content built around dad identity** — and @officialloucameron, a real, active, face-forward dad of 4 who talks about his kids and wife constantly but frames himself as a **freedom-flexing breadwinner who already escaped the job**, never as a **present-tense, caregiving-primary stay-at-home dad**. That's a materially different position than Farrice's "I get 30 minutes between a nap and a tantrum" framing. Verdict: the SAHD-specific identity is genuinely unclaimed, but the adjacent "dad who works from home and provides" territory has at least one active, real occupant (Lou Cameron) worth knowing by name before assuming a clean field.
- **`behavioral_claimed`: false.** Nothing in either 60-video digest or the 12-post IG scrape frames the offer around *why buyers quit* or ties the sale to a documented behavior-change methodology. The closest analogues are generic mindset lines — Lou Cameron's "you just haven't acted yet," "stop waiting for a better sign," "showing up consistently for ninety days moves her" — which gesture at consistency/follow-through but never name it as a trained skill, never cite a coaching background, and never pair it with a systematic diagnostic (the DWA "3 reasons you quit in week 2" mechanic has no analogue here).

**Where the white-space bet is weaker than assumed, stated plainly:** the thesis's own doc already flagged "male SAHD white space is directional, not measured" — this run confirms that caveat was correctly hedged. The identity is open, but it is open because almost nobody is playing it, not because it's a hot, proven-demand space with no supply — that distinction matters for expectation-setting. The behavioral/anti-guru layer, by contrast, is confirmed open with high confidence: every real post reviewed defaults to hype-and-DM-gate, and Lou Cameron — the single most-developed, most-family-coded, most consistently-posting competitor in this dataset — still runs on income-flex + comment-gate, not on any documented behavior-change credential or readiness screen. That's the strongest, most specific confirmation the whitespace thesis got this session: the exact operator most likely to already be "close" to Farrice's lane isn't there.

**One tactical counter-read surfaced by real data, not assumption:** Lou Cameron's funnel mechanic on IG is **comment-gate → DM**, not link-in-post. That's a different platform (IG, not Threads) so it's not a direct refutation of the Threads link-in-post bet, but it's evidence that at least one working competitor deliberately avoids the in-post link even where IG does allow bio links — worth testing both mechanics on Threads rather than assuming link-in-post wins by default per the thesis's own counter-read #1.
