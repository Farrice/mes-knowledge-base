## PRIVACY BOUNDARY — ABSOLUTE
Content may live the **pattern** (suppression, dimming, fear of being seen at full size, the "90% pattern" of stopping before full visibility) but must **NEVER narrate the private family autobiography** (mother/brother specifics, the "deep suppression wound"). Farrice has not cleared it. **"When in doubt, dramatize the feeling, not the family."** The softened public "father wound" language is a proxy — do not reach past it.

## The brand tension (hold this consciously)
Farrice's brand is **explicitly anti-hustle / anti-guru.** His prior ICP research even *bans* the words this offer sells on ("monetize," "hustle," "side income," "grind," "scale," "funnel"). **Do not resolve this by softening into hustle-speak.** Resolve it by making the anti-guru, behavioral-truth stance the *product*: he is the coach who tells you why you quit and screens you out, in a niche full of dream-sellers. Run correctly, the tension is the moat.

## Voice — required moves
- **Statement hooks, not question hooks** (cited ~52% conversion lift). Open IN the scene — the first 7–10 words ARE the moment.
- **Show, don't tell** at the sentence level. Stage the moment; don't describe the feeling.
- **Confession pivot** — drop an unexpected private detail mid-paragraph.
- **Reader-as-protagonist** for any bio/profile/offer/landing copy (never writer-as-monologue).
- **Pull-through** — engagement through the whole piece, not just the opener.
- Registers that are him: warm, direct, coaching-across-the-table, quiet defiance (no exclamation marks), emotionally precise without sentimentality, "here's what I've seen" not "here's what you must do."
- Voice-true closes: image close, declaration close, earned silence, naming what they feel, litotes, bookend admission.
- The test: *"Would Farrice say this to a smart friend over coffee?"* No → rewrite. Plus the wince test (if a friend would sense him *performing*, it's wrong).

## Voice — BANNED moves (treated as physical law; prose_classifier enforces)
- "Here's what / why / how…" openers · "Let's dive in" · "In a world where…" · "Picture this: / Imagine:" · "But here's the thing:"
- **"It's not X. It's Y." twin-reveals** · twin-sentence aphoristic endings · **triple anaphora** · italicized mid-paragraph aphorisms
- **> 2 em-dashes per piece**
- **Cheap question sign-offs** ("What's your version?", "Anyone else?", "Curious what you think") — banned even when they lift engagement. Use image / declaration / bookend closes instead.
- Status-words / defensive credentialing ("thought leader," "passionate about," "As an X, I…") · forced jargon that sounds smart and means nothing.
