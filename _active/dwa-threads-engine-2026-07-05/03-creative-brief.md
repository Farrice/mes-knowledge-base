# DWA Threads Engine — Master Creative Brief
_Test kit: face-forward, 5 posts, 3 spearhead angles + offer + integrity screen. Built from `00-ground-truth-compliance-and-offer.md`, `00-research-and-whitespace.md`, `00-farrice-voice-and-assets.md`. Every line below traces back to one of those three._

---

## 1. Positioning + Core Insight

**One-line positioning:** Farrice is the credentialed behavior-change coach who tells DWA buyers why they'll quit in week two and screens out the ones who won't do the work. Not another face chasing F-you money in public.

**The insight, in three moves:**

**Observation.** Forty thousand people are selling the identical $497 course through the identical link. They all look the same: a face, a screenshot, a countdown, the word "passive."

**Contrary truth.** The course was never the problem. Nobody who bought it and quit failed because the material was bad. They failed the same way every beginner fails a new gym program: no one taught them how to actually keep showing up.

**Point of view.** Farrice isn't selling the course. He's been running people through exactly this failure pattern for eighteen years, just in a squat rack instead of a Skool portal. He sells the reason you'll finish what you start, and he tells you honestly, upfront, if that's not going to be you.

That's the wedge. Not "buy the thing," but "here's why you'd quit the thing, and here's the coach who's watched that exact quit a thousand times and knows what actually prevents it."

## 2. Who / Belief / Bridge

**Who:** Mark, 33. Married, one or two young kids, a job that's fine and not thrilling: logistics, retail management, IT help desk, something with a badge and a lunch break. Household income in the mid-five-figures with no slack in it. He's on Threads on the couch after the kid goes down. He's bought a course before. He didn't finish it. He doesn't call himself lazy. He calls himself the guy who keeps starting things, which feels worse than lazy, because it means he already knows how this ends before he begins.

**What he believes right now:** *I'm not the kind of person this works for. I've tried this before and it didn't take. That's proof, not bad luck.*

**What he has to believe to buy:**
- This isn't the 41st AI-passive-income lie. It's a process, not a promise, and it's honest about what AI actually does.
- The goal is his first $1,000, not "quit your job." Small enough that failing doesn't humiliate him. Real enough that succeeding proves something his brain can't argue with.
- The reason he's quit before isn't a personality flaw. Follow-through is a trainable skill, and nobody ever coached him on it until now.
- If he buys and DMs the receipt, a real coach with eighteen years of practice hands him something extra. Not because he's special. Because that's the deal.
- He can run this in time he already has: commute, lunch break, the window after the kid's asleep. Not a second job.

**Bridge message:** *You don't need to believe you can build a business. You need one small proof your brain can't argue with, and a system built for the thirty minutes you actually have, not the eight hours you don't.*

**SAHD identity: texture, not arc.** Farrice's stay-at-home-dad reality shows up as proof of constraint ("built in naptime," "thirty minutes between a nap and a tantrum"). It's *evidence* that the system works inside a real, cramped schedule. It is never the story. The story is the coach and the behavior-change credential. If a draft starts explaining *how he became* a stay-at-home dad, or leans on the identity as the hook's center of gravity, that's a miss. Pull it back to texture.

## 3. Offer Mechanics (session-only attribution)

**The offer, in one line:** Buy DWA through my link. DM me the receipt. I send you my AI toolkit, free.

**Why this exact mechanic, under session-only attribution** [UNCONFIRMED whether truly session-only or short-cookie; build for session-only regardless, per ground-truth doc]:
- 40,000 affiliates share the identical product and the identical link. The only reason to click *his* link instead of the other 39,999 is the thing waiting on the other side of it: the toolkit.
- Session-only attribution means a delayed buyer, or a buyer who clicks a different link later, costs Farrice the credit entirely. So every post has to carry click-to-buy in one sitting. No "check the link in my bio," no nurture sequence, no "I'll follow up next week."
- Mechanically: **link lives in the post body** [LIKELY, practitioner-confirmed but not dataset-proven, that Threads doesn't throttle in-post links; this is one of the things the test kit exists to verify against the account's own text-post baseline], with the affiliate disclosure sitting right next to it, every time. If link-posts underperform text-posts over the first 5 to 10 posts, fall back to link-in-first-reply.
- The **pinned offer post is the whole funnel.** One post, not five steps. Every profile visit, from a reply, a DM, a shared post, lands on that pin and can convert in the same session.
- The **receipt-DM does double duty.** It confirms the sale actually routed to Farrice under an attribution model he can't fully verify from the outside, and it opens a live, warm conversation with a buyer who now has both the course and a reason to trust him: the seed of a future testimonial.

**Frame the toolkit honestly, every time it's mentioned.** It does not make money. It removes three specific things that make beginners quit: the blank page, tech overwhelm, and not knowing the next physical step. That's the entire claim. Anything more than that drifts toward an income claim.

## 4. Message Spine

**The rule that governs every piece:** a behavioral truth never runs alone. Every post pairs the psychological insight with a concrete money-or-action mechanic in the same breath. Insight without a mechanic reads as therapy, and a money-buyer scrolling Threads at 11pm didn't come for therapy. He came because he's tired of starting things. [This pairing is the named mitigation in the research doc for the "reads as therapy" risk. It is not optional texture; it is the load-bearing rule.]

**The spine, concretely:**
1. Name the real reason he quits (behavioral truth — this is the credential doing its job).
2. Attach it to something he can act on today, in the DWA system specifically (the mechanic: course module, toolkit function, the $1,000 target, the 30-minute split).
3. Land the offer or the disqualifier, with disclosure where a link appears.

A post that only diagnoses ("here's why you quit") and never hands him a next physical action fails the spine, no matter how sharp the insight. A post that only pitches the offer with no behavioral truth underneath it is indistinguishable from the other 39,999 affiliates. It fails the whole thesis.

## 5. The Five Posts: Angles, Offer, Integrity Screen

Three spearheads (the core belief-shift), one offer post (the funnel), one integrity screen (the trust mechanic). Each has a distinct job. None repeats another's belief.

**Spearhead A: The Adherence Coach.**
*Belief:* It was never the course. Follow-through is a trainable skill nobody taught you.
*Job in the kit:* Establishes the credential as the actual product. This is the anchor post; if only one post is judged as "on-thesis," it's this one. Behavioral truth = the week-two quit pattern. Mechanic = naming that DWA's system has a structure for exactly this, and the link.

**Spearhead B: Stay-at-Home Dad, Not a Guru.**
*Belief:* You don't need a nap-time-mom schedule or a Lambo-bro hustle. Thirty focused minutes counts.
*Job in the kit:* Delivers the SAHD white-space texture without turning it into the arc. Behavioral truth = constraint isn't the enemy, inconsistency is. Mechanic = the actual 30-minute daily split (reply sprint, one post, DMs) from the fast-first-sale playbook.

**Spearhead C: Progressive Overload for Income.**
*Belief:* Stop trying to deadlift $5K on day one. Run a beginner program.
*Job in the kit:* Carries the $1,000-first beachhead, the belief-unlock framing that lets Farrice use his gym-programming logic natively instead of borrowing hustle language. Behavioral truth = trying to hit the end-state goal on rep one is why people get hurt and quit. Mechanic = the first $1,000 as the specific, small target the system is built around.

**Offer post: First $1,000 = the Belief Unlock (pinned).**
*Belief:* The money isn't what changes. The proof is.
*Job in the kit:* This is the funnel, not a spearhead. It's built to sit pinned and convert same-session off of every profile visit the other four posts and the reply sprint generate. Carries the full offer mechanic (link, disclosure, receipt-DM-for-toolkit) explicitly. Every other post's job is to get eyes on this one.

**Integrity screen: The Coach Who Tells You Not to Buy.**
*Belief:* A real coach disqualifies people. A hustler sells everyone.
*Job in the kit:* This is the trust mechanic that makes the face-forward bet survive scam-radar. Farrice states plainly who this is not for (anyone wanting passive money, anyone who can't give 30 honest minutes a day) before saying who it's for. Disqualifying is not a rhetorical trick here. It's the compliance-safe, brand-true version of a conversion lever, and it's the post most likely to get replies, which is the currency the reply-native cold start runs on.

## 6. Guardrails at a Glance

**Voice, non-negotiable:**
- Statement hooks only. Open in the scene: the first 7 to 10 words *are* the moment. No question hooks as openers.
- Never "Here's what/why/how," never "It's not X. It's Y.," no triple anaphora, no italic mid-paragraph aphorisms.
- Max 2 em-dashes per post, total.
- No cheap question sign-offs ("What's your take?"). Close on an image, a declaration, or an earned silence.
- No status-words, no "As a coach, I..." credentialing crutch. The credential shows up as lived detail (naptime, eight years, the rack), never as a title he announces.
- Reader-as-protagonist in the offer and profile-facing copy: it's about what happens in Mark's week, not a monologue about Farrice's.
- The test for every draft: would Farrice say this to a smart friend over coffee. If a friend would feel him performing, rewrite it.

**Compliance, non-negotiable:**
- Zero income claims. No dollar figures for potential earnings, no "$X/month," no "you'll make." The $1,000 language refers only to the beachhead *target the buyer is working toward*, never a promised outcome.
- No income or earnings screenshots, ever.
- Affiliate disclosure at or before every link, plain language: "Affiliate link. I earn a commission if you buy through it, at no extra cost to you."
- No fake scarcity, no countdowns, no manufactured urgency. The only urgency is real and structural: click *this* link now or the toolkit isn't attached to your purchase.
- No invented guarantees. Only DWA's own official terms, never Farrice's language layered on top.
- Banned niche words carried over from Farrice's existing ICP research: hustle, grind, scale, passive income (used uncritically), escape the 9-to-5, fire your boss, financial freedom, guru, "proven method," "anyone can do this," "no experience needed."

**Privacy, absolute:** dramatize the pattern (suppression, dimming, fear of being seen, quitting before full visibility), never the family autobiography. Mother and brother specifics stay off the page in every post, no exceptions, no matter how well a line would land.

**Confidence labels: carry them into copy decisions, never into the copy itself.** The Fanbasis/payout detail, the truly-session-only-vs-short-cookie question, and the Threads no-throttle claim are UNCONFIRMED or LIKELY, not proven. Build every mechanic so it works whether or not each holds. Never state any of the three as settled fact to the reader, and never let an unverified detail become a claim in the post itself.

## 7. Success Definition

**The real KPI: receipt-DMs and sales.** Nothing else in this kit matters if it doesn't move that number. A post that gets replies but never turns into a DM with a receipt attached hasn't done its job yet. It's done half of it.

**Leading indicators, in order of how directly they predict the KPI:**
1. **Profile visits** off replies and posts. The top of the same-session funnel; without this, the pinned offer post never gets seen.
2. **Saves.** A save signals someone intends to come back and act, which matters more here than a like, given the entire mechanic depends on converting within a session rather than over time.
3. **Replies.** The currency of the reply-native cold start; also the direct evidence for or against the integrity-screen post's job.
4. **DMs.** The step immediately before a sale; track separately from receipt-DMs so it's visible when conversations start but don't close.

**What the 5-post test kit is actually testing, beyond conversion itself:**
- Whether Threads in-post links convert without throttling, against the account's own text-post baseline (the ground-truth doc's stated open question).
- Whether the behavior-change/coach frame reads as intended, or drifts toward "therapy," watched for directly in reply sentiment on Spearhead A and C.
- Whether the integrity-screen post generates the reply volume the reply-native strategy depends on.

If receipt-DMs stay at zero after the kit runs but profile visits and saves are strong, the read is a funnel problem (offer post, disclosure placement, or DM close), not a belief problem. If profile visits themselves are weak, the read is a hook problem in the spearheads, not an offer problem. Diagnose in that order before rewriting anything.
