## Why This Is the Right Frame

Behavioral insight (follow-through is trainable, nobody trained you) is paired with the concrete money/action mechanic (the $497 course as the skill, the AI toolkit as the friction-remover for the excuse that kills adherence) in the same chain — never introspection alone. Post 2→3 is the pivot: name the behavioral gap, then immediately name the mechanic that closes it. This satisfies the mitigation for the "reads as therapy" risk flagged in `00-research-and-whitespace.md` counter-read #2.

The SAHD detail ("the kid's finally down") appears exactly once, as sensory texture inside the 11pm-collapse image — it is not the subject of the post and never becomes the arc. The coaching credential and the behavioral mechanism carry the whole piece, consistent with the Frame 1 instruction to fold SAHD in as supporting texture only.

The disqualify line in Post 4 ("If you can't give this thirty honest minutes a day, don't buy it") does double duty: it's the honest-disqualification compliance lever from `00-ground-truth-compliance-and-offer.md` §6, and it's the credibility proof that this isn't a hustler selling everyone.

No dollar figure tied to potential earnings appears anywhere ("the number everyone in this space screams about" deliberately avoids naming it, even to disclaim it) — this is a deliberate tightening beyond the angle bank's original $5K/$1K framing, because the compliance ground-truth's "no earnings numbers" rule reads as absolute and takes precedence over the strategy doc's rhetorical use of dollar figures.
