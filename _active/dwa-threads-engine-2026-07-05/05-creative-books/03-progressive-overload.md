**Angle:** C (spearhead) — Progressive Overload for Income
**Frame:** Frame 1 — Anti-Guru Behavioral-Change Truth-Teller, SAHD identity as supporting texture (never narrative arc)
**Format:** Text, 4-post Threads chain
**Compliance:** No link in this post. No income claims. Disclosure lives on the pinned offer post this chain routes to.

## THE HOOK (line 1)

> Week one, every new client loads the bar to someone else's max, then acts surprised when their back gives out.

Statement hook. Opens in the literal scene (a beginner loading a barbell) before the reader knows this is about money at all — the S&C credential does the work of the reveal instead of a "here's why" preamble.

---

## POST 1 (root post)

```
Week one, every new client loads the bar to someone else's max, then acts surprised when their back gives out.

I've watched that exact move for eighteen years, just never with money attached until now.

Someone chases the number this whole space screams about, the big monthly figure, and tries to hit it in his first thirty days. He doesn't get injured. He just quits around week two and decides he's not built for this. The fix is the same one I'd use on the bar.
```

## POST 2 (reply 1 — the mechanic)

```
I coach it the way I'd coach a squat: pick a weight you can actually move today, then add one plate at a time. The starting weight isn't the big number. It's your first sale, the smallest rep that still counts. That's the one your brain will actually believe.
```

## POST 3 (reply 2 — the friction + the toolkit + SAHD texture)

```
Most people never even get to that first sale for reasons that have nothing to do with effort. Blank page. Tech they don't understand. No idea what to actually do at nine tonight. Friction, not motivation. Those are the three things I built my AI toolkit to strip out before they cost someone the rep.

(Writing this one-handed. Kid's monitor's on the desk. Naptime's the only gym I get most days.)
```

## POST 4 (reply 3 — disqualify + close, routes to pinned post)

```
If you want money that shows up while you sleep, this isn't for you. If you can give thirty honest minutes between naps and load one plate at a time, it is.

It's Digital Wealth Academy, and I built a toolkit on top of it. The link, the honest price, and the disclosure are all on the pinned post. Go load your first plate.
```

## Why this shape

- **Behavioral insight + concrete mechanic, same piece:** the bar-loading pattern (why beginners quit) is paired directly with two hard mechanics — the $1,000 starting-weight number and the AI toolkit's specific job (kill blank page / tech overwhelm / no-next-step). Never introspection alone, per the "reads as therapy" mitigation.
- **SAHD as texture, not arc:** one parenthetical, one post, zero story about the day. It confirms the visible-face/caregiver identity from the white-space map without turning the post into a dad-life narrative.
- **Disqualify honestly:** Post 4 screens out the "Lambo by June" reader before it screens anyone in — the integrity-screen move (angle G) folded into the close instead of run as a separate post.
- **No link, no disclosure needed here:** this chain's entire job is profile visits. The pinned offer post carries the link + inline disclosure per the reconciled-offer mechanic.
- **Bookend close:** opens on loading a bar, closes on "Go load your first plate" — image/declaration close, no question, no cheap sign-off.

## Face-forward shot note (naptime window)

Static or semi-static talking shot, natural light, home — not a gym. Sit or stand near where he actually works (desk, kitchen counter), toddler monitor visibly in frame or audible, no gym set dressing. The credential is spoken, not staged with equipment — a barbell prop would tip into "guru with a prop," which the frame is built to avoid. If shooting a companion clip: hold on his face for the whole first line before any cut, deliver it flat and certain, the way you'd actually correct a client's setup — no punch-in, no zoom, no captions doing the emphasis for him.
