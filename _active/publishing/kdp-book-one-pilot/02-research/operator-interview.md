# Book One Operator Interview

Captured: 2026-08-04  
Status: open; private input required

## Already locked from the brief

Book One is one useful work of nonfiction, not the start of an automatic catalog. It will use a market-first pen name without invented credentials or experience. The first launch assumes no audience and no paid ads.

AI may speed up research, structure, drafting, editing, and design. A real reader still has to find the work useful and human. The manuscript, cover, listing, organic launch, and evidence loop stay connected. No KDP action occurs without separate approval, and the goal of thousands per month remains an ambition until money is actually collected.

## Private evidence still needed

Answer plainly; “none” is useful and safer than borrowed authority.

1. Describe any first-hand experience with older relatives, scams, caregiving, consumer technology, cybersecurity, or helping nontechnical people.

2. Name any mistake, conversation, or small win that could become an honest example. Identifying details can stay private.

3. Would you permit five short research interviews before the full manuscript: three adult children and two adults over 60? No outreach will occur without separate permission.

4. Mark any subject that is off-limits, including cognitive decline, banking controls, romance scams, family conflict, or recovery after loss.

5. Choose the author voice: calm and protective, blunt and urgent, warm and family-centered, or another direction.

6. Point to a nonfiction book, writer, article, or teacher that represents your standard for clarity and humanity.

7. Decide whether Book One may have both Kindle and large-print paperback editions, or whether the first release must be ebook-only.

8. Set the amount of time you can give over the next 14 days for decisions, interviews, and edits.

## Authorship boundary

If the operator has no direct experience in the selected lane, the book must be framed as a carefully sourced practical system rather than expert testimony. Originality must come from synthesis, drills, decision tools, interviews, editorial judgment, and usability testing. It cannot come from invented authority.

## Decision dependency

Market research may continue while these answers are open. The outline and gold chapter cannot be approved until the voice, experience boundary, interview permission, and format decision are known.
