## Reading Rule

- BSR, ASIN, format, book age, page count, ratings, reviews, dimensions, and product dates are observed fields.
- Daily sales and daily royalties are CoAuthor estimates. They are `MODELED`, not verified author income.
- Kindle Free Rank is excluded from paid-demand counts.
- BSR is volatile. History is stronger evidence than one current reading, but neither proves future demand.
- Reviews show attention and reader language. They do not prove organic acquisition, and no manipulation claim is made.

## Reader-Language Findings

Visible reviews on the top paperback praised:

- clear photos and written instructions;
- common-error and modification guidance;
- 15-, 30-, and 45-minute options;
- a day-by-day plan that felt approachable after a layoff.

The practical 40–60+ book received praise for level-based routines, pictures, short sessions, and progressions. Its visible histogram was 90% five-star, 6% four-star, 2% three-star, and 2% two-star. The top book's visible histogram was 86% five-star, 13% four-star, and 1% three-star.

Critical-review pages required Amazon sign-in, so this run does not claim a complete negative-review corpus. The visible review set included both verified-purchase and unverified reviews. Review quality remains a caution, not an accusation.

## Product Implication

This cluster is not rewarding a text-only Kindle guide. It is rewarding large-format visual training products. Book One should use one source manuscript but ship as:

1. an illustrated 8.5×11 paperback-led product;
2. a separately reflowed Kindle edition;
3. no full upload or launch until both formats pass their own usability checks.
