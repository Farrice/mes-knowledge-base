## The short answer

This market is real, but the opportunity is not “use AI to make another military calisthenics book.” The opportunity is to take a market already paying $23.95–$24.99 for large-format illustrated workout manuals and beat the category on **trust, progression logic, and distribution**.

The 80/20 product is:

- a 120–136 page, 8.5 × 11 inch, black-and-white paperback;
- a 6-week program built around movement-specific starting rungs;
- original human exercise photography converted into a consistent photo-plus-diagram system;
- a qualified exercise professional reviewing the program and safety language;
- a $24.99 paperback with a $9.99 Kindle test;
- Amazon as the transaction engine, supported by a 90-day original-content engine outside Amazon.

The build should stop if we are unwilling to obtain original human imagery and qualified program review. Without those, we would be making a commodity copy with no durable advantage.

## What the leading book actually sells

The Amazon sample for Dominic Mann's 95-page book reveals the actual product architecture:

1. A 28-day plan followed by a second 28-day progression.
2. Most weeks contain four workout days, two active-recovery days, and one rest day.
3. Every workout is assembled from three 15-minute blocks:
   - **Main Mission:** a complete first 15 minutes;
   - **Bonus Block 1:** extends the session to 30 minutes;
   - **Bonus Block 2:** extends it to 45 minutes.
4. An illustrated warm-up and cool-down.
5. An exercise library with approximately 30 movements.
6. Each exercise page includes a visual sequence, instructions, common errors, a QR video, an easier version, a joint-friendly version, and a harder version.
7. The front matter teaches pain versus effort, how to modify, and how to count timed work.
8. The four-week labels are Boot Camp, Build, Forge, and Final Push; the second cycle is Advance, Overload, Steel, and Final Assault.

That is why the book behaves like a physical training tool rather than a reading experience. The buyer is paying to remove uncertainty during a workout.

## Why the $24.99 price works

The premium is not coming from literary quality or page count. It comes from five pieces of perceived utility:

1. **Large-format usability.** The book can lie open and be read from the floor.
2. **Visual certainty.** The reader sees the movement rather than translating prose into motion.
3. **Daily prescription.** The calendar removes programming decisions.
4. **Modification density.** Easier and joint-friendly options reduce fear of getting hurt.
5. **Time modularity.** A short session is framed as complete rather than as failure.

The CoAuthor model estimates roughly $12.68 royalty per unit for the leading title. That is consistent with, but does not verify, KDP's 60%-of-list-price-minus-printing-cost formula. CoAuthor revenue remains a model, not a bank receipt.

Our $24.99 price becomes defensible only if the interior feels like a real field manual: original imagery, clean exercise cards, durable binding-friendly margins, printable tracking pages, and a coherent progression. A thin prose book with stock or AI exercise figures would not deserve the price.

## Honest imagery verdict

Use **original human photography for every instructional movement**. Use AI only for concept exploration, non-instructional textures, or background ideation.

The best production system is hybrid:

1. Photograph one consistent 50–65-year-old male model in a half-day or full-day shoot.
2. Capture start, midpoint, and finish frames for every movement, plus common-error frames.
3. Convert selected photos into simplified high-contrast diagrams when a cleaner instruction is useful.
4. Add human-designed arrows, joint markers, stance lines, and captions.
5. Link QR codes to short videos from the same shoot.

If Farrice is visibly within the reader's age range, performs the movements well, and wants the book to build his personal authority, using his own imagery is a real advantage. If he is not visibly in the audience's age range, he should be the author/director, not the hero model. Hire an age-matched model and obtain a model release and photographer/IP assignment.

Do not use AI-generated anatomy for exercise instruction. Even when it looks plausible, small joint, grip, and weight-distribution errors can make a health-performance product less trustworthy and potentially unsafe.

## The differentiated workout mechanism

The category usually assigns the entire reader one global level. Our stronger mechanism is **movement-specific starting rungs**: a reader may be Foundation on push, Build on squat, and Advance on balance. Progress happens movement by movement.

### Six movement families

1. **Squat / rise:** chair sit-to-stand → supported squat → bodyweight squat → split squat.
2. **Hinge / hip:** bridge → hip-hinge drill → staggered hinge → supported single-leg hinge.
3. **Push:** wall push-up → counter push-up → knee/floor push-up → standard push-up.
4. **Pull / posture:** wall slide or prone W → towel isometric row → optional band row → stronger band row.
5. **Brace / carry:** wall plank → counter plank → floor plank or dead bug → loaded carry if available.
6. **Balance / capacity:** supported march and calf raise → step patterns → supported single-leg work → faster low-impact intervals.

The optional resistance band is a deliberate honesty decision. A strict “zero equipment” promise makes meaningful pulling progress difficult. The base plan can work with a chair, wall, towel, and floor; a $15 band materially improves program completeness.

### The 20-minute Field Session

- **2 minutes, Readiness and ramp:** pain check, breathing, and two movement rehearsals.
- **12 minutes, Strength Grid:** four movements, 40 seconds of controlled work and 20 seconds to transition, repeated for three rounds.
- **4 minutes, Balance or work-capacity block:** supported balance, marching, step patterns, or low-impact conditioning.
- **2 minutes, Downshift and log:** breathing, effort score, pain note, and next-rung decision.

On a compressed day, the 12-minute Strength Grid is the complete minimum session. A 10-minute optional extension adds pulling, walking, or mobility; it does not turn the base session into an apology.

### Weekly rhythm

| Day | Work |
|---|---|
| Monday | Field Session A: squat, push, hinge, brace |
| Tuesday | 10-minute walk + balance micro-session |
| Wednesday | Field Session B: split stance, pull/posture, hip, capacity |
| Thursday | Recovery walk or complete rest |
| Friday | Alternating Field Session A/B |
| Saturday | Optional mobility + real-life capacity challenge |
| Sunday | Full rest and one-minute review |

### Six-week progression

| Week | Job | Progression rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1: Find Your Rungs | Learn positions and establish starting versions | Stop every set with clear reserve; no testing to failure |
| 2: Own the Pattern | Repeat the same rungs with better control | Add clean reps or slower lowering, not both |
| 3: Add Range | Progress one or two movement families | Move only one rung at a time |
| 4: Build Volume | Increase productive work | Add a round or reps only where recovery is good |
| 5: Build Capacity | Reduce idle time and integrate balance | Keep technique; do not chase exhaustion |
| 6: Demonstrate | Repeat baseline tasks and select the next cycle | Compare quality, control, and capacity, not maximal pain |

Before publication, a qualified exercise professional must review exercise selection, contraindications, progression language, and all safety/medical claims. The evidence base supports multicomponent activity for older adults, including aerobic, muscle-strengthening, and balance work; the book still needs program-level review rather than a disclaimer alone. [US Physical Activity Guidelines](https://health.gov/paguidelines/second-edition/pdf/Physical_Activity_Guidelines_2nd_edition.pdf), [NSCA position statement](https://www.nsca.com/about-us/position-statements/resistance-training-for-older-adults/), [2024 resistance-circuit meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38627495/)

## Cover verdict

### A: Authentic photo-led editorial

Strongest immediate sales direction. It proves there is a real human behind the system. Replace the generated concept subject with original age-matched photography. Keep the warm paper, charcoal type, signal-orange rule, and handwritten training notation.

### B: Modern field manual

Strongest brand-system direction. It can carry directly into exercise cards, social carousels, videos, and A+ Content. Its weakness is emotional distance if used without any human photography.

### C: Type-dominant brutalist

Strongest Amazon thumbnail. Its weakness is that it could be mistaken for another designed information product with no human authority.

### Final art direction

Use **A's original human photograph with B's field-manual information system**. Use C only as a promotional/social style. The generated board is direction, not final art, and any AI-generated cover element would have to be disclosed to KDP.

## Distribution finding

The visible leading-book engine is Amazon-first:

- paperback and Kindle on Amazon;
- Kindle Unlimited, which confirms KDP Select digital exclusivity;
- Amazon search/category placement;
- a high-converting detail page and Look Inside sample;
- Goodreads and third-party retailer/price-tracker echoes.

Targeted searches did not reveal a credible exact-match Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, owned website, or author-led content channel for the three leading author/title combinations. Search results were dominated by Amazon/Goodreads/retail mirrors and unrelated people with the same names. This is evidence of **no discoverable off-Amazon content footprint**, not proof that no promotion exists.

Amazon pages also show sponsored books in this category, so Amazon Ads are present in the market. We cannot see which books' publishers are buying ads or what their economics are.

## Our distribution model

Use Amazon for the sale, but do not make Amazon the only source of demand.

### Pre-launch: 14 days

- Publish 8–12 real-image content tests around the starting-rung mechanism.
- Build one simple waitlist page and a free one-page “Find Your Starting Rung” scorecard.
- Recruit 15–25 neutral advance readers from the actual age/audience range; never require or reward a review.
- Photograph and film the exercise library before full manuscript production.

### Launch: days 1–30

- Publish paperback + Kindle together.
- Test KDP Select for the first 90-day Kindle term because the category leader is using KU; do not distribute the full digital book elsewhere during the term.
- Complete Author Central and A+ Content using original photography and the movement-rung visual system.
- Release three short videos per week and one longer demonstration per week.
- Use one attributable link per channel; do not turn view counts into claimed book sales.
- No ad spend until the listing, sample, and organic conversion signals are usable.

### Sustain: days 31–90

One book page becomes multiple original assets:

- one movement-rung short video;
- one common-error video;
- one 20-minute follow-along session;
- one carousel or email explaining the decision rule;
- one reader question/answer clip.

The five recurring content pillars are Starting Rungs, Pain vs Effort, Form Corrections, 20-Minute Field Sessions, and Real-Life Strength After 50. The book becomes the organized implementation of the content, not a disconnected product link.

## War game

| Failure | Early warning | Countermove | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commodity AI clone | Cover/interior could belong to any pen name | Real model, real reviewer, ownable title | No access to real imagery or review |
| False military authority | Copy implies service or tactical credentials | “Civilian” in title and explicit boundary | Any plan requiring fabricated credentials |
| Unsafe progression | Generic levels or AI anatomy errors | Movement rungs + qualified review + claim ledger | Reviewer rejects core program |
| Premium-price mismatch | Prose-heavy sample, low visual density | 24 two-page exercise cards and visible dashboards | Sample does not prove utility |
| No distribution | Activity without email clicks or sales | Attributed content links and weekly receipt | 90 days with no meaningful discovery-to-sale signal |
| Review dependence | Rank collapses without a launch spike | Search conversion + content + neutral readers | Success model requires policy-risk behavior |
| Scope creep | Manuscript becomes a 250-page fitness encyclopedia | 136-page hard cap and one promise | New chapters do not change workout behavior |
| Revenue distraction | Book consumes the main client-revenue lane | 21-day pilot, fixed production budget, explicit gates | It delays higher-probability cash work without proof |

## 21-day build sequence

### Days 1–3: Lock the product

- Lock title/subtitle and civilian boundary.
- Name the audience and exclusions.
- Hire/confirm the qualified reviewer.
- Lock the movement families, starting rungs, and session architecture.

### Days 4–7: Prototype before writing

- Create one finished two-page exercise card.
- Create one finished weekly dashboard.
- Test the 20-minute session with real users from the audience.
- Revise the visual and instruction system before producing 24 copies of it.

### Days 8–11: Original image capture

- Shot list, model release, photographer/IP agreement.
- Capture stills and QR-video demonstrations in one production block.
- Select images and verify every movement frame.

### Days 12–17: Manuscript and layout

- Draft front matter, gold chapter, exercise cards, six-week dashboards, and toolkit.
- Maintain a claim ledger.
- Send the complete program to the reviewer and resolve every material note.

### Days 18–21: Product and launch package

- Paperback PDF and separate Kindle file.
- Cover, description, keywords, categories, Author Central, and A+ Content pack.
- 30-day content calendar from the finished movement library.
- Print proof and device previews before any publication decision.
