## Brand Identity (One Paragraph)

**Parallax** — "For people who see everything from more than one angle." A Substack edition + LinkedIn presence for cross-domain thinkers, multi-cultural / multi-disciplinary professionals who hold multiple legitimate frames at once. Memoir-grade interiority. Show, don't tell. The reader is the protagonist; Farrice enters as the answer to a problem the reader can feel. Voice is calibrated, warm but uncompromising, emotionally precise without being sentimental.

## Voice Rules (Non-Negotiable — From MEMORY.md)

These rules are SOURCE-OF-TRUTH for Farrice's voice. They override any default Antigravity content workflow that suggests otherwise.

### Banned phrase-level moves
- ❌ "Here's what/why/how..." openers
- ❌ More than 2 em dashes per piece
- ❌ "It's not X. It's Y." reveals
- ❌ Twin-sentence aphoristic endings
- ❌ Triple-beat anaphora
- ❌ Italicized mid-paragraph aphorisms
- ❌ "Here is the part nobody..." framings
- ❌ Mic-drop + deflation endings
- ❌ Cross-piece rhythm repetition

### Banned closing patterns
- ❌ Generic question signoffs ("What's your version?", "Who was it for?", "Anyone else?")
- ✅ Use voice-true alternatives: image close, declaration close, bookend admission, concrete future image, litotes, naming what they feel
- ✅ Earned questions are OK (advance the specific argument, non-transferable)

### Required moves
- ✅ Show > Tell at the sentence level
- ✅ Pull-through (hooks + engagement through ENTIRE piece, not just opener)
- ✅ Reader-as-protagonist for About sections / profile copy (NOT writer-as-monologue)
- ✅ Open with statement hooks (52% conversion lift over questions — see Notes Trailer Playbook)
- ✅ Content ecosystem awareness: never reuse language across pieces in the same window

- **Banned structural tells** apply at the MOVE level, not just phrase level. Cross-piece rhythm variance is enforced (no two pieces in a window share closing structure).
- **Thought-bank capture is deterministic (2026-07-06).** When Farrice dumps a thought in conversation, run `python3 execution/thought_bank.py capture "<verbatim text>" [--theme <t>]` — it appends to `inbox/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, dedupes, and mirrors into sovereign memory for weekly distill. `/dump` routes through the same writer; a nightly episodic backstop sweeps missed markers. Silent capture stays the trigger, but the SINK is the script — never a bare file write (per `feedback_ai-memory-dependent-observability.md`).
- **Voice authenticity > engagement optimization.** Cheap-question closes that optimize for comment count are banned even when they would lift metrics.

## Anti-Patterns Specific to This Project

- ❌ "Structurally sound but flat" drafts (5/10 max). Structure without heartbeat = lecture.
- ❌ Talking-about-the-writer About sections — reader-as-protagonist is non-negotiable for profile/landing copy
- ❌ Forced jargon ("dangerous in a room") — voice test is "would Farrice say this to a friend"
- ❌ Cannibalizing language across pieces — content ecosystem awareness FIRST
- ❌ Performative interiority without earned specificity — vulnerability that names nothing concrete reads as posing
