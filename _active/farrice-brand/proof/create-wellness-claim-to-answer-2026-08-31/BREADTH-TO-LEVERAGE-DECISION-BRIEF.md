## Rival explanations

### Primary hypothesis: Master of Synthesis without a stable exposure loop

The evidence supports real synthesis because removing any one of three capabilities weakens the result. Research and claim judgment keep the work from becoming generic health marketing. Creative strategy keeps it from becoming a compliance memo. Visual and operating craft turn private analysis into a decision-ready artifact.

What is missing is repetition in one buyer-visible arena. The work has a destination, but the destination keeps receiving a new vehicle before the previous one reaches traffic.

### Secondary hypothesis: Pattern Hunter at the point of conceptual closure

The Pattern Hunter diagnosis still explains a specific moment. Once a framework, skill, or offer architecture becomes elegant, conceptual completion can feel like commercial progress. That is when another source, system, or offer becomes attractive.

This is an operating risk, not the core identity. The correction is simple: no new commercial frame until the current one produces a completed artifact and an authorized buyer test.

### Rejected hypothesis: Free Agent as a personality verdict

The records show an exposure boundary, but they do not show why it exists. Several sessions explicitly prohibited sending or required later approval. Calling that fear would turn missing evidence into psychology. Keep the behavioral warning. Reject the motive claim until there is direct evidence.
