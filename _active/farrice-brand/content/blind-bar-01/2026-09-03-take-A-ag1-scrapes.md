## Caption

410 AG1 ads were live on Meta when I opened the Ad Library this morning. [VERIFIED]

I read forty of them. One scoop. Seventy-five-plus ingredients. Foundational nutrition. A creator says the decade line in her kitchen. Hugh Jackman calls it a no-brainer. A $72 welcome kit waits at the bottom of every one of them. [VERIFIED]

Four hundred and ten costumes. Three sentences.

That is what creative fatigue looks like from the inside, and it is almost never a creative problem. The team keeps ordering formats because nobody chose the argument. A hook changes the doorway. An angle changes the argument.

AG1 has three it could lead with. The ingredient count, which Huel answers with 91 and Ka'Chava with 85, so it stopped being an argument years ago. [VERIFIED / LIKELY] The outcome, what a person actually feels by week three, which is honest and specific and almost never in the ads. And the ritual, the same glass on the same counter, still there in March.

That last one matters more than it used to. On 3 February 2026 a class action, Hoke v. AG1, was filed over subscriptions people say they did not choose. [VERIFIED, allegations not findings] Strip the legal language and it is a buyer sentence: I did not decide to still be paying for this. Four hundred and ten ads, and not one of them argues for month three.

Choose the argument before you multiply it. Three campaign angles, one lead recommendation, before the next creative round. DM ANGLE.
