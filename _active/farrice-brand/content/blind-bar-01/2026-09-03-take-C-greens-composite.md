## Caption

Open the ad library of any greens powder and read ten ads. One scoop. A number of ingredients: 75, 85, 91, depending on the bag. [VERIFIED across three product pages] Daily, foundational, complete. A creator in a kitchen, a welcome kit, a subscription pre-selected at checkout. [LIKELY]

Different costumes, one sentence. The whole aisle is running the same argument with a different count in the headline.

When everyone owns a claim, nobody does. So the teams do what teams do when the numbers soften: more UGC, more statics, another creator, another round. They call it creative fatigue. It is an unmade decision.

A greens powder can argue three things. The count, which is the category's wallpaper now. The outcome, what a person feels by week three, which is honest and specific and rarely in the ads. Or the ritual, the same glass on the same counter in March, which is the only argument that answers the question every subscription-first funnel begs: will I still want this next month.

Choose the argument before you multiply it. DM ANGLE.
