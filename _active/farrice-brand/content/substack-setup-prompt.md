Now, here's how Substack's setup works. There are several different places where different pieces of content go:

STEP 1 — CREATE THE PUBLICATION
When you first create a Substack, it asks you for:
- Your name (your personal name, not the publication)
- Publication name → put "Parallax"
- A short handle/URL → put "parallax" (gives you parallax.substack.com)
- What's your publication about? → paste the SHORT DESCRIPTION above

If I've already created my account, help me find these fields in Settings.

STEP 2 — SETTINGS > BASICS (or PUBLICATION DETAILS)
This is usually under the gear icon or "Settings" in the left sidebar. Look for:
- Publication name
- Publication URL
- Short description / "What's it about?" / Tagline
Help me find these. I'll tell you what I see on screen.

STEP 3 — THE ABOUT PAGE
This is a SEPARATE page from the settings. To find it:
- Go to your Substack dashboard
- Look for "Website" or "Pages" in Settings
- Or go directly to: parallax.substack.com/about and click "Edit"
- Or look for an "About" tab in your navigation settings
The About page has its own rich text editor (like writing a post). Paste the full ABOUT PAGE content there.

STEP 4 — PERSONAL PROFILE / AUTHOR BIO
This is separate from the publication:
- Click your avatar/profile picture (usually top right)
- Go to "Edit profile" or find it at substack.com/profile
- There should be a bio field — paste the AUTHOR BIO there

STEP 5 — WELCOME EMAIL
- Go to Settings > Emails (or Settings > Subscriber emails)
- Look for "Welcome emails" or "Automated emails"
- There should be a "Free subscriber" welcome email template
- Edit the subject line and body with the WELCOME EMAIL content

STEP 6 — PAID TIER
- Go to Settings > Payments
- Connect Stripe (you'll need a Stripe account)
- Set monthly price to $5, annual to $50
- Add the PAID BENEFITS description

STEP 7 — PUBLISH FIRST POST
- Click "New post" or the compose button

- Subtitle: The newsletter for people who were never meant to fit in one box.
- Paste the essay content
- Toggle "Email to subscribers" ON
- Set as Free (not paywalled)
