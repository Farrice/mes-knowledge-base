# Short video: Before the next brief

Status: **HUMAN_REVIEW_PENDING**  
Business job: Conversion  
Target: 35–50 seconds  
CTA: **DM ANGLE** once

You can spot an unmade campaign decision pretty quickly. The team keeps adding hooks, edits, creators, and versions.

Ask what the buyer needs to believe. If nobody owns the answer, the team is producing more guesses faster.

Before the next brief, write down the product truth you want to lead with, the person who needs to believe it, and the proof that can carry it.

Those choices form the angle. The hook opens the door.

Choose the argument first, then make the variants. If your team is stuck at that decision, DM ANGLE.

## Production brief

- Direct-to-camera with quiet conviction and no guru cadence. Farrice rewrites any phrase that does not sit naturally in his mouth.
- Open on a medium close-up. Use only a restrained caption of the first line.
- Use three full-frame text cards for product truth, buyer, and proof; one idea per frame.
- Stay in master-brand mode until the final Angle Map card: Canvas/Paper field, Ink type, Helvetica Neue.
- Use hard cuts and restrained type movement. No AI avatar, stock bottle montage, fake dashboard, or generated face.
- Hand off to `video-studio` only after voice/taste approval. No paid render is authorized.
