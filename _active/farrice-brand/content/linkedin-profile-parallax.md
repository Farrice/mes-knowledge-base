## About Section

I spent $14,000 on courses that taught me to be smaller.

Every business coach said the same thing: "Niche down. Pick one thing. Be the expert in that one thing."

So I did. I buried the psychology, hid the spirituality, never once mentioned anime to a client. I built a coaching business around "just fitness" and performed a version of myself that was marketable, digestible, and dead on arrival.

It took me 18 years of watching people hit the same wall to realize the "pick one thing" advice is the problem, not the solution.

The people who are struggling hardest aren't the scattered ones. They're the performers. The ones who picked one thing, got good at it, and can't figure out why success feels like a costume they can't take off.

Now I write Parallax, a newsletter for multi-passionate people who were never meant to fit in one box. Every edition: an essay from the intersection of everything I'm thinking about, plus a prompt pack that gives you a micro-transformation you can run yourself in 15 minutes.

I'm 37. Father. Creative strategist. I see patterns between anime and business strategy, between Neville Goddard and prompt engineering, between deadlifts and discipline. I built an AI system with 100+ expert agents, and the most important thing it taught me is that the tools only work when you do the inner work first.

If you've got 47 browser tabs open and a bookshelf that makes no sense to anyone else, you're in the right place.
