## Why Threads Now

Threads rewards exactly what Farrice is:
- **Conversational + confessional** — Threads punishes formal; rewards the cafe conversation
- **Text-first** — No algorithm bias toward video; thoughts land on their own merit
- **Lower polish** — Real voice + imperfect texture outperform branded polish
- **Polymath-friendly** — The fitness-to-AI-to-fatherhood seam is a differentiator, not a liability
- **Distribution channel, not repositioning** — Fully compatible with Path A (claim-safe content for funded brands); a channel is not a new offer

## The Threads Positioning

**What this page is:**
- A daily thought-space for cross-domain thinkers

- Proof that your fullness doesn't have to shrink to land

**What this page is NOT:**

## The Threads Formula (Non-Negotiable)

Every post:

| Element | Rule | Why |
|---------|------|-----|
| **Opening** | First 7–10 words ARE the scene, zero runway | Threads feeds punish setup; hook readers immediately |
| **One idea** | Not a thread, not "here's a list." One thought, one angle | Threads rewards digestibility; the seam is where the power lives |
| **Confession** | Drop a personal detail that proves you've lived this | This is the differentiator. Remove it, the post becomes generic |
| **Rhythm** | Short. Sentence. Land. Punches. Then build longer pressure. | Monotone rhythm reads as bot-generated |
| **Close** | Leave the reader with an image, a question that advances your argument, or earned silence | No "Curious what you think?" engagement bait; no summaries |
| **Length** | 60–240 characters (call it "under 3 short tweets worth") | Threads rewards brevity in the feed; room to breathe, not walls of text |

## Posting Cadence

**Week 1 (Launch):** All 5 posts, spaced Tuesday → Thursday → Friday → Monday → Wednesday  
(This seeds the algorithm and establishes voice without spam)

**Week 2+:** 3–5 posts/week minimum for organic discovery  
Rotate through the 6 pillars; never post same pillar twice in 14 days

**Spacing:** Post between 8–9am PT or 9–10pm PT (when cross-domain thinkers scroll)  
(Avoid lunch-scroll hours, early mornings when Threads defaults to friends-only)

## Voice Guardrails (Threads-Specific)

✅ **DO:**
- Open with confession or scene, not a question
- Use second-person sparingly; speak from your frame, let readers apply it
- Concrete details over abstract wisdom
- Shorter paragraphs (Threads breaks up long blocks)
- One-liners that snap
- "I used to…" / "I have a son…" as anchors

❌ **DON'T:**
- "Here's what I learned…" or "Here's the thing…"
- Threads-speak mimicry ("this deserves more attention")
- Questions designed to extract comments
- "Swipe this" or direct CTA language
- Personal brand theater ("Excited to share…")
- Hashtags (unless earned and specific; #WritersOfThreads is overdone)

## Success Metrics (Ignore These Until Week 4)

- **Engagement ≠ signal** — a post with 12 comments from 12 real people > a post with 200 likes from strangers
- **Watch for**: DMs from people who say "wait, are you me?" → that's a recognition hit
- **The real metric**: Do the first 50 followers feel like *your* people?

If after week 2 you're attracting randos instead of cross-domain thinkers, the voice is too polished. Make it weirder. More confession, less polish.
