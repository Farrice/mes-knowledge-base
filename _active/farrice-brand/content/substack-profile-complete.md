**Limit**: No character limit. Rich text editor (WYSIWYG — bold, italic, headings, links, images all work).

**You have 12 interests, a stack of unfinished projects, and everyone keeps telling you to niche down.**

Pick one thing. Be the expert in that one thing.

You keep trying. It keeps failing. Not because you lack discipline. Because the advice is wrong.

**2. A prompt pack.**
Not a motivational worksheet. A set of AI prompts you paste into Claude or ChatGPT that run a personalized coaching session in 15 minutes. You answer three honest questions. The AI does the rest. Something shifts.

**Who this is for:**
Multi-passionate people. Polymaths. Nerds who are also athletes. Spiritual people who also game. Parents building in compressed time windows. Anyone told their range is a liability when it's actually the most valuable thing they own.

**Who writes this:**
I'm Farrice Cain. 37. Father. Creative strategist. 18 years in psychology and personal transformation. I see patterns between anime and business strategy, between Neville Goddard and prompt engineering, between deadlifts and discipline.

I built an AI system with 100+ expert agents. The most important thing it taught me is that the tools only work when you do the inner work first.

**Subscribe and every week you'll get an essay that makes you think "I've never seen anyone connect those things before" — plus a prompt pack that turns the insight into something you can use today.**

If you've got 47 browser tabs open and a bookshelf that makes no sense to anyone else, you're in the right place.
