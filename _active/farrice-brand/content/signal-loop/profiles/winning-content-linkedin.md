## Transferable formulas

### `WCF-LI-01`: Scene, Costly Paradox, Operator-Agency Reframe

- **Transferable mechanic:** Open inside a recognizable category scene, reveal
  that the supposedly safe or polished choice creates the loss, then return
  agency to the founder with one plain causal mechanism.
- **Supporting items:** `LI-2026-06-15-BRAND-VERTICAL`
- **Evidence class:** Human taste verdict
- **Performance summary:** Farrice rated the item 8/10 and would post it as-is;
  no live metrics exist.
- **Trend direction:** Unknown
- **Best use:** Category sameness, careful-copy costs, AI-average critique,
  founder decision failures
- **Anti-patterns:** Generic listicle opening, villainizing AI, instructions
  without a lived or observed scene
- **Confidence:** Medium for taste transfer; low for predicted performance

### `WCF-LI-02`: Exact Private Language, Concrete Mechanism, Earned Tradeoff

- **Transferable mechanic:** Use the audience's real language, demonstrate how
  it changes the decision or behavior, and close by naming the trade being made.
  Reuse the mechanism, never the private story.
- **Supporting items:** `LI-2026-05-05-JJ-A2`
- **Evidence class:** Human taste verdict
- **Performance summary:** Farrice rated the source frame 9/10; analytics are
  absent.
- **Trend direction:** Unknown
- **Best use:** Buyer-language posts, founder-call versus product-page gaps,
  proof that specificity is an operating act rather than copy decoration
- **Anti-patterns:** Exporting family details, using clever simulated dialogue,
  turning recognition into an instruction
- **Confidence:** Medium for structural taste; low for performance

### `WCF-LI-03`: Admission, Self-Indicting Mechanism, Reader Recognition

- **Transferable mechanic:** Begin with a personally costly admission, expose
  the mechanism that made the rationalization possible, and let the reader
  recognize their version without being lectured.
- **Supporting items:** `LI-2026-05-05-SCREEN-B`
- **Evidence class:** Human verdict on source frame plus unscored draft
- **Performance summary:** Origin frame scored 8/10; standalone execution still
  awaits a direct taste verdict.
- **Trend direction:** Unknown
- **Best use:** Founder Fog, outsourcing a decision to a writer, hiding behind
  more variants or more research
- **Anti-patterns:** Confession as theater, family-specific reuse, moralizing
- **Confidence:** Low-medium for taste; low for performance

### `WCF-LI-04`: Reader's Tuesday, Evidence-Honesty Tension, Plain Decision

- **Transferable mechanic:** Put the reader inside the workday where the problem
  appears, make the contradiction between evidence and cautious language
  visible, then name the decision no one owns.
- **Supporting items:** `LI-ABOUT-2026-07`
- **Evidence class:** Human verdict on canonical profile copy
- **Performance summary:** The About surface is human-verdicted 9/10; it is not
  post-performance evidence.
- **Trend direction:** Unknown
- **Best use:** Positioning triage, angle bottlenecks, internal approval loops
- **Anti-patterns:** Biography-first framing, credentials as the hook, generic
  buyer-problem shorthand
- **Confidence:** Medium for voice and positioning; low for post performance

### `WCF-LI-05`: Credit First, Verbatim Gap, Claim-Safe Direction

- **Transferable mechanic:** Name what a real brand gets right, quote one public
  surface exactly, locate the unresolved decision, and show a direction bounded
  by the evidence the brand can defend.
- **Supporting items:** `LI-TEARDOWN-METHOD-2026-07`
- **Evidence class:** Approved strategy
- **Performance summary:** No taste score or live analytics supplied.
- **Trend direction:** Unknown
- **Best use:** Named brand teardowns and PDF carousels
- **Anti-patterns:** Dunking on brands, legal conclusions, invented metrics,
  critique without a same-day source capture
- **Confidence:** Low until a teardown is published and measured
