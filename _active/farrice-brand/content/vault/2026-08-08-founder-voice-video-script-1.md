# Video Script 1: "What They'll Actually Remember" (45-60s)

**VISUAL SEQUENCE:**

A founder at a desk, two browser tabs open. One: "agency brief" (polished, safe, approved). Two: "founder voice" (unpolished, specific, real stakes).

They click agency. Polished. Approved.

They click founder voice. Unpolished. Specific. Conviction.

**VO** (conversational, paced for realization):

"Here's what moves a buyer: the proof that you actually run this business. You already have that proof. You just keep trading it for safety.

The safest choice isn't the one that reads safest."

**RETENTION BEAT:**

"If you're choosing between founder and agency, ask yourself: which proof am I using? Your stakes or your word choice?"

**CLOSE:**

Text overlay: "DM ANGLE if you're choosing between founder and agency."

---

**Story-Seat Bench Applied:**
- **Kallaway (Hooks)**: Hook structure (question + evidence + turn) creates dopamine arc; realization moment lands hard
- **Cole (Series)**: Twin tabs as metaphor; tension between polish/authenticity; resolution in "what moves a buyer"
- **Lara (Platform)**: Native LinkedIn monologue register; conversational, specificity over entertainment
- **Hoyos/Puri (Narrative Beats)**: Open loop (two choices) resolved mid-VO; retention beat re-opens buyer decision
