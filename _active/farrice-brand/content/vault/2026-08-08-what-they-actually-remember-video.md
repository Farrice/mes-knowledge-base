# What They'll Actually Remember (Video Script)

## OPEN
A founder at a desk, two browser tabs. One: "agency brief" (polished, safe, approved). Two: "founder voice" (unpolished, specific, real stakes).

## TENSION
They click agency. Polished. Approved.

They click founder voice. Unpolished. Specific. Conviction.

## TURN
"Here's what moves a buyer: the proof that you actually run this business. You already have that proof. You just keep trading it for safety."

## CALL
Frame the choice as *which proof are you using*, not *which copy style*.

## RETENTION BEAT
"The safest choice isn't the one that reads safest."

## CLOSE
"DM ANGLE if you're choosing between founder and agency."
