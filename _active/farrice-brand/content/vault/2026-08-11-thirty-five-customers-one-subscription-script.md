# Thirty-five customers, one subscription (45-60s script)

**Story-seat gating:** Kallaway on the open (curiosity plus a number), Hoyos on retention beats (visual change or new information roughly every 5 seconds, loop closes on the last line), Cole on series placement, Lara on platform fit (vertical, burned-in captions, first frame legible on mute).

---

**[0:00 - 0:04] HOOK. On camera, close, no intro.**
"Thirty-five customers. One subscription. His whole business needed twelve."

**[0:04 - 0:10] Open the loop.**
"He spent three grand on ads to find out. And every single person he asked told him the same wrong thing."

**[0:10 - 0:18] The wrong answers. Fast cuts, on-screen text stacking.**
"Fix the funnel. Fix the offer. Kill the campaign. Try Google."
*(beat)*
"Nobody asked what the ad promised."

**[0:18 - 0:24] Turn one. Slow down, drop volume.**
"Nobody asked by when."

**[0:24 - 0:34] Proof. Cut to the quote on screen, read it out loud.**
"A woman explained exactly why she cancelled. Her words: I would have subscribed if I had gotten the results that they promised. It was supposed to take a few weeks."
*(beat)*
"She wanted it to work. She had a deadline. Nobody gave her one, so she made one up."

**[0:34 - 0:44] The counter scene. Personal, camera still.**
"I stood behind a supplement counter for eighteen years. Every week somebody came back at week three apologizing to me for not feeling anything. The bottle never told them what week three feels like. I did."

**[0:44 - 0:54] The thesis, plain.**
"So your subscription rate is the market grading the promise your ad made, on a deadline your ad implied. No retention app argues with that."

**[0:54 - 0:58] Close the loop. Residue.**
"Somebody in your company decided when your customer would feel something. Good chance nobody knows who."

---

**Retention notes (Hoyos):** the number in the first four words is the open loop and stays unresolved until 0:44. Three visual changes before 0:20. The personal turn at 0:34 lands after the proof, so credibility precedes intimacy. No CTA on screen; the final line is the share trigger.

**Better-off test (outer ring):** a viewer with no supplement brand still leaves with a usable idea about promises and deadlines in any subscription product.
