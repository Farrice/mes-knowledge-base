# Carousel: The wave lends you customers

**Slide 1 (hook)**
The 2026 wellness waves lent brands their cheapest customers in years.
Sub-line: Most brands will lose them by October. Ten slides on why, and what to check Monday.

**Slide 2 (the waves)**
Three waves stacked in one summer.
Cortisol content: ~800M TikTok views. Fibermaxxing: fiber category +20% YTD, psyllium searches +150%. Sleepmaxxing: routines became an equipment list.
Footer: sources on the last slide.

**Slide 3 (who the wave carries)**
The buyer the wave delivers.
53% of Gen Z say they buy wellness products partly to keep up. 70% feel pressure to keep up with trends. And the sentence they arrive saying, verbatim from this weekend: "I heard its good for it."

**Slide 4 (the invisible part)**
You cannot see them in attribution.
In UK fiber research, under 8% of shoppers credited social media for their changes, and half were unaware of the trend at all. The motivation that bought does not show up in your post-purchase survey.

**Slide 5 (the mechanism)**
Belief at purchase decides the curve.
Two buyers, identical first orders. One carried a problem they have felt for years. One carried the wave. Week-two behavior looks identical. Month-three behavior does not.

**Slide 6 (where it surfaces)**
"It didn't do anything."
Some of those churn rows never expected anything specific. You cannot keep a promise to someone who never heard one.

**Slide 7 (the turn)**
Retention is an acquisition decision.
The churn curve gets set at purchase, by the emotion in the winning ad. Month three only reports it. The angle selects the emotion.

**Slide 8 (the choice)**
An angle is the belief you acquire on.
Trend angle: you borrow the wave's belief, and the wave keeps it. Problem angle: you build your own, and the buyer keeps it. Both are legitimate. Choose knowingly, price the churn in.

**Slide 9 (the Monday cohort read — save this)**
Pull cohorts by creative, not by month.
Three columns: 1) which belief did the ad sell, in one sentence · 2) what did that cohort's week-2 engagement look like · 3) what percent reordered.
If you cannot fill column one for your top ad, that is the finding.

**Slide 10 (close)**
The wave lends you customers. The angle decides which ones you keep.
Save this for your next creative review. If the belief column comes back blank, that decision is still open: DM ANGLE.
