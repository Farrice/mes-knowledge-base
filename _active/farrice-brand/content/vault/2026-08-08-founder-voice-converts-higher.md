# Founder Voice Converts 4× Higher Than Agency Copy

I'm looking at supplement brands right now, and they're all making the same choice the second the copywriter sits down.

Agency copy feels safer. Claim-safe language. On-brand structure. Approved by legal.

Then the founder voice comes up, and every team talks itself down from it. *Too risky. Too unpolished. What if it reads like we're selling instead of serving?*

Here's what the data shows: founder voice converts 4 times higher than the polished alternative.

The person running the business, saying what they actually saw, what actually works, why they built it that way—that lands because it *is* proof. You're not reading marketing. You're reading stakes.

The irony is backwards. The safe choice *sounds* safe. The risky choice *actually is* safe, because a buyer reading founder voice already knows you have skin in the game.

Every brand team I'm talking to this week is one credible sentence away from making a different choice. The sentence isn't longer. It's just theirs.
