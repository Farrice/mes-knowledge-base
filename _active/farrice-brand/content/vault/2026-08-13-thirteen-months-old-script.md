# Thirteen months old

**[0:00 – 0:05] HOOK. On camera, close, no intro.**
"The best supplement ad on Meta right now is thirteen months old. Nobody has touched it."

**[0:05 – 0:12] Open the loop.**
"Meanwhile every agency in this category will tell you to refresh your creative every two weeks. Both of those things are true. That is the part that should bother you."

**[0:12 – 0:22] The numbers. On-screen text stacking, one per beat.**
"Somebody measured the 500 strongest supplement ads on Meta this year. Median survival: three hundred and ninety-four days."
*(beat)*
"Last year's edition: two fifteen."

**[0:22 – 0:32] The turn. Slow down.**
"So the winners are not being refreshed. They are being left alone. Which means fatigue is not happening to the good ones."

**[0:32 – 0:44] The mechanism.**
"And nobody quotes the next number. The opener most associated with a year-long ad is the one that names the problem first. It shows up in four percent of supplement ads. Because in this category, naming the problem is the line that goes to legal and comes back gone."

**[0:44 – 0:55] Land it.**
"So the ad opens on a jar. And three weeks later it needs replacing. That is not fatigue. That is an ad nobody decided anything about, aging on schedule."

**[0:55 – 0:60] Close the loop.**
"Open your ad library. Find three competitors. Read the start dates."
