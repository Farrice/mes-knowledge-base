# Day 36 Video Script

## OPEN

Film a cabinet. One bottle on the shelf. Put "Day 36" on screen.

## LINE

"This is where a lot of supplement marketing breaks."

## TENSION

"The buyer has been taking the product. The subscription email shows up. They still do not know if anything changed."

## TURN

"That does not automatically mean the product failed. It means the brand never gave them a fair checkpoint."

## PROOF BEAT

"In one live omega-3 thread, the buyer says they cannot tell if they notice a benefit or just keep buying out of habit."

## CALL

"If the benefit is quiet, the campaign has to tell them what to notice, when to notice it, and what proof counts if they feel nothing."

## CLOSE

"The claim gets the first order. The checkpoint protects the second."
