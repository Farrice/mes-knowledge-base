# Video Script 2: "The Irony Is Backwards" (45-60s)

**VISUAL SEQUENCE:**

Supplement bottle. Zoom on label copy. Cut to founder.

**VO** (building intensity, then settling):

"Every brand team says the same thing about founder voice: *Too risky. Too unpolished.*

Here's what the data says: founder voice converts 4× higher.

Because it's proof. You're not buying marketing. You're buying the stakes.

The moment a founder realizes: *I was protecting myself from the one thing that actually sells.*"

**RETENTION BEAT:**

"The risky choice is actually safe. The safe choice is actually risky."

**CLOSE:**

Text overlay: "If you're deciding what your buyer actually reads, it's not the polish. It's who's standing behind it."

"DM ANGLE"
