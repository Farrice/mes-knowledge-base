**On-screen title: "Should women take creatine?"**

Somebody's mom is buying creatine today for the first time, and no supplement brand had anything to do with it.

[beat, hold]

She heard a scientist on a podcast put midlife, muscle, and brain in the same sentence. She saw a morning-show segment. Then she typed the only question that matters into search: should women take creatine?

Now look at what answers her. An hour-long physiology lecture with 165,000 views. A podcast clip. Good Morning America. And a video tagged kidney failure with a tub like hers in the thumbnail.

[beat, lean in]

You know what never shows up? The brands. Twenty years of earning shelf space, and in the exact moment a brand-new buyer arrives asking who this is for, the category answers with a shirtless guy and a 5-pound tub.

I stood behind a supplement counter for five years. The question was never does it work. It was always: is this for me?

The first brand that answers her, at her age, in her language, owns this buyer for a decade.
