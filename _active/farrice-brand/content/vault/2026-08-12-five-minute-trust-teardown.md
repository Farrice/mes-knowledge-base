# The five-minute supplement trust teardown

Open the product page. Ignore the headline. Start a timer.

Find five things:

1. Who made the finished product?
2. Which batch will arrive?
3. Where is the document for that batch?
4. What did the test actually check?
5. Who explains what it does not prove?

Most pages give you a seal, a star rating, and “third-party tested.”

That is reassurance. The buyer is looking for a route.

The first place the route breaks is the real creative brief.

Show an invisible batch. Explain the scope of a generic document. Separate identity testing from any efficacy implication. Put a person beside an ownerless explanation.

The point is not to look more scientific. The point is to make the claim easier to check.

Five minutes. One broken link. That is where I would start the next creative round.
