# Founder Voice vs. Agency Copy (Flagship Post)

I'm looking at supplement brands right now. They all make the same choice the second a copywriter sits down.

Agency copy feels safer. Claim-safe language, on-brand structure, legal-approved. Safe.

The founder voice option comes up. Every team talks itself down. Too risky. Too unpolished. Not professional enough. The safer-sounding choice wins by default. The one that moves fewer buyers.

Here's what I'm seeing in the market: founder voice moves more buyers than agency polish.

Why. The person running the business, saying what they actually saw and what actually works, reads as proof. Not marketing. Proof. Stakes.

The backwards part is this: the safe-sounding choice moves fewer people. The risky-sounding choice moves more. Because a buyer reading founder voice already knows you have real money on the product.

Every brand team I talk to this week is one different sentence away from a different choice. The sentence isn't longer. It just sounds like them.
