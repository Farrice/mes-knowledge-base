# The Irony Is Backwards (Video Script)

## OPEN
Supplement bottle. Zoom on label copy. Cut to founder.

## TENSION
"Every brand team says the same thing about founder voice: *too risky, too unpolished.*"

## TURN
"The data says the opposite. Founder voice converts 4× higher because it's proof. You're not buying marketing. You're buying the stakes."

## CALL
Show the moment the founder realizes: *I was protecting myself from the one thing that actually sells.*

## RETENTION BEAT
"The risky choice is actually safe."

## CLOSE
"If you're deciding what your buyer actually reads, it's not the polish. It's who's standing behind it."
