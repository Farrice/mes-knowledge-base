# Drawer video script (45-60s)

This drawer cost me about six hundred dollars. [open drawer] Focus powder. Greens. The 1 a.m. bottle. Every one promised I'd feel something, and the honest review of almost all of them is: I couldn't tell. Now here's the thing. I don't think most of these are scams. I think the promise and the mechanism run on different clocks. The label says today. The biology says six weeks. And no one at these brands decided what I should be able to check in between. So the customer runs the only test available: does week two feel different than week zero? No? Canceled. If you run a supplement brand, that gap is the most expensive thing you own. And it's fixable with one decision about what you promise.
