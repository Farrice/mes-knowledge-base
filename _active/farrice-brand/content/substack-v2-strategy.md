## Lead Magnet Integration (Notion)

Each prompt pack will be hosted on a Notion page and linked from the Substack edition. This lets Farrice:
- Track which packs get the most traffic
- Update packs without editing published Substack posts
- Build a growing "Prompt Pack Library" that becomes a standalone product

**Notion structure:**
- Parallax Prompt Packs (database)
  - 01: The Interest Audit
  - 02: The Rest Architecture Blueprint
  - 03: The Emotional Operating System
  - 04: The Attention Audit
  - 05: The Moonshot Map
