## ★ Insight — Why this tier exists in the architecture

The paid tier is **not** a profit center. It's a **trust-bridge** between free Parallax and the high-ticket service offers. Most creators design paid tiers as the destination. You're designing it as a **filter + nurture mechanism** — the people who pay $96/yr for the Lab are the highest-intent qualified prospects in the funnel. That's where service inquiries originate. The Lab pays for itself, but the real ROI is conversion to retainers.

## Asset Vault Architecture

### Core Assets (What Makes Someone Subscribe — "The First Drop")

These are the 3 assets that justify the subscription on day-one. The Covers-the-Cost test says: **any one of these alone should be worth $96/yr to the right reader.**

#### Core Asset 1: The Resonance Diagnostic — Self-Run Workbook

**What it is**: A 90-minute self-guided workbook that walks an Invisible Expert through identifying:
- The conversation already happening in their ICP's head
- The 3-5 phrases their ICP uses that they need to be reflecting back
- The 1 reframe that turns their service from "nice to have" to "obvious"
- The "wince test" output — a piece of language so specific the right person flinches

**Format**: 25-page PDF + companion Notion template for ongoing use.

**Covers-the-cost test**: ✓ A consultant who runs this once and books 1 client from the resulting language has 30x'd the subscription.

#### Core Asset 2: The Voice Diagnostic Kit

**What it is**: The exact diagnostic process Farrice uses to extract a client's voice in 30 minutes — turned into a self-administered version.

Includes:
- 12-question voice extraction interview (audio-recordable)
- Voice fingerprint scoring rubric (rhythm, register, repeated phrases, anti-phrases)
- 3-sample comparison (annotated: AI mimicry vs. diagnostic extraction vs. true voice)
- A "voice mismatch detection" checklist for editing AI-assisted writing

**Format**: Workbook PDF + 3 audio sample comparisons + Notion template.

**Covers-the-cost test**: ✓ A coach who uses this to vet/edit their ghostwriter's output is paying ~$8/month to make sure their AI-augmented content doesn't sound like 50,000 other coaches.

#### Core Asset 3: The Customer-as-Media-Channel Playbook

**What it is**: A 40-page systematic guide to engineering word-of-mouth — turning the customer's customers into your media channel.

Includes:
- The 5-stage Resonance → Remarkability pipeline
- 18 client-engineered moments mapped to triggers
- The "remark trigger" diagnostic (what makes someone tell someone unprompted)
- Worked examples: 3 case studies dissected (anonymized) with before/after engineering
- A 30-day implementation calendar

**Format**: PDF + accompanying carousel-template library.

**Covers-the-cost test**: ✓ A consultant who books 1 referral they wouldn't have otherwise has paid for years of subscription.

### Ongoing Assets (What Keeps Someone Subscribed — "Why They Don't Cancel")

These assets ship monthly. The pattern: every month, paid subscribers get **one substantial new asset** + **commentary on the month's editions**.

#### Monthly Drop 1 (Month 1): The Coined-Term Registry — Volume 1
- 20+ coined terms with full definitions, source notes, and deployment instructions
- "How to coin your own" methodology

#### Monthly Drop 2 (Month 2): The 4-Question Stack
- The 4 questions Farrice uses to surface a client's frameworks they didn't know they had
- Worked examples + audio walkthrough

#### Monthly Drop 3 (Month 3): The Anti-AI-Prose Field Guide
- The full library of AI structural tells (per FARRICE.md banned moves)
- Detection + repair protocols for ghostwritten content
- 10 before/after rewrites annotated

#### Monthly Drop 4-12 (continued cadence — each ~$50-200 perceived value if sold separately):
- Edition deep-dives (the "DVD commentary" version of each Parallax edition)
- Subscriber-only edition variants ("the version I didn't publish")
- Quarterly archetype audits (Oracle drift detection)
- Semi-annual lineage updates (who Farrice is reading + why)
- Monthly Q&A digest (questions answered in long-form)

#### The "Lab Notes" cadence
A semi-monthly **2-paragraph note** — not an edition, not a polished asset. Just: "Here's what I'm thinking about this week, here's what I'm trying, here's what failed." Maintains intimacy. Replaces "live Q&A" capacity-burner.

### Exclusive Assets (What Drives Annual Plan Conversion)

The annual plan must contain things the monthly plan structurally cannot:

#### Annual-Only #1: The Founding Member Drop
- Bound PDF compilation: all 3 Core Assets + first 6 Monthly Drops in a single "Year One Vault"
- Founding member acknowledgment in the Year One Vault
- Available only to first 50 annual subscribers

#### Annual-Only #2: The Resonance Audit Lottery
- 2 per quarter (8 per year): Farrice audits an annual subscriber's existing content piece live (recorded, posted as Lab artifact for all subscribers)
- Lottery system; ALL annual subscribers eligible
- Recipients sign release; audit becomes Lab content

#### Annual-Only #3: 30-Day Founder's Lockbox
- First 30 days as annual subscriber: weekly "behind-the-publication" notes from Farrice that don't repeat publicly
- Why this edition got written, what didn't make it, what the next 6 months look like
- Maximum founder intimacy at exact moment of highest commitment

## Pricing Architecture

### Final Pricing

| Plan | Price | Discount Logic | Framing |
|:--|:--:|:--|:--|
| Monthly | $10/mo | — | "Try it" |
| **Annual** | **$96/yr** | 20% vs. monthly ($120) | "Default" — pre-selected |
| Founding Member (first 50) | $96/yr (locked) | Same price; perks differ | "Closing soon" |

### Why $10 / $96 (not $7, not $15, not $120)

- **$10/mo psychological anchor**: Below "real money" threshold for the Invisible Expert ICP. Doesn't require justification.
- **$96/yr vs $120**: 20% discount creates measurable urgency. $96 also lands below the $100 visual threshold (mental price line).
- **Not $7/mo**: Too cheap for an "Oracle" archetype. Suggests low value. Anti-positioning.
- **Not $15-25/mo**: Skipping into "premium" tier requires production volume Farrice can't sustain solo. Save that pricing for after team-build.

### Annual-First Default (Sinem Move 6)

- Substack Stripe page pre-selects annual
- Welcome email sequence reinforces annual value (re-frames monthly as "the trial")
- Monthly subscribers receive a 60-day "would you upgrade?" email with discount on first annual year

## Capacity Reality Check

**Production load per month**:
- 1 Monthly Drop (12-25 pages or audio equivalent): 8-12 hours
- 2 Lab Notes (2-paragraph): 1 hour
- Quarterly archetype audit: 4 hours / quarter = ~1.3 hours/mo
- Semi-annual lineage update: 4 hours / 6mo = ~0.7 hours/mo
- Annual lottery audits (2/quarter): 3 hours each = 2 hours/mo
- Email Q&A monthly: 3 hours

**Total**: ~16-19 hours/month for Lab production.

**Sustainable?** YES, if:
- Free Parallax cadence stays at 1 edition every 2-3 weeks (4-6 hours/edition)
- LinkedIn cadence stays at 5-7 posts/week (5-7 hours/week from Antigravity-assisted drafting)
- Service capacity capped at 4 retainers in Phase 1

If exceeding capacity: drop the lottery audits first (recover 2 hrs/mo), then reduce Monthly Drops to every 6 weeks (recover 4 hrs/mo).

## What This Tier Is NOT

- **Not a tip jar** — every asset has product-grade utility
- **Not a paywall on the publication** — free Parallax stays free; the Lab is additive
- **Not a Discord/community** — one-to-many, not many-to-many. Capacity-respecting.
- **Not a course** — assets, not lessons. Product, not curriculum.
- **Not a coaching tier** — service work happens at retainer pricing, not $96/yr
