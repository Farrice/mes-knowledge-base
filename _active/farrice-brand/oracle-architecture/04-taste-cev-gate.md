## ★ Insight — Why this matters BEFORE publishing

Most creators publish, then audit. That's how you learn slow. The taste-CEV gate is run **pre-publication** — not to slow you down, but to lock the quality floor so the audience never sees a 5/10 piece in the first 5 weeks. **The first 5 pieces set the brand expectation.** A 6/10 in week 1 anchors you as a 6/10 creator in everyone's memory. Better to publish 3 pieces at 9/10 than 7 pieces at 6/10.

## CEV Evaluation Matrix — Per Piece

> Scoring 1-10 on 3 axes (Craft, Experience, Value). Goal: 7+ on each. Below 7 = revise pre-publish.

### P1 — The Resonance Gap

| Axis | Predicted Score | Why | Risk |
|:--|:--:|:--|:--|
| **Craft** | 8 | Voice-locked, structurally varied from Edition 01-02, not derivative | Risk: too abstract for first-touch readers |
| **Experience** | 7 | Names something the ICP feels but can't articulate (per Sutherland frame) | Risk: feels like a definition piece, not a personal POV |
| **Value** | 8 | Reframes the entire content-creation problem the ICP has been working on | Risk: may need a concrete example mid-piece to land |

**Pre-publish edit prescription**:
- Open with a specific moment, not a definition. (Banned: "Most marketing fails because..." → use the FARRICE.md "Show don't tell" rule.)
- Add 1 concrete client/personal example mid-piece.
- Test ending against banned moves list — no "It's not X. It's Y." reveal, no twin-sentence aphorism.

**Verdict**: SHIP after pre-publish edits. Floor: 8/8/8.

**Verdict**: HOLD until equipment + real-session opportunity is in place. Floor when ready: 8/9/9.
