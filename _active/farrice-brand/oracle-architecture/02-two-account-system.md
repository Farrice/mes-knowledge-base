## ★ Insight — Why Two Accounts (and what most people get wrong)

The Two-Account method isn't just "post personal stuff and brand stuff." It's a **funnel architecture** where the founder account runs the awareness layer at high reach + low conversion intent, and the brand account runs the conversion layer at lower reach + high conversion intent. Most founders deploy two accounts as redundant duplicates — same content, two places. That fails. The accounts must have **different functions, different KPIs, and different risk tolerances.** Founder = wide bait, sharp opinions, controversial takes welcome. Brand = polished proof, curated artifacts, never controversial. The cross-pollination is engineered, not accidental.

## Platform-Specific Implementation

### LinkedIn

**Founder profile** (Farrice):
- Headline: locked from icp/audience research (deferred to deep-icp profile + headline engineer)
- Banner: Parallax-aligned visual identity
- Featured: 3 pinned (Parallax landing, $500 Proof Run, top TOF carousel)
- Posting cadence: 3-4 posts/wk, 1-2 carousels/wk
- Engagement: 30-min daily window, focused on 12-15 peer creators (algorithmic-reach skill)

**Brand page** (Antigravity):
- Bio: System-focused. "We engineer remarkability for invisible experts."
- Posting cadence: 2 posts/wk
- Content: 60% process artifacts, 30% repost-with-frame from founder, 10% case studies
- No engagement bait. Page is institutional voice.

### Substack/Parallax

**Founder** = the publication owner. Editions, Notes, lives here as Farrice the writer.
**Brand** = curation overlay. Antigravity-branded landing pages route to subscriptions.
**Cross-mechanic**: Each Parallax edition gets a paired Antigravity-branded "Methodology behind this edition" companion (LinkedIn post + brand carousel).

### Instagram

**Founder**: Selective. Only when on-camera presence builds (Phase 2). Stories for raw, Reels for trends — NOT originally produced content yet.
**Brand**: Visual-asset showcase. Workflow screenshots, system anatomy, Parallax edition visuals. 2 posts/wk max.

### YouTube (Phase 2 — Activation Trigger: $10K/mo revenue)

**Founder**: Long-form Loom-style "How I think" walkthroughs. Diagnostic sessions, ICP breakdowns. 1 video every 2-3 weeks initially.
**Brand**: System tours. Antigravity anatomy. Methodology series. 1 video/month.

### X / Twitter

**Founder**: Engagement-only. Reply to peer creators. No original posting.
**Brand**: Inactive. Reactivate only if community migrates back from Threads.

## Anti-Pattern Watch (per Oren Pattern 4)

**Red flag #1**: Brand account starts posting hot takes → loses institutional voice → cannibalizes founder reach. Fix: brand never posts opinion. Period.

**Red flag #2**: Founder account starts feeling like marketing → loses authenticity → audience disengages. Fix: 80% personal POV / 20% systems. Never invert.

**Red flag #3**: Cross-pollination becomes obligatory → audience smells the routine → engagement drops. Fix: cross-post 60% of pieces, not 100%. Some founder content stays founder-only. Some brand content stays brand-only. The selectivity preserves trust.

**Red flag #4**: Volume goes up, quality drops. Fix: hard cap at 5 founder + 2 brand + 1 edition per week regardless of capacity. Volume past quality threshold is anti-Oracle.
