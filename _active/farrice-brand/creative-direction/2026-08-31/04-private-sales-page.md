# Your team can make the work. The brief still has to decide what the work is.

Most supplement brands already have the ingredients, studies, designers, editors, and media buyers.

The break happens earlier.

The proof says one thing. The category looks like everything else. Then the creative team gets five possible directions and no good reason to choose one.

I spent eighteen years training the people who buy these products and three years behind the vitamin-shop counter. I was often the last person someone asked before they spent the money.

Now I do that job upstream.

Bring me the product, the proof you are cleared to use, the current creative, and the occasion. I will read it live with your decision makers. Then I will develop three different campaign directions and tell you which one I would build first.

Within 48 hours, you receive:

- the boundary between what the proof can carry and what stays out;
- three named campaign directions with a distinct argument, visual world, and proof object;
- one recommendation, including the risk and the first asset to build;
- a brief your designer, editor, or agency can start without another strategy meeting;
- a thirty-minute executor handoff within seven days.

I do not run the account, publish the ads, or approve your claims. You already have people for that. I decide what they should be making and why.
