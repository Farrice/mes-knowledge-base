# Blind direction review

Judge the world before you read its explanation. Each route uses true material already present in Farrice's brand history. None is cleared for public use.

## How to choose

Open the visual board in full screen. Spend no more than thirty seconds on each route. Record:

- **Choose:** the route that feels most like the public world only Farrice could own.
- **Keep:** one element from either losing route.
- **Kill:** the signal that feels performed, borrowed, or too expensive to sustain.

Do not choose the prettiest board. Choose the one that makes the next fifty pieces easier to recognize and harder to imitate.

## Direction A

### World sentence

**A buyer's private decision desk, opened to the public.**

### Felt impression

Precise, earned, skeptical, calm. Farrice has already believed the promise, lived with the result, and learned where the substance thins out.

### Visual grammar

- bone paper, black type, oxblood correction marks;
- claims, receipts, crossed-out language, shelf tags;
- warm portrait light with clinical composition;
- a black blazer or overshirt over a plain tee;
- `FINAL CHECK` and `RECEIPT MISSING` as restrained utility stamps.

### Recurring formats

- The Claim Table;
- Put It Back / Put It Forward;
- annotated “what I would let the team make” case files;
- one short personal field note for every four commercial pieces.

### Proof object

An original promise beside the later verdict: what Farrice once believed, what the evidence and lived result taught him, and how that changes a current campaign decision.

### Campaign sequence

1. **Tease:** cropped corrections and one unresolved buyer question.
2. **Unveil:** the full decision desk and Farrice's final-check role.
3. **Launch:** first Claim Table case.
4. **Sustain:** a rotating archive of decisions, receipts, and rejected language.

### Productive nervousness

Farrice publishes an artifact from an earlier version of himself and lets the audience see the distance between fluency and judgment.

## Direction B

### World sentence

**One frame holds two readings of the same person, claim, and category.**

### Felt impression

Composed, intimate, culturally specific, slightly unresolved. Authority comes from seeing the split that simpler identities miss.

### Visual grammar

- charcoal and warm cream with electric violet as a seam;
- diptychs, split captions, two camera positions, paired materials;
- polished portrait on one side, unguarded hands or family artifact on the other;
- structured tailoring crossed with a bomber or training layer;
- a vertical line becomes the recurring mark, then opens when the two readings reconcile.

### Recurring formats

- The Two Reads: buyer reading versus brand intention;
- Half the Brief: what the evidence says versus what creative must make felt;
- split-screen personal story and professional consequence;
- paired collaborator conversations between craft and compliance.

### Proof object

A single remembered sentence or object whose meaning changed with age, followed by the current brand decision that only this double reading makes possible.

### Campaign sequence

1. **Tease:** partial frames and conflicting captions.
2. **Unveil:** Farrice crosses the seam and names the final-check role.
3. **Launch:** one Two Reads diagnosis.
4. **Sustain:** the seam appears across category, identity, proof, and making.

### Productive nervousness

The public world acknowledges conditional belonging without asking the audience to reward disclosure.

## Direction C

### World sentence

**A working studio where evidence, imagination, and care become something people can carry.**

### Felt impression

Generative, humane, culturally alive. Farrice appears as the maker who turns pressure into useful form.

### Visual grammar

- black and white base with signal yellow and hand-drawn red;
- real portrait and illustrated counterpart sharing one frame;
- studio table, wearable object, index card, headphones, training texture;
- blazer, bomber, and creator uniform used as chapters rather than status signals;
- motion through sketch marks, assembly, and a recurring “essential” object.

### Recurring formats

- Built Under Pressure: the object, system, or campaign born from a real constraint;
- Field Notes: illustrated buyer truths from the gym floor and counter;
- Make It Carry: turn a safe claim into a physical or visual mnemonic;
- Studio Sessions with designers, formulators, coaches, and founders.

### Proof object

An artifact created during a consequential period, shown with the original language and the current lesson about making work that helps rather than exploits.

### Campaign sequence

1. **Tease:** the illustrated counterpart leaves handwritten clues on real artifacts.
2. **Unveil:** portrait and character occupy the same studio.
3. **Launch:** first Built Under Pressure story.
4. **Sustain:** one new field note or object enters the world each month.

### Productive nervousness

The anime language moves from side asset to an authored proof of Farrice's cultural and creative range.

## Reveal key

Read only after recording the choice.

### A: The earned skeptic

The human root is Farrice's path from bodybuilding-magazine and Zero-to-Hero fluency to a practitioner who refuses unsupported certainty. It is the cleanest bridge to the current paid lane and the lowest production burden.

**Commercial strength:** immediate fit with claim-safe health creative.  
**Risk:** can become an austere audit brand unless wit, warmth, and a real prior belief remain visible.

### B: The double reading

The human root is conditional belonging and “but dad, I'm also half Filipino.” The creative mechanism turns dual reading into a professional advantage: Farrice notices what gets erased when a category insists on one simple story.

**Commercial strength:** the most ownable philosophy and visual device.  
**Risk:** highest privacy sensitivity; can pull the brand toward identity commentary unless every use returns to the professional judgment.

### C: The essential studio

The human root is MyBPM's beginning as a pandemic mental-health affirmation, carried by a maker who later built systems in naptimes. It makes care, design, health, and AI feel like one life rather than separate resumes.

**Commercial strength:** widest creative range and strongest collaborator/event world.  
**Risk:** can blur the supplement/performance buyer and demand more production than the current schedule can sustain.

## Direction verdict before Farrice's taste vote

**A is the commercial control. B is the distinctiveness challenger. C is the long-horizon world.**

The recommended test is not to merge all three. Choose one spine, borrow one signal, and leave the third parked. A composite made before a taste verdict would protect every option and author none.
