# Farrice Cain brand direction pilot

## Decision

Keep **Commissioned Decision Object** as the primary direction. It already carries the approved Premium Minimal system and best balances commercial clarity with claim-safe restraint. Borrow two things without reopening the visual system: Farrice's unaltered portrait from **Human Signal Studio**, and proof-status/source-detail modules from **Evidence Field Review** for Featured content.

Public deployment remains unapproved. The pilot proves a clearer decision trail and a usable approval test. It does not yet prove fewer revision rounds; that result remains **UNTESTED** until a real approval cycle is measured.

## What the brand has to solve

| Design challenge | Evidence behind it | First surface that can falsify the direction |
|---|---|---|
| Feel commercially sharp without agency-bro or corporate-consulting theater | The live offer is a $750 Angle Map that must lead to one clear build decision | LinkedIn banner + headline + first Featured card |
| Signal evidence and claim safety without looking clinical or compliance-led | The audience sells in a claim-sensitive category and wants usable proof boundaries | Featured Angle Map card at feed and thumbnail size |
| Make Farrice recognizable without drifting into lifestyle-creator branding | Current system has an approved visual spine, while the preview still contains a private identity placeholder | Profile portrait beside banner at desktop and mobile crop |
| Keep the master brand broader than a single offer | Angle Map is the front door; Proof-to-Market Sprint is the higher-value continuation | Headline and Featured sequence, not the banner alone |
| Show health/performance relevance without stock supplement imagery | Audience is established supplement and performance brands; taste and evidence are the differentiators | Three-second top-of-profile scan by an unfamiliar buyer |

## Direction 01: Commissioned Decision Object

**Position:** The strategist who turns a noisy evidence pile into a commissioned decision artifact.

**Visual system:** Approved warm paper canvas, black Helvetica Neue, fine graphite rules, strict grid, generous open space, one dark recommendation. Keep the current Premium Minimal banner and Angle Map Featured card intact.

**Reference logic:** The approved `P2-01 Premium Minimal V1` banner and Angle Map Featured card are the source. Their useful qualities are decisive hierarchy, restraint, physical-paper tactility, and legible productization. A sterile management consultancy or anonymous template shop is the reading to reject. The assets are owned and approved, so this direction requires no new generation.

**What it makes the buyer believe:** “This person can reduce ambiguity without manufacturing claims.”

**Risk:** It can make the work more memorable than the person doing it.

**Proving-surface rule:** If the buyer cannot state “three angles, one recommended first move” after a three-second scan, revise hierarchy before changing style.

## Direction 02: Human Signal Studio

**Position:** A visible creative director with enough warmth to earn trust and enough structure to make the next decision.

**Visual system:** Preserve the Premium Minimal type, grid, and palette. Add only original, unaltered Farrice photography: natural black wardrobe, headphones when contextually true, warm real environments, direct but unforced eye contact. No beauty edits, synthetic portrait generation, or creator-lifestyle props.

**Reference logic:** Original `C5_authority_standing.jpg` and `C6_podcast_seated.jpg` portraits supply recognizability, lived authorship, warmth, and creative-director credibility. Avoid influencer lifestyle, podcast-bro identity, or a personality that replaces the offer. The original pixels are owned; only cropping is allowed.

**What it makes the buyer believe:** “A real operator with taste is accountable for this decision.”

**Risk:** Portrait energy can outrun category relevance and weaken the commissioned-object feel.

**Proving-surface rule:** The portrait may increase trust, but the offer and recommended decision must still win the first scan.

## Direction 03: Evidence Field Review

**Position:** The evidence-aware strategist who shows where a claim comes from, what it can carry, and what the brand should build next.

**Visual system:** Keep the same type family and neutral palette. Use restrained evidence crops, source labels, proof-status chips, and annotated margin notes inside Featured content. No topographic lines, serif revival, multi-color routes, faux-lab imagery, or dense dashboard UI.

**Reference logic:** The approved belief article card, current claim-safety language, and Angle Map proof-direction fields supply inspectability, category fluency, a proof boundary, and editorial judgment. Avoid the look of a compliance department, an academic paper, or “research” used as decoration. Only owned brand artifacts and verified source excerpts qualify.

**What it makes the buyer believe:** “The recommendation is traceable and safe enough to act on.”

**Risk:** It can make a fast commercial decision feel like a slow audit.

**Proving-surface rule:** Proof detail belongs one click deeper. The top-of-profile scan still leads with the decision, not the documentation.

## Proving-surface test

**Surface:** LinkedIn top-of-profile system: banner, portrait, headline, and first Featured card. Review it at desktop width, mobile crop, and small thumbnail.

**Prompt shown to the reviewer for five seconds:** “What does Farrice help a supplement or performance brand decide, and what should you look at next?”

**Pass conditions:**

1. The reviewer names campaign angles or what to build first.
2. The reviewer recognizes a real strategist rather than an anonymous studio.
3. Evidence safety is inferred without “compliance consultant” becoming the category.
4. The first Featured card is the obvious next click.
5. No reviewer mistakes Angle Map for the entire master brand.

**Pilot result from the current assets:**

- Offer clarity, hierarchy, and restraint pass under Direction 01.
- Personal recognition remains vulnerable because the system preview still contains a private identity slot.
- Human Signal Studio supplies the smallest useful correction: use the original portrait in the profile identity surface, without redesigning the approved banner.
- Evidence Field Review belongs mainly in Featured content and proof artifacts. It should not take over the banner.
- Commercial lift, approval speed, and revision reduction remain **UNTESTED**.

## Revision-drift measurement

Record these fields for the next real review: approval rounds, decisions reopened after approval, references added without a named role, taste calls left unresolved, and minutes from first presentation to locked direction. Compare them with the most recent comparable brand-direction project. One clean pilot is a signal; three independent approval receipts are the minimum before treating the spine as a reusable default.
