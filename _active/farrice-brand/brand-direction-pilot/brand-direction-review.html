Keep Commissioned Decision Object. Add Farrice's original portrait at the identity surface. Reserve evidence annotations for Featured content. Revision reduction remains UNTESTED until a real approval cycle is measured.

Three client directions

Each direction names what it borrows, what it rejects, and the first surface that can prove it wrong.
Direction 01 · incumbent

Commissioned Decision Object

A strategist who turns a noisy evidence pile into a calm, commissioned decision artifact.

Borrow
Decisive hierarchy, paper tactility, legible productization.
Reject
Corporate consultancy and anonymous template shop.
Risk
The work becomes more memorable than the person.
Test
Can a buyer repeat “three angles, one first move” after three seconds?
Direction 02 · challenger

Human Signal Studio

A visible creative director with warmth, taste, and direct accountability for the decision.

Borrow
Recognizability, lived authorship, warmth.
Reject
Influencer lifestyle and podcast-bro identity.
Risk
Portrait energy outruns category relevance.
Test
Does the person increase trust while the offer still wins the scan?
Direction 03 · challenger

Evidence Field Review

An evidence-aware strategist who shows what a claim can carry and what the brand should build next.

Borrow
Inspectability, proof boundaries, editorial judgment.
Reject
Compliance theater, faux-lab imagery, dense dashboards.
Risk
A fast decision starts to feel like a slow audit.
Test
Does proof detail support the next click without taking over the hero?
