# PLATFORM NARRATIVE CARD — Farrice "Fresh" Cain

> The Voice Card answers "how do I sound." This card answers **"how much story does this surface pay for, in what POV, at what finish."** Load BOTH for any Farrice-voice content; this card alone for platform-strategy calls.

## §1 THE UNIVERSAL SPINE (never dial these away)

1. **Reader-as-hero / writer-as-guide** — held on every surface tested, including X's how-to lists. Farrice's core frame is safe everywhere.
2. **Open loop before conclusion** — full arc where dwell allows; a beat that stops before the conclusion where it doesn't.
3. **Human > aesthetic** — 2026 macro-shift: casual-polished, journey-based beats personality-less perfection everywhere.
4. **I-narrative > you-dictation** (Farrice felt verdict, 2026-07-16) — confess first, hand the quest to the reader last third. The ONE exception is X (see §2).

## §2 THE THREE DIALS PER PLATFORM

| Platform | POV-mode | Arc dial | Polish dial | I-story temp | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | I-story arc + legible structure (Before-After-Bridge spine) | FULL | Polished-written | HOT | VERIFIED direction |
| YouTube long-form | First-person journey, character development | FULL (highest appetite anywhere) | Casual-polished | HOTTEST | LIKELY (strong) |
| Substack newsletter | First-person voice, strong POV ("subscribe to a voice, not a topic") | FULL essay | Crafted | HOT | LIKELY |
| Substack Notes | Compressed micro-I-story (tiny essay, not throwaway line) | MICRO-BEAT | Crafted-casual | WARM-HOT | LIKELY |
| TikTok | Face-to-camera I-story, personality-first | SINGLE BEAT | RAW (produced underperforms) | WARM | LIKELY |
| Instagram Reels | POV/direct-address, quick payoff | SINGLE BEAT | PRODUCED (inverse of TikTok) | WARM | LIKELY |
| IG feed/carousel | Value-first; story as wrapper, never payload | LIGHT | Produced | COOL-WARM | LIKELY |

## §3 THE COMPILE MOVE (one master story → every surface)

Never cross-post raw. Write the **master I-story once** (LinkedIn/newsletter grade, full arc), then compile per surface by turning the three dials:

- **Full-arc surfaces** (LinkedIn, YT long, Substack): keep the whole arc — verified scene open → confession → turn → reader handoff → reader-wins close. Add the platform's structural spine (LinkedIn: skimmable line breaks; YT: chapter beats; Substack: essay pacing).
- **Single-beat surfaces** (TikTok, Reels, Notes, Threads, Shorts): extract ONE beat of the arc (usually the confession or the turn), open mid-beat, stop before the resolution (Threads/Notes) or pay it off fast (Reels/Shorts). Re-finish to the surface: RAW for TikTok/Threads, PRODUCED for Reels/Shorts.
- **X**: strip the narrative entirely — compile the arc's LESSON into a numbered breakdown or one sharp claim. The story survives only as a single receipt line, not the frame.
- **Polish rule of thumb**: the most common cross-post failure is right clip, wrong finish (raw↔produced). Check the polish dial before the content dial.
