# REGISTER ATLAS — how Farrice actually talks, measured

> The first voice document in this system built from Farrice **off the clock**. Every prior
> extraction sampled him at work (measured: 4 humor markers in 50,825 work messages vs 4.9%
> in 8,866 messages to his wife — a ~600× gap). This atlas is the correction: four live
> registers, one invariant voice, and the dial that moves between them.

## The one-line finding

**Farrice has one voice at four volumes.** The unit never changes — short line, spoken
grammar, zero messages over 200 characters *in any register, out of 30,606*. What moves is
energy, profanity, dialect density, and who gets the exclamation points.

**Read the columns as a person:** calm and noticing at home · playful and dialect-forward
with the boys · loudest in the group chat · warmest, most energized, and *cleanest* with
clients. Questions are flat everywhere (~11–13%) — he checks in on people at the same rate
no matter who they are. That's not register; that's character.

## Register 1 — WIFE: the noticer

Calm, brief, domestic, affectionate. Lowest exclamation of all four registers — home is
where the energy performance stops. "lol" here is a **tone-softener closing ordinary
observations**, not a laugh: *"I feel like her head is getting bigger too lol"* · *"I don't
want to get up lol"* · *"The key is being outside lol."* Humor is observational and aimed at
what's in front of him: *"She straight up sleeping with the pillow on her face"* · *"The
walker be wiping him out."* Comedy mechanism when it appears: **reported speech** — *"He
woke up and was like 'I was watching' and I told him he fell asleep."*

## Register 2 — FRIENDS 1:1: the warm code-switch

Highest AAVE density (6.8%): *"What's good bro bro!"* · *"fasho"* · *"low key"* · habitual
be (*"one of your DJs be spinning"*). Profanity relaxed, anime surfacing naturally (*"I saw
the Hero Academia on your wallpaper you know what's up bro"*), gratitude embedded in banter
(*"Thank you brother!"*). This is the register his LinkedIn "charm" should borrow *rhythm*
from — never vocabulary wholesale.

## Register 3 — GROUP CHATS: full volume

Loudest room: laughter markers at 11.5%, emoji in triples (🤣🤣🤣, ☠️☠️), "Bruh" as the
universal opener, self-deprecation as a first move (*"Well I must be the slow one 🤣🤣🤣"* ·
*"Bruh my body wasn't ready for the Ciroc Buffet ☠️☠️☠️"*), affection routed through roast
(*"I wanted to be young and sexy like you Marco"*). In-group speech lives here that belongs
to that room only — the atlas notes its existence and deliberately does not export it.
**Nothing from this register ports to public writing directly; what ports is the
self-deprecation-first instinct.**

## Register 4 — CLIENTS: the encourager (closest ancestor of the brand voice)

The surprise of the corpus: with clients he is **more** energized, not less — 18.8%
exclamation, near-zero profanity, longest median line (60 chars — he explains more). The
mechanics:

- **Celebration is the default response to any client win:** *"That's awesome to hear!
  Proud of you & Katie, man, great job!"* · *"Sweet! If you need any adjustments let me know
  and I'll send it over."*
- **"Brother" is his professional endearment**, and gratitude language is structural, not
  garnish: *"Grateful and very appreciative of you brother! Thank you and very proud of you."*
- **The spiritual layer opens with clients** (highest of any register): *"Hold the faith and
  stand in your conviction. You're meant to do this you don't need any go ahead other than
  god's."* And received intimacy is honored straight, no deflection: *"You've been a massive
  part of my own remembering of truth so thank you brother! Love ya."*
- **Service reflex:** offers the adjustment before being asked, confirms logistics with a
  question, thanks people for *their* flexibility when rescheduling *his* conflicts.

## What never moves (the invariants — these ARE the voice)

1. **The short line.** Zero of 30,606 messages exceed 200 characters. When he writes long,
   he's working; it's not him.
2. **Question rate ~12% in every register** — he checks in on people identically whether
   it's his wife or a client.
3. **Warmth as the floor.** The dial moves energy and dialect, never care.
4. **Self-deprecation before any roast of others.**
5. **Reported speech as the comedy engine** — the funny lives in what someone actually said,
   never in his commentary on it.

## What this means for public writing (feed to VOICE-CARD v1.3)

The target LinkedIn voice is **the client register's warmth and energy, carried in the wife
register's noticing, with the friends register's rhythm** — and nothing from the group chat
except the instinct to make himself the joke first. Concretely: celebrate the reader's win
before teaching · one short line as the unit · "lol"-class softeners have written
equivalents (the aside, the shrug clause) · dialect appears as seasoning at friend-density
(≤7%), never as costume · spiritual vocabulary earns its place only where a client-grade
relationship exists with the reader.
