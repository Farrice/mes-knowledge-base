## 0 — THE RESHAPE IN ONE BREATH (what changed, and why)

The vehicle and the price survived the red-team untouched. The packaging did not. Five surgical changes `[VERDICT]`:

1. **Buyer:** gate on **revenue + one named bottleneck**, never on the "stuck / multi-passionate" identity. The identity is the highest-churn, lowest-willingness-to-pay profile in coaching. It magnetizes; it never qualifies.
2. **Promise:** kill "systems AND soul" as any public label (trademark-claimed twice in-category). One legible outcome up front. The AI build is the vehicle; the inner-game is the *how*.
3. **Sequence:** never launch $5–8K cold into the most skeptical buyer alive with zero proof. Front the ladder with a low-ticket diagnostic that manufactures real case studies in weeks.
4. **Name:** retire "The Final 10%" as the *offer* name (it names the buyer's problem, in the accountability-coaching category's own words, and may reinforce the perfectionism that causes the stall). Keep it as the content franchise + the diagnostic.
5. **First cohort:** lead with the **one bottleneck Farrice has already deep-researched** — the visibility / referral-ceiling gap (the Invisible Expert). The final-10% pattern becomes the *deeper cause* under a legible surface problem, not the headline.

## 4 — THE MECHANISM (how the Sprint + The Room actually deliver, at 1–3 hr/day)

The red-team validated exactly one thing about the vehicle: it works because it's **advisory that compounds without operator grind** — architect, not agency; data-review, not hand-holding; AI-built, so the labor is front-loaded into design and the delivery stays light. Here is the machinery.

### 4a — The Architecture Sprint (4–6 weeks, done-*with*-you)

**Phase 0 — GROUND (face the real Point A).** Extract their genius through conversation, not a blank page. Voice memos, structured interviews — "you talk, the system translates." `[ICP-SOURCED — the identity-safe extraction pipeline]` In parallel, map exactly *where the last build died.* The 90% is never random; it has a location. Find it.

**Phase 1 — ARCHITECT (design around the genius).** Design the custom AI-powered authority system fitted to *their* specific expertise and their 1–3 hr/day reality — not a generic stack copied off a template. This is Farrice's craft: MES 3.0, prompt engineering, business-model design. `[REAL]` Built *with* AI, which is what makes a bespoke system fast enough to be a light-touch vehicle instead of an agency build.

**Phase 2 — INSTALL (ship it, don't assign it).** Build the system and hand it over: the working stack + the playbook/SOP they run solo. Done-with-you means they leave with a *built machine*, not homework. This phase is the literal end of the 90% pattern — the deliverable is a finished, live system.

**Phase 3 — ALIGN (install the operator who runs it).** The differentiated half. Name the specific protection pattern that stalls them, and wire the alignment work *directly into the system they're building* — aimed at the exact point where execution historically breaks. `[REAL — 18 yrs applied transformation]` Not therapy. Not affirmations. Not general mindset floating free of the work. The alignment is what makes the architecture get used after Farrice is gone.

> **Why AI makes this a 1–3 hr/day vehicle, not an agency:** the build is done with AI (fast, bespoke, cheap to produce). The client runs the system solo (they own it). Farrice's ongoing role is design + direction, not execution. Solo + VA + AI. This is the vehicle the red-team called skill-fit HIGH / operator-load LOW. `[VERDICT]`

### 4b — The Room (opt-in advisory, after the sprint ships)

Light-touch by design. **Review the data, direct the move.** Async + a monthly review. The client already owns a live system; The Room is a light hand on the wheel, not a retainer they have to feed.

- The master question is Whiting's: **"What does the data say to do this month?"** No feeling-based decisions. `[method — jw]`
- One move directed per cycle: the one stage behind pace, funded from the stage ahead of pace.
- System tune-ups as real usage data arrives.
- **Graduating is a feature.** The crusade is anti-dependence — they can leave the day it stops earning its keep. An architect is happy when you don't need him. `[REAL — anti-guru]`
