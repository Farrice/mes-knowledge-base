Over the years I learned the difference between what really matters in products like this and what actually gets results. Through learning to market and position myself and my training business, I learned to fuse the two: the knowledge, and getting the right message in front of people. I'm very used to being the final checkpoint before someone purchases — my recommendation holds the weight. In the market right now everyone sounds the same. Everyone is too scared to say certain things and stand on something. Most of these supplement brands won't be around in the next year or two, because they're trying to seize a market opportunity to sell you something rather than actually help you with something. What I'm here to do is help brands, founders, and businesses with a legitimate product that could do real good get their messaging, positioning, and creative strategy done correctly — so the product stays on the market, reaches the people who need it, and people actually see relief and positive impact instead of having their money taken.
