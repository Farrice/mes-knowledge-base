# Theme — Presence vs. Screens (the discipline)

> **The discipline**: Work happens when he sleeps. Presence happens when he's awake. Screens are the easy temptation in the awake-time — the one I justify with "I'm sacrificing for him." That justification fails the moment I see what cheap dopamine actually does to him. If I'm going to use screens anyway, I better goddamn produce while he's on them — because I now know what I'm trading.

**Status**: Active theme — personal discipline, also a content thread on attention/dopamine economics.

---

## The two-sided commitment

### Side 1 — When JJ is asleep
Hustle. Bust ass. Get clients. Work the offer. Make the money. No guilt about working — this is the window for it.

### Side 2 — When JJ is awake
Presence. No screens for him as a babysitter unless I'm actively producing something that justifies the trade. **No half-presence.** Half-presence trains him to expect partial attention from people who claim to love him — and trains me to mistake proximity for connection.

The rule: the trade has to be honest both ways.

---

## Why JJ's outburst (2026-05-04) was the trigger

> "...we saw the effects of getting comfortable and relying on cheap dopamine and cheap entertainment at such a young age. It's crazy how numb we are as adults, but seeing JJ and how it affected him really opened my eyes."

Adults have built tolerance to cheap dopamine — we don't notice the cost anymore. A 2-year-old has no buffer. The outburst is the **uncompressed signal** of what screens cost. Watching it makes the cost legible again — for him, and for me.
