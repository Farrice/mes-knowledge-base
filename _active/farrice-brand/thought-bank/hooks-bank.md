## Hook quality bar (Farrice voice rules — see [pov-anchors.md](pov-anchors.md))
- ✅ Show, don't tell
- ✅ No forced jargon — would I say this to a friend?
- ✅ Open loop or pattern interrupt in first 7 words
- ✅ Specific (a 2-year-old, a real moment) > abstract (a child, the dynamic)
- ✅ Statement, not question (52% conversion lift)
- ❌ "Here's what / Here's why / Here's how" openers (banned)
- ❌ "It's not X. It's Y." reveal moves (banned)
- ❌ Cross-piece structural repetition
- ❌ **Cheap question signoffs** (added 2026-05-05 — see pov-anchors.md anchor #4)
- ❌ Marketing-craft commentary that trades personal vulnerability for observer-voice (C's failure mode)
