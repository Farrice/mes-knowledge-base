The portrait is intentionally excluded from the shareable core ZIP. It is available in the separately governed private identity add-on for authorized brand-production work.

Permitted:

- crop;

- nondestructive placement; and
- masking that leaves the original face pixels unchanged.

Prohibited:

- face regeneration or replacement;
- inpainting or identity reconstruction;
- facial reshaping;
- beauty filters;
- face-specific color alteration;
- AI upscaling that invents facial detail; and
- use in unrelated, deceptive, or unauthorized work.
