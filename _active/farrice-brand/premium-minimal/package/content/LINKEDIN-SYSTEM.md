# Farrice Cain — LinkedIn Personal-Brand System

## The buyer path

| Surface | Job | Protected implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Banner | Name the object and outcome | The Angle Map → three campaign angles → what to build first |
| Headline | Identify buyer, role, decision, and action | P1 B headline, verbatim |
| About | Recognition → distinction → mechanics → authority → boundary → CTA | P1 B About, verbatim |
| Experience | Prove this is an operational service | P1 B Experience, verbatim |
| Featured 1 | Earn belief before selling | Flagship article |
| Featured 2 | Make the commercial invitation | First Angle Map launch post |
| Featured 3 | Show real proof later | Empty until real, paid, permissioned work exists |
| Content | Teach two ideas repeatedly | Decision before creative + product truth a buyer can believe |
| Conversion | Invite one next action | **DM ANGLE** |

The exact protected profile copy remains in `P1-LINKEDIN-PASTE-MASTER.md`. This system does not silently revise it.

## Five-second profile fold

Banner and headline together must answer:

1. Who is this for? Supplement and performance brands.
2. What is the object? The Angle Map.
3. What do they get? Three campaign angles.
4. What decision does it support? What to build first.
5. What do they do? DM ANGLE.

Price and delivery time live in About and Experience. The LinkedIn top fold is an identity and relevance gate, not the complete sales page.

## Content rules

### Lane 01 — The decision before the creative, 60%

- Hooks versus campaign angles.
- Message fatigue disguised as creative fatigue.
- Why production cannot resolve an undecided campaign argument.

### Lane 02 — Product truth a buyer can believe, 40%

- Product evidence versus ingredient evidence.
- Proof scope and editorial boundaries.
- Why honest formulas disappear inside generic category language.

### CTA behavior

- Growth: ask for a concrete example, reaction, or disagreement.
- Authority: teach the decision; no pitch required.
- Personal: connect one lived scene to the buyer's current work.
- Conversion: use **DM ANGLE** only.
- Comments on other people's posts: never pitch and never append **DM ANGLE**.
- No booking link, newsletter CTA, second keyword, or alternate public offer while The Angle Map is the wedge.
