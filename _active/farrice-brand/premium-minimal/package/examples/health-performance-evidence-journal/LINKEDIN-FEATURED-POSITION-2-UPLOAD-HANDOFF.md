## Platform basis

- LinkedIn allows authored posts and uploaded media in Featured, but a post must already be published before it can be featured. [LinkedIn: Manage featured samples](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550399)
- Newly featured content appears first by default and can then be reordered when at least two Featured items exist. [LinkedIn: Featured section FAQs](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a548496)
- LinkedIn's photo composer supports alt text before posting; uploaded photos cannot be edited after publication. [LinkedIn: Share photos](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a527229)
