## 10. Final state labels

- `NO EVENT`: the paid event or outcome did not occur.
- `UNCONFIRMED`: someone asserted it, but no source receipt is attached.
- `UNVERIFIED`: a source exists but has not passed review.
- `PRIVATE ONLY`: evidence exists but reuse permission does not.
- `PERMISSIONED FOR REVIEW`: exact use is approved; Farrice has not promoted the asset.
- `APPROVED`: Farrice approved the exact proof asset.
- `PUBLISHED`: an authorized external action completed and was verified.
