# Angle Map Featured explainer — funded-founder copy critique

Perspective: founder or marketing lead at an established supplement, recovery, functional-nutrition, or performance brand with a creative round approaching.

Source under review: `fc-pm1_li-featured-angle-map-evidence_review_v01.svg`

## Five-second purchase read

| Question | V1 result | Buyer reaction |
|---|---|---|
| Is this for me? | PASS | Supplement and performance brands are named. |
| What is it? | PASS | The Angle Map is clearly the offer. |
| Why now? | WEAK | “One live message to settle now” is true but vague. The funded creative round is the sharper trigger. |
| What do I receive? | PARTIAL | “Three campaign arguments” names quantity, but the three fields describe the method rather than the purchased output. |
| What decision does it help me make? | PASS | “What to build first” is clear. |
| What does it cost? | PASS | `$750 prepaid` is visible. |
| How fast? | PARTIAL | The 60-minute session is visible. The 48-hour delivery clock is missing. |
| What do I do? | PASS | `DM ANGLE` is unmistakable. |

## Copy-only correction

1. Lead with the budget consequence: choose the argument before funding another creative round.
2. Use the offer’s canonical noun, `campaign angles`, instead of the broader `campaign arguments`.
3. Convert the three evidence fields from method language to a purchase sequence: `READ / MAP / DECIDE`.
4. Add the exact delivery condition: within 48 hours after payment and complete inputs.
5. Keep `$750 prepaid` and `DM ANGLE` visible.

## Protected elements

No color, typography, spacing, grid, stroke, field number, or composition change is authorized in this pass. V2 must be a text-only revision of V1.

## Purchase verdict

V1 earns attention. V2 should remove the last translation burden: the founder brings one live message, receives three named angles, and leaves with the lead decision before the next creative round consumes budget.
