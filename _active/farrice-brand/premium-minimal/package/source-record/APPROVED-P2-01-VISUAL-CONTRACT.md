# The Angle Map — Premium Minimal Design Contract V1

**State:** Approved visual primary for the Farrice Cain LinkedIn and personal-brand system.  
**Reference translation:** Kith-like restraint applied to an executive decision object; no imitation of Kith branding, layouts, logos, or campaign imagery.  
**Structural authority:** The approved 12-page Decision Book narrative remains unchanged. This contract replaces the rejected Editorial Cartography visual world only.

## Visual intent

The book should feel less like a decorated strategy deck and more like a commissioned object from a disciplined studio: quiet, exact, contemporary, and expensive because nothing is trying too hard.

The design must make three things feel immediately true:

1. A real review happened before the session.
2. The three campaign arguments are materially different.
3. Farrice is willing to recommend one and remove the weaker choice.

## Hard rules

### Typography

- Use **Helvetica Neue only**.
- Create hierarchy with size, weight, spacing, and position—not extra typefaces.
- Sentence case is the default. Uppercase is reserved for small functional labels.
- No serif, condensed display face, faux editorial italics, or decorative quotation styling.

### Color

- Use a near-monochrome system:
  - Canvas — #F3F3F0
  - Paper — #FAFAF8
  - Ink — #101010
  - Graphite — #555553
  - Line — #D8D8D3
  - Stone — #8C8C82, used sparingly
- Do not assign a different hue to each angle.
- A dark page may create one deliberate interruption; black-and-gold luxury theater is prohibited.

### Composition

- Work on a 12-column landscape grid with generous outer margins.
- Keep at least one-third of most pages visually open.
- Use one dominant idea per page and no more than three hierarchy levels.
- Prefer alignment, rules, and whitespace over boxes, borders, and cards.
- No contour lines, gradients, badges, seals, shadows, rounded pills, or ornamental icons.

### Route grammar

- Show the three campaign angles as three parallel horizontal strokes.
- Distinguish them by number, position, weight, and line style—not color.
- The recommended route is solid black and visibly heavier.
- Non-lead routes recede in light gray; they remain legible, not decorative.

### Content behavior

- Keep the fictional-demonstration boundary visible on every page.
- Preserve the offer's proof boundary and do not imply legal approval, performance outcomes, or completed campaign production.
- The page must still work when forwarded or viewed alone.
- A buyer should be able to find the recommendation, reason, proof direction, and boundary without presentation narration.

## Rejected V0 signals

- Editorial Cartography as the visual world.
- Iowan Old Style and mixed typography.
- Rust, forest, moss, and amber route coding.
- Topographic lines and map-coordinate ornament.
- Card-heavy layouts, rounded labels, and repeated micro-copy chrome.
- Visual variety that competes with the campaign decision.

## V1 taste gate

The direction is successful only if Farrice can say yes to all five:

1. It feels premium before he reads the copy.
2. It looks contemporary rather than editorial-retro.
3. It feels calm at contact-sheet scale.
4. The lead recommendation is unmistakable without bright color.
5. It could plausibly become the base of a coherent Farrice Cain personal-brand system.

## Build status

Farrice approved the accompanying V1 design board and 12-page prototype as the parent visual language. Production assets may vary in composition, but they must preserve this contract's type, tonal, spacing, route, and restraint rules. Public deployment remains separately approval-gated.
