## Cold-DM Openers (built off Fusion A's articulation)

### DM-1: The Direct Recognition

> Hey — saw your work with [specific thing they post about]. You're clearly good at what you do.
>
> Quick question: do you ever feel like you could explain your approach to a client in 10 minutes, but a single LinkedIn post takes you an hour?
>
> I help coaches turn voice memos into posts that sound like them. No writing, no ghostwriter pretending to be you. Just your voice, cleaned up, bringing clients in.
>
> Worth a conversation?

**Dies on**: "I don't use voice memos" — the mechanism feels foreign to their workflow.

### DM-2: The Invisible Expert Hook

> Hey — I keep seeing your content. The expertise is clearly there.
>
> Honest question: do clients find you, or do you find them?
>
> I work with coaches who can explain a $10K transformation in conversation but go blank at the keyboard. I turn their voice memos into posts that bring clients to them — in their voice, not mine.
>
> If you're getting referrals but not inbound, might be worth talking.

**Dies on**: "I'm getting plenty of inbound already" — no problem to solve. (Qualifier is built in: "if you're getting referrals but not inbound.")

### DM-3: The Scar-Tissue Opener

> Hey — saw your profile. You've got the receipts.
>
> Quick one: have you ever hired someone to write for you and hated how it sounded?
>
> I do something different. You record a voice memo about what you actually think. I turn it into a post that sounds like you said it — because you did. No ghostwriter borrowing your voice.
>
> If the old approach left a bad taste, happy to show you how this works.

**Dies on**: "I've never hired a ghostwriter" — scar-tissue hook doesn't land if there's no scar.

## Notes for Farrice

1. **DM openers assume some personalization** (e.g., "[specific thing they post about]"). Cold-spray without any personalization will tank trust; these are templates for manual sends, not bulk automation.

2. **IG hooks are bare-bones** — designed for the first 3 lines visible before "more." The proof, CTA, and mechanism expansion belong in carousel slides or caption body below the fold.

3. **All claims stay inside the quarantine**. No revenue figures, no client counts, no "results" beyond the implicit outcome (clients come to you). Proof to follow in separate assets.

4. **Cold-stranger test passed**: each DM and IG hook assumes zero prior awareness of Farrice, the system, or the offer.
