1. **Free public teardown** — run `/teardown <recognizable brand>` weekly, post the constructive before/after on LinkedIn. It borrows the brand's attention and demonstrates the engine live, on a name people know. No "trust me" — here's the rewrite, here's why it converts.

3. **Audit → Foundation** — the teardown surfaces the gap in their own positioning (their competitor's customer is their customer). Natural ascension.

## 6. The honest guardrails (why this is safe to run publicly)
- **Constructive only.** `teardown_ethics_gate.py` blocks hit-piece framing — we tear down the *copy*, never the *people* ("what they got right / what I'd improve").
- **No copyright reproduction.** The gate flags any verbatim reproduction of their copy beyond a short excerpt; we excerpt + transform.
- **Every brand claim sourced.** Factual claims about the target route through `verify_proof_ledger` — labeled or cut. We never assert what we can't source.
