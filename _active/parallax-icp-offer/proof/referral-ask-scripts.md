# Referral Ask Scripts — 3 Variants for Javier to Send On Farrice's Behalf

**Context:** Once Javier is on board with the testimonial ask (see `testimonial-ask-javier.md`), he becomes a referral engine. He has institutional access — CPS Music Content Specialist, plus a decade of nonprofit network across Chicago — to people who need exactly what Farrice sells. These three scripts give Javier ready-to-send language so he doesn't have to write anything himself. Low friction. High leverage.

**Critical rule:** Every script below has to feel like *Javier's voice*, not Farrice's. That's why the language is warm, conversational, Chicago-teacher-specific. Farrice should lightly customize each to match Javier's actual speech patterns before handing them over.

---

## VARIANT 1 — Warm Intro (Javier to 1-3 specific contacts)

**Use case:** Javier knows 1-3 specific people who are running something — a nonprofit, a coaching practice, a service business, a ministry — and are *stuck at the same articulation problem* HVC was stuck at. Javier reaches out personally, not on Farrice's behalf.

**Script:**

---

> Subject: *This is going to sound random but hear me out*
>
> Hey [Name],
>
> This is going to sound random but I've been thinking about you.
>
> Do you remember the HVC website six months ago? How every time I tried to explain what we did, I'd get 3 sentences in and watch the other person's eyes glaze over? That entire problem got fixed by one person.
>
> His name is Farrice Cain. He spent about a week with HVC and handed me a rebuild of our positioning, our ICP, our homepage copy, our newsletter architecture, and the Sunday Dinners growth strategy. Nine documents, all interconnected. I've been sitting with it for a while and I keep coming back to the same thought: this is the work you need for [THEIR THING].
>
> I'm not getting a kickback. I'm telling you because I watched him do it for me and I know the same specific stuckness in your work. Your stuff is *good*. The articulation hasn't caught up to the substance yet.
>
> Would it be okay if I intro'd the two of you? He does a free 20-minute fit call where he basically asks you one question and tells you whether he can help or not. No pressure either way.
>
> Let me know.
>
> — Javier

---

**Why it works:**
- Opens with a specific memory of Javier's OWN stuckness → recipient relates instead of feeling sold
- Names the mechanism ("articulation hasn't caught up to substance") without technical jargon
- Explicitly says "I'm not getting a kickback" (even if there is one — see Variant 2) → credibility
- Ends with a permission ask, not an intro-sent → recipient controls the pace
- The "one question" framing makes the free call sound valuable, not salesy

**Who Javier sends this to:** people he'd vouch for to his own mother. Not a blast. 1-3 specific names. Quality over quantity.

## VARIANT 2 — Paid Referral (HVC Treasury Kickback)

**Use case:** Same intro as Variant 1, but with a formalized referral structure that benefits HVC's nonprofit treasury. Farrice pays a percentage of any closed deal back to HVC as a tax-deductible donation. This is the *sustainable* version — Javier can send this 10+ times a year and HVC's treasury grows every time one closes.

**Pre-work before Javier sends anything (Farrice's side):**
- Set the rate. Suggested: **15% of closed-deal revenue, donated to HVC as a 501(c)(3) contribution.** That's a real number — on a $2,997 tier, HVC gets $449.55. On a $4,997 tier, $749.55.
- Send Javier the signup link / booking link to use (unique per referrer if Calendly or similar supports it, so tracking is clean).
- Commit to quarterly reconciliation. No opacity, no "I'll get to it."

**Script for Javier:**

---

> Subject: *Thought of you — quick intro*
>
> Hey [Name],
>
> I've been meaning to send this to you for a while.
>
> There's a guy I worked with on the HVC repositioning — Farrice Cain — and he just productized the exact system he used with us. I've been watching what you've been building with [THEIR THING] and you're running into the same problem I was: your work is strong, but the articulation around it isn't catching up. Homepage, positioning, ICP clarity, all of it.
>
> He's doing something called Parallax ICP Intelligence — comes in three tiers depending on how deep you need to go. The middle tier is pretty much what I got.
>
> Full disclosure: if you end up working with him, 15% of what he earns goes back to HVC as a donation. So I'm not pretending this is neutral. But I'm also not sending it to anyone I don't actually think it fits. You're on a short list.
>
> Here's the booking link if you want to just take a look at what he offers: [LINK]
>
> Or if it's a no, no hard feelings — just wanted it on your radar.
>
> — Javier

---

**Why this works:**
- Transparency about the kickback builds more trust than hiding it would. Recipients smell hidden incentives anyway.
- "You're on a short list" converts the financial disclosure into status ("he's being selective"), not a red flag
- Binary close: booking link OR "no hard feelings" → no awkward middle state
- HVC treasury benefit is structural, not exploitative

**Mechanics:**
- Farrice tracks the referral (via unique booking link or "how did you hear about us" field)
- On close, Farrice donates 15% to HVC within 30 days
- Javier gets a receipt he can include in HVC's annual impact report ("This year, our referral program generated $X in tax-deductible contributions toward the Sunday Dinners scaling strategy.")

## VARIANT 3 — Case Study Feature / Lead-Gen Magnet

**Use case:** Javier's network includes other nonprofit founders, coaches, ministry leaders, and mission-driven solopreneurs. Rather than asking them if they want Farrice's service, Javier offers them HVC's story as a *template they can study*. This is a soft-sell — it converts "here's an expensive service" into "here's a case study I think you'd learn from."

**Script:**

---

> Subject: *Something you might actually use*
>
> Hey [Name],
>
> Quick one. I don't know if you saw, but we just put out a case study of the full HVC rebuild — how we went from "nobody can explain what we do in 5 seconds" to having an actual positioning system, newsletter, and content strategy that holds together.
>
> It's a pretty detailed breakdown. Not a sales pitch, a teardown. You can steal from it — the ICP framework, the Grand Central approach to content, the way we restructured the homepage, the funnel from free newsletter to the Unstoppable Parent program. All of it is laid out.
>
> Honestly, the single highest-leverage thing in there is the StoryBrand before/after of our homepage hero. I bet [THEIR WEBSITE THING] has the same issue ours did. If you want to just read that section, it's probably worth 20 minutes of your life.
>
> Here's the link: [CASE STUDY URL]
>
> The guy who did the work with me is available if you want to go deeper, but most of what's in the case study you can implement yourself.
>
> — Javier

---

**Why this works:**
- Gives before it asks. Sends a resource, not a pitch. Positions Javier as generous, not salesy.
- The "you can steal from it" framing is counterintuitive — it makes the case study MORE trusted, not less. People who tell you to steal are telling the truth.
- Names a specific highest-leverage chunk ("StoryBrand before/after of our homepage hero") so the recipient gets an obvious entry point rather than a 900-word block of reading
- Soft-sells the service at the bottom ("available if you want to go deeper") without it being the main event
- Works as a recruitment tool — if 10 people read the case study and 1 books a call, the other 9 become future pipeline

**What Farrice needs to have ready before Javier sends this:**
- Public link to the case study (a nice-looking version, not the markdown)
- Tracking — who clicks, who scrolls, who books
- A "get the full methodology" CTA at the bottom of the case study that drops into an email list

## DISTRIBUTION STRATEGY (for Farrice's planning)

| Variant | Frequency | Target audience | Expected conversion |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Variant 1 (Warm Intro) | 1-3 sends, one-time | Javier's closest trust circle | 50-70% reply, 20-30% book call, 10-15% close |
| Variant 2 (Paid Referral) | 5-10 sends, quarterly | Javier's broader professional network | 20-30% reply, 8-12% book call, 3-5% close |
| Variant 3 (Case Study Feature) | Unlimited, anytime | Anyone Javier respects | 40-60% read, 5-10% book call, 1-3% close |

**Start with Variant 1.** If Javier is willing to do 1-3 warm intros, that's higher-conviction than any of the others. Variants 2 and 3 compound over the year as the top of a passive pipeline.

**Do not ask Javier to send all three.** Pick one based on which he's most comfortable with. Over-asking a friend is the fastest way to kill the referral channel entirely.
