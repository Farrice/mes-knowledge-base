# Language Map — Parallax ICP Intelligence™

Verbatim phrases, sourced from Recall grounding (real transcripts, real creator content, real forum language) and the Dai Media consumer-posture lens. Do NOT paraphrase these — when writing sales copy, emails, DMs, landing pages, use these strings verbatim or very close to verbatim.

The rule: if Maya Patel (Dr. Maya Patel, the composite from Deep ICP reference) or any of the three patient-zero humans would *wince* at a phrase, it's banned. If they'd *lean in*, it's the word.

## Avatar 01 — The Stalled Substack Operator

### AVOID-WORDS (trigger slop detection or identity flinch)
- "creator" — flattens them; they don't see themselves as Mr. Beast's cousin
- "content" (as noun) — they write essays, not content
- "hustle" / "grind"
- "monetize your audience" — nails-on-chalkboard
- "scale your newsletter" — corporate
- "funnel"
- "growth hack" / "growth hacking"
- "side hustle"
- "niche down" — they've been told this 400 times and it hasn't worked
- "personal brand" — feels grimy
- "subscriber acquisition"
- "email list" (as the noun for their work) — reduces the work to a database
- "content calendar"
- "productize your expertise"
- "the creator economy"
- "write for your ideal reader" — they hear this in every Substack tutorial and have no idea what it operationally means

### USE-WORDS (their native vocabulary — verbatim from Recall transcripts and Substack forums)
- "publication" (not "newsletter," not "blog")
- "shape" — "I want my work to have a shape" (high-resonance word for this avatar)
- "the reader on the other end of the sentence"
- "writing into a void" (from Cam Gunel transcript — exact phrase used by this avatar's forums)
- "posting into the void"
- "paid conversion"
- "archive" — their body of work
- "pillar posts"
- "my topic" (not "my niche")
- "the post I was proud of"
- "stalled"
- "crystallize" — matches Parallax product name; they use this word for their own clarity
- "who this is for"
- "the specific person"
- "the line I draw around who this is for"
- "a body of work"
- "the 99% who quit vs the 1% who built thriving writing businesses" (Cam Gunel, verbatim)
- "I wish I had this when I was starting out" (Justin Welsh, verbatim)

### IDENTITY PHRASES (how they describe themselves — lift verbatim for copy)
- "I write a newsletter about [X]."
- "I write a publication on [X]."
- "I'm a writer." (said cautiously, with the weight of not-yet-monetizing)
- "I write into the silence."
- "I know my topic better than anyone in my circle."
- "I have an audience, I don't have a business yet."
- "I've been writing for [X months/years] and I think I'm missing the ICP layer."

## Avatar 02 — The Signal Bleeder

### AVOID-WORDS
- "thought leader" — instant flinch
- "influencer" — lower-status than consulting to them
- "personal brand" — see above
- "LinkedIn ninja"
- "6-figure consultant" — outcome-as-identity, reads as guru marketing
- "lead magnet"
- "funnel" / "sales funnel"
- "monetize your expertise"
- "hot takes"
- "content machine"
- "authority positioning" — they are authority; they don't need "positioning"
- "build your empire"
- "productized consulting" (ironically — despite the offer shape, this *phrase* reads gimmicky to them; use "a specific shape to the work" instead)
- "coach" / "consultant coach" — they don't want to be coached, they want to be diagnosed
- "scale your practice" — feels MLM-adjacent

### USE-WORDS
- "practice" (what they run — "my practice") [not "business"]
- "engagements" (not "clients," when plural and formal)
- "pipeline"
- "the right clients" (from Deep ICP Language Map — verbatim USE word)
- "attract the right clients"
- "sustainable practice" (from Deep ICP)
- "professional authority" (from Deep ICP)
- "positioning" (yes; they use this word — it's "personal brand" that triggers the flinch)
- "ICP" (they use the acronym — "what's my actual ICP")
- "reach" / "impact" (from Deep ICP USE list)
- "client pathway" (from Deep ICP)
- "pick-your-brain" / "coffee chat" (they complain in this language)
- "Sunday dread"
- "hot-take theater" (derogatory term they use for bad LinkedIn)
- "signal" vs "noise"
- "receipts" ("I have receipts" — proof language they use)
- "I left [company] to do this on purpose"

### IDENTITY PHRASES
- "I'm an independent [craft] consultant."
- "I do [craft] for [vertical]."
- "I used to run [function] at [company]."
- "I'm not a coach." (a self-differentiator — lift for copy)
- "I'm post-VP. I'm not a creator."
- "I built real things inside real companies."
- "I left a real job to do this work."

## Avatar 03 — The Mission Founder With A Thin Room

### AVOID-WORDS
- "non-profit marketing"
- "impact storytelling" — feels Edelman-foundation
- "stakeholder alignment"
- "social entrepreneur" — feels TED 2014
- "mission-driven brand"
- "purpose economy"
- "value proposition" — reads corporate
- "target demographic"
- "impact metrics" (in copy — they use it in grants, but it's not emotional language)
- "movement" used as verb ("we movement-build") — they let *others* use the word for them
- "community" (as a brand-y noun — they use it real, but beware overuse that signals non-profit fluff)
- "disruption"
- "scale" as naked verb — say "grow," "spread," "open new cities," "reach new rooms" instead

### USE-WORDS
- "the work" (how they refer to what they do — "this work")
- "the room" / "the table" (literal or metaphorical gathering)
- "believers" vs "buyers" (their own felt distinction)
- "founder-dependent"
- "outliving me"
- "a sentence" — "I need a sentence for this"
- "hand off"
- "the pitch that requires me to be in the room"
- "crystallize" — yes, this word works here too, especially in grant context
- "the center of the table"
- "a practice" (weekly habit, ritual)
- "invitation" (vs "offer")
- "the thing" — their specific program/practice (they often call it "the thing")
- "a shape the work can hold"
- "I want this to outlive me"
- "we have believers, we need buyers"

### IDENTITY PHRASES
- "I started this because nothing like it existed."
- "I'm building [specific program name]."
- "I'm the founder of [org]."
- "I do this work full-time." (carries weight — means "I've staked my life on it")
- "I'm still in the classroom." (Javier's phrase — direct lift for mission-founders who are still practicing)
- "I'm not a marketer, I'm a [practitioner / teacher / convener]."
