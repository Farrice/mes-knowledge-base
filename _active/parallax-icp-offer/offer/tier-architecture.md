## TIER 1 — PARALLAX CRYSTAL

| | |
|---|---|
| **Price** | $1,497 |
| **Tagline** | *The reader on the other end of every sentence you write — named, placed, and handed back to you in a document another human can operate.* |
| **Who** | Avatar 01 (Stalled Substack Operator) primary. Avatar 02 early adopters. |
| **Timeline** | 7 days from kickoff to delivery |

### What's IN

- **The Crystal Document** — 6-8 page ICP profile modeled on the HVC grounding document. Patient Zero (3 named real humans the writer could DM tomorrow). Consumer Posture (occupation / activity / thought process — not demographics). Identity Sentence. Pain Ladder (active-buying vs pre-contemplation split). Trigger Event (specific moment they search for a solution).
- **The Language Map** — avoid-words list (what triggers identity flinch in this reader) + use-words list (their verbatim vocabulary, pulled from real transcripts) + 3 identity phrases they use to describe themselves.
- **The Five Pillar Posts** — five specific post premises with hooks drafted, each one mapped to a pain-ladder rung. Not "content ideas." The actual opening sentences.
- **One 30-day Publication Calendar** — 12 posts paced across 4 weeks, each tagged by ladder rung and by Jobs-to-be-done.
- **A 45-minute handoff call** — Farrice walks through the document live, answers questions, and coaches the first post-rewrite.

### What's OUT

- No brand voice guide (that's Tier 2).
- No revenue architecture (that's Tier 2).
- No done-with-you drafting (that's Tier 3).
- No testimonial collection or conversion copywriting.

### Ascension path

Day 30 post-delivery: Farrice reviews one shipped piece of content against the Crystal (free — built into delivery). If the writer has used the Crystal and wants the revenue architecture built on top of it, that's the Tier 2 upgrade at a $1,000 credit (they pay $1,997 instead of $2,997). If they want drafts ghostwritten off the Crystal, that's retainer conversation.

### Deliverable format

Google Doc (pageless — per `feedback_google-docs-pageless.md`). Owned by the client. Parallax retains derivative rights (can show anonymized version as case study).

## TIER 2 — PARALLAX ARCHITECTURE

| | |
|---|---|
| **Price** | $2,997 |
| **Tagline** | *Your reader, your voice, and the revenue ladder they climb once they find you — one integrated operating document.* |
| **Who** | Avatar 02 (Signal Bleeder) primary. Avatar 01 upgrade path. Avatar 03 entry point. |
| **Timeline** | 14 days from kickoff to delivery |

### What's IN

Everything in Tier 1, PLUS:

- **The Brand Voice Foundation** — essence, story, voice calibration ("sounds like" / "does not sound like"). Modeled on the HVC Brand Foundation doc. 4-6 pages.
- **The Grace City Blueprint** — editorial north star + destinations (specific 12-month targets) + content lines (what goes where) + passenger profiles (3 ICP people rendered as specific humans). The transit-map diagram. This is the "your content ecosystem, mapped" asset.
- **The Revenue Ladder** — 5 rungs from free to the top-tier offer they currently run (or the one they should run). Each rung has a name, a price band, a specific what-you-get, and the natural step from the rung below.
- **One 90-day Publication Calendar** — 3 months paced, tagged to ladder rungs AND ascension gates (what each piece is supposed to move the reader toward).
- **A StoryBrand-style Message Audit of their current homepage/About page** — current copy scored, 5 structural issues named, BEFORE/AFTER hero rewrite drafted. This is the single most visible proof artifact in the package.
- **A 75-minute handoff call + one 30-min follow-up call at Day 45.**

### What's OUT

- No drafting of ongoing content (Tier 3).
- No running the launch (Tier 3).
- No testimonial ask scripts (Tier 3).

### Ascension path

At Day 60 post-delivery: if the client is deploying the Architecture and wants Farrice to execute a launch on top of it, that's Tier 3 at $3,997 (instead of $4,997) — the Architecture investment counts as $1,000 credit.

### Why $2,997 not $1,997 or $2,497

Decoy structure is intentional and Priestley-correct. The middle tier sits at 2x the entry (not 1.5x) because the JUMP from "ICP document" to "ICP + voice + revenue architecture + audited homepage rewrite" is a CATEGORY jump, not a SIZE jump. Tier 2 is not "more Tier 1." It's a different object. Pricing it at $1,997 would signal it's just a bigger Tier 1 and collapse the perceived category boundary. At $2,997, it reads as what it is: the complete operating document. This also preserves the decoy effect on Tier 3 — the jump from $2,997 to $4,997 (+$2,000) feels proportional to the jump from $1,497 to $2,997 (+$1,500), so $4,997 doesn't feel like a cliff.

Priestley: price by stakes, not by inputs. Avatar 03's stakes if they DON'T solve this:

$4,997 is 2% of one lost grant. The price is priced at the *relief* of solving it, not at the *inputs* required to build it.

## DECOY STRUCTURE ANALYSIS

| Tier | Price | What's unique vs tier below | Why it's not "same thing, more of it" |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $1,497 | ICP document + language map + 5 pillars + 30-day calendar | The WHO |
| 2 | $2,997 | + Brand voice + Revenue ladder + 90-day calendar + Message audit | The WHO + HOW (systems) |
| 3 | $4,997 | + Identity architecture + co-written pillars + launch piece + testimonial system | The WHO + HOW + WE DO IT WITH YOU |

The three tiers are not "ICP / ICP+ / ICP++." They answer three different questions:
- **Tier 1**: "Who am I actually writing to?"
- **Tier 2**: "What's the full system that makes them find me and pay?"
- **Tier 3**: "Will you help me turn this on with my own voice on the launch piece?"

A prospect who only needs the first question answered cannot be upsold honestly to Tier 2. A prospect who needs the second cannot be talked down to Tier 1 — they'd get the wrong object. The tiering is a match-to-need function, not a volume ladder.

## THE "WHAT DO I GET?" SELF-INTEREST TEST

Per `feedback_three-beat-copy-standard.md`, each tier must answer "what do I get?" in concrete artifacts, not abstract benefits.

**Tier 1 — What do you get?** A 6-8 page document naming three specific real humans you could DM tomorrow as your ideal reader, their exact language (use-words, avoid-words), five post hooks ready to ship, and a 30-day publishing schedule.

**Tier 2 — What do you get?** All of Tier 1, plus a brand voice guide, a 5-rung revenue ladder with prices, a 90-day content calendar, and a BEFORE/AFTER rewrite of your current homepage hero section and CTAs.

**Tier 3 — What do you get?** All of Tier 2, plus three pillar pieces co-written with you and ready to ship, your launch announcement post drafted, a repositioning architecture (what category you actually occupy vs what you've been calling yourself), and testimonial scripts to send to 5-10 believers to convert them into public proof.

All three pass. No "clarity." No "alignment." No "visibility." Artifacts.

## PARALLAX-BRANDED, NOT GENERIC

Rejected names (flagged as generic):
- Starter / Standard / Premium — lazy SaaS ladder
- Basic / Pro / Agency — category theft from marketing tools
- Essentials / Complete / Concierge — McKinsey cosplay

**Chosen names and why:**
- **The ICP Tell** — "tell" is poker/writer language; it's the specific moment the reader gets accidentally revealed. Captures the diagnostic nature. Avatar 01 uses "tell" naturally.
- **Parallax Crystal** — "crystallize" is in the cross-avatar USE-words list (all three avatars use it). The product name IS one of their words.
- **Parallax Architecture** — "architecture" signals operating-system depth, not document. Avatar 02 is a practitioner; they respect architecture as a word.
- **Parallax Launch** — launches are events, not deliverables. The word activates Avatar 03's specific pain ("we opened Houston. 3 people came"). Launch is what they didn't get last time.

The names are the product's promise. Crystal = clarity, Architecture = system, Launch = event. Same method, three depths, three verbs.
