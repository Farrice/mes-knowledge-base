## What Each Surface OWNS (exclusive language territory)

### Parallax Substack OWNS
- The word "parallax" as an idea, a worldview, a way of seeing
- Zeitgeist riffs on AI, culture, manifestos (Artemis II Polymaths, Digital Nicotine, Anti-Hustle, AI Emotions)
- "For people who see everything from more than one angle"
- Prompt packs as micro-transformation product
- Polymath identity as worldview piece
- Essay-length (900-1800 word) patient reveals

**LinkedIn may reference Parallax. LinkedIn may NOT reuse Substack hook lines, subheadings, or closers.**

### LinkedIn About Section OWNS
- The reader's Sunday-night dashboard scroll (the moment of recognition)
- "Your work has a reader. You just haven't named the one."
- The language of "a shape to the work"
- Reader-as-protagonist frame ("You're the writer who…", "You've built…")
- The "one question" close: *"If a stranger landed on your homepage for five seconds, what would they say you do?"*

**Posts may NOT open with the Sunday-night dashboard scroll (it's profile territory).**
**Posts may NOT use "a shape to the work" as the signature hook (it's About territory).**

### LinkedIn Posts OWN
- Specific mechanism reveals — the 9-document system, the articulation layer, the "Broken Vase" story from HVC
- "You have a reader. We find the reader." as a working thesis they test
- ICP pain diagnosis in the form of specific dashboard scenarios ("42% open rate, 0.8% paid conversion")
- Contrarian frames that bait quote-shares ("You are not actually looking for more readers")
- Proof moments — Javier's "truly magical" quote, the 31/70 homepage score, the 0 digital footprint
- Signature format: **The Parallax Cut** (defined in `signature-format.md`)

**Substack may NOT use the Javier case study specifics (those are LinkedIn post fuel).**
**About may NOT name specific dollar figures (those are post + sales page territory).**

## Voice Registers (do not cross-contaminate)

**Substack register**: Slower cadence. Room for a 200-word paragraph. Permits abstraction when earned. "Essayistic."

**LinkedIn Posts register**: Farrice-to-a-friend-at-coffee. Short sentences punctuated by longer ones. Zero polish at sentence transitions. Specific > abstract. Would Farrice say this out loud? If no, rewrite.

**LinkedIn About register**: Reader-as-protagonist. Second person ("You"). The reader is the main character. Farrice enters as the answer to a problem the reader can feel. No monologue.

**Rule**: If you read a LinkedIn post and it sounds like a Substack essay, rewrite. If you read the About section and it sounds like a post, rewrite. Each surface has a different nervous-system frequency.

## How to Use This Map

1. Before writing any LinkedIn post, check which surface it's for.
2. Check the Banned Overlaps table. If a phrase lives elsewhere, pick a different phrase.
3. Check the USE-word allocation. If you're stealing from the About section's territory, stop.
4. Run the voice register test. Wrong register = rewrite.
5. After drafting, grep `_active/parallax-icp-offer/` for any distinctive phrase from the draft. If it appears in another surface's document, rewrite.

This map is the gate. Deliverables 1-5 in this folder were written against this map. If the map changes, they all get re-audited.
