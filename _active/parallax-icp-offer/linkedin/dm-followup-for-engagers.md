# DM Follow-Up Script — For People Who Engage on Posts

> The post is the hook. The comment is the confirmation. The DM is the conversation.
> Rule: the DM is a conversation, not a pitch. Free ICP Tell is offered only after 3+ exchanges — *earned*, not shoved.

---

## The Thread Architecture (Universal)

```
1. Comment on their post with a mini-post reply (substantive, not "great post!")
2. They engage on OUR post (comment, quote-share, reaction)
3. THEIR comment triggers our Turn 1 DM (warm open, question)
4. They respond → Turn 2 DM (deepen the specific thing they mentioned)
5. They respond → Turn 3 DM (optional ICP Tell offer, only if earned)
```

**Critical rules**:
- **Never DM from a cold comment with "I'd love to chat."** Always respond publicly in the comment thread first, continue the energy.
- **Turn 1 DM is NEVER about me.** It's about the specific thing they said.
- **No offer before Turn 3.** If they ask about working together earlier, answer briefly then redirect to the substance of the conversation.
- **If they ghost after Turn 1 or 2, let it die.** Never double-message. Ever. The follow-up is what turns a warm LinkedIn into a spam LinkedIn.
- **Friend test on every DM**: "Would Farrice actually send this to a smart friend?" If it reads like a template, rewrite.

## Variant 1 — The High-Engagement Commenter

**Who**: Someone who left a thoughtful multi-sentence comment on one of the 7 posts. They engaged with the specific idea, named their own experience, or asked a real question.

**Their comment might look like**:
> *"This is exactly where I am. I have 4K subscribers, I post twice a week, and I can feel the ceiling. Your line about 'admiration is free, recognition costs money' is going to live in my head."*

### Turn 1 DM (send within 60 minutes of their comment)

```
Hey [First name] — the fact that line stuck with you probably means you felt it before you read it, if that makes sense. 

Curious: when you say you can feel the ceiling, what does that feel like specifically? Is it the open-rate/paid-conversion gap, or something earlier than that — like a sense that the posts are drifting?

(Not asking to pitch anything — just genuinely curious because you named it cleanly.)
```

**Why this works**:
- Opens with the specific thing THEY said, not about us.
- Asks a real question that invites them to go deeper than their comment did.
- Explicitly disarms the sales-pitch suspicion ("Not asking to pitch anything").
- No "if you want to jump on a call" crap.
- Voice check: Farrice would absolutely send this.

### Turn 2 DM (if they reply with specifics)

```
That tracks. The "I can feel it but can't name it" feeling usually means the ICP is half-formed — you know some of the reader's texture but not the specific person. 

Random test that might help: if you had to pick one human you've met in real life who reads your stuff, and you had to write the next post *only* to that person — who would it be, and what would change?

(Most writers find that question more clarifying than the usual "describe your ideal reader demographics.")
```

**Why this works**:
- Validates their specificity without flattering them.
- Gives them a USEFUL diagnostic tool for free (the real-human test).
- Still no offer. Still just two writers talking.
- "Random test that might help" is voice-native Farrice.

### Turn 3 DM (only if they try the test and come back with a real answer, OR if they ask about the work explicitly)

```
Nice — the fact that you can name Sarah and see that the post would drop the preamble and get specific about the Tuesday-morning practice is about 70% of the move.

What I do for people stuck at this exact point is a 20-minute thing called the ICP Tell. We find the gap on a call and I tell you what the fix would be. Free, no pitch, useful whether or not we work together.

If it's nagging, here's the link: [Calendly]. If not, no pressure — your specificity is already ahead of most people I talk to.
```

**Why this works**:
- The offer is wrapped in validation of their OWN progress.
- "Free, no pitch" is stated twice in the sequence — it's the thing that buys the click.
- Explicit permission to decline: "If not, no pressure."
- No scarcity pressure. No "only 4 spots this month" (that belongs on the sales page, not in DMs).

## Variant 2 — The Simple Reactor (just liked or dropped a 🙌 emoji)

**Who**: Someone who engaged minimally — a like, a fire emoji, a one-word comment ("truth," "yes," "💯"). They're signaling interest but haven't invested yet.

**Their comment might look like**:
> *"🔥"* or *"So real"* or just a like on the post.

### Turn 1 DM (send within 2-4 hours — not instant, but same day)

```
Hey [First name] — saw you liked the [specific post reference, e.g., "niche / reader"] post. Short question: was it the "niche is a topic, reader is a face" framing that landed, or something else in there?

(Asking because I'm trying to figure out which part of the post is doing the work — your answer would actually help.)
```

**Why this works**:
- Gives them a reason to respond beyond small talk — you're asking for their feedback.
- Frames the ask as helping you, not interrogating them.
- Low friction — one-question reply possible.
- Voice: "Asking because I'm trying to figure out" — human, not templated.

### If they respond with a thoughtful answer

Pivot to the Variant 1 Turn 2 + 3 flow. They've moved from reactor to engaged commenter.

### If they respond with one word ("Yes" / "That one")

Drop. Don't push. Let the next post do the work.

### If they don't respond at all

**Do not follow up.** Let them stay warm in the feed. They'll engage again when the next post lands.

## Variant 3 — The Quote-Sharer (reposted with a screenshot highlighting The Cut)

**Who**: Someone who quote-shared or commented-and-shared one of the posts with their own framing. This is the highest-signal engagement — they've put their own reputation next to your line.

**Their share might look like**:
> *"This. The whole week summarized: [screenshot of The Cut line]"* — and then they tagged 2-3 peers.

### Turn 1 DM (send within 2 hours — this is a hot lead)

```
Hey [First name] — genuinely appreciate the share. The people you tagged (especially [specific name]) seem like they're in the same part of the journey you are, based on their recent posts. 

Curious: what was the context in your work when the line hit? Was it a specific call, a specific post that flopped, something more drifting?

(Asking because your quote-share added a frame to the post that I didn't put there — "the whole week summarized" is a better thesis than the thesis. Want to understand what you were seeing.)
```

**Why this works**:
- Flatters their judgment specifically (the tagged people thing shows you looked at their network).
- Asks about a specific moment in their life — invites a real story.
- Compliments their FRAMING of the post, not them as a person (harder to decline responding to genuine intellectual engagement than generic flattery).
- Positions them as a peer, not a prospect.

### Turn 2 DM (after they share the context)

```
That makes sense. The [specific pattern they described — e.g., "fourth call in a row where they loved the content and didn't buy"] is exactly the Signal Bleeder pattern, and it's usually fixable in a week once you get the reader crystallized.

Since you're clearly thinking about this at the meta level — what's the next move you were considering before you saw the post? Hiring, building a new offer, rewriting the about page?

(Trying to figure out where the friction actually is for you.)
```

**Why this works**:
- Validates their pattern by naming it (avatar-specific — "Signal Bleeder").
- Gives them a terminology they may adopt for themselves.
- Asks what they were already going to do, so the offer (if it comes) doesn't feel imposed.

### Turn 3 DM (earned)

```
Gotcha. Given where you are, my honest take is the fastest high-leverage move is the ICP crystal before you hire the ghostwriter or rewrite the page, because everything downstream compounds off the ICP being right. 

What I normally do for someone in your spot is a free 20-min ICP Tell — I pressure-test your current ICP sentence and you walk away with a clearer read whether you need a done-for-you sprint or just a tweak.

Here's the link if it's useful: [Calendly]. If not, shoot me the about-page rewrite when it's done — I'll read it.
```

**Why this works**:
- "My honest take" is voice-native Farrice.
- Concrete recommendation (ICP before ghostwriter) even if they don't buy.
- The offer is framed as useful-whether-or-not-they-take-it.
- "Shoot me the about-page rewrite — I'll read it" is a generous closer that keeps the relationship warm even if they never book.

## Universal DM Rules

### DO
- Use first names always.
- Reference their *specific* words or specific post.
- Ask one question per DM.
- Keep each DM under 100 words (ideally under 60).
- Use lowercase "hey" openers (matches LinkedIn DM native texture).
- Sign off with a period, not "best!" or "thanks!".

### DON'T
- Copy-paste the same DM.
- Start with "I just wanted to reach out because..."
- Send voice notes in LinkedIn DMs (weird platform, not the vibe yet).
- Use "a few thoughts on your situation" or other consultant-speak.
- Offer free calls / consultations / audits in Turn 1 or Turn 2.
- Mention price in DMs — ever. Prices live on the sales page and the offer one-pager.
- Use any emoji beyond the occasional unpretentious one (no fire, no star, no money-bag).

### HARD STOPS
- If they respond negatively ("not interested," "please remove me," "this feels like a pitch"), acknowledge once and drop forever.
- If they ghost for 48 hours, the thread is dead. Do not "following up!" them.
- If they say yes to the ICP Tell but don't book, send ONE follow-up with the link after 48 hours. If no book after that, drop.

## Tracking (goes into Notion or a simple sheet)

Columns:
- Date of their engagement
- Post that triggered it
- Variant used (1, 2, or 3)
- Turn 1 sent?
- Their reply?
- Turn 2 sent?
- Their reply?
- Turn 3 sent?
- Outcome (booked ICP Tell / ghosted / declined / parked)

**Why track**: after 30 days, you can see which posts drove real DM conversations (not just likes), which variant converts best, and at what turn people drop off. This feeds the Post 2 / Post 4 optimization for week 2+.

## One Master Template to NEVER Use

Banned forever:

```
❌ Hey [Name]! Saw you engaged with my recent post. I help [niche] with [service]. Would love to hop on a quick call to see if we'd be a fit. Here's my Calendly: [link]
```

This is everything the avatars have a flinch response to. It's what every LinkedIn DM from every fractional consultant looks like. Avatar 01 deletes it. Avatar 02 reports it. Avatar 03 blocks.

Every single DM in this doc is designed to be the opposite of that template.
