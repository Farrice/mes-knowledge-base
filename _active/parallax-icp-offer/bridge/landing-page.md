## [MECHANISM]

## Three lenses. One crystal.

Most positioning help is one-dimensional. You get a brand guide, or an ICP template, or a content calendar, and none of it connects.

Parallax runs three expert lenses on your work in parallel. The document you get is the braid.

### Lens 1 — Consumer Posture

Demographics lie. "35-45, urban, college-educated" does not tell you how your reader sits up when someone describes their life. We map occupation (their symbolic role at the dinner party), activity (what they actually do at 10:30pm), and thought process (the inner monologue when they open their laptop on Sunday night). The result is a reader who feels specific, not one who fits a spreadsheet.

### Lens 2 — Deep Canvass

Every publication has two reader pains: the one they'll pay to solve this month, and the one they feel but can't name yet. Most writers write to the second and wonder why nobody buys. We map both pains into a ladder — and tag every piece of your publication to a rung. You stop writing into the void because you know which rung each piece is speaking to.

### Lens 3 — Language Map

We extract the verbatim words your ideal reader uses to describe themselves. And the words that make them flinch. You start writing in their vocabulary. Paid conversion stops being a mystery.

**You have the work. We externalize the reader.**

## [FAQ]

### Isn't this just another ICP template?

No. Templates give you blanks to fill in. The Crystal is the filled-in document, specific to your publication, your reader, your language. You don't fill in the blanks. We fill them in with real humans' names.

### I've paid for positioning help before. Why would this work when that didn't?

Most positioning is inward-facing: "get clearer on what YOU do." Parallax is outward-facing: "get specific about who's watching." Inward-clarity without outward-specificity is why the last document didn't move your paid conversion. Those aren't the same document. One is a brand guideline. The other is an operating manual for your next 30 posts.

### I'm not a creator or solopreneur. Is this for me?

Right. Parallax is built for writers, consultants, and founders. You won't find "creator economy" language anywhere in the work. If you run a publication, a practice, or a mission-driven organization, you're the buyer.

### How is this different from a $497 course?

A course teaches you the method. Parallax runs the method for you, on your specific work, and hands you the finished artifact. You're not buying education. You're buying the output.

### Why not a $97 template to try the method first?

Because most of our buyers have already bought 2-3 ICP templates that didn't work. A $97 version of Parallax would be the thing you're already running from. The free 20-minute call is the low-risk gateway — and it surfaces more than any template would.

### Do I have to be on Substack?

No. Substack, LinkedIn, Twitter, an independent website, or a traditional client practice all work. The document is platform-agnostic. The calendars are platform-specific.

### What if I only have 500 subscribers?

Then Tier 1 might be early. The ICP Tell call will tell you. If the answer is "come back at 1,000 subscribers," you'll hear it free.

### Can I pay in installments?

Crystal: full pay only. Architecture: 50/50 split available. Launch: 50/25/25 across kickoff, Day 14, and launch day. All via Stripe.
