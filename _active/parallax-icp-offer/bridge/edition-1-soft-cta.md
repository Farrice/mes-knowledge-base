# Edition 01 Soft-CTA (and Edition 05 Variant)

**Constraint:** Max 80 words. PS-style. Thematically tied to the edition content. No "announcing my new service" voice. Passes the *"would this feel jarring to a polymath reader?"* test.

**Placement:** After the existing reply-prompt close. Before the signature line. Visually set off with a divider.

---

## Edition 01 — The Manifesto (recommended placement)

The Manifesto ends on the reply prompt:

> *Hit reply and tell me: what did you stop talking about publicly because it didn't fit your "brand"?*

Then the signature:

> *Written by Farrice Cain*
> *Parallax — see everything from more than one angle.*

**Soft-CTA goes between the reply prompt and the signature.** Divider above it. Set it off as a PS block.

---

### PS block (70 words)

---

*P.S. — Side note, since Edition 01 readers tend to ask.*

*Outside of Parallax, I spend most of my week doing one thing for writers and consultants: building the document that names the specific reader their work has always been for. It's called Parallax ICP Intelligence. There's a free 20-minute diagnostic if your paid conversion is stalling and you can't tell why. [Book the ICP Tell →]*

*Back to your regularly scheduled polymath programming on Thursday.*

---

### Why this works

**Thematic tie:** Edition 01 is about the cost of pretending to be less than you are — and the specific pain of writing without a named reader is the same pain at a narrower scale. The PS doesn't lurch into a new frame; it threads the same needle.

**Voice test:** *"Back to your regularly scheduled polymath programming on Thursday"* is a line Farrice would actually say to a friend. "Side note, since Edition 01 readers tend to ask" is a conversational move, not a pitch setup. No "announcing." No "I'm excited to share." No "introducing."

**Self-awareness:** The "side note" + "regularly scheduled programming" framing explicitly codes this as *not the point of the newsletter.* Which is the honest truth — Parallax is not an ad channel for ICP Intelligence. That honesty is the permission to mention it at all.

**Two links, one action:** "Book the ICP Tell" routes to the landing page, where the application form is the primary CTA. The landing page does the heavier qualification. The PS only needs to open the door.

**What it avoids:**
- "Announcing" / "Introducing" / "Launching" — any framing that suggests the newsletter is a launch vehicle
- Urgency language — no "limited spots," no "this week only"
- Benefit language — no "get clearer on your ICP," no "finally know who you're writing to"
- Proof — no testimonial in the PS. Proof lives on the landing page.

**Word count:** 70 words (under 80-word cap).

## Edition 05 — Polymaths to the Moon (alternative placement)

Edition 05 is the stronger Revenue District placement per the trust pathway map. Its ending beat is permission-to-ship. A reader finishing Edition 05 is emotionally primed to take an action.

### PS block for Edition 05 (78 words)

---

*P.S. — For the polymaths with a publication.*

*Christina Koch trained for years before NASA let her fly. The work you've been doing — the research, the drafts, the publication nobody can quite describe yet — that's your training arc. When you're ready to name the specific person on the other end of every sentence you write, Parallax ICP Intelligence has a free 20-minute diagnostic for that. [Book the ICP Tell →]*

*Next edition drops Thursday. It's about patience.*

---

### Why Edition 05's soft-CTA lands differently

Edition 01's soft-CTA is a "side note" — establishes the offer exists for new subscribers, no pressure.

Edition 05's soft-CTA is an *application prompt* — threads the Artemis training-arc frame directly into the reader's own work. A reader who just finished reading about a 15-year training arc for one mission is primed to see their own publication as a training arc, and the Crystal as the equivalent of "NASA giving you the mission."

The two editions carry different energies. Edition 01 plants the seed. Edition 05 activates it.

---

## Deployment Decision

**Ship both.** Edition 01 gets the soft-CTA for every new subscriber (the long tail of people who subscribe after the launch and read Edition 01 as their first piece). Edition 05 gets the soft-CTA for the launch-week readers who experience the full arc.

**Do NOT put soft-CTAs in Editions 02, 03, 04.** Those editions live in Attention and Trust districts. A CTA there would break the pathway.

---

## Test criteria (before publishing)

Read the edition with the PS included. Ask:

1. **Would the polymath reader finish the reply prompt and keep reading into the PS without friction?** If they'd stop, the PS is breaking the flow — rework.
2. **Does the PS sound like Farrice talking to a friend, or like Farrice talking at a prospect?** If it sounds like the latter, rewrite.
3. **If you removed the PS, would the edition still work?** Yes — and that's the test. The PS is additive, not load-bearing. It should never feel like the point of the edition.

If any of those three fail, the PS gets cut and redrafted.
