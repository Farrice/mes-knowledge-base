# Trojan Horse Partners — 10 Candidates

**Frame (Nate Herk):** The Trojan Horse Partnership borrows an agency/community's audience via white-label delivery. The partner wins (client retention, expanded service, revenue share). You win (pre-qualified pipeline, no cold-outreach tax). Client wins (McKinsey-depth ICP work without finding it themselves).

**Rule 1:** Lead with what the partner gets, not what you want. The pitch opens with the partner's bottleneck, not yours.
**Rule 2:** Offer free white-label delivery for client #1 (proof round). Rev share (20%) applies from client #2 forward.
**Rule 3:** Not Jen's real estate network. Not coaching adjacents. Not personal-brand gurus who sell their own ICP courses (competitive, not complementary).

## PITCH-TO-THEM SCRIPT TEMPLATE (universal)

**Use for:** Any partner on this list. Customize the bracketed pieces per partner.

### Script T1 — Cold Partner Pitch

> [Partner Name],
>
> Not pitching — proposing. I run Parallax ICP Intelligence™ (ICP crystallization + positioning for writers, consultants, and mission-founders). The method worked on a 9-year-old nonprofit in Chicago in 9 days. The founder called it "truly magical."
>
> Here's why I'm writing: [SPECIFIC PATTERN YOU'VE OBSERVED IN THEIR COMMUNITY — e.g., "Foster members keep asking in Slack about paid-conversion stall and there's no productized fix at the right price point"]. Their problem is diagnosable and solvable, but they need the document, not another course.
>
> The proposal: I deliver one free Parallax engagement/quarter, white-labeled as "[Partner] x Parallax" for a member you pick. No cost to [Partner] or the member. For any paying engagements that originate from [Partner] going forward, 20% revenue share to [Partner]. Structural, not opaque.
>
> If interesting, happy to send the HVC case study + a 2-page partnership doc before any call. Or 20 minutes on the phone next week: [LINK].
>
> — Farrice

**Why it works:**
- Opens with "not pitching — proposing" (Nate Herk Zero Proof — pattern interrupt)
- Names their bottleneck BEFORE naming the offer (Rule 1)
- Free first client is the structural gift (Rule 2)
- Explicit 20% rev share is trust-builder — opacity here kills partnerships
- Ends with "case study + 2-page doc BEFORE a call" — removes the "do I have time to meet" friction

## THE FREE-FIRST-CLIENT MECHANIC (critical)

Every partner above gets the option of **one free client as proof.** This is not a loss leader — it's the validation step. Here's why it matters:

- The partner sends a member they want to retain — so they only pick a strong one.
- Farrice delivers the Parallax method full-depth (no compromise on Crystal or Architecture).
- The case study Farrice produces afterward becomes the sales asset FOR the partner.
- From client #2 onward, 20% rev share compounds with zero marginal acquisition cost.

**Math check:** If 3 partners each close 3 paying engagements in 90 days at avg $2K each, that's $18K revenue, $3.6K rev-share out, $14.4K net. Compares favorably to $0 from cold outreach bouncing against a cold audience.

## WHAT TO REFUSE

- Any partnership that asks Farrice to reduce the method to a template or "ICP workshop" format. The 6-page document IS the product. Workshops commoditize.
- Any partnership where the partner wants >30% rev share. 20% is fair; above that, Farrice is a subcontractor, not a partner.
- Any partnership that requires exclusivity. Parallax stays open to all channels.
- Any partnership that includes the word "content" in the partner's brand positioning. Avatar 01 actively flinches at "content." Partnering with a "content community" inherits the flinch.
