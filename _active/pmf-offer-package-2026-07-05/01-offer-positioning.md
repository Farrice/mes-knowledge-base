# Offer One-Pager: Claim-Safe AEO Audit-to-Retainer Sprint

## 1. Positioning Statement

**Category:** Substantiation-aware content and AI-search-citation service for regulated health brands.

**For:** Marketing leads and founders at funded (VC-backed or profitably bootstrapped) sports-nutrition and supplement brands under $10M revenue: big enough that an FTC letter is a real threat, too small for a $10M+ revenue gate like Common Thread Collective or Croud.

**Unlike:** Compliance vendors (Truli, EAS, ProPharma, The Regulatory Company) who review claims but never write the content, and AEO/GEO agencies (Percepture, 1Digital, Onely) who chase AI citations with zero health case studies and zero claims literacy.

**Because:** One classification pass grades every claim on both gates that matter now (FTC substantiation risk and AI-citation-worthiness) because in a regulated category they've become the same gate. Compliance vendors can't tell you whether a claim is the kind ChatGPT cites. AEO agencies can't tell you whether it survives a regulator's read. The Trust Layer methodology (`skills/claim-safe-health-marketing/`) runs both checks in one pass, backed by 2026's most aggressive FTC enforcement year on record (Amare Global, June; TruHeight, June) and a live, nameable proof point: four of six top-rated Amazon creatine gummies tested at near-zero actual creatine.

**The bullseye:** brands say they want more content, or better rankings. What they're actually managing is exposure: the fear that the next AI answer, or the next FTC docket, names them for the wrong reason. Nobody sells "stop being exposed and start being cited" as one motion. That's the gap.

## 3. Guarantee / Risk Reversal Per Rung

- **Teardown:** No ask. If it's not useful, it cost the recipient nothing and cost the sender 15 minutes.
- **Audit:** Delivered in 3–5 business days or the slot is free. Founder-pilot conditions (feedback + testimonial + case-study rights) are named up front, never a silent discount. No more than one new audit starts per week, regardless of how fast the three slots sell, so the turnaround promise holds even if all three land in the same week.
- **Install:** Fixed scope, fixed price, fixed two weeks. If the flagship asset and the rewritten claim set aren't delivered on schedule, the second week is free.
- **Retainer:** Month-one make-good. Month one ships a defined artifact list (claim-safe content batch, citation-check baseline across the three engines). If that list doesn't ship, month two is free. The guarantee covers the work, never the engines' behavior: no promise of citations, rankings, or FTC outcomes, because no honest vendor can make that promise.

## 4. Offer Name Candidates

1. **The Two-Gate Audit**: say-what-it-is, names the actual mechanism (compliance risk + citation-worthiness, one pass). Zero cultural risk; the tradeoff is it needs one sentence of explanation before it lands.
2. **Claim-Safe Citation Sprint**: descriptive, mirrors the rung names already in use internally, reads as competent and specific rather than clever. Safe but slightly long for a headline.
3. **Cited Clean**: evocative, short, plays on the supplement world's existing "clean" vocabulary (third-party tested, nothing hidden). Flag: "clean" already carries a specific meaning in this vertical (anti-doping / NSF Certified for Sport testing). The name must never imply doping certification or third-party lab testing; pair it with the scope line every time it's used standalone.
4. **The Proof Carrier**: evocative, ties directly to the core insight (their proof is good, it just isn't getting carried by the machine). Low cultural risk, but risks sounding like a logistics or shipping brand out of context; needs the subhead every time.
5. **Named & Nothing to Hide**: evocative, plays on both halves of the offer (get cited, survive scrutiny). Flag: "nothing to hide" can read as a legal-clearance promise ("we guarantee you're safe"), which directly contradicts binding fix #1. If used, it must run with the scope line in the same breath, or it gets cut.

**Recommendation:** lead with **The Two-Gate Audit** for the entry rung (it's already load-bearing in existing sound-bite language) and **Cited Clean** as the retainer-level brand name once the scope line is standing next to it in every asset.

**Decision (shipped):** Claim-Safe Citation Audit ships as the live name across all seven package assets. It's already load-bearing everywhere else; renaming later is cheaper than re-shooting six assets to catch up with this doc. Two-Gate Audit and Cited Clean stay logged above as the next rebrand option once a case study exists to earn the shorter, evocative name.

## 6. FAQ: The Three Hardest Objections

**Why not just hire a compliance lawyer?**
A lawyer tells you what you can't say. This tells you what you can say that still gets cited by AI search, and flags the risky claims for your lawyer to review faster and cheaper, because the risk surface is already mapped.

**Why not just hire an AEO agency?**
Every AEO agency in this research pass (Percepture, RevenueZen, First Page Sage) sells to healthcare/wellness in their marketing copy and shows zero health case studies in their portfolio. They optimize for citations without knowing which claims are safe to make in the first place. A claim that gets you cited and gets you an FTC letter isn't a win.

**Why trust a solo operator over an agency with a name?**
The two skills this offer bundles, claims-substantiation literacy and AI-citation strategy, are currently sold by two entirely separate provider categories that have never combined them into one productized SKU. A solo operator who built the classification engine for both reads both gates in a single pass, which no agency in this research sweep currently does.
