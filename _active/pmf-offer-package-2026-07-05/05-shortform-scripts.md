# Short-Form Video Scripts: Claim-Safe Citation Audit

Three screen-recorded AI-answer teardowns built on Seena Rez's PSAEP structure. Each sells the $500 Claim-Safe Citation Audit (3 pilot slots), not the full retainer. Delivery method shown on-screen (typing directly into ChatGPT/Perplexity) is also the real fulfillment method: no paid citation-monitoring tool, per the manual-cadence constraint.

**Production standard (all three):** Captions in Seena Rez house style: single line, 7 words max, center safe zone. Yellow for standard lines, white for the solution reveal/value prop/CTA, red used once per script for the pain point only. Hook must hit within 3 seconds or the video is cut and reshot.

**Scope line, spoken once per script in the CTA beat:** "Marketing-side claim hygiene, not legal advice." Farrice is not a lawyer; this is a citation and claim-classification audit, and client counsel signs off on anything flagged.
