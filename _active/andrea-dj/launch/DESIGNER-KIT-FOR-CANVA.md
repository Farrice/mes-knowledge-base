## Brand At A Glance

**Resonance** is a daytime, sober, curated dance party in Chicago for people seeking a committed partner. "Heart encounters, not head encounters."

**The Core Promise**: The music does the emotional labor so you don't have to.

**Visual Spine**: A daytime photograph of real people in a real room, with one sentence over it. The photo is the protagonist. The type is a caption.

**Key Non-Negotiables**:
- ✓ Daytime only (never 11pm club lighting)
- ✓ Sober (no bar, no alcohol visible)
- ✓ Curated (specific people, not open signup)
- ✓ Real bodies, real moments (no AI, no stock dancers, no posed group shots)
- ✓ Chicago-specific (always name the city and event date)
- ✓ Couples count (success metric is connections, not reach)

## The Visual System

### Color Palette (Variant A: Orange)

| Color | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| **Terracotta 600** | `#B8492E` | Headlines, CTA links, wordmark accent |
| **Midnight 900** | `#0F1A2E` | Body copy, text on cream background |
| **Cream 100** | `#F5EFE3` | Card backgrounds, background fills |
| **Ink 900** | `#1A1814` | Primary body text (slightly warmer than black) |
| **Slate 500** | `#7A6F62` | Dates, metadata, captions (small text) |

**What NOT to use**: Hot pink, neon, pure white, gold (gold is founder-signature only), purple, electric blue.

### Typography Rules

**For Headlines** (announcement, hooks, event titles):
- Font: GT Sectra or IBM Plex Serif
- Weight: 500 (medium)
- Size: 64px–72px for main headlines, 44px–56px for subheads
- Color: Terracotta-600 (#B8492E) for primary headlines, Midnight-900 for secondary
- No all-caps unless it's a date (JUNE 14, 2026)

**For Body Copy** (descriptions, narrative, explanatory text):
- Font: Inter
- Weight: 400 (regular)
- Size: 18px–20px for featured, 16px for standard body
- Color: Ink-900 (#1A1814)
- Line height: 1.6 (loose, readable)

**For Metadata** (dates, captions, small type):
- Font: Inter
- Weight: 600 (semibold)
- Size: 12px–14px
- Color: Slate-500 (#7A6F62)
- Tracking: 0.12em (slightly spread out)

**Voice in Typography**: Let scale do the work. Never use weight above 500. Bold looks aggressive. Size carries the emphasis.

## Photography Rules (The 11pm Test)

**The Golden Rule**: If a photo could have been taken at 11pm in a nightclub, it fails. Every image must read as obviously daytime, in a real room, with real bodies.

### What Makes a Good Resonance Photo

✓ **Daytime light only**: Shot at 2pm–4pm, real window light, south-facing if possible  
✓ **Real people, mid-gesture**: Someone mid-laugh, body leaning in, arm mid-raise — candid moments, not posed  
✓ **Mixed representation**: Visible diversity in race, body type, gender presentation  
✓ **Minimal background**: The room's architecture + the people carry the image (no plants, no decorations)  
✓ **Slight motion blur**: One body slightly blurred = real movement, not posed  
✓ **Close crop on the action**: Tight on hands, faces, dancing torsos (not wide crowd shots)  

### What Kills a Photo

✗ Club lighting, neon, fog machines, lasers  
✗ AI-rendered faces, perfect skin, retouched smoothness  
✗ Stock photography or posed group portraits  
✗ All bodies facing the DJ (stadium pose)  
✗ Backs of heads in a line (yoga class vibe)  
✗ Phones, glasses, alcohol bottles visible  
✗ Black-and-white, stylized golden-hour filters  
✗ Wellness-retreat aesthetic (candles, linen, meditation pose)  

### How to Source Photos for Templates

**Best case**: Use approved Resonance hero shots from the event (provided separately in the shared folder).

**If sourcing new images**: Search stock photo sites for "people dancing daylight studio" or "afternoon dance class real movement." You'll reject 95% of results because of the 11pm test. When in doubt, reject.

**Photo placement rule**: Photos occupy 65–75% of the flyer/post design. Type is a caption to the image, never competing with it.

## Flyer Template Specs

### Digital Flyer (IG Portrait — 1080×1350px)

**Layout**:
- Top 65–75% of canvas: Photograph (1080×880px minimum)
- Bottom 25–35%: Type area
- Wordmark (top-left corner, 24pt GT Sectra, cream or navy depending on photo contrast)
- Date stamp: Below photo in label-caps (JUNE 14, 2026 · CHICAGO)
- Headline: ≤8 words, GT Sectra 64–72pt, Midnight-900 or Terracotta-600
- Body line: ≤30 words total, Inter 18–20pt, Ink-900
- CTA: Single line at bottom, Inter 14pt, Terracotta-600 color for link
  Example: "Application opens June 1 · resonance.com/apply"
- Spacing: At least 24px (lg margin) between all elements

**Headline Discipline**: 
- Use ONE mechanic in ≤8 words
- GOOD: "A daytime dance room. Sober. Chicago." (Five words, three filters)
- BAD: "Join Chicago's premier curated singles dance experience!" (Too long, too generic)

**CTA Discipline**:
- Point to APPLICATION, not ticket sales
- GOOD: "Application opens June 1"
- BAD: "Buy tickets — only 50 spots, going fast"

### Physical Flyer (Letter Size — 8.5×11in, 300dpi, CMYK)

Same principles as digital, scaled up:
- Photo occupies 60–70% (slightly less than digital for breathing room)
- Headline: GT Sectra 72–96pt
- All other type scales proportionally
- 0.25" bleed, 0.5" safe area (for print)
- Paper: 100lb uncoated text or 80lb cover (never glossy — reads as nightclub)

## IG Post & Story Specs

### IG Feed Post (1080×1350px, vertical)

**Copy Structure** (150–250 words):
- Headline: Hook with a question or moment (Pattern: "Out-Loud-Asking" — use the words your ICP actually says)
  Example: "Where are all the good guys? Real question. We've heard it five times this month."
- Body: Show the room (concrete moment > abstraction)
  Example: "It's 2pm. The floor is wood. Someone's mid-laugh across the room and you notice. No phones. No bar. Just the music doing what it's supposed to do."
- Close: Filter or invitation (Hell-Yes Filter pattern)
  Example: "If you've tried everything and you're still looking, the room is open. If you're not sure, this isn't your event yet."

**Visual**: Photo (daytime, real moment) with text overlay option (overlay is optional; photo-first is the rule)

**Hashtags**: NONE (flyer aesthetic, not IG-caption aesthetic)

**Link in bio**: Points to application or waitlist, never to ticket sales

### IG Story (1080×1920px, full vertical)

**Slide 1**: Photo (daytime, dancers, joy)  
**Slide 2**: Headline over photo
"A daytime dance party"  
**Slide 3**: When + where
"Saturday July 18  
2:00–5:00 PM  
Chicago"  
**Slide 4**: Filter (question or moment)
"Ready to meet someone new?"  
"Application opens June 1"  
**Slide 5**: CTA sticker
"Link in bio" or "Apply now"  

Each slide: Single focus, large type (readable at thumb scroll), 15–30% transparency on text overlays (so photo reads through).

## Voice Rules (Copy Checklist)

Before you write ANY copy:

1. **No AI tells**: Never start with "Here's what..." or "Here's why..." No more than 2 em-dashes per piece (zero is better).
2. **Show > tell**: Describe what happens in the room, not what Resonance is "like."
3. **Name the enemy**: Apps, bars at 11pm, speed dating — be specific when it clarifies.
4. **Body-first language**: Every line should honor dance as the way people meet.
5. **Warm, direct, confident**: No hedging, no apologizing, no soft language.
6. **Never preach sobriety**: No-alcohol is a fact, not a sermon. "The music does the warming" beats "Sobriety is powerful."
7. **One sentence, one idea**: Keep sentences short. Periods > commas.

**Red Flags (Things That Don't Belong in Resonance Copy)**:
- "community," "intentional," "conscious," "aligned," "high-vibe," "sacred"
- "Find your person," "your person," "the one"
- "Authentic," "genuine," "real" (overdone — show it don't say it)
- Emoji spam, sparkle, heart eyes, party poppers
- Exclamation marks (minimize — they feel desperate)
- Urgency language ("only 50 spots," "going fast," "limited time")

## Component Reference (For Canva)

### Wordmark
- Text: "Resonance"
- Font: GT Sectra 24pt, weight 500
- Color: Cream-100 (#F5EFE3) on dark photos, Midnight-900 (#0F1A2E) on light photos
- Placement: Top-left or bottom-left corner (never centered)

### Date Stamp
- Format: "JUNE 14, 2026 · CHICAGO"
- Font: Inter label-caps, 12–14pt, weight 600
- Color: Slate-500 (#7A6F62)
- Placement: Below photo, above headline

### CTA Link
- Format: "resonance.com/apply" or "Application opens [date]"
- Font: Inter 14pt, weight 400
- Color: Terracotta-600 (#B8492E) — make it clickable
- No button background — text link only

## Canva Setup & Template Navigation

### Brand Kit in Canva (If Available)
1. Go to Canva Brand Kit settings
2. Upload color palette (5 colors: Terracotta, Midnight, Cream, Ink, Slate)
3. Upload fonts (GT Sectra for headlines, Inter for body)
4. Save. Now every template will auto-suggest your brand colors.

### Template Folders (Suggested Organization in Canva)
```
Resonance Templates
├── Flyers
│   ├── Digital Flyer (IG Portrait 1080×1350)
│   └── Print Flyer (8.5×11)
├── Social
│   ├── IG Feed Post (1080×1350)
│   ├── IG Story (1080×1920)
│   └── IG Reel Thumbnail
├── Email
│   ├── Email Header (600×200)
│   └── Email Footer
├── Evergreen Templates
│   └── [5 locked templates for reuse]
└── Reference
    └── Brand color swatches
```

### Locked Templates Workflow
Once you create a template:
1. Go to template settings
2. "Lock as template" — this prevents accidental edits
3. Use "Create design from template" each time you need a new event version
4. Only the copy and photo change; the layout, colors, and typography stay protected

## Quick Checklist Before Finalizing Any Design

- [ ] Photo passes the 11pm test (obviously daytime, real people, real moment)
- [ ] Headline is ≤8 words
- [ ] No hashtags (flyer aesthetic, not Instagram-caption aesthetic)
- [ ] CTA points to application, not ticket sales
- [ ] Color palette matches: Terracotta (#B8492E) + Midnight (#0F1A2E) + Cream (#F5EFE3)
- [ ] Typography: GT Sectra for headlines (weight 500), Inter for body (weight 400)
- [ ] No QR codes as focal elements (if QR exists, it's <0.5" in corner)
- [ ] Wordmark is in top-left or bottom-left corner, never centered
- [ ] Spacing between elements: 24px minimum
- [ ] No AI-tell phrases ("Here's what...", "Here's why...")
- [ ] No drop shadows (tonal layering only — the photo carries depth)
- [ ] No sponsor logos or brand co-branding
- [ ] Date and location are specific (CHICAGO, JUNE 14, 2026 — never vague)
