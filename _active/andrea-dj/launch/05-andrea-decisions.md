## Decision 3 — Application + ticketing stack

**My recommendation: Tally form (application gate, free) + Stripe Payment Link (post-vet payment) + Beehiiv confirmation email.**

One-line reason: it's the only stack that enforces curation — you read every application before any payment link goes out, which is the moat. The alternatives (Eventbrite, Posh, Partiful) collect the payment first, which inverts the door.

| Stage | Tool | Why |

| Confirmation | Beehiiv automation | Address goes out 24h before, no fabrication, no leak. |

**Total monthly cost while in launch**: ~$0 (all free tiers until volume hits paid thresholds).
