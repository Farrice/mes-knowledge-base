# RESONANCE — Visual Identity Anchor

**Upload this file to any AI image generator as a system-level reference. It contains everything needed to produce on-brand Resonance assets.**

> Resonance is a daytime, sober dance party in Chicago for people who want to meet a partner. Heart encounters, not head encounters. The music does the emotional labor so the people don't have to. We count the couples, not the followers.

---

## THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT RULE

> **If a photo could have been taken at 11pm in a club, it fails.**

This is not a stylistic preference. It is the brand rendered as a visual law. Every image must read as obviously daytime, in a real room, with real bodies.

A second test runs alongside it:

> **Does this look like an AI render of a wellness retreat?** If yes, also kill.

## PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION

### What the room looks like
- A dance studio or converted loft in Chicago
- Hardwood floors (maple or worn industrial) or polished concrete
- Large windows — industrial, arched, or floor-to-ceiling
- Natural afternoon daylight streaming in
- NO plants, NO decorations, NO props
- Minimal: exposed brick, white walls, exposed ductwork, a wooden barre
- The architecture and the people carry the room

### What the people look like
- Adults aged **30 to 38** specifically. Not 20s. Not 40s+.
- Casual-elevated clothing: sundresses, linen shirts, earth-tone tops, dark jeans, midi skirts, loafers, clean sneakers, leather sandals
- Mixed-race, mixed body types, mixed gender presentation
- Specific ethnicities (name them, don't say "diverse"): Latina (Costa Rican/Mexican/Colombian), Black (Caribbean or African-American), white with Polish-Italian features, Filipino or Korean, mixed East-Asian or South-Asian
- Real skin, real texture, no skin-smoothing
- No phones visible. Phones are off the floor.
- No alcohol, no glasses, no bottles anywhere

### Body language — CONNECTION cues (at least 2-3 per hero shot)
1. One body leaning in to whisper to another, both mid-laugh
2. Two bodies facing each other on the dance floor (not facing the DJ — facing each other)
3. Hand on shoulder, gaze on a third person (active social attention)
4. Eye contact between two people across a small room, mid-recognition
5. Mid-laugh between strangers — teeth visible, eyes crinkled, head tilted toward each other
6. Multiple bodies blurred in motion, intermingled (not lined up)
7. A body in the foreground laughing, another body in the middle distance also reacting
8. One person in conversation with two others — three-body social geometry
9. A still body at the frame's edge, watching, also looking happy to be there

### Body language — DISCONNECTION cues (KILL on sight)
1. Backs of heads in a line (yoga-class lineup)
2. All bodies facing the DJ in a stadium-pose
3. Solo body isolated in center of frame, no other bodies
4. Hands-in-air arena pose (festival grammar)
5. "Smile at the camera" group portraits
6. Bodies in identical wellness uniforms (white linen lineup = retreat)
7. AI-perfect lighting on every face (real rooms have shadows)
8. Hands holding phones
9. Glass-of-wine signaling

### Partnership orientation
- Hero shots show **heterosexual M/F partnership recognition** — this is the founder's specific brand thesis
- Group shots may include mixed friendships, but the romantic recognition beat is always M/F
- In prompts, name M/F explicitly: "A Latina woman in her mid-30s and a Black man in his late 30s, facing each other..."

### Composition principles
1. Lead with the gesture, not the face
2. Crop into the body — tight crops on dancing torsos, hands, the side of a face listening
3. Decenter the subject — right third or lower-left, negative space carries weight
4. Capture transition, not climax — the moment a song shifts, two people make eye contact, the pause before a turn
5. Documentary photography style, slight natural motion blur from real movement
6. Candid only. No directing. No "everyone look here."

## LIGHTING — ALLOWED

- Natural daylight from real windows
- South-facing window light at 2pm-4pm
- Overcast Chicago afternoon (flat grey is on-brand — it's real)
- Sun on a wood floor, dappled light through a curtain
- Real golden hour when it is real (not a filter)

## LIGHTING — BANNED

- Flash of any kind (direct, bounced, ring)
- Club lighting (wash lights, gobos, color spots, lasers, strobes)
- Neon (signs, overlays, any neon in frame)
- Stylized golden-hour filters faking late-afternoon warmth
- Tungsten room lighting alone without daylight
- Candlelit, firelit
- Black and white
- Any AI-perfect even lighting (real rooms have shadows)

```

photograph, not a render.
```

## ITERATION PROTOCOL

If the first generation doesn't pass the 11pm test:

1. Strengthen daylight markers — add "shot at 2pm" / "real Saturday afternoon daylight"
2. Add specific Chicago context — "Chicago loft" / "Pilsen storefront" / "Wicker Park warehouse"
3. Cut adjective-heavy phrases — "atmospheric, intimate, beautiful, captivating" all drift toward faked light
4. If anatomy errors appear, move the anti-AI-slop paragraph to the TOP of the prompt and repeat twice
5. After 3 failed iterations — stop. Wait for a real photograph. Don't ship night-coded imagery.
