# 05 — Venue Partnerships & Pricing
## How to Find a Venue, What to Pay, and Your First Event Budget

---

**What this is:** Everything you need to secure a venue, price your tickets, and know exactly what Event #1 will cost and earn.

**What to do with it:** Email 3-5 venues this week using the template below. Set your ticket prices. Know your numbers.

---

## Top 5 Venue Types (Ranked by Fit)

### 1. Rooftop Bars / Lounge Spaces — BEST FIT

**Pros:**
- Built-in sound system, lighting, bar
- Venue makes money on drinks (they're motivated to host you)
- Intimate-but-open feel matches your vibe
- Weekend evenings are natural

**Cons:**
- May already have entertainment commitments
- Weather dependency
- Noise complaints possible

**What to offer them:**
- You bring 50-100 people who arrive early, stay late, and buy drinks
- You handle all marketing, ticketing, and curation
- Revenue share on bar sales (10-15%) OR flat venue fee

### 2. Art Galleries & Creative Lofts

**Pros:**
- Beautiful aesthetic — spirituality, art, creativity align
- Large floor space for dancing
- Often underutilized on weekends
- High ceilings, character, natural light

**Cons:**
- May have noise restrictions
- Limited bar setup (you may need to bring refreshments)
- Need to be careful around artwork

**What to offer them:**
- Cross-promotion of gallery exhibitions to your email list
- Your attendees become gallery visitors (foot traffic)
- 50/50 ticket revenue split or flat rental

### 3. Dance Studios (Black Box)

**Pros:**
- Sprung floors (ideal for dancing)
- Sound systems often pre-installed
- Studio owners understand dance culture

**Cons:**
- No bar revenue (less motivation for venue)
- Higher hourly rates ($75-150/hour)
- Evening-only availability

**What to offer them:**
- Hybrid: lower hourly rate + 15-20% of ticket revenue
- Cross-promotion to their members (20% discount)
- Monthly residency commitment (discount for guaranteed bookings)

### 4. Yoga Studios & Wellness Spaces

**Pros:**
- Values alignment (spiritual, holistic)
- Community-focused audience already
- Good sound systems + open floor

**Cons:**
- May be too small (need 1,500+ sq ft)
- Limited bar setup
- Daytime focus (evening availability varies)

**What to offer them:**
- Co-branded events ("Studio X Presents...")
- Their yoga teacher does the opening breathwork
- 80/20 ticket revenue split
- Wellness add-on upsells (massage, reiki)

### 5. Warehouse / Event Lofts

**Pros:**
- Total design freedom
- Raw, transformable aesthetic
- Can accommodate 100+ people

**Cons:**
- Expensive ($500-2,000+ per evening)
- You bring everything (sound, lighting, decor)
- Parking/accessibility issues possible

**What to offer them:**
- Flat rental + bar revenue share
- Multi-event discount (3+ months committed)
- Social media coverage and brand association

## Revenue Share Structures

| Model | Best For | You Keep | Venue Keeps |
|-------|---------|----------|-------------|
| **Bar Revenue Share** | Bars, rooftops | All ticket revenue | 100% of drinks + 10-15% of cover |
| **Hybrid (Flat + %)** | Dance studios | Tickets minus flat fee | $75-150/hr + 15% of tickets |
| **50/50 Ticket Split** | Galleries, lofts | 50% of gross tickets | 50% of gross tickets |
| **Pure Flat Fee** | Any (proven demand) | All ticket revenue minus fee | $400-800 flat |
| **Cover Charge Split** | Bars, rooftops | $20-30 per person | $5-12 per person |

**Negotiation tip:** If venue pushes back, offer a graduated model:
- Month 1-2: Lower flat fee + higher % share (you de-risk their commitment)
- Month 3+: Flat fee drops as you prove consistent turnout

## What to Negotiate For

| Item | Why It Matters | Your Ask |
|------|---------------|----------|
| Sound system | Core to event | Full PA system included, or budget $300-500 to rent |
| Lighting | Atmosphere | At minimum ambient + color options |
| Bar access | Attendee experience | Exclusive partnership — no competing events same weekend |
| Guest list control | Curation is essential | You manage the door and checklist |
| Setup/teardown time | Logistics | 2 hours before + 1 hour after |
| Insurance | Legal protection | You carry $1M liability; venue listed as additional insured |
| Exclusivity | Build momentum | No conflicting events 24 hours before/after |
| Marketing inclusion | Reach | Venue mentions you on their Instagram + email list |
| Cancellation terms | Protection | 30 days notice = full refund; 14 days = 50%; <7 days = full fee owed |

## Ticket Pricing

### Event #1 (Launch Pricing)

| Tier | Price | How Many | Sell By | Purpose |
|------|-------|----------|---------|---------|
| **Early Bird** | $25 | 20 tickets | Week 2 | Build momentum, reward early commitment |
| **General Admission** | $35 | 40 tickets | Week 3-4 | Your "real" price — standard rate |
| **Last Minute / Door** | $45 | 20 tickets | Final week + day-of | Reward advance buyers, capture walk-ups |

**Why these prices:**
- $25 feels accessible, not sketchy
- $35 feels professionally curated, real value
- $45 feels premium, "I'm really committing"
- 20-25% separation between tiers is the psychological sweet spot

### Events #2-3 (Proof of Concept)

| Tier | Price |
|------|-------|
| Early Bird | $30 |
| General Admission | $40 |
| Last Minute / Door | $50 |

VIP includes: 30-min early entry, reserved lounge, $10 drink voucher, first access to next month's early bird.

## When to Introduce Memberships

**After Event #3** (once you've proven 3 months of consistency).

| Tier | Cost | Period | Includes |
|------|------|--------|---------|
| Founding Member | $99 | 3 months | Unlimited entry + guest + VIP perks |
| Monthly Member | $40 | 1 month | Unlimited entry that month |

**Why memberships work:**
- Predictable revenue (8 members = $800 guaranteed baseline)
- Members become community owners and evangelists
- Gives you negotiating power with venues ("I guarantee 20-30 attendees monthly")

---

## Comps & Guest List Rules

- **You:** 3 free tickets per event (photographer, media, influencer)
- **Venue:** 2-5 free tickets (negotiate per contract)
- **Total:** Max 5-8 comps (10% of a 50-person event)
- **Guest list closes 48 hours before** — names must be attached to an attendee
- **Guest tickets:** 20% discount ($28 when GA is $35) — incentivizes group signups

Every comp is ~$35 in lost revenue. Cap them.

## Must-Haves vs. Nice-to-Haves

**You absolutely need (Event #1):**
1. Venue ($400)
2. Sound system (you or rental)
3. Liability insurance ($80)
4. Ticketing platform ($0 — Eventbrite is free)
5. Your DJ skills and presence

**Nice-to-have (add for Event #2):**
1. Professional photographer ($200)
2. Mood lighting ($150)
3. Printed materials ($40)
4. Decor ($120)
5. Door staff ($50)

**Skip until later:**
- VIP tiers (Event #2)
- Memberships (Event #3+)
- Paid advertising (organic first)
- Professional videography ($400+)

## Key Negotiation Phrases

**If venue says "We want 20% of ticket revenue":**
> "Let's start at 15% for months 1-2 as we prove the concept. Or — what if I pay a flat $300 and you keep 100% of bar? The bar revenue will likely exceed what 20% of tickets would be."

**If venue asks "What if you don't hit 50 people?":**
> "I still pay the full venue fee. That's my risk. But I'm confident because of [my email list / partner promotions / past event experience]."

**If venue is hesitant about an unproven event:**
> "Let me do one event as a test. You get $[X], we both see how it goes. If it works, we sign a 3-month partnership."
