all right, let's get started!
I want to create this document to outline my story and my desire to host dance parties for singles.
I think I would like to take a shot at the Story by talking about how I've been in spaces that were life giving and flourishing, spaces that were created just for me, and how I have created spaces.

When I think about spaces that have been created for me, I think about having coffee at my grandma's house. How, since I was young, my family created spaces to enjoy, to connect, to bond over things specific to our culture,. I am also remembering the way I had spaces in the music program that I grew up in. Where the music school didn't feel like an institution, like a hostile place, it felt like a second home. There I made my first close friends. That's where I met my first boyfriend. That's Where. Orchestra felt like another gathering. Space where I could be myself, do what I love doing, meet other people, have fun,. later on I want to talk about the spaces that have not built felt places where I could be authentic not have to perform, etc..

coming back to the music school, one of the best experiences I've ever had is to be in you, Orchestra, youth Orchestra. I was in the orchestra at my school, but then they open auditions for a national youth Orchestra for students from various music programs around the country to create one big Orchestra. It was such a meaningful experience for me because first, I got to meet people my age from different places in Costa Rica. Students from a lower socioeconomic status of me in some of a higher So economic status. Students, people from rural places, others from the city. People who had been playing for many years and others who had just started. We all audition to get in, but it was some sort of equalizer, it was a place where we could come to play our instrument which we love, grow and get better at it, meet some friends, and at some point, we traveled across the country and internationally. The sense of camaraderie made it a great place to be in. Of course it was not easy, I had to confront a lot of my insecurities, thoughts about the world at that point, coming of age issues, all these things that I would not have experienced if it was not for this environment that was created.

When I came to Chicago to study music, which by the way I got a scholarship for being part of one of these music programs in Latin America, I had the expectation that my orchestra experience in Chicago was gonna be the same as in Costa Rica. I came to Chicago with the intention of becoming a professional orchestra player, because that's just what makes sense based on my experience so far. But then, unfortunately, I learned more about the orchestra culture here in the US, characterized by intense competition and hostile relationships. I don't even know if we can call that relationships. But one time I heard that one member of the orchestra had sued another person in the same orchestra and had a restraining order against them. That blew my mind, and I felt very disappointed, not only to hear that, but also being confronted with the fact that that my not have been what I came for, what I was looking for.

before I keep going, let me make a l summarized version of my upbringing and how I got to Chicago. I grew up in Costa Rica, and a family with my two parents and my younger brother, I was the first daughter, Elder's daughter. That's relevant for Latino families lol
I went to a private school, which was not as common for the town that we lived in. I'm grew up as the nice and quiet, well behaved little Andrea that was very obedient! Ha ha! The school I grew up in was bilingual, I would say even more focus on English speaking since most of the classes were in English, including math, science, reading, etc. It was a very strict environment, also academically, demanding, and the classmates where people from different areas and let's say not as homogenized. I felt growing up like school was a bubble. I would go to school and then when I go back home or spend time with my extended family, that felt like a different world.

I noticed this the most when I got switched schools after sixth grade. I moved to another school that was semi-private and they had cafeteria and food store there which was mind blowing to me since at the other school. We also had a cafeteria, but all the food was super healthy, minimal salt and sugar, and we were forced to finish our plates and things like that. So the fact that I could go and buy an empanada at the school cafeteria, fried foods!? Was a culture shock lol

So coming back to the spaces: my education in the first years of my life was rigorous and a little bit out of touch with reality in Costa Rica, it probably helped me with my English right now, in writing, and other skills that I might not be aware of. But socially, emotionally, in terms of expression of myself that was not the place where I could feel I could be myself, where I could express fully. I find it so interesting and surprising when people now call me a "people person" or very social, social butterfly, outgoing. Because I grew up being the shy girl, didn't talk too much, keeping to myself.

I would say the rest of my education in that other high school was also not where I felt the most safe, but it was definitely a step up from previous school. In terms of, I was able to make different friends, experimented a little bit more with my personality and my likes and dislikes.

During this time, I was also spending a lot of time in my music school. I would say the music school was my main place for socializing, and where I felt the most safe and where I was able to express myself fully.

I remember bringing up stuff to my parents about how I felt about something or what I thought about something, or something I was struggling with, and it was usually met with disbelief. Let's say: What do you mean you feel that way? Why would you be sad? My parents, mostly my mom, would seem physically uncomfortable with my emotions. They both wanted me to get out of those feelings as soon as possible. Come on stop crying! OK OK, let's just be happy, eat this so you feel better, let's put on a happy face!
