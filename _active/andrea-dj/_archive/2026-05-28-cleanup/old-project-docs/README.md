## What This Project Is

Andrea is building an event brand around her DJing — the goal is to host curated events where singles meet, connect, and form real relationships. Her dream: couples saying *"We met at one of Andrea's events."*

She is spiritually-minded and wants to express human values through movement, music, and dance. The concept lives at the intersection of:

1. DJ personal brand
2. Spiritual / wellness grounding
3. Intentional matchmaking
4. Movement & dance as the medium

The market research confirms this is white space — nobody is currently doing all four together.

## Quick Reference

**The verdict:** Concept has STRONG legs. Three trends converging in her favor — dating app collapse (78% fatigue), in-person singles events booming (+49% YoY), conscious/sober nightlife mainstream (+73% globally).

**Closest analog:** Daybreaker (500K+ members, 7-figure business, zero paid marketing). Andrea's differentiator: explicit matchmaking + spiritual grounding.

**Break-even on Event #1:** 45 tickets at $35 average = covers all costs (~$1,410 budget).

**Key insight:** Her spiritual values are the moat — apps and corporate event companies cannot replicate authentic spiritual community. Lead with feeling, not terminology.
