# Venue Target List Framework — Event #1

*Part of the Pre-Launch Command Center (see `00-command-center.md`). Decision deadline: 2026-06-02 venue lock.*

*Use this to generate the actual target list. Aim: 12-15 venues across 3 tiers. Outreach in tier order. Lock by 2026-06-02 (14 days from venue outreach kickoff).*

---

## Non-Negotiables (must-haves)

- 2pm-5pm Saturday availability, mid-to-late July 2026 (preference July 18, July 25 as backup)
- Capacity 60-80 (50 attendees + 8-12 buffer for staff, photographer, Andrea's gear)
- Sound system on-site OR ability to bring our own (PA + 2 monitors)
- No alcohol service required (we don't sell drinks — confirms BOS Line 2)
- Wood / concrete / studio floor that survives 50 people dancing for three hours
- Real daylight (windows). NOT a basement. NOT a windowless club. NOT a hotel ballroom.
- Bathroom capacity for 50
- Loading access for Andrea's DJ setup (turntables, mixer, hard drive, headphones, cables)
- Insurance/permit-friendly venue (we carry one-day event liability insurance)

## Nice-to-haves

- Green room or back area for Andrea + staff briefing
- Sound check window (1-2 hours before doors)
- Aesthetic match: warm wood, terracotta, soft daylight. NOT chrome-and-black nightclub. The room's visual language follows the BOS DESIGN.md — daytime in real rooms with honest light.

## Tier 1 — Friend-of-friend connection venues (highest priority)

Andrea has been DJing in Chicago and has venue relationships through her music network. Tier 1 is where the lower-friction yes lives. Lower rental cost (friend rate / barter / favor), higher chance of saying yes to the unconventional ask (daytime + sober + curated 50-person event), shorter contract negotiation.

Probable venue archetypes here:
- Dance studios where Andrea knows the owner or studio manager
- Art galleries with friend curators / collective members
- Music venues from the DJ Homies network that rent daytime
- Photography studios with floor space owned by friends
- Private lofts that have hosted Andrea's friends' events before

**Instruction to Andrea (Week 1 homework):** list 4-6 specific names. For each: their name, the venue they have access to, your relationship to them, the ask you'd make.

Example structure:
```
1. [Name] — [venue] — knows Andrea through [music context]. Ask: daytime rental July 18 or 25, friend rate.
2. [Name] — [venue] — knows Andrea through [referral chain]. Ask: introduction + tour.
3. ...
```

These names go into Tier 1 outreach (warm DMs / texts) the same week the framework lands.

## Tier 2 — Arts/dance studios (rentable, brand-aligned)

When Tier 1 doesn't fill the calendar, Tier 2 is the workhorse. Chicago neighborhoods that index well for the brand:

**Target neighborhoods:** Logan Square, Pilsen, West Loop, Wicker Park, Bucktown, Avondale.

**Avoid:** River North (too clubby), Lincoln Park (too post-college / family-coded), Fulton Market (overpriced for first event), South Loop high-rise event suites (sterile).

**Venue archetypes that fit:**

1. Dance studios that rent for non-class events (Logan Square, Pilsen)
2. Photo studios with 800+ sq ft of open floor (West Loop, Wicker Park)
3. Art gallery + event-rental combos (Pilsen, Bucktown)
4. Indie performance venues that rent daytime (across the target neighborhoods)
5. Lofts and event spaces under $1,500 day rate

**Search method (Week 1):**
- Peerspace / Splacer search: "Chicago daytime event space, 50 capacity, [neighborhood]"
- Instagram search: search venue names in target neighborhoods, check IG bios for rental info
- Direct Google: "[neighborhood] event space rental daytime"
- Ask local arts friends for venue lists they've used

Aim: 8-10 cold pitches across 5-7 venue candidates.

## Tier 3 — House parties (backup, smaller scale)

Plan B. Activated if no Tier 1 or Tier 2 lands by 2026-06-02.

When this makes sense: friend's house with real daylight, accessible neighborhood, 50-person capacity, willing to host. Trade-offs: zero rental cost (or small thank-you gesture — donation, dinner, $200 fee), less polish, lower capacity ceiling, residential noise concerns.

Tier 3 setup is functional, not aspirational — the brand still works at a house party if the music + the curation + the daytime + the sober all hold.

## Outreach Cadence

- **Tier 1 outreach:** this week, warm DMs / texts / calls
- **Tier 2 outreach:** this week, cold pitch email (use `01b-venue-cold-pitch-email.md`)
- **Follow-ups:** 48 hours after first send (use `01c-venue-followup-and-decision-tree.md`)
- **Decision target:** 14 days from outreach kickoff (2026-06-02)
- **Plan B trigger:** if no Tier 1 or Tier 2 lock by day 14, activate Tier 3

## What we offer the venue

- Predictable 3-hour rental window
- Hard cap on bodies (50 attendees + 8-12 staff/photographer)
- Certificate of insurance documented and provided before event date
- No alcohol = no liquor liability for the venue
- Sound capped (we self-monitor decibels to respect neighbors / building code)
- Resonance promotes the venue post-event: IG tag, photographer shots shared with permission, post-event recap article naming the space
- For Tier 1 friends: friend rate or barter (Andrea DJs their next event, mutual cross-promotion, etc.)

## Decision questions for Andrea + Farrice (Week 1)

Forcing questions. Each requires a single answer before the target list locks.

1. Which 4-6 Tier 1 contacts does Andrea approach this week? (Names + venue + the specific ask. Andrea owns this list.)
2. What's the upper bound on venue spend before break-even math breaks? ($1,500 per Command Center Standard Path, hard cap before tickets need to clear $50+.)
3. Is the COI (certificate of insurance) bound and ready to attach to cold pitches, or do we need to bind the policy this week? (Affects Week 1 outreach credibility.)
4. Which mid-to-late July Saturday is the anchor? (July 18 default. July 25 backup. Both stay open in outreach until a venue forces the pick.)
5. Are we open to a Sunday daytime if a venue offers it, or is Saturday a hard line? (Sunday acceptable per the decision tree; Andrea adjudicates if it conflicts with her schedule.)
6. Who owns venue follow-up — Andrea (relational, brand-fit calls) or Farrice (cold pitches, budget math, calendar)? (Both. Andrea handles Tier 1 relationships; Farrice handles Tier 2 logistics.)
7. What's the Tier 3 (house party) candidate list if Plan B activates? (1-2 friends with the right space. Identify by end of Week 1 so Plan B is real not theoretical.)
