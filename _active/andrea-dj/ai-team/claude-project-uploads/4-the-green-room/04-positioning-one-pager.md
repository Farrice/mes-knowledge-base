# RESONANCE

*Heart encounters, not head encounters.*

---

## What

> A daytime, sober dance party in Chicago for people who want to meet a partner.

We hold our events during the day, sober, with carefully curated music and a carefully curated crowd. You meet someone here because you noticed each other on the dance floor — the way they moved, the way they listened, the way they were standing when the song shifted.

The dancing is the medium. The meeting is the point.

## Why

You've tried the apps. You've tried the bars. You've been to the blind dates your friends set up and the parties that promised connection. You've left a thousand rooms with a phone full of contacts and no one to call.

The format is the problem. Head-first meeting, mediated by alcohol, performed under a clock. We're not a hookup event in a singles-event costume. We're not speed dating dressed as a party. We don't believe a bar at 11pm is the only permission slip this city gives you to meet someone.

Resonance is the room you actually want. Clean meetings. In daylight. Sober. With your full self in the room.

## Who it's for

A 30–40 year old in Chicago. Single and employed. Has tried the apps, the bars, the speed dating, the hookups, the friend-of-a-friend setups.

Out loud asking *"Why is it so hard to meet a good, nice person?"*

Wants a committed partner. Ready, not exploring. Connected to their values, dedicated to growth. Has community of some kind, but the partner they want isn't in it. A *hell yes* to being in this room.

## Who it's not for

> If you're a hell-yes to this, we want you in the room. If you're not sure, this isn't your event.

Not for hunters. Not for tourists. Not for people who don't want to dance.

## The lines

Twelve non-negotiables hold the floor — the lines we will not cross even when it's expensive not to. The four that shape every decision:

- **Daytime.** Flagship events happen in daylight.
- **Sober.** No alcohol at flagship events.
- **Curated admission.** Every attendee is vetted. Hell-yes commitment is the floor.
- **No hookup culture.** The room and the language reflect commitment-seeking.

Variations on the format require a different name. Music is curated by Resonance — no sponsor stages, no guest brand DJs. Phones stay off the floor. Sponsor doctrine, in one line: *"If sponsorship requires drift, the sponsorship is wrong."*

## The promise

People leave lighter, smiling, and not wanting to leave. Roughly **25% of attendees make a real connection** — someone they would actually consider seeing again. Best case: at least one person leaves confident they've just met their person.

We count the couples, not the followers.

*Dance with someone new.*

## Why this room exists *(optional founder-side, for venue pitches and press)*

> Andrea grew up in Costa Rica, the older daughter at a strict bilingual private school where she could not be herself. The first room that fit was her music school. The second was a national youth orchestra that pulled students from across the country and made the audition the equalizer — kids from rural towns and kids from the city, students with money and students without, played together because someone curated who got in. She came to Chicago on a music scholarship expecting that same room and ran into US orchestra culture — intense competition, hostile relationships, restraining orders between members of the same ensemble. The room she came here looking for did not exist. So she's building it for adults who are out loud asking *"why is it so hard to meet a good person."* Resonance is what a curated room looks like when it's built for connection instead of evaluation.
