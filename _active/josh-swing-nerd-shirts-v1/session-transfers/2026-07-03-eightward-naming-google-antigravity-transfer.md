## Legal Consult Prep
Ask the lawyer:

1. Can Josh safely pursue **EIGHTWARD** for apparel, shirts, merch, or related goods under Class 25?
2. Does prior or current music/remix use of "Eightward" create any meaningful conflict for apparel?
3. Is **AIRSTEP/AIR STEP** too risky because of footwear/apparel marks?
4. Is **THE EIGHTH HOUSE** too crowded because of astrology, jewelry, PR, cannabis, and existing domains?
5. Should Josh file an intent-to-use application before launching publicly?
6. Are `Shop Eightward`, `Eightward Studio`, or `Eightward Club` better practical marks/handles if exact `eightward.com` is not available?
7. What should Josh avoid using from Lindy Hop history, song titles, event names, or Harlem/Savoy references?

## Cultural And Creative Guardrails

- Do not use direct song titles, lyrics, artist names, event marks, or borrowed logos.
- Treat Lindy Hop history as Black American cultural history, not a costume drawer.
- Use Savoy/Harlem/Lenox references carefully and only when the work feels respectful and grounded.
- Prefer encoded lineage: 8-count, Breakaway, air step, social floor, late-night exchange, moon/ballroom language.
- Do not turn the brand into poodle-skirt nostalgia, jukebox art, green-code hacker visuals, or fantasy-costume parody.
