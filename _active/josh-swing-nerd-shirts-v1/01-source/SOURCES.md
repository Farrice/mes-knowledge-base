## Bonfire

- [Bonfire Basics](https://help.bonfire.com/en/articles/1614140-bonfire-basics): Bonfire supports campaigns, batch windows, printing/shipping, and profit payouts after successful batches.
- [Campaign Batches](https://help.bonfire.com/en/articles/6993030-campaign-batches): Bonfire batch campaigns can run 2-21 days and can improve profit through grouped orders.
- [Print on Demand](https://help.bonfire.com/en/articles/5021301-print-on-demand): Bonfire print-on-demand processes faster but can produce lower profit than batch campaigns.
- [How Payouts Work](https://help.bonfire.com/en/articles/1614157-how-payouts-work/): Bonfire payouts are typically available after orders are sent to print and are usually requested through PayPal.
- [Content Guidelines](https://help.bonfire.com/en/articles/1614170-content-guidelines): Bonfire reviews campaigns for trademark, copyright, hateful, sexually explicit, fraudulent, and other restricted content.
