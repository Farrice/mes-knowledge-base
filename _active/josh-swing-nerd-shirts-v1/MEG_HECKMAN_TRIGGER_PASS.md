## Listing Copy Upgrades

### Commit On Eight

**Current strength:** clean, wearable, cross-audience.

**Meg trigger upgrade:** Make the buyer feel the moment of deciding to commit instead of drifting.

**Revised lead:**

For the dancer who stops overthinking and sends the count.

This sits in the tiny overlap between the social floor and the terminal window: timing, trust, and the nerve to commit when the phrase turns over.

### No Drops In Prod

**Current strength:** strongest social-floor joke.

**Meg trigger upgrade:** Name the public reaction and responsible insider identity.

**Revised lead:**

Social floor is production. Test the chaos in rehearsal.

For dancers who like the joke because they know the rule: no surprise aerials, no panic drops, and no "it worked once" energy when real humans are connected to you.

### Push / Pull Request

**Current strength:** most nerd-pure overlap.

**Meg trigger upgrade:** Make the connection metaphor feel human before it feels technical.

**Revised lead:**

Connection is a protocol.

Push, pull, wait, respond, adjust. A low-key partner dance tee for people who know a clean request beats a messy merge.

## Launch Decision

- Lead with **Commit On Eight** when Josh wants safest wearability.
- Lead with **No Drops In Prod** when Josh wants the strongest insider laugh and social currency.
- Keep **Push / Pull Request** if the final artwork stays simple and human.

## Buyer-Test Questions

Ask real dancers or overlap friends:

1. Which shirt made you think "that is me" fastest?
2. Which one would you send to a specific friend?
3. Which one would you wear outside a dance event?
4. Which one would make someone ask about it?
5. Which one feels like an inside joke without needing an explanation?
