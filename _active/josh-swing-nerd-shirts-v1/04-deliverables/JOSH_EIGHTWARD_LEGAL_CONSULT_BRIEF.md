The current risk check favors Eightward over Airstep and The Eighth House, but Josh should not launch publicly, buy a domain, or file anything until counsel confirms trademark availability and common-law risk.

## Why Eightward Works

- It encodes the 8-count without saying Lindy Hop, swingout, Savoy, or dance.
- It has direction in it: toward, forward, ward, way. That gives the brand a world beyond shirts.
- It is easy to say: eight-ward.
- It is easy to spell after hearing once.
- It can sit on a shirt tag, a Bonfire page, a small chest mark, or a back-print seal.
- It sounds like a place or movement, not a joke.
- It leaves room for sci-fi, fantasy, literary, and social-floor language without locking the brand into one costume.

Placek read: Eightward sits in the useful tension zone. It is familiar enough to process, but not so familiar that it disappears. The name has a little friction, which is good. The job now is not more naming; it is survival testing.

## What Eightward Should Not Become

- Not obvious dance-pun merch.
- Not generic programmer merch.
- Not fantasy cosplay.
- Not nostalgia swing graphics.
- Not a borrowed Harlem/Savoy costume drawer.
- Not a brand built on song titles, lyrics, artist names, event names, venue marks, or copied dance logos.
- Not "The Eighth House" as the company name unless counsel clears it.

Use the cultural source with care: Lindy Hop history is Black American cultural history. Eightward can carry lineage through count, movement, floorcraft, night-social language, and original illustration. It should not borrow protected or sacred symbols as decoration.

## Go / No-Go Tree

Green light:

- Counsel finds no material federal, state, common-law, or phonetic conflict for EIGHTWARD in apparel/merch.
- Counsel says an ITU filing is reasonable before public reveal.
- A practical domain and handle path is available.

Move: file or prepare filing, reserve domain/handles, then build the founder naming brief and first capsule.

Yellow light:

- Exact EIGHTWARD looks clean, but common-law/domain/social uncertainty remains.
- Counsel wants narrower goods language or more search.
- Domain path is workable but not ideal.

Move: keep Eightward internal, do not launch publicly, run the extra search counsel requests, and prepare a fallback naming sprint.

Red light:

- Counsel finds a meaningful conflict, likely confusion risk, or strong common-law user.
- Counsel advises against Class 25 use.
- Domain/handle path creates brand confusion.

Move: retire Eightward as the lead and restart from the existing naming pool with David Placek as owner. Do not default to Airstep or The Eighth House without clearance.
