# Josh Shirt Launch - 01 Executive Brief

## Project

Working name: **Social Dance Debug Club**

Purpose: help Josh test a small swing dance x nerd culture apparel concept without inventory, a full store, or a complicated brand build.

## Brand Premise

Regular, wearable shirts for people who live in the overlap between swing/social dance and nerd culture.

The shirt should look like a cool tee from across the room and become an inside joke when the right person reads it.

## Audience

Primary buyer:

- Swing dancers who like clever low-key apparel.
- Lindy Hop, West Coast Swing, Balboa, blues/fusion-adjacent, and social dance people.
- Nerd-adjacent people: software, engineering, data, tabletop, gaming, open-source, creative tech.
- Couples or friends inside that overlap looking for a gift that feels specific.

Emotional trigger:

> That is painfully specific. I need that.

Positioning line:

**Dance merch that does not look like dance merch.**

## Why It Is Worth Testing

1. The overlap is niche, but niche is the point. A small group can love this harder than a broad group likes it.
2. Swing dance already has shared language: timing, connection, lead/follow, swingout, anchor, phrase, floorcraft.
3. Nerd culture already has shared language: commit, debug, prod, protocol, patch notes, pull request, latency, state.
4. The best shirt ideas do not explain the joke. They reward the right person for recognizing it.
5. Bonfire lets Josh test demand without inventory, a full store, or a large upfront build.

## V1 Recommendation

Use Bonfire for a limited first batch campaign.

Recommended structure:

- One first-drop campaign.
- Three shirt concepts max.
- Ten-day campaign window.
- Start with unisex tees.
- Keep colors and products limited.
- Let real audience signal decide what becomes the next drop.

## Best Starter Concepts

| Concept | Text | Why It Works | V1 Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commit On Eight | COMMIT ON 8 | Code commit x 8-count timing | safest lead |
| No Drops In Prod | NO DROPS IN PROD | dance safety x production environment | funniest community signal |
| Push / Pull Request | PUSH / PULL REQUEST | partner connection x code review | nerdiest concept |

## Recommended Lead

**Commit On Eight**

Why: It has the clearest crossover, the least explanation burden, and the strongest wearability.

## Success Signal

This first launch does not need to prove a giant market. It needs to prove that the right people react with one of these:

- "I would wear that."
- "That is so specific."
- "Send me the link."
- A purchase during the batch window.
