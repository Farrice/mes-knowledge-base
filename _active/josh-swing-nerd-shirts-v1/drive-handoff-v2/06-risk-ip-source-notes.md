# Josh Shirt Launch - 06 Risk, IP, and Source Notes

## Purpose

Keep the launch original, safe, and grounded.

This is not legal advice. It is a practical risk filter before Josh publishes a Bonfire campaign.

## Red Team Summary

Main risk:

The idea is good, but it can collapse into novelty merch if the designs are too literal or too generic.

Best control:

Make the design system premium first, then let the joke reward insiders.

## Candidate Scorecard

| Candidate | Verdict | Why | Main Risk | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commit On Eight | Strongest lead | clearest crossover, most wearable | could become too plain | make it a club tee with small front mark and stronger back print |
| No Drops In Prod | Funniest community signal | dance safety x software production | "prod" may not land with non-software dancers | keep the design cool enough that the unknown term feels intentional |
| Push / Pull Request | Most nerd-pure | partner connection x code review | can become a dad joke if too literal | make it a dance diagram / connection protocol, not software UI |

## IP And Content Safety Check

Avoid:

- Sports teams.
- University marks.
- Superhero references.
- TV/movie/game characters.
- Song lyrics.
- Dance event logos.
- Organization names/logos without permission.
- Software company logos.
- Code platform icons or interface references.
- Famous meme layouts.
- Copied shirt/sticker/poster art.

Use instead:

- Original phrases.
- Abstract marks.
- Count grids.
- Floor maps.
- Connection lines.
- Club-label language.
- Josh's own sketches.

## Bonfire Notes

Bonfire is useful for V1 because it can handle public campaign pages, buyer checkout, printing/shipping, and payouts after batches.

Key practical points to confirm inside Bonfire:

- Campaign duration.
- Product costs.
- Profit preview.
- Payout method.
- Product colors.
- Print placement.
- Content review compliance.

## Research Boundary

The trend guidance in this package is directional, not a guarantee of sales.

The real validation is not trend alignment. It is whether actual swing dancers and nerd-adjacent people react with:

- "I would wear that."
- "That is so specific."
- "Send me the link."
- A purchase during the batch window.

## Final Go / No-Go Check

Go if:

- Josh would wear it.
- The joke makes him laugh.
- The design feels premium without explanation.
- It is original.
- Bonfire preview looks clean.

Do not go yet if:

- The design only works because the phrase is funny.
- It looks like generic programmer merch.
- It looks like costume swing merch.
- The art borrows protected references.
- The Bonfire preview looks low quality.
