# Josh Shirt Launch - 03 Concept Bank + Winning Signals

## Purpose

Give Josh a bank of ideas, territories, and research prompts so he can creatively shape the designs himself.

Research boundary: this is not a full live market study. Treat the trend and winning-pattern guidance as creative starting points, not guaranteed winners.

## Current Winning Signals To Use

Useful apparel directions for this project:

- Minimalist text: short, clean, wearable phrases.
- Pocket prints plus larger back designs: subtle front, stronger back moment.
- Nostalgia and vintage energy: not costume swing, but lived-in club merch.
- Repeating text: useful for rhythm/count ideas.
- Event or matching tees: dance communities already gather around weekends, workshops, exchanges, and social nights.
- Human-touch designs: hand-drawn marks, imperfect lines, and community feeling.

How that translates here:

- Do not chase generic viral memes.
- Make the shirts feel like scene artifacts.
- Use small details that reward the community.
- Build a visual system before building a giant catalog.

## Prompts For Josh To Find More Ideas

Ask dancers:

- What phrase do teachers say that everyone laughs at because it is painfully true?
- What is a moment every social dancer has experienced but outsiders would not understand?
- What mistake is funny only after you stop being embarrassed?
- What is the dance equivalent of a software bug?
- What would go on a shirt only volunteers, DJs, or late-night dancers would understand?

Ask nerds/coders:

- What engineering phrase becomes funny if it describes partner connection?
- What software phrase feels like a dance class correction?
- What tech word sounds romantic, physical, or social when moved into dance?
- What "do not do this in production" rule maps to the social floor?

## How To Pick Winners

Score each idea 1-5:

- Wearability: Would someone wear it casually?
- Insider clarity: Does the right person get it fast?
- Joke strength: Does it make Josh laugh now and still work tomorrow?
- Visual potential: Can it become more than text?
- IP safety: Is it original and free of borrowed references?
- Launch fit: Would it work as a Bonfire first drop?

Keep ideas that score 22+ out of 30.

Kill ideas that require a paragraph of explanation.
