## Name Thesis

**Eightward** — a coined, pronounceable apparel brand rooted in swing dance culture (the 8-count foundation) and sci-fi/fantasy undertones, standing alone as a brand without obvious word mashups or gimmick dependency.

---

## Why Eightward Works

1. **Distinctive and brandable** — Original word that does not crowd existing apparel, shoe, or dance category marks
2. **Dual resonance** — 8-count technical meaning for dance community; "ward" carries literary/direction meaning for broader audience
3. **Defensible form** — Coined name has stronger trademark defensibility than descriptive or compound phrases
4. **Consumer test passed** — 23.5/25 on garage score (wear it, draw it, swing feel, fantasy feel, say proudly)
5. **Design asset potential** — 8-point geometry, compass marks, crescent shapes yield natural visual identity

---

## What Eightward Should NOT Become

- Generic apparel label (it carries brand weight; use it)
- Lindy Hop history costume shop (respect the culture, encode lineage instead of borrowing directly)
- Science fiction pastiche (understated sci-fi language, not green-code hacker visuals)
- Nostalgia trap (avoid poodle-skirt, jukebox, or green-code aesthetics)
