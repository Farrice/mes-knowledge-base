## The Depth Standard (what makes the content revered, not just clean)

Every piece is built to the **Cognitive Signature** + **Aha** bar — this is the non-negotiable that separates world-class from competent:
- **Cognitive Signature** (`skills/fresh-voice-system/genius.md`): Paradox Reveal → False-Frame Demolition → Reframe Landing — the "Goddamn That's True" sequence.
- **Story-first, insight-second** — value is the new generic; engineer for what the reader *feels*, then for what they learn.
- **Aha standard** (`aha-engine` + `kobi-brown`): a post must create genuine cognitive change (aha → perspective shift → left-better-off → addictive-in-a-good-way), not merely inform.
- **Positioning through depth, not tool-talk** — lead with the human insight and the brand truth; AI is the how, never the headline (per the AI-stigma reframe).
- Enforced by `voice-gate.md` + the Aha gate in `/linkedin-daily` Step 8. **"Polished but flat" = fail.**

## Content → Client/Role Pipeline (the spine)

1. **CONTENT** — daily `/linkedin-daily`: Parallax-cooked, cognitive-signature posts in the brand lane → authority + recognition + live demo of the service.
2. **DISTRIBUTION** — 10 substantive comments/day on brand founders, marketing leads, and category voices → become a familiar name in the rooms where they hire.
3. **SIGNAL → DM** — profile views / saves / comments from brand decision-makers = warm signal → soft DM (a real idea for their brand, never a pitch).
4. **DISCOVERY** — give value first (a sharp teardown of their current content, or a custom sample built with his system) → call.
5. **CONVERT** — role conversation OR project/retainer.

**KPIs (priority order):** decision-maker profile views → decision-maker DMs/connection requests → discovery calls / role conversations → signed engagement or seat. Reach and likes are noise. Track in `performance-log.md` + a pipeline tally.
