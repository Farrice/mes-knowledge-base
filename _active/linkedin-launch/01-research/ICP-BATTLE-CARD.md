## Who buys

Growth-stage DTC supplement / wellness / performance brand, **$1M–$50M revenue**, founder-led voice or one drowning marketing lead, **no senior creative-strategy seat**.

| Brand size | Who signs | Door |
|---|---|---|
| Sub-$1M | Founder / CEO-as-CMO | Founder DM |
| $1M–$20M | CMO / VP Marketing / Head of Growth | Named-role DM; media buyer is gatekeeper AND referral source |
| Scaled | In-house team | Not your buyer — anchor content only |

**Named targets (June-verified, funding receipts):** Create Wellness ($20M Series B 03/2026) · Gorgie ($24.5M Series A, 5x YoY) · Gainful · Momentous · Four Sigmatic · Hilma · Bala · Joovv · Plunge · Rasa. Teardowns already out to: Transparent Labs, Momentous, Puori.

## Their world (pains, in their words)

- "Every marketer has access to the same AI models. So what separates the winners?" [V]
- "Sea of sameness" — every ad library in the category looks like one brand wrote it [V]
- Compliance strangles claims: FTC ~$43K/violation exposure, warning letters routine [V/L]
- AI-content trust collapse: consumer enthusiasm 60% down to 26% (2023 vs 2025) [V]
- "8–12 AI tools… none of them replace the thinking" [L]
- The honest claim needs a paragraph; the competitor's lie fits on a banner (the About's thesis — it's their lived Tuesday)

## Yes-signals for a no-logo outsider [V, June §4]

1. **Published, dated, named teardown/angle work** — "if yes, interview; if not, the resume is marketing copy." (= exactly the teardown portfolio motion)
2. **The paid case test: $300–500, 90 minutes, real account** — their own established screen. This is a *purchase behavior that already exists* in the exact $ band Farrice needs.
3. Permissionless audit of their actual brand (= the free teardown DM)
4. Niche compliance fluency, named repeatedly
5. Credible "what AI does well and where it fails" POV

## Disqualifiers (no pitch)

- No real revenue yet / pre-launch
- >$1M/mo ad spend with in-house creative team (they hire FTEs, not projects)
- Product Farrice wouldn't defend — the whole positioning is claim-honesty; one indefensible client kills it

## The one belief every piece of content must shift

> "Our copy problem is a writing/volume problem" shifts to **"It's an unmade decision: what we prove, who must believe it, what we'll defend."**

Every teardown demonstrates the decision missing. Every POV post argues why writers can't fix it. Every DM offers to show them theirs.

## Fresh-research deltas (2026-07-28 Gemini Deep, 146 sources — June brief CONFIRMED on ICP shape, buying doors, and pains; sharpened as follows)

**The market named Farrice's thesis.** "Founder Fog" : 73% of DTC founders can't articulate their positioning in one sentence without category jargon. The About's "unmade decision" argument is the market's own diagnosis. Use their term in comments/DMs when they use it first; keep his own language in posts.

**Purchase triggers (buy-moment is reactive — time outreach to these):**
1. **LTV:CAC collapse** — below 3:1 the model breaks, and founders go looking for "positioning triage"
2. **Scaling wall** — hero-product brand at $1–5M can't expand portfolio/retail without real architecture
3. **New exec or funding announcement** — the highest-reliability trigger. New VPs/CMOs re-evaluate partners in their first 60–90 days, response rates halve after day 90. Watch funding news; DM inside the window.

**Buying-band receipts ($500–$5K is a live micro-economy):** teardowns/audits $500–$1,000 · hybrid sprints ~$1,100 · concept sprints ~$1,450 · strategic ad sprints ~$3,500 · diagnostic roadmaps $5,000. Founders pre-$10M "highly active buyers of tactical $1,000–$5,000 sprints" to de-risk before retainers. Premium positioning sprints run $15K–$35K — Farrice's $2,500 sprint is priced for velocity, not against category ceiling.

**The "Teardown Wedge" is a documented 2026 cold-outreach category** — free audit/teardown DM converting into $1K–$5K paid pilot engagements. His exact motion, independently verified as the working play.

**2026 lexicon (mirror it, never explain it):** unit economics / CM3 · fractional operator, growth partner · creative fatigue, ad-library sameness · Data Mirage (distrust of agency ROAS reporting) · Founder Fog · claims matrix, friction tax. Hyper-vigilant against "AI Theater" — never lead with AI capability claims; lead with judgment and receipts.

**One correction to June:** blank connection requests still build the network, but calls come from **intent-hijack** — personalized notes referencing a prospect's actual comment/question on operator posts (documented 14% comment-to-booked-call). Weight effort there.
