## 3. The depth bar (non-negotiable — this is what makes it world-class, not noise)
Every post runs the **Cognitive Signature**: Paradox Reveal → False-Frame Demolition → Reframe Landing (the "Goddamn That's True" turn). Plus: story-first (engineer what they FEEL), the **Aha gate** (a real before→after perception shift, not information), and the **Empathy gate** (does it say her truth better than she can — "he gets it better than my own team"). Banned: "it's not X it's Y" reveals, twin-sentence aphorisms, "here's what" openers, cheap question closes, >2 em dashes. **Polished-but-flat fails. Informs-but-doesn't-make-her-feel-seen fails.**
