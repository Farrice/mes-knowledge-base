## 6. The conversion motion (signal → money, no step skipped)
Signal (profile view / comment / save / connection-accept from a brand lead) → **soft teardown DM** (§7-A) → they engage → **the read** (send 3 specific observations about THEIR citation gap, free, no pitch) → they ask "what would it take?" → **the audit close** (§7-D) → audit delivered → **the install attach at the walkthrough** (§7-E) → retainer conversation when the install ships. Parallel track: warm list → ICP interview (§7-B) → golden question (§7-C) → founder-offer email (§7-F). **Both tracks run every week. The DM block (§4-4) is where clients actually come from.**

## 7. The scripts (verbatim starting points — personalize the [brackets], never paste blind)
- **A · Soft teardown DM (to a signal or a target brand):** *"[Name] — pulled apart [Brand]'s last 5 posts and ran your top claims through the two gates I use (FTC-survivable × AI-citable). Found 3 things worth your attention — the citation gap on [specific category question] is the big one. Want the 3? No pitch, it's yours either way."*
- **B · ICP interview ask (warm list, week 1–3):** *"[Name] — building something that helps supplement/wellness brands get named by AI search without a claim blowing up. Research phase, and your read on [their world] would sharpen it. Open to 15 minutes this week? Not a sales call."*
- **C · The golden question (end of EVERY interview):** *"If it turns out to be a fit, want to know when it's ready? And is there anyone else you know wrestling with this?"*
- **D · The audit close (only when they ask what it would take):** *"The Claim-Safe Citation Audit: your top 8–12 claims graded on both gates, plus the 3–5 category questions where a competitor gets named and you don't, plus the fix map. $1,500 standard — I have [N] founder slots left at $500 in exchange for feedback and a case study. 3–5 business days. Want one?"*
- **E · The install attach (at the audit walkthrough, gaps on screen):** *"Most brands have me install the fixes and the trust layer over the next two weeks so this doesn't sit in a doc — the claim ledger, the flagship answer page, the entity pass. Want me to skip that and just leave you the map?"*
- **F · The founder-offer email (after 3–5 interviews, to the interested):** plain email, no deck. One result ("your top claims graded on both gates + the citation-gap map"), fixed timeframe ("delivered inside a week"), 3 bullets of what's included, founder price + the conditions, one-line close ("want one of the three slots?").
