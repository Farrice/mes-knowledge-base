- **Send Tier 1 today.** All five are US, ICP-fit (except Create trending large but reachable), founder-led, and carry a *this-week* trigger (active hire, fresh launch, live litigation, relaunch, new-brand positioning window).
- **The free-teardown play is the door for every name here:** run each brand's top category question through ChatGPT/Perplexity, screenshot where a competitor is named and they aren't, pull 2–3 claims — send it as the warm-DM opener ("*pulled your last posts + ran your top claims, here's the citation gap + 3 I'd ship. No pitch.*"). That IS the audit's teaser and it converts cold to warm.
