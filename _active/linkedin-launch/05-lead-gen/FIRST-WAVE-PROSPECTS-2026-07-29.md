# First-Wave Prospect Queue

## Evidence boundary

**[VERIFIED from linked sources]** Each brand has a public announcement for a live product, retail placement, or expansion, and each named buyer or role appears in the cited source.

**[UNCONFIRMED]** Company size, revenue, profitability, and available service budget are unknown and are not inferred.

Before sending, verify the buyer's current role, the live product or retail page, and a reachable channel. A press-release name is a researched candidate, not proof that a message can reach them.

## Source limitations and downside

- Brand-supplied announcements are not independent sales verification.
- A retail placement does not prove healthy sell-through, profit, or service budget.
- A founder title does not prove the named person personally approves outside strategy spend.
- A public profile does not prove the buyer will see or answer a message.
- The one-gap hypotheses are analyst inferences and must be rechecked against the live page before use.

## Send-readiness checklist

Mark a row send-ready only when:

- The named buyer's current role is verified.
- A direct LinkedIn profile, public business email, or credible introduction route is found.
- The launch/product page still resolves.
- The gap is grounded in the buyer's current language.
- No private email address is guessed or scraped.
- The message can be written in four sentences.

## Queue rule

The [approved operating floor](../03-launch/2026-07-29-CASH-FIRST-ACTIVATION.md) is 15 new qualified messages per selling day. This priority section contains 15 candidates, so it covers one day provided every row passes the final contact check. Replenish the queue before the next day, but do not postpone today's sends to build a 150-name database.
