# Design Brief: The Claim Ledger Artifact

## Visual Identity

The deliverable is **not a modern SaaS deck**. It is a **dossier-grade evidence document** designed to be forwarded from a founder to their co-founder, board, or compliance team.

### Register and Intensity

- **Visual register:** Evidence room. Archive. Legible proof hierarchy.
- **Intensity ceiling:** 6/10 — gravitas without theatricality.
- **What's NOT here:** Whimsy, lore characters, tongue-in-cheek tone, playful fonts, decorative elements.
- **What IS here:** Numbered sections. Stamps. Tables. Structured evidence. Authority.

---

## Design System

### Color palette (approved for generate craft-map)

- **Primary:** Dark charcoal or navy (authority, legibility)
- **Accent:** Muted gold or slate (stamp/authority, not bright)
- **Evidence stamps:** Three-color system:
  - ✓ VERIFIED — forest green (strong evidence)
  - ? LIKELY — slate gray (requires review)
  - ⚠ UNCONFIRMED — warm amber (preliminary, needs primary evidence)
- **Background:** Off-white or light gray (readable, not glowing)

### Typography

- **Headings:** Serif or modern sans-serif, all-caps for section headers (evidence-file register)
- **Body:** Clean sans-serif, single-space with breathing room (dossier readability)
- **Monospace for citations:** When referencing studies or evidence sources, use fixed-width font
- **No decorative fonts**

### Layout structure

- **Header block:** Brand name, recipient role, date prepared, revision status
- **Numbered sections:** 1, 2, 3... clear hierarchy
- **Tables for evidence:** Proof hierarchy table uses structured rows, not bullet points
- **Margin annotations:** Optional: small call-out boxes for "FLAG FOR LEGAL REVIEW" or "CLAIM-SAFE LINE"
- **Footer:** Timestamp, version, "Prepared by Farrice Cain"

---

## Sections and Visual Treatment

### Cover page / Title section

- Brand name centered, large
- Subtitle: "Proof-to-Market Blueprint – Founding Pilot Delivery"
- Recipient info (right-aligned, small): Buyer name, title, date
- No decorative imagery
- Optional: A single horizontal line divider

**Example format:**
```
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

THE CLAIM LEDGER OF RECOVER

Proof-to-Market Blueprint – Founding Pilot Delivery

Prepared for: Sarah Chen, Head of Brand
Prepared by: Farrice Cain
Date: 2026-08-05

Revision approved: [date or pending]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

### Frontmatter section

- **Your role in this proof:** Founding Source № [X of 3] — set in a distinct but not decorative box
- Font: Regular weight, clearly legible
- Box style: Simple border, no shadow or gradient

**Example:**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Your role in this proof:                │
│ Founding Source № 2 of 3                │
│                                         │
│ As a named source, you have access to   │
│ strategy updates, method refinements,   │
│ and case-study protocol before public   │
│ release. This is membership earned      │
│ through early commitment.               │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Section headers

- **All caps**, serif or modern sans-serif
- Numbered: SECTION 1 — THE DECISION
- Horizontal line above (optional)
- White space below

### Evidence tables

- **Claim Audit Stamp table:** Use three-color stamps (✓ VERIFIED / ? LIKELY / ⚠ UNCONFIRMED)
- **Proof hierarchy table:** Clear column headers, alternating row backgrounds (light gray / white)
- **Buyer decision map:** Structured rows, no bullets
- All table text: left-aligned, single-space

### Quotes and claims

- When a claim is quoted verbatim from the brand, use **blockquote styling:**
  - Left margin indent
  - Light gray background
  - No quotation marks (it's a claim statement, not a testimonial)

**Example:**
```
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Recover supports muscle adaptation and
performance recovery without known side
effects."
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

### Campaign angle copy blocks

- Angle name in **bold caps**
- **Core belief:** in italics
- **Lead promise:** in regular weight
- Hook directions: bulleted, short lines
- Proof direction: one line, clear
- Editorial claim-safety line: use three sections: "What may be said" / "What needs review" / "What is out of scope" separated by · (center dot)

## Page layout and pagination

- **Margins:** 1 inch all sides (letter size) or equivalent
- **Line height:** 1.5 in body text (readability)
- **Page numbers:** Bottom right, small
- **Section breaks:** Full page break between major sections (Frontmatter → Section 1 → Section 2, etc.)
- **Appendix:** Starts on new page
