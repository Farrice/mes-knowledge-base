# Ad Psychology Audit — Sprint Module (one-pager)

> Module inside the **Proof-to-Market Sprint** (fires in Stage 2: Diagnose, beside the AI-Search Shadow). Extends `PROOF-TO-MARKET-OS.md` — never a standalone offer, never the cold lead. Diagnostic spine: the six-tactic map from `skills/oren-dara-ad-psychology/`. Client-facing framing below is implementation-grade; internal notes in brackets.

---

## What it is (client-facing)

A structured read of your live ad account and organic channels against six consumer-psychology mechanisms that decide whether cold traffic converts — run before we lock your positioning, so the new message lands where your buyer's actual psychology already is.

**You get**: a scored six-line gap map, the 3 highest-leverage misses with evidence pulled from your own account, and the psychological brief your next creative round should be built on. Folds directly into the Blueprint's message architecture.

## The six diagnostic questions (the spine)

| # | Mechanism | The question we answer on YOUR account | Miss looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mirroring / safe space | Does any ad say the thing your customer only says privately (reviews, group-chat register)? | Ads in brand-speak; customer's real language nowhere |
| 2 | Purchase justification | Does anything hand the buyer their excuse (cost-per-use, investment framing) — and does anything filter for the buyer you actually want? | Price objection argued, never licensed; no status filter |
| 3 | Unfairness / scenario | Is the felt injustice in your category dramatized anywhere, with comments designed in? | Comparison claims stated flat; comment section ignored |
| 4 | Proof-in-frame | Is your #1 claim ever demonstrated on camera in under 60 seconds, or only narrated? | "Clinically dosed" said, never shown |
| 5 | Visual psychology | Does any asset carry the value prop visually before the copy is read (multiples, comparison, grid)? | Every ad is a headline plus a product shot |
| 6 | Credible explainer | Is real, scavenged authority (founder, formulator, practitioner) staged in a credibility-proving set — and pre-seeded on organic before it enters ads? | White-coat stock energy, or no authority at all |

Plus one cross-cut: **are you ideating from competitor ad libraries or from what your own audience loves organically?** (The second is the only compounding input.)

## How it runs [internal: 60-90 min inside Stage 2]

1. Pull the brand's live ads + top organic posts + review/comment sample.
2. Score each mechanism 1-10 with evidence quotes from THEIR material (never invented; thin comment volume gets flagged, not padded).
3. Rank the 3 highest-leverage gaps against the positioning thesis forming in Stage 3 — the audit serves the message, not the other way around.
4. Deliver as one page inside the Blueprint + a 15-minute walkthrough on the Stage 3 call.

**Claim-safe spine holds**: any tactic recommendation touching claims (esp. #3 and #4) routes through the claims-mapping tiers; your attorney has the pen. Comparison concepts stay opinion/experience-framed.

## Where it sells [internal]

- **Walkthrough ($250-500, cold tier)**: questions 1, 4, and 5 are demonstrable live on a screen-share in minutes — the audit's sharpest three become walkthrough moments that make the sprint's Stage 2 tangible. Never pitched standalone.
- **Sprint ($2,500, warm tier)**: full six-line audit is included in Stage 2 — named in the DM/proposal as "we also read your ad account's psychology, not just your message."
- **Founder psychology**: this module answers the founder's private line — "I know the product works; the ads just don't feel like us" — with evidence from their own account, which is the same proof-hunger the AI-Search Shadow feeds.
