## EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

**This Blueprint is editorial strategy, not legal or regulatory advice.**

Every proof direction above uses evidence provided or approved by the client. The client's attorney or compliance owner retains final approval on all claim language. Farrice reads for editorial signal strength and evidence fit; the qualified reviewer has the pen on legal clearance.

**What this Blueprint does:** Organizes the positioning, maps proof to claims, and provides language direction for your internal team and any content creators you work with.

**What it doesn't do:** Guarantee legal clearance, regulatory approval, retailer acceptance, revenue, rankings, or AI citations.
