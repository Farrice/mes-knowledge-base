"Why 32 NFL teams chose Momentous over the other NSF brands" — procurement as proof. Show the spec check. Make it transparent.

"Are Momentous a scam?" Run the autocomplete as the hook. Answer it with the Trustpilot stack, the subscription cancellation rate, the NSF chain. Nobody asks this question in testimonials. First one to answer it owns that conversation.

The Huberman stack for people who don't listen to 3-hour podcasts. Collapse the podcast funnel into 60 seconds. Which products. Why. Done.

Subscription transparency: "Here's how to pause or cancel in 30 seconds." That line alone converts fence-sitters and reduces the churn that kills their $45 LTV math.

Retail education for Vitamin Shoppe: "Here's what to look for when you see us on the shelf next to another NSF brand." Preemptive. You get to make the differentiation before they do.

**The real gap:**

Momentous built real products that work. Got real teams to use them. Earned real certifications. What's missing is the translation. The buyer already trusts the formula — they just don't know *why* your formula is different. And that's worth the premium.

Not *that* it's premium. Why it is.
