# The Angle Map: Activation Packet

## Offer card

**The Angle Map**  
**$750 prepaid · delivered within 48 hours**

A focused message decision for an established supplement or performance brand with a live launch, campaign, retail move, new ingredient, or repositioning.

You receive:

- A 60-minute live read.
- Three named campaign angles.
- For each angle: the core belief, hook directions, approved-proof direction, and an editorial claim-safety line.
- A direct recommendation on which angle to build first and why.

If the first read does not produce three usable angles inside the agreed scope, I keep working until it does.

This is editorial evidence mapping, not legal advice. Your attorney or compliance owner keeps final approval.

## What the buyer provides

Before the 48-hour clock starts:

1. The live campaign, launch, product, or repositioning brief.
2. Current product page, deck, or core message.
3. Any approved studies, substantiation, claim language, and internal claim boundaries that may be used.
4. The named buyer and final decision-maker.
5. The date the message must be ready.

The clock starts when payment and all required inputs are received.

## Fit screen

Proceed when the buyer:

- Already sells a legitimate supplement, functional-nutrition, recovery, or performance product.
- Has a real occasion inside the next 90 days.
- Can authorize $750 without an undefined committee process.
- Can provide approved evidence and claim boundaries.

Decline or re-scope when the buyer:

- Is pre-revenue and still deciding what to sell.
- Wants legal certification or regulatory sign-off.
- Wants guaranteed revenue, rankings, retailer acceptance, or AI citations.
- Cannot provide approved source material for claim-sensitive language.

## The paid read

### Before the call

- Audit the live product/campaign language.
- Capture direct source quotes.
- Map the likely buyer decision and the proof already available.
- Draft five candidate angles; bring the strongest three to the read.
- Mark every claim-sensitive line as usable, needs support, or out of scope.

### During the 60 minutes

1. Confirm the occasion and decision the brand must make.
2. Show the message gap in the brand's own words.
3. Present three named angles.
4. Pressure-test the belief, proof direction, and buyer relevance of each.
5. Choose the lead angle.
6. Identify whether the need stops at the Angle Map or requires the full Sprint.

### Within 48 hours

Deliver a concise decision document containing:

- Occasion and buyer.
- The current message gap.
- Angle 1, Angle 2, and Angle 3.
- Core belief and hook directions for each.
- Approved-proof direction and editorial claim-safety line for each.
- Recommended lead angle.
- Next campaign decision.

## Objection responses

### “Can you send more detail first?”

> Yes. The scope is one live occasion, one 60-minute read, and three usable campaign angles delivered within 48 hours. Each angle includes the belief it has to move, hook directions, the approved proof it should lean on, and a claim-safety note. If you tell me the occasion and deadline, I can tell you whether that scope is the right fit.

### “Can you guarantee the claims are compliant?”

> No honest strategist can replace your attorney or compliance owner. I map the language to the evidence you approve and flag where final review is needed. Your qualified reviewer keeps the pen.

### “Can you guarantee sales or AI rankings?”

> No. I guarantee the scoped decision product and the delivery window, not market behavior, rankings, or citations. The work is designed to make the campaign argument clearer and better supported before you fund execution.

### “Why not just use AI for this?”

> AI can generate options. The paid work is deciding which argument fits this buyer, this occasion, and the proof your brand can actually use. Then I turn that into three directions your team can ship.

### “We need more than three angles.”

> Then the Angle Map may be too small. If the need includes positioning, proof hierarchy, launch messaging, and a finished campaign asset, that is the $2,500 Proof-to-Market Sprint.
