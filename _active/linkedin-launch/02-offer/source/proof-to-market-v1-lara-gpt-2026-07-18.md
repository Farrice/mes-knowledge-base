# Proof-to-Market Sprint — 10-Day Launch Positioning & Messaging System

## Executive Premise
You have deep expertise in a specific domain (human performance, supplement innovation, sports science). Your barriers aren't expertise or results — they're *communication*. You can't easily translate what you know into a message that moves decision-makers to act.

The Proof-to-Market Sprint is a bounded, judgment-priced engagement that positions your brand or product for launch, repositioning, retail expansion, or category push — by clarifying:
- **What to say** (positioning + messaging, not general content)
- **What to prove** (claims audit + proof hierarchy)
- **Why buyers should care** (decision-maker motivation mapping)

Result: an 18-25 slide Proof-to-Market Blueprint that functions as your strategy deck + messaging playbook + client pitch deck.

## The Offer

**Duration:** 10 business days (intensive, time-boxed)

**Buyer Profile:**
- Head of Brand / VP Product Marketing at a supplement, performance nutrition, or sports-science company
- Preparing a launch, repositioning, retail expansion (Amazon, GNC, Direct-to-Consumer pivot), or new category push
- Budget: founder-band ($2-5K/mo), not agency-scale
- Mindset: science-led, wants differentiation that sticks

**Deliverable:** Proof-to-Market Blueprint
- 18-25 slides (strategy architecture + messaging examples + proof structure)
- Shaped for internal team alignment AND cold DM outreach to retailers/buyers
- Covers: positioning thesis, target customer psychographics, key messages (3-5), proof hierarchy (claims + verification), objection reframes, content pillars for post-launch phase

**Pricing Ladder:**
- $2,500 — founding pilot (limited capacity, proof-of-concept rate)
- $3,500-4,000 — standard engagement
- $4,500-5,000 — mature rate (after 2-3 case studies)

**Capacity & Timeline:**
- 1 sprint at a time
- 2 sprints per month maximum (10 business days each)
- 30-day feedback + refinement window (1 revision round included)

**Headline:** "Know what to say, what to prove, and why buyers should care | 10-day launch messaging sprint for supplement & performance brands | 18 yrs in human performance | DM PROOF"

## The Process — 5 Stages

### Stage 1: Extract (Days 1-2)
**Goal:** Unearth the real story.

- Deep-dive interview: founder/brand story, why you built this, what makes it different from competitor narrative
- Audit existing claims, positioning attempts, customer feedback
- Identify unstated assumptions in the category
- Map: what the founder knows (but hasn't articulated) vs. what the market believes

**Output:** Raw story deck (internal only, not polished)

### Stage 2: Diagnose (Days 2-3)
**Goal:** Find the wedge.

- **Buyer motivations audit:** why do retailers, practitioners, or end-users actually buy in this category?
- **Positioning stress-test:** which positioning angles have market proof (existing brand examples, search demand, retailer interest)?
- **Claims mapping:** which claims can you make? Which ones need proof tiers? (claim-safe taxonomy: general wellness → structure-function → reduction of disease → drug-equivalent claims)
- **AI-Search Shadow module** (NEW): Ahrefs Brand Radar pull showing how AI engines cite/describe competing brands vs. yours. Proof object for "our brand is visible in the AI layer."

**Output:** Positioning options + proof requirements matrix

### Stage 3: Decide (Days 3-5)
**Goal:** Lock the thesis.

- Position selection: choose the angle that has proof foundation + market differentiation
- Message architecture: headline + 3-5 core messages
- Proof hierarchy: tier the claims by evidence strength (study citations, third-party verification, proprietary data, consumer testimonial)
- Objection anticipation: the 2-3 most likely retail/buyer pushback scenarios + reframes

**Output:** Positioning statement (1 paragraph) + message frame (1 page)

### Stage 4: Translate (Days 5-7)
**Goal:** Build the deck.

- Slide-by-slide messaging (18-25 slides)
- Positioning thesis (visual + copy)
- Competitive landscape (claim differentiation)
- Proof architecture (why this product solves the buyer's core need)
- Example messaging for pitch, DM, retail outreach, content
- Post-launch content pillars (mapped to positioning)

**Output:** Proof-to-Market Blueprint (working draft)

### Stage 5: Align (Days 7-10)
**Goal:** Pressure-test and finalize.

- 1 iteration round (feedback loop with founder/brand team)
- Refine based on questions / resistance
- Final deck
- Delivery + kickoff brief for internal team or launch content creation

**Output:** Final Proof-to-Market Blueprint + launch brief

## Key Differentiators

**1. Positioning First (Not AI-Search First)**
This is not an SEO audit or AI-citation teardown. AEO/GEO is a *diagnostic tool* inside the sprint (the AI-Search Shadow module in Stage 2), not the lead. We lead with **messaging clarity** because that's what founder-band brands need most.

**2. Claim-Safe by Design**
Every claim maps to a question for your qualified reviewer (attorney, regulatory expert, or category specialist). We don't certify claims — we create a claims-question taxonomy so your team can vet independently. Uses the `skills/claim-safe-health-marketing/` framework (FTC/FDA buckets).

**3. Judgment-Priced Scope**
No billable hours debate. 10 business days, fixed deliverable, one revision. This keeps us from the unbounded "everything is a sprint" failure mode. Scope boundaries = quality boundaries.

**4. Founder-Band Budget Fit**
$2.5K-5K is real for bootstrapped founders and small brand teams. No $15K engagements. Accessibility + profitability.

## Content Strategy Integration (Post-Blueprint)

After the sprint, the Proof-to-Market Blueprint informs ongoing content:

**5 Content Pillars** (derived from positioning):
1. The science (claims + proof)
2. The user outcome (transformation stories)
3. The category shift (why the market is moving)
4. The founder story (credibility + conviction)
5. The evidence layer (third-party proof, case studies)

**Content Angles** (derived from pillars, 5-8 per month):
- Claim-to-question maps (transparency)
- Comparative positioning posts (vs. competitor category narrative)
- Founder insight threads (original thinking on the space)
- Retail/B2B positioning (different DM pitch for different buyer)
- Evidence posts (study citations, testimonials, efficacy data)

**Content Ownership:** You own the blueprint; we provide the pillar + angle bank. You (or a content creator) execute.

## Why This Works

1. **Founder-band budgets have ONE problem:** they can't afford to hire a CMO or brand agency, but they can afford a 10-day intensive.
2. **10 days is enough:** positioning isn't vague; it's a logical chain (target customer → motivation → message → proof). This can resolve in 10 days.
3. **The Blueprint becomes everything:** it's your pitch deck, your internal alignment doc, your content strategy foundation. It pays for itself in clarity.
4. **Claim-safe is table stakes:** in health/supplement, every claim is a risk. By building claims-question mapping, we own the regulatory risk surface.
5. **The AI-Search Shadow is the moat:** it proves Farrice understands the modern information layer (how AI systems know your brand), positioning you as AI-forward without the "AI specialist" identity.
