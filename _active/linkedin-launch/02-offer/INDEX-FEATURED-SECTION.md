## 💡 The Logic

**No pitch. Just proof.**

- **Featured section header** names the core insight (models quote brands, not ad spend)
- **Link 1 (Booking)** = intent (they want to book)
- **Link 2 (Teardown)** = competence (sample of your work)
- **Link 3 (Brand Map)** = market knowledge (you know the winners)
- **Link 4 (Process)** = transparency (they understand how you work)

Each link removes one objection. By the time they click Calendly, they're ready to book.
