I spent 18 years learning how humans change. Then I spent one year teaching it to a machine. The uncomfortable part is which one made me better at my job.

Most people hand AI a topic and ask for output. The result reads like the average of the internet, because that's what it is.

I did something different with the methodology I'd built coaching humans since 2008: the questions I ask first, the patterns I look for, the order I make decisions in, the things I refuse to do.
