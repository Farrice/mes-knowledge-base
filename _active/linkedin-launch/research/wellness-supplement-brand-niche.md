## 1. Brand ICP (the buyer persona)

**Who:** A growth-stage DTC wellness/supplement/performance brand ($1M–$50M rev) with a **founder-led or lean in-house** brand voice and no senior creative-strategy seat. Either the founder *is* the brand face or a single marketing lead is drowning.

**Decision-makers, by brand size** [V, 8+ live job posts]:
- **Founder / CEO-as-CMO** (sub-$1M) — strategist reports direct ("report directly to the CEO").
- **CMO / VP Marketing** (scaled) — Creative Director sits as a CMO direct report ($175–200K range).
- **Head of Growth / Dir. of Performance Creative / VP Performance Marketing** ($180–220K roles).
- **The media buyer** — day-to-day gatekeeper *and* top referral source ("senior buyers know which strategists actually shipped the angle that won").

**Their pains, in their words:**
- "Every marketer has access to the same AI models. So what separates the winners? Relevant context." [V, Salesforce State of Marketing 2026]
- Brands "risk blending into a sea of sameness." [V, Gartner]
- "The average DTC brand doing $2M is already paying for 8–12 AI tools… none of them replace the thinking, the creative judgment, or the execution." [L, admetrics]
- Consumer enthusiasm for AI-generated creator content fell **60%→26%** (2023→2025). [V, Billion Dollar Boy via eMarketer/Creativebrief]

**Their language:** angle library · variants/week · creative fatigue · hook rate · in-market ad-library evidence · compliance · "what's the angle." They buy *thinking that differentiates*, not volume.

## 3. What These Brands Need + The Wedge

Not more volume — a **differentiating thinking layer**: human judgment AI can't supply (strategy, POV, distinctiveness); an operating system that uses AI for research/ops while keeping the *visible* creative authentically human; and credibility to make claims land without disease-claim landmines. The cited fix: *"AI is your research engine, not your content engine — inject real experience, contrarian insight, and proof"* [L, izwiq].

Two places AI **provably** fails in this category:
1. **Body-contact trust** — *"AI content underperforms in high-consideration categories where trust is the main conversion driver… any product that contacts the body… strong hook performance but weak purchase rates."* [L, paid-social operator]
2. **Regulated claims** — compliance limits creative variety; FTC fines up to ~$43,792/violation in 2026; GLP-1/wellness warning letters now routine [V/L, ArentFox Schiff + Tonic Studio].

**THE SHARPEST WEDGE (18yr performance + AI-depth):** Most operators run the AI variant-machine; almost none supply *substantiated lived expertise* that survives BOTH the compliance gate AND the authenticity gate.
> **Position:** *"The AI creative system that's also the credible expert in the room — so your claims are defensible and your content doesn't sound like everyone else's bottle."*
> **Lead wedge line:** *Everyone has the same AI models — you have something they can't prompt: someone who actually knows what works in a body.*

## 4. Where & How They Hire/Retain — both entry paths

**Where they source** (best→last) [V, adlibrary.com + fractionalfridays]:
1. **Direct outreach sourced on OUTPUT** — pull a competitor's top live ad → trace who ran creative via LinkedIn job history. (Highest yield.)
2. **Twitter/X + Substack/YouTube teardowns** — public output = the case study.
3. **Agency-alumni networks** (cycle out every 18–30 mo). 4. **Media-buyer / network referrals.** 5. **Slack/Discord** (Demand Curve, Common Thread, OnlineGeniuses, Ecom Buyer Club). 6. **Fractional marketplaces** (MarketerHire, Toptal, Contra) — **job boards = last resort.**

**ROLE vs CLIENT — spend-tier decides the model** [V, adlibrary/fractionalfridays]:
- **<$300K/mo Meta spend → fractional first** ($4K–9K/mo, 1–2 days/wk, 10–14 day ramp).
- **$300K–$1M → FTE** ($130–260K loaded). **>$1M → in-house team.**
- **SEAT** (FTE/fractional/embedded): wants embedded strategic ownership, ~1-yr horizon; gated on hire-readiness (steady $15K+/mo spend, 8+ variants/wk).
- **CLIENT** (project/retainer): sold as a tight starter offer (audit/teardown/sprint), month-to-month, no lock-in.

**"YES signals" for a no-logo outsider** (most consistent finding):
- **Override signal:** a *published, dated, named* angle library / teardown thread with specific accounts + result deltas. "If yes, interview. If not, treat the resume as marketing copy." [V]
- **Pass the paid case test** ($300–500, 90 min, real account): 3 briefs, each angle/claim/proof/hook/format, 3 *different* angles. 8+ = hired — most predictive screen. [V]
- **Show thinking/decisions, not polish** (NDA-bound → demonstrate process). **Permissionless audit of the actual target brand.** **Niche compliance fluency** named repeatedly. **Credible AI-in-workflow POV** ("what it does well + where it falls short") is now an explicit ask. [V, 4am/Vysta/Mercator]

## 6. Winning Content Formats (what the daily engine biases toward)

1. **ALL-CAPS educational title + single-mechanism teardown** — Huberman "NEUROPLASTICITY: THE 4 STEPS" (45.9K), "FACE PULLS: SET YOUR DEFAULT POSTURE" (46.4K). Teaching one mechanism > motivation. [APIFY-IG hubermanlab]
2. **Comment-to-DM lead loop** — "Comment PEPTIDES & I'll DM the episode" = 20.5K likes / **10,259 comments**. Engagement bait that builds a list. [APIFY-IG]
3. **Myth-correction / counterintuitive truth** — "Intermittent fasting only works insofar as it restricts calories" (6.9K). Authority via nuance. [APIFY-IG]
4. **Source-cited claim cards** — AG1 captions append USDA/journal citations. Proof-stacking = the trust format for supplements. [APIFY-IG drinkag1]
5. **Stack / routine ritual** — "AG1 in the morning, AGZ at night." Habit-anchoring. [APIFY-IG]
6. **Expert-panel clip + named credential** — Huberman + Jeff Cavaliere "@athleanx, licensed PT, CSCS." Borrowed authority. [APIFY-IG]
7. **Product-drop / identity (weakest for education)** — Gymshark "new GSLC just dropped." Lower transfer; use sparingly. [APIFY-IG gymshark]

> **Bias the engine toward #1–#4** — mechanism teaching + cited proof are where 18yr lived authority compounds and AI alone can't. *Differentiation line: "True before it's scaled."*
