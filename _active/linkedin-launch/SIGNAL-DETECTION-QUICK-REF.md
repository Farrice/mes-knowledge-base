# Signal Detection Quick Reference
## What to Look for Every Day (Copy This to Your Phone)

> This is your daily checklist. Every end-of-day signal scan (15 min), run through this and tag your tracker. Over time, you'll know what "warm" looks like without checking.

---

## DAILY SIGNAL SCAN (End of Day, 9–10pm)

### CHECK 1: LinkedIn Notifications (2 min)
Open your LinkedIn notifications. Look for:

**Profile view notification**
```
"[Name] viewed your profile"
→ IF brand founder/CMO/head of content = SIGNAL HIT
→ Tag: "Profile view from [Name] @ [Brand]" in tracker
→ Next action: Warm them via comment on their brand's next post (within 24hr)
```

**Post save notification**
```
"[Name] saved your post"
→ IF brand founder/CMO/head of content = SIGNAL HIT (stronger than view)
→ Tag: "Save from [Name] @ [Brand]" in tracker
→ Next action: Warm them via comment on their brand's next post (within 24hr)
```

**Comment on your post**
```
"[Name] commented: '[comment text]'"
→ IF substantive (not "great post" or emoji) = SIGNAL
→ IF from brand founder/CMO/head of content = SIGNAL HIT
→ Tag: "Comment from [Name] @ [Brand]" in tracker
→ Next action: Reply with real depth (not a thank-you), leave door open for DM
```

**Connection request**
```
"[Name] wants to connect"
→ IF brand founder/CMO/head of content = SIGNAL HIT (they initiated)
→ Tag: "Connection from [Name] @ [Brand]" in tracker
→ Next action: Accept + DM within 24hr (soft opener, not a pitch)
```

**Message (DM)**
```
"[Name] sent you a message"
→ IF reply to your teardown DM = SIGNAL HIT (warmest signal)
→ Tag: "DM reply from [Name] @ [Brand]" in tracker
→ Next action: Respond within 2 hours. Qualify (role/client) + offer discovery call
```

### CHECK 2: Your Post Analytics (2 min)
Click into today's LinkedIn post (or check your analytics dashboard if you use LinkedIn Premium).

Look for:
- **Engagements from decision-makers.** Scroll the likes/comments. Any brand founders? CMOs? Note the names.
- **Engagement rate.** If >3% (your baseline for high-performing posts), it likely attracted quality eyeballs. Check who's engaging.

### CHECK 3: Your Recent Comments (1 min)
Scroll through your recent comments (on brand founders' posts + category voices).

Look for:
- **Replies to your comments.** Did anyone respond? If substantive = warm. Note it.
- **Engagement on your comments.** Did a brand's team like/reply to your comment? Signal.

## WEEKLY WARM-UP SEQUENCING (What to Do When You See a Signal)

### Signal Type: Profile View or Save
**Timing:** Same day or next morning.
**Action:** Leave a thoughtful comment on their brand's MOST RECENT post.
- Read the post carefully. Leave a 2–3 sentence comment that shows you know their brand.
- Goal: They see your name twice in 24hr (view + comment). Builds familiarity.
- Example:
  ```
  Their post: "We've been listening to our community on flavor variety..."
  Your comment: "The irony of 'variety' copy is [insight]. Your community's actually signaling [deeper pattern]."
  ```

### Signal Type: Substantive Comment on Your Post (from decision-maker)
**Timing:** Within 12 hours.
**Action:** Reply with depth. Not "thanks for the engagement!" but actual thinking.
- Respond to what they said. Build on it. Show you're not an automaton.
- Example:
  ```
  Their comment: "This is a different angle on retention..."
  Your reply: "Exactly. Most content strategists optimize for [X], but your instinct on [Y] is where the moat is."
  ```

### Signal Type: Connection Request
**Timing:** Accept immediately. DM within 24 hours.
**Action:** Warm, non-pitch opener.
- "Thanks for connecting. I've been following [brand]'s content—particularly [specific post]. There's an angle gap there I think about a lot."
- Goal: Open a door. Don't pitch. Read for whether they want to talk.
- If they engage back: "Would be great to grab a call and compare notes on [niche topic]. No pitch—just shop talk."

### Signal Type: DM Reply to Your Teardown
**Timing:** Respond within 2 hours (if morning/business hours; if evening, next morning).
**Action:** Qualify + book.
- "Thanks for the reply. Quick question—are you looking to hire someone for the team, or run a pilot project first?"
- Listen to their answer. Determine track (role vs. client).
- Offer discovery call. "Let's grab 30 min this week. I want to understand your situation better so I'm pitching the right thing."
- Send calendar link with two time options.

## SIGNAL CONFIDENCE LEVELS

Use these to prioritize which leads to warm up first:

**🔥 HOT (Urgent, warm within 12 hours)**
- DM reply to your teardown
- Comment on your post from brand founder (substantive)
- Connection request from decision-maker

**🟡 WARM (Warm within 24 hours)**
- Profile view from brand founder/CMO/head of content
- Save from brand founder/CMO/head of content
- Reply to your comment on their brand's post

**🔵 COOL (Warm within 3–5 days or accumulate 2+ signals)**
- Engagement on your posts from people you can't immediately identify
- Views/saves from accounts you can't confirm are decision-makers (yet)
- Profile views from agency accounts (referral potential, lower priority)

---

## RED FLAGS (Don't Engage; Move On)

- **Bot/spam profile:** No real posts, looks like a sales-automation account. Move on.
- **Direct sales pitch in DM from them:** They're not a prospect; they're selling to you. Politely pass.
- **Engagement from competitors' accounts:** You can engage, but don't expect them to convert. Acknowledge and move on.
- **Engagement on a controversial post (politics, polarizing):** Check if the engagement is thoughtful or trolling. Trolling = don't respond. Thoughtful = engage.

## TRACKER LOGGING FORMULA

Every time you spot a signal, log it like this (copy/paste into your tracker):

```
| [Name] | [Brand] | [Role: CMO/Founder/Head of Content] | [Track guess: role/client/unknown] | [Stage: identified → warmed → dm'd → replied] | [Date] | [Next action] |
```

Example:
```
| Dana | Gainful | Head of Growth | client | warmed | 2026-06-24 | warm comment on next brand post, then teardown DM in 2 days |
```

Keep it simple. This is a tool to help you remember who to follow up with, not a CRM replacement.

Healthy Month 2+: 30–50 total signals (decision-makers are now finding you proactively).

## COMMON MISTAKES (Avoid These)

**Mistake 1: Waiting for a DM reply before you warm them up.**
- Wrong. They need 2–3 touchpoints before they feel safe enough to reply.
- Do: View → warm comment on their brand → teardown DM (over 2–5 days). *Then* they're warm.

**Mistake 2: Mistaking engagement for signal.**
- Wrong. Likes from random followers ≠ decision-maker profile views.
- Do: Prioritize decision-maker engagement (brand founders, CMOs, heads of content, growth leads).

**Mistake 3: Responding to every comment instantly.**
- Wrong. Over-eager looks like automation.
- Do: Respond within 12–24 hours, on your schedule.

**Mistake 4: Pitching too early.**
- Wrong. They haven't decided whether they like you yet.
- Do: Warm (2–3 touchpoints) → teardown (free Elite Magnet) → discovery call (them asking for a call or you asking after good vibes) → pitch (Angle Audit).

**Mistake 5: Logging false signals.**
- Wrong. "They liked my post" ≠ "they're a decision-maker interested in working together."
- Do: Log only verified signals (verified as decision-maker via profile check).

## DECISION-MAKER ID CHEAT SHEET

When you see a profile view notification or engagement, open their profile and check:

**If they have any of these titles/roles = LOG IT**
- CMO, VP of Marketing, Head of Marketing
- Head of Content, Content Director, Social Media Director
- Founder, Co-Founder
- Head of Growth, Growth Lead
- VP of Brand, Brand Director
- "Ads/Creative" lead or Director

**If their company is in your beachhead niche = LOG IT**
- They work at a wellness/supplement/performance brand you've identified

**If their post history shows they make content/brand decisions = LOG IT**
- They post regularly about strategy, content, brand positioning (not just company news)

**If none of the above = Don't log it yet**
- Wait until you have 2+ signals from them before adding to your tracker
