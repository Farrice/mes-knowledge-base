## LAYER 1: DAILY CONTENT MOTION (The Foundation)

```
Every Day (Mon-Fri)
├─ 9-10am: POST 1x
│  └─ Via /linkedin-daily (Cognitive Signature gate + voice gate + fact-verify)
│     └─ Output: 1 post in feed
│
├─ 10am-11am: COMMENT 10x
│  ├─ 5 comments on target brand accounts (Gainful, Four Sigmatic, Create Wellness, etc.)
│  │  └─ Goal: Substantive, depth-demo comments (not "great post")
│  │
│  └─ 5 comments on category voices / content creators (coaches, strategists)
│     └─ Goal: Thought partnership, build relationship
│
└─ 9-10pm: SIGNAL SCAN (15 min)
   ├─ Check LinkedIn notifications for:
   │  ├─ Profile views from decision-makers
   │  ├─ Saves from decision-makers
   │  ├─ Comments on your posts from decision-makers
   │  ├─ Connection requests from decision-makers
   │  └─ DM replies to your teardowns
   │
   └─ Log to tracker: name, brand, type, date, next action
```

**Weekly Volume:** 5 posts + 50 comments = content foundation for all signal generation

## LAYER 2: SIGNAL DETECTION & LEAD WARMING (The Qualification Funnel)

```
SIGNAL HIERARCHY (Priority Order)

🔥 HOT SIGNAL (Act within 12 hours)
├─ DM reply to your teardown
├─ Comment on your post from brand founder/CMO (substantive)
└─ Connection request from decision-maker
   └─ Next action: Respond immediately. Offer discovery call.

🟡 WARM SIGNAL (Act within 24 hours)
├─ Profile view from brand founder/CMO/head of content
├─ Save from brand founder/CMO/head of content
└─ Reply to your comment on their brand's post
   └─ Next action: Leave another substantive comment on their next brand post.
               Then wait 2-5 days → send teardown DM.

🔵 COOL SIGNAL (Act within 3-5 days or accumulate 2+)
├─ Views/saves from accounts you can't immediately ID
└─ Agency profile views
   └─ Next action: Accumulate 2+ signals before warming up.
```

**Weekly Healthy Targets:**
- Week 1-2: 5-10 signals (building momentum)
- Week 3+: 15-25 signals (content compound effect)
- Month 2+: 30-50 signals (decision-makers finding you proactively)

## LAYER 3: TEARDOWN CADENCE (The Proof Asset)

```
Every Monday (≥1/week)

┌─ STEP 1: PREP (1 hr)
│  ├─ Run brand_radar.py if radar >1 week old
│  ├─ Pick target brand (look for: engaged community + sameness pattern visible + founder findable)
│  └─ Read their last 5-7 posts. Identify sameness.
│
├─ STEP 2: BUILD (1.5-2 hrs)
│  ├─ 6-part teardown template (per teardown-system.md):
│  │  1. Credit first (reassure the human)
│  │  2. Sameness tell (the structural pattern)
│  │  3. Body-level truth (Farrice's 18yr edge from icp-emotional-map)
│  │  4. 3 angles I'd ship (concrete, true-before-scaled)
│  │  5. Compliance-safe note (edge that builds trust)
│  │  6. Close (for public vs. DM versions)
│  │
│  └─ Quality gates: voice check + cognitive signature + no AI-slop check
│
└─ STEP 3: TRIPLE-ROUTE (1 hr)
   ├─ PUBLIC PROOF POST (LinkedIn)
   │  └─ Generalize the principle. Reference brand respectfully. This is your portfolio.
   │
   ├─ ELITE MAGNET DM (to founder/growth lead)
   │  └─ Full custom teardown. "Pulled apart your last 5 posts—here's the angle gap + 3 I'd ship. No pitch."
   │     └─ Log to pipeline.md: brand, stage = "dm'd"
   │
   └─ PROOF TRACKER (internal log)
      └─ Log to proof-tracker.md: date, type, brand, link. Never invent results.

OUTCOME: 1 teardown/week = 4-5 leads into discovery funnel per month
```

## LAYER 4: LEAD PIPELINE STAGES (The Journey)

```
Lead Path Through Pipeline:

identified → warmed → dm'd → replied → call booked → audit → proof run → embed → closed/won

IDENTIFIED
└─ You found them (via Brand Radar or comment history)
   └─ Next action: No action yet. Watch for signal.

    ↓ [They viewed your profile OR you warm them via comments]

WARMED
└─ They engaged with your content (view + comment, or you've left substantive comments on their brand)
   └─ Next action: If hot signal, reach out. If cool, wait for 2+ signals.

    ↓ [You send them a teardown DM]

DM'D
└─ You've sent the Elite Magnet teardown
   └─ Next action: Wait 5 days. If no reply, warm comment on their next brand post + follow-up DM.

    ↓ [They reply to your DM]

REPLIED
└─ They're responding. Warm.
   └─ Next action: Respond within 2 hours. Qualify (role/client). Offer discovery call (2 time options).

    ↓ [They accept call invite]

CALL BOOKED
└─ You have a 30-45 min discovery call scheduled
   └─ Next action: Before call: read their brand. Prepare ROLE/CLIENT questions (Section 4 of playbook).
                   After call: send Angle Audit proposal within 2 hours.

    ↓ [They buy the Angle Audit]

AUDIT
└─ $500-1,500 revenue. You're delivering a 2-3 hour diagnostic + 30-45 min walkthrough.
   └─ Next action: Deliver in 3-5 days. Close walkthrough with: "Want to run the Proof Run?"

    ↓ [They buy the Proof Run]

PROOF RUN
└─ $1,500-3,000 revenue. You're shipping 8 posts + 1 VSL script in 7 days.
   └─ Next action: 40-50% convert to Embed. Pitch Embed after week 1 of content.

    ↓ [They sign the Embed]

EMBED
└─ $4-9K/mo (fractional) or retainer. Ongoing creative strategy.
   └─ Outcome: CLOSED/WON. First customer lifetime value unlocked.
```

**Expected Conversion Rates (Per Playbook Math):**
- Warmed → Called: 1 per 3-5 warm leads
- Called → Audit purchased: 1 per 2 calls
- Audit → Proof Run: 1 per 1 audit (or 2 audits before proof run)
- Proof Run → Embed: 40-50% (1-2 per 3-5 proof runs)

## LAYER 6: CONVERSION SEQUENCE (The Offer Ladder)

```
Tier 0: THE TEARDOWN (Free Elite Magnet)
├─ What: Custom 6-part brand content teardown
├─ Price: $0
├─ Purpose: Proof + warm opener + on-ramp to Tier 1
├─ Triple-route: public post + DM + proof tracker
└─ Cadence: 1x/week minimum (4-5 leads/month into funnel)

    ↓ [If they reply / book call]

Tier 1: THE ANGLE AUDIT (Paid Diagnostic)
├─ What: 2-3 hour deep dive into their creative + 3-5 divergence angles + 30-45 min walkthrough
├─ Price: $500-1,500
├─ Timeline: 3-5 day delivery
├─ Conversion: 100% deliver (no risk, clear scope), close with "Want to test this in your feed?"
└─ Goal: Risk-free paid case test. Proves they're serious.

    ↓ [40-50% upsell to Tier 2]

Tier 2: THE PROOF RUN (7-Day Sprint)
├─ What: 8 brand-voice posts/ad concepts + 1 VSL script in their actual feed
├─ Price: $1,500-3,000
├─ Timeline: 7-day ship (refund guarantee)
├─ Positioning (ROLE track): "This is your audition. Prove I can write in your voice."
├─ Positioning (CLIENT track): "Prove the angle shift works before you commit to retainer."
└─ Conversion rate: 40-50% → Embed

    ↓ [40-50% convert to Tier 3]

Tier 3: THE EMBED (Fractional Seat or Retainer)
├─ What: Ongoing creative strategy + angle development + content system + monthly strategy
├─ Price: $4-9K/mo (fractional) OR project retainer
├─ Timeline: Hybrid role (embedded) or monthly retainer contract
├─ ROLE track: You're a fractional creative-strategist on their team (day-to-day or 2x/week strategic)
├─ CLIENT track: You're the retained creative partner (4-6 pieces/month + strategy + brand-POV coaching)
└─ Outcome: Recurring revenue. Path to $4-15K/mo by Month 3-4.
```

**Revenue Math (Month 1-3):**
- Month 1: 1-2 Audits sold ($500-3K) + 0 Proof Runs = $0.5-3K
- Month 2: 2-3 Audits + 1 Proof Run = $3-6.5K
- Month 3: 3-5 Audits + 1-2 Proof Runs + 1 Embed signed = $8-15K + $4K/mo recurring

## In Plain English

**You post and comment every day.** This builds your authority and keeps you visible to brand founders.

**You build 1 proof teardown per week.** This gets posted to your feed (proof of thinking), sent as a free Elite Magnet DM to a brand (warm opener + on-ramp), and logged to your proof library (proof tokens compound).

**When decision-makers engage, you warm them up.** 2-3 substantive comments before you send the teardown DM. This builds familiarity and reduces the ask.

**When they reply, you book a discovery call.** On the call, you ask different questions based on whether they're hiring (ROLE) or testing (CLIENT). But the conversion path is the same: Angle Audit → Proof Run → Embed.

**By Month 3, you're signing Embeds at $4-9K/mo.** That's recurring revenue. Each month, you're cycling new leads through the same funnel.

**The system is deterministic.** Do the daily motion (post + comment + scan). Build the teardown every week. Respond to signal same-day. The conversion will follow.
