## Filled Example - Standard Thursday Mechanism Post

### 1. Spine Reminder

Farrice helps health, wellness, supplement, and performance brands turn claims, buyer questions, founder judgment, and proof into source-backed content humans trust and AI search can retrieve.

Lead with being named, cited, carried, trusted, and proof that survives scrutiny. Do not lead with GEO/AEO.

### 2. Asset Job

Date: next standard production day

Day: Thursday

Asset type: mechanism text post

Primary job: show that smooth claims fail when proof is separated from the promise

Offer rung strengthened: Claim-Safe Citation Audit

Publish or produce only: publish

### 3. ICP And Audience State

Primary buyer: Dana, head of brand/content/growth at a health or supplement brand

Audience state: problem-aware

Private pressure: rankings and content look fine, but AI search and skeptical buyers may be trusting other sources

What they need to feel first: recognition

### 4. Reader Pressure

The exact tension: the brand has proof, but the public claim does not carry it

The buyer line: "Our best proof exists, but it is not where the buyer needs it."

The wrong easy answer: polish the product page headline

The useful turn: move the receipt closer to the promise

### 5. Source Packet

Source or input: Week 1 magnesium claim teardown

Claim, prompt, page, or buyer question: "Can the buyer repeat this claim without feeling sold to?"

Fact labels:

- VERIFIED: FTC expects health-related advertising claims to have competent and reliable scientific evidence
- LIKELY: buyers trust health claims more when the limitation and mechanism sit near the promise
- UNCONFIRMED: no real brand page tested

Boundary: do not claim observed AI-search behavior

What this piece refuses to claim: no claim that one brand is named or ignored by AI search

### 6. Format Spec

Format: LinkedIn text post

Length: 250 to 500 words

First comment needed: yes

Carousel needed: no

Proof object needed: yes, claim receipt row

DM bridge needed: only if someone asks

### 7. Structure Pattern

Selected pattern: mechanism post

Pressure, scene, mechanism, proof, GET.

### 8. Draft Task

Write: a LinkedIn mechanism post about proof being separated from the promise

Must include: buyer trust math, founder/source material, proof receipt

Must avoid: medical advice, hard CTA, fake AI-search claim

GET: put strongest claim next to the proof a skeptical buyer would need

CTA: none
