# 02 - Pod Architecture And Cadence

## Purpose

This turns the content strategy into a weekly production system. It is designed for a solo operator first, then expands into a small pod when revenue supports it.

## Starter Pod

At launch, Farrice plays every role. Each role still needs a clean job so the work does not blur into random posting.

| Role | Solo Hat | Job | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Editorial Owner | Farrice | Decide the weekly fight worth adjudicating | weekly thesis |
| Research And Receipts | Farrice plus Codex | Pull sources, label facts, collect proof | source packet |
| Claim-Safety Reader | Farrice | Separate support, risk, and wording boundaries | claim notes |
| Writer | Farrice plus Codex | Turn the source packet into sharp public assets | posts, article, carousel |
| Format Producer | Farrice | Choose text, carousel, article, comment, or video | platform-native asset |
| Distribution Lead | Farrice | Comments, replies, DM bridges, saved lists | distribution log |
| Proof Manager | Farrice | Preserve receipts, screenshots, audit examples | proof tracker |

## Future Pod

When the offer starts closing, add support in this order:

1. Research assistant: builds source packets and prompt logs.
2. Designer/editor: turns teardowns into carousels and one-page audit visuals.
3. Ops assistant: tracks leads, DMs, proof assets, and weekly metrics.
4. Junior writer: drafts from Farrice's voice notes, never from blank-page AI.

Do not hire for volume before the proof rail is working.

## Weekly Rhythm

### Monday - Source And Thesis

Create a one-page weekly source packet:

- the live category signal
- the buyer pressure
- the claim or proof boundary
- the AI-search question
- the asset this can become
- source labels: VERIFIED, LIKELY, UNCONFIRMED

Decision rule:

If the signal cannot become a buyer-relevant audit lens, do not build the week around it.

### Tuesday - Teardown Tuesday

Ship the flagship:

- named brand, category, claim, or AI answer
- due-diligence shape: thesis, bull case, bear case, verdict, receipt
- one practical GET
- optional soft bridge only if earned

This is the habit-forming series. Protect it.

### Wednesday - Distribution And Listening

Do not rush into another post.

Use the flagship as the conversation surface:

- reply to every useful comment
- leave 10 to 20 serious comments on ICP threads
- record new buyer language
- capture objections or strong phrases
- send private teardown only if specific and respectful

### Thursday - Mechanism Or Newsjack

Ship one faster asset:

- AI answer red-team
- mechanism reveal
- claim-boundary correction
- founder proof note
- "the machine flattened this because..." post

Keep it text-first unless the visual carries real proof.

### Friday - Proof Rail

Turn the week into a proof object:

- save screenshots
- clean prompt logs
- update proof tracker
- draft audit object
- add one reusable template line
- note any claim you refused to make

Friday is what keeps the launch from becoming performance without evidence.

### Saturday - Founder/Trust Post

Ship one human trust asset:

- what Farrice sees that a generalist misses
- one private decision rule
- one client-side anxiety named without shaming
- one line he refuses to cross in health claims
- one voice-memo-to-authority example

This is where the Marcus avatar starts to trust the person, not just the system.

### Sunday - Review

Score the week:

- Which asset had the highest save-to-like ratio?
- Which comments came from the right people?
- Which phrase should enter the language bank?
- Which proof object got stronger?
- Which CTA felt earned or forced?
- What should repeat next week?

## Weekly Output Standard

Minimum:

- 3 LinkedIn posts
- 15 high-quality comments
- 1 proof object
- 1 offer bridge
- 1 saved source packet

Strong:

- 3 posts
- 1 carousel or article
- 25 to 40 high-quality comments
- 1 named teardown
- 1 private audit bridge
- 1 updated tracker entry

Do not chase more posts until the minimum is repeatable.

## Production Stages

1. Source packet
2. Reader contract
3. Claim boundary
4. Core turn
5. Draft
6. Format pass
7. Proof pass
8. Voice pass
9. Publish
10. Reply and capture

Skipping the source packet is how the work becomes generic.

## The 10-To-3 Rule

Every Monday, collect 10 candidate angles.

Choose:

- 1 flagship
- 1 mechanism/newsjack
- 1 trust/founder post

Archive the rest. This prevents idea hoarding from becoming fake productivity.

## Commenting System

Commenting is not engagement theater. It is market listening plus trust building.

Daily comment targets:

- 3 ICP accounts
- 3 category experts
- 2 founders
- 2 content/growth operators

Comment shape:

- name the real tension
- add a useful proof or mechanism
- ask only if the question is genuinely useful
- do not pitch

Bad comment:

"Great post. So true."

Good comment:

"The risk is that most brands treat source notes as legal clutter, but AI search treats them like retrieval fuel. The brands that make the caveat travel with the claim will be easier to cite."

## Cadence Guard

The system is working if Farrice can keep it alive for 12 weeks.

Cut anything that requires constant heroics:

- daily carousels
- too many named series
- too much design before proof
- deep research on every post
- multiple CTAs per week
- format experiments that do not teach the offer
