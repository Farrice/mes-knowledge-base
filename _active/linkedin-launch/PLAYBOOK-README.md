## Document 3: SIGNAL-DETECTION-QUICK-REF.md
**Status:** Daily operating tool. Keep this on your phone or pinned.  
**Length:** 2 pages (fast checks + templates)  
**When to use:** Every end-of-day (5 min signal scan). Also when you're unsure what counts as a "signal."

**Covers:**
- **Daily Signal Scan (End of Day, 9–10pm, 5 min):** Check notifications for profile views, saves, comments, connection requests, DMs. Tag each in your tracker.
- **Weekly Warm-Up Sequencing:** What to do when you see each signal type (profile view → warm comment on their brand's next post; DM reply → respond within 2 hours + book call).
- **Signal Confidence Levels:** Hot (urgent, warm within 12 hr) vs. warm (within 24 hr) vs. cool (within 3–5 days).
- **Red Flags:** Bots, spam, sales pitches, competitors (don't engage).
- **Tracker Logging Formula:** Simple copy/paste format for logging signals.
- **Weekly Signal Totals:** Count them up every Friday (5 min). Healthy Week 1–2: 5–10 signals. Month 2+: 30–50 signals.
- **Common Mistakes:** Waiting for DM reply before warming them up. Mistaking likes for decision-maker engagement. Pitching too early.
- **Decision-Maker ID Cheat Sheet:** How to spot a real prospect vs. noise.

## Troubleshooting Index

**"I'm posting and commenting but getting no decision-maker signal."**
- This is normal Week 1–2. Decision-makers don't find you on Day 1. By Week 3, if still flat, check comment quality (is it insightful or cheerleading?) and post quality (are they proving Cognitive Signature?).
- See DUAL-TRACK-FIRST-WEEK-SETUP.md troubleshooting section.

**"No one's booking discovery calls."**
- Discovery calls are a lagging indicator. They come from 2–3 warm touchpoints + proof + timing (they need help NOW). Expect most leads to be "interested but not urgent" in Week 1–2. Keep nurturing. Call volume accelerates Week 3+.

**"How do I know if this is a ROLE track lead or CLIENT track lead?"**
- Ask them directly in the discovery call. Section 4 of the playbook has the opening frame: "Just want to make sure I'm pitching the right thing. Are you looking to hire, or run a pilot project?"

**"Should I customize every discovery call question?"**
- No. Use playbook Section 4 verbatim until you've run 5+ calls. Then optimize based on what you learn.

**"What if a lead books a call but I'm not ready to deliver the Angle Audit?"**
- Tell them straight: "I can deliver [date]. Does that work?" If they say no, let them go. Don't over-commit.
