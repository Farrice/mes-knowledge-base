## Zero-Audience Distribution Motion (Jasmin Alic model)

At 0 followers, **comments are the distribution channel; posts are the destination.** Daily quota: 10 substantive comments (5 on large in-lane accounts, 5 on peers) that add a missing mechanism or a respectful counter-read — each one a micro-demonstration of the wedge. Reply to every comment on own posts within 15 minutes of posting (dwell + comment-depth signals).

## What "Momentum" Means at Zero Proof (KPIs)

Track in `daily/performance-log.md`. In priority order: **(1) comment-thread depth** (multi-reply exchanges), **(2) profile views per post**, **(3) saves** (when visible), **(4) inbound DMs/connection requests from ICP**, (5) impressions last. Likes are noise. A 200-impression post that triggers 3 ICP profile views beats a 5K-impression post that triggers none.

## Standing Constraints

- Voice gate (`voice-gate.md`) is pass/fail on every draft. No exceptions for "but the hook is great."
- No fabricated numbers, citations, or "studies show" — receipts or cut (hook library Rule 11 + Chain Step 5.5).
- One idea per post (Persuasion Stack Layer 1). "Comprehensive" posts are system failures.
- Never reuse language across pieces in the same window (ecosystem awareness).
- Posts are written for ONE reader: the invisible expert / multi-passionate solopreneur at their stage of change — 60% are pre-contemplation, so most content educates the problem, not the solution.
