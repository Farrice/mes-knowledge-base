# Two-Ring Audience + ICP Resonance Dossier

*Built 2026-07-31. Doc 07 governs audience selection and performance interpretation; this document extends it one layer down (identity-level resistance) and one layer out (the engagement audience that never buys). Where 07 maps **objections**, this maps **resistance** — the thing underneath the objection, which no answer resolves. Nothing here contradicts 07. Evidence grades: **[OWNED]** Farrice's own material or taste verdict · **[PRIMARY]** first-party source with URL · **[INFERENCE]** multiple credible signals, unproven in his audience · **[HYPOTHESIS]** must be tested by publishing.*

---

## 1. One-screen read

**Inner ring (converts).** The $1M–$15M founder and the growth lead who champions him. Their stated problem is creative supply. Their actual problem is that the most valuable asset in the company — the founder's judgment about what the product means — never left his mouth. "We need more creative" is the safe version of that sentence, because it relocates the failure downstream into production, where it's a budget line instead of a verdict on him. Buying an outside decision admits two uncomfortable things: a stranger named it in an hour, and the ad currently paying the bills may be the one they can't defend. **[HYPOTHESIS]**

**Outer ring (resonates + amplifies).** Growth operators, media buyers, creative strategists, copywriters, coaches, and the self-instrumenting supplement buyer. Most will never pay Farrice anything. They are not a consolation prize — they are the distribution layer and the free primary research. Their reward is a sentence they can use on Monday.

**The bridge.** Conversion happens at three moments: a growth lead forwards a post *upward* because it makes the team look competent and the decision look missing; an operator forwards it *laterally* as a taste signal; a founder reads his own private sentence in public and recognizes himself. All three require the same discipline — the villain is never a person on the team. It's the unmade decision.

**Bridge Message:** *You already said it on the call. We decide which part the buyer has to believe. Your team builds from that.*

## 2. Inner Ring — identity-level ICP

### The identity being protected

**The founder protects "I am the person who understands this product better than anyone."** His authority inside the company is founded on conviction, not process. He can hold a room. He closes retail buyers in conversation. So when the message underperforms, the only diagnosis that leaves his identity intact is a *supply* diagnosis: not enough creative, wrong agency, wrong hooks. A *translation* diagnosis — my conviction doesn't survive contact with a page — is the one he cannot say out loud, because it means the thing he's best at doesn't scale. **[HYPOTHESIS]**, consistent with 07's "translation-burdened founder" and with the register reference line *"I can explain it on a call; none of that survives into the marketing."* **[OWNED]**

**The growth lead protects "I run a rigorous testing program."** Volume is the visible proof of rigor — it's what goes in the weekly deck. "Thirty hooks are not thirty angles" is not new information to her; it's an accusation that her scoreboard is theater. Doc 07 has this right at the pain level; the identity read is that throughput is her professional self-image, so any content that treats volume as stupidity loses her permanently, and any content that *credits* the throughput and relocates the gap ("you varied the wrapper, not the belief") makes her a champion. **[INFERENCE]** — corroborated by role-collapse pressure in the category, where the same person now owns research, hypothesis, brief, and read ([creative-strategy role descriptions cited in 07](https://nl.linkedin.com/jobs/view/creative-strategist-meta-ads-performance-creative-at-the-longevity-store%C2%AE-4404321055)).

### Resistance (not objection)

Doc 07 §7 lists the objections and how to answer them. Those are real and the answers hold. Underneath them sit four resistances that no answer resolves — only recognition does.

| Resistance | The sentence they'd never say in a meeting | Why an answer can't fix it | Content move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The cheap-answer humiliation** | "If a stranger can name it in an hour, what have the last three years been?" | It's a status wound, not an information gap | Credit the team's work explicitly before naming the gap; make the missing thing structural (nobody owns translation) not personal |
| **The retroactive indictment** | "If we only claim what we can defend, our best ad becomes the problem." | Buying the decision risks convicting the winner currently paying salaries | Show that a proof-aware angle can be *more* aggressive, not less; "legal-approved can still be creatively dead" runs in both directions |
| **Founder-as-bottleneck exposure** | "The reason nothing lands is that it all still runs through me." | The engagement makes his dependency legible to his own team | Frame the 60-min read as extracting an asset from him, never as auditing him |
| **Champion exposure** | "If I bring in an outsider, I'm saying I couldn't." | Career risk, not price risk | Design the map as something the growth lead *authors upward*; she presents the decision, Farrice supplies it |

All four: **[HYPOTHESIS]**. They are the layer 07 explicitly leaves open ("provisional until real sales conversations produce exact language") and they resolve on the same trigger 07 sets — three qualified DMs.

### Private language bank — graded honestly

There is a hard asymmetry in this dossier and it should not be papered over: **every verbatim quote Farrice currently owns comes from consumers, not buyers.** The founder-side lines below are composites built from 03 and 07, and they are marked as such. They are recognition language to *test*, never to quote as sourced.

- *"We did everything right and we're losing to worse products."* — **[OWNED-composite]** (03 emotional register)
- *"The copy got prettier and kept saying nothing."* — **[OWNED]** (register reference, 9/10 human verdict)
- *"First: that's not even true. Second: it's working."* — **[OWNED]** (register reference; the 11pm ad-library scene)
- *"Our dashboard is green. We still feel stuck."* — **[OWNED-composite]** (07 §3)
- *"What's the angle?"* / "angle library" / "ad-library sameness" / "creative fatigue" / "CM3" — **[INFERENCE]** (03, from Gemini Deep, 90 sources)
- *"It didn't do anything."* — **[PRIMARY]**, but note whose mouth it's in: this is the *churn survey* sentence, arriving from the consumer. It is the single most valuable line in the whole corpus because it is the one piece of buyer-adjacent language that is verifiably real ([r/Nootropics](https://www.reddit.com/r/Nootropics/comments/1r5pqlj/), [r/Supplements](https://www.reddit.com/r/Supplements/comments/1rfatzi/)).

### Buying-moment states (the hour, not the quarter)

**[HYPOTHESIS]**, each tied to a dated trigger already in 03/07.

1. **The night after a creative review that decided nothing.** Two hours, twelve opinions, next steps: "let's see more concepts." He drives home irritated and can't name why.
2. **The Monday a competitor's raise hits the feed.** Neutonic, $6M at $60M, Apr 28 2026 **[PRIMARY]** ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260428841018/en/)). The whole cognitive-energy lane is now on a growth clock it didn't set.
3. **The churn export.** The "didn't do anything" rows stack up in a spreadsheet and read like a verdict on the formula. They're a verdict on the promise window.
4. **The legal cut.** The strongest line is gone and the replacement is wallpaper.
5. **The agency asking, again, for the angle.** He realizes he's being billed to supply the thing he hired them for.

In all five states the buyer is not shopping. He is irritated and alone with a sentence he hasn't said. Content's job is to say it for him.

## 3. Outer Ring — the engagement audience

### Segments and states (Cimorelli-style: where they are when the post hits)

| Segment | State on arrival | Hot moment (perspective shift) | Amplifies by | Buys? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **In-house growth / creative lead** | Mid-sprint, briefs due, structurally stuck between the creative team and the growth team — practitioners describe the role as *"very siloed"* in exactly this position **[PRIMARY]** ([Motion/Denney interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HIUqsMLp7sc)) | Being handed language for the gap they feel but can't escalate | Upward forward | Sometimes (champion) |
| **Media buyer** | Scaling/killing, allergic to strategy that never touches spend | A positioning idea expressed in hook-rate and CAC arithmetic | Comment + referral (03 names them gatekeeper *and* referral source) | Rarely; refers |
| **Creative strategist / copywriter** | Ambitious, framework-hungry, quietly imposter-ridden — coaches of ~100 strategists describe *"deep down there's almost a bit of imposter syndrome"* **[PRIMARY]** ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kpr6AL8g4lM)) | A named, portable diagnostic they can run tomorrow | Lateral forward, taste signal | No |
| **Coach / practitioner** | Asked "should I take this?" weekly, no clean answer | Permission to say "put it back" without being anti-supplement | Share into a non-LinkedIn network | No |
| **Self-instrumenting buyer** | Day 12 of a tracked self-trial, wondering whether to keep going **[PRIMARY]** (07 delta, 07-31) | Someone from inside the industry confirming the gap isn't his fault | Long, data-carrying comments | No — but supplies the receipts |

The last row is the strategically interesting one. This person cannot buy, and his comments are the highest-weighted engagement on the platform (15+ words ≈ 7.5x, per 05) *and* they are free primary research the inner ring will never volunteer. He is a research asset dressed as an audience. Harvest every comment into `promises-not-kept.jsonl`.

### Save / share psychology, mapped to the weights in 05

- **Saves (~10x)** come from *reference value*, not agreement. Every piece needs one savable artifact: a checkpoint table, a proof hierarchy, a teardown method, a named frame (Proof Decay Stack, promise window vs benefit window). A post that is only an argument gets liked and forgotten. **[INFERENCE]**
- **Substantive comments (~7.5x)** come from asking for *the reader's version of a specific scene*, never "thoughts?". "What's the line legal cut from your best ad?" outperforms any question containing the word "agree." **[HYPOTHESIS]**
- **Dwell (>61s ≈ 4x)** comes from scene first, diagnosis withheld to the middle. This matches the structure 07 already found in the historical top three and the 8/10 approved draft. **[OWNED]**
- **Forwards** — unmeasured by the platform, decisive commercially — come from posts that make the *forwarder* look smart to the recipient. This is the whole bridge.

### The better-off contract

Farrice's mandate: content should leave people better off than when they arrived, whether or not they ever buy. Operationalized — **every piece ships with at least one of four takeaway types:**

1. **A diagnostic they can run Monday** (fill in day 7 / day 14 / day 30 for your product; if you can't, that's the finding).
2. **A sentence they can say in a meeting** ("that's a promise-window problem, not an efficacy problem").
3. **A boundary that protects them** (what an ingredient study does and does not license you to claim).
4. **A permission that reduces shame** (your reorders aren't dying because the product is weak).

If a draft delivers none of the four, it's commentary. Kill it. This is the outer-ring parallel to 07's five-question editorial rule, and it applies to the same drafts.

**Mirror these** (extends 07 §6, doesn't replace it): what's the angle · angle library · creative fatigue · scale, kill, iterate · hook rate · CAC · the ad library · churn survey · "it didn't do anything" · "couldn't tell" · reorder · week two · put it back · the drawer · proof · the claim we couldn't use · legal cut it · "I can explain it on a call."

## 5. The bridge

### Where resonance becomes a DM

1. **The upward forward.** A growth lead sends a post to her founder with "this is what I've been trying to say." Requirement: the post must contain zero blame directed at anyone doing the work. The villain is always the unmade decision. Violate this once and the amplifier stops forwarding, permanently. **[HYPOTHESIS]**
2. **The lateral forward.** A strategist shares a named frame to signal taste. Requirement: the frame must be portable and creditable — it has to survive being repeated without Farrice's name attached, and pull people back anyway.
3. **The self-recognition.** A founder reads his own private sentence in public. Requirement: the sentence has to be close to verbatim. This is why the language map outranks the argument — recognition is a *word-level* event, not an idea-level one. This is also the mechanism most at risk from paraphrasing buyer language into elevated prose.
4. **The receipt moment.** A named teardown shows the read on a real public surface, which converts "he gets the category" into "he could do that to my page in an hour."
5. **The bounded ask.** DM ANGLE is small enough to be a curiosity, not a procurement decision.

### Bridge Message

> **You already said it on the call. We decide which part the buyer has to believe. Your team builds from that.**

Three beats, McRaney-shaped: it *credits* (you already have it), it *relocates the failure* away from his competence and onto an unmade decision, and it *protects the team* (they build, they aren't replaced). It contains no word from the never-use list. Short form for tight surfaces: **"You can explain it. Let's decide what it has to prove."** Both **[HYPOTHESIS]** until a real DM quotes one back.

## 6. Content implications

**Pillar 1 — Proof (teardowns).** Inner ring needs the *judgment* visible: the read, the method, the restraint of crediting what's good first. Outer ring needs the *method reusable*: name the steps so a strategist can run it on their own brand. One teardown, two payloads. Never brand-shaming — the operator inside that brand may be the forwarder.

**Pillar 2 — Thesis from lived experience.** Inner ring needs recognition of a decision they've felt. Outer ring needs a frame they can carry. The thesis post fails if it argues without leaving an artifact.

| Bucket | Inner ring needs | Outer ring needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Growth** | Their scene rendered accurately enough to hurt | A confession they've also lived (the drawer) |
| **Authority** | A decision framed so it's defensible upward | A named frame that makes them look sharp for sharing |
| **Conversion** | One bounded, low-status-cost next step | Nothing — do not ask the outer ring to do anything |
| **Personal** | Evidence he's an operator, not a vendor | The reason to follow rather than just read |

### Ten hook territories (ring · pillar · bucket)

1. The kitchen drawer where supplements go to die — *outer · thesis · growth*
2. "It didn't do anything" is a positioning brief, and nobody's reading it — *inner · thesis · conversion*
3. A supplement doesn't fail in the body, it fails in the calendar — *both · thesis · authority*
4. The buyer graphed his own brain for a month and did more honest marketing than the brand — *outer · thesis · authority*
5. Thirty hooks, one belief: crediting the throughput before naming the gap — *inner · thesis · authority*
6. What legal cut was carrying your whole angle — *inner · thesis · authority*
7. Five proof surfaces, and they decay at different speeds (Proof Decay Stack) — *both · thesis · authority*
8. Your founder can explain it; your homepage can't — *inner · thesis · conversion*
9. Reading one funded brand's page for the three places it promises a feeling — *both · proof · authority*
10. Half that job was telling somebody to put it back — *outer · thesis · personal*

## 7. Falsifiers and gaps

**This dossier is wrong if:**

- Founders buy with no identity friction at all — fast, unbothered yeses. Then the resistance model is over-theorized and 07's plain objection table is sufficient.
- Outer-ring engagement compounds while forward-attributed DMs stay at zero after 20 pieces. Then the amplifier ring is a vanity ring and the bridge must move to DM-first outbound.
- Consumer commenters crowd out operators and LinkedIn's expertise-match starts serving Farrice to consumers. Then the self-instrumenting-buyer segment is a trap, not an asset.
- The recognition mechanic fails at word level — buyers say the composite sentences don't sound like them. Then the private language bank is rewritten from real DMs, not adjusted.
- Growth leads convert and founders never do (or the reverse). The two-ring split survives; the inner ring collapses to one.

**Missing until published and measured:** zero founder or growth-lead verbatim in hand (all inner-ring private language is composite — the single biggest gap in this document). Zero current-lane post metrics. No forward attribution — add "who sent you this?" to DM intake immediately; it is the only way the bridge ever gets measured. LinkedIn engagement weights are inherited from 05's research, not observed in Farrice's account **[INFERENCE]**. The better-off contract is unfalsifiable until at least one reader says a piece changed what they did.

**Update trigger:** rewrite §2 the day three qualified DMs exist, replacing every composite line with what the buyer actually typed.
