# 06 - Deployment Calendar - 90 Days

## Purpose

This gives the launch a practical runway. The goal is not to look like a full agency on day one. The goal is to compound visible judgment until the audit-first offer becomes obvious.

## 90-Day Thesis

For the first 90 days, the content system should prove three things:

1. Farrice can see claim, proof, and AI-search gaps other marketers miss.
2. Farrice can turn buried expertise into readable authority.
3. The audit is the natural first paid step.

## Phase 1 - Foundation And Proof Rail

Weeks 1 to 2.

Build:

- Featured section with AI-Search Visibility Test and Claim-Safe Citation Audit.
- Proof tracker.
- first Soft-Line Audit Demo.
- first Teardown Tuesday.
- first source-backed article or flagship post.

Publish:

- 2 Teardown Tuesday assets.
- 2 AI-Search Visibility Test posts.
- 2 founder/trust posts.
- 20 to 40 serious comments.

Goal:

Make the offer legible and honest.

Do not:

- overdesign the brand
- create a course
- launch multiple channels
- make a fake case study

## Phase 2 - Category Adjudication

Weeks 3 to 4.

Build:

- four-edition Teardown Tuesday base.
- one article from the strongest teardown.
- one Claim Receipt template.
- one DM audit response template.

Publish:

- 2 more Teardown Tuesday assets.
- 2 mechanism/newsjack posts.
- 2 founder proof posts.
- 1 article or newsletter.
- 40 to 80 serious comments.

Goal:

Train the audience to expect a specific judgment from Farrice.

Proof target:

One public example that shows a claim boundary and a stronger source-to-search asset.

## Phase 3 - Audit Demand

Weeks 5 to 8.

Build:

- repeatable AI-Search Visibility Test intake.
- paid audit delivery template.
- one named-brand teardown per week.
- one "audit before the pitch" post.

Publish:

- 4 Teardown Tuesday assets.
- 4 AI-search or claim-boundary posts.
- 4 founder/trust posts.
- 1 to 2 source-backed articles.
- 100 serious comments.

DM:

- only after specific value is created.
- send one-page reads to brands when the read is specific.

Goal:

Book first paid audit conversations.

Proof target:

At least 3 audit examples saved locally, even if anonymized.

## Phase 4 - Retainer Bridge

Weeks 9 to 12.

Build:

- first case-study-in-progress packet.
- retainer proposal from audit findings.
- founder voice intake.
- monthly content/source map.

Publish:

- 4 Teardown Tuesday assets.
- 4 proof-to-content posts.
- 4 founder/expert translation posts.
- 1 deep article.
- 100 to 150 serious comments.

Goal:

Convert audits into ghostwriting or creative strategy.

Proof target:

One documented path from public post to conversation to audit to next offer.

## Weekly Default Schedule

Monday:

- source packet
- 10 angle candidates
- choose 3

Tuesday:

- Teardown Tuesday
- comment/reply block

Wednesday:

- comments
- capture buyer language
- draft proof object

Thursday:

- mechanism/newsjack post
- soft listening in DMs

Friday:

- proof tracker
- audit object cleanup
- article seed

Saturday:

- founder/trust post

Sunday:

- 30-minute review
- next week setup

## First 10 Assets To Build

1. AI-Search Visibility Test.
2. Claim-Safe Citation Audit one-pager.
3. Teardown Tuesday edition 1.
4. Soft-Line Audit Demo 1.
5. Founder Proof Port example.
6. Claim Receipt template.
7. Source-To-Search Map template.
8. Flagship article based on the AI shelf.
9. Featured section refresh.
10. Proof tracker update post.

## Content Ratio

Per month:

- 40 percent adjudication
- 20 percent proof/audit objects
- 20 percent founder/trust
- 10 percent newsjack
- 10 percent offer bridge

If the feed feels too salesy, reduce offer bridge.

If the feed feels respected but not commercial, add audit objects.

If the feed feels technical but not human, add founder/trust posts.

## Launch Metrics

By day 30:

- 4 Teardown Tuesday editions.
- 1 Soft-Line Audit Demo.
- 1 Featured section funnel.
- 1 proof tracker.
- 1 qualified audit conversation or clear learning about the offer.

By day 60:

- 8 Teardown Tuesday editions.
- 3 audit examples.
- 2 strong articles.
- 2 to 4 qualified audit conversations.

By day 90:

- 12 Teardown Tuesday editions.
- first paid audit or documented offer feedback.
- retainer bridge package.
- one real proof path saved.

## Review Questions

Ask every Sunday:

- Which post made the buyer feel most seen?
- Which post proved the offer best?
- Which post created the best comment quality?
- Which proof object got stronger?
- Which series should repeat?
- Which format created work without learning?

The calendar is useful only if it keeps making the offer clearer.
