## HOW TO USE THIS ENGINE (60-second read)

1. **Pick a pillar by what you want to do today.** Make the wall visible (P1) · prove the moat (P2) · indict the sameness (P3) · stake the human premium (P4) · just show them how you think (P5). One pillar = one job.
2. **Pick an angle.** Each pillar has 7 seeds below. Read the titles, pick the one that's true for you this week.
3. **Answer its raw-take questions in a voice note.** Don't write — *talk.* The questions are designed to pull the specific moment, the contrarian belief, and the lived cost out of you. That recording is the raw material.
4. **That's your post.** Either hand the voice note to a writer with the seed's structural move, or run `/linkedin-daily --topic "[seed title]"` to cook it through the voice gate.

The engine is what `/linkedin-daily` points at. It never goes stale, because the five fears are evergreen — being named for someone else, competent-and-obsolete, the claim that blows up, no-edge, and (founder) becoming the thing he despises. The machine changes. The fears don't.

**One rule above all:** reassure the human, indict the machine. Never a line that shames Dana or makes Marcus feel more replaceable. The machine rewrote the rules; the buyer did everything right.

### P1 — THE NEW SHELF
*Visibility before everything. This is the front door of the system: make the wall visible so the rest of the ladder has something to fix.*

**Belief broken:** the buyer thinks visibility is a ranking problem they already solved. The shelf moved; the machine stocks it without asking; right now it's stocked with someone else's name. Being on page one of a place nobody reads anymore is not being seen.

**Themes:** The Rank Tracker Is Lying · The Empty Room · The Invisible Shortlist.

**The 7 seeds:**

1. **The dashboard that lies to your face** (Dana). Wince: *"Your rankings stayed flat but the clicks vanished."* Move: Paradox Reveal — the #1 ranking she's proud of is the thing hiding the wound.
   - *Raw-take:* A specific MOMENT a client showed you a metric they were proud of that was quietly measuring the wrong thing — what was the number, what did their face do when you named the gap? · What do you actually think nobody's saying about rank trackers in this shift? · When did *you* trust a measurement (a scale, a PR, a bodyfat read) that was lying to you, and what did it cost before you caught it?

2. **The most knowledgeable person in an empty room** (Marcus). Wince: *"reads like it was written by someone who's never actually done the thing,"* inverted — the real one is invisible while the loud lesser one becomes the name. Move: scene-first (the empty room) → Paradox Reveal — what made him the real one (head-down, quiet) is what made the machine unable to find him.
   - *Raw-take:* A MOMENT the most-qualified person in the room was the quietest and someone less-qualified got the credit — what physically happened? · What do you believe about expertise and visibility you'd never say at a networking event because it sounds bitter but is true? · Where in your 18 years did *you* stay head-down and let someone louder get named?

3. **I typed your category question into ChatGPT** (Dana, plants the wall). Wince: *"If you're not in the AI answer, you're not on the shortlist."* Move: Anecdote Anchor + False-Frame Demolition — broken premise: *"the brands that win have the budget."* Analogy: assuming the strongest guy in the gym is the one with the most expensive shoes.

   - *Raw-take:* A MOMENT a client kept paying for the thing that used to work long after it stopped — what was it, what made them stop? · The uncomfortable truth about "buying more traffic" most growth leads don't want to hear? · When did a middle-man quietly insert themselves between you and what you wanted, and you didn't notice until you'd paid the tax?

5. **The screenshot you can't put in the board deck** (Dana, status/stakes). Wince: Dana's midnight fear — *the founder types the category question into ChatGPT and sees three competitors, not us.* Move: scene-first (the slide, the meeting, the founder's hand on the keyboard) → Paradox Reveal — the metric that made her safe is the metric that exposed her.
   - *Raw-take:* A MOMENT someone got exposed in front of their boss by a number they thought was protecting them — what was the silence like? · What's true about the gap between "looking busy" and "being seen" that nobody in marketing says out loud? · A stat a client clung to because it made them feel safe, that you had to gently take away — how did you do it without breaking them?

6. **Reddit is writing your reputation and you can't edit it** (both, Dana-forward). Wince: *"Reddit is shaping what ChatGPT says about us and nobody on my team can edit it."* Move: False-Frame Demolition — broken premise: *"our website is our source of truth."* Analogy: thinking your résumé controls your reputation when the room's already talking in the hallway. (Otterly: Reddit is #1 cited domain across all three engines — woven, not led.)
   - *Raw-take:* A MOMENT a reputation formed about a person/gym/coach in a room they couldn't get to, and became "the truth" before they could correct it? · What do you believe about where authority lives now vs. where brands think it lives? · When has a story about *you* been decided in a room you weren't in — what did it cost?

7. **Could the machine have written your last five posts?** (Marcus, bridges to P3). Wince: *"Content that could be published by literally anyone — which means it sounds like it was written by no one."* Move: Paradox Reveal — the posts were "clean," "professional," "safe," and that's exactly why the machine had nothing of yours worth carrying. ### P2 — PROOF THE MACHINE CAN CARRY
*The two-gate moat. Farrice's sharpest wedge: he reads the actual physiology behind the claim, not the headline on the study.*

**Belief broken:** the buyer thinks proof and *carried* proof are the same thing. **Bridge:** the Claim-Safe Citation Audit ($500 pilot). CTA: *DM me "AUDIT."*
