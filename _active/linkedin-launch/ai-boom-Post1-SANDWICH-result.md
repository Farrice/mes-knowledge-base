## THE SHIP-READY POST (prose-doctor corrected)

A client read one of her posts last month, looked up, and asked who actually wrote it.

She runs content for three wellness brands. She had no clean answer. The pause told him everything.

The trust had been thinning for months before that room, across forty posts that were 85% her and 15% nobody, that smooth, competent, faintly-too-polished voice every brand now wears like a uniform.

Her content got cheaper to make. That was the whole wound. When words cost almost nothing, sounding like everyone else costs more than it ever has.

You are running two funnels now, and AI is pulling them in opposite directions.

At the top, where people discover you, the machines moved in. Someone asks ChatGPT which magnesium to take, and the answer gets written before you enter the room. The 5W AI Visibility Index ran 3,800 supplement prompts from January 6 to April 30, 2026, across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. Four brands, Thorne, Seed, AG1, Momentous, took almost half the citations it tracked. AG1, reportedly the largest greens brand by revenue, landed third. The winners published proof a machine could read, and the models built their "is it worth it" verdict mostly from community threads. Reddit loudest of all.

At the bottom, the moment a stranger trusts you with their body and their money, the math flips. That moment got expensive. Your buyer arrives having read AI-written everything, and they smell it by the second line.

Most founders watched content go cheap and bought more of it. They flooded the half AI already flattened, then automated the one thing it cannot fake: visible proof a real person stands behind the claim.

The brands pulling ahead ran it backwards. Let AI flood the top. Spend the saved hours making the bottom unmistakably human.

The client who asked who wrote it had handed her a map. He was telling her where the value moved. It moved to the moment a stranger decides to trust you with their body, and the only thing that survives that moment is a real person, visibly behind the claim.

Flood the top. Build the bottom by hand.

**Bucket:** Authority (save-worthy reference; growth pull via the ChatGPT/named-brand entities). **Visual:** two-funnel diagram, off-white card, "Discovery / AI writes the answer" narrowing to "Trust / a human stands behind it," signature mark at the base.
