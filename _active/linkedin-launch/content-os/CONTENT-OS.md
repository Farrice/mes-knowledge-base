## THE DOMINATION THESIS

Health/wellness/performance content splits into two camps that almost never overlap: the **trust-credentialed dry-explainer** (correct and ignored — doctors, PhDs, NASM trainers lecturing) and the **entertaining-but-shallow** (faceless reels and myth-bait that win attention carrying no defensible substance). The rare operators who *fuse* credibility + entertainment — Rajan, Nippard, ZDoggMD, Israetel — are proven to win [VERIFIED], but they live on consumer platforms chasing reach. On LinkedIn, the health lane is occupied by calm, framework-listy, infographic operators (Partaker, Dan Go, Ben Meer) running zero edutainment, zero newsjacking, zero niche-blending [VERIFIED]. The audience that medutainment ignores — operators and founders who lurk, save, and DM — is exactly the audience that builds Farrice's authority *and* pipeline. That position, "credentialed AND I'll make you want to read this about your own physiology and category," is close to empty on LinkedIn.

**The one-line strategy:** Become the credentialed insider who **litigates the category's live fights out loud** — adjudicating supplement truth, AI's physiology errors, and the operator's view of how this industry actually decides — from inside 18 years of credentialed physiology (NASM CPT/CES/PES + behavior change), with **edutainment as the doorway and a true, undiluted physiology idea as the payload.** Adjudication is the spine. Edutainment is the delivery (tone, not topic). The operator + claim-safety + AI-error overlay is the moat. **The gap:** nobody has ported the credible-entertainer fusion into LinkedIn, aimed at operators.

## HOW TO USE IT (the 60-second version)

Three functions, run in a loop. **RESEARCH → CREATE → DISTRIBUTE.**

1. **RESEARCH** — spot the live fight + the ride-able moment. Daily 10-min scan of the body-expert beat (FDA/FTC alerts, r/Supplements, new studies misreported, AI answers themselves) inside the **48-hour LinkedIn-lag window**. Maintain the format grid (pillar × blend) as an idea quarry.

Run the edutainment + aha + psychology stack, then the pre-publish checklist + `prose_classifier.py`.
3. **DISTRIBUTE** — format for dwell + save + citation. Carousel for save-worthy adjudications, direct-to-camera for trust, text for newsjack speed. Entity in first 2 lines on jacks. Stay on-platform the first 30–60 min. Strategic commenting is the *bigger* engine when small.

**Value-first law:** Every post hands a tangible GET and exists to settle a fight, not to sell. **Default = no CTA.** Roughly **1-in-5** posts carries a light discussion-CTA (invite the conversation, never pitch). The offer — GEO/SEO ghostwriting for health brands — is *earned by the body of work* and surfaced softly. The pipeline comes from being the source the operator-class quietly saves and DMs, never from asking.

**The daily / weekly loop:**
- **Daily (15–30 min):** scan the beat · strategic commenting on the ICP's threads (never "great post"; leave a real insight or a respectful correction).
- **Weekly (3–5 posts):** **Teardown Tuesday** (the named series, non-negotiable) · one newsjack or mechanism reveal · one volume-pillar post (overlay always on). Signature ~60% / volume ~40%. No format repeat in 7 days.
- **Monthly:** the metrics review — pattern, not post. Tune topic/format weights, never the voice rules.

**The wince test on everything:** if it sounds like a growth-hacker's LinkedIn post, it's wrong. If it sounds like a credentialed operator settling a live fight the buyer is already having, it's right.

## PART 2 — CONTENT CREATION (adjudicate + smuggle the vegetable)

Full source, with the two fully-written walk-throughs: `content-os/content-creation-system.md`.

### The signature repeatable post — "The Physiology Misconception Correction"

The one post Farrice writes more than any other (~3×/week). In a single shape it's the edutainment engine, the aha engine, AND the AI-citation engine — and only an 18-year credentialed body-expert can write it. Two modes: **evergreen** (correct a held myth) and **newsjack** (correct a viral study/AI answer/brand claim in the 48-hr window).

**The 5-layer stack:**
1. **STAKES OPENER** (Kallaway Stakes-as-Housing) — open inside a scene where a real person did everything right and the floor moved anyway. Character + something at risk + urgency. Personally relevant, not life-or-death. The scene IS the curriculum.
2. **BIG QUESTION** (Kallaway specificity) — load the curiosity gap. Specific enough to form a prediction, not enough to guess. Front-loaded.
3. **HEAD-FAKE / ACCOMMODATION SURPRISE** (Kallaway × McRaney, the aha) — the corrected mechanism IS the head fake. Their model genuinely can't explain Layer 1; you reveal the mechanism, unexpected but immediately logical. Must fire all three at once: **surprise** + **relevance** + **safety**. "Oh!" not "Huh?" and never "yeah I knew that." This is the undiluted physiology payload — never watered down.
4. **FACE-SAVING BELIEF SHIFT** (McRaney) — "I used to think X. Then I realized Y. Now I understand Z." The pivot sounds like discovery, not being wrong. The reader leaves *smarter for having changed.*
5. **RESTRAINT CUT CLOSE** (Kobi) — do the honest version once and stop. Hand the tangible GET, close on recognition (image/declaration/bookend). No mic-drop, no milking, no cheap question.

**The fill-in skeleton:**
```
[L1 STAKES]  A [specific real person] did [the correct, recommended thing] for
             [time] and [the promised result did NOT happen]. [The stake.]
[L2 BIG Q]   So [the specific, predictable-but-not-guessable question]?
[L3 HEAD     The answer isn't [what everyone/the label/the AI says]. It's [the
 FAKE]       corrected mechanism, one step at a time]. [The clue they had but
             missed.] [Optional: the claim-safety or AI-error read.]
[L4 SHIFT]   I used to [old belief, with the detail that made it feel true].
             Then [the moment that flipped it]. Now I [the new understanding].
[L5 CUT]     [The single tangible GET, stated plainly.] [Recognition close. Stop.]
```
> See `content-creation-system.md` §1 for the two fully-written walk-throughs (evergreen magnesium-form correction + newsjack ChatGPT-magnesium correction) and the mode-selection table.

### The edutainment + aha + psychology checklist (pre-build)

Run before drafting. These are the *water the writing swims in*, never enumerated in the post.
- ☐ **The Vegetable Smuggle** (Kobi) — entertainment is the doorway, the undiluted physiology idea is the payload. Lead with appetite, never "Today I'll explain." Never water the idea down.
- ☐ **Accommodation, not assimilation** (McRaney) — design for SURPRISE not clarity. Fires only if surprise + relevance + safety all hit. If they can absorb it without changing ("so true!"), it's assimilation — redesign.
- ☐ **The Four-Step Loop** (Kallaway) — Stakes → Big Question → Head Fake → Rehook, running invisibly. No gap >2 sentences without an unresolved prediction. Every head fake passes "Oh! not Huh?"
- ☐ **The psychology levers** (woven, never visible) — curiosity gap (predict, can't guess) · identity/tribe ("the kind of operator who reads the evidence, not the label") · cognitive dissonance (the experience their model can't explain, *before* the resolving frame) · pluralistic-ignorance exploit (name what everyone privately thinks) · the Pause (the line break before the turn) · production/evaluation asymmetry (credential as evidence *inside a scene*, never a résumé bullet).
- ☐ **Trust-before-persuasion** (McRaney) — accurate empathy → honest acknowledgment of what does NOT work (even in your own space) → then what does. Honest self-critique converts more than any benefit.
- ☐ **The Restraint Cut** (Kobi) — cover the hot take honestly ONCE. No part-2 milking, no mic-drop. The anti-grifter signal the founder avatar needs.

> **Anti-slop teeth:** a post can ace curiosity and clarity and still be NOISE if it produces no belief shift or rests on a copyable edge. The defensible edge is the mechanism + claim-safety + lived credential — what an AI or a marketer structurally can't fake this quarter.

### The hook system (summary)

Diandra's mechanics. One job with humans (earn the "see more"), one with the algorithm (360 Brew reads the first 40–50 words and tracks whether people stop). Every hook opens a gap. Pick exactly one format.

| Format | Shape | Ceiling | Lean when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dense** | Continuous text, full tension loop | 140–160 chars | Text-only, data/proof-heavy |
| **Punchy + Context** *(workhorse)* | L1 bold claim → blank → L2 rehook | L1 ≤50, L2 ≤50 | Default. Contrarian/headline openers |
| **Single-Line Bomb** | One charged sentence | ≤50 chars | A take too good to dilute; with strong media |
| **Stacked** | 2–3 patterned lines, completion bias | lines ≤60 | A natural series (before/after, escalating) |

**Hard bans (non-negotiable):** no questions in hooks · **zero em dashes in hooks** · no emojis · no LinkedIn clichés ("game-changer," "deep dive," "let that sink in," "read that again," "here's the thing," "the truth is," "then it hit me") · no filler (actually/basically/very/just/really/honestly/literally) · no stranger-blind hooks (must work with zero idea who Farrice is) · never fabricate numbers. **Newsjack rule:** the entity name appears in the first 1–2 lines (borrowed attention). 10 domain hook templates live in `content-creation-system.md` §4.

### The GET standard

**Every post hands the reader exactly one tangible thing** they can check, ask, run, or decide *tonight* without buying anything. "Flip the bottle and look for the form." "Run the 20-question test on your category." "Ask the brand for the certificate of analysis, not the claim." If the reader finishes and can't name what they got, the post is **polished-but-flat — and polished-but-flat is a FAIL.** Cole's craft governs the sentence: terminal-word power, three-pass compression (cut 30–50%), the New-Information filter (every paragraph advances or dies). Density beats completeness.

### The soft-CTA rule

**Default = no CTA.** Roughly **1-in-5** posts carries a light discussion-CTA (Diandra's growth bucket: invite the conversation, never sell). The offer is *earned by the work*, surfaced softly, never the reason the post exists. The Restraint Cut applies to selling too. **Never:** "DM me to learn more," "book a call," urgency-stacked pitch — those trip the founder-avatar wince and burn the trust the whole system earns. Exact soft phrasings to rotate (one max, never on a jack): `content-creation-system.md` §6.

### The 6-item pre-publish checklist
1. **THE GET** — can I name in one sentence what the reader walks away with? (No → not shippable.)
2. **THE AHA** — does Layer 3 produce "Oh!" not "Huh?" and not "yeah I knew that"? (All three: surprise + relevance + safety.)
3. **THE EDU-FIRST WINCE-TEST** — smart operator naming the 2am thing, or a growth-hacker? (Growth-hacker = wrong.)
4. **THE FACTS** — every real-world claim labeled VERIFIED/LIKELY. Zero UNCONFIRMED stats. Observed numbers stated as observed.
5. **THE BANS** — zero "It's not X, it's Y," zero twin-sentence aphorisms, zero triple anaphora, zero "Here's what/why/how" openers, zero mic-drop, zero cheap-question signoff. ≤2 em dashes total, 0 in hook. No filler.
6. **THE RESTRAINT CUT** — honest version once, then stop. Close on recognition (image/declaration/bookend), not a question.

> **Deterministic backstop:** `python3 execution/prose_classifier.py check <file>` before shipping. Flags reveal-leadins, em-dash overuse, contrast-reveal, question-close, LinkedIn clichés. The checklist is judgment; the classifier is the floor.
