## 0. THE ONE-SCREEN OPERATING TRUTH (read this even if you read nothing else)

Six things move the needle, in priority order. Everything in this document is downstream of these.

1. **The first 60 characters decide everything.** LinkedIn's current model (360 Brew) reads your first ~40–50 words *before it decides whether to distribute the post to anyone*, and it tracks whether humans stop and read. A weak hook kills human curiosity AND algorithmic reach simultaneously. The hook is the only lever that moves both. [VERIFIED]
2. **Saves beat likes ~5:1 on reach.** [VERIFIED — Authored Up] An adjudication that a head-of-brand bookmarks to defend a budget decision is worth five "great post" likes. Build for the bookmark.
3. **The format is a reach multiplier you pick before you write.** Document/PDF carousels lead at ~7.00% engagement, native video 5.9%, text 4.30%, links 3.70% [VERIFIED — Socialinsider 2026]. And for *this* niche specifically, direct-to-camera lifted +42.2% (education hooks +15.4%; entertainment-led ~−20%) [LIKELY — Manea]. Edu-first, face-forward, save-shaped.
4. **Small account is an advantage now, not a handicap.** The 2026 retrieval system matches by *interest*, not network size. Precise, lane-consistent content reaches CFOs and founders who've never heard of you. This is the best moment in LinkedIn's history to start from zero. [VERIFIED — Diandra Pattern 18]
5. **The first 30–60 minutes are life support.** Engagement *velocity* in the first hour tells 360 Brew the post is worth pushing. You must be present to reply, in threads, during that window. [VERIFIED — Lara]
6. **Distribution is two engines, not one.** Posting is half. Strategic commenting on the right people's posts is the other half — and when you're small, it's the bigger half.

## 1. LINKEDIN-DEEP — The 2026 Format Reality

### 1.1 The benchmark table (memorize the order; pick format before topic)

| Format | Engagement benchmark | What it's for in this OS | Save behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Document / PDF carousel** | **~7.00%** (+14% YoY) [VERIFIED] | The save-worthy adjudication. Tier-list teardowns, due-diligence verdicts, mechanism reveals. The flagship. | Highest. Screenshot-and-share native. |
| **Native video / direct-to-camera** | **5.9%** overall; **+42.2%** for this niche [VERIFIED / LIKELY] | The credentialed-face play. You *saying* the correction to camera is the single highest-trust unit you own. | Moderate, but trust is the payload. |

**The escalation ladder (Diandra Pattern 11 + Format Arbitrage):** Test cheap, promote winners. A text post that hits → rebuild as a carousel. A carousel that hits → reshoot as direct-to-camera. Your own data is the truth; these benchmarks are starting points. Never marry one format — the **Wallpaper Effect** is real: when the feed saturates a format, the eye treats it as wallpaper and stops registering it. Rotate.

### 1.2 The scroll-stop law: 60 characters, payoff in 3 lines

LinkedIn truncates a mobile text post after ~3 lines / a pixel-width budget (~110 width-units per line, *not* a flat character count — `W` is ~4× the width of `i`). The job of those 3 lines is **not** to summarize the post. It's to open a gap the reader cannot close without clicking.

- **Line 1 (≤8 words / scroll-stop):** the claim or the anomaly. *"ChatGPT rates half of supplements 'not effective.'"* / *"The athlete slept nine hours and felt worse."*
- **Line 2 (≤8 words / rehook):** raises stakes, gives no answer. *"The half it gets wrong is the dangerous half."* / *"His recovery score said he'd nailed it."*
- **Line 3 / fold:** end mid-thought. The brain seeks closure; force the "see more."

**No questions in the hook.** A question invites the reader to answer in their head and scroll on. Statements and incomplete thoughts force the click. (Only exception: a Stacked Data-Question opener *with a chart attached*.) **Zero em dashes in hooks.** Both rules are load-bearing and align with Farrice's own AI-tells ban.

**The mechanism note:** every hook is mined from the *finished body* (body-first), never manufactured at the top. The strongest line you already wrote becomes the hook. This is why the signature post and this playbook are joined at the hip: you write the adjudication first, then this layer extracts and packages the scroll-stop.

### 1.3 The engagement engine (manufacturing dwell + debate from zero)

**The first-comment play (clean post / dense thread).** Keep the post itself free of links and external CTAs. Drop your own first comment immediately, holding the receipt: the FTC citation, the study link, the "here's the exact 21 CFR line," or the one-sentence so-what. This (a) keeps the post clean for distribution, (b) spikes early engagement density, (c) gives the debate a place to start. For a Misconception Correction, the pinned first comment is where the *full source* lives — the post makes the argument, the comment proves it.

**The reply window (the 30–60 minute rule).** When a post goes live, stay on-platform 30–60 minutes and reply to *every* comment with a threaded follow-up — not "thanks!", an actual continuation that invites a counter-reply. Threaded replies double dwell and signal velocity. **Never schedule-and-leave** a flagship post; a scheduled post nobody nurtures in the first hour is a post the algorithm quietly buries.

**Manufacturing dwell.** Dwell time = how long people actually read. The save-architected carousel and the direct-to-camera correction are dwell machines by construction. For text, the F-shape (single-sentence lines, double breaks) keeps the eye moving down instead of bouncing. A "most people stop reading at the third point — that's where the real mechanism is" retention bridge mid-post pulls the reader past the drop-off.

**Manufacturing debate (the honest way).** The adjudication format is *built* to produce binary reaction: you settle a live fight, and half the room was on the losing side. That's the debate — and it's real, not forced. The Restraint Cut governs it: state the verdict cleanly *once*, defend it in the replies, do not milk it into five follow-up posts. Forced controversy (a take you don't actually hold) is the anti-pattern that makes Marcus close the tab.

**Out-of-network reach from zero.** This is the small-account superpower. Two levers: (1) the **5-Field Author Signal** — your name, headline, company, industry, title are the *only* author-level metadata the retrieval model uses to decide who sees you. Headline must carry the lane, not the job title (see §1.4). (2) **Semantic lane consistency** — post deeply in 2–3 lanes so the AI builds a reliable profile of what you're an authority on, then surfaces you to people who engaged with *adjacent* topics (someone who read about "GLP-1 muscle loss" gets your "recovery mechanism" post) without you ever posting on every adjacent thing. Depth, not breadth, is what reaches strangers.

### 1.4 The profile as distribution infrastructure (the 5-Field tune)

Before any post strategy, the profile is the metadata layer that decides *who* the algorithm even considers showing you to.

- **Headline — the highest-leverage field.** It is retrieval metadata, not a vanity tagline. It must contain the *lane your ICP searches in*, not your title.
  - ❌ "Performance Coach | NASM-CPT | Founder"
  - ✅ "I make health brands the source AI search names — without a claim blowing up | NASM CES/PES + GEO"
  - This carries: the buyer's word (*named / source*), the two-gate moat (*without a claim blowing up*), and the unfakeable credential — as metadata.
- **About section:** open with a Cluster-2/3 wince line verbatim ("Your rankings stayed flat but the clicks vanished"), then the bridge message, then the credential as evidence-in-a-scene, never a résumé bullet.
- **Featured:** the Claim-Safe Citation Audit one-pager + the best Misconception Correction carousel + (once it exists) the Pennock-play screenshot of Farrice himself cited by an AI engine. Show the moat, don't claim it.

## 2. THE NEWSJACK / BRANDJACK ENGINE (Diandra, tuned to the body-expert domain)

This is the home of Pillar #5 (Operator's Lens) and the timely half of #1/#2/#6. It is the single fastest path to out-of-network reach — because you're borrowing attention that already exists instead of building it from scratch.

### 2.1 The LinkedIn Lag Advantage — your 48-hour window

LinkedIn runs 2–4 days behind X, Google Trends, Reddit, and the LLM conversation. Source a moment *there*, post it *here*, and you're "first" on LinkedIn even on days-old news. [VERIFIED]

**Daily scan list (the body-expert beat — 10 minutes, morning):**

| Source | What you're hunting | Why it's yours and no one else's |
|---|---|---|
| **FDA alerts / warning letters** | A supplement recall, an adulterant alert (the tejocote/yellow-oleander pattern), a brand getting a letter | Only a credentialed body-expert can say *what the missing substantiation actually was* and *why the label lied to the buyer* |
| **FTC actions** | A settlement, a penalty, a "deceptive claim" press release ($53,088/violation) | You're the only voice who reads it as **claim-safety**, not legal news |
| **r/Supplements, r/Fitness, r/PCOS** | A claim catching fire, a "does X actually work" thread blowing up | Reddit is the #1 cited domain across all three AI engines — what trends here shapes what ChatGPT says next week |
| **New studies / preprints (PubMed, bioRxiv)** | A study getting *misreported* in the press | The misreport is the gift: you correct the physiology the headline got wrong |
| **X / Google Trends** | A celebrity protocol, a viral biohack, a wellness influencer claim | 48-hr lead before it hits LinkedIn |
| **AI answers themselves** | Type the category's top question into ChatGPT; watch it name a competitor or hallucinate a mechanism | The AI's own error *is* the newsjack — the most ownable angle you have |

### 2.2 Entity Assessment (the go/no-go gate before you write a word)

Score the moment before spending effort. A miss on any of these and you walk away.

| Test | Pass bar | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Recognition ≥ 6/10** | The brand/study/figure is known to wellness operators (not just to you) | Borrowed attention requires the lender to have attention |
| **Recency** | Sourced in the last 48 hrs; <10 LinkedIn posts on it already | Past the window or saturated = relabel as evergreen, not newsjack |
| **ICP overlap** | A head-of-brand or founder cares about this *today* | A trend your buyer doesn't track is reach without pipeline |
| **Boomerang potential** | The entity (brand, founder, study author) has an active LinkedIn presence that might respond | A correction sharp enough to earn a reply pushes you to *their* audience — the single biggest reach event available to a small account |

**Boomerang in practice:** the Liquid Death precedent — a client's brandjack was substantial enough that the brand's VP of Creative showed up to *correct a detail*, and that correction comment outperformed the original. For Farrice: a Misconception Correction on a specific brand's claim, accurate enough that their formulator feels compelled to engage, is the goal — the correction proves the post was real enough to warrant a real response.

### 2.3 The angle ONLY a credentialed body-expert can take (the moat, made public)

Generic marketers run "here's what they did right." That's commodity. Farrice runs one of three angles no marketer can fake:

1. **The Misconception Correction on the news itself.** A viral study or AI answer gets the physiology wrong; he corrects it step-by-step. This *is* the signature post pointed at a live moment — newsjack + Kobi aha shape + body-expert white space in one unit.
2. **The Claim-Safety read.** When a brand makes a claim, Farrice is the only voice who can say *"here's why that claim is FTC-exposed AND why the machine won't carry it"* — the two-gate seam, made public, in real time. This is Pillar #5's sharpest weapon.
3. **The Red-Team on the AI.** "I asked ChatGPT to defend the five most-cited claims about [the trending ingredient]. Three collapsed. Here's the autopsy." Performs a scarce capability (ChatGPT is ~57% accurate on supplement efficacy [LIKELY]) live, on a moment people are already watching.

### 2.4 The So-What gate (the difference between adjudication and summary)

After drafting, run the ladder. If you can't reach Round 3, you're summarizing — kill it.

- **Round 1:** "This happened." → so what? → first-order implication.
- **Round 2:** that implication → so what? → second-order.
- **Round 3:** → so what *for a head-of-brand reading this at 2am*? → the reader-level stake. **This is the post's argument.**

News-to-insight ratio: the post must be **>60% your analysis**, <40% summary. If the news takes more than two opening lines, you're a reporter, and nobody needs another reporter when the press release is public.

### 2.5 Construction (body-first, entity in the first 2 lines)

1. Write the **analysis first** (the so-what, the corrected mechanism, the claim-safety read). Mine the body for the hook.
2. **Entity name in the first 2 lines** — non-negotiable. That's the borrowed-attention mechanism; the algorithm and the human both need to see *whose* attention you're riding before the fold.
3. **Speed > polish** on newsjacks — this is the one place text-only beats a carousel, because a 6-hour carousel misses the window. Direct-to-camera works *if* you can shoot and post inside the window.
4. **CTA = invite the debate, not the pitch.** "Am I reading this wrong?" / "Founders — would your formulator survive this question?" Growth bucket. Never a sale on a jack.

**Counter-read (the wince guard):** a brandjack reads as cheap *if there's no real physiology aha inside it.* Marcus winces at borrowed-attention plays. Only jack a moment where the **credentialed correction is the payload** — never jack for attention alone. That violates both the Restraint Cut and the trust sequence, and it's the exact behavior that loses the founder.

## 5. WEEKLY OPERATING RHYTHM + CADENCE

### 5.2 The sustainable-from-zero version (3 posts/week, solo)

The 90-day content-market-fit frame governs the start: **posts 1–20 = practice** (get reps, test hooks, obscurity is your asset — nobody's watching your failures); **posts 21–40 = find the voice and the community**; **posts 41+ = momentum.** Don't judge the engine before post 40.

| Day | What | Why this is the minimum-viable spine |
|---|---|---|

| **Thursday** | One newsjack OR one mechanism reveal (whichever the week's scan surfaces) | Keeps the lag-advantage muscle warm; rides one live moment |
| **Saturday** | One volume-pillar text post (lighter lift, operator overlay still on) | Maintains cadence without burnout |
| **Daily, 15–30 min** | **Strategic commenting** on the engagement list (see §5.3) | When the account is small, this is the *bigger* distribution engine — more reach per minute than posting |

**The daily engage habit (non-negotiable, both versions).** Build an engagement list (spreadsheet: category, name, **recent-activity URL** not profile URL — the stalker-optics trick). 15–30 minutes daily, leave genuine value: an insight, a relevant data point, a respectful physiological correction. **Never "great post."** Comment on the head-of-brand and credentialed-founder accounts whose audiences *are* the ICP. The reciprocity compounds: people who saw you add real value to someone else's thread come check yours.

**The post-then-engage window:** whatever you post, stay on-platform 30–60 minutes after and reply to every comment in threads. This is life support, not optional.

### 5.3 Rotation discipline (the anti-staleness rules)

- **No format twice in 7 days** (the Wallpaper Effect — the eye stops registering a saturated format).
- **Signature ~60% / volume ~40%** across any rolling two weeks. Volume pillars *never run naked* — always carry the operator, claim-safety, or AI-error overlay so #1/#3/#6 never read as commodity myth-busting.
- **Seasoning ≤ 1-in-4** (gaming/anime/parenting), and only when genuinely fluent. The default is buyer-native finance-rigor. When in doubt, run the spine.
- **One named series, run relentlessly.** Teardown Tuesday never moves and never skips.

## 6. METRICS / FEEDBACK LOOP (signal over vanity)

The trap is optimizing for likes — a vanity metric the percentile model already discounts. Track the signals that map to the actual goal (audience + authority + *pipeline*), and feed them back into topic/format choices monthly, not weekly (weekly tweaks cause style whiplash; the system needs a month of data to show a real pattern).

### 6.1 The signal stack (what to actually track)

| Signal | Why it's the real metric | Target / read |
|---|---|---|
| **Saves** (and save-to-like ratio) | 1 save ≈ 5× the reach of 1 like; signals durable reference value to the algorithm AND that an operator is bookmarking to defend a decision | Save-to-like > 1:10. A post above that should show disproportionate 24–48h reach expansion |
| **Out-of-network reach %** | The small-account superpower made visible — proves the AI is surfacing you to strangers who match the lane, not just your followers | Trending up = the semantic lane signal is landing. The single best leading indicator of pipeline |
| **Profile views from target ICP** | Heads-of-brand and founders clicking through = the "who is this person?" test passing | Rises within 7–14 days of a headline (5-field) rewrite or a lane-consistent run |
| **Qualified DMs / follows** | The pipeline signal. A head-of-brand following or DMing is worth more than 1,000 likes | The real scoreboard. Track *who*, not just how many |
| **Dwell time / read-through** | The 360 Brew trust signal; proves the format is holding attention | Carousel completion rate, video watch-through. Low = the retention bridge or the body is leaking |
| **Engagement velocity** (first-hour, as a multiple of your 30-day average) | The percentile model compares you to *your* baseline, not to a 500K creator. 3× your average in hour one = algorithmically "viral" for you | Track as a multiple, never a raw count. Kills the "my numbers are too small" excuse |

**Explicitly de-prioritize:** raw like counts, follower count as a goal (interest-matching made it nearly irrelevant for reach), and impressions divorced from out-of-network % (impressions to your existing followers prove nothing).

### 6.2 The feedback cycle (monthly, pattern-not-post)

Run a monthly review (not weekly). Aggregate ≥10 posts or one month, whichever comes first — *one viral post is noise; ten posts performing 2× above average is a signal.*

1. **Collect:** 30 days of posts with the six signals above attached.
2. **Find the pattern, not the post:** which *pillars* over-index on saves? Which *formats* on out-of-network reach? Which *hook shapes* on velocity? Which named-series editions pulled qualified DMs?
3. **Extract winners → topic/format choices:** if "Teardown Tuesday" carousels out-save everything, that's the lane to deepen. If direct-to-camera red-teams pull the qualified DMs, shift more of #2 to video.
