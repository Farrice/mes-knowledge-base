# Carousel 03 — Puori

## Slide 1

You can do the hardest and most expensive version of the work.

And still have a customer tell you they can't tell you apart from the cheap one.

---

## Slide 2

Puori tests every batch of every product against more than 200 contaminants.

Publishes the results.

Puts a QR code on the package so you can pull up the lab report for the exact tub in your kitchen.

Not a category average. Not a sample.

Yours.

---

## Slide 3

I could not find another brand doing that.

Their homepage says "One Ingredient. Unmatched Standards."

The founder's line is "At Puori, we worry about what's in our supplements, so you don't have to."

---

## Slide 4

Now go read the fish oil page. 635 reviews, 87% five stars.

"No fish smell, no taste, and no fish burps!"
"The first fish oil that I have tried that does not have the fishy aftertaste"
"Best part~ no after fish taste"

---

## Slide 5

I read fifteen of the most recent reviews.

Eight were about the absence of a taste.

One mentioned an actual outcome: knees and hips.

One customer spelled the brand name wrong while praising it.

---

## Slide 6

And then this one, three weeks ago, in full:

"It's fine. Not sure if it's better than Costco's and also not sure if I even need it anymore."

That's a paying customer of the most tested fish oil I could find, on the brand's own product page, saying she can't tell it apart from a warehouse club.

---

## Slide 7

Every dollar of that testing failed to reach her.

Not because it isn't real.

Because 200 contaminants is a number with nothing human attached to it.

Nobody has a feeling about contaminant number 147.

---

## Slide 8

The scan is the thing.

One person. One tub. One phone.

Their own batch number. Their own lab report loading on screen in their own kitchen.

That's the most concrete proof anybody in this category can put on camera.

Puori is the only one who can shoot it.

Right now it's a sentence on a secondary page.

---

## Slide 9

This runs well past supplements.

Any company whose advantage is verification has this exact problem.

Security vendors and their audits. Insurers and their ratings. Food, freight, anybody who gets inspected.

The audit is real, the audit is dull, so the audit stays in a PDF and the customer buys on feel.

---

## Slide 10

Cher is still going to Costco.

She didn't leave because the product was worse.

If your product actually works and nobody can tell—
