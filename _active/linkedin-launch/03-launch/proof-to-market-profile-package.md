## 3. ABOUT SECTION (paste verbatim)

The founder reads the launch copy and says:

"That's not it."

The agency has a brief.

Growth has an angle.

Product has a deck full of proof.

The website says something else.

Everyone is working.

Nobody is telling the same story.

That becomes an expensive problem when a supplement or performance brand is about to launch, reposition, enter retail, or make a category push.

More copy will not fix it.

More content will not fix it.

The team needs one clear answer to three questions:

What should we say?

What can we prove?

Why should the buyer care now?

I help science-led supplement and performance brands answer those questions before they spend more money on execution.

Through a focused 10-business-day Proof-to-Market Sprint, I extract the strongest thinking from the founder, product team, customer language, and already-approved proof.

Then I turn it into a usable messaging system:

• Positioning direction
• Central launch message
• Buyer problem and desire hierarchy
• Proof hierarchy
• Message pillars
• Founder narrative
• Campaign territories
• Homepage and product-page direction
• 90-day priorities

I do not pretend to replace science, legal, regulatory, or your implementation team.

My job is to help the people doing the work know what the brand should say, why it matters, and which proof deserves the spotlight.

That role fits my background in a way generic marketing never did.

I spent 18 years in human performance, coaching people at the point where information either became behavior or got ignored.

That trained me to listen for resistance, translate complexity, and find the idea a person can actually understand and act on.

Now I apply that same judgment to health and performance brands.

The goal is not more messaging.

It is one clear market story the founder, website, agency, content, and launch team can all carry.

If you are preparing a launch or repositioning and the message still feels unsettled, DM me PROOF.
