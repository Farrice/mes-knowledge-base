## 3 · The scoreboard (actions, never outcomes)

Count only what you control. From the playbook + council protocol:

- **Daily line:** posted? (Y/N) · comments (…/10) · warm DMs sent · replies handled · logged?
- **KPI order when signals appear:** decision-maker profile views → qualified DMs → calls → signed. Likes and impressions are explicitly noise.
- **Tripwires (council-ruled):** 72 hours without a rep = flag it to Claude in the next session. Day 8 = watch for the improve-a-document urge. 14 days of 7/7 weeks unlocks the receipts-in-public flywheel (parked path #3 on the PMF shelf).
- **Zero traction ≠ failure for 14 days.** The protocol scores actions because early reach is not information. 45 days of genuine contact with zero paid audits and zero calls is the ruled falsification line — nothing shorter is.
