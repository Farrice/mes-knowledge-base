## What the fresh research changed (2026-07-28 Gemini Deep, 146 sources — full reports in `01-research/2026-07-28-FRESH-RESEARCH/`)

- **Cash in 14 days comes from DMs, not the feed.** Verified timeline: first organic inbound conversations arrive at the week 4–6 median; sustained revenue month 3–5. Content is the compounding asset and the credibility surface the DM recipient checks — it is not the 14-day cash engine. Judge week 1 by conversations started, never by reach.
- **Zero followers is not the handicap it was.** LinkedIn's 360Brew ranks by semantic interest match, not follower count — niche-specific content from a matching profile reaches the right strangers. But it cross-references post content against the profile ("expertise mismatch penalty") — so the profile MUST go live before or with the first post, saying exactly what the posts are about.
- **Format:** teardowns publish as **PDF carousels (8–12 slides)** — the highest dwell/save format, and saves carry ~10x algorithmic weight. Convert each teardown's core into slides; full text can live in comments or the article.
- **The golden hour is real:** engagement in the first 60–90 min drives ~70% of lifetime reach. Post, then stay on for 60–90 min replying to every comment.
- **The 5-6 rule:** before posting, spend 15–30 min leaving substantive (3+ sentence) comments on 5–6 posts from target founders/operators. Trains the algorithm's association and manufactures early velocity. Engagement pods = 60–90 day shadowban; never.
- **Intent-hijack beats blank invites:** monitor comments on 2–3 supplement/DTC operator accounts, list the people asking real workflow questions, send personalized connection notes referencing their exact comment (documented 14% comment-to-booked-call in one case study). Blank requests still fine for pure network-building; the personalized ones are where calls come from.
- **Cadence ceiling:** 3–5 posts/week on 2–3 fixed pillars. Topic-hopping is penalized. The plan below stays inside that.

## The three motions (every day is some mix of these)

1. **Sends** — the wargame's arithmetic: ~160 sends and ~15 fit conversations inside 10 working days makes one close likely instead of lucky. The unit is the **one-gap DM** (one verbatim quote from their site, one named gap, 4 sentences, to a named person at a sub-$15M brand — 20 min each, 15/day) plus intent-hijack connection notes. The 3 deep teardowns are public proof, not the at-bat engine. This motion is the only one that produces cash inside 14 days.
2. **Posts** — the public argument, 3–5/week on two pillars only: (a) teardowns/proof, (b) the About's thesis (the unmade decision / careful-sounds-like-maybe). Teardowns as PDF carousels.
3. **Comments** — the 5-6 rule before every post + golden-hour replies after. From zero followers, comments are distribution.
