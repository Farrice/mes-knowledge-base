I spent three years behind a vitamin-shop counter learning why.

At 11pm you're in the ad library reading a competitor's copy, and the second feeling is worse than the first. First: that's not even true. Second: it's working.

Their label is fillers and fairy dust, doses buried in a proprietary blend nobody can check. Nothing in it is real, so nothing in it needs defending. The honest claim needs a paragraph. The lie fits on a banner.

You've tried what careful brands try. A new agency. More variants. Another AI tool on the pile. The copy got prettier and kept saying nothing, because everyone you paid could phrase the product and nobody could tell you what to prove.
