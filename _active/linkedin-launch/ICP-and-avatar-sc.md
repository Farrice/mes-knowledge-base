**Established S&C coaches, sports scientists, and performance consultants with 10+ years of measurable athlete results whose LinkedIn doesn't reflect 10% of what they've built — because nobody ever taught them how to translate what they do in a weight room into words on a screen.**

## 3. The Avatar: "The Invisible Expert"

### Meet Coach Marcus — 21-Year NBA S&C Veteran

**Demographics:**
- Age: 42-60
- Male (S&C skews ~80% male at elite level, but female coaches are emerging fast)
- Income: $120K-$300K+ (salary + consulting + speaking)
- Education: MS or PhD in Exercise Science, Kinesiology, or related
- Location: Near a major sports market or university athletic program

**Professional Life:**
- 15-25 years in the field
- Has trained MVPs, Olympic medalists, or D1 championship teams
- Runs or has run a performance facility, university program, or pro team weight room
- Has 200+ podcast episodes, published research, NSCA awards, or a book
- Speaks at NSCA, ACSM, or team-level conferences annually
- Consults for 1-3 additional organizations beyond primary role
- Gets all clients through reputation — zero digital pipeline
- Weight room may literally be named after him

**LinkedIn Situation:**
- Profile reads like a 2014 résumé
- Banner is either default blue or a blurry team photo
- Posts 0-2x/month — usually an event flyer or "honored to speak at..." post
- About section is a credential dump, not a pitch
- 1,200 connections (mostly peers, not prospects)
- Zero content repurposing from podcasts, talks, or coaching sessions
- Has no newsletter, no email list, no lead capture mechanism from events
- Website either doesn't exist or returns a 404

**Second Sub-Avatar: Dr. Priya — Emerging Sports Scientist**

- Age: 32-42
- PhD in Biomechanics or Exercise Physiology
- 8-12 years in the field, transitioning from university to private consulting
- Brilliant researcher, zero marketing instinct
- Has published papers nobody in the coaching world has read
- Wants to build a consulting practice but doesn't know how to make her expertise visible
- Pricing: $150-$300/hr for consulting, could be higher with authority positioning

**What they actually want (even if they won't say it):**

- *Speaking gigs that find them — not ones they have to pitch for*
- *A digital presence that matches their in-room reputation*
- *To stop watching coaches with half their experience claim the authority*

| **Invisible expertise** | "My weight room is named after me. My LinkedIn has 47 posts in 15 years" |

| **Revenue plateau** | "I've hit my salary ceiling. Consulting could double my income. But nobody outside my team knows I exist" |

### Decision-Making Process

```
1. Doesn't think they need LinkedIn → Sees peer getting consulting gigs from content
2. Tries posting → Gets 11 likes from other coaches. Stops.
3. Hears about ghostwriting → Assumes it's for "influencers," not serious professionals
4. Sees YOUR content about S&C coaches specifically → "Wait, this guy gets my world"
5. Reads the Scorecard → Scores 1 out of 9. Realizes the gap.
6. DMs you or checks Featured section → Proof Run: 8 posts, 7 days, risk-free
7. Proof Run delivery → "This actually sounds like me. How do we keep going?"
```
