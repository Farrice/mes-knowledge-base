### A. The AI-Search Visibility Test (the free DIY lead magnet)
**What it is:** 7 self-checks a brand runs in 5 minutes to answer one question — *can ChatGPT even see and cite you?* It's the wall, made visible, by their own hand. It surfaces the gap without diagnosing it, which is exactly what the paid audit is for.

**The 7 checks (DIY):**
1. Type your category's #1 buying question into ChatGPT. Are you named? (If not, you're not on the shortlist.)
2. Run the same question in Google's AI Overview *and* Perplexity. (They're three different citation markets — being named in one isn't being named.)
3. Ask ChatGPT "who are the experts on [your category]?" Is your founder named, or just described?
4. Search your top 3 product claims. Does anything credible online back them — or does the machine find a contradiction?
5. Are you cited *anywhere off your own site* for your category? (If your authority only lives on your site, both engines are blind to you.)
6. Check Reddit for your category question. (Reddit is the #1 cited domain across all three engines — is the conversation about you there, and can you live with what it says?)
7. Read your last 5 posts. Could ChatGPT have written them? (If yes, the machine has nothing of yours worth carrying.)

**The reveal:** Most brands fail 5 of 7 and didn't know it. The test ends with: *this shows you the wall. The Claim-Safe Citation Audit shows you the door. DM me 'AUDIT.'*

### B. The named-brand teardown (the free proof + warm-DM opener — 1/week)
**What it is:** A custom teardown of a specific, named brand's content — *"here's why AI isn't carrying your proof + the 3 angles I'd ship."* Posted publicly on LinkedIn (constructively) it's the dated, specific proof brands hire on. Sent privately to that brand's founder/lead it's the warm-DM opener: *"pulled apart your last 5 posts and ran your top claims — here's the citation gap + 3 I'd ship. ## 4. THE GEO / AEO / SEO METHOD — 5 PLAIN STEPS

> Stated so a non-technical founder gets it in one read. This is the engine under every pillar.

1. **Listen first (find the questions worth owning).** Pull the real buyer questions and category conversations AI is already trying to answer — including the 25-word longtail questions that have *no good answer anywhere yet.* Those, you own by default.
2. **Grade the proof (two gates, one pass).** Every objective claim gets a receipt: source, evidence grade, the safe-vs-unsafe wording line, and a citation-worthiness read. Claims that can't survive FTC scrutiny can't survive the machine either — so they get fixed or cut before they ever ship.
3. **Build the one asset that wins (information gain over volume).** Concentrate effort on the few source-backed answer pages and the single "5% asset" that will earn the bulk of the citations — each saying something no existing result says, so the machine has a reason to credit you by name instead of summarizing you anonymously.
4. **Make it consistent everywhere AI reads (the retrieval layer).** Same brand story, same proof, same claims across site, LinkedIn, and the third-party sources AI actually cites — so the engines see one coherent, trustworthy entity, not a marketing page they filter out.
5. **Reinforce in the founder's voice + measure across three engines.** 8–12 founder posts/mo carry the proof in a voice the machine can't synthesize for free, and visibility is tracked separately across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity — because they disagree on whose proof counts, and winning one isn't winning.
