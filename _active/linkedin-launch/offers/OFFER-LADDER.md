## Tier 0 — The Teardown (the free Elite Magnet + the keystone)

**The asset that does everything.** A custom teardown of a specific brand's content, built from Brand Radar data + the divergence lens + the brandjack/jacking menu. It is simultaneously:
1. **Public proof** — posted on LinkedIn (constructively), it's the dated teardown the research says brands hire on.
2. **The Elite Magnet** — sent privately to that brand's founder/growth lead as the warm-DM opener ("pulled apart your last 5 posts — here's the angle gap + 3 I'd ship. No pitch.").
3. **The on-ramp** — "want the full version?" → the Paid Audit.

Process + a worked first example: `offers/teardown-system.md`. **Cadence: 1 named teardown/week minimum** (it's the highest-leverage proof + lead act there is).

**Why brands say yes (research-validated):** the override signal ("a published, dated angle library with specific accounts + result deltas → interview"); they buy "thinking that differentiates, not volume"; their language is "what's the angle." The teardown *is* the angle, on their brand, for free.

## Tier 1 — The Angle Audit (paid, $500–1,500)

The teardown's depth, paid and private: a full diagnostic of their creative system — the sameness diagnosis, the compliance-gate read (what's actually substantiable — Farrice's edge), the authenticity-gate read, and **3–5 specific divergence angles** with the body-level consumer truth behind each. No production. Deliverable: a tight doc + a 30–45 min walkthrough. This is the research's "paid case test" (brands already run a $300–500 paid test before hiring) turned into a product. Upsells straight into the Proof Run.

## Tier 2 — The Proof Run (paid sprint, $1,500–3,000)

Done-for-you: **8 brand-voice posts/ad concepts + 1 VSL script in 7 days**, built from the brand's real POV + founder truth + product mechanism (not AI averages). Refund guarantee ("if it doesn't sound like your brand"). The content ships to their feed = live proof. (Re-pointed from the coach-era Proof Run in `proof-run-offer-page.md`.) ~40–50% convert to the Embed (per `offer_market_fit_blueprint.md`).

## Tier 3 — The Embed (service, $4–9K/mo fractional or retainer)

The recurring engagement / fractional creative-strategist seat: ongoing angle development, the content system, the methodology install (clone the brand's POV into a voice-matched engine), monthly strategy. Hybrid win — sold as a fractional **seat** (role track) or a project **retainer** (client track) depending on the lead's spend tier (research: <$300K/mo Meta spend → fractional; $300K–$1M → FTE).

## Positioning spine (so we never force a solution the market rejects)

The offer solves the **validated** pains, in their words: *creative fatigue / "ads die in 5–7 days," "what's the angle," sea of sameness, the compliance gate, the authenticity gate, "same AI models — context wins."* The wedge across all four tiers: **"Everyone has the same AI models. You have someone who's lived the physiology your ad is selling — so the angle is true before it's scaled."** Reassure the human (the growth lead's taste is the moat), indict the machine (averaging is its physics). Never "10x your creative with AI" — that's the over-promise they've already been burned by (`icp-emotional-map.md` §2 Broken Promises).

## Demonstration surfaces (proof, beyond the teardown)
Teardowns (keystone) · before/after angle rewrites · infographics that visualize the sameness/divergence mechanism · short lead-magnet frameworks ("the 5 sameness tells," "the compliance-safe angle test"). Each is content AND proof. Track real outcomes in `proof-tracker.md`; never invent results.
