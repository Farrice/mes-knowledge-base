# The Teardown System — Farrice's Keystone Proof Asset

> The single highest-leverage act in the engine. A named-brand content teardown built from Brand Radar + the divergence lens + the brandjack menu + the body-level consumer truth (Farrice's edge). **Triple-routes:** public LinkedIn proof · the free Elite Magnet (DM'd to the brand) · the on-ramp to the Paid Angle Audit. See `offers/OFFER-LADDER.md`.
> **Why this is the keystone (research):** brands hire on OUTPUT — a published, dated teardown with specific accounts is the strongest "yes signal" for a no-logo outsider (the "override signal"). The teardown is proof you can do the work, on their brand, before they pay.

## Cadence
**≥1 named teardown/week** on a Top-10 (or any in-vertical) brand. It's content, portfolio, and lead-gen in one pass.

## Inputs (no fabrication)
- `daily/brand-radar-YYYY-WW.md` — their real recent posts + formats + engagement.
- `icp-emotional-map.md` §7 — the body-level reason their END consumer actually acts (Farrice's unfair input).
- The divergence lens (a model averages what you feed it) + the brandjack/jacking menu.
- Every named claim traces to their own content (primary). No invented metrics.

## The structure (the teardown template)
1. **Credit first** (reassure the human) — what they're genuinely doing well. Never open with a dunk; the growth lead already feels behind.
2. **The sameness tell** — the one structural pattern making them blend in (activity-not-angle, feature-as-benefit, category-mimicry). Named, specific, kind.
3. **The body-level truth they're missing** — the real reason their consumer acts, at the nervous-system/identity level (Farrice's 18yr edge). This is the line no marketing-first strategist can write.
4. **3 angles I'd ship** — concrete, true-before-scaled, compliance-aware. Each tied to a consumer emotion, not a feature.
5. **The compliance-safe note** — what's substantiable vs a warning-letter risk (the edge that earns trust).
6. **The close** — for the public post: a recognition line. For the DM: "want the full version on your last 30 days? That's the Angle Audit."

## Routing
- **Public post:** lead with the mechanism/principle; reference the brand with respect; the teardown *demonstrates* the thinking. (Keep public versions constructive — these are future clients.)
- **Elite Magnet DM:** the full custom teardown sent to the founder/growth lead. "Pulled apart your last 5 posts. Here's the angle gap + 3 I'd ship. No pitch."
- **On-ramp:** "Want this on your whole funnel / last 30 days?" → Paid Angle Audit ($500–1,500).

## WORKED FIRST TEARDOWN — Gainful (Elite-Magnet draft, DM-ready)

*Source: Gainful IG via Brand Radar 2026-W25 (primary). For the DM to Gainful's growth/brand lead; a public version would generalize the principle.*

**Credit first.** The "New Era of Protein" relaunch after 8 years of listening is a real story, and the NYC truck is a genuine moment — you're putting energy and budget behind the brand. That's not the problem.

**The sameness tell.** Most of the recent feed is *activity, not angle*: the truck, the Prime Day discount, the flavor vote, "your protein shouldn't bore you, ours sets you free." "Sets you free" is a feature (flavor packets) wearing a benefit's clothes. It's the exact register every protein brand is in right now — which is why a relaunch this big can still slide past the scroll. A model trained on "protein brand best practices" would have written most of it, because it *is* the category average.

**The body-level truth you're not using.** Nobody switches protein because of flavor variety. They switch in the 30 seconds after they skipped a workout and felt the small private shame of becoming someone who doesn't follow through. Protein is bought as *identity insurance* — "I'm still the kind of person who recovers." The flavor packet isn't freedom; it's the thing that removes the last excuse on the morning you almost didn't. That's the angle, and it's true before it's scaled — I've watched which cue actually gets someone off the couch a thousand times.

**3 angles I'd ship:**
1. **"The skipped-day protein."** Built for the morning after you fell off, not the highlight-reel morning. The flavor switch as the friction-killer on your worst day, not your best.
2. **"Unflavored is a power move."** Reframe the unflavored base as the operator's choice — you control the ritual, you're not sold a personality. Owns a position no flavored-tub competitor can take.
3. **Founder/lab POV on *why* most protein bores you** — the actual formulation reason, in a real human voice. Borrows the trust format (cited mechanism) the category rewards, with a point of view.

**Compliance-safe note.** All three avoid structure/function landmines — they sell identity and ritual, not a health outcome, so there's no substantiation gap or warning-letter exposure. (That's the gate most "AI variant machines" trip over.)

**Close (DM):** That's a 15-minute read of one week of your feed. Want it on your last 30 days + your top 3 paid angles? That's the Angle Audit.
