Most brands optimize for engagement. The winning brands optimize for being quoted.

## The 5-Step System

**Step 1: Brand Radar**
I pull your last 30 days of posts across all channels.
I log the format, the angle, the engagement, the message.
I identify the patterns you don't see — the things you default to.

**Step 2: The Divergence Lens**
I run your key category questions through ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google.
What brands show up? Why?

Does your brand appear? Are you quoted? Are you misrepresented?
(This is where most brands discover they're invisible upstream.)

**Step 3: Body-Level Consumer Truth**
I've watched what moves people in a real room for 18 years.
The reason someone switches brands is rarely the reason they'll tell you.

A protein buyer doesn't switch for "flavor variety."
They switch in the 30 seconds after they skipped a workout and felt 
the private shame of becoming someone who doesn't follow through.

That's the angle. That's what's quotable. That's what moves.

**Step 4: Three Angles I'd Ship**
Based on steps 1-3, I write three angles you could post tomorrow.

Each one is:
- True before it's scaled (not a marketing fabrication)
- Compliance-aware (no substantiation gaps, no warning letters)
- Tied to a nervous-system reason your buyer acts

One of them always lands.

**Step 5: The Recommendations**
I walk you through:
- Which angle to lead with (and why)
- How to sequence the other two
- What your profile/landing page needs to support this
- How to measure whether it's working

---

The goal is not more content. It's content that doesn't disappear.

Most brands produce 10 posts a week and are shocked when 9 vanish.
They're competing on format, reach, polish — the things a machine makes for free.

The one that lands is the one that names a truth about your buyer 
that only a human standing behind it could see.

That's what I audit. That's what I show you how to ship.

One call. $500. Three angles. Seven days.
