# Intake Questionnaire — Google Form Template (Copy-Paste)

> **For**: Angle Audit Discovery Call  
> **Platform**: Google Forms (free, integrates with Drive/Sheets)  
> **Time to create**: 5 minutes  
> **Submission handling**: Auto-responses + responses go to a Google Sheet for easy tracking

---

## SETUP STEPS (Google Forms)

1. Go to `forms.google.com`
2. Click "+ Create new form"
3. Name: `Angle Audit Discovery — Let's Find Your Winning Angle`
4. Copy the questions below into the form
5. Set form to auto-collect email addresses (Settings → Collect email addresses)
6. Set up form responses → Create a Google Sheet (auto-logs all responses)
7. Share the form link in your Calendly thank-you email or LinkedIn DMs

---

## FORM TEXT (Copy-Paste Into Google Forms)

### **Form Title**
```
Angle Audit Discovery — Let's Find Your Winning Angle
```

### **Form Description**
```
Before our audit call, I want to understand your brand situation 
so I can make the 60 minutes as targeted as possible.

Five questions. Two minutes. No wrong answers.
```

---

### **QUESTION 1: Brand & Positioning**

**Question Type**: Short answer  
**Required**: Yes

**Question:**
```
What does your brand do? (One sentence. Just so I understand the category.)
```

**Answer Help Text** (appears below the field):
```
E.g., "We make plant-based protein for active women" or 
"Executive coaching for solopreneurs" or "Personalized supplement recommendations"
```

---

### **QUESTION 2: The Real Pain**

**Question Type**: Short answer  
**Required**: Yes

**Question:**
```
What's the content problem you're facing right now? 
(Pick the one that costs you money.)
```

**Answer Help Text**:
```
E.g., "We post inconsistently and nothing goes viral" or 
"Engagement is good but we don't get actual leads" or 
"We don't know what angles to even try" or 
"Our AI-generated content isn't performing"
```

---

### **QUESTION 3: The AI Complaint**

**Question Type**: Multiple choice  
**Required**: Yes  
**(Choose one option to submit form)**

**Question:**
```
When you ask ChatGPT or Claude to write about your brand, what happens?
```

**Options:**
```
A) It sounds generic — could be any brand in our category
B) It has the facts right but sounds corporate / robotic
C) It's actually pretty good but doesn't sound like me/our founder
D) It gets facts wrong or misses stuff; I end up rewriting everything
E) I haven't tried it; I don't trust it
F) We don't use AI for content
```

---

### **QUESTION 4: Current Content System**

**Question Type**: Multiple choice  
**Required**: Yes  
**(Choose one option)**

**Question:**
```
How are you currently creating content?
```

**Options:**
```
A) Posting sporadically (0-2x per week)
B) We have an in-house person but they're overwhelmed
C) We hired a ghostwriter or agency but the output isn't working
D) Using a template-based service / batch content tool
E) Using AI + light editing
F) We're not posting; focusing on [other channel]
```

---

### **QUESTION 5: Decision Timeline**

**Question Type**: Multiple choice  
**Required**: Yes  
**(Choose one or more)**

**Question:**
```
What would success look like for you in the next 90 days?
```

**Options** (allow multiple selection):
```
☐ Posting consistently 3-4x per week with clear angles
☐ Generating 2-5 qualified leads per week from organic content
☐ Having a library of posts I can use and adapt
☐ Understanding what angles actually work for my brand
☐ Looking good on LinkedIn / building founder presence
☐ Something else (please describe below)
```

---

### **QUESTION 6: Constraints**

**Question Type**: Short answer  
**Required**: No

**Question:**
```
Any content format or topic we should avoid? 
(Regulatory, brand guidelines, anything I should know.)
```

**Answer Help Text**:
```
E.g., "We're in the supplement space so we can't make health claims" or 
"CEO prefers short form over long-form"
```

---

### **QUESTION 7: The Real Ask**

**Question Type**: Short answer  
**Required**: No

**Question:**
```
What's one question you want answered during our call?
```

**Answer Help Text**:
```
This helps me be more targeted. Could be about positioning, 
angles that worked for competitors, brand voice, anything.
```

### **QUESTION 8: Contact Info** (If Not Using Auto-Collect Email)

**Question Type**: Short answer  
**Required**: Yes

**Question:**


## GOOGLE FORM SETTINGS (After Creating All Questions)

1. **Settings tab → General**
   - ☑ "Show progress bar"
   - ☑ "Shuffle question order" (NO — keep as is)
   - ☐ "Show link to submit another response"

2. **Settings tab → Presentation**
   - Choose a simple, professional theme (blue or grey)
   - Keep the header image clean or skip it

3. **Settings tab → Responses**
   - Click "Select response destination"
   - Choose "Create new spreadsheet"
   - This auto-logs all responses for tracking

4. **Custom confirmation message**
   - After submit, show:
   ```
   Thanks for filling that out.
   
   I've got a clear picture of your situation. 
   
   During our call, I'll:
   - Audit your last 30 days against what AI models are quoting
   - Show you the structural pattern keeping your content invisible
   - Give you three angles I'd ship instead
   
   See you on [date/time]. Zoom link: [your link]
   
   Questions? Reply to this email.
   ```

## TRACKING THE RESPONSES

**After submissions come in:**

1. **Spreadsheet auto-created** — Google Forms linked to a Google Sheet
2. **Column structure**:
   - Timestamp
   - Email
   - Brand/positioning (Q1)
   - Content problem (Q2)
   - AI complaint (Q3)
   - Current system (Q4)
   - Success metric (Q5)
   - Constraints (Q6)
   - Key question (Q7)

3. **Before each call**:
   - Open the Sheet
   - Review their responses
   - Note: AI complaint + current system = diagnostic baseline
   - Note: Success metric = what to focus the audit on
   - Note: Key question = the first thing to address in the call

4. **Pattern tracking** (after 5-10 submissions):
   - Are most people using AI? (Q3)
   - What's the #1 pain point? (Q2)
   - Are they mostly founders or hired pros? (infer from answers)
   - This becomes your positioning + messaging refinement
