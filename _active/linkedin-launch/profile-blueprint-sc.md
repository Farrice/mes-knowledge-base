## Engagement Protocol — First 90 Days

> **Source**: Lara Acosta Patterns 21-22, 2025 LinkedIn algorithm intelligence

### Pre-Post Ritual (Post & Ghost Kill Switch — Pattern 22)

LinkedIn distributes based on network engagement. No engagement = content dies.

- **20 minutes BEFORE your post goes live**: engage in the feed — like, comment on industry peers, reply to yesterday's comments
- **Reply to EVERY comment within the first 30 minutes** (80% of reach is decided in the first hour — Hidden Knowledge #2)
- **If you can't be there to engage, DON'T POST that day** — a wasted post is worse than no post

### The 1+3 Comment Formula (Pattern 21 — credited to Jasmin Alic)

Systematic commenting as a growth engine:

1. Leave 1 substantive comment on a target account's post
2. Leave 3 replies to OTHER people's comments on that same post
3. Result: 4x more profile views daily

**Why**: When you reply to someone's comment, their *entire network* sees you — not just the original poster's audience.

**S&C-specific targets**: Comment on posts by NSCA, Mark Verstegen, Eric Cressey, Mike Boyle, Sue Falsone, Cal Dietz, Dr. Andy Galpin — the coaches you want to be SEEN near. Your comments should deploy expertise, not just agreement.

### Dual-Persona Content Architecture (Lara Acosta Pattern 2)

Every post must serve TWO audiences:

- **ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)**: S&C coaches who will pay you — the buyers
- **IFP (Ideal Friend Profile)**: Other content creators, sports journalists, performance professionals — the amplifiers who SHARE your content

IFP-designed content grows reach 3-5x faster (Hidden Knowledge #1).

**Practical application for S&C niche**:

- ICPs = S&C coaches, sports scientists, performance consultants
- IFPs = fitness industry commentators, LinkedIn growth people, personal trainers who aspire to S&C, sports media
- Posts should be specific enough for ICPs but insightful enough for IFPs to share

### Content Ratio (4-3-2-1 → Updated for 2026)

4 posts/week. The three content TYPES:

- **Educational**: Teaching YOUR frameworks (not generic S&C tips)
- **Storytelling**: Client transformations, field notes, career lessons
- **TAM (Total Addressable Market)**: Trend commentary, industry news, contrarian takes on S&C topics

### Algorithm-Optimized Post Formatting (March 2026)

> **2026 Context**: Organic engagement dropped 25% and follower growth is down 59%
> from 2025 levels. Quality gates are stricter. AI-sounding content is actively
> suppressed. Distribution is now "precision delivery" via connection strength,
> not broadcast. The golden hour still matters but is now more sensitive to
> engagement *type* (thread depth, saves, profile clicks) than volume.

| Signal                          | What to Do                                                                                                                     | Source         |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------- |
| **Dwell time**            | Write longer posts (1,200-1,500 chars) with white space — readers who scroll slowly signal quality                            | 2026 Algorithm |
| **"See More" expansion**  | NEW signal: LinkedIn tracks whether people click to expand your post. Write hooks that make expansion irresistible             | 2026 Algorithm |
| **Saves**                 | Strong value indicator, especially for educational content like Methodology Posts. Design posts worth bookmarking              | 2026 Algorithm |
| **Thread depth**          | Back-and-forth replies now outweigh one-off comments. Ask questions that generate real conversation, not just reactions        | 2026 Algorithm |
| **Native content**        | NEVER put external links in the post body — reach penalty. Links go in comments only                                          | 2026 Algorithm |
| **Hashtags**              | First 3 hashtags provide SEO boost. #StrengthAndConditioning #SCCoach #AuthorityBuilding. Beyond 3 = no value                  | 2026 Algorithm |
| **Expertise recognition** | Post consistently about ONE topic niche. The algorithm recognizes topical authority and rewards it with precision distribution | 2026 Algorithm |

## Revenue Bridge Architecture (Lara Acosta Pattern 23)

LinkedIn is NOT a direct conversion platform. It's top-of-funnel.

```
Content → Profile → Lead Magnet → Email List → Sales
(attract)  (convert)  (capture)     (nurture)    (sell)
```

- **Content's job**: Attract the right S&C professionals. NOT to sell.
- **Profile's job**: Convert visitors to followers. (Mental Folder Test.)
- **Lead magnet's job**: Capture emails. (Scorecard, 5-Post Template Kit)
- **Email's job**: Nurture and sell. **70-80% of revenue comes from email, not LinkedIn.**
- **One lead magnet post per week** + permanent lead magnets in the Featured section
