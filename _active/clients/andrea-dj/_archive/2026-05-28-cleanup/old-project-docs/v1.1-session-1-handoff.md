## The new Founder Story — Long version (About page anchor)

**This is the load-bearing version. Goes on the About page. Read it carefully. Edit anything that doesn't sound like you. We don't ship until you sign off.**

> I grew up in Costa Rica, the older daughter in a Latino family, the shy obedient kid at a strict bilingual private school where the cafeteria served minimal-salt, minimal-sugar plates we were forced to finish. I was the nice, quiet, well-behaved little Andrea. School was a bubble. Home was a different country inside the same country. The places I was supposed to belong didn't quite hold me.
>
> Then I found my music school. That was the first room that fit. I made my first close friends there. I met my first boyfriend there. Orchestra felt like a gathering, not an institution. At sixteen, I auditioned into a national youth orchestra that pulled students from across the whole country — kids from rural towns and kids from the city, students who'd played for a decade and students who'd just started, kids whose families had money and kids whose families didn't. The audition was the gate. The music was the equalizer. We traveled together. We came of age together. The room worked because someone had curated who got into it.
>
> I came to Chicago on a music scholarship expecting the same. What I found instead was orchestra culture characterized by intense competition and hostile relationships — one player with a restraining order against another in the same ensemble. That broke something in me. The room I came here looking for wasn't the room that existed.
>
> So I'm building it. Resonance is a daytime, sober, curated dance room for adults in Chicago who want to meet a real partner. The shy girl who became someone people now call a social butterfly didn't change. She found rooms that let her show up. If you've been out loud asking *"why is it so hard to meet a good person,"* the answer isn't another app. The answer is a room built differently.
>
> Come find your person where the music does the introducing.
