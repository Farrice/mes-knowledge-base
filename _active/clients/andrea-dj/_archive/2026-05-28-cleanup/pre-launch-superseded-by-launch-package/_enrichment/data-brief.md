## How to use this brief

- **VERIFIED** claims can be cited externally with the linked source.
- **LIKELY** claims can be cited internally to inform decisions; soften in external copy ("our read is...", "we expect...").
- **UNCONFIRMED** claims are working hypotheses. Do not put them in a venue pitch deck or press reply without secondary verification.
- Each finding ends with **Why this matters for Resonance** — operator-grade interpretation, one to two sentences.

# Section A — Dating App Fatigue (2025–2026)

The macro signal: dating apps are losing the demographic Resonance is built for. The data is now strong enough that "the apps have stopped working" is a citable claim, not a vibe.

## A.1 — Match Group Q1 2026: total payers down 5% year-over-year

Match Group's Q1 2026 earnings report disclosed total payers dropped to 13.5 million, a 5% year-over-year decline. Tinder revenue grew only 2% on the back of price increases, masking a paying-user decline. The deck shows a company that has stopped growing the population using its product and is now extracting more revenue per remaining user.

- **Source**: [Match Group Q1 2026 earnings release on PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/match-group-announces-first-quarter-results-302763209.html) — primary financial disclosure.
- **Confidence**: VERIFIED.
- **Why this matters for Resonance**: The "apps have failed" line is now a financial disclosure, not a feeling. Andrea can say "Tinder lost 5% of its paying users in one quarter" and be citing the company's own earnings deck. This is the single most defensible macro frame.

## A.2 — Match Group Q4/FY 2025: Hinge revenue +25%, Tinder payers –5%

The FY 2025 release showed Hinge revenue grew 25% year-over-year while Tinder paying users dropped 5%. Hinge has become the only profitable growth lever inside the Match portfolio. The category isn't dying uniformly — the "intent" app is growing while the "volume" app is shrinking.

- **Source**: [Match Group Q4/FY 2025 earnings release (mtch.com IR)](https://ir.mtch.com/investor-relations/news-events/news-events/news-details/2026/Match-Group-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-Results/) — primary disclosure.
- **Confidence**: VERIFIED.
- **Why this matters for Resonance**: Confirms Resonance is not positioning against "dating" as a category — it's positioning against the swipe mechanic specifically. Hinge users are the right ICP overlap (intent + sober + tired of apps). The brand line should not be "anti-dating-app" — it should be "anti-the-format-that-makes-you-perform-yourself-into-a-mood."

## A.3 — Pew Research: 30% of US adults have used a dating app — unchanged since 2019

Pew Research Center's most recent dedicated online-dating study, [The Who, Where and Why of Online Dating](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2023/02/02/the-who-where-and-why-of-online-dating-in-the-u-s/) (Feb 2 2023), reported that 30% of US adults had ever used a dating app — flat versus 2019. - **Source**: [Pew Research Center, Feb 2 2023](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2023/02/02/the-who-where-and-why-of-online-dating-in-the-u-s/).

- **Source**: [Pew Research Center, Feb 2 2023](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2023/02/02/the-who-where-and-why-of-online-dating-in-the-u-s/).
