## Three Trends Converging in Your Favor

### Trend 1: Dating Apps Are Collapsing

People are done swiping. The numbers are dramatic:

| App | Users Lost (2023-2024) |
|-----|----------------------|
| Tinder | -594,000 |
| Bumble | -368,000 |
| Hinge | -131,000 |
| Match Group (overall) | -3% paying users |

- **78%** of Americans report dating app fatigue (sometimes/often/always)
- **79%** of Gen Z prefer in-person interactions over dating apps
- **A third** of young adults say dating apps make finding long-term partners *harder*
- "Deep dating" has been declared the defining relationship trend of 2026

**What this means for you:** There's a massive, growing audience of people who want to meet someone real — and the apps aren't working. They need a better option.

### Trend 2: In-Person Singles Events Are Booming

- **+49% year-over-year** growth in singles event attendance (Eventbrite)
- **+42%** attendance growth the year before that
- **1.5 million+** users search for singles events on Eventbrite annually

### Trend 3: Conscious/Sober Nightlife Is Mainstream

- No/low-alcohol events grew **73% globally** (Eventbrite, 2023)
- "Soft socializing" events are exploding: flower-arranging +282%, puzzles +151%, music bingo +149%
- 58% of younger people want socializing without it being the sole focus
- Sobriety is now a status symbol among Gen Z — not a stigma

## Who's Doing Something Similar (And Where You're Different)

### The Closest Models — Study These

**Daybreaker** — The Gold Standard
- Early morning sober dance parties (6-9 AM yoga + 2 hrs dancing)
- Founded 2013 by Radha Agrawal after personal heartbreak
- 500K+ community members, 30+ cities, 7-figure revenue
- Zero paid marketing — pure word-of-mouth
- Started with $2,500 and friends
- **How you're different:** Daybreaker is community/wellness-focused, NOT matchmaking or couples-oriented. You take the community-through-movement model and add the intentional romantic connection layer.

**Single and the City** (NYC & Austin)
- Silent discos + speed dating + social mixers since 2008
- 3 DJs spinning 3 genres on wireless headphones simultaneously
- 40% of attendees come solo
- **How you're different:** They're an event company, not a personal brand. No spiritual grounding. Transactional feel vs. community feel.

**Ecstatic Dance Communities** (80+ locations globally)
- Freeform sober dance emphasizing self-expression, healing, connection
- One person documented replacing dating apps with ecstatic dance — and it worked
- **How you're different:** Not intentionally matchmaking. No business model. No "couples created here" positioning.

**Salsa with Silvia** (National)
- Speed dating combined with salsa dancing
- 45-min dance class + rotating partner format
- **How you're different:** Dance instruction focus, not DJ/party vibe. No spiritual dimension.

## The Founder Pattern

Every successful founder in this space shares these traits:

1. **Personal transformation story** as the origin (Radha's heartbreak, Jasper Vallance's sober dance epiphany)
2. **Mission over profit** (community healing comes first)
3. **Personal brand = business brand** (the founder IS the product)
4. **Zero paid marketing early** — pure word-of-mouth + social proof
5. **Vulnerability as the offering** — people come because the host is real
6. **Started small** — under $2,500, friends and friends-of-friends

You already have #1 (your spiritual journey), #2 (your values), and #5 (your authenticity). The rest is execution.
