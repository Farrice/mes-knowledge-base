# 04 — Instagram Launch Strategy
## Your Complete Social Media Playbook

---

**What this is:** A step-by-step Instagram launch plan — from setting up the account to building 500 followers before Event #1 to turning one event into a month of content.

**What to do with it:** Follow the weekly calendar. Start 4 weeks before your first event.

---

## Account Setup (Do This Today)

**Handle options** (check availability):
- @[brandname]events (e.g., @pulseevents)
- @[brandname]nights
- @[brandname]_official

**Bio (160 characters max):**
```
[Brand Name] | Curated singles connection events
Movement. Music. Real connection.
Next event: [Date] | Book below
```

**Profile photo:** You, mid-performance or a warm headshot. Not overly produced — approachable and confident.

**Link in bio** (use Linktree or similar):
- Event registration link (primary)
- "Coming soon" waitlist signup
- Your curated Spotify playlist
- Email newsletter signup

**Highlights to create:**
1. "About" — who you are, your story
2. "Events" — event recaps
3. "Community" — testimonials, attendee stories
4. "Music" — playlists, mixes

## Pre-Launch Content Calendar (Weeks 1-4 Before Event #1)

### Week 1: Plant the Seeds

Post 2x on feed + 3-4 stories daily.

**Post 1 — "Why I'm Doing This" (Carousel, 4-5 slides)**
- Slide 1: Your face with text overlay "Why I became a matchmaker DJ"
- Slides 2-4: Your realization that people need real connection, how dance heals, your spiritual philosophy
- Slide 5: "Something is coming. Are you ready?"
- Caption example:
```
I've been DJing for [X years], but I realized something missing:
We gather to party. But do we gather to *connect*?

Every song I play, every rhythm I choose — it's intentional.
I'm not just dropping beats. I'm creating the container for
something real to happen.

First event coming soon. Link in bio for early access.

#MatchmakerDJ #DanceConnection #IntentionalCommunity
```

**Post 2 — Music Curation Reel (30 sec)**
- You at your decks selecting tracks, adjusting EQ
- Text overlays: "Selecting the RIGHT songs for REAL connection"
- Caption: "Every song serves the night. No filler, only intentional curation."

### Week 2: Build the Philosophy

**Post 3 — Movement & Embodiment Photo**
- You dancing or in motion
- Caption:
```
The most underrated love language: being seen while you move.

In our events, no one's hidden in the corner. We dance like
we're meant to be found.

What would it feel like to move in a room where everyone's
actually looking? Where eye contact means something?

Coming soon.
```

**Post 4 — "What Makes Good Connection?" Carousel**
- Slide 1: "What makes a good connection?"
- Slides 2-5: Short answers — "Shared energy." / "When someone gets you without explanation." / "Dancing together." / "Vulnerability in a safe space."

### Week 3: THE ANNOUNCEMENT

**Post 5 — Event Launch (Carousel + Reel)**

Post on a Thursday evening. 4-5 slides:
- Slide 1: Eye-catching graphic with event name + date
- Slide 2: "What is this?"
- Slide 3: "Who it's for"
- Slide 4: Event details (time, location, price)
- Slide 5: "Only 50 spots. Link in bio."

Caption:
```
Introducing: [BRAND NAME]

A curated singles connection event where the music, the space,
and the intention are designed for one thing: real human connection.

THIS ISN'T:
- A hookup app IRL
- A high-pressure dating mixer
- A regular DJ set

THIS IS:
- Intentional curation (music, space, people)
- Movement & embodiment (we dance, we feel, we connect)
- Spiritual foundation (every element serves connection)

[DATE] | [TIME] | [VENUE]
[TICKET PRICE] (early bird pricing)
Limited to 50 people.

LINK IN BIO TO RESERVE

#[BrandName] #SinglesEvent #RealConnection
```

**Daily countdown stickers in stories.** "14 days..." "Only 20 spots left..." "Who's coming?" (poll)

**Post 6 — "72 Hours Left" (3 days before)**

```
If you're on the fence — jump. This is the kind of night
you'll think about for months.
```

**Reel 2: "The Solution" (15-20 sec)**

## Event Night — What to Capture (Shot List)

**Before (setup/arrival):**
- Wide shot of venue with lighting
- Your DJ booth setup
- People arriving, greeting each other
- The entrance and ambiance

**During:**
- Wide shots: full dance floor energy
- Medium shots: pairs connecting, dancing
- Close-ups: genuine joy, eye contact, laughter
- 15-30 sec video clips of key moments
- You mid-set, focused

**After:**
- People outside talking, exchanging numbers
- Group photo (with consent)
- You after the event — sweaty, happy, proud

Get verbal consent before photographing. Focus on genuine moments, not posed shots. Capture at least 15-20 short videos.

## Building Your First 500 Followers

**Week 1 (50-100 followers):**
- Follow 200 related accounts (singles events, DJs, wellness, local)
- Comment genuinely on 5-10 posts daily from related accounts
- Tell friends and family to follow + share

**Week 2-3 (100-300 followers):**
- Post the announcement — ask friends with 1K+ followers to share to their stories
- Engage with every comment within 2 hours
- Use hashtag strategy (below)

**Week 3-4 (300-500 followers):**
- Partner with 1-2 local wellness/mindfulness micro-influencers (500-2K followers) — they share event to stories, you feature their story
- Cross-post to TikTok (same content)
- Daily story engagement (polls, questions, countdowns)

## Your Voice Guidelines

- **Warm, confident, vulnerable** — not corporate
- **Spiritual but not preachy** — lead with feeling, not terminology
- **Conversational** — use "I," "you," "we"
- **Always end with a CTA** — question, link in bio, or invitation
- **Real over polished** — raw authenticity wins every time
