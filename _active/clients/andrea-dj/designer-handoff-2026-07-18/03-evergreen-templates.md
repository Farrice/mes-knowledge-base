# Evergreen Canva Templates

*Build these five once, save each as a Canva **Brand Template**, never start from a blank canvas again. After this, every future post is: open template → swap photo → swap copy → ship (4–8 min).*

*All specs are Variant A (the front-runner). If Andrea picks B or C, swap the palette + display font per the Designer Kit — the layouts are identical.*

---

## Setup before you build (≈20 min, one time)
1. Add the 10 brand colors (Designer Kit §2) to **Brand Hub → Brand Kit → Colors**.
2. Add fonts: IBM Plex Serif (display), Inter (body), Caveat (signature). Pin to top.
3. Confirm photo discipline: filter None, sliders 0 (Designer Kit §4).

---

## Template 1 — IG Feed Post  ·  1080 × 1350 (4:5)
- Wordmark **Resonance** top-left, serif 24pt — Cream `#FBF7F0` on photo, or Midnight `#0F1A2E` on cream.
- Photo region: top ~65% (1080×880), full bleed.
- Date/location stamp under photo: Inter SemiBold 12pt, all-caps, Slate `#7A6F62`, letter-spacing 0.12em → `JULY 18, 2026 · CHICAGO`.
- Headline: serif 64pt, Midnight `#0F1A2E`, max 2 lines / ≤8 words.
- Body line: Inter 18pt, Ink `#3A332B`, 1 line / ≤30 words.
- CTA: Inter 14pt, Terracotta `#B8492E` → `Application in bio · @resonance.chicago`.
- **Save as:** "IG Feed — Event Announcement"

## Template 2 — IG Story  ·  1080 × 1920 (9:16)
- Photo: full bleed.
- Text overlay (when used): serif 48pt headline + Inter 18pt sub, on Cream or Midnight depending on photo. Max ~12 words.
- One sticker per frame (poll / question / link), lower third, off-center.
- Wordmark optional, top-left, serif 18pt.
- **Save as:** "IG Story — Single Frame"

## Template 3 — Flyer  ·  two versions
- **Digital:** 1080 × 1350 (matches IG feed for cross-posting).
- **Print:** 8.5 × 11 in, 300dpi, **CMYK**, uncoated stock.
- Layout: photo upper 65–70%; below it date/venue stamp → headline → body line → application link. Wordmark top-left. (Full mockup in `02-this-week-flyer-and-ig.md` §A.)
- **Save as:** "Flyer — Event (Digital)" + "Flyer — Event (Print)"

## Template 4 — Event Ticket  ·  1200 × 1800 (4×6 postcard)
- **Type-led — NO photograph.** The ticket is restrained.
- Front: wordmark + a single manifest line. Back: event date, venue, doors, in label-caps; one sentence "You were in the room on [date]." QR for entry small, bottom corner.
- **Save as:** "Event Ticket — Digital"

## Template 5 — Email Header Banner  ·  600 × 200
- Cream-50 `#FBF7F0` background.
- Wordmark **Resonance** left, serif 32pt, Midnight `#0F1A2E`.
- Optional 1px hairline `#E1D6C2` below. That's it — it's letterhead, not a graphic.
- **Save as:** "Email Header — Newsletter"

---

## What Canva is NOT for
The press one-sheeter, the printed cardstock ticket (print partner), and the website are **not** Canva jobs — they use real design/print tools. Canva owns the **daily social + email surfaces**: the five above. Keep it to these and the brand stays disciplined.
