## Two heads-ups before you post

- **Application link:** the Instagram CTA says "Application in bio." That bio link needs the real form URL — Andrea/Farrice will confirm it. Don't make one up.
- **The flyer needs one real daytime photo** (Andrea at the decks, or a real afternoon room shot). **No AI faces, no stock dancers, no nighttime photos** — that's the one hard rule. If there's no photo yet, the Instagram posts can run first and the flyer follows once the photo exists.
