## Step 2 — Brand essentials  *(designer reference)*

### Palette — Variant A *(default; swap if Andrea picks B/C)*

| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Terracotta | `#B8492E` | The one accent — CTA, link, headline accent (once per layout) |
| Midnight | `#0F1A2E` | Headlines + body type |
| Cream (bg) | `#F5EFE3` | Page background |
| Cream (surface) | `#FBF7F0` | Cards |
| Ink | `#1A1814` | Body text (warm near-black, never `#000`) |
| Slate | `#7A6F62` | Dates, captions, metadata |
| Gold | `#8C6526` | Rare — couple quotes only |
| Border | `#E1D6C2` | Hairline dividers |

> **B:** Terracotta `#A8412A` · **Moss `#1F2E1E`** (replaces midnight) · Ochre `#B8843C` · Cream `#F2E9D4`. Squared 0px corners. **C:** **Ink `#16140F`** primary · Sage `#4B5849` · Gold `#9C7833` · Terracotta `#A14A30` (accent only). Lighter, smaller type. 4px corners.

**Banned everywhere:** hot pink, club neon, pure white `#FFFFFF` (use cream), pure black `#000000` (use ink), purple/magenta/electric blue, trending gradients, Instagram filter tones, wellness pastels.

### Fonts

| Role | Use (free, Google Fonts) |
|---|---|
| Display / headlines | **IBM Plex Serif** (A) · Fraunces (B) · Cormorant Garamond (C) |
| Body / labels | **Inter** — every variant |
| Signature only | Caveat (A) / Homemade Apple (B,C) — Andrea's signature, nothing else |

Serif headlines, Inter body. **Never two serifs. Never weight above 500.** Size does the work, not bold.

### Photos — do / kill

**DO:** real daytime light · adults ~30–38, mixed race/body · candid moments (mid-laugh, leaning in, hand on a record) · leave it alone (filter None, sliders 0).
**KILL:** anything 11pm/club/bar · flash, neon, strobe, candlelight, B&W · drinks in frame · phones in hands · AI faces · stock dancers · Canva auto-filters/duotone/drop-shadows · "smile at the camera" groups.

*No real daytime photo yet? Use a tonal cream/terracotta block as placeholder. A placeholder is honest; a wrong photo is brand damage.*

### Quick do / don't

**DO:** photo owns 65–75% of the surface · one accent color · name city + date (*"Chicago. July 18, 2026."*) · CTA points to the **application**, not tickets · white space, never drop shadows.
**DON'T:** QR as a focal point · hashtags on the flyer · sponsor logos · "limited spots!" urgency · more than 2 em-dashes · emoji-CTAs ("DM us YES 🌹", "tag a friend").
