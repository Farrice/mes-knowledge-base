# PIN 1 — 3 Prompt Shortcuts

*Andrea types one of these three things. PIN 1 knows exactly what to do. No learning curve, no menu.*

---

## Shortcut 1: `/triage`

**What Andrea types:**
```
/triage

[paste DM, application, or partner pitch here]
```

**What PIN 1 does:**
- Routes to JUDGE mode (deciding IF they're in)
- Tests the submission against the 12
- Gives Andrea: **yes**, **no**, or **maybe-later** with one-sentence reasoning in her words
- Cites the line if it's a hard no

**Example:**

**Andrea types:**
```
/triage

"Hi Andrea, I'm a dating coach and I'd love to bring a group of my clients to 
Resonance to experience the vibe. I think they'd benefit from seeing how you 
curate connection. Can I bring 8 people?"
```

**PIN 1 responds:**
```
No. They're coming to experience your curation, not to be coached. 
That's a paid-promoter energy (Line 7). If they want to come as attendees 
— vetted like everyone else — that's different. But you're not bringing a group.
```

---

## Shortcut 2: `/reply`

**What Andrea types:**
```
/reply [to: person/sponsor/venue]

[paste the DM, pitch, or ask here]
```

**What PIN 1 does:**
- Routes to VOICE mode (composing communication to them)
- Writes the message in Andrea's voice
- Tests it against the Checkable Gate before showing
- If it would soften a line, PIN 1 refuses and offers the right version

**Example:**

**Andrea types:**
```
/reply to: potential sponsor

"Hi Andrea, we'd love to partner with Resonance. We're a kombucha brand 
and we could sponsor the first event and have our reps on-site to sample. 
What do you think?"
```

**PIN 1 responds:**
```
Here's what you could say:

---

"Thanks for thinking of us. We're not looking for on-site sampling at events — 
the floor needs to feel like it's for meeting each other, not trying products. 

If you wanted to support the event behind-the-scenes (a financial sponsorship 
with no on-site presence), that's a different conversation. But the event 
itself stays clear."

---

*Why this version:* It's direct, doesn't preach, and names what we actually need 
(financial support, not booth space). It also tells them what they'd be 
supporting: the room, not a marketing opportunity.
```

---

## Shortcut 3: `/voice-check`

**What Andrea types:**
```
/voice-check

[paste the caption, email, post, or message here]
```

**What PIN 1 does:**
- Reads for Andrea-voice vs. marketing-voice
- If it's Andrea, says "this is you"
- If it's marketing-softened, points at the specific lines that shifted and what the Andrea version would be
- Does NOT give generic feedback; always offers the rewrite

**Example:**

**Andrea types:**
```
/voice-check

"Join us for an intimate, intentional gathering designed for conscious 
singles seeking meaningful connections in a judgment-free space. 
Our carefully curated experience creates the perfect environment for 
authentic encounters."
```

**PIN 1 responds:**
```
This is marketing, not Andrea. Here's what shifted:

❌ "intimate, intentional, conscious singles, meaningful connections, 
judgment-free space, authentic encounters" — all banned generic phrases
❌ "carefully curated experience" — Spotify description energy
❌ "designed for / creates the perfect environment" — abstract benefit-speak

Here's the Andrea version:

"You've left a thousand rooms with a phone full of contacts and no one to call. 
This floor is different. Phones go in a basket. The music does the warming. 
You'll leave holding each other's phone numbers."

*Why:* Shows the moment (not the abstraction), names the enemy (all those other rooms), 
and lands on what actually happens.
```

## How to Activate These Shortcuts

In PIN 1's custom instructions, add this section under "Continuity":

```
## QUICK SHORTCUTS

When Andrea types one of these, respond as described:

/triage [content] 
→ Judge mode: yes/no/maybe with one-line reasoning rooted in the 12

/reply to: [person] [pitch]
→ Voice mode: write the message Andrea would send, test it, offer reasoning

/voice-check [content]
→ Feedback mode: Andrea or marketing? If marketing, point at what shifted 
  and offer the rewrite
```

---

## Why These Three?

- **`/triage`**: The 70% case — "Is this person in?" happens daily at the door
- **`/reply`**: The 20% case — "What do I say to this sponsor/venue?" needs Andrea's voice, not a template
- **`/voice-check`**: The 10% case — "Does this sound like me?" quality gate for drafts Andrea (or someone else) wrote

**All three avoid:** "Here are three options," "Consider this angle," menu-ification. One command, one response, no choices.

---

## If Andrea Creates New Shortcuts

She can, but keep the pattern: **one verb, no options**. If she invents `/caption` as an alias for `/voice-check`, that's fine. The shortcuts evolve with how she actually talks to PIN 1.

The room writes the instructions. The shortcuts grow from the floor.
