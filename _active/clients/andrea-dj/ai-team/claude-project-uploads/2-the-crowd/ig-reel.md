## Section 1 — The Spine Reminder (paste-in)

> *Resonance is heart encounters, not head encounters — a daytime, sober dance party in Chicago for people who want to meet a partner. The mechanic is body-first: the music does the emotional labor so the people don't have to. The metric is couples, not followers.*

Verbatim at the top of every reel session. No paraphrase. No shortening.

## Section 2 — What This Brief Is For

This brief produces Instagram Reels (the 9:16 vertical-video unit — IG's highest-reach surface in 2026 and the channel where Resonance reaches **pre-contemplation** ICP who would never click a feed post). Its job is **demonstration through motion**: show the room (or the body-first mechanism in any room Andrea has already DJ'd) so a viewer who hasn't named the problem yet can *feel* the alternative existing. Reels sit at the **top of funnel** — they pull cold viewers into contemplation. Some reels (event-cycle, mechanic-led) pull contemplation into preparation. The one thing every reel must do: **grip in the first 1.5 seconds with a visual or text hook that makes Marcus stop scrolling, and end with one clean cue (link in bio / applications open / no CTA at all when the reel's job was contemplation).**

## Section 4 — Voice Rules (compressed)

Inherit voice from `00-foundation/03-voice-document.md` Section 6 — the AI Handoff Block — verbatim. Compressed:

> Warm, direct, confident, never preachy, never corporate. Show > tell at sentence level. Heart-encounter language as headline, body-first only as explanation. Em-dashes ≤ 2 per piece. No "Here's what / why / how" openings. No "It's not X. It's Y." reveal patterns. Name the enemy by name when it clarifies. Never preach sobriety. "Community" only when meant. Voice test: would Andrea say this to a friend over coffee?

Patterns this asset leans on most: **show-first opener** (the visual *is* the show), **mechanic-as-sentence** (P6, narrated over visual), **out-loud-asking** (P4, as on-screen text), **crystallized-phrase stack** (P3, as text-overlay creed).

The reel-specific voice rule: **the visual carries the show; the voice carries the tell.** Voiceover is rare and specific — Andrea's actual voice, never generic narration. On-screen text is more common than voiceover. Music is original (Andrea's set, room ambient) or genre-fit, never "trending Reels audio" that contradicts the spine.

## Section 5 — Format Spec

Hard, not soft.

- **Dimensions**: 1080×1920 (9:16 vertical). Full-bleed.
- **Length**:
  - **7-15 sec** — high-leverage sweet spot for reach and replay; mechanic-as-sentence reels, question-hook reels, B-roll loops.
  - **30-60 sec** — narrative reels (story of a song, exit-interview voiceover, Andrea-on-camera explaining why she's building Resonance).
  - **Never longer than 60 sec** for IG Reels — if it needs more, it's a feed video or a Substack piece.
- **Hook in first 1.5 seconds**: visual or text. The first frame must earn the second frame. If the first 1.5 seconds were on a billboard, would Marcus look up? If no, recut.
- **Captions**: ON-screen captions are **mandatory**. 80%+ of IG users watch with sound off. Captions go in the safe zone (avoid top 220px and bottom 250px to clear UI).
- **Audio sources** (in order of preference):
  1. **Andrea's own DJ sets** (most preferred — her actual mixes, beat-matched).
  2. **Exit-interview audio** (with explicit permission, used as voiceover under B-roll).
  3. **Ambient room sound** (the venue, real footsteps, real conversation murmur — used sparingly, anchors the reel in reality).
  4. **Andrea's voice on camera or voiceover** (her actual voice, conversational, never narrator-mode).
  5. **Genre-fit audio** (a track from the room's musical lineage — soul, deep house, classic disco, neo-soul). Only when the audio fits the spine.
  6. **Trending Reels audio** — almost never. Use only if a trending track is *already* one Andrea would play in the room. Riding a viral sound that doesn't fit the spine fails the voice test instantly.
- **CTA**: spoken or captioned in the **last 2 seconds**. Options:
  1. *"First event July 18, 2026 — link in bio."* (most-used)
  2. Hell-yes filter close (*"If you're a hell-yes, the application's in bio. If not, the next reel might be the one."*)
  3. **No CTA** — for contemplation-seeding reels. Let the reel do its job; don't force conversion on a cold viewer.
- **End frame**: a cream-or-terracotta card with `Resonance · Chicago · July 18, 2026` in vinyl-serif. Static, 1 second hold. Or no end-card if the reel ends on a real moment (the door closing, the song landing).
- **Banned**: trending dance-trend imitations that abandon the spine; flashy text animations (kinetic-typography, glitch effects, bouncing emoji); voice-cloned narration; AI-generated B-roll for Phase 1.

## Section 6 — Hook & Structure Patterns

Four named patterns. Each with a GOOD storyboard (shot-by-shot, with on-screen text, sound, and timing) and a BAD example plus a one-line diagnosis.

### Pattern A — Visual hook + voiceover

A real moment from a Resonance set (or Andrea's pre-Resonance DJ work as B-roll until Event #1 produces footage), under Andrea's voiceover narrating one manifesto line. The visual is 70% of the meaning; the voiceover is 30%. The cut from B-roll into the closing brand frame is one beat — never a fade.

**GOOD storyboard (target = Marcus, pre-contemplation, 14 sec total):**

| Shot | Length | Visual | On-screen text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–1.5s | Close-up: Andrea's hands on a mixer in real 2pm window light. Cream wall blur behind. | (none — visual hook) | The first beat of a slow-build track, just kicking in. |
| 2 | 1.5–4.0s | Wide: a wooden floor, sunlight in a slant across it, two pairs of feet (one in Stan Smiths, one in worn brown leather boots) about three feet apart. Both still. | "Movement before words." | Track building. Andrea VO begins, low: *"You meet someone here..."* |
| 3 | 4.0–7.0s | Medium: a back of a head, a person's shoulders dropping a half-inch as the song shifts. Light catches the side of their cheek. | "The way they were standing when the song shifted." | Andrea VO continues: *"...because you noticed each other on the dance floor. The way they moved. The way they listened."* |
| 4 | 7.0–10.0s | Wide: the same two pairs of feet from shot 2, now closer, three feet has become one. Still no faces. | "The body knows first." | Track at full. VO drops out. |
| 5 | 10.0–12.5s | Close: the door of the venue, soft-closing as someone walks in (back of a coat, no face). | "Daytime. Sober. Curated. Chicago." | Track sustains. |
| 6 | 12.5–14.0s | End frame: cream card with vinyl-serif text. | `Resonance · First event July 18, 2026 · Link in bio.` | Track tails out clean. |

*Why this works for Marcus*: zero faces (no demographic implication), zero performative dance, zero hookup-coded imagery. Two pairs of feet in real daylight is the body-first mechanism made visible without putting any specific identity on display. He projects himself in.

**BAD:**

> A 30-second reel using a viral TikTok dance trend. Three young women in workout clothes lip-syncing the trending audio in a fluorescent-lit dance studio at night, with overlay text reading *"POV: You finally found the conscious sober singles event of your dreams ✨💕 #manifesting #soberdating #chicagosingles"*. Quick zooms, cut to disco-ball B-roll, end on a "DM us 'YES'" call-to-action.

*Diagnosis*: Trending-dance-trend imitation that abandons the spine entirely (fluorescent night lighting fails the *"could this have been shot at 11pm"* test in shot one); "POV," "manifesting," "conscious sober," and "DM us 'YES'" stack four banned phrases in 30 seconds. Marcus, Nora, and Imani all close the tab simultaneously.

### Pattern B — Question hook (text-overlay → cuts as implied answer)

Open with the ICP's actual sentence as on-screen text, held for 2 seconds. Then cut to B-roll of the room as the implied answer. The question is asked; the room responds without language.

**GOOD storyboard (target = Nora, contemplation, 11 sec total):**

| Shot | Length | Visual | On-screen text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–2.0s | Static black slate, cream type center-screen. | "Why is it so hard to meet a good, nice person?" | One beat of silence, then a single piano note (no music yet). |
| 2 | 2.0–4.0s | Wide: a daytime room — wooden floor, real window light, twenty people standing at varying distances, none on phones, all listening. | "It's a real question. We've heard it five times this month." | Slow track fades up. |
| 3 | 4.0–6.5s | Medium: a hand reaching for a cup at the water station. Another hand, three feet away, doing the same. They don't quite meet. | (none) | Track building. |
| 4 | 6.5–9.0s | Close: Andrea at her kit, eyes closed for one beat, mixing into the next song. | "We hold our events during the day. Sober. The music is mine." | Mix transition lands. |
| 5 | 9.0–11.0s | End frame: cream card. | `Resonance · Chicago · First event July 18, 2026 · Link in bio.` | Track tails. |

*Why this works for Nora*: opens with her exact out-loud question (which she has said this year), pivots to the room without a single marketing word. The "we've heard it five times this month" line is recognition + receipts, not promise.

**BAD:**

> A reel opening with text *"Are YOU tired of dating apps??? 😩💔"* over a TikTok-sad-girl audio. Cut to Andrea on camera saying *"Well, I have GOOD news for you..."* with a smile and finger-point. Cut to a graphic announcing *"INTRODUCING: RESONANCE 🌟 The Conscious Singles Event of Your Dreams ✨"* with kinetic typography and a confetti burst.

*Diagnosis*: Marketing-question opener (Nora doesn't say *"Are YOU tired of dating apps???"* out loud), TikTok-influencer "I have good news" beat, then four banned phrases in the title card. The kinetic-typography + confetti pushes it from off-voice into actively repellent.

### Pattern C — Mechanic-as-sentence reel

Andrea on camera or voice-over, naming each of the four mechanics in a full sentence with stakes, with B-roll cuts illustrating each. Crystallized-phrase pattern executed as motion. Length sweet spot: 12-18 seconds.

**GOOD storyboard (target = umbrella, contemplation/preparation, 16 sec total):**

| Shot | Length | Visual | On-screen text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–2.5s | Close: Andrea's hand pulling back the curtain on a window at 2pm. Real daylight floods in. | "Daytime." | Andrea VO: *"The doors open at 2pm in real daylight."* |
| 2 | 2.5–5.5s | Wide: a long table where a bar would be, holding water carafes and a coffee setup. No bottles. Cream cloth. | "Sober." | Andrea VO: *"There's no bar. The music does the warming."* |
| 3 | 5.5–9.0s | Close: Andrea's hands on a record, sliding it from a sleeve. The label is visible (a real soul reissue — pick something specific). | "Curated music." | Andrea VO: *"I curate the music myself, beat to beat. The rule is whether the song bends the room toward someone you wouldn't have noticed."* |
| 4 | 9.0–12.5s | Medium: a single sheet of paper on a small table, a pen beside it, the application form folded in half. A thumb in frame. | "Curated crowd." | Andrea VO: *"Every person on the floor said yes to the same agreement before they got the address."* |
| 5 | 12.5–14.5s | Wide: empty room, sunlight on wood floor, no people. The room itself, breathing. | "Resonance." | Track sustains, no VO. |
| 6 | 14.5–16.0s | End frame: cream card. | `First event July 18, 2026 · Chicago · Link in bio.` | Track tails. |

*Why this works for all three*: each mechanic gets a sentence that earns it (not a feature bullet). Imani sees "the music does the warming" as the labor-doer-named-correctly. Marcus sees the application as the curation protecting him. Nora sees the daylight and the soul record and exhales.

**BAD:**

> A reel opening with text overlay reading *"THE 4 PILLARS OF RESONANCE ✨"* with a fast-cut sequence: emoji-laden bullet points appearing one after another — *"☀️ DAYTIME"* / *"🚫 ALCOHOL-FREE"* / *"🎵 CURATED MUSIC"* / *"💕 INTENTIONAL CROWD"* — over generic Pexels stock footage of women dancing in slow motion at night with a disco ball.

*Diagnosis*: Feature-bullet vertical (not mechanic-as-sentence), emoji-as-icon stack, "THE 4 PILLARS" headline that no human says, stock-footage night dancing footage that fails the *"could this have been shot at 11pm"* test, and "INTENTIONAL CROWD" as a wellness-decorator phrase. Six failures in twelve seconds.

### Pattern D — Quote-led reel (exit-interview voiceover under B-roll)

A consented attendee voice-recording (one sentence, ~6-8 seconds of speech) plays as voiceover under B-roll of the room. Andrea's voice doesn't appear. The attendee's voice is the proof; the B-roll is the room they're describing.

**GOOD storyboard (target = umbrella, contemplation, 13 sec total):**

| Shot | Length | Visual | On-screen text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–1.5s | Close: a hand placing a phone face-down in a small wooden bowl by the door. | "Phones go in the bowl at the door." | Soft ambient room sound. |
| 2 | 1.5–8.0s | Wide: the back of a person dancing alone in afternoon light, eyes closed, no performance. Slow pan right reveals two more people doing the same, distributed across a wood floor. | (caption tracks the VO verbatim) | **Exit-interview VO (consented):** *"I went home and texted my sister. I told her I'd just been in a room where I didn't have to be charming, and someone still saw me."* |
| 3 | 8.0–11.0s | Close: the same phone-in-bowl shot from shot 1, now the bowl is full. A hand reaches for one phone. | "You'll find yours when the music stops." | Track at full. |
| 4 | 11.0–13.0s | End frame: cream card. | `Resonance · Chicago · July 18, 2026 · Link in bio.` | Track tails. |

*Why this works*: the quote is doing the proof. The B-roll grounds the quote in real space. The phone-bowl detail is mechanic-as-sentence as visual — "phones off the floor" without ever saying it.

*Permission requirement*: Exit-interview audio requires explicit voice + posting permission, captured per `05-ops/exit-interview-protocol.md` (when populated). Consent on file before any reel using this audio is published.

**BAD:**

> A reel using a generic AI voice-cloned narration reading a fabricated testimonial: *"At Resonance, I finally found my soulmate. The energy was unlike anything I've ever experienced. Andrea has created a sacred space for conscious singles to manifest deep, authentic connection."* B-roll is stock footage of a couple holding hands on a beach at sunset.

*Diagnosis*: AI-cloned voice (lying about who's speaking), fabricated quote (zero specificity, zero behavior, every adjective is on the wince-list — "energy," "sacred space," "conscious singles," "manifest," "authentic"), and stock B-roll that has nothing to do with Chicago, daytime, or the room. Failure cascades through every section of the brief simultaneously.

## Section 7 — Visual Spec

Cite `01-visual/photography-rules.md` (extends to videography) and `01-visual/DESIGN.md`.

- **Photography/videography rule (non-negotiable)**: *If a frame could have been shot at 11pm, it fails.* Real daylight, real rooms, honest light. (Brand bible §8.)
- **Real footage > AI footage > stock footage**, in that strict preference order. Pre-Event #1, B-roll comes from:
  - Andrea's pre-Resonance DJ work where the lighting and energy match the spine (rare; vet each clip).
  - Practice runs in the venue (hire a one-day documentary shooter to capture B-roll without faces in clear focus).
  - Object-and-surface footage (records, water carafes, the application form, the door, hands, feet, light on wood).
  - Andrea on camera in real settings (her studio, the venue empty).
- **Color grading**: minimal. Preserve actual daylight color. No fake-warm filters, no "golden hour" stylization beyond what the actual light gives. Cream/terracotta/midnight-blue palette holds; the camera doesn't add color the room didn't have.
- **B-roll subject library** (the safe-and-on-spine list):
  - Hands at the water station; hands on a record; hands on a mixer.
  - Sunlight on a record being cued; sunlight slanting across a wood floor.
  - Feet on a wood floor (close-up, mid-dance or still).
  - The back of a head dancing; shoulders dropping a half-inch.
  - The door closing as someone enters or leaves.
  - The phone-in-bowl shot at the door.
  - The application form on a table, pen, a thumb in frame.
  - Andrea's profile at her kit, eyes closed for one beat.
  - Empty rooms in real daylight, breathing.
- **Faces and identities**: never in clear focus without explicit permission. The default subject is gesture, surface, object, light — not a face. When a face is in frame, it's because the person consented.
- **Music**: Andrea's mixes (preferred); exit-interview audio (with permission); ambient room sound. Never random Reels trending audio without spine fit. Never voice-cloned narration. Never AI-generated music.
- **Banned**:
  - Club B-roll (dim rooms, strobes, neon, bar glassware, bottle service).
  - Dark/strobe footage in any context.
  - Stock dancer footage from Pexels/Pond5/etc.
  - AI portraits or AI B-roll of people.
  - Flashy text animations (kinetic typography, glitch effects, zoom-bounce-spin).
  - Emoji-as-icon overlays (☀️🎵💕🚫 stacked as feature-bullets).
  - "POV:" caption opener.
  - Generic motivational voice-over narration ("at Resonance, we believe...").

## Section 9 — Self-Check Questions

Before any reel produced from this brief ships:

1. **Spine fidelity**: Does the reel honor heart-encounter, daytime, sober, curated? Does any shot, line, or audio cue drift?
2. **Profile fit**: Would Marcus (or Nora, or Imani) recognize themselves in the first 1.5 seconds? On a sound-off scroll, does the visual + caption stop the thumb?
3. **Voice match (audio)**: Could Andrea say this voiceover on-camera to a friend over coffee? If a line sounds like narration-mode, rewrite. Caption matches voiceover verbatim.
4. **AI tells**: Any "Here's what / why / how / the thing" openings? Any "It's not X. It's Y." reveals? Em-dashes ≤ 2? No twin-sentence aphoristic endings?
5. **Banned phrases (text and audio)**: *Manifest, sacred container, vibrational, conscious singles, sober-curious community, high-vibe, intentional (decorative), authentic, vulnerability, energy, sacred masculine, conscious king, find your queen, "POV:" captions, "I have good news for you" influencer beats.*
6. **Banned visuals**: No club lighting, strobe, neon, kinetic typography, emoji-as-icon stack, AI portraits, AI B-roll, stock dancer footage, fluorescent night scenes, fake golden-hour filters?
7. **Format spec hit**: 9:16 vertical, length within range, hook earned in 1.5 sec, captions in safe zone, end frame OR real-moment ending, no banned CTAs?
8. **Audio sourcing legitimate**: Andrea's own mix / consented exit-interview / ambient room / Andrea's voice / spine-fit genre track? Permissions on file for any attendee voice?

If any answer is no, recut. If all yes, ship.
