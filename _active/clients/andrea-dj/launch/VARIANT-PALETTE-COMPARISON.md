## VARIANT A — Editorial Broadsheet (Orange)
**Feeling**: Warm editorial, vinyl-record-jacket elegance, day-lit clarity. Most accessible, most familiar.  
**Primary**: Terracotta-600 `#B8492E` (warm orange, late-afternoon light on wood)  
**Secondary**: Midnight-900 `#0F1A2E` (deep navy)  
**Tertiary**: Cream-100 `#F5EFE3` (warm paper background)  
**Text**: Ink-900 `#1A1814`, Slate-500 `#7A6F62` (metadata)  
**Typography**: GT Sectra (serif headlines, bold weight presence), Inter (sans body, light)  
**Grid**: Generous margin-based layout, 24px spacing, 4px radius on components  
**Heritage**: First hero shots approved, website design response positive, matches original BOS

**When to choose Variant A**: You want the warm, accessible, editorial-magazine feel that feels both classic and contemporary. This is your "safe, beautiful default."

## VARIANT B — Latin American Modernism (Moss + Ochre)
**Feeling**: Architectural precision, earthy sophistication, cultural rootedness. More distinctive, design-forward.  
**Primary**: Terracotta-600 `#A8412A` (slightly darker terracotta, structural warmth)  
**Secondary**: Moss-900 `#1F2E1E` (deep forest green, replaces navy)  
**Tertiary**: Ochre-600 `#B8843C` (golden-ochre earth tone, accent)  
**Text**: Ink-900 `#16140F`, Slate-600 `#6F6A5E` (metadata)  
**Typography**: Tiempos Text (serif, higher contrast), Canela (serif secondary), refined sans for body  
**Grid**: Strict 12-column architectural grid, 0px radius (sharp corners), high precision, +0.16em tracking on labels  
**Heritage**: Draws from Andrea's roots (Costa Rican family lineage), earthy modernism aesthetic

## VARIANT C — Quiet Luxury (Sage + Gold)
**Feeling**: Intimate, essayistic, minimalist-elegant. Most restrained, most refined. Whisper instead of declaration.  
**Primary**: Ink-900 `#16140F` (deep warm-black, replaces terracotta as primary)  
**Secondary**: Sage-700 `#4B5849` (soft muted green, primary accent)  
**Tertiary**: Gold-600 `#9C7833` (warm gold, founder-voice only, rare use)  
**Text**: Ink-900 `#16140F`, Slate-600 `#6F6A5E` (metadata)  
**Typography**: Serif headlines at 56px (restrained weight 400, not bold), Inter sans body at smaller scale  
**Grid**: Generous 80px margins, 4px radius, white space does as much work as color  
**Heritage**: From "Kinfolk magazine meets handwritten journal" aesthetic

## Decision Framework

**Choose Variant A** if:
- You want maximum clarity and warmth on first glance
- The website response ("I love the orange") stays your anchor
- You want design that feels *effortless* (highest optical warmth)
- You're optimizing for broad appeal across all three profiles

**Choose Variant B** if:
- You want the design to signal cultural rootedness and architectural intention
- You want something more distinctive than "warm and orange"
- You're willing to lean into the Latin American modernism heritage
- You want design that feels *intentional* (highest design sophistication)

**Choose Variant C** if:
- You want the most refined, whisper-elegant version
- You're willing to give up warmth for restraint and taste-signal
- You want design that feels *intimate* (highest taste exclusivity)
- You're optimizing for readers who value essayistic, introspective language
