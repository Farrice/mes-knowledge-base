## The Three Visual Variants — Your Taste Call

Variant selection is the **first decision** that unblocks downstream work. Until one variant is picked, Phase 2 image direction stays on v1 DESIGN.md, which is fine but means the new launch ships on the existing visual register rather than a refreshed one.

Read [`03-visual-variants/README.md`](03-visual-variants/README.md) for the full comparison matrix. Quick summary:

| | **A — Editorial Broadsheet** | **B — Latin-American Daylight** | **C — Quiet Luxury Daylight** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Atmosphere** | 1978 vinyl jacket in real sunlight | Casa Wabi courtyard at 3pm | *Gentlewoman* spread on marble at 3pm |
| **Primary** | Terracotta + Midnight on cream | Terracotta + Moss-Green + Ochre on warmer cream | **Ink-black** + Sage + Gold on cooler cream (terracotta demoted to accent) |
| **Display type** | GT Sectra 72px / 500 | Tiempos Headline / Canela 68px / 500 | Domaine Display 56px / **400** |
| **Geometry** | 2px radius (editorial sharpness) | **0px** (architectural rigidity) | 4px (quiet softness) |
| **Best for** | Literary press, design-conscious arts ICP | Heritage made visible — Latin-American Chicagoans, architectural rooms | Restraint-fluent ICP — *New Yorker* readers, tired of brands trying too hard |
| **Andrea's gut test** | *Does this look like the room I'd want photographed by Sandro Miller?* | *Does this look like a room I brought from Costa Rica, rendered as discipline?* | *Does this look like a room that trusts me to read it without raising its voice?* |
| **Hardest to maintain** | No (most forgiving) | Yes — Latin-American kitsch is the failure mode | Yes — restraint with nowhere to hide |

### The 8 Selection Questions

To pick, answer these (full list in `03-visual-variants/README.md`):

1. Do you want the brand to feel **literary or architectural or restrained**?
2. Is Costa Rica **explicitly visible** in the visual register, or implicit through founder story only?
3. Which photographer are we briefing — the **gesture-led editorial** photographer, the **environmental architectural** photographer, or the **intimate-essayistic** photographer?
4. How disciplined is the daily-template throughput? (A is most forgiving; C is least.)
5. How important is print fidelity? (B is highest; C is hardest.)
6. Which sub-brand future are you protecting? (A scales easiest; C may require future sub-brands to use a different register entirely.)
7. Which competitor category does the visual most clearly differentiate from? (B is most uncategorized; C risks generic-quiet-luxury.)
8. Andrea's gut: which of the three "gut test" sentences would she most want to be true?
