## 1. BRAND AT A GLANCE

**Resonance** — A daytime, sober, curated dance party in Chicago for people who want to meet a partner.

**The Core Mechanic:** Heart encounters, not head encounters. The music does the emotional labor so the people don't have to. We count **couples formed and real connections made**, not followers or attendance.

**The 12 Non-Negotiables:**
1. Daytime (2pm–5pm, never evening/night)
2. Sober (no alcohol on premise or being sold)
3. Curated (specific people, specific space, specific intent—never open to anyone)
4. Chicago-first (founded and rooted in Chicago, period)
5. Couples count (the success metric is real partnerships, not footfall)
6. Body-first social (music carries the emotional labor, bodies are the primary language)
7. No performance mandate (people aren't forced to "be on")
8. Real photography (no AI, no stock dancers, no posed group shots)
9. Founder voice is direct (Andrea speaks in lines, not paragraphs; declarative, not hedging)
10. Application not ticket (the why-gate, not commerce)
11. No sponsor stage (sponsors don't get platform, ever)
12. Event name is locked (variations get different names, never "Resonance at night" or "Resonance expanded")

**Your Job:** Ensure every design honors rules 1–9 and carries the brand's warmth and confidence.

## 2. CANVA SETUP (45 MINUTES)

### Step 1: Create a Free Canva Account (or use existing)
- Go to canva.com
- Sign up or log in
- Create a new Team workspace called "Resonance" (optional but recommended)

### Step 2: Create Brand Colors
In Canva Brand Kit:
1. Click "Create a design" → Choose any template
2. Left sidebar → Click "Brand kit" (or add via settings)
3. Click "Create new brand kit"
4. Name it "Resonance — [Variant Name]" (e.g., "Resonance — Editorial Broadsheet")
5. **Add these brand colors:**

**For Variant A (Editorial Broadsheet):**
- Terracotta 600: #B8492E (primary accent)
- Midnight 900: #0F1A2E (primary text)
- Cream 100: #F5EFE3 (background)
- Cream 50: #FBF7F0 (light background)
- Gold 600: #8C6526 (rare accent)
- Slate 500: #7A6F62 (captions, dates)
- Ink 900: #1A1814 (body text)

**For Variant B (Latin-American Modernism):**
- Terracotta 700: #8A3220 (primary)
- Terracotta 600: #A8412A (accent)
- Moss 900: #1F2E1E (text)
- Ochre 600: #B8843C (accent)
- Cream 100: #F2E9D4 (background)
- Ink 900: #1C1611 (body text)

**For Variant C (Quiet Luxury):**
- Ink 900: #16140F (primary text)
- Sage 700: #4B5849 (secondary)
- Gold 600: #9C7833 (accent)
- Cream 100: #F7F2E6 (background)
- Cream 50: #FDFAF3 (light background)

### Step 3: Add Fonts
1. In Brand Kit, go to "Fonts"
2. **For Variant A/B:**
   - **Serif (headlines):** Search for "IBM Plex Serif" (Variant A) or "Crimson Text" (Variant B)
   - **Sans (body):** Inter or Söhne
3. **For Variant C:**
   - **Serif:** Playfair Display (400 weight only)
   - **Sans:** Söhne or Inter
4. Set "Primary font" to the serif for headlines
5. Set "Secondary font" to the sans for body

### Step 4: Lock a Base Template (Optional but Recommended)
1. Create a new design (1080×1350 for IG/digital flyers, 8.5×11 for print)
2. Set background color to your Cream 100
3. Add wordmark "Resonance" (top-left or bottom-left, 24pt serif)
4. Save as a locked template: Right-click → "Save as template"
5. Make it "Brand Template" so it's available for all future designs

## 5. PHOTOGRAPHY RULES (THE 11PM TEST)

### The Single Most Important Rule

**If a photo could have been taken at 11pm in a club, it fails.**

This is not stylistic preference—it is the brand rendered as visual law. Every image must read as obviously daytime, in a real room, with real bodies.

### What Passes the 11pm Test

✅ Natural daylight from real windows  
✅ South-facing window light at 2pm–4pm  
✅ Overcast Chicago afternoon (flat grey is on-brand—it's real)  
✅ Sun on a wood floor, dappled light through a window  
✅ Real golden hour when it is real (not a filter)  
✅ Outdoor afternoon light in a park or street (if using outdoor shots)  
✅ Real motion blur from actual movement, slight natural grain (35mm film aesthetic)  

### What Fails the 11pm Test

❌ Flash of any kind (direct, bounced, ring light)  
❌ Club lighting (wash lights, gobos, colored spots, lasers, strobes)  
❌ Neon (signs, overlays, neon in frame)  
❌ Stylized golden-hour filters (faking late-afternoon warmth)  
❌ Tungsten room lighting alone (without daylight)  
❌ Candlelit, firelit, or moody lighting  
❌ Black and white or heavily desaturated images  
❌ AI-perfect even lighting (real rooms have shadows and uneven light)  

### What the Room Looks Like

- A dance studio or converted loft in Chicago
- Hardwood floors (maple or worn industrial) or polished concrete
- Large windows (industrial, arched, or floor-to-ceiling)
- Natural afternoon daylight streaming in
- **NO plants, NO decorations, NO props**
- Minimal: exposed brick, white walls, exposed ductwork, a wooden barre
- The architecture and the people carry the room

### What the People Look Like

- Adults aged 30–38 specifically (not 20s, not 40s+)
- Casual-elevated clothing: sundresses, linen shirts, earth-tone tops, dark jeans, midi skirts, loafers, clean sneakers, leather sandals
- Mixed-race, mixed body types, mixed gender presentation
- Real skin, real texture (no skin-smoothing or airbrushing)
- No phones visible (phones are off the floor)
- No alcohol, no glasses, no bottles anywhere
- No posed group portraits; candid only

### Body Language — Connection Cues (Aim for 2–3 per hero shot)

- One body leaning in to whisper to another, both mid-laugh
- Two bodies facing each other (not facing the DJ—facing each other)
- Hand on shoulder, gaze on a third person (active social attention)
- Eye contact between two people across a small room, mid-recognition
- Mid-laugh between strangers (teeth visible, eyes crinkled, heads tilted toward each other)
- Multiple bodies blurred in motion, intermingled (not lined up)
- One person laughing, another in middle distance also reacting
- One person in conversation with two others (three-body social geometry)

### Body Language — Disconnection Cues (KILL THESE)

❌ Backs of heads in a line (yoga-class lineup)  
❌ All bodies facing the DJ in a stadium pose  
❌ Solo body isolated in center of frame, no other bodies  
❌ Hands-in-air arena pose (festival grammar)  
❌ "Smile at the camera" group portraits  
❌ Bodies in identical wellness uniforms (white linen lineup = retreat)  
❌ AI-perfect lighting on every face  
❌ Hands holding phones  
❌ Glass-of-wine signaling  

### Partnership Orientation

- Hero shots should show **heterosexual M/F partnership recognition** (the founder's specific brand thesis)
- Group shots may include mixed friendships, but the romantic recognition beat is always M/F
- In prompts, name M/F explicitly: "A Latina woman in her mid-30s and a Black man in his late 30s, facing each other..."

## 6. VOICE DISCIPLINE

### Core Voice Rules

**Warm, direct, confident, never preachy, never corporate.**

| Rule | Example (Yes) | Example (No) |
|------|---|---|
| Show > tell | "Two people mid-laugh" | "A celebration of authentic connection" |
| Mechanic-as-headline | "A daytime dance room" | "Join our premier curated singles experience" |
| No "Here's what/why/how" | "The music does the work" | "Here's why this is different" |
| Em-dashes ≤2 per piece | "Sober. Chicago. Real." | "The room is curated—and intentional—and safe" |
| Specific location | "Chicago. July 18. 2–5pm." | "Coming summer 2026 to a city near you" |
| Application not ticket | "Application opens June 25" | "Buy tickets — only 50 spots, going fast!" |
| Name the enemy by name | "You don't have to drink" | "Unlike other events (hint hint)" |
| Declarative voice | "This is what's different" | "We believe that connection is..." |

### Banned Phrases (Delete on Sight)

❌ "community" (generic, not specific to Resonance)  
❌ "intentional" (used 3+ times as generic adjective)  
❌ "conscious singles"  
❌ "sacred container"  
❌ "high-vibe"  
❌ "tribe"  
❌ "manifest"  
❌ "curated" × 3 (use 1x max in one piece)  
❌ "transformative"  
❌ "elevate"  
❌ "journey" (in wellness sense)  
❌ "healing space"  
❌ "aligned"  
❌ "energy" (without specificity)  
❌ "That's not X, it's Y" (structural AI tell)  
❌ Twin-sentence endings: "It's A. It's B." (if repeated, signals AI)  
❌ Triple anaphora (Rhetorical overkill)  

### What Andrea Actually Sounds Like

- Direct: "You've tried the apps. You've tried the bars. Here's what's different."
- Warm: "The music does the emotional labor so you don't have to."
- Confident: "This is what a real room in Chicago looks like at 2pm on Saturday."
- Conversational: "Strangers. 2pm. The room does the rest."
- Never hedging: Not "we hope you'll consider" — instead "Come see."
- Never corporate: Not "We're excited to announce" — instead "Saturday 2pm."

## 7. FLYER DESIGN SPEC

### Digital Flyer (1080×1350 px, 4:5 portrait)

**Key Dimensions:**
- Photo coverage: 65–75% of surface (photo is protagonist)
- Type occupies: 25–35% (caption to the image)

**Elements (top-to-bottom):**

1. **Wordmark** (top-left or bottom-left corner)
   - "Resonance" in serif (GT Sectra 24pt for Variant A, Crimson Text 24pt for Variant B, Playfair 24pt for Variant C)
   - Color: Cream-100 or Ink-900 depending on photo contrast behind it
   - No shadow, no outline; simple type over image

2. **Photograph** (fills 65–75% of design)
   - Daytime room shot (passes 11pm test)
   - 4–5 adults visible, mixed-race, mixed-body, mixed-gender
   - Real moment: mid-laugh, mid-gesture, mid-connection
   - Chicago architecture visible in background
   - Slight motion blur on one body (suggests real movement)
   - No phones, no glasses, no flash, no neon, no club lighting

3. **Date/Location Stamp** (label-caps, 14pt)
   - "JULY 18, 2026 · CHICAGO"
   - Positioned directly beneath photo
   - Color: Slate-500 (variant A) or matching secondary color (variants B/C)

4. **Headline** (≤8 words, mechanic-as-headline pattern)
   - Size: 64–72pt serif for digital
   - Color: Midnight-900 (Variant A), Terracotta-700 or Moss-900 (Variant B), Ink-900 (Variant C)
   - Below date stamp, with lg spacing (24px+)
   - Examples: "A daytime dance room." / "You don't have to drink to dance." / "The music does the work."

5. **Body Text** (≤30 words total, supporting line)
   - Size: 18–20pt sans
   - Color: Ink-900 (all variants) or secondary text color
   - Single block, not split across lines unless necessary
   - Below headline with lg spacing (24px+)
   - Example: "The music does the emotional labor so you don't have to."

6. **CTA** (single line at bottom)
   - "Application opens [DATE] · resonance.live/apply"
   - Size: 14pt sans
   - Color: Terracotta-600 (Variant A), Terracotta-600 (Variant B), Sage-700 (Variant C)
   - No button styling; text link is the CTA
   - Below body with spacing (24px+)

**Anti-Patterns (Kill on Sight):**
- ❌ NO QR code as focal element (bottom corner only, <0.5" if used)
- ❌ NO hashtags anywhere
- ❌ NO sponsor logos
- ❌ NO drop shadows behind type
- ❌ NO AI-generated portraits
- ❌ NO stock dancers
- ❌ NO club lighting
- ❌ NO neon colors, NO gradient backgrounds, NO "save the date" badge graphics

### Physical Flyer (8.5×11 in portrait, 300dpi CMYK)

**Same specs as digital, scaled for print:**
- Headline: 72–96pt (larger than digital for print legibility)
- Body: Same ≤30 words, scaled appropriately
- All proportions maintained
- Bleed: 0.25" on all sides
- Safe area: 0.5" from edge for critical text
- Paper stock: 100lb uncoated text or 80lb cover (never glossy)
- Color profile: CMYK (cream and terracotta print true on uncoated stock; terracotta loses saturation on glossy)

**File Export:**
- PDF with bleeds included
- CMYK color profile
- Submit to printer with color notes: "Cream background on warm uncoated stock / Terracotta accent in warm terracotta-red / Never glossy"

## 8. INSTAGRAM POST DESIGN SPEC

### IG Feed Post (1080×1350, 4:5 portrait)

**Visual:**
- Photo-led (same as flyer)
- Daytime room, real moment, 11pm test passes
- Optional light text overlay (headline / quote / supporting line)

**Copy (in caption, not design):**
Choose one of 4 hook patterns + one CTA option.

**Hook Pattern Examples:**

**A: Out-Loud-Asking**
- Voice: Intimate, editorial, listener-centric
- Length: 150–250 words
- Opener: "Why is it so hard to meet someone real?"

**B: Anaphora**
- Voice: Direct, naming the problem
- Length: 150–250 words
- Pattern: "You've tried X. You've tried Y. You've tried Z. And yet..."

**C: Show-First-Then-Pivot**
- Voice: Visual, then explanation
- Length: 150–250 words
- Pattern: Describe a real moment, then explain why it happened

**D: Quote-Led**
- Voice: Authority, external validation
- Length: 150–250 words
- Pattern: Open with something someone said after attending

**CTA Options:**

- **A: Hell-Yes Filter** — "Application opens June 25. resonance.live/apply if this is you."
- **B: Comment Ask** — "Interested? Say yes in the comments and we'll send you the application link."
- **C: Save-the-Date** — "Link in bio. Application opens June 25."

### IG Story (1080×1920, 9:16 portrait)

**3-card sequence:**

**Card 1:** Text + Image (establishing shot of room)
- "Saturday 2pm. Chicago."

**Card 2:** Quote or text + Image (two people connecting)
- "The music does the work"

**Card 3:** CTA + Image (energy, dancing, movement)
- "Application opens June 25"
- "resonance.live/apply"

## 9. CANVA TEMPLATE CHECKLIST

### 5 Locked Templates to Create

1. **IG Feed Post (1080×1350)**
   - Base: Photo placeholder + text layout
   - Locked: Wordmark, color palette, font choices, spacing
   - Editable: Headline text, body copy, image

2. **IG Story (1080×1920)**
   - Base: 3-card story template
   - Locked: Palette, fonts, text styling
   - Editable: Text content, images

3. **Digital Flyer (1080×1350)**
   - Base: Photo + copy layout (same as IG but different copy discipline)
   - Locked: Wordmark, spacing, color palette, font styles
   - Editable: Headline, body, date, image

4. **Physical Flyer (8.5×11)**
   - Base: Print-safe margins (0.5" from edge), bleed area marked
   - Locked: Wordmark, spacing, color palette, font styles, safe area guides
   - Editable: All copy elements, image

5. **Email Header (600×200)**
   - Base: Horizontal format, wordmark, CTA
   - Locked: Fonts, colors, spacing
   - Editable: Headline, subheadline, button text

### How to Lock Elements in Canva

1. Create your template design
2. Select an element (text, image, shape)
3. Click ⋮ (three dots) → "Lock"
4. Repeat for all non-editable elements
5. Test: Unlock a few elements to ensure they're individually lockable
6. Save as template: "Brand Template — [Name]"
7. Share with edit access so Andrea's friend can duplicate for events

## 10. QUALITY GATE CHECKLIST (BEFORE PUBLISH)

Before any flyer or IG post goes live, check all 7:

- [ ] **Spine Fidelity:** Does the photograph honor daytime? Does the 11pm test pass?
- [ ] **Profile Fit:** Does the photograph signal *not-wellness, not-club, not-recovery*? Are mixed-race/mixed-body/mixed-gender visible?
- [ ] **Headline Discipline:** ≤8 words? Names a mechanic, not a benefit?
- [ ] **Specificity:** Is the city + date explicit? ("Chicago. July 18, 2–5pm.") 
- [ ] **CTA Discipline:** Points to application (why-gate), not to ticket-sale checkout?
- [ ] **AI Tells:** Em-dashes ≤2? No marketing-cliché phrases? No "That's not X, it's Y" parallel structure?
- [ ] **Banned Phrases:** No community/intentional/conscious/sacred/high-vibe/curated-three-times?
- [ ] **Coffee-Shop Test:** Would Andrea be comfortable stapling this to a corkboard at a Pilsen coffee shop and feeling proud of it?
