How These Variants Were Built

Same brand DNA, different visual voice. All three carry Resonance's core mechanic: daytime, sober, curated, Chicago-first, couples-count, body-first social. All three honor the 12 non-negotiables.

The difference is aesthetic and operational: Variant A says "timeless warmth." Variant B says "architectural permanence." Variant C says "quiet depth." Pick the one that feels most honest to who you are as a curator and the space you're building.

Once you choose, we'll use that variant's palette, typography, and spacing philosophy for all downstream assets: flyers, IG posts, Canva templates, website, applications, everything.

Ready to decide?

Review each variant's full design system using the buttons above. Notice how each one feels different—not just in color, but in confidence, pacing, and what it emphasizes about the room.

Once you've chosen your variant, let me know and we'll build the July 18 flyer, IG post, and Canva templates using that direction.
