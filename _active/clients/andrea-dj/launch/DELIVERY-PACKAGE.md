## THE 11PM TEST (Critical for All Photography)

> **If a photo could have been taken at 11pm in a club, it fails.**

Before using any image, verify:
- ✅ Natural daylight visible (not flash, not club lighting)
- ✅ Room architecture readable (not club darkness)
- ✅ Real people, real bodies, mixed-race/mixed-body/mixed-gender
- ✅ No phones, no alcohol, no posed group shots
- ✅ Slight motion blur okay; AI-perfect lighting fails
- ✅ Daytime clothing, not clubwear
- ✅ No neon, no strobes, no "moody" filter

See **RESONANCE-DESIGNER-KIT.md section 5** for full photo specs.

## VOICE DISCIPLINE (One-Page Crib Sheet)

| Rule | Yes | No |
|------|-----|---|
| Show > tell | "Two people mid-laugh" | "Authentic connection" |
| Mechanic-as-headline | "You don't have to drink" | "Premier curated experience" |
| Specific location | "Chicago. July 18. 2pm." | "Coming summer 2026" |
| Application not ticket | "Application opens June 25" | "Buy tickets — 50 spots!" |
| Direct voice | "This is what's different" | "We believe..." |

**Banned phrases:** community, intentional (as generic), conscious singles, sacred container, high-vibe, tribe, manifest, transformative, "That's not X, it's Y"

See **RESONANCE-DESIGNER-KIT.md section 6** for full voice rules.

## FAQs

**Q: Can we use stock photos instead of shooting the room?**  
A: Not recommended. Stock photos fail the 11pm test. Resonance brand requires real room, real people, real daylight. If no event photos available yet, shoot a test session or use a real photo from past events.

**Q: What if Andrea wants to add more colors/fonts?**  
A: The variant palette is locked (these colors carry specific meaning). Additional fonts weaken consistency. Stay within the system. If Andrea wants something different, that's a new variant direction—out of scope for this package.

**Q: Can we use these templates for other events beyond July 18?**  
A: Yes! That's the point. Once templates are locked, duplicate them for every future Resonance event. Change only: date, headline (if new mechanic), photograph, application open date. Everything else stays the same.

**Q: Do we need Canva Pro?**  
A: Free tier works. Pro ($15/mo) adds brand kit live-sharing (Andrea can see updates in real-time). If designer + Andrea are collaborating actively, Pro pays for itself.

**Q: What's the wordmark asset?**  
A: "Resonance" in the variant's serif font (GT Sectra 24pt for Variant A, Crimson Text 24pt for B, Playfair 24pt for C). No logo asset; just the word. Top-left or bottom-left corner, small & tasteful.
