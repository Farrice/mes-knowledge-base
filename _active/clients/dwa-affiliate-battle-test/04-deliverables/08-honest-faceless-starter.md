## PIECE 2 — The 9-Day Publish Plan (naptime-sized)
Ends with your first live page, 12 scheduled posts, and a 3-email sequence.

- **Day 1** — Pick your one topic. Write 3 posts with Skeleton A + B (below). ~30 min.
- **Day 2** — Write 3 more posts (Skeletons C, D, E). Schedule all 6. ~30 min.
- **Day 3** — Write 6 more posts (reuse skeletons, new angle). You now have 12. ~40 min.
- **Day 4** — Open a free page builder. Draft an ugly landing page — headline + who it's for + one CTA. ~45 min.
- **Day 5** — Improve the page once. Add the fit check (Piece 4). ~30 min.
- **Day 6** — Set up a free email tool. Connect it to the page. ~30 min.
- **Day 7** — Write email 1 (welcome + the free thing). ~30 min.
- **Day 8** — Write emails 2–3 (the honest story + the offer, disclosed). Rewrite your page headline 5 ways; keep the truest. ~45 min.
- **Day 9** — Hit publish. Post #1 goes live; run the 10-minute reply sprint. ~30 min + replies.

That's the whole before→after: blank page → shipped, in 9 focused hours.

## PIECE 4 — The "Is this you?" Fit Check (10 questions)
Answer honestly. This decides it *before* you spend a cent.

1. Do you want a **skill you keep**, more than a fast payout?
2. Will you post content **consistently** (even faceless)?
3. Can you give it **6–12 months**, not 6 days?
4. Can you spend **~45 min/day** on this after the 9-day build?
5. Can you lose **$497** without it wrecking your month?
6. Are you okay that **most weeks are slow** at first?
7. Will you **talk to people** who reply (in DMs)?
8. Do you accept there are **no income guarantees**?
9. Are you learning to **build**, not to **flip**?
10. If someone showed you the boring daily work, would you still want in?

**8–10 yes →** you're the fit. Keep going.
**5–7 yes →** maybe — read the honest daily work again first.
**0–4 yes →** please don't buy. It'll frustrate you. Save the money.
