## Troubleshooting: What if...

**...I don't see any opt-ins after Day 2?**
Probably normal (you have no audience yet). If still zero on Day 5, the hooks might be wrong. Go to `/suzuki-post-lab` and test new ones.

**...I get lots of opt-ins but zero DMs after a few hours?**
Auto-DM may be delayed or the link might be broken. Check: Is the keyword in their comment? Did the tool trigger? Test it yourself: comment "HONEST" on your own post.

**...Someone asks "Will I make money?"**
Use the script: *"I can't promise that, and you should distrust anyone who does. Results depend on real, consistent work, and most beginners don't see fast results. What I can say is the skills (funnels, email, copy) are real and portable."*

**...I want to add income claims to make it convert better.**
Don't. That's a sign the *offer* is the problem, not the copy. Run `/suzuki-launch-debugger` instead.

**...My landing page isn't converting after 100 clicks.**
The LP is solid, but you may be reaching the wrong audience (skeptical non-beginners, or people with zero interest in digital marketing). Adjust the account bio or hook messaging, or test audience targeting.
