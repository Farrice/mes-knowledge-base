## 2. Competitive landscape (faceless MRR on X/Threads/IG)
- **Monoculture:** hundreds of accounts resell the same courses with near-identical "passive income / quit your 9-5 / copy my system" scripts. Saturation is structural (every buyer becomes a competitor with the same swipe copy).
- **Saturated/cringe (avoid):** income screenshots, "$10K months," "passive income while you sleep," boss-babe, luxury B-roll.
- **The open lane (our wedge):** honest **utility/skill framing with zero income claims** — the entire field avoids it because the courses teach income-dream copy. **The compliance constraint IS the differentiation.**

## 3. Buyer ICP — "Brittany, 34" (the quiet quitter with a phone)
- Not chasing wealth — chasing **exit + dignity**. Modest goal: "replace ~$2K so I can stay home." Already burned by one course → carries **shame + scam-vigilance**. Most of the market is pre-decided the category is a scam, so **honesty itself is the hook.**
- **Language they use:** "be home with my kids and still contribute," "without getting sucked into an MLM," "not trying to be rich," "don't want to sell crap to my friends," "what do you ACTUALLY do all day," "I've been burned before."
- **Wince words (banned):** passive income, six figures, $10K months, "proven system," financial freedom, boss babe, guaranteed, recruit/downline.
- **Who NOT to sell:** wants guaranteed/passive money, won't make content, FOMO buyers, expects linear ROI, can't afford to lose $497.
