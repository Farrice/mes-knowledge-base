# Agent Intake — Brand + Listing

Living doc: the two forms every new agent fills, both text-able. Part A once per agent (their brand, so every deliverable is bespoke). Part B once per listing. Answers land in `agents/<name>-brand-card.md` and drive voice, register, and design tokens for everything we make them.

---

## Part A — Brand intake (once, ~10 minutes, send as one text)

> Quick brand setup so everything I make you looks and sounds like YOU, not a template. Short answers are fine; "I don't know, you pick" is a valid answer anywhere.
>
> 1. Your Instagram handle (and any other platform you actually post on)
> 2. Your market + niche in one line (e.g. "SFV, first-time buyers" / "Calabasas, luxury")
> 3. Brand colors — pick ONE: (a) I have exact colors: [send hex codes or a screenshot of your logo/site] (b) I have a logo but no colors — here's the logo (c) nothing yet, show me 3 directions and I'll pick
> 4. Typography feel — pick one: (a) editorial serif (quiet luxury) (b) bold modern sans (confident, punchy) (c) clean minimal sans (calm, polished) (d) you pick from my profile
> 5. Headshots: send 2-3 you actually like (phone photos fine)
> 6. On camera, are you more: (a) warm + conversational (b) direct + authoritative (c) depends on the listing
> 7. Anything you refuse to say or do on camera?
> 8. Two agents or creators whose content you admire (any market)
> 9. What do clients say about working with you, in their words? (paste a review or text if easy)

**On receipt, we build `agents/<name>-brand-card.md`:**
- **Voice**: register pick (calm-warm floor / authority; the Jen register ladder generalizes — luxury inventory plays a register UP), banned moves, their verbatim client language from Q9
- **Design tokens**: 4-6 colors (from logo/site scrape or the direction they picked), 2 typefaces max with roles, accent rule (ONE accent, sparingly)
- **Platform + niche**: primary platform, market, buyer type
- Voice scrape of their last 15 posts merges in (existing step 2 of the delivery run)

**If they picked (c) on colors**: build 3 distinct direction boards (different type treatment + palette + layout logic — NOT three shades of one idea), send as one image, they reply 1/2/3. Approve direction before producing anything.

## Part B — Listing intake (per kit, ~10 minutes)

> Address + MLS link · 5 phone photos of the property · Who's the buyer for this home (first-timer? move-up? investor?) · One thing about this property you'd tell a friend · Anything you won't say on camera

## Rules

- Never invent brand facts. Missing answer = ask or offer a pick-one, never guess silently.
- Brand card is LIVING: one per agent, updated in place as their brand sharpens.
- Every deliverable built after intake runs under their tokens, not ours — Farrice's Premium Minimal is OUR brand, never theirs.
- Fair-housing floor applies regardless of any intake answer.
