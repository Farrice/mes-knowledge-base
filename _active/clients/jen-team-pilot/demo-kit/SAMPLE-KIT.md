## 5. The Don't-Say List: fair-housing screen, included with every kit

Every line above has been screened. When you improvise on camera or in DMs, stay off these:

- **Never describe the people, describe the property.** Not "perfect for young families / couples / professionals." Say "3 bedrooms and a flat, gated yard" and let buyers self-select.
- **No "safe" / "low-crime" / "good neighborhood" claims.** Say what's verifiable: "gated yard," "ten minutes from [named store/park]."
- **No school talk on camera.** Not even positive. Buyers can research schools; agents steering on them is a fair-housing risk.
- **No religious or demographic landmarks as selling points** ("close to the church/temple," "up-and-coming crowd").
- **No invented urgency.** No "won't last," no fake countdowns. If interest is real, name it precisely ("second showing booked for Saturday").
- **No payment promises.** Monthly figures only from a lender quote, labeled as such.
