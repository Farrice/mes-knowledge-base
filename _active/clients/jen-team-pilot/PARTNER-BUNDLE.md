# Partner Bundle — Jay (@justjaysunfilms)

Living doc. Status: OPTION, not dependency — the $200 pilot runs with or without this. Craft observations: `skills/jay-sun-films-video-craft/SKILL.md`.

## The thesis

Jay shoots and edits pro-grade real-estate video (exposure discipline, blue-hour scheduling, gimbal movement) but his weak layer is what's ON the footage — scripts, hooks, text, captions, strategy. Our kits are exactly that layer, but leave filming to the agent's phone. Zero overlap, full stack. He is already Jen's videographer (his whole grid is Jen collabs), so the bundle is proven-in-public before it's even named.

## What he gets / what we get

- **He gets:** pipeline (our agent intakes become his shoot bookings — he's at 192 followers and "DM to book"), scripts + shot plans that make his shoots faster, branded text/design that upgrades his edits, and a productized offer instead of one-off gigs.
- **We get:** a premium tier we can't deliver solo, his Jen footage as shared proof, and a second warm channel into agents (his DMs).

## The Jay conversation (agenda, ~30 min)

1. Show him the sample kit + one demand-research output — "this is my half."
2. Interview for the skill gaps: gear, edit stack, turnaround, run sheet, capacity, pricing, what he wants from a bundle.

## Risks

- **Friendship + money.** Terms in writing before deal one, however casual the relationship.
- **Jen optics again.** Her videographer + her husband selling to her teammates — fold into the same permission text; it strengthens it ("your whole content team, available to your people").
- **Capacity.** He may have a day job / limited slots; the bundle tier ships only at his confirmed capacity, never promised ahead of it.
