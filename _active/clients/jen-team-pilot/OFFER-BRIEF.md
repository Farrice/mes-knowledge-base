# Jen-Team Pilot — Offer Brief + Delivery Process

Living doc. Owner: Farrice. Serves: revenue-5k-incumbency (30-day trial lane).
Goal: 3 founding-rate sales × $200 = $600 inside 30 days, plus proof that pulls Jen on board.

## 1. The Offer (what you say you sell)

**Name: The Listing Launch Kit. $200 founding rate (standard $450).**

One active listing becomes a complete, ready-to-film content package in 48 hours:

1. **Shoot Sheet.** Exactly what to film at the property, shot by shot, in one walkthrough. No videographer needed. A phone is fine.
2. **Teleprompter Pack.** Three reel scripts written in the agent's own voice, word for word, readable off the phone.
3. **Caption + Hook Set.** Captions, hooks, and a carousel for the listing, each with 2 or 3 pick-one options.
4. **Stories Sequence + DM Keyword.** A 5-frame story arc ending in a keyword CTA ("DM me TOUR") so views turn into conversations instead of decoration.
5. **Compliance Pass.** Every line screened against fair-housing language rules before it reaches them, with a don't-say list included.

Delivered as one forwardable package: the SEND shape already proven with Jen. One text, pick-one options, don't-say list. They film, post, and answer DMs. That is their whole job.

**Why this SKU instead of a monthly retainer as the wedge:** a listing is urgent, attached to real money, and has a deadline. It is the easiest yes in an agent's world. A retainer asks for a relationship before trust exists.

**Fallback SKU when the agent has no active listing: Agent Engine Starter, same $200.**
Profile curb-appeal fix (bio, pins, link, top of grid) plus 10 ready-to-film scripts in their voice from their niche, plus a 2-week posting calendar. Scripts are demand-backed, not guessed: a live research pass on what buyers/sellers/relocators in their market are searching this month, prioritizing the questions no local agent is answering (Sherrard workflow 04, Stage 2). Built on the Sherrard four-mistakes audit. Agents who post consistently and still get zero leads are usually posting for views or for other agents, stating facts without translating them, skipping hook-value-CTA structure, and running a listings-only grid that reads like a homes magazine.

**Terms of the founding rate:** $200 instead of $450, first 3 agents only, in exchange for (a) permission to screenshot results and (b) one sentence of feedback after the first kit posts. Paid up front (Zelle/Venmo). The low price is a trade, not a discount, so it never anchors as your real price.

**Ascension path (do not pitch it yet, just know it):**
Kit at $200 founding → repeat kits at $450 → Monthly Content Engine around $500-750/mo (four kits' worth of content plus profile management, plus a monthly demand-backed carousel set — 3/week is the Sherrard cadence) → team deal through Jen. Video-production add-on slots into the engine tier if the partner bundle happens (open thread).

You are not "a guy trying content marketing." One line, true and verifiable:

> "I run the content system behind @realestatewithjing's listings: shoot sheets, scripts, captions, the whole package. I'm opening 3 founding spots for agents on the team."

Proof objects on hand: the Willis, Armida, Moonseed, and Bothwell listing packages. Show one on your phone in 30 seconds, the shoot sheet next to the finished reel script. The work sells itself better than any claim about it.

Production | Shoot sheet, teleprompter pack, captions/carousel/stories, keyword CTA — carousel built under the Sherrard design contract in THEIR tokens | `skills/jen-santulan-listing-content` + `/enrico-expand` + `skills/mike-sherrard-realtor-branding/workflows/04-market-demand-carousel-system.md` | 60-90 min |
| 4. Compliance | Fair-housing screen on every line; build the don't-say list | `skills/re-compliance-pack` | 10 min |

**Marginal delivery cost: about 2 hours per kit, $0 in paid API.** At $200 that is roughly $100/hr from work the harness already knows how to do. At the $450 standard rate, roughly $225/hr.

**Intake form (copy-paste, keep in this folder):**
> Address + MLS link · 5 phone photos of the property · Your IG handle · Who's the buyer for this home (first-timer? move-up? investor?) · One thing about this property you'd tell a friend · Anything you won't say on camera

## 5. 30-Day Trial Plan

- **Week 1:** Jen check-in (sample permission), then pitch all 3 agents. Target: 2 paid intakes. First kit delivered within 48h of first payment.
- **Week 2:** Deliver remaining kits. Day-3 follow-ups. Collect the first posted reel.
- **Week 3:** Results screenshots and feedback sentences go into the proof bank. Offer kit #2 at founding rate to whoever posted.
- **Week 4:** Verdict week. Count revenue and repeat buyers, then show Jen the agents' live posts ("this is your system working; want it back on?"). Decide: raise to $450, open beyond the team, or park.

**Honest math:** 1 sale = $200, which is under your $300 floor. 2 sales = $400, floor cleared. 3 = $600, target hit. The plan needs two yeses, not three.

**Kill criteria (decide now, not in week 4):** zero paid intakes after all 3 agents are pitched and followed up once means the channel is the problem, not the price. Park it and take the learnings to the LinkedIn lane. Do not drop the price below $200.

## 6. Risks + Objections

- **"My brokerage gives us marketing."** "Templates with your headshot dropped in. This is scripts in your voice for your listing, ready to film tonight. Different animal."
- **"We already pay a VA ~$1,200-1,400/mo for content."** Never knock the VA. Position a caliber jump, not a replacement: "Keep what works. This is the premium layer — demand-researched, designed to your brand, compliance-screened. Try one kit at $200 next to what you're getting; your feed will tell you." The POC carousels ARE this argument. If the caliber gap is obvious, the takeover conversation happens on their initiative, not yours.
- **"I don't have a listing right now."** Fallback SKU, same price, same day.
- **Ghosting after "show me."** One follow-up 48h later with the sample attached. Then stop and move to the next agent.
- **Jen optics.** Solved by asking first (§2). Frame to her: "I'm proving your system on the team so it's warm when you're ready."
- **Scope creep ("can you also post it for me?").** "That's the monthly engine. The founding kit gets you the package; posting stays yours." Never post for them at $200.
- **Fair housing.** The compliance pass is non-negotiable, and it doubles as a selling point: most agents have never had anyone screen their copy.
