The Listing Launch Kit

One active listing becomes a complete, ready-to-film content package in 48 hours. Founding rate $200 for the first three agents on Jen's team; standard rate $450.

Serves revenue-5k-incumbency Target $600 / 30 days Delivery cost ~2 hrs · $0 API

01The Offer

  1. Shoot Sheet. Exactly what to film at the property, shot by shot, in one walkthrough. No videographer; a phone is fine.
  2. Teleprompter Pack. Three reel scripts in the agent's own voice, word for word, readable off the phone.
  3. Caption + Hook Set. Captions, hooks, and a carousel, each with two or three pick-one options.
  4. Stories Sequence + DM Keyword. A five-frame story arc ending in a keyword CTA ("DM me TOUR") so views turn into conversations.
  5. Compliance Pass. Every line screened against fair-housing language rules, with a don't-say list included.

Delivered as one forwardable package: the SEND shape already proven with Jen. One text, pick-one options, don't-say list. They film, post, and answer DMs. That is their whole job.

Why this wedge, not a retainer: a listing is urgent, attached to real money, and has a deadline. It is the easiest yes in an agent's world. A retainer asks for a relationship before trust exists.

Fallback SKU — no active listing: Agent Engine Starter, same $200

Profile curb-appeal fix (bio, pins, link, top of grid) plus ten ready-to-film scripts in their voice from their niche, plus a two-week posting calendar. Built on the Sherrard four-mistakes audit of why consistent posters get zero leads.

Founding-rate terms

$200 instead of $450, first three agents only, in exchange for (a) permission to screenshot results and (b) one sentence of feedback after the first kit posts. Paid up front, Zelle or Venmo. The low price is a trade, not a discount, so it never anchors as the real price.

Ascension path (know it, don't pitch it yet)

Kit at $200 founding → repeat kits at $450 → Monthly Content Engine at $500–750/mo → team deal through Jen.

02Positioning

The one line — true and verifiable "I run the content system behind @realestatewithjing's listings: shoot sheets, scripts, captions, the whole package. I'm opening 3 founding spots for agents on the team."

Proof objects on hand: the Willis, Armida, Moonseed, and Bothwell listing packages. Show one on your phone in 30 seconds, the shoot sheet next to the finished reel script. The work sells itself better than any claim about it.

Check first: confirm with Jen that showing her packages to her own teammates is fine. Almost certainly yes, since it is her team and it flatters her system, but ask before the first pitch, not after.

Kill criteria, decided now: zero paid intakes after all 3 agents are pitched and followed up once means the channel is the problem, not the price. Park it and carry the learnings to the LinkedIn lane. Never drop below $200.

06Objections + Risks

  • "My brokerage gives us marketing." — "Templates with your headshot dropped in. This is scripts in your voice for your listing, ready to film tonight. Different animal."
  • "I don't have a listing right now." — Fallback SKU, same price, same day.
  • Ghosting after "show me." — One follow-up 48h later with the sample attached. Then stop; next agent.
  • Jen optics. — Ask first. Frame: "I'm proving your system on the team so it's warm when you're ready."
  • "Can you also post it for me?" — "That's the monthly engine. The founding kit gets you the package; posting stays yours."
  • Fair housing. — The compliance pass is non-negotiable, and it doubles as a selling point: most agents have never had anyone screen their copy.
