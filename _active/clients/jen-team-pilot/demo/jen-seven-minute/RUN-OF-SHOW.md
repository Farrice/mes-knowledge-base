# Jen Seven-Minute Demonstration: Run of Show

Status: **Draft for local rehearsal**  
Runtime: **7:00**  
Primary artifact: `index.html`

## The job

Show Jen useful work built for her first. Then reveal the bounded Listing Launch service behind it. Leave with three greenlights, not a pressured sale.

The demonstration does **not** claim that First Home Valley or Listing Launch has caused closings. It shows a designed path from useful content to measurable conversations and asks permission to test it.

## Before Jen arrives

1. Open `index.html` in a browser.
2. Click **Present** so the operator notes disappear.
3. Press **F** for fullscreen.
4. Keep the fictional full sample available through the link on slide 6, but do not open it unless Jen asks for detail.
5. Press **Space** when you begin to start the seven-minute rehearsal timer.

Keyboard controls: **← / →** slides · **Space** timer · **N** notes · **F** fullscreen.

## Exact seven-minute talk track

### 00:00–00:35: Set the frame

> I built two connected things. The first is for your business. The second is a small offer I could test with agents you trust, but I’m not moving that part without your okay.

Do not explain the production process. Let the work create curiosity.

### 00:35–01:35: Show First Home Valley

> First Home Valley is the buyer-education system I built for you. It isn’t a pile of posts. It is a recognizable series that teaches the real questions people need answered before they ever feel ready to call an agent.

Optional pause: “Which one makes you want to keep reading?” Save the full voice verdict for the final slide.

### 01:35–02:25: Show the conversation path

> The part I care about is not the view count. Each piece has a next step into a real conversation. That is what we can measure. I’m not saying this has caused closings yet; I’m showing you the path we can test.

Do not imply a result that has not happened.

### 02:25–03:10: Explain the honest hook

> The hook isn’t that people are behind or that buying is easy. It’s that they may be closer than they think once someone shows them the actual payment, cash, and home options.

Keep Jen positioned as the guide who makes the numbers understandable.

### 03:10–04:00: Reveal the reusable engine

> Then I pulled back and looked at what was making this repeatable. For another agent, it becomes one forwardable kit built around a single listing. This example is fictional. The system is real.

Do not call it an agency. It is one bounded service with one clear output.

### 04:00–05:05: Make Listing Launch tangible

> For another agent, this becomes one kit: the shoot sheet, three reel scripts, the captions and carousel, a story and DM sequence, plus a fair-housing language pass. They can film it with a phone instead of figuring out the whole campaign from scratch.

If Jen wants detail, open the fictional sample. Otherwise keep moving.

### 05:05–05:55: State the founding test

> I’m not asking you to sell this. I want to offer three founding kits at $200, learn from real use, and keep your name out of it unless you approve. If the work is useful and agents pay, then I’ll know there is something worth refining.

The immediate revenue hypothesis is three paid kits = **$600**. It is a target, not a projection. The $450 standard price is a later hypothesis; mention it only if Jen asks.

### 05:55–07:00: Ask, then stop

> That’s it. I want your honest reaction before anything moves. Which piece would you actually post first, and what line doesn’t sound like you? Is this worth a small live First Home Valley pilot? And is it okay for me to privately show the fictional Listing Launch sample to three agents you trust?

Stop talking. Capture her exact language. A “not yet” is evidence, not an objection to argue against.

## The three greenlights

1. **Work + voice:** Which First Home Valley piece should lead, and what needs to sound more like Jen?
2. **Jen pilot:** Does she want a small live First Home Valley channel test measured by conversations?
3. **Agent permission:** May the fictional Listing Launch sample be shown privately to three agents she trusts?

## Boundaries for this draft

- No use of Jen’s name, endorsement, or network without explicit approval.
- The Hazeltine property and all figures in the Listing Launch sample are fictional.
