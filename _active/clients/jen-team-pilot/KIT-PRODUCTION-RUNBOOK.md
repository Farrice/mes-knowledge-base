# Kit Production Runbook — one paid agent, start to ship

For the Cowork session that builds kits. Pair with `COWORK-CREATIVE-BRIEF.md`
(the why and the standards); this file is the how, in order.

Target: ~2 working hours from payment to delivered package. 48-hour promise.

---

## Session setup (once per Cowork session)

Attach, in this order:

1. `COWORK-CREATIVE-BRIEF.md` — mission, standards, guardrails
2. `AGENT-INTAKE.md` — the two intake forms
3. `poc-carousels/CLAUDE-DESIGN-HANDOFF.md` — carousel master prompt + reference deck
4. `agents/jen-brand-card.md` — worked example of a finished brand card
5. `demo-kit/SAMPLE-KIT.md` — worked example of a finished kit

Then create a folder for the agent: `agents/<firstname-lastname>/`. Everything for
that agent lives there: `brand-card.md`, `research.md`, `KIT.md`, `SEND.md`.

---

## Step 0 — Payment lands (5 min)

Log it in `proof/PROOF-LEDGER.csv` (payment column) and capture the before-state:
screenshot their current grid and profile now, before anything changes. That
screenshot is half of the case study later.

Send both intake forms in one text — Part A (brand, once per agent) and Part B
(this listing). Nothing else happens until Part B comes back.

## Step 1 — Brand card (20 min)

Read their last 15 posts. With their intake answers, write
`agents/<name>/brand-card.md` containing:

- **Voice**: register (calm-warm / authority / mixed), sentence rhythm, casing
  habits, emoji use, how they open and close, their own recurring phrases
- **Their words**: 3-5 verbatim lines from their captions or reviews — quoted
  exactly, never smoothed. This is what keeps scripts sounding like them.
- **Design tokens**: 4-6 colors (from logo, site, or the direction they picked),
  2 typefaces max with roles, the single accent and its rule
- **Market + niche + buyer**, primary platform
- **Their refusals**: anything they said they won't say or do on camera

If they chose "show me 3 directions" on colors: build three genuinely different
boards (different type treatment, palette, and layout logic — not three tints of
one idea), send as one image, they reply 1 / 2 / 3. No production before that pick.

**Gate:** an unanswered brand fact is asked, never invented.

## Step 2 — Demand research (25 min)

Live research on their market this month, with sources. Produce
`agents/<name>/research.md`:

- Buyer searches — the actual questions being asked
- Seller searches
- Relocation searches — the phrase plus the worry underneath it
- This month's local market news
- **The five questions nobody local is answering** — the priority queue

Every item carries a source. No claim from memory. These findings drive the hooks,
so weak research produces a generic kit.

## Step 3 — Build the five components (60-75 min)

Into `agents/<name>/KIT.md`, in this order:

1. **Shoot Sheet** — a table: shot number, where, the shot, what to say or capture.
   ~10 shots covering all three reels plus stories in ONE walkthrough. Structure:
   exterior agent open → detail tilt-ups → room wides → walking CTA close. Note
   blue-hour scheduling for luxury inventory.
2. **Teleprompter Pack** — 3 scripts, word for word, in their voice. Each opens by
   naming a real objection in eight words or fewer, then flips it with ONE proof
   point. Numbers and proper nouns, not adjectives. Map each script to its shots.
3. **Caption + Hook Set** — 2 caption options per reel, plus a carousel outline.
   Build the carousel graphics with the master prompt from the Claude Design
   handoff, swapping in THEIR brand card tokens.
4. **Stories Sequence** — 5 frames, ending in a keyword CTA that matches their
   voice ("DM me TOUR").
5. **Don't-Say List** — the fair-housing screen, presented as part of the product.

**Craft gate before moving on:** would this read as studio work or as default AI
output? One dominant idea per graphic, max 2 fonts, one accent used sparingly,
stats drawn as shapes, square corners, no stock photos or emoji as design elements.
If it looks generic, rebuild it — the caliber gap is what they paid for.

## Step 4 — Compliance pass (10 min)

Screen every line — scripts, captions, stories, slide copy — against:

- No describing the people ("perfect for families / couples / professionals")
- No "safe," "low-crime," "good neighborhood"
- No schools on camera, even positively
- No religious or demographic landmarks as selling points
- No invented urgency; real interest gets named precisely
- No monthly-payment figures without a lender's quote, labeled as such
- Market stats carry a source line and a sample-figures hedge

A flagged line gets rewritten, not softened. Then write the don't-say list into
the kit for when they improvise.

## Step 5 — Package and send (15 min)

Write `agents/<name>/SEND.md`: ONE forwardable message, reader-only language, zero
operator jargon. Structure: what's attached in one line · the five components as a
short list · what to do first (film the shoot sheet) · the don't-say note · a
closing line offering to tighten their raw take.

Farrice sends it. Never auto-send.

## Step 6 — Follow-up (10 min, across a week)

- **Day 3:** "filmed yet? send me the raw take and I'll tighten it."
- **Day 7:** ask for the results screenshot and the one-sentence feedback the
  founding rate was traded for.

Populate `proof/PROOF-LEDGER.csv` at each beat: delivery, use, 24h results, 7d
results, feedback, repeat or referral. A kit that ships without its ledger row is
half-delivered — that row is what sells kit #4.

---

## Ship gate (all five, before anything leaves)

1. Every script passes the voice test: would this agent say it out loud?
2. Every fact carries a source or a hedge; nothing asserted from memory.
3. Compliance screen run on all copy; don't-say list included.
4. Design meets the craft bar, in THEIR brand — never Farrice's.
5. The send message is reader-only: no workflow names, no internal language.

## Scope tripwires (say the line, don't absorb the work)

- "Can you post it for me?" → "That's the monthly engine. The kit gets you the
  package; posting stays yours."
- "Can you do my other three listings too?" → each listing is its own kit.
- "Can you manage my account / DMs?" → not this SKU.
- Endless revisions → one refinement pass on the raw take is included; a rebuild is
  a new kit.
