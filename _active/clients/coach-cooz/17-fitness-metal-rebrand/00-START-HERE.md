# Acusio Bivona website reset

## The decision

Build the next site under **Acusio Bivona**, with **fitness and nutrition coaching** as the offer and heavy metal as the cultural signature.

Do not rename the current business Heavy Metal Gym yet. That is Acusio's long-term destination: a small, community-led gym where fitness and metal belong together. The site should begin building that world now without implying that the gym already exists.

The immediate commercial job is narrower: help Acusio add a small number of in-person clients while protecting his work with Vital and giving him a public identity he can live in for years.

## What Acusio is leaving

- The Resurrection Coach as his public identity
- Trauma and rock-bottom stories as the price of making content
- The male executive recovery offer and $1,000 audit funnel
- Abstract performance language such as "system architecture," "dirty fuel," and "reactivating the machine"
- A brand that keeps Acusio emotionally tied to a chapter he considers complete

He is not disowning that history. He no longer wants to market from inside it.

## What he is choosing

- Straightforward, high-quality fitness and nutrition coaching
- High-touch, in-person work and real human connection
- A calm business with enough income, manageable stress, and room to do excellent work
- Local recognition before internet scale
- Black and gold carried forward in a less generic, more personal visual system
- Heavy metal as community, taste, and future direction
- Two additional in-person clients as the near-term target stated in the August conversation

## Brand architecture

| Layer | Decision | Role now |
|---|---|---|
| Public identity | Acusio Bivona | Stable umbrella that survives future offer changes |
| Descriptor | Fitness & Nutrition Coach | Immediately understandable category |
| Revenue offer | One-on-one in-person coaching | The offer Acusio wants and is permitted to grow |
| Core promise | Get stronger. Feel better. Build a body that fits your life. | Broad, useful, claim-safe result language |
| Cultural signature | Serious coaching. Heavy music. Real people. | Makes the brand his without excluding non-metal clients |
| Future destination | Heavy Metal Gym | A long-range community vision, not a current facility claim |
