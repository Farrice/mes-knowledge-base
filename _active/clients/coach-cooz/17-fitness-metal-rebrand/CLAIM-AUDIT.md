## Net impression

A reasonable reader will understand the page as an offer for personalized fitness and nutrition coaching. The copy does not suggest diagnosis, disease treatment, psychotherapy, medical care, guaranteed body change, or a substitute for any of those services.

## Verdict

**CLEAR AS A DRAFT.** The page has no health-claim hard stop. Location, offer scope, direct-reply workflow, experience wording, and current availability remain publication checks.

The old live copy should not be carried forward. In particular, remove statements that dismiss therapy, imply that Acusio can resolve exhaustion or cognitive symptoms, or present coaching as system repair for a malfunctioning body.
