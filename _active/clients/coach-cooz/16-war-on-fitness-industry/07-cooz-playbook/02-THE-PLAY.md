# THE PLAY

**After this doc:** the booking page tells the true thing instead of the almost-true thing, the flyer stays exactly as it is, and you've got a scoreboard on your phone that says print more or stop.

You were right about the burnout. Right about written proof. The idea was right. One sentence was off. Everything below is that one fix, applied.

One thing before you start: your booking page works. The booking button works. A lead can book a Triage on it today. The /book-in-person page mentioned at the bottom is a separate, later project. It doesn't hold up anything below.

### Say the true thing. Pick your line and lock it in.

Why it matters: "No one cares about abs" is wrong. A guy who just scanned your card will feel it. He cares about abs. He just can't say that out loud — admitting "I want to be wanted" feels vain. Tell him he doesn't care and he hears a coach who doesn't get him. Line A becomes the headline at the top of the page. Cards 3 and 4 below are the body copy underneath it. Get the top line right first.

Do it (5 min, alone): [ ] Read the three lines below out loud, one at a time
                       [ ] Notice which one sounds like something you'd actually say to a guy's face
                       [ ] We're building the page around Line A. If it doesn't sound like you, text Farrice before it locks in.

- **A. "Come for the body. Stay because you stopped hating the guy in the mirror."** ← the pick. Names the pain and the proof in one breath.
- **B. "You don't have to hate yourself to look good."** The permission line. Keeps showing up in Card 3 and Card 4 below.
- **C. "The body's the packaging. Feeling like yourself is what you paid for."** Retired. Too soft on a cold scanner.

Done when: you can say Line A out loud and it sounds like you, not like a strategist wrote it for you.

### Save this scoreboard to your phone before you print a single flyer

Why it matters: this tells you print more or stop. No waiting on a gut feeling.

Do it (10 min, alone): [ ] Screenshot the table below
                        [ ] Save it in Photos or Notes. Title it "Flyer Scoreboard."
                        [ ] After every batch of 500 cards, check your real numbers against it

| Stage | What you're counting | Stop and fix | Print more |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scans | QR scans per cards handed out | under 3% | 8% or higher |
| Clicks | scans that hit the booking button | under 15% | 30% or higher |
| Bookings | cards that turn into a booked Triage | under 0.6% (3 of 500) | 1% or higher (5 of 500) |
| Closes | Triage sessions that become 90-Day clients | under 30% | 50% or higher |

Scans and Closes are our best guess for the first batch. Real numbers from the first 500 lock in the real target.

Two batches of 500 in a row under 3 bookings means the page or the booking link is broken, not the flyer. Stop and fix it there. One batch clearing 5 bookings with at least 1 close means print the next run.

Done when: the table is saved in your Photos or Notes, titled "Flyer Scoreboard."
