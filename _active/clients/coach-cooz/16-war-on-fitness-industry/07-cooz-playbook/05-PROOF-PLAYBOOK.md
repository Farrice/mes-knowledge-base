### Start the weekly ask with Celine and the wedding couple this week
Why it matters: every quote above came from a client who already left. The next one only exists if you ask this week, not someday.

Do it (5 min, once a week, per client):
- [ ] Text or voice memo this line, verbatim, same day every week: "Quick one. How'd you feel this week? Not the workout. The whole week. One or two sentences, your words. Give me the real one, not the polished one."

### Say this when someone calls your proof fake
Why it matters: a photo can be lit and starved into anything. Forty-two testimonials naming their own cancer, their own gym-hatred, their own bad weeks, can't fake that the same way.

Do it (2 min, alone):
- [ ] Read the 3 sentences below out loud once.
- [ ] Say them again to a client or a friend this week until they're memory, not a script.

Done when: you can say all 3 without looking.

> A photo can be lit, dehydrated, and timed to look like anything you want. Nobody fakes naming their own cancer, or admitting they hate the gym, on the way to the mirror. Forty-two testimonials said it in their own words, and not one of them was selling anything for me.
