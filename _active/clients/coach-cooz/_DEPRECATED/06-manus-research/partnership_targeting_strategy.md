### 3. Partnership Presentation Strategy

#### Initial Meeting Approach
- Begin with questions about partner's business goals and challenges
- Listen actively for alignment opportunities
- Present partnership concept as solution to identified needs
- Focus on mutual benefit rather than one-sided advantage
- Use visual aids showing transformation methodology
- Share relevant success stories and testimonials
- Propose clear, actionable next steps

#### Objection Handling Preparation
- **"We don't have budget for this"**
  - Response: "This partnership requires minimal financial investment. The primary investment is collaborative promotion and space for workshops."

- **"We've tried partnerships before without success"**
  - Response: "I understand. That's why I propose starting with a single workshop to demonstrate value before expanding the relationship."

- **"Our customers/members aren't interested in transformation coaching"**
  - Response: "The workshop focuses on practical strategies anyone can implement, not on selling services. We can customize content to address specific interests of your audience."

- **"We don't have space/time for additional programming"**
  - Response: "We can work within your existing schedule constraints and adapt format to available space."

#### Follow-Up Protocol
- Send thank you email within 24 hours of meeting
- Include summary of discussion points and agreed next steps
- Attach formal proposal document
- Schedule specific follow-up date
- Maintain regular contact during decision period

## Risk Management & Contingency Planning

### Potential Challenges
1. **Difficulty Securing Initial Meetings**
   - Contingency: Utilize warm introductions through network
   - Contingency: Visit locations in person for direct introduction
   - Contingency: Expand to Tier 2 and 3 partners more quickly

2. **Workshop Scheduling Delays**
   - Contingency: Offer flexible timing options
   - Contingency: Propose alternative formats (shorter, virtual)
   - Contingency: Create recorded content as alternative

3. **Low Workshop Attendance**
   - Contingency: Enhance promotion through multiple channels
   - Contingency: Offer attendance incentives
   - Contingency: Adjust content for smaller group format

4. **Partner Hesitation on Formal Agreement**
   - Contingency: Extend trial period with clear metrics
   - Contingency: Adjust partnership structure based on feedback
   - Contingency: Demonstrate value through additional free content
