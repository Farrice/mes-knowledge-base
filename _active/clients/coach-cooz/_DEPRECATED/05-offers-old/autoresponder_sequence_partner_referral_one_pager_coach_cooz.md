# Partner Referral One‑Pager — For Therapists & Divorce Professionals

**Who We Help**  
Men in a 30–60 day acute‑life‑crisis window (divorce, separation, job loss, relapse scares, health scares) who need an **action‑first, faith‑friendly** structure alongside therapy or legal proceedings.

**What We Do (Coaching, not therapy)**  
A **body‑led recovery program** that installs daily anchors (sleep, hydration, breath, strength, nutrition) with **accountability** so clients regain self‑trust quickly. We coordinate with clinicians and legal pros when appropriate.

**Programs & Timing**  
- **Emergency Stabilization (6 weeks):** daily text check‑ins, weekly 45‑min calls, “First 48” protocol, warm referrals.  
- **90‑Day Resurrection:** structured rebuild—training, nutrition, identity habits, optional faith support.  
- **Brotherhood (ongoing):** weekly group call + challenges.

**Fit Checklist — Refer When Your Client…**  
- Can’t sleep/eat consistently; mornings feel impossible.  
- Is ruminating/panicking and needs **immediate structure**.  
- Lacks day‑to‑day anchors to implement your plan.  
- Would benefit from faith‑friendly support (optional).

**Scope & Boundaries**  
- We provide **coaching & accountability**, not diagnosis or therapy.  
- We do not manage medications or legal strategy.
