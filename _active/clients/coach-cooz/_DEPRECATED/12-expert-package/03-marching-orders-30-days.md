## HOW TO READ A DAY

Every day has a table that looks like this:

| Time Block | Activity | Exact Actions | Deliverable | Time |
|-----------|---------|---------------|-------------|------|

- **Time Block** = when to start and stop
- **Activity** = what bucket of work this is
- **Exact Actions** = the literal steps to execute
- **Deliverable** = what exists in the world when you're done
- **Time** = how long it should take (not to the minute, but the order of magnitude)

If a day has fewer blocks, that's intentional. Some days are light. Don't pad with busy work.

### 15-Minute Call Protocol (use this verbatim)

**Before the call (2 min)**: Open their LinkedIn profile. Read their bio. Note 2 things about their business.

**Call structure**:
1. **Minutes 0-2** — "Hey [Name], thanks for making time. Before we jump in, give me the 60-second version of what you're building right now." → Listen.
2. **Minutes 2-10** — "Can I ask you something direct? On a 1-10, how's your body holding up right now — sleep, energy, weight, all of it?" → Listen more than you talk. If they give you a low number → "What's one thing you know you should be doing that you're not?" → Listen.

### Triage Audit Delivery Protocol (use this)

**Structure (90 minutes)**:
- **0-10 min**: Rapport + context. "Walk me through your week — from Sunday night to Saturday morning. Don't edit."
- **10-30 min**: Fuel architecture deep dive. What he eats, when, how much, the honest version. Coffee count. Water intake. Meal timing. Snacks (especially the ones he doesn't want to name).
- **30-50 min**: Sleep + recovery. Bedtime. Wake time. Wakeups in the night. Bedroom environment. Screens. Partner sleep. Alcohol. Morning feel on a 1-10.
- **50-70 min**: Movement + stress. Workout history. Current activity. 2 PM and 4 PM energy patterns. Weekly stress peaks. 2 AM Slack pattern.
- **70-85 min**: Your synthesis. "Here's what I heard. Here's what's actually going on. Here are the 3 leverage points." **Be direct.** This is what he's paying for.
- **85-90 min**: The offer. "Here's the Resurrection Protocol. 90 days. $1,500-$2,500 (whatever your current price is). The $497 from today applies. Want me to send you the agreement?"

**After the audit**: Send him a 1-page written summary within 24 hours. The blueprint. That's the deliverable he's paying for on paper — the offer is the deliverable he's paying for in reality.

# THE EMERGENCY PROTOCOL

**When to use this**: Things went sideways. No engagement on a post. Someone said no harshly. A call fell through. You feel burnout creeping back. You want to quit.

**Here are the 3 steps, in order**:

### Step 1 — NAME IT (5 minutes)
Open a blank note. Write one sentence: "Right now I feel [X] because [Y]." Examples:
- "Right now I feel embarrassed because my LinkedIn post got 2 reactions."
- "Right now I feel defeated because [person] said no."
- "Right now I feel tired because I've been doing this for 11 days and nothing is closing."

Don't elaborate. One sentence. Close the note.

### Step 2 — DO ONE THING FROM THE DAILY DEFAULT (30 minutes)
Pick ONE item from the Daily Default list above. Just one. Most likely: send 28 DMs. Action breaks the spiral. Don't try to do all 4 — just one.

### Step 3 — SABBATH-LIKE REST, SAME DAY (rest of the day)
Close the laptop. Don't check anything. Walk. Eat. Call your wife. Be a human. Come back tomorrow and execute the next day's marching orders like it never happened.

**Non-negotiable rule of the Emergency Protocol**: You do NOT quit the program. You do NOT "rethink everything." You do ONE thing, then you rest, then you show up tomorrow. That's the entire protocol.

**What to NEVER do in an emergency**:
- Re-read the strategic dossier "to remember why you're doing this" (that's procrastination in disguise)
- Redesign the offer
- Rewrite your LinkedIn bio
- Ask Farrice to change the plan
- Post something emotional on LinkedIn about "being real about the struggle"

Those are all ways of staying in the spiral. The only way out is through a small action.

# THE MENTAL POSTURE RULES

**These are not mindset quotes. These are operating rules for how you show up.**

### Rule 1 — The 14-Day Rule
> **"If you catch yourself thinking 'this isn't working' before Day 14, you're cheating. It takes 14 days minimum for any pattern to show."**

Before Day 14, the data is noise. You haven't run enough reps. The DMs you sent on Day 3 are probably being answered on Day 11. The LinkedIn post from Day 7 is still being surfaced to someone on Day 13. You don't have the right to interpret the data until Day 14. Before then, you just execute.

### Rule 2 — Ship Over Polish
> **"A 7/10 post published today is worth more than a 10/10 post 'coming next week.' Shipping beats polishing. Every time."**

When you open the content pack, you have 30-45 minutes to edit and publish. If you find yourself at 60 minutes still tweaking, STOP — ship what you have and move on. The polish is a trap. The rhythm is the point.

### Rule 3 — One Decision Before 9 AM
> **"The only decision you're allowed to make before 9 AM is what to eat for breakfast. Everything else is already decided — it's in this doc."**

You don't wake up and decide whether to do the DMs. You wake up and do the DMs because the doc says so. Optionality before 9 AM is the trap that has stalled you for months. The doc takes the decision away on purpose.

### Rule 4 — The DM Is A Conversation, Not A Pitch
> **"Every DM you send is a conversation with one human. Not a cold outreach metric. If you wouldn't send the message to a friend, don't send it."**

When you're in the DM block and it starts to feel transactional, stop, take a breath, and read the next name out loud. Remember he's a real person. Then write the message like you would to that real person. This is how you avoid the "generic DM" spiral that kills reply rates.

### Rule 5 — Sabbath Is Sacred, Not Optional
> **"Sunday off is not a reward for a good week. It's a commitment regardless of the week. The tired version of you that wants to 'catch up' on Sunday is the version that will burn out by Day 21. Protect him from himself."**

The Sabbath you take on Day 7 protects the man you need to be on Day 14. The Sabbath on Day 14 protects Day 21. The system only works if the rest days are real. No exceptions.
