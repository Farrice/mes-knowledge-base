# Coach Cooz — LinkedIn Warm Network Outreach Strategy
## WS3 Deliverable | April 2026

---

## The Mission

DM every one of his 837 LinkedIn connections by end of April 2026.
Goal: Start conversations, not pitch. 3-5 qualified conversations per week.

---

## Daily System (30 min/day)

| Time Block | Activity | Volume |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| 10 min | Engage on others' posts (genuine comments, not "great post!") | 5-10 comments |
| 15 min | Send DMs (from templates below) | 10-15 messages |
| 5 min | Respond to any replies from yesterday | All |

**At 15 messages/day, he covers all 837 connections in ~56 days (8 weeks).**
To hit end of April, he needs 30-35/day — which is aggressive but doable at ~45 min/day.

---

## Connection Segmentation

Before blasting, Acusio should spend 1-2 hours sorting his connections into these buckets:

### Tier 1: Potential Clients (DM with Conversation Starter)
- Men 30-55 who work in business/leadership roles
- Founders, execs, professionals with high-stress titles
- Anyone who's ever engaged with his content
- People whose profiles mention fitness, health, or "balance"

### Tier 2: Referral Sources (DM with Reconnection)
- Other coaches, trainers, wellness professionals
- People in adjacent spaces (executive coaching, leadership dev, therapy)
- Anyone with large networks who might refer

### Tier 3: Engagement Allies (DM with Genuine Check-In)
- Friends, acquaintances, people he actually knows
- Other content creators
- Past colleagues from any context

### Tier 4: Cold/Unknown (Light Touch)
- Connections he doesn't recognize
- People who accepted his request but never interacted
- Generic/inactive profiles — skip or very light message

## DM Templates

### Template 1: The Genuine Reconnect (Tier 1 & 3)
> Hey [Name] — been a while since we connected on here. I've been heads down building something new and realized I haven't been great about actually talking to the people in my network. What are you working on these days?

**Why it works:** No pitch. No agenda. Just a human being a human. The conversation opens naturally.

### Template 2: The Specific Compliment (Tier 1 — people who post)
> [Name] — just saw your post about [specific topic]. That part about [specific detail] really resonated. Curious — how long have you been thinking about that?

**Why it works:** Shows he actually read their content. Creates a real conversation thread.

### Template 3: The Shared Experience (Tier 1 — founders/leaders)
> Hey [Name] — noticed you're running [company/role]. I've been working with a lot of guys in similar positions lately and one thing keeps coming up: the gap between how well the business is doing and how the person running it actually feels. Has that ever been your experience?

**Why it works:** This is a diagnostic question, not a pitch. If they say yes, the conversation naturally moves toward what Cooz does. If they say no, it's still a real conversation.

### Template 4: The Re-Engagement (Tier 3 — people he knows)
> [Name]! Man, I was just scrolling through my connections and realized I haven't reached out in way too long. Life's been intense — lots of pivots and growth. How are you doing? What's new?

**Why it works:** Warm, honest, no pretense.

### Template 5: The Podcast Drop (Tier 1 & 2 — timely, while episode is fresh)
> Hey [Name] — I just did a podcast episode about [topic from the episode] and it made me think of you because [specific reason]. If you have 15 minutes, I'd love your take on it: [link]

**Why it works:** Shares value, creates a reason for follow-up, positions him as someone who's doing things (podcast guest = authority signal).

### Template 6: The Referral Ask (Tier 2 — other coaches/professionals)
> Hey [Name] — hope you're doing well. Quick question: I work with high-achieving men who've let their physical health slide while building their careers. If you ever come across someone who's dealing with that — founder type, successful but running on fumes — I'd love an introduction. And same goes the other way — who's your ideal client? Happy to keep an eye out.

**Why it works:** Peer-to-peer. Reciprocal. Specific enough to be actionable.

## The Follow-Up Sequence (After Initial Reply)

### If they engage in conversation:
- Keep it conversational for 2-3 exchanges
- Listen more than you talk
- Look for the opening: "Yeah, I've been dealing with that too" or "I need to get back in shape"
- When the opening appears: "That's literally what I do. I work with guys in your exact situation. Want to hop on a quick call and I'll tell you what I'd do differently?"

### If they go quiet after initial reply:
- Wait 3-5 days
- Send something of value (a post, an article, a podcast clip) with: "Thought of you when I saw this"
- No follow-up pitch. Just stay present.

### If they don't reply at all:
- Move on. Don't send a follow-up for at least 2-3 weeks
- Instead, engage with their content (if they post) for a week, then try again

## The Conversation Bridge (From DM to Call)

When someone shows interest, Acusio should NOT send them to an application form. The form killed 26 leads last time. Instead:

> "I'd love to learn more about your situation. I do a thing called a Triage Audit — basically a 90-minute deep dive where I map out exactly what's going on with your energy, nutrition, recovery, and daily routine. It's $1,000 and that rolls into the full protocol if we decide to work together. But honestly? Let's just hop on a 15-minute call first and see if it even makes sense. No pressure. When's good this week?"

**Key elements:**
- Mentions the Triage Audit (establishes it's not free coaching)
- Says the $1,000 rolls into the protocol (reduces perceived risk)
- But FIRST offers a free 15-min call (low friction entry)
- "No pressure" — peer energy, not sales energy

## Daily Engagement (Non-DM)

Beyond DMs, Cooz should be visible in his network daily:

1. **Comment on 5-10 posts per day** — real, substantive comments. Not "love this!" but "This reminds me of something I see with my clients — the gap between the performance metrics looking great and the person behind them running on empty. Have you found anything that actually helps?"

2. **React to 20-30 posts per day** — fast, shows presence in feeds

3. **Share 1 post per day** (once the content batch is ready) — the flywheel content we produce

4. **Check "My Network" notifications** — congratulate promotions, new jobs, work anniversaries. These are natural DM openers.

---

## What NOT to Do

- Do NOT send the same message to everyone (LinkedIn flags this)
- Do NOT pitch in the first message. Ever.
- Do NOT send voice notes as a first message (save for follow-ups with warm leads)
- Do NOT use LinkedIn Sales Navigator language ("I noticed you're a decision-maker at...")
- Do NOT send connection requests with a pitch attached
- Do NOT follow up more than twice if someone doesn't respond
