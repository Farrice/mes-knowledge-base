## What the Flywheel Does

Turns Acusio's natural speech and real experiences into a consistent stream of
LinkedIn content that builds authority, drives engagement, and starts conversations.

**Input:** 1 voice memo per week (15-20 min)
**Output:** 5 LinkedIn posts + engagement strategy per week
**Operator:** Farrice (extraction + translation) / AI system (draft production)
**Quality gate:** Every post checked against voice-calibration-real.md

## Content Pillar Architecture

### Pillar 1: The Front Door (40% of content)
**Theme:** What it feels like to be successful and depleted
**Angle:** Scene-based recognition — paint the reader's life so accurately they feel seen
**Topics:**
- Walking through the door with nothing left
- The 3 PM crash
- Sunday night dread
- Snapping at your wife/kids
- The doctor visit you keep postponing
- The mirror moment

**Why this pillar works:** It's the entry point. Before anyone cares about your protocol, they need to feel understood. This pillar does zero selling. It builds the "this guy gets me" trust.

### Pillar 2: Body First (25% of content)
**Theme:** Why physical health is the foundation of everything
**Angle:** Reframes that challenge mindset-first culture
**Topics:**
- Why mindset coaches start in the wrong place
- Fix the hardware, then the software runs
- Sleep is not optional — it's your operating system's reset
- Nutrition isn't a diet — it's energy management
- The caffeine trap
- What happens to your decision-making when your body is depleted

**Why this pillar works:** This is his unique positioning. Every other coach starts with purpose/vision/mindset. He starts with the body. That's the differentiator.

### Pillar 3: The Resurrection Story (20% of content)
**Theme:** Acusio's own journey + client transformations
**Angle:** Confession + proof — vulnerability backed by results
**Topics:**
- His quarterback dormancy story
- The season he almost quit
- What his wife taught him
- Brian's 90-day transformation
- New client progress (ongoing)
- Lessons from his own rock bottom

**Why this pillar works:** People buy from people they trust, and trust comes from vulnerability. This pillar humanizes him and builds the emotional connection that the jargon never could.

### Pillar 4: The Invitation (15% of content)
**Theme:** What working with him actually looks like
**Angle:** Demystify the offer — show the process, not just the promise
**Topics:**
- What happens in the first week of the protocol
- What the Triage Audit actually covers
- A real meal plan for a guy who travels 3 days a week
- What "body-first" coaching looks like day-to-day
- FAQ/objection handling in content form
- Podcast recaps + guest appearance highlights

**Why this pillar works:** This is where passive followers become active leads. But it only works AFTER pillars 1-3 have built trust. That's why it's only 15%.

## Format Mix

| Format | Frequency | Best For |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Story post** (200-400 words) | 2-3x/week | Pillars 1 & 3 — scenes, confessions |
| **Insight post** (100-200 words) | 1-2x/week | Pillar 2 — reframes, body-first education |
| **Carousel/list** (5-8 slides or bullets) | 1x/week max | Pillar 4 — tactical content, "5 things" |
| **Photo + short caption** | 1x/week | Pillar 3 — gym shots, client moments, behind-the-scenes |
| **Video clip** (30-90 sec) | When available | Any pillar — repurpose from YouTube/podcast |

**NO LinkedIn articles** unless it's a monthly deep dive. Articles tank engagement compared to native posts. We learned this in March.

**NO double-posting** the same content in two formats. One topic = one post.

## CTA Strategy

**Rule: Never use the same CTA on consecutive posts.**

Rotation pool (see voice-calibration-real.md for full list):
1. End with a question (no sell)
2. End with a confession (vulnerability close)
3. End with a challenge (try this for one week)
4. Soft invite (link to podcast/resource)
5. Direct invite (limited — 1x per week max)
6. No CTA (let the story breathe)

**The "DM Resurrection" CTA is permanently retired.**

## Voice Memo Prompts for Acusio

When Acusio sends his weekly voice memo, he can talk about ANYTHING. But if he's stuck, here are prompts:

1. "Tell me about a session with a client this week. What happened?"
2. "What's something you believe about health that most coaches get wrong?"
3. "Describe a moment from your own life when you realized your body was failing you."
4. "What question do people always ask you, and what's your honest answer?"
5. "Talk about a time you had to practice what you preach — and it was hard."
6. "What does a typical day look like for one of your clients before they start with you vs. 30 days in?"
7. "What's something you're dealing with right now that your audience would relate to?"

**Key instruction to Acusio:** Don't perform. Don't try to be educational. Just talk like you're explaining something to a friend over coffee. The system handles the translation.

## Engagement Multiplier System

Content alone doesn't build authority. The flywheel needs engagement fuel:

### Daily (15 min alongside the outreach from WS3):
- Comment on 5-10 posts in your niche (executive coaches, health coaches, LinkedIn creators in adjacent spaces)
- Comments should be substantive (2-3 sentences that add value, not "great post!")
- React to 20-30 posts (stay visible in feeds)

### Weekly:
- Respond to EVERY comment on your posts within 24 hours
- Send a "thanks for engaging" DM to anyone who comments multiple times
- Share one piece of someone else's content with a thoughtful take

### Monthly:
- Review engagement metrics: which posts got comments vs. just reactions?
- Double down on topics/formats that drove comments (comments = conversations)
- Drop formats that consistently underperform
