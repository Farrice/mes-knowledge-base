# Coach Cooz Website: Permission-Safe Photography Brief

**Purpose:** Capture one honest asset library that can support The Iron Practice, The Long Set, or One Coach, In Frame without choosing a direction.  
**State:** Preproduction only; no direction selected  
**Primary subject:** Acusio Bivona  
**Setting:** the real North Hollywood training environment, subject to property permission  
**Story:** one coach paying close attention while real work happens.

## The non-generic solution

The shoot has two layers:

- **Common asset spine:** neutral masters that every direction can use.
- **Three short direction modules:** a few deliberately different frames captured during the same session. They preserve creative range without pretending a winner has been chosen.

The neutral masters receive a natural grade. Direction-specific color grading happens only after selection, from the same RAW files.

## Truth and permission locks

1. Photograph Acusio coaching an actual current client during a real or accurately reconstructed session.
2. If no client signs a release, shoot Acusio alone, the room, and equipment. Do not substitute a friend and imply a coaching relationship.
3. A photograph cannot prove relationship length, results, executive status, or a testimonial. Those claims remain in the proof ledger and require separate permission.
4. No before-and-after pairing, body-composition claim, or transformation caption without specific written consent for that exact use.
5. Do not imply that Heavy Metal Gym exists. Avoid branded facility claims, invented signage, or staged community scale.
6. Bystanders stay out of frame or remain unrecognizable. Gym logos, client screens, notes, and personal data must be cleared or obscured.
7. Existing Coach Cooz images remain `PARTIAL` until final usage permissions are confirmed; this brief does not silently invalidate or approve them.

## Cast and environment

### Required

- Acusio in normal coaching clothes: black, charcoal, bone, or muted neutral; no dominant unapproved logo.
- One current client only if they freely consent to the session and intended usage.
- The actual equipment Acusio uses. No trophy props, fake plans, or weights chosen for spectacle.

### Avoid

- Influencer poses and shirtless hero defaults. Do not spray sweat, fake chalk clouds, ask anyone to roar, or stage drill-sergeant behavior.
- Matching outfits that make the scene resemble a group-class campaign.
- Empty luxury-gym glamour, generic executives, staged trauma, or anonymous synthetic bodies.
- Prominent album art, band marks, or clothing graphics that create a separate licensing problem.

## Three modules from the same shoot

### Module A: The Iron Practice

- Three tight frames: plate edge and hand, program detail, Acusio's focused profile.
- Hard side light or controlled rim light; deeper shadows, visible texture, no colored gels.
- Rectilinear crops and one frame with poster-ready negative space.

### Module B: The Long Set

- Three open frames: coaching action, between-set exchange, paired coach/client context.
- Soft directional daylight or a large diffused key at roughly 4500–5000K.
- Bone/neutral background opportunities and clean central negative space for an inquiry unit.

### Module C: One Coach, In Frame

- Three portrait frames: direct Acusio portrait, seated environment portrait, quiet close.
- Low-key Rembrandt setup with a warm 4200–4600K key and controlled black detail.
- One centered frame and two off-center frames with black negative space.

These modules are capture variations, not approval of A, B, or C.

## Camera and capture rules

- 35mm for environmental truth, 50mm for human interaction, 85mm for portrait and detail.
- Minimum `1/500s` for moving sets; `1/250s` for gestures and portraits; choose aperture for readable action, usually `f/2.8–f/4`.
- Protect skin tone and highlight detail. Do not crush blacks in-camera; the dark directions need recoverable shadow information.
- Capture RAW plus full-resolution JPEG contact sheets.
- For every priority scene, make horizontal 3:2, vertical 4:5, and 9:16-safe options. Leave headline space on both left and right across the session.
- Keep white balance consistent within each setup. Avoid mixed green fluorescent and warm practical light unless deliberately corrected.
- No permanent filter, grain, vignette, or direction-specific color grade on the master files.

## Minimum viable schedule

| Time | Work |
|---:|---|
| 15 min | Property check, privacy sweep, equipment and empty-room frames |
| 20 min | Acusio hero and environmental portraits |
| 45 min | Real coaching sequence with the released client |
| 20 min | Detail, transition, and between-set frames |
| 15 min | A, B, and C module variations, five minutes each |
| 10 min | Missing-crop check and release/shot-ID reconciliation |

Total working time: roughly two hours. If the client cannot participate, use the 45-minute block for Acusio-only movement, equipment setup, plan review, and room interaction. Do not fake client proof.

## Permission ledger

Before the camera starts, record each applicable item as `CLEARED`, `HELD`, or `NOT APPLICABLE`.

| Permission owner | Required decision |
|---|---|
| Acusio | Approval for website, organic social, cropping, color grading, and named identity use. |
| Recognizable client | Written release naming the approved channels; paid-ad use should be a separate explicit choice. |
| Photographer | Ownership or license terms covering web, social, crops, derivative color treatments, duration, and territory. |
| Gym/property | Permission to photograph and publish identifiable interiors, equipment, and any location details. |
| Wardrobe/marks | Approval or removal of prominent third-party logos, band art, and copyrighted graphics. |
| Proof/caption owner | Separate clearance for any name, role, relationship length, result, quote, or transformation claim attached later. |

Use a standard release reviewed by the business owner or counsel. This brief defines the evidence boundary; it is not a substitute for legal review.

## Handoff deliverables

- Contact sheet with stable shot IDs.
- `9` common master selects minimum.
- `3` A-module, `3` B-module, and `3` C-module selects.
- RAW originals plus neutral high-resolution masters in sRGB.
- Web exports at 2400px long edge and responsive 16:9, 4:5, and 9:16 crops for priority scenes.
- Permission ledger mapping every recognizable person and location to allowed usage.
- `HELD` images stored separately from public-ready images. No held file enters the website build folder.

## Acceptance gate

The photography foundation passes only if:

- Acusio is unmistakably the coach, not a generic fitness model;
- at least three frames show attention, observation, or adjustment rather than posing;
- all three direction modules are usable without a reshoot;
- skin, black clothing, and gym shadows retain detail;
- mobile-safe crops exist for the hero and coaching action;
- every recognizable subject and location has a recorded permission state;
- no photograph is presented as proof of a result, relationship length, or community that it cannot establish.

**STOP:** This is a shoot brief, not authorization to contact a photographer, schedule a session, obtain releases, generate images, or select a visual direction.
