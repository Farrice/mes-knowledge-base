## Whole-page coherence

The current preview forms one argument:

1. Acusio is the coach.
2. The visitor wants strength without a second job.
3. Acusio adapts the plan when the week changes.
4. The image shows him in the real training environment.
5. The next move is to email him.

B contributes a useful composition idea, but the complete argument remains unbuilt. Its reference connects activity, community, product UI, and signup. Coach Cooz needs person, continuity, local trust, and inquiry. Replacing the current canvas would trade a coherent identity for an unproven promise of warmth.

## Specific repairs without selecting either

1. **Keep the current black/gold baseline.** Do not perform the light-canvas identity flip. This preserves the built hierarchy and Acusio's existing equity.
2. **Borrow only B's strongest structural move.** Use two permissioned coaching images to frame one concise inquiry or proof unit. This is a bounded composition borrow, not a new brand system.
3. **Move long-relationship evidence earlier.** A proof strip such as approved relationship lengths belongs directly after the hero, so the page differentiates Acusio before generic trainer expectations return.
4. **Repair the mobile first screen.** Reduce display scale enough to reveal either Acusio's face or the primary CTA earlier. Keep the force of the headline without making the first view almost entirely typography.

## Revised recommendation

**The Long Set does not survive the adversarial challenge as a replacement direction.**

The current black/gold preview becomes the recommended baseline, still unchosen. B remains valuable as one bounded composition idea: documentary coaching on both sides of a clear decision. That move should be tested only after the permission-safe photography exists.

## What could reverse this verdict

B earns another comparison only if all three conditions are met:

1. Two strong, permissioned Coach Cooz documentary images exist.
2. A Coach Cooz-specific B hero proves its hierarchy at desktop and mobile, with no Strava-like app or community cues.
3. The light canvas produces a clear identity or inquiry advantage in a rendered comparison, not merely a fresher mood.
