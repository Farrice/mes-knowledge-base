# Coach Cooz Website — Three Refero Directions

**Status:** VISIBLE · UNCHOSEN  
**Mode:** EXPLORE  
**Decision boundary:** Recommendation only. No selection, reference lock, website build, or live-site action has occurred.

## Identity boundary held constant

- Public identity: **Acusio Bivona / Coach Cooz**.
- Offer: **one-to-one, in-person fitness and nutrition coaching in North Hollywood**.
- Buyer: men and business owners with demanding schedules; metal affinity is not an entry requirement.
- Proof posture: emphasize long coaching relationships and ongoing adaptation; do not invent metrics, testimonials, methods, facilities, or a Heavy Metal Gym.
- Existing equity: black, bone/white, and brass-gold; direct human voice; email-first inquiry.

## A — The Iron Practice

**Primary Refero reference:** Riptype Foundry  
**Visual thesis:** Coaching feels like a precise practice: technical, tactile, disciplined, and a little dangerous. Metal culture appears in the grid, typography, and image crops—not skulls, flames, distress filters, or concert-poster cosplay.

**Preserve**

- Acusio as the named coach and human authority.
- One-to-one offer, North Hollywood, demanding-schedule buyer, email-first inquiry.
- Black/gold equity, with gold used sparingly.

**Bounded borrow**

- From **2020 / Album Colors**: zero-radius poster framing and one oversized condensed display move.
- No other music-site behaviors transfer.

**Media strategy**

- Real, permissioned documentary details: Acusio coaching, hands setting a plate, notebook/program detail, a client mid-set, the gym before sunrise.
- Tight crops and high texture; full-bleed only when the composition is strong.
- Until those assets exist, use fixed-ratio labeled shot placeholders—not stock physiques.

**Role rules and token commitments**

- Canvas `#121212`; raised surface `#292929`; primary text `#FFFFFF`; secondary text `#D0D0D0`.
- Existing brass `#C79A48` inherits Riptype's sparse focus role: tags, dividers, selected edges. It does **not** become a gold gradient or constant CTA fill.
- One rational grotesk family; narrow/condensed display only for the poster statement.
- Compact 6px spacing unit; 4px controls or fully pill-shaped controls; thin borders; no heavy shadows.

**Reject**

- Metalhead-only positioning.
- Skulls, flames, chains, distressed overlays, or fake band merch.
- Gold gradients, luxury gloss, and generic black-card layouts.
- Any implication that a Heavy Metal Gym already operates.

**Strength:** Most ownable expression of Acusio's cultural signature.  
**Risk:** If the technical/metal signal leads before the coaching relationship, it narrows the buyer and makes warmth harder to feel.  
**System impact:** Extends the existing black/gold identity but changes composition, density, and energy; no parallel brand system required.

## B — The Long Set

**Primary Refero reference:** Strava  
**Visual thesis:** The site opens on the real relationship: one coach, sustained work, and a life that does not need to revolve around fitness. The page feels active and decisive without selling intensity theater.

**Preserve**

- Acusio's name, local one-to-one format, demanding-schedule buyer, and email-first inquiry.
- Black/gold equity, rebalanced onto a bone/white canvas.
- Long relationships and adaptation as the proof spine.

**Bounded borrow**

- From **amp**: warm, naturally lit fitness photography and restrained neutral section bands.
- Do not borrow amp's device-led product framing, rounded softness, or tech claims.

**Media strategy**

- A three-part opening composition: real training image, concise central decision card, real Acusio/client image.
- Documentary sequences over hero worship: arrival, coaching cue, work, recovery, ordinary life continuity.
- Real permissioned images only; no fake community, stock group class, or fabricated progress data.

**Role rules and token commitments**

- Canvas `#FFFFFF`; section canvas `#F9F8F5`; text `#000000`; secondary text `#43423F`; dividers `#E0E0DE`.
- Existing brass `#C79A48` inherits Strava's accent role: primary CTA, active state, and rare emphasis only.
- Clean practical sans throughout; 32–56px confident headlines, no decorative serif word swaps.
- Comfortable spacing; 4px radii; flat surfaces; hierarchy through borders and canvas shifts, not shadows.

**Reject**

- Beige wellness softness or spa language.
- App-dashboard motifs and fitness tracking claims.
- Fake community scale, invented stats, or generic smiling group photography.
- Orange-plus-gold competition; brass is the sole chromatic action accent.

**Strength:** Clearest expression of buyer, relationship, and inquiry; widens appeal without erasing taste.  
**Risk:** It needs excellent real photography to keep the light canvas from feeling like generic mainstream fitness.  
**System impact:** Preserves identity roles while flipping the dominant canvas; this is the strongest test of whether the brand can feel premium without relying on black everywhere.

## C — One Coach, In Frame

**Primary Refero reference:** Sequel  
**Visual thesis:** A black gallery where Acusio's presence, eye, and judgment carry the page. Premium comes from restraint and portraiture, not from luxury objects or executive-status theater.

**Preserve**

- Current black/bone/gold identity and Acusio-first authority.
- One-to-one, local, schedule-aware offer and email-first inquiry.
- Human coaching relationship as the subject.

**Bounded borrow**

- From **amp**: warm, balanced light on real human subjects.
- Do not borrow product-tech styling, orange accents, or friendly pill saturation.

**Media strategy**

- One definitive Acusio portrait, one coaching-in-action frame, and a small permissioned proof/contact sheet.
- Full-bleed portrait cards with quiet overlays; no synthetic bodies or empty luxury gyms.
- The person must remain legible at mobile crop sizes.

**Role rules and token commitments**

- Canvas `#000000`; secondary surface `#202020`; primary text `#FFFFFF`; muted text `#C0C0C0`; off-white action `#F5F5F0`.
- Brass `#C79A48` is a micro-accent only—small active label or rule. Primary CTA remains off-white, preserving Sequel's neutral action role.
- Sculpted serif for major statements; precise sans for navigation, labels, and body.
- Spacious gallery rhythm; 10px image cards; pill CTAs; restrained shadow only on interactive elements.

**Reject**

- Venture-capital, private-club, or wealth-signaling language.
- A moody founder portrait with no evidence of coaching.
- Decorative serif word swapping, glassmorphism, and glossy gradients.
- Generic life-coach aspiration.

**Strength:** Most premium and most continuous with the current site direction.  
**Risk:** It is the least informative experiment; it can read as fashion/venture luxury and hide the everyday practicality that differentiates Acusio.  
**System impact:** Lowest implementation change and lowest transfer-learning value; it evolves the current canvas rather than proving a new one.

## Distinctness proof

| Axis | A — The Iron Practice | B — The Long Set | C — One Coach, In Frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| Palette / canvas | Deep charcoal, sparse brass edges | Bone/white, brass action | Pure black, off-white action, micro-brass |
| Typography | One technical grotesk + condensed poster move | Practical sans, direct hierarchy | Sculpted serif + precise sans |
| **Composition** | Narrow tool-grid and poster interruptions | Open three-part human/inquiry frame | Centered gallery with alternating portrait cards |
| **Media** | Tight documentary details and texture | Paired real-life training sequences | Singular cinematic portraiture |
| Density / surface | Compact, bordered, zero/4px geometry | Comfortable, flat, lightly divided | Spacious, dark, softly elevated |
| Energy / motion | Charged and precise | Active and approachable | Quiet and contemplative |

All three differ on six of six axes, including the structural axes of composition and media.

## Recommendation — B, The Long Set

**Why it wins:** Coach Cooz's strongest current truth is not a named method, luxury access, or a future metal community. It is a real coach adapting the work over a long relationship for people with full lives. Direction B makes that truth easiest to see, makes the inquiry path clearest, and still retains black/gold taste through disciplined role assignment.

Direction A is the most seductive stylistically, but its cultural signal can become a buyer filter. Direction C is the safest continuation, but it teaches us the least and risks turning human coaching into generic dark luxury. B gives the brand a more ownable commercial position: **serious coaching without fitness becoming your second job**.
