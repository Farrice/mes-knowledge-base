# COOZ VISUAL CONTENT PROMPT PACK

## AI Image Generation Prompts for The Resurrection Coach
## Date: 2026-04-13

> **Purpose**: 30 copy-paste-ready image generation prompts that create varied visual content assets while maintaining brand consistency. Designed for Higgsfield AI, Midjourney, DALL-E, Flux, and Leonardo.
>
> **The problem this solves**: Cooz has been using one photo for everything. YouTube thumbnails, Instagram posts, podcast artwork — same image, every time. It all blends together in feeds and kills his packaging. This prompt pack creates 20-30 distinct visual assets that feel like the same brand but never repeat.
>
> **How to use this file**:
> 1. Start every generation by pasting the **Reference Anchor Prompt** (Section 1) as the character description
> 2. Pick a prompt from the section that matches your content need
> 3. Copy the full prompt, paste it into your tool
> 4. Adjust aspect ratio and style settings per the tool-specific notes in Section 8
> 5. Generate 3-4 variations, pick the strongest
>
> **Brand color reference** (keep this open while generating):
> - Cathedral Black: `#0E0E0C` — dominant dark tone
> - Dawn Linen: `#F3EEE3` — warm light tone
> - Soil Brown: `#3A2A1F` — shadow tone, photo overlays
> - Stone Gray: `#5B5B55` — mid-tone, secondary text
> - Ember Gold: `#C08A3E` — accent, max 5% of composition
> - Blood Rust: `#6B2B1D` — reserved for emotional weight moments

1. **Character Reference**: Upload Cooz's reference photo as the character anchor before generating. Higgsfield uses face reference to maintain consistency across generations.

2. **Prompt Structure**: Higgsfield responds best to prompts structured as: `[Subject description] + [Action] + [Environment] + [Lighting] + [Mood]`. The prompts above follow this structure — paste them as-is.

3. **Style Keywords to Append**: For Lane A (practical) shots, add: `editorial photography, natural light, documentary style`. For Lane B (mythic) shots, add: `cinematic, film still, atmospheric, Fincher-esque lighting`. For thumbnails, add: `high contrast, bold, sharp focus`.

4. **Negative Prompts** (if supported): Always include: `neon lighting, bright gym, Planet Fitness, stock photo, airbrushed skin, HDR, oversaturated, shirtless, supplement bottles, protein shaker, text in image, watermark`.

### Consistency Tips Across Any Tool

- **Same reference photo every time.** Upload the same source image. Switching reference photos between sessions creates drift.
- **Save your best generation as a new reference.** When you get a generation that nails the look, save it and use IT as the reference for future generations in that series. This compounds consistency.
- **Batch by section.** Generate all Lane A images in one session, all Lane B in another. Switching between moods mid-session causes the tool to bleed styles together.
- **Color grading in post.** AI tools are inconsistent with specific hex colors. Generate the composition and lighting correctly, then adjust color temperature and grading in Lightroom or Canva to match the brand palette exactly.

## Section 9: ANTI-PATTERNS — What NOT to Generate

These are hard rules. If a generation hits any of these, discard it and re-roll.

### Never Generate:

1. **Shirtless or underwear shots.** Cooz is a body transformation coach, not a fitness influencer running a thirst trap. The brand is about what the body CAN DO, not what it looks like without a shirt. Tank tops are the maximum skin shown.

2. **Overly polished or airbrushed skin.** If the generation looks like a magazine cover with poreless, smooth skin — it fails. Cooz's brand is authenticity. Skin texture, visible pores, real lighting. The photo should look like it was taken on a good camera by a friend, not by a commercial studio.

3. **Group shots with stock-photo-looking people.** Generic diverse smiling gym-goers standing in a row. If the "clients" look like casting-call models, the image lies. Cooz works one-on-one in a small gym. If a client figure appears, they should look like a real 35-year-old man — not a fitness model.

4. **Neon, bright, or commercial gym aesthetics.** No purple and yellow Planet Fitness lighting. No massive commercial gym floors. No rows of treadmills. No LED strip lights. The brand is Cathedral Black — dark, warm, industrial, minimal.

5. **Supplements, protein shakers, or product placement.** No branded containers, no supplement stacks, no pre-workout bottles in frame. Cooz does not sell products. He coaches people. The only objects in his world are barbells, plates, chalk, notebooks, and coffee.

6. **Text baked into the image.** AI-generated text is almost always illegible or misspelled. Never include text, logos, watermarks, or typography in the generated image. All text (video titles, hook lines, brand name, episode numbers) gets added in Canva or post-production.

7. **Smiling, laughing, or hype energy.** Cooz's default expression is calm, direct, present. He is not a cheerful fitness influencer. The closest he gets to positive expression is a slight, controlled satisfaction (see YT4). If the generation shows teeth, a laugh, or a fist-pump celebration, it misses the brand.

8. **Gym selfie mirror shots.** No mirror reflections. No phone-in-hand selfie posture. This is not content from a client's Instagram story — it is professional visual packaging.

9. **Religious or literal resurrection imagery.** No crosses, halos, angel wings, rising-from-graves symbolism. The brand name is metaphorical. The visual language is grounded in physical spaces — doorways, light, empty rooms — not religious iconography.

10. **Corporate or office settings.** No suits, no boardrooms, no laptops, no conference tables. Cooz does not coach "executives" or "high performers" (those words are banned from the brand). He coaches men. In a gym.
