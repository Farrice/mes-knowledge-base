## PART 1: GOOGLE GEMINI GEM SETUP

### What a Gem is
A Gem is a custom AI assistant inside Google Gemini. You configure it once with instructions, and every time you use it, it already knows your voice, your rules, your offer, and your buyer. It's like a co-writer who read your entire strategy package before you sat down.

### Step 1: Open Gemini and create the Gem

1. Go to **gemini.google.com**
2. Click the **Gem Manager** (left sidebar, or search "Gems")
3. Click **"New Gem"**
4. Name it: **"Resurrection Coach Content Engine"**

### Step 2: Paste this EXACT system prompt

Copy everything between the `---START---` and `---END---` markers below and paste it into the Gem's instruction field. Do NOT edit it. Do NOT add to it. Do NOT "improve" it.

## PART 3: THE 5 GUARDRAILS (print this page)

These are the rules that prevent AI drift. Every time you use Gemini or NotebookLM, check the output against these 5 guardrails before posting or sending ANYTHING.

### Guardrail 1: The First Word Test
**Read the first word of the output.** If it's "you," "your," "here's," "are," "do," or "have" — the output is broken. Rewrite or regenerate. Every piece of content must open with "I" or a specific third-person observation ("A guy I know...").

### Guardrail 2: The Founder Scan
**Ctrl+F for "founder," "executive," "CEO," "C-suite," "high performer."** If any appear — the AI drifted to the old avatar. Reject the entire output and regenerate. Do NOT just edit the word out — if the AI used it, the whole framing is likely off.

### Guardrail 3: The Resurrection Copy Check
**Ctrl+F for "resurrection" in the body text.** It should appear ONLY as the brand name (in a header, bio, or brand reference). If it appears in the body copy of a post, DM, or sales conversation — replace it with "rebuild," "get back," or "comeback." The buyer does not use this word about himself.

### Guardrail 4: The Monk-Sacrifice Check
**Does the output make fitness sound like it requires a lifestyle overhaul?** If it implies 2-hour workouts, strict diets, waking up at 5 AM, or "monk-level discipline" — the output is wrong. The Ignition is 3x/week, 45 minutes, flexible nutrition, 200g protein. Specificity shatters the "monk lifestyle" fear.

### Guardrail 5: The Permission Check
**Does the output GIVE PERMISSION or DEMAND ACTION?** The Man in the Valley responds to permission ("you don't have to become a fitness person — you just have to stop being the man who puts himself last"). He shuts down when DEMANDED to act ("stop making excuses and commit"). Every output should feel like a door being opened, not a finger being pointed.

## PART 4: WEEKLY AI WORKFLOW (what Cooz does each week)

### Monday (15 min)
1. Record a 5-10 minute voice memo about whatever's on your mind (client sessions, gym moments, book chapter, a conversation that stuck)
2. Transcribe it (use Otter, Whisper, or just speak it into a Google Doc)
3. Paste the transcript into your **Gemini Gem** with: "Turn this into 3 Instagram posts using my 6-step structure and voice rules. One for Monday (Lane A), one for Wednesday (Lane A), one for Sunday (Lane B)."

### Tuesday (10 min)
1. Review the 3 Gemini outputs against the 5 guardrails
2. Edit to your final voice (the AI gets you 80% there — you add the 20% that makes it YOU)
3. Post Monday's piece (or schedule it)

### Wednesday-Friday (5 min each)
1. Post the day's scheduled piece
2. Check DMs — reply to anyone who engaged
3. If someone DMs about coaching, open your **NotebookLM** and paste: "A man just DMed me saying '[his words]'. Based on my DM conversion flow, what should I reply?"

### Sunday (20 min)
1. Post the Lane B (mythic/spiritual) piece
2. Open your **Gemini Gem** and paste: "Give me 3 reel script ideas for this week based on what I wrote about this week. Each script is 45-90 seconds, camera-to-face, one specific moment."
3. Pick one. Record it Tuesday or Thursday.

### After each Triage Audit (10 min)
1. Open **NotebookLM** and paste: "I just did a Triage Audit with [name]. He's [age], runs [business], his trigger was [trigger], and his main objection was [objection]. Based on my offer and the Provider Trap reframe, draft a follow-up voice memo I can send him tomorrow."
2. Review against the 5 guardrails. Record and send.

## PART 5: THE 3 THINGS AI CANNOT DO FOR YOU

No matter how good the tools are, these 3 things must be 100% you:

1. **The voice memos to your warm network (Tactic 1).** AI cannot record your voice. AI cannot know who in your life needs this. AI cannot personalize a message to a man you've trained with. Record these yourself. Every one.

2. **The Triage Audit calls.** AI can prep you. AI can help you write follow-ups. AI cannot sit across from a man on Zoom and hear the thing he's not saying. That's your superpower. Use it.

3. **The 2-minute video selfie and the exit interviews.** These are the proof assets. They must be real, unscripted, and shot on your phone in one take. AI can help you write the prompts. It cannot be the man on camera.

Everything else — content drafts, DM scripts, case study narratives, objection prep, blog posts, carousel text — the AI handles 80% and you finish the last 20%.

## PART 6: WHAT TO DO IF THE AI STARTS DRIFTING

**Signs of drift:**
- Outputs start using "you" in the opening
- "Founder" or "executive" language creeps back in
- Posts get longer than 300 words (Lane A) or 500 words (Lane B)
- The tone shifts from peer-to-peer to guru-to-student
- "Here's what/why/how" openers appear
- The word "resurrection" appears in body copy

**How to fix it:**
1. **Gemini Gem**: re-open the Gem settings and re-paste the system prompt. Gems can drift over long conversations. Starting a new conversation within the same Gem resets the context.
2. **NotebookLM**: check that you haven't accidentally added new sources to the notebook. Remove any source that isn't one of the 5 approved documents.
3. **Nuclear option**: delete the Gem and create a new one from scratch using this guide. Takes 5 minutes.
