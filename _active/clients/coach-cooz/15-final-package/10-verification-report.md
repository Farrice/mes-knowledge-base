## 3 STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS (from verification)

1. **The Channel Problem Is More Urgent Than the Messaging Problem** — the ICP and psychology are solid but this buyer finds coaches through referrals (69-84%), Google, and YouTube — not Instagram. The Ignition Plan's Tactic 1 (warm network voice memos) was already the right call. Instagram builds the proof shelf; referrals close the sale.

2. **The Trigger Hierarchy Should Be Medical-First** — content hooks and podcast guesting should lead with medical-trigger stories ("my doctor wrote 'obese' on my chart"), not occupational-trigger stories.

3. **"Resurrection" Works as Brand, Not Copy** — write all copy in the buyer's actual language. The brand name creates differentiation; the copy creates recognition.
