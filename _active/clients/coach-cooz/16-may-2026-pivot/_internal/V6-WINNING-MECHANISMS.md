### Mechanism 3 — Numbered Framework with Quotability Breaks

**Convergence**: Dan Go ("10 Universal Patterns I've Seen in People Who Get Into Incredible Shape"). McBroom (Hierarchy of Core Training — 4 levels). Galpin (9 types of exercise adaptations). Keuilian's "9 principles" in Man Up.

**Why this lands as Cooz, not as a copy**: McBroom's numbered framework is exercise hierarchy. Galpin's is biomechanical adaptation. Cooz's is a **whole-person-coaching framework** (Silence + Benevolent No are existential, not biomechanical). Different category.

**V5 deployment**: 8 Tenets become the scaffold for 8 Lane 1 posts over the next quarter.

## DISTRIBUTION ARCHITECTURE FOR V5 (Sean Macintyre lens)

**The cadence**: 3 posts per week on LinkedIn (Mon/Wed/Fri). NOT daily-volume Dan-Go-style. Cooz is one person; quality + retention + long-arc relationship-building is the brand. Volume would dilute the very thing that makes him different.

**The format mix**:
- 60% **text-only posts** (the V5 default — Cooz's actual voice + verified clients + numbered Tenet scaffold)
- 25% **photo + caption** (when there's a real client transformation OR a Cooz-in-room scene)
- 15% **carousel** (only for the Lane 3 case studies — Mike, Carron, Sam — where we can do the 6-image arc)

**The platform sequence** (modified from Dan Go's cascade — adapted for Cooz):
1. **LinkedIn primary** — every post lands here first. Test in market.
2. **The Resurrection Series podcast secondary** — high-engagement LinkedIn posts become podcast episode arcs (when a post resonates, Cooz records the deeper version)
3. **Substack tertiary** (future) — long-form deep dives ON the most-engaged LinkedIn posts. Build the subscriber asset off LinkedIn engagement. NOT today's priority.

**The travel mechanism**: V5 posts travel through THREE pathways:
1. **Specificity-driven retention** — readers save posts because of the specific real-client name + number (Mike 9 yrs, Carron CA squat record). Saved content gets the LinkedIn algorithm bump.
2. **Identity-shift closer engagement** — readers comment because the closer asked them an identity question, not for engagement-bait. Comment depth = algorithm signal.
3. **Cross-platform reinforcement** — the Resurrection Series podcast + Cooz's website link in the bio create the trust funnel for prospects who land from a viral post.

**The metric to watch (Week 4)**:
- Average engagement rate per post (LinkedIn target: ≥3% — currently ~0%)
- Number of profile visits / DMs / Triage Audit applications generated per post
- Saves + shares (the algorithm-amplification signal)
