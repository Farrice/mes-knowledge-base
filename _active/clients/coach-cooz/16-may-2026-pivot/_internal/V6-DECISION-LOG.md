## §1 — THE THREE DAY-0 DECISIONS (locked 2026-05-03)

These are the three decisions that resolve V5's internal forks. They were presented as multiple-choice options to Farrice on 2026-05-03 with strong recommendations from a Plan agent. All three confirmed with the recommended option.

### Decision 1 — ICP COLLAPSE

**The fork**: V5 had three running ICPs across its docs:
- V5-COOZ-HANDOFF leaned "Resurrection Candidate" (high-performing professionals running on fumes)
- V5-BRAND-STRATEGY-BRIEF Section 2 also leaned "Resurrection Candidate" but described it as "busy executives 35–55 who've hit the wall" in the Dunford section
- V5-BURBANK-ENGINE treated Hollywood production network as a separate Channel 2 vertical with its own ICP framing

**The decision**: **Resurrection-only.** One buyer (V6-CANON §2A — the Resurrection Candidate). Hollywood + Burbank are *referral source / geography*, not separate ICPs. Corey + Sam + Buzz are Resurrection Candidates who happen to work in production — same buyer, different traffic source.

**What this means in practice**:
- V6-BURBANK-ENGINE Channel 2 was reframed from "Hollywood production network as ICP" → "Hollywood production network as referral source for Resurrection Candidates"
- V6-CANON §12 explicitly anti-canons "Hollywood production network as a separate ICP"
- All content / copy / sales surfaces speak to ONE buyer

**Why not the other options**:
- **Resurrection + Hollywood subset** — kept a fork in the system; risked future drift; rejected
- **Burbank-Local only** — dropped national remote ICP; low ceiling; rejected

**If this gets reopened**: Don't. The collapse is clean and matches the proof spine (Mike, Robin, Sammy, Carron all fit; Corey + Sam are resurrection cases who happen to work in Hollywood). New evidence required to reopen — and "new evidence" means a 90-day window of content data showing Resurrection-only positioning is materially under-converting compared to a multi-ICP variant. Single-week signals are not evidence.

### Decision 2 — OFFER FORK

**The fork**: V5 swung between two offer framings:
- "Rebuild the body that built the life" (V5-COOZ-HANDOFF, Cooz's verified register, mechanism verb is "rebuild")
- "Brave Choice / permission to use the body you already have" (variant explored in V5 drafts; surfaced in some V5-LINKEDIN-PROFILE early drafts)

**The decision**: **Hybrid layered.** What Cooz sells = coaching that rebuilds the body. The "fork" was actually two correctly-layered things — Rebuild is the offer surface; Brave Choice is the proprietary methodology that runs through it AND becomes the LinkedIn lead-magnet asset (V6-BRAVE-CHOICE.md). V5's mistake was treating them as competing positionings.

**What this means in practice**:
- V6-CANON §3 documents the layered architecture (offer surface vs. methodology vs. lead magnet)
- V6-CANON §12 explicitly anti-canons "Brave Choice as the offer" and "permission to use the body you already have"
- V6-BRAVE-CHOICE.md productizes Brave Choice as the lead magnet (the biggest gap V5 left unfilled)

**Why not the other options**:
- **Rebuild only** — Brave Choice is too valuable as proprietary IP to leave unproductized; rejected
- **Brave Choice as offer** — softens the mechanism verb, harder for cold prospects to grasp; rejected

**If this gets reopened**: Don't, unless V6-BRAVE-CHOICE.md as a lead magnet underperforms (e.g., <20 email captures/month at Month 3) AND the Triage Audit conversion rate is also flat. Methodology productization needs runway — give it 90 days minimum.

### Decision 3 — LANE STRUCTURE

**The fork**:
- V5-NOTEBOOK-KNOWLEDGE mandated 3 topic lanes (Body-First Foundation 55% · Transformation Architecture 30% · Case Studies 15%)
- V5-LINKEDIN-CONTENT-SYSTEM organized the 10 paste-ready posts by 5 voice mechanisms (Scene · Confession · Reframe · Witness · Proof)
- The two systems didn't reconcile in V5 — readers couldn't tell which lane Post #6 (witness format) belonged to without manual mapping

**The decision**: **Both, orthogonally.** Lanes (55/30/15) govern *topic budget*. Voices (5-rotation) govern *structural variety*. Every post passes through both filters. They're not in conflict — they're orthogonal axes that BOTH apply to every post.

**What this means in practice**:
- V6-CANON §8 documents the orthogonal architecture — lanes × voices, both apply
- V6-LINKEDIN-CONTENT.md tags every post with BOTH lane (1/2/3) AND voice (Scene/Confession/Reframe/Witness/Proof)
- The Friday metric review tracks performance by BOTH axes (which lane converts? which voice drives engagement?)

**Why not the other options**:
- **Lanes only (3-lane mandate)** — drops the 5-voice rotation; risks all body-first posts sounding identical; rejected
- **Voices only (5-mechanism)** — drops the topic budget; harder for Cooz to self-audit; rejected

**If this gets reopened**: Don't. Both axes serve different purposes; collapsing to one regresses the system.
