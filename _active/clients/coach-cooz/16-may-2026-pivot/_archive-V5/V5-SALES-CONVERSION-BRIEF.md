## SECTION 1 — THE SALES MODEL IN ONE LINE

Triage Audit is the universal entry. Brave Choice methodology is the through-line. Four pathways. Diagnostic-led close (not menu-pitch close). Founding-rate pricing creates urgency without scarcity theater.

Total sales motion in five steps:

1. **Awareness** → LinkedIn / Instagram / YouTube / Blog / referral
2. **Lead capture** → Triage Audit booking page or DM warm-up
3. **Triage Audit (90 min)** → Listen 30 / Diagnose 30 / Recommend ONE 30
4. **Proposal** → Single pathway recommendation + onboarding logistics
5. **Close** → Founding rate locked OR 7-day decision window for prospects who need to think

That's the whole thing. No tripwire products, no ascension funnels, no challenge-launch theatrics. The sales model is the diagnostic relationship, mirrored in the coaching relationship.

### When each pathway fits

**Pathway A — In-Person Burbank ($1,275/$1,500)**: The buyer lives in LA / SFV / Glendale / Studio City / North Hollywood, can commit to 4x/week in-person, has the discretionary budget, and wants the daily presence of a coach in the room. **Best fit**: Mike (executive 9 yrs), Carron (athletic 3 yrs), Sam (Hollywood production 2.5 yrs), Mariano-tier Burbank professionals. Founding rate locks the slot for 12 months.

**Pathway B — Remote Resurrection ($1,500/$2,000)**: The buyer is anywhere in the US, travels significantly, can't commit to 4x/week in-person, but wants the depth of daily Voxer presence (text/voice-memo coaching every day). **Best fit**: Corey (Hollywood writer, travels for shows), Davonte (online client 1 yr), Reid (talent manager travels for work), high-performing professionals outside LA who've burned through app-based coaches.

**Pathway C — The Ignition ($3,500/$4,500)**: The buyer wants a defined endpoint (12 weeks), specific outcome (body recomposition / strength benchmark / readiness for an event), and packaged pricing. Ideal for prospects testing the relationship before committing to ongoing — OR for prospects who need a forcing function (a 12-week sprint with a defined deliverable). **Best fit**: Mari (3-month fastest transformation), prospects with a specific event date (wedding, photo shoot, athletic competition), prospects coming off another coach who want to commit to something defined first.

**Pathway D — Executive System Architecture ($5K-$10K/mo)**: The buyer is a founder, VP, public-facing creative, or senior leader whose body, schedule, team, and identity all need rebuilt around them. The coaching is one component of a wider system rebuild that includes nutrition, sleep architecture, training, recovery, daily team interaction patterns, and possibly travel/calendar redesign. **Best fit**: Mike (if he chose to upgrade), Karima (LAUSD high-stress leadership role), founders with $5M+ companies, public-facing creatives with daily output demands.

### Anti-pattern: pitching the menu

NEVER show the prospect all four pathways during the call. The diagnostic is the sales mechanism. Showing the menu signals "pick what you want" — which signals "I'm a vendor, not a specialist." Premium-tier coaching is sold from a single recommendation, not a menu.

The only time to mention multiple pathways: during the 30-min listen phase, IF the prospect explicitly asks "what are the options" — then the response is:

> *"Three or four pathways depending on what fits — but we'll figure out which one in the diagnosis. Let me hear more about what's actually breaking first."*

This deflects without being evasive. Returns the conversation to diagnosis.

## SECTION 3 — SABRI SUBY HALO STRATEGY (LEAD-GEN TO CLOSE)

Sabri Suby's Halo Strategy: **80% of the work is in the lead-gen and nurture; 20% is the close.** Most coaches reverse this and burn out cold-pitching.

### The V5 Halo funnel (Cooz-tuned)

```
COLD AUDIENCE (LinkedIn / IG / YouTube / blog organic)
          ↓
LEAD MAGNET (Brave Choice methodology — 3-question PDF + 1 worked example)
          ↓
EMAIL CAPTURE (coachcooz.com 50%-scroll pop-up + end-of-blog + LinkedIn Featured slot 3)
          ↓
NURTURE SEQUENCE (5 emails over 14 days — see Section 7)
          ↓
TRIAGE AUDIT BOOKING (the conversion event)
          ↓
TRIAGE AUDIT CALL (90 min — Listen 30 / Diagnose 30 / Recommend 30)
          ↓
PROPOSAL (single pathway recommendation, founding-rate locked OR 7-day decision)
          ↓
ONBOARDING (week 1: intake forms, baseline metrics, first session scheduled)
          ↓
RETAINED CLIENT (long-arc relationship — the V5 4-9 year wedge)
```

### Why this funnel works for V5

1. **Lead magnet is the methodology, not a generic checklist.** Brave Choice productized as the email-capture asset signals "specialist with proprietary IP" before the buyer has invested anything. Differentiates from the 12-week-sprint coaches whose lead magnets are calorie deficit calculators.

2. **Nurture sequence is voice-led, not promotional.** Cooz's voice in 5 emails. No sales pressure. The buyer self-qualifies by responding, replying, or booking.

3. **Triage Audit is the conversion event, not the website.** The website / LinkedIn / blog do the trust work. The Triage Audit is where money commits. Don't try to close on the lead magnet.

4. **Founding-rate locking is the urgency mechanism.** Not fake scarcity. Real 12-month rate-lock for first 8 founding clients across all pathways. Once filled, standing rate kicks in. Verifiable, defensible, doesn't read as Veblen-good theater.

### The 80/20 time allocation

Cooz's weekly time:
- **70% in the work** (existing client coaching — the relationships that produce the proof spine)
- **20% in lead-gen + nurture** (LinkedIn + IG + YouTube + blog + email writing — this brief's territory)
- **10% in the close** (Triage Audits + proposals + warm DMs)

If the close is taking more than 10% of weekly time, the lead-gen + nurture is broken (prospects aren't pre-qualified by the time they hit the audit). Diagnose upstream, not at the close.

## SECTION 4 — THE TRIAGE AUDIT CLOSE ARCHITECTURE (30/30/30)

The Triage Audit is 90 minutes, broken into three 30-minute phases. This is the single most important sales document in the V5 system. Run it the same way every time.

### PHASE 1 — LISTEN (Minutes 0-30)

**Goal**: Understand what's actually breaking. Use the prospect's verbatim language. No diagnosis yet.

**Opening line** (verbatim):

> *"Before I tell you anything about how I work, I want to hear what's actually happening in your body, your schedule, and your life right now. Take 5-10 minutes — what made you book this call?"*

**Then shut up and listen for 25 minutes.** Take notes on:
- Verbatim phrases the prospect uses (these become the close-back language)
- Specific moments they reference (e.g., "I noticed at my daughter's recital I couldn't lift her without my back catching")
- The gap between what they say externally and what's hitting internally
- Which of the 5 long-arc anchor profiles (Mike / Robin / Corey / Carron / Sam) their pattern most resembles
- Whether they've named the philosophical problem yet or are still stuck at external

**Listening rules**:
- DO NOT interrupt. DO NOT fill silence. DO NOT diagnose mid-listen.
- If the prospect goes off-track (talks about their job for 10 minutes), gently redirect: *"That's helpful context. Bring me back to the body — what's happening there right now?"*
- If the prospect runs out of things to say at minute 15, ask: *"What are you not saying out loud yet?"* — this is the Robin / McRaney deep-canvassing move that surfaces the philosophical problem.

**End of Phase 1**: At minute 28-30, transition: *"Okay. Let me read back what I'm hearing. Stop me if I get any of it wrong."*

### PHASE 2 — DIAGNOSE (Minutes 30-60)

**Goal**: Read back the prospect's verbatim language. Diagnose the pattern. Run the 4-pathway decision tree internally. Don't reveal the recommendation yet.

**Opening move** (the read-back):
> *"Here's what I'm hearing. [Verbatim phrase #1 from Phase 1]. [Verbatim phrase #2]. [Verbatim phrase #3]. The body that built [their specific life — career / role / family] is starting to break, and you've watched it happen for [time period they referenced]. You've tried [whatever alternatives they named — 12-week programs / Equinox / biohacking / nothing], and none of them held."*

This is the McRaney / Cimorelli reflective listening move. Read it back in their words, not yours. Two outcomes:
1. They say *"Yeah. Exactly."* → they feel heard. Trust opens. Close becomes inevitable.
2. They correct one detail → even better. Now they're collaborating on the diagnosis. Trust is even higher.

**Then run the diagnostic**:

> *"Three or four things are usually happening when this pattern shows up. Let me walk you through what I see in your case."*

Diagnose 3-4 specific patterns the prospect likely has, anchored in the V5 testimonial library. Examples:

- *"Your sleep architecture is broken — you're getting hours, not sleep. That's why the 4 PM fog is real and the 3 PM coffee isn't fixing it."*
- *"You're under-recovered, not under-trained. More gym time would make this worse, not better. Mike — 9-year client of mine, district manager 33 years at his company — was running into the same wall in year 2 with me. We backed off training volume by 30% and he started actually growing."*
- *"You've been performing energy for so long you've stopped distinguishing real energy from caffeine-and-cortisol energy. That's a calibration problem. I solve it by [specific protocol]."*

**The diagnose phase establishes specialist authority**. This is where the empathy-then-authority dual lock from V5-BRAND-STRATEGY-BRIEF locks. Empathy was Phase 1 (listening). Authority is Phase 2 (diagnosing).

**End of Phase 2**: Transition: *"Based on this, here's what I think the right pathway looks like for you. Let me walk you through it."*

### PHASE 3 — RECOMMEND ONE (Minutes 60-90)

**Goal**: Single pathway recommendation. Logistics. Founding-rate locking OR 7-day decision window.

**The recommendation move**:

> *"Three or four pathways exist depending on the situation. Yours is Pathway [A/B/C/D] — and here's why."*

Then explain ONLY that pathway. Geography fit · delivery fit · what week 1 looks like · what month 3 looks like · what year 1 looks like. Anchor in the V5 testimonial library — name the long-arc client whose arc the prospect's situation most resembles.

> *"Mike's been with me 9 years on Pathway A. The first 6 months we just rebuilt sleep and recovery. He started actually losing the weight in month 7 — not because the program changed, but because the foundation finally held. That's the timeline you should expect."*

**The pricing reveal**:

> *"Pathway A is $1,275/month at the founding rate — that locks you in for 12 months at this rate before it standardizes to $1,500. I have [N] founding spots open across all pathways and they're filling. Standing rate after that is non-negotiable."*

**The close-resolution move** (Donald Miller GP8 — conditional resolution):

> *"If what we just walked through matches what you actually need, the right move is to lock the founding rate now. If you need 7 days to think about it — that's fine, but the founding rate is gone if it's filled by the time you decide. I'd rather you commit at the right tier than the wrong one."*

This is the entire close. No high-pressure follow-up. No "let me hop on a follow-up call." The diagnosis sold itself.

**Three possible outcomes**:
1. **Lock immediately** → Run logistics. Send onboarding email same day. Schedule first session within 7 days.
2. **Need 7 days** → Send the proposal email (template in Section 7). Schedule a 15-min commit call in 7 days. Don't chase between then and now.
3. **Soft no** → *"What's the gap between yes and no for you right now?"* — listen to the answer. If the gap is solvable (logistics, scheduling, partner alignment), solve it now. If the gap is psychological (fear, identity, "I should do this myself"), name it gently and let them sit with it: *"That's real. Sometimes the work happens when the prospect realizes the gap themselves. Take the time."*

**End of Phase 3**: Whether they lock or don't, send a thank-you note within 4 hours. If locked — onboarding email. If not — proposal email + 7-day check-in scheduled. If soft no — a single email with one Brave Choice question and no CTA.

## SECTION 5 — DANIEL PRIESTLEY OVERSUBSCRIBED MECHANICS

Priestley's Oversubscribed model: **demand > supply, signaled visibly, drives premium pricing without scarcity theater**.

### The 4 Oversubscribed levers V5 deploys

**Lever 1 — Signaled scarcity (real, not theater)**

Cooz's actual capacity per pathway:
- Pathway A (in-person Burbank): max 3-4 clients (4x/wk in-room is the constraint)
- Pathway B (remote): max 5-7 clients (daily Voxer is the constraint)
- Pathway C (12-week packaged): max 3 cohorts at a time (delivery bandwidth)
- Pathway D (ESA premium): max 1-2 clients (ESA is hands-on systems work)

Total full-capacity: 12-16 clients across all pathways. Verifiable. When a slot fills, signal it: *"Pathway A in-person is full. Next opening is October."*

This is real scarcity. Not "5 spots left this week!" theater. Real enough that prospects who hesitate genuinely lose the opportunity.

**Lever 2 — The waitlist mechanism**

When Pathway A or D fills, prospects don't get rejected — they get waitlisted with the founding rate locked. The waitlist itself becomes a positioning signal: *"He has a waitlist for in-person."*

Waitlist mechanics:
- Locked founding rate held for 90 days from waitlist enrollment
- Email check-in monthly with one Brave Choice methodology question
- First-right-of-refusal when a slot opens
- If the waitlist exceeds 5 prospects on Pathway A, that signals the standing rate should bump 10% on next cycle

**Lever 3 — Status signaling through client roster**

The V5 testimonial library is the status signal: Mike (33-year executive), Carron (#2 national powerlifter), Corey (Hollywood writer on "The Night Agent"), Cody McBroom endorsement. Prospects don't pay $1,500/mo to look like Mike. They pay to be in the room with the coach who's coached Mike for 9 years.

The status signal works because it's verifiable (Carron's CA State Squat record, Corey's IMDb, Cody McBroom's public Instagram) — not constructed. Don't add fake-status signaling. The real signals are enough.

**Lever 4 — Premium pricing as quality signal**

The 4 pathways are tiered with intentional gaps. $1,275 → $1,500 → $3,500 → $5K-$10K. Each gap is large enough that the buyer self-selects into the right tier based on their actual need + capacity.

If a prospect tries to negotiate down ("can you do $1,000/mo for in-person?"), the answer is: *"The pathway you're describing is actually B or C, not A. Let me walk you through that one — it might fit better."* Never discount Pathway A. Discounting destroys the tier system and signals "the rate was inflated."

## SECTION 6 — OBJECTION HANDLING (THE 6 MOST COMMON)

The 6 objections Cooz will hear most. Each has a response anchored in V5 testimonial library + V5 voice register.

### Objection 1 — "It's too expensive."

**What they're really saying**: *"I don't yet see the value matching the price."* (NOT "I can't afford it.")

**Response**:
> *"Tell me what feels expensive — the monthly number, the 12-month commitment, or the comparison to what you've paid before? Because I'm not the cheapest coach in Burbank — I'm the most expensive — and I'm pricier than the apps. The reason: the apps don't have Mike at 9 years. The cheaper coaches don't have Carron at the #2 national level. You're not paying for sessions. You're paying for the practice that holds at year 5. If the math still doesn't fit, Pathway B or C might be the right entry."*

This response: (a) names the real objection (value, not price), (b) doesn't apologize for pricing, (c) anchors in the long-arc proof, (d) offers a tier-down without discounting Pathway A.

### Objection 2 — "I've tried programs before. They didn't work."

**What they're really saying**: *"I'm afraid this is just another program."*

**Response**:
> *"That's the right instinct. Most programs don't work because they're solving the wrong layer. They give you a workout plan when what you needed was a relationship that holds when the workout plan stops mattering. I don't sell programs. I sell a 9-year practice — the same one Mike's been on, the same one Carron's been on, the same one my dad named 'Brave Choice.' If you want a 12-week program, I'm not the right coach. If you want the work that holds in year 4 — that's me."*

### Objection 3 — "I don't have time."

**What they're really saying**: *"I'm afraid this will demand more than I can give."*

**Response**:
> *"Time is the wrong frame. Mike runs a national operation. Corey writes for two different shows simultaneously. Carron trains 5 days a week at the elite-athletic level on top of his career. None of them have time. They have a practice that fits inside the time they have. The Triage Audit is where we figure out how the practice fits inside YOUR time. If the answer is 'it doesn't,' I'll tell you that. The 4 pathways exist because time and geography vary."*

### Objection 4 — "Let me think about it / let me discuss with my partner."

**What they're really saying**: *"I'm not yet at conviction."* (Or sometimes literally: "I need partner alignment.")

**Response**:
> *"That's totally fair. The founding rate is locked for 7 days from this call — beyond that, it's not me being arbitrary, it's that the slot might fill. If you need to talk to your partner, I'd offer this: the conversation isn't 'should I hire a personal trainer.' The conversation is 'is the body that built this life going to hold for the next 30 years.' Frame it that way. If your partner pushes back on the tier, Pathway B or C might be a better fit and I'll happily restructure."*

### Objection 5 — "Why $1,500/mo when I can hire a trainer for $80/session?"

**What they're really saying**: *"Compare yourself to a known commodity for me."*

**Response**:
> *"$80/session × 4 sessions/week × 4 weeks is $1,280 — and that's with the trainer who shows up Tuesdays and Thursdays in Burbank. With me you get real change in 90 days AND a foundation that keeps producing at year 3, year 5, year 9. The pricing is built around the work that compounds, not the session count. If you want 4 sessions a week without the diagnostic + the daily Voxer + the nutrition protocol, hire the $80/session trainer — they're great for that. If you want what Mari got in 90 days and what Mike's been on for 9 years, that's me."*

This response refuses the apples-to-apples comparison without dismissing the prospect. Names exactly what they're getting that the $80/session trainer isn't providing. Allows them to choose the cheaper option without losing face.

### Objection 6 — "What if I cancel after 3 months?"

**What they're really saying**: *"I'm worried about getting locked in."*

**Response**:
> *"You can cancel anytime — but the founding rate is conditional on the 12-month commit. If you cancel at month 3, I'll prorate and refund what makes sense, and you'd come back at standing rate if you wanted to restart. The math: most people who want to cancel at month 3 are running into the 6-week wall, not the work failing. The 6-week wall is when the body starts pushing back on changes — and that's the moment most coaches lose their clients. I don't lose clients there because the practice is built to handle it. Mike was tempted to quit at month 4 of year 1. He's at year 9 now."*

### Universal objection-handling principle

Every objection response: (a) names what they're really saying, (b) doesn't argue or apologize, (c) anchors in V5 testimonial library, (d) offers a tier-down option that respects their capacity without devaluing the primary pathway, (e) ends without high-pressure CTA.

## SECTION 9 — WHAT TO IGNORE (TEMPTATIONS THAT DILUTE)

Five tempting moves that destroy the V5 sales engine. Don't:

1. **Run a webinar / challenge launch.** Wrong identity for V5. The diagnostic-led model is the differentiator. Webinars are for selling 12-week programs.
