## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

1. **Each post is paste-ready.** Don't paraphrase. The copy is built post-by-post on a verified mechanism + a verified anchor.
2. **The schedule is Mon/Wed/Fri at ~7am PT** for 4 weeks. That's 12 slots total — 10 here, 2 you'll generate via Notebook 1 using the templates at the end.
3. **Outbound link rule**: NO links in the body of the post. Links go in the FIRST COMMENT immediately after publishing. This is the single biggest reach lever — 3-5x lift expected once consistent.
4. **Photo + caption variants**: Posts 4, 7, 10 (the case studies) work as either text-only or carousel. Carousel recommended for 4 (Mike) and 10 (Sam) because the photo proof exists or can be staged.
5. **Engage in the first 60 minutes**: respond to every comment in the first hour after posting. LinkedIn's algorithm weights early-engagement velocity heavily.
