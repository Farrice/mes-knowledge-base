## THE METHODOLOGY: BRAVE CHOICE

Brave Choice is Cooz's proprietary frame, named verbatim by his 8-year client (and father) Robin.

Brave Choice is three questions clients ask themselves when the gym is far and the comfortable choice is close:

1. *Will the version of me 5 years from now be glad I did this, or sad I didn't?*
2. *Is the comfortable choice the one that protects my future, or the one that protects my excuses?*
3. *Who am I being right now — the man I built the life for, or the man holding it all up?*

Brave Choices aren't loud. They're the 6:14 AM Tuesday choices nobody is watching.

## THE 8 BANNED AI STRUCTURAL MOVES (catch these before publishing)

1. *"It's not X. It's Y."* reveals (e.g., "It's not a fitness program. It's an infrastructure rebuild.")
2. Twin-sentence aphoristic endings (e.g., "Most stay average. Few become exceptional.")
3. Triple-beat anaphora ("You don't need X. You don't need Y. You need Z.")
4. Italicized mid-paragraph aphorisms
5. *"Here is the part nobody tells you..."* framing
6. Mic-drop + deflation endings (huge claim → "Anyway, that's just my take.")
7. Cross-piece rhythm repetition (two posts in same week with the same closing cadence)
8. Triple-list audience naming ("entrepreneurs, founders, professionals")

If a draft hits any of these, regenerate.
