**Warm cinematic darkness.**

Black wardrobe + warm gold rim light + cinematic backgrounds + masculine-professional energy + controlled intensity. ## PALETTE

Derived from the photos themselves. Not invented. Hex codes are calibrated to the actual lighting.

| Role | Color | Hex (suggested) | Where it shows up |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Cinematic Black | `#0A0A0A` | Wardrobe · backgrounds · text on light surfaces |
| **Accent** | Warm Amber / Gold | `#C8943B` | Rim light · headlines · brand spine accent · podcast cover wash |
| **Mid** | Deep Brown / Leather | `#2D1F18` | Warmth fields · long-form copy backgrounds · About-page sections |
| **Highlight** | Warm Skin Tone | `#D4A47A` | Humanity · photo skin tones · used as transition between black + amber |
| **Optional** | Navy | `#1B2540` | ESA Tier D premium surfaces ONLY (matches Photo 17 suit) |
| **Neutral** | Off-White | `#F5F1EA` | Light text on black · contrast surface · NOT pure white (too clinical) |

### Palette deployment rules

1. **Black is the default ground.** 70%+ of any visual surface is black.
2. **Amber/gold is the accent — never the ground.** Amber is the rim light, the headline color, the spine. Never let amber dominate or it reads "podcast bro" instead of "Resurrection Coach."
3. **Brown is the warmth bridge.** Use brown to soften pure-black surfaces (About page sections, long-form blog) without going amber-heavy.
4. **Skin tone is the humanity field.** Whenever a portrait runs against pure black, let the skin tone breathe — don't crush the warmth into shadow.
5. **Navy only for ESA Tier D.** Pathway D / executive premium surfaces use navy as primary accent in place of amber. This keeps the executive register premium without breaking the brand spine.
6. **Off-white not white.** Pure white is too clinical for the warm cinematic register. Off-white (`#F5F1EA`) is the contrast surface.

## TYPOGRAPHY

Two fonts. One for headlines (high-impact, masculine, controlled). One for body (legible, warm, professional).

### Headline pairing

**Recommended primary**: **Editorial New** (or fallback: **Söhne Breit** / **PP Editorial New** / **Tiempos Headline**) — modern serif with masculine weight. Gives the "Resurrection Coach" essence editorial gravity without going stuffy.

**Recommended secondary** (if budget-constrained): **Inter Display** (free, Google Fonts) at 700-800 weight. Geometric, masculine, cinematic.

**Avoid**: Display fonts that read "podcast bro" (Bebas Neue, Oswald, Montserrat all-caps). Avoid hand-lettered scripts entirely.

### Body pairing

**Recommended primary**: **Inter** (free, Google Fonts) — workhorse sans, neutral enough to let the headline do the heavy lift.

**Recommended secondary** (long-form blog): **Source Serif** or **Charter** for blog body copy. Reads warmer, holds up at length.

### Hierarchy spec

| Use | Font | Weight | Size | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Banner headline | Editorial New / Inter Display | 700-800 | 48-64px | Off-white `#F5F1EA` |
| Section H1 | Editorial New / Inter Display | 700 | 36-44px | Black or amber |
| Section H2 | Editorial New / Inter Display | 600 | 24-28px | Black |
| Body | Inter | 400-500 | 16-18px | `#0A0A0A` on light, `#F5F1EA` on dark |
| Caption / metadata | Inter | 400 | 12-13px | Brown `#2D1F18` |
| Accent / methodology callout | Editorial New italic | 500 | 18-20px | Amber `#C8943B` |

## BANNER COMPOSITE SPEC (LinkedIn / Website / Podcast / YouTube)

The banner is the single most-deployed visual asset in the system. One composite, four format variations, identical brand frame across surfaces.

### Source photo

**Photo 01 — Stage Speaking** (cooz-stage-speaking-hero.jpg — file save naming per V5-PHOTO-INVENTORY)

Why: Captures the Resurrection Coach in his element. Negative space on left for text overlay. Vertical wood-slat structure with warm orange backlight is structurally Resurrection (warmth emerging from darkness). Banner-grade composition built in-camera.

### Composite layout

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                     │
│   [TEXT OVERLAY ZONE]                          [PHOTO 01 — COOZ    │
│                                                 ON STAGE — RIGHT   │
│   Headline                                      2/3 OF FRAME]      │
│   Subline                                                          │
│                                                                     │
│   [Optional: brand mark / logotype small bottom-left]              │
│                                                                     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- Photo 01 occupies right 60-65% of frame
- Left 35-40% is the text overlay zone (where the wood-slat backlight already provides controlled darkness)
- Vertical center-line of frame: where Cooz's gesture lands — nothing crosses that line in the overlay zone

### Text overlay copy (3 variants — pick one for the cycle)

**Variant 1 (default — brand essence)**:
> **The Resurrection Coach**
> *I rebuild the body that built the life.*

**Variant 2 (proof-led, dual-anchor)**:
> **Real change in 90 days.**
> *Clients who stay 4-9 years because the work compounds.*

**Variant 3 (Lane 1 emotional)**:
> **The body that built this is breaking.**
> *That's the work.*

### Typography spec for overlay

| Element | Font | Weight | Size | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | Editorial New (or Inter Display fallback) | 800 | 56-64px (LinkedIn 1584×396); scale per surface | Off-white `#F5F1EA` |
| Subline | Editorial New italic | 500 | 22-28px | Amber `#C8943B` |
| Brand mark (optional) | Inter | 600 | 14-16px tracked-out | Off-white `#F5F1EA` 70% opacity |

### Color treatment

- Photo 01 untouched — DO NOT add color washes, gradients, or filters. The original lighting IS the brand.
- Optional: 30-40% black gradient from left edge inward for the first 30% of frame, to ensure overlay text legibility against any lighter pixels in the wood-slat zone. Test before committing — Photo 01 may already have enough natural darkness.
- **Never** add amber/gold gradient overlays on top of the photo. The amber lives in subline text and rim light only.

## SPEC AS PHOTOGRAPHER BRIEF (next session)

If/when the next photo session happens, hand the photographer this section verbatim.

### Mandatory aesthetic continuity

- **Wardrobe**: Black t-shirt, black henley, black athletic top, dark blazer, navy suit. NO color, NO patterns, NO logos.
- **Lighting**: Warm gold/amber rim light. Cinematic deep darks. Golden-hour outdoor when present. NO clinical white, NO cool tones.
- **Backgrounds**: Rich dark interiors · cityscape glass · gym warmth · rooftop sunset · leather chair · wood-slat stage. NO white seamless. NO photo studio cyc walls.
- **Mood**: Masculine-professional, mythic-cinematic, controlled-intensity. NOT bro-fitness, NOT influencer-glossy, NOT clinical-medical.

### Shot list gaps (if next session happens)

The current 18-photo library covers most needs but has gaps. If a second session is scheduled, prioritize:

1. **Cooz training a real client** (action shot, gym, rim light — for Lane 3 case-study post headers and Instagram reel covers). Real client preferred (Mike or Carron); model-as-client acceptable if rights cleared.
2. **Cooz writing / at his desk** (lifestyle, indoor, warm lamp lighting — for blog headers and "the work behind the scenes" Instagram content).
3. **Cooz with kettlebell or barbell mid-action** (gym, dynamic, rim-lit — fills the action gap; current library has only static gym photos).
4. **Cooz on podcast equipment** (mic, headphones, dark room — Resurrection Series promotional content).
5. **Tight lifestyle close-ups** (hands, watch, water bottle, kettlebell grip — Instagram fill content; the brand needs more compositional variety on IG without needing Cooz's face in every frame).

### Technical specs

- Camera: Full-frame DSLR or mirrorless (Sony A7-series, Canon R-series, Fuji X-T4+). Phone-shot only acceptable if iPhone 15 Pro+ with ProRAW.
- Lenses: 35mm and 85mm primes preferred. Avoid wide-angle distortion on portraits.
- Format: 16:9 widescreen for all banner-eligible shots; 1:1 square for IG-eligible shots; 9:16 vertical for IG reel covers and YouTube Shorts.
- Color profile: Shoot RAW. Color-grade in post to match the existing 18-photo aesthetic (warm rim, deep darks, controlled saturation).

## ACCESSIBILITY + WCAG

The Resurrection Coach brand is high-contrast by design (off-white on cinematic black) — accessibility is mostly built-in. The 3 places to verify:

1. **Amber `#C8943B` on cinematic black `#0A0A0A`** — contrast ratio 6.91:1 ✓ (passes WCAG AA Large Text and AA Normal Text)
2. **Off-white `#F5F1EA` on cinematic black `#0A0A0A`** — contrast ratio 18.94:1 ✓ (passes AAA)
3. **Brown `#2D1F18` body copy on off-white surfaces** — contrast ratio 14.2:1 ✓ (passes AAA)

Avoid: amber `#C8943B` on white surfaces (fails contrast). When light surfaces need an amber accent, darken the amber to `#8B6428` (passes AA on white).
