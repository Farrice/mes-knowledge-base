## WIN #1 — Move all outbound links to first comment (the single biggest reach fix)

### What's wrong
Your last 10 LinkedIn posts all have outbound links in the body (YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, articles). LinkedIn's algorithm penalizes outbound links HEAVILY in main posts. This single behavior is the largest cause of your suppression — bigger than headline issues, bigger than voice issues, bigger than lane scatter.

### What to do
1. Go to your LinkedIn profile → Activity → All posts
2. For your TOP 5 most recent posts that have outbound links in the body:
   - Edit the post
   - REMOVE the link from the body text
   - Replace with a one-line callout: "Full episode in the comments." or "Full article linked in the comments."
   - Save
   - IMMEDIATELY post the actual link in the FIRST COMMENT yourself
3. Going forward, every new post follows this rule. NO outbound links in body. First comment only.

### Why this works
LinkedIn's 2026 unified Llama 3 algorithm reads outbound-link posts as "creator pushing readers off-platform" — bad signal. Posts with links suppressed by 50-70% versus link-free posts. The first-comment workaround keeps your distribution clean WHILE still letting interested readers find the link.

### Expected result
Within 14 days, your link-free posts should reach 3-5x more impressions than your link-loaded posts. Document the difference for your own confidence.

### Time required
30 minutes for the 5-post backfill. 30 seconds per future post (paste link in first comment after publishing).

## WIN #2 — Set LinkedIn industry to "Health, Wellness & Fitness"

### What's wrong
Your LinkedIn extraction returned "Industry: NOT VISIBLE" — meaning either it's empty or it's set to something that doesn't match your actual content. Either case hurts the algorithm's ability to match your content to interest-matched audiences.

### What to do
1. Go to LinkedIn → Edit profile → Intro
2. Find the "Industry" dropdown
3. Select **"Health, Wellness & Fitness"** (not "Professional Training & Coaching" — that one is B2B coaching, wrong category)
4. Save

### Why this works
The Industry field is one of the 5 Author Fields the AI uses for content-audience matching. An empty or mismatched industry forces the AI to guess based on content alone. A clean match removes ambiguity.

### Expected result
Improved organic discovery via topic search. Subtle but compounds.

### Time required
60 seconds.

## WIN #3 — A/B test the link-free post hypothesis

### What's wrong
You don't yet have direct evidence that outbound links are suppressing YOU specifically (vs. it being a general LinkedIn pattern that may not apply to your account). You should validate before betting the rebuild on it.

### What to do
1. Pick one of your strongest existing posts — recommended: the "It's 3:17 AM. The house is perfectly quiet, but you're wide awake fighting a ghost..." one (Post 3 from your last 10)
2. Re-craft it as a NEW post:
   - Same opener (vivid scenario)
   - Same body (the diagnostic + reframe)
   - REMOVE the link in the body
   - Add a one-line CTA: "If this lands, 90-min Triage Audit free during May beta — link in comments."
   - Post it as a new post (not edit of the old one)
   - Drop your Triage Audit link as the first comment IMMEDIATELY after publishing
3. Track impressions over 7 days

### Why this works
Same content, different distribution mechanic. If the link-free version reaches 2-3x more impressions than your link-loaded posts averaged, the suppression hypothesis is confirmed. If it doesn't, we need to look elsewhere.

### Expected result
Either:
- Link-free version dramatically outperforms (= confirm the algorithmic suppression hypothesis, double down on the no-links rule)
- Performs the same (= the suppression isn't from links, look at other factors next)

Either result is informative.

### Time required
15 minutes.
