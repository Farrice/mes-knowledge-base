## HEADLINE ENGINEERING (the most important word-by-word battle)

Per Maria Wendt's Headline Dominance Rule: 80% of marketing effort goes into the headline. Per Diandra Pattern 13: the headline is the strongest of the 5 Author Fields for AI semantic matching. Per Lara Acosta Pattern 20: a good headline = pain + for whom + proof.

| 4 | *"Body-first transformation coach for entrepreneurs and high-functioning professionals — sustainable change, real case studies, 1-on-1 coaching"* | 8/10 | 7/10 (lists features, not the wound) | High — "high-functioning" eats truncation space | 7.5 |
| 5 | *"You optimized everything except the operator \| Body-first transformation for entrepreneurs, founders & professionals"* | 8/10 | 9/10 (bridge message LIVES in headline) | High — first 60 chars cuts at "You optimized everything except the operator \| Body" — and "you" opener violates voice rule | 7.5 |

### Recommended Headline (Candidate #1)

> **Body-first transformation for entrepreneurs, founders & professionals | I rebuild the operator most people optimize around**

**Why this wins**:
- **AI retrieval**: Six topical anchors in the headline alone — body, transformation, entrepreneurs, founders, professionals, operator. The AI builds a clean creator profile from this single field.
- **Human conversion**: The pain ("most people optimize around") is named without naming the buyer's failure. The for-whom is explicit. The proof is the implicit verb "rebuild" — it presupposes a process.
- **Voice compliance**: Opens in I (the "I rebuild..." clause). Uses the "operator" self-concept noun. Uses verified vocabulary ("rebuild," "professionals"). Avoids all banned words.
- **Mobile truncation**: First 60 characters render as "Body-first transformation for entrepreneurs, founders &" — a clean partial that signals the topic without orphaning the meaning.

### Why NOT Candidate #5 (the "bridge message" version)

Candidate #5 puts the bridge message ("You optimized everything except the operator") directly in the headline. It's voice-strong but loses on two levels:

1. **Voice rule violation**: Profile copy must open in I, not You. The headline is the most-read piece of profile copy on the platform; it must lead the voice discipline, not break it.
2. **Mobile truncation**: The bridge message is 49 characters. The "| Body-first..." separator clauses get amputated on mobile, leaving only the wince — without the relief of "here's what we do about it."

**Where the bridge message belongs**: In the BANNER (visual/text overlay) and in the FIRST LINE of the About section. Both surfaces handle longer copy without truncation and don't break the I-led discipline.
