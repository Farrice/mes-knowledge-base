## Fix #4 — Lane Discipline Structural Mitigation (the deepest crack)

### What was wrong

90-day lane discipline assumed Cooz can suppress 30%+ of his content identity through willpower alone. No enforcement mechanism. Just an honor-system "temptation list" + a "send it to the book/podcast" deflection that doesn't have a real holding chamber.

### V2 Structural Mitigations

#### Mitigation A: Extend Farrice voice-check window from 4 weeks to **full 12 weeks**

Originally `00-START-HERE.md` set "Farrice voice-checks every Instagram/LinkedIn post for first 4 weeks." V2 extends this to **12 weeks** — the full lane-discipline window.

The voice check is also a LANE check: every post Cooz drafts is screened by Farrice for two things:
1. Voice compliance (no AI tells, no banned words, opens-in-I, etc.)
2. **Lane compliance** (post fits Lane 1, 2, or 3 — if not, it goes to the holding chamber)

This adds 5-15 min to Farrice's weekly load (3 post drafts/week × ~3 min each) — small operational cost for a major signal-protection benefit during the formative window.

#### Mitigation B: The Off-Lane Holding Chamber

Create a real holding chamber for off-lane content. Requirements:
- A Notion or Google Doc folder titled `cooz-off-lane-holding-chamber/`
- Subfolders: `book-material/`, `podcast-material/`, `private-only/`
- Every off-lane thought Cooz has during the 90-day window goes here, NOT into LinkedIn drafts
- Farrice reviews the holding chamber once per week during the Friday check-in
- Material Farrice tags as "this is real, save for book Chapter X" gets routed appropriately
- Material Farrice tags as "this is fine, just publish on the podcast next week" gets routed appropriately
- Material Farrice tags as "this isn't going anywhere, log and release" gets archived

This converts "send it to the book" from honor-system aspiration to documented workflow with a review gate.

#### Mitigation C: The 7-Day Cool-Down for Borderline Lane Posts

For posts that "sort of fit a lane" (the failure mode named in `02-SEMANTIC-LANES-90-DAY.md` Section 6.1), Cooz drafts the post and lets it sit in a `pending-7day-review/` folder. After 7 days:
- Cooz re-reads it
- If it still feels lane-aligned, it goes to Farrice voice-check, then publish
- If it now reads as forced or off-lane, it gets routed to the holding chamber (book/podcast/archive)

The 7-day delay alone kills 50%+ of borderline posts. The thought that felt urgent on Day 1 reveals itself as "off-lane impulse" by Day 7 most of the time.

### Why these matter

The reviewer's premise crack is real: lane discipline as willpower test will break in Week 4-7. The structural mitigations don't eliminate the temptation — they convert the temptation into REVIEWABLE INPUTS that have a productive destination. Cooz still feels the impulse. He just routes it instead of suppressing it.
