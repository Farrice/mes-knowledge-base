## WHY THIS PROMISE WORKS (the strategist note for Farrice)

This section is for Farrice, not for Cooz. Read it once and then forget about it.

**Why "REBUILT" beats every other one-word option:**

- **vs. "Optimized"** — every competitor owns "optimized." It's the failed frame for this buyer (Whoop, supplements, peptides — all software upgrades on a hardware that's broken).
- **vs. "Transformed"** — too generic. Every coach claims transformation.
- **vs. "Restored"** — too soft. Reads as wellness vocabulary.
- **vs. "Resurrected"** — Cooz's brand word, but too on-the-nose for body copy. Stays in brand only.
- **vs. "Built"** — close, but "built" without "re-" misses the substrate-was-broken narrative. Anyone can be built. Only Cooz's clients get rebuilt.

**Why the one-two-three structure works:**

- **Substrate → Body → Identity** is the actual mechanism. Most coaching skips substrate and starts with body. Cooz's differentiation IS the substrate work. Naming it is the moat.
- The progression is sequential and visible. You can FEEL substrate restored at week 4. You can SEE body rebuilt at week 8. You can IDENTIFY as rebuilt at week 12. The buyer's experience matches the promise structure.

**Why the cold pitch is exactly 74 words:**

- Long enough to deliver specifics (10-20 lbs, three lifts, sleep, week 12)
- Short enough to say in one breath at a dinner party
- Ends on the one-word identity for stickiness
- Uses Cooz's verified vocabulary (operator, optimized, fumes, substrate, adrenaline, rebuilt)
- Excludes every banned word (no founder/executive/wellness/journey/mindset)

**The campaign-design implication (Lynch frame):**

Once "rebuilt" is the one word:
- Every LinkedIn post answers "what does rebuilt look like?"
- Every Instagram carousel answers "how does someone get rebuilt?"
- Every case study answers "this is rebuilt — meet the person who became it"
- Every banner says "rebuilt" or carries the bridge message
- Every DM ends on "rebuilt"
- The book chapter titles are stages of the rebuild
- The podcast episodes are "Rebuilt: [Name]'s 12 Weeks"

The campaign builds itself once the word is locked.
