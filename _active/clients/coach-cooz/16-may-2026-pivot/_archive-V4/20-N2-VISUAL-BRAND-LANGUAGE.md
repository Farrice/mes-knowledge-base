## THE ONE SENTENCE

**Editorial premium athletic. Patagonia ambassador meets NYT Magazine portrait meets minimal Stripe-tier brand mark.**

If a generated image doesn't feel like one of those three references, it's wrong.

## COLOR PALETTE

### Primary

- **Charcoal / Off-Black** — `#1A1D21` to `#0F1114` range. The dominant background. Premium, hardware-coded, serious.
- **Off-White** — `#F5F5F2` to `#FAFAF7` range. NOT pure white (too clinical). Slightly warm off-white. The text and clean-surface color.
- **Mid-Grey** — `#3F4347` to `#5C6166`. The shadow and secondary tone.

### Accent (pick ONE per asset, stay consistent within a series)

- **Slate Blue** — `#3D5A6C` to `#4A6B7E` range. The default accent. Hardware-coded, executive-readable.
- **Burnt Orange** — `#B85728` to `#C56833` range. The aggressive accent. Use for case-study POP moments and ad-creative pattern-interrupts only.

### NEVER USE

- ❌ Pure black (`#000000`) — too synthetic, looks AI-generated
- ❌ Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) — too clinical, looks medical-spa
- ❌ Bright red, neon green, hot pink, electric blue — bro-marketing aesthetic
- ❌ Multi-color gradients — wellness-instagram aesthetic
- ❌ Pastel anything — feels dated and feminine in a way that misses Cooz's voice register

## TYPOGRAPHY

### Primary typeface family

**Sans-serif. Bold. High-contrast.**

Recommended (in priority order):
1. **Inter** — free, clean, modern, geometric. Default choice.
2. **Neue Haas Grotesk Bold** — premium, editorial, what NYT Magazine uses.
3. **Helvetica Bold** — fallback if neither above is available.
4. **Söhne** — premium alternative if budget allows (Klim Type Foundry).

### Weights to use

- **Bold (700-800)** for headlines
- **Regular (400)** for body copy
- **Medium (500)** for subtitles or pull quotes

### NEVER USE

- ❌ Script / decorative fonts (looks wedding invitation)
- ❌ Comic Sans or playful display fonts (obvious why)
- ❌ Serif fonts as the primary brand voice (Cooz is sans-serif — feels modern, hardware-coded)
- ❌ Multi-font combinations (one font family, vary weight only)

### Hierarchy rules

- Headlines: BOLD, large, all-caps OR sentence-case (consistent within a series)
- Subtitles: Regular weight, smaller, sentence-case
- Body copy: Regular, body-size, sentence-case
- Accent/keyword highlighting: bold + slate-blue color

## PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE

### The 3 reference categories

**1. Editorial portrait** (Patagonia ambassador, NYT Magazine)
- Subject is mid-action or mid-thought, NOT posed-smiling
- Natural environmental setting, not studio
- Natural light, single-source, often window-direction
- Documentary realism, not catalog perfection
- Composition shows the subject IN their environment, not against a backdrop

**2. Atmospheric environment** (Field Notes catalog, NYT Tools issue)
- Still lifes of working surfaces, equipment, notebooks
- No people in frame, just the artifacts of the work
- Low-key lighting, often single source
- Premium materials suggested (leather, brass, heavy paper, dark wood)
- Used for landing page heros, email banners, deliverable headers

**3. Clinical case study** (Precision Nutrition, MyBodyTutor)
- Side-by-side before/after with consistent lighting and crop
- Plain mid-grey backdrop (NOT pure white, NOT colorful)
- Subject in the same dark athletic apparel both shots
- Identical camera position, distance, angle
- Clinical evidence aesthetic — no motivational text, no arrows, no emojis

### NEVER use these aesthetics

- ❌ Influencer-instagram (smiling at camera, perfect lighting, posed body)
- ❌ Stock photo "fitness team workout" generic
- ❌ Neon-lit gym aesthetic (the "Equinox at night" look)
- ❌ Drone-shot landscape with subject as small figure
- ❌ Composite image with multiple subjects/scenes layered
- ❌ Quote-card with stock photo background

## COMPOSITION RULES

### For portraits

- **3/4 angle** preferred (not full-front, not pure profile)
- **Chest-up or half-body** (head-shot only feels corporate; full-body needs a reason)
- **Subject occupies left third or center** when text overlay is planned
- **Eye-line** slightly off-camera (not direct stare unless ad creative)
- **Hands** either visible and engaged with equipment, OR cropped out (Nano Banana struggles with hands)

### For environmental shots

- **Single focal point** (one notebook, one barbell, one pen — not a cluttered scene)
- **Asymmetric balance** — subject occupying 1/3 of frame, negative space the other 2/3
- **Foreground / background separation** via shallow depth of field

### For ad creative / pattern-interrupt

- **High contrast** — dramatic shadow, single light source
- **Subject filling frame** for thumb-stop on mobile feed
- **Eye contact** is acceptable here (one of the few cases) — but expression is FOCUSED, not smiling

---

## LIGHTING DIRECTION

### Default: Single-source natural

- Window light from camera-left or camera-right
- Soft shadow opposite the source
- Slightly cool color temperature (5000-5500K — not warm-orange tungsten)

### Alternative: Single-source overhead industrial

- For gym / training environment shots
- Creates dramatic shadow under subject
- Pairs well with charcoal backdrop

### Alternative: Golden hour

- For atmospheric outdoor shots (rare for Cooz)
- Use sparingly — fitness golden hour is influencer-coded

### NEVER

- ❌ Multi-source three-point studio lighting (too commercial)
- ❌ Ring light (selfie aesthetic)
- ❌ Flat overhead fluorescent (medical/corporate aesthetic)
- ❌ Colored gel lighting (nightclub / influencer reels aesthetic)

## WARDROBE / ATTIRE GUIDELINES (for Cooz's portraits)

### Always

- Dark long-sleeve athletic crew OR fitted dark t-shirt
- Black or charcoal training pants (NOT shorts unless mid-action)
- Athletic footwear visible only when relevant to action
- Watch: simple, dark, NOT a "look at my Rolex" situation

### Never

- ❌ Tank top (the LinkedIn extraction flagged this as a visual mismatch with Cooz's content register)
- ❌ Branded gym apparel with visible logos
- ❌ Bright neon athletic wear
- ❌ Dress shirt and jeans casual-Friday (mixed signal)
- ❌ Fully suited business attire (over-corporate)

The wardrobe goal: looks like a coach who could walk into a board meeting AND lift weights, but is currently doing the work.

2. **Typography compliance** — only sans-serif bold? Single accent color for highlighting? ✓
3. **Subject expression** — focused/mid-thought/serious, NOT smiling-at-camera? ✓
4. **Lighting** — single-source natural, not multi-source studio? ✓
5. **Wardrobe** — no tank top, no branded apparel, no shorts unless mid-action? ✓
6. **Background** — clean, single-color or environmental, NOT cluttered? ✓
7. **Mood register** — feels like Patagonia/NYT/Field Notes references, NOT influencer-instagram? ✓

If any check fails, iterate the prompt and regenerate.
