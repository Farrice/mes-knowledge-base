## §0 — Universal Voice Rules (read before deploying any sequence)

**Voice across all six sequences**: specific · story-led · slightly self-deprecating · warm · peer-to-peer · slightly funny when warranted · spiritually grounded without being preachy. (V6-CANON §5.)

**Subject lines**:
- Specific, not clickbait. 6–12 words ideally.
- No emoji. No "you'll never believe..." No `[FIRST NAME] —` template-field-as-status-signal.
- Test: does it read like the title of a real text message Cooz would send, or like a marketer trying to game an open rate?

**Pre-headers**: Real preview text. Not a duplicate of the subject. Not a sales line. The first sentence of the email or a clean re-frame of it.

**Openings**: Open in I, in scene, or with a named real client. NEVER opens in "you." (V6-CANON §5 pronoun architecture.)

**Closings**: One CTA per email. Inline link, never a button. The Sequence A Day 14 email is the only place that gets a final, slightly more direct invitation. No `P.S. — last reminder` theatrics. No "If you'd like to..." marketer-template framing.

**Signature**: `— Cooz` (two characters, every email, every sequence). No "Coach Cooz" formal. No "Acusio Bivona, The Resurrection Coach, Founder of [...]" marketer lower-thirds. Just `— Cooz`.

**Banned vocabulary** (V6-CANON §6): substrate · biological infrastructure · operating system · operator · crush it · level up · alpha · founder mode · holistic · journey · mindset · wellness · self-care · healing · reset · "Here's what / Here's how / Here's why" openers · "Resurrection" in body copy (brand word for banner / hero only).

**Banned AI structural moves** (V6-CANON §7): "It's not X. It's Y." closers · twin-aphoristic endings · triple-beat anaphora · italicized mid-paragraph aphorisms · "Here is the part nobody tells you..." · mic-drop + deflation · cross-piece rhythm repetition · triple-list audience naming.

**Proof rule**: Every long-arc client claim in any email traces verbatim to V5-TESTIMONIAL-LIBRARY. Mike is 9 yrs. Robin is 8 yrs. Sammy is 4+ yrs. Corey is ~4 yrs. Carron is ~3 yrs. Mari is 3 mo. Sam is 2.5+ yrs. Buzz is ~1 yr at age 65. Patricia is 9 mo (the "actually pretty terrible, lol" mom story — Cooz's signature self-deprecating opener). Don't invent specifics.

## §7 — Sequence F — Quarterly Maintenance (all past + active clients)

**Trigger**: Quarterly cadence (Q1 / Q2 / Q3 / Q4).

One email per quarter. Not a sales push. Not a newsletter. A check-in from the coach to everyone he's ever worked with. Past clients who haven't trained with him in 2 years still get this. Active clients still get this. The point is the relationship, not the conversion.

The voice: warm, observational, sharing one specific insight from the quarter — a real client moment, a methodology refinement, a Brave Choice realization. Real-life observability. Not "happy new year" content.

The CTA, if there is one, is soft and inline. About every other quarterly email has zero CTA at all.

---

### F1 — Quarter 1 (template)

**Subject**: One thing I noticed in Q1
**Pre-header**: From the gym, not the marketing department.

```
Hey [first name],

Q1 observation, from inside the gym — not from a marketing strategy:

The clients who came back to me in January didn't come back because
they failed at their resolution. They came back because they ran the
4-week experiment of "I'll handle this myself" and watched their
body's data tell them the truth before March did.

The 4-week experiment is honest. It's not a failure mode. It's how a
lot of high-performing professionals genuinely test whether they need
a coach or just need to recommit to the basics. About 40% of them
discover they CAN do it themselves — and they go run with it, and I
hear from them at year-end about how it held. The other 60% discover
```

## §8 — Tracking + Metrics

**Sequence A — Lead-Magnet Nurture**
- Day 0 open rate target: ≥45%
- Day 14 open rate target: ≥30%
- Reply rate Day 0: ≥5% (these are warm prospects who just downloaded)
- Click-through to Triage Audit booking: ≥8% across the 14-day window
- Audit booking attribution: tag which email's CTA last fired before the booking event

**Sequence B — Pre-Triage-Audit**
- Show-up rate at Triage Audit: ≥85%
- B1 → B2 → B3 open rate decay should be small (<10 percentage points across the 3 emails) — large decay means the prospect cooled off and the call may not happen

**Sequence C — Post-Audit**
- C1 onboarding-link click within 24 hrs: ≥95% (they already locked — friction here is a system bug, not an attribution metric)
- C2 reply or booking within 7 days: ≥40% (this is the audit-to-close conversion proxy — track separately from the in-call lock rate)
- C3 re-engagement within 6 months: ≥30% (these are soft-nos who came back)

**Sequence D — 7-Day Check-In**
- Reply rate: ≥35% (the 2 questions are designed to extract a real answer, not just a CTA click)
- Conversion lift over no-check-in baseline: ≥10% additional close rate on previously soft-no prospects

**Sequence E — 90-Day Post-Onboarding**
- Open rate: ≥75% (these are active clients — anything lower means deliverability or relationship problem)
- Reply rate to E1, E3, E4 (the ones with reply prompts): ≥50%

**Sequence F — Quarterly Maintenance**
- Open rate: ≥40% (broadcast list, lower expectation than active-client cadence)
- Past-client re-engagement attribution: ≥5% of recipients per quarter take some action (reply, click, re-book audit, refer)

**The metric that matters most**: Composite Triage-Audit-to-paying-client conversion rate, attributed back through which sequence touched them last. V6 target: ≥45% (V5 baseline: ≥40%).
