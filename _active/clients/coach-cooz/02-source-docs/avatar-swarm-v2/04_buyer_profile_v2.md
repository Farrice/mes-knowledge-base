## The Man We Are Selling To

He is 32 to 52. He runs a shop, a crew, a truck, a small practice. He does not call himself a founder. He calls himself "the guy who keeps this thing alive." His body is not a dashboard — it is a failure, a wound, a place he does not look. He is not optimizing. He is hiding.

The trigger already fired: back pain from the van, a blood pressure reading at a walk-in, a photo his wife posted that he deleted, a moment at his kid's birthday where he sat on the floor and could not get back up cleanly. He is done. He knows it is not another app.

This is the Man in the Valley. The $1,997 Ignition Offer exists for him.

---

## A. Beliefs That ENABLE the $1,997 Purchase

**1. "I'd rather try something honest at $2K than keep failing at zero."** He has a graveyard of free plans, half-used apps, and $39 programs. The cheap stuff is the reason he is where he is. Spending real money with a real human feels like a decision, not a browse. (Anchor: **Daniel, MyBodyTutor** — 30, 87 lbs lost, paid for a human coach after free tools failed.)

**2. "If one man sees me all the way through, I cannot disappear."** Every program he has quit, he quit quietly. *If somebody actually watched, I wouldn't have let it slide*. Daily Voxer is not a feature — it is the thing that kills the silence. (Anchor: **Richard Hughes** — 49, massage therapist, 103 lbs under Precision Nutrition 1-on-1. Being seen was the mechanism.)

**3. "The story sells me. Not the certificate."** He does not want the guy with the clipboard. He wants the guy who has been in the hole and climbed out. Credentials would make him trust it less — they smell like the thing that already failed him. Cooz's resurrection story IS the proof. Suby **Pattern 13 (Identity Trigger Targeting)** applied to the coach: the COACH's identity is the qualifier.

**4. "This is the cost of being witnessed."** He frames $1,997 as the price of a man looking him in the eye for 13 weeks. His wife is tired. His crew needs him strong. Cooz is the only adult in the room with him. (Anchor: **Patrick Hayes** — bouncer, 152 lbs. A bouncer does not pay for information. He paid to be held accountable.)

**5. "Founding client means I am not the 400th number. I am the first name."** The founding-client frame flips price anxiety into status. He is not buying a discount — he is buying a seat at the origin of something. For a man who has never been first in anything, *you are the founding case study* hits a wound that has been open 20 years. (Anchor: **David Sell** — 31, high school football coach, 82 lbs. A coach being coached.)

## B. Beliefs That BLOCK the $1,997 Purchase

**1. "I've already wasted money on programs."** The ghost of Noom, the $300 app, the 6-week bootcamp. Each taught him: *you spend, you quit, you feel worse*. $1,997 is not competing with zero — it is competing with a graveyard.

**2. "I'm not the guy in the transformation photos."** Every transformation he has seen is a 28-year-old bro with visible abs in the before photo. He is 44. He has never had abs. He wants to tie his shoes without making a sound. The photos kill the sale before the pitch starts.

**3. "I'm too broken for a quick fix and too tired for a long one."** He does not believe in 90 days. He does not believe he has 2 years. 13 weeks scares him as *not enough* and *too much* at the same time.

**4. "My wife will roll her eyes if I spend this on myself."** Not a budget problem. A permission problem. She has said *we can't afford that* about $200 things. He cannot imagine the conversation. Silent killer of the sale.

**5. "I cannot fail out loud again."** Every previous quit was private. Paying Cooz $1,997 and quitting would be the first time he failed where somebody watched. That terrifies him more than the money.

**6. "Online coaching is for people who have their shit together."** He thinks online programs are for guys who can already run 3 miles and want to run 5. He cannot run to the mailbox. Self-disqualifies before he clicks.

## C. The Godfather Offer Architecture

Sabri Suby's **Godfather Offer** standard: *"an offer so good they would feel stupid saying no."* The job is not to convince him the coaching is worth $1,997. The job is to make walking away feel more expensive than walking in.

**The $1,997 Ignition Stack**:

| Component | Why it lands for this buyer |
|---|---|
| 13 weekly 1-on-1 calls (Cooz, Zoom) | Kills "I'm just a subscription" fear |
| Daily Voxer access, no cap | Kills the disappearing quiet quit |
| The 8 Tenets curriculum (McBroom lineage) | System, not vibes |
| Half-money-back guarantee | Splits the risk visibly — skin in the game on both sides |
| Founding client case-study slot | Flips price into status — "spender" becomes "contributor" |
| Direct line to Cooz's resurrection story | The story IS the mechanism |

**Grading against Suby's Godfather criteria**:

- *Risk reversal*: Half-back guarantee is stronger than most founding offers. Pass.
- *Proof*: McBroom lineage + Cooz's body. Light on 3rd-party testimonials — the weakness. Per Suby **Hidden Knowledge 7 (Social Proof Deletion Protocol)**: *"When starting from zero, delete proof sections rather than fake them."* Lead with lineage + lived story. Founding cohort generates real proof.
- *Specificity*: 13 weeks, 8 Tenets, daily Voxer, half-back. Every number concrete. Pass.
- *"Feel stupid saying no"*: For the right buyer, yes — nowhere else can he pay $1,997 to have a man see him every day for 13 weeks.

**Suby Godfather grade**: 7.5/10 at Ignition. Ceiling 9/10 after first cohort generates real case studies.

## D. The Pain Amplification Sequence

Apply Suby **Pattern 3 ("Bleeding Neck" Pain Hierarchy)** and **Pattern 4 (Forum Foraging Intelligence)**. Hit in order. Every line is verbatim from exemplars + WS1.5 Reddit.

**Pain 1 — The fumes.** *"You're running on fumes. You're faking energy you don't have. You're drinking the third coffee at 2pm knowing it will not work and drinking it anyway."*
Daily, physical, undeniable. Recognition in the first line.

**Pain 2 — The silence.** *"Nobody talks about it. The silence makes it worse. Your wife stopped asking. Your guys stopped noticing. You stopped mentioning it at the doctor."*
Social isolation. The page stops being a pitch and becomes a mirror.

**Pain 3 — The clock you cannot see.** *"You haven't slept properly in 6 months. You've missed every family dinner this year. You don't remember the last time you played with your kid without sitting down after five minutes."*
The kid detail is the detonator. Do not soften it.

**Pain 4 — The failure graveyard.** *"You've tried the apps. You've tried the 6-week thing. You've paid for the program your buddy recommended. You quit every single one and you never told anyone."*
Suby **Pattern 14 (Objection Anticipation Architecture)** embedded inside pain.

**Pain 5 — The mirror he avoids.** *"There is a photo of you from three years ago you do not want anyone to find. You deleted the last one your wife posted. You have stopped being in pictures at all."*
Save for last. The sale closes in his head before the offer lands.

The wrong dream: *"Become a high performer. Unlock your potential. Optimize your biology."* He does not talk like that. Using that language signals wrong page.

The right dream — in HIS words:

- *"I want to tie my shoes without making a sound."*
- *"I want to get on the floor with my kid and get back up without thinking about it."*
- *"I want to sleep through the night without the sheet sticking to me."*
- *"I want to be in the photo again."*
- *"I want my wife to stop looking at me like she's worried."*
- *"I want to stop lying about how I feel."*

Quiet, physical, relational, dignified. None about appearance. None about a number. The offer promises *resurrection* — returning to a version of himself that already existed. That is the word his body recognizes.

## F. The Starving Crowd Test

Suby's foundational law: *"The first question is not what are you selling. The first question is: is there a starving crowd?"*

**Yes. But not at the surface.**

The surface of this market is saturated with **biohacker founder** buyers — Noah Kagans, Dan Go clients, Ultimate Performance demographic. Visibly starving, but Cooz cannot serve them yet (no published male case studies, no $10K proof stack).

Underneath sits the Man in the Valley. He is not searching *"best high-performance coach"*. He is searching nothing. He is listening — to Rogan, Bedros Keuilian, Andy Frisella, Ed Mylett — waiting for a story that sounds like his. He shows up in podcast comment sections, Reddit (r/loseit, r/Fitness30Plus, r/Dad), and YouTube channels where a guy with a beard tells his story without selling.

**Evidence**:

1. **Exemplar density.** All 7 verified exemplars span this behavioral profile and paid real money for 1-on-1 coaching. Not one outlier — a pattern.
2. **Richard Hughes is the closest match.** A 49-year-old massage therapist losing 103 lbs under a real human coach is not a high-performer arc. It is a resurrection arc.
3. **The language bank is saturated.** *"Running on fumes," "faking energy," "haven't slept in 6 months," "missed every family dinner"* — these recur across forums without prompting. Suby **Pattern 4 (Forum Foraging Intelligence)**: verbatim recurrence = demand signal.
4. **The Rogan/Frisella/Mylett podcast ecosystem is the watering hole.** Millions of men listen weekly and almost nobody is selling directly into their emotional state.

**Conclusion**: A starving crowd Cooz does not need to create. Suby **Pattern 2 ("Raging River" Market Selection)** — join the existing party, do not start a new one.

## H. Price Objection Handling (Scripts)

**Objection 1: "$1,997 is a lot of money."**
*Script*: "It is. You have spent more than that on things that did not work — the apps, the 6-week thing your buddy recommended. Those were you paying for information. This is paying for somebody to watch you, every day, for 13 weeks, so you cannot disappear again. The price is not the problem. The price is the permission."
*Why it works*: Names the graveyard (Block 1). Flips permission (Block 4). Closes with identity, not discount.

**Objection 2: "I should be able to do this myself."**
*Script*: "You are right. You should. And you have tried, and it has not held. The guys who do this themselves are rare, and almost none of them did it the first time. They had a coach, a training partner, a brother. Doing it alone is the reason you are where you are. The question is whether you are willing to stop pretending you have to."
*Why it works*: Does not fight the belief. Reframes solo as the cause. Hits pride without bruising it.

**Objection 3: "My wife will not understand me spending this on myself."**
*Script*: "Then do not frame it as spending on yourself. Frame it as the cost of coming back to your family. Your wife is not married to a bank balance. She is married to a man running on fumes and faking it. The $1,997 is for the version of you who shows up at dinner, gets on the floor with the kids, and sleeps through the night. In my experience — she has been waiting longer than you think."
*Why it works*: Names the real block (permission). Hands him language for the kitchen-table conversation — where the sale actually closes.

## I. What The Old Polluted Work Got Wrong

The prior profile declared *"$10K is market-center"* and built the entire psychology map around the high-performing executive founder. Perplexity-validated against the wrong buyer. The $10K price point is real — it belongs to **Dan Go** and **Ultimate Performance**, operators with decades of published male case studies and the proof stack to justify the premium. Cooz has none of that yet. Selling the Man in the Valley at $10K is not a pricing error — it is an archetype error. The real price center for a solo coach with zero published male case studies, a McBroom lineage, and a resurrection story is the $1,500-$4,000 gap zone — exactly where the Ignition Plan's $1,997 / $2,997 / $4,000 ladder sits. The old profile asked *what would a $10K buyer believe?* when the real question was *what does the Man in the Valley need to believe to say yes to $1,997 from a coach with a story and no roster?* Every downstream output was wrong because the upstream avatar was wrong. This rebuild fixes the upstream.
