# Recording this in Descript (+ B-roll / slide cue sheet)

## The fast way to record (Descript)

1. **New project → Record.** Turn your camera on if you want your face in it, or record **audio only** if you want a pure voiceover over B-roll and slides. Either works with these scripts.
2. **Just talk. Don't worry about flubs.** Descript writes a live transcript as you go. If you fumble a line, pause, and say it again. You'll delete the bad take later by deleting the *text* — that's the whole trick of Descript.
3. **Clean it up.** Read the transcript and delete any stumbles or filler by deleting the words. Turn on **Remove Filler Words** and **Studio Sound** (makes your audio sound pro).
4. **Add the visuals.** Split the project into Scenes (one per section below). Drag the **JCKED cover** image into the JCKED scene and the **Puravita cover** into the Puravita scene. Drop B-roll clips on the layer *above* your talking-head track at the spots in the cue sheet. Descript lets a clip sit on top of you so the viewer sees the B-roll while they hear you.
5. **Captions + export.** Add captions (most people watch muted at first), then export 1080p.

> Tip: record the **whole script in one pass**, even with mistakes. It's faster to delete bad lines than to do twenty perfect takes. Aim for energy over polish.

## B-roll / slide cue sheet (matches Version 2)

| When you say… | Put this on screen |
|---|---|
| Opening ("two ads for the same guy") | You on camera, or stock footage of a regular 40-something guy (gym, mirror, kitchen) |
| "First one's JCKED…" | **The JCKED cover slide** |
| "a locked vault… carnitine is the key" | A vault/lock opening, or the cinematic cell / mitochondrion clip |
| "most brands give you barely any" | A supplement label / a tiny scoop (shows the underdose idea) |
| "the usual before-and-after everyone runs" | A quick montage of typical shirtless before-and-after ads (or one with a red X over it) |
| "Second one's Puravita…" | **The Puravita cover slide** |
| "a battery you can't see" | A phone at 5% battery, or a glowing battery icon |
| "his blood test says he's fine" | A blood vial / a lab result on a screen |
| "running low… no one ever told him" | A tired man at dawn, soft and quiet |
| Close ("change the story he tells himself") | Back to you, or a title card with your name + email + phone |

## Where to get the B-roll fast
- **Slides:** screenshot the cover pages of the two brief PDFs (page 1 of each).
- **Stock B-roll:** Pexels / Storyblocks (free-ish), or Descript's built-in stock library.
- **The cell / vault / battery shots:** if you want them custom, the same tools the briefs call for (Veo, Kling) can make 3–5 second clips, or just use a clean stock equivalent. For a 3-minute Loom, stock is totally fine.

## Two ways to deliver it
- **Talking-head with cutaways** — your face is the main shot, B-roll and the two covers cut in over the top at the cues. Feels personal.
- **Full voiceover** — your voice over slides + B-roll the whole way, no face. Cleaner, faster to make. Either is fine for this; pick what feels comfortable.
