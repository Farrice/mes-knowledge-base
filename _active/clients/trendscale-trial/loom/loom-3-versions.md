# Loom Script — 3 Versions (low cognitive load, small words)

All three are written to be **spoken over B-roll/slides** and easy to read aloud. Small words, short sentences. The slides carry the detail; these just give the clear, dense breakdown.

- **Version 1 — Lean (~1:45).** The bare minimum. Two pictures, one idea. Lowest load.
- **Version 2 — Full but Simple (~3:00). ⭐ Recommended.** Still answers the client's three questions (what you'd test + why you're sure / the look + feel / the desire + how aware he is) — just in tiny words.
- **Version 3 — Talk-Me-Through-It (~2:15).** Loosest, most "you just talking." Leans on the slides for detail.

Pick the one that feels most like you out loud. If a word trips you, change it.

---
