# GenSpark Build Pack — TrendScale Creative Strategist Trial

> **How to use this.** Paste one asset section at a time into GenSpark (start a fresh project per asset). Each section is self-contained: it has the design direction, the exact copy, and the rules. **The copy below is final and fact-checked — render it, do not rewrite it.** If GenSpark wants to "improve" a line, stop it; the wording is load-bearing and the numbers have been verified against primary sources. Your job in GenSpark is the visual build: make it captivating, editorial, and premium.

---

## 0 · The aesthetic bar (applies to all three)

The register is **editorial science feature, not supplement ad.** Think *New York Times Magazine*, *The Atlantic*, *Wired* science covers, Apple product films. If a stranger scrolling past mistakes one of these pages for a real magazine feature for one second, the design is working.

- **Deep navy ground** (near-black blue), restrained single accent per asset, warm off-white type.
- **Source Serif 4** for display + serif headlines (use its optical-size axis — this is what gives the editorial register), **Inter Tight** for body and labels. (Fallbacks: Tiempos Headline / Söhne.)
- Generous whitespace. Hairline rules, not boxes-everywhere. One focused idea per card.
- **No** gym-bro gloss, **no** stock photos of tired men, **no** decorative gradients, **no** drop shadows, **no** emoji except the single "👇" already in the copy.
- Tasteful motion/parallax in GenSpark is welcome, but never at the cost of legibility.

## 1 · Shared design system

**Palette (use as the starting point — you may refine the exact hexes):**
```
Navy ground      #0E1F3A      (page background)
Navy card        #15294A      (lifted card surfaces)
Paper            #F4ECDC      (primary type)
Paper-dim        #C7BFB1      (secondary type)
Paper-mute       #8C8576      (labels / meta / footers)
Coral  #D77E5C   — sparingly, ONLY for "data the reader should feel" (e.g. a kill rule)
```
**Per-asset accent:**
- **JCKED brief → warm amber/gold** `#C8A560` (key words, top-rules, numbers).
- **Puravita brief → soft sage-green** `#A4C2A0`. Reserve warm amber for the Huberman/Attia card only.
- **Portfolio deck → gold** `#C8A560`.

**Recurring layout patterns (reuse across pages/slides):**
1. **Cover** — small letterspaced eyebrow, huge Source Serif display title (2 lines), one italic accent-color kicker line, a short lede, a 4-cell snapshot strip, a hairline footer signature.
2. **Card with accent top-rule** — navy card, 3px accent top border, a small caps label, then content.
3. **Dense table / grid** — hairline row dividers, tabular-figure numbers, the target column in the accent color.
4. **Chrome** — every page/slide: top-left eyebrow (section name), top-right page number (`02 / 08`), bottom hairline footer (`Hooks, Angles & Systems · Farrice Cain · Prepared for TrendScale Media`). The cover has no page number.

## 2 · Copy + voice rules (CRITICAL — do not violate)

- **Use the copy verbatim.** It's fact-checked; rewriting risks re-introducing errors that were specifically corrected.
- **Zero em-dashes.** Use periods, commas, colons, or middots (·).
- **No AI tells:** no "Here's what / Here's why / Here's how" openers, no "It's not X, it's Y" stacking, no triple-anaphora ("give him a, give him a, give him a").
- **Keep every citation exactly as written.** Do not add, round, or "punch up" any statistic. Do not name any competitor brand with a specific claim (e.g. never "Brand X gives you 500mg" — only "most brands").
- **Compliance language is exact.** Structure-function phrasing ("supports the body's mechanism…") and the Meta policy framing ("long-standing Personal Attributes standard, updated 2024") must stay as written.

### Page 4 — The Research
- Action title: **Three facts the avatar doesn't know. *Each one rewires his story.***
- **Fact 01 · The blood-test gap** — Serum magnesium reflects **less than 1%** of total body magnesium, and serum is a poor predictor of true status. The other 99% lives in bone, muscle, and soft tissue. A man can be severely depleted and still test "normal." This is the foundational caveat in every clinical magnesium reference. *Source: NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, Magnesium fact sheet (Health Professional).*
- **Fact 02 · The 600 processes** — Magnesium is a cofactor in **over 600 enzymatic reactions** (up from the older 300-enzyme estimate). Muscle contraction. Neurotransmitter synthesis. ATP production. Magnesium doesn't assist these reactions. They don't happen without it. When the body is low, the systems don't fail outright. They just perform worse, which is exactly the symptom pattern the avatar is living. *Source: de Baaij, Hoenderop & Bindels, "Magnesium in Man," Physiological Reviews, 2015.*
- **Fact 03 · The depletion curve** — Nearly half of US adults, **about 48%**, take in less magnesium than the estimated requirement, with the lowest intakes in men over 71 and adolescent males. Men 35-plus sit squarely on the curve. And refining whole wheat into white flour strips out roughly 80% of its magnesium, so the modern diet quietly removes it. This isn't a discipline problem. It's a food-supply problem. *Source: NIH ODS (intake / NHANES); USDA FoodData Central (grain-refining loss).*
