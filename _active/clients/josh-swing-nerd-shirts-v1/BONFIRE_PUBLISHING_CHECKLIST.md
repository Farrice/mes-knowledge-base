# Bonfire Publishing Checklist

## Campaign Setup

- Create or log into Josh's Bonfire account.
- Create a new campaign or store item.
- Choose **Sell products with campaigns**, not a bulk custom order.
- Choose **In batches** as the selling mode.
- Set first batch length to **10 days**.
- Turn **Smart Launch off** if the goal is a limited first test.
- Keep the product range tight: unisex tee only for V1 unless Bonfire's preview makes an additional product obvious.

## Product Setup

For each finalist:

- Upload or create the artwork.
- Confirm the design is original.
- Use 1-color or 2-color print only.
- Check legibility at small mockup size.
- Check that the design does not sit too low on the shirt.
- Confirm the design works on every selected shirt color.
- Remove any colorway where contrast is weak.

## Copy Fields

Use these base fields:

- Campaign name: `Social Dance Debug Club: First Drop`
- Candidate 1 title: `Commit On Eight Tee`
- Candidate 2 title: `No Drops In Prod Tee`
- Candidate 3 title: `Push / Pull Request Tee`
- Campaign story:

```text
This first drop is for the dancers who live somewhere between the social floor and the group chat.

Swing dance has its own tiny language: timing, connection, recovery, rhythm, restraint, and the occasional extremely specific joke that only makes sense after midnight. These shirts are built for that overlap. Low-key enough to wear anywhere, specific enough that the right person will notice.

First batch is open for 10 days. If one design clearly wins, it becomes the direction for the next drop.
```

## Pricing Check

Inside Bonfire's live pricing/profit preview:

- Confirm the base cost for each product.
- Set a sale price that feels normal for a premium community tee.
- Target meaningful profit per tee without pushing the price into "novelty gift" territory.
- If profit looks too thin, reduce product options before raising price too high.
- Do not add paid extras unless they clearly improve the test.

## Product Page Quality Gate

Before launch, check:

- Does the first campaign image show the shirt clearly?
- Can someone understand the joke without scrolling?
- Does the campaign page explain the drop without over-explaining each joke?
- Are all shirt colors wearable?
- Is the price visible and sane?
- Is the batch end date clear?
- Are payout details connected to Josh's preferred payout method?

## Legal And Policy Gate

Do not publish if any design includes:

- Sports team names, logos, or colors used as a reference.
- University names, logos, or colors used as a reference.
- Superhero names, logos, or costume references.
- TV/movie/game characters.
- Song lyrics.
- Event logos or dance organization marks.
- Artwork copied from another artist, meme page, shirt, sticker, or Pinterest image.
- A design that resembles unlicensed apparel.

## Final Approval

Josh should approve:

- The exact shirt text.
- The exact art files.
- The final product colors.
- The final price and profit preview.
- The campaign page copy.
- The launch post copy.

Only after those are approved should the campaign go public.
