# Josh Shirt Launch - 04 Bonfire Launch Checklist

## Default Campaign Shape

Platform: Bonfire

Format: Limited batch campaign

Default batch length: 10 days

Product count: 3 shirts max

Recommended first product: unisex tee

Primary goal: find one shirt people actually want

Secondary goal: learn what kind of humor and design direction gets real response

## Before Bonfire Setup

Josh should decide:

- Which shirt is the lead design.
- Whether to launch 1 shirt or all 3 finalists.
- Whether the design is front-only or front/back.
- Which shirt colors work with the art.
- Whether he wants a limited campaign or evergreen availability.

Recommended:

Launch all 3 only if the setup is simple and the designs feel polished. If not, launch Commit On Eight first.

## Product Setup

For each product:

- Upload original artwork only.
- Confirm the design is readable in the Bonfire preview.
- Keep colors tight.
- Avoid too many product types in V1.
- Check front/back placement carefully.
- Confirm the design does not sit too low on the shirt.
- Screenshot the preview for Josh's final approval.

## Pricing Check

Use Bonfire's live price/profit preview.

Do not finalize pricing from this document because product cost and options can vary.

Pricing rules:

- Keep the price in normal premium tee territory.
- Do not chase maximum profit on V1.
- If profit is too low, simplify the product options before raising the price too high.
- Confirm estimated profit before publishing.

## Campaign Page Copy

Campaign title:

**Social Dance Debug Club: First Drop**

Campaign story:

```text
This first drop is for the dancers who live somewhere between the social floor and the group chat.

Swing dance has its own tiny language: timing, connection, recovery, rhythm, restraint, and the occasional extremely specific joke that only makes sense after midnight. These shirts are built for that overlap. Low-key enough to wear anywhere, specific enough that the right person will notice.

First batch is open for 10 days. If one design clearly wins, that becomes the direction for the next drop.
```

Candidate titles:

- Commit On Eight Tee
- No Drops In Prod Tee
- Push / Pull Request Tee

## Field Checklist

Before publishing, confirm:

- Campaign title is correct.
- Product names are clear.
- Shirt color choices are intentional.
- Campaign duration is set.
- Smart Launch setting matches Josh's preference.
- Payout method is connected.
- Campaign story is pasted and proofread.
- Product descriptions are clear.
- Images/previews are clean.
- No generated mockups are used as if they are final product photos.

## Legal / Policy Check

Do not publish if any artwork includes:

- Sports team marks.
- University names or logos.
- Superhero references.
- Movie/TV/game characters.
- Song lyrics.
- Dance event logos.
- Organization marks.
- Copied shirt art.
- Famous meme layouts too close to the original.
- Software platform logos or interface marks.

## Final Josh Approval

Josh should approve:

- Exact shirt text.
- Exact artwork.
- Product colors.
- Final price and profit preview.
- Campaign page copy.
- Launch copy.
- IP/safety notes.

## Do Not Publish Until

- Josh likes the design enough to wear it.
- The joke is clear without over-explanation.
- The design still looks good at phone size.
- The shirt feels like a real club drop, not a novelty shirt.
