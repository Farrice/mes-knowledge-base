## Day 2: Concept Breakdown

Post:

```text
The first rule for this drop:

No clip-art dancers.
No retro jukebox energy.
No giant "I love swing dancing" slogan.

The goal was quieter:

Make shirts that feel like something a dancer would wear outside of a dance event.

The joke should be specific, but the design should still be normal enough to survive a coffee run.

That is the whole experiment.

[Bonfire link]
```
