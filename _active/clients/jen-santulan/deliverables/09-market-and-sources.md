## 2026 vs 2025 — what changed (why "2026 data" matters)
- **Inventory loosening** — SFV condo listings up ~31% YoY → more choice, more room to negotiate.
- **Entry prices flat/softening** — LA County −0.6% YoY; SFV condos −5.5% YoY.
- **Rates stuck mid-6s** — the late-2025 dip reversed; "wait for rates to drop" underperformed.
- **NAR commission rules now in force** — a buyer may owe their agent's fee directly = a new cash-to-close line to budget.
- **CA insurance / FAIR-Plan crisis** — in fire-adjacent foothills (Sylmar, Sunland, Chatsworth fringe), insurance is now a *qualification* check, not an afterthought.
