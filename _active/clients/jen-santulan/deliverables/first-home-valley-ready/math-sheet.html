03

Assistance and program status

A program only helps when it is open and you fit its current rules.

Open*

CalHFA MyHome

Up to 3.5% for eligible FHA borrowers or 3% for eligible conventional borrowers. LA County income cap: $214,000 as of June 30, 2026.

Open*

CalPLUS + ZIP

Closing-cost support that may be paired with MyHome for eligible borrowers.

Limited*

LA City LIPA / MIPA

City of Los Angeles only, with strict location and income limits. The July benchmark household may exceed one or both limits.

Closed*

Dream For All

The 2026 registration window closed March 16. No next round had been announced at verification time.

04

Three questions that decide readiness

Questions you can calculate instead of relying on whether you feel ready.

Is combined household income near the benchmark, or is there a realistic path there?Include any qualified co-buyer and verify debts, credit, and lender rules.
Can you reach roughly $20K–$25K in cash-to-close while keeping reserves?Count eligible gifts or assistance only after the lender confirms the rules.
Would you stay in the home for about five years?A longer horizon gives the upfront costs and monthly premium more time to make sense.
Two or three “yes” or “almost” answers? That does not mean “buy now.” It means the next useful step is a verified scenario using your income, debts, cash, and target property.
