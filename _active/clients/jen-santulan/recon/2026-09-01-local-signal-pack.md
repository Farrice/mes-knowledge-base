## Selected three

### 1. Porter Ranch Saturday test

- **Person and tension:** A Valley-curious renter who cannot learn lived routine from listing photos.
- **Why selected:** Maximum local specificity, genuine overlap with Jen’s music/lifestyle voice, simple on-location production, and a natural “what would you test?” conversation.
- **Real-estate memory:** A neighborhood choice is a repeated-life decision, not an event endorsement.

### 2. LACDA assistance source literacy

- **Person and tension:** A serious renter attracted by an assistance headline but unsure whether the actual conditions fit.
- **Why selected:** Maximum audience relevance and direct First Home Valley fit; lets Jen earn trust by refusing to overstate eligibility.
- **Real-estate memory:** Program fit depends on current rules, property location, price, lender process, education, repayment terms, and individual circumstances.

### 3. G Line path change

- **Person and tension:** A Valley renter or buyer who walks, bikes, or uses the G Line corridor and needs a current route fact.
- **Why selected:** Time-sensitive local utility, visible B-roll, and a clean distinction between current disruption and future improvements.
- **Real-estate memory:** A route should be tested against the buyer’s actual origin, destination, and schedule; it is not a proxy for value or neighborhood quality.
