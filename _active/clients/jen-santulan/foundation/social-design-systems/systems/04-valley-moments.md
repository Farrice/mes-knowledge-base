# Valley Moments

## Vibe

Contemporary, energetic, cinematic, and emotionally observant. It translates travel-journal energy into the small moments that determine whether a neighborhood works.

## Use it for

Day-in-the-life posts, commute and arrival rituals, neighborhood movement, food and street moments, and short narrative lessons.

## Visual recipe

- Full-bleed cinematic image with deep lower shadows.
- Bold yellow sans carries the main idea.
- One short yellow script phrase adds feeling or completes the thought.
- Thin top and bottom rules provide a modern magazine shell.
- Handle and slide number live in a permanent footer.
- Small white spark marks may punctuate, never decorate every empty space.

Palette roles: black `#0B0B0A`; yellow `#F7E174`; warm white `#FFF8EA`; smoke `#6A6661`; tobacco `#A6734C`; rule `#CFC7B8`.

Typography: Inter Bold/ExtraBold for the argument; SignPainter HouseScript for one emotional phrase; Inter Medium for supporting copy.

## Slide rhythm

1. **Cover thesis:** the familiar thing reframed.
2. **Moment one:** a concrete physical or sensory beat.
3. **Moment two:** a different part of the routine.
4. **Realization:** what those moments reveal about fit.
5. **Close:** one practical question or next step.

## Allowed variation

The script can overlap the final sans line or sit in a gap. Body copy can occupy one or two zones. Images may be wide streets, windows, food, movement, dusk, or backlit human figures.

## Failure signs

- Full sentences in script.
- The script obscuring the sans or becoming the primary information layer.
- Bright midday stock photography.
- Three or more type styles.
- A narrative with no specific moment.
