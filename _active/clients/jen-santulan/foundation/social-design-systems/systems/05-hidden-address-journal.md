# Hidden Address Journal

## Vibe

Vintage, discerning, urban, and collected. It should feel like an address book kept by someone who notices the building, the block, and the place around the corner.

## Use it for

Listing and neighborhood pairings, hidden features, property-detail collages, premium local editions, and stories about what exists beyond the front door.

## Visual recipe

- Darkened full-bleed architecture, interior, plant, storefront, or property-detail photography.
- Butter or ivory high-contrast serif establishes a collectible edition voice.
- Tiny edition masthead centered at the top.
- Supporting copy uses a narrow centered measure.
- Feature slides may include two or three rectangular inset photos with thin ivory edges.
- One butter hand-drawn arrow or smile keeps the system human.
- No rounded cards.

Palette roles: black `#0D0D0B`; butter `#F3DE73`; ivory `#F8F4E8`; olive `#35372D`; brown `#735135`; smoke `#8A8376`.

Typography: Bodoni 72 Book Italic or comparable condensed high-contrast serif for display; Bodoni Smallcaps or Inter Medium for the edition masthead; Inter or Bodoni Book for short body copy.

## Slide rhythm

1. **Edition cover:** one address, theme, or hidden-value thesis.
2. **Place/property feature:** the main visual and one observed truth.
3. **Detail collage:** two or three evidence frames, not decoration.
4. **Neighborhood pairing:** what outside the door changes the experience.
5. **Close:** next-edition invitation or a real question.

## Allowed variation

Insets may sit on the lower third or overlap slightly. Titles may be butter, ivory, or a controlled mix. The arrow can point to evidence; the smile belongs only on a warm close.

## Failure signs

- “Hidden gem” as an unsupported cliché.
- Tiny collages that do not prove or reveal anything.
- Fake vintage filters or distressed textures added over clean images.
- Multiple arrows and stickers.
- Listing claims without verified ownership and current facts.

## Transfer test

The edition/serif/collage/arrow grammar reproduced from written rules with Valley imagery: **PASS FOR DIRECTION REVIEW**. Property-specific use remains gated by listing and image verification.
