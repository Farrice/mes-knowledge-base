# After Hours Guide

## Vibe

Cinematic, unhurried, cultured, and locally in-the-know. The post should feel like a small independent city magazine discovered at a favorite table after dark.

## Use it for

Curated local guides, restaurants and businesses, premium neighborhood atmosphere, and listings where place-feel matters more than a feature checklist.

## Visual recipe

- Full-bleed photography on every slide.
- Deep blacks, amber wood, warm interiors, golden-hour streets, and selective highlights.
- Oversized white editorial serif occupying roughly 35–50% of the frame.
- One compact detail block; one location label; one recurring series label.
- No cards, rounded rectangles, gradients-as-decoration, or busy icon systems.
- Use a lower shadow veil only to protect legibility.

Palette roles: shadow `#17110D`; espresso `#542E18`; brown `#704226`; taupe `#A99788`; paper `#F1ECE4`; type `#FFFFFF`.

Typography: Bodoni 72 Oldstyle Book or comparable high-contrast editorial serif for headlines; Inter for labels and short utility copy. An italic word may add movement, but the full headline should not become decorative script.

## Slide rhythm

1. **Cover:** large opinionated title over one atmospheric place image.
2. **Named feature:** location and one reason to care.
3. **Life fit:** the ordinary routine or feeling the place supports.
4. **Recommendation:** a clear Jen observation, not a generic five-star claim.
5. **Close:** one human question or next-guide invitation.

## Allowed variation

Headline may sit high, center, or low depending on the image focal point. Body copy may be left or centered. The palette may lean warmer or more neutral as long as black depth, white type, and the editorial hierarchy remain intact.

## Failure signs

- Bright MLS-style HDR photography.
- Multiple boxes or equal-weight text blocks.
- A headline shrunk to preserve a paragraph.
- “Luxury” signaled with gold rules instead of photographic atmosphere.
- A generic local list with no Jen observation.

## Transfer test

The local proof rendered from these written rules and Valley photography is recognizably in the same visual family without loading the reference slides: **PASS FOR DIRECTION REVIEW**. Jen taste approval remains pending.
