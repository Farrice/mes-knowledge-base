# Sunlit Local Notes

## Vibe

Curious, friendly, lightly handmade, and genuinely local. It should feel like Jen marked up a neighborhood photograph for a friend—not like a municipal tourism campaign.

## Use it for

Local gems, weekend routines, parks, coffee, small businesses, neighborhood walks, and friendly saveable lists.

## Visual recipe

- Full-bleed green or textured street-level photography.
- Yellow editorial serif is the identity signal across every slide.
- One or two white hand-drawn marks per slide: arrow, circle, loose star, underline, or route.
- One small handwritten note or series label; never a paragraph in script.
- Asymmetric placement with visible breathing room.
- Body copy stays conversational and close to the observed detail.

Palette roles: forest `#172018`; moss `#344131`; sun `#F0DB6D`; parchment `#E8E0D4`; rust `#704530`; chalk `#FBF8F1`.

Typography: Bodoni 72 Book or soft editorial serif for the title; Inter for readable body copy; SignPainter only for annotations.

## Slide rhythm

1. **Cover:** a local promise with one memorable yellow title.
2. **Gem:** place or routine with a location cue.
3. **Why it matters:** Jen's specific lived-in observation.
4. **Try this:** a small routine or neighborhood test.
5. **Close:** save, follow, or tell Jen what to explore next.

## Allowed variation

Marks may be loose and imperfect. Yellow may appear as title or small repeated label. Image subjects can include foliage, storefronts, paths, benches, food, or local people with permission.

## Failure signs

- Scribbles added only because the reference had scribbles.
- Perfect symmetry or rigid card grids.
- Yellow used on pale areas without a shadow or crop adjustment.
- Tourist-board superlatives.
- An “undiscovered” or “best” claim without evidence.
