# Shared Jen Layer

This is the only layer shared across all five visual systems.

## Fixed

- Canvas: 1080×1350, Instagram portrait 4:5.
- Signature: `@_JIING`, small and secondary.
- Voice: calm, warm, specific, locally observant, and protective without hype.
- Copy: one idea per slide; 8–18 headline words; no more than 45 supporting words.
- Structure: cover promise, earned swipe, one idea per slide, human close.
- Imagery: owned or licensed, with provenance; correct property and owner every time.
- Claims: date/source volatile facts; distinguish fact, Jen POV, and placeholder proof copy.
- Fair housing: no steering, demographic proxies, coded neighborhood claims, or buyer qualification language.
- Brands: use only approved actual marks. Never redraw Equity Union or House Sellers from memory.
- Action: no publishing, scheduling, outreach, or connector writes without approval.

## Variable

- Palette, typography grammar, photo grade, compositional rhythm, motifs, masthead, and CTA treatment remain native to each system.
- Jen's face is optional. Use it when her presence strengthens trust; do not paste it into every cover.
- Slide count may vary from 3–10. Five to seven is the normal range.
- The CTA can invite a real conversation without forced keyword gimmicks.

## Release check

- Does the post sound like Jen after one natural rewrite?
- Is every image owned, licensed, or explicitly approved?
- Is any property actually hers or the named agent's?
- Are factual claims source-dated and still current?
- Does each slide have one dominant idea and readable contrast?
- Did the selected visual system stay internally coherent?
- Has Jen approved the final voice and visual direction?
