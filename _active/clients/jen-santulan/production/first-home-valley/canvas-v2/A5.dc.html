FIRST-TIME BUYER FILE
the three questions
that actually decide it
01
can you and whoever you’d buy with reach about $147K combined?
that’s what SRAR says it takes to carry the median valley condo right now. 37% of 2026 buyers team up with a sibling, friend, or parent to get there (u.s. news, spring 2026)
02
can you get your hands on $20–25K for cash-to-close?
a family gift counts — about 1 in 5 first-time buyers used one. almost nobody puts 20% down anymore (nar 2025)
03
would you stay in the place about five years?
that’s the line where the monthly premium stops being a cost and starts being equity that’s yours
