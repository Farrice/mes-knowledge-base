## THE DAY OF RECORDING (Single 2–3 hour session)

### Pre-Recording Logistics (1 hour before)
- [ ] **Room setup:**
  - Lighting: test daylight, adjust curtains for even face light (no hard shadows)
  - Background: clean, neutral, out of focus (white wall or soft living room)
  - Tripod/gimbal: camera locked, framed on Jen shoulder-up, eye-line to lens
  - Sound check: wireless mic battery at 100%, levels peaking at -12 to -6 dB
  - Teleprompter: script loaded on iPad/tablet, placed below camera lens (eye-line minimal drift)
- [ ] **Jen setup:**
  - Wardrobe: casual-warm (what she'd wear to a coffee date, not formal). Avoid high-contrast stripes, busy patterns. Solid jewel-tone or cream/tan.
  - Hair/makeup: natural, what she normally does (not over-done for camera)
  - Hydration: water nearby, lip balm
  - Energy: one informal chat before rolling (reset vibe to "talking to a friend, not performing")

### Recording Order (Efficiency Hack: NOT in script order, but by setup)
**Reason:** every setup change (location, lighting, B-roll) costs time; batch by location to minimize transitions

**Shot 1: Kitchen table B-roll (5–10 min)** `[0:00 cold open + 11:20 close, plus inserts]`
- Hands at notebook: writing a date, circling a number, pointing at a column
- Coffee sip

- DO: multiple takes of each gesture; use for inserts throughout

**Shot 2: On-camera warm sit (main VO, 30–45 min)** `[entire 12-min script, on-cam sections]`

- Teleprompter ready below camera

## CONTINGENCIES

### If Jen Can't Record in Person
- **Fallback:** remote Zoom recording (clean background, good mic, lighting from ring light)
- **Trade-off:** loses warmth of natural daylight + on-location B-roll authenticity
- **Mitigation:** double down on B-roll budget; use more screen-recording math moments; supplement with text overlays

### If Any On-Camera Take Doesn't Pass Vibe
- **Quick fix:** re-record that section same day (15–20 min, full reset)
- **If not available:** use second-best take; editor can tighten pacing/tighten audio to hide hesitation
- **Last resort:** record section via remote video call (quality drop, but acceptable for web)

### If a B-Roll Location Falls Through
- **Entry condo:** use nearby similar property (Zillow tours as reference), or high-quality exterior stock video
- **Foothill street:** Golden Valley or nearby SFV hill community works as substitute
- **Key-handoff:** use professional stock video if client not available

### If Thumbnail Generation Delays
- **Fallback:** use still from the 40-isn't-late segment (Jen on-camera + animated stat, bold text overlay) as temp
- **Timeline:** thumbnail can be updated up to 48 hours before publish (platform allows)
