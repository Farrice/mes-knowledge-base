# Creative brief — Gigi Mironova

**For a cowork session.** Everything needed to build the concept is here or one path away.
Research is done. The design standard is set. Do not re-derive either.

---

## The assignment

Build a finished concept — not a proposal — that makes Gigi want to hire Farrice for a
content package. She should see her own brand done properly and want the rest of it.

## Who she is

Gigi Mironova. Realtor at Equity Union, on the My House Sellers team — Jen Santulan's team.
Serves the San Fernando Valley and Conejo Valley, and takes deals well outside both.
English and Russian. **624 posts. 1,252 followers.**

She is one of the harder-working agents on the team. Jen rates her. Farrice has a real
relationship with her and wants her to win. This is a gift with an offer attached.

## The insight

Buried in a feed of listing announcements is the one post she clearly wrote herself:

> *"I work hard because I can't f*ck up. I don't have anyone to fall back on.
> **I'm the back up.**"*

She meant it about her own life — no safety net, no cushion, no one behind her.

But that is exactly the person you want handling the largest transaction of your life.
Someone who has never had a back up understands, in a way a comfortable person cannot,
what it means to *be* one. Her scarcity is the source of her clients' security.

**Her weaknesses are the proof, once you turn them over.** She takes a deal in Fresno
because someone needed it done. She has posted 624 times to an audience of 1,252 — which
is a content problem and a character reference at the same time. She does not stop.

## Single-minded proposition

> **No one was ever my back up. So I'll be yours.**

Everything in the concept ladders to that sentence. If a slide doesn't, cut it.

## Reasons to believe

- She works from Conejo to Fresno — she goes where her client needs the deal closed.
- English and Russian — she meets people in the language they think in.
- Her team is **#1 medium team in Calabasas** by transaction sides *and* sales volume,
  RealTrends verified. Her proof is already published; she buries it in a highlight.
- 624 posts. Say it as evidence of the person, never as a metric.

## Tone

Her voice is blunt, unglamorous, slightly profane, and completely unposed. She is not
luxury and should never be dressed as luxury — her buyers work for a living and so does she.

**The tension to hold, and the whole craft of this job: premium execution, plain-spoken
voice.** The design floor is editorial and expensive-looking. The words on it should sound
like a woman who has closed a deal at 9pm on a Sunday. If the copy starts sounding like a
brochure, the concept has failed even if it looks beautiful.

## What to make

Enough to win the meeting. Not a full system.

1. **Positioning line and bio rewrite.** Her bio currently opens "Helping you navigate LA
   Real Estate," which is what every agent says. Replace it. Her own line is the seed.
2. **One 6-slide carousel** in the house grammar, built on the back-up platform.
3. **Three reel covers.**
4. **The nine highlight covers.** Currently nine identical brokerage badges — the single
   cheapest visible win available, and it costs her nothing to adopt.
5. **The offer, one page.** The $200 founding rate already exists at
   `_active/clients/jen-team-pilot/OFFER-BRIEF.md`.

## Mandatories

- **The design floor is `DESIGN.md` in this folder.** Farrice's standard, in his words.
  Below it is a regression.
- **Give her her own colour.** Do not ship Jen's navy. Recommendation #2 in `DESIGN.md` —
  key the duotone to a different hue per agent. Same grammar, unmistakably hers.
- **Real photographs only.** CC0 or Public Domain Mark, provenance recorded. No generated
  images. No posed stock families.
- **Zero operator language in anything she sees.** No diagnosis of her mistakes inside the
  artifact. Operator notes go in a separate paired file.
- **Her brokerage lockup does not get to outrank her name.** It currently does, on nearly
  every graphic. Fixing that is half the concept.
- Fair-housing lint before delivery — `_active/clients/re-compliance/`.
- Label VERIFIED / UNCONFIRMED on every claim about her business.

## Parked, deliberately

**The Russian-language lane is the bigger long-term position** — uncontested, uncopyable,
and dead in her bio right now. It is lane two, not the lead, because national origin is a
protected class and the framing has to be exact before it ships: language is a service
claim, "where Russians buy" is a licence problem. Keep it as one honest line in the bio
and pitch it as the next engagement. Full constraint in `GIGI-KICKOFF.md`.

## What kills this

- Anything that reads as a teardown of her work. She is being generous with her time by
  looking at all.
- Luxury register. Wrong market, wrong woman.
- Turning "no safety net" into a sob story. It is a credential, not a wound. She says it
  flatly; the concept should too.
- Beautiful design carrying brochure copy.
