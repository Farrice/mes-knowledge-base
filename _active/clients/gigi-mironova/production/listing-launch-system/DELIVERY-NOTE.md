# Gigi's two-mode content identity

The Calm Closer remains the everyday identity. It gives Gigi a recognizable way to explain documents, market decisions, and the practical side of buying or selling.

Property Dossier now has a narrower, stronger role: it launches listings. The photographs are allowed to carry desire, while the typography and copy make the information feel considered. It is not a second brand competing for attention. It is the campaign expression of the same point of view.

The Unit 124 set proves four different content jobs. The reveal introduces the feeling of the space. The kitchen story turns one update into a lived benefit. The financial board explains a specific comparison and labels it as an estimate. The final board invites an in-person showing without inventing an event date.

The accompanying gift deck explains the system in six quiet pages. It presents the work as something useful, not as a demand that Gigi adopt a rebrand. The image standard then makes the direction portable: choose one dominant photograph, protect architectural depth, keep text off active details, and remove copy before shrinking it.

No existing Calm Closer or Property Dossier asset was changed. This package is an extension built entirely from Unit 124 photography already approved for use in the project.
