# Senior-pass review

## Verdict

The senior pass replaces the recovery fixture as the acceptance anchor. The earlier fixture remains visible for regression comparison.

## Why it wins

- The living-room crop enters closer and gives the furniture more scale.
- The kitchen panel starts lower, creating tension instead of a polite equal-height split.
- “The remodel has a date” expresses Gigi's practical authority rather than repeating listing language.
- The date block creates a second reading beat without adding decoration.
- The left clay rule, restrained labels, and Calm Closer file language make the board identifiable without overpowering the property.
- Supporting copy, address, price, license, and CTA remain legible at thumbnail scale.

## Regression guard

Future Property Dossier boards must preserve one property-led image argument, one concrete fact, one asymmetric editorial decision, and the modern sans backbone. A repeated photo-over-headline template fails even if its colors and fonts match.
