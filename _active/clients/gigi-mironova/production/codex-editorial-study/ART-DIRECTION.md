# The Property Dossier

## Thesis

A private real-estate intelligence briefing disguised as an architecture journal—not another agent carousel.

## Three anchors

- **Visual hook:** severe photo crops, oversized editorial numbers, asymmetric folios
- **Emotional core:** calm access to information other buyers overlook
- **Cultural anchor:** architecture-journal restraint crossed with a financial newspaper's evidence hierarchy

## Five-layer direction

- **Color:** preserved HouseSellers navy, warm paper and clay, used in flatter and more disciplined roles
- **Texture:** uncoated editorial paper; no glass, shadows, gradients or ornamental geometry
- **Typography:** Newsreader for high-authority display statements; Manrope for evidence and metadata
- **Photography:** real Unit 124 photographs, cropped as editorial plates rather than social-media backgrounds
- **References:** independent architecture publishing, private-bank reports, auction catalogues and Sunday financial supplements

## Deliberate departures from The Calm Closer

- No cards, pills or floating evidence panels
- No repeated centered social layout
- No background motifs
- Serif display type replaces highlighted sans headlines
- Vertical folios and hard rules create publication rhythm
- Three boards function as a cover, evidence spread and private briefing

## Acceptance

The direction succeeds only if it remains phone-readable, looks materially different beside the preserved system, and feels premium after every decorative element is removed.
