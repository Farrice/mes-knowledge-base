## Before anything posts · the don't-say list

- Describe the place and the process, never the people. No "families," "safe," "quiet neighborhood," "great for," "perfect for," no schools on camera, no landmarks tied to any group.
- Speaking Russian is a service you offer. It is never a description of an area or of who lives there, in either language.
- Keep "estimate, not a quote" wherever the $2,535 appears. Rate, credit, and insurance all move it.
- Say "about the same, before equity." Never "owning is cheaper than renting."
- The $620 is the dues on your listing. Say it plainly. Don't extend it to other units or other buildings.
- "Inspection and repairs completed" is the line on your listing. Don't say the association holds a certificate.
- The 17, 17, and 21-day windows are the standard contract's defaults and they are negotiable. Say so.
- The Valley concession figures in the seller pieces are one broker's compilation. They stay in captions with that label; don't quote them as fact on camera.
- No "guaranteed," no "can't lose," no "won't last."
- If you boost anything, never target by language or demographics. Housing ads have special rules on every platform.
