**2. The tenant-notice rule is stated backwards, and the error is put in CAA's mouth.**
Current: *"Timing: written notice of intent to sell 120 days before showings begin, then 24-hour notice per showing. Per California Apartment Association guidance."*
Where: SCRIPT-PACK Video 12 IG caption; same figures in CALENDAR Video 12 Ledger, the Pack B ledger row 12, DEMAND-REPORT seller item 2.
Wrong because: Civil Code §1954(d)(2) says the reverse. The written notice is not a lead time — it is written notice that the property is for sale, and **for 120 days after it is given** showings may be arranged by *oral* notice, in person or by phone, with 24 hours presumed reasonable; written evidence of entry is left in the unit. The CAA page cited states this correctly, so the citation also misattributes our misreading.
Replace with: *"Timing: your tenant gets written notice that the property is for sale. For 120 days after that notice, showings can be arranged with 24 hours' notice, and that notice can be by phone. California Civil Code 1954. Your lease and your attorney govern."*
