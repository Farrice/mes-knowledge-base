## Don't say

1. Describe the place and the process, never the people. No "safe", no "family", no "great for", no schools on camera.
2. Russian is a service you offer, never a description of an area or its people.
3. Keep "estimate, not a quote" anywhere the payment appears.
4. The balcony line is the listing's sentence about this unit, not a building certificate.
5. Never say the unit is listed for rent.
6. No "guaranteed", no "won't last".
