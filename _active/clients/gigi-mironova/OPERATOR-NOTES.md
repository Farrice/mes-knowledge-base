## Why escrow, and why the clock

The wedge is the Russian lane. The *subject* had to be something where the American system
genuinely differs from what a newcomer expects — otherwise "explained in Russian" is a
translation service, not an authority position. Escrow timing qualifies: the neutral
third-party holder and the contingency clock have no direct equivalent in the post-Soviet
property transaction, and the deadlines are the part that costs real money when missed.

It also passes fair housing cleanly, which most "content for Russian speakers" ideas do
not. **This is a service claim about a system, not a claim about a group.** No area is
described by who lives there. No language anywhere implies a neighborhood suits anyone.
Run `_active/clients/re-compliance/` before anything ships.

One refinement deliberately left off the slide to keep it clean, worth knowing: if a final

re-check the forms before publishing. The slide credits C.A.R. explicitly, which is the
right level of caution.
