## Definition of the gift (what "done" means)

A package Gigi can use same-day, containing:

| Piece | Standard |
|---|---|
| Carousel, 6 slides, export-ready | Real math if the HOA lands; her listing's real numbers; zero homework left to the reader that we could do ourselves |
| 3 reel scripts | Teleprompter-ready, ~80–90 words, HER voice (sampled from her own published language + client reviews), spoken numbers clean |
| Captions | Carousel + each reel; 2–3 pick-one options each |

| DM keyword | **"124"** — unit-specific, bilingual-neutral, replaces nothing she already runs |
| 9 highlight covers | Actual 1:1 uploadable files, not a mockup slide |
| Don't-say list | Fair-housing + condo-specific, one page |

## Voice rules

- Sample before writing: her EU bio paragraph, her IG bio, her one known personal post,
  and her Yelp client reviews (what clients say she's like = how she actually operates).
- The 16-years line must be accurate: litigation support = research/analysis/negotiation
  support. Never "I read contracts for buyers" as a claim of practice.
- No aphorism-writer flexes. If she wouldn't say it out loud unedited, rewrite it.
- Warm, direct, competent. Bilingual lines only where something real is published in
  Russian (C5RU + R3 + one story frame).

## Facts discipline

- HOA: hunt hard (Movoto, Rocket Homes, Shoreline, Redfin via browser). Found → the
  rent-vs-own math goes ON the slide with rate/tax/insurance assumptions labeled.
  Not found → the "fourth number" frame stays and the kit says plainly which number
  unlocks the upgrade. Never a guessed figure.
- Mortgage rate: verify current before any payment math; label as "at X.X%, estimate".
- Everything else already VERIFIED in DEMAND-BRIEF.md / OPERATOR-NOTES.md stands.
