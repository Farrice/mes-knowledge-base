# Gigi Mironova — The Calm Closer

The character is not a mascot or invented personality. It is the elevated visual form of
what her brokerage bio already proves: sixteen years reading consequential documents,
followed by a real-estate license.

## What people should feel

**Clear enough to trust. Warm enough to call.** Gigi should feel exacting without being
clinical, protective without being alarmist, and composed without becoming generic luxury.
Every visual move must help someone understand a consequential choice.

## Visual behavior

- Warm architectural paper carries explanation; softened twilight blue carries emotion.
- HouseSellers blue is the recognition anchor, not the whole canvas.
- Clay marks the one human or consequential detail on a board. It never carries body copy.
- Evidence cards, document tabs, receipt-like rules, and one clay marker repeat across assets.
- Background ornament is deliberately absent: no arches, ghost numerals, or decorative geometry.
- Figtree/Manrope carries everything. No script, handwriting, italic, or decorative serif.
- Large numbers are proof objects. Supporting copy lives beside or beneath them, never over them.
- Photography supplies place or person. It is never present merely to make a slide look expensive.

## Composition rule

Each slide gets one visual argument: comparison, cost stack, evidence card, document set,
timeline, portrait, or CTA. Repeat the character, not the layout.

## Imagery rule

Use Gigi's real portrait and her actual listing photography first. Atmospheric CC0 images
may establish Los Angeles or housing context, but cannot impersonate the property. The unit
photography remains a deliberate drop-in until rights-cleared originals are available.

## Non-negotiables

- Source and date consequential numbers.
- Russian is a service layer, not an invented identity claim.
- Fair-housing lint before delivery.
- Full-resolution inspection for Latin and Cyrillic boards.
- Client-facing surfaces contain no diagnosis of her current work.
