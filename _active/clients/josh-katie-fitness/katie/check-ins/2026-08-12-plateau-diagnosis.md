## Culprit
Three stacked, in order of weight:
1. **Recomp arithmetic (the big one).** She's swapping fat for muscle at roughly equal mass. A trained woman responding well can add ~0.25–0.5 lb muscle/month [LIKELY — trained-female hypertrophy rates]; recomposition at or near maintenance with heavy training + ~150 g protein is established in trained lifters [VERIFIED — Barakat et al. 2020 review; carried in the master brief]. Equal-mass swap = flat scale + smaller body (muscle is ~18% denser than fat).
2. **Compliance, not metabolism.** The #1 execution lever — ox bile + enzymes with fat-containing meals — is **not being taken at all**. Fat malabsorption bloat masks progress on both scale and waist [LIKELY — per her health brief]; it also degrades absorption of vitamin D, which her progesterone picture depends on. Additionally: NEAT (11–13k steps) is the entire designed deficit and is currently unverified, and zero check-ins are logged — the tracker's own check-in triggers cannot fire.
3. **Water noise on a small frame.** Late-luteal phase alone is +1–2 lb [VERIFIED — cyclical water retention]; on a 134-lb body that's the whole "distance to 133" some weeks.

**Not the culprit: a damaged metabolism.** "Slow metabolism" in dieters is dominantly a 200–300 kcal NEAT drop plus ~50–100 kcal adaptive thermogenesis [Aragon/Levine line] — controllable, not broken. Her plan already holds the three preservation levers (modest deficit, heavy lifting, high protein).
