*Low-CNS amplifier — band/metabolite bias on purpose: stimulus without recovery cost. Glute max recovers from Monday; glute med + quads get a different action; shoulders/arms are fresh; tomorrow's hip thrust is unaffected.*

1. [CIRCUIT] Glute-Med Burner — 3 rounds, 45 s rest between rounds:
   - Banded Lateral Walk [Glute Med] — x 12/direction — Cue: stay low, constant band tension.
   - Banded Abduction Bridge [Glutes, Glute Med] — x 15 + 10 s hold — Cue: knees drive out against band at top.
   - Curtsy Lunge (bodyweight or light DB) [Glutes, Quads] — x 10/leg — Cue: control the crossover, no knee cave.
2. Heel-Elevated Goblet Squat (plate under heels) [Quads] — 3 x 12-15 · Rest 60 s · Tempo 3-0-1-0 · RPE 9 — Cue: upright torso, knees travel forward, chase the quad burn.
3. [SS-B1] DB Lateral Raise [Side Delts] — 3 x 12-15 · Rest 0 → B2 · Tempo 2-0-1-0 · RPE 9 — Cue: lead with elbows, no swing.
4. [SS-B2] Seated DB Arnold Press [Delts] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 60 s after pair · Tempo 2-0-1-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: light-moderate; this is pump, not pressing strength.
5. [SS-C1] DB Bicep Curl [Biceps] — 2 x 12-15 · Rest 0 → C2 · RPE 8 — Cue: no swing, squeeze at top. **NEW v2.1: arms.**
6. [SS-C2] DB Triceps Kickback [Triceps] — 2 x 12-15 · Rest 45 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: elbow pinned high, full lockout. **NEW v2.1: arms.**
7. [CIRCUIT] Lower-Ab Finisher — 2 rounds, 30 s rest:
   - Hanging Knee/Leg Raise [Lower Abs] — x 8-12 — Cue: no swing; curl pelvis up at top.
   - Flutter Kicks [Lower Abs] — x 20 s — Cue: low back stays down.
   - Band Pallof Press [Obliques, Anti-rotation] — x 10/side — Cue: resist the pull, ribs stacked.
