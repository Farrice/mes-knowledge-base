# KATIE · MONDAY — Foundational 1 (Squat-led) · ~80 min
**Block:** Strong & Lean v2.1 · Master: [`../programs/Katie_FullBody_Recomp_v2.md`](../programs/Katie_FullBody_Recomp_v2.md)
*Format: `Name [muscles] — sets x reps · Rest · Tempo (ecc-pause-con-pause, X = explosive) · RPE — Cue`. `[SS-]` = superset pair, `[CIRCUIT]` = back-to-back rounds. "NEW v2.1" = added this pass.*

**Movement prep (5 min):** ankle rocks · deep-squat hold 2×20 s · leg swings · band glute activation.
**Warm-up ramp before squats:** 12 light → 8 medium → 3 near working weight, 30–60 s between.

1. Barbell Back Squat [Quads, Glutes] — 4 x 5 · Rest 2.5–3 min · Tempo 3-1-X-1 · RPE 8 — Cue: brace before descent; pristine form is the entry ticket for heavy — stop on any back pain, zero grinding reps.
2. Heel-Elevated Goblet Squat (plates under heels) [Quads] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 90 s · Tempo 3-1-1-0 · RPE 8-9 — Cue: upright torso, knees travel forward, sink deep — this is pure quad ROM the flat-foot back squat doesn't reach. **NEW v2.1: your quad-development add.**
3. Dumbbell Bench Press [Chest, Front Delts, Triceps] — 3 x 6-8 · Rest 2 min · Tempo 2-1-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: full stretch at the bottom, elbows ~45°.
4. [SS-A1] Bulgarian Split Squat [Quads, Glutes] — 2 x 10-12/leg · Rest 0 → A2 · Tempo 2-1-1-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: front-foot pressure through midfoot; torso slightly forward for glute bias.
5. [SS-A2] Single-Arm DB Row [Lats, Upper Back] — 2 x 10-12/arm · Rest 75 s after pair · Tempo 2-0-1-1 · RPE 8 — Cue: pull to hip, no torso twist.
6. [SS-B1] DB Incline Curl [Biceps] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 0 → B2 · Tempo 2-0-1-1 · RPE 8 — Cue: bench on a slight incline, elbows behind the body for the full biceps stretch. **NEW v2.1: arms.**
7. [SS-B2] DB Overhead Triceps Extension [Triceps] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 60 s after pair · Tempo 2-1-1-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: elbows tight, full stretch behind the head. **NEW v2.1: arms.**
8. Banded Hip Thrust [Glutes] — 2 x 15-20 · Rest 45 s · Tempo 1-2-X-0 · RPE 9 — Cue: 2-second squeeze at top; chin tucked.
9. [CIRCUIT] Lower-Ab Block — 2 rounds, 30 s rest between rounds:
   - Hanging Knee/Leg Raise [Lower Abs] — x 8-12 — Cue: no swing, curl the pelvis up. **NEW v2.1: harder lower-ab loader.**
   - Reverse Crunch [Lower Abs] — x 12-15 — Cue: posterior pelvic tilt starts the rep.
   - Dead Bug [Deep Core] — x 8/side — Cue: ribs down, low back glued to mat.
   - Hollow Hold [Abs] — x 20-30 s — Cue: lower back pressed into floor.
10. Farmer Carry [Grip, Core, Traps] — 2 x 40 yd · Rest 60 s · heavy DBs · RPE 7-8 — Cue: tall posture, quick steps.

---
**Session rules:** Load autoreg — all sets at top of range → +5 lb upper / +10 lb lower next session; 2+ reps under the bottom → −10%. **The v2.1 accessory adds (goblet squat, arm superset, hanging raise) are the FIRST cut if sleep drops under ~6 h two nights or the scale bounces with poor sleep — the barbell primaries are protected.** Performance week → foundationals-lite, RPE ≤7-8, drop the arm superset. Week 6 = deload (2×5 @ ~70%, half sets everywhere).
