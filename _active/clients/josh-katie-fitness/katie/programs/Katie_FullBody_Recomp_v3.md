**Phase posture:** Plateau-breaking recomposition. The scale is stuck at 134–135 but the recomp is working — muscle replacing fat, waist/thigh moving, strength adapting. v3 doesn't cut food. v3 changes the *training stimulus* to increase metabolic cost, EPOC, and lactate accumulation while preserving the heavy anchors that drive her recomposition. > ## v3 DESIGN NOTES — what changed and why
>
> **Current state (entering this block):** ~134-135 lb, recomping successfully (measurements improving, scale stuck). Strength held or improved on all primaries. Gallbladder protocol **NOT being taken** — the single biggest unforced error in the system.
>
> **The metabolic shift thesis:** v2 was built as a straight strength block with controlled pump volume. It worked — she got stronger and leaner. But the same stimulus at the same density with the same rest periods eventually becomes metabolically efficient (the body adapts to the energy cost, not just the muscular demand). v3 keeps the heavy anchors but transforms all accessory work into **density circuits, timed supersets, and EMOM blocks** — same movements, dramatically higher metabolic cost per minute.
>
> **Key changes from v2.1:**
> - Anchor lifts: **UNCHANGED** — 4 working sets, same progression logic. These are the recomp engine and they don't get touched.
> - Accessories: restructured from straight sets into **density blocks** — timed circuits, EMOMs, and supersets with controlled rest. Same movements, 30-40% more metabolic cost.
> - Triggers: overhauled from isolation work into **full-body metabolic circuits** with a conditioning bias. ~35 min sessions that drive heart rate and caloric burn while still targeting glutes/shoulders/abs.
> - Friday Foundational 3: finishes with a **5-minute AMRAP** (as many rounds as possible) density finisher — timed, scored, progressive.
> - Weekly Zone-2 walks: **unchanged** — the cheapest fat-loss tool in the system.
> - Rest periods: compressed on ALL non-anchor work (60-90 s → 30-45 s within blocks; anchor rest stays at 2.5-3 min).
>
> **VOLUME GOVERNOR (the honesty layer).** Density work is higher systemic stress than straight sets at the same volume. The total SET COUNT is the same or slightly lower than v2.1 — the cost increase comes from reduced rest and circuit structure, not added volume. **If sleep drops under ~6 h for two nights, or strength index falls two weeks running, the density blocks revert to straight sets with full rest** — the anchors are NEVER the variable. The Week-8 deload and the AUTOREG rules enforce this.
>
> **⚠️ GATING CONDITION: GALLBLADDER PROTOCOL.** This program will not produce scale movement if the ox bile + enzyme protocol is not being taken daily. Fat malabsorption → bloating → water retention → scale reads 1-2 lbs heavy → frustration → bad decisions. **The protocol is not optional — it's step 0.** If she's not doing it, the conversation starts there, not with program changes.

## PROGRESSION — 8-WEEK METABOLIC SHIFT WAVE
| Week | Heavy Anchors (squat / hip thrust / front squat / OHP / pull-up / DB bench) | Density Blocks | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 x 5 (4 x 5-6 hip thrust / OHP) — CALIBRATE all loads | Calibrate density loads — lighter than v2 straight-set loads; learn the pacing | RPE 7-8 |
| 2 | Same sets, +5 lb upper / +10 lb lower if all top-reps hit | As written, aim for target rounds | RPE 8 |
| 3 | Hold loads | **Extend density block time caps by 1 min** (8 → 9, 6 → 7) — earn more rounds | RPE 8 |
| 4 | Progress if reps hold | Hold extended caps | RPE 8-9 |
| 5 | 5 x 4 (add a set, drop a rep) | Hold caps, focus on faster transitions (less intra-block rest) | RPE 8-9 |
| 6 | 5 x 3-4, heavier | **Extend density block time caps by 1 more min** (9 → 10, 7 → 8) | RPE 9 |
| 7 | 4 x 3 — heaviest clean triples of the block | Density blocks at peak caps — score PRs this week | RPE 9 |
| 8 | **DELOAD:** 2 x 5 @ ~70% of week-7 load | Density blocks = half time caps, half effort. Triggers = walks only. | RPE ≤6 |

**Density progression logic:** The density blocks progress by TIME (longer caps = more rounds = more work in the same movements), not by load. Load increases happen on the ANCHORS only. This separates the strength signal (anchors) from the metabolic signal (density) — both progress, neither interferes with the other.

**Load autoregulation (anchors, every week):** all sets at/above the top of the rep range → +5 lb upper / +10 lb lower next session. Two or more reps below the bottom of the range → −10% and rebuild.

**AUTOREG: PERFORMANCE WEEK** — violin performance/travel week: run foundationals only (Mon/Wed/Fri), ALL density blocks revert to straight sets with full rest, triggers become optional walks. Performance quality outranks the program. No make-up sessions.

**AUTOREG: CYCLE** — late-luteal week (track in the tracker): expect +1-2 lb scale water — do not read it as stalling; optional RPE cap 8; swap Trigger B for the Zone-2 walk alone if sleep was poor.

**AUTOREG: SLEEP** — under ~6 h two nights running: cap anchors at RPE 7, density blocks revert to straight sets with full rest. Recovery is the growth phase, not the workout.

**AUTOREG: DENSITY REVERSION** — if strength index on ANY anchor falls two weeks running, ALL density blocks revert to v2-style straight sets until strength stabilizes. The density is the experiment; the anchors are the spine.

## WHAT CHANGED vs v2.1 — AND WHY (evidence-labeled)
1. **Accessories restructured from straight sets into timed density blocks.** Same exercises, same weekly volume (~14-16 sets for priority muscles), but compressed rest drives higher heart rate, greater lactate accumulation, and ~15-25% more caloric expenditure per session. [Density/circuit training producing greater EPOC and session caloric cost vs traditional resistance training at matched volume: **VERIFIED** — Paoli et al., 2012; Kelleher et al., 2010.]
2. **Anchor lifts UNCHANGED.** Heavy low-rep primaries are the recomp engine — they create the mechanical tension signal for muscle protein synthesis. Changing the accessory structure provides metabolic variety; changing the anchors would compromise the adaptation she's best at. [Heavy RT + high protein recomp in trained women: **VERIFIED** per the 2025 review.]
3. **Triggers overhauled into full-body metabolic circuits.** v2 triggers were isolation-biased (glute circuits + shoulder pump). v3 triggers alternate upper/lower movements to maintain elevated heart rate while allowing local recovery — metabolic conditioning disguised as hypertrophy. [Alternating upper/lower in circuits: higher HR, similar RPE, lower local fatigue: **LIKELY** — circuit training literature, widely practiced.]
4. **Friday AMRAP finisher added.** A scored, progressive, timed density test replaces the TGU/swing option. It's competitive against herself (beat last week's score), which drives effort without RPE guessing. Placed AFTER anchors with a kill-switch so it never cannibalized the strength work. [Scored conditioning finishers improving adherence/effort: **LIKELY** — coaching observation, widely reported in CrossFit/MAPS literature.]
5. **Density progression is by TIME, not load.** Longer caps = more rounds = more metabolic work, without requiring heavier weights that compete with anchor recovery. This separates the two training goals cleanly: strength (anchors, progress by load) and body composition (density, progress by time-under-tension and work capacity). [Periodization of training density as an independent variable: **LIKELY** — supported by block periodization principles, limited direct RCT.]
6. **Gallbladder protocol promoted from a tracking column to a GATING CONDITION.** This is a language change, not a protocol change. The science hasn't changed — but the coaching framing has. "It would be nice" → "the program literally cannot work without this." [Ox bile + enzymes post-cholecystectomy: physiologically sound and OTC-safe — **LIKELY**; direct RCT evidence is thin.]
7. **Performance-week, cycle, and sleep autoregulation preserved** with density-specific reversion rules added. If stress/sleep/cycle disrupts recovery, density blocks revert to straight sets FIRST — the anchors are protected, and the metabolic stimulus is the flexible variable.
