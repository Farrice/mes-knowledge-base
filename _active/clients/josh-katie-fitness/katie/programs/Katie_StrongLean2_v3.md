**Load autoregulation (every lift):** all sets at/above the top of the range → +5 lb upper / +10 lb lower next session. Two+ reps below the bottom → −10% and rebuild.

**AUTOREG: PERFORMANCE WEEK** — violin performance/travel week: foundationals only, no top sets (straight 3 x 5 @ RPE 7), triggers become walks. Performance quality outranks the program. No make-ups.
**AUTOREG: CYCLE** — late-luteal: expect +1–2 lb scale water, never read it as stalling; optional RPE cap 8; swap Trigger B circuits for the walk alone if sleep was poor. Judge trends same-phase to same-phase across cycles.
**AUTOREG: SLEEP** — under ~6 h two nights running: no top set that day (straight sets @ RPE 7), cut pump blocks one round.
**AUTOREG: VOLUME GOVERNOR (carried)** — strength index falls two weeks running OR sleep chronically <6 h: the accessories added in v2.1/v3 are the first cut; top sets and barbell primaries are protected.

## WHAT CHANGED vs Block 1 — AND WHY
1. **Top-set/back-off wave replaces straight sets** — she's a proven strength adaptor; the top set drives intensity progression while back-offs hold volume. Heavy tension remains the recomp engine [VERIFIED — heavy RT + high protein recomp in trained women].

3. **The full accessory layer rotates to lengthened-position variants** (see Stimulus Audit above) — same target muscles, different joint angles, contraction modes, and muscle lengths, so Block 2 is a fresh signal rather than Block 1 repeated. 4. **Zone-2 lengthened to 25-30 min and the step floor made verifiable** — expenditure levers that cost no recovery, per the standing rule that the deficit comes from NEAT, never from slashing food.
5. **The gallbladder protocol graduates from "logged metric" to precondition** — Block 1 proved an unlogged lever doesn't move. No protocol streak, no Mode A.
6. **Cycle-irregularity abort rule promoted to a hard nutrition law** — this is the actual "don't ruin her metabolism" mechanism, stated where she can read it.
