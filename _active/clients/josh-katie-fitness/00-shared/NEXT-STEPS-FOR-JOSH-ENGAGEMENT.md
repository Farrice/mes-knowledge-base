## KEY PRINCIPLES (don't deviate from these)

1. **The questionnaire is the filter.** If Josh won't fill it out or does it half-assed, he's not ready. Don't coach people who won't invest in self-diagnosis.

2. **The personalized brief is the trial close.** If Josh reads it and says "yep, that's exactly what I need," he's ready. If he has objections or asks questions, answer them but don't re-architect the offer.

3. **The price stays $500/mo.** This is non-negotiable as an opening position. If Josh can't do $500 right now, that's real, but the price doesn't move without your explicit decision. (The offer you gave him was $500 floor; that's a floor for a reason.)

4. **The structure is simple:** one 30-min check-in weekly, adjustments, diagnostics at Day 1 & 45, nutrition framework, direct access. He either does that or he doesn't.

5. **This is a test.** If the Josh engagement works (he commits, shows up, sees results), the Michelle pitch and every warm outreach after uses the same playbook. If Josh doesn't commit, that's data too — and you iterate on what didn't land.
