# COACHING ASSET TEMPLATE — Adaptation Guide
**How to Reuse the Josh Demonstration Asset for New Prospects**

---

## WHAT THIS IS

The Josh package (questionnaire + brief + intro letter) is a **demonstration asset template**. It shows a warm prospect:
1. How Farrice structures coaching engagements
2. The clarity and honesty he brings
3. A bounded, realistic offer ($500/mo, specific scope)

This template can be reused for Michelle, other warm-outreach prospects, or any potential client who needs to see what coaching with Farrice actually looks like before committing.

---

## THE ADAPTATION PATTERN

Each piece has sections that are **structural** (keep as-is) and **personalized** (adapt to the prospect). Here's the map:

### INTRO LETTER (`00-send-this-to-josh.md`)

**Keep:**
- The opening: "I want to be straight with you..."
- The structure: read → build → price → invitation
- The tone (warm, direct, no-BS)
- The meta-honesty (explaining why coaching costs what it costs)

**Change:**
- Name (Josh → Michelle, etc.)
- **One detail about your interaction with them** — what you saw in them that matters
  - *Josh example: "You trained with me yesterday, and I saw what I've always seen from you..."*
  - *Michelle example: "We talked about your brand positioning last month, and you got the insight immediately..."*
- If relevant, **one specific constraint they mentioned**
  - *Josh: "Your acting schedule isn't a limitation..."*
  - *Michelle example (if she's a founder): "Running a DTC brand isn't a limitation..."*

### QUESTIONNAIRE (`01-coaching-engagement-questionnaire.md`)

**Keep:**
- Questions 1, 6, 7 (these are universal)
- The tone (direct, honest, no fluff)
- The meta-framing ("This is for us to build something real, not fit you into something generic")

**Change:**
- Question 2 (Life constraints) — **Adapt to their world**
  - *Josh: "Walk me through a typical chaos week from your perspective"*
  - *Michelle (if she's a founder): "Walk me through a typical week in your business — when are you actually doing strategy work vs. fighting fires?"*
- Question 3 (What's blocking you) — Keep the structure, **adapt the example**
  - *Josh: "Is it nutrition discipline? Is it that you prioritize audition prep...?"*
  - *Michelle: "Is it that you're writing everything yourself? Is it positioning confusion? Is it that you're comparing yourself to 10x bigger brands?"*
- Question 4 (Specific constraints) — **Replace with their actual constraint if relevant**
  - *Josh has GLP-1 medication — something structural and real*
  - *If Michelle has no analogous constraint, skip this or replace with something like "What's the biggest friction point in your current offering?"*

### COACHING BRIEF (`02-coaching-brief-demonstration.md`)

**Keep:**
- The structure: The Read → The Structure → What's Not Included → The Outcome → The Investment → The Real Question
- The price ($500/mo) and reasoning — **unless Farrice wants to adjust**
- The tone (read to the truth, honest about limits, action-oriented)

**Change:**
- **The Read** section — This is the **diagnosis section**; it's the most personalized part
  - *Josh read: "You're a serious lifter in an unserious life..."*
  - *Michelle read: Would start from what Farrice actually sees in her — her positioning, her resistance, her real misalignment*
  - *The structure is: [What I'm seeing] → [The real pattern/misalignment] → [Why typical solutions don't work] → [What the leverage point actually is]*
- **Specific details in "What's Included"** — adapt examples to their world
  - *Josh: "If you skip a week, we reset the next one instead of spiraling guilt" (specific to his pattern of inconsistency)*
  - *Michelle: "If you change your offer three times in a quarter, we lock it and move forward instead of re-architecting" (specific to founder indecision pattern)*
- **What's Not Included** — adapt to clarify boundaries for *their* use case
  - *Josh: "This is not a training program on paper" (he might expect a PDF)*
  - *Michelle: "This is not a content calendar or a full-service marketing agency" (she might expect that)*

## SWAPPING WORKFLOW (step-by-step)

1. **Read the three Josh docs cold.** You need to know the structure and examples.

2. **Spend 10 minutes thinking about the prospect:**
   - What did you see in them in your interaction? (This becomes the intro letter detail)
   - What's their actual life constraint or pattern? (This shapes the questionnaire Q2-4 and the Read section)
   - What's the misalignment only you can name for them? (This is the heart of the Read section)

3. **Open each doc and make these changes:**
   - Intro letter: name + interaction detail + constraint callout
   - Questionnaire: Q2, Q3, Q4 adapted to their world (1-2 sentences per question)
   - Brief: The Read section (3-4 sentences), specific examples in What's Included/Not Included

4. **Keep everything else exactly the same.** The structure, the tone, the price, the reasoning, the close.

5. **Proof once for Farrice voice** — read it aloud as Farrice. Does it sound like him? If it sounds generic, the Read section probably needs more specificity.

---

## RED FLAGS (don't break these)

- ❌ Don't change the price unless Farrice explicitly says to
- ❌ Don't soften the "What's Not Included" section — the boundaries are the integrity
- ❌ Don't add a bunch of detail about your methodology — the Read proves you understand them; that's enough
- ❌ Don't make the questionnaire longer than 7 questions; keep it scannable
- ❌ Don't use their jargon — use your observations in Farrice's voice

## EXAMPLE: ADAPTING FOR MICHELLE (founder/brand)

**Intro letter change:**
- *"We talked about your brand positioning last month, and you got the insight immediately. That's not the most common reaction."*

**Questionnaire Q2:**
- *"You're bootstrapping, which means you're doing everything. Walk me through a week — when are you actually doing the work that grows the business vs. fighting operational fires? When do you sleep?"*

**Questionnaire Q3:**
- *"You've launched before. So what's the real reason your current brand isn't where you want it? Is it that positioning keeps shifting? Is it that you're writing everything yourself and it's unsustainable? Is it that you compare yourself to founders with 5x your runway?"*

**Brief: The Read**
- *"You're a great operator in a one-person business. You can solve any problem on the fly, ship fast, and iterate. But that speed is also your trap — you pivot every 90 days when you could compound instead. Most coaches see the operator and try to teach you strategy. What you actually need is someone who stops you from moving before the last move has time to land."*
