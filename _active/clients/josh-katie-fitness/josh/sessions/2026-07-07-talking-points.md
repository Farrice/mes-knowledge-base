## 3. The habit that's easy to slip: the morning pill window
Pill → empty stomach → **30+ min before anything** (coffee, creatine, shake). Mistiming cuts absorption ~40%. Confirm he's got the routine locked — everything else (creatine, breakfast) lines up after the window.

## 4. What to check with him

- Waist trending down faster than bodyweight? = the loss is fat. That's the win condition.
- Strength index holding on the anchors? If it slips 2 weeks running → muscle-loss flag (raise protein/calories, cut a trigger, check sleep — before cutting training).

## 5. Ramp the support habits before he needs them
Fiber 30–40 g + probiotic, water 100+ oz + electrolytes — build these now while it's easy, so they're automatic when the dose ramps and the gut/appetite effects get real.
