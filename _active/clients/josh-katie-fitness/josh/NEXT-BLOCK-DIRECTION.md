Trigger A becomes optional; Thursday's recovery valve stays untouched.
- **Protein becomes THE daily win.** As appetite falls, the day's scorecard is one number: protein floor hit or not. Liquid protein is the designed answer to a suppressed appetite, not a compromise. Morning pill protocol unchanged and non-negotiable.
- **Strength index = the muscle-loss alarm.** Two weeks of falling strength → cut remaining accessories, raise food quality/protein, check sleep — before touching anything else. - **NEAT floor gets enforced, not assumed.** GLP-1 users' spontaneous movement quietly collapses as intake drops — 10k steps logged daily. The scale falling fast while steps crater is how "weight loss" becomes muscle loss with extra steps.
- **Expect fast scale movement and treat it with suspicion.** The win condition is strength held + waist down, not pounds down.
