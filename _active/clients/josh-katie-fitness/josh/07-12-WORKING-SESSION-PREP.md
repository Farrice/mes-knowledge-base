# 7/12 Working Session Prep (Saturday, 30 min with Josh)

**Purpose:** Validate positioning, draft cold-email hooks, make the $500/mo ask  
**Timeline:** After Josh returns questionnaire (7/11 evening)  
**Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Outcome:** Josh either commits or clearly declines (no ambiguity)

---

## Before the Session (Farrice's prep)

### 1. Refine the Positioning Brief (1 hour)
Read Josh's questionnaire answers and update JOSH-CREATIVE-STRATEGY-BRIEF.md:
- Does your positioning hypothesis match his own words?
- Any surprises or corrections in his ICP definition?
- Any new angles in the messaging based on how he answered?

Update these sections with his language/validation:
- Section: IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE (refine based on Q2 answer)
- Section: KEY MESSAGING ANGLES (add Josh's own phrasing from Q8 + Q1)
- Section: POSITIONING PROOF POINT (use his success story from Q7)

### 2. Draft 3 Cold-Email Hooks (30 min)
Create 3 different cold-open email lines Josh can use to prospect his ideal buyer.

**Template structure for each:**
```
HOOK LINE: [The opening that grabs attention]

BODY ANGLE: [Why this angle works for his ICP]

EXAMPLE:
HOOK: "I coach performers who can't afford a body change to derail their career."
BODY: This immediately qualifies the ideal buyer (actor/performer) while positioning the value (professional stakes).
```

**Varied approaches:**
1. Problem-first hook (start with their pain)
2. Positioning hook (start with Josh's unique angle)
3. Social proof hook (start with a result)

### 3. Prepare the "First Offer" Frame (15 min)
Josh will ask: "So what does this engagement actually look like?"

Have a 1-page answer ready:
- **Name the offer:** "90-Day Positioning + Prospecting Sprint"
- **What's included:** Refined positioning, cold-email templates, messaging deck, 2-3 prospect conversations walked through
- **Timeline:** 12 weeks, X hours/week
- **Investment:** $500/mo (3 months = $1,500 total, or $1,500 one-time)
- **Success metric:** Josh lands 3+ qualified prospect conversations OR signs first $500 client

## The 30-Minute Session (Agenda)

### Opening (2 min)
"Thanks for sending that back. Your answers are solid. Here's what I validated: [one thing he said that confirms positioning]. And here's what surprised me: [one insight]. Let me show you what this looks like as an actual engagement."

### Section 1: Positioning Validation (8 min)
Walk him through the refined brief:
- "This is your positioning hypothesis now, based on what you told me..."
- "Your ICP is specifically..."
- "The messaging angle that's going to work is..."

**Ask:** "Does this feel right? Any adjustments?"

### Section 2: Cold-Email Hooks (12 min)
Show the 3 hooks:
"Here's what your cold outreach could look like. Each of these is a different angle on the same positioning. Which one feels closest to your voice?"

Walk through 1-2 in detail. Show how each one leads to a conversation starter.

### Section 3: The Engagement + Ask (8 min)
"If this positioning strategy is landing, here's how we work together..."

**Present the First Offer (see prep section).**

Then: "I can start Monday if you want. Literally Monday. The $500/mo covers the first month, and we build out your prospect list + refine your outreach based on what lands."

**Frame it as logistics, not a hard sell:** "If the $500 works for your situation right now, we go. If it doesn't, I'll help point you to someone solid. But I think we're sitting on something good here."

### Closing (no time left — this is tight by design)
Doesn't matter how he answers. If yes, lock in Monday start + first Monday check-in. If no, wish him well and move on.

## Mindset for the Session

**This is a working session, not a sales pitch.** You've already shown the work. Now you're walking him through it and asking if he wants to go deeper.

**30 minutes is short on purpose.** It forces clarity. No wandering, no second-guessing yourself, no "let me think about it" softness. Clear yes or clear no.

**You're not selling the idea.** You're selling the execution. The refined brief + cold-email hooks ARE the execution proof.
