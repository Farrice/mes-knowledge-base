The real misalignment: you're treating your coaching like your training — looking for the perfect program. But the program isn't the leverage point. **The leverage is someone who knows your life, knows your goal, and adjusts the structure every single week based on what actually happened**, not what was supposed to happen.

## THE STRUCTURE

**Duration:** 90 days (three 30-day blocks). One block gets real. Two blocks prove it works.

**What's Included:**

1. **Weekly Check-In** (30 min, async-first)
   - You send me: weights hit, how you felt, schedule changes, appetite/side effects if you're tracking
   - I send back: adjustments to next week, one specific coaching insight, one reframe on a pattern I'm seeing
   - This replaces the guessing game of a static program

2. **Baseline + Midpoint Diagnostics** (Day 1, Day 45)
   - Photos, bodyweight, waist, one max-effort lift to track strength
   - Not for vanity — for data. We're proving muscle is defended while weight goes down

3. **Adaptation Protocol**
   - If the on-set schedule shifts (you're filming Wed–Fri instead of Mon–Tue), the training reshapes that week
   - If you're in a tough Wegovy dose transition (9 mg jump hits different), we throttle intensity automatically
   - If you skip a week, we reset the next one instead of spiraling guilt

4. **Off-Ramp Nutrition Framework** (not a meal plan — a system)
   - How to eat when you're in a trailer with craft services as your only option
   - How to hit protein floors when appetite is suppressed
   - When to actually take a break instead of pushing through a rough patch

5. **Direct Access** (Slack/text for urgent questions)
   - Not 24/7 — I have a kid. But within 24 hours for real questions. Not "what's the best pre-workout?" — I mean "I got cast in something last-minute and the schedule breaks my entire program, what do we do?"
