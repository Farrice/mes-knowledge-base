# JOSH — Titration Defense · Block 2 (v3) · "Vault Door Block"
**Carbon Torch · 8-Week Block · spans the 4 mg → 9 mg dose step (calendar TBD — the rule follows the pill, never the calendar)**
**Phase posture:** Bishop Block banked the muscle and work capacity while appetite was near-normal. This block is the phase the whole program was built to survive: appetite drops for real at 9 mg, and the job flips from *building* to **defending what was banked**. Motto of the block: **holding load is winning.** The scale will fall regardless — the program's only question is *what* the loss is made of.

> ## READ FIRST — the posture shift
> - **Full menu while the dose is low; the block sheds weight automatically as the dose climbs.** At 4 mg appetite is still near-normal, so the foundationals and triggers run the complete physique menu (compounds + the V-taper accessory layer, tagged ADD). The dose-step autoreg strips every ADD-tagged exercise the week a new dose lands — the barbell anchors are what remains, and that compressed skeleton IS the 9/25 mg program. Same philosophy as Bishop: the adds are the volume that comes off first, the anchors are never touched.
> - **Protein floor is the day's scorecard.** One number. Hit it and the day succeeded even if training didn't happen.
> - **Strength index is the muscle-loss alarm** — the only trustworthy early-warning signal on GLP-1. Two weeks falling = act (rules below), before touching anything else.
> - **Steps are enforced, not assumed.** GLP-1 users' spontaneous movement quietly collapses as intake drops — 10k logged daily. Fast scale drop + cratering steps is how "weight loss" becomes muscle loss with extra steps.

**AUTOREG: DOSE-STEP WEEK** — first week at any new dose: cap every lift at RPE 7, drop 1 set from each anchor, **every ADD-tagged exercise comes off**, triggers off, food = floors + tactics. Built-in deload timed to the adjustment window — never push through it, never bank it. **After the step:** ADDs return one session at a time ONLY while appetite score stays ≥ normal and the strength index holds; if appetite doesn't recover at the new dose, the compressed anchor-skeleton is the program from there on — that was always the plan, not a failure.
**AUTOREG: SIDE-EFFECT SCORE** (0-3 in tracker) — score 2: swap the day for the on-set fallback + walk. Score 3: rest day, hydrate + electrolytes, log it; persists 3+ days → coach; anything severe → prescriber.
**AUTOREG: MISSED-SESSION RULE** — missed foundational rolls the week forward; triggers dropped first; never stack two heavy days.
**AUTOREG: MUSCLE-LOSS FLAG** — strength index falling 2 weeks running: raise protein to the top of the floor, add ~150-200 kcal, Trigger A off, audit sleep — BEFORE touching the training. This rule outranks the progression table.

## NUTRITION — FLOORS + TACTICS (the tactics are now load-bearing)
| Day type | Calories (floor) | Protein (floor) |
|---|---|---|
| Foundational | ~2,000 | 190-200 g |
| Trigger | ~1,900 | 185 g |
| Rest | ~1,800 | 180 g |

- **Never stack a manual deficit on the dose.** The dose IS the deficit engine. Eating to the floor is the discipline now — under-eating is the failure mode, not over-eating.
- **Protein-first sequencing at every meal** — protein eaten before anything else on the plate, because the appetite may not last the whole meal.
- **Shakes become scheduled, not reactive:** 1-2/day at fixed times (post-window morning + afternoon) regardless of hunger. Liquid protein is the designed answer to a suppressed appetite.
- **4-5 small meals beat 3 normal ones** at 9 mg; lower-fat choices on rough-stomach days; fiber 30-40 g + probiotic; water 100+ oz + electrolytes; creatine 5 g daily — all outside the 30-min pill window.
- Portable kit unchanged: shaker + protein, jerky, fruit, electrolyte packets in the set bag.

## TRACKING LOOP (weekly)

**Signal logic (unchanged, now primary):**
- Weight ↓ + waist ↓ faster + strength holding → **fat loss, muscle defended. Win condition. Change nothing.**
- Weight ↓ + strength falling 2 weeks → **muscle-loss flag** (autoreg above).
- Weight ↓ fast + steps cratering → NEAT collapse; fix steps before celebrating the scale.

## STIMULUS AUDIT — Bishop Block → Block 2 (every slot accounted for)
**Rule applied (Israetel):** rotate what's stale or repeated, keep what's progressing or load-bearing. The four anchors + OHP/chin-up stay — they're the defense instruments and rotating them would destroy the strength index as a muscle-loss signal. Every carried accessory rotates, biased toward lengthened-position and dead-stop work — Bishop's accessories were pump/squeeze-biased; the stretched-position versions are a genuinely different signal [VERIFIED — stretch-mediated hypertrophy literature]. Thursday's valve keeps its core (repeatability is its job) and gains only light ADDs.

## WHAT CHANGED vs Bishop Block — AND WHY
1. **Volume compresses to the anchors by default** — the v2.1 silhouette accessories eras off (lateral raises survive as the maintenance dose). In a deepening medication deficit, high-fatigue volume is junk volume; only near-failure tension defends muscle [VERIFIED — stimulating-reps/junk-volume model, Schoenfeld dose-response].
2. **Trigger A is officially optional** — the plan now says out loud what the autoregs implied: at 9 mg, recovery is the budget, and the anchors own it.
3. **"Holding load is winning" becomes the progression model** — post-dose-step weeks resume pre-step loads and treat a held load as a PR. Lean-mass share of GLP-1 loss runs 25-45% untrained; resistance training + protein floors is the countermeasure [VERIFIED direction — STEP-1 DXA lean −9.7% vs fat −19.3%; RT case-series].
4. **Minimum Effective Day is a written standard** — pill + protein + steps = success, so titration weeks are executed strategy instead of ambient guilt.
5. **Appetite score joins the tracker** — meals-finished is the leading indicator that the floors are about to be missed; it fires before the scale or strength do.
