# JOSH — Full-Body GLP-1 Muscle-Defense v3 · "Bishop Defense" Block
**Carbon Torch · 8-Week Block · spans the 4 mg → 9 mg dose step**
**Phase posture:** GLP-1 dose escalation (oral Wegovy stepping to 9 mg — the first "big" adjustment window). Muscle is defended — strength held or improved through the 4 mg phase. The build window is closed; this block *reveals* the silhouette he built, not adds to it. MAPS Anabolic hybrid: Phase I mechanical-tension anchors with tighter back-off economy. Volume is the pressure valve — it comes off before anything else.

> ## v3 DESIGN NOTES — what changed and why
> **Current state (entering this block):** ~230 lb (down ~15 lb), strength defended, minimal side effects at 4 mg. The 1.5→4 mg window was used correctly — muscle banked, work capacity high.
>
> **9 mg changes the game.** Appetite suppression becomes pharmacologically significant. Nausea is likely in weeks 1-2 at the new dose. Recovery capacity drops because fuel drops. The program must get SMALLER, not bigger.
>
> **Key changes from v2.1:**
> - Anchor lifts: 4 working sets → **3 working sets** (same intensity, less volume fatigue — the signal stays, the cost drops)
> - v2.1 silhouette adds (DB RDL, incline press, pullover, barbell curl, leaning lateral raise): **ship CONDITIONAL** — toggle ON only when appetite/recovery support it; toggle OFF first at any dose-step week or strength-index dip
> - Trigger A: slimmed from ~40 min → **~30 min** (kept the delt width work, cut the arm pump volume)
> - Trigger B: **walk-only by default** (carries become conditional; recovery is more valuable than grip work in a deep deficit)
> - Nutrition floors lowered to match likely 9 mg appetite reality; **protein floor stays untouched at 180-200 g**
> - Nausea-aware training: fasted morning training explicitly supported; post-workout protein window emphasized
>
> **VOLUME GOVERNOR (the honesty layer).** This block starts at reduced volume ON PURPOSE. If his appetite holds and strength stays defended through weeks 3-4, the conditional movements toggle on — earning volume back. If 9 mg hits hard, the program is already at its defense floor and nothing needs to be cut. **The conditional movements are the flex; the anchors are the spine; protein is the heartbeat.**

*Week 5 is the *planned* dose-step slot. **The rule follows the pill, not the calendar** — if 9 mg starts week 3, that's the AUTOREG week.

**AUTOREG: DOSE-STEP WEEK (9 mg)** — First dose at 9 mg: cap EVERY lift at RPE 7, all conditionals OFF, triggers = walks only, food = floors + appetite tactics (below). **Do not push through it.** This is not weakness — it's the program designed to absorb the transition.

**AUTOREG: SIDE-EFFECT SCORE** (0-3 in tracker):
- Score 0-1: Train as written.
- Score 2: Swap the day's foundational for the on-set fallback + walk. Hit protein.
- Score 3: Rest day, hydrate + electrolytes, log it. 3+ consecutive days at score 3 → prescriber.

**AUTOREG: NAUSEA TRAINING** — If nauseous pre-training: train fasted (water only) inside the pill window OR delay training to 2+ hours after eating. Post-workout protein shake within 30 min is the priority meal of the day at 9 mg — the post-exercise appetite window is the most reliable eating window on GLP-1.

**Load autoregulation:** All sets at/above the top of the range → +5 lb upper / +10 lb lower. Two+ reps below the bottom → −10% and rebuild. **At 9 mg, HOLDING load is the win condition.** A week where all sets hit the bottom of the range at the same weight = muscle defended = success.

## NUTRITION ALIGNMENT (defense floors — lower than v2 to match 9 mg appetite reality)
| Day type | Calories (floor) | Protein (floor) |
|---|---|---|
| Foundational (Mon/Wed/Fri) | ~1,850 | 190-200 g |
| Trigger (Tue/Thu) | ~1,750 | 185 g |
| Rest (Sat/Sun) | ~1,650 | 180 g |

- **Protein floor is SACRED.** At 9 mg, appetite drops and protein becomes hard to eat. Shakes are a primary tool — not a supplement, a STAPLE. Two shakes/day if needed. Protein-first at every meal means protein hits the plate before anything else.
- **"Eat to floors" becomes active work** at 9 mg. At 4 mg this was easy; at 9 mg it may require setting alarms, pre-making shakes, protein-dense snack stations.
- **The medication IS the deficit engine.** Never stack a manual calorie cut on top. If he's eating below floors despite trying, that's a medical conversation — not a coaching win.
- Fiber 30-40 g + probiotic · water 100+ oz + electrolytes · creatine 5 g daily — outside pill window.
- **Nausea tactics:** smaller/more frequent meals, lower-fat meals (fat slows gastric emptying → more nausea), ginger tea, cold foods over hot, protein shakes over solid protein if solid food is hard.
- On-set kit: shaker + protein, jerky, fruit, electrolyte packets — non-negotiable.

## WHAT CHANGED vs v2.1 — AND WHY
1. **Anchor volume: 4 sets → 3 sets.** The anabolic signal is in the near-failure reps, not the set count. At 9 mg, recovery capacity drops with fuel — 3 hard sets preserve the signal while cutting ~25% of the fatigue cost. [Dose-response plateaus beyond 3-5 hard sets per session: **VERIFIED** — Schoenfeld meta-analysis; the 4th set has diminishing returns even in a surplus, and in a deficit it's net-negative.]
2. **v2.1 adds ship CONDITIONAL.** The build window (1.5-4 mg) was the time to add volume. At 9 mg, those extras are the first thing the body can't recover from. They're still in the program — but earned, not assumed. [Recovery capacity drops in caloric deficit: **VERIFIED** — reduced mTOR signaling, elevated cortisol, lower glycogen stores.]
3. **Trigger B becomes walk-only by default.** Carries are good, but grip fatigue before deadlift day in a deficit is a bad trade. The walk provides the same caloric expenditure with zero recovery cost. [Zone-2 cardiovascular activity: negligible interference with RT adaptations: **VERIFIED**.]
4. **Nutrition floors lowered 150 kcal/day** from v2 to match 9 mg appetite reality. Protein floors are IDENTICAL — muscle defense is non-negotiable. The calorie reduction comes entirely from carbs. [Protein >1.6 g/kg during energy restriction + RT: optimal lean mass preservation: **VERIFIED** — Helms/Aragon/Fitschen position stand.]
5. **Nausea-aware training timing formalized.** Fasted training + immediate post-workout protein is the most reliable eating strategy on GLP-1. [Post-exercise appetite suppression is reduced vs pre-exercise on GLP-1 agonists: **LIKELY** — clinical observation, limited direct study.]
6. **"Holding load is winning" framing explicit.** At 4 mg, progression was expected. At 9 mg, maintenance IS progression — the same weight on the bar at a lighter bodyweight means pound-for-pound strength is increasing.
