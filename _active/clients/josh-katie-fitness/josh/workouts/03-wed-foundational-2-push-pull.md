# JOSH · WEDNESDAY — Foundational 2 (Push-Pull-led · V-TAPER DAY) · ~80 min
**Block:** Bishop Block v2.1 · Master: [`../programs/Josh_FullBody_GLP1_v2.md`](../programs/Josh_FullBody_GLP1_v2.md)
*This is the width day — the V-taper is built here. Back volume is deliberately concentrated (Ethier: pour volume into the muscle that transforms the silhouette). "NEW v2.1" = added this pass.*

**Movement prep (5 min):** scap pull-ups x8 · band dislocates · push-up plus x10 · thoracic rotations.
**Warm-up ramp before bench:** 12 light → 8 medium → 3 near working weight.

1. Barbell Bench Press [Chest, Front Delts, Triceps] — 4 x 4-6 @ ≥75% 1RM · Rest 3 min · Tempo 2-1-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: leg drive, controlled touch, no bounce.
2. Pull-Up (wide-ish grip) [Lats, Upper Back, Biceps] — 4 x 5-8 · Rest 2.5-3 min · Tempo 2-0-X-1 · RPE 8 — Cue: dead hang to chest-proud; over 8 clean reps every set → add load (DB between ankles). Widest lat sweep = the top of the V.
3. Barbell Overhead Press [Delts, Triceps] — 3 x 6-8 · Rest 2 min · RPE 8 — Cue: glutes tight, ribs down; delt width, second heavy press of the week.
4. Chest-Supported DB Row (incline bench, chest down) [Mid Back, Lats, Rear Delts] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 90 s · Tempo 2-1-1-1 · RPE 8 — Cue: chest glued to the pad, drive elbows back — pure back thickness with zero low-back fatigue before Friday's deadlift. **NEW v2.1: back width/thickness. (No incline bench? sub strict Pendlay Row.)**
5. DB Pullover (bench or on stability ball) [Lats, Serratus, Chest] — 3 x 12-15 · Rest 75 s · Tempo 3-1-1-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: big stretch overhead, pull with the lats, ribs down — the classic move that flares the lats and cinches the waist visually. **NEW v2.1: the V-taper illusion-maker.**
6. [SS-F1] Hinged DB Rear-Delt Fly [Rear Delts] — 2 x 12-15 · Rest 0 → F2 · RPE 8.
7. [SS-F2] Close-Grip Push-Up (or DB Floor Press) [Triceps, Chest] — 2 x 10-15 · Rest 60 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: elbows tight to ribs.
8. Suitcase Carry [Obliques, Grip] — 2 x 40 yd/side · Rest 45 s · RPE 7-8 — Cue: tall, no lean — midsection work without a single crunch.

---
**Session rules:** DOSE-STEP WEEK → cap RPE 7-8, drop 1 set from bench AND pull-up anchors, cut the two v2.1 adds (chest-supported row, pullover). Load autoreg — top of range all sets → +5 lb upper / +10 lb lower; 2+ under bottom → −10%. **Three back movements here is the deliberate V-taper concentration, chosen support-first to spare the low back for Friday — it's the first volume trimmed if recovery signals red.** Missed session rolls the week forward — never stack two heavy days to catch up.
