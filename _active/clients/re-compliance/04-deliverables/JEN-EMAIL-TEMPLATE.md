## Additional Context (For Jen, If Asked)

**Q: What exactly are you auditing?**

A: Your MLS listing copy against the Fair Housing Act. We're looking for:
- **Banned words** ("perfect for families," "ideal for retirees," "active individuals," etc.)
- **Protected-class coding** (demographic targeting that excludes certain buyers)
- **Dog-whistle language** ("safe neighborhood," "quiet area" — code for discrimination)

The audit produces a report: violations found, compliant rewrite, and legal citations.

**Q: Will this change the listing?**

A: Only if you want it to. I'll show you the compliant version, and you can decide whether to update your MLS copy. Either way, we use the audit as proof that the skill works.

**Q: Is this legal advice?**

A: No — it's a compliance tool. The audit documents your listings against HUD standards (24 CFR §100.75) and NAR Code of Ethics. Real legal questions should go to a lawyer, but this gives you defensible documentation of due diligence.

**Q: Why does this matter?**

A: First-time Fair Housing violation = ~$20K penalty + compliance labor + reputational damage. The skill catches violations before MLS publication. Every audit = a defensible record.
