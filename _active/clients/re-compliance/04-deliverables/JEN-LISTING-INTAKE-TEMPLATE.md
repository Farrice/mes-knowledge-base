## FAQ

**Q: Do you need my address or MLS password?**  
A: No — just the MLS remarks text. We're auditing the copy, not accessing your account.

**Q: What if a listing is already compliant?**  
A: Perfect! We'll generate a "PASS" receipt showing "0 violations found." That's powerful proof that your listings meet standards.

**Q: Can I use past listings?**  
A: Yes — either current or past. Past listings are safer for privacy if you prefer.

**Q: What if the audit finds violations?**  
A: We'll show you the compliant version. You can optionally update your MLS copy, or use it as a learning example.

**Q: Can I see a sample audit first?**  
A: Yes — check `skills/re-compliance-pack/receipts/audit-test-listing-1.json` for a complete example.
