## 2. The mechanism, in Farrice's words

- **80/20:** "The attention, like the on-screen text, needs to do 80% of the lifting, and then the payoff has to come off in the caption and give a lived-in experience or a preview experience of what it's like to work with Jen… so well that they go, 'Damn, I recognize that! Damn, that's me!… That's what I'm feeling!'" (2026-09-09)
- **Never resolve it on screen:** "If we deliver everything in the first on-screen text, there's no need to go to the rest of the caption and the payoff." (2026-09-09)
- **Clarity with craft:** "explain things to people as if they're a fifth grader, but still have that personality, rhythm, cadence, psychological triggers… It has to make sense when someone reads it. It can't just be this overblown way of doing it." (2026-09-09)
- **Specifics are the hit:** "'Oh damn, that's good!… Oh, that's me. Oh, I hate taking the 101!' Stuff like that." (2026-09-04)
- **Why the drafts failed:** "We had a winning specimen, and none of this followed any of it… it's doing too much and trying to paint too much of a picture with a lot of words, versus actually being in a conversational nature." (2026-09-09)

## 5. Rules that transfer (each tied to a row above)

1. On-screen text carries ~80% of attention; the caption carries the payoff. Never resolve the whole idea on screen (§2).
2. Open with a sentence the buyer could hear themselves saying (#1, the specimen).
3. Answer with a proportionate human reply, not a lesson; demonstrate the help ("screenshot with '???'") (#1).
4. Put a stake in the frame so the reassurance earns its place ("$800K") (#1).
5. Ordinary B-roll or her photos, no spoken part, no bolted-on CTA; the invitation lives inside the conversation (#1, ENGINE-V2 §2).
6. Aim for "damn, that's me" recognition; #4 hit 99.9× with 569 followers on recognition alone.
7. Life or feeling first, real estate second; property-as-hook is bottom quartile on her own grid (§4).
8. If the screen makes a promise, the caption keeps it in full (#3).
9. Specificity is the entertainment: the 101, the 11pm calculator app, dinner not figured out (§2).
10. Fifth-grade clarity with adult craft: short sentences, lowercase, one emoji, varied rhythm (§2).
11. Every post starts from an inspected complete source: overlay AND full caption AND performance. The landlord and open-house drafts died on invented premises (§6).
12. View count never qualifies a reference by itself (#11, Trips).
