## Format rules

- Silent/photo Reel: human or local evidence first; real estate enters on beat two.
- Decision-map carousel: one claim per frame; proof before CTA.
- Photo/caption handled moment: show what Jen removed from the client's plate.
- Stories: recognition, one useful fact, then a calm reply or DM door.
