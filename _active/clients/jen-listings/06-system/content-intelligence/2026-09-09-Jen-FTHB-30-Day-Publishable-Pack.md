## AUDIENCE STATE MAP (Kallaway Foundation)

**Four emotional buyer states** (not demographic ICPs—these are decision-problem archetypes from deep Reddit/community listening):

| State | Core Question | Emotional Friction | Content Job | Recognition Phrase |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Compromise Vertigo** | "Which compromise will I regret least?" | Condo vs. house, HOA vs. repairs, commute vs. space. Paralyzed by tradeoffs. | Compare specific tradeoff pairs (side-by-side, feeling-first). Show what she checks *before* the buyer falls in love. | "I didn't know there were that many [specific choice] options." |
| **Qualification Shame** | "Am I actually unqualified or has no one organized this properly?" | Creative income, family money, mixed W-2/1099—feels hidden from lenders. Fears: "What if I'm the exception?" | Decode lender language, acknowledge non-standard income as *real*, show step-by-step proof path. | "I didn't think I could do this. Turns out it's just [specific fact]." |
| **Solo Weight** | "Can I carry this decision alone?" | No second person to pressure-test, no accountability partner. Fears: FOMO, regret, wrong choice with no co-signer. | Bring her into Jen's thinking (what Jen checks, how Jen frames tradeoffs). Lower decision isolation. | "I felt like I had to know everything. Turns out [specific permission]." |
| **Rent Math Guilt** | "If buying makes monthly life worse, why buy?" | Payment shock ($2.6K rent vs. $5.4K payment). Fears: "I'll be house-poor, stressed, unable to travel." | Reframe payment ≠ life quality. Show real buyers with same payment, different life. | "Same payment as my rent, but I'm building something." |

## CONTENT PILLAR STRATEGY (4 Repeatable Formats)

### PILLAR 1: "Same Payment, Different Life" 
**Testing Status**: PRIMARY — test first, scale after 3 qualified conversations + 1 consultation  
**Target State**: Rent Math Guilt  
**Why It Works**: Directly addresses the buyer's #1 objection (payment shock). Proof lives inside content (real buyer story), not testimonials.

**Grammar**: 
- Hook: Specific monthly payment ($2,600–$3,200 range, SFV address band)
- Contrast: "Your rent vs. your mortgage+tax+insurance+HOA+reserves"
- Shift: Buyer realizes monthly payment doesn't measure life quality
- Integration: What changes from rent to own (can paint, host dinners, stop worrying about rent hikes)

**Reel Hook**: "Here's what $3,200 buys you in rent right now. Here's the same $3,200 in Tarzana."

**Story Hand-Raise**: Poll — "Shared walls or repairs you control?" / Emoji reaction to "Would you trade $200/mo more in payment for a house with a yard?" / Text reply invitation: "Tell me: what's keeping you renting?"

**Saved DM Reply**: 
> "Love that this landed 💙 Real talk: the payment shock is real, but when you break down actual monthly costs + what ownership means, the math shifts. I'm booking quick 20-min strategy calls with people who want to understand the real picture—not just the payment line. You interested? Just reply TOUR and let me know your rough budget + what neighborhood you're eyeing."

**Case-Study Shape**: Buyer name (anonymized as "Sarah, Encino") → monthly rent → monthly payment + tax + insurance + HOA → "First concern was the jump. Then she realized she'd covered her 15-year mortgage and had 10 years of payments saved vs. rent hikes." → Closed in 6 weeks.
