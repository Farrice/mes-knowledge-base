# Native replies and the smallest useful test

**Jen Santulan · Private drafts · September 4, 2026**

Use the buyer's words. One message should answer or clarify one thing. These are selectable patterns, not a sequence to paste at every person. [Decision brief](instagram-buyer-journey.md)

## Content and interaction patterns

### Carousel: coordination without a demographic label

An education post can end with this exercise:

> before you send each other more listings, each write down the two things you need the home to work around. compare those first.

Optional invitation, only when the post's job is to open a conversation:

> if you're stuck between two areas, tell me the tradeoff you're weighing. we can start there.

A recognition post can simply end. Do not append save, share, follow, comment and DM requests together. These are copy patterns; photos, current claims and required advertising identification still need the existing content review.

### Story: one question, one answer, one optional door

**Frame 1 ,  topic poll:** “what would be more useful to walk through?” Choices: “comparing areas” / “what happens first.” This asks for an editorial preference, not financial or personal disclosure.

**Frame 2 ,  useful answer:** “comparing areas? start with the places your week needs to reach. then compare the homes around that routine.” Both options should eventually get a useful answer; no obligation to respond.

**Frame 3 ,  invitation:** “have a specific question? send it to me here. no personal financial details needed.” Direct the viewer to the private message/reply control available on their version of Instagram. Do not confuse it with a public Story comment. If replies are unavailable, give the profile-message fallback. No broadcast DM to voters, no roster-building and no anonymous-response promise.

### Public comment

If asked a general question, answer it there. For a question needing private context:

> happy to talk through the search side with you. if you'd like, message me the question, keep personal financial details out of the comments.

Do not post “DM sent” unless Jen actually sent one in an authorized interaction. A compliment gets a normal acknowledgment, not intake.

## Keyword resource request: a fair contender

“Comment HOME” is eligible under Farrice's September 4 correction. The method must deliver what it names. Here is a complete testable pairing using a resource that fits in a text message, so the test does not depend on hosting a new page.

**Comment entry:** “comparing two areas? comment HOME and i'll send you three questions to work through before you look at more listings.”

**Natural DM entry:** “comparing two areas? message me for three questions to work through before you look at more listings.”

Both get the **same comparison note**, with no email, follow or qualification requirement:

> here's the comparison note:
> 1. which places does your week need to reach?
> 2. what would you give up in the home to make that routine easier?
> 3. what do you need to check before choosing, travel time, property condition, or the full monthly cost?
> if you're buying with someone, answer separately first, then compare. a lender can help verify the cost question. if you want to talk through the search side, you're welcome to reply here.

This short text is the whole promised resource. The buyer does not have to open the diagnostic to receive it. If the eventual offer is instead a complete guide, listing sheet or diagnostic, deliver that named object; do not under-deliver with these three questions.

Jen sends the requested resource once. A later substantive reply permits a relevant response, not indefinite follow-up. If the message cannot be delivered because of account controls, a public reply can say, “i couldn't send it through here, message me and i'll send the comparison note.” Do not claim delivery on a send attempt. No unsolicited DM to a follower, liker or topic-poll voter.

## First useful replies

### The person already names a coordination problem

> i’d start with the part you’re choosing differently. each write down the two things the home needs to work around, then compare. is the bigger gap the location or the kind of home?

Only ask the last question if their message has not already answered it. Do not infer romantic partnership, marital status, two qualifying incomes or disagreement from a shared purchase.

### The person says they are deciding mostly alone

> you don't have to figure out the whole purchase at once. let's pick the next question that would help you move forward. what feels least clear right now?

If they already named payment, condition or area, address that issue instead of asking again. Do not assume divorce, bereavement or a lack of support.

### The person asks whether freelance income can count

> a lender needs to review that with you. for now, you can write down the kinds of income you have and what records are available, keep the amounts and documents off instagram. would help putting the lender questions together be useful?

No eligibility prediction or income-document request. A lender introduction happens only with the buyer's permission, using the current broker-approved protocol.

### The arrival is just “hi”

> hey! what can i help you with?

A vague opening needs one question, not a menu of ICP identities. No external asset is required.

### The buyer asks about a particular home

> i can check [the specific property question] for you. is there something about the home you're trying to figure out?

Replace the bracket with the actual question; do not imply availability or a verified fact before checking. For a showing request, follow the broker's showing and representation procedure instead of offering the diagnostic.

## Optional structured help

Offer this only after a useful reply or when explicitly requested:

> if you'd rather think it through on your own, i have a short check with five choices that gives you a next step. we can also keep talking here. whichever is easier.

If accepted, a future approved delivery reply should explain the object and the return path:

> here's the check. nothing gets sent to me when you finish. if you want to talk afterward, come back here and tell me which next step helped.

The prototype currently has no public delivery URL and no summary-copy handoff. Do not paste a local file path to a buyer or pretend a link is live. The optional copy-summary feature specified in the decision brief must be built and tested before promising it.

## Personal service and permission

Before offering personal representation or a consultation, after general value:

> before we get into personal real-estate help, are you working under a written buyer-representation agreement with another agent?

**Yes:** “thanks for letting me know. your agent is the right person to work through your search with you. you're welcome to use the general information here.” No switching pitch.

**Unsure / prefer not to answer:** “we can keep this general for now. check your agreement or with your agent before we go further.” No consultation offer while status is unresolved.

**No, and help requested:** “it sounds like [their decision] is the thing to sort out next. would a short call to map the order help?” If yes, agree a time and channel manually. Do not assume a working calendar or force a phone/email handoff in advance.

**One resource only:** give the agreed relevant resource, with no call question attached.

**Later:** “would you like me to check back around [their stated time], or would you rather reach out?” No check-back unless explicitly chosen. No recurring nurture enrollment.

**Sensitive information arrives:** “please keep personal financial details and documents off instagram. the lender or adviser should collect those through their secure process.” Do not repeat, quote, download or put the details in the research log. Follow the broker's incident/retention procedure; do not promise retroactive deletion from Meta.

**Neighborhood judgment:** “what would you like to compare about the homes or your commute? i can help with those details and point you to public sources for the rest.” Do not rank areas for protected groups or make subjective safety guarantees.

## A. Five-person comprehension test ,  NOT RUN

First obtain approval to recruit; no invitations have been sent. Use five consenting adults who recognize first-home decisions. Use supplied fictional scenarios, no financial records, no live Instagram posting, no covert recording. People may leave at any point. Findings describe these participants only.

Show two comparable situations per person: an unanswered location tradeoff and an income-preparation question. Alternate the order of the native conversation mock and existing private check across participants; change the topic so the second experience does not just repeat a memorized answer. Keep a simple record of order and scenario.

Give the same task: “Find a useful next step in the way you would normally choose.” Do not explain where to tap or insist they finish. Let the DM mock use the scripts above; the facilitator gives no coaching beyond what Jen would say. Then ask what they believe Jen received, whether anyone will contact them, and what they would do next. Ask preference only after both trials.

Record participant code, starting surface, path chosen, assistance needed, time to useful next step, next step recalled, data-transmission understanding and optional reason for preference. Record no names, handles, income, relationship history or raw disclosures. Keep only counts and paraphrased friction themes after review; consented research notes are not brokerage transaction records.

**Success to advance for approval:** at least 4/5 can state a useful next step without coaching; at least 4/5 can navigate their preferred route unaided; all 5/5 understand that the prototype sends nothing to Jen. Jen accepts the reply burden in the mock. These are proposed quality thresholds, not statistical validation.

**Stop/revise:** any participant believes the check submitted a request, any required path invites sensitive data, or two participants cannot find a useful next step. Repair that handoff and retest it with fresh consenting participants before considering public use. Do not average away a privacy misunderstanding with good satisfaction scores.

**Choice rule:** if at least 3/5 prefer the check and it meets all comprehension conditions, keep it prominent for voluntary use. That finding would weaken the claim that conversation is the easier default. It does not validate conversion.

## B. Matched CTA pilot ,  PROPOSED, NO LAUNCH PERMISSION

After Jen/broker review and explicit publishing approval, use **four comparable buyer-education posts over 14 days**, replacing existing slots rather than adding volume. Two use the comment-HOME request and two use the natural DM request above. Order them A–B–B–A; keep topic family, format, resource, delivery text and reply hours comparable. Companion Stories use the corresponding invitation, with their source measured separately. This is a small matched field comparison, not randomized causal proof: audiences, repeat exposure and topic execution can differ.

The same native comparison note is delivered manually in both arms. No email gate, follow gate, paid traffic, public diagnostic link, booking automation or outreach to followers/voters. Spontaneous questions still get answered; tag them as spontaneous, not CTA conversions. Use the optional check only in the separate private comprehension test until its hosting and return path are approved.

**Capacity and cap:** aim for 10 unique resource requests per arm, stopping research enrollment at 20 total or day 14, whichever comes first. Do not ignore normal service requests after the cap. Jen must first agree a reply window; proposed next-working-day reply and 15-minute daily pilot budget are planning limits, not existing commitments. Record actual delivery delays because this manual test does not isolate automation's speed advantage.

**Denominators:** record unique requesters (deduped across both arms, assigned to first known entry), requested-resource send attempts, acknowledged receipts, substantive buyer replies, useful exchanges, qualified active conversations and accepted next steps. Exclude spam and agent-peer requests from buyer outcomes, but count and report their share of total requests. An unknown source stays unknown. A resource request is permission to fulfill that request; it is not a qualified lead.

**Useful exchange:** after resource delivery or a direct answer, the buyer names a clearer next decision, supplies a relevant missing detail or explicitly asks for appropriate help. “Thanks,” a keyword, or an emoji is not enough.

**Minimum to interpret an arm:** 10 unique requests with valid source attribution and comparable delivery performance. Fewer by day 14 means **INCONCLUSIVE** for comparative performance. Report actual requests per post and per 1,000 post accounts reached where Jen supplies Insights. Sum-of-post reach can count repeat viewers; label it exposure-normalized, not a unique-person conversion rate. Do not combine Story and feed reach into one denominator.

**Service success threshold per evaluable arm:** at least 4/10 useful exchanges, at least 2/10 accepted appropriate next steps, at least 8/10 substantive deliveries within the agreed reply window, median active handling time at most 10 minutes per requester, and zero privacy/representation failures. An appropriate next step is a new action after the original promise is fulfilled: an additional requested resource, a lender-question discussion or a call. Exclude the initial comparison-note delivery and report the later action types separately. These are precommitted management thresholds, not market benchmarks.

**Selection rule:** if only one arm clears those conditions and its exposure-normalized useful-exchange rate is also higher, provisionally favor it for the next bounded cycle. If both clear, prefer the arm producing more useful exchanges per 1,000 reached when its minutes per useful exchange are no worse; otherwise keep both and inspect the tradeoff. If reach or delivery timing is not comparable, make no efficacy ranking. One small cycle never proves a universal winning formula. If neither clears, inspect resource relevance and fulfillment before calling the CTA stale.

**Stop invitations and review:** any privacy/representation failure, two missed reply-window targets, more than 15 pilot minutes on three days, or three explicit negative reactions about unwanted contact, excessive questions or misleading resource value. Continue necessary existing service. Silence does not authorize a nudge. Resource volume that overwhelms Jen is an operations signal, not evidence that the CTA failed; investigate bounded fulfillment automation separately, with approval.

**Commercial evidence:** active qualification still requires relevant Valley/LA intent, a consequential blocker, plausible timing, expressed interest in Jen's help and clear representation status. Resource delivery can be successful service without being a qualified lead. Consultations booked, consultations held, signed clients and closings remain separate. All outcomes for this proposed pilot are **NO EVENT**.
