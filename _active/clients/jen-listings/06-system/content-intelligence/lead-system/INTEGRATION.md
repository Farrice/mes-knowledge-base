Event definitions:

**Draft:** local copy or image; no audience event.
**Attention:** reported reach, views, saves, shares, poll taps. Reach and views are different denominators.
**Request:** a person requests the named resource. A delivery attempt and acknowledged receipt are separate observations.
**Useful exchange:** after delivery or an answer, the person names a clearer decision, supplies relevant missing context or requests appropriate help. “Thanks” alone does not qualify.
**Active conversation:** relevant Valley/LA intent, a consequential blocker, plausible timing, interest in Jen's help and no conflicting representation. Service state, never a wealth score.
**Consultation:** booked and held are separate dated events.
**Signed client:** a verified signed representation event; not a closing.
**Closing:** a verified transaction close; not evidence of money collected by Jen.
**Revenue:** actual collected revenue supported by Jen's accounting record. Listing price, commission estimates and signed agreements are not collected revenue.
