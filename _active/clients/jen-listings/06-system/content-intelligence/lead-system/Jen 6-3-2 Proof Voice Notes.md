## Truth rules

- Two buyers do not automatically prove two incomes. If only one qualifying income was used, call it co-buyer coordination.
- A singular “she” does not prove that someone bought or financed alone.
- “Fresh start,” disagreement, fear, relief, and causation are never added unless Jen remembers the client saying them or another source records them.
- Jen may organize, translate, prepare, and coordinate. A qualified lender owns underwriting and eligibility.
- An old Instagram post is not client permission. Remove identifying details unless permission is documented, and return the final story to Jen before use.
- If no creative-income case emerges, #2 remains an education and empathy hypothesis. Do not manufacture a client outcome.
