## Why her Canva carousel felt right to her (and why it didn't work)

Felt right: it's beautiful, it's her, it took an afternoon, it looks like the agents she admires, and posting it felt like doing marketing. That feeling is the product she bought.

Didn't work, in Sherrard's four mistakes: (1) it's a seller-process post aimed at people already ready to list, the 1%, on a first-time-buyer grid; (2) six slides describe *her* process, none answers a question a reader has; (3) no hook, no reason to save or send, a "let's talk" close with nothing to reply to; (4) the same photo six times makes the swipe pointless. Result: cheap comments, no DMs. Not a taste failure. A job failure: the post had no job.

## Where we lost her

1. **Nouns.** Carousel, local signal, flywheel, stamp, file, saved reply. We named the machine. She heard homework.
2. **Effort frame.** We presented effort as honesty. To her it was a bill.
3. **Look.** We built an identity system. She wanted a wardrobe she already owns.
4. **Spine.** Four talking reels as the plan's heart, for someone who won't talk to camera.
5. **The pitch shape.** We showed the system before the post. Coffee & Contracts shows the post, then the calendar, then the price.

## What survives unchanged

The copy engine and its rules (plain words with punch, one job per post, the open door, her verbatim close), the local facts, the saved replies, the month-not-post rotation, fair-housing floor. All of that is invisible to her and it's the part that works.

## The deal that fits her (offer it, don't argue)

- **She does two things.** A thumbs-up on a preview once a week (30 seconds, no edits unless a line isn't her). Reply to DMs the same evening from the saved replies. That's it. No memos, no filming, no Canva.
- **We do everything else.** Research, writing, photos, build, captions, scheduling through Meta Business Suite with her login, reading what comes back, next month's plan.
- **Photos come from what exists.** Her listing photography (she owns the shoots), her portraits, the cleared Valley pool. New photos only when she happens to have them.
- **Reels without her voice.** Photo-motion reels (her listing photos with slow movement and a text hook, built by us) and street B-roll from the pool. Trending audio added at post time if she wants; not required.
- **Her voice comes from the archive.** Five memos already transcribed, thirty-plus captions, four listing descriptions. Enough for months. A twenty-minute car chat once a quarter, framed as coffee, not homework.
- **The look: Valley Native · Photo.** Full-bleed photo, dark wash, white serif, a handwritten accent, her lockup. The system underneath stays; the surface is hers.
- **Scoreboard she'll believe:** DMs with an address or a question, per month. Nothing else reported to her.

"You don't have to do anything except tell me if a line isn't you and answer the DMs. It'll look like your posts, with your photos. I'll handle the rest, and we'll look at the DMs together on the first of the month."
