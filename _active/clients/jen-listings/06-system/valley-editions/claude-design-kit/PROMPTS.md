## 0 · Ground it (paste once)

> You are designing a five-frame Instagram carousel (1080×1350 each) for Jen Santulan, @_jiing, a San Fernando Valley realtor. The attached DESIGN.md is the design system; COPY-DECK.md is the final copy and must not be rewritten, shortened or improved; GRAMMAR.md holds the exact geometry of the six Canva carousels this system is built from. The five PNGs in reference/template-local-gem are the target look: a tall condensed serif in every role, a light dark-to-clear wash over a warm real photo, thick hand-drawn doodles, a hand-drawn ellipse around the handle, two rounded photo panels on interior pages. Reproduce that confidence, then make it hers: swap the template's yellow for the pale blue #C9D4E2 (headline gradient dark end, pill stroke, doodles), swap its stock photos for the attached photos only, and keep cream/white type. No yellow, no orange, no navy color blocks, no emoji, no drop shadows on type, no second typeface on a page. Type never sits on a face. Every frame carries the masthead "THE VALLEY · TARZANA / EDITION 01" and the pill "@_JIING" in the template's position.
