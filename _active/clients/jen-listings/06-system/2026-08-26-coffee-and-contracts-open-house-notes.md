# Coffee & Contracts "Open House Live" — what Jen was sold on

Source: Zoom recording, Aug 26, 2026, 47:42, hosts Alyssa Stalker (Head of Member Education) and Haley Ingram (CEO/founder). Captured 2026-09-02 from the share link via the in-app browser (audio transcript panel, verbatim). Product: coffeecontracts.com, a template-and-strategy subscription for real-estate agents, founded Sept 2019.

## The product, as pitched

- **Dashboard:** Instagram analytics pulled from her account (top posts, follower count); "daily post + engagement task" for today; new template drops weekly and monthly.
- **Content vault:** Canva templates sorted by type and pillar; captions; email templates; landing pages; newsletters; lead magnets ("fall guide"); print.
- **Custom content calendar:** answer two questions (growth vs. authority; which days), click "Create My Content Strategy," get a month filled with templates. "Replace Post" swaps in any template. "Generate Caption" writes the caption with local keywords and even names local coffee shops and dinner spots.
- **Content studio:** talking-head reel scripts, "relocation B-roll hooks," case-study generator, email drip generator. "Strategic prompting on the back end so you're not getting generic content from Claude or ChatGPT."
- **Every template carries an "About this template / strategy" section** explaining why it works.
- **Claude connector:** in the Claude directory; "run my weekly content dashboard" produces a week of customized hooks from her analytics + ideal client. Canva connector can drop copy into the template.
- **Workflow they teach:** open template in Canva on desktop, customize, open Canva app on phone, download, post directly in Instagram with a trending audio. No auto-posting ("we want you to add trending audio").
- **Pillars:** Attract (local guides, local shareables, B-roll reels with a text hook) → Nurture (personal brand builders, "what's in my camera roll as a [city] agent") → Convert (stories, authority builders, lead magnets, DM conversations).
- **Content examples shown:** "[City] coffee shop quiz" carousel; "[City] date night" carousel; "Send this to a future [Texan]" butter-yellow B-roll reel ("5 minutes to post"); local shareables ("As an OKC local, of course I…"); authority builders ("Real estate school didn't teach me this," "What you see vs. what you don't see"); personal-brand "camera roll" photo dump.
- **Always two style options per template**; free Canva is enough.
- **Pricing:** $74/mo (first month $44 with OPENHOUSE30), quarterly $169, annual $710. Free trial with credits. Community: Facebook group since 2019, weekly live "playbook" call with live audits, onboarding twice a week.
- **Their own honesty, verbatim-ish:** "It's not a quick win, it is really something that you have to put a little bit into it as well"; "go all in for 90 days"; "it's a compounding effect, not an overnight success"; "it's a self-serve platform, we don't create custom content."
- **Testimonials cited:** "under contract in just under a month," "$20M sold from Instagram," "11 closings this year from socials," "$7M closed in January all from Instagram," "opened escrow on my first social lead after 5–6 months consistent."

## Why it landed with Jen (read honestly)

1. **It looks like her.** Photo-first Canva templates with serif text over a soft photo are exactly her current grid. The system validates her taste instead of replacing it.
2. **It promises the feeling of a plan without the work of a plan.** A calendar fills itself. A caption writes itself. "Log out and go be a real estate agent."
3. **It never asks her to talk.** B-roll with a text hook, photo dumps, quiz carousels. No talking-head required.
4. **Effort budget is explicit:** "5 minutes to post." Our plan said three hours a week and a weekly voice memo. To her that read as a job.
5. **Social proof in her currency:** dollar figures from Instagram, agents like her.

## What it actually requires (from their own mouths)

Customize every template to her market; pick or film local clips; add trending audio; post manually; do the daily engagement task; reply to DMs; stay consistent 90 days. The "hands-off" part is the blank page, not the posting.

## Where our system lost her (diagnosis)

- **Language:** carousel, local signal, flywheel, stamp, file, saved reply. Coffee & Contracts says "template," "post," "caption," "reel." We led with our nouns.
- **Effort framing:** we quoted three hours a week and a weekly memo as a feature. They quote five minutes as a feature. Same reality, opposite frame.
- **Look:** navy line drawings and a stamp read to her as "templatized packaging"; her photo-over-serif look reads to her as hers. We built a system and she wanted a wardrobe.
- **Talking:** four reels of her talking to camera was the plan's spine. She doesn't want to yap on camera.
- **What we got right and should keep:** the copy engine, the local facts, the plain-words rule, the open door on every post, the saved replies, her verbatim close.
