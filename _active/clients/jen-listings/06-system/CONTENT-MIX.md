## The mix (month one: 13 posts, three a week)

ENGINE-V2's four districts stay as job labels. Shares are reconciled from the table above.

| District (job) | Extraction basis | Share | Posts / month | Reader's action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Attract** — Valley place content with the price band in the hook ("what $850K buys in tarzana this month," a street, a coffee place, a farmers market that exists) | Alyssa "mostly local"; Sherrard "serve the 99%"; deep research "what $X buys" + hyperlocal | **45%** | 6 | share it, save it, DM a street or a number |
| **Connect** — her life and her feelings about the work; a feeling first, real estate second, offer last | her outliers (all life-first); Alyssa comfort content; Enrico personality; deep research "trust" 30% | **25%** | 3 | send it ("this is us"), reply with a feeling |
| **Position** — one market fact translated for buyer and seller, in her calm register | Sherrard "say who it's good for"; Alyssa "authority, the smallest"; deep research "local authority" | **15%** | 2 | DM to talk numbers |
| **Convert** — her active listings and just-solds (thesis first, address second) | Alyssa "listing, because you show what you sell" | **15%** while a listing is active; its slot goes to Attract otherwise | 2 | DM for a showing |

Week shape (Tue / Thu / Sat): attract · connect-or-position (alternate) · convert-if-listing-else-attract. Over four weeks that lands at 6 / 3 / 2 / 2.

## The hook rule (every district, no exceptions)

Every post opens on **her or the reader's situation**; the number or the house is beat 2. Evidence: every bottom-quartile post on her grid leads with the house; every top post leads with her or a feeling (outlier audit, 2026-09-02). Attract posts still carry the price band in the hook, but the first words are the reader ("you keep saving the finished ones," "if you're doing the math at 11pm") or her ("i drove tarzana at 7am so you don't have to"). Topic + Who + Lens on every hook (Alyssa). Convert keeps the weeks 1–2 Bothwell rule: the thesis leads, the address follows.

## The experiment (one per month, held across its slots)

Month one (from the outlier audit's 30-day test): hold "feeling-first or life-first hook, real estate as beat 2" constant on every post; vary the **who-clause** across two life situations, fair-housing safe (a Valley renter in their mid-30s doing the math; a household that outgrew the apartment). Read saves and DMs per 1,000 views, not likes. Pivot or double down at the 1st-of-month read.

## Cadence

Three posts a week (ENGINE-V2 §8). Alyssa: "never prescribe daily posting when capacity is three." Each post gets a story slide the same morning (Enrico: stories are the capture layer). Format is decided by the pulse, not taste: one Connect post a month runs as a carousel and one as a single card; read the saves (ENGINE-V2 §5).

## Capture (Sherrard: no asset, no leads)

Every post ends with a concrete thing to send: a street, a number, a photo, a quote, or "hi." The saved replies and the valley file are the asset behind every post (ENGINE-V2 §9). Keyword-comment automation stays off; Jen finds keyword CTAs cheesy (memory `feedback_jen-intake-question-design`).
