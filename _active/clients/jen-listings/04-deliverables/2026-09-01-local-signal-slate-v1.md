## filming notes

- hook lands in the first 2 seconds, no walk-up. put the hook text on screen in frame one... most people watch muted.
- 30 to 45 seconds. one idea, one ask.
- your serif single-word captions, same as always. one emoji per piece.
- post the reel, then the carousel the next day. reply to every comment on the reel the same evening... that's where the conversations start.
- reel 2 works best over your own phone footage of the boulevard. film the median, not the van nuys G line station (it's closed for rebuild).

## don't say

- "safe," "family-friendly," "great schools," "quiet neighborhood," or anything about who a place is for. describe the place and the process, never the people.
- "prices will go up near the station." nobody can promise that.
- "apply now" for dream for all. the 2026 window closed in march. it's "watch for the next round."
- "rates are about to drop." they're mid-6s and have been all year.
- any specific neighborhood price on camera until it's checked the day you post.
