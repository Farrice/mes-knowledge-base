## What the twelve say, structurally

- **Every post is a keyword door to a lead magnet** (REALTOR, STORY, HOOKS, FALL, SOCIAL, HOME, GUIDE). Her business is selling to agents, so the keyword funnel is the product. Comments-to-likes runs high (326 on 982, 326 on 434) because the comment IS the conversion; that is the same pattern the Sept 3 comment sample flagged for Jen (keyword posts draw the keyword and nothing else). Jen sells to buyers and sellers, not agents; the door stays a DM in her words.
- **The formats worth farming with a login:** the monthly key-dates carousel (a calendar shape we already run as the weeks builder), the "story ideas" carousel (stories are the capture layer we have zero of), the "8 visual hooks" and "home tour filming guide" reels (B-roll grammar for the day Jen films twenty clips), and the "hate being on camera" ladder (matches her no-talking-head rule).
- **What not to import:** the "let this be your sign" testimonial carousel and the "if I had to start over" listicle are agent-coaching content; nothing on this grid is written to a buyer.
