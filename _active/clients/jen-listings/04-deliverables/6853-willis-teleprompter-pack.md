## Filming Rule

Start with the ADU. Do not open on the kitchen, the living room, or a generic exterior. The detached second space is the scrollstop.

Use these safe phrases:

- household
- guests
- extended living
- private work space
- flexible layout
- outdoor gathering space
- potential rental support, after verification

Avoid:

- perfect for families
- great for kids
- safe neighborhood
- best schools
- guaranteed rental income
- any exact ADU rent number unless Jen runs live rental comps first

## Reel 1 - ADU First Tour

**Job:** Make the detached ADU the reason people stop scrolling.  
**Length:** 45-60 seconds  
**Energy:** Warm, curious, "I want to show you the part that matters."  
**Opening overlay:** `Start here: detached ADU`  
**Caption CTA:** `Comment WILLIS for the ADU question list.`

### Teleprompter Script

**Beat 1 - Outside ADU door**

"Most listing tours would start in the kitchen. I am starting back here, because this is the part of the property that changes the whole conversation."

**Beat 2 - Open ADU door / step inside**

"This is the detached ADU. It is approximately 400 square feet, and this is where the flexibility starts."

**Beat 3 - Pan ADU interior**

"For the right household, this can mean guest space, extended living, a private work setup, or potential rental support if the numbers, rules, and permits all check out."

**Beat 4 - Walk from ADU toward main house**

"And that is the key. I would never tell a buyer to count on income without verifying the rental comps and the paperwork first."

**Beat 5 - Reveal main house**

"Now come with me to the main home. The listing describes about 1,980 square feet in the main residence, with 4 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms."

**Beat 6 - Enter main house**

"So the story here is not just 'pretty remodel.' It is main house plus detached second space, on a gated Van Nuys lot, with modern upgrades and room to use the property in more than one way."

**Beat 7 - Close to camera**

"If you want my ADU question list before you tour this one, comment WILLIS and I will send it over. Let's take a look."

## Reel 2 - Jen Reacts: What Changed?

**Job:** Turn the price-history jump into a trust-building explanation, not hype.  
**Length:** 45-60 seconds  
**Energy:** Educator, receipts-first, calm confidence.  
**Opening overlay:** `Sold Feb: $750K -> Listed June: $1.349M`  
**Caption CTA:** `Comment WILLIS for the property sheet and buyer questions.`

### Teleprompter Script

**Beat 1 - Green screen or phone screen with listing**

"Okay, this is the kind of listing where I do not want you to just look at the pretty photos. I want you to look at the receipts."

**Beat 2 - Point to price history**

"Public price history shows this property sold in February for $750,000 and came back on the market in June at $1,349,000."

**Beat 3 - Pause, direct to camera**

"So the question is not, 'Is it pretty?' The question is, 'What changed?'"

**Beat 4 - Cut to gate/exterior/remodel detail**

"The listing points to a full remodel, gated access, solar, modern upgrades, and a detached ADU."

**Beat 5 - Cut to ADU**

"That ADU matters because it changes how a buyer thinks about the property. Not guaranteed income. Not automatic value. But real flexibility that deserves a closer look."

**Beat 6 - Cut to interior detail**

"This is where I would slow down and verify: permits, final sign-offs, solar terms, system warranties, and live rental comps if a buyer is thinking about income."

**Beat 7 - Close to camera**

"Pretty photos get attention. Good questions protect you. Comment WILLIS and I will send the question list I would use before writing an offer."

## Reel 3 - The Buyer Questions Walkthrough

**Job:** Give buyers a save-worthy checklist and position Jen as the trusted guide.  
**Length:** 40-55 seconds  
**Energy:** Helpful, grounded, protective without sounding alarmist.  
**Opening overlay:** `5 questions I would ask before offer`  
**Caption CTA:** `Save this before touring ADU properties.`

### Teleprompter Script

**Beat 1 - Jen in driveway or near gate**

"If I were advising a buyer on 6853 Willis, these are the questions I would ask before anyone writes an offer."

**Beat 2 - ADU exterior**

"Number one: is the ADU permitted, finaled, and documented clearly?"

**Beat 3 - Solar or exterior roof angle**

"Number two: is the solar owned, financed, or leased, and what transfers to the buyer?"

**Beat 4 - Interior system/detail shot**

"Number three: what system upgrades are documented, and what warranties come with them?"

**Beat 5 - Yard / ADU / main house relationship**

"Number four: if you are thinking about rental support, what do live rental comps actually say today?"

**Beat 6 - Neighborhood or front exterior**

"Number five: does the location fit your real life - commute, recreation, school fit if that matters to you, and the way your household actually uses space?"

**Beat 7 - Close to camera**

"That does not mean something is wrong. It means this is a high-flexibility property, and high-flexibility properties deserve clear questions. Save this before you tour."

### Shot List

1. Jen standing with phone or clipboard.
2. ADU door or exterior.
3. Solar/roof/electrical panel if available.
4. HVAC/system shot if accessible and appropriate.
5. Kitchen or remodel detail.
6. Backyard path between main home and ADU.
7. Street/front exterior.
8. Jen close-up for final line.

### On-Screen Text

- `ADU permits`
- `Solar terms`
- `Warranties`
- `Live rental comps`
- `Commute + fit`
- `Save before touring`
