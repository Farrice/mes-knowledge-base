# you love the house. try the drive home.

**Job:** discovery. For anyone comparing Valley homes whose regular trip could change the decision. Local relevance comes from route-testing, not a claim that one neighborhood is better. Emotional movement: attractive listing → overlooked daily trip → testable choice.

### Caption — ready to copy

you can love a house and still want to try the drive before you get attached.

when you’re looking around the valley, check the trip from the actual street at the time you normally leave. ventura boulevard and the freeway are two routes to compare when both work for your destination. include parking, and check the way back too.

you don’t need a perfect commute. you need a clear picture of the one you’d be choosing.

save this for your next tour.
