### Caption — ready to copy

buying on your own can still come with a lot of opinions.

one person loves the kitchen. someone else thinks you should wait. before you try to settle every opinion, write down the question that is actually yours.

is it about the loan, the condition of the home, or what happens next? that tells us who needs to explain it. i can help with the search and the next steps; the lender and inspector handle their parts.

you get to understand the decision you’re making.

keep this for the day the advice gets loud.
