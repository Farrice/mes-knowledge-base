### Caption — ready to copy

two people can like the same house and have very different trips home.

before your next valley tour, put both routes beside the same street. check the times you actually travel and how often you make each trip. a longer drive once in a while may feel different from one you make every day.

then talk about what each of you can live with. the house search gets a clearer question to answer.

stuck between two valley streets? send me the two options and what you’re trying to balance. street names or general areas are enough.
