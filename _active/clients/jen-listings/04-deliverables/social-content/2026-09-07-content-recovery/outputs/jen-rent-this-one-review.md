## B-roll

## Caption

i need to know who the “we” is in “we’ll manage it” 😂

if it’s you, okay. if it’s a property manager, okay. if you and your partner are pointing at each other… we should probably talk about that.

it’s easy to get all the way to the new house in your head. everyone has more room. the dining table is a dining table again. meanwhile, the house you moved out of still needs someone to answer the phone when something breaks.

maybe you’ve thought about all of this and you really do want to keep it. good. let’s see what that would take, including paying someone else to handle the parts you don’t want to do.

or maybe renting it out was just the first idea that made moving sound possible. we can look at selling, too. you don’t have to defend a plan you came up with in the kitchen last night.

send me the house you keep looking at. tell me what you’re hoping to do with this one. let’s put the two together and see what we’re actually working with.
