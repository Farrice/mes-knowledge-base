## Write for the post's actual purpose

Research informs subject selection and claims. The finished social copy should sound like Jen speaking to a person. Use her actual archived writing as voice evidence; assistant descriptions of her register are secondary.

A relationship post can deliver its value through recognition, humor and credible permission. Do not force it to teach a financial lesson. For this campaign, generic skill requirements that every piece teach something unexpected, prove authority, include a permission hook, or supply local facts do not apply universally. Expert answers may teach; comparison carousels may explain; comic posts may make a knowing observation. Select the purpose and format first.

Preserve the reference's full conversational movement, not merely a hook formula. Allow a thought to develop. There is no compulsory word count, sentence-length pattern, cliffhanger, checklist, keyword CTA, pet name, fabricated confession, or signature close. A genuine lived example must come from evidence; an ordinary hypothetical is not a claimed client story.
