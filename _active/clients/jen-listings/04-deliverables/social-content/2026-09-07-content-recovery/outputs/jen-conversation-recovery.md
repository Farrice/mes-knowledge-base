## Caption

you don’t need to come up with a more impressive timeline before you DM me.

if the honest answer is “i don’t know yet,” tell me that. i’d much rather start there than have you feel like you need to pick a month just to ask a question.

maybe you want to know what happens first. maybe there’s a house you keep looking at and you’re trying to understand what buying something like it would actually involve.

ask me the part you’re curious about. you don’t have to turn it into “can you help me buy a house?” if that’s not where you are.

and if talking to an agent feels like the moment this goes from a thing you’re thinking about to a thing you’re doing… we can slow that down.

a conversation is allowed to end with “okay, that helps. i’m going to think about it.”

you haven’t wasted my time.
