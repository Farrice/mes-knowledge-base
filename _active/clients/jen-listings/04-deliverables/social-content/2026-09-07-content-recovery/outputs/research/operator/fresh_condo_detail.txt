L38@P1: Project StandardsUpdates
L39@P1: In 2023, we incorporated significant deferred maintenance and unsafe condition (i.e., critical repairs) requirements for condo
L40@P1: projects into the Selling Guide. Since then, we have seen a correlation between condo projects with underfunded reserves for
L41@P1: capital expenditures and those in need of critical repairs. Condo projects with inadequate reserves typically do not have the
L42@P1: requisite resources to maintain the physical condition of the project or to fund unexpected operating expenses. As a result, unit
L43@P1: owners can experience substantial financial hardship from unexpected special assessments or higher regular assessments or
L44@P1: dues, leading to mortgage default or foreclosure.
L45@P1: Our revised requirements are intended to improve the financial health of condo projects and promote long-term sustainable
L46@P1: homeownership.
L47@P1: Expansion of Waiver of Project Review
L48@P1: We are expanding eligibility for a Waiver of Project Review to include new and established projects with ten or fewer units. For
L49@P1: projects consisting of five- to ten-units, the project must not be part of a master association or larger development. Lenders who
L50@P1: use this option must comply with all Requirements that Apply When the Project Review is Waived in Selling Guide B4-2.1-02, Waiver
L51@P1: of Project Review. This includes, but is not limited to, the following:
L52@P1: • the project does not have an “Unavailable” status in Condo Project ManagerTM (CPM™),
L53@P1: • the project meets all applicable insurance requirements in Selling Guide B7-3, Property and Flood Insurance, and
L54@P1: • there are no critical repairs or evacuation orders in place if the loan is a Fannie Mae to Fannie Mae limited cash-out
L55@P1: refinance.
L56@P1: N O T E : As stated in Selling Guide B7-4-01, General Liability Insurance Requirements for Project Developments, general
L57@P1: liability and fidelity insurance are not required for condo projects that qualify for a Waiver of Project Review.
L58@P1: Effective: Lenders may take advantage of this change immediately. The Desktop Underwriter® (DU®) messages related to Waiver
L59@P1: of Project Review will be updated in a future release.
L60@P1: Retirement of PERS review for new condo projects with attached units in Florida
L61@P1: We are retiring the requirement that new or newly converted projects with attached units in Florida must be submitted to Fannie
L62@P1: Mae’s Project Eligibility Review Service (PERS). Approval of these projects, like all other new projects with attached units, can be
L63@P1: reviewed under the lender-delegated Full Review process.
L64@P1: Effective: Lenders may take advantage of this change immediately. Regarding the state-specific project review guidelines, CPM
L65@P1: has been updated and DU messages will be updated in a future release.
L66@P1: Retirement of investor concentration limits
L67@P1: We are retiring the investment property concentration limit of 50% in established projects reviewed as part of the Full Review
L68@P1: option on investor loans.
L69@P1: N O T E : The presale requirement that at least 50% of the total units in the project or subject legal phase must have been
L70@P1: conveyed or be under contract for sale to principal residence or second home purchasers still applies as described in Selling
L71@P1: Guide B4-2.2-03, Full Review: Additional Eligibility Requirements for Units in New and Newly Converted Condo Projects.
L72@P1: Effective: Lenders may take advantage of this change immediately. Regarding the investor concentration guidelines, CPM has

L74@P2: Retirement of the Limited Review process
L75@P2: We are retiring the Limited Review process. Established projects previously eligible for Limited Review must now be reviewed
L76@P2: using the Full Review process or, when applicable, the Waiver of Project Review process in Selling Guide B4-2.1-02, Waiver of
L77@P2: Project Review.
L78@P2: N O T E : This change effectively retires the remaining geographic restrictions that apply to the state of Florida. Geographic
L79@P2: restrictions remain in effect until the Limited Review process is retired on August 3, 2026.
L80@P2: Effective: Lenders may implement the retirement of Limited Review immediately but must do so for all loan applications dated
L81@P2: on or after Aug. 3, 2026. DU messages related to the Limited Review process will be updated in a future release.
L82@P2: Enhanced reserve study requirements
L83@P2: Current policy allows lenders to obtain a reserve study to demonstrate a project has sufficient reserves when it is not budgeting
L84@P2: for replacement reserves that meet our Selling Guide requirements. We are updating this policy to clarify when lenders use this
L85@P2: flexibility, they must verify the project’s budget includes the highest recommended reserve allocation amount in the reserve
L86@P2: study to adequately cover the costs identified.
L87@P2: N O T E : Lenders are no longer permitted to use the baseline funding method which is the option that allows the reserve cash
L88@P2: balance to approach but never fall below zero.
L89@P2: Effective: Lenders are encouraged to implement this change immediately but must do so for all loan applications dated on or
L90@P2: after Aug. 3, 2026.
L91@P2: Increased replacement reserve requirements
L92@P2: We are revising our reserve allocation requirement for capital expenditures and deferred maintenance from a minimum of 10% to
L93@P2: a minimum of 15% of the annual budgeted income assessment. All other requirements related to replacement reserves and the
L94@P2: review of budget adequacy remained unchanged.
L95@P2: Effective: Lenders must comply with this requirement when utilizing the Full Review process for all loan applications dated on or
L96@P2: after Jan. 4, 2027. CPM lender certifications will be updated in a future release.
L97@P2: Property Insurance Updates
L98@P2: In February 2024, we made clarifications to our property insurance requirements. Since publication of these clarifications, we
L99@P2: have heard concerns from industry partners about lenders’ and servicers’ ability to comply with certain property insurance
L100@P2: requirements. In response to this feedback, we conducted extensive market outreach and are making various updates to our
L101@P2: requirements as a result of these collaborative efforts.

L131@P4: Requirements to Confirm Coverage Sufficiency for Project Developments
L132@P4: Coverage sufficiency The master property insurance policy coverage amount must equal at least 100% of the estimated
L133@P4: replacement cost value of the project improvements, including common elements and residential
L134@P4: structures.
L135@P4: The lender or servicer may rely on any one of the following to document that the coverage amount is
L136@P4: sufficient:
L137@P4: • guaranteed replacement cost coverage, or its equivalent;
L138@P4: • extended replacement cost coverage, or its equivalent;
L139@P4: • a replacement cost value estimate provided by the insurer;
L140@P4: • the project’s insurance risk appraisal; or
L141@P4: • a statement from the insurer or other applicable professional with appropriate expertise to
L142@P4: make such a determination.
L143@P4: Loss settlement The master property insurance policy must provide coverage on a replacement cost basis, with the
L144@P4: exception of roofs.
L145@P4: N O T E : Roofs must be insured, but do not have to be insured on a replacement cost basis.
L146@P4: N O T E : Fannie Mae recognizes that some insurers may issue policies that provide coverage on
L147@P4: an actual cash value basis for personal property and certain property elements. In the event the
L148@P4: lender or servicer sees such terms in a master property insurance policy covering a project
L149@P4: development, this is acceptable.
