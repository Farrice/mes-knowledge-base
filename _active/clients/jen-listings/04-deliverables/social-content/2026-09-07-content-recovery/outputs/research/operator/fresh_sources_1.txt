L14: The Dream For All Shared Appreciation Loan is a down payment and/or closing cost assistance program for first-generation homebuyers, used with the Dream For All Conventional first mortgage.
L15: When the home is sold, transferred or the first mortgage is paid in full, the homebuyer repays the original down payment loan, plus a share of the appreciation in the value of the home.

L18:

L20: ## Program Highlights
L21: 
L22:   * Offers up to 20% for down payment or closing costs, not to exceed $150,000
L23:   * Homebuyer must register for a voucher. A randomized drawing will select registrants who will receive the voucher. This will not be first come, first served.
L24: ## Eligibility
L25: 
L26:   * One borrower must be a cite16†first-generation homebuyer .
L27:   * One borrower must be current resident of California.
L28:   * All borrowers must be cite17†first-time homebuyers .
L29:   * Combined household income must meet cite18†CalHFA Income Limits for the county you are purchasing in.
L30: ## Next Steps

L32:     * Not Selected: You can still work with a cite19†CalHFA Approved Lender and explore other CalHFA down payment assistance options like cite20†MyHome and cite21†CalPLUS with ZIP .
L33:     * Waitlist: check your cite15†voucher portal account†calhfa.dreamforallvoucher.org periodically and monitor your email. If a voucher position becomes available and you are next on the waitlist, your status will change to Voucher Issued, and you will be notified right away by email.
L34:     * Voucher Issued: You will see this status if you are randomly selected to receive a voucher! We will email detailed instructions and next steps to you along with your voucher.

L60: ## Program FAQ
L61: ### California Dream For All Shared Appreciation Loan
L62: 
L63: When will I hear if I was successful in receiving a "Dream for All" voucher?
L64:   * CalHFA is expecting it will take several weeks to process and audit applications and conduct the randomized drawing.
L65:   * You will be notified through your portal account and email.
L66:   * All applicants will receive a notification, whether they were selected, waitlist, or not selected, but it may take several weeks as we work through the review and audit process, so please keep checking your portal account and email.
L67: 
L68: After March 16, 2026 can I apply for a Dream for All voucher?
L69: No the portal is closed. Please check with your lender for other cite28†downpayment assistance programs that may be available to you.
L70: 
L71: After March 16, 2026, can I still update my application?
L72:   * If you submitted your application and your status shows Application Received, and you have received a request for additional information, yes, you will be able to upload the additional information requested. Otherwise, no changes can be made after the application has been submitted.
L73:   * If you started an application but never submitted it, unfortunately, you will not be able to submit it because the portal closed on March 16th and no new applications can be submitted.
L74: If my application is in "Not Selected" status, can I cancel my application and start a new one?
L75: 
L76: The portal closed on March 16, 2026 and no new applications can be started.

L78: cite25†back to top L79: ## Shared Appreciation Examples
L80: 
L81: Shared Appreciation is a little more complex than a typical mortgage loan, so we’ve put together a few examples for you.
L82: 
L83: Shared Appreciation Examples
L84: Example 1
L85: ---
L86:  | Borrower is a moderate-income homebuyer
L87:   * Dream For All provides a loan for 20% of the home purchase price.
L88:   * The homeowner pays back the original loan amount plus 20% of any appreciation in the value of the home.
L89: Example 2
L90: ---
L91:  | Borrower income less than or equal to 80% AMI using the HomeReady Lookup Tool
L92:   * Reduced (0.75:1) program appreciation share
L93:   * Program appreciation share is equal to 0.75 times the Shared Appreciation Loan Amount (i.e., the original principal amount) as a percentage of the home value
L94:   * Dream For All provides a loan for 20% of the home purchase price.
L95:   * The homeowner pays back the original loan amount plus 15% of any appreciation in the value of the home

L108: 
L109: CalHFA firmly believes that homebuyer education and counseling is critical to the success and happiness of a homeowner, and requires homebuyer education and counseling for first-time homebuyers using a CalHFA program.
L110: 
L111: Who has to take this Homebuyer Education and Counseling course?
L112: 
L113: Only one occupying first-time borrower on each loan transaction.
