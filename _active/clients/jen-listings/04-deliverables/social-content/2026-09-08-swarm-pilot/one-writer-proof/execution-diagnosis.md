## Why the fluent explanation did not fix the work

In the recorded run, the diagnosis named standards without requiring a particular creative decision to satisfy them. “More curiosity” became another question about a worry. “Show what working with Jen feels like” became an invitation to call. The reviews recognized those surface features and accepted them without checking whether the complete reading experience paid the hook's actual promise.

That is the observable difference between explaining a defect and correcting this artifact. The missing step was choosing the specific discovery and the exchange that would demonstrate it, then letting those choices govern the whole piece. This is an assessment of this run, not a claim about the model's hidden reasoning or a general model comparison.

## What changed in this execution

One conductor writes one complete post, using the recovered two-resource craft approach and the current approved specimen. The source supplies a real tradeoff: quiet/shared walls versus the work of a house, alongside a favorable current rental. Before drafting, `decision-before-draft.md` fixes the intended discovery, exact hook promise and observable response from Jen.

The result is `jen-neighbors.md`. The opening frames current neighbors as influencing the first-home choice; the caption shows Jen asking what the buyer wants to preserve and how much work they actually want to take on. It ends on permission to admit that a fixer is unappealing, rather than a general offer to help. No quiet-home guarantee or fabricated client encounter is used.

The execution proof is a complete, reviewable post. It is **not** proof of superior taste, engagement, lower human effort or successful swarm adoption. Those remain unproven until the corresponding human judgments or observations exist.
