## Where the review lowered its own standard

The editor's preflight correctly required a specific discovery and a view of what Jen would do. It warned against generic reassurance. The final review then accepted an “unresolved worry” as enough evidence of curiosity and a “conversation and help with the decision” as enough payoff. Compare [editor-review.md](editor-review.md) lines 7–15 with lines 43–47.

The craft owner integrated that advice faithfully, and the conductor accepted the final. This was not a lost-message failure. The contributions survived; the editorial verdict was weak. Calling the result CLEAN or showing contribution coverage could not resolve that weakness.

The original primary Kallaway transcript supports developing substance and evidence before the hook, then preserving an established voice during scripting: `extractions/kallaway-content-system/transcript.txt`, lines 388–440, 554–564, 636–644 and 682–692. The corrective decision is to establish the specific earned discovery and evidence of Jen's response before approving a hook. That is different from simply polishing a question about a sourced concern.

## Decision

**Retain current production method.** The one bounded swarm repair is exhausted and failed its human quality comparison. Active human correction/preparation time remains unmeasured; extensive feedback is not a substitute for timed data. B-roll 2, carousel 1 and production rollout remain unstarted.

The next meaningful production unit, if continued, is one complete reading experience owned by one writer: a specific source-backed discovery, the concrete exchange that lets the reader experience Jen's help, and an on-screen hook that creates a compelling reason to reach that exact payoff. This audit has applied that check to the existing approved and rejected artifacts. It does not manufacture another candidate, new approval, additional rule, automated taste score or revised extraction pipeline.
