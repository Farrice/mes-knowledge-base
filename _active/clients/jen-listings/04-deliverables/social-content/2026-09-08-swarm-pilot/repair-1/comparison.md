# Jen — revised hook and caption comparison

### Version 1

## On-screen text

“What if I buy a home and the neighbors are still the problem?”

## Caption

“at least i own it now” would only make you feel better for so long 😅

you can have a great deal on rent and enough space, and still hear the arguing downstairs and think… i can’t keep doing this.

you might be months away from buying and still going back and forth between a condo and a house. being able to watch a movie in peace is the part you’re clear on.

tell me that part.

if shared walls are worrying you, i want to hear it. if taking care of a house sounds like another whole job, i want to hear that, too. we can talk through what each would mean for you.

you don’t have to decide between a condo and a house before we talk. telling me what you’re tired of is part of how we figure it out.


### Version 2

## On-screen text

“what if i buy my first home and the neighbors are just as loud?”

## Caption

how much of your neighbors’ business can you hear from your couch? 😂

because wanting to finish a movie without an argument coming through the floor doesn’t sound like a wild request to me.

and if your rent is actually a good deal, i get why “just move” isn’t much of an answer.

tell me that when we talk about what you’re looking for. i’ll ask about the budget and bedrooms, but i also want to know what you’re hoping will be different when you get home.

you can be months away, still going back and forth between a house and a condo. you don’t have to finish figuring that out before you call me. i can help with that part too.
