i can’t tell you a place will be quiet just because it doesn’t share a wall. we don’t have to pretend either option is an easy yes.

but if getting home and being able to unwind is a big part of why you’re moving, i want that in the conversation while we’re looking. even if the kitchen is giving you very good reasons to change the subject.

tell me what you’re hoping you won’t have to deal with in the next place. “i want to sit on my couch and relax” is plenty to work with.
