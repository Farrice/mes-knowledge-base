## Caption

the finished condo is very convincing when the other option comes with a whole list of things to fix 😂

you look at the photos and can already picture your couch in there. then you open the house listing again and start wondering what “needs work” would mean for your weekends.

but you’re still wondering what you’d hear through the walls.

please don’t talk yourself out of caring about that. if you’re tired of coming home and being irritated by noise, “i just want to relax” is enough of a reason for us to pay attention.

we don’t know yet whether this condo would have the same problem. i’m happy to look into it with you. liking the place doesn’t mean we have to start defending it.

and the house that needs work? we can look at that too, without deciding you’ve suddenly developed a passion for renovations. having your own walls doesn’t promise quiet either.

we can stay interested in the condo and take a little longer with the part that’s bothering you. i’d rather do that than have you sitting there after you move, trying to remember why you told yourself the noise probably wouldn’t matter.
