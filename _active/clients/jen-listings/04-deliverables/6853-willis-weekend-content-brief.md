## Strategic Read

This is above the 91405 median by a wide margin. Do not sell it as "accessible Van Nuys." Sell it as a flexible compound-style setup at a price where buyers usually get one house, not a main residence plus detached second space.

The content should answer:

- What changed since the February sale?
- What does the detached ADU make possible?
- What would a buyer need to verify before counting on rental income?
- What is better here than a similarly priced home without a second space?
- What is the honest trade-off of this exact pocket?

## Fair Housing Guardrails

Safer words:

- household
- guests
- extended living
- private work space
- flexible layout
- outdoor gathering space
- nearby parks and recreation

Avoid in public-facing copy:

- perfect for families
- great for kids
- ideal for young couples
- safe neighborhood
- best schools
- no kids / adults only / any protected-class cue

HUD identifies familial status as a protected class under the Fair Housing Act. The safe play is to describe property features and invite buyers to verify fit for themselves.
