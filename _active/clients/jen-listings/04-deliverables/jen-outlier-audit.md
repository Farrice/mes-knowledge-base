## Hypothesis

**This account moves when the hook is Jen's life or a feeling her person already has (toddler, marriage, dating humor, budget-vs-wishlist), and real estate rides in second. It flatlines whenever the property is the hook.**

Supporting numbers: personal-lens hooks median 161 likes vs property hooks median 78 (2.1×); the only 2× comment outlier in window is the one real-estate post shaped as a relatable feeling.

## 30-Day Test (one experiment)

Hold constant: **feeling-first or life-first hook; real estate as beat 2.** Change one variable across the set: **the who-clause** (mid-30s Valley renter vs. new-parent Valley household, life-situation framing only, fair-housing safe).

| # | Slot | Tag | Hook direction | Format | Expectation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | weekly | local (NEW) | Valley spots / Valley life through the who-clause ("…if you just signed another lease and told yourself one more year") | B-roll + text hook, A/B one as carousel | reach + follows; target > 2,578 views |
| 5–7 | every ~10 days | comfort (FTHB flip) | "self-care as someone who's 34, makes good money, and still hasn't bought in LA" → offer last as permission | carousel (first carousels on the account) | saves + comments; target > 13 comments |

Data window: 30 days from first post. Rule: if median views on slots 1–7 beat 2,578 by 1.5× **or** median comments beat 13 by 2×, double down (make comfort carousels the weekly anchor). If neither, pivot the who-clause, not the format. No second experiment until this one reports.
