five voice memos, whenever you have a minute in the car. sixty seconds each, first thing that comes out, don't fix it. each one becomes a reel and a carousel in your look; you read everything before it posts.

1. the 20% down myth.
   someone at dinner says “we'd buy, but we don't have 20% down.” what comes out of your mouth?

2. what your rent already buys.
   a friend pays around $3,000 a month in rent and thinks buying is out of reach. walk them through it the way you would in the car.

3. “i don't think we're ready.”
   what do you say to the couple who's been “almost ready” for two years?

4. the thirty days after yes.
   their offer just got accepted. what happens in the next thirty days, and where do people freak out?

5. how to spot a lipstick remodel.
   you walk into a flip. what do you touch first, and what tells you they cut corners?

that's it. no scripts, no reading anything. just talk.
