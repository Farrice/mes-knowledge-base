# Prompts for Claude Design

Paste one at a time. Attach `DESIGN.md`, `RULEBOOK.md`, `COPY-DECK.md` and the photos first, or select the Jen Santulan · Valley Native design system. Each prompt asks for distinct directions on one axis, so what comes back is a real choice.

---

## 0 · Ground it (paste first, once)

> You are designing Instagram carousel slides (1080×1350) for Jen Santulan, @_jiing, a San Fernando Valley realtor whose niche is first-time buyers. Use the attached DESIGN.md as the design system and RULEBOOK.md as the rules. The copy in COPY-DECK.md is final: do not rewrite, shorten, or "improve" any of it. Photography comes only from the attached photos; treat each as a print with a 3px navy border, a 14px mat, a 1.5° tilt, and a small tracked-caps caption. Palette is navy #1E3A5F, steel #4C7CA8, soft blue #C9D4E2, cream #F7F5F2 and nothing warm: no orange, terracotta, red, yellow or tan, ever. Fonts: Figtree, Playfair Display italic (one accent phrase per slide), Overpass for small tracked caps. Every slide carries the masthead (@_JIING · FIRST-TIME BUYER FILE) and the stamp (neighborhood · zip / FROM THE VALLEY) in the same position. No emoji as graphics, no gradients, no drop shadows on type, no rounded cards. The reference PNGs show the current build; match its quality, then go further.

## 1 · Cover composition, four directions

> Using slide 1 of the condo carousel ("what if the building is the problem... not me?"), give me four genuinely different cover compositions in the same system, one artboard each, side by side: (A) the current layout, headline bottom-left, print upper-right, ghost Valley map bleeding off the right edge; (B) headline at the top, the print bleeding off the bottom edge; (C) a full-width print in the upper two-thirds with a cream band carrying the headline; (D) no photograph, the ghost map at full size, the headline at 110px with the italic phrase on its own line. Under each, one line: what it does better and what it costs. Keep the stamp, masthead, palette and copy identical across all four.

## 2 · Photo treatment, four directions

> Take slide 5 of the condo carousel ("if your share of one claim goes over $50,000... the loan stops.") and show four photo treatments for the Van Nuys Blvd print: (A) the current bordered print; (B) the same print with a torn top edge drawn in a single navy line; (C) the photo as a navy-on-cream duotone with no border; (D) the photo masked inside the outline of the drawn mid-rise building from the design system. Same copy, same layout otherwise. One line each on which reads best at thumbnail size.

## 3 · Rhythm across the set, three directions

> Show the full seven-slide condo carousel three times as three rows: (A) navy slides at 4 and 7, the current rhythm; (B) navy at 1 and 7; (C) cream throughout with only slide 7 navy. Keep every other element fixed. Then tell me which row reads as one post on a nine-square Instagram grid and which reads as seven.

## 4 · Type scale, three directions

> Take slides 1, 3 and 6 of the condo carousel and show them at three type scales: (A) current, headline 70px and the italic accent 94px; (B) louder, headline 96px, one thought per line, the numeral slides at 240px; (C) quieter, headline 56px with more body text visible. Same copy, same photos, same palette. One line on which one a tired person scrolling at 1am reads first.

## 5 · The stamp, four directions

> Show slide 2 of the rail carousel ("eleven stations. van nuys to pacoima.") four times, changing only the stamp: (A) the current roundel plus two lines of text under the masthead; (B) text only, no mark; (C) a larger stamp, 2× size, in the same position; (D) the stamp turned into a vertical spine of tracked caps running down the left edge. Which one is still recognizable at 150px wide?

## 6 · A whole new set from the system

> Build a new seven-slide carousel in this system for the stamp RESEDA · 91335. Structure: slide 1 cover (fear or contradiction, ≤12 words, one italic phrase), slide 2 the keyed map (an italic line, a sans line, a body, four drawn stops with 01–04 markers and labels), slides 3–6 one idea each with a number that surprises or a scene, navy on slide 4, slide 7 the close on navy with the ask box "my DMs are open" and her exact close: "i'm here for you... i do this to protect you and your best interest." Copy must be plain words with punch: no term a renter has never heard survives without a plain-word gloss beside it. Use only the attached photos. Leave every number as [VERIFY] placeholders; I will fill them the day it posts.

## 7 · Presentation board variations

> Take board 3 of the presentation ("the flywheel") and show three different ways to draw the loop in navy line: (A) the current circle with six square stops and a label column; (B) a figure-eight where the content loop and the referral loop share the center; (C) a horizontal six-step strip with drawn glyphs above each step. Same words. One line on which one Jen would understand in five seconds.
