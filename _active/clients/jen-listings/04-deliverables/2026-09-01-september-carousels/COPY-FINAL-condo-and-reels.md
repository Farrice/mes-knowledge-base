## condo carousel

1. **what if the building is the problem... not me?** · the question under the 1am scroll.
2. **you were pre-approved. that was never the question.** · since august 3, 2026, banks check the building's books on almost every regular condo loan... the ordinary kind, not FHA or VA.
3. **i open the budget before i look at the balcony.** · i'm looking for one thing: the savings account. 10% of the building's budget today, 15% for loans dated january 4, 2027 or later.
4. *(navy slide)* **then twelve months of meeting notes.** · page four, last november... "roof discussed." that's a surprise bill split between every owner, sitting there months before it reaches your mailbox.
5. **then the building's own insurance policy.** · it has to cover a full rebuild, and your share of any claim has to stay under $50,000. over that, the loan stops.
6. **then the list of who's actually paying.** · 15% or more of units 60+ days late on dues and no bank will lend on any unit in it. yours included. · *small print: fannie mae LL-2026-03 · freddie mac bulletin 2026-C*
7. **send me the address before you write.** · we'll read the four documents together and go from there. i'm here for you... i do this to protect you and your best interest 🏡 · *button: my DMs are open*

**glossary card** *(8th slide or caption)*
- **full review** → the bank checks the building, not just you · **conventional loan** → the ordinary kind, not FHA or VA
- **reserve study** → the building's savings plan for roofs and pipes · **board minutes** → notes from its monthly meetings
- **HOA package** → the paperwork a building hands over · **special assessment** → a surprise bill, split between every owner
- **master insurance** → the building's own policy, separate from yours · **replacement cost** → enough to rebuild it
- **per-unit deductible** → your share of a claim before insurance pays · **delinquent** → behind on dues
- **non-warrantable** → no bank will lend on any unit in it

## reel 3 · the insurance quote moved

**hook:** "i moved the insurance quote to before the offer... and it's because of october 15th."

**other hooks:** "your loan can be approved... and the insurance still blows up the payment." · "october 15th changes the math on hillside homes."

**script:**
> "i used to get the insurance quote during the inspection window. now i get it before we write the offer.
> on october 15th the california FAIR plan goes up 29.1% on average. that's the state's backup fire policy... what you get when no regular company will cover the house. it's weighted to wildfire risk, so canyons and hillsides move a lot more than that.
> the day your policy starts decides your rate. one that starts before the 15th is generally priced at today's rate for its term.
> valley floor... just breathe. most of those homes still get a regular company. i want the quote on a tuesday, not in escrow.
> i do this to protect you and your best interest ✨"

**on screen:** insurance quote → before the offer · oct 15: FAIR plan +29.1% · hillside ≠ valley floor · the day it starts decides your rate · tuesday, not in escrow

**caption:**

## reel 4 · just breathe

**hook:** "if you're reading about rates at 11pm... just breathe."

**other hooks:** "nothing good gets decided right before bed." · "three questions i ask in the morning."

**script:**
> "if you're lying there at 11pm refreshing rates, here's what i tell my clients. just breathe. take a step back. let's sleep on it and talk in the morning... nothing good gets decided right before bed.
> then i ask three things.

> have you looked at a buydown. that's someone paying a little upfront so your rate is lower... either for good, or just the first couple of years.

> we're gonna sleep on it, get your numbers in the morning, and go from there. i'm here for you... that's my job 🤝"
