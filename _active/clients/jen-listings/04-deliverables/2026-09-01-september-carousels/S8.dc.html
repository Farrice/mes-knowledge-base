SAN FERNANDO VALLEY FROM THE VALLEY
the people who already love you.
you show up for everyone and you never ask. that’s why they love you, and it’s why the referrals stopped. the fix isn’t asking harder. it’s giving them a reason and a moment.
1
the quarterly valley note. one text to every past client and close friend, four times a year. what actually changed in the valley this quarter, in your words, useful whether they’re moving or not. last line, every time: “if someone you love is thinking about buying, i’d love to be the first call.”
2
the one-year text. a year after every closing: “one year in the house. how’s it treating you?” the easiest conversation you’ll ever restart.
3
the who-do-you-know moment. after every thank-you, one specific ask: “who’s the one friend who keeps saying they’ll never afford LA? send them my way, i’ve got them.”
4
the referral reply. when a name comes in, the reply is written: “thank you for trusting me with them. i’m here for them the way i was here for you.”
