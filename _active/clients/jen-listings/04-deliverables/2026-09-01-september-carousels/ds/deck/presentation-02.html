SAN FERNANDO VALLEY FROM THE VALLEY
four words, then plain english.
everything tonight uses these four. nothing else needs a definition.
1
a reel. a forty-second video of you talking, filmed on your phone. it’s how strangers find you.
2
a carousel. a post you swipe through, seven slides. people save these and send them to a friend. it’s how strangers trust you.
3
the stamp. a small mark on every post: the neighborhood and the zip. over time your grid becomes a map of the valley with you on it.
4
the file. three one-page guides in your look. you send them to anyone who writes. no form, no catch.
