SAN FERNANDO VALLEY FROM THE VALLEY
a post is a billboard.
a system is a door.
most agents post and hope. this is built so every post has a reason, a job, and a next step.
1
every post starts with something real. a rule that changed, a train that’s coming, a quote that moved. on your streets, this week. never a trend.
2
every post has one job. one person writes to you. not likes, not views. an address, a question, or just “hi, i’ve been meaning to reach out.” the door is open on every post, and people learn that.
3
every reply is already written. when they write, you answer the same evening from a saved reply, in your voice. paste, add their name, send.
4
what they write back decides what’s next. the questions in your DMs are the plan for next month. nothing is made in a vacuum.
