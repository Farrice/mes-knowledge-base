## Synthesis — 6 lines

1. **Keyed legend map** (Herb Lester, Providence, Sunset corridor) — numbered stops + hand-lettered labels. Ranks first; navy line on cream is literally this form.
2. **Facade/building typology tile** (Little Atlanta) — one drawn building per unit, tiled. Second; pure line work, zero color risk.
3. **Real signage as label** (Highland Park plaque, Valley Relics neon) — photograph the actual sign instead of typesetting the name. Third; needs photo, so it lives on photo slides only.
4. **Place-stamp badge** (zip/cross-street/neighborhood label on every post — Block Club, Valley Relics bio). Fourth; cheapest to add, highest consistency payoff.
5. **Postcard-script pictorial map** (Valley Mentality). Fifth — **clashes.** It's inherently multi-color and nostalgic-warm; forcing it to navy/cream kills it. Adapt as a single-ink outline map only.
6. Avoid the category default: white serif italic on full-bleed photo is what 16 of 18 competitors already do. Lead with the line system; let serif italic stay an accent word, not the identity.
