# Hotel Test — Jen Santulan, Luxury Listing Lane ($2M+)

Run 2026-08-05 via `/hotel-test` (oren-norton-world-building), first live deployment. Grounded in: register-ladder canon (Quiet Flex Elite Advisor, 2026-08-05), 6853 Willis golden ref, listing-package workflow.

## Verdict: FOG at practice level, VIVID-available at listing level

Imagining "Jen's luxury practice as a hotel," cold: a *register* arrives — quiet, private-market, no-hype, keyword-DM concierge — but almost no *rooms*. I get how the attendant TALKS (the Quiet Flex voice is genuinely defined — authority-POV, market-thesis-first, property-as-evidence) but not what the lobby smells like, what's on the pillow, or what trinket you leave with. That's the Ferrari/Hyundai midpoint: a voice without a world. Per the skill's rule, FOG doesn't get a faked battery — it gets the diagnosis and the route.

**What exists (keep, it's the hard part):** the attendant's language. "Private market brief" is not just a caption format — it's a latent INSTITUTION. The keyword-DM CTA is a latent INITIATION (you apply for access; you don't click a link).

**What's missing:** setting (whose world is this — quiet-money SFV? what decade of wealth?), institutions (nothing named beyond the caption register), props (no artifact a buyer or agent keeps), initiation artifact (the DM keyword confers access but delivers... a PDF like everyone else's).

## The route (3 moves, smallest first)

1. **Institutionalize "The Private Market Brief"** — make it a named, numbered publication ("Private Market Brief № 14 — Bothwell, Tarzana"), same masthead every listing, implied back-catalog. One institution, zero new work per listing, instant pre-history. (world-institutions)
2. **The exit trinket** — one open-house/showing artifact at zoom-in grade for $2M+ listings only: a printed brief card that reads like a private document, not a flyer. Strangers should comment on it. Fair-housing-safe: all vernacular describes the PROPERTY and the market, never the people. (prop-audit)
3. **Listing-as-micro-world** — next $2M+ listing runs `/campaign-world`: era battery per property (what's playing when you walk in, who greets you, the takeaway), throughline = the Quiet Flex register + Brief masthead. Each listing = an album era of one practice. (campaign-world)

**Brand Trip Test check (honest):** who's on Jen's luxury trip today — would people care? Right now the trip is competent listings, not a world; agents wouldn't post about it. After move 1+2, the Brief masthead and the takeaway card are the postable objects. That's the gap this closes.

**Don't-say (fair housing, standing):** every world element frames property, market, and provenance — never buyer identity, demographics, or "who belongs here."
