# ICP — Jen Santulan (@_jiing), San Fernando Valley

*Sibling brand context, populated 2026-09-02 from `06-system/ENGINE-V2.md`, `06-system/CONTENT-MIX.md`, client `CLAUDE.md`, and `strategy_briefs/Strategy_Brief_First_Time_Home_Buyers_in_SoCal.md`. Client brand: Farrice's own ICP does not apply here.*

## Who
Buyers $800K–$2M in the San Fernando Valley (Tarzana, Encino, Sherman Oaks, Woodland Hills, Reseda, Van Nuys, Studio City), including first-timers: dual-income couples, 30–42, $100K–$200K household income, with the down payment already saved. Sellers in the same neighborhoods deciding whether to list.

## The segment this post is for
Renters with capital who feel priced out. They have the down payment sitting in savings, pay $3,000–$4,000 a month in rent, scroll listings nightly, and everything they see reads $1.1M and up. Their belief: "we're priced out of the Valley." The truth on the ground: asking prices are not closing prices, four Tarzana houses closed at $840K–$950K in the last week of August, sellers are taking ~98% of list after ~58 days, and condos close near $490K.

## Their words
- "everything decent is over a million."
- "we make good money and still can't buy here."
- "we keep saving and the number keeps moving."
- "we're paying someone else's mortgage."
- "we don't want to overpay at the top."

## The one belief to shift
"we're priced out" → "we're priced out of the asking prices we keep looking at. the closed ones tell a different story, and someone local can send us the three worth seeing."

## What winning feels like
Someone who knows the street sends them three real options at their number, tells them which one she'd go see, and doesn't push. They finally write a number down instead of scrolling.

## Fair-housing floor (always)
No safe / family / great-for-kids / schools / church / demographic language. Describe the property and the numbers. Never another broker's address on a frame.

## Reader's action
DM a street or a number. "buying or selling" in the ask. Never a keyword-comment gimmick.
