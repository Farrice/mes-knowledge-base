## (b) The 30-Minute Daily Split

Run this every posting day, in this order:

1. **15 min — reply sprint.** Genuine, specific, useful replies in side-hustle, personal-finance, and dad/parenting-finance threads with real reach. This borrows reach for a 0-audience account and drives profile visits, which is the entire top of the same-session funnel. No pitching in the reply itself — the value in the reply IS the pitch. The link lives on the profile (pinned post), not in your reply text.
2. **10 min — one original post.** Post the day's angle from the rotation above. One idea per post, no exceptions.
3. **5 min — DMs.** Answer anyone who's engaged. Convert in the conversation itself. End with the link *right now* if they're ready — never "I'll follow up" or "check back later." Session-only attribution means a delayed click can cost the sale entirely.

## (c) What the Test Kit Is Actually Testing

**The real KPI: receipt-DMs and sales.** Everything below is a leading indicator, not the finish line. A post that gets replies but never turns into a receipt-DM has done half its job.

**Leading indicators, in order of how directly they predict the KPI:**
1. **Profile visits** — off replies and posts. Without this, the pinned offer post never gets seen.
2. **Saves** — signals intent to come back and act, which matters more here than a like given the whole mechanic depends on same-session conversion.
3. **Replies** — the currency of the reply-native cold start; direct evidence for or against the integrity-screen post's job.
4. **DMs** — the step immediately before a sale. Track separately from receipt-DMs so it's visible when conversations start but don't close.

**Three specific things this kit is designed to answer:**
- **Link-in-post vs. link-in-first-reply reach.** Posts 01 and 04 carry the live link; posts 02, 03, and 05 are text-only. Compare reach and engagement on the link-carrying posts against the text-only baseline. If the link posts underperform the text posts over these 5, that's the signal Threads is throttling in-post links after all [the practitioner claim in the ground-truth doc is LIKELY, not proven] — fall back to link-in-first-reply for the Starter Sprint.
- **Does the coach/behavior frame resonate, or does it read as therapy.** Watch reply sentiment directly on Posts 01 and 03 — the two most behaviorally-framed pieces. Every post pairs the insight with a concrete mechanic specifically to mitigate this risk; if replies still trend toward "so what do I actually do" with no mention of the course or the money angle, the pairing isn't landing hard enough.
- **Which of the three spearheads grips.** Compare profile-visit and save rates across 01 (Adherence Coach), 02 (SAHD), and 03 (Progressive Overload) to see which belief-shift the audience actually leans into. That's the angle to lead with in the Starter Sprint.

**Diagnostic order if receipt-DMs are at zero after the kit runs:**
1. If profile visits and saves are strong but receipt-DMs are zero → **funnel problem** (offer post, disclosure placement, or DM close). Fix Post 04 or the DM script before touching anything else.
2. If profile visits themselves are weak → **hook problem** in the spearheads, not an offer problem. Rewrite openers before rewriting the offer.

## (d) The Learn-Then-Scale Gate

Do not commit to the full 7-day Starter Sprint until at least **one** of these shows up by the end of the 5-post kit:

- At least one **receipt-DM** (the clearest possible green light — go straight to Starter Sprint).
- **No receipt-DM yet, but:** profile visits on at least 2 of the 5 posts visibly outpace your normal per-post baseline, **and** at least one reply thread runs 3+ genuine back-and-forth exchanges (evidence the reply-native mechanic is working, even pre-sale).
- **No receipt-DM yet, but:** at least one DM conversation opens organically off a post or reply and shows real buying intent (asking about price, asking "does this actually work," asking about the toolkit specifically).

If none of these three show up — no receipt-DM, no above-baseline profile visits, no DM intent — the read is not "post more of the same." It's "diagnose per part (c) before spending another 5 days." Rewrite the weakest link (usually the hook on the spearheads, per the diagnostic order above) and run a second 5-post cycle before committing to 7 straight days.

## Guardrails Carried Into Every Post (recap — full detail in `00-ground-truth-compliance-and-offer.md` and `00-farrice-voice-and-assets.md`)

- Zero income claims anywhere above. The $1,000 language is always a target the buyer works toward, never a promised outcome — check this phrasing every time before posting.
- No income/earnings screenshots.
- Affiliate disclosure sits at or before the link in every post that has one (01, 04) and in the warm-adjacent DM.
- No fake scarcity or urgency beyond the real, structural one: click this link now, in this session, or the toolkit isn't attached to the purchase.
- Family privacy boundary holds in every post: naptime, the toddler, the eighteen years — never mother/brother specifics, never the private wound underneath the SAHD texture.
- Confidence labels are not printed in the copy anywhere. Fanbasis/payout details, true session-only-vs-cookie attribution, and Threads no-throttle behavior stay UNCONFIRMED/LIKELY internally; nothing above states any of the three as settled fact to the reader.
