### The Philosophy: Education in Human Values (EHV)
Rooted in the teachings of **Sathya Sai Baba** (Indian guru and philanthropist), the EHV framework operates on two principles:
1. **There is inherent goodness in every human being.**
2. **Be the example of goodness you want to inspire in your students.**

| Value | Description |

| **Right Conduct** (Dharma) | Ethical action, responsibility, integrity |
| **Non-Violence** (Ahimsa) | Respect for all life, no harm in thought/word/deed |
