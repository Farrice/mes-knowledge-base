## Lane 1 — SEO Eligibility And Discovery

**Primary hypothesis:** After approved publication and indexation, the fixed target-query cluster may produce new non-branded GSC impressions relative to the preserved baseline and matched untouched questions.

**Primary stage:** `RANKED`  
**Window:** 56 days after indexation

### Receipts

| Moment | Required evidence | Permitted event |
|---|---|---|
| Before publication | GSC query/page export, index state, canonical, live URL, matched questions | None; preserve baseline only |
| Publication | Dated live URL and source hash | `PUBLISHED` |
| Indexation | Dated engine index receipt for the exact canonical | `INDEXED` |

**Falsifier:** The page is indexed but the target cluster shows no observable impression change relative to the preserved baseline or matched untouched questions during the fixed 56-day window.

No impressions after the fixed window is a negative observation, not a reason to relabel the prediction.

## Lane 2 — AEO Citation Observation

Use a fixed intervention prompt set and matched control set. Record each engine separately. When the interface exposes them, preserve model/version, locale, logged-in state, timestamp, prompt, repeat number, answer, cited URL, and screenshot or export hash.

Candidate engines for a later approved test: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI features, and Bing/Copilot. No engine was tested in this run.

Observe at baseline, day 28, and day 56. A single mention or citation is one `CITED` observation. It is not rank, traffic, validation, or causal lift.
