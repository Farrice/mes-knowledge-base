## Extracted Mechanics That Changed The Decision

| Mechanic | Source/system role | Changed decision |
|---|---|---|
| Message before visibility | Existing Proof-to-Market canon plus Search Content Mastery routing | Search follows an accepted Angle Map direction instead of becoming the cold offer |
| One intent and content object at a time | Nathan Gotch corpus recurrence; corpus consistency only | One answer page replaces a bulk article cluster |
| Information gain before production | SearchBrief and current people-first guidance | The page owns the evidence-to-belief chain instead of summarizing regulations |
| Complete claim impression | FTC/FDA owner guidance | “Safer words” is replaced by express and implied claim inventory plus qualified review |
| Ordinary search eligibility before AI novelty | Google, Bing, and OpenAI owner guidance | No secret AEO markup or citation promise |
| Independent outcome stages | Search Content Mastery data contract | Readiness scores cannot become ranking, citation, traffic, conversion, or revenue proof |

## What The Score Does Not Prove

- It does not prove search demand for the target wording.
- It does not prove that Farrice likes the article, post, or visual enough to publish.
- It does not substitute for a real client's product truth or qualified claim review.
- It does not prove a live site can crawl, index, rank, or earn a citation.
- It does not establish buyer demand, conversion, delivery margin, or repeatability.
