# Architecture Verdict — Angle Map To Search Implementation

**Verdict:** Keep The Angle Map unchanged. Put search implementation inside the existing $2,500 Proof-to-Market Sprint as a private priority-asset track. Do not launch a standalone AEO/SEO entry offer yet.

## The Commercial Sequence

> The Angle Map decides the belief and proof direction. The Search Answer track turns one accepted decision into one owned answer asset. Measurement records what happened without rewriting readiness as results.

The same buyer can need both services, but they are buying different jobs:

| Stage | Buyer job | Existing commercial home |
|---|---|---|
| Decide | Choose what the brand should say, what approved proof supports it, and which three arguments deserve production | The Angle Map, $750 prepaid |
| Implement | Turn one accepted decision into a source-backed answer page and distribution handoff | Proof-to-Market Sprint, $2,500 prepaid |
| Observe | Preserve baseline, indexation, ranking, citation, traffic, conversion, and collection as separate dated events | Import-first measurement spine |

Bundling implementation into the $750 offer would silently add intake, query/intent work, source review, an answer page, technical specifications, channel adaptation, scoring, and measurement design to a 48-hour strategy product. Leading with it as a new entry offer would create a proof objection before this system has one paid case or market outcome.

## Existing Sprint Mapping

| Canonical Sprint output | Search implementation expression |
|---|---|
| Buyer-decision map | Buyer question, intent, occasion, and conversion role |
| Positioning and proof hierarchy | Entity truth plus claim/source/limit/reviewer structure |
| Campaign-angle bank | One primary answer opportunity plus supporting channel opportunities |
| Launch messaging blueprint | Answer architecture, technical specification, and distribution handoff |
| One priority campaign asset | One finished source-backed answer page or article |
| Internal quality control | SearchBrief, ContentScoreReceipts, and dated measurement hypothesis |

The project pack, ledgers, scores, and receipts are delivery infrastructure. They are not nine bonus documents to pad the buyer-facing scope.

## Private Working Label

**Angle-to-Answer Prototype** — `UNTESTED`

Working promise:

> Turn one approved angle and its evidence into a useful, source-backed answer asset designed for human trust and clearer search-system inspection, with technical specifications and a dated measurement plan.

The public headline should not lead with AEO, GEO, AI optimization, or guaranteed organic traffic.

## Qualification Gates

Recommend the track only when all are true:

1. One Angle Map direction is accepted.
2. That direction corresponds to a real buyer question and dated commercial occasion.
3. A live or planned owned publishing surface exists.
4. Product truth, approved evidence, prohibited implications, and a qualified reviewer are available.
5. The CTA destination and conversion event are explicit.
6. The client can preserve a baseline and share dated observations after publication.

## Disqualifiers

- The message decision is still unresolved.
- No owned publishing surface exists.
- “AEO is hot” is the only urgency.
- The buyer wants bulk articles, backlink outreach, a site rebuild, full technical SEO, or an ongoing calendar.
- The brand cannot supply product truth, claim boundaries, or a qualified reviewer.
- The buyer expects guaranteed rankings, citations, traffic, leads, or revenue.
- The team cannot publish the asset or preserve a baseline.
- The request depends on disease claims or invented evidence.

## Proof Gaps

- `UNTESTED`: exact-offer willingness to pay for The Angle Map and this follow-on.
- `NO EVENT`: no approved client Angle Map has entered the track.
- `NO EVENT`: no prototype asset has been published or indexed.
- `NO EVENT`: no ranking, citation, traffic, qualified lead, conversion, or collection has been observed.
- `UNCONFIRMED`: delivery time, revision burden, reviewer latency, margin, and repeatability on a real client.
- `UNCONFIRMED`: which target query has enough buyer value and reachable demand; current candidates are hypotheses pending live inputs.

## Decision Lock

**LOCKED:** The Angle Map stays the only cold paid rung. Search is a private priority-asset track inside the current Sprint.  
**PARKED:** Standalone SEO/AEO offer, recurring content service, new price, public offer copy, outreach, publishing, live implementation, and paid distribution.
