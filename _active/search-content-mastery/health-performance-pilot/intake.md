# Intake — Source-to-Search Snapshot V2

## Client/context

Internal Health Performance prototype for an already-selling supplement, recovery, functional-nutrition, or performance brand whose useful proof is scattered across product pages, source links, founder judgment, and buyer questions.

No real client brand or live URL was supplied. This is an acceptance fixture, not a diagnosis of an unnamed company's website.

## Job to be done

Show how one commercially useful buyer question can become a source-backed answer asset that:

- makes the claim, source, limit, reviewer, and update state inspectable;
- gives a human buyer a useful answer;
- is structurally ready for traditional and AI-search inspection;
- carries a falsifiable measurement plan;
- does not promise retrieval, ranking, citation, traffic, or sales.

## Locked scope

- One portable project pack
- One baseline and opportunity decision
- One improved answer asset
- One visual/media brief
- One explainable content evaluation
- One experiment plan
- One bounded service receipt

## Source and claim boundaries

- Use primary/official guidance and approved first-party brand evidence.
- Health, product, performance, treatment, safety, or regulatory implications require the client's qualified reviewer.
- Farrice reads for signal, not law; the client's attorney/compliance/medical owner retains the pen.
- No invented testimonials, results, claims, citations, or market data.

## Commercial boundary

The Angle Map remains the live $750 prepaid offer. `Source-to-Search Snapshot V2` is a local prototype with `UNTESTED` buyer demand and no permission for outreach, pricing changes, payment setup, or public activation.
