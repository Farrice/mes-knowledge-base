## Brand/offer/audience/voice

- Plain-spoken, concrete, evidence-aware.
- Lead with a useful buyer answer, not “AI consulting” or backend system language.

- The proof boundary stays in the asset, not buried in a disclaimer appendix.

## Source policy

Allowed:

- official guidance;
- approved product labels, studies, technical files, and brand records;
- dated first-party observations;
- source-referenced buyer language and subject-matter interviews.

Rejected or review-required:

- invented or competitor-derived health claims;
- causal ranking/citation claims;
- treatment, efficacy, safety, regulatory, or legal conclusions without qualified review;
- modeled AI demand represented as first-party prompt volume.

## Operator taste decisions

- No false certainty.
- Show the receipt and the limitation together.
- Preserve the original score if taste overrides it.
