# Media Brief — The Five-Field Claim Receipt

## Purpose

Turn the improved answer asset into a seven-frame educational carousel or static visual system that makes claim inspection obvious without implying medical approval or search results.

No media is generated in V1. This is a local production brief; paid generation remains cost-gated and approval-bound.

## Audience and moment

Health-performance founder or marketing lead reviewing a claim before the page, ad, or post enters production.

## Core visual idea

One claim card is physically incomplete until five labeled evidence tabs lock into place:

1. Claim
2. Source
3. Limit
4. Reviewer
5. Updated

Avoid arrows from “ready” to “ranked” or “cited.” A footer rail shows that readiness and outcomes are different records.

## Frame sequence

1. **Hook:** “A source link is not a claim receipt.”
2. **The failure:** claim and source separated across tabs, decks, or memories.
3. **The object:** the five-field Claim Receipt.
4. **Worked example:** composition fact versus unsupported outcome implication.
5. **The human gate:** qualified reviewer retains the pen.
6. **The search boundary:** inspectable does not mean ranked or cited.
7. **Action:** “Review one claim against the receipt before publication.”

## Art direction

- Editorial evidence-card system, not futuristic AI imagery.
- Graphite background, warm white claim card, amber for limitations, blue for sourced/dated fields.
- Real document texture: label fragment, source line, reviewer stamp, date field.
- No medical imagery, pills-as-decoration, robot brains, glowing search bars, or fake dashboard metrics.

## Source and claim boundary

Use only the example and sources in `improved-answer-asset.md`. This brief cannot create new product facts, health outcomes, endorsements, rankings, or citation claims.

## Production route

Approved brief → `/create` → matching carousel/visual craft owner → claim/source review → Search Content Mastery score. External publishing remains `NO PERMISSION`.
