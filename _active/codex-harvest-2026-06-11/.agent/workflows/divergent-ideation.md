# `/divergent-ideation` — Three-Frame Content Sprint

Apply David Placek's Three-Brief Divergent Architecture to content ideation. Instead of brainstorming one angle, run three deliberately different creative frames in parallel (Direct, Transplant, Inversion), then cross-pollinate and converge on the strongest concepts.

## When to Use
- You have a topic but need fresh angles (not the obvious take)
- You're stuck in single-angle thinking on a subject you've covered before
- You want 15+ concepts to choose from instead of 3-5
- You want concepts that feed into `/ip-flywheel`, `/yt-flywheel`, or `/serial-arc`
- You want structural creativity, not brainstorming-style randomness

### 2. Gather Input
Ask the user:
- Topic or theme
- Platform(s) and format(s)
- Audience
- What they've already said on this topic

### 3. Execute
Follow the `divergent-content-sprint` prompt — all 6 steps:
1. Intake
2. Build three frames (Direct, Transplant from unrelated domain, Inversion)
3. Generate 5 concepts per frame (15 total) — NO evaluation during generation
4. Cross-pollinate (frame collisions, transplant imports, inversion tension)
5. Converge on top 5 with scores
6. Present with opening lines, sonic scores, downstream workflow routing

### 4. Quality Gate
- [ ] Three genuinely distinct frames (not slight variations)
- [ ] 15 concepts generated before any evaluation
- [ ] At least one frame collision identified
- [ ] Top 5 concepts draw from 2+ frames
- [ ] Each finalist tagged with downstream workflow
