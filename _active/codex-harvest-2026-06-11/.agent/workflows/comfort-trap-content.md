# `/comfort-trap-content` — Content Differentiation Audit

Apply David Placek's Comfort Trap framework — originally used to diagnose invisible brand names — to diagnose invisible content. Score any draft on 8 dimensions and prescribe specific rewrites that move it from safe (forgettable) to tension zone (memorable).

## When to Use
- A draft feels "fine" but not exciting — classic invisible zone signal
- You want to sharpen a post, email, article, or script before publishing
- Content is getting low engagement despite being well-written
- You're reviewing someone else's content and need a diagnostic framework
- Stack with `/authenticity-audit` for resonance + differentiation compound audit
