# Codex Antigravity Product Context

register: product

## Product Purpose

Codex Antigravity is an internal AI operating system for turning raw context into durable workflows, creative direction, client assets, design systems, research, extraction engines, and proof loops. It exists to reduce the user's routing burden: the system should infer the right operating mode, choose the right command surface, verify its work, and leave reusable infrastructure behind.

## Primary User

Farrice uses this workspace as a build environment for leverage that compounds. The system should help him move from messy thoughts, sources, strategy, and creative ideas into executable assets without forcing him to remember command names or babysit every handoff.

## Product Posture

- Operating-system first: route raw input into the right command, skill, workflow, or quality gate.
- Local-first evidence: prefer files, traces, reports, and deterministic checks that remain useful even when external systems fail.
- Creative taste as infrastructure: codify good judgment into briefs, DESIGN.md files, reusable skills, and QA gates instead of treating taste as a late-stage opinion.
- Execution after alignment: once a direction is approved, implement, verify, and close with the next useful move.
- Honest capability boundaries: name what was verified, what failed, and what still needs human judgment.

## Brand And Tone

- Clear, senior, and grounded.
- Useful over performative.
- Specific enough to execute today.
- Comfortable mixing strategic judgment with practical implementation.
- Warm and direct, with no hype cloak around weak evidence.

## Design Principles

- Dense but readable operating surfaces.
- Tooling should feel capable, calm, and decision-oriented.
- Design must reveal hierarchy, state, and workflow flow quickly.
- Brand, product, and creative work should feel chosen, not defaulted.
- Visual systems should prevent generic AI output before generation starts.

## Anti-References

- Purple-to-blue gradients as a default aesthetic.
- Glassmorphism by default.
- Nested cards and card grids as a substitute for hierarchy.
- Inter-only typography with no point of view.
- Hero-metric templates and oversized SaaS number blocks.
- Decorative motion that does not clarify state or action.
- Generic rounded icon tiles.
- Low-contrast gray text used to fake sophistication.
- Repeating white-gray-white page sections.
- Stock-feeling imagery that does not carry the actual product, place, state, or point of view.

## Success Criteria

The system is working when a user can drop messy context and receive a routed, taste-aware, evidence-backed result that is either ready to use or clearly sequenced into the next build, hardening pass, or expansion step.
