# Creative Brief: Living Operator System

## Core Idea

You are no longer building a library.

You are building a **creative operating system** where your curated experts, workflows, taste, tools, research, and judgment become callable operators.

The old system helped you ask:

> What knowledge do I have?

The new system helps you ask:

> Given this goal, what should move, in what order, with which agents, tools, quality gates, and verification?

That is the leverage shift.

## Strategic Role

This system exists to make you faster without making you shallower.

Its purpose is to let you move with:

- speed
- taste
- confidence
- depth when needed
- anti-slop discipline
- cross-domain synthesis
- tool-backed execution
- self-improvement over time

The operator suite should become your default way of working whenever the task matters.

## Highest-Leverage Use Cases

### 1. Moment-of-Work Routing

Use when you feel scattered, unsure, or overloaded.

```text
/orchestrate what should I do next to move this forward?
```

Best for:

- deciding what to work on
- choosing the right workflow
- sequencing messy projects
- turning vague ambition into command order
- avoiding "I know I have something for this, but I don't know what"

This is the habit that removes you as the routing bottleneck.

### 2. Creative Concept Development

Use the Ideation Agent when you need original angles, campaign concepts, frameworks, or non-obvious synthesis.

```text
/ideation-agent give me 10 remarkable campaign territories for this offer
```

Best for:

- brand ideas
- content angles
- product concepts
- proprietary frameworks
- naming directions
- campaign hooks
- cross-domain connections

Then run:

```text
/excellence-gate
/red-team-agent
```

This keeps ideas from being merely clever.

### 3. Content and Media Production

Use Content & Media when you want the system to turn insight into market-facing output.

```text
/content-media-agent turn this idea into a LinkedIn post, newsletter angle, and short-form script
```

Best for:

- daily authority content
- newsletters
- LinkedIn posts
- scripts
- atomized content
- proof-first content
- launch media

This is how the internal knowledge base becomes external demand.

### 4. Offer and Revenue Creation

Use Revenue & Offer when a capability needs to become money.

```text
/revenue-offer-agent turn this capability into a paid audit and productized service
```

Best for:

- first clients
- paid audits
- AI service packaging
- pricing ladders
- proposal angles
- productized offers
- $0-to-$1m path decisions

Pair with:

```text
/copywriting-agent
/proof-case-study-agent
/red-team-agent
```

This prevents "cool system, unclear offer."

### 5. Client Delivery

Use Client Delivery when you need to fulfill professionally and quickly.

```text
/client-delivery-agent turn this client context into an audit, SOP plan, and implementation roadmap
```

Best for:

- client audits
- strategy briefs
- SOPs
- AI adoption plans
- playbooks
- delivery reports
- implementation maps

This is where the system becomes a solo-operator leverage machine.

### 6. Copy, Messaging, and Positioning

Use these when language needs to sell, clarify, or carry meaning.

```text
/messaging-positioning-agent clarify the market position and core message
/copywriting-agent write the landing page and email sequence
```

Best for:

- landing pages
- sales pages
- VSLs
- emails
- positioning
- offers
- core messages
- belief-shift copy

Always pair high-stakes copy with:

```text
/excellence-gate
/red-team-agent
```

### 7. Research and Ground Truth

Use Research Intelligence when facts matter.

```text
/research-intelligence-agent research this market and verify the claims
```

Use Ground Truth when quality claims matter.

```text
/ground-truth-agent compare this against expert-level benchmarks
```

Best for:

- market research
- competitor analysis
- factual claims
- client-facing recommendations
- benchmark calibration
- "is this actually world-class?" checks

This protects you from polished false confidence.

### 8. Red Team and Anti-Slop Defense

Use Red Team when the work matters enough to be challenged.

```text
/red-team-agent stress-test this before I ship it
```

Use Excellence Gate as the universal floor.

```text
/excellence-gate review this for taste, originality, depth, proof, and AI slop
```

Best for:

- publishable content
- client deliverables
- offers
- landing pages
- research
- system changes
- anything you feel tempted to trust too quickly

This is the immune system.

### 9. Extraction With Deployment Intent

Keep extracting, but route every source through the Extraction Governor.

```text
/extraction-governor-agent what should this source become in my system?
```

Best for deciding:

- light extract
- full forge
- companion skill
- workflow only
- reference only
- agent capability
- productized service input

This turns extraction from collection into capability manufacturing.

### 10. Self-Improvement and System Evolution

Use Evolution Agent when something repeats, plateaus, or feels weaker than it should.

```text
/evolution-agent this workflow keeps producing good-but-not-great output. Diagnose and queue improvement.
```

Best for:

- recurring quality gaps
- weak routing
- stale workflows
- benchmark misses
- prompt/workflow evolution
- supervised self-healing

This is how the system compounds.

## Daily Muscle Memory

Start serious work with:

```text
/knowledge-librarian [focus]
/orchestrate [goal]
```

Then execute the recommended path.

Before shipping anything important:

```text
/excellence-gate
/red-team-agent
```

When facts matter:

```text
/research-intelligence-agent
/ground-truth-agent
```

When a weakness repeats:

```text
/evolution-agent
```

## Weekly Rhythm

Use this once per week:

```text
/knowledge-librarian revenue
/orchestrate what are the highest leverage moves this week?
```

Then pick one lane:

- revenue
- content authority
- client delivery
- offer building
- extraction expansion
- system improvement

Do not try to use every agent every day. Use the system to choose.

## Edge-Pushing Prompts

Use these when you want to stretch the system:

```text
/orchestrate --council what is the highest leverage move I am not seeing?
```

```text
/ideation-agent give me the version of this idea that feels too bold but might be right
```

```text
/red-team-agent attack this as if my reputation depends on finding the weak point
```

```text
/ground-truth-agent tell me whether this is actually expert-level or just polished
```

```text
/evolution-agent what keeps this system from compounding faster?
```

## The Rule

The system is best used as a loop:

```text
Surface -> Orchestrate -> Execute -> Judge -> Red Team -> Prove -> Evolve
```

Your job is not to remember every asset anymore.

Your job is to bring the system real goals, real constraints, real taste, and real pressure.

The muscle memory is simple:

**When you feel uncertainty, route.  
When you create, gate.  
When stakes rise, red team.  
When claims appear, verify.  
When patterns repeat, evolve.**
