## Executive Answer

No official Google source confirms that exporting data from Google Drive impacts Google Business Profile (GBP) rankings. The query appears to reference unverified speculation or misinformation, as search results lack any mention of "Drive export" or Google Drive in GBP ranking contexts.

### Verified Evidence
Google officially states that GBP (formerly Google My Business) local rankings are primarily determined by **three factors**: **relevance** (how well the business matches the search query), **distance** (proximity to the searcher), and **prominence** (how well-known the business is online, based on reviews, links, and web mentions).[1][2][3]
- **Relevance** is assessed via profile fields like categories, services, description, business name, and offerings.[2]
- **Prominence** draws from web-wide signals like reviews, citations, backlinks, and website strength.[1][2]
- Supporting signals include profile completeness, NAP (name, address, phone) consistency across directories, user interactions (e.g., calls, directions), and verification status.[1][3]
Official Google tips for improving rankings emphasize verifying the business, adding full address/phone/categories, and completing the profile—no reference to Google Drive or exports.[3]
GBP insights provide performance data on searches, views, and actions, but are based on public location data like reviews and trends, not Drive activity.[5]

### Directional Signals
Sources indicate Google prioritizes **accuracy, engagement, and trust signals** beyond the core three factors, such as:
- Positive reviews and sentiment.[1][2]
- Profile field optimization (e.g., 11 fields like address visibility confirmed to impact rankings via tests).[4]
- Citation consistency and website alignment with GBP data.[1][2]
Hiding address can cause ranking drops, per 2023 tests.[4]
Tracking tools require GBP CID (unique identifier) for accurate local rank monitoring.[6]

### Inference
GBP rankings rely entirely on public, location-based, and behavioral signals tied to search/Maps usage; private tools like Google Drive exports (e.g., sharing spreadsheets) would not influence them, as no algorithm integration is documented.[1][2][3] If "Drive export smoke" implies testing/exporting data for manipulation (e.g., fake reviews), it aligns with unconfirmed "other factors" surmised by experts but risks violating Google's spam policies.[2]

### Unverified Assumptions
- "Drive export" directly affects rankings: No evidence; absent from all sources, including official Google docs.[1][2][3]
- GBP rankings involve non-public tools like Drive: Contradicted by emphasis on web/public signals only.[1][2]
No data/pricing/market/competitor/social claims appear in results, so none verified; official GBP setup is free.[8]
