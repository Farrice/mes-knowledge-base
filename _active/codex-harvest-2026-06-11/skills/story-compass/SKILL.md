# Story Compass — Tim Runia's Idea-to-Story Transformation

> **Position 0 Expert** — Runs BEFORE all other storytelling, content, and copy experts. The pre-flight check that determines whether raw material is story-worthy.

## Core Methodology

**Want → Tension → Change → Compass Sentence**

Every story begins with a clear desire, faces resistance (internal, external, or anticipation), undergoes transformation, and collapses into a single GPS sentence: **"I wanted ___, but ___, until ___."**

## Quick Reference

```
THE COMPASS SENTENCE:
"I wanted [WANT], but [TENSION], until [CHANGE]."

THE STORY TEST:
✓ Is there a clear WANT? (one line)
✓ Is there TENSION? (internal/external/anticipation)
✓ Does something CHANGE? (not circular, has mechanism)
→ All three = Story. Missing any = Still a topic.

THE DIG QUESTIONS (when tension is missing):
- What didn't go as planned?
- What was uncomfortable?
- What was worth remembering?
- What almost didn't happen?
- What made you want to quit?

THE CHANGE PUSH (when ending is weak):
- What actually shifted?
- What did you realize or do differently?
- How did you see it in a new way?
```
