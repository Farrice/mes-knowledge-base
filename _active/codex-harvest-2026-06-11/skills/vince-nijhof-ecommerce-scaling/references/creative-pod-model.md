# Creative Pod Model

Use this for creative team structure and output design.

## Pod Structure

Base pod:

- 1 creative strategist or strategist lead
- 2 video editors
- Shared graphic design support
- Creative coordinator for creator/admin work
- Shared B-roll and asset bank
- Performance dashboard or leaderboard

## Role Boundaries

| Role | Owns | Should Avoid |
|------|------|--------------|
| Strategist | Research, ideation, emotional angle, concept brief, final intent | Creator negotiation, admin chasing, vague briefs |
| Video Editor | Making the concept alive through footage, pacing, B-roll, proof, captions | Guessing strategic intent |
| Creative Coordinator | Creator sourcing, creator communication, content chasing, asset intake | Strategy decisions |
| Lead | Output standards, pod comparison, training, AI workflow adoption | Micromanaging every headline |

## Output Targets

Use as starting points, not universal law:

- 60 final concepts/month per strategist is a strong baseline.
- 3-5 minute VSLs reduce raw count because every B-roll moment matters.
- If producing shorter videos and statics, volume targets can rise.
- Quality and intent set the floor; volume is added after the floor is stable.

## Incentives

Reward assets that spend profitably:

- Flat editor pay for reliable output.
- Bonus when an ad crosses a defined spend threshold at target economics.
- Public performance visibility so the team learns from winners.

## Quality Standard

A concept brief must make execution obvious:

- Buyer identity
- Emotion to trigger
- Awareness stage
- Source evidence
- Hook
- Story arc
- Required proof
- B-roll list
- CTA and funnel destination
- What must not be lost in the edit
