# Data Bank Sources

Use this when a workflow requires customer or market language.

## Source Priority

1. Long customer reviews: 150+ characters first.
2. Support tickets: complaints, confusion, repeated questions, refund reasons.
3. Trustpilot and review platforms: exact words around trust, hesitation, delight.
4. Amazon reviews: desired outcomes, failed alternatives, feature language.
5. Competitor reviews: gaps, unmet expectations, comparison language.
6. Social comments: raw objections, jokes, identity markers, recurring pain.
7. Email replies: high-intent buyer phrasing.
8. Winning ads: hooks, claims, pacing, proof, comments, share triggers.
9. Competitor funnels: page structure, proof order, mechanism explanations.

## Extraction Fields

For every useful item, extract:

| Field | What To Capture |
|-------|-----------------|
| Buyer Identity | Who is speaking? Role, life stage, self-image, context |
| Trigger Moment | What situation made the product relevant now? |
| Pain | What is frustrating, costly, embarrassing, or repeated? |
| Failed Attempts | What did they already try? |
| Desired Outcome | What better life scenario do they want? |
| Product Moment | What did the product do that mattered? |
| Objection | What would stop someone from buying? |
| Proof Language | What makes the claim feel believable? |
| Emotion | Fear, relief, confidence, convenience, control, identity, status |
| Angle Seed | The ad/funnel idea hiding inside the language |

## Long-Review Filter

Ignore shallow praise first:

- “Great product”
- “Love it”
- “Works well”

Mine specific stories:

- Who they are
- What their day looks like
- What failed before
- What changed after use
- What words they use without marketer polish

## Output Standard

Every extracted angle must cite at least one source item. If no source item supports it, mark it as a hypothesis.
