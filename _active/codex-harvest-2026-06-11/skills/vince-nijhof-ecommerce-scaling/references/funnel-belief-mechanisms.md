# Funnel Belief Mechanisms

Use this when building VSLs, pre-landers, listicles, authority pages, and product-page flows.

## Core Flow

1. **Top-of-funnel ad**: Trigger emotion and identity recognition before the buyer feels sold to.
2. **Pre-lander/listicle**: Convert attention into belief.
3. **Product page**: Present offer, options, proof, and checkout path.
4. **Upsells/bundles**: Increase AOV with complementary products.
5. **Email/retention**: Capture repeat purchase and improve blended economics.

## Belief Shift Sequence

| Stage | Buyer Thought | Page Job |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| Recognition | “That is me.” | Mirror the real pain and context |
| Failed Attempts | “I have tried things like this.” | Explain why old approaches failed |
| Mechanism | “This makes sense.” | Show the product reason visually and simply |
| Proof | “People like me got this result.” | Use reviews, authority, demos, stats where true |
| Objection | “But what about...” | Answer risk, comfort, cost, use, trust, delivery |
| Offer | “This is worth trying now.” | Bundle, guarantee, discount, urgency, clear CTA |

## Pre-Lander Types

- Tutorial page
- Listicle
- Third-party authority page
- Comparison page
- Problem explainer
- Founder/authority recommendation
- Visual mechanism explainer

## Guardrails

- Do not fake doctors, experts, publications, or authority.
- Do not make claims the product cannot support.
- Keep main brand trustable; direct response can be strong without being dirty.
