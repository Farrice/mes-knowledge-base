# New Brand Validation Sprint

> **Expert**: Vince Nijhof | **Tier**: Practitioner
> **Produces**: Validation sprint, budget, test sequence, kill/continue criteria

## Inputs Required

1. Brand/product idea
2. Target market
3. Budget range
4. Product economics
5. Existing research or competitors

## Skill Acquisition

Load:

- `genius.md`

## Execution

1. **Reverse-engineer the market**: Outcome first, product second.
2. **Build pre-launch data bank**: Competitors, reviews, Amazon, TikTok, comments, search.
3. **Pass economics gate**: Paid-scale score, AOV path, repeat/LTV path.
4. **Build first creative batch**: High-intent top/middle/bottom concepts.
5. **Build first funnel**: Pre-lander or product page based on education need.
6. **Set budget and pacing**: 5K/10K/20K/50K+ bands depending on operator confidence and cash.
7. **Define kill/continue signals**: Early proof, messaging traction, funnel metrics, buyer feedback.

## Quality Gate

- Do not validate a product with one ad and three creators.
- Do not recommend heavy spend if economics are unknown.
- Make the operator confidence level explicit.
