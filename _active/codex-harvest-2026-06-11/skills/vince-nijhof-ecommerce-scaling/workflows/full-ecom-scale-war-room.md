# Full Ecom Scale War Room

> **Expert**: Vince Nijhof | **Tier**: Stacking
> **Produces**: Complete scale diagnosis and 30-day execution plan

## Inputs Required

1. Product/category and offer
2. Revenue, spend, ROAS/CPA, AOV, margin, LTV
3. Current ads, funnels, reviews, support, competitors
4. Team structure
5. Inventory, payout, supplier, and fulfillment details
6. Growth target

## Execution

1. **Product gate**: Can this category carry paid scale?
2. **Economics gate**: Is scale blocked by margin, AOV, LTV, OPEX, or cash timing?
3. **Data-bank gate**: Is creative built from real buyer language?
4. **Creative gate**: Are concepts high-intent and mapped to awareness stages?
5. **Funnel gate**: Does the pre-lander/product page shift belief?
6. **Team gate**: Is the pod system built for speed and learning?
7. **Channel gate**: Does blended performance reveal hidden spillover or stock issues?
8. **Action plan**: Sequence the few changes that unlock the next stage.
