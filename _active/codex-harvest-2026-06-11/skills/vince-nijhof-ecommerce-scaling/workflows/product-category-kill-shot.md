# Product Category Kill Shot

> **Expert**: Vince Nijhof | **Skill**: vince-nijhof-ecommerce-scaling | **Tier**: Foundation
> **Produces**: Product/category scorecard, paid-scale verdict, and next validation steps

## Inputs Required

1. Product or category idea
2. Target market/geography
3. Estimated price, COGS, shipping profile, and margin
4. Competitors or comparable products
5. Repeat purchase, subscription, bundle, or accessory potential

## Execution

1. **Define the outcome**: State the buyer’s desired better scenario. If the product cannot be framed as an outcome, flag it.
2. **Score TAM**: Identify market size, search/social demand, competitor spend signals, and whether the audience is broad enough for paid acquisition.
3. **Score paid explainability**: Can the product be understood in a scroll, a VSL, and a pre-lander?
4. **Score unit economics**: AOV, gross margin, shipping weight, return risk, fulfillment complexity, and break-even ROAS.
5. **Score AOV/LTV path**: Bundles, accessories, subscriptions, replenishment, repeat purchase, and email revenue.
6. **Score defensibility**: Patent, hard sourcing, brand world, community, authority, superior product, or operational edge.
7. **Produce verdict**: Kill, constrained test, fix-before-test, or aggressive validation.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---------|------------|
| New DTC product | Focus on paid explainability, margin, shipping, validation budget |
| Product extension | Focus on customer overlap, bundle lift, repeat purchase |
| Acquisition target | Focus on hidden paid-scale ceiling and operational debt |
| Info/digital product | Replace shipping with fulfillment cost, trust, proof, and refund risk |

## Quality Gate

- No product passes without TAM and unit economics.
- No category passes on aesthetics alone.
- Every positive verdict must name the scale bottleneck.
- Every negative verdict must state the exact failure point.
