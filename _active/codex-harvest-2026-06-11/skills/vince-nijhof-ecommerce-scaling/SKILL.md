> **Agent**: vince-nijhof | **Domain**: E-Commerce Paid Acquisition + Creative Strategy + Funnel Economics
> **Source**: Elevator Talks interview transcript
> **Genius Patterns**: [`genius.md`](genius.md)

## Skill Overview

This skill operationalizes Vince Nijhof's e-commerce scaling methodology: pick products that can support paid traffic, mine real buyer language, build high-intent creative, move cold traffic through belief-building funnels, and protect scale with contribution-margin and cash-flow discipline.

**Core Thesis**: Scale comes from the full system, not the ad account alone. Product economics, customer language, creative intent, funnel belief, AOV, LTV, cash timing, and team structure all decide whether paid acquisition can grow.

## Routing Guide

| Situation | Workflow |
|-----------|----------|
| Need to judge a product/category before launch | `product-category-kill-shot` |
| Ads feel generic or guessed | `messaging-market-fit-miner` |
| ROAS looks okay but profit/cash feels wrong | `paid-scale-economics-audit` |
| Team is producing volume without winners | `intent-first-creative-strategy` |
| Need long top-of-funnel storytelling ads | `top-of-funnel-vsl-engine` |
| Cold traffic needs education before the product page | `belief-prelander-builder` |
| Creative team is slow, muddy, or disconnected from performance | `creative-pod-operating-system` |
| Need more AOV room for paid acquisition | `upsell-aov-expansion` |
| Starting a new brand or product from zero | `new-brand-validation-sprint` |
| Need AI inside creative ops without losing human judgment | `ai-creative-ops-blueprint` |
| Brand and direct response are fighting each other | `brand-performance-bridge` |
| Paid spend affects Amazon/Google/email/blended results | `omnichannel-scale-diagnosis` |
| Need a full e-commerce scaling diagnosis | `full-ecom-scale-war-room` |
