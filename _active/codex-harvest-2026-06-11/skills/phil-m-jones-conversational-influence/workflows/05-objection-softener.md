# Objection Softener

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this when someone pushes back, delays, cites price, asks for time, says they need another person, or gives a vague objection.

## Skill Acquisition

Load:
- `genius.md` - Soft Certainty Challenge, Conditional Commitment Close, Curiosity Recovery
- `references/phrase-library.md` - sequence families 17, 18, 19, 23

## Inputs

- Exact objection or paraphrase
- Context before the objection
- Desired outcome
- What can and cannot be changed

## Execution

1. **Separate surface from source**: identify whether the objection is real, polite delay, fear, missing information, or authority gap.
2. **Ask the diagnostic question**: use curiosity before counterargument.
3. **Test the condition**: if a barrier is removable, ask whether solving it changes the decision.
4. **Recover the maybe**: if they are near no, invite one more look before the final decision.
5. **Land on next action**: schedule, send, compare, decide, or close the loop.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Channel | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Live call | Ask one diagnostic question, then pause. |
| Email | Acknowledge, ask one question, suggest one next step. |
| DM | Keep the objection response under 45 words. |
| Proposal follow-up | Pair condition test with a clear decision date. |
| Client service | Preserve trust before solving. |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- Objection diagnosis
- Primary response
- Two softer variants
- Conditional close test
- Next-step recovery line

## Quality Gate

Reject any response that debates the objection before understanding it.
