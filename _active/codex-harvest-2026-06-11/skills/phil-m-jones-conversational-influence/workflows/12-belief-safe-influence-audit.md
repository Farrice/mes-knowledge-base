# Belief-Safe Influence Audit

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this before sending high-stakes sales, leadership, client, or belief-changing communication.

## Skill Acquisition

Load:
- `genius.md` - Ethical Gate and Anti-Patterns
- `references/quality-rubric.md`
- Optional stack: `skills/david-mcraney-belief-change/genius.md`

## Inputs

- Script, copy, or conversation plan
- Audience
- Desired action
- Relationship stakes
- Claims, urgency, social proof, and constraints

## Execution

1. **Find pressure points**: mark language that corners, rushes, shames, or overclaims.
2. **Check autonomy**: ensure the listener can say no, ask questions, or choose another path.
3. **Check truth**: verify social proof, urgency, and norms are accurate.
4. **Replace brittle persuasion**: rewrite pressure into clarity, curiosity, and choice.
5. **Score the result**: apply the quality rubric.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Channel | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Sales script | Check for overclosing and objection debate. |
| Landing page | Check urgency, proof, and CTA pressure. |
| Leadership | Check status threats and blame. |
| Client email | Check trust and boundary clarity. |
| Content | Check bait, shame, and false certainty. |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- Risk audit
- Line-level rewrite recommendations
- Revised version
- Autonomy safeguards
- Final score out of 10

## Quality Gate

Reject any output that increases conversion by reducing informed choice.
