# Lucas Alpay Storytelling

> "Reader engagement is neurological architecture, not just craft."
Lucas Alpay's storytelling methodology treats narrative as brain science. His 7-Element Formula creates approach-avoidance conflicts that make readers physically unable to stop reading. This skill spans fiction writing, business copywriting, and full book creation.

## Kishotenketsu Contrast Trigger

Use the Kishotenketsu workflow when the user asks for contrast-driven storytelling, process as plot, perspective shift, integration over victory, variable over villain, non-conflict story structure, texture-driven writing, world-building, ghostwriting texture, brand world invitation, ads/media/story-led campaign work, messaging, positioning, product/service/concept narrative, client-facing narrative service packaging, or a way to fix writing that feels like it is forcing fake conflict.
