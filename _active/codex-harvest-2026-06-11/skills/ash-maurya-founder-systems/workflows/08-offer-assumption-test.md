# Offer Assumption Test

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use when a founder has problem evidence and needs to test whether the offer is compelling enough to earn commitment.

Load:
- `skills/ash-maurya-founder-systems/genius.md`
- `skills/ash-maurya-founder-systems/references/hidden-knowledge.md`

## Execution

1. **Restate The Problem**
   - Use customer language.
   - Name the current alternative and why it is breaking.
   - Anchor the problem in a past behavior, not a future desire.
   - Identify the old-way pet peeve, struggle, or workaround the offer attacks.

2. **Map Forces**
   - Push: why change now?
   - Pull: what promised outcome attracts them?
   - Inertia: what keeps them in the old way?
   - Friction: what fears block action?

3. **Build The Mafia Offer**
   - Promise: specific outcome.
   - Demo: how the outcome is achieved.
   - Price: anchored to current cost.
   - Timeline: when value appears.
   - Risk reversal: reduce biggest fear.
   - Commitment ask: define the resource.
   - Buying criteria: state why this offer wins against the old way and rejected alternatives.

4. **Test The Offer**
   - Pitch only to qualified prospects with problem evidence.
   - Start by replaying what they said happened last time.
   - Show how the offer solves the buying criteria they revealed.
   - Measure questions, objections, pricing reaction, next-step behavior, and commitment.

5. **Interpret Results**
   - Weak push: they do not care enough.
   - Weak pull: promise is unclear or not valuable.
   - High inertia: old way is good enough.
   - High friction: risk reversal or proof is missing.
   - Wrong criteria: the offer solves a feature wish, not what they actually chose or fired.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| SaaS | Ask for pilot deposit, data access, or signed LOI |
| Service | Ask for paid diagnostic or implementation slot |
| Digital product | Ask for preorder, cohort seat, or paid beta |
| Enterprise | Ask for sponsor, budget process, and pilot success criteria |

## Output Requirements

- Offer script
- Force map
- Buying criteria map
- Commitment ladder
- Objection interpretation
- Next revision

## Quality Gate

Pass only if there is a clear ask. Fail if the offer test ends with "I'll keep you posted."
