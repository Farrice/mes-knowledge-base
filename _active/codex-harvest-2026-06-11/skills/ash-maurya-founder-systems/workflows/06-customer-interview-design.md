# Customer Interview Design

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use when customer evidence is missing, noisy, or opinion-heavy. The interview must reconstruct real behavior and switching forces.

- `skills/ash-maurya-founder-systems/genius.md`
- `skills/ash-maurya-founder-systems/references/genius-patterns.md`

## Execution

1. **Define The Learning Goal**
   - What problem, segment, price, or alternative must be understood?
   - What decision will this evidence affect?

2. **Recruit The Right People**
   - Prioritize triggered prospects.
   - Avoid friends who are trying to be supportive.
   - Segment by recent event, current workaround, spend, or role.

3. **Write The Interview Spine**
   - Context: "I'm studying how people handle [situation]."
   - The one useful question: "Walk me through the last time you encountered this situation and tell me where you struggled."
   - Trigger: "What happened right before you started looking for a solution?"
   - Current alternative: "What did you use or do?"
   - Old-way use: "When, why, and how did you use the current solution?"
   - Search: "What else did you consider?"
   - Firing criteria: "Why did those options not work?"
   - Buying criteria: "What made you choose solution X over solution Y?"
   - Tradeoffs: "What did you accept even though it was imperfect?"
   - Cost: "What did this cost you?"
   - Next time: "What would make you change?"

4. **Interview Rules**
   - Do not pitch.
   - Do not ask "Would you use this?"
   - Do not ask "Would you pay for this?"
   - Do not ask customers to rank your problem list.
   - Do not ask what features they want.
   - Ask for stories, not opinions.
   - Keep the interview in the past tense.
   - Keep asking "what happened next?"
   - Capture exact phrases when pain is concrete.

5. **Score Truth Signals**
   - Strong signal: recent trigger, real cost, current workaround, fired alternative, paid spend, or urgent deadline.
   - Medium signal: repeated frustration, named stakeholder, search behavior, or clear tradeoff.
   - Weak signal: compliments, future intent, feature wishlist, or polite agreement.

6. **Synthesize**
   - Map push, pull, inertia, friction.
   - Identify hiring and firing criteria.
   - Identify pet peeves, struggles, and workarounds in the old way.
   - Audit each insight against customer facts, not founder interpretation.
   - Rank problems by intensity.
   - Mark weak evidence separately.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| B2B | Ask about budget, stakeholder, approval, and workflow delay |
| Consumer | Ask about event, search behavior, and tradeoffs |
| Service | Ask about prior vendors, failed attempts, and buying triggers |
| Existing audience | Segment by recent action, not follower demographics |

## Output Requirements

- Interview guide
- Recruit criteria
- Evidence capture table
- Truth-signal score
- Old-way pet peeve/struggle/workaround map
- Customer forces synthesis
- Next decision recommendation

## Quality Gate

Pass only if the guide would prevent the founder from pitching. Fail if questions ask "Would you buy this?"
