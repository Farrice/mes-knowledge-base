# Learning Velocity Scorecard

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use when a founder or team is busy but not making clearer decisions. The scorecard measures learning velocity, not activity volume.

Load:
- `skills/ash-maurya-founder-systems/genius.md`
- `skills/ash-maurya-founder-systems/references/quality-rubric.md`

## Execution

1. **Define Current Decision Gate**
   - Problem proof
   - Solution proof
   - Offer proof
   - Demand proof
   - Build/scale readiness

2. **Set Learning Metrics**
   - Interviews completed.
   - Behavioral evidence captured.
   - Assumptions killed or validated.
   - Commitments requested.
   - Commitments received.
   - Revenue or pilot value.
   - Time from question to decision.

3. **Separate Signal Types**
   - Opinion
   - Intent
   - Behavior
   - Commitment
   - Revenue

4. **Create Weekly Review**
   - What did we believe on Monday?
   - What evidence changed that belief?
   - What assumption remains riskiest?
   - What do we stop doing?
   - What is the next test?

5. **Set Decision Rules**
   - Proceed: threshold met.
   - Pivot: evidence contradicts segment/problem/price.
   - Narrow: one segment has disproportionate intensity.
   - Pause: access or evidence quality is too weak.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Solo founder | Keep to one weekly scorecard and one next test |
| Team | Assign owners to assumptions, not tasks |
| Client sprint | Report evidence ladder and recommendation |
| Existing product | Add conversion, retention, and sales objection metrics |

## Output Requirements

- Scorecard table
- Evidence ladder
- Weekly decision log
- Next experiment
- Stop-doing list

## Quality Gate

Pass only if activity is converted into decisions. Fail if the scorecard rewards motion without learning.
