# Workflow 02: Stage-Gate Innovation Map

Use this before launching a product, SKU, offer, feature, or command surface.

## Inputs

- Product or offer idea.
- Target customer and channel.
- Margin or economic target.
- Differentiation claim.
- Buyer, partner, or customer request if relevant.

## Gates

1. Strategic sense: why should this exist now?
2. Category improvement: what does it do better than the existing option?
3. Margin creation: can the economics support growth?
4. Channel fit: does the intended channel have the right buyer and execution logic?
5. Brand/objective fit: does it strengthen or dilute the core?

## Decision

Return one of:

- Go.
- Revise.
- Park.
- Kill.

Include the evidence, the weakest gate, and the next proof needed.
