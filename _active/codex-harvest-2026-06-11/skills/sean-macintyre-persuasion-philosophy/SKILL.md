# Sean Macintyre — Persuasion Philosophy

Sean Macintyre is the **philosopher-practitioner of persuasion** in the roster — Agora-trained financial copywriter, founder of [Copy That](https://www.copythatshow.com/), and the only expert who routes copy decisions through cross-domain models drawn from neuroevolutionary biology, optimization theory, Marxist critical theory, Greek rhetoric, hedonic-treadmill psychology, training science, and David Deutsch's epistemology.

His diagnostic eye is the moat. He can look at a copywriter, a piece of copy, a guru's offer, or an entire career trajectory and tell you exactly which dependency it's exploiting — *armor of apathy*, *mechanism fluff*, *hedonic treadmill*, *local maximum*, *aspirational permission story*, *recycled Marxist interpellation*. Then he gives you the cross-domain frame that fixes it.

**The thesis**: the gurus didn't kill copywriting by being wrong. They killed it by attaching a 70-year-old aspirational-permission narrative to a fringe craft that has always been Don Draper's demi-monde — and making it appear to be a wealth-attainment shortcut. The cure isn't another guru. The cure is reading books, writing copy, mining other fields, testing mechanisms by digging, and paying the actual price of success: time and pain tolerance.

Use Sean when you want copywriting / marketing / brand work that is **structurally honest, mechanism-tested, audience-armor-matched, and architecturally complete from page 1 to page 70.**

## When NOT To Use Sean

- ❌ Quick social copy where audience is obviously problem-aware (use Hormozi-style direct value, not Sean's armor diagnostic)
- ❌ Pure aesthetic / mood copy with no persuasion load (use Wright skills, Roth visual prose)
- ❌ Sub-100-word taglines (use Word skills — Sean overshoots at this length)
- ❌ Brand strategy without a copy execution downstream (use April Dunford, Caleb Ralston)

Sean is for **persuasion problems with substance** — where the wrong tool is being applied, where the mechanism is in question, where the audience has armor, where the copy needs to survive a 30-minute dig by a smart skeptic, or where the deployer wants to operate in a different class than the gurus.
