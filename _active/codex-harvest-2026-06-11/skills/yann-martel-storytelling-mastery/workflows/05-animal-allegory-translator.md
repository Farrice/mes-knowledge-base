# Animal Allegory Translator

## Pre-Flight Gate
Inputs required:
- Heavy topic or argument.
- Audience sensitivities.
- Desired emotional entry point.
- Any banned symbols or tone limits.

Load `../genius.md` and `../references/source-map.md`.

## Skill Acquisition
Use animals and masks to create distance, reduce defensive reaction, and carry projected human meaning.

## Execution
1. **Define the Hard Topic**: State what direct treatment makes too heavy, over-familiar, or defensive.
2. **Pick the Distance Mechanism**: Choose animal, object, mythic figure, footnote, report, letter, or other mask.
3. **Map Symbolic Traits**: Name what the figure carries and what it must not carry.
4. **Build the Allegory Route**: Translate the topic into characters, pressure, conflict, and ending.
5. **Check Respect and Clarity**: Remove symbols that trivialize pain or confuse the moral question.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Fiction | Build a full allegorical cast |
| Brand Education | Use a simple symbolic world to teach a hard concept |
| Children's Story | Keep surface light and underlying question clear |
| Essay | Use the allegory as opening frame, not the entire piece |
| Campaign | Create one memorable symbol that carries the message |

## Output Requirements
Produce:
- `Direct Topic`
- `Distance Need`
- `Symbol Options`
- `Chosen Allegory Route`
- `Scene / Beat Map`
- `Respect Check`

## Quality Gate
- The symbol is functional, not decorative.
- The heavy topic is easier to enter.
- Seriousness is preserved.
- The audience can decode the allegory without a lecture.
