# Scene Card Orchestration

## Pre-Flight Gate
Inputs required:
- Story premise or project goal.
- Existing scenes, notes, or outline.
- Known ending or desired final question.

Load `../genius.md`.

## Skill Acquisition
Use the scene as the working unit: time, action, place, pressure, and reader movement.

## Execution
1. **Extract Scene Candidates**: Identify all units that can become scenes.
2. **Define Each Scene Job**: For every card, name what changes: information, emotion, relationship, pressure, or question.
3. **Order for Movement**: Sequence cards so pressure changes, not just events.
4. **Add Surprise Windows**: Mark places where discovery can happen during drafting.
5. **Group Into Chapters or Sections**: Only after scenes are clear, assign containers.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Novel | Build scene cards and chapter bundles |
| Memoir | Sequence lived moments by changed understanding |
| Case Study | Treat customer moments as scenes, not proof blocks |
| Video Script | Score visual beats and emotional turns |
| Launch Story | Move from status quo to rupture to new belief |

## Output Requirements
Produce:
- `Scene Deck`
- `Scene Job Table`
- `Pressure Curve`
- `Chapter / Section Grouping`
- `Drafting Order`

## Quality Gate
- Every scene changes something.
- No scene exists only to display research.
- The order creates movement.
- The final scene connects to the ending pressure.
