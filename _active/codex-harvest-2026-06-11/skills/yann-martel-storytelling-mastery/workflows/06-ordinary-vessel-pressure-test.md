# Ordinary Vessel Pressure Test

## Pre-Flight Gate
Inputs required:
- Character description or role.
- Extraordinary event, pressure, or question.
- Reader identification goal.

Load `../genius.md`.

## Skill Acquisition
Use Martel's vessel principle: when the pressure is the point, the character should be open enough for the reader to enter.

## Execution
1. **Find Over-Characterization**: Identify traits that turn the character into a caricature or close reader entry.
2. **Name the Human Core**: Define the ordinary wants that make the character legible.
3. **Apply Extraordinary Pressure**: State what event or question breaks ordinary life open.
4. **Select Only Necessary Specifics**: Keep details that create trust, not clutter.
5. **Build the Character Pressure Sheet**: Produce the final vessel design.

## Content Type Adaptations
| Type | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Fiction | Build an open protagonist under strong pressure |
| Memoir | Keep the narrator specific but not self-sealed |
| Customer Story | Make the customer recognizable without flattening them |
| Founder Story | Avoid genius mythology; show a normal person facing a hard decision |
| Speech | Use the speaker as witness, not hero sculpture |

## Output Requirements
Produce:
- `Over-Characterization Risks`
- `Ordinary Human Core`
- `Extraordinary Pressure`
- `Necessary Specifics`
- `Character Vessel Sheet`

## Quality Gate
- The character is not blank.
- The character is not over-described.
- The pressure becomes more visible.
- Reader entry is improved.
