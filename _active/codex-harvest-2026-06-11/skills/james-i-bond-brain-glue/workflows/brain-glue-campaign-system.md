# Campaign System

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this when the user wants a full campaign: content, social, video, email, landing copy, offer messaging, launch angles, or an entrepreneurial go-to-market push.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../genius.md`, `../references/genius-patterns.md`, `../references/hidden-knowledge.md`, and `../references/implementation.md`. Stack with platform, proof, offer, video, or positioning skills if the user requests those domains.

## Execution

1. **Clarify campaign goal**: Awareness, launch, lead generation, sales, waitlist, product education, or belief shift.
2. **Define audience and tribe**: Identity, problem, desire, objections, language, and fit.
3. **Forge campaign spine**: One sticky phrase or idea that everything repeats.
4. **Build message architecture**: Core message, mechanism metaphor, proof cues, rejection line, sensory motif.
5. **Create asset set**: Social posts, video scripts, email sequence, landing section, ad hooks, CTA language.
6. **Create deployment map**: Where each asset goes and what job it performs.
7. **Quality audit**: Confirm repetition, clarity, channel fit, and ethical claims.

| Channel | Campaign Job |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn/X | Belief shift and audience sorting |
| Short video | Attention and emotional demonstration |
| Email | Trust, story, proof, direct invitation |
| Landing page | Conversion and qualification |
| Ads | Hook testing and curiosity |
| Sales calls | Expectation setting and objection handling |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- **Campaign Goal**
- **Audience / Tribe Map**
- **Campaign Spine**
- **Message Architecture**
- **Asset Kit**
- **Deployment Map**
- **QA Rubric**

## Quality Gate

The campaign must be unified by one repeated sticky idea. If every asset has a different core message, rebuild the spine.
