# Brain Glue Diagnostic

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this when the user provides an existing message, offer, landing page, social post, script, product name, ad, pitch, or campaign and wants it improved. If no asset is provided, ask for the asset or run `brain-glue-core-message` instead.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../genius.md`, `../references/genius-patterns.md`, and `../references/implementation.md`.

## Execution

1. **Name the buying idea**: State the one idea the audience must remember.
2. **Score stickiness**: Rate clarity, emotional charge, memory, business function, audience fit, sensory vividness, and ethical force from 1-10.
3. **Diagnose the block**: Identify whether the asset fails from confusion, blandness, weak recall, wrong tribe, low trust, weak desire, or lack of sensory proof.
4. **Map missing Brain Glue levers**: Choose the 3 highest-impact STEAM ATTRACTORS.
5. **Produce fixes**: Generate concrete rewrites, not advice.
6. **Select a winner**: Recommend the highest-impact revision and explain why it wins.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Asset | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Social post | Prioritize hook, first 2 lines, repeatable phrase, and comment trigger |
| Video script | Prioritize opening visual, spoken line, pattern break, and retention beat |
| Sales page | Prioritize hero section, offer clarity, proof placement, and CTA language |
| Product name | Prioritize recall, category clarity, sound pattern, and trademark caution |
| Email | Prioritize subject, first sentence, tension, and one clear next action |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- **Sticky Diagnosis**
- **Scorecard**
- **Top 3 Failure Points**
- **Brain Glue Fixes**
- **Recommended Rewrite**
- **Deployment Notes**

## Quality Gate

The final rewrite must be easier to understand, easier to remember, and more emotionally specific than the original. Do not provide generic copywriting advice.
