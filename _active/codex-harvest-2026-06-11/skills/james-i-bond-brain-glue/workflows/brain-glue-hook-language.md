# Hook Language Forge

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this when the user needs repeatable phrases: hooks, slogans, captions, headlines, taglines, product names, book titles, ad lines, or signature quotes.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../genius.md`, `../references/genius-patterns.md`, and `../references/implementation.md`.

## Execution

1. **Extract keywords**: List the product words, buyer pain words, benefit words, competitor words, and sensory words.
2. **Build sound patterns**: Generate rhymes, near rhymes, matched beats, alliteration, and repeated syllables.
3. **Build reversals**: Generate chiasmus and antimetabole from the core idea.
4. **Add wake words**: Test trigger words that ethically intensify attention.
5. **Break expected phrases**: Twist cliches, known sayings, category phrases, or competitor claims.
6. **Score candidates**: Clarity, memory, business job, audience fit, risk.
7. **Recommend the winners**: Provide use-case-specific winners.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Format | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Hook | 1-line pattern break |
| Tagline | 3-8 words, memorable, category connected |
| Product name | Sound pattern plus meaning |
| Social post | Scroll-stopping first line |
| Video | Spoken line that sounds natural out loud |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- **Keyword Bank**
- **Rhymes and Near Rhymes**
- **Alliteration Lines**
- **Chiasmus Lines**
- **Trigger-Word Lines**
- **Odd/Unexpected Lines**
- **Final Shortlist**

## Quality Gate

Read each winner aloud mentally. If it is awkward to say, too long to remember, or unclear without context, cut it.
