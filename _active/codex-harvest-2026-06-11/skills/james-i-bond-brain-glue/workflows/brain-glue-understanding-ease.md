# Understanding Ease Engine

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this when the user has a complex, technical, abstract, new-category, or expert-level idea that prospects do not instantly understand.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../genius.md`, `../references/genius-patterns.md`, and `../references/implementation.md`.

## Execution

1. **Identify the misunderstanding risk**: State what prospects are likely to get wrong.
2. **Choose the one-feature focus**: Select the most important feature to understand first.
3. **Translate the scale**: Use "compared to what," "near what," "as fast as," "as easy as," or "the equivalent of."
4. **Create a demonstration**: Design a visual, verbal, tactile, screen, or participation demo.
5. **Rewrite in buyer language**: Remove expert phrasing that creates friction.
6. **Build the clarity ladder**: One-line explanation, 30-second explanation, full explanation.
7. **Add memory handle**: Use a metaphor, sensory cue, or sticky phrase.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Format | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Technical offer | Plain-language mechanism and analogy |
| AI workflow | Before/after demonstration and job-to-be-done framing |
| Pitch deck | One-slide visual and "compared to what" line |
| Sales call | Explanation script with check-for-understanding prompt |
| Video | Prop, screen demo, or visual metaphor |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- **Likely Misunderstanding**
- **One-Feature Focus**
- **Perspective Translations**
- **Demonstration Concept**
- **Clarity Ladder**
- **Final Buyer-Safe Explanation**

## Quality Gate

The final explanation must be shorter, more concrete, and less dependent on expert vocabulary than the input.
