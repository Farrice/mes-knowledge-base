# Social Content System

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this for LinkedIn posts, X posts, threads, carousels, captions, creator content, founder-led posts, or social campaign angles.

## Skill Acquisition

Load `../genius.md`, `../references/genius-patterns.md`, and `../references/implementation.md`. If a platform-specific style skill is available and relevant, stack it after Brain Glue.

## Execution

1. **Define the social job**: Awareness, belief shift, authority, lead generation, launch, nurture, or sales.
2. **Extract the sticky idea**: One repeatable line or contrast.
3. **Choose the hook lever**: Odd/unexpected, trigger word, chiasmus, metaphor, rejection, or tribal cue.
4. **Build the content body**: Make the idea easy to understand through example, perspective, demonstration, or sensory detail.
5. **Create shareable residue**: Add one line the reader can quote or comment on.
6. **Adapt to platform**: Adjust length, structure, density, and CTA.
7. **Provide variants**: Offer multiple hooks and one final post.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Format | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Strong first line, business insight, useful specificity |
| X | Compression, punch, quotable phrasing |
| Carousel | One sticky claim per slide |
| Instagram caption | Identity cue, sensory detail, conversational CTA |
| Thread | Curiosity loop, numbered progression, repeated phrase |

## Output Requirements

Produce:
- **Sticky Social Angle**
- **Hook Bank**
- **Finished Post or Thread**
- **Carousel Slide Outline when useful**
- **CTA Options**
- **Repurposing Notes**

## Quality Gate

The first line must earn attention without clickbait, and the post must contain one phrase worth remembering.
