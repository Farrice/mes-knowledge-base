# Brain Glue Implementation Reference

| "Explain this simply" | `brain-glue-understanding-ease` |

| "Give me hooks/headlines/taglines" | `brain-glue-hook-language` |

## Standard Execution Sequence

1. **Identify the buying idea**: What must the audience remember and repeat?
2. **Diagnose the block**: Confusion, blandness, weak recall, weak fit, low trust, low desire.
3. **Choose 2-4 Brain Glue levers**: Never force all fourteen.
4. **Generate candidates**: At least 10 short lines or 3 complete assets, depending on workflow.
5. **Select a winner**: Recommend the best option and explain the selection.
6. **Deploy across format**: Adapt the sticky idea for the requested channel.
7. **Run quality gate**: Clarity, emotion, recall, fit, sensory vividness, ethical strength.

## Content Type Adaptation

| Type | Brain Glue Priority |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn/X | Hook language, chiasmus, tribal alignment, odd/unexpected |
| Newsletter | Expectation, analogy, perspective, sensory storytelling |
| Short video | Pattern break, visual demonstration, sound cue, one repeatable line |
| Long video | Expectation, curiosity loop, analogy ladder, sensory proof |
| Sales page | Cognitive simplicity, proof framing, trigger words, rejection, repeated slogan |
| Email | Honest expectation, trigger word, single clear ask, memorable close |
| Product naming | Metaphor, alliteration, rhyme, anchoring, word ownership |
| Campaigns | One campaign spine repeated across every asset |

## Quality Gate

Before finalizing, verify:

- The message can be repeated by a non-expert.
- The sticky device serves the business goal.
- The phrase does not create legal, ethical, or brand-risk issues.
- The output includes at least one sensory, verbal-pattern, or analogy device.
- The final recommendation is clear, not a menu with no decision.
- The work does not reproduce long copyrighted passages.

## Sample Dry-Run Inputs

- **LinkedIn post idea:** "AI agents help entrepreneurs turn expertise into reusable business systems."
- **Short video topic:** "Why your offer is not selling even though it is valuable."
- **Landing page message:** "We build custom AI workflows for service businesses."
- **Campaign request:** "Launch a consulting offer that turns a founder's scattered knowledge into a deployable AI brain."
