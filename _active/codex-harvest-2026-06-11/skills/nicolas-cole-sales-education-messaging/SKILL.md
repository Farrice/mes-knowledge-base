# Nicolas Cole Sales Education Messaging

This skill turns Cole's "sales is education" method into an operating surface for sales calls, sales pages, launch emails, DMs, positioning bridges, and buyer-readiness content.

Use it when the goal is not to pressure a buyer, but to help them understand the problem, understand why it matters, understand the category of solution, and become informed enough to make a clean decision.

## Core Thesis

Sales works when the buyer becomes sufficiently educated. Objections are usually not fixed facts; they are unanswered questions, incorrect category comparisons, missing context, or unexamined beliefs. The seller's job is to educate the buyer on the problem and the category of solution so the buyer can either opt in with clarity or say no for a real reason.

## Decision Framework

Use this skill when the work is buyer education: objection diagnosis, sales-call framing, launch email logic, sales page education, problem-solution sequencing, or converting positioning into a buyer-ready message.

Do not use this skill when the issue is only lead generation volume, list building, sentence polish, broad niche choice, or product scoping. Use the adjacent Cole skills first, then return here once the buyer and offer are clear.
