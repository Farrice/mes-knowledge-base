# Buyer Belief Ladder

## Pre-Flight Gate

Use this workflow when the user needs to understand why a buyer is not ready, what objections are present, or what beliefs must change before a sale can happen.

Do not use it to bulldoze legitimate no-fit buyers. The goal is informed decision-making.

## Execution

1. Name the offer, buyer, desired action, and current resistance.
2. List what the buyer currently believes about:
   - the problem
   - the cause
   - the cost of inaction
   - available solutions
   - this category of solution
   - the seller or provider
3. Translate every objection into one of four gaps:
   - unanswered question
   - wrong comparison
   - missing proof
   - missing emotional relevance
4. Build the belief ladder from current belief to needed belief.
5. Define the education asset that moves each rung.
6. Mark which rungs belong before the sales call, during the call, after the call, or on the sales page.
7. Remove any rung that requires manipulation, exaggeration, or unsupported claims.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Type | Ladder Focus |
|---|---|
| High-ticket service | Trust, mechanism, specificity, category fit, risk reversal |
| Digital product | Problem cost, product vehicle fit, proof, urgency, implementation confidence |
| Course/cohort | Transformation path, completion belief, time belief, support belief |
| Newsletter/productized content | Tangible value, repeatability, relevance, category payoff |
| Agency/consulting | Business case, category maturity, internal capacity, ROI logic |

## Output Requirements

Return:

1. Current buyer belief map.
2. Needed belief ladder.
3. Objection-to-gap translation table.
4. Proof and education assets by rung.
5. Priority order for the next sales asset.

## Quality Gate

Pass only if every objection is translated into a teachable gap. Do not answer objections with generic reassurance.
