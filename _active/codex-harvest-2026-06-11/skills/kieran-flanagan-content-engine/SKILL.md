# Kieran Flanagan - Content Engine

The creation and production layer of Kieran's AI content team architecture. These tools generate, enrich, adapt, and multiply content across platforms. They work standalone OR pair with Audience Intelligence for maximum impact.

## Available Workflows

| # | Workflow | Produces | Use When |
|---|---------|----------|----------|
| 01 | [Talking Points](workflows/01-talking-points.md) | Categorized talking point library extracted from source material | You need to mine a body of work (podcasts, articles, notes) for unique perspectives before creating content |
| 02 | [Lookalike Content](workflows/02-lookalike-content.md) | Structural pattern analysis + 15-25 new content ideas | You want proven content architecture applied to your unique topics — battle-tested structures with original substance |
| 03 | [Content Enrich](workflows/03-content-enrich.md) | Enrichment modules (data, stories, quotes, case studies) for any draft | You have a draft that needs authority — statistics, case studies, expert quotes, and real-world connections |
| 04 | [Content Bundle](workflows/04-content-bundle.md) | Cross-platform content bundle from one core idea | You have one idea and want to publish it natively across LinkedIn, newsletter, X, and YouTube |
| 05 | [Platform Adapt](workflows/05-platform-adapt.md) | Platform-optimized version of any content piece | You have finished content from one platform and want to adapt it for another with true platform-native transformation |
| 06 | [Content Series Plan](workflows/06-content-series-plan.md) | Multi-part content series with threading and open loops | You want to plan a 5-7 part series that builds audience anticipation and compounds topic authority |
| 07 | [Hook Formula Extract](workflows/07-hook-formula-extract.md) | Personal hook formula library from your best-performing content | You want to mine YOUR specific hook patterns so you can replicate what works for YOU, not generic formulas |
| 08 | [Competitor Content Spy](workflows/08-competitor-content-spy.md) | Competitive content intelligence report with differentiation opportunities | You want to understand a competitor's content strategy and find gaps you can own |
