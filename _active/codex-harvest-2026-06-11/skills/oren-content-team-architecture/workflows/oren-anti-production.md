> **Load First**: [genius.md](../genius.md) — Pattern 11 (Anti-Overproduction Mandate), Pattern 6 (10-Asset Weekly Standard), Pattern 5 (Pod Cadence System)

---

## Step 1: Intake

Collect:
1. Planned content formats and weekly output target.
2. Production gear, edit workflow, shoot team, review chain, and publishing cadence.
3. Current bottlenecks from shot to published asset.
4. Which assets are daily engine content versus documentary or brand-IP assets.

---

## Step 2: Split Daily Engine From Brand IP

Classify each planned asset:
- **Daily Engine**: platform-native, fast, recurring, high-volume, learning-oriented.
- **Brand IP**: documentary, campaign film, launch hero, or evergreen flagship asset.

Only brand-IP assets may justify heavy production. Daily engine assets should optimize for speed, consistency, and usable creative volume.

## Step 4: Apply Oren Constraints

Default constraints:
- Daily engine gear should stay lean: phone, simple stabilizer, practical lights, clean audio.
- Shoot teams should stay small unless the asset is true brand IP.
- Editing should favor in-camera-to-usage speed.
- Approval should happen at concept/brief level more than endless post-production revisions.
- A production upgrade must either improve measurable performance or unlock a format that could not otherwise exist.
