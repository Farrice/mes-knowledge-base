# Genius Patterns

## 1. Monetizable Expertise First

Before drafting, translate expertise into a buyer outcome. The post should answer: what changes for the reader, why does it matter commercially, and why would this create demand for the expert?

## 2. Strategic Arbitrage

Borrow working attention patterns without copying ideas. Use timely trends, authority figures, high-performing post structures, industry tensions, and buyer frustrations as attention carriers for the creator's own insight.

## 3. Hook And Rehook

The hook stops the scroll; the rehook earns the click. The second line should add specificity, proof, tension, or consequence instead of falling into a generic list.

## 4. Broad-Narrow-Niche

Go broad in the hook, narrow in the buyer problem, and niche in the body. This keeps the post discoverable without flattening the expertise into generic advice.

## 5. SLAY Structure

Story gets attention, Lesson creates retention, Advice makes the post useful, and You points the reader toward reflection, reply, lead magnet, or offer.

## 6. PAS Sales Content

Problem-agitate-solution is the default for conversion content. Make the cost of inaction concrete, then bridge into the lead magnet or offer without begging.

## 7. AI Context Before Output

Do not ask AI for a post until it has identity, offer, proof, audience, voice constraints, swipe structure, monetizable expertise, and strategic arbitrage topics.

## 8. First 30 Minutes Guard

The post is not finished when it is scheduled. Reserve the first engagement window for replies, comments, and conversation routing.
