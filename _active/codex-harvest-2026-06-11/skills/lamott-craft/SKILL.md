# Lamott Craft — Skill Manifest

## Overview
Writing Craft & Creative Process Mastery extracted from Anne Lamott (author of *Bird by Bird*, 21 books, 45+ years in publishing). The only writing system in the Antigravity roster built on *relational* craft — managing your relationship with your inner critic, your material, your attention, and your reader.

**Core Thesis**: Writing is not a set of techniques to execute. It's a set of relationships to manage. The writer who befriends the process outlasts the warrior who fights through it.
