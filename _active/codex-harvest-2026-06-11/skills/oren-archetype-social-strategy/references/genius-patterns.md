# Oren Archetype Social Strategy — Genius Patterns

1. **Archetype As Customer-World Role**: Decide the role the brand plays for the customer before deciding topics or formats.
2. **Resource Reality Before Big Idea**: Map talent, proof, design capacity, budget, and risk tolerance before selecting the archetype.
3. **Exercise-Led Buy-In**: Use archetype selection as a workshop so clients and leadership co-own the strategy.
4. **Oracle**: Use expertise to explain history, comparisons, why things are the way they are, and predictions.
5. **Performer**: Build a show where the product is omnipresent but not over-explained.
6. **World Builder**: Create an intellectual, fictional, artistic, or cultural world that makes the brand magnetic by association.
7. **Catalyst**: Bridge the audience from current state to desired state with proof, guidance, and community.
8. **Helper**: Use practical peer-level content, tutorials, comparisons, interviews, and relatable stories.
9. **Product Mix As Price Psychology**: Separate entry, core, aspirational, and halo jobs in the offer/product system.
10. **Persona Data To Message**: Segment based on real behavior, LTV, buyer context, and repeat purchase, then map to ads, pages, and communications.
11. **Funnel And Calendar Planning**: Grade awareness, consideration, and conversion, then choose initiatives and calendar moments.
12. **Intellectual Content Plus Personality**: Pair researched, high-signal content with personality and story so attention can turn into trust.
13. **Offer Alignment**: Sell a named package competing on speed, price, or skill, supported by a lower-friction entry offer.
14. **Systems As Creative Leverage**: Reference repositories, calendars, updates, trackers, process docs, and pillar measurement make taste executable.
