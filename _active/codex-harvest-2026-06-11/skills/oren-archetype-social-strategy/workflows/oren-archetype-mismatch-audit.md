# Oren Archetype Mismatch Audit

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load `../genius.md`. Audit the system, not just individual posts. Bad performance often comes from asking a Helper team to execute a World Builder idea or asking a brand with no expert to act like an Oracle.

## Input Required

- Current content examples or description of recurring posts
- Business model and offer
- Audience and buying context
- Available team/resources
- Recent performance signals and qualitative feedback

## Execution

1. **Name The Implied Archetype**
   - Infer what the brand is currently acting like.
   - Separate declared strategy from what the content actually signals.

2. **Find The Mismatch Type**

| Mismatch | Symptom | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| Resource mismatch | Ideas require talent or budget the team lacks | Choose a lower-friction archetype or acquire the missing resource |
| Audience mismatch | Content entertains people who do not buy | Rebuild around buyer questions, proof, or persona split |
| Business-model mismatch | Content gets views but does not support offer | Add CTA, offer alignment, landers, and proof |
| Authority mismatch | Brand teaches without enough credibility | Move to Helper or add expert proof |
| Risk mismatch | Team wants World Builder but approves safe posts | Reduce to Helper/Oracle or secure brave approval |
| Funnel mismatch | All posts are top-of-funnel | Add consideration/conversion formats |

3. **Audit Content By Pillar**
   - Score each pillar on archetype fit, proof, audience value, production realism, and commercial connection.
   - Keep, revise, merge, or kill each pillar.

4. **Prescribe The New Role**
   - Decide whether to keep the current archetype, shift to a new one, or use primary/secondary.
   - Name the 3 behaviors the brand must stop immediately.
   - Name the 3 behaviors it must start.

5. **Create The Repair Sprint**
   - Build a 14-day sprint with replacement formats, owner assignments, proof needs, and measurement criteria.

## Content Type Adaptations

| Context | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Views but no buyers | Prioritize commercial connection and persona-to-offer fit |
| Low views but strong buyers | Improve hooks and top-of-funnel formats without losing proof |
| Leadership blocks content | Use exercise-led buy-in and resource-fit workshop |
| Creator burnout | Reduce format complexity and install the Creative Systems Stack |
| Paid ads underperform | Add organic recognition, lander alignment, and persona-specific messaging |

## Output Requirements

Produce:

- **Implied Archetype**
- **Mismatch Diagnosis**
- **Pillar Audit**
- **Keep/Kill/Repair Table**
- **New Archetype Verdict**
- **Stop/Start List**
- **14-Day Repair Sprint**
- **Risks And Required Decisions**

## Quality Gate

Do not blame "the algorithm" unless the content-role, resource, funnel, and offer alignment have been checked first.
