# Story Code Sales Story

## Pre-Flight Gate

Load:
- `skills/persuasion-story-code/genius.md`
- `skills/persuasion-story-code/references/story-taxonomy.md`
- `skills/persuasion-story-code/references/quality-rubric.md`

Use when the user needs sales copy sections that persuade through story: lead, origin, mechanism, proof, objection, guarantee, or CTA bridge.

## Input Required

- Offer and price if relevant.
- Audience and buying stage.
- Current sales asset or outline.
- Proof, mechanism, customer results, guarantees, and objections.
- Format: sales page, webinar, VSL, email sequence, pitch, or DM.

## Execution

1. **Map sales sequence**: Identify where the buyer needs belief, desire, clarity, reassurance, and proof.
2. **Pick story blocks**: Choose the smallest set of stories that move the sale.
3. **Write each block**: Make every story short, concrete, and placed where the question appears.
4. **Add bridges**: Connect story to offer without heavy explanation.
5. **Flag claims**: Mark proof gaps, regulated claims, or testimonial permissions.

## Output

Produce **Sales Story Sections**:

- **Story Stack Overview**: ordered blocks and jobs.
- **Finished Sales Sections**: ready-to-use copy.
- **Objection Story Bank**: answers to price, trust, usability, novelty, proof, risk, and alternatives.
- **Guarantee/Risk Story**: if available and appropriate.
- **Proof Notes**: what needs documentation.

## Quality Gate

- Each story advances the sale.
- No unsupported claims.
- The copy remains conversational and specific.
