## Core Source Concepts
- **AI-era survival**: designers win by adding psychology, meaning, human imperfection, and intentionality to clean generated output.
- **Why before what**: every element needs a reason or it gets removed.
- **Predictive empathy**: design for the feeling the viewer carries away.
- **Memory encoding**: use metaphor, absence, substitution, or unresolved visual questions to make a concept stick.
- **Movement and flow**: guide with direct cues, hierarchy, layered paths, implied motion, disruption, and temporal rhythm.
- **Grids**: use grids for hierarchy, whitespace, eye guidance, consistency, and controlled rule-breaking.
- **LIFT**: leverage point, internal rhythm, friction/flow, transferability.
- **Logo design**: compress the brief, use verbs before nouns, treat the mark as a memory hook, and present concepts in identical structures.
