# Offer Page - The Vibe Tax Brief

## Header

**Find the false signals before they cost you strategy, content, or clients.**

The Vibe Tax Brief is a 48-hour false-signal diagnostic for solo B2B operators, consultants, agencies, coaches, creators, and expert-led service businesses with a real offer but fuzzy buyer signal.

## The Problem

You may not have a content problem.

You may not have an offer problem.

You may be paying the vibe tax: making decisions from likes, peer praise, competitor noise, AI summaries, and half-remembered sales conversations instead of buyer reality.

Vibes have a tax. This brief finds it before you pay.

That gets expensive fast. You polish the wrong promise. You post from the wrong angle. You explain the solution before the buyer feels the problem. You collect attention from people who were never going to buy.

## Who It Is For

This is for you if:

- you have a real offer but the market response feels muddy

This is not for you if:
