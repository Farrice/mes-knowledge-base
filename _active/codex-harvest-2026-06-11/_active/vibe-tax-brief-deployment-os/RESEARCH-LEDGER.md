# Research Ledger - Vibe Tax Deployment OS

Updated: 2026-05-11  
Use: evidence base for Vibe Tax public copy, diagnostic framing, proof demos, and offer language.

## Research Posture

The launch post should not carry heavy statistics. The stats below are for internal confidence, diagnostic sales conversations, and future proof assets.

Public copy can use a claim only when it is either:

- directly cited,
- phrased as a local inference,
- or translated into a softer pattern without a number.

## Public Claim Guard

Avoid opening posts with statistics. Farrice's strongest public entry is a felt contradiction:

```text
The dashboard says the post worked.
The buyer still did not move.
That gap is the Vibe Tax.
```

Numbers can support comments, carousels, briefs, and proof pages after the idea is already emotionally clear.
