# Vibe Tax Validation Sprint Kit

- Date: 2026-05-27
- Purpose: convert the Vibe Tax PMF verdict into the first safe local build asset.
- External action status: draft-only; no messages sent, no publishing, no DMs.

## Sprint Goal

Validate whether real buyers will submit a concrete asset or decision for a Vibe Tax Diagnostic and whether at least one buyer will consider a 300-500 dollar paid brief.

Secondary validation: determine whether buyers want the paid Vibe Tax Brief itself or whether Vibe Tax should function as the free/low-cost audit that routes into a concrete Guessproof Workflow Rescue.

## Target Buyer

Start with people who already have:

- a paid offer
- public content, a sales page, outreach, or lead flow
- some visible signal they are tempted to trust
- uncertainty about whether that signal is real

Best first segments:

| Segment | Why |
|---|---|
| solo B2B consultants | They feel the cost of fuzzy positioning quickly. |
| small agency owners | They see lead-quality and client-conversion issues. |
| expert creators/coaches | They often have engagement without buying movement. |
| AI-heavy operators | They are producing more, but still need judgment. |
| service founders | They have real offers and can pay for decision clarity. |

## Qualification Filter

Only invite someone if at least two are true:

- They sell a service, offer, cohort, productized service, or B2B product.
- They have posted or launched something recently.
- Their content gets attention but unclear buyer movement.
- Their offer/page sounds polished but generic.
- They are about to rewrite, launch, reposition, pitch, or rebuild.
- They have mentioned leads, calls, DMs, conversion, or trust.

Avoid:

- no named offer
- no current decision
- pure idea-stage founders
- people asking for free full strategy
- people who only want viral content

## Manual Ask

```text
I am testing a small diagnostic called The Vibe Tax.

It is for people who are making a business/content/offer decision from a signal that might be lying to them: likes, comments, weak leads, peer praise, AI summaries, competitor noise, or a page that sounds clear but does not move buyers.

If you want, send me one asset or decision you are currently trusting:

- a post
- offer page
- service idea
- outreach message
- lead magnet
- sales page section
- positioning line

I will tell you the first signal I would distrust and whether I would keep, revise, stop, or test the current direction.

No pitch theater. I am looking for 10 real examples to validate whether this diagnostic is useful enough to become a paid 48-hour brief.
```

## Shorter Comment / Reply Version

```text
This is exactly the kind of thing I am calling the Vibe Tax: a signal that looks like progress but may not predict buyer movement.

If you want, send me the asset or decision you are trusting and I will name the first signal I would distrust.
```

## Intake Questions

Use the 6-question MVP, not the full 12-question diagnostic:

1. What signal are you trusting right now?
2. What action do you want that signal to create?
3. Who is the buyer this is supposed to move?
4. What asset or decision should I inspect?
5. What would happen if this signal is wrong?
6. What would make this useful enough to pay for?

## Triage Rubric

| Score | Meaning | Action |
|---:|---|---|
| 0-1 | No real decision or buyer context. | Do not sell; ask for a concrete asset. |
| 2-3 | Interesting but fuzzy. | Give one useful observation and request a sharper asset. |
| 4-5 | Real decision, real buyer, real risk. | Run mini diagnostic and invite paid brief if useful. |

## Mini Diagnostic Output

Keep each free diagnostic short:

```text
The signal I would distrust first:
[one sentence]

Why:
[one tight explanation]

Likely false-positive:
[what looks good but may not predict buying]

Keep / revise / stop / test:
[one decision]

If I were running the paid Vibe Tax Brief, I would inspect:
[buyer language / proof gap / competitor frame / hidden objection / sales opener]
```

## Paid Bridge

Use only if the person submits a real asset and responds seriously.

```text
There is enough here for the full Vibe Tax Brief.

The paid version is a 48-hour false-signal diagnostic. I would map:

- buyer language fit
- proof gaps
- hidden objections
- competitor/substitute frames
- false-positive signals
- what to keep, revise, stop, or test

Founding version is 300 dollars for the first few. If you want, I can scope the exact brief from this asset.
```

## Keep / Revise / Stop Rules

| Outcome | Rule |
|---|---|
| Keep | 3+ real submissions or 1 paid ask. |
| Revise | People like the phrase but cannot name a decision or asset. |
| Stop | People interpret it as generic content advice, AI consulting, or vibes-only branding. |
| Expand | A paid buyer asks for implementation, recurring support, or a reusable workflow fix. |
| Route to Guessproof | The buyer submits a recurring AI/workflow failure rather than an offer/content/market decision. |

## First Proof Demo Template

```text
Original signal:
[what looked like progress]

Why it might be false:
[buyer, context, proof, or timing mismatch]

What the buyer may actually need:
[hidden objection or decision pressure]

Vibe Tax verdict:
Keep / revise / stop / test

Revision:
[one better line, asset direction, or proof move]

What to watch next:
[reply, save, DM, booked call, paid ask, asset submission]
```

## Build Verdict

Use this sprint kit before building a new landing page.

The landing page should come after:

- 3 real submissions
- 1 paid brief request
- 1 proof demo that creates specific replies

Until then, the highest-leverage asset is manual validation with a clean diagnostic receipt.
