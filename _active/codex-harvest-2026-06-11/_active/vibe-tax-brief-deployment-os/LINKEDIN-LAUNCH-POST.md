## Draft

Most people do not have a content problem.

They have a false signal problem.

The dashboard says the post worked.

Likes up.
Comments up.
The hook was clean.
The carousel looked like it belonged on the feed.

But no serious buyer asked about the thing you actually sell.

That gap is what I am calling the Vibe Tax.

It is what you pay when you mistake visible reaction for buyer truth.

And I say that with my whole chest because I have been doing a version of it too.

Building in silence.
Polishing systems.
Trusting that if the asset is smart enough, the market will magically connect the dots.

It does not.

A buyer does not care that your content is impressive.

They care whether it answers the private questions they are already using to decide if you are safe:

Do they understand my actual situation?

Can they name the thing I have not been able to say cleanly?

Do they see what I am comparing them against?

Is there proof here that survives outside their own head?

AI made the tax easier to miss.

Not because AI is bad.

Because it can help you produce content that looks finished before the thinking is finished.

The post sounds clear.
The offer sounds polished.
The brand feels active.

But the buyer still does not know:

Why this?
Why now?
Why you?
Why should I trust it?

That is the false signal.

The Vibe Tax Diagnostic is my way of finding where the signal is lying:

Buyer language fit.
Shortlist readiness.
Proof gaps.
False signal risk.
Human resonance.
Social repeatability.

Basically:

Where are you getting attention that does not become trust?

Where are you sounding smart but not becoming buyable?

Where are you using polished language to avoid saying the scarier true thing?

I am opening a few founding diagnostics this week.

Comment or DM "vibe tax" and I will tell you where I would look first.

No pitch theater.
No automation.
Just a sharp read on the false signal that might be costing you clients.

## First Comment Option

The diagnostic is simple on purpose:

1. What signal are you trusting right now?
2. What buyer action is it supposed to create?
3. What proof do you have that the signal is real?

That is usually where the leak shows up.
