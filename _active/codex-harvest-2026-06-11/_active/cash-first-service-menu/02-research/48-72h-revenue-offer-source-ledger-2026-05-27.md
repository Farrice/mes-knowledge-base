## Evidence Handling

- verified: official report, marketplace page, first-party source, or local validated artifact.
- directional: useful marketplace/category signal but not enough for a standalone claim.
- verified-local: existing workspace artifact or operating package loaded from the repo.
- inference: a recommendation drawn from multiple verified or local sources.

## Decision Rule

For the 48-72 hour plan, prioritize offers that:

1. attach to a live buyer pain people already buy,
2. can be explained without category education,
3. can be delivered manually with the existing harness,
4. can produce a visible proof artifact,
5. can plausibly reach 1,000 dollars through 1-4 sales,
6. do not require publishing, paid ads, connector writes, marketplace approval, or new platform payout timing.
