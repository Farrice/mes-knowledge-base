# Proof Demo 2 - Lead List + Outreach Starter Pack

## Demo Buyer

Small agency or consultant selling a high-value B2B service but struggling to decide who to contact first.

## Promise

I find qualified prospects and turn each segment into a specific first-message angle.

## Lead Criteria

Contact prospects only when they show at least three signals:

- clear paid offer
- visible marketing or sales activity
- service price likely above $1K
- recent content, website update, launch, hiring, or offer signal
- observable gap between offer and buyer path
- reachable through a public, manual channel

## Source Searches

Use these manually:

- Upwork jobs for "AI integration", "marketing automation", "lead generation", "market research", "chatbot setup"
- Contra profiles/services for consultants and fractional operators
- Google search for niche consultants with active blogs/newsletters
- LinkedIn manual profile review without scraping or automation
- public newsletters where the author has a paid offer
- agency websites with case studies but weak lead magnets
