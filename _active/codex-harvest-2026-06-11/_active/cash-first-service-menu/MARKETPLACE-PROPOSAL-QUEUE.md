## Proposal Fit Filter

Apply only when at least three are true:

- buyer names a concrete deliverable
- budget can support at least $250
- project can be scoped to 48 hours or a small first milestone
- AI, automation, research, lead generation, email, or strategy is explicit
- listing shows business context, not just task dumping
- you can reference one specific detail in the first line

## First-Line Rules

Good:

```text
You do not need another generic AI consultant here; you need [specific deliverable] turned into a first usable workflow.
```

Good:

```text
The useful first milestone is not the whole system. It is [small scope] delivered cleanly enough to test.
```

Bad:

```text
I am an experienced AI consultant and I can help with all your needs.
```
