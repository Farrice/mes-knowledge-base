## 30-Day Operating Rule

Every day has four blocks:

| Block | Time cap | Output |
|---|---:|---|
| Market scan | 30 min | 3 buyer phrases, 3 prospects, 1 objection |
| Direct asks / proposals | 60 min | 5-10 manual asks or 2-3 marketplace proposals |
| Proof or delivery | 60-120 min | one demo, sample, client deliverable, or fulfillment step |
| Tracker closeout | 15 min | metrics, buyer words, next action |

If an action cannot fit inside 1-4 hours/day, reduce scope before selling it.
