# Outreach Scripts - Cash-First Service Menu

> Personalize every opener with one real observation.
> Do not bulk-send.
> Do not imply guaranteed leads, revenue, or automation.

## Universal First Ask

```text
[Name], I am testing a few small service sprints around AI, research, and client acquisition systems. Not broad AI consulting. Small 48-hour fixes: one workflow, one lead pack, or one buyer research brief. I noticed [specific observation]. Is there one part of your sales/marketing/admin process that feels more manual than it should right now?
```

## AI Workflow Integration Sprint

```text
[Name], I noticed [specific repeated task / workflow clue]. I am opening a few small AI Workflow Sprints where we pick one repeated task and turn it into a usable AI-assisted workflow in 48 hours: map, prompts, SOP, and acceptance checklist. No huge AI transformation project. Is there one workflow you keep rebuilding manually?
```

## Lead List + Outreach Starter Pack

```text
[Name], your offer around [specific service] seems clear, but the bigger question is probably who is most worth contacting first. I am testing a Lead List + Outreach Starter Pack: 25-50 qualified prospects, visible reason they fit, and first-message angles. No auto-DMs or scraping. Would a sample structure be useful?
```

## The Vibe Tax Brief

```text
[Name], I noticed [specific offer/category/buyer signal]. If you are making decisions around positioning, outreach, content, or client strategy, I am testing a 48-hour Vibe Tax Brief.

It finds the false signals before they cost you strategy, content, or clients: buyer language, competitor/substitute frames, hidden objections, proof gaps, and 3 sales angles.

Want me to run the quick diagnostic on one decision you are making?
```

## Email Follow-Up Rescue

```text
[Name], quick question. When someone inquires or shows interest, do they reliably get a useful follow-up sequence, or does it depend on you remembering? I am testing a small Email Follow-Up Rescue: 3 emails, timing, CTA, and a simple tracking workflow. It is built for interested leads who currently go quiet.
```

## AI Content Misfire Audit

```text
[Name], your post on [specific topic] had a real idea in it. The part I would check is whether the content creates a clean path back to your offer or mostly gets agreement. I have a 48-hour AI Content Misfire Audit for that: profile, offer, recent posts, then 3 sharper angles and a buyer-ready workflow. Worth sending the one-pager?
```

## Follow-Ups

### Bump

```text
Quick bump. If this is already handled, no worries. If one workflow, prospecting list, or buyer-language decision is currently slowing things down, I can keep the first version small.
```

### Example Bridge

```text
One easy way to tell if it fits: send me the repeated task, offer, or buyer segment you are stuck on. I can tell you which of the three small sprints fits before you pay.
```

### Breakup

```text
Closing the loop so I do not keep adding to your inbox. If you hit a point where AI/tools/research/prospecting feel useful but uninstalled, this is exactly the kind of small sprint I am testing.
```

## Marketplace Proposal Opener

Use with `/draft-proposal`, then customize before submitting.

```text
This caught my eye because you do not need someone to talk vaguely about AI. You need [specific deliverable from listing] turned into something usable quickly.

I can help with a tight first version: [workflow / lead pack / research brief / chatbot intake / follow-up sequence], delivered with a clear handoff so you can test it instead of buying a giant project upfront.
```

## Copy Gate Notes

Pass criteria:

- first line names a specific buyer problem
- one offer per message
- no "AI can transform your business"
- no needy language
- no guarantee language
- one next action
- sounds like a person, not a funnel
