# Client-Facing One-Person Marketing Operating Template

## What This Is

This is a practical marketing operating system for a service business that does not have a full marketing team.

The goal is not to do every marketing tactic. The goal is to make the highest-leverage parts repeatable:

- referrals,
- proof,
- buyer messages,
- follow-up,
- sales collateral,
- simple content validation,
- and a weekly execution rhythm.

Use real client language, objections, results, and proof. Avoid vague claims like "save time," "scale faster," or "work smarter" unless the proof makes them specific.

## 5. AI-Assisted Copy Setup

AI can help draft, organize, and adapt marketing assets, but it needs the right context.

Provide:

- service description,
- buyer type,
- proof points,
- testimonials or client notes,
- objections,
- voice examples,
- words or claims to avoid.

AI can help create:

- referral emails,
- landing page sections,
- case study snippets,
- follow-up emails,
- LinkedIn posts,
- sales one-pagers,
- FAQ answers.

Human review rule:

```text
Do not send or publish AI-generated marketing until it has been checked for truth, proof, specificity, and voice.
```

## 6. Weekly Marketing Rhythm

| Work lane | Weekly focus |
|---|---|
| Referral and proof | Ask past clients, clean up outcomes, capture testimonials. |
| Message and copy | Turn buyer questions into proof-backed copy. |
| Funnel and follow-up | Improve the page, PDF, email, CRM, or call follow-up. |
| Sales collateral | Build one useful asset that helps a buyer decide. |
| Organic validation | Test 1-3 messages in public or semi-public channels. |
| Logistics | Track assets, replies, next actions, and approvals. |

If time is limited, preserve this order: referral, proof, message, follow-up, collateral, then content.

## 7. First 7-Day Sprint

| Day | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gather proof and buyer questions | Proof inventory and bottleneck verdict |
| 2 | Build referral system | Referral ask and past-client list |
| 3 | Build message board | 3-5 proof-backed messages |
| 4 | Set up AI copy context | Brand/offer/proof prompt and review checklist |
| 5 | Improve follow-up surface | Email, PDF, page section, or call follow-up |
| 6 | Validate publicly | 1-3 posts or outreach tests |
| 7 | Review and choose next sprint | What worked, what to reuse, what to defer |

## 8. What Not To Do Yet

Do not start with:

- more channels,
- expensive paid ads,
- a full rebrand,
- daily content volume,
- complex automations,
- or creator campaigns.

Start where service businesses usually leak value first: relationships, proof, message clarity, and follow-up.

## 9. Next Implementation Path

After the first sprint, choose one:

| Path | Use when |
|---|---|
| Referral Sprint | Warm relationships are the fastest path to calls. |
| Proof Sprint | Outcomes exist but are not packaged. |
| Follow-Up Sprint | Leads inquire but do not convert. |
| Message Sprint | The offer is real but buyers do not understand why it matters. |
| Content Validation Sprint | The business needs public trust and authority. |
