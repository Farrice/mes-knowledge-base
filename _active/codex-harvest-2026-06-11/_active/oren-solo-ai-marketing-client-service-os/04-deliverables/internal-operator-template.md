## 9. Do-Not-Do List

Defer:

- paid ads before the follow-up and landing path can catch demand,
- new content channels before proof and referrals are organized,
- high-volume organic content before message validation,
- tool setup that does not ship client-facing proof,
- broad brand debates before the offer and buyer question are clear.

## 10. Scale Trigger

Graduate to pod/team architecture when:

- weekly marketing execution exceeds available solo hours,
- the same assets need repeated production,
- creator/vendor coordination becomes a bottleneck,
- paid creative needs more volume,
- the operator is dropping client delivery because marketing logistics are too heavy.
