# Start Here

## Fast Use

Use this package when a client or service business needs a practical one-person marketing operating system rather than a broad marketing plan.

Best first prompt:

```text
/oren-solo-ai-marketing-machine "Build a one-person AI marketing operating week for this service business: [business type], [offer], [sales goal], [current lead sources], [proof assets], [email/CRM state], [available weekly hours], [budget/tool constraints]. Use the client/service template bundle."
```

## What This Produces

- fit verdict
- referral and word-of-mouth fix
- monthly message board
- AI brand-voice and copy-machine brief
- 40-hour solo operator week
- sales collateral and email follow-up plan
- service-business channel priorities
- first 7-day sprint
- client-facing handoff
- proof and upsell path

## Good Fit

Use this for:

- consultants, coaches, agencies, freelancers, and expert services
- local or professional services
- B2B service businesses
- client/service offers with scattered proof and inconsistent marketing
- founders who need to turn client work into repeatable demand generation

## Poor Fit

Use a different Oren workflow first when:

- the business is mostly ecommerce or product-led
- there is a dedicated content pod or team
- the offer is undefined
- the buyer, service promise, and proof are too vague to create follow-up or collateral

## First Run

1. Read `01-source/source-grounding.md`.
2. Fill `04-deliverables/internal-operator-template.md`.
3. Convert the result into `04-deliverables/client-facing-operating-template.md` if a client handoff is needed.
4. Use `04-deliverables/48-72h-proof-sprint.md` when this should become a paid diagnostic or founding proof offer.
