# Oren Solo AI Marketing Client Service OS

## Purpose

This active project turns `/oren-solo-ai-marketing-machine` into a dual-use template package for client and service businesses.

It has two jobs:

- **Internal operator use**: help Codex and Farrice run a one-person AI marketing cadence for a service business without rebuilding the process each time.
- **Client-facing use**: provide a clean operating template and proof sprint that can become a paid diagnostic, implementation sprint, or retainer setup.

## Invocation

Use:

```text
/oren-solo-ai-marketing-machine [client/service business context]
```

Then apply this package when the context is a service business, consultant, agency, freelancer, professional practice, local service provider, coaching business, B2B service, or client-facing expert offer.

## Default Priority For Service Businesses

1. Referrals and word of mouth
2. Sales collateral and proof assets
3. Email and follow-up
4. Landing page and message match
5. LinkedIn or relevant organic validation
6. Paid, creator, and external content only after the funnel can catch demand

## Quality Bar

The package is good enough when a solo operator can open the internal template and know what to do this week, and a client can open the client-facing template and understand what to submit, what will happen next, and what proof will be produced.
