# Start Here - Ready Buyer Engine

## The 10-Minute Show Flow

Open with this sentence:

> This is not a content strategy. This is a way to turn attention, listings, referrals, and random leads into fewer but better client conversations while protecting your time with JJ.

Then show the package in this order:

1. **The problem:** Zillow-style leads and casual Instagram DMs create phone pressure without enough buyer readiness.
2. **The market shift:** first-time buyers are older, more financially complex, and need math, programs, buyer-rep clarity, and local tradeoffs before they tour.
3. **The engine:** one listing, one lead, and one comment become a buyer brief, lead score, content asset, DM path, and team handoff.
4. **The proof:** the dashboard tracks qualified leads, booked consults, signed clients, protected time, and proof assets before closings happen.
5. **The pilot:** 14 days to install the first version without publishing anything risky or changing live systems without approval.

## The Demo Moment

Use this mini-script:

> Let me show you the difference between casual ChatGPT and what we can actually build. Casual ChatGPT writes posts. This system reads the market, checks the claims, keeps your voice, scores the lead, routes the next action, protects your calendar, and gives us proof we can show your team later.

Show three sample inputs:

| Input | What The Engine Produces |
|---|---|
| One $850K-ish listing | Hooks, story prompts, buyer fit angle, seller proof angle, and an open-house DM path. |
| One Zillow-style lead | Buyer brief, readiness score, next question, and booking/nurture route. |
| One Instagram comment: `READY` | DM reply, intake questions, email capture, score, and consult criteria. |

## What To Emphasize

- This is designed to reduce chasing, not add another content burden.
- The system filters for seriousness before showings.
- Jen stays the trusted human; AI handles structure, recall, drafts, routing, and follow-up memory.
- The first revenue lane is ready first-time buyers and serious local sellers.
- The EDM/luxury dream stays alive as a second-phase network lane, not a distraction from cash flow.

- age-targeted ads

- dream-home hype
- EDM realtor

## The Ask

Ask Jen for one low-risk pilot:

> Give me 14 days, one listing or buyer scenario, your preferred lender contact, and permission to build the first version in a private sandbox. We will not publish, automate, advertise, or change your CRM without approval.

## First Calibration Questions

- Which exact area should this first serve: SFV only, greater LA, or current MyHouseSellers coverage?
- What split/expense model should the revenue math use?
- Which lender, title partner, and transaction coordinator should be included in the review loop?
- What current lead sources should be scored first: Zillow, Instagram, open houses, referrals, past clients, or website leads?
- What times are truly protected for JJ and family?
