## READY: $800K-$900K First Home Readiness Scorecard

### Cover Copy

You make real money. The Valley still feels tight. That does not mean buying is impossible. It means the next step needs to be honest.

Use this scorecard to understand whether you are ready for a buyer consult, need a lender review first, or should stay in the education path while you tighten the plan.

### Sections

1. Timeline: When would a move need to happen?
2. Payment comfort: What monthly range feels sustainable?
3. Cash picture: What funds are available now, pending, or gift-supported?
4. Lender status: Researching, referred, prequalified, preapproved, or not started.
5. Decision authority: Who needs to agree before an offer?
6. Area fit: Which neighborhoods or adjacent areas are realistic?
7. Tradeoff tolerance: Property type, condition, commute, parking, HOA, size.
8. Responsiveness: Can decisions happen quickly when the right fit appears?

### Result Bands

| Score | Route |
|---:|---|
| 75-100 | Buyer consult within 72 hours if geography and timing fit. |
| 55-74 | Lender review, Valley Match Map, and nurture sequence. |
| Under 55 | Education path until timeline, payment, or decision authority improves. |

### Delivery Email

Use it to clarify what is real, what needs review, and what should not become a showing yet.

If your score lands in the ready range, the next step is a buyer consult. If it lands in the middle, the next move may be lender review or area mapping. If it lands early, that is still useful: now you know what to work on.

## MAP: Valley Match Map

### Cover Copy

The home is not the only decision.

In the Valley, the right search usually comes down to tradeoffs: property type, commute, parking, noise, condition, HOA, school commute, family rhythm, renovation tolerance, and resale path.

This map helps you stop treating every listing like a separate emotional event.

### Map Categories

- Lake Balboa / Reseda: value, commute, condition, parking, lot variation.
- Canoga Park / Winnetka: entry opportunities, condition spread, commute and block-level research.
- Woodland Hills adjacent: lifestyle pull, price pressure, condo/townhome routes.
- North Hollywood / Valley Village adjacent: commute and lifestyle fit, higher competition pockets.
- Condo/townhome path: payment clarity, HOA review, lifestyle tradeoffs.
- Entry SFR path: condition tolerance, inspection discipline, offer speed.

### Buyer Questions

1. What tradeoff are you most willing to make?
2. What tradeoff will make you resent the home six months later?
3. What does your weekday need from the neighborhood?
4. What does your weekend need from the neighborhood?
5. What would make an adjacent area worth it?

## OFFER: Offer-Ready Weekend Kit

### Cover Copy

Before you spend a weekend touring, make sure the weekend can lead somewhere.

This kit helps you prepare for serious showings without turning every house into an emotional emergency.

### Checklist

- Payment comfort reviewed.
- Cash-to-close estimate reviewed.
- Lender status current.
- Decision makers aligned.
- Must-haves separated from preferences.
- Tradeoffs ranked.
- Showing schedule realistic.
- Offer terms understood.
- Documentation ready if family gift or co-buyer is involved.

### Offer Room Primer

Sellers may consider more than price. Terms, timing, financing confidence, contingencies, communication, and flexibility can matter. The goal is to understand the room before you are desperate to win one house.

### Delivery Email

Subject: Your Offer-Ready Weekend Kit

Here is the kit.

Use it before showings, not after you have already fallen in love with a property.

If the checklist exposes a gap, that is good. Fixing the gap before the weekend is calmer than discovering it when an offer is due.

## SELL: Seller Trust File

### Cover Copy

Calm selling starts before the listing goes live.

The Seller Trust File organizes the proof, prep, buyer questions, showing quality, and offer review details that help a seller make cleaner decisions.

### Sections

1. Property prep: repairs, cleaning, staging, access, disclosures.
2. Buyer questions: repeated objections, confusion points, showing feedback.
3. Showing quality: quantity, qualification, agent follow-up, repeat interest.
4. Offer stack: price, terms, contingencies, financing, timeline, risk.
5. Calm close: communication cadence, milestone tracking, issue resolution.

## SOUNDCHECK: Home Soundcheck

### Cover Copy

Some homes look right online and feel wrong in real life.

The Home Soundcheck looks at the parts of a home that do not always show up in photos: noise, flow, parking, night feel, hosting, work zones, creative recovery, and how the house supports the life around it.

### Checklist

- Street noise at different times.
- Neighbor rhythm.
- Parking reality.
- Guest flow.
- Work-from-home or creative zone.
- Sleep quality.
- Outdoor sound.
- Storage.
- Lighting.
- Kitchen and gathering flow.
- Post-event or late-night recovery fit.
