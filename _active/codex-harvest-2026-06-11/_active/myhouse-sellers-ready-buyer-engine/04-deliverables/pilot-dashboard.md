## Purpose

The dashboard proves whether the Ready Buyer Engine is making the business more controllable before closings appear.

Closings lag. Leading indicators tell us whether the system is working.

## Decision Rules

Continue the pilot if:

- Jen feels the voice is her
- lead scoring protects her attention
- at least one channel creates scored conversations
- dashboard reveals a real bottleneck
- family-time exceptions do not increase

Revise before launching if:

- content feels performative
- DMs invite too many casual browsers
- payment/program language needs review
- scoring rules are unclear
- Jen feels more burden, not less

Stop or narrow if:

- the system creates more reactive work
- the message attracts the wrong people
- compliance review flags unresolved risk
- Jen does not want to show up under the positioning
