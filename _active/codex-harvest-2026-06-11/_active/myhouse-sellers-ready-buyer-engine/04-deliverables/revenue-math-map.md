# Revenue Math Map

## Planning Purpose

This map translates Jen/Jiing's goal into observable pipeline math.

It is not an income promise. It is a planning tool that shows how many qualified conversations, consults, signed clients, and closings the engine needs to support 2-3 transactions per month while protecting family time.

## Core Assumptions

| Variable | Base Assumption | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Average closed price | $850,000 | User-stated target range is $800K-$900K. |
| Gross commission assumption | 2.5% | Must be replaced with Jen's actual agreement/split model. |
| Approx. GCI per closed client transaction | $21,250 | $850,000 x 2.5%. |
| Retained income estimate | 65%-70% | Placeholder for split, brokerage fees, expenses, referral fees, taxes. |
| Lag from activity to closing | 60-120 days | Pipeline health appears before income does. |

## Annual Production Scenarios

| Scenario | Closings / Month | Annual Closings | Approx. Annual GCI | 65% Retained | 70% Retained |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | 2.0 | 24 | $510,000 | $331,500 | $357,000 |
| Target | 2.5 | 30 | $637,500 | $414,375 | $446,250 |
| Upside | 3.0 | 36 | $765,000 | $497,250 | $535,500 |

## What This Means

If Jen wants consistent $300K+ income, the business should not aim vaguely at "more leads." It should aim for a reliable **24 annual closing conservative path**, a **30 annual closing operating target**, and a **36 annual closing upside path**.

The target should also be listing-led enough to reduce chasing, because seller-side work creates proof, leverage, and team-supported repeatability.

Recommended mix:

| Lane | Share | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Seller/listing-led | 60%-65% | More control, more proof, stronger team value, less random touring. |
| Buyer/referral-led | 35%-40% | Keeps first-time buyer engine alive and feeds seller proof. |

## Monthly Funnel Targets

| Stage | Monthly Target | Why It Matters |
|---|---:|---|
| Qualified new leads or owned contacts | 60-80 | Enough volume without treating every casual click as urgent. |
| Two-way conversations | 24-30 | Real intent appears through replies, not impressions. |
| Appointments set | 8-10 | The consult becomes the conversion point. |
| Consults held | 6-8 | Show-rate protects Jen's time before tours or listing prep. |
| Signed clients | 3-5 | Creates enough active pipeline for 2-3 closings/month with lag. |
| Closings | 2-3 | Final goal, but measured after pipeline health. |

## Conversion Targets

| Link | Target |
|---|---:|
| Outreach/contact rate | 15%-20% |
| Conversation-to-appointment | 30%+ |
| Appointment show rate | 75%+ |
| Consult-to-client | 70%+ |
| Client-to-close | 85%-90% |

If pipeline feels light, do not add content first. Diagnose the lowest-performing link first.

## The Chain

```text
Activities -> Leads -> Conversations -> Appointments -> Signed Clients -> Closings -> Proof -> Referrals -> Re-enter Funnel
```

Every weekly activity should strengthen one of those links. If it does not, it is probably noise.

## Weekly Capacity Model

| Block | Time | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Offense block | Mon-Thu, 90 minutes/day | Hot follow-up, partner leads, past-client check-ins, appointment setting. |
| Consult windows | 2 per week | Buyer strategy, listing prep, seller consults. |
| Optional Saturday window | 1 per week | A-leads, active clients, open-house strategy only. |
| Content/proof blocks | 2 per week | Record from actual work: listing, proof, buyer math, seller lesson. |
| Pipeline review | Friday | Scores, no-show risk, partner asks, stale leads. |
| Family protection check | Sunday, 20 minutes | Set the week and protect JJ time before the calendar fills. |

Evenings are exception-only: active offer, negotiation, inspection crisis, or real client emergency.

## Lead Score Thresholds

| Score | Business Meaning | Action |
|---:|---|---|
| 75+ | High-readiness | Book within 72 hours. |
| 55-74 | Potential, not urgent | Nurture and route to lender/title/market asset. |
| 0-54 | Low readiness | Education only; no time-heavy consult. |

## 72-Hour Appointment Rule

When a lead qualifies, book the consult within 72 hours where possible. Every appointment needs:

1. a clear outcome
2. value-restating confirmation
3. calendar invite
4. short prep note
5. buyer readiness or seller readiness inputs
6. route to the right partner before Jen spends extra time

## Dashboard Math

Use weighted pipeline to avoid emotional guessing:

| Stage | Suggested Weight |
|---|---:|
| Qualified lead | 5% |
| Consult booked | 15% |
| Consult completed | 30% |
| Signed client | 50% |
| Offer/listing active | 70% |
| Under contract | 90% |
| Closed | 100% |

Example:

```text
5 signed buyer/seller clients x $21,250 x 50% = $53,125 weighted GCI
3 under contract x $21,250 x 90% = $57,375 weighted GCI
Weighted pipeline = $110,500
```
