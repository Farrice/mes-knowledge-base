## Corrected Client-Facing Thesis

Jen does not need to become a content machine.

She already has the hard parts:

- A real personality people can remember.
- A live SFV/LA real estate identity.
- Seller-side/team experience.
- A community lane through music, events, and MyBPM/EDC culture.
- A first-time buyer opportunity with real market urgency.

The missing part is the bridge:

**Profile attention -> useful lead magnet -> webinar/email capture -> readiness score -> consult or nurture -> team handoff.**

That is the engine worth showing her.

## Compliance And Claim Boundaries

These claims are safe to use as internal operating assumptions, not public promises:

- The AI system can help reduce manual sorting and follow-up.
- Webinar attendees can become better-qualified leads than random inquiry traffic.
- DM keywords and lead magnets can convert social attention into owned contacts.
- Revenue modeling can show what 24, 30, or 36 closed transactions per year could mean before splits, expenses, taxes, referral fees, and brokerage economics.

These claims need broker, lender, legal, or market-source review before publishing:

- Any affordability, down-payment, DPA, or rate claim.
- Any buyer-agreement or compensation language.
- Any claim about approval likelihood.
- Any demographic targeting language.
- Any production or income projection.
