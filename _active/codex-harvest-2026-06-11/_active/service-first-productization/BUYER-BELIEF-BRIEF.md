# Buyer Belief Brief - AI Content Misfire Audit

## Buyer

Solo experts and creators who sell expertise and already believe content matters, but do not have a reliable path from raw thinking to client conversations.

## Current Beliefs

- "I have ideas, but they do not become useful posts consistently."
- "AI helps me start, then makes me sound generic."
- "People agree with my posts, but I do not know what that should turn into."
- "I do not want ghostwriting. I want leverage without losing my voice."
- "I probably need a system, but I do not want a giant content strategy project."

## Desired Belief Shift

From:

```text
I need more prompts, more content, or more discipline.
```

To:

```text
I need to fix the workflow leak between my expertise, my offer, the AI draft, and the next client ask.
```

## Buyer Words To Listen For

- "generic"
- "doesn't sound like me"
- "I have notes everywhere"
- "I rewrite everything"
- "people like it but nothing happens"
- "I do not know what to ask for"
- "my offer is hard to explain"
- "I want to use AI but not lose my voice"

## Core Objections

| Objection | Reply Direction |
|---|---|
| Is this ghostwriting? | No, it is workflow repair and diagnostic. |
| Can you guarantee leads? | No, this improves clarity and conversation path. |
| I already have prompts. | Prompts need source material, voice, buyer, and offer constraints. |
| I need broader strategy. | Audit first; implementation sprint only if diagnosis proves it. |
| Is $250 worth it? | Compare against the time lost rescuing drafts and publishing unclear posts. |

## Proof Needed

- before/after post angle
- weak AI draft vs constrained rewrite
- buyer-pathway map
- client quote about clarity or usability
- implementation request after diagnostic
