# Reply Handling Tree - AI Content Misfire Audit

> Use for DMs, comments, and warm referrals. Keep the promise practical and proof-safe.

## "What is this?"

```text
Short version: I review your profile, offer, recent content, and any AI drafts that felt wrong. Then I show where the content is leaking trust or buyer clarity, rewrite 3 angles, and give you a simple AI-assisted workflow within 48 hours. Founding diagnostic is $250.
```

## "Is this ghostwriting?"

```text
No. You keep the thinking, voice, approval, and publishing. I am fixing the workflow between raw ideas, AI drafts, offer clarity, and the next client conversation.
```

## "Can you guarantee leads?"

```text
No, and I would not sell it that way. This fixes clarity, voice, buyer path, and CTA. That can support better conversations, but I do not guarantee revenue or booked calls from one audit.
```

## "I already have prompts."

```text
Good. I would look at whether those prompts have enough source material, buyer context, voice guardrails, and offer context to protect your thinking. Most misfires are workflow problems, not one-prompt problems.
```

## "Can you look first?"

```text
Yes. Send your profile, what you sell, one post that sounds like you, and one draft or post that did not work. If the audit is not a fit, I will say that before you pay.
```

## "I need strategy, not AI."

```text
That may be true. The audit can focus on content-to-client flow even if AI is not the main issue. If the real problem is broader positioning, I will name that and keep the diagnostic scoped.
```

## Follow-Up Bump

```text
Quick bump. If your content already turns expertise into clear client conversations, ignore me. If strong thinking is getting flattened or not leading anywhere, this is the leak I am auditing.
```

## Breakup

```text
Closing the loop so I do not keep adding to your inbox. If the content/AI workflow starts feeling expensive in time or missed conversations, the audit will still be here.
```
