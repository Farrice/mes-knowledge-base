14-Day Marching Orders

Day 0

Finish Stripe, Tally, Calendly, sales doc, links, and first 30 prospects. Score each prospect before outreach.

Day 1

Publish process proof. Comment on 10 prospects. Send 5 warm DMs. Start mini-brief #1 notes.

Day 2

Publish misfire Note. Send 10 audit DMs. Track exact buyer language.

Day 3

Publish fake-voice post. Send 10 DMs. DM substantive engagers.

Day 4

Review reply rate. Under 5% means switch to direct failed-draft observations.

Day 5

Publish process receipt. Send 8-10 DMs. Offer $250 founding diagnostics in warm threads.

Days 6-7

Follow up, prep calls, capture proof, publish weekly report, decide keep/change/stop.

Days 8-10

Deliver audit if sold. If not, publish a public mini-audit teardown and tighten segment.

Days 11-14

Close old threads, extract buyer phrases, publish proof/failure audit, choose next sprint.
