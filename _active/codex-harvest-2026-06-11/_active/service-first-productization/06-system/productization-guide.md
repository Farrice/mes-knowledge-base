# Ash Maurya Founder Systems Productization Guide

## Productized Offer 1: Founder Validation Audit

**Promise:** Find the highest-risk assumptions in a founder's idea before they spend months building.

**Inputs:** idea notes, current ICP, current offer, any customer conversations, current pricing, competitor alternatives.

**Process:** `/ash-lean-canvas -> /ash-risk-map -> /ash-problem-validation`

**Output:** one-page business model, ranked risk map, early adopter definition, and next experiment.

**Price Logic:** Anchor against wasted build time, missed revenue, and contractor/developer cost.

## Productized Offer 2: 48-Hour Demo-Sell-Build Sprint

**Promise:** Turn a raw idea into a demo, offer script, and commitment test in two days.

**Inputs:** founder idea, target segment, current alternative, desired outcome, proof assets.

**Process:** `/ash-customer-interview -> /ash-offer-test -> /ash-demo-sell-build`

**Output:** demo outline, problem restatement, mafia offer, pricing anchor, and commitment ladder.

**Price Logic:** Anchor against a failed MVP cycle, not against a landing page or pitch deck.

## Productized Offer 3: Offer Risk Map

**Promise:** Explain why an offer is not converting by mapping push, pull, inertia, and friction.

**Inputs:** sales calls, landing page, offer copy, objections, customer notes, competitor alternatives.

**Process:** `/ash-risk-map -> /ash-offer-test -> /ash-learning-scorecard`

**Output:** force map, blockers, rewritten offer, experiment queue, and decision thresholds.

## Productized Offer 4: Business Model As Product Workshop

**Promise:** Convert a scattered idea into a validation-ready business model and 90-day roadmap.

**Inputs:** founder idea portfolio, constraints, audience, current revenue, build capacity, available proof.

**Process:** `/ash-founder-war-room -> /ash-traction-roadmap -> /ash-productized-validation`

**Output:** business model options, selected validation lane, weekly test cadence, and first paid pilot design.
