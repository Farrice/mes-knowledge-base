# AI Slop Cleanup Productized Service OS Brief

## Thesis

AI Slop Cleanup should be packaged as a small, no-call workflow repair service for consultants, experts, and solo operators who are tired of rewriting generic AI drafts by hand.

The buyer is not buying "better prompts." They are buying relief from the rewrite loop: clearer input constraints, sharper voice guardrails, cleaner drafts, and a repeatable way to turn AI output into usable material.

## Buyer

Best first buyer:

- solo consultants, coaches, creators, and expert-led service providers
- already using AI for content, offers, emails, scripts, or client-facing drafts
- spending too much time rewriting generic output
- worried their AI drafts sound flat, obvious, overconfident, or disconnected from their buyer
- able to buy a small cleanup without procurement or a long sales process

Not the first buyer:

- teams wanting broad AI transformation
- buyers asking for guaranteed leads, followers, revenue, or viral content
- clients with no clear offer, buyer, or sample material
- clients who need regulated legal, medical, financial, or compliance copy without expert review

## Service Promise

Plain-English public promise:

```text
AI Slop Cleanup turns your messy AI drafts into a usable workflow, so your next draft sounds more like you and takes less rewriting.
```

Operator promise:

```text
Within a tight cleanup sprint, diagnose where the AI output is going generic, install better constraints and voice guardrails, repair a small set of draft examples, and hand back a repeatable usable-draft protocol.
```

Avoid public promises about revenue, qualified pipeline, follower growth, lead generation, or a complete content operating system until paid proof supports them.

## Productized Service Contract

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| First value artifact | Slop Scan: a concise diagnosis of where the draft goes generic, why it is happening, and the first repair. |
| Intake | Async form plus uploaded AI drafts, best voice samples, offer notes, buyer notes, and intended use. |
| Queue rule | One cleanup request at a time; founding beta is one focused cleanup, not unlimited revision support. |
| AI role | Cluster symptoms, extract voice patterns, compare before/after drafts, generate candidate constraints, and run QA checks. |
| Human role | Decide what is actually true, buyer-relevant, voice-aligned, and safe to hand off. |
| Quality gate | Slop Check plus Publishable Copy Gate for public-facing copy; no unsupported performance claims. |
| Delivery surface | Rendered conversation document or clean local Markdown deliverable, plus copy-ready repaired examples. |
| Revision policy | One clarification pass for missed context; new assets or broader strategy become a new request or sprint. |
| Pause/cancel rule | One-off cleanup has no subscription lock-in; ongoing support can pause between monthly cleanup cycles. |
| Reuse hook | Each delivery improves the intake, voice guardrail library, constraint stack, and before/after proof bank. |

## Operating System

| Stage | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Intake | Collect offer, buyer, voice samples, weak drafts, desired channel, and constraints. | Cleanup brief. |
| Triage | Decide whether the request is a fit, needs clarification, or should be rejected. | Fit verdict and scope note. |
| Slop Scan | Identify generic language, false confidence, weak buyer path, voice flattening, and missing constraints. | Diagnostic table. |
| Voice Guardrails | Extract what the client's voice does and does not do. | Voice rules and banned patterns. |
| Constraint Stack | Build reusable instructions for audience, offer, claims, examples, tone, and channel. | Usable prompt/workflow block. |
| Draft Repair | Rewrite a small set of examples or content angles. | Before/after examples. |
| QA | Run slop, claim, voice, and usefulness checks. | Pass/fail notes and final edits. |
| Handoff | Explain what changed and how to use it next. | Cleanup deliverable and next 7-day protocol. |
| Proof Capture | Capture before/after, usefulness quote, implementation request, and repeated buyer phrase. | Proof record. |

## Do Not Build Yet If

- no one recognizes the rewrite pain in their own words
- the Slop Scan cannot be delivered from async inputs
- the buyer wants guaranteed leads, growth, or revenue
- every cleanup requires a different consulting process
- the before/after examples are not visibly better
- the proof bank has no paid delivery, quote, or implementation request
