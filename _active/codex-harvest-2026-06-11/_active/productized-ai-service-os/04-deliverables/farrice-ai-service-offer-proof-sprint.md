# Farrice AI Service Offer + Proof Sprint

## Offer Hypothesis

AI Service OS Sprint:

Turn a messy AI-assisted service, workflow, or content operation into a productized delivery system with a clear promise, async intake, fast first artifact, production queue, quality gate, and client handoff.

## Best Buyer

Start with founders, solo experts, consultants, or small agencies who already feel the pain of AI output becoming messy, generic, or hard to deliver consistently.

They are not buying prompts. They are buying a service container that helps them produce trusted work faster.

## V0 Offer Ladder

These are validation hypotheses, not fixed prices.

| Step | Promise | Deliverables | Price Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnostic Brief | Identify where the current AI/workflow/service delivery is leaking trust, speed, or quality. | Source review, bottleneck map, first repair recommendation, proof asset. | $750-$1,500 |
| OS Sprint | Turn one service into a productized delivery system. | Offer one-liner, intake, queue, AI role, QA gate, client handoff, starter assets. | $3,500-$7,500 |
| Support Retainer | Keep the system producing and improving. | Monthly reviews, workflow repairs, asset updates, quality checks. | $2,500-$6,000/month |

Do not lead with the retainer. Earn it from the diagnostic or sprint.

## Proof Sprint

Run this before building a bigger product or public launch.

| Day | Move | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Choose one service lane. | AI Slop Cleanup or AI Workflow Integration as the test lane. |
| 2 | Build the source-backed offer frame. | One-liner, buyer, pain, first artifact, exclusions. |
| 3 | Create the intake. | Questions, asset request list, examples, constraints. |
| 4 | Build the first diagnostic template. | Before/after brief structure and scoring rubric. |
| 5 | Create one sample proof asset. | A realistic teardown or repaired workflow example. |
| 6 | Run the quality gate. | Remove generic claims, unsupported promises, and vague language. |
| 7 | Draft the client-facing page copy. | Headline, promise, deliverables, who it is for, who it is not for. |
| 8 | Draft outreach and conversation prompts. | Human-reviewed scripts only; no sending without approval. |
| 9 | Package the delivery board. | Intake -> triage -> production -> QA -> handoff. |
| 10 | Score buyer proof. | Keep, revise, or stop verdict. |

## First Proof Asset

Create a "before/after AI workflow repair" using a realistic messy input:

- Before: generic AI-generated content/workflow with unclear buyer, weak taste, and no delivery path.
- Diagnosis: where trust leaks, where time is wasted, and where output becomes unusable.
- After: clearer promise, better intake, reusable production steps, and quality gate.

This becomes the first artifact to show a prospect or use internally.

## Quality Gate

The offer passes only if:

- a buyer can understand the promise in one sentence
- the intake can start the work without a call
- the first artifact proves value quickly
- the output has a visible human judgment layer
- the service has a clear stop point
- the pricing is tied to business value, not token usage
- the proof asset makes the transformation obvious

## Risks To Control

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Overpromising revenue | Use proof language, not earnings claims. |
| Becoming generic AI consulting | Anchor every deliverable in a concrete workflow, asset, or handoff. |
| Unlimited scope | Use one request at a time or one sprint outcome. |
| Weak buyer proof | Stop after the sprint if no one cares about the diagnostic. |
| Software competition | Sell judgment, implementation, and managed outcomes, not automation alone. |
