## Do Not Do Yet

- Do not publish the offer.
- Do not message prospects.
- Do not build a subscription or course before paid proof.
- Do not claim DesignJoy-level outcomes.
- Do not add more command surfaces until this one proves useful.
- Do not promise revenue, follower growth, lead generation, qualified pipeline, or a complete content operating system.
