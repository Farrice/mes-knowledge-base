## Extraction Standard

This source is not treated as a summary. It is treated as a copy behavior system:

1. What reader state is being moved?
2. What mechanical move creates that movement?
3. What proof, story, rhythm, or gap makes the reader keep going?
4. What failure does the mechanic prevent?
5. What does the system do differently on weak copy?
