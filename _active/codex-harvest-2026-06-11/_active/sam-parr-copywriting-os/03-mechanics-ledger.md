# Sam Parr Copywriting Mechanics Ledger

## How To Use This Ledger

Pick the smallest mechanic that fixes the real weak link. Do not stack every mechanic into every piece. The goal is sharper behavior, not a Sam Parr costume.

## Mechanics

| Mechanic | Evidence Anchor | Trigger | Rewrite Move | Misuse To Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Desired behavior before words | `00:00:41`, `00:01:01` | Copy sounds fine but has no action. | Write the Action Lock before rewriting. | Polishing lines without knowing what the reader should do. |
| AIDA as reader movement | `00:01:19`, `00:35:17` | Draft has a hook but no progression. | Mark each section as attention, interest, desire, or action; fill the missing state. | Treating AIDA as a rigid template. |
| Familiar energy | `00:02:26`, `00:11:50` | Copy sounds like a formal announcement. | Replace presentation voice with the language of someone already inside the reader's world. | Forced slang or fake intimacy. |
| Headline gravity | `00:03:01`, `00:04:24` | First line is a label, category, or summary. | Generate proof, gap, phrase-twist, and familiar-energy openers before choosing. | Clever headline with no payoff. |
| Mind the gap | `00:05:05`, `00:07:16` | Copy is informative but not pulling. | Open a specific question the reader wants resolved. | Clickbait, mystery, or withholding obvious facts. |
| Interest beats length | `00:07:10`, `00:10:00`, `00:24:30` | Someone wants to cut copy because it is long. | Keep sections that escalate story, proof, tension, desire, or clarity. | Long copy that repeats instead of building. |
| Known phrase, new turn | `00:14:10`, `00:15:37`, `00:16:20` | Hook needs memorability. | Borrow a familiar phrase structure and twist the end toward the offer or belief. | Wordplay that does not sharpen the idea. |
| Punch then pull | `00:16:20`, `00:17:13` | Copy explains before earning attention. | Start with a high-signal punch, then pull with story, proof, and rhythm. | Shock without relevance. |
| Rhythm as propulsion | `00:17:13`, `00:19:52`, `00:20:35` | Correct copy still feels dead. | Vary sentence length, remove clotted transitions, and make the next line easier to read. | Fake short-line theater. |
| Story-led desire | `00:23:14`, `00:24:30`, `00:42:30` | Product appears before the reader cares. | Put contrast, situation, or stakes before the product. | Story that entertains but does not sell the outcome. |
| Copywork rule extraction | `00:27:40`, `00:29:05`, `00:31:20` | Writer needs better instincts. | Manually reproduce an exemplar, mark moves, extract rules, then rewrite. | Saying "inspired by" without learning mechanics. |
| Proof-first ad | `00:33:11`, `00:34:17`, `00:34:46` | Claim is generic or buyer trust is low. | Lead with quote, before/after, metric, artifact, test, or comparison. | Inventing proof or using weak proof for a strong claim. |
| Visual proof translation | `00:38:29`, `00:40:30` | Fact is true but abstract. | Convert the fact into a concrete image, object, comparison, or visible contrast. | Overdramatic comparison that outruns evidence. |
| Objection by detail | `00:40:30`, `00:42:16` | Reader has a private "but what about..." doubt. | Answer through a specific use detail, test, or anecdote. | Defensive FAQ tone. |
| Humor fit | `00:43:56`, `00:44:05`, `00:45:15` | Brand can credibly be funny or warm. | Add humor only when it proves humanity, confidence, or shared context. | Joke imported from another voice. |

## Mechanic Selection

| Weak Link | Use First | Then Consider |
|---|---|---|
| No one would stop | Headline gravity | Known phrase, new turn |
| They stop but do not continue | Mind the gap | Rhythm as propulsion |
| They understand but do not want it | Story-led desire | Interest beats length |
| They want it but do not believe it | Proof-first ad | Visual proof translation |
| They believe it but hesitate | Objection by detail | Price desire |
| The writer cannot repeat the quality | Copywork rule extraction | Copy-hour practice |

## Output Requirement

Every mechanics pass must include:

- original weak line or section,
- selected mechanic,
- rewritten line or section,
- behavior delta,
- proof object or proof gap,
- next gate.
