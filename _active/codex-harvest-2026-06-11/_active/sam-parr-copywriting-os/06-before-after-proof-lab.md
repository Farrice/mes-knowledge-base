# Sam Parr Before/After Proof Lab

## Purpose

This is the acceptance artifact the first build was missing. It proves the extraction can change weak copy, not merely describe copywriting ideas.

## Weak-Copy Fixture

Audience: founder or operator whose AI workflows are producing generic outputs.

Desired action: book a workflow audit.

Available proof: the service reviews prompts, workflow steps, source evidence, and output gates; it returns a repair map and a copy-ready improved workflow.

Weak draft:

```text
Unlock the power of AI automation for your business. Our expert AI workflow audit helps you streamline operations, improve productivity, and get better results from your tools. Book a call today to transform your business with AI.
```

## Diagnosis

| Dimension | Finding |
|---|---|
| Weak link | Attention and belief. |
| Primary failure | Generic benefit language with no proof object. |
| Secondary failure | Product appears before the reader recognizes the specific pain. |
| Reader state gap | The reader has not yet felt "this is my exact problem." |
| Sam mechanics | Headline gravity, proof-first rescue, familiar energy, rhythm, objection by detail. |
| Evidence anchors | `00:03:01`, `00:05:05`, `00:17:13`, `00:33:11`, `00:40:30`. |

## Rewritten Copy

```text
Your AI tools are not broken.

Your workflow is asking them to guess.

In one audit, we map where your prompts lose context, where your source evidence disappears, and where your final output passes even though it still sounds generic.

You get a repair map, a better workflow, and one copy-ready example rebuilt from your own material.

If your team keeps saying "AI is close, but not usable yet," this shows you where the leak is.
```

## Behavior Delta

| Before | After | Behavior Change |
|---|---|---|
| "Unlock the power" starts as a generic category promise. | "Your AI tools are not broken" starts with a recognizable tension. | Reader is more likely to self-identify. |
| Benefits are abstract: streamline, productivity, results. | Proof is operational: prompts, context, source evidence, output gates. | Belief is grounded in inspectable work. |
| CTA arrives before the problem is felt. | CTA implication arrives after the leak is named. | Desire and trust come before action. |
| No objection handling. | Addresses the private objection that AI is close but unusable. | Reduces hesitation without sounding defensive. |
| Rhythm is corporate and even. | Short opener, specific middle, plain close. | Copy has more line-to-line momentum. |

## Copy Gate Pre-Result

| Criterion | Score | Reason |
|---|---:|---|
| Pull | 8 | Stronger pattern interrupt and buyer recognition. |
| Proof | 7 | Concrete process proof exists, but no quantified result is provided. |
| Desire | 8 | The pain and outcome are clearer before the offer. |
| Objection | 7 | Handles "AI is not usable" but could add a client example. |
| Rhythm | 8 | Better sentence movement without fake punchiness. |
| Voice fit | 8 | Plain, operational, not Sam-as-costume. |

## Remaining Risk

The rewrite still needs real client proof, a named example, or a result metric before it should claim high publishability. Send it to `/publishable-copy-gate` before public use.
