# Hybrid Morning Signal Radar Operating Brief

## Purpose

Give Farrice a 6:30 AM operating brief that turns market signal into one clear execution path for the day.

The brief should answer:

1. What should I pay attention to today?
2. Why does my ICP care?
3. Who should I review or reach out to manually?
4. What should I post, comment, or ask for?
5. Is the research budget safe?

## Daily Output

The morning radar should produce:

- Apify budget status
- top prospect segments to inspect
- reviewed prospect queue
- three content angles
- strategic comment prompts
- manual connection message angles
- one 45-minute execution loop
- any budget warnings or skipped paid/API sources

## Segment Priority

### 1. Founder-led experts

Examples:

- business coaches
- executive coaches
- consultants
- fractional CMOs
- brand strategists
- offer strategists
- expert service providers

Why:

They feel AI correction pain quickly because delivery depends on founder judgment.

### 2. AI-forward agencies

Examples:

- AI automation agencies
- AI consulting boutiques
- marketing automation agencies
- workflow consultants

Why:

They understand the AI problem faster, but may be more skeptical and more crowded.

### 3. Local premium services

Examples:

- boutique marketing agencies
- premium consultants
- brand studios
- web design agencies
- business consultants

Why:

They may have urgent workflow and content pain, and public business listings make compliant research easier.

## Daily Budget Policy

Use the existing Apify monthly budget guard:

- Soft warning: $20.30 spent.
- Hard stop: $26.10 spent.
- Monthly plan: $29.00.

Add a daily radar cap:

- Default daily cap: $0.20.
- Default morning run target: under $0.10.
- If today already used more than the daily cap, skip Apify and create a fallback brief.
- If monthly state is yellow, use only cheap actors or skip paid research.
- If monthly state is red, do not run Apify.

## Automation Boundary

Allowed:

- public web research
- Google Maps/public listing discovery
- Reddit/topic research when relevant
- website enrichment
- prospect queue generation
- outreach angle drafting
- content angle drafting

Not allowed:

- LinkedIn scraping
- auto-DMs
- auto-comments
- auto-likes
- fake engagement
- pretending Farrice has read work he has not reviewed

## 45-Minute Morning Loop

### 5 minutes: Read the brief

Pick one strongest angle and one prospect lane.

### 15 minutes: Manual prospect review

Review 5 to 10 prospects. Mark:

- worth connecting
- needs more research
- not a fit

### 15 minutes: Strategic comments

Use the comment prompts on relevant posts Farrice actually reads.

### 5 minutes: One warm connection message

Send only if there is a real reason to connect.

### 5 minutes: Signal tracking

Log:

- person
- source
- pain language
- next action
- whether they submitted an asset

## Morning Brief Quality Gate

| Gate | Pass Standard |
|---|---|
| Budget transparency | Shows monthly state and daily cap status |
| Prospect usefulness | Names a specific manual review queue |
| Content usefulness | Gives post/comment angles tied to AI Misfire Map |
| Safety | No LinkedIn automation or fake engagement |
| Actionability | Ends with one 45-minute execution loop |

## Fallback Mode

If Apify cannot run because of budget, network, auth, or actor errors:

1. Keep the morning brief alive.
2. Clearly mark Apify as skipped.
3. Use existing local strategy assets.
4. Produce manual research prompts.
5. Do not fabricate current trend claims.
