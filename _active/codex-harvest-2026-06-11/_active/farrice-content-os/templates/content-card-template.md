## Statuses

`captured -> researched -> queued -> drafted -> hook_pass -> taste_pass -> copy_gate -> visual_ready -> weekly_ready -> service_ready -> archived`

## Required Before `weekly_ready`

- Context Lock complete.
- Research claim evidence captured.
- Body-first draft exists.
- Hook Lab selected hook.
- Writers' room pass complete.
- Taste Evidence Ledger complete.
- Anti-slop and publishable-copy gate result captured when public or revenue-facing.
