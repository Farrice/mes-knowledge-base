## Hook Gate

No serious post advances to polish until it has:

- At least 10 hook candidates.
- Top 3 ranked.
- One selected hook.
- Rejected generic hooks with reasons.
- Mobile cutoff check.
- Farrice voice check.
- Anti-slop check.
