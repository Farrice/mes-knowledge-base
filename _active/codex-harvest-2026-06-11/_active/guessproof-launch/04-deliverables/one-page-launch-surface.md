## The Problem

The draft looks polished.

The sales email sounds fine.

The proposal section is structured.

The SOP technically works.

But your judgment says no.

So you rewrite it. Again.

That rescue work is not a personal failure. It is evidence. It shows the decision rules, source material, proof standards, audience context, and review boundaries the AI was missing.

The rewrite is the signal.

## What I Do

I map where AI was forced to guess and turn the missing judgment into reusable workflow assets.

That can include:

- Source-of-truth rules.
- Decision boundaries.
- Better instructions.
- SOP patches.
- Validation checks.
- Human review points.
- Reusable execution kits.

The goal is not to make AI sound smarter. The goal is to stop asking AI to invent the parts of the work that should be governed by your expertise.
