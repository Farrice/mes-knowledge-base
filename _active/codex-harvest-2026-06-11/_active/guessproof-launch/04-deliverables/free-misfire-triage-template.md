# Free Misfire Triage Template

Use this for the free `RESCUE` response. Keep it short. The free triage should create belief and fit, not deliver the full paid audit.

## Intake Required

Ask for:

- The AI output they had to rescue.
- The original prompt or instruction, if available.
- The intended audience.
- The context the AI was supposed to use.
- What they changed before sending, if they know.

If they only send the output, still triage it, but mark missing inputs clearly.

## Response Format

Subject line or opener:

> I mapped the first three places this was forced to guess.

Then send:

### 1. Where AI Guessed

Use one specific observation.

Example:

> It guessed at the buyer's real pain. The draft says "improve marketing," but your edit points toward a quality-control problem: client-facing AI output still needs founder rescue.

### 2. What Judgment Is Missing

Name the hidden expertise.

Example:

> The missing judgment is the difference between a generic benefit and the risk that makes this urgent: you are protecting client trust, not just trying to make the copy sound better.

### 3. Fit Verdict

Pick one:

| Verdict | Use When |
|---|---|
| Prompt issue | The input is vague, but the workflow is otherwise simple. |
| SOP issue | The work needs repeatable instructions and examples. |
| Workflow issue | The task requires sources, decision boundaries, and validation steps. |
| Not ready | The buyer cannot define the source material, decision owner, or success criteria yet. |

Example:

> Verdict: workflow issue. A better prompt will help, but the repeat failure is that the AI does not have your source-of-truth rules or decision boundaries.

## Bridge To Paid Audit

Use this only if the artifact shows a recurring, valuable pattern.

> This is fixable, but I would not start with a prompt pack. I would map the full misfire pattern first: where the AI guessed, what expert judgment is missing, what source rules it needs, and what validation checks belong before it goes out. That is the $750 AI Misfire Map. It is a 48-hour audit of one recurring AI-assisted workflow, and if you move into a sprint within 7 days, part of it credits toward the build.

## If It Is Not A Fit

> I would not pay to systemize this yet. The missing piece is not the AI workflow; it is the source material. First, write down the examples, rules, or buyer context the AI should have used. Once that exists, the misfire will be much easier to map.

## Free Triage Quality Bar

Pass if:

- It includes exactly three diagnostic bullets.
- It names one concrete misfire.
- It protects the buyer's status.
- It gives a fit verdict.
- It does not repair the entire workflow for free.

Fail if:

- It becomes a full audit.
- It gives generic AI advice.
- It says "just write a better prompt."
- It makes guaranteed outcome claims.
