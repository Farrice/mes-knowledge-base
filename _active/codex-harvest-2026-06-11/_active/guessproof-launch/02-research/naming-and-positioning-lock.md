# Naming And Positioning Lock

This locks the offer language so the launch does not drift into a generic AI service, prompt-pack pitch, or vague operating-system promise.

## Final Category

Use:

> AI Workflow Implementation for expert-led service businesses.

Do not lead with:

- Prompt engineering.
- AI automation.
- Agent-ready systems.
- Skill stacks.
- Passive income.
- AI operating system.

Reason:

Buyers already understand workflows, implementation, and service quality. "Agent-ready" and "skill stack" are useful differentiators after the pain is understood, but they are too insider-coded for cold entry.

## Naming Ladder

| Layer | Name | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Free entry | AI Misfire Map | Names the pain and invites artifact submissions. |
| Paid audit | AI Misfire Map | Makes the free asset deeper, paid, and diagnostic. |
| Alternate paid audit label | AI Misfire Audit | Use when the buyer wants a more formal/service-business frame. |
| DFY sprint | Guessproof Workflow Sprint | Names the mechanism and paid implementation. |
| Premium install | Guessproof Skill Stack Install | Use only after buyer understands the implementation value. |
| Proof artifact | Guessproof Receipt | Shows before/after, captured rules, validation checks, and review points. |

## Expert Composition Decisions

| Slot | Decision |
|---|---|
| Naming | Lead with AI Misfire because the buyer recognizes the failure before they understand the mechanism. Use Guessproof as the memorable proprietary method. |
| Positioning | Enter a known category first. AI Workflow Implementation is clear; agent-ready is the differentiator. |
| Belief change | Reframe the rewrite as evidence of hidden expertise, not evidence that the buyer is bad at AI. |
| Offer economics | Sell safety, quality control, and status protection from client-facing AI misfires. Do not sell "AI help." |
| LinkedIn acquisition | Optimize for artifact submissions. The public action is DM `RESCUE` with a real misfire. |
| Proof gate | Do not make broad launch claims until Proof Demo 001 exists and can be understood quickly. |

## Public Language

Use:

- "Your AI output looks finished, but you still have to rescue it."
- "The rewrite is the signal."
- "I map where AI was forced to guess."
- "I capture the hidden judgment behind the rescue."
- "I turn one recurring misfire into a reusable workflow kit with validation."
- "Send the misfire first. No call first."

Avoid:

- "I will automate your business."
- "I will replace your team."
- "I will build passive income AI products."
- "Never rewrite AI again."
- "Guaranteed leads."
- "Guaranteed revenue."
- "Perfect AI output."

More direct:

More premium:

More operator-led:

## Buyer Recognition Test

The buyer should understand AI Misfire before Guessproof is explained.

Pass:

- "I have that exact problem."
- "I keep rewriting the same kind of AI output."
- "I can send you an example."

Fail:

- "What is a skill stack?"
- "Is this a course?"
- "Is this a prompt pack?"
- "Is this for passive income?"
