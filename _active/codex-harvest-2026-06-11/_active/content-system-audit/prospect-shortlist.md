- Use visible public material only.

## Fast Search Prompts

Use these manually in LinkedIn, Substack, X, or your existing network:

- `"ChatGPT" "generic" "content" consultant`
- `"AI drafts" "LinkedIn" coach`
- `"content" "clients" fractional CMO`
- `"I help" consultant "LinkedIn" "ChatGPT"`
- `"creator" "consulting" "AI" "content"`
- commenters on posts about AI writing, ghostwriting, content systems, and "doesn't sound like me"

## Best First Prospect Type

Start with people who already believe content matters and already sell expertise. The goal is not to convince someone to care about content. The goal is to catch someone who feels the leak and wants a fast fix.
