# Intake Form - AI Content Misfire Audit + 48-Hour Fix

> **Tool**: Tally, Typeform, Google Form, or any simple intake form.
> **Length**: 10 questions, 7-10 minutes.
> **Purpose**: collect enough context to diagnose content/offer leaks and AI voice-flattening before the 48-hour fix.

## Form Header

**Title:** AI Content Misfire Audit - Intake

**Description:**

Send the raw material: profile, offer, recent posts, ideas, and AI drafts that did not work. The goal is to find where your expertise is getting flattened or disconnected from the buying path, then rebuild a simple workflow you can use immediately.

## Questions

### Q1: What do you sell?

Short answer. Ask for the plain-language offer, not the brand story.

### Q2: Who is the buyer?

Long answer. Ask for their best current guess at the person who should care.

### Q3: Paste your main profile, site, or public page.

URL field, required.

### Q4: Paste 3-5 recent posts, drafts, or ideas.

Long answer or file/URL upload.

### Q5: Paste 1-2 AI drafts that felt wrong, generic, or unusable.

Optional but strongly recommended.

### Q6: Paste 1-2 pieces that actually sound like you.

Optional but strongly recommended.

### Q7: Where do you think the content is failing?

Multiple choice plus optional explanation:

- people agree but do not inquire
- the offer is unclear
- AI makes the voice generic
- ideas stay scattered
- posts do not create conversations
- I do not know what to ask for
- I am not sure

### Q8: What would make this audit worth it?

Finish the sentence: "This would be worth it if I left with..."

### Q9: What do you want readers to do next?

Examples: reply, book a call, ask for a resource, buy a diagnostic, join a waitlist, request a proposal.

### Q10: Best contact method.

Email, LinkedIn URL, or preferred channel.
