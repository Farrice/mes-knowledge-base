# RISKS - AI Content Misfire Audit + 48-Hour Fix

> Review daily during the first 72-hour test.

## R1 - The Offer Drifts Into Broad Strategy (HIGH)

**Risk:** A $250 diagnostic becomes a full positioning, brand, AI ops, and content strategy project.

**Mitigation:** Keep the deliverable to the profile/offer/content leak, buyer-pathway map, 3 rewritten angles, simple AI workflow, and CTA path. Move tool installation or deeper strategy into the $750 implementation sprint.

**Status:** Open.

## R2 - Copy Overpromises Client Conversations (HIGH)

**Risk:** "Creates more client conversations" could be read as a guaranteed acquisition result.

**Mitigation:** Use "clearer client conversations," "conversation-ready," and "direct reply path." Do not guarantee leads, revenue, followers, virality, or booked calls.

**Status:** Open.

## R3 - Buyer Thinks This Is A Prompt Pack (MEDIUM)

**Risk:** The AI workflow output gets mistaken for cheap prompts.

**Mitigation:** Lead with diagnosis and custom workflow repair. Prompts are a byproduct of the diagnosis, not the thing being sold.

**Status:** Open.

## R4 - Buyer Thinks This Is Ghostwriting (MEDIUM)

**Risk:** Creators and experts may assume "content fix" means done-for-you writing.

**Mitigation:** State clearly: this is not ghostwriting. The buyer keeps voice, approval, and publishing control.

**Status:** Open.

## R5 - Too Much Long-Term Identity Language (HIGH)

**Risk:** Greatest Drivers, AI Operating Partner, and deep category language can make the first paid offer feel abstract.

**Mitigation:** Use those as internal positioning only. Public offer stays practical: profile, offer, recent content, AI drafts, 48-hour fix.

**Status:** Open.

## R6 - Prospect List Is Too Broad (MEDIUM)

**Risk:** "Creators" becomes too wide and pulls in people who cannot pay or do not sell expertise.

**Mitigation:** Only contact creators with a clear offer, consulting path, paid education product, coaching, advisory, templates, workshops, or services.

**Status:** Open.

## R7 - No Proof After First Delivery (HIGH)

**Risk:** You fulfill the audit but do not capture before/after evidence.

**Mitigation:** Use `proof-capture-template.md` for every delivery. No proof capture means the offer did not compound.

**Status:** Open.
