# Objection Handles - AI Content Misfire Audit + 48-Hour Fix

> Keep the offer narrow: diagnose the content-to-client leak, repair the AI-assisted workflow, and give the buyer clearer post angles and CTAs.

## 1. "Is this ghostwriting?"

No. You still own the thinking, voice, approval, and publishing.

This finds where your current content/AI process is losing trust or clarity, then gives you a workflow and examples you can keep using. I am not becoming your writer. I am fixing the system that turns your expertise into publishable, buyer-relevant content.

## 2. "Can you guarantee leads?"

No. I do not sell lead guarantees.

The promise is a clearer content system: sharper buyer path, less AI-flattened voice, stronger angles, and a better direct ask. That can support client conversations, but I will not pretend one audit controls the market.

## 3. "I already have prompts."

Good. Then the audit can look at whether the prompts have enough context to protect your voice, offer, buyer path, and examples.

Most weak AI drafts are not caused by one bad prompt. They come from missing source material and loose constraints.

## 4. "I need a full content strategy."

Maybe, but that is not the first sale here.

This audit finds the first leak and gives you the next usable workflow. If the diagnostic shows you need deeper strategy or tool installation, that becomes the 48-hour implementation sprint.

## 5. "Why $250?"

Because this is not a template pack.

I am reviewing your actual profile, offer, content, and AI drafts, then sending a specific diagnosis, 3 rewritten angles, and a workflow you can use. Founding price is $250 while I build proof. Standard diagnostic is $300 after that.

## 6. "Can I just send you my profile first?"

Yes. Send:

1. your profile or site
2. one post that sounds like you
3. one draft or post that did not work
4. what you sell

If the audit is not a fit, I will say that before you pay.

## 7. "I do not use AI much."

That is fine if the content still has a system problem.

The audit can focus on content-to-client flow: profile, offer clarity, buyer path, angles, and CTA. The AI workflow becomes lighter, not forced.

## 8. "I do not have a big audience."

You do not need a big audience for this to be useful.

This is for making the content clearer and more conversation-ready with the audience you already reach. It is not an audience-growth campaign.

## 9. "I am worried it will make me sound formulaic."

That is the exact failure the audit is designed to avoid.

The workflow starts from your strong samples, examples, language, and judgment. The point is not to impose a formula. The point is to stop AI or templates from sanding off what makes your thinking recognizable.

## 10. "I need to think about it."

Totally fair. What are you weighing: fit, price, timing, or whether the content problem is painful enough?

If fit is the question, send one strong post and one weak draft. I can usually tell quickly whether this audit would help.
