# DM Scripts - AI Content Misfire Audit + 48-Hour Fix

> **Rule**: personalize every opener with one real observation. Do not send generic pitch blasts.
> **CTA keyword**: MISFIRE.
> **Price to mention**: $250 founding diagnostic unless a warmer, lower-friction beta price is needed for a strong fit.

## First 10 Direct Asks

Use these as the first batch. Replace brackets before sending.

```text
[Name], your post on [specific topic] had a real idea in it. The part I would be curious about is whether the content is creating a clear path back to your offer, or just getting agreement. I am testing a $250 AI Content Misfire Audit for solo experts: profile, offer, recent posts, then a 48-hour fix with 3 sharper angles and a buyer-ready workflow. Worth sending the one-pager?
```

I am doing an AI Content Misfire Audit: I find where AI is flattening the voice/offer and rebuild a simple workflow in 48 hours. $250 founding round. Want the breakdown?

I am testing a 48-hour audit for solo experts/creators: diagnose the content-to-client leak, rewrite 3 angles, and install a simple AI-assisted workflow. Want me to send it?

## Follow-Ups

### Bump

```text
Quick bump. If your content system is already turning ideas into clear client conversations, ignore me. If it feels like strong thinking is getting flattened before it creates a next step, this audit is built for that.
```

### Proof-Oriented Follow-Up

```text
One useful way to tell if this fits: send one post that sounds like you and one AI/content draft you did not use. If the gap is obvious, I can tell you exactly what the audit would fix before you pay.
```

### Breakup

```text
Closing the loop so I do not keep adding to your inbox. If the content/AI workflow starts feeling expensive in time or missed conversations, the audit will still be here.
```

## Reply Handling

| Reply | Send |
|---|---|
| "What is it?" | Send the sales page and a 2-line fit explanation. |
| "How much?" | "$250 for founding diagnostics, 48-hour turnaround. Standard will be $300 after proof." |
| "Can you guarantee leads?" | "No. I do not sell lead guarantees. I fix the content/AI workflow so expertise becomes clearer, more specific, and easier to respond to." |
| "I already use AI." | "Good. This is for people already using it but still rescuing or rewriting the output." |
| "Can you look first?" | Ask for profile, one strong post, one weak/AI draft, and their offer. |
