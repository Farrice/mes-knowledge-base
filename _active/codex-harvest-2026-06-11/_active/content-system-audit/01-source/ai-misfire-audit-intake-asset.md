## Public Page Copy

Your AI may not be the problem.

It may be working from the wrong layer.

Most teams give AI prompts, SOPs, docs, transcripts, and examples. That helps, but it still leaves out the operating knowledge that makes the work actually work:

- customer language
- founder judgment
- quality standards
- edge cases
- decision rules
- source-of-truth rules
- approval boundaries
- examples of what "good" and "wrong" look like

When that knowledge is missing, AI guesses.

The output might look polished.
It might even sound correct.
But someone still has to rescue it.

That is the misfire.
