# Cross-Domain Kishotenketsu Playbook

## Domain Map

| Domain | Use The OS To | Common Failure | Proof Scenario |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fiction | Build a scene without forcing a villain: world, ritual, variable, integration. | "Nothing happens" because contrast is not staged. | A flat argument scene becomes a textured perspective-shift scene. |
| Social media | Use a familiar situation, then introduce a non-hostile variable that reframes it. | Manufactured contrarianism without texture. | A generic LinkedIn lesson becomes a post where one observed detail changes the whole lesson. |
| Content | Treat process as the retention engine: work, environment, sequence, friction, and adaptation. | Over-compressed advice that skips the human scene. | A "5 tips" article becomes a process-led piece with a felt before, middle, and after. |
| Ads | Hook with a real-world contrast, then reveal the changed variable that makes the offer feel timely. | Fake urgency, fake enemy, or cleverness with no proof path. | A generic paid ad becomes a buyer-world scene with a mechanism and CTA. |
| Media | Structure scripts, newsletters, podcasts, and video essays around state, process, turn, and integration. | Topic coverage without an experiential arc. | A flat explainer becomes a guided perspective shift. |
| Creative strategy | Build campaigns around coexistence of tensions: old/new, premium/access, control/play, tradition/disruption. | A clever contrast line with no world underneath. | A brief shifts from "beat competitor X" to "show how two customer truths coexist." |
| Branding | Design the brand as a state of being with rituals, symbols, language, sensory cues, and proof scenes. | Positioning as adjectives without behavior. | A brand foundation becomes a world map: tensions, rituals, objects, and adaptation moments. |
| Marketing | Replace villain/problem-agitation with variable/adaptation when the audience does not want battlefield energy. | Fake urgency or over-villainized status quo. | A launch page shifts from "your current way is broken" to "your environment changed, adapt this way." |
| Messaging | Turn benefits into a changed-world explanation: what used to work, what changed, what now integrates. | Benefit soup or generic transformation language. | A messaging house becomes a variable/adaptation map. |
| Positioning | Use contrast to locate the offer in a new category reality without overclaiming. | "We are better than X" without a buyer-world reason. | A positioning note becomes a category shift with rituals, proof, and boundaries. |
| Product/service/concept work | Make the offer understandable by showing the lived process it changes. | Abstract concept language that never becomes a usable world. | A concept brief becomes a story of use, friction, variable, and new operating ritual. |
| Copywriting | Make proof do the plot work through sensory before/after details, hidden variable, and perspective shift. | Direct-response pressure without emotional realism. | A sales section shifts from "defeat pain" to "carry the real constraint differently with this mechanism." |
| Ghostwriting | Capture lived texture: repeated phrases, rooms, rituals, scars, contradictions, and old memories seen newly. | Generic authority voice with no private-recognition moment. | An abstract founder lesson becomes a scene where one concrete memory reveals the point. |
| General writing | Use Ki-Sho-Ten-Ketsu as a diagnostic for missing state, process, turn, or integration. | Applying it as a rigid four-part template. | Mark a flat draft by state, process, turn, integration; revise only the missing layer first. |

## Application Recipes

### Fiction Scene

1. Identify the fake fight or missing pressure.
2. Define the world state and its rules.
3. Render one process the reader can follow.
4. Introduce a variable that changes meaning.
5. End with integration, not a win.

### LinkedIn Or Social Post

1. Open with a lived contrast instead of a take.
2. Show the process or ritual that reveals the problem.
3. Shift perspective with one line that changes the frame.
4. Close with an integrated action or question.
5. Run Kallaway/Shaan only if the platform needs more pull.

### Brand Or Creative Strategy

1. Name the world the audience wants to inhabit.
2. Name the contrast: old operating reality vs new reality.
3. Show the rituals, materials, rules, characters, and signals.
4. Introduce the variable that makes the old world insufficient.
5. Make the brand the integration layer, not the hero.

### Marketing And Copy

1. Replace enemy language with environmental change.
2. Render the buyer's current process in concrete detail.
3. Introduce the variable that makes adaptation necessary.
4. Show the mechanism as the natural new process.
5. Keep proof and CTA explicit after the story earns desire.

### Ads And Media

1. Start with the audience's recognizable world, not a slogan.
2. Introduce one contrast or variable that makes the old process feel newly insufficient.
3. Show the product, service, idea, or message as the integration path.
4. Keep the claim bounded, the proof visible, and the CTA clean.
5. Run platform, conversion, and publishable-copy gates before external use.

### Messaging, Positioning, And Concept Work

1. Name the old operating reality without insulting it.
2. Name the variable that changed the category, buyer, workflow, or culture.
3. Show the rituals, objects, language, or process that make the new reality felt.
4. Position the brand, product, service, or concept as the integration layer.
5. Keep a boundary line: what this is, what it is not, and when another frame is better.

### Ghostwriting

1. Extract the client's real scene, not the lesson.
2. Preserve their vocabulary, rhythm, and worldview.
3. Find the variable that changed how they saw the scene.
4. Let the perspective shift produce the lesson.
5. End with something carried forward, not a motivational bow.

## Quality Gate

A cross-domain application passes when the reader can name the variable, feel the texture, follow the process, experience the perspective shift, and leave with a changed but not over-solved understanding.
