# Satori Graphics — Mastery Extraction

## Executive Summary
- **Core Genius**: Satori treats graphic design as visual communication under psychological control: the designer’s job is to make meaning visible, guide attention deliberately, and remove anything that cannot justify its role.
- **What Makes Them Different**: He is anti-template without being anti-tool. AI can supply clean output, but the professional designer adds intent, controlled imperfection, emotional foresight, memory hooks, and transferability.
- **Deployable Skills**: Design audits, anti-amateur refinement, poster concepts, layout choreography, grid specs, LIFT diagnostics, logo concept strategy, client presentation structure, and AI workflow upgrades.
- **Hidden Knowledge Captured**: Strong design is rarely “more design.” It is clearer message, stronger focal control, better emotional aftermath, and fewer unsupported decisions.

## Genius Patterns

### Why Before What
- **Unconscious Behavior**: Every element must answer why it exists before it is allowed into the design.
- **Executable Behavior**: Remove decoration, effects, icons, motifs, or layout moves that do not sharpen message, emotion, or eye path.
- **Success Metric**: A viewer can state the message before admiring the style.

### Humanized Template Disruption
- **Unconscious Behavior**: Clean symmetrical layouts are intentionally disrupted with overlap, tension, asymmetry, controlled imbalance, or tactile detail.
- **Executable Behavior**: Start from the clean version, then add one purposeful human decision that AI would not invent safely.
- **Success Metric**: The piece feels crafted instead of generated.

### Predictive Empathy
- **Unconscious Behavior**: Design for what the viewer should feel after contact, not only what grabs them now.
- **Executable Behavior**: Set the post-view emotion, then tune headline tone, breathing room, color state, and trust cues around it.
- **Success Metric**: The viewer feels understood rather than pressured.

### Memory Encoding
- **Unconscious Behavior**: Create a small unresolved metaphor or visual twist that makes the brain pause.
- **Executable Behavior**: Turn the topic into a metaphor, absence, substitution, or object relationship the viewer has to resolve.
- **Success Metric**: The concept can be remembered from one sentence or thumbnail.

### LIFT Layout Control
- **Unconscious Behavior**: Diagnose layout through leverage point, internal rhythm, friction/flow, and transferability.
- **Executable Behavior**: Establish the dominant focal point, choreograph eye movement, add measured tension, then test the layout across sizes and formats.
- **Success Metric**: The hierarchy survives busy composition, thumbnail reduction, and format changes.

### Logo As Memory Hook
- **Unconscious Behavior**: A logo is not forced to tell the whole brand story.
- **Executable Behavior**: Compress the brief into one sentence, choose a verb-led action, and build a simple recognizable mark with shape psychology.
- **Success Metric**: The mark is easy to recognize, easy to remember, and aligned with the brand’s target feeling.

## Hidden Knowledge
- **Good-looking can still fail**: If the message is unclear, beauty is not design competence.
- **Friction is not automatically bad**: Tight spacing, jarring objects, awkward type, missing pieces, and disruption can work when they clarify emphasis.
- **Whitespace has scale**: Macro whitespace controls zones and breathing room; micro whitespace controls text, proximity, and close element relationships.
- **Grid obedience earns grid rebellion**: Breaking a grid only creates impact when enough of the design obeys the grid first.
- **Speed does not repair weak thinking**: Faster tools only accelerate the existing concept quality.
- **Client presentation creates decision bias**: Concepts must use the same presentation structure so the idea, not the mockup drama, wins.

## Hall of Fame Exemplars
- **Perfume Hero Humanization**: A clean AI hero becomes crafted when the bottle overlaps typography, a background text logo is blended subtly, and detail lines connect product to pricing and information.
- **AI Awareness Poster**: A generic AI face with tech decoration loses to a simple hand-drawn Thinker figure with an off-switch head because the second design makes human creativity visible.
- **High-Fidelity Music Poster**: Records arranged as a heart turn objects into emotion, creating a memory hook instead of a neutral grid of assets.
- **Logo Presentation System**: Every logo concept is shown in the same long structured format with thinking, iterations, color choices, and context, removing client bias.

## Signature Moves
- **Ask “What is this saying?” first**: Deploy when a design looks good but feels empty.
- **Evict non-paying elements**: Deploy when decoration, effects, or motifs accumulate.
- **Set the after-feeling**: Deploy before color, typography, or headline tone choices.
- **Break the grid after proving the grid**: Deploy when a layout needs impact without chaos.
- **Flip the design upside down**: Deploy during technical QA to inspect structure, spacing, and alignment without reading content.
- **Compress the brand into one sentence**: Deploy before logo sketching.
- **Use verbs before nouns**: Deploy when logo ideas drift toward generic objects.

## Quality Rubric
| Criterion | Score 4 | Score 7 | Score 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Message Clarity | Looks acceptable but message is vague | Main message is understandable | Message is immediate and visually embodied |
| Intentionality | Some elements are decorative | Most elements have a job | Every element pays rent |
| Eye Path | Viewer can navigate with effort | Hierarchy is mostly controlled | Eye journey is choreographed |
| Emotional Fit | Style is attractive but emotionally generic | Tone supports the brief | Post-view emotion is engineered |
| Memory | Design is competent but forgettable | One memorable motif exists | Concept creates a durable mental hook |
| Friction Control | Tension feels accidental | Tension adds emphasis | Friction and flow are balanced with precision |
| Transferability | Works only in one mockup | Works across major formats | Identity survives thumbnail, mobile, print, and campaign variants |

- **AI Refinement Layer**: Turn generic generated layouts into human-directed designs.
- **Logo Sprint Upgrade**: Compress briefs, generate verb-led concepts, and present options without bias.
