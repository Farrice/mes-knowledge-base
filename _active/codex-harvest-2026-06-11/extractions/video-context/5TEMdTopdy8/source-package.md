## Extracted Operating Method

1. **Identify the current category.** Find what people compare the brand to and the rules they assume.
2. **Map the hidden rules.** Capture pricing, proof, tone, behavior, content, and opportunity expectations.
3. **Define the unfair intersection.** Use real proof from background, method, worldview, assets, and behavior.
4. **Give it language.** A category does not travel until people can name and repeat it.
5. **Prove it repeatedly.** Use rituals, examples, content, before/after cases, and audience adoption signals.
6. **Avoid the gimmick trap.** A category must be grounded in truth, not novelty.
