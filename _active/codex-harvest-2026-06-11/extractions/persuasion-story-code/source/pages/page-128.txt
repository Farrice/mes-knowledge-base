Story vs. Story, And How I Discovered Persuasion Stories
We started with the distinction between dramatic stories (Story) and
persuasion stories (story). Persuasion stories are most commonly used in
conversation, and most effective to increase another person's willingness
to agree. All of us tell at least a few persuasion stories every day, often
without realizing we're doing so. But usually we don't tell them nearly as
effectively as we could.
Increasing your awareness of what persuasion stories actually are, and
improving your skill in telling and writing better ones, will help you
become far more effective.
Three main differences between the two types of stories:
• Persuasion stories are much shorter than dramatic stories.
• Persuasion stories do not attempt to teach universal life lessons, as
dramatic stories often do.
• Persuasion stories help one person persuade another person (or a group
of people) in a meaningful way.
