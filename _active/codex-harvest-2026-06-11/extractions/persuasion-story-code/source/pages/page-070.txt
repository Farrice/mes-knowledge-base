The company hired me to write a direct mail sales letter. Please
understand, all this took place before the internet provided an easy way
to book flights. We were years away from Expedia and Hotwire and
Kayak. Airplane tickets were still almost entirely printed on paper, by a
special machine only airline employees and travel agents could use.
The direct mail letter was a roaring success—the highest-revenue copy I
have ever written. Abacus's CEO, Bonnie Levine, also happened to be a
CPA. She calculated that the new clients they got from this letter ended
up spending $40 million with Abacus.
In the letter, here's the story I told to explain Abacus's unfamiliar new
idea:
Our business, Abacus Travel Management, specializes in
providing the best in flight schedules, hotel accommodations, and
out of town entertainment at the lowest possible price.
How? Through expert knowledge. Duane Goucher, our vice
president, has years of extensive sales and management
experience with two major airlines.
He and I make sure our staff is specially trained and thoroughly
up to date in key "insider" travel industry skills that many other
travel agents know little about.
If you want limousine service in a distant city, we will make sure
one is there to meet your flight whenever it arrives.
We monitor rapidly changing fares and issue clients refund checks
if the fare goes down before the plane leaves.
We research special meeting sites, restaurants and entertainment
events to help our clients do business better when they're out of
town.
And we even fly out "on our nickel" to scout meeting sites for our
business clients when they request it.
As far as I know, there's no other travel service that even comes
close to what we do. But I could be biased. I invite you to make an
appointment and come judge for yourself.
