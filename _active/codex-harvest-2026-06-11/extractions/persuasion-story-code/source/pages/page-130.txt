Stories About What Your Prospect Is Experiencing (Chapter 4)
These stories work well to deepen the human connection between
yourself and a prospect. They have to do with what you know your
prospect is experiencing. It's better to know than to guess. So listening or
research—or both—will serve you well in coming up with stories like
these that your prospect will accept as authentic and sincere.
Stories that work best here are not ones where you talk about what you
imagine, especially if you don't have personal experience yourself.
Instead, what you are looking for is what people describe and how they
describe it. What they actually say in real life.
We covered four types of stories about your prospect's experience:
• Stories About Your Prospect's Pain
• Stories About The World Today
• Stories About The Pain You Know Personally
• Stories About Pressures From Other People
One thing that's worth reminding yourself: People are motivated by
emotion, not logic. And one thing you can count on these stories to do is
to create emotion in your prospect. That's because they remind the
prospect of the facts and events in their own lives that created emotion
for them. Or, in the case of stories about the world today, they put into
sharp focus the feelings your prospect is currently having.
