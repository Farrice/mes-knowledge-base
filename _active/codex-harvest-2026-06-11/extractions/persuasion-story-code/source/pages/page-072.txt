STORIES ABOUT HOW OTHERS HAVE MADE IT WORK
Have you ever bought something, only to discover it doesn't work as
promised?
Of course you have. Everyone has. And because of this, people you are
trying to persuade are understandably skeptical. Even if you are 100%
sure that your product or service works fine, they're not.
This is where a reassurance story can come in very handy. A Story About
How Others Have Made It Work will go a long way towards easing the
mind of a prospect thinking about trying out something you are
promoting.
The purpose of a story like this is pretty straightforward. It is to show
how an ordinary person, perhaps just like your prospect (and certainly
no more talented or skilled), can use something new and unfamiliar
successfully.
Master Copywriter Million Dollar Mike Morgan shows us how to use
such as a story, in the form of a testimonial. Here, Mike is promoting a
home-training self-defense course called TFT. The initials stand for
Target Focus Training. It's based on an unarmed combat technique
taught to Navy Seals.
This is a customer testimonial Mike put in fighting form for a TFT sales
letter:
"I want to extend my heartfelt thank you for imparting your
knowledge to me. A week ago, my girlfriend was at a bar with a
couple of friends having a good time, and a predator chose her for
a victim. He followed her to the bathroom and pushed her out of a
nearby exit, pinning her right arm behind her back.
"She was terrified, but what came immediately to her mind was a
light-hearted conversation she and I had a few days prior.
"She jokingly told me that if something happened she'd 'punch
them in the forehead. I laughed, but showed her that she should
never use her fist and instead use her forearm, and aim for the
neck and throat.
