1. Expert Testimonials
These testimonials come from what we would call "self-evident experts."
Self-evident, because, everyone who is in the market for what you are
offering has probably heard of this expert.
Or, if they haven't, they will immediately recognize their authority.
That's because you do more than choose someone you think has a
famous name. You also credentialize this person by mentioning their best-
known accomplishment, or title, or something else that will identify this
person as an expert.
The purpose of an expert testimonial is identical to that of a certification
story-that is, to confirm that the person, skill, service or product
mentioned stacks up to everything you have claimed it is. The difference
is, while a certification story is in narrative form, an expert testimonial
story is in dialogue form. You are literally quoting the expert.
This kind of testimonial is often hard to get. It's easier to get when you
know the expert, or, better yet, the expert knows you and your work.
Such was the case for me with widely-known Guerrilla Marketing series
author Jay Conrad Levinson-I'd collaborated on two books with him.
Also, he and I had created an audiobook called "Guerrilla Copywriting"
(now, sadly, out of production).
I was delighted when he gave me the following testimonial:
"David Garfinkel is the best copywriter I know."
—Jay Conrad Levinson, bestselling author, Guerrilla Marketing
series
Jay's background as a senior executive at major ad agencies Leo Burnett
and J. Walter Thompson, as well as his wide knowledge of the small
business marketing world,
made him an ideal person to give me an
expert testimonial like that.
