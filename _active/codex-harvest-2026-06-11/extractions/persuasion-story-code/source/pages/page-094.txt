What's a case study? In short, it's a story about what happened to
someone else when they used your product.
Ideally, your case study will lead your reader or listeners smoothly and
inescapably to the conclusion you want it to lead them to.
Remember that besides being a natural trust builder, a case study is also
the origin story of a particular result or a particular decision. It's that
chronology of events that demonstrates something you want to show to
your reader or prospect. One of my clients sees case studies as war
stories, because good case studies usually show decisions made and
outcomes achieved in the course of battle (that is, under stress).
Case studies provide eyes-wide-open guided visualizations for your
prospect-of what it's like to work with you, or to use your product.
These stories bring your product or service to life, by showing it in
action.
