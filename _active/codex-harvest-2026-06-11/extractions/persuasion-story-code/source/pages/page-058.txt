Now we've got something that barely seems possible for most people,
although they probably imagine there are some people walking this Earth
who have these special qualities and skills.
Even if it's more common than most people think, it seems like a
superpower to most of your prospects.
What you have done in this story is, you have put together the combined
effect of learning and using the individual skills from your coaching into
a single astounding ability most people don't have-and few can
imagine they would ever have.
By the way, you don't need to call it a "superpower" in your story. In
fact, probably better that you don't, because too many people associate
the new, diluted meaning with that word.
My point here is that a Predicted Transformation Story should tell of
something really special, exotic and desirable. If you can't wrap your
mind around calling what you come up with "a superpower," that's OK.
Just make sure the story you tell is about something above and beyond
what your prospect initially would expect, and, that it describes a
transformation that will make a meaningful difference in their life.
How To Create A Predicted Transformation Story
These stories are great. But what if you're selling something that can't
legitimately deliver a big transformation? One that a prospect could
conceivably see as giving them a superpower?
If you can't do that, then don't force this kind of story. Only use it when
the change you can promise is awe-inspiring.
