Less is more.
Find one key fact, like Morgan did (his choice was, when Swiss Glo Dark
cream combines with the stratum microbiome, magic starts to happen)-
and, shazaam, science class is over.
That doesn't mean you can't mention other scientific facts and terms. Just
make sure the rest of them (Morgan mentioned DNA and inflammation)
are things people are familiar with.
Sometimes persuasion and education happen together. When you're
telling an Improved Health Story and science is involved, it's not one of
those times. Don't put the emphasis on educating. Use the bare
minimum of science class to get the persuasion done.
