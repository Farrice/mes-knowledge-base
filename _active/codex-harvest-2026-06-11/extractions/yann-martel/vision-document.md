# Yann Martel Extraction Vision

## Direction
Build Yann Martel as a practical storytelling and literary craft system for fiction, essays, founder stories, memoir, speeches, brand narrative, and deep content. The extraction should not become generic "write better" advice. Martel's value is specific: research as physical architecture, story as co-creation, character as vessel, animals as allegorical masks, endings as open closure, and punctuation as rhythm.

## Distinct Capability Added
Existing Antigravity writing skills already cover narrative journalism, screenwriting, vivid prose, perceptual language, and content structure. Martel adds four missing craft powers:

- **Analog story architecture**: turning research fragments into a physical sequence of scenes before drafting.
- **Co-creation restraint**: leaving the reader enough space to become a participant instead of a spectator.
- **Allegorical distance**: using animals, masks, and altered forms to approach difficult material without flattening it.
- **Ending-first wonder**: resolving reader expectations while keeping mystery alive after the last line.

## Business Uses
- Founder origin stories that feel personal without over-explaining.
- Book and essay outlines that start from fragments, research, and ending pressure.
- Brand allegories for hard topics where direct claims feel too heavy.
- AI-era authenticity audits for copy, wedding notes, speeches, and personal content.
- Editorial review systems that treat art as a gift: finished with full effort, then released without review addiction.

## Cross-Expert Stacking
- **Wright Thompson**: Martel builds wonder and allegorical reach; Thompson finds the central human pressure.
- **Eric Roth**: Martel protects mystery and prose rhythm; Roth adds cinematic scene craft.
- **Ocean Vuong**: Martel gives story containers; Vuong estranges perception at sentence level.
- **Mitch Albom**: Martel sharpens endings and moral residue; Albom turns them toward accessible legacy emotion.
- **Satori Graphics**: Martel's co-creation principle transfers to visual work: leave the viewer a role.

## Build Target
Create a 12-workflow skill with foundation, practitioner, and stacking workflows. The system should produce concrete artifacts: scene envelopes, ending maps, allegory translations, rhythm edits, genre contracts, and authenticity audits.
