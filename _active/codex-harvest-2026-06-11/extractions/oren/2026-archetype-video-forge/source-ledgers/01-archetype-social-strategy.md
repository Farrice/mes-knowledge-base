## Extracted Mechanics
- Brand archetype is a role the brand plays in the audience's world, not a decorative personality label.
- Archetype selection must be constrained by resources: who can be on camera, what can be shown, internal expertise, creative tolerance, design capacity, budget, and agency access.
- The five social archetypes are Oracle, Performer, World Builder, Catalyst, and Helper.
- Social strategy improves when the brand stops trying to be all archetypes at once and commits to the behavior pattern it can execute consistently.
- Archetype mapping works as a leadership/client buy-in tool because it turns abstract taste into visible strategic choices.
