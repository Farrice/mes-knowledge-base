# Duplicate And Overlap Notes

## Canonical Package Boundaries

| Topic | Existing Canonical Package | New Companion Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Social brand archetypes | New module | Core spine of `oren-archetype-social-strategy` |
| Content pod architecture | `oren-content-team-architecture` | Cross-link and repair validation gaps |
| Creative reference systems | `oren-operational-systems` | Reference in stack workflow only |
| Luxury buyer psychology | `oren-luxury-psychology` | Add pricing psychology bridge where product ladder and buyer signal matter |
| Taste and aesthetic judgment | `oren-taste-development` | Use when archetype selection depends on quality judgment |
| Brand repositioning/world-building | `oren-repositioning` | Use when archetype mapping reveals category-code repositioning need |

## Duplicate Avoidance Decisions
- Do not rebuild the full content-team architecture inside the archetype module.
- Do not merge the generic `/archetype-build` command into this module; it remains a Luke Iha-style audience typing factory.
- Do not treat creative systems as a new Oren module; the existing operational package remains canonical.
- Do not reframe pricing psychology as pure luxury psychology; pricing gets a narrower workflow because it affects archetype, offer, and product ladder behavior.

## Smart Pull-Through Additions
- Archetype workflows call out when to hand off to content-team architecture for pods, cadence, contributor network, and scale.
- Pricing workflow imports the product-role map: entry, core, aspirational, halo.
- Marketing plan workflow imports funnel grading, initiative options, content pillar scoring, calendar matrix, and campaign phases.
- Short-form revenue workflow imports personality/intellectual-depth wedge selection and offer-content alignment.
- Creative systems workflow imports only the minimum stack required to make archetype behavior repeatable.
