## Six Operating Levers

| Lever | Principle | Diagnostic Question | Best Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Premium Flip | Raise prices before chasing more demand. Price filters weak-fit buyers and signals value. | Have prices been tested in the last 12 months, and is conversion still strong enough to support a 10-30% test? | Service offers, memberships, education, consulting, local services |
| New Distribution Channel | Add one new buyer-access path instead of assuming more posting is the answer. | Where do adjacent buyers already buy, gather, trust, or transact? | Local services, B2B services, productized offers, media businesses |
| Invisible Raise | Cut waste so profit and company value improve together. | Which top 10 costs do not directly create revenue, retention, speed, quality, or defensibility? | Any growing business with unexamined spend |
| Rich Buyout | Acquire customers in groups by buying or partnering with businesses that already have them. | Who owns the customers we are trying to acquire one by one? | Operators with cash flow, SBA access, strategic partner paths |
| Back Pocket Revenue | Sell credible adjacent offers to existing customers before chasing cold demand. | What is the best customer buying from someone else that we could credibly offer? | Client services, home services, consulting, coaching, agencies |
| Rainmaker Rule | Hire people with customer trust attached, not just strong resumes. | Who has customers, trust, or market access attached to them? | Sales-led, professional services, local services, niche operators |

## Useful System Enhancement

Add this as a lens inside business and offer diagnostics:

1. Price before traffic.
2. Distribution before content volume.
3. Profit leaks before new revenue theater.
4. Buy or partner into customer pools before grinding one-by-one acquisition.
5. Expand wallet share before chasing cold demand.
6. Evaluate people by customer access and operator trust, not resume polish alone.

## Do Not Use It For

- Brand-new founders with no offer, no customers, and no conversion data.
- Justifying random acquisitions before the core offer works.
- Treating price increases as a universal fix without conversion, retention, and delivery checks.
- Hiring "rainmakers" without non-compete, reputation, compensation, and customer-ownership diligence.
- Replacing market validation with operator bravado.

## Best Immediate Use Cases

1. **Business Asset Audit upsell:** run the six-lever screen on a local service business or consultant after the basic offer/proof audit.
2. **Offer diagnosis:** identify whether growth is blocked by underpricing, weak distribution, low wallet share, or spend leakage.
3. **Client acquisition strategy:** stop defaulting to "more leads" and inspect partner, referral, acquisition, and talent-attached-customer paths.
4. **Antigravity self-audit:** test whether our own offers have pricing tiers, upsells, owned distribution, and partner channels before adding more content workflows.
