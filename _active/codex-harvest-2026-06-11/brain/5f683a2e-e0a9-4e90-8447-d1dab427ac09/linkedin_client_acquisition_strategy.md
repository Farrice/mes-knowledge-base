## 2. Recommended Workflows (The Tactics)

Run these specific slash commands depending on your primary bottleneck:

* **For Cold Outreach & Pipeline Building:**
  * `/linkedin-cs-outreach` - LinkedIn-specific creative strategist outreach (profile optimization, Trapdoor Posts, 5-10 daily outreach system).
  * `/zero-to-client-sprint` - The full Nicolas Cole 30-day client acquisition sprint (from removal list through first 3 paying clients).
  * `/outreach-and-follow-up-engine` - Builds a complete pipeline including Leaks/Faucets map and 5x follow-up system.

* **For Inbound Content & Authority (Letting them come to you):**
  * `/algorithmic-reach` - Build a daily engagement and commenting schedule to generate inbound awareness *before* publishing your own content.
  * `/cmo-for-a-day` - Generate a public pitch post that doubles as authority-building content and prospect outreach.
  * `/high-dwell` - Draft LinkedIn posts and carousels optimized for maximum dwell time and saves using visual/psychological triggers.

* **For Proposals & Closing:**
  * `/client-acquire` - Build proposals, prepare for objection handling, and map an over-delivery strategy for winning gigs.
  * `/draft-proposal` - Draft a tailored proposal from a description that auto-matches your skills and pulls proof points.
  * `/free-custom-sample` - Create an AI-accelerated free custom sample for a prospect (shows competence rather than telling).

## 3. Recommended Execution Chain

If you're starting from scratch or re-launching your client acquisition efforts, run this sequence:

1. **Foundations:** Run `/profile-conversion` to turn your LinkedIn profile from a resume into a landing page.
2. **First Touch:** Run `/algorithmic-reach` to establish relationships in the comments.
3. **Inbound Engine:** Start `/linkedin-cs-outreach` for your daily 'Trapdoor Posts'.
4. **Outbound Push:** Run `/zero-to-client-sprint` to systematize your outbound pitching.
