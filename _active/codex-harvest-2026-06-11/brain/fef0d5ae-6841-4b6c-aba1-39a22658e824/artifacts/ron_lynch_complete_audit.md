# Ron Lynch Campaign Mastery — Complete Operational Audit

> **Skill Domain**: `skills/creative-campaign-strategy/`
> **Expert**: Ron Lynch — Screenwriter → DRTV Pioneer → CEO
> **Core Thesis**: Identity-first campaign architecture. Sell who the customer *becomes*, not what the product *does*. Then engineer the customer into an unpaid media channel, collapsing acquisition costs to zero.

---

## 🎯 All 12 Slash Commands — What They Do & When to Use Them

### Tier 1 — Foundation (4 commands)

These are your daily drivers. Use them for any campaign, product launch, or brand positioning work.

| Command | What It Does | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| `/lynch-identity-campaign` | Names the identity transformation in **one word**, builds all creative around it. The GoPro "bravery" playbook. | Starting any campaign brief. Any commoditized product that needs differentiation. |
| `/lynch-customer-media` | Engineers the customer into an **unpaid media channel**. Designs shareable moments, removes broadcast friction, provides tools (templates, music, handles). | Products with visible usage. Any brand that can generate UGC. Goal: acquisition cost → zero. |
| `/lynch-transformation-sell` | Writes sales creative that leads with the **OUTPUT** (the food, the adventure, the result). Product is the "oh, you need this" reveal. | VSLs, landing pages, product demos. Any time you're tempted to list features instead of showing transformation. |
| `/lynch-campaign-ecosystem` | Designs the **full self-reinforcing loop**: identity → contest funnel → media-matched creative → customer-as-media tools. Everything compounds. | Full campaign architecture. Product launches where you need the complete machine, not a single ad. |

### Tier 2 — Practitioner (4 commands)

These are advanced execution tools for specific tactical situations.

| Command | What It Does | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| `/lynch-media-match` | Creates **segment-specific creative** matched to each channel's audience identity. Not one ad everywhere — a skiing ad on ski channels, a lifestyle ad on Oprah's network. | Multi-platform campaigns. Paid media strategy where one-size-fits-all creative is wasting budget. |
| `/lynch-modular-script` | Writes one comprehensive long-form script, then **"takes the staple out"** to produce 5-12 standalone assets from it. | Content production. VSL-to-ad atomization. When you need maximum assets from minimum creative effort. |
| `/lynch-counter-identity` | Audits the **real user behavior** vs. the **aspirational identity**, then designs creative that sells the aspiration. (Coke drinkers sit on couches — Coke sells energy and victory.) | Brand positioning where the typical user isn't the ideal spokesperson. Luxury, lifestyle, and identity brands. |
| `/lynch-discovery-protocol` | Launches with a **hypothesis audience**, monitors actual sales data, and pivots creative to speak to the **real buyer**. The George Foreman Grill discovery. | Post-launch optimization. Any campaign where you're guessing who the buyer is. |

The business architecture layer — not just *how to create campaigns* but *how to run a creative strategy business*:

- **Client Pipeline**: 3-question pre-qual → 14-20 page brief → $30-40K test weekend

## 📊 Quality Rubric — The Ron Lynch Campaign Standard

Every output is scored against 7 dimensions:

| Criterion | What "Savant" (10/10) Looks Like |
|---|---|
| **Identity Clarity** | One-word identity permeates every element — name, visuals, copy, CTA, social proof |
| **Customer-as-Media** | Customers become unpaid media producers with tools, templates, and social infrastructure |
| **Transformation-First** | Product is NEVER the subject — the transformation is the entire show |
| **Voice Accuracy** | Written from INSIDE the customer's head — their monologue, metaphors, sophistication level |
| **Media-Creative Match** | Creative reimagined for each audience's identity — the STORY changes, not just the format |
| **Campaign Ecosystem** | Self-reinforcing system where each element feeds the others with compounding returns |
| **Business Economics** | Campaign design starts from margin/CAC targets, not creative whims |
