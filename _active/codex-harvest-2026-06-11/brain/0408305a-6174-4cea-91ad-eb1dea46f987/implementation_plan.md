## Core Genius (1 Sentence)

Tim Runia's methodology converts raw ideas into story compasses through a 3-element diagnostic — **Want, Tension, Change** — then collapses the entire narrative into a single sentence that acts as a structural GPS for the entire creation process.

---

## Uniqueness Audit: What Does This Expert Do That No One Else Does?

| Dimension | Tim Runia | Closest Existing Expert |
|-----------|-----------|------------------------|
| **Idea → Story conversion** | Explicit 3-step diagnostic that takes a raw topic and reveals the story hiding inside it | **Shaan Puri** has frame-first storytelling but assumes you already HAVE a story |
| **The Compass Sentence** | Single-sentence formula (Want + But + Until) that serves as structural GPS | No existing expert has a one-sentence compass tool |
| **Tension Mining** | Teaches you to *dig for* tension when none is obvious — distinguishing internal vs. external | **Daniel Priestley** uses tension for buying decisions, not story architecture |
| **"Topic vs. Story" Diagnostic** | Binary test: does your idea have tension + change? If not, it's still a topic | No existing expert provides this pass/fail gate |
| **Positive Tension** | Explicitly notes anticipation (weddings, reveals) as valid tension beyond conflict | Most storytelling experts default to obstacle/struggle framing |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The gap this fills**: The roster has experts who make stories BETTER (Connelly, Wright, Roth) and experts who make stories SPREAD (Puri, Kallaway). No expert diagnoses whether you have a story AT ALL. Runia is the **pre-flight check** before every other storytelling expert.

## Gap Fill Analysis

**Before Runia**: You have a raw idea, concept, or trend → you jump into writing or filming → you get lost in the middle → the output is a *topic tour* instead of a *story*.

**After Runia**: Every raw idea runs through the Want/Tension/Change diagnostic → you know immediately if you have a story or just a topic → if it's a topic, you dig for the hidden tension → you produce a compass sentence → every downstream expert receives a story-ready input.
