# Coach Cooz Content Strategy V3
## The Content Thesis

Coach Cooz content should make the right buyer feel three things in this order:

1. "He sees the thing I have been minimizing."
2. "He can name the pattern without shaming me."
3. "There is a practical next move."

The mistake to avoid is self-proof without transfer.

Bad content says:

> Look how long my clients stay.

Better content says:

> The reason clients stay is that the plan still exists when the week stops behaving. If you keep restarting, that is the first structure to inspect.

The reader has to leave with a tool, distinction, checklist, question, or belief shift.

## Emotional Territory

The emotional center is not "get fit."

It is the fear that the diminished version is becoming normal.

The content should stay close to these tensions:

- The life got bigger. The body did not get the same support.
- They know how to work hard. They do not know why this will not hold.
- The calendar is not a surprise. It is the condition.
- Aging is not the enemy. Waiting without diagnosis is.
- The old body is not gone. But the old plan is not enough.
- Their family, team, and work are already feeling the cost of their leftovers.

## Semantic Lanes

### Lane 1: Body-First Diagnosis

**Role:** Give the buyer a practical explanation for what they feel.

**Reader payoff:** "I know what to check first."

**Topics:**

- 4 PM fog
- under-recovered vs under-trained
- travel week minimums
- caffeine, protein, alcohol, bedtime
- strength after 40
- why workouts disappear by Wednesday
- local private coaching when apps fail

**Best CTA:** Save this. Check it for seven days. Bring the pattern to the Triage Audit if it is still unclear.

### Lane 2: The Pattern That Keeps Repeating

**Role:** Make the buyer emotionally care without melodrama.

**Reader payoff:** "That is the loop I am stuck in."

**Topics:**

- third Monday restart
- pretending the calendar will calm down
- buying devices instead of changing the week
- self-reliance as a blind spot
- the comfortable choice
- family getting leftovers
- waiting until the body forces the decision

**Best CTA:** One honest question, no hard sell.

### Lane 3: Proof With Transfer

**Role:** Prove Cooz is not guessing, then make the proof useful to the reader.

**Reader payoff:** "That story teaches me what to inspect in my own life."

**Topics:**

- Mike and the plan that stays
- Corey and the schedule that moves
- Sam and joy as retention
- Carron and inputs matching outputs
- Robin and Brave Choice
- Mari as speed proof with measurement caveat
- Buzz and career-protective strength

**Best CTA:** If this pattern sounds familiar, book or message for the Triage Audit.

### Lane 4: The Doorway

Use sparingly. This is most-aware content.

**Role:** Explain the Triage Audit and reduce booking anxiety.

**Reader payoff:** "I know what happens if I reach out."

**Topics:**

- what the Triage Audit does
- who should not book
- how Cooz chooses one path
- why he does not pitch a menu
- Burbank private vs remote support

**Best CTA:** Book the Triage Audit or DM "audit" with the line that hit.

## The V3 Post Standard

Every post should contain:

1. A recognizable moment.
2. A useful distinction.
3. A real proof or observed pattern.
4. A practical next move.

If any post cannot answer "what does the reader get?", rewrite it.

## Reader Value Types

Rotate these so content does not stagnate:

| Value Type | What It Gives | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis | Names the pattern | "This is under-recovery, not laziness." |
| Checklist | Gives something to inspect | "Check caffeine, protein, water, alcohol, bedtime." |
| Distinction | Separates false problem from real problem | "The schedule is not the exception. It is the design condition." |
| Question | Slows a decision moment | "Is this protecting my future or my excuses?" |
| Proof transfer | Converts a story into a lesson | "Mike proves the plan has to be there when the title changes." |
| Minimum action | Lowers friction | "Write the minimum version before the week gets messy." |

## Hook Rules

Good Cooz hooks should feel like recognition, not performance.

Use:

- "The 4 PM fog is usually traceable."
- "Monday is not a plan."
- "Some people should not train harder yet."
- "The calendar keeps winning when the plan needs a calm week."
- "Mike did not need another restart."
- "Travel does not ruin training. Pretending it is a normal week does."

Avoid:

- "Here is how to..."
- "If you are a founder/executive..."
- "Want to level up..."
- "Most people do not know..."
- "The secret to..."

## CTA Rotation

Use action matched to intent.

| Intent | CTA |
|---|---|
| Save | "Save this and check it for seven days." |
| Share | "Send this to the friend who keeps restarting on Monday." |
| Comment | "Which one breaks first for you: sleep, food, calendar, or travel?" |
| DM | "Message me 'audit' and tell me which line sounded familiar." |
| Book | "If you want the pattern diagnosed with you, book the Triage Audit." |
| No CTA | Let heavy proof or emotional posts sit. |

Do not use the same CTA twice in a row.

## LinkedIn Execution

### 30-Day Cadence

Post 3 times per week:

- Monday: diagnostic utility
- Wednesday: proof with transfer
- Friday: belief or identity shift

Daily comments:

- 10 thoughtful comments per weekday.
- 3 on local Burbank/SFV posts.
- 3 on referral/client/peer posts.
- 3 on ICP-adjacent professionals.
- 1 on a high-authority fitness, wellness, or business post where Cooz can add a useful observation.

Comment formula:

> Specific observation + one useful sentence + no pitch.

### LinkedIn Quality Gate

- First three lines create recognition or curiosity.
- One semantic lane only.
- One clear reader payoff.
- No generic trainer advice.
- No proof without reader transfer.
- No hard CTA before value lands.
- No claim that cannot be backed by a client, call, measurement, or direct observation.

## Instagram Execution

Instagram should make Cooz feel real and shareable.

Weekly cadence for the first 30 days:

- 1 save-worthy carousel.
- 1 face-to-camera Reel.
- 3-5 Stories.

Carousel rule:

Each slide should give one thought the viewer can understand in under 3 seconds.

Reel rule:

First two seconds name the pattern. Do not open with an intro.

Story rule:

Use polls and questions to mine language:

- "What breaks your week first: sleep, work, food, travel, or motivation?"
- "When does your energy usually crash?"
- "What have you tried that worked for a month and then disappeared?"

## YouTube And Blog Execution

Do not treat YouTube and blog as daily content channels yet.

Use them as support assets for DMs, referrals, and search:

- YouTube: 4 videos in the first 45 days.
- Blog: 2 posts in the first 45 days.

Every YouTube video should answer an objection:

- Why your restart keeps breaking.
- Why the 4 PM fog is traceable.
- Why private coaching is different from a program.
- What the Triage Audit does.

Every blog post should answer search intent:

- private personal training Burbank
- personal trainer Burbank
- strength training over 40
- fitness restart after 40

## Quality Gates

### Reader Value Gate

The reader must leave with one of these:

- a checklist
- a question
- a distinction
- a diagnosis
- a minimum action
- a reason to believe

### Voice Gate

The piece should sound like Cooz talking to a serious person, not a coach performing online.

Reject if it contains:

- bro fitness language
- wellness language
- jargon-heavy strategist language
- inflated proof
- repeated "That's the work" as a lazy closer
- a CTA that arrives before value

### Proof Gate

Every claim must be tagged:

- Verified client fact
- Cooz observation
- Hypothesis to test
- Needs permission
- Needs measurement verification

### Emotion Gate

The post should have tension, but not manipulation.

Good tension:

> "The calendar has not slowed down in three years."

Bad tension:

> "Your family will lose you if you do not act now."

The reader should feel seen, not cornered.
