## Executive Summary

Brain Glue is a system for making messages stick by activating memory, emotion, pattern recognition, sensory imagination, and buyer identity. The practical output is not "better writing" in a broad sense. It is stronger names, hooks, slogans, analogies, videos, sales pages, campaign spines, and offer language that prospects can understand and remember.

## Core Methodology

1. Control the first frame.
2. Align with the audience's tribe.
3. Make the offer cognitively simple.
4. Translate abstraction into analogy or metaphor.
5. Anchor to existing mental real estate.
6. Deliver with calm confidence.
7. Lower resistance with humor or fascination.
8. Use rhyme and alliteration for memory.
9. Use chiasmus for quotable reversal.
10. Use trigger words to wake attention.
11. Break expected patterns.
12. Reject bad fits to attract good fits.
13. Add sensory evidence, words, gestures, demonstrations, or sounds.

## Capability Unlocks

- Generate sticky names and slogans for offers, products, businesses, books, and campaigns.
- Rewrite complex offers into simple buyer-safe language.
- Build Brain Glue hooks for LinkedIn, X, Instagram, YouTube, Reels, TikTok, and email.
- Produce video scripts with visible and spoken memory devices.
- Build sales assets with a repeated campaign spine.
- Create brand sensory cues that can travel across content and campaigns.
- Sharpen qualification language so premium offers feel selective and honest.

## Quality Standard

Brain Glue output should pass this test:

> If the buyer saw this once, could they explain it, feel why it matters, and remember the phrase later?

If not, the output is not sticky enough.
