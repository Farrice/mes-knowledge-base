# Brain Glue Forge Vision

**IsArtifact:** true

## Creative Direction

Farrice wants the book's persuasion knowledge turned into a dynamic agent and skill system for written content, video content, marketing, social media, business assets, and entrepreneurial deployment.

The selected strategy is a standalone James I. Bond / Brain Glue agent and skill, cross-linked to existing Made to Stick / Heath Brothers assets.

## Leverage Analysis

### Uniqueness Audit

Brain Glue is not just "make ideas sticky." Its distinct value is granular language mechanics for sales:

- Verbal patterning: rhyme, alliteration, chiasmus, trigger words.
- Cognitive ease: perspective, analogy, one-feature focus, demonstration.
- Emotional activation: humor, sensory cues, surprise, rejection, and expectation.
- Commercial deployment: names, slogans, hooks, sales copy, video opens, and campaigns.

### Gap Fill

The existing roster has broad persuasion, positioning, copy, and content systems. Brain Glue fills the phrase-engineering and sensory-memory gap: it creates the small verbal and sensory devices that make otherwise good strategy stick in a buyer's head.

## Vision Decision

Build Brain Glue as a standalone skill with 12 workflows. The system should produce finished assets, not explain the book.
