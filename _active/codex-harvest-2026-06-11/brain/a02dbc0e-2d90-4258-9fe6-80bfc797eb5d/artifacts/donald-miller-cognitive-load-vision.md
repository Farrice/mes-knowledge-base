## What's NEW in This Source (vs. Existing Skills)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This is NOT a re-extraction of StoryBrand. The existing StoryBrand skill covers the **SB7 BrandScript** (7-element story formula), one-liners, funnel architecture, and copy transformation. This new source introduces a **completely different system**: the **Sound Bite Strategy** and **Cognitive Load Scoring** methodology — a precision messaging architecture that is *upstream* of all StoryBrand outputs.

### New Concepts NOT in Existing Skills

| New Concept | What It Is | Why It Matters |
|-------------|-----------|----------------|
| **Cognitive Load Scoring** | Quantitative 0-100 scoring system for message weight — every word has a literal "weight" that can be measured | Turns messaging clarity from subjective opinion into a measurable, auditable metric |
| **Zero Cognitive Load Mandate** | The explicit goal: ALL customer-facing messaging must score ZERO — not "low," not "reduced," but **zero** | A harder, more operationally precise standard than the existing "clarity beats cleverness" pattern |
| **The PEACE Sound Bite Framework** | 5-sound-bite architecture: **P**roblem → **E**mpathy → **A**nswer → **C**hange → **E**nd Result | Different from the existing 9-sound-bite PEACE framework in Culture Turnaround — this is customer-facing, not internal culture |
| **Three-Phase Messaging Campaign** | Curiosity → Enlightenment → Commitment — the complete campaign architecture | Goes beyond individual copy pieces (StoryBrand) into full campaign orchestration |
| **The House Metaphor** | Front Steps (curiosity) → Front Porch (enlightenment) → Front Door (commitment) — structural diagnosis of business messaging gaps | Visual diagnostic tool that reveals which phase a business is missing |
| **Sound Bite Repetition Strategy** | Exact words repeated verbatim across ALL channels for 10+ years — not "consistent messaging" but literal word-for-word repetition | The most counterintuitive and powerful operational insight — repetition at a scale most marketers would resist |
| **Cognitive Load Autopsy** | Line-by-line deconstruction of copy with specific "pound" scores per phrase | Teachable diagnostic skill that turns vague "this doesn't feel right" into precise "this phrase adds 20 pounds" |
| **Campaign Collateral Architecture** | Curiosity (sound bites) → Enlightenment (3+ pieces: lead gens, social, blogs, videos) → Commitment (3+ incentives: bonuses, trials, contests) | Complete campaign build system from sound bite foundation through deployment |
