## First 30-Day Focus

The first 30 days are not a full brand relaunch.

They are a market proof sprint:

- profile clarity
- useful LinkedIn content
- daily LinkedIn distribution
- warm referrals
- Triage Audit calls
- proof-safe follow-up
- simple tracking

## What Success Looks Like

By the end of the first month, Cooz should know:

- which LinkedIn posts create profile views, comments, DMs, and booked audits
- which referral asks produce actual names, not encouragement
- whether the Triage Audit language gets serious buyers
- which offer paths people understand and resist
- which proof stories can be used safely

## Do Not Skip

Before public content or sales copy ships, check:

- Is the buyer clear?
- Is the next step clear?
- Does the reader get value?
- Is the claim safe?
- Does this sound like Cooz?

The safest default:

Start with the Triage Audit.

Cooz listens, diagnoses, and recommends one path.
