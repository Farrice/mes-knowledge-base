# Coach Cooz - Codex LinkedIn Profile And Content System

Purpose: Make LinkedIn clear enough to convert warm referrals and practical enough to create qualified conversations.

## Profile Strategy

LinkedIn is not the whole funnel. It is the trust check.

When Mike, Corey, Sam, Carron, or another trusted person sends someone to Cooz, that person will check LinkedIn before booking. The profile must answer:

- What does he do?
- Is this fitness or life coaching?
- Does he work with people like me?
- Is he premium for a reason?
- What do I do next?

## Headline

Use this:

> Strength and energy rebuilds for professionals over 40 | Burbank private coaching + remote daily support | Clients who stay 3-9 years

Why:

- "Strength and energy rebuilds" is concrete.
- "Professionals over 40" is clearer than "high-performing professionals."
- "Burbank private coaching + remote daily support" answers delivery.
- "Clients who stay 3-9 years" carries the proof without sounding mystical.

## Featured Section

Use 3 slots only:

1. Book the Triage Audit
2. Brave Choice: 3 Questions For The Moment Nobody Is Watching
3. Client proof: Mike, Corey, Carron, Sam, or a short proof page

If Brave Choice is not ready, do not fake it. Use one proof asset and one booking asset.

## Content Rules

### Cadence

- 3 posts/week for 30 days
- 12 total posts
- 30 minutes active after posting

### Weekly mix

| Post Type | Job |
|---|---|
| Client proof story | Proves "you can trust me" |
| Diagnostic utility | Proves "my method makes sense" |
| Belief/identity post | Proves "I understand you" |

### Hard rules

- Open with a real person, a real moment, or a concrete diagnostic.
- Do not open with "you" accusations.
- Do not use the same closer every time.
- Do not claim "last week" unless it was literal.
- Do not use a link strategy as religion. Test links.
- Do not post any claim that would embarrass Cooz if the client read it.

### Post 2 - 4 PM Fog Audit

```text
The 4 PM fog is usually not mysterious.

It is usually traceable.

Before I change someone's training, I want to see five inputs:

1. What time was the last coffee?

2. Was there protein before noon?

3. Was there water before coffee?

4. How many alcohol nights this week?

5. Was bedtime stable or improvised?

If all five are unstable, harder training is not the first answer.

The body is already asking for output it cannot recover from.

Most professionals over 40 do not need to prove they can work harder.

They need to stop making recovery impossible.

Fix the inputs.

Then earn the harder work.
```

### Post 3 - Corey

```text
Corey travels for work.

That used to be where programs died.

He writes for shows.

The schedule moves.

The room changes.

The city changes.

Most coaching breaks right there because the plan depends on life staying still.

But life is not still.

So the work has to survive movement.

When Corey is home, we train hard.

When he is gone, the relationship does not disappear.

The program adjusts.

The expectations adjust.

The return is already built in.

That is the difference between a plan and a practice.

A plan fails when the calendar breaks.

A practice gives you somewhere to come back.
```

### Post 4 - Triage Audit

```text
I do not sell the same path to everyone.

That would be easier.

It would also be lazy.

Some people need the room four days a week.

Some need remote daily support because they travel.

Some need a 90-day first win before they trust anything longer.

Some are not a fit at all.

That is why I use a Triage Audit.

I listen first.

Then I read back what I heard.

Then I diagnose the pattern.

Only after that do I recommend a path.

No menu pitch.

No pretending every person needs the same thing.

If the body carrying your life is starting to fall behind it, the first job is not to buy a program.

The first job is to name what is actually breaking.
```

## Measurement

Track every post:

- impressions
- reactions
- comments
- saves
- profile views
- DMs
- Featured clicks
- Triage Audit bookings
- call source
- close result

Decision rule:

- If proof posts get likes but no DMs, increase diagnostic posts.
- If diagnostic posts get saves/profile views, build more practical frameworks.
- If posts get profile views but no bookings, fix CTA or Featured assets.
- If bookings happen but do not close, fix Triage Audit/offer clarity.
