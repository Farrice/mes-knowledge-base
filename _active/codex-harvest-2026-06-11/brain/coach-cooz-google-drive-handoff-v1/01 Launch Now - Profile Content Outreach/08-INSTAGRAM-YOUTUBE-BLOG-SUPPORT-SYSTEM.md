# Coach Cooz - Instagram, YouTube, And Blog Support System

Purpose: Support discovery and trust without distracting from the 30-day LinkedIn/referral loop.

## Platform Roles

| Platform | Job |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Trust check, referral reinforcement, professional buyer conversations |
| Instagram | Visual proof, shareability, local familiarity, DM warmth |
| YouTube | Method conviction and searchable depth |
| Blog | Local/search discoverability and evergreen explanation |

## Instagram System

Instagram should not be LinkedIn screenshots.

It should show the work:

- Cooz coaching
- simple diagnostic carousels
- client-safe proof moments
- short face-to-camera explanations
- Burbank/local environment

### Weekly IG cadence

For the first 30 days:

- 1 carousel
- 1 Reel
- 3-5 Stories across the week

Do not daily-post if quality drops.

### Carousel format

Each slide = one useful idea.

Example carousel:

Title:

> The 4 PM Fog Audit

Slides:

1. The 4 PM fog is usually traceable.
2. Check caffeine timing.
3. Check protein before noon.
4. Check water before coffee.
5. Check alcohol nights.
6. Check bedtime consistency.
7. If all five are unstable, do not start by training harder.
8. Fix the inputs. Then earn the harder work.

Caption:

```text
Most people try to solve low energy by adding intensity.

Sometimes that works.

But if the inputs are unstable, harder training can make the system louder instead of better.

Save this and check it for seven days.

If the pattern is still unclear, that is what the Triage Audit is for.
```

### Reel format

Keep Reels plain and specific.

Structure:

- First 2 seconds: name the problem
- 15-30 seconds: explain one useful thing
- Last 5 seconds: small action

Reel script:

```text
If your workouts keep disappearing by Wednesday, the problem might not be discipline.

It might be that your plan only works on your best week.

A useful plan has a fallback built in.

Bad sleep? There is a minimum version.

Travel day? There is a minimum version.

Long workday? There is a minimum version.

Most people are stuck between perfect and nothing.

The middle option is where consistency gets built.

This week, write down your minimum version before the week gets messy.
```

### Story prompts

- "What usually breaks your training week first: sleep, work, travel, food, or motivation?"
- "Today's client lesson: the plan has to survive the week, not the other way around."
- "One thing I check before changing someone's workouts: last caffeine time."
- "Burbank private coaching slots: I am taking Triage Audits this month for people who want the room and the structure."

## YouTube System

Do not launch a high-volume YouTube channel yet.

Build 4 conviction videos that answer buyer objections and can be sent after DMs, referral intros, or Triage Audit applications.

### Video 1 - Why Your Fitness Restart Keeps Breaking

Purpose: Method conviction.

Outline:

1. Open: "If you keep restarting, the restart is not the problem."
2. The real problem: your plan assumes life will behave.
3. The three failure points: schedule, recovery, decision moments.
4. The fix: build the minimum version, the support layer, and the recovery inputs.
5. Client transfer: Mike/Corey examples.
6. CTA: book Triage Audit if you want the pattern diagnosed.

Hook:

```text
If you keep restarting your fitness, I do not think your discipline is the first thing I would question.

I would question the plan.
```

### Video 2 - The 4 PM Fog Audit

Purpose: useful diagnostic and search support.

Outline:

1. Define the 4 PM fog.
2. Five inputs: caffeine, protein, water, alcohol, bedtime.
3. Why harder training may be wrong first move.
4. Seven-day audit.
5. CTA: bring the notes to a Triage Audit.

### Video 3 - Private Coaching vs Another Program

Purpose: price and category clarity.

Outline:

1. Programs work when life is stable.
2. Coaching matters when life moves.
3. The relationship is the delivery mechanism.
4. What private coaching includes.
5. Who should not buy it.
6. CTA.

### Video 4 - What The Triage Audit Actually Does

Purpose: reduce booking anxiety.

Outline:

1. This is not a pitch call.
2. First: listen.
3. Second: diagnose.
4. Third: recommend one path.
5. Who is a fit.
6. Who is not.
7. CTA.

## Cross-Platform Repurposing

One idea becomes:

- LinkedIn diagnostic post
- IG carousel
- 30-second Reel
- YouTube segment
- Blog section

Example:

Core idea:

> The 4 PM fog is traceable.

Outputs:

- LinkedIn: full diagnostic post
- IG carousel: five inputs
- Reel: "If the inputs are unstable, harder training is not first"
- YouTube: 8-minute audit walkthrough
- Blog: section in "fitness restart after 40"

## Measurement

Track only conversion-adjacent metrics:

| Platform | Metrics |
|---|---|
| Instagram | saves, shares, profile visits, DMs |
| YouTube | watch time, comments, link clicks, use in sales follow-up |
| Blog | impressions, local keyword clicks, Triage Audit clicks |

Likes are secondary.

The question is not "did people approve?"

The question is:

> Did this make a qualified person more likely to talk to Cooz?
