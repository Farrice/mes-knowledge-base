## Executive Verdict

V6 has a real strategic spine. The strongest assets are Cooz's long-arc client proof, premium local delivery, named client specificity, and the diagnostic Triage Audit. The plan should not be thrown out.

But V6 overstates certainty. It treats several unproven assumptions as locked truth: that 3-4 clients can reach `$10k-$15k/mo`, that online can produce 2-4 clients in 60 days, that Hollywood/Vistage/EO access is available on the plan's timeline, and that first-comment links reliably create a 3-5x reach lift.

V6.1 should simplify the next 30 days to one job: prove the acquisition loop. The loop is:

Referral or LinkedIn/GBP signal -> Triage Audit booked -> one pathway recommended -> paid client closed -> source tracked.

No YouTube, blog, podcast expansion, or paid trial should distract from that loop until it produces calls.

## What Is Grounded

- Premium in-person pricing is plausible in Burbank/LA if the offer is framed as high-touch private coaching, not commodity training. Burbank Strength & Conditioning lists 1:1 coaching at `$997` every 4 weeks and personal training packages up to `$1,700` for 20 sessions.
- Google Business Profile matters for local discovery. Google states local ranking is mainly based on relevance, distance, and prominence, and that complete information plus review count/score can improve visibility.
- Burbank/Hollywood is a real referral context. Burbank officially markets itself as the Media Capital of the World, and the Media District contains major entertainment/media employers.
- The long-arc proof spine is a real differentiator if testimonial permission and claim accuracy are confirmed. Mike 9 years, Robin 8 years, Corey 4 years, Carron 3 years, and other named arcs are more credible than generic coach claims.
- A diagnostic sales motion fits the buyer better than a menu pitch. The Triage Audit is directionally right for self-reliant, affluent buyers who do not want to feel sold a commodity program.

## What Is Plausible But Not Proven

- "The Resurrection Coach" may be emotionally strong, but cold buyers may not immediately know whether it means fitness, executive coaching, therapy-adjacent work, spirituality, or life coaching.
- `$10k/mo` within 90 days is possible, but it likely requires 6-8 A/B clients or one ESA client. It is not supported by 3-4 standard clients.
- `$15k/mo` within 90 days requires ESA, near-full A/B capacity, unusually fast remote uptake, or a longer runway.
- LinkedIn can support referral reinforcement and inbound trust, but online-alone acquisition is unproven without profile view, DM, booking, and close-rate baselines.
- Hollywood, WGF, IATSE, EO, and Vistage can be strong environments, but only WGF public events appear immediately accessible. The rest require named gatekeepers.

## What Is Projected Or Risky

- "Online alone produces 2-4 clients in 60 days" is projected. No baseline proves it.
- "First-comment links create 3-5x reach" is overstated. Current guidance is mixed; test this against Cooz's own account.
- "Top-3 ranking for Burbank executive coach" is likely the wrong GBP target because that query may map to business coaching. Better local terms are "personal trainer Burbank," "private personal training Burbank," "strength coach Burbank," and "fitness coach Burbank."
- "$197 paid trial" conflicts with premium positioning and may attract bargain shoppers. It also violates the spirit of "no discounted intro tier below `$1,275`" unless tightly gated and positioned as a paid diagnostic, not cheap access.
- "16 lbs of pure muscle in 3 months" is powerful but proof-sensitive. If body composition measurement is not credible, revise to a safer claim such as "16 lbs gained in 3 months, visibly stronger and leaner" or cite the measurement method.
- Repeated "real call last week" language is dangerous if those posts are composites. Composite or anonymized material must not be presented as literal recent events.

Use this instead of the V6 target language:

> The 90-day target is to prove the acquisition loop and reach `$6k-$10k/mo` in new recurring revenue through 4-7 retained A/B clients, or exceed `$10k/mo` if one ESA client validates. The 6-month target is `$10k-$15k/mo` with a healthier mix of local anchors, remote clients, and one premium pathway.

## Required Evidence Before V6.1 Becomes V7

- 10-15 buyer interviews with current/past clients: why they bought, why they stayed, what almost stopped them, and how they describe Cooz to a peer.
- Referral source audit: who has sent leads before, who can send leads now, and which language creates actual intros.
- Proof validation: testimonial permissions, Mari measurement method, renewal history, and claim-by-claim source notes.
- LinkedIn funnel baseline: profile views, post impressions, saves, comments, DMs, Featured clicks, booked audits, closes.
- GBP baseline: rankings from Burbank/SFV for realistic terms, review count, review recency, category fit, services, photos, and owner replies.
- Sales economics: prep time, call time, follow-up time, close result, channel source, and hours per closed client.

## Immediate Corrections To Existing V6 Logic

- Replace "3-4 clients closed gets to `$10k-$15k/mo`" with accurate path math.
- Replace "online path alone produces 2-4 clients in 60 days" with "online path is unproven until it produces booked audits."
- Replace "GBP top-3 for Burbank executive coach" with realistic local fitness/private training terms.
- Delay YouTube/blog until LinkedIn produces at least 1 booked audit or clear profile-to-DM movement.
- Treat ESA as a pre-sell validation path, not a built product.
- Treat first-comment links as an experiment.
- Create two positioning tests: "Resurrection Coach" and "strength and energy rebuild for professionals over 40."
