# Offer Positioning North Star System

Purpose: Codify the process that produced the Coach Cooz Offer, Positioning, Service, And North Star V3 package so the same standard can be repeated for other businesses or future Coach Cooz passes.

## Command

```text
/offer-positioning-north-star [client/project + source files]
```

## What It Produces

A new artifact folder under `brain/` with:

- `README.md`
- `NORTH-STAR-MISSION-VISION.md`
- `POSITIONING-CLARITY-LOCK.md`
- `OFFER-ARCHITECTURE-V3.md` or next version
- `SERVICE-DESIGN-DELIVERY-MAP.md`
- `TRIAGE-TO-CLIENT-CONVERSION-SYSTEM.md`
- `PROOF-CLAIMS-VALIDATION-MAP.md`

## Source Stack

Use the best available source truth:

- audience/avatar/ICP artifacts
- current offer and positioning docs
- service delivery notes
- sales, referral, DM, or audit scripts
- proof and testimonial docs
- content strategy
- voice guide, canon, and ban list
- prior evidence audits

Do not overwrite source files. Create a new artifact folder.

## Workflow Stack

The workflow runs in this order:

1. Seth Godin strategic clarity for smallest viable audience, mission, and who/what lock.
2. April Dunford positioning for category, alternatives, differentiated value, and buyer context.
3. Jason Fladlien offer and identity-offer logic for premium offer stack, price resistance, and buyer transformation.
4. Vincent Hu / offer economics for revenue path, ascension, capacity, and MRR realism.
5. Service design logic for onboarding, weekly cadence, boundaries, measurements, and premium feel.
6. Triage conversion logic for diagnostic calls, one-path recommendations, objections, and close quality.
7. Proof and claims discipline for permission-sensitive, measurement-sensitive, speculative, and safe public claims.
8. Authenticity and anti-slop audit for plain buyer language and serious voice.

## Quality Standard

The final package should pass these gates:

- A cold buyer understands what the business does before branded language appears.
- The revenue math is true.
- The offer ladder protects delivery capacity.
- The service model makes the premium price visible.
- The conversion system recommends one path, not a menu.
- Every proof claim is classified before public use.
- The voice is plain, serious, emotionally alive, and free of strategist fog.
