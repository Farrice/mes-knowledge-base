# Coach Cooz Service Design And Delivery Map V3
## Executive Read

The premium price is not earned by saying "high-touch."

It is earned when the client can feel the difference between:

- buying workouts
- buying access
- buying a relationship that keeps the work alive when life moves

The service design has to make that difference visible.

## The Premium Service Promise

> You will know what is breaking, what we are practicing, what the next decision is, and how the plan adjusts when your real life shows up.

This is the service standard.

## Service Philosophy

The plan is not the product.

The relationship that keeps the plan alive is the product.

Training, nutrition, recovery, accountability, and Brave Choice all serve one job:

> Help the client rebuild a body that can carry the next chapter.

## The Client Experience Arc

### Stage 1: Diagnosis

The client should feel:

> "He heard the actual pattern."

Core artifact:

- Triage Audit notes
- path recommendation
- first 12-week target
- initial risk map

### Stage 2: Stabilization

The client should feel:

> "I know what to do this week, even if the week gets messy."

Core artifact:

- first-week plan
- minimum-version rules
- five-input baseline
- training baseline
- recovery baseline

### Stage 3: First Win

The client should feel:

> "This can work for my life."

Core artifact:

- first 30-day review
- strength/energy/consistency markers
- what changed
- what still breaks
- next 30-day focus

### Stage 4: Integration

The client should feel:

> "The work is becoming part of how I live, not a program I am trying to survive."

Core artifact:

- repeatable weekly rhythm
- travel protocol
- miss recovery protocol
- Brave Choice decision pattern

### Stage 5: Compounding

The client should feel:

> "The work keeps paying me back."

Core artifact:

- quarterly review
- long-arc progress markers
- next chapter target
- renewal recommendation

## Universal Onboarding Flow

### Before Start Date

1. Agreement signed.
2. Payment set.
3. Start date confirmed.
4. Intake completed.
5. Baseline photos/measurements only if appropriate and consented.
6. Training history reviewed.
7. Calendar constraints mapped.
8. First-week plan created.

### Welcome Message

Tone: calm, direct, no hype.

```text
We are not trying to fix everything this week.

This week is about learning the actual pattern:

what your body does
what your calendar does
what breaks first
what support you actually need

I will tell you what matters.
I will ignore what does not.
We will build from there.
```

### First Session Or First Call

Job:

- confirm the diagnosis
- set the first 12-week target
- explain how communication works
- define the minimum version
- identify the first likely failure point

## Weekly Service Cadence

### Burbank Private Rebuild

| Touchpoint | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Private training sessions | 3-4x/week | Strength, movement, feedback, relationship |
| Weekly review | 1x/week | Inputs, recovery, schedule, target adjustment |
| Minimum-version planning | As needed | Keep work alive during hard weeks |
| Nutrition/recovery check | Weekly | Ensure outputs match inputs |
| Progress marker | Monthly | Show what is changing |

### Remote Daily Rebuild

| Touchpoint | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Programming | Weekly | Training plan built around schedule |
| Async check-ins | 5 days/week | Daily support and accountability |
| Weekly review | 1x/week | Pattern read, adjustments, next-week focus |
| Travel/messy-week plan | As needed | Prevent full restart cycle |
| Monthly review | Monthly | Progress, friction, renewal of target |

Remote response standard should be explicit. Example:

> Cooz reviews client check-ins once per business day Monday-Friday, with urgent schedule/travel adjustments prioritized when submitted before the agreed daily window.

Do not promise 24/7 access.

## Boundaries

Premium does not mean unlimited.

Define:

- response windows
- what counts as urgent
- what Cooz reviews
- what client must submit
- what happens if the client disappears
- medical scope boundaries
- travel adjustment rules
- cancellation/no-show rules

Boundary language:

```text
The support is high-touch, not unlimited.

That matters because the goal is not dependency.

The goal is a structure you can trust and practice inside.
```

## Miss Recovery Protocol

When a client misses, do not shame or ignore it.

Use:

1. Name the miss.
2. Identify what broke first.
3. Choose the next minimum action.
4. Return to the plan within 24-48 hours.

Client-facing language:

```text
We are not restarting.

We are returning.

Tell me what broke first: sleep, work, food, travel, or avoidance.
Then we pick the next clean action.
```

## Travel Week Protocol

Default minimum:

- protein twice
- walk daily
- one short lift or mobility session
- no two chaotic days in a row
- return plan before travel begins

Travel week success is not perfection.

Travel week success is coming home with the thread intact.

## What Makes It Feel Premium

Premium markers:

- the diagnosis is specific
- Cooz remembers the client pattern
- the plan changes when life changes
- check-ins receive meaningful replies
- client has a minimum version before the hard week
- proof is shown in monthly reviews
- Cooz explains why something is changing
- the service has boundaries, not chaos
- the buyer never feels like they are shopping workouts

Non-premium markers:

- vague access
- unclear response windows
- no review rhythm
- same plan after life changes
- no proof of progress
- client has to self-diagnose
- Cooz improvises pricing or scope

## Delivery Risks

| Risk | Why It Matters | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Remote feels like cheap online coaching | Price resistance rises | Define daily support, review cadence, and deliverables. |
| Local capacity overload | Service quality drops | Cap local clients and protect session quality. |
| Triage Audit consumes too much free labor | Cooz burns time before revenue | Gate cold calls and track hours per close. |
| ESA overpromises | Premium path loses trust | Keep invite-only and validate with one pilot. |
| Proof not tracked | Long-arc value stays abstract | Monthly scorecards and proof notes. |

## Service Quality Gate

A client should be able to answer:

- What am I practicing this week?
- What is the minimum version if the week gets messy?
- What did Cooz change and why?
- What input matters most right now?
- What progress can I see?
- What happens if I miss?
- What are we building toward over 90 days?

If the client cannot answer those, the service is not yet premium enough.
