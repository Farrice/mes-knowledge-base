# Coach Cooz Positioning Clarity Lock V3
## Executive Read

The positioning should not make cold buyers decode the brand.

Lead with the practical category:

> Strength and energy rebuilds for professionals over 40 whose body is starting to fall behind the life they built.

Then deepen into the brand:

> The deeper work is Resurrection: coming back to a body you recognize before the diminished version becomes normal.

This order matters.

Cold buyers need context before poetry.

## The Positioning Lock

### Category

Long-arc strength and energy coaching.

Use this as the strategic category, not always the public label.

For search and cold surfaces, use:

- private personal training in Burbank
- private strength coaching
- strength and energy coaching after 40
- remote daily coaching for travel-heavy professionals

The market already understands personal training, private coaching, strength coaching, and remote coaching. Cooz should enter those mental shelves first, then separate himself.

### Target Buyer

Professionals over 40 with money, responsibility, and body memory whose strength, energy, or consistency is starting to fall behind the life they built.

### Market Insight

Most people in this buyer group do not fail because they lack effort.

They fail because they keep buying plans built for a calmer life than the one they actually have.

### Differentiated Value

Cooz combines:

- diagnosis before prescription
- high-touch private or daily coaching
- long-arc relationship proof
- training, nutrition, recovery, and decision support
- a plan that expects work, travel, family, stress, and inconsistency to show up

### One-Line Positioning

> Coach Cooz helps professionals over 40 rebuild strength, energy, and consistency when life keeps breaking the plan.

### Longer Positioning

> Coach Cooz is a private strength and energy rebuild coach for professionals over 40 whose body is starting to fall behind the life they built. He starts with a Triage Audit, names what is actually breaking, and recommends one path: in-person Burbank coaching, remote daily support, a 90-day first-win sprint, or an invite-only premium pilot if the problem is bigger than training.

## What Buyers Need To Believe

Before they buy, the right buyer needs to believe:

1. This is not a beginner fitness problem.
2. The old restart pattern is not likely to fix itself.
3. Their schedule is not an excuse. It is a design condition.
4. Coaching is not failure. It is a serious specialist decision.
5. The premium price makes sense if the work is diagnosis plus relationship plus implementation.
6. Cooz has proof that the work holds beyond a short burst.

## The Perfect-World Criteria

Before introducing the offer, set this context:

> A good solution for this buyer would not just hand them workouts. It would diagnose what is breaking, fit the level of support to their real life, adjust when the week moves, and keep the work alive long enough to compound.

That is the standard Cooz should sell against.

## Rejected Language

Do not lead with:

- "The Resurrection Coach" on cold search or cold LinkedIn surfaces without a plain explanation nearby.
- "Executive coach" as the primary category.
- "Founder/C-suite/operator" lists.
- "CEO body."
- "Wellness journey."
- "Holistic reset."
- "Optimize the man" as a primary cold headline.
- "12-week transformation" as the main promise.
- Any universal claim from one client result.

## Testable Positioning Statements

Run these in referrals, DMs, and profile tests.

### Test A: Plain Problem

> I help professionals over 40 rebuild strength, energy, and consistency when life keeps breaking the plan.

Success signal: the listener names someone specific.

### Test B: Body Falling Behind Life

> I work with successful professionals whose body is starting to fall behind the life they built.

Success signal: the listener says some version of "I know exactly who that is."

### Test C: Diagnosis Before Plan

> I do not sell everyone the same plan. I start by naming what is actually breaking, then recommend one path.

Success signal: the listener asks about the Triage Audit or says this feels different from training.

### Test D: Referral Ask

> Who do you know who is 40-plus, successful, and quietly frustrated that their strength, energy, sleep, or consistency is not where it used to be?

Success signal: actual names, not encouragement.

## Cold-Buyer Test

A cold buyer should understand these in 5 seconds:

- This is strength, energy, and consistency coaching.
- It is for professionals over 40.
- It is for people whose life keeps breaking their plan.
- It starts with diagnosis.
- It can be in-person in Burbank or remote.
- It is premium, not commodity training.

If any surface makes them wonder whether this is therapy, spirituality, business coaching, or generic motivation, the positioning has failed.

## Positioning Guardrails

### Do

- Say what it is before what it means.
- Use "rebuild" as the main verb.
- Use "Resurrection" as the identity layer.
- Keep the offer path simple: Triage Audit first, one recommendation after.
- Show long-arc proof.
- Make capacity constraints real and practical.

### Do Not

- Create a new category before the existing market shelf is clear.
- Publicly lead with all four pathways.
- Use ESA as a broad public promise.
- Let content become more sophisticated than the buyer's language.
- Treat emotional depth as a substitute for offer clarity.

## Final Positioning Decision

The final positioning is:

> Coach Cooz helps professionals over 40 rebuild strength, energy, and consistency when life keeps breaking the plan.

The final brand layer is:

> Resurrection is the deeper identity shift: coming back to a body you recognize before the diminished version becomes normal.

The final conversion path is:

> Start with the Triage Audit. Cooz listens, diagnoses, and recommends one path.
