# Yann Martel Storytelling Mastery - Creative Brief

## Core Idea
The Yann Martel system turns scattered material into story architecture, then edits that story so the reader becomes a participant. It is strongest when the work needs depth, sincerity, moral charge, or a memorable ending instead of clean-but-empty writing.

This is not a general writing helper. It is a craft engine for moments where the reader must feel: "I helped complete this in my own mind."

## What We Built
The new `yann-martel-storytelling-mastery` skill gives Antigravity a 12-workflow storytelling system:

| Command | Primary Use | Output |
|---|---|---|
| `/martel-envelope` | Turn notes, research, transcripts, and fragments into a story path | Envelope map |
| `/martel-scenes` | Convert material into scenes with movement | Scene deck |
| `/martel-cocreation` | Remove over-explanation and give the reader work | Restraint edit |
| `/martel-ending` | Create closure plus lingering mystery | Ending design |
| `/martel-animal-allegory` | Make heavy topics easier to enter | Allegory route |
| `/martel-vessel` | Build ordinary characters under extraordinary pressure | Character pressure sheet |
| `/martel-punctuation` | Improve rhythm, breath, and tempo | Punctuation edit |
| `/martel-rule-break` | Break form without losing the reader | Experiment plan |
| `/martel-genre-contract` | Map reader promises and payoff | Contract map |
| `/martel-sublime` | Add moral, spiritual, or existential depth | Larger-question map |
| `/martel-authenticity` | Decide where AI helps or harms trust | Authorship audit |
| `/martel-gift-release` | Publish without being captured by reviews | Release protocol |

## Strategic Value
Most AI writing systems help produce more words. This one helps decide what belongs, what should be withheld, what the ending is doing, and whether the final piece still feels authored by a person.

That matters because the highest-value writing problems are not "write me a post." They are:

- "I have too much material and no story."
- "This is technically clear, but nobody feels anything."
- "This topic is important but too heavy to approach directly."
- "The ending lands like a summary instead of a memory."
- "This personal message feels polished but not sincere."
- "AI helped write this, and now I do not trust the voice."

The Martel system is built for those problems.

## Quality Standards

A Martel-powered output is good only if:
- The raw material has been sorted into usable fragments.
- The reader gets meaningful work to do.
- The story has scenes, not just claims.
- The ending fulfills the right expectation.
- One question remains alive.
- The prose has rhythm.
- The writing feels like it came from a person.

## What Not To Use This For
- Simple SEO filler.
- Generic social captions.
- Pure instructional content where story would slow the reader down.
- Legal or technical drafts where precision matters more than felt authorship.
- Any intimate message where the user wants AI to fake sincerity rather than recover real memory.

## Success Metrics

### Creative Metrics
- Reader can retell the story after one read.
- Ending is remembered without needing a tagline.
- Draft has fewer explanations and more vivid cues.
- Reader response includes "I felt this" or "I kept thinking about this."

### Business Metrics
- Founder story increases trust on calls.
- Case study keeps prospects reading longer.
- Personal brand content earns higher-quality replies.
- Offer narrative reduces explanation needed in sales conversations.
- Clients can reuse the story architecture across pages, decks, talks, and posts.
