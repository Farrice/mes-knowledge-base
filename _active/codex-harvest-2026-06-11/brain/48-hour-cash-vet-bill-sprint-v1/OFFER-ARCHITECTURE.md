# Offer Architecture

## Offer Name

AI Business Asset Audit

## One-Liner

A 24-hour review of the customer-facing assets that affect whether people trust, contact, or buy from your business.

## Price

| Tier | Price | Use When |
|---|---:|---|
| Emergency close | $150 | Warm contact, same-day payment, simple business |
| Standard | $200 | Default offer |
| Strong prospect | $300 | Larger business, more assets, more ability to pay |

## Deliverables

The buyer gets:
- 1-page audit
- Scorecard across website, Google/profile, offer, proof, content, and follow-up
- 5 priority fixes
- 3 ready-to-use content or outreach ideas
- 1 offer improvement
- Optional next-step implementation menu

## Scope Boundaries

Included:
- Public website review
- Google Business Profile or social profile review if visible
- Offer clarity review
- Proof/trust review
- Basic follow-up path review

Not included:
- Full redesign
- Ad management
- CRM build
- Private dashboard analysis unless the client provides exports
- Guaranteed lead volume

## Risk Reversal

Use this:

```text
If the audit does not give you at least 5 specific fixes you can act on, I will refund it or redo it.
```

## First Paid Yes

Close with:

```text
If you want it, send me your website/profile link and I can do it today. It is $200, and I can send payment details now.
```

If they hesitate:

```text
We can do 50% now and 50% when I send it. I just need a paid yes before I block the time.
```

## Upsell

After delivery:

| Fix Type | Implementation Price |
|---|---:|
| Rewrite homepage hero + CTA | $300-$500 |
| Google/profile improvement pass | $250-$500 |
| 5 follow-up messages | $250-$500 |
| Offer page rewrite | $500-$1,000 |
| 1-week asset cleanup sprint | $750-$1,500 |
