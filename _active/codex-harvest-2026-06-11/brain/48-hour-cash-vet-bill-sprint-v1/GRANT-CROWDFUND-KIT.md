## Assistance Paths

| Program | Best Fit | What They Usually Need | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| RedRover Urgent Care | Urgent/lifesaving care with small funding gap | Diagnosis, treatment plan, estimate | https://redrover.org/relief/urgent-care-grants/ |
| Frankie's Friends | Emergency or specialty care where pet may suffer, be euthanized, or be relinquished without care | Vet has seen pet, good prognosis, estimate, treatment not yet paid | https://www.frankiesfriends.org/national-frankies-friends-fund |
| Waggle | Public fundraising where donors want funds sent directly to the vet | Vet info, estimate/invoice, photos, story | https://www.waggle.org/ |
| 211 | Other bills competing with vet care | ZIP code and bill details | https://www.211.org/get-help/utilities-expenses |

## Short Hardship Statement

Use this and edit the bracketed parts:

```text
I am seeking help with veterinary care for my dog, [DOG NAME]. I currently have about $300 available and am facing urgent bills, including this vet appointment and basic living expenses. I am trying to handle this responsibly by getting a written estimate, asking the clinic for payment options, and applying only for direct-to-vet assistance.

[DOG NAME] is important to me because [1-2 personal sentences]. I am asking for help only for the treatment costs the veterinarian confirms are needed. Any funds should be sent directly to the veterinary clinic whenever possible.
```

```text
Any help, even a small amount or a share, helps close the gap and gets [DOG NAME] closer to care.
```

## Grant Application Notes

- Do not say treatment is already paid if it is not.
- Do not pay the full bill before applying to programs that do not reimburse.
- Ask the vet for a clear prognosis, because many programs require a good prognosis with treatment.
- Keep screenshots, estimates, and emails in one folder.
- If one program declines, use the same prepared materials for the next one.
