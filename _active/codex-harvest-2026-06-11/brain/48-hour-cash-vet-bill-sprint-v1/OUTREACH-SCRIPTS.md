# Outreach Scripts

## Warm Contact Text

```text
Hey [Name], quick ask. I am doing a few 24-hour AI Business Asset Audits for local businesses today.

I review the website/profile/offer/follow-up and send a short action plan with the fastest fixes to get more trust or leads from what is already there.

I thought of you because [specific reason]. I am doing them for $200 today. Want me to look at yours?
```

## Warmer, More Personal Version

```text
Hey [Name], I am trying to generate some fast cash for an urgent vet bill, so I am selling a few useful 24-hour business audits today instead of asking for random help.

For $200, I will review your customer-facing assets and send a short action plan: 5 fixes, 3 content/outreach ideas, and 1 offer improvement.

I thought of you because [specific business reason]. Want one?
```

## Referral Ask

```text
Hey [Name], do you know any business owners who could use a quick outside review of their website, Google/profile, offer, or follow-up?

I am doing 24-hour AI Business Asset Audits today for $200. It is a short action plan, not a big agency pitch. If someone wants one, I can deliver it within 24 hours.
```

## Cold DM

```text
Hey [Name], I was looking at [business/profile] and noticed [specific fixable issue].

Most [business type] lose trust here because [short consequence].

I am doing 24-hour Business Asset Audits this week. It is a short review of your website/profile/offer/follow-up with 5 fixes and 3 quick content or outreach ideas.

It is $200 and delivered within 24 hours. Want me to send you what I would review?
```

## Cold Email

Subject options:
- Quick note on [Business Name]
- Saw one fix on your [website/profile]
- 24-hour asset audit for [Business Name]

Body:

```text
Hi [Name],

I was reviewing [Business Name] and noticed [specific observation].

That matters because [business consequence in plain language].

I am doing 24-hour Business Asset Audits for local service businesses. The audit covers your website/profile, offer, trust signals, content, and follow-up, then gives you:

- 5 priority fixes
- 3 content or outreach ideas
- 1 offer improvement

It is $200 and delivered within 24 hours. No retainer required.

Want me to review [Business Name]?

[Your Name]
```

## Follow-Up 1: Day 1 / Same Day

```text
Quick bump on this. I can still fit one more audit in today if you want the 24-hour turnaround.
```

## Follow-Up 2: Day 2

```text
One specific thing I would look at first: [insert visible issue]. That is usually where trust or lead flow leaks fastest.

Still want me to do the full pass?
```

## Follow-Up 3: Day 4

```text
Would it help if I sent the audit outline first so you can see exactly what you would get?
```

## Close If Interested

```text
Great. Send me the best website/profile links and anything specific you want me to check.

The audit is $200. I can start once payment is sent and deliver it within 24 hours.
```
