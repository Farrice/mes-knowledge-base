# Consumer Posture ICP

## One-Person Profile

Name: Local Owner "Maya"

Maya runs a service business with 2-10 people. She gets customers from referrals, Google, Instagram, or local word of mouth. She knows the business is good, but the online front door does not show it.

She is not shopping for a large agency. She is open to a sharp, low-risk review if it feels practical and specific.

## What She Wants

- More calls or bookings
- Better trust before the first conversation
- Fewer people asking basic questions
- A clearer offer
- Simple actions she can hand to herself, a VA, or a freelancer

## What She Fears

- Paying for vague marketing advice
- Getting sold a complicated monthly package
- Feeling embarrassed by weak website/social presence
- Spending money and still not knowing what to do

## Best Channels

| Channel | Use |
|---|---|
| Warm text | Fastest for people already known |
| Instagram DM | Best for local service businesses active on social |
| Email | Best for website-based businesses |
| Facebook Messenger | Good for owners already connected |
| LinkedIn DM | Good for coaches, consultants, realtors, B2B service owners |

## Best Prospect Types

Start with people who can say yes quickly:

1. Business owners you know personally
2. Friends of friends who own businesses
3. Local dog/pet businesses
4. Service businesses with weak websites
5. Coaches or consultants with unclear offers
6. Realtors with stale profiles
7. Contractors with weak Google profiles
8. Med spas, salons, chiropractors, dentists, gyms

## Minimum Viable Qualification

A prospect is worth contacting today if:
- You can find one visible issue in under 5 minutes
- They can likely afford $150-$300
- The owner or decision-maker is reachable
- The business would benefit from trust, clarity, or follow-up fixes
