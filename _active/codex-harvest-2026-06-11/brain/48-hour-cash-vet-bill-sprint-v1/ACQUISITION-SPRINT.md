# Acquisition Sprint

## Daily Numbers

| Metric | Day 1 Target | Day 2 Target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Warm contacts messaged | 15 | 10 follow-ups |
| Cold prospects researched | 25 | 25 |
| Cold messages sent | 25 | 25 |
| Referral asks sent | 10 | 10 |
| Paid audits sold | 1-3 | 1-2 |
| Cash target | $150-$600 | $300-$1,000 cumulative |

## Prospect List Criteria

Find businesses where one issue is visible fast:
- Website headline is vague
- No clear call to action
- Reviews are not highlighted
- Google profile is thin or stale
- Instagram has activity but no offer
- Service page is unclear
- No follow-up path is visible

## 5-Minute Research Pass

For each prospect:

1. Open website or profile.
2. Find one visible trust or clarity problem.
3. Write that problem in the tracker.
4. Pick the best script.
5. Send the message.

## Outreach Block

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0-20 min | Fill 10 warm contacts |
| 20-50 min | Send warm/referral texts |
| 50-110 min | Research 15 cold prospects |
| 110-150 min | Send 15 cold messages |
| 150-180 min | Follow up with responders and ask for payment |

## Payment Ask

Use one of these:

```text
The audit is $200. I can start once payment is sent and deliver within 24 hours.
```

```text
If easier, we can do $100 now and $100 when I send the audit.
```

```text
If you want the smaller version, I can do the top 5 fixes only for $150.
```

## Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning | Next Action |
|---|---|---|
| Not contacted | In list only | Send first message |
| Open | Message sent, no reply | Follow up |
| Responded | They replied | Ask qualifying question or close |
| Payment pending | They said yes but have not paid | Send payment reminder |
| Sold | Payment received | Deliver audit |
| Delivered | Audit sent | Offer implementation |
| Dead | Declined | Stop |
