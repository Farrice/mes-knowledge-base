## Cold Prospect Categories

Pick 3 categories and find 8-10 businesses in each:

| Category | Why It Works For This Audit |
|---|---|
| Dog groomers / trainers / boarding | Pet-related angle makes the outreach natural |
| Contractors | Often have weak websites and strong local demand |
| Realtors | Profile, trust, and follow-up matter immediately |
| Med spas / salons | Offers and proof can often be improved quickly |
| Chiropractors / wellness clinics | Trust and clarity gaps are common |
| Coaches / consultants | Offer clarity is often the main issue |
| Local ecommerce brands | Product pages, proof, and follow-up can be audited fast |
| Gyms / studios | Promotions and local trust signals matter |

## 5-Minute Prospect Scoring

Score each prospect from 0-2:

| Signal | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Can reach owner | No clear contact | Contact form only | Owner/profile visible |
| Visible issue | None obvious | One mild issue | One clear issue |
| Can pay $150-$300 | Unclear | Maybe | Likely |
| Would benefit fast | Low | Medium | High |

Contact anyone scoring 5+.

## Observation Bank

Use one of these only if it is true:

- Your homepage says what you do, but the next step is not clear.
- Your Google/profile has reviews, but they are not being used as proof on the website.
- Your Instagram shows good work, but there is no clear offer or booking path.
- Your website has services listed, but no simple reason to choose you over nearby options.
- Your profile looks active, but the call to action is buried.
- Your business has strong work, but the online first impression undersells it.
