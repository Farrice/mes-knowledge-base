## Same-Day Cash Target

Sell the `AI Business Asset Audit`.

Offer:

> A 24-hour review of a small business's website, Google profile, offer, follow-up, and content, with a short action plan showing the fastest fixes to get more leads or trust from what they already have.

Price:
- Emergency close price: $150
- Standard same-day price: $200
- Strong warm prospect price: $300

Rule:
- Ask for payment upfront.
- If they hesitate, accept 50% upfront and 50% on delivery.
- Do not start work with no payment unless it is a strategic sample for a high-quality referral source.

## Non-Negotiables

- No payday loans.
- No gambling, crypto flips, or "quick investment" schemes.
- No jobs that require upfront fees.
- No fake-check work, reshipping packages, or account access tasks.
- No private client dashboard claims unless the client gives exported data or access.

## Acceptance Criteria

This sprint is working when:
- The vet has provided a written estimate or next-step cost range.
- You know whether the clinic accepts direct third-party payment.
- At least one grant/crowdfund path is ready before the appointment.
- 40 prospects are listed and contacted.
- At least 3 calls/conversations are active.
- At least one paid audit is sold or a direct referral source is activated.
