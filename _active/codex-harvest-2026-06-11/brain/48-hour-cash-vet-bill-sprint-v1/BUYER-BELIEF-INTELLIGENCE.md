# Buyer Belief Intelligence

## Specific Buyer

The fastest buyer is not a large company. It is a local owner or solo operator who can approve a $150-$300 purchase today.

Best buyer:
- Owns or manages a local service business
- Knows their online presence is weak
- Has no time to diagnose the problem
- Wants more calls, bookings, trust, or referrals
- Can pay without a committee

## Buyer Worldview

They do not want an abstract AI service. They want someone to look at their business like a customer and tell them what to fix first.

They believe:
- "I know my marketing could be better, but I do not know where to start."
- "Agencies overcomplicate this."
- "I do not want a big retainer right now."
- "If someone shows me clear problems and quick fixes, I will pay for that."

## Decision Trigger

They are most likely to say yes when the outreach points to one visible issue:
- Confusing homepage
- Weak Google Business Profile
- No clear offer
- No recent reviews
- No simple follow-up path
- Content that does not make the business look active
- A competitor looks more trustworthy online

## Belief Shift Needed

Move them from:

> "This is another person selling marketing."

To:

> "This person already spotted something specific and can give me a short, useful plan today."

## Proof Needed

Because this is a fast, low-ticket audit, proof can be simple:
- Specific observation in the first message
- Clear deliverable list
- 24-hour delivery promise
- Sample of the exact audit format
- Small price with no retainer pressure
- Offer to apply the audit fee to implementation if they hire for fixes

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| I do not need AI | "This is not an AI pitch. It is a customer-facing asset audit. AI just helps move faster." |
| I am busy | "That is why it is built as a 1-page action plan. No long meeting required." |
| I do not have budget | "That is why I am keeping this at $150-$200 instead of selling a large package." |
| What do I get? | "A scored review, 5 fixes, 3 content/outreach ideas, and one offer improvement." |
| Can you just tell me now? | "I can share the first observation. The paid audit is the full review and action list." |
