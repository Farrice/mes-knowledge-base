# Coach Cooz LinkedIn Profile V5 - Launch Ready

## Profile Job

The V5 profile should help Cooz win three moments:

1. The profile check after a warm referral.
2. The profile visit after a DM or comment.
3. The trust check before someone replies with a real problem.

The profile should not sound like a trainer trying to motivate people.

It should sound like a serious coach naming the pattern the buyer has been minimizing.

## Recommended Headline

Use this:

> Private strength + energy coach for professionals over 40 whose body is no longer keeping up with their life | Burbank + remote

Why this is the V5 headline:

- "Private strength + energy coach" gives a clear category.
- "Professionals over 40" names the buyer fast.
- "Body is no longer keeping up with their life" is more immediate than "starting to fall behind" without sounding dramatic.
- "Burbank + remote" removes delivery friction.

## Why This Version Is Stronger Than V4

V4 was clean and clear.

V5 puts more pressure on the buyer's private reality:

- the decline does not happen all at once
- the buyer is not a beginner

- diagnosis makes Cooz feel premium before price is discussed
- Resurrection lands after the practical offer is already clear
