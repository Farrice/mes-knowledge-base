# Outreach And Feed Analysis Prompts

## Compliance Boundary

Use AI to prepare thinking, not to automate LinkedIn activity.

LinkedIn prohibits third-party software, crawlers, bots, plug-ins, extensions, scripts, and other tools that scrape data or automate activity such as messaging, commenting, liking, sharing, or fake engagement. Keep all comments and DMs manual, reviewed, and context-specific.

Source: [LinkedIn prohibited software and extensions](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387/prohibited-software-and-extensions?lang=en)

## Daily Manual Operating Loop

| Step | Time | Action |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 5 min | Search or browse manually for posts about AI workflows, founder-led marketing, content systems, agency delivery, SOPs, proposals, or sales follow-up. |
| 2 | 15 min | Analyze 10 posts with the prompt below. |
| 3 | 20 min | Leave 5-8 manual comments that add a specific distinction. |
| 4 | 10 min | Send 3-5 contextual DMs only when there is engagement or obvious public-fit signal. |
| 5 | 5 min | Log pain language, reply, artifact offered, and next action. |

## Browser Assistant Prompt: Feed Scan

Use this only for visible posts you manually select or text you paste. Do not scrape profiles, auto-comment, auto-like, auto-DM, or collect bulk data.

```text
You are helping me prepare manual LinkedIn comments.

Context:
- My positioning: Creative Strategist + AI Operating Partner.
- My offer: Marketing System Misfire Map / AI Misfire Express.
- My thesis: AI output looks finished but still needs expert rescue when customer language, founder judgment, proof standards, angle logic, or source-of-truth rules are missing.
- I will manually choose whether to comment. Do not automate LinkedIn actions.

Analyze the pasted post or visible post text below.

Return:
1. Is this a fit for my ICP? Score 1-5.
2. What buyer pain or belief is visible?
3. What is the non-obvious operating-layer angle?
4. What should I not say because it would sound pitchy?
5. Draft 3 manual comment options:
   - one short agreement + distinction
   - one contrarian-but-respectful reframe
   - one practical diagnostic question
6. If and only if the person signals a real problem, draft one contextual DM bridge.

Post text:
[paste here]
```

## Contextual DM Prompt

```text
Draft a short LinkedIn DM.

Context:
- This person engaged with my post/comment or publicly described an AI/workflow/content/proposal/SOP pain.
- I do not want to sound needy, spammy, or like I am selling automation.
- The goal is to invite one concrete artifact, not a call.

Use this frame:
"Where is AI already close to useful in your business, but still wrong enough that someone senior has to correct it?"

Write:
1. a soft version
2. a more direct version
3. a follow-up if they say "interesting"
4. a paid bridge only if they send an artifact and the problem is clearly deeper than a prompt tweak

Person/context:
[paste here]
```

## Manual DM Templates

### Warm Engagement

```text
Appreciate you jumping into that thread.

Quick question: where is AI already close to useful in your business, but still wrong enough that someone senior has to correct it?

If you have one output, prompt, SOP, or workflow like that, send it over. I can point out where the system is being forced to guess.
```

### Public Pain Signal

```text
Saw your post about [specific pain].

The part I keep seeing is that AI can do the task, but it starts guessing when the judgment behind the work is still invisible.

If you have one example that came back "almost right," I can show you the guess-points.
```

### Paid Bridge

```text
This looks bigger than a prompt tweak.

The useful next step is a Marketing System Misfire Map. I would map the guess-points, the missing judgment/source of truth, and the smallest fix: prompt rewrite, SOP cleanup, or work primitive.

The founding diagnostic is $300 while I am building proof.
```

## Prospect Fit Filters

| Signal | Fit |
|---|---|
| Founder/consultant/agency/operator posts about AI-generated work needing review | Strong |
| Talks about content systems, offers, customer language, proposals, SOPs, delivery quality | Strong |
| Complains AI sounds generic or misses nuance | Strong |
| Wants tool recommendations only | Medium |
| Talks about replacing humans with AI at scale | Weak unless they mention quality failures |
| Pure crypto, spam, engagement bait, or hustle content | Reject |
