# Market Research, ICP, And Product Offer

## Market Thesis

The winning lane is not "AI consulting" in the abstract.

The lane is:

> Founder-led experts are using AI, but their marketing and delivery systems still depend on invisible judgment. AI can produce the task, but it cannot reliably execute the work until customer language, angle logic, proof standards, and decision rules are made visible.

## Core ICP

### ICP 1: Founder-Led Expert Paying The AI Rescue Tax

| Dimension | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who | Consultant, coach, agency owner, fractional expert, advisor, specialist service founder. |
| Situation | Already using AI for content, sales, proposals, SOPs, or client delivery. |
| Felt moment | "It looks right, but I still cannot send it." |
| Hidden pain | Their judgment is protecting the work but not captured in a reusable system. |
| Trigger | They keep correcting the same output or team-generated AI drafts. |
| Best CTA | "Send the last output you had to rewrite." |
| Paid path | $300-$500 diagnostic, then $1,500-$2,000 implementation sprint if one workflow needs a work primitive. |

### ICP 2: AI-Forward Agency With Delivery Risk

| Dimension | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who | Small agency selling AI, content, ops, automation, or growth systems. |
| Situation | They can produce outputs quickly but need better diagnosis before implementation. |
| Felt moment | "The client wants AI, but their workflow is not ready for agents." |
| Hidden pain | They risk overbuilding automation on unclear work. |
| Trigger | Client output breaks because SOPs, proof, buyer language, or approval rules are vague. |
| Best CTA | "Use the misfire map before you build the automation." |
| Paid path | White-label diagnostic or implementation support. |

### ICP 3: Creator-Consultant Turning IP Into A System

| Dimension | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who | Expert with frameworks, content, audience, methods, or client delivery IP. |
| Situation | Wants scale without flattening voice, taste, or nuance. |
| Felt moment | "My best work still lives in my head." |
| Hidden pain | AI cannot replicate what has never been made explicit. |
| Trigger | Their content or offer drafts sound generic even after "voice" prompts. |
| Best CTA | "Show me where the AI flattened the judgment." |
| Paid path | Buyer-language system, content primitive, offer primitive, or delivery primitive. |

## Product Offer

### Offer Name

**Marketing System Misfire Map**

Secondary name when the buyer is more AI-aware:

**AI Misfire Express**

### One-Line Promise

Send one AI output, prompt, SOP, workflow, sales follow-up, proposal, or content process. I will show where the system guessed and what marketing judgment, customer language, source of truth, or work primitive would fix it.

### Deliverable

| Component | Buyer Value |
|---|---|
| Misfire Map | 3-7 places AI was forced to guess. |
| Judgment Diagnosis | What founder/strategist judgment was missing. |
| Customer-Language Gap | Where the output drifted from buyer reality. |
| Source-Of-Truth Fix | What rule, example, boundary, or proof source should exist. |
| First Work Primitive Sketch | The smallest reusable operating layer for that workflow. |
| Loom Walkthrough | 5-8 minute walkthrough of the diagnosis. |
| Next-Step Recommendation | Prompt fix, SOP cleanup, work primitive, or not ready to automate. |

### Pricing Ladder

| Tier | Price | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Founding Diagnostic | $300 | First proof-building buyers. |
| Standard Misfire Map | $500-$750 | Normal paid diagnostic. |
| Express Map | $1,000 | Faster turnaround or more complex workflow. |
| Implementation Sprint | $1,500-$2,500 | Convert one repeated workflow into an agent-ready primitive. |

### Stripe / Checkout Copy

**Title:** Marketing System Misfire Map  
**Subtitle:** Find where your AI output is being forced to guess.  
**Description:** Send one AI output, prompt, SOP, workflow, proposal, sales follow-up, or content process. I will map the guess-points, identify the missing judgment or source of truth, and recommend the smallest fix before you build more automation.  
**Buyer note:** This is a diagnostic, not a full automation build. If the fix is only a prompt, I will say that. If the workflow needs a deeper operating layer, I will show the first primitive to build.
