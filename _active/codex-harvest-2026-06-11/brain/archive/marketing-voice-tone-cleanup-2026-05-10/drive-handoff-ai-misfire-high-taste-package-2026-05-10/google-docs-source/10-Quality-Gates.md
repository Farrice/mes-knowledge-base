# Quality Gates

## Market Freshness Gate

Pass if:

- Every market claim is backed by a 2026 source dated on or before May 8, 2026.
- Current competitor claims are sourced from live web pages or labeled as observed competitor positioning.
- Reddit/community signals are used as buyer-language inputs, not as market proof when source dates are older.
- Future-dated sources are excluded.

Fail if:

- 2025 evidence becomes the primary proof layer.
- A claim says the offer is validated before real buyer signal exists.
- Search snippets are treated as confirmed outcomes without page support.

## Avatar Gate

Pass if:

- The avatar reads like one person.
- The profile names private thoughts, trigger events, failed attempts, objections, and trust bridges.
- The language bank sounds like a buyer, not a strategist.
- Secondary lanes are entry paths, not separate campaigns.

Fail if:

- The avatar is demographic-only.
- The buyer sounds too aware too early.
- The content assumes they already want "semantic document libraries."

## Oren Oracle/Catalyst Gate

Pass if:

- The post names a hidden pattern.
- The reader gets a practical next action.
- The tone is premium, specific, and non-hype.
- The content reinforces Creative Strategist + AI Operating Partner.

Fail if:

- The post is abstract insight with no use.
- The post is generic motivation with no diagnosis.
- The offer language sounds like commodity AI automation.

## Donald Miller Clarity Gate

Pass if:

- The customer is the hero.
- The problem is clear in the first few lines.
- The plan is simple: send one misfire, map the guess-points, decide the fix.
- The CTA is concrete.
- A non-technical founder can understand without "context engineering."

Fail if:

- The copy leads with the mechanism before the pain.
- The reader has to understand semantic documents.
- The CTA asks for a broad call before proof.

## Platform Gate

### LinkedIn

Pass if:

- First 50 words name a felt problem.
- First 50 words include tension, scene, confession, or specific recognition.
- One CTA.
- Comment-worthy distinction.
- No generic AI trend hook.
- No post sounds interchangeable with another AI consultant.
- Most posts use soft endings or open loops; hard CTA is reserved for bridge posts.

Fail if:

- The post explains the idea before the reader feels the problem.
- The opening could be written by any consultant.
- The post has no proof, scene, or lived detail.
- Every post asks for a DM keyword.

### Instagram

Pass if:

- Save/share reason is obvious.
- Carousel has one idea per slide.
- Reel can be understood with captions.
- Visuals show diagnostic clarity, not AI spectacle.

### YouTube/Loom

Pass if:

- Misfire appears in the first minute.
- Theory follows proof.
- Viewer sees where AI guessed.
- CTA asks for one artifact.

### Newsletter/Blog

Pass if:

- Durable distinction.
- Current source support.
- Practical diagnostic.
- Leads toward proof or audit.

## Offer Gate

Pass if:

- Every asset routes toward AI Misfire Map or AI Misfire Express.
- The audit is scoped to one artifact/workflow.
- The output does not promise full implementation.
- It names the possible outcomes: prompt fix, SOP rewrite, work primitive, or not ready to automate.

Fail if:

- The offer sounds like prompt cleanup only.
- The offer sounds like enterprise transformation.
- The offer overpromises ROI.
- The offer hides the price or the next step when selling directly.

## Red-Team Gate

Check:

- Is there hype?
- Is there fake validation?
- Are we implying guaranteed ROI?
- Are we claiming buyer demand before signal?
- Does any post sound like generic AI consulting?
- Does the package over-expand beyond the cash/signal path?

Required corrections:

- Label hypotheses.
- Remove broad claims.
- Replace jargon with buyer language.
- Add proof or soften claim.
- Keep the first action small.

## Anti-Slop Gate

Reject if the asset:

- could be written by any AI consultant
- says "unlock," "revolutionize," or "10x" as a promise
- explains AI trends without a buyer moment
- has no useful distinction
- has no clear CTA
- does not make the reader better off

## Voice + Proof Gate

Pass if:

- The post includes at least one lived proof source: personal story, process proof, internal audit, market source, or demonstrable example.
- The post contains a Farrice fingerprint: fatherhood, compressed work window, personal training, gaming/taste, anti-guru stance, systems-and-soul language, or a specific line only he would use.
- Every significant claim has proof within two sentences or is softened into a thesis.
- The post has at least one "laugh-exhale," wince, or "damn, true" moment.
- The reader can understand the point without knowing "semantic documents," "context engineering," or internal system language.

Fail if:

- The content is strategically correct but emotionally flat.
- The post names a pain point without staging a recognizable moment.
- The copy uses AI jargon to compensate for weak proof.
- The post teaches a lesson but creates no perspective shift.
- Farrice's lived judgment is hidden while the post talks about invisible judgment.
