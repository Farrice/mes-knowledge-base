# Message Clarity Pack: AI Misfire Map

## One-Hole Lock

| Field | Lock |
|---|---|
| Business | AI Misfire Map, delivered through Farrice's Creative Strategist + AI Operating Partner service |
| Customer | Founder-led experts, consultants, small agencies, and AI-forward service teams already using AI for marketing, content, offers, proposals, SOPs, or client work |
| Offer | Send one AI-assisted work artifact. Receive a Misfire Map, short Loom, first missing rules, first reusable primitive sketch, and next-step recommendation. |
| One-hole problem | Your AI output looks finished but you still cannot ship it. |
| Why this problem wins | It names the buyer's felt moment before the offer explains the mechanism. It is more repeatable than "agent-ready primitive," "semantic library," or "founding proof." |

### Naming Lock

Use **AI Misfire Map** as the cold public name.

Use **Marketing System Misfire Map** only when the buyer already understands this is a marketing-system problem.

Use **AI Misfire Founding Proof** as an internal sprint/package label for the first paid proof buyers, not as the main cold-market sound bite.

## Cognitive-Load Autopsy

| Current Or Candidate Phrase | Load | Why It Is Heavy | Zero-Load Rewrite |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Creative Strategist + AI Operating Partner | 20 | Strong positioning, but "AI Operating Partner" needs context before a cold buyer understands the outcome. | I help you turn your judgment into rules AI can use. |
| AI Misfire Founding Proof | 35 | "Founding Proof" is internal proof-building language. The buyer has to decode why it matters. | AI Misfire Map |
| Marketing System Misfire Map | 15 | Useful for warm buyers, but "marketing system" adds a layer before the pain is clear. | AI Misfire Map |
| Agent-ready primitive | 45 | Insider system term. Accurate backend language, heavy front-end language. | A reusable rule for this kind of work. |
| Semantic document library | 60 | Accurate architecture term, but cold buyers cannot picture it quickly. | The missing instructions behind the work. |
| Context engineering | 35 | Emerging expert term. Use only after the buyer recognizes the misfire. | Giving AI the context it needs before it decides. |
| Buyer language, proof logic, CTA timing, taste rules, source of truth | 25 | The list is valuable but too many categories at once for first-touch copy. | The rules the AI was missing. |
| Send one AI-assisted marketing artifact you had to rescue | 5 | Clear and buyer-felt, but "artifact" is slightly technical. | Send one AI output you still could not ship. |
| Your AI is not bad. It is blind. | 5 | Memorable, but metaphorical. Works as a post line, not the core offer lock. | Your AI is missing the rules behind the work. |

### Phrases To Avoid In First-Touch Copy

- AI transformation
- Agentic workflow governance
- Semantic infrastructure
- Future-proof your business
- Prompt engineering secrets
- Done-for-you AI automations
- Unlock the power of AI
- Founding Proof as the cold offer name

## StoryBrand One-Liner

- **Core:** Most founder-led experts use AI but still cannot ship the output without rescuing it. I map where the AI was forced to guess so they can make their judgment reusable and ship AI work they trust.
- **Conversation:** You know when AI gives you something that looks done, but you still cannot send it? I find where it guessed and turn that correction into a reusable rule.
- **Social Bio:** AI Misfire Map: send one AI output you cannot ship. I show where it guessed so your judgment becomes reusable.
- **Email Signature:** AI Misfire Map - Ship AI work you can trust.
- **Pitch:** Most founder-led experts are already using AI, but the output still needs a senior rescue before it can touch customers. The AI Misfire Map finds where the model guessed, names the missing rule or source of truth, and sketches the first reusable fix so the same rescue work stops staying in your head.
