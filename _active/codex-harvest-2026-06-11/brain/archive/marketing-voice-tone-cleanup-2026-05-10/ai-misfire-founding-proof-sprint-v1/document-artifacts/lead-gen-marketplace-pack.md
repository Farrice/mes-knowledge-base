## Marketplace Proposal Opener

<!-- publishable:marketplace-opener:start -->
Most AI workflow projects fail one layer too late.

They try to build before diagnosing where the model is already guessing.

For this project, I would start with one real artifact or workflow from your business and map the first three missing rules: source of truth, buyer language, proof logic, quality bar, taste boundary, or escalation trigger.

That gives you a cleaner build spec before anyone wastes time automating the wrong behavior.
