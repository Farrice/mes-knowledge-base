## Day 2 Before/After Teardown Post

```text
Here is what I look for when an AI draft feels almost usable.

Normal prompt:

"Write a follow-up DM to a founder-led consultant who asked about using AI to improve their content workflow."

The AI wrote something clean:

"I help consultants use AI to produce more content without losing their voice..."

That sounds fine.

It is also the problem.

Three places it guessed:

1. It assumed the next step was a call.

But the right next step was one submitted artifact. A call ask creates friction before proof.

2. It used generic AI benefit language.

"Produce more content" and "without losing your voice" make this sound like every AI content consultant.

The real pain is sharper:

"AI is close, but someone senior still has to rescue it."

3. It implied transformation before diagnosis.

That is false confidence.

The honest move is:

"Send one example. I will tell you if this is a prompt issue, SOP issue, or deeper operating-layer issue."

This is the difference between prompt polish and an AI Misfire Map.

The prompt is where the symptom appears.

The missing judgment is where the problem lives.

If you have one AI output that looks finished but still feels wrong, send it.

I will show where it guessed.
```
