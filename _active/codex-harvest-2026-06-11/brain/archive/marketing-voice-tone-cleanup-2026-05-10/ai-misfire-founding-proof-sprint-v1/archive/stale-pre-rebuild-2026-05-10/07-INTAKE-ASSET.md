## What This Is

Most AI failures are not obvious.

The output can look structured, polished, and helpful while still missing the work.

That usually happens when the prompt or SOP leaves out:

- customer language
- founder judgment
- source of truth
- examples
- counterexamples
- edge cases
- decision rules
- quality standards
- approval boundaries

A human fills those gaps from experience.

AI fills them from probability.

That is the misfire.
