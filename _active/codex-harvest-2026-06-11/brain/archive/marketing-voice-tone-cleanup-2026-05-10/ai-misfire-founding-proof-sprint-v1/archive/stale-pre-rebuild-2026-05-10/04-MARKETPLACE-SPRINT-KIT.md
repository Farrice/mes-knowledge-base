## Buyer Requirements

```text
1. What are you sending? Prompt, SOP, workflow, failed AI output, proposal, sales follow-up, content process, or other.
2. Paste or attach the artifact.
3. What was AI supposed to do?
4. What did it produce or misunderstand?
5. What did a human have to correct?
6. How often does this happen?
7. What does this cost you: time, trust, quality, revenue, delivery, or speed?
8. Are there any claims, private details, or client data I should avoid showing in the Loom?
```

For this kind of project, I offer a focused AI Misfire Founding Proof diagnostic:

- review one prompt, SOP, workflow, or failed output
- map 3-7 places AI is guessing

It is $300, delivered in 24-48 hours, and credited toward deeper implementation if you want me to build the fix afterward.

If you want, send the workflow or failed output first. I can tell pretty quickly whether this is a prompt issue or a deeper operating-layer issue.

## Apply / Skip Rules

Apply when:

- the buyer already uses AI
- they mention workflow, SOP, content, proposals, follow-up, documentation, prompts, or output quality
- they describe repeated correction or inconsistent output
- the job can start with one artifact
- the budget is at least $300 or the buyer seems serious

Skip when:

- they want cheap prompt packs
- they want data scraping or spam automation
- they want guaranteed revenue
- they need full implementation before diagnosis
- they are asking for banned platform automation
- the job is pure coding outside the offer
