Search people/businesses who visibly need judgment-heavy workflows:

1. AI-forward consultants selling automation but posting generic proof.
2. Coaches/consultants using AI content but still sounding generic.
3. Small agencies selling AI services without visible diagnostic frameworks.
4. Founder-led B2B experts posting strong ideas but no reusable content/workflow system.
5. Service businesses with sales follow-up, proposal, onboarding, or delivery workflows.

## Manual Prospect Filter

Pursue only if at least 3 are true:

- They already talk about AI.
- They sell expertise, not a commodity.
- Their work depends on voice, judgment, taste, trust, or nuance.
- They have a repeated workflow.
- Their content or offer suggests "almost useful but still needs rescue."
- They can pay $300 without a committee.

Skip if:

- They only want cheap prompts.
- They need full implementation immediately.
- They are pre-revenue with no workflow.
- They ask for scraping, spam, fake engagement, or banned automation.
- The problem is pure engineering outside this offer.

## General Proposal Template

```text
I would not start by adding more AI steps.

I would start by finding where the current workflow is forcing AI to guess.

That usually shows up when the output looks close, but someone still has to correct the same kinds of things: source of truth, examples, quality bar, edge cases, decision rules, or customer language.

I can start with a focused diagnostic on one prompt, SOP, workflow, or failed output:

- map 3-7 places AI is guessing
- identify missing judgment/source-of-truth
- sketch the first agent-ready fix
- recommend whether this needs a prompt tweak, SOP cleanup, or deeper workflow build

That keeps the project from scaling unclear work.
```
