## 90-Second Video Script

```text
Here is what I look for when AI-assisted marketing sounds right but misses the work.

This prompt looks reasonable:

"Write a follow-up DM to a founder-led consultant who asked about using AI to improve their content workflow."

The AI wrote something clean:

"I help consultants use AI to produce more content without losing their voice. Happy to hop on a quick call and show you how AI can transform your workflow."

Nothing about that is obviously broken.

That is the problem.

It made three guesses.

That is false confidence.

That is an AI misfire.

The model did the task.

It did not understand the marketing work.
```
