## Objection Handling

### "Is this just prompt engineering?"

```text
Sometimes the fix is a better prompt.

But if the same correction keeps happening, the prompt is usually where the symptom appears, not where the marketing problem lives.

The audit maps the missing customer perception, proof logic, judgment, and source of truth behind the prompt.
```

### "I do not have a clean workflow."

```text
That is fine. Messy is often better.

The goal is not to judge the document. The goal is to find the moment AI has to fill in blanks a human would fill from experience.
```

### "Can you just fix it?"

```text
Maybe.

The diagnostic tells us whether this needs a prompt fix, an SOP cleanup, a work primitive, or no automation yet.

I would rather find that before building the wrong thing faster.
```

### "Why pay for an audit before implementation?"

```text
Because faster flat marketing is not leverage.

If the customer perception, proof logic, and offer judgment are missing, implementation just gives the AI more room to guess.
```
