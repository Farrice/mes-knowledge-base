05 Revenue Outreach Signal Pipeline

Revenue Objective

Near-term cash goal:

Generate $500-$2,000 as quickly as legitimate buyer signal allows, without fake urgency, spam, or generic automation.

Primary vehicle:

AI Misfire Express.

Operating Principle

Do not sell a giant operating system first.

Sell a small diagnostic that proves the buyer has a repeated cleanup problem:

Send one misfire. I will show where AI is being forced to guess.

If the diagnosis reveals a repeated workflow problem, offer implementation.

72-Hour Sprint

Day 1: Proof And Direct Signal

Assets:

  • one flagship LinkedIn post
  • one 5-minute Loom script
  • one short intake page or Notion form
  • one manual outreach list
  • one signal tracker

Actions:

  • publish the flagship post
  • manually engage for 30 minutes
  • send warm DMs to people already interested in AI, content, operations, consulting, or service delivery
  • ask for one misfire, not a call
  • log responses

Day 2: Diagnosis And Follow-Up

Actions:

  • diagnose any submitted artifacts
  • record short Looms for qualified submissions
  • identify repeated pain language
  • ask if they want the paid audit when there is clear business pain
  • publish supporting post around the strongest repeated signal

Day 3: Close Or Learn

Actions:

  • follow up with qualified conversations
  • offer paid audit or implementation sprint
  • update offer language based on objections
  • decide whether the segment, CTA, or price needs adjustment

Outreach List

Start with:

  • founder-led consultants
  • coaches with content/proposal/sales workflows
  • small agencies using AI in delivery
  • AI-forward solo operators
  • expert businesses with repeated content or client-delivery processes

Do not start with:

  • enterprise AI teams
  • people who are not using AI at all
  • people who want only prompt packs
  • buyers with no repeated workflow

Manual Connection Message

Use when relevant:

Saw your work around [specific business/topic].

I'm talking with founder-led experts who are using AI but still having to rescue the output when it misses nuance, customer language, or judgment.

Curious if that is showing up anywhere in your workflow yet.

Warm DM After Engagement

Appreciate you engaging with that post.

Where is AI already close to useful in your business, but still wrong enough that someone has to correct it?

If you have one prompt, SOP, workflow, draft, or failed output, I can show you where it is being forced to guess.

Paid Audit Bridge

That sounds like the exact pattern I am mapping.

The paid version is a Misfire Map:

- one workflow or output
- one written diagnosis
- one Loom walkthrough
- the missing judgment/rule/source-of-truth map
- first recommended fix

For the first few, I am keeping it between $500 and $750 so I can build proof while making it genuinely useful.

Qualification Rules

Qualified:

  • has a real artifact
  • uses AI already
  • cares about quality, trust, voice, customer language, delivery, or revenue
  • has repeated cleanup
  • can name the cost of the problem
  • is open to paying for diagnosis or implementation

Not qualified yet:

  • wants generic AI tips
  • has no workflow or artifact
  • only wants free prompt advice
  • cannot name why the misfire matters
  • is not using AI at all

Signal Tracker Fields

Track:

  • name
  • company
  • LinkedIn URL or source
  • buyer segment
  • artifact type
  • pain signal
  • exact language
  • reason to contact
  • outreach angle
  • status
  • next follow-up
  • paid-audit interest
  • notes

Apify/Radar Boundary

Use Apify and public research only for:

  • public web research
  • lead discovery
  • topic intelligence
  • Reddit/YouTube/topic signals
  • website enrichment

Exclude:

  • LinkedIn scraping
  • auto-DMs
  • auto-comments
  • auto-likes
  • fake engagement
  • unapproved external outreach

Daily 45-Minute Loop

  1. 10 minutes: read morning radar or market brief.
  2. 10 minutes: comment on relevant posts with useful diagnosis.
  3. 10 minutes: send warm/manual outreach.
  4. 10 minutes: review submissions or draft one post.
  5. 5 minutes: update tracker and choose tomorrow's signal question.

What To Challenge

External reviewer should pressure-test:

  • Is the 72-hour cash path realistic?
  • Which buyer segment is fastest to cash?
  • Is the outreach too soft or too direct?
  • Does the price ladder match urgency and proof?
  • What minimal proof asset is needed before asking for money?
