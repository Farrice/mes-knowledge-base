Decision Rules

Continue the Hybrid path if:

  • buyers understand "AI output that looks finished but still needs rescue"
  • at least 5 people submit real artifacts
  • at least 3 people show paid audit interest
  • one buyer pays or asks for a scoped implementation sprint

Refine the offer if:

  • people like the posts but do not submit artifacts
  • "misfire" creates confusion
  • the price feels mismatched to perceived value
  • the buyer asks for content/positioning help more than AI workflow help

Change the first buyer segment if:

  • founder-led experts engage but do not buy
  • agencies show stronger urgency
  • local premium services show clearer workflow pain
  • content creators want voice/content diagnosis more than operating-system work

Do not expand into a larger AI Operating Partner offer until:

  • the diagnostic sells
  • the first before/after proof asset is strong
  • buyer language repeats
  • the implementation sprint has a clear scope

Red-Team Prompts

Ask weekly:

  • What are buyers actually asking for?
  • What are they ignoring?
  • Are they submitting artifacts or only liking ideas?
  • Is the paid ask too early, too cheap, or too vague?
  • What repeated correction pattern is showing up?
  • What proof would make the next buyer trust faster?

First Review Milestone

After 14 days, decide:

  1. Keep Hybrid Sequence as-is.
  2. Keep the Hybrid thesis but change the wedge.
  3. Keep AI Misfire Express but change the buyer segment.
  4. Pause and return to pure creative strategy for near-term cash.
  5. Build the AI Operating Partner offer only after more proof.
