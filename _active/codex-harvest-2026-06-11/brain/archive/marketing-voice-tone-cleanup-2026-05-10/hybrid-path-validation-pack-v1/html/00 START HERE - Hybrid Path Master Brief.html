00 START HERE - Hybrid Path Master Brief

Executive Verdict

The recommended path is the Hybrid Sequence:

Creative Strategist plus AI Operating Partner: turning customer language and founder judgment into agent-ready marketing and operating systems.

This path is stronger than choosing either lane alone.

Pure creative strategy is easier for buyers to understand, but crowded. Pure AI-native business architecture is more future-correct, but too abstract and likely too slow to cash if sold as "semantic document libraries" or "AI-native documentation." The Hybrid path keeps the front door clear while preserving the backend moat.

The Strategy In One Sentence

Sell the pain buyers already feel:

AI output that looks finished but still needs to be rescued.

Deliver the deeper capability:

The hidden judgment, customer language, rules, source-of-truth boundaries, examples, and quality tests that make the work clear enough for AI or an agent to execute.

Current Business Goal

The first business goal is not to build a big category.

The first goal is to generate cash and market signal by selling a narrow diagnostic:

AI Misfire Express.

Price ladder:

  • $500 starter audit for fast proof-building.
  • $750 standard audit.
  • $1,000 express audit.
  • $1,500-$2,000 implementation sprint as the immediate upsell.
  • Later: $3,000-$7,500 workflow build or operating-system sprint if demand appears.

First Buyer Segment

Primary buyer:

Founder-led experts paying the AI cleanup tax.

This buyer:

  • already uses AI
  • has prompts, SOPs, content workflows, proposals, follow-ups, client delivery flows, or internal processes
  • gets outputs that are close enough to be tempting but wrong enough to require rescue
  • depends on taste, judgment, nuance, customer language, examples, and quality standards
  • does not want generic automation
  • has limited time and wants useful diagnosis quickly

Secondary lanes:

  • AI-forward agencies with delivery/proof gaps.
  • Creator-consultants turning expertise into repeatable systems.
  • Premium local/service businesses with repeated sales, intake, proposal, or client-delivery workflows.

Core Thesis

Market thesis:

AI is moving from experimentation into workflow redesign, but trust, context, governance, human judgment, and proof are now the bottlenecks.

Buyer translation:

Your AI keeps guessing because the judgment behind the work is still trapped in your head.

Plain-language mechanism:

Find where AI is being forced to guess.

Deeper mechanism:

Convert one high-value workflow into an agent-ready work primitive with purpose, inputs, outputs, constraints, source of truth, execution protocol, decision rules, examples, counterexamples, quality tests, failure modes, and maintenance rules.

Positioning Lock

Use publicly:

Creative Strategist plus AI Operating Partner.

Use as a simpler tagline:

I help founder-led experts turn invisible judgment into AI-ready workflows that stop producing cleanup work.

Use as the direct CTA:

Send one failed AI output, prompt, SOP, workflow, or content process. I will show you where AI is being forced to guess.

Avoid as first-touch language:

  • semantic document library
  • work primitive
  • context engineering
  • agentic governance
  • AI-native documentation
  • operating-model transformation

Use those terms only after the buyer understands the pain.

What Makes This Different

Generic AI consultants often sell:

  • tool setup
  • automations
  • prompt packs
  • chatbots
  • content at scale
  • generic AI training

This path sells:

  • a diagnosis of why AI output still needs rescue
  • the missing human judgment behind the workflow
  • the difference between doing the task and understanding the work
  • a first executable layer that makes future AI work safer, clearer, and more repeatable

The sharp line:

AI did the task. It did not understand the work.

Evidence Status

Evidence supports the category:

  • 2026 sources show AI adoption is becoming workflow redesign.
  • Agent adoption is increasing.
  • Trust, governance, context, quality control, and human oversight are active concerns.
  • Professional-service buyers are using AI but often lack ROI clarity and governance maturity.

Evidence does not yet prove the offer:

  • No paid buyer signal yet for AI Misfire Express.
  • No external case studies yet.
  • LinkedIn posts have been internally revised, but not market-tested.
  • The proof demo exists, but needs to be turned into a buyer-readable before/after.

Main Risks

Risk 1: The offer sounds too abstract.

Countermove: Lead with "send one failed AI output" and diagnose a real misfire.

Risk 2: The content sounds like AI wrote it.

Countermove: Use Farrice's lived voice, proof, tension, narrative, and human judgment. Avoid clean but bloodless educational posts.

Risk 3: The audience agrees but does not buy.

Countermove: Use a 14-day cash test with DM conversations, submissions, and paid-audit asks.

Risk 4: The system becomes overbuilt before signal.

Countermove: Build only the assets needed to collect signal: post, Loom, intake, tracker, outreach scripts, proof demo.

Risk 5: The moat is too early to explain.

Countermove: Sell the diagnosis and proof first. Let the semantic library become the delivery method and later category.

14-Day Validation Decision

Go forward if the sprint produces at least one of these:

  • 3-5 qualified conversations with founder-led experts.
  • 5-10 submitted misfires, workflows, SOPs, or failed outputs.
  • 1-3 paid audit interests.
  • 1 paid audit sale.
  • Clear repeated buyer language around "AI is close but I still have to fix it."

Pause or reposition if:

  • people like the content but do not submit real workflows
  • the CTA confuses them
  • buyers frame the problem as tool setup only
  • the audience responds to creative strategy but not AI misfire diagnosis
