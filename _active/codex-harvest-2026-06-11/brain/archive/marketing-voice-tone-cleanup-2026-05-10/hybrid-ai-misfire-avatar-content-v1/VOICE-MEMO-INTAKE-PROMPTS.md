# Voice Memo Intake Prompts

## Purpose

Use these prompts when Farrice has raw thoughts, reactions, client conversations, proof moments, or market observations. The goal is to capture live judgment before it gets flattened into generic content.

Record 3-10 minutes. Do not polish while talking.

## Prompt 1: Buyer Recognition

```text
What did I see today that my buyer would recognize immediately?

What was the exact moment?
What would they say privately about it?
What phrase did I hear or think that should not be cleaned up?
What does this reveal about their AI/workflow frustration?
```

## Prompt 2: AI Misfire Field Note

```text
Describe one AI output, prompt, SOP, workflow, or draft that failed in an interesting way.

What was AI supposed to do?
What did it produce?
Where did it look finished?
Where did it miss the work?
What did a human have to know to fix it?
What rule, source, example, or boundary was missing?
```

## Prompt 3: Founder Judgment Capture

```text
What correction did I make instinctively today?

Why did I know it was wrong?
What did I see that the AI or junior person did not see?
Was it tone, proof, buyer state, source of truth, edge case, or stopping point?
How could that correction become reusable?
```

## Prompt 4: Customer Language

```text
What would the buyer actually say about this problem?

What would a bad marketer call it?
What would the buyer call it?
What words feel too abstract?
What words feel lived-in?
What sentence would make them think: "that is exactly it"?
```

## Prompt 5: Belief Shift

```text
What does my buyer currently believe that keeps them stuck?

What does that belief protect?
What new belief would make them ready for the AI Misfire Map?
What story, distinction, or proof would move them one step?
```

## Prompt 6: Proof With Transfer

```text
What proof did I create or notice today?

What happened before?
What changed after?
What can the reader learn from it?
What should be anonymized?
What would make this proof useful rather than braggy?
```

## Prompt 7: Offer Friction

```text
Where might someone misunderstand AI Misfire Express?

Would they think it is prompt engineering?
Would they think it is a full automation build?
Would they think it is too technical?
What sentence would make the offer clearer?
```

## Prompt 8: Visual/Video Moment

```text
What could I show on screen that would make this idea obvious?

What before/after would prove the point?
Where would I highlight the guess?
What would the viewer understand without me explaining much?
```


## Routing Rules

If the memo contains a felt buyer moment:

- route to LinkedIn or Instagram.

If it contains a before/after:

- route to Loom/YouTube and proof demo.

If it contains a market interpretation:

- route to newsletter or blog.

If it contains a repeated question:

- route to FAQ, DM script, or offer page.

If it contains a correction:

- route to AI Misfire Map and work primitive.

## Voice Gate

Keep:

- specific phrases
- imperfect but vivid wording
- concrete moments
- "I noticed..." observations
- buyer's actual language

Remove:

- generic AI consultant phrasing
- overexplained theory
- filler credibility statements
- broad trend claims without source
- wording that sounds written before it sounds felt
