## Operating Verdict

This market is real, but crowded around generic AI audits and automation roadmaps.

The point of differentiation is not:

> "We identify automations."

The point of differentiation is:

> "We find where your AI is being forced to guess because the work depends on invisible judgment."

That distinction must stay intact across every platform.

## First Use

1. Publish Post 1 from `LINKEDIN-POSTS-V3-VOICE-PROOF.md`.
2. Stay active for 30 minutes after publishing.
3. Reply manually to every relevant comment.
4. Invite qualified readers to send one misfire.
5. Log signal in the tracker before creating more content.

## Active Content Standard

V3 is the active content layer because it fixes the main gap in V2:

> The content was clear, but not yet lived.

Any new LinkedIn post should now pass the `FARRICE-VOICE-PROOF-STYLE-CARD.md` gate before it is treated as publish-ready.
