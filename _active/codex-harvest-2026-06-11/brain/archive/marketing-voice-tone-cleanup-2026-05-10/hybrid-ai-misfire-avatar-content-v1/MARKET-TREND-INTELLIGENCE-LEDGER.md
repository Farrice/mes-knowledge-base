## 2026 Market Spine

The market is moving from AI experimentation into workflow redesign, but buyers are discovering that tools alone do not create trust.

Supported claims:

- AI is being pushed deeper into daily work and professional workflows.
- Human judgment, validation, and quality control are still central.
- Context, governance, source-of-truth rules, and accountability are becoming bottlenecks.
- Buyers want proof and self-serve clarity before bigger buying conversations.
- The AI audit market is becoming crowded, so differentiation must move beyond "we find automation opportunities."

## Opportunity Gap

The market is crowded around:

- AI audits
- workflow audits
- ROI maps
- automation roadmaps
- prompt libraries
- implementation sprints
- agents for SOPs

The under-owned angle:

> AI fails because the work depends on judgment that was never made visible.

The practical market opening:

> Diagnose the misfire before selling the automation.

## Strategic Implications

1. Do not position as "AI audit consultant."
2. Position as the person who sees the invisible judgment gap.
3. Use AI Misfire Express as a narrow front door.
4. Use proof demos before broader AI Operating Partner language.
5. Teach the market that prompts, SOPs, and agents all fail when the work is unclear.
6. Let competitors educate the market on audits; win on diagnosis, language, taste, and operating clarity.
