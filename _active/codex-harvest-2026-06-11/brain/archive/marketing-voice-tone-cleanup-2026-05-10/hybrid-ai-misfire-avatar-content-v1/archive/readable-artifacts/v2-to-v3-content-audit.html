V2 To V3 Content Audit

The plain-English diagnosis of why V2 was right but not yet strong enough, and what V3 changes.

Verdict

V2 was strategically correct but emotionally under-built. It explained the thesis from the outside instead of proving it from the inside.

The posts talked about invisible judgment while hiding Farrice's judgment.

Failure Pattern

FailureHow It Showed UpWhy It Hurt
Propositional openings"The most dangerous AI output..."Clear, but not embodied. The reader nods instead of feels.
Repeated educational structureThesis, examples, CTA.Predictable shape creates AI-content smell.
Too many CTAsMany posts ask for GUESS or a submission.The offer appears before trust is fully earned.
Not enough proof adjacencyClaims are true but mostly asserted.Skeptical readers wonder whether the work has been lived.
Missing Farrice fingerprintsFew fatherhood, training, gaming, or work-window signals.The content could be posted by a competent AI consultant.
No visible valleyLittle self-correction, doubt, or messy middle.Reader cannot evaluate judgment under pressure.

What Stayed

  • AI did the task. It did not understand the work.
  • Where was AI forced to guess?
  • One artifact or workflow as the minimum action.
  • AI Misfire Express as the cash/signal wedge.
  • Founder-led experts as the first buyer.

What Changed

  • The first post opens with Farrice's own misfire.
  • Buyer pain is shown through scenes.
  • Personal training and fatherhood become proof lanes.
  • Gaming and taste become memory lanes, not gimmicks.
  • CTAs are limited to bridge moments.
