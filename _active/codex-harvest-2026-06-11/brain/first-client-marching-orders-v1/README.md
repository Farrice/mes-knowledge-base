# First Client Marching Orders

## Objective

Win the first paid client or first purchase for a service-first offer by creating conversations every day, demonstrating competence before asking for trust, and converting one useful diagnostic into a paid audit or implementation sprint.

Default offer:

**AI Business Asset Audit**

Plain version:

> I help expert-led service businesses find the missing sales, proof, content, and delivery assets that are blocking better clients, then I help build the first 3-5 assets in a focused implementation sprint.

## Revenue Target

Fastest path:

- 1 paid audit at $500-$1,000
- then convert to a $2,500-$5,000 implementation sprint

Acceptable first win:

- paid audit
- paid sprint
- paid strategy day
- discounted beta with public proof rights
- unpaid diagnostic only if it creates a strong case study, warm intros, or a public proof asset

## Non-Negotiable Daily Scoreboard

Track these every day:

| Metric | Minimum | Strong Day |
|---|---:|---:|
| New prospects researched | 5 | 10 |
| Personalized messages sent | 5 | 10 |
| Follow-ups sent | all due | all due |
| Proof/audit assets created | 1 | 2 |
| Public trust post/comment | 1 | 2 |
| Call asks made | 3 | 5 |

The daily win is not "posting content." The daily win is **qualified conversations created**.

## Buyer Target

Start with expert-led service businesses where the owner is visible and revenue depends on trust:

- consultants
- coaches
- small agencies
- fractional operators
- creators with services
- local premium service businesses with a visible founder
- boutique B2B service providers

Best first buyer:

- already sells something
- already has expertise
- has a messy website/profile/content trail
- depends on referrals or founder trust
- has no clean audit, proof map, offer ladder, or follow-up system
- can say yes without a committee

Avoid for the first 30 days:

- startups with no revenue
- people asking for "AI tools" with no business pain
- broad consumer brands
- companies where a committee must approve $500
- prospects who only want free brainstorming

### Door Opener: Free 5-Minute Asset Leak Loom

Promise:

Goal:

- book a 15-minute call
- show competence
- make the paid audit feel obvious

Name:

**Business Asset Leak Audit**

Promise:

Deliverables:

- buyer and offer clarity snapshot
- sales asset audit
- proof gap map
- content/trust asset map
- delivery asset map
- first 10 asset build queue
- 45-minute debrief

### Implementation Sprint: $2,500-$5,000

Name:

**Revenue Asset Build Sprint**

Promise:

> In 7-14 days, we build the first 3-5 assets your business needs to convert more qualified opportunities.

Possible assets:

- offer one-liner and service ladder
- proof-first landing section
- audit call script
- outreach sequence
- lead magnet/audit template
- testimonial capture flow
- onboarding checklist
- delivery report template
- follow-up sequence

   - Source
   - Problem observed
   - Message sent date
   - Follow-up count
   - Loom sent?
   - Reply
   - Call booked?
   - Offer made?
   - Status
   - Next action

2. Prepare the 1-sentence offer:

> I help expert-led service businesses turn scattered expertise, content, and delivery into reusable sales, proof, and delivery assets that help them win better clients.

3. Prepare the paid audit one-liner:

> I run a 72-hour Business Asset Leak Audit that shows which missing assets are costing you qualified clients and exactly what to build first.

4. Create a 1-page proof placeholder:
   - who you help

   - example deliverables
   - "beta clients get direct founder delivery and proof-friendly pricing"

5. Prepare a calendar link or a clear booking instruction.

## Daily Operating Block

Minimum: 2 hours/day.

Best odds: 3-4 hours/day for 30 days.

### Block 1: Prospect Research

Time: 30-45 minutes

Find 5 prospects and inspect:

- website homepage
- service page
- LinkedIn profile
- recent posts
- testimonials/case studies
- lead magnet or newsletter
- call-to-action

For each prospect, write one concrete asset leak:

- "Your offer is clear only after scrolling through three sections."
- "Your proof is testimonial-heavy but does not show before/after."
- "Your LinkedIn has strong insight but no path from trust to call."
- "Your service page lists activities, not transformation."
- "Your follow-up system is invisible after the first touch."

### Block 2: Create One Proof Asset

Time: 30-60 minutes

Create one of:

- 3-minute Loom teardown
- mini audit screenshot with notes
- before/after rewrite of their offer one-liner
- proof map from their public assets
- "first 3 missing assets" memo

The proof asset must show the guts of your thinking.

### Block 3: Send Messages

Time: 30-45 minutes

Send 5 personalized messages.

Daily ratio:

- 2 warm network messages
- 2 cold but highly researched messages
- 1 referral/faucet message

After day 10, if warm network is exhausted:

- 4 cold researched messages
- 1 referral/faucet message

### Block 4: Follow Up

Time: 15-30 minutes

Clear every due follow-up. No exceptions.

Follow-up schedule:

- Day 2
- Day 4
- Day 7
- Day 10
- Day 14

### Block 5: Public Trust Signal

Time: 20-30 minutes

Publish or comment once per day.

Use public content to show judgment:

- "3 reasons expert-led service businesses stay referral-dependent"
- "What I look for in a 5-minute business asset audit"
- "A service page mistake that makes premium buyers hesitate"
- "The difference between an activity and a business asset"
- "Why testimonials are not enough proof"

```text

Quick observation: [one concrete leak you noticed].

```

The easiest fit is a consultant, coach, agency owner, or founder who is good at delivery but whose offer/proof/follow-up assets are messy.

## Sales Call Flow

Keep the first call to 15-25 minutes.

### 1. Open

```text
I looked at the business from the outside, so I may be missing context. My goal is to show you what I saw, ask a few questions, and then tell you honestly whether I think an audit would be useful.
```

### 2. Diagnose

Ask:

- Where do most good clients come from right now?
- What happens after someone discovers you?
- What do prospects usually misunderstand about your work?
- Where do people get stuck before buying?
- What proof do you wish you had more of?
- What do you currently send after a sales call?
- If we fixed one sales/proof/delivery asset this month, what would matter most?

### 3. Reflect The Gap

```text
What I am hearing is not that you need more random marketing. It sounds like the business already has expertise, but the assets around the expertise are not doing enough work.

The gap seems to be [specific gap].
```

### 4. Offer The Audit

```text
The useful next step is not a big engagement yet. I would start with the Business Asset Leak Audit.

In 72 hours, I will map the missing assets, prioritize what to build first, and show where the highest-return fixes are.

That is $750. If the audit makes it obvious that we should build the assets together, I can credit that toward the sprint.
```

### 5. Close

```text
Does that feel like the right next step, or is there a constraint I should know about?
```

If yes:

```text
I will send payment and the intake questions today. Once both are in, I can deliver the audit within 72 hours.
```

If hesitation:

```text
That makes sense. Is the hesitation budget, timing, trust in the process, or uncertainty about whether these assets would move the needle?
```

## 30-Day Marching Orders

### Days 1-3: Foundation And First Proof

Daily required:

- Build list of 25 prospects.
- Send 5 warm/faucet messages per day.
- Create 1 sample Loom per day.
- Publish 1 post about business assets.

Day 1:

- Write your one-liner.
- Create tracking sheet.
- List 50 possible warm contacts and referral sources.
- Send first 5 warm messages.

Day 2:

- Build the proof placeholder page or doc.
- Record your first sample audit Loom on a public business.
- Send 5 more warm/referral messages.
- Follow up with day 1 contacts if any replied.

Day 3:

- Create a reusable 5-minute Loom outline.
- Build a 10-question audit intake form.
- Send 5 more messages.
- Publish one post: "The 5 business assets I look for before I trust a service provider."

### Days 4-7: Warm Network And First Calls

Daily required:

- Research 5 prospects.
- Send 5 messages.
- Record or prepare 1 Loom.
- Follow up all due contacts.
- Ask for at least 1 intro.

Goal by day 7:

- 35 total messages sent
- 5-10 replies
- 2-3 calls booked
- 3 Looms delivered

If no replies by day 7:

- Your messages are too vague or too self-focused.
- Make the first line more specific.
- Replace "I help businesses" with the exact leak you saw.
- Offer the Loom before asking for a call.

### Days 8-14: Cold Research Sprint

Daily required:

- Research 5 cold prospects.
- Send 5 cold messages.
- Send every follow-up due.
- Create 1 proof asset from a real prospect audit.
- Publish/comment once.

Goal by day 14:

- 70 total messages sent
- 10+ Looms offered
- 5+ Looms delivered
- 4+ calls booked
- 1 paid audit closed or in proposal

Daily prospect sources:

- LinkedIn service founders
- agency owners with weak proof pages
- coaches with generic offers
- consultants posting strong ideas but weak CTAs
- local premium businesses with founder-driven trust
- previous colleagues with growing businesses

### Days 15-21: Conversion Push

Daily required:

- Make 5 call asks.
- Send 5 follow-ups.
- Deliver any promised Loom within 24 hours.
- Offer the paid audit at the end of every qualified conversation.

Goal by day 21:

- 100+ total messages sent
- 8+ calls booked
- 1-2 paid audits closed

Call rule:

- If they have a real business, a visible asset gap, and authority to buy, offer the paid audit.
- Do not keep giving free consulting after the diagnosis is clear.

### Days 22-30: Close, Deliver, And Convert

Daily required:

- Keep outbound at 5/day even while delivering.
- Deliver paid audit within 72 hours.
- Ask for sprint conversion on every audit debrief.
- Capture proof immediately.

Goal by day 30:

- 150 messages sent
- 15+ meaningful replies
- 8-12 calls
- 1-3 paid audits
- 1 implementation sprint offer made
- 1 public or private proof asset captured

## Weekly Review

Every Friday, answer:

1. How many new prospects did I contact?
2. How many follow-ups did I send?
3. Which message got the most replies?
4. Which buyer type responded fastest?
5. Which asset leak showed up most often?
6. How many calls did I book?
7. How many times did I directly offer the paid audit?
8. What proof asset did I create this week?
9. What bottleneck matters next week: prospects, replies, calls, close, delivery, proof?

## Bottleneck Fixes

### If You Are Not Sending

Problem:

- avoidance disguised as strategy.

Fix:

- send 5 messages before learning, editing, posting, or planning.

### If Nobody Replies

Problem:

- message is too generic or too much about you.

Fix:

- lead with one concrete observation.
- offer a 5-minute Loom.
- reduce the ask.

### If They Reply But Do Not Book

Problem:

- the value is interesting but not urgent.

Fix:

- name the business cost of the asset leak.
- ask if they want the 3 highest-leverage fixes.

### If Calls Do Not Close

Problem:

- you are consulting for free instead of selling the audit.

Fix:

- stop after diagnosing the gap.
- say: "The next useful step is the paid audit."

### If They Ask For Proof

Problem:

- trust gap.

Fix:

- show the process map, Loom, sample audit, and risk reversal.
- use proof of thinking before proof of results.

### If You Feel Unqualified

Problem:

- waiting for permission.

Fix:

- sell the audit, not a miracle.
- demonstrate judgment.
- price beta fairly.
- capture results and improve.

## Minimum Viable Proof Kit

Build this in the first 72 hours:

1. One 3-5 minute Loom teardown.
2. One process map: how the audit works.
3. One sample asset queue.
4. One before/after offer one-liner rewrite.
5. One risk reversal:

> If the audit does not identify at least 10 concrete asset opportunities and the top 3 priorities to build next, I will refund it.

## The Daily Rule

Do not end a workday without:

- 5 new messages sent
- all due follow-ups cleared
- 1 proof asset created or improved
- 1 direct ask made for a call, Loom, audit, or intro

That is the loop.
