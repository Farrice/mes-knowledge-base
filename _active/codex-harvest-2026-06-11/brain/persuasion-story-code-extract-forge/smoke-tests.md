# Persuasion Story Code Smoke Tests

## 1. Founder Anecdote To Conversational Story

**Input**: Founder learned the offer worked after a client stopped rewriting every post from scratch and started keeping a story bank.

**Expected workflow**: `/story-code-conversational-story`

**Sample output shape**:

- Story job: explanation plus trust.
- Story type: problem/solution story.
- Finished story: starts with the client wasting time rewriting from zero, shows the story-bank change, ends with the practical result.
- CTA bridge: invite the reader to build their first five reusable stories.

**Pass**: Output is short, specific, and sounds spoken.

## 2. Offer Description To Sales Section

**Input**: A consulting offer helps service entrepreneurs turn expertise into reusable sales and content stories.

**Expected workflow**: `/story-code-sales-story`

**Sample output shape**:

- Origin story for why the offer exists.
- Prospect pain story for blank-page content and scattered proof.
- Mechanism story for the story bank process.
- Trust story for client or internal proof.

**Pass**: Each story block has a different sales job.

## 3. Weak Post To Audit And Rewrite

**Input**: "Storytelling is important for business. You should tell more stories because stories build connection."

**Expected workflow**: `/story-code-story-audit`

**Sample output shape**:

- Primary failure: vague and non-specific.
- Rewrite: a concrete moment where a prospect changes after hearing a short story.
- Proof note: ask for a real client example before publishing as a result claim.

**Pass**: The rewrite shows the idea instead of naming it.

## 4. Customer Result To Proof Story

**Input**: A customer used a story bank and cut weekly content creation from 6 hours to 90 minutes.

**Expected workflow**: `/story-code-proof-case-study`

**Sample output shape**:

- Before state.
- What changed.
- Measured result.
- Short proof story, social proof post, and 60-second video proof script.
- Evidence checklist: permission, screenshot, time tracking.

**Pass**: Result is qualified and evidence is requested.

## 5. Video Idea To Script Package

**Input**: "Why founder stories are not about sounding interesting; they are about removing buyer doubt."

**Expected workflow**: `/story-code-video-script`

**Sample output shape**:

- Hook.
- 60-second spoken script.
- 15-second cutdown.
- Visual beats: founder draft, buyer question, story card, CTA.
- Handoff notes for PJ/Tao/Remotion.

**Pass**: The script is speakable and the visuals support the persuasion job.
