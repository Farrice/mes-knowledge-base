# Persuasion Story Code Deployment Guide

## Core Objective

Use Story Code as the story layer for your business. Its job is to turn raw experience, proof, offers, objections, and ideas into short, true, conversational persuasion stories that can be deployed in content, sales, email, social media, video, and founder messaging.

The highest-value move is not "tell more stories." It is: choose the exact story that removes the next buyer barrier.

## Objectives By Business Function

### 1. Founder Messaging

Objective: Make your audience understand where your business came from, why you care, and why your method deserves belief.

Run:
- `/story-code-diagnostic` on your current website, bio, offer, and pinned social posts.
- `/story-code-entrepreneur-message` to create the main story-led positioning message.
- `/story-code-conversational-story` for the founder origin, discovery story, and failure-success story.

Best story types:
- Qualifications / Track Record
- Discovery
- Failure / Success
- I-couldn't-find-it-so-I-created-it

### 2. Content Engine

Objective: Stop posting generic advice and start publishing story-led content that creates trust, desire, recognition, and action.

Run:
- `/story-code-story-bank` to create reusable content source material.
- `/story-code-content-engine` for daily posts, emails, threads, captions, and newsletters.
- `/story-code-social-stack` when you want platform-specific social content with stronger hooks and retention.

Best story types:
- Prospect Pain
- World Today
- Pain You Know Personally
- Pressure From Other People
- Unexpected Benefit

### 3. Sales And Conversion

Objective: Make your sales assets feel less like claims and more like witnessed truth.

Run:
- `/story-code-sales-story` for sales pages, webinar sections, VSL sections, pitch sections, emails, and CTA bridges.
- `/story-code-proof-case-study` for proof blocks.
- `/story-code-conversion-stack` when the story needs stronger conversion architecture.

Best story types:
- Origin
- Mechanism
- Problem / Solution
- Case Study
- Results Testimonial
- Refund / Risk Reversal
- Eliminate Alternatives

### 4. Video And Short-Form

Objective: Make video scripts that carry a real persuasion job, not just a hook and a lesson.

Run:
- `/story-code-video-script` for 15s, 30s, 60s, and 2-5 minute scripts.
- `/story-code-video-stack` when you want production handoff notes for PJ Accetturo, Tao Prompts, Remotion, or `/create-video`.
- `/story-code-social-stack` for reels, shorts, TikTok, LinkedIn video, and X video posts.

Best story types:
- Prospect Pain
- World Today
- Unexpected Benefit
- Product Use
- Case Study
- Experience Testimonial

### 5. Proof And Trust

Objective: Turn results, screenshots, testimonials, customer wins, and case notes into proof that buyers can feel.

Run:
- `/story-code-proof-case-study` for proof assets.
- `/story-code-sales-story` to place proof inside sales assets.
- `/story-code-content-engine` to repurpose proof into posts and emails.

Best story types:
- Case Study
- Certification Story
- Expert Testimonial
- Results Testimonial
- Experience Testimonial

### 6. Objection Handling

Objective: Answer concerns without sounding defensive or pushy.

Run:
- `/story-code-diagnostic` to map recurring objections.
- `/story-code-sales-story` to create objection story blocks.
- `/story-code-conversion-stack` when the objection is tied to price, trust, identity, or alternatives.

Best story types:
- Hard-to-Believe Claim of Endurance
- Refund / Risk Reversal
- Unexpected Benefit
- Results Testimonial
- Eliminate Alternatives
- Mechanism

## Start Today: 90-Minute Deployment

### Step 1: Run The Diagnostic

Prompt:

```text
/story-code-diagnostic

Business context:
[Describe what you sell, who it is for, and the main outcome.]

Current assets:
[Paste your website copy, offer copy, bio, sales page, or content notes.]

Audience:
[Who needs to believe you?]

Desired action:
[Book a call, buy, subscribe, reply, watch, apply, etc.]
```

Output you want:
- Primary buyer barriers.
- Top 3 stories to create first.
- Missing proof and data to collect.
- Placement map for sales/content/video.

### Step 2: Build The First Five Stories

Run `/story-code-conversational-story` five times, once for each:

1. Founder origin story.
2. Prospect pain story.
3. Discovery or created-it-myself story.
4. Unexpected benefit story.
5. Proof or case-study story.

Prompt:

```text
/story-code-conversational-story

Raw story:
[Paste the real event, memory, proof point, or rough idea.]

Audience:
[Specific audience.]

Desired action:
[What the story should move them toward.]

Channel:
[LinkedIn, email, sales page, video, sales call, etc.]

Length:
[Short, medium, or long.]
```

### Step 3: Turn Those Into Content

Run:

```text
/story-code-content-engine

Use these five Story Code stories:
[Paste the five stories.]

Platform:
LinkedIn + email

Objective:
Create one week of story-led content that builds trust and demand for [offer].
```

### Step 4: Turn The Best One Into Video

Run:

```text
/story-code-video-script

Core story:
[Paste the strongest story.]

Platform:
Short-form video

Target length:
60 seconds

Desired action:
[Follow, DM, book, subscribe, buy, etc.]
```

## 7-Day Deployment Sprint

### Day 1: Story Audit

Run `/story-code-diagnostic` on your website, offer, bio, and 5 recent posts.

Goal: identify missing credibility, pain, future, reassurance, explanation, and proof stories.

### Day 2: Founder And Offer Stories

Run:
- `/story-code-entrepreneur-message`
- `/story-code-conversational-story`

Goal: create origin, discovery, created-it-myself, and mechanism stories.

### Day 3: Proof Stories

Run:
- `/story-code-proof-case-study`

Goal: create 3 proof stories from client results, testimonials, screenshots, or internal wins.

### Day 4: Objection Stories

Run:
- `/story-code-sales-story`

Goal: create story answers for price, trust, time, complexity, risk, novelty, and alternatives.

### Day 5: Content Batch

Run:
- `/story-code-content-engine`
- `/story-code-social-stack`

Goal: create 10-15 story-led posts/emails from the story bank.

### Day 6: Video Batch

Run:
- `/story-code-video-script`
- `/story-code-video-stack`

Goal: create 5 short-form video scripts and one production-ready video brief.

### Day 7: Conversion Integration

Run:
- `/story-code-conversion-stack`

Goal: integrate the best stories into a sales page, email sequence, webinar, pitch, or call flow.

## Workflow Decision Tree

- If you do not know what stories you need: `/story-code-diagnostic`
- If you need one finished story: `/story-code-conversational-story`
- If you need your offer or message shaped around stories: `/story-code-entrepreneur-message`
- If you need a reusable inventory: `/story-code-story-bank`
- If you need posts/emails/newsletters: `/story-code-content-engine`
- If you need video scripts: `/story-code-video-script`
- If you need sales copy sections: `/story-code-sales-story`
- If you need proof: `/story-code-proof-case-study`
- If current content is weak: `/story-code-story-audit`
- If you want platform social polish: `/story-code-social-stack`
- If you want conversion polish: `/story-code-conversion-stack`
- If you want video production handoff: `/story-code-video-stack`

## What To Measure

- More replies or DMs from posts.
- Higher booked-call rate from profile or website.
- Higher email clicks.
- Better sales-call flow because objections have stories.
- More usable proof assets.
- Lower time to create content because stories are reusable.
- More trust in offers because claims are carried by real events.

## Non-Negotiable Rule

Do not use this system to invent proof. Use it to find, shape, compress, and deploy real proof. If a story needs a fact you do not have yet, collect it before publishing.
