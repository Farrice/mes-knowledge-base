## Business Leverage Map

| Deployability | Differentiation | Priority |
|--------------|----------------|----------|
| **🔴 HIGH** — Distribution strategy is the #1 gap for solopreneurs | **🔴 HIGH** — No other system teaches "software is not a moat" from lived experience | **CRITICAL** |

### Specific Leverage Points for Farrice:

1. **Distribution-First Thinking** — Before building anything, diagnose your distribution advantage. This reframes every product decision.
2. **Ecosystem as Moat** — Stop building standalone products. Build platforms where creators/users create value for each other.
3. **The Loonshots Dual-Org** — Even as a solopreneur, you need a "fast brain" (innovation/design) and "slow brain" (operations/delivery). Structure your workflow accordingly.
4. **Design-as-Bottleneck** — Quality gatekeeping isn't friction. It's what creates cohesive customer experience.
5. **Close-Friends Distribution Hack** — Don't chase "most users." Chase the *right* users with the *highest-value connections*.
6. **The Screenshot Detection Mindset** — When everyone says "you can't," find the technical workaround that makes the impossible possible.
7. **Jobs-to-Be-Done Agent Architecture** — Map your entire operation to jobs-to-be-done, then build agents for each one.
