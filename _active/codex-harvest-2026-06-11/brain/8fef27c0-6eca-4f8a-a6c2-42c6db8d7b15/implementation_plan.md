## Gap Fill Analysis — What's Missing

The existing Seth Godin architecture has two poles:

| Existing Skill | Covers | Missing Gap |
|---------------|--------|-------------|
| `seth-godin-philosophy` | WHY you create, resistance, shipping, self-leadership | No brand-building mechanics |
| `seth-godin-ideavirus` | HOW ideas spread, sneezers, viral loops | No brand promise architecture, no trust engineering |

**The gap**: There's no skill for **WHAT** you build — the actual brand architecture, trust engineering, culture design, and marketing operations that sit between "overcome resistance" and "make it go viral."

This video fills that gap with 8 concepts not yet captured anywhere in the roster:

### 8 Net-New Concepts

1. **Brand = Promise (Not Logo)** — The Nike vs. Hyatt test. A brand is what people *expect*, not what you *display*.
2. **The False Proxy Trap** — Measuring what's easy instead of what matters. Instagram followers ≠ books sold. Stock ticker ≠ company health.
3. **Marketing-Driven vs. Market-Driven** — The VW diesel scandal as a marketing decision made without the marketing department.
4. **Consistency > Authenticity** — Already partially in philosophy (Pattern 9), but this source provides the **operational framework**: boiler repairman slippers story, George Clooney actor model, "playing a role" protocol.
5. **The Remarkability Engine** — Carmine's restaurant as a complete system: garlic + portions + party-of-six = built-in word-of-mouth. Goes beyond ideavirus's abstract "virusworthiness" to provide concrete engineering.
6. **Better Not Louder (Gajist Model)** — $30M/year from 130,000 email addresses. The anti-scale philosophy. "When you pick your customers, you pick your future."
7. **AI as Permission Deepener** — The Techlas tool-chest vision. AI that makes the brand MORE valuable by deepening permission, not cheapening service.
8. **Culture = Marketing** — Everything that touches the market is marketing. The boiler repairman's slippers are the marketing department.
