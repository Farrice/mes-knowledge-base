## THREE QUESTIONS BEFORE WE REBUILD

Before I rewrite the website (or anything else), I need clarity on these:

**1. Who are you actually happiest working with?**
Not "who pays the most" — who lights you up? You've coached hundreds of people over 18 years. Think about the 3-5 clients who were a genuine joy. What did they have in common? Were they coaches? Consultants? Founders? Something else entirely?

**2. Is "coaches and consultants" still the lane, or is that also dead?**
The council decision anchored to "coaches and consultants" broadly. You're saying fitness is dead. Is the coaching niche itself dead, or just the fitness sub-niche? Should we be looking at: business consultants, fractional CMOs/COOs, agency owners, course creators, info-product people?

**3. How central is "AI-powered" to your positioning?**
You said "leverage my AI skills and expertise." That's either:
- **A hidden differentiator** (you use AI to produce faster/better, but the client doesn't know or care)
- **A visible selling point** ("I use AI systems to produce 10x the output at half the cost")
- **The entire identity** ("I build AI content engines for \[ICP\]")

These are three completely different businesses and three different ICPs. Which direction pulls you?
