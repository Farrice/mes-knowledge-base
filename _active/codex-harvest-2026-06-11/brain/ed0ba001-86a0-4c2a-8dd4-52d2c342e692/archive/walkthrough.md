# Farrice Cain Website — Expert-Driven Copy Rewrite

## What Changed

Rewrote the entire website copy based on ICP intelligence, competitive analysis, and expert ghostwriting methodology. The original version was structurally sound but used generic language. The rewrite targets **S&C coaches, movement specialists, and fitness consultants** with surgical precision.

### Hero Section
- **Before**: "Ready to close the gap?" (generic CTA)
- **After**: "You've spent years mastering your craft. Online, nobody can tell." (identity-level truth)
- Added "AUTHORITY CONTENT FOR FITNESS INDUSTRY LEADERS" positioning line

Shows actual content structure — LinkedIn posts, newsletter editions — with hooks, preview copy, and performance metrics. This is the "show the work" section no competitor has.

### Systems Cards — Specific Deliverables
Expanded both cards with exact deliverables instead of vague promises:
- **Authority Build**: LinkedIn profile architecture, 30-day content sprint, positioning strategy, lead magnet design
- **Content Engine**: Voice-capture system, 8-12 posts/month, newsletter infrastructure, monthly performance reports

### Gap Section — Fitness-Specific Language
- References "squat patterns," "weekend certification holders," generic fitness content
- Positions the competitor as the noise, Farrice as the signal

### CTA — Scarcity Mechanics
- "4 fitness leaders. That's the roster." (concrete cap)
- Added qualification criteria (10+ years experience, content that matches expertise)
