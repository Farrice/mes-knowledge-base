## New Genius Patterns (From This Transcript)

Adding 10 new patterns to the existing 5:

1. **Visibility of the Invisible** — "I try to put visibility to that which is invisible." Making the hidden fears, invisible costs of inaction, and unspoken objections visible and addressable.
2. **The Double Bind** — "I make it more painful to stay the same than to change, and then change becomes automatic." Engineering situations where NOT buying is costlier than buying.
3. **Fear-First Conversion Physics** — "I get people to buy when they run out of reasons to say no, not when they decide to say yes." Inverse persuasion — subtract objections rather than add reasons.
4. **The Key Ring Close** — "I don't say which key to use. I just key one, two, three, four, five." Sequential multi-close where each attempt uses a different psychological lever without un-selling.
5. **Indirect vs. Direct Communication** — "At the highest levels of persuasion, indirect communication is preferable to direct communication." The Newtonian → Einsteinian shift in copy maturity.
6. **The 10% Self-Selection Close** — "10% of a market spends 10x more. I don't know which 10% of you that is." Getting prospects to self-identify as the elite buyer.
7. **Incomparable Offer Engineering** — "If we were to have versions of these ourselves... they say I have never seen anything like this." Stacking modalities until comparison is literally impossible.
8. **Radical Candor as Scarcity** — "It's good because of its scarcity. Nobody else does it." Radical honesty converts not because it's a technique — but because it's so rare it screams authenticity.
9. **Brand-First Direct Response Fusion** — "Branding is now way more important than direct response." The 2026 paradigm where DR pays the bills while brand builds the monopoly.
10. **The Beginner's Humility Principle** — "I've been trying to crack social media really ineffectively... you have to humble yourself back to the beginner framework." Even the GOAT starts over when the medium changes.
