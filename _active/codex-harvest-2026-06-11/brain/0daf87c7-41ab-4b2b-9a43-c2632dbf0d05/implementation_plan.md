## The 5 Haunting Workflows

> **Core principle**: Hooks capture attention for minutes. Thumbprints capture attention for years. The goal isn't eyeballs — it's permanent habitation. We don't want cheap dopamine; we want rich resonance that stays on someone's mind for months.

### 1. `/haunt` — The Haunting Engine (Master Workflow)

**Domain**: Any content, any platform, any written form
**What it does**: Takes ANY piece of writing — social media post, newsletter, copy, creative work — and applies the Haunting > Capturing operating system to elevate it from attention-capture to permanent-residue.

**The pipeline**:
1. Diagnose: Is this piece currently optimized for hooking or haunting?
2. Identify the haunting candidate: Which single line/image/idea has the potential to embed permanently?
3. Apply the Thumbprint Standard: Push the candidate toward syntactic uniqueness
4. Run the 20-Year Test: "Will someone think about this every other day for 20 years?" (Browning standard)
5. Strategic denial architecture: Restructure so the haunting moment arrives via denied expectation
6. Residue engineering: Craft the specific sensory/behavioral detail that makes the line stick

**Stacks with**: Everything. This is the universal elevation layer.

---

### 2. `/haunt-copy` — Haunted Copy Engine

**Domain**: Marketing copy, sales pages, ads, email sequences, landing pages
**What it does**: Applies haunting to commercial writing — copy that doesn't just convert but that the prospect **can't stop thinking about** days after reading. The anti-commodity copy engine.

**Why this is different**: Most copy is designed to create urgency and capture attention NOW. Haunted copy creates a recursive thought loop — the prospect keeps returning to the idea, the image, the reframing. The conversion happens because the copy has taken up residence in their mind, not because they were pressured.

**Stacks with**: Luke Iha (proof architecture + haunting = proof that embeds permanently), Cardinal Mason (persuasion that haunts instead of pushes), Nicolas Cole (rhythm + residue = kinetic prose that lodges in memory)

---

### 3. `/haunt-social` — Haunting Social Content

**Domain**: LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Instagram, Substack
**What it does**: Platform-specific haunting deployment. Instead of hook-first architecture, builds residue-first architecture — posts designed so that the reader encounters the subject again later that day and involuntarily thinks of YOUR post.

**Why this is different**: Every social media workflow in the system is hook-first. This inverts the architecture: what sentence will the reader still be carrying when they close their laptop? That sentence is the nucleus — everything else orbits it.

**Stacks with**: Lara Acosta (content that haunts builds deeper authority than content that hooks), Diandra Escobar (growth posts + haunting = borrowed attention that permanently re-routes), Grace (city blueprint content lines that haunt = durable audience architecture)

---

### 4. `/haunt-story` — Haunted Narrative Engine

**Domain**: Creative writing, literary prose, brand storytelling, ghostwriting, memoir, long-form
**What it does**: Full narrative haunting architecture. Deploys the Browning "Meeting at Night" standard — write scenes, stories, and narratives designed to haunt the reader for years. Uses poietic thresholds, strategic denial, behavioral displacement, and etymological depth in concert.

**Why this is different**: Individual Vuong techniques exist as separate workflows. This chains them into a narrative-level haunting system — not just a haunting line, but a haunting STORY. The whole piece is engineered for permanent habitation.

**Stacks with**: Eric Roth (cinematic displacement + narrative haunting = stories that "stay in your soul"), Pressfield (narrative physics + haunting architecture = structural resonance), Connelly (telling details that haunt instead of just signaling specificity)

---

### 5. `/haunt-audit` — The Haunting Residue Audit

**Domain**: Post-production quality gate for ANY content
**What it does**: Post-completion audit that scores any finished piece on its haunting potential. Not "is this good?" but "will this haunt?" The inverse of the Anti-Homogenization Audit — instead of diagnosing sameness, it diagnoses *forgettability* and prescribes specific residue-engineering fixes.

**Why this is different**: The existing audits check for originality (Species Test) and sameness (Anti-Homogenization). This checks for a third dimension: **persistence**. A piece can be original and distinctive but still not haunt — because it doesn't leave the specific kind of sensory/behavioral residue that creates permanent habitation.

**Stacks with**: All quality gates. This becomes the final layer: originality ✓, distinctiveness ✓, now… will it HAUNT?

## Additional High-Leverage Workflows (From Gap Analysis)

### 6. `/daring-disobedience` — The Skater's Mindset
Deploy Pattern 13 as a standalone creative courage protocol — diagnose where you're self-censoring, prescribe the eight-stair jump, and build the tolerance for "too much."

### 7. `/etymology-engine` — Etymological Depth Engine
Deploy Pattern 14 — take the 5 most important words in any piece, access their full OED/Webster's 1913 lineage, and let the etymological tree reshape how you use them.

### 8. `/literary-edging` — Strategic Denial Engine
Deploy Pattern 8 as a standalone composition tool — map where readers predict your next move and engineer the DJ-drop timing that creates "exhilaration embedded in linearity."

### 9. `/perception-lab` — Language Laboratory
Deploy Pattern 7 — dedicated R&D space for sentence experimentation without narrative obligations. Assignment-free writing to discover new syntactic moves.
