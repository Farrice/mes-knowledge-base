## Reader Contract

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Reader | Founder-led expert, consultant, or small agency operator using AI for marketing, proposals, SOPs, or client work. |
| First feeling | Private recognition: "I know exactly that almost-usable draft problem." |
| Live question | Why does decent AI output still feel unsafe to ship? |
| Belief move | From "I need better prompts" to "my judgment has not been turned into an operating system." |
| Next action | Send one AI-assisted artifact that looked close but still needed rescue. |

## Failure Diagnosis

| Layer | Previous Failure | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| Reader pull | The macro news-jack arrived before the private reader alarm. | Open with the AI-market shift, then immediately translate it into the founder's lived problem. |
| Flow | The draft stacked claims and examples in a visible sequence. | Move from market shift -> private alarm -> controlled demo -> x-ray interpretation -> artifact CTA. |
| Specificity | Some phrases still sounded like category language. | Add "not bad enough to reject quickly" and "good enough to waste your judgment slowly." |
| Sentence craft | Several short-line runs felt mechanically parallel. | Use fewer list-like sequences and more sentence variation. |
| Perspective shift | "Output is the x-ray" was good but underdeveloped. | Make the x-ray reveal that founder judgment only exists inside the founder, not the tool. |

## Final Draft

<!-- publishable:high-taste-ai-misfire-pilot:start -->
Microsoft, Anthropic, and Deloitte are all pointing at the same AI shift.

Prompts are becoming the least interesting part.

The expensive layer is implementation.

That sounds like an enterprise story until you look at founder-led marketing.

The same problem shows up there, just in a quieter form.

AI writes the post. It sounds professional enough.

AI writes the proposal. It feels plausible enough.

AI writes the SOP. It looks organized enough.

Then the founder reads it and feels the private alarm:

"This is close, but if I ship it, the right person will not feel anything."

That is the part most AI advice skips.

The dangerous draft is not the obviously bad one.

It is the one that is good enough to waste your judgment slowly.

Here is the controlled demo.

I gave AI a normal prompt:

"Write a LinkedIn post for a consultant launching an AI-assisted content system."

It returned the usual polished wallpaper:

"Create consistent, high-quality content faster."

"Turn your expertise into posts and emails."

"Book a call if you want help."

Nothing there is broken enough to laugh at.

That is why it matters.

The model guessed the buyer wanted speed.

The buyer actually wants their thinking to survive the tool.

It guessed the enemy was the blank page.

The real enemy is the polished draft that erases why anyone should care.

It said "the right system" while showing no rules for proof, taste, CTA timing, or when AI should stop improvising.

That is why I do not read AI misfires as bad copy.

I read them as x-rays.

They show where a founder's marketing system only exists inside the founder.

If you have one AI-assisted artifact that made you think, "close, but I still had to rescue it," send it.

I will show you the first three rules the model was missing.

## Taste Evidence Ledger

| Layer | Before | After | Why It Improved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reader pull | "Wall Street just gave away the AI play" was stronger but still a little external. | "Prompts are becoming the least interesting part" resolves faster and leads into the founder's private alarm. | Less clever distance, more immediate relevance. |
| Flow | Macro anchors, demo, and diagnosis felt arranged. | The sequence moves from market shift to quiet founder problem to demo to x-ray. | The reader gets a reason to continue at each turn. |
| Specificity | "Almost usable" and "rescue" were good but familiar. | "Good enough to waste your judgment slowly" gives the cost a sharper texture. | It names the pain without sounding like a framework. |
| Sentence craft | Too many consecutive one-line claims created visible AI rhythm. | Sentence lengths vary, and the list moments are fewer and more intentional. | The post feels composed rather than stacked. |
| Perspective shift | "Output is the x-ray" was present. | The x-ray reveals founder judgment trapped inside the founder. | The mechanism lands with more consequence. |

**Verdict:** PASS for pilot use, not proven-market final.
**User-calibrated baseline:** User scored the prior suite 6/10 overall and copywriting roughly 4/10 because flow, taste, and interweaving were weak.
**Score discipline:** No 9+ claim. This is a higher-taste pilot that still needs live reader response.
**Remaining risk:** The topical enterprise anchors should be refreshed before posting if delayed, and the final version should be judged by comments, DMs, artifact submissions, or buyer action.
