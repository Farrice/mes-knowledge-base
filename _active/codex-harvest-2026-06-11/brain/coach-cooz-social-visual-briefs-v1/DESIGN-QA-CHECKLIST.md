# Coach Cooz Design QA Checklist

Use this before publishing or handing an asset to Cooz.

## First Impression

Pass only if a cold viewer can understand the asset in 2-3 seconds.

Check:

- What is the one message?
- Is the focal point obvious?
- Is the text readable on mobile?
- Does the asset feel like private coaching?
- Would a professional over 40 feel seen rather than sold to?

## Brand Fit

Pass if it feels:

- serious
- grounded
- premium but not luxury-coded
- warm
- diagnostic
- strong without shouting
- local/human when relevant

Fail if it feels:

- generic fitness
- bro-fitness
- wellness coach
- motivational speaker
- cheap quote card
- fake executive coach
- transformation challenge
- stock personal trainer

## Copy Quality

Pass if:

- text is minimal
- the line creates recognition or tension
- the viewer gets a useful distinction
- CTA, if present, is light and clear

Fail if:

- it sounds like a slogan
- it says too much
- it uses jargon
- it overexplains the offer
- it says "Resurrection" before the buyer understands the practical offer

## Visual Quality

Pass if:

- one focal point dominates
- negative space is intentional
- color palette is controlled
- lighting feels warm and real
- typography is clean and adult
- no element feels decorative for its own sake

Fail if:

- text overlaps awkwardly
- crop cuts face/body badly
- type is too small
- contrast is weak
- there are too many effects
- it looks like an AI template

## Proof Safety

Pass only if:

- no fake client proof is implied
- no fake testimonial is implied
- no before/after claim appears
- no generated second person is framed as a real client
- no body transformation claim appears without validation
- Triage Audit concept images are labeled conceptually when needed

## Likeness Safety

Pass only if:

- Cooz remains recognizable
- facial structure is not altered
- age is not changed
- body proportions are not exaggerated
- edits look natural
- Cooz approves any edited/generated likeness before public use

## Platform Checks

LinkedIn:

- profile photo reads inside circular crop
- banner text avoids profile-photo overlap
- banner works on desktop and mobile crop

Instagram:

- feed/carousel text is readable on phone
- carousel gives a saveable insight
- Reel cover works in grid crop

YouTube:

- thumbnail reads at small size
- 3-5 words maximum
- one face or one symbolic object
- no exaggerated influencer expression

## Final Decision

Use only if all are true:

- clear
- premium
- grounded
- useful
- readable
- recognizably Cooz
- no unsupported proof
- no generic trainer energy
