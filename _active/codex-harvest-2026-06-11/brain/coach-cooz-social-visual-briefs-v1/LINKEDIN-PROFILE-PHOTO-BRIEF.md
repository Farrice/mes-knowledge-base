# LinkedIn Profile Photo Brief

## Asset Job

Create a LinkedIn profile photo that earns trust fast.

The viewer should think:

This person is serious, grounded, and probably good at diagnosing what is actually going on.

The photo should not make Cooz look like:

- a generic trainer
- a motivational speaker
- an executive coach
- a fitness influencer
- an AI-generated stranger

## Required Output

- Format: PNG
- Dimensions: 800x800
- Use: LinkedIn profile photo
- Crop: face and upper torso readable inside circular LinkedIn crop
- Background: quiet, dark, warm, premium but not luxury-coded

## Concept A: Serious Authority

Recommended source:

`approved-selects/11_seated_dark_authority_portrait.png`

Intent:

This should be the default profile photo. It makes Cooz feel like a private coach who can hold the room.

Edit direction:

- Keep likeness intact.
- Preserve serious eye contact.
- Add subtle contrast and warm edge depth.
- Keep dark background.
- Crop so face is clear in LinkedIn's circular display.
- Avoid heavy retouching.

Prompt:

```text
Create a LinkedIn-ready profile photo from this reference image of Coach Cooz.

Goal: serious private strength coach, calm authority, premium but grounded.

Keep his face, identity, beard, hair, and body proportions accurate. Do not make him look older, younger, more corporate, or like a different person. Preserve the serious seated-authority feel.

Output a square 800x800 PNG. Crop for LinkedIn so his face and upper torso remain readable inside a circular profile crop. Use a dark charcoal background, warm controlled edge light, natural skin texture, subtle contrast, and minimal retouching.

Do not add text, logos, fake luxury styling, gym slogans, dramatic smoke, neon, or motivational design elements.
```

Reject if:

- he looks too polished or synthetic
- the crop loses the face in LinkedIn circle view
- the image feels like a speaker headshot
- the retouching changes his likeness

## Concept B: Local Premium

Recommended source:

`approved-selects/19_city_standing_black_tshirt_upscaled.png`

Intent:

Use when Cooz wants to feel more local, approachable, and Burbank/LA relevant.

Edit direction:

- Keep city background soft and secondary.
- Preserve black t-shirt and local premium feel.
- Crop close enough for trust, not full-body lifestyle.
- Warm light, restrained contrast.

Prompt:

```text
Create a LinkedIn-ready profile photo from this city portrait of Coach Cooz.

Goal: local premium private coach, approachable but serious, Burbank/LA professional orbit.

Keep his identity and natural expression accurate. Crop to square 800x800 for LinkedIn, with face and upper torso readable inside the circular crop. Keep the black-shirt simplicity and warm city-light context, but soften background distractions.

Make it feel premium, grounded, and human. Do not make it look like a lifestyle influencer photo, corporate headshot, fake luxury portrait, or generic fitness trainer image.

No text. No logos. No overdone skin smoothing. No neon. No dramatic bodybuilding edit.
```

Reject if:

- it feels like a fashion/lifestyle post
- the city background competes with his face
- the crop is too distant
- it loses strength-coach credibility

## Concept C: Professional Trust

Recommended source:

`approved-selects/05_premium_blazer_headshot.png`

Intent:

Use only if the audience needs a more polished trust signal. This should not make him look like a corporate consultant.

Edit direction:

- Keep the blazer but reduce executive-coach energy.
- Crop close.
- Keep palette dark and grounded.
- Avoid office/luxury positioning.

Prompt:

```text
Create a restrained LinkedIn profile photo from this blazer portrait of Coach Cooz.

Goal: professional trust while still reading as a private strength and energy coach, not an executive coach.

Keep his face, hair, beard, expression, and proportions accurate. Crop square 800x800 for LinkedIn with face and upper torso readable in circular display. Use a charcoal/warm-gray treatment, natural texture, and low-key premium lighting.

Do not over-corporatize the image. Do not add business-coach styling, luxury office cues, glossy retouching, motivational graphics, or text.
```

Reject if:

- he looks like an executive coach
- the blazer overwhelms the strength-coach category
- the image feels fake-polished

## Final QA

Pass only if:

- Cooz is instantly recognizable.
- Face reads clearly at small size.
- The photo feels calm, serious, and trustworthy.
- It does not look like a generic trainer profile.
- It does not overpromise results.
- Cooz approves likeness comfort before publishing.
