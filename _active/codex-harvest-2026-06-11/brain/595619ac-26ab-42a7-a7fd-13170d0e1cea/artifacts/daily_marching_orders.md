## What NOT to Do

1. ❌ **Don't post before Day 8.** Comments-only for 7 days.
2. ❌ **Don't "great post!" anyone.** Every comment adds an insight, counterpoint, or evidence.
3. ❌ **Don't explain the Antigravity system.** Clients don't care about your tools.
4. ❌ **Don't position as "AI-powered."** Position as a strategist who moves fast.
5. ❌ **Don't wait for the profile to be perfect.** It's better than 95% of LinkedIn right now. Ship it.
6. ❌ **Don't build a course or product.** You have no audience to sell to yet.
7. ❌ **Don't try to run all 3 channels (LinkedIn + Substack + YouTube) at full speed.** LinkedIn is the only channel that matters for revenue right now.
