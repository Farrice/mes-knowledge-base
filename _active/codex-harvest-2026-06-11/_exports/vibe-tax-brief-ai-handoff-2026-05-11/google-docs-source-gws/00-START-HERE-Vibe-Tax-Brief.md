# 00 START HERE - Vibe Tax Brief AI Handoff

## What This Package Is

This is the GWS re-export of the Research Intelligence Entry Point package for **The Vibe Tax Diagnostic** and **The Vibe Tax Brief**.

It exists so Farrice can hand the package to another AI, client-facing tool, collaborator, or future workspace without losing the actual strategic logic behind the offer.

## Core Positioning To Preserve

The AI system stays backstage. The buyer-facing promise is not "I use AI." The buyer-facing promise is:

> I help you see what your buyers already believe, doubt, compare, fear, and need to hear before they ever talk to you.

## The Entry Point

**The Vibe Tax Diagnostic** is the free or low-friction entry point. It scores where a business is paying for bad assumptions, weak buyer language, false signals, proof gaps, or content that sounds plausible but does not move real buyers.

## The Paid Offer

**The Vibe Tax Brief** is the paid 48-hour research intelligence brief. It delivers buyer language, hidden objections, competitor frames, proof gaps, positioning angles, and sales conversation openers.

Founding proof-client price: **$300-$500**.

## Guardrails

- No fake engagement.
- No auto-DMs.
- No platform-rule-breaking scraping.
- Manual, qualified outreach only.
- Do not sell vague AI consulting.
- Do not make AI the headline unless the buyer explicitly asks how it works.
- Every output should make the buyer feel seen, not targeted.
