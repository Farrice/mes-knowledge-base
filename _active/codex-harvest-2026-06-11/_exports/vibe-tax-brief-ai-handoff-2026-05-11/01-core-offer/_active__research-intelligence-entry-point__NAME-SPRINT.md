# Name Sprint

Lead expert: **David Placek**

Support: James I. Bond / Brain Glue, Heath Brothers / Made to Stick, Luke Iha hooks.

## Naming Verdict

Default public name:

```text
The Vibe Tax Brief
```

Subtitle:

```text
A false-signal diagnostic for strategy, content, and client work.
```

One-line promise:

```text
Find the false signals before they cost you strategy, content, or clients.
```

## Why This Name Works

| Criterion | Score | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Names the wound | 9 | "Vibe tax" names the cost of guessing from vibes. |
| Works as a noun | 9 | "Run the Vibe Tax Brief" is easy to say. |
| Tension zone | 8 | It is slightly polarizing without insulting the buyer. |
| Processing fluency | 8 | Short words, easy sound, quick contrast. |
| Category clarity | 7 | Needs subtitle to explain the service. |
| Social repeatability | 9 | The phrase can travel in posts and DMs. |
| Premium fit | 7 | "Brief" helps lift it from meme to service. |

## Alternatives

| Name | Best Use | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| The Proof Gap Brief | more premium, safer B2B | less polarizing |
| The False Signal Brief | clearest and most credible | less magnetic |
| Signal Leak Audit | strong operational metaphor | better for internal systems work |
| The Proof Gap Brief | professional and client-safe | less emotionally sticky |
| Hype Debt Diagnostic | strong for AI/content trend-chasing | too narrow for the full package |
| The Mirage Brief | memorable and visual | may feel too poetic |

## Final Naming Rule

Use **The Vibe Tax Diagnostic** for the free scorecard.

Use **The Vibe Tax Brief** for the paid 48-hour service.

Use **Research Intelligence Brief** as the category descriptor, not the lead name.

Use **False Signal Diagnostic** as the explanatory mechanism.

**The Vibe Tax Brief** creates memory. **False Signal Diagnostic** creates trust. Keep them paired, but do not let the internal mechanism replace the public name.
