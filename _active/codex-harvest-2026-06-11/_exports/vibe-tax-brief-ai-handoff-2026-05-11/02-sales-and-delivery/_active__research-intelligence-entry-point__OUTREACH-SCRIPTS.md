# Outreach Scripts

## Direct Ask - Warm-ish Public Signal

```text
[Name], I noticed [specific offer/post/page].

The promise is interesting, but I would be curious whether the signal around it is clean or noisy.

I am testing a Vibe Tax Diagnostic: it scores whether an offer is being guided by buyer language, proof, and real objections, or by false signals like peer praise, AI summaries, and competitor sameness.

Want me to run the quick version on [specific asset]?
```

## Direct Ask - Paid Brief

```text
[Name], if the quick diagnostic shows a real gap, I am also opening a few founding Vibe Tax Briefs.

In 48 hours, I map the buyer language, hidden objections, competitor frames, proof gaps, and 3 sales angles so your next page, post, pitch, or campaign does not start from guesswork.

Founding price is $300-$500 while I build proof.
```

## Marketplace Proposal

```text
I can help you avoid rebuilding this from guesses.

For this project, I would run a 48-hour Vibe Tax Brief: buyer language, competitor/substitute scan, hidden objections, proof gaps, false signals, and 3-5 stronger offer angles.

The output is not a generic market report. It is a practical decision brief you can use for your sales page, outreach, content, or positioning.
```

## Follow-Up 1

```text
Quick follow-up. The useful part of this is not "research" in the abstract.

It is finding the signal you should trust before you spend more time on the offer, page, content, or campaign.
```

## Follow-Up 2

```text
Closing the loop here.

If this is not useful, no worries. If you are still trying to figure out why people like the idea but do not move, that is exactly the gap the Vibe Tax Diagnostic is built to find.
```
