# Prospect Scorecard

Use this before sending any direct ask. Contact prospects scoring **14+** first.

## Buyer Segments

| Segment | Best offer |
|---|---|
| solo consultant with repeated admin/sales tasks | AI Workflow Integration Sprint |
| agency/service owner needing pipeline | Lead List + Outreach Starter Pack |
| founder/creator with fuzzy offer, market, content, or client-strategy signal | The Vibe Tax Brief |
| solo expert with weak content-to-client path | AI Content Misfire Audit |
| SMB with inquiries but weak follow-up | Email Follow-Up Rescue |

## Qualification Score

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Active buyer pain | none visible | vague | possible | clear | urgent/repeated |
| Ability to pay | unknown | low | $500 offer | $1K+ offer | high-ticket service |
| AI/system relevance | none | curiosity | uses AI lightly | workflow gap visible | explicitly discussing AI/tools |
| Offer clarity | no offer | vague offer | simple service | clear paid offer | premium paid offer |
| Personalization hook | none | generic | broad | specific page/post/process gap | obvious mini-diagnosis |
| Reachability | hidden | low | public contact | active platform | clear DM/email path |

## Skip For Now

- no paid offer or business model
- only wants free advice
- wants guaranteed leads, virality, or revenue
- requires complex code/API work beyond current proof
- needs account access before trust exists
- cannot be contacted manually and specifically
