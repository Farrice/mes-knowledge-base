# Offer Cards - Cash-First Service Menu

Use these as one-page offer cards. Start with one offer per conversation. Do not pitch the full menu unless the buyer asks what else you can do.

## Offer 1: AI Workflow Integration Sprint

### One-Line Promise

I turn one repeated business task into a working AI-assisted workflow in 48 hours.

### Best Buyer

Solo B2B operators, consultants, agencies, coaches, fractional leaders, and service owners who already use AI lightly but have not connected it to a real workflow.

### Best Use Cases

- intake summaries
- sales-call notes into follow-up emails
- proposal first drafts
- client onboarding checklists
- content idea capture into usable briefs
- FAQ into response library
- research into decision brief

### Starter Scope

| Included | Not included |
|---|---|
| one repeated task | full business automation |
| workflow map | custom app build |
| prompt pack | complex API integration |
| SOP and checklist | managing their inbox/accounts |
| optional no-code setup if simple | guarantee of revenue or leads |

### First Price

Founding sprint: **$500**.

Raise to **$750-$1,500** after one paid delivery, a strong proof quote, or a buyer asks for deeper implementation.

### Buyer Intake

Ask for:

- the repeated task
- current tool stack
- example input
- desired output
- who uses it
- what breaks today
- what a successful first version must do

### Delivery

Within 48 hours:

1. current-state workflow diagnosis
2. before/after workflow map
3. AI prompt pack
4. simple SOP
5. acceptance checklist
6. optional Loom-style walkthrough script

### Direct Ask

```text
[Name], I am opening a few small AI Workflow Sprints. The promise is simple: pick one repeated task, and I turn it into a working AI-assisted workflow in 48 hours. No big AI transformation project. Just one workflow map, prompts, SOP, and a first usable version. Is there one task in your business that keeps repeating and still lives in your head?
```

## Offer 2: Lead List + Outreach Starter Pack

### One-Line Promise

I find 50 qualified prospects and give you the exact first-message angles to start conversations.

### Best Buyer

Consultants, agencies, service owners, creators with offers, coaches, and fractional operators who can serve clients but do not know who to contact or what to say first.

### Starter Scope

| Included | Not included |
|---|---|
| 50 manually qualified prospects | scraping private data |
| visible qualification notes | auto-DMs or spam |
| 3-5 segment patterns | contact guarantees |
| first-message angle for each segment | appointment setting |
| objection and follow-up prompts | revenue guarantee |

### First Price

Founding pack: **$250** for 25-50 leads depending on data availability.

Standard pack: **$500-$750** after first paid proof.

### Buyer Intake

Ask for:

- their offer
- best current buyer
- bad-fit buyers
- geography, platform, or niche preference
- proof they can show
- sales call or DM next step

### Delivery

Within 48 hours:

1. buyer-fit criteria
2. lead source map
3. 25-50 qualified prospects
4. specific observation per prospect
5. first-message angle bank
6. follow-up and objection notes

### Direct Ask

```text
[Name], if you already know what you sell but keep getting stuck on who to contact, I am testing a small Lead List + Outreach Starter Pack. I map 25-50 qualified prospects, why they fit, and the first-message angle for each segment. No auto-DMs, no scraping, no fake volume. Just a clean list and a sharper first ask. Want me to send the one-pager?
```

### Canonical Package

### Starter Scope

### Delivery

1. buyer language map
2. false-signal diagnosis
3. competitor/substitute map
4. hidden objection map
5. proof gap analysis
6. offer-angle shortlist
7. sales conversation openers
8. 7-day validation plan

It finds the false signals before they cost your strategy, content, or clients: buyer language, hidden objections, competitor frames, proof gaps, and 3 sales angles.

## Optional Offer 4: AI Content Misfire Audit

Use the existing package at `_active/content-system-audit/` only when the buyer has visible content, a real offer, and a content-to-client workflow leak.

Do not use this lane when the buyer mainly needs automation, lead sourcing, market research, email follow-up, or landing-page setup.

## Offer Selection Rule

| Buyer says | Offer to lead with |
|---|---|
| "I know I should use AI but I cannot make it fit my work." | AI Workflow Integration Sprint |
| "I know what I sell but I do not know who to contact." | Lead List + Outreach Starter Pack |
| "I do not know what my market actually wants." | The Vibe Tax Brief |
| "AI makes my content sound generic and nothing converts." | AI Content Misfire Audit |
| "People inquire but disappear." | Email Follow-Up Rescue, as a scoped variant of Workflow Sprint |
| "I need a page and checkout." | Landing Page + Intake + Checkout Setup, only if tools are simple |
