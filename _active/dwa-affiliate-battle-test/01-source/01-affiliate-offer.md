# 01 — DWA Affiliate Offer  (`/suzuki-product-source`, affiliate path)

## Path chosen: AFFILIATE
You have a DWA 3.0 affiliate link → no product to build, demand pre-validated. The job is honest distribution + a compliant funnel. (If your link is actually MRR/resell rights, see `../06-system/06-compliance-ledger.md` — the framing changes.)

## The offer, framed honestly
**What it is:** Digital Wealth Academy 3.0 — a digital-marketing *curriculum* (45+ modules: sales funnels, email, copywriting, social growth, AI, faceless content) plus a done-for-you template library and a community. ~$497 (confirm on checkout).

**What we sell it AS (the wedge):** a real skill education for someone who wants to build an online income *and is tired of being lied to about it*. Not a money machine. The skills are portable — usable for any business, even if they never resell the course.

## The honest angle (positioning)
> "The faceless digital-marketing course I actually use — taught straight, with no income promises and the real work shown up front."

Every competitor sells the dream. We sell the **curriculum + the truth about the work**. To a scam-burned buyer, honesty is the conversion lever.

## Buyer (from ICP — see research)
"Brittany, 34" — burned-out parent / 9-5er chasing **exit + dignity**, modest goals ("replace ~$2k so I can stay home"), already burned by one course, pre-decided the category is a scam. Speaks in: "without getting sucked into an MLM," "what do you ACTUALLY do all day," "I've been burned before."

## Offer ladder
| Tier | Asset | Price | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free (opt-in) | "The Honest Faceless Starter" — what the work actually is, the 5-skill stack, who it's NOT for | $0 | Lead magnet / DM bait; previews the curriculum + filters wrong-fit |
| Core | DWA 3.0 (your affiliate link) | ~$497 | The curriculum + templates + community |
| (Optional later) | Your own free "skill teardown" content as ongoing nurture | $0 | Trust + replay |

## Proof posture (NO income claims)
Use only:
- **Product proof:** the real module/lesson list, the template library, the community (verifiable facts about what's inside).
- **Process proof:** "here's the actual daily work" (specific, unglamorous).
- **Your honest experience:** what *you* found useful — without stating earnings.
- **Disclosure as proof:** naming the affiliate relationship + the skepticism builds trust where competitors lose it.
Banned: earnings figures, income screenshots, "typical results," "passive income while you sleep."

## Disclosure (use verbatim, before every link)
> Affiliate disclosure: if you buy through my link I earn a commission, at no extra cost to you.

## First-use path (what the buyer does after purchase — put in DM + LP)
Day 1: log in, watch the orientation + pick ONE skill module (start with content or funnels). Day 2-3: do the first build (a simple landing page or first post) using a template. Day 7: post your first piece of content. Frame: "you're learning a skill and doing reps — not flipping a switch."

## Who we tell NOT to buy (integrity screen)
Anyone who wants guaranteed/passive money, won't make content, is buying on FOMO, or can't afford to lose $497. Stated plainly in the LP + DMs.
