## The hard rules (enforced in every asset)
1. **No income claims.** No "$X/month," "six figures," "passive income while you sleep," "replace your salary," income screenshots, or implied typical earnings. (FTC 2025 proposed Earnings Claim Rule; deceptive main message isn't cured by a disclaimer.)
2. **Affiliate disclosure, clear & conspicuous, before the link** (16 CFR §255.5). Standard line used everywhere:
   > *"Affiliate disclosure: if you buy through my link I earn a commission, at no extra cost to you."*
3. **State the true offer** — price, model (affiliate), and refund policy. Never mix "resell for 100%" with an affiliate link.
4. **Results require work + not typical** — every results-adjacent statement pairs with "this takes real, consistent work; most beginners don't see fast results."
5. **Answer the MLM question honestly** — "no downline, no recruiting friends; you earn only on your own sales."
6. **Screen out the wrong-fit buyer** (see below) — selling someone their last $497 on a hope is the integrity line we don't cross.
7. **Auto-DM is opt-in only** — fires only when someone likes/comments to ask. No cold/mass DM. Opt-out honored.
8. **No fake scarcity** — no fake countdowns or invented "spots left."

## Who we deliberately screen OUT (stated in LP + DMs)
Wants guaranteed or fully passive money · won't create content or do outreach · buying on FOMO/screenshots · expects linear hours-in = dollars-out · can't tolerate a 6-12 month horizon or can't afford to lose $497. We tell these people, plainly, not to buy.

## Platform-risk notes
- **Stripe/processor risk:** processors have shut down MMO accounts over income claims — our no-claims approach lowers this, but the *product's* processor is the vendor's concern; your exposure is your *content*.
- **X automation:** opt-in DM only, within X ToS + rate limits; if you scale accounts, keep them genuinely distinct (no duplicated copy).
- **Multi-account:** distinct identities/niches only; no fake engagement.
