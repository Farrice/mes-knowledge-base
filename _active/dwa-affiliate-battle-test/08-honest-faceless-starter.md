## The 5 Skills You Actually Learn

**1. Content** — Writing posts people find useful. This is the hardest part and the most valuable. You learn to write about a thing you're learning so other people want to learn it too. Most people skip this; it's the whole game.

**2. Funnels** — Turning attention (likes, comments) into a simple page that explains an offer. It's not a secret. It's a landing page + email list. One tool, one afternoon, done.

**3. Email** — Staying in touch with people who raised their hand. You write an email once per week or once per month. They read it. Some reply. Some buy. Most don't.

**4. Copywriting** — Saying true things clearly so the right person acts. This is how you turn "I'm interested" into "I'm buying." You learn formulas; they work because they're honest.

**5. AI + Tools** — Doing all of the above faster, but not faking it. AI helps you write faster and think clearer. It doesn't replace the work. You still write the content; you still talk to people; you still think.

## The Real Daily Work (What Nobody Shows You)

This is what actually takes 2–3 hours per day:

✓ Write a few short posts (one topic, one insight, one question — maybe 200 words each)
✓ Check your messages (people who like/comment; answer them)
✓ Send one email (if you're building a list)
✓ Learn one small new skill (watch a lesson, or practice a formula)

That's it.

**What you DON'T do:**
✗ Cold message strangers  
✗ Recruit friends or family  
✗ Guarantee anyone any income  
✗ Spend 8 hours a day "building" — you learn by doing reps, not by learning

## Your First Week (Realistic Version)

**Day 1:** Watch the orientation. Pick one skill to start (content is easiest). Watch the first 3 lessons.

**Day 2–3:** Do the first build with a template. Content: write 3 sample posts. Funnel: set up a simple landing page. Email: draft your first newsletter.

**Day 4–7:** Post your first pieces. Announce what you're building. Talk to people who ask questions. Keep going.

**Week 2+:** Post consistently (1–2 per day). Answer messages. Learn the next skill. Month 2–3, the reps compound.

**Months 3–6:** You're posting regularly. You have a list. You're answering real questions. You've learned the stack. You're selling something (your own product, an affiliate offer, someone else's course). Most people quit here because they expected the money to come sooner.

**Months 6–12:** If you keep going, you start seeing results. Not always money — sometimes just: "I built this," or "I have a small audience," or "I made $300," or "I learned a skill I'll use forever." Results aren't typical. But if you stay, something compounds.
