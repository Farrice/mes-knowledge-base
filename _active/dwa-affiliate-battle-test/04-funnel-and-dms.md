# 04 — Funnel + DM Scripts  (`/suzuki-product-funnel`, `09-comment-dm-conversion-system`)

## Funnel map (faceless X, written + links)
```
Thread (honest hook) 
  → like/comment opt-in keyword (HONEST / WORK / BURNED)
  → DM: deliver free "Honest Faceless Starter" (no pitch)
  → soft bridge to Landing Page (03-landing-page.html)
  → official DWA checkout ({{DWA_AFFILIATE_LINK}})  [affiliate disclosed]
  → first-use path (Day 1 / Day 7 plan)
```
Keywords all deliver the same free starter (simple to run). Auto-DM is **opt-in only** — it fires because they asked.

## Intent routing
| Intent | Public signal | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Cold | Comments the keyword for the freebie | Send starter + ask one honest question; no link push |
| Warm | Shares their situation ("I'm a tired mom…", "got burned before") | Send starter + the LP; mirror their words; invite a reply |
| Ready | Asks "where do I buy / what's the price / is it worth it" | Disclose affiliate, send LP/checkout, remind them to confirm price + refund + fit |
| Wrong-fit | Wants guaranteed/passive money, won't do work | Tell them honestly it's not for them; send nothing to buy |

## Public reply scripts (in-thread)
- **Delivery:** "Sent you the free starter — it's the 5-skill map + what the first week actually looks like. No pitch in there."
- **Objection ("is this a scam/MLM?"):** "Fair to ask. No downline, no recruiting friends — you earn only on your own sales. The free starter spells out who it's NOT for too."
- **Proof ("does it work?"):** "Depends entirely on the work — most beginners don't see fast results, and I won't promise income. What I can say is the skills (funnels, email, copy) are real and portable. Starter shows you the work; you decide."
- **Ready ("where do I buy?"):** "Affiliate disclosure: I earn a commission if you buy through my link. Here's the honest rundown + link 👉 [LP]. Confirm the price + refund policy on the checkout before you buy."
- **Wrong-fit:** "Honestly? If you want guaranteed or hands-off money, skip this — it'll just frustrate you. No hard feelings."

## DM scripts (opt-in only)
**DM 1 — delivery (everyone who opts in):**
> Hey — thanks for the comment! Here's the free Honest Faceless Starter: [STARTER LINK]
> It's the 5-skill map, what the daily work actually is, and who it's honestly not for. No pitch — read it and see if it even sounds like you.
> Quick q so I point you right: what's making you look into this right now?

**DM 2 — warm (after they reply with their situation):**
> That makes sense, and you're not alone in it. The realistic version: it's a skill you learn + reps you put in — not a switch. If you want the deeper breakdown, I wrote it up here: [LP]
> Affiliate disclosure: if you go through my link I earn a commission, no extra cost to you. Zero pressure — the starter's yours either way.

**DM 3 — ready (they ask to buy):**
> Here's everything straight: [LP] → it links to the official checkout.
> Affiliate disclosure: I earn a commission if you buy through it, at no extra cost to you.
> Two things before you do: (1) confirm the current price + refund policy on the checkout, (2) only buy if the "who it's for" fit check is a yes for you. If it's not, don't — seriously.

**DM — wrong-fit:**
> Being real with you: if you're after guaranteed or fully passive income, this isn't it, and I don't want you spending $497 to find that out. Save it. If that changes and you want to learn the skills, the free starter's still yours.

## First-use path (send after a confirmed purchase)
> Nice — here's how to not let it sit in a tab: Day 1, watch the orientation + pick ONE module (start with content or funnels). Day 2–3, do the first build with a template. Day 7, post your first piece. You're learning a skill and doing reps — that's the whole game.

## Compliance (every link)
Affiliate disclosure appears **before** the link, every time. No income claims in any script. Wrong-fit buyers are actively turned away. Opt-out: if someone says stop, stop.
