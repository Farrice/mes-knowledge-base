## Example: What replies look like (Day 1)

Your posts will get a mix. Here's how to handle them with the scripts from `04-funnel-and-dms.md`:

**Comment: "HONEST"**
→ Auto-DM triggers. You also reply publicly: *"Sent you the free starter — it's the 5-skill map + what the first week actually looks like. No pitch in there."*

**Comment: "Is this an MLM?"**
→ Public reply script: *"Fair to ask. No downline, no recruiting friends — you earn only on your own sales. The free starter spells out who it's NOT for too."*

**Comment: "Does it work?"**
→ Public reply script: *"Depends entirely on the work — most beginners don't see fast results, and I won't promise income. What I can say is the skills (funnels, email, copy) are real and portable. Starter shows you the work; you decide."*

**Comment: "Where do I buy?"**
→ Public reply script: *"Affiliate disclosure: I earn a commission if you buy through my link. Here's the honest rundown + link 👉 [landing page]. Confirm the price + refund policy on the checkout before you buy."*

**Comment: "I want guaranteed passive income"**
→ Wrong-fit script: *"Honestly? If you want guaranteed or hands-off money, skip this — it'll just frustrate you. No hard feelings."*

(Don't auto-DM wrong-fit buyers.)

## Live Adjustments (OK to do mid-week)

If you notice a pattern:
- **Thread 1 bombs, Thread 2 pops** → the skepticism angle works better than the manifesto; shift later posts toward naming doubts
- **Replies drop on Days 4–5** → early audiences are engaged but new people aren't seeing the thread; try scheduling paid promo or a retweet from your other account
- **No wrong-fit comments** → your audience is pre-filtered (good); if sales are still zero, LP is the issue
- **Many "Will it work for [niche]?" questions** → you may have attracted a different ICP than expected; note it for Account #2 scaling

**Do NOT adjust:** the disclosure lines, the "actually requires work" framing, or the MLM-clear answer. Those are non-negotiable compliance lines.

## After Day 7: What's next?

Once you've collected Week 1 data:
1. **If metrics are healthy:** proceed to Week 2. Increase cadence to 2–3 posts/day (remix hooks, re-post the 3 flagships with new proof/buyer each time per `05-automation-launch-scale.md`).
2. **If opt-ins are high but sales are zero:** pause, run `/suzuki-launch-debugger` on the landing page + offer.
3. **If opt-ins are low:** revisit `/suzuki-post-lab`, test new swipes from the bank.
4. **If you're seeing sales:** ship the results back here for a wins-and-learning debrief, then scale per the multi-account plan in `05-automation-launch-scale.md`.
