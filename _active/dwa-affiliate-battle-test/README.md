## The verdict (does the OS work?)
Yes — and the most important output is *strategic*, not cosmetic: the OS's compliance gate **forced the one positioning the saturated market has left wide open.** Three independent research streams converged:
- The faceless-MRR field is a monoculture of income-claim copy ("passive income / $10k months / copy my system") — saturated and FTC-exposed.
- The buyer ("Brittany, 34" — exit + dignity, already burned, scam-vigilant) **winces at exactly that copy** and craves honesty.
- FTC is actively tightening on earnings claims (2025 Earnings Claim Rule; §255.5 disclosure).

→ **Our wedge: radical honesty + skill/utility framing + zero income claims.** The constraint is the differentiation. Everything below executes that.

## The strategy in one line
Sell the **skill education** (45 modules: funnels, email, copywriting, AI, faceless content), to the **burned, dignity-driven beginner**, by **showing the actual work and naming the skepticism** — never the dream. Affiliate link + full disclosure.

## Bridge message (the spine of every asset)
> "You learn a real skill you keep — even if you never sell the course. I'll show you exactly what the work is. No income promises. You decide if it's for you."
