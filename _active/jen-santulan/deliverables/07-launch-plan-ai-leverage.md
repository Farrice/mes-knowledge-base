# Jen Santulan — 2026 First-Time-Buyer Engine: 30/60/90 Launch + AI Leverage Map

*the system that turns one recorded video a week into an evergreen authority surface, a steady stream of pre-qualified inbound, and a pipeline that follows up while you sleep. you do less chasing and more closing.*

The whole engine exists to make one promise true to your buyer: **the down payment isn't the wall — income and access are, and you're closer than you've been told.** YouTube is where you prove it in long form, IG is where you atomize it, and the lead engine is where the buyer who's finally ready raises her hand. > **Standing rule for everyone who writes inside this engine:** every entry-tier number used in a live post (rent, PITI, qualifying income, condo price, neighborhood medians) gets a same-day verification pull before it ships. the figures in this doc are May-2026 SRAR/C.A.R.-current, but the market moves; verify same-day before posting, and never state a single neighborhood's price as hard fact without a fresh Redfin "trailing 3-month median" pull.

## Part 1 — The 30/60/90 Rollout (week by week)

Built for a working agent: **one long-form video a week, atomized into the feed, one webinar a month.** Nothing here asks you to be a full-time content team. it asks you to be on camera once a week and on calls when qualified people book.

The mistake most agents make is posting before there's anywhere for interest to land. we build the catcher first, then turn on the tap.

|---|---|---|

AI drafts it in your voice; you approve. Build the opt-in page + the DM keyword trigger ("send me MATH"). Wire the simple email follow-up (5 emails, drafted below). | ~90 min: record a 60-sec "why i made this" intro + approve the PDF and emails |
| **Week 2 — The channel** | YouTube channel set up: banner, "more than just a house" about section, the FTHB positioning stated plainly (this is the lane nobody else claims). AI writes the channel copy + first 4 video titles/descriptions. You record nothing this week except a 30-sec channel trailer. | ~45 min |
| **Week 3 — First flagship** | Record **Flagship Video #1: "the 20% down rule was your parents'. here's what it actually takes to buy in the Valley in 2026."** AI hands you a teleprompter-ready script (belief-dissolution structure: name the 1am thought, flip with one real number, invite). You record it in one sitting. AI edits + cuts the first 6 atomized clips. | ~2 hrs: record (the script is done for you) |
| **Week 4 — First atomization wave** | Flagship #1 publishes. The 6 clips + 2 carousels + 3 stories roll out across IG on the cadence below. The magnet goes live in the video description and IG bio. **End of Phase 1 you have:** a live YouTube channel, your first flagship + a week of atomized social, a working lead magnet capturing emails, and an email sequence nurturing every opt-in automatically. The two surfaces you were missing, evergreen authority + a pipeline, now exist.

## Part 2 — The Weekly Operating Rhythm (the repeatable week)

This is the whole job, once the system's built. if you can protect one 2-3 hour block a week, the engine runs.

| Day | You do | The AI does (overnight / in parallel) |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Pick this week's topic from the running list (or approve the AI's pick). 10 min. | Hands you a teleprompter-ready script in your voice by Monday night. |
| **Tuesday** | **Record the flagship.** One sitting, ~30-45 min on camera. Send the raw file. | Receives the file, edits the long-form, cuts 6-8 vertical clips, writes all captions. |
| **Wednesday** | Approve the edit + captions (15 min on your phone). | Schedules the week's IG posts on cadence; preps the YouTube description + chapters. |
| **Thursday-Sunday** | Reply to comments + DMs as they come (this is relationship work only you can do, ~15 min/day). | Posts on schedule, routes magnet opt-ins into nurture, flags any DM that's a real buyer. |
| **Daily, 10 min** | Glance at the inbound the AI flagged as "qualified, wants their numbers run." Book the call. | Pre-qualifies every opt-in (timeline, income range, area), so you only call people who are actually ready. |

**Posting cadence (atomized from the one video):**
- **YouTube:** 1 flagship/week (the source).
- **IG Reels:** 3/week (clips from the flagship).
- **IG Carousel:** 1/week (the "real math" visual, your highest-save format).
- **IG Stories:** 3-4/week (behind-the-scenes, a client win, the magnet reminder).
- **Webinar:** 1/month (assembled from that month's 4 flagships, no net-new work).

**The loop in one sentence:** record once Tuesday, approve once Wednesday, show up for the people who raise their hand. everything between those moments, the AI carries.

## Part 4 — The KPI Scorecard

Six numbers. not a dashboard, not a vanity metric in sight. each one tells you whether the engine is moving a stranger toward a booked call.

| Metric | What it tells you | "Good" by Day 90 | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Saves per IG piece** | Is the content useful enough to keep? (Saves beat likes for your buyer, she's filing it to act on later.) | 20-40 saves on the "real math" carousels; your winners will spike higher | The calculating buyer saves what she plans to use. Saves predict opt-ins. |
| **DM keyword triggers ("MATH")** | How many people raised a quiet hand. | 15-30/month and climbing | The lowest-pressure intent signal: she wants the number, not the pitch. |
| **Magnet opt-ins** | Strangers who gave you an email for the honest math. | 40-80 total opt-ins by Day 90 | Your pipeline. Every name here is being nurtured automatically. |
| **Webinar registrants** | How many will give you 30 minutes. | 25-50 registered per webinar; 30-40% show live | The deepest pre-sale trust signal short of a call. |
| **Booked consults** | The number that actually pays you. | 5-12 booked numbers-consults by Day 90 | This is the engine's real output. Everything above feeds this. |
| **Consult → active buyer** | Of the people you talked to, how many you're now working. | 30-50% of consults convert to an active, qualified buyer | The relationship metric. This is the part the AI can't do, and the part you're great at. |

**Two leading-indicator rules so you don't panic early:**
- weeks 1-4, watch **saves + opt-ins**, not bookings. trust builds before it books.
- if opt-ins are climbing but consults aren't, the nurture sequence needs tuning (AI fix, not a you problem). if saves are flat, the topics need a sharper 1am thought (AI surfaces better angles).

**Review cadence, once a month, 30 minutes:**
The AI hands you a **one-page readout** (not a spreadsheet): the three highest-performing pieces, the one email driving the most bookings, the topic with the most saves, and one recommendation for next month. you read it, you pick the winner to make more of, you move on. that's the entire analytics burden: half an hour a month, in plain English.
