## Recommendation Verdict

Run this content lane as a proof-led zero-to-inbound sprint.

Do not start with "AI content." Start with one visible business problem your ICP already feels:

> The page sounds close, but nobody can tell where the claim ends and the proof begins.

Your tangible asset is the GLP-1 Movement Receipt and 5-page audit sample. Your content job is to make the right buyer feel the cost of scattered claims, then show the method without overpromising search citations or medical clearance.

## Quality Gates Before Posting

- Avoid medical, legal, or regulatory advice.
- Only claim AI citations after testing and logging them.
- Every public claim uses VERIFIED, LIKELY, or UNCONFIRMED internally before posting.
- Keep generic AI content framing out of the main value prop.
- End without a cheap question close.
- Use real numbers or remove the number.
- Respect the operator's need for speed while naming the risk.
- The asset is visible in the post or first comment.
