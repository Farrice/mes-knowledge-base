# Source-to-Search Trust Layer

## For Health, Wellness, Supplement, And Performance Brands

Your buyers are asking sharper questions than your site, social content, and product pages are built to answer.

The Source-to-Search Trust Layer turns claims, buyer questions, founder judgment, and source material into content assets people can trust and AI search systems can understand more clearly.

AI citations are not promised here. The work makes your best knowledge easier to inspect, safer to publish, and better prepared for search, answer engines, and team use.

## The Problem

Most health brands have useful knowledge scattered across product pages, founder calls, research links, support tickets, sales decks, and old content.

That creates an expensive gap: strong claims sit without clear source support, safe language gets mixed with risky implication, buyer questions are answered in fragments, and AI tools can flatten nuance because the source of truth is not explicit.

## The Offer

I help health, wellness, supplement, and performance brands turn claims, buyer questions, and founder judgment into source-backed content buyers trust and AI search can inspect more clearly.

The backend system is practical, not abstract: source receipts, claim boundaries, answer assets, decision rules, examples, counterexamples, quality tests, and update rules.

## Three Ways To Buy

### 1. Audit: Source-to-Search Snapshot

Best for brands that want to see the gap before buying a larger build.

You get one claim, product page, or buyer question mapped into a claim type label, source grade, limitation note, safe wording map, buyer net-impression read, long-tail question map, and first trust primitive sketch.

Use this when your copy feels close, but you are not sure it is safe, specific, or search-ready.

### 2. Retainer: Monthly Source-Backed Content System

Best for founder-led brands that need ongoing posts, articles, answer assets, and claim discipline.

You get weekly signal briefs, monthly claim receipt updates, four to eight source-backed posts or article seeds, LinkedIn/entity reinforcement, answer-page briefs, and team-facing decision rules for what AI can draft, what needs review, and what should not be published.

Use this when the brand needs a living content system, not another batch of generic posts.

### 3. Intensive: Full Source-to-Search Trust Layer Build

Best for brands entering a high-scrutiny category, launching a new offer, or rebuilding content for AEO, SEO, and GEO.

You get the full operating layer: brand entity file, claim library, source hierarchy, buyer question map, safe and unsafe wording matrix, 5 Percent Asset plan, retrieval-ready answer-page draft, founder POV rules, examples, counterexamples, quality tests, failure modes, and measurement sheet.

Use this when the brand needs one shared system for content, claims, proof, and AI-assisted work.

## First Proof Asset

The first demo asset is the **GLP-1 Movement Receipt**: a claim-safe content rubric for wellness brands entering GLP-1-adjacent conversations without pretending to give medical advice.

It shows how the system separates market signal from medical claim, buyer anxiety from clinical proof, general behavior support from treatment implication, public content from team-facing source rules, and retrieval readiness from untested citation claims.

## Good Fit

This is a good fit if you sell in a low-trust or high-scrutiny health category, have good sources but no clear claim library, depend on founder judgment that AI cannot see, and want AEO, SEO, and GEO work that starts with proof.

## Not A Fit

Poor fit: guaranteed ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, or Google AI citations without live testing; medical, legal, or regulatory approval; volume content without claim review; or vague wellness copy that avoids proof.
