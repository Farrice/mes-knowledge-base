## Why This Matters for Health Brands

**Claim safety is not a compliance checkbox.** It's a market signal. Brands that document their thinking are trusted more by buyers and ranked higher by AI search. The Source-to-Search Trust Layer makes that visible.

- **Buyers see consistency:** Your posts, articles, and sales pages all say the same thing about the same claims because they inherit from one source.
- **AI search becomes more likely to cite you:** Structured, sourced content improves retrieval odds across engines like Perplexity, ChatGPT search, and Google AI Overviews. You can't control AI ranking, but you can make yourself citable.
- **Your team ships faster:** New content doesn't need approval if it matches the system. Faster + safer = competitive edge.
- **Founder judgment becomes teachable:** You're not just saying "trust my instincts." You're showing the thinking so new team members and AI tools get it right.

## Medical Claim Boundary

Nothing in the Source-to-Search Trust Layer produces medical claims or medical advice. If your brand is health-adjacent (supplements, performance, fitness, movement), the system keeps you in safe territory by documenting what you CAN claim, what needs qualification, and what's off-limits.

**No medical advice. No diagnosis language. No treatment claims.** We build the guard rails, not the medicine.

**FDA-Regulated Categories:** If your brand makes medical devices, digital therapeutics, or FDA-regulated claims, this system supports but does not replace legal and regulatory review. You own liability for your claims and messaging. We provide the structure; you verify with counsel.

**Claim Liability:** You own every claim you publish. If your "safe wording" is challenged by regulators, media, or buyers, you are responsible for substantiation and response. Our job is to document your thinking and sources so you can defend it. We are not your legal counsel.

## Questions?

**What does "agent-readable" actually mean in practice?**
It means your content is structured so an AI system can verify a claim, find the source, apply your brand rules, and know when to ask a human. Same structure that makes it trustworthy for buyers.

**Is this only for supplement brands?**
No. Health, wellness, performance, fitness, nutrition, longevity—any category where claims matter and buyer trust is fragile. Also works for medical device, digital therapeutics, and regulated brands that want to scale content safely.

**Can I do just the Snapshot?**
Yes. Snapshots are self-contained. But most brands find that one Snapshot reveals they need the Sprint or System because the issue is bigger than one claim.

**How does this fit with my current content system?**
It becomes the decision-making layer underneath your publishing. Your writing process doesn't change. But every piece inherits from one source of truth instead of reinventing the wheel.
