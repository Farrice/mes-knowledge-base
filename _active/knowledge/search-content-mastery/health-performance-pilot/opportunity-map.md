# Opportunity Map — Health Performance Search Pilot

## Selected opportunity

**Query:** How should a supplement brand structure claims so buyers and AI-search systems can inspect the evidence?

**Intent:** informational with commercial trust implications.

**Audience:** health-performance brand founder or marketing lead.

**Why selected:** it connects an existing buyer problem—scattered proof and ambiguous claims—to a concrete answer asset without requiring an invented product outcome. It also exposes a measurable internal behavior: can a reviewer correctly identify the claim, source, limit, owner, and update state?

## Asset architecture

```text
Primary answer asset
├── Claim Receipt anatomy
├── Worked label-fact example
├── Safe review sequence
├── Limits and qualified-review boundary
└── One next action

Supporting opportunities (parked)
├── Claim-type glossary
├── Source hierarchy template
├── Founder/SME question capture guide
├── Product-page claim receipt examples
└── Dated AI-search observation sheet
```

## Surface plan

| Surface | V1 action | Proof state |
|---|---|---|
| Owned answer page/article | Produce internal answer draft | `PREDICTED` |
| Carousel/visual | Produce brief only | `PREDICTED` |
| LinkedIn | Park; no publication permission | `NO EVENT` |
| Google organic | No live page or rank observation | `UNTESTED` |
| AI answers/citations | No dated observation set | `UNTESTED` |
| Publisher/directory/partner surfaces | Opportunity only; no outreach | `NO PERMISSION` |

## Information-gain requirement

Make the claim record inspectable as five adjacent fields—claim, source, limit, reviewer, and update date—and explicitly separate retrieval readiness from observed citation performance.

## Measurement hypothesis

The answer may improve correct source-and-limit identification in the next internal review. This is a `PREDICTED` internal quality hypothesis, not a search-performance forecast.
