# Opportunity Map — The Angle Map Search Prototype

## Selected Opportunity

**Target hypothesis:** How to create supplement marketing angles without overclaiming  
**Content object:** Practitioner answer page with an embeddable Proof-to-Angle Receipt  
**Intent:** Informational with a downstream commercial evaluation path  
**Primary reader:** Supplement-brand founder or marketing lead  
**Status:** `SUPPORTED AS A CONTENT PROTOTYPE`; demand and performance `UNTESTED`

## Why This One

The current result landscape splits the problem in half. Official sources explain substantiation and claim risk. Tactical marketing pages provide angle lists and wording substitutions. The Angle Map can show the missing decision path without pretending to certify compliance:

> source fact → evidence scope → buyer tension → defensible belief → named angle → hook direction → express claim → implied claim → editorial claim-safety line → qualified review

That chain is native to the existing offer. It adds a worked decision object instead of another summary of regulations or another list of ad ideas.

## Proven Structural Pattern

The selected page uses:

> direct answer → evidence boundary → five-step method → worked example → net-impression review → limits → action

The pattern follows current how-to intent and answer readability. The substance comes from Farrice's canonical decision product and current owner guidance, not competitor copy.

## Required Information Gain

Show one constructed evidence-to-angle transformation with nine adjacent decisions: source fact, evidence scope, buyer tension, defensible belief, named angle, hook direction, express claim, implied claim, and editorial claim-safety line. Clearly separate marketing judgment from qualified legal or regulatory review.

## Rejected Alternatives

1. **Regulation summary:** official owners already do this better.
2. **“Safe words” list:** words cannot determine the complete impression by themselves.
3. **AEO checklist:** Google says no special AI markup is required; ordinary search eligibility and useful content still govern.
4. **AI citation promise:** no deterministic formula or baseline exists.
5. **Client case study:** no paid client, approved evidence, publication, or outcome exists.

## Proof Boundary

The opportunity map supports a local content prototype. It does not establish search volume, ranking feasibility, AI citation likelihood, buyer willingness to pay, or causal lift.
