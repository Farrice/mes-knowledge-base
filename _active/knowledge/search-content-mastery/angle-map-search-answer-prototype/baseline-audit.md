## Claim And Evidence Baseline

The prototype has official process guidance but no real client product evidence. The worked example must therefore be explicitly constructed and non-client. It may demonstrate decisions. It may not become proof that a product claim is acceptable.

The governing distinction is:

- FTC advertising review: express claims, implied claims, substantiation, testimonials, disclosures, and overall impression.
- FDA supplement review: product/label claim categories, evidence fit, and regulatory requirements.
- Qualified client review: final decision for the real product, wording, audience, jurisdiction, and surface.
