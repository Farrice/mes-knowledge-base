# Brand Direction

## Positioning

**MyBPM is premium streetwear for the raver whose identity stays on after the wristband comes off.**

Primary identity driver: **subculture pride**. Secondary driver: **belonging**. A MyBPM piece should work as a quiet recognition signal between people who understand the reference.

## World

- Working drop: `DROP 001 / SUBLEVEL`
- Creative thesis: `THE FREQUENCY BELOW THE FLOOR.`
- Manifesto: `NOT FESTIVAL MERCH. A SIGNAL TO THE PEOPLE WHO WERE THERE.`
- Visual register: editorial streetwear after dark
- Environments: concrete, transit, loading docks, stairwells, quiet streets after the event
- Casting: credible friends-of-the-brand and community members; lived-in confidence over unreachable fashion poses

## Visual system

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Ink | `#0A0A0A` |
| Bone | `#F1EFE8` |
| Concrete | `#B7B3AA` |
| Signal acid | `#D7FF2F` |

The acid tone is a signal, not a wallpaper. Corners stay square. Type is large, compressed, and mostly monochrome. Motion is subtle and respects reduced-motion settings.

## Voice

Specific, grounded, and culturally fluent. Speak like someone who was there, not a brand trying to sound young. Product claims must be physical and inspectable.

## Do not use

- Generic neon gradients, lasers, equalizers, “rave fam” clichés, or psychedelic UI
- Luxury claims unsupported by blank, construction, sample, and photography quality
- Long SEO-stuffed product descriptions above the buy button
- Fake scarcity, vague sustainability claims, or invented technical performance
- Kith logos, layouts, copy, or proprietary brand elements
