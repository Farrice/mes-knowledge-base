# MyBPM Product-Image Standard — Product Truth / Night Proof

## Status

Photography V3, the white-premium material system, and the corrected typography/identity system are **LOCKED**. This is the next active gate.

No Shopify product media or live theme was changed.

## The verdict

**Product truth first. Culture proves it.**

MyBPM should not choose between sterile e-commerce cutouts and atmospheric rave photography. Every product receives a controlled proof sequence: first make the garment, fit, construction, and artwork unmistakable; then prove that the same person and look belong at a festival, in a club, and in daily life.

The single signature move is continuity: **the same hero look travels from white gallery to Night Society and into the next day.**

## Why the current catalog needs a standard

The connected Shopify catalog already contains useful raw material: large square images, front/back views, clean garment isolation, and genuine MyBPM community photography. But the featured frames vary by vendor and year, many lead with back views or generator mockups, and the visual language does not yet make a premium collection feel like one authored release.

The fix is not more imagery. It is a repeatable order, crop, light, cast, and evidence standard.

## The six-frame product sequence

Every core apparel product should ship with these six required images in this order:

| Order | Frame | Job | Direction |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Hero full look** | Desire + silhouette | 4:5 on-body, full garment visible, quiet white/gallery environment or the collection's approved lead environment |
| 02 | **Front truth** | Artwork + color accuracy | Straight or slight three-quarter, neutral gallery white, grounded soft shadow |
| 03 | **Back truth** | Rear artwork + construction | Same camera height, crop, scale, and light as frame 02 |
| 04 | **Fit profile** | Remove sizing uncertainty | Side/three-quarter body, sleeves/hem/waist visible, natural posture |
| 05 | **Material evidence** | Prove premium value | Fabric weight, rib, seam, embroidery, print edge, hardware, label; human scale visible |
| 06 | **Life proof** | Prove cultural belonging | Same person and look in Festival, Club, or Daily Life; product remains readable |

Recommended expansion:

- Frame 07: alternate environment from Photography V3.
- Frame 08: 6–12 second motion loop showing drape, movement, front, and back.

Do not add filler simply to reach eight frames.

## Collection-grid contract

- **Aspect ratio:** 4:5 across every featured product image.
- **Master size:** 3000 × 3750 px, sRGB.
- **First frame:** one person or one product, full silhouette, no copy, no floating logo, no collage.
- **Scale:** apparel occupies approximately 72–82% of frame height.
- **Alignment:** repeat shoulder and eye-line positions by product family.
- **Background:** optical white `#FFFFFF` or gallery white `#F5F5F1`; use one within a collection, not both randomly.
- **Shadow:** soft, grounded, directional enough to avoid a pasted-on mockup.
- **Hover frame:** back view or one controlled Night Society proof frame; never an unrelated campaign crop.

Shopify currently accepts product and collection images up to 5000 × 5000 px or 25 megapixels and under 20 MB. Its help guidance stresses consistent aspect ratios for side-by-side collection display. The 3000 × 3750 master stays comfortably inside those limits while preserving detail for zoom.

## Image balance by surface

| Surface | Product evidence | Cultural world | Rule |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Collection grid | 100% | 0% in featured frame | Consistency wins the first scan |
| Product detail gallery | 60% | 40% | Answer purchase questions before atmosphere |
| Homepage drop chapter | 35% | 65% | Lead with the world, return quickly to products |
| Campaign lookbook | 30% | 70% | Festival / Club / Daily Life remain one identity |
| Social launch carousel | 50% | 50% | Desire and evidence alternate |

## White-premium studio direction

- Seamless optical or gallery-white sweep; no beige, warm tan, or gray gradient.
- Soft, cool-neutral key light; black garments retain texture and white garments retain seam detail.
- Add one material cue only when it proves value: honed stone plinth for accessories, smoked metal hardware tray, or speaker-weave insert.
- Marble must be a real photographed surface and never a digital wallpaper.
- Models do something small and natural—adjust a cuff, turn, walk, sit, hold a record sleeve—rather than stand in catalog stiffness.
- Preserve skin texture, fabric grain, garment proportions, and true print color. No plastic smoothing, fake HDR, or crushed blacks.

## Night Society translation

The product does not become a different brand after dark.

- **Festival:** daylight or open shade, collective scale, complete styling, visible care and connection.
- **Club:** practical light plus softened direct flash, identifiable faces, detail held in black garments, no voyeuristic grit.
- **Daily life:** window light or open shade, modern city structure, friendship, work, travel, listening, calm confidence.
- **Afterimage transitions:** coat check, doors, food stop, ride, rooftop, blue hour, first daylight.
- Use the same cast, hero looks, 35mm/50mm lens family, and natural-skin baseline across all settings.

## Logo and wordmark inside imagery

- The exact heart/equalizer/headphones heritage symbol may appear only when it physically exists on the garment, label, packaging, hardware, or an intentional brand object.
- `My.BPM` may appear as a physical wordmark application or one campaign close.
- Never add either identity asset as a watermark.
- Never place the symbol and wordmark in opposite corners to “brand” a photo.
- Never repeat the symbol as a backdrop pattern.
- Never replace the `My.BPM` period with the heritage symbol.
- Product artwork may feature the symbol deliberately; the photography should not then add it again.

## Capture and export specifications

### Photography masters

- Portrait: 3000 × 3750 px, 4:5, sRGB.
- Landscape editorial: 3600 × 2400 px, 3:2.
- Full-bleed campaign: capture with safe crop room for 16:9 desktop and 4:5 mobile.
- JPEG for photographic masters; PNG only when transparency or hard-edged graphic preservation requires it.
- Keep Shopify uploads below 20 MB and below its 5000 × 5000 / 25 MP ceiling.
- Preserve the uncropped camera original separately.

### Color and retouch

- White balance and black point match across the entire SKU family.
- Skin remains natural; club color may affect the environment without recoloring the garment beyond recognition.
- Print color and placement may not be “improved” beyond the physical sample.
- Remove dust, temporary wrinkles, and sensor defects; do not reshape bodies or garment fit.

### Naming and alt text

- File: `mybpm-[drop]-[product]-[color]-[view]-01.jpg`
- Example: `mybpm-night-society-heavyweight-tee-black-front-02.jpg`
- Alt text describes product, color, view, and meaningful environment—not marketing copy.

## Shoot architecture

### The one thing

**One garment, one recurring person, three lives.** Photograph the same hero look in the white studio, inside the event world, and the next day.

### Shoot cheat sheet

- **Composition:** 35mm environment + 50mm human/product proximity; consistent camera height for proof frames.
- **Action:** adjust, turn, walk, exchange, listen, leave; no endless static posing.
- **Location:** white cyclorama or clean architectural daylight base, then one festival, one controlled club, one city continuation.
- **Lighting:** product-accurate white studio; open-air natural light; practical club light with softened direct flash; daylight continuation.
- **Sensory cue:** fabric movement and post-night cool air—not artificial fog or visual grit.
- **World thread:** the wristband may appear once as evidence of continuity, never as a recurring costume prop.

## First-drop production minimum

For a focused capsule of four hero products:

- 24 required PDP stills: 6 per product.
- 8 optional proof frames: two additional contexts per product.
- 4 short motion clips: one per product.
- One recurring cast of 3–5 people.
- Three hero looks repeated across Festival / Club / Daily Life.
- One studio day + one controlled nightlife block + one daylight city block can create the full system if planned together.

## Automatic rejection list

- Vendor mockup as the sole featured image.
- Back view as the featured collection image without a deliberate design reason.
- Random aspect ratios across a collection.
- Fake marble, beige luxury, or generic concrete grit.
- Garment lost in darkness, motion blur, colored light, or crowd density.
- Nightlife imagery with anonymous faces, crushed blacks, voyeurism, or costume-rave styling.
- Model poses with no action or social truth.
- Floating logo, corner watermark, or repeated heritage-symbol pattern.
- AI-generated garment details that do not match the physical sample.
- Different model identity, grade, or styling language in every environment.

## Approval language

- `LOCK PRODUCT IMAGES` — preserve this six-frame sequence and build the homepage/collection/PDP layout grammar next.
- `REVISE PRODUCT IMAGES: [specific frame, ratio, lighting, or environment]`
- `ELIMINATE: [specific image behavior]`

## Evidence and limits

- Existing product and brand assets were inspected read-only through the connected Shopify store.
- The heritage symbol was imported from the store's existing Shopify CDN asset and preserved unchanged.
- Third-party images in the visual board are directional references only and are not cleared for commercial use.
- No Firecrawl credits were used.
- No Shopify data, media, theme, or publishing state was changed.
