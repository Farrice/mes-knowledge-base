# Live Store Audit

## Verdict

The current store communicates a broad merch catalog, not a premium fashion label. The problem is not the absence of visual effects; it is the absence of a ruthless edit. Old mockups, placeholder content, weak product-card behaviors, overlong descriptions, nested genre navigation, and broken swatch/app assets make the store feel assembled over time rather than directed.

## Benchmark translation

Kith's useful grammar is editorial scale, disciplined typography, deliberate whitespace, high-quality on-body photography, and collection-led merchandising. MyBPM should borrow that grammar, not clone Kith's identity.

- Estimated current visual/merchandising match to that grammar: **24%**
- Estimated Horizon-based v1 ceiling without bespoke app development: **82%**
- The remaining gap is primarily photography, physical sample quality, campaign consistency, and advanced editorial/personalization—not theme code.

These percentages are directional audit estimates, not measured conversion forecasts.

## Product card audit

| Component | Current | Required v1 |
|---|---:|---|
| Image hover / alternate view | 2/10 | Consistent second image or controlled zoom |
| Inline quick add | 5/10 | Use only where size choice is unambiguous |
| Swatches | 1/10 | Variant image or Shopify-native swatch data; no guessed PNG files |
| Badges | 2/10 | Only real states: new, low stock, sold out |
| Variant siblings | 0/10 | Consolidate duplicate genre/color listings where feasible |

## Product strategy

1. Launch one four-piece edit, not all 29 products.
2. Lead with the Sublevel heavyweight tee because it has current lifestyle imagery and a stated 330 gsm blank.
3. Keep the hoodie, bucket hat, and one Defined tee behind a physical-sample gate.
4. Archive or hide redundant and mockup-only items from the primary navigation until they clear the same standard.

## Content strategy

1. Replace “all things EDM” catalog language with specific subculture recognition.
2. Use one campaign thesis per drop and short product intros above the buy button.
3. Move material, fit, care, production, and shipping into structured fields.
4. Use honest on-body photography as the primary sales proof.

## Navigation strategy

Proposed primary navigation: **New Drop / Shop / Editorial / About**. Genre taxonomies can remain as collection filters or editorial tags; they should not dominate the top-level experience.

## Build order

1. Curate and sample the capsule.
2. Produce the campaign imagery and product data.
3. Upload this Horizon theme as unpublished.
4. Configure menus, collection handles, metafields, policies, and redirects.
5. Run mobile, checkout, performance, accessibility, and analytics QA.
6. Publish only after approval.

## Honest capability gap

This build intentionally excludes loyalty, personalization, editorial publishing automation, and complex drop reservations. Those features do not solve the present problem and would add cost before the product proof is ready.
