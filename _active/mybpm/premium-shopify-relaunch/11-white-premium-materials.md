# MyBPM Material Gate — White, With a Pulse

## Status

Photography V3 is **LOCKED**. This is the next single-decision board.

## Decision this board serves

Does this physical material language feel premium, culturally credible to Night Society, and bright enough to support commerce without turning MyBPM into a luxury hotel, a cyberpunk costume brand, or a generic print-on-demand store?

## Creative brief

MyBPM should feel like a bright cultural gallery built for clothes that live after the wristband comes off: white plaster and archival paper create space and clarity; honed white stone adds permanence without decoration; dense cotton and ripstop keep the world grounded in garments; matte black speaker weave and smoked metal carry club culture into daylight; a rare oxblood or midnight-blue signal creates pulse without repainting the environment. The result is mineral light with nocturnal hardware—clean, tactile, serious, and still alive.

## Locked material ratio

| Share | Material family | Role |
|---:|---|---|
| **70%** | Luminous white | Commerce canvas, daylight, clarity, product truth |
| **15%** | Honed mineral | Permanence, tactility, selective editorial punctuation |
| **10%** | Night structure | Matte black, speaker weave, brushed/smoked metal |
| **5%** | Rare signal | Muted oxblood first; midnight blue as environmental support |

This is a hierarchy, not a recipe to fill every frame. Some pages should be nearly pure white.

## Material vocabulary

### 01 — Luminous white

- **Optical white** `#FFFFFF`: dominant storefront and product-page canvas.
- **Gallery white** `#F5F5F1`: quiet section changes, packaging interiors, editorial breathing room.
- **Cool line** `#D9D9D3`: hairlines, dividers, secondary borders.
- Physical references: limewashed plaster, uncoated archival paper, dense white cotton jersey.
- Finish: matte, dry, softly absorbent; never creamy beige or glossy plastic.

### 02 — Honed mineral

- White Carrara/Calacatta-family stone with thin, cool gray veining.
- Honed or softly polished; no dramatic gold veins, brown warmth, or bookmatched spectacle.
- Use in photographed plinths, one editorial divider, packaging inserts, retail fixtures, and occasional campaign surfaces.
- Never reproduce it as a loud site-wide CSS pattern. **Marble should feel quarried, not rendered.**

### 03 — Garment truth

- Dense cotton jersey, brushed fleece, washed nylon/ripstop, rib, embroidery, seam and hardware macro.
- Web use: product-detail zooms, collection stories, hover crops, garment construction callouts.
- The garment is the hero material. Stone and metal exist to make it feel more valuable, not to compete with it.

### 04 — Night structure

- **Soft black** `#0A0A0A`: navigation, type, buttons, club chapter, not the entire site.
- Woven speaker cloth / acoustic mesh: a direct music-world material without waveform clichés.
- Brushed aluminum and smoked chrome: precise, cool, functional hardware—not mirror chrome or Y2K liquid metal.
- Use in fixtures, button states, thin rules, packaging hardware, zipper and trim references.

### 05 — Rare signal

- **Muted oxblood** `#5A1722`: primary accent for a small number of calls, labels, lacquer/glass, or campaign details.
- **Midnight blue** `#121A46`: environmental support in night imagery, never a competing brand background.
- Combined accent usage stays under 5% on commerce pages.

## Surface application

### Website

- White remains the default canvas.
- Soft black carries structure and conversion actions.
- Mineral appears as real imagery or a restrained crop, never decorative wallpaper.
- Mesh and metal appear in Night Society transitions, product details, and micro-surfaces.
- Accent color is earned by hierarchy; it does not decorate empty space.

### Garments

- Prioritize fabric weight, dimensional ink, embroidery, trim, seams, labels, and tactile contrast.
- White/black garments can carry both minimalist and maximalist graphics because the material environment around them stays calm.
- Use oxblood as a rare print, stitch, label, or interior detail—not a mandatory colorway.

### Packaging and physical world

- Bright uncoated white stock; black type; blind deboss or restrained gray linework.
- Marble is a photographed insert or physical surface, not a printed faux-stone box.
- Brushed or smoked metal only where hardware has a reason to exist.

## Elimination rules

- No beige, tan, warm travertine, or hospitality-luxury palette.
- No green accent.
- No site-wide faux marble or loud gold veining.
- No glossy gold, rose gold, or ornamental luxury signifiers.
- No mirror-chrome cyberpunk world.
- No all-black storefront that hides product and community.
- No distressed concrete, grunge overlays, or grit as shorthand for nightlife.
- No texture that reduces readability, accessibility, garment clarity, or conversion.
