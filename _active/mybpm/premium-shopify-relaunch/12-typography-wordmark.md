# MyBPM Typography Lock — The Beat Gap, Corrected

## Status

Photography V3 and the white-premium material system remain **LOCKED**. Typography is **LOCKED WITH ONE IDENTITY CORRECTION — 2026-08-30**.

The type family, scale contrast, commerce hierarchy, and restrained mono utility layer remain approved. The rejected `MY/BPM` compact notation and generic all-uppercase wordmark do not.

## Correct brand signature

### Primary wordmark — `My.BPM`

- `My` is one unit: capital `M`, lowercase `y`, optically scaled to approximately **62%** of the `BPM` cap height.
- `BPM` remains uppercase and dominant.
- The period is the beat separator. There is **no slash**.
- The smaller `My` creates the original hierarchy Farrice wants: personal ownership first, beats-per-minute authority second.
- The visible wordmark may appear as `My.BPM` on the website, garment labels, packaging, and campaign signatures.

### Handle form — `MyBPM`

- Reserved for Instagram, URLs, usernames, file names, and systems that do not accept punctuation or scale changes.
- Never used as the preferred visual wordmark when the designed `My.BPM` lockup is available.

### Production note

This document defines behavior and hierarchy, not a replacement trademark drawing. Final production requires the original wordmark vector or an approved redraw, optical kerning, and small-size tests. Do not typeset the mark casually and call it final.

### Identity roles

| Element | Primary job | Best surfaces |
|---|---|---|
| `My.BPM` wordmark | Everyday brand signature | Header, packaging face, campaign close, garment wordmark |
| Heritage symbol | Recognition and owned history | Favicon, avatar, hardware, label stamp, select product applications |
| Symbol + wordmark lockup | Institutional identification | About page, packaging back, hangtag, legal/trademark context |
| `MyBPM` handle | Platform compatibility | Instagram, URL, account names, metadata |

### Combination discipline

- Use **one primary identity element per composition**.
- Default website header: wordmark alone. Do not force the symbol beside it at small navigation scale.
- Horizontal institutional lockup: symbol left, wordmark right, generous separation; the symbol never replaces a letter or the period.
- Symbol-only applications are earned by context: avatar, favicon, zipper pull, label stamp, or a product whose design intentionally features it.
- The logo appears in product photography only when it physically exists on the garment, label, packaging, or set object. Never add it as a floating watermark.

## Behavioral rules

- Preserve the beat through scale, the period, and spacing—not a slash or novelty music typography.
- Large all-caps remains reserved for short cultural statements. Product information stays calm and readable.
- Utility mono carries real facts only.
- Use the exact heritage symbol master. Do not redraw, simplify, stretch, crop, animate, or generate a new headphone/equalizer icon.
- Additional waveforms, heartbeats, headphones, equalizers, music notes, or play buttons are prohibited outside the heritage mark.
- One identity mark should lead. Repetition of the symbol across a page, garment, or campaign is not brand-building; it is visual dilution.

## Tasteful lockups

### 1. Wordmark alone — default

`My.BPM` with `My` at 62% scale and `BPM` dominant. Use for the storefront header, product cards, package face, and most campaign closes.

### 2. Heritage lockup — selective

Symbol left + wordmark right. Symbol height approximately 82% of the `BPM` cap height; separation approximately 45% of that cap height. Use on About, hangtags, packaging backs, and ownership contexts.

### 3. Symbol alone — iconic utility

Use for favicon, social avatar, small hardware, woven stamp, or an intentional hero product. Maintain clear space equal to at least one inner equalizer-bar width on every side.

## Elimination rules

- No `MY/BPM` slash notation.
- No all-uppercase `MY BPM` as the default identity.
- No symbol replacing the period, `M`, `y`, `B`, `P`, or `M`.
- No symbol behind the wordmark, inside a badge, or repeated as wallpaper.
- No symbol plus wordmark plus slogan fighting in one lockup.
- No new generic rave iconography. The exact heritage symbol is the only approved headphone/equalizer exception.
- No Kith-style script imitation, techno novelty display face, serif-as-fake-luxury, or logo dependent on accent color.

## Locked decision

**Typography is locked.** The corrected brand system is `My.BPM` (smaller `My`, dominant `BPM`) + the exact heritage symbol used selectively + `MyBPM` for handle-only contexts. The product-image standard is the next active gate.
