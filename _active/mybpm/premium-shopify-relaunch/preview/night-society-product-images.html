01 / Required sequence
Six answers before filler

THE PDP SHOULD REMOVE DOUBT IN ORDER.

The first five frames answer purchase questions. The sixth proves belonging. Additional images earn their place only when they add a new environment, construction fact, or motion truth.

01
Desire + silhouette

Hero full look

4:5 on-body. One complete look. Product fills 72–82% of frame height.

02
Artwork + color

Front truth

Neutral white, true print placement, quiet grounded shadow, repeatable crop.

03
Rear artwork

Back truth

Same camera height, product scale, background, and light as frame two.

04
Fit certainty

Fit profile

Side or three-quarter body. Sleeve, hem, waist, and drape remain visible.

05
Premium evidence

Material detail

Rib, seam, fabric weight, print edge, embroidery, hardware, or label at human scale.

06
Cultural belonging

Life proof

Same person and look in Festival, Club, or Daily Life. Atmosphere never hides the product.

03 / Photography V3
One grade across three worlds

THE PRODUCT DOES NOT BECOME A DIFFERENT BRAND AFTER DARK.

Repeat the cast, hero looks, 35mm/50mm lens family, and natural-skin baseline. Change the environment and light—not the person MyBPM is for.

01 / Festival

Collective scale.

Daylight expression, movement, care, and complete product-readable looks.

02 / Club

Intimate energy.

Practical light plus softened flash, identifiable faces, detail held in black.

03 / Daily life

Calm confidence.

Modern city structure, friendship, movement, listening, and ordinary life.

Cast3–5 recognizable people across the release.
LooksThree hero outfits repeat in every environment.
Lens35mm environment + 50mm human/product proximity.
GradeNatural skin, soft black, controlled white, restrained night color.
