Standing photography rules
Cast for credibility

Adult cultural participants and real friend pairings. A face must feel chosen for presence, not because a generic casting deck was available.

Show behavior

Choosing, listening, carrying, waiting, leaving, remembering. Records and equipment cannot function as instant cultural wallpaper.

Protect the product

Full silhouettes first; fabric and wear as evidence. The image world must elevate the garment while still feeling lived rather than staged.
