02 / TRANSLATION

BORROW THE PRINCIPLE. OWN THE TREATMENT.

The reference is a benchmark, not a template. Each transferable decision gets a MyBPM-specific reason, voice, and visual behavior.

KITH PRINCIPLE

Campaign-first entry

MyBPM opens on one current drop and one cultural proposition: “The Frequency Below the Floor.” No rotating promo carousel.

KITH PRINCIPLE

Quiet navigation

Four plain-language routes and a bag count. The header recedes so the campaign frame carries the first impression.

KITH PRINCIPLE

Dense product grid

Four columns on desktop, two on mobile, 4:5 imagery, minimal gaps, and product data that never competes with the garment.

KITH PRINCIPLE

Editorial chapters

Black campaign chapters and one mineral-white manifesto surface translate nightlife into restraint without neon spectacle.

KITH PRINCIPLE

Quiet PDP hierarchy

Gallery first, sticky buying column, then story, material, fit, care, and production notes as inspectable garment proof.

KITH PRINCIPLE

Discover layer

Editorial becomes culture documentation: specific scenes, people, sounds, and what the garment means after the event ends.

03 / PAGE GRAMMAR

EVERY PAGE HAS ONE JOB.

Premium comes from sequencing and omission. These modules are the v2 build contract for Shopify, not a loose mood board.

Home

Borrowed grammar

Full-bleed campaign, collection-led edit, editorial story.

MyBPM treatment

Sublevel hero → mineral manifesto → four-product Current Edit → blackout culture story → white garment proof.

Collection

Borrowed grammar

Full-width image, restrained controls, continuous product field.

MyBPM treatment

Dynamic collection hero, title + count, horizontal filters, portrait cards, near-zero gutters, optional bottom culture note.

Product

Borrowed grammar

Large gallery, quiet information architecture, persistent purchase path.

MyBPM treatment

4:5 media grid, square selectors, black purchase control, then a white evidence ledger for story, material, fit, care, and production.

Editorial

Borrowed grammar

Campaign storytelling that extends the product world.

MyBPM treatment

Scene-specific essays and photo stories—loading docks, transit, stairwells, concrete—never generic festival recaps.

04 / ORIGINALITY

THE LINE WE DO NOT CROSS.

Similarity should be legible at the level of taste and commerce discipline, not source code, signature modules, copy, imagery, or identity.

Use

  • Curation over catalog sprawl
  • Editorial image scale
  • Controlled hierarchy and quiet interface chrome
  • Collection-led storytelling
  • Consistent high-density product grids

Do not copy

  • Kith logos, typography lockups, copy, or naming
  • Page composition on a one-to-one basis
  • Proprietary modules, interactions, or source code
  • Campaign assets or product imagery
  • Brand-specific visual signatures that would create confusion
