## Archive from the primary drop

- Duplicate genre variants presented as separate near-identical products
- Old mockup-only listings without current samples
- Any item whose supplier app or personalization dependency is broken
- Placeholder products, weak images, or descriptions that cannot pass a 20-second proof check

## Sample acceptance bar

- Fabric weight and fiber content match the listing
- Decoration is centered, colorfast, and free of visible defects
- Measurements are recorded by size
- One wash test produces no unacceptable shrinkage, cracking, or twisting
- Production and shipping windows are confirmed
- At least four usable assets exist: front, back, detail, and on-body

Until a product passes this bar, its premium positioning is **UNTESTED**.
