# MyBPM Visual Reset — Gate 0

## Intent lock

MyBPM needs a photographic and cultural world before it needs another website design. The current White Signal build is retired as a visual direction. It remains staging evidence only.

The benchmark is Kith's discipline: curation, restraint, credible styling, consistent photography, and confident omission. The outcome must remain unmistakably MyBPM: adult electronic-music culture carried into everyday life.

## Locked

- White is the dominant commerce environment.
- Black supplies structure and contrast.
- “Raver as Identity Keeper” remains the cultural center.
- The adult raver is the primary customer: someone whose relationship with electronic music continues outside festivals and clubs.
- Moodboard approval precedes design tokens, page layouts, and Shopify changes.
- No additional Firecrawl usage is required for this reset.

## Rejected

- Acid or neon green
- Floating supplier-style product mockups as campaign imagery
- Faux CSS marble
- Repeated oversized all-caps slogans
- Shopper-facing sample, proof, or production-status labels
- Generic lasers, equalizers, waveforms, cyberpunk neon, or festival spectacle
- Kith logos, layouts, naming, or signature modules copied literally

## Gate 0 decision

Choose one primary brand world. The directions are intentionally different and should not be blended before selection.

### A — Afterimage Archive — recommended

Post-club daylight, transit, wet pavement, direct flash, real friends, garment evidence, and small artifacts from the night. This makes the existing identity thesis visible rather than explaining it.

### B — Resonance Lab

Sound-system engineering, garment construction, brushed aluminum, speaker components, water displacement, and precise studio product sculpture. Strong for a technical capsule; potentially too sterile as the permanent world.

### C — Night Society

Listening rooms, record shops, radio studios, mature styling, and portraits of credible cultural participants. Commercially mature; risks becoming a derivative lifestyle club without a sharper MyBPM tension.

## Gate 0 verdict — locked 2026-08-30

**Primary world: Night Society.** Its complete aesthetic, framing, positioning, maturity, and cultural credibility represent the long-term MyBPM vision.

**Emotional thesis: Afterimage Archive.** “The clothes live after the wristband comes off” is the ownable MyBPM idea that prevents Night Society from becoming a generic record-bar lifestyle campaign.

The hierarchy is deliberate:

- Night Society controls the permanent brand world, casting, styling, composition, and premium editorial restraint.
- Afterimage Archive controls the narrative tension, time progression, lived garment evidence, transit, daylight, and what a person carries home.
- Resonance Lab is parked as a future technical capsule language, not part of the permanent brand world.

The two chosen directions are not averaged. Night Society is the frame; Afterimage Archive is what happens inside it.

## Approval language

- `LOCK A`, `LOCK B`, or `LOCK C`
- `REVISE [direction]: [what feels wrong]`
- `REJECT [direction]: [why]`

Every rejection becomes a standing design rule. No homepage work begins at Gate 0.

## After Gate 0

The selected vibe becomes five separate tactical approvals:

1. Photography world
2. Material and texture
3. Typography and wordmark behavior
4. Product-image standard
5. Homepage entry frame

Only then is the approved system encoded in `DESIGN.md` and translated into one website section at a time.
