## Brief 1: "Still Synced (03:17 AM)"

### Design Role

**Primary visual.** This is the flagship of Capsule 1. It carries the brand's visual identity forward into premium-restrained territory (Stitch/Nana Banana parallel). The 03:17 AM timestamp is the *emotional anchor*; the EKG→BPM transformation is the *technical flex*.

### Generation Prompt (for Satori)

```
Design brief: "Still Synced (03:17 AM)" — premium apparel visual

Context: EDM streetwear for the raver whose body is still locked to the warehouse kick 
pattern days after the set ends. The shirt speaks to that 3:17 AM moment when they left 
the warehouse, body carrying the metronome home.

Visual direction:
- Center-chest placement
- Minimalist aesthetic (Stitch-inspired: negative space, high-end minimalism, not neon chaos)
- Core element: horizontal pulse-grid graphic (like an EKG line but more abstract)
- The line's rhythm transforms mid-way from irregular human heartbeat into perfectly metronomic 
  BPM pattern (visual metaphor: the set *reprograms* the heartbeat)
- Color: Electric blue (rhythmic accent) on black background or natural unbleached cotton
- Timestamp: "03:17 AM" in micro-text below the graphic, sans-serif, minimal weight
- Bottom right: "MY.BPM / STILL SYNCED" in small all-caps, matching micro-text weight

Aesthetic reference: Minimalist medical visualization meets electronic music precision. 
Not a literal EKG (avoid clinical feel). More like a rhythm visualization tool.

Mood: Elevated. Proof-of-presence. Specificity as luxury.

Deliverables:
- Hi-res PNG (300 DPI)
- Vector source file
- Color separations (for potential screen-print version)
- Mockup on gray hoodie + black tee + natural cotton variations
```

### Composition Constraints

| Element | Placement | Size | Notes |
|---------|-----------|------|-------|
| **Pulse-grid graphic** | Center chest, 5–7" wide | 5–7" | Horizontal orientation; should extend subtly toward shoulders |
| **Timestamp (03:17 AM)** | Below graphic, centered | 0.5–0.75" | Micro-text; creates moment-specificity visual anchor |
| **MY.BPM / STILL SYNCED** | Bottom right or centered below timestamp | 0.4–0.6" | Small all-caps; brand signature |
| **Breathing room** | 0.75–1" margin around graphic | — | Negative space is the premium feel |

### Typography

- **Sans-serif family:** Helvetica Neue / Inter / Montserrat (clean, minimal, no ornament)
- **Timestamp weight:** Light (100–300)
- **Logo/brand signature weight:** Regular (400)
- **All text:** Uppercase (consistency, modernity)
- **Hierarchy:** EKG graphic dominates; text supports

### Color Direction

| Version | Background | Graphic | Text | Mood |
|---------|-----------|---------|------|------|
| **Primary** | Natural (unbleached cotton) | Electric blue (#007FFF) | Black | Premium minimal; warehouse energy meets day-wear |
| **Alternative 1** | Black | Electric blue | White | High-contrast; rave-forward but restrained |
| **Alternative 2** | Heather gray | Teal (#20B2AA) | Black | Softer entry; still recognizable |

**Recommendation:** Primary version ships first. Test alternatives in Wave 2.

### Mockup Specifications

- Flat-lay on light background (natural cotton)
- Worn flat (full-body visible)
- Detail shot of graphic (EKG→BPM transformation clear)
- Close-up of timestamp micro-text (proof of detail-luxury)

## Brief 2: "Sunday 4AM ↔ Monday 9AM"

### Design Role

**Typography-forward visual.** This shirt is *copy-as-design*. Minimal graphical element; maximum type hierarchy. The split-screen timestamp is the *entire concept*; the matching heartbeat trace is the *subtle unifier*.

### Generation Prompt (for Satori)

```
Design brief: "Sunday 4AM ↔ Monday 9AM" — type-as-visual

Context: The raver living a split-screen life. Warehouse self. Office self. Same body, 
two different timestamps. The visual should make the *code-switching* visible.

Visual direction:
- Center-chest placement
- Pure typography (minimal icon/graphic element)
- Left side: "SUN 4:00 AM" in bold sans-serif, large scale
- Arrow or divider: ↔ (or a subtle vertical pulse line, same heartbeat trace as Brief 1)
- Right side: "MON 9:00 AM" in same-weight sans-serif, same scale
- Below both timestamps: a thin horizontal heartbeat trace (same visual language as Brief 1, 
  unifying the moment)
- Aesthetic: Corporate-meets-warehouse. Clean. No gradient, no shadow. Type only.

Mood: Clever. Recognizable. Works in 3 seconds (no decoder needed).

Deliverables:
- Hi-res PNG (300 DPI)
- Vector source file
- Color separations
- Mockup on gray tee + white tee + navy tee variations
```

### Composition Constraints

| Element | Placement | Size | Notes |
|---------|-----------|------|-------|
| **"SUN 4:00 AM"** | Center-left, chest height | 1.5–2" tall | Bold weight; easy to read at distance |
| **Divider (↔ or pulse line)** | Center, between timestamps | 0.5–1" wide | Creates visual unity; the moment is continuous |
| **"MON 9:00 AM"** | Center-right, same height as left | 1.5–2" tall | Matching weight; symmetry = same body |
| **Heartbeat trace** | Below both, spanning full width | 4–6" wide | Thin line; subtle connective tissue |
| **Breathing room** | 0.5–1" margin around text | — | Type-dominant, but clean |

### Typography

- **Sans-serif family:** Helvetica Neue / Gotham / Montserrat (bold, geometric, corporate-forward)
- **Timestamp weight:** Bold (700–900)
- **Heartbeat line weight:** Ultra-light (< 1pt)
- **All text:** All-caps (consistency)
- **Hierarchy:** Timestamps dominant; trace supports

### Color Direction

| Version | Background | Text | Trace | Mood |
|---------|-----------|------|-------|------|
| **Primary** | Natural (unbleached) | Black | Thin black line | Warehouse-meets-office; minimal contrast |
| **Alternative 1** | Navy blue | White | White trace | Rave-forward; high contrast for office wear |
| **Alternative 2** | White | Black | Electric blue trace | Clean corporate; rave accent hidden in detail |

**Recommendation:** Primary version ships first (easiest production, fastest turnaround).

### Mockup Specifications

- Flat-lay on light background
- Worn on model (shows how timestamp sits on chest)
- Angle shot showing how type reads from 3+ feet away
- Close-up showing heartbeat trace detail

## Brief 3: "Resting Heart Rate: 140 BPM" (5-Variant Family)

### Design Role

**Modular system visual.** One core design, five color variations tied to genre (house/techno/trance/dubstep/hardstyle). The EKG→kick-pattern transformation is the *core visual*; the BPM number is the *genre identifier*.

### Generation Prompt (for Satori)

```
Design brief: "Resting Heart Rate" — genre-family system

Context: The between-raves raver whose body is calibrated to a genre. House isn't what 
they listen to; it's what their body runs at. Five genre variants, one core visual concept.

Visual direction:
- Center-chest placement
- Core element: EKG line transforming into a kick pattern (visual metaphor: heartbeat 
  becomes BPM becomes genre becomes identity)
- The transformation should be *visible* and *smooth* (not jarring)
- Genre label: [GENRE] — [BPM] in sans-serif below the graphic
- Color per genre: House=golden yellow, Techno=teal/cyan, Trance=emerald green, 
  Dubstep=charcoal gray, Hardstyle=crimson red
- The EKG→kick graphic should show in full in the primary color, with a subtle secondary 
  accent if needed per genre

Mood per genre:
- House: steady, warm, building
- Techno: precise, cold, relentless
- Trance: emotional, expansive, climactic
- Dubstep: heavy, anticipatory, massive
- Hardstyle: relentless, energetic, punishing

Deliverables:
- Hi-res PNG per variant (300 DPI, 5 files)
- Vector source file (template with per-variant color swaps)
- Color separations per variant
- Mockups on natural cotton (all 5 colors) + gray hoodie variants
```

### Composition Constraints (All Variants)

| Element | Placement | Size | Notes |
|---------|-----------|------|-------|
| **EKG→kick graphic** | Center chest, 5–7" wide | 5–7" | Horizontal; extends subtly toward shoulders |
| **Genre label** | Below graphic, centered | 0.6–0.8" | [GENRE] / [BPM] format |
| **Breathing room** | 0.75–1" margin | — | Negative space keeps it premium |

### Typography (All Variants)

- **Sans-serif family:** Helvetica Neue / Inter / Gotham (clean, no serif)
- **Genre/BPM weight:** Regular (400–500)
- **All text:** All-caps
- **Hierarchy:** Graphic dominates; label supports

### Color Direction (Per-Variant)

| Variant | Genre | BPM | Graphic Color | Background | Text Color | Mood |
|---------|-------|-----|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | House | 124 | Golden yellow (#FFD700) | Natural cotton | Black | Warm, building, steady |
| **2** | Techno | 130 | Teal/Cyan (#20B2AA) | Natural cotton | Black | Precise, cold, relentless |
| **3** | Trance | 138 | Emerald green (#50C878) | Natural cotton | Black | Emotional, expansive, soaring |
| **4** | Dubstep | 140 | Charcoal gray (#36454F) | Natural cotton | White | Heavy, dark, anticipatory |
| **5** | Hardstyle | 150 | Crimson red (#DC143C) | Natural cotton | White | Relentless, energetic, punishing |

**Recommendation:** Dubstep (140 Charcoal) ships first as primary SKU (existing catalog shows dubstep affinity). Others follow in rotation.

### Modular System Notes

- All 5 variants use the *same* EKG→kick graphic template
- Color swaps only (no shape/composition changes)
- Allows for rapid variant production
- Per-genre text color choices (white on dark variants, black on light) for legibility

### Mockup Specifications

- Flat-lay on natural cotton (all 5 colors shown side-by-side for comparison)
- Close-up of EKG→kick transformation (shows the detail work)
- Worn on model (one per variant; shows how color reads on body)
- Social moment context: "Dubstep friend sees the 140" photo (aspirational)

## Next Phase: Phase 5 DIAGNOSE

After Shopify setup and email launch, install 6-metric instrumentation:

1. **Concept recognizability (50ms threshold):** Do customers recognize the moment before reading the description?
2. **Add-to-cart rate:** Which concept converts on recognition speed alone?
3. **Social sharing rate:** Which concept gets tagged/shared first?
4. **AOV (Average Order Value):** Does #1+#2 pair drive higher basket value?
5. **Email open rate:** Does the "moment" framing outperform existing MyBPM subject lines?
6. **Return rate:** Do buyers keep the shirt (satisfaction proxy)?

**Pre-committed kill/scale rules:**
- If any SKU drops below $150 first-week revenue: pause, audit messaging
- If AOV stays under $85 after 2 weeks: kill the pairing strategy, sell individually
- If email open rate < 18% (existing MyBPM baseline = 24%): revise subject line, test new lead
- If add-to-cart-but-abandon rate > 35%: reduce price to $40, test
