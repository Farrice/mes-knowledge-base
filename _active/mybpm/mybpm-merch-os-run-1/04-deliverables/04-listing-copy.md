✓ **Original phrases** — zero artist/festival/lyric IP
✓ **Premium-restrained tone** — Stitch/Nana Banana parallel (minimalist, confident, not neon chaos)
✓ **Social moments are the *real* copy** — the "stranger recognizes it" moment is the whole pitch
✓ **Price point embedded naturally** — $45 appears as "premium tee," not highlighted
