## DESIGN BRIEF 1: The Kandi Keeper

### Buyer Scene (who is wearing this, where, in what moment?)
A raver at 2 PM on a Wednesday, at work or home, reaches for this shirt because they're about to go through their kandi box. The shirt is a visual bookmark for that moment.

### Graphic System
- **Focal point**: A handful of kandi beads in the center of the chest, stylized but recognizable (not realistic, not abstract—somewhere between illustration and symbol)
- **Color palette**: Neon or jewel-tone beads reflecting traditional rave colors (rainbow, pastels, metallics)
- **Style**: Clean, vector-based, modern. Not retro-pixel, not hyper-realistic.
- **Composition**: One clear 50ms element (the kandi); text wraps or sits below

### Identity Micro-Text
- **Main text**: "I Still Have Your Kandi"
- **Placement**: Below the graphic (center or left-aligned)
- **Font**: Bold sans-serif, modern, readable at 50ms
- **Size**: Large enough to dominate without competing with graphic
- **Optional subtext**: Kandi-trading date or "Est. [Year]" if this becomes a limited run

### Niche-Specific Avoid-List
- ❌ No kandi that looks like candy/generic beads (must feel intentional, rave-coded)
- ❌ No party-focused imagery (champagne, crowds, glitter-only aesthetic)
- ❌ No overly cartoonish or cute style (ravers respect craft)
- ❌ No text effects that cheapen it (drop shadows, neon glow on every letter)
- ❌ No demographics ("women," "EDM lovers") — this is person-coded, not group-coded

### Typography & Composition Brief
- **Leading text**: 1–2 lines, strong kerning, readable at 50ms
- **Secondary elements**: Kandi graphic above or integrated into text
- **Negative space**: Significant breathing room around the focal point
- **Placement on garment**: Centered chest or slightly high-chest (not low belly)
- **Back option**: Optional: "Kandi is Memory. Memory is Love." with a single small kandi symbol

## DESIGN BRIEF 2: Stranger-to-Friend

### Buyer Scene
A raver who met their best friend at a rave, now at home or at another event, wearing this because it reminds them of THE moment. Or they're about to go to an event where they know they'll meet new people and form connections.

### Graphic System
- **Focal point**: Two connected figures (silhouettes or simplified) in a crowd, drawn in a way that they're distinguished from the crowd around them. Or: two hands reaching toward each other in a crowd.
- **Color palette**: High contrast (one figure lighter, one darker, or one highlighted). Suggest "finding each other" visually.
- **Style**: Minimalist line art or silhouette. Modern, not retro.
- **Composition**: The two figures are clear at 50ms, even in a busy composition

### Identity Micro-Text
- **Main text**: "Met Strangers, Found Family"
- **Placement**: Integrated with the graphic or above/below
- **Font**: Warm, modern sans-serif (not cold, not too tech)
- **Tone**: Intimate, not bombastic

### Niche-Specific Avoid-List
- ❌ No cliché crowd imagery (hands in the air, generic festival crowds)
- ❌ No couple focus (this isn't romantic—it's chosen family)
- ❌ No demographic labels ("friends," "besties")
- ❌ No alcohol/party props
- ❌ No text that sounds transactional ("Get free friends," etc.)

### Typography & Composition Brief
- **Leading text**: 2–3 words, conversational tone
- **Graphic-to-text ratio**: Graphic should dominate; text supports
- **Placement on garment**: Centered, chest-facing (wearer wants this visible)
- **Color contrast**: High enough that the connection between the two figures reads instantly

## DESIGN BRIEF 3: Sunrise Survivor

### Buyer Scene
A raver early in the morning after a show, or at work on a Tuesday, remembering the set that broke them open. Wearing this is a way to keep that feeling alive.

### Graphic System
- **Focal point**: A sunrise or dawn horizon, simplified and strong. Or: a silhouette of a person with arms open, framed by a rising sun.
- **Color palette**: Warm tones (oranges, purples, pinks) transitioning to the color of the rest of the shirt. Suggest the dawn moment.
- **Style**: Modern, clean, not overly detailed. Instantly recognizable as "sunrise" at 50ms.
- **Composition**: The horizon/sun occupies the upper half of the chest graphic; human figure (if included) is below

### Identity Micro-Text
- **Main text**: "I Walked Out Changed"
- **Placement**: Below the graphic, integrated or separate
- **Font**: Strong, confident sans-serif
- **Tone**: Declaration, not question

### Niche-Specific Avoid-List
- ❌ No generic nature imagery (hiking, landscape porn, that's not this)
- ❌ No hyper-realistic sunrise (this isn't a landscape photo—it's a symbol)
- ❌ No text about "partying" or "recovery" (this is transformation, not hedonism aftermath)
- ❌ No figure that looks exhausted or defeated (figure should look open, not tired)
- ❌ No small print; keep it bold and central

### Typography & Composition Brief
- **Leading text**: 1 line, powerful impact
- **Graphic placement**: Chest-centered, takes up a significant footprint
- **Color integration**: The sunrise colors should feel like part of the shirt, not an isolated graphic
- **Wearability**: Strong enough to see when worn, not so big it reads as costume-y

## DESIGN BRIEF 4: Flow Arts Soul

### Buyer Scene
A flow artist (poi, hoop, fan spinner) at home, at a festival, or at practice, wearing this because it names what they do and why it's spiritual, not just visual.

### Graphic System
- **Focal point**: A single flow tool (poi, hoop, or fan) rendered in motion. Not static—show the arc of movement, the spin.
- **Color palette**: Can be vibrant (neon, glow effects) OR minimal (monochrome). The movement is the star, not the color.
- **Style**: Dynamic but clean. Show the motion blur or the flow path, not just the object.
- **Composition**: The tool/motion occupies the center; minimal additional elements

### Identity Micro-Text
- **Main text**: "I Spin to Find Myself"
- **Placement**: Integrated with the graphic motion or below
- **Font**: Modern, flowing (not rigid, not overly decorative)
- **Tone**: Meditative, intentional

### Niche-Specific Avoid-List
- ❌ No performance-focused framing ("Showtime," "Watch me," "Look at this")
- ❌ No crowd/party context (this is solo, meditative, internal)
- ❌ No generic "flowing" aesthetic (that's every flow-art brand)
- ❌ No text that sounds like a trick or skill ("Advanced Flow," "Skill Level")
- ❌ No overcomplicated graphic (flow is about clarity of motion, not visual noise)

### Typography & Composition Brief
- **Leading text**: 5 words, clear and meditative
- **Graphic-to-text balance**: The motion should be the primary visual; text is secondary
- **Placement on garment**: Centered chest, high enough that the full motion arc is visible
- **Color and movement**: If using color, let the motion path create the focal point

## DESIGN BRIEF 5: Raver's Commitment

### Buyer Scene
A serious raver who's been in the scene for years, at an event or at home, wearing this to signal: *I'm not casual about this. This is my people.*

### Graphic System
- **Focal point**: A table (literal or symbolic—a set of hands meeting on a table, or a defined "seat" space) OR a symbol of permanence (a crown, an anchor, a monument—dealer's choice based on artist feel)
- **Color palette**: Strong, confident colors. Not bright and party-focused—deep, grounded tones that suggest commitment.
- **Style**: Bold, architectural. Not soft or cute. This is a declaration.
- **Composition**: The central symbol is unmistakable at 50ms

### Identity Micro-Text
- **Main text**: "This Is My Table"
- **Placement**: Integrated with or prominently displayed with the graphic
- **Font**: Bold, strong, serif OR bold sans-serif. Confidence over friendliness.
- **Tone**: Declaration. Final word. No question mark.

### Niche-Specific Avoid-List
- ❌ No casual "I'm here for a good time" energy (this is serious)
- ❌ No temporary/party imagery (confetti, balloons, short-term vibes)
- ❌ No demographic framing ("for everyone," "all are welcome"—this is identity-specific)
- ❌ No text that sounds uncertain ("I think this is my place," "Maybe my table")
- ❌ No overly ornate or decorative style (this is strong, not fancy)

### Typography & Composition Brief
- **Leading text**: 4 words, powerful and clear
- **Graphic placement**: Chest-centered, significant presence
- **Font weight**: Heavy, bold, no thin weights
- **Negative space**: Breathing room around the declaration (don't crowd it)
- **Impact**: Should read as "I belong here" from across a room
