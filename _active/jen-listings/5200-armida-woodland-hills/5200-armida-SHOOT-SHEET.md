## Phase 1: Strategy

- **Property Overview:** 5200 Armida Dr, Woodland Hills 91364. **$3,199,000.** 5 bed / 5.5 bath, **4,200 sqft total** on a **0.52-acre lot**, single story. Main residence ~3,600 sqft (4 bed / 4.5 bath) **plus a new, detached ~600 sqft ADU (1 bed / 1 bath with its own kitchen)**. Built 1957, fully transformed — *not* new construction. Private driveway at the end of a cul-de-sac. Private in-ground pool (**MLS says Spa: None — do not say "spa"**), pergola outdoor kitchen, big flat lawn, mature oaks. Chef's kitchen (large island, porcelain slab counters, Thermador appliances, custom cabinetry), wood-burning fireplace, cathedral ceilings, main-level primary, dedicated laundry, attached 2-car garage, central AC. **Listed today, 8/5/2026.** MLS #SR26169754 (CRMLS), co-listed **Marty Azoulay + Jennifer Santulan, Equity Union**. **Open houses: Fri 8/7 11–2, Sat 8/8 1–4, Sun 8/9 1–4.**

- **The "Magic Trifecta":** **the two kitchens a hundred feet apart** *(pattern interrupt — the fact that breaks the "ADU means converted garage" assumption)* + **4,200 sqft with zero stairs on half an acre** *(the real pitch)* + **the house wrapping the pool** *(bonus proof point — turns a backyard amenity into a floor plan)*.

- **The Woodland Hills Pivot:** At $3.2M, buyers arrive assuming Encino, Calabasas, or Hidden Hills. Jen names that in ≤8 words, then flips it with what the number actually buys *here*: half an acre, one level, five bedrooms, a pool the house wraps around, a real guest cottage, and Taft Charter High (9/10) four tenths of a mile away. The flip is **space and layout**, never a discount.

- **The one thing not to claim:** this is **$762/sqft against a ~$608/sqft zip median** — it is *above* the median on price-per-foot. There is no "below market" story here and no hook in this set claims one. The honest frame is *what you get*, not *what you save*. If a script drifts toward "deal" or "smart money," it's wrong and it will read as wrong to a $3M buyer.

- **Voice rules for this shoot:** her register is "wait till you see this," said quietly. Lowercase in captions, ellipses over exclamation points, one emoji max per script (🏡 🤝 ✨), closers are invitations ("let's chat," "i'd love to show you," "come see it"), never commands. She fronts the first 15–20 seconds on camera, then b-roll takes over. Every hook below is **walkable** — a line she can say while approaching or touching the thing it's about.

## ✅ Quick check before filming (Jen)

**Say these only after you've confirmed them on the walkthrough:**
- **"The house wraps around the pool"** is Hook 4's spine and appears in the caption. The aerials support it — **walk the perimeter and confirm the footprint actually curves around the water** before filming. If it reads as a straight run instead, change the line to "every room back here opens onto the pool," which the photos support either way.
- **"Almost every room" sees the pool** (Hook 4 lock-in). Count them on the walkthrough. If it's fewer than you expect, say "the great room, the family room, and the dining table all look at it" — three named rooms beats a vague "almost every."
- **🚩 There is no spa.** The MLS reads `Pool features: In Ground, Private` and **`Spa features: None`**. Nothing in this package says "spa" — keep it that way, and if a photo looks like one, confirm with Marty before calling it anything.
- **Confirm there's no HOA.** Zillow shows HOA fees as `$0`/blank, which is absence of data rather than a confirmed zero. If Marty confirms it, **add "no HOA" to the caption** — buyers cross-shopping gated Calabasas communities care a lot about that line.
- **Never put an age number on the trees** (Hook 6). They're clearly mature and the house dates to 1957, but no source states when they were planted. "you cannot grow that canopy in five years" is true and safe; "sixty-year-old oaks" is a guess.
- **"Not one stair"** (Hook 2) — the MLS says Levels: One, Stories: 1, All Bedrooms Down. **Confirm there's no step down into the living room or up to the ADU**, which is common in 1957 ranch layouts and would make the line literally false on camera.
- **"Thermador"** — the listing description says Thermador; the photographed range reads like one, but confirm the badge before naming the brand on camera.
- **"Porcelain slab countertops"** — the description says porcelain slab; the CRMLS facts field says *Granite* **and** *Solid Surface*. The description is likely right, but confirm, or say "stone slab" and stay safe.
- **Bed/bath count.** Zillow's header reads "5 beds / 6 baths." The description reads 4 bed / 4.5 bath main + 1 bed / 1 bath ADU = **5 bed / 5.5 bath**. Say it the description's way — "four bedrooms in the main house, plus a one-bedroom guest house" — it's both accurate and the better pitch.
- **"Basement: Finished"** appears in the CRMLS facts and there is **no basement in any of the 57 photos.** Do not mention a basement on camera until Marty confirms.
- **Sewer: Unknown** in the MLS. Don't field that question with a guess.
- **Furnished: Yes** is in the facts and the home shows fully furnished. Confirm with Marty whether furniture actually conveys before implying it does.
- **Hook 3's Encino comparison** is built off Zillow's own similar-homes set (15641 High Knoll Rd, Encino — $3,360,000 for ~3,200 sqft; 4423 Tedregal Ct, Calabasas — $3,190,000 for ~3,600 sqft).
