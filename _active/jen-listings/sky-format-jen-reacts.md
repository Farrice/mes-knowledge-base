> Generated via `sky-tan-format-engine` `/sky-find-format`, run through Jen's voice constraints (warm, educational, emoji-rich, never vicious/urgency). Test-drive 2026-05-30.

## Purpose (the offer)
Free tour/consult → buyer's-agent representation for SFV first-time buyers. **Conversion register = warm/educational/low-pressure** (ICP is anxious, not transactional).

## Avatar's #1 content pain
Not "show me houses." It's **"Can I actually afford this? Am I being financially irresponsible? Am I even ready?"** The format must answer the fear, not list amenities.

## Cross-niche hunt (steal from outside real estate)
- **Reaction-label** (Sky Tan's own content-niche format): doom-scroll + label what an expert notices → Jen reacts to real SFV listings, labeling what a first-timer would miss.
- **Symptom→root-cause** (health niche) → "This 'cheap' condo costs MORE than the $899K house — here's why" (HOA / Mello-Roos / old systems).
- **Process breakdown** (Sam O'Leary ads) → "What $850K actually buys in [neighborhood] vs [neighborhood]."

**WINNER: "Jen Reacts"** — a warm Zillow walkthrough-label. Reaction × process-breakdown hybrid, ported from Sky's label format, twisted for FTHB anxiety.

## The 4 build steps (locked before filming)

**1. Activity inventory:** preview listings, run comps, translate financing (DTI/PMI/down payment), spot red flags (roof/foundation/permits), SFV neighborhood knowledge, calculate true monthly cost, reassure anxious buyers. → Target pain: **readiness/affordability fear.**

**2. Visual pairing:** Jen on her phone/iPad scrolling a real listing, angled slightly to the side (advisor-beside-you, warm — not a staged tour). On-screen labels appear as she talks. (Can be filmed reading her own notes — the visual just has to read as "realtor previewing a home.")

**3. Repeatable structure (the lock):**
- **Frame 1:** the listing's most aspirational photo + on-screen text `SFV realtor 🏡 $850K → ~$5,400/mo`
- "Okay, let's see if this is actually a good first home…" *(warm verbal hook)*
- Walk the photos, dropping labels: ✅ what's genuinely great · ⚠️ the hidden cost a first-timer misses *(+ the real number)* · 💰 "here's what you'd actually pay monthly at today's rate"
- **Defuse the fear:** "and no — wanting this doesn't make you irresponsible. Here's the math 💛"
- **Close:** "Want me to pull the real numbers on a place like this? DM me 'TOUR' 💛"

**4. Three-hook stack (frame one):**
- **Visual:** the listing's best/most aspirational photo
- **Verbal:** "Let's see if this $850K starter is actually a trap or a steal…"
- **Written:** `SFV realtor 🏡 | $850K → ~$5,400/mo`

## The twist (Conversion Gate pass ✅)
Every video shows the **hidden cost a first-time buyer can't see** + the **real monthly number** — only a working SFV agent could surface that. Demonstrates expertise (not generic tips), in Jen's warm voice, while dissolving the readiness fear. → Books "TOUR" DMs.
