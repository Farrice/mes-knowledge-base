## Intent Lock

This pack is built for the buyer Jen actually wants to attract: someone serious enough to tour and buy, but skeptical enough to dismiss Van Nuys or the price too fast.

The creative job is to move that buyer from:

1. "I already know what Van Nuys is."
2. "That price feels high for the area."
3. "A pretty remodel is not enough."

to:

1. "Wait, what is behind that gate?"
2. "The ADU changes how I think about this."
3. "This might be the Valley option I was missing."

Success test: if a real buyer forwards the Reel with "I did not know Van Nuys had this," the hook is doing its job.

## Buyer Fit Without Unsafe Targeting

Use these as creative lenses, not exclusionary ad targeting:

1. **The first real house buyer:** renting or condo-living has started to feel too small, and they want a house that already feels finished.
2. **The ADU-aware buyer:** they understand that a second space can change the whole property plan if permits, rules, and numbers check out.
3. **The flexible-living buyer:** they need a private work setup, guest space, creative room, or extended-living option without giving up the main house.

Public-safe language:
- "household"
- "guests"
- "extended living"
- "private work setup"
- "flexible second space"
- "possible rental support, after verification"
- "school fit if that matters to you"

Avoid:
- "perfect for families"
- "great for kids"
- "safe neighborhood"
- "bad area"
- "hood"
- "guaranteed rental income"
- exact ADU rent numbers unless Jen runs live comps first

## Reel 1 - Before You Cross Off Van Nuys

**Job:** Reframe the neighborhood hesitation without insulting the area.  
**Length:** 35-45 seconds  
**First frame:** gate opening, Jen stepping through, clean exterior reveal.  
**On-screen opening:** `Before you cross off Van Nuys...`  
**CTA:** `Comment WILLIS and let's walk through it.`

### Teleprompter

"Before you cross Van Nuys off your list, walk through this gate with me.

Because I know what some buyers are going to do. They are going to see the address, see the price, and decide they already know the story.

But this one is different enough that you should see it before you judge it.

You have a gated property, a fully remodeled main house, solar, updated systems, and then the part I really want you to pay attention to: a detached ADU.

That second space changes the conversation. It can be guest space, a private work setup, extended living, or possible rental support after the numbers and paperwork are verified.

The remodel gets your attention, but the value is in the options.

Come with me. Let's take a look."
