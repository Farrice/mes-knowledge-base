# First Sale Sprint Plan: Fasting & Weight Loss

Based on the Maria Wendt Digital Product Mastery system, here is the exact, un-sexy, hyper-focused sprint to get your first $7 digital product sale in the next 48 hours.

## 1. The $10 Problem
*   **The Target Identity:** People who want to fast for weight loss but constantly fail on Day 2 or 3 because of headaches, cravings, and nighttime binges.
*   **The Verbatim Pain:** "I keep trying to do 16:8 or 24-hour fasts but I get horrible headaches and binge eat at night. How do you survive the hunger?"
*   **The Outcome:** Shifting their identity from "I can't stick to a fast" to "I completed my first 72 hours smoothly without crashing."

## 2. The 60-Minute Product Spec
*   **Format:** A simple 3-page Google Doc (exported to PDF) or Notion template. No videos. No complex modules.
*   **Name:** The 72-Hour Fasting Survival Protocol
*   **What's inside:**
    *   **The Hydration Code:** The exact ratio of sodium/potassium/magnesium to drink on waking to kill fasting headaches.
    *   **The "Binge-Breaker" Meal Formula:** What exact macro split to eat for your first meal to prevent the 8 PM binge.
    *   **The 3-Day Shift Checklist:** A simple daily checklist covering water intake, salt, and mental re-framing.
*   **Creation Rule:** Build this in less than 60 minutes. It must be brutally actionable.

## 3. The Checkout Setup
*   **Format:** Gumroad or Stripe payment link. Price it at $7.
*   **Destination Painting Copy (for the checkout page):**
    > "Stop giving up on your fast at 8 PM because of headaches, shakes, and cravings. *The 72-Hour Fasting Survival Protocol* gives you the exact electrolyte formulas and meal structures to transition your body into fat-burning mode effortlessly. By day 4, you'll wake up with flat-belly clarity, zero cravings, and the absolute confidence that you can actually do this."

## 4. The "Help One" Outreach Kit
*   **Where to look:** 
    *   Reddit: `r/intermittentfasting`, `r/fasting`. Search for terms like "headache," "failed," "binge," "too hungry to sleep."
    *   Facebook Groups: Beginners fasting groups. Look for the frustrated, vulnerable posts.
*   **The Script:**
    > "Hey [Name], I saw your post about struggling with headaches and evening hunger on day 2. I used to fail there every time too until I figured out the exact electrolyte ratio and 'break-fast' meal structure to stop the crashes. I put together a quick survival protocol for the first 72 hours that fixes this. Happy to send the link over if it'd be helpful?"

## 5. The 48-Hour Timeline
*   **Hours 1-2:** Write the 3-page Google Doc. Keep it entirely outcome-focused. Export to PDF.
*   **Hour 3:** Set up the Gumroad checkout page using the Destination Painting copy.
*   **Hours 4-5:** Prospect 10-15 people on Reddit/Facebook who posted about this exact pain point *in the last week*.
*   **Hours 6-48:** Send 1-to-1 DMs with the script. Answer their questions genuinely. Secure your very first $7 sale.

## 6. The Post-Sale Protocol
*   **The Follow-Up:** 24 hours after they buy, DM them: *"So excited for you. Check page 2 for the electrolyte mix—it changes everything. Let me know how you feel on Day 3?"*
*   **The Strategy:** This $7 product is your *Identity Shift* mechanism. You aren't trying to get rich off $7; you are turning a skeptic into a buyer. Once they succeed, they become the warmest possible lead for your future $47-$97 product (e.g., *The 30-Day Fasting Lifestyle Blueprint*).
