## The Trust Pathway

Your Instagram content moves people through 4 stages. Every piece of content has ONE job:

| Stage | Format | Purpose | Example |
|-------|--------|---------|---------|
| **1. Attention** | Reels | Hook them with a specific, challenging claim | "Why eating 6 small meals a day is making you fat and tired" |
| **2. Connection** | Stories | Show the real you — messy, honest, human | Behind-the-scenes: black coffee at 3 AM on shift, meal prep at midnight |
| **3. Trust** | Carousels | Teach something valuable — prove you know your stuff | "What biologically happens when you don't eat for 16 hours" (slide-by-slide) |
| **4. Conversion** | Bio Link + DMs | Turn followers into buyers | "Comment FAST to get my $7 Pre-Flight Checklist" |

## Content Pillars & Posting Cadence

**Weekly cadence:** 4 Reels + 1 Carousel + Daily Stories

### Pillar 1: The "Myth-Buster" (Attention) — Reels

Hook-driven short videos for top-of-funnel reach. These are your growth engine.

**Concept ideas:**
- "Why eating 6 small meals a day is making you fat and tired"
- "The 'healthy' breakfast habit that destroys your focus by 11 AM"
- "I eat one meal at 3 AM. Here's why I'm in the best shape of my life"
- "Every nurse on my unit struggles with weight. I do the opposite. Here's why"
- "The worst thing you can do after a 12-hour shift is eat immediately"

**Execution:**
- Fast cuts, B-roll of you in scrubs/gym/kitchen
- On-screen text matching your voiceover
- 45-90 seconds max
- Hook in the first 2 seconds — no warm-up, no "hey guys"
- End with a soft CTA: "Follow for more" or "Save this"

### Pillar 2: The "Fasting Blueprint" (Trust) — Carousels

High-dwell-time educational sliders. These build credibility and get saved/shared.

**Concept ideas:**
- "What biologically happens when you don't eat for 16 hours" (slide-by-slide timeline)
- "The 3 things you CAN consume during a fast without breaking it"
- "My exact eating window on a 12-hour night shift" (visual schedule)
- "The 5 signs your fast is actually working" (with biological explanations)

**Execution:**
- High-contrast designs, bold typography, simple diagrams
- 7-10 slides max
- CTA on last slide: "Want the full checklist? Link in bio"
- Design tip: use Canva's carousel templates, consistent brand colors

### Pillar 3: The "Lifestyle Proof" (Connection) — Stories & Reels

Show the system working in YOUR real life. This is where trust happens.

**Concept ideas:**
- "How I break a 16-hour fast at a business lunch" (vlog style)
- Massive dinner showcase: "This is what I eat in my window — restriction isn't necessary"
- Time-lapse of a full shift: when you eat, when you fast, energy levels
- "What I pack for a 12-hour shift" (meal prep walkthrough)
- Real-time "fasting check-in" during a shift — how you're feeling at hour 14

**Execution:**
- Raw, unpolished, iPhone aesthetic
- Authenticity beats production quality every time
- Talk to the camera like you're talking to a friend
- Show the real environment — hospital halls, nurse station, kitchen at 3 AM

## Quality Gate (Before Anything Goes Live)

Every piece of content must pass these 3 checks:

1. **The "Joe Rogan" Test:** Does this sound like a human talking, or a corporate brochure? If it sounds scripted, rewrite it.

2. **The Vicious Hook Check:** Do the first 3 seconds promise a specific payoff or challenge a specific belief? If the hook is generic ("Hey guys, today I want to talk about..."), kill it and start with the punch.

3. **The Proof Braid:** Are you demonstrating expertise (showing your body, your meals, your routine, your energy) rather than just stating it? Show, don't tell. Always.
