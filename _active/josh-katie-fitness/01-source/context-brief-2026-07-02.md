# CLIENT 1 — KATIE

World-class violinist and mother of two; trains 5×/week (Mon–Fri). **Goal: break the 134–135 plateau and reach 129–130 "strong and lean" via recomposition** (fat down, muscle up), with visible lower-body definition (quads, glutes, hamstrings) and lower abs.

**Life constraints (hard inputs):** demanding performance schedule drives fluctuating stress/sleep — **violin performance quality overrides any dietary protocol.** Manage cortisol as a real variable.

**Health considerations:**
- **Gallbladder removed** → impaired fat digestion. Keep meals lower-fat, **~10–15 g fat/meal spread across the day, adjusted to tolerance** (there is no strict clinical cutoff post-cholecystectomy and tolerance improves over time — comfort guardrail, not a hard rule). **Ox bile + digestive enzymes with fat-containing meals** (physiologically sound, OTC/safe; ~125–500 mg bile salts/meal scaled to fat). This is her chronically-under-executed lever — nail it.
- **Hormonal-imbalance history** → protect sleep, avoid extreme deficits, consider cycle-based training adjustments.
- **Back history — fully resolved.** Heavy is cleared, but pristine form is non-negotiable; stop on any pain. (Deadlift is `PAUSED` by default on her posterior day in favor of a spine-sparing barbell hip thrust; toggle on by request.)
- Left shoulder (paddleboard fall) — fully resolved as of May 2026; full bilateral upper pressing/pulling back in effect.

**Current training approach:** 3 full-body foundationals (squat-led / hinge-led / unilateral-athletic) with **heavy low-rep primaries** to leverage her response, + 2 triggers (a glute/quad pump-and-burn day; a posterior + lower-ab + Zone-2 day). Lower-body bias every session. Progressive overload waves with a week-6 deload.

**Current nutrition approach (maintenance-recomp):**
- **Modest deficit, close to maintenance** (~200–500 kcal/day, ~10–15%) with **most of the deficit from NEAT (11–13k steps/day) + the two trigger days**, not from cutting food hard.
- Macros: Foundational (Mon/Wed/Fri) ~1,850 cal / 145–150 g P / 200 C / 45 F · Trigger (Tue/Thu) ~1,750 / 145 P · Rest (Sat/Sun) ~1,500 / 140 P. *(Estimates — validate vs. 2 weeks of weigh-ins.)*
- **Protein 145–150 g (~2.4 g/kg)** — protective high end; appropriate, not excessive.
- **Scale is a lagging metric this phase.** Lead with mid-thigh (up = muscle), waist/hip (down = fat), photos, and strength PRs.

**Evidence base (verified):** heavy resistance + modest deficit + high protein reliably drives recomposition in trained women (2025 review; RT-only groups gained fat-free mass while losing fat). Cortisol/poor sleep/large deficits increase water retention that can **mask** ongoing fat loss (the real mechanism behind her bounce). Diet breaks improve fat-loss efficiency and blunt metabolic adaptation (MATADOR). The "whoosh effect" is an **anecdotal** label for the scale catching up to fat loss — judge on 2–4 week trends, not a guaranteed mechanism.

# CLIENT 2 — JOSH

**Snapshot:** ~247 lb, experienced lifter, strong responder to heavy compound work. Actor working toward a broad-shouldered, defined "Bishop" physique with a trimmer midsection. Trains 5×/week (Mon–Fri). **Goal: 225–235 lb, reduce body fat 2–3%, cut visceral/midsection fat, retain/build muscle.**

**Life constraints (hard inputs):** unpredictable on-set filming schedule (a design input, not an excuse — needs portable nutrition + flexible timing). Gut health/inflammation issues that affect midsection appearance independent of body fat.

**⚠️ NEW & CENTRAL — Josh is on a GLP-1:**
- **Oral Wegovy (semaglutide tablet), 1.5 mg daily, started ~June 25, 2026.** Titration: **1.5 → 4 → 9 → 25 mg, ~one month per step (~4 months to full dose).**
- **1.5 mg is the lowest, titration-ONLY starting dose** — gentle by design, minimal appetite/weight effect early. **Meaningful appetite suppression/weight loss begins ~9 mg.** So right now is a **gentle onboarding window — train and eat close to normal and build habits;** the muscle-defense and appetite-management posture scales UP at 9 mg and 25 mg.
- **Daily pill dosing rule:** taken **every morning on an empty stomach with a sip of water, then wait 30+ minutes** before any food, drink, coffee, meds, creatine, or protein shake (mistiming cuts absorption ~40%). This reshapes his morning and supplement timing.
- **Autoregulation is tied to DOSE ESCALATIONS, not a weekly injection** (it's a daily pill — no acute weekly side-effect window). Take each dose step-up week as a self-deload (cap RPE ~7–8, −1 set/primary), hardest at the 9 mg and 25 mg jumps.

**Current training approach:** clean **Mon/Wed/Fri heavy foundationals** (squat-led / push-pull-led / hinge-led) built on **mechanical tension** (compound anchors ≥75% 1RM, 4–8 reps) + tight hypertrophy back-off sets; **Tue/Thu triggers** (a moderate-load "Pump & Preserve" hypertrophy day; a low-impact Zone-2 + carries day). The old high-volume metabolic conditioning (EMOM/AMRAP/barbell complexes) was **removed** — once the dose creates a real deficit, that junk volume adds fatigue with no anabolic payoff. Core ≤3×/week (prior 5×/week ab overuse was corrected).

**Current nutrition approach (muscle-preservation on GLP-1):**
- **Eat to FLOORS, not a manual deficit** — as the dose climbs, appetite suppression creates the deficit; don't stack a manual one on top. **Protein floor 180–200 g/day (~1.6 g/kg) is the #1 daily metric.**
- Macros: Foundational (Mon/Wed/Fri) ~2,000 / 190–200 P · Trigger (Tue/Thu) ~1,900 / 185 P · Rest ~1,800 / 180 P (floors).
- Appetite tactics (protein-first, smaller frequent meals, shakes as a primary tool, lower-fat on rough days), fiber 30–40 g + probiotic, hydration 100+ oz + electrolytes — all **ramp up as he reaches 9 mg / 25 mg.** Creatine 5 g/day (outside the 30-min pill window).

**Evidence base (verified):** on semaglutide, **~25–40% of total weight lost is lean mass unless countered** (vs ~15–20% with diet+exercise alone). The two interventions that blunt it: **resistance training** (a 2024 RCT: trainers preserved ~60% more lean mass; some gain lean mass) and **adequate protein** (>1.2 g/kg preserves the large majority of muscle). Win condition = **bodyweight down while waist drops faster and primary-lift strength holds** = the loss is fat, not muscle.
