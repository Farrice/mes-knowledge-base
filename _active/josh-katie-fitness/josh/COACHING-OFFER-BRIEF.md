## How Carbon Torch Coaching Works

### Step 1: Diagnosis Before Prescription (Intake + Assessment)
We start with a detailed questionnaire that captures:

---

### Step 2: Individualized Program Design (Training + Nutrition)

**Training:** Your program is built on these principles:
- **Mechanical tension anchors** (heavy compound lifts) that drive muscle growth *and* defend muscle during fat loss
- **3 foundational days** (full-body) that hit every movement pattern — so a chaos week that kills a session still covers everything
- **2 trigger days** (targeted volume) on the exercises that move the needle for YOUR physique goal
- **Autoregulation** — built-in adjustments for medication escalations, travel weeks, or when you can't recover
- **No junk volume** — retired the metabolic conditioning that just adds fatigue without results

**Why it works:** Research shows trained lifters preserve ~60% more lean mass when they're on GLP-1 and lifting heavy + eating adequate protein. We stack that evidence into the program.

**Nutrition:** Your plan is built on floors, not a manual deficit.
- **Protein floor** (180–200 g/day for you) = the #1 daily metric, non-negotiable
- **Calorie strategy** that scales with your medication dose — as appetite suppression grows, the deficit creates itself; you don't stack manual cuts on top
- **Timing rules** (like your Wegovy pill window) that fit your life
- **On-set eating** strategies for when meal prep isn't possible

**Why it works:** When appetite suppression is doing 50% of the work, your job is to hit your protein floor and not starve. We make that simple.

---

### Step 3: Ongoing Coaching (Adjustments + Accountability)

Every week:
- You log your session data (weights, RPE, any notes) and how you felt
- We track bodyweight, waist circumference, and photos — the metrics that actually matter
- We read the signal: weight down + strength holding = recomposition working; weight down + strength falling = muscle loss flag (we adjust)

When things change (new medication dose, filming on location, unexpected plateau):
- We autoregulate the program — you apply a built-in rule and don't wait for permission
- Or we adjust: cut a trigger if recovery dips, protect foundationals, scale calories based on real data

No guessing. No generic "8-week transformations." Just evidence + your data.
