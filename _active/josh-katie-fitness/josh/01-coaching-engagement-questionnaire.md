# Josh — Coaching Engagement Questionnaire
**Purpose:** Diagnose readiness, constraints, and the real pattern standing between you and your goals.  
**Time:** 10–15 min to answer honestly. This is for us to build something real, not fit you into something generic.

---

## ABOUT YOU & YOUR GOAL

**1. Let's start simple — what's the actual outcome you want in the next 90 days?**
  
(Not "lose weight" or "get stronger." The specific, visible win. Example: *"Walk on set looking like the Bishop character instead of worrying about my midsection when the camera cuts low."*)

---

## THE LIFE PART (the part that actually matters)

**2. Your acting schedule is unpredictable — on-set filming, auditions, travel. Walk me through a typical "chaos week" from your perspective.**

(What does Wednesday look like? When do you sleep? How do you eat? Where's the friction? The real constraint is never "I don't have time to train" — it's always something deeper, like "I never know when I'll be on set until 48 hours before" or "I can't meal-prep because I'm sleeping in a trailer." Name the actual texture.)

---

## WHAT'S ACTUALLY BLOCKING YOU

**3. You've trained seriously before. You know how to lift. So what's the real reason your physique isn't where you want it to be right now?**

(This is the diagnosis question. Is it nutrition discipline? Is it that you prioritize audition prep over training? Is it that you don't actually believe you can look like that while acting? Is it that you've tried six programs and nothing stuck? Get specific — this is where the work starts.)

---

## THE GLP-1 REALITY (if applicable)

**4. You're on Wegovy (semaglutide). How's that actually going? What's the hardest part so far?**

(Side effects? Appetite gone and you're skipping workouts because you don't feel like eating? Confused about how to time it with your supplements? This shapes everything about the coaching structure.)

---

## PAST PATTERNS

**5. What's the graveyard look like? (What programs, diets, or coaching have you tried and abandoned?)**

(And be honest — why did they fail? Was it the program? Was it you? Was it both? I'm not here to sell you a better version of the thing that already didn't work.)

---

## THE COMMITMENT QUESTION

**6. If I built a coaching structure that worked with your acting chaos — not against it — and you could see real progress in 60 days, what would make you actually *do it* instead of saying you would?**

(Is it accountability? Is it someone who gets the acting world? Is it proof that it works before you pay? Is it a specific price point? Name the real condition. This isn't judgment — it's clarity. Coaching only works when you're being honest about what you need to show up.)

---

## CLOSE

**7. What would you want to tell me that I haven't asked?**

(The thing you think matters but I didn't ask about. The real fear. The doubt. The "yeah but what about..." Go.)
