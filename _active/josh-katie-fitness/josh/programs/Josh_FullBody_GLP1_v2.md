4. Early training is fine at 1.5 mg — lift inside the window if needed (water only), eat right after.

## FOUNDATIONAL 1 — MONDAY (Squat-led)
*Movement prep (5 min): ankle rocks, deep-squat holds, leg swings, dead bug x8/side.*

1. Barbell Back Squat [Quads, Glutes] — 4 x 4-6 @ ≥75% 1RM · Rest 3 min · Tempo 2-1-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: big brace, hit depth, no grinding — the anchor preserves muscle; it doesn't chase fatigue.
2. DB Bench Press [Chest, Front Delts, Triceps] — 3 x 8-10 · Rest 90 s · Tempo 2-1-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: full stretch at the bottom.
3. [SS-A1] Bulgarian Split Squat [Quads, Glutes] — 2 x 8-10/leg · Rest 0 → A2 · RPE 8 — Cue: torso forward slightly, control down.
4. [SS-A2] Single-Arm DB Row [Lats, Upper Back] — 2 x 10-12/arm · Rest 90 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: pull to hip, squeeze the lat.
5. [SS-B1] DB Lateral Raise [Side Delts] — 3 x 12-15 · Rest 0 → B2 · RPE 9 — Cue: lead with elbows — Bishop silhouette work, treat it like a main lift.
6. [SS-B2] DB Hammer Curl [Biceps, Forearms] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 60 s after pair · RPE 8.
7. [CIRCUIT] Core Block (direct core day 1 of 2-3/wk) — 2 rounds, 30 s rest:
   - Ab Wheel Rollout (knees) [Abs, Deep Core] — x 8-10 — Cue: hips tucked; stop the roll where the low back stays flat.
   - Band Pallof Press [Obliques] — x 10/side — Cue: ribs stacked, resist rotation.
   - AUTOREG: any returning ab soreness → drop this block for the week (bracing on squats/deadlifts/carries maintains the midsection; abs are made visible by the deficit, not by more ab volume).

## TRIGGER A — TUESDAY ("Pump & Preserve" · ~40 min)

Volume deliberately concentrated on side delts + upper back — the broad-shoulder half of the Bishop look — because the trim waist comes from the dose-driven deficit, not from extra ab work. 2. [SS-C1] DB Lateral Raise (strict) [Side Delts] — 4 x 12-15 · Rest 0 → C2 · RPE 9 — Cue: 2-s lowering, constant tension.
3. [SS-C2] Incline-Body DB Y-Raise (chest on bench or hinged) [Lower Traps, Upper Back] — 3 x 12-15 · Rest 60 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: thumbs up, reach long.
4. [SS-D1] Band Pull-Apart [Rear Delts, Mid Back] — 3 x 15-20 · Rest 0 → D2 · RPE 8.
5. [SS-D2] Band Face Pull [Rear Delts, Rotator Cuff] — 3 x 15-20 · Rest 45 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: pull to eyebrows, thumbs back — posture money for on-camera presence.
6. [SS-E1] DB Overhead Triceps Extension [Triceps] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 0 → E2 · RPE 8.
7. [SS-E2] DB Incline-Grip Curl (seated on bench) [Biceps] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 60 s after pair · RPE 8.
8. Standing DB Calf Raise (plate edge) [Calves] — 3 x 12-15 · Rest 45 s · RPE 9 — Cue: full stretch at the bottom, pause at top.

## FOUNDATIONAL 2 — WEDNESDAY (Push-Pull-led)
*Movement prep (5 min): scap pull-ups x8, band dislocates, push-up plus x10, thoracic rotations.*

1. Barbell Bench Press [Chest, Front Delts, Triceps] — 4 x 4-6 @ ≥75% 1RM · Rest 3 min · Tempo 2-1-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: leg drive, controlled touch, no bounce.
2. Pull-Up [Lats, Upper Back, Biceps] — 4 x 5-8 · Rest 2.5-3 min · Tempo 2-0-X-1 · RPE 8 — Cue: dead hang to chest-proud; over 8 clean reps every set → add load (DB between ankles).
3. Barbell Overhead Press [Delts, Triceps] — 3 x 6-8 · Rest 2 min · RPE 8 — Cue: glutes tight, ribs down; third weekly delt hit.
4. Barbell Bent-Over Row [Upper Back, Lats] — 3 x 8-10 · Rest 90 s · Tempo 2-0-1-1 · RPE 8 — Cue: hinge held, bar to lower ribs, no heaving.
5. [SS-F1] Hinged DB Rear-Delt Fly [Rear Delts] — 2 x 12-15 · Rest 0 → F2 · RPE 8.
6. [SS-F2] Close-Grip Push-Up (or DB Floor Press) [Triceps, Chest] — 2 x 10-15 · Rest 60 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: elbows tight to ribs.
7. Suitcase Carry [Obliques, Grip] — 2 x 40 yd/side · Rest 45 s · RPE 7-8 — Cue: tall, no lean — midsection work without a single crunch.

## TRIGGER B — THURSDAY (Zone-2 + Carries · ~35 min)
*Lowest-cost session of the week: adds fat-loss expenditure and work capacity with near-zero recovery draw, and it's gut-friendly (steady-state, no impact, no intra-abdominal pounding). Skip-without-guilt on set days — the walk alone still counts.*

1. Zone-2 Walk [Conditioning] — 25-30 min brisk walk, nasal-breathing pace (full sentences possible; Gear 1-2) — Cue: if nasal breathing breaks, slow down. This is fat-burn and recovery, not a workout to survive.
2. Farmer Carry [Grip, Traps, Core] — 4 x 40 yd, heavy DBs · Rest 75 s · RPE 8 — Cue: tall posture, crush grip, quick feet. AUTOREG: if grip feels torched by round 3, stop there — tomorrow's deadlift anchor outranks today's trigger.
3. [SS-G1] Banded Standing Kickback [Glutes] — 2 x 12/leg · Rest 0 → G2 · RPE 7 — Cue: blood flow for Friday's hinge, not fatigue.
4. [SS-G2] Band Pull-Apart [Rear Delts, Posture] — 2 x 15-20 · Rest 45 s after pair · RPE 7.
5. Optional mobility (5 min): couch stretch, hamstring sweeps, deep-squat hold — sneak it in; costs nothing.

3. DB Floor Press [Chest, Triceps] — 3 x 8-10 · Rest 90 s · RPE 8 — Cue: shoulder-friendly pressing volume; pause briefly at the floor.
4. Chin-Up (underhand) [Lats, Biceps] — 3 x 6-10 · Rest 2 min · RPE 8 — Cue: band-assist if needed; different grip than Wednesday.
5. [SS-H1] Stability Ball Hamstring Curl [Hams] — 2 x 12-15 · Rest 0 → H2 · RPE 9 — Cue: hips bridged throughout.
6. [SS-H2] DB Shrug [Traps] — 2 x 12-15 · Rest 60 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: straight up, 1-s hold — yoke work for the silhouette.
7. [CIRCUIT] Core Block (direct core day 2 of 2-3/wk) — 2 rounds, 30 s rest:

## PROGRESSION — 8-WEEK TITRATION-PROOF WAVE
| Week | Anchors (squat / bench / deadlift / pull-up) | Back-offs & Triggers | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 x 4-6 — CALIBRATE at RPE 7-8 | As written, RIR 2 | RPE 7-8 |
| 2 | +5 lb upper / +10 lb lower if all top-reps hit | As written | RPE 8 |
| 3 | Same scheme, heavier | +1 set on lateral raises & Y-raise (priority ramp) | RPE 8 |
| 4* | **AUTOREG: DOSE-STEP WEEK (see below)** | Triggers optional | RPE ≤7-8, −1 set/anchor |
| 5 | Resume week-3 loads (not week-3 + progression) | Week-3 volume | RPE 8 |
| 6 | Progress again if reps hold | Hold | RPE 8 |
| 7 | Heaviest clean sets of the block | Hold, RIR 1-2 | RPE 8-9 |
| 8 | Reassess: if the 9 mg step is near, this is the next AUTOREG week + block review | Taper | RPE 7 |

*Week 4 is the *planned* slot for the 4 mg step (~July 25). **The rule follows the pill, not the calendar.**

**AUTOREG: DOSE-STEP WEEK** — whichever week Josh takes his first dose at a new level (4 mg now; 9 and 25 mg are the big ones later): cap every lift at RPE 7-8, drop 1 set from each anchor, triggers optional, food = floors + appetite tactics. This is a built-in deload timed to the adjustment window — do not push through it, and do not bank it for later.

**AUTOREG: SIDE-EFFECT SCORE** (0-3 in tracker) — score 2: swap the day's foundational for the on-set fallback + walk. Score 3: rest day, hydrate + electrolytes, log it; call the coach if it persists 3+ days (and anything severe/unusual → prescriber, not program).

**AUTOREG: MISSED-SESSION RULE** — a missed foundational rolls the week forward (Mon→Tue etc.), triggers get dropped first. Never stack two heavy days back-to-back to "catch up."

**Load autoregulation (every lift):** all sets at/above the top of the range → +5 lb upper / +10 lb lower next session. Two+ reps below the bottom → −10% and rebuild. In a growing deficit, HOLDING load is winning — the strength index defends the muscle.

## TRACKING LOOP (weekly)
Log: bodyweight (daily, read weekly average) · waist (weekly, same conditions) · strength index per anchor · current dose · side-effect score (0-3) · steps · sleep.
**Signal logic:**
- Weight ↓ + waist ↓ faster + strength holding → **fat loss, muscle defended. Win condition. Change nothing.**
- Weight ↓ + strength index falling 2 weeks running → **muscle-loss flag:** raise protein to the top of the floor + add ~150-200 kcal, cut Trigger A, audit sleep — BEFORE touching the training.
- Weight flat at this dose → expected at 1.5 mg; do not force a deficit — the dose escalation is the deficit engine.
