## SESSION 1 — MONDAY · Foundational (Squat + Chest) · ~80 min
*Prep (5 min): ankle rocks · deep-squat holds · leg swings · band dislocates · dead bug x8/side. Ramp before squats: 12 light → 8 medium → 3 near-working.*

1. Barbell Back Squat [Quads, Glutes] — 4 x 4-6 @ ≥75% 1RM · Rest 3 min · Tempo 2-1-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: big brace, hit depth, no grinding.
2. DB Romanian Deadlift [Hams, Glutes] — 3 x 8-10 · Rest 2 min · Tempo 3-1-1-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: hips back, bar close, stop at the hamstring stretch.
3. Barbell/DB Bench Press [Chest, Front Delts, Triceps] — 3 x 8-10 · Rest 90 s · Tempo 2-1-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: full stretch at the bottom.
4. Incline DB Press (~30°) [Upper Chest, Front Delts] — 3 x 8-10 · Rest 90 s · Tempo 2-1-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: drive up and slightly together — the upper-chest shelf.
5. [SS-A1] Bulgarian Split Squat [Quads, Glutes] — 2 x 8-10/leg · Rest 0 → A2 · RPE 8 — Cue: torso slightly forward, control down.
6. [SS-A2] Single-Arm DB Row [Lats, Upper Back] — 2 x 10-12/arm · Rest 90 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: pull to hip, squeeze the lat.
7. [SS-B1] DB Lateral Raise [Side Delts] — 3 x 12-15 · Rest 0 → B2 · RPE 9 — Cue: lead with elbows — treat like a main lift.
8. [SS-B2] DB Hammer Curl [Biceps, Forearms] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 60 s after pair · RPE 8.
9. [CIRCUIT] Core — 2 rounds, 30 s rest: Ab Wheel Rollout (knees) [Abs] x 8-10 · Band Pallof Press [Obliques] x 10/side. *(AUTOREG: ab soreness returns → drop this block for the week.)*

## SESSION 2 — TUESDAY · Trigger A "Pump & Preserve" · ~45 min

2. [SS-C1] DB Lateral Raise (strict) [Side Delts] — 4 x 12-15 · Rest 0 → C2 · RPE 9 — Cue: 2-s lowering, constant tension.
3. [SS-C2] Incline-Body DB Y-Raise [Lower Traps, Upper Back] — 3 x 12-15 · Rest 60 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: thumbs up, reach long.
4. Leaning DB Lateral Raise (hold rack, lean away) [Side Delts] — 3 x 12-15 · Rest 60 s · Tempo 2-0-1-1 · RPE 9 — Cue: leaning opens the bottom of the range for extra width.
5. [SS-D1] Band Pull-Apart [Rear Delts, Mid Back] — 3 x 15-20 · Rest 0 → D2 · RPE 8.
6. [SS-D2] Band Face Pull [Rear Delts, Rotator Cuff] — 3 x 15-20 · Rest 45 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: pull to eyebrows, thumbs back.
7. [SS-E1] DB Overhead Triceps Extension [Triceps] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 0 → E2 · RPE 8.
8. [SS-E2] DB Incline-Grip Curl [Biceps] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 60 s after pair · RPE 8.
9. Standing DB Calf Raise (plate edge) [Calves] — 3 x 12-15 · Rest 45 s · RPE 9 — Cue: full stretch bottom, pause top.

## SESSION 3 — WEDNESDAY · Foundational (Push-Pull · V-TAPER DAY) · ~80 min
*The width day. Back volume is deliberately concentrated — this is where the V is built. Prep (5 min): scap pull-ups x8 · band dislocates · push-up plus x10 · thoracic rotations. Ramp before bench.*

1. Barbell Bench Press [Chest, Front Delts, Triceps] — 4 x 4-6 @ ≥75% 1RM · Rest 3 min · Tempo 2-1-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: leg drive, controlled touch, no bounce.
2. Pull-Up (wide-ish grip) [Lats, Upper Back, Biceps] — 4 x 5-8 · Rest 2.5-3 min · Tempo 2-0-X-1 · RPE 8 — Cue: dead hang to chest-proud; over 8 clean reps → add load.
3. Barbell Overhead Press [Delts, Triceps] — 3 x 6-8 · Rest 2 min · RPE 8 — Cue: glutes tight, ribs down.
4. Chest-Supported DB Row (incline bench, chest down) [Mid Back, Lats] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 90 s · Tempo 2-1-1-1 · RPE 8 — Cue: chest glued, drive elbows back — thickness with zero low-back fatigue. *(No incline bench? sub strict Pendlay Row.)*
5. DB Pullover (bench or stability ball) [Lats, Serratus, Chest] — 3 x 12-15 · Rest 75 s · Tempo 3-1-1-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: big overhead stretch, pull with the lats — flares the lats, cinches the waist visually.
6. [SS-F1] Hinged DB Rear-Delt Fly [Rear Delts] — 2 x 12-15 · Rest 0 → F2 · RPE 8.
7. [SS-F2] Close-Grip Push-Up (or DB Floor Press) [Triceps, Chest] — 2 x 10-15 · Rest 60 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: elbows tight to ribs.
8. Suitcase Carry [Obliques, Grip] — 2 x 40 yd/side · Rest 45 s · RPE 7-8 — Cue: tall, no lean — midsection work, no crunch.

## SESSION 4 — THURSDAY · Trigger B (Zone-2 + Carries) · ~35 min
*Lowest-cost day: fat-loss expenditure + work capacity at near-zero recovery draw, gut-friendly. Deliberately light — this is the recovery valve, protect it. Skip-without-guilt on set days; the walk alone counts.*

4. [SS-G2] Band Pull-Apart [Rear Delts, Posture] — 2 x 15-20 · Rest 45 s after pair · RPE 7.
5. Optional mobility (5 min): couch stretch · hamstring sweeps · deep-squat hold.

## SESSION 5 — FRIDAY · Foundational (Hinge + Shoulders/Arms) · ~80 min
*Prep (5 min): hamstring sweeps · glute bridge x10 · cat-cow · scap pull-ups · light single-DB swings x10. Ramp before deadlifts.*

1. Barbell Deadlift [Posterior Chain, Full Body] — 4 x 3-5 @ ≥75-80% 1RM · Rest 3 min · Tempo 1-1-X-0 (reset each rep) · RPE 8 — Cue: brace hard, bar tight to shins, crisp reps only.
2. Barbell Overhead Press [Delts, Triceps, Upper Chest] — 3 x 6-8 · Rest 2 min · Tempo 2-0-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: press the head "through" at the top — capped delts = the widest point.
3. Barbell Hip Thrust [Glutes, Hams] — 3 x 8-10 · Rest 2 min · Tempo 1-2-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: 2-s top squeeze.
4. DB Floor Press [Chest, Triceps] — 3 x 8-10 · Rest 90 s · RPE 8 — Cue: shoulder-friendly, pause at the floor.
5. Chin-Up (underhand) [Lats, Biceps] — 3 x 6-10 · Rest 2 min · RPE 8 — Cue: band-assist if needed.
6. [SS-H1] Barbell Curl [Biceps] — 3 x 8-10 · Rest 0 → H2 · Tempo 2-0-1-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: elbows pinned, no swing, full stretch.
7. [SS-H2] DB Shrug [Traps] — 2 x 12-15 · Rest 60 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: straight up, 1-s hold.
8. Stability Ball Hamstring Curl [Hams] — 2 x 12-15 · Rest 60 s · RPE 9 — Cue: hips bridged throughout.
9. [CIRCUIT] Core — 2 rounds, 30 s rest: Hanging Knee Raise [Lower Abs] x 8-12 · Side Plank [Obliques] x 20-30 s/side. *(AUTOREG: ab soreness → drop for the week.)*

## GLOBAL TRAINING RULES
- **Load autoreg:** all sets at top of range → +5 lb upper / +10 lb lower next session; 2+ reps under the bottom → −10%. In a growing deficit, HOLDING load is winning.
- **AUTOREG — dose-step week:** first week at any new dose (4 mg now; 9/25 mg later) → cap all lifts RPE 7-8, drop 1 set per anchor, cut the v2.1 accessory adds, triggers optional. Built-in deload — don't push through, don't bank it.
- **AUTOREG — side-effect score (0-3):** 2 → swap the foundational for the on-set fallback + walk; 3 → rest day, hydrate + electrolytes, log it. Persists 3+ days or anything severe → prescriber, not program.
- **AUTOREG — missed session:** rolls the week forward (Mon→Tue…); triggers dropped first. Never stack two heavy days to catch up.
- **Equipment:** garage only — barbell, DBs, bench, pull-up bar, bands, ball, BOSU, ab wheel. No cables/machines/landmine/trap bar/kettlebells.

# PART 2 — NUTRITION (GLP-1 Muscle-Preservation)
**Goal:** 225-235 lb, midsection/visceral fat down, **muscle defended.** On oral Wegovy (1.5 mg → titrating up).

**The philosophy: eat to FLOORS, not a manual deficit.** As the dose climbs, appetite suppression creates the deficit — **do not stack a manual one on top.** Protein floor is THE #1 daily metric.

**Day-type floors** *(don't go under these — estimates, validate vs. ~2 weeks of weigh-ins)*

| Day type | Calories (floor) | Protein (floor) |
|---|---|---|
| Foundational (Mon/Wed/Fri) | ~2,000 | 190-200 g |
| Trigger (Tue/Thu) | ~1,900 | 185 g |
| Rest (Sat/Sun) | ~1,800 | 180 g |

## ⭐ Morning pill window — reshapes the whole morning
Pill on waking → empty stomach → ≤4 oz plain water → **wait 30+ min** before ANYTHING (coffee, creatine, protein, food, other meds). Mistiming cuts absorption ~40%. **After the window:** coffee + creatine (5 g) + protein-forward breakfast/shake.

## Priorities (ramp UP as dose reaches 9/25 mg)
- **Protein floor = #1 metric.** Protein-first at every meal; shakes are a primary tool, not a cheat; smaller/more frequent meals and lower-fat on rough-stomach days. As appetite dips near the 4 mg step, **front-load protein earlier in the day** when appetite is highest.
- **Gut/inflammation:** fiber 30-40 g + probiotic daily.
- **Hydration:** 100+ oz water + electrolytes.
- **Creatine 5 g daily** — always outside the 30-min pill window.
- **Portable/on-set kit:** shaker + protein, jerky, fruit, electrolyte packets in the set bag.

## Muscle-defense signal (tracker)
Log weekly: bodyweight (daily → weekly avg) · waist · strength index (load ÷ baseline per anchor) · current dose · side-effect score (0-3).
- Weight ↓ + waist ↓ faster + strength holding = **fat loss, muscle defended. Win — change nothing.**
- Weight ↓ + strength index falling 2 weeks = **muscle-loss flag** → raise protein to top of floor + add ~150-200 kcal, cut a trigger, audit sleep — before touching training.
- Weight flat at low dose = expected; don't force a deficit.
