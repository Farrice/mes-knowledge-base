## SECTION 2: YOUR IDEAL BUYER

### Q3. Who is the ONE person you most want to coach?
Describe in 4–5 details: role/profession, age range, life circumstance, what they're trying to accomplish, what's preventing them from getting it.

*Example: "A 28–42 year old actor who just booked a film role that requires a specific physique in 12 weeks. They're terrified of losing strength while cutting. They have a chaotic on-set schedule and zero nutrition infrastructure."*

---

### Q4. What does your ideal client *think* they need vs. what do they *actually* need?
The gap between perceived problem and real problem is where your positioning lives.

*Example: They think: "I need to eat chicken and broccoli, hit a hard deficit, and train 6×/week." They actually need: "A periodized program that preserves muscle while I'm on medication, meal timing that survives on-set chaos, and a coach who understands performance pressure."*

---

### Q5. What's the biggest objection that stops them from buying coaching?
Fear, doubt, broken trust, past failures, feeling misunderstood — what's the real wall?

*Example: "I've tried 3 coaches and always lose strength when I cut. I don't believe anyone can help me stay strong while dropping fat."*
