# KATIE · FRIDAY — Foundational 3 (Unilateral-Athletic + Overhead) · ~65 min
**Block:** Strong & Lean v2 · Master: [`../programs/Katie_FullBody_Recomp_v2.md`](../programs/Katie_FullBody_Recomp_v2.md)

**Movement prep (5 min):** ankle rocks · wrist/front-rack opener · overhead band dislocates · split-squat pulses.
**Warm-up ramp before front squats:** 12 light → 8 medium → 3 near working weight.

1. Barbell Front Squat [Quads, Upper Back, Core] — 4 x 4-5 · Rest 2.5-3 min · Tempo 3-1-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: elbows high, upright torso; cross-arm grip is fine.
2. Barbell Overhead Press [Delts, Triceps] — 4 x 5-6 · Rest 2.5 min · Tempo 2-0-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: glutes tight, ribs down, bar path close to face.
3. [SS-E1] Walking Lunge (DBs) [Quads, Glutes] — 2 x 10/leg · Rest 0 → E2 · Tempo 2-0-1-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: long stride = glutes, controlled knee tracking.
4. [SS-E2] DB Step-Up (bench) [Glutes, Quads] — 2 x 8-10/leg · Rest 75 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: drive through the top foot, don't push off the floor leg.
5. Banded Hip Thrust [Glutes] — 2 x 15-20 · Rest 45 s · RPE 9 — Cue: high-rep finisher, chase the squeeze.
6. Turkish Get-Up (DB) OR Single-DB Swing [Full Body / Hinge Power] — 3 x 3/side (TGU) or 3 x 12-15 (swing) · Rest 60-75 s · RPE 7 — Cue: TGU = eyes on the bell; Swing = hips snap, arms are ropes; hold one DB by the head.
7. [CIRCUIT] Core Finisher — 2 rounds, 30 s rest:
   - Hanging Knee Raise [Lower Abs] — x 8-12
   - Hollow Hold [Abs] — x 20-30 s

---
**Session rules:** Load autoreg — top of range all sets → +5 lb upper / +10 lb lower; 2+ under bottom → −10%. Week 3: +1 set on Banded Hip Thrust (priority ramp). Week 6 = deload (2×5 @ ~70%, half sets, skip the finisher).
