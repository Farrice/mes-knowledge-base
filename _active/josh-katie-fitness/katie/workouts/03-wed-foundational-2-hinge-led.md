# KATIE · WEDNESDAY — Foundational 2 (Hinge-led) · ~80 min
**Block:** Strong & Lean v2.1 · Master: [`../programs/Katie_FullBody_Recomp_v2.md`](../programs/Katie_FullBody_Recomp_v2.md)
*"NEW v2.1" = added this pass.*

**Movement prep (5 min):** hamstring sweeps · glute bridges 2×10 · scap pull-ups x8 · cat-cow · 90/90 hip rotations.
**Warm-up ramp before hip thrusts:** 12 light → 8 medium → 3 near working weight.

1. Barbell Hip Thrust [Glutes, Hams] — 4 x 5-6 · Rest 2.5 min · Tempo 2-2-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: heavy top-end squeeze; shins vertical at lockout. Spine-sparing heavy hinge primary.
2. PAUSED: Conventional Deadlift [Posterior Chain] — 4 x 3-5 · Rest 3 min · RPE 8 — Toggle on by coach request only; replaces hip thrust as primary when active.
3. Goblet Cossack Squat [Adductors, Glute Med, Quads] — 3 x 8/side · Rest 75 s · Tempo 3-1-1-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: sit deep into one hip, other leg straight, heel down — opens the frontal-plane hip range that squats and hinges never touch. **NEW v2.1: your under-served hip plane — trains inner thigh + lateral glute as one.**
4. Pull-Up [Lats, Upper Back, Biceps] — 4 x 5-8 · Rest 2-2.5 min · Tempo 2-0-X-1 · RPE 8 — Cue: full hang to chest-proud finish; band-assist under 5 clean reps, DB between ankles over 8.
5. DB Romanian Deadlift [Hams, Glutes] — 3 x 8-10 · Rest 90 s · Tempo 3-1-1-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: hips back, soft knees, stop at hamstring stretch — moderate load, pristine hinge.
6. [SS-C1] Stability Ball Hamstring Curl [Hams] — 2 x 12-15 · Rest 0 → C2 · Tempo 2-1-1-0 · RPE 9 — Cue: hips stay bridged the whole set.
7. [SS-C2] Band Face Pull [Rear Delts, Upper Back] — 2 x 15-20 · Rest 60 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: pull to eyebrows, thumbs back.
8. [SS-D1] DB Hammer Curl [Biceps, Forearms] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 0 → D2 · Tempo 2-0-1-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: neutral grip, no swing. **NEW v2.1: arms.**
9. [SS-D2] Bench Dip (feet out, or plate on lap) [Triceps] — 3 x 10-15 · Rest 60 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: elbows straight back, chest tall — don't shrug into the shoulders. **NEW v2.1: arms.**
10. [CIRCUIT] Core Block — 2 rounds, 30 s rest:
    - Ab Wheel Rollout (knees) [Abs, Deep Core] — x 8-10 — Cue: hips tucked, only roll as far as the low back stays flat.
    - Hanging Leg Raise [Lower Abs] — x 8-12 — Cue: no swing, control the lower. **NEW v2.1: lower-ab loader.**
    - Side Plank [Obliques] — x 20-30 s/side — Cue: straight line ear-to-ankle.
11. Suitcase Carry [Obliques, Grip] — 2 x 40 yd/side · Rest 45 s · RPE 7-8 — Cue: don't lean away from the load.

---
**Session rules:** Load autoreg — top of range all sets → +5 lb upper / +10 lb lower; 2+ under bottom → −10%. Any back pain = set over, full stop. **v2.1 adds (Cossack, arm superset, hanging raise) drop first on a low-sleep or high-cortisol week; hip thrust + RDL are protected.** Week 6 = deload (2×5-6 @ ~70%, half sets).
