## SESSION 1 — MONDAY · Foundational (Squat + Quads) · ~80 min
*Prep (5 min): ankle rocks · deep-squat hold 2×20 s · leg swings · band glute activation. Ramp before squats: 12 light → 8 medium → 3 near-working.*

1. Barbell Back Squat [Quads, Glutes] — 4 x 5 · Rest 2.5-3 min · Tempo 3-1-X-1 · RPE 8 — Cue: brace before descent; pristine form is the ticket for heavy — stop on any back pain.
2. Heel-Elevated Goblet Squat (plates under heels) [Quads] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 90 s · Tempo 3-1-1-0 · RPE 8-9 — Cue: upright torso, knees forward, sink deep — pure quad ROM.
3. DB Bench Press [Chest, Front Delts, Triceps] — 3 x 6-8 · Rest 2 min · Tempo 2-1-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: full stretch bottom, elbows ~45°.
4. [SS-A1] Bulgarian Split Squat [Quads, Glutes] — 2 x 10-12/leg · Rest 0 → A2 · Tempo 2-1-1-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: torso slightly forward for glute bias.
5. [SS-A2] Single-Arm DB Row [Lats, Upper Back] — 2 x 10-12/arm · Rest 75 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: pull to hip, no twist.
6. [SS-B1] DB Incline Curl [Biceps] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 0 → B2 · RPE 8 — Cue: elbows behind the body for the full stretch.
7. [SS-B2] DB Overhead Triceps Extension [Triceps] — 3 x 10-12 · Rest 60 s after pair · RPE 8 — Cue: elbows tight, full stretch behind the head.
8. Banded Hip Thrust [Glutes] — 2 x 15-20 · Rest 45 s · Tempo 1-2-X-0 · RPE 9 — Cue: 2-s squeeze at top.
9. [CIRCUIT] Lower-Ab Block — 2 rounds, 30 s rest: Hanging Knee/Leg Raise [Lower Abs] x 8-12 · Reverse Crunch x 12-15 · Dead Bug x 8/side · Hollow Hold x 20-30 s.
10. Farmer Carry [Grip, Core, Traps] — 2 x 40 yd · Rest 60 s · heavy DBs · RPE 7-8.

## GLOBAL TRAINING RULES
- **Load autoreg:** all sets at top of range → +5 lb upper / +10 lb lower next session; 2+ reps under the bottom → −10%.
- **AUTOREG — performance week:** violin performance/travel → foundationals only (Mon/Wed/Fri), cap RPE 7-8, triggers become optional walks, drop the arm supersets. Performance quality outranks the program. No make-up sessions.
- **AUTOREG — cycle:** late-luteal week → expect +1-2 lb scale water (not stalling); optional RPE cap 8; poor sleep → swap Trigger B circuits for the Zone-2 walk alone.
- **AUTOREG — sleep:** under ~6 h two nights → cap primaries RPE 7, cut pump blocks one round. The v2.1 accessory adds are the FIRST cut; barbell primaries are protected.
- **Read the scale as lagging:** judge by mid-thigh (up = muscle), waist/hip (down = fat), photos, strength PRs.
- **Equipment:** garage only — barbell, DBs, bench, pull-up bar, bands, ball, BOSU, ab wheel. No cables/machines/landmine/trap bar/kettlebells.

# PART 2 — NUTRITION (Maintenance-Recomp)
**Goal:** recomposition (fat down, muscle up) → "strong & lean" ~129-130. **Scale is a lagging metric** — lead with mid-thigh (up = muscle), waist/hip (down = fat), photos, strength PRs.

**The philosophy:** modest deficit, close to maintenance (~200-500 kcal/day, ~10-15%), with **most of the deficit from NEAT (11-13k steps/day) + the two trigger days — not from cutting food hard.** She's a strength adaptor; heavy training + high protein + a gentle deficit is the recomposition engine.

**Day-type targets** *(estimates — validate vs. ~2 weeks of weigh-in trend)*

| Day type | Calories | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Foundational (Mon/Wed/Fri) | ~1,850 | 145-150 g | ~200 g | ~45 g |
| Trigger (Tue/Thu) | ~1,750 | 145 g | scale to fill | ~45 g |
| Rest (Sat/Sun) | ~1,500 | 140 g | scale to fill | ~45 g |

- **Protein 145-150 g (~2.4 g/kg)** — protective high end. Anchor every meal with it.
- **Fat ~45 g/day spread thin across meals** (~10-15 g/meal), adjusted to tolerance — see gallbladder protocol.

## ⭐ Gallbladder protocol — her #1 execution lever
No gallbladder → impaired fat digestion + reduced fat-soluble vitamin (A, D, E, K) absorption. **Ox bile + digestive enzymes with every fat-containing meal** (~125-500 mg bile salts, scaled to the meal's fat). Keep fat ~10-15 g/meal, spread across the day. **Log adherence daily** — this is the chronically under-executed piece, and fat malabsorption bloat masks progress on both scale and waist. *(Coordinate dose with her doctor.)*

## Hormone-supportive nutrition (progesterone finding)
*Full evidence-based detail + doctor questions live in her separate private brief. Nutrition levers only, here:*
- **Don't under-fuel.** A too-aggressive deficit is the biggest nutritional risk to her cycle/progesterone. Keep the deficit modest; consider eating **at/near maintenance across the luteal week** (metabolism runs 50-300 kcal hotter then). The diet-break structure already protects this.
- **Absorb your fat-soluble vitamins** — the gallbladder protocol above is doubly important now; it's how she gets vitamin D (relevant to hormone health). **Ask her doctor to check vitamin D.**
- **Food-first micronutrients:** vitamin C (citrus, peppers, berries), magnesium (pumpkin seeds, leafy greens — glycinate at night doubles as a sleep aid), B6 (poultry, fish, chickpeas, bananas), vitamin D (fatty fish, eggs — absorbed with the ox bile + some fat).
- **Protect sleep + manage cortisol** — the strongest non-food levers; already built into her lower-density training.
- *Support the terrain, don't chase the number with food. Clinical decisions stay with her doctors.*
