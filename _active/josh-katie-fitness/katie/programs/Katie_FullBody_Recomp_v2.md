## FOUNDATIONAL 1 — MONDAY (Squat-led)
*Movement prep (5 min): ankle rocks, deep-squat hold 2×20 s, leg swings, band glute activation.*

1. Barbell Back Squat [Quads, Glutes] — 4 x 5 · Rest 2.5–3 min · Tempo 3-1-X-1 · RPE 8 — Cue: brace before descent; pristine form is the entry ticket for heavy — stop on any back pain, zero grinding reps.
2. Dumbbell Bench Press [Chest, Front Delts, Triceps] — 3 x 6-8 · Rest 2 min · Tempo 2-1-X-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: full stretch at the bottom, elbows ~45°.
3. [SS-A1] Bulgarian Split Squat [Quads, Glutes] — 2 x 10-12/leg · Rest 0 → A2 · Tempo 2-1-1-0 · RPE 8 — Cue: front-foot pressure through midfoot; torso slightly forward for glute bias.
4. [SS-A2] Single-Arm DB Row [Lats, Upper Back] — 2 x 10-12/arm · Rest 75 s after pair · Tempo 2-0-1-1 · RPE 8 — Cue: pull to hip, no torso twist.
5. Banded Hip Thrust [Glutes] — 2 x 15-20 · Rest 45 s · Tempo 1-2-X-0 · RPE 9 — Cue: 2-second squeeze at top; chin tucked.
6. [CIRCUIT] Lower-Ab Block — 2 rounds, 30 s rest between rounds:
   - Reverse Crunch [Lower Abs] — x 12-15 — Cue: posterior pelvic tilt starts the rep.
   - Dead Bug [Deep Core] — x 8/side — Cue: ribs down, low back glued to mat.
   - Hollow Hold [Abs] — x 20-30 s — Cue: lower back pressed into floor.
7. Farmer Carry [Grip, Core, Traps] — 2 x 40 yd · Rest 60 s · heavy DBs · RPE 7-8 — Cue: tall posture, quick steps.

## PROGRESSION — 6-WEEK WAVE
| Week | Heavy Primaries (squat / hip thrust / front squat / OHP / pull-up / DB bench) | Accessories & Pump | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 x 5 (4 x 5-6 hip thrust / OHP) — CALIBRATE loads | As written, RIR 2 | RPE 7-8 |
| 2 | Same sets, +5 lb upper / +10 lb lower if all top-reps hit | As written, RIR 2 | RPE 8 |
| 3 | 5 x 4 (add a set, drop a rep) | +1 set on Banded Hip Thrust & Heel-Elevated Goblet (priority ramp) | RPE 8 |
| 4 | 5 x 3-4, heavier | Hold week-3 volume, RIR 1-2 | RPE 8-9 |
| 5 | 4 x 3 heaviest clean triples of the block | Hold, RIR 1 | RPE 9 |
| 6 | **DELOAD:** 2 x 5 @ ~70% of week-5 load | Half sets everywhere; triggers = walks only | RPE ≤6 |

**Load autoregulation (every lift, every week):** all sets at/above the top of the rep range → +5 lb upper / +10 lb lower next session. Two or more reps below the bottom of the range → −10% and rebuild.

**AUTOREG: PERFORMANCE WEEK** — violin performance/travel week: run foundationals only (Mon/Wed/Fri), cap RPE at 7-8, triggers become optional walks. Performance quality outranks the program. No make-up sessions.

**AUTOREG: CYCLE** — late-luteal week (track in the tracker): expect +1-2 lb scale water — do not read it as stalling; optional RPE cap 8; swap Trigger B circuits for the Zone-2 walk alone if sleep was poor.

**AUTOREG: SLEEP** — under ~6 h two nights running: cap primaries at RPE 7, cut pump blocks by one round. Recovery is the growth phase, not the workout (the session is only the signal).

## WHAT CHANGED vs v1 — AND WHY (evidence-labeled)
1. **Deficit resumes post-break.** The break did its metabolic/psychological work; extending it stops fat loss without further benefit. [Diet breaks improving fat-loss efficiency: **VERIFIED** — MATADOR-style intermittent restriction research.]
2. **Pump volume returns, but at controlled density** (60-90 s rests, band/metabolite bias — NOT the old 30-s Phase III grind). Her plateau signature was cortisol/water masking recomp; density was the stressor, so the pump comes back without the grind. [Cortisol/poor sleep/aggressive deficits driving water retention that masks fat loss: **LIKELY** — mechanistically supported, widely observed clinically.]
3. **Heavy low-rep primaries stay the spine** — she's a strength adaptor; heavy tension + high protein in a modest deficit is the recomposition engine. [Heavy RT + high protein recomp in trained women: **VERIFIED** per the 2025 review carried in the master brief.]
4. **Volume is concentrated, not spread**: quads/glutes/hams ~14-16 weekly sets (priority), upper body held at maintenance-plus (~8-10). Adding everywhere would blow the recovery budget that her cortisol load already taxes.
5. **Hip thrust stays the heavy hinge primary; deadlift ships `PAUSED`** — spine-sparing preference given back history (fully resolved; this is a comfort/margin call, not a clinical restriction).
6. **Cycle, performance-week, and sleep autoregulation formalized** as written rules so the program bends before she breaks it. Life-design is a hard constraint, not a footnote.
7. **Gallbladder protocol promoted to a tracked daily metric** — the cheapest unexploited lever in the whole system. [Ox bile + enzymes post-cholecystectomy: physiologically sound and OTC-safe — **LIKELY**; direct RCT evidence is thin, so it's dosed to comfort/tolerance.]
