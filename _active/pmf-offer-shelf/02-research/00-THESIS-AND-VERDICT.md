## The thesis (C1 — won unanimously, 8.17/10)

**The Claim-Safe Citation Audit → Trust-Layer Sprint → Retainer ladder**, sold to funded sports-nutrition/supplement brands under $10M. The mechanism is an empty square: compliance vendors ($350–2,000/SKU) produce no content, and AEO/content agencies ($5K–25K/mo) are compliance-blind. Nobody grades a brand's claims on both gates that now matter at once — FTC substantiation risk and AI-citation-worthiness. One classification pass (the `claim-safe-health-marketing` skill) does both.

**Ladder:** $500 Citation Audit (3 slots/mo) → Trust-Layer Install sprint $2,000–2,500 pilot / $3,500 std → retainer $2,500–3,500/mo pilot → $4,000–6,000/mo std, scaling to $8K+ only after a shipped case study.

**This is Path A sharpened, not a new offer.** All three judges ruled it path_a_compatibility 10/10. The Incumbency Rule is satisfied, not tested.

## Why now (the receipts)

- 2026 is the FTC's most aggressive health-claims year: Amare Global (June 17), TruHeight (June 12), Golden Sunrise (Jan). FTC–FDA MOU pulls influencer content into scope.
- Creatine-gummy scandal: 4 of 6 popular gummies tested near-zero actual creatine while holding 4.4★+ ratings. A nameable, current fear.
- AEO is verified spend: 65% of enterprises committing 25%+ of 2026 budgets to AI-search optimization (Branch, n=300); Sitecore bought Scrunch for ~$225M in June 2026. Only ~25% use outside vendors.
- Funded brands already self-hedge copy (AG1, Jocko, LMNT) and already buy outside content (EHPlabs sponsored UGC), while brand-owned accounts get 86–350 views vs. creators' 500K–2.5M.
- Solo-model proof: Hickman ($7.5–15K fixed-scope, 3–6 clients/mo, $25–35K/mo), Designjoy ($3.1M/yr solo).
