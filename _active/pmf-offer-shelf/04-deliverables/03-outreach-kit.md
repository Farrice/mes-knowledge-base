# Outreach Kit: Contact-Bottleneck Killer

Every dollar Farrice has ever collected came from a warm 1:1 relationship. Zero came from cold content or a funnel. The C1 ladder doesn't fail on delivery (a fulfillment template already exists) or on market pain (2026 is the FTC's most aggressive supplement-enforcement year on record). It fails, if it fails, on contact. This kit exists to put a stranger's name in front of Farrice and Farrice's name in front of a stranger, fast, using proof instead of a pitch.

Scope line for every artifact and message below: **marketing-side claim hygiene, not legal advice.** Never "compliance review," never "audit" in the regulatory sense. The classifier lives at `skills/claim-safe-health-marketing/`; it flags high-risk claims for the brand's own counsel, it doesn't clear them.

---

## 1. The Teardown Door-Opener SOP (60 minutes, one take)

The artifact is a 3-5 minute screen recording of what ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overview say about a named brand's money queries, held up next to one flagged claim line from that brand's own copy. No editing beyond a trim. No paid tools.

| Minutes | Step | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | Pick target + queries | Pull one target from the list below. Write 3 "money queries" a buyer would actually type: category ("best creatine gummies"), trust-check ("is [Brand] legit"), comparison ("[Brand] vs [competitor]"). |
| 3-18 | Record the AI answers | Start screen recording. Run all 3 queries through ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overview in the free web UI. Note whether the brand is even cited, and what claim the AI attributes to them if it is. |
| 18-33 | Pull one flagged claim | Open the brand's own site, IG, or TikTok. Find 2-3 live claim lines. Sort each against the 5-bucket risk taxonomy (disease-express, disease-implied, structure-function, qualified, puffery), the same lens `/claim-audit` runs. Mark the single highest-risk line. |
| 33-48 | Record the narration | Voice over the same recording, unscripted. Show the AI answer. Say the brand's name. Hold up the flagged claim line next to it. End on one sentence of what a tighter version would sound like. Don't rewrite it live, just point at the gap. |
| 48-58 | Trim and export | Cut dead air at the start and end only. Nothing else. Upload to an unlisted link. |
| 58-60 | Log it | Paste the link into the tracker row for that brand. Mark artifact ready. |

If minute 60 hits before the recording's done, ship the rougher cut. A finished 4-minute video beats a polished one that never leaves the queue.

## 2. Target List

**Selection criteria** (a brand needs all five before it goes on the list):

1. Visibly funded or established-bootstrap sports-nutrition/supplement brand, not a first-week startup.
2. Revenue estimate under $10M: check in 2 minutes via a funding line, LinkedIn team size, or a traffic-estimate tool before recording. Mark unconfirmed rather than guess.
3. Sits inside a live claim-risk surface: a category under active regulatory attention (creatine dosing, kids' supplements, structure-function claims) or already self-hedging its own language.
4. Enough real copy on IG/TikTok/site to pull a claim line, and enough search visibility to actually surface in an AI answer.
5. No visible outsourced compliance or AEO vendor credit anywhere in the brand's content.

## 3. Contact Sequences

Every message leads with the artifact link. None of them ask for a call in touch one or two.

### LinkedIn DM: 3 touches

**Touch 1 (Day 0, send the same day the teardown is recorded)**
> Hey [Name], ran [Brand] through ChatGPT and Perplexity this morning on "[money query]." Recorded what came back, 4 minutes: [link]. No pitch, just wanted you to see what your buyers are seeing before you do.

**Touch 2 (Day 4, if no reply)**
> One thing from that same teardown I didn't call out: "[flagged claim line]" is sitting close to a stronger claim than what an AI answer is repeating back about you. Timestamped it here: [link, jump to the moment].

**Touch 3 (Day 9, close, no chase after this)**
> Last one from me on this one. Keep the video either way, no follow-up needed. If you want a second set of eyes on that claim line before your team or counsel reviews it, I've got 3 audit slots open this month at $500. Door's open, no pressure.

### Email: 3 touches

**Email 1**: *What ChatGPT tells people about [Brand]*
> [Name], recorded a short one this morning: what ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overview say when someone asks about [Brand] and [category]. 4 minutes: [link]. Figured you'd rather see it than not.

**Email 2**: *One claim line worth a second look*
> Following up on the teardown from [date]. One specific line stood out: "[flagged claim]." Not flagging it as a legal problem, just marketing-side claim hygiene worth a look before it needs to be. Same link: [link].

**Email 3**: *Closing the loop*
> Not going to keep following up after this. If the video was useful, that's the whole point. If you want the claim line looked at properly, this month I've got 3 audits open at $500, fixed scope, one week. Otherwise, no reply needed, good luck with [Brand].

### Warm-referral variant (use when there's a mutual connection)

> [Connector] mentioned you're heads-down on [Brand] right now. I built a short teardown of what ChatGPT and Perplexity say about you when someone searches "[money query]." Thought you'd rather see it than not: [link].

Get the connector's yes before naming them.

## 4. Daily Contact-Unit Checklist (30 minutes, actions only)

1. Send today's scheduled touches (Day 0 / Day 4 / Day 9) from the tracker. Templates are already written, no drafting.
2. Log every reply (yes, no, or silence) as one line in the tracker. Nothing longer.
3. If a target replies interested, book the call inside 24 hours before building anything new.
4. Record one new teardown only if the queue holds fewer than 2 unsent artifacts (a separate 60-minute block, not inside this 30). Week 1 is the exception: hitting the pipeline target in `07-proof-plan.md` takes roughly 1-1.5 hrs/day of teardown production on top of this 30-minute box. Budget it as its own block until the queue is stocked, not as overflow from this checklist.
5. Close the window. Whatever's left waits for tomorrow's touches, not today's polish. The urge to open a document and improve something is the pattern wearing work clothes.
