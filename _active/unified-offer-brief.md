# The AI Creative Strategist Offer — 4 Pillar Service Ladder

**For Health, Wellness, Supplement & Performance Brands**

---

## The Positioning Spine (Why This Works)

Everyone has the same AI models. You have someone who's lived the physiology your brand is selling — so the angle is true before it's scaled.

Most health brands face the same three problems:
1. **Creative fatigue**: Posts die in 5–7 days because they're indistinguishable from competitors
2. **The compliance trap**: Claims sound persuasive, but you're not sure they're safe or source-backed
3. **The retrieval gap**: Your best insights, claims, sources, and founder judgment aren't structured for AI search to retrieve or preserve nuance

This ladder solves all three—starting free, scaling to strategy.

## Pillar 2: Audit ($500–$1,500 Pilot)

**The Angle Audit + Claim Safety Diagnostic**

### The Buyer Problem

"We got the teardown. We see the sameness. Now we need to know: What's the full system fix? Which of our claims are actually safe? How do we build content that earns trust *and* avoids legal risk?"

### What You Do

A deep diagnostic of their creative + content system:
- **Sameness diagnosis**: Map where they're losing differentiation
- **Claim-safety audit**: Inventory their health claims, grade sources, identify gaps, and provide safe-wording maps
- **Authenticity gate**: Separate what's founder-true from what's AI-averaged
- **Compliance read**: What's substantiable; what's at risk
- **Source-to-Search read**: Identify the first claim, buyer question, or page that should become a reusable trust asset
- **3–5 divergence angles**: Specific, proof-backed, category-breaking angles they can own

No production. Just clarity and a roadmap.

### Deliverables

- **Content gap map** (where they lose vs. competitors)
- **Claim inventory + source-grading sheet** (every claim, its evidence grade, safe wording, unsafe wording, risk level)
- **Source-to-Search Snapshot** (one trust-layer gap, one retrieval opportunity, and one first agent-readable primitive sketch)
- **Brand diagnostic brief** (2–4 pages: sameness problem, compliance gaps, authenticity fixes)
- **3–5 angle recommendations** (each with: audience, category convention, pattern break, emotional lever, proof gap, one-line creative brief)
- **Implementation roadmap** (quick wins vs. 30-day builds)
- **30–45 min walkthrough** (video or async, your choice)

### Timeline

- 5–7 business days turnaround
- Deliverables in a shared doc or PDF
- One revision round included

### Pricing

**Pilot: $500 | Standard: $1,000–$1,500**

(Raise after first 3 pilots—the proof token compounds. Research: brands expect to pay $300–$500 for a diagnostic before hiring.)

### Proof

- They have a written diagnosis they can trust
- They know which claims are safe and which need sources
- They have a specific angle roadmap (not "make it better")
- They can action 1–2 quick wins immediately

Refund guarantee: "If it doesn't sound like your brand, we don't charge."

### Deliverables

**Retainer (Ongoing):**

- Monthly strategy call (performance review + next month's theme)
- Continuous claim-safety vetting and Source-to-Search Trust Layer updates
- LinkedIn/entity reinforcement for the claims, definitions, and answer assets the brand wants AI search to understand
- Voice methodology documentation (so it scales if you bring in team)

### Timeline

**Proof Run:** 7 business days (results in feed same week)

**Retainer:** Monthly standing order; first month at sprint pace, then ongoing rhythm

## Pillar 4: Creative Strategy ($3–5K Sprint or $10K+ Intensive)

**The Full Market-To-Message System**

### The Buyer Problem

"Posts are better, but we need a system. We need to know: What's our market position? How do we build a narrative arc across content? How do we create demand, not just fill the feed? Where's the path from content to offer?"

### What You Do

The full market-to-message architecture: signal intelligence + claim safety + creative strategy + offer bridge + proof plan + Source-to-Search Trust Layer. This is the system that makes content non-interchangeable. Every asset (post, email, page, article) becomes part of one unified narrative, with source-backed material structured for GEO/AEO/SEO retrieval.

Not isolated posts. A strategy that compounds.

### Deliverables

**Creative Strategy Brief ($3–5K, 2–3 weeks):**
- Market signal brief (where your buyers' conversations are heading)
- Positioning statement (unique claim, proof lever, category convention break)
- Narrative arc (the story your content tells across 90 days)
- Content system architecture (which types of posts own which jobs; how they connect)
- Source-to-Search Trust Layer roadmap (claim library, source hierarchy, answer assets, and content decision rules)
- Offer bridge (how content funnel flows into paid)
- Proof plan (metrics, cadence, performance gates)
- Public content calendar (first 30 days, pre-written)
- Private strategy call (1 hour, walkthrough + customization)

**Strategy Intensive ($10K+, 30–45 days):**
- Everything in the Sprint, PLUS:
- Full 90-day content calendar (written, claimed, source-vetted)
- Long-tail question map + SEO/AEO asset list (retrieval-ready)
- Brand entity file, claim library, source hierarchy, decision rules, examples, counterexamples, quality tests, and update cadence
- 5–10 signature content pieces (articles, VSLs, email series)
- Monthly implementation calls (install the system + adjust)
- Performance tracking dashboard (what's working, what to iterate)
- Team training (so you can run the system after)

### Timeline

**Sprint:** 2–3 weeks, deliverables in Week 3

**Intensive:** 30–45 days, implementation calls throughout

### Pricing

**Sprint: $3–5K** (static deliverables + one call)

**Intensive: $10–25K** (full build-out + implementation support + team training)

### Proof

- You have a unified narrative the whole team can execute
- Every content piece has a job; nothing is accidental
- Content bridges naturally to offer (buyers see the path)
- The system is teachable (you can scale it)
- Retrieval-ready assets earn trust in AI search

### Next Step

→ Layer retainer on top (ongoing strategy + monthly content) *OR* Go back to retainer and iterate faster

## The Non-Negotiables (What Makes This Different)

**1. Claim Safety Is Built In**
Every angle, every post, every asset gets a source receipt. Safe vs. unsafe wording. Evidence grade. Compliance read. This isn't generic ghostwriting—it's market intelligence + proof discipline.

**2. You Stay Founder-True**
We're cloning your POV into the system, not averaging it away. If you've lived the physiology, that lived experience becomes the angle. AI scales it; it doesn't replace it.

**3. Retrieval-Ready from Day One**
Every asset is structured for AI search to cite, summarize, and preserve nuance. Long-tail question maps. 5 Percent Assets. Source-backed answer pages. The content future is GEO/AEO, not just social.

**4. Source-to-Search Trust Layer**
The brand's claims, sources, buyer questions, founder judgment, and content rules become a reusable operating layer: source receipts, safe wording, answer-page structure, examples, counterexamples, quality tests, and update rules.

**5. Measurable Proof at Every Stage**
Teardown → Audit → Proof Run in feed → Retainer performance → Strategy metrics. No phantom results. Real signal. Real proof. Real conversion.
