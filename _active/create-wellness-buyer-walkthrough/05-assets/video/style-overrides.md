# Create Wellness buyer walkthrough style

- Format: 1920 x 1080, 30 fps, exactly 60 seconds.
- Register: editorial evidence pulse. Calm, specific, lightly investigative.
- Palette: ink `#101010`, paper `#FAFAF8`, steel blue `#3D5A94`, soft gray `#D8D8D3`, muted text `#555553`.
- Display face: Iowan Old Style / Baskerville class. Body: Helvetica Neue.
- Motion: one lateral proof line, restrained wipes, hard cuts between claim states.
- No product glamour, supplement imagery, stock people, icons, gradients, shadows, or efficacy symbolism.
- Every claim state stays visible as `VERIFIED`, `LIKELY`, `UNCONFIRMED`, or `NO EVENT`.
- The unit mismatch is the opening evidence, not the commercial conclusion.
- Silent caption cut until Farrice records the prepared voice track. No TTS or voice clone.
