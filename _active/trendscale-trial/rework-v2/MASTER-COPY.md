# BRIEF 1 · JCKED · Liquid L-Carnitine 4000mg

## Strategic header
- **Strategist:** Farrice Cain
- **Client:** JCKED, Liquid L-Carnitine 4000mg
- **PDP:** https://jcked.com/products/liquid-l-carnitine-4000mg
- **Media buyer notes:** Working angle is mechanism-led education: the fat is locked, and the key is a real dose of L-carnitine. Offer: first bottle $49.95; multi-bottle bundles at $99.95 (buy 2 get 1) and $149.95 (buy 3 get 2). A $29.95 promotional usage guidebook is sold alongside the bottle, not as a discounted bottle price. Backed by a 365-day money-back guarantee, full refund even if the bottle is empty. Lead cold traffic to the single bottle, push bundles on warm for AOV. Audience signal: men 36 to 42 who already train and have tried fat-loss supplements; they reward a named mechanism and a real dose, they punish hype. Dose contrast is fair game: 4,000mg is the top of the studied range and most products stop near 500. Avoid: before/after bodies, scales, doctor-in-coat, urgency stingers, and "the clinical dose" as an absolute (use "top of the studied range").
- **Concept type:** Iteration
- **Iterated from:** The Locked Vault VSL (original Brief 01)
- **Avatar:** Men 36 to 42. Train 2 to 4 times a week. Have bought fat-loss supplements before and stopped trusting them. The line running in his head: "I am doing more than I did at 28 and getting less back." He wants the body that used to respond to his effort. He is skeptical of fat-loss marketing and rewards a clear, inspectable mechanism.
- **Awareness level:** Problem → Solution
- **Hypothesis:** Reframing stalled fat loss as a lock-and-key problem, not a discipline problem, should beat the willpower-framed control on hook rate for this skeptical, already-trying man. If the mechanism earns the first five seconds and the dose earns trust, click intent follows. Decision rule: if the lock-and-key hook does not clear the willpower control on three-second hook rate within about $2,000 of spend, the thesis is wrong and we cut it.

## Inspiration and editing
- **Concept inspo:** A locked vault and the one key that opens it. A single padlock, a single key, warm light when the door swings open. Editorial and clean, deliberately far from gym-bro.
- **Cast type:** AI UGC (HeyGen narrator) plus AI b-roll
- **AI avatar look:** Male, 40 to 45, lean, lived-in not model-polished. Dark crewneck or overshirt, no logos. Neutral kitchen or study, warm light. Still hands, eyes do the work, no smile through the hook.
- **Pacing:** Medium-fast. Measured narrator, quicker cuts inside the mechanism sequence.
- **Main emotion:** Recognition first ("that is me, still trying"), then relief that there is a specific, fixable reason.
- **Voiceover:** Calm, declarative, lightly conspiratorial. One smart skeptic leveling with another, not selling.
- **Music:** Sparse low pulse at -18 dB. Drops to silence on the final CTA line.
- **Caption style:** Burned-in, Inter Tight or Söhne, bottom third, max six words per frame, manual placement. Amber highlight reserved for the enzyme name and the dose number only. No auto-captions, no template transitions.
- **Overall direction:** Lead with the locked-vault story. The bottle earns its entrance only after the mechanism and the dose have created the question.
- **Editing notes:** The whole ad lives on one image: a locked vault and the key that opens it. Keep it that simple. The lock is the enzyme, the key is the nutrient, the open door is the result. Keep the full JCKED key visually distinct from the short stub-dose key. Real brand product photography for the bottle, clean source cards. No bodies, no scales, no before/after, no doctors, no urgency stingers.
