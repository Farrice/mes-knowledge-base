# Run-On-Yes Runbook (INTERNAL)

> **Do not build the audit until a client says yes.** This is the contact-first sequence: the DM ask ships first; this runbook fires only on a confirmed yes. Cheapest honest version — no pre-built audit document sitting unsent.

## 0. Trigger
A client replies yes to the DM ask. Only then do you open this. The audit output IS the scope — you are not building the knowledge base yet, you are producing the diagnostic that scopes (and sells) the build.

## 1. Intake request list (send the day they say yes)
Ask for access, not perfection. Expect mess — do not pre-tidy their story.

**Josh (fitness / physique personal brand + apparel):**
- Bio / origin story in his words (the physique + actor arc)
- Who the brand is for — audience notes, DMs, comments, buyer profile
- Apparel line: products, positioning, what makes it different, any drop plans
- Content archive: posts/captions/hooks that landed, the ones that flopped
- Any brand or visual files (logos, color, photo references, mood)
- Voice examples: 3–5 captions or clips that sound like him
- Access to where it all lives: IG, Notes, Drive, camera roll, any docs

**Andrea (Resonance — event / experience brand):**
- The existing brand-operating-system folder + the designer kit (already built — big head start)
- Venue and vendor contacts + the terms/relationships behind them
- Attendee / ticket data: Ticket Tailor exports, RSVP lists, who actually came
- Promo learnings: what filled the room, what channels worked, what didn't
- Playlists / set logic: the sets and the emotional-arc reasoning behind them
- Visual identity files (the Resonance look, image formulas)
- The voice document (curator-who-runs-the-door)

## 2. Execution sequence — `/ve-audit-offer` steps 1–6, `/library-retrieval-audit` as the technical layer
1. **Spine question** — "What's the one thing everything in the business ladders up to?" Ask it plainly ("what's the driving force here?"). For Josh: usually the brand/apparel objective. For Andrea: couples formed / connections made (her locked success metric — not attendance, not reach).
2. **Map the 7 data points** (domain-tuned sets below) — locate each: present / missing / conflicting.
3. **Run the messy pile** — find what's missing, what conflicts, where it actually lives.
4. **Sort every finding** into ON FIRE (broken/wrong) · OUTDATED (stale) · DISCONNECTED (non-obvious links). Surface ≥1 "did you know this was here" insight.
5. **Layer the retrieval score** — run `/library-retrieval-audit` four axes (connection / contradiction / freshness / provenance), /8, as extra scope justification.
6. **Write the scoped, priced build proposal** — reads directly off the findings. Route the build itself to `ve-scaling-path` (substrate: markdown first, Supabase only at a named threshold) + `ve-client-ui-layer` (packaging + voice charter). Refuse "easy"; say "straightforward."

## 3. Domain-tuned 7-datapoint sets

**(a) Fitness / physique personal brand (Josh):**
1. Brand Profile — who he is, the transformation/actor story
2. Audience — who it's for; followers and apparel buyers
3. Positioning — what makes his brand different from every other fitness-actor
4. Product / Offer line — the apparel, drops, any content products
5. Content Pillars / Messaging — recurring themes, the hooks that work
6. Visual Identity — apparel design language, photo style, the look
7. Voice & Tone — how he sounds on camera and in captions

**(b) Event / experience brand (Andrea / Resonance):**
1. Business Profile / concept — what Resonance is; the mission (heart encounters)
2. Audience — who shows up; the warm fans; attendee/ticket data
3. Venue & Vendor relationships — rooms, contacts, terms
4. Promo learnings — what filled the room, by channel
5. Musical identity — playlists, set logic, the emotional arc
6. Visual Identity — the Resonance look, designer kit, image formulas
7. Voice & Tone — curator who runs the door

## 4. Five-day schedule
- **Day 1** — Spine question + intake. Collect access to everything.
- **Day 2** — Map the 7 data points: locate each (present / missing / conflicting).
- **Day 3** — Run the messy pile + `/library-retrieval-audit` four-axis score. Sort on-fire / outdated / disconnected. Surface the ≥1 insight.
- **Day 4** — Write the findings sheet + the scoped, priced build proposal.
- **Day 5** — Package, deliver to the client folder, walk them through it live.
