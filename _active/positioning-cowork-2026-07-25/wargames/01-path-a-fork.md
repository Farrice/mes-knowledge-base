**The thing that does not survive is the fusion you'd be most tempted to build.** "Claim-safe content that gets you cited by AI" sounds like the perfect merger of your two directions. It's the one combination your own prior research says is unsupported and possibly backwards. Harmonize by fusing, and you build the offer on the single claim that failed verification.

So the harmonization is not a merge. It's a choice between two coherent routes that happen to share a production layer.

**The second finding is about the pattern, not the content.** Four offer definitions in 25 days: Path A claim-safe (07-01), $400 audit killed by red-team (07-21), Signal Pilot $2K exec ghostwriting (07-21), Answer-Market Fit $6-20K (07-25). The bundle says this about itself in Section 6: *"the 14-day validation is the next real step, and it hasn't been run yet."* The heavy brief says it too, in different words.

Both documents, built by different systems, months apart, independently reached the same instruction: **send something.** That convergence is the actual answer to the fork.

**Trigger:** the moment any DM asks what the engagement costs, that is the sprint's first live buyer conversation, and it outranks everything else in this document.

### Move 3 — Price-test in conversation, never in public

**Fail:** you publish a band from either document.

**Counter:** every number stays in conversation until three humans have reacted to it.
**Trigger:** if two of three land in the same range, that range becomes the offer, and it supersedes both documents' guesses.

## 7 — What survived contact

I attacked this route once, honestly, and it broke in one place.

**The attack:** Move 2B assumes the wedge post gets distribution. Your account has no baseline, low recent activity, and the bundle's own percentile-native model says the algorithm scores you against your own band. A wedge post to a cold, dormant account may return a null result that looks identical to a falsified category. You'd kill a live direction on no data.

**The patch:** Move 2B does not stand alone. It runs alongside J1 comment infiltration for the same week — enter rooms that already hold your buyers, so the wedge post lands on an account showing signs of life. The DM harvest then has a population to harvest from. Without this, the kill criterion is unreadable and the test proves nothing.

That patch is now part of the route. It is the difference between a test and a coin flip.

## 8 — The call

The fork does not resolve by analysis and I'm not going to pretend it does. Both routes are coherent and both are unvalidated. What resolves it is that they have different costs to test.

Claim-safe needs a findable candidate before it can even begin, and that candidate has sat unverified since July 7th. Answer-Market Fit needs a post plus a baseline you can capture in five minutes.

So run R1 once, at a hard 45-minute cap. If a candidate exists you have two live tests this week and that is a good problem. If it doesn't, the fork resolved itself and you stop carrying a direction that has been waiting eighteen days for a prerequisite nobody checked.

Either way the week ends with a send. That is the only outcome this wargame actually cares about.
