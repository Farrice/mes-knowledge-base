Today I asked my AI system to verify itself.

The first live probe found a dead Codex layer.

Then nothing fired.

A stale CLI version had quietly disabled the whole thing. The files had been sitting there doing a convincing impression of a working system.

That is the operator trap. We read the setup and assume the behavior.

The config can exist and the path can be right. Until the real event hits, you still have a theory.

Now I trust movement: send the command, watch the gate catch it, read the receipt it leaves behind.

A hook you believe is wired means nothing until it fires in front of you.

The screen finally caught the command.

That was when the automation became real.
