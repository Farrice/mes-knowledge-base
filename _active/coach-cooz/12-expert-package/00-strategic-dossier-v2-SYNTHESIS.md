## PART ONE — THE MAN YOU'RE REACHING

### His name is Mike.

He's 38. He runs a 9-person SaaS company doing $1.6M ARR. He raised a $900K seed in 2023 that was supposed to be $2M. He has two kids — 6 and 4. His wife is a therapist who works from home. He pays himself $142K a year and thought by now it would be $400K. He has never been to TIGER 21. He has never heard of Hampton. His peer group is a Slack channel of 14 founders who mostly post memes now.

He follows Dan Go on Instagram but has never finished a full video. He subscribed to Huberman four months ago and hasn't played an episode since. There's a dead WHOOP strap in his nightstand drawer.

He knows exactly when the last time was that he felt like himself. **It was a Tuesday in March 2023, three months before his co-founder stopped returning texts on weekends.** He doesn't talk about that Tuesday.

This is the man you're reaching. Not a demographic. A specific human being who is at this exact moment sitting in his car in his own driveway for 30 seconds because he can't make himself walk through the front door.

### What's actually happening inside him

The full interior world lives in `01-deep-worldview-canvas.md`. Here's the compressed version — the dimensions that matter most for the content, the offer, and the 30-day plan.

**1. The ambient anxiety never stops humming.** It's not a sharp edge — it's a dull 57-Hz hum of being responsible for something that could fail for reasons he won't see coming. He's not obsessed with his business. He's *hostage* to it. Those are different postures.

**2. He performs constantly.** For his team, his investors, his wife, his dead father, his 20-year-old self. The performance is more draining than the work. He's exhausted from *pretending he's fine while doing the work.*

**3. His real fear at 3 AM is one question he doesn't have language for**: *"Who am I if this doesn't work?"* The silence where the answer should be is what keeps him running.

**4. He's mourning the 2019 version of himself without knowing it.** Not the 20-year-old athlete version — the 2019 version. Three months after he quit his corporate job to build the company full-time. The last version of him that was *running toward something* instead of *defending something.*

**5. He doesn't want a new version of himself. He wants the 2019 version back.** Every other coach in the market is selling him "become a better you." He's not interested. He wants what was taken.

**6. He needs permission, not instruction.** The thing only a guide can give — not a program, not a book, not a podcast. Permission to stop pretending. Permission to put the body first. Permission to believe the man he used to be is still in there, dormant, not dead.

**7. His whole cosmology is half-broken.** He operates on an unexamined theology: *if I make enough of the right moves, the outcome I deserve will arrive.* The moves aren't working. The cosmology is silently rotting. He needs a new theology, not a new habit.

**8. His status game is older than he knows.** He's playing the Reluctant King — the man who inherited a duty he didn't fully choose, serves it loyally, and is watching his vitality drain into the crown. Jim O'Shaughnessy would name this archetype. Mike has no language for it. But he's in it.

### The Kristen Stewart Test

The question to ask before any content ships: *"Would Mike recognize himself in this sentence, or would he say 'that's close but not quite'?"*

Everything downstream — the content buckets, the hooks, the visual brand, the voice memos — is built to pass this test.

## PART THREE — THE RESURRECTION ARCHITECTURE

### Why "Resurrection" is your moat

The data is clean on this. After scraping real 2026 competitor content (Dan Go, Michael Sheedy, Soraya Zidane, Iskandar Zai) and 8 top LinkedIn creators:

- **Every other coach in this lane uses**: "transformation," "optimization," "high performance," "unlock potential." All of it presumes the reader is a working machine that needs tuning.
- **Nobody owns "Resurrection."** Not in this space. Not anywhere adjacent.

Resurrection is mythic, memorable, and — most importantly — it's the only word that acknowledges what Mike's body is telling him: *something has gone silent, and he's been pretending it hasn't.*

### The theological precision matters

- **Resurrection ≠ comeback.** A comeback implies you fell. Resurrection implies you *died* and came back as something more.
- **Resurrection ≠ optimization.** Optimization implies tweaking what's working. Resurrection acknowledges something has gone dormant.
- **Resurrection = the man you used to be is still in there.** He hasn't been replaced. He's been buried under 18-24 months of running on fumes. The work is *excavation*, not addition.

This is the frame Mike's 2019 ghost has been asking for. No other coach is offering it in this exact shape.

### The offer architecture (graduated pricing)

The research was clear: $5K-$10K is closable but creates unnecessary friction without proof. The path is graduated, not apex-first.

| Phase | Duration | Price | Purpose |
|-------|---------|-------|---------|
| **Phase 1 — Proof Sprint** | Months 1-3 (now) | **Triage Audit $497 + Protocol $1,500-$2,500** | 5 clients, 5 documented transformations, 5 testimonials |
| **Phase 2 — Authority Build** | Months 4-8 | Protocol $2,500-$3,500 | Use proof in content. Raise price. Start getting inbound. |
| **Phase 3 — Premium Position** | Months 9-18 | Protocol $4,000-$5,000+ | Full case study library. Podcast presence. Referrals flowing. |
| **Phase 4 — Apex Pricing** | 18+ months | $5,000-$10,000+ | Category authority. Waiting list. The original vision reached. |

**The $497 Triage Audit is a differentiator.** Dan Go has a gated-only funnel (no published price, application required). You're publishing the audit price and routing everything through it as the trust-building entry. That's unique.

**Why Phase 1 pricing isn't a step down.** Five proof clients at $2K = $10K and a portfolio. That portfolio is the foundation for $5K, which is the foundation for $10K. The path to $10K runs *through* $1,500. Not around it.

### What the offer actually includes (Phase 1 Resurrection Protocol)

Keep this list long and specific like Sheedy does. Long lists reduce perceived risk.

- Custom 90-day training program
- Custom nutrition plan (built around his actual life, not a textbook)
- Daily habit/protocol stack
- Weekly 1:1 check-in calls
- 24/7 communication channel
- Bloodwork interpretation (recommended, optional)
- Sleep + recovery protocol
- Mindset/spiritual integration (Cooz's signature — don't hide this)
- Private community access (when ready)
- Final "system handoff" — what to keep doing after the 90 days

Optional: **risk-reversal guarantee**. Dan Go has one. Most don't. Adding one creates a wedge.
