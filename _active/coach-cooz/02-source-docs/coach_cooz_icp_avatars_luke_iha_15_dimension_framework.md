## AVATAR 1 — “Jason Morales” (Acute Stabilization)
**Snapshot**  
- **Age / Role:** 39, union electrician, foreman‑track.  
- **Family:** Two kids (7 & 10), 50/50 custody pending.  
- **Location / Money:** Inland SoCal; $95k/yr; supporting two households feels impossible.  
- **Faith:** Grew up Catholic; “faith‑curious, not preachy.”  
- **Humanizing detail:** Keeps his kids’ drawings on the fridge in the now‑quiet rental; eats cereal over the sink because “nothing tastes right.”

### 1) One Central Problem (his words)
*“Up at 3am every night, no appetite, mind won’t shut off. I’m alone in this house with nobody to text back.”*

### 2) Emotional Architecture (drivers → triggers → intensity/frequency)
1. **Panic** → 3am wakeups, legal emails, bank app checks → **9/10, nightly**  
2. **Shame** → can’t eat, dropping weight, coworkers noticing → **8/10, daily**  
3. **Anger** → “I did everything right” rumination → **7/10, nightly**  
4. **Loneliness** → silent house after kids’ drop‑off → **8/10, 3–4×/wk**  
5. **Resolve/Hope** → gym sessions that quiet the noise → **5/10, intermittent**

### 3) Fear Scenarios (movie‑scene vivid)
- **Scene A:** Staring at the ceiling at 3:12am, heart hammering, kitchen clock ticking, scrolling the same texts—no replies.  
- **Scene B:** Kids ask, “Why aren’t you eating?”—he forces a smile; hands shake pouring cereal.  
- **Scene C:** Weekend alone; considers driving to the bar “just to be around people,” terrified of going backwards.

### 4) Social & Relational Impact
- **Kids:** Overcompensates on “fun weekends,” crashes Sunday night.  
- **Work:** Foggy on jobsite; attention slips near dangerous equipment.  
- **Friends:** Group chat gives gym memes, but no one checks in past 10pm.  
- **What he won’t say:** Afraid to be the dad who falls apart and loses custody.

### 5) Solution Graveyard (what failed & why)
- **White‑knuckling:** Nights got longer; no plan, no person.  
- **Therapy once a week:** Helped in session, collapsed at midnight.  
- **Drinking to sleep:** Stole his sleep; next day worse.  
- **Random “grind” routines:** Too big for his worst days.

### 6) Transformation Ladder
- **Current identity:** “Panicked, not hungry, alone.”  
- **Surface desire:** Sleep + appetite back.  
- **Deep desire:** Be a steady dad he respects.  
- **Ultimate transformation:** *Calm provider with simple rituals and backup at night.*

### 7) What he must give up
Numbing, late‑night scrolling, calling the ex, “I’ll tough it out alone.”

### 8) Messaging That Lands
- **Headline:** *Stop the 3am spiral—get your First 48.*  
- **Mechanism line:** *Body‑first anchors: Walk • Water • Lift • Text.*  
- **CTA:** *Text‑back stabilization starts tonight.*

### 9) Offer Fit
- **Emergency Stabilization (6 wks)** with AM/PM texts, First‑48, weekly calls; warm referrals if needed.

### 10) Content He Clicks
“Eat when food feels impossible,” “Night routine that actually knocks you out,” “Text this when panic spikes.”

## AVATAR 2 — “Evan Liu” (Structure & Rebuild)
**Snapshot**  
- **Age / Role:** 34, remote product manager in fintech.  
- **Family:** No kids; co‑owned condo being sold.  
- **Location / Money:** Austin; $160k + RSUs; overthinks big decisions.

*“My brain won’t turn off. I’m functional at work, wrecked at night. I need a plan I can do on my worst day.”*

### 2) Emotional Architecture
1. **Anxiety** → bedtime → **8/10, nightly**  
2. **Overwhelm** → condo sale logistics → **7/10, daily**  
3. **Skepticism** → “another coach?” → **6/10, initial**  
4. **Relief** → when given clear rules + check‑ins → **7/10, after week 1**  
5. **Pride** → strength returns → **6/10, weekly**

### 3) Fear Scenarios
- **Scene A:** Lying in blue phone‑glow; melatonin bottle on nightstand, calendar full tomorrow.  
- **Scene B:** Opens delivery app, stares, closes; protein bar again.  
- **Scene C:** Runs “what‑ifs” about the sale until 2am.

- **Friends:** Cancels plans; says he’s “training.”

- **YouTube advice:** Too many inputs; no one texting back.  
- **Therapy only:** Insight but no reps.

### 6) Transformation Ladder
- **Current identity:** Functional but frayed.  
- **Surface desire:** A plan that runs itself.  
- **Deep desire:** Self‑respect without performative hustle.  
- **Ultimate transformation:** *Calm, strong operator with boundaries and simple meals.*

### 7) What he must give up
Late‑night scrolling, solo optimization rabbit holes, perfectionism.

### 8) Messaging That Lands
- **Headline:** *Calm mornings in 90 days—without white‑knuckling alone.*  
- **Mechanism:** *AM/PM anchors + daily check‑ins beat hacks.*  
- **CTA:** *Install your anchors this week.*

### 9) Offer Fit
- **90‑Day Resurrection** with bi‑weekly 1:1, daily habit check‑ins, strength 3×/wk, portion‑based nutrition.

### 10) Content He Clicks
“5‑minute dinner when food feels impossible,” “Two‑exercise workout for anxious nights,” “Anchor stack for condo‑sale week.”

### 2) Emotional Architecture
1. **Guilt** → missed games, marital hindsight → **7/10, weekly**  
2. **Loneliness** → off‑shift nights → **8/10, 2–3×/wk**  
3. **Temptation** → post‑shift drinks invite → **6/10, weekly**  
4. **Resolve** → faith + daughter tether → **7/10, daily**  
5. **Pride** → strength & service identity → **6/10, weekly**

- **Scene A:** Off‑shift Friday, empty house, reaches for whiskey; stops, texts no one.  
- **Scene B:** Sunday pew, worship starts—eyes sting—leaves before anyone asks.  
- **Scene C:** Daughter says, “You seem far away, Dad,” and he has no words.

- **CTA:** *Join the Brotherhood—anchors that fit your shifts.*

### 9) Offer Fit
- **Brotherhood (ongoing)** + optional faith practice; quarterly resets; partner accountability.

### 10) Content He Clicks
“Shift‑proof anchor stack,” “Pray & Reset in 90 seconds,” “Friday night playbook without the bar.”

# Cross‑Avatar Patterns (Use in all copy)
- **Top pains:** 3am wakeups, loss of appetite, lonely house, temptation to numb, nobody to text.  
- **High‑leverage fixes:** AM/PM anchors, protein + water, 20‑min walk, simple strength, text‑back accountability, avoid ex‑entanglement at night.  
- **Emotional levers:** “Be a good dad,” “room to breathe,” “you won’t do this alone,” “strong body → calm mind,” “pray & reset” optional.  
- **Positioning:** Coaching, not therapy. Body‑first → brain follows. Faith optional. “Do the work; transform.”

# How to Use This Daily
1) Pick one avatar before you write; say his name out loud.  
2) Choose **one scene** (fear or transformation) and mirror it in your first paragraph.  
3) Offer **one anchor** + **one check‑in** + **one tiny win**.  
4) End with *“You won’t do this week alone.”*
