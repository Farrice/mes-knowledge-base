## Framework Anchor

Per Jun Yuh's **Pattern 10: Creative Vision Framework**, "The Who" of a personal brand is explicitly decomposed into **"demographics + psychographics"** — two distinct coordinates, not synonyms. Demographics are the body in the seat. Psychographics are the weather inside the body. The prior avatar-swarm output collapsed the second into the first. This rebuild maps the interior weather system of The Man in the Valley and lets the demographics scatter wherever they land. A 34-year-old massage therapist in Spokane and a 48-year-old body shop owner in Tallahassee share a psychographic fingerprint that a Park Avenue partner will never share with either of them. This map satisfies Pattern 10's success metric — Creative Vision complete before any content production — and supplies the compass for every pillar, offer, and sales page downstream.

## A. Core Values Stack (Ranked)

Ranked by the **Creative Vision Anchor Check** test: *which value does he sacrifice others for when the pressure is real?*

1. **Provision** — He got into his thing (shop, practice, route, crew) to take care of people. Richard Hughes kept working on clients with his body breaking under him because *his clients needed him on that table*. Daniel's 5K trigger happened in front of family. Provision is why he won't quit and rest.
2. **Honor / Word-Is-Bond** — "If I said I'd do it, I'm doing it." CEO Paul said yes to 14 weeks because his doctor handed him a direct challenge. Honor is the override on his body's protest.
3. **Competence / Being Good At The Thing** — Craftsman identity. Bouncer, massage therapist, coach, shop owner — *knows his job at a level most don't*. David Sell is still coded by "I was the best, I'm supposed to be the best." Competence-in-craft intact while competence-in-body erodes = the contradiction he feels but can't yet name.
4. **Independence / Not Being Managed** — Runs his own thing because he can't stand being told how to do his job. Why "executive coaching" language makes his skin crawl.
5. **Respect (earned, not performed)** — From peers doing the same work. Not LinkedIn. Joshua Davidson's Vegas mirror landed because he was with peers and saw himself as the one who'd slipped.
6. **Loyalty (to crew, to family)** — Eats his own suffering for years before letting people around him feel it. "I've missed every family dinner this year" (WS1.5) is a confession of failed loyalty — and it's the sentence that breaks him, not the weight.
7. **Peace (last, and secretly first)** — Won't admit he wants it; thinks wanting peace makes him soft. But peace — uninterrupted sleep, a quiet drive home, a Sunday without a fire — is what he fantasizes about when honest. The brand can never lead with this but must earn the right to deliver it.

**Sacrifice order**: Burns Peace → Loyalty → Honor → Competence-in-body → Independence → **never Provision**. Resurrection must be framed as *restoring capacity to provide*, never as self-care.

## B. The Non-Negotiables

Three tribal codes the content must respect or lose him in the first 4 seconds.

1. **No pity. No therapist voice. No "it's okay to not be okay."** Therapy-speak reads as women's-magazine or corporate-HR language, both of which he's allergic to. The Cooz voice guide's banned phrase list ("Trust the process," "You got this," "holistic," "alignment") is non-negotiable for exactly this reason. Anthony Ng Monica's "5 out of 10" is direct diagnosis, not permission-giving — that's the register.
2. **Don't talk down to him about his own business or his own body.** He knows his trade better than you do and he knows his body better than you think — he's been ignoring the signals, not missing them. Tone = *peer who noticed something* or *coach who's been there*, never *expert who figured out what he couldn't*. Richard Hughes walks away from any copy that treats him like a body he doesn't understand.
3. **Don't ask him to be vulnerable in public.** He accepts private confession, frameworks, diagnostic language, story-based proof. He rejects "share your journey" prompts and anything that smells like exposure. WS1.5 finding — "the most powerful pain language comes from peer witnessing, not self-description" — maps directly here. He wants to watch someone else say it first.

## C. The Secret Permission He's Seeking

The brief's hypothesis is close but needs sharpening. He isn't looking for permission to admit he's tired of being the man he's been. He's looking for permission to **stop proving something he never consciously agreed to prove**. The exhaustion isn't from being himself — it's from carrying a contract he can't remember signing.

Precisely: *permission to admit that the version of himself he's been defending for the last 5-15 years was never actually him — it was the version the job, the wife, the kids, the crew, the culture, and the father-figure-who-was-missing-or-too-present all installed. And permission to let that version die without it meaning he failed, quit, or got soft.*

This is why **"resurrection"** hits differently than "transformation" or "comeback." Transformation = become a new man. Comeback = return to the old one. Resurrection = *the real him was buried under a version he was performing, and the real him can come back*. He doesn't have to become someone new. He has to bury the imposter. This matches Cooz's voice DNA exactly — "the leader, the real one, the one who operated from his gut... got archived." Archived. Not dead. Not broken. The permission is to unarchive.

**Pull-quote test**: If a post makes him say *"thank god somebody finally said that"* out loud in his truck while reading alone, it hit. Every other reaction — agreement, interest, "good post" — is a miss.

## D. Worldview Anchors

Deep beliefs that run his operating system. Not opinions. OS.

1. **"The body is the last honest scoreboard."** Everything else can be spun, negotiated, or deferred. The mirror, the scale, the BBQ photo his wife took, the wince picking up his daughter — can't be negotiated with. Joshua Davidson's Vegas mirror is this anchor in action. He trusts the body more than any story he tells himself about the body.
2. **"Men hide the real stuff from each other, and the real stuff is what kills them."** He has a friend (or had one) who didn't make it — heart attack, suicide, divorce, the bottle. He has absorbed the lesson: *the ones who go down are the ones who looked fine*. This is why the WS1.5 witness-voice is so powerful — it speaks the language he already uses privately about other men.
3. **"Nobody is coming."** Not the doctor, not the wife, not the partner, not God the way he used to believe. If he doesn't handle it, it doesn't get handled. Load-bearing belief under everything — and the source of both his strength and his exhaustion.
4. **"Discipline is the tax you pay for being the kind of man who gets to decide things."** He doesn't believe in motivation. He believes in showing up. Productivity and motivation content bounce off him — they're aimed at people trying to trick themselves into moving. He's been moving for 20 years. The question isn't how to move. It's why the moving stopped working.
5. **"You can outrun a lot of things, but not your own body, and not for very long."** The new belief forming — not fully installed yet. This is the belief the brand installs. It is the bridge from his current OS to the one Cooz is selling.

## E. The Tribal Code Map

Critical distinction per **Pattern 10: Creative Vision Framework** — the content must respect the real tribe, not the performed one, or it reads as costume. The gap is where every grind-guru loses him.

| Performed Membership (public feed) | Actual Resonance (truck rides, 2 AM) |
|---|---|
| Hustle culture / grind mindset / "outwork everyone" | Slow craftsman / mastery / the guy doing it 25 years |
| Gym culture / lifting bros / PR chasing | Functional movement / "I want to not hurt when I stand up" |
| "Self-made entrepreneur" brotherhood | Tradesman dignity / his father's work ethic / men who build things |
| Red-pill / sigma grindset | Jocko / Cam Hanes / Huberman / Rogan long-form (NOT manosphere) |
| "Family man" performance (kids-at-games posts) | Actual fear of not being present and not knowing how to fix it |
| "I don't need help" stoicism | Quietly wanting a coach, a brother, a witness — no framework to get one |

**Rule**: Speak to the right column. Never the left, even though it's what his public feed looks like. The left is what he performs because it's the only public language available to him. Using it in content confirms his worst fear — that Cooz is just another grind guru.

## F. The Contrarian Take Map

Five positions that signal *"this guy sees me"* by cutting against the dominant industry narrative.

1. **"Discipline isn't the answer. Discipline is how you got here."** He's been using discipline as his life's engine, and it's what drove him into the valley. His discipline is intact. His infrastructure is broken. You can't discipline your way out of a broken machine — you just grind the gears faster. Direct port of Cooz's "fix the hardware, then the software runs."
2. **"Optimization is a trap for men hiding from themselves."** Every supplement stack, cold plunge, and sleep gadget is a way of not looking at the actual thing. You don't need more tools. You need to stop running and let the thing catch up with you. Aimed at the Dan Go / Huberman-optimization audience crossover.
3. **"You're not burnt out. You're dormant."** Cooz's signature. Burnout implies a diagnosis that requires time off and therapy. Dormant implies a state that requires *reactivation*, which is something he knows how to do. One framing sends him to HR. The other sends him to Cooz.
4. **"You don't need to work less. You need to work from your gut again."** The industry tells him to work less. He won't. What he lost, around year 3 of being "responsible," was his instincts. The Cooz "quarterback" line reframed.
5. **"Your belly isn't your fault. It's the receipt."** Body-shame content says he's weak. Body-positive says he's fine. Both miss. The belly is a printout — data about what the last five years actually cost him. Read it, then decide. Cooz voice guide line #13 deployed as pillar.

## H. The Identity Transformation Cooz Sells

In Jun Yuh's **Pattern 5 (Journey vs Expert POV Positioning)** language, Cooz operates from Expert POV — 2+ years of lived authority, McBroom lineage. But the *transformation he sells* is not an expert transformation. It is an identity restoration — the core of Pattern 10's "The Uniqueness" coordinate.

**External transformation** (what he thinks he's buying): 20-40 lbs off the midsection; sleep that isn't a negotiation; energy without caffeine as a brake-removal system; physical capacity to keep up with kids / crew / job.

**Interior identity shift** (what he's actually buying, which he cannot yet name):
- From **"the guy I'm supposed to be"** → **"the guy I actually am."** Dissolution of the installed version. Restoration of the operator who existed before he started calculating every move.
- From **"managing decline"** → **"commanding capacity."** Triage mode → expansion from a stable base.
- From **"nobody knows what it's actually like"** → **"at least one person sees me without flinching."** Cooz becomes the first honest mirror he's had in 5-15 years. That relationship, more than any protocol, is what he's buying.
- From **"I'm the guy who used to be..."** → **"I'm the guy who is..."** The tense shift is the most important artifact of the transformation. When he stops narrating himself in past tense, the resurrection is complete.

The valley exemplars — Daniel, Richard, Patrick, Joshua, David Sell — all describe it the same way in retrospect. None of them say "I lost the weight." They all say some version of *"I came back."*

## J. What the Old Polluted Swarm Got Wrong

The prior map pinned the avatar to credentialed upper-management operators in Tier-1 metros with $300K+ household income and board meetings as the trigger. It mistook a demographic snapshot for a psychographic fingerprint — the exact failure mode **Pattern 10: Creative Vision Framework** is built to prevent by splitting "The Who" into *demographics + psychographics* as two separate coordinates. The polluted swarm filled in only one. It also failed **Pattern 5 (Journey vs Expert POV Positioning)** by building toward an audience that would have required Cooz to posture up into a register (board rooms, investor dinners, credentialed peer language) his McBroom-lineage story and lived proof cannot credibly occupy — which would have pushed him straight into the "Generic Guru Talk" anti-exemplar. The real buyer — Daniel, CEO Paul (medical trigger, not career trigger), Anthony Ng Monica, Joshua Davidson, Richard Hughes (closest match), Patrick Hayes, David Sell, plus the WS1.5 verbatims — is not defined by tax bracket. He is defined by interior weather: the archived self, the broken hardware, the missing honest mirror, the word-is-bond contract slowly killing him, the body that has become the last honest scoreboard in a life full of negotiated truths. He can be in Spokane, Tallahassee, or Queens. He can make $95K or $240K. The $2K-$4K price is a *scheduling* decision, not a *category* decision — meaning the psychographic fingerprint is the qualifier and the demographic band is whatever statistical shape happens to fall out of that fingerprint. Build the brand to the weather, not the zip code.
