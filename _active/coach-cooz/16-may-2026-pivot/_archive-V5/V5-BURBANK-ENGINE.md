### CHANNEL 1 — REFERRAL ASKS FROM YOUR 5 LONG-ARC CLIENTS (this week)

This is the highest-leverage move you make in the next 30 days. Five people. Five conversations. Each one likely has 3-5 introductions to give.

The script (use the same one for all five — adjust for context):

> *"Hey [name] — I'm at a moment where I'm bringing on 3 more in-person clients this quarter and I want to do it through people I already trust. I'm not asking you to sell me. 3. **You commit 2 voice memos per week to Farrice (~10 min each)** to feed the cross-platform content engine. This is the single point of failure for the online track. No memos = no content = no online distribution.

4. **You show up to 1 industry event in the first 60 days.** WGA Foundation OR IATSE OR EO. One event. If you don't, you forfeit Channel 2 (Hollywood production network) — that's fine; the other 4 channels still produce clients.

If the warm track stalls AND the online track produces 1+ client by Week 4, we double down on online. If both stall, we re-diagnose with Farrice. **The online track alone, executed with discipline, produces 2-4 clients in 60 days.** That's the floor.
