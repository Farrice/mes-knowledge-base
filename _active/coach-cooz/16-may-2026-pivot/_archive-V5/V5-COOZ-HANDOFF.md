## THE HARD RULES (don't break for 90 days)

1. **No price improvising.** Four pathways, set rates. If asked: *"Three pathways depending on what fits — let's get on a Triage Audit and figure out which one."*
2. **Outbound links go in first comment, never in body of post.** Single biggest reach lever.
3. **3 lanes only on LinkedIn for 90 days.** Body-First Foundation · Transformation Architecture · Case Studies. Off-lane goes to book/podcast/private.
4. **No banned vocabulary in any client-facing surface.** Specifically: substrate, biological infrastructure, cognitive throttle, operator, operating system, "Here's what" / "Here's why" / "Here's how" openers.
5. **Triage Audit close opens with reflective listening.** Read back the prospect's verbatim language before giving your read.
6. **Friday check-in calls with Farrice — non-negotiable.** 30 minutes, every Friday, for 12 weeks.
7. **Voice memo input on a real client moment minimum 2x/week.** Notebook 1 needs ground-truth anchors to generate posts. No anchor → no post.
8. **Review every LinkedIn post against the 6-point quality gate before posting.** (In V5-NOTEBOOK-KNOWLEDGE.md.)
9. **First 60 minutes after posting**: respond to every comment. Algorithm weights early-engagement velocity heavily.
10. **No emoji-heavy posts. No engagement bait ("Comment below if..."). Trust the post.**
