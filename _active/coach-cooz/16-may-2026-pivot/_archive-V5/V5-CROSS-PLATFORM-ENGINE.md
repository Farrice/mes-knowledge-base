# V5 — CROSS-PLATFORM CONTENT ENGINE

## What this is
Your weekly content rhythm across LinkedIn + Instagram + YouTube + blog. ONE voice memo input → 5 platform outputs. Single source. Five surfaces.

## Why it exists
You're already on Instagram, YouTube, and the coachcooz.com blog. The V5 LinkedIn engine alone leaves 70% of your distribution on the table. This doc tells you how the platforms work together — without doubling the production load.

## The non-negotiable
**One voice memo per week is the engine.** No memo, no week's content. Every platform traces to one moment.

## THE PLATFORM ROLE HIERARCHY

Each platform has ONE job. Don't ask any platform to do another platform's work.

| Platform | Primary job | Secondary | Conversion mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Authority + lead capture | Distribution to peer-network | Profile-as-landing-page → Triage Audit booking |
| **Instagram** | Shareability + bottom-funnel CTA | Brand visibility | DM "Resurrection" → Triage Audit |
| **YouTube** | Depth + SEO compounding | Trust-building before buy | Description link → Triage Audit |
| **Blog (coachcooz.com)** | SEO foundation + email capture | Long-term compounding asset | Email capture → nurture → Triage Audit |
| **Podcast (Resurrection Series)** | Trust deepening + IP showcase | Guest appearances → audience inheritance | Listener → coachcooz.com |

### Why the hierarchy matters

If you treat all platforms equally, you spread thin and convert nothing. The hierarchy:

- **LinkedIn** is your highest-conversion-per-impression platform — the buyer (high-performing professional) is already there. **Lead capture is the priority.**
- **Instagram** is your highest-shareability platform — the buyer's friends/peers are there. **Brand visibility through saves and shares is the priority.**
- **YouTube** is your highest-trust-per-minute platform — long-form watch time signals depth. **Trust before the buy is the priority.** Also the strongest SEO compounding once you hit 20+ videos.
- **Blog** is your highest-SEO-per-post platform — Google indexes coachcooz.com long-term. **Compounding asset is the priority.** Email capture is the bonus.
- **Podcast** is the IP showcase — listener intent is highest. **Depth + audience inheritance is the priority.** Slowest to compound but highest-quality leads when it does.

## THE SINGLE-SOURCE PRODUCTION RULE

Every output traces to one voice memo. No multi-thread chaos.

### What this means in practice

- The Mon LinkedIn post, Tue IG carousel, Thu IG reel, YouTube long-form, and Fri blog all reference **the same anchor moment, the same named client, the same proof number**.
- They differ in: platform reading rhythm, length, visual treatment, CTA mechanism, and which V5 voice rotation slot they hit.
- They DO NOT differ in: the client named, the moment told, the methodology referenced, the truth claim made.

### Why this matters

Three reasons:

1. **Audience recognition compounds.** When the same person sees the LinkedIn post Monday and the IG carousel Tuesday referencing Mike's 9-year arc — the second hit lands deeper. The third hit (YouTube Friday) closes them.
2. **Production stays sustainable.** One memo + one Brief = 5 outputs. Five separate Briefs = 5x the cognitive load. The system collapses by week 4 if you try to feed each platform a unique anchor.
3. **Voice fidelity is enforceable.** When all 5 outputs trace to one moment, voice drift is detectable in 30 seconds — if the LinkedIn post sounds like Cooz but the IG reel sounds like AI, you regenerate the IG reel.

## REPURPOSING RULES (what changes between platforms, what stays)

Same anchor moment. Different platform shape. Specifics:

### What STAYS constant across all 5 platforms

- The named real client (Mike / Robin / Carron / Corey / Sam / Sammy / Buzz / Mari / Cody / Mariano)
- The specific moment (the scene, the number, the detail)
- The methodology callout (Brave Choice question, if applicable)
- The lane (Body-First Foundation / Transformation Architecture / Case Study)
- The voice register (testimonials-backstory voice)
- The brand frame (Resurrection Coach / rebuild the body that built the life)

### What CHANGES per platform

| Element | LinkedIn | Instagram | YouTube | Blog |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Length** | 120-450 words | Carousel: 7 slides + 2-3 sentence caption · Reel: 45-90 sec script | 8-12 minutes | 1,500-2,500 words |
| **Reading rhythm** | Tight, scannable, scroll-friendly | Visual-led, save-worthy, share-worthy | Conversational, depth, single-take feel | Structured, SEO-keyword-dense, internal-link-rich |
| **Hook** | First line of post | First slide / first 5 seconds of reel | First 15 seconds (cold open) | H1 + first 200 words |
| **Methodology depth** | Mentioned, not unpacked | One slide isolates one Brave Choice question | Unpacked at minute 7-9 | Full section (300-500 words) |
| **CTA mechanism** | Triage Audit link in first comment | "DM 'Resurrection'" or "link in bio" | Description link + verbal mention at end | End-of-post + mid-scroll email capture |
| **Visual treatment** | Text-only (LinkedIn algo penalty for image-only when text-led brand) | Photos from V5-PHOTO-INVENTORY + carousel typography | Talking head + B-roll, V5 cinematic palette | Hero photo + 1-2 inline photos + V5 typography |
| **Cross-link strategy** | First comment to coachcooz.com landing page | Bio link + occasional swipe-up | Description timestamps + blog link | Internal links to 2-3 prior posts + outbound to authoritative source |
| **Hashtag strategy** | None (LinkedIn doesn't reward) | 3-5 tasteful (#strengthtraining #executivefitness #burbankcoach — NOT #fitfam) | 3 in description (lane-specific) | None in body; SEO meta-tags handled at site level |

### Anti-pattern: the "paste-everywhere" trap

DON'T paste the LinkedIn post into the IG reel caption.
DON'T paste the IG carousel slides into the blog post.
DON'T paste the blog post into the YouTube description.

Every platform requires a platform-shape pass. The `/cooz-flywheel-v5` workflow handles this automatically — it generates each asset INDEPENDENTLY from the same Brief, not by repurposing the LinkedIn post 4 ways.

## INSTAGRAM-SPECIFIC GUIDANCE (Brock Johnson shareworthy mechanics)

Instagram is your shareability + brand visibility lever. The buyer (high-performing professional) is on IG, but they're not buying from IG — they're discovering and saving. The conversion happens later, on LinkedIn or coachcooz.com.

### What works on IG for the V5 brand

- **Carousel posts** with text-first slides (save-worthy frameworks). Brave Choice methodology breakdown, 8-Tenet single-Tenet explainers, Lane 1 protocol cards.
- **Reels with talking head + scene-led storytelling.** Sam's go-go boots deadlift story. Mike's 9-year executive arc. Carron's CA State squat record moment. Cooz on camera, real client name, 60 sec, no music doing heavy lifting.
- **Photo + caption posts** for lifestyle moments (training a client, behind-the-scenes, gym scene). Use Photo 06/07 (outdoor casual), Photo 12 (lifestyle reading), Photo 13/14/15 (sunset backpack).

### What DOESN'T work on IG for the V5 brand

- **Quote-card graphics.** They look like every other coach. V5 brand has more depth.
- **Before/after transformation photos** WITHOUT a real client narrative arc. The transformation porn aesthetic dilutes the long-arc positioning.
- **Daily posting cadence.** 2-3 IG posts/week is the V5 cadence. More dilutes; less is fine.
- **Engagement bait** ("Tag a friend who needs to hear this," "Save this for later"). V5-NOTEBOOK rule: trust the post.

The blog at coachcooz.com is your SEO foundation + email capture engine. Slowest to compound (3-6 months to rank). Most permanent asset (blog posts compound for years; LinkedIn posts decay in 72 hours).

  - "Burbank Executive Coach: Rebuilding the Body That Built Your Career" (target: Burbank executive coach)

### What DOESN'T work on the blog for the V5 brand

- **Short posts (< 1,000 words).** Don't rank. Use LinkedIn for short-form.
- **Listicle slop** ("10 best supplements," "5 morning routines"). Wrong register.
- **Generic "wellness" content.** V5 voice is diagnostic-specific. The blog inherits the LinkedIn voice register.

### Blog setup essentials

- **CMS**: Whatever's already on coachcooz.com (Squarespace / Webflow / WordPress). Don't migrate — just publish.

- **Outbound linking strategy**: Every blog post links to 1-2 authoritative external sources (peer-reviewed studies if cited, Cody McBroom's tailoredcoachingmethod.com, IATSE Local 600 if Hollywood-vertical post). Signals trustworthiness to Google.

## CROSS-PLATFORM ANTI-PATTERNS

Five patterns that look productive but kill the engine:

1. **Posting different anchor moments per platform.** One memo, one Brief, 5 outputs. If you're producing 5 anchors per week, the system is broken.
2. **Cross-posting LinkedIn copy to IG/YouTube/blog without platform-shape pass.** Each platform has its own reading rhythm.
3. **Treating Instagram as the conversion mechanism.** IG is shareability + brand visibility. The buyer converts on LinkedIn or coachcooz.com.
4. **Adding a 6th platform (TikTok / X / Threads) before LinkedIn + IG + YouTube + blog are all running smoothly.** Don't. The compounding math is in depth, not breadth.
5. **Letting the platforms set the cadence.** Platforms reward more posts. The brand rewards better posts. Your cadence is fixed: 1 voice memo → 5 outputs per week. Don't let platform pressure shift it.

## ROLLOUT ORDER (don't try to do everything Week 1)

| Week | What's live | Why this order |
|---|---|---|
| **1-2** | LinkedIn posts (3/week) + LinkedIn profile rebuild + GBP refresh | Highest-conversion-per-impression platform. Get the lead-capture spine running first. |
| **3-4** | + Instagram (2 posts/week — 1 carousel, 1 reel) | Shareability layer. Cross-pollinates with LinkedIn (peer-network discovery). |
| **5-6** | + YouTube (1 long-form/week) | Trust-deepening + SEO compounding. Slowest to traction; start it now so it has runway. |
| **7-8** | + Blog (1 post/week on coachcooz.com) | SEO foundation. Lowest immediate ROI but highest long-term compounding. |
| **9+** | All 4 platforms running. Resurrection Series podcast launches when 4 episodes are recorded and edited. |

If you try to launch all 5 platforms Week 1, you'll burn out by Week 3 and the LinkedIn engine — the highest-conversion lever — won't get the focus it needs to compound. Stage the rollout. LinkedIn first, then layer.
