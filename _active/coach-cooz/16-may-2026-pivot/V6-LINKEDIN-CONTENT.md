## IF A POST FALLS BELOW 1% ENGAGEMENT (DIAGNOSTIC PROTOCOL)

After 24 hours, if a post is below 1% engagement (unusually low for the audience):

1. **Don't repost.** Don't repurpose. Don't cross-post to Instagram as "salvage."
2. **Note the failure metadata**: which lane, which voice, which client anchor, which closer, what time of day, what other posts went up that morning in your network.
3. **Diagnose against the 6-point gate retroactively**: Did it open in I/scene/named client? Was the moment specific or generalized? Was the you-pivot late and short? Any banned vocabulary or structural move slip through?
4. **Diagnose for ecosystem cannibalization**: Did the post repeat language from a post in the prior 7 days? Cross-piece rhythm repetition is the V6-CANON §7 banned move #7 — most often the cause of flat performance for a structurally sound post.
5. **Adjust the next post in the same voice slot.** Try a sharper opener, a more specific named client, a tighter close, or a different lane pairing.
6. **Track over 4 weeks**: which voices + lanes travel best for Cooz's specific audience? Friday-morning metric review surfaces this. After 8 weeks, the rotation calibrates to actual audience response — not to the §8 starting targets.
7. **The escape valve**: if a Lane 2 confessional or a Lane 3 case study post underperforms, the diagnosis is rarely the post — it's the audience state mix. Cold audiences need more Proof (Lane 3) and Scene (Lane 1) before they'll engage with Confession or Witness. The first 4 weeks of Notes traffic build the surface area; Confession lands harder in Month 2.
