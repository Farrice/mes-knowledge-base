## The hard rules (don't break for 90 days)

1. **No price improvising.** Four pathways, set rates. If asked: *"Three pathways depending on what fits — let's get on a Triage Audit and figure out which one."*
2. **Outbound links go in first comment, never in body of post.** Single biggest reach lever.
3. **3 lanes × 5 voices on LinkedIn for 90 days.** Body-First Foundation 55% · Transformation Architecture 30% · Case Studies 15%. Voice rotation Scene 35% · Confession 20% · Reframe 20% · Witness 15% · Proof 10%.
4. **No banned vocabulary.** See V6-CANON §6 ban list.
5. **Triage Audit close opens with reflective listening.** Read back the prospect's verbatim language before giving your read. (V6-TRIAGE-AUDIT-SCRIPT Phase 2.)
6. **Friday check-in calls with Farrice — non-negotiable.** 30 min, every Friday, for 12 weeks.
7. **Voice memo input on a real client moment minimum 2x/week.** No anchor → no post.
8. **Review every LinkedIn post against the 6-point quality gate before posting.** (V6-CANON §8.)
9. **First 60 minutes after posting**: respond to every comment.
10. **No emoji-heavy posts. No engagement bait. Trust the post.**
