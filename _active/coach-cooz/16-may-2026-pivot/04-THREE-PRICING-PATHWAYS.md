# Three Pricing Pathways
## Coach Cooz — May 2026 Beta-Phase Offer Architecture
## Anchored to: just-signed $1,200/mo client + April 14 Ignition ladder + Hormozi/Suby grand-slam principles

---

## The Strategic Frame

You're not choosing ONE offer. You're building a **laddered pathway system** where:
- Pathway A = the entry (low commitment, recurring revenue base)
- Pathway B = the conversion (defined arc, case-study trade, the proof engine)
- Pathway C = the deepening (premium tier that establishes the ceiling and keeps top clients)

The just-signed client at $1,200/mo proved willingness exists at that floor. The April 14 Ignition ladder ($1,997 → $2,997 → $4,000) was calibrated to a different buyer (Man-in-the-Valley tradesman). The new pivot — entrepreneurs / founders / small business owners / six-figure professionals who can comfortably afford coaching — supports a higher floor and a wider ceiling.

**Financial target**: 3-5 new clients in beta phase. Math worked through below proves the financial goal is hit at any of the 3 pathways, but the BLEND is what matters most.

## PATHWAY A — The Resurrection Retainer

### Positioning
**Open-ended monthly coaching for entrepreneurs and high-functioning professionals who want a steady-state coach, not a 12-week sprint.**

For people who say things like:
- "I don't want to do another 12-week program. I want a coach for the long haul."
- "I'm not in crisis mode. I just want someone in my corner consistently."
- "Packages feel like pressure. Monthly works for how I run my life."

### Price
**$1,500 / month** with a 3-month minimum commitment, then month-to-month. Cancel anytime after the 3-month minimum with 30-day notice.

### Why $1,500 (not $1,200)?
- The new client signed at $1,200/mo. That's the FLOOR — a single data point that proved willingness, not the ceiling.
- $1,500/mo is the price for new entrants. The just-signed client stays at her grandfathered $1,200 rate (a positive — she becomes a referral source telling others "I got in early").
- Math: $1,500 × 12 months = $18,000/year per retainer client. That's professional-tier pricing without crossing the psychological $20K/year threshold that triggers different procurement behavior.
- Comparison: Dan Go's group programs are $200-$500/mo (lower touch). UP Fitness 1-on-1 is $700+/mo for in-person only. Daily Body Coach is $500+/mo for app-based. **Cooz's 1-on-1 high-touch coaching at $1,500/mo is the sweet spot for a buyer who wants real attention but doesn't want UP-tier price.**

### What's Included
- Weekly 30-minute 1:1 video call (4 per month)
- Daily Voxer voice coaching, Mon-Fri 9am-6pm PT (real responses within business hours)
- Custom training program in TrueCoach (updated monthly)
- Custom nutrition framework (200g protein floor, flexible architecture, NOT a meal plan)
- The 8 Tenets curriculum at the buyer's pace
- Quarterly 60-minute strategic deep-dive call
- Quarterly progress documentation (photos, lifts, measurements) — saved to a private shared folder
- Access to the private Cooz client text thread (if/when peer accountability layer launches)

### What's NOT Included
- Daily 24/7 messaging (Voxer windows are business hours only)
- Labs / biomarkers / hormone testing (referrals to MDs available)
- Therapy or life coaching (referrals available)
- Travel / retreats / in-person sessions
- Custom meal-planning service or grocery-delivery integrations

### Risk Reversal
**The First-Month Witness Guarantee**: If at the end of month 1 the client doesn't feel Cooz is the right fit — for any reason — they can cancel and get a 50% refund of month 1, no questions asked. This is the standard DTC retainer guarantee structure. It's a low-friction trial that respects both sides.

### Strategic Role
- **Predictable cash flow.** 5 retainer clients = $7,500/mo MRR = $90K/year base.
- **Lower friction at sale.** Buyer who balks at $3,750 12-week package will often say yes to $1,500/mo "let's see how this goes" — and stay 12+ months.
- **Long-term relationship.** Retainer clients generate 80%+ of word-of-mouth referrals over a 24-month window per industry data. They're the asset.

### When to Pitch Pathway A (vs. B or C)
- Buyer has expressed package fatigue ("I've done 3 of these things, never again")
- Buyer is solvent but allergic to large single payments
- Buyer is a referral source whose network you want access to over years
- Buyer is the just-signed client's archetype: high-up at small business, comfortable affordability, consistent professional life

## PATHWAY B — The Ignition

### Positioning
**A 12-week defined transformation arc. The body changes, the proof gets documented, the case study is the deliverable.**

This is the pathway from the April 14 package — refreshed for the new audience but architecturally unchanged. It works because it has a deadline, a deliverable, and the case-study trade.

For people who say things like:
- "I want a program with a beginning and an end. I'll commit to 12 weeks. After that we'll see."
- "I need to hit a specific outcome by [event/date/photoshoot/wedding]."
- "I'm willing to be a case study if it lowers the price."

### Price
**$3,750 single payment** — OR — **$1,250/month × 3 months** (12 weeks)

Note: This is up from the April 14 founding rate ($1,997) and list rate ($2,997), reflecting the upgraded buyer. The post-proof rate ($4,000) becomes the new founding rate. After 3 founding cohort case studies publish, the price moves to $4,500 for cohort 2.

### Founding Cohort (next 3 buyers only)
- $3,750 paid in full OR $1,250/mo × 3
- Trade: signed publication release form, full intake/midpoint/exit documentation, 10-min exit interview, 3-prompt written testimonial
- Founding clients are explicitly told: "This is the cheapest I'll ever run this. The price moves to $4,500 after the third founding slot fills."

### What's Included (12 weeks)
- 13 weekly 30-min 1:1 video calls (one per week + onboarding call)
- Daily Voxer voice coaching, Mon-Fri 9am-6pm PT
- Custom 12-week TrueCoach training program (3 phases: stabilization → installation → integration)
- Custom nutrition framework (200g protein floor, flexible)
- The 8 Tenets curriculum across all 12 weeks
- Week 6 deep strategy call (90 min)
- Week 12 exit interview + transformation documentation
- Full case study production (your photos + metrics + narrative becomes a published landing page on the site)

### What's NOT Included (same exclusions as Pathway A)

### Risk Reversal
**The Week-6 Reset Guarantee**: If by week 6 there's NO measurable body fat reduction AND no visible strength progress on the 3 benchmark lifts (squat, deadlift, press) — Cooz refunds the second half of the program ($1,875). This is the existing April 14 risk reversal — strong because it's outcome-tied, not "satisfaction-tied" (which is fluffy).

### Strategic Role
- **Case study engine.** Every Ignition client becomes 3 Instagram carousels, 1 reel, 1 landing page case study, 1 PDF case study, and at minimum 1 LinkedIn Lane 3 post. Multi-channel proof distribution.
- **Conversion bridge.** Many Pathway B clients become Pathway A clients at month 13 — they finished the arc, want to maintain, transition to retainer at $1,500/mo. Built-in upsell path.
- **Price anchor for Pathway A.** "$1,500/mo for ongoing — same level of coaching that runs $3,750 in the 12-week package — just spread across no time horizon" is an easy frame.

### When to Pitch Pathway B (vs. A or C)
- Buyer has a specific event or deadline they want to transform for
- Buyer values proof and is willing to be a public case study (or at least private documentation)
- Buyer is in the "skeptical that coaching works" camp and needs the deadline + risk reversal to commit
- Buyer wants a program with a clear arc, not an open-ended relationship

## PATHWAY C — The Resurrection Year

### Positioning
**A 6-month deep integration for high-net-worth professionals who want premium-level transformation work.**

This is the new offer — it didn't exist in the April 14 package. It establishes the ceiling.

For people who say things like:
- "I want the deepest possible coaching. Money isn't the issue. Time and quality are."
- "I'm running a complex life. I need someone who can hold the long arc."
- "I've tried everything. I want to do this once and do it right."

### Price
**$9,000 single payment** — OR — **$1,750/month × 6 months**

Single-payment pricing reflects ~14% discount vs. monthly ($9,000 vs. $10,500 if monthly). This is intentional — high-net-worth professionals prefer single-payment-with-modest-discount over monthly billing logistics.

### What's Included (6 months)
- Weekly 60-minute 1:1 video call (24 calls over 6 months — DOUBLE the duration of Pathway A/B)
- Daily Voxer voice coaching, Mon-Fri 9am-6pm PT, with priority response (within 60 min during business hours)
- Custom 26-week TrueCoach training program (4 phases: stabilization → strength → composition → integration)
- Custom nutrition framework + monthly nutrition recalibration based on outcomes
- The 8 Tenets curriculum + monthly thematic deep-dives
- Quarterly 90-minute strategic deep-dive calls (2 over the 6 months)
- Monthly progress documentation (photos, lifts, measurements, narrative entry)
- **VIP Inclusions** (the differentiators):
  - One in-person training session in Burbank (or Cooz travels to client's city for a $1,500 travel surcharge)
  - One live cooking + meal-prep session via video
  - Quarterly written transformation memo (Cooz's narrative analysis of what's changed and where to point next)
  - First access to any new client-facing tools / curriculum / events Cooz launches in the 6-month window
  - Direct text line for true emergencies (medical, motivation crisis, injury) — no Voxer queue

### What's NOT Included
- Same exclusions as A and B — but with one shift: travel beyond Burbank IS available at the $1,500 surcharge for true VIP integration

### Risk Reversal
**The Quarter-1 Strategic Review Off-Ramp**: At the end of month 3, if the client wants to step down — for any reason — they convert remaining $4,500 (half the program) into either: (a) 3 months of Pathway A at $1,500/mo (extends them another 3 months), or (b) a 50% refund. This gives the client an explicit "graceful exit" without making them feel locked in. Counter-intuitively, this REDUCES early-cancellation behavior because the buyer trusts they have an out.

### Strategic Role
- **Premium ceiling.** Establishes that Cooz operates at $9K-$10K-per-engagement tier. Anchors all conversation about Pathway A/B prices as "reasonable." When someone hears Pathway A is $1,500/mo and Pathway C is $9K, A starts to feel like a bargain.
- **Top-of-network access.** The buyer who pays $9K for 6 months has a network that pays $9K for things. These are the highest-value referral sources.
- **Brand signaling.** Having a $9K offer on the site changes how prospects read the entire offer architecture. Without C, the brand reads "moderate fitness coach." With C, the brand reads "transformation specialist who can hold the highest-stakes work."
- **Case study quality.** Pathway C clients become deeper, more nuanced case studies than Pathway B clients because the arc is twice as long. Higher proof asset value.

### When to Pitch Pathway C (vs. A or B)
- Buyer earns $400K+ HHI and time is the constraint, not money
- Buyer has been through multiple coaches/programs and explicitly wants "the deepest version"
- Buyer is regional (LA / SF / Phoenix) where in-person sessions are realistic without major travel
- Buyer signals long-term thinking: "I want to do this once and do it right"

## How to Present The Three Pathways (the conversation structure)

**During the Triage Audit (the 90-minute sales conversation)**, Cooz does NOT present the pathways as a menu. The pathways are diagnostic — Cooz LISTENS for signals and recommends the right one based on what he heard.

### The Diagnostic Signals

| Signal Cooz Hears | Pathway to Recommend |
|---|---|
| "I've done 3 programs, none of them stuck. I want a coach long-term." | **A** — Retainer |
| "I have a wedding / 50th birthday / photoshoot / specific event in 12 weeks." | **B** — Ignition |
| "I've spent $50K+ on coaches. I want the deepest version. Money is not the constraint." | **C** — Resurrection Year |
| "I'm not sure what I need. Tell me what you'd recommend." | **A** for under-$200K HHI, **B** for $200K-$400K with a deadline, **C** for $400K+ |
| "I want the case study trade. I'll trade documentation for a lower price." | **B** — Ignition founding cohort |
| "I just want to start. I don't want a whole package right now." | **A** — Retainer (3-mo minimum gives the buffer) |

### The Pitch Script (replaces the original Marching Orders pitch when running the Triage Audit)

**Minutes 75-90 — Close or Capture (modified)**:

> "Based on what you've told me, here's what I'd recommend. I run three different pathways depending on what fits the person. For you, I'd point at [Pathway A/B/C], here's why: [tie back to 2-3 specific things they said]. The price is [X]. Want me to walk you through what the first 30 days actually looks like, or do you have questions on the price first?"

**Three pathway anchor lines** (memorize these):

- **A**: "$1,500 a month, 3-month minimum, then month-to-month. The trade-off versus a package is you don't get a deadline. The benefit is I can be your coach for the long haul, not just a sprint."
- **B**: "$3,750 for 12 weeks, or $1,250 a month for 3 months. You get a defined transformation arc with full documentation. If you're willing to be a case study, that's the founding rate. After 3 case studies are published, this moves to $4,500."
- **C**: "$9,000 for 6 months, or $1,750 a month. Double the call time, in-person session, priority response. This is the deepest version of what I do. I take 4 of these per year."

| Objection | Response |

The price moves to $4,500 once the case studies publish." |

## Financial Goal Math (validating that 3-5 clients hits the target)

**Beta phase target**: 3-5 new clients in 60-90 days. Cooz's stated revenue floor (from the April context): $2K-$5K/mo ASAP.

### Scenario 1: Conservative (3 retainers only)
- 3 × $1,500/mo = **$4,500/mo MRR** ($54K annualized)
- Hits the financial floor. Predictable. Low complexity.

### Scenario 2: Mixed (2 retainers + 2 Ignition founding)
- 2 × $1,500/mo retainer = $3,000/mo MRR ($36K annualized)
- 2 × $3,750 Ignition (over 90 days) = $7,500 one-time ($30K annualized at quarterly cadence)
- **Combined: ~$5,500/mo equivalent MRR** ($66K annualized)
- Hits the goal AND produces 2 case studies in 90 days. Optimal blend for beta phase.

### Scenario 3: Premium (1 Resurrection Year + 2 Retainers)
- 1 × $9,000 over 6 months = $1,500/mo equivalent
- 2 × $1,500/mo retainer = $3,000/mo MRR
- **Combined: ~$4,500/mo MRR** ($54K annualized) PLUS premium tier proof
- Establishes ceiling. 1 deeply documented client. Highest case-study quality.

### Scenario 4: Stretch (5 clients across all pathways)
- 1 × $9,000 Resurrection Year ($1,500/mo equivalent)
- 1 × $3,750 Ignition over 90 days ($1,250/mo equivalent)
- 3 × $1,500/mo retainer = $4,500/mo MRR
- **Combined: ~$7,250/mo MRR** ($87K annualized)
- Stretch goal — viable if warm-network DM cadence delivers 5 closes in 60 days.

**My recommendation**: Aim at **Scenario 2** (mixed). It hits the financial goal, produces case studies for the proof shelf, AND gives Cooz operational variety so he doesn't get bored coaching only retainers.
