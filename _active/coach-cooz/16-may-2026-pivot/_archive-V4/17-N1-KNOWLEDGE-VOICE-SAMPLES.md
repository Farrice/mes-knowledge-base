## SAMPLE 1 — Diagnostic-confessional (Lane 2)

**Original opener:**
> "If we drained the business accounts and stripped the title tomorrow, who is the man left standing?
>
> For most highly successful operators, the honest answer is terrifying.
>
> You spent the last decade building a formidable empire. But along the way, you let the money start doing all the heavy lifting."

**What's working:**
- Opens with a question that names the wound directly
- Specific image (drained accounts, stripped title) — concrete, not abstract
- Three short punches in the lead-up
- ⚠️ **V4 KILL**: "Operators" / "highly successful operators" — used here historically, but V4-02 §3A buyer-corpus scan found ZERO real buyers self-describe as "operators." Replace in new content with "high-performing professionals" or "the man left standing" (gendered male-only) or "the version of you that's still in there." DO NOT propagate "operator" forward.
- The reframe is specific: "you let the money start doing all the heavy lifting" — not generic

**Voice ratio:** 60% medium / 40% short. Diagnostic register.

**Original opener:**

**What's working:**

**Voice ratio:** Confessional. Pure scene-setting. Diagnostic comes later in the post.

**Pattern note:** The 3:17 AM opener is a Cooz signature. Variations: "It's 11:00 PM," "It's 3:00 AM," "It's Tuesday afternoon." Always specific. Never generic.
