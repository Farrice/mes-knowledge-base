## THE MASTER PROMPT TEMPLATE

Every Nano Banana prompt for Coach Cooz follows this structure:

```
[SUBJECT — specific person/object + state] [in/with COMPOSITION — angle, framing, distance] [under LIGHTING — source, direction, quality] [in STYLE — reference style or mood] [TECHNICAL — lens, aspect ratio, photorealism/illustration] — [RESTRICTIONS — what to avoid]
```

The 5 elements + restrictions are non-negotiable. Without all 6, you get generic output.

### Brand visual language constants (apply to every prompt)

- **Color**: Black, white, charcoal — single accent (slate blue OR burnt orange, pick one and stay)
- **Mood**: Editorial, premium, hardware-coded, NOT influencer-instagram
- **Photography style references**: Patagonia ambassador, NYT Magazine portrait, Nike SB campaign, Field Notes catalog
- **Always exclude**: tank tops (visual mismatch with the corporate copy register), gym selfies, supplement product placement, motivational text overlays, bright/saturated colors, generic stock photo aesthetic

## USE CASE A — LINKEDIN BANNER / HERO IMAGE

### Purpose
1584 × 396 px banner at the top of LinkedIn profile. First visual impression. Carries the bridge message.

### Master template

```
Editorial banner-format composition: [SUBJECT — Cooz mid-action in coaching environment OR atmospheric environmental shot] in a [SETTING — dark gym, urban studio, modern athletic space] [COMPOSITION — half-frame composition with subject occupying left third, right two-thirds reserved for text overlay], [LIGHTING — single-source natural window light from camera-left creating dramatic shadow], in the style of a [STYLE — Patagonia ambassador feature / NYT Magazine portrait], shot on 35mm lens, 4:1 aspect ratio (LinkedIn banner format 1584x396), photorealistic — NO motivational text in the image (text added separately), NO bright colors, NO instagram-influencer aesthetic, NO supplement branding

(After generating, add text overlay separately in Canva: "YOU OPTIMIZED EVERYTHING / EXCEPT THE OPERATOR." in bold sans-serif white text positioned right two-thirds.)
```

### Variants

**A1 — Portrait-led banner**:
> "Editorial environmental portrait of a 30-something male coach (mid-action with a barbell or kettlebell) in a dark grey gym, half-frame composition with subject occupying left third, single-source natural window light from camera-left, in the style of a Patagonia ambassador feature, 4:1 aspect ratio, photorealistic — NO tank top, dark long-sleeve athletic tee, NO bright colors, NO motivational text overlay (added separately)"

**A2 — Atmospheric/no-portrait banner**:
> "Atmospheric photograph of a dark, empty gym at dawn, single barbell on the rack, soft natural light through east-facing windows, low contrast moody color grade, half-frame composition with subject elements on the right two-thirds and clean dark space on the left third for text overlay, 4:1 aspect ratio, photorealistic — NO color, monochrome charcoal palette, NO clutter, NO equipment branding"

**A3 — Coaching-in-action banner**:
> "Editorial photograph of a male coach giving form correction to a client mid-deadlift, shot from the side at hip-height, both subjects in athletic apparel against a dark gym backdrop, single-source overhead industrial lighting creating warm shadow, in the style of a New York Times Magazine athletic profile, 4:1 aspect ratio, photorealistic, shallow depth of field — NO smiling, focused expressions only, NO bright colors, NO branded equipment"

## ITERATION CHEAT SHEET (when first generation isn't right)

| Problem | Fix prompt |
|---------|-----------|
| Too bright / colorful | "Reduce saturation. Pull toward charcoal/black/white only with single slate-blue accent." |
| Too instagram-influencer | "Make it more editorial, less influencer. Reference Patagonia ambassador or NYT Magazine portrait styling." |
| Subject smiling | "Change expression to focused, mid-thought, not smiling. Documentary realism, not catalog modeling." |
| Wrong attire | "Replace tank top with dark long-sleeve athletic crew or fitted dark t-shirt." |
| Cluttered background | "Simplify background. Single-color dark backdrop or environmental dark gym only." |
| Looks AI-generated | "Add photorealistic skin texture, natural imperfection, real-environment lighting variation. Documentary photographic realism." |
| Hands look weird | "Reframe to remove hands from frame OR position subject's hands behind a piece of equipment / on equipment." |
| Text overlay garbled | "Remove text from the image generation. Generate the image without text. Add text in Canva separately." |
