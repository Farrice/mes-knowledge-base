## THE OUTBOUND LINK RULE (the single most important behavior change)

LinkedIn's algorithm punishes outbound links in main posts. Your last 10 posts all had outbound links. Your reach is suppressed because of it.

**New rule: NO outbound links in the body of the post. Period.**

Where the link goes:
- First comment, posted by you, immediately after publishing
