### Month-by-Month Progression

**Month 1 (Weeks 1-4): SIGNAL ESTABLISHMENT**
- Objective: Give the AI 12+ posts within declared lanes so it can build a topic profile
- Lane 1 emphasis: 70% of posts (over-index on primary while signal is being established)
- Cold-start posting cadence: 2 posts/week (weeks 1-2), 3 posts/week (weeks 3-4)
- Key Metric: Are impressions coming from OUTSIDE existing 837 connections?
- Watchpoint: First 3-5 posts will have low impressions due to cold-start penalty. Don't pivot.

**Month 2 (Weeks 5-8): DEPTH COMPOUNDING**
- Objective: AI starts proactively matching Cooz's content to interest-matched audiences (not just network)
- Distribution: Lane 1 (55%), Lane 2 (30%), Lane 3 (15%) — full target distribution
- Cadence: 4 posts/week
- Key Metric: New followers who match the new ICP (entrepreneurs/founders/professionals) but have NO mutual connections with Cooz
- Watchpoint: Resist the urge to add "spiritual" or "men's psychology" posts even if a thought feels timely. Save them for the book/podcast.

**Month 3 (Weeks 9-13): AUTHORITY RECOGNITION**
- Objective: AI considers Cooz a reliable source for "body transformation for high performers"
- Evidence: Impressions per post trending upward despite same posting volume
- Deploy: Save-worthy heavy weeks (3 of 4 posts in Lane 1, all save-architected)
- Add: Brian's case study published live + 3 derivative posts off it (Lane 3 saturation)
- Key Metric: Save-to-like ratio improving; posts reaching 3x+ follower count (so 2,500+ impressions on Cooz's 837 base)

## Phase 6: Anti-Scatter Guardrails

### Pre-publish gate (every post, no exceptions):

1. **Which of my 3 lanes does this serve?**
   - Lane 1 (Body-First OS) → ship
   - Lane 2 (Transformation Architecture) → ship
   - Lane 3 (Case Studies & Proof) → ship
   - "None" → don't publish, save for book/podcast/private channel
   - "Sort of fits Lane X" → reframe to clearly live in the lane, or save it

2. **Does the first 50 words contain 3+ topic anchors from this lane?**
   - Yes → ship
   - No → rewrite the open

3. **Is this save-worthy or narrative?**
   - Save-worthy → great for Mon/Fri/Tue
   - Narrative → reserve for Sun (Lane 2) — and even then, narrative must end with a teachable pattern, not a feeling

### The Temptation List (off-lane content Cooz will be tempted to post — flagged + redirected)

| Temptation | Why It Feels Right | Why It's Off-Lane | Alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saturn return / spiritual transformation reflection | It's authentic to Cooz, deeply held | Scatter signal — AI builds "spiritual coach" profile, blocks fitness reach | The book. The podcast. NOT LinkedIn during this 90 days. |
| Father wounds / men's psychology / dormant masculine | It's where Cooz's voice DNA was forged | Gender-exclusive + topical scatter | Fold into Lane 2 ONLY when discussing the inherited critic during transformation work. Reframe so the lane is "transformation architecture," gender-inclusive. |
