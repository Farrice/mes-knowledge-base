## PATHWAY A — IN-PERSON RESURRECTION

### Who this is for
The local Burbank/LA professional who wants Cooz physically in the gym with them. Either gender. They can drive to North Hollywood 4x/week. They want a coach in the room, not a coach on a screen.

The just-signed female client at a small business is the prototype. She's the proof this works.

### What's included
- **In-person training, 4x/week** — Cooz physically present, hands-on form correction, real-time intensity calibration
- **Nutrition coaching** — starts where they are, daily protein floor, evolves into custom framework as the client adapts
- **Daily text access** — questions, accountability, real-time problem-solving
- **Weekly check-ins via text** — nutrition-focused, progress audit
- **Life/mindset coaching during sessions** — they're already in the room, the coaching extends naturally
- **Workout tracking through TrueCoach**

### What it costs
- **Founding rate (first 3 in-person clients): $1,275/mo**
- **Standing rate (after founding cohort fills): $1,500/mo**
- 30-day notice to cancel after the first 30 days

### The math (for Cooz)
- $1,500/mo × 17 sessions/mo = $88/session — middle of LA market
- 5 in-person clients at $1,500 = $7,500/mo MRR base
- Hard cap: ~5 clients (4x/week × 5 = 20 sessions/week, ~half of Cooz's training time)

### The pitch
> "It's $1,500 a month. I'm in the gym with you four times a week. I see what your body's actually doing, not what you tell me it's doing on a video call. The work happens in the room. Most people can't get this — I take five clients local. If you're in the LA area and can make it to North Hollywood, this is the one I'd point you at."

### When to recommend it
- Client lives within 30 minutes of North Hollywood
- They've tried remote coaching and bounced
- They want hands-on form coaching
- They learn better in-person than on video

## PATHWAY B — REMOTE RESURRECTION

### Who this is for
The professional anywhere in the country who wants the daily-presence coaching but can't (or doesn't want to) drive to a gym. They run their own training session, but Cooz is in their pocket daily.

This is the SCALABLE tier. Not capped by Cooz's physical hours.

### What's included
- **Weekly 30-min 1:1 video call** (4 calls/month)
- **Daily Voxer voice coaching, Mon-Fri 9am-6pm PT** — not text. Voice. The difference matters.
- **Custom training program in TrueCoach** (updated monthly based on progress)
- **Custom nutrition framework** (200g protein floor, flexible architecture, NOT a meal plan)
- **The 8 Tenets curriculum** at the client's pace
- **Quarterly 60-min strategic deep-dive call**
- **Quarterly progress documentation** (photos, lifts, measurements)

### What it costs
- **Founding rate (first 3 remote clients): $1,500/mo**
- **Standing rate (after founding cohort fills): $2,000/mo**
- 30-day notice to cancel after the first 30 days

### The math (for Cooz)
- $2,000/mo × 5 clients = $10,000/mo MRR
- Plus the in-person base = $17,500/mo MRR ceiling on retainers alone
- Capacity: 7-10 clients depending on Voxer load tolerance

### The pitch
> "Two thousand a month. I'm in your pocket every weekday. We do a 30-minute call every week. You get a custom training program, a nutrition framework that fits how you actually live, and the 8 Tenets curriculum at your pace. The deal is daily voice coaching — when you have a question Tuesday at 2pm about whether to push through or rest, I'm there. Not next week. Now."

### When to recommend it
- Client is outside the LA area
- They prefer remote coaching for schedule flexibility
- They're already a regular trainer (don't need form-correction-in-room)
- They want daily voice presence > weekly in-person

## PATHWAY C — THE IGNITION (12-WEEK PACKAGED)

### Who this is for
The professional who wants a defined transformation arc with a deadline, a deliverable, and the case-study trade. Either local or remote. They want to commit to 12 weeks, hit a specific outcome, and walk away with documented proof.

This is the CASE STUDY ENGINE. Every Ignition client becomes published proof — landing page, Instagram carousel, reel, PDF, LinkedIn case study. Multi-channel proof distribution.

### What's included
- **12 weeks of either Pathway A (in-person) or Pathway B (remote) delivery** — client chooses
- **Intake protocol**: 4 baseline photos, weight + tape measurements, 3 benchmark lifts, 6-question intake form, 2-min video selfie, signed publication release
- **Week 6 mid-point**: progress photos + updated metrics + 1-min voice memo reflection
- **Week 12 exit**: final photos + final metrics + 10-min exit interview + written testimonial (3-prompt sheet)
- **Full case study production**: photos + metrics + 1,000-word narrative + pull quote + 90-sec video clip become a landing page on coachcooz.com

### What it costs
- **Founding rate (first 3 case study cohort): $3,500** paid in full OR $1,200/mo × 3 months
- **Post-proof rate (after 3 published case studies): $4,500** paid in full OR $1,550/mo × 3 months

### The math (for Cooz)
- 3 founding Ignitions at $3,500 = $10,500 cash in 90 days
- After case studies publish: every Ignition is $4,500 = ~$1,500/mo equivalent
- The case study assets compound forever — one Ignition becomes 5+ pieces of marketing collateral

### The pitch
> "Thirty-five hundred for twelve weeks if you're willing to be one of my first three case studies. The trade is I document the transformation — photos, metrics, a 1,000-word narrative, a video clip. Your story becomes the proof shelf. After three case studies publish, the price moves to $4,500. The cheapest I'll ever run this is right now."

### When to recommend it
- Client has a specific event/deadline (wedding, photoshoot, 50th birthday)
- Client values the case study trade (they want to be on the wall)
- Client is in the "skeptical that coaching works" camp and needs the deadline + risk reversal
- Client wants a defined arc with a clear endpoint

### Risk reversal
**Week 6 reset guarantee**: If by week 6 there's NO measurable body fat reduction AND no visible strength progress on the 3 benchmark lifts (squat, deadlift, press) — Cooz refunds the second half ($1,750 founding / $2,250 list).

## THE TRIAGE AUDIT (the front door for ALL pathways)

The 90-min Triage Audit is the diagnostic conversation that recommends WHICH pathway fits. Cooz doesn't pitch a menu. He listens, then recommends.

### The diagnostic flowchart

| Signal Cooz hears | Pathway to recommend |
|---|---|
| "I want a coach IN the room with me." + "I'm local LA." | **A** — In-Person |
| "I'm in [non-LA city]." OR "I prefer remote scheduling." | **B** — Remote |
| "I have a [specific deadline/event] in 12 weeks." OR "I want to be a case study." | **C** — Ignition |
| "Money isn't the issue. Time and quality are." OR "I've spent $50K+ on coaches." OR "I want the deepest version." | **D** — ESA |
| "I'm not sure what I need. Just tell me." | Default to **B** if remote, **A** if local; only suggest **D** if Cooz hears specific high-tier signals |

### The Triage Audit close

**Minutes 75-90 of the 90-min call**:

> "Based on what you've told me, here's what I'd recommend. I run four different pathways depending on what fits the person. For you, I'd point at [Pathway A/B/C/D], here's why: [tie back to 2-3 specific things they said]. The price is [X]. Want me to walk you through what the first 30 days actually looks like, or do you have questions on the price first?"
