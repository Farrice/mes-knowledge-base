## The 3 Highest-Leverage Fixes

### Fix #1: Resolve the audience contradiction (foundational)

**Problem**: The package conflates two pivots:
- **Small pivot** (validated): Gender expansion + slight upmarket from blue-collar to professional. The just-signed client (high-up at small business, $1,200/mo) supports this.
- **Large pivot** (unvalidated): Upmarket to Hampton-tier founders and Pulitzer-tier journalists. The Anne Marie/Anthony anchors represent this. The headline targets this. Pricing is calibrated to this.

The voice DNA was built for the small pivot. The downstream artifacts target the large pivot. Hidden assumption: Cooz's stated direction was treated as ground truth equal in weight to the signed-client signal. It isn't. Stated direction from a client mid-pivot is desire, not evidence.

**Fix**: Anchor to the just-signed client's actual archetype.
- Drop Anne Marie / Anthony as anchor buyers — keep them as ILLUSTRATION examples only
- Re-narrow income envelope to $150-$300K HHI (corporate professional, small-to-mid business owner)
- Lower Pathway A floor to **$1,200/mo** (matches validated price), Pathway B to $3,000, Pathway C to $7,500 OR remove it entirely
- Resurrection Year as currently scoped is a vanity ceiling without buyer-side evidence

### Fix #2: Fix the headline failure

**Problem**: `05-LINKEDIN-PROFILE-REBUILD.md` recommended headline:

> *"Body-first transformation for entrepreneurs, founders & professionals | I rebuild the operator most people optimize around"*

Tries to do four things at once: name niche, name 3 audience segments, name differentiator, signal buyer wound. Pattern 20 (pain + for whom + proof) wants ONE sharp thing per slot. This is comprehensive, not magnetic. The package's own anchor buyer (Anne Marie) wouldn't click it because "entrepreneurs, founders & professionals" skews male.

**Fix**: Lead with the buyer's actual self-concept moment, not Cooz's category. Test 2-3 alternates:
- *"For high-functioning professionals running on legacy infrastructure | Body-first 1-on-1 coaching"*
- *"The body that built this is breaking. I rebuild it. | 1-on-1 transformation coaching for professionals."*

These maintain topic anchors while leading with recognition. Current version leads with category — the LinkedIn coach failure mode.

### Fix #3: Surface the case study dependency as risk, not footnote

**Problem**: Lane 3 (15% of cadence) AND Featured Slot 2 (1 of 3 prime profile slots) both depend on Brian's case study being ready. Brian was an April 14 Week 4 deliverable. There's NO status confirmation in this May 2026 package. If Brian's isn't ready or doesn't exist:
- Lane 3 runs on substitute "industry case study commentary"
- Featured Slot 2 has placeholder copy
- Profile launches with visibly under-delivered proof at the moment Cooz needs MAXIMUM proof signal

The cold-start re-entry window is also when Featured slots get the most eyeballs.

**Fix**: Add a Day-1 status check to the START HERE Day 2 call:
- *"Is Brian's case study production complete? If not, by what specific date will it ship?"*
- Make Lane 3 cadence conditional on that answer
- If Brian's isn't ready by Week 3, Lane 3 reduces to 0% and Lane 1 absorbs the gap
- Don't post placeholder case study content during the rebuild window

## The 1 Deepest Premise Crack

**The 90-day lane discipline assumes Cooz can suppress 30%+ of his content identity for 90 consecutive days through willpower alone — and the package has no enforcement mechanism, only an honor-system "temptation list."**

The temptations named correctly: Saturn return, father wounds, men's psychology, business advice, McBroom appreciation, behind-the-scenes, crisis recovery. These are seven distinct content categories — and they map precisely to where Cooz's voice DNA lives most natively.

The package's solution is "send it to the book/podcast" — but neither the book nor the podcast has any production cadence specified. There's no holding chamber. There's no review gate. There's only Cooz, on Day 22 of no Saturn return content, with a thought he wants to share, and a list telling him to send it elsewhere.

`01-LINKEDIN-ALGORITHM-AUDIT.md` Section "Anti-Pattern Check" acknowledges this directly: *"The most common failure mode of this rebuild: Cooz reads this audit, agrees with everything, and then publishes a post tomorrow because 'I have good content I want to share.'"* The package names the failure mode and then provides no structural prevention — only the discipline reminder.

**Mitigation needed**: a pre-publish gate that's NOT Cooz's own discipline. Either:
- Farrice voice-checks every post for full 90 days (currently set to 4 weeks per `00-START-HERE.md`)
- OR a draft-and-hold queue where off-lane content goes for 7 days before it can ship

Both are real systems. "Send it to the book" is not a system.

**The Bridge Message itself — *"You optimized everything except the operator"* — would survive even if everything else broke.**

It's the rare deliverable element that satisfies all five axes simultaneously: premise integrity (names a real wound), evidence (matches verified vocabulary), voice alignment (hardware-coded, gender-neutral, lands in <10 words), structural soundness (works as headline / banner / About opener / DM hook), market resilience (would land on Anne Marie's tab, Anthony's Hampton thread, and the just-signed client's mental model equally).

If Cooz killed every other element of this package and kept only the bridge message and the Lane 1 commitment, the rebuild would still be net-positive. The line is durable because it does the hardest job in copy: it names a sophisticated buyer's specific wound in their own vocabulary without flattering them and without shaming them.

**This is the load-bearing asset of the package. Everything else is scaffolding around this sentence.**
