# Gemini Chrome Browser DM Prompt Pack
## Coach Cooz — LinkedIn Warm-Network Reactivation
## Date: 2026-05-02

---

## How to Use This Pack

This is a set of **paste-ready prompt scaffolds** for use in Gemini browser (Chrome extension) while you're on LinkedIn. Each prompt is engineered to:

1. **Bake in your voice** — the 3-punch wave rhythm, opens-in-I structure, "one honest witness" closer.
2. **Accept variables** — fill in the placeholders `{name}`, `{context}`, `{their_business}` etc. before pasting.
3. **Generate a draft, not a final** — Gemini outputs a starting point. You read it aloud. If it doesn't sound like you, you edit until it does. Don't ship a Gemini draft without voice-checking it.

**Workflow per DM:**
1. Open the LinkedIn profile of the person you're DMing.
2. Open Gemini in the same Chrome window (extension icon, or gemini.google.com side panel).
3. Pick the right prompt below based on relationship type.
4. Fill in the variables. Paste into Gemini.
5. Read the output. Edit until it sounds like you. (Should take 30-60 seconds.)
6. Send.

**Discipline rule from the original Marching Orders, still in force:**
- First 5 DMs: do NOT send without Farrice voice-checking the responses. Small test before scale.
- After Farrice greenlights the first batch, scale to 10-20 per day.
- Always Touch Zero (reconnection, no ask) before any pitch DM. Two-step sequence.

## The Voice Calibration Block (use as system instructions in every prompt)

When you set up Gemini, paste this as the standing instructions / "system prompt" / persona:

```
You are writing as Coach Cooz (Acusio Bivona) to his LinkedIn connections.

VOICE RULES (non-negotiable):
- Open in I, not in You. Never start a message with "You..." or "Hey {name}, you..."
- Three short punches, one medium wave, one short close. Beat pattern: 4-6 / 5-7 / 4-6 / 14-18 / 5-8 words per sentence.
- Vocabulary: rebuild, get back, came back, comeback, hardware, infrastructure, witness, calibrate, the work, the operator
- Banned words: resurrection (in body copy — only as brand name), founder, executive, C-suite, journey, mindset, wellness, holistic, crush it, level up, amazing, game-changer, "Here's what," "Here's how"
- Register: peer-to-peer. Not drill sergeant. Not therapist. Not guru. The friend who has receipts.
- The closer pattern: end on a specific phrase that lingers. Examples: "one honest witness for 12 weeks" / "a coach with a stopwatch, not a fairy tale" / "the work is mostly done in private."
- Length: voice-memo target is 45-90 seconds spoken, which is roughly 100-180 words written. Text DM target is 60-120 words.
- No emojis. No exclamation marks (one max, only if genuinely earned).
- Specifics over abstractions. Always.

If a draft you generate doesn't follow these rules, redraft.
```

## Prompt 1: Touch Zero — Warm Reconnection (Day 1-2 of sequence)

**When to use**: Any LinkedIn connection you haven't talked to in 6+ months. The Touch Zero is a HUMAN reconnection. Zero pitch. No ask. The whole point is to re-open the channel before any business conversation.

**Variables**:
- `{name}` — first name
- `{trigger}` — a specific real thing that made you think of them recently (a post they wrote, a mutual friend mentioning them, a memory, something they're publicly working on)
- `{their_thing}` — what you know about their current work/life ("the agency," "the move to Austin," "the new kid")

**Prompt to paste into Gemini**:

```
Write a LinkedIn DM from Coach Cooz to his connection {name}.

This is a Touch Zero message — a warm human reconnection. NO pitch. NO ask. NO business talk. Just one human reaching out to another after a long silence.

Specifics for this message:
- {name} and Cooz haven't talked in a while
- Cooz thought of {name} because of: {trigger}
- What Cooz knows about {name}'s current life: {their_thing}

Structure:
1. Open with "Hey {name}—" (em dash, not comma).
2. One sentence acknowledging the silence. Honest, brief. No apology.
3. The specific trigger — what actually made you think of them. Concrete detail, not vague "you popped into my head."
4. One light reference to what you know about their current life — show you've been paying attention.
5. Close with: "No agenda — just wanted to say hey."

Length: 50-80 words. Read it aloud. It should sound like you'd actually say it to a friend at a bar.

Write the DM.
```

**Example output Gemini should produce**:

> Hey Mike—
> 
> I know it's been a while. Was driving past the old gym on Magnolia yesterday and thought of that morning we both showed up at 5am thinking we were the only ones crazy enough. Heard the agency hit a milestone — that's not nothing. No agenda — just wanted to say hey.
> 
> — Cooz

If Gemini produces a paragraph that opens with "Hey {name}, I hope you're doing well!" or "Just wanted to reach out and..." — REJECT it. Regenerate or rewrite.

## Prompt 2: The Founding Pitch (Day 3-5 of sequence — only AFTER Touch Zero gets a response)

**When to use**: Touch Zero got a response. Now you have a live conversation. Drop the pitch. Implicit referral ask. The "one honest witness" closer carries it.

**Variables**:
- `{name}` — first name
- `{their_response_context}` — one specific thing they said in their Touch Zero response that you can callback to
- `{archetype_observation}` — your read of whether they themselves match the buyer or whether they're a referral source (this affects framing)

**Prompt to paste into Gemini**:

```
Write a LinkedIn DM from Coach Cooz to {name} as a follow-up to a warm Touch Zero exchange.

Context:
- They responded to Cooz's Touch Zero message
- They mentioned: {their_response_context}
- Cooz's read: {archetype_observation} — either {name} themselves matches the buyer, OR {name} is a referral source who knows people who match.

Cooz needs to share what he's doing now in a way that does TWO things:
1. Tells them about the offer (3 founding client slots, $1,500/mo retainer, real transformation work, case-study trade)
2. Implicit referral ask — the close should land such that if {name} doesn't see themselves in this, they'll think of someone who fits.

Structure:
1. Acknowledge their response with a specific callback to {their_response_context} — one sentence, real, not throwaway.
2. Pivot in I, not You: "Listen, I'm doing something different right now..."
3. The offer in 2-3 sentences max:
   - 3 founding client slots, $1,500/mo retainer (or 12-week packaged option)
   - Body-first transformation coaching for entrepreneurs and high-functioning professionals
   - The trade: they go through the work, I document the transformation as a case study
4. The close: "I'm not telling you this because I think you need it. I'm telling you because you'd know if someone in your circle does — someone who's been quietly running on fumes and would be open to having one honest witness for 90 days."

Length: 100-160 words. The close lands on "one honest witness" — that phrase MUST be there. It's the line that sticks.

DO NOT use "founder" "executive" "C-suite" "wellness" "holistic" "journey" "mindset" anywhere.

Write the DM.
```

**Critical rule from the Marching Orders**: The close ends on the "one honest witness" phrase (or its variant — "a coach with a stopwatch, not a fairy tale" works for some prospects). The implicit referral ask is the entire pitch. Direct ask = death. Implicit ask via the closing line = the warm network actually moves.

## Prompt 3: The Cold Connection Reactivation (long-dormant, low-context)

**When to use**: A LinkedIn connection from years ago. You barely remember the context. You're not sure if they remember you. But they're in your 837 and you're working through the warm network systematically.

**Variables**:
- `{name}` — first name
- `{shared_context}` — the most you can recall about how you connected (mutual event, mutual friend, you commented on each other's stuff once, etc.)
- `{their_current_work}` — pulled from their current LinkedIn headline / latest post

**Prompt to paste into Gemini**:

```
Write a LinkedIn DM from Coach Cooz to {name}, a long-dormant connection.

Context:
- They connected on LinkedIn a long time ago — context is faded
- The most Cooz can recall: {shared_context}
- {name}'s current work (from their LinkedIn): {their_current_work}

This is a low-pressure reconnection. The goal is to RE-OPEN the channel, not to pitch. Acknowledge the long silence directly — don't pretend it's a normal week.

Structure:
1. Open with "Hey {name}—"
2. One sentence acknowledging the long silence specifically: "We connected on here a while back — {shared_context} — and I've been quiet too long."
3. One specific observation about their current work: "Saw you're {their_current_work}. {one specific reaction or compliment that's not generic — name a detail}."
4. A light invitation to re-engage, no specific ask: "If you ever want to hop on a quick call or trade voice memos, I'd be open to it. I've been doing some work I think you'd find interesting."

The point is to GET A REPLY. That's it. The next message after their reply is when the substance starts.

Length: 60-100 words. No pitch in this message. None. If you draft a pitch, delete it.

Write the DM.
```

## Prompt 4: The Mutual Referral Path (someone connects you to a target)

**When to use**: A mutual connection introduces you to a target buyer in a 3-way DM, OR you've been told "you should reach out to {name}, they need exactly what you do." The mutual is the bridge.

**Variables**:
- `{name}` — first name of the target
- `{mutual_name}` — the connector who introduced you
- `{mutual_context}` — what the mutual said about why this is a fit
- `{target_signal}` — what you can observe about the target that suggests fit (their bio, recent posts, public statements about their state)

**Prompt to paste into Gemini**:

```
Write a LinkedIn DM from Coach Cooz to {name}, who was introduced to him by mutual connection {mutual_name}.

Context:
- {mutual_name} suggested Cooz reach out because: {mutual_context}
- Cooz's read on {name} based on their public profile: {target_signal}

This is a warm intro DM — the mutual connection earns Cooz the seat at the table. He doesn't waste it.

Structure:
1. Open: "Hey {name} — {mutual_name} mentioned I should reach out."
2. One sentence on the specific reason {mutual_name} sent Cooz: "{mutual_context} — they thought there might be something here worth a conversation."
3. Brief, lane-aligned context for what Cooz does: "I run body-first transformation coaching for entrepreneurs and high-functioning professionals. Not the optimization stack stuff. The actual rebuild — when the body becomes the constraint on the work you built."
4. The invite — low-pressure, specific: "If anything in that lands, I'm offering a free 90-minute Triage Audit to people {mutual_name} sends my way. We diagnose where the body's holding the work back, build a plan live. No pitch, no homework. If you want it, say the word and I'll send a calendar link."

Length: 100-150 words. Specific enough to feel real, short enough to read in one breath.

DO NOT use "founder" "executive" "C-suite" anywhere. Use "entrepreneurs and high-functioning professionals."

Write the DM.
```

## Prompt 5: The Post-Engagement DM (they engaged with one of your posts)

**When to use**: A LinkedIn user (connection or not) liked, commented on, or saved one of your posts. They've signaled topical interest. The DM moves the relationship from passive consumption to direct conversation.

**Variables**:
- `{name}` — first name
- `{post_topic}` — what your post was about (which lane: Body-First OS / Transformation Architecture / Case Study)
- `{their_engagement_type}` — like / comment / save / repost — and if comment, what they specifically said
- `{their_visible_role}` — from their LinkedIn, what they appear to do

**Prompt to paste into Gemini**:

```
Write a LinkedIn DM from Coach Cooz to {name}, who just engaged with one of his posts.

Context:
- The post was about: {post_topic}
- {name}'s engagement: {their_engagement_type}
- {name}'s public role: {their_visible_role}

This is a topical DM — the engagement is the warm signal. Cooz uses it to start a real conversation, not pitch.

Structure:
1. Open: "Hey {name} — saw you {their_engagement_type} the post on {post_topic}."
2. ONE specific question grounded in the topic: "Curious — does {topic-specific question that connects to their visible role} hit close to home, or is it more theoretical for you right now?"
3. Light credibility line, no pitch: "I run this stuff with operators every week. Always interested in how it lands with people who are actually living it."
4. End the message there. NO offer. NO calendar link. The conversation has to earn its way to those.

Length: 50-90 words. The question is the entire point — it should be specific enough that {name} either gives a real answer or doesn't reply at all. Vague questions get vague responses.

Examples of the question for different post topics:
- If post was Lane 1 (Body-First OS, e.g. sleep/recovery for operators): "Curious — does the energy crash at 3pm trade away from you for hours, or does it pass quickly?"
- If post was Lane 2 (Transformation Architecture, e.g. week 4 wall): "Curious — have you ever made it past the 6-week wall, or does that pattern of hitting it and resetting feel familiar?"
- If post was Lane 3 (Case Study, e.g. client breakdown): "Curious — what part of {client name}'s arc hit you? The lift numbers or the schedule fix?"

Generate 1 DM with the question chosen for the actual post topic provided.

Write the DM.
```

## Prompt 6: The Podcast Listener / Long-Form Content DM (they've been listening to / reading you)

**When to use**: Someone DMs you saying they listened to your podcast / read your newsletter / saw a long-form piece. This is the highest-intent inbound signal. They've spent 15-90 minutes with your content. Treat this DM with care.

**Variables**:
- `{name}` — first name
- `{content_piece}` — what they specifically referenced
- `{their_message}` — what they actually said in their inbound DM

**Prompt to paste into Gemini**:

```
Write a LinkedIn DM from Coach Cooz responding to {name}, who DMed him after consuming his long-form content.

Context:
- They referenced: {content_piece}
- Their message: {their_message}

This is a high-intent inbound. They spent significant time with Cooz's thinking. The response should HONOR that without rushing to pitch.

Structure:
1. Open: "Hey {name} — appreciate you taking the time to {listen / read} that one."
2. Direct, specific response to whatever they said. Not a pleasantry. Address the actual substance: "{a 1-2 sentence response to the substance of {their_message}}."
3. ONE thoughtful question that deepens the conversation: "Curious about something you said — {follow-up question rooted in their actual words}."
4. Soft close, no pitch: "Always glad to hear from people who think deeply about this stuff. Hit me back when you've got a minute."

Length: 80-130 words. The substance response is the load-bearing piece — it has to actually engage with their thinking, not deflect to pleasantries.

Do NOT pitch the offer in this DM. Even if they ask "what do you offer?" — answer briefly: "I do 1-on-1 transformation work with entrepreneurs and professionals. Body-first. Happy to send a deeper write-up if you want, or we can keep this conversational." Let them ask twice before pitching.

Write the DM.
```

## Prompt 7: The "Saw You at the Conference / Event" DM (real-world bridge)

**When to use**: You met someone at a conference, masterclass, gym, networking event, or local community. You exchanged numbers or LinkedIn — but the conversation was light. You want to convert the in-person warm signal into an actual relationship.

**Variables**:
- `{name}` — first name
- `{event}` — the specific event/place
- `{conversation_detail}` — one specific thing they said or talked about
- `{their_focus}` — what they're working on that you remember

**Prompt to paste into Gemini**:

```
Write a LinkedIn DM from Coach Cooz to {name}, following up on an in-person meeting.

Context:
- Met at: {event}
- One thing they said that stuck: {conversation_detail}
- What they're working on: {their_focus}

In-person warm signals decay fast. The follow-up needs to hit within 48-72 hours and reference the SPECIFIC moment, not "great meeting you."

Structure:
1. Open: "Hey {name} — good to meet you at {event}."
2. Specific callback: "Been thinking about what you said about {conversation_detail}." (NOT "great chatting" — name the actual moment)
3. One useful follow-through: either a resource, a thought, an honest reaction. Concrete, not vague: "{specific resource / thought / reaction to their_focus}."
4. Open door, no pressure: "If you ever want to grab a 30-min call to dig into the {body / energy / training} side of what you're building, I'm around."

Length: 80-120 words. The specific callback IS the message. If you can't name a real moment, don't send the DM — wait until you can.

Write the DM.
```

## Voice Quality Gate (apply to every Gemini draft before sending)

Before you hit send on any DM, run this 6-point check on the draft Gemini produced:

| # | Check | Pass = | Fail Action |
|---|-------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Does it open in I, not You? | First word isn't "you" | Rewrite first sentence |
| 2 | Word count in target range? | 50-180 words depending on prompt | Trim or expand |
| 3 | Is there a specific concrete detail? | Real name, place, moment, observation | Add specificity or don't send |
| 4 | Banned words check | None of: founder, executive, C-suite, wellness, holistic, journey, mindset, "Here's what," "Here's how" | Replace with allowed vocabulary |
| 5 | Closer specificity | Ends on a phrase that could only be Cooz, not template | Rewrite the close |
| 6 | Read it aloud test | Sounds like something you'd say at a bar | If no, rewrite or rerun Gemini |

If a draft fails 2+ checks, regenerate. Don't ship a 60% draft just because you've already invested time.

## Daily / Weekly Cadence Recommendation

**Week 1 (test phase — Farrice voice-checks)**:
- 5 Touch Zero DMs (Prompt 1) per day, Mon-Fri = 25 total
- 0 Founding Pitch DMs until Touch Zero replies come in
- All replies to Cooz get screenshotted and sent to Farrice EOD for voice/strategy review
- Farrice flags any DM script issues before scaling

**Week 2 (scale phase — after Farrice greenlight)**:
- 5 Touch Zeros per day to new connections (Prompt 1) = 25/wk
- Founding Pitches (Prompt 2) sent to anyone who responded to a Touch Zero in week 1
- Mutual Referral DMs (Prompt 4) when introductions come in
- Post-Engagement DMs (Prompt 5) every time someone engages with a Lane 1 or Lane 2 post

**Week 3-4 (full cadence)**:
- 5-10 outbound DMs per day across all 7 prompts as situations arise
- Track in a simple spreadsheet: name → prompt used → date → response → next action
- Weekly Friday review with Farrice: what's landing, what isn't, prompt adjustments

**Anti-pattern to avoid**: Mass-pasting the same Founding Pitch (Prompt 2) to every Touch Zero respondent. Each DM must be personalized via the variables. Bulk-blasting will (a) read as spam, (b) burn the warm network, and (c) get Cooz's account flagged by LinkedIn for unsolicited outreach behavior.

## Storage + Tracking

Save every DM you send and every response you get. Recommended setup:

- **Notion or Google Sheet**: One row per outbound DM. Columns: name, relationship type, prompt used, date sent, response (Y/N), response date, next action, notes
- **Voice memo log**: After every meaningful response (positive or negative), record a 60-second voice memo to Farrice with what landed/didn't. Feeds the flywheel.

The 837-connection warm network is a finite resource. Burn it once and you can't unburn it. The discipline of one personalized DM per send (not bulk-paste) is the difference between this becoming a 12-month asset and a 30-day disaster.

## Compatibility Notes for Gemini Browser Extension

- **Gemini in Chrome side panel**: Best UX. Side panel stays open while you're on LinkedIn. Paste prompts directly, copy outputs.
- **Gemini.google.com tab**: Works fine but requires tab-switching. Slower workflow.
- **Gemini for Workspace (Gmail integration)**: Different surface — these prompts are not optimized for that.
- **Free tier vs Advanced**: All prompts work on free Gemini. Advanced gets you longer context + better voice consistency on personalization, worth it if running 50+ DMs/week.
- **System instructions persistence**: If using the persistent persona feature, paste the Voice Calibration Block ONCE as the system instructions for your Gemini chat. Then every prompt below works without re-pasting it. Reduces friction by ~30% per DM.

## Final Discipline Reminders (from the Marching Orders, still in force)

1. **Two-step sequence is non-negotiable**: Touch Zero (Day 1-2) → Founding Pitch (Day 3-5). Never combine. Never skip Touch Zero.
2. **Voice memos beat text DMs for emotional impact**, but text DMs scale better. Use voice for the highest-stakes 5-10 prospects per week (real friends, top-tier referral sources). Text for the rest.
3. **Farrice voice-checks the first 5 DMs.** Always. Even if you've been writing your own DMs for years. Fresh eyes catch what you can't see.
4. **The phrase "one honest witness" is the closer.** It carries the implicit referral ask. It's the line that sticks. Don't replace it with something cleverer.
5. **No price discussion in DMs.** Price lives at the Triage Audit, not in cold outreach. If someone asks "what does this cost?" in a DM, the response is: "Happy to walk you through it on a 30-min call — pricing depends on the path that fits. Want a calendar link?"
