## IMPLEMENTATION INSTRUCTIONS

### Creating Professional Presentations

1. **Software Selection:**
   * PowerPoint, Google Slides, or Canva recommended
   * Choose template with clean, professional design
   * Ensure template supports co-branding

2. **Branding Guidelines:**
   * Use partner organization's color scheme
   * Include both logos on title and final slides
   * Follow partner's brand guidelines for fonts and styles
   * Maintain consistent visual identity throughout

3. **Content Development:**
   * Follow slide outlines provided
   * Keep text minimal (3-5 bullet points per slide)
   * Use high-quality, relevant images
   * Include data visualizations where appropriate

4. **Preparation for Delivery:**
   * Create presenter notes for each slide
   * Develop engaging opening and closing
   * Prepare answers for anticipated questions
   * Test all interactive elements

### Printing Workshop Materials

1. **Handout Production:**
   * Print on high-quality paper (24-32 lb)
   * Use color printing for key elements
   * Consider professional binding for multi-page handouts
   * Include space for participant notes

2. **Quantity Planning:**
   * Print 10% more than expected attendance
   * Bring digital backup files
   * Consider environmental impact with printing choices
   * Organize materials for easy distribution

3. **Additional Materials:**
   * Name tags or tent cards
   * Sign-in sheets
   * Feedback forms
   * Business cards or contact information

### Workshop Delivery Best Practices

1. **Room Setup:**
   * Arrive 30-45 minutes early
   * Test all technology
   * Arrange seating for interaction
   * Ensure materials are distributed

2. **Engagement Techniques:**
   * Begin with compelling story or statistic
   * Use interactive elements every 10-15 minutes
   * Incorporate partner-specific examples
   * Balance presentation with discussion

3. **Follow-Up Process:**
   * Collect all feedback forms
   * Send thank-you email within 24 hours
   * Provide access to digital resources
   * Schedule next steps with partner organization

These presentation outlines and implementation instructions provide ready-to-use content that can be quickly converted into professional workshop materials for each partnership opportunity.
