# Progressive Expansion Roadmap Summary

## Strategic Overview
- 90-day systematic approach to expanding Coach Cooz's audience and business
- Integrates audience growth with business development and transformation impact
- Structured in three phases with specific milestones

## Phase 1: Foundation Solidification (Days 1-30)
- Focus on establishing core foundations for sustainable growth
- Audience targets: 
  - LinkedIn: 150-250 targeted connections with 10-20% engagement
  - Instagram: 100-200 followers with 15-25% engagement
  - Email: 50-100 assessment completions
- Business targets: 5-10 Brave Transformation Experience participants, 1-2 high-ticket prospects
- Content milestones: 12-16 platform posts, 3-5 micro-lead magnets

## Phase 2: Controlled Expansion (Days 31-60)
- Selective expansion based on validated approaches from Phase 1
- Audience targets: 
  - LinkedIn: 350-500 connections with 15-25% engagement
  - Instagram: 250-350 followers with 20-30% engagement
  - Email: 150-200 assessment completions
- Business targets: 2-3 high-ticket client conversions, 5-10 active strategic partnerships

## Phase 3: Strategic Amplification (Days 61-90)
- Amplifying successful elements while introducing new growth catalysts
- Audience targets: 
  - LinkedIn: 500-750 connections with 20-30% engagement
  - Instagram: 500-750 followers with 25-35% engagement
  - Email: 250-350 assessment completions
- Business targets: 5-8 total high-ticket client conversions, 10-15 active partnerships

## Strategic Expansion Frameworks
1. **Audience Expansion Framework**
   - Validated Content Scaling
   - Strategic Relationship Expansion
   - Collaborative Audience Development
   - Community-Driven Expansion

2. **Content Expansion Framework**
   - Modality Diversification (text, video, audio, visual)
   - Length Stratification (micro to comprehensive)
   - Topical Expansion
   - Collaborative Content Development

3. **Offer Expansion Framework**
   - Service Tier Development (free to premium $1997-4997)
   - Format Diversification (individual, group, self-guided)
   - Specialization Development
   - Duration Diversification (1-5 days to 6-12 months)

4. **Channel Expansion Framework**
   - Platform Expansion (Primary: LinkedIn, Instagram)
   - Local Presence Development in Burbank
   - Referral Channel Activation
   - Media Exposure Development
