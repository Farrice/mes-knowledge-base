> Are you tired of feeling like shit?
> Do you not want to feel like shit anymore?

## Positioning spine

The middle position Cooz named: between "resurrection deep-pain story"
(too deep for a cold flyer) and "physical transformation coach" (the thing
he's zagging against). **"I specialize in helping you not feel like shit."**
Feeling-first coaching. Everything else in fitness (abs, bench numbers,
transformation photos) is a mask for the actual purchase: feeling better.

## Grounding cards (memory facade, 2026-07-08)

- **Name the Villain** (Browder): the adversary must be specific, concrete,
  human-scaled. "The fitness industry" as a faceless force won't frighten or
  rally — the landing page must name specific mechanisms (fake transformation
  photos, before/after theater, shame-based marketing, abs-as-proxy).
- **Strategic Wrongness** (Lulu Cheng): a precise, defensible, wrong-sounding
  take beats a safe forgettable right one. People laughing at the flyer is
  the signal it works. Don't sand it down.
- **Messenger Match** (Lulu Cheng): message must come from the credible
  messenger. Cooz's own story — leanest and most miserable at 14% BF, joyful
  at 22% — is what makes HIM allowed to say this.
- **Make them want it, then hold up the mirror** (Hawley): the squeeze page
  should make the reader feel the wanting (you scanned an unbranded card
  because of how you feel) before showing them why.
