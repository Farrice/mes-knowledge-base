# Build /book-in-person: the check before you print a single flyer

**Nothing prints until this page is live and tested.** Every flyer. Every QR code. Every click off the landing page. All of it ends here. Right now the page doesn't exist. Build it first.

**After this doc:** `coachcooz.com/book-in-person` exists. Real page, your words, price stated plainly, wired to your calendar, tested end to end on a phone. 30-45 minutes, start to finish.

Cards 1-4 are 40 min at your laptop. Card 5 is the phone test — do that from the couch.

Why this page, not the old one: `/briefing` talks to a founder about optimizing the man who built the empire. A guy who scanned a blank card because he feels like shit bounces off that in one second. Same scheduler underneath. New words on top.

### Duplicate `/briefing`, don't build from zero
Why it matters: `/briefing` already has a real scheduler wired to your calendar. Build from scratch and you risk losing that wiring. Duplicating keeps the machine. You just swap the words.

Do it (10 min, alone):
- [ ] Open Squarespace, click Pages in the left sidebar
- [ ] Find `/briefing`, hover it, click the "···" menu, click Duplicate
- [ ] Click into the duplicate, click the gear icon (Page Settings)
- [ ] In General, set Title to "Book the Triage Audit" and URL Slug to `book-in-person`
- [ ] Drag the new page into "Not Linked" so it's off your main menu (same spot your QR landing page lives)

Done when: Squarespace shows a second page, "Book the Triage Audit," living at `coachcooz.com/book-in-person`, unlinked, next to `/briefing`.

### The test: scan it, book a fake slot, confirm the email lands
Why it matters: this is the whole point of this doc. If it doesn't work end to end on a phone, printing flyers just burns money on a dead link.

Do it (10-15 min, alone):
- [ ] On your phone, scan the actual flyer QR code (or open `coachcooz.com/stop-feeling-like-shit` if no flyer is printed yet)
- [ ] Tap through to `/book-in-person`, read it on your phone screen start to finish
- [ ] Book a real slot using a name and email you control
- [ ] Confirm the booking email actually lands in that inbox
- [ ] Cancel the fake booking so it isn't sitting on your real calendar Monday morning

Done when: you're holding a real confirmation email from a slot you booked by tapping the actual QR code on your own phone.

Go deeper → the QR on the cards already carries tracking. Nothing for you to set up.
