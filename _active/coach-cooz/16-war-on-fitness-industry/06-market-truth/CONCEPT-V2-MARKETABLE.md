## 0. THE ONE THING THAT CHANGES

V1 was built on a slogan — "no one cares about abs" — that the live market **refutes at the surface and supports only as means-not-end** (`SOCIAL-LISTENING.md` H2; `ICP-DESIRE-LADDER.md` §3). ## 1. THE REFRAME — from "no one cares about abs" to the honest ladder

A small live corpus gives a **directional signal, not a proven law**: "feel better" converts as **pain, not promise**, and rides on a **concrete proof rung** rather than floating alone (`SOCIAL-LISTENING.md` H3; the single verified compressed instance, [21] upvotes: *"To feel better, look better, feel better about looking better, attracting a partner, being able to do things with them"*). Abs aren't the enemy — abs are the **proof that makes the feeling believable**. Denying the want condescends to the exact buyer Googling how to look better (`ICP-DESIRE-LADDER.md` §3).

Three articulations, each from the verbatim bank:

- **A — "Come for the body. Stay because you stopped hating the guy in the mirror."** Grounds in *"I let myself go... and kinda hate myself now"* [1] — **UNCONFIRMED**: this is a 1-upvote comment behind a collapsed "load more comments" node that didn't render this pass; the thread is real and its high-score lines verified verbatim, so fabrication risk is low, but confirm the exact string before this line anchors client-facing flyer/page copy. Validates the surface want, names the real pain, promises no vague feeling.
- **B — "You don't have to hate yourself to look good."** The ICP permission line (`ICP-DESIRE-LADDER.md` §3). Keeps rung 2 (attraction/status) intact, indicts only the *method*. Tightest — but abstract alone.
- **C — "The body's the packaging. Feeling like yourself is what you paid for."** V1's existing instinct. Weakest cold: leans on the terminal (a weak cold driver per H3), under-uses the pain.

**PICK: A, with B as the recurring permission beat.** A fuses a concrete rung and verbatim negative-terminal pain in one breath — the pairing a thin, directional corpus points toward, not a proven law across a large sample. B threads the industry-callout to keep both the 26-year-old and the operator dad in the room. C is retired; it repeats the "promise a vague feeling" pattern H3 flags.

**Trade-off, named honestly**: this reframe trades a fist for a ladder. "No one cares about abs" was polarizing, screenshot-able, argument-provoking — the exact edge the Strategic Wrongness principle (Lulu Cheng: a precise wrong-sounding take beats a safe right one; don't sand it down) says to protect. V2 doesn't abandon that principle; §3b-B still swings — *"A six-pack is real. It just isn't the thing you're chasing. I know — I had one and hated my life."* The bet is that this version is more marketable **on the page**, where nuance converts, not that it's simply safer.

**Hard constraint (`MCBROOM-READOUT.md` §5)**: even the most relationship-forward operator anchors his guarantee to *"measurable physical progress validated by trackable data."* The wedge is **feeling delivered *through* physical progress — not instead of physique.** The inverted-transformation story survives because it never denies the physique; it reframes the *method* as the lie. Hold that everywhere.

## 2. THE ENEMY, SHARPENED — extreme-transformation culture, not fitness

V1's villain list (faked photos, abs-as-proxy, shame marketing) is right but **aimed one notch too wide**. The live backlash is not at transformations or at wanting a body — it is at **hype, contradiction, and grind-as-identity**: the "optimal" arms race, *"magic pill... theatre sells"* [3], the *"anything else is useless" full-stop statements* [7] (`SOCIAL-LISTENING.md` H1). Readers name creators they *trust*. So the enemy is precise:

**The named enemy = extreme-transformation culture.** Challenges. No-days-off. 6am-or-you're-weak. The lifestyle porn that measures your worth by how much pain you can absorb (*"as if self-worth is measured by how much pain or exhaustion one can endure"*, H1). Cooz's position is **the sane coach for an adult with a real life** — job, family, calendar — not the anti-fitness guy.

The macro reality earns the trust: **~50% of new members quit within six months; ~65% of attrition is cost, time, lost motivation** (`SOCIAL-LISTENING.md`, LIKELY — sourced to gym-CRM marketing blogs, not primary research; treat as directional, never print the percentage). The market is defined by *abandonment*. "Sustainable, built for a real life" is the direct answer to the #1 thing that actually happens to buyers. And McBroom **already half-claimed the burnout frame years ago** but kept his front door outcome-first, treating mindset as an add-on ("shift the way you think about *dieting*") — leaving the worth/confidence layer unclaimed (`MCBROOM-READOUT.md` §1, §5). That gap is Cooz's lane.

## 6. WHAT WE'RE NOT DOING (and why)

- **No guru theater.** No "the secret," no mysticism, no "I've figured it out." Cooz is peer-with-receipts, two years ahead — not above the reader (`03-cooz-voice-profile.md` H2). Guru voice breaks the one asset the flyer buys: reading like a real person, not a brand.
- **No transformation-photo arms race.** The written-proof moat wins *because* faked photos are epidemic (`STRATEGY-SPINE.md` §3). Before/afters mean competing on the theater the enemy owns — and on McBroom's proof volume, which Cooz doesn't have yet (`MCBROOM-READOUT.md` §4).
- **No therapy positioning.** The abstract terminal ("you deserve to feel whole") with no proof under it reads as therapy-voice — condescension, not comfort, to this buyer (`ICP-DESIRE-LADDER.md` §1; voice profile I). Pain-first, proof-backed, always.
- **No medical claims.** No "I fix your depression / heal your trauma." Outside Cooz's lane and legally exposed (`STRATEGY-SPINE.md` §4) — also why the thyroid-cancer quote is under review.
- **Not competing on McBroom's turf.** No "sustainable fat loss / total body transformation" headline — his defended, scale-backed language (`MCBROOM-READOUT.md` §4). Cooz takes the unclaimed worth/confidence layer above it. No topical breadth; depth on one felt problem is a solo operator's only edge.
- **No fabricated proof, ever.** No composite case studies, no ad lines dressed as testimonials, no placeholder quotes in a real client's name (`RAW-PROOF-INVENTORY-V2.md` §4/4a). One invented quote kills the whole written-proof moat.
