You are a world-class Behavioral Economist and High-Ticket Business Strategist specializing in the high-performance executive space.

Your objective is to build a psychological and operational dossier on my exact target market. I sell a premium $5,000 - $10,000 consulting framework called "The Resurrection Protocol," preceded by a $1,000 "Triage Audit" diagnostic call.

THE TARGET (The Apex Operator):

He is a highly successful founder, VP, or managing partner (35-50 years old, making $300K+).
He has won the external game (company is scaling, metrics are good, financially secure).
He is losing the internal game (running on empty, hitting a massive 3 PM wall, carrying excess weight, relying on caffeine, and coming home with zero bandwidth for his wife and kids).
He despises therapy-speak, victim mentalities, and B2B corporate jargon. He thinks in terms of systems, frameworks, architecture, and execution.

Do not give me generic demographics. I need a deep, multi-layered behavioral analysis broken down into the following sections:

1. The Psychological Dark Matter:
What is the silent narrative he admits to nobody? What is his deepest fear regarding his physical decline and his ability to maintain his status? How does he justify his exhaustion to himself?

2. The Daily Friction Map:
Break down a typical, high-stress Tuesday. Map his energy levels, his exact points of friction, the specific moments he relies on artificial energy, and the internal dialogue he has when he walks through his front door at 6:30 PM.

3. The Bullshit Filter (Language to Avoid vs. Language to Use):
What specific marketing phrases, fitness industry cliches, and "biohacking" trends instantly make his eyes roll? Conversely, what specific words and concepts instantly signal that I am a peer and a trusted authority?

4. The Trigger Event:
He has lived with this exhaustion for years. What are the 3-4 specific, visceral "Code Red" trigger events that will finally force him to stop putting it off, pull out his credit card, and pay $5,000+ to fix the problem today?

5. The Watering Holes:
Where does this specific man go for high-level information when he is trying to optimize his life? Name the specific types of podcasts (like Human Performance Longevity), niche newsletters, private communities, or data-driven platforms he respects.
