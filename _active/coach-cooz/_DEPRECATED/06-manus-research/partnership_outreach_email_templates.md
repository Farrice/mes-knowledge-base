## EMAIL CUSTOMIZATION GUIDE

### Personalizing Your Outreach

For maximum effectiveness, customize each template with:

1. **Recipient Research**: Include specific details about the organization and recipient
2. **Personal Connection**: Mention any existing relationship or touchpoint
3. **Specific Value**: Tailor the value proposition to their exact needs
4. **Timely Relevance**: Connect to current initiatives or seasonal priorities
5. **Local Context**: Reference Burbank-specific elements when appropriate

### Follow-Up Best Practices

When following up on initial outreach:

1. **Timing**: Wait 5-7 business days before first follow-up
2. **Tone**: Maintain helpful, not pushy, communication
3. **Value-Add**: Provide additional information or insight
4. **Options**: Offer multiple ways to connect
5. **Exit Strategy**: Respect their time with a clear conclusion

### Email Signature Recommendations

Create a professional signature that includes:

1. **Full Name and Credentials**
2. **Business Name**: Brave Transformation Coaching
3. **Title**: Transformation Coach
4. **Phone Number**: Direct line for easy contact
5. **Email Address**: Professional email address
6. **Website**: Link to professional website
7. **LinkedIn Profile**: Link to professional profile
8. **Optional**: Brief tagline or value statement
