# Quick-Start: LinkedIn Warm Network Outreach
## Coach Cooz | Week 1 Action Plan

> Goal: Re-engage your 837 connections by end of April. Start conversations, not pitch.
> Daily commitment: ~30 minutes.

---

## The Daily 30 Minutes

| Block | Time | What |
|-------|------|------|
| Warm-up | 10 min | Comment substantively on 5-10 posts in your niche |
| Outreach | 15 min | Send 15-20 DMs from the templates below |
| Reply | 5 min | Respond to anyone who replied yesterday |

**Math**: At 15-20 DMs/day, you cover all 837 connections in ~45-55 days. To hit end of April, push to 30/day for 2 weeks of catch-up, then maintain at 15-20.

---

## Step 1: Sort Your Connections (Do This Once, 1-2 Hours)

Open LinkedIn, go to "My Network → Connections," and tag people into 4 buckets in your head (or in a spreadsheet):

| Bucket | Who | Approach |
|--------|-----|---------|
| **Tier 1: Potential Clients** | Founders, small business owners, executives 30-55 in stress-heavy roles | Use Templates 1-3 |
| **Tier 2: Referral Sources** | Other coaches, therapists, leadership consultants, wellness pros | Use Template 6 |
| **Tier 3: People You Know** | Friends, ex-colleagues, anyone you've actually met | Use Template 4 |
| **Tier 4: Cold/Random** | People you don't recognize, inactive profiles | Skip or use Template 1 lightly |

---

## The 6 DM Templates

### Template 1: The Genuine Reconnect (Tier 1, 3, 4)
> Hey [Name] — been a while since we connected on here. Been heads down building something new and realized I haven't been great about actually talking to the people in my network. What are you working on these days?

**Why it works**: Zero pitch. Zero agenda. Most "reach-outs" smell like sales — this one doesn't. The reply rate is way higher because there's nothing to defend against.

---

### Template 2: The Specific Compliment (Tier 1 — people who actually post)
> [Name] — saw your post about [specific topic] this week. The part about [specific detail] hit me because [your honest reaction]. Curious how long you've been thinking about that?

**Why it works**: Proves you read their content. Creates a real thread, not a transactional exchange. They'll usually respond because you complimented something specific, not them generally.

---

### Template 3: The Diagnostic Opening (Tier 1 — founders/leaders specifically)
> Hey [Name] — noticed you're running [company/role]. I've been working with a lot of guys in similar positions lately and one thing keeps coming up: the gap between how the business looks on paper and how the person running it actually feels. Has that ever been your experience?

**Why it works**: This is a diagnostic question, not a pitch. If they say yes → natural conversation. If they say no → still a real exchange. You're not selling anything, you're asking a question only someone with insight would ask.

---

### Template 4: The "Long Time No See" (Tier 3 — people you actually know)
> [Name]! Man, was just scrolling through my connections and realized I haven't reached out in way too long. Life's been intense — lots of pivots and growth on my end. How are you doing? What's new with you?

**Why it works**: Warm, honest, no pretense. Old contacts respond to this because it's how a friend would actually message them.

---

### Template 5: The Podcast Drop (Tier 1 & 2 — only when you have something to share)
> Hey [Name] — I just did a podcast episode about [topic] and it made me think of you because [specific reason]. If you have 15 minutes, I'd love your take on it: [link]

**Why it works**: You're sharing value, not asking for something. Positions you as someone who's doing things (podcast guest = authority signal). Use this when your podcast appearance goes live.

---

### Template 6: The Peer Referral Ask (Tier 2 — other coaches/professionals)
> Hey [Name] — hope you're doing well. Quick question: I work with high-achieving men who've let their physical health slide while building their careers. If you ever come across someone like that — founder type, successful but running on fumes — I'd love an introduction. And same goes the other way — who's your ideal client? Happy to keep an eye out.

**Why it works**: Reciprocal. Specific. Peer-to-peer energy. Gives them a clear way to help you AND a reason to engage (you're offering to help them back).

## Critical Rules

### Do
- Personalize the [Name] field at minimum (LinkedIn auto-detects copy-paste — vary your messages)
- Wait for a reply before sending more
- Reply within 24 hours when someone responds
- Move conversations to value/interest organically — don't force the pitch
- Track who you've messaged so you don't double-DM

### Don't
- Pitch in the first message. Ever.
- Send the exact same message to multiple people (LinkedIn flags this)
- Send voice notes as a first message
- Use Sales Navigator language ("I noticed you're a decision-maker...")
- Follow up more than twice if no response
- Send a connection request with a pitch attached

---

## When Someone Responds (The Conversation Bridge)

Your goal in the first 2-3 exchanges: **just have a real conversation.** Listen more than you talk. Look for the opening — when they mention being tired, stressed, out of shape, or "needing to get back into something."

When the opening appears:
> "That's literally what I do. I work with guys in your exact situation. Want to hop on a quick 15-minute call this week? No pitch — just want to hear what's going on and see if I can point you in a useful direction."

Notice:
- **15 minutes**, not 60. Low friction.
- **No pitch** — peer energy, not sales energy
- **Useful direction** — even if they don't buy, they leave with something

---

## The 15-Minute Call → Triage Audit Bridge

If the 15-minute call goes well and they want to go deeper:
> "Cool — I do something called a Triage Audit. It's a 90-minute deep dive where I map out exactly what's going on with your energy, nutrition, recovery, and daily routine. You get a personalized blueprint at the end. It's $497, and that rolls into the full protocol if we decide to work together. Want me to send you the link?"

This is the trust-building bridge that the old "fill out this application" model failed to provide.

---

## Tracking (Keep It Simple)

Use a Google Sheet or Notion table. 4 columns:
- Name
- Date Sent
- Status (sent / replied / call booked / not interested)
- Notes

Don't overcomplicate it. The goal is to know who you've messaged so you don't double up, and to follow up with people who showed interest.

---

## Week 1 Target

- Connections sorted (1-2 hours, one-time)
- 100 DMs sent
- 10-15 replies (10-15% reply rate is normal for warm network)
- 2-3 conversations that move toward a 15-min call
- 1 booked call (if you're running this consistently)

That's it. That's the week 1 win condition.
