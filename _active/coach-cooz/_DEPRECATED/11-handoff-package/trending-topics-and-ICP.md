## The Core Reframe

**The buyer is not asking for a 90-day body transformation.**

He's asking for two things:
1. **Permission to name what's happening** — "I'm not crazy, this is real, I'm not the only one"
2. **One thing he can cut** — "Show me the move that takes the pressure off"

The Resurrection brand is perfect for this because it implies: *you're not broken, you went dormant.* It gives him permission to name the death without admitting failure. That's the unlock.

You're not selling fitness. You're selling **the first honest mirror this guy has looked into in 18 months.**

## Trending Topics This Week (April 7, 2026)

These are conversations happening in your ICP's world right now. If your content speaks into one of these, you'll get organic reach.

### Topic 1: Q2 Reset Season
**Why it's hot**: It's early April. People are looking at the year so far and realizing they haven't done the things they said they'd do. New Year's resolutions are dead. Q2 = realistic restart point.
**Your angle**: The Q1 → Q2 transition is the most honest moment in the founder calendar. The lies you told yourself in January don't survive April. This is the moment to actually start.

### Topic 2: Post-Funding Burnout
**Why it's hot**: Founders who raised in late 2025/early 2026 are now in the "now what?" phase. The euphoria is gone, the goalposts moved, and the body is cracking.
**Your angle**: The day after you raise is the most physically dangerous day of your founder journey. Talk about the founders who collapse 3 months after the announcement.

### Topic 3: The "AI Burnout" Discussion
**Why it's hot**: 2026 is the year founders are realizing that AI didn't actually save them time — it just raised the expectation of output. They're working harder, not smarter.
**Your angle**: AI made the cognitive load worse, not better. The body is paying for it. Frame: "Your AI tools made you 10x more productive. Why are you 3x more exhausted?"

### Topic 4: The Slack-at-2-AM Phenomenon
**Why it's hot**: Real Reddit language from this week. The image of a founder checking Slack at 2 AM out of fear (not obsession) is having a moment.
**Your angle**: "It's not addiction. It's terror. And it's destroying your nervous system."

### Topic 5: The Co-Founder Silence
**Why it's hot**: Underdiscussed but felt by every founder team. Both partners are burnt, neither can admit it.
**Your angle**: The most dangerous lie in a startup isn't told to investors. It's told between co-founders.

### Topic 6: "I'm Not Burnt Out, I'm Just Tired"
**Why it's hot**: The denial language founders use to avoid admitting burnout. Recognizable to anyone who's done it.
**Your angle**: The reframe — "burnout doesn't always look like crying in the parking lot. Sometimes it looks like '4 more cups of coffee and a 6 PM energy drink.'"
