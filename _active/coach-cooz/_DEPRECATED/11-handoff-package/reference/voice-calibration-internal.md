## New Voice: "The Witness" (Added April 6, 2026)

The Reddit data surfaced a voice Cooz's Batch 01 never used: the peer observer. The most powerful burnout post on r/StartUpIndia is written in third person — *"I know a founder. Smart guy. Built something real."* This is safer for the reader to engage with than first-person confession (they don't have to admit it's them) and is highly shareable.

**When to use The Witness**:
- When the truth is too close for the reader to accept in first-person
- When you want to normalize an experience before naming it
- When you want a post to feel like gossip the reader wasn't supposed to hear

**Template**:
> "I know a guy. Built something real. Raised money. Then his CFO left. And his lead engineer quit. And suddenly he's answering 'why aren't you growing faster' on a Tuesday call while his wife is wondering when she's getting her husband back.
>
> He's running on fumes. He can't tell his co-founder because they're both pretending. He can't tell his team because they'll bail. He can't tell his wife because she's already the only one holding the house together.
>
> The people around him think he's fine. He's not fine. He's been dying in slow motion for about a year.
>
> If you think I'm talking about you — I'm not. I'm talking about the guy I met last week. But you're not alone in it. That's the part nobody tells you."

This is a voice Batch 01 doesn't have. It should be added to the rotation at roughly 10-15% of posts.

## The 5 Content Voices (Rotation — Updated April 6, 2026)

### 1. The Scene (35% of posts)
Paint a picture the reader recognizes from his own life. No diagnosis. Just recognition.
- "It's 6:30 PM. You just walked through the front door..."
- "Your wife asks what you want for dinner. And you snap."

### 2. The Confession (20% of posts)
Acusio tells his own story. Vulnerability without victimhood.
- "I used to think being exhausted meant I was working hard enough..."
- "I traded the edge for safety. The instinct for calculation."

### 3. The Reframe (20% of posts)
Flip a common belief. Challenge without diagnosing.
- "You're not setting boundaries. You're running on empty and calling it leadership."
- "That's not discipline. That's just surviving."

### 4. The Witness (15% of posts) — NEW
Third-person peer observation. The gossip-you-weren't-supposed-to-hear voice. Backed by Reddit data — this is the dominant voice in high-engagement founder burnout posts.
- "I know a guy. Built something real. Raised money. Now he's checking Slack at 2 AM, not because he's obsessed — because he's terrified."
- "There's a founder I've been watching quietly for about a year..."

### 5. The Proof (10% of posts)
Real results. Real people. Before/after. Narrative over metrics (competitors don't lead with DEXA data — they lead with stories).
- Brian's transformation story
- New client progress
- His own physical journey
- Podcast appearance recap

## CTA Rotation (NEVER repeat the same CTA twice in a row)

1. **The Question:** "When was the last time your family got your best? Not your leftovers."
2. **The Confession Ending:** "I didn't figure this out until I almost lost everything. Don't wait that long."
3. **The Challenge:** "Try this for one week: eat a real meal before noon. Track your energy at 3 PM. Tell me what changes."
4. **The Soft Invite:** "If this hit home, I wrote more about it [link/podcast]."
5. **The Direct (sparingly):** "I have 2 spots open for April. If you want to talk about what a reset looks like for you, DM me."
6. **No CTA:** Some posts just end. Let the weight of the story sit. Trust the reader to come back.

These are Cooz's real frameworks. They're good. But they should EMERGE from a story, never lead.
