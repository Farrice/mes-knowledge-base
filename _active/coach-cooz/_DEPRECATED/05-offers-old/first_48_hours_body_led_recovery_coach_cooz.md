# The First 48 Hours
**A body‑led recovery plan for men within 60 days of a life crisis**  
*(Faith‑forward and judgment‑free. This is coaching, not therapy or medical care.)*

> **If you are in immediate danger or considering self‑harm:** call **911** or **988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)** now. You are not alone.

---

## The Mission (48‑Hour North Star)
For the next two days, you will **stabilize your body**, **calm your mind**, and **anchor your faith/values** so you can make clear decisions and start your rebuild.

**3 Commitments**
1. **No big decisions** for 72 hours.  
2. **No substances** (alcohol, recreational drugs).  
3. **Micro‑wins over motivation**—we stack small reps.

---

## Hour 0–2: Stabilize
**1) Breathe (5 minutes)**  
- Sit/stand tall. Inhale **4s**, exhale **6s** × 20 breaths.  
- On exhale, say quietly: *“I’m here. I’m safe. One step at a time.”*

**2) Hydrate & Eat (10 minutes)**  
- 16–24 oz water.  
- Simple meal: **protein + color + starch + fat** (e.g., eggs + berries + toast + olive oil).  

**3) Text Your 48‑Hour Crew (5 minutes)**  
- Send: *“I’m taking 48 hours to reset. Checking in now and tonight. No need to fix—just asking you to be here.”*  
- Add one pro contact (therapist/faith leader/attorney if relevant).

**4) Clear Space (10 minutes)**  
- Make bed. Clear sink. Take a shower. Put on clean clothes.

**5) Light Movement (15 minutes)**  
- **10‑minute walk** outside.  
- **Micro‑circuit** (2 rounds): 10 push‑ups (elevate as needed), 15 air squats, 30‑sec wall plank.

---

## Day 1 Plan (Hours 2–24)
**Morning**  
- Water + **protein‑first** breakfast.  
- **Walk 20 minutes** (phone on Do Not Disturb).  
- **Journal (5 prompts, 7 minutes):**  
  1) What happened? (facts, not judgments)  
  2) What is **in my control** today?  
  3) One **courageous action** I can take.  
  4) Gratitude × 3.  
  5) (Optional) Short prayer: *“God, give me strength for the next right step.”*

**Midday**  
- **Strength Session A (20–25 min):**  
  - 4 rounds: 10 push‑ups • 12 hip hinges (backpack deadlifts) • 15 step‑backs/lunges • 30s bear hold • Rest 60s.  
- **Fuel:** protein + fruit + water.  
- **Boundaries:** mute socials; respond only to essential messages.

**Evening**  
- **Connection check‑in** (5–10 min voice/text) with your 48‑Hour Crew.  
- **Wind‑down stack (30 min):** hot shower • lights low • no screens • read 5 pages or devotional • write tomorrow’s top 3.  
- **Sleep target:** in bed at the same time; room cool & dark.

---

## Day 2 Plan (Hours 24–48)
**Morning**  
- Repeat water + protein breakfast.  
- **Walk 20–30 minutes** (same route).  
- **Journal (5 minutes):** What worked yesterday? What needs adjusting?

**Midday**  
- **Strength Session B (20–25 min):**  
  - 4 rounds: 12 backpack rows • 15 air squats • 8 slow tempo push‑ups • 30s side plank/side.  
- **Meal template:** protein palm‑sized • vegetables • rice/potato • olive oil/butter.  
- **One brave admin task:** pay a bill, book an appointment, or cancel a trigger.

**Evening**  
- **Brotherhood practice (10 min):** encourage another man—text or voice note.  
- **Reflection:** write 3 wins you want to repeat tomorrow.  
- **Sleep:** same wind‑down; lights out on time.

## Red‑Flag Escalation (Use Immediately)
- Thoughts of self‑harm or harming others → **911 / 988**  
- No eating/sleep for 24+ hours, or panic you can’t interrupt → call a professional (therapist/doctor).  
- Legal/emergency family issues → contact appropriate professional now.

## Nutrition, Caffeine, & Substances
- **No alcohol/recreational drugs** for 7 days.  
- Caffeine: cap at **2 cups before noon**.  
- Eat **3 simple meals**; don’t chase perfection—chase consistency.  
- Hydration target: **half your bodyweight (lbs) × 0.5 = ounces/day** (approximate). Keep a bottle nearby.

---

## Digital & Environment Boundaries
- Delete or hide trigger apps for 48 hours.  
- Use **Do Not Disturb** with an allow‑list (family/crew only).  
- Keep one room tidy (bedroom or kitchen) so your brain sees order.

---

## Optional Faith Anchors (adapt as needed)
- **Morning:** 60‑second prayer: *“Guide my steps. Give me strength for today.”*  
- **Midday:** read one Psalm or proverb; write one line that lands.  
- **Evening:** gratitude × 3; release what you can’t control.

---

## If You Only Do Three Things
1) **Walk 20 minutes** each day.  
2) **Eat protein** at each meal and drink water.  
3) **Text one man** morning and night: “Checked in. Still moving.”

---

## What Happens After 48 Hours
You now have momentum. Choose your next step:
- **Emergency Stabilization (6 weeks):** daily touchpoints + weekly calls to lock in your routines.  
- **90‑Day Resurrection:** a full rebuild of body → identity with accountability and faith‑optional support.  
- **Brotherhood:** weekly group support so you never go it alone.
