## Offer 1: Emergency Stabilization (6 Weeks)
**Best for:** Men inside ~30 days of crisis who need daily structure and a firm hand.  
**Outcome:** Stop the psychological bleeding, install anchors, regain control.

### Deliverables (fixed scope)
- **Rapid Intake & Safety Triage (Day 0–1)**  
  • 60‑min Zoom intake within 24–48 hours  
  • Personal **Safety Plan** (red‑flag protocol, hotline numbers, allow‑list)  
  • Deploy **First 48** plan + Morning/Evening Anchor cards
- **Daily Accountability (Mon–Fri)**  
  • **AM & PM check‑ins** via SMS/WhatsApp at agreed windows  
  • 10‑minute response window; patterns tracked in a shared habit tracker (Notion/Sheet)
- **Weekly Coaching (6× 45 min)**  
  • Agenda: review sleep/fuel/movement → remove friction → next micro‑commitments  
  • Adjust training, nutrition, and boundary scripts
- **Movement & Nutrition**  
  • Minimal‑equipment plan (20–25 min, 4 days/week)  
  • **3 Meal Templates** + 1 grocery list + caffeine/alcohol protocol (7‑day no‑alcohol recommendation)
- **Warm Referrals (as needed)**  
  • Therapist / faith leader / attorney network with hand‑off email  
  • One coordination note per week (client‑approved) to pro partners
- **Office‑Hour Access**  
  • **2 “SOS texts”/week** with guaranteed response **7am–9pm PT** (same‑day; not emergency care)

### What’s **not** included (to keep scope clean)
- 24/7 crisis response, therapy, medical/legal advice, custom meal plans beyond the templates.

### Milestones & Metrics
- **By Day 7:** 6+ hours sleep, 2 walks/day, protein at each meal, one “brave admin” task complete  
- **By Week 3:** panic episodes reduced 50%+, movement 4×/wk, two‑man support crew active  
- **By Week 6 (exit):** morning/evening anchors run on autopilot; readiness for 90‑Day

### Transformation (copy block)
> *“In six weeks you’ll go from white‑knuckling the day to knowing exactly what to do every morning and night. Your body settles, your mind quiets, and you start acting like the man you respect.”*

## Offer 2: 90‑Day Resurrection (12 Weeks)
**Best for:** Men who’ve stabilized and are ready to rebuild body → identity with structure.  
**Outcome:** Strong body, clear mind, anchored faith—habits that hold under stress.

### Deliverables (fixed scope)
- **Kickoff Map (Week 0)**  
  • 60‑min strategy session; define 3 outcomes (body / life / faith) + baseline tests  
  • Personal **90‑Day Map** (phases below)
- **Coaching Cadence**  
  • **Bi‑weekly 1:1 calls** (6× 45 min over 12 weeks)  
  • **Daily check‑ins (Mon–Fri)** with 3‑tap habit tracker (walk, lift, fuel)
- **Training & Fuel**  
  • Progressive strength (3 days/week) + optional conditioning (1 day)  
  • **Portion‑based nutrition** targets; weekly adjustments; dining‑out/life‑on‑the‑go scripts
- **Identity & Faith (optional)**  
  • Sunday Reset ritual; weekly journal prompt; micro‑service act  
  • Temptation bypass playbook (cue → alternative)
- **Community**  
  • **Brotherhood access** during the 90 days (group call + partner check‑ins)

### Phases
1) **Stabilize+ (Weeks 1–3):** tighten anchors; prove consistency (≥80%)  
2) **Rebuild (Weeks 4–8):** strength PRs, calorie precision, stress scripts  
3) **Fortify (Weeks 9–12):** relapse‑proof plan, identity statements, post‑program plan

### Milestones & Metrics
- **By Day 30:** 10–20 lb strength gain in major movements; 80%+ habit adherence; panic minimal  
- **By Day 60:** visibly leaner/stronger; 2+ identity wins (e.g., re‑enter gym, set boundaries)  
- **By Day 90:** “Unbreakable stack” locked (sleep, walk, lift, fuel, faith/values)

### Guarantee (performance‑based)
**Do the work; transform—or I coach you free until you do.**  
Client responsibilities: attend calls (5/6), log check‑ins (≥80%), complete weekly targets.

### Transformation (copy block)
> *“In 90 days you’ll look different in the mirror and talk to yourself differently in your head. You’ll have a plan you trust, muscles that answer, and rituals that hold when life hits again.”*
