## The 4 critical decisions baked into this package

These were resolved in planning conversations with Farrice before anything was written. They are load-bearing and should NOT be revisited mid-execution.

1. **Archetype**: The Man in the Valley (named and anchored to Richard Hughes, 49, massage therapist). Not "founders," not "executives," not "high performers." The behavioral fingerprint is what matters, not the demographic band.

2. **Gender inclusion during Ignition**: Mixed-gender public proof. Cooz positions marketing to The Man in the Valley (male-led voice, male-coded hooks) but the published case study stack includes women (Brian, Angie, Mari). Sheedy precedent. Farrice overrode the strict male-only ideal in favor of cash flow + existing proof.

3. **Price ladder**: $1,997 founding (first 3) → $2,997 list (clients 4–5) → $4,000 post-proof (cohort 2). The $4K rung is the bridge into Scale Phase. NOT $10K.

4. **Hybrid is OK during Ignition, fully online is the end state**: Cooz can take local hybrid clients during Ignition to keep cash flowing. The trigger to permanently close in-person intake is first $10K MRR from online-only clients OR 3 published online-only case studies — whichever comes first.
