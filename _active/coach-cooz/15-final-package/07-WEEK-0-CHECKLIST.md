## PART 4 — Hard rules that override everything else

1. **No Instagram post ships without Farrice voice check for the first 4 weeks.** No exceptions. If Farrice is unavailable, the post waits.
2. **No voice memo script deploys past the first 5 until Farrice reviews responses.** Test the script. Do not mass-deploy a broken script.
3. **No price conversation happens at $5K.** The price is $1,997 for the first 3 founding clients. If a prospect pushes for a higher or lower price, the answer is: *"This is the founding rate. It's the cheapest I'll ever run. The deal is you become a case study."* That's the whole script.
4. **No in-person client is taken during the Ignition if the client lives more than 60 miles from Burbank.** Hybrid is OK for LOCAL clients during Ignition. Remote in-person is not Ignition — that's a different offer.
5. **No Founder/Executive/Creator language in any DM, voice memo, post, or offer page.** If Cooz or Farrice catches the other slipping, call it out immediately. The old avatar is dead.
6. **No content ships that uses "Here's what" or "Here's how" as an opener.** The flywheel filters for this but manual edits can sneak it back in. Watch for it.
7. **Cash runway check every Friday.** If Cooz is at zero MRR by end of Week 3, stop the plan and reassess with Farrice. Do NOT push harder on the same plan. The failure is upstream of effort.

## PART 6 — What NOT to do in Week 0 / Week 1

- **Do not rewrite the offer page based on a hunch.** The $1,997/$2,997/$4,000 ladder is what we're testing. Changing it mid-test produces noise, not signal.
- **Do not post "sneak peek" content about the Ignition launch.** The launch is the warm network activation + the Loom. Teasers dilute the story.
- **Do not message people you haven't talked to in 5+ years with the founding offer.** Tier 3 gets a catch-up message (Template 1 or 4 from the old DM templates file). Tier 1 gets the founding offer voice memo. Do not confuse the tiers.
- **Do not run ads in Week 1.** Organic warm network + content + Loom is the test. Paid ads in Week 1 would muddy the attribution and burn cash that isn't needed yet.
- **Do not pitch podcast guest appearances to shows you don't actually listen to.** The pitch has to be real. Pitch 3 shows you genuinely care about, not 10 shows from a scraped list.
- **Do not add any new tools, apps, or software.** Whatever Cooz is using now (TrueCoach, Squarespace, Voxer, Loom, Notion) is the stack. No new stack in Week 1.
- **Do not mention the Cooz Flywheel to anyone.** It's internal infrastructure. If a client asks how you're producing content, the answer is: *"I work with my guy Farrice on it."* That's it.
