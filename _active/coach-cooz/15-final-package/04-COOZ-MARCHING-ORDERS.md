## THE VOICE (one card, for your desk)

### How every post works (the 6-step structure):
1. **One-line I-declaration** (never open with "you")
2. **Specific expansion in I** (2-3 concrete details from YOUR life)
3. **Turning point in I** ("I finally admitted..." / "The true moment happened when...")
4. **One-line public declaration** (optional)
5. **You-pivot, max 2 sentences** (the universal takeaway — the ONLY place "you" appears)
6. **Short closer** (brand line, period, symbol)

### Three lanes:
- **Mon / Wed / Fri**: Grounded practical. Warm. Body-first. "Resurrection" rarely.
- **Sunday**: Mythic. Full brand voltage. Confession + redemption.
- **Tues or Thurs**: One reel. Camera to face. 45-90 sec. You talking.

### Word count caps:
- Lane A (practical): 120-300 words
- Lane B (mythic Sunday): 200-500 words
- Reel scripts: 45-90 seconds spoken

### Words you use in copy:
"rebuild" / "get back" / "comeback" / "strongest version" / "came back" / "dormant" / "reactivate" / "hardware" / "infrastructure" / "the man"

### Words you NEVER use in copy:
"resurrection" (brand name only, not body copy) / "founder" / "executive" / "journey" / "mindset" / "wellness" / "holistic" / "crush it" / "level up" / "amazing" / "game-changer" / "Here's what" / "Here's how"

- 3 Instagram carousels (transformation arc, metrics, testimonial quote)
