The Deep ICP Profile at `_active/linkedin/01-research/deep-icp-profile-invisible-expert.md` is the primary ICP reference document for The Authority Sprint / authority-building content service.

**Key insights that must inform ALL content and messaging:**

1. **The resistance is IDENTITY-LEVEL, not skill-level.** "I am an expert, NOT a marketer." Content creation threatens who they believe they are. This is pre-rational — the brain treats identity threats like physical threats.

2. **The Authenticity Paradox** — they use "being authentic" as an excuse to stay comfortable. Rigid authenticity limits the impact they care most about.

3. **The Translation Gap** is a cognitive bias (Curse of Expertise), NOT a personal failure. The difficulty of creating content is actually EVIDENCE of depth. This reframe disarms shame.

4. **60% of TAM is PRE-CONTEMPLATION** — they don't even know they need this. Content must educate about the problem before it can sell the solution.

5. **Language Map (CRITICAL):**
   - AVOID: "personal brand," "content strategy," "lead generation," "influencer," "growth hacking," "hustle," "funnel," "monetize"
   - USE: "professional authority," "sharing your expertise," "attracting the right clients," "recognized expert," "building a system," "sustainable practice," "reach/impact," "client pathway"

6. **Bridge Message**: "You talk. We translate. Your authority grows."

7. **Composite Avatar**: Dr. Maya Patel — executive leadership coach, 42, $180-350K/year, 85% referral-dependent, paralyzed by LinkedIn, spent $3K on a branding course she couldn't finish. Her "wine conversation" is the emotional truth of the entire ICP.

8. **Content Themes That Generate Dwell Time**: Curse of Expertise, Referral Ceiling, Authenticity Paradox, Expert vs. Influencer, The 11pm Google Search, Permission to Be Found, The Invisible Expert Archetype, Voice-to-Authority Pipeline.

**How to apply:** Before writing ANY content, DM script, outreach email, profile copy, or soundbite — check it against the Language Map and the Dr. Maya Patel "wince test." If Maya would wince, it's the wrong word. If she'd lean in, it's the right one.
