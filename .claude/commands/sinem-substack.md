## Quality Gate

Every output must pass the 10-criterion Quality Rubric in `genius.md`. Key fail signals:
- Treating the publication as the product (not infrastructure)
- Notes that open with tips instead of stories
- Paid tier described as "exclusive content" instead of tangible assets
- Promotional campaigns without names, dates, or exclusive incentives
- Collaboration outreach that leads with "I want your audience"
