## Boundary Handoffs

### Evidence to Alex Diagnosis

- **Source evidence:** account mix, customer language, competitor/reference structures
- **Output:** evidence receipt plus named white space
- **Validation:** every claim labeled observed, supplied, inferred, or unavailable
- **Open risk:** impressions rank is not conversion proof

### Alex Diagnosis to Concept Slate

- **Output:** persona/funnel lock, format choices, acquisition headlines, selling-locus choice, eye-path order
- **Validation:** every concept closes a named gap and uses real or clearly placeholder customer language
- **Open risk:** concept quality is not market demand

### Concept Slate to Production Specialist

- **Next input:** locked Alex packet
- **Validation:** specialist preserves persona, phrase provenance, hierarchy priority, and isolated test variable
- **Open risk:** generated or designed asset still needs human taste review

### Production to Comprehension/Test Gate

- **Output:** production-ready asset/spec and test cells
- **Validation:** one-second comprehension, source fidelity, legal/claim review when relevant, and single-variable integrity
- **Open risk:** only live controlled results establish performance
