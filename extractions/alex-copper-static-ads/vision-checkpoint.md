### What this source adds or sharpens

1. **The Acquisition Null-Context Test:** a cold headline must matter to someone who neither knows nor cares about the brand.
2. **The Selling-Locus Decision:** choose clarity when the ad must do the selling; choose curiosity when the post-click asset carries it; reject the middle.
3. **The Static Eye-Path Contract:** order the hook, qualifier, product, and proof using sizing, contrast, and whitespace.
4. **Customer-to-Customer Copy Transfer:** preserve emotionally loaded first-party phrases instead of polishing away their native credibility.
5. **White-Space-to-Test Loop:** generate only after finding an untested persona, angle, or format gap in account and VOC evidence.
6. **Felt-Experience Visualization:** make an invisible sensation visible through a grounded, intentionally unrealistic metaphor.
7. **Atypical Surface Placement:** place copy on a context the audience associates with the problem.
8. **Portfolio Logic:** treat top- and bottom-funnel statics as complementary, not competing doctrines.
