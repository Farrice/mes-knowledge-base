## Claim Limits

- Alex reports making more than 10,000 static ads and later refers to 30,000 ads. These are not necessarily contradictory because the latter may include non-static work, but both remain SELF-REPORTED.
- Spend and client-scale claims shown or spoken in the video are SELF-REPORTED. This package does not establish account access, causal lift, profitability, or incrementality.
- Parker rankings, tagging, and automated production results are demonstrated by the source but not independently validated here.
- Facebook Ad Library impressions are a directional observation proxy, not proof that an ad converts.
- Brand examples are exemplars shown by the source, not independently audited case studies.
- Captions appear to misrecognize some tool names. Parker is visually supported; references sounding like “Park RMP,” “Hexfield,” or “Hickfield” should not be normalized into vendor claims without separate verification.
- The video teaches a practical system, but it does not provide a controlled experiment, account-level data export, or a complete implementation specification for the claimed Claude/Parker automation.
