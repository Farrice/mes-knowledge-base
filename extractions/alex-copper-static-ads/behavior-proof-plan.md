# Behavior-Proof Plan: Static Acquisition System

## Fixture

Use a clearly fictional composite ecommerce brand so the workflow can be judged without inventing real business facts.

- **Brand:** Morrow Sleep
- **Product:** adjustable cooling pillow
- **Current account symptom:** discount-led product shots dominate; no customer-language acquisition statics
- **Persona:** hot sleeper who flips the pillow repeatedly at night
- **Supplied customer phrases:** “I stopped flipping my pillow to find the cold side”; “I woke up without a sweaty neck”; “My husband stole mine.”
- **Post-click destination:** persona-specific cooling-pillow landing page
- **Constraint:** no medical claims; no invented performance data; concept spec only

## Before

> Upgrade your sleep with our advanced cooling pillow. Shop now.

Weaknesses:

- assumes generic interest rather than immediate personal relevance
- no evidence trail or customer phrasing
- no persona/funnel decision
- no visual hook or eye-path order
- no stated test variable
- product feature leads before the viewer recognizes themselves

## Expected After Behavior

The built workflow must produce, at minimum:

1. **White space:** the “pillow flipper” night ritual is absent from the account.
2. **Persona/funnel lock:** cold acquisition static for hot sleepers; post-click page continues the same problem frame.
3. **Headline:** “Still flipping your pillow at 2 a.m.?” It is null-context relevant and clear.
4. **Customer proof:** preserve “I stopped flipping my pillow to find the cold side” as supplied language.
5. **Selling locus:** clarity; the ad must communicate the problem and category before the click.
6. **Visual metaphor:** a heat-map glow on the used half of a pillow versus a cool surface, labeled as a concept rather than proof.
7. **Atypical-text option:** the headline appears on a bedside surface or pillow seam only if legibility survives.
8. **Eye path:** headline → heat/cool contrast → product → supplied testimonial → CTA.
9. **Test cells:** isolate headline, visual metaphor, and testimonial format rather than changing all three at once.
10. **Proof honesty:** no prediction of ROAS, CPA, spend, or conversion lift.

## Acceptance Gate

- The after output is materially different from the before output because of source mechanics, not merely better prose.
- Every customer phrase is supplied or explicitly marked placeholder.
- The concept closes a named account gap.
- The persona, selling locus, and eye path are explicit.
- The test plan isolates one variable per comparison.
- Dara/Fraser/Omar handoffs preserve the locked Alex hypothesis.
- Remaining risk states that no live market performance has been proven.
