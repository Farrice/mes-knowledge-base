# Deep Extraction: Alex Cooper Static Ads 2026

## Extraction Boundary

This extraction is grounded in *The ULTIMATE Guide to Static Ads in 2026* and the preserved transcript and frames listed in `source-ledger.md`. It separates the video's observable method from Alex Cooper's self-reported outcomes and from Antigravity's operational synthesis.

## Operating Thesis

A static ad is not a small design project. It is a compressed acquisition system. Evidence decides the person and message; the funnel decides where the selling happens; the format carries that decision; visual hierarchy routes attention; the test plan isolates what the market is actually judging.

## Mechanics

### 1. Proven-Format Compression

- **Source evidence:** 02:12–05:19.
- **Mechanic:** Start from an established static structure, then change the evidence, persona, message, and offer fit.
- **Boundary:** A familiar format is a directional prior, not conversion proof.

### 2. Message Before Format

- **Source evidence:** 01:30 and the strategy-to-message-to-design lesson order at 00:42.
- **Mechanic:** Decide what must be understood or felt before choosing the visual container.
- **Failure prevented:** attractive layout carrying a generic or irrelevant proposition.

### 3. Funnel Portfolio, Not Format Monoculture

- **Source evidence:** 02:12–03:40.
- **Mechanic:** Treat headline-benefit, testimonial, offer, persona, collection, comparison, publication, press-hit, native, apology, post-it, and meme statics as a portfolio serving different funnel jobs.
- **Boundary:** The video does not establish a universal allocation or budget split.

### 4. Customer-to-Customer Language Transfer

- **Source evidence:** 02:07 and 14:31–15:04.
- **Mechanic:** Preserve emotionally loaded review or comment phrasing so the ad reads like one customer speaking to another.
- **Hard rule:** Never invent a customer phrase or clean away the part that carries lived meaning.

### 5. Persona-to-Path Continuity

- **Source evidence:** 06:43–09:18.
- **Mechanic:** A persona ad is the beginning of a full-funnel path; the landing page and product framing must continue the same person's problem and desired outcome.
- **Failure prevented:** a specific ad that drops into a generic page.

### 6. Acquisition Null-Context Test

- **Source evidence:** 10:59–11:36.
- **Mechanic:** Judge the headline as if the viewer has never heard of the brand and does not care yet.
- **Pass condition:** the right person can recognize relevance without prior product knowledge.

### 7. Ranked-Swipe Discovery as a Proxy

- **Source evidence:** 04:46–05:19.
- **Mechanic:** Use impression-ranked examples to find structures worth examining.
- **Boundary:** Impressions are an observation proxy, not proof of profitability, incrementality, or causal performance.

### 8. White-Space-First Ideation

- **Source evidence:** 13:50–14:05.
- **Mechanic:** Inventory current persona, message, format, and funnel coverage; every proposed concept must close a named gap.
- **Failure prevented:** adding more variations of what the account already overproduces.

### 9. Cross-Brand Structure Transplant

- **Source evidence:** 12:26–13:18.
- **Mechanic:** Borrow the information architecture of a strong ad while replacing its claims, voice, proof, and audience logic with brand-specific evidence.
- **Hard rule:** A reference ad donates structure, never claims.

### 10. Selling-Locus Decision

- **Source evidence:** 16:33–16:41.
- **Mechanic:** Choose whether the ad does most of the selling through clarity or earns the click through curiosity while the destination completes the sale.
- **Failure prevented:** an ad too vague to persuade and too explanatory to create curiosity.

### 11. Static Eye-Path Contract

- **Source evidence:** 17:01–18:27.
- **Mechanic:** Define the scan order before layout: hook, qualifier, product, proof, action. Use size, contrast, and whitespace to enforce that route.
- **Insight:** Visual hierarchy is message routing, not decoration.

### 12. Micro-Formatting Comprehension

- **Source evidence:** 19:00–19:22 and preserved frame `evidence/frames/frame_0136.jpg`.
- **Mechanic:** Small changes to line breaks, emphasis, and density can materially change first-impression comprehension.
- **Gate:** Audit what lands first, not merely whether every word is technically present.

### 13. Make the Invisible Felt Experience Visible

- **Source evidence:** 20:06–20:34.
- **Mechanic:** Convert an evidence-grounded sensation into an intentionally unrealistic visual metaphor.
- **Boundary:** The exaggeration may visualize a supplied feeling; it may not invent a product outcome.

### 14. Atypical Surface Placement

- **Source evidence:** 21:11–22:14.
- **Mechanic:** Put the message on a surface native to the audience or problem, such as a body area, fogged mirror, product seam, or bedside object.
- **Gate:** Novel placement loses if legibility or relevance suffers.

### 15. Tool-Agnostic Evidence-to-Variant Loop

- **Source evidence:** 13:18–16:00 and the demonstrated AI-assisted process.
- **Mechanic:** Use tools to compress account audit, review mining, and variant generation, while human judgment owns phrase selection, strategic fit, taste, and final quality.
- **Boundary:** Parker and other vendor mechanics are examples, not required infrastructure.

## Hidden Knowledge

1. **Narrowing and coverage are compatible.** Each ad should make one person feel specifically seen; the portfolio restores breadth across personas and funnel stages.
2. **The headline and hierarchy are the same strategic system.** Copy decides the first idea; design decides whether that idea actually arrives first.
3. **Competitor evidence is structural, not transferable proof.** A swipe can reveal ordering and format, but the new brand still needs its own customer language and substantiation.
4. **Unrealistic imagery works only when the feeling is real.** The visual may exaggerate a supplied sensation, never manufacture one.
5. **AI shortens the distance from evidence to options, not from options to truth.** Market feedback and human QC remain outside the generation loop.

## Hall of Fame Exemplars

### O Positive: “Faking It With My Husband”

The headline carries immediate emotional tension, while the visual and supporting copy create a deliberate scan path. The transferable lesson is not the subject matter; it is the compression of a private truth into a cold-viewer headline.

### Nerve Pain Killer: Formatting Comparison

The source shows how a small headline-formatting change improves consumption. The lesson is that comprehension can fail even when the words are correct; line breaks and emphasis decide whether the message survives a scan.

### Needles-in-the-Foot Visual

An invisible customer sensation becomes visually obvious. It works as an example of felt-experience translation, not as medical evidence or a claim that a product resolves the depicted condition.

### Bathroom-Mirror Condensation

### Persona Ad to Persona Page

A persona-specific static continues into a matching landing experience. It demonstrates that relevance is lost when the post-click destination broadens back to everyone.

## Signature Moves

1. **The Stranger Read:** hide the logo and ask whether a cold prospect can still identify relevance.
2. **The White-Space Receipt:** name the missing persona, message, format, or funnel job before generating a concept.
3. **The Selling-Locus Lock:** write one sentence stating what the ad sells and what the page must finish.
4. **The Phrase-Provenance Mark:** tag every emotionally loaded phrase as supplied, observed, inferred, or unavailable.
5. **The Eye-Path Sentence:** describe the intended scan order in one line before any layout work.
6. **The One-Variable Cell:** compare concepts only when the changed message, visual, format, or proof element is explicit.

## Quality Rubric

| Criterion | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Null-context relevance | The intended persona recognizes the problem or desire without brand familiarity. |
| Evidence fidelity | Customer language and claims have explicit provenance. |
| Persona continuity | The ad and destination maintain the same person and problem frame. |
| Format-message fit | The selected structure carries the intended message and funnel job. |
| Selling locus | Clarity or curiosity is chosen deliberately, with the destination's job stated. |
| Eye path | Hook, qualifier, product, proof, and action have an intentional order. |
| Felt-experience integrity | Any exaggerated visual maps to supplied or observed language. |
| Test isolation | Each comparison changes one named variable. |
| Tool independence | The method remains executable without a named vendor. |
| Proof honesty | No concept is described as a winner before controlled market evidence exists. |
