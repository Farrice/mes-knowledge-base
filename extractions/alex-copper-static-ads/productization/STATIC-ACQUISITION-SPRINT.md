## Client-facing offer candidate

### The problem

More static ads do not help when the account keeps repeating the same persona, message, format, or offer. A useful batch starts by finding what the account is missing. It then turns that gap into controlled creative tests.

### What the sprint does

The Static Acquisition Sprint turns an approved offer, customer language, landing page, and current ad inventory into five source-grounded static concepts. One lead concept becomes a three-asset test: a clean control and two challengers, each changing one variable.

### Best fit

An established DTC or performance brand should already have one hero product or offer, an approved landing page, real customer language, approved claims, current paid-social creative, and someone who can traffic and read the test.

This is a poor fit for a pre-evidence brand seeking invented proof, a full rebrand, or a guaranteed winner.

## Deliverables

1. **Evidence receipt:** separates supplied, observed, inferred, and unavailable material.
2. **Account white-space map:** reviews persona, message, format, offer, and funnel coverage.
3. **Five acquisition concepts:** ties each idea to one gap, one persona, one job, and one claim boundary.
4. **Locked copy and design specs:** records phrase provenance, selling locus, eye path, visual direction, and post-click continuity.
5. **One three-asset production batch:** supplies a control, visual challenger, and headline or information-order challenger in one feed placement.
6. **Comprehension audit:** returns a keep, fix, or kill decision for every produced file.
7. **Test handoff:** gives the media owner the launch order, isolated variable, metric definitions, decision rule, source ledger, and re-run instructions.

## How the engagement runs

### Evidence intake

The client provides the landing page, product and offer facts, approved substantiation, customer language, account inventory, brand assets, and rights status. Missing evidence stays marked unavailable.

### White-space decision

The sprint finds what the account overproduces and chooses one acquisition gap worth testing. Every concept must close it.

### Concept slate

Five concepts use a varied format mix. Each does one job and carries no more than two copy mechanics.

### Lead-test lock

One concept becomes the control. Two challengers isolate one variable each. Persona, offer, destination, and measurement stay fixed.

### Production and audit

The three lead files are produced and inspected. The comprehension gate fixes or kills weak work instead of padding the delivery count.

### Launch handoff

The client or media owner receives the files, claim ledger, test cells, decision rule, and internal run order. Account changes and media buying remain with that owner.

## Input gate

1. **Approved product and offer facts** keep the copy tied to a real promise. Missing facts put concept copy on hold.
2. **Claims matrix or substantiation** sets the implication boundary. Its absence stops production.
3. **Customer language with provenance** grounds the headlines. If none is supplied, the sprint uses no testimonial and records the research gap.
4. **Approved landing page** locks post-click continuity. An unsettled page pauses acquisition production.
5. **Current account inventory** makes the white-space diagnosis factual. No inventory means no account-gap claim.
6. **Rights-cleared product and brand assets** make the files usable. Unclear rights limit delivery to specifications.
7. **Named media owner and metric definitions** make the test interpretable. An ownerless test stops at creative readiness.

## Scope

The sprint covers one product or offer, one primary persona, five concept specifications, one three-asset lead test, one agreed feed aspect ratio, source and claim receipts, a comprehension audit, and one focused correction pass.

It does not include media buying, ad-account changes, landing-page production, legal or regulatory approval, a full campaign, video production, creator sourcing, unlimited variations, performance guarantees, or testimonial-rights clearance on the client's behalf.

## Completion standard

Every material phrase needs provenance. Each concept must close the named account gap. The ad and landing page must serve the same person, problem, promise, and proof standard. A cold viewer should identify what is sold in one second. The lead batch must change one variable at a time.

No file with an unresolved comprehension or substantiation fault may be called launch-ready. The handoff must distinguish implementation readiness from market proof.
