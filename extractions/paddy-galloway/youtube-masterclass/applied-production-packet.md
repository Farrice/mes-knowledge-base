# Can an AI video analyst earn your trust?

**Original worked application; unpublished.** This is a reduced creative sprint using this session's actual evidence. It demonstrates source-to-decision transfer; it is not an audience-performance test or a personal taste verdict.

Route: Paddy's connected sprint, audience map, idea funnel, title/thumbnail and intro workflows. Anti-slop pre-draft grounding: named anchor Paddy Galloway; sources are the full transcript, recovered Gemini response and independent visual/caption checks; stakes are trusting an automated analyst without wasting time or paying for repeated errors; mechanism is a specific test with an inspectable contradiction. Voice: plain internal production copy, with a tentative spoken draft for Farrice to judge.

## Goal and audience

Teach a creator how to decide what can be trusted in an AI video analysis. Core viewers already interested in Farrice's systems should get the operating method. Casual AI users should get a visible comparison. New viewers should understand the problem without knowing Codex or the skill library. These are hypothesized familiarity groups; no channel audience analytics were supplied.

Business goal: demonstrate useful judgment. A broad AI spectacle may attract more views while doing less to establish that judgment. No demand, retention or conversion baseline is available, so the content is a production hypothesis.

## Three distinct thumbnail briefs

**A. Same time, different scene.** The main subject is one large paused video frame with its timecode visible. Beside it, a small model excerpt names the astrophotography graphic it claims is there. A single line points to the missing graphic's claimed location. Optional text: “Wrong scene”. The title supplies the act of checking; the image supplies the contradiction. Capture: recreate the exact paused source frame and crop only the relevant model claim. Keep the excerpt legible at mobile size; if it cannot be read, replace the sentence with a labeled simple graphic and show the full evidence in the video. Never fabricate a source screenshot.

**B. The inspection moment.** Over-the-shoulder desk composition: one hand on the video timeline, model report visibly open beside it. The focal point is the timecode about to be checked. No shocked reaction, fake error dialog or invented bill. Optional text: “Check this”. Capture requires an original desk photo and staged screen composition; this brief is not a completed image. The question is whether the confident answer survives inspection.

**C. The useful answer with a broken coordinate.** A large excerpt card says an astrophotography comparison appears at 01:15:36. A second, smaller frame shows the actual desk conversation at that timestamp, connected by a visibly broken locator line. Minimal text: “Where is it?” Unlike A's evidence comparison or B's documentary scene, this is an explanatory composition. Render through the existing visual owner; verify legibility and avoid making the model's entire answer appear blank or useless.

Choose A for the first render because it can be built from real evidence and its disagreement is concrete. Test whether a viewer can describe that disagreement at a glance. These are briefs only; no images were generated and no design-performance claim is made.

## Capture and assembly plan

- Record a privacy-clean source player at 01:15:36, keeping the timecode readable. Crop account identity, recommendations and unrelated browser tabs.
- Record only the matching model claim. Label it “Gemini output” and retain its original wording in the evidence archive.
- Capture the late-video dog-profile example at 02:44:39 as a useful perception success, with the model's earlier timestamp qualified.
- Show the original timeout and recovered completed status as separate events. Hide project identifiers, keys and unrelated logs.
- Show the usage estimate as “about $0.81; invoice unverified”. Never display $10 as actual spending.
- End on one corrected title/thumbnail/capture decision. The viewer should leave able to apply the method without adopting the whole system.
