## Executive Summary
The central skill is allocating effort before production: define who should care, generate enough real alternatives, reject what cannot be packaged or delivered, then engineer an honest click-to-payoff path. A video can fail because its idea addresses too few relevant people even when its execution is polished. The reverse is also possible: a broad idea may bring viewers the business does not need.

Paddy’s strongest contribution here is the dependency chain. The audience through-line constrains the idea pool; the title helps define the thumbnail; the thumbnail brief constrains what must be captured; the intro confirms the package; the expected outcome constrains the post-release response. Breaking any handoff creates expensive downstream repair.
