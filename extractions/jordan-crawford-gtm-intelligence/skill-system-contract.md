## Cold-start command

“Use `jordan-crawford-gtm-intelligence` on this GTM situation. Diagnose the earliest missing truth layer, cite the evidence paths you used, run only the matching workflow, preserve fact/quote/inference/unknown separation, and return the artifact, validation, open risk, and next gated action.”

## Authority boundary

This system may research public sources when authorized and may create local artifacts. It does not send messages, mutate CRMs, publish, purchase enrichment, invoke paid tools, or promote itself globally without explicit approval.
