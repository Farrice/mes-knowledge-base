# Behavior-Changing Proof Report

## Proof A — Conflicting mature-company evidence

- **Input tested:** fictional product events, calls, support, CRM labels, sales claims, and counterexamples.
- **Weakness diagnosed:** the naive baseline treated `PRICE` as causal and jumped to demographic targeting and discount messaging.
- **Source mechanics used:** actions > voice > CRM; durable evidence versus inference; conflicts preserved; worst-version problem; prediction-saturation research.
- **Output produced:** `mature-company-fixture.md`.
- **Behavior delta:** the system found an adoption-conditioned value mechanism, retained uncertainty, and chose a causal research action before copy.
- **Validation:** every material interpretation cites fixture evidence; no unsupported market claim or external action.
- **Remaining risk:** synthetic data proves operating behavior, not real demand or causal truth.

## Proof B — Zero-data founder

- **Input tested:** idea, founder belief, no customer data, public-research permission, no outreach permission.
- **Weakness diagnosed:** the naive baseline fabricated an ICP, pains, and positioning from category stereotypes.
- **Source mechanics used:** known-good reverse engineering, Five Cs, observable traces, PQS before PVP, prediction saturation, protected unknown state.
- **Output produced:** `zero-data-fixture.md`.
- **Behavior delta:** the system refused false confidence and returned a bounded research path with permission boundaries.
- **Validation:** no invented customer quote, behavior, market size, or outreach action.
- **Remaining risk:** public traces must be tested against direct customer evidence before qualification.
