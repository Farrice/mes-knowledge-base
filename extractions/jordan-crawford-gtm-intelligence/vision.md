# Jordan Crawford GTM Intelligence OS — Extraction Vision

## Checkpoint 1 decision

Build Jordan Crawford as a **new expert and one evidence-first GTM Intelligence OS**, not as a cold-email persona and not as an unbounded marketing mega-skill.

The system's job is to turn customer truth into a narrow market choice, a defensible evidence model, a problem-qualified target universe, an independently valuable message, and a reusable learning loop. It should improve Antigravity's marketing judgment upstream of positioning, offers, copy, campaigns, and outreach.

## The truer job

Farrice's recurring GTM problem is not primarily a shortage of copy prompts. It is that market choice, customer evidence, targeting, message, and feedback have often lived as separate activities. Jordan's public methodology gives those parts a causal order:

```text
customer actions and voice
  -> evidence-safe dossiers
  -> narrow problem/segment selection
  -> external indicators and target universe
  -> independently useful value
  -> recipient-ready message
  -> market response and learning
  -> better dossiers and sharper selection
```

That loop is the proposed operating spine.

## Uniqueness audit

Jordan adds a combination that the current roster does not own end to end:

1. **Information asymmetry as the GTM objective.** Know the next customer's situation better than they can cheaply assemble it themselves.
2. **The list is the message.** Targeting quality reduces the amount of persuasion the copy must perform.
3. **Evidence before judgment.** Build durable customer/prospect dossiers with field provenance and surfaced conflicts; score or recommend only downstream.
4. **Actions > customer voice > CRM-derived state.** A practical truth hierarchy when systems disagree.
5. **Problem-qualified segmentation.** Segment by shared costly situation and observable tension, not employee-count bands or generic personas.
6. **Segment -> company -> person fit.** The concentric-circle test separates market selection, account selection, and buyer-language discovery.
7. **High-DMI market choice.** Prefer markets where the product's value becomes obvious by describing what it does in that buyer's actual workflow.
8. **Permissionless Value Proposition.** Synthesize internal insight and public data into something the prospect would value even without taking a meeting.
9. **Work backward from known-good examples.** Manual research becomes the training evidence for programmatic research; code follows observed truth.
10. **Cost per right answer.** Use deterministic/public data first, escalate uncertain cases, and reserve expensive model judgment for work that actually needs judgment.
11. **Compounding work units.** Every reusable unit has named inputs, outputs, evaluation, conservative edge-case handling, and human observability.
12. **Safe operational boundary.** AI can research and prepare at high speed; irreversible CRM writes, publishing, deletion, and uncontrolled spend remain gated.

Exact workflow names, inputs, outputs, tier assignments, and prompt count belong to Checkpoint 2 after deep MES extraction. This Vision does not pre-authorize a bloated workflow count.

## Production standard

The built system must:

- trace every Jordan-specific mechanism to the source ledger;
- label expert claim, synthesis, inference, and current-tool fact separately;
- keep evidence artifacts distinct from scores and recommended actions;
- output explicit unknowns and failed searches instead of converting silence into absence;
- preserve a human checkpoint for identity joins, high-stakes scoring, message taste, and irreversible actions;
- include local fixtures that test a mature-customer case and a zero-data founder case;
- prove runtime behavior with a realistic GTM problem, not file presence alone;
- remain useful without paid vendor APIs by providing a deterministic/manual fallback path;
- treat public-data acquisition, privacy, outreach, CRM writes, and paid tools as separate permission gates;
- ship as a workspace-native skill system first. Plugin packaging is a later readiness decision, not part of the initial promotion claim.

## Senior-partner verdict

**Build the OS. Park the plugin.**

The unfair advantage is an evidence-to-message learning system that improves every marketing decision in Antigravity. A plugin may eventually be a strong product container, but only after the underlying skill system passes cold-start, applied-output, privacy, and portability tests. Packaging it earlier would add surface area before proving repeatable value.
