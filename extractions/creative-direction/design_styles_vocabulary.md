# 61+ Design Style Vocabulary Research (DesignZig + Kittl + Multiple Sources)

## Complete Design Style Vocabulary with AI Prompt Keywords

1. Aurora — Luminous gradients, dreamy color transitions (purples, pinks, blues, greens), Northern Lights effect
2. Coquette — Soft romantic, delicate florals, pastel tones, lace, ribbons, flirty
3. Bento Grid — Compartmentalized layout, modular, Japanese-inspired organization
4. Cybercore — Neon-lit, circuit board motifs, metallic finishes, hard drop shadows, digital speed
5. Memphis Revival — Loud, geometric, bold, playful postmodern, experimental patterns
6. Brutalism — Stark layouts, bold monochrome, unrefined typography, raw, anti-conventional
7. Vaporwave — Old-school computer graphics, Greek busts, neon hues, glitchy, nostalgic/satirical
8. Synthwave — 80s nostalgia, neon grids, palm trees, sunset gradients, arcade aesthetic
9. Y2K — Grungy textures, neon/bubbly/futuristic typography, tech-optimistic millennium look
10. Flat Design — 2D elements, bright colors, simple icons, no shadows/gradients
11. Minimalism — Clean lines, white space, restricted palette, "less is more"
12. Japandi — Japanese minimalism + Scandinavian warmth, neutral, natural materials, serene
13. Organic Design — Natural forms, fluid lines, earthy textures, nature-inspired palette
14. 3D Design — Depth, volume, shading, perspective, objects popping off surface
15. Photorealism — Photographic accuracy, highly detailed, realistic rendering
16. Illustrated Design — Custom illustrations as primary visual, whimsical to sophisticated
17. Playful Design — Bright colors, whimsical characters, rounded shapes, lighthearted
18. Masculine Design — Darker palettes, strong lines, bold sans-serif, metal/wood textures
19. Feminine Design — Soft hues, delicate fonts, floral motifs, graceful lines
20. Corporate Design — Professional, clean layouts, sans-serif, muted palette, trust
21. Ethereal — Soft hues, delicate layering, airy, otherworldly, dreamlike
22. Mystical Western — Cowboy + cosmic/spiritual, desert landscapes, celestial symbols
23. Farmhouse — Rustic wood, muted earth tones, vintage signage, handwritten fonts
24. Kitsch — Bright colors, tacky charm, retro pop culture, tongue-in-cheek
25. Conceptual Sketch — Doodle art, hand-drawn feel, rough outlines, creative process visible
26. Utilitarian Design — Function-first, grid-based, practical typography, no decoration
27. Anthropomorphic Design — Animals/objects with human traits, character-driven
28. Pixel Art — Retro gaming, blocky, limited palette, nostalgic 8-bit/16-bit
29. Grunge — Dirty textures, torn edges, distressed type, raw, rebellious
30. Art Nouveau — Flowing organic lines, floral motifs, decorative, nature-inspired curves
31. Art Deco — Geometric patterns, metallic colors, symmetry, luxury, 1920s glamour
32. Bauhaus — Form follows function, primary colors, geometric shapes, grid-based
33. Swiss/International Style — Grid systems, sans-serif type (Helvetica), clean, objective
34. Pop Art — Bold colors, comic book dots, mass media imagery, Warhol/Lichtenstein
35. Psychedelic — Vibrant swirling colors, distorted typography, trippy, 60s counterculture
36. Punk/DIY — Cut-and-paste, ransom note type, anarchic, raw, anti-establishment
37. Gothic — Dark, ornate, blackletter type, medieval references, dramatic
38. Baroque — Ornate, dramatic, rich colors, gold, elaborate detail, grandeur
39. Neoclassical — Greek/Roman references, columns, symmetry, restrained elegance
40. Risograph — Limited color overlap, grain, halftone dots, imperfect registration
41. Acid Graphics — Warped, melting, chrome, psychedelic, rave culture, distorted
42. Kawaii — Cute, pastel, rounded characters, Japanese cute culture
43. Steampunk — Victorian + industrial, brass, gears, clockwork, sepia
44. Retro-Futurism — Past visions of the future, space age, atomic, optimistic tech
45. Mid-Century Modern — Clean lines, organic curves, bold color, 1950s-60s
46. Dark Academia — Scholarly, muted browns/greens, classical literature, old libraries
47. Cottagecore — Rural idyll, floral, handmade, pastoral, warm, cozy
48. Bohemian — Eclectic, layered patterns, earthy + jewel tones, free-spirited
49. Maximalism — More is more, layered, pattern-heavy, bold color, eclectic
50. Skeuomorphism — Realistic textures mimicking real objects, leather, wood, glass
51. Glassmorphism — Frosted glass effect, transparency, blur, floating layers
52. Neumorphism — Soft shadows, extruded shapes, monochrome, subtle 3D
53. Claymorphism — Clay-like 3D, soft rounded forms, pastel, tactile
54. Afrofuturism — African diaspora + technology/sci-fi, vibrant, cultural, futuristic
55. Wabi-Sabi — Imperfection as beauty, asymmetry, natural wear, organic
56. Kinetic Typography — Moving text, animated type, dynamic, motion-based
57. Duotone — Two-color treatment, high contrast, bold, modern
58. Gradient Mesh — Complex color blending, photorealistic gradients, depth
59. Isometric Design — 3D objects on 2D plane, 30-degree angles, technical
60. Collage/Mixed Media — Cut-and-paste, layered textures, analog + digital, eclectic
61. Signal Graphics — Retro computer graphics, CRT aesthetic, early digital, scan lines

Source: DesignZig, Kittl, UX Planet, Looka, Big Human, Noun Project
