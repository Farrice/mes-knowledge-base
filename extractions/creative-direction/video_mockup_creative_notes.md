# Video Prompting, Mockups & Creative Direction Research

## Six-Layer Video Prompt Framework (Venice.ai)
1. Subject & Action: Who/what, movement, emotional state
2. Shot Type & Framing: Wide/medium/close-up, angle (eye level, low, high)
3. Camera Movement: Static, tracking, panning, dolly (slow = cinematic)
4. Lighting & Atmosphere: Golden hour, blue hour, studio, fog, rain
5. Technical Specs: Lens (35mm wide, 50mm natural, 85mm portrait, macro), f-stop, film grain, color palette
6. Duration & Pacing: 3-10 seconds, slow motion, time-lapse, transitions

## Camera Movement Vocabulary
- Dolly in/out: Smooth forward/backward
- Dolly Zoom (Vertigo Effect): Zoom while moving opposite direction
- Dutch Angle: Slight tilt for tension
- Slow Pan/Tilt: Horizontal/vertical rotation
- Tracking shot: Following subject
- Crane/Jib: Vertical sweeping movement
- Crash zoom: Fast dramatic zoom
- Orbit: Circular movement around subject
- Steadicam: Smooth handheld feel

## Modular B-Roll System (Hailuo AI)
- Master Reference Image (MRI): Spend 30% of time on this anchor image
- Environment Module: "where" and "what" — setting + primary subject
- Lighting & Mood Module: Chiaroscuro, Golden Hour, High-Key, Cinematic Sidelight
- Motion Module: ONE primary motion per prompt (avoid contradictions)
- Cinematic Grammar Module: Exact terminology for camera + lens
- Reroll Budget: 3-5 variations per desired clip
- Clip Length: 4-6 seconds optimal for temporal stability

## AI Mockup Formula (Blanca Villalobos)
Formula: Style + Subject + Action + Setting + Background + Special Instructions

### Breakdown:
- Style: "sleek, minimalist product photography with Scandinavian aesthetics"
- Subject: Be hyper-specific about the product
- Action: Add movement/interaction (pouring, holding, placing)
- Setting: Studio, outdoors, indoors — unexpected settings = more interesting
- Background: Use contrasts (modern product + cement wall)
- Special Instructions: Camera type, lighting, quality, motion blur, depth of field

### Example Prompts:
- "Mockup_photography of a vibrant yellow serum bottle with a dropper, placed on a top of a rock covered in moss, floral arrangement, green-gradient, studio lighting, hyper-detailed texture, 4K resolution, best quality, natural beauty aesthetic, earthy tones"
- "Realistic product editorial photography, a green glass serum bottle with a dropper, placed on a moss-covered rock floating on water, soft natural lighting, hyper-detailed texture, best quality, 4K, minimalist style"

## Key Principles Across All Platforms
- Describe what you WANT, not what you don't want (positive framing)
- Start with strong verb
- Be specific but not contradictory
- One primary motion per video prompt
- Use real cinematography/photography terminology
- Iterate conversationally
- Reference real camera hardware for "visual DNA"
- Specify film stock/color grading for emotional tone
