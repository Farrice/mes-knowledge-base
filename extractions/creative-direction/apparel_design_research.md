# Apparel Design, T-Shirt Placement & Production Research

## T-Shirt Design Placement Standards (2026)
### Front Placements
- Left Chest: Classic logo spot. Under 4" wide. Subtle, versatile. Brand mark, minimal streetwear.
- Center Chest: 3-3.5" below collar. 8-10" wide. Logos, text-based, simple artwork.
- Full Front: 11"x11" adult standard. Large graphics, photo prints, statement pieces.
- Oversized Front: 12-14" wide. Streetwear staple. Bold, graphic-heavy.
- Pocket Print: 3-4" square. Small graphic or logo on pocket area.

### Back Placements
- Center Back: 6" below collar hem. 12" wide standard. Large graphics, brand statements.
- Upper Back (Yoke): Between shoulders. 10-12" wide. Clean, athletic.
- Full Back: 13x19" maximum film size. Statement graphics, all-over designs.
- Lower Back: Rarely used in streetwear. More common in workwear.

### Specialty Placements
- Sleeve Print: 3-4" wide. Logo, text, small graphic on upper sleeve.
- Collar Print (Inside): Brand tag replacement. Inside neck label.
- Hem Tag: Woven label at bottom hem. Brand identifier.
- Side Seam: Small logo or text along side.

## Streetwear Graphic Design Principles
1. SCALE CONTRAST: Mix oversized graphics with small details
2. PLACEMENT HIERARCHY: Front = hero graphic, back = supporting, sleeve = accent
3. NEGATIVE SPACE: Let the garment breathe, don't fill every inch
4. COLOR LIMITATION: 1-4 colors maximum for screen print. Constraint breeds creativity.
5. TYPOGRAPHY DOMINANCE: Text-heavy designs are streetwear's signature
6. DISTRESS & TEXTURE: Vintage, worn, cracked effects add authenticity
7. CULTURAL REFERENCE: Music, art, film, subculture references create depth
8. IRONY & WIT: Humor, parody, subversion of corporate/luxury aesthetics
9. SEASONAL STORYTELLING: Each collection tells a cohesive story
10. WEARABILITY: The design must work ON a body, not just on screen

## Mockup Creation Methods
- Adobe Illustrator Mockup Tool: Paste design + garment, click "Mockup" — AI-powered
- Photoshop Smart Objects: PSD templates with smart object layers for easy design swap
- AI Mockup Generation: Use Midjourney/Flux to generate photorealistic mockups
- Online Tools: Placeit, Printful mockup generator, Canva
- 3D Mockups: CLO3D, Browzwear for realistic 3D garment visualization
