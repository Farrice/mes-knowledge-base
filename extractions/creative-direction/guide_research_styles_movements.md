## Key Design Movements Timeline
- Arts & Crafts (1880s): Handmade, anti-industrial, ornamental
- Art Nouveau (1890s): Organic curves, flowing lines, nature-inspired
- Art Deco (1920s): Geometric, symmetrical, luxurious, metallic
- Bauhaus (1919-1933): Form follows function, grid systems, sans-serif, primary colors
- Swiss/International Style (1950s): Grid-based, clean, objective, Helvetica
- Pop Art (1960s): Bold colors, mass culture, comic-style, Ben-Day dots
- Psychedelic (1960s-70s): Swirling, vibrant, distorted, trippy
- Punk/DIY (1970s-80s): Ransom note, collage, raw, anti-establishment
- Memphis (1980s): Bold patterns, clashing colors, geometric shapes, playful
- Grunge (1990s): Distressed, dirty, layered, photocopied textures
- Y2K (1999-2003): Chrome, bubble fonts, iridescent, digital optimism
- Minimalism: White space, reduction, clean lines, less is more
- Brutalism: Raw, unfinished, monochrome, heavy typography, confrontational
- Maximalism: More is more, layered, dense, ornamental, overwhelming
- Vaporwave: 80s/90s nostalgia, pastel, Roman busts, glitch, retro-digital
- Synthwave: Neon, dark, 80s retro-futurism, grid landscapes, chrome

## Lighting Setups (from StudioBinder)
- Natural Light: Sun, moon, fire — organic, uncontrolled
- Artificial Light: LED panels, tungsten, HMI — controlled
- Ambient Light: Existing light on set (neon signs, streetlights)
- Practical Light: Light source visible in frame (lamp, candle, TV)
- High-Key: Bright, even, minimal shadows — comedy, beauty, commercial
- Low-Key: Dark, dramatic shadows — noir, thriller, horror
- Chiaroscuro: Extreme contrast between light and dark — Renaissance, dramatic
- Rembrandt: Triangle of light on cheek — classic portrait, dramatic
- Split Lighting: Half face lit, half in shadow — mystery, duality
- Butterfly/Paramount: Light from above, shadow under nose — glamour, beauty
- Loop Lighting: Small shadow of nose on cheek — flattering, natural
- Rim/Back Light: Light from behind, edge glow — separation, ethereal
- Golden Hour: Warm, soft, low sun — romantic, warm, cinematic
- Blue Hour: Cool, twilight — mysterious, melancholic
- Magic Hour: Just after sunset — soft, diffused, magical
- Motivated Lighting: Matches a logical source in the scene
