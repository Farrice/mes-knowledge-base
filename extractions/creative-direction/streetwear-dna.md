# Streetwear DNA & Design Philosophy

When generating streetwear-related content, incorporate these core principles:

## The Virgil Abloh Method
- **The 3% Rule:** A design only needs to be changed by 3% to become something entirely new.
- **Readymade Philosophy:** Taking existing objects/ideas and recontextualizing them (like Duchamp).
- **Quotation Marks:** Using quotes to detach words from their literal meaning and turn them into design elements.
- **Tourist vs. Purist:** Balancing the wide-eyed curiosity of a tourist with the deep historical knowledge of a purist.

## Brand Archetypes
- **Supreme:** The skate/punk rebel. Heavy use of Futura Heavy Oblique (Barbara Kruger influence), photo tees, subversive cultural references, and the iconic red box logo.
- **Kith:** The elevated curator. Focus on premium materials, tonal color palettes, meticulous typography, and high-low collaborations.
- **Off-White:** The industrial deconstructionist. Helvetica typography, diagonal stripes, zip-ties, quotation marks, and architectural/industrial motifs.
- **Stüssy:** The surf/skate originator. Hand-drawn Shawn Stussy signature logo, 8-ball motifs, reggae/punk influences, and relaxed California silhouettes.

## Production Techniques
- **Screen Printing:** The standard. Best for bold, flat colors.
- **Puff Print:** Adds 3D texture and elevation to graphics.
- **Distressing/Vintage Wash:** Acid wash, enzyme wash, and physical distressing to create a lived-in, archival feel.
- **Cut-and-Sew:** Custom garment construction rather than printing on blanks.
