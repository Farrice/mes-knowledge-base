# Kittl Video & Canvas Workflow Research

## Kittl Video (Launched Feb 3, 2026)
- Design → Motion workflow directly on the canvas
- No timelines, no keyframes, no separate export
- Best for 4-8 second clips (social ads, product highlights, launch announcements, brand posts)

## Workflow Steps:
1. Open Kittl, start a new design / create an image / animate text
2. Open AI panel → "Generate your video"
3. Choose Start frame (required): design, image, text, or Smartboard
4. (Optional) Choose End frame to guide how motion finishes
5. Write prompt describing the motion
6. Choose settings: Model, Duration, Audio
7. Click Generate → video appears as tile on canvas
8. Export as MP4

## 5 Prompting Best Practices:
1. Use modular structure: CAMERA, ACTION, AUDIO, TEXT blocks
2. Describe camera motion with clear terms: push-in, pull-back, slow pan, subtle handheld
3. Describe subject first, then action
4. Focus on one main action (multiple actions break consistency)
5. Avoid over-directing (fewer, clearer instructions = smoother motion)

## Example Prompt:
"Camera: perfectly static shot, camera frozen in place on a tripod
Action: The subject drops onto the rock surface. Subtle dust and small debris lift and disperse on impact.
SFX: Soft thud, faint grit scattering."
