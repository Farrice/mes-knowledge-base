# Cinematography Research Notes

## Shot Sizes (from widest to tightest)
- Extreme Wide Shot (EWS/ELS): Subject tiny against vast landscape. Use: establishing scale, isolation, epic scope.
- Wide Shot / Long Shot (WS/LS): Full subject with environment. Use: context, geography, movement.
- Full Shot (FS): Head to toe, filling frame. Use: body language, action, costume.
- Medium Wide Shot (MWS): Knees up. Use: group shots, movement with context.
- Cowboy Shot (CS): Mid-thigh up. Use: power, confrontation (from Westerns).
- Medium Shot (MS): Waist up. Use: dialogue, interaction, most versatile.
- Medium Close-Up (MCU): Chest up. Use: facial expressions with some body language.
- Close-Up (CU): Face fills frame. Use: emotion, reaction, intimacy.
- Extreme Close-Up (ECU): Single feature (eye, mouth, hand). Use: tension, detail, emphasis.

## Camera Angles
- Eye Level: Neutral, natural, relatable.
- High Angle: Looking down. Subject feels small, weak, vulnerable.
- Low Angle: Looking up. Subject feels powerful, dominant, heroic.
- Dutch Angle / Tilt: Camera tilted. Disorientation, unease, chaos.
- Bird's Eye View: Directly overhead. God-like perspective, patterns, scale.
- Worm's Eye View: From the ground looking up. Extreme power, towering.
- Over-the-Shoulder (OTS): Behind one character's shoulder. Connection, conversation.
- Point of View (POV): What the character sees. Immersion, empathy.

## Camera Movements
- Static / Fixed: No movement. Stability, observation, formality.
- Pan: Horizontal rotation. Reveal, follow, survey.
- Tilt: Vertical rotation. Reveal height, scale.
- Dolly In/Out: Camera moves toward/away. Intimacy vs. isolation.
- Tracking / Dolly: Moves alongside subject. Connection, energy.
- Crab Shot: Horizontal dolly. Lateral reveal.
- Arc Shot: Circles around subject. Drama, transformation, importance.
- Crane / Jib: Sweeping vertical movement. Epic reveal, grandeur.
- Steadicam: Smooth handheld. Fluid, immersive, following.
- Handheld: Shaky, raw. Urgency, documentary, chaos.
- Whip Pan / Swish Pan: Fast pan with blur. Transition, energy, surprise.
- Zoom: Focal length change. Emphasis, shock (crash zoom), reveal.
- Dolly Zoom (Vertigo): Dolly + opposite zoom. Disorientation, realization.
- Drone: Aerial. Scale, beauty, establishing.

## Focus Techniques
- Shallow DOF (f/1.4-2.8): Subject sharp, background creamy bokeh. Intimacy, isolation.
- Deep Focus (f/8-16): Everything sharp. Context, environment, Wes Anderson.
- Rack Focus: Shift focus between subjects. Redirect attention, reveal.
- Split Diopter: Two focal planes simultaneously. Tension, dual narrative.
- Tilt-Shift: Selective focus, miniature effect.
- Soft Focus: Dreamy, romantic, ethereal.

## Lens Focal Lengths
- Ultra Wide (14-24mm): Distortion, epic landscapes, architectural.
- Wide (24-35mm): Environmental portraits, street, documentary.
- Normal (50mm): Natural perspective, closest to human eye.
- Short Telephoto (85mm): Portrait king, flattering compression, shallow DOF.
- Telephoto (100-200mm): Compression, voyeuristic, sports, wildlife.
- Macro (100mm+): Extreme detail, texture, product.
