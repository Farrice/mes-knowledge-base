# Trailer Sound Design & Music Architecture Research

## 10 SIGNATURE SOUND ELEMENTS OF MODERN TRAILERS (Audio Imperia / A Sound Effect)

1. The "Alarm" — Prometheus teaser (Audiomachine "Judge and Jury"). Recurring alarm/siren motif that creates urgency.
2. The "BRAAAM" — Inception (Zack Hemsey "Mind Heist"). Long low brassy foghorn notes. Most copied sound in trailer history.
3. "Distortion Booms" & "Stutter Downers" — Elysium (Hi-Finesse "Radius"). Heavy bass impacts with stuttering decay.
4. "Pulsating Atmospheres" — Titanfall (Danny Cocke "Sinister Intent"). Rhythmic atmospheric textures that create tension.
5. "Distorted Drum Kits & Synthesizers" — Ninja Tracks style. Aggressive electronic percussion with synth layers.
6. "Distorted Risers" — Avengers: Age of Ultron (Twelve Titans Music "Artifice"). Pitched-up noise sweeps building to impact.
7. "Filtered Guitar Alarm" — Mission Impossible: Rogue Nation (Think Up Anger "Mission Ready"). Guitar processed through filters.
8. "Bowed Metal Sounds" — Interstellar (Confidential Music "View From The Voyager"). Eerie metallic resonance.
9. "Trailerized Song Covers" — Mad Max: Fury Road (Cat Stevens "Wild World" remix). Familiar songs recontextualized.
10. "Machine Gun Electric Guitar" — Oblivion, World War Z, Star Trek Into Darkness (Attila Ats "Shredder"). Rapid-fire guitar staccato.

Key insight: "Musical USPs" — single signature sounds that make a trailer instantly recognizable.

## THREE-ACT STRUCTURE OF TRAILER MUSIC (Tonal Chaos, 2026)

### Act 1: The Setup (first 20-30 seconds)
- Atmospheric foundation: minimal, mysterious, tension-building
- Single piano notes, subtle string pads, distant percussion, ethereal synth textures
- Goal: establish mood and intrigue without revealing your hand
- Drawing audience in, asking questions not providing answers

### Act 2: The Escalation
- Rhythm enters: ticking clock motif, war drum pattern, hybrid electronic pulse
- Melodic elements emerge more clearly
- Strings swell, brass hints at what's coming, tempo pushes toward climax
- CRITICAL: restraint — building tension without prematurely spending dynamic capital

### Act 3: The Climax
- Full orchestral or hybrid impact moment — the payoff
- Everything converges: massive brass hits, thunderous percussion, soaring strings, choir swells, processed vocals, sub drops
- "Money note" — synchronized with hero shot, title reveal, or final montage
- Works BECAUSE you earned it through the buildup

### The "Money Note" Landing Requirements:
1. Dynamic Contrast — climax must be noticeably bigger than everything before
2. Harmonic Resolution — moving from dissonant/suspended to powerful major chord/tonic
3. Rhythmic Alignment — hit must land frame-perfect with hero shot or title card

### Building Tension Techniques:
- Negative Space — sparse arrangement with well-placed pauses
- Risers & Sub Drops — pitched-up synth sweeps, reverse cymbals, low-frequency rumbles
- Evolving Textures — subtle harmonic shifts, incremental new elements

### 2026 Trailer Music Trends:
1. Hybrid orchestral-electronic fusion (dominant aesthetic)
2. Vocal chops and processed choir
3. Minimalist piano openings that explode into full production
4. Retro synthwave elements in sci-fi trailers
5. World music percussion for global appeal

## DEREK LIEU — 4-ACT TRAILER STRUCTURE (not just 3)
- Cold Open (Act 0): Hook before the title card. Its own mini-act.
- Act 1: Setup — world, character, premise
- Act 2: Escalation — conflict, stakes, complications
- Act 3: Climax — rapid montage, peak intensity, title reveal

## MAPPING TO AI CREATIVE WORKFLOWS

### For Higgsfield Video Sequences:
- Cold Open = First 3 seconds, maximum visual impact, no context needed
- Act 1 = Establishing shot, world-building, atmospheric
- Act 2 = Character/product introduction, rising action, faster cuts
- Act 3 = Rapid montage of best shots, hero moment, title/logo reveal

### For Social Media Content:
- Hook (0-3s) = Cold Open — pattern interrupt, curiosity gap
- Context (3-10s) = Act 1 — establish what this is about
- Build (10-30s) = Act 2 — escalate value, tension, or entertainment
- Payoff (30-60s) = Act 3 — deliver the climax, CTA, or resolution
