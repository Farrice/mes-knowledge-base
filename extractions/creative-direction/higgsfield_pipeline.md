## The 9-Step Production Pipeline (One-Person Studio)

### Step 1: Sketch-to-Video (Pre-visualization)
- Upload product silhouettes, scene layouts, or quick sketches
- Sketch-to-Video reads perspective and motion intention
- Converts lines into animatic-style motion
- Get immediate sense of pacing, composition, camera placement
- This becomes your "living blueprint"

### Step 2: Generate Core Footage (Sora 2 / Seedance / Veo / Kling)
- Describe scene, mood, action in text prompt
- For product work: be explicit about materials, light sources, camera behavior
- For lifestyle: give location cues, time of day, movement verbs
- Iterate quickly because generations are fast

### Step 3: Direct the Look with Presets & Camera Control
- Preset Library locks tone and pacing:
  - "Luxury Ad" = soft gradients, slow moves
  - "Japanese Ad" = minimal composition, precise typography
  - "Dynamic Sport Ad" = tight cuts, high contrast
  - "Gen-Z TikTok Edit" = beat-matched transitions, vertical timing
  - "Unpacking Video" = hands, packaging, in-use immersion
  - "First-Person POV" = immersive perspective
- WAN Camera Controls: push-ins, pans, focus pulls, reveal paths

### Step 4: Remove AI Artifacts (Sora 2 Enhancer)
- Analyzes frame sequences
- Normalizes tone, color, motion
- Removes micro flicker, exposure drift, inconsistencies
- Preserves creative intent
- "Non-negotiable" for ad work

### Step 5: Upscale Legacy/Non-AI Clips (Higgsfield Upscale)
- Designed for non-AI footage
- Reconstructs fine detail, improves micro-contrast
- Preserves natural gradients
- 1080p archive clips can match AI footage quality

### Step 6: Character Consistency (SoulID)
- Stores stable facial structure and style
- Same face across scenes/episodes
- Solves continuity problem across multi-video narratives

### Step 7: Trend Alignment (Sora 2 Trends)
- Align pacing, composition, motion energy with current platform trends
- Guidance for rhythm and structure
- Trend awareness without copycat output

### Step 8: Stills & Thumbnails (Photodump Studio)
- Generate cohesive stills matching motion work
- Consistent color logic and framing
- For grids, banners, thumbnails

### Step 9: Standardize Visual Identity (Preset Library)
- Lock brand into repeatable look
- Primary preset for tone/motion
- Variants for product lines or seasonal edits
- Functions as lightweight brand system

## Workflow Organization Principles:
1. Define creative objective first (end use: marketing, teaching, art, social)
2. Build modular generation stages (intro, focus scene, emotional peak, ending)
3. Refine consistency and style (Enhancer + Upscale as standard, not repair)
4. Maintain visual identity (SoulID + Presets)
5. Organize camera movement early (WAN Camera Controls)
6. Use trends for strategy and timing
7. Manage assets in unified workspace
8. Plan distribution and output in advance (vertical, square, 16:9)

## Sample One-Day Build:
- Morning: Draft sketches → Generate base shots → Choose presets + camera moves
- Afternoon: Run Enhancer → Upscale legacy clips → Generate SoulID shots
- Evening: Align pacing with Trends → Build platform variants → Export multi-format
