# Design Principles Research (Figma + Multiple Sources)

## 13 Core Graphic Design Principles
1. Alignment - arrangement of text/elements to create order, visual connection (left, center, right, justified)
2. Contrast - noticeable difference between elements (color, size, texture, shape, weight)
3. Balance - distributing visual weight (symmetrical vs asymmetrical)
4. Hierarchy - arranging/emphasizing elements by importance (size, position, color, weight)
5. Color - evoking emotions, communicating messages (color theory + color psychology)
6. White Space - empty area between elements, breathing room, prevents clutter
7. Proportion - size/weight of elements relative to each other
8. Repetition - reusing identical/similar elements for cohesion (colors, textures, shapes, patterns, fonts)
9. Rhythm - sense of movement/flow through placement/spacing (regular, random, flowing, progressive)
10. Movement - guiding user's eye through design (Z-pattern, F-pattern, layer cake)
11. Emphasis - highlighting main focal point
12. Proximity - placement of objects to show relationship (closer = related)
13. Unity - all elements flow seamlessly into unified composition

## Composition Techniques (28+ from PetaPixel)
- Rule of Thirds: Grid divides frame into 9 equal sections, place subjects at intersections
- Golden Ratio/Spiral: 1:1.618 proportion, natural spiral guiding eye
- Leading Lines: Lines that guide viewer's eye through image (roads, fences, rivers, architecture)
- Symmetry: Mirror image balance, formal, powerful, architectural
- Asymmetry: Intentional imbalance, dynamic, modern, energetic
- Fill the Frame: Get close, eliminate distractions, maximize impact
- Frame Within a Frame: Use doorways, windows, arches to frame subject
- Negative Space: Empty space around subject, minimalist, breathing room
- Patterns & Repetition: Repeating elements create rhythm and visual interest
- Breaking the Pattern: Disrupting repetition to create focal point
- Depth/Layers: Foreground, midground, background for 3D feel
- Juxtaposition: Placing contrasting elements together for tension/meaning
- Diagonal Lines: Create dynamic energy, movement, tension
- Centered Composition: Subject dead center, formal, powerful, confrontational
- Rule of Odds: Odd numbers of subjects (3, 5, 7) feel more natural
- Color Contrast: Complementary colors create visual pop
- Simplicity: Remove everything unnecessary
- Triangles: Three-point compositions create stability
- Golden Triangle: Diagonal line corner-to-corner, perpendiculars from other corners
- Viewpoint/Perspective: Change angle to change story (high, low, eye-level)

## Color Theory Foundations
### Color Wheel Relationships
- Complementary: Opposite on wheel (red/green, blue/orange) — maximum contrast, vibrant
- Analogous: Adjacent on wheel (blue/blue-green/green) — harmonious, serene
- Triadic: Three equally spaced (red/yellow/blue) — vibrant, balanced
- Split-Complementary: Base + two adjacent to complement — contrast with less tension
- Tetradic/Square: Four colors equally spaced — rich, complex
- Monochromatic: One hue, varying saturation/value — elegant, cohesive

### Color Psychology
- Red: Passion, urgency, danger, energy, love, power
- Orange: Enthusiasm, creativity, warmth, adventure, confidence
- Yellow: Optimism, happiness, warmth, caution, attention
- Green: Nature, growth, health, wealth, balance, freshness
- Blue: Trust, calm, professionalism, stability, depth, intelligence
- Purple: Luxury, creativity, mystery, royalty, spirituality
- Pink: Femininity, romance, playfulness, tenderness, youth
- Black: Sophistication, power, elegance, mystery, authority
- White: Purity, cleanliness, simplicity, innocence, space
- Gray: Neutrality, balance, sophistication, timelessness
- Gold: Luxury, prestige, quality, tradition, wealth
- Silver: Modern, sleek, industrial, futuristic

### Color Temperature
- Warm colors (red, orange, yellow): Advance, feel closer, energetic, inviting
- Cool colors (blue, green, purple): Recede, feel farther, calm, professional
- Neutral colors (black, white, gray, brown): Ground, balance, support
