## Genius Patterns

### 1. Topics-Over-Hooks Inversion
- **Unconscious behavior**: Ranks the creator stack Topics → Formats → Curiosity/Storytelling →
  Data analysis (base). Refuses to let hook craft lead.
- **Executable**: Before any hook work, force the question "is the IDEA itself the hook?" If the
  idea needs a hooky rescue, the idea fails.
- **Deploy**: Every content ideation session. **Metric**: ideas that perform when stated plainly.

### 2. Trust-Recession Non-Hook
- **Unconscious behavior**: Reads 2026 social as a low-trust environment (his Germany storefront
  anecdote): overt hooks now signal manipulation; "the best hooks are non-hooks. They are just
  good ideas."
- **Executable**: Strip clickbait posture; state the valuable idea in insider language, plainly.
- **Deploy**: Sophisticated/saturated niches especially. **Metric**: comments citing the idea,
  not the packaging.

### 3. Psychological Alignment Venn (Proper TAM × Specific Psychology × Unique/Novel)
- **Unconscious behavior**: Scores every idea against three simultaneous conditions; the center
  is "psychological alignment" — the audience's ears perk up.
- **Executable**: Gate each idea: (a) TAM sized to THIS video's goal, (b) niche-insider keyword
  psychology, (c) "never heard it that way" novelty. Two of three = rework or discard.
- **Deploy**: Idea gate before production. **Metric**: perk-up reactions (saves, "I felt seen"
  comments, DMs).

### 4. Proper-TAM Goal Matching
- **Unconscious behavior**: Sizes audience breadth to the video's JOB, not to maximum reach. His
  own receipt: "how to unlock creativity" (~250K views) earned less than "how to build a media
  team" (10K views) — both correct because both matched their goal.
- **Executable**: Declare the goal (growth / monetization / nurture) BEFORE sizing the idea; a
  10K-view video can be the win.
- **Deploy**: Every idea; also diagnosis of "too high TAM" follower-quality damage (dog-driving-
  car founder video: mega-viral, zero qualified followers). **Metric**: goal-denominated outcome
  (revenue, qualified follows), not raw views.

### 5. Keyword Sniping / Perk-Up Psychology
- **Unconscious behavior**: Hunts the 3-5 things his audience already thinks about daily, then
  compresses them into 1-3 word triggers ("yapping" resonates now; "viral" is burned out).
- **Executable**: Build a niche keyword ledger; validate each word with the insider test — would
  a practitioner at the target level actually say this? ("Teach you a takedown" outs you to
  wrestlers; "90-minute carb-load blueprint after a 5-lb weight cut" = let's go.)
- **Deploy**: Topic selection, hooks, titles. **Metric**: ears-perk engagement from true niche
  members, not tourists.

### 6. Five-Input Ideation Hour (the sacred weekly hour)
- **Unconscious behavior**: One protected hour weekly; notes app out; jots keywords, never
  scripts. Inputs: (1) inspiration list — 15-20 creators on a burner account, in and out of
  niche; (2) Reddit — 15-20 subreddits ≥50-100K members, sorted top, threads pasted into Claude
  ("what are people talking about here?"); (3) YouTube thumbnails via outlier finders (1of10 /
  TubeLab / TubeBuddy) on 5-10 niche keywords; (4) external signal — sales calls, email CTRs,
  paid-ad winners, community FAQs; (5) internal signal — own comments, DMs, view outliers.
- **Executable**: 2 ideas per input = 10 data-backed ideas/week minimum. He claims ~400/week at
  agency scale.
- **Deploy**: Weekly, non-negotiable cadence. **Metric**: never starting a week idea-poor; every
  idea has a named signal source.

### 7. Baseline-Relative Outlier Detection
- **Unconscious behavior**: Never trusts raw view counts. Clicks the profile, computes the
  creator's baseline average, judges the video as a multiple: 100K views on a 200K-baseline
  account is a flop; on a 20K baseline it's a 5x outlier — "rip it."
- **Executable**: outlier_ratio = video_views / creator_baseline_avg; act only on ≥3-5x.
- **Deploy**: Any inspiration scroll, any competitor scan. **Metric**: hit rate of adapted ideas.

### 8. Thumbnail Attribution Shortcut
- **Unconscious behavior**: Uses YouTube thumbnails as "a concentrated point of data" — YouTube
  is click-and-watch, so an outlier's performance attributes mostly to packaging (thumbnail +
  title), pre-isolating the topic variable for him.
- **Executable**: Mine YT outliers for short-form topic ideas; skip wondering whether it was the
  edit/storytelling — on YouTube it usually wasn't.
- **Deploy**: Input #3 of the ideation hour. **Metric**: short-form winners sourced from YT packaging.

### 9. Four-Layer Contextualization Filter (Following / Strategy / Niche / Creator)
- **Unconscious behavior**: Before adapting anyone's winner, walks the transfer bridge (their
  account → your account): Did it work because of their FOLLOWING size? Their STRATEGY (growth vs
  monetization vs book-title testing)? Their NICHE's psychology? Or the CREATOR themselves
  (charisma, intellect, looks)? Subtract what doesn't transfer; twist what remains; only then make it.
- **Executable**: 4-question filter with explicit subtract-or-twist verdict per layer. His
  anti-example: swimsuit-mom motherhood hook → sales-guy account = zero transferable layers.
- **Deploy**: Every borrowed idea. **Metric**: adapted videos that hold ≥ your own baseline.

### 10. Chris Rock Arsenal Loop (the testing economy)
- **Unconscious behavior**: Runs the Rock small-club→MSG testing model, then BREAKS it where
  content differs: there is no single key event — "we play Madison Square Garden every night" —
  so the arsenal must loop infinitely. Rules: test each new idea 3-6 times before discarding;
  winners bank into a persistent arsenal; run ~4:3 proven-to-test ratio (never below 70/30
  testing floor); content lifecycle is 4-6 weeks; deliberately bench winners (use 4 of 6) so the
  audience never fatigues on them.
- **Executable**: A written weekly ledger: slots, sources, attempt counts, win/flop verdicts,
  arsenal bank, bench rotation.
- **Deploy**: Weekly planning; the 8-week install produces ~12 banked winners vs 7. **Metric**:
  arsenal size growth + stable views without fatigue bleed.

### 11. Four-Question Win Audit (Topic → Format → Hook → Body)
- **Unconscious behavior**: Postmortems winners in strict order — was it the topic? the visual
  format? the hook (incl. location, wardrobe)? the body (a 30-second story/statistic that drove
  comments)? — isolating variables like an experimenter, building FOUR arsenals (topics,
  formats, hook principles, body principles).
- **Executable**: Audit every outlier (yours) with the 4 questions; write the verdict into the
  matching arsenal. His own ledger: topics = counter-positioning, data analytics, posting
  frequency; formats = basement yap, green screen (dead lanes: object lessons, brackets); hooks =
  blue color, basement, thinking nonverbal; body = Liquid Death mentions, Cashvertising references.
- **Deploy**: After every posted video with signal. **Metric**: arsenal entries per week.

### 12. Signal-Not-Metrics Doctrine
- **Unconscious behavior**: Quotes Ogilvy via Caleb Ralston — "content is not a game of guessing,
  it's a game of knowing." Dismisses retention-curve/watch-time obsession as post-100K-average
  concerns; the real 80/20 of "data analysis" is the winners ledger.
- **Executable**: Before making anything, name its signal source; before opening an analytics
  dashboard, update the arsenal ledger instead.
- **Deploy**: Always; especially for sub-100K-average creators. **Metric**: % of published
  content with named pre-existing signal.

### 13. Follower Quality Economics (enrichment video)
- **Unconscious behavior**: Values audiences in dollars-per-follower, not size: his client
  receipts — business influencer 400K followers/$25K mo = $0.06/follower; marketing creator
  13K/$45K = $3.46; sales creator 700 followers averaging $1M/mo = $1.43+ (and a $2.2M day);
  fitness influencer 1.7M/$125K = $0.07. Educators with pure niches and high-ticket offers beat
  influencers 50x on this ratio. Paired lens: CCN (core/casual/new, via Paddy Galloway) — content
  must ladder top/middle/bottom funnel on ONE through-line (his Gatsby green-light party metaphor).
- **Executable**: Track follower-to-dollar + returning-viewer ratio; kill follower-quality
  killers (bad paid ads = "paying someone for their love," niche swaps, too-high TAM,
  off-through-line content); ration posting to avoid auto-scroll fatigue.
- **Deploy**: Account strategy layer above the weekly loop. **Metric**: $/follower trend,
  follower-view ratio in first 3h (~67% is his observed norm), story views ≥10% of followers.

## Hidden Knowledge

- **Fatigue is the shadow constraint**: The arsenal isn't a trophy case, it's a RATIONING device.
  Over-indexing on winners "spirals fatigue into the ground" — repetition converts winners into
  auto-scroll triggers ("shadow banned by the follower," not the platform). Nobody else frames
  winner-benching as the point of a content system.
- **Packaging attribution is a variable-isolation trick**: YT thumbnails = pre-cleaned topic
  data. He's doing causal inference casually; the skill transfers to reading ANY platform where
  one element dominates attribution.
- **Insider-language as a credibility gate**: The wrestler takedown test — wrong-level language
  doesn't just miss, it actively OUTS you as an outsider and burns trust. Keyword selection is
  identity verification, not SEO.
- **Comment-bait is engineerable from the body arsenal**: Proven resonance references (his:
  Liquid Death, Cashvertising) get deliberately woven in ~30 seconds deep as comment triggers —
  audience-specific, discovered via win audits, not generic engagement hacks.
- **Reddit's anonymity = uncensored psychology**: People say what they actually think only when
  un-named; and two-sided conversation topics reproduce their argument in your comments, which
  the algorithm reads as depth.
- **The follower graph is the algorithm's first jury**: ~67% of first-3-hour views are followers;
  they vote on whether non-followers ever see it. Follower quality is therefore a DISTRIBUTION
  variable, not a vanity variable — bad paid ads and niche swaps poison the jury.

## Hall of Fame Exemplars

### Exemplar 1: The Kolby Keyword Cascade (live case, Video 1 close)
- **Context**: Briar plays a reel from Kolby Kirschner (a creator he consults) "going absolutely
  nuclear... close to 5,000 followers every 48 hours" — as proof that pure keyword ideation can
  carry a video with no narrative at all.
- **The Example** (verbatim from the reel): "Let me guess, human nature, building in solitude,
  studying the world's greatest minds, meta-cognition, collective unconscious, delusional
  self-belief, business that is also art, neurodivergent, obsessive learning, pattern
  recognition, long walks to think, genius maxing, speaking your truth through content. If you
  see this video and you like all these topics, welcome. You found your people." (Visual: collage
  images swap per keyword — Jung's book, da Vinci/Newton/Tesla portraits, tiger cartoon.)
- **Why it's a calibration anchor**: Thirteen perk-up keywords in linear fashion + a belonging
  close ("you found your people") = specific psychology with ZERO story, zero hook craft. The
  purest possible demonstration that the keyword IS the content.

### Exemplar 2: The Wrestler Carb-Load Calibration (Video 1)
- **Context**: Live demonstration of specific psychology vs generic language.
- **The Example**: "If you would have said, 'Hey guys, today I'm going to teach you how to do a
  takedown,' I would have said, 'You're stupid.'... Instead if you would have said, 'Here is the
  90-minute blueprint to carb load after a 5-lb weight cut so that you can be ready to wrestle
  your first match,' I'd be like, 'Let's go.'"
- **Why it's a calibration anchor**: Shows the insider-language gate in one before/after — level-
  wrong language destroys credibility; level-right specificity ignites. The template for testing
  any niche keyword.

### Exemplar 3: The Chris Rock → Infinite Arsenal Walkthrough (Video 2, whiteboard)
- **Context**: Full worked example, night-by-night grid on the whiteboard, then deliberately
  broken and rebuilt for content.
- **The Example**: Rock tests 7 jokes at small clubs, carries winners forward night to night
  until MSG is all-killer. Briar: "You cannot do this in content... we play Madison Square Garden
  every single night." Rebuild: 8-week grid, 4:3 winners-to-tests, stars for new winners, arsenal
  row beneath — ending with 12 banked winners instead of 7, having never fatigued the audience.
- **Why it's a calibration anchor**: A borrowed model EXTENDED where it breaks — the unique/novel
  move he preaches, demonstrated on his own flagship framework.

### Anti-Exemplar: The Verbatim Rip
- **What mediocre looks like**: "Female creator wearing a swimsuit talking about motherhood...
  stolen as a hook for a dude in sales." Or: copying a 100K-view video without checking that the
  creator's baseline was 200K (it was a flop). Or: metrics cosplay — a 2K-average creator
  obsessing over retention curves.
- **Why it fails**: Skips the outlier check, the four-layer transfer filter, and the goal-TAM
  match — all three gates at once. Views may even come, but they arrive as unqualified followers
  who poison first-3-hour distribution forever after.

## Signature Moves

- **The Baseline Click**: Sees an inspiring video → clicks the profile → computes baseline
  average → only then decides if it's signal. → **Deploy when**: any scroll session.
- **The Reddit-to-Claude Dump**: Copies an entire top subreddit thread, pastes into Claude, asks
  "what are people talking about here?" → both sides of the argument → video. → **Deploy when**:
  input #2 of the ideation hour.
- **Keywords, Never Scripts**: Ideation hour output is 1-3 word units in a notes app — never
  drafts. → **Deploy when**: all idea capture.
- **The Deliberate Bench**: Uses only 4 of 6 proven winners in a week, banking the rest. →
  **Deploy when**: weekly slate planning, the moment winners exceed slots.
- **The 30-Second Weave**: Inserts a proven body-arsenal reference ~30s into a video as comment
  bait. → **Deploy when**: a video needs engagement insurance.
- **The Insider Read-Aloud**: Says a candidate keyword as a niche practitioner would hear it;
  wrong-level phrasing gets cut on the spot. → **Deploy when**: any title/hook/topic wording.

1. **Gate ideas** (Venn) → 2. **Generate weekly** (five inputs, sacred hour) → 3. **Verify

audience economy** (fatigue rationing, follower quality, through-line).
