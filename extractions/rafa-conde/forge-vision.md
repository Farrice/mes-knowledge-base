# Rafa Conde Forge Vision

## Creative Direction

Create a standalone Antigravity category for **feeling-led product memorability**. This is not another visual design or messaging skill. The category owns the gap between a product being useful and a product being remembered.

## Uniqueness Audit

Existing Antigravity skills cover:

- Visual direction and production: `creative-direction`
- Message memory: `james-i-bond-brain-glue`, `made-to-stick-messaging`
- Taste and critique: `oren-taste-development`
- Cinematic narrative: `eric-roth-writing-mastery`
- Naming: `david-placek-naming`
- Video ads: `pj-accetturo-ai-video`

Rafa adds a product-native layer: the emotional design of software moments, feature demos, first-run flows, small-app scope, tactile details, and agent workflow experience.

## Business Use

- Audit client products that feel useful but forgettable.

- Upgrade onboarding, launch pages, release notes, and agent workflows.
- Create a productized service around memorable product experience audits.
