# David Perell Writing — Blind-Pass Review

Four short pieces are presented without source labels. Two are real published David Perell posts. Two were generated from the new Tier-1 workflows by a detached cold runner that was prohibited from reading the reference corpus.

## Recognition Test

Read these as finished micro-pieces, not as framework demonstrations.

1. Which two feel most recognizably David Perell: A, B, C, or D?
2. Which two do you prefer as writing?
3. Does any generated-feeling piece give itself away through polish, setup, genericness, or over-explanation?

`PASS` means the generated pieces are indistinguishable from or preferred to the real pieces. A-tier promotion requires Farrice's judgment; structural and model-judged fixture results cannot substitute for it.
