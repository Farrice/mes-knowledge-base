## MAP 1 — The Cash Outcome

**Q2 — What would I have to be known for, for that to happen?**
"The guy who shows supplement and performance brands exactly which angles their buyers actually search — with receipts." That's it. One association. Notice what's NOT in there: AI, ghostwriting, ten other things you can do. The buyer for a $750 Angle Map doesn't need your range. Your range confuses them. It's much easier for someone to know one thing about you and expand over time.

**Q3 — In order to be known for that, what do I need to DO?**
You don't get known for this by posting about it. It's actions:
- Ship angle teardowns on real brands in the niche — named brands, real search data, the struggle called out with uncomfortable precision. These are sandbagged pieces. I don't care if they get 40 views. The creative director at the brand is the view.
- Send the five. The map exists to make the sends land, not to replace them.
- Deliver one Angle Map so well the buyer describes it in one sentence to another founder. That sentence becomes the brand.

**Q4 — What do I need to learn?**
Honestly? Less than you think — the skills exist. What's actually on the learn list: the vocabulary gap. You know the SEO/GEO layer cold; the learn item is how supplement operators talk about it when they complain to each other. That language goes in the teardowns. An hour in their forums beats another certification.

**Reconciliation:** Current reputation is "smart, wide, hard to summarize." The gap isn't capability, it's compression. Existing proof to deploy now: the Jen listings engine, the health-performance brief work. Proof to build: one named-brand teardown this week.
