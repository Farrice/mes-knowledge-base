## Uniqueness Audit — what no one in the roster does

The roster is deep on the **creative side** of paid (Dara Denney, Alex Copper, Omar Eddaoudi, Sarah Levinger, Fraser Cottrell) and on **attribution philosophy** (Vince Nijhof's blended attribution). Nobody owns the **media buying and measurement system itself**. Benoit's genius is exactly that seat:

1. **Full-funnel media audit** — categorize every dollar by funnel stage before touching anything; most consumer brands are all top-funnel, most B2B all bottom-funnel.
2. **Incrementality doctrine** — "I don't need perfect, I need signals." New-to-brand % × ROAS as the fast incrementality proxy; geo holdout tests (NY spend vs LA dark) when you need truth; "incremental ROAS" as the metric he's building toward.
3. **Home-run-only testing** — only run tests that can show 20%+ gains; if data is inconclusive at day 4, the outcome was never going to be big — kill it. Magnitude over predictability.
4. **Channel-capability matching** — pick platforms by the **levers they let you control**, not by where your customers are. No frequency control = no awareness (so social is NOT an awareness channel, period; CTV/radio/podcast are). Consideration = optimize against the click-out, measured at cost-per-product-page-view, not CTR.
5. **One-job-per-creative funnel mapping** — awareness creative has one job: remember the name (Liquid Death's lane: comedy). Consideration: link name → benefits → where to buy. Retarget: a NEW angle (they clicked and didn't buy — why?). Lapsed: different message again. "You cannot do the full funnel in a single ad unit."
6. **The Golden Core failure thesis** — THE metric that destines marketers to fail: CAC:LTV chased from day one. Every brand has a golden core that converts cheap; past it, CAC inverts and people blame the algo. Bottom-funnel-only spend "is counterintuitively false — you'd get a lot of that revenue anyway."
7. **The control doctrine** — let go of control on WHERE you scale (retail > your Shopify; most scale lives where you have least control); take control on HOW you buy (never let Meta's algo pick creatives — it sees one fragment of the funnel and will distract the whole system).
8. **Retail media for CPG** — his time-machine lesson: day-one retail media (Amazon/Walmart/Target) over D2C obsession. TikTok Shop / social commerce = discovery tool, not a scale tool.
