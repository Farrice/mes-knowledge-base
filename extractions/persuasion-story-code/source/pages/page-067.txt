STORIES ABOUT SOMETHING NEW OR UNFAMILIAR
There's an old joke in Hollywood:
A nervous screenwriter sits before an imposing studio chief. The chief
explains what he's looking for:
"I want something different—but familiar."
Stan Laurel (of Laurel and Hardy fame) put it bluntly: "Humor is the
truth." Just about every other successful comedian will tell you the same
thing, in so many words.
And the joke does convey truth. People often say they'd like something
different, but what they really want is a slightly different version of what
they're already familiar with.
Most people, but not all people. Some people shy away from anything the
slightest bit familiar. Like composer John Cage. He says, "I can't
understand why people are frightened of new ideas. I'm frightened of the
old ones."
Let's see what he means.
