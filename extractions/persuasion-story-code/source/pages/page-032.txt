write your story using this outline:
1. The motivating moment - What happened in the person's life that
set them on the journey to become who they are today?
2. Important touchstones along the way - What are the key points
in their journey that contributed to their unique expertise?
3. What others say - If you have accolades or awards you can
mention that further certify this person's expertise, mention them.
4. The result - Where did this person end up, and what can you
credibly say about their expertise after the journey you just
described?
You can use this kind of origin story to explain why you, or someone
you're writing about, is uniquely qualified to deliver a particular product
or service. On a landing page, this, like most origin stories, comes in right
after the lead and you're ready to introduce the expert.
DISCOVERY STORY
This is a short story from the past where the discovery itself is the story's
hero. What you do here is show how the discovery led to—or is, by itself
—a product or service you're offering.
This kind of story works far better than simply proclaiming that you
have something new... ditterent... better. By weaving the discovery into
a larger story, you make it more emotionally compelling. And that sets
up what you will say later on.
Here's an example of what a Discovery Story looks like:
For years, I did about half an hour of interval training, three times
a week. It got me in pretty good shape. My only issue was there
was some stubborn belly fat that did not want to go away.
A hard-core athlete friend told me about this device originally
developed by NASA to train astronauts. He suggested I add it to
my routine.
