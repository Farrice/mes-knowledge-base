A few things to look at in this story:
First, it's easy for many people to identify with the characters and
activities in the story. A man, his girlfriend who's a single mom with
three kids. Going out to the bar for drinks with friends.
Then there's a disaster—a bad guy who slips something in the woman's
drink, and later assaults her near the bathroom.
And, finally, a technique the woman learned from her boyfriend (which
ne learned trom the product, FI) that saves the day. In fact, when she
uses that technique, she knocks out the bad guy—cold.
Because of the casual but focused attention to detail in this story, Morgan
creates a lot of conviction (in the interested prospect) that they, too, will
be able to learn TFT and make it work when they need to. The
testimonial he used has the same format as a case study, which he could
have used instead. But presenting the information as a quote from a
person who experienced how TFT worked for his girlfriend makes the
story more compelling than a more emotionally distanced case study.
