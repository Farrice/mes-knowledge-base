When to use:
If you've got an amazing "aha!" moment story from when you
discovered the solution you are offering.
How to craft:
1. Write down the following:
a. All the frustrations you experienced before you made the discovery.
b. The concrete difference the discovery made in your life.
c. The other problems that went away or became less of a problem.
2. Use the following structure to write the discovery story:
a. What were you doing and what was life like before you made your
discovery?
b. What was the discovery you made?
c. What changed for the better as a result?

steps and that makes it so much easier to write. You get a separate
