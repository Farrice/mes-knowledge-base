Uncertainty-Some of the most common worries people have come in
these often unspoken questions:
• Does it really work?
• OK, even if it does work, will it work for me?
• What happens if something goes wrong?
Stories that explain, stories that build trust, and reassurance stories can
work wonders soothing these worries.
A story about how to use a product can help your prospect visualize
successfully using it. A case study or experience testimonial where a
customer explains how easy it was to use will help your prospect
understand that your product indeed really does work. A story about if

Outright Objections—Persuasion stories are a gold mine for handling
objections without coming across as pushy or defensive.
If you get a question about product construction or how long it's going to
last, you can tell a convincing story about a hard-to-believe claim of
endurance.
If you're challenged about the price being too high, you can tell an
unexpected benefit story and a results testimonial.
If your prospect says they want to shop around, there may be nothing
you can do, but you can at least try a story that eliminates all other
alternatives. Sometimes just one fact, feature or benefit can turn things
around. And a persuasion story is the best way to deliver it.
