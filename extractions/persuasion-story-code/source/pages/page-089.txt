Now-what to notice about this story?
First, it's remarkably clear and simple. Focused on one thing: a system to
pick stocks with the potential for increasing in value by 10, over the
course of the year.
Second, information is spooned out a little at a time. There's no
information overload. The story is carefully crafted to reveal what the
system is and what it does, one step at a time.
Third, it doesn't outright state, "This is the only system you will ever
need, because all others are inferior!"
It doesn't state that because it doesn't have to. For people used to
considering stock-picking services, the promise is so unique and clear
that they will recognize pretty quickly that this system could replace
anything and everything they're already doing. And most will also
conclude that there's nothing else like it on the market.
