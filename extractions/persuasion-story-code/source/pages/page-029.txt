CHAPTER 3
ORIGIN STORIES
People everywhere are curious about where other people, things, ideas—
even the universe itselt—all come trom.
The Old Testament's first chapter is called "Genesis," a Greek word
meaning... you guessed it... "origin." Indeed it does describe the origin
of the universe. And different cultures around the world have their own
versions, from long ago, of how the Earth came into being.
Here in the 21st century, things are not that different. I just finished
watching the third season of the Amazon streaming series "Jack Ryan."
After the last episode, Amazon suggested I might want to watch the 2014
movie "Jack Ryan, Shadow Recruit." It was Jack's origin story. (Of course
I watched it.)
In persuasion, origin stories have a special format to be as believable as
possible:
• They contain enough convincing detail and fascinating facts to begin
a persuasion process... which could end up in a closed sale.
• Origin stories nearly always contain proof. But even when they
don't, origin stories have enough concrete elements so you will be
in a perfect position to add convincing proof soon enough.
