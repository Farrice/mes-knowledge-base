STORIES ABOUT THE PAIN YOU KNOW PERSONALLY
This kind of story is like the first-prospect's pain story-but with an
added twist. It shows that you know from personal experience the pain
the prospect was going through, and you explicitly say so.
In the 1960s, the telephone company in the U.S. ran a series of ads
promoting long-distance calls. Talking to someone in another state was
considered exotic at the time. Talking to someone in another country was
rare and almost unimaginable. How far we've come since then!
The tagline for the Bell System's ads in the 1960s: It's the next best thing to
being there.
Good tagline. But you know what's not the "next best thing," but actually
is the best thing?
Being there!
And this doesn't apply just to phone calls compared to in-person visits. It
also applies to suffering a specific pain.
Because once you have "been there"-been in the same situation as a
fellow pain sufferer—you are connected to that person in a way that's
hard to duplicate with mere understanding or empathy.
