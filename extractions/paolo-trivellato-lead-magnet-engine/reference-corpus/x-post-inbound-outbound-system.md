linkedIn is the BEST way to sign high-ticket deals. specifically for SaaS, Agencies & Consultants. i've had clients consistently book 200 meetings in a month, close $10-30k deals & scale to over $100k/mo only using LinkedIn. just copy & paste this inbound + outbound system:

linkedIn works because your buyers are already there during work hours with budget authority and an active problem they need solved. if they wanted entertainment, they'd be scrolling on tiktok. but they're not. they're looking for solutions. and I found that yes, content works. yes, outreach works. but when we put them both together... the results are actually insane.

INBOUND - building trust at scale

post once per day. every post you make drives profile views. every profile view is a potential $20K client doing their research before they reach out. your profile has three conversion mechanisms: 1. DMs - High-ticket buyers just hit you up saying "let's talk" 2. Custom Button - this is your biggest traffic driver. link it to your Calendly or LP. 3. Featured Section - case studies, testimonials, free breakdowns. this is for people who need more convincing.

LEAD MAGNET POSTS

these are perfect for top of funnel traffic. give away something insanely valuable in exchange for a comment. hook formula: [outcome] + [timeframe] + [mechanism] (steal my system for free) "i closed $240K in 120 days using only LinkedIn DMs" in the post, make sure to: - preview the lead magnet content. - add bonuses. - make a low friction CTA. your goal is to build as much FOMO as possible. one post like this = 500-1000 comments = hundreds of your exact ICP in your network. use Leadshark to automate sending out the resource & auto-connecting to everyone

AUTHORITY POSTS

this is super super super important for lower down the funnel. it's what's going to convince people to buy your service over everyone else. screenshot the testimonial. show the before/after metrics. break down exactly how you got them there. as a general rule, make it 80% educational, 20% promotional. "if you want the same results, DM me" you're proving you can deliver while making it brain-dead simple for them to work with you.

OUTBOUND - Turning attention into conversations

now you have all of these warm leads, it's time to convert them. scrape your commenters on a specific post you made with Prosp filter by job title, company revenue, industry. get surgical with it. check who viewed your profile in the last 7 days. these are warm leads already researching you. ask a Poke-The-Bear type question to get them to self-diagnose their pain point. E.g., "How are you thinking about increasing [metric]?" if you get a positive response, mention the reason you were asking and a case study that shows why you could do it for them finally, a low-friction assumptive CTA to get them onto a call like "Willing to chat for 20/30 mins and see if it could work for you guys too. When are you free?"

your content is working 24/7 building credibility with thousands of potential buyers. some will reach out directly (pure inbound revenue). but most won't reach out even if they're interested. they're busy, skeptical, or waiting to see more proof. that's where your outreach changes everything. you're reaching out to people who already consumed your content. they've seen your posts. they know your name. they've probably stalked your profile. warm outreach closes at 5-10x the rate of cold outreach. i dare you to implement this system and see how fucking full your pipeline gets.
