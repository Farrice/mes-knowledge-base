## The three things this source uniquely adds

**1. The 3-Layer Static System** (her core mental model, never captured before):
- **Layer 1 — Strategy** (button up BEFORE writing a line of copy): pick ONE goal · pick a *specific* persona (stage + objection, not "small business owners") · pick a format · match a level of awareness (the bridge connecting persona+goal to framework).
- **Layer 2 — Design**: visual hierarchy (headline first → product/key visual → supporting) · the **1-second comprehension test** ("if a stranger can't tell what you're selling, kill it") · clarity > creativity · focal point on the message 9/10 times.
- **Layer 3 — Copy** (the most important): 8 static-copy mechanics — specificity + lead-with-a-number, self-selection callout, lean into the taboo, tap a primal desire (product = mechanism, desire = headline), open a curiosity loop, negative marketing, golden-nugget testimonial mining (run the review CSV through an LLM), show-don't-tell transformation.

**2. The 7 Static Format Archetypes** (distinct from the 8 video archetypes in the current skill):
educational infographic · headliner ad (the Ogilvy print lineage) · benefits callout · comparison / us-vs-them · transformation · grid static · text-only ad. Each mapped to a **production level**: graphic-style / hi-fi production / **lo-fi creator content** (the one she says is the biggest current gap + needle-mover).

**3. The AI Static Production Pipeline** (the "in minutes" payload): Claude builds the **brand brain** → research-FIRST (competitor ad-library + gap analysis) → generate in **3-variation batches** → **edit-to-refine loop** (natural-language edits, not regenerate). She demos this on SuperScale, but the *method* is tool-agnostic — it ports to our own stack (fantastic-posters / Higgsfield / gpt-image).
