## The Research SOP (her agency's actual pipeline — 4 steps, 4+ artifacts)

**Step 1 — Reputation Analysis** (manual, "very very strategic on our end"; she's run it for "hundreds of brands… if not thousands"; runs it whenever nervous about a new brand and "always comes out with a few really great ideas"):
1. **Brand & product analysis** — Brand: founder, founding story/the big WHY, best-selling products, offers (zoom on paid-social offers), merchandising strategy. Product (start with the Meta-focus product): the promise, the transformation claimed, evidence/proof it works, features, benefits, bundles or potential to bundle. Bundle lens: a creative strategist can re-merchandise — raise AOV via problem-solution-oriented bundles when a $20 product can't survive Meta CAC.
2. **First page of Google** — red flags a first-time customer would hit.
3. **Press** — earned vs bought detection; brand news (IPO, nationwide retail launch) unlocks formats/offers/objectives.
4. **Reddit** — most honest; anonymized ruthlessness; pain points + brand complaints; recent threads, most-upvoted comments, recurring objections & desires; learn to speak like the customer.
5. **Amazon** — Amazon best-seller vs DTC best-seller deltas; rankings/review counts/stars; **Ask-Amazon AI persona probe**: "what type of people are buying this product?" → Amazon-specific persona breakdown (underutilized).
6. **Social media** — core content strategies working, community cultivation, storytelling, employee-generated content, behind-the-scenes transparency, creator roster.
7. **YouTube** — reviews (even bought ones show what attracts buyers through the buying experience); **comments = objection/skepticism/FAQ goldmine**.
   Synthesis targets: common objections; the big friction point; trust-in-efficacy vs worth-it skepticism vs price/value framing.
   → Output doc is "an excellent context document for your LLM of choice."

**Step 2 — Customer Review Mining**: export ALL reviews from the review platform into the mining sheet; ALSO manually collect standout Reddit/Amazon/press quotes as you go; rank products by review count AND rating; **mine the comments of the top ~20 ads of the last year** (tools like Motion/"Runith" can automate; manual pass still matters — "machines don't quite have the judgment of what could be a really impactful review that could lead to a top performing ad"). AI analysis questions: strengths/weaknesses as a brand; most common complaints/objections (vs reputation-analysis findings); golden-nugget phrases + trends among them; trigger points that cause purchase. → second LLM context doc.

**Step 3 — Persona and Desire Segmentation** (THE MOAT — see dedicated section below).

**Step 4 — Mission Doc** (from her SOP quick-links; the strategy synthesis the other three feed).

Ranking axes she uses: **frequency of appearance** (evidence volume) and **emotional intensity of language** (e.g., Oats Overnight satiety persona "kept them full" had the most emotional language AND was underrepresented in the creative mix → gap → "FULLNESS CHASER STATICS" + "'THIS KEEPS ME FULL' CREATOR" tests).

- Persona activation move: take a top-performing creative and **replicate it across all core personas** (her example: David & Goliath for 45+ perimenopausal women → same construct for men on GLP-1, new moms).
- Persona gap analysis: cross-analyze persona SOP output vs personas currently targeted in the ad account — untargeted personas; over-indexed personas vs bigger opportunities "in terms of volume or in terms of emotional intensity."

## The Three-Gap Analysis (strategy = current-gap identification)

1. **Persona gaps** — persona deck vs live ad account (above).
2. **Awareness-level gaps** — audit levels of awareness across the entire creative account; common finding: over-index on MOF/BOF, top performers are BOF/offer-oriented → diagnosis: build TOF to widen the ecosystem while keeping BOF winners.
3. **Creative diversity gap** — the account should show: multiple personas attacked × **2-3 unique angles each** ("angles, not concepts — big pillar ideas for those specific personas") × a range of awareness levels per persona; PLUS creator-program diversity (lifestyle, gender, age, job, vibe).

Platform trends sit above formats (macro): employee-generated content, partnership ads. Signal that strategy (not just a creative) is right: **it's reflected in organic creative too**; a single creative test can't validate a strategy.

## Planning Cadence (the anti-churn move)

Weekly-sprint-forever = the trap ("never able to see the bigger picture or take bigger swings"). Her cadence: **quarterly planning** (product launches, seasonal persona pushes) → **monthly roadmap** (strategic approach) → weekly execution. "You can actually just add strategy to your overall creative approach if you implement this thinking instead of churn-and-burn every single week."
Artifact: **Creative Testing Roadmap** sheet — Test # / Concept Name / Nº Variations (3-6) / Winning Element (Hook/Angle/Format/Storyline) / Creative Type (UGC/Video/Image/Carousels).

"Part psychologist, part marketer, part data analyst, part creative director, and increasingly part creator."
**Anti-pattern:** combo roles ("video editor slash creative strategist") — one brand alone is 40+ hrs/week of this role done well.

**Hiring rubric (= the skill rubric):** (0) the unnameable spark — motivated by performance/revenue, not brief-checking; (1) consumer psychology — core desires/motivators, awareness levels, can spot emotionally-resonant content in the wild (her drill: "find organic content that represents a certain persona for a brand"); (2) own research process (reviews, Reddit, top-ad comment mining); (3) hooks & concepting — formulas + outside-formula judgment, "second-zero experience," story structures, evergreen formats vs arbitrage trends (AI ads, yapper); (4) data fluency — hook rate/hold rate/CTR as **secondary storytelling metrics** vs spend/purchases/revenue as **primary KPIs**; can argue "55% hook rate + high CTR but no conversions, and here's why"; (5) **AI specialist as non-negotiable in 2026** — video gen, brief writing, PM, and "probably the biggest thing is reporting."

## New Genius Patterns (this layer — numbered continuing the existing 10)

11. **Research-as-Customer-Journey Simulation** — the reputation analysis retraces the exact research journey a skeptical first-time customer takes (Google → press → Reddit → Amazon → social → YouTube comments). You're not "doing research," you're pre-living the purchase decision. Action: run the journey in customer order and log every friction point they'd hit.
12. **Every Artifact Is LLM Fuel** — each research doc is explicitly built to be "an excellent context document for your LLM of choice." The SOP produces a context engine, not a binder. Action: format every research output as a promptable context doc (clean structure, verbatim quotes, ranked findings).
13. **Evidence-Ranked Personas** (moat) — personas are named from N reviews + M survey responses, then ranked by evidence volume × emotional intensity; segments without evidence don't ship. Action: every persona carries its receipts (count + hottest verbatim quotes).
14. **Desire Segments Ride Alongside Personas** — segmentation is dual: WHO (persona) and WANT (desire-based segments); angles are mined from both. Action: output both segment families, never persona-only.
15. **Persona Injection for Net-New Audiences** — the highest-value strategist move is identifying a persona the brand has never targeted and building a wedge creative (Rhode 40+ partnership play). Action: in every gap analysis, propose ≥1 net-new persona with its entry format.
16. **Winner × Persona Replication** — top performer becomes a template replicated across every core persona (D&G: perimenopause women → GLP-1 men → new moms). Action: when a winner emerges, generate the persona-replication matrix before generating new concepts.
17. **Three-Gap Diagnosis Before Any Concept** — persona gaps, awareness gaps, diversity gaps vs the LIVE account; concepts exist to close named gaps ("why this creative? why this persona? why this angle?"). Action: no concept without its gap.
18. **Bundle-as-Creative-Lever** — merchandising is inside the strategist's remit: propose problem-solution bundles to fix AOV when unit price can't survive paid CAC. Action: check price/AOV viability in Step 1; propose re-merchandising when blocked.
19. **Quarterly→Monthly→Weekly Altitude Ladder** — strategy dies in permanent weekly sprints; big swings need quarterly planning + monthly roadmaps. Action: install the Creative Testing Roadmap + quarterly/monthly cadence at engagement start.
20. **Organic-Echo Validation** — a strategy (vs a creative) is validated when the same idea works reflected in organic content; single ad tests can't validate strategy. Action: pair paid tests with an organic echo check.
21. **Own-the-Outcome Pricing** — deliverable-submitters get commodity rates; outcome-owners set the price ($2K floor → $8-10K/brand). Action: package = outcome + strategy governance, never "N ads/month."
22. **Spec-Work Flywheel** — public breakdowns + unsolicited spec ads convert at absurd rates (75% response; instant interview) because they demonstrate the process ON the target's own brand. Action: for any dream client, ship a mini reputation analysis + 2-3 spec concepts before asking for the call.

## Hidden Knowledge (this layer)

- Ask-Amazon persona probe is a free, underutilized persona source.
- Press reviews pattern-match as bought vs earned by their shape (batches of formulaic placements = bought).
- Ad-comment mining beats site-review mining for objection freshness; top-20-ads-of-the-year is the corpus bound.
- Beauty vs problem-solution categories need different strategist postures (vibes-with-performance vs direct punch).
- "Angles ≠ concepts" — angles are pillar pain-point ideas per persona; concepts instantiate them.
- The Meta Ad Library top-impressions ranking misleads — she reads PAST it to infer the real top performer.
- Manual judgment stays in the loop specifically at golden-nugget selection — the automation boundary is explicit.

## Quality Rubric (for any deliverable from this layer — 8 criteria)

1. Persona receipts: every persona/desire segment cites evidence count + verbatim quotes. No invented segments.
2. Journey completeness: reputation analysis covers all 7 stations (brand/product, Google, press, Reddit, Amazon, social, YouTube).
3. Objection synthesis names THE friction point (trust/worth-it/price-value), not a list of ten.
4. Every proposed concept traces to a named gap (persona/awareness/diversity) or named research finding — the Oats test ("DEAR DIY'ers" ← DIY objection) is the traceability standard.
5. Dual altitude present: quarterly/monthly plan, not only a test batch.
6. Roadmap sheet columns complete: variations count, winning-element hypothesis, creative type per test.
7. ≥1 net-new persona injection OR explicit statement that saturation is verified.
8. Deliverables formatted as LLM context docs (promptable, ranked, quote-rich) AND client-readable.
