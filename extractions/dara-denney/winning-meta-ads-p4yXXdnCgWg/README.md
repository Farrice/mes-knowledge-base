# Dara Denney Source Enrichment — Winning Meta Ads (2026)

Primary source: [The Easiest Way To Make Winning Meta Ads (in 2026)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p4yXXdnCgWg), published 2026-08-26, 15:37.

This package enriches the existing `dara-denney-meta-ads` owner. It does not create a competing Dara skill. Its bounded contribution is the decision layer between research and format production:

1. compress an ad into its big idea rather than its surface format;
2. rank candidate ideas by evidence strength;
3. choose the cheapest test that can discriminate the idea;
4. expand a validated idea into a cross-format, multi-creator creative ecosystem;
5. use organic-pattern and persona-gap research when account history is thin.

## Evidence Boundary

`VERIFIED SPOKEN` means the claim is present in the preserved captions. `VERIFIED VISUAL` means the mechanism is visible in a preserved frame. Self-reported spend or results are verified only as statements made in the video, not independently audited business outcomes. Organic virality is a research proxy, not paid-conversion proof. The source's exact evidence ladder is preserved as a dated expert heuristic, not an automatic promotion policy.
