# Source Ledger — Meta Ad Creative Format Intelligence

Source: [Dara Denney, “I Ranked 51 Meta Ad Creative Types (Tier List),” 2026-08-13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KmbEjy0UBT8)

All tiers below are visually observed on the final board. Mechanism notes are paraphrases of Dara Denney's spoken rationale. Timestamps point to the relevant caption segment, not necessarily the first frame of the title card.

## S Tier — Scale Anchors

| Format | Timestamp | Source-grounded rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Founder's Ads | 06:51 | Often a brand's first top performer; the founder's reason for building creates direct problem and identity connection. |
| Partnership Ads | 14:19 | Persona access and net-new reach through the creator's seed audience; described as vital under Meta's persona-based delivery. |
| VSL (Video Sales Letter) | 23:46 | High-trust, high-education conversion format, especially for health, wellness, and fitness. |

## A Tier — High-Conviction Plays

| Format | Timestamp | Source-grounded rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Amateur Investigation | 01:30 | A creator investigates the product or niche; Dara reports strong scaling when her team deploys it. |
| Yapper Ads | 03:01 | Can scale with the right script, creator, and setting, but creator sourcing and controlled looseness are hard. |
| David & Goliath | 03:45 | Positions the brand as the challenger overcoming an industry or obstacle, giving the audience a reason to root for it. |
| Grid Style Statics | 05:31 | Low-lift multi-SKU or bundle format that has become a top-performing unicorn for some brands. |
| Authority Ads | 16:46 | Transfers expert trust in competitive categories such as supplements and beauty and helps overcome objections. |
| Greenscreen Commentary | 24:17 | Strong when commentary is paired with an educational angle; Dara also reports apparel success. |
| Catalog Ads and DPA Ads | 25:26 | Can prospect broadly when feed systems and templates are built correctly; one current client top performer. |

## B Tier — Supporting Cast and Funnel Builders

| Format | Timestamp | Source-grounded rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| TikTok Love Letter | 02:02 | Easy top-of-funnel execution and a fast hit when persona and messaging are already known. |
| Reels Short | 02:29 | Top-of-funnel supporting format that expands a concept or persona without usually becoming the account's scale anchor. |
| EGC (Employee-Generated Content) | 06:10 | Useful authenticity and behind-the-scenes access; consistency suffers unless content is part of the employee's job. |
| Callout Ads | 08:23 | Effective iteration when winning messaging, angles, or creative concepts are already known. |
| Before and After | 08:50 | Mid-funnel proof and conversion support; less likely to puncture net-new audiences. |
| Progression Ads | 09:17 | Shows change across time; strong mid-funnel support with the same scale limitation as before/after. |
| Tweet/Reddit Ads | 10:55 | Capacity-efficient static support for brands around $100K-$250K spend; also useful as a video first frame. |
| Headline Ads | 12:26 | Works when messaging itself can stop the scroll; stronger when combined with a callout. |
| Us vs Them | 13:51 | Still reaches the top performers for some accounts, but usually does mid-funnel comparison work. |
| Hot Girl IG Stories | 15:50 | Native lifestyle and product-context support when the right creator can execute believable story behavior. |
| Creator Lo-Fi Statics | 16:15 | Useful companion asset around partnership creators; creates a small creator-specific funnel. |
| Objection Handling | 18:09 | Converts mid-funnel concerns and can enter the top 15 quickly when persona objections are known. |
| Founder's Letter | 19:19 | Can perform during sale periods, but is usually not the main scaling unicorn. |
| Conversation Ads | 20:18 | Two or more people create a natural discussion format; can perform but is difficult for creators to execute well. |
| Educational Infographics | 20:40 | Masquerades as useful organic education and supports already-proven big ideas. |
| Moodboard Ads | 21:21 | Strong category-native support in apparel when personas already consume moodboards organically. |
| Ugly Ads or Handwriting Ads | 22:18 | Intentionally unpolished sale-period creative; especially useful for urgency and posted-note-style promotions. |
| Comment Reply Ads | 24:34 | Answers FAQs and objections in mid/lower funnel; can move higher if the question is broad enough for prospecting. |
| Challenging Your Beliefs | 29:08 | Uses persona limiting beliefs plus available B-roll; reported to work across industries and clients. |

## C Tier — Conditional or Short-Lived

| Format | Timestamp | Source-grounded rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| AI Animation Ads | 00:34 | Novel and capable of reaching a new pocket, but operationally complex and often short-lived. |
| Statistics | 13:07 | Better for awareness or traffic and some large retail/luxury brands than direct-response DTC ROI. |
| Celebrity Ads | 17:24 | Highly variable: some celebrity matches work, many become expensive underperformers. |
| AI Avatar Ads | 19:39 | Can become a top performer, but disclosure, brand stance, trust, and likely fatigue make it unstable. |
| Warehouse Ads | 22:40 | Useful behind-the-scenes and sale energy; operationally hard and rarely top-five unless combined with founder content. |
| Street Interviews | 23:25 | Can perform, but production is painful enough that staged execution may be more practical. |
| Duet Reaction / Stitch-Style Ads | 26:46 | Can enter the top five but introduces usage-rights and production complexity. |
| ASMR Ads | 27:39 | Strong category fit in pets and beauty with specialist creators; not usually a scale-to-the-moon winner. |
| UGC | 27:59 | Still produces hits through volume, but manufactured problem-solution voiceover plus B-roll is fatigued. |

## D Tier — Low-Priority Experiments

| Format | Timestamp | Source-grounded rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Breaking News | 10:27 | Can generate sales but has not recently been a business-transforming format in Dara's experience. |
| AI Billboard Ads | 21:48 | Overdone and cheesy; Dara reports no recent wins in her accounts, while noting second-hand category exceptions. |
| GRWM / DITL | 28:42 | Often fails to scale on paid unless the product fits naturally into the routine and the creator is unusually well matched. |

## E Tier — Usually Not Worth the Production Time

| Format | Timestamp | Source-grounded rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Text Only Ads | 07:27 | Weak in a visual feed and often made worse by bland AI copy; sale-period founder letters are the named exception. |
| Listicles | 12:08 | Produced hits one or two years earlier but, in Dara's current account view, has not done meaningful work lately. |
| Carousel Ads | 26:01 | Multiple required assets and uncertain story sequencing make the production cost hard to justify. |

## F Tier — Reject by Default

| Format | Timestamp | Source-grounded rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Press Ads | 09:45 | Publication permissions and paid usage rights create a practical legal and operational veto. |
| Testimonial Statics | 11:43 | A quote slapped onto a static rarely performs; only unusually strong golden-nugget proof is a plausible exception. |
| Podcast Ads | 18:41 | Recent tests have not moved the needle; a real founder appearance on a known podcast is the named exception. |
| Notes App UX Ads | 24:57 | Familiar UI stops too broad an audience and may send poor targeting signals; also consumes heavy production bandwidth. |
