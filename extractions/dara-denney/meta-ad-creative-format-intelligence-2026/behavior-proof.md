# Behavior Proof — Claim-Sensitive Health Performance Brand

## Input Tested

A funded direct-to-consumer hydration brand has one flagship powder, a credible founder story, access to a registered dietitian, an immature creator program, a $15,000 four-week Meta test budget, and strict claim-safety requirements. The objective is cold-audience acquisition plus enough education to overcome skepticism.

This is a **simulated fixture**, not a live client or market result.

## Cold-Start Control — Before the New System

Given only the fixture input and the older format menu, the plausible generic
answer is: produce several UGC problem-solution videos, a founder testimonial,
podcast-style clips, testimonial statics, and headline variations. That answer
names deliverables but does not assign distinct strategic jobs, test access or
rights, separate message from format, use a claim veto, name rejection logic,
or define evidence that would promote or stop each cell.

## Weakness Diagnosed

The prior generic plan would likely overproduce standard UGC, podcast-looking clips, testimonial statics, and headline variants. That creates volume without separating scale, trust, education, and mid-funnel jobs—and it ignores creator-access and claims risk.

## Source Mechanics Used

- **Founder's Ads (S)** for origin, identification, and a human reason to care.
- **VSL (S)** for high-education health conversion, subject to a factual/claim gate.
- **Authority Ads (A)** for dietitian-led objection handling and trust transfer.
- **Educational Infographics (B)** as a supporting-cast, organic-feeling education asset.
- **Partnership Ads (S)** held until creator fit and content proof exist.
- **UGC (C)** kept as a bounded probe, not the portfolio owner.
- **Podcast Ads (F)**, **Testimonial Statics (F)**, and **AI Billboard Ads (D)** rejected by default.

## Output Produced

### Recommended portfolio

1. **Founder Ad × "why ordinary hydration advice failed me"**
   - Job: net-new emotional access and problem identification.
   - Production: phone-first founder story with product mechanism shown, not inflated.
   - Proof gate: every physiological statement mapped to approved evidence.

2. **Authority Ad × "what hydration products can and cannot claim"**
   - Job: trust and objection handling.
   - Production: registered dietitian answering one real skepticism point; credentials and usage rights verified.
   - Proof gate: authority cannot substitute for source quality.

3. **VSL × "the three reasons your hydration routine feels inconsistent"**
   - Job: education plus conversion for a skeptical cold audience.
   - Production: 45-90 second explainer with a narrow problem, mechanism, evidence boundary, and offer.
   - Proof gate: factual veto before script lock.

4. **Educational Infographic × one evidence-backed category insight**
   - Job: supporting-cast education and retargeting bridge.
   - Production: chart-first static with one claim and one source, not a benefits collage.

### Holds and rejects

- **Hold Partnership Ads** until three creators produce organic or low-spend proof and rights are clear.
- **Bound UGC** to one cheap cell; do not let volume masquerade as strategy.
- **Reject Podcast Ads** unless the founder has a real, recognized podcast appearance.
- **Reject Testimonial Statics** unless review mining finds a specific golden-nugget proof statement that survives the claim gate.
- **Reject AI Billboard Ads** because the source prior is weak and the category's compliance cost raises the downside.

### Cross-domain transfer

| Paid format | Content/strategy adaptation | Mechanism preserved |
|---|---|---|
| Founder Ad | LinkedIn origin series or founder newsletter | human why + shared problem |
| Authority Ad | Expert FAQ carousel or cited short explainer | trust transfer + objection resolution |
| VSL | Webinar, landing-page explainer, or long-form YouTube | education sequence + conversion |
| Educational Infographic | LinkedIn chart, sales-enablement slide, or client education asset | useful information that earns attention |

## Behavior Delta

The new system changes the same input from a flat list of familiar deliverables
into a portfolio with four distinct jobs, explicit access and claim constraints,
named holds, and source-grounded rejects. It also transfers mechanisms across
channels without copying Meta's surface aesthetics. The observable behavior
change is recommendation plus veto plus proof design—not merely a longer answer.
