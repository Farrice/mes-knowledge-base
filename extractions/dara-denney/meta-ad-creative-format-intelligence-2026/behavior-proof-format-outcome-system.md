## Output Produced: F01 Complete Concept and Production Brief

### Decision Lock

- **Format:** Founder’s Ad; `SOURCE PRIOR S`; `NO LIVE EVIDENCE`.
- **Job:** Earn recognition from an established health/performance founder or growth lead before an offer conversation.
- **Message × persona:** Product facts do not automatically become buyer beliefs × translation-burdened founder.
- **Mechanism:** A lived founder observation makes the hidden message decision visible.
- **Hypothesis:** The human explanation will earn more target-role recognition language than a faceless explanation of the same message.
- **Non-goal:** This asset does not promise conversion lift, claim legal compliance, or prove exact-offer demand.

### Concept Core

A 45–60 second direct-to-camera video opens on the counter-side observation: buyers ask simple questions that brand language often fails to answer. It distinguishes a true product fact from the belief a buyer must hold, shows why more wrappers do not resolve that decision, and introduces the Angle Map as the decision surface.

### Content Strategy

- **Viewer state:** The target has seen plenty of creative activity but cannot name the strategic argument being tested.
- **First value moment:** “Your product fact and your buyer’s belief are not the same sentence.”
- **Retention chain:** lived observation -> familiar production symptom -> belief distinction -> usable four-part decision -> safe next step.
- **Share/save reason:** The line gives a growth lead language to explain the bottleneck internally.
- **Distribution role:** Organic LinkedIn recognition asset and direct follow-up proof object; not a paid-scale claim.

### Copy System

- **Primary hook direction:** “Three years behind a supplement counter taught me where marketing actually lands: in a question the brand rarely hears.”
- **Alternates:** “Your product fact and your buyer’s belief are not the same sentence.” / “Five new assets can still be one old argument.”
- **Spine:** counter observation -> buyer question -> unresolved belief -> Angle Map decision -> public-material invitation.
- **Proof placement:** Verified lived background supports the observation. No client result or performance number is inserted.
- **CTA state:** Invite review of a public page or campaign until payment and confidential-input procedures are active.
- **Voice boundary:** Load the Farrice Voice Card at scripting; use his words and plain register, not expert vocabulary or theatrical polish.

### Visual Direction

- **Visual hook:** Direct eye contact with the words “PRODUCT FACT” and “BUYER BELIEF” appearing as two visibly separate objects.
- **Emotional core:** Relief from creative churn; calm recognition rather than alarm.
- **Cultural anchor:** Founder work session, not laboratory authority or glossy supplement advertising.
- **Sequence:** face/open -> two-part text split -> five-wrapper montage or simple repeated-card treatment -> three-angle decision surface -> direct close.
- **Production level:** Phone-first, controlled daylight, clean audio, restrained captions, no stock medical imagery, white coats, efficacy graphics, or fake dashboards.
- **Required assets:** Approved Angle Map sample, exact offer canon, verified background facts, brand typography/colors, and public example if used.

### Claims and Proof

| Claim or implication | State | Evidence | Allowed expression |
|---|---|---|---|
| Three years behind a supplement counter | `APPROVED: LOCAL CANON` | Farrice background canon | Plain lived observation |
| Angle Map scope and 48-hour delivery | `APPROVED: LOCAL CANON` | Current offer canon | Exact current scope only |
| Improves conversion or lowers acquisition cost | `OUT OF SCOPE` | No outcome evidence | Do not imply |
| Named product health outcome | `HOLD` | Finished-product evidence not supplied | Exclude from this asset |
| Legal or compliance approval | `OUT OF SCOPE` | Editorial service only | “I read for signal, not law” |

**Net-impression verdict:** `SHIP` as a production brief; the final script still requires claim audit and human taste approval before publication.

### Production Plan

- **Deliverables:** 45–60 second script, two openings, vertical shot list, caption file, thumbnail/frame, and claim inventory.
- **Sequence:** script -> claim audit -> human voice/taste pass -> shot list -> record -> edit -> final claim/main-impression check.
- **Owners:** Farrice on camera; Dara integrates; named editor/producer remains `UNCONFIRMED`.
- **Rights:** Use owned material and approved public examples only.
- **Budget:** `UNCONFIRMED`; no paid generation or media authorized.
- **QA:** one job, one message, audible speech, legible captions, no unsupported result, correct CTA state.
- **Stop:** claim `HOLD`, unavailable rights, unverified background line, or publication without approval.

### Test and Outcome-Ledger Registration

The production brief carries a ready-to-fill observation command. It requires actual spend, hook numerator/denominator, hook definition, conversion receipt state, fatigue window, category, persona, message, campaign, and asset IDs. No real observation is written until those values exist.

## Behavior Delta

The input now produces one shootable brief with bounded craft ownership and a ready evidence-write path. A future agent no longer has to invent owner order, reconcile four separate outputs, or decide whether hook rate alone means promotion.

## Validation Run

- Unit negative control: 90% hook rate -> `NO DECISION`.
- Weighted aggregation uses hook-event totals divided by hook opportunities.
- Conversion evidence above `none` fails without a receipt.
- The task-specific system verifier checks wiring, prompts, wrappers, schema, and cold-start behavior.

## Remaining Risk

No live campaign evidence exists for F01. Production thresholds, fatigue windows, and conversion attribution must be set from the real account or organic test plan. This proof validates system behavior, not creative performance.
