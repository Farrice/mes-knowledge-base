## Behavior Deltas

### Competitor Messaging

- **Input tested:** Three supplied homepage excerpts plus a challenger brief.
- **Weakness diagnosed:** Summary was being mistaken for positioning evidence; whitespace was asserted without alternatives or proof.
- **Source mechanics used:** Common comparison matrix, crowded-trope scan, and defensible-direction follow-up.
- **Output produced:** Evidence-locked matrix, alternatives/status quo, supported-vs-hypothesis whitespace, and proof requirements.
- **Behavior delta:** The system can refuse invented whitespace while still producing a useful positioning direction.
- **Remaining risk:** Current competitor truth and buyer preference require live evidence.

### Email Nurture

- **Input tested:** Calculator signup to paid trial.
- **Weakness diagnosed:** Repetitive benefit emails with a CTA in every message.
- **Source mechanics used:** Generate the full sequence in one context and preserve pacing.
- **Output produced:** Belief/objection journey, education-first emails, proof placement, CTA progression, and voice dependency.
- **Behavior delta:** The sequence moves reader belief rather than producing isolated copy variations.
- **Remaining risk:** Conversion remains `NO EVENT` until sent under approval and measured.

### Campaign Diagnostics

- **Input tested:** Channel spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, and attributed revenue.
- **Weakness diagnosed:** Metric narration and platform ROAS were being treated as causal budget truth.
- **Source mechanics used:** Diagnostic interpretation and ordered action sequence.
- **Output produced:** Observed-vs-inferred decomposition, alternative explanations, reversible allocation, confirming signal, stop, and rollback.
- **Behavior delta:** The system can recommend a bounded experiment without pretending attribution proves incrementality.
- **Remaining risk:** Definitions, margins, cohorts, holdouts, and post-change observation determine the real verdict.
