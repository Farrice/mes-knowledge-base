# Deep Extraction: Marketing Specialist Workbench

## The Hidden Method

The seven demonstrations are not primarily about writing faster. They use one durable operating pattern:

1. Frame a real marketing decision.
2. Supply the commercial context a senior specialist would receive.
3. Demand a structured artifact, not open-ended advice.
4. Ask for the mechanism or diagnosis behind the artifact.
5. Choose and test rather than copy and publish.

The source's strongest contribution is the distinction between **artifact production** and **decision support**. Its weakest contribution is proof: it frequently treats plausible model interpretation as if it were enough to guide budget, positioning, or performance decisions.

## Workflow 1 — Strategic Ad Variation

The operative unit is not five rewrites. It is five different reasons to care. A useful concept changes the buyer tension, awareness stage, proof burden, and visual vessel while holding product, audience, platform, and goal constant.

Antigravity amplification:

- Oren × Dara selects the psychological mechanism before format.
- Dara's test plan prevents five concepts from becoming an unprioritized pile.
- Claims without proof become hypotheses, not ad facts.
- Benoit owns economics and performance diagnosis; creative strategy cannot infer ROAS.

Expected artifact: mechanism-distinct concept matrix, awareness/funnel fit, proof requirement, CTA, format, failure mode, and optional test cell.

## Workflow 2 — Evidence-Locked Competitor Messaging

The source's five comparison dimensions are valuable because they force like-for-like comparison. The danger is inventing whitespace from incomplete or stale copy.

Antigravity amplification:

- Raw competitor language stays traceable to the supplied page or source row.
- April Dunford expands “competitor” into product alternatives, non-product alternatives, and status quo.
- Jordan Crawford preserves facts, quotes, unknowns, and the exact decision a positioning owner may change.
- A gap is labeled defensible only when the evidence supports it; otherwise it is a research hypothesis.

Expected artifact: source inventory, comparison matrix, crowded tropes, alternative/status-quo map, whitespace verdict, two evidence-bounded directions, and proof/research gaps.

## Workflow 3 — Campaign-Progression Calendar

A calendar is useful when weeks change buyer state. The source's progression—urgency, practical problem, proof, conversion—provides a campaign spine, while channel-native adaptation prevents repurposing mush.

Antigravity amplification:

- A creator-led business routes to Jun Yuh's sustainable content-to-revenue system.
- A company or brand routes to Oren Content Team Architecture and its flywheel nodes.
- Each piece names its campaign job, platform-native format, CTA friction, and measurement signal.
- The calendar cannot claim a content-market fit win before publication.

Expected artifact: four-week progression table plus anchor/derivative map, capacity check, CTA ladder, and review cadence.

## Workflow 4 — Nurture as a Belief Journey

The source correctly keeps the sequence in one context so pacing and trust compound. The deeper unit is a belief transition: each email must move the reader one believable step without forcing the sale early.

Antigravity amplification:

- Craig Clemens owns education-first sequence architecture.
- Entry event and reader awareness control the opening state.
- Objections, proof, and CTA pressure are sequenced rather than repeated.
- Voice comes from Voice OS or the client voice document, not a generic tone adjective.

Expected artifact: belief/objection map and 5–9 complete emails with subject lines, standalone value, proof placement, CTA intensity, and test map.

## Workflow 6 — Voice as Testable Behavioral Rules

The useful move is converting successful examples into observable rules: rhythm, vocabulary, punctuation, structure, channel shifts, and prohibitions. The validation transformation turns the guide from description into a working standard.

Antigravity amplification:

- Farrice's work loads `/voice-os` and the canonical Voice Card; it does not rebuild his voice from three samples.
- Client work uses `/voice-document`, then `/voice-audit` on a neutral transformation and a real draft.
- Samples must be approved and representative; performance success and voice representativeness are separate questions.

Expected artifact: voice reference or grounding brief plus an A/B transformation, fidelity breaks, and a reuse boundary.

## Workflow 7 — Diagnostic Analysis With Causal Restraint

The source correctly distinguishes reporting from analysis and sequences action. Its example becomes unsafe if observed metric relationships are treated as causal truth.

Antigravity amplification:

- Benoit's Golden Core diagnostic owns platform-performance diagnosis.
- Spend Map owns full-funnel allocation imbalance.
- BV × Vince owns blended attribution and incrementality design.
- Recommendations identify what is observed, what is inferred, what evidence would discriminate between causes, and what reversible test runs next.

Expected artifact: metric decomposition, observed drivers, alternative explanations, reversible allocation proposal, confirming signal, and stop/rollback condition.

## Insight-Density Rule

During QA, every retained section must do at least one job:

- present evidence;
- state a labeled inference;
- make a decision;
- deliver the requested artifact; or
- define a test or next action.

Generic explanations of AI, marketing, or the selected framework are deleted unless omitting them would cause misuse. This rule governs the conductor's result surface, not the specialist's native craft method.

## Promotion Boundary

Explicit invocation may run after deterministic validation. Natural-language routing remains `SHADOW`: it may recommend the command but cannot silently execute, block work, or become a global default. Promotion requires three distinct real production receipts, a no-regression blind comparison, preserved creative range, and Farrice's explicit approval.
