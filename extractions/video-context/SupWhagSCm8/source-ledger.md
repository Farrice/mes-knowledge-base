## Evidence Boundary

- `VERIFIED` is reserved for facts independently checked in the current run.
- `TRANSCRIPT_BACKED` means the presenter said or demonstrated the method verbally.
- `LIKELY` and `UNCONFIRMED` identify interpretations with incomplete evidence.
- `UNTESTED` identifies predictions or proposed effects without an observed result.
- `NO EVENT` identifies a required market or production event that has not happened.
