# Label-Blinded Comparison — Production Cycle 03

This is a label-blinded structural comparison using the declared production
rubric. It is not an independent human preference panel.

## Variant A

Lead-to-Proposal wins the weighted tournament by 4.33 to 4.30. Use the next 20
qualified conversations to test it. Another candidate must show stronger buyer
cash evidence and a faster credible demonstration to change the verdict.

## Variant B

Keep the locked Lead-to-Proposal offer and prepare the existing 30-message test.
Freeze the buyer, offer, price, and scope. Record sent, replies, calls, sold, and
collected. Treat no replies as a list/message problem, qualified calls without
deposits as an offer or proof problem, one collected deposit as exact-offer
proof, and 20 qualified conversations with no deposit as the reversal gate.

## Result

| Criterion | A | B | Result |

| Decision change | Selects a winner and a conversation count | Preserves the winner and makes each test outcome actionable | B materially improves the decision |

| Creative range | Not applicable | Alternative offer remains visible but parked | No regression |
| Native-owner fit | Focuses the offer | Focuses the offer and prevents simultaneous variable changes | B |

## Verdict

Variant B materially improves strategic decision quality without overturning
the locked offer. Reviewer limitation: Codex applied the declared rubric; no independent human preference panel occurred.
