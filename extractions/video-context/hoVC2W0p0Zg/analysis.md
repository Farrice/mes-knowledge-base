## Durable Mechanics

1. **Decision-retaining automation.** The course repeatedly separates production from judgment: AI performs bounded work, the operator makes the call, and the result must still sound like the operator. The durable unit is a human gate, not a model name.
2. **Portable context before production.** Identity, audience, voice, offer, visual taste, and refusals are prepared before asking for assets. The visual brain is described as intelligence, not decoration.
3. **Brain + generator + skills.** Context holds durable truth; a generator turns a chosen direction into options; skills encode repeated jobs. Treating all three as prompts collapses distinct maintenance and quality responsibilities.
4. **Surplus changes selection quality.** A month of content is framed as inventory in front of the operator, not a commitment to post every item. Generation creates choice; judgment creates the calendar.
5. **One repeated job earns one skill.** The speaker explicitly warns that not everything needs to become a skill. A skill becomes justified by repeatability, not by the fact that a task can be described.
6. **Ordered production spine.** `Focus → Style → Plan → Create → Package` prevents premature production. Each stage reduces a different kind of ambiguity before handing work forward.
7. **Person → Tension → Path.** The focus stage uses a compact monetization spine: who the content is for, the live tension it names, and the path it opens.
8. **Refusal is positive context.** Anti-style examples and what the operator refuses are treated as part of the brain. This is stronger than relying on a late polish pass to remove generic output.
9. **Model/tool choice follows task shape.** The course maps models by thinking/building/judging demands and shows platform connections, but also allows substitutions. The transferable rule is task-to-adapter fit, not product loyalty.
10. **First-week adoption over full-stack completion.** The source closes by shrinking the build: make one foundation, create one test, turn one repeated job into one skill, run the system for one week, and expand only from observed use.
