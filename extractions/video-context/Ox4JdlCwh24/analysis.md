- **Expert:** Nicolas Cole, digital writer and operator behind Ship 30 for 30 and multiple writing businesses.
- **Domain:** digital-writing formats, skill acquisition, content libraries, cross-format leverage, prompt productization.
- **Depth:** practitioner-level with several reusable strategic mechanics; limited craft demonstration.
- **Virtuoso patterns detected:** 12 source-backed or source-derived mechanics.
- **Extraction value:** HIGH. The source adds a missing orchestration layer across several existing Nicolas Cole capabilities, but it does not justify treating five dated formats as permanent doctrine.

## Verdict

Build a cold component skill under the existing Nicolas Cole agent: `nicolas-cole-library-first-writing`.

Do not add a new global command or a general content mega-skill. Existing owners already cover sentence craft, newsletters, digital products, carousel production, rhetoric, and multi-format multiplication. The missing capability is the sequencing and save-back logic that turns one useful idea into a durable library unit, selects the next fitting format, and harvests proven processes into prompt assets.

## Connected Route

```text
raw idea or existing asset
  -> source/proof check
  -> library-unit card
  -> format-rung decision
  -> one primary asset plus at most two useful derivatives
  -> craft-owner handoff
  -> save-back with performance evidence
  -> optional prompt/product handoff after the process is articulated
```

## Systems-Thinking Decision

The wider outcome is not more posts. It is an owned, inspectable idea library that becomes easier to reuse without flattening every idea into every format. Nicolas Cole remains the method owner. Visual owners certify carousel taste; product owners certify monetization. The paved default is the library-unit card and save-back rule, with platform choice left variable.
