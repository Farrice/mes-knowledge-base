# Systems Thinking Overlay: Three-Mission SHADOW Evaluation

## Evaluation Question

Does the Systems Thinking and Expertise Intelligence Overlay improve materially different Antigravity missions without adding questions, blocking safe work, diluting specialist craft, or creating another control plane?

The evaluation replayed three recent non-extraction missions: the God Agent offer, The Angle Map and LinkedIn system, and the Health Performance GEO Daily Brief. All were real completed tasks with recoverable artifacts and explicit proof boundaries. The overlay was applied retrospectively, so this tests portability and explanatory usefulness rather than prospective causal lift.

## Cross-Mission Result

The overlay found material decision changes in all three missions.

The God Agent replay preserved the large category ambition while narrowing the first buyer, responsibility, permissions, paid foundation, and proof event. The Angle Map replay separated craft readiness from demand proof, protected Farrice’s taste authority, and stopped full-arsenal blending. The Health GEO replay traced a failed output to its upstream premise, preserved ledger history, and selected repair plus supersession instead of duplicate production or a new gate.

No replay needed an added user question, mandatory field, readiness score, safe-work block, router, command, skill, agent, hook, dependency, external write, or mutation to the source mission.

## Composition Ledger

The evaluation owner was the Systems Thinking Overlay in SHADOW mode. Revenue Offer remained the native owner for God Agent. Revenue Offer, copy craft, visual design, and Farrice’s taste gate retained bounded authority for The Angle Map. Research Intelligence, Content and Media, claim safety, and the automation owner retained bounded authority for Health GEO. Knowledge Librarian supplied prior-session retrieval, while Expert Composition prevented those task owners from becoming a new universal council.

Accepted contributions changed named decisions and appear in receipts 02–04. The suggested Extract Mastery and video routes were rejected because no new source extraction or media production was required. System Audit was also skipped because the task tested advisory behavior rather than repairing broken firing or routing.

Expert soup check: **PASS**.

## Promotion-Bar Audit

The three independent task-type replay requirement is met: Revenue Strategy, Offer Copy and Acquisition, and Research Content Operations. All three preserved specialist depth and human accountability. The observed replay burden was zero additional questions and zero new safe-work blocks.

The blind-comparison requirement is not met. These sessions were selected with knowledge of their outcomes, so selection and hindsight bias remain. Prospective causal lift is also untested. Explicit promotion approval was not requested or inferred.

## Global-Use Verdict

**GO for global SHADOW availability; NO-GO for promotion or enforcement.**

The sensible global behavior is quiet and conditional: load the cold primitive only when its activation conditions are met, expose a trace only when a decision materially changes, and stay silent on tiny or already paved work. The overlay should not become a hot skill, command, router, mandatory schema, score, hook, or automatic task generator.

No global files were changed during this evaluation. Global availability would require a separate, explicit, thin-pointer implementation decision after a prospective blind comparison.
