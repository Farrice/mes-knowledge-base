# Architecture: Systems Thinking and Expertise Intelligence Overlay

## Intent and Outcome Lock

The build must help Antigravity's existing operators, experts, skills, and agentic workflows make coherent system decisions at higher velocity without weakening specialist judgment, adding a new control plane, or turning an advisory heuristic into a block.

Source-to-Skill-System is the function and integration owner. Extract-Forge supplies the Deep evidence layer. Expert Composition governs contribution boundaries. Existing contracts remain authoritative for their domains.

1. **Target:** a new cold semantic primitive plus narrow advisory references in operating alignment, expert composition, skill systems, agentic engineering, maintenance learning, and Operator School.
2. **Scope:** add the five decisions Zoom, Craft, Pave, Own, and Learn; add source paths, SHADOW status, proof fixtures, and deterministic verification. Exclude routers, hooks, global mirrors, Mission mutation, hot commands, new skills, new agents, and external writes.

6. **Measurable stop:** all required files exist; five owner integrations are detected; three cold-start cases show a changed decision; relevant existing verifiers pass; prose and grounding guards pass.
7. **Turn cap:** one implementation pass and one repair pass per failing verifier.
8. **Evaluator:** deterministic verifier, existing control-plane checks, source package verifier, and before/after proof review.
9. **Wake-up check:** `python3 execution/verify_systems_thinking_expertise_overlay.py`.

11. **Rollback rule:** remove the five narrow references, verifier, proof fixture, and primitive together. Do not leave authoritative-looking historical claims that contradict the restored runtime.

## Promotion and Kill Rules

The layer remains `SHADOW`. It may inform decisions but cannot block work, add mandatory questions, create a score, or claim universal applicability. Promotion requires three independent production receipts, blind preference, zero false blocks on frozen safe controls, preserved creative range and specialist depth, and explicit Farrice approval.

Kill or keep cold if it produces generic abstraction, duplicates current owners, adds ceremony without changing a decision, or makes clear tasks slower and less decisive.
