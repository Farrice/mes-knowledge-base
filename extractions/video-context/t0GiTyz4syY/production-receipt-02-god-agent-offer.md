# Production Replay Receipt 02: God Agent Offer

## Status

This is an independent retrospective replay of a real Revenue Strategy mission. The overlay remained `SHADOW`. The source mission is the Codex task `Revenue: God Agent Offer - AI-Native Business Verdict`, and the primary evidence artifact is the `god-agent-commercial-viability-dossier.md` in the `bf76` Codex worktree.

The mission began with a broad ambition: an AI employee that knows a company and takes meaningful work off the team. Its final commercial state was `CONDITIONAL GO` and `TEST`, with zero exact proposals, collections, installations, outcomes, renewals, or referrals.

## Material Systems-Thinking Trace

### Zoom

The local question was which AI employee to sell. One level up, the expensive problem was that the company’s best operating judgment was fragmented across people, policies, exceptions, and systems. This changed the sellable object from generic AI capability to conversion of company truth into one governed employee responsibility. It also preserved the big category ambition while refusing whole-company omniscience as the founding contract.

### Craft

Revenue Offer and Expert Practice judgment owned the commercial decision. Deep Research owned current evidence, AI Employee OS supplied the operating mechanism, and Farrice retained authority over the category promise and founder-fit claims. This changed the composition from a general AI council into bounded evidence, mechanism, and risk contributions under one commercial owner.

### Pave

The reusable default became a paid Company Intelligence Foundation inside the install: approved truth, authority, exceptions, permissions, unresolved knowledge, and representative evaluation cases before activation. The bounded last mile was one top-five member-service preparation responsibility for one warm-access fitness operator. This changed the offer from “build an agent” into a staged company-intelligence asset with a fail-closed `STOP`, `NARROW`, or `BUILD` path.

### Own

The buyer needed a policy owner, centrally authorized access, human review, least privilege, and explicit recovery conditions. Farrice’s delivery exposure was capped to one install, one platform, buyer-hour and provider-hour limits, no sensitive health data, and no consequential autonomous action. This changed success from an impressive demo into an explainable, testable, reversible operating responsibility.

### Learn

The completed research and eight-point execution wargame were local readiness evidence, not demand. The overlay changes the next system move from more architecture, more wargaming, or a broader content funnel to one unchanged paid proof test after approval. A signed contract and cleared Foundation payment—not enthusiasm or another build artifact—become the next learning event.

## Decision Delta

1. The founding product is company-truth conversion plus one employee responsibility, not agent plumbing.
2. Fitness becomes a proof beachhead rather than the permanent ceiling of the category.
3. The Company Intelligence Foundation stays paid and inside the $12,000 install instead of becoming free discovery.
4. One governed member-operations job replaces broad day-one autonomy.
5. The next learning event is an exact-offer payment test; additional internal design work is parked.

## Verdict

Because it was applied retrospectively after the successful narrowing was known, it does not satisfy the blind-comparison requirement by itself.
