## Desire-Based Hook Extraction

| Hook Component | Operational Definition | Radar Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Dream outcome | The hook names a concrete result the viewer wants. | `desire_template()` detects get/grow/build/make/create/win language and maps it to `Dream Outcome`. |
| Relatable character | The hook has a person the audience can project into. | First-person, creator/founder/beginner, or regular-person framing maps to `Relatable Character`. |
| Condition-free method | The hook removes an assumed requirement or constraint. | "Without", "even if", "using only", "no X required", and time-box constraints map to `Condition-Free Method`. |
| Unfair-advantage filter | The hook suggests a system, template, edge, mechanism, or hidden leverage. | Secret/template/system/framework/machine language maps to `Unfair-Advantage Filter`. |
| Conversion-oriented scoring | Views alone are not enough; lead or buyer-quality signals matter when available. | `OutlierScore` multiplies baseline outlier score with engagement and lead-rate signals, then labels confidence. |

## Five Initial Hook Templates

1. Concrete outcome from constrained input: "How to get [dream result] with [small/limited input]."
2. Relatable character breakthrough: "How [ordinary/recognizable person] got [desired result] after [constraint]."
3. Condition-free method: "Get [result] without [old prerequisite]."
4. Unfair advantage reveal: "The [system/template/mechanism] behind [specific result]."
5. Specific declarative amplifier: "[Concrete claim]. Here is the counterintuitive reason it works."
