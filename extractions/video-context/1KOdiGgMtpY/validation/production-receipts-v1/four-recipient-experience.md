# What Signal Fidelity Feels Like to Four Different Recipients

The repaired layer does not give everyone the same receipt. It preserves one
intent while changing how much structure becomes visible.

| Recipient | Before the repair | After the repair |
|---|---|---|
| Farrice making a consequential decision | A natural memo can leave the decision owner implicit. | A compact decision receipt names owner, promise, limit, proof state, and what must survive. |
| Human collaborator | A universal capsule would make a readable handoff feel procedural. | The collaborator receives the natural summary; the structured capsule stays cold. |
| AI agent executing work | A conversational handoff can be readable but underspecified for execution. | The agent receives explicit fields, constraints, proof state, owner, and stop condition. |
| Public reader | Visible audit scaffolding would interrupt the writing. | The reader sees the original post unchanged; the audit remains owner-only. |

## 1. Farrice: Consequential Strategy

### Normal experience

> Run a seven-day proof sprint aimed at earning the first $500 from an
> Authority Flywheel pilot. Choose three warm strength-and-conditioning coaches
> whose ideas are stronger than their current content.

The memo is easy to read, but it leaves the decision owner implicit.

### Signal Fidelity experience

> **Recipient:** Farrice deciding whether to run the seven-day revenue sprint.  
> **Promise:** Seek one paid 30-day pilot while producing reusable proof assets.  
> **Limit:** Response and close rates are assumptions; external action requires
> separate approval.  
> **Proof state:** `NO EVENT`.

Visible effect: recoverability moves from 5/6 to 6/6 for nine normalized words.

## 2. Human Collaborator: Conversational Handoff

### Before the repair

The earlier universal approach risked showing the collaborator a labeled
recipient, promise, limit, proof-state, source-path, and must-survive form even
when a normal summary was easier to use.

### After the repair: what the human actually sees

> Resume the Jen-Team Pilot offer packaging; don’t rebuild the brief. The
> current direction is a $200 founding Listing Launch Kit with a fallback offer,
> one clear positioning line, three pitch drafts, a repeatable six-step delivery
> process, a 30-day trial with kill criteria, and objection handling.
>
> Read the existing deliverable before editing anything. The package is
> prepared, not demand-proven. Farrice owns offer, price, and kill-criteria
> changes.

Visible effect: 6/6 recoverability with no structured capsule shown.

## 3. AI Agent: Execution Handoff

### Before the repair

The AI would receive the same conversational summary as the human and infer
which details were instructions, context, boundaries, or proof claims.

### After the repair: what the AI actually receives

> **Intended change:** Complete the existing package without reconstructing or
> expanding it.  
> **Promise:** A $200 founding Listing Launch Kit.  
> **Limit:** Do not rebuild shipped material; external pitching is unapproved.  
> **Proof state:** `UNTESTED`.  
> **Must survive:** fallback SKU, positioning line, three pitches, six delivery
> steps, 30-day trial, kill criteria, objection handling, and the existing work.

Visible effect: the same 6/6 recoverability as the human version, with 25 extra
normalized words buying explicit machine-execution boundaries.

## 4. Public Reader: LinkedIn Post

### Before the repair

A universal receipt could have added claim labels, audit notes, or fidelity
scaffolding beside the prose simply to prove the check ran.

### After the repair: what the public actually sees

> USANA paid $205 million for Hiya, a kids' vitamin brand built on zero sugar.
> Ask an AI for the best no-sugar kids vitamin and it answers Flintstones.

Later, the native post carries its own boundary:

> I measured visibility, not vitamins. 24 runs across 2 surfaces, below my own
> 3-surface standard since Perplexity's API was down that morning.

Visible effect: the source artifact is byte-for-byte unchanged. Signal Fidelity
checks the literal boundaries and leaves voice, metaphor, and composition to the
content owner.

## Bottom Line

Signal Fidelity is not a better writing format. It is a selective translation
layer: explicit where execution or consequential compression benefits from
structure, invisible where human readability or public craft already carries
the signal.
