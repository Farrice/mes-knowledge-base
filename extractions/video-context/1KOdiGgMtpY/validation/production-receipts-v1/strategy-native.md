# Native Strategy Memo

Run a seven-day proof sprint aimed at earning the first $500 from an Authority
Flywheel pilot. Choose three warm strength-and-conditioning coaches whose ideas
are stronger than their current content. For each, study their public voice,
turn one underdeveloped post into a newsletter and three LinkedIn posts, record
a short walkthrough, and use the result as both outreach and portfolio proof.

Start only after the profile, landing page, scorecard, first post, Loom account,
and three targets are ready. Offer the first one or two 30-day pilots at
$500-$750 in exchange for permission to use the before-and-after and a
testimonial. The expected response and close rates are assumptions, not results;
there is currently no payment event. Sending DMs, publishing examples, and
contacting targets remain separate approval decisions.
