# Signal Fidelity Keep / Change / Remove Decision Card

Status: `PRECOMMITTED`. No result has permission to change these rules after the
arms are produced.

## KEEP COLD SHADOW

All of the following are required:

1. At least two of three treatment wins and no baseline wins.
2. At least two material decision details recovered that would otherwise be lost.
3. Zero proof inflation, false blocks, owner displacement, or mandatory questions.
4. Added operator burden is no more than one minute or one reused checksum per
   selected consequential task; clear and ineligible work receives no questions.
5. Creative range is preserved: metaphor, ambiguity, voice, and approved
   reframing remain available and are not exact-match failures.
6. An evaluator independent of the builder blindly prefers or more accurately
   plays back the treatment in at least two cases, with no baseline preference.
7. Farrice explicitly approves continued cold SHADOW use.

KEEP means continued optional observation only. It does not authorize automatic
activation, enforcement, mandatory schemas, commands, hooks, routing, or global
promotion.

## CHANGE SHADOW

Use one bounded repair when the core hypothesis shows value but any one condition
holds:

- one false-positive family has a narrow identifiable cause;
- treatment improves fidelity but adds avoidable words or questions;
- creative language is mistaken for literal loss;
- owner-approved evolution is reported as distortion;
- a native owner already carries most of the signal and the companion duplicates it.

After one repair, replay only the failed and frozen controls plus one untouched
case. Do not redesign the entire layer around a favorable example.

## REMOVE

Remove the prototype when any condition holds:

- zero treatment wins across the three heterogeneous cases;
- the baseline wins any two cases;
- warnings are mostly false positives;
- operators must fill new fields or answer questions on clear work;
- the layer narrows voice, metaphor, ambiguity, or deliberate evolution;
- it displaces the native intent, strategy, content, handoff, proof, or human owner;
- independent recipients show no material comprehension improvement.

Removal is additive rollback: revert the prototype files and three optional owner
pointers. No state migration exists.

## Evidence Boundary

Local marker coverage is implementation evidence. Human blind playback is
preference/comprehension evidence. Production receipts are field evidence.
Payment, adoption, trust, and commercial value remain separate events.
