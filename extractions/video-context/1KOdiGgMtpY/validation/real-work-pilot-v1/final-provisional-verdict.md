## What The Companion Should Become

Signal Fidelity should operate as a two-surface companion:

- **Machine-facing execution capsule:** explicit, structured, exact, and cold;
  used only when an AI agent must preserve constraints across execution.
- **Human-facing native artifact:** conversational and owner-shaped; the capsule
  stays behind it and reveals only a material difference, limit, or open risk.

For content, it is an audit layer—not an author. For strategy, it may become
visible when a threshold, falsifier, scope boundary, or proof state would
otherwise disappear. For human handoffs, readability wins. For AI handoffs,
specificity wins.
