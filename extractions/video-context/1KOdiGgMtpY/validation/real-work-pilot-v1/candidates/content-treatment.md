A client read her work last month, looked up, and asked who actually wrote it.

The pause told him everything.

You are running two funnels now, and AI is doing the opposite thing to each.
Discovery is becoming machine-owned: a January-to-April 2026 5W study ran 3,800
supplement prompts across the major AI systems. Four brands took almost half the
citations; AG1, reportedly the largest greens brand by revenue, landed third.

But trust got expensive. Your buyer arrives after reading AI-written everything,
and polish without a person behind it now feels like absence.

Use AI to widen the top. Spend the saved time making the bottom unmistakably
human. The cheaper your content gets, the more your humanity is worth.

Run the two-number test this week: ask AI your ten category questions, then read
your last ten posts. How often is your brand found? How often could a stranger
prove a human made the work?

One number is too low. You know which one.
