A client read her work last month, looked up, and asked who actually wrote it.

The pause told him everything.

AI has split your funnel in two. At the top, machines increasingly decide which
brands enter the recommendation. A January-to-April 2026 5W study ran 3,800
supplement prompts across the major AI systems. Four brands took almost half the
citations; AG1, reportedly the largest greens brand by revenue, landed third.

At the bottom, the economics reverse. Your buyer has read AI-written everything.
Trust now belongs to the brand that still sounds inhabited.

Most founders answered cheaper content by making more. The better move is to let
AI widen discovery and spend the saved judgment where a person must believe you.

This week, ask AI your ten category questions. Then read your last ten posts and
count how many a stranger could prove a human made.

One number is too low. You know which one.
