# Signal Fidelity Real-Work Pilot V1 — Precommit

Status at creation: `PRECOMMITTED_NOT_RUN`.

This pilot compares strong native transformations with the same task performed
with the Signal Fidelity checksum available. It uses three existing, real-work
artifacts: a strategy map, a publishable content asset, and an agent handoff.
Their paths and SHA-256 hashes are frozen in `manifest.json`.

## Fairness Rules

1. The native arm receives the real source, task, audience, word cap, and native
   owner standard. It may not be weakened to manufacture a treatment win.
2. The treatment arm receives the same inputs plus the compact Signal Contract.
3. Neither arm may ask Farrice another question; all checksum fields must be
   recovered from existing artifacts or left uncertain.
4. Both arms remain local and unpublished. No live workflow, canonical state,
   route, hook, command, or proof state may change.
5. Candidate labels are deterministically scrubbed after production. The local
   scorer checks literal boundary coverage without using arm names.
6. Metaphor, ambiguity, voice, and creative equivalence are not machine-scored.
   They remain a separate human blind judgment.
7. Every loss, tie, false warning, proof change, and burden delta is retained.

## Local Measures

- literal must-survive coverage;
- proof-state preservation;
- word-count burden under the same cap;
- added user questions;
- false blocks and distortion warnings;
- material decision details recovered by treatment but absent from baseline;
- manual creative checks still requiring human judgment.

## Conclusion Ceiling

This local run can conclude only:

- `ADVANCE_TO_HUMAN_BLIND`;
- `REVISE_SHADOW`; or
- `RETIRE`.

It cannot conclude `KEEP`, `PROMOTE`, `ENFORCE`, `FIELD_VALIDATED`, or
`BLIND_PREFERRED` because the builder is not an independent recipient.

## Precommitted Thresholds

- Advance to human blind: at least two treatment coverage wins, no baseline
  wins, no proof inflation, no false blocks, no added user questions, and no
  detected narrowing of the content arm.
- Revise SHADOW: one bounded defect, one baseline win, avoidable warning burden,
  or a treatment that needs materially more words to preserve the signal.
- Retire: no treatment wins, repeated baseline superiority, proof inflation,
  owner displacement, mandatory-question burden, or creative-range reduction
  that cannot be fixed with one bounded repair.

The later human decision card adds the independent preference requirement for
any keep decision.
