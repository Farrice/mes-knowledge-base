## REMOVE

Verdict: `FAIL`.

- Strategy produced an independent treatment preference.
- The AI-agent interpretation of the handoff produced an independent treatment
  preference.
- Local controls found five additional decision details, zero proof inflation,
  zero false blocks, and zero added questions.
- The weakness is bounded to presentation and recipient fit; it does not require

## CHANGE SHADOW

Verdict: `PASS`.

The core hypothesis shows bounded value, but the current prototype exposes too
much explicit structure on human and creative surfaces. The precommitted repair
path applies because native owners already carry much of the signal and the
treatment adds avoidable words or duplicates reader-facing work.

## Required Bounded Repair

1. **AI execution capsule:** retain explicit recipient, intended change, source
   of conviction, promise, limit, proof state, human owner, and must-survive
   details for AI-agent handoffs.
2. **Human surface:** preserve the native conversational summary. Keep the
   capsule cold or collapsible and surface only a material delta or open risk.
3. **Creative content:** native content owner writes the artifact. Signal
   Fidelity may silently inspect literal claims, limits, proof labels, and
   omitted human-owned meaning; it may not rewrite or lengthen the public draft.
4. **Strategy:** allow explicit Signal Fidelity treatment only for selected
   consequential decisions where thresholds, falsifiers, scope, or proof state
   are likely to disappear in compression.
5. **Burden:** no mandatory fields or questions. Reuse existing context; if no
   material delta exists, produce no visible output.

## Replay Contract

After one bounded repair, replay only:

- the failed frozen content case;
- the handoff case in separate human and AI recipient modes;
- the frozen strategy case as the untouched positive control;
- the creative/manual and sparse-work negative controls.

Stop after that replay. No merge, promotion, enforcement, automatic activation,
new command, route, hook, skill, or global mirror is authorized.
