# Signal Fidelity — What Survived and What Got Parked

## Verdict

The original insight was good. The broad implementation was not.

Signal Fidelity is worth retaining only as a mostly invisible protection
against material loss during AI execution. It is not a new way to write, brief
humans, explain strategy, or annotate public content.

The minimal candidate is validated on `codex/signal-fidelity-minimal`. It may
merge only through the clean-main lane gate; otherwise it remains safely parked
for integration after the active main writers finish.

## What Each Person Sees

| Recipient | Final experience |
|---|---|
| Public reader | The original content. Nothing else. |
| Human collaborator | A natural, readable handoff. No receipt. |
| AI agent | A compact execution capsule: goal, must survive, constraints, proof state, owner, and stop condition. |
| Farrice on a consequential strategy | If needed, three lines only: decision, must not lose, proof boundary. Otherwise the native memo. |

## What Was Wrong

The prototype made its internal accounting visible. That increased field
recoverability but often reduced human clarity and made the work feel AI-shaped.
The mechanism began competing with the thing it was supposed to protect.

## What Changed

- The broad `codex/signal-fidelity-shadow` branch is parked and will not merge.
- The evaluator, universal receipts, public audit explanation, and visible human
  capsule are not part of the final candidate.
- The source insight is preserved in a compact evidence note.
- Only two existing owners receive pointers: Skill System for recipient-native
  handoffs and Agentic Engineering for AI execution boundaries.
- No command, router, hook, skill, score, automatic activation, or new question
  surface was added.

## Evidence State

- Source insight: `VERIFIED` against the harvested transcript timestamps.
- AI-agent handoff usefulness: `SUPPORTED` by the blind choice and Farrice's
  qualitative verdict.
- Natural human handoff: `SUPPORTED`.
- Public invisibility: `SUPPORTED`; the public artifact remains unchanged.
- Three-line strategy spine: `BOUNDED / OPTIONAL`; useful only when it preserves
  a consequential boundary.
- Trust, adoption, creative improvement, and commercial effect: `UNTESTED`.

## Final Boundary

If the layer is noticeable during ordinary work, it is probably doing too much.
Its highest expression is not a better receipt. It is the absence of a preventable
loss.
