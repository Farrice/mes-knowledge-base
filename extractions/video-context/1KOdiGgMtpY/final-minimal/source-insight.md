## What The Source Supplied

The source supplied a strong diagnosis: as AI makes execution abundant, the
scarce value moves upstream to human judgment and downstream to whether that
judgment survives transformation.

## What Antigravity Inferred

“Signal Fidelity” is Antigravity's implementation inference, not Lena Hall's
named product or proven architecture. The strongest inference is now narrow:

- make AI execution boundaries explicit when loss would be consequential;
- preserve the promise with its limit and proof state;
- test what a recipient can recover;
- keep the mechanism silent when the native owner already communicates well.

## What The Pilot Rejected

- one universal visible receipt for every recipient;
- framework language inside public or human-native work;
- deterministic field coverage treated as proof of usefulness;
- a new composition, strategy, proof, or routing authority;
- ceremony added merely to demonstrate that the layer ran.

The extraction remains valuable. The broad presentation architecture did not.
