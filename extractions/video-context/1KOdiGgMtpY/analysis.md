# Source-to-System Analysis — Signal Fidelity

## Source Class

Lena Hall's conference talk is a primary practitioner source for a thin signal-preservation function. The source supports an advisory companion layer more strongly than a new expert persona, command, or large workflow system.

## Verified Mechanics

1. **Define before emitting (02:28–03:58).** The human must identify the specific point of view, problem choice, and reason the work is not average before AI performs convergent execution.
2. **Preserve domain proximity (07:03–09:43).** Future-facing judgment and relationship-specific context are scarce inputs. AI should not silently invent them from common training data.
3. **Check three distortion boundaries (12:22–15:29).** Source compression, organizational handoffs, and machine remixing fail differently and need separate diagnosis.
4. **Weld promise to scope (16:05–16:53).** A distinctive claim becomes unsafe when its limit can be removed without changing its apparent meaning.
5. **Use recipient playback (16:55–17:21).** The operative evidence is the difference between the intended meaning and what an unfamiliar recipient describes back.
6. **Optimize for trust, not a universal score (17:42–19:19).** Trust is relational and cannot be fully automated or reduced to one reward signal.

## Build-Shape Decision

Build a `SHADOW` companion primitive, an optional deterministic advisory evaluator, narrow references from three existing owners, proof fixtures, and a verifier. Do not create a command, skill, agent, router entry, hook, mandatory field, global mirror, or dependency.

## Limits Carried Forward

- A deterministic marker check cannot judge semantic equivalence, truth, taste, trust, or market value.
- Original intent can improve during collaboration. The human owner may approve a deliberate change rather than preserve a weak starting point.
- A warning is evidence for human review, not evidence that distortion occurred.
- Production value remains `UNTESTED` until blinded recipient comparisons and real task receipts exist.
