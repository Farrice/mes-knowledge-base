## Core judgment

Dominik's distinctive contribution is not a new Reel trick. It is a debugging model: read the Instagram account backwards from revenue, assign one job to each layer, and refuse to prescribe more reach when the upstream buying system is broken.

The tacit move is asymmetrical sequencing. The audit can inspect `Offer → Attraction → Profile → Trust → Offer visibility` to locate the leak, while the repair queue stays `Offer → Trust → Profile → Attraction`. That distinction prevents a viral-content prescription from hiding an offer or trust failure.

## Twelve operational patterns

| Pattern | Why it works | Deploy | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reverse-funnel debugging | Revenue is downstream of every visible metric. | Start at offer coherence, not content volume. | Every recommendation names the layer and its downstream handoff. |
| One layer, one job | It prevents mixed objectives and vanity-metric confusion. | Offer=desire; Trust=skepticism reduction; Profile=binge; Attraction=qualified reach. | No tactic is justified by two conflicting jobs. |
| Inspect order vs repair order | Diagnosis needs telemetry; repair needs dependencies. | Inspect offer, attraction, profile, trust, visibility; repair offer, trust, profile, attraction. | Downstream work is blocked only by a real upstream failure. |
| Non-follower reach as attraction evidence | It separates distribution from follower familiarity. | Compare non-follower share with the account's own history and targets. | `UNVERIFIED` when the metric is absent; no guessed reach diagnosis. |
| Two-to-five-second profile proxy | Cold visitors decide quickly whether the grid is for them. | Test name, bio, covers, highlights, pins, proof, and link as one scan. | A cold reader can state who, outcome, proof, and next step. |
| Trust is embodied evidence | Voiceless, generic content transfers little personhood or judgment. | Add voice, face, opinion, gap, proof, and format variety where authentic. | Content contains a sourced opinion or lived proof, not synthetic personality. |
| Hook handoff | Clarity earns orientation; curiosity earns the next seconds. | 0.5s visual clarity → three-second curiosity → delivered promise. | Hook, body, and CTA address the same person and problem. |
| Visual comprehension before personality | A cold viewer needs the topic before the creator's identity. | Lead with legible B-roll/context, then reveal face where useful. | First frame remains understandable with audio muted. |
| Beginner volume as learning throughput | More controlled attempts create a faster evidence loop. | Increase repetitions only within sustainable capacity and one-variable tests. | More usable experiments, not merely more posts. |
| Repurpose by changing trust surface | A repost is stronger when the creator adds voice/face and removes platform residue. | Voiceover + face + Trial Reel + feed version; no watermark. | Two native variants with traceable source ownership. |
| Gap matrix | Personality and production are separable axes. | Map competitors, then choose an evidence-backed empty or under-served quadrant. | The gap is supported by observed competitors, not novelty theater. |
| One-link monetization | It removes competing exits and makes attribution legible. | One primary destination; calculator only when inputs and claims are honest. | Each CTA maps to one offer, event, and revenue outcome. |

## Surpass path

AI can exceed a live teardown on consistency, coverage, and replayability—not on access it does not have. The durable advantage is a ten-component system that:

1. ingests the same evidence packet every time;
2. separates observed facts, source-reported claims, inference, and missing data;
3. runs compliance before restricted-category generation;
4. scores all ten layers/components with explicit evidence;
5. generates testable alternatives without pretending to predict conversion;
6. measures funnel ratios and attributable revenue over time; and
7. preserves human approval for voice cloning, publishing, DMs, and external system writes.
