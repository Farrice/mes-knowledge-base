## Step order

1. Capture a real resolved case.
2. Verify facts, result, permission, privacy, brokerage, and disclosure safety.
3. Select one competence moment.
4. Decide narrative dosage.
5. Route the lightest credible format.
6. Build the source-first spine and attach proof.
7. Choose trust compounding or demand capture.
8. Hold the human voice/compliance checkpoint.
9. Hand approved real assets to production.
10. Attribute conversations and business outcomes.
11. Revise from first-party evidence.

## Handoff summary

| From | To | Required payload | Rejection condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agent/team | Enrico | Case Receipt and proof | Central facts or permission absent |
| Enrico | Shaan dosage | Fact-traced candidate | Full story requires invention |
| Enrico | Social production | Approved packet, real assets, redactions, voice | Synthetic proof or unsafe disclosure |
| Production | Human agent | Draft plus claim/compliance receipt | Voice or fact mismatch |
| Human agent | CRM/learning | Identified response and source note | Attention-only event |

## Human checkpoint

The approved owner confirms: I would say this; the interpretation is mine; facts/results are correct; client, brokerage, privacy, and disclosure boundaries are cleared; the piece does not steer by protected identity; and a human owns replies. Until then: `DRAFT / NOT READY TO PUBLISH`.
