## Mastery patterns

1. **Casework is the moat.** A resolved transaction contains a proprietary combination of stakes, constraint, judgment, action, and result.
2. **The competence moment is atomic.** One professional decision can carry a Reel without forcing a dramatic arc.
3. **Specificity carries credibility.** Dates, amounts, durations, document locations, showing response, and comparisons make work legible when verified and safe.
4. **Format follows evidence density.** One decision fits video; rich chronology fits a caption; several receipts fit a carousel; safe real-time stakes fit a bounded series and recap.
5. **Visual repetition improves scalability.** One photograph can remain while short text advances the case.
6. **Proof sits beside the claim.** Comparisons, response, timing, and outcome appear at the beat they support.
7. **Relatability is self-recognition.** The viewer recognizes a constraint—stale listing, distance, deadline, failed offer—not a demographic caricature.
8. **Safety changes the publishing clock.** Capture important details when they happen; publish only after the case is safe.
9. **Scale the chassis, not the persona.** Standardize receipts, proof types, slide functions, redactions, and CRM fields.
10. **Content has two business clocks.** Demand capture can be immediate; proof-story recall may appear weeks later.
11. **Substance sets the ceiling.** Content reveals competent work; it cannot manufacture it.
12. **Resolved failure can outperform an easy win.** Diagnosis and recovery make professional judgment visible.

## Relatability system

| Constraint | Prospect recognition | Agent proof opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Listing is stale | What would another agent actually change? | Diagnosis and repositioning |
| Seller is remote | Can someone manage this without me present? | Coordination and risk control |
| Deadline is tight | Is there a path that protects time and proceeds? | Option design and execution |
| First offer fails | What happens after the apparent win collapses? | Recovery and relaunch |
| Property is hard to imagine | Who is this useful for? | Lifestyle/use-case translation |

## Scalability system

Standardize the Case Receipt, evidence labels, permission gate, proof taxonomy, narrative dosage, format router, slide functions, production handoff, and attribution fields. Keep case selection, voice, permission, redaction, delivery, and human replies human-owned. Never scale fabricated wins, copied persona, active negotiation disclosure, forced daily volume, or engagement-as-lead reporting.

## Story-to-lead system

```text
trust path: proof story → later recall → identified conversation → CRM source note
demand path: current listing/resource → explicit value CTA → routing question → human reply → CRM next action
```

| Stage | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Attention | Reach, hold/watch behavior, saves, shares, profile activity |
| Story-attributed conversation | Identifiable person, two-way exchange, and referenced post/first touch |
| Appointment | CRM event and service lane |

| Sourced closing | Closed transaction linked to the original conversation |

## 30-day mastery progression

- **Week 1:** inventory ten resolved transactions, complete five receipts, gather proof and permission, reject at least one unsafe case.
- **Week 2:** build one competence-moment Reel packet and one proof carousel; record production time and gaps.
- **Week 3:** subject to approval, publish no more than two; log identified conversations and story source.
- **Week 4:** compare burden, qualified responses, and recall; keep or revise formats from first-party evidence; do not infer stable closing lift.

## Weakest assumptions

- Source-reported production growth may have multiple causes.
- Visible engagement does not prove lead quality.
- Instagram format performance changes; the interview's algorithm comments are not platform law.
- Jen's audience may respond differently.
- A 30-day pilot can reveal pickup and conversation quality, not stable closing lift.

## Final standard

The system succeeds when a stranger can see what the agent noticed, what they changed, what evidence supports the result, and how to start a relevant human conversation. It fails when storytelling becomes decoration around an unsupported claim.
