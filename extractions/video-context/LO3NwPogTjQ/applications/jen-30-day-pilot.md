# Jen Santulan: 30-Day Proof-Story Pilot

## Locked architecture

- Jen remains the umbrella.
- First Home Valley remains the education lane.
- Listing Launch remains the property-proof lane.
- Personal/lifestyle content remains the affinity engine.
- No rebrand, daily-posting mandate, separate creator persona, or new automation.

Proof stories bridge Jen's human affinity to visible competence. They do not replace the existing system.

## Pilot question

Can two sustainable, evidence-rich transaction stories per month create identifiable qualified conversations or stronger story recall without harming Jen's natural voice or expanding the posting burden?

## Week 1 — Case inventory and permission

Mine ten resolved transactions from the last 18 months. Score each 0–2 on relevance to a Valley question, competence moment, available proof, permission/disclosure safety, and fit with Jen's calm-warm voice. Complete Case Receipts for the top three. Reject or hold at least one case so the safety gate is proven.

## Week 2 — Build two packets

### Packet A: human competence moment

- Natural talking-head Reel or single image plus caption.
- Make one decision or protection moment visible.
- Conversational, calm, specific; no inflated hero framing.
- Trust-compounding lane.

### Packet B: property proof

- Simple proof carousel under Listing Launch.
- Show situation, diagnosis, actions, receipts, and supported result.
- Use real property media and redacted proof.
- Demand capture only if a current property or useful deliverable exists.

Both remain drafts until Jen confirms the voice, interpretation, facts, disclosure safety, fair-housing boundary, and response owner.

## Week 3 — Bounded publication test

Subject to approval, publish a maximum of two pieces. The proof story replaces the week's real-estate post rather than adding a third. Use an authentic relevance bridge. Avoid a keyword CTA unless a real listing/resource is being delivered and the response flow is ready.

## Week 4 — Evidence review

| Attention ledger | Pipeline ledger |
|---|---|
| Reach, watch/hold behavior, saves, shares, profile activity, relevant replies | Identified two-way story conversations, service lane, appointment set/held, signed business, later contract/closing link |

- `KEEP`: sustainable and creates qualified recognition or conversations.
- `CHANGE`: case is strong but hook, format, proof order, or response path is weak.
- `STOP`: voice strain, disclosure risk, production burden, or no useful signal after a bounded sample.

Do not claim stable closing lift from 30 days.

## Jen Case Receipt questions

1. Which resolved transaction made you think the client may never realize how much one decision mattered?
2. What was the client's actual constraint, in language you can safely share?
3. What did you notice that changed your recommendation?
4. What did you do next, and what proof still exists?
5. What result can be verified?
6. What must stay private or wait?
7. Who in the Valley would recognize this situation?
8. Would Jen rather speak this moment, write it, or show it through screenshots?

## No-event boundary

Nothing in this pilot has been published. Jen-specific performance is `UNTESTED / NO EVENT`. The first proof gate is three completed Case Receipts, two human-approved packets, and a clean permission/compliance review—not more ideas.
