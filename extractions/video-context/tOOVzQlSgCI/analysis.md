# Analysis: Creator Niche Discovery

## Core Method

Cole turns niche selection from an identity decision into a learning-and-evidence loop. The creator chooses a workable umbrella, publishes enough volume to create a baseline, detects relative winners, and expands them through adjacent audiences, problems, and outcomes.

## Decision Sequence

1. Remove three false premises: permanence, self-as-niche, and opportunity loss.
2. Find the overlap between sustained creator interest and audience questions/problems/outcomes.
3. Lead with a comprehensible tip of the spear.
4. Publish 30 pieces in 30 days.
5. Treat consistent relative lift, even only about 2x, as an early signal.
6. Repeat winners across audience/problem/outcome variations.
7. Turn the winner into an umbrella plus a content or client constellation.
8. Diagnose supporting skill gaps before blaming the niche.

## What This Adds To The Existing Skill

The prior Nicolas Cole niche skill starts with person-level specificity. This source adds a different discovery path: beginners can create evidence through publishing before pretending they already know the perfect person. Specificity remains useful downstream for communication and packaging.

## Extraction Inference

The 30-day loop is a safe-to-fail niche probe, not market validation. Distribution is evidence of resonance; it is not evidence of willingness to pay. Commercial confidence still requires buyer behavior.
