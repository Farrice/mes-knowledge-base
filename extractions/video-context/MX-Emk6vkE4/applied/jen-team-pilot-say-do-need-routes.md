## Gap diagnosis

The operator can deliver a concrete one-listing package, and one practitioner has shown a preference for specific, authority-led creative. The three target agents have said and done nothing yet, so the safest wrapper sells the package itself and asks for one observable next action.

## Route 1: practical value

**Promise:** One active listing becomes a complete, ready-to-film package within 48 hours.

**Draft:**

> Hey [name], I build Jen's listing-content packages: the shoot list, 3 scripts, captions, carousel, stories, and the fair-housing don't-say list. I'm opening 3 founding spots at $200. Send me one active listing and I'll turn it into the complete film-and-post kit within 48 hours. Want to see the sample?

**Integrity note:** Every named component exists in the sample and delivery process. The message makes no performance claim.

**Risk:** “I build Jen's listing-content packages” requires Jen's permission before it is used with her teammates.

## Route 2: identity led

**Promise:** The package is built from the agent's listing facts and existing voice rather than a generic content template.

**Draft:**

> Hey [name], your listing, your voice, one package you can film from your phone. I use your last 15 posts and verified listing facts to build the shot plan, 3 reels, captions, carousel, stories, and compliance notes. The first 3 kits are $200, delivered within 48 hours. Want to see the sample?

**Integrity note:** The intake and delivery process include a 15-post voice scrape, listing-fact verification, and per-agent brand card.

**Risk:** Whether agents value voice matching enough to buy is **UNTESTED**.

## Route 3: bounded negative

**Promise:** This is a one-listing deliverable with a clear division of labor, not an open-ended content service.

**Draft:**

> Hey [name], one listing, one finished film-and-post package. No monthly retainer and no posting handoff: you film and post; I supply the shot plan, 3 scripts, captions, carousel, stories, and fair-housing screen. The founding rate is $200 and delivery is 48 hours. Want the sample?

**Integrity note:** The scope matches the offer brief and avoids implying account management or video production.

**Risk:** Whether the absence of a retainer is attractive is **UNTESTED**. This route tests scope clarity, not a known objection.

## Selected route

Use **Route 1: practical value** first, after Jen grants permission. It contains the fewest buyer assumptions and gives the agent a simple decision: request the sample or do nothing.

Route 2 is the second test if a real agent mentions generic or off-voice content. Route 3 should be used only when a real conversation raises scope, posting, or retainer concerns.
