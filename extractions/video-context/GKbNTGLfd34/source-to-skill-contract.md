## Build-shape decision

This is an additive workflow and deterministic runtime inside the existing Diandra system. It is not a new expert, a new mega-skill, or a replacement for the current content-production line.

## Evidence limits

- The video and guide attribute the 1K-to-840K result to Ben Meer; this build did not independently reproduce that outcome.
- “Viral potential,” algorithm effects, and relative signal strength are planning hypotheses until account data confirms them.
- The operating system is verified locally. Live distribution, follower growth, leads, and revenue remain `NO EVENT` until the account publishes and records results.
