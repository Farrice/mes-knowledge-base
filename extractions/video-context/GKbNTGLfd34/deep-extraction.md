# Ben Meer and HubSpot LinkedIn growth extraction

## Source boundary

The source set contains the 13:34 HubSpot Marketing video, its auto-generated English transcript, 107 reviewed frames, the complete HubSpot seven-part guide, and the linked 13-slide hook deck. Spoken and visual evidence is recorded in `video-context-ledger.*`; uncertainty is recorded separately.

## Seven operating mechanics

1. **Creator endurance**: use a fixed production commitment and judge controllable inputs before judging follower outcomes.
2. **Smart Post queue**: score each idea from 1-5 for attention potential and 1-5 for speed to create; work highest totals first. The guide rejects totals below 6 by default.
3. **Dopamine density**: make each post surprising or actionable using a supported device such as a script, named method, useful resource, true contrarian position, or intentional story.
4. **Two-line hook**: draft multiple first-window options after the body exists. The linked deck contributes ten source-specific hook families.
5. **Peer feedback**: keep a small group of same-stage creators for weekly learning and draft feedback. Do not coordinate fake engagement, require reactions, or automate a pod.
6. **Profile conversion**: align photo, banner, headline, About, Featured, Experience, and keywords around what the account does, who it helps, and the outcome.
7. **Analytics loop**: record impressions, saves, reposts, follower growth, and business outcomes; inspect the top patterns after 10 posts and perform a full top-10 review after every 100.

## Ten hook families from the HubSpot deck

1. How to `[X]`: `[action] [Y]`
2. It took me `[X years]` to learn this: `[truth]`
3. Give me `[X minutes]`, and I’ll teach you `[skill]`
4. `[Number]` free `[items]` to `[benefit]`
5. I’ll let you in on a secret: `[counterintuitive truth]`
6. `[Number]` ways to `[result]` in `[timeframe]`
7. Most people `[common behavior]`; the top `[X%]` `[contrasting behavior]`
8. `[Impressive result]`; the exact `[X]-step system`
9. If I had to start `[goal]` from zero, I’d do these `[X]` things
10. The `[X]-second rule that changed how I `[area]`

These are idea frames, not permission to fabricate results, secrets, elite percentages, timelines, or first-person experience.
