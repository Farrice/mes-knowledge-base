## Ben Meer's 7-Part LinkedIn Growth System

Part One

## Adopt the Creator Mindset

Before we get into specific tools and tactics, we need to lay the right foundation.

What separates creators who flame out from those who last and leave an impact isn't talent or luck. It's the mindset they build to stay in the game through the highs and lows.

### 5 Keys to Install the Creator Mindset

### Understand the Ramp-Up Period

Most creators quit before they’ve even given themselves a chance.

The beginning stages feel slow because you’re laying foundations you can’t see yet: learning the craft and figuring out your voice.

Remember the Bamboo Rule : Bamboo spends years growing its roots under the soil before you see a single shoot. But once it breaks the surface, it can grow three feet in a single day.

### Expect Imposter Syndrome (and Keep Going)

Here’s a secret: every creator you admire still feels like an imposter sometimes (even me sometimes). But imposter syndrome is rarely a sign to stop—it’s usually proof you’re growing by stepping outside your comfort zone.

Instead of asking, “Am I qualified to post this?” ask, “Could this help one person?”.

### Apply the 10% Hater Rule

You could post the most wholesome piece of content on the internet, and someone will still find a way to leave a negative comment.

Tim Ferris has dubbed it the “10% Hater Rule:” ~10% of people will dislike anything you do. Prepare for it and treat it like math

Make sure to never feed the trolls. If feedback is valid, leave a thoughtful response. If it’s just noise, ignore it and move on.

### Don’t Sell Your Soul For Engagement

There's nothing wrong with following trends, but when your entire strategy revolves around chasing virality, your content loses its soul, and your audience can feel it.

The accounts that last aren't built on engagement shortcuts; they're built on authenticity, real stories, and a distinct creative voice that people recognize and return to.

Learn to turn your experiences into lessons, share your losses as freely as your wins, and develop a calling card that makes your content unmistakably yours, because trust compounds in ways that viral hacks never will.

### Focus on Inputs, Not Outcomes

If your mood rises and falls with your follower/like/impression count, you’ve handed control of your happiness to an app.

Focus less on what you can’t control (the outcomes): vanity metrics and followers

Focus more on what you can control (the inputs): writing good posts, building a strong network, and continuously refining your craft.

Part Two

## Be Efficient with Your Posts

Time is always a constraint to great content creation, but there is a solution. I built my first one million followers across platforms while holding down a full-time job. I couldn't afford to waste my limited creator hours on the wrong ideas.

So I built this Smart Post Algorithm that anyone can use:

### Score Every Idea on 2 Criteria

1. Viral Potential: How shareable is this idea? Does it educate, inspire, or entertain? 5 = high potential, 1 = low potential.

2. Time to Create: How long will this take to execute? 5 = 15 minutes or less, 1 = 2+ hours.

### How to Prioritize

Score Combo

What to Do

High Viral + Fast to Create

Write this FIRST. Your highest-leverage content.

High Viral + Slow to Create

Schedule and batch these. Worth the effort.

Low Viral + Fast to Create

Only if you have nothing better queued up.

Low Viral + Slow to Create

Skip. This kills your momentum and your time.

### How to Apply It (Step-by-Step)

- Set aside 1 hour every two weeks for brainstorming new ideas.

- List 20 ideas in your preferred system (Sheets, Notion, etc.).

- Score each idea 1-5 on Viral Potential and Time to Create.

- Add the two scores. Sort your list from highest to lowest.

- Your top-scoring idea = what you write next. Simple.

This system is more art than science, but it's directionally accurate. The real win is what it prevents: spending 3 hours on a post that gets low engagement. Time is your most valuable asset.

## Publish Dopamine-Dense Content

Let’s be real: not all viral content is created equal. There’s the shallow kind like clickbait and outrage.

Then there’s the other kind: the content that pulls you in because it’s useful and easy to remember. The kind that leaves a little mark on your brain that makes you say, “I’ve gotta save this.”

That’s what we’re aiming for. Not just engagement, but dopamine-dense content.

Dopamine density = how much psychological reward a piece of content delivers per unit of time.

LinkedIn rewards what people can't stop reading and sharing. Your goal is to pack as much value and usefulness into every post as possible.

## Write Viral Hooks

On LinkedIn, the first two lines of a post determine if a reader keeps scrolling or stops to read.

The harsh truth: you can be the smartest person in the world, but if your hooks are weak, your content will never get seen. A strong hook can 10x-50x the virality of a post, everything else being equal. It is the single highest-leverage skill in all of content creation.

One of the best ways to get better at writing hooks is to study the ones that already work. The ones that stop you mid-scroll and make you want to keep reading.

That’s why I put together this collection:

### 6 Proven Hook Frameworks

1. "How to [x]. [Action] [y]:"

e.g., "How to avoid burnout. Get 7 types of rest:"

2. "It took me [X years] to learn this: [truth]"

e.g., "It took me 5 years to learn this: working harder is not the same as making progress."

3. "Give me 2 minutes, and I'll teach you [important skill]:"

e.g., "Give me 2 minutes, and I'll teach you 6 rules to be charismatic."

4. "[Number] free [important item] to [desirable benefit]:"

e.g., "9 free design tools to make your presentations beautiful."

5. "I'll let you in on a secret: [counterintuitive truth]"

e.g., "I'll let you in on a secret: consistency matters more than motivation."

6. "[Number] ways to [result] in [timeframe]:"

e.g., "9 tips to turn your LinkedIn profile into a job-generating machine."

Part Five

## Form Synergy Buddies

One of the most underrated pieces of advice I got when I first started writing online? Don't lone-wolf it. Build a group of 3-5 creator friends who actively support each other. A group like this can be an invaluable resource for:

• Getting feedback on your posts

• Discussing content strategies

• Collecting testimonials for your products/services

• Keeping up to date with the latest trends/algorithm changes

### How to Set Up Your Synergy Group

- Find 3-5 creators in your niche

- Create a dedicated group chat

- Share learnings weekly

- Celebrate wins together

Look for people at a similar stage (within 2-3x your follower count). Different enough that your audiences don't fully overlap, similar enough that they understand your content.

WhatsApp, Slack, or any other preferred messaging app work. The key: everyone is reachable.

What hooks performed? What format went viral? What flopped? Speed of learning is your competitive advantage.

Accountability and positive reinforcement create consistency. Consistency creates compounding growth.

### Where to Find Your Synergy Buddies

- LinkedIn comments sections of top creators in your niche

- Creator communities and cohort-based courses

- Twitter/X—search your niche, find creators at your size

- In-person events, conferences, and mastermind groups

## Build a Compelling Profile

Your profile is your first impression and your best-converting sales page. Whether you're trying to convert followers or new clients for your business, having an optimized LinkedIn profile is crucial.

Every post you publish drives people to your profile, and it's your profile's job to turn them into followers or customers.

## The 6 Profile Elements to Optimize

### Available

- Profile photo

- Banner image

- Headline

- About section

- Featured section

- Experience and skills

1. Profile Photo : First impressions happen in milliseconds. Invest in a quality headshot. Clear background, good lighting, professional but approachable.

2. Banner Image : Use this real estate to communicate your value proposition in one line. e.g., 'I write about systems, productivity & wealth. Every Sunday.' Use Canva to design it.

3. Headline : Don't just list your job title. State what you do + who you help + the outcome. Weak: 'Marketing Manager at XYZ Corp.' Strong: 'I help B2B founders grow on LinkedIn.'

4. About Section : Use this storytelling framework:

- Before state: Highlight some pain points that you (or a client of yours) were going through

- Epiphany: Talk about your “aha!” moment. Something you learned that changed everything.

- After state: Highlight the results that have come after the “aha moment”. Try to be as specific as possible here.

- Plug services: Sell the before/after you just outlined to your reader. Tell them how you can help them achieve the same results.

5. Featured Section : This is where you convert profile visitors into followers and customers. Create Featured cards linking to your best lead magnet, newsletter, or top post.

6. Experience & Skills: Keyword-optimize your experience descriptions. LinkedIn search rewards profiles that include the words your ideal audience is already searching for.

## Tap into Analytics

Analytics matter. Plain and simple. When leveraged correctly, they can unlock extraordinary growth for you on any social platform (especially LinkedIn). And while I urge you not to get too caught up in the numbers, you’d be selling yourself short by not tracking a few key metrics on the platform.

Before I tell you the metrics to track, I want to share a few main reasons why you should track:

### Determining which content to post again

Reusing high-performing content is essential to sustained growth on social media. Keeping track of your numbers can give you these insights automatically.

### Identifying which post types are performing best

Tracking analytics is great for identifying trends in post performance, which will help you determine which content type to use most often (Text only, Single Image, Carousel, Video).

## The Key Metrics to Track on LinkedIn

Reposts : LinkedIn's key signal that a post is going to "take off." When someone reposts, you've struck such a chord that they feel the overwhelming urge to share it. This is your strongest engagement signal.

Impressions : How many times your post was shown. Use this as a baseline for distribution and to spot when the algorithm is amplifying your content.

Saves : One of the most powerful engagement signals. LinkedIn boosts posts with high saves. If people are saving it, it means it's highly valuable to them.

Follower Growth : Track weekly. Spikes in follower growth tell you exactly which content types are attracting new people to your profile.

### Recommended Tools

### LinkedIn Native Analytics

Free. Solid for impressions, follower growth, and post engagement basics.

### HubSpot CRM

Track where your LinkedIn audience goes after they click your links. Connect your content to your pipeline and measure real business impact.
