## Minimum Capture Sequence For The First Paid Kit

1. **Before payment:** record the prospect, listing, baseline content, current
   workflow, current proof permission, and the exact problem in the agent's own
   words.
2. **At payment:** save the redacted receipt, price, date, scope, and promised
   delivery window.
3. **At delivery:** freeze the package manifest, delivery time, production time,
   compliance result, and any client-requested changes.
4. **At use:** record which exact components were filmed, posted, or declined.
5. **At 24 hours and 7 days:** capture descriptive platform metrics and keyword
   conversations without claiming causality.
6. **At Day 7:** request one specific feedback sentence and permission choices;
   do not draft the testimonial as if it were the agent's words.
7. **At the second buying decision:** record repeat purchase, referral, team
   interest, or explicit non-repeat. This is stronger commercial proof than a
   compliment.
