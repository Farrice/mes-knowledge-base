# Jun Story Engine — Behavior Proof

## Proof boundary

These are constructed fixtures for deterministic behavior testing. They are not Jun Yuh examples, client results, audience tests, or revenue proof. The source-derived mechanics are verified against the transcript package; downstream effectiveness remains `UNTESTED` until used on real work.

## Positive control: ordinary expertise becomes story material

### Baseline input

“Claim-safe content matters for health and performance brands.”

On its own, this is a teaching claim. It contains no lived movement and does not justify a story.

### Constructed supplied material

- A consultant posted broad wellness advice daily.
- Funded health and performance brands could not safely reuse it.
- He began attaching one named evidence source, one claim boundary, and one buyer-use case to every idea.
- His posts became demonstrations of a claim-safe content method.

### Story Material Packet result

- `LIFE`: Earnings
- `SAFE`: the posts were broad and hard for a buyer to reuse.
- `REAL`: the activity created visibility without demonstrating the service.
- `RAW`: posting more was not the same as becoming buyable.
- `PROBLEM`: generic advice hid the method.
- `PURSUIT`: source + claim boundary + buyer-use-case became the working system.
- `PAYOFF`: the content could now function as proof of method.
- `NARRATIVE STATUS`: `FULL STORY CANDIDATE`

### Changed behavior

Before this expansion, the system could structure a supplied 3P origin story but had no explicit route for recovering story material from an ordinary moment. After the expansion, it can trace the moment through LIFE and Safe/Real/Raw, refuse unsupported depth, preserve Pursuit as the method, and hand the supported packet to one deployment owner.

## Positive control: unresolved journey remains honest

A creator tracks which topics she would discuss without an audience and pauses the rest. She has not found the final format. The supported Payoff is a changed diagnosis—topic ownership rather than discipline—not a fabricated success state. Result: `FULL STORY CANDIDATE` with an unresolved Payoff.

## Privacy control

A private family event changes how a teller organizes work. The triggering event and other person remain `WITHHELD`; only the teller's supplied boundary and changed behavior are available. Result: `STORY FRAGMENT CANDIDATE`. The system preserves teller-owned evolution without exposing or inferring third-party detail.

## Negative control: incident status stays direct

Input: “The publishing queue stopped after a credential expired. The credential was restored and the failed item replayed.”

Result: `NO STORY`. The system returns the incident spine—cause, repair, current state, remaining risk—without adding a protagonist, dramatic scene, emotional stakes, or transformation.

## Negative control: missing Pursuit blocks a full story

Input supplies a generic Problem and a later improvement but no action, experiment, decision, or causal path between them. Result: `NEEDS SOURCE`. The smallest missing fact is requested; the system does not infer the method.
