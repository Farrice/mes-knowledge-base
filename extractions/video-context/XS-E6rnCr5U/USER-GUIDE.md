# Jun Story Engine — Operator Guide

## What it does

`/jun-story-engine` turns supplied lived material into the smallest truthful story shape that can help the work. It can mine an ordinary moment, protect sensitive details, structure a supported Problem/Pursuit/Payoff candidate, and route the result to one social or cross-domain owner.

It may return `FULL STORY`, `STORY FRAGMENT`, `NO STORY`, or `NEEDS SOURCE`. A refusal to force a story is successful behavior.

When two dated artifacts prove a changed position but not the turning event, it returns `DOCUMENTED CONTRAST` and caps the result at a fragment. For commercial work, it also names the highest verified proof level: `EXPERIENCE`, `METHOD`, `DELIVERABLE`, or `MARKET`.

## Default invocation

```text
/jun-story-engine
Objective: [what the audience should understand, feel, decide, remember, or do]
Lived material: [facts, notes, memory, draft, transcript, or authorized source path]
Share boundary: [what may not be used]
Domain/truth risk: [personal | ordinary real-world | health/finance/legal/safety/research]
Destination: [social | offer | presentation | email | article | education | client asset | unknown]
Voice owner: [person or brand]
```

## Use by destination

| Need | What to supply | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Social post, reel, carousel, or short video | One lived moment, mission, assets, privacy boundary, voice owner | Jun mines and owns the social body |
| Offer or sales asset | Founder/customer facts, proof boundary, buyer outcome, prohibited claims | Jun mines; Shaan decides dosage and one body owner |
| Presentation or keynote | Real moment, audience, decision or teaching objective, stage constraints | Jun mines; Shaan routes narrative architecture |
| Email or article | Source material, relationship to reader, purpose, voice samples | Jun mines; Shaan selects story depth and writing owner |
| Health or performance education | Evidence spine, claim boundary, audience, any verified lived moment | Usually story fragment or no story; evidence remains primary |
| Incident, status, procedure, or specification | Direct facts, current state, next action | `NO STORY`; communicate directly |

## Fast material-mining prompt

```text
Use Story Material Miner only. Read these supplied notes as an interviewer, not a biographer. Return a fact-traced LIFE + Safe/Real/Raw + 3P packet. Mark missing beats [NEEDS SOURCE], protect the share boundary, and let NO STORY CANDIDATE win.
```

## Documented-contrast prompt

```text
Run /jun-story-engine on these two dated artifacts. Treat them as DOCUMENTED CONTRAST, not proof of the conversion scene between them. Keep the interval visible, mark the turn [NEEDS SOURCE], and produce the strongest truthful fragment the evidence supports.
```

## Commercial proof prompt

```text
Build the asset, then state its commercial proof ceiling: EXPERIENCE, METHOD, DELIVERABLE, or MARKET. Do not let a sample deliverable imply demand, results, sales, or revenue. If no verified buyer event exists, write MARKET PROOF: NO EVENT.
```

## Privacy-safe prompt

```text
Mine the teller's own evolution while withholding the triggering event and all third-party identity, motive, and private detail. Use WITHHELD where needed. Do not infer emotion or transformation. Return the smallest useful story fragment and its open risk.
```

## Cross-domain prompt

```text
Run /jun-story-engine on the supplied lived material. Jun should return the fact-traced Story Material Packet; then /shaan-story-deploy should decide FULL STORY, STORY FRAGMENT, or NO STORY and assign exactly one body owner for [destination]. Include the Story Engine Receipt.
```

## What the system will ask for

Only facts that change truth, privacy, scope, or the narrative decision should block execution. The most common useful question is the missing Pursuit: “What did you actually try, change, decide, or build between the problem and the payoff?”

## Quality bar

- The story cannot contain more lived detail than the operator supplied or authorized.
- Pursuit shows a concrete action, experiment, method, or decision.
- An unresolved journey stays unresolved.
- A documented contrast never becomes an invented turning point.
- Commercial storytelling stops at the highest verified proof level.
- Illustrative footage is labeled and never presented as event evidence.
- Exactly one workflow owns body writing.
- The final receipt names facts used, exclusions, missing evidence, and remaining risk.
