## Preservation Lock

- **Keep:** source grounding; Safe/Real/Raw ownership; Problem/Pursuit/Payoff; `FULL STORY`, `STORY FRAGMENT`, `NO STORY`, and `NEEDS SOURCE`; Jun-to-Shaan ownership; proof ceilings; ordinary language.
- **Change:** add a personal four-state field proof and a cold natural-language routing check.
- **Do not disturb:** the original Jun fixtures, the Shaan dosage boundary, private family exclusions, or the rejected Bayer experiment.
- **Risk:** turning Farrice’s private archive into content without tact, or treating a strong fact as permission to invent a scene or transformation.
- **Gate:** every personal output must show its packet, decision, facts used, missing facts, and proof limit before it is judged as writing.

## What Forge Actually Built

The useful power is the sequence:

`real material → evidence map → bounded moment → Safe/Real/Raw → 3P decision → optional method or angle branch → one format owner → receipt`

This matters because the engine can do four different jobs that ordinary “storytelling prompts” collapse into one:

1. **Find the story** when a complete change is supported.
2. **Use a fragment** when the meaning is real but the turn is missing.
3. **Ask for one exact fact** when a good-sounding story would require invention.
4. **Stay direct** when story would get in the way.

## Live Test 1 — Full Story From Farrice’s Training Years

### Gate A

`anchor=Farrice’s gym-floor and seminar experience · artifacts=3 · stakes=make deep expertise visible without performing a louder personality · mechanism=change the selling environment so teaching becomes proof`

### Story Material Packet

**Objective:** Help an expert who dislikes self-promotion see that the problem may be the selling environment, not the depth of their expertise.
**Truth-risk class:** Ordinary real-world.
**Source boundary:** `FARRICE-MASTER-CONTEXT.md` §§1–2 and `_active/farrice-brand/thought-bank/inbox/2026-07-21-wizard-seminar-ai-passion.md`.
**Share boundary:** No family autobiography, private wounds, invented seminar details, or invented client outcomes.

#### Evidence Map

- **SOURCE-REPORTED:** Farrice was not good at approaching people on the gym floor.
- **SOURCE-REPORTED:** He would put on seminars instead.
- **SOURCE-REPORTED:** When he spoke, people listened and came to him.
- **SOURCE-REPORTED:** This is how he started building his training business.
- **SOURCE-REPORTED:** He was strong at client results but struggled with sales and visibility because of rejection fear.
- **SOURCE-REPORTED:** He later built a 90+ agent AI system solo in naptimes.
- **UNKNOWN:** Seminar subject, venue, attendance, conversion rate, revenue, exact dates, and any single turning conversation.

#### Moment

- **Bounded moment:** Farrice stopped relying on gym-floor prospecting and used seminars to let people encounter his expertise through teaching.
- **Moment type:** `LIVED MOMENT`
- **LIFE domain:** `EARNINGS`; secondary `IDENTITY`.

#### Safe → Real → Raw

- **Safe:** He struggled to approach strangers on the gym floor, so he taught seminars.
- **Real:** His knowledge became easier to see when he was teaching instead of interrupting.
- **Raw:** He had treated a mismatch between personality and sales setting as proof that he was bad at putting himself out there.

#### 3P Candidate

- **Problem:** Strong training knowledge stayed hidden because gym-floor prospecting triggered avoidance.
- **Pursuit:** He put on seminars and taught what he knew.
- **Payoff:** People listened, came to him, and the format helped him begin building the business.

#### Boundaries and Decision

- **Narrative status:** `FULL STORY CANDIDATE`
- **Commercial proof ceiling:** `EXPERIENCE`
- **Market proof state:** A source-reported historical client-attraction event exists; no quantity, rate, revenue, or current-offer validation is available.
- **Missing facts:** Exact seminar scene, topic, attendance, offer, dates, and measured outcome.
- **Selected angle:** The sales room mattered. His depth became usable when the format let him teach.
- **Mission:** `POSITION`
- **Destination:** LinkedIn text post
- **Body owner:** Jun social story owner with Farrice BLEND voice
- **Open risk:** Turning the seminar into an anti-outreach excuse would overstate the lesson.

### Meaning-Distinct Angle Map

| Angle | Supported meaning | Why it is distinct |
|---|---|---|
| The wrong room | A person can look bad at selling when the environment rewards interruption instead of teaching. | Sales-channel diagnosis. |
| Teaching made the proof visible | The seminar demonstrated judgment before asking for trust. | Authority and proof diagnosis. |
| A format he could sustain | He did not need a louder personality; he needed a way of showing the work that fit how he operated. | Identity and energy diagnosis. |

**Selected:** The wrong room. It carries the other two meanings without turning the piece into a personality lesson or an anti-outreach rule.

### Finished Asset — LinkedIn Draft

I was terrible at walking up to people on the gym floor.

The training part was where I was strongest. Getting people results was what I did best.

Walking over to a stranger and selling myself was the part I hated.

So I started putting on seminars.

At the front of the room, I could teach what I knew. People listened. Then they came to me.

That is how I started building my training business.

For years, I called that a weakness: good at the work, bad at putting myself out there.

Looking back, I kept asking myself to sell in the exact setting I avoided.

My knowledge was the same on the gym floor and at the seminar. The setting changed what I could do with it.

That pattern is showing up again with AI.

A 90+ agent system is sitting in this workspace because I built it. I can explain how the pieces work together. Then I can still freeze when it is time to say, plainly, “This is what I do. You can hire me for it.”

That old seminar is a clue.

When I teach the work in public, people can decide whether it is useful without me chasing them around the room.

That is the kind of selling I know how to do.

### Story Engine Receipt

- **Narrative decision:** `FULL STORY`
- **Facts used:** Gym-floor discomfort, seminar pursuit, people listening and coming to Farrice, training-business beginning, 90+ agent system.
- **Excluded:** Family history, private wounds, seminar details not in the source, revenue, conversion rate, and client-result claims.
- **Illustrative footage:** `NONE`; any future gym/seminar visuals require classification before use.
- **Commercial proof ceiling:** `EXPERIENCE`
- **Remaining risk:** The last third links the historical pattern to current AI visibility; Farrice must decide whether that recognition feels exact.

## Live Test 2 — Pursuit-to-Method Recovery

This is a separate invocation on the same source. It tests the method branch instead of the story-angle branch.

### Method Card

**Working name:** Seminar-first client attraction
**State:** `METHOD_CANDIDATE`
**Problem:** Direct floor prospecting made strong expertise hard to see and easy to avoid sharing.
**Pursuit:** Farrice repeatedly used seminars to teach what he knew; people listened and came to him.
**Mechanism:** Teaching let prospective clients inspect his judgment before deciding whether to approach him.
**Repeatable actions:**

1. Choose one problem you can teach from direct experience.
2. Put that teaching in a room or format people can opt into.
3. Demonstrate how you think instead of leading with a self-description.
4. Give interested people a clear way to continue the conversation.

**Decision rule:** Use this when subject mastery is strong and interruption-based prospecting is the constraint. Do not use it as a reason to avoid direct follow-up or a clear offer.
**Observable output:** A seminar or teaching artifact plus qualified follow-up conversations.
**Proof boundary:** Farrice’s source supports the seminar behavior and that people came to him. It does not supply attendance, conversion, revenue, repeatability across operators, or current-market demand.
**Commercial state:** `METHOD_CANDIDATE`, not an offer and not market validation.
**Next owner if commercialized:** `/ml-validate-offer`.

### Finished Asset — Grounded Fragment

In 2018, I wrote an offer called Zero to Hero.

The promise is still in my notes: help ordinary guys in their 30s become extraordinary and get into the best shape of their lives.

In 2026, the sentence I work from is different:

“Real data makes you careful. Careful sounds like maybe, and maybe loses to a lie that will say anything.”

Both are mine.

The records prove that my standard changed. They do not tell me when it happened or which moment caused it.

I could smooth that gap over and give you a clean conversion story.

I will not.

The older offer led with the promise.

My work now asks for the evidence first.

Eight years apart.

The middle is still missing.

## Live Test 4 — Strong Fact, Missing Story

### Input

“I built a 90+ agent AI orchestration system solo, in naptimes.”

### Engine Decision

**Status:** `NEEDS SOURCE`

This is an impressive fact, but it is not yet a story. It supplies a result and a constraint. It does not supply one bounded Problem, the specific Pursuit, or the Turn.

**The one question that unlocks the next pass:**

> What was the first moment the system did something you could not have done alone, and what exactly did you build or change to make that happen?

The engine correctly refuses to write “a father built an empire while his child slept,” invent a late-night scene, or treat file count as transformation.
