In 2018, I wrote an offer called Zero to Hero.

The promise is still in my notes: help ordinary guys in their 30s become extraordinary and get into the best shape of their lives.

In 2026, the sentence I work from is different:

“Real data makes you careful. Careful sounds like maybe, and maybe loses to a lie that will say anything.”

Both are mine.

The records prove that my standard changed. They do not tell me when it happened or which moment caused it.

I could smooth that gap over and give you a clean conversion story.

I will not.

The older offer led with the promise.

My work now asks for the evidence first.

Eight years apart.

The middle is still missing.
