- `VERIFIED-SPOKEN`: Jun states or demonstrates the mechanic in the transcript.

- `SYSTEM-SYNTHESIS`: Antigravity translates a source mechanic into a reusable workflow or guardrail.

## What Is Actually New To The Existing System

### Reinforced, not new

- `You Are The Niche`
- psychographic targeting
- pain-first authority
- multiple origin stories
- 3P arc
- silent film
- read-aloud cadence

These already exist in `skills/jun-yuh-creator-vision/` and `skills/jun-yuh-personal-brand/`. The masterclass provides stronger source evidence and examples; it does not justify duplicate workflows.

### Net-new or materially sharper

1. The `LIFE` story-mining wheel.
2. `Safe → Real → Raw` as a source-owned three-why interview.
3. Unresolved payoff as a valid journey-stage ending.
4. Pursuit as the creator's method/IP bridge.
5. The explicit ground-up pipeline from moment to format.
6. Six named 3P placement formats.
7. Third-party privacy and non-oversharing rules.
8. AI-as-interviewer, not biographical ghost-inventor.

## Proof Limits

- Jun's audience, revenue, view, follower, brand, client, and challenge results are `SOURCE-REPORTED`, not independently verified.
- One masterclass does not prove that the six formats work in every platform, industry, audience, or commercial context.
- Captions omit visual verification, so the exact composition of slides and example assets is unavailable.
- The connected system can be called locally operational only after its positive and negative fixtures pass. Audience response, publication quality, conversion, and revenue remain `UNTESTED` until real deployment.
