For three years, I worked behind a vitamin-shop counter.

My recommendation was often the final checkpoint before somebody bought the bottle or put it back on the shelf.

Eighteen years of training turned the same question into a thousand different conversations:

What should I take, and is this worth it?

That checkpoint is still how I read health and performance messaging.

I look at four things:

1. What is the brand asking the buyer to believe?
2. What approved evidence can carry that belief?
3. Which message decision is still unresolved?
4. Where does the language need support, review, or removal?

The Angle Map turns that read into three campaign directions for one live occasion, with hooks, approved-proof direction, and an editorial claim-safety line for each.

This work sharpens the argument and makes its proof boundary visible.

Legal certification stays with the brand's qualified reviewer.
