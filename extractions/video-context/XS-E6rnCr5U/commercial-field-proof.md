## 4. Field-Test Repair Decision

The live run exposed two reusable gaps and repaired only those gaps:

1. **Documented contrast:** Two artifacts can prove a changed position without proving the conversion scene. The system must label the interval and cap the result at a fragment.
2. **Commercial proof ceiling:** A story can prove experience, method, or the existence of a deliverable without proving demand, outcomes, sales, or revenue.

No new standalone workflow was added. The existing material miner, front door, execution prompts, fixture suite, and verifier were strengthened in place.
