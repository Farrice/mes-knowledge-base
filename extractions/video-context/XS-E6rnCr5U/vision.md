# Extraction Vision — Jun Yuh Story Engine

## Objective

Give Antigravity a truth-safe way to find story material in ordinary lived experience, connect that material to a creator's distinct method, and deploy it into the right content format or downstream storytelling owner.

## Uniqueness Audit

| Existing owner | What it already owns | What this source adds |
|---|---|---|
| Jun Yuh Creator Vision | psychographic audience, creator-as-niche, multiple origins, 3P, silent film, sustainable content | LIFE retrieval, Safe/Real/Raw interview, unresolved payoff, six explicit 3P placements, privacy boundary, AI-as-interviewer |
| Shaan Puri Storytelling | universal narrative dosage, factual integrity, cross-domain production routing | an upstream material-mining packet when the operator has an ordinary moment but no articulated story |
| Kallaway | attention, retention, hooks, content psychology | no ownership change; Kallaway may audit a completed social draft, never mine biography or own the body |

## Leverage Map

Highest-leverage uses:

1. personal-brand social content from ordinary lived moments;
2. founder and origin material collection;
3. extracting a teachable method from a personal pursuit;
4. privacy-safe storytelling from sensitive events;
5. creating a story-material packet for offers, presentations, emails, education, or client work;
6. deciding that a technical or evidence-sensitive output should not become a story.

## Build Verdict

`EXPAND EXISTING JUN OWNER + CONNECT TO SHAAN FRONT DOOR`

Do not create a new storytelling expert or mega-skill. Add three focused workflows and one command front door to `jun-yuh-creator-vision`, update Shaan's upstream handoff map, and validate the boundary with positive and negative fixtures.
