## Core Thesis

AI should remove bounded tasks from a human-owned project, not absorb the project itself. The human chooses the change, the audience, the consequential judgments, the acceptable risk, and the public artifact they will own. AI accelerates research, drafts, variants, organization, and other task packets. The practice earns expansion only when a small audience demonstrates real traction.

## Reusable Mechanics

### 1. Project Ownership Before Delegation

At 00:02:35–00:03:02 and again at 00:49:30–00:50:02, the source draws the decisive boundary: give AI tasks; humans do projects. This is not a semantic distinction. A project contains the desired change, audience, risk, quality judgment, and responsibility. A task has a bounded input, output, acceptance test, and review owner.

### 2. Reclaimed Time Must Return to Creative Work

At 00:04:55–00:06:13, AI-cleared tasks are framed as an obligation to spend more time solving problems, not as permission to produce more filler. The workflow therefore asks where reclaimed effort goes and rejects “more volume” as a sufficient outcome.

### 3. System Gaps Produce Better Projects

At 00:42:07–00:47:51, the source looks for a gap in a system, who the system fails to serve, and where a small intervention can change the pattern. This activates the existing systems-thinking overlay for exactly one stage. It does not promote that overlay or turn every creative ask into systems analysis.

### 4. Proud Artifacts Separate Exploration From Slop

At 00:48:31–00:48:43, 00:51:31–00:51:39, and 00:52:27–00:53:28, rough internal generation is separated from a selected public artifact the human is willing to name and own. The artifact must have a user, a promise, a usable form, and a minimum spec—not merely be an impressive AI demo.

### 5. Cheap Failure Is a Ladder, Not a Slogan

At 00:08:01–00:08:24 and 00:38:25–00:38:52, creative uncertainty is accepted and learning is bought through low-cost iteration. The workflow operationalizes this as three progressively exposed tests: internal proof, smallest-audience use, and bounded release. Every rung has a time/cost cap and a decision it informs.

### 6. Traction Decides What Deserves More

At 00:37:27–00:37:52 and 00:48:01–00:48:28, traction means observed behavior: people return, use again, bring others, commit, or pay. Output volume, likes from spectators, and the model’s confidence do not authorize scale.
