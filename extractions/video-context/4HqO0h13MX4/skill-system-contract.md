## 18. Economic Model Contracts

### Annual six-figure model

After activation, the module reverse-engineers revenue and owner income separately. It uses price, offer mix, client count, delivery hours, sales capacity, acquisition actuals, retention, costs, margin, founder calendar, life constraints, and cash needs. It produces base, conservative, and stress cases plus the limiting constraint and smallest next paid action.

### Monthly six-figure model

After activation and company-stage evidence, the module adds headcount, compensation, utilization, sales capacity, acquisition cost and payback, client setup, practitioner ratios, QA, escalation, management load, rework, retention, churn, expansion, cash runway, and non-founder delivery proof. It must reject a solo-only design when capacity cannot support the target.

Both models label assumptions, use actuals where available, expose missing inputs, and prohibit guarantees.
