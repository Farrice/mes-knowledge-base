## Expanded Reuse Contract

### Invariant Spine

Every self-use or client-use run must preserve:

1. practitioner qualification, scope, and life/capacity constraints,
2. a named buyer with an observable problem and outcome,
3. a credible protocol or named mechanism that can keep the promise,
4. a paid POP before a full curriculum, funnel, or content machine,
5. a single primary acquisition path at a time,
6. live delivery, adherence, and evidence capture,
7. actuals tracked as `sent / held / sold / collected`, and
8. proof-gated advancement rather than revenue theater.

### Modular Adapters

| Adapter | What changes by person or niche | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Practitioner / Protocol Packet | Expertise, lived experience, credentials, method, evidence | No invented authority; distinguish experience from qualified practice. |
| Niche / Domain | Buyer, language, stakes, alternatives, market conditions | One specific buyer and problem per run. |
| Transformation Mechanism | Client-owned IP or a routed domain protocol | Must plausibly produce the promised outcome and fit scope of practice. |
| Business Model | 1:1, cohort, advisory, productized consulting, hybrid | Capacity and margin math must work before scale. |
| Acquisition | Warm network, content, partnerships, paid traffic | One primary path; others deferred with re-entry triggers. |
| Scale | First client, repeatability, capacity multiplication, organization | A later stage cannot borrow proof from an earlier stage. |
| Claims / Safety | Domain claims, testimonials, ROI, permissions | Separate source, practitioner, demand, and client-outcome proof. |

### Proof-to-Scale Ladder

| Stage | Intended outcome | Advancement gate |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 0: Paid proof | First paid diagnostic, pilot, or founding engagement | Collected payment, delivered unit, buyer language, and permissioned evidence. |
| Stage 1: Repeatable practice | A credible path toward six figures annually | Several similar clients, repeatable delivery, known margin, and capacity fit. |
| Stage 2: Productized practice | Multi-six annual revenue or sustained roughly $20K-$50K months | Repeatable acquisition, retention, documented IP, and multiplied delivery capacity. |
| Stage 3: Scaled company | Explore six figures per month | Explicit organizational redesign: team, QA, unit economics, management, risk, and non-founder delivery capacity. |

These are planning stages, not earnings guarantees. A revenue target becomes reverse-engineered offer, capacity, acquisition, retention, and margin math only after honest initial conditions are recorded.
