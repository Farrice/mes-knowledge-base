## User Context Added During Extraction

Farrice wants two result surfaces from this source:

1. a system he can use to launch and improve his own coaching or consulting practice, and
2. a reusable capability for helping a qualified practitioner in any niche or protocol build a coaching offer and work toward six-figure annual or monthly economics.

This is user-supplied design intent, not a claim made by the video. It changes the proposed build from a single additive workflow into a thin conductor plus modular adapters. It does not change the proof boundary: revenue targets are planning inputs, not promised outcomes.

## What The Source Actually Teaches

The source is an ordered coaching-business loop:

1. **North Star from earned expertise** — choose who to serve and the transformation based on the work, experience, and advice people already seek from you (`01:18–02:25`).
2. **Transformation statement** — define a specific person, zero state, hero state, and desired feeling (`02:47–03:01`).
3. **Validation before selling or building** — interview people who need the outcome; learn their real requirements and disqualify poor-fit clients (`03:07–04:44`).
4. **Profitable Offer Prototype (POP)** — pre-sell a founding version, build it with the first clients, and let their feedback shape the program (`04:44–04:58`, `25:57–26:21`).
5. **Magnetic messaging by awareness stage** — map cold, warm, and hot leads; match content and calls to action to their readiness (`06:12–09:03`).
6. **Search-intent authority content** — think like a solutions provider, not a general creator; optimize for the right viewer, click, retention, and a business-relevant CTA (`10:26–17:55`).
7. **Minimum viable production** — prioritize clear audio, usable light, one-person connection, and transformation clarity over expensive gear (`17:55–23:58`).
8. **Sales as a continuation of listening** — use the same questions and attention learned in validation interviews; adapt the call load to the founder's energy and strengths (`23:58–28:41`).
9. **Three Cs of delivery** — curriculum supplies the road map, coaching helps implementation in context, and community creates support and accountability (`28:41–30:44`).
10. **Proof and iteration before scale** — build live, capture friction and results, improve the method, then convert it into a more scalable asset (`31:09–33:29`).
11. **Life-first profitability and role progression** — let the desired life shape the target; use profit to buy back time; move from cog to engine to engineer to inventor (`33:34–36:23`).

The visually reviewed checklist at `28:41` reinforces that the source intends these as connected handoffs: transformation → messaging → platform → production → sales → delivery.

## Strongest Reusable Mechanics

### 1. Paid Co-Creation Loop

The POP is not merely a beta discount. It is a control loop:

`interviews → paid founding offer → live delivery → observed friction/results → revised method → proof → scalable version`

This is the source's most useful business mechanic because it collapses research, sales practice, curriculum design, confidence, cash flow, and proof into the same buyer event.

### 2. One Transformation As The System Spine

The transformation statement is not copy decoration. It becomes the shared constraint for client selection, content, calls to action, sales conversations, curriculum, and proof. If downstream parts cannot trace back to the named transformation, the system is drifting.

### 3. Delivery Is The Marketing Engine

The three Cs make delivery the source of future curriculum, proof, testimonials, referrals, and product improvement. The marketing system is incomplete until the delivery loop produces usable evidence.

### 4. Capacity-Aware Founder Design

The video does not prescribe one sales or operating model for everyone. It explicitly uses founder energy, family constraints, and desired life to decide call volume, delegation, profit targets, and what the business should eventually remove from the founder.

## Source Claims That Stay Source-Reported

The video reports thousands of people helped across 600 niches; client businesses at $75K–$100K months; a 60% close rate; and other revenue outcomes. The local capture verifies that these statements were made. It does not independently verify them. None of these claims may be reused as proof for Farrice's offer.

## Existing-Coverage Verdict

Most of the method already exists in `skills/sunny-lenarduzzi-youtube/`:

- ideal-viewer decoding and transformation statements
- reverse-funnel authority content
- offer/program packaging
- pre-sell validation before building
- one-platform, profit-over-reach operating logic

The useful uncovered delta is narrower:

- POP as an explicit paid co-creation loop
- validation interviews as sales rehearsal
- the three-Cs live-delivery loop
- capacity-aware sales/delegation inside the same proof system

Therefore the source-specific mechanic should extend the existing Sunny system if approved. Farrice's broader client-use requirement belongs in a thin, stage-gated conductor that routes existing skills and performs no lane work. It should not create a new Authority.io agent, an all-purpose offer template, or a combined life-coach/solopreneur/AI-consultant mega-skill.
