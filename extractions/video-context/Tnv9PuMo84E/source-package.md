## Source thesis

Wiebe argues that durable brand belief is built through a dependency chain rather than a slogan or isolated persuasion device:

1. **Shiny new lens:** changes how the audience interprets the world.
2. **Devil:** gives the belief contrast by naming a broken behavior, belief, status quo, or institution.
3. **Visible symbol:** lets people signal the belief to others.
4. **Ritual:** turns belief into repeated, self-performed behavior.
5. **Shared language** compresses the worldview into portable phrases.
6. **Prophet:** states a future with conviction only after the first five elements have earned it.

The deployable insight is the sequence. Conviction without the prior layers is posture; a symbol without a belief is merch; ritual without value is friction; an enemy without truth is scapegoating.
