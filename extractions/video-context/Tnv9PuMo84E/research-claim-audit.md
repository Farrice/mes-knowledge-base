# Research Claim Audit

## Audit standard

This table checks whether independent evidence supports the source's factual claim. It does not validate the source's recommended marketing use.

| Source claim | Verdict | What the evidence supports | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Belonging is a powerful human motivation. | PRIMARY-CORROBORATED | Baumeister and Leary's 1995 review argues that forming and maintaining interpersonal attachments is a fundamental, pervasive motivation. | The video's implied placement beside food and safety is rhetorical compression, not the paper's literal hierarchy. |
| Brand communities share consciousness, rituals/traditions, and moral responsibility. | PRIMARY-CORROBORATED | Muñiz and O'Guinn define brand community and identify those three core markers. | The paper does not make shared language a fourth universal marker in the exact form used by the video. |
| Severe initiation can increase liking for a group. | PRIMARY-CORROBORATED | Aronson and Mills' 1959 experiment found greater liking after severe initiation than mild or no initiation. | One historical study does not justify coercive, humiliating, or harmful onboarding. |
| Performing a ritual can increase consumption value. | PRIMARY-CORROBORATED | Vohs and colleagues reported four experiments in which rituals increased consumption and self-performance mattered. | “Zero friction = zero devotion” is not the study's conclusion and should not be generalized. |
| Visible brand markers signal identity and association. | PRIMARY-CORROBORATED / PARTIAL | Han, Nunes, and Drèze analyze brand prominence and signaling among consumers with different status needs. | Preferential treatment is supported by separate luxury-label research, not established by this paper alone. |
| Google acquired Nest for $3.2 billion in cash. | VERIFIED | Google's official 2014 announcement states the transaction value. | The announcement does not prove that Nest's worldview caused the valuation. |
| Berkshire annual-meeting attendance fell sharply after Buffett stepped down. | LIKELY / PARTIAL | AP reported the 2026 arena was a little over half full after prior meetings drew more than 40,000. | The exact “almost 50%” figure is an approximation, and causality cannot be isolated. |
| People use confidence as a cue to correctness or competence. | PARTIAL | Peer-reviewed confidence-heuristic research supports confidence as a cue in social decision-making. | The exact 2012 citation and the video's broad wording were not conclusively matched. |
| Dark-group examples and their practices. | SOURCE-OBSERVED | The examples are present in the talk. | Not independently verified in this pass because they are not needed to deploy the ethical mechanism. |

## Operational consequence

The system may use the corroborated mechanisms as design hypotheses. It must preserve the distinction between research-supported observations, Joanna's interpretation, and Antigravity's operational synthesis. Claims of causal marketing efficacy require production evidence.
