Current positioning: “We create accurate, useful, engaging content that grows trust.” Available proof: a claim-state vocabulary and review process. No verified buyer replies, paid conversions, or adoption evidence supplied.

## Workflow-applied output

### 1. Worldview lens

- **Most people think:** safer health content comes from making claims sound weaker.
- **Here is what is actually true:** safer content comes from making the evidence state impossible to miss.
- **Evidence state:** OPERATIONAL-SYNTHESIS; efficacy UNTESTED.

### 2. Honest devil

- **Broken pattern:** confidence theater:the practice of using tone to make uncertain evidence sound settled.
- **Mechanism:** the sentence becomes more certain while the source does not.
- **Protected boundary:** marketers, founders, clinicians, customers, and competitors are not the enemy; the reviewable behavior is.

### 3. Belief signal

- **Primary symbol:** a visible `[VERIFIED] [LIKELY] [UNCONFIRMED]` claim-state strip on working drafts and proof receipts.
- **Meaning without logo:** this content shows what is known, inferred, and still open.

### 4. Participation ritual

- **Cue:** before an asset is approved.
- **Action:** run the Proof Pass:label each material claim and attach its strongest source.
- **Immediate value:** the reviewer sees where scrutiny belongs.
- **Visible receipt:** a claim ledger travels with the asset.
- **Earned threshold:** rejected for audience access; no exclusion creates additional value here.
- **Exit:** no membership, lock-in, collateral, or penalty.

### 5. Shared language

- **Proof-bounded:** strong enough to act on, honest enough to inspect.
- **Confidence theater:** certainty added by tone instead of evidence.
- **No-event:** readiness or activity without an observable buyer or market event.

### 6. Earned conviction

> The strongest health content will make uncertainty easier to see, not easier to hide.

- **Evidence state:** frontier thesis / UNTESTED.
- **Disconfirming condition:** revise if buyers consistently prefer and reward less explicit evidence-state communication without increasing claim risk.

### Cross-channel expressions

| Channel | Finished expression |
|---|---|
| Founder writing | “The problem is not that health brands sound too confident. It is that the confidence level and evidence level often belong to different sentences.” |
| Recurring content | A weekly “Proof Pass” showing one claim moving from UNCONFIRMED to VERIFIED:or staying open. |
| Marketing | “Every material claim ships with its evidence state.” |
| Landing-page copy | “Claim-safe does not mean timid. It means the proof and the sentence agree.” |
| Offer delivery | Each asset includes a claim ledger and one explicit no-event section. |
| Community | Members voluntarily run the Proof Pass on one claim and share the receipt, not a score. |
| Visual signal | The three-state claim strip appears on drafts, case studies, and proof receipts. |
