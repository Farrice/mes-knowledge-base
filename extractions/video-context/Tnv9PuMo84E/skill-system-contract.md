## 7. Validation loop

- Source-package verifier passes.
- Prompt audit and prompt-library wiring pass.
- Custom verifier passes positive fixtures and rejects coercion, scapegoating, and unearned certainty.
- Behavior proof shows a material before/after output delta on the same input.
- Human taste, production use, audience adoption, market response, and revenue remain separate evidence gates.

## 8. Non-goals

- No standalone “cult branding” skill.
- No universal checklist across all writing.
- No mandatory friction, exclusivity, merch, or in-group language.
- No fabricated enemy, protected-class targeting, coercive initiation, secrecy, collateral, humiliation, or exit penalties.
- No promise that community mechanics automatically increase conversion or retention.

## 9. Runtime posture
