# Analysis: Service And Industry Specialization

## Core Method

Cole frames niching as an operating-model choice, not merely a positioning sentence. A service provider selects one repeatable service, accumulates industry fluency, and protects the capacity needed to repeat the work until pricing power and efficiency emerge.

## Decision Sequence

1. Choose the service before forcing an industry on a cold start.
2. Use real work to learn which industry has fit.
3. Capture vernacular, regulations, workflows, and recurring problems as an information advantage.
4. Combine service and industry into a comprehensible tip of the spear.
5. Map adjacent buyers and related services as a constellation rather than broadening the lead claim.
6. Say no to work that breaks the factory unless there is explicit strategic evidence for changing it.

## What This Adds To The Existing Skill

The prior skill narrows the audience and differentiating mechanism. This source adds delivery economics, a service-first cold-start path, an industry-learning ledger, and a boundary for accepting or rejecting adjacent work.

## Extraction Inference

Desk research can identify candidate industries, language, constraints, and visible pain. It cannot replace the behavioral evidence produced by publishing, conversations, replies, deposits, and delivery. The system therefore separates research confidence from market proof.
