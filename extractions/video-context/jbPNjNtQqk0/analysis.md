## Executive Synthesis

The source is most valuable as a buyer-decision diagnostic, not as a list of universal biases. Each section identifies a receiver state and an applied response: belief defense, cognitive overload, desire for recognition, triviality drift, constrained fit, decision burden, cross-surface mismatch, or affective risk distortion.

Five of the eight mechanics add meaningful capability beyond the current Jason package: Focus, Priority, Fit, Congruence, and Affect. Belief, Recognition, and Choice deepen or correct existing belief, hypnotic-language, and offer mechanics.

## Field Corrections

### Attention

The source's communication advice is closer to limited working memory and chunking than the standard concept of attentional bias. The operational label is therefore **Focus**. The system may use a short roadmap, but it may not claim a universal fixed capacity from Jason's explanation.

### Bounded rationality

`Bounded reality` is retained only as Jason's phrase. The research-backed translation is Herbert Simon's bounded rationality and satisficing: people decide under limited information, time, and cognitive capacity. The applied question becomes best fit under constraints, not objective inferiority.

### Choice overload

The source's Launchpad example usefully shows a narrow segment, five fixed funnels, limited customization, and more done-for-you copy. That is an observed case description. It does not prove that fewer choices are generally superior; meta-analytic work reports substantial variation across contexts.

### Congruence versus dissonance

The beta-launch example is best operationalized as signal congruence. A beta claim paired with a fully polished surface can create an inference mismatch. The system should repair the mismatch without pretending that every messy reality must look neat.

### Affect

Research supports affect influencing risk and benefit judgment. The source's body-location elicitation, exact loss/reward ratios, and implied decision code remain practitioner or NLP claims. The safe use is to make legitimate emotion visible and then restore evidence and reversibility.

## Deep Practitioner Pattern

The source's recurring algorithm is:

1. identify the receiver's current state;
2. avoid the reflexive opposite response;
3. change the decision environment, not merely the argument;
4. reduce the mental or emotional work required to evaluate the next step;
5. preserve enough agency that the receiver can ask for or select the move.

The ethical Antigravity translation adds two requirements Jason does not consistently supply: the state must be grounded rather than invented, and the receiver's agency, material options, and evidence must remain visible.
