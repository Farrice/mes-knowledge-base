# Uncertainty Report

## Confirmed source coverage

- English auto-captions were acquired and normalized into a clean transcript plus timestamped segments.
- The full 29:29 video was sampled scene-aware across 100 frames; every selected frame was inspected.
- The central title cards, seven-attribute explanation, 3-2-1 card, and Sandcastles demonstration are represented in the evidence package.

## Limits

- The captions are automatic. The opening appears to mistranscribe “Kallaway” as “Chalene,” and product/model names may contain similar errors.
- The scene sampler reached its 100-frame cap and reported sparse coverage for a video of this length. This is sufficient for the major visual mechanics, but it is not a frame-by-frame reconstruction.
- No independent web research was performed to validate algorithm claims, business outcomes, follower counts, revenue, client results, Sandcastles product behavior, or the stated 2% filter rationale.
- Sandcastles screens establish that controls and outputs were shown in the video; they do not independently prove accuracy, completeness, or current product availability.
- “Authority density,” “trust accrual per view,” and the relationship between engagement thresholds and paid brand deals are not formally defined or empirically validated in the video.

## Claim handling

- Creator outcome claims are labeled `SELF_REPORTED_UNCONFIRMED`.
- Platform-mechanism claims are labeled `SOURCE_CLAIM_UNCONFIRMED_EXTERNALLY`.
- Synthesis that combines separate passages is labeled `BOUNDED_INFERENCE`.
- The operational framework is safe to test as a hypothesis; it should not be presented as universally proven.

## Missing evidence that would raise confidence

1. Platform documentation or controlled data supporting the three claimed distribution shifts.
2. Raw account analytics connecting topic buckets to qualified followers, email capture, and revenue.
3. A disclosed definition and calculation method for authority density.
4. Before/after results across several niches, including failed 3-2-1 trials.
5. Independent validation of the Sandcastles analysis and its classification accuracy.
