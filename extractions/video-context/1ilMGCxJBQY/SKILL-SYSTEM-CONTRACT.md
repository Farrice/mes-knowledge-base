# Skill System Contract: Micro-Fame Authority Density Companion

Status: **Candidate for human judgment; not promoted**

## Job to be done

Help an operator design and evaluate a personal-brand content portfolio that grows qualified category authority rather than merely increasing follower volume.

## Primary user

A creator, founder, advisor, or content strategist who has a defined category and wants a testable position plus a two-week content learning loop.

## Trigger

Use when the user asks to:

- build authority or micro-fame in a category;
- find a distinct personal-brand position;
- decide whether growth is attracting the right audience;
- select broad and narrow topic buckets;
- review a 3-2-1 content test.

Do not trigger for pure scriptwriting, visual design, or one-off post ideation without a positioning or portfolio decision.

## Owner and composition

- **Function owner:** Kallaway Content OS.
- **Companion:** Micro-Fame Authority Density workflow.
- **Support only when needed:** claim-risk review for high-stakes categories; source research for current competitor data.
- **Human owner:** final positioning taste, acceptable evidence proxies, and keep/modify/kill decisions.

## Inputs

Required:

- desired authority category;
- high-value viewer or buyer;
- creator capabilities, proof, constraints, and preferred delivery mode;
- current or proposed competitors;
- available content and outcome data.

Optional:

- recent post analytics;
- buyer language and client-call notes;
- outlier dataset;
- offer and conversion path;
- adjacent/distant fields for transpositioning.

## Outputs

1. Authority objective and observable proxy dashboard.
2. Seven-axis category baseline and whitespace map.
3. Smallest-sufficient positioning stack with capability evidence.
4. One broad and two narrow topic buckets with distinct jobs and thresholds.
5. One controlled-chaos hypothesis.
6. Two-week, seven-post-per-week test plan or cadence-adjusted equivalent.
7. Keep/modify/kill/inconclusive review with evidence and next-batch changes.

## Decision rules

- Do not collapse reach, fit, trust, and commercial action into one score by default.
- Do not choose whitespace that the creator cannot execute credibly.
- Add positioning attributes only until blinded contrast is clear.
- Judge broad and narrow buckets by different jobs.
- Require four meaningfully different executions before a negative bucket verdict unless a hard factual, safety, or permission veto fires.
- Use `INCONCLUSIVE` when distribution and qualified-response evidence conflict.
- Keep one bounded exploration slot so the strategy can discover new winners.

## Evidence contract

Every recommendation must identify its evidence class:

- `OBSERVED_INPUT`: supplied analytics, content, or buyer language.
- `SOURCE_MECHANIC`: directly grounded in the video package.
- `CURRENT_RESEARCH`: externally verified competitor or platform evidence.
- `BOUNDED_INFERENCE`: synthesis that extends beyond a direct statement.
- `UNTESTED`: a proposed bucket, positioning move, or threshold awaiting a live trial.

Creator-reported outcomes remain self-reported unless independently verified.

## Failure and refusal behavior

- Missing category or audience: return the minimum missing decision, not a confident strategy.
- No competitor evidence: produce a provisional scan labeled `UNTESTED` and a research plan.
- No outcome instrumentation: separate what can be learned from reach from what remains unknowable about authority.
- High-stakes claims: route to claim-risk review before publication.
- Paid-tool dependency: offer a tool-neutral research path and preserve provenance.

## Validation set

1. **Broad but wrong:** follower growth rises; qualified replies fall. Expected: diagnose dilution, not success.
2. **Narrow but valuable:** low views; strong saves and buyer conversations. Expected: preserve or modify, not kill by reach.
3. **Copied aesthetic:** differentiated visuals but no substantive moat. Expected: reject as fragile positioning.
4. **New creator:** no analytics. Expected: provisional plan with explicit uncertainty, not false authority scoring.
5. **High-stakes health:** compelling claim without support. Expected: factual/claim-risk hold on the claim, while continuing safe positioning work.
