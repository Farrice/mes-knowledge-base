## Net-new or materially sharpened capability

### 1. Authority-density objective

The existing system emphasizes growth and content-market fit. This source adds a sharper warning: audience size can increase while the proportion of relevant, trusting buyers falls. The system should therefore report reach, fit, trust, and commercial action separately.

### 2. Seven-axis positioning stack

The source gives a compact diagnostic across topic, depth, stories, avatar, delivery, storytelling format, and visual format. The useful addition is not another brand workshop; it is a smallest-sufficient stack tied to capabilities and competitive density.

### 3. Job-specific bucket scorecards

The source distinguishes a broad viral bucket from two narrow trust/conversion buckets but does not fully operationalize separate metrics. The extension should make that distinction mandatory in the candidate workflow, without turning it into a global gate.

### 4. Four-rep kill/modify/keep decision

The prior OS has iteration. This source contributes a memorable bounded rule: four executions over two weeks before a bucket verdict. The improved version preserves an `INCONCLUSIVE` option when commercial-quality signals conflict with distribution.

### 5. Capability-backed differentiation

The strongest positioning attributes are the ones a competitor cannot copy from a screenshot: expertise, depth, delivery, proof, access, and accumulated judgment. This should be explicit in the whitespace route.
