## Verdict and build shape

The valuable system is a repeatable apprenticeship loop: curate actual posts, analyze how they are constructed, apply the resulting guide to personal material, retain the useful procedure, and participate in relevant conversations. Image-led packaging is one technique inside it.

This is a practitioner demonstration, not a controlled beginner-growth experiment. At 04:41–05:23 Dan shows his established LinkedIn account and reports improved engagement. It establishes what he did and what he reports, not the causal effect of images or a timetable from zero to 10,000 followers. The product demo also sells Eden and its bootcamp. None of the operating methods require buying those products.

Extend `dan-koe-multipassionate-mastery` with three components, under one LinkedIn front door. Reuse `dan-koe-ai-leverage` for later taste repair. Do not create a third Dan persona or import his multipassionate positioning into an unrelated B2B offer.

## Hidden knowledge: labeled inferences

1. **The breakdown is an interface between taste and execution.** Selection communicates what the operator likes before they can explain all of it. Inference from 11:14–17:24. It fails if we substitute our own taste for Farrice's and call that personal calibration.
2. **Image and caption form two levels of the argument.** The compact idea stops attention; the caption supplies an application or explanation. Inference from the visible angle choice at 08:50 and generated caption at 09:39. It fails when both carry the same meaning.
3. **The durable asset is a compact decision guide, not the chat transcript.** Saving the breakdown and resetting context makes the method portable. Inference from 18:09–19:10; the oversized breakdown demonstrates the opposing risk.
4. **Distribution is part of the method's input conditions.** He brings prior attention and explicitly adds replies and credited commentary. Inference from 02:00–02:34 and 26:23–30:30. Copying only the post shape removes part of the system.
5. **Selective examples can teach construction while misrepresenting expected performance.** The examples are curated winners and the account shown is established. Inference, not an accusation of misreporting. Add ordinary comparisons and prospect fit before making a strategy decision.
6. **The tutorial is itself a product funnel.** It demonstrates a useful method inside Eden, offers reusable assets, then invites the bootcamp. Observable sequence at 03:01, 06:05 and 25:34. We can port the operating sequence without importing the subscription.

## Three calibration demonstrations

These are descriptions of observed demonstrations, not reproductions of complete third-party posts or independently retrieved reference-corpus pieces.

**A. One idea, three caption directions — 08:31–09:18.** The interface presents an economic framing, a self-assessment framing and a positive-opportunity framing for the same AI/skill seed. Dan chooses the first. The resulting caption expands the seed. What to learn: angle selection precedes sentence polishing. What is not proven: that the chosen angle caused engagement or that the generated prose is his final voice.

**B. Four-post board to teaching guide — 14:17–18:59.** He saves four posts, requests a breakdown, waits, and opens the resulting document. At 18:29 a frame shows an analysis section describing an identity reframe and numbered construction. What to learn: teach from artifacts and preserve the resulting guide. What to reject: promoting the model's explanation of causality to evidence; measuring quality by document length.

**C. Fresh-context personal interview — 19:05–20:39.** A new chat receives the saved breakdown and asks for domain, story, audience and goal before production. What to learn: separate source analysis from personal material and reuse context. What is not shown: a fully completed interview, final human-approved post or later growth from that post.

## Signature moves

- Save posts you would willingly emulate before asking AI for a style guide.
- Separate the original seed from each platform variant.
- Ask for an angle before a full caption.
- Delete or rewrite anything that fails the author's actual beliefs.
- Save a useful breakdown, then restart with the condensed artifact.
- Put a follow-worthy thought in a relevant reply rather than generic praise.
- Attribute a borrowed idea and add a perspective the source did not supply.

## What would surpass the demonstration

Better source controls, original proof, faster reuse, and a measured learning loop are achievable design improvements. Surpassing Dan's follower growth, revenue, or writing quality remains untested. A model preference does not equal Farrice's taste verdict, and neither equals customer behavior.

Start with a bounded comparison of formats on the same subject family, measured at the same post age. Track substantive responses, profile visits, qualified conversations and time spent alongside impressions. Keep topic, audience and posting cadence stable enough to learn. Small samples supply directional evidence, not causal certainty.
