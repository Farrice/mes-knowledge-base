# Shaan Puri Storytelling — Mechanics Delta

## Distinct-method thesis

Shaan's method is not “turn everything into a story.” It is to decide whether story is the right tool, choose the emotional change the communication must create, make intention, obstacle, and stakes legible, dramatize the five-second turn, calibrate frame and pacing to audience buy-in, and improve through intelligent repetitions.

## Coverage summary

The comparison tested 11 source mechanics against the current Shaan system:

- **6 are already strongly operationalized** and need no new component.
- **3 are present but inconsistently routed** and should sharpen the front door.
- **2 are behaviorally missing** and warrant bounded expansion.

This is a canonical evidence upgrade and a targeted expansion, not a new expert extraction.

## Existing-system defects that block reliable deployment

- `SKILL.md` is visibly truncated at `1. Content i`.
- `genius.md` contains an incomplete Hall of Fame bullet and a duplicated Hall of Fame heading.
- All six workflows refer to nonexistent `genius.md` sections named **Decision Framework** and **Voice DNA**.
- The agent and at least one workflow repeat audience and sale-size claims that the source ledger marks **UNCONFIRMED**.
- First-person synthetic exemplars are not clearly labeled as constructed examples.
- The internal Transformation Isomorphism extension is not distinguished clearly enough from source-derived Shaan mechanics at point of use.
- The source ledger's statement that no `extractions/` package exists is now stale.
- Factual guards are strong in several v2 prompts but are not a uniform invariant across the six workflows.

## Verdict

**BOUNDED EXPAND.** Preserve the existing Shaan skill and add only the missing judgment layer: a story/no-story deployment router and an adjacent-field mechanic-transfer protocol. Reuse the existing Story Compass for story-vs-topic diagnosis and the existing How I Write truth/fact gates for high-stakes work. Do not create a second Shaan skill, a second writing conductor, or a full channel OS from this duplicate interview.
