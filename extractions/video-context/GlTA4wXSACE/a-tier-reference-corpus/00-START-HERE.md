# Shaan Story Deployment: Six-Domain Reference Corpus

This corpus tests whether `/shaan-story-deploy` can choose and execute the smallest truthful narrative dosage across six real Antigravity briefs without broadening the router.

## Status

- **Corpus source:** six first-party workspace briefs with local provenance.
- **Blind protocol:** artifact-only generation; references and expected routes withheld from replay workers.
- **Replay result:** five local A-tier candidates; sales remains pending after the one permitted repair replay selected too much story.
- **Reference versions:** v1 files remain sealed as the pre-replay judgment targets; v2 files are the current length- and prose-hardened reference set.
- **Failure ratchet:** five replay failures, two corpus-QA failures, and one operator-handoff failure became eight regression fixtures; seven are repaired and one is pending.
- **Promotion boundary:** the corpus remains a local A-tier candidate until Farrice completes the recognition checkpoint and the pending sales dosage fixture is resolved.
- **Router boundary:** unchanged: `FULL STORY`, `STORY FRAGMENT`, or `NO STORY`; one production owner; no new expert, domain adapter, or route.
