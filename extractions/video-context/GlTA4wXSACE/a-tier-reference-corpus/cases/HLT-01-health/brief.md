## Truth Constraints

- Evidence is the spine. A personal moment may aid recognition but cannot prove efficacy, safety, or market size.
- No dosing recommendation, benefit claim, contraindication, medical advice, or generalization from 36 participants.
- State dates or "when measured" for view counts.
- Preserve `VERIFIED`, `LIKELY`, and `UNCONFIRMED` distinctions in the reasoning and wording.
- Do not identify or criticize a specific brand.
- No claim that the audience shift, virality, or commercial opportunity has been quantitatively measured.
