## Reference Asset

Women used to stop me behind a Vitamin Shoppe counter and ask whether creatine would make them look like the man on the tub.

I answered that question for five years.

The category is finally speaking to that buyer at scale. The strange part is who is doing the speaking.

When this brief was measured on August 1, a scientist's midlife creatine masterclass had 165,455 YouTube views. A podcast clip titled with the buyer's literal question had 62,944. Good Morning America had another 29,034.

The brand page was not the center of her decision.

That matters because the evidence underneath the new conversation is narrower than the conversation itself.

One randomized trial indexed on PubMed enrolled 36 peri- or menopausal women across four arms for eight weeks. It used creatine hydrochloride at 750 to 1,500 mg.

The tub a buyer picks up is commonly creatine monohydrate around a 5 g serving. That category description was not a product-by-product audit, but the distinction still matters: different form, different dose, and a very small population.

This does not make creatine useless. It does not establish a consumer recommendation either.

It tells a founder exactly where trust is being won or lost.

The buyer is asking whether this is for someone like her, at her age, in the form and amount sitting in her cart. Scientists and long-form interviews are willing to stay with that question. Morning television makes the category feel mainstream. Scare content answers it with urgency, even when the underlying evidence is weak.

Legacy gym creative often answers a different question for a different buyer.

That is the evidence-fit gap.

Before the next creative round, separate three things:

- what the deep monohydrate literature actually supports;
- what this 36-person hydrochloride trial examined;
- what the buyer is being led to believe about the product in front of her.

The honest page may feel less dramatic than the headline. It will be more useful.

The founder who names the form, dose, population, and remaining uncertainty is not weakening the sale. They are giving the new buyer the decision path the rest of the category left blank.
