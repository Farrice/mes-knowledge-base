## What changes when the reader becomes specific

Human Values Collective had a real mission and a homepage that made the organization the subject:

> Parents and teachers working together to create courageous hearts in children.

The documented five-second test scored it 1 out of 3. A stranger could see the mission without seeing their own life inside it.

The rewrite began with the parent's private stake:

> You want your child to be strong enough to face the world — and open enough to come home when it hurts.

Same organization. Same work. A different person at the center of the sentence.

That before-and-after is one of three HVC artifacts prepared for the Parallax proof spread. The material still requires Javier's approval before public use, so this provisional page does not display the pending testimonial or claim publication clearance.

The point is not that one headline fixes a business. The point is that reader specificity changes what the homepage, offer, content, and revenue ladder have to do next.
