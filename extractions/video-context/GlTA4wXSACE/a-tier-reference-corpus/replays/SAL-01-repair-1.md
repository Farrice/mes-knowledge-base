# You have a reader. We find the reader.

Your work can be sharp in conversation and still go blurry on the page. You know what you believe. You know what you can do. Then a stranger lands on your homepage, reads a paragraph broad enough for almost anyone, and leaves with one quiet problem: they cannot tell if you mean them.

A qualified reader should not need to reverse-engineer their way into your work.

Parallax ICP Intelligence gives your publication, practice, or mission-driven organization a named reader, that reader’s verbatim language, and a publishing system built around the choice. The work takes 7 to 21 days, depending on the tier.

## Strong work. Indistinct reader.

This shows up in small decisions. A homepage tries to hold the whole mission. Hooks reach for the widest possible audience. A calendar gets filled before anyone can say who should care about the next 30 posts.

Because nothing looks obviously broken, the blur can linger.

The page can sound careful and credible while leaving the reader to supply the missing connection.

Parallax makes that connection specific enough to use. Three real humans. Their words. A clearer homepage. Hooks with an address. A calendar that knows who it is for. The exact deliverables change by tier, but the principle stays put: your work stops talking to a market and starts talking to a person.

## The turn can happen in one sentence

In March 2026, HVC’s homepage led with this headline:

“Parents and teachers working together to create courageous hearts in children.”

The line carried the organization’s mission. It named parents and teachers, but kept the parent-level tension in the background. The recorded five-second test was 1/3, and its SB7 score was 31/70.

The rewrite was:

“You want your child to be strong enough to face the world — and open enough to come home when it hurts.”

The work already had worth. The rewrite made the reader more distinct.

Together, the rewrite, its associated subhead, and the CTA turned the mission into a parent-recognition surface. No sales, traffic, or conversion result is claimed here. The proof is narrower and more useful: the before language, the recorded scores, and the rewritten surface show what changes when the reader is named with more precision.

The current proof spread also includes the Grace City Blueprint transit map with its five-rung revenue ladder, plus the Family Conversation Card and newsletter variant. These are real artifacts, but they are not publicly cleared. HVC use remains subject to Javier’s veto. His testimonial wording is still pending and does not appear here. All three proof artifacts need client approval and visual production before publication.
