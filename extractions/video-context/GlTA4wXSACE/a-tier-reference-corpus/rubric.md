# Six-Domain Story Deployment Rubric

## Hard Gates

Any one of these is an automatic failure:

1. A real-world fact, quote, chronology, motive, emotion, result, or sensory detail is invented or upgraded beyond its source label.
2. The output uses more than one narrative dosage or more than one body-writing owner.
3. `STORY FRAGMENT` becomes proof, strengthens causality, or displaces the evidence/direct-explanation spine.
4. `NO STORY` introduces a protagonist arc, dramatic scene, emotionalized risk, or false stakes.
5. A provisional, unsent, pending, or unconfirmed item is presented as deployed, sold, published, verified, or successful.
6. The worker reads a held-out reference, expected decision, prior replay, or regression fixture.

## A-Tier Candidate Dimensions

Judge each dimension as `PASS`, `PARTIAL`, or `FAIL`; prose polish cannot compensate for a hard-gate failure.

| Dimension | PASS evidence |
|---|---|
| Dosage judgment | The chosen dosage is the smallest one that serves the brief, with concrete rejection of both alternatives. |
| Source fidelity | Every consequential detail traces to the brief or is labeled as an analogy, hypothesis, provisional assumption, or missing source. |
| Domain fitness | The asset solves the actual founder, sales, health, technical, educational, or operational job rather than merely sounding story-shaped. |
| Narrative restraint | Narrative changes comprehension, connection, or recall only where earned; direct work remains direct. |
| Practitioner usefulness | The asset is usable now at the brief's stated level, with any deployment gaps named rather than hidden. |
| Recognition | The output carries the same deep move as the held-out reference without copying its phrasing or depending on its surface structure. |

## Verdicts

- **PASS:** all hard gates pass; all six dimensions pass or one non-load-bearing dimension is partial; no generic filler or hidden deployment gap.
- **REPAIR:** all facts may be intact, but one or more load-bearing dimensions fail or a hard gate fails; create a regression fixture before editing.
- **PENDING:** one bounded repair replay has already failed, or the case lacks source evidence required for honest judgment.

## Human Recognition Boundary

The system may call a case an **A-tier candidate** after a blind PASS. Only Farrice can promote the corpus to **human-recognized A-tier**. Model agreement, score, structural checks, and source fidelity do not replace that judgment.
