# After-Action Review: Six-Domain Story Deployment Corpus

## Mission

Build a source-traceable storytelling reference corpus from six real briefs, run blind replays, convert every miss into a regression fixture, and preserve the existing three-state router.

## Result

**Mission deliverable: KEEP. Behavioral promotion: PARTIAL.**

- Six domains covered.
- Six first-pass blind replays completed.
- Two bounded repair replays completed.
- Final behavior: five pass, one pending.
- Original blind/corpus QA ratchet: seven fixtures, six repaired, one pending.
- Post-delivery usability miss: one additional fixture, repaired by this guide and verifier update.
- Complete ratchet after AAR: eight fixtures, seven repaired, one pending.
- Router scope, decision set, production-route count, and one-owner limit remained unchanged.

## What Worked

1. **Preservation before repair.** V1 references were sealed before replay and remain the historical judgment targets. Improved references were written as V2 rather than overwriting the evidence.
2. **Real-domain restraint.** The system selected full story for founder transformation, fragments for evidence-led health and education, and no story for technical and operational decisions.
3. **Independent replay.** Fresh workers received the router and one normalized brief, not the hidden references or expected decisions.
4. **Failure became system knowledge.** Each observed miss was named before repair, attached to an existing owner, and bounded by a no-router-expansion rule.
5. **Honest stopping.** Sales remained pending after the permitted repair still chose too much story. The system did not widen the router or grant itself unlimited attempts.
6. **Quality assurance found a second class of defect.** Post-replay review caught reference-body word-count contamination and a prose-surface issue that the behavior evaluations did not detect.

## What Did Not Work

1. Owner scope: a bounded landing-page request silently overrode a broader funnel owner contract.
2. Process invention: the first replay invented what occurred inside the free ICP Tell.
3. Social-owner scope: a single-post request silently overrode a broader content-dossier contract.
4. Narrator inference: the health replay invented Farrice's reaction to a sourced customer question.
5. Dosage error: the repaired page treated one real copy before-and-after as a complete transformation story.
6. Counting error: receipts and scaffolding were included in the original totals, leaving all six V1 bodies below their requested ranges.
7. Prose surface: the sales V1 reference carried an avoidable warning. The separately versioned V2 reference repaired it.
8. Handoff failure: Farrice saw that work had happened without receiving a plain explanation of what the corpus changed or how to use it.

## Root Causes

- Broad production owners did not explicitly support router-supplied subsets.
- Attractive service-process and emotional language was treated as harmless texture instead of factual claims.
- A vivid before-and-after was mistaken for sufficient full-story movement.
- Mechanical QA counted the whole file rather than the actual asset body.
- The closeout prioritized verification receipts and next-step gates over operator comprehension.

## Prevention Rules

1. Seal every judged reference before replay; a hash mismatch fails verification.
2. Put post-judgment improvements in a new version; never rewrite the evaluated target.
3. Create a named fixture before changing shipped behavior.
4. State the exact router-supplied subset when a broad owner produces a smaller artifact.
5. Treat service process, narrator reaction, chronology, and emotional state as sourced claims.
6. A before-and-after is not a full story without a supported want, obstacle, turn, and change.
7. Measure requested length on the asset body only.
8. Every corpus closeout must include a user guide explaining capability gained, practical uses, proof limits, and a default invocation.
9. Stop at the repair cap; unresolved behavior remains pending.

## Effective Pairings

- Source-to-skill-system plus Repeatability Spine: connected capability with preservation and replay discipline.
- Shaan router plus domain truth gates: narrative improvement without letting story replace evidence or operations.
- Fresh generation workers plus main-thread evaluation: independence without surrendering integration judgment.
- V1 immutability plus V2 versioning: honest historical evidence and practical improvement at the same time.

## Capability Gained

The system can now make and explain a defensible narrative-dosage decision for founder stories, offers, education, health evidence, engineering decisions, and operating handoffs. It can produce a finished asset through one owner, preserve fact boundaries, return an inspectable receipt, and replay the same six cases after future changes.

The new capacity is therefore not “write more stories.” It is:

- use story when transformation genuinely carries the job;
- use one fragment when evidence must remain primary;
- refuse story when direct communication is stronger;
- preserve voice without inventing life details;
- expose unsupported claims before publication;
- test future improvements against real briefs instead of taste claims alone.
