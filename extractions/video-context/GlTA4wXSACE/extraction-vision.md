# Extraction Vision: Shaan Puri Storytelling Deployment Layer

**Checkpoint:** 1 of 3; approved by Farrice on 2026-08-02

**Source:** `GlTA4wXSACE`, a My First Million republication of the Shaan Puri interview originally published by David Perell's How I Write
**Build verdict:** Bounded expansion of `skills/shaan-puri-storytelling/`

## The vision

Turn Shaan Puri's storytelling method into a reliable communication layer that can travel across domains, niches, formats, and objectives **without pretending every task should become a story**.

The finished system should be able to do three things before it writes:

1. decide whether the communication needs a full story, a story fragment, or no story;
2. select the smallest source-backed Shaan mechanic that fits the objective, audience buy-in, medium, and truth risk;
3. route complex writing, story diagnosis, and factual verification into the strong systems that already exist rather than rebuilding them.

“Any domain, niche, objective and task” therefore means **cross-domain communication adaptability**, not universal task competence or guaranteed performance. The layer can improve how a health explanation, internal memo, founder narrative, product story, lesson, pitch, or presentation is communicated. It does not replace the domain expert, evidence, calculation, code, or operating procedure underneath that communication.

## Uniqueness audit

The roster already has strong owners for story diagnosis, narrative structure, literary voice, long-form nonfiction, conversion, retention, and full writing composition. Shaan does **not** uniquely own “storytelling.” His locally distinct value is the compact creator-to-audience operating sequence:

`creator state → target feeling → story fit → frame → intention/obstacle → five-second change → audience-buy-in pacing → repetition/distribution`

The current system captures nearly all of that sequence. What it lacks is the judgment that sits between the user's objective and the sixteen available prompts.

### Gap 1: Story Deployment Judgment

The source explicitly warns that story is not the right tool for every piece. The system currently offers many ways to produce a story but no mandatory decision about whether one should be produced.

The future front door should classify:

- **objective:** connect, explain/remember, persuade, transform, entertain, build audience, or transfer voice;
- **story fit:** full story, partial story, or no story;
- **audience relationship:** stranger, curious, or fan;
- **medium and attention contract;**
- **truth risk:** creative/personal, ordinary real-world, or high-stakes/evidence-sensitive;
- **best existing Shaan workflow or external handoff.**

Its most important output is sometimes a refusal: “Do not narrativize this; preserve the direct explanation and borrow only framing, specificity, pacing, or memorable language.”

### Gap 2: Adjacent-Field Mechanic Transfer

The source's MrBeast example supplies an operational principle the current skill names but does not execute: when a capability is weak, study the field where that exact function is mission-critical, extract the mechanism rather than the style, adapt it to the target constraints, and test one realistic use.

This should produce a bounded transfer brief, not a generic inspiration collage:

`one weakness → best outside field → mechanism → adaptation → risk → one test → keep/reject`

### Governance gap: Truth without narrative distortion

A uniform factual-integrity invariant is necessary for the user's cross-domain goal, especially in health, finance, law, and other evidence-sensitive contexts. Technical explanations and internal decisions need it too. This is **system governance, not Shaan-derived genius**.

It should reuse existing verification and How I Write gates:

- preserve material facts and uncertainty;
- never invent dialogue, metrics, chronology, outcomes, or sensory details as real;
- label constructed examples and analogies;
- keep evidence primary in high-stakes work;
- let story support comprehension or recall, never substitute for proof.

No additional expert stack is approved at this stage. More names would create expert soup without adding a missing function.

## Proposed behavior proof

The expansion must pass three detached fixtures before it is called deployable:

1. **Full story helps:** a founder or customer transformation with real raw material. The router should choose a story path and improve intention, obstacle, stakes, and the five-second turn without inventing facts.
2. **Only a fragment helps:** an evidence-sensitive health explanation. The router should preserve the evidence spine and use only a truthful analogy, frame, or concrete moment to improve comprehension and recall.
3. **Story should be refused:** a B2B/internal operating memo where direct decision logic is primary. The router should reject a dramatic arc and improve only the frame, pace, or plain-language compression.

Each fixture must record the weak input, diagnosis, selected mechanic, transformed output, observable behavior delta, factual-integrity result, and remaining risk. These prove repository behavior only; they do not prove market performance.
