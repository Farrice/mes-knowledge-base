## An Average Is Not an Individual Guarantee

This is a fictional example, not medical evidence or medical guidance.

Group A’s mean marker moved from 8.4 to 7.9 units over eight weeks. Group B’s mean marker moved from 8.3 to 8.1 units over eight weeks.

Those are changes in group means. They do not show what happened to any one individual.

**Labeled analogy—for explanation only, not study evidence:** If a class’s average score changes, that does not mean every student’s score changed in the same way. The average summarizes the group; it is not a guarantee for each student.

The card does not provide sample size, confidence intervals, adverse-event data, or a statistical-significance result. It does not establish individual benefit or causality.

Bottom line: The fictional card reports average group changes, not an individual guarantee.
