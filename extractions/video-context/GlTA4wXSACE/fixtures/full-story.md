## Final asset

### The handoff that stayed ownerless

We were building a support-triage tool to keep customer issues from disappearing during handoffs.

The problem became specific when one test incident sat unassigned in a shared inbox for 36 hours. When its details were copied into a spreadsheet, severity and the next owner were no longer visible in the same place.

That was the turn: the team saw that the two fields needed for the handoff decision were no longer visible together.

So we built a prototype queue with owner, severity, and next action side by side. In an internal pilot with 12 seeded cases, every case received an owner within 10 minutes.

That result is narrow: it shows what happened in the seeded pilot. It does not establish the same outcome in live customer operations. The prototype put ownership, severity, and next action in one view at the handoff.
