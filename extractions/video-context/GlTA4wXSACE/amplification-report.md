## Four-scan findings

### Gap scan

The missing source-backed decision is whether the communication needs a full story, a story fragment, or no story. Shaan states the boundary at 00:25:37–00:25:58. Story Compass asks whether candidate material contains want, tension, and change. It does not decide the appropriate narrative dosage for the objective, attention contract, or truth risk.

### Depth scan

Do not split the six existing workflows. Their weakness is routing and governance, not insufficient granularity. The current narrative, viral, and sales surfaces tend to assume story should fire. All six workflows also point to nonexistent `Decision Framework` and `Voice DNA` sections.

Factual guards are uneven. The active viral-content, email-sequence, and video-script prompts need a complete story-fact invariant. The current source ledger also identifies credential claims that remain unconfirmed in active surfaces.

### Stacking scan

- Story Compass remains the story-existence diagnostic.
- How I Write remains the long-form writing conductor and owns the single body voice.
- Shaan contributes the selected frame, feeling, pacing move, or story architecture.
- Existing fact and truth checks remain the closing gate.
- Adjacent-field transfer uses an existing specialist route that matches the transfer type.

### Creative-application scan

One calibrated router produces three distinct behaviors across domains:

1. narrative-first work when full story is warranted;
2. evidence-first explanation with one truthful narrative fragment;
3. direct operational communication that refuses narrative theater.

## Component decision

### Build: Story Deployment Router

Its question is: **What narrative dosage fits this objective?**

Inputs include objective, raw material, audience relationship, medium, attention contract, and truth risk. It returns `FULL STORY`, `PARTIAL STORY`, or `NO STORY`, then selects one existing Shaan workflow/prompt or one external handoff.

This does not duplicate Story Compass or How I Write.

### Integrate, do not build: Adjacent-Field Mechanic Transfer

Its question is: **Which outside field is best at the named weak function, and what operation can be tested here?**

The Shaan router should create this handoff:

`named weakness → field where the function is mission-critical → existing transfer route → target constraints → one detached test → keep/reject`

Use the existing route that fits the material:

- narrative skeleton transfer: `/narrative-transfer`;
- cross-industry copy or market pattern: David Deutsch's Cross-Industry Translation;
- outlier content mechanic: Diandra's Outlier Study and Remix;
- fourth-wall or experience mechanic: Rafa Conde's Fourth-Wall Domain Transfer.

A new generic transfer prompt would overlap those systems.

## Highest-value actions

1. Build one born-v2 `/shaan-story-deploy` router with explicit full, partial, and no-story behavior.
2. Add adjacent-field transfer as a router handoff, not another production engine.
3. Repair the truncated front door, broken exemplar, invalid references, stale provenance, and unconfirmed credential claims.

## Proof fixtures

### Full story

A clearly synthetic founder packet supplies a goal, obstacle, documented failed handoff, decisive observation, first prototype action, and internal-pilot result. It should route through `FULL STORY`, pass Story Compass, use the current Story Architecture prompt, and enter the How I Write founder-origin lane if public-facing. Flat chronology should become an intention-and-obstacle engine organized around the supplied decisive moment. Any invented dialogue, emotional state, metric, chronology, or exaggerated transformation fails the fixture.

### Partial story

An evidence-sensitive patient explainer supplies a clinician-approved evidence card, cited claims, uncertainty language, and a comprehension objective. It should route through `PARTIAL STORY`, keep evidence primary, add at most one labeled hypothetical analogy or concrete moment, and pass the truth gate. The intended change is clearer comprehension without turning medical evidence into persuasive anecdote. The fixture fails if a fictional patient appears real, analogy replaces evidence, or causality becomes stronger than the evidence supports.

### Refuse story

A synthetic internal API-migration memo supplies the requested decision, incident count, error-budget status, owner, deadline, options, and tradeoffs. It should route through `NO STORY` to a direct decision-memo owner; only frame, hierarchy, and audience-buy-in pacing may transfer. The result should be faster to understand without a dramatic arc. An invented outage scene, protagonist arc, emotionalized risk, or buried decision fails the fixture.

The detached fixtures and cold-start route test are therefore mandatory, not optional polish.
