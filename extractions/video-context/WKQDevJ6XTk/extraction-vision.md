# Extraction Vision: Matt Haig — The Reader Bridge

## Verdict

Build a **cold, source-grounded Matt Haig skill system** that helps a writer carry a strange, private, emotionally true idea to a broad reader without trend-chasing, literary posturing, or sanding away the life in it.

This is not a “write a bestseller” system. The source does not prove a causal bestseller formula. It does support a coherent craft method for joining personal truth, simple language, emotional transmission, structural editing, reader accessibility, and protected taste.

## Creative Direction

- **Emotional center:** writing that reaches many people without becoming generic, market-chased, over-polished, or false to the writer
- **Deployment target:** generate and edit essays, stories, books, brand narratives, and thought leadership so the reader feels the writer's presence and recognizes something of themselves
- **Latitude:** 3 — Autonomous, inferred from the combined `/watch` + `/extract-forge` + `/source-to-skill-system` invocation
- **Depth:** Mastery, but source-shaped rather than count-shaped
- **Non-negotiable:** preserve Haig's contradictions: private yet accessible; dark yet hopeful; strange yet simple; edited yet alive; ambitious yet self-protective

## The “Only Haig” Insight

**Mass readability is not produced by studying the mass market. It is produced by protecting a personally necessary, slightly strange story and building a clean bridge through which many readers can carry their own feeling.**

Existing Antigravity systems own nearby terrain, but not this whole move:

| Existing owner | Already owns | Haig must not duplicate | Haig adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mitch Albom | theme-first emotional architecture and restraint | universal truth, earned feeling, anti-sentimentality | the private-strange-to-broad-reader bridge and market-lag refusal |
| Lamott / Allen | messy first drafts, truth, plain force, reader trust | permission, humane honesty, reader care | percolation, child-story reset, feeling contagion, and mass accessibility without people-pleasing |

## Composition Rule

- **Function owner:** Matt Haig Reader Bridge owns accessibility without self-erasure.
- **Bounded support:** Mitch Albom may supply theme/restraint; Lamott/Allen may supply truth and reader care; Fresh Voice may supply Farrice-specific voice preservation.
- **Integration rule:** Haig decides whether the private material can travel. Support owners work inside that decision and may not replace the writer's felt verdict.
- **Skipped:** generic storytelling stacks, conversion experts, and trend systems do not enter unless a specific downstream artifact requires them.

## Vision Statement

This extraction will let Farrice and future agents take material that is alive but private, strange, dark, guarded, or hard to enter—and make it easy for a reader to carry without making it generic. It adds a missing middle layer between emotional-story architecture and line-level polish: **the bridge that preserves the writer while inviting the reader in.**
