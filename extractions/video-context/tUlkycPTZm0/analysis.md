## Durable Mechanics

1. **Role ladder:** creative strategy generates and briefs concepts and reads analytics; content operations adds scheduling, people, and vendors; creative direction owns the aesthetic through-line, campaigns, brand meaning, collaborators, merchandising, and the wider world.
2. **World over account:** the founder, team, creators, UGC, influencers, and customers collectively carry more marketing force than the core brand account alone, so creative direction must enable the whole world.
3. **Enablement economics:** mailers, locations, tools, and production choices should be judged by whether they increase participation and produce reusable content, not by polish alone.
4. **Personal Brand 2.0:** authority, career growth, teaching, power, and intellectual influence require a coherent personality and narrative, not attention-only lifestyle display.
5. **Biweekly conversational mining:** every two weeks, debate niche news, recent life, camera-roll history at one/three/five-year intervals, outlier formats, and AI suggestions; capture the conversation and tier the resulting ideas.
6. **Content grammar:** build from recurring topics, recurring formats, and stories about events, people, and products.
7. **Distribution as world surface:** social networks, merchandise, events, collaborations, physical spaces, and launches are connected expression surfaces, not isolated channels.
8. **Creative-direction toolkit:** sets, lighting, visual systems, typography, series lockups, styling, collaborators, and campaign sequencing translate the world into repeatable content.
9. **Core-skill prerequisite:** a designer, editor, copywriter, or other maker earns creative-direction leverage by layering strategy, briefing, and operations over a real execution craft.
