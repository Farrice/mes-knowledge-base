# Source Analysis — Jun Yuh Selling Course

Source: [How To Sell Absolutely Anything Online](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6r-HF9K030A) by Jun Yuh, 1:04:21. Evidence status is `VERIFIED AGAINST SOURCE` for the mechanics below and `SOURCE-REPORTED` for Jun's business results.

## Decision-changing evidence

| Time | Evidence | System consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 21:24–23:59 | Information alone does not create connection; stories build identification, trust, and belief. The slide at 23:17 states that storytelling sells the reason to care. | Keep story as an identification layer, not a universal replacement for direct evidence. |
| 34:12–36:35 | Selling stories use Problem → Pursuit → Payoff. Problem creates relevance, Pursuit carries the method, and Payoff demonstrates a supported change. | Protect Pursuit as the transferable-IP bridge. |

| 43:23–44:47 | Three prompts recover method: repeated behavior no longer noticed, step-by-step advice to a friend with the exact problem, and what was wrong before it became right. Specific answers sound like method; vague answers sound like advice. | Make specificity and repeatability explicit gates. |
| 47:42–51:52 | One moment can yield distinct mental, spiritual, physical, and relationship angles; each angle gets its own 3P, and one Payoff can seed the next Problem. | Add an evidence-bound angle expander, distinct from hook-permutation ideation. |

## Interpretation boundary

The source supports a useful causal sequence: lived pursuit → articulated method → offer hypothesis. It does not support: story → market demand, framework naming → product-market fit, or payoff anecdote → customer result. The connected system therefore stops at `OFFER_HYPOTHESIS` and hands validation to `/ml-validate-offer` before packaging or launch work.
