## Evolution Council Verdict

The skeptic's strongest failure case is an eloquent story being promoted to an offer despite a vague Pursuit and no buyer event. The smallest defensible change is two internal branches with explicit state transitions, not a new command or expert. The no-regression condition is that Jun remains upstream material owner, Shaan remains cross-domain dosage owner, and market validation remains independent.
