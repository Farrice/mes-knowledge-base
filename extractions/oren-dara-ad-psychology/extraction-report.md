## The Six Tactics (spine of the skill)

### 1. Lean Into the Taboo (Dara-led) — psychology: **mirroring + safe space**
- Not shock value: "really think about your customers' innermost secret thoughts and how certain creators can actually create more of that community in a safe space for them to actually say the thing that they don't really feel like they should say." [00:59]
- Exemplar: **Grüns** — organic inspiration: creator, "How life feels after having the most waist-slimming diarrhea episode" (frame t=01:55) → the paid yapper: "For the girls and madams who need to poop… I take Grüns every single day. Scouts honor." [02:49]
- Oren's mechanism: **mirroring** — "our lives, our group chats, the way we talk with our friends, are significantly more raw and niche than what you can put online… when you see it, it feels really honest." [03:24–03:46]
- Comments are the product: "it's actually what's going on in the comments… you're saying the thing that I wish I could say" [02:24]
- Taboo category map (Oren): politics ("red pill tallow brand"), spirituality, health, money-honesty ("I blew my money on this purchase") [04:53–05:21]
- **Failure mode (frame-verified)**: URO statics — "We are having sex all over the house" (t=05:55) and "My vagina feels like silk" (t=06:22). Dara's veto: "I actually don't think a woman who has this problem would ever think to say that. I could see a young male creative strategist looking through the reviews and being like, that's the one." [05:57–06:06] → the **golden-nugget review trap**.
- Done right, adjacent: yoga-pants ad, husband slapping wife's ass casually — "It's not about sex, it's about attention… the dynamic… deeper level." [06:37–06:51]
- Vulnerability rule (Dara): real vs forced — "a lot of people use this mask of authenticity… forcing the vulnerability… that's when you get the cringe feeling." [04:06–04:23]

### 2. Selfish Desires / Justification Math (Oren-led) — psychology: **purchase justification**
- Anchor: Patek Philippe "you don't own a Patek Philippe, you merely carry it on for the next generation" — "how can I justify someone blowing absolute money on a watch… all that does is check a box in a certain type of guy's or girl's mind that says, yeah, cool, I can justify this purchase." [07:04–07:23]
- Modern: girl math / boy math ads — "it's only $6 a brush… on the ads it's just giving people a justified reason to purchase." [07:34–07:57]
- Dara's live case: luxury bed sheets, $400+ AOV — broke cost to "$1 per week"; paired with the inverse: **"We're not cheap because we don't want to be"** — objection handling that doubles as a status filter: "if you can't afford it, that's kind of the point… we actually don't want to cater to the person who wants to buy $30 polyester sheets." [08:00–08:56]
- Every price point has an angle (Oren, masstige): pinned toolkit message — **"Expensive doesn't always equal better."** [09:01–09:24]

### 3. The Armchair Investigation (Dara-led) — psychology: **unfairness + curiosity + virtue activation**
- Format: true-crime-structured comparison. The ad: "same car, same problem, three different mechanics… First shop… O2 sensor, quoted me $480… Second shop… $1,800 for the same car." (frames t=11:56, 12:33) [11:53–12:18]
- **100% AI** persona, deliberately cast as "a younger woman who looked like the daughters of our core demographic" → opened delivery to women (demographic puncture). [12:25, 13:56–14:22]
- Why it works: "It works really well because people are getting infuriated in the comments" — rage-bait as designed engagement anchor. [12:46–12:57]
- **Virtue mining**: "the big virtue that we got from this ad specifically from the comments was how obsessed our audience was with honesty… people putting Bible verses in there… we launched some of those [honesty-leaning] ads yesterday and they're already starting to spike." [13:21–13:52]
- Audience overlap discovery: core audience × true crime — found by accident, now replicated deliberately. [10:02–10:18]
- Oren's generator: "unfairness is just such a massive one in every walk of life. Nepotism… make a scenario around it… draw from true crime, soap opera, dramas… go study what people chose to watch in that demographic on TV 10 years ago." [14:37–15:18]
- Flywheel rule (Dara): "we don't look at our competitor ads anymore. We only look at what our audience loves on organic and try to replicate more of those formats." [15:20–15:31]

### 4. Prove It (Oren-led) — psychology: **claim → demonstration in-frame**
- Anchor: Shot on iPhone (frame t=16:35) — "you're proving how good the iPhone camera is." Executive-translation move: "how do I grab something they might know? … this is the principle we're using for this social media thing." [16:22–16:40]
- Method: "how do I within a 60-second prove the value proposition?" — cosmetics: 24-hour-hold vlogs, eating with makeup on, viral-LA-food carrier ("you get that visual hook of the amazing food. You're proving the makeup doesn't crease"). [16:47–17:22]
- Real case (Dara): **Laura Geller** ad built from a review by a chef in Arizona — makeup lasts in heat + kitchen — "did really really well for that audience." [17:23–17:45]
- Extreme-circumstance variant: sharpest chef knife × 400 tomatoes — "that hype factor you see on organic." [17:46–18:02]
- Brainstorm toolkit line (Oren): "make a list of these principles… put it into a Claude: what are taboos for our target about this thing?" [18:04–18:26]

### 5. Personified Us-vs-Them + Visual Psychology (Dara-led) — psychology: **comparison leveling + eye mechanics**
- Exemplar (frame t=18:50): creator comparison — **Kettle & Fire vs Nutra Organics** bone broth: "Tell me this doesn't just look like dirty dishwater… blind taste test with my daughter." [18:46–19:07]
- Legal line: cleared because "we're not technically making any claims about the product. It was opinion-based." [19:16–19:24]
- The deeper point is VISUAL: "creative strategists will focus on the messaging or the written hook and not as much on what can you do psychologically visually." [20:04–20:15]
- **Use of multiples**: stacked product "is one of those really easy hacks that stops the scroll"; the discovered value prop was **space savings** (boxes vs one jar), above taste. [19:45–20:03, 20:51–21:05]
- **Grid formats** (title card t=20:30): "your eye actually trails in a Z or F format. So it has this natural hooking effect." [20:30–20:38]
- Domain transfer (Oren): fashion = fit-of-the-tee comparisons; any pain point can be visualized. [21:05–21:12]

### 6. The Credible Explainer (Oren-led) — psychology: **authority + scarcity of expert access**
- The uncomfortable truth: "with so much noise and so much advice, people just want to be told what to do. And people will never admit it… they just want someone credible to just tell them the answer." [23:47–23:56]
- Casting: real doctor/scrubs (Dara: "Do not put on a white coat" — no fake authority), degrees, artisans. **Authority chain mining**: "Do you have a board? Do you have investors? People inside really technical roles with any bit of charisma? Inside your warehouse or factory" — the bag-brand artisan, grizzled hands, "Why this stitch is better" (frame t=26:05). [24:44–26:09]
- **Set as credibility**: "if that person's talking in a car or doing it like UGC, that's not there. Put them in the office, the warehouse, the lab — it immediately has that visual credibility." [26:13–26:22]
- Scarcity driver: "the average doctor's visit in the US is like 11 minutes… you never get as much info and time to engage as you want" → a brand whose expert answers comments becomes the anchor. [24:36–24:54]
- **Organic→ads pipeline**: establish the person on organic first, then put them in ads — "people in your ASC likely already have seen their content organically… those begin to be really high performers." [25:02–25:19]
- Dara's frame: authority ads = the trust layer; founder slides in first, then "an outside verified check mark." Founder-warehouse content: Zach Stuck / Hollow Socks top performer, ran ~a year (speaker-attributed, UNCONFIRMED externally). [25:20–26:34]

## Dara's 6-Part Creative-Strategy Process [21:24–23:30]

1. **Research** — reputation analysis ("how the general public feels about my brand"), customer reviews, persona mapping.
2. **Briefing & concepting** — angles and specific concepts pulled from the research.
3. **Roadmapping** — mapped against what's already in the ad account; correct the drift: "they're defaulting really heavy to lower funnel. We need to open up more top of funnel… problem-aware or even unaware type creatives."
4. **Production** — weekly sprints, fixed creative count per brand.
5. **QA**.
6. **Analytics** — Motion (she's chief evangelist — sponsor-adjacent, note bias); daily/weekly/monthly reports; **winner-softening watch**: for the hero ad, daily — "are results starting to soften? Is CTR starting to decrease? Are hook and hold rates starting to soften?"

## Genius Patterns (10)

1. **Inner-Monologue Sourcing** — every concept starts from something the customer already thinks/says privately (group chat, review, comment), never from a brand message. Success metric: a customer could mistake the line for their own thought.
2. **Golden-Nugget Review Veto** — a review only qualifies as a hook if the actual customer would *say it out loud in their world*; run the lived-experience test (URO failure). Amateur tell: the review that excites the strategist most.
3. **Comment-Section-as-Strategy** — comments are simultaneously the community payoff (taboo), the engagement anchor (rage-bait), and next quarter's messaging research (virtue mining).
4. **Justification-as-Checkbox** — the buyer already wants it; the ad hands them the excuse (girl math, $1/week, heirloom framing). Don't sell desire; license it.
5. **Anchor-to-Iconic Translation** — explain any new tactic to executives via an iconic ad they already know (Patek, Shot on iPhone), then map it to the social execution.
6. **Organic→Paid Flywheel** — stop studying competitor ads; study what YOUR audience loves organically, replicate the format in paid, feed learnings back.
7. **Demographic Puncture Casting** — cast the persona (human or AI) as the adjacent segment you want delivery to reach (the daughters of your core demo), and let the algorithm follow the face.
8. **Set-as-Credibility Staging** — the environment does the authority work before a word lands: lab, warehouse, workshop, office. Car/UGC kills expert credibility.
9. **Proof-in-Frame Compression** — restate the claim as something demonstrable inside 60 seconds, ideally riding an organic trend carrier (viral food, extreme circumstance).
10. **Awareness-Balance Roadmapping** — audit the live account for funnel drift (default = lower-funnel), deliberately re-open TOF with problem-aware/unaware creatives.

## Hidden Knowledge (7)

- **The taboo is a gift, not a grenade**: the test is "does the customer feel *seen and safe*," not "is this edgy." Shock-for-shock is the amateur inversion.
- **Rage-bait is load-bearing**: the mechanic ad "doesn't necessarily work because it's such amazing compelling content. It works really well because people are getting infuriated in the comments." Comment fury is designed, not accidental.
- **AI disclosure calculus**: "we knew for the demographic they would not be able to tell — and they haven't." AI casting is a demographic decision, and craft still matters ("well done AI," native fonts/formats).
- **Virtues > desires for iteration**: the comments revealed a *virtue* (honesty) stronger than any feature; virtue-led iterations spiked within a day.
- **Both-ends price play**: the same brand runs "$1 per week" AND "We're not cheap because we don't want to be" — justification for the objectors, status filter for the aspirationals. Not a contradiction; a segmentation.
- **Credibility is scavenged, not hired**: every company already contains authority (board, investors, technical staff, artisans) — "you'll be surprised where you can find in the chain that credibility."
- **Spec-work as career leverage**: "pick a brand, write out some scripts… go to that next interview and say I wrote out this ad for you" — the same toolkit doubles as a hiring/pitch wedge.

## Signature Moves

- **Female-Gaze Veto** (Dara): before any taboo/vulnerability concept ships, ask "would a woman with this problem actually say this?" → **Deploy when**: any intimate/health/body category concept.
- **Break-It-To-A-Dollar** (Dara): reframe AOV as $/week over ownership life → **Deploy when**: price is the #1 objection and AOV ≥ ~$100.
- **Study-Their-Old-TV** (Oren): pull formats from what the demo watched 10 years ago (Maury, soap opera, true crime) → **Deploy when**: scenario/drama ad ideation.
- **Mine-The-Chain** (Oren): walk board/investors/technical staff/factory for a credible face → **Deploy when**: brand says "we can't do authority ads."
- **Morning Softening Check** (Dara): daily hero-ad check on CTR, hook rate, hold rate → **Deploy when**: any ad is scaling.
- **Anchor-to-Iconic** (Oren): open every exec explanation with a legendary ad → **Deploy when**: selling a tactic upward.
