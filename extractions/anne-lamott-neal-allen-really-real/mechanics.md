# Mechanics - Really Real Writing

## 1. The Reader Must Want The Next Sentence
Start with grammar, then ask whether the sentence creates forward desire. Replace dull verbs, soft transitions, abstract nouns, and status language with words that move.

## 2. Realness Is Not Rawness
The goal is not dumping emotion. The goal is truthful selection: what should the reader be trusted with, what should be withheld, and what should be made clear enough to hold?

## 3. Compassion Beats Performance
Snark, irony, loftiness, and cleverness are often defenses. The craft move is to find the human thing underneath and write from there.

## 4. Plain Words Carry Weight
When the draft reaches for fancy abstraction, test a shorter, more bodily word. The plain word often lands closer to the nerve.

## 5. Cut Defensive Overproof
Much boring prose exists because the writer does not trust the reader. Remove the second explanation, the apology, the padded transition, and the extra proof where the point is already felt.

## 6. Use Music As Diagnosis
- Melody: surprise, metaphor, bright leaps, curiosity.
- Rhythm: sentence movement, breath, read-aloud ease.
- Harmony: the felt relationship between writer and reader.

## 7. Hard Stuff Is A Depth Signal
When syntax fails, the writer may be near something important. Do not flee the hard subject too quickly. Draft badly, then revise toward clearer truth.

## 8. Silence Lets The Reader Participate
Omission is not vagueness. It is the deliberate space where a reader fills the emotional connection. Use it only after the piece has enough clarity to be trusted.

## 9. Endings Must Be Emotionally Fair
Do not over-explain, moralize, or fake closure. End when the reader has been brought to the true recognition the piece has earned.

## 10. Editors Protect Blind Spots
Truth deepens through trusted outside attention. A good edit improves more than it damages and shows love for the work, not dominance over the writer.
