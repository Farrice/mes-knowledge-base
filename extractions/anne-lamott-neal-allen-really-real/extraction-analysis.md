# Extraction Analysis - Anne Lamott + Neal Allen Really Real Writing

## Extraction Verdict
Build as a dedicated companion skill. The source is not only "Lamott craft" and not only "sentence rules." Its power comes from the fusion:

1. Neal Allen protects the reader's attention at the sentence level.
2. Anne Lamott protects the reader's humanity at the soul level.
3. Together they define a depth pass where realness is earned through clarity, compassion, plainness, rhythm, hard truth, and restraint.

## Core Capability
This package should answer one question:

> Does this writing feel true enough, clear enough, and humane enough for a real reader to keep going?

It is best used as a depth layer after a draft exists or when the writing goal explicitly asks for resonance, heart, trust, voice, or emotional truth.

## What To Extract
- Reader continuation mechanics: every sentence should make the next sentence feel wanted.
- Compassion-first targeting: write away from snark, loftiness, and performance.
- Plain-force language: choose shorter direct words when abstraction weakens contact.
- Anti-overproving: remove explanations the reader can feel or infer.
- Hard-stuff permission: write the material that first resists articulation.
- Musical self-knowledge: notice whether a draft fails in melody, rhythm, or harmony.
- Collaborative blind-spot repair: outside readers help the writer see what ownership cannot.

## What Not To Extract
- Do not create a generic "write like Anne Lamott" imitation engine.
- Do not make all writing slower, heavier, memoir-like, or confessional.
- Do not replace conversion structure, platform strategy, plot architecture, or client clarity.
- Do not overuse source terminology in outputs. The user should feel the craft, not see the scaffolding.

## System Shape
The package is a skill plus workflows:
- Flagship command: `/really-real-writing`
- Audit: `/really-real-audit`
- Rewrite: `/really-real-rewrite`
- Trust: `/really-real-reader-trust`
- Source material: `/really-real-attention`
- Restraint: `/really-real-silence`
- Stakes: `/really-real-oh-no`
- Ending: `/really-real-ending`
- Domain adapters: social, marketing, book, client

## Quality Bar
A successful run makes the draft:
- less performative
- more specific
- clearer to the reader
- more emotionally fair
- more alive at the sentence level
- more willing to name the hard thing without theatrics
- lighter when the medium needs lightness
