TARGET STATE: anger — at invisible loss, not at a competitor.

**SETUP (indirect vector):**
"I want to tell you about a grocery store in my neighborhood. Family-run, thirty years. Best hot sauce selection in the city — the owner would tell you that, and he'd be right. Two years ago a distributor redid the planogram, and his best sellers moved to the bottom shelf, ankle height, behind an endcap. Nobody told him. His products were still in the store. Still great. Still priced right. And his numbers just... sagged. For eight months he blamed the economy. The products weren't losing. The SHELF was losing. He never saw it because he still walked past his own products every day — at eye level, because he knew where to look.

**(direct vector):**
Now let me ask you something. This morning, somebody asked ChatGPT what creatine actually works for women over 40. Somebody asked which greens powder isn't just marketing. Go ahead — type your own category in right now, I'll wait. Did your brand come up? Do you even know?

**PAYOFF:**
That's the bottom shelf. That's where you are while you're spending forty grand a year on content that ranks on a Google page fewer of your buyers open every month. You're not losing to a better product. You're not even losing to better marketing. You're losing to a shelf you've never once looked at. And the brands getting recommended in those answers right now? Most of them didn't earn it. They're just standing at eye level because nobody else in your category showed up yet.

**TIE-DOWN:**
That's not really acceptable to you, is it? So here's what I want from you before we go one step further. If I showed you exactly where your brand sits on that invisible shelf — every answer, every category question that matters to your buyers — would you commit to actually looking at it? Even if it stings? Because I can't fix a shelf you won't look at."
