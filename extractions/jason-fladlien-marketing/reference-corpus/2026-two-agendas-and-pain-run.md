# Reference Piece — The Two Agendas Opener + The Confidence Paradox Pain Run (verbatim)

**Source**: "How To Make $250,000,000 Online | Jason Fladlien" (Charlie Morgan Business, YouTube SvKEwpVkzaU, published 2026). Both segments are Fladlien delivering his own working scripts in-interview — real published spoken copy, not paraphrase.

---

## Piece A — The Two Agendas Opener (invented 2008, still his standard)

"I have two agendas today. My first agenda is to sell you something at the end of this training. And if you're the right fit for it, then you should buy it. And if you're not the right fit, then I don't want you to buy it. So I absolutely positively know that if you're the right person, buying what I sell at the end is going to be better than not buying it.

And then my second agenda is to give you the very best ever training and experience that you've ever had on the topic at hand. Okay? And then if you don't want to buy, you don't have to buy. But this is my goal — to give you the very best when it comes to [topic]. And if you're the right fit, sell you a product at the end that you'll find more expensive not to own than to own.

Does that sound good to you?"

*(His own annotation: "It wasn't that elegant when I first did it... essentially I just told them: I have two agendas today. One is to give you the best training on the topic, and the second thing is to sell you a product. And the audience was like, okay, cool. Sounds good.")*

## Piece B — The Confidence Paradox Pain Run (webinar pain section)

"Hey, listen. You don't think you have the skills in order to make this happen. And you definitely don't have the money to make this happen because you're broke, right? And god forbid this concept called confidence — this is elusive to you. The only thing you're confident about is your lack of confidence. Your time is so limited — even though you're here on this webinar right now — but somehow you don't have any time, right? And everybody's telling you to do things that confuse you. And you're afraid that if you screw this up, that you're going to feel like a terrible, miserable failure.

[audience pushes back: 'I'm not that bad, Jason.']

Are you sure you're not that bad? You're not a hopeless case? Maybe there's hope for you after all. I don't know. I'm skeptical. But I'll entertain the notion that maybe there's a shred of hope for you."

*(His own annotation: "You're only confident in your lack of confidence — what I've done there is actually demonstrate to them that they have the capacity to be confident... I'm undermining the current identity and making it hard for them to hold on to it. It's less about changing them to a different identity — it's more about making it hard for them to hold on to the same identity.")*
