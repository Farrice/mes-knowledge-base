# Reference Piece — The Marshmallow Save (live spoken copy, verbatim)

**Source**: "How To Make $250,000,000 Online | Jason Fladlien" (Charlie Morgan Business, YouTube SvKEwpVkzaU, published 2026; retold by Fladlien as delivered live on an Iman Gadzhi challenge, day one, ~60,000 concurrent viewers, hostile chat). Real published spoken copy — Fladlien performing his own methodology, captured near-verbatim in his retelling.

---

"Hey, listen. I want to tell you a little bit about a study that if you truly understand this, this can completely change your life.

Years ago, Stanford did this marshmallow experiment where they put these little kids in front of marshmallows and they said, 'If you eat it now, that's all you get. But if you wait 15 minutes, we'll give you two of them.' And then they watched these kids grow up through life and they tracked them — and the kids that could delay gratification for just a little bit became very successful later on in life. But the kids who couldn't delay gratification did not become successful later in life.

And then they asked a question: could we train this? Could we teach this? So they did another test and they took the kids that would eat the marshmallow right away, they taught them how to delay gratification for just a little bit, and then they measured their success later on in life — and it turned out they were just as successful as the kids that could delay gratification immediately.

Let me ask you a question. Your favorite movie. Think of it. Now, if you just skipped to the last five minutes and watched it, do you think it would still be your favorite movie?

Listen, I want to get right down to it as fast as anybody does. I want the answer immediately with no effort or no work involved in it. But I've discovered in my life that if I can delay gratification just for a little bit — not forever, but just for a little bit — that serves me the best.

When you show up in this way, you have to ask yourself a question. Does this really serve you? Do you think that if you continue to operate this way in your life, that this is going to get you the things that you want? Or do you think this is going to get in the way of what you want?

Okay — let's get down to business. Because I am here to help you do things in your life that are going to serve you better. So let's talk about what could serve you better."
