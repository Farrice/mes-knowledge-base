## Post-Approval Verification Receipt

The verifier distinguishes:

- `PASS_INCREMENTAL_BEHAVIOR`: content thresholds pass with trusted `RUNTIME_OBSERVED` provenance; registration may be considered.
- `PASS_DIAGNOSTIC_BEHAVIOR`: content thresholds pass but provenance is only orchestrator/operator attested; registration remains blocked.
- `FAIL_NO_REGISTRATION`: any content, acceptance, fidelity, fairness, or integrity condition fails.

The current desktop worker surface is frozen at `ORCHESTRATOR_ATTESTED`, so formal route-unlocking proof cannot be produced here. Generation is held rather than spending a large test wave on evidence that the approved registration policy cannot accept.

## Implementation Resolution

Architecture approval exposed four details that the design documents had left implicit. They are now locked for the build:

1. `transcript_segments.json` has no native IDs. The machine ledger derives zero-based IDs in the form `UFNlbNa2T4w:s000000` and pins the transcript, VTT, and canonical-ledger SHA-256 values so regenerated captions cannot silently move an anchor.
2. Spoken, visual, and synthesized rows use separate schema branches. Spoken rows require exact segment boundaries; visual rows require a frame path and timestamp; synthesized rows require observed anchor-row IDs.
3. Human ledger rows that mix an observed method with an illustrative, third-party, or self-reported fact split into atomic machine rows. One row never carries two truth classes.
4. The immutable RelayNote input fixture is frozen before generation. Route receipts, label maps, and evaluator scores are created afterward in a separately hash-committed run-evidence bundle.
5. Acceptance decisions are parsed from exact output markers and checked against frozen routes and forbidden behavior. Receipt-supplied `observed` and `pass` values are not evidence.
6. Intermediate Swipe Packets, Promise Cards, method audits, and initial-to-final revisions must be structurally non-empty and hash-bound to the same replicate.
7. Method influence requires allowlisted Kyle-observed source rows plus substantive same-replicate spans across at least two replicates; arbitrary mechanic names and same-file deltas fail.
8. Final-bundle hashes prove integrity, not chronology or actual reads. Registration therefore requires `RUNTIME_OBSERVED` provenance; the current runtime can produce diagnostic evidence only.

The comparison baseline is also made output-equivalent. Arm B uses the nearest existing Luke lead-production workflow plus Matthew's audit, then obeys the same three-opening output contract as Arm C. A mass-hook generator is not used as the comparator because its native deliverable is materially different.

## Workflow Contracts

### 1. `531-swipe-discipline`

- **Source anchor:** 24:42–25:12; reinforced at 58:58–59:54.
- **Inputs:** offer facts, audience, desired action, mechanism or opportunity facts, five accessible comparable promotions, and provenance.
- **Procedure:** score relevance, read five, map claims/proof/argument for three, choose one primary, and separate transferable structure from non-transferable facts and wording.
- **Output:** five-source relevance map, three breakdowns, one primary swipe, and borrowing boundary.
- **Stop:** fewer than five relevant sources; unknown offer or mechanism; unavailable provenance; regulated evidence without review.
- **Handoff:** compact Swipe Packet to `unique-promise-spine`.

### 2. `unique-promise-spine`

- **Source anchor:** 10:19–16:07; simplicity reinforced at 61:21–61:55.
- **Inputs:** Product Truth Packet, Swipe Packet, verified mechanism or catalyst, supported result, and audience.
- **Procedure:** classify mechanism-led or opportunity-led, extract the durable force or recurring pattern, connect it to the result, reject competing promises, and run NESB restraint.
- **Output:** one Promise Card with rejected alternatives and evidence gaps.
- **Stop:** backward-invented mechanism; unverified catalyst; unsupported result; unresolved promise bundle.
- **Handoff:** valid card to the opening builder; uncertain mechanism to Luke.

### 3. `four-beat-opening-builder`

- **Source anchor:** 51:37–51:43 and 66:48–67:04; additional pattern-interrupt discussion later in the reviews.
- **Inputs:** Promise Card, primary swipe, audience, format, voice constraints, verified credibility, and demonstrated-result evidence.
- **Procedure:** choose a researched interrupt, state a consequential claim, attach relevant credibility, demonstrate the result, produce three candidates, and recommend one. Credibility and results may swap.
- **Output:** annotated three-opening set and recommendation.
- **Stop:** no result proof; implied endorsement; adjective escalation; unsupported move.
- **Handoff:** recommendation to `first-four-lines-audit`.
- **Naming veto:** never call this the Four-Punches builder. Four Punches is the broader precedent doctrine.

### 4. `first-four-lines-audit`

- **Source anchor:** 03:24–03:30, 06:03–06:11, and 65:10–65:56.
- **Inputs:** first four lines, audience, offer, context, desired action, and intended promise.
- **Procedure:** map each line's job, test why here, why this speaker, what is in it for the reader, promise clarity, and reason to continue.
- **Output:** `ADVANCE`, `REPAIR`, or `EXIT/RESTART`, plus the first exact failure.
- **Stop:** incomplete context; idea-level failure; fewer than four lines.
- **Handoff:** to workflow 3, 2, 7, or 8; on pass, to the existing body-copy owner.

### 5. `thumbtack-continuity-audit`

- **Source anchor:** 71:48–72:20; repeated around 96:37–96:54.
- **Inputs:** one 4–12-line section, its job, and controlling promise.
- **Procedure:** name the thumbtack concept, record what each sentence inherits and advances, mark broken strings, and prescribe the smallest repair.
- **Output:** Sentence Dependency Map with deletion, reorder, or bridge actions.
- **Stop:** no controlling concept; scope exceeds one section; the promise itself is broken.
- **Handoff:** repaired opener to workflow 4; full-copy flow to Matthew; rhythm-only issue to Sam.

### 6. `proof-texture-dimensionalizer`

- **Source anchor:** 39:30–44:48, 52:42–55:05, and 90:26–90:52.
- **Inputs:** one claim, expected skepticism, proof ledger, exact facts, usage constraints, and voice.
- **Procedure:** name the skeptical gap, classify proof form, test truth and relevance, create three to five grounded texture options, and map every detail to evidence.
- **Output:** Credibility Texture Packet with selected option and proof gaps.
- **Stop:** missing or self-reported proof without disclosure; implied endorsement; unverifiable quote or amount; regulated claim without review.
- **Handoff:** to the opening or body owner; full proof architecture to Luke; external use to factual gates.

### 7. `mumbo-jumbo-pruner`

- **Source anchor:** 68:20–68:56 and 95:55–96:21.
- **Inputs:** one to six lines, intended promise or section job, and definitions for every proposed mechanism.
- **Procedure:** identify unresolved constructs, count simultaneous concepts, select one essential concept, define/delete/postpone the rest, and make one minimal repair.
- **Output:** Undefined Concept Register, primary-concept decision, and minimal rewrite.
- **Stop:** unresolved product mechanism; broken top-level promise; request expands into full body copy.
- **Handoff:** to workflow 4 or 5; mechanism uncertainty to Luke.

### 8. `negative-space-copy-chief`

- **Source anchor:** Matthew's repair taxonomy at 09:21–10:03; Kyle's missing-move diagnosis at 98:32–99:50.
- **Inputs:** draft or excerpt, Product Truth Packet, intended promise, desired action, proof ledger, and domain constraints.
- **Procedure:** inspect idea/promise, argument, section job, and line language; separate weak present moves from missing moves; preserve what works; select one highest-priority failure and one owner.
- **Output:** Copy-Chief Repair Order with preservation lock, prohibited lower-level work, and one dispatch packet.
- **Stop:** missing context or proof; two failures cannot be prioritized without a human choice; one dispatch has already been issued.
- **Handoff:** one Kyle procedure or one existing owner, then one recheck.
- **Orchestration veto:** never run all eight automatically.

## Behavior-Proof Architecture

### Fixture Contract to Freeze Before Running

`RelayNote` is a fictional post-call follow-up tool for 5–20-person agencies. The fixture provides only these synthetic facts:

- 30-day test across 12 agencies and 1,248 calls;
- 23% of assigned next steps absent from first manual follow-ups;
- median drafting time of 18 minutes manually and 4 minutes with RelayNote;
- 91% owner/date extraction accuracy on a held-out 200-call fixture;
- no revenue, conversion, closed-won, or live-market result.

Before the fixture is called frozen, it must contain:

- exact Product Truth IDs for every capability and number;
- the exact weak draft below;
- full text and synthetic performance labels for five control swipes;
- a locked breakdown set of `S2`, `S1`, and `S3`;
- `S2`, the agency-specific transcript-to-follow-up control, as the primary swipe even if a less relevant control has a higher synthetic score;
- a prohibition against transferring control brands, metrics, source-video facts, Kyle credentials, or finance/health examples;
- model/version, run settings, output contract, and context packet;
- randomized evaluator-label rules and the post-run receipt schema.

Every file carries `Fixture data. Not market evidence.`

Exact weak draft:

> Revenue teams are under pressure to move faster. RelayNote uses AI-powered conversation intelligence and workflow automation to help agencies optimize follow-up and improve pipeline efficiency. Our platform turns calls into action items and emails. Book a demo today.

### Three Arms

- **Arm A:** original weak draft.
- **Arm B:** `luke-iha-vsl-leads/vsl-lead-writer` plus Matthew's existing copy audit, with all Kyle ledger and workflow material excluded.
- **Arm C:** Kyle build mode under the same facts, model, and output contract.

Run three cold-start replicates for Arms B and C and compare medians. Randomize labels before evaluation and hide route receipts until scoring ends.

### Pass Bar

- Arm C scores at least 80/100.
- Arm C beats Arm B by at least 15 points.
- Arm B first clears a 65/100 fairness floor and shows Luke/Matthew load receipts. If it misses, repair and rerun B once before comparison.
- Blind evaluation prefers Arm C in at least two of three paired comparisons.
- Every number matches the fixture ledger.
- No unsupported mechanism, endorsement, revenue, conversion, or causal claim appears.
- At least three Kyle mechanics create visible, explainable changes.
- Attribution and non-shaming tone remain intact.
- The receipt labels the result behavior proof, not market evidence.

Hard failures override the score: unsupported outcomes, copied control facts, invented authority, speaker errors, output that attacks the writer, or any claim that fixture results are market evidence.

Failure means no registration. Keep the source as reference-only or merge only the winning mechanics into existing owners.
