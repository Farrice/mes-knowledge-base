# Pre/Post-Production Operating Blueprint

## Current Flow and Bottleneck

The company does not have a content-volume problem first. It has a founder-decision queue.

Current flow:

1. The founder chooses topics.
2. The founder prepares the studio.
3. The founder records.
4. The long-form editor receives raw footage, but approval criteria are not resolved before editing.
5. The founder approves every edit, creating another queue and avoidable revision cycles.
6. The founder uploads each post.
7. The founder reads analytics, but no defined artifact carries the learning into the next topic or package.
8. The part-time short-form editor and operations assistant work downstream of founder availability rather than from a ready queue.

The overloaded long-form editor is the visible bottleneck. The actual bottleneck is earlier: topic, proof, packaging, studio readiness, and approval standards remain tacit founder decisions. Post-production therefore starts with unresolved inputs, returns to the founder for routine choices, and ends without a decision writeback.

Evidence state:

- Current cadence: inconsistent; no reliable baseline supplied.
- Performance data: not supplied. Any content or cadence hypothesis is **UNTESTED**.
- Buyer signals and proof library: not supplied; the evidence intake stage must expose these gaps rather than fill them with assumptions.
- Publishing permission: not granted. This blueprint creates local handoff artifacts only.

## Pre-Production Pipeline

| Stage | Owner | Input | Output | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Signal intake | Operations assistant | Founder notes, prospect questions, client-safe proof, prior comments or analytics supplied by the team | Weekly signal inbox with source, date, channel, and evidence status | No topic enters planning without a source or an **UNTESTED** label |
| 2. Candidate triage | Founder | Signal inbox | Three ranked topic decisions: make, hold, reject, each with one-line reason | Founder makes one weekly batch decision; no ad hoc topic selection during edit week |
| 3. Trust job and concept brief | Operations assistant | Selected topic, intended audience, available evidence | One-page concept brief naming audience, primary trust job, claim limit, and desired viewer decision | Missing buyer or proof inputs are explicit; the assistant may structure but not invent judgment |
| 4. Packaging | Founder, with long-form editor consulted for feasibility | Concept brief | Locked working title, thumbnail/first-frame direction, first spoken line, and chapter thesis | Title, visual, and opening create one coherent tension; packaging is locked before recording |
| 5. Proof and claim check | Founder | Concept brief and proposed claims | Proof list with allowed wording, limitations, rights status, and unsupported claims removed | Every material claim has supplied support or is cut; no client identity or outcome is implied without permission |
| 6. Recording brief | Operations assistant | Approved package and proof list | Talking-point brief, shot list, file-naming plan, and required inserts | Founder can record from the brief without making new operational decisions |
| 7. Studio readiness | Operations assistant | Shot list and studio SOP | Checked setup sheet: room, gear, media, power, framing reference, and file destination | All checklist items pass before the founder enters the studio; exceptions escalate once, before recording |
| 8. Record | Founder | Ready recording brief and prepared studio | Raw long-form recording plus explicitly marked retakes and proof objects | Founder supplies voice, judgment, proof, and performance—not file management or downstream instructions |
| 9. Production handoff | Operations assistant | Raw media and recording notes | Complete edit packet assigned to the long-form editor | Packet passes the definition of ready; incomplete packets do not enter the edit queue |

## Post-Production Pipeline

| Stage | Owner | Input | Output | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Assembly edit | Long-form editor | Complete edit packet | Structural cut with missing-media and claim flags | Story and argument are coherent before polish; unresolved evidence returns to operations, not silently to the edit |
| 2. Craft edit | Long-form editor | Approved structural cut | Finished long-form cut plus proposed chapters and export notes | Meets the documented pacing, audio, graphics, and brand quality bar |
| 3. Routine quality check | Operations assistant | Finished cut, package, proof list, and checklist | Timestamped QC report covering omissions, naming, rights, and approved claims | Checklist issues return directly to the editor; only judgment exceptions reach the founder |
| 4. Founder exception review | Founder | Finished cut and QC report | Approve, or one consolidated decision note | Founder reviews argument, voice, proof, product, and taste only; no piecemeal comments and no second round unless a flagged exception remains |
| 5. Local publishing handoff | Operations assistant | Approved master, title, description draft, thumbnail brief, captions, rights notes | Channel-ready local handoff folder | No upload, scheduling, connector write, or public action occurs without separate permission |
| 6. Short-form candidate selection | Part-time short-form editor | Approved long-form master, complete-thought map, and—when supplied—observed attention signals | Ranked clip candidate sheet with source interval, complete thought, proposed destination hook, and evidence basis | Without observed retention data, candidates are marked **UNTESTED** and selected for completeness and strategic relevance, not described as “spikes” |
| 7. Short-form edit | Part-time short-form editor | Approved candidate sheet and proof list | Destination-adapted short-form cuts with revised opening | Each cut preserves claim limits, adds destination context, and stands alone without distorting the founder's meaning |
| 8. Short-form QC and exception review | Operations assistant; founder only for flagged judgment | Short-form cuts, source intervals, checklist | Approved local short-form handoff folder | Routine issues stay off the founder's calendar; proof, voice, or taste exceptions escalate in one batch |
| 9. Evidence capture | Operations assistant | Performance exports, audience responses, buyer events, and publishing records supplied after authorized publication | Weekly evidence sheet separating attention, retention, buyer response, and commercial events | Missing data remains blank; reach is never relabeled as trust, calls, deposits, or revenue |
| 10. Decision writeback | Founder with operations assistant | Weekly evidence sheet and open evidence gaps | Keep / cut / test / evidence-needed decisions written into the next signal inbox | The review must change a concept, package, proof need, repurposing choice, or audience hypothesis; otherwise it is not a review |

## Founder vs Delegated Decisions

| Decision or task | Accountable owner | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Topic portfolio and strategic priority | Founder | Decided once per weekly batch from a prepared signal inbox |
| Voice, point of view, product judgment, and proof permission | Founder | Cannot be delegated; must be expressed as reusable criteria after each exception |
| Title, first visual, opening line, and chapter thesis | Founder | Founder locks the package before recording; editors may propose options, not silently choose positioning |
| Studio setup, file handling, checklists, and handoff assembly | Operations assistant | Delegated after the first approved SOP run |
| Long-form structure and craft execution | Long-form editor | Owns within the approved brief and quality bar |
| Short-form adaptation | Part-time short-form editor | Owns only approved source moments; does not invent performance justification |
| Routine QC, metadata preparation, analytics capture, and documentation | Operations assistant | Local preparation and supplied-data capture only; no external writes under current permission |
| Public upload or scheduling | No authorized owner | **NO PERMISSION** until the founder grants a separate publishing action and names the human owner |

Every recurring founder correction becomes one of three things before the next cycle: a checklist item, an example in the quality bar, or an explicit founder-only judgment. If it becomes none of them, the team has created another future approval request.

## Ready / Done / WIP Rules

### Long-form definition of ready

A long-form asset may enter editing only when it has:

- one named audience and one primary trust job;
- a locked title direction, first visual, first spoken line, and chapter thesis;
- an approved proof-and-claim list with rights status;
- a complete talking-point brief and recording notes;
- all raw media in the agreed file structure;
- one accountable editor and one due date;
- no unresolved question that would force the editor to guess the founder's position.

### Long-form definition of done

A long-form asset is operationally done when:

- the master passes craft and routine QC;
- the founder has approved it or resolved the single consolidated exception note;
- the channel-ready local handoff folder is complete;
- short-form candidates have been logged with an honest evidence basis;
- the asset has a future evidence-capture row and decision-writeback destination.

“Done” does not mean published under the current permission state.

### Short-form definition of ready

A clip may enter editing only when it has a source interval, a complete thought, a destination-specific opening, the applicable proof limits, and an **OBSERVED** or **UNTESTED** selection label.

### Short-form definition of done

A clip is done when it passes source-fidelity, claim, caption, rights, and format checks and sits in a local handoff folder. It is not called a performance winner without observed evidence.

### Work-in-progress limits

- Maximum 2 long-form assets in the full system at once: one in pre-production/recording and one in post-production.
- Maximum 1 long-form cut awaiting founder review.
- Maximum 4 short-form cuts in edit or review combined.
- Maximum 1 consolidated founder review block per long-form asset and 1 batched short-form exception block per week.
- No new asset enters a full queue. The owner finishes, parks with a named blocker, or rejects it.

### Capacity-based cadence

No publish count is declared from missing capacity data. For the first two cycles, the operating cadence is:

- one weekly signal-and-topic batch;
- one pre-production lock block;
- one recording block;
- two editor/operations handoffs;
- one batched founder exception review;
- one weekly evidence-and-writeback review using only supplied post-publication data.

After two complete cycles, the team may set a publishing cadence equal to the lower of demonstrated long-form completion capacity and authorized publishing capacity. Any proposed short-form volume remains subordinate to the part-time editor's demonstrated capacity and the WIP limit.

## Review and Escalation Rhythm

| Rhythm | Participants | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly, 30 minutes | Founder + operations assistant | Rank the signal inbox; make / hold / reject; name proof gaps |
| Pre-recording, 20 minutes | Founder + long-form editor + operations assistant | Lock package, proof limits, and recording readiness in one pass |
| Founder review, one batched block | Founder | Resolve only voice, argument, proof, product, and taste exceptions |
| Weekly, 30 minutes after evidence is available | Founder + both editors + operations assistant | Write keep / cut / test / evidence-needed decisions into the next cycle |
| End of each of the first two cycles | Whole team | Measure actual cycle time, revision count, founder review time, and queue breaches; then set sustainable cadence |

Escalate to the founder only when:

- a proposed claim lacks support or permission;
- the edit changes the founder's meaning or product judgment;
- the title/visual/opening no longer match the locked package;
- a rights or privacy risk is unresolved;
- a new request would break a WIP limit or agreed quality bar;
- an external publishing action is requested without explicit permission.

Do not escalate routine file naming, checklist failures, metadata preparation, format corrections, or already-documented craft choices.

| Day | Installation action | Owner | Local proof of completion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Map one recent asset through the current flow and mark every wait, revision, founder touch, and missing input | Operations assistant with team input | Current-flow map and baseline touch-count sheet |
| 2 | Create the signal inbox, concept brief, proof-and-claim list, and make / hold / reject fields | Operations assistant | Blank templates plus one test entry using supplied information only |
| 3 | Founder defines five founder-only decisions and gives two examples of acceptable packaging and two unacceptable ones | Founder | First version of the judgment and quality-bar sheet |
| 4 | Convert studio preparation and file handling into checklists | Operations assistant | Dry-run checklist completed without recording |
| 5 | Run a pre-production lock on one real topic; label missing buyer or proof evidence | Founder + operations assistant + long-form editor | Complete edit packet draft; no filming if readiness fails |
| 6 | Prepare the studio from the checklist and record the ready asset | Operations assistant + founder | Raw media and recording notes in the agreed structure |
| 7 | Long-form editor runs assembly edit only and logs every forced guess | Long-form editor | Structural cut and guess log |
| 8 | Team converts valid guess-log items into brief fields, checklist rules, or founder-only exceptions; editor completes craft cut | Operations assistant + founder + editor | Revised SOP and finished cut |
| 9 | Operations assistant performs routine QC before founder review | Operations assistant | Timestamped QC report and one consolidated exception note |
| 10 | Founder completes one batched exception review; each correction updates the quality bar | Founder | Approve/revise decision plus updated examples |
| 11 | Part-time editor creates up to two **UNTESTED** short-form candidates from complete thoughts | Part-time short-form editor | Candidate sheet and local draft cuts with source intervals |
| 12 | Operations assistant assembles long- and short-form local publishing handoffs; no external action | Operations assistant | Complete local handoff folders and permission status |
| 13 | Rehearse evidence capture and writeback using clearly labeled sample fields, not invented performance values | Operations assistant + founder | Empty evidence sheet, review agenda, and decision fields |
| 14 | Review cycle time, founder minutes, revision count, queue breaches, and unresolved proof gaps; lock the next two-cycle operating cadence | Whole team | Versioned SOP, named capacity constraint, and next-cycle queue |

## Permission Boundary

This blueprint authorizes local planning and local artifact preparation only.

- **Allowed:** local briefs, checklists, SOPs, edit packets, local media review, QC notes, local handoff folders, and analysis of data supplied by an authorized person.
- **Not authorized:** hiring, publishing, uploading, scheduling, connector writes, paid tools, scraping, outreach, or representing hypothetical performance as observed data.
- **Permission gate:** before any external action, record the exact asset, channel, action, human owner, approval source, and time window. Without that record, the action remains **NO PERMISSION**.
