# Dhar Mann Transformational Community Storytelling System

This package converts one Marketing Against the Grain interview, one TheWrap production-operations panel, sparse visual evidence, and two published story transcripts into a source-bounded Antigravity capability.

The new expert owns one distinct job:

> Design content around a viewer after-state, then preserve the package, recognition, consequence, shift, action, and mission as the approved story crosses into production.

It is not a Dhar Mann imitation engine. It does not claim therapy, guaranteed engagement, directing, editing, screenplay, proprietary software, or complete studio-operations expertise.

## What is built

- An expert skill with nine source-earned workflows and ten born-v2 prompts.
- A Dhar Mann agent for transformation, mission, packaging, and community strategy.
- A thin `/dhar-transformational-content-factory` conductor.
- David Bayer mode routing:
  - recognition-you for audience-centered insight,
  - testimony-I for creator-led lived experience.
- Narrative and educational handoffs to existing craft systems rather than copied methodologies.
- A mission and claim gate that separates discovery, retention, connection, and transformation evidence.
- Two real published-work references and a behavior-proof fixture.
- A Story-to-Production Continuity Packet and Production Capacity Learning Loop.
- Three finalized local component-validation receipts with distinct routing records.
- A plugin-readiness verdict with promotion criteria.
