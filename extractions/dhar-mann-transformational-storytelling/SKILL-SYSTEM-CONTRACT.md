# Skill System Contract: Transformational Community Storytelling

## Objective

Turn a raw, provenance-bearing creator thought into a mission-safe content packet that makes a defined audience feel recognized, earns a perspective shift, offers one non-coercive positive action, creates a measurable community feedback loop, and preserves those decisions through production handoffs and capacity learning.

## Dependency rules

1. No content body is authored without a real source or an explicit `[PROVENANCE MISSING]` stop.
2. Packaging cannot proceed without a one-sentence viewer promise.
3. Recognition-you and testimony-I are mutually exclusive body-author modes.
4. Narrative transformation routes to Stanton; educational/cognitive transformation routes to Kobi. Do not load both by default.
5. Kallaway may audit promise match and drop risk only after the transformation spine exists.
6. Kieran Content Ops may store, queue, and analyze results; it never writes the content.
7. Views, retention, connection, and transformation remain separate scoreboards.
8. Production continuity activates only after story structure exists; efficiency cannot become the creative owner.
9. Source-two crew counts, studio counts, set counts, cycle times, and review cadence remain descriptive and may not become defaults.
10. Directing, editing, screenplay, software, casting, budget, legal, labor, and safety gaps remain explicit handoffs.

## Human checkpoint decision

Farrice explicitly requested the expert, operating system, integrations, and full build. Local reversible implementation therefore proceeds after the visible architecture checkpoint. The system still stops before publication, global installation, plugin installation, or fabricated authorship.

## Promotion stop condition

Do not call the system a production-ready portable plugin until all are true:

- at least three independent real creator runs, not merely local source-corpus receipts,
- one clean cold-start run in a fresh task,
- dedicated verifier PASS,
- no unresolved mission/claim failure,
- one Farrice felt verdict on the behavior proof,
- and plugin-readiness score of `IMPROVE FIRST` or better with a documented remediation path.
