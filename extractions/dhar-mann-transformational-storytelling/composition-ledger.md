## Handoff packets

### Viewer Transformation Contract

- starting feeling and belief
- private sentence
- intended after-state
- exact perspective shift
- one safe positive action
- mission value
- harm and claim boundary

### Bayer Mode Packet

- selected mode: recognition-you or testimony-I
- private-recognition lines
- one everyday analogy
- empathetic bridge
- source/provenance path
- preserve list

### Transformation Craft Packet

- route: narrative or educational
- premise or core claim
- intention and obstacle, or belief-before/belief-after
- change moment or aha bridge
- earned ending
- source boundary

### Packaging Integrity Packet

- title/thumbnail promise
- 30-second pitch
- first 10-30 second fulfillment
- tension-to-positive-payoff covenant
- retention-drop risks
- mission-safe verdict

### Factory Run Card

- source and concept
- selected workflow state
- human checkpoint
- body-author route
- published version, if any
- discovery, retention, connection, and transformation signals
- next iteration

### Production Continuity Packet

- provenance-bearing development intake
- concept-to-capacity verdict
- production-unit map
- transformation-invariant map
- department handoff matrix
- freshness and continuity review
- unsupported craft-gap register
- production-lock verdict

### Capacity Learning Packet

- asset topology
- local concurrency assumptions
- bottleneck log
- smallest evidence-supported response
- freshness and transformation veto
- review cadence
- next evidence that can reverse the decision
