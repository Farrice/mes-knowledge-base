## Metric definitions for future runs

- **Discovery:** impressions, click-through rate, direct/search source.
- **Retention:** first-30-second hold, average view duration, marked drop points.
- **Connection:** returning viewers, shares, saves, substantive comments, survey participation.
- **Transformation:** self-reported perspective shift or completed positive action, with collection method stated.
