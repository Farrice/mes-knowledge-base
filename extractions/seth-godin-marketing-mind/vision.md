## What This Layer Is

The existing Godin trio captures his **frameworks**: brand-as-promise (seth-godin-brand), spread mechanics (seth-godin-ideavirus), shipping/resistance (seth-godin-philosophy). None of them captures **how he thinks in real time** — what he does when someone hands him a question. This interview is 61 minutes of exactly that: Mel asks practitioner questions ("how do I apply this Saturday at the farmers market?", "you have to show up consistently, right?") and Seth demonstrates, over and over, a repeatable strategic-reasoning sequence:

1. **Detect the trap in the question** ("What a trap, Mel. What a trap.")
2. **Refuse or replace the premise** ("So, I want to answer a different question and then we're going to come back")
3. **Split the fuzzy word** (entrepreneur vs freelancer, decision vs outcome, hobby vs business, brand vs logo)
4. **Reset to first principles** ("Who's it for and what's it for? If you can't answer... rewind 30 seconds and start over")
5. **Interrogate the measurement** ("Compared to what?")
6. **Ground in a micro-case with an exclusion clause** (curly-hair hairdresser: "If you're bald, don't come")
7. **Convert the emotion into a designed experiment** ($10 bill at the bus station; Girl Scout question redesign)

That sequence — the *marketing mind*, not the marketing frameworks — is the extraction target.
