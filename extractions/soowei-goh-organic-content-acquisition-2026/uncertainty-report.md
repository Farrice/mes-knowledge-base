# Uncertainty Report

## Known limitations

- Captions are YouTube-generated and normalized mechanically. Exact wording may contain transcription errors; raw VTT is preserved for audit.
- The source metadata confirms titles, channel, dates, duration, and source URLs. It does not independently verify business outcomes.
- Revenue, client-value, conversion-share, and view-performance statements remain self-reported.
- The videos teach from SooWei’s own business and audience. Transfer to other categories is a hypothesis until tested.
- The dream-follower percentages are illustrative heuristics from one demonstrated script, not validated universal thresholds.
- Targeted frame capture favors moments already identified from the transcript. It is not an exhaustive visual-style analysis.

## What would change the architecture

- Evidence that another current skill already owns Buyer Mirror-to-content operations end to end would favor a handoff instead of a SooWei expansion.
- A behavior test showing no meaningful improvement over the current skill would reduce the build to a reference package.
- Repeated production receipts showing the same workflow combination is valuable could later justify a public front door; one extraction cannot.
