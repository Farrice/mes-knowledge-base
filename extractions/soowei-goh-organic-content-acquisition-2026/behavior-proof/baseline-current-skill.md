# Baseline — Current SooWei Skill on the Same Fixture

## Route available before expansion

The closest existing route is `high-ticket-conversion-engine`. Applied faithfully, it would produce:

- an ICP and desired-reality map centered on safer, decision-ready content;
- a qualification intake covering budget, authority, readiness, and review constraints;
- a one-page proposal and walkthrough protocol;
- objection-preemption topics such as “how to separate evidence from interpretation” and “why more posting does not solve review risk”;
- a referral moment and founder calendar recommendation.

## What the baseline gets right

- It uses buyer objections before the sales call.
- It avoids direct outcome claims.
- It connects content to qualification and decision facilitation.
- It protects founder time and treats content as leverage.

## What the baseline cannot yet produce

- no six-transition Buyer Mirror attribution map;
- no admirable → likable → credible → trustable diagnosis;
- no explicit broad-to-buyer audience curve;
- no coordinated title, visual, and verbal hook brief;
- no viewer-useful proof-format decision;
- no pre/post-production handoff system;
- no retention-spike repurposing decision;
- no evidence-class separation between 18,000 impressions and zero commercial events.

## Baseline proof state

The route is useful for conversion infrastructure, but it leaves the user to connect buyer evidence, trust, asset craft, operations, and learning manually.
