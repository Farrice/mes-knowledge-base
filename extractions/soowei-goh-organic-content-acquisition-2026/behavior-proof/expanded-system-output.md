Buyer-only destination: marketing leaders at health-performance brands need a repeatable brief that lets creative, science, and legal see the same boundary before production.

## Flagship three-hook brief

- **Title:** “The sentence your content team should challenge before legal does”
- **Visual:** freeze on one highlighted claim inside P1, with the allowed statement and unsupported inference visibly separated.
- **Verbal:** “This sentence sounds responsible. One missing boundary turns it into a claim your evidence may not support.”
- **Proof state:** P1/P2 are synthetic fixture artifacts; no real brand result is claimed.
- **Viewer value:** a three-question boundary check: What does the source observe? What are we inferring? Who owns final review?
