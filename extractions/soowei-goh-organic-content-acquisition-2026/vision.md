# SooWei Organic Content Acquisition — Extraction Vision

## Verdict

These two sources should expand the existing `soowei-consulting-leverage` capability. They do not justify a second SooWei agent or an independent content mega-skill.

The existing system already owns Buyer Mirror, objection preemption, founder leverage, content-as-business-oxygen, and operating-system design. The new evidence fills a narrower gap: turning those principles into a repeatable organic content acquisition system spanning audience architecture, trust formation, proof formats, production, repurposing, and learning.

## What the sources add

### Source 1 — the operating model

`Y388trCakrs` explains the business system beneath the content:

- Trust is built in sequence: admirable, likable, credible, then trustable.
- Buyer Mirror interviews reveal the content that first created attention, later created conviction, and finally triggered a booking.
- Client proof works best when the viewer receives a useful case walkthrough, not a self-serving testimonial.
- A founder-led brand should operate two connected pipelines: pre-production and post-production.
- Packaging is decided before filming; retained-attention spikes become repurposing signals; weekly outliers feed the next concept cycle.

### Source 2 — the craft layer

`PRqSCE8uZns` explains how an individual asset earns and narrows attention:

- Top-of-funnel packaging begins in the overlap between the ideal client and a wider “dream follower.”
- An authentic “I made it” moment documents achieved reality without staging a lifestyle performance.
- A strong opening coordinates title, visual, and verbal hooks.
- Proven formats are borrowed at the mechanics level, then differentiated using Buyer Mirror evidence.
- The script deliberately narrows: broad shared interest first, ideal-client specificity by the end.
- Middle-funnel content should break one limiting belief surfaced in sales conversations.

## Capability vision

The expanded SooWei system should be able to take a high-value service, real buyer language, proof assets, and recent content performance, then produce:

1. A Buyer Mirror evidence map that separates attention, conviction, booking, hesitation, and differentiation signals.
2. A trust-sequenced content architecture that does not mistake authority claims for earned trust.
3. A broad-to-specific audience and script plan that attracts adjacent attention without diluting the buyer message.
4. Viewer-useful proof formats, including authentic achievement moments and case walkthroughs.
5. A three-hook asset brief with title, visual, and verbal openings working as one unit.
6. A media-company operating plan connecting research, packaging, filming, editing, repurposing, and weekly learning.
7. An outlier loop that uses retention and buyer response to improve the next production cycle.

The system must produce usable artifacts, not explain content theory.

## Preservation lock

The build must preserve these boundaries:

- Keep `agents/soowei` and `skills/soowei-consulting-leverage` as the single expert owner.
- Extend by gap-diff; do not rewrite or weaken the existing consulting-leverage patterns.
- Keep the new layer cold and workspace-local until realistic application and regression checks pass.
- Do not edit vendored Scrapes skills. They may consume a SooWei strategy artifact later, but they do not own this strategy.
- Do not add auto-posting, outreach, connectors, paid tools, or external writes.
- Treat revenue, client-count, and causal performance statements as SooWei’s self-reported claims unless independently verified.
- Treat the 20/50/0 dream-follower ratios as a demonstrated heuristic, not a universal law.
- Do not convert an optional quality heuristic into a mandatory global content schema.

## Proposed proof standard

Before promotion, the expansion must pass three tests:

1. **Source traceability:** every new pattern and workflow points to exact transcript timestamps or a named visual artifact.
2. **Behavior change:** on a realistic high-trust consultancy brief, the expanded system must produce materially better buyer evidence, trust sequencing, asset packaging, and learning-loop decisions than the current skill alone.
3. **No regression:** existing offer, conversion, delivery, and founder-exit workflows remain intact and routable.

Local verifier success proves structural readiness only. It does not prove audience demand, client acquisition, or revenue lift.

## Architecture question for Checkpoint 2

If this vision is approved, the next phase will decide the smallest workflow set and routing surface. The working hypothesis is a bounded module inside the existing SooWei skill, with no new agent and no new public front door unless discoverability evidence requires one.
