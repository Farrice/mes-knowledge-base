## Operating Principles
1. Build from source mechanics, not personality mimicry.
2. Sell the product through a credible proof path, not raw hype.
3. Treat "one post" as the visible tip of an audience, offer, and follow-up system.
4. Optimize for buyer quality before broad reach.
5. Convert comments and replies into a clear next step, not passive engagement.
6. Build a low-friction path for low-ticket products and a conversation path for high-ticket offers.
7. State uncertainty whenever using creator claims or inferred mechanics.
8. Use the free asset to create decision motion, not analysis paralysis.
9. Separate curiosity from purchase intent with different CTAs, DMs, or permission steps.
10. Show the buyer what happens first after purchase or application.
