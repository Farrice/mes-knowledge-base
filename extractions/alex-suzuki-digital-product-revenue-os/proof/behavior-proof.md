## Old Weak Output Class

The old floor was useful but too passive:

```text
I made a guide that helps you decide whether this product is right for you.

Comment GUIDE and I will send the questions I would ask before buying.
```

Failure:
- Buyer receives more questions, not a decision path.
- Ready buyer has no clean link/application lane.
- No first-use plan.
- No routing difference between curious and ready buyers.
- No replay sprint after the post.

## New OS Floor Test

An output passes only if it includes:

| Criterion | Pass Standard |
|---|---|
| Buyer self-recognition | The buyer sees themselves in the hook or first frame. |
| Purchase-adjacent asset | The free asset gives a score, sample, lane, first-use path, or worked example. |
| Value lane | Curious buyers can ask for the asset without pressure. |
| Purchase lane | Ready buyers can ask for link, checkout, application, or conversation by permission. |
| Intent routing | Cold, warm, ready, and wrong-fit buyers receive different replies or DMs. |
| Price path | Checkout, application, call, or trust container matches buyer risk. |
| First-use path | The buyer knows what to do after buying. |
| Replay sprint | The launch includes reusable frames after the first post. |
| Proof ledger | Claims are concrete, sourced, or marked missing. |

## Fixture 1 - `$200` Ebook

Input: a `$200` ebook for independent consultants called `Client Signal Map`. It helps them find high-intent buyer signals and turn them into posts, emails, and sales call prompts.

### V4-Level X Post

```text
Most consultants do not need more "content ideas."

They need a way to spot buying pain before it becomes a sales call.

So I built Client Signal Map:

a $200 ebook for consultants who want to turn market signals into:

- 7 posts from real buyer frustration
- 5 email openings tied to active pain
- 3 sales-call prompts
- 1 weekly signal review

The free version is a 20-signal checklist plus one worked example.

Comment SIGNAL and I will send the checklist.

Already considering the ebook? Comment MAP and I will send the fit check before the checkout link.
```

### Purchase-Adjacent Free Asset

`20-Signal Fit Map`

Contents:
- 20 buyer signals to scan.
- A 0-2 score for "do I have enough signals to use this ebook?"
- One worked example turning a complaint into a post, email, and call prompt.
- First 24 hours after buying the ebook.

Decision lanes:
- 0-5: collect more signals first.
- 6-10: use the free example and test one post.
- 11-15: ebook is likely useful if the buyer wants the full system.
- 16+: ready for checkout if budget and fit are clear.

### Intent Routing

| Intent | Signal | Public Reply | DM |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold | Comments `SIGNAL` only | Sending it. Start with the score first. | Send checklist, ask for score and niche. |
| Warm | Shares score or client type | Good, that is enough to test. | Send worked example and first 24-hour path. |
| Ready | Comments `MAP`, asks price/link | I can send it after the fit check. | Confirm budget/use case, then send checkout by permission. |
| Wrong fit | Wants fast clients without outreach or proof | I would not start with the ebook. | Send no-cost signal collection step. |

### Paid Path

Low-ticket route:
1. Comment.
2. DM asset.
3. Fit check.
4. Checkout link by permission.
5. Day 1 email with first signal exercise.
6. Day 3 email with before/after post rewrite.
7. Day 7 email with FAQ and sample page.

### First 24 Hours After Purchase

1. Pick one niche.
2. Collect 10 raw signals.
3. Score three strongest signals.
4. Turn the strongest signal into one post.
5. Turn the same signal into one email opening.
6. Save one call prompt.

### 7-Day Replay Sprint

| Day | Public Asset | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Main post above | SIGNAL / MAP |
| 2 | Before/after: weak post to signal post | SIGNAL |
| 3 | "3 signals consultants miss" thread | SIGNAL |
| 4 | Sample page proof post | MAP |
| 5 | Objection: "Will this work in my niche?" | NICHE |
| 6 | First 24-hour path post | PLAN |
| 7 | Recap and fit reminder | MAP |

### Proof/Risk Ledger

- Product proof available: page count, signal list, worked example, sample page.
- Missing proof: beta feedback and buyer outcomes.
- Revenue claims: none.
- Risk note: do not promise clients or sales.

## Pass Verdict

The repaired floor passes for all three fixtures.

The strongest proof is not that the copy is louder. The proof is that each fixture now gives a serious buyer a path to decide, ask for the paid next step, and start after purchase while still protecting low-fit buyers from a bad decision.
