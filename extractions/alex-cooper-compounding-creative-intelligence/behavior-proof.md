## Creative Transformation

- **Input tested:** “Healthy ingredients support better skin and a shiny coat.”
- **Weakness diagnosed:** Benefit language; no concrete moment, intolerance transition, or subsequent action.
- **Source mechanics used:** Intolerance Transition, Evidence Before Drama, Narrative Hinge.
- **Evidence fixture:** “At 2:00 one night I woke up because my dog was chewing his paws until they bled.”

- **Remaining risk:** The fixture proves detection and evidence discipline, not ad performance or product efficacy.
