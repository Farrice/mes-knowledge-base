## Hidden Operating Knowledge

1. Discovery and positioning are different jobs. Discovery produces evidence; positioning makes that evidence legible.
2. The creator and service paths need different first moves. Publishing produces creator signal; repeating one service produces delivery and buyer signal.
3. Specificity is not synonymous with smallness. It is a communication and operational constraint that can increase adjacent inference.
4. A niche is partly a capacity design. Every yes shapes repetition, learning speed, and the factory the operator is building.
5. Pivot logic must preserve transferable assets. A changed vehicle does not erase skills, proof, audience knowledge, or owned content.

## Operational Translation

The source mechanics become a router, evidence research, a 30-day creator experiment, service × industry selection, constellation mapping, a reusable niche brief, a content experiment plan, and a commit-or-pivot audit. Each workflow has a born-v2 prompt and confidence-label contract.

## Anti-Patterns Extracted

- treating a niche as an existential identity decision;
- choosing a fashionable industry before a deliverable service;
- reading low absolute reach as failure without comparing relative lift;
- confusing content response with commercial demand;
- changing niches before diagnosing content, funnel, sales, delivery, or capacity;
- accepting adjacent work until the specialist factory becomes a generalist queue;
- turning one umbrella into unrelated content sprawl.

## Quality Bar

The system passes when it chooses the right evidence path, produces a deployable artifact, exposes uncertainty, and identifies a reversible test. It fails if it fabricates demand, forces specificity before discovery, or calls clarity validation.
