## The Core Genius This Source Adds

Rory is not simply applying behavioral biases to marketing. He is running a recurring innovation sequence:

1. **Question the nominal problem.** Ask whether the expensive physical fix is solving the feeling customers actually experience.
2. **Look for the psychological bottleneck.** A tiny uncertainty, response mechanism, rejection fear, or expectation mismatch can suppress adoption more than product quality.
3. **Translate the product into the buyer’s native currency.** Horsepower worked because mine owners could convert it directly into horses no longer fed.
4. **Move attention to a neglected dimension.** Once customers notice an unexpected strength, they reweight the whole category around it.
5. **Test at the extremes.** Change one tiny variable so causality is visible, or test an entirely different approach. Avoid five-variable mush.
6. **Pool risk across a portfolio.** A locally risky experiment can be globally rational when multiple teams take favorable, fat-tailed bets.
7. **Make the trade-off explicit.** Customers accept less when the bargain is chosen in advance; they resent the same reduction when it is imposed silently.
8. **Use reason as verification, not as the only searchlight.** Intuition and anecdote direct attention; evidence decides whether the idea survives.

This sequence is the bridge from “Rory-inspired marketing” to a universal behavioral-alchemy operating system.

## Provisional New Workflow Architecture

These are capability slots, not final command names. Later full-book access may justify a source-grounded revision, but it will not be treated as evidence in the current build.

### Tier 1: Universal foundation

1. **Psychological Bottleneck Finder**
   - Finds the tiny uncertainty, friction, rejection fear, response-method mismatch, or expectation gap suppressing behavior.
   - Produces a ranked bottleneck map and a one-variable test.

2. **Adoption Unit Architect**
   - Converts specs, benchmarks, and technical performance into the unit the buyer already thinks in.
   - Produces a named “horsepower” unit, adoption claim, and behavior-change proof plan.

3. **Explicit Trade-Off Architect**
   - Designs a product or offer that deliberately underdoes the category while overdelivering on one chosen dimension.
   - Produces the chosen bargain, expectation contract, market language, and mismatch safeguards.

### Tier 2: Practitioner deployment

4. **Innovation Risk Pool**
   - Reframes team-level career risk as portfolio-level expected value.
   - Produces a bet portfolio, risk-pooling rules, scout allocation, and experiment charter.

5. **Uncertainty & Progress Designer**
   - Reduces anxiety through estimates, progress cues, perceived agency, and staged certainty rather than expensive specification increases.
   - Produces an uncertainty map and redesigned experience sequence.

6. **Irritation-to-Innovation Reversal**
   - Converts recurring annoyance into a category-opposite product, service, format, or process.
   - Produces a Room 101 inventory, opposite-world concepts, and smallest testable reversal.

### Tier 3: Growth and media expansion

7. **Hidden Secondary-Market Finder**
   - Identifies valuable audiences created as a byproduct of focused product design, including audiences the category fails to name directly.
   - Produces primary-use logic, hidden-market hypotheses, proxy-safe positioning, and discovery tests.

8. **Advertising Archaeology Lab**
   - Mines old direct-response and media practices that disappeared from fashion, not effectiveness, and re-expresses them in a current format.
   - Produces a source-era mechanism map, modern adaptation, test cell, and novelty-without-amnesia rationale.

## Universal Deployment Map

**Marketing:** find the psychological variable competitors do not measure and turn it into the category’s new decision criterion.

**Copywriting:** translate specs into buyer-native outcomes, remove rejection fear, create certainty, and make the audience complete the inference.

**Strategy:** search outside the logical stepping stones competitors share; preserve fat-tailed exploration while validating cheaply.

**Entrepreneurship:** design chosen trade-offs, outcome-linked pricing, focused products, and new markets that emerge from eccentricity.

**Media:** select media for the psychological job, not fashion; revive proven discarded formats when the moving parade has forgotten them.

**Social media:** teach how to think through vivid examples and perception shifts, rather than talking about what the marketer does.

**Content:** use irritation, counterintuitive units, visible progress, and advertising archaeology as repeatable idea engines.

**Thinking:** use metacognition, anecdote as investigative compass, reason as sense-check, and the tiny-detail-or-new-approach testing rule.

**Any niche:** start from `[outcome]`, `[current frame]`, `[human anxiety or status risk]`, `[neglected dimension]`, and `[reversible test]` rather than assuming a marketing-only context.
