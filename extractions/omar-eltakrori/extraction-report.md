## EXTRACTED FRAMEWORKS

### 1. Visibility Beats Ability
**Core Thesis:** The most known person typically gets the most business. Not the most talented — the most visible.  
**Mechanism:** Intentional consistency → indirect opportunity. People who show up consistently create optionality for everything that's possible.  
**Key Distinction:** Being good is the long game (unseen work). Being known is the marketing game. Both matter, but visibility without ability catches up to you eventually.  
**Proof Point:** Amateur-hour videographers get $8K wedding packages because they came to mind. Talent without visibility gets nothing.

---

### 2. The Go-To Expert Framework (Student → Leader → Master)
Three compounding identities for becoming the recognized authority in any niche:

| Layer | Identity | Function |
|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | **Student's Identity** | Commitment to perpetual learning. Consume with intent. "You'll always lose to the person who enjoys learning." |
| 2 | **Leader's Responsibility** | When you consume with a leader's responsibility, it changes what you consume. You consume knowing you'll share it. This filters doom-scrolling and entertainment-first consumption. |
| 3 | **Mastered Deliverability** | The ability to formulate HOW something is said, not just WHAT is said. Creating frameworks. Using alliteration, visual models, memorable structures. |

**Key Insight:** "When you consolidate what you consume, it changes what you create."

---

### 3. Framework Creation Method
How to build proprietary frameworks from internal processes:

1. **Give yourself permission** — You already have frameworks. They're just internal/uncoined. Unpack the steps or processes you consistently take people through.
2. **Give it a name** — Use alliteration, same suffixes, memorable language. (Crispy Content Checklist, Content Quadrant, Business Blueprint Framework)
3. **Use visual shapes** — Draw it. Sad face → arrow → happy face. Frameworks communicated visually signal "I've thought about this before you have." The person who appears most obsessed gets the business.
4. **Use AI as an assist** — Ask AI for alliterative alternatives ("Give me a P word for consistent" → "persistent")

**Source of technique:** Preaching. Great preachers create memorable points using alliteration and structured delivery.

**Why it works psychologically:** "What it's doing is communicating to a person that you've thought of this before they have or you've thought of this more than they have."

---

### 4. Picture → Parable → Principle (PPP)
Content creation framework (credited to David Simmons):

| Element | Function | Example |
|---------|----------|---------|
| **Picture** | The thing, person, or event | Watching the movie "Up" with his kids |
| **Parable** | The story | Fredrickson can't take off → starts throwing out dead weight → house rises |
| **Principle** | The lesson | "If you want to go to the next level, you got to get rid of the things carrying you down" |

**Why it works:** AI can't take a story and draw an original principle from it. This is the human advantage. Every web class, conference keynote, and thought leader is doing this — shifting beliefs by sharing stories and pulling principles.

### 5. The Business Blueprint Framework
Three-phase online business model:

#### Phase 1: Unlock Infinite Content
- You should never run out of things to say. If you do, you're not consuming enough (go back to Student's Identity).
- Sources of infinite content: live streaming, reactions, conversations, classified content (phone album photos), real stories
- **Content to Cash formula:** 1 long-form/week + 5 feed posts/week + 3-5 stories/day. Do that for a year and your life looks different.
- **Cutless content:** Unedited, raw, sneeze-and-keep-going. If you make videos knowing they won't be edited, your approach changes — no restarts, just go.
- **Classified content:** Your phone album is content. Photos of gear, locations, moments → add copy → people hold down and read → boosts algorithm dwell time.
- **Communicate two things at once:** Share a business tip WHILE in a gym, cooking, or golfing. People connect to the lifestyle context AND absorb the information.

#### Phase 2: Usher Into an Intimate Conversion Event
- Don't sell on social media. Social is for awareness, not conversion.
- TV analogy: "We weren't watching commercials being sold. We were being made aware."
- Conversion events = web class, sales call, discovery call, challenge, boot camp, workshop, in-person event — anything OFF-platform where you control the environment.
- "The greatest personal brands that make money online are not selling on social, they're selling behind the curtain."

#### Phase 3: Uncover Your Core Irresistible Offer
- One offer can get you to $1M. Just do the math: 200 × $5,000 = $1M. Or 100 × $10,000.
- People aren't buying information. They're buying:

| What People Buy | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| **Acceleration** | Speed. 1-page doc vs 100-page doc. |
| **Transformation** | Who they are now → who they become. Fitness, money, relationships, health. |
| **Organization** | Scattered info → organized delivery. YouTube University takes years; organized program compresses it. |
| **Proximation** | Access. Being in the room. Context determines value — same info on YouTube vs in a paid room hits different. "Price creates the context for you to pay attention." |
| **Identification** | A new identity. Why luxury brands exist. |
| **Community** | Belonging. Being a facilitator of community is powerful. |
| **Consolidation** | Everything in one place vs scattered. |

### 6. The Challenge Model (Omar's Sales Engine)
Step-by-step revenue engine:

1. **Run ads** (~$40K spend) to a **free master class/web class** — teaches "5 mistakes entrepreneurs make building their personal brand"
2. Master class **kills limiting beliefs** through the 5 mistakes, then offers a **$197-$400 ticket** to a 5-day challenge
3. Challenge: daily Zoom sessions (Q&A + teaching) with ~300+ people
4. On the final day: present the **$25K coaching offer** — 25 spots, application-based
5. **Results:** $400K-$722K per challenge. Run 4-6 times per year.

**Automated version:** Replayed recorded challenge sessions from September in December. Used ChatGPT to find and cut dated references. Only showed up live for the offer presentation. Still made $400K.

**Key insight on replays:** "If you deliver something live, you can replay it live. An unedited message comes off as live in the moment."

### 7. The Four Pillars of Business
Linear business flow:

1. **Lead Generation** — How people give you their information
2. **Lead Conversion** — How people buy
3. **Fulfillment** — Deliver so well it sells the next thing
4. **Ascension / Retention** — Renewals, upsells, staying

**Key insight:** "When you fulfill well, it sells them on ascending or staying." Omar makes multiple six figures in renewals alone — income he never planned for.

### 9. Theory vs Principle
| Concept | Definition | Test |
|---------|-----------|------|
| **Theory** | "You succeeded once and now teach it" | Works for you but unproven for others |
| **Principle** | "Weathered storms, failed, recovered, taught others who succeeded" | No respecter of persons — everyone can get something from it |

"You can't teach principle by succeeding in something once. You can only do it if you've weathered storms."

### 10. Micro-Famous Strategy
Being micro-famous in your sector > trying to be mega-famous.

**Know-Like-Trust building:**
- **Trust:** Share educational information consistently. Be the source of updated, relevant info in your niche.
- **Know:** Be involved. Show up. Be the recognizable face in your sector.
- **Like:** Open up your life (5 F's). Share things people can relate to.

**Broad appeal + niche business:** "The riches are in the niches, but that's more about the business you offer. Having broad appeal with a niche business is really the game."

**Local content going global:** Example of Tommy Oswald (Homeboy coffee shop, Australia) — vlogs about his container coffee shop, gets hundreds of thousands of views, grows his local business through content.

### 11. Urgency & Scarcity (Manufactured)
"Two things that will increase your sales: urgency and scarcity."

- **Manufactured scarcity:** "As business owners, we can decide when we open up." Rolex manufactures scarcity. Omar opens 25 coaching spots per challenge.
- **Manufactured urgency:** "If you get off this call, the price will be different. If you say yes right now, this will be your price."
- **Founders pricing:** When launching, seek "founders" — people who want to be part of something new. Give them identity + locked-in pricing.

### 12. Price = Attention Framework
"When you pay, you pay attention. When you pay more, you pay more attention."

**Evidence:**
- Founders paying $2K/year aren't implementing as aggressively as new members paying $25K
- A founder who left, came back at full $25K price, had an $80K week running the prescribed play
- Omar paid $3K when broke — never worked harder to earn it back
- "The psychological thing that happens when you pay for something is it creates a void for your creativity to fill."

**Corollary:** "If you want your clients to make fast buying decisions with you, you should be a fast buyer." Omar shook hands on a $10K two-day intensive without hesitation.

**Anti-pattern:** "If you're cheap, if you're always trying to look for the discount, don't be surprised when people want discounts from you."

### 13. Self-Concept Sales Principle
From Brian Tracy's *Sales Psychology:*

"Before you get better at sales, how you think about yourself starts with sales."

**Omar's proof:** Lost 35 lbs (200 → 165). Ran next challenge — jumped from $400K average to $722K. "I was more confident in myself subconsciously."

**Takeaway:** Physical health, showing up for yourself → confidence → better energy in sales → better results. "Becoming the person before the outcome."

### 14. The $100K Lesson (Devaluation of Money)
Lost $100K in a Ponzi scheme. Key realizations:

1. "I lost $100,000 and nothing changed" — mortgage paid, food on table, life continued
2. "If $100K isn't a lot of money, maybe $1M isn't a lot of money"
3. "The decision to be bigger than $100,000 happened when I lost it"
4. "The pursuit of money should always be met with the devaluation of it — all money is is a tool"

**Business math:** First seven-figure year ($1.2M revenue) cost ~$300K in hires and contractors. "Business is: would you trade $300K for $1M? How many times would you make that trade?"

### 15. Do It for the Data
"If you're afraid to do something or you've been putting off something, it's because you are overthinking the perfectionism and underthinking the benefits."

**Reframe:** "I'm gonna do this for the data" → removes emotional weight → increases likelihood of action.

**Iteration principle:** "We gave Apple 17 iterations. They still haven't gotten it right from their point of view, but they're going to still sell it to us."

### 16. One-to-Many Business Model
Everything in your business should be structured one-to-many:

- Content: one piece → many viewers
- Web class: one presentation → many attendees
- Challenge: one week → 300+ participants
- Coaching: one Tuesday call → 80-90 people
- Podcast: one conversation → infinite consumption

"The one-to-many business model can really change people's lives."

### 17. Charge More by Focusing on Payoff
"If someone arrives to the idea that your offer is expensive, you didn't articulate the payoff value good enough."

**Formula:** Focus on what's possible WITH the information, not the information itself.

**Proof:** Omar's offer went from $2K → $25K (same core offer). Justified by: faster client results, his own improvement, supply/demand dynamics.

"When you pay somebody once and you can implement it infinitely, that's where the value comes from."

### 20. Faith & Wealth Theology
**Core stance:** "God wants you to be rich."

Key arguments:
1. Wealth is a biblical concept (Deuteronomy — "God gives us the ability/power to get wealth")
2. "The love of money is the root of all evil" — not money itself, not the pursuit of money
3. Parable of the talents: multiply what you're given = "well done, good and faithful servant." Do nothing = "wicked and lazy servant."
4. "You can't pour from an empty cup." Jesus said "love your neighbor as yourself" — can't love others if you're not taken care of.
5. Tithing principle: first 10% to God → He blesses the 90 more than you could do with 100.

**Anti-patterns around money:**
- If money stresses you out, it might be a god (idol)
- If you're afraid to lose it OR afraid to gain it → you serve money
- Rich people ≠ evil (cultural programming from superhero movies — villains are always rich)
