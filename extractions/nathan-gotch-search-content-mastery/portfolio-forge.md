# Nathan Gotch Search Content Mastery — Portfolio Forge

## Forge verdict

Eleven source packages support one connected practitioner operating spine. They do **not** support eleven separate mega-skills, a universal ranking formula, or guaranteed SEO outcomes.

The durable capability is a sequence:

**truth pack → eligibility → live search evidence → opportunity decision → measurable brief → reviewed production → channel adaptation → independent outcome receipts → human-promoted learning**

This portfolio extends `skills/nathan-gotch-ai-seo/` for search mechanics and feeds the non-persona `search-content-mastery-os` conductor. Existing Ethan Smith, `/create`, voice, copy, image, carousel, audio, and video capabilities retain ownership of their specialties.

## Evidence language

| Label | Meaning in this system |
|---|---|
| `OBSERVED` | The creator said or demonstrated the mechanic in a captured source. This is source evidence, not causal or market proof. |
| `CORROBORATED` | The mechanic recurs across two or more captured sources or aligns with an existing source-backed workflow. Recurrence is not causation. |
| `INFERRED` | The build adapts the source mechanics into a reusable record, check, or routing decision. |
| `UNCONFIRMED` | The source asserts a number, causal relationship, platform behavior, forecast, or broad rule without sufficient evidence here. |
| `UNTESTED` | The proposed artifact, service, ranking effect, citation effect, traffic effect, conversion effect, or revenue effect has no dated external outcome receipt. |
| `NO EVENT` | A future source or market event has not occurred and cannot produce a finding yet. |

## Deduplicated practitioner doctrine

### 1. Establish portable truth before content

The project begins with entity, brand, offer, audience, voice, source, claim, competitor, search-input, and operator-taste records. The seed shows why raw transcripts, scattered brand facts, and model memory are weak context; the local, ecommerce, audit, and content-production sources show the same gap from different angles.

The system therefore creates a portable `SearchProjectManifest` and project pack before a brief. This is `INFERRED` implementation built from `OBSERVED` context-first mechanics.

### 2. Establish eligibility before optimization

Check crawl access, status, indexability, canonical state, retrievable content, correct entity/location, and the complete CTA path before prescribing more content. A page that cannot be retrieved, belongs to the wrong entity, or breaks at conversion is not fixed by adding keywords.

The deterministic layer can identify these conditions. The action—repair, consolidate, redirect, rebuild, or preserve—remains expert judgment.

### 3. Use live, source-labeled search evidence

Every opportunity record keeps its source: GSC, current SERP, AI observation, citation set, YouTube result, directory, marketplace, competitor asset, or first-party customer evidence. Search volume is a proxy; synthetic AI prompts are a designed sample; tool scores are not ground truth.

Unknown schemas and unlabeled estimates are rejected instead of silently mapped.

### 4. Choose one coherent cluster and one intent

Depth precedes spread. The unit can be one category, one service/location pair, or one commercially coherent topic family. Page splits follow materially different buyers, jobs, or decision stages—not arbitrary exact-match variants or universal word-count targets.

Fixed prescriptions such as 15, 25–30, 163, 200, or 250–500 assets remain `UNCONFIRMED` creator heuristics. The system scales only when uniqueness, capacity, and outcome receipts support it.

### 5. Brief from evidence, not from a blank prompt

`SearchBrief` carries the target query, intent, audience, information-gain requirement, source receipts, proven structural pattern, channel, CTA, risk boundary, and falsifiable measurement hypothesis. It can reuse a competitor's structural pattern but cannot copy its substance or inherit its unsupported claims.

### 6. Generate quickly; judge and edit deliberately

AI expands, restructures, prototypes, and adapts. It does not adjudicate truth, usefulness, compliance, or taste. The sources repeatedly pair fast generation with multiple edit passes. The edit requirement governs over “one prompt” production language.

Approved briefs route to existing production workflows. The search conductor evaluates and hands off; it does not recreate writing or media craft.

### 7. Build the primary answer before channel expansion

Owned conversion/service/category assets come first, followed by supporting intent coverage. Then adapt the same evidence and topic spine for YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram, directory profiles, marketplaces, or publishers where live observations justify the surface.

Adaptation changes format, depth, proof, CTA, and audience posture. It is not identical-copy syndication.

### 8. Treat citations and third-party surfaces as evidence queues

Recurring sources across a diverse query set become opportunity records. A source's recurrence does not authorize outreach, sponsorship, profile creation, review solicitation, community manipulation, or a claim of independent endorsement.

Selective, retrieval-led citation work supersedes static bulk-citation checklists. Sponsored opportunities require disclosure; covert community tactics are excluded.

### 9. Keep every outcome stage independent

`PREDICTED`, `PUBLISHED`, `INDEXED`, `RANKED`, `CITED`, `TRAFFIC`, `CONVERTED`, and `COLLECTED` are separate records. A high readiness score does not create a later outcome. An AI citation does not prove traffic. A conversion does not prove collected revenue.

The unit of AI measurement is a dated observation that records surface, model/version when exposed, locale, personalization state, exact prompt, run, response, cited sources, source hash, and sampling limits.

### 10. Let observations propose, never self-mutate

The append-only learning loop may detect a missing stage and propose a review. Every proposal remains `UNCONFIRMED` and `HUMAN_REQUIRED`. Workflow changes require a Goal Packet, evidence, regression checks, and explicit promotion.

## Contradiction and temporal-change register

| Tension | Governing resolution |
|---|---|
| “Grounding prevents hallucination” versus warnings that AI still invents | Grounding lowers risk; source receipts and a human fact/claim gate remain mandatory. |
| Fast one-day strategy versus 90–180 day category execution | Planning can be fast; execution and market proof are slow. |
| “Almost guaranteed” gains versus stochastic AI-measurement critique | Preserve the method; reject the guarantee. Results remain `UNTESTED`. |
| Mass asset counts versus information gain and iterative craft | Counts are hypotheses and capacity planning inputs, never scope defaults. |
| Bulk citations versus newer selective retrieval-led citations | Dated live recurrence outranks static bulk checklists. |
| Model-alone strategy is criticized while AI-generated plans are demonstrated | AI drafts a grounded plan; senior judgment selects, limits, and promotes it. |
| A page may rank while mixing intents, but conversion suffers | Page splitting is a buyer/goal decision, not a universal indexing law. |
| Exact-match variants are deemphasized, but entity/location clarity remains | Use semantic sufficiency while preserving explicit disambiguation where ambiguity is real. |
| Public audits demand GSC/GA4/call data but often lack it | Public-demo recommendations stay `INFERRED` or `UNCONFIRMED`; no fabricated baseline. |
| Sponsored/community placement presented as search tactic | Paid placement requires disclosure and approval; covert community manipulation is excluded. |
| Tool vendors critique other tool scores while presenting proprietary scores | Source hashes, mappings, observation receipts, and independent events outrank vendor scoring. |
| Platform/crawler/model claims drift rapidly | Date every observation and review platform-specific rules before operational use. |

## Excluded claims and tactics

- Guaranteed rankings, citations, traffic, leads, conversions, or revenue.
- Tool scores represented as outcomes.
- AI “search volume” without first-party platform provenance.
- Stable AI rank inferred from one prompt or one run.
- Automatic publishing, outreach, profile creation, sponsorship purchase, review solicitation, or marketplace distribution.
- Undisclosed paid endorsements, aged-account promotion, or covert community manipulation.
- Health/product claims copied from competitor pages or generated without primary evidence and review.
- Automatic skill mutation from source advice or observed performance.

## Runtime and market proof boundary

The system may be promoted to `RUNTIME_OBSERVED` only after the local command routes, records, import rejections, six evaluator fixtures, three cold starts, service pack, registries, and skill/harness checks pass. That promotion says the **local operating behavior ran**.

It does not promote a project, service offer, page, citation strategy, or growth forecast. Rankings, citations, traffic, leads, conversions, and collected revenue require their own dated `SearchEvent` evidence and remain `UNTESTED` here.
