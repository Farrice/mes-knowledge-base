## Unified Operating Spine Extracted

1. **Eligibility before creativity** — crawl access, indexability, valid response, retrievable/server-rendered content, canonical state, and multi-engine presence precede content optimization.
2. **Live intent before format choice** — the current SERP and retrieval surfaces determine page type, feature eligibility, and viable channels.
3. **Relevance before volume** — topic coverage and audience intent matter more than raw word count; thresholds are review triggers, not automatic actions.
4. **Conversion continuity inside content quality** — transactional pages are not complete until the CTA, form, confirmation, trust, and next-step experience work.
5. **Data chooses the review set; judgment chooses the action** — crawl/GA4/GSC/speed/links identify candidates, then a practitioner decides refresh, consolidate, redirect, delete, or build.
6. **Owned rank, external rank, AI citation, traffic, and conversion are different states** — none can stand in for another.
7. **Core plus vertical surfaces** — traditional and AI search are universal; local, ecommerce, YouTube, social, and app-store branches activate only when the project requires them.
8. **Citation opportunity is source coverage, not generic link building** — observe recurring retrieval sources, record gaps, and keep any external outreach approval-bound.
9. **Agent execution requires process, checkpoints, and persisted receipts** — model capability does not replace SEO judgment or prove outcomes.
10. **Learning remains human-promoted** — tool scores, agent outputs, and source heuristics may recommend a change; none should mutate the skill automatically.
