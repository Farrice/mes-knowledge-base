## Deduplicated practitioner system

1. **Create a portable project truth pack before planning content.** Entity record, brand/offer/customer language, proof and policies, 3-5 source-labeled SME artifacts, dated first-party data imports, and an append-only action ledger are the foundation.
2. **Treat retrieval as a data-shaping problem.** Broad source coverage is useful only after raw sources are normalized into lean, provenance-preserving records. Raw transcript dumping increases noise and retrieval ambiguity.
3. **Build a source-labeled opportunity database.** Every query carries origin, cluster, intent, SERP composition, current position/URL, commercial proxy, feasibility, relevance, and manual judgment. Reject incomplete records rather than manufacturing a priority.
4. **Prioritize multi-surface opportunities, not volume alone.** Evaluate traditional organic, local pack/GBP, YouTube, directories/earned sources, commerce surfaces, and AI citations/answers independently. Zero-volume and emerging topics become hypotheses with dated tests.
5. **Use proven structure without copying substance.** Sample top traditional and AI-retrieved assets, extract recurring page structures, then add brand expertise, original evidence, customer language, design, and information gain through an explicit `SearchBrief`.
6. **Sequence depth before spread.** Choose one category/cluster, cover it deeply on the owned site, then adapt the same intents for suitable channels and distribution surfaces.
7. **Separate production gates.** Target -> context -> competitor/citation research -> outline -> draft -> human fact check -> conversion design -> local approval receipt. Publishing, outreach, and paid generation remain external approval boundaries.
8. **Measure work, not dashboard motion.** Baseline, execute, annotate the dated action, rescan, and record the outcome stage separately. Do not credit one asset causally when only category-level movement is observed.

## Portfolio-level contradictions resolved

| “Grounding means AI will not make things up” vs seed hallucination warning | Grounding reduces risk; a human claim/fact gate remains mandatory. |
| “Any model works” vs powerful model waste | Model vendor is secondary; task-to-model routing matters. Use deterministic tooling for compression/imports and strong reasoning only where judgment is required. |

| “Zero volume / low DR means easy win” | These are opportunity signals, not outcome guarantees. Require feasibility review and a dated measurement hypothesis. |
| Creator product dashboards as proof | Interface behavior is `OBSERVED`; rankings, citations, traffic, conversion, and automation effects remain `UNCONFIRMED` or `UNTESTED`. |

## Required boundaries for the build

- Never promise rankings, citations, traffic, leads, conversions, or revenue.
- Preserve separate outcome states and evidence timestamps.
- Do not operationalize covert community manipulation, noncompliant GBP naming, automated outreach, publishing, or marketplace distribution.
- Preserve original evidence and score when a human overrides the recommendation.
- Do not auto-mutate the skill from outcomes; create a proposal and require explicit human promotion.
- Future episodes announced in the seed remain `NO EVENT`; ingest only after a dated source exists.
