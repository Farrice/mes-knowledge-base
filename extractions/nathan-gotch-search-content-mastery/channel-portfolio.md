## Unified practitioner doctrine

1. **Fix the truth layer before the content layer.** Entity, business name, location, service, product, offer, audience, and ownership facts should be verified once and propagated consistently across the site, GBP, directories, citations, social/video, and AI tests.
2. **Baseline before intervention.** Preserve point-in-time evidence for each applicable surface before changing even an obvious mismatch: local pack/grid, traditional organic, AI brand mentions, AI citations, video/social discovery, GSC, GA4, calls, leads, conversions, and collected revenue.
3. **Work one commercially coherent cluster at a time.** Local work begins with one real service/location; ecommerce begins with one category; omnichannel work begins with one topic/intent family. Breadth follows demonstrated depth, not a fixed page quota.
4. **Let intent and conversion control architecture.** A ranking-capable page can still fail the buyer. Split pages when the buyer, job, decision stage, or required answer materially differs; preserve exceptions where one page genuinely serves the same intent.
5. **Build the primary answer asset before multiplying formats.** Establish a source-grounded conversion/service/category asset, then expand into support pages and adapted video, social, profile, directory, and publisher assets.
6. **Ground briefs in first-party truth plus live competitive/retrieval evidence.** Brand differentiators, voice, product/service facts, query intent, competitor patterns, recurring citation sources, and risk boundaries belong in the brief before drafting.
7. **Use AI for expansion and transformation, not adjudication.** Generated topic maps, inconsistency reports, prospect lists, wireframes, and drafts are candidate artifacts. Human review decides truth, relevance, information gain, compliance, and taste.
8. **Treat recurring third-party sources as opportunity records.** Sources that recur across queries/platforms deserve review. Recurrence does not authorize outreach, payment, profile creation, sponsorship, community promotion, or a claim of independent endorsement.
9. **Adapt by channel instead of copying unchanged content.** Reuse the topic and evidence spine, but change format, depth, proof, CTA, and audience posture for the website, YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram, directories, marketplaces, and publishers.
10. **Measure stages independently.** `PREDICTED`, `PUBLISHED`, `INDEXED`, `RANKED`, `CITED`, `TRAFFIC`, `CONVERTED`, and `COLLECTED` are separate facts. No earlier state implies a later one.

## Lane-specific operating mechanics

### Omnichannel (`qzMAGdzra88`)

- Defines searchable content across pages, posts, video, and indexable social assets, then benchmarks each surface separately.
- Uses semantic topic/entity sufficiency rather than multiplying exact variants, while preserving location disambiguation.
- Prioritizes one buyer intent, a conversion-focused service wireframe, topic-gap coverage, a localized support map, and later external validation.
- Mines citations across a diverse query set and prioritizes recurring non-competitor sources.
- Requires three to four editorial/design iterations despite initially fast AI prototyping.

### Local (`53h_-LoEGiw`)

- Verifies real location/entity truth and records a baseline before correcting the GBP.
- Separates local pack, organic SERP real estate, third-party directory rank, AI mentions, citations, GSC/GA4, and calls.
- Replaces bulk citation volume with a selective, query-specific set of recurring surfaces.
- Treats third-party profiles as constrained content assets and AI inconsistency reports as verification queues.
- Completes core and secondary service/location pages before localized informational support.

### Ecommerce (`6o0mabKRmIo`)

- Selects one category and runs a category-scoped crawl/performance audit before strategy expansion.
- Builds an awareness-stage intent map from most-aware/product-adjacent outward, grounded in first-party product/category sources.
- Blends traditional results, AI recommendations/citations, video, and social observations; deduplicates and prioritizes recurring sources/entities.
- Builds briefs with intent, desired positioning as a hypothesis, differentiators, voice, sources, structure, internal links, and measurement targets.
- Treats AI output as a first draft, calls for three to five iterations, and measures secondary queries before splitting new assets.

### Measurement honesty (`LiLD7_tjn4o`)

- Separates generated-answer brand mentions from retrieval citations and from observable referral traffic.
- Rejects unlabeled claims of true LLM prompt volume; absent first-party platform provenance, demand is modeled or `UNCONFIRMED`.
- Records personalization, model/version, response, fan-out queries, citation sets, and run variance as measurement confounders.
- Uses disclosed synthetic prompts as a designed sample, not a known population, and aggregates visibility across variants/reruns rather than declaring one prompt's “rank.”
- Explicitly rejects clean causal attribution across multi-platform, long-lag journeys; visibility, traffic, conversion, and revenue remain separate receipts.

## Contradictions and temporal drift to preserve

1. **Aggressive certainty versus measurement honesty.** The 2026 strategy videos use “almost guaranteed,” “dominate quickly,” and high-volume prescriptions. The 2025 measurement source says AI tracking is stochastic, synthetic, personalized, and not a perfect science. The system preserves the methods but rejects the guarantees.
2. **Data-first principle versus public-demo limits.** The ecommerce and local sources say decisions should use GSC/GA4/call data while admitting those inputs are unavailable for the examples. Public diagnoses remain `INFERRED` or `UNCONFIRMED`.
3. **Mass output versus information gain.** Recommendations range from 15 or 25–30 support topics to 163, 200, or 250–500 assets. These counts are creator heuristics, not scope defaults. Scale only after capacity, uniqueness, and observed outcomes support it.
4. **Bulk citations versus selective retrieval-led citations.** The local source explicitly revises older bulk-citation practice toward a small query/industry-specific set. Recency and observed recurrence should outrank static checklists.
5. **Sponsored/community tactics versus trust.** The corpus presents sponsored publisher placements and aged-account Reddit promotion as visibility paths. Paid placement must be disclosed; covert community manipulation is excluded.
6. **Local pack versus organic route.** A real GBP/location can support local-pack work. When it is absent for a target city, the corpus switches to organic clustering rather than pretending content replaces proximity or eligibility.
7. **Fast generation versus craft.** The sources sometimes describe one-prompt completion, yet also require multiple iterations and show visible first-draft defects. The iteration requirement is the governing rule.
8. **Agent/training claims versus platform opacity.** Claims about CCBot inclusion, agent-share percentages, LLM search providers, freshness preferences, model menus, and market share are time-sensitive or `UNCONFIRMED`; they cannot become permanent causal laws.
9. **Tool critique versus vendor interest.** The measurement source says every tracker is guessing while previewing the creator's own analyzer. Transparent provenance and repeatable receipts outrank vendor scoring.

## Measurement contract derived from the portfolio

For every AI observation, preserve:

- platform and product surface;
- model/version when exposed;
- timestamp and locale;
- authenticated/personalized versus neutral session;
- exact prompt and seed cluster;
- run number and total reruns;
- generated answer and brand position/presence;
- citation links and whether they were native or simulated;
- capture/source hash;
- sampling limitations;
- independent downstream events, if any.

The portfolio supports an import-first observation system. It does **not** support labeling synthetic-prompt counts as search volume, one run as stable rank, citation presence as referral traffic, traffic as conversion, or conversion as collected revenue.

## Excluded from the operational system

- Guaranteed ranking, citation, traffic, lead, conversion, or revenue claims.
- Automatic publishing, outreach, sponsorship purchase, review solicitation, directory/profile creation, or marketplace distribution.
- Aged-account/community manipulation or undisclosed sponsored endorsement.
- Automatic learning-loop mutation based on these creator sources.
- Health/product claims generated from competitor summaries without primary evidence and human review.
- Fixed page/asset quotas presented as universal best practice.

## Proof boundary

`OBSERVED` in these packages means the creator said or showed the mechanic. `CORROBORATED` means it recurred across sources, not that it caused a market result. `INFERRED` means a system adaptation derived from the evidence. Vendor scores, broad causal claims, forecasts, market statistics, and proposed effects remain `UNCONFIRMED` or `UNTESTED`.

The corpus can support a rigorous operating process, artifact schema, review system, and experiment cadence. It cannot support a promise of rankings, citations, traffic, leads, conversions, or revenue on command.
