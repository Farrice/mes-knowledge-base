## Genius Patterns

### 1. Format-Over-Ideas Inversion
- **What he does unconsciously**: Refuses to generate "30 content ideas." Treats the unit of strategy as the *repeatable format*, not the individual post.
- **Executable behavior**: When asked for a content plan, deliver ONE format spec (visual + script pattern) designed to run 50+ times, not a list of topics.
- **Deploy when**: Any founder/client asks "what should I post?"
- **Success metric**: Could you script 10 videos of this in one hour? If yes, it's a format. If each needs fresh invention, it's an idea.

### 2. The Conversion Gate ("views ≠ sales")
- **What he does unconsciously**: Filters every format through "does this demonstrate expertise only I could provide?" before adopting it.
- **Executable behavior**: After choosing a format, run the test — *"Did the viewer learn something they could only learn from someone with my level of expertise?"* If no, the format is entertainment, not lead-gen — reject or rebuild it.
- **Deploy when**: Choosing/auditing any format for a business that sells a service.
- **Success metric**: Sales calls booked per 100k views, not raw views.

### 3. Cross-Niche Format Theft + Twist
- **What he does unconsciously**: Hunts winning formats *outside* his niche, never inside it (inside = first-mover already gone).
- **Executable behavior**: Find a format ripping in an unrelated niche → port it to your niche before anyone → add the twist that makes it demonstrate YOUR expertise (he turned Adam's entertainment-labeling into jargon/concept-labeling).
- **Deploy when**: Searching for a winning format.
- **Success metric**: You are first-to-market with that format in your niche.

### 4. The 20-Minute Strategy Spine (Purpose → Avatar → Market → Format → Hook)
- **What he does unconsciously**: Always builds in this exact dependency order. Purpose dictates avatar; avatar dictates positioning; positioning dictates format; format dictates script.
- **Executable behavior**: Never touch formats/scripts/edits until Purpose (what does the brand *sell*?) is locked.
- **Deploy when**: Any net-new content strategy.
- **Success metric**: Every downstream choice traces back to a single stated purpose (the offer).

### 5. Counter-Positioning by Outlier Map
- **What he does unconsciously**: Reads a niche as a positioning map of 20–40 competitors, then occupies the visible empty gap.
- **Executable behavior**: Pull 20–40 competitors, filter for 2–3x+ outlier videos in the last 6 months, catalog their topics/hooks/formats, then deliberately pick a *different* axis. (In health: everyone used podcast clips/talking heads → gap = sophisticated short-form social.)
- **Deploy when**: Entering or repositioning in a niche.
- **Success metric**: A buyer can spot you as standing apart "in one spot," not crowded against a rival.

### 6. The Four Money Formats (each = a distinct expertise-demonstration mechanism)
- **Reaction** — continuation of the scroll; leech the source video's virality; label concepts to show your work.
- **Process Breakdown** — "free consulting"; walk a real deliverable step-by-step on screen with real artifacts.
- **Authority Hijack** — borrow a famous figure's credibility as the hook; framing transfer.
- **Live Strategy Session** — raw, unpolished, solving a specific prospect's problem in real time; the "streamer" of business content.
- **Deploy when**: Selecting a format for a service business.
- **Success metric**: The chosen format both fits the creator's personality AND demonstrates the exact skill they sell.

### 7. Three-Hook Stacking in Frame One
- **What he does unconsciously**: Loads visual hook + verbal hook + written hook into the first ~1 second, all pulling the same direction.
- **Executable behavior**: For every video, spec all three: (1) visual = first frame the eye sees, (2) verbal = first words spoken, (3) written = caption/on-screen text. Borrow the reacted-video's visual hook; put authority label (e.g., "content strategist") + view-count in the written layer.
- **Deploy when**: Scripting any short-form video.
- **Success metric**: First 3 seconds do more work than the rest of the video combined.

### 8. The Format-From-Your-Own-Process Engine
- **What he does unconsciously**: Mines his *actual daily workflow* for the format (he already doom-scrolled to label videos → that became the format).
- **Executable behavior**: 4 build steps — (1) list every skill/activity you do for clients, (2) pair each with its on-camera visual, (3) lock a repeatable visual+script pattern, (4) stack 3 hooks. Pick the activity that targets your avatar's biggest pain.
- **Deploy when**: Inventing an original format.
- **Success metric**: The format showcases the literal work you sell.

### 9. The 2x / 4-Video Format Test
- **What he does unconsciously**: Treats format validation as a controlled experiment, not a vibe.
- **Executable behavior**: Post 4 videos of the same format. Average their views. If avg ≥ 2x your prior baseline → format has legs, double down. If not → fix execution or kill it.
- **Deploy when**: Validating any new format.
- **Success metric**: Hard 2x threshold met across 4 posts.

### 10. Saturation Blitz as Copy-Proof Moat
- **What he does unconsciously**: The moment 3–4 videos confirm a format, he floods (6/day) to own it before copycats.
- **Executable behavior**: On confirmation, record/script/edit ~6 videos/day across main + trial-reel feeds. Attach the format to your face so every copycat becomes your social proof.
- **Deploy when**: Right after the 2x test passes.
- **Success metric**: You saturate before a competitor's first copycat ships.

### 11. Framing > Production
- **What he does unconsciously**: Spends his leverage on framing, not edit quality. Strong framing lets script/edit be loose.
- **Executable behavior**: Engineer the frame (the authority keyword, the positioning, the on-screen label) first. When framing is strong, "let the leverage of the framing do the work" and post more rather than polish more.
- **Deploy when**: Allocating effort between framing and production.
- **Success metric**: Bad-shooting, no-editing videos still rip because the frame carries them (the "Citadel" case).

### 12. Volume → Feedback → Evolution
- **What he does unconsciously**: Uses volume not just for reach but as a sensor. More posts = more data on what attracts vs. what converts; he evolves execution while holding the core constant.
- **Executable behavior**: Keep the format's core fixed; vary execution (which authority to react to, which avatar flavor) based on data. When the audience's appetite for one target is satiated, switch the target, not the format.
- **Deploy when**: A confirmed format's views start decaying.
- **Success metric**: Format longevity — it keeps converting because execution adapts.

## Hidden Knowledge

- **The 45° advisor geometry**: Filming at a 45° angle (not head-on) puts you "on the same side of the table" as the viewer — peer/advisor, not authority lecturing. Head-on = "you, you, you" interrogation energy; 45° = "come here, let me show you something." A physical posture that changes the parasocial relationship.
- **Reaction = continuation, not interruption**: The viewer is already on IG to watch videos; reacting to one is a *continuation of their flow*, so they don't switch gears. The original video keeps them watching — you sneak expertise in while their attention is on someone else, and they can't scroll without missing what they came for.
- **"Satiate the appetite" replaces length rules**: 30–45s is a myth. A video only needs to satiate the avatar's appetite. Reaction breakdowns can run long because the source is viral AND the breakdown adds value (Pack Leader Dogs' long videos get 100k-like essay comments).
- **The visual just has to MATCH the activity — it can be faked**: Sky read jargon off Apple Notes and wasn't even watching the video he "reacted" to; the editor cut it. The viewer reads "expert at work" from the *visual matching the claimed activity*, not from authenticity of the moment.
- **Framing keyword as 10x lever**: One word ("Citadel" for a college-success avatar) multiplied a creator's framing tenfold with no production upgrade. Find the single in-group authority keyword your avatar reveres.
- **Authority-hijack search string**: To find hijack clips, search `[famous authority name] + [your niche keyword]` (e.g., "Jordan Belfort sales"). The internet already has the clip.
- **Polished now reads as fake**: The market is so aware of scripted talking-heads that polish no longer signals expert. Unpolished + concrete outcome + sounding smart in real time now signals "real person who knows their stuff." (Mr. Beast Sidemen: shorts creator < YouTuber < streamer in audience respect → live strategy session is the "streamer.")
- **Authoritative brands need relatable formats to convert low-ticket**: Huberman is authoritative-but-not-relatable; selfie/day-in-the-life formats contrast his produced podcast clips and pull low-ticket buyers. Match format register to the conversion goal.
- **Sends are the virality metric**: Adam Mosseri (IG CEO) flagged sends as a top ranking signal; expertise-breakdowns are inherently shareable ("passed this to my clients"), so they compound.

## Hall of Fame Exemplars

### Exemplar 1: Pack Leader Dogs (Steve) — the reaction format at its ceiling
- **Context**: Dog-training client, 150K→750K followers, ~1M views/video at ~8 min recording time per video, fully organic, no ads.
- **The Example**: Take a viral dog clip that *opens with conflict/interaction* (inherently a strong hook) → one full watch → open the talking head with a **bold claim** ("99.9% of the public shouldn't have their dog off leash") → then frame-by-frame slow-mo breakdown on iPad with a pencil of exactly how the interaction unfolded and what the owner missed. Language is *sharing not teaching*: "I want to show you this," "take a look at this" — never "you should." Result: 27M-view and 4M-view videos; comment sections with 100k-like essay-length comments.
- **What makes it excellent**: Every element earns its place — viral source supplies the hook, the bold claim re-hooks post-reaction, the frame-by-frame *demonstrates* expertise (not asserts it), and the "sharing" verb choice keeps it peer-level. It satisfies the Conversion Gate: only a real dog trainer could deliver that breakdown.

### Exemplar 2: The "Citadel" framing keyword (Kevin) — framing > production
- **Context**: Client selling a program teaching students to publish AI papers; avatar = high-performing college students.
- **The Example**: Bad shooting, no editing, just yapping — but he inserted the word **"Citadel"** (the elite hedge fund his avatar reveres) into his videos. Views went 50k → 100k → 600k. The single in-group authority keyword carried videos that violated every production "best practice."
- **What makes it excellent**: Isolates the variable — proves framing is the lever and production is downstream. Calibration anchor for "where to spend effort."

### Exemplar 3: The 20-Minute Huberman Build — the strategy spine in action
- **Context**: Live bet — build a $1M content strategy for Andrew Huberman in 20 minutes.
- **The Example**: Lock Purpose (sell the Huberman Premium subscription) → Avatar (25–45 optimizing professionals) → Market (mapped Bryan Johnson, Gary Brecka, Peter Attia, Dr. Mike via outlier scores; found the gap — *everyone uses podcast clips/talking heads, nobody does sophisticated short-form social*) → 5 Formats (reaction, science day-in-the-life, symptom→root-cause, podcast-clip volume page, selfie AMA) → one scripted hook ("If you're struggling with weak knees after workouts… you're looking at the completely wrong place. You should look at your hips instead.") revealing info one beat at a time with an open loop.
- **What makes it excellent**: Demonstrates the dependency chain — each layer is *derived* from the one above, never invented free-floating. The hook shows the "one beat at a time + open loop" reveal discipline.

### Anti-Exemplar: Adam's entertainment-labeling (and Nosh's unmonetized 43M)
- **What mediocre looks like**: Adam runs the *identical* reaction format, 50M views in 2 weeks — but labels things for *entertainment* ("seed oils," "switch slop"). Nosh got 43M views on a live-build of an egg timer but never branded herself as an authority or made an offer.
- **Why it fails**: Both violate the Conversion Gate. Adam's labels teach nothing only-he-could-teach → views without sales calls. Nosh demonstrated expertise but attached no authority/offer → the leads evaporated. Views are necessary, not sufficient; without the expertise-demonstration + offer, the format is a vanity engine.

## Signature Moves

- **Lock Purpose first** — Before formats/scripts/edits, answer "what does this brand actually *sell*?" (low-ticket vs high-ticket, leads vs inspiration). → **Deploy when**: starting any strategy.
- **Hunt outliers, not averages** — Pull 20–40 competitors, filter to 2–3x+ outlier videos in the last 6 months, sort by outlier score, English only, catalog topics/hooks/formats. → **Deploy when**: market research / counter-positioning.
- **Steal from outside, twist for authority** — Port a winning format from an unrelated niche and add the twist that makes it demonstrate *your* expertise. → **Deploy when**: finding your format.
- **Sit at 45°** — Physically angle off head-on to become advisor-on-the-same-side, not authority-across-the-table. → **Deploy when**: filming reaction/selfie formats.
- **Open the talking head with a bold claim** — After a reaction watch, lead with a statement bold enough to re-hook attention before breaking it down. → **Deploy when**: reaction format.
- **Stack three hooks in frame one** — Spec visual + verbal + written hooks all firing in the first second, same direction. → **Deploy when**: scripting any short-form.
- **Run the 2x test before scaling** — Post 4, average, demand ≥2x baseline. → **Deploy when**: validating a format.
- **Blitz on confirmation** — 6 videos/day the moment a format proves out; saturate before copycats. → **Deploy when**: the 2x test passes.
- **Read low views as data** — A 300-view post is information, not failure; the discipline to keep posting the same format separates finders from the stuck. → **Deploy when**: early posts underperform.

| Criterion | Score 4 (Acceptable) | Score 7 (Good) | Score 10 (Savant) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Conversion Gate** | Demonstrates *some* skill but mostly entertains | Teaches something useful | Viewer learned something only someone of this expertise could teach; a sales call is the natural next step |

| **Test Discipline** | Posts once, judges on vibes | Posts a few, eyeballs it | Runs the 2x/4-video test; reads low views as data; evolves execution on the core |

## Methodology (progression)

1. **Strategy spine** — Purpose → Avatar → Market(counter-position) → Format → Hook. Never skip down-chain.
2. **Format discovery** — steal cross-niche → twist for authority → build 4 steps (list activities → pair visuals → make repeatable → stack 3 hooks).
3. **Format selection** — map business to one of the 4 money formats (or an original from the creator's process), filtered by the Conversion Gate.
4. **Validation** — the 2x/4-video test; low views = data.
5. **Scale & defend** — saturation blitz (6/day), face-attachment, copycats-as-social-proof.
6. **Evolve** — hold core constant, vary execution on data; switch reaction-targets when appetite satiates.
