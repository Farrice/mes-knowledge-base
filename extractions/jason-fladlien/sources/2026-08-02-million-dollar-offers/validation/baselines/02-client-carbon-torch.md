## Primary Friction

The largest removable weight is not weak copy. It is an ambiguous execution load:

> “Three foundational days plus two optional or targeted trigger days” can be interpreted as a five-day weekly obligation.

That makes the offer feel harder to start than the facts require.

Secondary friction:

- The outcome can be mistaken for a promised result rather than the stated program goal.
- The intake CTA is authentic, but the sequence after intake is not shown.
- `$500/month` could be incorrectly converted into an invented total.
- The fixture contains no complete checkout or commercial-terms path.

## Concrete Offer Decisions

### Decision 1: Make the offer a three-day core, not a five-day default

Package and operate the program as:

- **Core commitment:** three foundational days.
- **Adaptive extension:** up to two optional or targeted trigger days only when the individualized plan calls for them.

This is a fulfillment and expectation decision, not merely a wording change. It preserves every fixed delivery fact while lowering perceived and actual default complexity.

### Decision 2: Use one entry action

The only buyer-facing action is:

> **Complete and return the intake.**

Do not add a competing “book a call,” “DM me,” or “buy now” action without confirmed operational facts.

The intake is the entry point; its exact relationship to payment remains unresolved and must not be invented.

### Decision 3: Sell the controlled process; classify the body result as the goal

What the buyer can clearly rely on is the defined service:

- individualized training and nutrition planning;
- kickoff;
- weekly tracking;
- autoregulation;
- evidence-led adjustments around real schedule, equipment, and health context.

The bodyweight, waist, strength, and camera statement remains the **program goal**, not a guarantee or typical-results claim. It should not ship publicly until its health and performance evidence review is complete.

### Decision 4: Add no bonuses, guarantee, scarcity, or price anchor

The pre-expansion incomparability workflow requires competitor research that is absent. The offer-anatomy workflow asks for risk reversal and scarcity, but the fixture expressly forbids inventing either.

Therefore:

- no category-of-one or “incomparable” claim;
- no bonus stack;
- no self-funding bonus;
- no invented guarantee;
- no limited-spots claim;
- no “value if purchased separately” anchor;
- no cost-of-inaction health pressure.

Subtraction is the correct baseline move.

### Decision 5: Do not calculate a total program price

State only:

> **$500/month for the described eight-week program and coaching.**

Do not infer `$1,000 total`, “two payments,” a billing start date, or any other commercial term.

- payment method or checkout destination;
- billing start date and number or timing of charges;

- cancellation, refund, pause, or rescheduling terms;
- what the client receives immediately after returning the intake.

Until then, the safe CTA is the authentic intake action, not a fabricated checkout flow.
