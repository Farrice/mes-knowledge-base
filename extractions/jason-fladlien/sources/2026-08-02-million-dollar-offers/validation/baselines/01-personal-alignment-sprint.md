## Sole material change: DECIDED

### Gated delivery sequence

1. **Activation gate**
   - The 21-day sprint clock begins only after the client supplies:
     - three real client artifacts; and
     - the warm-network list.
   - The paid audit remains separate and is not silently folded into or credited against the sprint.

2. **Decision gate**
   - Buyer, positioning, and priced package are developed first.
   - The client provides written offer approval before the messaging bank and dated warm-network plan are finalized.

3. **Rep gate**
   - The client personally sends the required five messages.
   - Fulfillment does not expand into provider-sent outreach or done-for-you selling.

### Decision language

> The 21-day sprint begins once the client has supplied the three real client artifacts and warm-network list. The buyer, positioning, and priced package are presented for written approval before the messaging bank and dated warm-network plan are finalized. The client remains responsible for personally sending the required five messages.

This is material because it protects the fixed delivery window from client-input delay while preserving every purchased output and client rep requirement.
