# Offer TERMS Rebuild Packet: Official/Internal Offer Decision Pilot

## Executive Verdict

- **Lane:** Official/internal
- **Game state:** `NOT ASSESSED` for external commercialization. This bounded internal process pilot can proceed without asserting market viability.
- **Decision:** `REBUILD` for the next two internal runs; `HOLD` external pricing, packaging, copy, acquisition, and market claims.
- **Primary burden:** `FAT-T / Thinking`: the owner is being asked to perform the operator’s analysis and navigate multiple precursor decisions before reaching the one decision the service exists to produce.
- **Why this wins:** Both observed failure modes point to decision load: one owner abandoned the worksheet while questioning whether they were doing the work correctly; another completed the process but could not identify which decision came first. The burden has direct evidence and is locally controllable.
- **Maximum change count:** `3/3 proposed`; `2 accepted`, `1 held`.
- **Smallest defensible rebuild:** Transfer classification and sequencing work to the operator inside the two existing meetings, then put the single `REBUILD / PRESERVE / HOLD` decision first in the existing brief and review. Add nothing else before the two-run pilot.

## Real-Burden Reconstruction

The current one-time path requires **105 minutes of scheduled live participation**, a 45-minute call and a 60-minute review, plus an unknown amount of worksheet time. Before receiving the operator’s analysis, the owner must classify, score, synthesize objections, choose a promise, and inventory all assets. The operator then returns an 18-page brief, scorecard, and 22-example library, after which the owner still has to identify and record the one final decision.

Observed consequences:

- One owner stopped during the preliminary analysis.
- One owner completed the full path but did not record a decision.
- One owner completed and recorded `PRESERVE`.

The proposed path keeps the same two meetings and delivery window. The owner supplies facts, constraints, proof, and fixed elements during the existing 45-minute intake. The operator owns classification and analysis. The existing brief and review then surface one recommendation and one explicit `REBUILD / PRESERVE / HOLD` decision before secondary detail. No additional meeting, software, dashboard, example library, public asset, or outcome claim is added.

The owner’s broader day, existing workload, preparation time, emotional state, external buying context, and the operator’s revised fulfillment time remain unknown.

## Rebuild Decisions (Maximum Three)

### Change 1: Move analysis out of prework and into operator ownership

- **Before:** The owner completes the 14-question analytic worksheet before the 45-minute call.
- **After:** Remove the worksheet as a completion gate. During the existing 45-minute call, the owner supplies current facts, constraints, proof, and fixed elements. The operator, not the owner, classifies elements, assesses risks, sequences objections, and forms the recommendation.
- **TERMS trace:** `[Effort / Thinking / FAT-T / 19:26–22:45 / OBSERVED_IN_SOURCE / FRICTION / NONE / two direct comments plus worksheet demands / shifting analysis may increase operator fulfillment effort]`; supporting traces `FAT-A`, `RAW-A`, and `HOP-P`.
- **Input evidence:** Abandonment after question six and uncertainty about whether the owner was answering correctly or creating more work.
- **Preserved:** Existing 45-minute call, required factual inputs, one accountable owner, one final decision, two-day delivery, no added meeting or software.
- **Trade-off:** Operator work may rise and the 45-minute intake may prove insufficient; neither effect is currently measured.
- **Diagnostic recommendation:** `OFFSET`: accept the live-interview direction only as an operator-led factual intake inside the existing call. Do not turn it into a verbal version of all 14 analytic questions and do not add a preparation call.

### Change 2: Make the single owner decision the first decision in the existing brief and review

- **Before:** The owner receives an 18-page brief, scorecard, example library, and review but may not know which part to decide first.
- **After:** Without adding pages or a deliverable, open the existing brief and review with: the operator’s recommendation, the minimum supporting rationale and known uncertainties, and one explicit owner choice, `REBUILD`, `PRESERVE`, or `HOLD`. Complete the existing decision record before moving to secondary analysis or examples.
- **TERMS trace:** `[Time / Win / RAW-W / 09:15–11:42 / OBSERVED_IN_SOURCE / FRICTION / NONE / one completed process produced no decision / front-loading a choice must not suppress evidence or agency]`; supporting traces `FAT-T` and `HOP-O`.
- **Input evidence:** Completed-but-undecided run and direct statement that the owner could not identify which part to decide first.
- **Preserved:** The 18-page brief, 60-minute review, scorecard, 22-example library, evidence boundaries, and the owner’s authority to choose `HOLD`.
- **Trade-off:** A decision presented too early could feel premature or pressured.
- **Diagnostic recommendation:** `ACCEPT` with the protection that the owner sees the essential rationale and uncertainties before recording the choice.

### Change 3: Add a positive-intent re-entry message

- **Before:** No stated re-entry or clean-exit path follows incomplete intake.
- **After proposed:** If an owner leaves factual intake incomplete, send one non-coercive message offering either completion of only the missing facts in the existing intake path or a pause.
- **TERMS trace:** `[Status / Social / RISE-S / 48:04–50:12 / OBSERVED_IN_SOURCE / FRICTION / ANTIGRAVITY / one observed worksheet abandonment / follow-up could create pressure or unnecessary contact]`.
- **Input evidence:** One observed drop-off.
- **Preserved:** No guilt, extra meeting, new dependency, urgency, compelled participation, or public reuse.
- **Trade-off:** The accepted worksheet removal may eliminate the exact drop-off condition; deploying a check-in now would add a second intervention without evidence it remains necessary.
- **Diagnostic recommendation:** `HOLD` for the next two-run pilot. Reconsider only if incomplete intake or re-entry risk persists under Change 1.
