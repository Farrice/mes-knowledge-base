### Change 1: Install a Three-Win Ladder Without Changing the Purchased Outcome

- **Before:** The ladder gives duration and final deliverables but no initial-decision or first-tangible-win event.
- **After:**
  - **Initial decision win:** At the end of the paid 90-minute audit, the buyer receives a documented `sprint fit / no-fit` decision. This does not substitute “clarity” for the sprint’s purchased outcome.
  - **First tangible win:** At sprint onboarding, set a dated milestone inside the fixed 21-day window for the buyer’s written approval of the one-buyer, positioning, and priced-package build. It must occur before the messaging bank and five-message action gate.
  - **Ideal win:** By the fixed sprint stop date, complete the existing promised package: one buyer, positioning, priced package, messaging bank, and dated warm-network plan. The five messages remain the buyer’s separate narrow rep gate on the plan’s dates.
- **TERMS trace:** `[Time / Win / RAW-W / 09:15–11:42 / OBSERVED_IN_SOURCE / FRICTION / ANTIGRAVITY / 21-day and 30-day horizons supplied but win events absent / milestones must not imply sales, demand, or guaranteed conversation]`
- **Input evidence:** Paid audit, fixed sprint, written approval, fixed promise, and stop-date lock.
- **Preserved:** Audience, ladder, 21-day duration, full sprint promise, five-message gate, guarantee, and prices.
- **Trade-off:** A milestone can create artificial pressure or imply a result.
- **Diagnostic recommendation:** `ACCEPT`, provided the exact date is set from actual delivery capacity and the event is labeled process progress, not market proof.
