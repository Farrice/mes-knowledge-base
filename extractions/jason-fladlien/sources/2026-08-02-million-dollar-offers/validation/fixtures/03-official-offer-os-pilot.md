# Fixture 3: Official/Internal Offer Decision Pilot

## Scenario

An internal operator team is piloting a one-time decision service for offer owners. The service is meant to decide whether an offer should be rebuilt, preserved, or held before copy or acquisition work begins.

## Current offer

- **Deliverable:** One written Offer Decision Brief with a recommendation and decision record.
- **Intended user:** The responsible owner of an existing offer.
- **Input:** Current offer, intended buyer, price, proof, delivery facts, constraints, and fixed elements.
- **Current intake:** A 45-minute call plus a 14-question worksheet completed beforehand.
- **Worksheet requirements:** The owner must classify 12 offer elements, score six risks, write the three strongest objections, choose a target promise, and identify every supporting asset before the call.
- **Delivery:** An 18-page brief within two business days, a 60-minute review, a spreadsheet scorecard, and a separate library of 22 examples.
- **Pilot scope:** The next two internal offers. No external price has been approved.
- **Capacity:** One operator can fulfill two briefs per week with current tools.

## Evidence available

- Three prior internal owners entered an earlier version.
- One owner completed the worksheet and review but left without recording a decision.
- One owner abandoned the worksheet after question six.
- One owner completed the process and recorded `PRESERVE`.
- Direct comment from the abandoned run: “I kept wondering whether I was answering this right or just creating more work for you.”
- Direct comment from the completed but undecided run: “The report made sense, but I could not tell which part I was supposed to decide first.”
- No controlled time study, outcome comparison, external buyer evidence, testimonial permission, conversion data, or revenue evidence exists.

## Candidate changes already proposed by the team

- Add a second preparation call.
- Add more examples to the library.
- Replace the worksheet with a live interview.
- Shorten the written brief.
- Add a dashboard that shows progress through the process.

None of these candidates is approved. The current evidence must decide what, if anything, changes.

## Preservation locks

- Keep one accountable offer owner and one recorded final decision.
- Do not add software, dependencies, meetings, or public-facing assets without a demonstrated need.
- Do not invent buyer motives, efficiency percentages, testimonials, demand, conversion, or revenue claims.
- Do not reuse internal comments outside this validation package.
- No copy, acquisition, publishing, outreach, or external deployment.

## Test question

What is the smallest defensible change to this internal offer before the next two-run pilot, and what should remain unchanged or unknown?
