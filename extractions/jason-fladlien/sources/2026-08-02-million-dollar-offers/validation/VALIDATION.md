## Comparative Result

- Personal: enhanced wins narrowly but clearly. It adds a bounded audit decision, first-win milestone, and guarantee go-live hold without changing price, duration, promise, or unsupported proof.
- Client: enhanced wins narrowly. It makes the first ordinary week executable and the first process milestone truthful while preserving medical, commercial, and evidence holds.
- Official: tie. Both systems make the same material repair. The baseline's `PRESERVE core + PATCH` label and two-run rule offset the enhanced packet's better trade-off and informed-decision protections.

The suite therefore supports bounded usefulness, not universal superiority. See `blind-review/mapping-and-suite-verdict.md` and `decision-delta-ledger.md`.

The official blind tie exposed an overstated `REBUILD` label. The final runtime now distinguishes `PATCH` from `REBUILD`; a sealed fresh-agent replay returned `PATCH` without reading the prior outputs or verdict. This targeted repair does not change the original tie or the `2/3` suite result.

## Stop Conditions

Stop or hold when:

- Source contradictions are hidden or a nineteenth lever is invented.
- A buyer motive, proof point, guarantee, anchor, scarcity, capacity, authority, consent, or demand fact is fabricated.
- More than three offer decisions are proposed.
- An accepted change worsens another term without disclosure and offset.
- Adoption or proof design runs without a named trigger.
- Usage, observed result, voluntary sharing, permission, and reuse are collapsed into one proof event.
- Framework detail is counted as uplift when the accepted decisions are unchanged.
- A bounded fixture result is reported as live or market proof.
