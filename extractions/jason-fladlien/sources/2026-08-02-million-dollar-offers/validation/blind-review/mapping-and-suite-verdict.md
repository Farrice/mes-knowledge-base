## Suite Verdict

- **Comparative component validity:** `PARTIAL PASS — 2/3`. TERMS produced a material, non-regressive decision improvement in two blinded fixtures and no material uplift in one.
- **Material-regression signal:** No judge found a material regression in the winning enhanced cases. On the tied official case, the enhanced `REBUILD` label overstated a bounded patch and should be treated as a calibration defect, not promoted as a win.
- **Cold-start portability:** `PASS` at the bounded fresh-agent fixture level. This is separate from comparative superiority.
- **Negative-path restraint:** `PASS`. The internal adoption component returned `NOT TRIGGERED` and proposed zero changes when no named trigger existed.
- **Operator-approved:** `PENDING`.
- **Live-tested:** `NO`.
- **Market-proven:** `NO`.

## What the Suite Actually Supports

The evidence supports retaining TERMS as a bounded diagnostic and rebuild layer when an offer has unresolved buyer burden, first-win, adoption, permission, or proof-boundary decisions. It does not support forcing TERMS to create novelty when the existing offer owner has already found the minimum viable repair.

The official tie adds a runtime calibration rule: if the Revenue Offer Agent and TERMS converge on the same accepted changes, prefer the narrower change classification and do not count framework detail as material uplift.

## Post-Judge Calibration Replay
