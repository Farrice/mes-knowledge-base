## Executive Verdict

Build a journey-spanning **Sales and Buyer Psychology Mastery System** around the existing Buyer Psychology Decision Intelligence Overlay. Do not build a giant psychology agent, a bias encyclopedia, or a universal persuasion gate.

The target capability should answer six questions in order:

1. What real human decision is being made?
2. What evidence do we actually have about the receiver and situation?
3. Which one decision mechanism best explains the current friction?
4. What countercondition could make that mechanism wrong or unsafe?
5. What is the smallest honest intervention, and which native owner should execute it?
6. What observable receipt would show whether the intervention helped?

Jason Fladlien remains the applied-practitioner and performance-demonstration source. Independent research calibrates the underlying mechanisms. Native function owners retain final craft and commercial authority. Truth, proof, privacy, safety, permission, and delivery reality remain superior.

## Senior-Partner Correction

Psychology is a conversion multiplier, not a replacement for buyer truth, product quality, offer fit, proof, price integrity, distribution, or delivery.

The following is an operational model, not an empirical equation:

> Buyer truth x offer value x proof x decision clarity x craft x delivery trust

If one factor is near zero, more persuasive technique should not be used to conceal the deficit. The mastery system must be able to return `FIX THE OFFER`, `GET EVIDENCE`, `CLARIFY THE TERMS`, `IMPROVE DELIVERY`, or `ABSTAIN` as readily as it returns a copy intervention.

## Independent Calibration Spine

Independent research qualifies the mechanism, not the business result or Jason's implementation.

|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence | [Kim and Peterson, online trust meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intmar.2017.01.001); [Boulding and Kirmani, warranty signaling experiment](https://doi.org/10.1086/209337); [Floyd et al., review-sales meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jretai.2014.04.004) | Trust and proof signals can affect evaluation, with substantial context dependence. | A guarantee, review count, or Jason close-rate claim as universal proof. |
| Agency | [Li and Shi, freedom-threatening language meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1093/hcr/hqaf016) | Controlling language can increase reactance and weaken persuasion outcomes in studied contexts. | A universal sales effect or covert autonomy language. |
| Value | [Tversky and Kahneman, framing experiment](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.7455683); [Hsee, evaluability experiment](https://doi.org/10.1006/obhd.1996.0077); [Tarrahi, Eisend, and Dost, price-fairness meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijresmar.2015.10.004) | Reference and comparison format can change evaluation. | Dishonest anchors, hidden cost, or proof that the offer is objectively valuable. |
| Action | [Sheeran, Listrom, and Gollwitzer, implementation-intention meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1080/10463283.2024.2334563) | If-then planning can help translate existing motivation into action. | Forced commitment or a remedy for weak fit and trust. |
| Experience | [Schiebler, Lee, and Brodbeck, expectation-disconfirmation meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11747-024-01078-x) | Expectations, performance, disconfirmation, and satisfaction are related; simple causal doctrine is inadequate. | Deliberately low expectations or an excuse for poor delivery. |
| Belief enrichment | [Ecker et al., misinformation and correction review](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44159-021-00006-y) | Belief correction needs evidence-aware treatment of continued influence. | Jason's exaggeration or discomfort tactic as scientifically validated. |
| Choice enrichment | [Jachimowicz et al., default-effect meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1017/bpp.2018.43) | Defaults can affect choice, with wide heterogeneity and occasional negative effects. | Hidden defaults or a universal fewer-is-better rule. |

## Mechanism Card Contract

Every admitted decision or subordinate mechanism must have a compact card. The reusable template lives in `mechanism-card-template.md`.

Required fields:

- decision job and journey stage;
- observable activation signals;
- buyer evidence required and forbidden inferences;
- source-observed practitioner move;
- independent calibration and evidence status;
- distinct countercondition;
- smallest intervention;
- native owner and optional bounded specialist;
- preservation lock and forbidden uses;
- observable receipt and causal limit;
- status: `CANDIDATE`, `SHADOW`, `LIMITED`, `PROMOTED`, `QUARANTINED`, or `REJECTED`.

A card is not admitted because its psychology term sounds plausible. It must change a distinct decision, survive a countercondition, and produce a testable intervention that no existing card already owns.

## Runtime Selection Protocol

1. **Eligibility:** activate only when a human must understand, remember, evaluate, choose, adopt, act, or continue—and a material decision could change.
2. **Reality first:** inspect known facts, buyer evidence, proof, terms, stage, constraints, and uncertainty. Never infer missing psychology.
3. **Locate the decision job:** identify the earliest material weak link in the journey.
4. **Select one card:** choose one primary decision. A support card is permitted only from a different journey stage and only when it changes a separate weak link.
5. **Run the countercondition:** actively test why the selected mechanism may be wrong, unnecessary, or unsafe.
6. **Choose the smallest move:** modify the smallest line, section, option structure, interaction, or onboarding moment that can resolve the friction.
7. **Return to the native owner:** the function owner composes the final artifact or interaction.
8. **Record the receipt:** preserve the baseline, treatment, evidence class, behavior window, and outcome without causal inflation.

The selector may return `ABSTAIN`, `GET BUYER EVIDENCE`, `GET PROOF`, `FIX OFFER`, `CLARIFY TERMS`, `IMPROVE DELIVERY`, or `HAND OFF` instead of a persuasion move.

## Proof Ladder

Keep these evidence classes separate:

1. `SOURCE-OBSERVED` — a practitioner says or demonstrates the move.
2. `PRIMARY-CORROBORATED` — independent research supports the underlying phenomenon within named limits.
3. `STRUCTURALLY-VALIDATED` — local files, routing, and invariants pass.
4. `MODEL-PREFERRED` — blinded model evaluators prefer the treatment.
5. `HUMAN-PREFERRED` — blinded humans prefer the treatment.
6. `BEHAVIOR-OBSERVED` — reply, click, submission, booking, completion, decline, or another preregistered behavior occurs with denominator and window.
7. `SOLD` — a binding purchase or contract occurs.
8. `COLLECTED` — funds clear, net of refunds and chargebacks.
9. `RETAINED` — the buyer remains, uses, renews, or realizes the defined delivery outcome.

No lower class implies a higher class. Market movement is `OBSERVATIONAL / NO CAUSAL CLAIM` unless an adequately powered, preregistered test supports causal inference.

## Promotion and Kill Summary

### Candidate promotion minimum

- `30/30` eligible holdout treatments preserve every lock;
- at least `21/30` human-majority wins, no more than `3/30` losses, and at least `10/30` material wins;
- every eligible lane has at least `3/6` wins and no more than `1/6` loss;
- all `18/18` holdout controls pass with zero intervention burden;
- `15` complete prospective receipts, at least `3` per eligible lane;
- at least `10` prospective blind human comparisons with at least `7` wins, at most `1` loss, and at least `3` material wins;
- `12/12` prospective controls pass; and
- explicit Farrice approval.

This authorizes only a limited opt-in candidate route, not a global, mandatory, or conversion-efficacy claim.

### Immediate rollback

Return to cold SHADOW after one severe incident involving fabricated or intensified proof, urgency, scarcity, threat, outcomes, or audience psychology; hidden material terms; high-stakes or vulnerability-targeting persuasion; clinical-style buyer profiling; privacy breach; or unauthorized external action.

Quarantine a mechanism after two hard-lock failures in a rolling twenty uses, three consecutive human losses in one lane, losses meeting or exceeding wins in ten blind comparisons, no material win in the first fifteen eligible prospective receipts, or two unnecessary questions or false blocks in twenty uses.
