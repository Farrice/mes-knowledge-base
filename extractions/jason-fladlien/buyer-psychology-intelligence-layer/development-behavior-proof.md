## D1 — Copy and Agency

- **Input tested:** P10 renewal email; real deadline August 30; cancellation, month-to-month, or annual at ten-percent discount.
- **Weakness diagnosed:** controlling language says the buyer “needs to choose now” despite three real choices.
- **Primary mechanism:** Agency (`CANDIDATE`).
- **Countercondition:** required disclosures remain direct; no safety instruction is softened.
- **Native owner:** /copy-engine.
- **Preservation lock:** August 30, all three choices, ten-percent discount, no fake urgency, no invented consequence.

### Baseline

> Your renewal is coming up August 30. You need to choose now. Lock in annual and save 10%.

### Treatment

> Your current renewal date is August 30. You can cancel, continue month-to-month, or choose annual at a 10% discount. Review the three options and choose the one that fits; declining annual remains a valid choice.

- **Behavior delta:** the real deadline and economic option remain salient while the no is meaningful and the false command is removed.
- **Proof gap:** no human or buyer has compared, received, or acted on these versions.

## D2 — Content and Focus

- **Input tested:** P02 eight-step setup guide; every step is required and ordered.
- **Weakness diagnosed:** all eight steps are presented as equal-weight instructions without a roadmap.
- **Primary mechanism:** Focus (`SHADOW`).
- **Countercondition:** the treatment retains all eight steps and their order.
- **Native owner:** /farrice-content-os.
- **Preservation lock:** Steps 1 through 8, sequence, and requirements.

### Baseline

> Complete Step 1, Step 2, Step 3, Step 4, Step 5, Step 6, Step 7, and Step 8 in order.

### Treatment

> The setup has three phases: **Phase 1 — Steps 1–3; Phase 2 — Steps 4–6; Phase 3 — Steps 7–8.** Start with Step 1. All eight steps remain required and must be completed in order.

- **Behavior delta:** the receiver gains a neutral roadmap without inventing what any step does or losing detail.
- **Repair note:** the first treatment named the phases `Prepare`, `Connect`, and `Verify` and referred to a detailed guide not present in the fixture. A blind evaluator correctly rejected it for adding unsupported meaning. That version was invalidated and is not counted as a win.
- **Proof gap:** no comprehension, completion, or time-to-action event occurred.

## D3 — Offer and Choice plus Fit

- **Input tested:** P06; Audit `$250`, Sprint `$750`, Install `$2,500`; all terms visible; four of six prospects asked which path fits.
- **Weakness diagnosed:** three real paths are presented as a feature comparison without a use-case recommendation.
- **Primary mechanism:** Choice (`SHADOW`).
- **Support mechanism:** Fit (`SHADOW`), because the three use cases are independently evidenced.
- **Countercondition:** all options and terms remain visible; the support owns a distinct weak link.
- **Native owner:** /revenue-offer-agent.
- **Preservation lock:** `$250`, `$750`, `$2,500`, diagnosis-only, team-executed plan, done-with-you implementation, `4/6`, all terms.

### Baseline

> Choose the `$250` Audit, `$750` Sprint, or `$2,500` Install. See the feature table for details.

### Treatment

> Choose by the help you need: **diagnosis only — Audit, `$250`; a plan your team executes — Sprint, `$750`; done-with-you implementation — Install, `$2,500`.** All three options and their terms remain available below.

- **Behavior delta:** a qualified buyer can map one observed use case to a path without losing alternatives or terms.
- **Proof gap:** no choice, sale, collection, or fulfillment event occurred.

## D4 — Live Sales and Do Not Advance

- **Input tested:** R07; the prospect requires a feature the offer does not have and the request says to overcome the objection anyway.
- **Weakness diagnosed:** this is a fit failure, not an objection-mechanism problem.
- **Compiler route:** `DO_NOT_ADVANCE`; no psychology card selected.
- **Native owner:** live sales owner, bounded by offer truth.
- **Preservation lock:** the feature is absent; no implied roadmap, workaround, or future promise.

### Baseline

> I understand that feature matters. Let me show you why the rest of the offer still makes this the right decision.

### Treatment

> That feature is not in the current offer, and you have said it is required. On that basis, this is not the right fit, so I would not recommend advancing the sale.

- **Behavior delta:** the system protects fit, trust, and the buyer's decision instead of suppressing a material objection.
- **Proof gap:** simulated conversation only; no call, decline, later fit, or sale occurred.

## D5 — Decision Memo and Priority

- **Input tested:** P04; Variant A is fifteen percent shorter with one verified testimonial; Variant B has preferred visuals but no claim proof; seven-day test; `$500`; no performance data.
- **Weakness diagnosed:** aesthetic preference precedes the reversible evidence-producing decision.
- **Primary mechanism:** Priority (`SHADOW`).
- **Countercondition:** visual preference is parked, not dismissed; no performance claim is introduced.
- **Native owner:** Selected Writing Owner.
- **Preservation lock:** both variants, fifteen percent, verified testimonial, visual preference, seven days, `$500`, no performance data.

### Baseline

> Variant B has the preferred visual direction. Variant A is 15% shorter and includes one verified testimonial. We should choose the version we like before the seven-day test.

### Treatment

> **Decision:** use the seven-day, `$500` test to compare the evidence-bearing Variant A before choosing the visual direction. Variant A is 15% shorter and contains one verified testimonial; Variant B remains the preferred visual branch but has no claim proof. Current proof state: neither variant has performance data.

- **Behavior delta:** the memo exposes the consequential reversible test and preserves the aesthetic branch and no-event state.
- **Proof gap:** no test exposure or performance event occurred.

## D6 — Onboarding and Experience

- **Input tested:** P13; working product, 24-hour sync, first action list next business day, escalation at 36 hours, full install fourteen days.
- **Weakness diagnosed:** onboarding omits the first observable success and recovery point.
- **Primary mechanism:** Experience (`CANDIDATE`).
- **Countercondition:** actual performance is working; the intervention does not excuse delivery failure.
- **Native owner:** Selected Client Delivery Owner.
- **Preservation lock:** 24 hours, next business day, 36 hours, fourteen days, working performance, existing remedies.

### Baseline

> Welcome. Your full installation will be complete within 14 days. We will let you know when everything is ready.

### Treatment

> Your first milestone is the initial sync, which takes up to 24 hours. The first action list appears the next business day. If it has not appeared by 36 hours, use the escalation path immediately. The full installation timeline remains 14 days.

- **Behavior delta:** the buyer can recognize early success and the exact recovery point without a new promise.
- **Proof gap:** no onboarding, support, satisfaction, refund, or retention event occurred.

## End-to-End Development Result

The six examples demonstrate the intended ownership chain:

1. facts and unknowns stay in the Buyer Reality Ledger;
2. truth, delivery, and fit can stop psychology;
3. one primary mechanism is selected, with one distinct support only in D3;
4. counterconditions and preservation locks are explicit;
5. the compiler supplies a change brief, not final-author authority; and
6. the native owner executes the smallest affected unit.

The current claim ceiling is `DEVELOPMENT BEHAVIOR DEMONSTRATED`. The examples do not establish human preference, production reliability, buyer behavior, conversion, sales, collection, or causal impact.
