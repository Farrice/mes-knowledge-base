## Verdict

The registry contains thirteen decision cards: eight existing `SHADOW` mechanisms and five `CANDIDATE` mechanisms. A card becomes eligible because it owns a distinct buyer decision, has an observable activation signal, survives a countercondition, names the smallest honest intervention, and preserves native function ownership. A psychology label alone is never enough.

The five candidates remain cold. They may be used in development fixtures only; they do not alter owner pointers, routing, workspace authority, or promotion status.

## Card Admission Contract

Every card in the JSON registry records:

- identity, journey stage, status, native owner, and optional specialist;
- decision job, activation codes, observable signals, buyer evidence, forbidden inference, and earlier weak link;
- practitioner source, source-observed move, independent calibration, evidence status, and causal limit;
- smallest intervention, countercondition, truth-first handoff, preservation lock, and forbidden uses; and
- initial outcome class plus review or quarantine trigger.

The JSON is the verifier-bound canonical record. This document is the readable map. The reusable blank schema remains in `mechanism-card-template.md`.

## Selection Discipline

1. Establish that a human must understand, remember, evaluate, choose, adopt, act, or continue.
2. Build the Buyer Reality Ledger from facts, direct language, observable behavior, proof, terms, constraints, stage, and unknowns.
3. Resolve truth defects before psychology: unsupported claim, broken offer, unclear terms, or delivery failure.
4. Select the earliest material weak link represented by an observed friction code.
5. Use one primary card. A support card is permitted only for a separate, evidenced weak link at a different journey stage.
6. Run the countercondition and preservation lock.
7. Return the intervention to the native owner.
8. Record craft preference, behavior, sold, collected, refunded, and retained as separate evidence classes.

## Candidate Boundaries

### Evidence

Evidence is not “add testimonials.” It asks what the buyer cannot verify and which truthful proof object is diagnostic for that uncertainty. A popularity signal, warranty, or review can be irrelevant or misleading. Any unsupported claim returns `GET PROOF` before this card is applied.

### Agency

Agency is not covert “you are free to choose” language. It removes controlling language and protects a meaningful decline. It cannot soften required disclosures or conceal unequal choices. Any no-escape framing returns `DO NOT ADVANCE`.

### Value

Value makes an offer evaluable; it does not manufacture value through framing. Actual price, total cost, comparison basis, proof state, and alternatives remain visible. A weak or undeliverable offer returns `FIX OFFER`.

### Action

Action begins only after explicit motivation, fit, proof, affordability, and permission. A small yes is not permission for a larger commitment. The only admitted intervention is a voluntary, buyer-authored, reversible next plan.

### Experience

Experience is a delivery card, not a post-purchase persuasion tactic. It aligns promise, performance, onboarding, feedback, and remedies. Delivery failure returns `IMPROVE DELIVERY`; expectation framing may never substitute for performance or obstruct refunds.

## Evidence Boundaries

- Jason is the applied-practitioner source, not scientific or clinical authority.
- Independent research supports or qualifies underlying mechanisms; it does not validate Jason's scripts, explanations, ratios, or business outcomes.
- `Experience` is an explicit independent extension because the captured Jason corpus lacks an adequate post-purchase model.
- `Action` is primarily research-grounded; Jason's closing sequences are contextual, not implementation-intention evidence.
- `Priority` and `Congruence` remain practitioner observation plus operational synthesis where direct independent calibration is not asserted.

## Hard Vetoes

No card may invent or intensify beliefs, emotions, identities, urgency, scarcity, threats, status, proof, outcomes, or psychographics. No card may hide price, terms, options, limitations, or disclosures; diagnose a buyer; exploit health, financial, trauma, employment, housing, or status vulnerability; use affect as evidence; weaken the right to decline; or substitute framing for offer and delivery quality.
