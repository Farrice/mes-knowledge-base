## Failure Found and Repaired

The first D2 Focus treatment grouped eight steps under `Prepare`, `Connect`, and `Verify` and referred to a detailed guide not present in the fixture. The blind evaluator selected the baseline and flagged the invented meanings.

The repair:

- removed invented semantic phase names;
- removed the nonexistent guide reference;
- used neutral Phase 1, Phase 2, and Phase 3 grouping;
- explicitly preserved all eight required steps and fixed order; and
- corrected the evaluator packet to include the source requirement for a three-phase roadmap.

A fresh blind evaluator selected the repaired treatment and passed every preservation and manipulation lock. The initial loss and the intermediate incomplete-packet evaluation remain in `development-evaluator-receipts.json`; neither was deleted or counted in the final six-case result.
