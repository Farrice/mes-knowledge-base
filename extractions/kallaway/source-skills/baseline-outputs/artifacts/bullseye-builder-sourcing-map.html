The Sourcing Map

"Nobody makes content in my exact niche — who do I study?" The two-zone rule: topics come from the ring your bucket sits on; craft can come from anywhere. If a ring is empty of creators, that's not a problem — it's your opening: pull from the adjacent ring and re-aim at your founder.

🎯 TOPICS — from your bucket's ring

Narrow buckets (Claim Autopsies, Behind the Counter): source Rings 1–3. Broad bucket (AI Killed the Average Brand): source Ring 4 — then always re-aim the delivery at your founder. Never source topics from Ring 5.

🎨 CRAFT — from anywhere

Formats, hooks, storytelling structures, editing, pacing. A cooking creator's countdown format can carry a compliance topic. Study the best executors on the whole board, any niche.
