The Format Matrix

A format = a storytelling structure (rows — how the spoken words are organized) × a visual layout (columns — how it's shot and edited). They recombine freely. Tap any ⓘ for the plain-English explainer of that row or column.

Busting a myth × You on camera

Pick 1 · S4 × V1
What it is: take a thing your founder believes and show why it's wrong — you, straight to the lens, one take.
12-second example
"'Clinically proven' doesn't mean what your label thinks it means" — then one warning-letter receipt.
Skeleton
1. State the belief in his words → 2. Break it with one receipt → 3. Replace it with the decision to make.
