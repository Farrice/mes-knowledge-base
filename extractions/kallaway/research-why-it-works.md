# Why the Kallaway Play Works — and What It Teaches Ours

Research memo · 2026-08-27 · confidence-labeled, receipts inline

## Thesis

The free skills are not a lead magnet for the subscription — **they are the meter.** Kallaway gives away the reasoning layer (six SKILL.md files, zero marginal cost, infinitely copyable) precisely because each one is written to *spend* the layer he sells: his own Topic Scanner asks to deep-analyze 50 videos at 1 credit each, against a Pro plan that ships 100 credits a month. The giveaway and the consumption engine are the same artifact. Farrice cannot clone the middle layer — he owns no proprietary social dataset — so the only version of this that works for him meters **his judgment**, not API credits, and aims at the platforms Sandcastles structurally cannot see.

## Why This Matters

Read this as "give away Claude skills to sell a course" and you build a lead magnet that leaks, because the skills work fine standalone. Read it as "the free artifact must create a metered need only you can fill" and you build the funnel with the leak already closed.

## 4. The gap he leaves open

- [VERIFIED] Coverage is *"Instagram, Tiktok and YouTube Shorts"* (live sandcastles.ai copy). **No LinkedIn. No X. No newsletters. No long-form.** Farrice's actual buyer — a B2B service owner — is invisible to the entire machine.
- [VERIFIED] Without the subscription the skills degrade to *"the user pastes a list of top videos; label output as unvalidated by performance data"* (topic-scanner) and *"the user pastes links to 10–15 favorite niche videos and describes them"* (format-finder). The free experience is **paste your own links, receive output stamped unvalidated.** That stamp is the wedge.
- Uncovered, in order of neglect: LinkedIn post/format outliers → newsletter open-and-click outliers → B2B offer-and-proof patterns. None has a metered-data incumbent.

## 5. Risk check — is the outlier score commoditizing?

Yes on price, not yet on trust. [VERIFIED] Two credible tools give outlier detection away free (1of10 unmetered, ViewStats Basic), a third gives away the MCP transport, and OutlierKit undercuts Sandcastles 5:1 on credits. [LIKELY] What's left defensible is three things: multi-platform short-form coverage nobody matches, verified first-party channel analytics, and Kallaway's face. The score itself is a commodity within roughly 12 months.

## Counter-Reads

1. **Growth may be free signups, not skills.** 140K is self-published and likely counts free accounts; the May→June jump could be a promo or a definition change. If so the real engine is the 1M-follower channel, which Farrice doesn't have. Falsifier: any revenue or paid-seat number. None exists publicly.
2. **The metering could be honest cost, not design.** Analyzing 50 videos may just be what good analysis costs — and the skills gate credits carefully ("never spend an unapproved credit"). Steel-manned, the design is sincere, which is exactly why it converts.
3. **Skills may be a fad surface.** Anthropic reshapes the skills/connector UX quarterly; `.skill` bundles could be dead packaging by Q4. Hedge as he did — ship the reasoning as portable prose, not one vendor's file format.

## Recommended Next Move

Build the LinkedIn/B2B analogue and **meter the one thing that cannot be free-tiered: the verdict.** Ship three free skills that produce a genuinely useful artifact from data the user pastes in himself — no API dependency, so no leak to plug — and make the output's honest ceiling the offer: *"unvalidated against your buyer; a Signal Pilot validates it."* That inverts his structure. He degrades free output to sell credits; Farrice degrades free output to sell judgment, which nobody can price at $24.90/mo. Do not build a data layer. Do not resell outlier scores.
