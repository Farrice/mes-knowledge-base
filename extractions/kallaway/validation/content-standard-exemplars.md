# Content standard — what a production-grade ring/zone card reads like

Two exemplars assembled by the diagnosis pass ENTIRELY from files already on disk (dossier §4, whitespace-map W1–W4, topic-buckets + enrichment). This is the bar for every regenerated surface: verdicts first, receipts quiet, every word earning its place, immediately actionable. (Register contract: coach-to-reader + strategist authority, low jargon, quiet confidence — Farrice 2026-08-28, verbatim in genius.md.)

## Exemplar A — Ring 2 zone card (narrow buckets: The Defensible Claim, The Counter Files)

**Pillar:** Claim substantiation as a growth decision. Zero of the 50 top-performing videos in this niche touch claims, compliance, or message defensibility — and the only supplement-specific channel teaches launch mechanics instead. The lane is open, and the incumbent is actively manufacturing the problem you fix.

**What to make here:** *The $53,088 sentence — what one over-claim costs a supplement brand* · *Nine supplement homepages, one voice: the sea-of-sameness teardown* · *Why your honest claim loses to their lie, and the one way it wins.* Your buyers are already asking for this in their own words: "My ads are FDA compliant, but what can I do to not be flagged?" and "most brands look crowded because they're all selling the exact same formulas with the exact same marketing."

**Who to study, and for what:** Marketing Operators and Chew On This — watch them for *how an operating founder talks about a launch*, not for topics.

**The honest caveat, printed here not buried:** zero breakouts in this lane means no incumbent has cracked it — and also means no demand proof yet. Both readings stay on the table until batch 3.

## Exemplar B — Ring 4 zone card (broad bucket: The Fix Aisle)

**Pillar:** The mis-fix teardown. Every channel in this niche sells a mechanism; nobody runs the named critique. The raw material is the niche's own content.

**What to make here, and the weekly engine:** Mechanism Waves is the rising pool (12x median, all rows from the last 60 days, fueled by new-tool hope). This week's live waves, with sources: Meta's AI ad push "causing chaos for brands," ChatGPT in-chat checkout, Etsy's policy tightening plus a 12% staff cut. The move each time: grant what the mechanism does, then name the decision it cannot make.

**The re-aim rule, as a sentence:** borrow the news-jack skeleton (64x ceiling in this pool) and the weekly-wave format — then land it on the operating supplement founder by name in line one. Broad in audience, never broad in address.

**The trap at this altitude:** two of the three winning rows are one creator. That's a person winning, not a topic winning — source the shape, not the subject, or the channel drifts generic within a month.

## What makes these production-grade (the checklist per card)
1. A named pillar with the evidence claim built into the sentence, not appended.
2. Concrete things to MAKE (titles, not categories), tied to buyer language.
3. Named creators to study WITH the reason ("for how X, not for topics").
4. The trap/caveat stated plainly where the reader will act on it.
5. Zero process residue: no "proxy:", "(assumed base)", "on file", tier names, tool names.
6. Reads aloud like a sharp strategist talking to you — quiet confidence, no filler.
