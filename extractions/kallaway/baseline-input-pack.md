## The business behind the content

- **Offer ladder (canonical, ratified 2026-07-29):** free teardown (door opener) → **$750 prepaid Angle Map** — 60-minute live read + three named campaign angles, delivered within 48 hours, each angle with core belief, hooks, proof direction, and an editorial claim-safety line → **$2,500 prepaid Proof-to-Market Sprint** (founding pilot) — 10-business-day launch-messaging intensive, one revision round → $3,000–$5,000/mo recurring implementation (parked until paid proof exists; "never sell during the first 14 days").
- **What the Sprint delivers:** buyer-decision map, positioning + proof hierarchy, campaign-angle bank, launch messaging blueprint, one priority campaign asset. Five stages: Extract → Diagnose → Decide → Translate → Align. Deck optional, not the product.
- **The promise in the OS's words:** Angle Map — "Know which three arguments are worth building before you fund the campaign." Sprint — "Settle what to say, what approved proof supports it, and why the buyer should care."
- **Who pays:** authorized founder, Head of Brand, VP Marketing, or product-marketing lead at an already-selling supplement, performance-nutrition, recovery, or sports-science brand, generally below $15M revenue (battle card band: $1M–$50M growth-stage DTC, no senior creative-strategy seat). Founder with personal spend authority is the preferred cold buyer. Must have a **dated occasion inside 60–90 days**: launch, retail push, reformulation, new ingredient, or repositioning.
- **Scope boundary, his verbatim:** *"I don't build the ads, run the account, or write your calendar. You've got people for that. I decide what they should be making, and why."*
- **Ruled out:** content creation/ghostwriting, social-media management retainers, standalone GEO/AEO audits, solo-coach/trainer positioning, "AI-expert" branding.
- **Cash state (CAMPAIGN.md standing facts):** 15 researched candidates, 7 verified profiles, **0 sent, 0 held, 0 sold, $0 collected**; Day 1 blocked on the $750 payment link. Lifetime client cash ~$7,200, 100% warm 1:1, 100% health/fitness-adjacent.
- **Capacity:** one Sprint at a time, two per month max during founding phase.

## Pain points bank (ranked, from research files)

1. **"Sea of sameness"** — every ad library in the category looks like one brand wrote it [V]; "I lined up nine supplement homepages last week. I couldn't tell you which brand was which. Neither can your buyer."
2. **Founder Fog** — 73% of DTC founders can't articulate their positioning in one sentence without category jargon [2026-07-28 Gemini Deep]; "the market named Farrice's thesis."
3. **Compliance strangles claims** — FTC exposure $53,088/violation (corrected figure; older briefs say $43,792 — do not use), warning letters routine; "the biggest risk isn't under-promising. It's over-claiming without the data to back it up." [V/L]
4. **The honest-claim asymmetry (his About thesis, their lived Tuesday):** the honest claim needs a paragraph; the competitor's lie fits on a banner.
5. **LTV:CAC collapse** — below 3:1 the model breaks and founders go shopping for "positioning triage"; churn as a verdict on the promise.
6. **AI-content trust collapse** — consumer enthusiasm 60% → 26% (2023 vs 2025) [V]; "AI didn't flatten brand voice; it revealed that most brands never had one."
7. **Creative fatigue** — ads die in 7–14 days (down from 30+); "your winning ad didn't die because the market got bored. It died because you did." [V]
8. **AI search names competitors, not them** — "Our organic rankings are strong but ChatGPT recommends our competitors instead"; "If you're not in the AI answer, you're not on the shortlist." [V]
9. **Scaling wall** — hero-product brand at $1–5M can't expand portfolio/retail without real message architecture.
10. **The customer's BS-detector** — "You're paying more for the marketing than for what's actually in the bottle." [V]

## Delivery style (from VOICE-CARD v1.4)

- **Registers:** warm, direct, coaching-across-the-table; quiet defiance ("the settled tone of someone who stopped trying to convince anyone"); emotionally precise without sentimentality; confident not prescriptive ("here's what I've seen," never "here's what you must do"); imperfect on purpose — uniform polish reads as AI.
- **The one test:** would Farrice say this to a smart friend over coffee? The wince test is final — "effort showing" (jargon, trying to impress) is the failure he most fears.
- **Spoken, not writerly.** His natural unit is one short line: median message 45 characters, 71% under 60, zero of 30,606 over 200. Polished pieces run ~⅓ one-to-five-word punch sentences against 15–45-word pressure builds.
- **Speech is the ore; the page is the refinery** — he's a world-class speaker and an uneven writer; production starts from his spoken material; "when he has said the thing himself, use his words, not a better-written version of his words."
- **Spine choice:** Confession-I (his story is the proof) or Recognition-you (Bayer Mirror — reader's interior said back, one seven-word receipt after the cold open). Dictation-you is BANNED.
- **Humor:** reported speech only — the funny lives in what someone actually said; "lol" as tone-softener; self-deprecation before any roast; never write jokes for him.
- **Hard bans:** "Here's what/why/how" openers · "It's not X. It's Y." · twin-sentence endings · triple anaphora · >2 em dashes · cheap question closes · status-words ("thought leader") · ghost citations · italicized aphorisms.
- **Dial:** BLEND is default (~70% — "the better version of me"); MIRROR for DMs/personal; Practitioner Test governs topics (actively doing it → share it; not → don't post). Privacy law: family specifics are his to tell; other people's specifics default to soft reference or omission.
- **Taste signature:** bimodal grader — clear PASS or clear FAIL; 7.0–8.0 "almost there" is actively suspicious; structure without heartbeat caps at 5/10.
