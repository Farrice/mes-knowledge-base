## Understanding the /analyze Tool

The `/analyze` tool is a powerful feature within Sandcastles designed to help video creators by providing in-depth analysis of video content. This tool can be accessed through the Claude desktop app or directly within the Sandcastles web app.

## Accessing and Using the /analyze Tool in Claude

1. Navigate to Cowork: Open your Claude desktop app and select the "Cowork" tab.
2. Create a New Task: Click on "+ New task" to initiate a new prompt.
3. Invoke the /analyze Skill: Type `/analyze` followed by a space.
4. Paste the Video URL: Paste the URL of the video you wish to analyze (supports YouTube Shorts, Instagram, and TikTok).
5. Add Specific Prompts (Optional): You can further refine your request by asking for specific details. For example, you could ask for:
   - "Give me the full details, including transcript, format analysis, hook breakdown, etc."
   - "Analyze the video for its narrative structure and key takeaways."
6. Run the Analysis: Press Enter or click the send button to initiate the analysis. Claude will process the video and provide a detailed breakdown.

## Using the /analyze functionality in the Sandcastles Web App

1. Navigate to Videos: In the Sandcastles web app, go to the "Videos" section.
2. Add Video URL: Click on the "Add Video URL" button.
3. Paste the Video URL: A pop-up will appear. Paste the URL of the video you want to analyze into the provided field. The tool currently supports YouTube Shorts, Instagram, and TikTok.
4. Save Video: Click "Save video" to begin the analysis.

## Understanding the Analysis Output

Once the analysis is complete, you will receive a comprehensive report that typically includes:

- Performance Metrics: Views, likes, comments, outlier score, engagement rate, and posting date.
- Transcript: A full transcription of the video's audio.
- Hook Breakdown: Analysis of the video's hook, including its category, formula, and effectiveness.
- Format: Details about the video's category, type, flavor, and visual layout.
- Why This Format Works: An explanation of the strategic reasons behind the video's format and its potential impact.
- Narrative Structure: A breakdown of the video's structure into beats (e.g., Hook, Premise, Breakdown, Strategy Application, Call to Action) with corresponding timestamps.
- Topic/Angle/Substance: Identification of the video's core topic, the angle it takes, and its underlying substance or common beliefs it addresses.
- What Makes This Video Work (Meta-Pattern): An analysis of the underlying patterns or strategies that contribute to the video's success.
- Sources: The original source of the video.

## Bulk Analysis

The Sandcastles web app also offers a "Bulk Analyze" option, allowing you to analyze multiple videos at once. This is particularly useful when you have a list of video URLs you want to process. You can copy/paste bulk URLs into Claude Cowork to run this flow in Claude.
