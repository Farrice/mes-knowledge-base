## Understanding the /channels-recap Skill

The `/channels-recap` skill is a powerful tool within Sandcastles designed to provide a comprehensive analysis of a specific creator's recent content performance. It allows you to get a brief overview of what a channel has been doing, focusing on key metrics and insights.

## How it Works

1. Initiate the Skill: To use this skill, you'll type `/channels-recap` followed by the URL of the creator's channel (e.g., `https://www.tiktok.com/@briancochran`).
2. Specify Your Query: After providing the URL, you can ask specific questions about the channel's performance. For example, you might ask:
   - "What has Briar been winning with lately?"
   - "I wanna look back 30 days, and get a synthesis of his best performing topics and take."
   - "If there are any of his videos that I haven't deep analyzed yet, tell me, so we can analyze them."
3. Deep Analysis: The tool can perform a deep analysis of the videos. You can ask it to analyze the last 30 days, or even the last year. It can also analyze all videos from a specific niche or a set of channels.

## Example Usage

Let's say you want to understand the performance of Briar Cochran. You would input:

`/channels-recap https://www.tiktok.com/@briancochran`

Then, you can ask follow-up questions like:

"What has Briar been winning with lately? I wanna look back 30 days, and get a synthesis of his best performing topics and take. If there are any of his videos that I haven't deep analyzed yet, tell me, so we can analyze them."

## Interpreting the Results

The `/channels-recap` skill provides a detailed breakdown, including:

- Headline Analysis: Information on the title's effectiveness, including views, average outlier score, and follower count.
- Content Focus: Insights into what the creator's content is about (e.g., creator coaching, hooks, strategy, branding).
- What's Actually Winning: Identification of top-performing videos, often with specific metrics like views and engagement rates.
- Solid B-Tier Analysis: Videos that performed well but might have room for improvement.
- Videos You Haven't Deep-Analyzed Yet: Highlights videos that may require further examination.
- Worth Analyzing (ranked by ROI on your credits): A prioritized list of videos based on their potential return on investment.
- Pattern Identification: Analysis of recurring themes, hooks, and content styles that contribute to success.

## Advanced Usage

- Multiple Channels: You can analyze multiple channels by listing their URLs or using a tool to generate a list of channels from a specific niche.
- Custom Timeframes: Specify different timeframes for analysis (e.g., last 90 days).
- Deeper Insights: Ask follow-up questions to delve deeper into specific aspects, such as:
  - "Analyze the remaining top 3 and let me know if this changes the pattern."
  - "What are his content pillars?"
  - "What are his common hooks?"

The `/channels-recap` skill is designed to be flexible, allowing you to extract valuable insights by combining the tool's capabilities with specific, targeted questions.
