## Understanding the `/topics` Tool

The `/topics` tool within Sandcastles is designed to help video creators identify trending topics within their niche. It analyzes your watch list and provides a clustered summary of the most relevant topics, allowing you to see which themes are currently performing well.

## How it Works

When you use the `/topics` command, the tool will:

1. Analyze your watch list: It looks at videos you've watched in the last 30 days.
2. Rank by views: It ranks these videos based on their view count.
3. Identify topics: It then links all individual videos to their respective topics.
4. Cluster similar ideas: The results are synthesized into distinct themes, with 3-5 themes identified as currently working.
5. Provide examples: For each theme, it describes what makes it distinctive and cites 2-3 example videos from your watch list.

## Using the `/topics` Command

To use this tool, navigate to the "Cowork" section and type `/topics` into the chat input. You can then add specific prompts to refine your search. For example, you might ask:

`/topics What are the highest-performing topics right now in my niche in the last 30 days? Can you rank them in order based on views and then link all the individual videos per topic? When I say topic, I don't mean topic category. I mean the idea seed. I want to have clustered videos around the same idea, maybe with different takes. I want them separated at a granular enough level so that I can look and see should I make this or not.`

This prompt asks for:

- Top performing topics in your niche.
- Ranking based on views.
- Linking individual videos to topics.
- Clustering videos around a core idea with different takes.
- Separating these clusters at a granular level.

## Interpreting the Results

The tool will then process your request and return a list of topics, ranked by views. Each topic will include:

- The topic name: A concise description of the theme.
- View count: The total views for videos related to that topic.
- Example videos: A list of specific videos that fall under that topic, often with their view counts and source information.
- "Your fit" analysis: This section offers insights into how the topic aligns with your content strategy, suggesting whether it's a good fit or if you should skip it.

## Turning Insights into Action

Once you have the analysis, you can use it to inform your content creation. The tool provides:

- Top plays: Identifies the highest-performing topics.
- "Your fit" analysis: Helps you determine if a topic aligns with your brand and audience.
- Actionable insights: Suggests how to approach a topic, such as bundling micro-tips or replicating successful formats.

You can also ask the tool to turn this analysis into a live artifact that can be refreshed weekly or used to drill down into specific clusters.
