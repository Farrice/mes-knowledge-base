## Understanding the /video-suggest Tool

The `/video-suggest` command is a powerful tool within Sandcastles designed to provide you with ranked recommendations for your next video content. It analyzes your existing watch list and suggests relevant topics or formats based on what's performing well.

## How it Works

When you use the `/video-suggest` command, Sandcastles leverages its integration with various data sources to:

1. Analyze Top Performing Videos: It identifies the best-performing videos within your niche or on your watch list.
2. Identify Trends: It looks at what topics are currently trending.
3. Provide Ranked Recommendations: Based on this analysis, it offers a ranked list of suggestions for what you should create next.

This tool is designed to help you:

- Discover new content ideas: Get inspiration based on data-driven insights.
- Optimize your content strategy: Focus on topics and formats that are likely to resonate with your audience.
- Stay ahead of the curve: Understand what's working now and what might work in the future.

## Using the /video-suggest Command

To use this tool, simply type `/video-suggest` into the chat interface. You can then follow up with a more specific prompt, such as "What should I make today?" or "Suggest video ideas for my niche."

Sandcastles will then process your request and provide a list of ranked recommendations.

## Tips for Best Results

- Be specific: While a general prompt like "What should I make today?" works, providing more context about your niche or current content can lead to more tailored suggestions.
- Review the research: The tool often provides supporting research or data points for its recommendations. Take the time to review these to understand the reasoning behind the suggestion.
- Integrate into your workflow: Consider setting up automations or regularly using the `/video-suggest` command to ensure you always have a pipeline of relevant content ideas.
