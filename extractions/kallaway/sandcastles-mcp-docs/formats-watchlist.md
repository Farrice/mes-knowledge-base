## Understanding the /formats-watchlist Tool

The `/formats-watchlist` tool is a powerful feature within Sandcastles designed to help video creators analyze the performance of different video formats within their watchlists. This tool allows you to understand the distribution of the best-performing formats across your watch list, look back at the last X days of videos, and identify which formats are most successful.

## Key Features and Benefits:

- Analyze Watchlist Performance: Gain insights into the formats of videos you've already analyzed within your watchlist.
- Identify Top Performing Formats: Discover which formats are driving the most engagement and success.
- Break Out Visual Reports: Generate visual reports that highlight the top-performing formats.
- Explore Video Links: When you click on or ask for any of the formats, you can access individual links to the videos to watch them and gain inspiration.
- Discover Sub-Flavors: Identify specific sub-flavors within formats that you notice, allowing for a deeper understanding of what makes them successful.
- Automated Analysis: The tool can automatically process videos that meet certain criteria, saving you time and effort.

## How to Use /formats-watchlist

1. Access Cowork: Navigate to the Cowork section within Sandcastles.
2. Create a New Task: Click on "+ New task" to begin.
3. Input the Command: Type `/formats-watchlist` into the task input field.
4. Specify Your Request: You can then ask for specific insights, such as:
   - "I want to understand a distribution of the best-performing formats across my watch list."
   - "I want to look back at the last 60 days at all the videos that we've already analyzed across the watch list."
   - "I want to look at those formats and then I want you to break out a visual report showing me the top-performing formats."
   - "When I click on or ask for any of those formats, I want to be able to see the individual links to any of the videos below so that I can watch them and get inspiration."
   - "If there are sub-flavors that you notice within one format, break those out separately so that we know the differences."

## Understanding the Output

Once you run the `/formats-watchlist` command, Sandcastles will process your request and generate a report. This report typically includes:

- Data Summary: Information on the number of videos analyzed and the time period covered.
- Visualizations: Charts like a pie chart for "Format Mix (Video Count)" and bar graphs for "Total Views by Format Type" to visually represent the data.
- Detailed Breakdown: A list of specific formats, often ranked by performance, with details such as video count, total views, and peak outliers.
- Individual Video Links: For each format, you can often click through to see individual videos, allowing you to study them directly.

## Tips for Effective Use:

- Be Specific: The more specific your prompt, the more targeted and useful the results will be.
- Leverage Automation: Understand that Sandcastles can automatically analyze videos based on certain criteria, so you don't always have to manually trigger every analysis.
- Explore Sub-Flavors: Pay attention to the sub-flavors identified within formats, as these can offer nuanced insights.
- Use as a Starting Point: The reports generated by `/formats-watchlist` are excellent starting points for further research and inspiration.

By utilizing the `/formats-watchlist` tool, you can gain a deeper understanding of your content's performance and make more informed decisions about your video strategy.
