## Understanding the /channels-suggest Tool

The `/channels-suggest` tool is a powerful feature within Sandcastles that helps you discover new content creators based on your existing watchlist. It analyzes your current selections and suggests new channels that align with your interests and audience.

## How it Works

When you use the `/channels-suggest` command, Sandcastles looks at the channels you've already added to your watchlist. It then identifies creators with similar audience demographics, content strategies, or engagement patterns. This allows you to efficiently expand your research and find relevant content.

## Using /channels-suggest in Sandcastles

1. Navigate to the "Cowork" section in Sandcastles.
2. Click on "New task."
3. In the prompt area, type `/channels-suggest`.
4. Follow the command with your specific request. For example, you can ask:
   - "Can you pull the best five channels between 100 and 250,000 followers on Instagram based on my marketing watchlist?"
   - "Find the top 5 Instagram channels in the 100K-250K range, pulled from recommendations based on your Kallaway Marketing watchlist."
   - "Want me to add any of these to your watchlist, or pull a second batch (either under 100K, or 250K-1M)?"

Sandcastles will then process your request and provide a list of suggested channels, often including:

- The channel's username.
- Follower count and total views.
- A brief description of their content strategy (e.g., "Marketing + branding for creators and entrepreneurs. Algorithm breakdowns, hook frameworks, organic vs paid. The highest-engagement pick of the bunch – this view-to-follower ratio is wild.").

## Combining Tools for Complex Workflows

The real power of Sandcastles lies in its ability to chain these tools together. For instance, you could:

1. Use `/channels-suggest` to find a list of relevant channels within a specific follower range.
2. Use `/channels-add` to add these suggested channels to your watch list.
3. Then, use a tool like `/video-suggest` or `/video-watchlist` to analyze the performance or content formats of the channels you've just added.

This allows you to build sophisticated research workflows by combining multiple tools in a single prompt, automating complex tasks and saving you significant time.

## Example Workflow

Let's say you want to find the top 5 Instagram channels with 100K-250K followers based on your current marketing watchlist, analyze their top-performing videos, and then extract the hooks from those videos.

You could construct a prompt like this:

`/channels-suggest: Can you pull the best five channels between 100,000 and 250,000 followers on Instagram based on my marketing watch list? Then, for each of those channels, find their top 5 performing videos and extract the hooks from those videos.`

Sandcastles will execute each step sequentially, providing you with the requested information.
