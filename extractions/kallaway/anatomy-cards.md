# Anatomy Cards — Kallaway Growth System (Strategy Bundle)

Per-artifact teardown of the six declared outputs, from the six SKILL.md files under `source-skills/strategy-bundle/unpacked/` + setup PDF + PROVENANCE.md. Purpose: drive the surpass build.

Standing context: the bundle assumes Claude desktop + Sandcastles MCP, and the PDF gates the data layer — Sandcastles "must have Pro, Visionary, or Titan plan to access MCP." Every data-driven skill's declared fallback is manual input "clearly label[ed] 'unvalidated by performance data.'" That label is the free-tier user's actual experience — and our wedge.

---

## 1. Positioning Doc (`positioning.md` — Niche Interviewer)

**Anatomy** (7 sections, verbatim structure):
1. The business — offer, price point, buyer
2. Ideal Viewer Avatar — *named* archetype ("Tom/Tamara"), portrait paragraph, psychographic bullets
3. Dream outcome — one sentence
4. Pain point bank — ranked, "*add to this forever; source = comments + DMs*"
5. Target Authority Statement — final + two alternates ("I help X with Y" / "I help X do Y to achieve Z" / "Y for X")
6. Unique expertise inventory — 7-attribute hypothesis table (topic selection, substance depth, unique stories/proof, avatar specificity, delivery style, storytelling format, visual format), strong/possible/no + evidence, "to be validated against competitors"
7. Two filter questions — "*Would this be worth [avatar]'s time? Would it make them trust me more?*"

**Data dependencies**: None — zero MCP calls; a ~5-minute voice interview (6–10 questions, five blocks). Nothing to degrade; also nothing verified.

**Craft strengths**: Excellent interview mechanics. "One question at a time. Never stack." Reflect-back-as-sharpening ("reflect a *sharper guess* back… instead of asking a follow-up"). The Chair Test for person-level specificity. The anti-outsourcing push: "Who's a *real* person you've sold to who fits this?" Viewer=buyer check anchors content to revenue in Block 1. And real honesty: "this table is a *hypothesis* built from self-knowledge."

**Where it stops one level deep**: Everything is self-report, and the promised validation (step 2) checks only *competitor content*, never buyers — no comment mining, no review verbatims, no DM-language capture despite telling the user pain points "live in the wild." Psychographics stop at "believe, fear, have tried" bullets — no resistance mapping, no identity-level cost-of-admitting, no belief-chain to the offer. Pain rank = seller memory, not observed language frequency. No receipts anywhere.

**Surpass levers** (ranked): (1) **Substance** — McRaney-grade identity ICP: belief/resistance/identity layers plus buyer-language verbatims from real comments/reviews/DMs with URLs — the pain bank arrives pre-stocked, not a "grow it forever" IOU. (2) **Data** — cross-validate self-report against live audience language day one; VERIFIED/LIKELY/UNCONFIRMED labels. (3) **Form** — branded client-grade durable doc, not chat-folder markdown. (4) **Utility** — wire each pain point to the offer it feeds (pain → product → CTA).

**Surpass bar**: A judge reads both avatar sections: ours quotes ≥10 real buyers verbatim with sources; his contains zero external evidence — his own doc calls itself "a hypothesis built from self-knowledge."

## 2. Whitespace Map + Positioning Wheel (`whitespace-map.md` + HTML — Unique Positioning Builder)

**Anatomy**: Watchlist table (8–12 channels, five build paths) → per-channel 7-attribute grid → saturation map (niche default + crowding per attribute) → whitespace list ("note *which watchlist channels prove it's empty*" + unclaimed-vs-graveyard rating) → UPA verdict per claimed edge: **confirmed / contested / redirect** → one-paragraph Unique Positioning Angle. The Wheel: 7-slice radial, channel bubbles sized by followers, saturation heat (red crowded → green "WHITE SPACE — open lane"), "YOU?" stars, animated reveal, clickable channel cards with 7-row fingerprint strips, and a 5-beat teaching panel per slice (what it is → total menu → niche scoreboard → white space → what it means for you).

**Data dependencies**: Heaviest Sandcastles consumer — `ping`, `discover_channels`, `add_channels_to_watchlist`, `channel_recap` (free), `top_topics`/`top_formats`/`top_hooks`, gated `analyze_video` (1 credit, ~60s each) + `get_video_details`, `create_automation_rule` (outlier ≥2x). Fallback: pasted handles + web research, "clearly label the output 'unvalidated by performance data'" — the free user gets the signature artifact on vibes.

**Craft strengths**: Best-made skill in the bundle. The unclaimed-vs-graveyard test is real analytical craft: "a topic with zero videos is unclaimed; a topic with many low-outlier videos is a graveyard." The bluntness mandate: "A comforting whitespace map that hides a dominant incumbent costs the user six months." Exemplary credit UX ("Never spend a credit the user didn't approve"). The interaction contract — "A click that does nothing is a bug" — and 5-beat beginner panels are pedagogy most agencies don't ship. Scoring hygiene: "if every channel is in every slice… tighten it."

**Where it stops one level deep**: The 7-attribute scores are Claude's impressions — no per-claim receipts (which videos prove "substance depth: surface"?), no verification labels or confidence tiers. 8–12 channels on one platform is a thin sample sold as a market map; "what it would take to win" verdicts carry zero evidence. The Wheel is chat-scoped HTML: unbranded, no export, gone with the session. Behind the subscription gate the evidence layer evaporates while the artifact keeps its authoritative look.

**Surpass levers**: (1) **Data** — live outlier pack from open sources, no subscription gate: every attribute score cites ≥2 specimen videos with URL/view/date receipts. (2) **Substance** — the missing 8th dimension: *belief positioning* (what each competitor makes the audience believe), untouched by his attribute set. (3) **Form** — durable branded interactive doc + export; the Wheel survives the session. (4) **Utility** — each whitespace entry ends in a decision-ready move ("claim it with these 3 topics this batch").

**Surpass bar**: A judge clicks any competitor claim in both artifacts — ours opens receipts (specimen URLs + numbers) on every score; his has receipts only if the user paid for Sandcastles, and none at all on the fallback he himself labels "unvalidated by performance data."

## 3. Bullseye Map + 3 Visualizations (`bullseye-map.md` — Bullseye Builder)

**Anatomy**: Center Statement (`[person] who [situation] and struggles with [problem]`) → 5-ring ladder, each ring relaxing ONE constraint, with directional size/conversion/competition estimates → 3-2-1 mix (3 buckets × 2 videos +1 chaos = the 7-video batch; buckets live Rings 2–4 only — Ring 1 is "the person you *speak to*, not a content lane," Ring 5 banned) → candidate menu (8–10 buckets) + recommended trio + **bench** → chaos reserve (4–5 ideas, each "wild in a *different way*") → sample batch of 7 → calibration tracker template → sourcing rules → **Ring 5 trap list** (personalized too-broad examples with failure logic) → mantra: "Define. Hit. Expand. Aim smaller to grow bigger." Three HTML visualizations: the Bullseye (animated Define→Hit→Expand, size-vs-conversion bars), the 3-2-1 Mix (bucket arcs, no-fly hatching, card rack with live bench-swap, rebalancing toggle), the Sourcing Map (topics-from-your-ring vs. craft-from-anywhere + per-ring search terms).

**Data dependencies**: None — pure reasoning over the positioning doc; its search terms *feed* `discover_channels` downstream. He's honest about the numbers: "directional estimates, not fake precision."

**Craft strengths**: Densest thinking in the bundle. The algorithm-confidence causal model taught as plain cause-and-effect; personalized Ring 5 traps ("would LOOK like reasonable ideas to a beginner") — rare, effective negative-example teaching. Menu-not-verdict everywhere: "pick three that fit… their swap beats your ranking." The bench and chaos reserve give the doc a future. The reach-vs-conversion split ("will usually point at different buckets, that's by design") is an insight most creator advice misses.

**Where it stops one level deep**: Every ring estimate is invented — acknowledged, but unanchored even where data is fetchable. Buckets get a "strategic job" but no revenue wiring — nothing connects a bucket to offer economics (which bucket feeds the $X offer, what a Ring 2 vs Ring 4 lead is worth), so "rebalance toward what's working" has no dollar definition of *working*; conversion tracking is a blank template column. Single-platform short-form assumed throughout.

**Surpass levers**: (1) **Utility/revenue** — wire each bucket to offer economics: funnel role, lead value, and a dollar-defined "working =" per bucket, so calibration decides in dollars not views. (2) **Data** — anchor ring estimates with real proxies (search volume, actual channel counts per ring). (3) **Substance** — chaos candidates pre-screened against the whitespace map so the rogue slot doubles as a positioning probe. (4) **Form** — one durable branded board with the calibration tracker live in it, not three chat artifacts plus a markdown template.

**Surpass bar**: A judge asks "which bucket makes this business money, and how would you know by batch 3?" — ours answers with named offer linkage and a dollar-defined kill/keep rule per bucket; his answers with a blank tracker template and view-based rebalancing.

## 4. Topic Buckets + Top-50 Table + Performance HTML (`topic-buckets.md` + `top-50.md` — Topic Scanner)

**Anatomy**: `topic-buckets.md`: working-set definition → topic bucket table (5–8 clusters: count, median outlier, total views, top-3 linked examples; one-channel-dominated buckets flagged — "a person winning, not a topic winning") → format bucket table → bullseye overlay (ring tag per bucket; broad-skewed data means "consciously protect their narrow picks") → the 3 chosen buckets (2 narrow + 1 broad, weighted on performance / positioning fit / substance advantage) → refresh note "~6–8 weeks." `top-50.md`: ranked table — rank, **✓/✗ with a two-or-three-word reason on every ✗**, bucket (★ on chosen buckets), one-liner, channel, views, outlier, video link, `sandcastles_url` — "**Every ✗ row is rendered with a strikethrough**… a beginner learns as much from the struck rows as the ✓ ones." HTML: performance bars (chosen buckets "crowned") + bullseye placement.

**Data dependencies**: `search_my_videos` (~180-day, outlier sort — "it normalizes for channel size"), `analyzed` flags, gated `analyze_video` ×50 + `get_video_details`, `top_topics`/`top_formats`; automation rule as the standing fix. Credits framed as infrastructure: "spent once and reused four times." Fallback: user pastes videos, "label output as unvalidated by performance data."

**Craft strengths**: The ✓/✗ strikethrough table is the bundle's best pedagogy — "a visible verdict, not a silent drop," exclusions kept in rank position and overridable. Honest trap flag: "A high-performance bucket with zero substance advantage is a trap." Budget empathy (top-25 cut, "the map will be coarser"). Sound stats instincts (median outlier over totals, one-channel-dominance discount).

**Where it stops one level deep**: Outlier score is a *reach* proxy; the 3 buckets are picked with zero conversion or revenue signal — not tied to offer economics, so the "trust and conversion engines" claim is asserted, never measured. Fully subscription-gated: without Sandcastles, the "shared data core" four downstream skills read is user-pasted anecdote. 180 days/50 videos/one watchlist = one platform's recent past; no trend direction (rising vs. decaying bucket). Sandcastles links are receipts of a sort, but why-it-won claims carry no verification labels.

**Surpass levers**: (1) **Data** — ungated live outlier pack: same top-50 with receipts, no plan requirement, plus trend deltas per bucket. (2) **Utility/revenue** — a conversion column mapping each bucket to funnel role and offer, so "pick 3" optimizes for buyers not views. (3) **Substance** — per-bucket *why it works* mechanism, not just stats. (4) **Form** — durable sortable branded table with refresh runs appending history, not regenerated markdown.

**Surpass bar**: A judge asks both tables "which of these 50 makes the avatar *buy*?" — ours ranks on a modeled reach×conversion score with the mechanism named per bucket; his ranks on outlier alone and requires a paid Sandcastles plan to exist at all.

## 5. Format Playbook + Structure×Layout Matrix (`format-playbook.md` — Format Finder)

**Anatomy**: Built on the core split — "**the storytelling structure** (how the spoken words are organized) and **the visual layout** (how it's filmed and edited)… separating them is the unlock." Structure database (10 named skeletons, beat-by-beat, stats + linked examples) → layout database (7 styles, with "difficulty/effort level for a solo business owner") → combination matrix, whitespace pairs flagged → 2–3 test picks scored on best-vehicle-for-their-information / proven-in-niche (max one whitespace bet) / fits-their-constraints / carries-all-3-buckets → hero-format rule ("testing ends, reps begin"). Naming rule, four things always together: plain-English name ("Countdown list" not "listicle"), one-sentence what-it-is, 12-second example, real specimen video. Matrix HTML: thumbnail-filled cells ("thumbnails, not numbers, so a beginner reads it by recognition"), dim "nobody's doing this" cells with whitespace glow, "Try these 3 first" strip.

**Data dependencies**: Reads `top-50.md` as "the data core," layers `top_formats`, pulls `get_video_details` for 8–12 videos (narrative structure + `thumbnail` URLs — free on analyzed pool). Thin pool (<15) → run topic-scanner first or gated top-up. Fallback: user pastes 10–15 favorites, classified from titles/descriptions, "label the output accordingly."

**Craft strengths**: The structure/visual decomposition is a real conceptual contribution — recombination thinking ("a ranking structure can be shot as a talking head or a cinematic b-roll piece, and those are different videos"). The four-part naming rule is the bundle's strongest plain-English discipline. Honest production-cost scoring ("a cinematic layout is a bad pick for someone filming between client calls"). The hero-format endgame gives testing a terminus.

**Where it stops one level deep**: Format-performance attribution is confounded and unflagged — a structure's median outlier bundles topic, channel, and hook quality; no isolation of the format's contribution, no verification labels on "why this combo works here." Inherits the scanner's gates: subscription-locked thumbnails and payloads, single-platform sample, no conversion signal (a format that reaches isn't a format that sells). The matrix — the bundle's most visual artifact — is chat-scoped HTML that can't be sent to anyone.

**Surpass levers**: (1) **Substance** — per-format *mechanism* card: the retention/psychology reason the skeleton works plus its failure mode, so picks transfer instead of copying winners. (2) **Data** — cross-niche specimen sourcing for the craft layer (his own sourcing-map doctrine, under-used here) with receipts. (3) **Form** — branded exportable matrix with embedded specimens; a client could receive it. (4) **Utility** — per-pick production recipe at the user's real constraint level (gear, minutes per video).

**Surpass bar**: A judge asks of a recommended format "why does this work, and will it work for *my* topic?" — ours answers with a named mechanism and transfer conditions; his answers "it's proven in-niche" with stats that can't separate the format from the channel that used it.

## 6. The Growth-System State-Folder Pattern (Orchestrator)

**Anatomy**: A routing skill that "writes no content itself — it navigates." One-breath mental model (Strategy once → 7-video production loop → weekly feedback loop). Phase map with per-file state, real dependency edges only ("enforce these; ignore ceremony"), per-video rhythm ("Every video is its own chat; the `growth-system/` folder is the memory, not the chat history"). Routing: read folder → find deepest completed step → confirm in one line → invoke, never describe → "Never make the user re-explain." Returning users route via `idea-batch-N.md` statuses (`idea → scripted → posted`); 7+ silent days → nudge Channel Coach. Standing rules for every routed session: credit gating, voice-first, beginner-first glossing, one CTA per session (shortform.academy), answer-then-return for off-map asks.

**Data dependencies**: `ping` only; otherwise reads the folder. Missing skill → "do a best-effort inline version rather than blocking." Note the funnel: the four production-loop skills the map references (`engine-builder`, `topic-brainstormer`, `video-maker`, `channel-coach`) are *not in the free bundle* — the map itself is the upsell surface.

**Craft strengths**: Folder-as-memory is the pattern most worth stealing — durable state across ephemeral chats, each skill reads/writes named files, handoffs prove context ("Your bullseye centers on high-end plastic surgery patients in LA — the Topic Scanner will screen against that"). Route-by-invoking. Honest expectation setting ("this is a system, not a hack… the users who hit 100K are the ones still running the loop in month four"). One-CTA discipline keeps six skills from becoming six ads.

**Where it stops one level deep**: State is write-once markdown — no schema, no versioning, no staleness detection beyond hand-noted cadences nothing enforces. No cross-artifact consistency check: edit positioning.md and the downstream docs silently rot. The feedback loop's learning lives in prose ("batch bias written into the system files") — no structured performance memory. And the map funnels to one destination that isn't the user's business.

**Surpass levers**: (1) **Utility** — enforced refresh cadence: dated state files, orchestrator flags staleness and drift. (2) **Data** — a structured performance ledger (per-batch, per-bucket numbers) instead of prose coach-logs, so calibration compounds. (3) **Form** — the state folder doubles as a client-visible living dashboard. (4) **Substance** — dependency checks that validate *content* coherence (does the trio still match the current whitespace verdict?), not just file existence.

**Surpass bar**: A judge changes the positioning and asks each system "what's now stale?" — ours names the affected artifacts and the refresh order; his reads file presence only and cannot tell that the whole downstream chain just went out of date.

## Cross-Cutting Patterns

**Adopt outright (7):**
1. **Folder-as-memory** — named state files as the spine; chats ephemeral, artifacts durable, every skill reads before it asks. "The folder is the memory, not the chat history."
2. **Reflect-back interviewing** — mirror every answer in one tightened sentence; sharpen by *guessing more specifically* instead of stacking follow-ups; never re-ask known info.
3. **Credit bills** — itemized cost + list + "approve, trim, or skip" before any paid call; cheaper cut offered. Maps directly onto our cost-gate doctrine.
4. **Plain-English glossing** — every term of art defined inline at first use; four-part naming (plain name / one-liner / 12-second example / specimen); "the artifact should need no external glossary."
5. **Menu-not-verdict** — recommend a trio, keep the bench visible; "their swap beats your ranking."
6. **Chaos agent + reserve** — one structured rogue slot per batch, candidates each "wild in a different way," unused ones banked; exploration with a hard boundary (never Ring 5).
7. **Teach-the-beginner viz panels** — 5-beat slice panels, ✓/✗ strikethrough rows, "a click that does nothing is a bug." Visualizations that teach, not decorate.

Also worth keeping: negative-example pedagogy (Ring 5 trap lists) and honest-hypothesis labeling ("to be validated against competitors").

**System-level gaps (4):**
1. **No receipts** — competitor, format, and why-it-works claims never carry URLs-as-evidence or VERIFIED/LIKELY/UNCONFIRMED labels; the self-reported interview layer is never cross-validated against real buyer language.
2. **No identity depth, no revenue wiring** — the avatar stops at demographics+psychographics (no belief/resistance mapping, no verbatims), and buckets never touch offer economics — the system optimizes reach proxies while claiming to optimize trust with a buyer.
3. **Subscription-gated data** — the whole evidence layer requires a paid Sandcastles plan; the declared fallback ships the same authoritative-looking artifacts "unvalidated by performance data."
4. **Chat-scoped artifacts** — the signature HTML outputs are session-bound, unbranded, unexportable, single-platform; nothing a client could receive, nothing that survives.
