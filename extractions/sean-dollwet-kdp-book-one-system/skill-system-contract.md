## Step order

1. Initialize the cockpit and proof ledger.
2. Run the operator interview and define low-risk topic boundaries.
3. Scan 5–10 current niches and approve one evidence-backed topic.
4. Build the source dossier, reader promise, outline, metadata draft, and rights plan.
5. Approve one gold-standard chapter before drafting the rest.
6. Complete manuscript, claim, originality, redundancy, and reader-quality gates.
7. Produce and approve a rights-cleared cover and upload package.
8. Run policy preflight; stop at upload permission.
9. After explicit permission and external publication, measure discovery, sales, refunds, and net collection separately.

## Handoff summary

Each stage passes only the approved artifact path, compact decision, evidence class, open risk, and next gate. Raw transcripts, rejected concepts, and full upstream context stay cold.

## Composition rule

Sean Dollwet remains the function owner. A supporting component gets one bounded job and cannot change the reader, promise, evidence state, or approval status. The conductor reconciles every contribution into the cockpit; expert names are not integration proof.
