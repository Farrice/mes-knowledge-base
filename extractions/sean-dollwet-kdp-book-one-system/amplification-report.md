# Amplification Report

## Primary amplification

Sean's useful method is preserved: choose a market before a manuscript and draft in controlled sections. It is amplified into a replayable system by adding current policy truth, persistent state, stage-specific evidence, editorial gates, approvals, and an outcome ledger.

## Corrections

- BSR and review counts are signals and creator heuristics, not universal pass/fail laws.
- AI editing improves quality but does not erase KDP disclosure duties or automatically establish copyright.
- Review exchanges, points, compensation, sentiment-conditioned asks, and close-relationship reviews are excluded.
- PDF is a print format and a limited ebook input, not the universal Kindle default.
- Amazon traffic and new-release timing do not guarantee discovery or sales.
- Formats become measured distribution surfaces; they are not called income streams before revenue exists.

## New operating loop

`INTERVIEW → MARKET DOSSIER → NICHE APPROVAL → BLUEPRINT → OUTLINE APPROVAL → GOLD CHAPTER → MANUSCRIPT QA → COVER APPROVAL → COMPLIANCE → UPLOAD APPROVAL → DISCOVERY → SOLD → NET COLLECTED`

The first eight transitions are local and reversible. Upload and every later external action require explicit permission.
