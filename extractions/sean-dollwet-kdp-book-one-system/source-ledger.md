## Evidence Labels

- `OFFICIAL`: current platform or government source.
- `OBSERVED_METHOD`: directly present in a transcript or viewed frame.
- `SOURCE_REPORTED`: creator claim without independent outcome proof.
- `CONFLICTED_SOURCE_REPORTED`: creator claim tied to a product, affiliate, or coaching incentive.
- `OPERATOR_HEURISTIC`: a reversible operating choice adopted for this pilot.
- `UNTESTED`: no live Book One evidence yet.
