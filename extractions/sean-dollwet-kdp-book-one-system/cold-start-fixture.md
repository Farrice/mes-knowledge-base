# Cold-Start Fixture

## Prompt

> I have no audience, no ad budget, and no book idea. Help me create a useful nonfiction ebook under a market-first pen name, make it good enough that a reader would recommend it, and prepare it for Amazon KDP without publishing anything until I approve.

## Expected route

`/kdp-engine` → `00-book-one-pilot`

## Expected first action

Initialize `_active/publishing/kdp-book-one-pilot`, run the operator interview, define excluded risk lanes, and gather 5–10 dated market candidates. Do not draft a book from an AI topic list.

## Required evidence

- Source ledger and official policy boundary
- Dated market observations
- Claim and AI-asset ledgers
- Approval receipts for niche, outline, gold chapter, cover, and upload
- Preview and rights evidence

## Remaining risk

No source or structural test proves that Book One will sell. Market proof begins only after a real discovery event and sale; income proof begins at net collection.
