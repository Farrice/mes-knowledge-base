# Cross-Vertical Transfer

## Input

- Original product/offer/page/content/client objective.
- Target buyer.
- Conversion surface.
- Existing copy, creative, or concept.

## Transfer Map

| Apparel Mechanic | Cross-Vertical Translation |
|---|---|
| Shirt says something about buyer | Offer/page names who the buyer becomes or what they privately know. |
| Fast visual recognition | Headline or opening moment creates immediate "that's me" recognition. |
| Specific design | Specific buyer scene, pain, desire, or inside language replaces generic market label. |
| Public social currency | Buyer imagines looking smart, prepared, funny, advanced, or understood by sharing/using it. |
| Familiar plus unexpected | Known problem/category plus unexpected truth, mechanism, or angle. |
| Feeling before reason | Copy creates desire/relief/recognition before stacking rationale, proof, or features. |

## Steps

1. Load `references/source-ledger.md` and `references/genius-patterns.md`.
2. Name the source timestamp anchors used.
3. Identify the buyer self-statement.
4. Compress the recognition moment.
5. Replace broad market language with a specific person and scene.
6. Add the future social/professional reaction.
7. Build familiar/twist phrasing.
8. Put emotion before proof, then let proof support the feeling.
9. Separate `Source Mechanics` from `Domain Extrapolation`.
10. Produce a before/after transformation.

## Output

- Transfer diagnosis.
- Trigger Fit Table.
- Before/after copy or concept.
- Behavior delta.
- Risk and next gate.
