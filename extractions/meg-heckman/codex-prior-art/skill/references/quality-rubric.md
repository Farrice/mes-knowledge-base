# Quality Rubric

Use this rubric before calling a buyer-trigger output ready.

| Criterion | Pass | Revise | Kill |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity Signal | Buyer can say what the product says about them. | Buyer identity is implied but soft. | Product only describes an interest or category. |
| Recognition Speed | Right buyer gets the hit quickly. | Requires a short caption or small context bridge. | Requires explanation, lore, or decoding. |
| Specificity | Concept paints a person and situation. | Niche is visible but not vivid. | Broad category label. |
| Social Currency | Buyer can imagine a reaction, gift, share, or public moment. | Reaction exists but is weak. | No one has a reason to mention it. |
| Familiar/Twist | Familiar element plus surprising true twist. | Familiar or twist side is underdeveloped. | Too obvious or too confusing. |
| Emotion First | Feeling arrives before rational reason. | Feeling exists but copy/design leans on logic. | Utility, price, or aesthetics carry the whole sale. |
| Original/IP Safety | Original expression with no protected logos, lyrics, event names, or copied designs. | Needs naming/reference cleanup. | Depends on protected or copied material. |
| Print/Product Fit | Works at product-card and real-use size. | Needs simplification or placement fix. | Too detailed, fuzzy, or unwearable. |

## Minimum Pass

A concept is testable when it passes identity signal, recognition speed, specificity, and original/IP safety. Social currency, familiar/twist, and emotion-first should be strengthened before paid traffic or client delivery.
