### Output contract for the new workflow

The workflow should produce:

1. **Current-strategy diagnosis:** what the brand is repeating and whether the proposed variants are structurally different.
2. **Evidence table:** verified product truths, customer evidence, unsupported assumptions, and prohibited claims.
3. **Three whitespace hypotheses:** one each for product/use-case or life stage, adjacent identity/community/distribution, and language/geography.
4. **One chosen hypothesis:** selected with explicit upside, risk, and reason for rejecting the other two.
5. **Creative handoff packet:** audience, buying situation, angle, proof requirement, and exclusions.
6. **Smallest valid test:** metric, sample, stop condition, and what result would disconfirm the hypothesis.

### Quality and behavior gates

A rewritten hook with the same audience, problem, buying situation, channel, and market **fails**.

Product properties and customer facts need evidence links; health/science claims remain blocked until independently cleared.

Language expansion requires cultural validation, translation ownership, legal/licensing review, channel economics, and a native-speaker check.

Analogous past work cannot be presented as direct e-commerce proof.

The frozen fixture passes only if the workflow changes a strategic variable and produces a testable decision.
