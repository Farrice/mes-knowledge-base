## Naming Decision

The Vision used **Profit-Finder Market Whitespace Scan**. Architecture refines this to **Profit-Finder Opportunity Scan**.

The source phrase `Profit Finder` is retained as provenance. `Opportunity Scan` is more accurate because account absence does not prove market whitespace or profit. The workflow still distinguishes account, category, and market evidence internally.

## Workflow Execution Contract

Phase | Required behavior
---|---
0. Strategy fingerprint | Record product truth, audience/segment, consequence/problem, buying situation/use case, channel/surface, geography/language, offer, proof burden, and destination; classify the proposal `COSMETIC`, `STRUCTURAL`, or `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`
1. Evidence boundary | Record fact or claim, source, evidence state, use state, and missing proof; every selected hypothesis remains `UNTESTED`
2. Three-lane scan | Produce at most one hypothesis for product/use case, adjacent identity/community/distribution, and language/geography; each names the changed variable, evidence, reachable surface, dependency, risk, and state
3. One-hypothesis lock | Compare evidence strength, product fit, structural difference, reachability, testability, operating burden, and risk; lock one or hold all without fake numeric precision
4. Creative handoff | Pass hypothesis ID, audience, situation, product truth, channel, locale, angle direction, available and missing proof, excluded claims, and may-change/may-not-change fields
5. Smallest valid test | Name control, variant, one primary strategic variable, fixed adaptations, channel, supplied sample/window parameters, metric, guardrail, stop rule, falsifier, and `UNTESTED / NO EVENT`

If product or buyer evidence is absent, return `EVIDENCE HOLD` plus the shortest discovery queue. If the target market is already locked and the request is for copy or assets, route directly to the existing Creative Strategy Brief.

## Workflow Output Contract

1. Evidence receipt with `SUPPLIED`, `SOURCE-REPORTED`, `INFERRED`, `UNAVAILABLE`, and `PROHIBITED` labels.
2. Current strategic fingerprint and cosmetic-versus-strategic diagnosis.
3. Account, category, and market evidence-scope table.
4. One product/use-case hypothesis, one adjacent-surface hypothesis, and one geography/language hypothesis; any lane may return `HOLD`.
5. One selected hypothesis or `NO EVIDENCE / HOLD`, with explicit rejection reasons for the others.
6. Strategic delta table showing changed and held-constant fields.
7. Compact Creative Strategy Brief handoff with proof requirements, exclusions, destination implications, and falsifier.
8. Smallest valid test design with metric, comparison, non-target constants, stop condition, and next branch.
9. Proof-state footer that does not claim execution or market performance.

The workflow produces no hooks, ads, creative concepts, persona deck, localization package, outreach, or live test.
