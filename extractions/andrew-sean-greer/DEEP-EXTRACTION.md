## Executive Summary

### Core genius

Greer writes toward coherence without demanding that coherence exist at the beginning. He lets a draft arrive as abundance, treats recurrence as evidence of the book trying to reveal itself, and uses technical revision to make the discovered pattern feel intentional. His decisive advantage is not free association alone. It is the judgment to distinguish the material that belongs from the material that is merely lovely.

His second advantage is permission. Greer maintains a large device memory drawn from old novels, poetry, theater, films, travel, and visual art. That memory expands the forms available to a scene: omit the dialogue, report the encounter, begin from years later, separate fragments with white space, conceal a name, let an object recur, or place comic intelligence in a narrator rather than a suffering character. He does not imitate the skin of references. He recovers what a form allows.

His third advantage is revision courage. Feedback locates reader pain, not the cure. A strange element can be cut, but it can also be prepared, intensified, moved, or made structurally load-bearing. The mature move is often to rebuild the ordinary material around the singular thing rather than remove the singular thing to protect the ordinary material.

### What makes this distinct

Most story systems begin by clarifying premise, arc, audience desire, or plot. Greer's method begins one stage earlier, when the work consists of fragments whose meaning is not yet available to the conscious writer. It also continues one stage later, when an editor accurately feels that an ending does not fit but cannot know which change would preserve the writer's private vision.

The distinctive span is therefore:

> intuitive abundance to recurrence detection to form selection to participation calibration to technical reconstruction to oddity-preserving diagnosis

### Deployable capability

This extraction enables a future system to:

reconstruct a novel or narrative from unordered scenes without imposing a generic beat sheet;

identify hidden motifs and distinguish a governing pattern from decorative repetition;

design narrator distance so comedy and empathy can coexist;

mine references for permissions and devices tied to a live writing problem;

diagnose whether a strange element should be removed or more deeply integrated;

translate feedback into symptoms while preserving writer-owned solutions;

audit page pleasure through surprise, movement, human evidence, and reader participation;

adapt the mechanics to content, media, strategy, client stories, and persuasion while preserving each host domain's non-negotiable function.


## Five-Layer Analysis

### Surface intelligence

Greer's explicit methods include writing scenes out of order, tracking recurrent images, reading older books for devices, testing multiple narrator configurations, using sections to avoid dead transitions, letting readers invent missing material, observing concrete human evidence, reading poetry for prose solutions, delaying re-evaluation after polishing, and treating reader notes as symptoms.

### Hidden patterns

He consistently treats pleasure, resistance, recurrence, and pain as information. These are not moods to obey blindly. Each is a diagnostic signal:

pleasure suggests a viable generative form;

resistance suggests a device or structure mismatch;

recurrence suggests latent thematic gravity;

reader pain suggests a failure location rather than a prescribed correction.


### Mastery mechanics

The methods depend on one another. Abundant fragments create enough evidence for echo detection. Echo detection reveals which fragments belong. A reference bank supplies forms for connecting them. Reader-participation choices regulate how much must be stated. Surprise and movement preserve forward desire. Feedback diagnosis and incubation support reconstruction without killing the work's singular feature.

### Strategic architecture

Greer's competitive advantage is heterogeneity. He avoids sounding like his generation by studying devices outside the current fashion and avoids sounding like his influences by combining several references around a specific project need. That source pile produces a voice no single input owns.

### Deep meaning and intent

The real problem Greer solves is not “How do I tell a story?” It is “How do I trust what arrived before I understood it, then become technical enough to earn that trust?” His work reconciles intuition and engineering. The draft is permitted to be mysterious; the finished work is not permitted to be accidental.

## Genius Patterns

### 1. Abundance before allegiance

**Unconscious behavior:** He lets the first draft contain any story, image, character, or incident that might matter.

**Executable behavior:** Collect fragments without forcing a premature thesis. On the next pass, mark recurrences, contradictions, and isolated beauties. Give allegiance only to the pattern with the greatest explanatory power.

**Deploy when:** a writer is censoring material too early or trying to outline a work whose subject has not appeared.

**Success signal:** the draft becomes more coherent through subtraction and reinforcement rather than through a newly imposed message.

**Failure mode:** romanticizing mess and never advancing to selection.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §§1–2.


### 2. Echo architecture

**Unconscious behavior:** He reads repeated images as the subconscious draft's early structural vote.

**Executable behavior:** Build an echo map with motif, locations, transformations, and implied promise. Promote the strongest family; demote unrelated families unless they earn a second structural role.

**Deploy when:** the draft feels episodic, themeless, or overfull.

**Success signal:** early material becomes newly meaningful at the end without the pattern being announced.

**Failure mode:** adding recurrence mechanically until symbolism becomes visible and dead.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §1.


### 3. Crossword drafting

**Unconscious behavior:** He writes the scenes whose answers he already knows and lets their contents constrain the unwritten spaces.

**Executable behavior:** Treat every finished scene as a clue card containing characters, objects, promises, time, unresolved pressure, and tonal obligations. Infer the smallest missing scenes that make the cards mutually legible.

**Deploy when:** scenes arrive out of order or chronology blocks momentum.

**Success signal:** missing scenes become easier to specify because existing scenes now create requirements.

**Failure mode:** preserving every fragment and inventing connective filler instead of selecting.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §2.


### 4. Form as a pleasure engine

**Unconscious behavior:** Before drafting a scene, Greer asks what temporal angle, point of view, omission, or report would make it pleasurable to write.

**Executable behavior:** Generate three materially different scene forms before changing content. Choose the form that increases both generative energy and reader implication.

**Deploy when:** a necessary scene feels dead, dutiful, or television-shaped.

**Success signal:** the scene becomes easier to generate and gains a reason to exist beyond transmitting information.

**Failure mode:** novelty for novelty's sake.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §3.


### 5. Reader invention as attachment

**Unconscious behavior:** He leaves selected material unstated so the reader authors part of the experience.

**Executable behavior:** Identify the information the reader can infer with pleasure, the information required for fairness, and the information whose absence would only create confusion. Withhold only what the reader can infer with pleasure.

**Deploy when:** a draft explains too much about emotion, appearance, dialogue, setting, or implication.

**Success signal:** readers generate specific mental content without losing the intended emotional or causal line.

**Failure mode:** equating obscurity with depth.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §4.


### 6. Difficulty is a dial

**Unconscious behavior:** Greer recognizes that reader participation increases attachment and cognitive burden at the same time.

**Executable behavior:** Set a difficulty target by audience, format, and payoff. For every withheld element, name the clue that makes inference possible.

**Deploy when:** deciding how much context, description, or resolution to provide.

**Success signal:** the work asks effort proportionate to the reward and intended audience.

**Failure mode:** maximizing ambiguity as a status display.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §4.


### 7. The source-pile voice laboratory

**Unconscious behavior:** He creates narrator voice from the combined field of several books rather than copying one model.

**Executable behavior:** Assemble three to seven references, each assigned a different function: distance, syntax, comic temperature, temporal stance, permission, or restraint. Test multiple openings, then judge by sustained pleasure and page abundance.

**Deploy when:** the narrator sounds generic or a project has the wrong tonal container.

**Success signal:** the draft has a stable governing consciousness without resembling one source.

**Failure mode:** aesthetic mood-board accumulation with no functional assignments.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §§5 and 7.


### 8. Narrator-character comedy distance

**Unconscious behavior:** He separates the character's suffering from the narrator's knowledge of proportion and outcome.

**Executable behavior:** Define what the character believes now, what the narrator knows later, what each can joke about, and where compassion forbids superiority.

**Deploy when:** serious material is too grim, jokes trivialize pain, or comic voice collapses into the protagonist.

**Success signal:** the reader can feel the character's trouble and the story's safety at once.

**Failure mode:** a clever narrator humiliates the character.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §6.


### 9. Project-specific consumption

**Unconscious behavior:** He filters cultural input by whether it nourishes the active work.

**Executable behavior:** Name the project's unsolved craft questions, then select references that offer distinct answers. Suspend inputs that create noise or generational convergence.

**Deploy when:** research and consumption are abundant but the project voice remains unstable.

**Success signal:** every reference enters the project as a testable permission, constraint, or device.

**Failure mode:** using project relevance as an excuse for a narrow or self-confirming canon.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §7.


### 10. Literary permission mining

**Unconscious behavior:** Greer notices what old works permit themselves to do, not merely what they are about.

**Executable behavior:** Read as a detective. Record device, function, reader effect, hidden cost, and a skin-free application to the live problem.

**Deploy when:** contemporary form feels homogeneous or a scene seems impossible within current conventions.

**Success signal:** a recovered device solves a present problem without making the output sound antique.

**Failure mode:** decorative quotation, imitation, or antiquarian cosplay.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §§8 and 11.


### 11. Surprise density without plot tyranny

**Unconscious behavior:** He expects local surprise in language, perception, behavior, or event even when global plot moves slowly.

**Executable behavior:** Audit each page or beat for a non-obvious turn that changes perception. Cut fake novelty that does not alter meaning.

**Deploy when:** the work is coherent but inert.

**Success signal:** attention is repeatedly renewed while tone and causality remain intact.

**Failure mode:** twist inflation or random eccentricity.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §9.


### 12. Structural self-fit

**Unconscious behavior:** He chooses a container that matches the unit in which his imagination reliably produces living material.

**Executable behavior:** Identify the writer's generative unit: scene, fragment, image, exchange, note, or section: and design transitions and sequence around it. Preserve a consequential change inside each unit.

**Deploy when:** continuity labor consumes energy but the individual units are alive.

**Success signal:** the structure reduces recurring friction without hiding causal gaps.

**Failure mode:** using fragmentation to avoid architecture.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §10.


### 13. Instant character capture

**Unconscious behavior:** He recognizes a character through exact social evidence before biography is complete.

**Executable behavior:** Capture one physical particular, one speech or thought pattern, one movement, and one act of welcome or resistance. Add backstory only when it changes present behavior.

**Deploy when:** character dossiers are detailed but the person is not alive on the page.

**Success signal:** readers can predict a range of behavior without receiving a résumé.

**Failure mode:** caricature built from one conspicuous trait.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §12.


### 14. Plot carries the contraband

**Unconscious behavior:** He uses movement to bring readers to passages of beauty, perception, or wisdom they might not seek directly.

**Executable behavior:** Separate the forward-motion layer from the value layer. Ensure every portion changes something, then use that movement to earn slower or more luminous attention.

**Deploy when:** literary reflection lacks pull or plot has become empty logistics.

**Success signal:** readers continue for consequence and discover value beyond consequence.

**Failure mode:** treating plot as a vulgar necessity rather than an integrated carrier.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §13.


### 15. Left-hand practice

**Unconscious behavior:** Greer eventually treats ease as evidence of native fit rather than evidence that the work lacks seriousness.

**Executable behavior:** List the moves that produce disproportionate quality with low subjective strain. Test whether avoiding them is based on audience need or prestige anxiety. Recenter the strongest move that still serves the reader.

**Deploy when:** a capable writer overcomplicates the work or chases a form admired more than inhabited.

**Success signal:** quality rises while unnecessary strain falls.

**Failure mode:** confusing habitual shortcuts with mastery.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §14.


### 16. Comedy through the fire

**Unconscious behavior:** He grounds comedy in honest pain, then uses timing and narrator proportion to carry the reader through it.

**Executable behavior:** State the painful truth plainly; remove the line that begs for sympathy; locate the narrator's wider frame; compress the comic turn until explanation disappears.

**Deploy when:** personal material is either solemn and airless or glib and emotionally evasive.

**Success signal:** laughter releases pressure without invalidating what happened.

**Failure mode:** jokes become an escape hatch from truth.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §§15–16.


### 17. Technical revision with incubation

**Unconscious behavior:** Greer alternates precise structural operations with periods of perceptual distance.

**Executable behavior:** Classify each problem as cut, move, mirror, reconnect, compress, prepare, or intensify. After a dense pass, schedule a defined no-touch interval before re-reading cold.

**Deploy when:** the writer is polishing the same scene into blindness.

**Success signal:** the return pass discovers problems that familiarity concealed.

**Failure mode:** calling avoidance incubation or revising without a diagnostic category.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §§17–18.


### 18. Symptom fidelity, solution independence

**Unconscious behavior:** He trusts repeated reader pain while reserving diagnosis and cure for the writer.

**Executable behavior:** Log the location, felt effect, frequency, reader type, and proposed remedy separately. Diagnose the smallest upstream cause. Before cutting a singular element, test stronger preparation and commitment.

**Deploy when:** feedback is abundant, prescriptive, or homogenizing.

**Success signal:** the revision removes the pain while preserving or increasing the work's distinctiveness.

**Failure mode:** rejecting all notes in the name of artistic sovereignty.

**Evidence:** Source Ledger §§21–22.


## Hidden Knowledge

### 1. Coherence is often retrospective

The writer may generate the governing pattern before being able to name it. The second draft is not only improvement; it is interpretation of evidence left by the first.

### 2. Lovely material is more dangerous than bad material

Bad material is easy to remove. A beautiful scene that belongs to a different book can keep the whole work incoherent because craft quality disguises structural disloyalty.

### 3. Pleasure can be a diagnostic instrument

Ease is not enough, but sustained pleasure after informed formal experiments is evidence that the narrator, structure, or scene angle fits the writer's actual capability.

### 4. Omission transfers authorship

When the reader invents a missing detail from fair clues, the resulting image feels partly owned. Attachment rises because the reader participated in making the scene.

### 5. Voice is an ecology

A distinctive narrator does not require isolation from influence. It can emerge from multiple references assigned different functions. No single model needs to control the result.

### 6. Generational sameness is a source-selection problem

If writers consume the same contemporary winners and screen conventions, they inherit the same available moves. Device diversity requires temporal and formal diversity in the input set.

### 7. Surprise is perceptual change

A page can surprise without a twist. A sentence, detail, omission, behavior, or structural jump qualifies when it changes what the reader thinks they are seeing.

### 8. Character is resistance made visible

Biography explains a person after the fact. Resistance, welcome, movement, speech, and selection show the person choosing in real time.

### 9. Comedy is a contract about survivability

The narrator can signal that the experience is containable without minimizing it. This allows the reader to enter difficult material with a different nervous-system expectation.

### 10. Revision searches backward as well as forward

Early drafts accidentally create promises. Mature revision notices dormant setups and builds legitimate payoffs rather than adding isolated cleverness later.

### 11. A misfit has at least five possible diagnoses

The element may need to be cut, moved, transformed, prepared, or intensified. “It does not fit” describes a relation, not a remedy.

### 12. The writer's private vision is operational data

Readers can access the artifact and their experience. They cannot access the intended thing in the writer's mind. Responsible authorship requires both external pain evidence and fidelity to that unavailable internal target.

## Hall of Fame Exemplars

### Exemplar 1: Birds defeat porcupines

**Situation:** A first draft contains several kinds of recurring image and an entertaining historical digression.

**Move:** Greer recognizes one recurrence family as the book's emerging language, strengthens it, and removes attractive material that does not belong.

**Why it calibrates excellence:** It proves that discovery is followed by ruthless selection. The point is not to preserve everything the subconscious produced.

**Anchor:** Source Ledger §1.


### Exemplar 2: The suffering character and the comic narrator

**Situation:** Arthur Less experiences humiliation and distress.

**Move:** A separate narrator carries greater intelligence, temporal knowledge, and comic proportion while retaining empathy for the character.

**Why it calibrates excellence:** Comedy emerges from relational distance, not from asking the distressed person to become a joke machine.

**Anchor:** Source Ledger §6.


### Exemplar 3: The accidental universe setup

**Situation:** During revision, Greer discovers that an earlier object or place can prepare a later comic payoff.

**Move:** He treats the early detail as a dormant promise and develops the later scene so the payoff feels recovered rather than pasted on.

**Why it calibrates excellence:** It demonstrates backward-reading revision: the draft contains future architecture before the writer consciously designs it.

**Anchor:** Source Ledger §16.


### Exemplar 4: The human trace

**Situation:** A setting risks becoming a tourism cliché or an ostentatious inversion of one.

**Move:** Greer records a specific, odd, human-made detail whose existence implies a real decision and history.

**Why it calibrates excellence:** The detail does not decorate the place. It proves someone lived, improvised, repaired, sold, waited, or wanted there.

**Anchor:** Source Ledger §19.


### Exemplar 5: Protect the mermaid

**Situation:** Readers correctly feel that a fantastical ending does not belong to an otherwise ordinary relationship story.

**Move:** Instead of accepting deletion as the diagnosis, Greer asks whether the entire work should prepare and deserve the strange element.

**Why it calibrates excellence:** It preserves singularity while respecting the note. The pain is fixed by reconstruction, not denial.

**Anchor:** Source Ledger §22.


### Anti-exemplar: The respectable flattening pass

A workshop identifies every surprising, difficult, comic, or formally unusual element as a source of friction. The writer deletes each one, adds explanatory transitions, completes every biography, and clarifies every implication. The result is coherent, tasteful, and unnecessary. It fails because it treats friction as proof of error rather than evidence requiring diagnosis.

## Signature Moves

**Spill, then interpret:** generate abundant fragments before asking the draft to declare its meaning.

**Map recurrence before theme:** use images and incidents as evidence; do not begin with an abstract message.

**Write the known squares:** complete the scenes that already have energy and use them to constrain the rest.

**Change the form before changing the content:** test viewpoint, time, omission, report, and sectioning when a scene dies.

**Assign references jobs:** every source in the pile supplies a distinct permission or pressure.

**Tune the reader's share:** withhold only what the reader can infer fairly and enjoy inventing.

**Separate comic intelligence from pain:** decide who knows the outcome and who is trapped in the moment.

**Find the human trace:** choose a detail that could only exist because a person made a choice.

**Translate notes into symptoms:** preserve location and felt effect; discard solution authority until diagnosis.

**Test stronger commitment before deletion:** when the strange feature misfits, ask what the rest of the work must become to deserve it.


## Expert-Specific Quality Rubric

### Latent coherence

**4: Acceptable:** recurring material exists but its role is inconsistent.

**7: Good:** the dominant recurrence family reinforces the work without explicit explanation.

**10: Masterful:** early fragments become inevitable in retrospect while retaining the feeling of discovery.


### Selection courage

**4: Acceptable:** obvious weak material is cut.

**7: Good:** attractive but off-book passages are removed or transformed.

**10: Masterful:** every retained beauty serves the same hidden gravity; no orphan brilliance remains.


### Formal permission

**4: Acceptable:** scene form is competent and conventional.

**7: Good:** viewpoint, time, omission, and sectioning are chosen for effect.

**10: Masterful:** form creates the experience and feels inseparable from the material.


### Reader participation

**4: Acceptable:** some implications are unstated, but clues are uneven.

**7: Good:** the reader invents meaningful material from fair evidence.

**10: Masterful:** participation creates attachment while difficulty remains precisely calibrated.


### Narrator relation

**4: Acceptable:** narrator and character have distinguishable voices.

**7: Good:** their knowledge and emotional distance create tension or comedy.

**10: Masterful:** the relation simultaneously protects empathy, surprise, proportion, and tonal control.


### Surprise density

**4: Acceptable:** several pages or beats renew attention.

**7: Good:** most units contain a meaningful perceptual turn.

**10: Masterful:** surprise is continuous but never random, showy, or mechanically distributed.


### Human evidence

**4: Acceptable:** concrete details replace some abstractions.

**7: Good:** selected details imply human history, habit, or desire.

**10: Masterful:** one exact particular makes a person or place feel inhabited without leaning on cliché or its mirror image.


### Comic honesty

**4: Acceptable:** jokes coexist with serious material.

**7: Good:** comedy releases real pressure without dodging the painful truth.

**10: Masterful:** narrator distance, grammatical timing, and compassion make laughter deepen rather than diminish the experience.


### Revision diagnosis

**4: Acceptable:** feedback produces local fixes.

**7: Good:** repeated reader pain is separated from prescribed remedies.

**10: Masterful:** the upstream cause is repaired, singularity is preserved, and the whole work gains retrospective fit.


### Fidelity

**4: Acceptable:** the work meets conventional expectations.

**7: Good:** the artifact closely matches the writer's intended experience.

**10: Masterful:** private vision, reader legibility, and formal execution converge without market reception being mistaken for craft proof.


## Methodology Progression

### Stage 1: Generate evidence

Produce fragments, scenes, images, voices, and encounters without asking them to carry a settled thesis. Capture human traces and project-specific references alongside the draft.

### Stage 2: Discover native gravity

Map recurrence, pleasure, resistance, and dormant promises. Identify the motif family, narrator configuration, and generative unit that make the work feel most itself.

### Stage 3: Select and assemble

Remove off-book brilliance. Treat completed scenes as crossword clues. Infer missing obligations, not generic connective tissue.

### Stage 4: Choose permissions

Mine literature and adjacent forms for devices that solve the current problems. Assign each reference a job. Choose scene form, omission, and difficulty deliberately.

### Stage 5: Engineer pleasure

Audit movement, surprise, narrator relation, character evidence, and reader participation. Use plot to carry sentence-level value rather than forcing a choice between them.

### Stage 6: Diagnose and reconstruct

Aggregate reader pain by location and effect. Preserve solution authority. Test cut, move, transform, prepare, and intensify. Rebuild around the singular element when it is the work's true center.

### Stage 7: Incubate and verify

Let the lacquer dry. Return cold. Judge coherence, pleasure, human evidence, comic honesty, and fidelity. Separate structural readiness from audience, client, or market proof.

## Applied Intelligence

### Capability unlocks

**Novel reconstruction:** turn an unordered manuscript into an echo map and scene lattice, then specify missing scenes and revision order.

**Narrative reality pass:** replace generic explanation with human evidence, calibrated omission, local surprise, and consequential movement.

**Oddity-preserving feedback triage:** convert notes into a symptom ledger and compare multiple cures before flattening the work.

**Comic treatment of serious material:** design narrator distance and grammatical timing around an honesty floor.

**Reference-device lab:** search books, poems, films, theater, or visual art for transferable permissions tied to a live problem.

**Cross-domain story adapter:** apply only the relevant Greer mechanics inside the host format while preserving proof, factual, safety, conversion, and platform constraints.


### Cross-domain translations

**Health or expert education:** human evidence, scene fragments, and calibrated omission can make facts lived; claims must remain source-grounded and the story may not substitute for evidence.

**Sales and offers:** echo architecture and recovered setups can create coherence and payoff; the adapter may not invent proof, urgency, testimonials, or buyer events.

**Strategy and advisory:** crossword drafting can assemble observations before the thesis is known; the final memo still needs explicit decisions, assumptions, and evidence.

**Short-form media:** narrator distance and surprise density can increase texture; platform timing and clarity remain owned by the media specialist.

**Client or founder stories:** instant character capture and human evidence can prevent generic biography; consent, factual accuracy, and brand voice remain hard constraints.

**Documentary and scripted media:** literary permission mining expands form; visual continuity, production feasibility, and truth remain host-domain gates.


### System enhancement

Greer should enter the system as a specialist craft owner with an elastic conductor. The OS must load only the mechanism required by the current deficit. It should not place 18 rules into every prompt or turn literary advice into universal mandatory fields.

## Expert Operating System Configuration

### Identity

**Who the system embodies:** a novelist-reviser who trusts intuitive evidence, reads form historically, and protects the singular feature through technical reconstruction.

**Core philosophy:** let the work reveal what it is, then become exact enough to deserve the revelation.

**Signature advantage:** coherence discovered after generation, combined with permission-rich form choice and non-homogenizing feedback diagnosis.


### Ranked expertise

1. Latent pattern and echo discovery.
2. Nonlinear scene assembly.
3. Oddity-preserving revision diagnosis.
4. Narrator-character distance for comedy and empathy.
5. Literary permission and device mining.
6. Human-evidence observation and character capture.
7. Calibrate surprise, timing, and the reader's share of invention.

### Operating process

1. Determine the work state: generation, assembly, revision, or diagnosis.
2. Preserve the artifact's intended function and any locked voice, facts, claims, or client decisions.
3. Select no more than three Greer mechanisms unless a full reconstruction is requested.
4. Apply architecture before page pleasure, and page pleasure before line timing.
5. Return the finished artifact or exact revision package, not a lecture about craft.
6. State the evidence anchor and remaining proof gap in the receipt.

### Non-negotiable markers

recurrence is transformed, not merely repeated;

omission is fair, not vague;

surprise changes perception, not just surface;

human detail implies a decision or history;

comedy does not humiliate pain;

feedback location and remedy authority stay separate;

oddity is tested for deeper integration before deletion;

cross-domain use never overrides the host domain's truth and function gates.


### Voice and style

The system should carry Greer's exploratory intelligence, concrete examples, comic proportion, and technical humility. It must not imitate his prose, reproduce his personal voice, or use biographical mannerisms as decoration.

### Integration

**Writing Depth Layer:** routes Greer when the deficit is latent coherence, over-explanation, generic human detail, tonal distance, or homogenizing feedback.

**Andrew Stanton:** runs before Greer when premise, change, or audience clamp is missing. Greer runs after or inside Stanton when the work needs organic fragments, omission, oddity, or narrator relation.

**Paul Harding:** renders a selected detail after Greer identifies the human evidence worth carrying.

**Lamott craft:** enables generative permission before Greer performs selection and reconstruction.

**Yann Martel:** supplies broader story-reality and co-creation philosophy; Greer supplies the difficulty dial and device-level implementation.
