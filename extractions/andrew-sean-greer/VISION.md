## Vision

Build a narrow, practitioner-grade **novel craft and revision companion** that helps a writer discover the book already latent in a messy draft, strengthen its hidden pattern, choose structural permissions from literary history, and preserve the strange material that makes the work singular.

This is not a general writing OS. Its home lane is fiction and narrative long-form. Its distinctive promise is:

> Turn intuitive fragments into a coherent, surprising novel without sanding away the oddity that made the material worth writing.

## Senior-Partner Verdict

The interview is rich enough for a forge-scale build, but the scope should stay bounded. Greer owns the middle passage between generative discovery and finished architecture: finding echoes, selecting the book's native form, creating narrative pleasure, diagnosing reader pain, and revising toward stronger commitment.

## Uniqueness Audit

The roster already covers architecture, perception, cadence, dialogue, emotional truth, and reader co-creation. Greer's defensible additions are the following connected mechanisms:

1. **Echo Architecture:** detect recurring images and incidents, remove unrelated motifs, and intensify the pattern without making it obvious. The birds-versus-porcupines example begins at [01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=65s).
2. **Crossword Drafting:** write the scenes that are knowable and pleasurable first. Completed scenes reveal constraints and clues for the missing scenes. Greer explains the method at [03:55–04:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=235s).
3. **Narrator/Character Comedy Distance:** the character may experience pain while a narrator who knows the ending establishes safety, proportion, and comic permission.
4. **Literary Permission Mining:** read old and unfashionable books as a device library. Study what a work permits itself to omit, compress, conceal, or leave to the reader.
5. **Structural Self-Fit:** choose a form that removes the writer's recurring friction, such as using white-space sections instead of forced transitions.
6. **Surprise Density:** require a meaningful surprise of image, language, dialogue, character, or event on every page, without reducing literary fiction to plot. The phrase arrives at [21:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=1293s).
7. **Instant Character Capture:** build vivid characters from observed speech, movement, dress, resistance, and social behavior rather than compulsory biography.
8. **Left-Hand Practice:** locate the capability that feels suspiciously easy because it is native, then stop hiding it behind more prestigious difficulty. Greer develops the idea at [36:34–37:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=2194s).
9. **Comedy Through the Fire:** convert personally serious material into comedy through honesty, timing, and proportion. Touch the hard material briefly enough that comedy can carry the reader through it.
10. **Grammatical Timing:** revise comic and emotional pace through compression, omission, paragraph breaks, and ellipsis rather than explanation.
11. **Recovered Setups:** find an object, place, or promise planted unconsciously and make the later scene cash it in.
12. **Lacquer-Dry Revision:** after polishing a scene, create enough distance for perception to reset before diagnosing it again. The painter analogy appears at [55:41 and 57:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=3341s).
13. **Editor-as-Patient Diagnosis:** feedback identifies where the reading experience hurts; the writer owns the diagnosis and cure. The discussion starts at [1:09:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=4185s).
14. **More Mermaid:** when strange material does not fit, test stronger preparation and commitment before defaulting to deletion. The example begins at [1:11:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=4267s).
15. **Human-Evidence Observation:** collect details that prove a place was made and inhabited by people; avoid both cliché and its performative opposite.

## Existing-System Fit

**`writing-depth-layer` remains the conductor.** It owns architecture-first routing and vertical dosing. Greer supplies a fiction-specific discovery-to-revision method and is called from that conductor.

**`andrew-stanton-audience-engineering` keeps audience architecture.** Stanton owns spine, change, engagement, and surprising/inevitable endings. Greer adds latent motif discovery, non-linear scene assembly, and narrator-comedy distance.

**`lamott-craft` gets material onto the table.** It retains draft permission, inner-critic work, and relational writing practice. Greer takes the next pass: pattern selection and technical reconstruction.

**`paul-harding-lyric-prose` owns rendering.** Harding keeps perception, exact detail, cadence, and sentence music. Greer owns arrangement, omission, page-level surprise, and narrative pleasure.

**`yann-martel-storytelling-mastery` keeps reader participation.** Martel owns co-creation and satisfying/mysterious design. Greer adds the difficulty dial and a broader library of recovered devices.

## Use Map

**Draft-to-novel reconstruction:** high deployment value and high differentiation for books, fiction, and narrative IP.

**Strange-material preservation:** high deployment value and high differentiation for client manuscripts and distinctive long-form work.

**Literary device archaeology:** medium deployment value and high differentiation for creative direction and research-led writing development.

**Comedy from serious material:** medium deployment value and high differentiation for personal narrative, essays, scripts, and brand storytelling.

**Writer career guidance:** low value for this build. Keep it as reference material, not a core workflow.

## Recommended Build Shape

**Skill:** `andrew-sean-greer-novel-craft`.

**Role:** specialist companion under the existing writing-depth conductor.

**Size:** 10–12 workflows, not 15 padded commands.

**Workflow families:** draft discovery, revision architecture, narrator/character relation, page pleasure, comedy timing, feedback diagnosis, and literary permission mining.

**Evidence discipline:** one local ledger with timestamped anchors. Interviewer interpretations must be separated from Greer's own method.

**Prompt discipline:** one execution prompt per distinct deliverable. Six to eight prompts should cover the likely output families without duplicating them.
