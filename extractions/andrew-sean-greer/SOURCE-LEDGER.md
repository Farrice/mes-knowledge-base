## Evidence anchors

### 1. Echo architecture

**Anchor:** [00:00:31–00:03:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=31s)

**OBSERVED:** Greer spills abundant material into a first draft, then uses the second draft to notice repeated images and discover what the book is about. His concrete test is birds versus porcupines: strengthen the recurring family and remove lovely material that does not rhyme.

**INFERRED capability:** A recurrence map can expose the draft's latent subject before the writer can state its theme.


### 2. Crossword drafting

**Anchor:** [00:03:53–00:05:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=233s)

**OBSERVED:** Write scenes that are already knowable or pleasurable, even out of order. Like filled answers in a crossword, completed scenes reveal constraints and clues for the missing scenes.

**INFERRED capability:** Scene fragments can be treated as evidence-bearing nodes rather than as failed chronology.


### 3. Scene-form permission

**Anchor:** [00:05:11–00:08:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=311s)

**OBSERVED:** Greer chooses point of view, temporal distance, omission, and reporting based on what makes a scene fun to write. He contrasts literary options with television-shaped scenes that enter a room, record everything, and leave.

**INFERRED capability:** Diagnose a dead scene as a form-choice problem before treating it as a content problem.


### 4. Reader co-creation and the difficulty dial

**Anchor:** [00:08:51–00:11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=531s)

**OBSERVED:** A narrator can omit the final sentimental explanation and let the reader invent rooms, appearances, dialogue, and implications. Greer says readers become more attached to what they help create, while acknowledging that more invention also makes the reading experience harder.

**INFERRED capability:** Withholding is a calibrated participation mechanism, not automatically sophistication.


### 5. Narrator source pile and pleasure signal

**Anchor:** [00:11:39–00:13:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=699s)

**OBSERVED:** Greer assembles a pile of books whose combined sound approximates the narrator he wants, tries multiple starts, and recognizes a viable voice when pages become pleasurable and abundant even if some will later be cut.

**INFERRED capability:** Generative ease after informed experimentation is diagnostic evidence of voice fit.


### 6. Narrator-character comedy distance

**Anchor:** [00:13:12–00:14:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=792s)

**OBSERVED:** The narrator of *Less* is not Arthur Less. The narrator is smarter, funnier, and knows the story is a comedy; Arthur is upset and does not. Knowledge of the ending lets the narrator create safety without denying the character's pain.

**INFERRED capability:** Comedy can live in the relation between consciousnesses, not only in jokes or events.


### 7. Project-specific consumption vector

**Anchor:** [00:14:46–00:18:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=886s)

**OBSERVED:** While writing, Greer becomes attuned to books, films, plays, and music that help the current project and abandons inputs that do not. The criterion is usefulness to the book, not cultural status.

**INFERRED capability:** A project can have an intentionally bounded inspiration diet.


### 8. Literary permission mining

**Anchor:** [00:18:10–00:21:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=1090s)

**OBSERVED:** Old and unfashionable books preserve devices lost through generational imitation: concealed names, under-dashes, omitted speech, different narrator permissions, and structures current work may no longer attempt.

**INFERRED capability:** Reading history is a device-recovery practice and a defense against same-generation stylistic convergence.


### 9. Surprise density

**Anchor:** [00:21:33–00:22:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=1293s)

**OBSERVED:** Greer says Proust contains a surprise on every page. Surprise may come through language, thought, behavior, image, or event rather than conventional plot.

**INFERRED capability:** Page pleasure can be audited without converting literary work into cliff-hanger machinery.


### 10. Structural self-fit

**Anchor:** [00:22:05–00:24:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=1325s)

**OBSERVED:** Something must happen in each portion. Greer also recognizes that sections separated by white space suit how he naturally writes and remove transitions that otherwise create friction.

**INFERRED capability:** Structure should exploit the writer's reliable generative unit while preserving movement.


### 11. Detective reading and reference-bank intuition

**Anchor:** [00:24:05–00:29:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=1445s)

**OBSERVED:** Greer reads like a detective, asking how a writer achieved an effect. He studies punctuation, syntax, lineation, omission, and nested narration until a large reference bank becomes intuition.

**INFERRED capability:** Expert intuition is compressed device memory and can be deliberately enlarged.


### 12. Instant character capture

**Anchor:** [00:29:25–00:32:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=1765s)

**OBSERVED:** Greer resists compulsory backstory. A person can become alive through a few observed features, speech habits, movements, clothing choices, and the way they welcome or resist others.

**INFERRED capability:** Character vividness depends on behavior under social pressure more than on biographical completeness.


### 13. Plot as a vehicle for sentence-level value

**Anchor:** [00:32:35–00:35:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=1955s)

**OBSERVED:** Readers may continue for plot, but plot carries them to paragraphs of beauty, wisdom, and poetry. Movement can also occur in the background while a foreground conversation appears static.

**INFERRED capability:** Narrative propulsion and literary value are complementary layers with different jobs.


### 14. Left-hand practice

**Anchor:** [00:35:27–00:39:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=2127s)

**OBSERVED:** Greer uses the Whitey Ford story to argue that native capability may feel too easy to count as talent. He stops treating effort and visible difficulty as proof of artistic value.

**INFERRED capability:** The system should diagnose displaced competence before prescribing more technique.


### 15. Comedy through the fire

**Anchor:** [00:39:44–00:42:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=2384s)

**OBSERVED:** Comedy begins by deciding a painful event is a funny story. Greer prefers honesty to wit and touches serious material long enough to make it real, then leads the reader through the fire while the comic telling carries them.

**INFERRED capability:** Comedy needs an honesty floor and a proportion decision, not a joke-density target.


### 16. Grammatical timing and recovered setups

**Anchor:** [00:42:38–00:46:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=2558s)

**OBSERVED:** Timing is revised through grammar, compression, omission, spacing, and ellipsis. On a later pass, Greer notices jokes and objects he set up unconsciously and finds where they can pay off.

**INFERRED capability:** Revision can search backward for dormant promises rather than merely polishing forward.


### 17. Fidelity before reception

**Anchor:** [00:53:30–00:55:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=3210s)

**OBSERVED:** Greer's artistic satisfaction came from making the thing in his head as closely as possible before reviews or sales could validate it.

**INFERRED capability:** Internal fidelity is a legitimate pre-publication completion criterion, separate from market success.


### 18. Lacquer-dry technical revision

**Anchor:** [00:55:41–00:58:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=3341s)

**OBSERVED:** Revision is technical: cut a scene, move it, mirror it, reconnect it. After polishing, Greer lets the lacquer dry so familiarity can leave his mind before he judges again.

**INFERRED capability:** Incubation is an explicit step in perception reset, not procrastination.


### 19. Human-evidence observation

**Anchor:** [01:01:48–01:06:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=3708s)

**OBSERVED:** Greer replaces phone attention with a paper pad, records exact human traces, and looks for details that are neither the cliché nor its conspicuous opposite. The target is something utterly itself.

**INFERRED capability:** Reality is felt when an exact detail implies a human decision, compromise, habit, or history.


### 20. Poetry as a prose laboratory

**Anchor:** [01:06:55–01:09:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=4015s)

**OBSERVED:** Greer treats poets as miners of compressed language and narrative invention. He reads aloud, notices pleasure and resistance, and searches poems for answers to prose problems.

**INFERRED capability:** Cross-form study should begin with a live problem, not imitation of poetic surface.


### 21. Editor-as-patient diagnosis

**Anchor:** [01:09:45–01:11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=4185s)

**OBSERVED:** The editor is the patient and the writer is the doctor. Readers accurately locate pain but often misdiagnose the cause and prescribe the wrong cure. Repeated pain locations deserve high weight; suggested fixes do not.

**INFERRED capability:** Feedback needs a symptom ledger separated from solution authority.


### 22. More Mermaid

**Anchor:** [01:11:07–01:12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kkAl04_0og&t=4267s)

**OBSERVED:** When a mermaid at the end does not fit, the answer may be more mermaid: prepare it earlier, commit to it, and rebuild the surrounding work. Greer describes protecting the revelation at the end of *Less* and changing the rest of the novel until it landed.

**INFERRED capability:** Misfit can indicate insufficient commitment, not incorrect inclusion. The diagnosis must compare commit, prepare, transform, move, and cut rather than defaulting to deletion.
