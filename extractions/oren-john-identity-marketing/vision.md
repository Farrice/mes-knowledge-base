A complete, deployable OS for identity-first brand marketing that can: (1) launch an identity brand **from zero** with momentum — viral-capable, true-follower-first, targeting 1,000 true fans and beyond; (2) wrap identity-aligned branding around **any content creator or founder** and scale them toward their mission. ### Workflows (target 13–14)

**Tier 1 — Foundation (from-zero lane)**
1. `identity-brand-os` (front door): founder intake diagnostic → stage + driver diagnosis → routed mission plan
2. `identity-zero-point`: public-identity excavation from raw founder material (origin, place, obsessions, weak points) — before archetype/content decisions
3. `identity-driver-diagnostic`: which of the Five Identity Drivers the brand activates, per segment; primary + secondary driver selection with receipts
4. `true-fan-density-engine`: 1,000-true-fans protocol — tribe design, chapter/congregation model, homie-lookbook proof loop, third-space replacement, depth-over-reach cadence

**Tier 2 — Practitioner**
5. `signature-design-audit`: colorway / form factor / pattern / tag / iconography menu + color-ownership + logo-vs-subtlety sophistication axis
6. `persona-reality-audit`: actual-buyer vs imagined-buyer gap (list evidence first), marketing-to-the-wrong-person diagnosis
7. `third-space-replacement`: locate the congregation your category lost; become its host (events, run-club pattern, pop-up logic)
8. `identity-weak-point-map`: identity-beyond-affiliation — internal weak points your product resolves (Lift Foils pattern)
9. `aspiration-gap-split-test`: see-myself vs see-who-I-want-to-be avatar testing + split-audience routing plan
10. `two-demographic-split`: gender-performative algorithm rule → separate creative strategies per demo
11. `homie-lookbook-brief`: peer-proximity content brief (friends-not-models casting, shot logic)

**Tier 3 — Integration & gates**
12. `identity-injection-pass`: pour identity into the hook/offer/copy triad of any existing asset
13. `identity-control-strategy`: top-down (Stussy) vs bottom-up (Lo Life) tribe-control fork + mirror-test ethics gate
14. `identity-scale-router`: thresholds + handoff map into oren-content-team-architecture / oren-luxury-psychology / build-bos / oren-one-person-ai-marketer
