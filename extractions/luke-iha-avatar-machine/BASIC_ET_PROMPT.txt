Amazing\! Now I'd like to introduce you to something I call the epiphany threshold, goldilocks zone and BS Limit.

First, imagine a sliding scale going from obvious beliefs on one end to outlandish beliefs on the other.

Obvious beliefs in the dating market might be:

- You must be masculine to attract women  
- Taller guys get more women  
- You must have good photos on Tinder to get women

These are so obvious they don't draw any attention.

Outlandish beliefs in the dating market might be:

- Shorter guys get laid more often  
- It’s better to be ugly than attractive to get women  
- The less money you make, the more women you get

These are so unbelievable that they create too much resistance.

So if we say obvious beliefs are a 0 on the believability scale...

And outlandish beliefs are a 10 on the believability scale...

We want to create hooks within a certain zone called the goldilocks zone, which is about a 7-9 on this scale.

The 7 is what we call the "epiphany threshold" – enough to give genuine insights.

The 9 is the "BS limit" – too outlandish.

The zone in between is the “goldilocks zone.”

NOTE: in general, it's better to push more into unbelievable territory than believable territory.

Here’s an example of a good goldilocks zone hook in dating space:

- Acting like a “beta male” can sometimes get you laid more often

See, how it’s different from beliefs too obvious and too outlandish? Like these:

- Beta Males Get Less Women (Below Epiphany Threshold)  
- Beta Males Get More Women (Above Bullshit Limit)

The idea is unexpected because it contrasts typical ideas (being a beta male is good for dating) and provides a small counterexample that makes it believable still (vs. just saying ALWAYS be a beta male).

Here are some more examples of ideas within the Goldilock’s Zone (from different markets):

- Over-moisturizing can actually age your skin faster   
- Intermittent exposure to UV rays may have an anti-aging effect  
- The real reason frequent fliers get more wrinkles   
- Boring her on the first date is the secret to making her chase you

- The best copywriters are the ones who hate writing and struggle with every word  
- The less you know about copywriting, the more authentic and persuasive your copy will be  
- The most lucrative copywriting opportunities are in the most saturated and competitive niches
