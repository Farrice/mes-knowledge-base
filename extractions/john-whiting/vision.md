## The IP Cluster (5 systems, 1 hero)

### ★ HERO — The Propaganda Machine (the thing Farrice wants harvested)
A content + paid-retargeting system that pre-sells and indoctrinates prospects so they **close themselves with zero human sales conversation.** Mechanics extracted:
1. **Move objection-handling OUT of the sales call and INTO content.** Take every objection/belief you'd handle live, make a content piece for each, force it in front of prospects at controlled frequency.
2. **The virtual army of setters & closers.** Each content clip is a "setter/closer" you control word-for-word, deployed at scale, never off-script.
3. **Sell them what they want, give them what they need.** Lead the hook with the prospect's surface desire ("2026 funnel"), switch to the real mechanism. Also a self-selection filter.
4. **Self-selection via polarization.** Tell the people you DON'T want to leave; tell your people "you're my people." Engineers client quality *before* the sale → kills fulfillment drag.
5. **Pattern interrupt on objections.** Don't engage objections in DMs (combative + trains dependence). Lean back, then hit them with the objection-killing content asset.
6. **Force-feed the 7-hour rule via content.** Replace 1 DM hour + 2 calls with 7 hours of consumed content; prospect feels stupid NOT buying.
7. **Retargeting frequency architecture.** BOF (warmest ~600 ppl): logistics + assumptive-close onboarding clips at ~5×/day. MOF (~1,000): ~50 belief-shift pieces at 2×/7days. TOF: book/SLO testimonial ads → feed retargeting.
8. **Borrowed authority testimonial ads.** Recognizable names as tip-of-spear → higher AOV (used with care; association risk noted).
9. **Record sales calls → clip objection-handling → content.** Turn live reps into a permanent content arsenal.
10. **Assumptive close via content.** Onboarding clips run to warmest audience as if they've already joined.

### Supporting systems (the "packaging")
- **The Map / 7-Component Road Trip** — every goal is point A→B, reverse-engineered. 7 components: driver (mental performance), destination (freedom number), vehicle (business model), GPS (tracking), speed (monthly income), fuel efficiency (margin + effort), AAA (advisor/lifeline). Two ways off track: "mentally drunk" (upset) / "mentally coked up" (shiny object).
- **The Tone & Awareness Scales** — emotional tone scale (create/cause at top → anger/antagonism in middle → stop/destruction at bottom) run parallel to an awareness ladder; the **"buying line" = demand for improvement** (angry at the *problem*, not at you). Sales = raising awareness level by level.
- **"What does the data say to do?"** — bottleneck diagnosis as the master question; releasing a bottleneck is always psychology. Cause vs. Effect operating basis.
- **The Record Month Formula** — when you have a record month, document everything (KPIs, marketing, sales, fulfillment, ops, data, accounting, personal) → voice-ramble → LLM-compress to scannable playbook → isolate top-3 drivers → "how do I strengthen it?" → rerun.
- **The Wealthy Coach** — Freedom Number (4% rule → portfolio size), profit math, income allocation (50/treats/education), "if you can't buy it twice, don't buy it once," investment priority ladder, "things die as fast as they grow."
- **One Best Client** — build the entire business around your single perfect client (paid most + executes + you'd hang out free). Offer = one audience, one problem, one organized repeatable process.
