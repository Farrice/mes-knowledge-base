## The Gap This Expansion Fills

The proposed layer answers six questions in sequence:

1. Is there a real idea here?
2. Has it been developed enough to become a repeatable bit?
3. Does weak response point to craft, current velocity, or both?
4. Can the frame change while the idea remains intact?
5. Does the work still sound as if it came from a particular person and place?
6. Is current riding funding the soul work, or replacing it?

## Proposed Workflow Set

Names remain provisional until the Architecture checkpoint. **Preserve** means the workflow and its current evidence state stay unchanged unless a later line-level source match justifies a bounded addition. **Enrich** permits only that bounded addition.

1. **diagnose-and-rebalance, preserve:** retain the existing POP diagnostic. Make no claim that this transcript verifies POP.
2. **compress-to-memorable, enrich carefully:** test a line-level addition for the jargon trial. Keep the older compression ladder under its original provenance.
3. **draft-pop-first, preserve:** retain the existing POP draft method. Stories/examples/takes may become an upstream payload check rather than new POP evidence.
4. **idea-courage-craft-triage:** separate an empty idea, a timid idea, and an execution problem. This corrects the current agent's blanket assumption that the idea is always fine.
5. **observation-mind-mine:** join the public-writing observation loop with the paper-and-no-phone restaurant exercise.
6. **60-20-10-bit-refinery:** fully express an idea, compress it twice, and identify the part worth repeating. Nathan Barry's optional re-expansion remains attributed to him.
7. **public-reps-learning-loop:** use bounded practice and audience response as diagnostic evidence. Never publish autonomously or assume the audience is always right.
8. **current-fit-diagnostic:** separate idea quality, execution quality, current velocity, and brand fit. Delegate live-signal sourcing.
9. **timely-shell-timeless-core:** change the why-now frame while preserving the underlying argument.
10. **scheduled-current-archive:** map existing ideas to known releases, anniversaries, and events through the dated-signal systems already in the roster.
11. **past-present-braid:** alternate historical material with present-day contrast.
12. **placeful-voice-audit:** detect placeless template prose. Send deeper craft repair to Ocean Vuong or the high-taste system.
13. **current-or-soul-portfolio:** use currents tactically without letting them replace the work the creator wants to be known for.
