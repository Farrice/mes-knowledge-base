# Jun Yuh Two-Masterclass Source Coverage Audit

## Verdict

The prior work did not harvest “nothing.” It built a unusually strong truth-safe material engine. But it compressed both masterclasses around story eligibility and method recovery, leaving much of Jun's content-marketing and selling system either documented only, implied by a generic playbook, or absent as a deployable workflow.

Status meanings:

- `DEPLOYABLE`: source mechanic has a usable workflow/prompt and a verification surface.
- `PARTIAL`: mechanic is recorded or lightly routed, but the task-specific execution surface is incomplete.
- `CAPTURED ONLY`: mechanic appears in a ledger or genius file but future runs still have to interpret it manually.
- `MISSING`: no durable deployment path existed before this build.

## System-Level Gaps

1. The old `/junyuh-marketing` playbook was generic. It did not cite these masterclasses, had no matching v2 execution prompt, and treated “content-to-revenue” as a planning table rather than a proof-bearing sequence.
2. Natural-language searches for a full Jun storytelling/content-marketing system surfaced scattered Jun tools, not the connected `/jun-story-engine` or a clearly expanded `/junyuh-marketing` front door.
3. The six formats were selectable, but there was no task matrix showing how expertise, products, services, coaching, founder stories, and offers should use them differently.
4. ATTRACT, NURTURE, POSITION, and CONVERT were mission labels, not separate deliverables with different evidence jobs and CTAs.
5. The system could recover a Method Card but did not reliably turn one expert method into a sequence of story-led educational, authority, proof, and conversion content.
6. There was no source-grounded story-led masterclass architecture despite the 2h20 source demonstrating one.
7. There was no Story Bank/Payoff-to-Problem flywheel or measurement spine connecting audience response to the next story choice.
8. “ROI” had only a warning boundary. It lacked an operational measurement chain from attention to qualified buyer behavior.

### Rejected Inferences

- `Promise` is not a fourth element of Jun's 3P. It is a downstream repository synthesis used only after Problem, Pursuit, Payoff, proof ceiling, and audience fit are locked.
- Story does not cause ROI by itself. The local system may form a commercial hypothesis; market proof begins with deployed buyer behavior and ROI requires attributable collected revenue and cost.
- Jun's own challenge price, guarantee, bonuses, scarcity, or results do not transfer to a user's offer.
- A repeated behavior is not automatically a sellable method. It still needs steps, decision rules, a bounded output, and a demand event.
- A strong client fact or content contrast is not automatically a full story. Missing Pursuit remains `NEEDS SOURCE`.

## Build Decision

Preserve `/jun-story-engine` as the focused truth-safe material engine. Upgrade `/junyuh-marketing` into the connected deployment orchestrator and add small task-specific components. This is a paved path around the current foundation, not a replacement mega-skill.
