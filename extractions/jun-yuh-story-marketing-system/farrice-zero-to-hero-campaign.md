### Caption

I found the old note in my own files.

Zero to Hero.

I help single ordinary guys in their 30s become extraordinary. Best shape of your life.

That was my copy.

Now I spend a lot of my time checking whether a health or performance claim can carry the weight we put on it.

The old note does not give me a neat conversion story. I still need the middle of that story. But it does show that I know how easy it is to make the sentence bigger than the evidence.

So I work the other way around now. Before we make the promise louder, we put the proof on the table.

## Nurture

I used to think the strong part of an offer was the outcome.

Become extraordinary. Best shape of your life.

That is what I wrote in 2018.

The problem is not that the words sound good. Good words can make a thin claim feel finished.

Now I start with the record. I look at what happened, what we can show, what is still a guess, and what sentence the evidence can carry without help.

That often gives you quieter copy.

It also gives you copy that does not fall apart when a buyer, a regulator, or an AI answer asks where it came from.

I do not have a clean hero story for how I changed. I have the old note, the rule I use now, and the work in between that I am still tracing.

For this piece, that is enough.

## Position

### The proof gets the last word

When I review health or performance content, I mark each important sentence four ways.

First, what does the source say?

Then, what did the copy add?

Next, what proof would that addition need?

Last, what is the strongest version we can stand behind now?

Some claims survive. Others get smaller.

If the evidence is missing, the honest answer is “needs source.”

That is the point where the content stops borrowing confidence from the writer.

## Convert

If you run content for a health or performance brand, send me one public claim and the source you use to support it.

I will show you what the sentence is actually claiming, where the proof holds or breaks, and the strongest version I would publish.

This is a draft offer test. It is not a promise of reach, compliance, or sales.
