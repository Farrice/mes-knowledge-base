# Jun Yuh Story Marketing System — Skill System Contract

## Decision

The existing Jun Story Engine is a strong truth-and-privacy foundation, but it is not the full deployment system taught and demonstrated across the two masterclasses. This build preserves the engine and adds a connected content-marketing layer instead of replacing it or creating a duplicate expert.

## Dependency Rules

1. The operator supplies lived facts, expertise evidence, results, motives, emotions, and privacy permissions.
2. Jun's source formula is `Problem → Pursuit → Payoff`. `Promise` is a downstream marketing translation, not a fourth Jun P.
3. Problem creates selective relevance; Pursuit carries the method; Payoff closes at the highest supported proof ceiling.
4. A CTA may capture a next action, but it does not convert an anecdote into demand or ROI proof.
5. One asset has one primary mission: `ATTRACT`, `NURTURE`, `POSITION`, or `CONVERT`.
6. Each mission may reuse the same 3P only when its angle, evidence job, and next action are materially different.
7. Social/personal-brand bodies may stay with Jun. Non-social bodies route through Shaan to one destination owner.
8. `NO STORY`, `NEEDS SOURCE`, `NO OFFER`, and `NO EVENT` remain valid terminal states.
9. The story bank stores source paths, meanings, missions, proof state, and results; it never upgrades performance claims from memory.
10. ROI is recorded only from observed buyer behavior such as qualified replies, calls, deposits, payments, or collected revenue.

## Build Boundary

- Expand the existing Jun skill system; do not add another expert.
- Keep both current command surfaces and make their division explicit.
- Add no dependency, plugin, paid tool, external action, publication, or global mirror.
- Do not rewrite unrelated content, sales, copy, or story systems.
- Preserve all current Story Engine negative controls and source boundaries.

## Measurable Stop

The build stops when the source-coverage map has no unexplained `MISSING` deployment rows, the focused and full-system verifiers pass, natural-language routing surfaces the correct Jun front door, the before/after proof demonstrates task-specific behavior, and no unsupported ROI or Jun-attribution claim remains.
