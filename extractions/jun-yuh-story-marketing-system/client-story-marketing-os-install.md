# Client Story Marketing OS — Productized Installation

**Purpose:** Install the Jun story-and-format system for a founder, expert, or business without making Farrice hand-prompt every asset.  
**Best fit:** Evidence-sensitive founder-led businesses, especially health, wellness, fitness, and performance.  
**Boundary:** This is a delivery system, not a promise of virality, demand, compliance, or revenue.

## What the Client Receives

1. **Message Lock** — what the business means, who should care, and the founder's sourced right to say it.
2. **Story and Expertise Bank** — lived moments, methods, proof objects, claims, privacy limits, and missing facts.
3. **Concept Engine** — five meaning-distinct concepts per planning cycle, ranked by evidence, audience meaning, format fit, and business use.
4. **Format Intelligence Board** — a bounded 7x7 reference library, Format Cards, and clear non-copy boundaries.
5. **Four-Mission Campaign Map** — separate ATTRACT, NURTURE, POSITION, and CONVERT jobs.
6. **Production Cards** — script/slide plan, visuals, pacing, CTA, owner, production load, and missing assets.
7. **Claim and Privacy Gate** — proof ceiling, prohibited implications, third-party boundaries, and `NEEDS SOURCE` stops.
8. **Learning Ledger** — attention, recognition, intent, sales, collected revenue, and format/concept learnings kept separate.

## Intake That Finds the Material

Ask in this order:

1. What do customers misunderstand before they are ready for you?
2. What decision do you make differently from others in the category?
3. What happened that taught you that decision?
4. What do you do, inspect, remove, or refuse during delivery?
5. What artifact can we show: note, prototype, label, email, screenshot, test, checklist, before/after process, or product constraint?
6. What result is directly observed, and what remains assumed?
7. Which people, events, diagnoses, results, and business facts stay private?
8. What action is a reader currently allowed to take?
9. What can the team produce each week without borrowing footage, time, or taste it does not have?
10. Which public creators or pieces feel structurally useful, even outside the niche?

## Operating Cadence

### Monthly

- refresh Message Lock only when the business or audience changed;
- add source material and proof objects;
- inspect three to seven new public references or maintain the full 7x7 board;
- choose one campaign theme and four mission jobs;
- set the production mix from actual capacity.

### Weekly

- select one sourced concept;
- compare three format candidates;
- make one Format Card;
- produce the smallest complete asset;
- run claim, privacy, voice, and anti-slop review;
- publish only after client approval;
- record the event after the measurement window.

## Client-Facing Status Language

- `READY`: sources, format, destination, and boundaries support production.
- `PROVISIONAL`: concept is strong but one asset, fact, or permission is missing.
- `NEEDS SOURCE`: a load-bearing fact, pursuit, result, or claim is absent.
- `NO STORY`: direct education, status, evidence, or explanation is better.
- `NO OFFER`: content can ship without a commercial CTA.
- `NO EVENT`: nothing has been deployed or observed yet.

## Sparse Health-Business Cold Test

### Fixture boundary

`PulseForm` is a synthetic test business created only to test portability. These are the complete founder notes:

- The founder coached strength clients for 12 years.
- He personally stopped using high-stimulant pre-workout before late sessions because he felt wired at bedtime.
- He developed a caffeine-free drink mix for his own late sessions.
- The current product label and ingredient list exist.
- No efficacy study, customer outcome, sales result, or demand evidence is supplied.
- The founder wants educational social content and can film in a gym for two hours each week.

### Questions returned before production

1. Which ingredient and label facts are verified in the current product record?
2. What exact steps did the founder take from personal workaround to product?
3. Was the product made only for him at first, or were other people involved?
4. What can be shown: first formula, shaker, label draft, training log, or current product?
5. What may be said about caffeine, sleep, energy, and performance under the brand's evidence and legal review?
6. What happened after anyone else tried or bought it?
7. Is the founder willing to say “this was my experience,” without turning it into a general outcome claim?
8. What is the authorized next action: follow, waitlist, product page, sample request, or none?

### Cold Concept Brief

- **Concept:** Why the founder removed caffeine from his own late-session drink.
- **Audience Problem:** People who train late may dislike the founder's personal tradeoff between a stimulant ritual and how he felt at bedtime. This is a recognition hypothesis, not a medical fact.
- **Proof object:** current caffeine-free label plus any recovered early formula artifact.
- **Problem:** founder felt wired at bedtime after his own late-session high-stimulant use.
- **Pursuit:** he developed a caffeine-free mix for himself; exact formulation steps are `NEEDS SOURCE`.
- **Payoff:** a product and label exist. Sleep, energy, performance, customer, and market effects are unproven.
- **Mission:** POSITION first; ATTRACT may use the personal tradeoff only as personal experience.
- **Proof ceiling:** DELIVERABLE / EXPERIENCE.
- **Claim boundary:** no “improves sleep,” “supports recovery,” “boosts performance,” or similar effect claim without evidence and review.
- **Commercial state:** `NO OFFER` until an authorized next action is supplied.

### Format decision

| Candidate | Decision |
|---|---|
| Label-and-shaker proof carousel | **SELECT** — makes the product constraint visible without implying a result. |
| Gym talking-head with the personal tradeoff | PROVISIONAL — works if the founder approves first-person wording. |
| Before/after sleep transformation reel | **REJECT** — no measured sleep outcome or supported transformation. |

### Small campaign at the current proof ceiling

- **ATTRACT:** “I stopped putting caffeine in my late-session drink.” The caption must say this is the founder's experience, not a general sleep claim.
- **NURTURE:** Show the decision questions behind a caffeine-free product: what to remove, what the label can say, and what still needs evidence. Exact formulation details remain `NEEDS SOURCE`.
- **POSITION:** A label walkthrough that distinguishes product facts from hoped-for effects.
- **CONVERT:** `NO OFFER`. Replace with a non-commercial request for the audience's late-training questions only if comment collection is authorized.

### Measurement plan

- ATTRACT: hold rate/profile visits, not “virality.”
- NURTURE: saves and specific late-training questions.
- POSITION: requests to inspect ingredients, formulation logic, or label evidence.
- CONVERT: `NO EVENT` until a real authorized offer exists.
- Never treat views, likes, or comments as efficacy or demand proof.

### Cold-test verdict

**PASS.** The system found a concrete product-story angle, asked bounded questions, rejected the most dramatic format, produced a campaign subset, and stopped commercial/efficacy claims without hidden prompting. It did not require Farrice's personal archive or Jun-specific vocabulary to function.
