## Machine 3 — Soft-Life Consumption

But the buyer is the educated consumer: 40th-percentile-and-up shoppers have watched greens-powder teardowns and know the "proprietary blend" objection. Current creative assumes less education than the buyer has.

## Machine 4 — Iterative AI
Benefit-statement statics at volume read as the category's "good answer" — competent, safe, idea-free. No visible human idea on top of the recent feed output; the mascot world is the unexploited human-idea surface.
