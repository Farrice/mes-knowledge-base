## Prop-Grade Briefs

### 1. Laminated Night Shift Operator Card

- **Derivation:** Concern-issued credential for the playable Night Shift Operator role; it makes belonging portable.
- **Issue/use:** Received after signup with route number, standing order, and “awake on purpose” classification.
- **Form:** Cream laminate, rounded corners, midnight-blue type, chrome-percolator seal, punched shift code.
- **Functional job:** Stores membership/route details and can unlock future member access.
- **Predicted behavior:** Photograph, post, quote the assigned title, keep behind an existing ID.
- **Artifact-vs-merch:** ARTIFACT—the institution issues a role-bearing credential; no logo dependency.

### 2. Dispatch Manifest Packing Slip

- **Derivation:** The Concern dispatches supplies to operators; fulfillment paperwork is an institutional document.
- **Issue/use:** Ships with every order as a route manifest with dispatch time, weather line, standing order, and refill-right notice.
- **Form:** Warm white continuous-feed paper, monospaced blue/black type, red time stamp, perforated acknowledgment stub.
- **Functional job:** Confirms order contents, exceptions, and support route.
- **Predicted behavior:** Read rather than discard; quote the dispatch note; retain the stub.
- **Artifact-vs-merch:** ARTIFACT—still legible as Concern paperwork with the mark removed.

### 3. Certified Counter Placard

- **Derivation:** Route Certification is the world’s public institution; counters need issued proof.
- **Issue/use:** Granted after a counter meets the three rules; displayed behind the register.
- **Form:** Small enamel-steel placard, counter number, certification year, “Refills are a right, not a favor.”
- **Functional job:** Identifies participating locations and standards.
- **Predicted behavior:** Display, photograph, route-collect, ask how certification works.
- **Artifact-vs-merch:** ARTIFACT—it records institutional status and prompts participation.

### 4. Night Formula Thermos Tin

- **Derivation:** Coffee for people awake on purpose should look issued for a thermos, dashboard, and double shift.
- **Issue/use:** Primary shelf package and reusable canister.
- **Form:** Tall dark-navy tin with cream route stencil, riveted-looking seam, shift table, brewing instructions written as dispatch protocol.
- **Functional job:** Protects coffee, measures portions, survives transport.
- **Predicted behavior:** Double-take on shelf, reuse as shift-supply container, visible placement at work.
- **Artifact-vs-merch:** ARTIFACT/PRODUCT—the package is itself a functional world prop.

### 5. Route Service Work Shirt

- **Derivation:** Clothing is the operator’s costume; the Concern’s field staff need a believable uniform.
- **Issue/use:** Optional earned item after three route check-ins, not generic launch merch.
- **Form:** Dark-navy short-sleeve work shirt, stitched first name, route-number tab, small chrome-percolator service patch.
- **Functional job:** Durable workwear with pen/thermometer pocket.
- **Predicted behavior:** Wear on shift, personalize, explain earned status.
- **Artifact-vs-merch:** ARTIFACT only when earned and numbered; a retail logo shirt is DEFAULT MERCH.

### 6. Chrome Refill Bell

- **Derivation:** Internal culture needs a physical ceremony commensurate with the refill promise.
- **Issue/use:** Kept at Dispatch Office #2; rung once when a counter is certified, twice when an operator completes a double.
- **Form:** Heavy chrome counter bell on a dark-oak base with an engraved route ledger strip.
- **Functional job:** Marks operational milestones audibly and consistently.
- **Predicted behavior:** Team performs the ritual, records the moment, remembers the standard.
- **Artifact-vs-merch:** INTERNAL TALISMAN—meaning comes from repeated ceremony, not branding.

### 7. Annual Night Route Map

- **Derivation:** A campaign era should leave a dated relic and turn the institution’s geography into participation.
- **Issue/use:** Foldout map for one annual “Keep the Lights On” route season.
- **Form:** Gas-station fold map, midnight/cream/red print, certified counters, blank check-in stamps, era code on spine.
- **Functional job:** Finds locations and records visits.
- **Predicted behavior:** Mark, compare, complete routes, retain after the era closes.
- **Artifact-vs-merch:** CAMPAIGN RELIC—dated usefulness and memory carrier.

### 8. 02:00 Dispatch Notice

- **Derivation:** A digital service touchpoint should be issued by the Concern, not styled like a normal newsletter.
- **Issue/use:** Scheduled member message with one route note, one standing order, and one counter report.
- **Form:** Plain-text dispatch header, route/time code, restrained cream/navy HTML fallback, no generic promo blocks.
- **Functional job:** Communicates useful availability, route, or member information.
- **Predicted behavior:** Open at shift time, screenshot one line, reply in register.
- **Artifact-vs-merch:** DIGITAL ARTIFACT—the institutional job and voice survive without decorative graphics.

### 9. Refill Right Coin

- **Derivation:** The refill promise needs a portable ritual token between operator and counter.
- **Issue/use:** One numbered coin issued after first certified-counter visit; may be presented for a ritual refill where operationally allowed.
- **Form:** Nickel-colored coin, percolator seal, route number, edge text “RIGHT, NOT FAVOR.”
- **Functional job:** Records/mediates a participation ritual; exact commercial entitlement remains unapproved.
- **Predicted behavior:** Carry, show, exchange, photograph; ask where it works.
- **Artifact-vs-merch:** PROVISIONAL ARTIFACT—fails if it has no real use agreement with counters.

## Artifact-vs-Merch Verdicts

- Keep cards, manifests, placards, map, and dispatch notice: role, institution, use, and behavior are complete.
- Prototype the tin and bell: strong story/function, moderate production burden.
- Hold the shirt until earned-status logic exists; otherwise it becomes merch.
- Hold the coin until a real operational right can be honored; a fake entitlement would break trust.

## Commit Harder

Prototype the Dispatch Manifest on real continuous-feed stock with a detachable acknowledgment stub rather than printing a retro-styled insert on normal card stock. The escalation is material and functional, not a heavier layer of vintage decoration.

## Prototype Plan

1. **First reversible prototype:** Laminated Operator Card plus Dispatch Manifest for an internal five-person test.
2. **Evidence gate:** all five can explain their role; at least three keep/share the card without prompting; the manifest remains operationally clear; no one confuses fictional lore with a real historical claim.
3. **Next only if passed:** Thermos Tin mockup and one Certified Counter Placard with a real, documented standard.
4. **Explicit hold:** no production purchase, partner promise, public issue, or use-right claim without approval.
