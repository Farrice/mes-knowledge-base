## The Core Thesis (his words, distilled)

> "You are the thought leader. AI is your thought partner."

Everything he teaches hangs off three skills:
1. **Target the 20%** — don't look for AI use cases; your biggest problem IS the use case. "If you aim it at something that doesn't matter, it doesn't matter."
2. **Communicate via CRIT** — Context (verbose, "assume you haven't given enough, ask what else"), Role (vivid, specific expertise), **Interview** (make AI ask YOU one question at a time, 3-5 — "the most important part"), Task.
3. **Remain the thought leader** — every output is "the bad answer" / a first draft. Respond with *what I like / what I don't like / top changes*. Iterate over a conversation, not a prompt.

## Genius patterns to extract (in-source, verified present)

1. **The Interview Inversion** — turn the tables; AI interviews you (signature move, appears 8+ times)
2. **CRIT** — Context / Role / Interview / Task
3. **"This is the bad answer"** — pre-judgment ritual before reading any output; first-draft framing
4. **The Feedback Triad** — like / don't like / top changes (his refinement loop)
5. **20% Targeting** — paper-and-pen list → star test ("does this drive 80%?") → the ONE
6. **Persona Flip / AI vs. Itself** — challenger → ideal customer → aggressive board member; "best I can do ≠ best that can be done"
7. **Stakeholder Simulation** — personality profiles from interviews → simulate the room → pre-test the deck → calibrate against real transcripts (hostile-board case)
8. **The AI Board** — skill-gap interview → custom-augmented advisors with permission rules (Jobs: no husband/father advice) → future self as most valuable member
9. **Anti-sycophancy Custom Instructions** — "red team everything, don't fluff me" baked into global instructions
10. **Environmental Triggers** — two sticky notes ("How can AI help me do this?" / "CRIT")
11. **Job = Skills + Processes → Markdown → Agent** — document via CRIT interview, save as markdown, agent replicates you
12. **Cognitive Sovereignty** — thinking-time chair, pen-and-paper first, "can I still fly without it?" self-test; CRIT as brain-engagement (not replacement) mechanism
13. **Dominoes Sequencing** — three skills daily × 30 days before any agent talk; "you haven't seen color yet"
14. **Conductor Creativity** — you're no longer the player of one instrument; creativity = vision-casting + role-casting + task design
15. **Own 100% of Your Job** (Keller lineage) — leadership layer; thinking leverage; "I'm excited for you to figure that out"
