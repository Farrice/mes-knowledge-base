# Fixture 01 — The Seeds

## Request

Rewrite this short scene so grief is carried by story rather than explained. Keep the uncertain seed jar, preserve the fact that the mother has died, and allow credible hope without implying the loss was good or necessary.

## Deliberately Weak Draft

After my mother passed away, I experienced the profound and multifaceted nature of grief. Her kitchen represented the tapestry of our shared memories, and every object reminded me that life is both fragile and beautiful. I found an unlabeled jar of seeds. It made me realize that even after tremendous loss, new beginnings are always possible. I decided to plant them, because this symbolized my healing journey and showed that hope can grow from pain.

## Constraints

- Fictional material.
- 250–450 words.
- No claim that grief has a necessary lesson.
- Do not imitate Matt Haig’s prose voice.
