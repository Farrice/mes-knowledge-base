# Existing-Route and Ownership Map

## Build Decision

Extend `oren-one-person-ai-marketer`. Its weekly OS already reserves a four-hour Funnel block but does not define the end-to-end journey, route choice, capture-to-call handoff, offer ladder, or learning loop. The source fills that specific gap.

A new Oren skill, funnel mega-skill, agent, global mirror, or plugin would duplicate existing ownership.

## Composition Ledger

| Function | Owner | Contribution | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| End-to-end funnel journey | `oren-one-person-ai-marketer` | Function owner; composes route, handoffs, and loop | Does not absorb specialist execution. |
| Observed funnel evidence | `/funnel-hack` | Captures ad/page/upsell/email evidence | Does not design the new funnel. |
| Audience and channel circuit | `/oren-content-flywheel` | Supplies entry traffic and conversion-node diagnosis | Does not own capture-to-call detail. |
| Lead-magnet design | `stockton-walbeck-lead-magnets` | Scores and designs the magnet | Invoked only when the magnet itself is the constraint. |
| Paid-media economics and attribution | `benoit-vatere` | Owns CAC, incrementality, stage measurement, and media diagnosis | Oren does not diagnose ROAS. |
| Full VSL or conversion copy | Existing copy/VSL specialist | Writes the asset after route choice | Oren only defines the job and handoff. |
| Referral and retention | Existing Oren referral engine | Completes the post-delivery loop | New layer hands off after fulfillment. |

## Skipped Routes

- Proof-to-Market: explicitly excluded by Farrice.
- Generic funnel builders: useful comparisons but not the source owner.
- `/system-audit`: no broken control plane was reported; this is an approved source-to-system build.
