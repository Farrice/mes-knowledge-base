# Mastery Extraction — Funnel Flywheel Layer

## Build Verdict

This MID-band source earns five workflows and six execution prompts inside the existing Oren activation skill. It does not earn a new expert, new skill, or universal funnel doctrine.

## Twelve Operating Patterns

1. **Journey Before Page.** Design the transitions from attention to sale; a landing page is one handoff, not the funnel.
2. **Offer Before Traffic.** Name the buyer, purchased job, and bounded offer before selecting pages or tools.
3. **Capture Creates an Owned Asset.** Attention that cannot be followed up is rented; permissioned contact plus source and status creates a lead.
4. **Commitment Determines Route.** Free capture, low-price tripwire, group presentation, long-form video, DM conversation, and direct call solve different commitment and explanation problems.
5. **The Thank-You Page Is a Transition.** It confirms the first action and offers the next proportionate commitment.
6. **Qualification Protects Both Sides.** Location, fit, capacity, budget, authority, timing, and evidence requirements decide who advances.
7. **Show-Up Is Designed.** A booked call is not the conversion; preparation, expectations, reminders, and ownership reduce the gap between booking and attendance.
8. **Conversation Converts Social Attention.** Social engagement becomes commercial only when the operator initiates or invites a relevant, permission-aware conversation.
9. **Swipe Files Preserve Disappearing Evidence.** Capture the full spine and decompose it before borrowing a pattern.
10. **Test Meaning, Then Treatment.** Test human narrative, evidence-led, and direct-response messages before cosmetic page changes.
11. **Nurture Starts Immediately.** Confirm the action and expectations now; send the best relevant education next; continue only with a stated learning or conversion job.
12. **The List Compounds Optionality.** A permissioned audience can support future offers, but list size is not revenue and cannot substitute for payment evidence.

## Six Hidden Mechanics

1. The highest-value output is the architecture and handoff logic; tool operation is increasingly commoditized.
2. Each funnel transition should earn exactly one next action. Multiple equal CTAs obscure the route.
3. A lead magnet is not automatically the correct entry; it must bridge to the purchased job or route to Stockton for redesign.
4. The popular call-price threshold is an Oren heuristic. Complexity, risk, buyer process, and trust can matter more than price.
5. Tripwire revenue is not proof of break-even acquisition. Cost and downstream-conversion data must be present.
6. Retention and referral close the flywheel but come from the existing Antigravity operating system, not this video alone.

## Signature Moves

- Convert the offer into a one-line buyer/job/offer lock before route selection.
- Build a Route Card that states why each alternative was rejected.
- Turn every page or DM step into a handoff with one action, one owner, and one measurement.
- Separate qualification from persuasion so weak-fit leads do not consume sales capacity.
- Hand missing economics to the measurement owner instead of producing confident math.
- Turn each experiment into a retained learning that changes the next test.

## Hall-of-Fame Source Examples

1. **Interior-design checklist → consultation → call preparation.** Clear free-to-consult progression for a local, higher-consideration service.
2. **High-value service lead magnet → long nurture.** Educational capture can serve a long sales cycle when only a few qualified buyers are needed.
3. **Funnel archaeology → three message treatments.** Preserve observed structures, then test human, evidence-led, and direct-response meaning rather than cloning surface design.

## Anti-Patterns

- Calling a landing page a complete funnel.
- Selecting Framer, Kit, HubSpot, or any tool before defining the handoff.
- Treating a download, booked call, or list size as revenue.
- Adding a tripwire because it is fashionable without economics or bridge logic.
- Sending automated DMs or emails without permission.
- Diagnosing ROAS from funnel structure when the operator lacks ad, offer, page, and targeting evidence.
- Writing a full VSL inside a route-selection workflow.
- Copying a screenshot's visual surface without preserving the buyer, claim, context, and next action.

## Quality Rubric

A complete output must: lock buyer/job/offer; name one route and rejected alternatives; map every handoff; distinguish attention, lead, qualified lead, sale, retention, and referral; label missing economics; keep tool choices optional; preserve permission boundaries; show the source-derived mechanics; and end with one measurable next test.
