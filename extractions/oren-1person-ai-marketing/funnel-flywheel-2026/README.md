# Oren Funnel Flywheel Source Package

This package preserves the evidence used to extend `oren-one-person-ai-marketer` with a funnel operating layer. It is an extension of the existing Oren skill, not a new expert or standalone funnel system.

## Evidence

- `transcript.txt` — normalized auto-captions used as the primary source.
- `raw/transcript-segments.json3` — raw YouTube caption events with millisecond timing.
- `raw/storyboard.mhtml` — recovered YouTube storyboard payload. The decoded frames were corrupted and are not evidence for visual claims.
- `timestamped-mechanics.md` — the source mechanics and their time ranges.
- `video-context-ledger.md` — what was observed, unavailable, and inferred.
- `uncertainty-report.md` — source limitations and claim labels.
- `mastery-extraction.md` — operating patterns, hidden mechanics, failure modes, and quality criteria.
- `duplicate-route-map.md` — existing owners and the boundaries this extension preserves.
- `skill-system-contract.md` — the build contract and validation surface.
- `validation-fixtures.json` + `validate_funnel_flywheel.py` — deterministic regression checks.
- `blind-source-fidelity-review.md` — source/enhancement separation checkpoint.
- `validation-report.md` — validation receipt and open gate.

## Behavior Proofs

- `behavior-proof-paid-social-creative-test-sprint.md` applies the system to Farrice's active offer without changing its scope or proof state.
- `behavior-proof-neutral-interior-design.md` proves portability outside health, AI, and creative strategy.

## Evidence Labels

- **SOURCE-DERIVED** — directly supported by the transcript.
- **SYSTEM ENHANCEMENT** — Antigravity judgment added to make the mechanic reliable and reusable.
- **CONDITIONAL SUPPORT** — handed to another established owner when its inputs exist.
- **UNVERIFIED HEURISTIC** — Oren's practitioner judgment or self-reported result, not an established universal fact.
- **UNAVAILABLE** — capture was attempted but cannot support an inference.
