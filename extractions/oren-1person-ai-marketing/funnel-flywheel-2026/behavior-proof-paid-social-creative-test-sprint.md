# Behavior Proof — Paid Social Creative Test Sprint

## Input Tested

- **Buyer:** established sports-nutrition or recovery brand already running Meta, TikTok, or YouTube ads, with creators/editors or an agency able to produce and deploy work.
- **Purchased job:** turn customer, competitor, product, and existing-ad evidence into the next concepts, hooks, scripts, and test logic worth producing.
- **Primary offer:** one product and customer segment; two distinct concepts; twelve hooks; four creator-ready UGC scripts; one production brief; one test matrix; one 30-minute handoff; one consolidated revision; five business days after complete inputs.
- **Boundaries:** strategy and scripts only. No filming, editing, production, creator sourcing/management, posting, media buying, landing pages, legal/compliance approval, unlimited revisions, daily Slack, or performance guarantee.
- **Price state:** $1,500 design-partner hypothesis; 50% to reserve and 50% before final files.
- **Commercial state:** sent 0 / held 0 / sold 0 / collected $0.

## Weakness Diagnosed

The offer is bounded, but a cold buyer cannot yet see a five-second proof that Farrice can turn their evidence into ads worth testing. The acquisition system must make the proof object visible, qualify operational fit, and ask for a deposit without expanding the service.

## Source Mechanics Used

- Journey before page.
- Proof-bearing free entry followed by one proportionate next commitment.
- Qualification before bottom-of-funnel attention.
- Call preparation and immediate/next-day nurture.
- Owned follow-up and retained funnel learning.

## Capture and Conversation Sequence — Draft/Build Specification Only

1. **Proof asset:** public-data sample sprint for one real product. One CTA: request a fit review.
2. **Fit gate:** live paid social; creators/editors/agency available; live product and customer reviews; approved claims/substantiation; existing ads and basic performance context; one accountable decision-maker.
3. **Confirmation:** confirm request, state that the offer is strategy/scripts only, list the five inputs, explain who attends, and state the next step.
4. **Next-day message:** send the most relevant sample breakdown or test-logic explanation; one CTA to keep or cancel the fit conversation.
5. **Sales conversation:** diagnose the product/segment/creative-testing constraint, verify scope, and ask for the 50% reservation deposit only if fit holds.
6. **Pipeline states:** researched → contacted → replied → qualified → booked → held → offered → deposit collected → inputs complete → delivered → final collected → deployed → reviewed.

No message is sent and no pipeline record is created by this artifact.

## Automation Handoff

This is a specification, not an authorization to configure tools. A future implementation may map the named pipeline states, owners, required fields, reminder timing, and stop conditions into the existing CRM/email stack. It must preserve manual approval before every outbound message, record bookings separately from held calls and deposits, and stop automation when fit fails, claims are unapproved, inputs are incomplete, or a buyer opts out.

## Measurement Plan

- **Attention:** approved contacts and sample inspections.
- **Lead:** substantive buyer replies or fit requests.
- **Qualification:** buyers passing every input/operational criterion.
- **Show-up:** held qualified conversations, separate from bookings.
- **Commercial proof:** deposits collected, then final payments collected.
- **Delivery proof:** complete sprint within five business days and target 8–12 operator hours, still an estimate until measured.
- **Use proof:** client deploys at least one concept and provides clean context.
