# The Context Engineering Operating System — Design Spine

## The one inversion it is built on
**STOP engineering outcomes. Engineer the context where the desired outcome becomes inevitable.** Behavior is a downstream byproduct of permission; permission of context/category; context of perception. So you never push the outcome — you build the conditions upstream of it, and the outcome arrives as an automatic, self-chosen byproduct. Hughes: *"if you're good, what you engineer are conditions. And if I can engineer the right conditions, I can get you to do anything."* The planning verb is **"What is upstream of the thing I want?"** and its sharp form **"What is the context where the behavior I want is automatic?"**

## The input → output contract
**INPUT:** one desired end-state (a behavior, decision, belief, or felt-state you want a target audience — or yourself — to reach), plus who the target is and the channel.
**OUTPUT:** a **Context-Design Spec** — the conditions to build, named, sequenced, force-mapped, and ethics-gated, ready to hand to a production expert (or to execute on yourself).

## The engine — 5 stages (each is a persona/workflow file run in sequence)

### Stage 0 — UPSTREAM (the Context Architect)
Take the desired end-state and run the upstream question repeatedly: what must already be true — in perception, identity, permission, belief — for this behavior to be automatic? Produce the **condition chain** (end-state ← conditions ← conditions). Refuse to write a script or CTA here. If you can't name the context where the behavior is automatic, you're not done.

### Stage 1 — FORCE-MAP (the Force-Mapper)
Before designing anything, name the forces **already acting** on this audience so you're not fighting an invisible current. Map:
- **FEAR loop / fractionation** — what novelty, emotional up-down, and environmental destabilization is the audience already inside (their feed, their news, their market anxiety)?
- **Engineered division / prepackaged enemies** — what destabilization has already dropped their critical thinking and primed them to grab a clean answer?
- **Algorithm bidirectionality** — how has the platform already flattened their preferences toward predictability?
- **Their source code** — what childhood friends/safety/rewards driver is pre-installed and triggerable?
Output: the ambient field your design will operate inside.

### Stage 2 — PCP DESIGN (the Context Architect, second pass)
Design the drift: the **Perception** shift, the **Context/Category** reclassification (pick the single loaded category word — "this isn't a purchase, this is reclaiming your mornings"; "you're not being sold to, you're being helped"), and the **Permission** that makes the behavior feel obviously allowed. Name the move that builds the *perfect recipient first* and deliberately delays the ask.

### Stage 3 — CONDITIONS BUILD (the Conditions Builder)
Turn the PCP design + condition chain into concrete, sequenced build steps: funnel touchpoints, content sequence, onboarding flow, environment design, the order of belief/identity/permission installation. This is where the abstract becomes a spec a copywriter or designer can execute.

### Stage 4 — DEFENSE / ETHICS GATE (the Defense/Ethics Auditor) — MANDATORY, BLOCKING
Run the dual-use gate on your own design:
1. **What technique am I running?** Name it honestly (PCP, fractionation, category-flip, recipient-building).
2. **Defensive mirror:** if this were run on me, how would I detect it, and would I feel manipulated or helped?
3. **The surface test:** would I defend this if the target saw the full design? (Hughes: every implied move must be defensible if surfaced.)
4. **Outcome-on-merits:** stripped of the engineered receptivity, does the end-state genuinely serve the target?
5. **Destabilization check:** am I *reducing* the audience's destabilization and supplying genuine clarity, or *manufacturing* chaos to sell the cure? (The latter is the line not to cross.)
If it fails, the design is blocked or rewritten. This gate is the Plato's-Cave ethic operationalized.

### Stage 5 — FOLLOWABILITY PASS (the Followability Engineer)
Tune the delivery state so the conditions actually transmit: eliminate micro-hesitations, drop to low grade level, paint pictures, lead from genuine (not performed) confidence, set your own resonant frequency first ("where you speak from, you will speak to"). Separate "most followable" from "most correct" and make sure the design is BOTH.

## Self-application mode
The same engine runs on yourself. Desired end-state = a behavior YOU want to be automatic (post daily, ask for the sale, train). Force-map your own feed/source-code. PCP-design your own environment so the good behavior is the path of least resistance (gym bag by the door = permission engineered). The Defense gate becomes self-honesty; the Source-Code Diagnostic (`/hughes-source-code`) surfaces the childhood app driving the resistance.

## Standalone vs integrated into The Chain
- **Standalone (`/ce-design`):** run the 5 stages, emit a Context-Design Spec. No finalize for a pure spec; finalize fires if it yields deliverable copy/content. Defense/Ethics gate is always on.
- **Integrated (Chain Step 3.5 / 4):** after routing, `/ce-design` produces the conditions spec; the routed production expert (Luke Iha, Lara Acosta, etc.) writes INTO it; Step 5.5 verification and Step 6 finalize run normally; the Defense/Ethics gate maps onto `directives/quality_assurance.md` plus the dual-use veto. Add the Mandatory-Routing binding so "engineer conditions / context design / make behavior automatic" deploys `/ce-design`, never a single-tactic copy workflow alone.

## The output spec format (Context-Design Spec)
1. **Desired end-state** (one sentence).
2. **Condition chain** (end-state ← conditions, upstream).
3. **Ambient force-map** (what's already acting on them).
4. **PCP design** (perception shift / category word / permission).
5. **Recipient-build sequence** (touchpoints, order, where the ask is deferred to).
6. **Defense/Ethics verdict** (technique named, surface test passed, destabilization check).
7. **Followability notes** (delivery state, hesitation kills, grade level).
8. **Handoff** (which production expert writes this, and into what format).
