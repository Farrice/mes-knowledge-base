# Offer Stack Blueprint — Authority Flywheel (Ghostwriting → Authority System, $2K–$5K)

*Blind-pass generation artifact — produced via /jh-offer-stack + cold-offer-stack-blueprint.md prompt, 2026-07-15. Narrative grounded in `_active/linkedin/01-research/deep-icp-profile-invisible-expert.md`.*

## Audience State

**Needs-convinced** — and you have to be honest about that before you spend a dollar. The invisible expert isn't searching "hire a ghostwriter." Most of them have never typed that phrase in their life. They're searching nothing. They're scrolling LinkedIn at 9pm watching some guy with half their expertise get 400 likes explaining a concept they taught their staff three years ago, and they're feeling something they don't say out loud. That's not an in-market buyer. That's a person who needs the problem named before they'll ever evaluate a solution.

Funnel implication: this doesn't go to a call funnel cold. Education carries the persuasion load — content that names the invisibility problem, then the mechanism, then the ask.

## The Narrative (what the stack is read off)

They're excellent at the work and invisible in the market. They've been told "post more" and it felt like being told to dance at a party they didn't want to attend. What have they already tried and failed? That's the part that decides what we're allowed to sell them: they tried posting themselves — three weeks, seven likes, quit. Some hired a content agency that made them sound like a LinkedIn motivational poster and they fired them out of embarrassment. So look at the scar tissue: **generic done-for-you content is radioactive.** You cannot walk in saying "we'll write your posts." They've been burned by exactly that, and worse, the burn confirmed their private suspicion that visibility means sounding fake.

- **Problems**: expertise invisible; referral pipeline is a ceiling; watching lesser operators win deals on reach.
- **Circumstances**: every lost deal now has a "they went with someone they'd heard of" flavor; pricing pressure because nobody knows what makes them different; the resentment compounds weekly.
- **Desired outcomes**: inbound that references their thinking ("I read your piece on X — is that how you'd handle us?"); premium pricing defended by visible authority; never sounding like someone else wrote it.

## Component Stack

| Component | Neutralizes | Core/Edition | Proof attached |
|---|---|---|---|
| Voice extraction session (recorded deep interview, their words become the corpus) | Scar: "ghostwriters make me sound fake" | CORE | Play-back test: excerpts read blind to the client — "which of these did you say?" |
| Weekly authority piece FROM their calls/voice notes (never templated) | Problem: invisible expertise; Circumstance: no time to write | CORE | Before/after of an actual client post vs. their old self-written attempt |

| Inbound handling script (what to say when a post generates a DM) | Next problem: "a lead came in and I fumbled it" | CORE | — |
| Monthly metrics readout: SSI/impressions/inbound count, not vanity likes | Scar: "I posted and nothing happened" (invisible ROI) | CORE | Sample readout |
| Founder-led comment strategy sprint | Fragment: the extra-ambitious ones who want speed | EDITION — deploy when they ask "how fast can this work" | — |
| Sales-page/bio rewrite to match the new authority frame | Fragment: raised on commit signal when their profile embarrasses them mid-engagement | EDITION | — |

Every one of those traces. The voice extraction isn't a feature — it's the direct answer to the agency burn. The inbound script is next-problem absorption: the deal these people are actually afraid of isn't "content fails," it's "content works and I don't know what to do with the attention." Cover the problem after the purchase inside the purchase.

## Bridge Articulation (sequence locked)

You're the best-kept secret in your market, and it's costing you. Deals you never heard about went to people you're better than, because they were visible and you were busy doing the actual work. You've tried posting — it felt fake and died in three weeks. Maybe you paid someone to do it and fired them because they made you sound like everyone else. Here's what you actually want: buyers arriving already convinced, quoting your own thinking back at you, at prices nobody haggles because the authority is on the table before you are. That's the bridge this builds — your voice, extracted from how you already talk, published as a system, with the inbound handled. You don't learn to dance. We turn the way you already think into the asset.

**Core message line**: I turn established experts who hate self-promotion into cited authorities in their niche — using their own recorded voice, not a content template — so inbound arrives pre-sold. (Qualifies: proven expertise, revenue, hates posting. Disqualifies: beginners hunting a personal-brand shortcut.)

**Urgency**: quantified cost of inaction only — count the last quarter's "went with someone they'd heard of" losses with them on the call. No countdown timers. If they can't name a lost deal, they're not in the narrative and shouldn't be sold.

## Cold-Read Verdict

PASS with one watch-item: "authority" is a crowded word in this feed. The disqualifier ("using their own recorded voice, not a content template") is the recognition hook — it names the scar. Weakest line is the metrics component articulation; "SSI" means nothing to a cold reader — say "inbound conversations started" instead.

## Migration Note

This stack is built for needs-convinced. If it ever runs against in-market buyers (people actively shopping ghostwriters), the education components demote and differentiation/proof leads — same service, different frame. Trigger to watch: prospects arriving having already compared providers.
