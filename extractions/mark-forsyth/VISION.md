each with a mechanism and a deployment answer. Plus two things the first interview never reaches:

1. **The enchantment thesis.** *"Don't write just to be efficient and get the meaning across. Write to

   the designed room, Soylent vs a cooked meal, music, decorated houses. Then the killer observation:
   list the most-quoted lines in history, list what schools teach, *"they're the antithesis of each other."*
2. **grammar = glamour.** A *grammar* was something written down; a *glamour* was a spell you cast to
   enchant someone and carry them off. Same word. *Spelling* / *spell*. This is not decoration — it is the
   etymological argument that memorable writing IS enchantment, and it is the root metaphor for `/enchant`.

Together: 24 distinct devices, a thesis, a calibration heuristic, and a practice regimen.

## The one live contradiction between the sources (decision required)

The two interviews disagree about whether rhetorical ornament can be overdone.

- **2024, asked "do you see people overusing this?"** — *"No, no, no, I don't. I've never seen somebody
  overusing the figures of rhetoric."*
- **2026, asked when figures become "baroque and rococo… eye-rolly"** — *"there will be a point… it's like
  how many spices and how many side dishes before it all becomes a bit silly… there's always going to be a
  limit where that was too many chilies and now you're ill. But those first few flakes of chili are always
  going to be good."*

**The reconciliation is in the 2026 answer itself:** *"certainly in terms of how most people write now, you
can just throw in a few in a paragraph and you'll get something so much more than what most people write."*
The ceiling exists. Almost nobody is near it. Forsyth's practical position across both interviews is that
the live risk is **under-ornamentation**, not over-.

**Why this is a decision and not a footnote:** it runs against the grain of three things already binding in
this harness — the AI-slop ban bank, `prose_classifier.py`, and the density-over-completeness rule. Those
exist to subtract. Forsyth's method adds. If the Enchantment OS defers to the slop gates by default it will
systematically under-deploy the very thing it was built for; if it overrides them it can produce purple
prose that fails Farrice's own taste bar. The lane contract has to say which way the default runs.
