**[Ambiguity]** "Most product failures I've seen over the years had less to do about the product, and more to do with their ability to communicate its value to their audience… Communication is almost always the missing piece."

**[Appeal]** "That ONE 'Simple' Method Has Helped Me Develop Hundreds of Profitable Copy Pieces FAST…" + CTA.

**Calibration notes**: one contention carried across all seven beats; ellipses and fragments as pacing; second-person throughout; no stated deep benefit — implication carries it; conversational register with an honest blunt beat ("you're screwed").
