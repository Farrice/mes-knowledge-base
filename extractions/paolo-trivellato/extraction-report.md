## Genius Patterns

### 1. Boring-Offer Inversion
- **Unconsciously**: Frames "most boring niche on the internet" as the reason the system works — no flashy income claims available means credibility must come from the authority line and specificity, which converts higher-trust buyers.
- **Executable**: When the offer is unsexy (ops, finance, accounting, systems), do NOT manufacture sexy hooks. Lead with rare credentials + ICP-verbatim pain.
- **Deploy**: Any professional service that can't ethically make income claims. **Metric**: qualified inbound from small-engagement posts (his bar: 40 comments → 4 qualified calls).

### 2. Authority Line First
- **Unconsciously**: Opens every lead-magnet hook with a sub-8-word credential sentence, then a full stop. "I am an eight-figure agency COO." Used on 10–15+ consecutive lead magnets — named "the number one factor for the success of Kyle's hooks."
- **Executable**: Derive one authority line from personal background that (a) few can replicate, (b) is exactly relevant to the pain. Open every lead magnet with it. Full stop before anything else.
- **Deploy**: every lead-magnet hook; profile headline. **Metric**: "What's next?" pull — reader continues past line one.

### 3. One Resource = One Main Pain (the plaster model)
- **Unconsciously**: "Prospect has a bunch of scars... each lead magnet is putting a plaster. Eventually there's some scars so big they need to work with you." Rejects broad lead magnets on sight.
- **Executable**: Each resource solves exactly one painful problem. Pick one; ignore everything else that week. Each ICP pain point becomes its own future resource (a content roadmap for free).
- **Deploy**: lead-magnet planning. **Metric**: pain named in the ICP's own phrasing; resource maps 1:1.

### 4. ICP-Verbatim Pain Sourcing
- **Unconsciously**: Pulls pain language from call transcripts — "I cannot step out of client work without things breaking," "Delivery is messy and every client gets a different experience," "Margins disappear past five or six team members," "No weekly dashboard, so I'm flying blind."
- **Executable**: Mine call transcripts / competitor content / 50×15-min paid market-research calls (Amazon gift cards if needed) for first-person pain sentences. Use them verbatim as hooks and section headers.
- **Deploy**: before any content or lead-magnet build. **Metric**: reader reaction "this person is in my head."

### 5. Copy-Paste Solution Bias
- **Unconsciously**: "People are so lazy they want it handed on a silver platter... that's why all the lead magnets going viral right now are Claude-related." Nobody reads 50 pages of plain text — "how concisely can I convey the 20% that gets 80% of the results."
- **Executable**: Shape every resource as a blueprint/prompt/template usable today. If it's a prompt they can paste into Claude and get instant output, it's the ceiling of the format.
- **Deploy**: resource design. **Metric**: recipient gets a win within 24h without asking questions.

### 6. Comment-Gate Distribution Physics
- **Unconsciously**: Explains comment CTAs as network mechanics, not engagement hacks — each comment surfaces the post to that commenter's connections; influencer comments chain it further ("huge spider web"); commenters must connect to receive the DM, and connections auto-follow.
- **Executable**: Gate the resource behind one relevant keyword comment. No links in the post while it's climbing; add the link only after virality tapers, to optimize for conversion.
- **Deploy**: every lead-magnet post. **Metric**: comment velocity; follower growth per post.

### 7. Perceived-Value / FOMO Stack (honest)
- **Unconsciously**: Just before the CTA: "This is what we only give to paid clients" / "My team is not going to be happy about this" / "I'm taking this down in 24 hours" — and follows through on the claims.
- **Executable**: Stack 1–2 exclusivity/urgency lines before the comment CTA. Everything stated must be true or become true.
- **Deploy**: lead-magnet posts, sales emails. **Metric**: skeptics comment anyway ("no harm in a quick comment").

### 8. Opt-In Interposition (rented → owned)
- **Unconsciously**: The DM never contains the resource — it contains the **opt-in page** (name + email only; screenshot preview; instant delivery). "LinkedIn is a rented audience... Email is owned. Without LinkedIn, email does not exist — LinkedIn is the perfect segue."
- **Executable**: Comment → reply "check your DMs" → DM opt-in link → email delivers resource → welcome sequence triggers. If someone refuses to opt in, send the resource directly ("you're only losing a subscriber").
- **Deploy**: every lead magnet. **Metric**: hundreds of subscribers per viral post; list growth per week.

### 9. Awareness-Stage Rotation (anti-content-pillars)
- **Unconsciously**: "I'm very against content pillars." Plans by how the audience FEELS: problem-unaware / pain-aware / solution-aware. Volume mix he runs: personal 20%, authority 40%, educational 30%, social proof 10%; or 1 lead magnet + 2 posts per awareness stage per week, cycled.
- **Executable**: Map ICP awareness stages; assign each post a stage job (name the unnamed problem / show the cost / prove the solution + why you). Objections from sales calls become solution-aware hooks in quotation marks.
- **Deploy**: weekly content planning. **Metric**: leads arrive at different awareness stages; compounding after ~6 months, first leads fast.

### 10. Trust-Stack Ascension ($100 workshop → consulting)
- **Unconsciously**: Front offer = $100 workshop solving ONE narrow problem ("low enough people do not overthink it, high enough to filter out anyone who just wouldn't pay"). Backend = done-with-you/done-for-you at a few thousand/mo. "The upgrade conversation is not a sales call — the experience did the selling."
- **Executable**: One narrow promise with exact before/after wording; deliver live with templates; show "doing this every week"; naturally point at where more help speeds them up; 2-min pitch at end; then 3–5 follow-up emails where **most upgrades actually happen**.
- **Deploy**: monetizing a warm email list. **Metric**: workshop→consulting upgrade rate in the follow-up window.

### 11. Emotion Over Information
- **Unconsciously**: From analyzing 2,000+ viral LinkedIn posts (self-reported): "the algorithm doesn't reward information, it rewards emotion." "We're selling to businesses, but we're selling to people" — what keeps the CEO up at night, who they idolize, worst nightmares.
- **Executable**: Before writing, answer desire (who do they want to be) and fear (what would break them) for the ICP. Dwell time is the ranking metric; emotion earns dwell.
- **Deploy**: all content. **Metric**: dwell/comments from ICP, not raw impressions.

### 12. Name-Jacking / Trend-Jacking
- **Unconsciously**: Rides attention pools that are already full (Claude launches, LLM releases, funding news). Recipe he hands out: take a prompt that automates one step of your own service delivery → give it away tied to the trending tool → "mentioning Claude in the hook... is overpowered."
- **Executable**: Follow key X accounts 15 min/morning (X moves faster than LinkedIn); when an LLM/tool launches, ship a lead magnet tying YOUR SOP to it within days. 3,000 comments on one AI-creative-strategy magnet; ~7,000 across 4 variations (self-reported).
- **Deploy**: 1–2×/month when a genuine trend intersects your service. **Metric**: comment count vs. account baseline.

### 13. Assume-the-Yes DM Close
- **Unconsciously**: Opener after delivering the resource: "Just curious — what caught your attention?" (booked 72 calls in 26 days for one client, self-reported). Then one deeper question on their challenge, then case study, then: don't ask for a call — **offer a specific slot and time**. 3–5 messages max; never 10.
- **Executable**: Script: deliver → curiosity question (follow up once if silent) → challenge question → "funnily enough, I help people just like you" + case study → assume the yes with a concrete slot.
- **Deploy**: every lead-magnet commenter DM thread. **Metric**: booked calls per 100 comments.

### 14. Self-Serving Proposition (first-client-is-you)
- **Unconsciously**: "Treat yourself as your first client" — your own account is the case study that answers "how do I know this works?" First case study > early revenue; pay-on-performance ($100/booked meeting or retainer-after-milestone), never free trials (free clients "don't care and won't continue").
- **Executable**: Run your own system publicly; convert the first result into a case study placed everywhere (site, headline, featured, post, lead-magnet breakdown).
- **Deploy**: cold-start operators. **Metric**: first pay-on-results client signed; conviction transfer on calls.

## Hidden Knowledge

- **Sundays outperform**: "one of the top performing days of the week" — against the standard weekdays-only advice. Never skip Sundays.
- **Daily posting is a testing cadence**, not a presence ritual — faster format testing → faster doubling-down.
- **Profile = the funnel's landing page**: every post's third job is driving profile views; a weak profile "just burns the audience you are building."
- **About section can't hold links** → the only zero-friction CTA there is "DM me one word."
- **Link-after-viral timing**: links suppress reach only while the post climbs; after taper, add the link to convert stragglers.
- **$100 filter economics**: "people who wouldn't pay you $100 probably wouldn't pay you $1,000" — and paying once makes paying again easy.
- **Eleven-email effect**: two weeks on the list ≈ 11 emails read → "the sale is almost made before the pitch lands."
- **Case-study lead magnet paradox**: lowest virality, highest revenue per impression — "tens of thousands of impressions on a sales asset is unheard of."
- **Client fleet as trend radar**: 50 client accounts = 50 home pages of signal; solo operators substitute their own feed + X follows.

## Methodology (the spine)

**MAP → PROFILE → ROTATE → MAGNET → GATE → CAPTURE → NURTURE → ASCEND**
1. MAP: ICP pains from transcripts/research calls (each pain = future resource) + desire/fear + awareness stages.
2. PROFILE: 4 elements as landing page — headline (outcome+timeframe+mechanism+authority), custom button (calendar), featured (opt-in/lead magnet), About (ICP call-out → pain → mechanism → proof bullets → one-word DM CTA). Two paths: high-intent → button; curious → featured/email.
3. ROTATE: daily post, no exceptions incl. Sundays; 20/40/30/10 personal/authority/educational/social-proof; planned against pain map, not reactive.
4. MAGNET: 1–3/week; authority line + in-my-head pain + copy-paste solution; perceived-value stack; one keyword comment CTA; no link until taper.
5. GATE: reply "check your DMs"; manual (30–60 min time block or VA) or automation at own risk.
6. CAPTURE: DM opt-in page (name+email, preview screenshot); deliver by email; fallback = send direct.
7. NURTURE: welcome E1 delivery/orientation → E2 story (no pitch) → E3 quick win (24h actionable) → E4 case study (decisions, fears, mindset) → E5 clear invite. Then 3 emails/week (e.g. Tue/Thu/Sat), 2 value + 1 sell; every CTA → $99 workshop or book a call.
8. ASCEND: $100 workshop, one narrow promise, live delivery + templates + Q&A, 2-min pitch; follow-up 3–5 emails over weeks (content upgrade / social proof / new capacity / case study / honest scarcity); warmest leads get ≥2 personal invitations; most upgrades happen here.
