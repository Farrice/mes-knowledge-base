## Visual observations not fully carried by the transcript

- The Art-SYNc 2025 poster is built **live across levels 2–5** — same layout progressively gaining subhead, micro-routes, tapering pattern, then the rotated disruption block. It is the canonical visual exemplar for the movement ladder.
- The flow line Satori draws over the Buck poster is a smooth S/heart curve — the three anchors are corners of a triangle (bottom-right → top-left → bottom-left), not a straight scan line.
- In the Dazed example the masthead is *black on dark green* — hierarchy is engineered so the lit face wins first fixation, proving hierarchy > salience defaults.
- The 3-forms-of-contrast Nike poster keeps the shoes black-on-black in one zone — Satori flags it as a deliberate near-miss, showing contrast is zonal, not global.
- The app-UI contrast walkthrough (24:00–25:30) shows a fashion e-commerce app: black labels on white ground, bold color photo cards each internally contrasting (red-on-blue, blue-on-orange), red notification dots contrasting the phone UI itself — the "double whammy" (element contrasts its container AND its own content contrasts internally).
