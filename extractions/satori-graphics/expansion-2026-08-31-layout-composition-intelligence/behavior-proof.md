# Behavior-Changing Proof

## Cold-Start Positive Control

**Input:** “Create a premium health-performance campaign system titled `TRAIN FOR THE NEXT DECADE`, with proof copy and a `Book your baseline` CTA, for a desktop hero, square post, and vertical story.”

### Generic Route Baseline

“Use a premium dark palette, strong headline, athlete image, clean spacing, and a bold CTA. Adapt it for desktop, square, and vertical.”

This is compatible with many layouts but makes no composition decision. It leaves focal competition, eye path, grid, tension, and cross-format hierarchy to the executor.

### New Route Output

# Composition Intelligence Brief — Train For The Next Decade

**Communication problem:** Make long-term training feel urgent without making it feel like another short-term fitness challenge.  
**Audience action / feeling:** Feel the cost of drift, then book a baseline.  
**Composition status:** APPLIED

## Leverage Point

- Winner: `NEXT DECADE`, not the athlete portrait and not the CTA.
- Dominance mechanism: 2.5× scale plus isolation in the upper-left third.
- Competing primary refused: the athlete image stays lower-contrast and points back toward the title.

## Internal Rhythm / Eye Path

- Primary path: `NEXT DECADE` → one proof line → `Book your baseline`.
- Micro-route: small diagnostic markers around the athlete image, rejoining at the proof line.
- Trusted rhythm: 8-point baseline; wide title-to-proof pause; tight proof-to-CTA grouping.

## Grid Commitment

- Grid: asymmetric 12-column grid; title spans 7 columns, image spans 5.
- Deliberate break: the word `DECADE` crosses one column boundary to create forward pressure; all other elements stay on-grid.

## Movement & Temporal Beats

- Level: 6 for the scroll hero; Level 2 for the static adaptations.
- Beats: **Punch** — isolated title; **Slow** — one proof sentence; **Pull** — diagnostic markers and athlete gaze; **Release** — quiet CTA field with extra surrounding space.

## Friction Budget

- Pause: partially crop the final letters of `DECADE` against the right boundary.
- Meaning served: the future extends beyond the current frame.
- Release zone: clear field around the CTA; no texture or competing markers.

## Transfer Adaptations

| Format | Invariant | Adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Desktop hero | Title remains leverage; CTA remains final stop | Horizontal 7/5 split; complete four-beat scroll sequence |
| Square post | `NEXT DECADE` remains dominant | Stack title over image; remove micro-route markers; retain one boundary crop |
| Vertical story | Title and CTA remain first/final | Use tall condensed title; athlete sits between proof and CTA; move release zone to bottom 20% |

## Production Constraints

- Must preserve: title dominance; one grid break; quiet CTA release.
- Must avoid: generic neon fitness glow; equal-weight proof cards; cropping each format from desktop.
- Thumbnail test: at 64 px, `NEXT DECADE` must remain the only readable phrase and the CTA must remain the final contrast stop.

## Human Taste Checkpoint

Does the controlled crop read as intelligent forward pressure—or as an overfamiliar editorial trick? Farrice decides after the first visible artifact.

### Evidence Of Change

The source changes five observable decisions that the baseline leaves unspecified: one leverage point, ordered eye stops, purpose-selected asymmetric grid with one break, four temporal beats, and format-specific hierarchy adaptations. Structural behavior change: **PASS**. Visual taste quality: **UNTESTED**.
